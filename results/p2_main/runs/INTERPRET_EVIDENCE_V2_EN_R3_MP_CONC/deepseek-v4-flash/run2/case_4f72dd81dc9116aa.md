### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process was restructured: activities A05 and A08 became less frequent and moved earlier in the trace, while A10 and A16 became more frequent and moved later, and the routing shifted so that A10 replaced A05 as a central hub for many transitions. The number of qualifying evidence items (55) far exceeds the 0.46 expected by chance, and the changes are consistent across occurrence, position, successor, and co-occurrence indicators. A competing explanation that the differences are due to random sampling is effectively ruled out by the volume and consistency of the evidence. A limitation is that all evidence is control-flow based; no timing or resource changes are observed, so the hypothesis concerns only the structural reorganisation of the process.

Other differences: The successor of A06 changed from A05 to A10, and A17 appears as a new successor of A05, while A09 becomes a successor of A10 instead of A05. These are part of the same routing shift but are not central to the main hypothesis.
