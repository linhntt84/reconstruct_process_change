### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed in the repetition and ordering of activities A15 and A16. Both activities are executed more often per trace, and the order between them shifted: A16 now more often precedes A15, while A15 immediately preceding A16 became less common. This is directly shown by the increased share of A16→A15 transitions and the decreased share of A15→A16 at lag 1. The screening comparison (4 control-flow items vs 0.47 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely. A limitation is that all items reflect the same underlying change, and no time or resource evidence is available; also, the lag evidence only covers the A15→A16 order, so the A16→A15 lag is inferred from the successor distribution rather than measured directly.
