### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: activities A14, A15, and A20 moved from before A21/A22/A23 to after them, and A40 now connects directly to A21 instead of to those activities. This is shown directly by relational order reversals (A21/A22/A23 now precede A14/A15/A20), by the later mean positions of A14/A15/A20, and by the successor changes of A14/A15/A20 and A40. The number of control-flow items (24) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.4), so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. A limitation is that the exact new order among A14/A15/A20 and A23/A27 is not fully fixed, as some pairs show mixed order.

Other differences: The time between A31 and A22 decreased by about half, from a median of about 1.01 hours to about 0.50 hours. This is a separate timing change, supported by a single strong item. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a different variant mix rather than a change in the underlying process, but the consistency across many indicators makes that unlikely.
