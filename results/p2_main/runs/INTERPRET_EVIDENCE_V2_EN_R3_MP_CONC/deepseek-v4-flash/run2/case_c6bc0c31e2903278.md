### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process was modified to replace activity A01 with activities A08 and A09, and to make A03 and A10 optional. In the earlier window, A01 was executed in about a third of cases, always after A03, A07, or A10; in the later window A01 is absent, while A08 and A09 appear in new positions and the routing from A06, A03, A10, and A07 now leads to A08 and A12 instead of A01. A03 and A10, previously mandatory, are now each present in only half of the cases. The large number of qualifying evidence items (49 vs. 0.32 expected by chance) and the consistency across occurrence, routing, and ordering indicators make a chance explanation unlikely.

Other differences: A04 and A12 increase in participation, and the lag between A06 and A07/A10 changes, consistent with the new routing. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is a shift in case mix or a new variant that happens to use the new activities, but the direct routing and ordering evidence makes a structural change the most plausible interpretation.
