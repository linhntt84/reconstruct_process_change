### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained A22 and A23, with A21 before A22 before A23 (A21→A22→A23). [OCC001, REL001, REL005, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, about half of the cases take a new alternative path between A21 and A23 that includes the activities A27, A28, and A33 (in any order) and bypasses A22, while the other half still follows the original A21→A22→A23 path. This is directly shown by the drop in A22 participation from 100% to 51%, the appearance of A27/A28/A33 in 49% of traces, the redistribution of A21's outgoing edges, and the shift in the A21-to-A23 lag from 2 to 4 steps. The number of qualifying items (26) far exceeds the 0.4 expected by chance, and all items are consistent with this single structural change. A competing explanation that the new activities are simply rare variants is ruled out by their substantial and coordinated presence. The main limitation is that the evidence is purely control-flow; no timing or resource changes are observed, but none are needed for this structural hypothesis.

Other differences: None beyond the described path change.
