### Observed differences

- A11 appears in about 57% of traces in the later window, up from about 29% earlier; its mean occurrences per trace rose correspondingly [OCC001].
- A09 and A11 became an exclusive alternative pair: earlier, 41.5% of traces contained neither, while after the change every trace contains exactly one of them (A09 in about 44%, A11 in about 57%), and they never co-occur in either window [REL003].
- A06 now leads to A09 and A11 much more often: its outgoing transitions to A11 rose from about 4% to 31% and to A09 from about 6% to 23% [DIR002]. A06 and A11 now co-occur in 57% of traces (vs 29%) and A06 precedes A11 in all those cases [REL001]. The gap between A06 and A11 shortened: lag 1 rose from 13% to 55% [LAG003]; similarly A06-A09 lag 1 rose from 22% to 53% [LAG002].
- A08's link to A11 weakened: earlier A08 always preceded A11 when both occurred and A11 never appeared alone; after the change A11 appears alone in about 27% of traces and the order between A08 and A11 is mixed (A08 before A11 16%, A11 before A08 15%) [REL002]. The A08-A11 lag increased: lag 1 fell from 66% to 39% [LAG004].
- A09 and A11 moved earlier in the trace: A09's mean position fell from 0.97 to 0.75 [POS002], A11's from 0.96 to 0.74 [POS003]. A08 moved later: mean position rose from 0.66 to 0.75 [POS001]. The gap between A06 and A08 widened: lag 1-2 fell from about 80% to 38% [LAG001].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed so that A11 became a much more common activity, and A09 and A11 now form an exclusive alternative pair that appears in every trace, typically reached directly from A06, whereas before they were optional and A11 was more often reached via A08. This is supported by the large increase in A11 participation [OCC001], the shift of A06's outgoing transitions toward A09 and A11 [DIR002], the increased co-occurrence and fixed order of A06 before A11 [REL001], the shortened lags between A06 and A11/A09 [LAG003, LAG002], and the fact that A09 and A11 now cover all traces without co-occurring [REL003]. The weakening of the A08-A11 link [REL002, LAG004] is consistent with A11 no longer being tied to A08.

Limitations: Some earlier-window distributions for A05, A09, and A11 are based on few traces [DIR001, DIR004, DIR005], so the exact before state for those activities is uncertain. The evidence cannot rule out that the change was gradual rather than a single shift, or that the case mix changed.

Other differences: A08 moved later in the trace [POS001], A09 and A11 moved earlier [POS002, POS003], and the gap between A06 and A08 widened [LAG001]. These are consistent with the main hypothesis but not required for it.
