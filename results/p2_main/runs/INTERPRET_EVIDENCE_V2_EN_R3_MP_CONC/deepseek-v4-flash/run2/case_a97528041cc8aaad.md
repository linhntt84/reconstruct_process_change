### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed so that A02 became a common early step inserted between A14 and A15, while A06 and A10 were largely dropped. In the earlier window, A14 usually went directly to A15 (about 84% of its transitions) and sometimes to A06 or A10; A02 was rare (15% of traces). In the later window, A14 goes to A02 in about 60% of its transitions, A02 goes to A15 in about 87% of its transitions, and A02 appears in about 65% of traces. Positional and lag evidence confirm that A14 now immediately precedes A02 and A02 precedes A15. A06 and A10 participation dropped to about 5% each. This is consistent with a new path A14→A02→A15 replacing the previous A14→A15 and A14→A06/A10 paths. The large number of qualifying items (87 vs 1 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely. A competing explanation that the observed changes are due to sampling variation is ruled out by the screening comparison, though the exact business reason for the change is not identifiable from this evidence.

Other differences: A19, A21, and A24 now lead to A25 instead of A30, while A20, A22, and A23 now lead to A30 instead of A25; A29 now leads to A20/A22/A23 instead of A21/A19/A24. These are separate routing changes not part of the main hypothesis.
