### Observed differences

- Activities A27, A36, A37, and A42 each occurred in essentially every trace before (100% participation) but in only about half the traces after (53%, 47%, 48%, and 48% respectively). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A25's outgoing transitions changed from a split among A42 (34%), A36 (32%), and A37 (30%) to almost exclusively A28 (97%). [DIR001]
- A26's outgoing transitions changed from 99% to A27 to a split: A27 (52%), A37 (16%), A42 (16%), and A36 (13%). [DIR002]
- A36, A37, and A42 each gained A31 as a new successor (35%, 26%, and 31% respectively) and almost lost A28 as a successor (from 34%, 31%, and 32% to 1% each). [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- The order between A28 and the group A36/A37/A42 reversed: before, A36/A37/A42 preceded A28 in about 99% of co-occurring traces; after, A28 precedes them in about 45–46% of co-occurring traces. [REL014, REL015, REL016]
- The order between A26 and the group A36/A37/A42 also reversed: before, A36/A37/A42 preceded A26 in about 99–100%; after, A26 precedes them in about 45–46%. [REL006, REL007, REL008]
- A27 became mutually exclusive with A36/A37/A42: before they co-occurred in 100% of traces; after they co-occur in about 0.5–1% of traces, with A27 alone in about 53% and the other alone in about 47%. [REL011, REL012, REL013]
- A25 and A28 became adjacent: before they were 4–5 steps apart in 91% and 9% of traces; after they are 1 step apart in 98% of traces. [LAG001]
- A37 and A31 became much closer: before they were 5 steps apart in the only trace where both appeared; after they are 1–3 steps apart in 93 traces. [LAG008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the control flow: the process moved from a path where A25 led to A36/A37/A42 and then to A28, and A26 led to A27, to a new structure where A25 leads directly to A28, A28 then leads to A36/A37/A42 (in about half the cases), A26 also leads to A36/A37/A42, and A27 becomes an alternative to that group. Specifically, A25's successor changed from A36/A37/A42 to A28, the order between A28 and A36/A37/A42 reversed, A26's successor changed from A27 to a split including A36/A37/A42, and A27 became mutually exclusive with A36/A37/A42. The participation of A27, A36, A37, and A42 dropped from 100% to about half, indicating that these activities are now optional or belong to alternative branches. The new activity A31 appears as a successor of A36/A37/A42.

Other differences: A26 and A31 became further apart (lag increased from 2 to 4–5 in about 43% of traces). [LAG005] This is consistent with the reordering but not part of the main hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a shift in case mix rather than a change in the process model, since the windows are finite samples. However, the large number of consistent indicators (48 items vs 0.73 expected by chance) makes this unlikely.
