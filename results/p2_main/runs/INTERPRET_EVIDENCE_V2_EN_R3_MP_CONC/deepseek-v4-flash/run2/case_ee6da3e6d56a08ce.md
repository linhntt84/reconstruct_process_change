### Observed differences

- In the later window, activity A02 appears in about 58 in 100 traces (mean 2.37 occurrences per trace), whereas it was completely absent in the earlier window. [OCC001]
- In the later window, A15's outgoing transitions are exclusively to A02 (100% of observed transitions), whereas before they went to A07 (about 52%), A08 (about 25%) and A12 (about 23%). [DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new activity, A02, was inserted between A15 and the subsequent activities A07, A08 and A12. In the earlier window, A15 led directly to A07, A08 and A12; in the later window, A15 leads to A02, which then leads to A07, A08 and A12. This is directly shown by the change in A15's outgoing transitions (from A07/A08/A12 to A02), the appearance of A02 with its own outgoing transitions to A07/A08/A12, the co-occurrence of A02 with A15 and with A07/A08/A12, and the consistent one-step increase in the distance between A15 and the downstream activities. The screening comparison (16 items observed vs. 0.2 expected by chance) indicates the overall difference is unlikely to be a sampling artefact.

Other differences: The participation of A07, A08 and A12 increased slightly (e.g., A07 from about 35% to about 39% of traces), and the distance between A07 and A15 shifted from a mix of 3 and 5 steps to mostly 3 steps. [LAG004] These are minor and consistent with the insertion of A02.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A02 is not a new activity but an existing one that was previously unobserved in the sample; however, its complete absence in the earlier window and its systematic position between A15 and the downstream activities make the insertion hypothesis the most direct interpretation.
