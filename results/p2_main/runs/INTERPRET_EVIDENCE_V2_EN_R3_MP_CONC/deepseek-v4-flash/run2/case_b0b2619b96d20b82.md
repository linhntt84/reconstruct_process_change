### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control-flow order: the path that previously ran A04 → A01 → A06 now runs A01 → A04 → A06. This is directly shown by the successor changes for A01, A04 and A06, and is corroborated by the relational and lag evidence, which consistently show that the adjacency between A01 and A04 and between A04 and A06 swapped, while A01 and A06 became farther apart. The frequency changes (A01 decreasing, A04 increasing) are consistent with this reordering. The main limitation is that the evidence covers only control flow; no timing or resource changes are observed, and the frequency shifts could partly reflect a change in how often the new path is taken rather than a pure reordering. Other differences: A06 still leads to A05 in both windows, but its share of outgoing transitions to A05 increased slightly, and it no longer leads to A04. [DIR003]
