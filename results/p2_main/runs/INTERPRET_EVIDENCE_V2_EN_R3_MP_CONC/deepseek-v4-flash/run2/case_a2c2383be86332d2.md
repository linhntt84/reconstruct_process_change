### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process changed by removing activity A07 from the flow: A08 now connects directly to A05 and A06 instead of passing through A07. This is supported by A07's complete disappearance, the change in A08's successors, and the corresponding shortening of lags between activities that previously surrounded A07. The evidence count (35 items vs 0.19 expected by chance) is far above what a stable process would produce.

Other differences: A new activity A01 appears in about 35% of later traces, always followed by A03; A02 becomes less frequent (from about 48% to 28% of traces). These are separate from the A07 removal.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 was always a rare variant that happened to appear only in the later sample, but the large number of consistent items makes this unlikely.
