### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A28, A29, A30, A32 and A33 each appeared in about 48% of traces; in the later window they appear in about 99.5% of traces. Activities A34–A38 similarly rose from about 52% to about 98% of traces. [OCC001–OCC010]
- In the earlier window, A25's outgoing transitions were spread across both A28–A33 and A34–A38 (each roughly 8–15% of transitions). In the later window, A25's outgoing transitions go almost exclusively to A28–A33 (A28 0.195, A29 0.2, A30 0.15, A32 0.21, A33 0.24), with only 0.5% to A35. [DIR001]
- In the earlier window, A28–A33 and A34–A38 never co-occurred (cooccurrence 0.0); in the later window they co-occur in about 97.5% of traces, with A28–A33 always before A34–A38. [REL025–REL029, REL033–REL037, REL040–REL044, REL046–REL050, REL051–REL055]
- In the earlier window, A25 co-occurred with A28–A33 in 48% of traces and with A34–A38 in 52%; in the later window it co-occurs with both in about 99% of traces, always before them. [REL001–REL010]
- Positions shifted: A28–A33 moved earlier (mean position from about 0.73 to about 0.63), while A34–A38 moved later (mean from about 0.72 to about 0.76). A25 moved earlier (0.635 to 0.554) and A26 later (0.82 to 0.845). [POS001–POS012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from two alternative branches — one containing A28–A33 and the other A34–A38 — to a single sequential path in which A28–A33 are executed before A34–A38. In the earlier window, A25 led to either the A28–A33 group or the A34–A38 group, and the two groups never appeared together. In the later window, A25 leads only to the A28–A33 group, which is then followed by the A34–A38 group, and all these activities appear in nearly every trace. This is directly shown by the cooccurrence changes (from 0 to about 0.975 for cross-group pairs) and by the redistribution of A25's outgoing transitions. The position shifts (A28–A33 earlier, A34–A38 later) are consistent with this sequential ordering.

Other differences: The participation of all ten activities increased from about half of traces to nearly all traces, which is part of the same change (the two branches merged into one path). No other differences are indicated.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two branches were not mutually exclusive before but simply rarely co-occurred due to sampling; however, the cooccurrence of 0.0 in 200 traces makes that unlikely, and the DIR and POS evidence corroborate the sequential change.
