### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A30, A31, and A36 each occurred on average about 3.4–3.5 times per trace and were repeated in about 75% of traces; in the later window they occur about once per trace and repeat in about 1–2% of traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a repeated loop involving A30, A31, and A36 to a single pass through these activities, and that A36 now routes to A25 instead of back to A30. The repetition of A30, A31, and A36 dropped from about 3.4 occurrences per trace to about 1, and the interleaving/repetition between A30 and A36 fell from 75% to 2%. The successor change of A36 from A30 to A25 is directly observed. The evidence is consistent and based on full trace support for the occurrence and relation indicators. A competing explanation that this is a rare variant is unlikely given the number of qualifying items (13 vs 0.46 expected by chance). A limitation is that the lag indicators for repeated occurrences have very low support in the later window, but the occurrence and relation evidence is strong.

Other differences: The outgoing distributions of A30 and A31 show minor redistributions to a few additional activities, but their dominant successors (A31 and A36 respectively) remain the same. [DIR001, DIR002]
