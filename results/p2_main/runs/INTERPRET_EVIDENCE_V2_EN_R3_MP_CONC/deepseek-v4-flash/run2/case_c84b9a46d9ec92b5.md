### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A19, A20, and A25 each appeared in about 31–37% of traces (A19 36.5%, A20 31%, A25 35%). In the later window, A19 appears in 0.5% of traces, and A20 and A25 do not appear at all. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the earlier window, A41 was followed by A19, A20, or A25 in about 86.5% of its outgoing transitions (A19 29%, A20 26%, A25 31.5%). In the later window, A41 is followed by A26 in about 86% of its outgoing transitions, and A19/A20/A25 are no longer among its successors. [DIR005]
- In the earlier window, A19, A20, and A25 were followed by A26 in about 30–35% of traces (A19→A26 35%, A20→A26 30%, A25→A26 35%). In the later window, these activities are essentially absent, and A41 is followed by A26 after one step in about 86% of traces, whereas before it was after two steps in about 85% of traces. [REL004, REL009, REL013, LAG001]
- The routing from A21 changed: before, A21 went to A31 in about 54% of its transitions, A23 in 29%, and A24 in 14%; after, A31 is 38%, A23 25%, and A24 35%. [DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed such that activities A19, A20, and A25, which were previously executed in about a third of cases between A41 and A26, are no longer executed, and A41 now leads directly to A26. This is supported by the disappearance of these activities, the change in A41's successors, and the shortening of the A41-to-A26 lag. The evidence cannot rule out that these activities were moved elsewhere or renamed, but within the observed activities they are absent.

Other differences: The routing from A21 shifted, with A24 becoming more common and A31 less common, while A21 remains present.
