### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process replaced a set of alternative optional activities (A14, A17–A20) with a new set of activities (A15, A16, A25–A27) that are all executed together in every trace, between A43 and A28. The old activities disappeared entirely, the new ones appeared in every trace, and the new ones co-occur with each other without a fixed order, whereas the old ones were mutually exclusive alternatives. The screening comparison shows 92 control-flow items against 0.72 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is far beyond sampling variation. A competing explanation that the new activities are simply renamed versions of the old ones is unlikely because the old activities are absent and the new ones are all executed together, not as alternatives.

Other differences: A28 moved later in the trace and A43 moved earlier, consistent with the insertion of the new block of activities between them. [POS011, POS012]
