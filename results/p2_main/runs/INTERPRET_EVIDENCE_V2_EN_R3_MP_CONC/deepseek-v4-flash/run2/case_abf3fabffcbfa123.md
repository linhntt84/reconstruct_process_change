### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed from having a branch that included A04 and a cluster of A32/A33/A38 to a main path that goes through A14, A15, A16, A26, A27 and A28, with A04 and the A32/A33/A38 cluster becoming rare or absent. In the earlier window, A04 was present in about 68% of traces and A32/A33/A38 in about half, while A27 was present in about half; in the later window, A04 dropped to about 21.5%, A32/A33/A38 to 0.5%, and A27 rose to 99.5% and now co-occurs with A14, A15, A26 and A28 almost always. Routing also shifted: A14 now goes to A15 instead of A04, and A26 now goes to A27 instead of to A32/A33/A38. The large number of qualifying items (62 vs. 0.79 expected by chance) makes it unlikely that this is a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A04 and the A32/A33/A38 cluster still exist but are executed only rarely in the later window; the evidence shows their near-absence, not their removal from the process.

Other differences: A08 and A02 increased in participation (from 5% to 20.5% and from 5% to 15% respectively), but these activities are not part of the main hypothesis.
