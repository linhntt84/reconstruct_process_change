- In the earlier window, A14 and A15 occurred in every trace; in the later window they occur in about 52 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports that in the later window, about half the cases skip a block of activities (A14, A15, and the alternative A20/A21 or A22) that used to be executed between A38 and A23; instead A38 is directly followed by A23. This is directly shown by the new successor A23 from A38, the lag-1 adjacency between A38 and A23, and the drop in participation of the skipped activities. The block still occurs in about half the cases, so the change is a partial bypass rather than a complete removal. A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the process now has two distinct variants (one with the block, one without) rather than a conditional skip within a single flow.
