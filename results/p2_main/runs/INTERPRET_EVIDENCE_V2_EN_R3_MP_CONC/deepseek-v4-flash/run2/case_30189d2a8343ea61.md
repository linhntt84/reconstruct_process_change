### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20, A25, A26, and A27 appear in about 20% of traces each (A19 22%, A20 22.5%, A25 18.5%, A26 19.5%, A27 17.5%), whereas in the earlier window they were essentially absent (A27 1%, the others 0%). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

- Each of these new activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) in the later window leads to A28 in about 95–98% of its outgoing transitions. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new intermediate step was added between A43 and A28: in the later window, after A43, one of A19, A20, A25, A26, or A27 is executed, and then A28, whereas in the earlier window A43 led directly to A28. This is supported by the shift in A43's outgoing distribution, the new activities' outgoing to A28, and the lag change from one to two steps. The number of consistent indicators (50 items vs. 0.68 expected by chance) makes sampling variation an unlikely explanation.

Other differences: A06 becomes more frequent and moves to a later position, and A48 sometimes goes through A06 before A49. These are separate from the main hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities existed but were simply not observed in the earlier window; however, their absence in 200 traces and the coherent pattern of insertion make this unlikely.
