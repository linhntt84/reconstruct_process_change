### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A01 was replaced by activity A13 in the process, preserving the same position and connections: A10 now leads to A13 instead of A01, and A13 leads to A03 as A01 did. The relative position of A13 (mean about 0.68) matches A01's previous position, and the participation and recurrence levels are similar. This substitution is directly shown by the disappearance of A01, the appearance of A13, and the unchanged predecessor–successor chain. The screening comparison (12 items vs 0.16 expected by chance) rules out random fluctuation as a whole.

Other differences: A10 participation increased slightly from about 46% to about 53% of traces. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 and A13 are the same activity under a different label, which would mean no actual process change; however, the evidence treats them as distinct activities.
