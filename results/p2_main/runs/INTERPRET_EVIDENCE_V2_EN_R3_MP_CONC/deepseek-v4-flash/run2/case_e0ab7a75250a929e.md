### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in process structure: a set of activities (A25, A26, A27, A28, A31, A36, A37, A42, A43, A44, A47, A52, A53) that were previously optional, occurring in about half the cases, became mandatory, occurring in every case, and A02, previously rare, became a common early step that precedes them. This is directly shown by the large increases in participation, the consistent A02-before-activity ordering, and the successor changes (e.g., A47 now leads to A02, A26 now leads to A27). The number of qualifying evidence items (145) far exceeds the 1.36 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a shift in case mix rather than a modification of the process itself, but the consistency across many activities and indicators makes that less likely. Other differences not part of the main hypothesis: A04, A06 and A10 became less frequent, and some activities changed their relative order (e.g., A36 now occurs before A26, whereas before A26 often preceded A36).
