- In the later window, the outgoing transitions from A22 shifted: the share going to A25 dropped from about 53% to 13%, while the share going to A21 rose from about 18% to 58%; the share to A23 stayed around 29%. [DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the routing after A22: in the later window, A22 is less often followed directly by A25 and more often by A21, and when A25 does follow, it tends to occur later (2 or 3 steps after A22) rather than immediately. This is directly shown by the shift in outgoing transition shares and the increased lag between A22 and A25. The effect is large and consistent across the two indicators, and the number of qualifying items far exceeds what would be expected by chance.

Other differences: The routing after A16 also changed, with A18 becoming less frequent and A17 more frequent as successors. Additionally, the time between completions of A33 and A34 decreased by about half, indicating a change in timing. These are separate from the A22 routing change.

A limitation is that the A22-related evidence is based on a moderate number of traces (49 before, 31 after), but the magnitude and consistency of the shift make a chance explanation unlikely.
