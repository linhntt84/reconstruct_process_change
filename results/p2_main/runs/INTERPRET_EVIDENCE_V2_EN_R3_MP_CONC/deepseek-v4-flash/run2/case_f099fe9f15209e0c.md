### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A02 is no longer executed in the later window. In the earlier window A02 was present in every trace and had a stable set of outgoing transitions to A01, A03, A04, and A05; in the later window it is completely absent, and the activities that previously followed it now occur without it. This is directly shown by the disappearance of A02's occurrences, its outgoing transitions, and the loss of co-occurrence with all four neighbours. The earlier positions of A03 and A04 and the shorter A03–A04 lag are consistent with the removal of an intermediate step (A02) from the sequence. The evidence is strong: 11 control-flow items, all pointing to the same change, far exceed the 0.15 items expected by chance. A competing explanation that A02 became a rare variant is implausible because it was present in 100% of traces before and 0% after, but the sample cannot prove that A02 is permanently removed from the process rather than simply not observed in this window.

Other differences: A03 and A04 shifted to earlier positions, and the A03–A04 lag decreased; these are likely consequences of A02's removal but could also reflect a separate reordering of the remaining activities.
