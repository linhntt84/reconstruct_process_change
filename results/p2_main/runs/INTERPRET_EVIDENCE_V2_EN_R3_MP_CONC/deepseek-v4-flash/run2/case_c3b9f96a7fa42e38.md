### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of a block of activities: A26 and A27 now occur earlier in the trace, while A35, A36, and A41 occur later, reversing their previous order. This is directly shown by the reversal in the order relations (REL001–REL006) and by the consistent shifts in positions (POS002–POS006). Additionally, A25 now routes directly to A27 instead of to A36/A41/A35, and A26 no longer routes to A30 but to A35/A36/A41, indicating a change in the routing structure around these activities. The large number of qualifying items (33 vs. 0.76 expected by chance) makes it unlikely that these differences are due to random sampling.

Other differences: A06 decreased in frequency and moved later in the trace, and A02 increased in frequency; these are not part of the main reordering hypothesis. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a shift in the composition of cases rather than a change in the process itself, but the consistency of the order reversal across multiple activity pairs makes this less likely.
