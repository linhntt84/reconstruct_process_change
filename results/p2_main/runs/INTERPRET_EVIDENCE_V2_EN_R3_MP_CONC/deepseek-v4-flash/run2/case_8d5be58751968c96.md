### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in process structure: a block of activities (A33, A35, A36, A37, A38, A39) that was mandatory in every trace is now executed in only about half the traces, and when executed, the order and connections have changed. Specifically, A31 now appears as a successor of A33/A35/A36 (previously it came after the whole block), and the distance between A30 and the later activities shortened. The large number of qualifying items (57 vs. 0.79 expected by chance) rules out chance as an explanation. The main limitation is that some lag items for the later activities have very low support in the later window (e.g., A33–A37 with only 4 traces), so the exact new order is not fully pinned down.

Other differences: A30's successor distribution became more even, and A31's position shifted slightly earlier, but these are consistent with the main change.
