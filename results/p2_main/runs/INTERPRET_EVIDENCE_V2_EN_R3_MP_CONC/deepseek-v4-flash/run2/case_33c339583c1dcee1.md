### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the ordering between two groups of activities reversed: a group containing A26 and A27 now occurs before a group containing A35, A36, A41, A42 and A43, whereas before it occurred after them. This is directly shown by the reversal in order relations (REL011–REL020) and by the consistent positional shifts (POS002–POS008). A02 became less frequent and moved later relative to these activities, and A46's link to A02 weakened (OCC001, POS001, REL001–REL010, DIR010). A limitation is that some items rest on few observations in the later window (e.g., A02 appears in only 20 traces, and many LAG items have support of 1–3 traces), but the core ordering and routing changes are supported by hundreds of traces.
