### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a major control-flow restructuring: a previously dominant branch that included A10 and a set of subsequent activities (A27, A36, A37, A42, A43, A44, A25, A26, A28, A31) is now largely bypassed. In the later window, A14 leads to A15 instead of A10, A25 leads to A28 instead of the set, and the activities that used to follow A10 are often absent. The direct successor changes (DIR003, DIR005) and the widespread drop in co-occurrence (REL items) consistently point to this shift. The evidence cannot rule out that the change reflects a different mix of case types rather than a process change, but the consistency across many activities makes a structural change the most plausible interpretation.

Other differences: A02 and A08 appear more often, and A10’s outgoing transitions become more diverse, but these are likely part of the same restructuring rather than independent changes.
