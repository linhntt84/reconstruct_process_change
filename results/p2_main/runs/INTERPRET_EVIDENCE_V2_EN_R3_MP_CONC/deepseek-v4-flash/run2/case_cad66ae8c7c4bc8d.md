### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process now bypasses a set of activities that were previously executed between A48 and A49 and between A43 and A28. In the later window, A48 leads directly to A49 instead of going through A10, and A43 leads directly to A28 instead of going through A19, A20, A25, A26, and A27. This is directly shown by the successor changes for A48 and A43, and by the co-occurrence changes that show A10 and those intermediate activities no longer appear together with their former successors. The large number of qualifying items (89 vs. about 1 expected by chance) makes it unlikely that this is a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A02, A04, and A06 increased in participation, suggesting that additional or alternative activities are now used, but this is not part of the bypass hypothesis. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the process switched to a different variant altogether, but the direct successor changes make a simple bypass the most consistent interpretation.
