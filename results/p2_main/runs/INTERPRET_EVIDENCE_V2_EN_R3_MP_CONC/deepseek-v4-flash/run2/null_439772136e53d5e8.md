### Observed differences

- Participation of A17 fell from about 60% of traces to about 46%; A18 fell from about 60% to about 47%. [OCC001, OCC002]
- A27's outgoing transitions shifted: before, about 53% went to A30, 29% to A28, and 18% to A26; after, about 13% went to A30, 29% to A28, and 58% to A26. [DIR001]
- When A27 leads to A30, the distance between them increased: before, 53% were 1 step apart and 22% were 3 steps apart; after, 13% were 1 step apart and 42% were 3 steps apart. [LAG001]
- A33 and A22 became farther apart: before, 69% were 4 steps apart and 31% were 5 steps apart; after, 45% were 4 steps apart and 55% were 5 steps apart. [LAG002]
- A33 and A23 became closer: before, 34% were 4 steps apart and 66% were 5 steps apart; after, 56% were 4 steps apart and 44% were 5 steps apart. [LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control flow around A27 and A33. A27 now much more often leads to A26 instead of A30, and when it does lead to A30, more steps intervene between them. At the same time, A33's position relative to A22 and A23 shifted: it is now farther from A22 and closer to A23. These changes are consistent with an activity being inserted or removed between these points, or with a reordering of activities in that region. The number of qualifying items (6) is well above the 0.5 expected by chance, and the routing and lag evidence for A27 agree with each other. The A33 lag changes are based on larger support (90–117 traces) and point in opposite directions for A22 and A23, which strengthens the interpretation of a positional shift.

Other differences: A17 and A18 participation decreased by about 13–14 percentage points each. This is not part of the main hypothesis and may reflect a separate change in how often these activities are executed.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the A27 and A33 changes are independent of each other, and that the A17/A18 decrease is a third, unrelated change. The A27 routing evidence rests on only 49 and 31 traces, so that part is less robust, though it is corroborated by the lag change.
