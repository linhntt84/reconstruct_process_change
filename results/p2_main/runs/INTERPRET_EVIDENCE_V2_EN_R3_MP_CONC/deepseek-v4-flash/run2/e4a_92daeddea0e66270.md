### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the timing between consecutive completions of the activity pairs A38–A41 and A40–A41 changed substantially: in the later window, the elapsed time between these completions is about half of what it was in the earlier window, and the variability around that time also decreased. The two time indicators are consistent with each other and far exceed the number of time items expected by chance (2 observed vs. 0.008 expected), so this is not a chance fluctuation. The evidence is direct for a timing change; it does not indicate any structural or resource-related change. A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that an unobserved structural change (e.g., a reordering of activities) caused the shorter intervals, but no control-flow evidence supports that.
