### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a set of alternative intermediate activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) was introduced into the process between A29 and A31, so that the previously direct path A29→A30→A31 now often includes one of these new activities either before or after A30. The new activities appear from zero participation, are positioned in the middle of the trace, and the lags between A29, A30, and A31 increase by one step, directly showing the insertion. The new activities are alternatives, as they rarely co-occur in the same trace. The number of qualifying evidence items (35) far exceeds the expected chance count (0.6), so the difference is not a random artifact. A limitation is that the exact routing shares are approximate, and the choice among the new activities is not explained.
