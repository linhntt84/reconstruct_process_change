### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the process: the earlier path centred on A03 (with A05 and optional A04/A06) was replaced by a path centred on A01 and A02, with A05 now following them and A04/A06 still optional. This is directly shown by the complete disappearance of A03, the appearance of A01 and A02 in every trace, and the consistent ordering of A02 before A01 before A05. The number of evidence items (29) far exceeds the 0.17 expected by chance, and all items are control-flow related. A competing explanation of random sampling is ruled out by the scale of the difference. A limitation is that the exact placement of A04/A06 relative to A01/A02 is not fully fixed (interleaved in about 22% of traces), but this does not affect the main conclusion.

Other differences: The repetition of A03 (mean 2.01 per trace) disappeared; A01 and A02 each appear exactly once per trace. The order between A04/A06 and the new activities is mixed (sometimes before, sometimes interleaved). [OCC003, OCC001, OCC002, REL003, REL005]
