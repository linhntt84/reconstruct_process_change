### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change: the process likely changed from a choice between two alternative branches — one containing A28–A33 and the other containing A34–A38 — to a sequential execution of both branches, with A28–A33 before A34–A38, A25 at the start, and A26 at the end. This is directly shown by the large increase in participation and co-occurrence of both activity sets, the shift in direct successors, and the corresponding position changes. The timing decrease between A14/A15 and A20 is a separate observed difference; it is not linked to the structural change by the available evidence. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two windows come from different process variants rather than a single process that changed over time.

Other differences: the elapsed time between A14 and A20 and between A15 and A20 roughly halved in the later window. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
