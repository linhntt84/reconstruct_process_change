### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activity A20's outgoing transitions were spread across A36 (23%), A37 (18.5%), A30 (19%), A38 (20.5%) and A31 (19%). In the later window, A20 leads to A22 in about 99.5% of cases, with only 0.5% to A31. [DIR001]
- Activity A21's outgoing transition was entirely to A25 in the earlier window. In the later window, A21 goes to A30, A37, A36, A38 and A31 (each roughly 16–23%), and almost never to A25 (0.5%). [DIR002]
- The order between A21/A22 and the group A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 reversed: in the earlier window A21 and A22 followed the group in 100% of traces; in the later window they precede the group in about 99.5% of traces. [REL001–REL010]
- Positions shifted accordingly: A21's mean relative position fell from 0.65 to 0.52, A22's from 0.52 to 0.39, while A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 all rose from about 0.44 to about 0.60. [POS001–POS007]
- Timing between consecutive completions decreased sharply: A09→A10 from about 1.01 hours to 0.50 hours, A11→A10 from 1.02 to 0.50 hours, and A24→A28 from 1.00 hours to essentially zero. [TWAIT001–TWAIT003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in control-flow order: activities A21 and A22 now occur before the group A30, A31, A36, A37, A38, whereas before they occurred after them, and A20's outgoing edge shifted from that group to A22. This is directly shown by the successor distributions (DIR), the pairwise order relations (REL), and the position shifts (POS), all with full support in both windows. The large number of control-flow items (34 vs. 0.55 expected by chance) rules out a chance sampling effect.

Other differences: The elapsed times between consecutive completions for A09→A10, A11→A10, and A24→A28 decreased substantially, indicating a separate change in timing. The LAG items are based on only one trace in the later window and are therefore not reliable for confirming the order change, but the REL and POS items already provide strong evidence. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the timing change is independent of the structural reordering, but the structural change itself is well supported.
