### Observed differences

- In the later window, three activities A19, A20 and A25 appear that were absent before: each occurs in about 37%, 29% and 34% of traces respectively, always once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the later window, A41's outgoing transitions shift from almost always to A26 (93% before) to A19 (34%), A25 (31%) and A20 (27%), with A26 dropping to about 1%. [DIR004]
- The new activities are mutually exclusive: A19, A20 and A25 never co-occur with each other; each appears alone in its traces. [REL001, REL002, REL007]
- In the later window, A19, A20 and A25 are followed by A26 and A31 in about 37%, 29% and 34% of traces respectively, whereas before A26 and A31 were always present without these predecessors. [REL003, REL004, REL008, REL009, REL011, REL012]
- A41 now precedes A19, A20 and A25 in the later window (37%, 28% and 33% of traces), whereas before A41 was always present and these activities were absent. [REL006, REL010, REL014]
- The gap between A41 and A26 increases: before A41 was immediately followed by A26 in 94% of traces, after only 1% are immediate and 90% have one activity in between. [LAG002]
- The gap between A31 and A35 increases: before 55% had one step between, after 62% have two steps. [LAG001]
- The position of A26 and A31 shifts slightly later in the trace (mean from 0.374 to 0.393 for A26, from 0.693 to 0.708 for A31). [POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new step, consisting of one of three alternative activities (A19, A20, A25), is inserted between A41 and the subsequent activities A26/A31 in the later window. Before, A41 led directly to A26; after, A41 leads to one of the three new activities, which then lead to A26 and A31. This is directly shown by the change in A41's outgoing transitions, the appearance of the new activities, their ordering relative to A41 and A26/A31, and the increased lag between A41 and A26. The three new activities are mutually exclusive, indicating a choice among alternatives. The slight later positions of A26 and A31 are consistent with an extra step before them.

Other differences: The gap between A31 and A35 also increases, consistent with the same inserted step before A31. No time or resource changes are reported.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities were always possible but unobserved in the before window; however, their appearance in a substantial share of traces and the systematic shift in A41's routing make this unlikely. The evidence count (28 items vs 0.57 expected by chance) strongly supports a real change.
