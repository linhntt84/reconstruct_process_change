### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A26 and A27 almost always occurred before A35, A36, and A41 (about 98–99% of traces). In the later window, the order reversed: A35, A36, and A41 now occur before A26 and A27 in about 97–98% of traces. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

- A26 and A30 changed from being far apart (lag 4–5 in 92.5%) to adjacent (lag 1 in 98.5%). [LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reversal in the ordering of two groups of activities: A26 and A27 moved from before A35, A36, and A41 to after them, while A30 moved from after A35/A36/A41 to after A26. This is directly shown by the relational order items (REL001–006), the positional shifts (POS001–005), and the successor changes (DIR001–005), all with full support in both windows. The main limitation is that some lag items (e.g., LAG002, LAG004–010) have very low support in one window, but the core reversal is well supported by the high-support items.

Other differences: A25’s successor changed from A27 to A36/A41/A35, and the A25–A27 distance increased, which is consistent with the same reordering but does not involve A25 in the main reversal. The evidence cannot rule out that additional activities not covered by the items also shifted, but no competing explanation for the observed order reversal is apparent.
