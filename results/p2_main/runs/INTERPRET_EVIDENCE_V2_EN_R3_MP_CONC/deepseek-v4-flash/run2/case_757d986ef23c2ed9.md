### Observed differences

- A20's outgoing transition changed: before it always went to A22; after it goes to A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 (each about 20%) and almost never to A22 (0.5%). [DIR001]
- A21's outgoing transition changed: before it went to A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 (each 15–23%); after it goes almost always to A25 (99.5%). [DIR002]
- A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 each used to have A25 as a successor (18–25% of outgoing transitions); after, A25 is almost absent (0.5% or less) and A22 appears as a successor (17–22%). [DIR003–DIR007]

- Before, A21 and A22 preceded each of A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 in all traces; after, these activities precede A21 and A22 in about 99.5% of traces. [REL001–REL010]
- The distance between A21 and the group, and between the group and A25, increased: in the after window only 1 trace has these pairs within 5 steps, whereas before they were spread across 1–5 steps. [LAG001–LAG010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: the block of activities A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 moved from after A22 to before A22, and A25 moved from after that block to after A21, while A21 moved from before the block to after it. Concretely, before the change the flow appears to be A20 → A22 → block → A25, with A21 also feeding the block; after the change it is A20 → block → A22, and A21 → A25, with the block also preceding A21. This is directly shown by the successor changes (A20 no longer leads to A22, A21 now leads to A25, the block no longer leads to A25 but to A22), the position shifts (A21 and A22 later, the block earlier), and the order reversals in the REL items. The LAG items are consistent but rest on only one trace in the after window, so the exact spacing is uncertain; however, the reordering itself is well supported by the other indicators. A competing explanation that the differences are sampling noise is ruled out by the screening comparison (34 items vs 0.55 expected by chance).
