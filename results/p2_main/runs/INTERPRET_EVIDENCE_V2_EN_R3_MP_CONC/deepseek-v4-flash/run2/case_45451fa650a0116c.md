### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed from executing a branch containing A24, A25, and A26 (often together with A06) to a branch containing A21, A22, and A23, with A20 as a common predecessor. This is directly shown by the large shifts in participation, in A20's successor distribution, and in co-occurrence patterns between A20 and the two activity groups. The number of qualifying items (73) far exceeds the 0.9 expected by chance, and the pattern is consistent across occurrence, routing, and relation indicators.

Other differences: A06 decreased in participation and moved earlier in the trace, and A34's successor changed from A06 to A35 more directly. These may be related to the same shift but are not part of the main hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed change reflects a shift in the frequency of pre-existing process variants rather than a structural replacement, but the magnitude and consistency of the evidence make a structural change the more plausible interpretation.
