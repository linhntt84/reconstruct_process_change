### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A08 was removed from the process in the later window. A08 is absent from all traces, its outgoing edges disappear, and A07, which previously always led to A08, now leads to A02–A05 instead. The co-occurrence and lag changes are consistent with this removal. The number of qualifying evidence items (13) far exceeds the 0.21 expected by chance, making a chance explanation unlikely. A competing explanation that A08 is merely rare in the later sample is ruled out by its complete absence and the consistent redirection of A07.

Other differences: The lag distributions for A07→A03/A04/A05, A04–A04 and A07–A07 also changed, but these are secondary to the removal of A08.
