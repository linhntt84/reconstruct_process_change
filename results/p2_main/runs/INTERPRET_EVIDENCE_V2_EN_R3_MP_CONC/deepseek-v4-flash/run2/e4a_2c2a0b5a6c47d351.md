### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process likely changed from executing exactly one of A14/A15/A16 between A39 and A24 to executing all three of A21/A22/A23 (in any order) between A39 and A24. This is supported by the complete disappearance of A14/A15/A16, the appearance of A21/A22/A23 in every trace, the shift in A39's outgoing edges from the former set to the latter, and the increase in the A39-to-A24 lag from 2 to 4 steps. The order among A21/A22/A23 is mixed (about half A-before-B, half B-before-A), so no fixed ordering is introduced. The timing between A24–A31 and A32–A31 also shortened, which is a separate change.

Other differences: The inter-event times between A24 and A31, and between A32 and A31, both decreased substantially.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the activities were renamed (e.g., A14/A15/A16 replaced by A21/A22/A23) rather than genuinely added or removed, but the evidence of all three new activities co-occurring in every trace, while the old ones were mutually exclusive, makes a simple renaming unlikely.
