### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A27, A36, A37, A42, A43, and A44 occur in every trace (100%) and on average once per trace, whereas in the earlier window they occurred in about half the traces (49–53%) and on average about 0.5 times per trace. [OCC001–OCC006]
- A25's outgoing transitions changed from almost always to A28 (98%) to a distribution over A44, A42, A43, A37, and A36 (each 14–24%). [DIR001]
- A26's outgoing transitions changed from a mix (47% to A27, plus A36–A44) to almost always A27 (98%). [DIR002]
- The successors of A36, A37, A42, A43, and A44 no longer include A31; instead they include A28 and each other. [DIR004–DIR008]
- Positions: A36, A37, A42, A43, and A44 moved from a mean position around 0.58 to around 0.40 (earlier in the trace); A26, A27, and A28 moved later (A26 from 0.53 to 0.61, A27 from 0.59 to 0.63, A28 from 0.39 to 0.48). [POS002–POS004, POS006–POS010]
- Relational: A25 now always precedes A36–A44 (before it was alone in about half the traces). [REL001–REL006]
- A36–A44 now always precede A26, A27, A28, and A31 (before they were alternatives or A26/A28 preceded them). [REL008–REL024, REL025–REL029]
- A28 now always precedes A27, and A27 always precedes A31 (before A28 preceded A27 in 48% and A27 preceded A31 in 48.5% of traces, with the other order rare). [REL013, REL014]
- The order among A36–A44 remains mixed (no fixed order) in both windows. [REL030–REL039]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the process: activities A36, A37, A42, A43, and A44 became mandatory (present in every trace) and moved earlier in the trace, while the routing changed so that A25 now leads to this group instead of to A28, and the group precedes A26, A28, A27, and A31 in a fixed order. The order among the group itself remains unordered. This is supported by the OCC items (participation doubled), DIR items (successor changes), REL items (order reversals and co-occurrence changes), and POS items (positional shifts), all pointing to the same reorganisation. The screening count (66 items vs 0.89 expected by chance) is far above what an unchanged process would produce.

Other differences: A26's successor changed from a mix to A27, and A31's position shifted slightly later. These are consistent with the main reordering but not part of the core hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a change in case mix (e.g., different types of cases being processed) rather than a change in the process itself, since the evidence is purely control-flow and does not include case attributes.
