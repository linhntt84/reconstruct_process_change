### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a choice between two alternative branches after A21 to an almost exclusive single path. In the earlier window, about half the cases followed A21 → A22 → A23, and the other half followed A21 → A27/A28/A33 → A23. In the later window, the A22 branch is taken in about 99.5% of cases, and the A27/A28/A33 branch is nearly eliminated. This is supported by the large increase in A22 participation, the near disappearance of A27/A28/A33, the redistribution of A21's outgoing transitions, and the relational and lag evidence showing the alternative branch is gone. The evidence count (34 items vs 0.45 expected by chance) makes random fluctuation unlikely. A competing explanation that the alternative branch still exists but was not sampled is unlikely given the near-zero participation, but cannot be fully excluded because the after-window contains only one trace with those activities.
