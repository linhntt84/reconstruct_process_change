### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A14, A15 and A20 each occurred in all 200 traces; in the later window each occurred in about 53 in 100 traces (106 of 200). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A36's outgoing behaviour changed: before, every observed transition from A36 went to A14; in the later window about 53 in 100 went to A14 and about 47 in 100 went to A21. [DIR001]
- The gap between A36 and A21 changed: before, A21 always followed A36 after 4 intervening positions; in the later window about 47 in 100 cases A21 followed A36 after 1 position, and about 53 in 100 still after 4 positions. [LAG001]
- The co-occurrence patterns show that in the later window, in about 47 in 100 traces A14, A15 and A20 are absent together, while A21 and A36 remain present; when A14/A15/A20 are present, their order relative to each other and to A21/A36 is unchanged. [REL001-REL009]
- Elapsed time between A23 and A24 decreased from about 1.0 hour to about 0.5 hour. [TWAIT001]
- Elapsed time between A36 and A14 increased from about 1.0 hour to about 2.0 hours, but this transition was observed in only 106 traces in the later window (vs 200 before). [TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that a segment of activities A14, A15 and A20 became optional/skipped in about 47% of cases, with A36 then leading directly to A21 instead of through that segment. In the earlier window all traces contained A14, A15 and A20 in a fixed order after A36 and before A21; in the later window about 53% of traces still follow that path, while about 47% skip A14/A15/A20 and go directly from A36 to A21. This is directly shown by the drop in occurrence of A14/A15/A20, the new A36→A21 successor, the lag change from 4 to 1, and the co-occurrence patterns. The number of qualifying control-flow items (16) far exceeds the 0.35 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. A limitation is that the two windows are finite samples, and the indicators cannot distinguish a separate variant from a conditional branch within one process. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, LAG001, REL001-REL009, screening]

Other differences: The elapsed time between A23 and A24 halved from about 1.0 to 0.5 hours, and the elapsed time between A36 and A14 increased from about 1.0 to 2.0 hours when that transition occurs (in 53% of traces). These timing changes are not part of the structural hypothesis. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
