### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity A01 was added to the process, appearing in about 60% of later traces, and that its presence is associated with a shift of the existing activities A03, A04, A07 and A10 to later positions and an increase in the distance between them. The main signs are the appearance of A01, new outgoing transitions from A03 and A10 to A01, later positions of A04/A07/A10, and increased lags. The order between A01 and A03/A10 is not fixed (both orders occur), so A01 is not strictly a new first step; it may be inserted at a variable point early in the process. Also, A01 does not always co-occur with A04 and A07, so it is not a mandatory predecessor for all activities.

Other differences: None beyond the main hypothesis.
