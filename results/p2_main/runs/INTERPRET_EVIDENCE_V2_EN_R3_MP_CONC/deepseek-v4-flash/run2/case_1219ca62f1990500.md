### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a group of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) that were executed in a substantial share of traces before are now almost never executed, and that A43 now leads directly to A28 instead of to those activities. This is supported by the consistent decrease in occurrence of these activities, the change in A43's outgoing transitions, the disappearance of co-occurrence and ordering relations involving them, and the shift in the A43–A28 lag from 2 to 1. The later window contains very few observations of the disappearing activities (0–3 traces), so the exact routing after them cannot be characterized, but the absence itself is consistent across many indicators.

Other differences: A02's successors changed and its participation halved; A15 and A16 shifted slightly later in position. These are not part of the main hypothesis. The evidence cannot rule out that these activities became optional and were simply not taken in this sample, but the consistency across many indicators supports a real change.
