### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A01 replaced activity A03 at the same point in the process. In the earlier window, A03 was executed in about 45% of traces, positioned around the middle of the trace, followed mainly by A11, A10, A09 and A12, and co-occurred with A05, A09, A10, A11 and A12. In the later window, A03 is absent and A01 appears with the same position, similar outgoing transitions (mainly to A11, A12, A09, A10) and co-occurs with the same set of activities. A01 and A03 never appear together, and the successor relations of A09 and A11 switch from A03 to A01. The number of qualifying evidence items (19) is far above the 0.33 expected by chance, and all items point in the same direction.

Other differences: None beyond the replacement.

Limitation: The evidence cannot distinguish between a renaming of the activity and a removal of A03 with a new activity A01 inserted at the same place with the same connections; both are consistent with the data.
