### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38, and A39 each occurred in essentially every trace (about 100% participation, mean ~1.0 per trace). In the later window, each occurs in only about 46–57% of traces (A33, A35, A36 ~46–47%; A37, A38, A39 ~56–57%). [OCC001-OCC006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a block of activities (A33, A35, A36, A37, A38, A39) that previously was executed in every trace became optional or partially skipped in the later window. In the earlier window, these six activities always occurred, with A30 leading to A33/A35/A36 and then to A37/A38/A39, and A31 following them. In the later window, each of these activities occurs in only about half of the traces; when they do occur, they are placed much closer to A30 and A31 (lags of 1–3 instead of 4–5), and A30's outgoing transitions are now spread across all six instead of only the first three. This is consistent with a process that now has two variants: one that includes the block and one that skips it, with the included variant being more compressed. The large number of qualifying items (54 vs. 0.8 expected by chance) rules out random fluctuation as an explanation.

Other differences: A30's position shifted later and A31's position shifted slightly earlier, which is consistent with the compression of the middle block. The small edges from A30 to other activities (e.g., A04, A08, A10, A20, A45, A46) appear only in the later window, but each is tiny (about 0.5% of transitions) and may be noise.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the process now has two distinct case types (one with the block, one without) rather than a single changed process; however, the evidence does not distinguish between these, and both are consistent with the observed optionality of the block. Also, some LAG items with very low support in the later window (e.g., LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011) are based on only 4–5 traces and are not relied upon for the main conclusion.
