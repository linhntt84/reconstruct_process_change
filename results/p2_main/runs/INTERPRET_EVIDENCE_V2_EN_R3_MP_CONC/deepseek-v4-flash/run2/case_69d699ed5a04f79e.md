### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activity A05 occurred in about 46.5% of traces (mean 0.86 occurrences per trace); in the later window it was absent. Activity A01 was absent earlier and occurred in 100% of traces later, always at the last position. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS003]
- A05's outgoing transitions earlier went almost entirely to A04 (99.4%); later A05 had no observed outgoing transitions. A07 earlier had no observed outgoing transitions; later A07 always led to A01. [DIR002, DIR003]
- A04's outgoing transitions earlier went to A05 (46.2%) and A06 (52.4%); later they went to A04 (49.0%) and A06 (50.3%), with A05 no longer a successor. [DIR001]
- A07 moved from the last position (mean 1.0) to an earlier position (mean 0.826), while A01 appeared at the last position. A04 also moved slightly earlier (mean position from 0.496 to 0.422) and became less variable. [POS002, POS004]

- A04-to-A04 lag shifted: earlier there was no A04 self-loop at lag 1; later about 20.5% of A04-A04 pairs were one step apart. A04-to-A07 lag shifted from mostly 2 steps (50.9%) to more 3 and 5 steps and fewer 2 and 4 steps. [LAG001, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which activity A05, present in about 46.5% of earlier traces, is no longer observed, and activity A01, absent earlier, appears in every later trace as a new final activity. This is directly shown by occurrence counts, positions, and relations: A05 disappears from all relations and outgoing transitions, while A01 co-occurs with A04 and A07 and is always preceded by A07, and by A04 in about half of traces. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]

Other differences: A07 moved from the last position to an earlier position (mean 1.0 to 0.826), and A04 gained a self-loop (about 49% of its outgoing transitions later go to A04, with A04-to-A04 lag 1 appearing in about 20.5% of A04-A04 pairs). These are separate from the A05/A01 change. [DIR001, POS004, LAG001, LAG002]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A05 was removed and A01 was added independently rather than A01 replacing A05; the two activities never co-occur, so the data cannot distinguish substitution from independent addition and removal. [OCC001, OCC002, REL002, REL004, REL005]
