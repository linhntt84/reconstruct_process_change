### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation for activities A01, A03, A17, and A22: work that was previously spread across many resources became more concentrated on a few specific resources (notably User_40, User_3, User_49, User_4, User_51), with a corresponding reduction in the long tail of other resources. This is supported by four independent ASSIGN indicators, all showing the same direction, and the number of resource items (4) far exceeds the expected number by chance (0.005). The main limitation is that we cannot tell whether this reflects a deliberate reassignment or a change in the pool of available resources, but the change itself is clearly indicated.

Other differences: The elapsed time between activities A16 and A24 decreased substantially (mean from about 76 to 44 hours, median from about 93 to 45 hours), based on TWAIT001. This is a separate timing change, not part of the resource allocation hypothesis.
