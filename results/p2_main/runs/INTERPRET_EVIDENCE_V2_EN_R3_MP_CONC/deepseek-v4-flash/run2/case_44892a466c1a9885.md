### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a previously mandatory block of activities (A19, A20, A28, A43, A48, A49) is now executed in only about half of the cases, and that the routing among these activities has changed. Specifically, A43 now often leads to A28 instead of A19, A48 now leads to A49 more directly, and A06 no longer leads to A49. The large number of qualifying items (59 vs. 0.85 expected by chance) and their consistency across occurrence, relation, and successor indicators make a chance explanation unlikely. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window contains a different mix of case types, some of which skip this entire block, rather than a change in the process logic itself. Other differences include the reduced participation of A06, A25, A26, and A27, and the earlier position of A06, which are consistent with the main hypothesis but not part of it.
