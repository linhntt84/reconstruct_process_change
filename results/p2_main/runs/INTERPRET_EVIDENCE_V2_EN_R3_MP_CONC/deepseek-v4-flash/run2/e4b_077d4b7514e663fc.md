### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: several activities (A01, A03, A08, A13, A14, A17, A21) are now performed by a different set of resources, and activity A08 became highly concentrated on a single resource (User_121). This is supported by 8 resource-related items (7 assignment and 1 concentration) against an expected 0.005 by chance, and the change is consistent across these activities. A separate timing difference is also present: the wait between A04 and A01 increased substantially, supported by a strong item (200 traces in each window). The main limitation is that resource identifiers are anonymous, so we cannot infer roles or reasons for the shift; the timing change is based on two items, one of which (TWAIT002) has limited support.

Other differences: The increased wait between A04 and A01 is a distinct timing change, not part of the resource-allocation hypothesis. The small change in the A20–A10 wait is too weakly supported to interpret.
