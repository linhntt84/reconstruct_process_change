### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed so that A19 and A20 are now executed in almost every case, whereas before they were executed in about half, and A25, A26, and A27 are executed more often (roughly doubling). This is accompanied by A43 now routing to A19 instead of directly to A28, so the path from A43 to A28 is lengthened by the insertion of A19 and A20 (and possibly A25/A26/A27). The evidence is direct and consistent across participation, routing, co-occurrence, and lag indicators. A competing explanation that this is a rare variant is unlikely given the large number of qualifying items (28 vs. 0.7 expected by chance).

Other differences: A27's outgoing distribution shifted from a few alternatives to almost always A20, but this is minor and consistent with the main change. [DIR001]
