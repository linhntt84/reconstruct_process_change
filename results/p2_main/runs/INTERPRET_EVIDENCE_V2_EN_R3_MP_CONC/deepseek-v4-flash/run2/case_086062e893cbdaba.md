### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity A01 was introduced into the process in the later window, appearing in about 46% of traces and inserted between existing activities. This is directly shown by its absence before and presence after, its incoming and outgoing edges from/to existing activities, and the increased lags between activities that are now separated by A01. The large number of evidence items (18 vs 0.17 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely. A limitation is that A01 appears in only about half of the traces, so it may be optional or conditional, and its exact position relative to some activities is not fixed (e.g., it appears both before and after A05 in different traces).

Other differences: The outgoing edge distributions of A03, A05, A06, and A11 shifted to include A01, and A10's position shifted slightly later, but these are consistent with the insertion of A01. A competing explanation that A01 is a rare variant that was not sampled before cannot be fully ruled out, but the systematic pattern across many indicators makes it unlikely.
