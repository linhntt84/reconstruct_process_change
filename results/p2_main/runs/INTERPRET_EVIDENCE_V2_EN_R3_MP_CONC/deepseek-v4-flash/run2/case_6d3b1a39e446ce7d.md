### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control-flow structure: the process shifted from having two alternative branches — one with A25 → A28 → A27 and another with A26 → A36/A37/A42 — to a single, more linear path in which A36, A37, and A42 occur early, followed by A26, then A28, then A27. This is directly shown by the near-universal participation of A27, A36, A37, and A42 in the later window, the reversal of order between A26 and A36/A37/A42, the change in A25's routing from A28 to A36/A37/A42, and the disappearance of A31 as a successor of A36/A37/A42. The large number of qualifying items (48 vs. 0.74 expected by chance) makes it unlikely that these differences are sampling noise.

Other differences: A25's position moved slightly earlier, and A26's routing became almost exclusively to A27. These are consistent with the main hypothesis but not central to it.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed change reflects a shift in the mix of existing process variants rather than a structural modification, although the consistency and breadth of the evidence make this less plausible.
