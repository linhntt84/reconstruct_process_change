### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process changed by removing (or bypassing) the intermediate activities A14, A15, and A20 between A36 and A21. In the earlier window, A36 led to one of these three activities, which then led to A21; in the later window, A36 leads directly to A21, and the three activities no longer appear. This is supported by the disappearance of these activities, the change in A36's successors, and the reduced lag between A36 and A21. The evidence cannot rule out that these activities still exist but are never executed in this sample, though with 200 traces and a complete shift in A36's routing, that is unlikely.

Other differences: A19's routing changed, with A26 becoming more frequent and A16/A17 less frequent. This is a separate shift in path frequencies and does not involve the removed activities.
