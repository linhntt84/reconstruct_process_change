### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering between A30 and A31 after A26. In the earlier window, A26 more often led to A31 and then to A30 (with A30 then going to A27); in the later window, A26 more often leads to A30, then to A31, and then to A27. This is directly shown by the reversed successor shares of A30 and A31 and by the corresponding lag shifts for the pairs A26–A30, A26–A31, A30–A27, and A31–A27. The number of qualifying items (7) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.33), and the items are mutually consistent. A competing explanation, such as a change in case mix, cannot be fully excluded, but the direct positional evidence makes it unlikely.

Other differences: A26 now also more often goes to A32 (from about 22% to 30%) and less to A31; this is a separate shift in routing that does not involve the A30/A31 ordering.
