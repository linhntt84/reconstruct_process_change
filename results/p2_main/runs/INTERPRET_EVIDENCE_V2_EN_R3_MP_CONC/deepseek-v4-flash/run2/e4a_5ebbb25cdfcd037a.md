### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained A14 and A15, and A38 always led to A14, which then led to A15 and optionally to A20/A21 or A22, then to A23. In the later window, A14 and A15 appear in only about 52% of traces, and A20/A21/A22 in about 24–29% (down from about 50%). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- A38's outgoing transitions: before, all went to A14; after, about 47% go to A23 and 53% to A14. [DIR001]
- A23 now appears one step after A38 in about 47% of traces (previously it was 4–5 steps after). [LAG001]
- A23 now appears without A14, A15, A20, A21, or A22 in about 47% of traces, whereas before it always co-occurred with A14 and A15. [REL005, REL010, REL014, REL017, REL019]
- Timing: wait between A28 and A34 dropped from about 0.91h to 0.45h; between A35 and A34 from 0.20h to 0.08h; between A38 and A14 increased from about 1.99h to 3.03h, with the number of such transitions halved. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new direct path from A38 to A23 was introduced, bypassing A14, A15, and the A20/A21/A22 branch. In the later window, about half the traces take this direct path, while the other half still follow the original longer path. This is supported by the direct successor change (A38 now goes to A23 in about 47% of cases), the lag change (A23 now appears one step after A38 in about 47% of cases), the halved participation of the intermediate activities, and the appearance of A23 without those activities. The increased A38–A14 wait time and its halved count are consistent with only the longer path still using A14. Limitations: the timing changes for A28–A34 and A35–A34 are separate and not explained by this structural change; they could be an independent timing change. Also, we cannot rule out that the direct path existed but was too rare to be observed before, though the before window had 200 traces with no such case.

Other differences: The wait times between A28–A34 and A35–A34 decreased substantially. Also, A23's position shifted slightly earlier and A38's slightly later, but these are minor and consistent with the new path.
