### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: a direct path from A36 to A21 was replaced by a path that inserts exactly one of three alternative activities (A14, A15 or A20) between A36 and A21. The new activities appear only in the later window, A36’s outgoing distribution shifts from A21 to these alternatives, each alternative always leads to A21, and the lag between A36 and A21 increases from 1 to 2. The three alternatives are mutually exclusive, indicating a choice among them. The evidence is direct and consistent across occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators, and the number of qualifying items (21) far exceeds the 0.37 expected by chance. A competing explanation that the new activities existed but were simply unobserved in the earlier window is unlikely given their substantial participation in the later window and the disappearance of the direct A36→A21 edge.
