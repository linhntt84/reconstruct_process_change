### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a set of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) that were previously executed in a substantial share of traces was removed or made optional, so that the main flow now goes directly from A29 to A30 to A31, bypassing these activities. This is supported by the large drops in participation of these activities, the shift in outgoing transitions of A29 and A30 from these activities to A30 and A31 respectively, the drop in co-occurrence of these activities with A29/A30/A31, and the lag changes showing A29->A30 and A30->A31 now occur with lag 1 almost always. [OCC004-OCC008, DIR009, DIR010, REL005-REL007, REL013-REL015, REL019-REL021, LAG007, LAG010] The evidence cannot rule out that these activities still exist but are extremely rare, but the consistency and size of the changes make that unlikely.

Other differences: A06 decreased in participation and its successors became more diverse, and A02 and A10 increased in participation. These are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001]
