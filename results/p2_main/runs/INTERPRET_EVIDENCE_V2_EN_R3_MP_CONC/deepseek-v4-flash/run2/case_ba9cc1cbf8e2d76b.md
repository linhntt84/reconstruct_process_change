### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new step is inserted between A27 and A28/A29, and between A28 and A29, in most traces. This step is one of three alternative activities — A19, A20 or A25 — each trace typically contains exactly one of them, placed around the middle of the trace. The insertion is directly shown by the appearance of these activities, the shift of outgoing transitions from A27 and A28 toward them, their consistent placement before A29, and the increased lags between A27, A28 and A29. The three activities are almost mutually exclusive, so they act as alternatives rather than a sequence. A competing explanation that the new activities are simply rare variants is ruled out by the large number of qualifying items (28 vs. 0.56 expected by chance) and by the coherent relational and positional pattern.

Other differences: A27 moved slightly earlier and A29 slightly later in the trace, consistent with the inserted step. No other independent differences are indicated.
