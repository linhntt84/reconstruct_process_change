### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a single, mostly ordered execution of activities A30, A31, A36, A37, and A38 to a repeated, interleaved execution of these activities, with A38 now often leading to A30 instead of A25. This is supported by the consistent increase in occurrence counts and recurrence, the shift from strict ordering to interleaving, the increased lags, and the change in A38's successor. The main limitation is that these indicators all reflect the same underlying repetition, so they are not independent confirmations, but the magnitude and consistency make a real change likely.

Other differences: A04 participation increased from about 4.5% to about 15% of traces, a separate minor change. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is a change in case mix or the introduction of a new process variant, but the large number of consistent indicators makes this less likely.
