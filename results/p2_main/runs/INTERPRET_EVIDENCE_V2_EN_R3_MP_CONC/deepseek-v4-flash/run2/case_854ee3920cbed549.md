### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 appear in about 16–23% of traces, while they were absent from the earlier window; A06 participation rises from about 8% to 28% of traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- A29 and A30, which before led almost always to A30 and A31 respectively, now split their outgoing flow: A29→A30 drops from about 99% to 47%, and A30→A31 drops from about 98% to 51%, with the remaining flow going to A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27. [DIR007, DIR008]
- The new activities lead to A30 and A31: A19 to A30 about 50% and A31 about 46%; A20 to A30 about 43% and A31 about 52%; A25 to A30 about 50% and A31 about 48%; A26 to A30 about 61% and A31 about 36%; A27 to A30 about 49% and A31 about 51%. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- The lag between A29 and A30 changes from 99% one step to 47% one step and 51% two steps; A29–A31 changes from 97% two steps to 95% three steps; A30–A31 changes from 97% one step to 51% one step and 47% two steps. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- A06 now often follows A46 and precedes A47: A46→A06 rises from about 1% to 32% of A46 outgoing transitions, and A06→A47 rises from about 6% to 85% of A06 outgoing transitions; A06’s mean position shifts from about 0.48 to 0.81. [DIR001, DIR009, POS001]
- A06 co-occurs with A29, A30 and A31 in about 28% of traces in the later window, up from about 8%, and the order shifts so that A29/A30/A31 precede A06 in about 23% of traces, up from about 3–4%. [REL005, REL006, REL007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a set of activities A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 was inserted between A29/A30 and A31, so that the previously dominant path A29→A30→A31 is now often interrupted by one of these new activities before reaching A31. This is directly shown by the routing changes at A29 and A30, the outgoing edges of the new activities to A30 and A31, and the one-step increase in lags between A29–A30, A29–A31 and A30–A31. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003] The main competing explanation, that these activities existed but were simply not sampled in the earlier window, is weakened by the consistent routing and lag changes, but the exact branching or loop structure among the new activities is not fully determined.

Other differences: A06 increased in frequency, moved later in the trace, and appears to be inserted between A46 and A47 in a substantial share of cases, while also increasingly occurring after A29/A30/A31. This is separate from the main insertion. [DIR001, DIR009, POS001, REL005, REL006, REL007]
