### Observed differences

- A06 participation rose from about 24% to 73% of traces, and its mean occurrences per trace from 0.25 to 0.76; A08 fell from 20% to 8%. [OCC001, OCC002]
- A19, A20 and A25 became near-universal: participation rose from about 48% to about 99.5% for each. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- A06's outgoing transitions became concentrated on A16 (about 88% of its successors, up from a spread over many activities). A15's successors shifted from mostly A16 (94%) to A16 (58%) and A06 (40%). A41's successors shifted from A26 (45%) and A19 (37%) to A19 (96%). [DIR001, DIR003, DIR006]
- A06 moved earlier in the trace (mean relative position from 0.50 to 0.25). [POS001]
- Relational evidence shows A06 now co-occurs with A14, A15, A16, A19, A20, A25, A26 and A41 in about 73% of traces (up from 24%), with A14/A15 before A06 and A06 before A16/A19/A20/A25/A26/A41. A14, A15 and A16 now almost always precede A19/A20/A25 (cooccurrence ~99.5%, up from ~48%). [REL001–REL008, REL009–REL017]
- Lag evidence places A06 immediately before A16 (lag 1 in 97% of cases, up from 18%) and A15 immediately before A06 (lag 1 in 45%, up from 31% at lag 4). [LAG002, LAG009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports that A06 became a regular step in the process, inserted between A15/A14 and A16, and that the downstream activities A19, A20 and A25 became near-universal. In the earlier window A06 was present in about 24% of traces and its successors were varied; in the later window it appears in about 73% of traces, sits earlier in the trace, and almost always leads to A16, while A15 now often leads to A06 instead of directly to A16. Relational and lag evidence consistently show the order A14/A15 → A06 → A16 → A19/A20/A25 → A26, and A41 now leads to A19 rather than A26. The large number of qualifying items (62 vs. 0.84 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A06 was an optional activity that became much more frequently used rather than a newly mandatory step, since it is still absent in about 27% of later traces.

Other differences: A08 decreased in participation; A41's successor changed from A26 to A19; A26's position shifted slightly later in the trace.
