### Observed differences

- A01 occurred in every trace before (mean 1.0 per trace) and disappeared completely after (0 traces). [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001-003]
- A04 occurred in about 51% of traces before and disappeared completely after. [OCC002, DIR003, POS004, REL003-005]
- A02 remained present (about 51% before, 46.5% after) but its outgoing transitions changed: before it went to A06 in about 79% of its occurrences, to A04 in 14% and to A01 in 7%; after it always goes to A06. [DIR002, POS002, REL001, REL004]
- A03 remained present (about 49% before, 53.5% after) and moved to the start of traces (mean position 0.0 after vs 0.44 before). [POS003, REL002, REL005]
- A06 was a non-terminal activity in a few traces before (14 traces had outgoing edges, to A04 71% and A01 29%) and had no outgoing edges after, so it became a terminal activity. [DIR004]
- The co-occurrence structure changed: before, A01 always occurred, and A02/A04 co-occurred in about 51% of traces while A03 occurred in the other 49%; after, A01 and A04 are absent, and A02 and A03 are mutually exclusive alternatives, each occurring in about half the traces. [REL001-005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in process structure: the process before had a mandatory A01 followed by two alternative branches (one containing A02 and A04, the other containing A03), with A06 sometimes appearing as a non-terminal step. In the later window, A01 and A04 are no longer observed, A02 and A03 are alternative starting activities (each in about half the traces), A02 always leads to A06, and A06 is always terminal. This is directly shown by the disappearance of A01 and A04, the simplification of A02's successors, the shift of A02 and A03 to the first position, and the loss of A06's outgoing edges. The large number of qualifying items (15 vs 0.1 expected) rules out chance. A competing explanation that the later window simply sampled a different variant is unlikely given the complete absence of A01 and A04 in all 200 traces, but the evidence cannot rule out that A01 and A04 still exist in the process but were not executed in this window.
