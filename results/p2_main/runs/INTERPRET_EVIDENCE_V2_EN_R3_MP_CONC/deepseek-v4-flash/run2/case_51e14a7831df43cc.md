Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activities A09 and A16 effectively swapped their roles in the process. In the earlier window, A09 was a frequent, recurring activity that occurred in every trace and was the target of A04 and A10; in the later window, A16 takes that role, occurring in every trace, repeating, and receiving the same incoming edges, while A09 becomes optional, less frequent, and moves earlier in the trace. The number of qualifying evidence items (39) far exceeds the 0.39 expected by chance, so this is not a random fluctuation.
