### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the block of activities A26–A30 was replaced by A21–A25 in the main flow. A21–A25 went from about 19 in 100 traces to about 77 in 100, while A26–A30 went from about 82 in 100 to about 24 in 100. The same reversal appears in their relations with A14, A20 and A34: these activities now co-occur with A21–A25 instead of A26–A30. The number of qualifying control-flow items (103) is far above the 1.15 expected by chance, and the relational evidence is consistent across many activity pairs. [OCC001, OCC006, REL011, REL016, REL021, REL026, REL040, REL075]

Other differences: A20’s successor distribution shifted from A29 to A24, and A21–A25 are executed together without a fixed order. These are consistent with the main replacement but are not needed to support it. [DIR001, REL031, REL032]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two activity groups are alternative branches and the observed shift reflects a change in the mix of case types rather than a modification of the process structure.
