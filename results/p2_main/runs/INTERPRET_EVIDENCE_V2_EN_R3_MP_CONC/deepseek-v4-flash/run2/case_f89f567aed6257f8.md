### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the process: a new activity A01 was introduced and activity A14 was removed, with a corresponding rerouting of successors. In the earlier window A01 was absent and A14 was present in every trace; in the later window A01 appears in about 56% of traces (often repeated) and A14 is absent. Many activities that previously had outgoing transitions to A14 or to a set of alternatives now lead to A01 (e.g., A09, A13, A17, A08, A10). The number of qualifying evidence items (129) far exceeds the expected 0.98 under no change, so this is not a chance fluctuation. The main limitation is that the evidence does not reveal the semantic meaning of A01 or A14, so the exact nature of the replacement (e.g., whether A01 is a renamed or repurposed activity) cannot be determined; however, A01's repeated execution and partial participation distinguish it from a simple one-to-one replacement of A14.

Other differences: Activity A05's participation decreased and its successor changed from A16 to A13; activity A11's successor changed from A05 to A16 and its position shifted later; activity A03 now always occurs at the end with no outgoing transitions. These changes are consistent with the overall restructuring but are not part of the main A01/A14 hypothesis. [OCC002, DIR003, DIR008, POS005, POS002]
