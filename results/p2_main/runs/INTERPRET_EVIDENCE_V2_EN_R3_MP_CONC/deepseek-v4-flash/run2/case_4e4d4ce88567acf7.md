### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity A01 was introduced and is now executed in every case, typically after A08 and often as the final activity, while A08 now directly precedes A01 instead of preceding A05/A06. This is directly shown by the appearance of A01, the shift in A08's outgoing transitions, the co-occurrence and ordering relations, and the increased lag between A08 and A05/A06. The large number of evidence items (12 vs 0.1 expected by chance) rules out chance as an explanation. A competing explanation that A01 existed but was unobserved in the earlier window is unlikely given its complete absence in 200 traces and presence in all 200 later traces.

Other differences: The positions of A05, A06, and A08 shifted earlier, which is consistent with the addition of A01 at the end of the trace. No other independent differences are indicated.
