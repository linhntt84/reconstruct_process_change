### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a sequence where A19, A20, A25 (and A45) were early, followed by A26, then A27, then A28, then A32, to a different sequence where A26 and A27 come first, then A19/A20/A25, then A28 and A32, with some ordering flexibility between A28 and the middle group. This is supported by the consistent reversal of order in REL items, the shift in positions, and the changes in outgoing transitions and lags. The number of evidence items (27) far exceeds the expected 0.63 by chance. The main limitation is that some LAG items have very low support in one window (e.g., LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG006), but the overall pattern is consistent across many items.
- Other differences: The routing of A45 changed from feeding into A19/A20/A25 to feeding into A26, which is consistent with the reordering. Also, A28 now sometimes precedes A19/A25, indicating a less strict order.
- A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a shift in the relative frequencies of existing variants rather than a structural reordering, but the direct reversal of order in REL items and the large number of consistent indicators make this less likely.
