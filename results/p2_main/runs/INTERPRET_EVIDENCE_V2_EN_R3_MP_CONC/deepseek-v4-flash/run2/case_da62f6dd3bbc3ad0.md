### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change: the process moved from having two alternative continuations after A38 — one going directly to A23 and one going through A14/A15 — to always going through A14/A15, making A14 and A15 mandatory and removing the direct A38→A23 path. This is shown directly by the complete disappearance of the A38→A23 transition [DIR001], the loss of adjacency between A38 and A23 [LAG001], and the rise of A14 and A15 to full participation [OCC001, OCC002]. The order A38 → A14 → A15 → A23 becomes the standard backbone, with A20/A21/A22 as optional side activities between A14 and A15. A competing explanation of a simple shift in variant frequencies is ruled out because the A38→A23 edge is no longer observed at all, not merely less frequent.

Other differences: A20, A21, and A22 increased in participation but remain optional and mutually exclusive with each other [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL012, REL013]. A23 moved later in the trace, consistent with it now always following A15 [POS001].
