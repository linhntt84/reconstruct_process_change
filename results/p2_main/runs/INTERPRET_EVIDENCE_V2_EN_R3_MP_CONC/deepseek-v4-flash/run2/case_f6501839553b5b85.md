### Observed differences

- Activity A01 is absent in the earlier window and appears in the later window in about 30% of traces (59 of 200), with a mean of 0.655 occurrences per trace, and it is positioned around the middle of traces (median relative position 0.41). [OCC001, POS001]
- In the later window, A01's outgoing transitions go to A04 (about 53%) and A13 (about 47%). [DIR001]
- A12's outgoing transitions change from A04 (52%) and A13 (48%) in the earlier window to A01 (100%) in the later window. [DIR002]
- A01 co-occurs with A04 and A13 in about 30% of traces in the later window, always in an interleaved/repeated manner (no fixed order), whereas before A01 was absent and A04/A13 appeared alone in about 50% of traces. [REL001, REL003]
- A01 co-occurs with A12 in about 30% of traces, with A12 before A01 in about half of those and interleaved in the rest. [REL002]
- The distance (lag) between A12 and A04/A13 shifts from 1–2 steps to 2–3 steps, and repetitions of A04, A12, and A13 become further apart (lag 5 more common). [LAG002, LAG004, LAG001, LAG003, LAG005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new activity A01 was introduced in the later window, inserted between A12 and A04/A13. In the earlier window, A12 led directly to A04 or A13; in the later window, A12 leads to A01, which then leads to A04 or A13, and A01 appears in about 30% of traces, often interleaved with A04 and A13. This is supported by the appearance of A01, the change in A12's outgoing transitions, the outgoing transitions of A01, and the increased lag between A12 and A04/A13. The evidence is direct and consistent.

Other differences: The self-repetition lags of A04, A12, and A13 increased (e.g., A12–A12 lag shifted from 4 to 5, A04–A04 and A13–A13 shifted toward lag 5), which may be a consequence of the insertion or a separate change in repetition patterns. [LAG001, LAG003, LAG005]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 existed in the earlier window but was not observed in the 200-trace sample; however, its complete absence and the systematic change in A12's outgoing transitions make this unlikely.
