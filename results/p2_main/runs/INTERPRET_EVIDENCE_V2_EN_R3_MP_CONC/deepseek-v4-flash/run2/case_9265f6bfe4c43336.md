### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which a block of activities A16–A20 was replaced by a block A21–A25, with A15 (and A29) always present. The participation of the old block dropped from about 85% to 18%, the new block rose from about 16% to 82%, and A15's successor shifted from A19 to A24. This is directly shown by occurrence, co-occurrence and successor evidence. The large number of qualifying items (78 vs 0.84 expected) rules out chance as a whole. A competing explanation that the two blocks are simply alternative variants that swapped frequency is not supported because the change is a near-complete replacement, not a shift in shares of existing paths. The internal order within the new block is not fully fixed, but that does not contradict the main change.

Other differences: Within the new block, the routing from A22 changed: before, A21 was the most common successor (about 58%), after A23 and A25 became more common (about 36% and 33% respectively). [DIR002] This is a secondary change in the internal structure of the new block. The lag between A18 and A17 shifted toward shorter distances, but the after-window support is only 17 traces, so this is weak. [LAG001]
