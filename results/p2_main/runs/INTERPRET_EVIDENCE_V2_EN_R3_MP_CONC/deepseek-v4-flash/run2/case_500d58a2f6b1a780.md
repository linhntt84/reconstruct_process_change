### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a repeated, interleaved execution of activities A25, A26, and A31 to a single, fixed-order execution in which A25 occurs before A31 (typically with one activity in between) and A31 then leads to A20 instead of looping back to A25. The drop in mean occurrences and recurrence for all three activities, the shift from interleaving to a strict A25-before-A31 order, and the change in A31's successor all point to the removal of a loop that previously cycled through these activities. The evidence is consistent and direct, and the number of qualifying items (9) far exceeds the 0.3 expected by chance for control-flow indicators. A competing explanation that the activities were simply moved to a different part of the process is unlikely because their positions remain similar, and the ordering and successor changes directly show the loop removal. A limitation is that the evidence covers only control flow; no timing or resource data are available to assess whether the loop removal also affected durations or assignments.
