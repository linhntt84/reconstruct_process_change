### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the allocation of work for activities A13, A14, A17, and A22 changed, with work being redistributed from a set of previously dominant resources to a broader set of resources. The four assignment items all show the same direction of change, and the number of items (4) far exceeds the number expected by chance for the resource family (0.004). The support is based on substantial numbers of traces (122–282 per window).
