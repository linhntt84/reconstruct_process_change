### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A25's outgoing transitions were spread over A41 (23%), A42 (18%), A35 (18%), A43 (20%), and A36 (19%); in the later window, about 96% of A25's transitions go to A27. [DIR001]

- The mean position of A26 and A27 moved earlier (A26 from 0.65 to 0.52, A27 from 0.52 to 0.39), while A35, A36, A41, A42, and A43 moved later (from about 0.44 to about 0.60). [POS001–POS007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a specific structural change: the two early activities A25 and A26 swapped their downstream paths. In the earlier window, A25 led to a set of activities (A35, A36, A41, A42, A43) which then led to A27, while A26 led to A30. In the later window, A25 leads directly to A27, and A26 leads to that same set of activities, which now lead to A30. This is directly shown by the successor changes (DIR), the reversal of order between A26/A27 and the middle set (REL), the shift in positions (POS), and the changes in co-occurrence within five steps (LAG). The evidence is consistent across all these indicators and is far more numerous than expected by chance (43 items vs 0.8 expected). A competing explanation that the change is merely a reordering of activities without a swap of branches is unlikely because the successor distributions of A25 and A26 changed in opposite directions, and the middle set's successor changed from A27 to A30.

Other differences: None beyond the main hypothesis.
