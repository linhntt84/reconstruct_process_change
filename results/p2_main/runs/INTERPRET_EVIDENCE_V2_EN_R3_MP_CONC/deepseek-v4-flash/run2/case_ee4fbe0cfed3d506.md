### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The number of qualified control-flow items (112) far exceeds the 1.28 expected by chance, so the overall difference is not a sampling artefact. The evidence supports a hypothesis that a group of activities (A33–A38 and A39–A43) that were previously executed in every case are now executed in only about half of the cases, and the two subgroups have become alternatives: when one subgroup is executed, the other is typically not. This is directly supported by the participation drop (OCC) and the co-occurrence changes (REL). The appearance of A31 as a direct successor of A33, A34, and A38, and the diversification of A30's outgoing transitions, are additional structural changes that may be part of the same reconfiguration.

Other differences: A30's position shifted later, and A33–A38 shifted later while A39–A43 shifted slightly earlier. Also, A31 becomes a direct successor of several activities.

The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows contain different case types rather than a change in the process, but the magnitude and consistency across many activities support a structural change.
