### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from having A10 as a common activity, often executed without A19 or A20, to having A19 and A20 as mandatory activities, with A10 becoming a rare optional activity that only occurs together with them. This is directly shown by the large drop in A10 participation, the rise of A19 and A20 to 100% participation, and the relational changes indicating A10 no longer appears alone while A19 and A20 now co-occur with all core activities. The evidence is extensive (73 items vs 0.96 expected by chance) and consistent across occurrence, relation, and direction indicators.

Other differences: A25, A26, and A27 also increased in participation, and A10's position shifted later. These are separate from the main hypothesis.

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the observed differences reflect a change in the mix of case types rather than a modification of the process itself.
