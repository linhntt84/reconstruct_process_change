### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the control flow: after A15, the process now follows a path through activities A21–A25 instead of A16–A20. Specifically, A15's successor changed from A19 to A24, and the group of activities executed after that point switched from A16–A20 to A21–A25. This is directly shown by the large drop in participation of A16–A20 and rise of A21–A25, the change in A15's outgoing distribution, and the co-occurrence patterns between A15 and the two groups. The number of qualifying control-flow items (79) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.84), so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact.

Other differences: The elapsed times between A11–A13 and A12–A13 increased substantially in the later window, indicating a separate timing change. This is supported by two time items against a chance expectation of 0.006.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed shift reflects a change in case mix or a different variant of the process, but the consistency across participation, routing, and co-occurrence makes this unlikely.
