### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the group of activities A26, A27 and A28 was replaced by the group A19, A20 and A21 in the same position of the process: A44 now leads to A19/A20/A21 instead of A26/A27/A28, and A19/A20/A21 lead to A29 as A26/A27/A28 did before; A11/A12 now precede the new group instead of the old one. The change is shown directly by occurrence, successor, relation and position indicators, and the number of qualifying items (70) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.78). [OCC001–OCC006, DIR001–DIR007, REL001–REL039, POS001–POS008, LAG010]

Limitations: the new activities appear in only about a third of traces, whereas the old ones appeared in all, so the replacement is not one-to-one in participation; and the few later traces still containing A26/A27/A28 (1–2 traces) make their post-change behaviour unreliable. [OCC001–OCC006, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

Other differences: A29's position shifted slightly earlier (mean 0.43 to 0.40) and A44's slightly later (0.31 to 0.33), consistent with the reduced distance between A44 and A29. [POS007, POS008, LAG010]
