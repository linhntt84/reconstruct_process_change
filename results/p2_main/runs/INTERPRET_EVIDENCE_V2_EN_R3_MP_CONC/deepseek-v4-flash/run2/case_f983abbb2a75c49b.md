### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a single interleaved set of activities with mixed ordering to a strict sequential execution of two blocks: first a block containing A30, A19, A15, A16, A17 and A20, then a block containing A21, A22, A23, A24, A25 and A31. This is shown directly by the consistent reversal of order between the two groups (REL), the shift of the first group to earlier positions and the second group to later positions (POS), the increase in lag between the groups and the decrease in lag within each group (LAG), and the redirection of successors so that the first group no longer connects to the second group except through the fixed sequence (DIR). The number of evidence items (93) far exceeds the 0.72 expected by chance, and the items are mutually consistent.

Other differences: A20’s successor changes from A31 to A21, and A22 becomes the only predecessor of A31; A30’s successor changes from A21 and A19 to only A19. These are part of the same block reorganisation.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the before window contained a different variant mix that happened to show mixed ordering, but the magnitude and consistency of the changes make this unlikely.
