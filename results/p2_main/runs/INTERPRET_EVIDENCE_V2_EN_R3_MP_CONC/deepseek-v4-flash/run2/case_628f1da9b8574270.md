### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the process: the earlier fixed sequence, in which A20 was followed by a block of activities (A31, A32, A37, A38, A39), then A23, A21, A22 and A26, appears to have been replaced by a structure in which A20 leads directly to A23, and the block and A22 are now optional and occur later, after A21. This is supported by the large drop in participation of A22 and the block, the change of A20's successor from the block to A23, the reversal of order between A21 and the block, and the earlier positions of A23 and A21. The evidence cannot fully determine the exact new sequence, because the later‑window LAG items rest on a single trace; in particular, the precise placement of A22 relative to the optional block is uncertain. A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the process now has two distinct variants (one with the block and A22, one without) rather than a single new sequence, but the overall change in structure is clear.

Other differences: A21's outgoing now splits between A22 and the block, whereas before it always went to A22; and the block now leads to A26 instead of A23. These are consistent with the same structural reorganisation.
