### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a group of activities (A21, A22, A23) was replaced by another group (A24, A25, A26) as the main continuation after A20. In the earlier window, A20 was followed by A21–A23 in about 75% of traces; in the later window, A20 is followed by A24–A26 in about 75% of traces, while A21–A23 almost disappear. A10 also becomes more frequent and shifts later, consistent with joining the new branch. The number of qualifying evidence items (62) far exceeds the 0.91 expected by chance, so the overall difference is not a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two groups are alternative paths whose usage changed, rather than a literal replacement of activities, but the near-total disappearance of A21–A23 and the symmetric rise of A24–A26 make a substitution the most direct reading.

Other differences: A02 participation decreased from about 9% to about 1%, which is not part of the main hypothesis.
