### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: a set of alternative activities (A14, A15, A16) that were executed one per trace before A24 was replaced by a set of three activities (A21, A22, A23) that are all executed in every trace, in variable order, before A24. This is directly shown by the disappearance of the old activities, the appearance of the new ones in every trace, the change in A39's outgoing edges, and the consistent co-occurrence and ordering relations among the new activities. The number of qualifying evidence items (48) far exceeds the 0.5 expected by chance, making random sampling an unlikely explanation. A limitation is that the exact reason for the replacement is not inferable, and the order among A21/A22/A23 is not fixed, so the change is to a set of three activities rather than a fixed sequence.
