### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that a segment involving activities A25, A26, and A31 became repeated within cases. In the earlier window each of these activities occurred exactly once per trace and A25 always preceded A31; in the later window they occur multiple times in about 68% of traces, and A31's successor often becomes A25 instead of A20, with A25 and A31 interleaved or repeated. This is consistent with the introduction of a loop or repeated subprocess. The control-flow evidence is far above the chance expectation (8 items vs 0.3 expected). A competing explanation that the repetition is due to a different path cannot be fully ruled out, but the direct relational and positional evidence supports the repeated-segment interpretation.

Other differences: The timing between A34 and A14 completions roughly doubled, indicating a separate change in process timing. [TWAIT001]
