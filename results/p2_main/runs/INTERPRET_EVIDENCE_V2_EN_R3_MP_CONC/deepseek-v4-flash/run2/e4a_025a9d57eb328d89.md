### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in timing: the elapsed time between the completion of activities A16, A17, A18, A19 and the completion of A23 increased from about 1 hour to about 2 hours. This is directly shown by four consistent TWAIT items, far more than expected by chance. The change is consistent across all four pairs and the distributions shifted upward. A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that an unobserved structural change (e.g., additional activities or waiting steps) between these activities caused the longer gap, but the timing change itself is well supported.

Other differences: Activities A39 and A40 increased in occurrence and participation, and the routing after A05 shifted from A04/A09 to A03. These are separate from the timing change and are supported by control-flow items, but they do not directly relate to the timing hypothesis.
