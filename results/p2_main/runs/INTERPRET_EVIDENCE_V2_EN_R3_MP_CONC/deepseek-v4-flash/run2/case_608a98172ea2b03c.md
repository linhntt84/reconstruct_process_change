### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a flexible ordering with multiple possible paths to a more deterministic sequence, specifically involving activities A01, A04, and A06. In the later window, A01 always leads to A04, A04 no longer leads back to A01, and A06 always leads to A01, with A01 now always occurring before A04. This is directly supported by routing changes (DIR), the ordering change between A01 and A04 (REL), position shifts (POS), and lag changes (LAG) that consistently show a fixed sequence replacing earlier alternatives. The main limitation is that all evidence is control flow; no time or resource changes are observed, and the business meaning of the activities is unknown. Other differences, such as the specific lags for A02, A03, and A06, are consistent with the same reordering and do not require a separate hypothesis.
