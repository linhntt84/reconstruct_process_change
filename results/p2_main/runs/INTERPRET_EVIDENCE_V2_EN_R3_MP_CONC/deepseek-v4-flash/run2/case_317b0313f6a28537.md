- A15 occurs less often per trace: mean occurrences per trace fell from 1.89 to 1.58, and the share of traces with repeated A15 fell from about 47% to 38% (participation unchanged at 100%). [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering and routing of activities: A19, A20, and A25 now occur earlier in the trace, before A26, A27, and A28, whereas before they occurred after. This is directly shown by the reversal in relative order for all six pairs (REL001–REL006) and by the earlier positions of A19/A20/A25 and later positions of A26/A27/A28 (POS001–POS006). The routing changes are consistent with this reordering: A19/A20/A25 now lead to A26, A27 leads to A28, and A28 leads to A32, while A45 now leads to A20/A19/A25 instead of A26 (DIR001–DIR006). The lag changes for A27–A28, A28–A32, and A27–A32 also fit a tighter sequence after the reordering (LAG004–LAG006). The evidence is strong: 26 control-flow items were flagged against an expected 0.6 by chance, and the items are mutually consistent.

Other differences: A15 shows a decrease in repetition (OCC001), which is not part of the main ordering hypothesis. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two windows sampled different variants of a process that already allowed both orders, but the consistency across position, order, and routing indicators makes this unlikely.
