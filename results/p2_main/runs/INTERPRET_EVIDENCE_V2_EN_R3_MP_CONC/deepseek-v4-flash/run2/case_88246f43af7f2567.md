### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a restructuring of the flow around A03 and A12: in the earlier window, A02 and A04 typically led through A03 to A12, with A12 at the end of the trace; in the later window, A02 and A04 lead directly to A12, while A03 no longer connects to A12 and instead is followed by A11 or A10, with A16 now feeding into A03 and A03 co-occurring with A10 and A16. This is supported by consistent successor changes, co-occurrence changes, and lag changes. Limitations: some after-window support counts are modest (e.g., A02 in 28 traces, A04 in 35, A16–A10 in 41), and the new A16–A03–A10 relation is not strictly ordered in all cases, with interleaving observed.

Other differences: A16’s successor shifted from A10 to A03, A10 no longer immediately follows A16, and A12’s position became more variable, sometimes appearing earlier than before. The evidence cannot fully rule out that some of the new co-occurrences reflect repeated or interleaved executions rather than a single linear path.
