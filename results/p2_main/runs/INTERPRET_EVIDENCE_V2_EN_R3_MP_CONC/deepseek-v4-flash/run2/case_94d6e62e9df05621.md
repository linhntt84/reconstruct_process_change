### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A07 and A08 each appeared in about 53% of traces; in the later window, A07 appears in about 22% and A08 in about 28% of traces. [OCC001, OCC002]
- In the earlier window, A07 and A08 co-occurred in about 53% of traces, with A07 before A08 in 28% and A08 before A07 in 25% (both orders roughly equal). In the later window, they never co-occur; each appears alone (A07 alone 22.5%, A08 alone 28.5%) or neither (49%). [REL001]

- A08's position shifted later in the trace: its mean relative position increased from about 0.72 to 0.82, and median from 0.67 to 1.0. [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A07 and A08 changed from being executed together (in either order) to being mutually exclusive alternatives. The co-occurrence of A07 and A08 dropped from 53% of traces to 0%, and the direct transitions between them disappeared, while both activities still occur but in separate traces. The decrease in participation of each activity is consistent with them becoming alternatives. The position shift of A08 is a secondary effect of this change.

Other differences: none beyond the above.

The main limitation is that the outgoing-transition evidence in the later window rests on only 18 and 17 traces respectively, but the co-occurrence evidence is based on 102 traces in the later window, so the core conclusion is robust.
