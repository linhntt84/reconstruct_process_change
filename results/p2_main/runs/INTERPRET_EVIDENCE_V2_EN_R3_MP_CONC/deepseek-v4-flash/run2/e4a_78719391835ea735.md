### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the relative order of two groups of activities reversed: activities A14, A16, A19 moved from early to late in the process, while A15, A17, A18 moved from late to early. This is directly shown by position shifts, order reversals in pairwise relations, successor changes, and lag changes. The number of control-flow items (39) far exceeds the 0.46 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that the activities simply changed frequency without reordering is ruled out by the relational and positional evidence showing explicit order reversal.

Other differences: The elapsed times between A02 and A08 and between A06 and A08 increased in the later window, indicating a timing change as well. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
