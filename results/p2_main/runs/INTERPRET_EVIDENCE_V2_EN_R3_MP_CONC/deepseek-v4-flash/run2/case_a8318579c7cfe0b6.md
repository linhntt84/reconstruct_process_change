### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which a block of activities (A20, A21, A30, A31, A32) that was mandatory in the earlier window was removed, and a new set of activities (A19, A22, A23, A24, A25) was introduced in the later window. A04 and A48 now route to the new activities, while A33 remains present but occurs earlier in the trace. This is directly supported by the complete disappearance of the old activities, the appearance of the new ones, the corresponding changes in outgoing transitions and co-occurrence patterns, and the positional shift of A33. The number of qualifying evidence items (165) far exceeds the 1.27 expected by chance, so the overall difference is not a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A06, A08, and A10 increased in participation, and A04 decreased; these are consistent with the rerouting but are not part of the main hypothesis. A limitation is that the new activities appear in only about 20% of traces, so the exact routing among them is less certain; the disappearance of the old block is complete, indicating removal rather than a shift in path frequencies.
