### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed so that activity A10 is now executed much more often and earlier, and it is now followed by a set of activities (A15, A16, A28, A43, A48, A49) that previously were alternatives to it, while activities A19 and A20 are now executed in only about half of the traces instead of all. The large number of qualifying items (91 vs. about 1 expected by chance) makes it unlikely that these differences are due to random sampling. The main supporting signs are the sharp increase in A10 participation and its earlier position, the corresponding drop in A19/A20 participation, and the new co-occurrence of A10 with the previously alternative activities. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the case mix changed, bringing in cases that naturally use A10 and omit A19/A20, rather than a change in the process logic itself. The exact ordering between A10 and A15/A16 is not fully consistent across indicators, so the hypothesis is about participation and co-occurrence rather than a precise new sequence.

Other differences: A02, A04, A06 and A08 became rare, and A15/A16 shifted slightly later in the trace. These are not part of the main hypothesis.
