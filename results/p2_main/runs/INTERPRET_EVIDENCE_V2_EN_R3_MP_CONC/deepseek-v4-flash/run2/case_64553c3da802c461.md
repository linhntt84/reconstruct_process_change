### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from having two alternative branches after A21 to predominantly one branch via A22, with the alternative branch (involving A27, A28, A33, A34 and A35) becoming nearly unused. In the earlier window, about half the traces followed A21 → A22 → A23, and the other half followed A21 → (A27, A28, A33, A34, A35 in varying order) → A23. In the later window, about 99% of traces follow A21 → A22 → A23, while the alternative activities appear in only about 1% of traces. This is directly shown by the increase in A22 participation and its co-occurrence with A21 and A23, the corresponding drop in the other activities, and the shift in A21's outgoing transitions. The number of qualifying evidence items (62) far exceeds the 0.62 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact.

Limitations: The after-window support for the disappearing activities is very small (only 2 traces), so their internal routing after the change cannot be characterised, but the near-absence across 200 traces makes it unlikely that the alternative branch is still regularly used. The slight positional shifts of A21 and A23 are minor and consistent with the structural change.

Other differences: The relative positions of A21 and A23 moved slightly (A21 later, A23 earlier), but these are small and likely consequences of the branch change.
