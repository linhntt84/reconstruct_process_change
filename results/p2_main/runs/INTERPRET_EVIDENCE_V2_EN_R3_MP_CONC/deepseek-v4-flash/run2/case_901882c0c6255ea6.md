### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 each occurred on average about 3.5 times per trace and were present in every trace; in the later window they occur about once per trace, and the share of traces with more than one occurrence dropped from about 70% to about 1–2%. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

- In the earlier window, these five activities were interleaved or repeated in about 70% of traces (e.g., A30–A31 interleaved in 69.5%, A30–A36 in 70%, A31–A36 in 70%); in the later window this interleaving almost disappeared (about 2%) and was replaced by a fixed order: A30 before A31 in 98% of traces, A30 before A36 in 98%, and A31–A36 in both orders (about 50% each) but not interleaved. [REL001, REL002, REL007, REL012]

- The distance between these activities shrank: for example, the lag between A31 and A36 was spread over 1–5 steps before, and in the later window about 63% of occurrences are 1 step apart and 31% are 2 steps apart. [LAG003, LAG006, LAG009]

- The relative position of these activities became more concentrated: the standard deviation of the position of A30 dropped from 0.15 to 0.06, and similarly for A31, A36, A37 and A38. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

- A38's outgoing transitions changed: in the earlier window about 71% went to A30 and 28% to A25; in the later window about 96% go to A25 and 2% to A30. [DIR002]

- A06's outgoing transitions changed: in the earlier window about 42% went to A45 and the rest were spread over many successors; in the later window about 82% go to A45. [DIR001]

- A02 became much rarer: mean occurrences per trace dropped from 0.145 to 0.045 (participation from 13.5% to 4.5%). [OCC001]

- A44's outgoing transitions: before 84% to A45 and 9% to A06; after 69% to A45 and 30% to A06. [DIR003]

- A06 moved later in the trace: its mean relative position increased from 0.66 to 0.81. [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a repeated, interleaved execution of a block of activities (A30, A31, A36, A37, A38) to a single, ordered execution of that block. In the earlier window these activities were executed on average about 3.5 times per trace and were interleaved with each other in about 70% of traces; in the later window they occur once per trace, in a fixed order (A30 before A31, A30 before A36, etc.) with almost no interleaving, and the gaps between them shrank to 1–2 steps. This is directly supported by the occurrence counts, the relation indicators, the lag distributions and the reduced spread in positions. The main limitation is that some lag items have very few supporting traces in the later window (2–3 traces), but the overall pattern is consistent across many items with full support.

Other differences: A02 became much rarer; A38's successor changed from A30 to A25; A06's successor distribution shifted strongly to A45; A44's successor increased to A06; and A06 moved later in the trace. These are not part of the main hypothesis and may reflect separate changes. A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the change in A38's successor is an independent modification rather than a consequence of the loop removal.
