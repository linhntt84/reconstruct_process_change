### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A14, A15, A20, A21, and A22 each occurred in about 15–23% of traces (A14 15.5%, A15 23%, A20 20%, A21 18.5%, A22 23%). In the later window, each occurs in at most 0.5% of traces, with A20 and A21 not observed at all. [OCC001–OCC005]
- In the earlier window, A24 had a varied set of successors: about 61% to A25, and about 5–9% each to A14, A15, A20, A21, and A22. In the later window, A24 goes to A25 in about 98.5% of its outgoing transitions, with only tiny shares to A14, A15, and A22. [DIR005]
- In the earlier window, A25 had successors to A26 (39%), A22 (15.5%), A15 (13.5%), A21 (13%), A20 (12.5%), and A14 (6.5%). In the later window, A25 goes to A26 in 100% of its outgoing transitions. [DIR006]
- The intermediate activities A15, A20, A21, and A22 no longer appear together with A24, A25, or A26: in the earlier window they co-occurred with A24/A25/A26 in about 20–23% of traces; in the later window they co-occur in at most 0.5% of traces. [REL004–REL006, REL009–REL011, REL013–REL015]
- The distance between A24 and A26 shrank: in the earlier window A24 was always three steps before A26 (lag 3); in the later window it is almost always two steps before (lag 2), consistent with one intermediate activity (A25) instead of two. [LAG006]
- Similarly, A25 and A26 were separated by one or two steps in the earlier window (lag 1 39%, lag 2 61%); in the later window they are always adjacent (lag 1 100%). [LAG007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from having a set of alternative intermediate activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) between A24 and A25/A26 to a direct path A24 → A25 → A26, with those intermediate activities no longer executed. The occurrence of these activities drops from about 15–23% of traces to near zero, A24 now routes almost exclusively to A25, A25 now routes exclusively to A26, and the relational and lag indicators show that the intermediate activities no longer co-occur with A24/A25/A26 and that the gap between A24 and A26 has shortened from two intermediate events to one. The core routing items (DIR005, DIR006, REL004–006, REL009–011, REL013–015, LAG005–007) are based on all 200 traces in both windows and are mutually consistent. A competing explanation that the intermediate activities were simply rare in the later window is ruled out by the fact that they were common before and are now almost absent, and by the simultaneous shift in the A24 and A25 successor distributions.

Other differences: The position of A26 shifted slightly earlier (mean from 0.565 to 0.546), which is minor and likely a consequence of the shorter path. The position changes for A15, A20, A21, and A22 are not meaningful because those activities are nearly absent in the later window.
