### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control-flow structure: the process moved from a path where A20 led to one of A31, A32, A37, which then led to A23, to a direct path A20→A23, while the group A31/A32/A37 became optional and, when executed, is no longer followed by A23 but by A26. This is directly shown by the routing changes (DIR001, DIR003-005), the shortened lag between A20 and A23 (LAG001), and the drop in participation of A22, A31, A32, A37 (OCC001-004). The reversal of order between A21 and the group, and the mutual exclusivity of A22 with the group, are consistent with a reorganisation of the middle section. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window reflects a different variant mix rather than a process change, but the large number of consistent items (47 vs 0.51 expected by chance) makes this unlikely.

Other differences: A21 now branches to A22 or to the group, whereas before it always went to A22. [DIR002, REL006-008]
