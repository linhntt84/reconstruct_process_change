### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: activities A19, A23, A26, A27, and A28 moved from early to late positions, while A20, A21, A22, A24, A25, A29, and A33 moved from late to early positions, and the routing between A29 and A34 swapped accordingly. This is directly shown by positional shifts (POS), order reversals (REL), successor changes (DIR), and lag changes (LAG), with 73 evidence items against 0.9 expected by chance. The pattern is consistent across many high-support items (e.g., all REL and POS items have support 200). Some LAG items have very low support in one window (e.g., LAG001 after support 1), but the overall picture is corroborated by the high-support indicators.

Other differences: A44's outgoing transitions also shifted from the early group (A19, A23, A26, A27, A28) to the late group (A20, A21, A22, A24, A25), consistent with the same reordering. [DIR012, LAG011]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a change in the mix of process variants rather than a single reordering, but the direct and consistent evidence across multiple indicators makes this unlikely.
