### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the routing between activities A28, A31, and A32: A31 and A32 swapped their dominant successor, so that the typical order of these activities reversed (e.g., from A27→A32→A31→A28 to A27→A31→A32→A28). This is directly shown by the three DIR items, which are consistent and far exceed the chance expectation (3 items vs 0.29 expected). The timing change between A24 and A25 is a separate difference, not part of this routing hypothesis.

Other differences: The time between A24 and A25 decreased by about half, from roughly 1 hour to 0.5 hours. [TWAIT001]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the routing shift reflects a change in case mix rather than a structural reordering, but the consistency across the three related transitions and the high empirical strength make this unlikely.
