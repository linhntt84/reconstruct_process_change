### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A14, A15, and A20 were not observed in any trace (0% participation). In the later window, they appear in about 29%, 41%, and 30% of traces respectively, and they never co-occur with each other (each trace contains at most one of them). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006]
- In the earlier window, A22 always led to A23, and A23 always led to A24. In the later window, A22 leads to A23 in about 44% of its outgoing transitions and to A14, A15, or A20 in about 16%, 24%, and 17% respectively; A23 leads to A24 in about 57% and to A14, A15, or A20 in about 14%, 18%, and 13%. [DIR004, DIR005]
- The new activities A14, A15, and A20 lead to A23 or A24 in the later window (e.g., A14 to A23 53% and A24 47%; A15 to A23 57% and A24 43%; A20 to A23 58% and A24 42%). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The distance between A22 and A24 increased: before it was always 2 steps, after it is 3 steps in about 99.5% of cases. Similarly, A22 to A23 and A23 to A24 sometimes have an extra step in the later window. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- The mean position of A22 shifted slightly earlier (from 0.48 to 0.46) and that of A24 slightly later (from 0.55 to 0.56). [POS004, POS005]
- Timing: the elapsed time between A26 and A32 increased from about 0.89 hours to 1.81 hours (median from 0.88 to 1.85); the elapsed time between A33 and A32 increased from about 0.17 to 0.35 hours (median from 0.15 to 0.28). [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed by introducing a set of optional, mutually exclusive activities (A14, A15, A20) that are executed between the existing activities A22/A23 and A24, altering the routing. In the earlier window, the process followed a fixed sequence A22 → A23 → A24. In the later window, A22 and A23 can route to A14/A15/A20, and these new activities then lead to A23 or A24, so the sequence becomes more variable, with A24 always occurring after these new activities. The order between A23 and the new activities is not fixed (both orders occur). This is directly supported by the occurrence, direction, relation, and lag evidence, and the number of control-flow items (28) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.4). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Other differences: The timing between A26–A32 and A33–A32 increased roughly twofold, indicating a change in timing for those pairs. This is separate from the structural change and is supported by the two time items, which also exceed their chance expectation (2 vs 0.01). [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
- A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities existed before but were not sampled; however, their substantial participation in the later window and the consistent routing changes make this unlikely. The exact placement of the new activities relative to A23 is mixed, so the change is better described as a more flexible routing than a simple insertion.
