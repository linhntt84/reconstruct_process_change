### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a group of activities — A19, A20, A25, and to a lesser extent A02 — that were previously executed in essentially every case are now skipped in about half of the cases, while activities A26, A41, A46 and A47 continue to be executed. This is directly shown by the drop in participation of A19/A20/A25 from about 100% to about 53%, the corresponding drop in co-occurrence with the continuing activities, and the appearance of "neither" or "B_only" patterns in the later window. The number of qualifying evidence items (41) is far above the 0.72 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window contains a different mix of case types rather than a change in the same process.

Other differences: A41's successor shifted from A19 toward A26, and A46's successor shifted from A02 toward A47; these are consistent with A19/A20/A25 being skipped. A02 also moved earlier in the trace, but the supporting evidence in the later window is weak.
