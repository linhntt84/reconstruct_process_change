### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A19, A20, and A21 each appeared in about 30–35% of traces; in the later window each appears in about 0.5% of traces (1 of 200). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- In the earlier window, A26, A27, and A28 were essentially absent (0%, 0%, and 0.5% of traces); in the later window each appears in 100% of traces (200 of 200). [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]
- A10 participation dropped from about 25% to 11% of traces. [OCC001]
- The outgoing transitions of A19, A20, and A21 previously went almost exclusively to A29 (97–98% of their outgoing transitions); in the later window these activities are nearly absent, and their rare occurrences lead to A36, A47, and A26 respectively. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- A26, A27, and A28 now have outgoing transitions to A29 and to each other; A44's outgoing transitions changed from leading to A10/A19/A20/A21 to leading to A26/A27/A28. [DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009]
- In the later window, A26, A27, and A28 co-occur with A29 in 100% of traces, and they co-occur with each other in 100% of traces, with no fixed order between them (about half A before B, half B before A). [REL031, REL032, REL037, REL033, REL038, REL042]
- The lag between A28 and A29 changed from 1 step to 2–3 steps; the lag between A44 and A29 changed from 2–3 steps to 4–5 steps. [LAG003, LAG005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a path in which A19, A20, and A21 (and sometimes A10) led to A29, to a path in which a new set of activities A26, A27, and A28 now occurs in every trace before A29, while the old activities are almost never executed. The new activities appear in any order among themselves and always precede A29. This is supported by the near-total disappearance of A19/A20/A21, the appearance of A26/A27/A28 in all traces, the change in outgoing transitions of A44 to the new activities, and the increased lag between A44 and A29. A competing explanation that the new activities are just renamed versions of the old ones cannot be ruled out from the evidence, but the data are consistent with a structural replacement.

Other differences: A10 participation halved, and its position distribution became more spread out, but it remains present. [OCC001, POS001]
