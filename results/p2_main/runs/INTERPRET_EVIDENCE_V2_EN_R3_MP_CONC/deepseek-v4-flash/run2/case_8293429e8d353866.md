### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A25, A26, A31, A32 and A33 each occur on average about 3.06 times per trace instead of exactly once, and they repeat within a trace in about 67% of traces (recurrence support 0.665) whereas before they never repeated. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

Similarly, A25 previously always preceded A26/A31/A32/A33; now it precedes them in 34% and is interleaved in 67%. A17 previously preceded A25/A26/A31/A32/A33 in 55% of traces; now it precedes them in 19% and is interleaved in 28%. [REL006-REL010, REL011-REL014, REL001-REL005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of activities A25, A26, A31, A32, A33 changed from a single sequential execution to a repeated, interleaved execution: in the later window these activities occur about three times per trace, repeat in about two-thirds of traces, and their pairwise ordering is no longer fixed but interleaved, with A33 now often followed by A25 instead of A20. This is directly shown by the occurrence counts, the successor change, the ordering relations, and the increased positional spread and lag distances. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is whether the repetition arises from a loop or from parallel execution of these activities; the data do not distinguish those structures.

Other differences: A17 and A23 shifted to later positions, and A17's co-occurrence with the repeated block decreased slightly (from 55% to 46% of traces); these may be consequences of the added repetitions rather than independent changes.
