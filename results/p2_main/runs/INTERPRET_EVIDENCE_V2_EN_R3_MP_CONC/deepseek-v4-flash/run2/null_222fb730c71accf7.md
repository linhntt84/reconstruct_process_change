### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering of activities A36 and A37 relative to each other and to A33. In the earlier window the dominant sequence was A37 → A36 → A33; in the later window it became A36 → A37 → A33. This is directly shown by the reversed outgoing transition shares of A36 and A37 and by the tightening of the A36-to-A37 lag to always one step. The three evidence items are consistent and far exceed the chance expectation (3 items vs 0.48 expected), so the difference is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the change reflects a shift in the frequency of two existing alternative paths rather than a strict swap, but the directional reversal of both outgoing profiles and the universal adjacency make a reordering the most plausible interpretation.
