### Observed differences

- In the later window, activity A01 appears in all 200 traces (mean 2.075 occurrences per trace, repeated in about 53% of traces), whereas it was absent in the earlier window. [OCC001]
- A01's outgoing transitions go to A12 (about 53%) and A03 (about 47%) in the later window; it had no outgoing transitions before. [DIR001]
- A02's outgoing transitions changed from A03 (49%) and A12 (51%) to A01 (100%). [DIR002]
- A09's outgoing transitions changed from A12 (55%) and A03 (45%) to A01 (100%). [DIR004]
- A08's outgoing transitions changed from A13 (100%) to A01 (51%) and A13 (49%). [DIR003]
- A13's outgoing transitions changed from A12 (54%) and A03 (46%) to A08 (100%). [DIR005]
- A01 co-occurs with A02, A03, A12 in all traces in the later window (previously those activities occurred without A01). A01 appears after A02 in 47% and interleaved/repeated in 53% of traces; it appears before A03 and A12 in 47% and interleaved in 53%. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- Co-occurrence of A13 with A02, A03, A08, A12 dropped from about 38% to 17% of traces. [REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]
- The distance (in event positions) between A02 and A03/A12 increased: lags of 1 step largely disappeared, replaced by lags of 2–3 steps. [LAG001, LAG004]
- Similarly, distances between A09 and A03/A12 increased, and between A13 and A03/A12 increased. [LAG011, LAG014, LAG016, LAG018]
- The relative position of A03, A08, A12 shifted later in the trace (mean position increased by about 0.05–0.07). [POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that a new activity A01 was introduced into the process, appearing in every trace, and that it is placed between A02 and A03/A12, while also receiving control from A08 and A09. In the earlier window, A02 and A09 led directly to A03/A12; in the later window they lead to A01, which then leads to A03/A12. A08 now sometimes leads to A01 instead of A13, and A13 now leads to A08 instead of A03/A12. This is consistent with the observed co-occurrence and lag changes: A01 appears after A02 and before A03/A12, and the increased lags between A02/A09 and A03/A12 reflect the inserted A01 step. The shift of A13's successor to A08 is a separate routing change.

Other differences: The co-occurrence of A13 with A02, A03, A08, A12 decreased, and A13's successor changed to A08. Also, repetitions of A03 and A12 now tend to be further apart (lag 5 increased), which may be a consequence of the inserted A01.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 existed but was not observed in the earlier window due to sampling, but its appearance in all 200 later traces and the systematic routing changes make this unlikely.
