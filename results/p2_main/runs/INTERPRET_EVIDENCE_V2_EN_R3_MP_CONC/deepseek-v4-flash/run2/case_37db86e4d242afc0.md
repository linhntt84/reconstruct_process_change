### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which activity A01 was replaced by A03 as the step between A02 and A07, and activity A06 was added as an alternative final activity after A05. In the earlier window every trace followed A02→A01→A07→A05→A08; in the later window every trace follows A02→A03→A07→A05, and about 46.5% of traces continue to A06 instead of A08. This is directly shown by the complete disappearance of A01 and appearance of A03 in all traces, the new A02→A03 and A03→A07 edges, and the new A05→A06 edge with the corresponding co-occurrence and order relations. The number of qualifying items (24) far exceeds the 0.12 expected by chance, so the difference is not a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 and A03 are the same activity under a different label, but the evidence treats them as distinct and the change is consistent across all indicators.

Other differences: The position of A07 and A05 became more fixed (standard deviation dropped to 0) in the later window, but this is a minor secondary effect. [POS003, POS005]
