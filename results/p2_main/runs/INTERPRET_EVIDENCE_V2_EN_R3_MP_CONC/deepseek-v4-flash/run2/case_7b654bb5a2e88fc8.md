### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: activities A14, A15, and A20 moved from early positions (before A21) to later positions (after A21 and after A22), while A21 moved earlier, and A40 now connects to A21 instead of to A14/A15/A20. This is directly shown by positional shifts (POS), order reversals (REL), successor changes (DIR), and lag changes (LAG), with consistent support across many items. The screening comparison (24 items vs 0.4 expected by chance) rules out chance as a whole.

Other differences: A22 and A23 now also have outgoing edges to A14/A15/A20, and the lags between A22–A23 and A23–A27 became more variable, suggesting an extra step is sometimes inserted. These are consistent with the same reordering.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the change reflects a different mix of process variants rather than a single reordering, but the consistency of the order reversal across many activity pairs makes that less likely.
