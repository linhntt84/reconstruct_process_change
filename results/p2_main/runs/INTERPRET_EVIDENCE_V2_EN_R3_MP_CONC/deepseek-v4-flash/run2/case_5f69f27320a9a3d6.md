- The distance between A02 and A03 increases by one step (lag 3 to lag 4), while A03 and A07 become adjacent (lag 2 to lag 1). [LAG001, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which activity A04 is removed and a new activity A01 is inserted between A02 and the later activities (A03, A05, A06, A07), while A03 now leads directly to A07 instead of through A04. This is directly shown by A01 appearing in all traces and A04 disappearing, by A03's successor changing from A04 to A07, and by A02, A05 and A06 gaining transitions to A01. Relational evidence confirms that A01 co-occurs with A02 (A02 before A01) and with A03/A05/A06/A07 (A01 before them, with mixed order for A05/A06), and lag evidence shows A02–A03 distance increasing and A03–A07 distance decreasing, consistent with A01 inserted between A02 and A03 and A04 removed. The main limitation is that the order between A01 and A05/A06 is not fixed (both orders occur), so the insertion is not strictly sequential for those pairs; also, the evidence cannot rule out that A01 is a renamed or repurposed activity rather than a completely new one, but its position and relations differ from A04.

Other differences: A03, A05 and A06 shift to later positions in the trace, and the lag distributions between A02 and A05/A06 become more spread (some at lag 3), which are consistent with the insertion of A01.
