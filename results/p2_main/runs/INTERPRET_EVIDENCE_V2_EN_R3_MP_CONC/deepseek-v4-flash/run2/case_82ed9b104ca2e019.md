### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that a new step was inserted between A04 and A28: in the later window, A04 is followed by one of A19, A20, A25, A26, or A27, which then lead to A28, whereas before A04 could lead directly to A28 (among other successors). This is supported by the increase in A04 participation, the appearance of these five activities, their outgoing transitions to A28, the increase in A04–A28 co-occurrence and A-before-B, and the shift in lag from 1 to 2. A43 now often precedes A04, which may be part of the same flow (A43→A04→X→A28). The exact order among the five new activities is not fixed; they appear as alternatives rather than a fixed sequence. A competing explanation that these activities were always present but rare is unlikely given their near-zero occurrence before, but the evidence cannot fully rule out that A06 and A08 were replaced by these new activities.
