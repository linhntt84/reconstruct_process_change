- In the later window, activities A19, A20, and A25 appear in about 99.5% of traces, up from about 47% in the earlier window; their mean occurrences per trace roughly doubled from about 0.5 to about 1.0. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A41's outgoing transitions shifted from about 44% to A19 and 52% to A26 in the earlier window to about 92% to A19 and 0.5% to A26 in the later window. [DIR002]

- The distance between A41 and A26 increased: in the earlier window A41 was immediately before A26 in 52.5% of traces, while in the later window A41 is 4 steps before A26 in 88.9% of traces. [LAG001]
- A45 changed from appearing alone in about 33% of traces and co-occurring with A19 in 27.5% to never appearing alone and co-occurring with A19 in 62.5% of traces. [REL006, REL011, REL015]
- A20's successor distribution became slightly more concentrated on A26 (from 95.8% to 99.0%). [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a segment consisting of activities A19, A20, and A25 became mandatory and is executed in a fixed order between A41 and A26, replacing a direct A41-to-A26 path. In the earlier window, these activities were present in about half the traces, and A41 often went directly to A26; in the later window, they are present in almost all traces and are ordered A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 → A26. This is supported by the large increase in participation of A19, A20, A25, the shift in A41's successor from A26 to A19, the near-universal co-occurrence and fixed ordering of these pairs, and the increase in lag between A41 and A26. The number of qualifying evidence items (23) far exceeds the 0.64 expected by chance, ruling out random variation as an explanation. A limitation is that the evidence is purely control-flow; no timing or resource data are available to assess whether the change also affected those dimensions. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the process always had two alternative paths (one with the segment, one without) and the later window simply shows a shift in path frequencies; however, the near-total disappearance of the direct A41-to-A26 path and the near-universal presence of the segment suggest a structural change rather than a mere frequency shift.

Other differences: A45's behaviour changed from sometimes appearing alone to always co-occurring with A19, and A42's interleaving with A19/A20/A25 increased. These are not part of the main hypothesis.
