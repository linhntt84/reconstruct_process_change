### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the typical order was A01 → A04 → A07 → A09 → A05 → A06: A01 went to A04 in about 76% of cases, A04 to A07 in about 80%, A07 to A09 in about 90%, A09 to A05 or A06 (51% and 47%), and A05 to A06 in about 96%. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR005, DIR006, DIR003]
- In the later window, A05 and A06 moved much earlier: A05's mean position dropped from 0.89 to 0.32, A06's from 0.89 to 0.65, while A07 moved from 0.36 to 0.74 and A09 from 0.59 to 0.98. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- The order between A05/A06 and A07/A09 reversed: before, A07 and A09 preceded A05 and A06 in about 48% of traces (e.g., A07 before A05, A09 before A05, A07 before A06, A09 before A06); after, A05 precedes A07 in 55% and A06 precedes A07 in 41%, and A05 precedes A09 in 55% and A06 precedes A09 in 50%. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]
- The distance between A01/A04 and A05/A06 shrank: e.g., A01–A05 lag changed from mostly 4–5 to 1–2, A04–A05 from 3–4 to 1–2, while A01–A07 and A01–A09 lags increased. [LAG002, LAG006, LAG004, LAG005]
- A09 became a terminal activity: in the later window it had only 12 outgoing transitions (vs 96 before) and its position moved to the end. [DIR006, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering of activities: A05 and A06 moved earlier in the process, and A07 and A09 moved later, so the typical sequence changed from A01-A04-A07-A09-A05-A06 to A01-A04-A05-A06-A07-A09. This is directly shown by the reversal of order between the pairs (A05,A06) and (A07,A09) in the relational evidence, by the positional shifts, and by the shrinking lags between A01/A04 and A05/A06. The screening count (28 items vs 0.26 expected by chance) makes it very unlikely that this is a random sampling artifact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that some activities became optional or that alternative branches were introduced, but the consistent reversal of order across multiple pairs makes a reordering the most plausible interpretation.

Other differences: A01 now also leads directly to A05 (45% vs 0% before), and A04 now leads to A05, A01, and A06 instead of predominantly to A07. These are consistent with the reordering.
