### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a flexible, branching structure with multiple possible orders to a fixed linear sequence: A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32. This is directly shown by the successor distributions, the ordering relations, and the lag distances all converging to a single chain. The large number of qualifying items (58 vs 0.76 expected) makes chance an unlikely explanation.

Other differences: A08 participation decreased from 32% to 13%, A10 increased from 3% to 13%, and A08's successors changed from predominantly A15 (91%) to a more diverse set (A15 48%, A16 15%, A46 11%, etc.). A14's successor also shifted from A08 (29%) to A15 (90%). These are not part of the main linear chain.

The evidence cannot rule out that the change is due to a different mix of process variants, but the consistency and volume of evidence make that unlikely.
