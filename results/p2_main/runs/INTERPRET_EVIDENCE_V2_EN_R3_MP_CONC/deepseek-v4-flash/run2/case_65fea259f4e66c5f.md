### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new alternative path was introduced: in the later window, about 44% of traces skip A27 and instead execute a group of activities A32, A33, A38, A39, and A40 after A26, before continuing to A28. The earlier window had a single path A26 → A27 → A28 in all traces. The later window still has that path in about 56% of traces, and the new path in the rest. This is supported by the drop in A27 participation, the appearance of the new activities, the change in A26's outgoing transitions, and the relational evidence showing A26 with the new activities instead of A27, while A28 remains present. The new activities appear together with no fixed order among them. A competing explanation that the new activities are a parallel branch rather than an alternative is unlikely because A26–A27 cooccurrence drops exactly when the new activities appear, and A27 and the new activities are almost never observed together.

Other differences: A26's position shifted slightly earlier (mean 0.49 to 0.46), likely a consequence of the shorter path in the alternative branch. [POS001]
