### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process changed so that a group of activities (A19, A20, A25) that previously occurred early and were followed by A26 (and A27) now occur later, after A26/A27. This is supported by the consistent reversal in ordering (REL), the later positions (POS), the changed successors (DIR), and the lag shifts (LAG). The evidence is strong: 31 control-flow items vs 0.62 expected by chance. Limitations: some LAG items have very low support (e.g., LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010), but the main items (DIR, POS, REL) have high support. The evidence cannot rule out that the reordering is accompanied by other changes, but the main hypothesis is clear.

Other differences: A24 participation increased from about 43% to 58% of traces (OCC001). This is separate from the reordering.
