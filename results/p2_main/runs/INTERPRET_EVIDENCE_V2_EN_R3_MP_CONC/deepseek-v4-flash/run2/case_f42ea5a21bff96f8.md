### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: after A19/A20, the process now takes a branch through A24–A26 instead of A21–A23, and A06 has been inserted as a step between A19/A20/A29/A30/A34 and A35, with A34 now leading to A06 rather than directly to A35. This is supported by the large, consistent shifts in participation, outgoing transitions, and ordering relations across many indicators, far exceeding the number expected by chance (92 items vs 1.09 expected). The main limitation is that the two windows are finite samples, but the magnitude and consistency of the changes make a sampling artefact unlikely.

Other differences: A02 and A08 participation decreased from about 17% and 13.5% to 7.5% and 5% respectively, but these are minor and not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001, OCC003]
