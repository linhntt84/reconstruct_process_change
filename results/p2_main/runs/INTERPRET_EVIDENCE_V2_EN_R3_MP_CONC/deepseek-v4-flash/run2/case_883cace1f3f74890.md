### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20, and A25 appear in about 30%, 41%, and 30% of traces respectively, whereas they were absent in the earlier window. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- These three activities are mutually exclusive: they never co-occur in the same trace; each trace contains at most one of them. [REL001, REL002, REL006]
- In the earlier window, A27 led almost always to A28 (about 99%) and A28 led almost always to A29 (about 99%). In the later window, A27 leads to A28 in about 44% of its outgoing transitions, and to A19, A20, A25 in about 15%, 22%, and 17% respectively; A28 leads to A29 in about 55% and to A19, A20, A25 in about 13%, 17%, and 12%. [DIR004, DIR005]
- The new activities are placed between A27 and A29: A27 occurs before A19/A20/A25 in about 29%, 40%, and 30% of traces respectively, and A19/A20/A25 occur before A29 in about 30%, 39%, and 30% of traces. [REL003, REL007, REL010, REL005, REL009, REL012]
- The distance between A27 and A28 increased: in the earlier window they were adjacent (lag 1) in 99% of traces; in the later window they are adjacent in 44% and separated by one activity in 55%. Similarly, A28 and A29 were adjacent in 99% of traces before, and in the later window they are adjacent in 56% and separated by one activity in 44%. [LAG001, LAG003]
- A10 participation increased from about 5% to about 14% of traces. [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new optional step was introduced: one of three alternative activities (A19, A20, or A25) is executed in a substantial share of traces, inserted between the previously adjacent A27 and A29, with A28 still present but its position relative to the new activity varying. This is directly shown by the appearance of these activities, their mutual exclusivity, their ordering relative to A27 and A29, and the increased lags between A27–A28 and A28–A29. The number of qualifying items (29) far exceeds the 0.54 expected by chance, so the overall difference is not a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities are not a single inserted step but a set of alternative branches that sometimes occur before or after A28; the mixed order with A28 (e.g., A19 before A28 in 16% and after in 13%) leaves some ambiguity about the exact position of A28 relative to the new activity.

Other differences: A10 participation increased from about 5% to about 14% of traces, and the mean position of A27 shifted slightly earlier and A29 slightly later, consistent with the insertion of new activities between them. [OCC001, POS004, POS005]
