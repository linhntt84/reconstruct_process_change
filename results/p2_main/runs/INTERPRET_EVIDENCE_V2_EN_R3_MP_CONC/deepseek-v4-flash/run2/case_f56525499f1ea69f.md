### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from having two alternative branches (one containing A33/A35/A36 and the other A37/A38/A39) to a single sequential path that includes all of these activities, with A33/A35/A36 before A37/A38/A39. This is supported by the increase in participation of these activities from about half to nearly all traces, the co-occurrence of these activities in almost all traces after (whereas before they were mutually exclusive), and the increased lag between them (now 4–5 steps apart). The DIR changes show that A33, A35, A36 now lead to A37/A38/A39, and A30 now leads to A33/A35/A36, consistent with a sequential chain. The main limitation is that we cannot rule out that the process now has a different structure that still produces these observations, but the consistency across OCC, REL, DIR, POS, and LAG strongly supports this interpretation.

Other differences: A04 decreased in participation and its outgoing transitions shifted from mostly A16 to A15/A48; A06 and A10 increased slightly; A30's outgoing transitions shifted from a broad split to A35/A33/A36; A31's position moved later. These are not part of the main hypothesis.
