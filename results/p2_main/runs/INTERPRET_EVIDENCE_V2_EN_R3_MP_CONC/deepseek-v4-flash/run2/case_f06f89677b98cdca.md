### Observed differences

- A01 was absent in the earlier window and appears in about 48 in 100 traces in the later window, positioned late in the trace (mean relative position about 0.77). [OCC001, POS001]
- A18's outgoing transitions shifted strongly to A01: about 65 in 100 transitions from A18 go to A01 in the later window, whereas A18 never led to A01 before. [DIR004]
- A18 and A01 co-occur in about 48 in 100 traces in the later window, always with A18 before A01; before, A18 occurred alone in about 41 in 100 traces. [REL001]
- A02 increased from about 0.48 to 1.01 occurrences per trace, and repetition appeared (recurrence support from 0 to 0.255). [OCC002]
- A08 decreased from about 0.87 to 0.475 occurrences per trace, and repetition disappeared (recurrence support from 0.2 to 0). [OCC003]
- A09's outgoing transitions: the share to A02 rose from about 38% to 65%, while shares to A04, A07 and A08 fell. [DIR002]
- A14's outgoing transitions: the share to A08 fell from about 63% to 24%, while shares to A06 and A15 rose to about 21% and 19%. [DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity A01 was introduced, executed after A18 in about half of the cases. A01 was absent before, appears in about 48% of traces after, A18's outgoing transition to A01 is about 65% after, and A18 precedes A01 in all co-occurrences. This is directly shown by OCC001, DIR004, POS001 and REL001. The main limitation is that we cannot tell whether A01 is a genuinely new activity or an existing one that was previously not executed, but the change is clear.

Other differences: A02 became more frequent and sometimes repeated; A08 became less frequent and no longer repeated; A09 and A14 changed their routing (A09 more to A02, A14 less to A08 and more to A06/A15). These are not part of the main hypothesis. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 existed before but was too rare to be sampled; however, the consistent relational and positional evidence makes this unlikely.
