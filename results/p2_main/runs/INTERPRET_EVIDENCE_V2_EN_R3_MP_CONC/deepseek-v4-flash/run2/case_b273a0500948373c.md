### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which a set of activities executed after A15 switched from A19, A20 and A21 to A16, A17 and A18. In the earlier window, A15 led predominantly to A19–A21, which were executed together; in the later window, A15 leads predominantly to A16–A18, which are now executed together, with no fixed order among them. This is directly shown by the reversed participation shares, the redistributed outgoing transitions from A15, and the co-occurrence patterns. The number of qualifying items (34) far exceeds the 0.52 expected by chance, and the pattern is consistent across all indicators. A competing explanation of a rare variant is implausible given the magnitude and consistency of the changes.

Other differences: A25 shows increased co-occurrence with the new activity set and decreased co-occurrence with the old set, but it is not part of the main hypothesis. The evidence cannot rule out that the change reflects a different underlying process variant, but the consistency across many indicators supports the stated hypothesis.
