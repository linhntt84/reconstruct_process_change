### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process now has two alternative branches: one that includes A27 (the earlier path) and one that includes the new activities A32, A33, A38. In the later window, about half the traces follow the new branch, while the other half still follow the old path with A27. The new branch appears to involve A14 → A04 → (A32/A33/A38) → A28, as shown by A14 now often preceding A04, A04 preceding the new activities, and the new activities preceding A28. The old branch retains A14 → A15 and A26 → A27 → A28. A27 participation halved, and A32/A33/A38 appeared in about half of traces, with A27 and the new activities almost never co-occurring, indicating they are alternatives. A26 now branches to the new activities as well. A04 moved earlier in the trace, consistent with its new position after A14. The large number of qualifying items (55 vs 0.73 expected) rules out chance as a whole.

Other differences: A06 participation dropped from about 20% to 4%, and A14 to A15 lag shifted from mostly 1 to mostly 2. These are not part of the main branch hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities are not a separate branch but a replacement of A27 in some cases, with A27 still present in the remaining cases; however, the mutual exclusivity and the specific successor changes support an alternative branch.
