### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which A10 became a frequent early step, often between A14 and A15, while before it was rare and occurred later. Specifically, A14 now leads to A10 in about 66% of its outgoing transitions (up from about 1%), and A10 leads to A15 in about 85% of its outgoing transitions (up from about 5%), while A14's direct transition to A15 dropped from about 86% to 33%. A10's participation rose from about 19% to 70% of traces, and its position moved earlier. This is consistent with A10 being inserted as an intermediate step between A14 and A15. The evidence is strong: 77 control-flow items were flagged against 1 expected by chance, and the directional and relational items agree.

Other differences: The ordering among the later activities (A20, A22, A23, A24, A25, A26, A31, A32) changed, with A24, A25, and A26 moving earlier relative to A20, A22, and A23. Also, A04 and A08 decreased in participation. These are not part of the main hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A10 was always present but rare, and the change is a shift in frequency rather than a new activity; however, the new A14→A10 and A10→A15 edges suggest a structural insertion.
