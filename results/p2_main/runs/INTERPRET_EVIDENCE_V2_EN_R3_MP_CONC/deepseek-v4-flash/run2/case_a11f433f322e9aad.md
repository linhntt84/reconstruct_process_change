### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained A26 followed by A27 and then A28: A26 preceded A27 in about 99.5% of traces, A27 preceded A28 in about 99.5%, and the gap between A26 and A28 was 2 steps in about 98% of traces. [OCC001, REL001, REL005, LAG001]
- In the later window, A27 appears in about 51% of traces (down from 100%), while A32, A33 and A38 each appear in about 49% of traces (up from 0%). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- In the later window, A26 leads to A27 in about 50% of its outgoing transitions and to A32, A33 or A38 in the remaining ~48% (A33 about 17%, A38 about 16%, A32 about 15%). [DIR001]
- In the later window, A32, A33 and A38 co‑occur with each other in about 49% of traces, in mixed order (e.g., A32 before A33 in about 22% and after in about 27%), and they all lead to A28 (A32 to A28 about 28%, A33 to A28 about 27%, A38 to A28 about 43%). [REL012, REL013, REL014, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- In the later window, the gap between A26 and A28 is 2 steps in about 50% of traces and 4 steps in about 48%, consistent with the inserted activities. [LAG001]
- In the later window, A28 appears without A27 in about 49% of traces, whereas before A27 and A28 always co‑occurred. [REL005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, about half of the cases follow a new path in which A26 is followed by a group of activities A32, A33 and A38 (executed together in varying order) before A28, instead of the previous A26 → A27 → A28 path. The other half still follow the original path. This is supported by the drop in A27 participation from 100% to about 51%, the appearance of A32/A33/A38 in about 49% of traces, the routing from A26 to these activities, their co‑occurrence and mixed order, and their subsequent routing to A28, together with the increased lag between A26 and A28.

Other differences: A27’s outgoing transitions now include small shares to A09, A34 and A25 (about 1–2% each) in addition to the dominant A28. [DIR002] A26’s outgoing share to A27 decreased from about 98.5% to about 50.5%. [DIR001]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new branch is a rare variant that became frequent; however, the large number of consistent items makes this unlikely.
