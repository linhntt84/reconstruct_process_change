### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the process: the path that previously went A49 → A02 → A50 is now largely A49 → A50 directly, with A02 becoming much less frequent; A25 now leads directly to A27 instead of to A35/A36/A41; A26 now leads to A35/A36/A41 instead of to A30; and A35/A36/A41 now lead to A30 instead of to A27. The order between A26/A27 and A35/A36/A41 reverses, and the lag changes (A25–A27 becoming adjacent, A26–A30 becoming distant) are consistent with this re-routing. The large number of qualifying items (45 vs. 0.83 expected by chance) and the directness of the successor and order evidence make a chance explanation unlikely.

Other differences: A06 and A10 increase in participation, and A02's position moves earlier; these are not part of the main hypothesis. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the observed changes reflect a different mix of case types rather than a modification of the process itself, but the direct successor and order changes are strongly indicative of a structural shift.
