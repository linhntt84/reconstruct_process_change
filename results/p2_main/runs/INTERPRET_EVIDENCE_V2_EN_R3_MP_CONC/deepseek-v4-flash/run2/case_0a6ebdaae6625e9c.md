### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a branching after A26 among several activities (A32, A33, A38, A39, A40) to a single path through A27 (and A28). In the earlier window, A26 was followed by one of several activities with roughly equal probability, and those activities appeared in about half the traces. In the later window, A26 is almost always followed by A27, and A27 appears in almost all traces, while the other activities are almost never observed. This is directly shown by the shift in A26's outgoing transitions and the corresponding changes in occurrence and co-occurrence. The large number of qualifying items (63 vs 0.76 expected by chance) rules out chance as an overall explanation. The main limitation is that the later-window observations of the disappearing activities are based on very few traces (3–5), so their exact behaviour after the change is uncertain, but their near-absence is well supported.

Other differences: The position of A26 shifted slightly later in the trace (mean from 0.46 to 0.49), and A28's position shifted slightly earlier (mean from 0.588 to 0.561), but these are minor and may be consequences of the structural change. [POS001, POS002]
