### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process was restructured around A08: A08 became a much more common early activity, and the routing of several activities changed, with A15 now often leading to A08, and A19, A20, A25, A26, A27 now leading to A30/A34 instead of A28, while these activities moved later in the trace. This is supported by the large increase in A08 participation and its earlier position, the direct successor changes for A08, A15, and the A19/A20/A25/A26/A27 group, and the corresponding positional shifts and relational order changes. The screening shows 84 control-flow items vs 1.02 expected by chance, so the overall difference is not a sampling artifact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A30 and A34 were already present but rare and became more frequent; however, the successor changes are direct and consistent.

Other differences: A02, A06, A10 decreased in participation, and A15 now also leads to A08.
