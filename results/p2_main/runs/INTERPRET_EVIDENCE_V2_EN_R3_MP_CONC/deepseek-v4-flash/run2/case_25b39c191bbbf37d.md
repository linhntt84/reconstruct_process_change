### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 never occurred; in the later window each appears in about 17–22% of traces, once per trace, at roughly the middle position of the trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- A24 always led to A25 before; in the later window it leads to A25 in about 49% of its transitions and to each of the five new activities in about 8–12%. [DIR006]
- A25 always led to A26 before; in the later window it leads to A26 in about 52% of its transitions and to each of the five new activities in about 6–11%. [DIR007]
- The five new activities never occur together in a trace; each appears alone in about 16–22% of traces, so they behave as alternatives to one another. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL007, REL008, REL012]
- Where a new activity co-occurs with A24 or A26, the new activity comes first; with A25, both orders occur in similar proportions. [REL004, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL013, REL014, REL015]
- Distances between the original activities grew: A24→A25 was always one step apart, now one step in about 49% and two steps in about 51%; A24→A26 was always two steps apart, now three steps in about 99%; A25→A26 was always one step apart, now two steps in about 48%. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a set of mutually exclusive activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) was introduced into the process as an alternative branch inserted between the A24→A25→A26 chain: A24 and A25 now sometimes route through one of these new activities instead of directly to A25 or A26, which lengthens the chain by one step. This is supported directly by the appearance of the five activities, the rerouting of A24 and A25 towards them, the consistent precedence of the new activities before A24 and A26, and the increased lags between A24, A25 and A26. The screening count (34 items vs 0.44 expected by chance) makes random fluctuation an unlikely explanation for the whole set.

Other differences: the order between the new activities and A25 is mixed (both orders occur), whereas with A24 and A26 the new activities always come first; this suggests no fixed ordering constraint between the new activities and A25.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that these activities existed before the window but were too rare to be sampled in the 200 earlier traces; the evidence cannot exclude that possibility, though the consistency of the routing, order and lag changes makes it less plausible.
