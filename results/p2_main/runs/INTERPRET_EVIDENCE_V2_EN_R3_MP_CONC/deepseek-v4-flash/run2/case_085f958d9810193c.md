### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed in two related ways: activity A02 was introduced or greatly expanded as an early step, and the ordering of a block of activities reversed. Specifically, A26 and A27 used to precede A35, A36, A41, A42, A43, but in the later window those activities precede A26 and A27. At the same time, A30, which was a common successor of that block, is no longer used, and A27 takes its place. A25's successor also changed from A27 to that block. These changes are directly shown by the successor distributions (DIR), the relative order (REL), and the position shifts (POS). The large number of qualifying items (64 vs <1 expected) makes chance an unlikely explanation. A limitation is that some LAG items have very small support in the earlier window, so the exact lag distances before are uncertain, but the order reversal is well supported by REL and DIR.

Other differences: A46's successors changed from A19/A20 to A02 as well, which may be part of the same expansion of A02.
