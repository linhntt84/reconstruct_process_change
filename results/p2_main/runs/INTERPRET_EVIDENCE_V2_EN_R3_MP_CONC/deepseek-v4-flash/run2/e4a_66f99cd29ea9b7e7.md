### Observed differences

- In the later window, activity A20 almost always leads to A22 (about 99 in 100 transitions), whereas before it led to A30 (40%), A31 (33%) and A36 (28%). [DIR001]
- Activity A21, which before always led to A25, now leads to A30 (36%), A31 (33%) and A36 (31%), with A25 almost disappearing (1%). [DIR002]
- Activities A30, A31 and A36, which before each had A22 as a successor (about 28–38% of their transitions), now almost never lead to A22 (0.5%) and instead lead to A25 (about 30–36%). [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- A21 and A22 occur earlier in traces (mean position from about 0.62 to 0.53 for A21, and from 0.47 to 0.38 for A22), while A30, A31 and A36 occur later (mean position from about 0.41 to 0.58). [POS001–POS005]
- The order between A21/A22 and A30/A31/A36 reversed: before, A30/A31/A36 preceded A21 and A22 in essentially all traces; after, A21 and A22 precede A30/A31/A36 in about 99 in 100 traces. [REL001–REL006]
- A20 and A22 became adjacent (lag 1 instead of lag 4), while A21 and A25 became far apart (lag 4 instead of lag 1). [LAG001, LAG004]
- The time between A01 and A04 increased from about 0.84 hours to 1.75 hours; the time between A24 and A28 decreased from about 1.02 hours to near zero. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: A21 and A22 now occur earlier in the trace, before A30, A31 and A36, whereas before they occurred after these activities. This is directly shown by the reversal of order between A21/A22 and A30/A31/A36 (REL001–REL006), the shift in positions (POS001–POS005), and the change in adjacency between A20 and A22 (LAG001). The routing changes (DIR001–DIR005) are consistent with this reordering: A20 now feeds directly into A22, and A25, which used to follow A21, now follows A30/A31/A36 instead. The evidence is extensive (22 control-flow items vs. 0.42 expected by chance) and internally consistent.

Other differences: The timing between A01–A04 increased and between A24–A28 decreased (TWAIT001, TWAIT002). These are separate from the structural reordering and are supported by two time items vs. 0.009 expected by chance.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a shift in the mix of case types rather than a single reordering of the same process, although the consistency of the order reversal across multiple activity pairs makes this less likely.
