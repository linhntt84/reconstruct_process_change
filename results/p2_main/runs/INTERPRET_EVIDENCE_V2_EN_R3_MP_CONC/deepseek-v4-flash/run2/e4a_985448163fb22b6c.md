### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A25, A26, A31, A32, and A33 each occur on average about 3.06 times per trace instead of exactly once, and they are repeated in about 67% of traces (recurrence support 0.665) whereas before they never repeated. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- In the later window, A33's outgoing transitions: about 67% go to A25 and 33% to A20, whereas before all went to A20. [DIR001]
- The relative positions of these five activities shifted later in the trace (mean position increased, spread increased). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- The order between these activities changed: before, A25 always preceded A26, A31, A32, and A33; and A26, A31, A32 had mixed order among themselves but no interleaving. In the later window, for every pair among these five, about 67% of traces show interleaving or repetition, and the fixed order decreased correspondingly. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]
- The lag between occurrences of these activities (within five steps) shifted from mostly 1–2 steps to spread over 1–5 steps, with the share of lag 1 dropping from about 64–69% to about 10–13%. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]
- The elapsed time between completion of A09 and A10 increased from about 0.98 hours to about 1.98 hours (median), and similarly between A11 and A10 from about 1.03 to 1.99 hours. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that a block of activities (A25, A26, A31, A32, A33) is now executed repeatedly and in an interleaved manner within each case, rather than once in a fixed order. This is directly shown by the increase in mean occurrences and recurrence, the shift in A33's successor to A25 (consistent with looping back to the block), the change in pairwise order from fixed to interleaved/repeated, and the spread of lags from 1–2 steps to 1–5 steps. The timing increase between A09–A10 and A11–A10 is a separate change in timing, not part of this structural hypothesis.

Other differences: The elapsed time between A09 and A10 and between A11 and A10 roughly doubled in the later window. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the repetition arises from a new parallel branch rather than a loop, but the interleaving and recurrence patterns strongly favour repetition.
