### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A19, A20, and A25 each occurred in about 30–36% of traces (A19 35.5%, A20 30.5%, A25 36% mean occurrences). In the later window, A19 and A25 are absent (0%), and A20 occurs in only 0.5% of traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the earlier window, A41's outgoing transitions were distributed among A19 (33%), A20 (27%), and A25 (33%), with small shares to others. In the later window, A41 goes to A26 in 92% of its transitions, with only small shares to other activities. [DIR004]
- In the earlier window, A19, A20, and A25 each had outgoing transitions predominantly to A26 (97–99%). In the later window, these activities have no outgoing transitions because they are absent. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- In the earlier window, A14 co-occurred with A19, A20, and A25 in about 30–35% of traces, always with A14 before them. In the later window, A14 occurs alone in 100% of traces, with no co-occurrence with these activities. [REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Similarly, A19, A20, and A25 co-occurred with A26, A31, and A41 in about 30–35% of traces in the earlier window, with the former before the latter. In the later window, these co-occurrences disappear; A26, A31, and A41 occur alone. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021]
- In the earlier window, A41 and A26 were separated by two steps in 93.5% of traces; in the later window, they are adjacent (lag 1) in 92.5% of traces. [LAG002]
- In the earlier window, A24 and A31 were separated by two steps in 59% of traces and one step in 39%; in the later window, one step in 59% and two steps in 39%. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a set of activities (A19, A20, A25) that were previously executed in a substantial share of traces (about 30–35% each) is no longer executed in the later window, and that the process now proceeds directly from A41 to A26 instead of passing through these activities. This is directly shown by the disappearance of these activities (OCC), the change in A41's outgoing transitions from A19/A20/A25 to A26 (DIR004), and the loss of co-occurrence between these activities and their neighbours (REL). The lag between A41 and A26 changing from 2 to 1 further confirms that the intermediate activities are bypassed. The large number of qualifying items (36 vs 0.61 expected by chance) makes it unlikely that this is a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A24 and A31 are now closer together (lag 1 more common than lag 2), which may reflect a separate change in ordering. The positions of A14 and A26 shifted slightly, but these are minor and may be consequences of the removal of the intermediate activities.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A19, A20, and A25 were moved to a different part of the process not captured by the observed windows, but the evidence shows no trace of them in the later window, and the direct A41→A26 link suggests they were removed rather than relocated.
