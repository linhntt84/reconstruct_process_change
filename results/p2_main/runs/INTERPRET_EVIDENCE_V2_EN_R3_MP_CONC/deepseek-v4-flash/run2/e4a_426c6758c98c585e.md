### Observed differences

- In the later window, the process follows a nearly fixed sequence: A26 → A17 → A15 → A18 → A19 → A21 → A20 → A27. Each of these activities has one dominant outgoing transition (about 99–100% of cases) in the later window, whereas in the earlier window each had several alternatives with substantial shares (e.g., A15 went to A18 in 62% of cases, to A19/A20/A21 in 12–14% each; A18 went to A27 in 53%, to A21/A19/A20 in 12–19% each). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]
- Ordering between pairs became fixed: for example, A15 before A19 rose from 26% to 99.5% of co-occurring traces; A18 before A19 from 12% to 99.5%; A15 before A21 from 50% to 100%; A17 before A19 from 50% to 100%. [REL001, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL008]
- Distances between activities became concentrated at the values expected from the linear sequence: e.g., A15 and A18 are adjacent in 99.5% of traces (was 62% at lag 1), A15 and A20 are four steps apart in 99.5% (was spread over lags 1–4), A17 and A15 adjacent in 99.5% (was 56% at lag 1). [LAG001, LAG003, LAG006, LAG013, LAG017, LAG020, LAG022, LAG023, LAG024, LAG025, LAG027, LAG029]
- Positions of activities shifted: A15, A17, A18 occur earlier in traces (mean position decreased by about 0.05–0.06), while A19, A20, A21 occur later (mean position increased by about 0.03–0.06). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Timing between two activity pairs increased: the gap between A22 and A29 rose from about 0.90 hours to 1.88 hours (median), and between A30 and A29 from about 0.18 to 0.34 hours (median). [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a flexible, branching order to a fixed linear sequence among activities A26, A17, A15, A18, A19, A21, A20, A27. The DIR, REL, LAG, and POS indicators all consistently show this: outgoing transitions become deterministic, pairwise orders become fixed, distances match the linear chain, and positions shift accordingly. The number of control-flow items (50) far exceeds the 0.47 expected by chance, so this is not a sampling artifact. A competing explanation that the observed differences are due to random variation is ruled out by the screening comparison. However, the evidence cannot rule out that some activities (e.g., A22, A29, A30) are part of a separate sub-process; the time items indicate a separate change in timing.

Other differences: The elapsed time between A22 and A29 and between A30 and A29 increased roughly twofold in the later window, indicating a timing change independent of the structural change. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
