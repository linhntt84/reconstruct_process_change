### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the routing after A20: instead of executing the group A24–A26, the process now executes the group A21–A23, and these three activities are now executed together rather than being rare alternatives. This is supported consistently by occurrence, direct successor and co-occurrence indicators, with strong support (200 traces for A20 and its successors). Both groups are present in both windows, so this is a shift in the frequency of existing paths, not an addition or removal of activities.

Other differences: A10’s role diminished and moved earlier, but this rests on only 33 and 14 traces, so it is a weaker observation. A02 increased slightly.

The main limitation is that the A10 change could be a sampling artifact given its low support; the A20 routing shift itself is well supported.
