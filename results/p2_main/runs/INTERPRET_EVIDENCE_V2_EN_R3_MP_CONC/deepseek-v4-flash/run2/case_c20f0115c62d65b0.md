### Observed differences

- Activities A19, A20, and A25 occurred in about 32%, 28%, and 40% of traces in the earlier window, respectively, and in about 0.5% of traces (1 trace each) in the later window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the earlier window, A19, A20, and A25 co-occurred with A27, A28, and A29 in about 28–40% of traces; in the later window, these co-occurrences dropped to about 0.5%. For example, A19 with A29 co-occurred in 32% of traces before and 0.5% after; A25 with A29 in 40% before and 0.5% after. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- The core activities A27, A28, and A29 now follow each other more directly: A27 is followed by A28 within one step in about 98.5% of traces after vs 53.5% before; A28 is followed by A29 within one step in about 98.3% after vs 45% before; A27 to A29 within two steps in about 96.8% after vs 0% before (before it was mostly three steps, 94.3%). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- The outgoing transitions of A27 and A28 shifted strongly toward A28 and A29 respectively: A27's share to A28 rose from about 53% to 97%, and A28's share to A29 rose from about 45% to 98.5%. [DIR004, DIR005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activities A19, A20, and A25 were removed from the process (or became extremely rare). They were present in about 30–40% of traces before and in less than 1% of traces after, and their co-occurrence with the core activities A27, A28, and A29 dropped correspondingly. The core sequence A27→A28→A29 became the dominant path, with A27 and A28 almost always leading directly to the next core activity. This is supported by the large number of qualifying items (27 vs 0.55 expected by chance) and the consistency across occurrence, relation, and lag indicators. A limitation is that the after window contains only one trace with each of A19, A20, and A25, so their new role cannot be characterized; they may still exist as rare alternatives.

Other differences: The ordering between A27, A28, and A29 tightened, with A27→A28 and A28→A29 now almost always adjacent. This is likely a consequence of removing the intermediate activities, but the evidence does not directly show that A19, A20, or A25 were positioned between them. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is a change in case mix, but the magnitude and consistency of the evidence make this unlikely.
