### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: activities A19, A21, A24, and A25 now occur earlier relative to A20, A22, A23, and A29, and A02 has been introduced earlier, leading into A19/A21/A24, which then lead to A25 instead of A30. This is directly shown by the order reversals in many activity pairs, the position shifts, and the successor changes. The large number of qualifying items (58 vs. 0.75 expected by chance) makes random fluctuation an unlikely explanation.

Other differences: A41 shows increased repetition, and A02's participation increased substantially. These are not part of the main reordering hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two windows contain different case types or populations, which could produce the same observed differences without a change in the underlying process logic. However, the consistency and breadth of the relational evidence make this less likely.
