### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control flow in which the order of two blocks of activities reversed: activities A14, A16, and A19 moved from early to late, while A15, A17, and A18 moved from late to early, and the routing between these groups and A20/A25 changed accordingly. This is directly shown by consistent positional, relational, and successor evidence across many items. The main limitation is that A14, A16, and A19 appear in only about a third of traces, so their positional change rests on fewer observations, but the relational evidence with full support corroborates the same reordering.

Other differences: A24 and A35 also changed their successor sets in line with the same reordering, and A20 and A24 shifted slightly later in position. [POS007, POS008]
