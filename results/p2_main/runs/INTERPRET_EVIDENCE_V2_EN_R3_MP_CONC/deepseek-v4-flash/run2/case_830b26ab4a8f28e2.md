### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activity A01 occurred in every trace, on average about 3.5 times per trace, and was often repeated (about 74 in 100 traces). In the later window, A01 is completely absent: it occurs in no trace and has no outgoing transitions. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001]
- In the earlier window, A01 co-occurred with A03, A05, A08, A09 and A10 in most traces (between about 47% and 100% of traces). In the later window, A01 never co-occurs with any of them; those activities appear alone or without A01. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]
- The direct successors of A03 and A05 changed: before, about 88% of A03's outgoing transitions and about 85% of A05's went to A01. In the later window, neither A03 nor A05 leads to A01; instead they lead to A08, A09 and A10, with A09 and A10 each taking roughly 40% of the transitions. [DIR002, DIR003]
- Among the remaining activities (A03, A05, A08, A09, A10), the distance between them in the trace shifted: pairs like A03–A09, A03–A10, A05–A09 and A05–A10 now occur more often at lag 1 (immediately adjacent) and less often at lags 3–4 than before. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG005, LAG006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A01 was removed from the process, so that the activities that previously flowed through A01 (A03 and A05) now go directly to A08, A09 and A10. This is shown directly by A01's complete disappearance in the later window (zero participation, zero occurrences, zero outgoing transitions), by the loss of all co-occurrence between A01 and the other activities, and by the redirection of A03's and A05's outgoing edges away from A01. The lag shifts among the remaining activities are consistent with this removal, since they now appear closer together without A01 in between. The evidence count (17 items vs. 0.26 expected by chance) is far above what a stable process would produce. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 still exists but was not observed in the 200 later traces; however, the complete absence across all indicators and the coherent redirection of successors make this unlikely.

Other differences: The lag distributions among A03, A05, A08, A09 and A10 changed, with several pairs becoming more likely to be adjacent. This is best explained as a consequence of A01's removal rather than an independent change.
