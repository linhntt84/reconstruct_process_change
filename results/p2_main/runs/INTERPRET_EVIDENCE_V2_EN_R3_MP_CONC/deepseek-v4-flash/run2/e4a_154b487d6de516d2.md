### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the routing from activity A06: in the later window, A06 more often leads directly to A07 (within one step) and less often to A08, with A07 becoming the dominant successor instead of A08. This is directly shown by the redistribution of A06's outgoing transitions and by the shortened lag between A06 and A07. The two control-flow items are consistent and far exceed the number expected by chance (2 items vs. 0.32 expected). The change appears as a shift in the frequencies of existing paths, since A06, A07, A08, and A09 all occur in substantial shares of traces in both windows. A minor additional shift is the slight increase in the share going to A09, which is part of the same redistribution. The evidence cannot rule out that the change is limited to the specific traces containing A06, but the sample sizes (about 90–96 traces for the routing, 47–51 for the lag) are adequate.
