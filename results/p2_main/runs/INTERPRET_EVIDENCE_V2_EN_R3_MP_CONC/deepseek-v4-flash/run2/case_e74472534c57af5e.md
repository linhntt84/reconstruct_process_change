### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A30, A31, and A36 occur about 3.5 times per trace on average instead of about 1 time, and the share of traces containing them more than once rose from about 1% to about 68% [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].
- The outgoing edge from A36 shifted: before, about 98% of its transitions went to A25; in the later window, about 71% go to A30 and about 28% to A25 [DIR005].
- The order between A30 and A36 changed: before, A30 preceded A36 in about 99% of traces with a lag of 2 steps; in the later window, A30 precedes A36 in only about 32% of traces, and the two activities are interleaved or repeated in about 68% of traces [REL005, LAG001]. Similarly, A14 and A36, A14 and A30, A05 and A30, and A05 and A36 lost their fixed order, with interleaving/repetition rising from near 0% to about 34–68% [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004].
- A36 now precedes A30 in about 68% of traces (136 of 200) in the later window, whereas this was almost never observed before (1 of 200) [LAG002].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a loop was introduced or became active: A36 now often returns to A30 instead of proceeding to A25, so the sequence A30–A31–A36 repeats multiple times per trace and the fixed order between these activities is lost. This is directly shown by the increased occurrence and recurrence of A30, A31, and A36 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003], the shift in A36's outgoing edge from A25 to A30 [DIR005], and the change from a fixed A30-before-A36 order to interleaving/repetition and occasional A36-before-A30 [REL001–REL005, LAG001, LAG002]. The evidence is limited to control-flow indicators; no time or resource items are available. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that a different variant containing a loop replaced the previous linear variant, but the routing change itself is clearly observed.

Other differences: The positions of A05 and A14 shifted slightly earlier in the trace, and the positions of A30 and A36 became more variable, but these are consistent with the loop and do not form a separate hypothesis [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004].
