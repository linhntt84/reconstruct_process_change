### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A20, A21, A22, A25, A26, A27, A28, A29, and A30 each had many outgoing transitions (e.g., A20 had 12 successors, A25 had 21). In the later window, each has only a few: A20 → A21/A22/A25, A21 → A20/A22/A25, A22 → A20/A21/A25, A25 → A26 (about 98% of transitions), A26 → A28/A29/A30, A27 → A36 (about 77%), A28 → A27/A29/A30, A29 → A27/A28/A30, A30 → A27/A28/A29. [DIR001–DIR010]
- The order between two groups of activities became fixed. In the earlier window, pairs such as (A20,A26), (A20,A28), (A21,A26), (A22,A26), (A25,A26) had mixed order (e.g., A20 before A26 in about 23% of cases, A26 before A20 in about 74%). In the later window, A20 always precedes A26 (99.5%), and similarly for all pairs between the first group (A20, A21, A22, A24, A25) and the second group (A26, A27, A28, A29, A30). [REL001–REL022]
- Positions shifted: A20, A21, A22, A24, and A25 moved earlier (mean position decreased by about 0.03–0.10), while A26, A27, A28, A29, and A30 moved later (mean increased by about 0.03–0.10). [POS001–POS010]
- Lags between the two groups increased: for pairs like (A20,A26), (A20,A28), (A21,A26), (A22,A26), (A25,A26), the lag distribution shifted from mostly 1–2 steps to 3–5 steps. Within the first group (e.g., A20–A21, A20–A22, A21–A22), lags became shorter (mostly 1–2 steps). [LAG001–LAG054]
- A35 now leads to A24 almost exclusively (99.5% after vs 45.5% before), and A25 now leads to A26 almost exclusively (98% after vs 3% before). [DIR010, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process was restructured so that a set of activities (A20, A21, A22, A24, A25) now always occur before another set (A26, A27, A28, A29, A30), with a fixed order between the two groups, whereas before the order was mixed or reversed. This is directly shown by the relational items (all pairs between the two groups changed from mixed order to a single fixed order) and is consistent with the positional shifts and the increased lags between the groups. The routing of A25 and A35 also became more deterministic (A25→A26, A35→A24). The number of evidence items (100) far exceeds the 1.01 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact.

Other differences: The outgoing transitions of many activities were reduced to a small set, indicating a simplification of the control flow. A27 now goes to A36 more often (77% vs 42%). These are separate from the main ordering hypothesis.

Limitations: Some lag items involving A25 with A26, A27, A28, A29, and A30 have very low support in the later window (1–3 traces), so those specific lag estimates are weak. However, the main ordering change is supported by many high-support relational and positional items.
