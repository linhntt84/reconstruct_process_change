### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the process: a set of activities (A19, A22, A23, A24, A25) that was present in a substantial share of earlier traces is no longer observed, while a new set of activities (A20, A21, A30, A31, A32) appears in every later trace, with A04 becoming a common predecessor to this new set. The number of qualifying control-flow items (130) far exceeds the expected 1.06 under no change, and the relational and positional evidence directly shows the replacement of the old activities by the new ones and the new ordering of A04 before them. A competing explanation that the old activities still exist but were not sampled is unlikely given their previous frequencies and the complete absence in 200 later traces. Other differences include A33 shifting later and A48 now immediately preceding A04, which are secondary to the main structural change.
