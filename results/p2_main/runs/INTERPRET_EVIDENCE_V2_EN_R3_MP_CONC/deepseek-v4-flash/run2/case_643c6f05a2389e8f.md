### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A21–A25 each appeared in about 85% of traces; in the later window they appear in about 19% of traces. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- In the earlier window, activities A26–A30 each appeared in about 16% of traces; in the later window they appear in about 82% of traces. [OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process switched from executing a set of activities A21–A25 to a set A26–A30, with A20 as the branching point that now routes to A29 instead of A24. This is directly shown by the mirror-image participation changes of the two activity groups and the shift in A20's outgoing transitions. [OCC002–OCC011, DIR003] The change is consistent across all five activities in each group and far exceeds what chance would produce (120 qualified items vs 1.27 expected). [screening]

Other differences: A new path through A02 emerged: A02 increased in participation and now leads to A37, and A36 now sometimes leads to A02, so a chain A36→A02→A37 appears in the later window. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR005, LAG001, LAG006] This is separate from the branch switch.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two windows represent different case types rather than a temporal change, but the magnitude and consistency of the shift make that unlikely.
