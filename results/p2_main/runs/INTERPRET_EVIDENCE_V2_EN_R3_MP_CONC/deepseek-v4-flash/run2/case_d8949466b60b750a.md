### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from having two alternative branches after A26 – one through A27 to A28, and another through A32/A33/A38 (in varying order) to A28 – to a single branch A26→A27→A28, with A32/A33/A38 no longer executed. This is directly shown by the near-disappearance of A32/A33/A38, the shift of A26's outgoing transitions to A27, and the corresponding changes in co-occurrence and lag patterns. The large number of qualifying items (35 vs 0.6 expected) rules out chance as the overall explanation. A competing explanation that the alternative branch still exists but was not sampled is unlikely given the drop from ~49% to 0.5% in 200 traces, but cannot be entirely excluded.

Other differences: A28's position shifted slightly earlier in the trace, which is consistent with the removal of the longer alternative branch, but is a minor effect. [POS001]
