### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a single mandatory sequence to a process with optional branches and altered routing. Specifically, activities A22, A31, A32, and A37 became optional, appearing in about half the traces instead of all; A20 now leads directly to A23 instead of through A31/A32/A37; and A21 now branches to A22 or to A31/A32/A37, which then lead to A26 instead of A23. The order between A21 and A31/A32/A37 reversed. This is supported by a large number of control-flow indicators (47 items vs 0.51 expected by chance) that consistently show these changes. A competing explanation that the differences are due to random sampling is unlikely given the magnitude of the evidence, though the exact mechanism (e.g., whether activities were added/removed or just skipped) cannot be fully determined from these indicators. The evidence also cannot rule out that the observed differences arise from a mixture of two stable process variants rather than a single changed process.

Other differences: The time between A23 and A24 doubled from about 1.0 to 1.9 hours, indicating a separate timing change. [TWAIT001]
