### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which A09 becomes a more central, repeated activity, while A03 and A14 become less frequent and non-repeating, and the ordering between A11 and A03/A14 becomes fixed rather than interleaved. Specifically, A13 and A15 now feed into A09 instead of into each other or A01, and A09 itself repeats more often. At the same time, A11 now precedes A03 and A14 in a consistent order, and A03/A14 no longer repeat. The large number of control-flow items (29 vs. 0.43 expected by chance) and the consistency across occurrence, direction, position, relation, and lag indicators make a real change likely. A competing explanation is that the observed differences reflect a shift in case mix or a different variant becoming dominant, but the breadth and coherence of the evidence argue against pure sampling variation. Other differences not part of the main hypothesis: A06 now goes almost exclusively to A01, and A14's successor shifts from A11 to A12.
