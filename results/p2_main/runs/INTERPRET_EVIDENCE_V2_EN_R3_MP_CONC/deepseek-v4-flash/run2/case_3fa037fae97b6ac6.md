### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity A01 was added early in the process, executed in all traces, and placed before many existing activities (A04, A05, A02, A12, A06) with some flexibility in order. This is directly shown by the appearance of A01 (OCC001), its new incoming and outgoing transitions (DIR001-004), its co-occurrence with other activities (REL001-005), and its early position (POS001). The increased lags between existing activities (LAG001-010) are consistent with an added step that pushes some activities further apart. A competing explanation that A01 was a rare variant in the earlier window is ruled out by its 100% participation in the later window and the large number of qualifying items (23 vs 0.23 expected by chance). A limitation is that the exact placement of A01 is not fully fixed, as it appears both before and after A02 and A12 in similar proportions.

Other differences: A05's position shifted later (POS002), and A12's position shifted slightly later (POS003), which are consistent with the insertion of A01 but could also reflect other changes.
