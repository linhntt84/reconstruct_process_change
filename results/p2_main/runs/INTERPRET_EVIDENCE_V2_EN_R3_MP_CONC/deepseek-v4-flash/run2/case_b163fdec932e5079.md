### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a previously mandatory activity, A27, became optional and was replaced in about 44% of traces by a new block of activities (A32, A33, A38, A39, A40) that occur together in no fixed order, with both branches converging on A28. This is directly shown by the drop in A27 participation from 100% to 57%, the appearance of the new activities from 0% to 44%, the mutual exclusivity between A27 and the new block, and the routing change at A26 from A27 to the new block. The large number of qualifying items (72 vs. 0.83 expected by chance) rules out random sampling as an explanation.

Other differences: A06 became less frequent and shifted later in the trace; A10 became more frequent. These are not part of the main hypothesis. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities represent a rare variant that happened to be sampled in the later window, but the consistency and volume of evidence make this unlikely.
