### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured by removing activities A02, A03, and A08, and rerouting the flow so that A10 now leads to A01, A01 leads to A11, and A16 leads directly to A06/A07/A14, bypassing the removed activities. The large number of qualifying items (129 vs 0.85 expected) makes chance an unlikely explanation. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window simply does not contain cases that use those activities, but the systematic rerouting of all related edges makes that less plausible.

Other differences: A07 and A14 become less frequent and shift to the very end of the trace (POS004, POS006). Many LAG items show reordering among remaining activities, but these are consistent with the main rerouting.
