### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process likely changed from a choice among alternative middle activities (A14, A17–A20) to a fixed set of middle activities (A15, A16, A25–A27) that are all executed, with the same start (A43) and end (A28). This is supported directly by the complete replacement of the activity set, the change in A43's outgoing transitions, and the co-occurrence patterns: the old activities were mutually exclusive, while the new ones always appear together. The evidence is consistent across many indicators and far exceeds the number expected by chance. A competing explanation that the old activities simply became rare is ruled out by their total absence in the later window and the new activities' presence in every trace.
