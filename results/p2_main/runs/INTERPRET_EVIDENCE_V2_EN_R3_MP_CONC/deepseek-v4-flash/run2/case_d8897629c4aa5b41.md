### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A14, A15, and A20 appear in about 29%, 41%, and 30% of traces respectively, whereas they were completely absent in the earlier window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- These new activities occur around the middle of the trace (mean position about 0.51) and never co-occur with each other; they are alternatives. [POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL006]
- In the earlier window, A22 always led to A23 and A23 always led to A24. In the later window, A22 leads to A23 (44%), A15 (24%), A20 (17%), A14 (16%); A23 leads to A24 (57%), A15 (18%), A14 (14%), A20 (13%). [DIR004, DIR005]
- The new activities lead to A23 and A24 in roughly equal shares. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The distance between A22 and A24 increased: before always 2 steps, after almost always 3 steps. The distance between A22 and A23 increased from always 1 step to 44% 1 step and 56% 2 steps; similarly A23 to A24 from always 1 step to 57% 1 step and 44% 2 steps. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the process introduced new activities A14, A15, and A20, which are executed as alternatives in about 30–40% of traces, inserted between A22 and A24. The new activities appear in the middle of the trace, never co-occur, and are connected to the existing activities: A22 and A23 now have outgoing edges to them, and they lead to A23 and A24. The increased lags between A22–A24, A22–A23, and A23–A24 are consistent with an extra step being inserted. The evidence is extensive (27 items vs. 0.4 expected by chance) and directly shows the appearance of these activities and their placement. A competing explanation that the new activities are simply rare variants is ruled out by their substantial participation and consistent relational evidence. A limitation is that the new activities are not present in all traces, so they are optional or conditional, and the exact routing logic (e.g., why some traces include them) is not observable.

Other differences: A22's position shifted slightly earlier (mean from 0.479 to 0.459), and the order between the new activities and A23 is mixed (both A_before_B and B_before_A occur), while with A22 and A24 the order is fixed (A22 before new, new before A24). [POS004, REL004, REL008, REL011]
