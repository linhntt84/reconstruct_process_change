### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A30, A31, and A36 each occur on average about 1.05 times per case instead of about 3.45 times per case; they still appear in every case, but the share of cases with more than one occurrence dropped from about 75% to about 1–2%. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the later window, A30 and A36 always co-occur, but the order became fixed: A30 appears before A36 in about 98% of cases, whereas before only about 25% had that order and about 75% were interleaved or repeated. [REL001]
- The distance between A30 and A36 (when A30 precedes A36) became concentrated at lag 2 (about 89% of cases) instead of being spread over lags 2–5. [LAG001]
- The main successor of A36 changed: before, about 70% of its outgoing transitions went to A30 and 28% to A25; after, about 92% go to A25 and 4% to A30. [DIR002]
- The position of A30 and A36 within the trace became more consistent (standard deviation roughly halved), though their average positions changed little. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activities A30, A31, and A36 changed from being repeated multiple times per case to occurring once per case, which also fixed the order between A30 and A36 (A30 before A36). In the earlier window, these activities occurred on average about 3.5 times per case and were interleaved in about 75% of cases; in the later window they occur about once per case and A30 precedes A36 in about 98% of cases. The change is directly shown by the occurrence counts, the ordering relation, and the lag distribution. A competing explanation that the activities were removed or became optional is ruled out because they still appear in every case. The main limitation is that the routing change for A36 (its successor shifting from A30 to A25) is a separate difference not explained by the repetition change.

Other differences: The successor of A36 changed from predominantly A30 (70% of outgoing transitions) to predominantly A25 (92% of outgoing transitions). This is a separate change in control flow routing.
