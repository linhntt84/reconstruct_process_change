### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, about half of the traces contained A27, and each of A32, A33, A38, A39, and A40 also appeared in about half of the traces. In the later window, A27 appears in about 99% of traces, while each of those five activities appears in only about 1–2% of traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- A26 and A28 appear in all traces in both windows. [POS001, POS002]
- The co-occurrence of A26 with A27 rose from about 50% to about 99% of traces, while co-occurrence of A26 with each of A32, A33, A38, A39, and A40 fell from about 50% to about 1–2%. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Similarly, co-occurrence of A28 with A27 rose from about 50% to about 99%, while co-occurrence of A28 with each of the five activities fell from about 50% to about 1–2%. [REL007, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017]
- Before, A27 and the group A32–A40 were mutually exclusive: A27 co-occurred with each of them in only about 1% of traces. After, A27 is almost always present and the group almost never, so the mutual exclusivity is no longer relevant. [REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- The outgoing transitions of A26 shifted: before, A26 went to A27 in about 48% of its transitions and to A32, A33, A38, A39, and A40 in about 10% each; after, A26 goes to A27 in about 96% of transitions. [DIR001]
- The position of A26 shifted slightly later (mean from 0.46 to 0.49 of the trace), and A28 slightly earlier (mean from 0.58 to 0.56). [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process changed from having two alternative branches after the always-present activities A26 and A28: one branch containing A27, the other containing a group of activities A32, A33, A38, A39, and A40. In the later window, the A27 branch is taken in almost all cases, and the other branch is almost never taken. This is supported by the large increase in A27 participation and the corresponding decrease in the group's participation, the shift in A26's outgoing transitions to A27, and the co-occurrence patterns. The screening comparison shows 63 qualified items against 0.8 expected by chance, so the overall evidence is far beyond what a stable process would typically produce.

Limitations: The activities that disappeared have very low support in the later window (3–5 traces), so their exact behaviour after the change is uncertain, but the main shift is clear. The evidence cannot rule out that the group still exists but is extremely rare, or that it was replaced by something not captured.

Other differences: The slight position shifts of A26 and A28 are minor and may be a consequence of the changed branch structure. No other differences.
