### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: A04, which was present in about 59% of traces and typically occurred before a set of activities, becomes much rarer (about 26% of traces), while the activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38, A39, which were previously each present in about half the traces and often executed as alternatives, become almost always executed together (about 98–99% co-occurrence). The ordering also shifts: A33, A35, A36 occur earlier relative to A37, A38, A39, and the distance between A30 and the later activities increases, consistent with more activities being inserted between them. [OCC002, OCC005-010, REL001-012, REL013-036, REL037-059, LAG005-008, LAG011, LAG013, POS001, POS005-010]

Other differences: A14’s outgoing transitions shift from mostly A04 (57%) to mostly A15 (79%), and A30’s successors shift from A37/A38/A39 to A35/A33/A36. [DIR004, DIR005] These are consistent with the main reordering but are not part of the main hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two windows represent different case types or populations rather than a temporal change in the same process, but the magnitude and consistency of the differences across many indicators make a genuine behavioural change the more plausible interpretation.
