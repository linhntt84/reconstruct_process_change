### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the timing between activities A11/A12 and A13 increased in the later window. The mean and median wait times roughly doubled for both pairs, and the distributions shifted upward (quartiles also increased). The two time items are consistent and far exceed the number expected by chance (2 items vs 0.006 expected for the time family). The main limitation is that the counts of observed intervals are moderate (63/50 and 56/41), but the consistency and magnitude support the change.

Other differences: The control flow after A22 changed, with the next activity shifting from A25 to A21. This is a separate structural change, supported by one item (DIR001) with high empirical strength, though the expected count for control flow was 0.27, so this single item is less conclusive on its own. A competing explanation for the timing change could be a general slowdown in the process rather than a specific change between these activities, but the evidence does not allow distinguishing that.
