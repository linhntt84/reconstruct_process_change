### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activity A20 led to a set of five activities (A30, A31, A36, A37, A38) with roughly equal shares (each about 20%); in the later window, A20 leads almost exclusively to A22 (about 99.5% of its outgoing transitions) [DIR001].
- In the earlier window, A21 led exclusively to A25 (100%); in the later window, A21 leads to the same set of five activities (A30, A31, A36, A37, A38) with roughly equal shares (each about 20%) and almost never to A25 (0.5%) [DIR002].
- For each of A30, A31, A36, A37, A38, the share of outgoing transitions to A22 dropped from about 16–22% to near zero, while a new edge to A25 appeared with about 18–23% in the later window [DIR003–DIR007].
- The relative order between the two groups reversed: in the earlier window, A30/A31/A36/A37/A38 occurred before A21 and A22 in essentially all traces (100%); in the later window, A21 and A22 occur before that group in about 99.5% of traces [REL001–REL010].
- Positions shifted accordingly: A21 and A22 moved earlier (mean position decreased by about 0.13), while A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 moved later (mean position increased by about 0.16) [POS001–POS007].
- Lag evidence is based on only one trace in the later window for each pair, so it is not reliable; it is consistent with the order reversal but not used further [LAG001–LAG010].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which the block of activities A30, A31, A36, A37, A38, previously occurring before A21 and A22 and receiving control from A20, now occurs after A21 and A22, receives control from A21, and passes control to A25 instead of to A22. This is directly shown by the routing changes (DIR), the order reversal (REL), and the position shifts (POS), all based on 200 traces per window. The number of qualifying items (34) far exceeds the 0.55 expected by chance, and the indicators are mutually consistent. A competing explanation of a rare variant is unlikely given the scale and consistency of the evidence, though the lag items, based on a single trace in the later window, do not add independent confirmation.

Other differences: None beyond the main hypothesis.
