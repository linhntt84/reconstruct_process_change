### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the relative order of two groups of activities reversed between the windows. In the earlier window, activities A14, A18, A21, A22, and A23 occurred early and were followed by A24, while A15, A16, A17, A19, and A20 occurred later; in the later window the latter group occurs early and the former group occurs late, and the order between them and A24 is reversed. This is supported by consistent positional shifts, direct order reversals in relational evidence, and successor changes in directional evidence. The evidence cannot rule out that the change is a re-labelling of activities or a swap of two process blocks, but the behavioural difference is clear. [POS001–POS010, REL001–REL045, DIR001–DIR012]
