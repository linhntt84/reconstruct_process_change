### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: the set of activities executed between A48 and A33 changed from a choice among alternatives (A19, A22, A23, A24, A25) to a block of activities (A20, A21, A30, A31, A32) that are all executed together, with no fixed order among them. This is supported by the near-complete replacement of participation, the change in A48's successors, and the co-occurrence patterns. The main limitation is that the evidence does not reveal the exact control flow among the new activities (e.g., parallel vs. sequential), but that does not affect the main change.

Other differences: The position of A33 shifted later and A48 shifted earlier, consistent with the insertion of a longer block.
