### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process was restructured: A08, previously a common early activity, became much less frequent and moved later; and a group of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) that previously followed A28 now precede it, while A47 now leads to that group instead of to A28. This is supported by the large drop in A08 participation and its changed successors, the reversal of order between A28 and those activities, and the change in A47's outgoing transitions. The number of qualifying items (74 vs <1 expected) makes chance an unlikely explanation.

Other differences: A30 now almost always leads to A34, and A29 now almost always leads to A30, forming a new dominant chain. Also A02, A04, A10, A17 increased in participation. The evidence cannot rule out that some of these changes are due to sampling variation, but the overall pattern is consistent.
