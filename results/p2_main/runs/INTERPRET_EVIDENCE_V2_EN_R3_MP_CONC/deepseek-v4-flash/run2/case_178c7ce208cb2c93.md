### Observed differences

- In the later window, activity A01 appears in every trace (200/200) with a mean of 1 occurrence per trace, whereas it was absent in the earlier window (0/200). [OCC001, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity A01 was introduced and inserted between the existing activities A02/A04 and A03. In the earlier window, A02 and A04 led directly to A03 (with some A02–A04 alternation); in the later window, A02 and A04 lead to A01, and A01 always leads to A03, so A01 now sits between them. This is directly shown by the appearance of A01 in all traces, the new outgoing edge A01→A03, the shift of A02's and A04's outgoing edges from A03 to A01, the co-occurrence and ordering relations, and the increased lag between A02/A04 and A03. The evidence is consistent across all indicators and the number of qualifying items (11) far exceeds the 0.06 expected by chance. A competing explanation that the process simply reordered existing activities is ruled out because A01 was not present at all in the earlier window.

Other differences: A02's position moved earlier, consistent with the insertion of A01 after it. No other differences are indicated.
