### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A20 was always followed by A22; in the later window A20 is followed by A30, A31 and A36 (each about a third of the time) and almost never by A22 (0.5%). [DIR001]
- In the earlier window, A21 was followed by A36, A30 and A31 (roughly 38%, 34%, 28%); in the later window A21 is almost always followed by A25 (99.5%). [DIR002]
- A30, A31 and A36 each previously had A25 as a successor (about 28–37%); in the later window A25 is almost absent as a successor of these activities, and A22 appears as a successor instead (about 31–34%). [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- A21 and A22 previously always occurred before A30, A31 and A36; in the later window they almost always occur after them (99.5% of cases). [REL001–REL006]
- The relative positions of A21 and A22 moved later (mean position from about 0.53 to 0.62 and from 0.38 to 0.47), while A30, A31 and A36 moved earlier (mean position from about 0.59 to 0.41). [POS001–POS005]
- The gap between A20 and A22 changed from 1 step to 4 steps, and the gap between A21 and A25 changed from 4 steps to 1 step. [LAG001, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: the block of activities A30/A31/A36 now occurs before A22 and A21, whereas previously A22 and A21 occurred before that block, and A25 now follows A21 instead of following A30/A31/A36. Concretely, the earlier order appears to have been A20 → A22 → A21 → A30/A31/A36 → A25, and the later order appears to be A20 → A30/A31/A36 → A22 → A21 → A25. This is directly shown by the successor changes (A20 no longer to A22, A21 to A25, A30/A31/A36 to A22 instead of A25), the complete reversal of the order between A21/A22 and A30/A31/A36, the consistent position shifts, and the lag changes for A20–A22 and A21–A25, all with full support in both windows. The main limitation is that several lag items (LAG003–LAG008) rest on a single trace in the later window, but the core ordering evidence does not depend on them. A competing explanation that the process simply switched between two existing variants is unlikely given the magnitude and consistency of the changes, but cannot be fully excluded.
