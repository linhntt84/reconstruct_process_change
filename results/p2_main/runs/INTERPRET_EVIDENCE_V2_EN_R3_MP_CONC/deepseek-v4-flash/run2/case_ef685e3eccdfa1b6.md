### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A28, A30, A31, A32, A33 and A34 each occurred in every trace (100% participation). In the later window, A28, A30 and A31 occur in about 46.5% of traces, and A32, A33 and A34 in about 55.5% of traces. [OCC001–OCC006]
- In the earlier window, A25 always preceded all six activities, and all six always preceded A26. In the later window, A28–A31 and A32–A34 are almost never observed together: A28 with A32, A33 or A34 cooccur in only about 2% of traces (down from 100%), and the same holds for A30 and A31 with A32–A34. [REL015–REL017, REL019–REL021, REL022–REL024]
- New direct transitions appear: A25 now leads to A32, A33 and A34 (previously only to A28, A30 and A31). [DIR001]
- A28, A30 and A31 now lead to A26 in about 26–35% of their outgoing transitions, a successor not observed before. [DIR002–DIR004]
- The lag between A25 and A32–A34 decreased from mostly 4–5 steps to mostly 1–3 steps; similarly, the lag between A28/A30/A31 and A26 decreased from mostly 4–5 steps to mostly 1–3 steps. [LAG001–LAG003, LAG004, LAG008, LAG011]
- A25 moved slightly later in the trace (mean position from 0.62 to 0.67), A26 moved slightly earlier (from 0.83 to 0.81), and A28–A31 moved later while A32–A34 moved earlier, though these position changes are based on reduced support in the later window. [POS001–POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a single path through a block of six activities to two alternative branches: one branch contains A28, A30 and A31, the other contains A32, A33 and A34, and both branches are still preceded by A25 and followed by A26. This is directly shown by the drop in participation of the six activities to about half, the near-total mutual exclusivity between the two groups, the new edges from A25 to the second group and from the first group to A26, and the shortened lags between A25 and the second group and between the first group and A26. The large number of qualifying items (58 vs. 0.63 expected by chance) rules out sampling variation as the explanation. A competing explanation that cannot be fully excluded is that the two branches are not strictly exclusive in the underlying process but the sample happened to show almost no cooccurrence; however, the consistency across many indicators makes this unlikely.

Other differences: A25 and A26 shifted slightly in position (A25 later, A26 earlier), and the order within the first group (A28–A31) remains mixed in both windows, so no fixed ordering constraint changed there. [POS001–POS002, REL013–REL014, REL018]
