### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering of activities: a group consisting of A14, A16, and A19, which previously occurred after a core group (A15, A17, A18, A20, A24), now occurs before that core group. This is directly shown by the reversal of order in many activity pairs (e.g., A15–A20, A14–A15, A16–A17) and by the positional shifts of the involved activities. A14 also became more frequent. The successor changes (A20 vs. A25) and the changes in A24 and A35 successors are consistent with this reordering.

Other differences: A20 and A25 swapped roles as successors for the two groups, and A24/A35 successor changes align with the reordering. The evidence is entirely control-flow; no timing or resource changes are observed. A competing explanation that the reversal is a sampling artefact is ruled out by the large number of qualifying items (40 vs. 0.46 expected by chance) and the consistency across many independent pairs.
