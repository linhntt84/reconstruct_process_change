### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: A06 is now executed between A14 and A15, and A29 now precedes a group of activities (A20, A21, A22, A24, A25) that previously followed it, with A34 being replaced by A29 as a common successor for several activities (A19, A23, A26, A27, A28). This is directly shown by the successor changes [DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, DIR009, DIR012-014] and the order flips in REL items [REL019, REL023, REL027, REL034, REL038]. The large number of qualifying items (105 vs 1.15 expected) makes it very unlikely that these differences are due to chance. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the case mix changed, but the direct successor and order changes are strong evidence of a structural reordering.
