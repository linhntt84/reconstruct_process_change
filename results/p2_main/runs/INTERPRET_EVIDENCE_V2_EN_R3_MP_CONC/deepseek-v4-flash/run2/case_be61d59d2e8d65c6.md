### Observed differences

- Activities A19, A20, and A25 were present in about 33%, 29%, and 41% of traces in the earlier window, but in the later window each appears in only about 0.5% of traces (1 trace each). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The outgoing transitions of A27 and A28 became much more concentrated: A27 went to A28 in about 52% of its transitions before and about 98% after; A28 went to A29 in about 43% before and about 97% after. [DIR005, DIR006]
- Co-occurrence of A19, A20, and A25 with A27, A28, and A29 dropped from about 28–41% of traces to about 0.5% of traces. [REL003–REL012]
- The distance between A27 and A28 shrank: before, about 52% of pairs were adjacent and 45% had one step between; after, about 98% are adjacent. Similarly, A28–A29: before about 43% adjacent and 52% one step between; after about 96% adjacent. A27–A29: before about 91% had two steps between (lag 3), after about 96% have one step between (lag 2). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed so that activities A19, A20, and A25 are no longer executed (or become extremely rare), and the path A27 → A28 → A29 becomes the dominant direct sequence, replacing a longer path that included A19/A20/A25 between A27 and A28 or between A28 and A29. This is supported by the near-disappearance of A19, A20, and A25, the concentration of A27's successors on A28 and A28's successors on A29, the reduction in lag between A27 and A28 and between A28 and A29, and the drop in co-occurrence of A19/A20/A25 with A27/A28/A29. A limitation is that the DIR and POS evidence for A19, A20, and A25 after the change is based on a single trace, so the exact new successors of these activities are not reliable; we also cannot rule out that these activities still exist but are extremely rare.

Other differences: The small shifts in position of A27 and A29 (POS004, POS005) are minor and consistent with the structural change.
