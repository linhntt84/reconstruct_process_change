- In the later window, activities A22, A31, A32, and A37 appear in almost every trace (about 100% vs 54% for A22 and 46% for the others before). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a branching structure to a sequential one: in the earlier window, either A22 or A31/A32/A37 were executed (with A23 and A21 before them); in the later window, A31/A32/A37 are always executed before A23, A21, A22, and A26. This is directly shown by the increased participation of A22, A31, A32, and A37, the shift of A20's successor from A23 to A31/A32/A37, the reversal of order between A23 and A31/A32/A37, and the change of A22 and A31/A32/A37 from mutually exclusive to co-occurring. The screening count (41 items vs 0.49 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely.

Other differences: A21's successor became exclusively A22, and A26 is no longer a successor of A31/A32/A37.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a re-labelling of activities rather than a true process change, but the consistent relational and positional evidence makes this less plausible.
