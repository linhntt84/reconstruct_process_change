### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A08, previously a central activity present in about 60% of traces and typically followed by A50, became much rarer (about 20% of traces) and no longer a common predecessor of A50. In the later window, a group of activities A33–A43 (together with A14, A15, A30, A31) became almost always present and are executed together in a fixed order: A14, A15, and A30 precede A33–A43, which are followed by A31, then A49 and A50, then A51 and A52. This is directly supported by the large increases in co-occurrence among these activities, the directional relations (A14/A15/A30 before A33–A43; A33–A43 before A31; A33–A43 before A49/A50), and the position shifts. The number of qualifying evidence items (219) vastly exceeds the 2.05 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that some of the lag changes are based on very few observations (e.g., support of 1 or 2 in the later window), but the relational and occurrence evidence is consistent and strong.

Other differences: A04, A06, and A10 increased in participation, and A08’s successor set broadened from A50 to a variety of activities. These are secondary to the main structural shift.
