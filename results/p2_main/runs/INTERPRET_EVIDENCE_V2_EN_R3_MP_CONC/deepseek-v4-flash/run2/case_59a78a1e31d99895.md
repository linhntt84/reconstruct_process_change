### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control flow: activities A04, A06, and A10 are executed much less often (from about half of cases to about a fifth), and when they are executed they are now always followed by A08, whereas before they were not associated with A08. A02 no longer directly precedes these activities; its successors shifted to A05, A03, A09, and A01. This suggests that a branch that used to include A04/A06/A10 after A02 is now less common and rerouted through A08. The large number of evidence items (45 vs 0.34 expected by chance) and the consistency across occurrence, direction, position, relation, and lag indicators support this. A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the decrease in A04/A06/A10 reflects a change in case mix rather than a process modification, but the direct routing changes (A02's successors, A04/A06/A10 leading to A08) point to a structural change.

Other differences: A03 and A09 also changed their successors: they no longer lead to A08, but now lead to A04/A06/A10. A08's position moved later, and A02's position moved later. These are consistent with the main hypothesis.
