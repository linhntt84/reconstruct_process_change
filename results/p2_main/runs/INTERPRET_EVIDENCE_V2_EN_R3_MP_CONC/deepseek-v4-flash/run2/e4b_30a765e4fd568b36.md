### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation for activities A03, A13, A14, and A22. In the later window, assignments became more concentrated on a set of specific resources (e.g., User_67, User_79, User_104, User_42, User_28, User_58) and less on a broad "OTHER" group. This is supported by four resource items, far exceeding the expected number by chance (4 vs 0.005), and the pattern is consistent across the four activities.

Other differences: The elapsed time between A22 and A03 increased, as described above. This is supported by one time item, also far exceeding chance (1 vs 0.017).

The evidence cannot rule out that the resource change reflects a change in the mix of cases rather than a direct change in assignment behaviour, and the timing change might be related to the resource change, but the evidence does not allow separating these.
