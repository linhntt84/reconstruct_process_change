### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 appeared in every trace and were repeated on average about 3.5 times per trace, with about 70% of traces containing more than one occurrence. In the later window they still appear in every trace but on average about once per trace, and recurrence dropped to about 1–2%. [OCC006–OCC010]
- A10 appeared in about 21% of traces earlier and in about 71.5% later; its mean occurrences per trace rose from 0.225 to 0.775. [OCC005]
- A10's outgoing transitions changed from being spread over many targets (A47, A16, A15, etc.) to being concentrated on A19 and A20 (about 44% each). [DIR004]
- A38's outgoing transitions changed from mostly A30 (70%) and A25 (27%) to mostly A25 (97.5%) and A30 (2%). [DIR006]
- A41's outgoing transitions changed from mostly A19 (44%) and A20 (32.5%) to mostly A10 (69.6%). [DIR007]
- In the earlier window, A10 co-occurred with A14, A15, A19, A20, A30, A31, A36, A37, A38, A41, A46, A47 in about 21% of traces (or less for A19/A20); in the later window it co-occurs with them in about 71.5% of traces. [REL001, REL002, REL005–REL012]
- The order between A10 and the block changed: in the later window A10 appears after A14 and A41 (about 64% of traces) and before A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 (about 65% of traces). [REL001, REL010, REL005–REL009]
- The block A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 used to be interleaved or repeated in about 70% of traces; in the later window they appear in a fixed order with almost no interleaving (1–2%). [REL013–REL017, REL033–REL036, REL040–REL042, REL046–REL047, REL051, REL056–REL057]
- LAG: A10 immediately precedes A19 and A20 (lag 1 in about 97–100% of co-occurring traces). [LAG001, LAG002]
- A14 occurs about 5 steps before A10, A15 about 4 steps before A10, and A41 immediately before A10 (lag 1). [LAG005, LAG008, LAG023]
- Activities A02, A04, A06, A08 decreased in participation: e.g., A08 from 31% to 8.5%, A06 from 24% to 5%, A04 from 23% to 6%, A02 from 24.5% to 10.5%. [OCC001–OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed from a repeated/interleaved execution of a block of activities (A30, A31, A36, A37, A38) to a single sequential execution, and that A10 became a more central activity that now appears in most traces, positioned after A14/A41 and before A19/A20 and the block. The recurrence drop for the block, the shift from interleaving to fixed order, the increase in A10 participation, and the LAG evidence showing A10 immediately before A19/A20 and A41 immediately before A10 are consistent and direct. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the decrease in A02, A04, A06, A08 reflects a separate change in case mix, but this does not undermine the main hypothesis.

Other differences: A02, A04, A06, A08 decreased in participation, which may indicate that these activities are now less frequently executed, but they are not part of the main hypothesis.
