### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activity A03 never occurred; in the later window it occurs in every trace (about 100 in 100 cases) [OCC001].
- In the earlier window, A07 had outgoing transitions to A02 (about 33%), A06 (about 32%), and A10 (about 36%). In the later window, A07's only outgoing transition is to A03 (100%), and A03 in turn has outgoing transitions to A02 (about 39%), A06 (about 33%), and A10 (about 29%) [DIR001, DIR002].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A03, was inserted between A07 and the group A02/A06/A10. In the earlier window, A07 directly led to A02, A06, and A10; in the later window, A07 leads to A03, which then leads to those three activities, preserving the same routing shares. This is directly shown by the appearance of A03 in all traces, the change in A07's outgoing transitions, the co-occurrence and ordering relations, and the increased lags. The evidence is consistent across all control-flow indicators. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A03 is a renamed or split version of part of the previous path, but the data do not support that distinction; the observed change is best described as an added activity.

Other differences: The relative positions of A02, A06, and A10 shifted later, and A07 slightly earlier, which is a direct consequence of the insertion. No other differences are indicated.
