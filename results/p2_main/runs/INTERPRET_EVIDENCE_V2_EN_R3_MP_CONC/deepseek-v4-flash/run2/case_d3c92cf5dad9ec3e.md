### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the relative order of two groups of activities reversed: in the earlier window, A19, A21, and A24 typically occurred before A20, A22, and A23; in the later window, A20, A22, and A23 typically occur before A19, A21, and A24. This is directly shown by position shifts and pairwise order reversals across many pairs, with full support in both windows for the relational items. The number of qualifying items (46) far exceeds the 0.69 expected by chance, making a random sampling explanation unlikely.

Other differences: The successor of many activities changed from A25 to A30 and vice versa, and A29 and A40 changed their successor sets. These may be related to the reordering but are not part of the main hypothesis. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the process underwent a more complex restructuring involving the introduction or repositioning of A30 as a new hub, rather than a simple swap of two activity groups.
