### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A14, A15, A20, A21, and A22 are absent from all traces; in the earlier window each appeared in about 17–25% of traces (mean occurrences per trace 0.165–0.245). [OCC001–OCC005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process was simplified by removing a set of intermediate activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) that previously lay between A38 and A23. In the earlier window, A38 was followed by one of these five activities, which then led to A23; in the later window A38 goes directly to A23. This is directly shown by the disappearance of those activities, the change in A38's outgoing transitions, and the shift in the A38–A23 lag from two steps to one. The large number of qualifying items (32 vs. 0.42 expected by chance) rules out chance as an overall explanation. A competing explanation that cannot be fully excluded is that the five activities were not removed but simply not observed in the later window; however, their complete absence across all 200 traces, together with the routing and lag changes, makes this unlikely.

Other differences: A23's relative position shifted slightly earlier (mean from 0.397 to 0.378), consistent with the removal of preceding steps. [POS006]
