### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activity A01 was not observed in any of the 200 traces; in the later window it appears in all 200 traces, always at a late position (mean relative position about 0.83) and always followed by A03. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A01, was introduced and inserted between the existing activities A05/A07 and A03. In the earlier window, A05 and A07 could lead directly to A03; in the later window, they lead to A01, which then always leads to A03. This is directly shown by the appearance of A01 in every trace, its position before A03, the replacement of A03 by A01 as a successor of A05 and A07, and the increased lag between A05/A07 and A03. The evidence is consistent across 12 indicators, far exceeding the 0.06 expected by chance. A competing explanation that A01 existed but was unobserved in the earlier window is unlikely given its complete absence in 200 traces and its complete presence in the later window.

Other differences: A05 and A07 still have a choice between each other and A01, but this is part of the same insertion of A01. No other differences are indicated.
