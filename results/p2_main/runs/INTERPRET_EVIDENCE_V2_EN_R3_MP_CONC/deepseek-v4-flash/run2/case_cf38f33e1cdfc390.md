### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process replaced a group of activities (A16–A18) with another group (A19–A21) after A15. In the later window, A15 leads to A19–A21 instead of A16–A18, and these new activities are executed together, while the old ones are largely absent. The shift is consistent across occurrence, routing and co-occurrence indicators, and the number of qualifying items (35) far exceeds the 0.53 expected by chance for control-flow indicators.

Other differences: A25 now co-occurs with A19–A21 more often, and the lag between A15 and A20 shortened; these are consistent with the same replacement. The evidence is purely control-flow, so no timing or resource changes are observed. A competing explanation that the old activities became optional rather than removed cannot be fully ruled out, but the strong and coherent pattern makes a substitution the most plausible interpretation.
