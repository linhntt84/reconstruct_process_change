### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: a group of activities (A19, A23, A26, A27, A28) that previously occurred late in the trace now occurs early, while a group (A20, A21, A22, A24, A25) that previously occurred early now occurs late. The pattern is consistent across many items with high support (200 traces for most POS and REL items). The main limitation is that the evidence is purely structural; no timing or resource changes are observed, and the exact mechanism (e.g., a swap of two blocks) is inferred from the aggregate pattern rather than from a single trace-level view.
