### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a block of activities (A26, A27, A28) was replaced by a new block (A19, A20, A21) in the process. In the earlier window, A26–A28 were executed in nearly all traces and were followed by A29 and related activities; in the later window, these activities are almost absent, while A19–A21 appear in about a third of traces, are preceded by A11/A12, and are followed by A29. The successor changes of A44 (from A26–A28 to A19–A21) and the shift in A44–A29 lag are consistent with this substitution. The large number of qualifying control-flow items (81 vs. 0.89 expected by chance) makes it very unlikely that these differences are sampling noise.

Other differences: A10 participation increased, which is not part of the main substitution. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities are not a direct replacement but an additional optional branch, since they appear in only about a third of traces rather than in all of them.
