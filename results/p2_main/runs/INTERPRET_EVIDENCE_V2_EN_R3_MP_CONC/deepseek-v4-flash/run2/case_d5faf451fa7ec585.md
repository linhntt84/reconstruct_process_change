### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A14, A15 and A20 each occurred in a substantial share of traces (about 32%, 28% and 40% respectively); in the later window none of them occurs at all. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process lost a set of alternative activities — A14, A15 and A20 — that previously appeared between A22 and A23/A24, leaving a direct A22 → A23 → A24 sequence. The disappearance of these activities is consistent across occurrence, position, direct succession, co-occurrence and lag indicators, and the routing of A22 and A23 narrowed to single successors. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Other differences: The relative positions of A22 and A24 shifted slightly (A24 moved earlier relative to A22), which is consistent with the removal of intermediate activities. [POS004, POS005]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A14, A15 and A20 still exist in the process but were simply not executed in the later 200-trace sample; however, their complete absence across all indicators makes this unlikely.
