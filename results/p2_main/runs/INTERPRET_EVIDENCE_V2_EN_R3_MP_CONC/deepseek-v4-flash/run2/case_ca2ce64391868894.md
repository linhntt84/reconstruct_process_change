### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A01, A02 and A03 occurred in every trace (A01 and A02 once per trace, A03 on average about 2.1 times per trace). In the later window, none of these three activities appears in any trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- Activity A04 was absent in the earlier window and appears in every trace in the later window, always as the final event (position 1.0). [OCC004, POS004]
- The successor of A05 changed: in the earlier window A05 was always followed by A02; in the later window it is always followed by A04. [DIR003]
- The successors of A08 changed: in the earlier window A08 was always followed by A03; in the later window it is followed by A06 in about 46% of cases and by A07 in about 54%. [DIR004]
- A06 and A07 moved earlier in the trace: A06’s mean position fell from about 0.27 to 0.18, and A07’s from about 0.48 to 0.30. [POS005, POS006]
- A06 and A07, which previously co‑occurred with A01–A03, now co‑occur with A04 instead; for example, A04 and A06 went from never co‑occurring to co‑occurring in about half of traces. [REL020, REL022]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: activities A01, A02 and A03 were removed, and A04 was added as a new final activity. The remaining activities A05, A06, A07 and A08 were reconnected: A05 now leads to A04 instead of A02, and A08 now leads directly to A06/A07 instead of via A03. A06 and A07 also occur earlier in the trace, consistent with the removal of the A03‑centred segment. This is directly shown by the disappearance of A01–A03, the appearance of A04, and the successor changes in DIR003 and DIR004, together with the position shifts of A06 and A07. The evidence cannot rule out that the process was replaced by a different one rather than modified, but the continued presence of A05–A08 with changed connections makes a modification more plausible.

Other differences: The lag between A06 and A05 changed from 4 steps to 3 or 5 steps, and the self‑repetition lags of A06 and A08 shifted from 3 steps to 2 or 4 steps; these are consistent with the structural reorganisation. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]
