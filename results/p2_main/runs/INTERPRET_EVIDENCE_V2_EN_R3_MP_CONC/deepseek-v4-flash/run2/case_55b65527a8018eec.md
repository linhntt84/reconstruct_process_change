### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a single path in which activities A33–A43 were always executed to a process with two alternative branches: one branch containing A33, A34, A35, A37, and A38, and the other containing A39, A40, A41, and A43. In the later window, about half the cases execute one branch and half the other, and the two branches are mutually exclusive. A30 and A31 remain present in all cases. The position shift suggests the branch activities now occur later in the trace. The large number of qualifying items (93 vs 1.1 expected) rules out chance as an explanation.

Other differences: The position of A30 shifted later, and the positions of A33–A38 shifted later. The new outgoing edges to A31 from A33, A34, A37, and A38 are also observed, but they are not part of the main branch-splitting hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the activities became optional independently rather than as two coherent branches; however, the consistent mutual exclusivity across many activity pairs makes the branch-splitting interpretation more plausible.
