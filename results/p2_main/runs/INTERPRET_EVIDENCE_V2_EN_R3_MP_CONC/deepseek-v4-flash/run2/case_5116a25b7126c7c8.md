### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control-flow structure: a block of activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) moved from an early position in the process, before A23 and A24, to a later position after A23 and A24, and their outgoing routing changed from A23 to A25 or A29. In the earlier window these five activities appeared around position 0.3, were always followed by A23, and always preceded A23 and A24; in the later window they appear around position 0.6, are followed by A25 or A29, and always follow A23 and A24. [POS001-POS005, DIR001-DIR005, REL001-REL008] This reordering is also reflected in the lag changes: A24–A25, A24–A29, and A25–A29 distances increased, while A42–A23 distance decreased, consistent with A42 now leading directly to A23 instead of to the moved block. [LAG001-LAG004, DIR008] The number of qualifying items (28) far exceeds the 0.43 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. [screening.by_family.control_flow]

Other differences: A23, A24, and A25 shifted slightly earlier in the trace (mean positions decreased by about 0.02–0.04), which is consistent with the block moving past them. [POS006, POS007, POS008] A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed windows represent two distinct process variants rather than a single process that changed over time, but the consistency and volume of the evidence make this less plausible.
