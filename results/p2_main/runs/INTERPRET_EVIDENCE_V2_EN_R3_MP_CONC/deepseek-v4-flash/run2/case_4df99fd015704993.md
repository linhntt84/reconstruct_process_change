### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A14 is removed and A02 is introduced, with the routing re-wired: A12 now leads to A03 instead of A14, A03 now leads to A02 instead of A13, and A02 leads to A13. This is directly shown by the successor changes (DIR002, DIR003, DIR004), the co-occurrence and order of A02 with A03 and A13 (REL001, REL006), and the lag shifts (LAG002, LAG003). The disappearance of A14 is consistent across OCC, DIR, POS, and REL. A01 also appears as a new activity, but its position and relations are not evidenced, so its role is unclear. The decrease in participation of A05, A07, A10, and A11 is a separate difference, possibly reflecting that these activities became less frequent, but it is not part of the main routing change.

Other differences: A05, A07, A10, A11 participation drops from about 50% to about 34% of traces. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences are due to sampling variation, but the number of qualifying items (40) far exceeds the 0.32 expected by chance, and the pattern is internally consistent across multiple indicators.
