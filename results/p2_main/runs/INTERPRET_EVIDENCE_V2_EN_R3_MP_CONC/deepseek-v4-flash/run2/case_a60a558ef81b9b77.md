### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was re-sequenced: a group of activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) that previously occurred after A23 and A24 now occur before them, and the routing changed so that these activities now lead to A23 instead of to A25 and A29, while A42 now leads to this group instead of directly to A23. Additionally, A24 and A25 now form a tighter chain A24→A25→A29. This is directly shown by the outgoing transition changes (DIR001–DIR008), the position shifts (POS001–POS005), and the order relations (REL001–REL008, LAG007, LAG009, LAG010). The number of qualifying items (34) far exceeds the 0.48 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. A limitation is that some lag items (LAG003–LAG006) have very low support in the later window (only 1 trace), so those specific distances are not reliable, but the main re-sequencing is supported by items with full support.
