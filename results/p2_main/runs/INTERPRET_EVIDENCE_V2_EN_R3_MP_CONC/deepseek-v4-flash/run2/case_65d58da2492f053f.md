### Observed differences

- In the later window, the order between two groups of activities became fixed: A20, A21, A22, A24 and A25 now almost always occur before A26, A27, A28, A29 and A30. For example, A20 before A26 rose from 23% to 97.5% of traces, A25 before A27 from 49% to 99.5%, and A20 before A28 from 57.5% to 97.5%. [REL001, REL019, REL002]
- The positions of the first group moved earlier (mean position of A20 from 0.61 to 0.55, A25 from 0.70 to 0.60), while the second group moved later (A26 from 0.53 to 0.63, A28 from 0.62 to 0.68). [POS001, POS005, POS006, POS008]
- The distance between the two groups increased: lags of 4–5 steps became common for pairs like A20–A28 (from 33% to 81% of traces) and A20–A30 (from 35% to 87%). [LAG005, LAG007]
- Routing changed: A25 now goes to A26 in about 99.5% of its outgoing transitions (before it went to A36 45%, A27 18%, A30 13%, etc.). A26 now goes to A29, A28 and A30 (before also to A24, A20, A21, A22 and A25). A27 now goes to A36 in 83.5% of transitions (before 45%). A35 now goes to A24 in 100% of transitions (before 45.5% to A24 and 53.5% to A26). [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which a group of activities (A20, A21, A22, A24, A25) now consistently precedes another group (A26, A27, A28, A29, A30), whereas before their order was mixed, and the routing between them changed: A25 now leads to A26, A26 leads to A29/A28/A30, A27 leads to A36, and A35 leads to A24. This is supported by consistent directional changes in successor distributions, position shifts, order relations and lag distances, with 100 evidence items against 0.94 expected by chance. The main limitation is that some lag items have very low support in one window (e.g., only 1 trace in the later window for a few pairs), but the overall pattern rests on many items with full support. A competing explanation of random sampling variation is effectively ruled out by the large excess of qualifying items.

Other differences: A20, A21 and A22 now have fewer successors (only A20, A21, A22 and A25), and A27 now leads to A36 more often; these are consistent with the reordering and routing change described above.
