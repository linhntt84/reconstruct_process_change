### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A14, A15, and A20 each occurred in every trace (100% participation, mean 1.0 per trace). In the later window, each appears in about 53% of traces (mean 0.53 per trace), so they are skipped in about 47% of cases. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the earlier window, activity A36 always had A14 as its only successor. In the later window, A36 leads to A14 in about 53% of cases and to A21 in about 47% of cases. [DIR001]
- In the earlier window, the order was fixed: A36 before A14 before A20 before A15 before A21, with all five activities co-occurring in every trace. In the later window, about 53% of traces still follow this full sequence with the same order; in the remaining 47% of traces, A14, A20, and A15 are absent, and only A36 and A21 occur, with A21 immediately after A36 (lag 1 instead of lag 4). [REL001–REL009, LAG001]
- The position of A21 shifted slightly earlier on average (median relative position from about 0.43 to 0.41) and became more variable, consistent with A21 sometimes occurring right after A36. [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window, about half of the cases bypass the middle activities A14, A20, and A15, going directly from A36 to A21, while the other half still execute the full sequence A36 → A14 → A20 → A15 → A21. This is directly shown by the drop in participation of A14, A15, and A20 from 100% to 53%, the appearance of a direct A36→A21 transition in 47% of cases, the co-occurrence patterns where A14, A15, and A20 are either all present or all absent together with A21 and A36, and the lag between A36 and A21 changing from 4 to 1 in the bypassed cases. The evidence is consistent across multiple indicators (OCC, DIR, REL, LAG, POS) and far exceeds the number of items expected by chance (15 vs 0.35). A competing explanation that this is a random sampling fluctuation is ruled out by the screening comparison. A limitation is that the evidence does not show why the bypass occurs, but that is outside the scope.

Other differences: None beyond the described structural change.
