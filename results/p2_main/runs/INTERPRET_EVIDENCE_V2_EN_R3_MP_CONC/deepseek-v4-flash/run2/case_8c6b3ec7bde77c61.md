### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed its control flow by replacing a set of activities that were previously executed in almost every case (A20, A21, A30, A31, A32) with a different set of activities (A04, A19, A22, A23, A24, A25), while the core activities A15, A16, A33 and A48 remained. The new activities are executed in a substantial share of cases (16–34%) and are positioned in the middle of the process, often leading to A33 or A16. The old activities were almost completely absent in the later window. The number of qualifying items (158) far exceeds the expected 1.22 under no change, so this is not a chance sampling effect. The successor, co-occurrence, positional and lag data are all consistent with this replacement.

Other differences: The relative order of the core activities shifted slightly (A33 earlier, A48 later relative to A15/A16), but these are secondary to the main replacement. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the process now has multiple variants, with the new activities appearing in only a subset of cases, but the near-total disappearance of the old activities makes a simple replacement the most direct interpretation.
