### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, activities A03 and A11 are no longer executed, and the path that previously went through them is replaced by a direct connection from A06 to A12/A19. In the earlier window A03 and A11 were present in about 29% and 20% of traces, respectively, and A06 led to A03 in about 86% of its outgoing transitions; in the later window A03 and A11 have zero participation and zero outgoing transitions, while A06's outgoing transitions go to A19 (about 58%) and A12 (about 37%). A12 and A19 remain but occur earlier and closer to A06, consistent with A06 now leading directly to them. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS003, POS004, LAG002, LAG003]

Other differences: A12 and A19 participation decreased from about 29% to 22%, and their relative positions moved earlier; repeated occurrences of A06, A12 and A19 became closer together. These are consistent with the same structural change but are not needed for the main hypothesis. [POS003, POS004, LAG001, LAG004, LAG005]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A03 and A11 still exist but were not observed in the later 200-trace sample; however, their previous participation was substantial and the screening comparison (18 items vs 0.22 expected by chance) makes this unlikely.
