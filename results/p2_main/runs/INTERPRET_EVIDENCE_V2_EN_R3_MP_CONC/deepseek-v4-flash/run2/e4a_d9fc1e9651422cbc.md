### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained A20, A21, A22, A23, A26, A31, A32, A37, A38 and A39. In the later window, A22 appears in about 45% of traces, and A31, A32, A37, A38 and A39 each appear in about 55% of traces; the other activities remain in all traces. [OCC001–OCC006, POS001–POS010]
- The outgoing behaviour of A20 changed: before it split roughly equally among A31, A32, A37, A38 and A39 (each about 17–24%); after it goes to A23 in about 99.5% of its outgoing transitions. [DIR001]
- A21 before went only to A22; after it goes to A22 in about 45% of its outgoing transitions and to the group A31, A32, A37, A38, A39 in the remaining about 55%. [DIR002]
- The group’s outgoing edges before included A23 (about 19–26%); after they no longer go to A23 and instead go to A26 (about 14–25%) and among themselves. [DIR003–DIR007]
- Order relations reversed: before, A31, A32, A37, A38 and A39 occurred before A21 and before A23; after, A21 and A23 occur before the group when both occur. [REL008–REL012, REL020–REL024]
- A22 and the group were both present in all traces before; after they are almost never in the same trace (co-occurrence about 0.5%), so they became alternatives. [REL015–REL019]
- The mean position of A21, A23 and A22 moved earlier (e.g., A23 from 0.48 to 0.38 of the trace), while the group moved later (e.g., A31 from 0.40 to 0.59). [POS002–POS010]
- The elapsed time between A26 and A19 dropped from about 0.94 hours to 0.45 hours on average. [TWAIT001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change: the process moved from a single mandatory sequence in which a group of activities (A31, A32, A37, A38, A39) and A22 were executed before A21 and A23, to a structure where A20 leads to A23, then A21, and then either A22 or the group, with the group and A22 becoming optional alternatives, before A26. This is supported by the large number of control-flow items (72 items vs 0.72 expected by chance) and the consistent direction of participation, order and position changes. The timing between A26 and A19 also halved, which is a separate timing change.

Other differences: The timing change between A26 and A19 (mean from 0.94 to 0.45 hours) is not part of the structural hypothesis. The LAG items are based on a single trace in the later window and do not provide reliable independent confirmation.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants rather than a modification of the process, but the sheer number and consistency of the control-flow items make this unlikely.
