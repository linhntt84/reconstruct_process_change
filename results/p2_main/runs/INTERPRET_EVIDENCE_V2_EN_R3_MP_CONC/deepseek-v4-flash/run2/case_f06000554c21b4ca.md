### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the relative order of two groups of activities reversed: A26 and A27 now occur after A35, A36, and A41, whereas before they occurred before them. This is directly shown by the reversal in pairwise order (REL001–REL006) and by the position shifts (POS001–POS005). The successor changes (DIR001–DIR005) are consistent: A25 now leads to the A35/A36/A41 group instead of A27, and A35/A36/A41 now lead to A27 instead of A30, while A26 now leads to A30. The lag changes (LAG001, LAG003) also reflect the new distances. The evidence is strong: 26 control-flow items against 0.63 expected by chance, and the items are mutually consistent. A limitation is that some LAG items have very low support in one window (e.g., LAG002, LAG004–LAG010), but the REL and POS items have full support and are sufficient.

Other differences: A30's role changed from a common successor of A35/A36/A41 to a successor of A26; A27 became the successor of A35/A36/A41. This is part of the same reordering, not a separate hypothesis. The evidence cannot rule out a more complex restructuring, but the simplest explanation is the order reversal.
