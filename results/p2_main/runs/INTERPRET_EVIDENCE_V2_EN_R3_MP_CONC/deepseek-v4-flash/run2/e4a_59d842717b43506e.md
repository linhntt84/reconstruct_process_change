### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A14, A15, A20, A21, and A22 appear in about 16–22% of traces, whereas they were completely absent in the earlier window. [OCC001–OCC005]
- These five activities never co-occur in the same trace; they are mutually exclusive alternatives. [REL001–REL003, REL007–REL008, REL012]
- In the earlier window, A24 always led to A25 and A25 always led to A26. In the later window, A24 leads to A25 in about 49% of cases and to each of the five new activities in about 8–12% of cases; A25 leads to A26 in about 52% and to each new activity in about 6–11%. [DIR006–DIR007]
- The new activities lead to A25 and A26 in roughly equal proportions. [DIR001–DIR005]
- The distance between A24 and A25 changed: previously always adjacent (lag 1), now adjacent in 49% and lag 2 in 51%. Similarly, A25–A26 was always adjacent, now adjacent in 52% and lag 2 in 48%. A24–A26 was always lag 2, now lag 3 in 99.5%. [LAG001–LAG003]
- The new activities appear around the middle of the trace (mean position ~0.5). [POS001–POS004]
- The time between A10 and A11 halved: mean from about 1.0 hour to 0.5 hours, median from 1.01 to 0.51 hours. [TWAIT001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new optional step is introduced into the process, consisting of one of five alternative activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22), executed in about 20% of traces and inserted either between A24 and A25 or between A25 and A26, replacing the direct sequence in those cases. The new activities were absent before, appear in the middle of traces, are mutually exclusive, and their insertion changes the lag structure between A24, A25, and A26. The order between the new activity and A25 is not fixed (both orders occur), but the new activity always follows A24 and precedes A26. This is supported by the large number of control-flow items (34 vs 0.44 expected by chance) and the consistent pattern across occurrence, direction, position, relation, and lag indicators. A competing explanation that these are rare variants is ruled out by the scale and consistency of the evidence.

Other differences: The time between A10 and A11 decreased by about half (mean from ~1.0 to ~0.5 hours), a separate timing change not directly linked to the structural change. [TWAIT001]
