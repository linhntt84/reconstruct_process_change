### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A20 led to A30 (40%), A31 (32.5%) and A36 (27.5%); in the later window it leads to A22 in about 99% of cases, with A30/A31/A36 almost disappearing as successors. [DIR001]
- A21 previously went to A25 in all observed cases; in the later window it goes to A30 (36%), A31 (32.5%) and A36 (30.5%), with A25 dropping to about 1%. [DIR002]
- A30, A31 and A36 now go to A25 (about 35%, 30% and 33% respectively) instead of to A22, which fell from roughly 29–38% to near zero for each of these activities. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- The order between A21/A22 and A30/A31/A36 reversed: in the earlier window A30/A31/A36 always occurred before A21 and A22; in the later window A21 and A22 occur before A30/A31/A36 in about 99% of traces. [REL001–REL006]
- Positions shifted accordingly: A21 and A22 moved earlier (mean position down by about 0.09), while A30, A31 and A36 moved later (mean position up by about 0.17–0.18). [POS001–POS005]
- The gap between A20 and A22 changed from 4 steps to 1 step, and the gap between A21 and A25 changed from 1 step to 4 steps. [LAG001, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: A21 and A22 now occur before A30/A31/A36, whereas before they occurred after. Concretely, A20 now goes directly to A22, and A21 now goes to A30/A31/A36, which then lead to A25; previously A20 went to A30/A31/A36, which led to A22, and A21 went to A25. This is directly shown by the reversal in relative order (REL), the shift in positions (POS), and the changed successor relations (DIR). The lag evidence for A20–A31, A20–A36, A31–A22 and A36–A22 is based on only 2 traces in the later window, so those specific distances are weakly supported, but the main ordering change rests on all 200 traces in each window.

Other differences: none beyond the reordering described.
