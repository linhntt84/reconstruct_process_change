### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that resource allocation changed: activity A12 became fully concentrated on a single resource (User_36) in the later window, and several other activities (A01, A03, A13, A14, A17, A22) experienced a redistribution of assignments among resources, with some resources gaining and others losing share. This is supported by 8 resource-related evidence items, far exceeding the 0.005 expected by chance, and the changes are consistent across activities. A competing explanation that this is due to random sampling is unlikely given the magnitude and consistency.

Other differences: The elapsed time between A03 and A13 decreased substantially (median from ~168h to ~66h), which is a separate timing change. This is based on a single time item, but it is a large and unusual change (1 item vs 0.02 expected). The evidence does not link this timing change to the resource allocation change.
