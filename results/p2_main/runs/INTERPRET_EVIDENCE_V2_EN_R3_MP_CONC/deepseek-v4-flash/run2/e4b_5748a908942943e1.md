Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: activities A01, A03, A13, A14, A16, and A17 are now performed by a different set of resources, and A16 in particular is now executed by a single resource. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the resource redistribution reflects a staffing or assignment policy change rather than a change in the process structure; no control-flow differences were detected.
