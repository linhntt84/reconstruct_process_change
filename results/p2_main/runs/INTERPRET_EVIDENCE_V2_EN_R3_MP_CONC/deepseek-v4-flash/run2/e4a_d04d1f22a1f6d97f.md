### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a single path A21→A22→A23 to two alternative paths: one still via A22, and a new path via A27, A28, or A33 before A23. In the later window, about half the cases take the new path, where A22 is absent and A21 leads directly to one of the new activities, which then precede A23. The order among A27, A28, and A33 is not fixed. This is directly shown by the appearance of the new activities, the drop in A22 participation, the redistribution of A21's outgoing edges, and the relational and lag changes. The large number of control-flow items (26 vs 0.4 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely.

Other differences: The timing between A25-A29 and A30-A29 decreased by about half in the later window, indicating a separate change in timing. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities existed before but were too rare to be observed; however, the consistency and volume of evidence make this unlikely.
