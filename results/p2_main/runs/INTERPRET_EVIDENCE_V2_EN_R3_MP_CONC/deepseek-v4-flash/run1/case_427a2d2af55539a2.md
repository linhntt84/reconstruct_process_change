### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a choice among three alternative activities (A14, A15, A16) after A39 to a sequence of three activities (A21, A22, A23) that are all executed, in any order, before A24. The earlier window shows exactly one of A14/A15/A16 per trace (never co-occurring) and each leading to A24; the later window shows all three of A21/A22/A23 in every trace, co-occurring with no fixed order, and each leading to A24. A39's outgoing edges switch from the old set to the new set. A24 is still the final activity but occurs later, consistent with the added activities. The evidence is direct and consistent across occurrence, direction, relation, position and lag indicators. A competing explanation that the old activities were simply renamed is unlikely because the new activities co-occur with each other while the old ones were mutually exclusive, and the order among the new ones is not fixed. The main limitation is that we cannot rule out that the new activities are a different representation of the same underlying work, but the behavioural change in structure is clear.
