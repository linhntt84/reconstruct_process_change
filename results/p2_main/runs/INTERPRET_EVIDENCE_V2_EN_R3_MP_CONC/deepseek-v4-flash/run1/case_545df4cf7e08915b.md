### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that activity A01 was replaced by activity A08 in the same position in the process. In the earlier window A01 was present in about half the traces and was followed by A09, A10 or A16; in the later window A01 is absent and A08 appears in about half the traces with the same outgoing transitions. Activities that previously led to A01 (A17, A03) now lead to A08, and co-occurrence with A03, A09, A10, A16, A17 and A18 shifts from A01 to A08. This is directly shown by occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators.

Other differences: The ordering of A11 and A15 changed – A11 moved from the end to an earlier position, A15 moved to the end. Additionally, A05 now leads to A15 instead of A11, A11 now leads to A03, and A18 now leads to A11 instead of A15. These are separate from the A01/A08 replacement.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the change is not a simple substitution but a broader reconfiguration of the process; however, the direct inheritance of outgoing transitions and co-occurrence patterns strongly points to a replacement of A01 by A08.
