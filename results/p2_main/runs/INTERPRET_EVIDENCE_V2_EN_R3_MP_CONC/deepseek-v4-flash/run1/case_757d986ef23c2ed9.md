### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A20 always led to A22; in the later window, A20 leads to A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 (each about 20%) and almost never to A22 (0.5%). [DIR001]
- In the earlier window, A21 led to A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 (each about 20%); in the later window, A21 leads almost always to A25 (99.5%). [DIR002]
- In the earlier window, A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 each led to A25 (about 18–25%) and to each other; in the later window, they each lead to A22 (about 17–22%) and no longer to A25 (A25 share drops to ~0). [DIR003–DIR007]
- A21 and A22 moved later in the trace (mean position from ~0.52 to 0.65 for A21, from 0.39 to 0.52 for A22); A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 moved earlier (mean from ~0.60 to ~0.44). [POS001–POS007]
- For each pair of A21 or A22 with A30, A31, A36, A37, A38, the order reversed: before A21/A22 always preceded the other; after the other always precedes A21/A22 (99.5% of cases). [REL001–REL010]
- LAG items show a shift to a fixed lag, but they are based on only 1 trace in the later window, so they are not reliable. [LAG001–LAG010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: the block of activities A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 now occurs before A21 and A22, whereas before it occurred after them. This is directly shown by the reversal of order in all ten REL pairs and by the position shifts. The routing also changed consistently: A20 now leads to the block instead of A22, A21 now leads to A25 instead of the block, and the block now leads to A22 instead of A25. The screening count (34 items vs 0.55 expected) rules out chance. A competing explanation that the windows sample different process variants is unlikely given the consistency across many activities, but cannot be fully excluded.
