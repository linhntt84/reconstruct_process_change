### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change: the process now predominantly executes a branch containing activities A21–A25 instead of a branch containing A26–A30, and A20 now routes to A24 rather than A29. This is supported by the large, consistent shifts in participation, co-occurrence, and routing across many indicators, with 103 control-flow items against an expected 1.15 by chance. The main limitation is that the two branches are mutually exclusive alternatives, so the change could be described as a shift in which alternative path is taken; the evidence cannot rule out that both branches existed before and after, with only their frequencies changing. Other differences: A34 now appears interleaved with A21–A25 rather than with A26–A30, and A05’s relationship with the two groups shifted accordingly.
