### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A28–A33 appeared in about 48% of traces and A34–A38 in about 52%, and the two sets never co-occurred (e.g., A28 and A34 co-occurrence 0). In the later window, each of these activities appears in about 98–99.5% of traces, and A28–A33 always precede A34–A38 (e.g., A28 before A34 in 97.5% of traces). [OCC001–OCC010, REL025, REL033]

- A25's outgoing transitions changed: before it led to both groups with roughly equal shares (each about 8–15%); after it leads almost exclusively to A28–A33 (A32 21%, A33 24%, A29 20%, A28 19.5%, A30 15%) and almost never directly to A34–A38 (each ≤5%). [DIR001]

- A26 moved from being after each branch to the end: before, A26 was after A28–A33 (A28 before A26 in 48%) and after A34–A38 (A34 before A26 in 52%); after, A26 is after both groups (A28 before A26 in 99.5%, A34 before A26 in 98%). [REL011, REL016]

- Positions shifted accordingly: A25 and A28–A33 moved earlier (A25 mean position from 0.64 to 0.55; A28 from 0.74 to 0.64), while A34–A38 and A26 moved later (A34 from 0.73 to 0.76; A26 from 0.82 to 0.84). [POS001–POS012]

- Within each group, the order is not fixed: A28–A33 appear in both orders in similar proportions (e.g., A28 before A29 50% vs 49.5% after), and similarly A34–A38. [REL021, REL056]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from two alternative branches to a single sequential execution: in the earlier window, traces followed either a branch with A28–A33 or a branch with A34–A38 (each about half the traces), with A26 after each branch; in the later window, almost all traces execute A28–A33 first, then A34–A38, and finally A26. This is directly shown by the co-occurrence of the two groups (from 0 to ~98%), the fixed order A28–A33 before A34–A38, and the position shifts. The large number of qualifying items (95 vs 1 expected by chance) rules out random sampling as an explanation. A competing explanation that the LAG items reflect a specific lag is not reliable because they are based on a single trace in the later window, but the main conclusion does not depend on them.

Other differences: A25's direct successors now only the first group; A26 moved to the end of the sequence.
