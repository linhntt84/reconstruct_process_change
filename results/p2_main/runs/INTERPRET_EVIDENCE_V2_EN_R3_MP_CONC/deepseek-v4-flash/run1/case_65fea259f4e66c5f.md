### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A26, A27 and A28 occurred in every trace, with A26 before A27 in about 99% of traces and A27 before A28 in about 98.5% of traces. [OCC001, REL001, REL007, DIR001]
- In the later window, A27 occurs in about 56% of traces (down from 100%), while A32, A33, A38, A39 and A40, which were absent before, each occur in about 44–45% of traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

- A26's position shifted slightly earlier in the trace (mean relative position from 0.49 to 0.46). [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in routing after A26. In the earlier window, the process followed a single path A26 → A27 → A28 in essentially all traces. In the later window, the process branches: about 56% of traces continue through A27 to A28, while about 44% take an alternative path through a set of new activities (A32, A33, A38, A39, A40) that also lead to A28. This is supported by the drop in A27 participation from 100% to 56%, the appearance of the new activities in about 44% of traces, the redistribution of A26's outgoing transitions, the mutual exclusivity between A27 and the new activities, and the fact that the new activities co-occur and precede A28. The internal order among the new activities is not fixed (both orders appear in similar proportions), so the exact structure of that branch is not fully determined. A competing explanation that the new activities are a rare variant is ruled out by their frequency and the large number of consistent indicators.
