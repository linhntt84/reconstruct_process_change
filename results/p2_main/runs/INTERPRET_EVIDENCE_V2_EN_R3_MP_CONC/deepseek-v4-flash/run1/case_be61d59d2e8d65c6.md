- In the later window, activities A19, A20, and A25 appear in about 0.5% of traces, down from about 33%, 29%, and 41% respectively. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

- In the later window, A19, A20, and A25 are almost entirely absent from traces (pairs among them appear in neither trace in about 99% of cases). [REL001, REL002, REL006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural simplification: activities A19, A20, and A25, which previously appeared in a substantial share of traces (about 30–40%) and often preceded A27/A28/A29, are now almost never observed, while the flow through A27 → A28 → A29 becomes direct and dominant. This is directly shown by the near-disappearance of those activities, the tightening of the A27–A28 and A28–A29 adjacency, and the shift of A27 and A28 to almost single successors. The large number of consistent items (30 vs. 0.56 expected by chance) rules out random sampling as an explanation. A competing explanation that cannot be fully excluded is that the later window represents a different case mix or a separate process variant, but the evidence strongly points to a change in the process structure itself.

Other differences: A10's outgoing transition distribution was also redistributed, but this is not part of the main hypothesis and may reflect a separate adjustment.
