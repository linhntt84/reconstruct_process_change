### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A19, A20 and A21 each occurred in about 30–36% of traces; in the later window they occur in about 1% or less (1–2 traces). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the earlier window, activities A26, A27 and A28 were absent; in the later window they occur in every trace (100%). [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- In the earlier window, activity A44 was followed by A19, A20 and A21 (about 30%, 24% and 30% of its outgoing transitions); in the later window A44 is followed by A26, A27 and A28 (about 31%, 27% and 26%). [DIR007]
- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A21 each led almost always to A29 (95–99% of their outgoing transitions); in the later window these activities are nearly absent, and A26, A27 and A28 lead to A29 and to each other in roughly equal shares. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- In the earlier window, A26, A27 and A28 did not co-occur with each other or with A29; in the later window they co-occur in all traces, with both orders between A26, A27 and A28 appearing in similar proportions (e.g., A26 before A27 about 50%, A27 before A26 about 48%). [REL019, REL020, REL023]
- The gap between A44 and A29 increased from typically 2–3 steps to 4–5 steps. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process changed from a path A44 → (A19 or A20 or A21) → A29 to a path A44 → (A26, A27, A28 in any order) → A29. The old activities A19–A21 were replaced by new activities A26–A28. This is supported by the near-total disappearance of A19–A21, the appearance of A26–A28 in all traces, the change in A44's successors, and the increased lag between A44 and A29. The evidence is direct and consistent across occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators. A competing explanation that the old activities still exist but were not sampled is unlikely given their near-total absence (1–2 traces) and the simultaneous appearance of the new ones in all traces.

Other differences: A29's position shifted slightly later (mean relative position from about 0.40 to 0.44). [POS007] This is consistent with the added activities before it.
