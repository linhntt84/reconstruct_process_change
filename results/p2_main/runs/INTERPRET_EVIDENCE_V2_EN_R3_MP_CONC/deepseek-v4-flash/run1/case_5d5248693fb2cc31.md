### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the control-flow order changed: activities A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 moved from before A28/A29 to after them, and their outgoing paths changed from A28 to A30/A34. This is directly shown by successor changes (DIR), position shifts (POS), order reversals (REL) and lag changes (LAG), all pointing to the same reordering. The number of qualifying items (28) far exceeds the 0.64 expected by chance, and the items are consistent across indicators. A limitation is that the exact new sequence is not fully specified, and A19's cooccurrence with A28/A29 decreased, suggesting it may be less often in the same trace, but the order reversal itself is clear.

Other differences: A29 and A30 also changed their outgoing behaviour: A29 now leads to many activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) instead of almost exclusively A30, and A30 now leads to those activities in addition to A34. These changes are part of the same reordering rather than separate phenomena.
