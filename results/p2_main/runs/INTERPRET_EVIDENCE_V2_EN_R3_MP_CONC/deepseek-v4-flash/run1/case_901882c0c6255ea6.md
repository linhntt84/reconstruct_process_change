### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process changed from a repeated, interleaved execution of a block of activities (A30, A31, A36, A37, A38) to a single execution of each of these activities, with the loop broken: A38 now leads to A25 instead of back to A30. This is supported by the drop in mean occurrences and recurrence, the disappearance of interleaving, the concentration of lags to 1–2 steps, and the change in A38's successor. The evidence is consistent and based on full support (200 traces) for the OCC and REL items.
