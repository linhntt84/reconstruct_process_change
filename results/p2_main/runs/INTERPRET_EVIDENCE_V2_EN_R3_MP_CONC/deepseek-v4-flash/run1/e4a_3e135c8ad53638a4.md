### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A22, A31, A32, and A37 occurred in every trace; in the later window, A22 occurs in about 53% of traces and A31, A32, A37 each in about 47%. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A20's outgoing transitions changed from a split among A37 (35%), A31 (34%), and A32 (31%) to almost exclusively A23 (99.5%). [DIR001]
- A21's outgoing transitions changed from always to A22 to a split: A22 (53.5%), A37 (16.5%), A32 (16%), A31 (14%). [DIR002]
- A23 now directly follows A20 (lag 1 in 99.5% of cases) instead of lag 4 as before. [LAG001]
- The order between A21 and A31/A32/A37 reversed: before, A31/A32/A37 preceded A21; after, when both occur, A21 precedes them, and A21 often occurs without them. [REL006, REL007, REL008]
- A22 and A31/A32/A37 became almost mutually exclusive: before they always co-occurred; after, co-occurrence is only 0.5% for each pair. [REL011, REL012, REL013]
- A31, A32, and A37 now often appear together in only 47% of traces and are both absent in 53% of traces, whereas before they always co-occurred. [REL020, REL021, REL022]
- When A31, A32, or A37 occur, their position in the trace shifted later (mean position from about 0.395 to about 0.576). [POS006, POS007, POS008]
- The waiting time between A23 and A24 increased from a mean of about 1.0 hours to about 1.95 hours. [TWAIT001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed so that a set of activities (A22, A31, A32, A37) that were previously mandatory and executed in a fixed order are now optional and often skipped, and the main path now goes directly from A20 to A23, bypassing those activities. Specifically, A20 now almost always leads to A23 instead of to A31/A32/A37, and A21 now splits among A22, A31, A32, and A37 instead of always going to A22. The order between A21 and A31/A32/A37 reversed, and A22 and A31/A32/A37 became mutually exclusive. The large number of control-flow indicators (47 items vs 0.51 expected by chance) makes it very unlikely that these differences are due to random sampling.

Other differences: The waiting time between A23 and A24 increased substantially (from about 1.0 to 1.95 hours mean), indicating a change in timing as well. [TWAIT001]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a shift in the mix of process variants rather than a modification of the process itself, but the consistency and magnitude of the control-flow evidence make this less plausible.
