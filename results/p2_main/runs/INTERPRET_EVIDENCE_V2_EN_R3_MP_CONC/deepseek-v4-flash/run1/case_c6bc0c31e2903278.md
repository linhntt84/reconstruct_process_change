### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: the previously mandatory activities A03 and A10 became alternatives (each in about half of the traces instead of both always), A01 was removed, and new activities A08 and A12 were added, with A06 now leading to A09. The large number of control-flow items (49 vs 0.32 expected by chance) and the consistency of the relational and positional evidence make this a supported hypothesis. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a rare variant that became dominant, but the magnitude and coherence of the changes make this unlikely.

Other differences: A04 participation increased, and A12 moved from an early position to a later one [OCC003, POS005].
