- Activity A02 became much more common: its share of traces rose from about 14% to 41%, and its mean position moved from about 0.60 to 0.32 of the trace length (earlier). [OCC001, POS001]

- A25's outgoing edge changed from almost always A27 (99%) to a spread over A43, A41, A36, A42, A35 (each about 15–22%). [DIR002]
- A26's outgoing edge changed from a spread over A41, A42, A35, A36, A43 to almost always A30 (98%). [DIR003]

- A02 now co-occurs with many activities (A19, A25, A26, A27, A30, A35, A36, A41, A42, A43) in about 41% of traces, up from 13.5%, and in most of those it appears before them (e.g., A02 before A19 in 21.5% vs 1.5% before). [REL001–REL010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control-flow structure: activity A02 is now executed more often and earlier, and the routing among activities A25, A26, A27, A30, A35, A36, A41, A42, A43 changed. Specifically, A26 now leads to A30, while A35/A36/A41/A42/A43 now lead to A27 instead of A30, and the order between A26/A27 and that group reversed. This is supported by the large number of qualified items (64 vs 0.97 expected by chance) and by consistent, well-supported DIR and REL items. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed shifts reflect a re-labelling of activity codes rather than a true behavioural change, but the evidence is strong enough to favour a structural change.

Other differences: A02's outgoing edges shifted to A19/A20, and A25 no longer goes to A27. These are not part of the main hypothesis but are consistent with the overall re-routing.
