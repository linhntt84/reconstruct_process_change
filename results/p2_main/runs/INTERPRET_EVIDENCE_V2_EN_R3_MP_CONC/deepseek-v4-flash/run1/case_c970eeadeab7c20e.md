### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A06 was removed from the process. In the earlier window, A06 occurred in every trace and was involved in a two-way order with A03 (both A03→A06 and A06→A03 were observed). In the later window, A06 is absent, A03 always occurs as the final activity, and A04 directly precedes A03. This is a structural change: an activity was removed and the ordering changed.

The evidence is direct and consistent across occurrence, transition, relation, position, and lag indicators. The complete absence of A06 in 200 later traces, together with the disappearance of all its incoming and outgoing transitions, makes sampling variation an unlikely explanation. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A06 was renamed or merged with another activity, but no evidence supports that.
