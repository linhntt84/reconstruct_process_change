### Observed differences

- Activities A19, A20, and A25 occurred in about 31–36% of traces in the earlier window (A19 36.5%, A20 31%, A25 35%) and were almost absent in the later window (A19 0.5%, A20 0%, A25 0%). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

- A41's outgoing transitions changed: earlier it went to A19 (29%), A25 (31.5%), and A20 (26%) among others; later it goes mostly to A26 (85.6%) and no longer to A19/A20/A25. [DIR005]

- The lag between A41 and A26 shifted: earlier A26 followed A41 after 2 steps in 84.5% of traces; later it follows after 1 step in 85.8% of traces. [LAG001]
- A21's outgoing routing shifted modestly: A24 increased from 14% to 34.7% and A31 decreased from 53.5% to 37.6%. [DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process stopped executing activities A19, A20, and A25 in most cases, and that A41 now leads directly to A26 (and some other activities), bypassing these activities. This is directly shown by the near-disappearance of A19, A20, and A25, the change in A41's outgoing transitions from these activities to A26, the loss of co-occurrence between these activities and A26/A31/A41, and the reduced lag between A41 and A26. The number of evidence items (29) far exceeds the 0.57 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A21's routing shifted (A24 increased, A31 decreased), and A26 and A41 positions shifted slightly (A26 earlier, A41 later), but these are minor and may be consequences of the removed steps.

Limitation: The evidence cannot rule out that these activities were moved to a different part of the process not captured by the observed windows, but their complete absence in the later window makes removal or bypass the most plausible interpretation.
