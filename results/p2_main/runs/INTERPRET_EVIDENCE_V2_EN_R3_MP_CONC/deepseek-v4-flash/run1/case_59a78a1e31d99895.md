### Observed differences

- A04, A06, and A10 are executed much less often: participation drops from about 50% to about 20% of traces, and mean occurrences per trace from ~0.5 to ~0.24–0.26 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].
- A02 no longer routes to A04, A06, or A10; its successors shift from those (together ~50% of transitions) to A05 (46% vs 23%), A03 (21% vs 12%), A09 (21% vs 12%), and A01 (12% vs 3%) [DIR001].
- A04, A06, and A10 now lead to A08 instead of their previous successors (A04: A01→A08; A06: A10→A10/A08; A10: A06→A08/A06) [DIR003, DIR004, DIR006]. A03 and A09 no longer lead to A08 [DIR002, DIR005].
- Positions shift: A04, A06, A10 move earlier (mean from ~0.99/0.89/0.88 to ~0.60/0.63/0.64), while A02 and A08 move later (A02 mean 0.45→0.62, A08 0.57→0.72) [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005].
- Relations confirm: A02 and A04/A06/A10 co-occurrence drops from ~0.5 to ~0.2, with A02 alone in ~80% of traces [REL001, REL002, REL003]. Lags increase for A02→A04/A06/A10 (e.g., A02-A04 lag 1 from 61% to 0%) and decrease for A03/A09→A04/A06/A10 (e.g., A03-A04 lag 1 appears at 38%) [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG009, LAG013, LAG014, LAG016].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed such that a branch involving activities A04, A06, and A10, which previously occurred in about half of the traces after A02 and near the end, is now much less frequent (about 20% of traces), and when these activities occur they appear earlier and lead to A08 instead of their previous successors. A02 now routes to A05, A03, A09, and A01 instead of to A04/A06/A10. This is supported by the consistent drop in participation, the change in successors, the position shifts, and the relation/lag changes. A competing explanation that the differences arise from random sampling is unlikely given the large number of consistent items (45 items vs 0.34 expected by chance). A limitation is that some individual items have low support (e.g., DIR003 before has only 1 trace), but the overall pattern is robust. Other differences, such as A08 moving later and A03/A09 no longer leading to A08, are part of the same rerouting.
