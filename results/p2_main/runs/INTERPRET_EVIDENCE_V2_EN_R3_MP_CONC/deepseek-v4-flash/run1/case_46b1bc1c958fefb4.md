### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A02 was replaced by activity A01 in the later window. A01 appears where A02 was previously present, the two never co-occur, and the relations of A02 with A03 and A06 are mirrored by A01's new relations with those activities. The change is direct and consistent across occurrence, successor, and co-occurrence indicators. A competing explanation that A01 is an entirely new activity added while A02 was removed independently cannot be fully ruled out, but the mutual exclusivity and the parallel relational structure make a substitution the most parsimonious interpretation.

Other differences: The outgoing distribution of A03 shifted slightly (e.g., more to A10, less to A11), but this is minor and may be a consequence of the A01/A02 change. [DIR003]
