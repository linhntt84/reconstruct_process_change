### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A28–A33 each occurred in about 48% of traces and A34–A38 in about 52%; in the later window, all of A28–A38 occurred in about 98–99.5% of traces. [OCC001–OCC010]

- Elapsed time between A14–A20 and A15–A20 decreased from about 1.0 hour to about 0.5 hours. [TWAIT001–TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process likely changed from a choice between two alternative sets of activities (A28–A33 or A34–A38) to a sequential execution of both sets, with the first set (A28–A33) before the second (A34–A38). This is supported by the near-universal participation of all these activities in the later window, the shift from mutual exclusion to co-occurrence with a fixed order, the change in A25's outgoing edges to only the first set, and the positional shifts (first set earlier, second set later). The evidence cannot rule out that the two sets were always intended to be sequential but the second set was skipped in the earlier window; however, the consistent pattern across many activity pairs makes a change in process structure the most direct explanation.

Other differences: The elapsed time between A14–A20 and A15–A20 halved, indicating a timing change independent of the structural change. [TWAIT001–TWAIT002]
