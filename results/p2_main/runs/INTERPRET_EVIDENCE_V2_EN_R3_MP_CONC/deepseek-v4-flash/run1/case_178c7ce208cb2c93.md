### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A01, was inserted between the existing activities A02/A04 and A03, so that the path now goes through A01 before reaching A03. In the earlier window, A02 and A04 could go directly to A03; in the later window, they go to A01, which then goes to A03. This is directly shown by the appearance of A01 in all traces, the routing changes (A02 and A04 now target A01 instead of A03), the ordering relations (A02 before A01, A01 before A03, A04 before A01), and the increased lag between A02/A04 and A03. The number of evidence items (11) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.06), so the overall pattern is not a random artifact.

Other differences: A02's position moved earlier, consistent with the insertion of A01 after it. No other differences were observed.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 existed in the earlier window but was never observed; however, its appearance in 100% of later traces and absence in all earlier traces makes this unlikely.
