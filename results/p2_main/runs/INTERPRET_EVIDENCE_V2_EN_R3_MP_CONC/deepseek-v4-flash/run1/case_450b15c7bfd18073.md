### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a single fixed sequence A21→A22→A23 to a choice between that sequence and an alternative sequence A21→(A27, A28, A33 in any order)→A23, with A22 skipped in the alternative. This is directly shown by the drop in A22 participation, the appearance of the three new activities, the redistribution of A21's outgoing edges, the mutual exclusivity between A22 and the new activities, and the increased lag between A21 and A23. The evidence is consistent across occurrence, direction, relation, and lag indicators, and the number of qualifying items (26) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.4). A competing explanation that the new activities are simply added alongside A22 is ruled out by the absence of co-occurrence between A22 and any of the new activities. The main limitation is that the exact order among A27, A28, and A33 is not fixed, but that is part of the observed alternative path rather than a separate change.

Other differences: none beyond the described change.
