### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the process replaced a group of activities (A16–A20) with a different group (A21–A25), and the routing from A15 changed accordingly, from leading to A19 to leading to A24. This is directly shown by the large drop in participation of A16–A20 and rise of A21–A25, the change in A15's successor, and the co-occurrence patterns indicating that A15 now co-occurs with A21–A25 instead of A16–A20. The number of control-flow evidence items (79) far exceeds the expected 0.84, so this is not a chance fluctuation. [OCC001–OCC010, DIR001, REL001–REL010, REL051–REL063]

Other differences: The waiting times between A11–A13 and A12–A13 increased substantially (about 0.93 hours and 0.24 hours respectively). These are timing changes not part of the structural swap. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed shift reflects a change in case mix rather than a change in the process itself, but the consistency across many activities and the routing change make this less likely.
