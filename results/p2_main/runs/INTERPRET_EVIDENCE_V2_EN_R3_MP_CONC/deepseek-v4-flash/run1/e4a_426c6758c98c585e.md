### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities had multiple outgoing edges: e.g., A15 led to A18 (62%), A21 (12%), A19 (13.5%), A20 (12.5%); A18 led to A27 (53%), A21 (18.5%), A19 (12%), A20 (16.5%). In the later window, each of these activities has a single dominant successor: A15→A18 (99.5%), A18→A19 (99.5%), A19→A21 (99.5%), A21→A20 (100%), A20→A27 (99.5%), and A17→A15 (99.5%), A26→A17 (100%). - The order between many activity pairs became fixed. For example, A15 before A19 occurred in 25.5% of cases earlier and 99.5% later; A18 before A19 occurred in 12% earlier and 99.5% later; A15 before A20 in 69% earlier and 100% later. [REL001-REL008]
- The distance between activities in the sequence became more concentrated. For instance, A15 and A18 were adjacent (lag 1) in 62% of cases earlier and 99.5% later; A15 and A19 were two steps apart in 47% earlier and 100% later; A15 and A20 were four steps apart in 33% earlier and 99.5% later. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- The elapsed time between consecutive completions of A22 and A29 increased from a mean of about 0.90 hours to 1.88 hours; for A30 and A29, from about 0.20 to 0.40 hours. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a flexible order with multiple branches to a fixed sequential chain. The activities involved are A15, A18, A19, A21, A20, A27 (with A17 and A26 as earlier steps). In the earlier window, each of these activities had several possible successors and the order between pairs varied; in the later window, each activity has a single dominant successor and the order between pairs is almost always fixed, forming the chain A26 → A17 → A15 → A18 → A19 → A21 → A20 → A27. This is supported by the DIR items showing redistribution to a single successor, the REL items showing fixed order, and the LAG items showing consistent distances. The number of control flow items (50) far exceeds the expected 0.47, so this is unlikely to be due to chance. A competing explanation that the process always had this chain but the sample changed is unlikely given the large variation in the earlier window.

Other differences: The timing between consecutive completions of A22-A29 and A30-A29 increased roughly twofold, indicating a change in timing as well. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
