### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a previously common segment of the process, involving A10 and the group A27, A36, A37, A42, A43, A44, is now often skipped, while A02 and A08 are executed instead. The participation drops are large and consistent across many activities, and the routing change from A25 to A28 instead of to that group provides a direct structural counterpart. The large number of qualifying items (152 vs 1.48 expected by chance) rules out chance as an overall explanation.

Other differences: A14 now more often leads to A15 than to A10 (DIR003), and A10's own successors have shifted (DIR001). These are consistent with the main hypothesis but not part of it.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a different variant that existed before but was rare; however, the magnitude and consistency of the changes make this unlikely. Also, the exact order of the new path is not fully specified by the evidence.
