### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A33, A34, A35, A37, A38, A39, A40, A41, A42, and A43 each appeared in essentially every trace (about 100% participation, mean occurrences ~1.0). In the later window, each of these activities appears in only about half the traces: A33–A38 in about 46–47% of traces, and A39–A43 in about 54–55% of traces. [OCC001–OCC010]
- In the earlier window, A30 and A31 co-occurred with all of A33–A43 in every trace. In the later window, A30 and A31 still appear in every trace, but they co-occur with A33–A38 in about 46–47% of traces and with A39–A43 in about 54–55% of traces. [REL001–REL018]
- In the earlier window, A33–A38 and A39–A43 all co-occurred with each other in every trace. In the later window, any pair consisting of one activity from A33–A38 and one from A39–A43 co-occurs in only about 1% of traces; instead, about 45–54% of traces contain only the A33–A38 activity and about 53–54% contain only the A39–A43 activity. [REL023–REL026, REL030–REL033, REL036–REL039, REL041–REL048]
- Within the A33–A38 group, co-occurrence remains high (about 46% of traces) when they are present, and within the A39–A43 group, co-occurrence remains high (about 54.5% of traces) when they are present. [REL019–REL022, REL027–REL029, REL034–REL035, REL040, REL049–REL054]
- The outgoing transitions of A30 changed: in the earlier window A30 led mainly to A33–A38; in the later window it also leads to A39–A43, and the shares are redistributed. [DIR001]
- A31, which was not a successor of A33–A38 in the earlier window, becomes a major successor of A33, A34, A37, and A38 in the later window (about 23–26% of their outgoing transitions). [DIR002–DIR005]
- The position of A30 in the trace moved later (mean from 0.55 to 0.64 of the trace length). [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a single path in which activities A33–A38 and A39–A43 were always executed together, to two alternative branches: one branch includes A33–A38 and the other includes A39–A43, with A30 and A31 common to both. In the later window, A30 and A31 are always present, but a trace contains either the A33–A38 group or the A39–A43 group, not both. This is directly shown by the near-zero co-occurrence between the two groups (REL023–REL026, etc.) and the halved participation of each activity (OCC001–OCC010). The routing changes (A30 now leading to A39–A43, and A31 becoming a successor of A33–A38) are consistent with this split. The large number of qualifying items (93 vs 1.1 expected by chance) rules out chance as an overall explanation.

Other differences: The position of A30 moved later in the trace, and the positions of A33–A38 moved later while A39–A43 moved earlier when they occur. These are secondary to the structural split.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two groups were always alternative branches but the earlier window happened to sample only traces that took both branches; however, the complete absence of any trace with both groups in the later window, combined with the high co-occurrence within each group, makes this unlikely.
