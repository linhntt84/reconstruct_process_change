### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the order of two blocks of activities reversed: activities A14, A18, A21, A22, A23 moved from early to late, while activities A15, A16, A17, A19, A20 moved from late to early, with A24 and A28 shifting accordingly. This is directly shown by positional shifts and pairwise order reversals. The main limitation is that we cannot rule out that this reflects a change in the process model (e.g., a swap of two subprocesses) rather than a change in routing, but the evidence is consistent and strong.

Other differences: A29 appears as a new successor for the first group, and A28/A39 successor changes are part of the same overall reversal.
