### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control-flow structure: the process previously had a choice among alternative activities (A14, A17-A20) after A43 and before A28; in the later window these are replaced by a set of activities (A15, A16, A25-A27) that are all executed together in each case, in a flexible order, still between A43 and A28. The shift of A28 to a later position is consistent with a longer middle segment. A competing explanation that the process was completely redesigned with different activities cannot be fully ruled out, but the shared start (A43) and end (A28) in both windows support a change in the middle part.

Other differences: The relative positions of A43 and A28 shifted (A43 earlier, A28 later), consistent with the change in the middle segment.
