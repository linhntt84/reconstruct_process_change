### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a block of activities A25, A26, A31, A32 and A33 is now repeated multiple times per case, with the repetitions interleaved rather than executed in a fixed sequence. This is directly shown by the increase in mean occurrences and recurrence, the shift from fixed order to interleaved/repeated in all pairs, the spread of lags, and the new A33→A25 edge that can act as a loop back. The timing change (TWAIT) is a separate difference and is not part of this hypothesis.

Other differences: The elapsed time between A09–A10 and A11–A10 doubled in the later window; this is a distinct timing change.

A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the repetition is a parallel execution of the activities rather than a sequential loop, although the new A33→A25 edge favours a loop.
