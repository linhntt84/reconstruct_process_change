### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A33, A34, A35, A37, A38, A39, A40, A41, A42, and A43 each occur in about 99% of traces with about one occurrence per trace, whereas in the earlier window they occurred in about 50% of traces with about 0.5 occurrences per trace. [OCC001–OCC010]
- The two groups A33–A38 and A39–A43 were almost never in the same trace before (co-occurrence about 0–1%), but in the later window they co-occur in about 98% of traces, with A33–A38 before A39–A43 in about 96% of traces. [REL025–REL029, REL033–REL037, REL040–REL044, REL046–REL050, REL051–REL055]
- A30, already present in all traces, now always precedes A33–A43; before it preceded them in about half the traces (the other half had A30 without them). [REL001–REL010]
- A31, also always present, now occurs after all these activities; before it also occurred after them, but its relative position shifted later (mean position from 0.82 to 0.84). [POS002, REL011–REL020]
- The positions of A33–A38 moved earlier (mean from about 0.73 to 0.63) and A39–A43 moved later (mean from about 0.72 to 0.76). [POS003–POS012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a choice between two alternative sets of activities (A33–A38 and A39–A43) to a single path that executes both sets in sequence, with A30 first, then A33–A38, then A39–A43, and A31 last. This is directly shown by the increase in participation of these activities from about half to nearly all traces, the change from near-zero co-occurrence between the two groups to near-universal co-occurrence with a fixed order, and the consistent ordering relations. The large number of qualifying items (99 vs 1.2 expected by chance) rules out random variation. A competing explanation that the two branches were simply merged into one is essentially the same hypothesis; the evidence cannot distinguish whether the second group was added or the branch choice was removed, but the behavioural change is clear.

Other differences: A30's position moved earlier and A31's later, consistent with the reordering.
