### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reversal in the order of a group of activities: A26 and A27 now occur before A19, A20 and A25, whereas before they occurred after them. This is directly shown by the reversal of the A-before-B / B-before-A shares in the REL items, the shift of A19/A20/A25 to later positions and A26/A27 to earlier positions, and the successor changes in which A19/A20/A25 no longer lead to A26 but to A28/A32, while A45 now leads to A26 instead of to A19/A20/A25. The evidence is consistent across DIR, REL, POS and LAG indicators, and the number of qualifying items (27) far exceeds the 0.63 expected by chance. A limitation is that the full process structure is not specified, so the exact position of A28 and A32 relative to the reversed group is only partially captured, but the core order reversal is well supported.

Other differences: The successor distribution of A28 broadened from a dominant A32 to a mix of A32, A25, A19 and A20, and A27 now branches to several activities instead of a single A28. These are consistent with the same reordering rather than an independent change.
