### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a set of alternative activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) was introduced into the process, appearing in about 20% of traces and acting as an optional extra step between the previously fixed sequence A24 → A25 → A26. In the earlier window, the process had a simple path A24 → A25 → A26 with no other activities between them. In the later window, one of these new activities is executed in about 20% of traces, and it is placed either between A24 and A25 or between A25 and A26, as shown by the mixed order with A25 and the increased lags between the old activities. The new activities are mutually exclusive alternatives, never co-occurring. The large number of consistent control-flow items (34 vs 0.44 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that these activities are a rare variant that happened to appear only in the later window, but the consistency and volume of evidence argue against that. The exact placement of the new activities relative to A25 is not fixed, and the evidence does not reveal why they were introduced. [OCC001-005, DIR001-007, REL001-015, LAG001-003]
