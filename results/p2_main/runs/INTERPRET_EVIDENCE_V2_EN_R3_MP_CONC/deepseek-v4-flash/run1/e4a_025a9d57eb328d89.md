### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the timing of the process: the elapsed time between the completion of activities A16, A17, A18, A19 and the completion of A23 increased from about 1 hour to about 2 hours. This is directly shown by four TWAIT items, all with the same direction and magnitude, and with high empirical strength. The number of observed transitions is similar between windows, so the change is not an artefact of sample size. A competing explanation, such as a change in the number of cases or a shift in the distribution of other activities, is not supported by the evidence.

Other differences: Activities A39 and A40 became more frequent and more often repeated, and the routing from A05 shifted towards A03. These are separate structural changes that are also supported by the evidence, but they are not part of the main timing hypothesis.
