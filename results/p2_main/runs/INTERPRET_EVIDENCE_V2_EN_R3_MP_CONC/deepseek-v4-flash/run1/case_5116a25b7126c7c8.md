### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: the group of activities A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 moved from before A23/A24 to after them, and their outgoing edges changed from A23 to A25/A29. In the same change, A42 now leads to A23 instead of to that group. This is directly shown by the reversal of order in the REL items, the later positions of the group in POS, the changed successors in DIR, and the lag shifts in LAG. The number of qualifying items (28) far exceeds the 0.43 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A24 and A25 now have multiple successors instead of a single one, which is consistent with the same reordering but is a separate structural detail. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed windows capture a rare variant that happens to be overrepresented in the later sample, but the consistency across many indicators makes this unlikely.
