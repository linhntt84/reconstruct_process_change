Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that resource allocation changed for several activities (A01, A03, A13, A14, A17, A22): work previously distributed among many resources, especially User_39, became more concentrated on User_46, with some increase for User_61 and others. The six ASSIGN items are far more than the 0.006 expected by chance for the resource family, and they are consistent across activities.
