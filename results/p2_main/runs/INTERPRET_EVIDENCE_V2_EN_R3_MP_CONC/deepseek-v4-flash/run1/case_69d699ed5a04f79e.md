### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a sequence containing A05 and ending with A07 to a sequence without A05, with A01 added at the end, and A07 moved earlier (before A01). A04 remains but its routing changed (no longer to A05, sometimes to itself). The large number of evidence items (16 vs 0.09 expected) and the consistency across occurrence, position, routing and relation indicators support this. A competing explanation of a rare variant is ruled out by the full presence/absence in the later window.
