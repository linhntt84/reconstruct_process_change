### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A01, was inserted between the earlier activities A05/A07 and the later activity A03. In the earlier window, A05 and A07 led directly to A03; in the later window, A05 and A07 lead to A01, and A01 then leads to A03. This is directly shown by the successor changes (DIR002, DIR003), the co-occurrence and ordering relations (REL001–REL003), the appearance of A01 in every trace (OCC001), and the increased lag between A05/A07 and A03 (LAG001, LAG002). The screening expectation was 0.06 items by chance, but 12 control-flow items qualified, far exceeding chance. The evidence is consistent and based on all 200 traces in each window.

Other differences: A05 and A07 now occur earlier in the trace than before, consistent with the insertion of A01 before A03. No competing explanation is apparent; the only limitation is that the exact position of A03 itself was not directly measured, but the ordering relations fully determine the sequence.
