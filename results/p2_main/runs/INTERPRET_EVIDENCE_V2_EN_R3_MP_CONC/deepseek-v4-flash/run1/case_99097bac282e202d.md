### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed its routing after A20: instead of continuing to A21–A23, it now continues to A24–A26. This is directly shown by the swap in participation of the two groups, the shift in A20's outgoing transitions, and the mutual exclusivity relations between the groups. The change is consistent across occurrence, direction and relation indicators, and the number of qualifying items (62) far exceeds the 0.91 expected by chance. [OCC003–OCC008, DIR003, REL007–REL012, REL015–REL017, REL022–REL024, REL028–REL030]

Other differences: A10 became more frequent and moved later in the trace, and A32 now more often leads to A10, which then leads to A33. These changes may be part of the same routing shift, but they are not directly tied to the A20 branch swap in the evidence. [OCC002, POS001, DIR004, DIR002]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two groups are alternative paths whose usage changed due to a shift in case mix rather than a structural modification; however, the consistency and magnitude of the observed differences make a routing change the most plausible interpretation.
