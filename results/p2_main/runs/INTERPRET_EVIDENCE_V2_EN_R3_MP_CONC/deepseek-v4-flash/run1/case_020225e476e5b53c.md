- In the later window, activities A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 each occur about 3 times per trace on average (mean occurrences per trace ≈ 3.06–3.08) instead of about 1 time, and they repeat in about 66–68% of traces, whereas before they almost never repeated (0–0.5% of traces). [OCC002–OCC006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a group of activities (A30, A31, A36, A37, A38) that previously occurred once per case in a fixed order now occurs multiple times per case (about 3 times) and in an interleaved or repeated manner, and that the routing from A38 changed from going to A25 to going to A30. This is supported by the consistent increase in occurrence counts and recurrence, the shift from fixed order to interleaved/repeated in all pairwise relations, the increase in lags, and the change in A38's successor. The evidence is strong (29 items vs 0.6 expected by chance) and all items point to the same underlying change.

Other differences: A04 appears more often (from about 5% to 15% of traces), which is a separate, smaller change.

The evidence cannot rule out that the apparent repetition is due to a change in how the process is executed (e.g., a loop) rather than a change in the set of activities, but the direct evidence of repeated occurrences and interleaving supports the hypothesis.
