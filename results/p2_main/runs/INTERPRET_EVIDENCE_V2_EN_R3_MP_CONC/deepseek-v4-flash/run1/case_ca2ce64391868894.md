### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process replaced the initial activities A01, A02, and A03 with a single final activity A04, and the routing from A05 and A08 changed accordingly: A05 now leads directly to A04 instead of A02, and A08 now leads to A06/A07 instead of A03. The relative order of A06 and A07 shifted earlier relative to A05 and A08. This is supported by the disappearance of A01–A03 and the appearance of A04, the changed outgoing edges of A05 and A08, the earlier positions of A06 and A07, and the reduced step distances between A06/A08 and A05/A07. The evidence is all control-flow; no time or resource data are present. A competing explanation that A01–A03 still exist but were not observed in the later window is unlikely given their complete absence in all 200 traces, but cannot be entirely ruled out.
