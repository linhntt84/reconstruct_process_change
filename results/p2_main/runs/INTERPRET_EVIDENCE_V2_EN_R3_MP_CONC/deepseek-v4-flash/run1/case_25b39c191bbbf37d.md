### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 each appear in about 17–23% of traces; in the earlier window none of them appeared at all. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- These five activities are mutually exclusive alternatives: they never co‑occur with each other, and each appears alone in its trace. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL007, REL008, REL012]
- In the earlier window, A24 always led to A25 and A25 always led to A26. In the later window, A24 leads to A25 in about half of traces and to the new activities in the other half; A25 leads to A26 in about half and to the new activities in the other half. [DIR006, DIR007]
- The new activities lead to A25 or A26 in roughly equal proportions. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- The distance between A24 and A25, and between A25 and A26, increased by one step in about half of traces; the distance between A24 and A26 increased by one step in almost all traces. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- The new activities appear around the middle of the trace (position about 0.5). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a set of alternative activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) was introduced into the process, inserted between A24 and A26, with A25 still present. In the earlier window the process followed a fixed sequence A24 → A25 → A26; in the later window, A24 and A25 can branch to one of the new activities, which then lead to A25 or A26, lengthening the path by one step. The support is direct and consistent across occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators, and the number of qualifying items (34) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.44), ruling out random sampling as the explanation. A limitation is that the exact order between the new activities and A25 is not fully fixed (mixed order is observed), but the overall insertion of an alternative middle step is clearly supported.
