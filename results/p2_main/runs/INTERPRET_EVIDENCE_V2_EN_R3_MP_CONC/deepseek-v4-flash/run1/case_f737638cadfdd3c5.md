### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20, and A25 appear in about 37%, 29%, and 34% of traces respectively, whereas they were absent in the earlier window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the earlier window, A41 led to A26 in about 93% of its outgoing transitions; in the later window, A41 leads to A19 (34%), A25 (31%), and A20 (27%) instead, and to A26 in only about 1% of transitions. [DIR004]
- The new activities A19, A20, and A25 each lead to A26 in about 96–98% of their outgoing transitions in the later window. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- In the later window, A19, A20, and A25 occur before A26 and before A31 in the traces where they co-occur (e.g., A19 before A26 in 37% of traces, A19 before A31 in 37%). [REL003, REL004, REL008, REL009, REL011, REL012]
- A41 now occurs before A19, A20, and A25 in the later window (e.g., A41 before A19 in 37% of traces). [REL006, REL010, REL014]
- The gap between A41 and A26 increased: in the earlier window A41 was immediately followed by A26 in 93.5% of cases; in the later window A41 is followed by A26 after one intermediate step in about 90% of cases. [LAG002]
- The gap between A31 and A35 also shifted: the share with a lag of 2 increased from 42% to 62%. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new set of activities (A19, A20, A25) is inserted between A41 and A26, so that the direct A41→A26 path is replaced by A41→(A19/A20/A25)→A26. This is directly supported by the appearance of these activities, the change in A41's outgoing transitions, the order relations showing A41 before the new activities and the new activities before A26, and the increased lag between A41 and A26. The new activities are alternatives to each other (they do not co-occur). A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that these activities existed in the earlier window but were not observed; however, their complete absence in 200 traces makes this unlikely. Other differences: the lag between A31 and A35 increased, possibly as a consequence of the inserted activities, but this is not part of the main hypothesis.
