### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reversal of the order between two groups of activities. In the earlier window, the main activities (A20, A21, A22, A24, A25) occurred early, followed by A29, then the early-group activities (A19, A23, A26, A27, A28) late, with A34 as a successor of the early group. In the later window, the early-group activities occur first, followed by A29, then the main activities, with A34 now a successor of the main group. This is directly shown by position shifts (POS), successor changes (DIR), order reversals in co-occurring pairs (REL), and lag changes (LAG). The number of qualifying items (66) far exceeds the 0.88 expected by chance, and the items are consistent across indicators. A competing explanation of random sampling variation is ruled out by the screening comparison. A limitation is that the exact process structure (e.g., whether A34 and A29 are single activities or gateways) is not fully specified, but the behavioural reversal is clear.
