### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new block of activities (A27, A28, A33, A34, A35) is executed between A21 and A23 in about 43.5% of cases, bypassing A22, while the original A21 → A22 → A23 path is retained in the remaining cases. The new activities are executed together in those cases, with no fixed order among them. This is directly shown by the appearance of these activities (OCC), the redistribution of A21's outgoing transitions (DIR), and the relational patterns placing them after A21 and before A23 (REL). The number of qualifying items (45) far exceeds the 0.49 expected by chance, so the overall difference is not a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities are an alternative to A22 rather than a bypass, but the evidence that they always occur before A23 and that A22 is absent in those cases makes the bypass interpretation more consistent.

Other differences: A21's position shifted slightly earlier in the trace, but this is minor and not part of the main hypothesis.
