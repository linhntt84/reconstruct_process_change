### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A14, A15, and A20 each occur in about 53% of traces, down from 100% in the earlier window; they are absent from about 47% of traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the earlier window, A36 was always followed by A14; in the later window, A36 is followed by A14 in about 53% of cases and by A21 in about 47% of cases. [DIR001]
- The lag between A36 and A21 changed: in the earlier window A21 always occurred 4 positions after A36; in the later window, in about 47% of cases it occurs immediately after A36 (lag 1), and in the remaining 53% it still occurs at lag 4. [LAG001]
- Co-occurrence of A14 with A15, A20, A21, and A36 dropped from 100% to about 53% of traces; in the later window, in about 47% of traces A21 and A36 occur without A14 (and similarly for the other pairs). [REL001–REL009]
- The wait between A23 and A24 decreased from about 1 hour to about 0.5 hours (median from ~1.0h to ~0.5h). [TWAIT001]
- The wait between A36 and A14 increased from about 1 hour to about 2 hours, but this is observed only in the 106 traces (53%) where A14 still occurs. [TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, about half of the cases skip a block of activities A14, A15, and A20, so that A36 is followed directly by A21 instead of by A14 and the subsequent block. This is directly shown by the drop in participation of A14/A15/A20, the change in A36's successor from A14 to A21, the shortening of the A36-to-A21 lag from 4 to 1 in those cases, and the loss of co-occurrence between the skipped activities and A21/A36. The number of qualifying control-flow items (16) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.35), so the pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact.

Other differences: The wait between A23 and A24 halved, and the wait between A36 and A14 roughly doubled (in the remaining cases). These are timing changes independent of the structural skip.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the skipped activities are not a single block but become individually optional; however, the consistent co-occurrence pattern across all pairs suggests a single skipped segment.
