### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a shift in the routing after activity A20: in the later window, the process more often executes activities A21, A22 and A23 instead of A24, A25 and A26. Before, about 82% of traces contained A24/A25/A26 and about 19% contained A21/A22/A23; after, about 21% contain A24/A25/A26 and about 79% contain A21/A22/A23. This is directly shown by the occurrence changes, the change in A20's outgoing transition shares, and the co-occurrence patterns between A20 and the two activity groups. The new group (A21/A22/A23) also now co-occurs with each other in about 79% of traces, indicating they are executed together in the new path. The evidence count (60 items vs 0.9 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation very unlikely.

Other differences: A10 moved earlier in the trace and its outgoing transitions became more varied, but the supporting sample is small (14 after traces), so this is a weaker observation. A02 also increased slightly.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A24/A25/A26 are still possible but rarely taken; however, the consistent shift across occurrence, direction and relation indicators strongly favours a change in the preferred path after A20.
