### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from two alternative branches to a single sequence. In the earlier window, about half the traces followed a path A20 → A23 → A22 → A26, and the other half followed A21 → (A31, A32, A37, A38, A39) → A26, with the two groups never co-occurring. In the later window, all these activities occur in every trace, with A20 first, then the group A31/A32/A37/A38/A39 (in mixed order), then A23, then A21, then A22, then A26. This is directly supported by the OCC increases, the DIR changes (A20 no longer to A23, A21 only to A22, A23 becoming a successor of the group), the REL changes (co-occurrence from 0 to 1, order reversals), and the POS shifts. The large number of qualifying items (62 vs. 0.67 expected by chance) rules out a chance explanation. A competing explanation that cannot be fully excluded is that the before window sampled a different variant mix of an otherwise stable process, but the mutual exclusivity of the two groups and the consistent reordering make this unlikely.

Other differences: The internal order among A31, A32, A37, A38 and A39 remains mixed in both windows; A26 still appears after A22.
