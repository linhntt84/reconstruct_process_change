### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A14, A15 and A20 each occurred in all 200 traces (100% participation, mean 1.0 per trace). In the later window, each occurred in about 106 of 200 traces (53% participation, mean 0.53 per trace) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].
- In the earlier window, activity A36 always led to A14 (100% of its outgoing transitions). In the later window, A36 led to A14 in about 53% of transitions and to A21 in about 47% [DIR001].
- In the earlier window, A14, A15, A20, A21 and A36 co-occurred in every trace, with a fixed order: A36 before A14 before A15 before A20 before A21. In the later window, A14, A15 and A20 co-occur with A21 and with A36 in only about 53% of traces; in the remaining 47% of traces, A21 occurs without A14, A15 or A20, and A36 occurs without A14 [REL001–REL009].
- The distance between A36 and A21 was always 4 steps in the earlier window. In the later window, it was 1 step in about 47% of traces and 4 steps in about 53% [LAG001].
- A21's relative position shifted slightly earlier (mean from about 0.43 to 0.40), consistent with the direct A36→A21 path [POS001].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, about 47 in 100 cases take a direct path from A36 to A21, skipping the block A14–A15–A20, while the remaining 53% still follow the original sequence A36 → A14 → A15 → A20 → A21. This is directly shown by the drop in participation of A14, A15 and A20 from 100% to 53%, the split of A36's outgoing transitions between A14 and A21, the co-occurrence patterns (A21 and A36 appearing without the skipped activities in 47% of traces), and the lag between A36 and A21 changing from always 4 to 1 in 47% of traces. The number of qualifying items (15) far exceeds the 0.35 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A21's position in the trace became slightly earlier, which is a natural consequence of the bypass. No time or resource evidence was present.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the 47% of traces represent a separate, previously unobserved variant rather than a change in the existing process, but the consistency across all indicators and the large share of traces make this unlikely.
