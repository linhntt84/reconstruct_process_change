### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity A01 was added to the process, appearing in about half of the cases, early in the trace, and followed by several existing activities. Some existing paths were rerouted: activities A08 and A09, which previously led to A03, A10, or A16, now lead to A01 instead. This is supported by the appearance of A01 from zero participation, its outgoing edges, and the co-occurrence with many activities. The large number of evidence items (25 vs 0.43 expected) rules out chance.

Other differences: Some routing shares among existing activities changed, e.g., A03 and A16 now more often go to A02, and less to A05. These may be secondary to the insertion of A01.

Limitation: The evidence is based on a finite sample, but the magnitude and consistency of the changes strongly support the hypothesis. We cannot rule out that A01 existed but was not observed before, but that is unlikely given 200 traces.
