### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from using A04 as a start activity that precedes A03 to using A01 instead, in about half of the cases. In the earlier window, A04 appeared in about 49% of traces, always at the start and always followed by A03. In the later window, A04 is absent, and A01 appears in about 49.5% of traces, also at the start and always followed by A03. The two activities never co-occur, and the share of traces with a predecessor to A03 remains roughly constant, indicating a replacement rather than an addition. This is directly supported by the occurrence, direction, position, and relation evidence. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS002, REL001, REL002, REL003]

Other differences: A07's outgoing transitions now include A03 and A05, and A13's outgoing transitions now include A07, while their previous dominant edges (A07→A13 and A13→A03) decreased. These changes are based on about 50 traces per window and may indicate a separate restructuring of the path through A07 and A13. [DIR003, DIR004]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 and A04 are distinct activities that happen to occupy the same position and predecessor role, but the consistency of the evidence makes a replacement the most plausible interpretation.
