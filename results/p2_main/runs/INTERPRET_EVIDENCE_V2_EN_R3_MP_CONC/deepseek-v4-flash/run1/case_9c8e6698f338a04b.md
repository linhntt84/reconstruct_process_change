### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed by removing activity A17 and introducing activity A01, with a corresponding shift in the routing after A12. In the earlier window, A17 was a repeated activity (about 1.96 occurrences per trace) that followed A12 in about half of the traces and co-occurred with A02, A14, and A15. In the later window, A17 is absent, A01 appears in about 39% of traces, and A12's successors are now A16, A15, and A14 instead of A17 and A16. The large number of qualifying items (27 vs 0.3 expected by chance) and the consistency across occurrence, position, direction, and relation indicators make a real change highly likely. A competing explanation that the differences are due to random sampling is effectively ruled out by the screening comparison. However, the exact role of A01 (whether it replaces A17 in a subset of cases or is a new step) is not fully determined, and the decrease in A06 and A08 participation may be a separate effect.

Other differences: A06 and A08 participation decreased by about 20 percentage points each, which is not directly tied to the A17/A01 change. [OCC002, OCC003]
