### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering of activities: A05 and A06 moved from the end of the process (after A09) to the middle (before A07 and A09), and the order between A05 and A06 became fixed (A05 before A06) instead of mixed. This is directly shown by positional shifts (POS), relational order reversals (REL), and successor changes (DIR). The large number of qualifying items (28 vs 0.26 expected by chance) rules out chance as a whole. A competing explanation that cannot be fully excluded is that the observed differences reflect a shift in variant frequencies rather than a hard structural change, but the consistent reversal of order between A05 and A07/A09 and the fixed A05–A06 order point to a genuine reordering.

Other differences: A01 now sometimes goes directly to A05, and A04 sometimes loops back to A01 or goes to A06; these are consistent with the same reordering but are not part of the main hypothesis.
