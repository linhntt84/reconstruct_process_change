### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from having two alternative branches (one including A14 and A15, the other not) to always executing A14 and A15, with A38 always leading to A14. This is directly shown by the increase in A14/A15 participation to 100%, the change in A38's outgoing transition to always A14, and the co-occurrence of A14/A15 with A23 and A38 becoming 100%. The position and lag changes are consistent with A23 moving later because A14 and A15 are now always in between.

Other differences: A20, A21, and A22 became more frequent and now always occur with A14, but they are not part of the main branch change; they appear to be optional activities that became more common.

A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the branch still exists but A14/A15 are now always executed; however, the change in A38's outgoing transition to always A14 directly eliminates the alternative path.
