### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the ordering of a group of activities reversed: activities A19, A20, and A25 now occur later in the process, after A26, A27, and A28, whereas before they occurred before them. This is directly shown by the reversal of order in the REL items, the shift in positions in POS items, and the change in successors in DIR items. The change is consistent across many activity pairs and is far more than expected by chance (31 items vs 0.62 expected). A competing explanation of a rare variant is unlikely given the breadth of the change. However, the evidence is limited to control flow; no timing or resource changes are observed. Also, some LAG items have very low support in one window (e.g., LAG001 after only 2 traces), but the overall pattern is robust.

Other differences: A24 appears more often in the later window. A45 now leads to A26 instead of A19/A20/A25. These are consistent with the reordering but are not part of the main hypothesis.
