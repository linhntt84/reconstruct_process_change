### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A20, A21, A22, A25, A26, A27, A28, A29 and A30 each had many outgoing edges to a wide set of activities (e.g., A20 went to A25, A22, A21, A26, A27, A28, A29, A30; A25 went to A36, A27, A30, A28, and many others). In the later window, each of these activities has only a few outgoing edges: A20, A21 and A22 go only to each other and to A25; A25 goes almost exclusively to A26 (about 98% of its outgoing transitions); A26 goes to A29, A28 and A30; A27 goes mostly to A36 (about 77%); A28, A29 and A30 go only to each other (A27, A28, A29, A30). [DIR001–DIR010]
- For many pairs involving A20, A21, A22, A24 or A25 on one side and A26, A27, A28, A29 or A30 on the other, the earlier window had a mix of both orders (e.g., A20 before A26 in 22.5% of cases and A26 before A20 in 73.5%; A25 before A26 in 2.5% and A26 before A25 in 95%). In the later window, the first group always precedes the second group (e.g., A20 before A26 in 99.5%, A25 before A26 in 97.5%, A22 before A27 in 96%). [REL001–REL022]
- Activities A20, A21, A22, A24 and A25 moved earlier in the trace (mean relative position decreased, e.g., A20 from 0.61 to 0.55, A25 from 0.70 to 0.60), while A26, A27, A28, A29 and A30 moved later (e.g., A26 from 0.53 to 0.63, A28 from 0.62 to 0.68, A30 from 0.61 to 0.68). [POS001–POS010]
- The distance between the early-group activities decreased (e.g., A20–A21 now mostly 1–2 steps apart, previously spread over 1–5 steps), while the distance between early and late groups increased (e.g., A20–A26 now 2–4 steps apart, previously mostly 1 step; A20–A28 now 4–5 steps apart, previously spread over 1–5). [LAG001–LAG058]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process was restructured from a flexible, interleaved execution of a set of activities into a more sequential, ordered execution. Specifically, a group of activities (A20, A21, A22, A24, A25) now always occurs before a second group (A26, A27, A28, A29, A30), and the routing between activities became more deterministic: each activity now has fewer possible successors, often only one or two. This is directly shown by the ordering changes (REL), the position shifts (POS), the distance changes (LAG), and the reduction in outgoing edges (DIR). The number of qualifying evidence items (100) is far above the 1.01 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed windows capture two different process variants rather than a change over time, but the consistency across many independent indicators makes that less plausible.

Other differences: A35's outgoing transition changed from going to A26 and A24 in the earlier window to going almost exclusively to A24 in the later window (about 99.5% of its outgoing transitions). [DIR010] This is not part of the main hypothesis but is consistent with the general trend toward more deterministic routing.
