### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering of activities: A08 moved from before A10/A11 to after them, so the typical sequence changed from A08 → A10/A11 → A06/A07 to A10/A11 → A08 → A06/A07. This is directly shown by the successor changes (A10 and A11 now go to A08 instead of A06/A07, and A08 now goes to A06/A07) and by the consistent lag shifts: the distance between A10/A11 and A06/A07 increased, while the distance between A10/A11 and A08 and between A08 and A06/A07 decreased. The main limitation is that some lag items rest on small numbers of observations (e.g., A10→A08, A11→A08), but the successor changes are based on substantial support and are consistent with the positional shifts.

Other differences: The number of observed outgoing transitions from A08 increased from 123 to 203, which may reflect a higher frequency of A08 in the later window, but this is not directly measured by an occurrence indicator.
