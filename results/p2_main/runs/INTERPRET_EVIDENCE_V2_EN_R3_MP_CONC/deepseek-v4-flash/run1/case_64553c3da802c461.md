### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which the process now almost always executes A22 after A21, and almost never executes the set of activities A27, A28, A33, A34, A35 that were previously executed in about half the traces. The dominant path in the later window is A21 → A22 → A23, whereas in the earlier window about half the traces followed A21 → (one of A27, A28, A33, A34, A35) → A23. This is supported by the large increase in A22 participation, the corresponding decrease in the other activities, the shift in A21's outgoing transitions, and the co-occurrence patterns showing A21 before A22 and A22 before A23. The alternative activities are now observed in only about 1% of traces, so their exact routing is based on very few observations, but the overall shift is clear from the 200-trace co-occurrence data. Other differences: A21's position in the trace shifted slightly later (mean from 0.46 to 0.49) and A23's slightly earlier (mean from 0.59 to 0.56), but these are minor. [POS001, POS002] A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the alternative activities still exist but are rarely executed; the evidence cannot distinguish between their removal and a very low execution frequency.
