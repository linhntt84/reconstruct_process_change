### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a major restructuring of the process: activities A01 and A17 were removed, A07 was added, and the routing among the remaining activities changed substantially, with A02 becoming much more repeated and A06/A16 forming a mandatory loop. This is directly shown by the disappearance of A01 and A17, the appearance of A07, the complete change in successor sets for A02, A03, A04, A10, A12, A13, A14, and the new fixed adjacency between A06 and A16. The large number of qualified items (171 vs. 0.94 expected by chance) rules out random sampling variation as the explanation.

Other differences: A14 and A15 increase in participation and repetition, and A02–A04 ordering becomes more interleaved rather than fixed. These are consistent with the restructuring but are not part of the main hypothesis. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a shift in case mix rather than a change in the process itself, since the evidence does not include case attributes.
