### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A20, A21, A22, A24, A25 typically occurred before A26, A27, A28, A29, A30. For example, A20 preceded A26 in about 99% of cases, and A25 preceded A26 in about 99.5% of cases. In the later window, these orders reversed: A26 preceded A20 in about 73% of cases, and A26 preceded A25 in about 93% of cases. [REL001, REL018, REL005, REL010]
- The relative position of the first group shifted later: the mean position of A20 increased from about 0.56 to 0.61, A25 from 0.61 to 0.70. The second group shifted earlier: A26 from 0.63 to 0.55, A28 from 0.68 to 0.62, A30 from 0.68 to 0.62. [POS001, POS005, POS006, POS008, POS010]
- The distance between the two groups changed: pairs like A20-A26 were previously 2–4 steps apart, now often 1 step apart; pairs like A20-A21 were previously 1–2 steps apart, now often 3–5 steps apart. [LAG004, LAG001]
- The outgoing transitions of many activities became much more diverse. For example, A25 previously went to A26 in 100% of cases; in the later window it goes to A36 (37%), A27 (16%), A29 (14%), A30 (8%), A28 (8%), and many others. A35 previously went to A24 in 100% of cases; later to A24 (53%) and A26 (45%). [DIR005, DIR010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process order between two groups of activities reversed: activities A20, A21, A22, A24, A25, which previously occurred before A26, A27, A28, A29, A30, now tend to occur after them, or at least the order is no longer fixed. This is directly shown by the reversal of order in many REL pairs (e.g., A20-A26, A25-A26, A21-A28), the shift in mean positions (first group later, second group earlier), and the change in lag distances (second group now closer to the start). The DIR changes indicate a broader restructuring of the process flow, with many new successor relations appearing. The large number of evidence items (98 vs 0.94 expected by chance) rules out chance as an explanation.

Other differences: The process appears to have become more complex, with many more possible successors for each activity and less fixed ordering between activities. This is a separate observation but consistent with the main hypothesis.

Limitation: The evidence is based on a single case with 200 traces per window; the reversal is consistent across many pairs, but we cannot rule out that the process changed in a more complex way than a simple reversal, e.g., a complete redefinition of the process flow.
