### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a restructuring of the process: activity A14 was removed and activity A01 was introduced, with a corresponding reduction in the role of A05. In the earlier window A14 was mandatory and many activities led to it; in the later window A14 is absent, A01 appears in a majority of traces, and the same activities now lead to A01 or to other successors. The number of qualifying control-flow items (129) is far above the 0.98 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two windows represent different process variants rather than a temporal change, but the consistency of the A14/A01 replacement across many activities makes that less plausible.

Other differences: A11's successor changed from A05 to A16, and A05's successor changed from A16 to A13; these are consistent with the broader re-routing but are not part of the main A14/A01 replacement. [DIR003, DIR008]
