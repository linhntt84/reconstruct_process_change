### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A27, A36, A37, and A42 each appear in about half the traces (47–53%) instead of essentially all traces before (100%); their mean occurrences per trace dropped from about 1.0 to about 0.5. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

- A26's outgoing transitions shifted from A27 in 99% of cases to A27 in about 52% and A37, A42, A36 each in about 13–16%. [DIR002]

- The lag between A26 and A31 changed from 2 steps in 97.5% of traces to 2 steps in 53% and 4 steps in 42.5%. [LAG005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in process structure: A25 now leads directly to A28 (immediately in about 96% of cases), and A36, A37, and A42 have become optional activities that occur in about half the cases, appearing later in the trace. A26 now sometimes leads to these optional activities instead of always to A27. A31 is always present and appears later, likely on the main path after A28, though its direct predecessor is not directly evidenced. This is supported by the large drop in participation of A36/A37/A42, the new immediate A25→A28 link, the new A31 successor for A36/A37/A42, and the co-occurrence drops. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a sampling artifact, but the number of qualifying items (48 vs 0.73 expected by chance) makes this unlikely.

Other differences: The order between A26 and A36/A37/A42 reversed, and A28 now precedes A36/A37/A42 in about half the co-occurring traces, whereas before it followed them. These are consistent with the main hypothesis but are not part of the core claim.
