### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a linear sequence (A25 → A31 → A20, with A26 also occurring once) to a loop in which A31 can return to A25, causing A25, A26, and A31 to repeat and interleave. The repetition of these activities, the new A31→A25 edge, the interleaving of A25 and A31, and the loosened distance between them all point to the same structural change. The main limitation is that the exact control flow is not directly observed, but the combination of indicators is consistent and strong.
