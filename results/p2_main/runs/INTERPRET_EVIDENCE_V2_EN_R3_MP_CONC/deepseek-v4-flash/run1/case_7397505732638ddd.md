### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, each of the activities A15, A17, A18, A19, A20, A21, A26 had exactly one observed successor, forming a strict chain (e.g., A15→A18, A18→A19, A19→A21, A21→A20, A20→A27). In the later window, each of these activities has multiple successors; for example, A18 now goes to A27 in about half of cases (0.515) instead of only A19, and A15 now goes to A18 (0.675) plus A19, A20, and A21. [DIR001, DIR003]
- The relative order of many activity pairs reversed or became mixed. For instance, A18 before A19 in all cases before, but after A19 before A18 in 85% of cases; A15 before A19 in all cases before, after A19 before A15 in 74%; A15 before A21 in all cases before, after roughly half each way. [REL001, REL003, REL006]
- The distance between activities in the trace changed: many pairs that were at a fixed lag now show a spread of lags. For example, A15 and A18 were always adjacent (lag 1), now lag 1 in 68% and lag 2 in 22%; A15 and A27 were always 5 steps apart, now spread across lags 2–5. [LAG001, LAG005]
- The positions of activities shifted: A15, A17, A18 occur later in the trace (mean position increased by about 0.05, 0.03, 0.06 respectively), while A19, A20, A21 occur earlier (mean decreased by about 0.06, 0.03, 0.04). [POS001, POS003, POS004, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a fixed sequential order to a more flexible ordering with alternative paths. Specifically, the activities A15, A17, A18, A19, A20, A21, A26, and A27 were previously executed in a strict linear order (A26→A17→A15→A18→A19→A21→A20→A27). In the later window, this order is no longer fixed: many pairs that were strictly ordered now appear in both orders, activities have multiple successors, and the distances between them vary. The change is consistent across DIR, REL, LAG, and POS indicators, all based on 200 traces per window. A competing explanation that this is due to random sampling is ruled out by the screening: 45 control-flow items were flagged when only about 0.5 would be expected by chance. The main limitation is that the evidence does not indicate why the order changed or whether it is a permanent drift, but the behavioural change itself is well supported.

Other differences: The direct transition A18→A27 appears in the later window, bypassing A19, A21, and A20 (DIR003). Also A26 now sometimes goes to A19 instead of only A17 (DIR007). These are part of the same loosening of the sequence.
