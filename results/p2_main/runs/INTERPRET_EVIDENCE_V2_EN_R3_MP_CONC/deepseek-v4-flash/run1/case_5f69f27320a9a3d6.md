### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process was restructured by removing activity A04 and introducing activity A01, with a corresponding change in routing: A03 now leads directly to A07 instead of through A04, and A01 is inserted early in the flow (after A02) with branches to A03, A05 and A06. This is supported by the complete appearance/disappearance of A01 and A04, the successor changes for A01, A03 and A04, and the relational and positional evidence showing A01 co-occurring with the other activities and A04 no longer doing so. The number of qualifying items (34) vastly exceeds the 0.17 expected by chance, so the overall pattern cannot be attributed to random sampling. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 and A04 are the same logical step renamed, but their positions differ (A01 early, A04 late) and A01 has multiple successors while A04 had one, making this unlikely.

Other differences: The order between A01 and A05/A06 is mixed (about half in each direction), so A01 does not have a fixed order with those activities. The changes in lags (e.g., A02–A03 increased, A03–A07 decreased) are consistent with the insertion of A01 and removal of A04.
