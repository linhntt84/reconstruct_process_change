### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of two blocks of activities: the block containing A14, A16 and A19 (together with A20) now occurs after the block containing A15, A17 and A18, whereas before it occurred before them. This is shown directly by the reversed order in many activity pairs, the large positional shifts, and the changed successor relations. The number of control-flow items (39) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.46), so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A24 and A35 changed their outgoing transitions, and the step distances between A24–A25 and A35–A20 changed. Waiting times between A02–A08 and A06–A08 increased. These are separate from the main reordering hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a shift in the frequencies of existing variants rather than a true structural reordering, but the complete reversal of order for pairs like A15–A20 (from 100% one direction to 100% the other) makes a simple frequency shift unlikely.
