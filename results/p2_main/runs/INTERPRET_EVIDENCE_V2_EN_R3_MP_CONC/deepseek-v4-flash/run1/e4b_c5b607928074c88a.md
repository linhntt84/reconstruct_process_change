### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: for activities A01, A03, A13, A14, A17 and A22, work previously done by a set of resources including User_65 and User_80 is now done by a different set led by User_69, with several other resources also taking on work. The pattern is consistent across all six activities, and the resource family produced 6 items when only 0.005 were expected by chance. [ASSIGN001-006, screening]

Other differences: The elapsed time between A04 and A01 and between A09 and A23 decreased substantially in the later window (means down by about 12h and 6.5h respectively); the time family produced 2 items vs 0.016 expected by chance. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

Limitation: The resource change could reflect a re-encoding of resource identifiers (the same people under new codes) rather than a genuine change in who performs the work; the evidence cannot rule this out. The timing change could be a separate phenomenon or a consequence of the resource change, but the evidence does not link them.
