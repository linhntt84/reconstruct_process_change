### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A25, A26, and A31 each occur on average about 1.0 times per trace instead of about 3.4 times, and the share of traces with more than one occurrence dropped from about 75% to about 0.5% (participation remains 100% in both windows). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the later window, after activity A31 the next activity is A20 in about 96% of observed transitions instead of about 29% before; the share going to A25 dropped from about 71% to about 4%. [DIR001]
- In the later window, A25 and A31 still co-occur in every trace, but the order is almost always A25 before A31 (about 99.5%) instead of the earlier pattern where about 75% of traces had them interleaved or repeated and 25% had A25 before A31. [REL001]
- The distance between A25 and A31 shrank: in the later window, A25 is followed by A31 within 2 steps in about 92% of traces, compared with about 12% before; the reverse order (A31 before A25) became negligible (only 1 trace in the later window). [LAG001, LAG002]
- The position of A25 and A31 within traces shifted slightly earlier and became more concentrated (e.g., A25 median position from 0.43 to 0.40; A31 median from 0.48 to 0.48, but interquartile range narrowed). [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a specific structural change: activities A25, A26, and A31 stopped repeating within traces and now occur once each, with A25 always before A31, and A31 then leading to A20 instead of back to A25. This is directly shown by the drop in recurrence for all three activities, the shift in A31's outgoing transition from A25 to A20, the change from interleaving to a fixed A25-before-A31 order, and the reduced lag between A25 and A31. The evidence is consistent across multiple indicators and based on 200 traces in each window.

Other differences: The position distributions of A25 and A31 became slightly earlier and tighter, which is consistent with the removal of repetitions rather than an independent change. [POS001, POS002]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the change occurred gradually over the later window rather than as a single switch, but the direction and consistency of the evidence are clear.
