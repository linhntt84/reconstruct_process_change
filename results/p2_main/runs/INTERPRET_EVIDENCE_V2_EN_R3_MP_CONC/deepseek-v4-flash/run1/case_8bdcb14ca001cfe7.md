### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the routing around A15, A13 and A09: in the later window, A15 and A13 both lead predominantly to A09 (instead of A15 leading to A01 and A13 leading to A15), and A09 is executed more often and repeated more. This is directly shown by the successor shifts in DIR006, DIR004, DIR001 and DIR002, and by the increased occurrence and recurrence of A09 in OCC002. The increased interleaving between A09 and A13 (REL003) and the lag changes (e.g., A15–A01 gap widening, A09–A01 gap widening) are consistent with this re‑routing.

Other differences: A03 and A14 become less frequent and no longer repeat, and A11 appears earlier and co‑occurs with A03 and A14 in an ordered way (A11 before them). A14’s successor shifts from A11 to A12. These changes are not part of the main routing hypothesis but are clearly present.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a rare variant that became dominant in the later sample; however, the large number of qualifying items (29 vs. 0.43 expected by chance) and the consistency across multiple indicators make this unlikely.
