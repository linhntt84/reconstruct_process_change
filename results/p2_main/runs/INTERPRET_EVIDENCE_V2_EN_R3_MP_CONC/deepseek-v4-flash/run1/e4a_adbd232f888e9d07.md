### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: A14, A15 and A20 moved from before A21/A22 to after them, and A40 now goes directly to A21 instead of to A14/A15/A20. This is directly shown by the reversal of order relations (REL), the shift in positions (POS), the change in successors (DIR), and the lag changes (LAG). The control-flow evidence is far above the chance expectation (24 items vs 0.4 expected), so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. The timing change (TWAIT) is a separate difference: the gap between A31 and A22 halved, but it is not linked to the structural change by the evidence.

Other differences: The timing between A31 and A22 decreased by about half. [TWAIT001] A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed reordering is accompanied by other unmeasured changes, or that the timing change is independent of the structural change.
