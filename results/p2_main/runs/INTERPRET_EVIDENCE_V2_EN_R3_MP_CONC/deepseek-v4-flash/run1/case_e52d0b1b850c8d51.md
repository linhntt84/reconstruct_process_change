### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control-flow structure: the previously mandatory sequence involving A06, A19, A20, and A25 became optional, and the routing from A14 and A41 changed. In the earlier window, A14 almost always led to A06 (69% of transitions) and A06 to A15 (92%), and A41 almost always led to A19 (96%). In the later window, A14 leads to A15 in 78% of transitions and to A06 in only 12%; A06 leads to A15 in only 37% and to a variety of other activities; A41 leads to A26 in 39% and to A19 in 47%. Correspondingly, A06, A19, A20, and A25 appear in far fewer traces (A06 from 71% to 26%; A19, A20, A25 from 100% to about 53–54%), and their cooccurrence with each other and with A14 drops sharply. This is consistent with a process that now often bypasses these activities. The increase in A04, A08, and A10 (from about 5% to 15–20% participation) suggests additional or alternative activities, but their role is not clear. A competing explanation is that the later window contains two distinct process variants, one similar to the old and one new, rather than a single modified process; the evidence cannot rule this out.
