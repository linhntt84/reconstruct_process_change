### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that A30 and A36 changed from single occurrences to repeated occurrences (about 3.5 per trace), and the previously fixed order A14 before A30 before A36 (with A05 sometimes before them) became interleaved with the repetitions, so that A14 and A05 no longer strictly precede A30/A36.
