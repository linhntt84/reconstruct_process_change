### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which activity A11 is executed much more often and earlier, typically shortly after A06, while A09 also becomes more frequent and earlier, with A09 and A11 acting as mutually exclusive alternatives that now appear in every case (previously some cases had neither). A08 no longer consistently precedes A11 and moves later in the process. This is directly shown by the increased participation and earlier position of A11, the shift of A06's outgoing transitions toward A11 and A09, the shortened A06–A11 lag, the new co-occurrence of A06 and A11, and the loss of a fixed A08-before-A11 order. The main limitation is that some routing changes (e.g., from A05, A09, A11) rest on small numbers of observations in the earlier window, but the core A06–A11 relationship is well supported.

Other differences: A05's outgoing transitions shifted from A01/A03 to A09/A11, and A09's outgoing transitions became more diverse, but both have limited support before. These are consistent with the main hypothesis but not required for it. A competing explanation that the observed windows sampled different pre-existing variants cannot be fully ruled out, but the magnitude and consistency of the changes make that unlikely.
