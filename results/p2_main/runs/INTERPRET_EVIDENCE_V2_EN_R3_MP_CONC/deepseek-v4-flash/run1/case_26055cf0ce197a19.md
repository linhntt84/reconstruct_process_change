### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering of activities in the main loop: the sequence A01 → A07 → A05 → A01 appears to have been replaced by A01 → A05 → A07 → A01, with A06 as an alternative to A05 after A01. This is directly shown by the successor shifts (A01 no longer leads to A07, A05 now leads to A07, A07 now leads to A01) and by the lag changes (A01–A05 and A01–A06 become closer, A01–A07 becomes more distant). A03 and A04 now feed into A01 instead of A07, consistent with the same reordering. The large number of qualifying items (35 vs. 0.29 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely.

Other differences: A01 increased in participation and repetition, while A07 decreased, which may reflect a shift in how often the loop is entered or repeated, but this is secondary to the ordering change. A limitation is that A06's outgoing transitions before the change are based on only 11 observations, so some details of A06's role are less certain.
