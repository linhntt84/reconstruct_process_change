### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process changed its routing after A15, shifting from predominantly executing a branch containing A16, A17, and A18 to predominantly executing a branch containing A19, A20, and A21. A25 follows the chosen branch. This is supported by the consistent drop in participation of A16–A18 and rise of A19–A21, the shift in A15's outgoing transitions, and the co-occurrence patterns. The lag change for A15–A20 is consistent with A20 now being more directly after A15.

Limitations: The evidence is based on 200 traces per window; the change could reflect a different case mix, but the consistency across many indicators makes that unlikely. The cause of the shift is not inferable from this evidence.
