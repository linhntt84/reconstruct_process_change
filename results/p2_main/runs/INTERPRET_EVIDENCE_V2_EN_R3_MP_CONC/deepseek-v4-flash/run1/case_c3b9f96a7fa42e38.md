### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: A26 and A27 now occur before A35, A36, and A41, whereas before they occurred after them; A30 now occurs after A35/A36/A41 instead of directly after A26. This is directly shown by the reversed relative orders (REL), the shifted positions (POS), the changed successor distributions (DIR), and the changed lags (LAG). The number of qualifying items (33) far exceeds the 0.76 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact. A separate change is that A06 became less frequent and moved later, but this is not part of the main reordering hypothesis.

Other differences: A06 participation decreased and its successors became more varied; A02 participation increased, though with limited support.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed reordering reflects a change in the mix of variants rather than a single uniform process change, but the consistency across multiple indicators makes this unlikely.
