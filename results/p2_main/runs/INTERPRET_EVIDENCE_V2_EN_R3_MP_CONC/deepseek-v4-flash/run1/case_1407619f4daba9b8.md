### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a branching structure with two alternative paths to a single sequential path. In the earlier window, after A20 and A21, the process split into either a path containing A22 or a path containing the group A31/A32/A37, with A23 occurring early (right after A20) and A26 occurring late. In the later window, A20 leads to A31/A32/A37, which are then followed by A23, A21, A22 and A26 in sequence; A22 is no longer an alternative but occurs after the group, and A23 is now later, separated from A20 by four steps. This is directly shown by the successor changes (DIR001, DIR002, DIR003-005), the order reversals in REL006-019, the co-occurrence increases in REL001-004 and REL020-022, and the lag shift in LAG001. The number of qualifying items (41) far exceeds the 0.49 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed windows come from different case populations rather than a change over time, but the internal consistency of the relational and positional evidence makes this less likely. Other differences, such as A21 now always leading to A22 and A26's position relative to the group, are part of the same sequential reorganisation rather than separate changes.
