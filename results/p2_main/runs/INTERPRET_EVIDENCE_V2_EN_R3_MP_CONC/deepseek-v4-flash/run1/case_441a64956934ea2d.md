### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a shift from A01 to A13 as a central activity in the process. A01 is executed less often, later, and less connected to other activities, while A13 is executed more often, earlier, and more connected. This is consistent across occurrence, transition, position, co-occurrence and lag indicators.

Other differences: A06 now receives from A01 instead of A13, and A13 no longer leads to A06. [DIR004, DIR006] A01's own repetition lag changed from 3 to 2, and A13's from 2 to 3. [LAG001, LAG012] These are consistent with the same shift.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 and A13 are alternative paths whose frequencies swapped, but the positional and relational changes point to a replacement rather than a simple frequency shift.
