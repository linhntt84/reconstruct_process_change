### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed from having a branch that included activities A02, A03, and A08 (and often A07, A14) to a branch that instead uses A10, A11, and related activities. In the earlier window, about 47% of traces contained A02, A08, and A07, and A03 in about 21%; in the later window, A02, A03, and A08 are absent, and A07 and A14 are much less frequent. The successor of A01 changed from A06/A07/A14 to A11, and A10 now leads to A01 instead of A08, indicating a re-routing of the main flow. This is supported by the disappearance of A02/A03/A08 in OCC, POS, and DIR, and by the co-occurrence and lag changes.

Other differences: A07 and A14 still occur but less often; A05, A09, A10, A11, A17, A18, and A19 appear or increase in the later window. These are consistent with the replacement of the old branch.

Limitation: The evidence does not show why the change happened, and we cannot rule out that the later window is a different process variant rather than a change over time, but the consistency across many indicators supports a structural change.
