### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a timing change: in the later window, the time between activity A09 and each of activities A03–A08 is consistently about half of what it was earlier, with reduced variability. This is directly shown by six independent wait-time indicators, all with very high empirical strength, and the number of time items (6) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.022). The change appears uniform across all six activity pairs, suggesting a systematic reduction in the elapsed time around A09 rather than an isolated effect. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is a change in how completion times are recorded, but the consistency and magnitude make a real timing shift the most plausible interpretation. No structural or resource changes are indicated by the evidence.

Other differences: The frequency of waits between A05 and A09 decreased and between A07 and A09 increased, but these are minor and not part of the main timing hypothesis.
