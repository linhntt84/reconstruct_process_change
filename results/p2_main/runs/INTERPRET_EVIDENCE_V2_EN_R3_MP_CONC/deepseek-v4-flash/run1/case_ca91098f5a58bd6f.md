### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed by removing the alternative activities A14, A15 and A20 that used to be executed between A36 and A21 (and A26). In the later window, A36 leads directly to A21, and A14/A15/A20 no longer appear. This is supported by the complete disappearance of these activities, the change in A36's outgoing transitions from A14/A15/A20 to A21, and the lag between A36 and A21 shrinking from two positions to one. The evidence is strong (28 items vs 0.41 expected by chance). A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A14/A15/A20 are still possible but were not observed in the later sample; however, the direct A36→A21 path and the lag change make removal the most plausible interpretation.

Other differences: A19 no longer co-occurs with the removed activities and appears alone more often; slight position shifts of A21 and A36 are consistent with the removal but are not part of the main hypothesis.
