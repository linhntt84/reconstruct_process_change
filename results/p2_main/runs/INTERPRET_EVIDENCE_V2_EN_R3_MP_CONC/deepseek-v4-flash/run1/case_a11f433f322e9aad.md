### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A26 was followed by A27 in about 99% of traces, and A27 was followed by A28 in about 99% of traces; A27 occurred in every trace. [DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, REL001, REL005]
- In the later window, A27 occurs in about half of traces (51%), and A26's outgoing transitions split: about 50% to A27, and about 17% to A33, 16% to A38, 15% to A32. [OCC001, DIR001]
- Activities A32, A33, A38 were absent in the earlier window and appear in about half of traces in the later window (49–50%). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A28 remains present in all traces in both windows; in the later window it is often reached without A27 (about 49% of traces have A28 without A27). [REL005]
- The distance between A26 and A28 increased: in the earlier window the lag was 2 steps in about 98% of traces; in the later window it is 2 steps in about 50% and 4 steps in about 48%. [LAG001]
- The new activities A32, A33, A38 appear after A26 and before A28, and they occur in mixed order relative to each other (e.g., A32 before A33 in about 23% and after in about 27% of traces). [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new alternative path was introduced between A26 and A28, using activities A32, A33, and A38, while the original path through A27 continues in about half of the cases. The earlier window showed a single dominant path A26 → A27 → A28 in nearly all traces; the later window shows A26 still always present and A28 always present, but A27 only in about half, with A32/A33/A38 appearing in the other half and leading to A28. The increase in the A26–A28 lag from 2 to 4 steps in about half of the traces is consistent with the insertion of these activities. The evidence is direct and consistent across occurrence, direction, relation, and lag indicators. A competing explanation that A27 was simply replaced is not supported because A27 still occurs in about half of the traces and the new activities are alternatives to A27 (they rarely co-occur with it). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001]

Other differences: A27's outgoing transitions now include small shares to A09, A34, and A25 (about 1–2% each), which were not present before. [DIR002] The new activities A32, A33, A38 have no fixed order among themselves, appearing in both orders in similar proportions. [REL012, REL013, REL014]
