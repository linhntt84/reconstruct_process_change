### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering and routing of activities: A26 and A27 moved earlier relative to a group of activities (A35, A36, A41, A42, A43), and the successor of A25 changed from that group to A27, while A26's successor changed from A30 to that group. This is directly shown by the reversal of order between A26/A27 and the group (REL011–REL020) and by the position shifts (POS002–POS008), as well as by the successor changes (DIR003–DIR009). Additionally, A02 participation dropped sharply, and A06 increased.

Other differences: A46's successor changed from A02 to A19/A20, and A46 became closer to A19, A20, A25. A06 increased in participation.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that some of the changes reflect sampling variation in rare activities (e.g., A02 after only 20 traces, many LAG items have very low after support), but the core reordering is supported by items with full support.
