### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A01, was inserted between A08 and A10 in a substantial share of cases, replacing the direct A08→A10 path. This is shown directly by the successor changes (A08 now leads to A01 instead of A10, and A01 leads to A10), the appearance of A01 in the middle of traces, the co-occurrence of A01 with both A08 and A10, and the increase in the step distance between A08 and A10 from 1 to 2 steps. The number of evidence items (9) far exceeds the 0.13 expected by chance, and the items are consistent with a single structural insertion.

Other differences: The A03/A12 loop was removed. In the earlier window A12 led to A03 and A03 led back to A12; in the later window both A12 and A03 lead to A13, so the loop no longer exists. This change is separate from the A01 insertion and is supported by DIR002 and DIR004.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 is a rare variant that happened to appear in the later window, but the consistency and volume of evidence make this unlikely.
