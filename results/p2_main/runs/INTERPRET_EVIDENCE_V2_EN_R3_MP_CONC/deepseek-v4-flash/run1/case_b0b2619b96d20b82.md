### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering of activities A01, A04, and A06. In the earlier window the typical sequence was A04 → A01 → A06 (with A06 also able to go to A05), while in the later window it is A01 → A04 → A06 (with A06 also able to go to A05). At the same time, A01 became much less frequent and A04 much more frequent, consistent with the same reordering. The evidence is strong: 16 control-flow items were flagged against an expected 0.1 by chance, and all items point in the same direction. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a rare variant that happens to dominate the later sample, but the consistency across multiple indicators makes this unlikely.
