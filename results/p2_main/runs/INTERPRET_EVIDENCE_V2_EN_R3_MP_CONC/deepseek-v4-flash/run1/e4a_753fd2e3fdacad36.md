### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process was reordered: a block of activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) now occurs after A23 and A24, whereas before it occurred before them, and the routing from A42 changed to go to A23 instead of to that block. This is directly shown by the reversal of order in REL items, the later position of these activities in POS items, and the change in outgoing transitions in DIR items. The timing change (halving of elapsed times between A09 and A03–A08) is a separate difference not part of this hypothesis.

Other differences: The elapsed time between consecutive completions of A09 and each of A03–A08 halved from about 1 hour to about 0.5 hour. [TWAIT001–TWAIT006]

A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a change in the mix of case types rather than a change in the process itself, but the consistency and volume of evidence make this less likely.
