### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that resource allocation changed: work that was previously spread across many resources became concentrated on a smaller set, with User_56 and User_46 taking on larger shares for activities A01, A03, A13, A14, and A17, and activity A16 becoming exclusively performed by User_64. The resource family shows 7 evidence items against 0.005 expected by chance, far exceeding the chance expectation, and the changes are consistent across activities. [ASSIGN001-006, CONC001]

Other differences: The elapsed time between A04 and A01 increased substantially in the later window (median from ~5.6 to ~17.6 hours). This is a separate timing change, supported by one time item (TWAIT001) against 0.012 expected by chance. [TWAIT001]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the resource changes reflect a re-labelling of resources (e.g., a new user ID) rather than a change in actual allocation, but the consistent pattern across many activities and the extreme concentration for A16 make this less likely.
