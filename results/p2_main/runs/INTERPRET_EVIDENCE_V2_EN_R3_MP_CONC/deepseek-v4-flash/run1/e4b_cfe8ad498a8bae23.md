### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: activities are now assigned to a different set of resources, and A16 in particular became fully concentrated on a single resource. This is directly shown by the assignment and concentration indicators across multiple activities, with far more items than expected by chance. The timing change between A07 and A13 is also present but rests on few observations; it is reported as an other difference.

Other differences: The wait time between A07 and A13 decreased substantially, but the support is limited (6 vs 17 traces) and it is a single item, so it is a weaker separate observation.

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the resource changes are accompanied by unobserved changes in case composition, but the direct evidence does not require that.
