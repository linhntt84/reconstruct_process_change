### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from executing a set of activities A16, A17, A18 after A15 to executing A19, A20, A21 after A15, with A25 now associated with the new set. This is directly shown by the drop in participation of A16–A18 and rise of A19–A21, the shift in A15's outgoing transitions, and the corresponding changes in co-occurrence and ordering. The number of control-flow items (35) far exceeds the 0.53 expected by chance, and the patterns are consistent across indicators. A competing explanation that the two groups are alternative variants whose frequencies changed cannot be fully ruled out, but the evidence strongly favours a structural change.
