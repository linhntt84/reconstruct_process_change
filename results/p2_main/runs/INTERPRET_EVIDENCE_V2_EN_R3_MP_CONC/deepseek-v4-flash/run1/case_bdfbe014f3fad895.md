### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new alternative path is introduced in the later window: A14 now often goes to A04 before continuing to A15, and A26 now often goes to A32/A33/A38 before continuing to A28, while the previous direct A14→A15 and A26→A27→A28 paths still occur in about half of the cases. A27 becomes optional (present in about half of traces), and A04, A32, A33 and A38 appear as new activities. This is directly shown by the changes in outgoing transitions (DIR004, DIR006, DIR001), co-occurrence and order (REL001, REL002, REL017, REL018, REL021, REL025), and lag distributions (LAG001, LAG002, LAG003), together with the occurrence and position changes (OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, POS001). The screening count (55 items vs. 0.73 expected by chance) makes it very unlikely that these differences are random sampling noise.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities A32, A33 and A38 are not a single ordered block: their mutual order is mixed (both orders appear in similar proportions), so the exact internal sequence among them is not fixed. Also, the decrease of A06 (OCC002) is not clearly connected to this main path and may reflect a separate change.

Other differences: A06 participation drops from about 20% to 4% [OCC002].
