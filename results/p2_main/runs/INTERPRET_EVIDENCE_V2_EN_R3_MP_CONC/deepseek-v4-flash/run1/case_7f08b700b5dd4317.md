- In the earlier window, activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38, and A39 each appeared in essentially every trace (participation 1.0, mean occurrences ~1.0). In the later window, each appears in about 46–56% of traces (A33: 46.5%, A35: 46.5%, A36: 47%, A37: 56.5%, A38: 56.5%, A39: 56%). [OCC001–OCC006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change from a single path that always executed all six activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38, A39 (with flexible order within subgroups) to two alternative branches: one branch executes A33, A35, A36 (then A31), the other executes A37, A38, A39 (then A31). A30 and A31 remain always present. This is supported by the halving of participation for all six activities, the near-zero co-occurrence between the two groups while within-group co-occurrence remains high, the change in A30's outgoing distribution to include all six, and the shortening of lags between A30 and the second group and between the first group and A31. A competing explanation that the process simply skips some activities in some traces is ruled out by the fact that activities from the two groups almost never appear together, indicating they are alternatives rather than optional additions. Limitations: the evidence is based on 200 traces per window, and the exact order within each branch remains flexible.

Other differences: A30's position shifted slightly later (mean from 0.62 to 0.67) and A31's position slightly earlier (0.82 to 0.80), but these are minor and consistent with the branch structure. [POS001, POS002]
