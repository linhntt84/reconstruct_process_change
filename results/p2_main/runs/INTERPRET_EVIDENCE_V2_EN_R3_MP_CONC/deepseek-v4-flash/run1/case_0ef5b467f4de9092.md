### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change: the process switched from executing a set of activities A21–A25 to a set A16–A20, and the path from A15 now leads to A19 instead of A24. In the earlier window, about 81.5% of traces contained A21–A25 and A15 led to A24; in the later window, about 76.5% contain A16–A20 and A15 leads to A19. This is directly shown by the participation changes, the successor change, and the co-occurrence patterns. The large number of qualifying items (80 vs 0.84 expected) rules out chance as a whole. A competing explanation that the observed differences are due to sampling variation is unlikely given the consistency and magnitude. The main limitation is that we cannot infer the cause or intention behind the change.

Other differences: The lag distributions shifted for several pairs, but these are consistent with the same structural change (different activities involved). No time or resource evidence was provided.
