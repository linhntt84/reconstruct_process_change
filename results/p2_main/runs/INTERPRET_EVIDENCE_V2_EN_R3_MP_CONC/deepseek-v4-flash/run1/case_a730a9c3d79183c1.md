### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which a set of activities (A33, A35, A36, A37, A38, A39) became almost always executed together, while A04 became much less frequent, and A31 is no longer a common successor of that set. This is supported by the large increase in participation and co-occurrence of those activities, the drop in A04, and the routing changes away from A31. The lag changes indicate these activities are now spread over more steps, consistent with a different ordering or additional intervening steps.

Other differences: A02, A08, A10 increased in participation; A30's routing shifted from a balanced distribution to a preference for A35, A33, A36; A04's position shifted later in the trace. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is a change in case mix, but the consistency across many indicators makes a structural change in the process more likely.
