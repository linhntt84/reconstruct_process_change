### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that a set of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) were inserted between A43 and A28 in the later window. In the earlier window, A43 led directly to A28 in about 95% of cases; in the later window, A43 instead leads to these five activities (each about 20% of the time), and each of them then leads to A28. This is directly shown by the successor changes (DIR007, DIR002–DIR006) and by the lag shift between A43 and A28 from one step to two steps (LAG002). The appearance of these activities in the later window (OCC002–OCC006) and their positions around 0.38–0.41 (POS002–POS006) are consistent with an inserted block. The evidence is strong: 50 control-flow items were flagged against 0.68 expected by chance, and the items are mutually consistent.

Other differences: A06 became more frequent (9% to 27% of traces) and moved later in the trace (mean position 0.54 to 0.91); it now often leads to A49 (89% of its outgoing transitions) and more often precedes A28 (4% to 25% of traces). [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, REL006] These changes are not part of the main insertion hypothesis but indicate a separate shift in A06's role.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities were already present but below the detection threshold in the earlier window; however, their absence in all indicators and the direct successor changes make this unlikely.
