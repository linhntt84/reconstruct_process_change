### Observed differences

- In the later window, activity A02 occurs on average about 2.1 times per trace instead of exactly once, and in about half the traces it occurs more than once (recurrence support 49.5% vs 0%). [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control flow from a fixed linear sequence A02 → A04 → A03 to a structure where A02 can be repeated, A04 can be skipped, and A04 leads back to A02. This is directly shown by the successor changes (A04 now always goes to A02 instead of A03; A02 now sometimes goes to A03 instead of always to A04), the increased occurrence and recurrence of A02, and the reduced presence of A04. The many consistent control-flow items (11 items vs 0.06 expected by chance) rule out a chance sampling difference.

Other differences: The relative order and lag between A02 and A03 changed from a fixed A02-before-A03 with lag 2 to a mixed order with shorter lags, and the lag between A04 and A03 increased. These are consistent with the main structural change and do not require a separate hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the after window contains a rare variant that happens to be overrepresented, but the volume and consistency of the evidence make this unlikely.
