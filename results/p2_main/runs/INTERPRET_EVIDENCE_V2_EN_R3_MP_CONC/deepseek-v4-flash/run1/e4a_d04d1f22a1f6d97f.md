### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A22 occurred in every trace (mean 1.0 per trace); in the later window it occurs in about half of traces (0.51 per trace, 51% participation). [OCC001]
- Activities A27, A28, and A33 were absent in the earlier window; in the later window each occurs in about half of traces (0.49 per trace, 49% participation). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- The transition from A21 was exclusively to A22 in the earlier window; in the later window it goes to A22 in about half of the outgoing transitions, and to A27, A28, A33 in smaller shares (about 15–18% each). [DIR001]
- A27, A28, and A33 had no outgoing transitions in the earlier window; in the later window they lead to A23, A28, and A33. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- A21 and A22 co-occurred in all traces earlier; later they co-occur in about half, with A21 alone in the other half. [REL001]
- A21 with each of A27, A28, A33: earlier A21 alone; later they co-occur in about half, with A21 before them. [REL002, REL003, REL004]
- A22 and A23: earlier always co-occurred with A22 before A23; later they co-occur in about half, and A23 occurs alone in the other half. [REL005]
- A22 with each of A27, A28, A33: earlier A22 alone; later A22 alone in half and the other alone in half, never together. [REL006, REL007, REL008]
- A23 with each of A27, A28, A33: earlier A23 alone; later they co-occur in about half, with the other before A23. [REL009, REL010, REL011]
- A27, A28, A33 among themselves: earlier none of them occurred; later they co-occur in about half, with mixed order (A before B and B before A both present). [REL012, REL013, REL014]
- The gap between A21 and A23 shifted: earlier always 2 steps; later about half still 2 steps and half 4 steps. [LAG001]
- Elapsed time between A25 and A29 dropped from a median of about 0.90 hours to about 0.48 hours. [TWAIT001]
- Elapsed time between A30 and A29 dropped from a median of about 0.15 hours to about 0.08 hours. [TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which about half of the cases in the later window take a new path that skips A22 and instead uses A27, A28, and A33 (in any order) before reaching A23, while the other half still follows the earlier path through A22. This is directly shown by the appearance of A27/A28/A33, the drop in A22 participation, the new outgoing transitions from A21, the co-occurrence patterns (A22 never co-occurs with the new activities, and A23 co-occurs with them), and the shift in the A21–A23 lag from 2 to 4 steps. The large number of control-flow items (26 vs. 0.4 expected by chance) rules out a chance sampling effect. A competing explanation that the new activities are a rare variant is not plausible because they appear in about half of traces. The timing changes (TWAIT001, TWAIT002) are separate and not part of this structural hypothesis.

Other differences: The elapsed times between A25–A29 and A30–A29 decreased substantially in the later window, indicating a timing change independent of the structural shift.
