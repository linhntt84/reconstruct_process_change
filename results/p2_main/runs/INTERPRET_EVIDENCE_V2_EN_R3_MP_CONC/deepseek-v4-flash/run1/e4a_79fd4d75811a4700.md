### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering of activities around A22 and A25: in the later window, A22 is more often followed by A21 instead of A25, and when A25 does follow, it occurs later (larger lag). This is directly shown by the shift in A22's outgoing transitions and the increased lag between A22 and A25. The two items agree, and the overall number of control-flow items (4) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.34), making a chance explanation unlikely. The support for the A22-related items is moderate (about 49 and 31 traces containing A22 in the two windows), but the consistency and the screening count support the hypothesis.

Other differences: The relative ordering of A16 with A17 and A18 also changed: A17 tends to occur closer to A16, while A18 tends to occur further. This is shown by LAG001 and LAG002. These may be part of the same reordering or a separate change; the evidence does not link them to the A22-A25 change. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the A16-related shifts are independent of the A22-A25 change, but the overall evidence still points to a structural reordering.
