### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A20, A21, A22, A24, and A25 consistently occurred before A26, A27, A28, A29, and A30: for example, A20 before A26 in 97.5% of cases, A21 before A26 in 97.5%, A22 before A26 in 97.5%, and A25 before A26 in 97.5%. In the later window, the order reversed: A26 before A20 in 72% of cases, A26 before A21 in 72.5%, A26 before A22 in 73.5%, and A26 before A25 in 92.5%. [REL001, REL005, REL009, REL017, REL019]

- The mean relative position of A20, A21, A22, A24, and A25 increased (moved later) by about 0.06–0.09 (e.g., A20 from 0.556 to 0.618, A25 from 0.610 to 0.704), while the mean position of A26, A27, A28, A29, and A30 decreased (moved earlier) by about 0.06–0.09 (e.g., A26 from 0.634 to 0.547, A28 from 0.686 to 0.624). [POS001–POS010]

- The distance between these activities increased: for example, A20 and A21 were adjacent (lag 1 or 2) in all cases before, but after, lags of 3–5 appeared (lag 3: 15%, lag 4: 12%, lag 5: 2%). Similar increases in distance occurred for A20–A22, A21–A22, and A25–A26. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG009, LAG017]

- The outgoing transitions of A20, A21, A22, A24, and A25 changed from a few successors (mainly A22, A25, A21, A20) to a wider set including A26, A27, A28, A29, and A30. For example, A25 before went to A26 in 99.5% of transitions, after to A36 in 39% and to many others (A27 16%, A29 14%, etc.). [DIR001–DIR005, DIR011]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering of activities: in the earlier window, activities A20, A21, A22, A24, and A25 preceded A26, A27, A28, A29, and A30 in a fixed sequence; in the later window, the latter group often occurs before the former, and the order between the two groups is no longer fixed. This is directly shown by the reversal of order in many activity pairs, the shift in positions (the first group moved later, the second group moved earlier), and the increased distances between them. The evidence is extensive (102 control-flow items vs 0.97 expected by chance) and consistent across indicators.

Other differences: A25's successor changed from A26 to A36 and several other activities, indicating a new or alternative path after A25. The outgoing transitions of A20–A24 also became more diverse.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a change in the mix of process variants rather than a single reordering, but the consistency across many pairs and positions makes this less likely.
