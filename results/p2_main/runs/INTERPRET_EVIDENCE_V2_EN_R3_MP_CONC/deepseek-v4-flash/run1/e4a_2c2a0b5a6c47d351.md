### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the process: after A39, the earlier window had a choice among three mutually exclusive activities (A14, A15, A16), each followed by A24; in the later window, A39 is followed by all three activities A21, A22 and A23 (in any order) before A24. This is directly shown by the disappearance of the old activities, the appearance of the new ones in every trace, the change in A39's outgoing edges, and the co-occurrence and ordering relations among the new activities. The screening shows far more control-flow items than expected by chance (48 vs 0.5), so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities are renamed versions of the old ones, but the change from mutual exclusivity to co-occurrence indicates a genuine difference in routing.

Other differences: The elapsed times between A24 and A31 and between A32 and A31 both decreased substantially (about half and about 40% respectively), indicating a separate change in timing. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
