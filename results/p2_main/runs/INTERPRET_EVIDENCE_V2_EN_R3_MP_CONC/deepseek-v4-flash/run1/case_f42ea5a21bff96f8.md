### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which a group of activities (A21, A22, A23) that were commonly executed became much less frequent, while a different group (A24, A25, A26) became much more frequent, and the position of A06 moved later, now typically occurring after the new group and immediately before A35, with A34 now feeding into A06. The participation swap is large and consistent across many occurrence and relation items, and the ordering changes are directly shown by relation and lag items. The number of qualifying evidence items (92) far exceeds the 1.09 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artifact.

Other differences: A02 and A08 also decreased in participation (from about 18% to 8% and from about 14% to 5%, respectively). [OCC001, OCC003] These are smaller and not part of the main hypothesis.

The evidence cannot rule out that the observed differences reflect a change in the mix of cases rather than a change in the process itself, but the screening indicates the differences are unlikely to be due to random sampling.
