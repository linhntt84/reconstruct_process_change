### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed by adding two new activities, A01 and A05, and re-routing the flow: in the later window, A03 and A04 lead to A01, A01 leads to A06, and A06 leads to A05, whereas in the earlier window A03 and A04 led to A06 and A06 led to A02/A04/A03. This is directly shown by the appearance of A01 and A05 in all traces, the new outgoing edges from A01 and A05, the shift of A06's outgoing edge to A05, and the relational order (A03/A04 before A01 before A06 before A05). The large number of qualifying items (21 vs 0.13 expected by chance) rules out random fluctuation. A competing explanation that A01 and A05 were simply rare in the earlier window is unlikely given they appear in 100% of later traces and 0% of earlier ones, but the evidence cannot exclude that the earlier window happened to miss a rare variant; however, the consistency of the routing changes makes this implausible.

Other differences: The positions of A04 and A06 shifted earlier, and the lags between A03-A04, A03-A06, and A04-A06 increased, consistent with the insertion of A01 and A05 between them. These are part of the same structural change rather than independent changes.
