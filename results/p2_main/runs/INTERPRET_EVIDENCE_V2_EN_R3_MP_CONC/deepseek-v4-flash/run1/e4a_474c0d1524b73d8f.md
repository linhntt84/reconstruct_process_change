### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The main hypothesis is that the routing between A31, A32 and A28 changed: in the later window, A31 and A32 each more often lead to A28 and less often to each other. This is directly shown by the shift in outgoing transition shares for both activities [DIR001, DIR002]. The two items describe the same underlying change, so they are not independent confirmations, but the screening comparison (2 control-flow items vs 0.27 expected) makes a chance explanation unlikely. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed shift is a sampling artifact, though the support counts (90 and 91 outgoing transitions) are substantial.

Other differences: The elapsed time between A11 and A12 roughly doubled in the later window, with mean and median both increasing from about 0.9 hours to about 1.8–1.9 hours [TWAIT001]. This timing change is separate from the routing shift and is supported by a single strong item (1 time item vs 0.006 expected).
