### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from having two alternative branches after A26 — one via A27 and one via A32/A33/A38 — to a single branch via A27, with the alternative activities A32, A33, and A38 no longer executed. In the earlier window, about half the traces took the A26→A27→A28 path and the other half took a path through A32, A33, or A38 before reaching A28. In the later window, essentially all traces follow A26→A27→A28, and A32, A33, and A38 have almost disappeared. This is directly shown by the occurrence changes, the routing of A26, the co-occurrence and ordering relations, and the lag between A26 and A28. The evidence is extensive and consistent, with 35 control-flow items against an expected 0.6 by chance.

Other differences: The position of A28 moved slightly earlier in the trace, consistent with the removal of the intermediate branch. [POS001]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the alternative activities still exist but were not observed in the later window due to sampling; however, their near-total absence (1 trace out of 200) and the simultaneous increase in A27 make this unlikely.
