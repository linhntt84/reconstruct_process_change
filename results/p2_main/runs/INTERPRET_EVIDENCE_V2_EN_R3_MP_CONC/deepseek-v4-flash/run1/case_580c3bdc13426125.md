### Observed differences

- In the later window, activity A02 disappeared completely: it was present in all 200 earlier traces (mean 2.2 occurrences per trace, 59% of traces with repeats) and in none of the later traces. [OCC001]
- Activity A03 appeared in the later window: absent earlier, present in all 200 later traces (mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, no repeats). [OCC002]
- The outgoing edges of A02 (to A06 and A07, about 55% and 45%) vanished, while A03 now leads to A06 and A07 in about equal shares (50% each). [DIR001, DIR002]
- A05's successor changed: earlier it always went to A02; later it goes to A03 in about 97% of cases, with small shares to A06 and A07. [DIR003]
- A06's successor changed: earlier it always went to A02; later it goes to A07 (51%), to itself (48%) and to A03 (2%). [DIR004]
- Positionally, A02 moved from a mean relative position of 0.64 to absent, while A03 moved from absent to a mean position of 0.64. [POS001, POS002]
- Relations confirm the replacement: A02 and A03 never co-occur; earlier traces contained only A02, later traces only A03. [REL001]
- A05 and A02 co-occurred in all earlier traces (41% A05 before A02, 59% interleaved/repeated); later A05 occurs alone, without A02. [REL002]
- A05 and A03 co-occur in all later traces, with A05 before A03 in every case; earlier A03 was absent. [REL005]
- A03 and A07 co-occur in all later traces, with A03 before A07 in about 98% of cases; earlier A07 occurred alone. [REL007]
- The lag between A06 and A07 shifted: earlier the pair was typically 2 steps apart (48%) or 4 steps (17%); later it is most often 1 step apart (35%) or 2 steps (12%). [LAG004]
- Repetitions of A06 changed: earlier repeats were usually 2 steps apart (27%) or 4 steps (6%); later they are mostly immediate, 1 step apart (26%). [LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural replacement of activity A02 by activity A03 as the central step of the process. In the earlier window, the typical path was A05 → A02 → A06/A07, with A02 often repeated and interleaved with A06/A07. In the later window, A05 leads to A03, which then leads to A06/A07, and A03 almost always precedes A07. This is directly shown by the disappearance of A02 and its edges, the appearance of A03 with the same successor pattern, and the relational changes involving A05, A06 and A07. The evidence is extensive (21 items vs 0.12 expected by chance) and consistent across occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators.

Other differences: A06 repetitions became more immediate (lag 1 instead of lag 2), and A06's position shifted slightly later in the trace. These are secondary to the main replacement and may reflect a change in how A06 is executed relative to the new central activity.

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A02 and A03 are the same logical activity recorded under different identifiers, which would make the change a labelling artefact rather than a behavioural change; however, the data as provided show a clear shift from one activity to the other.
