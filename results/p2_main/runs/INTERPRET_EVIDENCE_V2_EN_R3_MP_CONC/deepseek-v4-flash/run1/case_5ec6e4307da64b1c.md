### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 did not occur in any trace (0% participation). In the later window, each appears in about 17–23% of traces (A19 23%, A20 21%, A25 23%, A26 17%, A27 17.5%). [OCC001–OCC005]
- In the earlier window, A29 led almost always to A30 (98%) and A30 led almost always to A31 (95.5%). In the later window, A29 leads to A30 in 47.5% of its outgoing transitions and to A19, A20, A25, A26, A27 in about 9–12% each; A30 leads to A31 in 51.2% and to the same new activities in about 7–11% each. [DIR006, DIR007]
- The new activities, when they occur, are placed around the middle of the trace (mean position ~0.51–0.52). [POS001–POS004]
- In the later window, the new activities co-occur with A29, A30 and A31. For A29, it almost always precedes the new activity (e.g., A29 before A19 in 23% of traces, A29 before A20 in 21%). For A30, the order is mixed (A19 before A30 in 12%, A30 before A19 in 10.5%). For A31, the new activity almost always precedes A31 (A19 before A31 in 22.5%, A20 before A31 in 20.5%, A25 before A31 in 23%). [REL004–REL006, REL009–REL011, REL013–REL015]
- The lag between A29 and A30 increased: before, A29 was immediately followed by A30 in 98.5% of cases; after, only 46.8% are immediate, and 50.2% have one step in between. Similarly, A30 to A31: before immediate 96.5%, after immediate 51.5% and one step in between 45.8%. A29 to A31: before one step in between 95%, after two steps in between 94%. [LAG001–LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a set of new activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) are introduced and executed between the existing activities A29/A30 and A31, so that the original direct sequence A29→A30→A31 is partially replaced by paths that go through these new activities before reaching A30 or A31. This is directly shown by the appearance of these activities only in the later window, their outgoing edges to A30/A31, the branching of A29 and A30 to them, the ordering relations (A29 before them, they before A31, mixed with A30), and the increased lags between A29–A30, A30–A31 and A29–A31 by one step. The change is partial: the new activities occur in only about 20% of traces, and the original direct edges still exist in about half of the cases. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that these activities existed in the earlier window but were not observed in the sample, although the consistency and volume of evidence make this unlikely.

Other differences: A31's position shifted slightly later (mean from 0.54 to 0.56) [POS005], which is consistent with the insertion of activities before it.
