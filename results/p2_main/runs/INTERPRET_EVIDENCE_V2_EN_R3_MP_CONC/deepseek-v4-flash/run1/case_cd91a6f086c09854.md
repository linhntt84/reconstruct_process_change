### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: in the later window, A21 and A22 are executed before A30, A31 and A36, whereas in the earlier window they were executed after them. A20 now leads directly to A22, and A25 is reached later, via A30/A31/A36, instead of directly from A21. This is directly shown by the successor changes (DIR), the position shifts (POS), the pairwise order reversals (REL) and the lag changes (LAG). The number of qualifying items (22) is far above the 0.42 expected by chance, and the main items have full support in both windows. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that some of the lag items with very low support in the later window (only 2 traces) reflect sampling noise, but the core ordering change rests on items with 200 traces in both windows.

Other differences: none beyond the reordering described.
