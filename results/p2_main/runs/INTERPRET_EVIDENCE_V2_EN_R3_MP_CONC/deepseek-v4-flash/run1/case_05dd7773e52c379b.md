### Observed differences

- Participation of A28, A30, A31 rose from about 48% of traces to about 98%; A32, A33, A34 rose from about 51% to about 99%. [OCC001–OCC006]
- A25’s outgoing edges shifted: before it went to A28/A30/A31 and A32/A33/A34 roughly equally; after it goes almost exclusively to A28, A30, A31 (about 0.31, 0.375, 0.3) and almost never to A32/A33/A34. [DIR001]

- Lag between A25 and A32/A33/A34 increased from 1–3 steps to 4–5 steps; similarly between A28/A30/A31 and A26. [LAG001–LAG006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process changed from having two alternative branches (one containing A28, A30, A31, the other containing A32, A33, A34) to executing both groups sequentially in the same trace, with A25 first, then the first group, then the second group, then A26. This is supported by the shift from mutual exclusivity to co-occurrence, the routing changes (A25 now leads to the first group, which then leads to the second group), and the positional and lag changes consistent with the second group moving later.

Other differences: A26 is no longer a direct successor of A28/A30/A31; it now occurs after all the other activities. Also A25’s position shifted slightly earlier.

Limitation: The evidence is all control-flow; no timing or resource data. The change is consistent with a structural reconfiguration, but we cannot rule out that the process was always intended to have both branches and the earlier window sampled only one branch per case due to some external factor; however, the consistency across many indicators makes this unlikely.
