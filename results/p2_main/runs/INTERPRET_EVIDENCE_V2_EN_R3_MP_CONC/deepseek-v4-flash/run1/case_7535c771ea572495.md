### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process replaced the sequence A06 → A01 → A03 with A05 → A06 → A07, while A02 remains but now feeds into A05 instead of A06. In the earlier window, every trace contained A01 and A03, with A06 before A01 and A01 before A03; in the later window, every trace contains A05 and A07, with A05 before A06 and A06 before A07. A02 continues to appear in every trace, but its outgoing transition to A06 was replaced by a transition to A05, and A06's successor changed from A01 to A07. The positional and lag evidence are consistent with this reordering. The large number of qualifying items (28 vs 0.16 expected by chance) makes it unlikely that these differences are sampling noise. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 and A03 were not removed but simply not observed in the later window; however, their complete absence in all 200 traces makes this unlikely.
