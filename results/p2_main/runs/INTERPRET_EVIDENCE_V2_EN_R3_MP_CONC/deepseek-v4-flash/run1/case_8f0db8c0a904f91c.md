### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which a block of activities (A19, A20, A25) is no longer always executed; in the later window these activities appear in only about half the traces, and the control flow bypasses them: A41 now leads directly to A26 (instead of through A19), and A46 now leads directly to A47 (instead of through A02). A02 itself becomes much rarer and its outgoing no longer goes predominantly to A47. The large number of qualifying items (41 vs. 0.72 expected by chance) and the consistent relational and positional evidence make a real change highly likely. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window simply contains a different mix of process variants, but the magnitude and coherence of the differences make this unlikely.

Other differences: A04 and A06 increased in participation, and A02's outgoing transitions became more diverse, but these are not part of the main hypothesis.
