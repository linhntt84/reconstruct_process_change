### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A15, A16, A17, and A20 occur earlier in traces (mean position decreased by about 0.06–0.10), while A21, A22, A23, A24, and A25 occur later (mean position increased by about 0.03–0.10). [POS001–POS010]
- The outgoing transitions of A15, A16, and A17 changed: before they went to a wide set including A21–A25, after they go only to A15, A16, A17, and A20. A20 before went to A31 and others, after it goes only to A21. [DIR001–DIR004]
- The order between the early group (A15, A16, A17, A20) and the late group (A21–A25) became fixed: in the later window, for every pair between these groups, the early activity always precedes the late one, whereas before both orders occurred in substantial proportions (e.g., A15 before A21 in 23% before vs 100% after; A20 before A21 in 2.5% before vs 100% after). [REL001–REL022]
- The positional distance between the early and late groups increased: for example, A15 to A21 before had lag 1 in 57% of cases, after lag 1 never occurs and lags 2–4 dominate; A15 to A23 before had lags 1–5 spread, after lags 4–5 dominate. [LAG004, LAG005, LAG011, LAG012, LAG018, LAG019]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process changed from a flexible ordering, where activities A15–A17 and A20 could be interleaved with A21–A25, to a strict sequential structure: the early group (A15, A16, A17, A20) always executes before the late group (A21–A25), with A20 leading to A21. This is supported by the consistent shift in relative order (REL), the increase in positional separation (LAG), the earlier positions of the early group and later positions of the late group (POS), and the change in outgoing transitions (DIR). The evidence cannot rule out that this reflects a change in the mix of existing process variants rather than a modification of the process model, but the magnitude and consistency of the change across many indicators support a real behavioural change.

Other differences: A30 now always goes to A19 (before it went to A21 in 54% of cases) [DIR010]; within the late group, the order among A21–A25 also became more fixed (e.g., A21 before A23, A24, A25; A23 before A24, A25) as shown by REL and LAG. These are consistent with the overall reordering.
