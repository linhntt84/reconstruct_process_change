### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reversal in the ordering between two groups of activities: A26 and A27 now occur after A35, A36, and A41 instead of before them. This is directly shown by the relational items (full support in both windows) and is consistent with the positional shifts and successor changes. Additionally, A30’s role changed: it is now a direct successor of A26 rather than of A35/A36/A41. The main limitation is that some lag items have very low support in one window, but the core ordering evidence is strong and consistent.

Other differences: A25’s successor changed from A27 to A36/A41/A35, which is part of the same reordering of the two groups. The evidence cannot rule out that additional, unobserved changes occurred, but the observed pattern is coherent.
