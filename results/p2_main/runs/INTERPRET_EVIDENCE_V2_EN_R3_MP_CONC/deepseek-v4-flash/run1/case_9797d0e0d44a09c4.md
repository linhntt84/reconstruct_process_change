### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20, and A25 appear in about 99.5% of traces, up from about 47% earlier, and their mean occurrences per trace rise from about 0.47 to about 1.0. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the later window, A41's outgoing transitions go to A19 in about 92% of cases, down from about 44% earlier, while transitions to A26 drop from about 52% to about 0.5%. [DIR002]
- The order between these activities becomes fixed: A19 before A20 in about 98% of traces (up from 46%), A19 before A25 in about 98% (up from 47%), A25 before A20 in about 99.5% (up from 46%), and A41 before A25 in about 98% (up from 47%). [REL001, REL002, REL007, REL013]
- The lag between A41 and A26 shifts from mostly 1 step (52.5%) to mostly 4 steps (88.9%), consistent with A41 no longer directly preceding A26. [LAG001]
- A42 co-occurs with A19/A20/A25 in about 99.5% of traces (up from 47%), and A45 co-occurs with them in about 62.5% (up from 27.5%). [REL005, REL010, REL014, REL006, REL011, REL015]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a segment involving A19, A20, and A25, which was previously executed in only about half the cases, became part of the standard flow in nearly all cases, and that these activities are now executed in a fixed order (A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 → A26), with A41 no longer going directly to A26. This is supported by the near-universal participation of A19/A20/A25, the fixed ordering relations among them, and the successor change of A41. The number of qualifying control-flow items (23 vs 0.64 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely. A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is a shift in case mix (different types of cases) rather than a modification of the process itself.

Other differences: A42 also becomes near-universal in co-occurrence with these activities, and A45 increases but remains less than universal; these may be part of the same change or separate.
