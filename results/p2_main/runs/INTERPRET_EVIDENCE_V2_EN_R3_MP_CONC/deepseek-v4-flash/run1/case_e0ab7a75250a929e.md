### Observed differences

- Participation: A02 increased from 17.5% to 83.5% of traces; A27, A36, A37, A42, A43, A44 increased from about 48–53% to 100% of traces. A04, A06, A10 decreased from 16–20% to 6–8%. [OCC001, OCC005–OCC010, OCC002–OCC004]
- Co-occurrence: A27 with A36, A37, A42, A43, A44 went from near 0% co-occurrence to 100% co-occurrence; A25 and A26 with those activities went from about 50% to 100% co-occurrence. [REL042–REL046, REL028–REL033, REL034–REL039]
- Order: A26 used to occur before A36/A37/A42/A43/A44 in about 52% of traces; in the later window those activities occur before A26 in about 99% of traces. [REL035–REL039, POS005, POS009–POS013]
- Successor changes: A25's main successor changed from A28 (98%) to a spread of A37, A44, A42, A43, A36; A47's main successor changed from A20/A19 to A02 (81.5%); A02's successors became A20 (47%) and A19 (37%). [DIR005, DIR012, DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which a set of activities (A27, A36, A37, A42, A43, A44) that were previously executed as alternatives in about half the traces became executed together in every trace, and A02 became a regular part of the flow, while A04, A06, A10 became less frequent. The order of A26 relative to that group also reversed, with those activities now occurring before A26. This is supported by the large increase in participation, the co-occurrence going from near zero to 100%, and the consistent order flips. The evidence cannot rule out that the change reflects a different mix of process variants rather than a modification of a single process, but the magnitude and consistency of the changes make a structural change likely.

Other differences: The successor of A25 changed from A28 to a set of other activities, and A47's successor changed to A02. These are consistent with the reordering but are not part of the main hypothesis.
