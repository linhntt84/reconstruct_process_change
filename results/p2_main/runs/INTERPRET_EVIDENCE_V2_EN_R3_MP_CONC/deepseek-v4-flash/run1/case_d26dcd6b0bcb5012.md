### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a shift from a process that had two roughly equal variants — one with a short path from A43 directly to A28, and one with a longer path through A19 and A20 — to a process that almost always takes the longer path. In the later window, A19 and A20 are near-universal, A43 almost always leads to A19 instead of A28, and A25/A26/A27 appear more often as intermediate steps, with A25 and A26 forming a pair and A27 acting as an alternative to that pair. The screening count (28 items vs 0.7 expected by chance) is far above what a stable process would produce, and the items are consistent across occurrence, routing, and relational indicators. A competing explanation that the difference is due to random sampling is effectively ruled out by the screening comparison. The exact ordering of the intermediate activities is not fully pinned down, but the relational evidence is coherent.

Other differences: A27's outgoing transitions became more concentrated on A20, and A28's position shifted slightly later while A43's shifted earlier, consistent with the longer path.
