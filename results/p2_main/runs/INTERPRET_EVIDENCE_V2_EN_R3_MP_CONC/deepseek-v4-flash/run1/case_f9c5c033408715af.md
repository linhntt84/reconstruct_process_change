### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A07 is no longer executed in the later window. A07 was present in about 23% of traces before and is completely absent after, its outgoing transitions disappear, its relative position vanishes, and its co-occurrence with A10 drops from 23% to 0% while A10 alone rises to 100%. These signs are direct and consistent across occurrence, routing, position and relation indicators. The screening comparison (6 control-flow items vs 0.14 expected by chance) makes it unlikely that this is a random sampling artefact.

Other differences: A10's outgoing routing shifted among its remaining successors (A04 decreased, A01, A02, A03 and A13 increased, and A09 appeared), and the lag between A10 and A03 shortened, with more pairs occurring within one step. These changes are consistent with the removal of A07 but are not directly linked to it in the evidence.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A07 became extremely rare rather than completely removed, and simply did not appear in the 200-trace sample; however, its prior frequency (23%) and the consistent disappearance across multiple indicators make this unlikely.
