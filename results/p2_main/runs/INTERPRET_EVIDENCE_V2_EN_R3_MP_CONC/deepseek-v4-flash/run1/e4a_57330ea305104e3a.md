### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the routing among activities A28, A31, and A32. In the earlier window, A31 most often led to A28 and A32 most often led back to A31, forming a loop with exits to A28. In the later window, A31 more often leads to A32, and A32 more often leads to A28, so the path A31 → A32 → A28 becomes the dominant route. At the same time, A27 now sends more cases to A31 and fewer to A32, feeding this new route. The three control-flow items are consistent and far exceed the number expected by chance (3 vs 0.29). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

Other differences: The time between completions of A24 and A25 halved, indicating a separate timing change. This is supported by a single time item that is also far above chance expectation (1 vs 0.006). [TWAIT001]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed routing shift reflects a rare variant that became more frequent rather than a systematic change, but the consistency across three related successor distributions makes this unlikely.
