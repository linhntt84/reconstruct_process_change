### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a repeated loop involving A30, A31, and A36 to a single execution of these activities, with a fixed order A30 before A36, and A36 now leading to A25 instead of back to A30. The drop in repetition is shown directly by the occurrence counts and recurrence shares for all three activities, the fixed ordering is shown by the A30-before-A36 share and the near-disappearance of the reverse order, and the successor change is shown by A36's outgoing transition distribution. The screening comparison (11 items vs. 0.47 expected by chance) makes it unlikely that these differences are sampling noise.

Other differences: A30's outgoing transitions show a slight redistribution (DIR001) but the dominant successor remains A31. The position distributions of A30 and A36 became more concentrated (POS001, POS002), consistent with the removal of repetition.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the process switched to a different variant that happens to have these characteristics, but the consistency across multiple indicators makes this less plausible.
