### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which the six activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38, A39 changed from being mandatory and executed together in every trace to being optional and often mutually exclusive, while A30 and A31 remain present in every trace. Specifically, in the earlier window these six activities co-occurred with A30 and A31 in all traces, and A30 led to them in a concentrated way (mostly A33, A35, A36). In the later window, each of the six appears in only about half the traces, A30's outgoing edges are spread more evenly across them, and A33, A35, A36 now have substantial direct edges to A31 (about 24–34% of their outgoing transitions), which were absent before. The lag between A30 and A37/A38/A39 also decreased from mostly 4–5 steps to mostly 1–3 steps, indicating these activities now occur closer to A30. The large number of qualifying items (57 vs 0.79 expected by chance) rules out chance as an explanation.

Other differences: A30's position shifted later in the trace, and the positions of A33, A35, A36 increased while those of A37, A38, A39 decreased slightly, but these are based on reduced support in the later window.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window reflects a different process variant rather than a temporal change, but the evidence is consistent with a change in the process structure.
