### Observed differences

- In the later window, A14, A15 and A20 no longer lead to A21; instead they lead to A23 and A27, each about half of the time (A14: 52% A23, 48% A27; A15: 54% A23, 46% A27; A20: 39% A23, 61% A27). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- A40, which before went to A14, A15 and A20 (38%, 31%, 32% respectively), now goes exclusively to A21. [DIR006]
- A22 and A23, which before each had a single successor (A23 and A27 respectively), now branch to several activities: A22 to A15 (18%), A23 (52%), A20 (15%), A14 (16%); A23 to A27 (48%), A20 (23%), A14 (15%), A15 (15%). [DIR004, DIR005]
- The order between the group {A14, A15, A20} and the group {A21, A22, A23} reversed: before, A14/A15/A20 occurred before A21/A22/A23 in about 30–38% of traces; after, A21/A22/A23 occur before them in about 30–37% of traces. For A14–A23 and A20–A23 the order becomes mixed rather than fixed. [REL001–REL008]
- Positions shifted accordingly: A14, A15 and A20 moved later (mean position from about 0.31–0.33 to 0.59–0.60), while A21, A22 and A23 moved slightly earlier (A21 from 0.35 to 0.32, A22 from 0.58 to 0.55, A23 from 0.61 to 0.59). [POS001–POS006]
- Lags changed: A22–A23 went from always lag 1 to lag 1 or 2; A23–A27 similarly; A22–A27 went from lag 2 to lag 3; A40–A21 went from lag 2 to lag 1. [LAG001–LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering and rerouting of the process: the group of activities A14, A15 and A20, which previously occurred early and led to A21, now occurs later and leads to A23/A27, while A40 now leads to A21 instead of to A14/A15/A20. This is directly shown by the reversal of order in the REL items, the shift in positions, the change in outgoing transitions, and the lag changes. The evidence is consistent across all four indicator families.

Other differences: A22 and A23 now have multiple successors, indicating more branching after those activities, and the order between A14–A23 and A20–A23 becomes mixed rather than fixed.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a reconfiguration of the process rather than a simple swap of two groups, but the evidence strongly favours a genuine change in ordering and routing.
