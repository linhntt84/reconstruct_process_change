### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that a repetition or loop involving activities A25, A26 and A31 was introduced in the later window. These activities, which previously occurred exactly once per trace in a fixed order (A25 before A31), now repeat in about 68% of traces and are interleaved, and A31 now often routes to A25 instead of A20. The control-flow evidence is far more numerous than expected by chance (8 items vs 0.3 expected), and the items are consistent: increased occurrence counts, changed successor, relaxed ordering, and changed lag all point to the same underlying change. A limitation is that the exact loop structure (e.g., which activities are in the loop and how they connect) is not fully specified by the available evidence, and the time change (TWAIT001) is a separate timing difference not directly tied to the loop.

Other differences: The time between A34 and A14 completions roughly doubled, indicating a timing change independent of the structural loop. [TWAIT001]
