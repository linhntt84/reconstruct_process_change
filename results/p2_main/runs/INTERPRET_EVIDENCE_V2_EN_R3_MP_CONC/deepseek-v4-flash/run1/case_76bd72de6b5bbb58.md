### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A01 replaced activity A03 in the process. In the earlier window, A03 was a central step that co-occurred with A05, A09, A10, A11, and A12, and was the main successor of A09 and a frequent successor of A11. In the later window, A03 is absent and A01 appears with the same co-occurrence partners and takes over the successor role from A09 and A11. The two activities never appear together, so they are alternatives. The large number of consistent control-flow items (19 vs 0.33 expected by chance) rules out chance as a whole. A competing explanation that the process simply added A01 while removing A03 without a direct replacement is indistinguishable from a replacement, but the relational evidence (A09 and A11 now lead to A01) directly shows the substitution.

Other differences: None beyond the replacement.
