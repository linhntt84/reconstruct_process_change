### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which A10 is executed earlier and more often, and is inserted between A15 and A16, while the path through A19 and A20 becomes less frequent. In the earlier window, A15 typically led to A16, and A19 and A20 were always present. In the later window, A15 often leads to A10, which then leads to A16, and A19 and A20 are present in only about half the traces. This is supported by the increase in A10 participation, the change in A15's and A10's successor distributions, the earlier position of A10, the increased co-occurrence and adjacency of A10 with A16, and the decreased co-occurrence of A19 and A20 with each other and with A15/A16.

Other differences: A02, A04, A06, A08, A25, A26, and A27 become less frequent. These are not part of the main hypothesis but are additional changes.

Limitation: The evidence is entirely control-flow based; the observed changes could also be explained by a different underlying restructuring, but the consistency across many indicators makes the stated hypothesis the most direct interpretation.
