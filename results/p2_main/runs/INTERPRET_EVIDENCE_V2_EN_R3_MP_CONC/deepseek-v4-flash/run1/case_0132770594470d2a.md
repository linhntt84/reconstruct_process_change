- In the later window, activities A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 almost disappear: their trace participation drops from about 15–24% to 0–1.5%, with A26 no longer observed at all. A06 also drops from about 29% to 7% of traces. [OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]

- The outgoing transitions of A29 shift from a mix (about 61% to A30, 9% to A19, 9.5% to A20, 7.5% to A25, 5% to A26, 7.5% to A27) to almost exclusively A30 (about 97%). - Position of A06 shifts earlier in the trace (mean relative position from about 0.84 to 0.56); A20 also shifts earlier (from about 0.50 to 0.33), though the later support for A20 is only 3 traces. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which a set of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) that were previously executed in a substantial share of cases are now rarely or never executed, and the routing from A29 and A30 now bypasses them, leading directly to A30 and A31 respectively. This is directly shown by the drop in participation of those activities, the near-complete shift of A29’s outgoing transitions to A30 and A30’s to A31, and the collapse of co-occurrence between these activities and A29/A30/A31. The large number of qualifying items (59 vs. 0.73 expected by chance) makes it very unlikely that this is a sampling artefact. A06 also participates less often, but it is not absent and its outgoing transitions become more varied, so it may be a separate, weaker change. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the after-window sample is small for the disappearing activities (e.g., A20, A25, A26, A27 have only 0–4 traces), so the exact routing after the change is based on few observations.

Other differences: A02 and A10 increase in participation, and A06’s outgoing transitions become more evenly distributed across many targets; these are not part of the main hypothesis.
