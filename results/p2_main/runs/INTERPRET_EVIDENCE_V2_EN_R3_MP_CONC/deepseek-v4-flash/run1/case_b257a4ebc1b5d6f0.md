### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the control flow was reordered: a block of activities (A20, A21, A22, A24, A25) that previously preceded another block (A27, A28, A29, A33) now follows it, and the successor of many activities changed from A34 to A29, while A06 became a much more frequent early step. The large number of qualifying items (105 vs. about 1 expected by chance) and the consistency across occurrence, routing, order and position indicators make a chance explanation implausible. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a single large restructuring rather than several independent modifications, but the evidence does not distinguish between those.

Other differences: A04 participation decreased, and A14's successor changed from A15 to A06; these are consistent with the main reordering but are not part of the core hypothesis.
