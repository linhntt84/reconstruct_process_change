### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that, in the later window, a new step consisting of one of A19, A20 or A25 is inserted between A41 (and A02) and A26, replacing the direct A41→A26 and A02→A26 edges. A41 and A02 now lead to one of these new activities, which then lead to A26. The new activities are alternatives, not executed together, and appear in about two-thirds of traces. This is directly shown by the redistribution of A41's and A02's outgoing transitions, the new outgoing edges from A19 and A25 to A26, the relational order (A41 before the new activity, new activity before A26), and the increased lag between A41 and A26. The screening expectation of 0.52 items is far exceeded by 21 qualified items, so the overall difference is not a chance artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A02's change is a separate modification, but the pattern is consistent with the same insertion. Other differences: A02's outgoing distribution changed more broadly, and A26's position shifted slightly later, both consistent with the insertion.
