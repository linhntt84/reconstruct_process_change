### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a single path to two alternative branches. In the earlier window, every trace followed a path where A20 led to a block of five activities (A31, A32, A37, A38, A39), then A23, then A21, then A22, then A26. In the later window, the process splits: about 55% of traces contain the block of five activities (without A22), and about 45% contain A22 (without the block). A20 now leads to A23 instead of to the block, and A21 now leads to either A22 or the block. The order also changed: A23 and A21 now occur before the block, whereas before the block occurred before them. This is supported by the large number of qualified items (72 vs 0.72 expected by chance), the consistent participation drops, the routing changes in DIR, and the mutual exclusivity shown in REL. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two windows represent different case types rather than a temporal change, but the evidence is consistent with a structural change in the process.

Other differences: The position of A20 shifted slightly later (mean from 0.32 to 0.35) and A26 shifted slightly earlier (mean from 0.67 to 0.65), but these are minor and may be consequences of the order changes. [POS001, POS005]
