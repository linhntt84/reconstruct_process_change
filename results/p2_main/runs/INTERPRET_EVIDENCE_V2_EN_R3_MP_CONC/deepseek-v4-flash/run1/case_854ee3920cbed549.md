### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window a set of new activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) are introduced and executed between the A29/A30 step and the A31 step, so that the direct path A29→A30→A31 is often replaced by A29→(one of the new activities)→A30/A31. This is directly shown by the new outgoing edges from A29 and A30 to these activities, the outgoing edges from these activities to A30 and A31, and the increased lags between A29 and A30, A30 and A31, and A29 and A31. The number of evidence items (49) far exceeds the 0.68 expected by chance, and the pattern is consistent across DIR, LAG, and REL indicators. A competing explanation that these are just rare variants is unlikely given the magnitude and consistency, but the exact routing among the new activities is not fully specified.

Other differences: A06's participation increases, its position moves later, and its successor changes to A47; A46's successor changes to include A06. These changes are also supported by the evidence but are not part of the main insertion hypothesis. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR009, REL008, REL009]
