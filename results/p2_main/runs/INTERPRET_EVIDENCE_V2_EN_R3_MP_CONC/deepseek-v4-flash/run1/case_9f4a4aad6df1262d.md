### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A20, A22, and A23 almost always occurred before A24, A25, and A26 (e.g., A20 before A24 in 98% of traces, A23 before A24 in 99%, A23 before A26 in 99%). In the later window, the order reversed: A24 before A20 in 73%, A24 before A23 in 84%, A26 before A20 in 51%. [REL001, REL006, REL003]
- The outgoing transitions of A20, A22, and A23 changed. A20 went to A23 in 99% of its transitions before, but in the later window only 67%, with about 11% each to A24, A25, and A26. [DIR001]
- A22 went to A20 in 99.5% before; later it goes to A20 in 56%, to A24 in 27%, to A26 in 11%, and to A25 in 6%. [DIR002]
- A23 went to A24 in 100% before; later it goes to A32 in 49%, to A26 in 17%, to A25 in 16%, and to A24 in 15%. [DIR003]
- The mean position of A20, A22, and A23 increased (moved later) by about 0.05, 0.03, and 0.06, while the mean position of A24, A25, and A26 decreased (moved earlier) by about 0.06, 0.02, and 0.04. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- The lag between A20 and A23 changed from always adjacent (lag 1) to a spread over lags 1–4. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering and routing of activities: in the later window, A24, A25, and A26 tend to occur before A20, A22, and A23, whereas the reverse order held earlier. The routing from A20, A22, and A23 also changed: they now branch directly to A24, A25, and A26, and A23 additionally leads to A32, instead of the earlier funnel through A23→A24. This is supported directly by the reversal in pairwise order (REL), the shift in outgoing transitions (DIR), the movement of positions (POS), and the change in lags (LAG). The number of qualifying items (49) far exceeds the 0.66 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that some of the observed changes reflect a rare variant that became more frequent, but the consistency across many indicators makes this unlikely.

Other differences: A32 appears as a new successor of A23, and A31's outgoing transitions also changed (DIR007), but these are consistent with the same reordering rather than a separate change.
