### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A20's outgoing transitions were spread across A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 (each about 20%). In the later window, A20 goes to A22 in about 99.5% of cases. [DIR001]
- In the earlier window, A21 always went to A25. In the later window, A21 goes to A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 (each about 20%) and almost never to A25. [DIR002]
- For each of A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38, the share of outgoing transitions to A22 dropped by about 0.18–0.22, while the share to A25 increased by a similar amount. [DIR003–DIR007]
- The order between A21/A22 and the group A30/A31/A36/A37/A38 reversed: before, each of the group came before A21 and A22 in all traces; after, A21 and A22 come before the group in about 99.5% of traces. [REL001–REL010]
- The positions of A21 and A22 moved earlier (mean position from about 0.65 to 0.52 for A21, and from 0.52 to 0.39 for A22), while the positions of A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 moved later (from about 0.44 to 0.60). [POS001–POS007]
- Co-occurrence of A20 with A30/A31/A36/A37/A38, and of those activities with A22, almost disappeared in the later window (only 1 trace out of 200). [LAG001–LAG010]
- Waiting times between A09 and A10, and between A11 and A10, dropped from about 1.0 hour to about 0.5 hours. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
- Waiting time between A24 and A28 dropped from about 1.0 hour to essentially zero (median 0). [TWAIT003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control-flow structure: the order of activities A21 and A22 relative to the group A30/A31/A36/A37/A38 reversed, and the routing from A20 changed from going to that group to going to A22, while the group now routes to A25 instead of to A22, and A21 now routes to the group instead of to A25. This is directly shown by the successor changes (DIR), the reversal of pairwise order (REL), the positional shifts (POS), and the near-disappearance of co-occurrence between A20 and the group and between the group and A22 (LAG). The large number of control-flow items (34 vs. 0.55 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely.

Other differences: Waiting times between A09–A10, A11–A10, and A24–A28 decreased substantially, indicating a separate timing change. The evidence cannot rule out that the timing change is unrelated to the structural change, and the LAG items in the later window rest on only 1 trace, though this is consistent with the routing change.
