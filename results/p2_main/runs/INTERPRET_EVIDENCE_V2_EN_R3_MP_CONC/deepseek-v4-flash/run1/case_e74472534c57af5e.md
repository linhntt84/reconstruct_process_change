### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A30, A31, and A36 occur on average about 3.5 times per trace instead of about 1 time, and they are repeated in about 68% of traces instead of about 1% (OCC001, OCC002, OCC003).

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, activities A30, A31, and A36 are executed multiple times per trace (about 3.5 times instead of once), and the ordering between A30 and A36 (and A14 with them) changed from a fixed sequence to an interleaved or repeated pattern. Additionally, A36 now often leads to A30 instead of A25. This is supported by the large increase in occurrence and recurrence, the change in successor distribution, and the shift from fixed order to interleaving in REL and LAG items. The evidence is consistent across many indicators (19 items vs 0.5 expected by chance). A competing explanation is that the process was redesigned to include a loop or repetition of these activities, but the exact nature (e.g., a loop vs. multiple separate executions) cannot be determined from this evidence.

Other differences: A05's position shifted earlier and its successor distribution changed (A07 increased, A14 decreased) (POS001, DIR001). These may be related to the same underlying change but are not part of the main hypothesis.
