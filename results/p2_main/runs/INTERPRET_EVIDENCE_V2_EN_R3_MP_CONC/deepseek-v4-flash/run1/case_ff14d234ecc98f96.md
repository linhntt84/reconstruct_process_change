### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control‑flow structure: the process now executes a sequence in which A44 is followed by one of A19, A20 or A21, and that activity is then followed by A29. In the earlier window the corresponding sequence was A44 followed by A26, A27 and A28 (all three executed) and then A29. The new activities are mutually exclusive alternatives (they rarely co‑occur), whereas the old ones were all executed together. The change is directly shown by occurrence, successor and relation indicators, and the number of qualifying items (70) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.78). The lag between A44 and A29 also shortened, consistent with the removal of the intermediate A26–A28 block.

Other differences: The position of A29 shifted slightly earlier (mean from 0.434 to 0.398) and A44 slightly later (mean from 0.308 to 0.329), but these are minor and consistent with the structural change. [POS007, POS008]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new path coexists with the old one as a rare variant, but the old activities are observed in only about 1% of later traces, so the evidence strongly favours a replacement rather than a parallel branch.
