### Observed differences

- Activities A28, A30, A31, A32, A33, and A34 were present in about half of the traces before (48–52%) and in almost all traces after (98–99%) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006].
- From A25, the outgoing distribution changed: before it split roughly equally among A34, A28, A32, A33, A30, and A31 (each 14–19%); after it goes mainly to A30 (38%), A28 (31%), and A31 (30%), with almost none to A34, A32, or A33 (0.5–1%) [DIR001].
- From A28, A30, and A31, the share going to A26 dropped from about 30–36% to about 0.5%, while new successors A32, A33, and A34 appeared (each 8–13%) [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004].
- The distance between A25 and A32/A33/A34 increased from 1–3 steps to 4–5 steps; similarly, the distance between A28/A30/A31 and A26 increased from 1–3 to 4–5 steps [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006].
- Position shifts: A25, A28, A30, and A31 moved earlier (mean position decreased by 0.05–0.07), while A32, A33, and A34 moved later (mean position increased by 0.04) [POS001, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008].
- The elapsed time between A20 and A27 increased from a mean of 0.93 hours to 1.85 hours; between A29 and A27 it increased from 0.19 to 0.40 hours [TWAIT001, TWAIT002].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the process: a set of activities (A28, A30, A31, A32, A33, A34) that were executed in only about half of the cases before are now executed in almost all cases, and the order of execution has been rearranged. Specifically, A25 now leads to A30, A28, and A31 first, while A32, A33, and A34 are executed later (lag 4–5 instead of 1–3), and A26 has moved later relative to A28/A30/A31. This is directly shown by the large participation increase, the redistribution of successors from A25, the increased lags, and the position shifts. The number of control-flow items (51) far exceeds the 0.59 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact.

Other differences: The elapsed time between A20 and A27 and between A29 and A27 increased substantially (roughly doubled and more than doubled, respectively). This is a separate timing change, not explained by the structural reordering.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the participation increase reflects a change in case mix rather than a change in the process itself, but the consistent routing and ordering changes make this less likely.
