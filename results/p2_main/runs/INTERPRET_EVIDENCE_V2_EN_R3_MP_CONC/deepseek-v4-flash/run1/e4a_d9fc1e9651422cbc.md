### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A22, A31, A32, A37, A38, and A39 each occurred in all 200 traces; in the later window, A22 occurs in about 45% of traces and each of the other five in about 55%. [OCC001-OCC006]
- A20's outgoing transitions: before it went to A31, A32, A37, A38, A39 (each 17–24%); after it goes to A23 in about 99.5% of cases. [DIR001]
- A21's outgoing transitions: before it went to A22 in 100%; after it goes to A22 in about 45% and to A31/A32/A37/A38/A39 in about 55%. [DIR002]
- The group A31/A32/A37/A38/A39 used to lead to A23 (each about 17–26%); after they lead to A26 (each about 14–25%) and essentially no longer to A23. [DIR003-DIR007]
- Ordering: before, the group occurred before A21, A22, and A23; after, A23 and A21 occur before the group when they co-occur, and the group is absent in about 45% of traces. [REL008-REL012, REL020-REL024, REL030-REL039, POS001-POS010]
- The elapsed time between A26 and A19 decreased from about 0.94 hours to 0.45 hours (median from 0.93 to 0.45). [TWAIT001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed from a fixed sequence in which a block of activities (A31, A32, A37, A38, A39) always followed A20 and preceded A21, A22, and A23, to a variant in which this block is skipped in about 45% of traces and, when executed, occurs later (after A21 and A23) and leads to A26 instead of to A23. [OCC001-OCC006, DIR001-DIR007, REL008-REL012, REL020-REL024, REL030-REL039, POS001-POS010]

The supporting signs are the large drop in participation of the block, the redirection of A20 to A23, the redirection of the block to A26, and the reversal of order between the block and A21/A23. The main limitation is that the LAG items are based on a single trace in the later window and are not used; the conclusion rests on the OCC, DIR, REL, and POS indicators, which are consistent.

Other differences: The elapsed time between A26 and A19 decreased by about half (from about 0.94 to 0.45 hours). [TWAIT001] This is a separate timing change that is not part of the structural hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two windows sample different process variants that already existed, rather than a change over time; however, the large number of consistent indicators makes a real change more likely.
