### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new final activity A02 was added to the process. In the later window, A02 is executed in about 56% of traces, always at the end, and is preceded by many activities (A03, A07, A09, A10, A13, A14, A16, A19). In the earlier window A02 was absent. This is directly shown by its occurrence, its position, and the many incoming edges and co-occurrences. A01 also appears as a new activity that leads to A16, but it is less frequent (15%). The decrease in A09 and A13 participation, the earlier placement of A14, and the fact that A16 and A19 are no longer always final but now precede A02 are consistent with A02 becoming the new terminal step. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window contains a different mix of case types, but the appearance of a new activity and new edges is a strong structural signal.

Other differences: A09 and A13 are executed less often, A14 moves earlier, and A09–A14 and A09–A19 co-occurrence drops. These may be related to the addition of A02 but are not part of the main hypothesis.
