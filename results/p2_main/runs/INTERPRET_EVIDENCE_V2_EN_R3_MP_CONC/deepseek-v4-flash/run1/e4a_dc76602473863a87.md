### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process was simplified by removing the intermediate activities A14, A15, and A20 between A36 and A21. In the earlier window, A36 was followed by one of these three activities (each in about a third of traces), and that activity then led to A21. In the later window, A36 leads directly to A21, and A14, A15, and A20 no longer occur. This is directly shown by the disappearance of these activities, the loss of their outgoing edges, the change in A36's successors, and the lag between A36 and A21 dropping from 2 to 1. The large number of qualifying items (23 vs. 0.37 expected by chance) rules out a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that the activities were simply not executed in this particular sample is unlikely given the consistent relational and positional evidence, but cannot be fully excluded.

Other differences: A19's outgoing routing shifted toward A26 and away from A16 and A17, a separate change in path frequencies not involving the removed activities.
