### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained A38, A14, A15, and A23, with A38 always followed by A14, and either A20→A21 (about 50% of traces) or A22 (about 50%) appearing between A14 and A15. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL010, REL011]

- A38's outgoing transitions changed from 100% to A14 to 52.5% to A14 and 47.5% to A23. [DIR001]
- The distance between A38 and A23 changed: before they were always 4–5 steps apart; after, they are adjacent in 47.5% of traces and 4–5 steps apart in the rest. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a direct path from A38 to A23 was introduced, bypassing A14, A15, and the branch (A20→A21 or A22). In the later window, about half the traces take this direct path, while the other half still follow the original longer sequence. This is directly shown by A38's new outgoing edge to A23 [DIR001] and by A38 and A23 being adjacent in 47.5% of traces [LAG001], together with the corresponding drops in participation of A14, A15, A20, A21, and A22 [OCC001–OCC005, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL010, REL011]. The number of qualifying items (29) far exceeds the 0.45 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact.

Other differences: The branch activities A20→A21 and A22 still appear only in the full path, and their frequencies are reduced proportionally, which is consistent with the bypass rather than an independent change. The slight shifts in relative positions of A23 and A38 [POS001, POS002] are also consistent with shorter traces in the later window.

A competing explanation that the middle activities were simply removed from some traces without a new direct edge is ruled out by the direct evidence of A38→A23 in DIR001 and LAG001.
