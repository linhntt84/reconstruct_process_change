### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20, and A25 appear in about 30%, 41%, and 30% of traces respectively, while they were completely absent in the earlier window. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- These three new activities are mutually exclusive alternatives: they never co-occur in the same trace (e.g., A19 and A20: A_only 29.5%, B_only 41%, neither 29.5%, cooccurrence 0). [REL001, REL006]
- They are positioned between A27 and A29: A27 precedes them (A27 before A19 in 28.5% of traces, before A20 in 40%, before A25 in 30%), and they precede A29 (A19 before A29 in 29.5%, A20 before A29 in 39%, A25 before A29 in 29.5%). [REL003, REL007, REL010, REL005, REL009, REL012]
- The direct path A27→A28→A29 is partially replaced: A27 previously went to A28 in 98.5% of its outgoing transitions; now it goes to A28 in 43.8%, and to A19, A20, A25 in 15.4%, 22.4%, 16.9% respectively. [DIR004]
- A28 previously went to A29 in 99% of its outgoing transitions; now it goes to A29 in 55.4%, and to A19, A20, A25 in 12.9%, 17.3%, 12.4% respectively. [DIR005]
- The lag between A27 and A28 increased: before, A28 followed A27 immediately in 99% of traces; after, it follows immediately in 44% and with one intervening step in 54.5%. [LAG001]
- Similarly, the lag between A28 and A29 increased: before, A29 followed A28 immediately in 98.8%; after, immediately in 56% and with one intervening step in 43.5%. [LAG003]
- A10 also increased slightly (from 5% to 13.5% of traces), but with low support. [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a specific structural change: a set of alternative activities (A19, A20, A25) was introduced into the process between A27 and A29, either before or after A28, partially replacing the previously fixed A27→A28→A29 sequence. The signs are direct and consistent: these activities were absent before and appear in a substantial share of traces after, they are mutually exclusive, they are positioned between A27 and A29, and the routing from A27 and A28 now branches to them, with corresponding increases in the lags between A27–A28 and A28–A29. The screening count (29 items vs. 0.54 expected by chance) rules out a chance explanation.

Other differences: A10 also appears more often in the later window, but this is a minor change with limited support and is not part of the main hypothesis.

Limitation: The exact placement of the new activity within each trace (whether between A27 and A28 or between A28 and A29) cannot be determined from the aggregate lags, but the overall insertion between A27 and A29 is clear.
