### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A01 and A02 appear in about 28% and 39% of traces respectively, while they were absent in the earlier window. [OCC001, OCC002]
- A14 disappears: it was present in about 26% of traces (mean 0.53 per trace) with some repetition in the earlier window, and is absent in the later window. [OCC007, POS002, DIR004]
- A05, A07, A10, and A11 each drop from about 50% to 34% of traces. [OCC003–OCC006]
- A03's outgoing transitions shift from A12/A13 (about 51%/49%) to A02/A12 (about 54%/46%). [DIR002]
- A12's outgoing transitions change from all to A14 to all to A03. [DIR003]
- A02's outgoing transitions are all to A13 in the later window (it had no observed outgoing transitions before). [DIR001]
- A02 and A03 now co-occur in about 39% of traces, with A03 before A02 in about 21% and interleaved/repeated in about 19%; before they never co-occurred. [REL001]
- A02 and A13 now co-occur in 39% of traces, always with A02 before A13; before A13 appeared alone in about 51% of traces. [REL006]
- A12 and A14 no longer co-occur; before they co-occurred in about 26% of traces. [REL021]
- A03 and A14 no longer co-occur; before they co-occurred in about 26% of traces. [REL011]
- Lag distributions shift for several pairs: A03–A03 from lag 3 to lag 2, A03–A13 from lag 1 to lag 2, A12–A03 from lag 2 to lag 1, A12–A12 from lag 3 to lag 2, and A12–A13 from lag 3 to a mix of lag 3 and lag 5. [LAG001–LAG005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which activity A14 is removed and activity A02 is introduced, with the flow rerouted: A03 now leads to A02 (and A12) instead of A13, A02 leads to A13, and A12 now leads back to A03 instead of A14. This is directly shown by the disappearance of A14, the appearance of A02, the routing changes in DIR001–DIR004, and the co-occurrence changes in REL001, REL006, REL011, and REL021. The lag shifts are consistent with the new ordering.

Other differences: The participation of A05, A07, A10, and A11 decreased from about 50% to 34% of traces, which is not directly tied to the A02/A14 change and may reflect a separate reduction in an alternative branch. [OCC003–OCC006]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the decrease in A05/A07/A10/A11 and the A02/A14 change are independent modifications rather than one single change, but the routing evidence strongly links the A02/A14 replacement.
