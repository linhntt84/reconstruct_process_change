### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A03, was introduced in the later window, appearing in about half of the traces and inserted between existing activities. Specifically, A03 is placed after A05 (or interleaved with it) and before A11, and it also connects to A08 and A12 in both orders. This is directly shown by the appearance of A03, the new outgoing edges from A05, A08 and A12 to A03, the outgoing edges from A03 to A12/A11/A08, and the relational and positional shifts (A05 moving earlier, A03 always before A11). The large number of qualifying items (17 vs. 0.17 expected by chance) makes it very unlikely that this is a random sampling artefact.

Other differences: A05 moved earlier in the trace, and the lags between A08/A12 and A11 changed; these are consistent with the insertion of A03 but could also reflect a separate reordering. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A03 is a rare variant that happened to appear only in the later window, but the consistency across many indicators and the high item count make this implausible.
