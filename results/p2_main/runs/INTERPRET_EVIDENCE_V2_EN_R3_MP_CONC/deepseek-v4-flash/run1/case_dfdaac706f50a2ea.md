### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: a block of activities that was previously always executed (A27, A36, A37, A42) is now executed in only about half of cases, and when it is executed it appears as either A27 or the group A36/A37/A42, not all together. At the same time, A25 now connects directly to A28, bypassing the block that previously lay between them. This is directly shown by the drop in participation of A27/A36/A37/A42, the shift of A25's successor from those activities to A28, the adjacency of A25 and A28 in the later window, and the mutual exclusivity between A27 and the A36/A37/A42 group. The large number of qualifying items (50 vs. 0.72 expected by chance) makes it unlikely that these differences are sampling noise. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window reflects a different but stable process variant rather than a change over time, but the evidence is consistent with a genuine behavioural shift.

Other differences: A26's routing changed from almost always going to A27 to splitting between A27 and the A36/A37/A42 group, which fits the same alternative structure. Some lag items involving A36/A37/A42 have very low support in the later window (one trace), so the exact new positions of those activities are less certain, but the main bypass and participation changes are well supported.
