### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A16–A20 appeared in about 85% of traces (169 of 200); in the later window they appeared in about 18% (36 of 200). [OCC001–OCC005]
- In the earlier window, activities A21–A25 appeared in about 16% of traces (31 of 200); in the later window they appeared in about 82% (164 of 200). [OCC006–OCC010]
- Activity A15 always appears; its outgoing routing changed from mostly to A19 (85% before) to mostly to A24 (82% after). [DIR001]
- The two groups are mutually exclusive: A16–A20 and A21–A25 never co-occur in either window; the share of traces containing only one group flipped correspondingly. [REL015–REL019, REL024–REL028, etc.]
- Activity A29 co-occurs with the majority group in each window: with A16–A20 in 85% before, with A21–A25 in 82% after. [REL020, REL029, etc.]
- Within the A21–A25 group, co-occurrence among these activities rose from about 16% to 82%, and their relative order became more mixed (e.g., A21 and A22 appear in both orders in about 41% each after, versus 4.5% and 11% before). [REL051, REL056]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process shifted its dominant execution path from a branch containing activities A16–A20 to an alternative branch containing A21–A25, with activity A15 routing to the latter. In the earlier window, about 85% of traces took the A16–A20 branch; in the later window, about 82% took the A21–A25 branch. This is directly shown by the reversal in participation, the change in A15's outgoing routing, and the mutual exclusivity of the two groups. The large number of qualifying items (78 vs. 0.84 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely. The minority branch remains present in both windows, so this is a shift in routing frequencies rather than a replacement of one path by another. A competing explanation that the process changed in other ways (e.g., internal ordering within the A21–A25 group) cannot be fully ruled out, but the main shift is well supported.

Other differences: The internal ordering within the A21–A25 group became more mixed in the later window, but this is secondary and based on the same branch shift. [REL051, REL056]
