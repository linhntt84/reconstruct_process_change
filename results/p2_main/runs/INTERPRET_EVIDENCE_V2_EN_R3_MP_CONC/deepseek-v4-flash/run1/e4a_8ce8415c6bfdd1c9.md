### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A21 always led to A22, and A22 always led to A23; A22 occurred in every trace. In the later window, A21 leads to A22 in about 56.5% of traces and to a set of five activities (A27, A28, A33, A34, A35) in the remaining 43.5% of traces. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, REL007]
- The five activities A27, A28, A33, A34, A35 were absent in the earlier window and each appears in about 43.5% of traces in the later window, always together in the same traces, with no fixed order among them (both orders occur in similar proportions). [OCC002–OCC006, REL018–REL027]
- In the later window, A23 still occurs in every trace, but it is reached either via A22 (56.5%) or via the new block of activities (43.5%). [REL007, REL013–REL017]
- The position of A21 shifted slightly earlier (mean from about 0.49 to 0.46 of the trace). [POS001]
- The elapsed time between A39 and A41 increased from about 1.0 hour to about 2.0 hours on average. [TWAIT001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control-flow structure: the process now has two alternative paths after A21. One path still goes through A22 to A23; the other path goes through a block of new activities (A27, A28, A33, A34, A35) that are executed together in variable order before reaching A23. This is directly shown by the consistent co-occurrence and ordering patterns across OCC, DIR, REL, and POS indicators. The large number of control-flow items (45 vs. 0.49 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely.

Other differences: The timing between A39 and A41 roughly doubled in the later window, which is a separate change not linked to the structural shift. The slight earlier placement of A21 is a minor positional change.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new block of activities is a rare variant that happened to appear in the later window, but the high frequency (43.5%) and the coherent relational structure make this unlikely.
