### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A14, A17, A18, A19 and A20 appeared in about 17%, 25%, 27%, 14% and 19% of traces respectively; in the later window none of them appear (0% participation). [OCC001, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]

- A28 remains in all traces, but its position shifted later (mean relative position from about 0.39 to 0.47) and it now always follows the new activities (e.g., A15 before A28 in all cases, A16 before A28 in all cases). [POS011, REL018, REL027, REL053, REL056, REL058]
- The new activities A15, A16, A25–A27 co-occur with each other in every trace and appear in mixed order (both orders roughly equal). [REL010, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL051, REL052, REL055]
- The elapsed time between A44 and A45 decreased from a mean of about 1.03 hours to about 0.50 hours. [TWAIT001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: the earlier optional activities A14, A17–A20 were removed and replaced by a new set of mandatory activities A15, A16, A25–A27 that now occur in every trace, followed by A28. This is directly shown by the disappearance/appearance of these activities, the change in outgoing transitions from A43, and the new ordering relations with A28. The timing between A44 and A45 also halved, indicating a separate change in timing. A competing explanation that the activities were merely renamed is unlikely because the new activities appear in every trace while the old ones were optional, and the ordering with A28 changed. However, the evidence cannot rule out that the old activities still exist but were not observed in the later window; given the complete absence and the new mandatory set, the replacement is strongly supported.
