### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the repetition and routing around activities A15 and A16. In the later window, both activities occur more often per trace and are repeated more frequently, while the direct transition from A16 to A15 becomes more common and the transition from A16 to A42 becomes less common. This is consistent with a shift toward a path that repeats A15 and A16 more often, possibly with other activities inserted between them, and away from the A16→A42 path. The four control-flow items are far above the chance expectation (4 items vs 0.47 expected), and they point in the same direction. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a rare variant that became more frequent, but the consistency and magnitude of the changes make this unlikely.
