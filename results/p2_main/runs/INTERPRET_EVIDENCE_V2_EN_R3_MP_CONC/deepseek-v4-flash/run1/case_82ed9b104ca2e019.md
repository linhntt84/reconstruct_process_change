### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which a new step, consisting of one of a set of alternative activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27), is inserted between A04 and A28, and A43 now leads to A04 instead of directly to A28. This is directly shown by the appearance of these activities, their outgoing transitions to A28, the increased co-occurrence and ordering of A04 before A28, the increased lag between A04 and A28, and the shift of A43's outgoing from A28 to A04. The alternative activities are mutually exclusive, as they rarely co-occur. The large number of qualifying items (62 vs. 0.73 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely.

Other differences: A06 and A08, which were present in about 20% of traces before, become rare (under 5%), suggesting they are no longer used. Also, A04's position moves earlier in the trace (mean from 0.54 to 0.36). [OCC002, OCC003, POS001]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities were always possible but simply not observed in the earlier window due to sampling, though the consistency and volume of evidence make this unlikely.
