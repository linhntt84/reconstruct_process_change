### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process shifted from a path that executed activities A21–A25, with A20 leading to A24 and A38 handing over to A04, to a path that executes A26–A30, with A20 leading to A29 and A38 handing over to A39. This is supported by the large, consistent changes in participation, co-occurrence and successor distributions across many activities. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two windows reflect different case types or a change in case mix rather than a modification of a single process.

Other differences: A02, A06, A08 and A10 increased from about 2–4% to 14–19% participation, and A04’s position moved earlier; these may be part of the same shift but are not required for the main hypothesis.
