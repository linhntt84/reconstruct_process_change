### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a single sequence through all six activities (A28, A30, A31, A32, A33, A34) to a choice between two alternative branches, each containing three activities, followed by A26. In the later window, A25 leads to either the A28/A30/A31 group or the A32/A33/A34 group, and the chosen group then leads to A26; the two groups are mutually exclusive and the overall sequence is shorter. This is supported directly by occurrence drops, routing changes, co-occurrence patterns, and lag reductions. A competing explanation that the observed differences are sampling artefacts is ruled out by the large number of qualifying items (58 vs. 0.63 expected by chance). The main limitation is that the exact internal order within each branch is not fully specified, but the mutual exclusivity and shortened lags are clear.
