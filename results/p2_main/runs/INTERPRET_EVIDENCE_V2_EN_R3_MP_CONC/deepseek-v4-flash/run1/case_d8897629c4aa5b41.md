### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A14, A15 and A20 appear in about 29%, 41% and 30% of traces respectively, whereas they were absent in the earlier window. They never occur together in the same trace, so they behave as alternatives. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006]
- In the earlier window, A22 always led to A23 and A23 always led to A24. In the later window, A22 leads to A23 in about 44% of cases and to A14/A15/A20 in the remaining 56%; A23 leads to A24 in about 57% of cases and to A14/A15/A20 in the remaining 43%. [DIR004, DIR005]
- The new activities A14/A15/A20 lead to A23 and A24 in roughly equal shares (about 53–58% to A23, 42–47% to A24). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- In the later window, A14/A15/A20 occur around the middle of the trace (mean position about 0.51). [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- The distance between A22 and A23 changes from immediately adjacent to one step apart in about 56% of cases; A22 to A24 changes from two steps to three steps in about 99.5% of cases; A23 to A24 changes from immediately adjacent to one step apart in about 44% of cases. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- In the later window, A14/A15/A20 always occur after A22 and before A24, while their order relative to A23 is mixed (both orders occur). [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a simple linear sequence A22 → A23 → A24 to a structure in which a set of alternative activities (A14, A15, A20) is introduced in a subset of cases, inserted between A22 and A24 and also interacting with A23. The appearance of these activities, the redistribution of outgoing transitions from A22 and A23, and the increased lags between A22–A23, A22–A24 and A23–A24 all point to the insertion of new steps. The new activities are mutually exclusive alternatives, and their order relative to A23 is not fixed. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities are not truly alternatives but simply never co-occur in this sample; however, the consistency of the routing and lag changes makes the insertion hypothesis the most direct interpretation.

Other differences: The position of A22 shifted slightly earlier (mean from about 0.48 to 0.46), which is consistent with the insertion of new activities after it. [POS004]
