### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process changed from a branching after A26 into several alternative activities (A27 and a group A32, A33, A38, A39, A40) to a single dominant path through A27, with the other activities becoming nearly obsolete. In the earlier window, about half the traces contained A27 and about half contained the other activities, and A26's outgoing transitions were split among them. In the later window, A27 is present in almost all traces, A26 almost always leads to A27, and the other activities are almost absent. This is supported by the large increase in A27 participation, the large decrease in the other activities, the shift in A26's successor distribution, and the corresponding changes in co-occurrence relations. The evidence is consistent across OCC, DIR, and REL indicators, and the number of qualifying items (63) far exceeds the 0.76 expected by chance.

Limitations: The LAG and POS items for the disappearing activities are based on very few traces in the later window, so they do not add reliable detail. Also, we cannot rule out that the other activities still exist but are executed in a different part of the process not captured by these relations, though their near-zero participation makes that unlikely.

Other differences: A26 and A28 shifted slightly in their relative positions (A26 later, A28 earlier), which may be a consequence of the structural change. [POS001, POS002]
