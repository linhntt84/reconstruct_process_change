### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the ordering between A01 and A04 changed: in the earlier window A01 and A04 could appear in either order or interleaved, while in the later window A01 consistently precedes A04, with A01 moving earlier, A04 moving later, and the direct successor relations changing accordingly (A01 now always leads to A04; A04 no longer leads to A01). This is supported by direct relational, positional, and lag evidence. The control-flow evidence is extensive (20 items vs 0.13 expected by chance), so the difference is not a chance artifact.
