### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20, and A25 occur much earlier in the trace: their mean relative position dropped from about 0.60–0.61 to about 0.32–0.34. [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- The order between these activities and A26/A27 reversed. Before, A26/A27 preceded A19/A20/A25 in about 31–38% of traces; after, A19/A20/A25 precede A26/A27 in about 30–36% of traces, and the reverse order essentially disappeared. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Outgoing transitions changed: A19, A20, and A25 now go to A26 in about 88–94% of their outgoing transitions, whereas before they went mainly to A28 and A32 (each about 45–56%). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- A27 now goes to A28 in about 98% of its transitions (before about 47% to A28, with the rest split among A20, A19, A25). [DIR004]
- A28 now goes to A32 in about 96% of its transitions (before about 50% to A32, with the rest split among A20, A25, A19). [DIR005]
- A45 no longer goes to A26 (before about 85% to A26); now it goes to A20, A19, and A25 (about 29% each). [DIR006]
- A15 occurs less often per trace: mean occurrences dropped from 1.89 to 1.58, and its recurrence rate fell from about 47% to 38%. [OCC001]
- Distances between some activity pairs changed: A27–A28 and A28–A32 became more often adjacent (lag 1 instead of lag 2), A27–A32 became closer (lag 2 instead of lag 3), and A45–A26 became further apart (lag 2–3 instead of lag 1). [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process was reordered so that a group of activities (A19, A20, A25) now occurs earlier in the trace, before A26 and A27, and that the control flow from these activities now leads to A26 instead of A28/A32. This is directly shown by the consistent reversal in relative order (REL), the earlier positions (POS), and the changed outgoing transitions (DIR). The LAG items for A27–A28, A28–A32, and A27–A32 are consistent with this reordering, though some LAG items (LAG001–LAG003) have very low support in the later window and are not relied upon. The large number of qualifying items (26 vs. 0.6 expected by chance) rules out random sampling as an explanation.

Other differences: A15 occurs less often per trace and is less frequently repeated, which is not part of the main reordering hypothesis. A45 also changed its outgoing target from A26 to A20/A19/A25, which may be a separate routing change. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a shift between two distinct process variants rather than a single reordering, but the consistency across multiple indicators makes this less likely.
