### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A01, was introduced into the process, appearing in every trace in the later window and placed between existing activities, thereby increasing the distance between them. The screening count (23 items vs 0.23 expected by chance) is far above what an unchanged process would produce. The direct evidence of A01's occurrence, its connections to many activities, and the consistent increase in lags all point to this single change. A competing explanation that A01 existed but was unobserved in the earlier window is unlikely given it appears in 100% of traces later and is connected to many activities. The exact position of A01 varies (sometimes before, sometimes after other activities), so it is not a single fixed insertion point, but the overall addition is clear.

Other differences: A05 and A12 occur later in the trace, which is consistent with A01 being inserted before them in many cases. No other independent differences are evident.
