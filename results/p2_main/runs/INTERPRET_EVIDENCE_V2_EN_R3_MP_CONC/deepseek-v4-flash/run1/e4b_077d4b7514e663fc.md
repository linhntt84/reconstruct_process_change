### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: several activities (A01, A03, A08, A13, A14, A17, A21) shifted from being handled by a broad set of resources to a different, more concentrated set, with A08 becoming fully dedicated to a single resource. This is supported by 8 resource-related evidence items, far exceeding the 0.005 expected by chance, and the pattern is consistent across activities. The timing change (increased wait between A04 and A01) is also supported by a strong item, but it is a separate difference.

Other differences: The wait time between A04 and A01 increased, especially in the upper tail, and the wait between A20 and A10 became more frequent but slightly shorter. These timing changes are not part of the resource allocation hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the resource changes reflect a change in the pool of available users rather than a deliberate reallocation, but the evidence does not allow distinguishing that.
