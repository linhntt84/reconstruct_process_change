### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A02 was removed from the process, while activity A10 became more central and partially took over the role A02 used to play. In the earlier window A02 was present in about half of the traces and co-occurred with A03, A07, A09, A13 and A14; in the later window A02 is entirely absent, and A10's participation roughly doubles while its co-occurrence with A07, A09 and A13 increases correspondingly. The successor changes (A08 now leading to A03 instead of A10, A10 now leading to A07/A11 instead of A02/A13) are consistent with a re-routing around the removed activity. The number of qualifying control-flow items (60) far exceeds the 0.67 expected by chance, so the overall difference is not a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A03 and A14 participation halves, and their relative positions shift later (median position for A03 from 0.36 to 0.55, for A14 from 0.44 to 0.63). These may be consequences of the same re-routing, but they are not part of the main hypothesis. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A02 was not removed but simply became extremely rare in the sampled window; however, its complete absence across all 200 traces and the consistent disappearance of all its outgoing edges make this unlikely.
