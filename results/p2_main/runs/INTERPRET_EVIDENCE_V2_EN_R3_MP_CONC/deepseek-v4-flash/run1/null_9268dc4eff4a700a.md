### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a swap in the relative order of activities A30 and A31 between A26 and A27. In the earlier window the typical sequence was A26 → A31 → A30 → A27; in the later window it is A26 → A30 → A31 → A27. This is directly shown by the successor distributions (DIR002, DIR003) and the lag shifts (LAG001–LAG004), all with high empirical strength. The change is observed in about half of the traces (support ~100/200 for the A30/A31 items), so it reflects a dominant path rather than a universal one. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed shift is a sampling artifact of a rare variant, but the consistency across seven items and the screening count (7 items vs 0.33 expected) make this unlikely.

Other differences: A26 also increased its share of successors going to A32 (from 22% to 30%) and decreased to A31 (from 31% to 17%), which is partly consistent with the order swap but also indicates an additional routing change toward A32. [DIR001]
