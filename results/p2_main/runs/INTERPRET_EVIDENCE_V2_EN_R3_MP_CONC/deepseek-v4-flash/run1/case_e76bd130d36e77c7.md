### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process has been reordered: A26 and A27 now occur earlier in the trace, before the group A35/A36/A41/A42/A43, whereas previously they occurred after that group. Additionally, A27 and A30 have swapped roles as successors: A27 now follows A25, while A30 now follows the group A35/A36/A41/A42/A43, and A26 no longer leads to A30. The supporting signs are consistent across DIR (successor changes), POS (position shifts), and REL (order flips), and the number of evidence items (43) far exceeds the 0.8 expected by chance. Some LAG items have very low support in one window, so exact lag distances are less reliable, but the overall pattern is robust. A competing explanation that the changes reflect a shift in the mix of process variants rather than a single reordering cannot be fully ruled out, but the direct relational evidence makes a reordering the most plausible interpretation.
