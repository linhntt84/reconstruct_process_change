### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a single sequence in which A25 was followed by all activities A28–A38 and then A26, to a process in which A25 is followed by either the A28–A33 block or the A34–A38 block (but not both), and then A26. The two blocks are mutually exclusive in the later window, each occurring in about half the traces, and A25 and A26 remain present in all traces. This is directly shown by the drop in participation of the middle activities, the near-zero co-occurrence between the two groups, and the redistribution of A25's outgoing transitions. The LAG items are based on a single trace in the later window and are not used. A competing explanation that the two groups are simply skipped independently is ruled out by their mutual exclusivity; the evidence cannot, however, determine the exact routing logic (e.g., a decision rule) behind the choice.

Other differences: A25's position shifts later and A26's position shifts slightly earlier, consistent with a shorter middle section, but these are secondary to the main structural change. [POS001, POS002]
