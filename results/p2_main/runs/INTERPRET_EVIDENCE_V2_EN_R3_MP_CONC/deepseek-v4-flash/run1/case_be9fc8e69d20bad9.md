### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 did not occur at all (0% of traces). In the later window, each occurs in about 18–22% of traces (A14 21.5%, A15 17.5%, A20 19.5%, A21 20.5%, A22 21%). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new step was inserted between A38 and A23, where the new step is one of five alternative activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22). In the earlier window, A38 was directly followed by A23; in the later window, A38 is followed by one of these five activities, which is then followed by A23. This is directly shown by the change in A38's outgoing transitions (from A23 to the five new activities), the new outgoing edges from each new activity to A23, the relational order (A38 before new activity before A23), and the lag increase from 1 to 2. The five activities are mutually exclusive and together cover all traces in the later window, so the insertion is systematic rather than a rare variant. The number of qualifying items (34) far exceeds the 0.43 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A23's position shifted slightly later (mean relative position from 0.376 to 0.401), consistent with the inserted step. No timing or resource changes are present in the evidence.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the five activities existed before but were unobserved in the earlier window; however, their complete absence in all 200 earlier traces and their appearance in every later trace makes this unlikely.
