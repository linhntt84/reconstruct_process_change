### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process was extended with two new activities, A01 and A02, with A01 inserted between the early activities (A06/A13) and A04, and A02 added as a final activity after A12 and after A04. In the later window, A01 and A02 appear in every trace, A06 and A13 now lead to A01 instead of A04, A01 leads to A04, and A12 leads to A02. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 and A02 existed but were simply not executed in the earlier sample; however, their complete absence in 200 traces and the coherent routing changes make this unlikely.

Other differences: The existing activities A04, A06, A12, A13 all shifted to earlier positions, and the lag distributions between A06/A13 and A04/A12 changed accordingly. These are consistent with the insertion of A01 and A02 and do not require a separate hypothesis.
