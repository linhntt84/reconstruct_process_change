### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed such that a previously common segment involving A10 and the activities A19, A20, A25, A26, A27 was largely removed or made optional, while activities A02, A04, A06 became more frequent, and the routing from A43 and A48 changed to go to A28 and A49 respectively instead of to A10 and the removed activities. In the later window, A10 is rarely executed, and when it is, it is no longer followed by A49 as before; instead the flow goes to A28, A47, A16 and others. The co-occurrence and lag data consistently show that A10 is no longer part of the main flow with A14, A15, A28, A43, A44, A48, A49. The large number of qualifying items (89 vs 0.99 expected by chance) and the consistency across participation, successor, co-occurrence and lag indicators make a real change likely. A competing explanation that this is a rare variant that existed before is unlikely given the magnitude and breadth of the shift, but the evidence cannot rule out that the process changed to a different variant that was already present in small numbers.
