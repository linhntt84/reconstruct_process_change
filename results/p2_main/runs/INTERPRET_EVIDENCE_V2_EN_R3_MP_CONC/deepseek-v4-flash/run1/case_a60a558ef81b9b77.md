### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural reordering of the process: the block of activities A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 now occurs before A23 instead of after it, and A42 now leads into that block rather than directly into A23. This is shown directly by the reversal in order between these activities and A23 (REL), the shift of these activities to earlier positions (POS), and the change in their outgoing edges from A25/A29 to A23 (DIR). The number of qualifying items (34) is far above the 0.48 expected by chance, and the pattern is consistent across many activities and indicators. A competing explanation of a rare variant is implausible given the volume and consistency of the evidence.

Other differences: A12 became more frequent (19% to 33% of traces). A24 and A25 now form a tighter chain A24 → A25 → A29, with A24 no longer branching to the A14/A15/A20/A21/A22 block and A25 no longer branching to those activities. These changes are not part of the main reordering hypothesis but are consistent with a general simplification of the control flow around the same activities.
