### Observed differences

- In the later window, A26 and A27 now occur before A35, A36, and A41 in almost all traces, whereas before they occurred after them. For example, A26 before A35 went from 0% to 98% of traces, and A27 before A35 from 0% to 97.5%. [REL010, REL013]
- A02 participation dropped from about 75% of traces to about 21%, and its mean occurrences per trace from 0.81 to 0.23. [OCC001]
- A02's successor changed: before 91% of its outgoing transitions went to A50, after only 13% went to A50, with many other successors appearing. [DIR001]
- A06 and A10 increased: A06 from 6.5% to 19% participation, A10 from 8.5% to 21.5%. [OCC002, OCC003]
- A25's successor changed from A35/A36/A41 to A27 (96% of transitions). [DIR005]
- A26's successor changed from A30 (98.5%) to A35/A36/A41. [DIR006]
- A49's successor changed from A02 (72%) to A50 (83%). [DIR011]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the order of a block of activities reversed: in the earlier window A35, A36, and A41 were executed before A26 and A27; in the later window A26 and A27 are executed before A35, A36, and A41. This is supported by the near-complete flip in pairwise order for all six pairs (A26/A27 with A35/A36/A41) [REL010–REL015], by lag changes such as A26–A30 moving from lag 1 to lag 4 and A26–A36 moving from lag 5 to lags 1–3 [LAG005, LAG006], and by the successor changes of A25 and A26 [DIR005, DIR006]. The change is consistent across many pairs and is based on all 200 traces in each window.

Other differences: A02 became much less frequent (from 75% to 21% participation) and its successor changed from A50 to a spread of activities [OCC001, DIR001]; A06 and A10 increased [OCC002, OCC003]; A49's successor changed from A02 to A50 [DIR011]. These are separate from the order reversal.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the order reversal and the A02 decline are part of a single larger restructuring, but the evidence does not require that connection.
