### Observed differences

- Activities A19, A20, and A25 were present in about 35%, 30%, and 35% of traces in the earlier window, respectively, and are essentially absent in the later window (A19 0%, A20 0.5%, A25 0%). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

- The co-occurrence of A19, A20, and A25 with A14, A12, A24, A26, A31, and A41 dropped from about 10–35% of traces to 0% (or near 0%), while the other activities continue to appear alone. [REL001–REL021]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activities A19, A20, and A25 were removed from the process (or are no longer executed) in the later window. In the earlier window, these activities appeared in about 30–35% of traces, typically after A41 and before A26. In the later window, they are essentially absent, and A41 now leads directly to A26 in about 92% of its transitions, consistent with the removal of the intermediate activities. This is directly supported by the drop in occurrence, the change in A41's successor distribution, the disappearance of co-occurrences, and the change in lag between A41 and A26 from 2 to 1. The large number of evidence items (36 vs 0.61 expected by chance) makes it unlikely that this is a sampling artifact.

Other differences: The lag between A24 and A31 decreased (more often adjacent), and small positional shifts for A14, A26, and A41 were observed; these are likely consequences of the removal of the intermediate activities but are not part of the main hypothesis. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the activities still exist but were not observed in the sample; however, their complete absence from 200 traces after being present in 30–35% of 200 traces before makes this unlikely.
