### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from having A10 as a common early step to having A19 and A20 as mandatory activities, with A10 becoming optional and less frequent. In the earlier window, A10 occurred in about two-thirds of traces and typically led directly to A16; in the later window, A10 occurs in about a quarter of traces, and when it occurs it is always accompanied by A19, while A19 and A20 now appear in every trace and are preceded by A14/A15/A16. This is consistent with A10 being demoted from a standard step to an optional one, and A19/A20 becoming a required core. The evidence is direct and consistent across occurrence, co-occurrence, and lag indicators. A competing explanation that the change is only a shift in variant frequencies cannot be fully ruled out, but the magnitude and the many agreeing indicators make a real structural change more plausible.

Other differences: A25, A26 and A27 also increased in participation (e.g., A27 from about 20% to 49% of traces), and A43's outgoing transitions shifted from A28 (46%) to A19 (83%). [OCC009, DIR007] These are consistent with the same overall shift toward A19/A20 as a central part of the process.
