### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A06 was introduced as a step between A46 and A19/A20: in the later window, A46 leads to A06, A06 leads to A19/A20, and A06 is present in most traces, whereas before A46 led directly to A19/A20 and A06 was rare. This is directly shown by the successor changes and the co-occurrence/order relations. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A06 existed before but was too rare to be captured reliably; however, the magnitude and consistency of the change make this unlikely.

Other differences: the relative order of A26/A27 and A35/A36/A41/A42/A43 reversed, with A25 no longer leading to A27 and A26 now leading to A30; A30 no longer follows the group of A35/A36/A41/A42/A43. A02 and A08 also became less frequent. These appear to be separate structural changes rather than consequences of the A06 insertion.
