### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: after activity A15, the process now executes the group A16–A18 instead of the group A19–A21. This is shown directly by the shift in participation of these activities, the redistribution of A15's outgoing transitions, and the corresponding changes in co-occurrence with A25. The order within the new group is not fixed (both orders occur), similar to the earlier group. The large number of qualifying items (34 vs. 0.52 expected by chance) rules out a chance sampling difference. A competing explanation that the earlier group was simply skipped is not supported, because the new group appears in the same position and with the same relationship to A15 and A25.
