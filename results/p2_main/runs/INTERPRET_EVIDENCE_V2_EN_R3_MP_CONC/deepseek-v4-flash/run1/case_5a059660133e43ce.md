### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that a new activity A01 was introduced as an alternative start to A06, and that the routing around A02, A06, A08, and A12 changed. In the later window, A01 appears in about 22% of traces as the first event, and it is mutually exclusive with A06 and A15 (they never co-occur). A06, which was previously always first, is no longer always first and now leads to A15. A02's successors now include A01 and A12, while A15 disappears from A02's successors. A08 no longer leads to A12, instead leading to A09, A04, and A02. A12 becomes less frequent and moves earlier, and its lag relative to A02 shortens while its lag relative to A08 lengthens. The number of evidence items (14) far exceeds the 0.23 expected by chance, and the changes are consistent across occurrence, position, and routing indicators.

Other differences: A12's overall participation and recurrence decrease, and its position shifts earlier. These are consistent with the rerouting but are not part of the main hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that some of the observed changes reflect sampling variation in a process that already had rare variants, but the magnitude and coherence of the evidence make this unlikely.
