### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a fixed linear sequence A01 → A02 → A04 → A03 to a more flexible structure in which A02 and A04 can be executed in either order, A02 can be followed directly by A03 (bypassing A04), and A01 now also branches directly to A04. This is directly shown by the successor distributions (new A02→A03 and A04→A02 edges, increased A01→A04), the loss of a fixed order between A02 and A04, and the corresponding shifts in positions and lags. The screening count (10 control-flow items vs 0.1 expected by chance) makes it very unlikely that these differences are random sampling artefacts. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the process always had these alternative paths but they were too rare to be observed in the earlier window; however, the magnitude and consistency of the changes make a genuine structural shift more plausible.
