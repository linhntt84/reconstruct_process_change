### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports that the process changed by adding a new set of activities (A07, A15, A16, A17) that were not observed before, and re-routing existing activities to connect to them. This is supported by the appearance of these activities in the later window, the new cooccurrences and successor relations involving them, and the shifts in positions and lags. The number of qualified control-flow items (92) far exceeds the 0.6 expected by chance, indicating a substantial change.

Other differences: A05's mean occurrences increased, A18's mean decreased, and several existing activities changed their successors (e.g., A10, A12, A08). These are likely part of the same re-routing.

Limitation: We cannot rule out that some of these activities existed but were not observed in the earlier window due to sampling, but the magnitude and consistency make that unlikely.
