### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A33, A34, A35, A37, A38, A39, A40, A41, A42, and A43 each appeared in essentially every trace (about 100% participation, mean occurrences ~1.0). In the later window, each of these appears in only about 46–55% of traces, with mean occurrences ~0.5. [OCC001–OCC010]
- In the earlier window, A30 was followed directly by A33, A34, A35, A37, and A38 (each about 17–23% of outgoing transitions) and rarely by others. In the later window, A30 also leads directly to A39, A40, A41, A42, and A43 (each about 8–13%), while the shares to A33–A38 drop to about 7–12%. [DIR001]
- In the earlier window, A33–A38 and A39–A43 always co-occurred in the same trace (cooccurrence 1.0), with A33–A38 before A39–A43. In the later window, A33–A38 and A39–A43 almost never co-occur (cooccurrence about 1–2% for pairs like A33–A39), and each group appears in about half the traces. [REL025, REL033, REL040, REL046, REL051, etc.]
- In the earlier window, A30 co-occurred with each of A33–A43 in all traces. In the later window, A30 co-occurs with A33–A38 in about 46–47% of traces and with A39–A43 in about 54–55% of traces, meaning A30 is present but the downstream groups are alternatives. [REL001–REL010, REL006–REL010]
- Positions shifted: A30 moved later (mean position from 0.55 to 0.64), A33–A38 moved later (from ~0.62–0.63 to ~0.72–0.73), and A39–A43 moved earlier (from ~0.75–0.76 to ~0.71–0.72). [POS001–POS010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a single sequential path through a set of activities to a choice between two alternative branches. Specifically, in the earlier window, A30 was followed by a sequence that included A33–A38 and then A39–A43, all in the same trace. In the later window, A30 leads either to the group A33–A38 or to the group A39–A43, but not both; each group occurs in about half the traces, and the two groups almost never co-occur. This is directly shown by the drop in participation of all these activities, the new direct successors of A30 to A39–A43, and the near-zero cooccurrence between the two groups. The position shifts are consistent with the two groups becoming alternatives rather than a sequence.

Other differences: A31, which previously preceded all of A33–A43, now co-occurs with each group only in about half the traces, and A33 now has a direct successor A31 that it did not have before, though the overall order between A31 and A33 remains mostly A31 before A33. [DIR002, REL011]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window simply contains a different mix of case types (e.g., some cases skip the middle group), but the consistency across many activities and the direct successor changes make a structural change the most plausible interpretation.
