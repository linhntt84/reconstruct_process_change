Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process order changed such that a group of activities (A14, A16, A19) that previously occurred early now occurs late, and a group (A15, A17, A18) that previously occurred late now occurs early, with corresponding successor changes involving A20 and A25.
