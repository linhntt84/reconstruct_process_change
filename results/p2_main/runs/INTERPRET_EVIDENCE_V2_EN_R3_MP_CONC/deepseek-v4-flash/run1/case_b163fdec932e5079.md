### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activity A27 occurred in every trace (mean 1.0 per trace); in the later window it occurred in about 57% of traces (mean 0.565). [OCC003]
- Activities A32, A33, A38, A39 and A40 were absent in the earlier window (0 traces) and appeared in about 44% of traces in the later window (each with mean occurrences around 0.44). [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]
- The outgoing transitions of A26 changed: before, 98.5% of its transitions went to A27; after, only 55.7% went to A27, with the remainder distributed among A32, A33, A38, A39 and A40 (each about 6–10%). [DIR004]
- A27 and the new activities are mutually exclusive in the later window: for example, A27 and A32 co-occur in 0% of traces, with A27-only in 56.5% and A32-only in 43.5%. [REL026, REL028, REL029, REL030]
- The new activities appear after A26 and A28: e.g., A26 before A32 in 43% of traces after (was 0% before), and A28 before A32 in 43% after. [REL020, REL031]
- The new activities are executed in a flexible order among themselves: e.g., A32 and A33 appear in both orders (A before B in 23.5%, B before A in 20%) in the later window. [REL036]
- A06 became less frequent (participation dropped from 24% to 10%) and its position shifted later in the trace (mean relative position from 0.31 to 0.49). [OCC001, POS001]
- The gap between A06 and A16 increased: before, A06 was always immediately followed by A16 (lag 1 in 100% of cases); after, only 50% at lag 1, with lags 2–4 appearing. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which a previously mandatory step, A27, became optional and is often replaced by a set of new activities (A32, A33, A38, A39, A40). In the earlier window, A27 was present in every trace and always followed A26. In the later window, A27 appears in about 57% of traces, and in the remaining 43% it is replaced by one of the new activities, which appear after A26/A28 and are executed in a flexible order among themselves. This is directly supported by the drop in A27 participation, the appearance of the new activities, the shift in A26's outgoing transitions, the mutual exclusivity between A27 and the new activities, and the positional and ordering evidence. The main limitation is that the new activities were not observed in the earlier window, so we cannot fully rule out that they existed but were never sampled; however, with 200 traces per window, their complete absence before is unlikely to be a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A06 became less frequent and moved later in the trace, and its distance to A16 increased. These changes may be related to the same underlying shift (e.g., A06 no longer always leads directly to A16) but are not part of the main hypothesis.
