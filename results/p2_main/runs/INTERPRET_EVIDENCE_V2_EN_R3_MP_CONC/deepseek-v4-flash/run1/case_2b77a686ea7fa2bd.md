### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a major restructuring of the process: a set of activities that were present in a minority of traces in the earlier window (A19, A22–A25, A10, A08) are no longer observed, while a new set of activities (A20, A21, A30–A33) appears in essentially all traces in the later window, and A04 becomes a common intermediate step between A48 and the new activities. The number of qualifying items (130 vs. 1.06 expected by chance) is far beyond what an unchanged process would produce, and the pattern is consistent across occurrence, successor, position and co-occurrence indicators. The main limitation is that zero participation only means the activity was not observed, but the consistency across many indicators supports the change.

Other differences: A33 remains universal but its position shifts later (mean from 0.39 to 0.48), and A48's position shifts slightly earlier (mean from 0.33 to 0.28); A04's position also shifts earlier (mean from 0.54 to 0.33). These are consistent with the new segment after A04.
