### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a previously mandatory block of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) is now executed in only about half of the cases, while activities A28, A43 and A48 remain present in all cases. This is directly shown by the halving of participation for those five activities and by the drop in co-occurrence of A19 and A20 with each other and with A28, A43, A48 and A49. The screening count (59 items vs. 0.85 expected by chance) makes it very unlikely that these differences are random sampling noise. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL010, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017]

Other differences: The routing around A06 changed – A06 no longer leads to A49, A48 no longer leads to A06, and A43 now leads to A28 instead of A19. These routing changes are consistent with the block of activities being skipped or replaced, but they are not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR002, DIR005, DIR007]

A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the later window contains a different mix of case types, so the observed drop in participation reflects a change in the population of cases rather than a change in the process itself.
