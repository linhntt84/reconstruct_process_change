### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process likely changed from a branching structure where activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38, and A39 were alternatives (only one executed per case) to a sequential structure where all of them are executed in a fixed order (A33/A35/A36 before A37/A38/A39). This is supported by the doubling of participation of these activities, the change from zero co-occurrence to near-universal co-occurrence with a consistent order, the change in outgoing transitions from A31 to A37/A38/A39, and the increase in lag between A30 and the later activities. The evidence cannot rule out that the process was always sequential but with a large optional branch that became mandatory; however, the relational data showing mutual exclusivity earlier (e.g., A33 and A37 never together) strongly suggests an alternative structure.

Other differences: A04 became less frequent and moved later in the trace; A06 and A10 increased slightly. These are not part of the main hypothesis.
