### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a timing change: the time between activities A14/A15 and A20 increased substantially, roughly doubling, in the later window. This is directly shown by two time indicators (TWAIT001, TWAIT002) with very high empirical strength, and the number of time items (2) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.008). The change is consistent across both predecessor activities, pointing to a slower handoff or longer interval before A20. A limitation is that these are elapsed times between completion events, not pure waiting or service times, but the consistent doubling is unlikely to be a sampling artefact.

Other differences: Activity A32 occurred less often in the later window, both in mean occurrences per trace and in trace participation. This is a separate structural change, but it is based on a single control-flow item (expected by chance 0.25) and could reflect a shift in how often that activity is executed or repeated. The evidence cannot rule out that this occurrence change is unrelated to the timing change.
