### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process structure changed: activity A08, previously a common early step, became much less frequent and moved later, while a group of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) that used to occur late and lead to A30/A34 now occur earlier and lead to A28, and A47 no longer feeds A28 but instead feeds that group. This is supported by the large number of qualified items (74 vs 0.93 expected by chance) and by the consistency across occurrence, position, direction, relation and lag indicators. A competing explanation that the differences are due to random sampling is unlikely given the volume of evidence, but the exact nature of the new process (e.g., whether A08 is optional or replaced) cannot be determined from this evidence.

Other differences: A02, A04, A10 and A17 increased in occurrence, and A08's successor set became more diverse. These may be related to the same restructuring but are not part of the main hypothesis.
