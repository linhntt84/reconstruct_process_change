### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the relative order of two groups of activities reversed: activities A19, A21, and A24 moved from early to late, while A20, A22, A23, A25, and A29 moved from late to early. This is directly shown by positional shifts (POS), order reversals (REL), and distance changes (LAG). Additionally, A30 replaced A25 as the dominant successor for several activities (DIR), which may be part of the same reordering or a separate change. The large number of qualifying items (46 vs 0.69 expected) and their consistency across indicators make a chance explanation unlikely. A competing explanation is that the process was redesigned with a new activity A30 and a different sequence, but the evidence cannot separate that from a simple reordering of existing activities.

Other differences: A40's relations to A20, A22, A23, and A25 changed substantially (LAG006–LAG009), and A29's successors shifted from A20/A23/A22 to A24/A21/A19 (DIR007). These may be part of the same reordering but are not included in the main hypothesis.
