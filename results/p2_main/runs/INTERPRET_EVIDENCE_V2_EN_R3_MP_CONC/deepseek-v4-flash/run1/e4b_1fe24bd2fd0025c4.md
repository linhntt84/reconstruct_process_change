### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: activities A01, A03, A13, A14, A17 and A22 are now performed by a largely different set of resources than before. The same group of previously frequent resources (User_2, User_24, User_40, User_42, User_5, User_61, User_66) decreased across all six activities, while a new group (User_84, User_96, User_65, User_104, User_56, User_48) increased, with the pattern consistent across every affected activity. The resource family produced 6 evidence items against 0.005 expected by chance, far more than a stable process would typically produce. [ASSIGN001, ASSIGN002, ASSIGN003, ASSIGN004, ASSIGN005, ASSIGN006]

Other differences: The elapsed time between A05 and A09 increased by about a third in the later window, a separate timing change that is not part of the resource hypothesis. [TWAIT001]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the resource change reflects a routine rotation or reassignment of staff rather than a structural change in how work is allocated; the evidence shows that the set of resources changed, but not why.
