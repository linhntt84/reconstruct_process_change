### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that A01 moved from an early position to a late position in the process, now occurring after A02, A04, A05 and A07 and before A03. This is directly shown by the positional shift of A01 (mean position from 0.26 to 0.65) and by the reversal of order relations with A02, A04, A05 and A07 (all now predominantly before A01). The successor changes are consistent: A07 now leads to A01 instead of A03, and A01 now leads to A03 instead of A02/A04. The lag distributions confirm that A01–A03 become adjacent (lag 1 dominant) while A02–A01 become farther apart. The evidence is strong: 21 control-flow items were flagged against 0.18 expected by chance, and all point to the same reordering.

Other differences: A02's successors shift from A01 to A05 (A05 share rises from 27% to 40%, A01 falls from 26% to 11%). A04's successors shift further toward A07 (54% to 70%, A01 disappears). These are consistent with the main reordering but not part of the core hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a change in the frequency of alternative paths rather than a strict reordering, but the relational and positional evidence directly shows a change in order, so this is unlikely.
