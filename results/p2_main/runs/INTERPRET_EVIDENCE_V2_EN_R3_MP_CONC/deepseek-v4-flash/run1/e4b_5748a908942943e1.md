### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation, most clearly for activity A16, which moved from being handled by many different resources to being handled exclusively by one resource in the later window. This is directly shown by the assignment distribution and the concentration index. The same pattern of redistribution, though less extreme, appears for several other activities (A01, A03, A13, A14, A17), where the set of resources changed substantially. A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the observed resource changes reflect a change in case mix or staffing rather than a deliberate process modification.

Other differences: The elapsed time between A04 and A01 decreased markedly in the later window, suggesting a change in timing. This timing change is not necessarily linked to the resource allocation changes.
