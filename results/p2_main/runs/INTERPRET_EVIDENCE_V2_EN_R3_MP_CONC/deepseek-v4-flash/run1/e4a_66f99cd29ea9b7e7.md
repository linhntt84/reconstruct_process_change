### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering of activities: A22 now occurs immediately after A20, and A25 now occurs later, after A30/A31/A36, while A21 now precedes A30/A31/A36 instead of following them. This is directly shown by the successor changes (A20→A22, A21→A30/A31/A36, A30/A31/A36→A25), the position shifts (A21 and A22 earlier, A30/A31/A36 later), and the order relations (A21 and A22 now before A30/A31/A36). The number of control-flow items (22) far exceeds the 0.42 expected by chance, and the items are consistent across indicators.

Other differences: The timing between A01 and A04 increased, and the timing between A24 and A28 decreased to near zero. These are separate from the structural reordering and are supported by two time items against an expected 0.009.

A limitation is that some lag items (A20–A31, A20–A36, A31–A22, A36–A22) have only 2 supporting traces in the later window, so those specific lags are weak; however, the main lag items (A20–A22, A21–A25) have full support. The timing changes could be independent of the structural change, but the evidence does not require them to be linked.
