### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that resource allocation changed: the resources assigned to several activities were replaced by a different set, and activity A11 became performed exclusively by one resource. The screening shows 8 resource items when about 0.005 were expected by chance, far more than a stable process would typically produce, and the items agree in direction across many activities. The concentration change for A11 is direct and large (18 resources to 1). A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that this reflects a routine staff reassignment rather than a change in how the process is executed; no control-flow or timing changes were detected.
