### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a group of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) that were executed between A43 and A28 are no longer executed, and A43 now leads directly to A28. This is shown by the near-disappearance of those activities, the change in A43's outgoing transitions from them to A28, the loss of the A43-before-them order, and the shortening of the A43–A28 lag from 2 to 1. The number of qualifying evidence items (57) far exceeds the 0.72 expected by chance, and the pattern is consistent across occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators.

Other differences: A02's behaviour also changed (reduced participation, different successors, later position), and A15/A16 positions shifted slightly later, but these are not part of the main hypothesis. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is a change in case mix (different types of cases entering the process), but the direct successor change of A43 to A28 and the lag change make a structural change more likely.
