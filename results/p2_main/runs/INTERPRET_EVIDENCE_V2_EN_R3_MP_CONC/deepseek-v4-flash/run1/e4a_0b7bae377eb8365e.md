### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the process inserted a new optional step: one of A14, A15 or A20 is executed between A22 and A23/A24, replacing the direct path A22→A23→A24. This is directly shown by the appearance of these activities, their routing from A22 and A23, their co-occurrence with the surrounding activities, and the increased step distances between A22, A23 and A24. The timing increase between A26–A32 and A33–A32 is a separate change, not part of this structural hypothesis.

Other differences: The elapsed times between A26–A32 and A33–A32 roughly doubled in the later window, indicating a timing change independent of the structural insertion. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities were always possible but simply not observed in the earlier window; however, the large number of consistent control-flow items (28 vs. 0.4 expected by chance) makes this unlikely.
