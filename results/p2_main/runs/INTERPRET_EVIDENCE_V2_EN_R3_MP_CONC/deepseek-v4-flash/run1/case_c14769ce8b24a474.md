### Observed differences

- In the later window, the outgoing transition of activities A19, A23, A26, A27, A28 changed from predominantly A29 to predominantly A34. For example, A19's successor went from A29 in about 95% of cases to A34 in about 91% [DIR001, DIR005, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010].
- The relative order of a group of activities reversed. In the earlier window, A29 preceded A20, A21, A22, A24, A25 in almost all cases; in the later window, those activities precede A29 (e.g., A20 before A29 went from 0% to 99%) [REL011, REL016, REL021, REL029, REL033].
- Similarly, A19 and A23 preceded A20, A21, A22, A24, A25 earlier, but now those activities precede A19 and A23 (e.g., A20 before A19 went from 0% to 16% while A19 before A20 dropped from 21.5% to 0.5%) [REL001, REL008, REL013, REL018].
- Positions confirm: A20, A21, A22, A24, A25 moved earlier (mean position from about 0.62 to about 0.39–0.40), while A19, A23, A26, A27, A28 moved later (mean from about 0.33 to about 0.67–0.71) [POS002–007, POS001, POS005, POS008–010].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process reordered a block of activities: activities A20, A21, A22, A24, A25 (and possibly A19, A23, A26, A27, A28) swapped their relative order with A29 and A34. In the earlier window, A29 occurred before these activities and A34 after them; in the later window, A34 occurs before them and A29 after them. Also A19 and A23 moved from before to after this group. This is directly shown by the order reversals in REL items and confirmed by position shifts. The number of qualifying items (73) far exceeds the 0.9 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artifact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a change in the set of traces (e.g., different case mix) rather than a change in the process itself, but the consistency across many activities and the direct order reversals make this less likely.

Other differences: The LAG items show changes in distances between activities, but many have very low support (e.g., only 1 trace in one window), so they are not reliable. Also, the DIR changes for A20 and A21 show redistribution among many successors, but these are consistent with the order change.
