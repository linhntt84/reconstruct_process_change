### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a loop that repeated a block of activities (A30, A31, A36, A37, A38, and related A14, A15, A19, A20, A41, A46, A47) to a single execution of that block in a fixed order, and that activity A10 became a common step that occurs before the block. This is supported by the drop in mean occurrences and recurrence of the block activities, the replacement of interleaving with fixed orders (e.g., A14 before A30, A30 before A38), and the increase in A10 participation and its placement before the block. The large number of qualifying items (116 vs 1.16 expected by chance) makes random fluctuation an unlikely explanation. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two windows sample different process variants, but the consistency across many indicators and activities makes this less plausible.

Other differences: Activities A02, A04, A06, A08 decreased in participation, and A38's successor changed from A30 to A25; these are not part of the main hypothesis but are consistent with a broader restructuring of the process.
