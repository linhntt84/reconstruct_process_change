### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activities A19, A20 and A25 were removed from the process, so that the flow now goes directly from A27 to A28 to A29, whereas in the earlier window one of A19, A20 or A25 was executed in between. This is supported by the near-total disappearance of A19, A20 and A25, the loss of their co-occurrence with A27/A28/A29, the shift of A27's and A28's successors to A28 and A29 respectively, and the shortening of the lags between A27, A28 and A29 to consecutive steps. The number of qualifying evidence items (27) far exceeds the 0.55 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact. A limitation is that the exact role of A19/A20/A25 (e.g., whether they were mandatory or optional branches) cannot be determined from this evidence, but their absence and the direct A27→A28→A29 chain are clearly indicated.

Other differences: none beyond the removal of A19, A20 and A25 and the resulting direct chain.
