Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from having two alternative branches (one containing A27, the other containing A36/A37/A42) to always executing both sets of activities, with A36/A37/A42 now occurring earlier in the trace (before A28, A26, and A27) and A25 now leading to them instead of A28.
