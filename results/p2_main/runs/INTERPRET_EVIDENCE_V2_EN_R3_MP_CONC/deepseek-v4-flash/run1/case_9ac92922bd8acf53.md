### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed from a single sequence in which A25, A26, A27, A28, A31, A36, A37, A42, A43, and A44 were always executed together, to a process in which A27, A36, A37, A42, A43, and A44 are now executed in only about half of the cases, and when they are executed, A26 now precedes them instead of following them. A25 now leads to A28 instead of to that group. This is supported by the drop in participation of those activities, the routing change from A25 to A28, the branching of A26, the reversal of order between A26 and the group, and the corresponding position shifts. The evidence cannot rule out that the change reflects a different mix of process variants, but the consistency across indicators makes a real change likely.
