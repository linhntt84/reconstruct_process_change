### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the routing between activities A36, A37, and A33: the sequence A36 → A37 → A33 became more frequent, while the direct paths A36 → A33 and A37 → A36 became less frequent. This is directly shown by the successor shifts in DIR001 and DIR002 and by the tighter adjacency of A36 and A37 in LAG001. The change is consistent across all three items, and the number of items (3) is well above the 0.48 expected by chance. The activities are all present in substantial shares in both windows, so this is a shift in routing shares among existing paths rather than a replacement. Minor new successors (e.g., A01, A27, A30 for A36; A10, A22, A32, A43 for A37) appear in small proportions and may reflect rare variants; they do not affect the main hypothesis. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed shift is due to sampling variation in a stable process, but the consistency and magnitude of the changes make this unlikely.
