### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that resource allocation changed: activities A01, A03, A11, A13, A14, A17, and A22 became more concentrated on a few resources (notably User_3, User_78, and User_61) instead of being spread across many resources, with A11 becoming exclusively assigned to a single resource. This is directly shown by eight resource-related items (ASSIGN and CONC), far exceeding the 0.006 items expected by chance for that family, and the pattern is consistent across all affected activities. The main limitation is that the control-flow item (DIR001) is a single item against an expected 0.2, so it could be a chance fluctuation and does not by itself support a structural change.

Other differences: The control-flow shift for A10 (fewer transitions to A16, more to A07) is observed but is not part of the resource hypothesis; it may be a separate, weaker signal that cannot be confirmed with the current evidence.
