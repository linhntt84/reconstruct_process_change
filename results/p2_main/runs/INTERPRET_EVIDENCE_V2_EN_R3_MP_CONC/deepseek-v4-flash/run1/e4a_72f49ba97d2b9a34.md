The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed the order of two groups of activities: a group consisting of A14, A18, A21, A22, A23 now occurs after a group consisting of A15, A16, A17, A19, A20, A24, A28, whereas before it occurred before them. A competing explanation that the two groups are alternative paths is ruled out because co-occurrence remains high (e.g., A15 and A24 co-occur in all traces in both windows).
