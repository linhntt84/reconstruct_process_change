- In the later window, A27 appears in about 99% of traces (up from about 50%), while A32, A33, A38, A39, and A40 each appear in about 1–2% of traces (down from about 51–52%). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

- A26 and A27 co‑occur in about 99% of traces (up from about 50%), and A26 precedes A27 in about 99% (up from about 48%). [REL001]

- The group A32, A33, A38, A39, and A40 used to co‑occur with each other in about 51% of traces with mixed order; in the later window they almost never co‑occur (about 1%) and are mostly absent (neither in about 97–98%). [REL018–REL027]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from having two alternative continuations after A26 to a single path. In the earlier window, about half the traces followed A26 with A27, and about half followed A26 with a group of activities (A32, A33, A38, A39, A40) that then led to A28. In the later window, A26 is almost always followed by A27, and A27 is almost always followed by A28; the group activities are nearly absent. This is supported by the large increase in A27 participation and A26→A27 transitions, the large decrease in the group activities’ participation and their co‑occurrence with A26 and A28, and the increase in A26–A27 and A27–A28 co‑occurrence and order. The evidence cannot rule out that the group activities were moved to a rare variant or that they still exist in a small share of cases, but the overall pattern is consistent with a structural simplification.

Other differences: A26’s position shifted slightly later (mean from 0.46 to 0.49) and A28’s slightly earlier (mean from 0.58 to 0.56), which are consistent with the changed path. Lag distributions for pairs involving the disappearing group changed, but support in the later window is very low (1–3 traces), so these are not reliable.
