### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed so that a block of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) now executes before A28 instead of after it, and A47 now routes to that block rather than directly to A28. This is directly shown by the successor changes, the position shifts, and the relational order reversals. The number of qualifying items (30) far exceeds the 0.61 expected by chance, and the items are consistent across indicators. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed traces reflect a new variant replacing an old one, but the consistency of the ordering evidence makes that less likely.
