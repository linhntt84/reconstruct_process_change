### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: the branch that previously executed A24, A25, A26 (and A06) has been replaced by a branch that executes A21, A22, A23. In the earlier window, A20 led to A24/A25/A26 in about 80% of traces, and A06 co-occurred with these activities; in the later window, A20 leads to A21/A22/A23 in about 79% of traces, and A06 is much less frequent and appears earlier. This is directly shown by the shifts in participation, outgoing transitions, and co-occurrence patterns. The number of qualifying evidence items (73) far exceeds the 0.9 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact. [OCC004–OCC009, DIR005, REL011–REL016, REL001, REL005–REL007]

Other differences: A06's outgoing transitions diversified (no longer predominantly to A35), and A34 now goes directly to A35 rather than via A06. The lag between A06 and A35 increased, while A34 to A35 became more immediate. [DIR001, DIR006, LAG001, LAG006]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a replacement of one set of activity labels by another (e.g., A24/25/26 renamed to A21/22/23) rather than a change in routing logic, but the consistent pattern across many indicators supports a structural change.
