### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering of activities: a group that used to occur late (A14, A16, A19) now occurs early, while a group that used to occur early (A15, A17, A18) now occurs late, with A20 and A24 acting as intermediate steps. The main limitation is that some of the moved activities (A14, A16, A19) are not present in all traces, so the change could partly reflect a shift in which variants are executed rather than a pure reordering, but the consistent reversal of order relations across many pairs makes a structural reordering the most plausible interpretation.

Other differences: A25 moved from an early to a late position, and A35's successors changed from the late group to the early group. These are consistent with the same reordering and do not require a separate hypothesis.
