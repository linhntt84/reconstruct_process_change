### Observed differences

- In the later window, activity A10 appears in about 70% of traces (up from about 19%), and its mean occurrences per trace rose from 0.21 to 0.78. [OCC003]
- Activities A04 and A08 became less frequent: A04 participation fell from about 21% to 10.5%, and A08 from about 19.5% to 4%. [OCC001, OCC002]
- The outgoing behaviour of A14 changed: before, about 86% of its transitions went to A15 and about 1% to A10; after, about 33% went to A15 and about 66% to A10. [DIR003]
- The outgoing behaviour of A10 changed: before, about 5% of its transitions went to A15; after, about 85% went to A15. [DIR002]
- A10 now co-occurs with A14, A15, A20, A22, A23, A24, A25, A26, A31, A32, A45, A46, A47 and A48 in about 70% of traces, whereas before it co-occurred with them in about 19% (or less for some). [REL001–REL014]
- The order between A10 and A15 reversed: before, A15 preceded A10 in about 12.5% of traces and A10 preceded A15 in 0.5%; after, A10 preceded A15 in about 37.5% and A15 preceded A10 in 2.5%. [REL002]
- Many lag distributions spread out, e.g., the lag between A20 and A23 was 1 step in about 99% of traces before but only about 67.5% after, with lags 2–4 appearing. [LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A10 became a regular step between A14 and A15. In the earlier window, A14 led directly to A15 in about 86% of its transitions, and A10 was rare (about 19% participation). In the later window, A14 leads to A10 in about 66% of transitions, A10 leads to A15 in about 85% of its transitions, and A10 is present in about 70% of traces. This is directly shown by the successor changes (DIR003, DIR002), the co-occurrence and order changes (REL001, REL002), the participation increase (OCC003), and the earlier position of A10 (POS001). The number of qualifying evidence items (77) far exceeds the 1 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A04 and A08 participation decreased, and several ordering constraints among later activities (e.g., A20–A24, A23–A24) reversed or became less fixed. These are not part of the main hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A10 was already present but rarely used, and the change reflects a shift in routing rather than the introduction of a new step; however, the consistent placement between A14 and A15 and the large participation increase make the insertion of A10 into the main flow the most direct interpretation.
