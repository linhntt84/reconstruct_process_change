### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A08 was largely bypassed: A49 now leads directly to A50 instead of routing through A08, and A08, when it still occurs, is earlier and leads to different successors. At the same time, a group of activities A33–A43 that previously appeared as alternatives (each in about half the traces) are now executed together in nearly every trace, with fixed ordering relative to A14, A15, A30 and A31. The number of qualifying items (219 vs. about 2 expected by chance) is far above what a stable process would produce, and the OCC, DIR, REL and POS indicators agree on the same routing and participation shift. The main limitation is that the LAG items with very small support (1–4 traces) are weak on their own, but they are not needed for the conclusion. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window reflects a different variant mix rather than a process modification, but the consistency across indicators makes a real change the more plausible reading.

Other differences: A04, A06 and A10 increased their participation, and A31 moved later in the trace; these are not part of the main hypothesis.
