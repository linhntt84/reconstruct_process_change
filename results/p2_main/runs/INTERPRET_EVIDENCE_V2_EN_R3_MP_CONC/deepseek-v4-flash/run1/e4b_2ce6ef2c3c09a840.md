### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that resource allocation changed: activity A11 became exclusively performed by a single resource (User_115) in the later window, and several other activities (A01, A03, A13, A14, A17, A22) shifted from a set of previously common resources to a new set of resources, with a larger share of executions attributed to "OTHER".
