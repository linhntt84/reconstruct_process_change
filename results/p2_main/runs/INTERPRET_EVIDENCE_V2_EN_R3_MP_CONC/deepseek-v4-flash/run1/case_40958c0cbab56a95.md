### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the core path of the process: activities A26, A27 and A28, which were executed in almost every trace in the earlier window, are almost never executed in the later window, while activities A19, A20 and A21, which were nearly absent before, are now executed in a substantial share of traces and lead to A29. This is supported by the consistent drop in participation of A26/A27/A28, the rise of A19/A20/A21, the change in their outgoing transitions to A29, and the relational co-occurrence patterns. The screening count (81 items vs 0.89 expected by chance) rules out a chance artifact. The evidence cannot rule out that the new activities are optional alternatives rather than a full replacement, since they appear in only about 30–37% of traces, while A29 remains in all traces.

Other differences: A10 participation increased from about 13.5% to 22.5%. [OCC001] The positions of A29 and A44 shifted slightly, but these are minor. [POS007, POS008]
