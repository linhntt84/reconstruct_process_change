### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A20 now leads directly to A23, bypassing the group of activities A31, A32, and A37. In the earlier window, A20 always led to one of A31/A32/A37, which then preceded A23, A22, and A26. In the later window, A20 almost always goes to A23, and A31/A32/A37 become optional, occurring in only about 47% of traces and later in the trace, after A23. A22 also becomes optional, occurring in about 54% of traces. This is directly shown by the change in A20's outgoing transitions, the drop in participation of A31/A32/A37 and A22, the reversal of order between A23 and those activities, and the shift of A20 and A23 to adjacent positions. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL014, REL015, REL016, POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG001]

Other differences: A21's outgoing transitions now split between A22 and the A31/A32/A37 group, and the order between A21 and that group reversed, with A21 now preceding them when they co-occur. [DIR002, REL006, REL007, REL008] A26 now sometimes occurs without A22, consistent with A22 becoming optional. [REL010]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a rare variant in the later window, but the large number of consistent indicators (47 items vs 0.51 expected by chance) makes this unlikely. Some LAG items are based on only one trace in the later window, but the main evidence from OCC, DIR, REL, and POS is based on substantial support.
