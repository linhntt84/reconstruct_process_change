### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which the block of activities A21, A22, A23, which were always present between A39 and A24 in the earlier window, is replaced in the later window by a set of alternative activities A14, A15, A16 that now appear between A39 and A24. The occurrence, direction, relation, and lag indicators all consistently show this replacement. The new activities are not in every trace (each appears in about a third of traces), so the replacement is not complete, but the old activities are almost entirely gone. A competing explanation that the new activities are a rare variant is ruled out by the large number of consistent evidence items (71 items vs 0.64 expected by chance). A limitation is that the new activities are alternatives rather than a single new activity, and the exact routing among them is not fully specified.

Other differences: A24 and A39 shift slightly in position (A24 earlier, A39 later), and A14-16 also relate to A40 and A41, but these are consistent with the main replacement. [POS007, POS008, REL008, REL016, REL023]
