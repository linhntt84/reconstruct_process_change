### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20, and A25 each had A26 as the dominant successor (about 92–99% of their outgoing transitions). In the later window, their successors are mostly A28 and A32 (e.g., A19: A28 48%, A32 45%; A20: A28 43%, A32 56%; A25: A32 56%, A28 39%). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- A27's successor changed from A28 (98%) to a mix: A28 54%, A20 15%, A19 16%, A25 11%. A28's successor changed from A32 (99%) to A32 45%, A20 21%, A19 16%, A25 17%. [DIR004, DIR005]
- A45's successor changed from A25/A19/A20 (35%, 35%, 24%) to A26 (94%). [DIR006]
- The relative order of A19, A20, and A25 with respect to A26 and A27 reversed: before, these activities mostly occurred before A26/A27 (e.g., A19 before A26 in 36% of traces, A20 before A26 24%, A25 before A26 38%); after, they mostly occur after A26/A27 (A26 before A19 31%, A26 before A20 37%, A26 before A25 29%). [REL001–REL008]
- The positions of A19, A20, and A25 shifted later in the trace: their mean relative position increased from about 0.33 to about 0.6. [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- Lag distances changed: e.g., A27→A28 changed from mostly lag 1 (98%) to lag 1 55% and lag 2 43%; A27→A32 changed from lag 2 (97%) to lag 3 (93%); A28→A32 changed from lag 1 (99%) to lag 1 45% and lag 2 54%. [LAG005, LAG006, LAG009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering and routing of the process: activities A19, A20, and A25, which previously occurred early and were followed by A26, now occur later and are followed by A28 and A32, while A26 and A27 now occur before them. This is directly shown by the reversal in relative order (REL), the later positions of A19/A20/A25 (POS), the shift in successors (DIR), and the changes in lag distances (LAG). The number of qualifying items (31) far exceeds the 0.62 expected by chance, and the items are consistent across indicators.

Other differences: A45's successor changed from A25/A19/A20 to A26, and A27 and A28 now lead to a mix of A20/A19/A25 instead of a single dominant successor. These are consistent with the same reordering but involve additional activities.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a change in the mix of process variants rather than a single uniform reordering, but the consistency of the directional changes across multiple indicators makes this less likely.
