### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process likely changed from a path that included A10 and then a set of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) to a path that includes A02, A04, and A06 and leads to A28, A47, or A16 instead. This is supported by the sharp drop in A10 and those downstream activities, the rise of A02/A04/A06, and the change in A43's successors from those activities to A28. The evidence is consistent across occurrence, routing, and co-occurrence indicators.

Other differences: A14 and A43 show small shifts in their average position within traces, and several lag distributions (e.g., A10–A49, A48–A49) changed, but these are minor and not part of the main hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two windows represent different case mixes rather than a change in the process itself, but the large number of consistent indicators makes this unlikely.
