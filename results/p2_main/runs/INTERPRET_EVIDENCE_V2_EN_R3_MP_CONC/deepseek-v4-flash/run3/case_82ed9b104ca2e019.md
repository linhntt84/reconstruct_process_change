### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a set of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) is inserted between A04 and A28, and A04 becomes a more frequent intermediate between A43 and A28. The typical path changes from A43 → A28 (with A04 occasionally before A28) to A43 → A04 → (one of A19/A20/A25/A26/A27) → A28. This is supported directly by the increase in A04 participation, the appearance of the new activities all leading to A28, the shift of A43's outgoing transitions to A04, the lag of A04–A28 becoming 2, the lag of A43–A28 becoming 2–3, and the lag of A43–A04 becoming 1. The number of qualifying items (62) far exceeds the 0.73 expected by chance, and the indicators are consistent across occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag. A competing explanation that the new activities form a separate branch rather than an insertion is largely ruled out by the ordering and lag evidence, though the exact order among the new activities is not fixed; they appear as alternatives (they rarely co-occur with each other).
