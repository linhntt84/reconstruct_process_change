- In the earlier window, activities A28, A30, and A31 each occurred in about 49% of traces (97 of 200), and A32, A33, and A34 each in about 52% (103 of 200). In the later window, each of these six activities occurred in about 98–99% of traces (197–199 of 200). [OCC001–OCC006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from executing one of two alternative sets of activities to executing both sets in every case. Before, each case contained either the group {A28, A30, A31} or the group {A32, A33, A34}, but not both; after, both groups are executed together in nearly all cases, with A25 before them and A26 after. This is directly shown by the co-occurrence and ordering relations, and by the shift in positions and lags. The timing evidence also shows a separate change: waiting times between A20–A27 and A29–A27 roughly doubled, indicating slower completion between those activities. A competing explanation that the two groups were always present but one was unobserved is unlikely because the before window shows them strictly mutually exclusive with no co-occurrence. The evidence cannot rule out that the timing change is unrelated to the structural change, but both are supported.
