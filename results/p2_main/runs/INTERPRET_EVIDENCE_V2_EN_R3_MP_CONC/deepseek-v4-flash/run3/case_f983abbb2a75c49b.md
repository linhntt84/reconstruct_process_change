### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a flexible, interleaved execution of activities A15–A25 to a strict sequential order with two distinct phases: an early phase consisting of A15, A16, A17, A19 and A20 (with A30 immediately before A19), and a later phase consisting of A21, A22, A23, A24, A25 and A31. In the later window, the early activities always precede the later ones, the early activities are now close together (lag 1–2), the later activities are also close together, and the gap between the phases is 4–5 steps. Fixed successor relations also emerged: A20 always leads to A21, A22 always leads to A31, and A30 always leads to A19. The 93 control-flow items far exceed the 0.72 expected by chance, and the DIR, POS, REL and LAG indicators all point to the same reordering. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that some of the specific successor changes (e.g., A20→A21) reflect a rare variant in the earlier window, but the overall pattern is consistent and strong.

Other differences: A30's successor changed from A21 to A19, and A22's successor changed to A31; these are part of the same phase separation.
