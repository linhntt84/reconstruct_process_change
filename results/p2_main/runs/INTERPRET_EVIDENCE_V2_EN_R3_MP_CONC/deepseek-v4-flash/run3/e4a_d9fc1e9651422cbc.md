- The elapsed time between A26 and A19 decreased from about 0.94 hours to 0.45 hours on average. [TWAIT001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: in the earlier window, A20 led to a set of activities (A31, A32, A37, A38, A39) which then led to A21, A22, A23, and A26; in the later window, A20 leads almost always to A23, then A21, and then a choice between A22 and that set of activities, with A26 after. This is directly shown by the changed outgoing transitions of A20 and A21, the reversal of order between A21/A23 and the set, and the near mutual exclusivity of A22 with the set. [DIR001, DIR002, REL008-012, REL015-019, REL020-024, POS002, POS004, POS006-010]

Other differences: The elapsed time between A26 and A19 decreased substantially, indicating a separate timing change. [TWAIT001]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a rare variant that became dominant, but the large number of control-flow items (72 vs. 0.72 expected by chance) makes this unlikely. [screening.by_family.control_flow]
