### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new set of activities (A32, A33, A38, A39, A40) was introduced as an alternative path between A26 and A28, replacing the direct A26→A27→A28 path in about 44% of cases. The direct path still exists in about 56% of cases, with A27 still leading to A28. The new activities are executed in a flexible order among themselves before leading to A28. This is supported by the consistent drop in A26–A27 and A27–A28 co-occurrence, the appearance of the new activities with A26 before them and A28 after them, and the mixed order among the new activities. The number of qualifying items (45) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.65), so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A26’s position shifted slightly earlier, which is minor and may be a consequence of the new branch. The evidence contains no time or resource items.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities are not strictly between A26 and A28 but occur in a different part of the trace; however, the directional and relational evidence strongly points to their placement between A26 and A28.
