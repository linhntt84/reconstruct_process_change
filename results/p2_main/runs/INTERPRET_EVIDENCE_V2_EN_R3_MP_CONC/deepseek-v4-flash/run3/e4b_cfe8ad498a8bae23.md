### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: activities A01, A03, A13, A14, A16, A17, and A22 are now performed by a different set of resources, with A16 becoming fully concentrated on a single resource. The number of resource-related evidence items (8) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.005), and all show consistent redistribution with high empirical strength. The main limitation is that resource identifiers are anonymous, so the nature of the change (e.g., a different team or a deliberate reassignment) cannot be determined.

Other differences: The time between A07 and A13 decreased substantially, but this is based on few observations (6 before, 17 after) and is a separate timing change.

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the resource change reflects a change in the available resource pool rather than a change in allocation rules, but the evidence clearly shows a shift in which resources perform these activities.
