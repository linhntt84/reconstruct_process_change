### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the relative order of two groups of activities has been reversed: a group consisting of A19, A23, A26, A27, and A28, which used to occur early in traces, now occurs later, while a group consisting of A20, A21, A22, A24, and A25, which used to occur late, now occurs earlier. This is directly shown by the position shifts, the reversal of order relations in REL items, and the lag changes (e.g., A19–A29 lag changed from 1 to 4 steps, A20–A29 from 5 to a spread of 1–5). The successor changes involving A29 and A34 are consistent with this reordering, as activities that moved later now lead to A34 instead of A29, and activities that moved earlier now lead to A29 instead of A34. The evidence is extensive (73 control-flow items vs. 0.9 expected by chance) and internally consistent.

Other differences: A29 and A33 also shifted to later positions, and A34 replaced A29 as the main successor for the first group while A29 appeared as a successor for the second group. These are part of the same reordering rather than independent changes. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that some activities were added or removed, but the consistent reversal of order across many pairs makes a simple reordering the most plausible interpretation.
