### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured: activities A01, A02, and A03 were removed, and a new activity A04 was added at the end of the process. The routing changed accordingly—A05 now leads to A04 instead of A02, and A08 now leads to A06/A07 instead of A03. The overall flow shifted from a path A08→A03→(A05→A02→A01) with A03 also branching to A06/A07, to a path A08→A06/A07→A04 and A08→A05→A04. This is directly supported by the complete disappearance of A01/A02/A03, the appearance of A04 in all traces, the successor changes for A05 and A08, and the new co-occurrence patterns of A04 with A05, A06, and A07. The screening expected 0.24 items by chance; 43 items were observed, far exceeding that, so the evidence is not a chance artifact. A competing explanation—that the process was replaced by a completely different one—is weakened by the persistence of A05, A06, A07, and A08 in both windows.

Other differences: The relative order among the remaining activities shifted slightly (e.g., A06–A07 and A08–A07 lags shortened), and A06 occurs in slightly fewer traces (56% to 50.5%). These are secondary to the main structural change.
