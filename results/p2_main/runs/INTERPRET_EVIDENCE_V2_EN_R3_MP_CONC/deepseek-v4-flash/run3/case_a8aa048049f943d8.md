### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from two alternative branches to a single sequential path. In the earlier window, traces contained either the group {A28,A29,A30,A32,A33} or the group {A34,A35,A36,A37,A38}, but never both. In the later window, both groups are executed in the same trace, with A25 first, then the first group, then the second group, and A26 last. This is directly shown by the co-occurrence increase from 0% to about 98% between the two groups, the change in A25's outgoing edges to only the first group, and the positional shifts of the activities. The number of evidence items (95) far exceeds the 1.01 expected by chance, so this is not a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A26's successor relationships changed (it is no longer a successor of A28 and A30), but this is consistent with the reordering.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two groups were always on a single path but the earlier sample never captured a trace with both groups; however, the consistency and magnitude of the changes make this unlikely.
