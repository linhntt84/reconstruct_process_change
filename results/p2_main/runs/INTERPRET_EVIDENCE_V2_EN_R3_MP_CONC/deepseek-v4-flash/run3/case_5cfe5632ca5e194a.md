### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed so that a set of intermediate activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) is no longer executed, and A38 now leads directly to A23. In the earlier window, A38 was followed by one of these five activities (which were mutually exclusive alternatives), and that activity then led to A23; in the later window, A38 is directly followed by A23. This is supported by the disappearance of the five activities (OCC), the change in A38's outgoing transitions (DIR006), the change in the A38–A23 lag from 2 to 1 (LAG001), and the loss of co-occurrence between A14/A21 and A23 (REL005, REL013). The number of qualifying items (32) far exceeds the 0.42 expected by chance, and all items point to the same structural change. A competing explanation that the five activities were simply not sampled in the later window is unlikely given the large number of items and the consistent relational evidence, but the sample is finite.

Other differences: A23's position shifted slightly earlier, which is a natural consequence of the removed intermediate steps. No time or resource evidence was present.
