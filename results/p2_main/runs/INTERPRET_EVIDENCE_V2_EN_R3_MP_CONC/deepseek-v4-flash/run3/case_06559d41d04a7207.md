### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20 and A21, which each appeared in about 30–35% of traces before, are almost absent (each in 0.5% of traces). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- In the later window, activities A26, A27 and A28 appear in essentially all traces (100%), whereas they were absent before (A28 in 0.5%). [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]
- A10 participation dropped from about 26% to 11% of traces. [OCC001]
- In the later window, A26, A27 and A28 co-occur with each other and with A29 in all traces, with no fixed order (A_before_B and B_before_A each about half). [REL031, REL032, REL037, REL033, REL038, REL042]
- In the later window, A19, A20 and A21 no longer co-occur with A29 (before they co-occurred in 35%, 30% and 35.5% of traces; now 0.5%). [REL012, REL020, REL027]
- A10's outgoing edges changed: before it led mainly to A21 (40%), A19 (26%) and A20 (19%); after it leads to A27 (17%), A15 (13%), A26, A48, A47 and A50. [DIR001]
- The lag between A44 and A29 increased from mostly 2–3 steps to 4–5 steps. [LAG005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the process: the earlier window used a set of activities A19, A20 and A21 as alternatives that led to A29, while the later window uses a new set A26, A27 and A28 that are executed together (in any order) and also lead to A29. This is directly shown by the near-disappearance of the former activities, the appearance of the latter in all traces, and the co-occurrence and ordering patterns among A26, A27, A28 and A29. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL031, REL032, REL037, REL033, REL038, REL042]

Other differences: A10 became less frequent and its successors changed from A19/A20/A21 to a different set including A27 and A15. [OCC001, DIR001] The lag between A44 and A29 increased. [LAG005] A46 also became less frequent. [REL015, REL023, REL030, REL036, REL041, REL045]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities are simply renamed versions of the old ones, but the change in co-occurrence and ordering (from alternatives to a jointly executed group) makes a genuine structural change more plausible.
