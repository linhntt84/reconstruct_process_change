### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A14, A15 and A16 each occurred in about 30–35% of traces; in the later window they never occur. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the earlier window, activities A21, A22 and A23 never occurred; in the later window they occur in every trace. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- A39’s outgoing transitions changed: before, it led to A14, A15 and A16 (about 35%, 30% and 35% of its transitions); after, it leads to A21, A22 and A23 (about 36%, 33% and 32%). [DIR007]
- A24 remains present in all traces, but its relative position shifts later (mean position from 0.394 to 0.437). [POS007]
- The lag between A39 and A24 changed from 2 steps to 4 steps. [LAG001]
- The elapsed time between A24 and A31 decreased from about 0.90 hours to 0.46 hours (median). [TWAIT001]
- The elapsed time between A32 and A31 decreased from about 0.15 hours to 0.08 hours (median). [TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process likely changed its control flow by replacing a set of activities (A14, A15, A16) with a new set (A21, A22, A23) in the middle of the process. In the earlier window, A39 led to A14/A15/A16, which then led to A24; in the later window, A39 leads to A21/A22/A23, which then lead to A24. This is supported by the disappearance of A14–A16, the appearance of A21–A23, the redistribution of A39’s outgoing edges, and the shift in A24’s position and lag from A39. The order among A21–A23 is not fixed: about half the traces have one order and half the other. [OCC001-006, DIR001-007, POS001-007, REL001-027, LAG001]

Other differences: The timing between consecutive activities decreased. The elapsed time between A24 and A31 dropped from about 0.90 to 0.46 hours (median), and between A32 and A31 from about 0.15 to 0.08 hours. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the change reflects a different mix of case types rather than a modification of the process itself, but the consistency across many indicators makes a real process change more likely.
