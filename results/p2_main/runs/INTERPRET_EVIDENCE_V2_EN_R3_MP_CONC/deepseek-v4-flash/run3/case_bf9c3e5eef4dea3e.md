### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the hypothesis that A08 was introduced as an early step in the process, and that a group of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) that previously occurred before A28 now occur after A28. This is directly shown by the large increase in A08 participation and its earlier position, the later positions of A19/A20/A25/A26/A27, and the reversal of their order relative to A28. [OCC003, POS001, POS002–POS006, REL016, REL018, REL020, REL021]
- The change is consistent across many indicators: A08's successor distribution becomes dominated by A16, A15's successor shifts from A16 to A08, and A08 now precedes A28 in most traces. [DIR004, DIR006, REL009]
- Other differences: A02, A06 and A10 become much less frequent, and A08's co-occurrence with many activities rises from about 21% to 71%. These are not part of the main hypothesis but accompany it. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, REL001–REL015]
- A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a re-labelling or re-sequencing of activities rather than a genuinely new activity, but the consistent positional and relational evidence makes this less likely.
