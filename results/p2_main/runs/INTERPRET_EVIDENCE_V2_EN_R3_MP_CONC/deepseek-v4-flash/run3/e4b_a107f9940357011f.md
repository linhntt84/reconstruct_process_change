### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: activities that were previously spread across many resources became concentrated on a smaller set, with A12 becoming exclusively handled by one resource (User_36) and User_71 taking a much larger share of A13, A14, and A17. This is supported by eight resource-related items, far exceeding the 0.005 expected by chance in that family. The timing difference (TWAIT) is an additional observed difference but rests on a single item and is not sufficient to infer a timing change on its own.

Other differences: The time between A03 and A13 completions decreased substantially, but this is a single item and cannot be distinguished from chance given the screening expectation.

Limitation: The resource changes could reflect a single underlying shift (e.g., a new resource taking over) rather than independent changes per activity.
