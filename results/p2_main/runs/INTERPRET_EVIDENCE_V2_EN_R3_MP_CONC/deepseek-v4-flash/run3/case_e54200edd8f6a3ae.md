### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window a new activity A03 was inserted between A07 and the group A02/A06/A10, changing the typical order from A07 → A02/A06/A10 to A07 → A03 → A02/A06/A10. This is directly shown by A03 appearing in all later traces and none earlier [OCC001], A07's outgoing transitions becoming exclusively A03 [DIR002], A03's outgoing transitions to A02/A06/A10 [DIR001], and the relational order A07 before A03 before A02/A06/A10 [REL001-REL004]. The increased lag between A07 and the later activities is consistent with an inserted step [LAG001-LAG003]. The position shifts of A02/A06/A10 later and A07 slightly earlier are also consistent [POS001, POS003, POS005, POS004]. The number of qualifying control-flow items (15) far exceeds the 0.12 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artifact. A competing explanation that A03 is an alternative path is ruled out because A03 occurs in every trace and A07 always leads to it. The main limitation is that the evidence comes from a single case sample, but the consistency across multiple indicators is strong.

Other differences: None beyond the insertion of A03 and the associated position shifts.
