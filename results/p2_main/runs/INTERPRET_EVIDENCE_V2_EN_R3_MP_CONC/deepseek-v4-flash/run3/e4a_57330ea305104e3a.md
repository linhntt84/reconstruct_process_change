### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the routing between activities A31 and A32: they swapped their dominant successors. In the earlier window, A31 usually led to A28 (about 64%) and A32 usually led to A31 (about 64%). In the later window, A31 usually leads to A32 (about 62%) and A32 usually leads to A28 (about 62%). Additionally, A27 now more often leads to A31 and less often to A32, consistent with a shift in flow towards A31 and away from A32. This is supported by three DIR items with high empirical strength and consistent direction, based on about 90 traces containing A31/A32 in each window.

Other differences: The time between A24 and A25 halved (from about 1 hour to 0.5 hours) with reduced variability, indicating a separate timing change. [TWAIT001]

Limitations: The three control-flow items all describe the same underlying swap between A31 and A32, so they are not independent confirmations. The timing change is independent but we cannot rule out that it is unrelated to the routing change. No causal interpretation is possible.
