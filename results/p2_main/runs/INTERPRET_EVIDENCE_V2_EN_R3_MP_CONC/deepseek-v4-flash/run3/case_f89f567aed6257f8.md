### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a structure that always included activity A14 (often in a cycle with A03) to a structure where A14 is no longer executed, and a new activity A01 is introduced in about half of the cases, while activity A05 is executed less often. The change is directly shown by the disappearance of A14 (from 100% to 0% participation) and the appearance of A01 (from 0% to 56% participation), and by the routing changes where many activities that previously led to A14 now lead to A01 or have no successor. The large number of qualified items (129 vs 0.98 expected) rules out chance as a whole.

Limitations: A01 is not a direct replacement for A14 because A14 was in all traces while A01 is in only about half; also A05's decrease may be a separate change. The evidence cannot rule out that A01 existed before but was not observed due to sampling, though its absence in 200 before traces makes that unlikely.

Other differences: A05 participation drops, and many LAG items show shifts in the distance between activities (e.g., A05 and A06, A11 and A03), but these are consistent with the structural change.
