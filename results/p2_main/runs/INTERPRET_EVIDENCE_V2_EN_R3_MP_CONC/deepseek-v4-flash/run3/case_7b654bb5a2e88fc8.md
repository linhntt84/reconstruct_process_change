### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the main sequence: A14, A15 and A20, which previously occurred before A21 (and before A22/A23), now occur after A21, and A40 now directly precedes A21 instead of preceding A14/A15/A20. This is shown directly by the reversal of the A14/A15/A20–A21 order in REL items, by the change in successors of A14/A15/A20 and A40 in DIR items, by the shift of A14/A15/A20 to later positions in POS items, and by the changed lags involving A22, A23, A27 and A40. The number of qualifying items (24) far exceeds the 0.4 expected by chance, and the items are consistent across indicators. A limitation is that A14/A15/A20 are present in only about 30–38% of traces, so the reversal applies to the subset of cases containing them; the evidence cannot rule out that the process now has a different set of variants rather than a single reordered sequence.

Other differences: A22 and A23, which previously had fixed successors, now split among several successors (A14, A15, A20, A23, A27), indicating a broadening of routing options after these activities. This is a separate change in control flow, not part of the order reversal itself.
