### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A15, A16, A17, and A20 occur earlier in traces (mean position decreased by about 0.06–0.10), while A21–A25 occur later (mean position increased by about 0.03–0.10). [POS001–POS010]
- The outgoing transitions of A15, A16, and A17 were reduced to only A16/A17/A20 (for A15), A15/A20/A17 (for A16), and A20/A15/A16 (for A17), whereas before they also went to A21–A25 and others. A20 now goes only to A21 (100%), and A22 now goes only to A31 (100%). [DIR001–DIR010]
- For pairs such as A15–A21, A16–A23, A17–A24, and A20–A25, the order became fixed: before, the second activity often preceded the first (e.g., A21 before A15 in 77% of cases); after, the first always precedes the second (100%). [REL001–REL022]
- The positional distance between A15/A16/A17 and A21–A25 increased (lags of 4–5 became common, e.g., A15–A23 lag 5 rose from 17% to 56%), while the distance among A15, A16, and A17 decreased (lags 1–2 dominate). [LAG001–LAG050]
- The wait between A31 and A38 increased from about 0.9 to 1.8 hours, and between A37 and A38 from about 0.19 to 0.36 hours. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process appears to have been restructured so that a group of activities (A15, A16, A17, A20) now occur early and in a fixed order, followed by a later group (A21–A25), with A20 always leading to A21. This is supported by the consistent changes in outgoing transitions, positions, ordering, and lags. The evidence cannot rule out that some activities are now skipped or that the case mix changed, but the consistency across many indicators makes a structural change likely.

Other differences: The timing between A31–A38 and A37–A38 increased (about doubled for A31–A38). This is a separate timing change.
