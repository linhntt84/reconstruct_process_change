### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reversal in the relative order of two groups of activities: the group {A14, A16, A19} moved from early to late in traces, while {A15, A17, A18} moved from late to early, with A20 and A25 swapping their roles as successors. This is directly shown by consistent position shifts, successor changes, and order reversals across many activity pairs. The number of control-flow items (39) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.46), and the time items (2) also exceed their expectation (0.007), so the overall pattern is unlikely to be random. A competing explanation that the observed differences reflect only a change in variant frequencies rather than a structural reordering cannot be fully excluded, but the direct relational and positional evidence makes that less plausible.

Other differences: The waiting times between A02–A08 and A06–A08 increased, which is a separate timing change not part of the ordering hypothesis.
