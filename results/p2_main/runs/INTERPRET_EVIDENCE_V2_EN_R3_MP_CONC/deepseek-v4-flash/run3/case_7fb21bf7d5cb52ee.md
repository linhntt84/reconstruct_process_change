### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed so that activity A10 is now executed in most cases and occurs early in the trace, before many activities that previously preceded it. This is directly shown by the increase in A10's participation and its earlier position, and by the relational and lag evidence showing A10 now precedes A20, A22, A23, A24, A25, A26, A31, A32, A47, A48 in the majority of cases. The large number of qualifying items (77 vs 1 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely. A competing explanation that the observed differences stem from a different variant being used cannot be fully ruled out, but the consistency across indicators supports a genuine change.

Other differences: The ordering among later activities (A20, A22, A23, A24, A25, A26, A31, A32) also changed, with some pairs reversing order (e.g., A20-A24, A23-A24, A23-A26) and many lags spreading out. Additionally, A08 and A04 decreased in frequency. These may be part of the same overall change or separate modifications.
