### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the ordering between two groups of activities reversed: activities A20, A21, A22, A24, and A25, which previously occurred before A26, A27, A28, A29, and A30, now occur after them in most traces. This is directly shown by the reversal in the relative order of these pairs (REL items) and by the opposite shifts in their mean positions (POS items). The large number of qualifying items (98 vs 0.94 expected by chance) rules out chance as a whole. The reversal is not absolute – some pairs became mixed order (e.g., A20–A28) – but the dominant direction changed consistently. The DIR items are consistent with this reordering, showing that the earlier group now leads to the later group more often. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the process now has two alternative orderings rather than a single reversed one, since some pairs show near-equal proportions in the later window.

Other differences: The outgoing transition profiles of these activities also show a broader redistribution, with new successors appearing (e.g., A20 now leads to A26, A27, A28, A29, A30) and previous successors decreasing. This is consistent with the order reversal but also indicates a wider change in control flow. [DIR001–DIR010]
