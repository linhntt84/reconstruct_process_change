### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20, and A25 appear in about 30%, 41%, and 30% of traces respectively, while they were absent in the earlier window. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A10 also increased from about 5% to 13.5% of traces. [OCC001]
- The outgoing transitions of A27 changed: before, about 99% went to A28; after, about 44% to A28, 22% to A20, 17% to A25, and 15% to A19. [DIR004]
- The outgoing transitions of A28 changed: before, about 99% went to A29; after, about 55% to A29, 17% to A20, 13% to A19, and 12% to A25. [DIR005]
- The new activities A19, A20, and A25 are alternatives: they almost never co-occur with each other. [REL001, REL002, REL006]
- They appear after A27/A28 and before A29: e.g., A19 before A29 in about 30% of traces, A20 before A29 in about 39%, and A25 before A29 in about 30%. [REL005, REL009, REL012]
- The lag between A27 and A29 increased from 2 steps (about 99%) to 3 steps (about 97%). [LAG002]
- The lag between A28 and A29 increased from 1 step (about 99%) to 1 step (56%) and 2 steps (about 44%). [LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a new optional step is introduced between A27/A28 and A29, consisting of one of three alternative activities A19, A20, or A25. The process now sometimes routes from A27 or A28 through one of these activities before reaching A29, while still sometimes going directly from A28 to A29. This is supported by the appearance of these activities, the changed outgoing transitions of A27 and A28, the relational order (they appear after A27/A28 and before A29), and the increased lags between A27/A28 and A29. The three new activities are mutually exclusive alternatives, as they never co-occur. A limitation is that the new activities were not observed before, so we cannot rule out that they existed but were too rare to sample; however, the screening comparison (29 items vs. 0.54 expected by chance) indicates the overall change is unlikely to be due to chance.

Other differences: A10 also increased in participation, but it is not part of the main hypothesis. Slight shifts in the positions of A27 and A29 are likely a consequence of the inserted step.
