### Observed differences

- Activity A02 appeared in about 8 in 100 traces before and in about 34 in 100 traces after; it now occurs early and its outgoing transitions are mainly to A19, A21 and A24 (together about 88% of its successors), whereas before it was rare and its successors were spread across many activities. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001–REL009]
- A19, A21 and A24 changed their main successor from A30 to A25: before, each went to A30 in about 97–99% of its occurrences; after, each goes to A25 in about 95–100% of its occurrences. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR007]
- The order between the block A20/A22/A23 and the block A25/A29 reversed. Before, A20 preceded A25 in 98% of traces, A22 preceded A25 in 99%, and A23 preceded A25 in 99%; after, A25 precedes A20 in 100%, A25 precedes A22 in 99%, and A25 precedes A23 in 99.5%. - Positions shifted accordingly: A20, A22 and A23 moved later (mean position from about 0.39 to about 0.64), while A25 and A29 moved earlier (A25 from about 0.45 to about 0.39, A29 from about 0.65 to about 0.58). [POS003, POS005, POS006, POS008, POS009]
- A29’s successors changed from A19/A21/A24 to A20/A22/A23, and A20’s successors changed from A25/A22/A23 to A22/A30/A23, so A30 now follows the A20/A22/A23 block instead of the A19/A21/A24 block. [DIR008, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006]
- The distance between A29 and A20 shrank (lag 5 before, lag 1–3 after), while the distance between A29 and A30 grew (lag 2 before, lag 4 after). [LAG003, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering of the main activities: the block A20/A22/A23, which previously occurred before A25 and A29, now occurs after them, and the activities A19/A21/A24 (together with the newly appearing A02) now occur before A25 instead of after A29. This is directly shown by the reversal of order between the two blocks in nearly all traces, by the shift in successors of A19/A21/A24 from A30 to A25, and by the corresponding shift in successors of A20/A22/A23 from A25 to A30. The large number of qualifying items (58 vs. 0.75 expected by chance) rules out chance as an overall explanation.

Other differences: A02 is a new early activity in about a third of traces, and A19’s participation increased from about 31% to 44% while A21’s decreased from about 38% to 29%. These are consistent with the reordering but are not part of the main hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed windows come from two distinct process variants rather than a single process that changed over time; however, the consistency of the order reversal across many activity pairs makes this less likely.
