### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: activity A14 and its loop are removed, and a new activity A02 is introduced between A03 and A13. Before, A03 could lead to A12, which led to A14, which led back to A03; after, A12 leads directly to A03, and A03 can lead to A02, which then leads to A13. This is directly shown by the disappearance of A14, the appearance of A02, the changed successors of A03 and A12, and the co-occurrence and ordering relations between A02, A03, and A13. The number of evidence items (40) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.32), so the overall pattern is unlikely to be random. A competing explanation of sampling variation is not credible given the consistency of the relational and positional evidence.

Other differences: A01 appears, and A05, A07, A10, and A11 decrease in participation; these are not part of the main hypothesis. The evidence cannot rule out that these changes are related to the same underlying modification, but they are not directly connected to the A14/A02 change.
