- Activities A16–A20 appeared in about 84.5% of traces before, but only about 18% after; activities A21–A25 appeared in about 15.5% before and about 82% after. [OCC001–OCC010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed which set of activities is executed after A15: in the earlier window, A15 was followed by activities A16–A20; in the later window, it is followed by A21–A25. This is directly shown by the successor change of A15, the matching swap in participation of the two activity groups, and the co-occurrence patterns with A15. The timing between A11–A13 and A12–A13 also increased, indicating a separate change in timing.

Other differences: The routing from A22 within the new group shifted among A21, A23, and A25. The lag between A18 and A16/A17 also changed, but the after-window support is small (17 traces), so this is weak.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two activity groups represent different case types whose mix changed, rather than a modification of the process itself. However, the direct successor change and the consistent co-occurrence shift make a process change the more plausible interpretation.
