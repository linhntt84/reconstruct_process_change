### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A14, A15 and A20 were absent from all 200 traces; in the later window they appear in about 37%, 29% and 34% of traces respectively, each exactly once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the earlier window, A36 always led directly to A21 (100% of outgoing transitions); in the later window, A36 leads to A14 (37%), A15 (29%) or A20 (34%) in about 99% of traces, and directly to A21 in only about 1%. [DIR004, LAG001]
- In the later window, A14, A15 and A20 are mutually exclusive: no trace contains more than one of them, and together they appear in about 99% of traces. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- In the later window, each of A14, A15 and A20 occurs after A36 and before A21: A36 precedes A14/A15/A20, and each of those precedes A21. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- The gap between A36 and A21 changed from adjacent (lag 1) in all traces to lag 2 in about 99% of traces, consistent with an intervening activity. [LAG001]
- A21's position shifted slightly later in the trace (mean from about 0.37 to 0.39), consistent with an extra step before it. [POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new step was inserted between A36 and A21: one of three mutually exclusive activities (A14, A15 or A20) now occurs after A36 and before A21, replacing the direct A36→A21 link that existed in the earlier window. This is directly shown by the appearance of these activities, the redistribution of A36's outgoing transitions, the ordering relations, and the lag change. The three alternatives together cover about 99% of traces, so this is a systematic change rather than a rare variant. The screening comparison (21 items vs 0.37 expected by chance) rules out chance as an overall explanation. A competing explanation that cannot be fully excluded is that the process now has a mandatory choice among three new activities, but the evidence does not reveal why or how that choice is made.

Other differences: A21's position shifted slightly later, which is consistent with the inserted step and not a separate change.
