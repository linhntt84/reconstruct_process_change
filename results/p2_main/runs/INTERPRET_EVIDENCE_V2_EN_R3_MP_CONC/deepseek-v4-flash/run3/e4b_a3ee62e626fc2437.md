### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation for activities A13, A14, A17, and A22, with work being redistributed from a set of previously dominant resources to a different set and to a broader pool of resources. The supporting signs are four assignment indicators, all showing the same pattern of redistribution, which is far more than the 0.004 items expected by chance for the resource family. The change is consistent across all four activities and is based on substantial numbers of executions. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that this reflects a temporary fluctuation, but the strength and consistency of the evidence make that unlikely. The evidence does not indicate why the allocation changed, and it does not cover other activities.
