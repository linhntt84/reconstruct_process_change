### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reversal in the relative order of two groups of activities: A19, A21 and A24 moved from before A20, A22, A23, A25 and A29 to after them, while A30 replaced A25 as the main successor of A19, A21 and A24. This is directly shown by the relational items (nearly all pairs between the two groups flipped their order) and by the positional items (the early group became late and the late group became early). The support is strong: most relational and positional items are based on all 200 traces in each window. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A30 is a newly introduced activity that takes over the role of A25 for the early group, but the order reversal between the two existing groups is independent of that.

Other differences: A29's successor set changed from A20/A23/A22 to A24/A21/A19, and A40's successors changed from A21/A24/A19 to A22/A20/A23; these are consistent with the same reordering. Some lag items have very low support in one window (e.g., LAG001 after support 1, LAG003 after support 2), so they are not used as primary evidence.
