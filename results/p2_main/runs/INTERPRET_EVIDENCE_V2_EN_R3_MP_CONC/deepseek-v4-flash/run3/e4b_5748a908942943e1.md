### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that resource allocation changed for activities A01, A03, A13, A14, A16, and A17. In the later window, these activities are performed by a different set of resources, with a clear shift from previously common resources to new ones, and for A16 the work became fully concentrated on a single resource. The consistency of this pattern across six activities and the extreme concentration change for A16 make a chance explanation very unlikely (7 resource items vs. 0.006 expected by chance). A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the pool of available resources changed (e.g., staff turnover), but the evidence does not indicate any structural change in the process itself (no control-flow items were flagged).

Other differences: The elapsed time between A04 and A01 decreased in the later window. This is based on a single time item and is not sufficient on its own to infer a timing change, but it is reported as an observed difference.
