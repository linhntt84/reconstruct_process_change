### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20, and A25 appear in about 37%, 29%, and 34% of traces respectively; in the earlier window they were not observed at all. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the later window, these three activities have outgoing transitions almost exclusively to A26 (about 96%, 98%, and 97% of their outgoing transitions); in the earlier window they had no observed outgoing transitions. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The outgoing transitions of A41 changed: in the earlier window about 93% went to A26; in the later window about 34% go to A19, 31% to A25, 27% to A20, and only about 1% to A26. [DIR004]
- In the later window, A19, A20, and A25 occur before A26 and A31 in a substantial share of traces, and A41 occurs before them. For example, A19 occurs before A26 in 37% of traces, and A41 occurs before A19 in 37% of traces. In the earlier window these activities did not co-occur with A26, A31, or A41. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL014]
- The distance between A41 and A26 increased: in the earlier window A26 followed A41 within one step in about 94% of traces; in the later window it follows within two steps in about 90% of traces. [LAG002]
- The distance between A31 and A35 shifted: A35 followed A31 within one step in about 55% of traces earlier, but within two steps in about 62% later. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a set of activities (A19, A20, A25) are newly executed and are inserted between A41 and A26. Specifically, A41 previously led directly to A26; now it leads to A19, A20, or A25, which then lead to A26. This is supported by the appearance of these activities, their outgoing transitions to A26, the shift in A41's outgoing transitions, and the increased lag between A41 and A26. The large number of qualifying items (28 vs. 0.57 expected by chance) makes it unlikely that this is a sampling artefact.

Other differences: The relative order of A31 and A35 shifted, with A35 occurring later relative to A31. This may be a separate change, but it could also be a consequence of the new activities being placed before A35.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A19, A20, and A25 existed in the earlier window but were not observed in the 200-trace sample; however, their complete absence and substantial later presence make this unlikely.
