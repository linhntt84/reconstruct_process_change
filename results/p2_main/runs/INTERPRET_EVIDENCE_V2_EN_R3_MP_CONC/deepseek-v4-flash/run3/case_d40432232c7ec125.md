### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process control flow changed: activity A08 became much less frequent, and the ordering between two groups of activities reversed, with A19, A21, A24, A25 and A29 moving later relative to A20, A22 and A23. Additionally, A19, A21 and A24 now lead to A30 instead of A25. The large number of consistent relational and positional items (94 items vs about 1 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a different variant mix rather than a single process modification, but the consistency of the order reversal across many pairs supports a genuine change.

Other differences: A04, A06 and A10 increased in participation, and A08's successor distribution broadened; these may be part of the same change but are not central to the main hypothesis.
