### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed its resource allocation for activities A01, A03, A13, A14, and A22, shifting work from a set of previously used resources to a different set (e.g., User_56, User_66, User_77, User_58, User_51, User_48, User_49) while reducing the shares of others (e.g., User_3, User_18, User_59, User_60, User_61, User_132, User_28, User_7, User_52). This is supported by five assignment indicators, all showing the same direction, and the number of items (5) far exceeds the expected by chance (0.005). The change is consistent across activities. A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the pool of available resources changed (e.g., staff turnover) rather than a deliberate reallocation.

Other differences: The timing between activities A09 and A23 increased substantially (mean from ~3.9h to ~15.3h, median from ~1.0h to ~14.3h). This is a separate timing change, supported by one item with high empirical strength, but with moderate support (29 and 25 traces). It could be a change in waiting or processing time between those activities, but the small sample size limits certainty.
