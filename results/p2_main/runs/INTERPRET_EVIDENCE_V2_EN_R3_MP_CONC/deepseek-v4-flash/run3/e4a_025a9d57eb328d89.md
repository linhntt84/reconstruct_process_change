### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process changed in timing: the time between the completion of activities A16, A17, A18, A19 and the subsequent completion of A23 roughly doubled, from about 1 hour to about 2 hours. This is supported by four consistent time items, far exceeding the chance expectation for the time family (4 items vs 0.012 expected). The change is consistent across all four predecessor activities and also shows increased variability. A competing explanation that the number of observations changed is not supported because counts are similar (e.g., 58 vs 51, 52 vs 59). The evidence cannot rule out that the activities themselves changed in duration, but the indicator measures elapsed time between completions, so it is a timing change.

Other differences: Activities A39 and A40 increased in occurrence and repetition, and the routing from A05 shifted towards A03 and away from A04 and A09. These are separate control-flow changes, supported by three items vs 0.28 expected by chance. They are not part of the timing hypothesis.
