### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process changed by removing the intermediate activities A14, A15, and A20 from the path between A36 and A21. In the later window, A36 leads directly to A21, whereas earlier A36 went to one of A14/A15/A20 and then to A21. This is supported by the complete disappearance of these activities, the change in A36's successor from A14/A15/A20 to A21, the reduction in lag between A36 and A21 from 2 to 1, and the corresponding positional shifts. The evidence cannot rule out that A14/A15/A20 were moved to a part of the process not captured, but no trace in the later window contains them.

Other differences: A19's outgoing routing shifted toward A26 and away from A16/A17; this is a separate change in path frequencies among existing alternatives.
