### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A27, A36, A37, A42, A43, and A44 each occurred in about half of the traces (49–53%); in the later window each occurs in every trace (100%) and on average once per trace. [OCC001–OCC006]
- A25's outgoing transitions were almost always to A28 (98%) earlier; later they are spread among A36, A37, A42, A43, and A44 (each 14–24%), and A28 is no longer a direct successor. [DIR001]
- A26's outgoing transitions earlier went to A27 (48%) and to A36, A37, A42, A43, and A44 (9–13% each); later they go almost exclusively to A27 (98%). [DIR002]
- The activities A36, A37, A42, A43, and A44 earlier had A31 as a successor (14–27%) and A28 almost never; later they have A28 as a successor (14–24%) and A31 disappears. [DIR004–DIR008]
- Positions: A36, A37, A42, A43, and A44 moved earlier (mean position from about 0.58 to 0.40), while A26, A27, and A28 moved later (A26 from 0.53 to 0.61, A27 from 0.59 to 0.63, A28 from 0.39 to 0.48). [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS006–POS010]
- Relational evidence: A25 was before A27/A36/A37/A42/A43/A44 in about half the traces earlier and in all traces later. [REL001–REL006] A26 was before A27 in about half the traces earlier and in all traces later, but A26 was before A36–A44 earlier and after them later. [REL007–REL012] A27 and A36–A44 were almost never in the same trace earlier (cooccurrence ~1%) but are always together later, with A36–A44 before A27. [REL015–REL019] A28 was before A36–A44 earlier and after them later. [REL020–REL024]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a process with two alternative branches after A26—one containing A27 and one containing the group A36, A37, A42, A43, A44—to a single sequential path in which A36, A37, A42, A43, and A44 are always executed, and they occur before A28, A26, A27, and A31. In the earlier window, A25 led to A28, then A26, and then either A27 or the A36–A44 group; in the later window, A25 leads to the A36–A44 group, which is followed by A28, then A26, then A27, then A31. The change is supported by the near-universal participation of the A36–A44 group, the reversal of order between A28 and that group, and the shift of A27 from an alternative to a successor of the group. A competing explanation that the data cannot fully rule out is that the observed windows come from different case populations, but the consistency across occurrence, direction, position, and relation indicators makes this unlikely.

Other differences: A25's direct successor changed from A28 to the A36–A44 group, and A26's direct successor became almost exclusively A27; these are consistent with the reordering described above. [DIR001, DIR002]
