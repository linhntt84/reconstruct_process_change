### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that a new activity A01 was added to the process, appearing in every trace near the end, and that previously optional activities A02, A06, and A12 became mandatory (present in every trace). The routing from A02 also shifted from leading to A03/A07 to leading to A06. This is directly shown by the appearance of A01, the participation increases, and the successor changes. The number of qualifying evidence items (58) far exceeds the 0.53 expected by chance, and the changes are consistent across occurrence, position, relation, and lag indicators. A competing explanation that the two windows sample different process variants cannot be fully excluded, but the magnitude and coherence of the changes make a real behavioural change likely.

Other differences: A11 participation increased but not to 100%; the lag between A02 and A03/A07 increased; A15 and A16 now also lead to A01; A05 now also leads to A01. These are consistent with the main change.
