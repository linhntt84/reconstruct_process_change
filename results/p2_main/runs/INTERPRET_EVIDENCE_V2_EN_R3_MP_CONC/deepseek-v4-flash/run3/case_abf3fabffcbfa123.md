### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A32, A33, and A38 almost disappear: their trace participation drops from about 49% each to about 0.5% (1 trace out of 200). [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]
- Activity A04 drops from about 68% of traces to about 22%, and its mean occurrences per trace fall from 0.71 to 0.24. [OCC002]
- Activity A27 becomes nearly universal: participation rises from about 52% to about 99.5%, and its mean occurrences per trace rise from 0.52 to 1.01 (it is now often repeated). [OCC004]
- Activities A08 and A02 increase: A08 from 5% to 20.5% participation, A02 from 5% to 15%. [OCC003, OCC001]
- A27 now almost always co-occurs with A14, A15, A26, and A28 (cooccurrence about 99.5% vs about 51.5% before), and in the later window A27 almost always follows those activities (A_before_B about 96–98% for A14, A26, A28; about 63% for A15 with more interleaving). [REL009, REL013, REL017, REL021]
- The old activities A32, A33, A38 no longer co-occur with A04, A14, A15, A26, A28, or A27; instead, in the later window they are absent in about 78% of traces (neither present). [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL028, REL029, REL030]
- The lag between A14 and A15 shifts: in the later window they are more often adjacent (lag 1) and less often two steps apart. [LAG002]
- The lag between A26 and A28 shifts from mostly lag 2 or 4 to almost always lag 2. [LAG003]
- A04's position in the trace shifts later (mean relative position from 0.21 to 0.40), but this is based on fewer traces in the later window. [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a specific structural change: the process moved from a variant that included a set of activities (A32, A33, A38) and a frequent A04, to a variant that almost always includes A27 after the always-present activities A14, A15, A26, and A28, with the old activities almost never executed. The disappearance of A32/A33/A38 (from about 49% to 0.5% participation) and the near-universal presence of A27 (from about 52% to 99.5%) are directly shown by occurrence and relational evidence, and the ordering (A27 after A14/A15/A26/A28) is confirmed by the high A_before_B shares and the lag shifts. The number of qualifying items (62 vs 0.79 expected by chance) rules out chance as a whole.

Limitations: The later window contains only 1 trace with A32/A33/A38, so their absence is based on very few observations, though the drop from about half the traces is large. The evidence cannot distinguish whether A27 was added, made mandatory, or whether the old activities were removed; it only shows the observed change in behaviour.

Other differences: A08 and A02 increase in participation, and A04 decreases but remains present in about 22% of traces. These are not part of the main hypothesis.
