### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in control flow: A06 became a frequent early step, executed after A14 and before A15, and the relative order of two groups of activities reversed—A20–A25 now occur later and are followed by A34, while A19, A23, A26–A28 now occur earlier and are followed by A29. This is supported by the large number of control-flow items (105 items vs 1.15 expected by chance) and by the consistent directional changes across occurrence, routing, position, and ordering indicators. The main limitation is that all evidence is control-flow; no timing or resource changes are assessed, and some individual items rest on few observations, but the overall pattern is strong.

Other differences: A04 participation dropped substantially, and A06 now co-occurs with many activities it previously rarely accompanied. These are consistent with the main reordering but are not part of the core hypothesis.
