### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed from having two alternative branches after A26 — one via A27 and one via A32/A33/A38 — to a single branch A26 → A27 → A28, with the alternative branch removed. This is supported by the consistent increase in A27 participation and its co-occurrence with A26 and A28, the near-disappearance of A32, A33, and A38, the change in A26's successor distribution from multiple targets to almost exclusively A27, and the lag between A26 and A28 becoming fixed at 2 steps. The evidence is direct and consistent across multiple indicators. A limitation is that the after window contains only one trace with A32/A33/A38, so their near-absence is based on very few observations, but the before window had substantial support. Other differences: A28's position shifted slightly earlier, which is minor and not part of the main hypothesis.
