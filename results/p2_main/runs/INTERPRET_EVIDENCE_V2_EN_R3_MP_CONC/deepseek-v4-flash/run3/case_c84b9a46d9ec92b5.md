### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports that a middle segment of the process, consisting of activities A19, A20 and A25, is no longer executed between A41 and A26. In the earlier window these activities appeared in about a third of traces, always leading to A26, and A41 was typically two steps before A26. In the later window these activities are essentially absent, A41 leads directly to A26 (lag 1 in about 86% of traces), and the co-occurrence of A19/A20/A25 with both A41 and A26 has disappeared. The number of qualifying items (29) far exceeds the 0.57 expected by chance, and the relational, positional and lag evidence all point to the same structural change. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that these activities still exist but became extremely rare in the sampled window; however, the direct lag change and the complete absence of their outgoing transitions make a bypass or removal the most consistent interpretation.

Other differences: A21's routing changed, with a shift from A31 toward A24 as the most common successor. This is not part of the main hypothesis and may reflect a separate adjustment in the process.
