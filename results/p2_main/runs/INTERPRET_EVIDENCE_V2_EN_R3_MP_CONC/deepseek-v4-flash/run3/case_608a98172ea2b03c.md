### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a flexible ordering with multiple alternative successors to a more fixed sequence: A01 is now always followed by A04, A04 is no longer followed by A01, and A06 always leads to A01. This is directly shown by the successor distributions (DIR001-003) and the ordering relation between A01 and A04 (REL001). The position shifts (A01 earlier, A04 later) and the lag changes (A01 to A04 immediate, A04 to A01 delayed) are consistent with this reordering. The screening shows 20 control-flow items against 0.13 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be random. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed windows sample different variants of the process, but the consistency across many indicators makes this less likely.

Other differences: The lag changes for pairs involving A02, A03, A06 also indicate a reordering of the sequence, but they are part of the same structural shift.
