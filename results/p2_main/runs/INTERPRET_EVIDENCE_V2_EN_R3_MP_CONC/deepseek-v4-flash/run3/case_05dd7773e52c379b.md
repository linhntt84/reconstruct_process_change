### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a choice between two alternative sets of activities after A25 to a fixed sequence in which all six activities are executed: A25 → (A28/A30/A31) → (A32/A33/A34) → A26. The occurrence, routing, order, position, and lag indicators all point to the same structural change, and the number of qualifying items (51) far exceeds the 0.59 expected by chance for the control-flow family. The main limitation is that the internal order within each group (e.g., whether A28 precedes A30) is not established, but the overall sequence and the replacement of the alternative branches by a sequential execution are clearly supported.

Other differences: None beyond the described change.
