### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 each appear in about 16–22% of traces (33–45 of 200 traces), always once per trace; in the earlier window none of them appeared at all. [OCC001–OCC005]
- These five activities are alternatives to each other: no trace contains more than one of them, and together they cover essentially all traces in the later window. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL007, REL008, REL012]
- In the earlier window A24 always led to A25 and A25 always led to A26. In the later window A24 leads to A25 in about half of traces and to the new activities in the other half; A25 leads to A26 in about half and to the new activities in the other half; each new activity leads to A25 and A26 in roughly equal shares. [DIR001–DIR007]
- The new activities sit in the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.5). [POS001–POS005]
- A24 always precedes each new activity, and each new activity always precedes A26; A25 occurs both before and after the new activity in similar proportions. [REL004–REL006, REL009–REL011, REL013–REL015]
- The distance between A24 and A26 grew from 2 steps to 3 steps, and the distances A24→A25 and A25→A26 grew from 1 step to sometimes 2 steps. [LAG001–LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, chosen from the alternatives A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22, was inserted into the process between A24 and A26. In the earlier window the process ran A24 → A25 → A26 directly. In the later window one of these new activities appears in essentially every trace between A24 and A26, and A25 can occur either before or after it. This is supported directly by the appearance of the new activities from zero participation, by the new outgoing edges from A24 and A25 to them and from them to A25/A26, by the ordering relations (A24 before the new activity, new activity before A26, A25 on either side), and by the one-step increase in the distance between A24 and A26. The main limitation is that the exact placement of A25 relative to the new activity is not fixed: both orders occur in similar proportions, so the process does not impose a single position for A25.

Other differences: none beyond the insertion described. A competing explanation that the new activities existed before but were simply not observed is unlikely given their near-universal presence in the later window (about 99% of traces combined).
