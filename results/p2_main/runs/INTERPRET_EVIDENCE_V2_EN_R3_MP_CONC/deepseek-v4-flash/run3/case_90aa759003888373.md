### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, about half of the cases take a direct path from A36 to A21, skipping A14, A15 and A20, while the other half still execute the original full sequence. This is shown consistently by the drop in participation of A14, A15 and A20 from 100% to 53%, the appearance of A21 as a successor of A36 in 47% of cases, the co-occurrence reductions and the new one-step lag between A36 and A21. The number of qualifying items (15) far exceeds the 0.35 expected by chance, so the pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the shortcut existed before but was not observed in the 200-trace earlier sample; however, the earlier window showed no trace of it.

Other differences: none beyond the described shortcut and its positional effect on A21.
