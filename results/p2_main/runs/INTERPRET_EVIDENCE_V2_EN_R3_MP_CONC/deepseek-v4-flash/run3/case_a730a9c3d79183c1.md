### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A04 is now often skipped, while activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 are now almost always executed, whereas before they were executed in about half the cases. This is directly shown by the drop in A04 participation, the rise in A33–A39 participation, the change in A14's successor from A04 to A15, and the corresponding drop in co-occurrence of A04 with other activities. The change in A14's outgoing transitions indicates a structural bypass of A04 rather than a mere shift in case mix.

Other differences: A02, A08 and A10 increased in participation, and the lag between A30/A33/A35/A36 and A31 increased, suggesting a change in ordering. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed shifts reflect a change in the population of cases rather than a modification of the process itself, but the direct successor change of A14 makes a structural change more plausible.
