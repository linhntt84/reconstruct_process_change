### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A02 was removed from the process. In the earlier window A02 was executed in every trace and was directly connected to A01, A03, A04 and A05; in the later window it is absent from all traces, has no outgoing transitions, and no longer co-occurs with any of those activities. The number of qualifying evidence items (11) far exceeds the 0.15 expected by chance for the control-flow family, and all items point in the same direction. A competing explanation that the later window simply sampled a rare variant is implausible because A02 was present in 100% of the earlier traces and absent in 100% of the later ones.

Other differences: A03 and A04 occur earlier in traces and closer together in the later window, which is consistent with the removal of A02 but is reported separately because it involves positional and lag changes rather than direct participation. The evidence contains no timing or resource indicators, so no conclusions about those aspects are possible.
