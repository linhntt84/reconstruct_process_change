### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from executing either one of two alternative sets of activities (set X: A33, A34, A35, A37, A38; set Y: A39, A40, A41, A42, A43) to executing all of them together in every case. In the earlier window, each set appeared in about half the traces and the two sets were almost mutually exclusive; in the later window, all ten activities appear in nearly every trace and co‑occur with each other. The order is consistent: A30 before them and A31 after them. This is directly shown by the co‑occurrence and order components of the REL items and by the participation increases in OCC. The main limitation is that the exact internal order within the combined set is not fully specified, but the change from alternatives to joint execution is clear.

Other differences: The position of A30 moved earlier (mean from 0.64 to 0.55 of trace length) and the positions of A33–A38 moved earlier, while A39–A43 moved later, consistent with the new combined order.
