### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from executing a fixed sequence of all activities to a choice between two alternative branches. In the earlier window, every trace contained A25, then A28/A30/A31, then A32/A33/A34, then A26. In the later window, traces either contain A25, A28/A30/A31, and A26, or A25, A32/A33/A34, and A26; the two groups of activities are now mutually exclusive alternatives. This is directly shown by the drop in participation of both groups, the near-zero co-occurrence between the groups, the new direct transitions from A25 to A32/A33/A34 and from A28/A30/A31 to A26, and the shortened lags between these activities. The large number of qualifying items (58 vs 0.63 expected by chance) rules out chance as an overall explanation. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two branches are not alternatives but that one group is sometimes skipped; however, the mutual exclusivity (co-occurrence ~2%) and the fact that A26 is always present after either group make the alternative-branch interpretation more consistent.

Other differences: A25's position shifted later (mean from 0.62 to 0.67), and A28/A30/A31 also shifted later, while A32/A33/A34 shifted earlier; these are consistent with the reordering of the branches. [POS001, POS003–POS008]
