### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering of activities A31 and A32 relative to A27 and A28. In the earlier window the typical order was A27 → A32 → A31 → A28; in the later window it became A27 → A31 → A32 → A28. This is directly shown by the reversed routing of A31 and A32 (DIR001, DIR002) and by the consistent shifts in pairwise distances: A27 became closer to A31 and farther from A32, while A32 became closer to A28 and A31 became farther from A28 (LAG001–LAG004). The change is consistent across all six items, and the number of qualifying items (6) far exceeds the 0.31 expected by chance for the control-flow family, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. The support is based on about 90–91 traces per window for each item, a substantial share of the 200 traces per window.

Other differences: none beyond the ordering change.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed shift reflects a change in the frequency of two alternative paths rather than a strict swap of positions, but the combined directional evidence from both DIR and LAG makes a simple reordering the most parsimonious interpretation.
