Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a mostly sequential flow in which A10 was a common step to a flow in which A10 is rarely executed and a set of activities (A30, A31, A36, A37, A38) are repeated about three times per trace and executed in a less fixed order.
