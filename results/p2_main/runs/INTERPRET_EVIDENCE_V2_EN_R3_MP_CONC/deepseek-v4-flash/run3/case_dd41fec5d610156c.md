### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a structure centered on A03 (repeated, leading to A04 or A05) to a structure where A02 and A01 form a new sequence (A02 before A01, A01 leading to A05), with A03 removed. A06's successor changed from A03 to A01 or A04. The large number of evidence items (29 vs 0.17 expected) and the consistent relational and directional changes support this. A competing explanation that this is a rare variant is ruled out by the scale of the change (A03 and A01/A02 are mutually exclusive across all traces). Limitations: The exact purpose of A01 and A02 is not known, and the change in A04/A06 participation is small.

Other differences: The lag between A04 and A04 changed from 3 to 2 steps, and A06-A04 lag changed from 2 to 1, but these are secondary.
