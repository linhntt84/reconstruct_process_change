### Observed differences

- A11 participation increased from about 29% to 57% of traces, and its mean position moved earlier (from 0.96 to 0.74 of the trace) [OCC001, POS003].
- A06 and A11 now co-occur in 57% of traces (up from 29%), always with A06 before A11, and the gap between them shortened: lag 1 rose from 13% to 55% [REL001, LAG003].
- A09 and A11 are mutually exclusive alternatives: before they never co-occurred and were both absent in 42% of traces; after, every trace contains exactly one of them (A09 alone 44%, A11 alone 57%) [REL003].
- A09's position moved earlier (mean from 0.97 to 0.75) [POS002].
- A08's position moved later (mean from 0.66 to 0.75) [POS001]. Its relation with A11 changed: before A11 always followed A08 and never occurred without A08; after, A11 occurs without A08 in 27% of traces and precedes A08 in 15% of co-occurrences [REL002, LAG004].
- A06's outgoing transitions shifted from mainly A03 (28%), A02 (18%), A08 (15%) to mainly A11 (31%) and A09 (23%) [DIR002]. A05's outgoing transitions shifted from A01 (67%) and A03 (22%) to A09 (39%) and A11 (39%) [DIR001]. A08's outgoing transitions shifted from A11 (32%) and A09 (31%) to A02 (33%) and A06 (23%) [DIR003]. A09 and A11 outgoing transitions became more diverse [DIR004, DIR005].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed so that A11 is now executed much more often and earlier, typically immediately after A06, and that A09 and A11 became mutually exclusive alternatives that are always present. Specifically, A11 participation rose from about 29% to 57% of traces, it now always co-occurs with A06 with A06 before A11, and every trace now contains exactly one of A09 or A11. This is supported by OCC001, REL001, REL003, POS003, DIR002, LAG003, and POS002.

Other differences: The ordering between A08 and A11 changed from a fixed A08-before-A11 to a mixed order, with A11 now able to occur without A08 and before A08 (REL002, LAG004, POS001). Also, A05's outgoing transitions shifted to A09/A11, and A08's outgoing transitions shifted away from A11/A09 (DIR001, DIR003). A09 and A11 outgoing transitions became more diverse (DIR004, DIR005).

The evidence cannot rule out that the observed changes reflect a shift between two process variants rather than a single modified process, and some before-window transition distributions are based on few traces (e.g., A05, A09, A11 had fewer than 10 outgoing transitions before), limiting precision.
