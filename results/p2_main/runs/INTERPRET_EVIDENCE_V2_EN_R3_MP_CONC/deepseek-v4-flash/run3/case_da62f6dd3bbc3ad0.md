### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the control flow: the process moved from a branching structure in which A38 could lead directly to A23 or go through A14, to a single path in which A38 always leads to A14, A14 and A15 become mandatory, and a mandatory choice between A20/A21 and A22 is inserted before A15. This is directly shown by the disappearance of the A38→A23 edge, the increase of A14/A15 to full participation, and the consistent ordering A38→A14→A15→A23 with A20/A21 or A22 in between. The number of qualifying items (24) far exceeds the 0.42 expected by chance, and the relational and positional evidence agree. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is a change in case mix that makes the A14-containing variant the only one executed, but the evidence is consistent with a deliberate restructuring of the path. Other differences: the slight position shifts of A38 and A23 are a consequence of the removed direct edge and do not indicate an independent change.
