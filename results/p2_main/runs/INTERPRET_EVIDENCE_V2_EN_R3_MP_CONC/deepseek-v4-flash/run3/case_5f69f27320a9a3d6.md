### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports that the process changed by removing activity A04 and introducing activity A01, with corresponding routing adjustments. Specifically, A03 now leads to A07 instead of A04, and A01 is connected to A03, A05 and A06, appearing in every trace. The positions of A03, A05 and A06 shifted later, and A01 appears in the middle. This is supported by the complete disappearance of A04 and appearance of A01, the direct successor change from A03 to A07, and the co-occurrence of A01 with all remaining activities. The number of evidence items (34) far exceeds the 0.17 expected by chance, so the overall difference is not a sampling artefact.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 is a renamed A04 with additional changes, but the positional difference (A04 was at 0.8, A01 at 0.39) suggests it is not a simple rename.
