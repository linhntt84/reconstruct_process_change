### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the allocation of work for activities A01, A03, A13, A14, A17, and A22 was redistributed among resources. The consistent pattern across all six activities—with the same set of users losing share and another set gaining—and the fact that six items were observed when fewer than one was expected by chance make a change in resource allocation highly likely. The main limitation is that we cannot tell whether this reflects a deliberate reassignment or a change in the pool of available resources, but the change itself is well supported. No control-flow or timing changes were detected.
