### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed so that a previously mandatory segment consisting of A19, A20 and A25 became optional, and that A06, which was a common step between A14 and A15, is now often skipped as A14 goes directly to A15. This is supported by the drop in participation of A19/A20/A25 from 100% to about 53%, the drop in A06 from 71% to 26%, the shift in A14's successor from A06 to A15, and the corresponding cooccurrence drops. The large number of qualifying items (59 vs 0.88 expected) rules out chance as an overall explanation. A limitation is that the increase in A04, A08 and A10 is not directly linked to this hypothesis, and the exact relationship between the optional segment and the bypass is not fully specified.

Other differences: A41's successor changed from A19 to A26; A06's position shifted later; A26's position shifted slightly earlier; several lag distributions changed (e.g., A14–A06, A14–A15, A41–A26, A15–A26, A46–A47). A competing explanation that the observed changes reflect a reordering rather than a removal is weakened by the cooccurrence drops with "neither" categories (e.g., A19–A20 both absent in 46.5% of traces), which indicate that these activities are indeed not executed in many traces, not just moved.
