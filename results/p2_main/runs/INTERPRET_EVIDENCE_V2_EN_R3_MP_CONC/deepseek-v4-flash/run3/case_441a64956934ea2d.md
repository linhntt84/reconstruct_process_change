### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A13 largely replaces A01 in the main flow. In the earlier window, A01 was frequent, early, and connected to many activities; in the later window, A01 is rare, late, and its outgoing edges are reduced to A06, while A13 becomes frequent, early, and takes over many of A01's predecessor and successor relations. This is consistently shown by occurrence, direction, position, co-occurrence, and lag indicators. The number of qualifying control-flow items (38) far exceeds the 0.46 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact.

Other differences: none beyond this substitution.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 and A13 are two labels for the same underlying activity that was renamed, but the evidence does not allow confirming that interpretation.
