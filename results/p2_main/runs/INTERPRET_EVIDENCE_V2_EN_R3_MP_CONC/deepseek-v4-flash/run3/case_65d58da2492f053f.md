### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a specific hypothesis: the process changed from a flexible ordering between two groups of activities to a fixed sequence where the group {A20, A21, A22, A24, A25} always precedes the group {A26, A27, A28, A29, A30}. This is supported by the consistent reversal of order in all 22 REL pairs, the shift in positions, and the increased lag between the groups. The routing within the first group also became more internal, and A25 now leads to A26, A27 to A36. The number of qualified items (100) far exceeds the 0.94 expected by chance, so the overall difference is not a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the process also changed the exact sequence within each group, but the ordering change itself is strongly supported.

Other differences: The routing of A35 changed from A26/A24 to exclusively A24. Also the number of successors for A20, A21, A22 reduced to a small set. These may be part of the same restructuring.
