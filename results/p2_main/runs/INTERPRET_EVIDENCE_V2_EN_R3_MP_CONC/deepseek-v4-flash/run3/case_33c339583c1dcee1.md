### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: A26 and A27 now occur before the group A35, A36, A41, A42, A43, whereas before they occurred after them. This is directly shown by the reversal of order in REL items (from ~99% one way to ~99% the other) and by the corresponding position shifts in POS items, all with full support. In addition, A25 now leads to A27 and no longer co-occurs with that group, and A02 participation dropped from about 32% to 10%.

Other differences: A02's successors changed from A19/A20 to A52/A38 etc.; A06 participation increased; A30 appears as a successor of A35/A36/A41/A42/A43; A25's successors changed to A27.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the process was split into different variants rather than a single reordering, but the order reversal is consistent and directly observed. The LAG items for A25 and A27 with the group have very low support in the after window (1–2 traces), so the disappearance of co-occurrence is based on few observations, but the REL and POS items are strong.
