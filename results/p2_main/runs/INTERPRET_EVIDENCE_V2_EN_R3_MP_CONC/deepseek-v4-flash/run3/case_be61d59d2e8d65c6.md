### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20, and A25 are essentially absent: they appear in about 0.5% of traces, down from about 33%, 29%, and 41% respectively. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The routing from A27 and A28 changed: A27 now goes to A28 in about 98% of its outgoing transitions (up from about 52%), and A28 now goes to A29 in about 97% (up from about 43%). The previous alternatives to A19, A20, and A25 are no longer taken. [DIR005, DIR006]
- The co-occurrence of A19, A20, and A25 with A27, A28, and A29 dropped from about 28–41% of traces to about 0.5%. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- The distance between A27 and A28, and between A28 and A29, became mostly one step (about 98% and 96% respectively, up from about 52% and 43%). The distance between A27 and A29 shortened from mostly 3 steps to mostly 2 steps. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed by removing (or making extremely rare) the activities A19, A20, and A25, which previously served as intermediate steps between A28 and A29 (and also between A27 and A28). In the later window, the path from A27 to A28 to A29 is taken directly, without these intermediate activities. This is supported by the near disappearance of A19, A20, and A25 in occurrence, the shift of A27 and A28 to almost exclusively target A28 and A29 respectively, the collapse of co-occurrence between the removed activities and the remaining ones, and the shortening of lags between A27–A28 and A28–A29 to one step. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, DIR006, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the after-window observations for A19, A20, and A25 themselves rest on only one trace each, so their exact successors in the later window are not reliable; however, the full-support REL and DIR items for A27 and A28 independently show the disappearance of the alternative paths.

Other differences: The outgoing distribution of A10 also changed (DIR001), and small shifts in the positions of A27 and A29 were observed (POS004, POS005), but these are not part of the main hypothesis.
