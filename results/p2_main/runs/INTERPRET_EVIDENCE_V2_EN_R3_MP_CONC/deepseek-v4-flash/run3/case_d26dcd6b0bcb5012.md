### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19 and A20 each appeared in about 53% of traces; in the later window, they appear in about 99% of traces. [OCC001, OCC002]
- A25, A26 and A27 also became more frequent: A25 rose from about 24% to 51% of traces, A26 from 24% to 52%, and A27 from 30% to 49%. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- A43 and A28 are present in essentially all traces in both windows, but their relationship changed. Before, A43 led directly to A28 in about 45% of traces (lag 1) and was 4–5 steps away in about 52%; after, A43 and A28 are almost always 4–5 steps apart (about 99%). [LAG001]
- A43's outgoing transition shifted: before, about 49% went to A19 and 45% to A28; after, about 90% go to A19 and only 1% to A28. [DIR002]
- A19 and A20 now almost always co-occur, with A19 before A20 (about 99% of traces, up from 53%). A28 follows A20, and A43 precedes A19, in nearly all traces. [REL001, REL005, REL006, REL010, REL011]
- The branch between A25/A26 and A27 was previously absent in about 46% of traces; after, one of these branches occurs in about 99% of traces, with A25/A26 in about half and A27 in about half. [REL013, REL016, REL012, REL014]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from having two variants — a short one (A43 → A28 directly) and a long one (A43 → A19 → [A25→A26 or A27] → A20 → A28) — to almost always executing the long variant. The short variant, present in about 47% of traces before, is now observed in only about 1% of traces. This is directly shown by the drop in A43→A28 transitions, the shift in A43–A28 lag from 1 to 4–5 steps, the near-universal presence of A19 and A20 between A43 and A28, and the disappearance of traces without the A25/A26 or A27 branch. The branch structure itself existed before within the long variant, so the change is the elimination of the short variant, not the introduction of the branch.

Other differences: The relative frequency of the two branches shifted slightly — before, within the long variant, A25/A26 appeared in about 45% and A27 in about 55%; after, they are roughly equal (about 50% each). [REL002, REL004, REL013, REL016] A27's outgoing transition also became more concentrated on A20 (from about 93% to 99%). [DIR001]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the short variant still occurs rarely (about 1% of traces) and was simply observed less often in the later window; however, the magnitude and consistency of the shift across multiple indicators make this unlikely to account for the overall change.
