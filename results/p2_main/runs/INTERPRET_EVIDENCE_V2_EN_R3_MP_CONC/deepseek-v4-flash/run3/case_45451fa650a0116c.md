### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the main routing: after A20, the process now proceeds to A21–A23 instead of A24–A26, and A06, which previously appeared between A20 and A35, is largely skipped. This is shown directly by the shift in A20’s successors [DIR005], by the large increase in co‑occurrence of A20 with A21–A23 and the corresponding decrease with A24–A26 [REL011–REL016], and by the matching participation changes of A21–A23 and A24–A26 [OCC004–OCC009]. The drop in A06 participation and its reduced co‑occurrence with A20 [OCC003, REL001] are consistent with A06 becoming a rare alternative rather than a regular step.

Other differences: A34 now goes directly to A35 instead of via A06 [DIR006, LAG006]. A06’s position in traces moved earlier, but this is likely a consequence of its reduced participation [POS001].

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the change was gradual or that a small share of traces still follows the old path; however, the magnitude and consistency of the successor, co‑occurrence and participation evidence make a structural routing change the most plausible interpretation.
