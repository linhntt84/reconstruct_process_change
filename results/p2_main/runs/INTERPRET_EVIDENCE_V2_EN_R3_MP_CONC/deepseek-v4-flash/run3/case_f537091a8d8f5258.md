### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the main execution path: activities A05 and A06, which previously came after A07 and A09, now come before them. This is shown directly by reversed order relations (REL), by successor changes (A04 now leads to A05 instead of A07; A06 now leads to A07 instead of A05), and by consistent position shifts (A05/A06 earlier, A07/A09 later). The large number of qualifying items (28 vs. 0.26 expected by chance) rules out chance as the overall explanation. A competing explanation—that two separate variants swapped in frequency—is unlikely because the order within co-occurring traces changed, not just the share of traces following each path. Limitations: a few supporting items have low support in the later window (e.g., A09's successors, some A07–A06 lags), but the core evidence is based on about 100 traces per window.

Other differences: A01's outgoing distribution also shifted (more to A05, less to A04/A07), which is consistent with the reordering but not part of the main hypothesis.
