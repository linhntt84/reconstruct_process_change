### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: activities A19, A20, and A25 moved from a later position (after A26/A27/A28) to an earlier position (before A26/A27/A28), and the routing from these activities changed from going to A28/A32 to going to A26. This is directly shown by the reversal in order between the two groups (REL001–REL006), the shift in positions (POS001–POS006), and the changes in outgoing transitions (DIR001–DIR006). The number of qualifying items (26) far exceeds the 0.6 expected by chance, so the pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that some activities were added or removed around the same time, but the consistent reversal of order and routing across multiple indicators makes a reordering the most plausible interpretation.

Other differences: A15's occurrence and repetition decreased, which is not part of the reordering hypothesis.
