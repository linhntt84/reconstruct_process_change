### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained exactly one A02, and the sequence was A02 → A04 → A03 (A02 always followed by A04, A04 always followed by A03). In the later window, A02 occurs on average 2.11 times per trace (vs 1.0), with about half the traces containing repeated A02. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002]
- In the later window, A02 is followed by A04 in about 53% of its outgoing transitions and by A03 in about 47%, whereas before it was always A04. [DIR001]
- In the later window, A04 is always followed by A02 (before it was A03), indicating a loop back to A02. [DIR002]
- A04 now appears in only about half the traces (49.5% participation) instead of all traces, and its position is earlier and more variable. [POS002, REL002, REL003]
- The order between A02 and A03 changed: before A02 always preceded A03 with a lag of 2; in the later window, about half the traces still have A02 before A03, but the other half show interleaving or repetition, and the lag distribution is spread (lag 1 in about 40% of the pairs, lag 3 in 11%, lag 5 in 5%). [REL001, LAG001]
- The lag between A02 and A04 changed from always 1 to a spread (lag 1 in about 18% of the pairs that co-occur within 5 steps). [LAG002]
- The lag between A04 and A03 changed from always 1 to lag 2 in about 46% and lag 4 in about 18% of the pairs. [LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a single pass through A02 → A04 → A03 to a loop in which A02 may be repeated via A04 before finally reaching A03, and A04 is optional. The direct successor change (A04 now always leads to A02) and the increased repetition of A02 are the strongest signs, together with the loss of a fixed order between A02 and A03. The evidence cannot rule out that the observed pattern arises from a mixture of two distinct variants (one with the loop, one without), but the consistency of the successor and repetition changes makes this unlikely to be a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A04 now appears in only about half the traces, and its position is earlier and more variable; the lag between A04 and A03 increased. These are consistent with the loop hypothesis but are not part of the main claim.
