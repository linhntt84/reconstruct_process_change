### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the middle segment of the process changed: between A44 and A29, the activities A26, A27 and A28 — all executed in every trace in variable order — were replaced by A19, A20 and A21, of which about one is executed per trace. The successor change of A44, the new successors of A19/A20/A21, the ordering relations (A44 before A19/A20/A21 before A29) and the shortened gap between A44 and A29 all point in the same direction. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the activities were relabelled rather than genuinely replaced; the evidence also shows that the old activities were all executed together while the new ones are alternatives, so the structure changed from "all three" to "one of three".

Other differences: A29's mean position shifted slightly earlier (from about 0.43 to 0.40 of the trace) and A44's slightly later (from about 0.31 to 0.33), consistent with the shorter middle segment. [POS007, POS008]
