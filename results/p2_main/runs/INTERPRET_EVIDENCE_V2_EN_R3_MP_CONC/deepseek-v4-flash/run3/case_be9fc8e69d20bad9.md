- In the earlier window, activities A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 were absent from all 200 traces. In the later window, each appears in about 18–21% of traces (A14: 21.5%, A15: 17.5%, A20: 19.5%, A21: 20.5%, A22: 21%). [OCC001–OCC005]

- In the later window, A23 still occurs in every trace, but its position shifts slightly later (mean relative position from about 0.376 to 0.401), consistent with an inserted step before it. [POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a set of five mutually exclusive activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) was inserted between A38 and A23. In the earlier window, A38 was directly followed by A23 in every trace; in the later window, A38 is followed by exactly one of these five activities, which is then followed by A23. The new activities are alternatives, not a sequence, and each occurs in roughly a fifth of traces. This is directly shown by the successor changes (A38 no longer goes to A23, the new activities all go to A23), the lag increase from 1 to 2, and the relational pattern (A38 before the new activity, new activity before A23). The number of qualifying items (34) far exceeds the 0.43 expected by chance, and all items point to the same structural insertion.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that these activities existed in the earlier process but were never executed in the 200-trace sample; however, their complete absence and the consistent, direct evidence of the inserted step make this unlikely.

Other differences: none beyond the described insertion.
