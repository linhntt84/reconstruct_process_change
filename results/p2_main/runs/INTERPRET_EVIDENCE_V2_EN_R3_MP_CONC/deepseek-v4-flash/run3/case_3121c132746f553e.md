- In the earlier window, A14 and A15 occurred in every trace; in the later window they occur in about 53% of traces. [OCC001, OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that in the later window about half of the cases take a direct path from A38 to A23, bypassing a block of activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) that were previously always executed. This is shown by the consistent drop in participation of those activities, the new direct transition from A38 to A23, and the reduction in the distance between A38 and A23. The screening comparison (29 items vs. 0.45 expected by chance) rules out chance as an overall explanation. A competing explanation that the process simply has two variants without a bypass is less likely because the direct transition and lag change point specifically to a shortened path.

Other differences: The relative position of A23 shifted slightly earlier (mean from 0.44 to 0.41) and A38 slightly later (mean from 0.30 to 0.32), consistent with the shorter path. [POS001, POS002]
