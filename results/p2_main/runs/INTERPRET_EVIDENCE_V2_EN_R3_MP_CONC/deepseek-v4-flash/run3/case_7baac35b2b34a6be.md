### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process replaced a set of alternative activities (A14, A17–A20) with a new set of activities (A15, A16, A25–A27) that are all executed together in a flexible order, between A43 and A28. The old activities disappeared and the new ones appeared in all traces, with direct successor changes (A43 now leads to the new set, and the new set leads to A28). The number of evidence items (92) far exceeds the 0.72 expected by chance, and the relational and positional evidence is consistent.

Other differences: The order among the new activities is not fixed (mixed order), and the positions of A43 and A28 shifted accordingly. These are part of the same structural change.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the activities were relabelled rather than replaced, but the evidence clearly shows a change in the middle segment of the process.
