### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the control flow: A21 and A22 now occur before A30, A31 and A36, whereas before they occurred after them; A20 now leads directly to A22, and A25 becomes a successor of A30/A31/A36 instead of A22. This is directly shown by the reversal of order relations (REL001–REL006), the position shifts (POS001–POS005), the successor changes (DIR001–DIR005), and the lag changes for A20–A22 and A21–A25 (LAG001, LAG004). The number of control-flow items (22) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.42), so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that some activities were added or removed rather than merely reordered, but the consistency across indicators makes a reordering the most plausible interpretation.

Other differences: The timing between A01 and A04 increased, and the timing between A24 and A28 decreased (TWAIT001, TWAIT002). These are separate from the control-flow reordering and are supported by a time-family item count (2) well above its chance expectation (0.009).
