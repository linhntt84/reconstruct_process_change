### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process changed from a structure where, after A25 and A26, there was a choice between two alternative sets of activities (A28–A33 or A34–A38) to a structure where both sets are executed sequentially, with A28–A33 before A34–A38. This is supported by the large increase in participation of all these activities, the change from zero co‑occurrence to near‑universal co‑occurrence with a fixed order, and the redirection of A25’s outgoing edges and the successors of A28–A33. The evidence cannot rule out that the earlier window sampled only one branch per case, but the consistency across many activity pairs makes a real change likely.

Other differences: The timing between A14–A20 and A15–A20 decreased by about half, indicating a change in timing. A25 moved earlier and A26 later, but these are minor and do not affect the main structural hypothesis.
