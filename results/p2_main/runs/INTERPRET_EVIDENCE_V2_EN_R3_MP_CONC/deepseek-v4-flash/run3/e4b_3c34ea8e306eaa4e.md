### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: a group of resources that previously handled several activities (A01, A03, A13, A14, A17, A22) was replaced by another group, and activity A07 became fully concentrated on a single resource (User_100). This is supported by eight resource-related items, all with high empirical strength, far exceeding the chance expectation of 0.006 items. The support is based on nearly all traces in both windows (about 200 per window for most activities, about 160 for A07). A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the set of resources available changed, which we cannot distinguish from a deliberate reassignment.

Other differences: The timing between A09 and A23 lengthened considerably, but this rests on only 26 and 14 observed intervals, so it is a weaker observation and is not part of the main hypothesis. [TWAIT001]
