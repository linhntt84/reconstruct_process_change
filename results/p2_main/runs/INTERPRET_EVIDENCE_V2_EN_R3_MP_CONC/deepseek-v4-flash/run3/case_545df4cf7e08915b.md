### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which activity A01 was replaced by A08 at the same position in the process, and activities A11 and A15 swapped their order relative to A03. In the earlier window A01 appeared in about 48% of traces and was followed by A09/A10/A16; in the later window A08 appears in about 48% of traces with the same successors, while A01 disappears. Predecessors A17 and A03 that previously led to A01 now lead to A08. In parallel, A11 moved from the end of traces to a middle position and A15 moved from a middle position to the end; A05 now leads to A15 instead of A11, and A11 now leads to A03 where A15 did before. The number of qualifying control-flow items (53) far exceeds the 0.48 expected by chance, so this is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that A01 and A08 are the same activity under a different label, but the behavioural pattern is consistent with a replacement.
