### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A20 always led to A22; in the later window, A20 leads to A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 (each about 20%) and almost never to A22 (0.5%). [DIR001]
- In the earlier window, A21 led to A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 (each about 20%); in the later window, A21 leads almost exclusively to A25 (99.5%). [DIR002]
- In the earlier window, each of A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 had a transition to A25 (about 18–25%); in the later window, that transition is replaced by a transition to A22 (about 17–22%), and A25 is no longer a successor. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]
- The relative order of A21 and A22 with respect to A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 reversed: in the earlier window A21 and A22 always occurred before each of those activities; in the later window they occur after them in about 99.5% of cases. [REL001–REL010]
- The mean position of A21 and A22 shifted later (by about 0.13 of the trace length), while the mean position of A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 shifted earlier (by about 0.15–0.17). [POS001–POS007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process was reordered: A21 and A22 moved from before to after the group A30/A31/A36/A37/A38, and the successor relations changed accordingly (A20 no longer goes to A22 but to the group; A21 goes to A25 instead of the group; the group goes to A22 instead of A25). This is directly shown by the reversal in relative order (REL) and the position shifts (POS), and is consistent with the successor changes (DIR). The LAG items are based on only one trace in the later window and are not used. A competing explanation that the observed differences are due to random sampling is unlikely given that 34 items were found when fewer than one was expected by chance.

Other differences: The change in A20's successor from A22 to the group, and the group's successor from A25 to A22, are part of the same reordering; no separate hypothesis is needed.
