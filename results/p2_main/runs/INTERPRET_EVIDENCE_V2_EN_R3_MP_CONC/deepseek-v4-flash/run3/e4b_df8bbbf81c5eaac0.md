### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that resource allocation for activities A01, A03, and A07 changed, with work redistributed from a set of previously frequent resources to a broader set including new resources. The three resource items are far more than expected by chance (3 vs 0.004 expected), and they consistently show the same pattern across three activities. The change is not a simple swap but a broadening of the resource pool, as the "OTHER" share increased. A competing explanation that this is random sampling variation is unlikely given the screening comparison. However, the resource identifiers are anonymous, so we cannot infer roles or teams.

Other differences: Activity A24 shows a decrease in occurrence and participation, but this rests on a single control-flow item (expected 0.16 by chance) and is not sufficient to infer a structural change.
