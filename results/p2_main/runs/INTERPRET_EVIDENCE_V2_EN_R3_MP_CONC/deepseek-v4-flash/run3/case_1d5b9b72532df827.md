### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed such that the relative order of two blocks of activities reversed: activities A19, A23, A26, A27, and A28, which previously occurred before A34, now occur before A29, while activities A20, A21, A22, A24, and A25, which previously occurred before A29, now occur before A34. This is directly shown by the reversal of successor relations (DIR) and the reversal of order in co-occurring traces (REL), and is consistent with the positional shifts (POS). The number of qualifying items (66) far exceeds the 0.88 expected by chance, and all items point to the same reversal. A competing explanation that this is a rare variant is ruled out by the scale and consistency of the evidence. A limitation is that the evidence is from a single case sample, but the magnitude of the change is large.

Other differences: The successor sets of A33 and A44 also swapped accordingly, and the positions of many activities shifted, but these are part of the same underlying reversal.
