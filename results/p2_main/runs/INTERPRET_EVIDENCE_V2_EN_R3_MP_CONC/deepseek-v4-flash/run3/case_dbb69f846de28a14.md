### Observed differences

- Activities A27, A36, A37 and A42 were present in essentially all traces before (about 100 in 100) and in about half the traces after (47–54 in 100); their mean occurrences per trace dropped from about 1.0 to about 0.5. [OCC001–OCC004]
- A25's outgoing behaviour changed: before it went to A42, A36 and A37 in roughly equal shares (about 30–34% each); after it goes to A28 in about 97% of its transitions. [DIR001]
- A26's outgoing behaviour changed: before it went to A27 in about 99% of transitions; after it goes to A27 in about 52%, and to A37, A42 and A36 in about 13–16% each. [DIR002]
- For A36, A37 and A42, the successor A28 dropped from about 31–34% to about 1%, and A31 became a successor (about 26–35%). [DIR003–DIR005]
- The order between A26 and A36/A37/A42 reversed: before, A36/A37/A42 preceded A26 in about 99% of cooccurring traces; after, A26 precedes them in about 45–46% of cooccurring traces, and they cooccur in only about half the traces. [REL006–REL008, REL014–REL016]
- A25 and A28 became adjacent: before they were 4 steps apart in about 91% of traces; after they are 1 step apart in about 98% of traces. [LAG001]
- A27 and A36/A37/A42 became almost mutually exclusive: cooccurrence dropped from 100% to about 1%. [REL011–REL013]
- A36, A37 and A42 now often occur without each other: in about 52% of traces none of the pair is present, whereas before they always cooccurred. [REL020–REL022]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process was restructured into two alternative branches. In the earlier window, A25 led to A36/A37/A42, which then led to A28, and A26 led to A27; all these activities were present in every trace. In the later window, A25 always leads to A28, and the process splits after A26: one branch contains A27, the other contains A36, A37 and A42 (which are now mutually exclusive with A27 and with each other). A26 now precedes A36/A37/A42 when they occur, reversing the previous order. A28 is always present and immediately follows A25. The large number of qualifying items (48 vs 0.73 expected by chance) and the consistency across OCC, DIR, REL and LAG make this a supported hypothesis.

Other differences: A25 moved slightly later in the trace, A26 and A28 moved earlier, and A36/A37/A42 moved later when they occur. These are consistent with the reordering but are not part of the main hypothesis. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed split reflects a change in case mix rather than a change in the process model itself, but the routing changes (A25→A28, A26 branching) are direct evidence of a structural change.
