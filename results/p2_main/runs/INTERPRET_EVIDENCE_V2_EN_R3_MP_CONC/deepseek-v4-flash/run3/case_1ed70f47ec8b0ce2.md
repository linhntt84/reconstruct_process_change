### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A14, A16, and A19 occurred early in traces (mean position about 0.34–0.35) and were followed by A20; in the later window they occur late (mean position about 0.67–0.68) and are followed by A25. [POS001, POS003, POS006, DIR001, DIR003, DIR006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reversal in the order of two groups of activities: the group {A14, A16, A19} moved from occurring before the group {A15, A17, A18, A20, A24} to occurring after it. This is directly shown by the consistent reversal in all tested pairwise orders, the corresponding positional shifts, and the swapped successor relationships. The change is not limited to a single pair but affects the whole set of activities, and the number of qualifying items (39) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.46), so the pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A20 and A24 also shifted slightly later in position (A20 from 0.38 to 0.44, A24 from 0.57 to 0.64), and the lag between A24 and A25 shortened while that between A35 and A20 lengthened. [POS007, POS008, LAG001, LAG002]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed reversal reflects a change in the relative frequency of two alternative process variants rather than a uniform reordering of all traces, but the consistency across many independent pairs makes this less likely.
