### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation for several activities, most notably A08, which became fully assigned to a single resource. The resource-family items (8 items vs. 0.005 expected by chance) far exceed chance, and the changes are large and consistent across A01, A03, A08, A13, A14, A17, and A21: the set of resources used shifted, and concentration increased. The hypothesis is that the process now allocates work to a different set of resources, and for A08 to a single dedicated resource. Limitations: resource identifiers are anonymous, so we cannot infer roles or reasons; the change could reflect staffing changes or case mix, but the evidence directly shows a change in assignment distributions.

Other differences: Timing between A04 and A01 increased, with longer waits especially in the upper tail (time-family items 2 vs. 0.017 expected). This is a separate change in timing. The minor TWAIT002 change is weak due to small support. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the resource changes are a consequence of a change in case mix or activity frequencies, but no control-flow evidence suggests a structural change.
