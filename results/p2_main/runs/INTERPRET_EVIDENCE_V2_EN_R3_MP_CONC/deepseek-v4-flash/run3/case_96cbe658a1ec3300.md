### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of activities: A26 and A27, which previously occurred before A35, A36, and A41, now occur after them. This is directly shown by the reversal of relative order in REL items, the shift in positions (A26/A27 later, the cluster earlier), and the successor changes (A25 now routes to the cluster instead of A27; A26 now leads directly to A30). The screening comparison (26 control-flow items vs 0.63 expected by chance) rules out chance as a whole. The main limitation is that some lag items have very low support in one window (e.g., LAG002, LAG004–LAG010), but the core items (REL, POS, DIR, LAG001, LAG003) have full support. The evidence cannot rule out that the later window reflects a different variant of the process rather than a gradual modification, but the consistency across multiple indicators supports the reordering hypothesis.
