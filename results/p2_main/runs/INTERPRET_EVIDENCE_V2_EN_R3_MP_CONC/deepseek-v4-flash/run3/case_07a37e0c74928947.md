### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from executing a set of activities (A19, A22, A23, A24, A25) in about 20% of cases to executing a different set (A20, A21, A30, A31, A32) in almost all cases, while the surrounding activities A33 and A48 remain present in all traces. The new activities occupy the same position relative to A33 and A48 as the old ones did (after A48, before A33). This is a structural change in the activities executed. The evidence is direct and consistent across participation, successor, and co-occurrence indicators. A competing explanation that the old activities still exist but are extremely rare cannot be ruled out, but the magnitude of the change (from ~20% to ~1%) makes it unlikely.
