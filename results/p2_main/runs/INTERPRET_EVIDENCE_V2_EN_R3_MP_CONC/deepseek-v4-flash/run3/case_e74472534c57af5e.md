### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that a loop involving A30, A31, and A36 was introduced or became frequent, causing these activities to repeat multiple times per trace. In the earlier window each occurred about once per trace; in the later window each occurs about 3.5 times per trace, and the share of traces with repeats rose from about 0.5% to about 68%. The order between A30 and A36 changed from a fixed A30-before-A36 (about 98.5% of traces) to interleaved or repeated (about 68.5% of traces), and A36's outgoing edge shifted from mostly to A25 (98%) to mostly to A30 (71%). These signs are direct and consistent, and the number of qualifying evidence items (19) far exceeds the 0.5 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the repetition arises from a different loop structure or from multiple separate executions of the same segment, but the pairwise relations point to a single loop.
