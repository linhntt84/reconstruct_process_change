### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of a block of activities: in the earlier window, A14, A15, A20, A21, and A22 occurred later in the trace (after A24) and were followed by A25/A29; in the later window they occur earlier (before A23) and are followed by A23. This is directly shown by the shift in their positions (POS), the reversal of their order relative to A23/A24 (REL), the change in their outgoing edges to A23 (DIR), and the lag changes (LAG). The pattern is consistent across many activities and backed by full-trace evidence (support 200 for several items). A random-sampling explanation is unlikely given 34 qualified items versus 0.48 expected by chance.

Other differences: A12 becomes more frequent; A24 and A25 routing becomes more deterministic (A24→A25, A25→A29); A42 no longer leads to A23 but to the moved block. These are consistent with the reordering but not part of the main hypothesis.

Limitation: Some lag items (LAG003–LAG006) have very low support in the later window (1 trace), so those specific lag shifts are weak, but the main pattern is supported by many other items.
