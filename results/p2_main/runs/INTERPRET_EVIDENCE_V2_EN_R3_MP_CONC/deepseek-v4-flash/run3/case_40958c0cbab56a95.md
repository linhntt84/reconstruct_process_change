### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: the path A44 → A26/A27/A28 → A29, which was present in all traces before, was replaced by a path A44 → (A19 or A20 or A21) → A29, with A26/A27/A28 almost disappearing and A19/A20/A21 appearing as mutually exclusive alternatives in about a third of traces. A11 and A12 now precede the alternative activities. This is supported by the consistent changes in activity occurrence, outgoing transitions, co-occurrence relations, and the shortening of the A44–A29 lag from 4–5 steps to 2–3 steps. A limitation is that A19/A20/A21 appear in only about a third of traces, so the new path is not universal; the remaining traces still contain A29 but without these activities, and the evidence does not show what precedes A29 in those cases.

Other differences: A10 increased in participation from about 14% to 23% of traces. The order between A11/A12 and A19/A20/A21 reversed (A11/A12 now come before them). A29 and A44 shifted slightly in position (A29 earlier, A44 later).
