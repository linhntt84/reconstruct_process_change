### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the relative order of activities A30 and A31 between A26 and A27 swapped. In the earlier window, the typical sequence was A26 → A31 → A30 → A27; in the later window it became A26 → A30 → A31 → A27. This is directly shown by the successor changes (A30 now more often leads to A31 than to A27, and A31 now more often leads to A27 than to A30) and by the lag changes (A26–A30 became closer, A26–A31 became farther, A30–A27 became farther, A31–A27 became closer). The number of qualifying items (7) far exceeds the 0.33 expected by chance, and all items point to the same swap. The support is based on about 100 traces per window for the involved activities, which is moderate but consistent.

Other differences: A26's outgoing distribution also shifted toward A32 (from about 22% to 30%) and away from A31, which is not part of the main swap hypothesis. This could reflect an additional change in routing preferences, but it does not contradict the ordering swap. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a rare combination of sampling fluctuations, but the high number of consistent items makes this unlikely.
