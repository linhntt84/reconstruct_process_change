### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that, in the later window, a group of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) that were absent before now appear in about 16–23% of traces, positioned around the middle of the process, and that these activities are inserted between the A29/A30 pair and A31, so that the previously dominant path A29→A30→A31 is now often interrupted by these new activities. This is supported by the appearance of these activities, the shift in A29's and A30's successors toward them, and the increased lag between A29–A30 and A30–A31. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG003]

Other differences: A06's participation increased and its main successor changed to A47, and A46's successor changed to include A06. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR009]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities existed before but were not executed in the earlier sample; however, the large number of consistent indicators makes this unlikely. Also, the exact order among the new activities is not determined.
