### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control-flow structure: the previously fixed sequential order A01 → A02 → A04 → A03 has become more flexible. In the later window, A02 and A04 can appear in either order, and new direct transitions (A01→A04, A02→A03, A04→A02) allow bypassing or reordering of the original sequence. The consistent shifts in outgoing transitions, positions, and lags all point to this relaxation of ordering constraints and the introduction of alternative paths. The number of qualifying items (10) far exceeds the 0.1 expected by chance, and the changes are large and mutually consistent. A competing explanation that the process simply switched to a different fixed order is ruled out by the mixed order between A02 and A04 (about half each direction) and by the simultaneous shifts in lags that show both one-step and two-step gaps. The main limitation is that the evidence is based on 200 traces per window, but the magnitude and consistency of the changes make a real behavioural shift highly likely.

Other differences: The direct transition from A01 to A04 and from A02 to A03 are part of the same hypothesis, as they enable the reordering and bypassing. No other independent differences are present.
