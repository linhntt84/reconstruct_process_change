- Activity A07 was present in all 200 traces before (mean 2.08 occurrences per trace) and completely absent in all 200 traces after. [OCC003, POS003, DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process was restructured: activity A07 was removed, activity A01 was introduced, and the direct successor of A08 changed from A07 to A05/A06. Before the change, A08 always led to A07, and A07 then led to A05 or A06; after the change, A08 leads directly to A05 or A06, and A01 (when present) leads to A03. This is directly shown by the disappearance of A07, the appearance of A01, and the change in A08's outgoing edges. The decrease in A02 participation is an additional difference but not part of the main structural change.

Other differences: A02 participation decreased from about 47.5% to 27.5% of traces. Also, the relative positions of A03 and A08 shifted slightly, and the lag between some activity pairs changed (e.g., A02 to A05/A06 from 4 steps to 3 steps), but these are consistent with the removal of A07 and the new routing.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two windows represent different process variants rather than a change over time, but the evidence is strong and consistent.
