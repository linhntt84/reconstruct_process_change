### Observed differences

- In the later window, each of A15, A17, A18, A19, A20, A21 and A26 has a single dominant outgoing transition (about 99–100% of cases) instead of several alternatives. For example, A15 goes to A18 in about 99.5% of cases (was 62%), A17 to A15 in 99.5% (was 56%), A18 to A19 in 99.5% (was 12%), A19 to A21 in 99.5% (was 55.5%), A20 to A27 in 99.5% (was 47%), A21 to A20 in 100% (was 65.5%), and A26 to A17 in 100% (was 50%). - The order between activities became fixed: for example, A15 before A19 in about 99.5% of cases (was 25.5%), A15 before A20 in 100% (was 69%), A15 before A21 in 100% (was 49.5%), A17 before A19 in 100% (was 50%), A18 before A19 in 99.5% (was 12%), and A18 before A21 in 99.5% (was 30.5%). - The distance between activities became concentrated at a single lag: e.g., A15 and A18 are adjacent (lag 1) in 99.5% of cases (was 62%), A15 and A19 are two steps apart in 100% (was 52.9% at lag 1 and 47.1% at lag 2), A17 and A18 are two steps apart in 99.5% (was 31.5% at lag 2), and A18 and A27 are four steps apart in 99.5% (was 12% at lag 4). [LAG001–LAG029]
- Positions shifted: A15, A17 and A18 moved earlier (mean position decreased by about 0.05, 0.02 and 0.06 respectively), while A19, A20 and A21 moved later (mean position increased by about 0.06, 0.03 and 0.04). [POS001–POS006]
- The elapsed time between A22 and A29 increased from about 0.90 hours to 1.88 hours (mean), and between A30 and A29 from about 0.20 to 0.40 hours. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a flexible ordering with multiple possible successors to a fixed sequential chain: A26 → A17 → A15 → A18 → A19 → A21 → A20 → A27. This is directly shown by the DIR items (each activity now has a single dominant successor), the REL items (the order between pairs became fixed), the LAG items (the distance between activities became concentrated at a single value), and the POS items (activities shifted to positions consistent with this chain). The timing items show a separate increase in elapsed time between A22–A29 and A30–A29, roughly doubling, which is not part of the structural chain.

Other differences: The two TWAIT items indicate a timing change (increased elapsed time) for the pairs A22–A29 and A30–A29.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed chain is an artefact of a rare variant in the later window, but the large number of consistent items (50 control-flow items vs. 0.47 expected by chance) makes this unlikely.
