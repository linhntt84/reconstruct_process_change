### Observed differences

- In the later window, A02 is present in every trace (100%) and occurs on average about 1.8 times per trace, whereas earlier it was present in about 49% of traces and occurred once per trace. [OCC002]
- A01, A06, A07 and A08 all become less frequent: A08 drops from 100% to 45% of traces (mean from 2.0 to 0.8), A01 from 49% to 31% (mean from 1.0 to 0.4), A06 from 49% to 29% (mean from 1.95 to 0.7), A07 from 31% to 15% (mean from 0.95 to 0.33). [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- The direct successor of A02 changes from always A01 to a split between A04 (about 57%) and A03 (about 43%). [DIR001]
- The direct successor of A08 changes from A03/A04 (50/50) to always A02. [DIR004]
- A03 now sometimes leads to A01 (about 52%) instead of always to A06. [DIR002]
- A06 now leads to A08 (about 53%) instead of A02, while still leading to A07. [DIR003]
- A02 and A04 become always co-occurring (100% of traces) with A02 before A04 in about 55% of traces, whereas earlier A04 occurred without A02 in about 52% of traces. [REL006]
- A02 and A01 no longer always co-occur: A02 appears without A01 in about 70% of traces in the later window, compared to 0% earlier. [REL001]
- A04 and A08 no longer always co-occur: A04 appears without A08 in about 55% of traces later, compared to 0% earlier. [REL011]
- The lag between A02 and A04 shortens from 3 steps to 1 step in most cases, and the lag between A08 and A02 shortens from 3 steps to 1 step. [LAG011, LAG033]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the main path: in the later window, A02 leads to A03/A04 instead of A01, and A08 leads back to A02 instead of to A03/A04. This makes A02 the central activity, present in every trace, while A01, A06, A07 and A08 become less frequent or optional. The direct successor changes (DIR001, DIR004) and the co-occurrence and lag changes (REL006, REL011, LAG011, LAG033) are consistent with this reordering. The large number of qualifying items (64 vs 0.36 expected by chance) rules out chance as the sole explanation. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that some activities simply became less frequent without a structural reordering, but the direct successor changes and the consistent shift in order make that less likely.

Other differences: A03 now sometimes goes to A01 instead of always to A06, and A06 now goes to A08 instead of A02; these are consistent with the reordering but are not part of the main hypothesis.
