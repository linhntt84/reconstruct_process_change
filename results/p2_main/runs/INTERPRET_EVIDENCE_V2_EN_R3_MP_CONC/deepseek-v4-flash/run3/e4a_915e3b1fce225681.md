- In the earlier window, A20 had roughly equal outgoing edges to A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 (each about 20%). In the later window, A20 goes almost exclusively to A22 (about 99.5%). [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which A22 and A25 swapped their roles in the process, and A21 moved from after the group of activities A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 to before them. In the earlier window, A25 was the successor of A21, while A22 was a successor of the group; in the later window, A22 is the successor of A20, and A25 is a successor of the group, while A21 now precedes the group. This is directly shown by the outgoing-edge changes (DIR), the position shifts (POS) and the ordering reversals (REL). The number of control-flow items (34) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.55), and the items are mutually consistent.

Other differences: The timing between A09–A10, A11–A10 and A24–A28 decreased substantially, with the A24–A28 gap becoming essentially zero. This is a separate timing change, supported by three time items against a chance expectation of 0.018.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a rare variant that happens to dominate the later window, but the large number of consistent items makes this unlikely.
