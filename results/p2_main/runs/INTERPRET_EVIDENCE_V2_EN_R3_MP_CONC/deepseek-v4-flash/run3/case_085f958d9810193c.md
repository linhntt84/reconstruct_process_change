### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process was restructured so that the group of activities A35, A36, A41, A42, and A43 now occurs before A26 and A27 (previously after), and A30 is no longer a successor of that group; instead A27 is a successor. Additionally, A02 is now executed earlier and more often, leading to A19/A20. This is directly shown by the reversal of order in REL items, the disappearance of A30 as a successor in DIR items, and the appearance of A27 as a successor, all with strong support (200 traces per window). The position shifts are consistent with this reordering. A competing explanation that the observed differences are due to random sampling is ruled out by the screening: 64 control‑flow items were flagged when fewer than 1 was expected by chance.

Other differences: A25's outgoing transition changed from A27 to the group A35/A36/A41/A42/A43, and A26 now leads to A30. These are consistent with the same restructuring but are not part of the main hypothesis. The LAG items with support of only 1–2 traces in the later window (e.g., LAG011–LAG019) are too weak to interpret individually, but they do not contradict the main pattern.
