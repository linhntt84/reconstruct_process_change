### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: A14, A15 and A20 now occur before A21 and A22, whereas before they occurred after them. This is directly shown by the reversal in co-occurrence order (REL) and is consistent with the position shifts (POS) and the new outgoing edges (DIR) that connect A14/A15/A20 to A21, A40 to A14/A15/A20, A22 to A23, and A23 to A27. The large number of qualifying items (24 vs. 0.39 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a shift in the mix of variants rather than a change in the underlying process, but the direct relational evidence of order reversal in the same traces makes this less plausible.

Other differences: A10 and A11 are repeated less often per trace (mean occurrences and recurrence both decrease), which is not part of the main reordering hypothesis.
