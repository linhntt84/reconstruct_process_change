### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed from a flexible structure with optional activities and variable ordering to a more linear, mandatory sequence. Specifically, activities A27, A36, A37, A42, A43 and A44 became mandatory (present in all traces) and are now executed after A02, while A47 now precedes A02. A26 and A28 now occur later relative to that block. The large number of qualifying items (145 vs. 1.36 expected by chance) and the consistency across occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators make a chance explanation unlikely. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is a shift in case mix rather than a change in the underlying process, but the evidence is strongly consistent with a structural reordering and the addition of a mandatory block of activities.

Other differences: Activities A04, A06 and A10 became less frequent, which may reflect that they are no longer used as alternatives in the new structure.
