### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control-flow structure: the process shifted from a flexible, multi-branching routing with variable ordering to a more deterministic, sequential sequence. Many activities now have a single dominant successor, and the order between many activity pairs became fixed, with activities appearing closer together in the trace. This is directly shown by the successor redistributions (DIR), the ordering changes (REL), and the distance changes (LAG). The large number of qualifying items (58 vs. 0.76 expected by chance) makes it unlikely that these differences are due to random sampling.

Other differences: A08 decreased in frequency while A10 increased, which may be part of the same restructuring but is not directly linked to the routing changes. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is a change in the mix of case types, which could produce similar shifts in routing and ordering without a change in the underlying process model.
