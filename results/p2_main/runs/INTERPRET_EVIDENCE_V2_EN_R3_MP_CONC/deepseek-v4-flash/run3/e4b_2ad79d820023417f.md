### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: the set of resources performing several activities (A01, A03, A13, A14, A16, A17) changed, with a shift towards a smaller set of resources, and A16 became fully concentrated on a single resource. This is supported by 7 resource-family items against 0.005 expected by chance, all with high empirical strength and full support. The change is consistent across multiple activities. A competing explanation that this is a random sampling effect is unlikely given the number of items and their consistency.

Other differences: The timing between A04 and A01 increased, but this rests on a single time-family item (1 vs 0.012 expected), so it is not sufficient on its own to infer a timing change; it is reported as an observed difference only.
