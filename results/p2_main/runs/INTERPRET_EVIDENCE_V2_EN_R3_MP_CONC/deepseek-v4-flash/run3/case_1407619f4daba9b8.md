### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a process with two alternative branches to a single sequential path that includes all the previously alternative activities. In the earlier window, traces either contained A22 (with A23 before it) or contained A31/A32/A37 (with A20 before them); A21 branched to either A22 or one of A31/A32/A37. In the later window, A22, A31, A32, and A37 are all executed in nearly every trace, with A20 leading to A31/A32/A37, A21 leading to A22, and A23 appearing later (four steps after A20). The order among A31, A32, and A37 remains mixed, but they now always co-occur. The large number of qualifying items (41 vs 0.49 expected by chance) and the consistency across occurrence, direction, position, relation, and lag indicators make a chance explanation unlikely. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window simply reflects a different variant of the process that was already possible but rare; however, the magnitude and coherence of the changes make this less plausible.

Other differences: A26's position shifted slightly later, and its relation to A31/A32/A37 changed from being after them to being after them (already noted). No time or resource changes were observed.
