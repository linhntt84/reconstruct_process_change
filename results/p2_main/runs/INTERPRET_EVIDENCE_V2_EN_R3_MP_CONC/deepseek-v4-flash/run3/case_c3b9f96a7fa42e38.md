### Observed differences

- In the later window, A06 appears in about 12.5% of traces (down from 27%), with mean occurrences per trace dropping from 0.285 to 0.145; A02 appears in about 11.5% of traces (up from 4.5%). [OCC001, OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the relative order of two groups of activities reversed: A26 and A27 now occur before A35, A36, and A41, whereas before they occurred after. This is directly shown by the reversal in all six REL pairs and corroborated by the position shifts (POS) and the lag changes (LAG). The routing also changed consistently: A25 now leads to A27 instead of to A35/A36/A41, and A26 no longer leads to A30; instead A35/A36/A41 lead to A30. The large number of qualifying items (33 vs. 0.76 expected by chance) makes it unlikely that these differences are sampling noise.

Other differences: A06 became less frequent and its outgoing transitions more varied, and A02 became more frequent. These are not part of the main hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a single large reconfiguration of the process rather than two independent changes, but the consistency across indicators strongly supports a real change in ordering and routing.
