### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the order of execution between two groups of activities reversed. In the earlier window, activities A15, A16, A17, A19, and A20 were executed before A14, A18, A21, A22, A23, A24, and A28; in the later window, the latter group is executed before the former. This is directly shown by the complete reversal of order for pairs with A24 and A28, the reversal for pairs with A18, A22, and A23, and the consistent position shifts. The successor changes (A29 to A24, and the swap between A28 and A39) are consistent with this reordering. The co-occurrence rates are stable, so this is not a change in which activities are executed together but a change in their sequence.

Other differences: The successor of A14, A18, A21, A22, A23 changed from A29 to A24, and the successors of A28 and A39 swapped between the two groups. These are part of the same reordering but could be considered separate.

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the process has alternative branches and the observed reversal reflects a change in which branch is taken, rather than a global reordering. However, the consistency across many pairs and the complete reversal for A24/A28 make a simple reordering more likely.
