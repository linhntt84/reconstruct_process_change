### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a single path in which A30, A31, and the six activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38, A39 all occurred in every case, to a process with two alternative branches: one branch containing A33, A35, A36 and the other containing A37, A38, A39, each taken in about half the cases, while A30 and A31 remain mandatory. The later window shows the two groups are mutually exclusive (cooccurrence near zero), each group's members still co-occur, and A30 now directly connects to both groups, with A31 following the chosen branch more closely. The large number of consistent control-flow items (54 vs. 0.8 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely.

Other differences: A30's position shifted later in the trace (mean from 0.62 to 0.67), and A31's position shifted slightly earlier (0.82 to 0.80). [POS001, POS002] These are minor and not part of the main hypothesis.

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window contains two distinct case types rather than a single process with a branch, but the evidence cannot distinguish between these.
