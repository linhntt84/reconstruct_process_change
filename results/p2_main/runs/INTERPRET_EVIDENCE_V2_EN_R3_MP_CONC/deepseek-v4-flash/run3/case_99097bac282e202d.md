### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: the branch that previously executed A21, A22 and A23 after A20 was replaced by a branch executing A24, A25 and A26 after A20. In the earlier window, A20 was followed by A21–A23 in about 75% of traces; in the later window, A20 is followed by A24–A26 in about 75% of traces. The participation of A21–A23 dropped from about 75% to 25%, while A24–A26 rose from about 25% to 75%. This is directly shown by occurrence, routing, and co-occurrence indicators, and the lag between A20 and A24 also shifted consistently. The number of qualifying items (62) far exceeds the 0.91 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A10 increased in frequency and moved later, with its outgoing transitions becoming dominated by A33; A02 decreased; A32's outgoing to A33 decreased slightly. These are not part of the main hypothesis.

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the activities A21–A23 and A24–A26 are the same underlying activities with different labels, which would not represent a process change.
