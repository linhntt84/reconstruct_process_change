### Observed differences

- In the later window, activity A01 appears in every trace (100% participation), whereas it was completely absent in the earlier window. [OCC001]
- In the later window, A04 and A09 now route to A01 in about 88% and 91% of their outgoing transitions respectively, instead of to A08, A06, and A07 as before. [DIR002, DIR003]
- A01 is positioned late in the trace (median position 0.8) and always precedes A08; A04 and A09 occur before A01 in most traces. [POS001, REL004, REL001, REL005]
- The distance between A04/A09 and A08/A06/A07 increased by one or two steps, e.g., A04 to A08 changed from mostly 1 step to 2–3 steps. [LAG003, LAG007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG006, LAG004, LAG005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A01, was inserted into the process between the earlier activities A04 and A09 and the later activities A08, A06, and A07. In the later window, A01 appears in every trace, is preceded by A04 and A09, and is followed by A08 (and sometimes A06/A07). The routing changes and increased lags are consistent with this insertion. The number of qualifying items (19) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.18), and the pattern is direct and consistent across occurrence, routing, position, and lag indicators. A limitation is that the order between A01 and A06/A07 is not fixed, and we cannot fully rule out that A01 existed but was unobserved in the earlier window, though its absence in all 200 traces makes that unlikely.

Other differences: The order between A01 and A06/A07 is mixed: A06 precedes A01 in about 36% of traces and A01 precedes A06 in about 10%; similarly, A07 precedes A01 in about 36% and A01 precedes A07 in 17%. [REL002, REL003] This suggests that A01 is not strictly ordered relative to these activities, but that is a secondary detail.
