### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed its routing after A20: instead of executing the group A24–A26, it now executes the group A21–A23. This is directly shown by the shift in A20's outgoing transitions and by the corresponding co-occurrence changes, with all involved activities present in substantial shares in both windows. The number of qualifying items (60 vs 0.9 expected by chance) rules out chance as a whole. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed shift reflects a change in the mix of cases rather than a change in the process logic itself.
