### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from executing a set of activities A21–A25 (and A04) after a common predecessor A20 to executing a different set A26–A30 (and A02, A06, A08, A10) instead. The participation of A21–A25 and A04 dropped sharply, while A26–A30 and A02/A06/A08/A10 rose correspondingly, and the successor of A20 shifted from A24 to A29. This is directly shown by occurrence, direction, and relation indicators. The large number of qualifying items (138 vs 1.29 expected) rules out chance as an overall explanation.

Other differences: A04's position moved from near the end of traces to the middle [POS001], and the lag between A38 and A39 shortened (1-step share from 45% to 90%) [LAG010]. These are consistent with the main shift but not part of the main hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the old activities are not removed but become optional or are executed in a different part of the process; the evidence shows they are rarely observed in the later window, but zero participation is not proven. However, the scale and consistency of the shift make a replacement of the main path the most plausible interpretation.
