### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A20, A21, A22, A24, A25 occurred early in the trace (mean position about 0.55–0.61), while A26–A30 occurred later (mean about 0.63–0.74). In the later window, A20–A22 moved later (mean about 0.62), A25 moved much later (mean about 0.70), and A26–A30 moved earlier (mean about 0.55–0.63). [POS001–POS010]
- The order between these two groups reversed. Before, A20–A25 almost always preceded A26–A30 (e.g., A20 before A26 in 97.5% of traces, A25 before A26 in 97.5%). After, A26–A30 often precede A20–A25 (e.g., A26 before A20 in 72%, A26 before A25 in 92.5%). [REL001, REL005, REL009, REL017, REL019, REL021]
- The outgoing transitions of A20, A21, A22, A24, A25 changed from mostly going to A20–A25 and A26 to going to A26–A30 and others. For example, A25 before went to A26 in 99.5% of transitions; after, it goes to A36 (39%), A27 (16%), A29 (14%), A30 (8%), etc. [DIR001–DIR005]
- The distance between these activities changed: e.g., A20–A26 before had lag 2–4, after lag 1 in 46% of cases; A25–A26 before lag 1 always, after lag 1 in 93% (but support low). [LAG004, LAG027]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the ordering between two groups of activities reversed: activities A20–A25, which previously occurred before A26–A30, now occur after them. This is supported directly by consistent positional shifts, relational order reversals, and successor changes across many items. The screening shows 102 control-flow items against 0.97 expected by chance, so the overall difference is far beyond what a stable process would typically produce. A competing explanation that the activities were replaced by different ones is unlikely because the same activity codes appear in both windows and co-occurrence remains 1.0 for these pairs. However, the evidence cannot rule out that some activities, particularly A25, also changed their routing to new successors like A36, which may indicate a separate structural change beyond the order reversal.

Other differences: The successors of A25 changed from almost exclusively A26 to a wide set including A36, A27, A29, and A30, suggesting a change in the path taken after A25. [DIR005]
