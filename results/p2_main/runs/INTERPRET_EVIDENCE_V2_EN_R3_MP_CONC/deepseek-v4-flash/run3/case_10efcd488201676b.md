### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a substantial restructuring of the process: activities A01 and A17 were removed, activity A07 was introduced, and activities A06 and A16 became mandatory single final steps, while A02 became a heavily repeated central activity that connects to many other activities. This is supported by the disappearance/appearance of activities, the shift of A06 and A16 to the end, the change in successor relations, and the increased co-occurrence and interleaving of A02 with other activities. The number of qualifying evidence items (171) far exceeds the 0.94 expected by chance, so the overall difference is not attributable to random sampling.
