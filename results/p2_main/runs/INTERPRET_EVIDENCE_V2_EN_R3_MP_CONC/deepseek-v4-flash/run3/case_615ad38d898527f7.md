### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the process: a large set of activities (A27, A36, A37, A42, A43, A44, A25, A26, A28, A31) that were previously executed in nearly every trace are now executed in only about half of the traces, and the control flow between them has been rearranged. Specifically, A14 now leads to A15 instead of A10, A25 leads to A28, and A31 becomes a common successor of the A36–A44 group. A10's participation dropped from 77% to 25%. The number of qualifying evidence items (152) far exceeds the expected 1.48, so this is not a chance fluctuation. The evidence is consistent across OCC, DIR and REL indicators.

Other differences: A02 and A08 increased in participation, and A10's outgoing transitions became much more varied. These are not part of the main hypothesis but are additional changes.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a change in case mix or sampling rather than a true process modification, but the magnitude and consistency of the changes make this unlikely.
