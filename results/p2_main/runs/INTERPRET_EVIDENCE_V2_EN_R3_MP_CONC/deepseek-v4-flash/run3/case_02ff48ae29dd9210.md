### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from having a set of optional or alternative activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) that were executed in a substantial share of traces to almost never executing them, so that the main flow now goes directly from A24 to A25 to A26. The participation of these activities dropped from about 15–23% of traces to at most 0.5%, and the routing of A24 and A25 shifted to exclusively A25 and A26 respectively, with the lag between A24 and A26 shortening from 3 to 2 steps. The large number of consistent items (38 vs 0.46 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely. A limitation is that the after-window support for the disappearing activities is very low (0–1 traces), so their exact new role cannot be characterized beyond their near-absence; the evidence cannot rule out that these activities still exist but are executed extremely rarely or in a different context not captured by the windows.
