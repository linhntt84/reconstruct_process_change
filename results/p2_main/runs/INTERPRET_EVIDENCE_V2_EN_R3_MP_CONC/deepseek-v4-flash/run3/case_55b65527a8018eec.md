### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A33, A34, A35, A37, A38, A39, A40, A41, A42, and A43 each occurred in essentially every trace (participation 1.0, mean occurrences ~1.0). In the later window, each of these occurs in only about 46–55% of traces (e.g., A33 in 46.5%, A39 in 54.5%). [OCC001–OCC010]
- In the earlier window, A30 and A31 each co-occurred with all of A33–A43 in every trace (cooccurrence 1.0). In the later window, A30 co-occurs with A33–A38 in about 46–47% of traces and with A39–A43 in about 54–55% of traces; the remaining traces contain A30 alone. The same holds for A31. [REL001–REL018]
- In the later window, the activities split into two mutually exclusive groups: A33, A34, A35, A37, A38 co-occur with each other (about 46% of traces) and never co-occur with A39, A40, A41, A43 (cooccurrence ~0.01–0.02); A39, A40, A41, A43 co-occur with each other (about 54–55% of traces). Before, all these activities co-occurred in every trace. [REL019–REL054]
- The positions of the two groups shift: A33–A38 move later in the trace (mean position from ~0.62–0.63 to ~0.69–0.73), while A39–A43 move earlier (from ~0.75–0.76 to ~0.71–0.73). [POS003–POS010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from executing a single set of activities (A33–A43) in every case to having two alternative branches: one branch containing A33, A34, A35, A37, A38 and the other containing A39, A40, A41, A43 (and possibly A36/A42). In the later window, each branch is taken in about half the cases, and the two branches are mutually exclusive. This is directly shown by the drop in participation of these activities from ~100% to ~46–55% and by the co-occurrence patterns: before, all these activities co-occurred; after, the two groups never co-occur. The screening comparison (93 control-flow items vs 1.1 expected by chance) rules out chance as an explanation. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two branches existed before but were not observed because the sample was small; however, the earlier window had 200 traces with 100% participation, making that unlikely. Other differences: the successor distribution of A30 changes (e.g., A39–A43 appear as successors in the later window), and A33 gains A31 as a successor in about 26% of its outgoing transitions; these are consistent with the branching but not needed for the main hypothesis.
