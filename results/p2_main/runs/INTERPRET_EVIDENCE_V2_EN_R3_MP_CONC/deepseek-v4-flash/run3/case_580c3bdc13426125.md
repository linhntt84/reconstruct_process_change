### Observed differences

- Activity A02 disappears: in the earlier window it occurred in all 200 traces, on average 2.2 times per trace and recurring in 59% of traces; in the later window it is absent. Activity A03 appears: absent before, it now occurs in all 200 traces, on average once per trace, with no recurrence. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, REL001]
- Routing changes: A05 previously always led to A02; in the later window it leads to A03 in about 97% of transitions, with small shares to A06 and A07. A06 previously always led to A02; in the later window it leads to A07 in about 51%, to A06 in 48%, and to A03 in about 2% of transitions. [DIR003, DIR004]
- Ordering changes: A03 now precedes A07 in about 99% of traces, whereas before A02 preceded A07 in about 41% and was interleaved with it in 59%. A05 now precedes A03 in all traces. [REL004, REL007, REL005]
- Spacing between activities shifted: lags between A05/A06/A07 were mostly 2 or 4 steps before; in the later window they spread over 1–5 steps, with A06–A06 and A06–A07 now often adjacent (lag 1). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A02 was replaced by activity A03 as a central step in the process. Before, A02 occurred in every trace, was the successor of A05 and A06, and preceded A07; after, A02 is absent, A03 occurs in every trace, is the successor of A05 (and partly of A06), and precedes A07. This is shown directly and consistently by occurrence, routing, position, and relation indicators. A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that A02 and A03 are the same activity under a different label, but the data show a complete swap in participation and routing.

Other differences: A06 now has self-loops and also leads to A07, and the spacing between activities changed (lags shifted from 2/4 to 1–5). These may be part of the same restructuring but are not needed for the main hypothesis.
