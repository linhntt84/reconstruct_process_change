### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a shift in the process from one branch to another after activity A15. In the earlier window, the process typically continued from A15 into the A16–A20 group; in the later window, it typically continued into the A21–A25 group. This is directly shown by the change in A15's successor (A19 to A24) and by the corresponding co-occurrence changes. The number of qualifying items (78) far exceeds the expected 0.84, and the pattern is consistent across occurrence, direct succession, and co-occurrence indicators.

Other differences: within the new branch, the routing after A22 changed, with A21 becoming less dominant and A23 and A25 more frequent. The order between A18 and A17 also shifted, but this rests on few observations in the later window.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two windows sample different case types rather than a change over time, but the magnitude and consistency of the evidence make a real change likely.
