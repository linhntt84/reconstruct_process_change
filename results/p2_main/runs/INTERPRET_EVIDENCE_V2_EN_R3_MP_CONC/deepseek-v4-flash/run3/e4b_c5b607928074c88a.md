### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that resource allocation changed: for activities A01, A03, A13, A14, A17 and A22, assignments shifted from a set of resources led by User_65 (with User_80 second) to a new set led by User_69, with several other resources gaining share. This is supported by six consistent assignment items across six activities, far more than the 0.005 expected by chance for the resource family. The main limitation is that the change could reflect a change in the available resource pool rather than a change in how assignments are made; the data cannot distinguish these.

Other differences: The elapsed time between A04 and A01 completions decreased substantially (mean from about 34 to 22 hours, median from about 28 to 19.5 hours), and between A09 and A23 (mean from about 9.8 to 3.2 hours, median from about 11.2 to 1.3 hours, based on fewer observations). These timing changes are supported by two time items versus 0.016 expected by chance, but they are not part of the resource-allocation hypothesis. A competing explanation for the timing change is a change in workload or processing conditions that the data cannot rule out.
