### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: a new resource (User_98) took over a substantial share of work on several activities (A01, A03, A08, A11, A13, A14, A17, A22), and activity A04 became performed by a single resource. This is supported by 9 resource-related evidence items against an expected 0.006 by chance, with a consistent pattern across activities and an extreme concentration change for A04. The resource identifiers are anonymous, so the change cannot be attributed to a specific role or team; a competing explanation is that the resource pool itself changed (e.g., a new staff member) rather than the process logic.

Other differences: Control flow also changed: A09 is more often executed after A05, A04 occurs later in the trace, and A16's successor distribution shifted from A11 toward A07. These are separate from the resource hypothesis and could reflect a different routing or variant, but the evidence does not link them to the resource change.
