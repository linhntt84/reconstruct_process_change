### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a block of activities (A25, A26, A31, A32, A33) changed from a single execution in a fixed order to repeated, interleaved executions, most plausibly a loop or repeated segment. This is directly shown by the increase in mean occurrences and recurrence, the relaxation of ordering constraints (from fixed order to interleaved/repeated) both among these activities and with A23, and the increased spread in positions and lags. A33’s new outgoing edge to A25 is consistent with this repeated block. The screening comparison (39 items vs 0.51 expected by chance) makes it very unlikely that these differences are sampling noise.

Other differences: A17 appears less often and later in the trace, and A23 also shifts later; these may be consequences of the added repetitions rather than independent changes. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the repetition is a parallel execution rather than a loop, but the interleaving pattern and the recurrence support point to a repeated block.
