### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A02 is no longer executed in the later window. In the earlier window A02 was present in about half of the traces and had a rich set of outgoing transitions; in the later window it is completely absent, with no occurrences, no outgoing transitions, and no co-occurrences with any other activity, while those other activities (A07, A09, A10, A13, etc.) appear alone or in new combinations. This is directly and consistently shown by OCC001, DIR001, POS001, and all REL items involving A02 (REL001–REL009). The number of qualifying items (60) far exceeds the 0.67 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact. The main limitation is that the evidence cannot rule out that A02 was replaced by another activity or that its label changed, but within the observed data it disappears entirely.

Other differences: A03 and A14 participation decreased from about 49% to 19%, and their positions shifted later; A10 participation increased from 24% to 46%; many successor distributions changed (e.g., A08, A14, A15). These are separate from the disappearance of A02 and may reflect a broader restructuring, but they are not part of the main hypothesis.
