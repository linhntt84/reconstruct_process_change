### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from executing both a group of activities (A28–A33) and another group (A34–A38) sequentially to choosing one of the two groups per case, with about half the cases taking each branch. This is directly shown by the drop in participation of each group to about half, the near-zero cooccurrence between the groups, and the routing of A25 to either group while A26 follows whichever group is present. The LAG items are based on a single trace in the later window and do not add support. A competing explanation that the later window reflects a different case population cannot be fully ruled out, but the evidence is consistent and extensive.

Other differences: A26 now appears as a successor of A28, A29, and A33, and the positions of activities shifted slightly; these are consistent with the main structural change.
