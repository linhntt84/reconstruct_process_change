### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process was restructured: a block of activities A20, A21, A30, A31 and A32, which were always executed and preceded A33, was removed, and a new set of activities A19, A22, A23, A24 and A25 was introduced, which now appear before A33 (and before A48). The change is directly visible in participation, successor, relational, positional and lag indicators, all consistently showing the disappearance of the old block and the appearance of the new activities in the same relative position. A04 participation also dropped sharply, which may be part of the same restructuring. A limitation is that the new activities appear in only about 16–24% of traces, so they may be optional or conditional rather than mandatory replacements; the evidence cannot rule out that the old block still exists in a small, unobserved share of cases.
