- Activities A25, A26, and A31 each occurred on average about 3.4 times per trace in the earlier window and about 1.0 times per trace in the later window. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a repetitive loop in which A25, A26, and A31 were executed multiple times per case to a single execution of each, with a fixed order A25 before A31 and A31 now followed by A20 instead of A25. The strong drop in mean occurrences and recurrence for all three activities, the shift in A31's successor from A25 to A20, and the near-total replacement of interleaving/repetition by a fixed A25→A31 order with a short lag (2 steps) are directly consistent with this interpretation. The screening comparison (9 control-flow items vs 0.3 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the loop was replaced by a different structure not captured by the available relational evidence for A26, but the direct ordering and successor evidence for A25 and A31 strongly supports the stated hypothesis.
