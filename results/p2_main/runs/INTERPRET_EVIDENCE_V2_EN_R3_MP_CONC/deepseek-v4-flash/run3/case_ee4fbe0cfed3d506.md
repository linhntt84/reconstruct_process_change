- A30's outgoing transitions changed from a small set (A33–A38 plus A41) to a wider set including A39–A43, with more even shares. [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a single sequential block to two alternative branches. In the earlier window, every trace contained A30, then a block of activities A33–A38, then a block A39–A43, then A31. In the later window, A30 and A31 are still always present, but the middle block is split: about half the traces execute A33–A38 and the other half execute A39–A43, and the two groups are almost never executed together. This is directly shown by the drop in co-occurrence between the groups from ~100% to near 0%, while within-group co-occurrence remains high, and by the convergence of their positions. The number of qualifying items (112) far exceeds the 1.28 expected by chance, so this is not a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that the two groups are simply skipped independently is ruled out by the near-zero co-occurrence: they are alternatives, not independent options.

Other differences: A30's position moved later in the trace (mean from 0.55 to 0.64), and its successor distribution became more even. These are consistent with the restructuring but not part of the main hypothesis. The LAG evidence is too sparse (many items have support of 1 or 2 in one window) to add reliable detail.
