### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in timing: the elapsed time between the completion of activities A16–A19 and A26 decreased by about half in the later window. This is supported by four time indicators, all showing a consistent drop in mean and median hours from about 1.0 to about 0.5, with high empirical strength and substantial support (46–59 intervals per window). The time family screening expected only 0.013 items by chance, but 4 were observed, far exceeding chance. The main limitation is that the evidence does not identify what caused the time reduction (e.g., schedule or resource changes), but the consistency across four activity pairs makes a timing change the most plausible interpretation.

Other differences: The routing from A06 changed, with A08 becoming less frequent and A07 and A09 more frequent. However, this rests on a single control-flow item, and the control-flow family expected 0.28 items by chance, so this difference alone does not strongly support a structural change; it could be a chance variation or a separate, less certain change.
