### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a switch between two alternative branches after A15: in the earlier window the process executed a branch containing A19, A20 and A21 (with A25), and in the later window it executes a branch containing A16, A17 and A18 (with A25). The routing from A15 changed accordingly, and the two groups remain mutually exclusive, so this is a shift in which existing path is taken rather than a replacement of activities. The evidence is direct (DIR001) and consistent across participation, co-occurrence and routing indicators. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the activity labels A16–A18 and A19–A21 were renamed between the windows, but the observed routing change and the mutual exclusivity pattern are equally consistent with a genuine switch in process behaviour.
