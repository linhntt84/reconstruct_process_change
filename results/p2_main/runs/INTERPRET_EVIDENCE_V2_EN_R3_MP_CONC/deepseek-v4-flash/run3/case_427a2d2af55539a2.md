### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process replaced the set of intermediate activities A14–A16 with A21–A23, and changed from selecting one of three alternatives to executing all three new activities in a variable order. This is directly shown by the complete disappearance of A14–A16 and the appearance of A21–A23 in every trace, the change in A39's outgoing edges, and the increase in the A39–A24 lag. The mixed order among A21–A23 indicates no fixed sequence between them. A competing explanation that the old activities still exist but were not sampled is unlikely given they are absent in all 200 later traces, but cannot be fully ruled out.

Other differences: A24's position shifted slightly later, consistent with the longer path from A39 to A24. [POS007, LAG001]
