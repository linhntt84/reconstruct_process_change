### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A14, A15, and A20 appear in about 29%, 41%, and 30% of traces respectively, while they were completely absent in the earlier window. They never co-occur; each trace contains exactly one of them in about 99.5% of traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL006]
- In the earlier window, A22 always led to A23 and A23 always led to A24. In the later window, A22 leads directly to A23 in about 44% of traces and to A14/A15/A20 in the remaining 56%; A23 leads directly to A24 in about 57% of traces and to A14/A15/A20 in the remaining 43%. [DIR004, DIR005]
- The new activities are placed within the A22–A23–A24 chain: the lag between A22 and A23 is 1 in 44% and 2 in 56% of traces; between A23 and A24 it is 1 in 57% and 2 in 43%; between A22 and A24 it is 3 in about 99.5% of traces (previously 2). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- The new activities appear around the middle of the trace (mean position about 0.51), while A22 shifted slightly earlier and A24 slightly later. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- Timing: the elapsed time between A26 and A32 increased from about 0.89 to 1.81 hours on average, and between A33 and A32 from about 0.17 to 0.35 hours. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new step (one of A14, A15, or A20) is inserted into the existing A22→A23→A24 sequence, appearing in almost all traces, either between A22 and A23 or between A23 and A24. The specific new activity and its exact position vary across traces. This is directly shown by the appearance of A14/A15/A20, the redistribution of outgoing edges from A22 and A23, the increased lags between A22–A23, A23–A24, and A22–A24, and the middle-of-trace positions of the new activities. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: The elapsed times between A26–A32 and A33–A32 increased substantially in the later window, indicating a timing change independent of the structural insertion. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities are not inserted but replace an existing step; however, A22, A23, and A24 remain present in all traces, so insertion is the more consistent interpretation.
