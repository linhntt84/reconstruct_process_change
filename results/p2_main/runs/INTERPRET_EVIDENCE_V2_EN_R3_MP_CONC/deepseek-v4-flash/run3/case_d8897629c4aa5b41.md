### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the process changed from a simple linear path A22→A23→A24 to a structure that includes a set of alternative activities (A14, A15, A20) that can appear between A22 and A23/A24, and can also be revisited from A23, before eventually reaching A24. This is directly shown by the appearance of the new activities, the redistribution of outgoing edges from A22 and A23, and the increased lags between A22, A23, and A24. The new activities are mutually exclusive, indicating they are alternative branches. A competing explanation that the new activities are simply rare variants is ruled out by their substantial participation (29–41%) and the consistent relational and lag evidence. A limitation is that the exact control flow (e.g., whether A23 can loop back to A14/A15/A20 multiple times) is not fully specified, but the evidence clearly indicates an insertion of new activities into the original path.

Other differences: The new activities are mutually exclusive alternatives, and A22 and A23 now have multiple outgoing paths, which is part of the main hypothesis. No other differences are indicated.
