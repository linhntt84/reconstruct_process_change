### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural shift in which A10 replaced A05 as the central, near-mandatory activity. In the earlier window A05 was present in every trace and A10 in fewer than half; in the later window A10 is present in every trace and A05 in about half. Successor changes and co-occurrence patterns consistently show that many activities that previously connected through A05 now connect through A10, while A05 itself became optional and less frequent. A08 also decreased and A16 increased, but these are secondary to the main rerouting. The number of qualifying items (55) is far above the 0.46 expected by chance, and the pattern is coherent across occurrence, direction, and relation indicators. A competing explanation that the sample composition changed rather than the process itself cannot be fully excluded, but the consistency of the rerouting across many activities makes a genuine change in process structure the more plausible interpretation.

Other differences: A08 participation halved, A16 participation more than doubled, and some ordering constraints shifted (e.g., A10 now tends to precede A11 and A15, whereas before A11 and A15 tended to precede A05). [OCC002, OCC004, REL022, REL024]
