### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which A06 became a common intermediate step: A46 now leads to A06 (79% of A46's outgoing transitions vs 0% before), and A06 now leads to A19 and A20 (about 90% combined) instead of A15/A50/A16. Consequently, A06 appears in 82% of traces (up from 14%), and A46 precedes A06 in 78% of traces (up from 5.5%). This is directly shown by the successor changes and the co-occurrence/order relations. The large number of qualifying items (85 vs 1.08 expected by chance) rules out chance as an explanation.

Other differences: The order between A26/A27 and A35/A36/A41/A42/A43 reversed, with A26/A27 now following those activities instead of preceding them. Also, A30 was replaced by A27 as a successor of A35, A36, A41, A42, A43, and A25's successor changed from A27 to a spread of activities. These may be part of the same restructuring but are not directly linked to the A06 insertion.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a different variant mix rather than a true process change, but the direct successor and ordering evidence supports a structural change.
