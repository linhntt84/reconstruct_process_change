### Observed differences

- In the later window, the outgoing transitions of activities A20, A21, A22, A25, A26, A27, A28, A29, A30 and A35 are concentrated on a few successors instead of many. For example, A20 previously had successors A25, A22, A26, A21, A27, A30, A29, A28, etc.; in the later window it goes only to A22, A21, A25 (each about a third). A25 previously had many successors, with A36 at 40%; in the later window it goes to A26 in about 98% of cases. [DIR001, DIR004]
- The relative order between the early group (A20, A21, A22, A24, A25) and the late group (A26, A27, A28, A29, A30) reversed and became fixed. In the earlier window, for many pairs the order was mixed or the late activity came first (e.g., A26 before A20 in 74% of cases, A28 before A25 in 80%, A29 before A25 in 85%). In the later window, the early activity precedes the late activity in at least 96% of cases for every such pair. [REL001, REL018, REL020, REL021, REL022]
- The mean relative position of the early activities decreased (moved earlier): A20 from 0.61 to 0.55, A21 from 0.61 to 0.55, A22 from 0.61 to 0.54, A25 from 0.70 to 0.60. The late activities moved later: A26 from 0.53 to 0.63, A28 from 0.62 to 0.68, A29 from 0.60 to 0.67, A30 from 0.61 to 0.68. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS005, POS006, POS008, POS009, POS010]
- The number of steps between early and late activities increased. For example, A20 and A28 were within 1–2 steps in about 45% of cases before, but in the later window they are 3–5 steps apart in all cases (56% at 5 steps). Similarly, A21 and A30 were within 1–2 steps in about 41% before, but in the later window they are 3–5 steps apart in all cases (49% at 5 steps). [LAG005, LAG014]
- Within the early group, the steps between activities decreased: A20 and A21 were 1–2 steps apart in about 61% before, and in the later window they are 1–2 steps apart in 100% of cases (69% at 1 step). [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a relatively flexible ordering among activities A20–A30 to a strict sequential order in which a set of early activities (A20, A21, A22, A24, A25) always occur before a set of late activities (A26, A27, A28, A29, A30). This is directly shown by the reversal and fixing of order in the REL items, the shift in positions in the POS items, the concentration of successors in the DIR items, and the change in lags in the LAG items. The number of evidence items (100) far exceeds the 1.01 expected by chance, so the overall difference is not a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A35's successor changed from A26 and A24 to A24 almost exclusively, and A27 now leads to A36 more often (from about 42% to 77% of its outgoing transitions). These are not part of the main ordering hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a change in the case mix or a different variant being used, rather than a change in the underlying process model. However, the consistency across multiple indicators and the large number of items make this less likely.
