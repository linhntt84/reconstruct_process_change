### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which activity A04 is now executed in most traces, occurs later in the process, and leads predominantly to A47, while a set of activities (A20, A25, A26, A27) that were previously present in about a fifth of traces are now almost never executed. This suggests that a previously existing path through those activities has been replaced by a path through A04 and A47. The relational and positional evidence consistently shows A04 now co-occurs with the main flow activities and precedes A47. A competing explanation that the change reflects a shift in case mix rather than a process modification cannot be fully ruled out, but the consistency across many indicators makes a structural change the most plausible interpretation.

Other differences: A46 now leads to A04 more often, and A04's position moved later. Also A02, A06, A10, and A19 decreased in participation. These are consistent with the main hypothesis.
