### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which a group of activities (A21–A25) replaced another group (A26–A30) as the main executed path. In the later window, A21–A25 are executed in about 77% of traces instead of about 19%, while A26–A30 drop from about 82% to about 24%. This is accompanied by a change in the successor of A20 from A29 to A24, and by A14 and A20 now co-occurring with the new group rather than the old one. The number of qualifying items (103) is far above the 1.15 expected by chance, so the difference is not a sampling artefact. The main limitation is that the evidence does not show whether the old activities were removed or simply became rare; zero participation in a window is not proof of absence. Other differences: the lag between A20 and the new group shortens, and A21–A25 now also co-occur with A34, suggesting the new group is placed closer to A20 and interacts with A34.
