Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the timing between certain activities changed: in the later window, the intervals from A18 to A17 and from A29 to A17 became substantially longer, roughly doubling in mean and median. The main limitation is the moderate sample sizes behind these intervals (27 and 50 before, 23 and 45 after), but the consistency and magnitude make a real change plausible.

Other differences: Activity A43 showed a reduction in repetition – it still appears in every trace, but less often per trace and with fewer repeated occurrences. This is a separate control-flow change, not directly linked to the timing shift.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the longer intervals reflect a general slowdown in the process rather than a change specific to these activity pairs, but the evidence directly shows the interval increases.
