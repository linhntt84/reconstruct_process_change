### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new activity, A03, was introduced into the process, appearing in about half of the cases and inserted between existing activities. Specifically, A03 is placed after A05 and before A11, and also between A08 and A12 in both orders. This is supported by the appearance of A03 only in the later window, its middle position in traces, its outgoing transitions to A08/A11/A12, its incoming transitions from A05, A08, and A12, and the relational and lag evidence showing that A03 co-occurs with these activities while the distances between existing activities increased, consistent with an extra step.

Other differences: A05 moved earlier in the trace (median position from ≈0.18 to 0.0). This is consistent with A03 being inserted after A05, but could also reflect a separate change in the start of the process.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A03 existed before but was not observed due to sampling; however, its high frequency in the later window (52%) makes this unlikely. The exact placement of A03 is inferred from relational and lag data, and the process may contain additional variations not captured here.
