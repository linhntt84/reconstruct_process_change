### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which A01 was removed from the main flow and became a standalone start activity (appearing alone at the beginning of about one third of traces), while a new activity A17 was introduced into the main flow, and the ordering of several activities (A05, A08, A16, A09, A04, A13) was rearranged. The successor and relational evidence is consistent: A01 no longer connects to A09, A17 connects to A09, and the old successors of A05, A08, A16, A09, A04, and A13 are replaced by new ones. The number of qualifying items (42) far exceeds the 0.39 expected by chance, so this is not a sampling artefact. A limitation is that the exact new sequence is not fully determined, but the direction of change is clear.

Other differences: A02 and A03 became less frequent, and A05 became much rarer; these may be secondary effects of the same restructuring but are not part of the main hypothesis.
