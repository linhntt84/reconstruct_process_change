### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: activities A03, A11, and A21 became more concentrated on fewer resources, with A11 becoming fully assigned to one resource (User_123) and A03/A21 shifting a larger share of work to User_3. The four resource-related evidence items far exceed the chance expectation (0.006 expected), and each has high empirical strength, making random sampling an unlikely explanation. The change is consistent across these activities, though it is limited to the observed activities and does not indicate a process‑wide resource shift.

Other differences: The time between A04 and A01 decreased substantially (median from ~70h to ~12.5h). This is a separate timing change, supported by one strong item (TWAIT001), but it is not part of the resource allocation hypothesis and could be an isolated effect.
