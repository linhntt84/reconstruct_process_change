### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A16, A17 and A18 each occurred in about 75% of traces; in the later window, each occurred in about 25% of traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the earlier window, activities A19, A20 and A21 each occurred in about 25% of traces; in the later window, each occurred in about 75% of traces. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

- The lag between A15 and A20 shortened: before, the gap was 3 steps in about half of the cases; after, it was 1–2 steps in about two-thirds. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a shift in the branch taken after A15: in the earlier window, A15 was followed by the group A16–A18 in about 75% of cases and by A19–A21 in about 25%; in the later window, the proportions reversed, with A15 followed by A19–A21 in about 75% and by A16–A18 in about 25%. This is directly shown by the occurrence changes, the redistribution of A15's outgoing transitions, and the co-occurrence patterns between A15 and each group. The screening comparison (35 items vs. 0.53 expected by chance) makes it very unlikely that these differences are random sampling artefacts. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the activities A16–A18 were removed and A19–A21 added, rather than a rerouting between existing alternatives; however, both groups are present in both windows, so a shift in path frequencies is the more parsimonious interpretation.

Other differences: The lag between A15 and A20 shortened, consistent with the branch change. No other differences were observed.
