### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A27, A36, A37 and A42 each appeared in about half of all traces (46–54%), with about 0.5 occurrences per trace on average. In the later window, each of these activities appears in essentially every trace (99–100%) and about once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a mutually exclusive choice between two branches — one containing A27 and the other containing A36, A37 and A42 — to a single path that executes all of A36, A37, A42, A28, A26, A27 and A31 in sequence, with A36, A37 and A42 occurring early and in any order. This is directly shown by the rise in participation of A27/A36/A37/A42 from about half to nearly all traces, the redirection of A25 from A28 to A36/A37/A42, the redirection of A26 from a split to A27, and the relational evidence that A36/A37/A42 now precede A26, A28, A27 and A31, whereas before they were alternatives to A27. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019]

The main limitation is that the exact order among A36, A37 and A42 is not fixed (both orders occur in roughly equal proportions), and the relative order of A28 and A26 is not directly evidenced, though the overall sequence is clear. A competing explanation that the process simply added the previously alternative activities without changing their relative order is ruled out by the consistent reversal of predecessor–successor relations for A26, A28 and A27. [REL020, REL021, REL022, LAG001, LAG003]

Other differences: A25’s position shifted slightly earlier (mean from 0.362 to 0.346), and A31’s position shifted later (mean from 0.630 to 0.657); these are minor and consistent with the reordering. [POS001, POS005]
