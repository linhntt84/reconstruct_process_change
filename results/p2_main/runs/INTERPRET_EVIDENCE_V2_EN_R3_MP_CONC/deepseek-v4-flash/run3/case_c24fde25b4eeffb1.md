### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A25, A26, A31, A32, and A33 each occurred on average about 3.6 times per trace and were repeated in about 70% of traces; in the later window they occur about once per trace and are repeated in about 1% of traces. [OCC001–OCC005]
- The successor of A33 changed: before, about 72% of its outgoing transitions went to A25 and 28% to A20; after, about 98% go to A20 and 2% to A25. [DIR001]
- The order between A25 and each of A26, A31, A32, A33 changed: before, about 30% had A25 before the other and 70% were interleaved or repeated; after, about 99% have A25 before the other and 1% interleaved. [REL001–REL004, REL007, REL009, REL010]
- For pairs among A26, A31, and A32, before about 12–18% had one order, 16–18% the other, and 70% interleaved; after about 50% each order and 1% interleaved. [REL005, REL006, REL008]
- The lag between these activities shifted from spread across lags 1–5 to concentrated at lags 1 and 2: for example, for A26–A31, lag 1 rose from about 8% to 63% and lag 2 from 5% to 32%. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008]
- The relative position of each activity within the trace became much more concentrated: the standard deviation of relative position dropped from about 0.15 to about 0.05–0.06 for all five activities. [POS001–POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a repeated, interleaved execution of activities A25, A26, A31, A32, and A33 to a single, ordered execution of these activities, with A25 occurring before the others and A33 after the others, and with A33 now followed by A20 instead of A25. The large number of control-flow items (32 vs 0.49 expected by chance) and the consistency across occurrence, order, lag, and position indicators support this. The main limitation is that the exact order among A26, A31, and A32 is not fixed (both orders appear about equally), and the evidence does not show what happens after A20.

Other differences: The successor change of A33 to A20 is part of the main hypothesis. No other differences.
