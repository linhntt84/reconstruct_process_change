### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A01 and A05 were absent from all 200 traces; in the later window, each occurs exactly once in every trace (participation 0% → 100%) [OCC001, OCC002].
- A01 appears around the middle of traces (mean position 0.47) and A05 near the end (mean position 0.96) in the later window [POS001, POS003].
- A06's outgoing transitions shifted from mostly A02 (53%), A04 (29%) and A03 (19%) to predominantly A05 (84%) [DIR005].
- A03's outgoing transitions shifted from A06 (39%), A02 (38%), A04 (23%) to A01 (45%) as the most common successor [DIR002].
- A04's outgoing transitions shifted from A06 (74%) to A01 (52%) as the most common successor [DIR003].
- A01's outgoing transitions in the later window lead to A06 in 80% of cases [DIR001].
- Co-occurrence of A01 with A03, A04, A05 and A06 changed from none (or A01 absent) to co-occurrence in all traces; A01 precedes A05 and A06 in all such traces, while A03 and A04 precede A01 in the majority of traces [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL007].
- The distance between A03 and A04, and between A04 and A06, increased: lags of 4–5 steps appear in the later window where they were absent before [LAG001, LAG003].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The process changed by adding two new activities, A01 and A05, which now occur in every trace, and by rerouting the existing activities around them. In the earlier window, traces consisted of A03, A04 and A06 without A01 or A05; in the later window, A01 appears in the middle of the trace (typically after A03/A04 and before A06) and A05 appears near the end (typically after A06). This is supported directly by the zero-to-full participation of A01 and A05 [OCC001, OCC002], the new co-occurrence relations [REL001–REL007], and the changed successor distributions [DIR001–DIR005]. The increased lags between A03–A04 and A04–A06 are consistent with the insertion of A01 and A05 between them [LAG001, LAG003]. A competing explanation that the process was entirely replaced is unlikely because A03, A04 and A06 remain present in all traces, but the evidence cannot rule out that the earlier window represents a different process variant rather than a simple extension.

Other differences: The order between A03 and A01 is not fixed (A03 precedes A01 in 87% of traces, A01 precedes A03 in 13%), and similarly A04 precedes A01 in 69% of traces, indicating some flexibility in the placement of A01 relative to its predecessors [REL001, REL002].
