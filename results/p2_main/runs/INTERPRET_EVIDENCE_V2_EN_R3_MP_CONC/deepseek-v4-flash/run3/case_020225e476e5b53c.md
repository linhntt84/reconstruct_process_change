### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A30, A31, A36, A37, and A38 each occurred exactly once in every trace (mean 1.0, no recurrence). In the later window, each occurs on average about 3 times per trace, and about two-thirds of traces contain multiple occurrences (recurrence support ~0.67). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- In the earlier window, A38's outgoing transition went to A25 in about 99% of cases. In the later window, it goes to A30 in about 67% and to A25 in about 33%. [DIR001]
- In the earlier window, the relative positions of A30, A31, A36, A37, and A38 were tightly clustered (standard deviation about 0.05–0.06 of trace length). In the later window, their positions are much more spread out (standard deviation about 0.14), with some occurrences earlier and some later. [POS001–POS005]
- In the earlier window, for pairs among these five activities, they always co-occurred and usually had a fixed order (e.g., A30 before A31 in 100% of traces; A31 and A36 had mixed order in about half each). In the later window, for every pair, about two-thirds of traces show the two activities interleaved or repeated, and the fixed-order shares drop to about 15–34%. [REL001–REL010]
- In the earlier window, when A31, A36, and A37 occurred close together, they were typically 1 or 2 steps apart (about 65–70% at lag 1, 30–36% at lag 2). In the later window, lag 1 drops to about 10–13% and lag 2 to about 5–7%, with lags 3–5 appearing in small shares. [LAG001–LAG006]
- A37, which almost never repeated in the earlier window (1 trace), repeats in about 55% of traces in the later window, with repeats spread over lags 3–5. [LAG007]
- A04 participation increased from about 4.5% to 15% of traces. [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed from a single, ordered execution of activities A30, A31, A36, A37, and A38 (with A38 then moving to A25) to a repeated, interleaved execution of these same activities, with A38 now often looping back to A30. The repetition is shown by the increase in mean occurrences and recurrence support for all five activities; the loss of fixed order is shown by the shift from fixed A-before-B relations to interleaved/repeated relations; the routing change is shown by A38's successor shifting from A25 to A30; and the increased spread in positions and lags is consistent with these activities being repeated and interleaved rather than executed once in sequence. The evidence is direct and consistent across 29 items, far exceeding the 0.6 expected by chance.

Other differences: A04 also appears more often (from about 4.5% to 15% of traces), but this is a minor participation increase and not part of the main hypothesis. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a different trace variant that was rare before and became dominant, but the consistency across all five activities and the routing change make this less likely.
