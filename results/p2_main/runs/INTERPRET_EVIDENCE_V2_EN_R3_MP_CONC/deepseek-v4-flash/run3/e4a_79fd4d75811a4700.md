### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the routing after A22: A22 now more often leads to A21 instead of A25, and when A25 is reached, it is reached later (the lag between A22 and A25 increased). This is directly shown by the shift in A22's successor distribution [DIR001] and the corresponding increase in the distance between A22 and A25 [LAG003]. The change is large and consistent, and the number of qualifying items (4) far exceeds the 0.34 expected by chance for control-flow indicators, so it is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the observed shift reflects a change in case mix or in the frequency of A22 itself, but the successor distribution is conditional on A22 occurring, so the change in routing is independent of that frequency.

Other differences: The relative ordering of A16 with A17 and A18 changed: A17 became closer to A16, while A18 became further from A16 [LAG001, LAG002]. This appears to be a separate change, not directly linked to the A22 routing change.
