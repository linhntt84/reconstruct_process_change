### Observed differences

- In the later window, A01 is no longer observed at all: it was present in all 200 traces before (mean 1.0 per trace) and in none after. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003]
- A04 similarly disappears: it was present in 102 of 200 traces (51%) before and in none after. [OCC002, DIR003, POS004, REL003, REL004, REL005]
- A02 remains present (102 before, 93 after) but its outgoing behaviour changes: before it went to A06 in about 79% of its outgoing transitions, to A01 in 7% and to A04 in 14%; after it goes to A06 in 100% of its outgoing transitions. [DIR002]
- A03 remains present (98 before, 107 after) but its position shifts from a later point (mean relative position 0.44) to the very start (mean 0.0). A02 also shifts from mean 0.53 to 0.0. [POS002, POS003]
- The co-occurrence of A01 with A02 and A03 disappears: before A01 co-occurred with A02 in 51% of traces and with A03 in 49%; after A01 is absent, so those traces now contain only A02 or only A03. [REL001, REL002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change: the process no longer executes A01 and A04, and now starts with either A02 or A03 (mutually exclusive), with A02 always followed by A06. Before, A01 was always present and preceded a choice between A02 and A03, with A04 sometimes present. The disappearance of A01 and A04 is directly observed across multiple indicators (occurrence, outgoing transitions, position, and relations). The change in A02's outgoing transitions to exclusively A06 and the shift of A02 and A03 to the start are consistent with this. A competing explanation that A01 and A04 simply became rare in this sample is unlikely given their complete absence in 200 traces, but the sample cannot rule out their occasional occurrence outside the window.

Other differences: A06 becomes the sole successor of A02, and A02 and A03 are now the only starting activities, with no trace containing both (93+107=200). These are part of the same structural change.
