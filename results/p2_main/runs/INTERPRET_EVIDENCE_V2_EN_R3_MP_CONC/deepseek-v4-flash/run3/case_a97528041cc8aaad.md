### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 was introduced as an early step between A14 and A15, while A06 and A10 became less used. In the earlier window, A14 led almost directly to A15, and A02 was rare and late; in the later window, A14 often leads to A02, A02 then leads to A15, and A02 appears much earlier and in many more traces. This is directly shown by the successor changes of A14 and A02, the increased participation and earlier position of A02, and the large rise in co-occurrence of A02 with A14, A15 and the main body of activities. The evidence cannot rule out that the order reversals among A19, A20, A22, A23, A25 and A29 are a separate modification, but they do not undermine the main hypothesis.

Other differences: The order between several pairs of activities (e.g., A20/A25, A22/A25, A23/A25, A20/A29, A22/A29, A23/A29, and A19 with A20/A22/A23/A25/A29) reversed, with the second activity now occurring before the first in most traces. This is a distinct change in ordering constraints, not explained by the A02 insertion.
