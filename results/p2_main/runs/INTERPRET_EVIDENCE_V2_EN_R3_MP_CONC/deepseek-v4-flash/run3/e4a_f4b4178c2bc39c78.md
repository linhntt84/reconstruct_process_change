### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that a block of activities (A25, A26, A31) is now repeated multiple times per case, and the control flow between A25 and A31 changed from a fixed order (A25 before A31) to interleaved/repeated execution. This is directly shown by the increased occurrence and recurrence of these activities, the shift of A31's successor from A20 to A25, and the change in order and lag between A25 and A31. The timing change (TWAIT001) is a separate difference.

Other differences: The time between completions of A34 and A14 increased from about 1 to 2 hours, indicating a timing change. [TWAIT001]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the repetition is not a fixed loop but a conditional or optional repetition that occurs in a subset of cases; the exact structure of the repeated block is not fully specified.
