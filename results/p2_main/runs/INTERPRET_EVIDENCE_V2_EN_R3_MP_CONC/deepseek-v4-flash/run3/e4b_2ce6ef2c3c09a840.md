### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: the set of resources performing several activities (A01, A03, A11, A13, A14, A17, A22) changed, and for A11 work became fully concentrated on a single resource (User_115). The change is directly observed in assignment shares and concentration indices, and it is consistent across all eight items, far exceeding the chance expectation of 0.004 items for the resource family. A competing explanation that this is a random sampling artefact is unlikely given the magnitude and consistency, though the analysis cannot rule out that the change reflects a deliberate reassignment of staff or a change in the available resource pool. No control-flow or timing evidence was provided, so no other differences are inferred.
