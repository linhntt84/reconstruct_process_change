### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: in the earlier window, the path was A25 → A28 → A26 → (one of A27/A36/A37/A42) → A31; in the later window, the path is A25 → (A36/A37/A42) → A28 → A26 → A27 → A31. The activities A27, A36, A37, and A42 changed from being mutually exclusive alternatives to being executed together in every trace, and A28 moved from immediately after A25 to after the A36/A37/A42 group. This is directly shown by the occurrence increases, the redirection of A25's outgoing edges, the reversal of order between A28 and the A36/A37/A42 group, and the lag change. The number of qualifying items (48) far exceeds the 0.74 expected by chance, and the items are consistent across indicators.

Other differences: A26's outgoing behaviour narrowed from branching to A27/A36/A37/A42 to almost exclusively A27, which is part of the same reorganisation. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a re-labelling of activities or a different trace sampling, but the internal consistency of the relational and positional evidence makes this unlikely.
