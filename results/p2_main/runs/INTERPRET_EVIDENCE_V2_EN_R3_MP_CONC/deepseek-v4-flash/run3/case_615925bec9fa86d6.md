### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a branching structure with two alternative paths (one involving A22, the other involving the group A31/A32/A37/A38/A39) to a single sequential flow where A20 leads to the group, then A23, then A21, then A22, with the group always executed and occurring before A21 and A23. The group activities became mandatory and moved earlier, while A21, A22, and A23 moved later. A26 remains present but is no longer a direct successor of the group. The number of qualifying items (62) far exceeds the expected 0.67 by chance, and the OCC, DIR, POS, REL, and LAG indicators consistently reflect this shift. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the change is a reordering of existing activities rather than a replacement of paths, but the evidence strongly favours a structural change.
