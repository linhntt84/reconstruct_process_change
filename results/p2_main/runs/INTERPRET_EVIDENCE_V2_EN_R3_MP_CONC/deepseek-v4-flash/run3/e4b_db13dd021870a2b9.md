### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation for several activities (A01, A03, A10, A13, A14, A17, A22). In the later window, a different set of resources performs these activities, with previously common resources becoming less frequent and new resources becoming more frequent; for A10, the work became much more spread out across many resources. This is supported by seven resource-assignment items, far exceeding the 0.005 expected by chance for that family. The change is consistent across all seven activities, though the exact pattern varies (e.g., A10 shows a large increase in the "OTHER" share, while others show a moderate shift). A limitation is that resource identifiers are anonymous, so we cannot infer roles or reasons for the change.

Other differences: The lag between consecutive occurrences of A17 shifted from longer to shorter distances in the trace. This is a single control-flow item (1 observed vs 0.2 expected by chance) and is not sufficient to infer a structural change on its own.
