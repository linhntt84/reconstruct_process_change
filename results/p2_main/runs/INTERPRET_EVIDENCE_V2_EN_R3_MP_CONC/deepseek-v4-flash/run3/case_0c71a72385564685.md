### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20, and A25 appear in about 38%, 30%, and 35% of traces respectively, while in the earlier window they were essentially absent (0%, 0.5%, and 0%). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A41's outgoing transitions changed: in the earlier window about 82% went to A26; in the later window about 33% go to A19, 30% to A25, 24% to A20, and only about 1.5% to A26. [DIR004]
- A19 and A25, when they occur, almost always lead to A26 (about 95% and 90% of their outgoing transitions). [DIR002, DIR003]
- The lag between A41 and A26 changed: in the earlier window, A26 followed A41 within one step in about 83% of traces; in the later window, the most common lag is 2 steps (about 83%), with about 14% at 3 steps. [LAG001]
- Relational evidence shows that in the later window A41 precedes A19, A20, and A25 in about 38%, 28%, and 33% of traces respectively, and these activities precede A26 in similar shares. In the earlier window these pairs did not co-occur. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- A26's position shifted slightly later in the trace (mean from 0.37 to 0.40). [POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a set of new activities (A19, A20, A25) was inserted between A41 and A26, so that A41 now leads to one of these activities, which then leads to A26, instead of A41 directly leading to A26. This is directly shown by the appearance of these activities, the change in A41's outgoing distribution, the new outgoing edges from A19/A25 to A26, the lag shift from 1 to 2, and the relational order. The number of qualifying items (21) far exceeds the 0.52 expected by chance, and the items are consistent across indicators.

Other differences: A02's outgoing distribution also changed (DIR001), but this is not part of the main hypothesis. A26's position shift is consistent with the insertion.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that these activities existed in the earlier window but were too rare to be observed; however, their substantial presence in the later window and the coherent relational and lag evidence make this unlikely.
