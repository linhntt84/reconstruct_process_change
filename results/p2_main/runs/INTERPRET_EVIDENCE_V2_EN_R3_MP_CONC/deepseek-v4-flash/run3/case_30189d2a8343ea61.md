### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a set of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) was introduced between A43 and A28, so that A43 now leads to one of these activities, which then leads to A28, instead of A43 leading directly to A28. This is directly shown by the shift in A43's outgoing transitions, the uniform outgoing transitions of the new activities to A28, and the change in the A43–A28 lag from 1 to 2 steps. The new activities are alternatives to each other (they rarely co-occur) and appear in about 20% of traces each. [DIR007, DIR002–DIR006, LAG002, REL009–REL026]

Other differences: A06's participation increased and its position moved later, and A48 now often goes to A06 before A49. These may reflect a separate change in the path around A06/A48/A49. [OCC001, POS001, DIR008, LAG003]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities existed but were too rare to be observed in the earlier window; however, their consistent appearance in about 20% of traces and the clear structural shift in A43's outgoing transitions make this unlikely.
