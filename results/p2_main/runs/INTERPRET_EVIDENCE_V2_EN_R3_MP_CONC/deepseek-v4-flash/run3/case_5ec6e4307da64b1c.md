### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20, A25, A26, and A27 appear in about 17–23% of traces each, while they were completely absent in the earlier window. [OCC001–OCC005]
- These new activities are mutually exclusive alternatives: they rarely co-occur in the same trace (e.g., A19 and A20 co-occur in 0.5% of traces). [REL001, REL007, REL012]
- A29's outgoing transitions changed: before, A29 went to A30 in 98% of its transitions; after, it goes to A30 in 47.5% and to the new activities in about 51% combined (A19 12%, A20 9%, A25 12%, A26 9.5%, A27 8.5%). [DIR006]
- A30's outgoing transitions changed: before, A30 went to A31 in 95.5%; after, it goes to A31 in 51.2% and to the new activities in about 47% combined (A19 9.9%, A20 11.3%, A25 9.9%, A26 6.9%, A27 8.9%). [DIR007]
- The new activities lead to A30 or A31: for example, A19 goes to A30 in 52% and to A31 in 46% of its transitions. [DIR001]
- The lag between A29 and A30 increased: before, 98.5% of traces had lag 1; after, 50.2% have lag 2. [LAG001]
- The lag between A30 and A31 increased: before, 96.5% had lag 1; after, 45.8% have lag 2. [LAG003]
- The lag between A29 and A31 increased: before, 95% had lag 2; after, 94% have lag 3. [LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new set of alternative activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) was inserted into the main path between A29 and A31, either between A29 and A30 or between A30 and A31. In the earlier window, the path was almost always A29 → A30 → A31. In the later window, about half of the traces still follow this direct path, while the other half include one of the new activities, which appear as mutually exclusive alternatives and are placed either before or after A30. This is directly shown by the appearance of the new activities, the redistribution of successors from A29 and A30 toward them, the mixed order between the new activities and A30, and the increased lags between A29–A30, A30–A31, and A29–A31. The evidence count (35 items vs. 0.6 expected by chance) is far above chance, and the items are consistent across occurrence, direction, relation, and lag indicators. A competing explanation that these activities existed but were unobserved is unlikely given their complete absence in 200 earlier traces and the systematic lag increases. The exact placement (before or after A30) varies per trace, but the overall insertion is well supported.

Other differences: None beyond the described insertion and the corresponding routing shifts.
