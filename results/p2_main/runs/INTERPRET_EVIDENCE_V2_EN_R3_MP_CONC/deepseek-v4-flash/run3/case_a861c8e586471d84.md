### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20, and A25 essentially disappear: A19 and A25 drop from about 35% of traces to 0%, and A20 from about 30% to 0.5%. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- Their outgoing transitions vanish: A19 and A25 have no observed outgoing transitions in the later window; A20's only observed transition is to A30 in a single trace. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- Activity A41's outgoing transitions shift from mostly to A19, A20, and A25 (together about 94% before) to mostly to A26 (about 92% after). [DIR004]
- Co-occurrence of A14 with A19, A20, and A25 drops from about 30–35% to 0–0.5%; A14 now occurs alone in all traces. [REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Pairs involving A19, A20, and A25 with each other and with A24, A26, A31, and A41 change from co-occurring or alternating to one activity alone or neither. [REL007, REL009, REL014, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021]
- The lag between A41 and A26 changes: before, A41 was followed by A26 after two steps in about 94% of cases; after, A41 is immediately followed by A26 in about 93% of cases. [LAG002]
- The lag between A24 and A31 shifts from mostly two steps to mostly one step. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activities A19, A20, and A25 were removed from the process, and A41 now leads directly to A26, bypassing those activities. In the earlier window, A41 was followed by one of A19, A20, or A25 (about 94% of its outgoing transitions), which in turn led to A26; in the later window, A41 goes directly to A26 (about 92% of its outgoing transitions), and A19, A20, and A25 are essentially absent. This is directly shown by the disappearance of these activities, the change in A41's outgoing transitions, the loss of co-occurrence with A14, and the shortening of the A41–A26 lag from two steps to one. The large number of qualifying items (36 vs 0.61 expected by chance) rules out chance as a whole.

Other differences: The lag between A24 and A31 also shortens, suggesting a similar removal of an intermediate step between them, but this is not part of the main hypothesis. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window is a different process variant rather than a modification of the same process, but the consistent pattern across many indicators makes this unlikely.
