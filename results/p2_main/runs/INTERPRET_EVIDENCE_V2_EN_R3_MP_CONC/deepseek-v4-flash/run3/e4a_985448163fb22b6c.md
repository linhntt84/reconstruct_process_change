### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a block of activities A25, A26, A31, A32, and A33 is now repeated multiple times per case, with the activities interleaved or repeated rather than executed in a fixed order, and that A33 can now be followed by A25, forming a loop back into the block. This is directly supported by the increase in occurrence counts and recurrence, the change in A33's successor distribution, the appearance of interleaving in all pairs, and the spread of lags. A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the repetition reflects multiple sub-cases per trace rather than a true loop, but the interleaving and lag patterns are consistent with a loop.
