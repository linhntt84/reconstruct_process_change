### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a repeated loop of activities A30, A31 and A36 (each executed about 3.5 times per trace) to a single execution of each in a fixed order A30 → A31 → A36, and that after A36 the process now continues to A25 instead of returning to A30. This is directly supported by the drop in occurrences and recurrence of A30, A31 and A36, the shift in A36's successor from A30 to A25, and the change in the A30–A36 relation from interleaved/repeated to a fixed A-before-B order with a lag of 2 (consistent with A31 in between). The evidence is strong: 11 control-flow items were flagged against an expected 0.47 by chance, and the key items have full or near-full support in both windows.

Other differences: The positions of A30 and A36 became less variable (standard deviation decreased), but this is a minor effect and does not alter the main hypothesis. [POS001, POS002]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the change in A36's successor is independent of the loop removal, but the two changes are consistent with a single underlying modification. The evidence does not directly show A31's outgoing transitions, so the exact position of A31 in the sequence is inferred from the lag and the A30→A31 edge.
