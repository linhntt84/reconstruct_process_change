### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from having two alternative paths between A21 and A23 — one via A22 and one via a group of activities A27, A28 and A33 — to almost always taking the A22 path, with A27, A28 and A33 becoming nearly absent. In the earlier window, about half the traces went A21→A22→A23 and about half went A21→(A27/A28/A33)→A23; in the later window, about 99.5% of traces follow the A22 path. This is directly shown by the increase in A22 participation, the decrease in A27/A28/A33 participation, the shift in A21's outgoing transitions, the co-occurrence changes, and the lag change between A21 and A23. The number of qualifying items (34) far exceeds the 0.45 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact.

A limitation is that the later window contains only one trace with A27/A28/A33, so the exact routing after those activities is based on a single observation; however, their near-disappearance itself is supported by items with full support in both windows.

Other differences: The relative position of A23 shifted slightly earlier (mean from about 0.55 to 0.53 of trace length), consistent with the shorter path. [POS001] The order among A27, A28 and A33 was mixed before (no fixed order) and is now essentially unobservable. [REL012, REL013, REL014]
