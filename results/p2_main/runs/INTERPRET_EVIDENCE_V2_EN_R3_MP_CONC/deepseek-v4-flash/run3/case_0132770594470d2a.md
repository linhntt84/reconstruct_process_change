### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a specific hypothesis: a set of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) and A06 were largely removed from the process, so that the main flow now goes directly from A29 to A30 to A31, whereas earlier it branched through these activities.
