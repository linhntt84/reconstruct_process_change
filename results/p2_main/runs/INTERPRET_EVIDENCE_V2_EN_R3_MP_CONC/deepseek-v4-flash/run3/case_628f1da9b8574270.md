### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a fixed sequence in which a block of activities (A31, A32, A37, A38, A39) was mandatory and occurred between A20 and A23/A21, to a process in which A20 leads directly to A23, the block is optional (present in about 55% of traces), and when present it occurs later, after A21 and A23, and leads to A26 instead of A23. This is supported by the drop in participation of the block and of A22, the redirection of A20's successor from the block to A23, the appearance of A26 as the block's successor, and the reversal of order between the block and A21/A23. The number of qualifying items (72) is far above the 0.72 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A22 became optional (from 100% to about 45% participation) and is now often absent; when present, it still follows A21. A21 now sometimes routes to the block instead of to A22. The lag distances are too weakly supported to add detail. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the block is not a single unit but a set of activities that happen to change together; however, the relational evidence shows they still co-occur as a block when present.
