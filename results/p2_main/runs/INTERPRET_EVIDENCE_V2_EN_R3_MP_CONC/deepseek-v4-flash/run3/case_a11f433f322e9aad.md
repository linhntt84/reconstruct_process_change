### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, about half of the cases no longer execute A27 after A26; instead they execute a set of activities A32, A33 and A38 (in a flexible order) before A28. The earlier window had a fixed sequence A26→A27→A28 in all cases; the later window has that sequence in about half of the cases and A26→(A32/A33/A38)→A28 in the other half. This is supported by the drop in A27 participation, the appearance of A32/A33/A38 from zero, the shift in A26's outgoing edges, the co-occurrence of the new activities with each other and with A28, and the increased step distance between A26 and A28. The number of evidence items (27) far exceeds the 0.57 expected by chance.

Other differences: Minor new outgoing edges from A27 to A09, A25 and A34 (about 1–2% each) and from A26 to A23 (about 0.5%) appear in the later window; these are small and may be noise. [DIR002, DIR001]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window represents a different process variant that was simply absent in the earlier sample, rather than a modification of the same process. However, the consistency across indicators makes a change the more plausible interpretation.
