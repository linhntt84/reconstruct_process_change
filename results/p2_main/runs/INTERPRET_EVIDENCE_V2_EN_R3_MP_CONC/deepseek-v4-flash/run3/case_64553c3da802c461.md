### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, about half of the traces (49%) contained A22, and about half (51%) contained each of A27, A28, A33, A34, and A35. In the later window, A22 appears in about 99% of traces, while each of the other five activities appears in only about 1% (2 traces). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- The outgoing transitions of A21 changed: before, A21 led to A22 in 49% of its transitions and to A27, A28, A33, A34, A35 in about 7–12% each; after, A21 leads to A22 in 99% of transitions and to the others in under 1% each. [DIR001]
- The group activities (A27, A28, A33, A34, A35) were executed together in about 51% of traces before, and in about 1% after; they were also followed by A23 in about 51% of traces before, and in about 1% after. [REL013–REL017, REL018–REL027]
- A22 and A23 co-occurred in 49% of traces before and 99% after, always in that order. [REL007]
- A21 and A22 co-occurred in 49% before and 99% after, always A21 before A22. [REL001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from having two alternative branches after A21 – one leading to A22 and the other to a group of activities (A27, A28, A33, A34, A35) that were executed together before A23 – to almost always taking the A22 branch. In the later window, A22 is executed in nearly all traces, while the group activities are almost never executed. This is directly shown by the large increase in A22 participation and the corresponding decrease in the group activities' participation, the shift in A21's outgoing transitions, and the co-occurrence changes. The number of qualifying evidence items (62) far exceeds the 0.62 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact.

Other differences: The positions of A21 and A23 shifted slightly (A21 later, A23 earlier), but these are small and likely a consequence of the changed path composition. The internal order of the group activities was mixed before (both orders observed) and is now unobservable due to their near absence.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the group activities still exist but are executed extremely rarely in the sampled window; however, the near-total absence in 200 traces, combined with the consistent routing change, makes this unlikely. The after-window support for the group activities is only 2 traces, so their behaviour in the later window cannot be characterized further.
