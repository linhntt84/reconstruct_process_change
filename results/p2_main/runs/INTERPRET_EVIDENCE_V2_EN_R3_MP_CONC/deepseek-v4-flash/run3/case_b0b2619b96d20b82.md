### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the ordering and routing of activities A01, A04, and A06. In the earlier window, the typical path was A04 → A01 → A06, with A06 also leading to A05 or back to A04. In the later window, the path became A01 → A04 → A06, with A06 now leading to A01 or A05. This reverses the order of A01 and A04, makes A04 more central (its participation and co-occurrence with A06 increase), and reduces A01's frequency and its co-occurrence with A06. The directional evidence (DIR001–DIR003) directly shows the successor changes, and the relational and lag evidence (REL003, LAG001, LAG004) consistently reflects the new adjacency and order. A competing explanation that this is a rare variant is unlikely given that 16 control-flow items qualified when fewer than one was expected by chance, and the items are mutually consistent. A limitation is that some positional and lag items for A01 rest on only 40 traces in the later window, but the core routing changes are supported by larger samples (e.g., DIR002 and DIR003 with 181 traces each).
