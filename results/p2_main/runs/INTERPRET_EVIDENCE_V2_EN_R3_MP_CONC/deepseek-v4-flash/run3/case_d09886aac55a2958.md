### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A01, was introduced into the process in the later window. A01 was absent in all 200 earlier traces, appears in about half of the later traces, is placed early in the trace, and is connected to many existing activities (A03, A05, A06, A08, A09, A12, A15) through co-occurrence and successor relations. The screening count (25 control-flow items vs 0.43 expected by chance) far exceeds what a stable process would produce, and the pattern is consistent across OCC, DIR, POS, REL, and LAG indicators. The main limitation is that the exact role of A01 (e.g., whether it is a new mandatory step or an optional branch) cannot be determined from the evidence.

Other differences: Some routing changes among existing activities are not directly tied to A01, notably A16's shift toward A02 (43% vs 9%) and away from A05 (10% vs 34%), and A03's increased routing to A12 (17% vs 1%) and A02 (17% vs 6%). These could be independent adjustments or indirect consequences of A01's insertion.
