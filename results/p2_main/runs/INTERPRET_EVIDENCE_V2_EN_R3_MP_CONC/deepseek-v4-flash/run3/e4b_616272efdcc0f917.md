### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: a different set of resources now performs activities A01, A03, A11, A13, A14, A17 and A22, and A11 in particular became concentrated on a single resource (User_100). The resource family produced 8 evidence items against 0.006 expected by chance, so the pattern is far beyond what a stable process would typically produce, and the same direction of change appears consistently across all seven activities. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the resource changes reflect a staffing or availability change rather than a deliberate process modification; the data show who performed the work, not why.
