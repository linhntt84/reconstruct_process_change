### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change from a single flow in which activities A27, A36, A37, A42, A43 and A44 were always executed to a process in which they are executed in only about half the cases, while the core activities A15, A16, A25, A26, A28 and A31 remain present in all traces. The routing also changed: A25 now always leads to A28 instead of directly to those activities, and A26 now leads to those activities instead of to A27. A10 became less frequent. The large number of qualifying items (106 vs. about 1 expected by chance) rules out random sampling as an explanation. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window contains two distinct variants, one with and one without the A27/A36/A37/A42/A43/A44 group, rather than a single modified flow; the evidence does not directly show the path after A28 in the variant that skips that group.

Other differences: The order between A26 and the A36/A37/A42/A43/A44 group reversed, and A27 and A28/A31 became partially alternative rather than always co-occurring. These are consistent with the main hypothesis but are not needed to state it.
