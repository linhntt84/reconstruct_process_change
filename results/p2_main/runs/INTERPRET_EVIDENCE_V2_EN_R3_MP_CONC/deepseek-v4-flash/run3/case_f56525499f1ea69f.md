### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed from a mutually exclusive choice between two groups of activities — one group containing A33, A35 and A36, the other containing A37, A38 and A39 — to executing both groups sequentially, with the first group before the second, and the second group occurring later in the trace. This is directly shown by the co-occurrence changes (from 0% to ~98%), the appearance of A37/A38/A39 as successors of A33/A35/A36, the increased lags, and the opposite position shifts. The screening comparison (89 control-flow items vs 1 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation very unlikely.

Other differences: A04 decreased in frequency and its outgoing transitions became more distributed (A16 fell from 81% to 36%, with A15 and A48 appearing). A30's outgoing distribution also changed as described above. A06 and A10 increased slightly, but with small support. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is a change in case mix rather than a change in the process logic itself, but the consistency and volume of the relational and positional evidence make that less plausible.
