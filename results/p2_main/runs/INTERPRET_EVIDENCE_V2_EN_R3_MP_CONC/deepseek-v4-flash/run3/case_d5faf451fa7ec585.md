### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed from one that included activities A14, A15, and A20 (as alternatives or optional steps) to a simpler linear sequence A22 → A23 → A24, with those three activities no longer executed. The disappearance of A14, A15, and A20 is directly shown by their zero participation and zero outgoing transitions in the later window, and the routing among the remaining activities simplifies to a fixed chain (A22 always followed by A23, A23 always followed by A24). The number of evidence items (28) far exceeds the 0.39 expected by chance, and all items are consistent with this single structural change.

Other differences: The slight shift in position of A22 (later) and A24 (earlier) is a minor difference, but it is not part of the main hypothesis. The evidence cannot rule out that A14, A15, and A20 were moved to a part of the process not captured, but they are absent from all traces, so they are not executed.
