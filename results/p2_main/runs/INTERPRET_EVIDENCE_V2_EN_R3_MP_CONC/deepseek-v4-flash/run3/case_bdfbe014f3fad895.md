### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the process structure: A27, previously mandatory, becomes optional and is replaced in about half the traces by a block of activities A32, A33 and A38, which still lead to A28. In the later window, A26 often routes to A32/A33/A38 instead of A27, and A28 is still always present, reached either via A27 or via the new block. At the same time, A04 becomes more frequent and is inserted between A14 and A15 in many traces. These changes are directly shown by the participation drops, the shift in A26's outgoing transitions, the new co-occurrences of A32/A33/A38 with A28, and the positional and lag evidence. The large number of qualifying items (55 vs 0.73 expected by chance) makes it unlikely that these are random fluctuations.

Other differences: A06 nearly disappears (from about 19.5% to 4%). A15's outgoing to A16 increases slightly (from 91.7% to 97.2%). A44 and A46 show small increases in their dominant outgoing targets (A45 and A47 respectively).

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window contains a different mix of process variants rather than a single changed process, but the consistency of the relational and routing evidence makes this less plausible.
