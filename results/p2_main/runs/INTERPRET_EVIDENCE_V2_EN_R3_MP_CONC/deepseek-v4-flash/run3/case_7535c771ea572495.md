Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the process: the activities A01 and A03 were removed, A05 and A07 were added, and the ordering changed from a path A06→A01→A03 (with A02 also preceding A01/A03) to a path A02→A05→A06→A07. This is directly shown by the disappearance/appearance of activities, the changed successors of A02 and A06, the new co-occurrence and ordering relations, and the increased distance between A02 and A06.
