### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed by removing (or making extremely rare) activities A19, A20 and A25, and that the main flow now goes directly from A27 to A28 to A29 with A28 and A29 adjacent. The disappearance of these activities is directly shown by their occurrence drop and by the loss of co-occurrence with the main activities. The shortening of the lags between A27, A28 and A29 is consistent with the removal of intermediate steps. The routing changes of A27 and A28 (concentrating on A28 and A29) are part of the same change. A competing explanation that the later window simply samples a different variant is unlikely given the large number of qualifying items (27 vs 0.55 expected by chance) and the consistency across indicators. The DIR and POS items for A19, A20 and A25 in the later window are based on a single trace, so they are not used as evidence for their successors.

Other differences: None beyond the main hypothesis.
