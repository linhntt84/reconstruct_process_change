### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: activities A19, A20 and A25, which previously occurred before A26 and A27, now occur after them, and A45 now connects to A26 instead of to A19/A20/A25. This is directly shown by the reversal of the relative order in the REL items, the later positions of A19/A20/A25 in POS, and the changed successor distributions in DIR (A19/A20/A25 now lead to A28/A32, A45 leads to A26). The LAG items further show that A28 and A32 are now reached later relative to A27 and A28, consistent with the moved group appearing after them. The number of qualifying items (27) far exceeds the 0.63 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact. A limitation is that some LAG items (e.g., A20–A28, A20–A32, A25–A26) have very low support in one window, but the main reversal is supported by many items with full support.

Other differences: A27's successors changed from almost exclusively A28 (98%) to a mix of A20 (17.5%), A28 (51%), A25 (14.5%) and A19 (15.5%); A28's successors changed from A32 (94%) to A32 (48%) plus A25 (22%), A19 (14%) and A20 (15%). These are consistent with the same reordering, as the moved group now appears after A27/A28.
