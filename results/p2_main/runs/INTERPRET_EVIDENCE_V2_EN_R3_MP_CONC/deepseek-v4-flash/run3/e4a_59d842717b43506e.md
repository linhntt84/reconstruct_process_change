### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 appear in about 16–22% of traces (45, 42, 44, 33 and 35 out of 200 respectively), whereas they were completely absent in the earlier window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- These new activities are mutually exclusive: they never co-occur with each other; each trace contains at most one of them. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL007, REL008, REL012]
- In the earlier window, A24 always led to A25, and A25 always led to A26. In the later window, A24 leads to the new activities in about half of its transitions (combined share ≈ 0.52) and to A25 in the rest; A25 leads to the new activities in about half of its transitions (combined share ≈ 0.48) and to A26 in the rest. - The new activities themselves lead to A25 and A26 in roughly equal shares. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- The distance between A24 and A25 increased: before always one step apart, after about half the time two steps apart. Similarly A25–A26: before always one step, after about half two steps. A24–A26: before two steps, after three steps in almost all cases. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- The new activities appear around the middle of the trace (mean position ≈ 0.5). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]
- The elapsed time between A10 and A11 halved from about 1.0 hours to about 0.5 hours (median from 1.01 to 0.51 hours). [TWAIT001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a set of alternative activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) was introduced, appearing in about 20% of traces, inserted between A24 and A25/A26, and also reachable from A25, while the original A24→A25→A26 path still exists but is taken less often. The new activities are mutually exclusive, appear mid‑trace, and have outgoing edges to A25 and A26; the routing from A24 and A25 is redistributed accordingly. The number of control‑flow evidence items (34) far exceeds the chance expectation (0.44), and the pattern is consistent across occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators.

Other differences: The elapsed time between A10 and A11 halved, a separate timing change not linked to the structural modification.

Limitation: The new activities are observed in only about 20% of traces, so the exact routing shares are based on moderate support, but the overall pattern is consistent and unlikely to be due to chance.
