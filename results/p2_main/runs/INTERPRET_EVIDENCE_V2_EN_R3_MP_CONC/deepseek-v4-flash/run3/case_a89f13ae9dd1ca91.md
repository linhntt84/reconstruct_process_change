### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A21, A22 and A23 occurred in every trace (200/200) and always together, in a mixed order (about half A21 before A22, half after). In the later window, each of these activities appears in only 1 trace (0.5%), so they are effectively absent. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL025, REL026, REL031]
- In the earlier window, activities A14, A15 and A16 did not occur at all. In the later window, each occurs in about 30–37% of traces (60, 65 and 74 traces respectively), and they never occur together in the same trace, so they are alternatives. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL010]
- The routing from A39 changed: in the earlier window A39 was followed by A21, A22 or A23 (each about a third of the time); in the later window it is followed by A14, A15 or A16 (each about a third of the time). [DIR007]
- A14, A15 and A16 each lead to A24 in the later window, whereas before A21–A23 led to A24 (among other successors). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- A24 remains in all traces but its relative position moved earlier (mean from 0.43 to 0.39), while A39 moved later (mean from 0.31 to 0.33). The gap between A39 and A24 shrank from 4 steps to 2 steps. [POS007, POS008, LAG010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process replaced a mandatory block of activities A21, A22 and A23 with a set of alternative activities A14, A15 and A16. In the earlier window, every trace contained A21, A22 and A23 together, in a variable order, and A39 led to one of them. In the later window, these three activities are almost absent, while A14, A15 and A16 appear in about a third of traces each, never together, and A39 now leads to one of them; each of the new activities then leads to A24. This is directly shown by occurrence, routing and co-occurrence evidence. The screening comparison (71 control-flow items vs 0.64 expected by chance) makes it very unlikely that this is a random sampling artefact. A limitation is that the later window is a finite sample, but the near-total disappearance of A21–A23 and the appearance of A14–A16 with consistent routing make the replacement interpretation the most plausible.

Other differences: A24 and A39 shifted their relative positions (A24 earlier, A39 later), and the distance between A39 and A24 decreased from 4 to 2 steps. These are consistent with the structural change but are reported separately. [POS007, POS008, LAG010]
