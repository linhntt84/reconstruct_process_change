### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the order of a group of activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) relative to A23 and A24 reversed. In the earlier window these activities occurred before A23/A24; in the later window they occur after. Additionally, A42 now directly precedes A23 instead of preceding the group. This is supported by consistent successor changes (DIR), position shifts (POS), order reversals (REL) and lag changes (LAG). The number of qualifying items (28) far exceeds the 0.43 expected by chance, and the relational and positional evidence is based on all 200 traces in each window. A limitation is that the exact new sequence is not fully determined; for instance, whether A25 and A29 occur after the group or interleaved is not fully specified, and the DIR items for the group have moderate support (30–45 traces).

Other differences: A24 and A25 now have more varied successors, and A23, A24 and A25 shifted slightly earlier in position; these are consistent with the same reordering rather than independent changes.
