### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A25 led to A42 (about 35%), A36 (about 34%), and A37 (about 31%). In the later window, A25 leads to A28 in about 99% of its outgoing transitions. [DIR001]
- Activities A27, A36, A37, and A42 occurred in every trace before (participation 1.0, mean ~1.0). In the later window, each occurs in about half the traces (participation 0.47–0.54, mean ~0.48–0.55). [OCC001–OCC004]
- A26 before went almost exclusively to A27 (about 98%). In the later window, A26 goes to A27 (about 53%), A42 (17%), A37 (16%), and A36 (14%). [DIR002]
- A36, A37, and A42 as sources: before they routed to A28 and to each other; in the later window they route to A31 and to each other, and A28 is no longer a target from them. [DIR003–DIR005]
- Ordering: before, A26 and A28 followed A36/A37/A42 (B_before_A ~0.98–1.0). In the later window, when they co-occur, A26 and A28 precede A36/A37/A42 (A_before_B ~0.46–0.47). [REL006–REL008, REL014–REL016]
- A27 and A36/A37/A42 co-occurred in every trace before; in the later window they co-occur in about 1% of traces, becoming alternatives. [REL011–REL013]
- A25 and A28 were separated by 4–5 steps before; in the later window they are adjacent (lag 1) in about 99% of traces. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in the control-flow structure: A25 now routes almost exclusively to A28, whereas before it routed to A36, A37, and A42. The activities A36, A37, A42, and A27, which previously occurred in every trace, now occur in only about half of the traces. When they do occur, A26 and A28 now precede them, whereas before they followed them. This is consistent with a new direct path from A25 to A28 and the previous path through A36/A37/A42 becoming optional or alternative.

Other differences: A26 now also routes to A36/A37/A42 in addition to A27, and A36/A37/A42 now route to A31 instead of A28. These are consistent with the main change but not part of the core hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a shift in path frequencies rather than a structural change, but the directness of the routing and ordering changes makes this less likely.
