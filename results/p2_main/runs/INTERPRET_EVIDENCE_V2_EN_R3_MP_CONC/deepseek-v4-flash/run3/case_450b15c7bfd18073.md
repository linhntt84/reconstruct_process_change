### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that about half the traces now take a new branch in which A22 is skipped and replaced by three activities, A27, A28 and A33, executed in arbitrary order between A21 and A23, while the other half still follow the original A21→A22→A23 path. The screening count (26 items vs 0.4 expected by chance) is far above what an unchanged process would produce, and the OCC, DIR, REL, POS and LAG indicators all point to the same structural alternative. The evidence cannot rule out that the new branch represents a distinct case type rather than a routing choice within the same cases.
