Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activities A14, A15, and A20 were removed from the process, and that A36 now leads directly to A21, whereas before A36 led to one of A14, A15, or A20, which then led to A21. This is directly shown by the disappearance of these activities, the change in A36's outgoing transitions, and the reduction in the A36–A21 lag from two steps to one. The number of qualifying evidence items (28) far exceeds the 0.41 expected by chance, and the pattern is consistent across occurrence, direction, relation, position, and lag indicators. A competing explanation that these activities were simply not sampled in the later window is implausible given their substantial prior participation (30–35% of 200 traces) and the simultaneous structural change in A36's routing.

Other differences: Activity A19 remains present but no longer co-occurs with the removed activities; A21 and A36 show small positional shifts. These are consistent with the main hypothesis and do not require a separate explanation.
