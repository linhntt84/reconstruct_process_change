### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a structure where A26 could lead either to A27 or to a set of activities (A32, A33, A38, A39, A40) to a linear path A26 → A27 → A28, with the alternative activities almost never executed. This is supported by the large drop in participation of A32, A33, A38, A39 and A40, the rise of A27 to near-universal participation, the concentration of A26's outgoing transitions on A27, and the near-universal co-occurrence and order of A26→A27 and A27→A28. The later window contains very few observations of the alternative activities (3–5 traces), so their internal behaviour after the change cannot be described, but their near-absence is clear. A competing explanation is that the alternative activities still exist but are extremely rare; however, the change in frequency is still a change.

Other differences: A26's position shifts slightly later (mean from 0.46 to 0.49) and A28's slightly earlier (mean from 0.58 to 0.56), which is consistent with the new linear order. [POS001, POS002]
