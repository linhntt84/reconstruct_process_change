### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a major restructuring of the process: activities A02, A03, and A08 were removed, A07 and A14 became much less frequent, and the routing between the remaining activities changed substantially. Specifically, A01 now leads to A11 instead of A06/A07/A14, A10 now leads to A01 instead of A08, and A16 now leads to A06/A07/A14 instead of A01. This is directly shown by the disappearance of those activities, the changes in successor distributions, and the relational changes. The number of evidence items (129) far exceeds the 0.85 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A07 and A14 decreased but were not removed; A01 and A16 shifted positions (A01 earlier, A16 later); many lag distributions changed, indicating reordering of activities. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the exact new order of activities is not completely specified by the evidence, but the overall restructuring is strongly supported.
