### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38, and A39 each appeared in essentially every trace (about 100% participation, mean occurrences ~1.0). In the later window, each appeared in only about 47–56% of traces (mean occurrences ~0.47–0.56). [OCC001–OCC006]
- A30 and A31 remained present in all traces in both windows. A30's mean relative position shifted later (from about 0.62 to 0.67), while A31's shifted slightly earlier (from about 0.82 to 0.80). [POS001, POS002]
- The direct successors of A30 changed: before it went mainly to A33, A35, and A36 (about 34%, 36%, and 29% of its outgoing transitions); after it goes to A36, A35, A39, A37, A33, and A38 with roughly equal shares (13–20% each). [DIR001]
- A33, A35, and A36 now often have A31 as a successor (about 24–34% of their outgoing transitions), which was absent before. [DIR002–DIR004]
- Co-occurrence of A30 with A33–A39 dropped from 100% to about 47–56% of traces; in the remaining traces A30 appears without them. Similarly, co-occurrence of A31 with A33–A39 dropped from 100% to about 47–56%. [REL001–REL012]
- Among A33, A35, and A36, co-occurrence dropped from 100% to about 46%, with both activities absent in about 53% of traces. [REL013, REL014, REL018]
- Pairs like (A33,A37), (A33,A38), (A33,A39), (A35,A37), etc. dropped from 100% co-occurrence to about 2–3%, with each activity appearing alone in about 44–53% of traces. [REL015–REL017, REL019–REL024]
- The lag between A30 and A37/A38/A39, and between A31 and A33/A35/A36, changed from mostly 4–5 steps to mostly 1–3 steps. [LAG001–LAG004, LAG007, LAG010]
- The lag items involving pairs among A33–A39 (without A30/A31) have very low support in the later window (4–5 traces), so they are unreliable. [LAG005, LAG006, LAG008, LAG009, LAG011, LAG012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38, and A39, which previously occurred in every trace between A30 and A31, now occurs in only about half of the traces; when it does occur, these activities are placed closer to A30 and A31 (shorter lags), and A31 is now a direct successor of A33, A35, and A36. This is supported by the large drop in participation of these activities, the drop in co-occurrence with A30 and A31, the appearance of A31 as a successor, and the shortening of lags. The evidence is consistent and direct.

Other differences: A30's outgoing transitions now include A37, A38, and A39 directly, and A30's position shifted later; these are part of the same restructuring.

Limitations: The lag items for pairs among A33–A39 are based on very few traces in the later window, so the exact ordering within that block is uncertain. The change could be interpreted as these activities becoming optional or being replaced by a shorter path, but the evidence does not distinguish between those.
