### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that resource allocation changed for activities A01, A03, A17, and A22: in the later window, work is more concentrated on a few resources, especially User_3, and less spread across a long tail of many resources. This is directly shown by the consistent redistribution across four activities, with the share of the top resource increasing and the "OTHER" share decreasing. The number of resource items (4) far exceeds the expected by chance (0.006), and the pattern is consistent across all four activities. A competing explanation that this is just random variation is unlikely given the magnitude and consistency.

Other differences: Activities A13 and A14 are repeated less often per case: mean occurrences per trace decreased from 1.48 to 1.255, and the share of cases with multiple occurrences dropped from 34% to about 20%. This is a separate structural change in the frequency of these activities, supported by two items against an expected 0.21. It is not part of the resource allocation hypothesis.
