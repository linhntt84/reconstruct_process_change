### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a block of activities A33–A43, which previously occurred in only about half of the cases, became nearly always executed together in the later window, in a fixed order following A14/A15/A30/A31 and with A33–A38 preceding A39–A43. This is shown directly by the rise in participation of each of these activities to near-universal levels, by the rise in their pairwise co-occurrence to near-universal levels, and by the positional shifts that place them in a consistent sequence. [OCC005–OCC014, REL019–REL048, REL059–REL062, REL072–REL074, REL084–REL085, REL095, POS006–POS015]

Other differences: A08 became much less frequent, dropping from about 62% to about 20% of traces, and its dominant successor changed from A50 to a more varied set of successors. [OCC003, DIR004, REL001–REL004, REL017, REL018]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows contain different case populations, so the shift reflects a change in the mix of cases rather than a modification of the process itself.
