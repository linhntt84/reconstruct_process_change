### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed from a fixed sequential order to a more flexible ordering among activities A15, A17, A18, A19, A20, A21, A26, and A27. In the earlier window, each of these activities had a single successor and pairs had a fixed order; in the later window, multiple successors appear, many pairs occur in both orders, and the distances between activities increase. This is consistent with a relaxation of ordering constraints. The number of evidence items (45) far exceeds the 0.47 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be due to random variation. The position shifts are consistent with the reordering. A competing explanation is that the process now includes additional alternative paths rather than a simple reordering, but the co-occurrence of all pairs remains 100% in both windows, so the activities are still executed together; the change is in the order and spacing, not in participation.

Other differences: None beyond the ordering and spacing changes described.
