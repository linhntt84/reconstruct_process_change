### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from having two alternative paths after A36 — one leading to A14 and later to A21, the other leading directly to A21 — to always taking the A14 path and then A21. In the later window, A36 almost always goes to A14, and A21 always follows A14, so the direct A36-to-A21 path was eliminated. This is directly shown by the routing change at A36, the co-occurrence and order of A14 and A21, and the lag shift from 1 to 4 steps. The change is consistent across multiple indicators and far exceeds the number of items expected by chance (22 items vs 0.41 expected). A competing explanation that the before-window sample simply missed a rare variant is unlikely given the large sample sizes, but the evidence cannot rule out that the before-window process had additional unobserved variants.
