### Observed differences

- In the later window, activity A01 appears in every trace (200/200) and exactly once per trace, whereas it was absent from all earlier traces (0/200). [OCC001, POS001]
- In the earlier window, A05 and A07 each had about half of their outgoing transitions going to A03 (A05: 48% to A03, 52% to A07; A07: 52% to A03, 48% to A05). In the later window, A05 and A07 no longer go to A03; instead A05 goes to A01 in about 47% of cases (and to A07 in 53%), and A07 goes to A01 in about 53% (and to A05 in 47%). A01 always goes to A03. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- In the later window, A01 always occurs before A03, and A05 and A07 always occur before A01. In the earlier window, A03 occurred without A01, and A05 and A07 occurred without A01. [REL001, REL002, REL003]
- The distance between A05 and A03 increased: in the earlier window they were 1 or 2 steps apart (about 48% and 52%), in the later window 2 or 3 steps apart (about 47% and 53%). The same shift is seen for A07 and A03. [LAG001, LAG002]
- The relative position of A05 and A07 in the trace moved earlier (mean from about 0.70 to 0.58 for both), while A01 appears near the end (mean position about 0.83). [POS002, POS003, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity A01 was introduced and inserted between the existing activities A05/A07 and A03, replacing the direct transitions from A05 and A07 to A03. In the earlier window, A05 and A07 each led to A03 (and to each other) with roughly equal shares; in the later window, both lead to A01, which then always leads to A03. This is directly shown by the occurrence of A01, the successor changes, and the relational and lag evidence. The change is consistent across all control-flow indicators. A competing explanation that this is a rare variant is ruled out by the large number of items relative to chance expectation (12 items vs 0.06 expected). The main limitation is that we cannot infer the cause or intent of the change, only the structural modification.

Other differences: The positions of A05 and A07 shifted earlier, and the lag between them and A03 increased, which are consequences of the insertion of A01. No other independent differences.
