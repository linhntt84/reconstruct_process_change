### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 each had A28 as their almost exclusive successor (about 97–100% of outgoing transitions). In the later window, these activities instead go to A30 and A34 (e.g., A19: A30 56%, A34 38%; A20: A30 50%, A34 50%; A25: A34 57%, A30 38%; A26: A34 51%, A30 37%; A27: A30 55%, A34 40%). [DIR001–DIR005]
- In the earlier window, A29 went almost always to A30 (98.5%); in the later window it goes to A30 only 50% and to A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 (each 7–11%). [DIR006]
- In the earlier window, A30 went almost always to A34 (97.5%); in the later window it goes to A34 49% and to A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 (each 6–13%). [DIR007]
- In the earlier window, A47 went to many activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27 etc.); in the later window it goes to A28 in 91% of cases. [DIR008]
- The positions of A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 shifted later: their mean relative position increased from about 0.34–0.37 to about 0.58–0.63. [POS001–POS005]
- For each pair (A19,A28), (A19,A29), (A20,A28), (A20,A29), (A25,A28), (A25,A29), (A27,A28) and (A27,A29), the order reversed: before, the first activity always preceded A28/A29; after, A28/A29 precedes it in about 16–22% of all traces, while the first activity precedes A28/A29 in only 0–1%. [REL001–REL008]
- The lag between A29 and A30 increased: before A30 followed A29 immediately in 98.5% of traces; after, only 50.5% immediate, 48% with one activity in between. [LAG001]
- The lag between A29 and A34 increased: before A34 was two steps after A29 in 97%; after, three steps in 94%. [LAG002]
- The lag between A30 and A34 increased: before A34 immediately after A30 in 98.5%; after, immediate in 49% and one step in between in 50%. [LAG003]
- The lag between A47 and A28 decreased: before A28 was two steps after A47 in 90%; after, A28 immediately follows A47 in 91%. [LAG005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: a group of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) that previously occurred early and were followed by A28 now occur later, after A28/A29, and are followed by A30/A34 instead. This is directly shown by the reversal of order in the REL items and the later positions of these activities, and it is consistent with the successor changes (DIR) and lag changes (LAG). The screening count (28 items vs 0.64 expected by chance) is far above chance, so the overall pattern is not a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A29's successor set broadened from A30 to many activities, A30's successor set broadened from A34 to many activities, and A47's successor narrowed to A28. These are consistent with the same reordering, as A29 and A30 now sit between the moved group and A34.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a shift between existing variant paths rather than a new process structure, but the direct order reversal and the large number of consistent indicators make this unlikely.
