### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in which activity A10 is now executed in most cases and early, often immediately before A16 and other activities, while activities A19 and A20 are no longer always executed and are skipped in about half the cases. This is supported by the large increase in A10 participation and its earlier position, the drop in A19/A20 participation, and the relational and lag evidence showing A10 now directly precedes A16 and others. The number of qualifying items (91) far exceeds the 1.05 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact.

Other differences: A43's successor changed from A19 to A28 in about 45% of cases, and A15's successor changed from A16 to A10 in about 32% of cases. These may be part of the same reordering.

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that these are separate, independent changes rather than one single modification, but the consistency of the relational and positional evidence makes a single underlying change more plausible.
