### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 each occur on average about 1.0 times per trace instead of about 3.6 times, while still appearing in every trace; the share of traces with repeated occurrences dropped from about 70% to about 1–2%. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- In the later window, these five activities are no longer interleaved or repeated with each other; instead they appear in a fixed order. For example, A30 occurs before A31 in about 98% of traces (up from 30%), and A31 and A36 occur in both orders in about 50/50 of traces (up from about 12/18%) with interleaving nearly disappearing. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL007, REL012, REL016]
- The successor of A38 changed: in the earlier window about 71% of its outgoing transitions went to A30 and 28% to A25; in the later window about 96% go to A25 and 2% to A30. [DIR002]
- The successor of A06 became more concentrated: A45's share rose from about 42% to 82% of outgoing transitions. [DIR001]
- A44's successor to A06 increased from about 9% to 30%, while A45's share decreased from 84% to 69%. [DIR003]
- A06's position in the trace shifted later (mean from 0.66 to 0.81). [POS001]
- The positions of A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 became more concentrated (standard deviation roughly halved) and their upper quartile moved earlier. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- For pairs among A31, A36, A37, the lag between them shifted to 1 or 2 steps in the later window (e.g., A31–A36: lag 1 in 63% vs 8% before). [LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG009, LAG010]
- A02's occurrence decreased from about 14.5% of traces to 4.5%. [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a repeated, interleaved execution of a block of activities (A30, A31, A36, A37, A38) to a single execution of each of these activities in a fixed order, and that the routing after A38 changed from going back to A30 to going to A25. This is directly shown by the drop in mean occurrences and recurrence, the shift from interleaving to fixed ordering in the REL items, the change in A38's successor distribution, and the concentration of positions and lags. The main limitation is that some LAG items for pairs involving A30 or A38 have very low support in the later window (2–3 traces), so those specific lag changes are weak, but the OCC, REL and DIR items are based on all 200 traces and consistently point to the same structural change.

Other differences: A06's outgoing routing became more concentrated on A45, and A44's successor shifted partly from A45 to A06. A02's participation also decreased. These are not part of the main hypothesis but are consistent with a broader reorganisation of the process flow.
