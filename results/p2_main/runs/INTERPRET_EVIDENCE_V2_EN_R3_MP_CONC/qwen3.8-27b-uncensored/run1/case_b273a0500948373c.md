### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the hypothesis that, after A15, the process more often follows the A16-A18 alternative and less often follows the A19-A21 alternative. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Supporting signs are direct successor-share changes from A15, opposite participation changes for the two groups, and consistent co-occurrence changes with A15 and within the groups.
