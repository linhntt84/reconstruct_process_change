

### Observed differences

- The earlier window showed a near-universal A26-A27-A28 chain: A26 was followed by A27 in about 99 in 100 outgoing transitions, A27 was followed by A28 in about 99 in 100, A27 occurred in all traces, and A26-A28 separation was 2 steps in about 98 in 100 traces; in the later window, this chain remained in about half of traces but was no longer universal. [DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, LAG001, REL005]
- In the later window, A27 occurred in about 51 in 100 traces, while A32, A33, and A38 each occurred in about 49-50 in 100 traces; A26 and A28 still occurred in all traces, whereas A32, A33, and A38 were absent in the earlier window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL009]
- A26's successors changed from about 99 in 100 to A27 in the earlier window to about 50 in 100 to A27 and about 15-17 in 100 each to A32, A33, and A38 in the later window. [DIR001]
- A27 and A28 co-occurred in all earlier traces but only about 51 in 100 later traces, with A28 occurring without A27 in about 49 in 100 later traces. [REL005]
- A27 and the new activities behaved as alternatives: in the earlier window A27 occurred without each of A32, A33, and A38 in all traces, while in the later window A27 occurred without each in about 50-51 in 100 traces, each new activity occurred without A27 in about 49 in 100 traces, and co-occurrence with A27 was at most about 1 in 100. [REL006, REL007, REL008]
- A32, A33, and A38 were absent in the earlier window, but in the later window they co-occurred in about 49 in 100 traces and appeared in both orders in similar proportions, with no fixed order among them. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- In the later window, the new activities' outgoing transitions were mostly to the other new activities and to A28, with A28 receiving about 27-43 in 100 of each activity's observed outgoing transitions; no outgoing transitions for these activities were observed in the earlier window. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- The new activities were placed between A26 and A28: in the later window, A26 preceded each in about 49 in 100 traces, each preceded A28 in about 49 in 100 traces, and their event positions were around the middle of traces; in the earlier window they were absent. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL009, REL010, REL011, POS001, POS002, POS003, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A26-A28 separation changed from 2 steps in about 98 in 100 traces in the earlier window to 2 steps in about 50 in 100 traces and 4 steps in about 48 in 100 traces in the later window. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- Main hypothesis: The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A27 became optional and a new unordered block containing A32, A33, and A38 was added as an alternative between A26 and A28; the new block appears in about half of traces, while A27 appears in the other half. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, LAG001]
- Supporting signs: A27's participation fell from all traces to about half, while A32, A33, and A38 appeared in about half; A26's successors split between A27 and the new activities; A27 and the new activities are mutually exclusive; the new activities co-occur, are preceded by A26, and precede A28; and A26-A28 lag increased from 2 to 4 steps in about half of traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, LAG001]
- Other differences: A27's successor distribution changed only slightly, with A28 remaining dominant at about 96 in 100 and small shares to A09, A34, and A25 of about 1-2 in 100 in the later window. [DIR002]
- Competing explanation: The evidence cannot rule out that A27 and the new activities are alternative implementations of the same step, because their participation is complementary and the internal order of A32, A33, and A38 is not fixed.
