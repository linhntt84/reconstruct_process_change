### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- Main hypothesis: The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, in about 199 in 200 traces, the segment between A39 and A24 is executed as a single alternative among A14, A15 and A16, in place of the earlier segment in which A21, A22 and A23 were all executed as an unordered group; A39 and A24 remain present in all traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, REL001, REL002, REL010, REL025, REL026, REL031, POS007, POS008, LAG010]
- Supporting signs: A39's observed outgoing transitions shift from A21/A22/A23 to A14/A15/A16; A14/A15/A16 are mutually exclusive and each leads to A24; A21/A22/A23 are present in only 1 in 200 later traces; and the A39-A24 distance shortens from 4 steps to 2 steps in 199 in 200 later traces. [DIR007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL010, REL006, REL014, REL021, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, LAG010]
- Limitation: The evidence cannot rule out that A14/A15/A16 are renamed or re-coded versions of the earlier A21/A22/A23 group, or that the single later trace containing A21/A22/A23 is a rare residual variant in finite windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL025, REL026, REL031]
