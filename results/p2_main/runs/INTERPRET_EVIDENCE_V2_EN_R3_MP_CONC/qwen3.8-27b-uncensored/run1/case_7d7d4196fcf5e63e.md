

### Observed differences

- In the later window, A02 occurred in all 100 traces and about 1.8 times per trace, compared with about 49 in 100 traces and once per trace earlier; A08 occurred in about 45 in 100 traces and about 0.8 times per trace, compared with all 100 traces and twice per trace earlier. [OCC002, OCC005]
- A01, A06, and A07 became less frequent: A01 from about 49 to 31 in 100 traces, A06 from about 49 to 29 in 100 traces, and A07 from about 31 to 15 in 100 traces, with lower mean occurrences. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]
- A02 became the universal activity around which the others were observed: A02 occurred without A01 in about 70 in 100 traces, without A03 in about 55 in 100, without A06 in about 71 in 100, without A07 in about 85 in 100, and without A08 in about 55 in 100, while A08 occurred without A02 in about 52 in 100 traces earlier and not later. [OCC002, REL001, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- The outgoing transitions of A02 changed from always A01 to A04 in about 57 in 100 observed transitions and A03 in about 43 in 100; A08 changed from A03 in about 50 in 100 and A04 in about 50 in 100 to always A02; A06 changed from A02 in about 51 in 100 and A07 in about 49 in 100 to A08 in about 53 in 100 and A07 in about 47 in 100; A03 changed from always A06 to A01 in about 52 in 100 and A06 in about 48 in 100. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- A02 and A04 co-occurred in about 49 in 100 traces earlier and in all 100 traces later, with A02 before A04 increasing from about 26 in 100 traces to about 55 in 100 traces; A02 before A01 was observed in about 26 in 100 traces earlier and not observed later. [REL006, REL001]
- Event positions shifted: A02's median position moved from about 0.51 to 0 and its distribution became more spread out; A08's median position moved from about 0.10 to 0.50; A01's median position moved from about 0.57 to 0.33. [POS002, POS004, POS001]
- Several ordered-pair gaps changed, for example A02 followed by A04 shifted from a three-step gap to a one-step gap, and A08 followed by A02 shifted from a three/five-step gap to a one-step gap. [LAG011, LAG033]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the hypothesis that the control-flow structure changed so that A02 became a universal, early, repeated activity and A08 became a less frequent, later activity, with routing reorganized around A01, A02, A03, A04, A06, and A08; the differences are broad across occurrence, successor, co-occurrence, position, and lag indicators. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG011, LAG033]
- Supporting signs are that A02 went from about 49 in 100 traces to all 100 traces and from once to about 1.8 times per trace, A08 went from all 100 traces to about 45 in 100 traces and from twice to about 0.8 times per trace, A02's successors changed from A01 to A03/A04, A08's successors changed from A03/A04 to A02, A06's successors changed from A02/A07 to A08/A07, and A03's successors changed from A06 to A01/A06. [OCC002, OCC005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- The same reorganization is visible in co-occurrence and position evidence: A02 became universal and early, A08 became later and only observed with A02, and A02/A04 co-occurrence became universal with A02 before A04 in about 55 in 100 later traces. [REL001, REL006, REL009, POS002, POS004]
- A limitation is that the evidence is control-flow only, so it does not establish non-control-flow behaviour, and event positions are not durations. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001, LAG001]
- Other differences: A07 became less frequent and less co-occurring with A01, A02, and A08, and its median event position moved slightly later. [OCC004, REL003, REL008, REL013, POS003]
- An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that a new dominant trace variant or a different mix of trace variants produced the same reorganization, rather than a single structural modification.
