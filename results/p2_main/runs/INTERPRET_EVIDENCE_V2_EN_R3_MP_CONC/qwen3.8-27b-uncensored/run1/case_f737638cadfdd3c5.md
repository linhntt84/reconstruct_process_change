- A19, A20, and A25 are mostly alternatives rather than a sequence: in the later window, A19 and A20 co-occur in 0 in 100 traces, A20 and A25 in 0 in 100, and A19 and A25 in about 1 in 200. [REL001, REL007, REL002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, an optional alternative step was inserted between A41 and A26: after A41, one of A19, A20, or A25 is executed in about one third of traces, and then A26 follows; in the earlier window, A41 led directly to A26 in about 93 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and A19, A20, and A25 were not observed. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, REL003, REL006, REL008, REL010, REL011, REL014, LAG002]
- The signs are consistent: A41's routing shifts to the three activities, the three activities are mutually exclusive, they are followed mainly by A26, the A41-to-A26 lag increases from immediate to one or two intervening steps, and A26's median event position shifts slightly later. [DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG002, POS004]
- An important competing explanation is that A19, A20, and A25 were already part of the process but were not sampled in the earlier 200 traces; the evidence is control-flow only and cannot rule this out, although their complete absence in the earlier window and the coordinated routing, relational, and lag changes make a new optional branch more plausible. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, REL003, REL006, REL008, REL010, REL011, REL014, LAG002]

Other differences:
