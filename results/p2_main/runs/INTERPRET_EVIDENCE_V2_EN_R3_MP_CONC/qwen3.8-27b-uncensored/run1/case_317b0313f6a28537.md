### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A19, A20 and A25 were placed earlier in the process and now lead into A26, while A45 routes to them instead of directly to A26 and A27/A28 proceed directly to A28/A32. This is shown by the earlier positions of A19/A20/A25, their reversal from following to preceding A26/A27, their successors changing to A26, A45's successors changing to A19/A20/A25, and A27/A28 successors changing to A28/A32.
