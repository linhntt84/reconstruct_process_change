### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The main hypothesis is that the later window follows a different control-flow structure: A01 and A17 are not executed, A07 is executed in about 68 in 100 traces, A06 and A16 are executed once in all traces near the end, and A02 is repeated much more often in a block with A11, A12, A14 and A15, with A13 leading to A02 and A10 leading to A06/A16 [OCC001, OCC004, OCC008, OCC002, OCC003, OCC007, DIR002, DIR006, DIR009, DIR007, DIR010, REL045, REL022]

- This is supported by direct participation changes, replacement of successor profiles, positional shifts, and relational changes that all point to the same reorganisation rather than isolated rare variants [OCC001, OCC004, OCC008, DIR001, DIR012, DIR002, DIR006, DIR009, POS002, POS004, POS011, POS005, REL045, REL020, REL021, REL023, REL024, LAG004, LAG011]

- The qualified control-flow items are far more numerous than the chance expectation for the tested units, and the items are consistent across occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators [OCC001, OCC004, OCC008, DIR002, REL003, POS004, POS011, LAG004]

- A limitation is that the windows are finite samples, so zero participation means an activity was not observed in that window, and some successor shares are based on observed outgoing transitions rather than all traces [OCC001, OCC008, OCC004, DIR005, DIR011]

- Other differences: A04 and A10 have lower mean event positions in the later window, A09 has a higher mean event position, and A03's relation to A02 is replaced by a relation to A07 [POS003, POS007, POS006, DIR003, REL028]

- An important competing explanation is that the later window samples a different case population or a rare variant that was absent earlier, but the consistent disappearance, appearance, successor replacement and repetition change make a structural change the more direct explanation [OCC001, OCC004, OCC008, DIR002, REL003, REL045]
