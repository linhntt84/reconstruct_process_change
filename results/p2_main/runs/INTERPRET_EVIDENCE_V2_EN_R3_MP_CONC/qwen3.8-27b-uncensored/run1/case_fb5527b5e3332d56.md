

### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, about 52 in 100 of A21's observed outgoing transitions went to A22 and about 48 in 100 went to A27, A28, or A33; in the later window, about 99 in 100 went to A22 and about 1 in 200 went to A28. [DIR001]
- A22 was present in about 52 in 100 traces before and about 99 in 100 traces after, while A27, A28, and A33 were each present in about 49 in 100 traces before and about 1 in 200 traces after. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A27, A28, and A33 co-occurred in about 49 in 100 traces before and about 1 in 200 traces after; in the earlier window they appeared in mixed order before A23, while in the later window they are almost absent. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- A21 and A22 co-occurred in about 52 in 100 traces before and about 99 in 100 traces after, with A21 before A22; A22 and A23 co-occurred in about 52 in 100 traces before and about 99 in 100 traces after, with A22 before A23. [REL001, REL005]
- The lag between A21 and A23 was split between 2 steps and 4 steps in about half of traces each before, but was 2 steps in about 99 in 100 traces after; A23 remained present in all traces, and its mean position moved slightly earlier from about 55 in 100 to about 53 in 100 of the normalized trace position. [LAG001, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the process mostly follows the short path A21 -> A22 -> A23, whereas in the earlier window it split roughly evenly between that short path and a longer branch involving A27, A28, and A33 before A23. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL005, LAG001]
- Supporting signs are the near-complete shift of A21's outgoing transitions to A22, the rise of A22 participation and A21-A22 co-occurrence, the fall of A27, A28, and A33 participation and their co-occurrence with A21 and A23, and the concentration of the A21-A23 lag at 2 steps. [DIR001, OCC001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL009, REL010, REL011, LAG001]
- A main limitation is that the later long branch is observed in only one trace, so the evidence cannot rule out that A27, A28, and A33 were removed except for a residual trace or that the long branch became very rare. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- Other differences: the one later trace containing A27, A28, and A33 shows a fixed order A28 -> A27 -> A33, unlike the mixed order among these activities in the earlier window, but this is based on one trace. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL012, REL013, REL014, LAG007, LAG008]
