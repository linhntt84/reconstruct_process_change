

### Observed differences

- In the later window, A04 is much more common and earlier: it is present in about 60 in 100 traces instead of about 19 in 100, and its median position moves from about 0.55 to about 0.17. [OCC001, POS001]
- A14's outgoing transitions shift toward A04: A14 to A04 rises from about 4 in 100 to about 54 in 100, while A14 to A15 falls from about 87 in 100 to about 44 in 100. [DIR004]
- A04's outgoing transitions shift toward A15: A04 to A15 rises from about 20 in 100 to about 87 in 100; A04 co-occurs with A15 in about 60 in 100 traces instead of about 19 in 100, and A04 is before A15 in about 31 in 100 traces and interleaved with A15 in about 27 in 100, compared with about 3 and 6 before. [DIR001, REL002]
- The A14-A04 relation becomes common and immediate: A04 co-occurs with A14 in about 60 in 100 traces instead of about 19 in 100, A14 is before A04 in about 56 in 100 traces, and the A14-to-A04 lag is one step in about 96 in 100 observed pairs instead of about 57 in 100. [REL001, LAG001]
- The A14-to-A15 lag becomes less immediate: one-step lags fall from about 46 in 100 to about 24 in 100, while two-step lags rise from about 8 in 100 to about 31 in 100. [LAG002]
- A04 also becomes an earlier predecessor of later activities: A04 is before A26 in about 55 in 100 traces instead of about 8 in 100, before A28 in about 55 in 100 instead of about 10 in 100, and before A32, A33 or A38 in about 29 to 30 in 100 traces. [REL003, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]
- A27 is no longer universal: it appears in about 52 in 100 traces instead of 100 in 100, and A26 is before A27 in about 51 in 100 traces instead of about 99 in 100. [OCC003, REL017]
- A26's outgoing transitions now include A32, A33 and A38: A26 to A27 falls from about 95 in 100 to about 51 in 100, while A26 to A32, A33 and A38 are about 15, 18 and 17 in 100 respectively. [DIR006]
- A32, A33 and A38 are not observed in the earlier window and appear in about 50 in 100 traces in the later window; they co-occur with one another in about 49 in 100 traces and are executed without a fixed order, with both orders around 21 to 27 in 100. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL028, REL029, REL030]
- The new activities are placed after A26 and before A28: A26 is before A32, A33 and A38 in about 50, 49 and 48 in 100 traces, and A32, A33 and A38 are before A28 in about 49, 48 and 48 in 100 traces; A28 occurs without A27 in about 49 in 100 traces. [REL018, REL019, REL020, REL025, REL026, REL027, REL021]
- The A26-to-A28 lag shifts from mostly two steps to a mix of two and four steps: two-step lags fall from about 91 in 100 to about 50 in 100, while four-step lags rise from 0 in 100 to about 46 in 100. [LAG003]
- A27 and the new activities are mostly alternatives: A27 co-occurs with A32, A33 or A38 in at most about 2 in 100 traces, while A27-only and new-activity-only shares are about 50 in 100 and about 49 in 100 respectively. [REL022, REL023, REL024]
- Other smaller differences include A06 dropping from about 20 in 100 to about 4 in 100 traces with a changed successor profile, and A10's outgoing profile redistributing among many successors, but these have lower support. [OCC002, DIR002, DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- Main hypothesis: In the later window, the process more often follows a route A14 to A04 to A15, and after A26 it follows a route through A32, A33 and A38 to A28, with A27 bypassed in about half of traces. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL005, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030, POS001, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Supporting signs: A04's increased participation and earlier position, A14-to-A04 and A04-to-A15 routing, the immediate A14-to-A04 lag, A26's branching to A32/A33/A38, their co-occurrence without fixed order, their placement before A28, and A27's reduced participation and reduced co-occurrence with A26 and A28. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR004, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL025, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030, POS001, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Limitation: The finite windows and the zero participation of A32, A33 and A38 in the earlier window mean the evidence cannot rule out that these activities were already possible rare variants that became more common rather than newly introduced. [OCC001, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, REL001, LAG001]
- Other differences: A06 becomes much less frequent and its successor profile changes, and A10's successor profile redistributes, but these are not part of the main hypothesis and have lower support. [OCC002, DIR002, DIR003]
