### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The main hypothesis is that A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 changed from two alternative groups, each present in about half of traces, to a nearly universal sequential block after A30 and before A31: in the later window, A33/A35/A36 are executed together in mixed order, then A37/A38/A39 are executed together in mixed order, and A30 precedes the block while A31 follows it. The 109 qualified control-flow items, against about 1 expected without change, are consistent with this hypothesis rather than with a few chance items.
