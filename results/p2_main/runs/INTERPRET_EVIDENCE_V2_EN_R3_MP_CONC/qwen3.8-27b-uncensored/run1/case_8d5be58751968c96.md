### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the middle part between A30 and A31 is executed as one of two alternative subparts with mixed internal order: A33/A35/A36 in about 47 in 100 traces and A37/A38/A39 in about 56 in 100 traces, with only about 2-4 in 100 traces containing both groups, instead of both groups being present in about 100 in 100 traces in the earlier window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027]

- The main supporting signs are the direct relational change from co-occurrence to alternatives, the new A30 successors to A37/A38/A39, the new A33/A35/A36 successors to A31, and the shorter event lags that are consistent with bypassing the other group; the pattern appears across many control-flow items, not just one unusual indicator. [REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG007, LAG010]

- Other differences: A30's event position is later and A31's is earlier, and the event positions of A33/A35/A36 and A37/A38/A39 shift in the same direction as the alternative-path pattern, but these positional shifts are not by themselves the main structural hypothesis. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

- A limitation is that the windows are finite samples, so the exact branch shares are approximate and the evidence cannot rule out that the later window contains a different mix of cases that trigger an existing branch rather than a new structural rule.
