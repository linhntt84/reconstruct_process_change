

### Observed differences

- In the later window, A19, A20 and A25 occur in about 38, 30 and 35 in 100 traces, respectively, whereas in the earlier window A19 and A25 were absent and A20 occurred in only 1 in 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A41's outgoing transitions changed from a dominant A41→A26 path (about 82 in 100) to mostly A41→A19, A41→A25 and A41→A20 (about 33, 30 and 24 in 100), with A41→A26 reduced to about 1–2 in 100. [DIR004]
- A19, A20 and A25 are positioned before A26: A19, A20 and A25 are observed before A26 in about 38, 29 and 33 in 100 traces, and A41 is observed before them in about 38, 28 and 33 in 100 traces. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- A19 and A25, when they occur, are mostly followed immediately by A26 (about 95 and 90 in 100 outgoing transitions). [DIR002, DIR003]
- A19, A20 and A25 are rarely together: pairwise co-occurrence is about 1 in 100 traces, while each occurs in about 30–38 in 100 traces, so they behave as alternatives rather than a fixed sequence of all three. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- The A41–A26 gap increased: before, about 83 in 100 observed A41→A26 pairs were one step apart and about 18 in 100 were two steps apart; after, about 1 in 100 were one step apart, about 83 in 100 were two steps apart and about 14 in 100 were three steps apart. [LAG001]
- A26 remains in all traces, but its event position shifts slightly later (mean position from about 0.37 to 0.40; median from 0.36 to 0.39), while A19, A20 and A25 appear around position 0.37. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]
- A02's successor distribution also changes, with A26 falling from about 26 in 100 outgoing transitions to no observed outgoing transitions, and small shares appearing to A19, A20 and A25; the support is small (35 and 44 outgoing transitions), so this may be a related but separate routing shift. [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a new alternative segment is executed after A41 and before A26, with A19, A20 or A25 as the alternative activity. The supporting signs are the rise of A19, A20 and A25 from absent or rare to about 30–38 in 100 traces, the shift of A41's successors from A26 to these activities, the observed order A41 → one of A19/A20/A25 → A26, and the increased A41–A26 gap. A limitation is that the three activities are alternatives, not a fixed sequence, and the evidence does not show why the branch is selected. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]
- An important competing explanation is that A19, A20 and A25 were already possible but rarely selected before, or that A26 was delayed by other unobserved activities; the evidence cannot fully rule this out, although A19 and A25 were absent before and A41→A26 was immediate in most earlier traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, LAG001]
- Other differences: A02's routing also changes, with A26 no longer observed as a successor and small shares to A19, A20 and A25; because the support is small, this may be a related but separate routing shift. [DIR001]
