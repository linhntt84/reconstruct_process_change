

### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 each occurred in a substantial minority of traces (about 32, 28 and 40 in 100, respectively); in the later window, each occurred in only 1 in 200 traces, about 0.5 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the earlier window, A27 led to A28 in about 53 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions, to A25 in about 20, to A19 in about 13 and to A20 in about 10; in the later window, A27 led to A28 in about 97 in 100. [DIR004]
- In the earlier window, A28 led to A29 in about 45 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions, to A25 in about 19, to A19 in about 18 and to A20 in about 17; in the later window, A28 led to A29 in about 99 in 100. [DIR005]
- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 did not co-occur in the same trace, while in the later window pairs among them were almost always absent (about 99 in 100 traces contained neither activity). [REL001, REL002, REL006]
- In the earlier window, A27 was followed by A28 after one step in about 54 in 100 traces and after two steps in about 44 in 100; in the later window, A27 was followed by A28 after one step in about 99 in 100. [LAG001]
- In the earlier window, A28 was followed by A29 after one step in about 45 in 100 traces and after two steps in about 53 in 100; in the later window, A28 was followed by A29 after one step in about 98 in 100. [LAG003]
- In the earlier window, A27 was followed by A29 after three steps in about 94 in 100 traces; in the later window, A27 was followed by A29 after two steps in about 97 in 100. [LAG002]
- The single later occurrences of A19, A20 and A25 each had a different successor (A44, A08 and A42, respectively), unlike their earlier successors, but these later successor profiles are based on one trace each. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the hypothesis that the earlier branch involving A19, A20 and A25 became largely absent or bypassed in the later window, so that A27 proceeds directly to A28 and A28 proceeds directly to A29. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- The supporting signs are the drop in A19, A20 and A25 participation from about 28-40 in 100 traces to about 0.5 in 100, the shift of A27 and A28 outgoing transitions toward A28 and A29, and the shortening of the A27-A28-A29 sequence to immediate steps. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Other differences: the single later occurrences of A19, A20 and A25 had successors A44, A08 and A42, but these are not part of the main hypothesis because each is based on one trace. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- An important competing explanation is that the later window contains a rare variant or small-sample effect in which A19, A20 and A25 still occur, but the consistent disappearance of these activities and the direct A27-A28-A29 pattern make this less likely as the sole explanation. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003]
