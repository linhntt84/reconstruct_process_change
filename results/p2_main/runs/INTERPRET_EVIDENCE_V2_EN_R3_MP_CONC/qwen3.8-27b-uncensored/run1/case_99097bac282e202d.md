### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- Main hypothesis: The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the existing alternatives after A20 are taken with different frequencies: the A21-A22-A23 alternative is executed less often and the A24-A25-A26 alternative is executed more often; the control-flow family has 62 qualifying items, far above the 0.91 expected without change. [screening, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR003, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- Supporting signs: A20's outgoing shares shift from the A21-A22-A23 group to the A24-A25-A26 group, the group activities' trace participation shifts in opposite directions, and A20's co-occurrence and before-order relations shift consistently. [DIR003, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- Limitation: The evidence is control-flow only, so it does not show elapsed-time or resource behaviour; the step-distance changes are event positions rather than clock time, and many items reflect the same A20 branch shift, so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Other differences: A10 becomes more frequent and later, A32 routes more often to A10, A10 routes to A33 more often, and A10 is more often in traces with A24-A25-A26 while traces with A21-A22-A23 without A10 become less common; A02 becomes rarer with a weakly supported outgoing change. [OCC002, POS001, DIR004, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, OCC001, DIR001]
- Competing explanation: The evidence cannot rule out that the A10 and A02 changes are rare-variant sampling effects or that the two windows contain different case compositions, although the main A20 alternatives are common in both windows and the control-flow item count is far above the chance expectation. [screening, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC002, OCC001]
