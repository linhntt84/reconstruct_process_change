[OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL005, DIR001, LAG001]
- Limitations are that the evidence is limited to control flow, and the direct A36-to-A21 route was not observed in the earlier window, so the evidence supports a bypass or optional group rather than identifying a cause. [DIR001, LAG001, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
