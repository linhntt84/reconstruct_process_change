

### Observed differences

- A06 became much more frequent and earlier: it occurred in about 73 in 100 traces after, versus about 24 in 100 before, with mean occurrences per trace rising from about 1 in 4 to about 3 in 4, and its median event position moving from about 2 in 5 to about 1 in 5. [OCC001, POS001]
- A19, A20 and A25 became nearly universal: each occurred in about 99 in 100 traces after, versus about 47-48 in 100 before, with mean occurrences per trace rising from about 1 in 2 to about 1 per trace; they now occur together in about 99 in 100 traces in the order A19 before A25 before A20, whereas before they occurred together in only about 47 in 100 traces. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL018, REL019, REL022, DIR004, DIR005]
- A06 is now strongly coupled to the rest of the process: it co-occurs with A14, A15, A16, A19, A20, A25, A26 and A41 in about 73 in 100 traces after, versus about 13-24 in 100 before; it is before A19, A20, A25, A26 and A41 in about 65 in 100 traces, after A14 in about 69 in 100 traces, and immediately before A16 in about 97 in 100 of observed close A06-A16 pairs. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG002]
- A14, A15 and A16 now almost always co-occur with A19, A20 and A25: co-occurrence is about 99-100 in 100 after, versus about 47-48 in 100 before; A14 is before A19, A20 and A25 in about 96-98 in 100 traces, while A15 and A16 are before them in about 57-58 in 100 traces with a large interleaved/repeated share. [REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017]
- A06's outgoing transitions became concentrated on A16: about 88 in 100 after, versus about 8 in 100 before, when A06 also went to A15, A47, A45 and other activities. [DIR001]
- A15's outgoing transitions shifted from about 94 in 100 to A16 before to about 58 in 100 to A16 and about 40 in 100 to A06 after. [DIR003]
- A41's outgoing transitions shifted from about 45 in 100 to A26 and about 37 in 100 to A19 before to about 96 in 100 to A19 after. [DIR006]
- A08 became less frequent: it occurred in about 8 in 100 traces after, versus about 20 in 100 before. [OCC002]
- A26 remained in about 100 in 100 traces, but its median event position moved slightly later, from about 2 in 5 to slightly above 2 in 5. [POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A41 routes to A19 much more often and the A19-A25-A20 segment became nearly mandatory, while A06 became a frequent early activity associated with that segment: A41's outgoing transitions to A19 rose from about 37 in 100 to about 96 in 100, A19, A25 and A20 rose from about 1 in 2 to about all traces, and A06 rose from about 1 in 4 to about 3 in 4 traces and moved earlier. [DIR006, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC001, POS001, REL018, REL019, REL022, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005]
- Supporting signs are that A06 now co-occurs with A14, A15, A16, A19, A20, A25, A26 and A41 in about 73 in 100 traces, is before A19, A20, A25, A26 and A41 in about 65 in 100 traces, is immediately before A16 in about 97 in 100 of observed close A06-A16 pairs, and A15 now sends about 40 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A06. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG002, DIR003]
- A main limitation is that the evidence is control-flow only and cannot rule out that A06's increase and the near-mandatory A19-A25-A20 segment are separate changes rather than one newly dominant path; it also cannot identify a cause, and the windows are finite samples. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR006, REL004, REL018]
- Other differences: A08 decreased from about 1 in 5 to about 1 in 13 traces, and A26's median event position moved slightly later while A26 remained in about all traces. [OCC002, POS002]
