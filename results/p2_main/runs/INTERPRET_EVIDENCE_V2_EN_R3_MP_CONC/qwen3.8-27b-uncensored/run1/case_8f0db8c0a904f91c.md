

### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 were present in all 200 traces and were ordered as A41 before A19, A19 before A25, A25 before A20, and A20 before A26, A46 and A47; in the later window, each was present in about 53 in 100 traces, their mutual co-occurrence fell to about 53 in 100 with neither in about 47 in 100, and their co-occurrence with A26, A46 or A47 fell to about 53 in 100, with the other activity alone in about 47 in 100. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022]
- The successor distribution of A41 changed: before, about 96 in 100 observed A41 transitions went to A19; after, about 50 in 100 went to A19 and about 44 in 100 went to A26. [DIR005]
- The A41-to-A26 distance changed: before, about 93 in 100 observed A41-A26 pairs were four steps apart; after, about 44 in 100 were one step apart and about 49 in 100 were still four steps apart. [LAG002]
- A02 became rarer and less end-anchored: before it occurred in about 29 in 100 traces, with a median position near the end and about 94 in 100 A02-A47 pairs one step apart; after it occurred in about 8 in 100 traces, with a median position near the middle, and among the few observed A02-A47 pairs only about 20 in 100 were one step apart. [OCC001, POS001, LAG001]
- A02 also became less associated with the middle block: before A02 co-occurred with A19, A20 or A25 in about 29 in 100 traces, after in about 5 in 100, and about 44 in 100 traces contained neither A02 nor the other activity. [REL001, REL002, REL003]
- Among observed A02 transitions, before about 85 in 100 went to A47; after they were spread over A45, A12, A20 and other activities, with A47 only about 6 in 100. [DIR001]
- A04 and A06 became more frequent: A04 occurred in about 4 in 100 traces before and about 12 in 100 after; A06 occurred in about 4 in 100 before and about 13 in 100 after. [OCC002, OCC003]
- A46 became more directly followed by A47: before about 75 in 100 A46 transitions went to A47 and about 25 in 100 went to A02; after about 96 in 100 went to A47, and about 97 in 100 A46-A47 pairs were one step apart. [DIR006, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- Main hypothesis: In the later window, the middle block A19 -> A25 -> A20 was executed in only about half of traces, while A41 and A26 remained present in all traces and A41 sometimes went directly to A26. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, POS002, POS003, DIR005, LAG002, REL009, REL014]
- The supporting signs are the drop in participation of A19, A20 and A25 from all traces to about half, the fall in their co-occurrence with A26, A46 and A47 to about half, the A41-to-A26 successor share, and the shorter A41-to-A26 distance. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL010, REL012, REL013, REL015, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL021, REL022, DIR005, LAG002]
- The evidence cannot rule out that the same behaviour is due to a direct A41-to-A26 path rather than the middle block being made optional, because both would produce the same observed participation and distance changes. [DIR005, LAG002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- Other differences: A02 became rarer, earlier, less tied to A19/A20/A25 and A47, and its successors redistributed; A04 and A06 became more frequent; and A46 became more directly followed by A47. [OCC001, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL007, LAG001, DIR001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR006, LAG003]
