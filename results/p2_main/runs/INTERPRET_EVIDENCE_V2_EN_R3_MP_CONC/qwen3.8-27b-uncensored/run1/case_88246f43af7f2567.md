### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The main hypothesis is that the control flow around A03 changed: in the later window, A02 and A04 go directly to A12 instead of A03, A16's outgoing transitions include A03, and A03's outgoing transitions include A10 instead of A12, so A03 and A12 become alternatives. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Supporting signs are the direct A02/A04-to-A12 transitions, the A16-to-A03 transition, the A03-to-A10/A11 transitions, the loss of A03-A12 co-occurrence, the new A03-A10/A16 co-occurrence, the later event position of A03, and the immediate A02/A04-A12 pairs. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Limitations are that the evidence is control-flow only, some profiles rest on modest counts (A02 after 28 outgoing transitions, A04 after 35, A02-A12 after 28 pairs, A04-A12 after 35 pairs), and the data do not identify the cause. [DIR001, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002]
- A competing explanation is that the later window contains a different mix of cases, but the consistent successor, co-occurrence, and lag changes make a structural routing change more direct. [DIR001, DIR003, REL004, LAG001, LAG002]
- Other differences: A10's event position moved slightly later and A16's moved slightly earlier, while both appeared in about 30 in 100 traces after versus about 27 in 100 before. [POS002, POS004]
