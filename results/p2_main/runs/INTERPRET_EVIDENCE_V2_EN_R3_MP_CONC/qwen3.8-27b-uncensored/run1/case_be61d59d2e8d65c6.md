

### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20, and A25 were mostly alternatives and each occurred in about 29–41 in 100 traces; in the later window, each occurred in only about 1 in 200 traces, and A27, A28, and A29 appear without them in about 99–100 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- In the earlier window, A27’s outgoing transitions included A25 in about 20 in 100, A19 in about 13, and A20 in about 10, while A28’s included A25 in about 19, A19 in about 18, and A20 in about 16; in the later window, A27 went to A28 in about 98 in 100 observed outgoing transitions and A28 went to A29 in about 97 in 100. [DIR005, DIR006]
- The event-position gaps shortened: A27→A28 was immediate in about 52 in 100 earlier cases and about 99 in 100 later cases; A27→A29 was mostly 3–4 steps earlier and mostly 2 steps later; A28→A29 was mostly 2 steps earlier and mostly 1 step later. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG004]
- A27’s mean event position moved slightly later, from about 46 in 100 to 48 in 100, while A29’s moved slightly earlier, from about 57 in 100 to 55 in 100, with both remaining in all traces. [POS004, POS005]
- The rare later occurrences of A19, A20, and A25 point to different successors, A38, A37, and A31 respectively, but each successor profile is based on only one trace. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- Main hypothesis: The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the branch containing A19, A20, and A25 is bypassed or made rare, so A27 proceeds to A28 and A28 proceeds to A29 instead of routing through A19, A20, or A25. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, DIR006, POS004, POS005, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004]
- Supporting signs: The same change appears across participation, routing, co-occurrence, and lag indicators: A19, A20, and A25 become rare, A27 and A28 route to A28 and A29, and the A27–A28–A29 gaps shorten. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, DIR006, POS004, POS005, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004]
- Limitation: The residual later occurrences of A19, A20, and A25 and their successors A38, A37, and A31 are based on one trace each, so the exact replacement path for the rare branch is weak. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- Competing explanation: The evidence cannot rule out that A19, A20, and A25 became a rare optional variant rather than being structurally removed, but the consistent routing and lag changes make a bypass/rare-branch explanation more direct than random sampling. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004]
