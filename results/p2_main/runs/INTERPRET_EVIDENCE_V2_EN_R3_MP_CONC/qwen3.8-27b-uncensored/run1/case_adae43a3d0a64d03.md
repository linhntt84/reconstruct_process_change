### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- Main hypothesis: A05 was replaced by A01 as the activity at the same position, so A01 now appears where A05 appeared and A05 no longer appears. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, REL003]
- Supporting signs: A01 is absent before and present in all later traces; A05 is present in all earlier traces and absent later; both have the same event-position value; A01 precedes A03 as A05 did; A01 follows A02, A06, and A07 where A05 did; and the successor shares of A02, A06, and A07 shift from A05 to A01. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- Limitation: The evidence is control-flow only and uses anonymous activity codes, so it cannot rule out that A01 was added and A05 removed separately rather than as a direct replacement, nor any timing or resource change. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- Other differences: A02 and A07 remain partially present, with A02 in about 47 in 100 earlier traces and about 50 in 100 later traces, and A07 in about 53 in 100 earlier traces and about 50 in 100 later traces; these changes are small and are not part of the main replacement hypothesis.
