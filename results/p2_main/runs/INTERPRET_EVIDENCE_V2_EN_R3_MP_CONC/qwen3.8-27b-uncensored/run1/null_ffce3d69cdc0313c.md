

### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A31 was followed by A28 in about 64 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions and by A32 in about 36 in 100; in the later window, A31 was followed by A32 in about 62 in 100 and by A28 in about 38 in 100. [DIR001]
- In the earlier window, A32 was followed by A31 in about 64 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions and by A28 in about 36 in 100; in the later window, A32 was followed by A28 in about 62 in 100 and by A31 in about 38 in 100. [DIR002]
- Relative to A27, A31 was two steps away in about 64 in 100 of observed ordered pairs in the earlier window but one step away in about 62 in 100 in the later window, while A32 was one step away in about 64 in 100 earlier but two steps away in about 62 in 100 later. [LAG001, LAG002]
- Relative to A28, A31 was one step before A28 in about 64 in 100 of observed ordered pairs in the earlier window but two steps before A28 in about 62 in 100 in the later window, while A32 was two steps before A28 in about 64 in 100 earlier but one step before A28 in about 62 in 100 later. [LAG003, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The main hypothesis is that, between A27 and A28, in the later window the A31-before-A32 path was more common and the A32-before-A31 path was less common. In the earlier window, the more common pattern was A27 followed by A32 and then A31 before A28; in the later window, the more common pattern was A27 followed by A31 and then A32 before A28. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

The supporting signs are consistent: the successor shares of A31 and A32 shifted in the same direction, and A31 shifted from two steps after A27 to one step after A27 and from one step before A28 to two steps before A28, while A32 shifted from one step after A27 to two steps after A27 and from two steps before A28 to one step before A28. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

The affected profiles are based on about 90 observed outgoing transitions or ordered pairs in each window, so the shift is not driven by a rare variant. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

A limitation is that both A31->A32 and A32->A31 remain observed in both windows, so the evidence supports a shift in the relative frequency of these orders rather than proving that one order was completely removed. [DIR001, DIR002]
