### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A04, A06 and A10 became less frequent and their routing changed to involve A08: A04 leads to A08, and A06 and A10 partly lead to A08 instead of only to each other. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, REL008, REL011, REL014]
- Supporting signs are the drop in participation of A04/A06/A10, the new A08 successors, and the co-occurrence of A04/A06/A10 with A08 when present. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, REL008, REL011, REL014]
- A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A04/A06/A10 became a less frequent optional loop with A08 rather than a single moved branch, because the evidence shows changed successors and co-occurrence but not a complete path. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, REL008, REL011, REL014]
