### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A01 is a new mandatory late activity connected to the A15/A16 loop, and A02 becomes mandatory and is routed mainly to A06, making A06 a repeated activity in all traces. Supporting signs are A01's absence before and presence in all later traces near the end, transitions among A01, A15 and A16, A02's successor shift to A06, and A06's increased participation and repetition [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, REL001, REL008, REL009, REL012, LAG001, LAG003].
- The 58 qualifying control-flow items are far above the 0.53 expected without change, so the pattern is not what an unchanged process typically produces [screening].
- An important competing explanation is that A01's addition and the A02-to-A06 routing are separate structural changes rather than one structural change; the evidence shows both but does not identify a common cause [OCC001, DIR002].
- Other differences: A03 and A07 appear later relative to A02, A11 and A12 become more common, and A05 now sometimes leads to A01 [POS003, POS004, LAG001, LAG003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR003, REL014, REL015, REL025, REL026].
