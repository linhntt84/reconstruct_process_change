- A supported hypothesis is that, after A36, the route in which A14, A20 and A15 occur between A36 and A21 became almost universal, while the direct A36-to-A21 route became rare. [DIR001, LAG001, REL001, REL002, REL007, REL003, REL008, REL012, REL004, REL009, REL013, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
