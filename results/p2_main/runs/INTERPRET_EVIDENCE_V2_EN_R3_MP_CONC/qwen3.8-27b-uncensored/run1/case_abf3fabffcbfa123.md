### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the process more often follows a route in which A14 proceeds to A15 and A26 proceeds to A27 before A28, while A04 becomes less frequent and the A32/A33/A38 group becomes rare; in the earlier window, A14 more often proceeded to A04 and A26 more often proceeded to A32/A33/A38 before A28. [OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR004, DIR006, REL001, REL009, REL013, REL017, REL021, REL028, REL029, REL030, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Supporting signs: A27 rose from about 52 to 99 in 100 traces; A32/A33/A38 fell from about 48 to 49 in 100 traces to about 1 in 200; A14's outgoing share to A15 rose from about 36 to 82 in 100 while its share to A04 fell from about 63 to 10 in 100; A26's outgoing share to A27 rose from about 50 to 98 in 100; relational evidence shows A27 now co-occurs with A14, A15, A26 and A28, while A32/A33/A38 no longer co-occur with them; lag evidence shows A15 is more immediate after A14 and A28 is more often two steps after A26. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR004, DIR006, REL009, REL013, REL017, REL021, REL010, REL014, REL018, REL025, LAG002, LAG003]
- The later-window absence of A32/A33/A38 rests on one trace each, and the A02/A08 increases rest on 30 and 41 traces. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC001, OCC003]
- The evidence is limited to control-flow indicators. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001, LAG001]

Other differences:
