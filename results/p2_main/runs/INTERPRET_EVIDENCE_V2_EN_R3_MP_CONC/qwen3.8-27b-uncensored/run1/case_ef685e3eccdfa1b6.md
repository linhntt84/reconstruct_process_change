

### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A28, A30, A31, A32, A33 and A34 each occurred in all 200 traces; in the later window, A28, A30 and A31 occurred in about 47 in 100 traces, while A32, A33 and A34 occurred in about 56 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- A25 and A26 occurred in all traces in both windows; A25's mean event position moved from about 62 to 67 percent of the event sequence, and A26's moved from about 83 to 81 percent. [POS001, POS002]
- In the earlier window, A25's observed outgoing transitions were only to A28, A30 and A31; in the later window, A25's outgoing transitions were redistributed to all six activities, with A32, A33 and A34 receiving about 16, 18 and 20 in 100. [DIR001]
- In the earlier window, A28/A30/A31 pairwise co-occurred in all traces and A32/A33/A34 pairwise co-occurred in all traces; in the later window, A28/A30/A31 pairwise co-occurred in about 47 in 100 traces, A32/A33/A34 in about 56 in 100 traces, and the two groups co-occurred in only about 2 in 100 traces. [REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027]
- In the earlier window, A28, A30 and A31 preceded A32, A33 and A34 in essentially all traces where both groups occurred; in the later window, A25 is much closer to A32, A33 and A34, mostly 1 to 3 steps away, and A28, A30 and A31 are much closer to A26, mostly 1 to 3 steps away. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG008, LAG011, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024]
- In the later window, A28, A30 and A31 each had outgoing transitions to A26 in about 34, 35 and 26 in 100 of their observed outgoing transitions, while their outgoing transitions to A32, A33 and A34 became rare. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the hypothesis that A25 and A26 remain in all traces, but the activities between them changed from a mandatory sequence of the A28/A30/A31 group followed by the A32/A33/A34 group to an alternative in which the later window contains either the A28/A30/A31 group or the A32/A33/A34 group. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG008, LAG011]
- Supporting signs are the drop in participation of both groups, the near disappearance of co-occurrence between the groups, the new A25-to-A32/A33/A34 transitions, the new A28/A30/A31-to-A26 transitions, and the shorter event distances from A25 to A32/A33/A34 and from A28/A30/A31 to A26. - Other differences: A25's event position is later and A26's is earlier, and the internal order within A28/A30/A31 and within A32/A33/A34 remains mixed rather than becoming fixed. [POS001, POS002, REL013, REL014, REL018, REL025, REL026, REL027]
