

### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A25, A26 and A31 each occurred once in every trace and had no recurrence; in the later window, each occurred about 3.5 times per trace on average, still occurred in every trace, and recurrence support rose to about 68 in 100 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].
- For A31, the observed outgoing transitions changed from all going to A20 in the earlier window to about 71 in 100 going to A25 and about 29 in 100 going to A20 in the later window [DIR001].
- A25 and A31 were always co-occurring and always in the order A25 before A31 in the earlier window; in the later window they still co-occur in every trace, but only about 32 in 100 traces show A25 before A31, while about 68 in 100 show interleaved or repeated occurrences [REL001].
- The fixed two-step separation between A25 and A31 seen in all earlier traces is much less common later, with only about 12 in 100 traces showing that separation and about 5 in 100 showing a five-step separation [LAG001].
- The event positions of A25 and A31 became more dispersed and slightly later in the later window: A25's median position moved from about 0.40 to about 0.43 and its spread roughly doubled, while A31's median moved from about 0.47 to about 0.48 and its spread roughly doubled [POS001, POS002].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A25 and A31 became repeated and are now executed in an interleaved/repeated pattern, with A31 more often followed by A25 than by A20; A26 also became repeated in the same window [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001, LAG001].
- The main signs are the rise from one to about 3.5 occurrences per trace for all three activities, the shift of A31's successors from A20 to A25, and the replacement of a fixed A25-before-A31 relation by interleaved or repeated occurrences [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001, LAG001].
- A limitation is that A26's direct transitions and its relation to A25 and A31 are not separately evidenced, and the position changes are event-position changes rather than clock-time changes [POS001, POS002].
- A competing explanation not ruled out is that the later traces contain a new repeated sub-process or loop rather than a simple reordering of the same activities [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001].
- Other differences: the later-window position distributions for A25 and A31 are more spread, which is consistent with repeated occurrences but does not by itself identify a moved activity [POS001, POS002].
