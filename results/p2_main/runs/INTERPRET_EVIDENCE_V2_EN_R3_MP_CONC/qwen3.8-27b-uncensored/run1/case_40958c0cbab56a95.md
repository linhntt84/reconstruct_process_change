

### Observed differences

- In the later window, A26, A27 and A28 are almost absent: each appears in about 1 to 2 in 100 traces, compared with about 100 in 100 traces in the earlier window, where they were executed together with mixed order; their mutual co-occurrence falls from about 100 in 100 traces to about 1 in 200 traces. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL036, REL037, REL042]
- In the later window, A19, A20 and A21 become common as mostly mutually exclusive alternatives: A19 appears in about 30 in 100 traces, A20 in about 33 in 100, and A21 in about 38 in 100, while their co-occurrence is about 0 to 1 in 200 traces, so nearly every later trace contains one of them; earlier each appeared in about 1 to 2 in 100 traces. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL013, REL014, REL021]
- A44's observed successors shift from A26, A27 and A28 (about 28, 25 and 28 in 100 of its outgoing transitions earlier) to A19, A20 and A21 (about 25, 27 and 27 in 100 later), while A44 remains in all traces in both windows. [DIR007, POS008]
- The observed successors of A19, A20 and A21 become A29: in the later window A29 is about 95 in 100 of their outgoing transitions, whereas earlier, based on few observations, their successors were split among A11, A34, A39, A08, A47 and A36. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the earlier near-mandatory group A26, A27 and A28 between A44 and A29 is largely replaced by a single alternative activity, one of A19, A20 or A21, in nearly all later traces. Supporting signs are the disappearance of A26/A27/A28, the rise of A19/A20/A21 as mostly mutually exclusive alternatives, A44's successors shifting to A19/A20/A21, A19/A20/A21's successors becoming A29, and the shorter A44-to-A29 lag. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a different case composition rather than a direct replacement.
