### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- A supported possibility is that, in the later window, the alternative activities A14, A15 and A20 were removed from the route between A22 and A24, leaving a fixed adjacent sequence A22-A23-A24. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- The evidence is direct and consistent: one of the three alternatives was present in every earlier trace, none was present in the later window, and the remaining activities became directly adjacent. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Earlier, A22 and A23 were one step apart in about 55 in 100 cases and two steps apart in about 45 in 100 cases; A23 and A24 were one step apart in about 46 in 100 cases and two steps apart in about 55 in 100 cases; A22 and A24 were three steps apart in all earlier cases. In the later window, A22-A23, A23-A24 and A22-A24 were one, one and two steps apart in all cases. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- A limitation is that the evidence cannot rule out that A14, A15 and A20 were renamed, merged or replaced by unobserved activities; it only shows that they were not observed and that A22 was directly followed by A23 and A23 by A24. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005]
- Other differences: the small position shifts of A22 and A24 are consistent with a shorter route but are not independent structural evidence. [POS004, POS005]
