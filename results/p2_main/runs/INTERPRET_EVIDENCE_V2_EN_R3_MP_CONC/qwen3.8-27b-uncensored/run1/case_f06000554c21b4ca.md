

### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A25 almost always went to A27 (about 99 in 100 outgoing transitions); in the later window, A25 instead split roughly equally among A35, A41 and A36 (about 33 in 100 each), with A27 only about 1 in 200. [DIR001]
- In the earlier window, A26 led mainly to A41, A35 and A36 (about 37, 34 and 28 in 100 outgoing transitions); in the later window, A26 led to A30 in about 99 in 100 outgoing transitions. [DIR002]
- For A35, A36 and A41, A30 was a common next activity in the earlier window (about 28 to 37 in 100 outgoing transitions), while in the later window A27 became a common next activity (about 31 to 34 in 100) and A30 was only about 1 in 200. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- The order of A26 and A27 relative to A35, A36 and A41 reversed: in the earlier window, A26 and A27 usually came before A35/A36/A41 (about 99 to 100 in 100 co-occurring traces); in the later window, A35/A36/A41 usually came before A26 and A27 (about 98 to 100 in 100). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Event positions shifted in the same direction: A26 and A27 appear later in the later window (mean position about 62 and 47 in 100 of trace length, versus 53 and 38 in 100), while A35, A36 and A41 appear earlier (about 41 in 100, versus 58 to 59 in 100). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- Lags show the same reordering: A27 moved from one step after A25 to four or five steps after A25; A30 moved from four or five steps after A26 to one step after A26; A35 now appears within one to three steps after A25 in about 98 in 100 later traces; and A35 is followed by A27 within one to three steps in about 97 in 100 of the later traces that contain the pair. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG007]
- The earlier following of A35, A36 and A41 within one to three steps after A26 is largely absent in the later window, where those ordered pairs are observed in only one later trace each. [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]
- The earlier following of A30 within one to three steps after A35, A36 and A41 is also largely absent in the later window, where those ordered pairs are observed in only one or two later traces. [LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the possibility that the control-flow route was reorganized around A25, A26, A27, A30, A35, A36 and A41: in the later window, A25 enters the A35/A36/A41 group; the group is followed by A27 rather than A30; and A26 occurs after the group and then goes to A30. - The signs are consistent across successor shares, pair order, event positions and lags, and the 26 control-flow items are far above the 0.63 expected without change.
