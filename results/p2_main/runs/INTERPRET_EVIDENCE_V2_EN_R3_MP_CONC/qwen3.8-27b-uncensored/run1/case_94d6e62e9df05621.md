### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The main hypothesis is that A07 and A08 changed from being executed together, in either order, to being executed as alternatives that are not observed in the same trace, with both activities becoming less frequent. [REL001, OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002]
- The supporting signs are the direct relation change from co-occurrence in about 53 in 100 earlier traces to zero co-occurrence in the later window, the observed A07-to-A08 and A08-to-A07 successor shares falling to zero, and the reduced participation of both activities. [REL001, DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, OCC002]
- A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A07 and A08 became optional branches on separate paths, or that one or both were bypassed in many cases, rather than a single clean alternative choice; the evidence does not identify the exact structural mechanism. [REL001, OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002]
- Limitation: the later successor shares for A07 and A08 rest on only 18 and 17 observed outgoing transitions, so the exact successor mix is less certain than the co-occurrence change. [DIR001, DIR002]
- Other differences: A08's event positions shifted later in the later window, but a position shift alone does not show that A08 was moved. [POS001]
