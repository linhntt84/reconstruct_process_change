### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The main hypothesis is that, in the later window, A41 more often proceeds directly to A26, bypassing A19, A20 and A25, which were previously observed as intermediate activities after A41 and before A26. [DIR005, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL004, REL009, REL013]
- Supporting signs are the shift in A41's successors from A19/A20/A25 to A26, the disappearance of A19/A20/A25 from most traces, the immediate A41-to-A26 lag, and the loss of co-occurrence between A19/A20/A25 and A26. [DIR005, DIR002, DIR004, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL004, REL009, REL013]
- A limitation is that the finite windows cannot distinguish whether A19, A20 and A25 were removed, made conditional on unobserved attributes, or bypassed by A41, and the single later A19 trace is weak. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, DIR001]
- A competing explanation is that A19, A20 and A25 became optional or were removed from the process rather than A41's routing being changed; the evidence cannot rule this out. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005]
