

### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A02 occurred in all 200 traces, about 2.2 times per trace, and was repeated in about 59 in 100 traces; in the later window, A02 was not observed. [OCC001, POS001]
- In the earlier window, A03 was not observed; in the later window, A03 occurred once in all 200 traces, with a mean position of about two-thirds, similar to A02's earlier mean position of about two-thirds. [OCC002, POS001, POS002]
- A02 and A03 were mutually exclusive: before, all traces contained A02 without A03; after, all traces contained A03 without A02. [REL001]
- A05 led to A02 in all observed outgoing transitions before; after, A05 led to A03 in about 97 in 100 cases, with about 1 or 2 in 100 cases to each of A06 and A07. [DIR003]
- A06 led to A02 in all observed outgoing transitions before; after, A06 led to A07 in about 50 in 100 cases, to itself in about 48 in 100 cases, and to A03 in about 1 or 2 in 100 cases. [DIR004]
- A02's outgoing transitions before were split about 55 in 100 to A06 and about 45 in 100 to A07; A03's outgoing transitions after were split about 50 in 100 to A06 and about 50 in 100 to A07. [DIR001, DIR002]
- A05 and A07 were present in all traces in both windows, but their relation to the middle activity changed: before they co-occurred with A02, after they co-occurred with A03, with A05 before A03 and A03 before A07 in almost all cases. [POS003, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL007]
- A03 and A06 were not observed together before; after, they co-occurred in about 49 in 100 traces, with A03 before A06 in about 21 in 100 cases and interleaved or repeated in about 27 in 100 cases. [REL006]
- A06 was less frequent and occurred later in the later window: it occurred in about 59 in 100 traces before and about 49 in 100 traces after, and its mean position moved from about three-fifths to about three-quarters. [POS004]
- The gap between A06 and A07 became shorter: before, the most common gaps were 2 or 4 steps; after, about 35 in 100 cases had A06 immediately before A07. [LAG004]
- The gap between repeated A06 occurrences also became shorter: before, the most common gap was 2 steps; after, about 26 in 100 cases were immediate repeats. [LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 was replaced by a non-repeating A03 in the later window: A02 disappeared, A03 appeared once in all traces at a similar position, A05 now leads to A03 instead of A02, A06 no longer leads to A02, and A03 leads to A06 and A07 in a split similar to A02's earlier outgoing split. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL005, REL007]
- A key limitation is that A02 was repeated and was linked to A06 in a loop, while A03 is observed only once per trace; the evidence does not show that A03 reproduces A02's repetition behaviour, and it cannot rule out that A03 is a renamed or restructured version of A02 rather than a simple one-for-one replacement. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR004, REL003, REL006, LAG003, LAG004]
