### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- Main hypothesis: in the later window, A01 is a new step that appears in every trace, occurs late, is placed before A08, and is reached from A04 and A09, so the observed flow from A04 and A09 to A08 is no longer direct. [OCC001, POS001, REL004, DIR002, DIR003, LAG003, LAG007]
- Supporting signs: A01 is absent before and present in all later traces; A01 is before A08 in all later traces; A04 and A09 successors shift strongly to A01; A04 and A09 positions move earlier; and A04/A09 to A08 lags lengthen. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL004, DIR002, DIR003, LAG003, LAG007]
- Limitation: the evidence cannot rule out that A01 was already present but not logged in the earlier window, and it does not fix A01's exact order relative to A06 and A07 because those relations are mixed. [OCC001, REL002, REL003, DIR001]
- Other differences: A01 also leads to A07 and A06 in some later traces, and lags between A06/A07 and A08 become longer, but these are not needed for the main A01-before-A08 hypothesis. [DIR001, LAG004, LAG005]
