

### Observed differences

- In the later window, A20 is followed by A23 in about 99 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, while in the earlier window A20 was followed by A37, A31 and A32 in about 35, 34 and 31 in 100 respectively and A23 was not observed as a successor. [DIR001]
- The A20-A23 pair is one step apart in about 99 in 100 of the later-window pairs, whereas in the earlier window the same pair was four steps apart in all observed pairs. [LAG001]
- A31, A32 and A37 occur in about 47 in 100 traces in the later window, compared with all traces in the earlier window; A22 occurs in about 54 in 100 traces in the later window, compared with all traces in the earlier window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A21 leads to A22 in about 54 in 100 of its later-window outgoing transitions and to A31, A32 or A37 in about 14 to 17 in 100 each, while in the earlier window A21 led to A22 in all observed outgoing transitions. [DIR002]
- A22 and A31, A32 or A37 are almost mutually exclusive in the later window, co-occurring in about 1 in 200 traces, whereas in the earlier window they co-occurred in all traces. [REL011, REL012, REL013]
- A23 precedes A31, A32 and A37 in the traces where both occur in the later window (about 47 in 100 traces), while in the earlier window A31, A32 and A37 preceded A23 in all co-occurring traces. [REL014, REL015, REL016]
- A31, A32 and A37 appear later in traces in the later window (mean position about 0.58) than in the earlier window (about 0.39), while A23 appears earlier (about 0.39 versus 0.45). [POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008]
- A31, A32 and A37 no longer have A23 as a successor in the later window; their outgoing transitions are to A26 and to each other, whereas in the earlier window each had a substantial share to A23 (about 33 to 35 in 100). [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A20 almost always goes directly to A23, A31/A32/A37 are placed after A21 instead of before A23, and A22 is an alternative to A31/A32/A37 after A21. - The supporting signs are the near-total A20-to-A23 successor shift, the one-step A20-A23 lag, the reduced participation of A22 and A31/A32/A37, their later positions, A23's earlier position, their reversal of order relative to A23, and their mutual exclusivity with A22 after A21. [DIR001, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL011, REL012, REL013, DIR002, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]
- A competing explanation is that A22 and A31/A32/A37 simply became optional and were sampled in fewer traces, but the consistent successor, co-occurrence and position changes make a placement/routing change more direct than optionality alone. [DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, POS006, POS007, POS008]
- A31, A32 and A37 still co-occur in about 47 in 100 traces in the later window, with no fixed order among them, as in the earlier window, so the hypothesis does not claim a fixed order among them. [REL020, REL021, REL022]
- Other differences: A21 and A22 occur earlier in the later window (mean positions about 0.54 and 0.59 versus 0.60 and 0.63), A26 occurs slightly earlier (about 0.63 versus 0.66), and A20 occurs slightly later (about 0.36 versus 0.34). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS005]
