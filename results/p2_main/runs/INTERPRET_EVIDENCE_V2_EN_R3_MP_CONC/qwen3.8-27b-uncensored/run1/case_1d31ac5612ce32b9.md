

### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A02 occurred once in every trace, A02 always went to A04, A04 always went to A03, and A02, A04 and A03 co-occurred in a fixed A02-A04-A03 order with one-step gaps. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- In the later window, A02 occurs about 2.1 times per trace, with recurrence support of about 50 in 100, while about 53 in 100 of observed A02 outgoing transitions go to A04 and about 47 in 100 go to A03. [OCC001, DIR001]
- In the later window, A04 is observed in about 50 in 100 traces, all observed A04 outgoing transitions go to A02, and A02 and A04 are interleaved or repeated in the traces where they co-occur. [POS002, DIR002, REL002]
- In the later window, A02 and A03 still co-occur in every trace, but about 50 in 100 of traces show A02 before A03 and about 50 in 100 show interleaved or repeated A02/A03; A03 and A04 co-occur in about 50 in 100 traces, with A04 before A03 in about 22 in 100 traces and interleaved or repeated in about 28 in 100 traces. [REL001, REL003]
- The event-position distributions also changed: A02 became more variable and later on average, with mean about 0.59 versus 0.50 and median about 0.67 versus 0.50, while A04 became less frequent and earlier on average, with support about 0.50 versus 1.00 and mean about 0.56 versus 0.75. [POS001, POS002]
- The lag patterns changed from fixed one- or two-step gaps to more variable gaps: A02-to-A03 lags include one step in about 40 in 100, three steps in about 11 in 100, and five steps in about 5 in 100, and A04-to-A03 lags include two steps in about 46 in 100 and four steps in about 18 in 100. [LAG001, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The main hypothesis is that, in the later window, A02 became a repeated activity and A04 became an optional intermediate that returns to A02, so a trace may go directly from A02 to A03 or execute one or more A02-A04-A02 loops before A02-A03. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG003]
- This is supported by the earlier fixed A02-A04-A03 chain, the later increase in A02 occurrences, the split of A02 successors between A04 and A03, the absence of A04 in about half of traces, the return of A04 to A02, and the interleaved or repeated A02/A03 and A02/A04 relations. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG003]
- The evidence cannot rule out that the later window is a mixture of the old chain and a new loop variant, or that the number of A02-A04-A02 repetitions is unbounded; it does not identify a cause. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, REL002, REL003]

Other differences:
- The event-position distributions of A02 and A04 also shifted and became more variable, which is consistent with repeated A02 and optional A04 but is not required for the main control-flow hypothesis. [POS001, POS002]
