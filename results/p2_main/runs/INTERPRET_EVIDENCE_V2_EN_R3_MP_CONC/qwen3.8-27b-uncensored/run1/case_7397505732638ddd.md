### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The main hypothesis is that, in the later window, the fixed ordering among A15, A17, A18, A19, A20 and A21 was no longer maintained: these activities still occur together, but their relative order, successor choices and step distances are no longer fixed. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG006, LAG013, LAG017, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- The supporting signs are direct and consistent: successor choices are redistributed, previously fixed pairs now appear in both orders, fixed step distances become variable, and relative event positions shift in the direction expected from the new orderings. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG006, LAG013, LAG017, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- The evidence supports an order-change interpretation rather than disappearance of the activities because the listed pairs still co-occur in all traces in both windows, even though their order and distances change. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG006, LAG013, LAG017]
- A limitation is that the evidence is control-flow only and the windows are finite samples; it does not show why the order changed, and it cannot rule out that the later window mixes the earlier fixed path with one or more alternative paths rather than a uniform relaxation of the order.
