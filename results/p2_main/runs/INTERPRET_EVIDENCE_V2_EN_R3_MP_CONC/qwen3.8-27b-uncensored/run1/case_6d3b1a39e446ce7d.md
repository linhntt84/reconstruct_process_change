### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A36, A37 and A42 became a mandatory, unordered group after A25 and before A28, A26, A27 and A31, while A27 became mandatory and is after A26 and A28 in almost all traces [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, LAG001, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008].
- Participation and routing support this: A36, A37 and A42 rose from about half to almost all traces; A25's outgoing transitions moved from A28 to the trio; A26's outgoing transitions moved from the trio to A27; and the trio now leads to A28 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005].
- Co-occurrence, lag and position support this: the trio now co-occurs and is before A28, A26 and A31; A27 now co-occurs with the trio and is after A26 and A28; A25-A28 lag increased; and the trio's position moved earlier while A28, A26 and A27 moved later [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, LAG001, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008].
- The control-flow family has 48 qualifying items, far above the 0.74 expected without change, so the pattern is not what an unchanged process typically produces [screening].
- A competing explanation is that the later window contains a different case mix, but the evidence cannot rule this out; the same direction appears across participation, routing, co-occurrence, lag and position [screening, OCC001, DIR001, REL001, LAG001, POS006].
- The evidence does not directly show the order between A26 and A28, although A28 is positioned earlier on average, and A36, A37 and A42 remain unordered among themselves [POS002, POS004, REL020, REL021, REL022].
