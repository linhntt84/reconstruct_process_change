### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A36, A37, and A42 became an almost always executed early group placed before A28, A26, and A27, and A25 routes to this group instead of A28; in the earlier window, A27 was an alternative to A36/A37/A42 and A28 followed A25 directly. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, REL011, REL012, REL013, LAG001]
- The signs are consistent: A36/A37/A42 participation rose to almost all traces, A25 stopped routing directly to A28, A26 stopped routing to A36/A37/A42, A36/A37/A42 now precede A27/A28/A26, A28 is no longer one step after A25, and A36/A37/A42 are together without a fixed order among themselves. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS003, POS006, POS007, POS008, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL020, REL021, REL022, LAG001]
- A competing explanation is that the later window contains a different mix of cases that happen to include the A36/A37/A42 branch more often, but the evidence cannot separate case mix from a process-structure change. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013]
- The conclusion is limited to control-flow behaviour in 200 traces per window and does not establish causes outside activity participation, order, and placement. [OCC001, DIR001, REL011, LAG001]
