### Observed differences

- Activity A03 disappeared from the sample: in the earlier window it occurred exactly once in every trace (200 of 200 traces, participation 100%); in the later window it was not observed in any of the 200 traces. [OCC001, POS002]
- The hand-off from A02 to A03 vanished: in the earlier window every observed outgoing transition of A02 led to A03 (100% of A02's outgoing transitions); in the later window A02 was never observed to be followed by another activity. [DIR001]
- Accordingly, the pair A02–A03 changed from always being executed together in a fixed order (co-occurrence in 100% of traces, A02 before A03 in 100%) to A02 appearing alone (A02-only in 100% of traces, co-occurrence 0%). [REL001]
- A02 itself still appears in all traces in both windows, but its event position moved from a constant relative position of 0.8 to 1.0, the final position, in the later window — consistent with the trace now ending at A02. [POS001, DIR001]
- For context: 5 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.04 expected if nothing had changed (9 units tested), and each difference is larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, POS002, REL001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One possibility directly supported by the evidence is that the final step A03, which previously followed A02 in every case, is no longer executed: cases that previously ended A02 → A03 now end at A02. In the earlier window A03 ran once per case (200 of 200 traces), always directly after A02; in the later window A03 appears in none of the 200 traces, A02 has no observed successor, and A02 sits at the final trace position. All five qualifying items describe this one difference consistently, and five items is far more than the roughly 0.04 expected by chance for the 9 tested units. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, POS002, REL001]

Limitations: zero participation only establishes that A03 was not observed in the later window, so a rare residual execution of A03 that the 200-trace sample missed cannot be excluded, and the evidence cannot distinguish removing the step from it simply never being triggered. The five items all reflect the same trace-level change, so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, POS002]

Other differences: none — every qualifying item describes the same underlying change to A02 and A03. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, POS002, REL001]
