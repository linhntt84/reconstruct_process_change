### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening expected fewer than one qualifying item without a change, yet 74 control-flow items qualified, so the volume of signals cannot be explained by random re-splits of a stable process [OCC003, DIR005, REL016, POS003, LAG027]. The evidence directly and consistently supports the hypothesis that in the later window the group A19, A20, A25, A26, A27 runs early in the case — entered from A47 and leading to A28 — whereas in the earlier window it ran late, after A29 and A30, and led back to A30/A34; accordingly A47 now routes into the group instead of directly to A28, and A29 → A30 → A34 became a tight tail. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: reversed order relations at stable co-occurrence [REL015, REL016, REL018, REL020, REL023], successor changes at A47, A29, A30 and the five activities [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011], a uniform shift to earlier event positions [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006], and longer A47/A16-to-A28 distances consistent with the group now running in between [LAG027, LAG014]. Limitations: the evidence shows the group's old and new contexts but cannot establish that this is one relocation rather than a removal at the old position combined with an insertion at the new one, and a few gap claims rest on single observations [LAG013, LAG020].

Other differences: A08's participation collapsed from about 74 to about 24 in 100 traces and its adjacency to A16 loosened; this is a distinct participation change, and the evidence cannot show whether it is connected to the reordering [OCC003, REL001, DIR001, LAG001]. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the five activities now belong to a different, earlier-running route rather than having moved within one flow; the stable co-occurrence with A28/A29/A30 and the clean, unimodal position shift make a pure sampling artefact unlikely, but both readings of the rerouting remain open [REL016, REL020, POS002, POS005].
