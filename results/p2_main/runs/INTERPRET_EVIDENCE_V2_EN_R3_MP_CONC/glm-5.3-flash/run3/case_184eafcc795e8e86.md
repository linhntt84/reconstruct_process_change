### Observed differences

- Two activities that never appeared in the earlier window — A01 and A02 — are executed exactly once in every trace of the later window (200 of 200 traces, up from 0 of 200). [OCC001, OCC002]
- A01 is wired in between the A06/A13 stage and A04. Earlier, A06 and A13 each passed control directly to A04 in about half of their observed outgoing transitions (roughly 50 in 100, with the rest going to each other); in the later window neither leads to A04 any more — A06 hands over to A01 in about 48 in 100 transitions and to A13 otherwise, A13 hands over to A01 in about 52 in 100 and to A06 otherwise — while A01 leads to A04 in every observed case, and A06 and A13 precede A01 in all traces. A01 precedes A04 in about 49 in 100 traces, with the remainder recorded as interleaved or repeated. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, REL002, REL003, REL005]
- A02 is a new closing step. Earlier, A12 was the final event of every trace (position 1.0 throughout, no outgoing transitions observed); in the later window A12 leads to A02 in all traces, A02 is always the last event, and A01, A06, A12 and A13 all precede A02 in every trace. [DIR003, POS002, POS005, REL001, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- Execution distances shifted by roughly one step, as an insertion would produce: one-step A06→A04 and A13→A04 successions (about 21 and 20 in 100 earlier) disappear in favour of two-to-three step gaps, and A06→A12 and A13→A12 gaps move from two steps (about 33 and 30 in 100) to three or four steps; these pairs fall within five steps in about 123–127 of 200 later traces, down from 149. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]
- Existing activities sit slightly earlier in the traces: mean relative position falls for A06 (about 0.73 to 0.65), A13 (0.72 to 0.65), A04 (0.84 to 0.81) and A12 (1.0 to 0.94), while the new A01 appears around position 0.73 on average. [POS001, POS003, POS004, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, two steps were added to the flow: A01 is executed between the A06/A13 stage and A04 — A06 and A13 now reach A04 only via A01, to which they hand over in about half of their outgoing transitions and which always leads to A04 — and A02 is executed as a new final activity after A12, which previously ended every trace. The placement is shown directly by the successor changes and the before/after relation data, is consistent with the one-step shift in execution distances and the slightly earlier positions of A04, A06, A12 and A13, and the 25 flagged control-flow items far exceed the roughly 0.19 items expected by chance across the 39 tested units, so chance re-sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, LAG001, LAG002]

Competing explanations the evidence cannot rule out: the anonymous activity codes cannot distinguish genuinely new activities from renamed or split existing ones, and zero participation earlier strictly shows only that A01 and A02 were not observed before. In addition, many items describe the same traces (the occurrence, relation and distance items for the same insertions), so they are not independent confirmations, and the interleaved-or-repeated ordering between A01 and A04 in about half of the later traces is not resolved further. [OCC001, OCC002, REL002, REL006]
