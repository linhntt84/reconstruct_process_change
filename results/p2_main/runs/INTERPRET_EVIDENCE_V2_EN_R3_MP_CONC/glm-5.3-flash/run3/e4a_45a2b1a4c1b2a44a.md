### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A change in timing around A32 is supported: in the later window, the elapsed time from the completion of A25 to the completion of A32 dropped from a median of about 0.89 hours to about 0.43 hours, and the elapsed time from the completion of A34 to that of A32 dropped from about 0.12 hours to about 0.04 hours. The time family produced 2 qualifying items where only about 0.012 would be expected if nothing had changed — far above the chance expectation — and both items point in the same direction with high re-split strength, while the control-flow family produced no items (0 of 59 units against about 0.29 expected by chance) and the frequencies of both consecutive pairs were stable, which argues against a structural rewiring or a participation change as the explanation. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

Limitations: T_WAIT measures elapsed time between consecutive completion events, so the evidence cannot distinguish a shorter wait before A32 from faster completion of the predecessors or a different relative timing of A32 itself; and because both items share A32, they may reflect one underlying difference rather than two independent changes. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of cases reaching A32, which could shorten these intervals without any change to the process steps. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
