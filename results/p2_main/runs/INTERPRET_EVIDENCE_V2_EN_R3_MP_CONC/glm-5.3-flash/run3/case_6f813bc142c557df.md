- The group of activities A20, A22, A23, A24, A25 and A26 changed from variable ordering to a single fixed sequence. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering constraints among A20, A22, A23, A24, A25 and A26 changed: in the earlier window these activities were executed in variable orders, with A24/A25/A26 often preceding A20/A22/A23, a return path from A24 to A22, and exits to A32 from A23 as well as A25; in the later window they run as one fixed sequence entered from A31 — A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32 — with A32 reached only after A25. The change is shown directly by the order reversals between the two halves (shifts of about 0.22–0.87 in order shares), by each activity's outgoing transitions concentrating on a single successor, and by the fixed lags, with the position shifts agreeing in direction. Screening also shows the evidence as a whole exceeds chance: 58 control-flow items qualified where fewer than 1 was expected without a change. The main limitation is that the evidence cannot rule out that the earlier window mixed several fixed execution patterns and the later window is dominated by one of them — a shift in the mix of existing variants rather than a change in ordering constraints; POS and LAG also reflect event positions, not clock time. [REL002, REL005, REL007, REL009, DIR003, DIR005, DIR009, LAG003, LAG004, LAG011, POS002, POS005]

Other differences: A08 participates in far fewer traces, occurs later when it does, and redirects away from A15 toward A16, A46 and other successors, with A14 feeding it less often; A10 participates in more traces. These participation and routing changes are separate from the reordering hypothesis above. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, REL001]
