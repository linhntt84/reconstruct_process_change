### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A structural rework around A09, A01 and A02 is supported by the evidence: A09, which earlier ran in about 54 in 100 traces and led mainly to A03, is no longer observed, while the previously unobserved A01 and A02 now run in about 59 and 87 in 100 traces; A01 leads mainly to A03, A16 now hands over to A01 where it previously handed over to A09, and A02 runs before A12, so that A05 and A13 reach A12 through A02 rather than directly. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR006, DIR009, LAG001, LAG006, REL017]

This is shown directly rather than read off frequencies: presence and absence, successor changes, co-occurrence shifts and the one-step-to-two-step lag change all describe the same rewiring, and the 67 qualifying items are many times the fewer than one expected without change, so sampling variation cannot explain the ensemble. An important competing explanation remains: the windows are finite samples, so the evidence cannot distinguish a modification within one process from a shift in the mix of process variants between the windows (for example, traces of a variant containing A09 giving way to traces of variants containing A01 and A02); also, A09's absence in the later window means only that it was not observed there, not that it was removed from the process. [OCC003, DIR006, LAG001, LAG006]
