### Observed differences

- The clearest difference is a reversal at the branch point A20, which appears in essentially every trace in both windows. In the earlier window, A20 was followed by A24 in about 85 in 100 traces and by A29 in about 15; in the later window the shares reversed, with A29 following in about 82 in 100 traces and A24 in about 18. [DIR003, REL011, REL014, REL016, REL019]
- Participation moved with this reversal: A21, A22, A23, A24 and A25 each fell from roughly 85% to about 18–19% of traces, while A26, A27, A28, A29 and A30 each rose from about 15–16% to about 82%. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011]
- The two groups were and remain mutually exclusive alternatives: for example, A21 and A26 hardly ever occur in the same trace in either window; earlier, A21 ran without A26 in about 85 in 100 traces while A26 ran without A21 in about 15, and in the later window this inverted to about 18 versus about 81. [REL025, REL029, REL036, REL040]
- Activities that run alongside both groups changed partners: A34, present in nearly all traces in both windows, co-occurred with A21–A25 in about 85 in 100 traces earlier and now co-occurs with A26–A30 in about 82; A36 and A37 moved from co-occurring with A21–A25 in about 52 in 100 traces to co-occurring with A26–A30 in about 50. [REL030, REL041, REL075, REL031, REL032, REL076, REL077]
- The paths themselves look structurally stable: within-group orders are unchanged (A21 and A22 occur in both orders in similar proportions in both windows; A29 precedes A26 and A26 precedes A30 whenever those pairs occur), the average event positions of A21 and A24 shift only slightly (about 0.03–0.05 on the position scale), and repetition stays near zero — so the difference is how often each existing path is taken, not a move, a bypass or new repetition. [REL021, REL022, REL073, REL074, POS001, POS002, OCC002, OCC007]
- A separate change concerns A02: it appeared in about 5 in 100 traces earlier and about 24 in 100 in the later window, where it almost always leads immediately to A37 (about 82% of its outgoing transitions, at a one-step gap in all 41 observed pairs) and often follows A36 (about 25% of A36's outgoing transitions, up from about 1%). The earlier-window picture rests on only 10 traces containing A02. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR005, LAG001, LAG006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two existing alternative continuations after A20 swapped take-up rates: earlier, about 85 in 100 cases ran from A20 through A24 into the group A21–A25 (with A34, A36 and A37 accompanying that group), while about 15 in 100 continued through A29 into the group A26–A30; in the later window, about 82 in 100 cases take the A29/A26–A30 path and only about 18 take the A24/A21–A25 path. [DIR003, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011, REL011, REL016, REL025]

The support is direct — the successor shares at the branch point itself flipped — and consistent across occurrence, direction and relation items, all describing the same complementary swap. The shape of both paths is unchanged (mutual exclusivity, internal orders, positions and near-zero repetition are the same in both windows), which fits a shift in how often existing paths are taken rather than activities being added, removed, moved or repeated, and 120 qualifying items against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed cannot be explained by chance sampling. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window draws a different mix of cases (for example a different share of case types) from an otherwise unchanged process; the traces alone cannot separate this from a routing change. [DIR003, REL025, REL021, POS001, POS002, OCC002, OCC007, OCC001, DIR005, LAG001]

Other differences: A02 became more frequent (about 5 in 100 traces earlier, about 24 in 100 later) and in the later window typically sits immediately between A36 and A37, which now accompany the A26–A30 group; because only 10 earlier traces contain A02, this secondary change is indicative rather than established. A few lag redistributions (for example the A22-to-A36 and A22-to-A37 pairs occurring at later positions) rest on few later observations (20 and 8 pairs).
