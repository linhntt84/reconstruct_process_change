### Observed differences

- Activities A01 and A03 disappear in the later window: each occurred exactly once in every trace before (200 in 200 traces) and in no trace after. A03 was always the final activity of a trace, and A01 sat late in the trace (median position about two-thirds in) and always led into A03. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, DIR001, REL002]

- Two activities appear that were not observed before: A05 and A07 each now occur exactly once in every trace (200 in 200), after being absent from all 200 earlier traces. A07 always sits at the final position — the role A03 had — while A05 sits earlier (median position about one-third in). [OCC003, OCC004, POS003, POS005]

- The routing around A06 was rewired. Before, A06's outgoing transitions went 100% to A01; after, they go 100% to A07. A05, previously unobserved, now always leads to A06. A02's branch target changed: before, its outgoing transitions split about 53% to A06 and 47% to A04; after, about 50% to A04 and 50% to A05 — the A04 share is essentially unchanged, while the branch into A06 was replaced by a branch into A05. A02 itself continues to occur roughly twice per trace in both windows (about 375 vs 402 outgoing transitions over 200 traces). [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

- A06 now happens later in the trace: its median position moved from about one-third of the trace to about two-thirds (mean from about 0.50 to 0.75). [POS004]

- Pairwise relations follow the same pattern: A05, A06 and A07, which never appeared together before (A06 occurred without them; A05 and A07 were both absent), now always co-occur in the fixed order A05 before A06 before A07. A01 and A03, which always co-occurred with A01 first, now never do (both are gone). A02, which previously occurred without A05 and without A07, now always co-occurs with each of them, with A02 first in about 52 in 100 traces and an interleaved or repeated order in about 48 in 100. [REL002, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL012, REL013, REL014]

- The distance from A02 to A06 grew: before, the pair was most often one step apart (about 42 in 100 observed pairs); after, it is typically two steps (about 51 in 100) or four steps (about 16 in 100), consistent with A05 — or a longer A04 detour — now sitting between them. [LAG001, DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the closing segment of the process was replaced. In the earlier window, traces ran A02 → (A06 or A04), with A06 always continuing to A01 and A01 always closing into A03, the final activity. In the later window, A01 and A03 are no longer observed at all; A02 branches to A04 or A05, and the A05 branch always continues A05 → A06 → A07, with A07 now the final activity. On this reading, A01 and A03 dropped out of the observed behaviour, A05 and A07 entered it, and A06 was repositioned after A05 and now leads to A07 instead of A01. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL012, REL013, REL014, POS004, POS005]

The support is direct and consistent: 28 evidence items qualified against an expectation of about 0.16 items if nothing had changed (all tested units are control-flow), and the participation flips are complete (200 in 200 vs 0 in 200) rather than marginal. The occurrence, routing, ordering, position and distance indicators all describe one coherent restructuring, so they should be read as facets of a single change, not as independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, LAG001]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that each window captures one coherent process variant and the case population switched wholesale from the A01/A03 variant to the A05/A07 variant, rather than a single process having been modified; also, because the activity codes are anonymous, the evidence cannot show whether A05 and A07 are genuinely new steps or the former steps under new codes. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
