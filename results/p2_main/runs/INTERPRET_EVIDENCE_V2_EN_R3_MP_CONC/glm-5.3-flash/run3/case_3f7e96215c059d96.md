### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the later window replaces the previously mandatory two-stage sequence with an exclusive choice between two five-activity stages: stage one (A28, A29, A30, A32, A33) and stage two (A34, A35, A36, A37, A38) previously ran in every case, stage one always before stage two; in the later window each case runs exactly one stage — stage one in about 46 in 100 cases, stage two in about 54 in 100, both in about 1 in 200 — with A25 routing into either stage and stage one now leading directly to A26, which previously followed stage two. [OCC001, OCC006, REL025, REL021, DIR001, DIR002]

The screening produced 106 qualifying items where about 1 would be expected without a change, so the volume of flagged behaviour cannot be explained by random re-splits of a stable process [OCC001, REL025, DIR001]. The participation drop, the near-zero cross-stage co-occurrence, the all-or-nothing co-movement within each stage and the successor changes at A25, A28, A29 and A33 describe one coherent structural picture, and the counts check out exactly (92 + 109 − 1 = 200 traces, one stage per case) [OCC001, OCC006, REL021, REL025, DIR002]. The OCC, REL and DIR items reflect the same trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations, but they agree in direction and magnitude.

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a modified single process from a shift in the mix of two pre-existing case variants that were already mutually exclusive; the LAG gap distributions rest on a single later-window observation each and support nothing on their own; and the position shifts are consistent with shorter traces rather than direct evidence that any activity was relocated. [LAG001, POS001]
