### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the segment after A25 runs as a mandatory chain rather than an either-or choice: A25 feeds the group A36/A37/A42/A43/A44 (still without a fixed internal order), the group is followed by A28 and A26, and A27 — previously an alternative to the whole group, taken alone in about 48 in 100 traces and practically never together with it — now always executes after it. The activities involved are A25, A26, A27, A28, A36, A37, A42, A43 and A44. Supporting signs: participation of A27 and all five group members rose from about half of traces to 200 of 200; co-occurrence between A27 and each group member went from about 0 to 100 in 100; routing and order relations flip consistently (A26 → A27 from about 46 to 98 in 100 of outgoing transitions; A28 before A36 from about 51 to about 2 in 100); and the position distributions of A26, A27, A28 and the group shift accordingly. With 145 qualifying items against roughly 1.36 expected by chance across 273 tested units, the differences as a whole cannot be explained by random re-splits, although many items describe the same underlying restructuring rather than independent confirmations.
