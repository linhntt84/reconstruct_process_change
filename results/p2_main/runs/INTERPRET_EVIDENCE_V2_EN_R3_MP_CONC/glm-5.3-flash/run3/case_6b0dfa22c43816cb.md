### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening produced 45 qualifying control-flow items where fewer than 1 (0.83) would be expected without a change, so this pattern as a whole is not what a stable process typically yields. The evidence supports the hypothesis that the fixed order of the late segment was reorganised: earlier the flow ran A25 into the group A35/A36/A41, then A26 → A30 with A27 after the group; in the later window A25 feeds A27 directly and A26 feeds the group A35/A36/A41, which now precedes A30. This is shown directly by the complete reversal of the order between A26/A27 and each of A35, A36, A41 at unchanged co-occurrence (100 in 100 traces in both windows), by the successor changes at A25, A26, A35, A36 and A41, and by the gap changes (A25→A27 from four steps to one; A26→A30 from one step to four), with the position shifts agreeing; because all involved activities still occur in every trace, this is a change in ordering constraints rather than a participation or path-frequency shift. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, LAG002, LAG005, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

Other differences: A02's participation collapsed (about 75 to 21 in 100 traces) and the tail A49 → A02 → A50 became A49 → A50 directly; A06 and A10 participation roughly doubled on small supports, and A47 now leads less exclusively to A48. [OCC001, DIR011, LAG009, OCC002, OCC003, DIR010]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window mixes several behaviour variants rather than one modified flow; moreover, a few gap items behind the new chain rest on very few observations (for example the A26→A36 and A36→A30 pairs were observed only once in the earlier window), so the fine structure of the new sequence is less certain than the reversal itself.
