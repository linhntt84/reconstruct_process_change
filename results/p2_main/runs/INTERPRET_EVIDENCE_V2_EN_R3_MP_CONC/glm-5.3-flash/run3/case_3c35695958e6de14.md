### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added as a new step in the later window, inserted between the A02/A09 segment and the A03/A12 branch: A02 and A09, which previously led directly to A03 or A12, now lead exclusively to A01, which occurs in every trace (about twice per trace) and leads on to A03 or A12. The support is direct and consistent — A01 rises from 0 of 200 traces to 200 of 200, the outgoing transitions of A02, A09 and A01 change exactly at A01, A01's co-occurrence with A02/A03/A12 goes from never to always, and the A02→A03/A12 and A09→A03/A12 distances shift about one step later, as expected if A01 now sits between them. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL005, LAG001, LAG004, LAG011, LAG014]

The volume of flagged evidence far exceeds what an unchanged process produces: all 39 items qualified where about 0.31 would be expected by chance, and each difference exceeds those seen in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly means only that A01 was not observed then, so an A01 that existed at a very low rate before cannot be entirely excluded; and since all items are control-flow, the hypothesis covers structure only, leaving open whether A01 was inserted once on a shared path or separately after A02 and A09. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR004, LAG001]

Other differences: the rewired A08/A13 branch described above — A13 now always leading to A08, A08 leading to A01 in about half of its outgoing transitions, and changed A08/A13 self-repetition distances on small supports (5→35 and 5→19 observations) — is a further structural difference that the A01 hypothesis alone does not explain.
