### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a much larger share of cases runs through A10 early in the process: A10 is executed in about 64 in 100 cases (versus about 20 in 100), is often placed directly after A15 and almost always immediately before A16 (A15→A10 up from about 1 to about 32 in 100 of A15's outgoing transitions; A10→A16 up from about 7 to about 84 in 100), and it now precedes the always-present late activities A28, A43, A48 and A49 in most of its occurrences. Supporting signs: the occurrence, position, direction, lag and relation indicators all describe the same A10 behaviour, and the screening flagged 91 items where about 1 would be expected if nothing had changed, so sampling variation cannot explain the pattern as a whole. Limitations: the earlier-window lag profiles around A10 rest on only 9–13 observations, and the evidence cannot distinguish a newly inserted A10 step from an existing optional A10 branch that is now chosen far more often, since both the A15→A10 and A10→A16 edges already occurred weakly before; that re-routing interpretation is the main competing explanation. [OCC005, POS001, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG007, REL001, REL002, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011]
