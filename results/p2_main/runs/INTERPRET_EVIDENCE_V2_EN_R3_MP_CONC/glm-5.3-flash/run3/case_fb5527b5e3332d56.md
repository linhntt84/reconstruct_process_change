### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, cases split into two routes after A21: about 52 in 100 traces ran A21 → A22 → A23, while about 48 in 100 first passed through A27, A28 and A33 — each executed exactly once, in varying order — before reaching A23 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL012, REL013, REL014, LAG001].
- In the later window, more than 99 in 100 traces ran A21 → A22 → A23: A21's outgoing transitions led to A22 in about 99.5 in 100 cases, whereas before this held for about 52 in 100, with the rest split across A27, A28 and A33 [DIR001, REL001, REL005, LAG001].
- A27, A28 and A33 each fell from participation in about 48 in 100 traces to only 1 in 200 traces, while A22 rose from about 52 in 100 to more than 99 in 100; A23 was present in every trace in both windows [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS001].
- Before, A22 and each of A27, A28, A33 were strict alternatives that never shared a trace (roughly 52 vs 48 in 100); in the later window only A22 appears alongside A21 and A23, and the trio members no longer co-occur [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL012, REL013, REL014].
- The distance from A21 to A23 collapsed from a mix of 2 steps (about 52 in 100 traces) and 4 steps (about 48 in 100) to 2 steps in more than 99 in 100 traces [LAG001].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the detour through A27, A28 and A33 between A21 and A23 is skipped in nearly all cases, so execution runs almost uniformly A21 → A22 → A23, whereas in the earlier window about 48 in 100 cases inserted all three detour activities, once each and in varying order, between A21 and A23. The screening produced 34 qualifying items where roughly 0.45 would be expected without a change, and the occurrence, routing, co-occurrence and lag items all describe this same difference; the drop from 97 to 1 of 200 traces per detour activity is far beyond what these finite windows produce by random re-splitting. Limitations: the detour is not shown to be impossible — it still occurs in 1 later trace (there as A28 → A27 → A33 between A21 and A23) — and a shift in the mix of cases entering the process, rather than a change in routing, would look the same in these windows; moreover, many items restate the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations.

Other differences: A23's relative position moved slightly earlier in the later window (mean about 0.55 to about 0.53 of the trace), consistent with the shorter traces [POS001].
