### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window a step is executed between A24 and A26 that was not observed before: exactly one of five mutually exclusive activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) runs in essentially every trace (about 199 of 200), placed between A24 and A25 in about half of the traces and between A25 and A26 in the other half, whereas the earlier window showed the direct, consecutive chain A24 → A25 → A26 with none of the five present. The support is direct and consistent: participation rises from zero to about 17–23 in 100 traces per activity with zero co-occurrence among them; A24 and A25 reroute roughly half of their outgoing transitions to the new activities; relational items place every new activity after A24 and before A26; and the lag distributions show the one-step insertion before A25 (A24→A25 at lag 2 in about 52 of 100 traces) or after it (A25→A26 at lag 2 in about 48 of 100). The control-flow family produced 34 qualifying items against roughly 0.44 expected if nothing had changed, far beyond what sampling alone yields. Limitations: these items largely describe one underlying insertion rather than independent confirmations; zero participation in the earlier window strictly means "not observed," so a previously extremely rare execution of these activities cannot be fully excluded; and the evidence does not show what governs the choice among the five activities or between the two insertion positions.
