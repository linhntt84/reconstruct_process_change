### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the opening activity A01 and the mid-process activity A04 are no longer executed in the observed traces. Earlier, every trace began with A01 and about half of the traces continued through A04 to A02; in the later window, no trace contains A01 or A04, and A02 (about 47 in 100 traces, always followed by A06) and A03 (about 54 in 100) have taken over as the alternative first activities. The support is direct and consistent: participation of A01 and A04 fell from 100 and about 51 in 100 traces to zero, their outgoing transitions disappeared, A02 and A03 moved from positions after A01 to the first position, and every relational item shows A01 and A04 no longer co-occurring with the remaining activities. Screening also weighs against chance: 15 items qualified in the control-flow family against roughly 0.1 expected without a change, and every item ranks above about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]

An important competing explanation remains: a change in recording rather than in execution. If A01 and A04 events were no longer logged, the profiles would look the same, so the evidence shows only that these activities no longer appear in the traces, not that they were removed from the process itself. [OCC001, OCC002]

Other differences: A06 showed outgoing transitions in only about 7 in 100 earlier traces (mostly to A04) and none in the later window; given the very small support (14 traces before, 0 after), no inference is drawn about a change in A06's behaviour. [DIR004]
