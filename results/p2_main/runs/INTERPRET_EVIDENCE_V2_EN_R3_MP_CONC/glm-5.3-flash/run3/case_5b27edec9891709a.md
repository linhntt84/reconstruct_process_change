### Observed differences

- Before, A31, A32 and A37 each ran in every trace (200 of 200) as a block of three activities sitting after A20 and before A23; in the later window each appears in only about 47 in 100 traces (94 of 200). A22, previously in every trace, appears in about 54 in 100 (107 of 200). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A20's routing changed completely: before, its outgoing transitions split roughly evenly between A37 (~35 in 100), A31 (~34) and A32 (~31); in the later window about 99.5 in 100 go to A23, and the A20-to-A23 distance shrank from always 4 positions to 1 position in about 99.5 in 100 of later traces. [DIR001, LAG001]
- A21's routing changed: before it always led to A22; in the later window it leads to A22 in about 54 in 100 of observed transitions and to A31, A32 or A37 in the remaining ~46 in 100 (roughly 14–17 in 100 each). [DIR002]
- A22 and the A31/A32/A37 block, which co-occurred in every earlier trace (block before A22), are now almost mutually exclusive: they co-occur in about 1 in 200 later traces, with about 53 in 100 traces showing A22 alone and about 47 in 100 showing the block alone. [REL011, REL012, REL013]
- The block's order relative to its neighbours reversed: before, A31/A32/A37 ran before A23 and before A21 in every trace; in the later window A23 and A21 each run before the block in about 47 in 100 of the traces where they co-occur. The block still runs before A26 in both windows. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019]
- The block's exit changed: before, transitions out of A31, A32 and A37 went to one another and to A23; in the later window they go to one another and to A26, with A23 almost never following. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- The A21-to-A26 span reflects the new branch: before, A21 reached A26 at a distance of 2 positions (via A22) in every trace; in the later window the distance is 2 in about 54 in 100 traces and 4 in about 47 in 100, consistent with the three-activity block sitting between them. [LAG005, REL005, REL010]
- Inside the block, behaviour is unchanged: A31, A32 and A37 still occur only together and in no fixed order in both windows. [REL020, REL021, REL022]
- Event positions moved accordingly: A23's mean relative position moved earlier (about 0.45 to 0.39), A31/A32/A37 moved later (about 0.40 to 0.58) and A21 earlier (about 0.60 to 0.54), while A20 and A26 shifted only slightly. [POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS002, POS001, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a restructuring of the control flow around the block A31–A32–A37 and A22. In the earlier window, every trace ran the block between A20 and A23 (A20 → A31/A32/A37 in mixed order → A23), and A21 always led to A22 and on to A26. In the later window, A20 is followed directly by A23, and the block now runs between A21 and A26 as an alternative to A22: about 54 in 100 traces take A21 → A22 → A26, about 47 in 100 take A21 → A31/A32/A37 → A26, and the two branches almost never occur in the same trace. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG005, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Supporting signs: 47 evidence items qualified against about 0.51 expected by chance if nothing had changed — roughly 90 times that expectation — so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of a stable process produce. The redirection is shown directly by successor profiles (A20 now to A23, A21 split between A22 and the block, the block's exit moved from A23 to A26), by the reversed and now-exclusive relations, and by the distance changes; the key relations are observed in 94–200 traces per window. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, LAG001, LAG005]

Limitations: the few items describing distances from A20 or to A23 within the block branch in the later window rest on a single observation each and were not relied on; the evidence cannot show what determines which branch a case takes, and it cannot fully rule out that the later window mixes two coexisting behaviour variants, although only about 1 in 200 later traces still shows the old A20 → block → A23 ordering. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR001]
