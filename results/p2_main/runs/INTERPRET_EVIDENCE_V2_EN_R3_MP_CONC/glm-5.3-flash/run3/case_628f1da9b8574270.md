### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every one of the 200 traces contained all ten activities involved: A20, A21, A22, A23, A26 and the five activities A31, A32, A37, A38, A39 (participation 100%, one occurrence each). The behaviour was: A20, then the five in no fixed order, then A23, A21, A22 and A26 — A20's outgoing transitions went only to the five (about 17–24% each), the five led on to A23, A21 led only to A22, and A22 preceded A26. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the five-activity block A31, A32, A37, A38, A39 became skippable and now runs after A21 instead of between A20 and A23, with A22 executed only in traces without the block: in the later window about 55 in 100 traces run A20 → A23 → A21 → the five → A26 without A22, while about 45 in 100 traces skip the five and keep A22 (A20 → A23 → A21 → A22 → A26), whereas earlier every trace ran the five between A20 and A23 and included A22. The hypothesis rests on direct relational and routing evidence — successor changes at A20, A21 and the five, the reversed order between the five and A21/A23, and the near-exclusive co-occurrence of A22 and the five — with the participation drops and position shifts in agreement, and a pre-existing skip variant is ruled out because every activity appeared in all earlier traces. The screening produced 72 qualifying items against fewer than 1 (0.72) expected without a change, and each item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits, so the pattern as a whole is not what a stable process typically produces. The evidence cannot show whether the later window reflects one process with a genuine choice between the two paths or a mixture of two case populations, nor why A22 and the block exclude each other. Other differences: small mean-position shifts for A20 (about 0.32 → 0.35) and A26 (about 0.67 → 0.65) are consistent with the changed trace composition rather than separate changes; the ten LAG items each rest on a single after-window observation of the ordered pair and merely mirror the rare residual transitions, adding no independent support.
