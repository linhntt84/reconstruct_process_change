### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A25's outgoing transitions spread across five activities — roughly 23 in 100 to A41, about 20 to A43, and 18–19 each to A35, A36 and A42 — whereas in the later window about 96 in 100 of them go directly to A27 [DIR001].
- In the earlier window, A26 led almost exclusively to A30 (about 98 in 100 outgoing transitions); in the later window it leads to the same five activities A35, A36, A41, A42 and A43 (about 16–22 in 100 each) and almost never directly to A30 [DIR002].
- In the earlier window, each of A35, A36, A41, A42 and A43 passed on to A27 in roughly 16–21 in 100 of its outgoing transitions; in the later window these hand-offs to A27 have practically disappeared and about 18–22 in 100 go to A30 instead, while transitions among the five activities themselves continue in both windows [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007].
- The execution order between A26 (and A27) and those five activities reversed: earlier, each group activity ran before A26 in about 97–99 in 100 traces and before A27 in about 98–99 in 100; in the later window A26 precedes each of them in about 96–98 in 100 traces and A27 does so in about 97–98 in 100, with every pair co-occurring in all traces in both windows [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010].
- In step with this, the five activities occur later within the case in the later window (mean event position about 0.44 before versus about 0.60 after), while A27 (about 0.52 to 0.39) and A26 (about 0.65 to 0.52) occur earlier; these are event positions, not clock times [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007].
- Adjacencies changed accordingly: in the later window A27 immediately follows A25 in about 97 in 100 cases (199 traces, against 1 trace earlier), A26 is followed by the group activities within one to five steps in about 195 of 200 traces (1–2 earlier), and the group is followed by A30 within five steps in about 195–197 traces (1–2 earlier); the earlier adjacencies from A25 to the group and from the group to A27 drop to about 1–2 traces [LAG001, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG013, LAG015, LAG017, LAG019, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG011, LAG012, LAG014, LAG016, LAG018].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of five activities A35, A36, A41, A42 and A43 is executed at a different point in the case: in the earlier window it ran between A25 and A27 (A25 fed into it, it flowed into A27, and A26 came later and led to A30), while in the later window it runs between A26 and A30 (A26 feeds into it, it flows into A30, and A25 leads straight to A27). All involved activities occur in essentially every trace in both windows (co-occurrence 100 in 100; about 200 outgoing transitions per activity), so this is a change of placement and ordering constraints, not an activity becoming optional, repeated or removed [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL001, REL006, POS003, POS006].

The hypothesis is direct and consistent rather than a coincidence of weak signals: ten relation items reverse the order almost completely in both directions across 200 traces per window, five direction items show the matching successor rewiring at about 200 transitions each, and the lag items agree; overall 43 control-flow items qualified where fewer than 1 (0.8) would be expected if nothing had changed [REL001, REL010, DIR001, DIR007, LAG001, LAG007, screening].

Limitations: positions and lags are measured in event positions, so the evidence shows a different place in the case sequence, not a shift in clock time; the data cannot distinguish whether the five-activity block moved later or the A27–A26 segment moved earlier, since both descriptions fit the same rewiring; and several lag items are strongly supported on only one side (about 195–199 observations on one side, 1–2 on the other), although the order reversals themselves rest on about 200 traces per window. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows sample two pre-existing variants whose shares flipped, so that the later arrangement existed before but was rarely sampled [REL001, LAG002, LAG011].

Other differences: within the five-activity block some transition shares shifted modestly (for example A35 to A43 fell from about 23 to 18 in 100, A35 to A42 rose from about 19 to 22 in 100, A42 to A43 rose from about 13 to 25 in 100), and A36's position spread widened slightly; these are minor adjustments that do not change the overall picture [DIR003, DIR006, POS004].
