### Observed differences

- In the later window, A19 and A20 each occur in all 200 traces, about once per trace, whereas in the earlier window each ran in about 52 in 100 traces. The two already ran as a pair before (A19 then A20 whenever both appeared), but in the later window they co-occur in every trace, with A19 before A20 in about 99 in 100 traces; before, both were absent together in about 48 in 100 traces [OCC005, OCC006, REL021].
- The pair now always sits between A43 and A28: A43 precedes A19/A20 in about 99 in 100 later traces (before, A43 co-occurred with A19 in only about half the traces), and A19/A20 precede A28 in about 98 in 100, whereas before, A28 appeared without the pair in about 48 in 100 traces [REL025, REL026, REL030, REL031]. A28 no longer appears among A43's observed outgoing transitions (about 46 in 100 before), while A43's transitions to A19 rose from about 51 to 83 in 100, and the A43→A28 distance moved from often 1 step (about 46 in 100 of observed pairs) to always 4–5 steps [DIR007, LAG016]. A28's mean position shifted slightly later and A43's slightly earlier [POS003, POS004].
- A14, A15 and A16 occur in every trace in both windows; A19/A20, which previously followed A14 only in the half of traces where they appeared, now follow A14 in about 99 in 100 traces [REL011, REL012, POS002].
- A10 dropped from about 68 in 100 traces before to about 26 in 100 after, and its ordering loosened: A15's outgoing transitions to A10 fell from about 38 to 8 in 100 (mostly shifting to A16, up from about 62 to 86 in 100), A10's outgoing transitions to A16 fell from about 89 to 44 in 100, and the A10→A16 distance spread from almost always 1 step to 1–5 steps [OCC004, DIR003, DIR005, LAG001, POS001].
- Smaller shifts: A02, A06 and A08 rose from about 7 in 100 traces to about 19–22 in 100 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]; A25, A26 and A27 rose from about 20–34 in 100 to about 48–53 in 100 and, when present, typically run after A19 and before A20, while the observed pairs among them still rarely co-occur [OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL032, REL033].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One hypothesis supported by the evidence is that, in the later window, A19 and A20 became mandatory and are executed as a fixed A19 → A20 sequence between A43 and A28: before, cases branched after A43 either into A19 → A20 (about 52 in 100 traces) or on to A28 without them, whereas after, A43 is always followed by A19 then A20 before A28, and A28 no longer appears among A43's direct successors. Screening qualified 73 control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected without a change, so chance sampling cannot account for the ensemble, and the ordering claim rests on direct relational evidence (co-occurrence and precedence), not on position shifts alone [OCC005, OCC006, REL021, REL025, REL026, REL030, REL031, DIR007, LAG016]. Limitations: the many relation items largely re-describe the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot show whether the individual cases that previously skipped A19–A20 now pass through them, nor rule out that the case mix itself differed between the two windows.

Other differences: A10 is executed in about 26 in 100 later traces, down from about 68 in 100, with its links to A15 and A16 weakened and its relative position more variable; this decline is consistent with the restructuring above, but the evidence does not directly show A10 cases being rerouted into the A19–A20 sequence [OCC004, DIR003, DIR005, LAG001, POS001]. Participation also rose for A02, A06 and A08 (to about 19–22 in 100 traces) and for A25, A26 and A27 (to about 48–53 in 100), the latter typically running between A19 and A20 when they appear [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL022, REL024, REL029].
