### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the previously fixed sequence A20 → A31/A32/A37 → A23 → … → A21 → A22 → A26 became a branch after A21: in the later window a case runs either A22 followed by A26 (about 54 in 100 cases) or the block A31, A32, A37 in mixed order followed by A26 (about 47 in 100 cases), and the block now executes after A21 and A23 rather than before them. The support is direct and consistent: relational items show the order reversals between the block and A21/A23 and the near-perfect exclusivity between A22 and the block; A20 and A21 show the corresponding new and vanished outgoing transitions; participation of A22 and of the block drops from 100% to about 54% and about 47%; and 47 control-flow items qualified where fewer than 1 would be expected without change, all describing this one restructuring. The change is structural rather than a shift of shares between always-present paths, since both A22 and the block are (nearly) absent in part of the later window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016]

The evidence cannot show what determines which branch a case takes. The timing increase between A23 and A24 rests on a single qualifying item, the kind an unchanged process still produces occasionally at this screening strictness (1 item versus about 0.01 expected in the time family), so it remains tentative; a few lag items likewise rest on a single later-window observation and were not relied upon. Other differences: the elapsed time between the completions of A23 and A24 roughly doubled (about 1.0 → 1.9 hours); as a change in timing it stands on its own, and the evidence cannot establish whether it is connected to the structural change. [TWAIT001]
