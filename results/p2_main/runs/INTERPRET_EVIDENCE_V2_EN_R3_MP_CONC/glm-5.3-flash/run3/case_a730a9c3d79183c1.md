### Observed differences

- Two alternative activity groups became one sequential run. In the earlier window, A33, A35 and A36 each appeared in about 49 in 100 traces and A37, A38 and A39 in about 52 in 100, and the two groups almost never shared a trace (co-occurrence about 1 in 100 or less). In the later window, all six activities appear in about 98–99 in 100 traces, and the first group precedes the second in about 95–98 in 100 of them. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, REL039, REL041, REL045, REL047, REL050, REL052]
- Each group also became internally mandatory: A33 and A35 went from running together in about 49 in 100 traces (both absent in about 51 in 100) to running together in about 98–99 in 100; A37 and A38 went from about 52 together to about 99.5 in 100. Order inside each group stays mixed in both windows, with no fixed sequence. [REL037, REL044, REL055, REL056, REL059]
- Activities that already ran in every trace now hold fixed positions around the blocks: A14 and A30, which earlier preceded whichever single group ran (about 47–52 in 100 traces), now precede the first group in about 95–98 in 100; A31, which followed the running group, now follows the second group in about 95–98 in 100; A47 and A48, which earlier replaced the first group (exactly one of the two per trace), now run after it in about 97–98 in 100 traces. [REL013, REL018, REL025, REL030, REL031, REL036, REL042, REL043]
- Direct successions moved with the blocks: A30's outgoing transitions now go almost only to the first group (A35 about 36%, A33 about 31%, A36 about 29%, versus a spread over both groups before), and A33/A35/A36 now lead into the second group (A33 to A37/A38/A39 together about 35% of its transitions, from about 1%) instead of to A31 (from about 34% to under 1%). [DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008]
- Distances match this: gaps from A30 to A37/A38/A39 and from A33/A35/A36 to A31 moved from mostly 1–3 steps to 4–5 steps; A30→A31 within five steps fell from 199 to 5 observations and A33→A47 from 26 to 1, while A33→A37 and A36→A37 rose from 1 observation each to about 195. [LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG011, LAG013, LAG004, LAG010, LAG009, LAG014]
- A04 receded: from about 59 in 100 traces to about 26 in 100; when present it sits later and more variably in the event sequence (mean position 0.20 → 0.38), its transitions to A15 fell from about 90% to about 40% of its outgoing transitions, A14 now continues to A15 rather than to A04 (about 79% vs 43% before, A04 about 12%), and A14→A04 within five steps fell from 114 to 33 observations. [OCC002, POS001, DIR002, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, REL001]
- Smaller shifts: A02, A08 and A10 now appear in roughly three to four times as many traces (about 5–8 in 100 to about 18–23 in 100); A30's mean position moved slightly earlier (0.67 → 0.62) and A31's slightly later (0.80 → 0.82). [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the later window no longer chooses between two alternative activity groups but executes both in sequence: traces that previously contained either the group A33/A35/A36 or the group A37/A38/A39 (about 50/50, essentially never both) now contain both groups, first A33/A35/A36 and then A37/A38/A39, with A14 and A30 before the first group, A31 after the second, and A47/A48 now following the first group instead of substituting for it. In participation terms, activities that were skipped in about half of the cases are now executed in nearly all of them, and a fixed order between the groups appears where none existed. [OCC005, OCC008, REL039, REL045, REL050, REL013, REL025, REL031, REL042]

The support is direct and consistent: the relational items show the co-occurrence and ordering change directly, and the occurrence, direction, position and distance items describe the same underlying difference rather than independent anomalies. The screening produced 109 qualifying control-flow items where about 1.2 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the evidence as a whole cannot be explained by random re-splits of a stable process. [REL039, REL050, OCC005, LAG009, DIR006, screening]

Limitations: the relational evidence covers co-occurrence and order within five event steps, so it cannot show whether the first group must always precede the second or only typically does, and A15's relation to the first group remains mixed (about 41 in 100 interleaved), leaving its placement unresolved. A shift in the mix of complete process variants — a variant containing both groups becoming dominant — would produce the same indicator pattern, and the evidence cannot separate rerouting within one process from such a variant-mix shift. [REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024]

Other differences: A04 became rare (about 59 → 26 in 100 traces), sits later when it occurs, and connects far less deterministically to A15, while A14 now continues to A15 instead of A04; A02, A08 and A10 participate in roughly three to four times as many traces. The evidence does not show how these shifts relate to the main change. [OCC002, POS001, DIR002, DIR004, LAG002, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]
