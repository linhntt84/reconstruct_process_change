### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in timing as a possibility: in the later window, the elapsed time before A31 is completed lengthened substantially, regardless of whether the preceding completion was A23 (median about 0.9 → about 1.9 hours) or A30 (median about 0.13 → about 0.29 hours). Supporting signs: both predecessor pairs into A31 show the same direction and a similar rough doubling, each backed by roughly 100 observations; both differences exceed essentially all reference comparisons; and the time family yielded 2 items where about 0.008 would be expected without change, while no control-flow items appeared, so the timing change stands on its own, as it may. Limitations: TWAIT measures elapsed time between consecutive completion events, so the evidence cannot show whether the extra time reflects waiting, handover, or longer processing; and because both items share A31, they may reflect one underlying change rather than two independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is a shift in the mix of traces between these activity pairs rather than a pure lengthening of the intervals, although no structural difference was detected.
