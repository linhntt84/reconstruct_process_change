### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in which A02, A03 and A08 are no longer executed and the surrounding flow was rewired: A18 and A05 now lead to A10 where they previously led to A02; A10 leads to A01 where it previously led to A08; A13 and A19 lead to A05/A18 where they previously led only to A03; and the branch to A06/A07/A14 moved from A01 to A16, with A01 now leading to A11. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, DIR004, DIR008, DIR009, DIR013, DIR014, DIR001, DIR011]

The signs are direct and consistent: the disappearances rest on 94, 41 and 94 of 200 traces before against 0 of 200 after, which sampling variation cannot plausibly produce; the successor changes are read directly from the outgoing profiles of A05, A10, A13, A18 and A19 with substantial observation in both windows; relation and distance items show the same picture (A10, A11 and A16 now run without A02, and A10 reaches A01 immediately); and 129 control-flow items qualified where fewer than 1 (0.85) would be expected if nothing had changed, so the collection as a whole is far beyond chance. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, DIR004, DIR013, REL012, REL013, REL016, LAG022, screening]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the work of A02, A03 and A08 was dropped or continues under other anonymous activity codes, pairwise indicators cannot fully reconstruct the new end-to-end order, and the rerouting of A13 and A19 rests on fewer observations (roughly 24–50 outgoing transitions per window). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, DIR009, DIR014]

Other differences: the A07–A14 alternation at the end of traces stopped, leaving A07 (about 17 in 100 traces) and A14 (about 31 in 100) as single, terminal steps that never co-occur, and A09 and A17 now often hand over to each other while carrying more flow; these differences are not part of the main hypothesis above. [OCC003, OCC005, REL036, DIR007, DIR012]
