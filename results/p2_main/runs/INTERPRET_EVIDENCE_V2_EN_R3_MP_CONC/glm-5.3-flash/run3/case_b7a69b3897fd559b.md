### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, each of the ten activities A33, A34, A35, A37, A38, A39, A40, A41, A42 and A43 appeared in only about 48–53 in 100 traces; in the later window each appears in about 98–100 in 100 traces, with roughly one occurrence per trace in both windows (no repetition emerged). A30 and A31 occur in every trace in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010]
- In the earlier window the two five-activity groups were alternatives: a trace contained either A33/A34/A35/A37/A38 or A39–A43, and cross-group co-occurrence was at most about 1–2 in 100 traces; in the later window both groups run in the same trace in about 96–98 in 100 traces. [REL025, REL028, REL033, REL036, REL040, REL043, REL046, REL050, REL051, REL055]
- In the later window the groups run in a fixed sequence: A30 precedes the first group in about 97–99 in 100 traces, the first group precedes the second in about 96–97 in 100, and the second group precedes A31 in about 97–99 in 100. Earlier, A30 preceded whichever single group ran (about 47–52 in 100 of traces) and that group preceded A31. [REL001, REL006, REL010, REL011, REL016, REL020, REL025, REL040, REL046]
- Within each group the relative order stays mixed in both windows (for example A33 before A34 in about 20 in 100 traces earlier and 50 in 100 later, A34 before A33 about 28 and 49), so no fixed internal sequence appears in either window. [REL021, REL030, REL039, REL045, REL056, REL060, REL065]
- Routing shifted accordingly: A30's outgoing transitions earlier went about 48 in 100 to first-group activities and about 51 in 100 to second-group activities; later about 98–99 in 100 go to first-group activities. Transitions from first-group activities to A31 (about 15–27 in 100 of their outgoing transitions earlier) essentially disappear later, replaced by transitions into second-group activities (about 18–21 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- Mean event positions moved consistently with this: first-group activities earlier (about 0.72–0.73 to about 0.63 of the trace), second-group activities later (about 0.72 to about 0.76), A30 earlier (0.64 to 0.55) and A31 slightly later (0.82 to 0.84); these are position-based and corroborate rather than establish the reordering. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS012]
- The LAG items rest on only 1–4 observations in one window (for example A30–A40 within five steps: about 103 earlier vs 1 later) and are not relied on here. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two five-activity blocks that were previously mutually exclusive alternatives are now executed together in sequence: in the earlier window a case ran A30, then exactly one of the blocks {A33, A34, A35, A37, A38} or {A39, A40, A41, A42, A43} (about 50/50), then A31; in the later window nearly every case runs A30, the A33–A38 block, then the A39–A43 block, then A31, with no fixed order inside either block and each block still executed once per case. [OCC001, OCC006, OCC010, REL025, REL033, REL040, REL046, REL051]

The support is direct and consistent: relational evidence shows the cross-group co-occurrence rising from at most about 2 in 100 to about 96–98 in 100 traces with the first block first in about 96–97 in 100, participation of all ten activities roughly doubled to near-universal, and the routing changes at A30 and from first-block activities away from A31 agree with the same picture; with 99 qualifying items against about 1.2 expected by chance in the control-flow family, the set of differences is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [REL025, REL033, REL040, REL046, REL051, OCC001, OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR005]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows draw on differently composed case populations (for example a different mix of process variants) rather than a modified routing rule, and the thinly supported LAG spacing items add little; the evidence contains no time-based items, so nothing can be said about clock timing. [LAG001, LAG005, LAG006]

Other differences: a few very rare new transitions appear, each about 1 in 200 of the source's outgoing transitions, such as A30 to A08/A10 and isolated edges from A33, A35 and A37 to scattered activities. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005]
