### Observed differences

- The routing after A20 flipped: in the earlier window about 84% of A20's outgoing transitions went to A24 and about 15% to A29; in the later window about 82% go to A29 and only about 18% to A24 [DIR006].
- With this, two five-activity groups swapped roles. A21–A25 each ran in about 85 in 100 traces in the earlier window but only about 18–19 in 100 in the later window [OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010]; A26–A30 each ran in about 16 in 100 traces earlier and about 82 in 100 later [OCC011, OCC012, OCC013, OCC014, OCC015].
- The two groups were and remain mutually exclusive alternatives: earlier, A20 co-occurred with each of A21–A25 in about 85% of traces (A20 first in ~84%) and with each of A26–A30 in about 16%; later this is mirrored (co-occurrence ~82% with A26–A30, ~18% with A21–A25), and cross-block pairs such as A21 with A26 practically never share a trace in either window [REL015, REL020, REL024, REL029].
- Each group runs as a block with a fixed frame and mixed middle order: earlier, A24 preceded A21 in ~84% of co-occurrences and A21 preceded A25 in ~85%, while A21/A22 and A21/A23 appear in both orders; later, A29 precedes A26 in ~81% and A26 precedes A30 in ~81%, while A26/A27 appear in both orders [REL027, REL028, REL025, REL077, REL078, REL075].
- A04 fell from about 56 in 100 traces to about 12 in 100 [OCC002]. Earlier, A38 led to A04 in ~54% of its outgoing transitions (with A04 then always preceding A39) versus ~45% direct to A39; later, A38 leads directly to A39 in ~89% and to A04 in ~2%, and the A38→A39 distance shortened from typically two steps (~55% of pairs) to one step (~90%) [DIR011, REL014, LAG010]. A38 and A39 themselves still run in every trace in both windows [POS003, REL014].
- Minor: A02, A06, A08 and A10 rose from about 2–4 in 100 traces to about 13–18 in 100, and A36/A38 now occasionally lead into them (A36→A37 fell from ~96% to ~81% of A36's outgoing transitions) [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR010, DIR011].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports that the exclusive branch after A20 flipped: earlier, roughly 85 in 100 traces continued from A20 into the A21–A25 group (entered via A24, which preceded the other members, with A25 last); in the later window, roughly 82 in 100 continue into the A26–A30 group (entered via A29, with A30 last), leaving A21–A25 at about 18 in 100. The successor change at A20 is observed directly, the co-occurrence and order relations of all ten involved activities flip consistently, and the participation shifts rest on roughly 165–170 traces per window; the screening produced 138 qualifying control-flow items against about 1.3 expected if nothing had changed, far beyond what a stable process yields. The many relation items largely re-describe this one block-level difference, so they are consistent rather than independent confirmations [DIR006, REL015, REL020, REL025, REL027, REL077, OCC006, OCC011].

Because both groups still occur in a minority of traces in both windows (about 15–19 in 100), the evidence shows a flip in the shares of two mutually exclusive alternatives; it cannot distinguish a changed routing rule from a shift in the mix of two pre-existing process variants, and it cannot show what drove the flip [OCC006, OCC007, OCC011, OCC012].

Other differences:
- A04, which ran between A38 and A39 in about half of the earlier traces, is now mostly bypassed: A38 leads directly to A39 in about 89% of its outgoing transitions (earlier ~45%, with ~54% via A04), the A38→A39 distance shortened from two steps to one, and A04 appears in only about 12 in 100 traces, with its residual occurrences sitting around mid-trace rather than near the end [DIR011, LAG010, REL014, OCC002, POS001].
- A02, A06, A08 and A10 rose to about 13–18 in 100 traces and receive sparse new edges from activities such as A36 and A38; the evidence does not establish a specific structure for this minor fragment [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR010, DIR011].
