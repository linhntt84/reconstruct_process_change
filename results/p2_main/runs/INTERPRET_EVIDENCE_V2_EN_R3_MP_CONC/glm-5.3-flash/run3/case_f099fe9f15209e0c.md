### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 — previously a step executed in every case, early in the trace and before A01 in about 9 of 10 cases — is no longer executed in the later window, with the activities that followed it (A01, A03, A04, A05) now running without it, A03 routing directly on to A01, A04 and A05, and A03 and A04 occurring earlier with a shorter A03→A04 gap.
