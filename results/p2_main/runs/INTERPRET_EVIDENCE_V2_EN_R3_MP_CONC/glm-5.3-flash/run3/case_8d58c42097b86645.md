- Activity A01 is absent from the earlier window (0 of 200 traces) but appears in the later window in about 21 in 100 traces (42 of 200), at roughly 0.26 occurrences per trace, and it rarely repeats within a trace (about 3 in 100). [OCC001]
- In the earlier window, the outgoing transitions of A08 led to A10 in about 55 in 100 cases and to A09 in about 45 in 100; in the later window, A10 is no longer observed as a direct successor of A08, and A01 has taken over that share (about 55 in 100), while the share going to A09 is essentially unchanged (about 45 in 100). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added as a new step between A08 and A10. In the earlier window, cases leaving A08 went directly to A10 in about 55 in 100 of A08's outgoing transitions (or to A09 in about 45 in 100); in the later window the direct A08→A10 step is no longer observed, A08 instead leads to A01 in about 55 in 100 of its transitions, and A01 always leads on to A10. A01 occurs only in traces that also contain A08 and A10 (about 21 in 100 traces), around the middle of the trace, and the A08→A10 distance moving from mostly 1 step to mostly 2 steps matches exactly one activity inserted between them. The nine qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.13 expected by chance in a stable process, and the successor, relational and lag evidence shows the placement directly rather than inferring it from frequencies alone. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR003, REL001, REL002, LAG001, POS001]

Limitations: all items describe one underlying trace-level behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations of it; zero participation in the earlier window only means A01 was not observed there, and the evidence cannot fully rule out that A01 occurred before at a rate too low to appear in 200 traces, or that it replaces a previously unobserved step rather than being genuinely new. [OCC001, DIR001]

Other differences: the rerouting around A03 and A12 — A03 no longer observed going to A12, and A12 now followed by A13 instead of A03 — is not explained by the A01 insertion and rests on modest samples (20, 16 and 17 outgoing transitions), so it is reported separately. [DIR002, DIR004]
