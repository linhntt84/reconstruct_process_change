### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative block A14/A15/A20 was relocated from the front of the process to the end: in the earlier window A40 routed each case through one of A14/A15/A20 into A21, whereas in the later window A40 feeds directly into A21 and the three alternatives are inserted after A22, on either side of A23, closing into A23 or A27. The successor changes at A40, A22, A23 and the block activities show this directly, and the complete reversal of the order relations with A21 and A22, the roughly 0.27 position shift of the block, and the changed distances between A40, A21, A22, A23 and A27 all point to the same relocation; each item's difference is also larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits, and 24 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.4 would be expected without a change, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [DIR006, DIR004, DIR005, DIR001, REL001, REL004, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: the position indicator uses event positions rather than clock time, so nothing follows about timing in clock terms; and the evidence cannot show whether the choice among the three alternatives and their placement before or after A23 follow a case-specific rule or are random, nor distinguish a single move of the block from a removal plus a separate re-insertion at the new location, which would look identical here. [POS001, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003]

Other differences: the split among the three alternatives tilted slightly away from A14 towards A20 (A14 from about 38 to about 31 in 100 traces, A20 from about 32 to about 37), which the relocation alone does not require. [POS001, POS002, POS003]
