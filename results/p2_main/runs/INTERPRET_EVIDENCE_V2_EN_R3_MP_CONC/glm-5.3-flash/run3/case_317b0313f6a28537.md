### Observed differences

- The three activities A19, A20 and A25 kept roughly the same participation — each appears in about 31–39 in 100 traces in the earlier window and about 32–36 in 100 in the later window — but they now execute much earlier in the case: their mean event position moved from about 0.60–0.61 to about 0.32–0.34 [POS001, POS002, POS003].
- Their order relative to A26 and A27 reversed. In the earlier window, A26 ran before A19, A20 or A25 in about 31–38 in 100 traces and the reverse never occurred; in the later window, A19, A20 or A25 ran before A26 in about 30–36 in 100 traces and the A26-first order almost disappeared [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006].
- Routing around these activities changed accordingly. In the earlier window, the outgoing transitions of A19, A20 and A25 went mainly to A28 (about 47–56 in 100) and A32 (about 43–48 in 100); in the later window they go to A26 in about 88–94 in 100 of outgoing transitions [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]. Conversely, A27 and A28, which earlier passed control on to A19/A20/A25 in about half of their outgoing transitions (about 51 and 47 in 100), now lead almost exclusively to A28 (98 in 100) and A32 (96 in 100) [DIR004, DIR005]. A45, which earlier led to A26 in about 85 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, now leads to A19, A20 or A25 in about 85 in 100 [DIR006].
- Step distances fit this picture: A45 to A26 stretched from one step (86 in 100) to mostly two steps (82 in 100); A27 to A28 became immediate (98 in 100 at one step, from an even split of one and two); A27 to A32 shortened from three steps (96 in 100) to two (94 in 100); A28 to A32 became immediate (96 in 100) [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]. The block's old edges towards A28/A32 became rare, and the few later-window observations (1–3 cases) are too thin to establish their new lengths [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003].
- A26, A27 and A28 themselves kept full participation and near-constant positions (means about 0.33/0.56/0.61 earlier versus 0.36/0.59/0.62 later) [POS004, POS005, POS006].
- A15 still occurs in every trace but repeats less often: mean occurrences per trace fell from about 1.9 to about 1.6, with recurrence support down from 0.47 to 0.38 [OCC001].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the group of activities A19, A20 and A25 — each running in about a third of cases — now executes earlier in the case sequence: in the earlier window it ran late (mean position about 0.6), after A26 and A27, fed by A27/A28 and leading on to A28/A32; in the later window it runs early (mean position about 0.32–0.34), between A45 and A26, and leads almost exclusively to A26. The order reversal is shown directly by the relation items on all 200 traces per window, the rerouting by the direction items, and the distance changes (A45 to A26 now two steps, A27 to A28 now adjacent) fit the group sitting in its new place; participation of every involved activity stayed stable, so this reads as a reordering of existing behaviour rather than activities being added, removed or taken more often. The screening flagged 26 qualifying items where fewer than 1 would be expected without change, so the pattern as a whole is unlikely for an unchanged process — though the items largely reflect one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, POS001, POS002, POS003].

Limitations: the lag items on the group's old edges towards A28/A32 rest on only 1–3 later-window observations, so the new wiring in that direction is not firmly established [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]; positions measure event order within traces, not clock time, so "earlier" means sequence position [POS001, POS002, POS003]. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that this is not one relocation of the group but a combination of separate edge changes — A27 and A28 stopped feeding it, A45 was rerouted into it, and it was rerouted to A26 — which jointly produce the same observable picture [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006].

Other differences: A15 repeats less often per trace (mean occurrences about 1.9 down to about 1.6; recurrence support 0.47 down to 0.38) while still appearing in every trace [OCC001].
