### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the later window contains a new group of activities — A07, A15, A16 and A17 — that was absent earlier and is now integrated into the flow: one chain runs from A08 through A07 to A18, which now continues to A17 (leading to A09) or A14; a second links A10 with A15 and A16, with A16 continuing to A02 or A03. Earlier direct connections such as A08 → A05, A18 → A02 and A10 → A03/A02 are no longer observed [OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009].

Supporting signs: the four activities move from zero participation to 33–70 of 100 traces with solid backing (65–139 of 200 traces each); the successor profiles show the new edges directly; the relation and distance items agree with the same wiring; and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data. The screening produced 92 qualifying control-flow items where fewer than 1 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces [OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR002, DIR004, DIR009, REL007, LAG011, LAG027].

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window only means these activities were not observed there, and the evidence cannot rule out that they are relabelled or split versions of earlier activities rather than genuinely new ones; the exact entry and exit conditions of the new chains, beyond the observed successor shares, also remain undetermined [OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR002, DIR004].

Other differences: A05 and A13 now repeat about twice as often per trace and A18 about half as often; A05 occurs earlier and A18 later within traces; A05's outgoing transitions shifted from A09/A14 to A02, and A12's from A18 to A05 [OCC001, OCC003, OCC007, POS002, POS007, DIR001, DIR005].
