### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new segment was inserted between A43 and A28: A43 is now typically followed by A04, A04 by exactly one of the five alternatives A19, A20, A25, A26 or A27, and that activity by A28 — whereas before, A43 was followed directly by A28 and A04 was a rare, loosely placed activity. The change is shown directly by relational and lag evidence (A43→A04 at lag 1 in ~96 of 100 pairs; A04→A28 at lag 2 in ~95; the A43→A28 gap moving from one step to two or three), by consistent successor changes at A43, A04 and the alternatives, and by the participation shifts; with 62 qualifying items against roughly 0.7 expected without any change, chance cannot account for the evidence set as a whole. Limitations: in roughly 38 of 100 cases an alternative follows A43 directly without A04, so A04 is not an unconditional part of the segment, and "inserted" cannot be distinguished from "existed but was practically never used before", since zero participation only means the activity was not observed. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the near-disappearance of A06 and A08 is connected to this restructuring (for example, a previous alternative path they participated in was retired), because no relational evidence links them to the rest of the flow.
