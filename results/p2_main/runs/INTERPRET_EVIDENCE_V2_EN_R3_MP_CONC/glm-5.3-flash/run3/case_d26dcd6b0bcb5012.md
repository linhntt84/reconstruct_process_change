### Observed differences

- Five activities became far more prevalent: A19 and A20 appear in about 53 in 100 traces in the earlier window and about 99 in 100 in the later window; A25 and A26 rise from about 24 to about 51 in 100; A27 from about 30 to about 49 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- The direct step from A43 to A28 largely disappeared: earlier, about 45% of A43's observed outgoing transitions went straight to A28 and about 49% to A19; in the later window only about 1% go to A28, while about 90% go to A19. [DIR002]
- A28 and A43 still occur in every trace in both windows, but A28 now almost always appears together with A19 and A20 (co-occurrence about 99 in 100 traces, up from about 53), in the order A43, then A19, then A20, then A28. [REL001, REL005, REL006, REL010, REL011]
- The gap between A43 and A28 grew from mostly one event position (about 45% of traces) to mostly four or five positions (about 99%), matching the segment now running between them. [LAG001]
- The internal make-up of that segment is unchanged: A19 precedes A25 and A27, A25 precedes A26, and A25/A27 precede A20 in both windows; A25/A26 and A27 remain mutually exclusive alternatives (co-occurrence about 0–2% in both windows), taken about 45% vs 55% earlier and about 51% vs 49% later. [REL002, REL004, REL007, REL009, REL012, REL013, REL016]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment A19 → (A25 → A26 or A27) → A20, sitting between A43 and A28, was previously executed in only about half of the cases (about 53 in 100 traces) and skipped in the rest via a direct A43→A28 transition, whereas in the later window it is executed in nearly every case (A19 and A20 in about 99 in 100 traces) and the direct A43→A28 step survives in only about 1% of A43's outgoing transitions. The order of activities within the segment, and the roughly even choice between the A25→A26 branch and A27, are unchanged. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL010, LAG001]

The hypothesis rests on direct and mutually consistent signals across occurrence, relation, direction and event-distance indicators, with 28 qualifying items against roughly 0.7 expected by chance for an unchanged process, and profiles backed by roughly 100–200 traces per window. [OCC001, DIR002, REL001, LAG001]

Limitations: the direct A43→A28 transition is reduced but not eliminated (about 1 in 100 cases), so the segment is nearly rather than strictly always executed; several items describe the same underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations; and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows contain systematically different mixes of cases, which could produce this shift without a change to the process itself. [DIR002, LAG001]

Other differences: only minor shifts accompany the main change — A28's relative event position moved slightly later and A43's slightly earlier, and A27's outgoing transitions concentrated further onto A20 (about 93% to about 99%), all consistent with the segment now running in almost every trace. [POS001, POS002, DIR001]
