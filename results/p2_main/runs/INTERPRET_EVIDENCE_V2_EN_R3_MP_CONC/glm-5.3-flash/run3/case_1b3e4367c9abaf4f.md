### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, two previously alternative paths are always combined and a new closing step is added. Every case now starts with A02 and runs into the A06/A12 branch (A06 repeated about twice per case) before reaching A03/A05/A07, whereas earlier each case took either the A02 opening (about 48 in 100) or the A06/A12 branch (about 52 in 100), never both. In addition, A01 — not observed in any earlier trace — now runs exactly once per case near the end, adjacent to the A15/A16 pair that previously always followed one another directly. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC005, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, REL008, REL012, REL015]

This rests on direct and consistent signs rather than on item count alone: participation moved from zero to every case (A01) and from about half to every case (A02, A06, A12); the successor profiles of A02, A15 and A16 changed directly; and the relation items show the earlier either/or pattern becoming universal co-occurrence in the same direction across many activity pairs. With 58 qualifying items against fewer than one expected by chance among 107 tested units, the ensemble as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits of an unchanged process produce. [OCC001, DIR002, REL012, REL015]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly establishes only that A01 was not observed there, and two finite samples of 200 cases cannot rule out that the windows draw different case mixes (for example a case type that always contains A02 and the A06/A12 branch and ends with A01) rather than that the process itself behaves differently. The many agreeing relation items largely describe the same trace-level changes and are not independent confirmations; positions and gaps are event-order based, so no clock-time timing is implied. [OCC001, POS001, REL012]
