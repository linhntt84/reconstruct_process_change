### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One possibility directly supported by the evidence is a structural change in which the one-of block A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 — which before executed between A29/A30 and A31 in nearly every trace — was bypassed in the later window: A29 now proceeds almost directly to A30, A30 almost directly to A31, and the five activities are executed in at most about 2 in 100 traces, if at all. The change is shown directly by the successor profiles of A29 and A30 and by the one-step shortening of the A29-to-A31 distance, not only by the block's disappearance. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR009, DIR010, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010]

The reading is consistent across participation, co-occurrence and ordering evidence: ordered pairs such as A20 before A31 collapsed in step with the block's participation (about 23 to about 2 in 100 traces), while the surrounding frame stayed intact — A29, A30 and A31 still occur in every trace at nearly unchanged positions, and the A14 to A15 to A16 spine is essentially unchanged. Screening also exceeds what chance produces by a wide margin: 59 control-flow items qualified where fewer than one (0.73) were expected if nothing had changed. [REL015, REL021, POS006, POS007, DIR002, DIR003, OCC005, DIR010, LAG008]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish complete removal of the block from its survival as a rare optional detour, since only 0–4 later-window observations remain per block activity, and several lag items about pairs involving the block rest on 1–5 later-window observations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows draw different case mixes in which this detour is naturally rare, although the stable frame activities argue against that. [OCC007, OCC008, LAG002, LAG005, LAG009]

Other differences: the A46 to A06 to A47 branch lost A06 (participation about 29 to 7 in 100 traces; A46 now routes to A47 in about 94 in 100 of its outgoing transitions), and A02 and A10 occur somewhat more often (about 2 to 10 and 4 to 11 in 100 traces); these are not part of the bypass hypothesis. [OCC002, DIR011, OCC001, OCC003]
