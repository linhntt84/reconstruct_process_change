### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the five-activity block A35, A36, A41, A42, A43 was repositioned: in the earlier window the flow ran from A25 to A27, both A27 and A26 preceded the block, and the block led on to A30; in the later window A25 feeds directly into the block, the block precedes both A27 and A26, and A26 leads straight to A30 — in behavioural terms, the block moved from after A26 to before A27. The order reversal itself is observed directly: the previously dominant directions (A26-first and A27-first, each in about 98–100 in 100 traces) are replaced in the later window by the block-first direction in similar proportions. [REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021]

The surrounding evidence is consistent with this single reorder: the successor profiles changed exactly at the block's old and new boundaries (A25 and A26 before it, all five block members at the exit), the position distributions shifted in the same direction, and the lag data show the old adjacencies disappearing. Screening qualified 64 items where about 1 would be expected if nothing had changed, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, so the pattern as a whole is not what a stable process typically produces. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013, LAG014, LAG015, LAG016, LAG017, LAG018]

Two limitations remain: the evidence cannot distinguish whether the block moved earlier or the A27–A26 segment moved later, since both readings fit the same order reversals; and the relative order of A26 and A27 in the earlier window is not directly observed (no relational item covers that pair), so the exact earlier chain between A25, A27 and A26 is inferred from positions alone. The agreeing items also largely reflect one underlying reorder rather than independent confirmations. [REL012, REL017, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: A02, A46, A19 and A20 show a further change not covered by the main hypothesis — A02 roughly tripled its participation, appears earlier, is now typically preceded by A46 and followed by A19 or A20, and typically precedes the main-chain activities. The lag detail for some A02 pairs rests on very few before-observations (1–5 pairs), so only the participation and routing shifts are well supported here.
