### Observed differences

- **Repetition increased sharply.** Before, A30, A31 and A36 each occurred almost exactly once per case (means 1.00–1.02, present in 100 in 100 traces, with repeats in at most about 2 in 100 traces). In the later window, each of the three occurs about 3.5 times per case, and about 68 in 100 traces contain repeats of each of them. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- **Routing out of A36 was redistributed.** Before, about 97 in 100 of A36's observed outgoing transitions led to A25. In the later window, about 70 in 100 lead to A30 and about 28 in 100 to A25, with only small shares to other activities. [DIR001]
- **The fixed order around A14 broke down.** Before, A14, A30 and A36 co-occurred in every case and kept a fixed sequence (A14 before A30 in about 99.5 in 100 traces, A14 before A36 in about 98.5, A30 before A36 in about 98). In the later window they still co-occur in every case, but the fixed order holds in only about 32 in 100 traces, while about 68 in 100 show interleaving or repetition. [REL003, REL004, REL005]
- **The tight A30→A36 spacing largely disappeared.** Before, A30 was followed by A36 two positions later in about 98 in 100 traces; in the later window that share is about 12 in 100, with small shares at larger distances. [LAG002]
- **A05's relation to the repeated activities loosened.** A05 co-occurs with A30 (and similarly A36) in about half the cases in both windows, but A05 coming first fell from about 54 in 100 traces to about 16 in 100, while interleaving or repetition rose from under 2 to about 35 in 100. [REL001, REL002]
- **Minor shifts.** The gap between A05 and A14 moved from mostly one to three positions apart toward two to three. [LAG001] A14's mean relative position shifted slightly earlier (about 0.14 to 0.12) and A30 and A36 sit slightly later with their spread roughly doubled; these are event positions, not clock times. [POS001, POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment formed by A30, A31 and A36 became repeatable: before, every case ran these three activities exactly once, after A14 and in a fixed order ending with A36 flowing to A25; in the later window the segment runs about three to four times per case. A36's outgoing flow leads back to A30 in about 70 in 100 observations (before, about 97 in 100 went to A25), each of the three activities recurs in about 68 in 100 traces, and the previously fixed order around A14 now appears as interleaving or repetition in about 68 in 100 traces. The roughly 70/28 split of A36's outgoing transitions is consistent with about two to three returns to A30 plus one final exit to A25 per case. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG002]

The screening tested 99 control-flow units and about 0.5 items would qualify by chance if nothing had changed, yet 14 qualified, and every item moves in the same direction; the repetition is measured directly on occurrence counts and the new dominant successor of A36 is observed directly in the routing, so the hypothesis does not rest on indirect signs alone. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001]

Limitations: all items come from the same 200+200 traces and describe one underlying difference, so they are not independent confirmations. The evidence cannot distinguish a strict sequential repetition (each pass returning via A36 to A30) from other forms of re-execution, because interleaved and repeated cases are combined in the relation items; it also leaves open whether the internal order within each pass changed, since the tight two-step A30→A36 spacing dropped from about 98 to about 12 in 100. A31's place in the segment is inferred only from its occurrence counts, as no relation or spacing item covers it. [REL003, REL005, LAG002, OCC002]

Other differences:

- A05 still takes part in about half of the cases in both windows, but its ordering relative to the repeated activities loosened, as described above; its own participation did not change. [REL001, REL002]
- The A05→A14 gap widened slightly toward two to three positions apart. [LAG001]
- A14's mean relative position moved slightly earlier while A30 and A36 spread later with roughly doubled variability; these are event positions and do not show that an activity was moved in clock time. [POS001, POS002, POS003]
