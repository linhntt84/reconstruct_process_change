### Observed differences

- Before, A25, A26 and A31 each occurred about 3.4 times per trace, appeared in every trace, and repeated within the same trace in about 75 in 100 traces. In the later window, each occurs about once per trace (1.04), still in every trace, but repeats in only about 1 in 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- Before, about 71 in 100 of A31's observed outgoing transitions led to A25 and about 29 in 100 to A20; in the later window, about 96 in 100 lead to A20 and only about 4 in 100 to A25 (based on 689 outgoing transitions before and 208 after). [DIR001]
- A25 and A31 still co-occur in every trace in both windows, but their relationship changed: before, they were interleaved or repeated in 75 in 100 traces (A25 strictly before A31 in the remaining 25); in the later window, A25 comes before A31 in about 995 in 1000 traces. [REL001]
- In the later window, A31 follows A25 within two steps in about 92 in 100 cases, whereas before the pair's distance was spread across the trace; the reverse relation (A31 before A25 within five steps) was observed in 150 of 200 traces before but only 1 of 200 after. [LAG001, LAG002]
- The average position of A25 and A31 within the trace barely moved, but their positions became much more concentrated (standard deviation roughly halved, e.g. A25 from about 0.12 to 0.06). [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the repeated execution of the block containing A25, A26 and A31 stopped. Before, these three activities formed a repeating pattern: each ran about 3.4 times per trace, A25 and A31 were interleaved or repeated in three quarters of traces, and A31 frequently led back to A25. In the later window, each activity runs once per trace in a fixed order — A25 followed by A31 about two steps later — and A31 now continues to A20 instead of returning to A25. Supporting signs: the near-total loss of within-trace recurrence for all three activities, the redistribution of A31's outgoing transitions from mostly A25 to mostly A20, the collapse of the interleaved/repeated relation into a fixed A25-before-A31 order, and the near-disappearance of the A31-before-A25 relation. With 9 qualifying items against roughly 0.3 expected by chance in a stable process, the pattern as a whole is unlikely to be a sampling artefact, and the occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators all point in the same direction and magnitude. These indicators describe overlapping aspects of the same traces, so they are consistent rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a fully removed loop from one that is still taken in about 1 in 200 traces (residual recurrence support of 0.005), and A26's position relative to A25 and A31 is not covered by any relational evidence, so its role in the sequence is inferred only from its identical occurrence profile. The main competing explanation — that the loop still exists but is now taken very rarely rather than having disappeared — cannot be ruled out. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: the concentration of A25's and A31's positions within the trace (much smaller spread, unchanged average position) is consistent with single execution at a fixed spot instead of a repeating block, and is best read as a side-effect of the repetition change rather than an independent relocation of these activities.
