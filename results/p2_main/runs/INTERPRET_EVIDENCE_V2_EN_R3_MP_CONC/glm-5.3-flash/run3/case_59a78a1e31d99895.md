### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace executed exactly one of two alternative endings: A04 alone in about 50 in 100 traces, or A06 together with A10 in the other 50 (A04 never co-occurred with A06 or A10). In the later window, A04 appears in about 20 in 100 traces and A06 with A10 in about 21 in 100, and in about 66 in 100 traces none of the three appears at all. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL007, REL010, REL013]
- Routing into this block changed. A02 previously sent about half of its outgoing transitions to A04, A06 and A10 (about 25%, 13% and 13%); in the later window none of these routes is observed and A02's outflow concentrates on A05 (about 23% to about 46%). A03 and A09, which previously transitioned only to A07/A08 (A08 taking about 47% and 62% of their outgoing transitions) with no observed transitions to the block, now send roughly 45–50% of their outgoing transitions to A04/A06/A10 and none to A08. The earlier clean precedence of A02 before the block (about 25–30 in 100 traces) is no longer observed. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003]
- The order between A08 and the block reversed. Earlier, A08 ran before A06/A10 whenever they coincided (A08-first in about 13–14 in 100 traces) and A04 usually occurred without A08; in the later window A04, A06 and A10 practically never occur without A08 and precede it (block-before-A08 in about 10–12 in 100 traces). A06 and A10, which previously led only to each other (in both orders), now also lead to A08 in about 44% and 56% of their outgoing transitions; A04, previously almost always the final event so that only one outgoing transition was observable, now leads to A08 in all 47 observed transitions. [REL008, REL011, REL014, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006]
- Event positions shifted accordingly (positions, not clock time): A04, A06 and A10 now occur much earlier when they occur (mean relative position about 0.60–0.64, down from about 0.88–0.99; A04's median fell from the final position to about 0.6), while A08 occurs later (mean about 0.57 to about 0.72) with unchanged participation of about 33 in 100 traces in both windows. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- Distances moved in the same direction: A02 used to be immediately before A04 (lag 1 in about 61 in 100 cases; afterwards lag 1 is unobserved, with the pair at 2–4 steps); A03 and A09 now reach A04/A06/A10 within 1–2 steps (e.g., A03→A04 at lag 1 rose from none to about 38 in 100), whereas A03/A09→A08 moved from lag 1 to later lags. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG004, LAG006, LAG008, LAG013, LAG015, LAG016]
- A02 itself now occurs later in the trace (median relative position about 0.5 to about 0.73) while still appearing in every trace. [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the closing stage of the process was restructured. In the earlier window, every case executed one of two alternative endings — A04 alone, or A06 together with A10 — reached directly from A02 and placed at the end of the trace, with A08 (fed by A03 and A09) running before A06/A10. In the later window, this block is executed in only about a third of cases and skipped in about two-thirds; when executed, it is triggered from A03/A09 rather than A02, sits earlier in the trace, and is followed by A08, which in turn occurs later and is no longer reached from A03/A09. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL008, REL011, REL014, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG001, LAG008, LAG013]

The hypothesis is shown directly by successor changes and order reversals, not only by frequency shifts, and participation, routing, positional and lag items all point the same way; every qualifying item shows a difference larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits, and with 45 qualifying control-flow items against well under one expected for an unchanged process, chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole. The items nevertheless overlap heavily (many describe the same traces), so they are not independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR002, REL007, REL011, POS002, LAG001]

Limitations: some earlier-window profiles rest on very few observations (A04's outgoing transitions were observed once, the A03→A04 pair on about 15 cases), and the evidence cannot show what, if anything, the roughly two-thirds of skipping cases do instead, since no activity shows a compensating rise in participation. An important competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the block becoming optional and its repositioning relative to A08 are two separate changes rather than one coordinated restructuring. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS002, POS004, LAG006]

Other differences: A02 routes to A01 more often (about 3% to about 12% of its outgoing transitions) and A09 routes to A07 more often (about 37% to about 50%); A04, A06 and A10 occasionally repeat within a trace in the later window (about 3–4 in 100 traces), which was not observed earlier. [DIR001, DIR005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
