### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative pair A09/A11 changed from jointly skippable to mandatory-in-one: in the earlier window about 42 in 100 cases executed neither A09 nor A11, while in the later window every case executed exactly one of them — A11 in about 57 in 100 cases (up from 29) and A09 in about 44 (up from 30) — and both are now reached mainly immediately after A06, which sends over half of its outgoing transitions to them (about 10 in 100 before) within one to two steps instead of three to four. [REL003, OCC001, POS002, POS003, DIR002, LAG002, LAG003, REL001]

The change is shown directly by relational evidence: A09 and A11 co-occur in no trace in either window, while the share of traces containing neither fell from about 42 in 100 to zero. The routing shift at A06 rests on 186–200 observed transitions, and the pieces are numerically consistent — the participation gains of A09 (about +14 in 100) and A11 (about +28 in 100) together match the disappearance of the neither-cases (about 42 in 100). Sixteen qualifying items against roughly 0.16 expected by chance among the 32 tested control-flow units also makes a chance origin of the whole set implausible. [REL003, DIR002, OCC001, POS002]

Limitations: the indicators cannot show whether the former neither-cases were rerouted into A09/A11 because a skip option was removed or because an upstream routing change (for example at A06) now sends every case through one of them; the before-successor profiles of A09 and A11 rest on only about 7–10 observed transitions, and several items (A11's participation, position, order after A06 and lag) describe the same underlying behaviour rather than independent confirmations. [DIR004, DIR005, OCC001, POS003, REL001, LAG003]

Other differences: A08's slightly lower participation, its later and more variable position, the shift of its outgoing flow from A09/A11 to A02/A06, and the loosening of its fixed order before A11 are consistent with the same restructuring, but the evidence does not establish whether A08's rerouting drove or followed the change around A09/A11.
