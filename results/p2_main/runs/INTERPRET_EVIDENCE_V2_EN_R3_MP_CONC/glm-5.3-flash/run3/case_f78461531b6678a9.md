### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment A22 → A21 and the five-activity block {A30, A31, A36, A37, A38} exchanged their place in the flow. In the earlier window, a case ran A20 into the block, the block executed in a mixed internal order, then A22, then A21, then A25; in the later window, A20 leads to A22, then A21, and the block executes afterwards and exits to A25 — that is, A22 and A21 now run before the block instead of after it.
