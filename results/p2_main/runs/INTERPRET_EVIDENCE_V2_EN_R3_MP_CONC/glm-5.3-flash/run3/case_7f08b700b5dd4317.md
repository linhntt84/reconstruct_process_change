### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 each ran in every trace (200 of 200, about once per trace, never repeated). In the later window, A33, A35 and A36 each appear in about 47 in 100 traces, and A37, A38 and A39 in about 56 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

- Before, the two groups of three always ran together and in sequence: an A33/A35/A36 activity preceded an A37/A38/A39 activity in about 99 in 100 traces, with no fixed order inside either group. In the later window, the three activities of each group still appear together whenever the group runs (A33 with A35 in about 47 in 100 traces, A37 with A38 in about 56 in 100), but an activity from the first group and one from the second share a trace in only about 3 in 100 cases. [REL013, REL014, REL015, REL018, REL019, REL022, REL025, REL026, REL027]

- A30 and A31 still occur in every trace in both windows. Before, A30 preceded A33/A35/A36 in essentially all traces, and A31 came after them (A33 before A31 in about 99 in 100 traces); in the later window, A30 shares traces with A33/A35/A36 in only about 47 in 100 cases and with A37/A38/A39 in about 56 in 100. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, POS001, POS002]

- Routing into the block changed: before, A30's outgoing transitions went almost only to A33 (about 34 in 100), A35 (about 36) and A36 (about 28); in the later window they spread over six targets, with A37 (about 15), A38 (about 17) and A39 (about 19) now receiving transitions they did not receive before. A31, previously not observed as a successor of A33, A35 or A36, now receives about 26–34 in 100 of their outgoing transitions. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

- The block also sits differently: pairs such as A30–A37 or A33–A31 were typically 4–5 event positions apart before, whereas in the later window about 95 in 100 of these observed pairs are 1–3 positions apart. Mean positions shifted accordingly: A30 moved later (0.62 to 0.67 of the trace), A33/A35/A36 later (about 0.68 to 0.74), A39 earlier (about 0.77 to 0.73) and A31 slightly earlier (0.82 to 0.80). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG009, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the six-activity block between A30 and A31 — previously executed in full in every case as two ordered groups, {A33, A35, A36} followed by {A37, A38, A39} — became optional and split into mutually exclusive alternatives: in the later window a case typically contains A30, then one of the two groups, then A31, with the A33/A35/A36 group in about 47 in 100 cases, the A37/A38/A39 group in about 56 in 100, and both groups together in only about 3 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL015, REL022, REL025]

Supporting signs: participation fell from 100 in 100 traces to about 47 and 56 in 100 on samples of 200 traces per window; between-group co-occurrence collapsed from 100 to about 3 in 100 while within-group co-occurrence persisted; A30 gained direct transitions into the second group and A33/A35/A36 gained A31 as a direct successor; and the lag distributions shrank from 4–5 positions to 1–3, which fits a group being skipped rather than relocated. The screening found 54 qualifying items where fewer than 1 would be expected without change, so the pattern as a whole is not what a stable process produces, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits — although many items describe the same underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG004, LAG006, LAG009, REL013, REL025]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show where or how the choice between the two groups is enforced, nor whether the roughly 3 in 100 cases containing both groups form a separate behaviour; a competing explanation it cannot fully rule out is that the later window draws differently on pre-existing variant populations, although with 200 of 200 earlier traces containing the full block, such a draw would itself represent a large behavioural difference. [REL015, REL022]

Other differences:

- A39's position distribution became more variable (standard deviation about 0.064 to 0.094) alongside its earlier placement. [POS006]
- A few lag items for pairs that almost never share a trace in the later window (for example A33 with A38, A35 with A37) rest on only 4–5 after-window observations and were not used as support. [LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011]
