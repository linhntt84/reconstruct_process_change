### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the sequence A19 → A25 → A20, sitting between A41 and A26, changed from optional to mandatory: in the earlier window it executed in about 47 in 100 cases and A41 otherwise passed directly to A26, while in the later window it executes in essentially every case, A41 leads into A19, and the direct A41 → A26 step has nearly disappeared. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL007, DIR002, LAG001] The signs are direct and mutually consistent — participation rose for all three activities, their co-occurrence and internal order are near-constant, A41's outgoing flow rerouted from A26 to A19, and the A41–A26 distance moved from immediate to a four-position gap — and 23 items qualified against roughly 0.64 expected if nothing had changed, more than chance can explain. [DIR002, LAG001, REL003, REL004, REL008, REL009, REL012, REL013] Limitations: the positional readings use event positions rather than clock time, and the many agreeing items largely describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations.
