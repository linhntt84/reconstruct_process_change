### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A27 — previously executed in every case between A26 and A28 — is skipped in about 43 in 100 cases, and that in exactly those cases a newly appearing group of activities, A32, A33, A38, A39 and A40, runs instead between A26 (and A15/A16) and A28, in a variable rather than fixed order. This rests on direct and mutually consistent evidence: the participation flip for A27 and the five new activities, the split of A26's outgoing transitions, the mutual exclusivity between A27 and the new group, and the relational evidence placing the new group after A26 and before A28. With 72 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.83 expected for an unchanged process, and differences of this magnitude (0 vs 87–89 of 200 traces; 200 vs 113 of 200), chance sampling cannot account for the ensemble. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR004, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030, REL031, REL032, REL033, REL034, REL035, REL036, REL037, REL038, REL039, REL040, REL041, REL042, REL043, REL044, REL045, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009]

Limitations: many qualifying items describe the same underlying traces and are not independent confirmations; the evidence shows the new group is executed instead of A27 but cannot establish that it takes over A27's function, and the internal sequencing of the group remains unresolved because both orders occur in similar proportions. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the new group existed as a rare path before and was simply not sampled in the 200 earlier traces, although 0-in-200 versus 87–89-in-200 makes this unlikely. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, REL036, REL043, REL044, REL045]

Other differences: A06 participates in fewer traces and now distributes its successors broadly rather than concentrating on A16; A10 occurs somewhat more often; A04's successor mix changed on a very small base; A15, A16 and A26 each sit slightly earlier within traces; and A06-to-A16 gaps lengthened. These are secondary and not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG001]
