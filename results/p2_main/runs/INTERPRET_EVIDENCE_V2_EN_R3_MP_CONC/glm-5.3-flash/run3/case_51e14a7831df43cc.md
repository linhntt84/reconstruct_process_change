### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A09 and A16 exchanged their places in the control flow. Earlier, A09 was the mandatory, late, often-repeated step — present in every trace at a median relative position of about 0.86, repeated in about 48 in 100 traces, fed by A04 and A10 and leading to A05, A06 and A14 — while A16 was an optional, earlier, single step (about 50 in 100 traces, median position about 0.40) fed by A01 and A13 and leading to A01, A04, A10 and A13. In the later window these roles are exchanged: A16 is mandatory, late and repeated, preceded by A04 and A10 and followed by A05, A06 and A14, while A09 is optional (about 49 in 100 traces), earlier, preceded by A01 and A13 and followed by A01, A04, A10 and A13. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS001, POS002, REL001, REL002, REL007, REL008, REL010, REL014, LAG003, LAG005, LAG006, LAG010, LAG012]

The placement hypothesis is shown directly by relational and positional evidence rather than by frequency shifts alone, and chance is an unlikely explanation for the findings as a set: 39 items qualified where about 0.4 would be expected if nothing had changed. Limitations: positions and distances are measured in event order, not clock time; the windows show the exchanged end state but not how the rewiring came about; and items sharing A09 or A16 largely describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows sampled different mixes of coexisting process variants rather than reflecting a change over time. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, POS001, POS002]

Other differences: minor redistributions remain in the outgoing shares of A01 and A13 that do not involve the swap — for example, A01 to A13 rose from about 40 in 100 to about 43, and A13 to A15 from about 21 in 100 to about 26; these are small and may reflect sampling variation between the windows. [DIR001, DIR005]
