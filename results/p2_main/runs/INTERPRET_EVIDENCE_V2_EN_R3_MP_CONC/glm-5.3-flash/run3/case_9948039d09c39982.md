### Observed differences

- Activity A01 is new to the later window: it appears in 0 of the 200 earlier traces but exactly once in every one of the 200 later traces, at a late position (median about four-fifths through the trace). [OCC001, POS001]
- The A01–A08 relation flipped completely: earlier, A08 was present in all 200 traces and A01 in none; in the later window both are present in all 200 traces, and A01 comes before A08 in every single case. A08 itself remains in every trace in both windows. [REL004]
- A04 and A09 now route into A01 instead of A08: earlier, about 60 of 100 observed outgoing transitions of A04 went to A08 (about 20 to A07, 19 to A06), and about 69 of 100 for A09; in the later window about 88 of 100 (A04) and 91 of 100 (A09) go to A01, and A08 is no longer observed as a direct successor of either. Their own participation stayed at roughly half of traces in both windows (about 93–107 traces per window). [DIR002, DIR003]
- When they occur in the later window, A04 and A09 always precede A01 (in every trace containing them, about 53 and 47 of 100 traces respectively), whereas earlier the two never co-occurred with A01. [REL001, REL005]
- Step distances to A08 lengthened: A04→A08 went from 1 step in about 60 of 100 cases to 2–3 steps in all observed cases; A09→A08 from mostly 1 step to 2–3 steps; A06→A08 and A07→A08 now also show gaps of 4–5 steps in roughly a third of cases where earlier gaps were 1–3. [LAG003, LAG007, LAG004, LAG005]
- A04 and A09 now appear earlier within traces (mean position from about 0.65 to 0.50 for A04, and from about 0.74 to 0.62 for A09). [POS002, POS003]
- A06 and A07 now usually share traces with A01 and mostly run before it, but in both orders (A06 before A01 in about 36 of 100 later traces versus about 11 the other way round; A07 about 37 versus 17). [REL002, REL003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new, mandatory activity A01 was placed late in the process between the mid-process activities and A08: traces that earlier moved from A04 or A09 directly to A08 now pass through A01 first, and A01 precedes A08 in every later trace. Screening qualified 19 of 36 control-flow units where about 0.18 would be expected without a change, so the differences as a whole are far beyond what random re-splits of a stable process produce. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

The support is direct and consistent: A01 goes from 0 to 200 of 200 traces with one occurrence each [OCC001]; the A01-before-A08 relation holds in all 200 later traces after complete separation earlier [REL004]; the outgoing transitions of A04 and A09 shift from mainly A08 to mainly A01 on substantial samples [DIR002, DIR003]; and the A04→A08 and A09→A08 distances grow by about one step, as expected if one activity was interposed [LAG003, LAG007]. The earlier position of A04 and A09 within traces is consistent with this rerouting rather than pointing to a separate shift [POS002, POS003].

Limitations: the items overlap heavily — REL004, DIR002, DIR003, LAG003 and LAG007 largely describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. How A01 is reached in the roughly half of later traces without A04 or A09 is only partly resolved, since A06 and A07 usually precede it but sometimes follow it [REL002, REL003]. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 is a relabelled activity that already existed rather than a genuinely new step, and the branching after A01 (to A08 in about 73 of 100 cases, otherwise to A07 or A06) remains unresolved [DIR001].

Other differences: the remaining gap shifts among A04, A06, A07 and A09 (for example A04→A06 and A04→A07 moving from 1 step to mostly 2 steps) point in the same direction but rest on only about 11–33 observations each and add little independent weight. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG006]
