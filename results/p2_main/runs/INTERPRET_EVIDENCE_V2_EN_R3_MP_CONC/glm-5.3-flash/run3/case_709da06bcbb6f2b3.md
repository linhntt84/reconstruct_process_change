- Activity A01 was not observed at all in the earlier window (0 of 200 cases); in the later window it runs in about 119 of 200 cases (roughly 60 in 100), at most once per case, and early in the case (median relative position about 0.17, with three quarters of its occurrences in the first quarter of the case). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was added to the early part of the flow: it appears in about 60 in 100 later cases (it was entirely unobserved earlier), always together with A03 and A10, with direct handovers in both directions between A01 and A03/A10 and onward from A01 to A04 and A07; A04 and A07 now occur only in cases that also run A01, at later positions, and the step distance from the early activities to A04/A07 grew by roughly one step. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, POS002, POS003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG005]

The change is shown directly (appearance of A01, new edges on both sides, co-occurrence and order relations) and consistently across occurrence, direction, relation, position and lag indicators; moreover, 17 evidence items qualified against roughly 0.19 expected if nothing had changed, so the set of differences is far larger than random re-splits of a stable process produce. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS002, POS003, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: the items largely describe one underlying trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations; "not observed" in the earlier window strictly means zero participation in that sample, so a previously unobserved rather than genuinely new activity cannot be ruled out; positions and lags use event order rather than clock time, so nothing follows about timing; and the exact routing around A01 (for instance whether it is mandatory within the cases containing it) cannot be pinned down. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is a changed case mix in the later window — a variant involving A01 becoming common — rather than a modification of one process, although with about 119 of 200 cases affected, chance sampling alone cannot explain the evidence as a whole. [OCC001, POS001]

Other differences: the small participation increases of A03 and A10 (about 56 to about 60 in 100 cases) and A04 (about 29 to about 32 in 100), with A07 essentially unchanged, are minor and may reflect sampling variation rather than a separate change. [DIR002, DIR003, POS002, POS003]
