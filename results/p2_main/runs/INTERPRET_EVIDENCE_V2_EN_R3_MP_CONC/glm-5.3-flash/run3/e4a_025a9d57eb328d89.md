### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Main hypothesis: a change in timing — in the later window, the stretch between the completions of A16, A17, A18 or A19 and the next completion at A23 takes about one hour longer, roughly doubling from about 1 hour to about 2 hours. The time family produced 4 qualifying items where about 0.01 would be expected if nothing had changed (24 units tested), each difference larger than all but about 1 of 2000 random re-splits, and all four pairs agree in direction and magnitude (increases of roughly 0.9–1.1 hours across the quartiles); the roughly stable pair counts indicate the pairs still occur as before, so the longer gap is not explained by a new activity appearing between these completions. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004]

Limitations: TWAIT measures elapsed time between consecutive completion events, not pure waiting or service time, so the evidence cannot show whether the extra hour reflects slower handling, queueing or scheduling effects upstream of A23 — an important competing explanation it cannot rule out. Only these four of the 24 tested time pairs qualified, so the slowdown appears specific to this segment rather than process-wide, but a subtle structural contribution cannot be fully excluded. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004]
