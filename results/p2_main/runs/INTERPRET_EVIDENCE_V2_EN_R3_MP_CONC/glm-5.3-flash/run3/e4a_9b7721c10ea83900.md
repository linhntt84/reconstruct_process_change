### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the elapsed time between the completion of A26 and the completion of A34 was about 1 hour (median ~1.0 h, middle half roughly 0.9–1.1 h) in all 200 traces; in the later window it was about 2 hours (median ~2.0 h, middle half roughly 1.8–2.2 h), and the spread roughly doubled (std ~0.16 h → ~0.31 h) [TWAIT001].
- The same pattern appears for the pairs A35→A34 and A37→A34: median elapsed times moved from about 1.0 h to about 2.0 h, with similar increases across the whole distribution [TWAIT002, TWAIT003].
- The A35→A34 pair was observed in about 40 in 100 traces before (76 of 200) and about 30 in 100 after (66 of 200); the A37→A34 pair in about 40 in 100 before (82 of 200) and about 25 in 100 after (52 of 200) [TWAIT002, TWAIT003].
- In the screening, the time family produced 3 qualifying items where about 0.01 would be expected if nothing had changed, while the control-flow family produced none (0.31 expected) [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a timing change: the elapsed time from the completion of A26, A35 and A37 to the completion of A34 roughly doubled, from about 1 hour per case to about 2 hours per case, and became about twice as variable. This is shown directly by three T_WAIT items that agree in direction and magnitude (each about +1 h), each with a difference larger than essentially all of 2,000 random re-splits of the pooled data (empirical strength ~0.9995 in every item) [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003]. The screening comparison supports this as a whole: the time family yielded 3 items against an expectation of about 0.01 without change, a count far too large to attribute to chance [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003]. The A26→A34 pair remained consecutive in all traces in both windows (200 of 200 in each), so the longer interval is not an artefact of the pair disappearing [TWAIT001].

Limitations: the evidence shows only elapsed time between consecutive completions, so it cannot distinguish whether A34 was started later or the intervening work took longer; and no structural counterpart was observed (the control-flow family produced no qualifying items), so a structural cause cannot be fully excluded, although none was detected [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003].

Other differences: the pairs A35→A34 and A37→A34 were observed somewhat less often in the later window (about 40→30 and 40→25 in 100 traces); since no control-flow item qualified at the configured thresholds, this may be a sampling difference rather than a participation change [TWAIT002, TWAIT003].
