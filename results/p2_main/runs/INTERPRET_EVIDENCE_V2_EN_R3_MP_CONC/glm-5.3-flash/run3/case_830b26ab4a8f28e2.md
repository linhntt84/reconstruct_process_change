### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was removed from the process (or is no longer executed) and that the flows which previously ran through it were redirected: A03 and A05, which previously passed to A01 in about 88% and 85% of their outgoing transitions, now lead directly to A01's former successors A08, A09 and A10, which continue to run but now always without A01 and closer to A03 and A05. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Because A01 appeared in every earlier trace but in no later trace, this is a structural change — an activity removed or bypassed — not a shift of shares between existing alternatives. The screening produced 17 qualifying items against roughly 0.26 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what an unchanged process typically yields; the participation, successor and relational items (each difference larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits) all describe the same underlying difference rather than independent phenomena. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR003, REL003, REL004, REL005]

The evidence cannot rule out that A01 events merely stopped being recorded in the later window, or that A01 became optional and was never triggered in the sampled traces; both would look identical to a removal here. [OCC001, POS001]

Other differences: the spacing between A08 and A09 shifted slightly (within-one-step occurrences fell from about 14% to 7%, four-step ones rose from about 4% to 7%), possibly a knock-on of A01's disappearance [LAG007]; and A05's observed outgoing transitions rose from about 330 to about 450 while its trace participation stayed near two-thirds, suggesting A05 repeats somewhat more often per trace in the later window [DIR003, REL002].
