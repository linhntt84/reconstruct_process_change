### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A change in timing is supported: in the later window, the elapsed time between the completion of A16, A17, A18 or A19 and the subsequent completion of A26 is consistently about half an hour shorter than before (median about 1.0 h versus about 0.5 h), with a visibly tighter distribution. The time family produced 4 qualifying items where only about 0.013 would be expected if nothing had changed — far beyond what chance produces — and all four agree in direction and magnitude, each difference exceeding about 9995 in 10000 random re-splits and resting on 41–59 observed intervals per window. The unchanged interval count for A18–A26 rules out occurrence changes as the driver of the time shift. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004]
