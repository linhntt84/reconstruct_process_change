### Observed differences

- **Repetition of A03 and A14 stopped.** In the earlier window each of the two appeared in about 50 of 100 traces and ran about twice within those traces (mean about 1.1 occurrences per trace overall, with repetitions in about 27 of 100 traces). In the later window each still appears in about 47 of 100 traces but occurs only once — mean about 0.5 occurrences per trace and no repetitions at all. [OCC001, OCC003]

- **A09 now repeats far more often.** Occurrences per trace rose from about 1.9 to about 4.3 while participation stayed similar (about 62 vs 68 of 100 traces), and repetition support rose from about 39 to 60 in 100 traces. [OCC002] A13 now leads to A09 in about 71 of 100 of its outgoing transitions (before about 35) and A15 leads to A09 in about 95 of 100 (before about 41), while A09 itself leads more often to A15 (about 48 vs 37 in 100) and less often to A13 (about 11 vs 22). [DIR003, DIR004, DIR006] A09 and A13 are now interleaved or repeated in about 60 of 100 traces (before about 39), and the gaps between consecutive A09 events and between consecutive A15 events moved from roughly 3–5 steps towards about 2 steps. [REL003, LAG007, LAG013] A13 and A15 also occur somewhat more often overall (outgoing transitions up from 374 to about 410 and about 440). [DIR004, DIR006]

- **A03, A11 and A14 turned from a loose, interleaved grouping into a fixed, compact early sequence.** Before, A03 appeared without A11 in about 23 of 100 traces and A14 without A11 in about 23, and each pair was interleaved or repeated in about 27 of 100 traces with no consistent order. In the later window A03 and A14 never appear without A11, interleaving is gone, A11 precedes A14 in about 39 of 100 traces (about 9 the other way) and precedes A03 in about 33 (about 14 the other way), and all three activities sit much earlier in the trace (medians from about 0.32–0.40 down to about 0.11–0.20 of the trace). A11 itself now appears in about 47 of 100 traces, up from about 28. [REL001, REL002, REL004, POS001, POS002, POS003] Of the observed A03→A11 and A14→A03 pairs, about 9 in 10 are now only one to two steps apart, where before they were spread across one to five steps (based on 28 and 41 pair observations). [LAG005, LAG010]

- **Other routing and gap shifts.** A06 now leads almost exclusively to A01 (about 99 of 100 outgoing transitions vs about 86), with its transitions to A14 and A03 no longer observed [DIR002]; A01 no longer leads to A15 (about 9 of 100 before) and sends slightly more to A13 and A09 [DIR001]; A14's remaining outgoing transitions shifted away from A11 (about 18 to 4 in 100) towards A12 (about 10 to 34) [DIR005]; and event gaps between several pairs involving A01, A09, A13 and A15 redistributed, mostly towards longer gaps for the A13/A15 pairs and shorter ones for A01→A11. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG008, LAG009, LAG011, LAG012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the repetitive part of the process shifted: in the later window, A03 and A14 no longer repeat and instead run once, early in the trace, as a fixed sequence together with A11, while A09 repeats about twice as often as before within a repeating sequence that A13 and A15 now feed almost exclusively. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR006, REL001, REL004]

Supporting signs: the occurrence indicator changes directly and in opposite directions for the two activity groups while participation stays stable, so this is not an activity appearing or disappearing; the successor profiles of A13 and A15 collapse onto A09; and the relational and positional items show the A03/A11/A14 pairs moving from interleaved, order-less co-occurrence to fixed orders with earlier positions and one-to-two-step gaps. Screening tested 86 control-flow units and flagged 29 items where fewer than 1 (0.43) would be expected without a change, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, so sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG005, LAG007, LAG010]

Other differences: the rerouting of A06 almost entirely to A01, the disappearance of observed A01→A15 transitions, A14's outgoing shift from A11 to A12, and the redistributed event gaps among A01/A09/A13/A15 pairs are not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG008, LAG009, LAG011, LAG012]

Limitations: all items come from the single control-flow family and many describe the same traces (the A03/A14 occurrence, position and relation items overlap heavily), so they corroborate rather than independently confirm one underlying change; the evidence cannot establish whether the A03/A14 and A09 changes are two sides of a single restructuring or two independent changes. DIR shares are over outgoing transitions rather than cases, and the position and gap indicators use event positions rather than clock time, so no timing claim is implied. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003]
