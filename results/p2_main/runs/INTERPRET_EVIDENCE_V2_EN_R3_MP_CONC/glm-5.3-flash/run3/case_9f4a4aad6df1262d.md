### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the ordering constraints between two groups of activities changed. In the earlier window, A20, A22 and A23 always executed before A24, A25 and A26 in a single fixed chain; in the later window, that fixed sequence was replaced by several coexisting orders in which A24, A25 and A26 typically run before A20, A22 and A23 (for example, A24 before A23 in about 84 in 100 traces versus about 1–2 in 100 before), enabled by new direct transitions between the groups such as A20 or A22 leading to A24/A25/A26 and A24 leading back to A22. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

The support is direct and consistent: all eight relation items flip in the same direction on about 200 co-occurring traces per pair while co-occurrence stays complete, so sampling or participation differences do not explain it; the successor profiles show the matching structural counterpart, and the position and distance profiles move in the same direction. Chance is also implausible at the screening level, since 49 items qualified where roughly 0.7 would be expected for an unchanged process. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG001, LAG006, LAG013, LAG027]

Limitation: the evidence cannot establish the exact shape of the new flow — some pairs are now near unordered while others are predominantly reversed, so a mixture in which only part of the cases follow a new alternative sequence while the rest keep the old order cannot be ruled out. A few distance profiles also rest on very few observations in one window (for example, A24→A22 had only 1 supporting case before versus 94 after), and the smallest new transitions (about 1 in 200) may be noise. [LAG014, LAG015, LAG019, LAG021, REL003, DIR001]

Other differences: beyond the group reordering, A31 now branches directly to A24 in about 47 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (before: only to A22), and A23 now leads directly to A32 in about 49 in 100 of its transitions, shortening their typical distance from about 4 steps to 1 — the entry and exit points of the segment changed alongside the reordering. [DIR007, DIR003, LAG013, LAG027]
