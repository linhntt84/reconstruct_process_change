### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A25 and A20 ran in every trace (200 of 200) in a fixed order — A19, then A25, then A20 — after A41/A14/A15 and before A26/A46/A47. In the later window, each of the three appears in only about 53–54% of traces (about 106–108 of 200), and in about 46 in 100 traces none of the three appears at all; in the earlier window that never occurred. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL016, REL017, REL022]
- When the three activities do run in the later window, their order is intact: A19 before A25, A25 before A20, A41 before A19, and A26/A46/A47 after, each in essentially every trace where the pair co-occurs (about 52–53 in 100 traces overall, matching the co-occurrence rate of about 53 in 100). Repetition did not change; each activity occurs at most once per trace in both windows. [REL016, REL017, REL019, REL022, REL023, REL027]
- When the block is skipped, A41 often connects straight to A26: A41's outgoing transitions to A26 rose from none to about 39 in 100, and A41 and A26 are now adjacent (one step apart) in about 40 in 100 cases, whereas previously they were about four steps apart in 91% of cases. [DIR008, LAG008]
- A06 is executed far less often: about 26 in 100 later traces versus about 71 in 100 earlier (52 vs 142 traces). Earlier, A06 preceded A19/A20/A25 and A26/A41/A46/A47 in about 65 in 100 traces; now it co-occurs with them in only about 14 in 100, and in about 35 in 100 traces neither A06 nor the block appears. When A06 does run, it sits at a later event position (mean about 0.18 → 0.43 of the trace), and its outgoing transitions are dispersed (about 92% went to A15, now about 37%). [OCC002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, POS001, DIR003]
- A14 now routes to A15 in about 78 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (about 31 before), while its transitions to A06 fell from about 69 to about 12 in 100. [DIR005]
- Three previously rare activities occur more often: A04 in about 15% of traces (was 5%), A08 in about 20% (was 4%), A10 in about 16% (was 5%). [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment A19 → A25 → A20, which ran in every earlier trace between A41/A14/A15 and A26/A46/A47, became optional: in the later window it is skipped in about 46 in 100 traces, its internal order and its neighbours are unchanged whenever it runs, and A41 then frequently proceeds directly to A26. This rests on direct participation counts (200 → about 106–108 of 200 traces for each of the three activities, with no repetition change), on pairwise relations showing the whole block absent together in about 46 in 100 later traces (never earlier), and on the bypass being visible directly in routing and adjacency data. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL016, REL017, REL022, DIR008, LAG008]

For scale, the control-flow family produced 59 qualifying items across 176 tested units, versus fewer than one expected if nothing had changed, so these behaviours are part of a far larger coherent set than chance would produce. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, REL001, LAG001]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows sample pre-existing process variants at different ratios (for example, more cases of a variant without this segment) rather than a modified single flow; also, only the A41→A26 connection is directly observed as a skip route, so other ways of bypassing the segment may exist. [REL016, DIR008]

Other differences: A06's sharp decline (about 71% → 26% of traces), its later event position when present, its dispersed successors and A14's rerouting from A06 to A15 are not covered by the segment-skip hypothesis alone and may reflect a separate rerouting of the early flow; A04, A08 and A10 also became more frequent (from about 4–5% to about 15–20% of traces). Some lag-based items rest on very few observations (for example, A15–A26 with one earlier observation) and were not relied on. [OCC002, POS001, DIR003, DIR005, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, LAG007]
