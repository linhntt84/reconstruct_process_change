### Observed differences

- The two windows swap the roles of two activity groups. In the earlier window, A21–A25 each ran in only about 19–21 of 100 traces; in the later window, each runs in about 77 of 100. The mirror image holds for A26–A30: about 82 of 100 traces earlier, only about 24–26 of 100 later. Each of these activities occurs at most once per trace in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010]
- The two groups are strict alternatives, and the preferred one flipped. Earlier, A21 and A26 never appeared in the same trace (A21 without A26 in about 18 of 100 traces, A26 without A21 in about 82); in the later window they still almost never co-occur, but the shares reversed (about 77 vs about 23 in 100). [REL035, REL039, REL044, REL048, REL059, REL063, REL065, REL069]
- The entry into the groups flipped at A20: earlier, about 81 of 100 outgoing transitions from A20 went to A29 and about 18 to A24; in the later window, about 75 go to A24 and about 23 to A29. [DIR001]
- The surrounding anchors are unchanged: A14, A20 and A34 still appear in every trace in both windows. The A21–A25 group now typically follows A14 (A14 before A21 in about 76 of 100 traces, versus about 18 earlier), while A26–A30 now typically do not appear alongside A14 (co-occurrence down from about 82 to about 24 in 100). [REL011, REL016, REL021, REL026]
- Each branch kept its internal shape, so the branch content itself is unchanged. In the A21–A25 branch, A24 precedes A21, A22 and A23, and all three precede A25, in both windows (about 76 of 100 traces each in the later window), while A21, A22 and A23 remain mutually unordered; in the A26–A30 branch, members still co-occur whenever it runs, with A29 before A26. A25 still leads almost exclusively to A34 (about 95 of 100 outgoing transitions earlier, about 99 later). [REL031, REL033, REL034, REL042, REL043, REL050, REL051, REL058, REL071, REL073, DIR002]
- A34 accompanies whichever branch runs: earlier it co-occurred with A26–A30 in about 82 of 100 traces; in the later window it co-occurs with A21–A25 in about 77 of 100, interleaving or repeating with A21 in about 40 of 100 traces (about 6 in 100 earlier). Smaller shifts: A29 sits slightly earlier and more variably within traces, and step gaps inside the now-dominant branch redistributed (e.g., A22-to-A34 gaps moved from mostly 2–3 steps to 3–5 steps). [REL040, REL075, REL085, POS001, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the two alternative branches of the process swapped how often they are taken. In the earlier window, roughly 4 in 5 cases ran the branch containing A26–A30 (A20 typically passing to A29, with A34 following the branch), and roughly 1 in 5 ran the branch containing A21–A25 (A24, then A21/A22/A23 with no fixed order among them, then A25, then A34); in the later window this reversed, with about 3 in 4 cases taking the A21–A25 branch and about 1 in 4 the A26–A30 branch. The change is in the selection frequencies of existing paths, not a replacement of one path by another: both branches are well observed in both windows (roughly 37–51 versus 153–155 supporting traces per activity), their internal orderings and strict alternation are preserved, and the anchors A14, A20 and A34 still occur in every trace. The routing flip is observed directly at A20, the participation changes are large and symmetric on both sides, and 103 qualified evidence items against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process rules out chance as an overall explanation. [OCC001, OCC006, DIR001, REL011, REL016, REL035]

An important competing explanation remains: the evidence cannot distinguish a change in the branch-selection rule itself from a shift in the mix of cases reaching this decision point (for example, more cases of the kind that uses the A21–A25 branch); both would produce the same picture. Position and gap evidence is based on event positions, not clock time. [DIR001, REL035, POS001]

Other differences: minor redistributions of step gaps within the now-dominant branch (A20-to-A22 and A24-to-A22 gaps spread out, A22-to-A34 and A25-to-A34 gaps lengthened slightly) and a slightly earlier, more variable position of A29 within traces. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, POS001]
