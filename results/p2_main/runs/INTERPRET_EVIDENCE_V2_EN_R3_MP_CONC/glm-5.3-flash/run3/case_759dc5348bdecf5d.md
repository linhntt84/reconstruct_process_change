- In the earlier window, A03 and A11 were regular activities: A03 appeared in about 29 in 100 traces (57 of 200, averaging roughly 1.2 occurrences per trace) and A11 in about 20 in 100 (39 of 200). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening tested 44 control-flow units and 18 items qualified, against roughly 0.22 expected if nothing had changed — about 80 times the chance expectation — so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005]

The evidence supports the possibility that the repetitive cycle through A03 and A11 was removed. Before, A06 typically led to A03; A03 led to A11 (which led back to A03), A12 or A19; and A06, A12 and A19 repeated at longer trace distances. In the later window, A03 and A11 are no longer executed, A06 leads directly to A19 and A12, and A12 and A19 occur earlier, in fewer traces, and closer after A06. The support is direct — the complete disappearance of A03 and A11 across 200 traces and the successor change at A06 — and consistent across the co-occurrence, position and distance indicators. Limitations: zero participation only means the activities were not observed, so the evidence cannot distinguish full removal of the cycle from it becoming rare enough to miss in 200 traces, and the earlier positions of A12 and A19 (measured in event positions, not clock time) may simply reflect the missing A03/A11 segment rather than an independent move. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the cycle still exists but was not triggered in the later sample.
