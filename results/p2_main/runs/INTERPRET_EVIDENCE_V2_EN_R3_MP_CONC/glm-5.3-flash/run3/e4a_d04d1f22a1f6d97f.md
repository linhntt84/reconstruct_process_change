### Observed differences

- **A22 became intermittent.** Earlier, A22 appeared in every trace (participation 100%, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace); in the later window it appears in about 51 in 100 traces (mean 0.51). [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, an alternative branch consisting of A27, A28 and A33 — executed together in no fixed order — sits between A21 and A23 and is taken in about half of the cases, replacing the former A21 → A22 → A23 segment (A22 skipped) in those traces, while the original segment with A22 still runs in the remaining ~51 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL005, REL009, REL010, REL011, LAG001]

The change is shown directly and consistently across occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators rather than resting on a single item, and its volume far exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 26 control-flow items against about 0.4 expected by chance, and 2 time items against about 0.008. A limitation is that zero participation earlier only means A27/A28/A33 were not observed, so they could have existed at a very low rate below detection rather than being genuinely new; the evidence also cannot show what constrains the trio's internal order beyond its being mixed. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL012, REL013, REL014]

Other differences: The elapsed time between consecutive completion events around A29 roughly halved in the later window (A25→A29 from about 0.9 to about 0.5 hours; A30→A29 from about 0.2 to about 0.1 hours, each based on only some 40–60 observations) — a timing change in its own right that the evidence does not connect to the branch change above. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
