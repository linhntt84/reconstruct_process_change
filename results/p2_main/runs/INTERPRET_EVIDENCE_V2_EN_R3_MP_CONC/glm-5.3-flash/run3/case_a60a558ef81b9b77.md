### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the five-way alternative block A14, A15, A20, A21, A22 was relocated. In the earlier window each case ran A42 → A23, and the block activity ran late, between A24/A25 and A29, alternating in order with A25; in the later window each case runs A42 → one block activity → A23, while A24 → A25 → A29 runs as a direct chain with no block activity in between. [DIR008, DIR001, DIR006, DIR007, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, REL001, REL005]

The change is shown directly on both sides of the move: successor changes at A42, at all five block activities, and at A24 and A25, plus a complete order flip between the block and both A23 and A24, with the block's mean position moving from about 0.60 to about 0.31 while A23 and A24 stayed near their old positions. Participation still sums to one block activity per case in both windows, which fits a move rather than an added copy, and 34 qualifying items against roughly 0.5 expected without change cannot be explained by chance as a whole. [DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS006, POS007, REL001, REL002, LAG003]

Limitation: a few residual transitions in the later window still point to old successors (about 2–3 in 100 for A20→A25 and A22→A29, about 1 in 100 for A42→A23), so the evidence cannot rule out that a small share of cases still routes the block through its old position — a partial rather than complete relocation. [DIR003, DIR005, DIR008]

Other differences: A12's participation rose from about 19 to 33 in 100 cases, and the mix within the block shifted slightly towards A21; the evidence does not tie either to the relocation. [OCC001, POS003, POS004, POS005]
