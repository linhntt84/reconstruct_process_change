### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the relative execution order of two groups of activities is reversed: A19, A21 and A24, which previously executed after A20, A22 and A23, now execute before them, with A25 and A29 remaining between the two groups; consequently A30, which previously followed A19, A21 and A24, is now reached after A20, A22 and A23 and after A29. The same reshuffle includes A02 becoming a frequent early step between A40 and the A19/A21/A24 group, rising from about 8 to about 34 of 100 traces. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, REL010, REL015, REL017, REL019, REL024, REL027, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG004, OCC001, POS001, DIR001, LAG005]

Supporting signs: the order reversals are observed directly within co-occurring traces rather than inferred from positions alone, all group members occur in essentially every trace in both windows (so this is not a rare variant appearing in one window), position shifts and successor changes point in the same direction, and all 30 relation items are consistent with the reversal. The number of qualifying items (58, all control-flow) is many times the roughly 0.75 expected for an unchanged process, so chance sampling cannot account for the ensemble. [REL010, REL017, REL024, DIR002, DIR004, POS003, LAG004]

Limitations: many items describe the same trace-level reshuffle, so they are not independent confirmations; positions are event positions, not clock times; and the evidence cannot establish whether the ordering constraint between the groups itself changed or whether upstream routing (entry via A40 and A29 now feeds A02/A19/A21/A24 first) produces the same reversal — both fit the data. The mix within the early group also shifted (A19 up to about 44 of 100 traces, A21 down to about 28–29), which the order hypothesis alone does not explain. [DIR008, DIR009, POS002, POS004, POS007]

Other differences: A41 now recurs more often within traces (about 1.5 → 1.8 occurrences per trace; recurrence support about 0.33 → 0.40) while still appearing in every trace. [OCC002]
