### Observed differences

- The clearest change is a reversal of execution order between the activities A26 and A27 on one side and the three activities A35, A36 and A41 on the other. In the earlier window, A35, A36 and A41 each ran before A26 in essentially every trace (about 100, 99 and 99 in 100 co-occurring traces) and before A27 as well (about 99, 99 and 98 in 100); in the later window, A26 and A27 each run before all three in about 97–99 in 100 traces. All six activities co-occurred in every trace in both windows. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Routing into and out of this block changed accordingly. In the earlier window, A25's outgoing transitions went to A35 in about 39 in 100 cases, A36 in 32 and A41 in 27, and A26 led to A30 in about 99 in 100. In the later window, A25 leads to A27 in about 98 in 100, A26 leads to A35, A36 and A41 in roughly 34, 32 and 30 in 100, and the three block members lead to A30 in about 30–35 in 100 each, while A27 has all but disappeared from their successors. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]
- Distances moved in step: A25 reached A27 about four steps later in about 94 in 100 traces before, but immediately after it in about 99 in 100 later; A26 reached A30 immediately in about 99 in 100 before, but about four to five steps later in about 98 in 100 after. A25 and each of A35, A36, A41 appeared within five steps of one another in all 200 earlier traces but in only about 2 later traces. [LAG005, LAG009, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG012]
- Event positions agree: A26 and A27 moved earlier (mean relative position about 0.62 to 0.53 and 0.47 to 0.38), while A35, A36 and A41 moved later (about 0.41–0.42 to 0.58–0.59). [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Away from this block, the path through A06 became rarer: A06 appeared in about 27 in 100 traces before and about 13 in 100 after (54 vs 25 traces), its occurrences sit later within traces, and its outgoing transitions spread from about 86 in 100 leading to A16 to about 39 in 100, with the rest scattered over many activities; A15 now leads to A16 in about 95 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (before about 85) and to A06 in only about 3 in 100 (before about 15). These later-window figures rest on only about 25 traces. [OCC002, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001]
- A16 now precedes A27 within five steps in about 149 of 200 traces (usually five steps apart); in the earlier window this ordered pair was observed only once, so the earlier behaviour is unclear. [LAG003]
- A02 appeared in more traces (about 4.5 in 100 before, 11.5 in 100 after; 9 vs 23 traces — a small base). [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of three activities A35, A36 and A41 changed its position in the flow. In the earlier window, A25 led into the block (A35/A36/A41 in roughly equal shares), the block preceded both A27 and A26, A27 followed about four steps after A25, and A26 was directly followed by A30. In the later window, A25 leads directly to A27, A26 leads into the block, and the block precedes A30, which now sits about four steps after A26 — that is, the block swapped from the segment between A25 and A27 to the segment between A26 and A30. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG005, LAG009]

The support is direct and consistent: six near-complete order reversals, each based on about 200 co-occurring traces per window; matching successor changes at A25, A26 and the block members; matching distance changes for A25–A27 and A26–A30; and matching position shifts. The screening also produced 33 control-flow items where only about 0.76 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the set of differences is far larger than random re-splits of a stable process would produce. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG005, LAG009, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

Limitations: the evidence establishes the relative reorder but cannot tell whether the block moved later or A26 and A27 moved earlier, and it does not establish the internal order or possible repetition of the block; a few lag profiles after the change (for example A25 with A35/A36/A41) rest on only about 2 observations, although the collapse from 200 to 2 in five-step co-distance is itself informative. [LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG012]

Other differences: the decline of the A06 path (participation down from about 27 to 13 in 100 traces, later positions, outgoing transitions spread across many successors, and A15 leading to A06 in only about 3 in 100) and the increase of A02 (about 4.5 to 11.5 in 100 traces, on bases of 9 and 23 traces) are not explained by the block relocation; the A06 details are uncertain because only about 25 later traces contain A06. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, LAG001, LAG002]
