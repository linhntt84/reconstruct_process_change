### Observed differences

- The start of roughly half of the cases changed. In the earlier window, about 49 in 100 traces began with A04, always as the first event, and A04 then always led to A03; A01 was not observed in any trace. In the later window, about 50 in 100 traces begin with A01, again always as the first event and always leading to A03, while A04 is not observed in any trace. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS002]
- The relation to A03 mirrors this swap. Earlier, A04 preceded A03 in about 49 in 100 traces and never followed it; later, A01 precedes A03 in about 50 in 100 traces, likewise never in the other order. A03 itself appears in every trace in both windows. [REL001, REL002, REL003]
- Routing around A07 and A13 also differs. Earlier, A07 led to A13 in about 92 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions and A13 led to A03 in about 77 in 100. Later, A07 leads to A13 in only about 56 in 100, with transitions to A03 (about 29 in 100) and A05 (about 10 in 100) now observed, and A13 leads to A07 in about 32 in 100 while its share to A03 falls to about 47 in 100. These shares rest on 48 versus 59 observed outgoing transitions. [DIR003, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One possibility supported by the evidence is that the entry step for about half of the cases was exchanged: cases that earlier began with A04 and then ran A03 now begin with A01 and then run A03, while A03's presence in every trace is unchanged. The supporting signs are the mirrored participation (A01 from 0 to about 50 in 100 traces, A04 from about 49 in 100 to 0, with 200 traces per window), the first-position placement of both activities, the single successor A03 for each, and the relational items showing A04-before-A03 disappearing as A01-before-A03 appears. With 11 qualifying items against roughly 0.14 expected if nothing had changed, chance as a whole is an unlikely explanation. The evidence cannot show whether A04 was removed entirely or only became much rarer, since zero participation only means it was not observed, nor whether A01 and A04 represent the same step under a different label. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS002, REL001, REL002, REL003]

Other differences: the rerouting around A07 and A13 — newly observed transitions A07→A03, A07→A05 and A13→A07 alongside reduced shares for A07→A13 and A13→A03 — is a separate structural difference not covered by the main hypothesis; because an unobserved edge does not prove absence, these could be newly added or previously rare edges.
