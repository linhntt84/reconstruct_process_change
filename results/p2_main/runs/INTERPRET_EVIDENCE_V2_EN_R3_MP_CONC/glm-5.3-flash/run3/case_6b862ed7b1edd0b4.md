### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the rear block A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 (with A26 following) is executed in virtually every case, whereas in the earlier window about half of the cases bypassed it via a direct A41 → A26 transition, leaving A19, A20 and A25 optional. The bypass is shown directly on A41's outgoing transitions (A41 → A26 about 45 → under 1 in 100; A41 → A19 about 37 → 96 in 100), and the block's participation rose from about 48 to about 100 in 100 traces on large supports (about 95 → 199 of 200 traces per activity). All pairwise relations inside the block moved from partial co-occurrence to near-universal co-occurrence with the same dominant order and tight, stable step distances, and the always-present front chain A14 → A15 → A16 now connects to the block in essentially every case. Overall, 62 control-flow items qualified against fewer than 1 expected without change, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, so the set as a whole is not a chance artefact of the two samples. The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply contain a different mix of case types (for example, a variant that always includes the block becoming dominant) rather than a change in the shared flow itself.
