### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural control-flow change as a possibility: A01, not observed earlier, appears as a new final activity executed after A07 in every trace (A07 thereby ceases to be the last step), while A05 — previously run in a repeating loop with A04 in roughly half of the traces — is no longer observed, and A04's repetition, which earlier passed through A05, now happens through a direct self-loop. [OCC001, POS001, DIR003, REL003, OCC002, DIR002, DIR001, LAG001]

The hypothesis is direct and consistent. The placement of A01 rests on relational and successor evidence (A07→A01 in all observed outgoing transitions; A07 before A01 in every later trace), not on position alone; the participation changes rest on large supports (A01 in 0 of 200 versus 200 of 200 traces; A05 in 93 of 200 versus 0 of 200); and A04's successor and lag profiles shift exactly as replacing the A04–A05 loop with a self-loop implies. [DIR003, REL003, OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, LAG001] In screening, sixteen control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.09 expected if nothing had changed, so the ensemble of differences is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002] Note that several items describe the same underlying behaviour — for example OCC001, POS001 and REL003 all reflect A01's addition — so agreeing items corroborate but do not independently confirm the hypothesis. [OCC001, POS001, REL003]

The evidence cannot rule out that A05 still occurs but went unsampled in the later window (zero participation only means not observed), nor can it distinguish a modified process from a shifted mix of process variants between the two windows; it also shows what changed, not why. [OCC002, POS003]

Other differences: A04's relative position shifted slightly earlier and became less variable, and the A04→A07 spacing redistributed towards slightly larger distances; both are consistent with by-products of the changes above rather than separate hypotheses. A02 remains a rare successor of A04 (about 1 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions) in both windows. [POS002, LAG002, DIR001]
