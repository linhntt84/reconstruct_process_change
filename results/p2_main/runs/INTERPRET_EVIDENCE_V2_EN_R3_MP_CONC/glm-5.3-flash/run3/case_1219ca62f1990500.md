### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the block of alternative activities A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 that used to run between A43 and A28 is largely skipped: earlier, A43 passed through exactly one of the five in virtually every trace before continuing to A28, whereas later it proceeds straight to A28 in about 93 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and the five activities are almost never executed. The rerouting is observed directly at A43, the A43→A28 succession became immediate, the five activities' participation collapsed, and A43 and A28 now typically occur without them; occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators all describe this one underlying difference. Far more items qualified (57) than the roughly 0.72 expected by chance for the 144 tested units, so sampling noise cannot account for the evidence as a whole. [DIR007, LAG011, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL017, REL018, REL021, REL023, REL024]

Limitations: the five activities were observed in at most 3 of 200 later traces, so the evidence cannot distinguish complete removal from execution in a very small share of cases, and the roughly 7 in 100 later outgoing transitions of A43 that bypass A28 are too few to characterise. The competing explanation that the five activities moved to another position is largely ruled out — A15 and A16 occur in every trace, so any occurrence of the five would still co-occur with them, yet co-occurrence falls to 0–1.5 in 100 traces — but the evidence cannot rule out that the mix of cases entering this part of the process differed between the windows rather than the routing itself. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007]

Other differences: A02's participation halved (about 27 in 100 traces earlier to about 14 in 100 later), A15 rarely transitions to A02 any more (about 13 in 100 of its outgoing transitions earlier, about 1–2 in 100 later), and A02's outgoing transitions, earlier mostly back to A16, became diffuse in the later window on few observations; A15, A16 and A28 shifted only slightly in position. These may belong to the same restructuring, but the evidence does not directly connect A02 to the bypassed block. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, POS002, POS003, POS009]
