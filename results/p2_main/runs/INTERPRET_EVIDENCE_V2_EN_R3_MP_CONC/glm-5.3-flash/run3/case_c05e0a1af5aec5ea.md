### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the control-flow segment running through A05, A16 and A01 was replaced by one running through A08, A16 and the newly observed A17. Earlier, A05 (about 1 in 5 traces) led mainly to A01 and A16, A16 led back to A05, and A01 closed the path into A09; in the later window A05 survives only marginally and leads to A13/A04 instead, A16 feeds A08, A08 feeds A16 and A17, A17 feeds A09, and A09 feeds back into A05 rather than into A08, while A01 now stands at the first position of its traces with no observed successor and no co-occurrence with any tested partner. [DIR003, DIR007, DIR004, DIR008, DIR005, OCC003, OCC004, POS001]

The screening found 42 qualifying control-flow items where roughly 0.39 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits produce. The rewiring is shown directly by complete successor replacements at A05, A16, A08 and A09, and the participation, position and distance items (A17 appearing mid-late in traces, A01 at position 0, pair distances swapping between 1–2 and 4–5 steps) line up with the same restructuring rather than with isolated fluctuations. [DIR003, DIR007, DIR004, DIR005, OCC004, POS001, POS002, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: no outgoing transition from A01 and no co-occurrence with any tested activity are observed in the later window, so A01's new role — for example whether it opens a separate variant or is an isolated step — cannot be determined; several after-window distance estimates rest on very few observations (about 4–6). [DIR001, LAG004, LAG006]

Other differences: participation dropped for A02 (about 50% to 38% of traces) and A03 (about 29% to 16%); the evidence contains no items linking them to the restructuring above. [OCC001, OCC002]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out: the later pattern may have existed earlier as a rare variant that simply went unobserved in 200 traces (A17, the A08→A16/A17 branch) and whose share then grew, rather than the branch being newly introduced; either way, the two windows show materially different control flow. [OCC004, DIR008]
