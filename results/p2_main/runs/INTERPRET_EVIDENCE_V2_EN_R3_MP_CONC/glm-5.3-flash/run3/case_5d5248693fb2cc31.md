### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of five alternative activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) was repositioned from before A28 to after A29: earlier it ran between A47 and A28 and flowed into A28; in the later window it runs after A29, is reached from A29 or A30, and flows into A30 or A34. The placement change is shown directly by the order reversal in all eight relation pairs, the successor rewiring of the five activities and of A29, A30 and A47, the one-step insertion between A29 and A34 together with the one-step removal between A47 and A28, and the position shift of the five activities while A28 and A29 stayed in place.
