### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, five activities — A19, A22, A23, A24 and A25 — each ran in a minority of cases (A19 and A22 in about 23 in 100 traces, A24 about 21, A25 about 20, A23 about 15) and never occurred together in one trace; their shares sum to roughly all cases with zero co-occurrence, so almost every case contained exactly one of them. [OCC001, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL003, REL031, REL039, REL044]
- In the later window these five have nearly disappeared (0–2 traces each), while five other activities — A20, A21, A30, A31 and A32, absent from the earlier window — now each appear in about 97–98 in 100 traces, once per trace. [OCC001, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC002, OCC003, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010]
- The five new activities always run together (every pair co-occurs in about 97.5 in 100 traces) and appear in both orders in similar proportions (for example A20 before A21 in about 46 in 100 traces, A21 before A20 in about 52), i.e. with no fixed order among them. [REL012, REL017, REL019, REL026, REL028, REL053, REL057]
- The new block sits between A48 and A33: A48 precedes each of the five in about 96–97 in 100 traces, and each of the five precedes A33 in about 96–97 in 100. The old alternatives occupied the same slot earlier: whenever A19, A22 or A24 occurred, A48 came before and A33 after in practically all such cases. [REL020, REL021, REL029, REL030, REL055, REL056, REL058, REL059, REL060, REL061, REL010, REL011, REL037, REL038, REL048, REL049]
- Successor behaviour moved accordingly: earlier, about 93 in 100 of A48's outgoing transitions went to one of the old five (A22 about 21%, A25 and A19 about 20% each, A24 about 19%, A23 about 14%), and A19, A22 and A24 led almost exclusively to A33; later, about 91 in 100 of A48's outgoing transitions go to the new five, and each of the new five leads to the other four and to A33 in similar shares (roughly 15–26% each). - Consistent with a block inserted between them, A33 now occurs later in the trace (mean relative position about 0.43 before vs about 0.48 after) and A48 earlier (about 0.36 vs about 0.31), while both still appear in every trace. [POS011, POS012]
- The few later-window occurrences of the old activities (1–2 traces each) show outgoing edges such as A19 to A07 and A22 to A05, but with two observed transitions each these cannot be interpreted. [DIR001, DIR004, OCC001, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A48 and A33 changed from a single-choice step into an always-executed block: in the earlier window each case executed exactly one of five alternative activities (A19, A22, A23, A24, A25 — together covering essentially all cases, each in about 15–23 in 100), whereas in the later window five activities A20, A21, A30, A31 and A32 are all executed in about 97–98 in 100 cases, in no fixed order among themselves, after A48 and before A33. Since the old activities are nearly absent later and the new ones absent earlier, this is a replacement of one group of activities by another — steps removed and steps added — not a re-weighting of unchanged paths. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, REL003, REL012, REL020, REL021, REL029, REL055, REL056, REL060, DIR009]

The volume of qualifying evidence backs this: 92 control-flow items qualified where about 1 would be expected if nothing had changed, far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces; and although the many relation items describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, participation, successor and relation indicators all point to the same single difference, and the opposite position shifts of A33 and A48 fit the inserted block. [OCC002, DIR009, POS002, POS011, POS012, REL012, REL020]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the five new activities are relabelled versions of the old five whose execution rule changed from "exactly one of five" to "all five", since only anonymous activity codes are available; and the 1–2 residual occurrences of the old activities in the later window are too few to establish whether the old alternative path still exists. [DIR001, DIR004, OCC001, OCC004]
