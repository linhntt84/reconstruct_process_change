### Observed differences

- The five activities A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 occur in every trace in both windows, but their repetition collapsed: in the earlier window each ran about 3.6 times per trace and repeated within about 70 in 100 traces, while in the later window each runs about once per trace, with repetition in only about 1–2 in 100 traces [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006].
- Ordering around this group became fixed. In the earlier window A30 came before A31, A36, A37 or A38 in only about 30 in 100 traces, with about 70 in 100 interleaved or repeated; in the later window A30 comes first in about 98 in 100 traces and interleaving has nearly disappeared (about 2 in 100) [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL009, REL013, REL016].
- Between A31, A36 and A37 there is no fixed order in either window — in the later window each pair occurs in both orders at roughly 50/50 shares — but the earlier interleaving (about 70 in 100) gave way to strictly separate executions (about 2 in 100) [REL007, REL008, REL012].
- Successive executions of these activities are now adjacent: transitions such as A31→A36, A31→A37, A36→A37 and A37→A36 fall at a distance of one position in about 58–68 in 100 later cases and at two positions in most of the rest, whereas earlier the gaps were spread across distances one to five; self-repetitions such as A31→A31 and A37→A37 all but vanished (about 125 observations earlier, 2 later) [LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG009, LAG010, LAG002, LAG011].
- A38's routing changed: in the earlier window about 71% of its observed outgoing transitions led to A30 and about 28% to A25; in the later window about 96% lead to A25 and about 2% to A30 [DIR002].
- The positions of A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 became much more uniform (spread of relative position about 0.15 earlier vs about 0.06 later) at a slightly earlier mean position, consistent with a single pass instead of repeated occurrences [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006].
- Secondary differences: A02 now appears in fewer traces (about 14 in 100, i.e. 27 of 200, vs about 5 in 100, i.e. 9 of 200); A06's outflow concentrated on A45 (about 42% vs 82% of its outgoing transitions) and A06 appears somewhat more often and later within traces; A44 leads to A06 about three times as often (about 9% vs 30%); A44 and A45 appear slightly less often (about 60 vs 55–56 in 100 traces) with less interleaving against the A30/A31/A36/A37/A38 group [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, DIR003, POS007, POS008, REL005, REL010, REL014, REL019].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- The evidence supports the hypothesis that the interleaved, repeated execution of the activity group A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 stopped: in the earlier window these activities each ran about 3.6 times per case in heavily interleaved fashion, whereas in the later window each runs exactly once per case in one consecutive pass — A30 first (about 98 in 100 traces), then A31, A36 and A37 back-to-back in varying order, and A38 last, continuing mainly to A25 (about 96% of its outgoing transitions) instead of returning to A30 (about 2%, down from about 71%) [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL012, REL013, REL016, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG009, LAG010, DIR002].
- The support is direct and consistent across indicator families: the repetition collapse is measured on all five activities with full participation (200 of 200 traces) in both windows, the ordering change is shown directly by relational evidence rather than inferred from position shifts, the lag evidence independently shows the activities became adjacent, and the routing change at A38 shows the return flow to A30 disappeared. The screening context also fits: the control-flow family produced 51 qualifying items against roughly 0.81 expected if nothing had changed, so the ensemble cannot be explained as random re-splitting noise [OCC002, OCC006, REL001, REL007, LAG003, LAG011, DIR002].
- Limitations: the two windows are finite samples, so the evidence cannot rule out that a non-repeating case variant replaced a repeating one in the case mix rather than a change within one shared behaviour; the many agreeing items largely reflect one underlying trace difference and are not independent confirmations; and several reverse-direction lag items rest on only 2–3 later observations, as do the rare new successors of A06 and A38, so those details are weakly supported [LAG005, LAG008, LAG012, LAG013, LAG014, DIR001, DIR002].
- Other differences: A02 participation dropped (about 14 → 5 in 100 traces, based on 27 vs 9 traces); A06 now sends most of its flow to A45, occurs slightly more often and later in traces, and receives more flow from A44; A44 and A45 occur slightly less often and interleave less with the A30/A31/A36/A37/A38 group [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, DIR003, POS007, POS008, REL005, REL006, REL010, REL011, REL014, REL015, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020].
