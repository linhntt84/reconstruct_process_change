### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural substitution at the start of the process: A01 was replaced by A03 between A02 and A07. Earlier, every trace executed A02→A01→A07 with A01 early in the case; later, A01 is entirely unobserved and every trace executes A02→A03→A07, with A03 at A01's former position and inheriting its relations (always preceded by A02, always followed by A07, running before A05). The change is shown directly by participation flipping from 200 of 200 traces to none and vice versa, the complete redirection of A02's outgoing flow, the A01→A07 edge being replaced by A03→A07, and near-identical position profiles. Chance is implausible as an overall explanation: 24 items qualified where roughly 0.12 would be expected without a change among the 25 tested control-flow units, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. One competing explanation cannot be ruled out: A01 may simply have been relabelled as A03 rather than removed and added — the two are behaviourally indistinguishable in this evidence. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, REL006, REL008, REL010]

Other differences: A06 was introduced as a new optional ending. About 47 in 100 later traces finish with A06 immediately after A05, while A05 otherwise still leads to A08 (about 53 in 100 of A05's outgoing transitions). Because A06 was not observed at all in the earlier window, this is an added path rather than a re-weighting between previously existing alternatives; relatedly, A05 no longer appears as a possible last step (earlier it had no observed successor in about half of the traces, with positions up to the trace end; later it always sits at position 0.8 with a successor), and the positions of A05 and A07 became fixed.
