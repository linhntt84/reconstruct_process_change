### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured around the group of activities that previously ran together: A02 is no longer observed in any trace (about 97 of 200 before), the A03–A14 branch is executed in far fewer cases (about 19 in 100 traces, down from about 49), and A07, A09 and A13 — which previously ran together with A02 — now run together with A10, whose participation roughly doubled (about 24 → 46 in 100 traces). The rerouting is visible on the successor side as well: A09, A15 and A06 now lead predominantly to A10 rather than A03, A14 leads to A13 rather than A07, and A08 leads to A03 rather than A10. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR010, DIR011, REL002, REL004, REL007, REL017, REL021, REL023]

This is not a shift in how often existing paths are taken: A02 is entirely absent from the later window and co-execution partners changed, so the pattern indicates that part of the process was removed or became unobserved while other activities changed their execution context. With 60 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.67 expected for an unchanged process, the ensemble is far beyond what random re-splits of a stable process produce. The main limitation is that the evidence shows the two windows side by side but cannot establish that the A02 disappearance and the rerouting are one coordinated modification rather than two changes that coincide; zero participation also means only that A02 was not observed in the later window, and several items (the A02 relation items together with the occurrence, position and direct-succession items for A02) describe the same trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, REL002, REL004, REL007]

Other differences: A11 now occurs slightly earlier within traces (mean position about 0.49 → 0.40) at essentially unchanged participation; A05's outgoing transitions changed (to A02 about 27 → 0 and to A10 about 5 → 0, while links to A03 and A14 appeared at about 16 each); and pairwise spacing shifted in several places, for example A03→A09 now falls about 3 steps after A03 in most cases (previously 1–2) and A14→A09 about 2 steps (previously 1).
