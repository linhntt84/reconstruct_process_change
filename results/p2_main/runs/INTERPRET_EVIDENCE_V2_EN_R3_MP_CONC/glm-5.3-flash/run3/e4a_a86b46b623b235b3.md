### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in timing: the elapsed time between consecutive completion events around A20 — for both the A14–A20 and the A15–A20 pairs — roughly doubled, from about one hour to about two hours. Supporting signs: both time items show the same ~1-hour upward shift across the entire distribution rather than a change in a few extreme cases, each rests on about 100 observations per window, each difference exceeds all but about 1 in 2,000 random re-splits of the pooled data, and the time family produced 2 qualifying items where fewer than 0.01 would be expected if nothing had changed — a gap chance cannot explain as a whole. The main limitation is that both pairs involve A20, so the two items likely reflect one underlying shift rather than two independent ones, and the pairwise elapsed times cannot show whether the added hour accumulated before or after A20's completion.
