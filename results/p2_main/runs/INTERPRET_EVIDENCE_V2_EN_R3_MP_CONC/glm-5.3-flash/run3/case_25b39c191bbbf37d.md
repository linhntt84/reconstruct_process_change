### Observed differences

- In the earlier window this part of the process was a rigid chain: A24, A25 and A26 each appeared in all 200 traces, A24 was always immediately followed by A25 and A25 by A26, and A24 always sat exactly two positions before A26. [DIR006, DIR007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- In the later window, five activities that were not observed at all earlier — A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 — each take part in about 17 to 23 in 100 traces (33 to 45 of 200), always exactly once per trace, around the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.5). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]
- A24 and A25 still appear in every later trace, but A24 now leads directly to A25 in only about 49 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (going to one of the five new activities otherwise), and A25 now leads directly to A26 in about 52 in 100 (going to a new activity otherwise). [DIR006, DIR007]
- Each new activity is followed by A25 or A26 in roughly even shares, for example A14 to A25 in about 53 in 100 cases and to A26 in 47, and A15 to A26 in about 55 in 100 and to A25 in 45. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- The new activities always follow A24 and always precede A26; against A25 both orders occur, with A14 before A25 in about 12 in 100 traces and A25 before A14 in about 10 in 100. [REL004, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL011, REL013, REL015]
- Pair distances shifted by one position: A24 to A26 is now three steps apart in about 995 in 1000 later traces (previously two in all traces), while A24→A25 and A25→A26 are each split between immediate and one activity apart, at about 49/51 and 52/48. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- These pieces fit together: the five participation shares sum to about 995 in 1000 traces, matching the share of later traces in which exactly one activity now lies between A24 and A26. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening tested 89 control-flow units and 34 items qualified, against about 0.44 expected if nothing had changed, so the volume of qualifying evidence alone exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces. The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, exactly one of five previously unobserved activities — A14, A15, A20, A21 or A22 — is executed in almost every case within the formerly fixed sequence A24 → A25 → A26, placed between A24 and A25 in roughly half of the cases and between A25 and A26 in the other half, so that A24 and A26 are now typically three positions apart. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001, DIR006, DIR007, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The support is direct and consistent across indicator families: the appearance of the new activities, the new outgoing edges of A24 and A25 and of the new activities themselves, their fixed position after A24 and before A26, and the one-position shift in pair distances all describe the same behaviour, and the numbers cohere almost exactly (the about 49 in 100 direct A24→A25 transitions match the share of A24→A25 pairs at distance one, and the summed participation matches the share of traces at the new three-step distance); every item also exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that the five activities were not observed there, and the evidence, based on event positions, cannot show what determines which of the five activities or which position is chosen — the choice is roughly but not exactly even, with A21 and A22 somewhat rarer. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that these activities existed as a rare path that the earlier 200-trace sample happened to miss and has only now become frequent, rather than being newly added. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG001, LAG002]

Other differences: none beyond the main hypothesis; the residual variation — the uneven split among the five activities and about 1 in 200 later traces with no such activity between A24 and A26 — belongs to the same behaviour. [OCC004, OCC005, LAG002]
