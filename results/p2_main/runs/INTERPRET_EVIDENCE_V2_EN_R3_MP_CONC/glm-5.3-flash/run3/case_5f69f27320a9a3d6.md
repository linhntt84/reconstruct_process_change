### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in which A01 was added to the process and A04 was dropped from it. Earlier, every case ran A02, then A05 and A06 in either order, then A03, A04 and A07; in the later window every case runs A02, then A01 together with A05 and A06 in varying order, then A03 directly followed by A07. The participation changes are categorical (0 vs 200 of 200 traces), the routing, positional, relational and lag items all agree with this single picture, and 34 control-flow items qualified where fewer than one would be expected by chance in a stable process, each difference larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, DIR003, REL004, LAG004].

Limitations: the evidence shows execution behaviour, so it cannot distinguish a genuine addition of A01 or removal of A04 from A01 becoming always executed and A04 becoming never executed; positions are event positions, not clock times, so no movement in time is implied. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 already existed in the process without ever being triggered before, and A04 still exists but is never taken — behaviourally indistinguishable here. The evidence also shows only that A01 joined the A05/A06 segment: its order relative to A05 and A06 varies from case to case, so no single fixed placement can be inferred [REL004, REL005].
