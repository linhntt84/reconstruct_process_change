### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the five-activity group A35, A36, A41, A42, A43 is executed at a different place in the later window: previously it ran after A26 (and after A27) and exited to A30, whereas in the later window it runs directly after A25 and before A27 and A26, with A26 now passing straight to A30. The support is direct and consistent: all ten measured order relations between A26/A27 and the group members reversed (from 97–99 in 100 one way to 96–98 in 100 the other), the successor profiles of A25, A26 and all five group members changed accordingly, and event positions and five-step co-occurrences shifted in the same direction. Screening also makes chance an unlikely account of the pattern as a whole: 85 control-flow items qualified where roughly 1 would be expected for an unchanged process. Limitations: the evidence shows relative order only, so it cannot distinguish the group moving earlier from A26/A27 moving later; positions and lags are sequence positions, not clock times; and no outgoing profile for A27 qualified, so the exact connection from the group via A27 onwards is not directly observed.
