### Observed differences

- The clearest change is in how often a middle group of activities runs. Earlier, A19 and A20 were executed in every one of the 200 cases, and A25, A26 and A27 each in about 50 in 100 cases; in the later window A19 and A20 each appear in about 52 in 100 cases, and A25, A26 and A27 in about 26 in 100 each. - Orderings are preserved whenever the activities do run: A43 still precedes A19 and A20, A19 still precedes A20, A19 and A20 still precede A28 and A49, A25 and A26 still run together in that order, A27 still never runs together with A25 or A26, and A20 still leads to A28 in about 97 in 100 of its outgoing transitions. Earlier, A43 was followed by A19 in about 96 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and never led directly to A28; in the later window A43 leads directly to A28 in about 46 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, and the distance from A43 to A28 fell from 4–5 steps in essentially all cases to 1 step in about 48 in 100 cases. [DIR002, DIR007, POS001, LAG001, LAG004, REL006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the middle segment of the process — A19 and A20 together with the alternative branch A25→A26 or A27, which earlier every case executed between A43 and A28 — is not executed in about half of the later cases, with A43 then leading directly to A28. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the participation of all five activities halved in the same window; the missing cases show up as "neither activity present" rather than as a reordering, since all internal orderings are unchanged when the segment runs; a direct A43→A28 edge appears that did not exist earlier; and the A43→A28 distance falls to 1 step in about 48 in 100 cases, matching the participation drop. The volume of qualifying evidence (59 control-flow items against roughly 1 expected without a change) makes a chance explanation of the overall picture implausible. Limitations: the evidence shows the bypass from A43 onwards but cannot show what decides whether a case skips the segment, since nothing upstream of A43 is flagged, and the exact skip share is uncertain in two 200-case samples. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL010, REL011, DIR005, LAG003]

Other differences: A06's decline and repositioning (from about 32 to about 10 in 100 cases, and no longer running between A48 and A49) is not part of the main hypothesis; its new placement rests on only about 19 later cases and about 21 outgoing transitions, so the exact new routing of A06 remains open. [OCC001, DIR002, POS001, LAG001] A04's outgoing profile changed completely between the windows, but only about 11 outgoing transitions were observed in each window, so this is weak evidence. [DIR001]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window mixes in a case type that reaches A28 without this segment because of routing decided upstream of A43, rather than the segment itself being skipped conditionally within one and the same flow. [REL010, DIR005, LAG003]
