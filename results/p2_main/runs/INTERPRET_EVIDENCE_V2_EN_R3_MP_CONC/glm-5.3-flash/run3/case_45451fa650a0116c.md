### Observed differences

- The branch after A20 flipped. In the earlier window, about 82 in 100 of A20's outgoing transitions led into the block {A24, A25, A26} (A24 ~29%, A26 ~28%, A25 ~24%) and only about 19 in 100 into {A21, A22, A23}; in the later window, about 78 in 100 lead into {A21, A22, A23} (A23 ~29%, A21 ~28%, A22 ~21%) and about 21 in 100 into {A24, A25, A26}. A20 itself runs in essentially every trace in both windows [DIR005, REL011, REL014].
- Participation swapped accordingly: A21, A22 and A23 each appeared in about 19–20 in 100 traces before and about 79–80 in 100 in the later window (e.g., A21: about 39 vs 158 traces), while A24, A25 and A26 fell from about 81 in 100 to about 21–22 in 100 (e.g., A24: about 163 vs 42 traces). Each block keeps its internal shape in both windows — its three activities occur together and in no fixed order (A21/A22 co-occurrence 0.185 → 0.79, both orders near 0.4 in the later window) — and the blocks remain mutually exclusive alternatives (A21 with A24/A25/A26 co-occur in about 1 in 100 traces before and essentially never in the later window) [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL017, REL018, REL025, REL019, REL026, REL032].
- Downstream companions switched in step. Before, A24 preceded A34 in about 80 in 100 traces (A21 in about 20) and A30 travelled with the A24–A26 block (co-occurrence about 0.81); in the later window A21, A22 and A23 each precede A34 in about 77–79 in 100 traces, and A30 travels with the A21–A23 block (co-occurrence about 0.79) [REL041, REL042, REL023, REL024, REL030, REL031, REL022, REL029, REL035, REL040].
- The tail towards A35 changed. A34 is present in all traces in both windows near the end, but before, about 56 in 100 of its outgoing transitions went to A35 and about 44 in 100 to A06; in the later window about 90 in 100 go to A35, at a distance of one event in about 92 in 100 cases (about 56 before), while exits to A06 fell to about 2 in 100 [DIR006, LAG006].
- A06 receded. Before, it appeared in about 46 in 100 traces, typically late (mean event position about 0.91), usually after A34 (A34 first in about 42 in 100 traces) and leading to A35 in about 92 in 100 of its outgoing transitions; in the later window it appears in about 12 in 100 traces, earlier (mean position about 0.52), its co-occurrence with the A24–A26 block fell from about 38 to about 4 in 100 traces, and its exits spread over many targets (A15 ~23%, A33 ~15%, A35 ~15%, based on about 26 outgoing transitions) [OCC003, POS001, DIR001, REL005, REL009].
- Smaller shifts: A02 (about 8 → 17 in 100 traces) and A04 (about 5 → 19 in 100) became somewhat more frequent, though both remain rare [OCC001, OCC002].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the dominant route between A20 and A35 was rerouted: traces that previously ran A20 → {A24, A25, A26} → A34 and then typically through A06 to A35 now predominantly run A20 → {A21, A22, A23} → A34 → A35. Concretely, the share of A20's outgoing transitions entering the A21–A23 block rose from about 19 to about 78 in 100 while the A24–A26 block fell from about 82 to about 21 in 100; A06's participation fell from about 46 to about 12 in 100 traces and it is no longer reached from A34, which now passes almost directly to A35. Because both blocks, A06 and A34 are observed with substantial support in both windows, the blocks' internal structure and their exclusivity are unchanged, and only the shares between the existing alternatives shift, this reads as existing alternative paths being taken with different frequencies, plus A06 being largely skipped — not as activities being added, removed or moved [DIR005, OCC004, OCC007, OCC003, REL011, REL014, REL017, REL019, REL023, REL041, DIR006, LAG006, DIR001].

This is unlikely to be a sampling artifact: the screening qualified 73 items where fewer than 1 (0.9) would be expected if nothing had changed, the branch shares at A20 rest on about 200 outgoing transitions per window, and the rarer block still has about 38–44 supporting traces in each window. Limitations: the many indicators describe largely the same traces and are not independent confirmations; A06's post-change successors rest on only about 26 outgoing transitions; and the evidence cannot distinguish a modified routing decision from a changed mix of two pre-existing process variants, which is the main competing explanation it cannot rule out [DIR005, OCC004, OCC007, DIR001].

Other differences: A02 and A04 became modestly more frequent (about 8 → 17 and 5 → 19 in 100 traces), which the rerouting hypothesis does not cover [OCC001, OCC002].
