### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Main hypothesis: the evidence supports an exchange of execution order between two blocks of activities. The block A14, A18, A21, A22, A23, which earlier ran early in each trace — before A24 and A28 and before the second block — and flowed into A24, in the later window runs late, after A28 and after A15, A16, A17, A19 and A20, and flows into A29; the block A15, A16, A17, A19, A20 moved from after A28 to before A24 and now flows into A24, with A28 and A39 each feeding the opposite block than before. [REL012, REL013, REL001, POS001, POS002, POS005, DIR001, DIR011, DIR012]

Supporting signs: the order change is shown directly by the relation items, including ten pairs that flip from a 100%-fixed order to the opposite fixed order, and it agrees across all 45 relation items, all 12 direction profiles and all 12 position items; stable participation rules out the blocks becoming optional, repeated or replaced; and the screening flagged 69 control-flow and 3 time items where about 0.65 and 0.015 would be expected without a change, a volume an unchanged process does not produce. [REL001-REL045, DIR001-DIR012, POS001-POS012, TWAIT001-TWAIT003]

Limitations: the position indicator uses event positions within the trace, not clock time, so the exchange concerns execution order and does not show when work happens in wall-clock terms; the evidence also cannot fully distinguish one exchange of the two blocks from two independent relocations that mirror each other, and the findings for A14, A18, A21, A22 and A23 rest on about 32–46 traces per activity per window.
