### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the previously mandatory sequence containing A36, A37, A42, A43, A44 and A27 became optional and split into two mutually exclusive branches: in the earlier window every trace ran the block and then A27, whereas in the later window about 45 in 100 traces run A27 without the block, about 54 in 100 run the block without A27, and almost none run both. The surrounding flow fits one coherent rewiring: A25 now enters A28 directly instead of the block, the block flows on to A31 instead of A28, A28 now precedes the branch and A26 precedes both A27 and the block instead of sitting between them, while A28 and A31 still occur in every trace and frame the branch. [OCC004, OCC009, REL047, REL049, REL057, REL062, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]

The supporting signs are direct and converging: participation fell from all 200 traces to 92–112; A27's co-occurrence with each block member collapsed from 100 to about 1–2 in 100; the order reversals around A26 and A28; and the new successions are visible in within-five-step lags (A25→A36 fell from 198 to 1 observations, A26→A44 rose from 1 to 107, A44→A31 from 1 to 104). Screening qualified 152 control-flow items where about 1.5 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the ensemble is far beyond what random re-splits of a stable process produce. [OCC004, OCC009, REL049, REL042, REL057, DIR005, DIR007, LAG011, LAG016, LAG022]

Limitations: many relation and lag items restate the same trace-level change and are not independent confirmations; the 45/55 branch split is a sample estimate from 200 traces per window; and the evidence cannot show whether the either-or split is one new routing decision per case or a mixture of two case populations that the earlier window did not contain. [REL049, LAG008]

Other differences: A10's early-stage role largely disappeared (25 instead of 77 in 100 traces, with A14 now bypassing it towards A15), and A02 and A08 occur in roughly three to four times as many traces as before with more varied successors; the available relations do not tie these to the block/A27 restructuring.
