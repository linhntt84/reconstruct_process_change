### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of alternative activities A19, A20 and A25 — which earlier acted as alternatives between A45 and A26 — was relocated. In the earlier window it sat between A45 and A26 (A45 → one of A19/A20/A25 → A26, with A25 immediately before A26 in about 63 traces); in the later window A45 leads directly to A26, and A19, A20 and A25 execute in the later segment after A27 and A28, from where they lead into A28 or A32 and interleave with A28, in some traces forming a two-way A28–trio succession. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, LAG003, LAG008, LAG006, REL003, REL008]

The hypothesis is direct and consistent across the indicator families: the successor profiles of A19, A20, A25 and A45 changed almost completely, the execution order relative to both A26 and A27 reversed in all six pairs, the trio's mean position moved later by about a quarter of the trace while A26 and A27 moved slightly earlier, and the direct A25→A26 succession disappeared. With 27 qualifying items against roughly 0.63 expected for an unchanged process, the ensemble cannot be explained as a sampling artefact of the two windows. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG003]

Limitations: the items are strongly interdependent — the six order reversals and several successor and distance changes describe the same underlying movement, so they do not confirm it independently. The evidence also cannot distinguish one coordinated relocation from two coupled routing changes (A45 rerouted to A26 while the trio was rerouted into the A27/A28 segment), the loop-like repetition in the later window rests partly on distance items with only a single before-window observation, and the positional evidence reflects event positions rather than clock times. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG006]

Other differences: participation within the trio shifted slightly — A19 appears in about 31 in 100 later traces versus about 38 earlier, A25 in about 38 versus about 32, A20 roughly unchanged — a secondary effect not needed for the relocation hypothesis. [POS001, POS002, POS003]
