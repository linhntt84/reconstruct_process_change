### Observed differences

- A01 is a new presence: in the earlier window it appears in 0 of 200 traces; in the later window it occurs in every trace, exactly once per trace, always at the same event position (about two-thirds of the way through the trace) [OCC001, POS001].
- In the earlier window, A02's outgoing transitions went directly to A03 in about 77 in 100 cases and to A04 in about 23 in 100; in the later window, A02 goes to A01 in about 76 in 100 cases and to A04 in about 24 in 100, with no direct transition to A03 observed [DIR002].
- A04 shows the same redirection: earlier it led to A02 (about 78 in 100) or directly to A03 (about 23 in 100); later it leads to A02 (about 76 in 100) or to A01 (about 24 in 100) [DIR003].
- A01's only observed outgoing transition is to A03, in 100 in 100 cases [DIR001].
- Relationally, A01 never co-occurred with A02, A03 or A04 in the earlier window; in the later window it co-occurs with each in every trace, always after A02 and after A04, and always before A03 [REL001, REL002, REL003].
- The sequence distance from A02 to A03 grew by exactly one step: earlier it was 1 step in about 77 in 100 traces (2 steps in the rest); later it is 2 steps in about 76 in 100 (3 steps in the rest). The distance from A04 to A03 likewise shifted from 1–2 steps to 2–3 steps [LAG001, LAG002].
- A02's typical event position moved slightly earlier (median from 0.5 to about 0.33), consistent with a step appearing after it [POS002].
- The choice between the two orderings of A02 and A04 barely moved: about 77.5/22.5 earlier versus 76/24 later [DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Main hypothesis: A01 appears as an additional step between the A02/A04 pair and A03. In the earlier window, traces ran either A04 → A02 → A03 (about 77 in 100) or A02 → A04 → A03 (about 23 in 100); in the later window the same two orderings of A02 and A04 persist with nearly unchanged frequencies, but both A02 and A04 now lead to A01, and A01 always leads directly into A03 [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002].

The support is direct and consistent: the direct A02→A03 and A04→A03 transitions disappear in favour of transitions to A01; A01's sole outgoing edge is to A03; every A02/A04-to-A03 distance increases by exactly one step, which is what one expects when a step is added between them; and the co-occurrence relations place A01 after both A02 and A04 but before A03. The 11 qualifying items (all control-flow) far exceed the roughly 0.06 expected by chance for 12 tested units, so sampling variation alone cannot explain the set as a whole [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002].

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly means A01 was not observed there, not that it was impossible, so the evidence cannot fully rule out that A01 occurred at a very low, unobserved rate before [OCC001]. Positions are event positions rather than clock times, so the finding concerns sequence structure, not timing [POS001, POS002].

Other differences: the split between the two orderings of A02 and A04 shifted only marginally (about 77.5/22.5 to 76/24), a difference well within what two 200-trace samples can show by chance, so it is not treated as a separate change [DIR002, DIR003].
