### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the recurring A01–A04–A06 sequence: in the earlier window the flow ran A01→A06→A04 and back to A01, with an exit from A06 to A05; in the later window it runs A01→A04→A06 and back to A01, with the same exit from A06 to A05. This is shown directly by the successor changes of all three activities and is corroborated by the positional shifts, the exchanged participation, and the flipped order relations and gaps; all 16 qualifying items fit this single reordering, against roughly 0.1 items expected by chance across the 20 tested units, so chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole.
