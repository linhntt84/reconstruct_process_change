### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a specific possibility: a change in resource allocation, under which work on activities A01, A03, A13, A14, A17 and A22 was reassigned from one group of resources to another in the later window, with User_49 among the main gainers (from about 4–6 in 100 assignments to about 10–19 in 100 per activity) and User_3 and User_37 among the main losers.
