### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural reconfiguration of the flow between A03 and A04: A01, which earlier formed a small fragment A14 → A01 → A04 in about 20 in 100 traces, is no longer observed, A14 now feeds A04 directly, and a new segment A02 → (A12, looping back to A02) → A13 → A04 runs between A03 and A04, replacing the former direct A03 → A04 route (about 46% of A03's outgoing transitions earlier, about 1% later) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG004]

The participation shifts are large and backed by many traces (A01 falls from 40 to 0 of 200 traces; A02 rises from 0 to 95; A12 and A09 from 0 to 51 each), the successor-profile changes are observed directly rather than inferred, and the relational and gap evidence agrees with the rerouting (A12 and A13 occur only alongside A02; the A14→A04 gap shortens from two steps to one); every item also exceeds the differences seen in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, and 54 items qualified against roughly 0.36 expected if nothing had changed, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole [OCC001, OCC002, DIR003, REL012, REL013, LAG004]

Limitations: positions and gaps are measured in event steps, so the "between A03 and A04" reading concerns sequence order rather than clock time; and the window-level evidence cannot rule out that the two windows draw on different case variants — one carrying the A01 fragment disappearing while one carrying the A02 segment appears — rather than a modification applied within a single process [OCC001, OCC002, POS003]
