### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of five mutually exclusive activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22 — one per case) was relocated: it sat between A42 and A23 in the earlier window and sits after A24 in the later window, inserted either directly after A24 (before A25) or directly after A25 (before A29) in roughly equal shares, while the surrounding chain A42 → A23 → A24 → A25 → A29 kept its order; the small backward shifts of A23, A24 and A25 are consistent with one event being removed from before them. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Supporting signs: the change is shown directly by successor profiles (A42 now always goes to A23; the five now go to A25 or A29; A24 and A25 gained transitions to the five), by the complete order flip in all eight pairs with A23/A24, by the position shift of the five from about 0.3 to about 0.6, and by the gap changes; 28 control-flow items qualified against about 0.43 expected without any change, so chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole, and the stable participation of the five (one per trace in both windows) fits a move rather than the activities becoming optional, mandatory or repeated differently. [DIR001, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS005, REL001, REL008, LAG001, LAG004]

Limitations: positions are event positions, not clock time; the windows cannot distinguish a literal move of the same occurrence from removing the early block and inserting a new occurrence later, and the evidence shows but cannot explain why the block lands after A24 in about half of the later cases and after A25 in the other half, with that split varying by activity.
