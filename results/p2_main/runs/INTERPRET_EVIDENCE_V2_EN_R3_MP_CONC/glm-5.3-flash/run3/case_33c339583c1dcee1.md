### Observed differences

- **Order flip around a five-activity group.** In the earlier window, A35, A36, A41, A42 and A43 each ran before A26 and before A27 in about 99 of 100 traces; in the later window, A26 and A27 each run before these five activities in about 98 of 100. Both sides still occur together in every trace in both windows, so only their order changed, not their participation. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020]

- **Rewired successors.** Earlier, A25 spread its outgoing transitions nearly evenly over A35, A36, A41, A42 and A43 (about 18–22% each) and A26 led directly to A30 in about 98 of 100 transitions, while each group activity led on to A27 in roughly 17–21% of its outgoing transitions. In the later window, A25 leads to A27 in about 99 of 100 transitions, A26 spreads over the five group activities (about 16–21% each), and the group leads to A30 (about 18–21% each) while A27 has practically disappeared as its target. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009]

- **Consistent position shifts** (event positions, not clock times): A27's median position moved from about 0.53 to 0.38 of the trace and A26's from about 0.67 to 0.52, while A35, A36, A41, A42 and A43 each moved from a median of about 0.44 to about 0.60. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

- **A02 became rarer and later.** It appeared in about 63 of 200 traces earlier versus about 20 of 200 later (participation down from about 32% to 10%); when present, its median position moved from about 0.32 to 0.75 with much higher spread, and its outgoing transitions shifted from being dominated by A19 (about 38%) and A20 (about 46%) to a spread over many activities — the later profile rests on only about 22 outgoing transitions. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001]

- **Smaller differences.** A06 appeared in about 6 of 200 traces earlier and about 24 of 200 later (about 3% to 12%, based on small counts). Several lag distributions changed, but most rest on only 1–3 after-window observations (for example A25→A36 now always at distance 1 from just 2 observations); a better-supported minor change is A46→A25 concentrating at distance 2 (about 70% up to 93%). [OCC002, LAG011, LAG022, LAG020, LAG021]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A27 and A30 exchanged their places in the control flow while the group A35/A36/A41/A42/A43 moved from before A26/A27 to after them: earlier, A25 fanned out into the group, the group led on to A27, and A26 led directly to A30; in the later window, A25 leads to A27, A26 leads into the group, and the group leads on to A30. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, REL011, REL016, REL018, POS002, POS003, POS008]

The signs are direct and consistent: the order flip appears in about 98–99 of 100 traces on both sides with co-occurrence unchanged at 100%, so it is not a participation effect; all seven affected source activities show the same successor rewiring; and the position shifts match the flip. The 65 qualifying control-flow items are roughly 65 times the number expected if nothing had changed (about 1), so chance alone cannot account for the set. Limitations: the outgoing edges of A27 and A30 themselves were not among the tested units, so the rewiring around them is inferred from their partners' profiles, and most lag-based distances rest on 1–3 after-window observations. [REL011, REL018, DIR003, DIR009, POS003, POS008, LAG011, LAG012]

Other differences: A02's decline in participation (about 32% to 10% of traces) with a later, more variable position and changed successors (the latter weakly supported); A06's rise (about 3% to 12%); and a modest tightening of the A46→A25 lag. The evidence cannot show whether A02's decline is part of the same change as the reordering or an independent one. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, OCC002, LAG022]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a broader resequencing of the middle of the process (including the entry points A25 and A26) rather than a strict two-activity swap of A27 and A30, since the edges out of A27 and A30 are unobserved. [DIR003, DIR004]
