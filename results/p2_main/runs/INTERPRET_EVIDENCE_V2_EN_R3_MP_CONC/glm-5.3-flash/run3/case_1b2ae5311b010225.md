### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the alternative block of activities A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 — which earlier executed after A29 and A30 in roughly one in five traces — is almost never executed, leaving A29 → A30 → A31 as the dominant route. The participation loss is observed directly (each of the five falls from about 34–47 to 0–2 of 200 traces), the severed relationships are observed directly (co-occurrence with ever-present activities collapses from about 23–24% to about 1%), and the rerouting at A29 and A30, including the new immediate handovers and the A28→A31 pair at five steps, is read off the observed outgoing transitions rather than inferred; the altered outgoing profiles of the vanished activities themselves add no independent weight. With 87 qualifying items against about 1 expected without change, sampling variation cannot explain the pattern as a whole. What the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows draw on different case mixes or process variants rather than a single modified process, and a near-zero participation strictly shows only that the activities were not observed.
