### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the segment A30 → A31 → A36 is repeated rather than executed once per case: the block runs about 3.5 times per case on average, driven by a new dominant return edge A36 → A30 (about 71 in 100 of A36's outgoing transitions, versus about 0.5 before), while the exit to A25 falls from about 98 to about 28 in 100. Occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators all describe this same loop, and the screening found 19 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.5 expected without a change, far beyond what random re-splits of a stable process produce. A limitation: about 31 in 100 later cases still show a single pass, and the evidence cannot distinguish whether every case can now repeat the block a varying number of times or whether only a subpopulation of cases repeats it at all.
