### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, A01 was added as a new step that runs exactly once per case, early in the flow and typically between A02 and A04. The signs are direct and consistent: participation jumps from 0 to 200 of 200 traces with a mean of one occurrence; A02 routes a majority of its outgoing transitions to A01 while its direct edge to A04 shrinks; the A02→A04 distance lengthens by about one step, as expected when a step is inserted between them; A01 co-occurs with A02 and A04 in every later trace; and downstream activities (A03, A06, A07, A10) shift later by roughly one step. [OCC001, DIR002, LAG002, REL001, REL003, POS001, POS004, POS007]

The screening comparison supports treating this as more than sampling noise: 34 items qualified from 57 tested units against fewer than 1 expected without change, and the qualifying items agree across occurrence, direction, position, relation and distance indicators rather than resting on a single measure. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL003, POS001, POS006]

Two limitations remain. The insertion point is not one fixed slot — A01 precedes A04 in 55 of 100 traces but follows it in 45 — and the evidence cannot rule out that A01 is a renamed successor of an earlier activity rather than a genuinely new one, since only behaviour, not activity identity, is observed. [REL003, DIR001]

Other differences: A09 was not observed in the later window after appearing in 45 of 100 earlier traces near the end of the flow, usually after A10 and reached from A06; zero participation shows only that it was not observed, so removal and a fall to extreme rarity cannot be distinguished.
