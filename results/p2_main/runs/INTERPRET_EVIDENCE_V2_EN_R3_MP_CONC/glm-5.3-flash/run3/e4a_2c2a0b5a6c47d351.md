### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative branch consisting of A14, A15 and A16 — of which exactly one was executed per case between A39 and A24 — was replaced by a block in which A21, A22 and A23 are each executed exactly once per case, in varying order, between A39 and A24. In the earlier window every case ran A39, then one of the three alternatives (about 35, 30 and 36 in 100 cases respectively), then A24; in the later window every case runs A39, then all three of A21, A22 and A23 in no fixed order, then A24. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, REL019, REL020, REL023, REL021, REL024, REL026, LAG001, POS007]

The support is direct and consistent: the participation changes are maximal and backed by full samples on both sides (0 versus 200 traces); A39's outgoing transitions switched wholesale from the old trio to the new one; the relational items show the new trio co-occurring in every trace, always after A39 and always before A24, with a mixed internal order; and the lag and position items corroborate the insertion of two additional activities between A39 and A24. Screening found 48 control-flow items where about 0.5 would be expected by chance, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process produces. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL019, REL021, REL022, LAG001, POS007]

Limitations: zero participation only means the old activities were not observed, so complete removal versus the old branch becoming extremely rare cannot be distinguished; and whether the new three-activity block is mandatory in the process, or whether the near-50/50 internal ordering hides a mild preference, cannot be settled from 200 traces per window. [OCC001, OCC004, REL019]

Other differences: the elapsed times between consecutive completions changed independently of structure — A24 to A31 halved to about 0.46 hours and A32 to A31 shortened to about 0.10 hours; the evidence does not link these timing changes to the structural replacement above. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
