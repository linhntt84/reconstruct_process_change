### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new, always-executed block of activities — A20, A21, A30, A31 and A32 — now runs between A48/A04 and A33, while the former minority branch (A19, A22, A23, A24, A25), which ran in roughly 14–27 in 100 cases and fed A33, is no longer observed. The ordering claims rest on direct relational evidence: A48 immediately followed by A04, A48 and A04 preceding the block, and the block preceding A33, A53 and A54, together with the successor changes at A48 and A04 and the opposite position shifts of A04 (earlier) and A33 (later); occurrence, successor, position and relation indicators tell the same story, and the count of qualifying items (about 130 against roughly 1 expected without change) is far beyond what an unchanged process produces. Many relation items, however, restate the same block behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, DIR013, POS001, POS012, LAG001, REL012, REL024, REL032]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the old branch still exists but was not sampled in the later window, since zero participation only means the activities were not observed in those 200 traces; the evidence also cannot show that the new block was introduced in place of the old branch rather than the two differences arising separately.
