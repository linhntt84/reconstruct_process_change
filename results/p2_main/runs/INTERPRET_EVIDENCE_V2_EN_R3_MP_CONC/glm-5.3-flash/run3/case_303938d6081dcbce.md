### Observed differences

- Six activities became mandatory: before, A27 and each of A36, A37, A42, A43 and A44 appeared in only about 49–53 of 100 traces (once per trace when present); in the later window, each appears in 100 of 100 traces, still about once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- Entry routing changed at two points. Before, about 48 of 100 outgoing edges of A26 led to A27 and about 52 of 100 led to one of the five group activities; in the later window, about 98 of 100 lead to A27. Before, about 98 of 100 outgoing edges of A25 led to A28; in the later window nearly all (about 99 of 100) lead into the group, most often A37 (about 24 of 100) and A42/A44 (about 21–22 each). [DIR002, DIR001]
- The group's exit changed: before, about 14–27 of 100 outgoing edges of each group activity led to A31; in the later window these edges lead to A28 instead (about 14–24 of 100). A36 or A43 followed by A28 within five steps was seen in only 1 trace before versus about 197 traces in the later window. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG003]
- A27 and the group went from alternatives to always executed together: before, they co-occurred in only 1–2 of 100 traces (usually exactly one of them ran per trace); in the later window they co-occur in 100 of 100 traces, with the group before A27 in about 98–99 of 100. [REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019]
- The group's order relative to A28 and A26 reversed: before, A28 preceded the group (about 51 of 100 traces) and the group followed A26 (about 51 of 100); in the later window the group precedes A28 in about 99–100 of 100 and precedes A26 in about 99 of 100. A28 now also precedes A27 in about 99–100 of 100 traces, up from about 48 of 100. [REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL008, REL013, REL007]
- A31 is now reached after A27 rather than directly after the group: A42 followed by A31 within five steps fell from about 102 traces to 2, while A27 still precedes A31 in about 98–99 of 100 traces in both windows and remains A27's dominant successor (about 96–98 of 100) throughout. [LAG002, REL014, DIR003, REL025]
- Event positions moved accordingly: the group shifted from about 0.58–0.59 to about 0.39–0.40 of the trace, A28 from about 0.39 to 0.48, A26 from 0.53 to 0.61 and A27 from 0.59 to 0.63, while A25 moved slightly earlier (0.35 to 0.32). [POS006, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010, POS004, POS002, POS003, POS001]
- Inside the group, the five activities remain mutually unordered in both windows (each pair occurs in both orders in substantial shares, roughly 40–60 of 100 each), but pairs that co-occurred in about 52 of 100 traces before now always co-occur. [REL030, REL037, REL039]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural reorganisation around A27 and the activity group A36, A37, A42, A43, A44. In the earlier window, a trace ran A25, then A28, then A26, and then took one of two alternatives — A27 in about half of the traces or the group in the other half — before reaching A31. In the later window, every trace runs A25, then the whole group, then A28, then A26 and A27, with A31 following A27: the group became a mandatory stage placed before A28, A27 became mandatory as well, and the former either/or routing at A26 disappeared. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, REL015, REL020, REL013, LAG002]

This rests on direct relational evidence rather than position shifts alone: the switch from mutually exclusive to always co-occurring with a fixed order is observed directly between A27 and each group member, the order reversals against A28 and A26 appear in both windows, and the successor changes at A25, A26 and all five group activities are consistent with them. A25, A26, A28 and A31 were present in every trace in both windows, so the change concerns when the group and A27 run, not whether these anchor activities occur. With 66 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected by chance among the 178 tested units (all in the control-flow family), chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [REL015, REL020, REL008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005]

Limitations: the step distances of the new group→A28 adjacency are established only for the later window (before-window support of 1 trace each), POS and LAG measure event positions and step counts rather than clock time, and the internal order of the group stays mixed — so the evidence cannot rule out that the five activities form a loosely ordered set rather than one fixed sequential stage, and the residual minority orders (about 1–2 of 100 traces) could reflect a rare variant. [LAG001, LAG003, REL030, REL016]
