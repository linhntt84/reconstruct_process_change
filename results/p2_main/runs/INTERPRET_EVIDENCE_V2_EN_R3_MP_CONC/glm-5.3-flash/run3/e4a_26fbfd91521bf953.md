### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a change in timing around A13: in the later window, the elapsed time between consecutive completion events roughly doubled for both observed predecessor pairs, A11→A13 (mean about 0.9→1.8 hours) and A12→A13 (mean about 0.17→0.32 hours). The signs are direct and consistent: the time family produced 2 qualifying items where only about 0.006 were expected if nothing had changed (12 units tested), a count many times the chance expectation; both pairs move in the same direction with similar relative size; each difference exceeds all but about 1 of 2,000 random re-splits; and the counts behind them are moderate (63 vs 50 and 56 vs 41 occurrences). The main limitation is that TWAIT measures elapsed time between consecutive completions, so the evidence cannot distinguish longer waiting from longer processing or a different clock-time placement of A13 — that is the competing explanation it cannot rule out. Since both items share A13, they most likely reflect one underlying change rather than two independent ones. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
