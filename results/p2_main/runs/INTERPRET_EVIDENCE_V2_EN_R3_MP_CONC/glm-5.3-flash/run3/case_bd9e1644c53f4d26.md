### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A02, was introduced into the flow and became a mandatory, once-per-case step. In the earlier window A02 never appeared; in the later window it runs in every case, early in the trace, receives transitions from A01, A03 and A06, and hands control on to A04, A06, A01, A03, A05 and A07 in shares that mirror A01's earlier outgoing profile. It is woven between the existing activities rather than appended at the end: its order relative to A01, A03 and A06 is unfixed (both orders occur in similar proportions), it usually precedes A04, and the step distances from A01, A03 and A06 to A04 grew, consistent with A02 sitting between them in part of the cases. Thirteen control-flow items qualified where about 0.14 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by sampling; the items are direct (participation, successor and relation changes on A02 itself) and mutually consistent, and the other observed activities continue to appear in a similar share of traces in both windows. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: because A02 occurs in both orders relative to A01, A03 and A06, the evidence cannot pin it to a single fixed position in the sequence; POS and LAG use event positions rather than clock time, so placement statements concern order within the trace only; and a relabelling of a previously unobserved step as A02 cannot be fully excluded, although nothing was removed and no other activity's participation dropped.
