### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the either/or choice after A21 was replaced by a single flow: the A31/A32/A37 group, which in the earlier window alternated with A22 (group in about 46 in 100 traces, A22 in about 54) and ran between A21 and A26, in the later window always executes directly after A20 and before A23, while A22 became mandatory in its previous slot between A21 and A26. In short, both previously optional segments became mandatory and the group was relocated from after A21 to between A20 and A23. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL006, REL011, REL014, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, POS006]

This rests on direct relational and successor evidence rather than on frequency shifts alone: the group's predecessor changed from A21 to A20 and its successor from A26 to A23, A21 now leads exclusively to A22, the A20–A23 gap grew from one to four positions, and A22 went from never co-occurring with the group to always co-occurring — a pattern sampling variation in 200-trace windows cannot plausibly produce. All 41 qualifying items belong to the control-flow family, against roughly 0.5 expected by chance, and every item shows empirical strength near 0.995. [REL011, REL012, REL013, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the earlier window's two variants corresponded to different case types, so a change in case mix rather than a redesigned single flow cannot be ruled out; positions are event positions, not clock times, so no conclusion about clock-time timing is possible. [DIR001, POS001]

Other differences: about 1 in 200 later traces still routes A20 directly to A23, a remnant of the earlier edge, and the mean relative positions of A20 (about 0.36 to 0.34) and A26 (about 0.63 to 0.66) shifted only slightly. [DIR001, POS001, POS005]
