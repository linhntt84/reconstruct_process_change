### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The screening flagged 63 control-flow items where fewer than one would be expected by chance among 161 tested units, so the volume of flagged behaviour cannot be explained by random re-splits of an unchanged process, and the items describe one coherent restructuring rather than scattered noise. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, REL001, REL007, REL018]

A supported hypothesis is a participation and routing change between two alternative routes from A26 to A28: the route running through the group A32, A33, A38, A39, A40 (executed between A26 and A28 in varying order) was largely abandoned, while the route through A27 became the standard path — A27 changed from optional (about half of traces) to nearly always executed, and A26 → A27 → A28 now dominates. The participation shifts are large and consistent (about 51 of 100 traces down to 1–3 of 100 for each group activity; about 50 up to 99 of 100 for A27), the relational items — each backed by all 200 traces in both windows — show the same activities moving from being alternatives of A27 to being almost never executed, and A26's routing shifted accordingly. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL001, REL007, REL008, REL013, REL018, DIR001]

The evidence cannot distinguish whether the group A32/A33/A38/A39/A40 was removed from the process or merely became a rare path, since it still appears in about 1–3 of 100 later traces, and the structure of that residual branch is uncertain because its successor profiles rest on 3–5 transitions. [OCC002, DIR002, DIR006]

Other differences: A26's mean event position moved slightly later (about 0.46 to 0.49 of the trace) and A28's slightly earlier (about 0.58 to 0.56); these are small position-based shifts consistent with the changed sequence composition and do not by themselves indicate a relocation. [POS001, POS002]
