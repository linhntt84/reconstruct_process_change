### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a timing change: in the later window, the elapsed time between the completions of A41 and A18 is about one hour shorter per occurrence, down from about 2 hours to about 1 hour. The shift is direct (the indicator measures exactly this elapsed time), consistent (mean, median and both quartiles each dropped by roughly 0.9–1.1 hours and the spread halved), rests on about 100 occurrences per window, and is far beyond what random re-splits produce; the time family yielded 1 qualifying item where about 0.007 would be expected if nothing had changed. Limitations: the indicator captures elapsed time between consecutive completions and cannot show whether waiting or processing shortened, and a change in the mix of cases reaching the A41→A18 pair remains a competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude.
