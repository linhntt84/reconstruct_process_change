### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 became part of the flow in the later window: an activity not observed in the earlier window now occurs in about half of the traces at early positions and is followed mostly by A05, A03, A06 and A15. The change is visible from both sides — A05, A06, A08, A09 and A12 gained A01 as a successor, and pairs that were previously adjacent, such as A08 before A03 and A09 before A03, now occur separated by larger gaps, consistent with A01 sitting between earlier activities and the activities that follow them. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG002, LAG005]

The 25 qualifying items are many times the fewer than 1 expected by chance in an unchanged process, so the differences as a whole are unlikely to be a sampling artefact. Limitations: the occurrence, position, relation and direction items largely reflect the same underlying behaviour and are not independent confirmations; zero participation in the earlier window strictly means only that A01 was not observed there; and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different mixes of process variants rather than a single modified process. [OCC001, DIR001, REL002]

Other differences: the rerouting at A16 towards A02, the shift in A03's outgoing transitions away from A05, and the A03–A08 pair moving closer together are observed but are not part of the hypothesis about A01. [DIR008, DIR002, LAG001]
