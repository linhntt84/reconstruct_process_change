### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change in which the block A31, A32, A37, A38, A39 — earlier an alternative to A22, run in about half of the cases after A21 — became a mandatory block executed once in every case and now sits directly after A20 and before A23, while A22 also became mandatory and always follows A21; A26 now closes the trace after A22 instead of directly following the block. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]

The change is shown directly by relational and successor evidence rather than inferred from frequencies alone: co-occurrence between A22 and each of the five went from never to always with the five now first, the order between A21/A23 and the five reversed, and the edges A20→block, block→A23 and A21→A22 replaced the earlier routing. The participation rise from about half of traces to all traces and the consistent position shifts agree with this reading. With 62 qualifying items against roughly 0.67 expected if nothing had changed, chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole. [REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024, DIR001, DIR002]

Limitations: the evidence is control-flow only, and many items describe the same underlying trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations. The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different case populations with a different variant mix rather than a modified process, although the later pattern — every trace containing both A22 and the five — appears nowhere in the earlier window.
