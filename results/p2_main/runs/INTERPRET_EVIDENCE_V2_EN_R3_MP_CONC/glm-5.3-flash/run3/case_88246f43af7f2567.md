### Observed differences

- The successors of A02 and A04 changed completely. In the earlier window, about 91 in 100 of A02's observed outgoing transitions went to A03 (about 6 to A12), and about 67 in 100 of A04's went to A03 (about 20 to A12, 13 to A11); in the later window, every observed outgoing transition of both activities goes to A12. [DIR001, DIR003]
- A03 and A16 rewired their outputs. Earlier, A03's outgoing transitions split about evenly between A11 (45 in 100) and A12 (45 in 100), with small shares to A02 and A04; later they go to A11 (about 55 in 100) and A10 (about 45 in 100), with no transitions to A12 observed. A16 earlier sent about half of its transitions to A10 and half to A09; later it sends about half to A03 and half to A09, with none to A10 observed. [DIR002, DIR004]
- A03 and A12 moved from being executed together to being alternatives. Earlier they co-occurred in about 49 in 100 traces — whenever one appeared, the other did too, with A03 first in about 26 in 100 traces and interleaved or repeated in about 24 in 100; in the later window they never share a trace (A03 without A12 in 30 in 100, A12 without A03 in 45 in 100). [REL004]
- The other co-occurrences flipped in the same direction. A03 with A02 fell from about 26 in 100 traces to none, and A03 with A04 from about 24 in 100 to none, while A03 with A10 and A03 with A16 rose from none to about 30 in 100 traces each; in the later window every trace containing A03 also contains both A10 and A16, in mixed orders. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL005]
- Step distances changed accordingly. A12 now follows A02 and A04 exactly one step later in every observed pair, where earlier the gaps spread over two to four steps; conversely, A10 no longer immediately follows A16 (one step later in about 85 in 100 pairs earlier, spread over two to five steps later). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Positions moved with the rewiring. A12 earlier sat at the end of traces (mean relative position about 0.97, with at least three in four of its occurrences at the last position) and appeared in about 49 in 100 traces; later its mean position is about 0.71, at least a quarter of its occurrences are at the very first position, and it appears in about 45 in 100 traces. A03 now appears in fewer traces (about 30 in 100, down from 49) and later within them (mean relative position about 0.67, up from 0.44). [POS003, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two previously separate fragments were reconnected around A03 and A12. In the earlier window, A02 and A04 typically fed into A03, A03 passed on to A12, and A12 closed the traces it appeared in, always together with A03; A16 and A10 formed a separate fragment in which A10 immediately followed A16. In the later window, A12 is executed directly after A02 or A04 — one step later in every observed pair, and at the very start of traces in about a quarter of its occurrences — and never together with A03, while A03 has joined the other fragment: A16 now leads to A03 instead of to A10, A03 leads to A11 and A10, and A03, A10 and A16 always appear in the same traces. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS003]

Supporting signs: sixteen control-flow items qualified, against roughly 0.2 expected by chance among the 42 tested units, so the set as a whole cannot be explained by sampling; every item's difference exceeds all but about 1 in 200 random re-splits of the pooled traces; and the rewiring is shown directly by successor profiles, co-occurrence flips and one-step lags rather than inferred from position shifts alone. The largest signals — A03–A12 co-occurrence falling from about half of traces to none, and A02 and A04 routing entirely to A12 — are far beyond what two 200-trace samples produce without a change. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: the routing shares for A02 and A04 rest on fewer observations (roughly 28–47 transitions or pairs per window), so their exact proportions are uncertain; the order among A03, A10 and A16 is mixed rather than fixed, so their internal sequence is not established; and the window comparison cannot distinguish a rewiring of continuing paths from a wholesale replacement of one variant family by another — the behavioural difference itself is what the evidence establishes, not the mechanism behind it. The DIR, REL and LAG items also partly describe the same traces, so they are consistent rather than independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR003, REL003, REL005]

Other differences: two minor position drifts — A10 now appears slightly later and more tightly clustered (mean relative position about 0.83 earlier, 0.90 later) and A16 slightly earlier (about 0.53 earlier, 0.43 later); both are consistent with the rewiring above but are small on their own. [POS002, POS004]
