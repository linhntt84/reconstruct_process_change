### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A change in timing is supported: the elapsed time between consecutive completion events on the transitions into A39 — from A11 and from A38 — roughly halved, from about 1 hour in the earlier window to about 0.5 hours in the later one. The two time items agree in direction and magnitude, each difference is larger than in about 999.5 of 1,000 random re-splits, and the time family produced 2 items where only about 0.01 would be expected if nothing had changed; the absence of any control-flow item is consistent with a timing change without a structural counterpart.
