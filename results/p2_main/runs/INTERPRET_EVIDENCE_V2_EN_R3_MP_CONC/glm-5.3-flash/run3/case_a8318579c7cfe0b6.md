### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the mandatory five-activity segment A20, A21, A30, A31 and A32 — executed in every earlier case in mixed internal order, between A14/A15/A48 and A33/A53/A54 — was replaced in the later window by a single step chosen from five mutually exclusive alternatives, A19, A22, A23, A24 and A25, at the same point of the flow. The signs are direct and consistent across indicators: the old five fell from 100% to 0–1% participation, the new five rose from 0% to roughly 16–24% each with near-zero pairwise co-occurrence and shares summing to one per case, the connections were rewired (A48 now leads mainly to the new activities, which in turn lead to A33), and A33 runs earlier as the segment shortens. The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 165 control-flow items qualified where about 1.27 would be expected by chance. [OCC006, OCC007, OCC012, OCC013, OCC014, OCC005, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011, REL035, REL042, REL050, DIR014, DIR005, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, POS013]

Limitations: the later-window profiles of A30, A31 and A32 rest on only 1–2 traces; POS and LAG reflect event positions rather than clock time; and many items describe the same underlying replacement, so they are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows draw different case types or process variants in different proportions, rather than the flow itself having been modified. [DIR011, DIR012, DIR013, POS010, POS011, POS012]

Other differences: A04 participation dropped from about 76% to about 20% of cases and, when present, occurs later in the case; A06, A08 and A10 each roughly doubled (about 8–9% to about 19–21%); the immediate A53→A54 succession weakened slightly (about 96% to 85% of pairs one step apart).
