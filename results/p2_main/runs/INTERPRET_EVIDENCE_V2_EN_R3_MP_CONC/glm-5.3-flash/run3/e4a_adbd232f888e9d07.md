### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of three alternative activities A14, A15 and A20 — of which every case executed exactly one — was relocated from the start of the case to its late stage. In the earlier window a case ran A40, then one of the three, then A21, and later A31 directly followed by A22, A23 and A27; in the later window A40 led directly to A21, and the chosen alternative was executed between A22 and A27, inserted either directly after A22, before A23 (about 48 in 100 cases), or directly after A23, before A27 (about 52 in 100 cases), each time continuing to A23 or A27 instead of back to A21. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

The screening context supports this reading: the control-flow family produced 24 qualifying items against roughly 0.4 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by random re-splits. The placement change is shown directly by the successor changes and is consistent across positions, order relations and adjacency. Stable participation throughout — the block still appears once per case, and A21, A22, A23, A27, A40 and A31 in all cases — rules out the competing readings that the block became optional, was replaced by other activities, or changed how often it repeats; the difference is one of placement and routing, not of participation. [REL001, REL002, LAG001, LAG002, POS004, POS005, POS006, TWAIT001]

What the evidence cannot establish: whether the three rewiring points (A40, A22, A23) reflect one coordinated relocation of the block or three separate routing changes, and whether anything also changed in segments without qualifying items (for example between A21 and A31) — the absence of items there is not proof of stability. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

Other differences: the elapsed time between the completions of A31 and A22 halved (mean about 1.0 h to about 0.5 h, all quartiles halved; 200 of 200 cases per window; the time family produced 1 item against about 0.01 expected without change, with a difference larger than in about 9995 of 10000 random re-splits). This is a timing change in its own right; whether it is connected to the structural relocation or independent of it cannot be determined from this evidence. [TWAIT001]
