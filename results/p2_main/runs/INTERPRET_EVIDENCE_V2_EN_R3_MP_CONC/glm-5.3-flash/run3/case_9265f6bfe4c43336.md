### Observed differences

- The routing at A15 reversed: in the earlier window about 85 in 100 of A15's outgoing transitions led to A19 and about 16 in 100 to A24; in the later window about 82 in 100 led to A24 and only about 18 in 100 to A19. [DIR001]
- Two five-activity blocks swapped their level of use: A16, A17, A18, A19 and A20 each ran in about 85 of 100 traces earlier but only about 18 of 100 later, while A21, A22, A23, A24 and A25 each ran in about 16 of 100 traces earlier and about 82 of 100 later; each of these activities appears at most once per trace in both windows. [OCC001, OCC006]
- The blocks behave as coherent, mutually exclusive alternatives in both windows: members of the same block always run together when the block runs (for example A16 with A17 in 85% of earlier traces, A21 with A22 in 82% of later traces), activities from different blocks never share a trace, and A15 precedes whichever block runs (A15 before A16 in 85% of earlier traces; A15 before A21 in 82% of later traces). [REL001, REL006, REL011, REL015, REL051]
- A29 is present in every trace in both windows but changes company: it co-occurs with the A16–A20 block in about 85 of 100 earlier traces and with the A21–A25 block in about 82 of 100 later traces. [REL020, REL055]
- Minor: A22's successor mix evened out (earlier about 58% to A21, 29% to A23, 13% to A25; later roughly a third each), and the A18→A17 pair sits one step apart more often (about 82% of later pairs at distance 1 versus about 54% earlier, based on only 17 later pairs). [DIR002, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the choice between two existing alternative branches after A15 flipped. In the earlier window the dominant path ran A15 → A19 into the A16–A20 block (with A29 alongside), taken in about 85 of 100 cases, while the branch A15 → A24 into the A21–A25 block was taken in about 16 of 100; in the later window this reversed, with about 82 of 100 cases taking the A21–A25 branch and about 18 of 100 still taking the A16–A20 branch. The signs are direct and consistent: the routing shares at A15 themselves changed, the participation of all ten block activities moved in mirror image by the same amount, and the blocks remain mutually exclusive with fixed internal togetherness in both windows — so only the shares between the alternatives shifted, with every activity on both branches still exercised in both windows (the rarer branch has 31 of 200 earlier and 36 of 200 later cases). The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 78 control-flow items against roughly 0.84 expected by chance, each difference larger than about 199 of 200 random re-splits. Limitations: all items reflect the same underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot show where the branch decision is actually made — an upstream change that reroutes cases before reaching A15 would look identical. [OCC001, OCC006, DIR001, REL001, REL006, REL015, REL051]

Other differences: within the A21–A25 branch, A22's outgoing mix changed from concentrated on A21 to roughly even across A21, A23 and A25, and the A18→A17 pair became more often immediately adjacent in the later window, though only 17 later pairs support that positional shift. [DIR002, LAG001]
