### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the step between A10 and A03 is now carried out by A13 instead of A01: traces that previously ran A10 → A01 → A03 (with A01 occasionally repeating) now run A10 → A13 → A03, A01 is no longer executed, and A13 repeats somewhat more often within traces than A01 did. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, REL004]

The change is shown directly by the successor evidence — A10's outgoing flow moved from A01 to A13, and A13's flow into A03 reproduces exactly the edge A01 used to have — and by the positional match; participation moved from about 46 in 100 traces (A01) to about 53 in 100 (A13), and all five relation items change consistently with this substitution. Twelve control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.16 expected if nothing had changed, so chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, REL002, REL003, REL005]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that A13 is an unrelated addition that coincides with A01's removal rather than taking over its role; the identical predecessor, successor and relative position favour the substitution reading but do not prove it, and the finite windows leave small share differences uncertain. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, DIR003]

Other differences: A10's own participation rose from about 46 in 100 traces in the earlier window to about 53 in 100 in the later window, a small shift that may reflect sampling variation between the two 200-trace samples. [REL002, REL005]
