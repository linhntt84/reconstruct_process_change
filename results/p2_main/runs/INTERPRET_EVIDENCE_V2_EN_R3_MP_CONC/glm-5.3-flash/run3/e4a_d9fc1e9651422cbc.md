### Observed differences

- Six activities that ran in every earlier trace now run in only a subset: A22 appears in about 46 in 100 later-window traces (91 of 200, down from 200 of 200), and each of A31, A32, A37, A38 and A39 appears in about 55 in 100 (110 of 200, down from 200 of 200). A20, A21, A23 and A26 still appear in every trace in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005]
- In the later window, A20 leads almost always directly to A23 (about 995 in 1000 of its outgoing transitions), whereas earlier its transitions spread roughly evenly over A31, A32, A37, A38 and A39 (about 17–24 in 100 each). [DIR001]
- In the later window, A21 splits its outgoing transitions between A22 (about 46 in 100) and the five block activities (together about 54 in 100, led by A32 at about 17 and A38 at about 12 in 100), whereas earlier every A21 transition went to A22. [DIR002]
- The block's exit changed: earlier each of A31, A32, A37, A38 and A39 led to A23 in about 17–26 in 100 of its outgoing transitions; in the later window that link has all but disappeared (at most about 1 in 100) and each block activity instead leads to A26 in about 14–25 in 100. - A22 and the block became alternatives: earlier every trace contained A22 together with each block activity (block member first); in the later window they share a trace in only about 1 in 200 cases, with A22 alone in about 45 in 100 and the block member alone in about 55 in 100. The five block activities still occur together in the same traces (about 55 in 100) and keep no fixed order among themselves in either window. [REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL030, REL039]
- Ordering around the block reversed: earlier each block activity ran before A21 and before A23 in every trace; in the later window A21 and A23 each precede the block in about 55 in 100 traces, while the block still precedes A26 whenever both occur. [REL008, REL020, REL025]
- Event positions shifted accordingly: the block's mean position moved from about 0.40 to about 0.57–0.59 of the trace, A23 moved earlier (about 0.48 to 0.38) and A21 earlier (about 0.62 to 0.53). [POS006, POS004, POS002]
- The elapsed time between consecutive completions of A26 and A19 fell from a median of about 0.93 hours to about 0.45 hours, with 56 intervals in each window. [TWAIT001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The volume of qualifying evidence rules out chance as a whole: 72 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.7 would be expected without a change, and the single time item compares with an expectation of roughly 0.02; each item's difference also exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, DIR001, REL015, POS006, TWAIT001]

Main hypothesis: in the later window, the five-activity block A31, A32, A37, A38, A39 and the activity A22 became mutually exclusive alternatives executed after A21. About 55 in 100 traces run A20 → A23 → A21 → the block → A26 with no A22, and about 45 in 100 run A20 → A23 → A21 → A22 → A26 with no block; earlier, every trace ran the block between A20 and A23 and A22 between A21 and A26 in the same trace. The relational and successor evidence shows this directly: A20 now leads to A23 without the block, A21 gained the block as a successor alongside A22, the block now leads to A26 instead of A23, and A22 and the block co-occur in only about 1 in 200 later traces. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019]

The signs are consistent across indicator families: the participation drops match the two branch frequencies, the order reversals between the block and A21/A23 match the rerouting, the block's precedence over A26 is preserved, and the position shifts (block later, A23 and A21 earlier) agree with the new sequence. The ten lag-distance items each rest on a single later-window observation and were not used as support. [OCC002, REL008, REL020, REL025, REL030, POS004, POS006, LAG001]

Limitations: the per-trace paths are reconstructed from aggregate indicators, the evidence cannot show what determines which alternative a trace takes, and it cannot distinguish a stable roughly 45/55 routing split from a mixture of distinct sub-populations; a pre-existing rare variant skipping the block or A22 cannot be fully excluded, although it would have had to occur in well under 1 in 100 earlier traces, since all 200 earlier traces contained both. [OCC001, OCC002, REL015]

Other differences: the elapsed time between the completions of A26 and A19 more than halved (median about 0.93 hours to about 0.45 hours, with 56 intervals in each window) — a timing change in its own right that the structural hypothesis above does not by itself explain. [TWAIT001]
