### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activity A03 was not observed at all (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it appears in about 103 in 200 cases (roughly half of all traces), at most once per trace. [OCC001]
- In the earlier window, about half of the traces (roughly 51 in 100) contained a fixed middle block — A05 early, then A08 and A12 back-to-back in either order, then A11 later — and A05, A08, A11 and A12 always occurred together in those traces, never with A03. In the later window, A03 co-occurs with each of these four in about 52 in 100 traces, i.e., it appears inside this same block. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]
- When present, A03 takes a middle position: median event position about 0.38 of the trace length (middle half roughly 0.25–0.42). [POS001]
- A03's ordering is specific: it always completes before A11 (in about 52 in 100 traces, never the reverse), but relative to A05, A08 and A12 there is no fixed order — e.g., A05 comes first in about 28 in 100 traces with interleaving in about 24, and A08 precedes A03 in about 29 in 100 while A03 precedes A08 in about 23. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]
- Routing changed accordingly: A05, A08 and A12, which never led to A03 before, now send about 16%, 38% and 33% of their outgoing transitions to A03 (A05's share to A07 fell from about 58% to 45%); A03's outgoing transitions split roughly evenly across A12 (~36%), A11 (~35%) and A08 (~29%). [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR001]
- Distances shifted as if one step had been added in the middle: A05 to A11, previously most often 3 positions apart (sometimes 5), is now exactly 4 positions apart in every observation; A08→A11 and A12→A11, previously 1–2 positions apart, now spread over 1–3; and A08/A12, previously always immediately adjacent, are 2 positions apart in about 32–40% of later cases. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG005, LAG003, LAG004]
- A05 now runs slightly earlier (mean position about 0.18 → 0.11); A11's position is essentially unchanged (mean about 0.55 in both windows). [POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A03 appears as an additional step in the middle of the process in the later window: it now executes in about half of the cases, after A05 and before A11, sharing the middle stretch with A08 and A12 in a flexible order (it may precede or follow each of them, but always completes before A11). This is shown directly — participation rises from none to about half of traces, A05/A08/A12 gain transitions into A03, A03 gains its own outgoing transitions, and the A05→A11 distance lengthens uniformly by one position. Seventeen items qualified against roughly 0.17 expected for an unchanged process (about 100 times the chance expectation), so the pattern as a whole cannot be attributed to sampling variation in the two windows. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, LAG001, POS001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely new activity from one that existed before but was previously skipped or recorded differently, and because A03's order relative to A08 and A12 is mixed, no single fixed placement can be stated; the items also largely describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, REL002, REL004]

Other differences: none stand apart from the above — A05's slightly earlier position, its reduced share to A07, and A11's stable position are all part of the same reorganisation of the middle block. [POS002, POS003, DIR002]
