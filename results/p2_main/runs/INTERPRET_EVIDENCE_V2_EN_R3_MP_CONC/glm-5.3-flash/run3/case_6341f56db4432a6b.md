### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the closing activity A01 was dropped from executions that previously ended with the pair A12 → A01, leaving A12 as the final step of those traces: in the earlier window about half of all traces (103 of 200) ended with A12 followed by A01, whereas in the later window A01 is unobserved in all 200 traces, A12's participation is essentially unchanged (about 51 in 100 traces) but it now occupies the last position in every case, and its transition to A01 is no longer observed. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, REL001, DIR003]

A chance origin of these differences is implausible: 9 evidence items qualified across the 27 tested units, against roughly 0.13 expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference is larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, REL001]

Limitations: zero participation only means A01 was not observed, so a residual, much rarer execution of A01 in the later window cannot be excluded; and the evidence contains no item linking the routing changes reported below to the A01/A12 ending, so it cannot show whether they belong to the same modification. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005]

Other differences: the rerouting around A06/A14 (both now always proceed to A10; A06→A14 and A14→A15 no longer observed) and around A07/A13 (new observed transitions A07→A13, A13→A07 and A13→A08, with A05's share halved) are not connected by any evidence item to the disappearance of A01 and may constitute separate changes.
