### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the group of activities A30, A31 and A36 now sits between A21 and A25, whereas in the earlier window it sat between A20 and A22: earlier, A20 fed into the group and the group led on to A22, while A21 later ran directly to A25; in the later window, A20 runs directly to A22, A21 feeds into the group, and the group leads to A25. The group's internal routing among A30, A31 and A36 is largely preserved; mainly its entry and exit points changed. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG001, LAG004]

Supporting signs: the successor profiles at both attachment points changed directly and in opposite directions [DIR001, DIR002]; the group's exit edge flipped from A22 to A25 at roughly unchanged rates [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]; the order relative to A21 and A22 reversed almost completely while co-occurrence stayed at 100 in 100, so the group still runs in every trace and was neither removed nor made optional [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]; and both distance changes match the insertion or removal of a three-activity block exactly [LAG001, LAG004]. Screening qualified 22 items against about 0.42 expected if nothing had changed, so the ensemble is far beyond what random re-splits of an unchanged process produce. [screening]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a literal move of the group from two separate rewirings — dropping it from after A20 and inserting an equivalent group between A21 and A25 — which would look identical here; position indicators use event positions rather than clock time, so the placement claim rests on the relational and successor evidence; and four corroborating gap items rest on only 2 of 200 later-window observations each.
