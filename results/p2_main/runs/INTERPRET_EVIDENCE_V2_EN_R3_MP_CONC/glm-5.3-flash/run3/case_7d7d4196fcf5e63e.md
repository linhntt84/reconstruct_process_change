### Observed differences

- The set of always-executed activities changed: in the earlier window A08 and A04 were executed in every trace (A08 about twice per trace), while in the later window A02 and A04 run in every trace and A08 appears in only about 45 in 100 traces, with its mean occurrences per trace falling from 2.0 to about 0.8. [OCC002, OCC005, REL006, REL011]
- A02 grew from about 49 in 100 traces (once per trace) to every trace, now with about 1.8 occurrences per trace and clearly more repetition; A01 shrank from about 49 to about 31 in 100 traces (mean 1.0 to about 0.4), A06 from about 49 to about 29 (mean about 2.0 to 0.7), and A07 from about 31 to about 15 (mean about 0.95 to 0.33). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Successors were rewired around the loop: before, A02 always led to A01, A03 always to A06, A08 split about 50/50 between A03 and A04, and A06 led back to A02 (about 51%) or on to A07 (about 49%); in the later window A02 leads to A04 (about 57%) or A03 (about 43%), A08 always leads to A02, A03 leads to A01 (about 52%) or A06 (about 48%), and A06 leads to A08 (about 53%) or A07 (about 47%). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- The company activities keep changed accordingly: A02 and A03, previously always executed together, run apart in about 55 in 100 later traces; A02 and A04, previously together only in the roughly 49% of traces where A02 ran, now always co-occur (A02 before A04 in about 55 in 100, interleaved otherwise); A04 and A08, previously always together, are apart in about 55 in 100 later traces. [REL005, REL006, REL007, REL011]
- Typical positions and gaps shifted as well: A02 now typically sits at the very start of traces (median position about 0 versus about 0.51), A01 earlier (mean about 0.37 versus 0.58) and A08 later (median about 0.50 versus 0.10); gaps moved too, e.g. A08→A04 from immediate to two steps apart and A02→A04 from three steps to mostly immediate. These are event positions, not clock times. [POS001, POS002, POS004, LAG011, LAG035]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural rerouting of the control flow together with changed participation, centred on A02, A04 and A08: in the earlier window the flow passed through A08 in every case (feeding A03→A06→A02→A01 and A04), whereas in the later window A02 and A04 are executed in every trace, A02 is followed by A04 or A03 instead of A01, A08 occurs in only about 45 in 100 traces and now leads to A02, and A06 leads to A08 rather than back to A02, with A01, A06 and A07 participating far less often. The successor changes are shown directly by the outgoing-transition profiles, participation and repetition moved in the same direction for every affected activity, the pairwise relations show the same traces switching from A08/A03 company to A02/A04 company, and 64 qualifying items against roughly 0.36 expected by chance for the 72 tested units rules out sampling variation as an explanation of the pattern as a whole. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL009, REL011]

Limitations: the items overlap heavily, since many describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations; the evidence cannot distinguish a redesigned single flow from a shifted mix of existing variants, nor determine whether A01's branch was removed or only taken much less often, as A01 still appears in about 31 in 100 later traces. [OCC001, REL001]

Other differences: positional and gap shifts accompany the rerouting — A02 now typically opens the trace, A01 sits earlier and A08 later, and several pairwise gaps changed by about one step — but these rest on event positions rather than clock time. [POS001, POS002, POS004, LAG008, LAG011, LAG035]
