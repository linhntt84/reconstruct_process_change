### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, ten activities formed two five-activity groups — A28, A29, A30, A32, A33 on one side and A34–A38 on the other — and each activity ran in only about half of the traces (about 48 in 100 for the first group, about 52 in 100 for the second), at most once per trace. In the later window, participation rose to about 99–100 in 100 for the first group and about 98 in 100 for the second, still with no repeats. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010]

- The two groups were mutually exclusive alternatives before: cross-group pairs such as A28 and A34 co-occurred in 0 of 100 traces (one side or the other was always absent). In the later window they co-occur in about 97–98 of 100 traces, always with the A28-side activity first (A28 before A34 in about 97.5 in 100; the reverse never observed). [REL025, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL033, REL040, REL046, REL051]

- Inside each group the order stayed unfixed in both windows: pairs such as A28–A29 and A34–A35 appear in both orders in similar proportions, while their co-occurrence rose from about 48–52 in 100 traces to about 98–99.5 in 100. [REL021, REL030, REL038, REL045, REL056, REL060, REL065]

- A25 preceded and A26 followed the block activities in every co-occurrence in both windows; the share of traces containing A25 or A26 without the block activity fell from about 48–52 in 100 to about 0–2 in 100. [REL001, REL006, REL011, REL016]

- Routing followed the same pattern: before, A25's outgoing transitions split about 48/52 between the two groups' activities; in the later window about 99.5 in 100 go to A28/A29/A30/A32/A33 and only about 0.5 in 100 to A35. Direct transitions from the first group to A26 disappeared — A28→A26 (about 28% of A28's outgoing transitions before) and A30→A26 (about 17%) are no longer observed — while A28 and A30 now send roughly 20% of their outgoing transitions combined to A34–A38. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

- Relative event positions shifted consistently with longer traces: A25's mean position fell from about 0.64 to about 0.55, the A28-side activities from about 0.73 to about 0.63, the A34-side activities rose from about 0.72 to about 0.76, and A26 from about 0.82 to about 0.84 (event positions, not clock time). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010, POS011, POS012]

- The measured gap from A25 to each A34-side activity, previously spread over steps 1–5, falls within five steps in only 1 later-window trace, consistent with the other group now lying between them — but this item rests on a single later trace and carries little weight. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two blocks of five activities that were previously mutually exclusive alternatives — A28/A29/A30/A32/A33 versus A34/A35/A36/A37/A38, framed by A25 before and A26 after — are in the later window executed together in sequence in almost every case: about 48 in 100 earlier traces ran only the first block and about 52 in 100 only the second, whereas about 98–99 in 100 later traces contain all ten activities, first block before second, with no fixed order inside either block. [REL025, REL033, REL040, REL051, OCC001, OCC006, REL001, REL011, DIR001]

The support is direct and consistent: the relational items show the change from alternatives to joint execution with a fixed inter-block order, the participation and routing items show the same trace behaviour from the activity and edge side, and the screening produced 95 qualifying items against about 1 expected if nothing had changed, so chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole; every item's difference also exceeds those in about 199 of 200 random re-splits. Note that several indicators describe the same underlying trace changes, so they are not independent confirmations. [REL025, OCC001, DIR001, DIR002]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a change in the case mix — the later window being dominated by cases that always executed both blocks — which would look identical here; the evidence also says nothing about what precedes A25 or follows A26, and the lag items' later profiles rest on a single trace each. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005]

Other differences: none — every qualifying item is consistent with the single structural change described above. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, REL025]
