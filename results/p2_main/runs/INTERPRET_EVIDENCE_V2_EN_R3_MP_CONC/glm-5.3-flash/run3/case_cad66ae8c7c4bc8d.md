### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative block of A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 between A43 and A28 is no longer executed in the later window: earlier, nearly every case contained exactly one of the five in that position, whereas in the later window they are essentially absent and A43 passes flow on to A28 directly, typically within one step. This is shown directly by the change in A43's successor profile, and corroborated by the collapse of the five activities' participation, the disappearance of their co-occurrence with A43, A28 and each other, and the shortened step gaps to A28. Screening qualified 89 control-flow items where about 1 would be expected without a change, so the overall pattern is far larger than random re-sampling of a stable process produces. Limitations: zero participation only shows the five activities were not observed in the 200 later traces, so a rarely triggered residual execution cannot be excluded, and the evidence cannot indicate why the block disappeared.
