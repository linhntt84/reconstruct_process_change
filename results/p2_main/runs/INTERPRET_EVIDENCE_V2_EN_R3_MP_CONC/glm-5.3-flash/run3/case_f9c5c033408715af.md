### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A07 was no longer executed in the later window: an activity that previously ran in about 23 in 100 traces — always alongside A10, late in the trace, and leading mainly to A03 and A13 — is absent from all 200 later traces. The change is shown directly: A07's participation, positions and outgoing transitions all fell to zero, its co-occurrence with A10 disappeared, and A10 no longer transitions to A07. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001, DIR002]

The volume of qualifying evidence also fits a real change rather than sampling noise: six items qualified against roughly 0.14 expected by chance for the control-flow family, and the items largely describe one underlying disappearance (A07's absence) rather than independent differences. The main limitation is that zero participation strictly means only that A07 was not observed, so the evidence cannot rule out that the later window's cases simply never reach A07 — although complete absence after 46 occurrences in 200 earlier traces is hard to explain by sampling alone. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001, DIR002, LAG001]

Other differences: Beyond the loss of the A10→A07 edge itself, A10's remaining outgoing mix was redistributed (more to A01, A03 and A13, a new edge to A09, less to A04, A08 and A06), and the A10→A03 gap shifted toward shorter distances. These shifts are consistent with flow that used to pass through A07 now continuing directly from A10, but the evidence does not establish that link. [DIR002, LAG001]
