### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative step between A24 and A26 is no longer executed: in the earlier window every trace contained exactly one of A14, A15, A20, A21 or A22, placed after A24 in about 39 in 100 cases and after A25 in about 61 in 100, while in the later window cases run directly from A24 via A25 to A26 and each of the five appears in at most 1 of 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR005, DIR006, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

The screening produced 38 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.46 expected if nothing had changed, so this set of differences cannot be explained by chance sampling of rare variants. [screening] The support is direct and consistent: participation collapsed from 37–46 traces per activity to 0–1, the outgoing routing of A24 and A25 consolidated with full support in both windows (200 traces each), and the A24-to-A26 lag shortened in step. [OCC003, OCC004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

Limitations: zero participation only means the activities were not observed — they could still occur at a rate too low to surface in 200 traces — and the after-window routing detail for A15 and A22 rests on a single trace each. [DIR001, DIR004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC005] The many agreeing items largely describe one underlying difference and are not independent confirmations. A competing reading the evidence cannot rule out is that the five activities were not removed but became an optional branch that is now almost never taken; the observed behaviour is the same either way, but the evidence cannot distinguish removal from near-zero usage.
