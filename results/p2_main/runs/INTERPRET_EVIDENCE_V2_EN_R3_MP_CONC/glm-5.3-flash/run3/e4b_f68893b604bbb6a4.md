### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation as a specific possibility. Across A01, A03, A13, A14, A17 and A22, work moved from a former leading group of resources — previously headed by User_37 at about 10–12 in 100 assignments — to a largely different group now headed by User_48 at about 6–9 in 100, with the former leaders dropping out of the leading shares altogether. In addition, A11, previously spread across about 18 resources with User_115 taking about 18 in 100, is in the later window assigned exclusively to User_115.
