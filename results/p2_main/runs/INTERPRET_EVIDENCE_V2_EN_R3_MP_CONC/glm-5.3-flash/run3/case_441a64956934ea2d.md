### Observed differences

- A01 and A13 exchanged their overall levels of participation. In the earlier window A01 appeared in about 66 in 100 traces (about 2.2 occurrences per trace) and A13 in about 23 in 100 (about 0.4 per trace); in the later window A01 appears in about 28 in 100 (about 0.6 per trace) and A13 in about 61 in 100 (about 2.1 per trace). Self-repetition moved the same way: A01's recurrence support fell from about 0.45 to about 0.18, A13's rose from about 0.12 to about 0.44. [OCC001, OCC002]
- The two activities exchanged their outgoing behaviour. Before, A01's outgoing transitions went mainly to A08 (about 48 in 100), A15 (about 26) and A07 (about 26), while A13 led almost exclusively to A06; in the later window A01 leads almost exclusively to A06, and A13's outgoing transitions go to A08 (about 45 in 100), A15 (about 28) and A07 (about 28). [DIR001, DIR006]
- Neighbouring activities re-pointed accordingly: A07 and A15, which each sent about half of their outgoing transitions back to A01, now send about half to A13; A02 and A04, which each sent about a quarter to A01, now send a similar share to A13; and A06, which previously led to A13 in about 47 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, now leads to A01 in about 54 in 100, while its link to A14 stayed similar. [DIR005, DIR007, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- Their typical positions swapped: A01, when present, moved from early in the trace (median relative position about 0.44) to late (about 0.82), while A13 moved from late (about 0.82) to early (about 0.32). [POS001, POS002]
- Co-occurrence patterns mirror the exchange: A01 with A08 co-occurred in about 66 in 100 traces before versus about 18 after, while A08 with A13 rose from about 17 to about 61 in 100; A01's co-occurrence with A07 and A15 fell from about 43 to about 11 in 100 while A13's with them rose from about 11 to about 39 in 100; A02 and A04 now usually co-occur with A13 (about 39 in 100) where before they usually appeared without it (about 14 in 100). - Distances changed in the same direction: A13 now follows A02, A04, A07 or A15 within 1–2 steps (before it followed them 4–5 steps later and rarely), whereas A01, in the few traces where it still follows them, now does so 4–5 steps later; A01 is now followed immediately by A06, a relation that before belonged to A13. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 and A13 exchanged their roles in the process. In the later window, A13 occupies the position A01 held before — an early, frequently repeated step that loops with A07 and A15, leads to A08, and is reached from A02 and A04 — while A01 has taken over A13's former role as a late, much rarer step that leads on to A06. The exchange is shown directly rather than inferred from frequencies alone: the outgoing profiles of the two activities are near mirror images across the windows, five neighbouring activities re-pointed their edges, and the position, co-occurrence and distance distributions all swapped consistently. The volume of qualified evidence (38 items against roughly 0.46 expected if nothing had changed, all in the control-flow family) cannot be explained by chance sampling, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR006, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR007, POS001, POS002, REL005, REL012, LAG002, LAG004]

Other differences: none of comparable size; A06's link to A14 (about half of its outgoing transitions in both windows) and the wiring among A02, A04 and A05 were broadly stable. [DIR004, DIR002, DIR003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot fully separate a literal exchange of the two activities' places from a replacement in which the A01-centred behaviour was abandoned and an A13-centred equivalent introduced, since only frequencies and orders are observed; some distance items rest on few traces (about 11–15 after-window observations for A01's new late position); and positions are measured in event order, not clock time, so no statement about actual timing is implied. [LAG003, LAG005, LAG007, LAG013, POS001, POS002]
