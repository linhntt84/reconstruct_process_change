### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A08 acts as a regular, fixed early step between A15 and A16: it is executed in about 71 in 100 traces (previously about 21 in 100), appears in the first quarter of the trace rather than mid-trace, is reached directly from A15 (about 45 in 100 of A15's outgoing transitions, previously about 1 in 100) and leads directly to A16 (about 86 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, previously about 9 in 100), and it now consistently precedes ever-present later activities such as A28, A29 and A52, whereas previously it was an occasional mid-trace activity with varied successors. The supporting signs are direct and consistent across occurrence, position, direction, relation and lag indicators and rest on large samples (142 traces contain A08 in the later window versus 42 before; 295 observed outgoing transitions of A15), and the screening flagged 84 control-flow items where about 1 would be expected without a change, so the overall difference pattern is far larger than random re-splits of the same traces produce. An important competing explanation that the aggregate windows cannot fully exclude is a shift in the mix of pre-existing trace variants rather than a modified process; positions and lags also measure event positions, not clock time.
