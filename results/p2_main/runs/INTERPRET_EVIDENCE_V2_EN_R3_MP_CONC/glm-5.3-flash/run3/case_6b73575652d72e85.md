### Observed differences

- Activity A01 is the clearest difference: in the earlier window it does not appear in any of the 200 traces, while in the later window it occurs in about 47 in 100 traces (94 of 200), just under once per trace on average, recurring within the same trace in roughly 23 in 100 of the traces that contain it. It sits in the first half of the trace (median relative position about 0.34). [OCC001, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Twenty control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.23 expected by chance if nothing had changed, so the volume of differences far exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces. The evidence supports the hypothesis that a step A01 was added and inserted on the routes into A04: earlier, A03, A10 and especially A11 led largely directly into A04, whereas in the later window a large share of that flow passes through A01 first (A03→A01→A04, A11→A01→A04, A10→A01→A04). The signs are direct and mutually consistent: A01 appears only in the later window, in about 47 in 100 traces [OCC001]; three predecessors redirect their outgoing flow to A01 while their flow to A04 drops [DIR002, DIR004, DIR005]; A01's own transitions lead mostly to A04 [DIR001]; A01 runs before A04 in about 20 in 100 traces [REL002]; and the A03→A04 distance shifts from adjacent to two apart, the signature of an activity placed in between [LAG001].

Limitations: the insertion is partial — direct A03→A04, A10→A04 and A11→A04 flows persist, and A01 sometimes leads to A13 or A11 instead of A04 [DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, DIR001]. With A11, A01 is always interleaved or repeated rather than in a fixed order [REL004], so the evidence cannot fully rule out that part of A01's execution runs concurrently with the surrounding flow rather than strictly between it and A04. Zero participation earlier strictly means only that A01 was not observed in the earlier sample, although its appearance together with the consistent rerouting makes an added step the best-supported reading.

Other differences: repeats of A04 and A11, and the distances from A03 and A10 to A11, are spaced about one position further apart in the later window [LAG003, LAG008, LAG002, LAG006]; these are consistent with the same insertion but are not part of the main hypothesis.
