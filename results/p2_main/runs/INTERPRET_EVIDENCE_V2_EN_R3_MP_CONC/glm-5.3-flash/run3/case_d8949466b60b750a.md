### Observed differences

- Participation changed sharply. In the earlier window, A27 appeared in about 52 in 100 traces, and A32, A33 and A38 each in about 48–49 in 100 traces, while A26 and A28 appeared in every trace. In the later window, A27 appears in about 99–100 in 100 traces (once per trace, mean 1.01), whereas A32, A33 and A38 each appear in only about 1 in 200 traces; A26 and A28 are still present throughout. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS001]
- In the earlier window the process alternated between two branches after A26: either A27 alone, or the group A32, A33, A38, which always ran together (co-occurrence about 48–49 in 100 traces) and practically never together with A27 (co-occurrence at most about 1 in 200). In the later window, A27 runs in nearly every trace and the three group activities are almost never seen, alone or together. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- Routing out of A26 concentrated on one successor. Before, about 51 in 100 of A26's outgoing transitions went to A27 and about 48 in 100 to A32/A33/A38; in the later window, about 97 in 100 go to A27. A27's own successor is essentially unchanged: A28 immediately follows in about 97 in 100 cases before and in virtually all cases after. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG004]
- Ordering and distance: before, A26 preceded A27 and A27 preceded A28 on one branch, while A32, A33 and A38 ran between A26 and A28 in varying internal order (A28 came two steps after A26 in about 50 in 100 traces but four steps after in about 47 in 100). In the later window, A26 → A27 → A28 with a two-step distance holds in about 97 in 100 traces. [REL001, REL005, REL009, REL010, REL011, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001]
- A28's relative position moved slightly earlier, from about 0.55 to about 0.53 of the trace. [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch in which A32, A33 and A38 were executed between A26 and A28 is no longer executed: in the earlier window roughly half of the cases ran A26 → A27 → A28 and the other half ran A26 → A32/A33/A38 (in varying order) → A28, while in the later window virtually all cases run A26 → A27 → A28, with A27 executed once per case and A32, A33 and A38 observed in only about 1 of 200 traces each. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL005, REL012, REL013, REL014, LAG001]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: 35 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.6 expected if nothing had changed, and the occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators all show the same difference — the group's co-occurrence collapsed from about 48–49 in 100 traces to about 0, A26's outgoing transitions concentrated on A27 from about 51% to about 97%, and the four-step A26→A28 distance disappeared. All items exceed the differences seen in about 99 of 100 random re-splits, although that alone cannot separate a real change from a rare variant that vanished from the sample. [DIR001, REL012, REL013, REL014, LAG001]

The evidence cannot distinguish complete removal of the branch from it becoming optional and skipped in every case, since each of A32, A33 and A38 still appears once in the later window; nor can it rule out that the two windows cover case populations that differ for reasons other than a process modification. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Other differences: A28's relative position shifted slightly earlier (mean about 0.55 to about 0.53 of the trace), consistent with traces becoming shorter once the branch disappeared; A27's successor structure is essentially unchanged. [POS001, DIR002, LAG004]
