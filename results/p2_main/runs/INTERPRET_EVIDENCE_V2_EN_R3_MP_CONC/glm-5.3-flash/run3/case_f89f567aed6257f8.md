### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A01 was not observed in any of the 200 traces; in the later window it runs in about 56 in 100 traces (112 of 200), averages about 2.4 occurrences per trace overall (roughly four per trace that contains it) and sits around the middle of the trace (median position about 0.49). [OCC001, POS001]
- A01 has become a routing hub: of its observed outgoing transitions in the later window, roughly a quarter each go to A09 (about 27%), A02 (about 25%), A11 (about 24%) and A05 (about 23%); earlier it had no observed outgoing transitions. Flow into A01 is also new: A09 and A13 now send all of their observed outgoing transitions to A01 (previously to A02/A11), and A08, A10 and A17 send about 53%, 53% and 58% respectively (previously none). [DIR001, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR009, DIR011]
- A01 now co-occurs with activities it never appeared together with before: with A03 and with A06 in about 56 in 100 later traces, with A08 in about 47 in 100, and with A02, A11 and A16 in about 46 in 100; all of these co-occurrence shares were 0 in the earlier window. [REL002, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL012]
- A14 disappears: it appeared exactly once in every earlier trace (200 of 200), at the end of the trace (median at the final position), and in no later trace (0 of 200). Before, A03 and A14 always ran together in mixed order (A03 first in about 53 in 100 traces, A14 first in about 48 in 100); in the later window A03 appears in every trace and A14 in none. [OCC003, POS006, REL017]
- The trace ending is reshaped: A06, which earlier split its outgoing flow between A03 (about 52%) and A14 (about 47%), now goes to A03 in all observed cases and immediately so (one event apart in essentially all later cases, versus about half before), and A03 sits at the final position in every later trace (mean position 1.0, previously about 0.95) with no observed outgoing transitions. [DIR002, DIR004, LAG015, POS002]
- A05 is executed much less often: about 62 in 100 traces before (123 traces, mean about 2.0 occurrences) versus about 29 in 100 after (57 traces, mean about 1.2). Secondary rewiring accompanies this: A05's outgoing flow now goes to A13 (about 49%) where it previously went to A16, A11's outgoing flow now goes to A16 (about 50%) where it previously went to A05 (about 54%), and A11's typical position moved later (mean about 0.36 to 0.46). [OCC002, DIR003, DIR008, POS005]
- Many pairwise event distances shifted consistently with this rewiring, for example A11 to A03 is now typically 4 events apart instead of 5, and A16 to A03 is 3 apart in nearly all cases instead of a 3/4 split. [LAG037, LAG061]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the flow's wiring: A01, unobserved in all 200 earlier traces, became an active, often repeated step in the later window (about 56 in 100 traces, around mid-trace), receiving flow from A08, A09, A10, A13 and A17 and distributing it to A02, A05, A09 and A11, while A14 — previously executed once in every trace at the end — is no longer observed, and A03 has become the terminal step that always follows A06 directly. [OCC001, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS006, DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR009, DIR011, REL017]

This is supported directly and consistently: the participation counts have full support on both sides (0 vs 112 of 200 traces for A01; 200 vs 0 for A14), which sampling variability cannot plausibly produce, the occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators all point the same way, and the screening comparison expected about 1 qualifying item by chance while 129 were found. The pattern is one of activities becoming observed and unobserved with rewired transitions, not a shift in how often existing paths are taken. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR006, REL002]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether A01 is a genuinely new step or one that previously occurred so rarely it never appeared, nor whether A14 was dropped or effectively replaced (A01 now appears in about 56 in 100 traces where A14 previously appeared in all, and the two never appear together in either window); zero participation only means an activity was not observed. All items are control-flow measures based on event positions, so no timing or resource aspect is covered. [OCC001, OCC003, REL011]

Other differences: A05 is executed in far fewer traces (about 62 in 100 before versus 29 in 100 after) with shifted outgoing flow (A16 to A13), A11's outgoing flow moved from A05 to A16 with a later typical position, and numerous pairwise event distances changed (for example A11–A03 and A16–A03 now one event closer). These may be downstream consequences of the same rewiring rather than independent changes; the evidence cannot separate the two. [OCC002, DIR003, DIR008, POS005, LAG037, LAG061]
