### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new activity A01 was appended as the final step of every case, executed exactly once and immediately after A06, which had previously been the last activity of a trace. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: A01's participation went from none to all cases, a new edge A06 → A01 now carries all of A06's outgoing flow, the pair co-occurs in every later trace with A06 first, and A01 takes the end position while A06 slips from the end to just before it [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, POS001, POS002]. The item count (five versus about 0.04 expected without change) rules out chance sampling as an explanation for the pattern as a whole.

Limitations: the occurrence, direction, position and relation items partly describe the same underlying trace change, so they are consistent but not independent confirmations. Zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A01 was not observed there; the evidence cannot rule out that A01 previously ran under a different label. Only these activities were screened, so differences outside them would not appear in this evidence.
