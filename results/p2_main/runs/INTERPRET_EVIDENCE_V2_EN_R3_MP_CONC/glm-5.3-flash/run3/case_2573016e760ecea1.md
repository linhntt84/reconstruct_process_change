Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A06 was removed from, or is now bypassed in, the sequence between A05 and A04: in the earlier window roughly half the traces executed A05 → A06 → A04, and in the later window those traces execute A05 → A04 directly, with A06 no longer observed. Screening produced 9 qualifying items where only about 0.07 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is unlikely under an unchanged process. The redirection is shown directly by A05's outgoing transitions switching from A06 to A04 and by the A05→A04 distance shrinking from two steps to one, and it is consistent across the occurrence, position and relation indicators, which largely reflect the same underlying traces.
