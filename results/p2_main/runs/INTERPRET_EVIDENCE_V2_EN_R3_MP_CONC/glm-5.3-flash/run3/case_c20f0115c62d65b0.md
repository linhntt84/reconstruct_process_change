### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every case executed exactly one of three alternative activities — A19 in about 32 of 100 cases, A20 in about 28, A25 in about 40 — and no case contained more than one of them. In the later window, each of the three appears in only about 1 of 200 cases. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006]
- Before, each of the three always ran between A27 and A29: each co-occurred with A27 and with A29 in every case where it appeared, always after A27 and before A29, while its position relative to A28 varied (before or after it). In the later window, A27, A28 and A29 still occur in essentially every case, but without any of the three. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, DIR004, DIR005, POS004]
- Routing consolidated around the remaining backbone: before, about 53 in 100 of A27's outgoing transitions went to A28 and about 43 went to A25/A19/A20; about 45 in 100 of A28's transitions went to A29 and about 54 went to the three. In the later window, about 97 in 100 of A27's transitions go to A28 and about 98 in 100 of A28's go to A29. [DIR004, DIR005]
- The A27→A29 distance shortened from typically three steps (about 94 in 100 cases) to typically two (about 97 in 100), and A27→A28 and A28→A29 became immediate neighbours in about 98 of 100 cases, versus roughly half before. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- The single later trace containing each of A19, A20 and A25 shows a successor not seen in the earlier window, but with one observation each these profiles cannot be characterised. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the per-case intermediate step between A27 and A29 — exactly one of A19, A20 or A25, previously executed in every case — is no longer performed in the later window, so cases run directly A27 → A28 → A29. The change is shown directly and consistently across indicator families: participation of the three collapsed from 64, 56 and 80 of 200 traces to 1 each; A27 and A28 now route almost exclusively to A28 and A29; the A27→A29 distance shortened by one step; and A27 occurs without any of the three in about 995 of 1000 later cases. For context, 27 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.55 expected if nothing had changed, so the set of findings as a whole is far beyond what chance sampling produces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG002, LAG003, REL003, REL010]

The evidence cannot distinguish whether the step was removed from the process, became optional but is now always skipped, or was replaced by behaviour not visible in these observations; the later profiles of A19, A20 and A25 rest on a single trace each and were not used to characterise the change. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: A29's relative position within the trace shifted slightly earlier (mean relative position about 0.56 to 0.55) while it still occurs in every case in both windows — a minor shift consistent with the main hypothesis rather than a separate behaviour. [POS004]
