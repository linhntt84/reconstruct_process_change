### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A change in timing is supported: in the later window, the elapsed time between consecutive completions leading into A17 was about twice as long as before, both from A18 (median about 0.14 → 0.30 hours) and from A29 (median about 0.91 → 1.81 hours). [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

Supporting signs: the time family produced 2 qualifying items where roughly 0.007 would be expected if nothing had changed, so chance alone cannot account for the pair; both items show every quantile shifted upward by a similar factor, both exceed about 1999 of 2000 random re-splits in difference size, and only these 2 of 15 time units qualified, which localises the change to the transitions into A17 rather than a uniform slowdown of the whole process. Limitations: TWAIT measures elapsed time between consecutive completion events, so the evidence cannot distinguish whether A17's own completion was delayed, the predecessors finished later, or waiting between the two events grew. The gap counts are modest (27 vs 23, and 50 vs 45), and because both gaps end at A17 the two items may reflect one underlying difference rather than two independent confirmations.
