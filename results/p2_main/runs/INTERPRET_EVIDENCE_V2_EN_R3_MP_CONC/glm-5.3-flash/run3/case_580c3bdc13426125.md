### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 was replaced by A03 at the same point in the flow, with the repetition loop restructured around A06. In the earlier window every trace ran A02 (about 2.2 times per trace): A05 led to A02, A02 branched to A06 or A07 (about 55/45), and A06 always returned to A02, so A02 repeated in about 59 in 100 traces. In the later window A02 is gone; A03 runs exactly once per trace between A05 and the same branch (about 50/50 to A06 and A07), and the loop now repeats A06 itself (A06 leading back to itself in about 48 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, with adjacent repeats) before the flow exits to A07. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG003, LAG004, REL005, REL007]

Supporting signs: the screening produced 21 qualifying control-flow items where about 0.12 would be expected if nothing had changed, far beyond what random re-splits of a stable process yield; participation, routing, relative-position and relation indicators all describe the same swap consistently, with A02 and A03 never co-occurring and A05 preceding A03 in every later trace. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, REL001, REL005]

Limitations: the activity codes are anonymous, so the evidence cannot rule out that A03 is simply A02 under a new label rather than a genuinely different activity; positions are event positions, not clock times, so "same place" refers to sequence position only, and the evidence cannot establish what caused the difference.
