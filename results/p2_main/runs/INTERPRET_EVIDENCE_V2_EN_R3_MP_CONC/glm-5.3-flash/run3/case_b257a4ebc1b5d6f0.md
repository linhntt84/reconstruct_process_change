### Observed differences

- Two multi-activity segments exchanged their places in the sequence. In the earlier window, A44 led into the block A20/A21/A22/A24/A25 (each 12–19% of A44's outgoing transitions), that block ended in A29 (each member reached A29 in about 16–28% of its outgoing transitions and never A34), then A33 led to one of A19/A23/A26/A27/A28 (each 16–22%), which ran on to A34 (90–98%). In the later window the mirror image holds: A44 leads to one of A19/A23/A26/A27/A28 (each 16–24%), which ends in A29 (about 90 in 100), while A33 opens the A20/A21/A22/A24/A25 block (each 16–22%), which now ends in A34 (16–22%) with A29 no longer observed as its target. The five choice activities each occur in roughly 20–27 in 100 traces (essentially one per trace), the five block activities in essentially every trace, in both windows. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, two segments run in exchanged order: the five-way choice between A19, A23, A26, A27 and A28 — previously placed after A33 and ending at A34 — now runs after A44 and ends at A29, while the block A20, A21, A22, A24 and A25, executed in every trace in varying order — previously after A44 and ending at A29 — now runs after A33 and ends at A34. This rests on direct relational and successor evidence: near-complete switches of the dominant successor of all ten activities (about 90 or more in 100 outgoing transitions), reversed before/after relations in roughly twenty activity pairs with co-occurrence levels unchanged, opposite position shifts, and matching adjacency changes; the screening context also makes chance an unlikely explanation for the ensemble, with 105 control-flow items qualifying where about 1 would be expected without a change. Limitations: the evidence uses event positions rather than clock time, and it cannot distinguish a genuine exchange of the segments from an equivalent re-routing of the connectors A44, A33, A29 and A34 to the other segment; small residuals of each order (about 2–3 in 100) remain in both windows.
