### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A43 and A28 was restructured: the five activities A15, A16, A25, A26 and A27, which executed in every case in varying order, are no longer executed, and in their place exactly one activity chosen from the alternatives A14, A17, A18, A19 and A20 runs in each case, again after A43 and before A28. The participation flips (100 of 100 traces to 0, and 0 to about 16–24 of 100, on 200-trace windows) are direct; the relational items show A43 now precedes only the new activities and the new activities lead only to A28, mirroring how the old block was anchored; and the five alternatives are mutually exclusive while jointly covering all later traces. The screening flagged 94 control-flow items where fewer than 1 (0.73) would be expected without a change, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process produces. [OCC002, OCC008, OCC001, OCC004, DIR011, DIR001, REL019, REL035, REL010, screening]

Limitations: zero participation shows only that the old activities were not observed later — removal from the process versus simply no longer being exercised cannot be distinguished — and the evidence cannot show how the choice among the five alternatives is made. An important competing explanation is that the two windows sample two coexisting process variants whose mix shifted completely between the windows; such a variant-mix shift would look identical here and cannot be ruled out from these windows alone. [OCC002, OCC001]

Other differences: apart from the position shifts of A28 (earlier) and A43 (later), which fit the replacement above rather than being separate changes, the evidence shows no further distinct behavioural differences at these thresholds. [POS011, POS012]
