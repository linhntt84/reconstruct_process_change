### Observed differences

- The successors of A10 and A11 changed almost completely. In the earlier window, A10's outgoing transitions went to A06 (about 51 in 100) and A07 (about 49 in 100), and A11's to A06 (about 58 in 100) and A07 (about 42 in 100); in the later window, essentially all observed outgoing transitions of both A10 and A11 go to A08. - A08's outgoing behaviour spread out: before, it led almost only to A10 (about 50 in 100) and A11 (about 48 in 100); in the later window its outgoing transitions divide across A06 (about 29 in 100), A10 (about 27 in 100), A07 (about 24 in 100) and A11 (about 19 in 100). [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Twenty control-flow items qualified, against roughly 0.24 expected by chance among the 48 tested units, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled traces, so this ensemble is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013, LAG014, LAG015, LAG016, LAG017]

The evidence supports the possibility that the hand-over between the A10/A11 block and the A06/A07 block was re-routed through A08: previously A08 led to A10 or A11, which led directly on to A06 or A07, whereas in the later window A10 and A11 lead to A08, and A08 leads on to A06 or A07 and back to A10 or A11. The near-total successor change at A10 and A11 shows this directly, the gap shifts (A10/A11→A06/A07 no longer adjacent, A08→A06/A07 closer) match it, and A08's increased recurrence — with A08 still directly preceding A10 or A11 in part of the later window (gap 1 in about 13 and 24 in 100 observations) — fits a repetition between A08 and A10/A11. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013, LAG015]

The evidence cannot fully distinguish a repositioning of A08 from a new repetition between A08 and A10/A11, since both produce these signatures; nor can it rule out that A10 and A11 still occasionally lead directly to A06/A07, because an unobserved transition need not be absent, which would make this a drastic share shift rather than a complete re-routing. Several spacing profiles rest on few traces (for example A11→A10 with about 10–11 traces), so the detailed gaps are tentative. [DIR002, LAG017]

Other differences: A10 and A11 occur somewhat less often (outgoing transitions about 63 to 55 and about 60 to 39 per 200 traces), and the A06→A07 and A06→A06 sequences appear in fewer traces (about 46 to 34 and 25 to 17); these may be consequences of the same re-routing rather than separate changes.
