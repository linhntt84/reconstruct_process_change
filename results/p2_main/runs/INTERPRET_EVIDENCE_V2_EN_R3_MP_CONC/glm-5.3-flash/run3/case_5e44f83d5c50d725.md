### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 is no longer executed, so that the sequence A05 → A01 → A07 is replaced by a direct A05 → A07 step. A01 fell from participation in all 200 earlier traces to none and its outgoing flow vanished; A05's successor share moved from A01 (about 51 in 100) to A07 (about 49 in 100) while its other branch to A06 stayed at about 45–46 in 100; the A05→A07 distance shrank from typically 2 steps to 1; and A01's co-occurrence with A05 and A07 dropped from universal to zero. Ten qualifying items against roughly 0.08 expected by chance for the 16 tested units makes a chance origin of the ensemble implausible. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR003, LAG001, REL001, REL002]

Other differences: A04's outgoing transitions shifted mildly — A01 (about 3–4 in 100) is no longer observed as a destination and A07 rose from about 2 to about 7 in 100, with A05 still dominant (about 92 vs 87 in 100); this mirrors the same underlying difference but involves A04, which is not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR002]

An important competing explanation remains: zero participation only shows A01 was not observed in the later window, so the evidence cannot distinguish a complete removal of A01 from the step becoming very rare or its branch never being taken. [OCC001]
