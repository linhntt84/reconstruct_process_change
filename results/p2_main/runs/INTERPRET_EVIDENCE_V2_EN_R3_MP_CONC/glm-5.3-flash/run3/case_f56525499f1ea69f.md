### Observed differences

- Six activities — A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 — moved from optional to near-universal: in the earlier window each took part in about 49–52 of 100 traces; in the later window each takes part in about 98–100 of 100. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009]
- In the earlier window these six formed two mutually exclusive groups: A33/A35/A36 ran together in about 49 of 100 traces (never separately), A37/A38/A39 ran together in about 52 of 100, and cross-group pairs such as A33 with A37 never co-occurred — each trace executed exactly one group, split roughly 50/50. In the later window every cross-group pair co-occurs in about 98 of 100 traces. [REL037, REL049, REL039, REL046]
- In the later window the order between the groups is fixed: A33, A35 or A36 precedes A37, A38 or A39 in about 97–98 of 100 traces (essentially every co-occurrence). Within each group both orders still appear in similar proportions (about 50/50), in both windows. [REL039, REL044, REL048, REL037, REL050]
- A30's outgoing transitions changed: earlier they spread almost evenly over all six group activities (about 14–18 per 100 each); in the later window they go almost only to A33, A35 and A36 (about 30–37 per 100 each), and direct transitions to A37, A38 or A39 have nearly disappeared (about 1 per 100 or fewer). [DIR006]
- A31 is now almost always preceded by the first group: A33/A35/A36 before A31 rose from about 48–50 of 100 traces to about 98 of 100. [REL031, REL032, REL036]
- Spacing grew accordingly: the distance from A30 to A37/A38/A39, and from A33/A35/A36 to A31, was mostly 1–3 event positions in the earlier window and is mostly 4–5 positions in the later window, consistent with the other group now being executed in between. [LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]
- Event positions shifted consistently: A33/A35/A36 now sit earlier in the trace (median about 0.76 to about 0.69 of trace length), A37/A38/A39 later (about 0.74 to about 0.77–0.78), A30 earlier (mean about 0.67 to 0.62) and A31 later (about 0.80 to 0.83). [POS006, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010, POS011, POS004, POS005]
- A04 became rarer and rerouted: participation fell from about 31 to 14 of 100 traces, and its outgoing transitions moved from about 81 per 100 towards A16 to about 36, with new transitions to A15 (about 18), A48 (about 14) and A46, A30, A34 (about 7 each); this rests on 64 and 28 observed outgoing transitions. [OCC001, DIR002, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the routing between the two groups changed from exclusive alternatives to sequential execution: in the earlier window each trace executed either {A33, A35, A36} or {A37, A38, A39} (about 50/50, never both), while in the later window nearly every trace executes both groups in sequence — first A33/A35/A36, then A37/A38/A39 — with A30 leading into the first group and A31 following it. The signs are direct and consistent: cross-group co-occurrence reversed from 0 to about 98 of 100 traces with a fixed between-group order, participation of all six activities rose from about 50 to about 99 of 100, A30 lost its direct transitions to the second group, and step distances grew from 1–3 to 4–5 positions, as expected if the second group now runs between A30 and A37/A38/A39 and between the first group and A31. [REL039, REL046, OCC004, OCC007, DIR006, LAG003, LAG006]

This is a structural and participation change, not a re-weighting of unchanged paths: cross-group pairs never co-occurred earlier, so a mere shift in path shares cannot produce the later pattern. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is a rare pre-existing variant executing both groups (below roughly 2 of 100 traces) that the 200-trace earlier window could have missed; the volume of qualifying items (89 control-flow items against about 1 expected without change) makes chance an unlikely explanation of the pattern as a whole. Within-group order remains mixed, so the evidence fixes the sequence of the groups but not a fixed order inside either group. [REL039, REL037, REL050]

Other differences: A04's falling participation and rerouting (above) are not part of this hypothesis; A04 also now appears almost only in traces that contain the six group activities (in about 85 of 100 later traces the groups run without A04, and A04 never runs without them). A06 and A10 became more frequent, from about 3 to about 11–12 of 100 traces, but on few observations (6 before/22 after and 5 before/24 after traces). The outgoing transitions of A02 and A08 also changed markedly, but rest on only 13/23 and 9/28 observed transitions. [OCC001, DIR002, REL001, POS001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003]
