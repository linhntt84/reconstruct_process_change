### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the control flow around A12: the repeating step A17, which earlier followed A12 in about half of the traces and then led to A15 and A14, is no longer executed in the later window, and A12 now passes directly to A15 (about 29 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) and A14 (about 22), while its link to A16 continues at a similar share; at the same time, a new step A01 appears early in about 38 in 100 later traces, from which the flow branches to several activities (A09, A07, A12, A04), typically after A02 has run. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS003, REL001, REL006]

Supporting signs: 27 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.3 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the set as a whole cannot be a sampling artefact; the central items show the change directly — participation of 105 out of 200 traces down to 0 for A17 and 0 up to 77 for A01, the altered successor profiles of A12 and A01, and the collapse of A17 co-occurrences — and the shortened gaps between A12, A14 and A15 fit the repeated A17 events no longer sitting between them. Several items describe the same underlying behaviour (the A17 items in particular), so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC004, DIR002, REL006, LAG003, LAG004]

Limitations: zero participation means only that A17 was not observed in the later sample; the evidence cannot rule out that the later window simply contains no traces of a variant that includes A17 (or excludes A01), although the disappearance of an activity seen in over half of 200 earlier traces is unlikely to arise by chance. The gap findings rest on small pair counts. [OCC004, POS003, LAG007]

Other differences: the share of traces containing A06 fell from about 52 to about 31 in 100, and A08 from about 47 to about 30; the evidence does not show how these decreases relate to the restructuring above. [OCC002, OCC003]
