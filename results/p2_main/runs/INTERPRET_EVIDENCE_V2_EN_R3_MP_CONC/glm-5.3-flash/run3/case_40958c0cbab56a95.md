### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the always-executed trio A26–A27–A28, which earlier ran together in every case between A44 and A29, no longer forms part of the flow in the later window, and that a single activity chosen from the alternatives A19, A20 and A21 (about 30, 33 and 38 of 100 traces, mutually exclusive in this sample) now executes in the same position: after A44, before A29, about one third into the trace. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL013, REL014, REL021, REL018, REL019, REL025, REL026, REL032, REL033, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, LAG009]

The change is not a re-weighting of existing paths: the old activities fell from 100 of 100 traces to about 1–2 of 100 while the new ones rose from about 1–2 to 30–38 of 100, indicating removal and insertion rather than shifted path shares. The relational and lag evidence ties both old and new activities into the same slot (A44 before, A29 after; the A44-to-A29 distance shrank from 4–5 to 2–3 steps), and with 81 qualifying items against roughly 1 expected by chance in the control-flow family, sampling alone cannot explain the pattern as a whole. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL018, REL019, REL038, REL043, DIR007, LAG009]

Limitations: the later routing profiles of A26/A27/A28 and most lag distributions of the old block rest on only 1–3 observations, and the earlier profiles of the new activities on 2–4, so behaviour at the block's edges is weakly documented; the evidence also cannot rule out that the old path survives as a rare optional branch, since A26/A27/A28 were still observed in about 1–2 of 100 later traces, making a partial rather than complete replacement possible. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]
