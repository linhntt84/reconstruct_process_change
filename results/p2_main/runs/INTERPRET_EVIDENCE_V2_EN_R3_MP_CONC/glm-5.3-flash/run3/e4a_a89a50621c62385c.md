### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a change in timing: the elapsed time between consecutive completion events increased on the transitions ending at A17 — from A16 the typical gap roughly doubled (median about 4 to about 8 minutes) and from A28 it roughly doubled (median about 47 to about 95 minutes), in both cases with the entire distribution shifted upward while the number of observed gaps stayed nearly the same (45 → 37 and 42 → 41). Supporting signs: two time items against roughly 0.014 expected without change in the time family, both differences larger than all but about 1 in 2000 random re-splits, a consistent direction across all quantiles, and no control-flow items, which fits a timing change that needs no structural counterpart.
