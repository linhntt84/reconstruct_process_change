### Observed differences

- Before, A07 and A08 always appeared together: both activities were present in the same case in about 53 in 100 cases, and neither ever appeared without the other. In the later window they never share a case: A07 appears alone in about 23 in 100 cases and A08 alone in about 29 in 100, while the share of cases containing neither stayed roughly stable (about 47 before, about 49 after). [REL001]
- Before, the pair was executed in both orders in similar proportions (A07 before A08 in about 28 in 100 cases, A08 before A07 in about 25), i.e. without a fixed order between them; in the later window neither order occurs at all. [REL001]
- Before, the two activities mostly followed each other: about 65 in 100 outgoing transitions of A07 went to A08, and about 61 in 100 of A08 went to A07. In the later window these mutual transitions are no longer observed; both activities now lead mainly to A01 (about 72 from A07, about 71 from A08), with the remainder going to A05 (about 28 and 29). [DIR001, DIR002]
- Participation of each activity roughly halved: A07 from about 53 in 100 cases to about 23, A08 from about 53 to about 29, with no repetitions within a case in either window. Their combined footprint stayed about the same (about 53 in 100 cases involved both before; about 51 involve exactly one after). [OCC001, OCC002, REL001]
- A08's median event position moved slightly later (from about 0.7 to 1.0 in event positions). [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the joint execution of A07 and A08 was dissolved into mutually exclusive execution: in the earlier window a case typically contained both activities in either order, whereas in the later window a case contains at most one of them, and whichever executes flows mainly to A01 instead of to the other activity. This rests on direct relational evidence — co-occurrence falling from about 53 in 100 cases to zero while one-of-the-two shares rose to about 23 and 29 — corroborated by the disappearance of the mutual A07↔A08 transitions, the rerouting of both activities to A01, and the halving of each activity's individual participation while their combined footprint stayed at about half of the cases. All six qualifying items (against roughly 0.11 expected by chance) describe this one coherent shift, and each difference is larger than about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the data. [REL001, DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, OCC002]

Limitations: the later-window routing profiles rest on few observations (about 18 outgoing transitions per activity), so the exact new split between A01 and A05 is uncertain, and a low residual rate of joint execution below the observable level cannot be ruled out; the evidence also cannot show what, if anything, replaced the joint execution of the pair. [DIR001, DIR002, REL001]

Other differences: A08's event position shifted slightly later (median from about 0.7 to 1.0), which is plausibly a byproduct of the changed relation rather than an independent relocation. [POS001]
