### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the routing through A31 and A32 reversed: earlier the dominant sequence ran A32 into A31, which then exited to A28, whereas in the later window A31 typically feeds A32, which then exits to A28, with A27's entries into the pair shifting in the same direction (more to A31, fewer to A32). This rests on mirrored successor-share reversals at both A31 and A32 (about 64/36 becoming about 38/62), a corroborating shift at A27 while A33 and A34 remained stable, three control-flow items against roughly 0.29 expected by chance for that family, and differences larger than about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits. Limitations: DIR002 and DIR003 view the same underlying behaviour from two endpoints rather than independently confirming it, and because DIR shows only successor shares, a strict exchange of the two activities' order versus a change in routing probabilities between the same activities cannot be separated without positional or relational evidence, which this evidence does not contain.
