### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative block A19/A20/A25 — one of which executes in essentially every case — moved from before A26 to after A27. In the earlier window the flow ran from A45 into one of A19/A20/A25, then to A26, with A27 → A28 → A32 further on; in the later window A45 connects straight to A26, and the block executes after A27, leading into A28 and A32 instead of into A26. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL001, REL003, REL006]

The support is direct and consistent: all six order relations between the block and A26/A27 reversed [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007]; the successor changes in DIR show the same rewiring from both directions [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006]; the A27-to-A32 gap grew by one step, as expected when the block is inserted after A27 [LAG006, LAG009]; and the block's positions shifted later while A26 and A27 held theirs [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]. Screening qualified 31 control-flow items where roughly 0.62 would be expected without a change — about 50 times the chance expectation — so the ensemble cannot be explained as sampling noise [OCC001, DIR001, REL001].

Limitations: the later structure is not one fixed sequence, since after A27 the block activity and A28 occur in either order before A32 [DIR004, DIR005, LAG005, LAG009, REL005, REL008]; and two finite windows cannot rule out that the difference reflects a shift in the mix of two coexisting process variants rather than a modification of a single process [REL001, DIR001].

Other differences: A24's participation rose from about 43 to 58 of 100 traces; the evidence does not connect this to the relocation above. [OCC001]
