### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the direction in which the A36/A37 pair is traversed reversed. In the earlier window the pairwise distances are consistent with a typical flow of A32 → A37 → A36 → A33 (A32→A37 mostly at distance 1, A37→A36 about 62 in 100 of A37's transitions, exit mainly via A36→A33 at about 59 in 100); in the later window it typically runs A32 → A36 → A37 → A33 (A32→A36 mostly at distance 1, A36→A37 about 62 in 100 of A36's transitions and always at distance 1, exit mainly via A37→A33 at about 58 in 100) [DIR001, DIR002, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]. The signals are direct and mutually consistent: the successor shares of A36 and A37 flipped in opposite directions, the distance profiles from A32 to each of them flipped in step, and the A36→A37 step became uniformly immediate and more frequent; seven control-flow items qualified in total against roughly 0.48 expected if nothing had changed, so chance alone cannot account for the set, and each item's difference exceeded about 199 of 200 random re-splits [DIR001, DIR002, POS001, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]. Limitations: both orderings still occur in both windows (for instance, A32→A37 at distance 1 still accounts for about 38 in 100 pairs in the later window), so this is a reversal of the dominant order rather than a complete replacement of one path by another; the five items around A32, A36, A37 and A33 describe overlapping trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations, and the shares are over observed transitions and pairs rather than over cases [DIR001, DIR002, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004].

Other differences: A07 occurs in a larger share of traces in the later window (107 versus 85 of 200) and A14 follows it at a shorter event distance (distance 1 in about 46 in 100 pairs versus about 28 in 100); with only positional and lag evidence for these two activities, the evidence cannot show whether an activity between them was removed, their order changed, or only their spacing shifted, so this remains a separate, less specified difference [POS001, LAG001].
