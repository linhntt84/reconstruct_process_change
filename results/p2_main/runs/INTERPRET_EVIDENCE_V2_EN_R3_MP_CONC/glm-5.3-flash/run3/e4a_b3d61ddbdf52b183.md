### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in timing: in the later window, the elapsed time between the completion of A33 and the following completion of A34 is about 0.5 hour instead of about 1.0 hour. This is shown directly by the interval indicator for exactly this pair of consecutive completions; the mean, median and both quartiles each drop by roughly half an hour and the distribution tightens; the interval is measured in all 200 traces of both windows; and the difference exceeds about 999.5 of 1,000 random re-splits, in a time family that produced one item where only about 0.005 were expected without change.
