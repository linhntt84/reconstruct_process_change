### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in timing as a possibility: in the later window, the elapsed time between consecutive completions roughly doubled for two activity pairs — A24 to A16 (median from about 55 to about 112 minutes) and A04 to A03 (from about 3.5 to about 9 minutes). Supporting signs: both items measure exactly these gaps, both show consistent increases across the distribution, both differences exceed essentially all random re-splits, and the screening yield far exceeds chance (2 qualifying time items among 54 tested units where about 0.027 would be expected without change), while the control-flow family produced no items, so no accompanying structural reconfiguration was detected [TWAIT001, TWAIT002].
