### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The screening produced 114 qualifying control-flow items where about 1.2 would be expected without a change, far beyond what an unchanged process yields, and each difference exceeds about 199 in 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. The behaviour they consistently describe supports the possibility that, in the later window, the segment formed by A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 — previously executed exactly once per trace in a fixed order — is repeated about three times per trace, with A38 leading back to A30 in about two thirds of its outgoing transitions, consistent with the segment being restarted from its beginning. The occurrence items show this directly (means tripling while participation stays at 100 in 100 and within-trace recurrence rises from about 0 to 67 in 100); the many relation and lag items largely mirror this same underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: the exact internal structure of the repeated segment cannot be established, and the before-window self-repetition profiles rest on single observations. [OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, REL034, REL049, REL053, DIR007, LAG002, LAG004]

Other differences: A10's strong decline (about 71 to about 25 in 100 traces, with appearances moving to early trace positions and A44 now leading mostly to A45 instead of A10), A22's slightly lower and later participation, and the higher participation of the rare activities A02, A04, A06 and A08 are observed but are not part of this hypothesis. [OCC005, POS001, DIR008, POS004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the three-fold execution reflects three separate passes through the activities rather than one repeated segment, and that A10's decline is an independent shift rather than connected to the repetition; the evidence shows the behaviours consistently but not the mechanism linking them. [OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, DIR008]
