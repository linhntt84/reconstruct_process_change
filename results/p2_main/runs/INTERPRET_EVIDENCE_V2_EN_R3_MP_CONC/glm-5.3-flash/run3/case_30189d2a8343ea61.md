### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A possibility supported by the evidence is that, in the later window, one of five previously unobserved alternative activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) is executed between A43 and A28, which previously followed one another directly: each alternative runs in roughly one in five traces, exactly one of them appears per affected trace, it follows A43 and precedes A28. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG002, REL013, REL014, REL020, REL021]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the five activities move from zero (or near-zero) participation to about 18–23 in 100 traces; A43's outgoing transitions shift from A28 to the five, and each of the five leads to A28; the A43–A28 distance shifts from one event to two; and the order relations place each new activity after A43 and before A28 in every trace in which it occurs. The volume of qualified evidence (50 items, all control-flow, against about 0.68 expected without a change) is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, LAG002, REL013, REL014, REL020, REL021, screening]

Limitations: absence from the earlier window means only that the four activities were not observed there, not that they were absent; whether a direct A43→A28 path still exists in the later window rests on about 1 in 100 of A43's outgoing transitions; and the evidence cannot show whether the new step is mandatory in the traces that contain it. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the rerouting of A43 and the appearance of the five activities are independent changes that happen to align, although their tight co-occurrence and consistent ordering make a single connected change the more direct reading.

Other differences: A06 changed from a rare activity around the middle of the trace with outgoing transitions spread over many successors to a late activity: in about a quarter of later traces it runs after A48 (and after A43 and A28) and directly before A49, and A48 now leads to it in about 24 in 100 of its outgoing transitions. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR008, LAG003, REL006, REL007, REL008]
