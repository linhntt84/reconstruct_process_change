### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening qualified 49 control-flow items against about 0.3 expected by chance alone if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeds those seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled traces, so the pattern as a whole is not what a stable process typically produces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, DIR002, REL012]

The evidence supports the possibility that the control flow was restructured in the later window rather than that existing branches were merely taken with different frequencies: A01, which earlier closed about a third of the traces, is no longer observed; a new closing block in which A08 and A12 run adjacently in either order now ends about 48 in 100 traces, ending A12's strictly final role; A09 now runs between A06 and A07 on a path present in about 18 in 100 traces (A06 leads to A09 in every observed case, and A09 leads on to A07 in about 9 of 10 cases); and A03 and A10, previously executed together in every trace without a fixed order, became mutually exclusive alternatives, each appearing in about half of the traces and never together. [OCC001, OCC004, POS001, POS003, OCC007, POS005, DIR003, DIR006, REL023, OCC005, DIR002, DIR004, LAG002, OCC002, OCC006, REL012] Because activities appear, disappear and switch between joint and exclusive execution, this reads as a structural change rather than a re-weighting of unchanged paths.

An important competing explanation remains: the windows are finite samples of 200 traces each, and the evidence cannot distinguish a modified process from a shifted mix of case types (for example, a new variant covering about half of the later cases); a zero participation share only means an activity was not observed in that window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004]

Other differences: A04 participates in more traces (about 37 in 100 earlier, 52 in 100 later) and A06 in somewhat fewer (about 25 in 100 earlier, 18 in 100 later); A07 is present throughout in both windows at a slightly later median relative position (0.4 to 0.5). [OCC003, REL008, POS002, REL003]
