### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the elapsed time between consecutive completions from each of A16, A17, A18 and A19 to A28 sat at about 1 hour (means roughly 1.0–1.1 hours, medians about 1.0–1.1 hours). In the later window the same four intervals sat at about 2 hours (means roughly 2.0–2.1 hours), an increase of about 1 hour — roughly a doubling — on every one of the four transitions [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004].
- The intervals also became more spread out: standard deviations rose from about 0.14–0.16 hours to about 0.26–0.30 hours, with the whole interquartile range shifting upward by a similar amount [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004].
- How often these transitions occur changed only modestly and in mixed directions: A16→A28 in about 28 of 100 traces before versus about 24 after, A17→A28 about 22 versus 27, A18→A28 about 22 versus 25, and A19→A28 about 29 versus 25 [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004].
- Screening is lopsided by family: the time family produced 4 qualifying items where about 0.01 would be expected if nothing had changed (several hundred times the chance expectation), while control flow produced none against about 0.28 expected, so the qualifying differences are confined to timing [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a timing change centred on the approach to A28: in the later window, the elapsed time between the completion of the preceding activity — A16, A17, A18 or A19 — and the completion of A28 roughly doubled, from about 1 hour to about 2 hours. The support is direct and consistent: all four incoming transitions show the same shift of about 1 hour, each with an empirical strength of about 0.9995 (a difference larger than in about 1999 of 2000 random re-splits), and the number of time items far exceeds what an unchanged process would produce, while no control-flow item qualified. Limitations: T_WAIT measures elapsed time between consecutive completion events, so the extra hour could accumulate anywhere inside the interval and the indicator does not distinguish waiting from processing; the four items also share the endpoint A28, so they likely describe one underlying difference rather than four independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is a subtle structural change below the control-flow thresholds (for example something occasionally executed between these activities), though the near-unchanged occurrence counts and the absence of control-flow findings make this less likely.

Other differences: the small shifts in how often each transition occurs (for example A17→A28 up from about 22 to about 27 in 100 traces, A19→A28 down from about 29 to about 25 in 100) move in mixed directions and are not part of the main hypothesis; with no control-flow item passing screening, they are consistent with sampling variation between the two windows [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004].
