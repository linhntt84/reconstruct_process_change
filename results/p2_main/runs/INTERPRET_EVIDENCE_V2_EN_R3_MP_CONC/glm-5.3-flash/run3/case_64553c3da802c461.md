### Observed differences

- In the later window, activity A22 appears in about 99 in 100 traces, up from about 49 in 100 in the earlier window; whenever present it runs after A21 and before A23. [OCC001, REL001, REL007]
- Each of the five activities A27, A28, A33, A34 and A35 drops from about 51 in 100 traces (102 of 200) in the earlier window to about 1 in 100 (2 of 200) in the later window. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- Outgoing transitions from A21 were earlier split roughly half to A22 and half spread across the five activities (about 8–12% each); in the later window about 99 in 100 go to A22. [DIR001]
- The relational pattern mirrors this: A21–A22 and A22–A23 co-occurrence rises from about 49 in 100 traces to 99 in 100 (always in that order); A21 followed by each of the five, and each of the five before A23, falls from about 51 in 100 to about 1 in 100; the five activities occurred together in about half of the earlier traces in either order (no fixed sequence among them) and practically never co-occur in the later window. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL025, REL027]
- Relative positions shift slightly: A21's mean position moves from 0.46 to 0.49 of the trace and A23's from 0.59 to 0.56, converging as the middle block disappears; the many lag-distribution changes in the later window rest on only 1–2 traces each. [POS001, POS002, LAG001, LAG007, LAG018]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the branch through A27, A28, A33, A34 and A35 between A21 and A23 is effectively skipped: earlier, about half of the cases inserted these five activities (executed together, in varying order) between A21 and A23 while the other half went directly via A22; in the later window virtually all cases run A21 → A22 → A23 and the five activities are near-absent. Because the five activities are nearly absent in the later window, this is a path that has become effectively unused rather than a mere re-weighting between equally present alternatives. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, REL001, REL007, REL008, REL013, REL018]

The support is direct and consistent across participation, routing and relational indicators, which all describe the same underlying difference rather than independent confirmations: participation fell from 102 to 2 of 200 traces per activity, A21's routing collapsed onto A22 (based on all 200 traces in both windows), and the pairwise relations with A22 and A23 moved in lockstep. The screening produced 62 qualifying items where about 0.62 would be expected if nothing had changed, so chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [OCC002, DIR001, REL001, REL007, REL013, REL018]

Limitations: the later-window behaviour of the five activities themselves (their order, successors and lags) rests on only 2 traces, so the evidence shows they are nearly unused but not how they would behave if triggered; and the evidence cannot rule out a case-mix change in which later cases simply never required that branch, as an alternative to the branch having been removed or made skippable. [OCC002, DIR002, LAG001]

Other differences: the small positional shifts of A21 and A23 are consistent with the same shortening of traces rather than a separate change, and the lag-distribution differences are based on 1–2 later-window observations and are not relied upon. [POS001, POS002, LAG001, LAG018]
