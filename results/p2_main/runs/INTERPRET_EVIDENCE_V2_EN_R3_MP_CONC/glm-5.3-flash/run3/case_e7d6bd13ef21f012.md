- In the earlier window, every one of the 200 traces followed the same four-step sequence A02 → A04 → A05 → A06: A04 ran exactly once per trace, while A01 and A03 never appeared. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL007, REL011]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change in the middle and at the end of the process: A04, which previously always ran between A02 and A05, is no longer executed; A03 now runs in that position, so the segment A02 → A04 → A05 became A02 → A03 → A05; and A01 was added as a new final step, running after A06 in every trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]

The support is direct and consistent: 24 evidence items qualified where only about 0.12 — far fewer than one — would be expected by chance for an unchanged process, and the occurrence, successor, position and relation indicators all describe the same single restructuring, with each pattern holding in 200 of 200 traces on both sides rather than in a fluctuating minority of cases. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish A04 being replaced by A03 from A04 being removed while A03 was separately inserted, and it cannot rule out that the two windows sampled different pre-existing variants rather than a modified one — unlikely, since neither pattern appears even once in the other window's 200 traces, but not testable from these windows alone; zero participation in a window only means the activity was not observed there. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
