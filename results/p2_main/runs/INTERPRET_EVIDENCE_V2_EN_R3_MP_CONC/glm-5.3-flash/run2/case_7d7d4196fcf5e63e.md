### Observed differences

- The mandatory/optional roles of two activities flipped. In the later window, A02 occurs in every trace (about 49 in 100 before) and repeats more, with about 1.8 occurrences per trace versus 1.0 before; A08 dropped from every trace (about 2 occurrences per trace) to about 45 in 100 traces (about 0.8 per trace). [OCC002, OCC005]
- A01, A06 and A07 all became rarer: A01 appears in about 31 in 100 traces (about 49 before), with mean occurrences down from 1.0 to about 0.4; A06 in about 29 in 100 (about 49 before), mean down from about 2.0 to 0.7; A07 in about 15 in 100 (about 31 before), mean down from about 0.95 to 0.33. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]
- Successors were rewired. Before, A02 always led to A01 and A03 always led to A06; in the later window A02 leads to A04 (about 57% of its outgoing transitions) or A03 (about 43%), and A03 leads to A01 (about 52%) or A06 (about 48%). A08 previously split its outgoing transitions evenly between A03 and A04 and now always leads to A02; A06 previously led to A02 (about 51%) or A07 (about 49%) and now leads to A08 (about 53%) or A07 (about 47%). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- Pairs changed between being independent and being tied. A03 and A08, which before often appeared in the same traces but separately (A08 without A03 in about 52 in 100), now only occur together (45 in 100) or both absent (55 in 100), usually A03 first. A04 and A08, previously always together, now come apart, with A04 without A08 in about 55 in 100 traces. A02 and A04 now always co-occur, A02 first in about 55 in 100 (before, A04 often appeared without A02). [REL010, REL011, REL006]
- Typical positions moved: A02 now sits at the very start of traces (median position about 0 versus about 0.5 before), A08 moved from near the start (median about 0.1) to the middle (about 0.5), and A01 moved earlier (median about 0.57 to about 0.33). [POS002, POS004, POS001]
- Typical distances shifted consistently with the new wiring: A08→A04 was immediate and is now usually two steps apart; A02→A04 moved from about three steps to immediate (about 62 in 100 pair occurrences); A03→A08 shortened from about four steps to two. [LAG035, LAG011, LAG020]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the repetitive core was rewired and its anchor moved from A08 to A02. In the earlier window, traces were anchored on A08 — present in every trace, about twice each — with the cycle A08 → A03 → A06 → A02 → A01 → back to A08 and the exit to A04 taken from A08. In the later window, A02 anchors the process: it opens the trace and runs in every case, the cycle appears to run A02 → A03 → A06 → A08 → back to A02, the exit to A04 now leaves directly from A02, and A01 has left the main cycle to become a side detour reached from A03 (about half of A03's outgoing transitions) that rejoins via A08; A08 itself became an intermediate step present in only about 45 in 100 traces. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, OCC002, OCC005, POS002, LAG011]

The rewiring is shown directly by the successor changes at A02, A03, A06 and A08, and the occurrence, co-occurrence, position and distance shifts all line up with one and the same structure (for example, A02→A04 shortening to one step matches the new direct exit, and A03→A08 shortening to two steps matches A01 leaving the cycle). With 64 qualifying items against roughly 0.36 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [DIR001, DIR004, LAG011, LAG020, LAG034]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different mixes of pre-existing process variants rather than a single modified structure, and several items describe the same underlying traces (A02's new role shows in occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators alike), so agreeing items are not independent confirmations; positions and distances are event positions, not clock times, so no timing claim is made. [OCC002, DIR001, REL006]

Other differences: A07 became markedly rarer (about 31 → 15 in 100 traces) while its typical position within traces stayed similar; this appears to follow from A06 being executed less often, since the share of A06's outgoing transitions going to A07 barely moved (about 49% → 47%). [OCC004, POS003, DIR003]
