### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the order between the group A35/A36/A41/A42/A43 and the pair A27/A26 reversed and the surrounding connections were rewired: in the earlier window A25 fed the group, the group ran before A27 and A26, and A26 passed to A30; in the later window A25 leads almost directly to A27, A27 and A26 precede the group, A26 leads into the group, and the group passes on to A30. The activities involved are A25, A26, A27, A35, A36, A41, A42, A43 and A30.
