### Observed differences

- Routing around the five-activity block A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 changed at both ends. Earlier, A20 spread its outgoing transitions almost evenly over the five (about 19–23% each) and A21 led to A25 in essentially all cases; in the later window A20 leads to A22 in about 995 of 1,000 outgoing transitions, while A21 now spreads its outgoing transitions over the same five activities (about 16–23% each) and rarely reaches A25 directly. [DIR001, DIR002]
- The block's exit changed: earlier, each of A30–A38 sent roughly 18–22% of its outgoing transitions to A22 (besides transitions within the block); in the later window they send about 18–24% to A25 instead, and A22 has all but disappeared as a target. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]
- The order between the block and A21/A22 reversed: earlier, each block member came before A21 and before A22 in 100% of the traces containing both; in the later window A21 and A22 each come first in about 199 of 200 such traces, while the activities still co-occur in every trace. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]
- Mean relative positions within the trace moved consistently: A21 from about 0.65 to 0.52, A22 from about 0.52 to 0.39, and the five block members from about 0.44 to 0.60. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007]
- The old adjacencies nearly vanished: A20 followed by a block member within five steps, and a block member followed by A22 within five steps, occurred in all 200 earlier traces but in only 1 later trace each; these items rest on a single after-window observation. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]
- Elapsed time between consecutive completions fell for three activity pairs: A09→A10 from about 1.0 to about 0.5 hours and A11→A10 likewise (about 125 vs 110 observations), and A24→A28 from about 1.0 hour to essentially zero (median and upper quartile 0 in the later window). [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of five activities A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 was repositioned along the flow: earlier it sat between A20 and A22 — A20 fed into it, it exited to A22, and A21 ran later on to A25 — whereas in the later window it sits between A21 and A25, with A21 feeding into it, it exiting to A25, and A20 leading directly to A22. Every activity involved is observed in essentially all traces of both windows (co-occurrence 100%, support 200 of 200), so this is a change of ordering and connections, not of participation. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007]

The volume of qualifying control-flow evidence (34 items against roughly 0.55 expected for an unchanged process) makes chance sampling implausible as a whole, and the relational items show the reversal directly and consistently, with the positional shifts agreeing; the agreeing items likely reflect the same underlying rerouting rather than independent confirmations. [REL001, REL006, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Limitations: the lag items agree with this reading but each rests on one after-window observation and was not relied upon; and because the codes are anonymous and the profiles of A20 and A21 (and of the exit targets A22 and A25) mirror each other almost exactly, a relabelling of these codes between the windows would produce the same signature, which the evidence cannot rule out. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, DIR001, DIR002]

Other differences: a timing change stands apart from the structural hypothesis — the elapsed time between consecutive completions halved for A09→A10 and A11→A10 (about 1.0 to 0.5 hours) and collapsed to near zero for A24→A28; these activities do not appear in the control-flow items, and with 3 time items against roughly 0.02 expected by chance, the difference far exceeds random re-splitting. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003]
