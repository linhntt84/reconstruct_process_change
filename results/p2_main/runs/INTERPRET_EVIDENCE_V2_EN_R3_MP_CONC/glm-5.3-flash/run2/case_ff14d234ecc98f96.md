### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every one of the 200 cases contained A26, A27 and A28 exactly once each (participation 100 in 100 cases, about one occurrence per trace), as a group running after A44 and before A29. In the later window this trio is nearly gone: A26 and A28 appear in about 1 in 100 cases, A27 in about 1 in 200. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL031, REL032, REL035]
- The reverse holds for A19, A20 and A21: none was observed before (A21 in 1 of 200 cases), while in the later window A19 runs in about 31 in 100 cases, A20 in about 34 and A21 in about 37, each at most once per case. The three almost never run together (pairwise co-occurrence at most about 1 in 100) and together cover about 99 in 100 later cases, so they behave as per-case alternatives. - The new activities occupy the old group's slot. A44's outgoing transitions now lead to A21 (about 36 in 100), A20 (about 30) and A19 (about 27), where earlier they led to A26, A28 and A27 (about 28–33 each); A19, A20 and A21 in turn lead to A29 in about 97 of 100 observed transitions. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR007]
- Relationally, A19, A20 and A21 now co-occur with A29 in about 31–37 of 100 cases and always precede it, and are preceded by A44 in about 30–37 of 100 cases. The old trio previously always co-occurred with A44 before it and A29 after it; in the later window it co-occurs with neither. [REL018, REL019, REL024, REL029, REL033, REL034, REL036, REL037]
- The distance from A44 to A29 shortened: about 4 event positions in roughly 93 of 100 earlier cases versus about 2 positions in roughly 92 of 100 later cases. A29's mean position moved slightly earlier (about 0.43 to 0.40 of the trace) and A44's slightly later (about 0.31 to 0.33). [LAG010, POS007, POS008]

- The remaining lag items concern pairs inside or around the old group; their after-window support is only 1–3 observations, so they mainly confirm that the old group's internal distances are no longer observed. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: the earlier behaviour, in which A26, A27 and A28 each ran once in every case between A44 and A29, appears in the later window replaced by a single activity per case chosen among A19, A20 and A21 (roughly one third each), executed once after A44 and before A29. The signs are direct and consistent across indicator families: near-complete participation flips (0 to about 31–37 in 100 for the new activities; 100 to about 0–1 in 100 for the old trio), successor changes at A44 and at A19/A20/A21, relational profiles binding the new activities to A44-before and A29-after while the old trio lost both partners, and the A44-to-A29 distance shortening from about four to about two positions, as expected when three activities give way to one. With 70 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, REL018, REL019, REL024, REL029, REL031, REL033, LAG010]

Limitations: the items are not independent — the occurrence, direction and relation evidence for a given activity largely describes the same traces — and the evidence cannot rule out that the A19/A20/A21 path existed earlier as a variant rare enough to be missed by 200 traces, or that A26–28 were simply no longer triggered rather than removed from the process.

Other differences: A11 and A12 now occur in about 56 of 100 cases (up from about 47) and no longer precede the removed A26–28 group, instead preceding the new activities in about 16–20 of 100 cases; the evidence shows this shift but cannot establish whether the rise in their share belongs to the same restructuring. [REL001, REL003, REL004, REL007, REL010]
