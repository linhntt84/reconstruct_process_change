### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a step, A10, was inserted into the flow between A05/A11 and A07. In the earlier window the observed backbone ran A05 → A11 → A07 (with A05 → A01 as the other main branch); in the later window about 47 in 100 traces additionally execute A10, reached from A05 or A11 and leading on to A07 (or to A11), which matches the stretched A05→A07 and A11→A07 distances. A11's participation stayed roughly stable (about 51 in 100 traces before, 47 in 100 after) and A07 still occurs in every trace, so A10 was added alongside the existing steps rather than replacing one of them. [OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS002]

The set of differences is too large to attribute to sampling: 15 control-flow items qualified against fewer than 1 expected by chance if nothing had changed, and the participation, routing, relation, position and lag indicators all describe the same insertion consistently. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: the indicators are computed from the same traces, so their agreement is not independent confirmation, and the exact slot varies — A10 sometimes follows A05 before A11 and sometimes follows A11, so no single fixed position can be claimed. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that A10 is a pre-existing path that was simply never sampled in the earlier window, although 0 of 200 versus 94 of 200 traces makes pure sampling variation implausible.

Other differences: A06 appears in about 11 in 100 later traces (not observed before) and receives about 2 in 100 of A05's outgoing transitions; A09 appears in about 6 in 100 later traces (not observed before) with no relational evidence about where it sits. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001]
