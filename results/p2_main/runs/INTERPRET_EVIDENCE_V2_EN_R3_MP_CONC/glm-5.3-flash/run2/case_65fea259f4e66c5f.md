### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening produced 45 qualifying control-flow items where fewer than 1 (0.65) would be expected without a change, so the differences as a whole are far beyond what an unchanged process typically yields. The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window an alternative segment between A26 and A28 is executed in about 44 in 100 cases: A27 is not executed in those cases, and A32, A33, A38, A39 and A40 run instead, after A26 and before A28, with no fixed order among themselves; in the remaining about 56 in 100 cases the earlier sequence A26 → A27 → A28 is retained. This rests on direct, mutually consistent signs: A26's outgoing transitions split from almost exclusively A27 to a majority-A27 / minority-new-activities split, the new activities are placed after A26 and before A28 in the relation data, they occur as one set, and they are nearly mutually exclusive with A27.
