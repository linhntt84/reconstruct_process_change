### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was relocated to the start of the process and that A05 is now executed after the A11/A12 block instead of after A01. Before, A11 and A12 led to each other and exited to A01, which was immediately followed by A05 near the end of the trace; in the later window, A01 opens the trace and leads to A06, while A05 directly follows A11 or A12. This is shown directly by the successor change at A01, the reversed relative order of A01 against A11 and A12, the new direct exits from A11 and A12 to A05, and the A01-to-A05 distance growing from one to four steps, with the positional shifts of A01, A11 and A12 pointing the same way. The 25 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.22 expected by chance for the 44 tested units, and every activity involved is well observed in both windows (A01, A11 and A12 in about 22–27 of 100 traces, A05 in about 46–54), so the pattern is not a rare variant. [DIR001, REL002, REL003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG004, LAG005, POS001]

The items are not independent confirmations, since several describe overlapping aspects of the same traces (for example, the REL items involving A02 all reflect its disappearance), and the evidence cannot rule out the competing explanation that the windows sample different mixes of process variants — such as a previously rare variant beginning with A01 becoming dominant — rather than a relocation of A01 and A05.
