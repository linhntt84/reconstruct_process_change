### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative step formed by A19, A20 and A25 — one of which runs per case — was relocated from the late stage of the process to an early one. Before, the step ran after A26 and A27 and led on to A28 or A32; in the later window A45 feeds it and it leads directly into A26, so the local flow reads A45 → {A19|A20|A25} → A26 instead of A45 → A26 … A27/A28 → {A19|A20|A25} → A28/A32 [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG007].

The relational items show the order flip directly; the successor changes on both sides (the block now followed by A26, A45 now followed by the block) and the roughly 0.28-earlier positions agree with it; and the collapse of the old detour into direct, adjacent A27 → A28 → A32 succession corroborates that the step left its old place. With 26 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.6 expected without change, the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces [DIR004, DIR005, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, screening].

The evidence cannot distinguish a literal relocation of the step from removing it at the old position and reinserting it at the new one, which would look identical here; three lag items consistent with the move rest on only 1–3 after-window observations, and the exact before-window wiring around A28 (which alternated between A32 and the block) is only partially resolved [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003].

Other differences: A15 remains in every trace but repeats less often (mean about 1.9 → 1.6 occurrences per trace); the evidence does not tie this to the relocation [OCC001].
