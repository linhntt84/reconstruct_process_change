### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the block A36, A37, A42, A43, A44 was repositioned and made optional: it previously always ran between A25 and A28, whereas it now runs in about 55 in 100 traces after A28 and A26 and before A31; A25 proceeds directly to A28, and A27 — previously always executed after A26 — now alternates with the block, so a trace typically takes either A27 (about 45 in 100) or the block (about 55 in 100), with A31 following either branch. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, DIR010, REL026, REL033, REL038, REL043]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: participation drops, successor changes at A25, A26 and the block members, order reversals relative to A26 and A28, position shifts, and the adjacency changes all describe the same rewiring; and with 106 qualifying items against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed, random re-splits of the pooled traces cannot account for the evidence as a whole. [OCC003, DIR003, POS008, REL026, LAG010] Limitations: the many relation items describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations, and the exact post-change split (for example whether the block is skipped in a stable share of cases) is estimated from finite samples. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is the precise decision structure — whether A27 and the block are direct alternatives at a single gateway after A26 or become exclusive through earlier routing choices.

Other differences: A10 declined and decoupled — participation fell from about 32 in 100 traces to about 13 in 100, its co-occurrence with A27 and the block fell from about 32 in 100 to under 8 in 100, and its outgoing transitions shifted away from A16 (about 79% to 38%); A15 now leads to A16 in about 94% of its outgoing transitions (about 84% before), and A15 and A16 occur without the block in about 45 in 100 traces, consistent with the block being skipped.
