### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change in how A03 and A10 relate: in the earlier window both always executed within the same case, in either order and with direct hand-offs between them, while in the later window exactly one of the two executes per case (about 50 in 100 traces each), never both, with no direct transition between them and A07 adjacent to whichever one runs. This is shown directly by the pair's relation profile (co-occurrence from 100 to 0 in 100 traces, one-only shares of exactly 50/50), by the disappearance of the A03→A10 and A10→A03 edges from the direction profiles, and by the participation drop of both activities from 100 to 50 in 100 traces. The screening context makes chance an unlikely explanation overall: 49 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.32 would be expected without a change. A limitation: zero co-occurrence in the later window means A03 and A10 were never observed together in 200 traces, not that joint execution has become impossible, and the evidence cannot distinguish a single new either/or decision point from two case variants that each contain only one of the activities.
