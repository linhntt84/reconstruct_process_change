### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the second half of the process was restructured. In the earlier window a typical case ran the block A35/A36/A41 first, then A26/A27 with A30 directly after A26, and ended A49 → A02 → A50, with A02 present in about 75 in 100 cases. In the later window, A02 takes part in only about 21 in 100 cases and no longer channels work to A50; A49 passes straight to A50; the middle block is reversed so that A26/A27 run before A35/A36/A41; A25 links directly to A27; and A30 is reached after the A35/A36/A41 block instead of directly after A26. A02 and the middle-block activities also changed from usually being executed together (about 75 in 100) to being near-alternatives (together in about 21 in 100). [OCC001, DIR001, DIR011, LAG009, REL010, REL013, LAG002, LAG005, DIR006, REL001]

The ordering changes are shown directly by relational evidence: the six A26/A27-versus-A35/A36/A41 pairs co-occur in every trace in both windows and flip from about 97–99 in 100 one way to about 97–98 in 100 the other, a magnitude that random re-splits of a stable process do not produce; successor and adjacency items point the same way, and 45 items qualified overall against roughly 1 expected without a change for this family, so the volume cannot be explained by chance sampling. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR011, LAG002, LAG005, LAG006, LAG008, LAG009]

Other differences: A06 and A10 are executed more often (about 7→19 and 9→22 in 100 traces, on 13→38 and 17→43 traces); A47's outgoing transitions to A48 dropped from about 97 to about 81 in 100, with small new shares to A10, A06, A02 and A04; A49's position moved slightly later (mean relative position about 0.94 to 0.96). [OCC002, OCC003, DIR010, POS007]

Limitations: the windows are aggregates, so a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants between the windows cannot be ruled out as an alternative to a single modified process; and A02's post-change routing profile rests on only about 46 observed outgoing transitions (about 42 traces), so its new behaviour is estimated on a small base. [DIR001, OCC001]
