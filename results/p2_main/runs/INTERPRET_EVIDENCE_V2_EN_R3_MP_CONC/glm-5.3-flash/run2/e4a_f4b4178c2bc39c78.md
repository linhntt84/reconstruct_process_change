### Observed differences

- The activities A25, A26 and A31 were each executed exactly once in every trace in the earlier window (participation 100 in 100 traces, no repeats). In the later window they still appear in every trace, but occur about 3.5 times per trace on average and repeat within a trace in about 68 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The routing out of A31 changed: in the earlier window all of its observed outgoing transitions led to A20, while in the later window about 71 in 100 lead to A25 and about 29 in 100 still lead to A20 (based on 697 observed transitions). [DIR001]
- The relation between A25 and A31 changed from a fixed sequence — co-occurring in every trace with A25 before A31 in 100 in 100 traces — to a strict A25-before-A31 order in only about 32 in 100 traces, with about 68 in 100 traces showing the two interleaved or repeated. [REL001]
- The tight spacing of this pair largely disappeared: before, A25 and A31 were almost always about 2 event positions apart; afterwards that gap accounts for only about 12 in 100 pairs, gaps of about 5 positions now appear, and the positional spread of both activities roughly doubled (A25's standard deviation from about 0.06 to about 0.12). [LAG001, POS001, POS002]
- The elapsed time between consecutive completions of A34 and A14 roughly doubled, from a median of about 1.0 hour to about 2.0 hours, with the whole distribution shifted (quartiles from roughly 0.9–1.1 hours to roughly 1.8–2.3 hours), based on about 93–95 such intervals per window. [TWAIT001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the group comprising A25, A26 and A31 is executed repeatedly within a case instead of once: previously each ran exactly once per trace in a fixed sequence (A25 before A31, with A31 continuing to A20), whereas now they occur about 3.5 times per trace, A31 flows back to A25 in about 71 in 100 of its executions, and the A25–A31 pair is interleaved or repeated in about 68 in 100 traces. The occurrence, transition and relation indicators show this directly and agree with one another, and the screening produced 8 control-flow items where only about 0.3 would be expected if nothing had changed; note that the three occurrence items carry identical values and likely describe one underlying repetition rather than three independent confirmations. Limitations: the relational and transition evidence covers only A25 and A31, so A26's place in the repeated group is inferred from its identical occurrence pattern, and the indicators cannot show what triggers or terminates the repetitions. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the interleaving reflects concurrent execution of the repeated activities rather than a strictly sequential rework cycle — the relation indicator merges these cases — although the direct A31-to-A25 transition favours a sequential cycle.

Other differences: a timing change in its own right — the elapsed time between consecutive completions of A34 and A14 doubled from about 1.0 to about 2.0 hours (median), consistently across the quartiles; no structural counterpart for this timing shift appears in the evidence. [TWAIT001]
