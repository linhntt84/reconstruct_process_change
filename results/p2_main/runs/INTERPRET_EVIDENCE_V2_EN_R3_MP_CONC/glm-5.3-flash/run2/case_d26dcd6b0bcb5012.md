### Observed differences

- The A19/A20 segment became near-universal. In the earlier window, A19 and A20 each ran in about 53 in 100 traces (about 0.53 occurrences per trace, essentially never repeated); in the later window, in about 99 in 100 traces (about 1.0 per trace). [OCC001, OCC002]
- The branch at A43 flipped. Earlier, about 49% of A43's observed outgoing moves led to A19 and about 45% went directly to A28; in the later window, about 90% lead to A19 and only about 1% directly to A28. [DIR002]
- A43 and A28 moved apart. Earlier, the pair was adjacent (one step apart) in about 45 in 100 cases and 4–5 steps apart in about 52 in 100; in the later window it is adjacent in about 1 in 100 and 4–5 steps apart in about 99 in 100. [LAG001]
- Ordering stayed the same while co-occurrence became near-total. A19 before A20 holds in both windows (A-before-B about 0.52 earlier, 0.985 later; co-occurrence 0.53 → 0.99), as do A43 before A19/A20 and A19/A20 before A28; traces containing neither A19 nor A20 fell from about 47 in 100 to about 1 in 100. - The inner choice block kept its shape but doubled in absolute frequency. A25 followed by A26, or alternatively A27, remain mutually exclusive alternatives sitting between A19 and A20, in a roughly even split in both windows; participation rose for A25 from about 24% to 51%, A26 from 24% to 52%, A27 from 30% to 49%, and the block still appears in essentially every run of the segment in both windows. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment running between A43 and A28 — A43, then A19, then either A25 followed by A26 or alternatively A27, then A20 — changed from being executed in about half of the cases to being executed in nearly every case: earlier, about 47 in 100 traces went directly from A43 to A28 without it, whereas in the later window only about 1 in 100 do, so A19 and A20 effectively became part of nearly every trace. This is a change in how often an existing segment executes, not a repositioning: the same order A43 → A19 → block → A20 → A28 is observable in both windows, the participation of A19/A20 rose from about 53% to 99% with no change in repetition, the rerouting is visible directly at A43, and the growing A43–A28 distance matches activities now sitting between them; the inner block's doubling mirrors the segment while its internal split stayed stable. The 28 qualifying items (all control-flow) far exceed the roughly 0.7 expected without change, so chance cannot account for the set as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, LAG001, REL001, REL012, REL013, REL016]

The evidence cannot rule out that an upstream change in which cases reach A43, rather than a change at the branch itself, now routes every case through the segment — the windows show where the split changed but not why; the residual direct A43 → A28 share (about 1 in 100) also rests on very few later-window cases, and the many participation and relation items describe overlapping aspects of the same traces rather than independent confirmations. [DIR002, LAG001, REL001]
