### Observed differences

- A02 was present in about 92 of 200 traces (46 in 100) in the earlier window — once per trace, early in the trace (median at about 10% of the event positions) — and was not observed at all in the later window (0 of 200). A01 shows the mirror image: not observed earlier (0 of 200), but present in about 101 of 200 traces (roughly 50 in 100) later, once per trace and also early (median at about 14%). Earlier, A02's outgoing transitions went mostly to A03 (about 32 in 100), A06 (about 22), A11 (about 14) and A13 (about 11); later, A01's go to A06 (about 24), A03 (about 19), A10 (about 15), A11 (about 15) and A13 (about 11). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was introduced into the process while A02 disappeared from it — in effect, a substitution in which A01 took the position A02 previously held. Both activities occur at most once per trace in roughly half of the traces, at a similar early position; both lead to essentially the same successor activities (A03, A04, A05, A06, A09, A10, A11, A13); A03's direct edge to A02 was replaced by an edge to A01; and their co-occurrence patterns with A03 and A06 mirror each other across the windows, with A01 and A02 never appearing together. Twelve evidence items qualified in screening, against roughly 0.21 expected for the 43 tested units if nothing had changed, so a pattern of this size is unlikely for an unchanged process. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]

The evidence cannot show that A01 performs exactly A02's former role: the routing shares differ in detail (A01 leads to A10 in about 15 in 100 of its outgoing transitions versus about 7 for A02, and to A03 in about 19 versus about 32). Nor can it exclude that A02 still occurs extremely rarely, since absence here means only that it was not observed in 200 traces, or that the addition and removal are independent rather than a substitution. [DIR001, DIR002, OCC002]

Other differences: A03's remaining outgoing shares shifted slightly (A10 up about 5 points in 100, A11 down about 5), a small redistribution that may belong to the same change or be sampling noise. [DIR003]
