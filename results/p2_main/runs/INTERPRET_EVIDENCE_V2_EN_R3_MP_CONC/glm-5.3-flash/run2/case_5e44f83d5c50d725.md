### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A01 ran in every one of the 200 traces (about one occurrence per trace, typically around two-thirds of the way through the case); in the later window it appears in none of the 200 traces. [OCC001, POS001]
- Earlier, A01 sat between A05 and A07: about 51 in 100 of A05's outgoing transitions went to A01, about 99 in 100 of A01's outgoing transitions went to A07, and A01 co-occurred with both in every trace (A05 before A01 in about 55 in 100 traces, interleaved or repeated otherwise; A01 before A07 always). - In the later window, A05 leads directly to A07: A05's transitions to A07 rose from none to about 49 in 100, the transition to A01 disappeared, and the A05–A07 gap is now typically one step (about 40 in 100 observed pairs) where it was typically two steps (about 50 in 100). [DIR003, LAG001]

- Smaller shifts: A05's median position moved slightly later (0.4 to 0.5 of the case) and A07's slightly earlier (0.8 to 0.75); from A04, transitions to A07 rose from about 1.5% to about 7% of observed outgoing transitions, while the transition to A01 is no longer observed. [POS002, POS003, DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 is no longer executed and that the sequence which previously ran A05 → A01 → A07 now goes directly from A05 to A07, while the alternative branch A05 → A06 continues essentially unchanged (about 46 in 100 of A05's transitions before, about 45 in 100 after). [OCC001, DIR003, LAG001, REL001, REL002]

The change is shown directly and consistently across participation (200 of 200 traces to 0 of 200), the routing out of A05 (A01 successor share of about 51% replaced by A07 at about 49%, backed by roughly 378 and 377 observed transitions), and the A05–A07 distance (typically two steps to typically one); these indicators reflect the same underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations. The volume of qualifying items (10 against roughly 0.08 expected without change) also far exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC001, DIR003, LAG001, REL001, REL002]

Limitation: the evidence cannot distinguish a removal of A01 from A01 becoming conditional and not executing in any of the 200 later traces, and a very rare residual execution of A01 cannot be excluded by a finite sample; the empty outgoing profile of A01 in the later window is not by itself proof of absence. [OCC001, DIR001]

Other differences: the slight position shifts of A05 and A07 and the small rise in A04 → A07 transitions (with A04 → A01 no longer observed) are consistent with consequences of the same change in A01's execution rather than independent changes, but the evidence cannot confirm this. [POS002, POS003, DIR002]
