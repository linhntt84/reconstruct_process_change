### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the either/or choice between two mid-process activity groups was replaced by a fixed sequence of both. In the earlier window, cases ran either the group A33–A35–A36 (no fixed internal order) or the group A37–A38–A39, each in about half of the cases, with A31 following whichever group ran; in the later window, nearly every case runs A33/A35/A36 first and then A37/A38/A39, with A31 after the combined block and A30 feeding mainly into A35/A33/A36. As part of the same restructuring, A04 lost its frequent early place between A14 and A15: it now appears in only about a quarter of traces, later in the case, with A14 usually passing straight to A15. The relational evidence shows this directly (group co-occurrence rising from about 1 in 100 to about 98 in 100 with a consistent order), and participation, successor and position indicators all move consistently; the number of qualifying items (109 control-flow items against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process) cannot be explained by chance sampling as a whole. Limitations: positions and lags are event-position based, so no clock-time claim is made, and the evidence cannot rule out that the later window reflects a different mix of case types rather than a modified process. [REL039, REL050, REL031, LAG008, DIR005, OCC002, DIR004]

Other differences: A02, A08 and A10 became more frequent and redirected their outgoing edges, but each still appears in fewer than a quarter of traces, so these proportions rest on modest counts; A47 also reaches A48 in two steps somewhat more often (about 16 in 100 cases, versus about 3 in 100 before).
