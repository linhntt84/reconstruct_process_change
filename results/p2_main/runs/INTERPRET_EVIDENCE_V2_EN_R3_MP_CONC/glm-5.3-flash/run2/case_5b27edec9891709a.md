### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, all eight flagged activities (A20, A21, A22, A23, A26, A31, A32, A37) ran in every one of the 200 traces, in one fixed order: A20, then the group A31/A32/A37 (whose internal order was mixed), then A23, then A21, then A22, then A26. [OCC001, OCC002, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL014, REL020]
- In the later window, A20 leads almost directly to A23: about 995 in 1,000 of A20's outgoing transitions go to A23 (before, they split roughly equally over A31, A32 and A37), and the gap between A20 and A23 shrank from 4 event positions to 1. [DIR001, LAG001]
- The group A31/A32/A37 now executes in only about 47 in 100 traces (94 of 200, down from 200 of 200), and A22 in about 54 in 100 (107 of 200, down from 200 of 200); where they always ran together before, they now co-occur in only about 1 in 200 traces — they changed from always combined to almost mutually exclusive alternatives. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013]
- The group also changed place: before, A31/A32/A37 always ran before A21 and before A23, and each member sent about a third of its outgoing transitions to A23; after, A21 precedes the group in about 99 in 100 jointly occurring cases, A23 precedes it likewise, and its members send roughly three in ten transitions to A26 and virtually none to A23. A21 now routes just under half of its transitions into the group, where before it always went to A22. [REL006, REL014, REL017, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- Relative event positions moved consistently with this: A23 earlier (mean about 0.45 to 0.39), A21 earlier (about 0.60 to 0.54), the group later (about 0.40 to 0.58); the A21-to-A26 gap, always 2 positions before, is now 2 positions in about 54 in 100 traces and 4 positions in about 46 in 100 — one for each branch. [POS002, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG005]
- Four lag comparisons between A20 or A21 and the group rest on a single after-window observation each (those gaps grew beyond the 5-step window) and carry little weight. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural restructuring around an exclusive choice: in the later window, A20 connects directly to A23, so the group A31/A32/A37 no longer runs between A20 and A23; instead, after A21 the flow takes either A22 (about 54 in 100 traces) or the group A31/A32/A37 (about 47 in 100), which then continues to A26, and the two branches are almost never combined. Before, both the group (placed between A20 and A23, exiting to A23) and A22 (between A21 and A26) executed in every trace; the group's internal mixed order is unchanged. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL006, REL011, REL014, REL017, LAG001, LAG005]

Supporting signs: the successor changes are observed directly at A20, A21 and the group members; the order between the group and A21/A23 flips; A22 and the group fall from perfect co-occurrence to about 1 in 200 traces; participation drops from 200 of 200 to 107 and 94 of 200; and the lag structure matches the two branches. Screening flagged 47 items against roughly 0.5 expected if nothing had changed, so chance cannot explain the pattern as a whole, and each item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013, LAG001, LAG005, screening]

Limitations: the many items largely re-describe one underlying restructuring rather than serving as independent confirmations, and a few lag items rest on single observations. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the exclusive A22-or-group behaviour already existed earlier at a frequency low enough that none of the 200 earlier traces showed it; the exactly full participation in the earlier window makes a pure sampling explanation unlikely but not impossible. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Other differences: only small residual position drifts remain — A20 slightly later (mean relative position about 0.34 to 0.36) and A22 and A26 slightly earlier (about 0.63 to 0.59 and 0.66 to 0.63) — consistent with knock-on effects of the branch change rather than separate shifts. [POS001, POS003, POS005]
