### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A15, A16, A25, A26 and A27 were mandatory and singular: each of the five appeared exactly once in all 200 of 200 cases. In the later window, none of these five was observed (0 of 200 cases). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010]

- In the later window, five activities that were absent earlier now appear, one per case: A17 in about 24 in 100 cases (47/200), A19 in about 22 (44/200), A18 in about 21 (42/200), A14 in about 18 (36/200) and A20 in about 16 (31/200). These shares sum to all cases and no two of the five ever occur in the same case, so each later case contains exactly one of them. [OCC001, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL003, REL029, REL037, REL044]

- Earlier, the five old activities formed one unordered block between A43 and A28: every pair always co-occurred with either order about equally likely (for example A15 before A16 in about 49 in 100 cases, A16 first in about 51), each block activity was always preceded by A43 and followed by A28, and each one's outgoing transitions were spread almost evenly over the other four block activities and A28 (shares roughly 0.14–0.26). [REL010, REL015, REL053, REL057, REL018, REL027, REL019, REL028, DIR002, DIR003, DIR008]

- In the later window, each new activity sits directly between A43 and A28: all observed outgoing transitions of each of the five go to A28 (share 1.0), and each co-occurs with A43 in every case in which it occurs, always after A43. A43's successors changed as a complete set from the old five activities to the new five. [DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR011, REL035, REL036, REL042, REL043]

- Event positions (not clock time) are consistent with a shorter segment: the new activities occupy mean relative positions of about 0.35–0.37, close to the old block's 0.37, while A28's mean position moved earlier (about 0.46 to 0.39) and A43's later (about 0.28 to 0.33). [POS001, POS002, POS004, POS011, POS012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening tested 146 control-flow units and about 0.73 qualifying items would be expected by chance if nothing had changed; 94 items were reported, many times that expectation, so the volume of evidence alone cannot be a sampling artefact [OCC001, OCC002, DIR011, REL010].

Main hypothesis: the segment between A43 and A28 was restructured from five always-executed activities in free order to a single choice among five alternatives. In the earlier window, every case executed A15, A16, A25, A26 and A27, each exactly once and in no fixed order, between A43 and A28; in the later window, those five are no longer observed, and every case executes exactly one of A14, A17, A18, A19 and A20 (about 24, 22, 21, 18 and 16 in 100 cases respectively), always directly between A43 and A28 [OCC002, OCC008, OCC001, OCC004, DIR011, REL010, REL029, REL035].

The support is direct and consistent: participation flipped from 200/200 to 0/200 for the old five and from 0/200 to 31–47/200 for the new five; the new five are mutually exclusive with shares summing to every case; A43's successors moved as a complete set from the old five to the new five; and each new activity's only observed successor is A28. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the five new labels are renamed counterparts of the old five; even under that reading, the executed structure changed from "all five, any order" to "exactly one of five". The spread among the alternatives' shares (about 16–24 in 100) is not established as a real difference and may reflect sampling with 200 cases per window [DIR011, DIR001, REL029, REL035, OCC001, OCC004, OCC007].

Other differences: none beyond this segment. The mean event-position shifts of A28 (about 0.46 to 0.39) and A43 (about 0.28 to 0.33) follow from the same shrinkage of the segment rather than being independent changes [POS011, POS012].
