### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new mandatory step, A01, was inserted between A02/A09 and A03/A12: earlier, A02 and A09 handed over directly to A03 or A12 in roughly even splits, while in the later window both always pass through A01, which then routes to A12 (about 53%) or A03 (about 47%); A01 runs about twice per trace and repeats within about half of them. The change is shown directly by participation rising from 0 to 200 of 200 traces, the successor rewiring at A02, A09 and A01, co-occurrence with A02/A03/A12 rising from 0 to 100%, and the one-step increase in pairwise distances; 39 items qualified against roughly 0.31 expected for an unchanged process, so the set as a whole exceeds what chance produces, though most items describe this single insertion rather than independent confirmations. A limitation is that zero participation earlier only means A01 was not observed, so a pre-existing but never-exercised step cannot be ruled out, and the evidence cannot show why A01 recurs about twice per trace.
