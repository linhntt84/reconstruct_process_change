### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a timing change centred on A28: in the later window, the elapsed time between the completion of each of A16, A17, A18 and A19 and the subsequent completion of A28 is about one hour longer, roughly doubling from about 1 hour to about 2 hours, and the intervals are more spread out. Supporting signs: the timing indicator measures this directly for all four activity pairs, the differences agree in direction and magnitude, each rests on 43–58 traces per window, and the time family's yield (4 items) is far above the chance expectation (about 0.011) while control flow produced nothing. Limitations: all four items share A28, so they most likely describe one underlying difference rather than four independent ones, and elapsed time between completions cannot by itself show whether A28 completes later, the earlier activities complete sooner, or an inserted or reordered step lengthens the interval; the absence of control-flow findings makes a structural explanation less likely but does not rule it out.
