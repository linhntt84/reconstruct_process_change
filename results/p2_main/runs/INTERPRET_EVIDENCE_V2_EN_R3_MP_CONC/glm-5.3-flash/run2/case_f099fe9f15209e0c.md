### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A02 was removed from the process, or is no longer executed: it appeared exactly once in all 200 earlier traces and in none of the 200 later traces. This is shown directly by its participation and position support dropping from 100% to zero, by the loss of all its outgoing transitions, and by its former partners A01, A03, A04 and A05 now occurring without it. A03's routing matches: its transition to A02 (about 38 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) disappeared and the flow shifted to its remaining successors. Eleven control-flow items qualified where about 0.15 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the ensemble is far beyond what an unchanged process produces, and every item describes one coherent structural difference, each larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A02 was relabelled to another activity code rather than truly removed, since activity identity is assumed from the codes themselves; a drop from universal occurrence to unobserved across 200 traces remains the strongest reading. [OCC001, DIR001]

Other differences: A03 and A04 occur at earlier relative positions and the A03→A04 step gap shortened; these are consistent with knock-on effects of removing A02 from the early part of each trace, but position and lag shifts alone do not establish that an activity was moved. [POS002, POS003, LAG001]
