### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the block of five mutually exclusive activities A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 sitting between A43 and A28 is bypassed. Earlier, traces passed from A43 through exactly one of the five and then to A28; later, A43 leads directly to A28 in about 92 of 100 of its outgoing transitions, the five activities appear in at most 3 of 200 traces (A26 and A27 not at all), and the A43→A28 distance shrank from two steps to one. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: collapse of the block's participation, redirection of A43's outgoing edges to A28, co-occurrence of A43 and A28 without the block, and the shortened step distances. The volume of flagged items (57 against roughly 0.72 expected without a change) is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, LAG011, LAG008, REL017, screening]

The evidence cannot fully distinguish a complete removal of the block from it having become extremely rare; the few residual A20 occurrences also show different successors, but that rests on only 3 observations. [DIR003, OCC003]

Other differences: A02's participation halved (27 to 14 of 100 traces), its position moved later, and its outgoing transitions redistributed away from A16; this decline is not explained by the bypass hypothesis and may be a separate change. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002]
