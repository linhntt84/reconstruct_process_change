### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A14, A15 and A20 were minority, mutually exclusive activities: A14 ran in about 32 in 100 traces, A15 in about 28 and A20 in about 40, the three never co-occurred, and their shares sum to roughly every trace — so each earlier case contained exactly one of them. In the later window, none of the three appears in any of the 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006]

- When present, the three sat around the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.51–0.52) and each led either to A23 or to A24 (roughly 40–60 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions to each). [POS001, POS002, POS003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

- Routing around them changed completely. Earlier, A22 led to A23 in about 55 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and to A20, A14 or A15 in most of the rest (about 21, 14 and 11 in 100), while A23 led to A24 in about 45 in 100 and to A14, A20 or A15 otherwise (about 19, 19 and 17 in 100). In the later window, A22 leads only to A23 and A23 only to A24. [DIR004, DIR005]

- Relationally, each of A14, A15 and A20 previously always co-occurred with A22, which preceded it, and with A24, which followed it; against A23 the order was mixed, each coming before A23 in roughly 11–21 in 100 traces and after it in roughly 17–19. In the later window, none of the three shares a trace with A22, A23 or A24. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]

- The distance from A22 to A24 shortened from 3 steps in every earlier trace to 2 steps in every later trace; A23 now immediately follows A22, and A24 immediately follows A23, in all later traces, whereas earlier each was immediate in only about 55 and 45 in 100 of cases respectively. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

- Consistent with a shortened trace, A22 now sits slightly later (mean relative position about 0.46 to 0.47) and A24 slightly earlier (about 0.57 to 0.55). [POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of three mutually exclusive activities A14, A15 and A20 — previously executed once per case between A22 and A24, inserted on either side of A23 — is no longer executed, so that in the later window every case runs directly from A22 to A23 and on to A24. The signs are direct and consistent: participation of all three fell from 28–40 in 100 traces to 0 of 200 against solid earlier support (64, 56 and 80 traces); the successor profiles of A22 and A23 collapsed to single targets; and every relation and lag item moves the same way, including the A22-to-A24 distance shrinking from 3 to 2 steps. Screening also qualified 28 items where about 0.39 would be expected if nothing had changed, and each item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled traces, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: the qualifying items largely re-describe one underlying difference rather than confirming it independently, and zero participation strictly means only that the three activities were not observed in the later sample. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the branch through A14, A15 and A20 is still possible but has become so rare that none of the 200 later traces contains it. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
