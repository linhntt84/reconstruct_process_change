Before, A21 preceded each of A30, A31 and A36 in all 200 traces, and A22 likewise preceded each of them in all 200 traces; in the later window, each of A30, A31 and A36 precedes A21 in about 199 of 200 traces and likewise precedes A22, while all six pairs still co-occur in every trace in both windows. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the three-activity block A30, A31, A36 exchanged places with the segment containing A22 and A21: before, a case ran A20, A22, then later A21, then the A30/A31/A36 block, then A25; in the later window it runs A20, the A30/A31/A36 block, A22, then later A21, then A25. All six activities still occur in essentially every trace in both windows, so this is a reordering of existing activities, not activities becoming optional, repeated or replaced. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

The reordering is shown directly by the near-deterministic order reversals and by successor changes at exactly the block's old and new entry and exit points, with the position and distance shifts agreeing; each profile rests on all 200 traces per window. At the family level, 24 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.41 expected by chance in an unchanged process (about 60 times the chance expectation), so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of these windows produce. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

Limitations: the qualifying items largely describe one underlying rearrangement rather than independent confirmations; the six distance items about gaps inside the block and from the block to A25 rest on a single after-window observation each and were not used; A22's outgoing transitions were not profiled, and positions are event positions rather than clock times. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the segment between A22 and A21 also changed in unobserved ways, so the exact new sequence around A22 and A21 remains uncertain.
