### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 were executed in every trace (participation 100 in 100, about one occurrence per trace). In the later window, each appears in only about 53 in 100 traces (106–107 of 200), and the three still occur virtually only together: for each pair, co-occurrence is about 0.53 and "neither present" about 0.47, with almost no traces containing one of them without the others. - A26, A41, A46 and A47 remain in every trace in both windows (100 in 100 participation). [REL010, REL015, POS002, POS003, POS004]
- When the three activities do run, their placement is unchanged: A41 still precedes A19, A25 and A20 (in about 52 of 53 joint appearances), A25 still leads to A20 (about 98 in 100 of A25's outgoing transitions) and A20 still leads to A26 (about 96 in 100). [REL011, REL016, REL020, DIR004, DIR003]

[OCC002, OCC003, LAG003, DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment A19 → A25 → A20, which ran between A41 and A26, became optional: in the earlier window every trace executed it, while in the later window roughly 47 in 100 traces skip it, with A41 then passing on to A26, often in the immediately following step. The change is direct (participation fell from 100 to about 53 in 100 on 200-trace windows) and consistent: the three activities still appear only as one block, keep their internal order and their position between A41 and A26 whenever they run, and the A41→A26 handover with a shortened distance appears exactly in the traces without the block. The evidence cannot show whether this skip path existed but was unobserved among the 200 earlier traces or is newly available, and it cannot point to a cause; a shift in the mix of pre-existing process variants would look the same. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL008, REL009, REL014, REL010, REL011, DIR005, LAG002]

Other differences: A02 is executed much less often (from 29 to about 9 in 100 traces) and lost its fixed place between A46 and A47 near the end of the trace, now appearing earlier, more variably placed and partly independent of the A19/A20/A25 block; A04 and A06 are somewhat more frequent; A46 and A47 are now almost always adjacent. These are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001, POS001, DIR006, REL001, REL004, OCC002, OCC003, LAG003]
