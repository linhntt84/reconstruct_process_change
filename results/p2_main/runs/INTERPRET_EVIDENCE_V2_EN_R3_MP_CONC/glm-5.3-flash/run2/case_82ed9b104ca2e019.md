### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a block of five mutually exclusive activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27) is executed between A04 and A28, with A04 placed directly after A43 — that is, the earlier direct step from A43 to A28 appears to have been replaced by a chain A43 → A04 → one of the five alternatives → A28, with A43 also reaching the alternatives directly in a minority of cases. The signs are relational and form a consistent chain: A43's outgoing traffic moved from A28 (about 83 in 100) to A04 (about 60 in 100) and the five alternatives (about 38 in 100 combined); A04 immediately succeeds A43 in about 96 in 100 cases; A04's outgoing traffic concentrates on the five; each of the five leads to A28; the A04 → A28 gap is exactly two steps in about 95 in 100 cases while the A43 → A28 gap moved from one to two–three steps; and participation shifted accordingly (A04 from about 16 to 65 in 100 traces, the five from at most about 1 to about 19–24 in 100). [DIR008, LAG002, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG001, LAG004, REL006, OCC001, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008] Screening recorded 62 qualifying control-flow items against fewer than one expected if nothing had changed (0.73), so this ensemble of differences is far beyond what random re-splits of a stable process produce; and because the five activities were nearly absent earlier, this reads as a new segment being executed between existing activities rather than a re-weighting of previously existing paths.

The evidence cannot show whether the direct A43 → A28 step was removed entirely or only made rare, since an immediate A43 → A28 succession was not observed at all in the later window; nor can it establish that the near-disappearance of A06 and A08 belongs to the same change rather than being a separate one. A competing reading — that A04 and the five-activity block form a newly added parallel branch beside an unchanged A43 → A28 step — is not fully ruled out, although the loss of the immediate A43 → A28 succession argues against it. [LAG004, OCC002, OCC003]
