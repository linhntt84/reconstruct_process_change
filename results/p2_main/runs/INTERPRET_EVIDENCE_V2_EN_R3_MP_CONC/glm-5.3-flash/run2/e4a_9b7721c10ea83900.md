### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a timing change centred on A34: in the later window, the elapsed time between the completion of each of its predecessors A26, A35 and A37 and the completion of A34 itself is about one hour longer than before, roughly doubling from about one hour to about two hours, consistently across all three pairs and across the whole distribution [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003]. The main limitation is that this indicator measures elapsed time between consecutive completion events, not pure waiting or service time, so the evidence cannot distinguish whether A34 was completed later, its predecessors earlier, or whether about an hour of other work now lies between them; the three items also share A34 and may reflect one underlying change rather than three independent ones [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003].

Other differences: the number of observed consecutive A37→A34 completion pairs fell from 82 to 52 and A35→A34 pairs from 76 to 66; no control-flow indicator qualified at these thresholds, so this may be sampling variation, though a structural shift (other events now interleaving between these predecessors and A34) cannot be ruled out [TWAIT002, TWAIT003].
