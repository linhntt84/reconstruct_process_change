### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Main hypothesis: in the later window, a new branch formed by A32, A33, A38, A39 and A40 runs between A26 and A28 in roughly 43–45 in 100 traces, as an alternative to A27; A27, which earlier followed A26 in every trace, is skipped in exactly those traces, changing from mandatory to conditional while the new group was added. The hypothesis is supported directly and consistently: A26 now routes about 43 in 100 of its outgoing transitions into the new group, the group runs after A26 and before A28 in about 43 in 100 traces, A27 and the group never occur together, and the occurrence, routing and relational indicators all describe the same share of traces. The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces here: 72 items qualified against roughly 0.83 expected by chance in the control-flow family. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC008, DIR004, REL019, REL020, REL026, REL031, REL036]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different mixes of coexisting process variants whose shares shifted, rather than a modification taking effect at this point; and because the new activities appear in both orders among themselves, their internal structure can only be described as an unordered block, with positions based on event order rather than clock time. [REL036, REL043, REL045]

Other differences: A06 became rarer and decoupled from A16 (halved participation, later position, dispersed successors, lost immediate-successor relation), A10 became more frequent, and A04's successors changed on weak support; these are not part of the main hypothesis.
