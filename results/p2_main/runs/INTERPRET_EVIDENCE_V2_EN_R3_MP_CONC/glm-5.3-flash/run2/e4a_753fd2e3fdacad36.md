### Observed differences

- **The five-activity block changed its place in the case.** In the earlier window, exactly one of A14, A15, A20, A21, A22 ran in each case (about 18–23 in 100 cases per activity; the five shares sum to all cases), positioned at about 0.30–0.33 of the trace. In the later window these activities sit at about 0.58–0.61 of the trace, while participation is essentially unchanged (about 15–23 in 100 cases each, still one block member per case). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

- **Their order relative to A23 and A24 flipped.** Earlier, whenever a block member co-occurred with A23 or A24 (about 18–23 in 100 cases), the block member always came first; in the later window it always comes after both, with co-occurrence shares barely changed and no interleaving in either window. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

- **Routing at the block's edges changed.** Earlier, all observed outgoing transitions of each block member went to A23, and A42's exits were spread over the five members (about 18–23% each), so A42 reached A23 with one block member in between (two steps apart in every case). In the later window, A42 leads directly to A23 (one step apart in every case), and the block members exit to A25 and A29 instead: about 45%/55% for A14, 37%/63% for A15, 51%/49% for A20 and A21, and 68%/33% for A22. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR008, LAG004]

- **The late segment absorbed the block.** Earlier, A24 led only to A25 and A25 only to A29, directly consecutive, with A24 and A29 always two steps apart. In the later window, A24 leads to A25 in about 49% of its exits and to a block member in about 51%; A25 leads to A29 in about 51% and to a block member in about 49%; A24 and A29 are now always three steps apart, so exactly one block member sits between them in every case. [DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS006, POS007, POS008]

- **Timing.** The elapsed time between the completion of each of A03, A04, A05, A06, A07, A08 and the completion of A09 halved, from means of about 0.96–1.07 hours to about 0.49–0.51 hours, consistently across all six pairs (22–51 observations per pair per window). [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004, TWAIT005, TWAIT006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the five-activity block A14/A15/A20/A21/A22 — exactly one of which executes per case — now runs in the late segment of the case instead of the early one. In the earlier window the flow ran A42 → block member → A23, with the block member before A23 and A24; in the later window A42 connects directly to A23, and the block member runs after A24, inserted between A24 and A29 (before A25 in about half the cases, after A25 in the other half) and exiting to A25 or A29 rather than to A23. The block remained mandatory and its per-member participation is unchanged, so no activity became optional, repeated or rare. [DIR001, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL008]

Supporting signs: 28 control-flow items qualified, far more than the about 0.4 expected if nothing had changed, and they all describe this one change consistently rather than as independent findings — successor changes at A42, the block members, A24 and A25; order flips between every block member and A23/A24; step-gap changes on A42→A23, A24→A25, A25→A29 and A24→A29; and the block's position shift. The relational items show the placement change directly, so it does not rest on position statistics alone. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Limitations and competing explanation: the two windows are finite samples and only the tested units produced evidence, so additional changes in untested parts of the process (for example between A23 and A24) cannot be ruled out; the exit split of each block member between A25 and A29 rests on only 30–45 traces per member; and the evidence shows only the aggregate roughly 50/50 split of the insertion point, not what decides it in an individual case (A22 leans to before A25 at about 68%, A15 to after A25 at about 63%). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003]

Other differences: the elapsed time from the completion of each of A03–A08 to the completion of A09 fell from about 1.0 hour to about 0.5 hour, consistently across all six measured pairs and clearly beyond chance (six qualifying items against fewer than 0.01 expected). This is a timing change in its own right; the evidence cannot show whether it is connected to the block's change of place, because A03–A08 and A09 appear in none of the control-flow items. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004, TWAIT005, TWAIT006]
