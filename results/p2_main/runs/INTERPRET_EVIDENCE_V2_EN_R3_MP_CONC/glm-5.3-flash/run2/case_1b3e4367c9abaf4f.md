### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural reorganisation of the flow: A01, not observed in the earlier window, now executes once in every case at a late position, is reached from A15, A16 and A05, and exchanges order freely with A15/A16, while the former alternatives A02 and A12 became a mandatory A02 → A12 sequence (A02 precedes A12 in about 97 in 100 cases and precedes A01 in every case) and A02 now routes mainly to A06, which executes about twice as often per case. The supporting signs are direct and consistent across participation, outgoing-transition, position and pairwise-relation indicators, each item rests on 96–200 traces per window, every difference is larger than in 199 of 200 random re-splits, and 58 qualified items against roughly 0.5 expected for an unchanged process cannot be explained by sampling. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, REL008, REL001, REL007, OCC002, OCC005, REL015, DIR002, OCC003]

The evidence cannot show whether A01 is a genuinely new activity or a re-labelled existing one, and it cannot rule out that the two windows simply sample different pre-existing variants (one with, one without A01) rather than a modification at the change point. [OCC001, POS001]

Other differences: A11's participation roughly doubled (about 26 to 53 in 100 traces) and it now co-occurs with A02 in about half the traces where the two were previously mutually exclusive [OCC004, REL014]; A03 and A07 occur later within traces and farther after A02 than before [POS003, POS004, LAG001, LAG003].
