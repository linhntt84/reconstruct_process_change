### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: in the earlier window A44 was typically followed by exactly one of the mutually exclusive activities A19, A20 or A21 (each taken in roughly a third of the cases), which then led to A29, whereas in the later window a block of three activities, A26, A27 and A28, runs together in every case between A44 and A29, without a fixed internal order. The signs are direct and consistent: participation flips from absent to universal for the block and from about a third of cases to near zero for the old trio, A44's outgoing transitions moved from the old trio to the block, the block's order relative to A44 (before it) and A29 (after it) holds in about 99–100 in 100 traces, and the typical A44-to-A29 distance grew from 2 to 4 positions, matching the larger block. This is a replacement of one group of activities by another rather than a re-weighting of existing paths, since the block was unobserved earlier and the trio is nearly unobserved later; screening also qualified 74 control-flow items where fewer than one would be expected without a change, all with differences larger than about 995 in 1000 random re-splits. The main limitation is that the later-window profiles of A19, A20 and A21 rest on a single trace each, so outright removal cannot be distinguished from a drop to very low frequency.
