### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the sequence A19 → A20 ran in every trace (both in 200 of 200 traces, A19 before A20 in about 99 in 100); in the later window each appears in only about 52 in 100 traces, about 48 in 100 traces contain neither, and when both occur their order is unchanged [OCC002, OCC003, REL010].
- The exclusive choice between the branch pair A25+A26 (always executed together) and A27 was resolved in every earlier trace (A25+A26 in about 50 in 100, A27 in about 50 in 100, never both); in the later window A25 and A26 each appear in about 27 in 100 traces, A27 in about 26 in 100, and in about 48 in 100 traces neither option runs, while the two options still never occur together [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL025, REL026, REL031].
- The choice block now runs only together with A19/A20: A19 co-occurs with A25 in about 27 in 100 later traces and with A27 in about 25 in 100, which sums to A19's own participation, so the segment appears to be skipped as one unit [REL011, REL013].
- A28, A43, A48 and A49 still occur in every trace in both windows, but their co-occurrence with A19 and A20 fell from 100% to about 52 in 100, i.e., they now also run in traces without the segment [REL014, REL015, REL021, REL022, REL023].
- Routing out of A43 changed accordingly: before, about 96 in 100 of A43's outgoing transitions led to A19; in the later window about 49 in 100 lead to A19 and about 46 in 100 lead directly to A28, and the A43→A28 pair, previously always 4–5 steps apart, is immediately adjacent in about 48 in 100 cases [DIR005, LAG003].
- A06 participation fell from about 32 in 100 traces to about 10 in 100; transitions from A48 to A06 nearly disappeared (about 28 in 100 of A48's outgoing transitions before, under 1 in 100 later), and A48 is now immediately followed by A49 in about 97 in 100 cases versus about 71 in 100 before [OCC001, DIR007, LAG004].
- A06's own outgoing transitions shifted from mostly A49 (about 85 in 100) to a spread over many targets (largest share A47, about 24 in 100), and its median position moved from near the end of the trace to mid-trace; both rest on few later observations (21 outgoing transitions, 19 traces) [DIR002, POS001, LAG001].
- Smaller or weakly supported movements: A04's outgoing transitions (11 per window) point to an almost completely different successor set in the later window, while several other routing and position indicators (A20→A28, A27→A20, A46→A47, the A26→A28 lag, positions of A28 and A43) moved only negligibly [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, POS002, POS003, LAG002].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment consisting of A19, A20 and the following exclusive choice between A25+A26 and A27 became optional: before, every trace executed A19 → A20 and exactly one of the two branch options, whereas in the later window about 48 in 100 traces contain none of these activities, traces that do execute the segment show the same internal order and exclusivity as before, and control passes from A43 directly to A28 instead of through the segment [OCC002, OCC003, REL010, REL011, REL013, DIR005, LAG003].

Supporting signs: 59 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.85 expected by chance for 170 tested units, so the volume of agreeing evidence is far beyond what an unchanged process produces; the participation drop is measured directly on 200 traces per window (100% to about 52% for A19 and A20, with about 48 in 100 traces containing neither), and the occurrence, relation, direction and lag indicators all describe the same skip consistently [OCC002, REL010, DIR005, LAG003].

Limitations: the evidence shows that traces skip the segment but not why; it cannot rule out that the later window pools two case variants that already differed in this segment rather than a newly added skip, and the per-trace coupling of the A19/A20 part with the choice part is inferred from co-occurrence margins, although these align almost exactly (neither in about 48–49 in 100 for every pair in the segment) [REL010, REL013, REL018, REL020, REL026].

Other differences: A06 became much rarer (about 32 to about 10 in 100 traces) and lost its previous placement between A48 and A49, which now connect directly; its rerouting to a spread of successors rests on only about 19–21 later observations [OCC001, DIR007, LAG004, DIR002, POS001]. A04's successor set differs almost completely between windows but is based on 11 outgoing transitions per window [DIR001].
