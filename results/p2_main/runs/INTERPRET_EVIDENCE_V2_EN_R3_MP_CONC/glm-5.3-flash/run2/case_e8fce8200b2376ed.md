### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a group of new activities — A07, A15, A16 and A17 — was wired in between established activities in the later window, replacing several direct successions: A08 now routes through A07 (which leads on to A18) where it earlier led straight to A05; A10 now routes through A15 and A16 (A15 back to A10, A16 on to A02 and A03) where it earlier led to A03, A02 and itself; A17 leads on to A09; and A18 now continues to A14 and A17 instead of only to A02 [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009].

The support is direct and consistent: the four activities are absent from all 200 earlier traces yet appear in 65–139 later traces with well-observed outgoing edges, the predecessors' old targets disappear from their successor profiles just as the new edges appear, and the relational items show the new activities co-occurring with, and ordered against, established activities rather than appearing in isolation; with 92 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.6 expected there if nothing had changed, chance sampling cannot account for the ensemble, and each item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, OCC002, OCC005, REL007, REL013]. The evidence cannot show whether these four activities were truly absent before or only unobserved in the earlier sample, nor whether the later window reflects a single modified process or a mixture of variants; positions and distances are measured in event steps, so nothing follows about clock-time timing [OCC002, POS003, POS005].

Other differences: A05 and A13 repeat more often per trace (A05 about 1.9 to 4.0; A13 about 1.0 to 2.1) and A05 sits earlier, while A18 repeats less (about 1.8 to 0.9), participates in more traces and sits later; these fit the rewiring above, but the evidence does not establish them as part of the same mechanism [OCC001, OCC003, OCC007, POS002, POS007].
