### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the control flow around the middle block was restructured. In the earlier window, A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 formed a repeating, interleaved loop (about 3.6 runs per trace, interleaved in about 70 in 100 traces) that A14 and A15 accompanied without a fixed order, A10 appeared only in about 21 in 100 traces, and A38 typically returned to A30. In the later window, the block runs exactly once per trace in a fixed sequence (A30 first, then A31/A36/A37 in either order, then A38, which now exits to A25), A14 and A15 precede the block in a stable order, and A10 has become a regular step fed by A41 and typically followed by A19 or A20 before the block. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, REL005, REL010, REL013, REL018, REL033, REL037, REL042, LAG001, LAG023]

The ordering claims rest on direct relational, successor and gap evidence for activity pairs that co-occur in all traces in both windows, so they are not rare-variant artefacts; the repetition and participation changes rest on all 200 traces per window; and 116 items qualified against about 1.16 expected if nothing had changed, which chance cannot explain as a whole — though many items describe the same underlying restructuring and are not independent confirmations. [REL013, OCC006, OCC005]

Limitations: control-flow evidence alone cannot distinguish a modification of one process from a shift in the mix of two pre-existing variants (for example a previously rare single-pass variant dominating the later window), and the rerouting detail for A02, A04 and A06 rests on only about 9–20 observations in the later window. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

Other differences: A02, A04, A06 and A08 became rare, dropping from about 23–31 in 100 traces to about 5–11 in 100, with changed but weakly supported outgoing profiles; A44 and A46 now lead almost exclusively to A45 and A47 respectively, and A46 is followed by A47 at a gap of one event in about 995 in 1000 cases (before about 835 in 1000).
