### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence directly supports the hypothesis that A03 now runs where A01 used to, in the segment between A02 and A07: earlier every case ran A02 → A01 → A07 with A01 at about one-fifth of the trace, whereas in the later window every case runs A02 → A03 → A07 with A03 at the same relative position and the same successor, and A01 is no longer observed in any of the 200 later traces. The all-or-nothing participation swap, the redirected successor of A02, the new A03→A07 edge, and the matching position and pairwise relations all describe this one difference, and 24 qualifying items against roughly 0.12 expected by chance in a stable process is far beyond what sampling noise produces (each item's difference also exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits). A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 and A03 are the same step recorded under a different code, since the behaviour around the slot is otherwise identical. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, REL001, REL002, REL006, REL008, REL010]

Other differences: a new final activity, A06, was introduced after A05. Earlier, about half of the traces ended with A05 itself (A05 had an observed successor in only about 101 of 200 traces, always A08); in the later window A05 always has a successor — A06 in about 47 in 100 cases and A08 in the remainder — and A05's position is fixed at 0.8.
