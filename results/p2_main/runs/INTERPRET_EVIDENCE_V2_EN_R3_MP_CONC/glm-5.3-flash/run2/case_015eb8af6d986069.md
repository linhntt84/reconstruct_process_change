### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering of the activity group A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25 and A32 changed from variable, partly mixed orders to a single fixed, evenly spaced sequence A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32. Earlier, several of these pairs appeared in both orders in similar shares (A20/A26 about 49 vs 50 in 100; A24 before A20 in 73 in 100) and at varying distances, and successors were split across several activities; later, each member has one dominant successor (about 98–99 in 100), each pair follows one order, and inter-member distances are constant. Direction, relation, lag and position evidence all show this directly and consistently, every involved activity occurs in essentially all traces of both windows, and the screening produced 82 qualifying items against about 1 expected without change, so chance is implausible for the pattern as a whole. [DIR007, DIR013, REL016, REL019, LAG006, LAG007, LAG010, POS002, POS005]

Limitations: positions and lags are measured in event positions, not clock time, so the change concerns sequence rather than timing. The evidence also cannot establish whether this fixed sequence is a newly imposed ordering or a pre-existing rare variant that is now always taken; such a reweighting of earlier variants is the main competing explanation, made less plausible — but not excluded — by the near-total disappearance of the old orders (A24 before A20: 73 in 100 earlier, essentially 0 later). A few lag and direction items rest on only 1–13 observations and were not relied on. [REL014, LAG019, LAG024, LAG027]

Other differences: A08 became optional on the earlier main path — its participation fell from 72 to 29 in 100 traces and the route A15 → A08 → A16 is now typically shortcut to A15 → A16 (A15→A16 up from 58 to 87 in 100; A15→A08 down from 42 to 7 in 100); A10 participation rose from 7.5 to 22 in 100 traces; A02 and A04 show successor redistribution on few observations. [OCC001, DIR006, LAG001, OCC002, DIR004, DIR001, DIR002]
