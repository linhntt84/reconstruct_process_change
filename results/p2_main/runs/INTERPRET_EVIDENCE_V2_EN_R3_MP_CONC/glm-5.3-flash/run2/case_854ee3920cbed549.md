### Observed differences

- Five activities that never appeared in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces each) occur throughout the later window: A19 in about 23 in 100 traces, A20 and A25 in about 22 each, A26 in about 17 and A27 in about 18. Their shares sum to roughly 100 in 100 traces and they hardly ever occur together (co-occurrence at most about 1 in 100), so almost every later-window trace contains exactly one of them. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL010, REL011, REL016]
- The new activity runs inside the sequence A29 → A30 → A31, which earlier was consecutive: A30 immediately followed A29 in about 99 in 100 traces and A31 immediately followed A30 in about 97 in 100. In the later window A31 still follows A29 in essentially every trace, but two events later (distance 3 in about 95 in 100 traces, previously distance 2 in 97 in 100), and each adjacent pair is split roughly half and half between "immediate" and "one event in between" — consistent with the new activity sitting directly before A30 in about half of the traces and directly after A30 in the other half. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, DIR007, DIR008]
- Relations and positions match this reading: the new activities appear around the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.5), co-occur with A29, A30 or A31 in about 22–23 in 100 traces (never earlier), always run after A29 and before A31 (for example A19 precedes A31 in about 22 in 100 later-window traces and never follows it), and lead onward to A30 or A31 in roughly equal shares. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL013, REL015, REL020, REL024, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- A06 became more frequent and moved: about 8 in 100 traces contained it earlier (15 of 200) versus about 28 in 100 later (55 of 200); its median relative position moved from about 0.55 to about 0.90; and its outgoing transitions changed from a spread over many activities (A28 about 25 in 100, A31 about 13 in 100) to A47 in about 85 in 100 cases. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001]
- A new connection around A46 and A47 appeared: earlier, A46 led to A47 in about 91 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and virtually never to A06; later, about 32 in 100 of A46's transitions go to A06 (A47 down to about 68 in 100), A06 follows A46 within one step in about 3 of 4 of the observed pairs (52 such pairs later versus 2 earlier), and A06–A47 co-occurrence rose from about 5 to 26 in 100 traces with A06 preceding A47. [DIR009, LAG004, REL008, REL009, LAG005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that an additional step, drawn from five previously unexercised activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, A27), now runs inside the sequence A29 → A30 → A31 in essentially every later-window trace, where earlier that sequence ran consecutively; the new activity sits between A29 and A31, directly before A30 in about half of the traces and directly after A30 in the other half. This is shown directly and consistently: the A29→A31 distance moved from two to three events in about 95 in 100 traces while the adjacent pairs each split between immediate and one-event gaps, the five activities occur only in the later window with 33–55 supporting traces each, and the predecessor, successor, co-occurrence and position evidence all agree. With 49 qualifying control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected without a change (0.68), the ensemble is far beyond what a stable process typically produces. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR002, DIR007, DIR008, REL013, REL015]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that these activities were not exercised in that sample, not that they are new to the process; positions are event positions rather than clock times; and the evidence cannot establish whether the five activities are alternatives for a single new step or five separate options that happen not to combine. The main competing explanation — that these paths existed before but were simply not triggered in the earlier 200 traces — cannot be ruled out, although 0 of 200 earlier traces versus 33–55 of 200 later traces makes it implausible. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL010, REL011, REL016]

Other differences: A06, A46 and A47 show a second change outside the main hypothesis. A path A46 → A06 → A47 appears in roughly a quarter of the later-window traces (A06 participation up from about 8 to 28 in 100 traces, now late in the trace, with A46 sending about 32 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A06 and A06 sending about 85 in 100 of its transitions to A47), where earlier A46 led to A47 directly in about 91 in 100 of its transitions. The evidence cannot rule out that A06 simply became more frequent on a rare existing path, but its near-complete redirection towards A47 and its new predecessor A46 support the redirection reading. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR009, LAG004, REL008, REL009]
