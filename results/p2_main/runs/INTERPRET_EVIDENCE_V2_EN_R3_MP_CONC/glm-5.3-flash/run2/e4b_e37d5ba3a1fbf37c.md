### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation. In the earlier window, assignment work on A01, A03, A13, A14, A17 and A22 was led by one group of resources (including User_5) and A24 was shared across 13 resources; in the later window a new group (including User_49) has taken over those six activities, and A24 is handled exclusively by User_30, which previously performed only about 19 in 100 of its assignments.
