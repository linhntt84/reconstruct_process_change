### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace ended with the pair A02 → A01: both activities ran once in all 200 traces, all of A02's outgoing transitions went to A01, and A01 always sat at the final relative position (1.0). In the later window, neither A01 nor A02 appears in any of the 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, POS001, POS002, REL001]
- A03 was also present in every earlier trace, about 2.1 times per trace (repeating within the trace in about 55 in 100 cases), placed early (in about a quarter of its occurrences at the very start), and led to A06 in about 53 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and to A07 in about 47 in 100. In the later window, A03 is not observed at all. [OCC003, DIR002, POS003, REL002]
- A04 shows the reverse pattern: not observed earlier, it now runs exactly once in every trace, always at the final relative position. [OCC004, POS004]
- A05 runs in every trace in both windows, but its successor changed completely: earlier it always led to A02; in the later window it always leads to A04. [DIR003, REL004, REL010, REL019]
- A08 likewise changed successor: earlier it always led to A03; in the later window it leads to A06 in about 46 in 100 cases and to A07 in about 54 in 100. [DIR004]
- Pair distances in the remaining cycle each moved one step shorter: A06→A05 from 4 to 3 steps, A06→A07 from 3 to 2, A08→A06 and A08→A07 from 2 to 1, and the repeat distances of A06 and A08 from 3 to 2 steps. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]
- A06 and A07 still occur in similar shares of traces (A06 in about 55 in 100 traces earlier vs about 50 in 100 later; A07 in all traces in both windows) but sit earlier within the trace (A06 mean relative position about 0.27 → 0.18; A07 about 0.48 → 0.30). [POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One structural reading supported by the evidence is that the end of the process was reorganised. The closing sequence A05 → A02 → A01, previously executed in every trace, is no longer observed and appears replaced by a single final activity A04 executed directly after A05; in the same restructuring, A03 — which earlier sat inside the repeating cycle (A08 always led to A03, A03 led to A06 or A07, with pair distances consistent with a cycle A08 → A03 → A06 that exited via A07) — is no longer observed, and A08 now leads directly to A06 (about 46 in 100) or A07 (about 54 in 100), shortening each pass through the cycle by one step. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS004, REL019, LAG001, LAG005, LAG007]

Supporting signs: the successor changes are observed directly (A05: A02→A04; A08: A03→A06/A07) rather than inferred from positions alone; the appearance and disappearance are measured over all 200 traces per window (participation 1.0 → 0.0 for A01–A03, 0.0 → 1.0 for A04); all seven lag items shift by exactly one step in the same direction; and 43 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.24 expected if nothing had changed, with each item's difference exceeding about 995 in 1,000 random re-splits, so sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [DIR003, DIR004, OCC001, OCC004, LAG001, LAG007]

Limitations: zero participation shows only that A01, A02 and A03 were not exercised in the later sample, not that they were removed from the process definition; the lag shares rest on moderate numbers of observed pairs (roughly 50–110 per window); and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows capture different coexisting variants or a shifted case mix rather than a change over time. It also does not show what now opens a trace, since the previously early A03 is gone and no replacement entry activity is identified. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, LAG002, LAG005]

Other differences: A06 and A07 now occur slightly earlier within traces, and A06 appears in a slightly smaller share of traces (about 50 vs 55 in 100); these shifts are consistent with the shortened cycle rather than an independent change. [POS005, POS006]
