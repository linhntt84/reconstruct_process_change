### Observed differences

- The three activities A14, A15 and A20 were not observed at all in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces each); in the later window A15 runs in about 41 in 100 traces, A20 in about 30 and A14 in about 29, each at most once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The three never appear together: no later trace contains two of them, and about 199 of 200 later traces contain exactly one of the three, so they behave as mutually exclusive alternatives. [REL001, REL002, REL006]
- In the earlier window the mid-process sequence was rigid: A22 was always directly followed by A23, A23 always directly by A24, and A24 stood exactly two positions after A22 in every trace. [DIR004, DIR005, LAG002]
- In the later window exactly one event is inserted between A22 and A24 in almost every trace: the A22→A24 distance is three positions in about 199 of 200 traces, versus two positions in all 200 earlier traces. [LAG002]
- The insertion point varies: A22 now leads directly to one of the new activities in about 56 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (A15 about 24, A20 about 17, A14 about 16) and still to A23 in about 44; A23 now leads directly to a new activity in about 44 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and to A24 in about 57. Accordingly, A22 and A23 are two positions apart in about 56 in 100 later traces (previously always adjacent), and A23 and A24 are two positions apart in about 44 in 100 (previously always adjacent). [DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003]
- Each new activity is followed directly by A23 in about 53–58 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and by A24 in the remainder; A22 always precedes the new activity and the new activity always precedes A24, so the new step sits strictly inside the former A22–A24 span. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012]
- The new activities occur at a stable mid-trace position (mean about 0.51 of the trace, narrow spread), and A22's mean position moved slightly earlier (about 0.48 to 0.46), consistent with traces becoming one event longer; A22, A23 and A24 still occur in essentially all traces in both windows. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the later window contains a structurally extended variant of the earlier process: one extra step is inserted into the previously fixed sequence A22 → A23 → A24, performed by exactly one of three alternative activities — A15 in about 41 in 100 traces, A14 in about 29 and A20 in about 30 — placed either directly after A22, before A23 (about 56 in 100 traces) or directly after A23, before A24 (about 44 in 100). Since none of the three activities was observed earlier, this is an addition of activities woven into an existing chain, not a re-weighting of previously observed paths. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG002, REL005, REL009, REL012]

The change is shown directly and consistently at every level: participation jumps from zero to 29–41 in 100 traces [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]; the direct-succession profiles of A22 and A23 gain the new activities as successors while A22→A23 and A23→A24 lose their exclusivity [DIR004, DIR005]; the new activities' own successors and order relations fix them between A22 and A24 [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012]; and the lag profiles show exactly one inserted position [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]. Overall, 27 items qualified against about 0.4 expected by chance from 81 tested units (all control-flow), and every item's difference exceeds those seen in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, so the item volume alone cannot be explained by sampling noise. [OCC001, DIR004, REL003, LAG002]

The main competing explanation — that A14, A15 and A20 existed before as a very rare variant that the 200 earlier traces failed to sample — cannot be fully excluded, since zero participation only means "not observed"; at 29–41 in 100 participation afterwards, with equal 200-trace windows and 27 qualifying items against 0.4 expected, it is nevertheless implausible. The evidence also cannot show what determines which of the three alternatives runs in a given trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: none beyond the insertion itself; A22's slight position shift and the split A22→A23 and A23→A24 distances are consequences of the same inserted step. [POS004, LAG001, LAG003]
