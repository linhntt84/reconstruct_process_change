Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: in the later window, the work on A01, A03, A13, A14, A17 and A21 was reassigned from one group of resources to a largely different group (for example User_49 out, User_56 in), and A08, previously shared across about 17 resources, came to be performed exclusively by User_121.
