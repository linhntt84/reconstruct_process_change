### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of five alternative activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) between A38 and A23 is no longer executed, so that in the later window A38 leads directly to A23. Earlier, every trace ran exactly one of the five between A38 and A23; in the later window none of the five appears in any trace, A38's outgoing transitions go to A23 alone, and the A38-to-A23 distance shrank from two positions to one. [DIR006, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, LAG001] The volume of qualifying evidence far exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces (44 control-flow items against roughly 0.5 expected by chance, and 4 time items against roughly 0.01), and the control-flow items all describe this one coherent redirection, so a chance sampling difference is not a plausible account of the pattern as a whole. [DIR006, LAG001, TWAIT001] A limitation: zero participation only means the five activities were not observed in the later window, so the evidence cannot rule out that they still occur but are no longer recorded under these codes; it also cannot show why the segment disappeared.

Other differences: The elapsed time between consecutive completions of A16, A17, A18 or A19 and of A28 roughly doubled, from about 1 hour to about 2.1 hours, consistently across the four pairs; this is a timing difference in its own right, and since no evidence relates these activities to the removed block, the data cannot show whether the two differences are connected or independent. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004] In addition, A41 and A42 each ran in about 67 in 100 traces in the later window, up from about 53 in 100; the evidence does not show how these activities are reached, so this increase stands as a separate, unexplained participation change.
