### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the final step A03, which previously executed in every trace directly after A02, is no longer executed, leaving A02 as the last observed activity: before, every trace ran A02 then A03; in the later window every trace still runs A02, A03 is entirely absent, A02 shows no observed successor, and A02 now occupies the final position. The five items all describe this one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, but chance is very unlikely as a whole: 5 control-flow items qualified from 9 tested units, against roughly 0.04 expected if nothing had changed (well over a hundred times the chance expectation), and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data.
