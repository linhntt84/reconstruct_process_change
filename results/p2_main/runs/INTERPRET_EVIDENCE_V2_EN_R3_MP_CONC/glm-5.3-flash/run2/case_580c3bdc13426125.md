### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural rework of the loop section involving A02, A03, A05, A06 and A07: earlier, A05 always fed into A02, which repeated (about 2.2 times per trace, alternating with A06 via A06 → A02) before the trace closed with A07; later, A05 feeds into A03 exactly once, A03 routes to A06 or A07, and the repetition is carried by A06 repeating itself. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the full participation flip of A02 (100 to 0 in 100 traces) and A03 (0 to 100), the successor changes at A05 and at the loop activities, the co-occurrence flips, and the appearance of immediate A06 self-repetition. With 21 qualifying items against roughly 0.12 expected by chance across the 24 tested units, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole.
