### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering constraints among A20, A22, A23, A24, A25 and A26 were tightened: a group of activities that previously appeared in variable and partially mixed orders is now executed as one fixed sequence, A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25, entered from A31 via A22 and exiting to A32 via A25. The signs are direct and consistent: pairwise relations flipped or became fixed, successor profiles collapsed to one dominant target each, and step distances concentrated on single values. [REL002, REL005, REL007, REL009, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, LAG003, LAG011]

Chance is an unlikely explanation: 58 items qualified where fewer than 1 was expected without a change, and the items behind this hypothesis rest on about 200 traces per window with full co-occurrence in both windows, so rare-variant sampling cannot account for them. [screening]

Limitations: the evidence shows pairwise relations and one-step transitions, so it cannot establish whether the earlier variability was a single variant with an unordered block or a mixture of ordered variants; and the finding concerns ordering constraints, not a swap of two activities' positions. [REL002, REL004, REL005]

Other differences: A08 occurs in far fewer traces and is rerouted away from A15 towards several targets, and A10 occurs in more traces; these are separate from the main hypothesis. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, OCC002]
