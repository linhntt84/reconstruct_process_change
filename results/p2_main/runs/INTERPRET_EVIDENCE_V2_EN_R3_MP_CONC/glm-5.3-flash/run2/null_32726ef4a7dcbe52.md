- The main successor of A36 flipped: before, about 59 in 100 of A36's outgoing transitions led to A33 and about 34 in 100 to A37 (with a few rare successors at 1–2 in 100); in the later window, about 62 in 100 led to A37 and about 38 in 100 to A33, and those rare successors were no longer observed. - Separately, A07 appeared in more traces (about 54 in 100 versus about 42 in 100 before) at slightly later and more variable relative positions (mean about 0.07 versus 0.05), and A14 followed A07 more immediately (one step later in about 46 in 100 of pairs versus about 28 before, while three steps later fell from about 42 to 25 in 100). - Every difference above was larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [DIR001, DIR002, POS001, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the dominant order of A36 and A37 reversed in the stretch following A32, with A33 now following the second activity of the pair: the earlier window is consistent with a dominant flow A32 → A37 → A36 → A33, the later window with A32 → A36 → A37 → A33. The successor shares of A36 and A37 reversed together, the lags of both activities after A32 swapped almost exactly, and the direct A36→A37 link became near-universal while its observed pairs rose from 33 to 56 traces; all five items concern the same four activities, agree in direction, and each difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

The screening context reinforces this: seven control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.5 expected by chance in an unchanged process, so sampling alone is unlikely to explain the set. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that this is a shift in how often two alternative orders are taken rather than an exchange of the two activities' places — both fit the data equally. The rare-successor changes rest on only one or two transitions each and may be sampling noise. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG002, LAG003]

Other differences: the A07/A14 change — higher participation of A07, later and more variable relative positions, and A14 following A07 more closely — is a participation and closeness change separate from the A32–A36–A37–A33 reordering. [POS001, LAG001]
