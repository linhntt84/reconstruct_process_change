### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the previously optional segment A19 → A25 → A20 became mandatory: in the earlier window the three activities were executed together in about 47 in 100 cases and skipped together in about 52 in 100, while in the later window they execute in virtually every case, once per case and in the same order (A19 before A25 before A20). The change is shown directly and consistently by occurrence and relation indicators with substantial support in both windows (94–96 versus 199 traces per window), the always-present activities A14, A15, A16, A26 and A41 now co-occur with the segment in about 995 in 1000 traces, and A41 now feeds into it almost exclusively via A19. With 62 qualifying items against roughly 0.84 expected for an unchanged process, sampling variation cannot explain the pattern as a whole. The evidence cannot show how the skip branch disappeared — for example whether a routing option was removed or the two windows simply contain a different mix of cases — and the samples are finite (200 traces each). [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL018, REL019, REL022, REL009, REL021, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

Other differences: A06 became much more frequent (about 24 to about 73 in 100 traces), appears early in the trace, and now channels flow from A15 to A16 (about 40 in 100 of A15's outgoing transitions go to A06, and about 88 in 100 of A06's go to A16, usually adjacent); A08 declined from about 20 to about 8 in 100 traces. Whether these belong to the same underlying change cannot be determined from the evidence. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, DIR001, DIR003, LAG002, LAG009]
