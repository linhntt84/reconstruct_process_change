- In the earlier window, the activities A21, A22, A23, A24 and A25 were rare, each appearing in about 19 in 100 traces (37–41 of 200) and essentially never repeated within a trace; in the later window each appears in about 77 in 100 traces (153–155 of 200). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two alternative paths exchanged how often they are taken. Earlier, about 82 in 100 traces ran the block A20 → A29 → A26/A27/A28 → A30 (activities A26–A30) and about 19 in 100 ran A20 → A24 → A21/A22/A23 → A25 (activities A21–A25); in the later window the proportions are roughly reversed, with the A21–A25 block in about 77 in 100 traces — typically after A14 and alongside A34 — and the A26–A30 block in about 24 in 100. [OCC001, OCC006, DIR001, REL011, REL016, REL021, REL026, REL040, REL075]

This reads as a change in how often existing alternative paths are taken rather than activities being added, removed or reordered: both blocks are observed in substantial shares in both windows (at least about 19 in 100), the blocks are mutually exclusive before and after, and each block's internal order is unchanged. The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process would produce — 103 control-flow items against about 1.15 expected by chance. [REL035, REL044, REL058, REL073]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out: a single decision point whose branch shares flipped and two independent changes to each block's occurrence conditions would look the same here; the evidence locates the change in the frequencies and stable structures of the two blocks, but not in the control point that selects between them. [DIR001, REL035]

Other differences:
- A34 now interleaves with or is repeated around the A21–A25 block in about 40 in 100 traces, up from about 5 in 100 — a relational change beyond the share flip itself. [REL040, REL049, REL057, REL064, REL070]
- Positional gaps between block activities and A20 or A34 shifted by a few tenths of a step (e.g., A20→A22 from mostly 3–4 steps to 2–4; A22→A34 slightly longer), and A29's position spread widened as its participation fell. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, POS001]
