### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured so that the block A36, A37, A42, A43, A44 — joined by A27, previously its alternative — became mandatory, executed in every later-window trace after appearing in only about half of the earlier ones, and that the flow around it was re-ordered: A47, A02, A19/A20 and A25 now run before the block, A26 runs after it (previously before it), A28 follows A44 rather than A25, and A31 follows A27 rather than the block. The screening expected about 1.4 qualifying items if nothing had changed, while 145 control-flow items qualified — more than a hundred times that expectation — so chance sampling cannot account for the ensemble. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation counts on full support (104–107 of 200 traces rising to 200 of 200), co-occurrence rising to 1.0 with stable order shares of about 98–99 in 100, and the re-orderings visible directly in successor and adjacency evidence (A26→A27, A47→A02, A25→A28 disappearing, A44→A28 appearing). [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, REL042, REL028, REL035, DIR005, DIR006, DIR012, LAG009, LAG016]

Other differences: A04, A06 and A10 were executed in about 16–20 in 100 traces before and about 6–8 in 100 after; A53 now always closes the trace; the outgoing profiles of A08 and A10 changed on small support. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS016, DIR002, DIR003]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in which process variant is executed — the earlier traces without the block may simply no longer occur, rather than a single process having been made mandatory — and two aggregate windows cannot show whether the transition was abrupt or gradual. Many items describe the same underlying restructuring (for example the occurrence, relation and direction items on A36–A44), so they are not independent confirmations, and direction shares are computed over outgoing transitions rather than cases.
