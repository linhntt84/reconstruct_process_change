### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained A21 and A23, and cases split after A21 into two mutually exclusive routes: about 52 in 100 traces ran A21 → A22 → A23, while about 48 in 100 ran A21 → A27/A28/A33 → A23, with A27, A28 and A33 always occurring together and A22 never sharing a trace with any of the three. [OCC001, OCC002, REL006, REL012, POS001]
- In the later window, the A22 route dominates: A22 appears in about 995 of 1000 traces (199 of 200), always between A21 and A23, whereas A27, A28 and A33 each occur in only 1 of 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, REL005]
- A21's outgoing transitions shifted from a four-way split (A22 about 52%, A33 about 18%, A28 about 16%, A27 about 14%) to almost exclusively A22 (about 99.5%). [DIR001]
- The three-activity route collapsed: A27, A28 and A33 co-occurred in about 48 in 100 earlier traces, with both orders for every pair (so no fixed sequence among them), while in the later window these pairs co-occur in essentially no trace (neither in about 995 of 1000). [REL012, REL013, REL014]
- The distance from A21 to A23 shrank accordingly: before, A23 followed A21 two steps later in about 52% of traces and four steps later in about 48%; after, the two-step gap holds in about 99.5% of traces, and A23 sits slightly earlier in the trace (mean relative position about 0.55 versus about 0.53). [LAG001, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative route through A27, A28 and A33 between A21 and A23 was (nearly) abandoned: earlier, roughly half of the cases executed A27, A28 and A33 together, in varying order, between A21 and A23 while the other half went directly A21 → A22 → A23; in the later window essentially all cases take A21 → A22 → A23, A22 became nearly mandatory (up from about 52 in 100 traces to about 199 in 200), and A27, A28 and A33 are each observed in only 1 of 200 traces. Because these three activities are nearly absent in the later window, this is a route being skipped or removed rather than a re-weighting between still-used alternatives. The signs are direct and consistent across participation, routing, co-occurrence and lag items, and the number of flagged control-flow items (34 against roughly 0.45 expected without a change) rules out chance sampling as an explanation for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, REL001, REL005, REL012, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish complete removal of the A27/A28/A33 route from a route that still exists but is taken extremely rarely — one later trace still shows A21 → A28 → A27 → A33 → A23 — and the internal ordering of that remaining route rests on that single trace; several items also describe the same underlying traces and are not independent confirmations. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Other differences: none beyond this route change; A23's slight positional shift and the later-window routing, position and lag profiles of A27, A28 and A33 (each based on one trace) are downstream reflections of the same behaviour rather than separate differences. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]
