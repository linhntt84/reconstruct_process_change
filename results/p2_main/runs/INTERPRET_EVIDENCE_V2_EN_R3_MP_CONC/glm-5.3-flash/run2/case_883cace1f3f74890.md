### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 were not observed in any of the 200 traces. In the later window, A19 runs in about 30 in 100 traces, A20 in about 41 in 100 and A25 in about 30 in 100, each essentially exactly once per trace when present, and always around the middle of the trace (mean position ≈ 0.51 of the trace length). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- The three activities are mutually exclusive: A19 and A20 never share a trace, and the other pairs co-occur in only about 1 in 200 traces. Since their shares sum to roughly the whole window, virtually every later-window trace executes exactly one of them. [REL001, REL002, REL006, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Routing around this point changed: earlier, about 99 in 100 of A27's outgoing transitions went to A28, and about 99 in 100 of A28's went to A29. In the later window, A27 goes to A28 in about 44 in 100 transitions and to A19, A20 or A25 in about 55 in 100, while A28 goes to A29 in about 55 in 100 and to A19, A20 or A25 in about 43 in 100. [DIR004, DIR005]
- The new activities lead onward: each of A19, A20 and A25 goes to A28 in about 53–56 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and directly to A29 in about 42–46 in 100. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- Ordering is consistent: A27 precedes the new activity in essentially every trace containing both, and the new activity precedes A29 in essentially every such trace; relative to A28 both orders occur (for example, A19 before A28 in about 16 in 100 traces and after A28 in about 13 in 100). - Distances grew by about one step: A27 followed by A28 within one step fell from 99 in 100 to 44 in 100 (two steps now 55 in 100); A28 followed by A29 within one step fell from 99 in 100 to 56 in 100 (two steps 43 in 100); A27 followed by A29 moved from two steps (99 in 100) to three steps (97 in 100). - Other differences: A10's participation rose from about 5 to about 14 in 100 traces and it no longer recurs within a trace; A27's median position shifted slightly earlier and A29's slightly later, consistent with the extra mid-trace event. [OCC001, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new mandatory step was inserted into the existing sequence between A27 and A29: every trace now executes exactly one of the three previously unobserved activities A19, A20 or A25 as mutually exclusive alternatives, placed either between A27 and A28 (about 55 in 100 traces) or between A28 and A29 (about 43 in 100 traces), whereas earlier the flow ran directly from A27 through A28 to A29 and none of the three appeared. The signs are direct and mutually consistent — appearance from zero participation to 30–42 in 100 traces with single executions, new edges from A27 and A28 into the new activities and from them onward to A28 or A29, stable ordering around them, and the one-step growth of the A27–A29 distance in about 97 in 100 traces — and every item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. The screening context supports treating the signals as a set: 29 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.54 expected for the 109 tested units if nothing had changed, far beyond what an unchanged process produces, though many items describe the same underlying insertion and are not independent confirmations. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The evidence cannot rule out that the three activities are separate optional insertions that only happen to be mutually exclusive rather than a single choice point, and the per-trace insertion point is inferred from ordering and distance distributions rather than observed directly; behaviour outside the tested units is not covered.
