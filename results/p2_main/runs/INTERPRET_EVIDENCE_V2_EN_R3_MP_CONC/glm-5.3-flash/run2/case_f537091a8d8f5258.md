### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the closing block of the process was reordered: A05 and A06, which previously ran at the end of the case after A09 in either mutual order, now run in the middle — directly after A04, or directly after A01 — and before A07, in the fixed order A05 then A06; consequently A07 and especially A09 now execute after them, with A09 typically ending the trace. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL005, DIR002, DIR004, DIR006, POS002, POS005, LAG006]

The order flips are shown directly by the relation items, and the position shifts, gap changes and successor redistributions at A04 and A06 all agree with one coherent rearrangement; participation and co-occurrence stayed flat, so this is a reordering rather than activities becoming optional or mandatory. With 28 control-flow items qualifying against roughly 0.26 expected by chance for the 52 tested units, sampling noise cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [REL002, REL003, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, DIR002, DIR004, LAG006, LAG008]

Limitations: the evidence shows the two blocks swapped order but cannot tell whether A05/A06 were pulled earlier or A07/A09 pushed later; A09's post-change successor profile rests on only about 12 observed outgoing transitions; and the new order is dominant rather than universal (A06 precedes A07 in about 41 of 100 co-occurring traces, with about 14 in the reverse order). [DIR006, REL004, DIR005]

Other differences: slightly more back-and-forth between A01 and A04 — A04 now returns to A01 in about 23 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (before about 10 in 100), and A01→A04 gaps spread from mostly 1 step to 1–2 steps. [DIR002, LAG001]
