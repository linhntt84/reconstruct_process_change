### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A01 was not observed at all (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it appears in every trace, exactly once per case on average, at an early event position (median about one-fifth into the trace). [OCC001, POS001]
- In the later window, A01's observed outgoing transitions go mainly to A02 (about 30 in 100) and A12 (about 27 in 100), with smaller shares to A04, A03 and A05; in the earlier window A01 had no observed outgoing transitions. [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was added to the process, or executed for the first time, and is now performed exactly once per case at an early position, between the A02/A06/A12 cluster and the downstream activities A04 and A05: A02, A06 and A12 now route roughly a quarter to a third of their observed outgoing transitions to A01, A01 leads mainly back to A02 and A12 or onward to A04 and A05, the pre-existing activity pairs are spaced one or more steps further apart, and A05 and A12 sit at later positions in the trace. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL002, REL003, LAG004, LAG006, POS001, POS002]

The support is direct and consistent: 23 evidence items qualified against roughly 0.23 expected for an unchanged process (about 100 times the chance expectation), and the picture is shown directly rather than inferred — A01's participation moved from none of 200 traces to all 200, new transitions appear on both the incoming and outgoing side of A01, co-occurrence with A02, A04, A05 and A12 went from never to (near) always, and every lag distribution between the pre-existing pairs shifted the same way. Many of these items describe the same underlying behaviour and are not independent confirmations, but each perspective agrees. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL005, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether A01 is a genuinely new step or an existing step that was simply never executed in the earlier window, and A01's exact place within the repeating A02/A12/A06 cycle remains undetermined because both orders occur with A02 and A12; all position statements concern event positions, not clock time. [REL001, REL005, DIR002, DIR004]

Other differences: A06 now appears in about 59 in 100 traces versus about 48 in 100 earlier; the evidence does not show whether this follows from the rerouting through A01 or is an independent shift in how often that path is taken. [REL004, DIR003]
