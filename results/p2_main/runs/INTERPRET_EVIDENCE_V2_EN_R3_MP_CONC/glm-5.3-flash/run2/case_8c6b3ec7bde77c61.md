### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in the segment between A48 and A33. In the earlier window, virtually every case ran all five activities A20, A21, A30, A31 and A32 there, in mixed order, between A48 and A33; in the later window that five-activity block is essentially gone (1–3 traces of 200 each) and in nearly every case a single activity drawn from A19, A22, A23, A24 and A25 runs in its place, preceded by A48 and followed by A33. Supporting signs: the participation collapse and appearance are large and well-supported (199 → 1–3 and 0–3 → 32–49 traces out of 200); A48's outgoing transitions rerouted from the old block to the new set; the new activities connect onward to A33; and the A48–A33 distance collapsed to about two steps. The screening scale corroborates that chance cannot explain the pattern as a whole: 158 control-flow items qualified where about 1.2 would be expected without change. Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish removal of the block from it being skipped in almost all executions (it still appears in 1–3 traces), and it cannot rule out that the two windows sample a shifted mix of coexisting case types rather than a modified process. Other differences: A04's increased participation and its new, nearly fixed position directly before A16 in about one third of later traces (with A15 leading to A04 more often) behave as a change distinct from the A48–A33 segment and are not part of the main hypothesis.
