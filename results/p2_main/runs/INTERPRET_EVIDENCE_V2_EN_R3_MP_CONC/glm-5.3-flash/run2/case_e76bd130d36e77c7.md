### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the five-activity block A35, A36, A41, A42, A43 moved from the segment between A25 and A27/A26 to the segment after A27/A26 and before A30, with A27 and A26 taking earlier positions. In the earlier window A25 fed the five directly, the five fed A27, and A26 closed to A30; in the later window A25 leads almost immediately to A27, A26 precedes the five, and the five now lead to A30. Participation itself did not change: all seven activities occur in essentially every trace in both windows, so this is a reordering of the same activities, not activities appearing, disappearing or becoming optional. [DIR001, DIR002, REL001, POS001, LAG001]

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: the order reversals are relational and near-complete in both directions with co-occurrence unchanged, the successor profiles of all seven sources change in the matching way, the position shifts agree in direction for every affected activity, and the screening produced 43 qualifying control-flow items where about 0.8 would be expected without a change — far beyond what chance produces. [REL006, DIR003, POS003, screening]

Limitations: some LAG items rest on only 1–2 traces in one window and are weak on their own, though the order reversals in REL (about 200 traces per window) carry the same conclusion independently; the indicators are overlapping views of one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations; and the evidence cannot distinguish a reordering within one process definition from a complete switch to a different variant that executes the same activities in the new order. The exact position of A26 relative to A27 in the later window is also not pinned down. [LAG003, LAG013]
