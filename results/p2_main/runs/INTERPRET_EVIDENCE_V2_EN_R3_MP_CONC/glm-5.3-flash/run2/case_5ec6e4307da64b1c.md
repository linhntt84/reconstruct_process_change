### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, none of the activities A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 appears at all (0 of 200 traces). In the later window each occurs in roughly 17–23 in 100 traces (A19 and A25 about 23, A20 about 21, A26 about 17, A27 about 18), never more than once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- The five behave as alternatives: in the later window any two of them appear together in only about 0–1 in 100 traces, one or the other appears in about 38–46 in 100, and their five shares together amount to about one such activity per trace. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL007, REL008, REL012, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- Before, A29 passed directly to A30 in about 98 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and A30 passed directly to A31 in about 96 in 100. In the later window, A29 sends about 51 in 100 transitions to one of the five new activities (about 8–12 to each) and about 48 to A30, while A30 sends about 47 in 100 to a new activity and about 51 to A31. [DIR006, DIR007]
- The new activities in turn lead almost exclusively to A30 or A31 — about 45–59 in 100 of their outgoing transitions go to A30 and about 38–52 to A31, with only tiny shares elsewhere. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- Ordering: a new activity co-occurs with A29 in about 21–23 in 100 traces and then virtually always follows it, and co-occurs with A31 in about 21–23 in 100 traces and then virtually always precedes it; towards A30 both orders occur in similar shares (about 10–12 in 100 each way), so the new step sits before A30 in some traces and after it in others. [REL004, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL013, REL014, REL015]
- The A29–A31 stretch became longer in event steps: the A29→A30 gap went from 1 step in about 99 in 100 cases to about 47 at 1 step and 50 at 2 steps; A30→A31 from about 97 at 1 step to about 51 at 1 and 46 at 2; and A29→A31 from 2 steps (95 in 100) to 3 steps (94 in 100). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- The new activities sit near the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.51–0.52), and A31 now occurs slightly later than before (mean about 0.54 to 0.56). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new step was added inside the A29–A31 segment: in the earlier window the segment ran directly A29 → A30 → A31 with none of the five activities present, while in the later window nearly every trace contains exactly one of A19, A20, A25, A26 or A27 (together about one per trace, co-occurring in only about 0–1 in 100), placed between A29 and A30 in about half of the traces and between A30 and A31 in the other half. The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process would produce: 35 control-flow items qualified where about 0.6 were expected by chance, and every item's difference exceeded about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL001, REL007, DIR006, DIR007, LAG002]

The hypothesis is direct and internally consistent: the five activities rise from zero participation to 17–23 in 100 traces; new edges lead from A29 and from A30 into them and from them onward to A30 and A31; they virtually always follow A29 and precede A31, with a mixed order towards A30 that matches the two positions where the new step appears; the A29→A30, A30→A31 and A29→A31 step gaps lengthened accordingly; and A29, A30 and A31 themselves still occur in essentially every trace in both windows, so the later behaviour is additive rather than a replacement of existing steps. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG003, POS005]

Limitations: the position of the new step in an individual trace is inferred from aggregate transition shares rather than traced case by case; with about 203 participations across 200 later traces, the evidence cannot establish that the step is strictly mandatory in every trace. An important competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that these activities existed before at a very low rate that the earlier 200-trace window failed to sample, although the coherent relational structure across indicators makes a rare-variant artefact unlikely. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL004, REL006]

Other differences: A29 and A30 each show a few additional outgoing edges with very small shares (about 0.5 in 100 transitions each, for example to A04, A24, A32 and A28), too small to characterise further. [DIR006, DIR007]
