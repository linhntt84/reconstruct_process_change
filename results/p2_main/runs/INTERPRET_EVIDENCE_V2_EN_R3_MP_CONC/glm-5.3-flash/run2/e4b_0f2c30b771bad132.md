### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that resource allocation changed in the later window: A11, previously handled by about 21 different resources with no dominant one, is now assigned exclusively to User_123, and assignments for A03 and A21 concentrated on User_3, whose share roughly doubled for A03 (about 8 to 19 in 100) and nearly tripled for A21 (about 11 to 30 in 100), while several earlier contributors were reduced or replaced by a few new resources with small shares. [ASSIGN001, ASSIGN002, ASSIGN003, CONC001]

This rests on direct allocation measurements rather than indirect signals: all four resource items show differences larger than in all but about 1 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data, and the family-level screening counts far exceed what an unchanged process produces (4 resource items against about 0.006 expected; 1 time item against about 0.017). The two A11 items agree but come from the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations; A03 and A21 are distinct activities showing the same direction. No control-flow item qualified (0 against about 0.17 expected), so no structural difference is detected at these thresholds. [ASSIGN001, ASSIGN002, ASSIGN003, CONC001, TWAIT001]

Limitations: the resource codes are anonymous, so no roles or teams can be inferred; the evidence cannot show whether the A11 concentration and the A03/A21 shifts are one coordinated reallocation or independent shifts, and it cannot rule out that a different case mix in the later window, rather than a changed allocation itself, produced these assignment profiles. [ASSIGN001, ASSIGN002, ASSIGN003, CONC001]

Other differences: the elapsed time from the completion of A04 to the completion of A01 shortened markedly (median about 70 hours to about 12.5 hours; mean about 53 to about 23 hours) — a timing change that stands as a hypothesis in its own right. Because this measures elapsed time between consecutive completions, it can include waiting or intervening work, and the evidence cannot separate those components or establish whether the shortening is connected to the reallocation.
