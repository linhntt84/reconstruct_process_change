- The block A21–A25 collapsed: in the earlier window each of these five activities ran in about 85 in 100 traces and they typically occurred together in the same trace (co-occurrence about 84–85 in 100, usually after A20); in the later window each appears in only about 18–19 in 100 traces, and in about 80 in 100 traces none of them appears at all. - A02 became more frequent and now sits on a short segment: its participation rose from about 5 to about 24 in 100 traces; about 25 in 100 of A36's outgoing transitions now lead to A02 (about 1 in 100 earlier), A02 leads mainly to A37 (about 82 in 100 of its transitions versus about 20 earlier, on 50 versus 10 observed transitions), and A02 is followed by A37 within one step in essentially all later observations (41 of 41; 3 earlier). - The event positions of A21 and A24 moved only slightly earlier (median shift about 0.03 of a position) with somewhat more spread, so the dominant change for them is frequency, not placement. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the two alternative continuations after A20 swapped how often they are taken: in the later window, about 82 in 100 traces continue from A20 into the block A26–A30 (with A20 directly before them), whereas earlier about 85 in 100 traces continued into the block A21–A25, which now appears in only about 18–19 in 100 traces. The flip is observed directly at the branch point (A20's successor shares swapped, with A20 observed in all 200 traces of each window) and is consistent across the participation of all ten activities, the collapse of co-occurrence within A21–A25, the rise of co-occurrence within A26–A30, and the near-perfect exclusivity between the blocks in both windows. Screening qualified 120 control-flow items where about 1.3 would be expected by chance without a change, so the pattern as a whole is not what a stable process produces. The evidence cannot show whether the branch choice itself changed or only the mix of cases entering the branch, and the windows are finite samples. [DIR003, OCC002, OCC007, REL011, REL016, REL021, REL071, REL025]

Other differences: A02, A36 and A37 show a secondary change — A02's participation rose from about 5 to about 24 in 100 traces and it now forms the segment A36 → A02 → A37 — but this rests on fewer observations (10–50 outgoing transitions, 3–41 lag pairs) than the main flip and is therefore less firmly established. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR005, LAG001, LAG006]
