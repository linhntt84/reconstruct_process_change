### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, two segments of the process became optional and are frequently skipped. The block A19 → A25 → A20 between A41 and A26, previously executed in every trace, is omitted in about 46 in 100 later cases, with A41 then connecting directly to A26; and A06 between A14 and A15, previously present in about 71 in 100 traces, now appears in about 26 in 100, with A14 then connecting straight to A15. The support is direct and consistent: relational items show the block's co-occurrence with the rest of the process falling from 100 to about 53 in 100 while A14, A15, A26 and A41 continue in every trace; routing items show the direct edges A41 → A26 and A14 → A15 emerging; and distance items show the corresponding gaps shrinking. The screening comparison supports that the pattern as a whole is not a sampling artefact: 59 control-flow items qualified where fewer than 1 would be expected without a change. A limitation is that the two omissions need not have a single cause, since the skipped sets do not coincide — A19 runs without A06 in about 39 in 100 later traces. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL003, REL013, REL016, REL018, DIR005, DIR008, LAG004, LAG008]

Other differences: the increased occurrence of A04, A08 and A10 described above is not part of this hypothesis. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of two coexisting process variants — a complete path and a shortened path — rather than a modification of one process; the later window looks like a blend of full and shortened executions. [REL016, DIR005, DIR008, LAG008]
