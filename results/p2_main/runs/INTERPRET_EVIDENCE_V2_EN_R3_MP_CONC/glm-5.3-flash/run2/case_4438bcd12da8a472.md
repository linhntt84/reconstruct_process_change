### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of activities A14, A20 and A15, executed between A36 and A21, is now part of virtually every case: in the earlier window about 47 in 100 cases ran A36 → A14 → A20 → A15 → A21 while the remaining ~53 in 100 went from A36 directly to A21, whereas in the later window about 99.5 in 100 cases run the full sequence and the direct A36→A21 step survives in only about 1 in 200 cases. The order within the block is the same in both windows; what changed is that the block is no longer bypassed. The change is shown directly by the participation jump of A14, A15 and A20 (94 to 199 of 200 traces), the rerouting of A36's outgoing transitions, the A36–A21 distance moving from adjacency to four positions, and A21's near-universal co-occurrence with the block; 22 qualifying items against roughly 0.41 expected for an unchanged process makes chance sampling implausible as a whole. Limitations: with 200 traces per window a rarely used direct A36→A21 step cannot be excluded, and the evidence cannot separate a routing change from a shift in the mix of cases reaching each path. Other differences: The relation changes involving A37 and A40 largely follow from A14 now being present in almost every case rather than from independent shifts in their own frequencies: A37 was present throughout in both windows but now always co-occurs with A14, and its order relative to A14 became less fixed (interleaved or repeated in about 46 in 100 later cases versus about 17 in 100 earlier); A40's frequency is stable at about 6 in 10 cases, but it no longer appears in cases without A14.
