- In the earlier window, A25, A26 and A31 were present in every trace but each occurred about 3.4 times per trace, appearing more than once in about 75 in 100 traces. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the repetition involving A25, A26 and A31 was largely removed: in the earlier window these activities ran roughly 3–4 times per trace, with A31 frequently returning to A25 and the two interleaved; in the later window each runs once per trace in a fixed sequence, with A25 preceding A31 by about two positions and A31 then leading to A20 instead of back to A25. A26 shows the same drop from repeated to single execution, though no relational evidence directly places it in the sequence. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001, LAG001, LAG002]

Supporting signs: nine evidence items qualified against an expectation of only 0.3 items without change, so chance alone cannot explain the set; the relational and directional items show the ordering and successor change directly, and all items point consistently to one underlying difference, each with empirical strength of about 0.995 and profiles resting on all 200 traces in each window. [REL001, LAG001, LAG002, DIR001, OCC001]

Limitations: the indicators overlap and likely describe the same underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations; A26's role rests only on its occurrence profile; and the change is not complete — residual repeats (about 1 in 200 traces) and a residual A31-to-A25 share (about 4 in 100 transitions) remain, so the evidence cannot exclude that a small loop persists. [OCC001, DIR001]
