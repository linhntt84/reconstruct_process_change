- Participation dropped sharply for six activities. Before, A22 and each of A31, A32, A37, A38 and A39 ran in every trace; in the later window A22 runs in about 46 in 100 traces and each of the five others in about 55 in 100, while A20, A21, A23 and A26 still run in all traces. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the case branches after A21: in about 55 in 100 cases the group A31, A32, A37, A38, A39 executes in a variable order between A21 and A26, while in about 45 in 100 cases A22 executes in that slot instead, making the group and A22 mutually exclusive alternatives. Before, every trace ran the fixed chain A20 → group → A23 → A21 → A22 → A26; in the later window A20 leads directly to A23, A23 and A21 run earlier, and the group runs later than before. [DIR001, DIR002, REL007, REL008, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL020, REL025, OCC001, OCC002, POS004, POS006]

The hypothesis is shown directly rather than inferred from frequencies alone: the direction items capture the successor changes at A20, A21 and the group's exit, the relation items capture the order flips and the near-zero co-occurrence between A22 and each group activity, and the occurrence and position items match the same relocation and optionality. Screening qualified 72 items where about 0.72 would be expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled traces, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [DIR001, DIR002, REL015, REL020, OCC001, POS006]

Limitations: the branch split (about 45 versus 55 in 100) is an estimate from 200 traces per window and carries sampling error; the lag-based items add little because their later-window profiles rest on a single observation each; and the evidence cannot rule out that the later window reflects a shifted mix of two coexisting case types rather than one modified process, since only aggregate behaviour is observed. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]
