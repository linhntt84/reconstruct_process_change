### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the relative order of A08 and the A10/A11 step is reversed around the A06/A07 block: in the earlier window the flow typically ran A08 → A10 or A11 → A06 or A07, while in the later window it typically runs A10 or A11 → A08 → A06 or A07. This is shown directly by the successor changes (A10 and A11 now lead only to A08, whereas A08 now leads mainly to A06/A07 instead of A10/A11) and corroborated by the distance shifts, which move in exactly the compensating directions (direct pairs became two-apart, two-apart pairs became direct). With 20 qualifying items against roughly 0.24 expected by chance for the 48 tested units, the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces.
