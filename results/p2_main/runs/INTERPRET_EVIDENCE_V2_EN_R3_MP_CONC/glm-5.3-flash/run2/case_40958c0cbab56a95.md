### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural replacement in the segment between A44 and A29: earlier, every trace executed A26, A27 and A28 together (in no fixed order) after A44 and before A29, while later a trace executes exactly one of A19, A20 or A21 there — an exclusive choice with roughly 30/33/38 shares — so the path from A44 to A29 now passes through a single activity instead of three. This is shown directly by the participation shift (200 vs 3 supporting traces for the old block; 2–4 vs 60–75 for the new trio), the successor change at A44 observed on about 200 outgoing edges in each window, relation items with full 200-trace support on both sides, and the shortened A44-to-A29 gap (195 and 200 observations). With 81 qualifying items against roughly 1 expected without change in the control-flow family, chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, REL018, REL025, REL032, REL038, REL039, REL043, REL044, REL047, REL048, LAG009]

Limitations: the two windows are finite samples, so the evidence cannot rule out that they capture different case populations or variants rather than a modification of one process; the earlier behaviour of A19, A20 and A21 rests on only 2–4 traces, and about 3 later traces still contain A26–A28, so complete removal of the old block is not established. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]

Other differences: A10 participation rose from about 13.5 to 22.5 in 100 traces; A29's mean event position moved slightly earlier (about 0.44 to 0.40) and A44's slightly later (about 0.31 to 0.33), which are position shifts and not evidence of clock-time moves; most lag items about the old block's internal distances rest on only 1–3 later observations and should be treated as weak.
