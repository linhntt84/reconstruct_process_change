### Observed differences

- The five activities A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 changed where they run. In the earlier window they sat late in traces (mean position about 0.60 of the trace length) and always came after A23 and A24; in the later window they sit early (mean position about 0.32) and always come before A23 and A24. Their participation stayed similar, at roughly 15–25 in 100 traces in both windows, so they did not become more or less frequent — their placement changed. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

- The exits of these five activities were redirected. Before, each split its outgoing transitions between A25 (about 35–61%) and A29 (about 39–65%); in the later window each leads to A23 in about 97–100% of observed transitions. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

- A42 switched from a direct predecessor of A23 to a predecessor of this group: before it led to A23 in 100% of exits at a one-step distance; in the later window about 99% of its exits go to A14, A15, A20, A21 or A22, and A23 follows two steps later in 99% of traces. [DIR008, LAG010]

- The tail around A24 and A25 became a near-fixed sequence. A24 previously spread its exits over six activities (A25 about 53%, the rest over the block members); in the later window 99.5% go to A25. A25 previously split exits between A29 (about 48%) and the block members (about 52%); now 99.5% go to A29. Both hops are one step apart in nearly all traces, and A24 reaches A29 in two steps (99%) instead of three. [DIR006, DIR007, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]

- The old adjacencies have practically disappeared: ordered pairs that placed A20 or A22 directly after A24, or leading into A29 within one to two steps, were seen in 47–49 traces before and in only 1 trace after. [LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

- A23 and A24 kept their positions (mean position about 0.33 and about 0.56–0.58 in both windows), so the reference points around which the five activities moved stayed put. [POS006, POS007]

- A12 now appears in about 33 in 100 traces, up from about 19 in 100, and its distance to A23 grew from 2 to 3 steps; A11 reaches A23 in 4 steps in almost all traces, where before the distance mixed 3 and 5 steps. [OCC001, LAG001, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of five activities A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 was relocated: in the earlier window it executed after A23 and A24, with each member continuing to A25 or A29; in the later window it executes before A23, directly after A42, with each member leading into A23, while the tail A24→A25→A29 became a near-fixed sequence. This is a possibility supported by the evidence, not a confirmed modification. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]

The support is direct and consistent. The order flip relative to A23 and A24 is observed relationally in all eight tested pairs: before, A23 or A24 came first in every co-occurrence (15–25 in 100 traces); after, the block member comes first (18–25 in 100). The redirection is visible in the successor profiles of all five block members, of A42 and of A24/A25, all moving the same way. The block's participation was stable while its position shifted by about 0.29 of the trace for every member, and the five members' shares together sum to about one occurrence per trace in both windows, so this is a placement change rather than activities being added, removed or repeated differently. The screening also qualified 34 control-flow items where about 0.48 would be expected without a change, far beyond what a stable process produces. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Limitations: the 34 items are not independent confirmations — each block member contributes direction, position and relation items describing the same relocation, so the item count overstates the number of distinct differences. The evidence also cannot show how the five members are arranged among themselves (a fixed sequence or one-of-five alternatives per trace), and it cannot distinguish a relocation of the block within one process from a switch to a differently sequenced way of running cases, since both would produce these window-level aggregates. [DIR001, POS001, REL001, REL002, LAG010]

Other differences: A12 participates in more traces in the later window (about 33 in 100, up from about 19) and its distance to A23 grew from 2 to 3 steps, while A11's distance to A23 became almost uniformly 4 steps where before it mixed 3 and 5; these are consistent with an additional step having appeared on the paths into A23, but the evidence does not show which activity was inserted on those paths, and the rise in A12's participation is not explained by the block's relocation. [OCC001, LAG001, LAG002]
