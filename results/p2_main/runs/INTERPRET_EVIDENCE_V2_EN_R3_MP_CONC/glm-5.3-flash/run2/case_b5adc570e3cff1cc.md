### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the exclusive choice block A14/A15/A20 was relocated. Earlier it was entered from A22 or A23 in the late part of the case and flowed on to A23 or A27; later it is entered directly from A40 and flows exclusively into A21, so the block runs before A21 instead of after it. As its counterpart, the late segment lost its branches into the block and became a fixed, immediately sequential chain A22 → A23 → A27. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: every involved successor profile changed completely, all six order relations between the block and A21/A22 flipped from one exclusive direction to the other, the block's position shifted by about 0.28–0.29 of the trace while A21, A22 and A23 stayed in place, and the distance changes (A40–A21, A22–A23–A27) match the rerouting. The screening context supports this: 24 control-flow items qualified where about 0.39 would be expected without a change, and each item exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Limitations: positions and distances are based on event order, not clock time, so this is a change of sequence rather than of timing; and the indicators cannot distinguish a genuine relocation of the block from the old late placements being dropped while separate executions were added after A40, which would look identical here. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that the two windows sample two coexisting process variants whose relative frequencies shifted, although the exact one-directional order flips and all-or-nothing successor changes make plain sampling fluctuation unlikely. [REL001, REL003, REL005, DIR001]

Other differences: A10 and A11 still occur in every case but repeat less often (about 1.9 down to about 1.6 occurrences per case; recurrence in about 47 down to about 38 in 100 cases); their identical profiles suggest one underlying behaviour, and this repetition change is separate from the relocation hypothesis. [OCC001, OCC002]
