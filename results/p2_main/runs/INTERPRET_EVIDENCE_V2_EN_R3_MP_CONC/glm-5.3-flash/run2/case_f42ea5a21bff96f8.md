### Observed differences

- The largest change is a swap between two mutually exclusive activity blocks. In the earlier window, A21, A22 and A23 each took part in about 75 in 100 traces and ran together in about 75 in 100, while A24, A25 and A26 each took part in only about 26 in 100 (together in about 25 in 100). In the later window the shares flipped: A21–A23 each appear in about 25 in 100 traces (together in about 25 in 100), and A24–A26 each in about 76 in 100 (together in about 75 in 100). The two blocks exclude each other in both windows, co-occurring in at most about 2 in 100 traces. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the two alternative paths through A21–A23 and A24–A26 swapped how often they are taken: the A21–A23 block, previously executed together (in no fixed internal order) in about three quarters of the traces, was taken in only about a quarter, while the A24–A26 block rose from about a quarter to about three quarters. Both blocks were well observed in both windows (roughly 25–76 in 100 traces per activity), keep the same relations to A19/A20 upstream and A34/A35 downstream, and never run together, so this reads as a shift in the shares of two existing paths rather than activities being added, removed or repositioned. Supporting signs are the mirrored participation changes, the mirrored co-occurrence flips with A19/A20 and with A29/A30/A34/A35, and the redirect of A20's outgoing transitions. The screening context fits: 92 control-flow items qualified against about 1 expected for an unchanged process, so chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole. Limitations: the many relation items describe one underlying behaviour and are not independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot distinguish a changed routing share within individual cases from a changed mix of case types between the windows. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL013, REL016, REL019, REL022, REL025, REL049, REL055, DIR002]

Other differences: A06 became a frequent closing step — in the later window about half of the traces run A34 → A06 → A35, where before A34 led to A35 directly and A06 was a rare mid-trace activity with varied successors; A02 and A08 also became rarer. These changes are not part of the block-share hypothesis above. [OCC002, POS001, DIR001, LAG001, DIR005, LAG013, OCC001, OCC003]
