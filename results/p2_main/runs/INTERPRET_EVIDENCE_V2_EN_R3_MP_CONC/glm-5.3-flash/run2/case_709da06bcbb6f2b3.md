### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was not observed at all in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces). In the later window it runs in about 119 of 200 traces (roughly 60 in 100), exactly once per trace, and early in the case — its median relative position is about 0.17 of the trace length. [OCC001, POS001]
- Routing around A01 changed: in the later window about 34 in 100 of A03's observed outgoing transitions and about 39 in 100 of A10's go to A01 (neither edge was observable before), while A01's own transitions spread over A03 (about 32%), A10 (about 27%), A04 (about 23%) and A07 (about 18%). Shares towards the existing successors shrank, e.g. A10→A03 from about 55% to 29% and A03→A07 from about 29% to 12% of outgoing transitions. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- Ordering with A01: in the later window A01 always comes before A04 (about 32 in 100 traces) and before A07 (about 28 in 100), never the reverse, and A04/A07 no longer occur without A01 (previously each appeared alone in about 28-29 in 100 traces). With A03 and A10 both orders occur in similar shares (A01 first about 26 in 100, A03/A10 first about 34 in 100). In the earlier window A01 co-occurred with none of these activities. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]
- Downstream placement and spacing: A04 now occurs later in the trace (mean relative position about 0.31 vs 0.22) and A07 likewise (about 0.67 vs 0.57, median 0.75 vs 0.5); the A03→A04, A03→A07 and A10→A04 gaps now span three events in roughly 38-41 in 100 co-occurrences (previously only one or two), and A03/A10, previously always adjacent, are separated by one event in about 29-36 in 100 cases. [POS002, POS003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG005, LAG003, LAG004]
- Participation of the pre-existing activities barely moved (A03 and A10 about 56% to 60% of traces, A04 about 29% to 32%, A07 about 28% in both windows), so the difference concentrates on A01's appearance and the rerouting around it. [DIR002, DIR003, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 appears as a new step woven into the early stage of the process, between the A10/A03 pair and the A04/A07 stage. In the earlier window, traces containing A10 and A03 (about 56 in 100) ran with the two adjacent in either order and then optionally on to A04 and A07; in the later window those traces also execute A01, A03 and A10 frequently hand over to it, it leads back to A03/A10 and on to A04/A07, and it always precedes A04 and A07. The roughly one-event widening of the gaps into A04/A07 and the later positions of A04 and A07 fit an added step between these stages. All 17 qualifying items (against roughly 0.19 expected by chance if nothing had changed) describe this one difference consistently, though they largely re-express the same trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG005, POS002, POS003]

Limitations: the evidence does not pin down a single placement for A01 — it precedes A03/A10 in about 43% of their co-occurrences and follows them in about 57%, so its position in the early region is flexible rather than a fixed swap or move. Also, zero participation in the earlier window strictly means only that A01 was not observed there; the competing explanation that A01 existed as a very rare variant that the 200-trace sample missed cannot be fully ruled out. [REL001, REL004, OCC001]

Other differences: none beyond the routing-share redistributions already described (for example A10→A03 falling from about 55% to 29% of A10's outgoing transitions), which belong to the same integration of A01. [DIR002, DIR003]
