### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch in which A32, A33 and A38 are executed together between A26 and A28 is (nearly) no longer taken: in the later window almost every case runs A26 → A27 → A28, whereas before about half of the cases inserted that three-activity block in varying order. The screening produced 35 qualifying control-flow items against 0.6 expected without change, so the ensemble is far beyond what a stable process produces, and the change is shown directly and consistently on participation (A27 up from about 52 to about 199 of 200 cases; A32/A33/A38 down to 1 of 200 each), on routing (A26 → A27 in about 97 of 100 transitions, up from about 51) and on relations (A26–A27 co-occurrence up from about 52 to about 99.5 in 100; the trio's co-occurrence collapsing to near zero). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL012, REL013, REL014, LAG001]

Limitations: the trio was still observed in one later case, so the evidence cannot distinguish a fully removed branch from one that is now taken extremely rarely, and the block's internal order after the change rests on that single case; the competing explanation — that two 200-case windows merely sampled almost no trio-variant cases from an unchanged process — is made implausible by the screening result and the consistency across indicators but cannot be excluded with certainty. The A27 → A28 step itself is unchanged. [DIR002, LAG004, OCC002]

Other differences: A28's relative position moved slightly earlier (mean about 0.55 to about 0.53), which matches the average case becoming shorter rather than an independent change. [POS001]
