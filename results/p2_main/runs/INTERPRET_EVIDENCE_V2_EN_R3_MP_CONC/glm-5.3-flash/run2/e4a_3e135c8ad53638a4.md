### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window the behaviour is consistent with a rerouting in which A20 leads directly to A23, the segment A31/A32/A37 no longer runs between A20 and A23 but after A21, and there it alternates with A22 (roughly 46 vs 54 in 100 cases) before both alternatives close into A26; as a result, A22 is not executed in about 46 in 100 cases. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the near-universal A20→A23 adjacency with the step distance collapsing from four to one, the reversed order between A23 and each group member, the near-perfect mutual exclusivity of A22 and each group member, the split of A21's outgoing transitions, the group's exits moving from A23 to A26, and participation falling from every trace to about half for A22 and the group. Screening produced 47 qualifying control-flow items where about 0.5 would be expected without any change, so sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole.
