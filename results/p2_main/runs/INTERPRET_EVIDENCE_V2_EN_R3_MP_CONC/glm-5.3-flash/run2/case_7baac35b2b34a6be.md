### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the step between A43 and A28 was restructured: in the earlier window each trace passed from A43 through exactly one of five optional alternatives (A14, A17, A18, A19, A20) to A28, whereas in the later window that single optional step is replaced by a mandatory block of five activities (A15, A16, A25, A26, A27), each executed once per trace in no fixed internal order, with A43 before the block and A28 after it. The screening produced 92 qualifying control-flow items against fewer than one expected without a change, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole; the occurrence, routing and relation evidence all show the same replacement directly and consistently, and the position shifts of A28 and A43 match a longer intervening segment [DIR011, OCC002, OCC004, REL018, REL019, POS011, POS012].

Limitations: the data cannot distinguish genuinely new work from the old steps being recorded under different activity codes, although either way the executed behaviour changed from one-of-five optional alternatives to five co-occurring mandatory steps [OCC001, OCC002, REL010, REL029]. The internal sequencing rule of the new block also cannot be pinned down beyond "no fixed order", since all pairwise order splits stay near even [REL010, REL015, REL052].
