### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new step is executed between A38 and A23 in every case: A38 is now followed by exactly one of five alternative activities — A14, A15, A20, A21 or A22 — executed once, which is then followed by A23. The change is shown directly at both ends of the insertion (A38's transitions changed from A23 alone to the five alternatives, and each alternative leads on to A23), by the A38–A23 distance shifting from one step to two steps in all 200 traces, and by the ordering relations A38 before the new activity before A23. The pattern is internally consistent — the five never co-occur, their shares sum to one, and each occurs exactly once per trace — and the volume of qualifying evidence (34 control-flow items against roughly 0.43 expected by chance for the 87 tested units) far exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces. Limitations: the five activities were entirely absent from the earlier sample, and zero participation only means they were not observed there; the evidence therefore cannot fully rule out that these paths existed before but were missing from that particular 200-trace sample.
