### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the final part of the process was restructured. In the later window, a new activity A01 runs exactly once at a fixed final relative position in about 22 in 100 traces, is reached directly from A02, and never shares a trace with A06 or A15, so it behaves as a new alternative ending alongside the A06/A15 branch. A06, which previously always occupied that final position with no observed outgoing transitions, now appears earlier within traces and its observed transitions all lead to A15; the A02→A15 distance grew from mostly one step to mostly two, consistent with A06 now sitting between A02 and A15, although the evidence does not show what occupies that intermediate step. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR002, REL002, REL003, LAG002]

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: fourteen control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.23 expected by chance for the 46 tested units, and every item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled traces; A01's appearance is established directly by participation, position and the new A02→A01 transition (whose count, about 44, matches the number of A01 traces), and its exclusivity with A06 and A15 by the relation profiles. Limitations: the participation, position and relation items all describe one underlying difference — A01's appearance — and are not independent confirmations; positions and lags are event positions, not clock times; and the later A06 and A08 profiles rest on only 32 and 26 observed transitions. An important competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that A01 existed earlier at a low rate that the 200-trace before-window did not capture: 0 of 200 versus 44 of 200 traces makes pure sampling noise unlikely, but the design cannot prove the activity is new. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Other differences: A12 occurs in fewer traces and earlier within them, and is now observed as a direct successor of A02 (about 8 in 100 of A02's outgoing transitions, typically one step after), whereas before it typically followed A02 two to three steps later; A08 no longer leads mainly to A12 but to A09, A04 and A02. These changes fit the same restructuring of the final part of the process, but the evidence does not tie them directly to A01, so they are reported separately.
