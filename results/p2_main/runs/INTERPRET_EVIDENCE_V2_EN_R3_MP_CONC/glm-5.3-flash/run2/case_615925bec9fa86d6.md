### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the exclusive choice between A22 and the five-activity block A31, A32, A37, A38, A39 became a mandatory sequence. In the earlier window, a case ran either A22 (about 48 in 100 cases) or the whole block (about 52 in 100) after A21, with both branches leading to A26; in the later window, every case runs the entire block directly after A20 and before A23 and A21, and then A22 before A26. The signs are direct and consistent: co-occurrence of A22 with each block member went from 0 to all cases with a fixed relative order, the block's order against A21 and A23 reversed, the successor profiles of A20, A21 and the block members changed in the same direction, and participation of all six activities reached every case in both 200-case windows. In total 62 items qualified against roughly 0.67 expected for an unchanged process, and each difference exceeded about 199 of 200 random re-splits, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001-OCC006, REL015-REL019, REL008-REL012, REL020-REL024, DIR001-DIR007]

Limitations: the evidence contains no relation between A21 and A23, so their relative order in the new flow is not shown, and A20's entry into the internally unordered block is visible only through successor shares; whether the five block members run in a variable sequence or in parallel cannot be distinguished from these indicators, leaving the exact wiring between A20, the block, A23 and A21 a plausible reading rather than a fully mapped flow. [DIR001, DIR002, REL030-REL039]

Other differences: the spacing between block members tightened — A31 and A39 were one event step apart in about 52 of 100 observed pairs in the later window versus about 30 of 100 earlier, with the remaining gaps spread over two to four steps. [LAG001]
