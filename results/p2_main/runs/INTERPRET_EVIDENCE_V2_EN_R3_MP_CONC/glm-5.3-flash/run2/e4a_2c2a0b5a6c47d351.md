- In the earlier window, A39 (present in all 200 cases) was followed by exactly one of three alternative activities: A14 in about 70 of 200 cases, A15 in about 59, and A16 in about 71. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment after A39 was restructured: the earlier pattern, in which exactly one of A14, A15 or A16 executed (in roughly a third of cases each) and was followed by A24, was replaced in the later window by a mandatory block in which A21, A22 and A23 each execute exactly once per case in varying order before A24, while A14, A15 and A16 disappeared entirely. This is shown directly and consistently across occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators, and the volume of qualifying items (48 control-flow items versus about 0.5 expected by chance; 2 time items versus about 0.01) cannot be explained by random re-splits of an unchanged process. A limitation is that zero participation only means an activity was not observed in that window, and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different coexisting case types or variants rather than a change over time. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, REL019, REL021, LAG001]

Other differences: the two timing changes — the shorter A24→A31 interval and the shorter A32→A31 interval — are a change in timing in their own right and are not implied by the structural replacement above; they could also stem from causes unrelated to it. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
