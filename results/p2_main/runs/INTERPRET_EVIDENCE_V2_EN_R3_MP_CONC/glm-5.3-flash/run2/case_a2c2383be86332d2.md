### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A07 dropped out of the flow (was removed or became unreachable) and that its branch decision moved up to A08: earlier, A08 always led to A07, which then routed about 52/48 to A05 or A06 roughly twice per trace; in the later window A07 is unobserved in 200 traces, A08 connects directly to A05 and A06 with the same split, and every measured distance across the remaining cycle shortened by about one step, as expected when one activity leaves a repeating sequence. The rewiring is observed directly in A08's successor profile, A07's disappearance is total (200 of 200 traces earlier versus none later) rather than a sampling fluctuation, A05 and A06 participation is essentially unchanged, and the screening volume (35 qualifying items against roughly 0.19 expected without a change, each difference larger than in about 99.5 of 100 random re-splits) cannot be explained by chance. Limitations: many of the REL and LAG items restate the same non-occurrence and are not independent confirmations, and control-flow evidence alone cannot distinguish A07 being deleted from it merely becoming unreachable; zero participation strictly means only "not observed in this sample". A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a change in recording or an unobserved variant (for example, A07 events no longer being logged in the later window).
