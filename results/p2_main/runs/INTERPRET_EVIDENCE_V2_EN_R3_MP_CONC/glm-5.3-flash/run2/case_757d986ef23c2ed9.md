### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A20 was followed by A22 in essentially all of its occurrences (about 100 in 100 outgoing transitions); in the later window A20's outgoing transitions spread almost evenly over A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 (about 17–23 in 100 each), with A22 following A20 in only about 1 in 200 transitions. [DIR001]
- In the earlier window, A21 led to each of A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 in roughly equal shares (about 16–23 in 100 outgoing transitions each); in the later window A21 led to A25 in about 995 in 1000 transitions. [DIR002]
- In the earlier window, each of the five activities A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 led on to the other four and to A25, with A25 taking about 18–25 in 100 of their outgoing transitions; in the later window A25 has practically disappeared from these transitions (at most about 0.5 in 100) and A22 appears instead (about 17–22 in 100). [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]
- The order between A21/A22 and the five-activity group flipped completely: earlier, A21 preceded each of A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 in 100% of traces, and A22 likewise preceded each of them in 100%; in the later window A21 and A22 each followed these activities in all but about 1 in 200 traces. The activities still co-occur in essentially every trace in both windows, so participation itself did not change. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]
- Event positions moved accordingly: the five group activities now occur earlier in the trace (mean relative position about 0.60 before vs about 0.44 after), while A21 (about 0.52 → 0.65) and A22 (about 0.39 → 0.52) occur later. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007]
- Ordered pairs within five steps corroborate this: earlier, A21 was followed by each group activity within five steps in about 200 traces, and each group activity was followed by A25 within five steps in about 200 traces; in the later window each of these ordered pairs appears in only 1 trace. The later-window distance profiles rest on a single observation each, so only the near-disappearance of these pairs, not the exact step distances, is reliable. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a reordering of control flow around the five activities A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38. In the earlier window the behaviour is consistent with the chain A20 → A22 → A21 → {A30, A31, A36, A37, A38} → A25; in the later window it is consistent with A20 → {A30, A31, A36, A37, A38} → A22 → A21 → A25. That is, the five-activity group, which used to run after A21 and typically exited to A25, now runs directly after A20 and before A22 and A21, while A21 now proceeds almost immediately to A25. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the order flips between A21/A22 and every group activity are near-total while co-occurrence stays at 100% in both windows, so this is a sequencing change rather than activities appearing or disappearing; the successor changes at A20, A21 and the five group activities match the same reordering; and the position shifts plus the disappearance of the A21→group and group→A25 ordered pairs within five steps agree with it. Screening flagged 34 control-flow items against roughly 0.55 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]

Limitations: the evidence establishes only that the two groups exchanged their relative order; it cannot distinguish whether the five-activity group moved earlier or A22 and A21 moved later, and whether A22 still precedes A21 within individual later-window traces is inferred from position summaries rather than a direct relational item. The later-window distance profiles rest on one trace each, and the many items describe overlapping aspects of the same traces rather than independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the later window is dominated by a different variant that almost entirely replaces the earlier one, rather than the same cases being re-sequenced. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]

Other differences: none beyond this reordering; the routing shares among the five group activities themselves are broadly stable across the windows (most internal transitions about 16–23 in 100 in both), apart from the exit change from A25 to A22 described above.
