### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that repetition behaviour changed around A09, A03 and A14: in the later window A09 is repeated about twice as often per trace (roughly 6 versus 3 occurrences per containing trace), A03 and A14 stopped repeating (repeats in about 28 in 100 traces before, none after), and A15 and A13 now hand control almost entirely to A09 instead of the earlier spread over A01, A13 and A15. The occurrence changes are measured directly on 94–135 traces per window and agree with the direction, relation and lag indicators (A09's outgoing volume doubled, A09 and A13 became mostly interleaved, self-repeats of A09 and A15 now cluster at shorter gaps); the screening produced 29 qualifying items against roughly 0.43 expected without a change, so the pattern as a whole is not what sampling variability typically produces. The evidence cannot show whether the A09 increase and the A03/A14 stop-repeating are one coordinated change or two simultaneous ones, and it cannot rule out a shift in the mix of cases between the windows as an alternative to a modification of the process itself.
