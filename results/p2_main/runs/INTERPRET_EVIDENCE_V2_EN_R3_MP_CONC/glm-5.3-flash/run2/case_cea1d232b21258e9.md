### Observed differences

- A02 is the sharpest difference: in the earlier window about 48 in 100 traces contained it (97 of 200, roughly one occurrence per trace, with about 200 observed outgoing transitions towards ten activities, most often A13 at about 42% of them). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A02 was removed from the process and that the tightly coupled group A02–A03–A13–A14, which executed together in about half of the earlier traces, dissolved. In the later window A02 is not observed at all; A03 and A14 execute in only about 19 in 100 traces (down from about 48) and later within each trace; A13 continues at about 46 in 100 but now runs together with A10, whose participation roughly doubled (about 24 → 46 in 100) and moved earlier, with A07, A09 and A11 now following A10 where they previously followed A02 and A14. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS002, POS003, POS005, REL001, REL023, REL025, DIR007, DIR009, DIR010]

The signs are direct and consistent: occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators all show the same picture, the redirected edges indicate where the flow moved, and the screening flagged 60 qualifying items in the control-flow family against fewer than 1 expected without change, so the pattern as a whole is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine removal of A02 from it simply never being selected in the later window, nor show what (if anything) took over its role; position and lag results count event steps, not clock time; and the many relation items involving A02 all mirror the same single disappearance rather than independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of cases between the windows — the later window being dominated by cases that never used the A02 path — rather than a modification of one and the same flow. [DIR005, DIR011, OCC001, REL001]

Other differences: A11 occurs somewhat earlier in the later window (mean position about 0.49 → 0.40), and A10's self-repetitions became more frequent (about 16 → 42 observed pairs) with lags spread over 3–5 steps instead of concentrated at 4. [POS004, LAG007]
