### Observed differences

- The branch choice after A15 flipped. Earlier, about 75% of A15's outgoing transitions led to A16, A17 or A18 (roughly 27%, 23% and 26%) and about 25% led to A19, A20 or A21 (14%, 4% and 8%); in the later window this reversed to about 24% versus 76% (A19 about 25%, A20 about 22%, A21 about 28%). [DIR001]
- Participation mirrored the flip: A16, A17 and A18 each ran in about 75 in 100 traces earlier but only about 25 in 100 later; A19, A20 and A21 each rose from about 25 to about 76 in 100. Each of these activities occurs at most once per trace in both windows (mean occurrences equal participation, no recurrence). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- A15 now keeps different company: earlier it co-occurred with A16, A17 or A18 in about 75% of traces (always before them) and with A19/A20/A21 in about 25%; later it co-occurs with A19, A20 or A21 in about 76% of traces (A15 first in each case) and with A16/A17/A18 in about 25%. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- The two triples behave as mutually exclusive alternatives: a member of {A16, A17, A18} and a member of {A19, A20, A21} never share a trace in either window; earlier the A16-group member was the one present in about 75% of these pairings, later the A19-group member is, in about 76%. [REL009, REL014, REL018]
- Within-branch co-occurrence flipped in step: A16–A18 pairs appeared together in about 75% of traces earlier (in both orders, no fixed sequence) but rarely later (about 25%, with neither present in about 76%); A19–A21 pairs show the reverse, co-occurring in about 25% earlier and about 76% later, again in both orders. [REL007, REL013, REL022, REL023, REL025]
- A25, present in essentially every trace in both windows, switched branches: earlier it co-occurred with A16/A17/A18 in about 75% of traces; later it co-occurs with A19/A20/A21 in about 76% and appears without the A16-group in about 76%. [REL012, REL021, REL024, REL027]
- Among traces containing both A15 and A19, the step gap shifted from mostly 1 step (about 54%) toward 2–3 steps (3 steps about 39% later). [LAG001]
- The elapsed time between consecutive completions of A10 and A11 roughly doubled: mean about 1.0 hour earlier versus about 2.0 hours later (median about 0.99 vs 1.99 hours; quartiles 0.88/1.10 vs 1.76/2.20 hours), based on 344 versus 365 intervals. [TWAIT001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports that the dominant branch after A15 flipped: earlier, roughly three quarters of the flow leaving A15 passed through A16, A17 and A18 — executed together, in no fixed order among themselves, alongside A25 — while about a quarter passed through A19, A20 and A21; in the later window these shares are reversed, so A16–A18 now appear in only about a quarter of traces, A19–A21 in about three quarters, and A25 now accompanies the A19-branch. All six successors of A15 and both triples remain observed in substantial shares in both windows, so this is a change in how often existing alternative paths are taken, not a replacement of one path by another; the internal order within each branch stays unfixed in both windows. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC004, REL001, REL004, REL009, REL022, REL024]

The change is shown directly in A15's outgoing-transition profile and corroborated consistently by mirror-image participation and relation changes of about 0.505 on every affected activity and pair, each larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits; the control-flow family produced 35 qualifying items against roughly 0.5 expected if nothing had changed, far beyond what chance re-splits produce. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the flip originates not in the decision at A15 itself but in an upstream change in the mix of cases reaching A15. [DIR001, OCC001, REL001, REL009, REL022]

Other differences: the elapsed time between consecutive completions of A10 and A11 roughly doubled (mean about 1.0 to about 2.0 hours, 344 vs 365 intervals, larger than in about 999 of 1000 reference comparisons; the time family produced 1 item against about 0.005 expected by chance); this is a timing change in its own right and shows no structural counterpart in this evidence. [TWAIT001]
