### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the step between A38 and A23 was removed: in the earlier window every case executed exactly one of the five alternative activities A14, A15, A20, A21, A22 between A38 and A23, while in the later window none of the five is observed and A38 leads directly to A23 in every trace. The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation of all five drops to zero out of 200 traces, A38's outgoing transitions move entirely from the five to A23, and the A38→A23 distance shortens from 2 steps to 1 while A23's participation remains at 100 in 100 traces with only a slight position shift. The screening recorded 32 qualifying items where roughly 0.42 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is not what a stable process typically produces.
