### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A36 occurred in every trace and was followed by exactly one of three alternative activities: A20 in about 35 in 100 traces, A14 in about 35 and A15 in about 30 (shares of A36's outgoing transitions; the three never occurred in the same trace). In the later window, A36 is followed directly by A21 in all 200 traces, and A14, A15 and A20 are not observed in any trace, down from roughly 60–70 in 200 traces each. - Before, each of A14, A15 and A20 led exclusively to A21, which occurred in every trace in both windows. The distance from A36 to A21 shortened from two steps to one, and their positions moved slightly closer together (A21's mean position about 0.39 to 0.37 of the trace, A36's about 0.32 to 0.33). - At A19, the outgoing branch shares shifted: A19→A26 rose from about 41% to 60% of observed outgoing transitions, while A19→A16 fell from about 31% to 16% and A19→A17 from about 28% to 23% (based on 97 traces containing A19 before and 111 after). [DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the block of three alternative activities A14, A15 and A20 that previously sat between A36 and A21 is bypassed: traces that earlier ran A36 → (A14 or A15 or A20) → A21 now run A36 → A21 directly, and the three activities are no longer observed. This rests on direct relational evidence — A36's outgoing transitions changed from the three alternatives to A21 alone in all traces, and the A36→A21 distance fell from two steps to one — together with the disappearance of A14, A15 and A20 from all 200 later traces and consistent co-occurrence and position shifts. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, LAG001, REL003, POS004]

The 23 qualifying control-flow items are roughly sixty times the 0.37 expected by chance for the 74 tested units, so the set as a whole cannot be explained by random sampling, and three activities each seen in about a third of earlier traces dropping to 0 of 200 is not a rare-variant fluctuation. Note that the many OCC, DIR, REL, LAG and POS items largely describe this one trace-level change and are not independent confirmations. A limitation: zero participation strictly means "not observed", so the evidence cannot distinguish a removal of A14, A15 and A20 from these activities remaining in the process but being skipped in every case, and it cannot show why the behaviour changed. [screening]

Other differences: At A19, the shares of existing branches shifted toward A26 (about 41% to 60% of A19's outgoing transitions) at the expense of A16 (about 31% to 16%) and A17 (about 28% to 23%); all three successors remain observed in both windows with substantial support, so this is consistent with a change in how often existing paths are taken. It lies away from the A36–A21 segment, and the evidence does not connect it to the main hypothesis. [DIR003]
