### Observed differences

- In the later window, A19 and A20 each run in all 200 traces, with A19 before A20 in about 99 in 100 of them; in the earlier window each appeared in only about 53 in 100 traces, and both were absent together in about 48 in 100. [OCC005, OCC006, REL021]

[POS002, POS003, POS004, DIR001, DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the A19–A20 block became a mandatory, fixed-order part of every case — A19 executed before A20 in nearly all traces — while the previously common A10-based path became optional, running in only about a quarter of cases and now only alongside A19/A20; A14, A15, A16 and A43 now lead into A19 and A28 follows it, whereas before they frequently ran in A10-containing traces without A19/A20. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL004, REL011, REL021, REL025, REL026, DIR003, DIR007]

This rests on direct participation counts in both windows (A19: 105 of 200 traces before versus 200 of 200 after; A10: 135 of 200 versus 52 of 200), on relational and routing evidence that consistently points the same way, and on a screening yield far above chance (73 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 1 expected without change), with each item's difference exceeding about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC004, OCC005, REL001, REL011, REL015, REL018, REL021, REL025]

The many agreeing items largely describe one underlying shift rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot show whether cases that previously took the A10 path now run the A19–A20 block instead or whether A10's decline and the mandation of A19/A20 are independent changes; a few successor redistributions (A04, A08) rest on too few outgoing transitions to interpret. [DIR001, DIR002]

Other differences: A25/A26 and A27 became strictly exclusive alternatives executed in virtually every case; A02, A06 and A08 participation roughly tripled; A16 and A43 occupy slightly earlier and A28 slightly later event positions. [REL032, REL033, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS002, POS003, POS004]
