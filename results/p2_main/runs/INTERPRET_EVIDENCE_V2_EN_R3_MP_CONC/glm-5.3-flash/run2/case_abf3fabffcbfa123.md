### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch executing A32, A33 and A38 together — an alternative to A27 taken in about half of the earlier traces — is no longer executed in the later window, while A27, previously the other alternative, now runs in virtually every trace, after A14/A15/A26 and before A28. The participation changes are large and mirrored (A27 about 52 to 100 in 100 traces; A32/A33/A38 about 49 to 0), the relational evidence shows the new behaviour directly (A27 co-occurs with A14, A15, A26 and A28 in about 99 of 100 traces; A26 leads to A27 in about 98 of 100 transitions), and the screening produced 62 qualifying items where fewer than 1 would be expected without a change, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically yields. Because the A32/A33/A38 path is nearly absent later, this is a change in participation — a path becoming skipped and A27 becoming effectively mandatory — not a re-weighting among paths that all remain in use. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL009, REL013, REL017, REL021, REL022, DIR006]

Limitations: near-zero participation shows only that A32, A33 and A38 were not observed in the later window, not that they are impossible there; and many relational items restate the same trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the A32/A33/A38 branch still exists but was simply not exercised in the later sample. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL022]

Other differences: A04 lost most of its participation (about 68 to 22 in 100 traces) and A14 now typically leads directly to A15 instead of via A04; the evidence does not show whether this bypass is part of the same rerouting or a separate change. A02 and A08 appear in somewhat more traces, though the redirection seen at A02 rests on few observations (about 10 outgoing transitions before, 30 after). A14 sits slightly later and A28 slightly earlier within traces. [OCC002, REL001, DIR004, OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, POS002, POS003]
