### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new step, A01, was added to the case flow between A02/A04 and the downstream activities A03, A04, A06, A07 and A10: earlier, the flow ran from A02 almost directly to A04 and on to the downstream steps, whereas in the later window A02 leads to A01 in about half of its outgoing transitions, A01 always follows A02, precedes A03/A06/A07/A10 in nearly all joint occurrences, and every measured distance from A02 or A04 to those activities grew by about one step while their participation stayed unchanged. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS007]

Supporting signs: 34 control-flow items qualified against fewer than 0.3 expected for an unchanged process, so chance cannot account for the evidence as a whole; the insertion is shown directly by the new successor relations (A02→A01, A04→A01, A01→A04/A07/A03/A10), by the co-occurrence and order changes, and by the uniform one-step shift across all eight distance measures, with A01 appearing in 200 of 200 later cases versus 0 of 200 earlier, which rules out a rare variant. Note that the many agreeing items largely describe this one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL003, LAG002, LAG004, LAG007]

Limitations: POS and LAG use event positions rather than clock time, so on their own they would not show that work moved; here they are corroborated by direct successor and order changes. The evidence does not pin down a single fixed slot for A01, since in about 45 of 100 cases A04 runs before A01, and it cannot show whether A01's addition and A09's disappearance are one restructuring (for example a relocated or re-labelled step) or two independent changes. [REL003, LAG002, OCC001, OCC002, DIR004]

Other differences: The end-of-case branch around A09 was removed: earlier, A06 always led to A09 as the final event in the roughly 45 in 100 cases containing them; in the later window A09 is not observed at all and A06 ends the case. [OCC002, POS004, POS006, DIR004, REL011]
