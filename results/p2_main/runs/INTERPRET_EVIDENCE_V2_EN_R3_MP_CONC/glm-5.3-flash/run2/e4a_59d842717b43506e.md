### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a step drawn from five alternative activities — A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 — runs in essentially every case and is inserted into the sequence between A24 and A26: in about half of the traces directly after A24 (before A25) and in the other half directly after A25 (before A26), whereas in the earlier window the flow ran directly from A24 to A25 to A26 with none of the five present. The change is shown directly and consistently across indicator types: the rerouting of A24 and A25, the successors of the five activities, their fixed order after A24 and before A26 with mixed order around A25, and the A24-to-A26 distance moving from two to three positions. The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process produces (34 control-flow items against roughly 0.44 expected and 1 time item against roughly 0.01, each family compared with its own expectation). Limitations: because the five activities had zero participation in the earlier window, the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely added step from one that existed but was not exercised before; and the "exactly one of the five per trace" reading rests on the six tested pairs together with the near-complete coverage of the shares. Other differences: The elapsed time between consecutive completions of A10 and A11 halved (median about 1.0 hour to about 0.5 hour) and became markedly more consistent; this is a timing change in its own right, and the evidence provides no link between A10/A11 and the inserted step. [TWAIT001]
