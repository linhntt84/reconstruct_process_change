### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A14, A15 and A20 were not observed in any of the 200 traces; in the later window each occurs in a substantial share of traces — A14 in about 37 in 100, A20 in about 34 in 100 and A15 in about 29 in 100 — and never more than once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the earlier window, A36's outgoing transitions led to A21 in essentially every trace; in the later window they are redistributed almost entirely towards the three new activities: about 37 in 100 to A14, 34 to A20, 29 to A15, and only about 1 in 100 directly to A21. [DIR004]
- Wherever the new activities occur, their outgoing transitions lead exclusively to A21. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The three new activities are mutually exclusive alternatives: no pair of them ever occurs in the same trace in the later window. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- The pairwise relations place the new activities between A36 and A21: A36 co-occurs with each new activity exactly when it occurs and always precedes it, and each new activity co-occurs with A21 exactly when it occurs and always precedes A21. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- The gap from A36 to A21 changed from immediate succession (distance 1 in all earlier traces) to distance 2 in about 99 in 100 later traces, i.e. one activity now sits between them. [LAG001]
- A21 still occurs in every trace in both windows; its mean relative position moved slightly later (about 0.37 to 0.39 of the trace), while the new activities appear at about one third of the trace (mean relative position roughly 0.35). [POS004, POS001, POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a set of three mutually exclusive activities — A14, A15 and A20 — sits between A36 and A21: A36, which previously led directly to A21 in essentially every trace, is now followed by exactly one of the three (about 37, 34 and 29 in 100 traces respectively), and that activity then leads on to A21, which still occurs in every trace. The support is direct and consistent across indicator families: A36's outgoing transitions redistributed towards the three activities [DIR004], the new activities' transitions all point to A21 [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003], the relations show A36 before the new activity before A21 [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009], the three never co-occur [REL001, REL002, REL005], and the A36-to-A21 distance moved from 1 to 2 [LAG001]. The volume of qualifying items (21, against roughly 0.37 expected by chance for the 74 tested control-flow units) is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. Limitations: the many agreeing items largely describe one underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot fully rule out that this path existed at a very low rate before and was missed by the 200-trace earlier sample.
