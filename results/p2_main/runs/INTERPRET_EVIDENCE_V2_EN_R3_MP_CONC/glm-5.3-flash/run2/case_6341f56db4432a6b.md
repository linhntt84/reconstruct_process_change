### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the closing activity A01 is no longer executed: in the earlier window about half of the cases (103 of 200) ended with A12 followed by A01 as the final step, whereas in the later window A01 does not appear at all and A12, still present in about half the cases, now itself concludes those traces. [OCC001, REL001, DIR003, POS002] This hypothesis is direct and consistent: the participation drop, the disappearance of A12's only observed successor, the pair changing from always co-occurring (A12 first) to never co-occurring, and A12's relative position moving from about 0.82 to 1.00 all describe the same behaviour, and the 9 qualifying control-flow items far exceed the roughly 0.13 expected if nothing had changed, so sampling variation alone cannot account for the set. [OCC001, DIR003, REL001, POS002] An important competing explanation remains: zero participation only establishes that A01 was not observed in the later window, so it may have become an optional step that was skipped in every later case rather than being removed from the process — the evidence cannot distinguish these.
