### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block A35, A36, A41, A42, A43 changed its placement relative to A26 and A27, together with a rewiring of its entry and exit: in the earlier window A25 led almost exclusively to A27, A26 led into the block, the block followed A26 and A27 and led on to A30; in the later window A25 leads into the block, the block runs before A26 and A27 and leads to A27, and A26 goes directly to A30, bypassing the block. The order reversal is shown directly and near-deterministically (about 97–100 in 100 traces one way before, about 98–99.5 in 100 the other way after, at unchanged co-occurrence of 100 in 100), and it is consistent across the successor changes, the position shifts and the collapse of close pairs with A30. Screening supports that this is not a chance pattern: 64 control-flow items qualified against roughly 1 expected without change among 195 tested units. Limitations: the after-window distance profiles for the pairs involving A30 rest on only 1–2 observations, and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample a different mix of coexisting process variants rather than a single reordering of one process.
