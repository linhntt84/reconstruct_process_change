### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the five activities A20, A21, A22, A24 and A25 always ran before the five activities A26–A30: for every monitored pair the first-group activity came first in about 98–99 in 100 traces, while both activities still occurred together in all traces in both windows. In the later window this fixed order largely dissolved: A26 now precedes A20 in about 73 in 100 traces and A25 in about 93 in 100 (before roughly 1 in 100), A28–A30 precede A25 in about 74–82 in 100, and pairs such as A20/A28, A21/A29 or A25/A27 now appear in both orders in roughly equal shares (about 50 vs 45–48 in 100). [REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022]
- The shift is uneven across pairs: for A24 against A28, A29 and A30, and for A21 against A27, the old order still holds in about 75–79 in 100 traces, but a reversed minority of about 20–23 in 100 appeared that was essentially absent before. [REL006, REL015, REL016, REL017]
- The two groups also swapped typical positions in the trace: A25's mean relative position moved from about 0.61 to 0.70 of the trace and A20's from about 0.56 to 0.61, while A26's fell from about 0.63 to 0.55 and A30's from about 0.68 to 0.62. [POS001, POS002, POS005, POS006, POS010]
- Routing followed the same pattern: before, A20, A21, A22 and A24 led almost exclusively to their own group (about one third of transitions each to A20/A21/A22/A25); in the later window about 28–45 in 100 of their outgoing transitions go to A26–A30. Conversely, A27–A30, which previously led almost only within their own group (A27 to A36 in about 82 in 100 transitions), now lead back to A20–A25 in about 35–47 in 100 transitions. A25, previously always followed by A26, now goes to A26 in only about 7 in 100 transitions. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009]
- Distances changed consistently with the reversal: the A20→A26 pair, never at distance 1 before, now occurs at distance 1 in about 46 in 100 of its remaining occurrences; A24→A26 moved from always 5 steps apart to 1–3 steps in about 76 in 100 of cases; and close reversed sequences that were nearly absent before now occur often (A26→A24 in about 92 traces vs 2 before, A29→A24 about 46 vs 3, A30→A24 about 42 vs 2). Lags inside the first group also stretched, e.g. A20→A21 from only 1–2 steps to a spread over 1–5 steps. [LAG001, LAG004, LAG026, LAG032, LAG043, LAG048]
- Two further shifts concern downstream activities: A36 now tends to occur later relative to A28–A30 (distance-5 share up from about 5–7 in 100 to about 26–36 in 100, and A25 now leads to A36 in about 38 in 100 of its transitions), and A35, previously always followed by A24, now splits about 53 vs 45 in 100 between A24 and A26. [LAG042, LAG047, LAG052, DIR005, DIR010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering constraint between two groups of activities changed: the block A20, A21, A22, A24, A25, which in the earlier window executed before the block A26, A27, A28, A29, A30 in nearly every trace, lost this fixed precedence in the later window — the second block now precedes the first in a large share of traces (up to about 93 in 100 for A26 before A25), several pairs occur in both orders, and the first block sits later in the trace while the second sits earlier. This rests on direct relational evidence agreeing across all 22 monitored pairs, corroborated by the opposite position shifts, the new backward transitions from A26–A30 into A20–A25, the collapse of the A25→A26 bridge, and a qualifying-item count (98) roughly one hundred times the number expected without a change (about 1). [REL001, REL005, REL010, REL018, REL020, POS005, POS006, DIR005, DIR006, LAG032]

The evidence does not show what the new per-trace structure is: the roughly 50/50 pairs are consistent both with a mixture of traces (some keeping the old order, some reversing it) and with a genuinely relaxed constraint, and per-trace consistency cannot be checked from these indicators; interleaving within a trace remains rare (about 1–3 in 100), so freely interleaved execution is not supported. [REL002, REL007, REL011, REL019]

Other differences: the later occurrence of A36 relative to A28–A30 together with the new A25→A36 transition, and the split of A35's successors between A24 and A26, are observed but are not part of the main hypothesis above. [LAG042, LAG052, DIR005, DIR010]
