### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the routing into A02 was restructured. In the earlier window, every trace reached A02 directly from A01 (about half of traces) or from A04 (the other half); in the later window, A01 is no longer observed, the A04 branch reads A04 → A05 → A02 with the newly observed A05 in about 46 of 100 traces, and the traces that previously began with A01 now begin with A02 itself. The hypothesis rests on direct relational evidence — A04's successor switched entirely from A02 to A05, A05's successor is entirely A02, and the A04→A02 distance moved from one to two steps in every observed case — plus consistent occurrence counts (A01: about 101 of 200 traces earlier versus 0 later; A05: 0 earlier versus about 92 of 200 later). Screening qualified 15 control-flow items against roughly 0.09 expected for the 18 tested units if nothing had changed, so the item set as a whole is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces.
