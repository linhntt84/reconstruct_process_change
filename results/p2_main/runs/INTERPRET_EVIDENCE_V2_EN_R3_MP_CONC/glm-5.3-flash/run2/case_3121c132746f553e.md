### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A14 and A15 each appeared exactly once in every trace (participation 100%); in the later window they appear together in about 53 in 100 traces, and in about 47 in 100 traces neither appears. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001]
- The middle block also changed: before, every trace contained either A20 followed by A21 (about 51 in 100 traces) or A22 (about 49 in 100); in the later window A20+A21 occur in about 29 in 100, A22 in about 24 in 100, and neither in about 47 in 100. The split between the two branches, where the block does run, stayed roughly even. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL012, REL013, REL016]
- A38 and A23 occur in every trace in both windows. Before, all of A38's observed outgoing transitions led to A14, and A23 followed A38 at a distance of 4–5 positions; in the later window about 48 in 100 of A38's outgoing transitions lead directly to A23, and in about 48 in 100 traces A23 immediately follows A38 (lag 1, which did not occur before). [DIR001, LAG001, REL005, REL006]
- When the block does run in the later window, its internal order is unchanged: A38 → A14 → (A20 → A21 or A22) → A15 → A23. [REL001, REL002, REL005, REL006, REL012, REL019]
- A23's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (about 0.44 to 0.41) and A38's slightly later (about 0.30 to 0.32), consistent with the block being skipped in about half the traces. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, in about 48 in 100 cases the segment A14 → (A20 → A21 or A22) → A15 is skipped entirely: A38 is followed directly by A23. In the earlier window this direct succession was not observed (A38 always led to A14; lag-1 A38→A23 in 0 traces), and all block activities appeared in every trace; in the remaining later-window traces the earlier structure and branch split are preserved. The screening found 29 qualifying items where about 0.45 would be expected without a change, so the pattern as a whole cannot be attributed to chance sampling, and the participation drops, the "neither" co-occurrence shares, the A38 successor change and the new lag-1 relation all point to the same behaviour with matching sizes (about 47–48 in 100) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001, LAG001, REL001, REL013, REL016].

Limitations: the direct A38→A23 path was simply not exercised in the earlier window, so the evidence cannot distinguish a newly added path from one that existed but was rarely used before; and it cannot fully rule out that A14/A15 and the A20/A21/A22 block became optional through separate changes rather than one bypass, although the exactly matching shares and the redirection at A38 favour a single skip.

Other differences: none independent of the skip; the small position shifts of A23 and A38 follow from the same behaviour [POS001, POS002].
