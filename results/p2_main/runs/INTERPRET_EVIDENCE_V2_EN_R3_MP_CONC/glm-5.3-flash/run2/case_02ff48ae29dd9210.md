### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the five-way choice block (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22) is bypassed in the later window: earlier, every trace executed exactly one of these five activities between A24/A25 and A26, whereas later traces run directly from A24 through A25 to A26. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR005, DIR006, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

The collapse is consistent across indicators: participation fell for all five activities, the routing of A24 and A25 (each observed in all 200 traces of both windows) converged on the direct chain, and the A24-to-A26 distance shortened from 3 to 2 steps. The screening context also fits: 38 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected without change is far beyond what random re-splits of a stable process produce. [DIR005, DIR006, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

Because the five activities are nearly absent in the later window, this is a structural change — the block became skipped or removed — not a shift in how often existing paths are taken. The evidence cannot distinguish a complete removal of the block from a block that still exists but is entered in almost no cases (isolated later traces still show the old pattern), nor can it rule out that the later window does not cover process variants in which these activities occur.
