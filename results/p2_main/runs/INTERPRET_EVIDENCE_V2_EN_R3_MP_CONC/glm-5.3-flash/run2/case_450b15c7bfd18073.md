### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the process appears to run a new alternative path between A21 and A23: in roughly half of the cases the A22 step is replaced by a block of the three activities A27, A28 and A33, each executed once in varying order before A23, while the original A21 → A22 → A23 path continues in the remaining half. In the earlier window, only A21 → A22 → A23 was observed [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, REL006, LAG001].

The hypothesis is shown directly by the successor redistribution at A21, the appearance of A27/A28/A33 at about 49 in 100 participation, their exclusive relation to A22, their precedence between A21 and A23, and the segment-length change from 2 to 4 positions [DIR001, OCC003, REL006, REL009, LAG001]. The volume of qualifying evidence (26 items against fewer than 1 expected if nothing had changed, all in the control-flow family, each difference larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits) cannot be explained by chance sampling as a whole [OCC001, DIR001, REL012]. Because A27, A28 and A33 were absent earlier, this is an added alternative path rather than a re-weighting of previously observed paths.

Limitations: with finite windows of 200 traces each, the evidence cannot show whether the trio path is genuinely new or existed before at a rate too low to appear; and whether the choice between A22 and the trio is fixed per case or decided at run time cannot be determined from these indicators.

Other differences: none — all qualifying items describe this single replacement of the A22 segment between A21 and A23 [OCC001, DIR001, LAG001].
