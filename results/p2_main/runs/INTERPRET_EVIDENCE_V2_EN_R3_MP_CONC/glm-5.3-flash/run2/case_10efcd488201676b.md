### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process was structurally reworked between the two windows. In the later window, A01 and A17 are no longer executed (earlier about 64 in 100 and all 200 traces respectively), A07 appears near the start in about 68 in 100 traces and hands over to A03 or A04, every trace closes with A04 → A10 followed by A06 and A16 executed exactly once each and directly adjacent in either order (whereas A06 and A16 were previously optional, mid-process and sometimes repeated), and A02, A14 and A15 are repeated far more often (A02 about 9 vs about 2 occurrences per trace). This rests on direct relational evidence — A04 precedes A16 and A06 in 100 in 100 traces, A10 precedes A06 in 100 in 100 traces, A06 and A16 follow one another at lag 1, and A07 leads to A03/A04 — and on agreement across the occurrence, position, successor, co-occurrence and lag indicators, with 171 qualifying items against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC007, OCC008, DIR004, DIR006, DIR011, REL031, REL035, REL039, REL045, LAG022, LAG058]

Limitations: only control-flow evidence is available, positions and lags refer to event positions rather than clock time, and zero participation shows only that A01 and A17 were not executed in the later sample, not that they were removed from the process definition. [OCC001, OCC008] An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in case mix or between coexisting variants rather than a modification of one shared process; the later window is internally very uniform, but the finite samples cannot show whether the rework happened at once or gradually. [OCC003, OCC007]

Other differences: A13 now always leads to A02 and A12 leads to A09 instead of A01; A09, A11 and A12 run slightly more often and slightly later in the trace; and several pairwise event distances shifted, for example A11 to A12 from typically three to typically four events apart.
