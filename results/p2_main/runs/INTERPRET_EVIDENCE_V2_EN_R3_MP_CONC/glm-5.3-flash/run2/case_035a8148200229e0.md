### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the optional opening activity before A03 changed from A04 to A01: in the earlier window about half of the traces began A04 → A03 and A01 never occurred, while in the later window about half begin A01 → A03 and A04 no longer occurs; A03 is present in every trace in both windows, and the other half of traces still start directly with A03. With 200 traces per window, a participation flip of this size is unlikely to be a sampling artefact, and the 11 qualifying items far exceed the 0.14 expected for an unchanged process.
