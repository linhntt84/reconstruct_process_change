### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a timing change centred on A14: in the later window, the elapsed time between the completion of the preceding activity and the completion of A14 is roughly halved, both when the predecessor is A22 (about 0.93 to about 0.44 hours) and when it is A17 (about 0.19 to about 0.08 hours).
