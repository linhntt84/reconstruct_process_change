### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A06 was dropped from the flow between A05 and A04: traces that previously executed A05 → A06 → A04 (about 52 in 100) now execute A05 → A04 directly (about 50 in 100), with A06 no longer executed between them. This is shown directly by the complete switch in A05's successor from A06 to A04, the disappearance of A06 and of its outgoing edge to A04, the closure of the A05–A04 gap from two steps to one, and the collapse of A06's co-occurrence with both activities — while participation in the affected path stayed about the same, so the path was not taken more or less often, its content changed. Nine control-flow items qualified against an expectation of 0.07 items without a change (14 units tested), far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces, and each difference is larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits; the items are mutually consistent, though they describe overlapping views of the same traces rather than independent confirmations [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, LAG001, screening].

Limitation: zero participation in the later window means only that A06 was not observed in these 200 traces; the evidence cannot rule out that A06 still occurs in rare or unsampled cases. About half of the traces in both windows contain none of the three activities and produced no flagged differences, so this evidence says nothing about the rest of the process.
