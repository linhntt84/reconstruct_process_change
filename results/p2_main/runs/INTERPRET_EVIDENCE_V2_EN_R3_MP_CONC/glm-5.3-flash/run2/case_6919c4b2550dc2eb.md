### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two activities, A01 and A05, were added to the process: in the later window A01 runs once per trace between the existing middle activities and A06 (typically A03 or A04 → A01 → A06), and A05 runs once per trace after A06, usually as the new final step. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, REL003, REL004, REL007]

Supporting signs: screening flagged 21 control-flow items where about 0.13 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the volume of flagged behaviour cannot be explained by chance sampling; the addition is shown directly by participation rising from 0 to 200 of 200 traces for both activities; the new edges A03→A01, A04→A01, A01→A06 and A06→A05 appear with strict co-occurrence orders; and the widened A04→A06 gap matches an insertion between them. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, REL004, REL007, LAG003]

Limitations: placement is established through event positions and co-occurrence relations rather than clock time, and the evidence cannot show why the activities were added; a wholesale switch to a different process variant that always contains A01 and A05 would look identical across these two windows and cannot be ruled out. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS003]

Other differences: the old exits lost share (A06 → A02 fell from about 53 in 100 to about 8 in 100, and A04 → A06 from about 74 in 100 to about 6 in 100); in about 29 of 200 traces A05 now leads back to another activity (A04 in about 59 in 100 of those, A02 about 31, A03 about 10), a new repetition possibility; and the distances from A03 to A04 and from A03 to A06 lengthened.
