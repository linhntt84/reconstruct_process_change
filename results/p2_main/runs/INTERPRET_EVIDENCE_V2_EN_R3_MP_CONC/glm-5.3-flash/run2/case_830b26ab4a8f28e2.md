### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was present throughout the earlier window: it appeared in all 200 traces, about 3.5 times per trace, recurred in about 74 in 100 traces, and sat roughly a third of the way through the trace (median relative position about 0.34). In the later window, A01 was not observed at all — 0 of 200 traces, no occurrences and no observed outgoing transitions. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001]
- The flows into A01 disappeared. Earlier, A03's outgoing transitions led to A01 in about 88 in 100 cases and A05's in about 85 in 100; in the later window, neither A03 nor A05 led to A01 in any observed case. A03 now leads to A10 (about 42 in 100), A09 (about 39) and A08 (about 19); A05 now leads to A09 (about 41), A10 (about 38) and A08 (about 21). [DIR002, DIR003]
- A01's own outgoing edges vanished: earlier it led mainly to A10 (about 35 in 100), A09 (about 33) and A08 (about 28), with small shares to A12, A11, A06 and A05; afterwards no outgoing transitions from A01 were observed. [DIR001]
- Partnerships broke up: earlier, A08, A09 and A10 each co-occurred with A01 in every trace (mostly interleaved or repeated); in the later window each still occurs in every trace but never together with A01. A03 and A05 keep roughly the same trace participation as before (about 47 and 65 in 100 traces) but now never co-occur with A01. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]
- The remaining activities sit closer together: A03–A09 pairs one event step apart rose from about 3 in 100 observations to about 20, and A03–A10 from about 3 to about 23, while pairs four steps apart became rare (A03–A08: about 28 in 100 down to under 1). This is consistent with the activity that used to sit between them disappearing. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A01 was removed from the process, or is no longer executed in any observed case. Earlier, A01 ran in every trace about 3.5 times, about a third of the way through, and acted as a hub: A03 and A05 led into it in roughly 85–88 in 100 of their outgoing transitions, and it shared traces with A08, A09 and A10 in all cases. In the later window, A01 is entirely unobserved, A03 and A05 proceed to A08/A09/A10 instead at unchanged participation, the surviving activities now execute without A01, and their mutual distances shrank. Chance is an unlikely explanation overall: 17 control-flow items qualified where about 0.26 would be expected without a change, and every item's difference exceeded roughly 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

The main limitation: zero participation only shows that A01 was not observed in the later sample of 200 traces; the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine removal from A01 being renamed or re-coded, or from it having become conditional and simply never triggered in this window, nor can it show whether one of the remaining activities took over A01's role. [OCC001]

Other differences: a modest redistribution in the distance between A08 and A09 (one-step pairs fell from about 14 in 100 observations to about 7, four-step pairs rose from about 4 to about 7); both activities remain in every trace, so this looks like a side-effect of A01's disappearance rather than an independent change. [LAG007]
