### Observed differences

- Participation changed in opposite directions for two pairs of activities. In the earlier window A05 ran in every trace (about 4.2 occurrences per trace) and A08 in about 68 in 100 traces; in the later window A05 runs in about 50 in 100 traces (about 2.2 occurrences per trace) and A08 in about 34 in 100 (about 1.1 per trace) [OCC001, OCC002]. Conversely, A10 grew from about 47 in 100 traces (about 1.9 occurrences per trace) to every trace (about 4.1 per trace), and A16 from about 30 to about 68 in 100 traces [OCC003, OCC004].
- The two pairs also exchanged their typical placement: A05 and A08 sat around two-thirds through the trace in the earlier window and around one-third in the later window (mean relative positions 0.67→0.33 and 0.66→0.34), while A10 and A16 moved from about one-third to about two-thirds (0.32→0.67 and 0.33→0.67) [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004].
- Incoming routing changed almost completely: A06 led exclusively to A05 before and exclusively to A10 in the later window; A11 and A15, which previously sent about half of their outgoing transitions to A05, now send about half to A10 and none to A05; A14's main successor switched from A10 (about 51 in 100 outgoing transitions) to A05 (about 53 in 100) [DIR002, DIR004, DIR006, DIR005].
- The branches below the two activities were partially exchanged: A05's outgoing transitions moved from A08/A09 (about 52/48) to A17/A08 (about 52/48), and A10's from A16/A17 (about 46/54) to A16/A09 (about 52/48) [DIR001, DIR003].
- Co-occurrence ties flipped accordingly: A05 appeared together with A11, A14 and A15 in every trace earlier, but in the later window about 50 in 100 traces contain only those activities without A05, while A10 now co-occurs with each of them in every trace (about 47 in 100 before). A09 ran exclusively alongside A05 before and now exclusively alongside A10; A17 moved from A10's company to A05's [REL005, REL006, REL007, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL003, REL020, REL009, REL026].
- Typical distances moved with the repositioning, e.g. A14→A08 is now usually 2 steps apart (almost always 4 before), A14→A10 now 3–5 steps (about 1 before), and A17→A10 now consistently 5 steps [LAG006, LAG007, LAG011].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A05 and A10 exchanged their places in the flow. In the earlier window A05 was mandatory (every trace, about 4 occurrences), placed late in the trace, and fed by A06, A11 and A15, while A10 was optional (about 47 in 100 traces), placed early, and fed by A14. In the later window these roles are reversed: A10 runs in every trace (about 4 occurrences), late, and receives the transitions from A06, A11 and A15 that previously went to A05, while A05 runs in about half the traces (about 2 occurrences), early, and receives A14's transition. A08 declined and moved earlier together with A05, A16 grew and moved later together with A10, and the downstream wiring was exchanged crosswise: A09 now succeeds A10 instead of A05, and A17 now succeeds A05 instead of A10 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL020, REL009].

The hypothesis is supported directly and consistently: the rerouting appears as near-total successor replacements (A06 went from 100% of its outgoing transitions to A05 to 100% to A10) rather than as a shift between retained alternatives, and the occurrence, position, relation and distance indicators all describe the same exchange. Screening qualified 55 items where fewer than one (0.46) would be expected by chance for an unchanged process, and every item's difference exceeds those of about 995 in 1000 random re-splits; since many relation items restate the same rerouting, the judgement rests on this consistency across indicator families rather than on the item count [DIR002, OCC003, POS003, REL022].

The evidence cannot exclude that the old routing persists at a rate too low to surface in 200 traces per window, and it cannot distinguish a wholesale exchange of the two activities' roles from a re-wiring of the mid-process hub in which each kept part of its old branch (A05 kept A08 and gained A17; A10 kept A16 and gained A09) [DIR001, DIR003].

Other differences: self-repetition gaps of the same activities shifted as a side effect of the repositioning — A05's and A08's repeats now cluster at shorter distances (about 2 steps) while A10's spread to 3–5 steps — and read as part of the same change rather than a separate one [LAG001, LAG003, LAG004].
