### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a mutually exclusive choice among five activities — A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 — was inserted between A43 and A28. Earlier, cases flowed from A43 directly to A28 in about 95 in 100 cases; later, A43 routes to exactly one of the five (each taken in roughly 18–23 in 100 cases, essentially never together, together covering nearly all of A43's outgoing transitions), and that activity then leads to A28, moving the A43-to-A28 distance from one event step to two. The change is shown directly by routing, relation and distance evidence rather than by frequencies alone, and 50 qualifying items against roughly 0.7 expected for an unchanged process makes a chance explanation of the set implausible; note that the occurrence, routing, relation and distance items largely describe this one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG002, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL009, REL013, REL020]

Limitations: positions and distances are event positions, not clock time, so nothing can be said about duration, and zero participation earlier strictly means only that the four activities were not observed in that sample. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window follows a different end-to-end variant altogether rather than a single local insertion. [LAG002, OCC002, POS002]

Other differences: A06 now typically sits between A48 and A49 in about a quarter of later cases (A48 before A06 in about 24 in 100 traces, A06 leading to A49 in about 89 in 100 of its outgoing transitions), whereas earlier it was a rare mid-process activity (about 9 in 100 traces, median position about 0.44) with outgoing transitions spread over many activities; A48's direct edge to A49 correspondingly fell from about 95 to about 76 in 100. This second change is reported separately and is not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR008, REL008, LAG003]
