### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new block of activities sits between A03 and A04: A03 now routes about 46 in 100 of its outgoing transitions through A02, where previously they went directly to A04; A02 either repeats through A12 (A02→A12→A02) or continues via A13, and A13 leads on to A04, making this route to A04 several steps longer than before. The change is shown directly: the new transitions out of A02, A12 and A13 are observed in the later window, the ordering matches the chain (A03 before A02/A13, A13 before A04), participation rises from zero to about a quarter (A12) and about half (A02, A13) of traces, and A04's later position together with the longer A03→A04 distance follow from the new route. With 54 qualifying items against roughly 0.36 expected if nothing had changed, chance cannot account for the evidence as a whole. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, DIR007, REL013, REL017, REL020, POS003, LAG001]

The two windows are single snapshots, so the evidence cannot rule out that they capture a switch between pre-existing process variants rather than a modification of one process; the complete absence of A02, A09, A12 and A13 from 200 earlier traces makes plain sampling fluctuation implausible, however. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
