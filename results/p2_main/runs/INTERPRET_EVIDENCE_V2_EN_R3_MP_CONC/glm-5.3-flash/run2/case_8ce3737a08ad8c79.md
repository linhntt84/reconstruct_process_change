### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative executed after A20 flipped between the windows: in the earlier window a case typically ran A20 followed by the block A24–A26 (the three executed together in varying order, alongside A30), with the block A21–A23 taken in only about 19 in 100 cases; in the later window a case typically runs A20 followed by A21–A23 (again together and unordered, alongside A30), with A24–A26 taken in only about 21 in 100 cases. All six activities and both blocks are observed in substantial shares in both windows and remain mutually exclusive throughout, so this reads as the same two existing paths being taken with very different frequencies, not as activities being added, removed or repeated. [DIR003, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL009, REL012, REL015, REL017, REL023, REL036]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor distribution at A20 itself flipped with 200 supporting traces in each window [DIR003], the participation swap is large in both directions with tens to over a hundred observations per window [OCC002, OCC005], and the co-occurrence and ordering items agree across both blocks and with A30's realignment [REL009, REL014, REL020, REL038]. In total 60 items qualified, far more than the roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process at these thresholds, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of a stable process typically produce. The main limitation is that these indicators cannot distinguish a re-routing at a single decision point directly after A20 from a shift in the mix of two complete process variants between the sampled windows; both would look identical here. Moreover, the many agreeing relation items largely restate one underlying block swap and are not independent confirmations. [DIR003, OCC002, OCC005, REL009, REL014, REL020, REL038]

Other differences: A10 became rarer (about 17 to about 7 in 100 traces) and, when present, appears earlier and more variably within the trace and distributes its outgoing transitions across more targets, with A32's transitions to A10 nearly disappearing and the A32→A10 spacing spreading from mostly one step to one to three steps; these rest on modest support (14 after-traces with A10, and only 4 observations behind the lag change). A02's participation rose from about 2 to about 9 in 100 traces with a redistributed outgoing profile based on very few observations. [POS001, DIR002, DIR004, LAG001, OCC001, DIR001]
