### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A13 and A14 each occurred in every trace, with about 1.4 occurrences per trace, and about 33 in 100 traces contained the activity more than once. In the later window, both activities still appear in every trace, but mean occurrences fell to about 1.2 per trace and the share of traces with a repeat fell to about 19 in 100; the difference is larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002]
- The spacing between A17 and A10 shifted toward larger distances: in the earlier window, about 9 in 100 observed pairs were one step apart and about 25 in 100 were three steps apart, whereas in the later window one-step pairs fell to about 3 in 100 while three-step pairs rose to about 44 in 100; the profile rests on roughly 145 pair observations before and 143 after, and the redistribution is again larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the repeated execution of A13 and A14 decreased in the later window: both activities remain present in every trace in both windows, so this is not a participation change, but traces containing a repeat dropped from about a third to about a fifth and occurrences per trace from about 1.4 to about 1.2, consistent with a repeating part of the process involving A13 and A14 now running in fewer traces [OCC001, OCC002]. Supporting signs: the occurrence profiles of the two activities agree, the change is measured directly on about 200 traces per window, its size is exceeded by only about 1 in 200 random re-splits, and the three qualifying control-flow items are many times the roughly 0.2 expected if nothing had changed [OCC001, OCC002, LAG001]. Limitations: A13 and A14 show identical profiles, so the two occurrence items very likely mirror one underlying trace behaviour rather than two independent changes, and the evidence cannot show which structural element produces the repeats; a competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that an unobserved change elsewhere in the process drives both the reduced repetition and the wider A17–A10 spacing.

Other differences: The redistribution of the A17→A10 distance toward three-step gaps [LAG001] is a separate positional difference; no relational evidence in this window pair connects it to A13 or A14, and since it is measured in event positions it does not by itself show that an activity was moved.
