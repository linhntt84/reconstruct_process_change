### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the process ran as a single fixed chain, A26 → A17 → A15 → A18 → A19 → A21 → A20 → A27: each of these activities sent all of its outgoing transitions to exactly one successor (all of A15's went to A18, all of A18's to A19, all of A26's to A17). In the later window every one of them splits its outgoing transitions over several successors: A18 now goes to A27 in about 52 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and to A19, A20 or A21 in about 15–17 each; A26 goes to A19 in about 47 in 100 cases where it previously went only to A17; A15 keeps A18 as its main successor (about 68 in 100) but adds A19, A20 and A21 at roughly 11 each. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]

- Activity pairs that always appeared in one order now appear in both. Before, A15 always preceded A19, A21 and A20, A18 always preceded A19, A21 and A20, and A17 always preceded A19 and A21 (100 in 100 traces each). In the later window A19 precedes A18 in about 85 in 100 traces and A15 in about 74 in 100; A21 precedes A18 in about 68 in 100 and A15 about half the time; A17 and A19 are nearly even (A17 first in about 53 in 100). All these pairs still co-occur in every trace in both windows, so no activity appeared, disappeared or became optional — only the relative order changed. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

- Previously fixed distances loosened. Before, each ordered pair sat at one fixed distance (A26→A17, A17→A15, A15→A18, A19→A21, A21→A20 and A20→A27 always one step apart; A15→A20 always four). In the later window those direct adjacencies hold in only about 49–68 of 100 cases (A15→A18 about 68, A21→A20 about 67, A19→A21 about 61, A26→A17 about 53, A17→A15 about 56, A20→A27 about 49), with the remainder spread over two to five steps. Ordered pairs whose direction reversed now occur within five steps in far fewer traces: A15→A19 in about 52 of 200 traces (200 before) and A18→A21 in about 64 of 200 (200 before). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG006, LAG012, LAG013, LAG015, LAG017, LAG018, LAG021]

- Mean event positions shifted consistently with this mixing: A15, A17 and A18 now occur later in the trace (A15 from about 0.59 to 0.64 of the trace length, A18 from about 0.62 to 0.68), while A19, A21 and A20 occur earlier (A19 from about 0.65 to 0.59, A21 from about 0.68 to 0.64). These are event positions, not clock times. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the strict sequential ordering among A26, A17, A15, A18, A19, A21 and A20 was relaxed into a flexible order. In the earlier window every trace followed the single fixed sequence A26 → A17 → A15 → A18 → A19 → A21 → A20 → A27; in the later window the activities A19, A21 and A20 frequently execute before A15 and A18, each activity hands over to several successors instead of exactly one, and the previously fixed one-step adjacencies survive in only about half to two-thirds of cases. The order within each group is partly preserved (A17→A15, A15→A18, A19→A21, A21→A20 remain the majority successor), while the order between the groups is now mixed, A26 enters at A17 or directly at A19, and the run still ends at A27, reached directly from A18 in about half of A18's outgoing transitions or from A20 in about half of A20's. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR005, DIR007, REL001, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG006, LAG013, LAG015, LAG017, LAG018]

This rests on direct, mutually consistent relational, distance, successor and positional evidence rather than on the number of items alone: the control-flow screening expected fewer than 0.5 qualifying items by chance, yet 45 qualified — roughly a hundred times the chance expectation, so the ensemble cannot be explained by random re-splits of an unchanged process. Competing explanations are largely excluded because every pair still co-occurs in 100 of 100 traces and every activity appears in all 200 traces of both windows, making this a reordering of unchanged participation rather than activities becoming optional, repeated or removed. [DIR001, DIR003, REL001, REL006, LAG001, LAG013, POS001, POS004]

The evidence cannot distinguish whether each trace now picks its order freely within this block of activities or whether a small set of new fixed variants emerged; the per-pair mixed orders are compatible with either. Since positions and distances are measured in event positions rather than clock time, no conclusion about timing can be drawn. [REL001, REL004, LAG003, LAG009]
