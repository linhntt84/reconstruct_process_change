### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, three activities — A19, A20 and A25 — never appear in any of the 200 traces; in the later window they run regularly: A19 in about 29 in 100 traces, A20 in about 41 and A25 in about 31, each at most once per trace and around the middle of the trace (mean position about 0.52). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- The three are mutually exclusive: A19 and A20 never share a trace, and A19/A25 as well as A20/A25 co-occur in only about 1 in 200 later traces; their shares sum to essentially all traces, so exactly one of them executes in nearly every later case. [REL001, REL002, REL006]
- In the earlier window the surrounding segment ran directly: A27 was followed by A28 in about 97 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, A28 by A29 in about 98 in 100, and A27 reached A29 two steps later in about 96 in 100. [DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- In the later window these flows are redirected: A27 now leads to A28 in only about 43 in 100 transitions and to A20, A25 or A19 in about 22, 16 and 14; A28 leads to A29 in about 57 in 100 and to A20, A19 or A25 in about 16, 13 and 12. Each new activity in turn leads next to A28 (about 53–56 in 100) or to A29 (about 41–47 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- Order and distances change accordingly: A27 always precedes the new activity when both run (co-occurrence in about 29–41 in 100 traces), the new activity always precedes A29 (about 29–40 in 100), while around A28 both orders occur (about 13–23 in 100 each way). A27 and A28 are immediately consecutive in only about 43 in 100 later traces (about 98 in 100 earlier), A28 and A29 in about 56 (about 98 earlier), and A27 reaches A29 two steps later in only about 1 in 100 later traces (about 96 in 100 earlier), now typically three steps apart (about 92 in 100). [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new step was added inside the former A27 → A28 → A29 sequence: in the later window nearly every trace executes exactly one of three previously unobserved activities — A19 (about 29 in 100 traces), A20 (about 41) or A25 (about 31) — placed either between A27 and A28 (roughly half of the traces) or between A28 and A29 (roughly four in ten), after which the flow rejoins A28 or A29 and continues as before, while the uninterrupted direct run from A27 to A29 is essentially no longer observed. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The signs are direct and mutually consistent, though they describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations: the three activities appear from zero with solid support (58, 82 and 61 of 200 traces) and are mutually exclusive; A27's and A28's dominant outgoing transitions are redirected to them while the order relations hold in every co-occurrence (A27 first, the new activity before A29, mixed around A28); and the step distances between A27, A28 and A29 widen by about one step exactly as expected when an activity is placed between them. The volume of flagged evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 28 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.56 would be expected by chance across 113 tested units. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether A19, A20 and A25 were newly added to the process or already existed but were never triggered in the earlier window, nor what determines which of the three runs or on which side of A28 it is placed. The main competing explanation — that the earlier window simply sampled a variant in which these activities never ran — is implausible given their complete absence in 200 earlier traces against later shares of 29–41 in 100, but it cannot be fully ruled out. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006]

Other differences: only minor position shifts — A27's median position moves slightly earlier (about 0.48 to 0.46) and A29's slightly later (about 0.56 to 0.57) — which are consistent with the new step rather than a separate change. [POS004, POS005]
