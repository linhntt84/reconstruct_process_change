### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 became an executed activity in the later window, running in about half of the cases and placed between existing activities: it now receives flow mainly from A08 and A09 (less often A06, A12, A05) and passes it on mainly to A05, A03, A06 and A15, so that the previously immediate hand-overs A08→A03 and A09→A03 are no longer observed and distances to A05 and A12 lengthened. The signs are direct and consistent: participation rises from 0 to about 101 in 200 traces [OCC001], A01 appears as a new successor of five activities [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007], co-occurrence with seven partners rises from never to about 32–51 in 100 with A01 mostly first [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007], and the loss of immediate succession with longer lags matches that placement [LAG002, LAG005, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007]. With 25 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected if nothing had changed, a chance explanation of the pattern as a whole is implausible. Limitations: the many items largely describe one underlying behaviour rather than independent confirmations; the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely new activity from a relabelled one, and it does not establish a single fixed insertion point, since A01 precedes different partners in different traces and is interleaved or repeated with them in about 18–33 in 100 of the pair profiles.

Other differences: A03 and A16 changed their outgoing shares (A03 away from A05 toward A12 and A02; A16 strongly toward A02), and A03 now follows A08 at shorter lags; these may be side effects of the same restructuring or independent routing changes, which the evidence cannot separate [DIR002, DIR008, LAG001].
