### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural rework around the A04 loop and the end of the trace. Earlier, A05 acted as an optional detour from A04 — A04 passed to A05 in about 46 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and A05 returned to A04 in about 99 in 100 — whereas in the later window A05 is unobserved and A04 instead repeats directly (about 49 in 100 of its outgoing transitions are self-transitions, with back-to-back repetitions at distance one now the most common spacing). The screening count alone (16 control-flow items against roughly 0.09 expected without change) rules out chance as an overall explanation, and the key claims rest on direct participation and relational evidence rather than on aggregate frequencies alone: A05's participation fell from 93 of 200 traces to 0 of 200, A07-to-A01 is observed in 100 in 100 later traces, and A04's branch structure shifted from A05 to itself while A06 and A02 stayed put.
