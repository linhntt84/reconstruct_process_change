- A19 and A25 were not observed at all in the earlier window, and A20 appeared in only about 1 in 200 traces; in the later window A19 occurs in about 38 in 100 traces, A25 in about 35 in 100 and A20 in about 30 in 100, each roughly once per trace where it appears and essentially never repeated. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new step was inserted between A41 and A26: in the earlier window a case typically ran A41 and then A26 directly, while in the later window A41 is followed by exactly one of three alternative activities — A19, A20 or A25 — each of which leads on to A26, so that A26 now follows A41 two positions later instead of one. This is shown directly by A41's successor distribution, the A41-to-A26 distance, and the co-occurrence and fixed ordering of the new activities with A41 and A26; it is consistent across occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators, and rests on ample observations (about 200 traces for the relation and lag items, 202–203 of A41's outgoing transitions, and 59–76 traces containing each new activity). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]

The volume of qualifying evidence also exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 21 items qualified where about 0.5 would be expected by chance across the 104 tested units, and activities absent from 200 earlier traces appearing in about a third of 200 later traces is not a rare-variant artefact. What the evidence cannot establish is what selects among A19, A20 and A25 in a given case, and it cannot rule out that the two windows sample case populations that differ in other, unrecorded respects. [REL001, REL002, REL005]

Other differences: A02's outgoing transitions no longer include A26 (about 26 in 100 earlier) and are spread over more successors in the later window, but this rests on only about 35–44 outgoing transitions and is weak. [DIR001]
