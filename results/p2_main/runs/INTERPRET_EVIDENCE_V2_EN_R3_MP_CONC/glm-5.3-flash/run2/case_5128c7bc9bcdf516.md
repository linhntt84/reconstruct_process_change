### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the six activities A15, A17, A18, A19, A20 and A21 appeared in every case but with no fixed order between many pairs: A17 preceded A19 in about 50 of 100 cases and followed it in the other 50, A15 preceded A21 in about 50 of 100, and A18 preceded A19 in only about 12 of 100. In the later window, every one of these pairs runs in a single direction — A17 before A15, A15 before A18, A18 before A19, A19 before A21, A21 before A20 — in at least 199 of 200 cases each. [REL001, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008]
- Successor choices collapsed onto single targets. Before, A15's outgoing transitions went to A18 in about 62 of 100 cases and to A21, A19 or A20 in the remainder; after, about 995 in 1000 go to A18. The same holds for A17 → A15 (about 56 to about 995 in 1000), A18 → A19 (about 12 to about 995 in 1000, while A18 → A27 fell from about 53 to under 1 in 100), A19 → A21 (about 56 to about 995 in 1000), A21 → A20 (about 66 to all), A20 → A27 (about 47 to about 995 in 1000) and A26 → A17 (about 50 to all; A26 previously led to A19 about half the time). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]
- Distances between activities became fixed at a single value. A15 reached A18 within one step in about 62 of 100 cases before versus about 995 in 1000 after; A17 reached A20 at one-to-five steps before but at exactly five steps after; A15 reached A27 at two-to-five steps before but at five steps after; A18 reached A27 directly in about 53 of 100 before but at four steps after. Pairs running against the new sequence largely vanished: A19 before A15, A19 before A18, A20 before A18 and A21 before A18 occurred within five steps in roughly 100–180 of the 200 earlier traces but in at most 1 of 200 later traces. [LAG001, LAG003, LAG005, LAG009, LAG013, LAG014, LAG015, LAG019, LAG021]
- Relative positions shifted consistently with the new sequence: A15, A17 and A18 now occur earlier (mean relative position about 0.64 to 0.59, 0.59 to 0.56 and 0.68 to 0.62), while A19, A20 and A21 occur later (about 0.59 to 0.65, 0.68 to 0.71 and 0.64 to 0.68), and the spread of positions narrowed for all six. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Participation itself did not change: every activity involved still occurs in all 200 traces of both windows and each pair still co-occurs in every case, so the difference lies in ordering, not in which activities run. [REL001, DIR001, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a specific possibility: the ordering constraints among A15, A17, A18, A19, A20 and A21 changed from a flexible, per-case-varying order to one fixed sequence, A17 → A15 → A18 → A19 → A21 → A20, with A26 now always feeding into A17 at the start and A27 now always following A20 at the end (previously A27 followed A18 directly in about half of A18's outgoing transitions). The relational items show this order change directly for every pair, the successor and distance items collapse onto single targets and lags in the same direction, and the position shifts of all six activities match the new sequence; the number of qualifying items (50 versus about 0.47 expected for an unchanged process) rules out chance sampling as an explanation of the evidence as a whole. Limitations: the DIR, LAG, REL and POS items all describe the same underlying re-sequencing, so they are consistent rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot distinguish whether the earlier flexibility existed within single cases or across a mixture of fixed orderings that the later window replaced with one sequence. [REL001, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL008, DIR001, DIR003, DIR007, LAG013, POS004]
