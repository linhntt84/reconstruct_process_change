### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A02 and A01 take part in the process while A09 does not: A02 appears early in about 87 in 100 cases and typically precedes A12 (and A07/A08), A01 appears mid-case in about 59 in 100 cases and typically precedes A03, and A09 — previously executed in about half the cases and usually leading to A03 — is no longer observed. The step-distance shift of A05→A12 and A13→A12 from one step to two, together with A05 and A13 now routing to A02, indicates A02 now sits between these predecessors and A12, and A16's handover moved from A09 to A01. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR009, REL017, LAG001, LAG006]

The screening flagged 67 of 153 tested units where fewer than 1 would be expected without a change, so chance sampling cannot account for the ensemble, and the participation, routing and step-distance indicators point to the same reading. An important competing explanation remains: zero participation only means an activity was not observed, so the evidence cannot exclude that A01/A02 existed at very low frequency before, or that A09 still occurs but is now too rare to appear in 200 traces; moreover, the many relation items reuse the same traces and are not independent confirmations.
