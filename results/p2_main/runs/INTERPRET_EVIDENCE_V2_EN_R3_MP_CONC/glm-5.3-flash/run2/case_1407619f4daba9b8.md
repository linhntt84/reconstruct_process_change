### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural rerouting in which the exclusive branch after A21 disappeared and its two alternatives became fixed sequential segments: the block A31/A32/A37, previously an alternative to A22 taken in about 46 in 100 cases after A21, is now executed in every case directly after A20 and before A23, while A22, previously taken in about 54 in 100 cases, now always executes after A21 — traces that earlier contained one branch or the other now contain both, in the order A20 → A31/A32/A37 → A23 → A21 → A22 → A26. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL006, REL007, REL008, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Supporting signs: the screening produced 41 qualifying control-flow items against about 0.49 expected without a change, so the volume of differences is far beyond what random re-splits produce, and every item's difference also exceeds about 99.5 in 100 reference comparisons. The rerouting is shown directly by relational and successor evidence — exclusivity becoming co-occurrence, order flips around A21 and A23, successor replacement at A20 and A21, the block's exit changing from A26 to A23, and the A20–A23 gap growing from one to four steps — not by position shifts alone, and occurrence, direction, position, relation and gap items all describe one coherent picture. [screening, OCC001, REL011, DIR001, DIR003, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence establishes the resulting order and frequencies but not which underlying modification produced them — a relocation of the block combined with removal of the branch cannot be distinguished from an equivalent rewiring — and it contains no timing or resource items, so nothing can be said about those aspects; the internal order of A31/A32/A37 remains unfixed in both windows and is reported only as observed.
