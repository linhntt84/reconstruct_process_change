### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window the alternative block A14/A15/A20 — one of which executes in roughly a third of cases — runs after A22/A23 and before A27, whereas before it ran between A40 and A21. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the successors of A14/A15/A20 switched from exclusively A21 to A23/A27, A40 lost them and now reaches A21 directly, A22 and A23 gained them as successors, every measured activity pair reversed or mixed its order exactly as this requires, and the three activities' positions moved from about 0.32 to about 0.59 of the trace while A21/A22/A23 stayed in place. Their participation shares remained roughly stable (about 30–38 in 100 traces in both windows), which fits the same activities changing place rather than disappearing and being added elsewhere. The control-flow family produced 24 qualifying items against about 0.4 expected by chance, so the ensemble is far beyond what random re-splits of a stable process produce, although the items largely describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Limitations: POS and LAG use event positions rather than clock time, so the placement reading rests mainly on the relational and successor evidence; the later window shows the block both before and after A23, so its exact slot relative to A23 varies rather than being fixed. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later occurrences of A14/A15/A20 are a newly added optional branch that replaced the earlier one, rather than the same block changing place; the stable participation shares make this less likely but do not exclude it. [REL003, REL008, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: The elapsed time between consecutive completions of A31 and A22 halved, from about 1.0 hour to about 0.5 hour; this is a timing change in its own right and does not require a structural counterpart. It rests on a single time item (against about 0.01 expected by chance), and since A31 appears in no control-flow item, the evidence cannot show whether or how it relates to the structural difference above.
