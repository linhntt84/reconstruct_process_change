### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a timing change around A08: in the later window, the elapsed time between consecutive completion events leading into A08 lengthened, whether the preceding completion was A02 (median about 0.9 → 1.7 hours) or A06 (median about 0.12 → 0.23 hours), and both gaps became more variable. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

The support is direct and consistent: TWAIT measures exactly this elapsed time, both pairs move in the same direction, and each difference exceeds about 999.5 of 1,000 random re-splits. At the family level, 2 time items qualified where only about 0.01 were expected if nothing had changed, while the control-flow family produced 0 items against about 0.29 expected, so chance alone is an unlikely explanation and no structural re-routing accompanies the timing shift. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

Limitations: both items share A08 and may reflect a single underlying change rather than two independent ones; TWAIT is elapsed time between consecutive completions, so the evidence cannot show whether the added time is waiting, handover or processing; and the pairs occurred somewhat less often in the later window (about 33–35 vs 40–45 of 200 traces), a small difference the control-flow screening did not flag. The evidence covers only these two activity pairs, so a broader timing shift in the process cannot be ruled out. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
