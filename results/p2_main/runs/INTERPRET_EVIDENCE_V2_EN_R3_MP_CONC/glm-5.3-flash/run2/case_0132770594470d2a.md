### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the alternative branch formed by A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 — which previously sat between A29/A30 and A31 and was executed in roughly 15–24 in 100 cases (one of the five per affected case) — is essentially skipped, with flow running directly from A29 through A30 to A31. The signs are direct and consistent: participation of all five collapsed to 0–2 in 100 [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]; A29 and A30 now route almost exclusively to A30 and A31 [DIR009, DIR010]; the A29–A31 distance shortened by one step and A30–A31 became an adjacent pair [LAG007, LAG008, LAG010]; and co-occurrence of the five with A29, A30 and A31 fell to near zero [REL013, REL014, REL015, REL019, REL020, REL021]. Screening also qualifies far more items than an unchanged process would produce (59 against about 0.73 expected), so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits typically generate [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR009, DIR010, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL019, REL020, REL021].

Several items describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations, and zero participation only means the activities were not observed in the later window. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in case mix: if the kinds of cases that used this branch simply no longer occur in the later window, the observed pattern would look the same as the branch being skipped [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, REL013, REL014, REL015].

Other differences: A06 and its path to A47 became rare (about 29 to 7 in 100 traces, with A46 now bypassing it towards A47), and A02 and A10 participation rose modestly [OCC002, DIR011, POS001, OCC001, OCC003].
