- In the earlier window, A17 was a recurring activity: it appeared in about 53 in 100 traces (105 of 200), averaged roughly two occurrences per trace, and repeated within the same trace in about 37 in 100 traces. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change around A17, A01 and A12: in the earlier window A17 was executed in about half of the traces (often repeated, after A12 and before A14/A15), while in the later window A17 is not observed at all, A01 appears in about 38 in 100 traces near the first half of the trace with a wide fan-out of successors, and A12 now leads to A15 and A14 instead of to A17. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS003]

The change is shown directly in participation counts (A17: 105 of 200 traces before versus 0 after; A01: 0 versus 77) and in successor profiles, and it is consistent across the occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators; with 27 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.3 expected without change, sampling variation cannot account for the evidence as a whole. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL006, screening]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A17 was not observed in the later window, so a permanent removal and a temporary non-execution cannot be distinguished; and because A01's observed successors (mainly A09, A07, A12) differ from A17's (A14, A15), the evidence cannot establish that A01 took over A17's role — the disappearance of one and the appearance of the other may be independent changes. [OCC004, DIR001, DIR003]

Other differences: participation fell for A06 (about 52 to 31 in 100 traces) and A08 (about 47 to 30 in 100), and pairwise distances within and between A14 and A15 shortened by about one step, though several of those comparisons rest on only about 20–55 paired observations per window.
