### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the predominant execution order of A30 and A31, between A26 and A27, reversed: in the earlier window the segment typically ran A26 → A31 → A30 → A27, and in the later window it typically runs A26 → A30 → A31 → A27. The signs are direct and consistent: the main successors of A30 and A31 exchanged (A30 from mostly A27 to mostly A31, A31 from mostly A30 to mostly A27) [DIR002, DIR003]; all four position-gap distributions moved one step in the direction the reversal predicts [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]; and A26's direct-successor shares moved accordingly, with A31 dropping from about 31% to about 17% and A30 rising from about 21% to 27% [DIR001]. Screening qualified 7 control-flow items where fewer than one (about 0.33) would be expected without a change, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, so the item set as a whole is unlikely for an unchanged process [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004].

All items also rest on the same trace base (103 and 87 traces) and therefore describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, and the gaps concern event positions within five steps, not clock time.
