### Observed differences

- The activities A04, A06 and A10 each appeared in about 50 in 100 traces in the earlier window but in only about 20–22 in 100 in the later window. Earlier, every trace contained either A04 alone or A06 together with A10 (the two options were strict alternatives, never both, never neither); in the later window, about 67 in 100 traces contain none of the three. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL007, REL010, REL013]

- The block also changed its place in the trace: earlier, A04, A06 and A10 sat at the end (mean relative positions about 0.89–1.0, with A04 the final event in nearly all of its roughly 99 occurrences). In the later window their mean positions are about 0.60–0.64, and A04 now always has a successor. [POS002, POS003, POS005, DIR003]

- The ways the block is entered changed. A02, which earlier sent about half of its outgoing transitions to A04/A06/A10, showed none in the later window and now sends about 46 in 100 to A05 (previously about 23); A03 and A09, which earlier showed no transitions to the block, now send about 45–49 in 100 of their outgoing transitions to it, typically only one to two steps after A03 or A09. - The ways the block is left changed. A04 now leads to A08 in all 47 of its observed outgoing transitions (earlier its only observed successor, on a single transition, was A01); A06 and A10, which earlier each led only to the other, now split roughly 44/56 between each other and A08. A04, A06 and A10 now almost never appear without A08, whereas earlier each frequently did. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, REL008, REL011, REL014]

- A09 and A03 no longer show outgoing transitions to A08 (about 62 and 47 in 100 of their transitions earlier) and favour A07 instead; A08 keeps a stable participation of about 33 in 100 traces but occurs later (mean position about 0.57 → 0.72). [DIR002, DIR005, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the end-of-process block formed by A04 and the A06+A10 pair is, in the later window, executed in only about a third of cases (each activity in about 20 in 100) instead of virtually all, and that when it does run it no longer closes the case: it is inserted mid-trace, entered from A03 or A09 one to two steps after them, and followed by A08, which in turn occurs later than before. Participation, successor, co-occurrence, position and distance indicators all point in the same direction, and the volume of qualifying items (45 control-flow items against roughly 0.34 expected for an unchanged process, each difference larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits) rules out sampling variation as the overall explanation. Limitations: the later-window profiles of the block rest on about 40–50 traces each and some lag shares on fewer than 30 observations, so the exact routing shares are uncertain; positions are event positions, not clock times; and the evidence cannot show whether the reduced execution and the rewiring are one modification or two coupled ones. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window mixes a block-free variant with a partly retained earlier variant, which would look behaviourally identical here.
