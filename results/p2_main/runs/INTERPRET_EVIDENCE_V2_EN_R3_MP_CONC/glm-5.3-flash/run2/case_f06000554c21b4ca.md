- Participation is unchanged: A26, A27, A35, A36 and A41 occur together in 100 in 100 cases in both windows; only their order and placement differ. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the three-activity block A35, A36 and A41 — executed once per case in a varying internal order — is placed differently: in the earlier window it ran after A26 and immediately before A30 (A25, then A27, later A26, then the trio in varying order, then A30), while in the later window it runs directly after A25 and before A27, with A26 now flowing straight to A30 (A25, then the trio in varying order, then A27, later A26, then A30). Since all five activities still occur in every case in both windows, this is a change in ordering and placement, not in participation or in how often existing paths are taken. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

The support is direct and consistent: all six measured order relations flip almost completely (about 98–100 in 100 cases each way, with 200 cases per window), each difference being larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]; the successor redistributions of A25, A26 and the trio cohere with the same rewiring [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]; the A25→A27 and A26→A30 distances exchange values of 1 and about 4 steps [LAG001, LAG003]; and the position shifts point the same way [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]. With 26 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected by chance among 126 tested units, sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole, although these items largely describe one underlying reordering rather than independent changes.

Limitations: the relative order of A27 and A26 themselves was not directly measured as a pair, so the placement of any activities between them rests on position statistics only; and because all evidence is relational, it cannot distinguish whether the trio moved earlier or the A27–A26 segment moved later — both describe the same reversal. A few corroborating gap items rest on 1–2 observations. [LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]
