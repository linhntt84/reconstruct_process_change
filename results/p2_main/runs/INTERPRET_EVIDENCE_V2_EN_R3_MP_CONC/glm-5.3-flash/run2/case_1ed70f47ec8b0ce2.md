### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is that two groups of activities traded places in the flow. In the earlier window, A14, A16 and A19 each sat about one third of the way through the case (mean relative position about 0.34–0.35), while A15, A17 and A18 sat about two thirds through (about 0.64). In the later window this reversed: A15, A17 and A18 sat at about 0.38, and A14, A16 and A19 at about 0.67–0.68. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Every measured order relation between the two groups flipped completely rather than partially. Before, A20 and A24 each ran before A15, A17 and A18 in 100 in 100 traces, and A14, A16 and A19 ran before A20 and A24; in the later window A15, A17 and A18 each ran before A20 and A24 in 100 in 100 traces, and A14, A16 and A19 ran after them. Cross-group pairs flipped the same way; for example, A16 preceded A15 in about 35 in 100 cases in the earlier window, while A15 preceded A16 in about 29 in 100 cases in the later window. [REL001-REL021]
- The connections at the edges of the two groups were rewired. Before, A35's outgoing transitions went to A14, A16 or A19 (about 30–36 in 100 each) and A24's went to A15, A17 or A18 (about 30–37 in 100 each); in the later window A35 fed A15, A17 or A18 and A24 fed A14, A16 or A19. Before, A14, A16 and A19 were each followed by A20 in all of their observed outgoing transitions, and A15, A17 and A18 could each be followed by A25 (about 30–38 in 100); in the later window A14, A16 and A19 were each followed by A25, and A15, A17 and A18 could each be followed by A20 (about 28–37 in 100). [DIR001-DIR008]
- Step distances match a one-activity block and a three-activity block exchanging places: the distance from A35 to A20 grew from 2 to 4 steps, and from A24 to A25 shrank from 4 to 2 steps, in every trace of both windows. [LAG001, LAG002]
- Participation stayed essentially stable: A15, A17 and A18 ran in every trace in both windows, and exactly one of A14, A16 and A19 ran per case (shares of about 30–39 in 100 that sum to about 100 in each window). A20 and A24 kept their places between the groups but drifted about 0.07 later in relative position (A20 mean 0.38 to 0.44; A24 0.57 to 0.64), consistent with three activities now running before A20 instead of one. [POS001-POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two consecutive blocks of activities exchanged their placement in the flow. In the earlier window the sequence ran from A35 through one of A14, A16 or A19 to A20 and A24, then through A15, A17 and A18 to A25; in the later window A15, A17 and A18 ran directly after A35 and before A20, while A14, A16 and A19 ran after A24 and before A25. [REL001-REL021, DIR001-DIR008, POS001-POS006, LAG001, LAG002]

The change is shown directly and consistently: all 21 measured activity pairs reverse from a one-way order in the earlier window to the opposite one-way order in the later window, the entry and exit connections around A35, A20, A24 and A25 are rewired accordingly, and the step-distance changes equal the block sizes (2 to 4 steps and back). Screening tested 93 control-flow units and would produce about 0.46 qualifying items by chance if nothing had changed; 39 items qualified, many times that expectation, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [DIR001-DIR008, POS001-POS008, REL001-REL021, LAG001, LAG002]

The evidence cannot distinguish a literal relocation of the two blocks from an equivalent rewiring of the edges around A35, A20, A24 and A25, since both produce the same observable ordering; the order of the three activities inside each block appears variable in both windows, so the hypothesis concerns where the blocks sit, not their internal sequence; and the small participation shifts of A14, A16 and A19 cannot be separated from sampling variation in 200-trace windows. [POS001, POS003, POS006]

Other differences: none beyond the block exchange and its direct footprint (the later relative position of A20 and A24); the shares of traces containing A14, A16 and A19 moved by about 3–6 in 100 cases between the windows (for example A16 from about 35 in 100 to about 29 in 100), which may be sampling noise rather than a behavioural change. [POS001, POS003, POS006]
