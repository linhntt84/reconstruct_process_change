### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace ran the same fixed chain: A24 was always followed directly by A25 (lag 1 in all 200 traces) and A25 always directly by A26 (lag 1 in all 200 traces); A24 led only to A25 and A25 only to A26. [DIR006, DIR007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, one additional activity runs between A24 and A26 in nearly every case, whereas before, A24, A25 and A26 always ran in immediate succession. This additional step is one of five mutually exclusive alternatives — A14, A15, A20, A21 or A22, each in about 17–23 in 100 traces — it always follows A24 and precedes A26, and it is placed directly after A24 (before A25) in about half of the traces and directly after A25 (before A26) in the other half, while A24, A25 and A26 themselves still occur in every trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR006, DIR007, REL004, REL006, REL009, REL011, REL013, REL015, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The screening produced 34 qualifying items against roughly 0.44 expected if nothing had changed, so the set of differences cannot be explained by sampling alone; occurrence, direction, relation, position and lag indicators all describe this one difference consistently, the redirection of A24 and A25 matches the lag shifts exactly, and each item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [DIR006, DIR007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that the five activities were not observed there, not that they could not occur — a 200-trace sample cannot exclude that they were triggered only under rare conditions before. The per-activity shares rest on 33–45 traces each, and the evidence shows placement only; it cannot distinguish whether the five activities are five distinct steps or variants of a single step. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

Other differences: none — every qualifying item describes the same added step between A24 and A26. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR006, DIR007, REL004, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
