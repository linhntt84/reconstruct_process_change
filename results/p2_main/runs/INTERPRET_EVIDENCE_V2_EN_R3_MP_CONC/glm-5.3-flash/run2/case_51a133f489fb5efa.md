### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the A19/A20/A25 step — of which about one runs per case — is now placed in the early phase between A45 and A26, reached from A45 directly or through A08 and followed almost always by A26, whereas earlier it ran in the late phase after A27 (directly or via A28) and was followed by A28 or A32. A08 is part of this change: it grew from about 13 to about 30 in 100 cases and now acts as an optional step immediately before the block. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL001, REL005, REL007, REL009, LAG014]

The hypothesis is shown directly by relational and positional evidence — the order flip with A26, the near-complete redistribution of predecessors and successors, and the position shift of A19/A20/A25 from about 0.6 to about 0.33 of the trace — and it is consistent across the occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators; 40 qualifying items against roughly 0.72 expected by chance in the control-flow family is far beyond what an unchanged process produces. The participation of A19, A20 and A25 stayed roughly stable (A25 slightly down, 73 to 61 traces), so this is a change of placement rather than of frequency. [POS002, POS003, POS004, REL005, REL007, REL009, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG010, LAG013, LAG014]

The evidence cannot distinguish a genuine relocation of the step from the late-phase executions stopping while the same activities were (re)introduced earlier, nor can it show whether the step keeps its earlier role in the new position; a few lag profiles also rest on very few observations (for example A08→A19, A16→A19 and A19→A26 had only 1 observation in the earlier window), though the hypothesis does not rest on them. [LAG001, LAG003, LAG007]

Other differences: A16→A26 and A18→A26 moved one step later (3→4–5 and 2→3–4 steps), consistent with the same insertion before A26, and A26 and A27 sit slightly later; A48→A49, A50→A51 and A18→A45 changed negligibly. [LAG004, LAG005, POS005, POS006, DIR008, DIR009, LAG006]
