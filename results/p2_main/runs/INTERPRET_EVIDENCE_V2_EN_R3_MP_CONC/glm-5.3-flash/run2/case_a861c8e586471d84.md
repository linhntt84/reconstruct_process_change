### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch formed by A19, A20 and A25 — three mutually exclusive alternatives, each executed in roughly a third of cases between A41 and A26 — is no longer executed in the later window, so that A41 now leads directly to A26 and the group is bypassed. The signs are direct and consistent: the three activities fell from 71/61/70 to 0/1/0 of 200 traces, A41's outgoing transitions shifted from the three alternatives to A26 in about 92 in 100 cases, and the A41→A26 distance shortened from two steps to one; the relational items agree, with the three activities no longer co-occurring with partners that run in every case (A14, A26, A31, A41). The screening context makes a sampling artefact unlikely for the set as a whole: 36 control-flow items qualified where about 0.61 would be expected without a change. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, LAG002, REL004, REL010, REL015, REL019]

The evidence cannot distinguish a permanent removal of this branch from it becoming optional and simply not taken in the later sample, since zero participation only means the activities were not observed; the single residual A20 execution (one trace, continuing to A30 at a later relative position) is too weak to characterise any remaining path. [OCC002, DIR002, POS003]

Other differences: the A24→A31 distance shortened from mostly two steps to mostly one, which is consistent with the same bypass but involves activities outside the main hypothesis, and A14, A26 and A41 show small relative-position shifts. [LAG001, POS001, POS005, POS006]
