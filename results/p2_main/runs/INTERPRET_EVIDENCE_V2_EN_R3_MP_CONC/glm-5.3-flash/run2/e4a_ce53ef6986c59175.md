### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A43 and A28 was restructured from a choice into a mandatory block: earlier, each case executed exactly one of A14, A17, A18, A19 or A20 (about 13–27 in 100 cases each), always followed directly by A28; in the later window those five are unobserved and every case executes A15, A16, A25, A26 and A27 once each, after A43 and before A28, in an order that varies from case to case. This is shown directly and consistently by participation, successor and relational items with substantial support on both sides (27–53 of 200 cases for the old alternatives; 200 of 200 for the new block and for A43 and A28), and the screening context makes a sampling artefact implausible: 92 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.7 would be expected if nothing had changed, and most of these items describe this one underlying restructure rather than independent effects. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, OCC008, DIR011, REL003, REL010, REL018, REL019]

Limitations: zero participation strictly means only that the old activities were not observed in the later window, and because the identifiers are anonymous codes the evidence cannot rule out that A15, A16, A25–A27 are relabelled counterparts of A14 and A17–A20 rather than different work; the behavioural finding — a one-of-five choice replaced by an all-five block without a fixed internal order — holds either way. [OCC001, OCC002, REL010]

Other differences: the elapsed time between consecutive completions of A44 and A45 roughly halved (mean about 1.03 hours to about 0.50 hours, medians about 1.02 to 0.50); this is the single time item qualified where roughly 0.01 was expected without a change, and the evidence provides no link between it and the restructured segment.
