### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment formed by A25, A26, A31, A32 and A33 — previously executed exactly once per case in a largely fixed order (A25 first, A33 last, before A23 and A17) — is now executed as repeated passes: each of the five activities occurs about three times per trace, A33 frequently leads back into A25, and the previously fixed order between the group and A23 has become interleaved or repeated in about two of three traces. Chance is an unlikely explanation for this pattern as a whole: 39 evidence items qualified where roughly 0.5 would be expected for an unchanged process, and the repetition is visible directly in the occurrence counts (1 to about 3 occurrences per trace, recurrence in about 66 in 100 traces) and in the new A33 → A25 flow. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish one repeated pass through the whole group from independent repetition of the individual activities, and it cannot show whether the remaining third of traces kept the old single-pass behaviour or follow some other routing. A competing explanation it cannot rule out is that A17's lower participation (about 46 in 100 later traces vs about 56 in 100 earlier) is an independent routing change rather than a consequence of the repeated group. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]

Other differences: A17 is now skipped more often, and both A17 and A23 sit at later event positions; these shifts are consistent with the repeated group pushing later work back, but the evidence does not establish that link directly. [POS001, POS002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]
