### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 entered the flow between the A03/A06/A11 stage and the A05/A10 stage in the later window: previously unobserved, A01 now runs in about 46 in 100 cases — often twice, around the middle of the trace — receives transitions from A03, A06, A11 and A05, and leads on to A05 and A10. The direct edges that bypass it (A03→A05, A06→A05, A05→A10) remain in use but carry a much smaller share, and every measured distance across the affected segment (A03–A05, A03–A10, A05–A06, A05–A10, A06–A05, A06–A10) lengthens by about one step. After the change, A03, A05, A06 and A10 occur only in traces that also contain A01, whereas the two never coincided before. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

Supporting signs: 18 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.17 expected for an unchanged process, and the routing, co-occurrence and distance items show the new wiring directly and consistently across indicators — with the caveat that they largely describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, and every item's difference also exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL002, REL004, LAG006]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A01 was not observed then. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 already existed but went unrecorded in the earlier window, so "newly added" cannot be distinguished from "newly recorded"; the exact conditions under which A01 repeats also remain unclear from these aggregates.
