### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, each case executes one of two alternative segments between A30 and A31, whereas previously every case executed both in sequence: about 46 in 100 cases run A33, A34, A35, A37 and A38 and then pass to A31, while about 54 in 100 skip that segment and run A39, A40, A41, A42 and A43 between A30 and A31. The mutual exclusivity of the two segments is observed directly in the relational evidence, the participation drops of both segments match the two branch sizes, A30's outgoing transitions split accordingly, and the volume of flagged evidence (93 control-flow items against roughly 1 expected by chance when nothing changed) rules out sampling variation as an explanation of the pattern as a whole. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows sampled different case populations rather than a re-routing within one population. [REL023, REL030, REL041, OCC001, OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL006, REL010, REL015]

Other differences: the positional shifts (A30 and the earlier-group activities later, the later-group activities slightly earlier) are consistent with the branch structure, since cases running only one segment contain fewer activities, but they are position-based and not independent confirmation; the lag-distribution items each rest on about one observation in one window and are not treated as separate findings.
