### Observed differences

- Two groups of activities exchanged their places in the case sequence. In the earlier window, A35 led into one of the three alternative activities A14, A16, A19 (occurring in about 30, 35 and 36 in 100 cases respectively — together exactly one per case), and each of them was always followed by A20. In the later window, A14, A16 and A19 sit later in the case and each is always followed by A25 instead, while A24 now leads into them. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008]
- The cluster A15, A17, A18 (present in every case in both windows) moved the opposite way. Earlier, about 30–38 out of 100 outgoing transitions of these activities went to A25 and none went to A20; in the later window, about 28–37 out of 100 go to A20 and none to A25, while their transitions among themselves stay similar. A35, which earlier led into the A14/A16/A19 branch, now leads into this cluster. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, DIR008]
- Event positions mirror the exchange: A14, A16 and A19 moved from a mean position of about 0.34–0.35 of the case to about 0.67–0.68, while A15, A17 and A18 moved from about 0.64 to about 0.38; A20 and A24 each shifted later by roughly 0.07 (0.38→0.44 and 0.57→0.64). - Pairwise order reversed wherever the groups meet: A20 preceded A15 in all cases earlier and follows it in all cases later; A14 preceded A15, A17, A18, A20 and A24 in the roughly 30 in 100 cases where they co-occurred earlier, and follows each of them in roughly 33 in 100 later; analogous complete reversals hold for A16 and A19 against the cluster members, A20 and A24. - Distances changed consistently with the exchange: the gap from A24 to A25 shrank from 4 to 2 event steps, and the gap from A35 to A20 grew from 2 to 4 steps. [LAG001, LAG002]
- On the timing side, the elapsed time between consecutive completions rose on both transitions into A08: from A02 the mean rose from about 0.9 to about 1.7 hours, and from A06 from about 0.12 to about 0.26 hours (based on 26 vs 28 and 37 vs 27 observations). [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two fragments exchanged their placement in the process: the alternative branch formed by A14, A16 and A19 (exactly one of them executed per case in both windows) and the always-executed cluster A15, A17, A18 swapped positions. Earlier, the branch sat between A35 and A20 and the cluster between A24 and A25; in the later window the cluster sits between A35 and A20 and the branch between A24 and A25, with the branch now exiting to A25 and the cluster exiting to A20. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG001, LAG002]

The change is shown directly and consistently: successor sets were replaced rather than re-weighted (A14/A16/A19 led to A20 in all their outgoing transitions earlier and to A25 in all later; the cluster's exits to A25 disappeared in favour of A20), the pair relations show complete order reversals rather than shifts between fixed and mixed orders, the two groups moved to opposite positions, and the gap changes match. The cluster's internal wiring and the one-of-three branch participation are preserved, which fits an exchange of placement rather than activities being added, removed or made optional. With 39 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.5 expected if nothing had changed, sampling noise cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL008, REL015, POS001, POS002, POS006, LAG001, LAG002]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that each window captured a single, different pre-existing way of executing the process rather than a newly introduced arrangement, since both windows show one fully deterministic ordering; the completeness and cross-indicator consistency of the reversal make a rare-variant sampling artefact unlikely, but the evidence cannot exclude it. Positions are event ranks, so the conclusion concerns sequence placement, not clock time. [REL008, REL015, POS007]

Other differences: the elapsed time between consecutive completions into A08 roughly doubled on both incoming transitions — from A02 (mean about 0.9 to about 1.7 hours) and from A06 (about 0.12 to about 0.26 hours). This timing change is a hypothesis in its own right and is not part of the re-sequencing above; it rests on few observations (26 vs 28 and 37 vs 27), although two qualifying time items against roughly 0.01 expected makes pure chance unlikely. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
