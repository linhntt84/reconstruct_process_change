### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured: in the earlier window, A39 was followed by exactly one of three alternative activities — A14, A15 or A16 — each leading on to A24, whereas in the later window A14, A15 and A16 were not exercised at all, and A21, A22 and A23 each ran once in every case between A39 and A24 in varying order, lengthening the A39–A24 span from two to four steps and moving A24 slightly later in the case. [DIR007, LAG001, OCC001, OCC004, REL021, POS007]

The screening comparison supports treating these differences as more than sampling noise: 48 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.5 would be expected if nothing had changed, and the occurrence, direction, relation, position and lag indicators all describe the same replacement consistently and directly rather than through a single unobserved relation.
