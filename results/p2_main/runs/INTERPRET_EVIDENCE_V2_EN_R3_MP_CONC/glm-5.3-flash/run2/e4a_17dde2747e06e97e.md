- Before, A14, A15 and A20 each ran exactly once in every trace; in the later window each appears in only about 53 in 100 traces (106 of 200) and never more than once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the block A14 → A15 → A20 between A36 and A21 is skipped in about 47 in 100 cases: A36 leads directly on to A21 instead of into the block, whereas before, every trace executed the full sequence A36 → A14 → A15 → A20 → A21. The three block activities thus run in about half as many cases and appear to have become optional, while A36 and A21 remain mandatory. [DIR001, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]

The support is direct and internally consistent: the new A36 → A21 transition, the new one-step A36–A21 distance, and the joint absence of A14, A15 and A20 all concern the same share of traces, the block's internal order is preserved whenever it does run, and sixteen control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.35 would be expected for an unchanged process, with 2 time items against roughly 0.01 expected. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, POS001, POS002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001, TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window draws from a shifted mixture of two case cohorts, one of which never contained the block; this would look the same as a newly available skip at this sample size. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: The elapsed time from A36 to A14 roughly doubled on the traces that still run the block, a timing change in its own right that the bypass alone does not explain, although its observation count fell from 200 to 106 in line with the skipped cases. [TWAIT002] The elapsed time between consecutive completions of A23 and A24 halved with unchanged counts; the evidence does not connect this timing change to the skipped block. [TWAIT001] The small position shifts of A21 and A36 are consistent with traces becoming shorter or longer and are not, by themselves, evidence that either activity was moved. [POS001, POS002]
