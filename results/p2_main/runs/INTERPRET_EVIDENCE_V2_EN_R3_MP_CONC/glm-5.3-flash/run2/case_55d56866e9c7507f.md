### Observed differences

- A11 was executed in about 29 in 100 traces in the earlier window and in about 57 in 100 traces in the later window (58 vs 113 of 200 traces), never repeating within a trace; A09 rose from about 30 to about 44 in 100 traces. [OCC001, POS002]
- A09 and A11 never occur in the same trace in either window, so they behave as alternatives; in the earlier window neither of them appeared in about 42 in 100 traces, while in the later window every trace contains exactly one of the two. [REL003]
- The way the A09/A11 branch is entered changed. In the earlier window, A11 appeared only together with A08 and always after it (co-occurrence about 29 in 100 traces; A11 without A08 was not observed), late in the trace (mean relative position about 0.96). In the later window, A11 appears without A08 in about 27 in 100 traces, sits earlier (mean relative position about 0.74), and when A08 and A11 both occur their order is mixed (about 16 vs 15 in 100 traces). [REL002, POS003]
- From A06, the share of outgoing transitions to A11 rose from about 4% to about 31% and to A09 from about 6% to about 23%, while shares to A03, A02, A08 and A10 fell (for example A03 from about 28% to 17% and A08 from about 15% to 7%); this profile rests on 186 vs 200 traces. [DIR002]
- Succession tightened around A06: it precedes A11 in every trace where both occur in the later window (about 57 in 100 traces, up from about 27), A06 is followed by A11 within one step in about 55% of such pairs (about 13% before) and by A09 within one step in about 53% (about 22% before), while A06-to-A08 gaps lengthened (within one or two steps from about 80% to about 38%). [REL001, LAG003, LAG002, LAG001]
- From A08, transitions to A09 fell from about 31% to about 11% and to A11 from about 32% to about 17%, while transitions to A02 rose from about 15% to about 33% and to A06 from about 9% to about 23%; A08-to-A11 succession also loosened (within one step from about 66% to about 39%). [DIR003, LAG004]
- Smaller, less certain differences: A05's successors shifted from mostly A01 (about 67%) to mostly A09 and A11 (about 38% each), on only 9 vs 26 observations; the outgoing profiles of A09 and A11 spread across more successors, with earlier-window profiles based on only about 7–10 observations; A08 sits slightly later in the trace (mean relative position about 0.66 vs 0.75). [DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative branch formed by A09 and A11 is now entered from A06 instead of after A08, and that the choice between the two is made in every case. In the earlier window, A09 or A11 was executed in about 59 in 100 traces, typically after A08 and late in the trace, with neither executed in about 42 in 100 traces; in the later window, one of the two is executed in every trace, usually immediately after A06 and earlier in the trace, while A08 now more often continues to A02 or back to A06 rather than leading to A09 or A11. [DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG002, LAG003, POS002, POS003, OCC001]

Chance is an unlikely explanation for the pattern as a whole: 16 control-flow items qualified from 32 tested units, against about 0.16 expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeded about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. The main limitation is that these items are not independent confirmations — the direction, lag, position and relation results for the same activities largely reflect one underlying trace behaviour — and the earlier-window successor profiles for A05, A09 and A11 rest on about 7–10 observations, so those finer details may be sampling noise. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that part of the rise in A06–A09/A11 co-occurrence is a mechanical consequence of A09 and A11 simply being executed more often; however, A11's earlier position, its occurrence without A08, and the redirection of A08's own outgoing transitions fit a change in where the branch is entered rather than a pure frequency increase. [REL002, POS003, DIR003]

Other differences: A05 now leads mostly to A09 and A11 instead of mostly to A01, though this rests on few observations; A09 and A11 themselves now distribute their outgoing transitions across more activities (including A04, A05, A07, A08 and A10); A08 sits slightly later in the trace. [DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, POS001]
