### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the mandatory five-activity stage A20, A21, A30, A31, A32 — executed by every earlier trace between A48/A04 and A33 — is no longer performed, and in the same stage a single optional activity is now drawn from five mutually exclusive alternatives (A19, A22, A23, A24, A25), each running in roughly one in five traces and leading almost directly to A33; A48 feeds these alternatives directly instead of routing through A04, whose participation falls from about 76 to about 20 in 100 traces. [OCC006, OCC007, OCC012, OCC013, OCC014, OCC005, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011, DIR014, DIR005, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, LAG010, OCC001]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: participation flips of 200 of 200 to 0–2 of 200, and 0 to 32–47 of 200, are far beyond what sampling variation in 200-trace windows produces; the rewiring is observed in the outgoing transitions of A48, A04 and the new activities; the relational items show the old co-occurrences collapsing while the alternatives take over the slot between A14/A15 and A33; and the A48-to-A33 distance shrinking to 2 steps in most later traces fits one activity replacing five. With 165 qualifying items against about 1.3 expected if nothing had changed, chance cannot account for the evidence as a whole. [OCC006, OCC008, DIR014, DIR008, REL017, REL019, LAG010]

Limitations: the evidence compares two windows and cannot show when or how the difference came about; in particular, it cannot rule out that the two windows sample two coexisting case types whose proportions shifted, rather than a modification applied at one point. A few details rest on very few observations: the residual occurrences of A30, A31 and A32 (1–2 traces) and their outgoing edges, and A04's new profile (39 traces). [OCC012, OCC013, OCC014, DIR013, OCC001]
