### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the control flow was restructured so that A02, A03 and A08 are no longer executed and the paths that ran through them are rewired: the earlier connection A05 → A02 → A10 → A08 → A11 now runs A05 or A18 → A10 → A01 → A11; A13 and A19, which earlier always led via A03 to A05 and A18, now lead to them directly; and A01 vacated its former position after A16 — A16 now connects straight to A06, A07 and A14 — while A07 and A14 shrank to rare occurrences that only appear as the final step of a trace and no longer co-occur. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR008, DIR009, DIR011, DIR013, DIR014, REL036]

The screening context makes chance an implausible explanation: 129 control-flow items qualified where fewer than one would be expected without a change. The core differences are also direct and mutually consistent: the disappearances rest on 94, 94 and 41 supporting traces in the earlier window, too common to be sampling artefacts; the successor changes sit exactly on the edges touching the vanished activities and are backed by roughly 150–170 outgoing-transition observations for the main rerouted sources; and the relational, positional and distance items move in the direction the rewiring predicts (A10 followed by A01 within one step in about 57 in 100 later cases versus four steps earlier; A16 followed by A01 within one step in about 56 in 100 earlier cases versus five steps apart in about 83 in 100 later cases). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, DIR004, DIR008, DIR013, REL005, POS001, LAG022, LAG033]

Limitations: zero participation shows only that A02, A03 and A08 were not observed in the later sample, not that they cannot occur, and the design cannot rule out that the two windows draw different case mixes — for example different variants entering the log — rather than a change at a point in time; the A16–A01 distance item also rests on only 15 supporting traces in the later window. [OCC001, LAG033]

Other differences: beyond the rewiring above, A09 and A17 gained direct transitions to each other (about 51 and 49 in 100 of their outgoing transitions respectively) that were not observed earlier. [DIR007, DIR012]
