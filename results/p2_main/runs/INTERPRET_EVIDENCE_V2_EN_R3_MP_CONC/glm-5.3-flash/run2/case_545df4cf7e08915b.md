### Observed differences

- **A01 out, A08 in.** In the earlier window, A01 ran in about 48 in 100 traces (about once per trace, repeating within the trace in roughly a quarter of the cases containing it); in the later window, A01 is not observed at all. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the control flow was rewired so that A08 now runs where A01 used to: in the earlier window A01 sat between A03/A02/A17 and the branch to A09/A10/A16 in roughly half of the traces, while in the later window A01 is not observed and A08 occupies the same position with the same successor set, with A17 always, and A03 and A02 occasionally, now leading into A08. The signs are direct rather than circumstantial: the observed outgoing transitions of A17, A03 and A02 changed target, the successor profiles and positions of A01 and A08 match closely across windows, and the relation pattern flips from "A01 without A08" to "A08 without A01" in about 48 in 100 traces on both sides. Fifty-three control-flow items qualified against 0.48 expected without change, so the set of differences is far beyond what random re-splits of stable behaviour produce, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR005, DIR008, DIR003, DIR002, REL003, REL011]

Limitations: non-observation only shows that A01 did not appear in the later sample, not that it was removed from the process, and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows capture different mixtures of pre-existing variants rather than a single modification at the boundary; it also cannot show whether A01 and A08 are genuinely distinct steps or the same step recorded under different labels. [OCC001, OCC002, REL003]

Other differences: A11 and A15 exchanged places, a second directly observed structural change. Earlier, A11 was the terminal activity after A05 (always the final event, in about 52 in 100 traces) and A15 ran mid-flow, fed by A05 and A18, always leading back to A03; in the later window, A15 is the terminal activity after A05 (always the final event, about 52 in 100) and A11 leads back to A03, with A18 now feeding A11 instead of A15; the step-distance distributions mirror this swap on all four measured activity pairs. [POS003, POS004, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, DIR009, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

A further minor difference outside both hypotheses is the slight redistribution of A03's and A02's outgoing shares noted above. [DIR003, DIR002]
