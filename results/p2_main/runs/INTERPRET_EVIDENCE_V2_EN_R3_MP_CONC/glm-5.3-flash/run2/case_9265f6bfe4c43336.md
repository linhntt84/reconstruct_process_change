### Observed differences

- The five activities A16–A20 each appeared in about 85 in 100 traces in the earlier window but in only about 18 in 100 in the later window; each occurred at most once per trace in both windows (no repetition in either). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- The five activities A21–A25 show the mirror image: each appeared in about 16 in 100 traces in the earlier window and in about 82 in 100 in the later window. [OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010]
- The two groups behave as strict alternatives: a pair of one A16–A20 activity and one A21–A25 activity never occurs in the same trace in either window — earlier, about 85 in 100 traces contain only the A16–A20 partner; later, about 82 in 100 contain only the A21–A25 partner. [REL015, REL019, REL024, REL032, REL039, REL045]
- A15's routing flipped: earlier, about 85 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions led to A19 and about 15 in 100 to A24; in the later window, about 82 in 100 led to A24 and about 18 in 100 to A19. [DIR001]
- A15 ran together with A16–A20 in about 85 in 100 traces earlier (always with A15 first) but only about 18 in 100 later; with A21–A25 the reverse holds — about 15 in 100 earlier, about 82 in 100 later, again with A15 first. [REL001, REL005, REL006, REL010]
- A29 occurred in essentially all traces in both windows; earlier it co-occurred with the A16–A20 block in about 85 in 100 traces, later with the A21–A25 block in about 82 in 100 (A21 before A29 in about 47 in 100, interleaved in about 35 in 100). [REL020, REL029, REL050, REL055, REL059, REL065]
- Each block kept its internal order in both windows: earlier A19 preceded A16, A17 and A18 (which ran in no fixed order among themselves) and A16–A18 preceded A20; later A24 preceded A21, A22 and A23 (again unordered among themselves) and A21, A23 and A24 preceded A25 — and these orderings also hold in the minority window for each block. [REL011, REL013, REL014, REL021, REL030, REL038, REL051, REL053, REL054, REL056, REL060, REL063]
- Two weaker signals: A22's outgoing shares redistributed (A21 down from about 58 in 100 to about 31 in 100; A23 and A25 up to about 36 and 33 in 100), based on only 31 earlier observations; and the A18→A17 position gap was one step in about 82 in 100 later cases versus about 54 in 100 earlier, based on only 17 later observations. [DIR002, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the two windows differ in how often two existing alternative paths are taken. Earlier, roughly 85 in 100 cases ran the path built around A16–A20 — entered from A15, typically via A19, with A16/A17/A18 in no fixed order, closing with A20, and running alongside A29 — while roughly 16 in 100 ran the alternative path built around A21–A25. In the later window the shares reversed: about 82 in 100 took the A21–A25 path (entered from A15 via A24, closing with A25, alongside A29) and about 18 in 100 took the A16–A20 path. [OCC001, OCC006, DIR001, REL001, REL006, REL015, REL020, REL055]

This reading is direct and consistent: 78 control-flow items qualified where fewer than 1 would be expected if nothing had changed, every item's difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits, and the participation, routing and relational indicators all show the same reversal. Both blocks, A15 and A29 are observed in substantial shares in both windows, and each block's internal order is unchanged, which fits a shift between existing alternatives rather than activities being added, removed or bypassed. Note, however, that the many items overlap heavily and largely re-express one underlying swap rather than serving as independent confirmations. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC006, REL001, REL006, REL020, REL055]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows simply contain different mixes of cases (for example, case types that take different paths) rather than a modified process, since only the two aggregate windows are available. [OCC001, OCC006]

Other differences: A22's outgoing transition shares shifted toward A23 and A25, and the A18→A17 position gap concentrated at one step; both rest on 31 or fewer observations in one window and should be treated as tentative. [DIR002, LAG001]
