### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, A01 appears as a new step between A08 and A10: the earlier direct hand-off from A08 to A10 (about 55 in 100 of A08's outgoing transitions) now runs from A08 to A01 and then from A01 to A10, with A01 present in about 21 in 100 traces around mid-trace and always followed by A10. Nine control-flow items qualified in total, far more than the roughly 0.13 expected if nothing had changed, and this hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: the edge from A08 to A10 was replaced by an edge to A01 while the share to A09 stayed the same, A01 always leads to A10, A01 co-occurs with A08 after A08 and with A10 before A10, and the A08-to-A10 distance grew by exactly one step. [DIR001, DIR003, LAG001, OCC001, POS001, REL001, REL002]

The main competing explanation that cannot be fully excluded is that A01 already existed earlier but was too rare to be observed, since zero participation only means it was not seen in that window; the contrast of 0 of 200 versus 42 of 200 traces and the coordinated edge and distance changes make this unlikely. The interleaved-or-repeated shares in the co-occurrence data also leave open a partly parallel arrangement rather than a strictly sequential placement. [OCC001, REL001, REL002]

Other differences: A03, A12 and A13 were rewired independently of A01 — earlier, A03 split its outgoing transitions between A13 (about two-thirds) and A12, with A12 leading back to A03, whereas later A03 always leads to A13 and A12 leads to A13, and A03's observed outgoing transitions dropped from about 47 to about 20, consistent with less traffic through A03 once the return edge from A12 is no longer observed.
