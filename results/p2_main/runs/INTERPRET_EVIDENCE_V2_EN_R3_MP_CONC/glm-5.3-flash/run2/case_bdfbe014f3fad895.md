### Observed differences

- Three activities that never appeared in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces) — A32, A33 and A38 — each occur in about 50 in 100 traces in the later window, around the middle of the trace (mean relative event position about 0.5) [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, POS003, POS004, POS005].

- The segment between A26 and A28 changed: before, about 95 in 100 of A26's outgoing transitions went to A27, which then always preceded A28 (about 90 in 100 at a distance of two steps); in the later window about 51 in 100 go to A27 and about 15–18 in 100 each to A32, A33 and A38, each of which precedes A28 in about 48 in 100 traces, and the A26-to-A28 distance is now about half two steps and about 46 in 100 four steps. A28 itself still runs in every trace in both windows [DIR006, REL017, REL021, LAG003, REL025, REL026, REL027, POS002].
- The three new activities occur together in about 49 in 100 later traces (never before), in both orders in similar proportions, and each can be followed by either of the others or by A28, so they run without a fixed order among themselves [REL028, REL029, REL030, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009].
- A04 rose from about 19 in 100 earlier traces to about 60 in 100 later traces, and from around the middle of the trace (mean relative event position about 0.54) to near the start (about 0.21) [OCC001, POS001].
- A04's surroundings changed: A14 now routes to A04 in about 54 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (before about 4 in 100, with routes to A15 falling from about 87 to about 44 in 100), A04 immediately follows A14 in about 96 in 100 of the observed pairs, and A04 in turn leads to A15 in about 87 in 100 of its transitions, whereas before its successors were spread over many activities (largest share about 20 in 100) [DIR004, LAG001, DIR001, REL001, REL002].

- Smaller, weakly supported redistributions appear in A10's outgoing transitions (about 28 and 22 observations in the two windows) [DIR003].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the middle of the process changed structurally around A26, A27, A28, A32, A33 and A38: in the earlier window every trace ran the fixed sequence A26, A27, A28, while in the later window about half the traces still take A27 (unchanged in its position between A26 and A28) and the other half skip A27 and instead run the three previously unobserved activities A32, A33 and A38 between A26 and A28, without a fixed order among themselves. This is shown directly by the routing split out of A26, by the co-occurrence and order relations (A27 and each new activity together in only about 1 in 100 traces; each new activity before A28 in about 48 in 100), and by the step distance between A26 and A28 doubling in about half the traces [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, REL017, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030, LAG003].

The reading is consistent across participation, routing, relational and distance indicators, the key relational profiles rest on about 200 traces per window, and the screening produced 55 qualifying items where fewer than 1 would be expected if nothing had changed, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole [OCC003, DIR006, REL022, REL025, LAG003]. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the three-activity block existed before as a rare path: with 200 earlier traces, a path taken in roughly 1–2 in 100 cases or fewer would go unobserved [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006].

Other differences: A04 now runs between A14 and A15 in roughly half the later traces — it appears in about 60 in 100 traces (about 19 in 100 before), sits near the start of the trace, immediately follows A14 in about 96 in 100 observed pairs, usually precedes A15, and leads to A15 in about 87 in 100 of its transitions — whereas before, A14 led to A15 directly in about 87 in 100 of its transitions and A04 was a rare, mid-trace activity [OCC001, POS001, DIR004, LAG001, REL001, REL002, DIR001]. A06 declined from about 20 to about 4 in 100 traces with a changed successor profile, though the later profile rests on only about 8 transitions [OCC002, DIR002]. A10 shows a smaller outgoing redistribution on few observations [DIR003].
