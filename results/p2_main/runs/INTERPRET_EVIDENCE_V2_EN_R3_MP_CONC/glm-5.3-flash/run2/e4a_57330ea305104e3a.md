### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A31 and A32 typically execute in the opposite order relative to each other and to A28: in the earlier window the flow typically ran from A32 to A31 and then closed to A28, whereas in the later window it typically runs from A31 to A32 and then closes to A28. The entry from A27 moved in step, now reaching the block at A31 (about 28 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, up from about 16) rather than at A32 (about 18 in 100, down from about 29). Supporting signs: the control-flow family yielded 3 items against a chance expectation of about 0.29, each routing change is unusual for random re-splits (strength about 0.995), and every involved transition is observed in both windows (about 90 outgoing transitions of A31 and of A32 per window; A27 in essentially all traces), so this is a shift between existing behaviours rather than the appearance of a rare variant. Limitations: DIR shares are computed over outgoing transitions rather than cases, and the two mirrored items likely describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations.
