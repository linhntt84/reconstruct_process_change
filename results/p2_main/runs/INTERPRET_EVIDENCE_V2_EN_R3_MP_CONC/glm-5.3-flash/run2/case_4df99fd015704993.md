- Two activities are new in the later window: A02, previously unobserved, now runs in about 39 in 100 traces (never twice in one trace), and A01, also previously unobserved, now runs in about 28 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural rework of the branch around A03: A02 now runs between A03 and A13, and A14 no longer runs in the A03–A12–A14 repetition, so that A12 leads directly back to A03. Earlier, A03-led traces went from A03 either straight to A13 or into a three-step A03→A12→A14→A03 cycle; in the later window they go from A03 to A02 (which always leads on to A13) or into a two-step A03→A12→A03 cycle. This is not what an unchanged process typically produces: 40 control-flow items qualified where about 0.32 would be expected by chance, each item rests on dozens of observations in the window where it occurs, and every item's difference exceeds what random re-splits of the same traces produce. The hypothesis rests on direct evidence: the successor changes at A03, A02, A12 and A14, the appearance and disappearance of participation (A02 from 0 to about 78 of 200 traces; A14 from about 51 to 0), and step-distance shifts that match the new wiring (A03-to-A13 from one step to two; A03-to-A03 and A12-to-A12 from three steps to about two; A12-to-A03 from two steps to one). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, OCC002, OCC007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Other differences: A01 appeared in about 28 in 100 later traces, but no directional or relational evidence shows where it runs or what precedes or follows it. The shrinkage of both pre-existing branches, and the roughly 28 in 100 later traces containing neither A03 nor A07/A09/A10 (a situation not observed earlier), are additional changes not explained by the rewiring alone. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL013, REL016, REL019]

The evidence cannot rule out that the branch shrinkage and the neither-group traces reflect a changed mix of trace variants — for example an additional path entering the process, possibly involving A01 — rather than only the rework described above. Two further limitations: A03 and A02 do not stand in a single fixed order (in about 19 in 100 later traces their order is interleaved or repeated, likely because A03 still repeats via A12), and A03 still sends about 46 in 100 of its outgoing steps to A12, so A02 need not follow A03 on every pass. [REL001, DIR002, REL008]
