### Observed differences

- Participation: in the earlier window, A27 and each of A36, A37, A42, A43 and A44 appeared in only about half of the cases (49–53 in 100, at most once per case); in the later window, each of the six appears in every case (200 of 200), still at most once per case. [OCC001–OCC006]
- In the earlier window, A27 and each of the five group activities were alternatives: each appeared without the other in about 48–51 in 100 cases, and they co-occurred in only about 1 in 100; in the later window they co-occur in 100 in 100 cases, with the group activity before A27 in about 99 in 100. [REL015–REL019]
- Routing: earlier, A26's observed outgoing transitions went about 48 in 100 to A27 and about 52 in 100 into the group; later, about 98 in 100 go to A27. Earlier, A25 led to A28 in about 98 in 100 of its outgoing transitions; later it leads into the group (about 15–24 in 100 per member) and no longer to A28. [DIR001, DIR002]
- Successors: earlier, each group activity led to A31 in about 14–27 in 100 of its outgoing transitions; later that edge is unobserved and each leads to A28 in about 14–24 in 100 instead, while A27 still leads to A31 (about 96 in 100 earlier, 98 in 100 later). [DIR003–DIR008]
- Order flips around the group: earlier, A28 preceded each group member and A26 preceded each group member in essentially every joint occurrence; later, the group precedes A28 in about 99 in 100 cases and precedes A26 in about 99 in 100. [REL008–REL012, REL020–REL024]
- Positions: the group activities moved from a mean relative position of about 0.58 to about 0.39 of the case; A28 moved later (about 0.39 to 0.48), as did A26 (0.53 to 0.61) and A27 (0.59 to 0.64). [POS002–POS010]
- Within the group there is no fixed internal order in either window (both orders in similar shares; e.g., A36 before A37 in about 25 in 100 joint cases earlier versus 27 the other way, and about 47 versus 53 later). [REL030–REL039]
- Adjacency: A42 followed by A31 within five steps fell from about 102 of 200 cases to 2 of 200; A36 followed by A28 and A43 followed by A28 within five steps rose from about 1 of 200 cases to about 197 of 200 (each earlier side rests on a single trace). [LAG001–LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the earlier choice between two alternative branches was replaced by a single fixed sequence. In the earlier window, A26 routed each case either to A27 (which led to A31) or to the group A36/A37/A42/A43/A44 (which also led to A31), the two running together in only about 1 in 100 cases; in the later window, every case runs A25 into the group, the group (in no fixed internal order) before A28 and A26, A26 to A27, and A27 to A31, so the group and A27 are both mandatory and A31 is reached only after A27. [OCC001–OCC006, REL015–REL019, REL008–REL012, REL020–REL024, DIR001–DIR008, REL014, REL025–REL029]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the participation rise from about 50 to 100 in 100 cases is observed for all six activities; the alternatives-together change and the order flips around A28 and A26 are shown by relational evidence, not inferred from positions alone; the rerouting at A25 and A26 and the replacement of the group's edges to A31 by edges to A28 are directly observed; and the screening produced 66 qualifying items where fewer than 1 would be expected without a change, so chance cannot account for the set as a whole — the items describe facets of one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001–OCC006, REL015–REL019, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004–DIR008, LAG001–LAG003, screening]

Limitations: no relational item covers the pair A28–A26 and A28 has no outgoing profile in the evidence, so their mutual order in the later window rests on relative positions only; the LAG support for the group-to-A28 adjacency had a single earlier trace each. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window reflects wholesale replacement of the running cases by one new way of executing them rather than a modification of the earlier branches, since the two windows show what ran in each period but not whether the earlier branches persist outside the sample. [POS002–POS004, LAG001, LAG003, REL015–REL019]

Other differences: A25's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (about 0.35 to 0.32) and A31's slightly later (about 0.64 to 0.66), consistent with the relocation described above. [POS001, POS005]
