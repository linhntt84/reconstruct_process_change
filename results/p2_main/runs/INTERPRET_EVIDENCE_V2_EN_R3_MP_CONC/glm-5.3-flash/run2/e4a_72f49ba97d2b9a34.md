### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the two activity groups exchanged their order around the A24–A28 segment: earlier a case ran one of A14/A18/A21/A22/A23, then A24, then A28, then A15/A16/A17/A19/A20 closing into A29; in the later window A15/A16/A17/A19/A20 run first, followed by A24, A28, one of the five alternatives, and A29. The support is direct and consistent: all ten group members shift position in opposite directions, all 45 order relations between the groups and the handover activities reverse (deterministically so against A24 and A28), every boundary successor profile changes in the matching direction, and participation of every involved activity is unchanged. The volume of qualifying items (69 control-flow items against roughly 0.65 expected for an unchanged process, and 3 timing items against roughly 0.015) makes sampling variation implausible as an overall explanation. Limitations: positions are measured by event order rather than clock time, so the placement reading rests on the order-relation and successor evidence, and the data cannot distinguish whether the two groups moved around a fixed A24–A28 segment or that segment moved between them. [DIR001, DIR011, DIR012, POS001, POS002, POS011, REL001, REL012]

Other differences: the elapsed time between consecutive completions into A40, from its predecessors A30, A41 and A43, roughly halved from about 1.0 hour to about 0.5 hour, consistently across all three pairs; this is a timing change in its own right, and the evidence cannot establish whether it is connected to the reordering or is an independent change. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003]
