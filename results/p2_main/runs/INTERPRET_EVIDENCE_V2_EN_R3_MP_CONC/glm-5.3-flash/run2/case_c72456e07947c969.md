### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening tested 218 control-flow units and 85 items qualified, where about 1 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the flagged volume cannot be explained by random re-splits of an unchanged process. [OCC002, DIR007, REL015]

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment around the five-activity group (A35, A36, A41, A42, A43) was re-arranged: in the earlier window A25 led almost exclusively to A27, A26 fed the group, the group exited to A30, and both A26 and A27 came before the group; in the later window A25 feeds the group, the group runs before A27 and A26 and exits to A27, while A26 now leads to A30. [DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011, DIR012, DIR013, REL015, REL020, POS002, POS003, POS004]

The reversal is direct and consistent: the pairwise order between the group and A26/A27 flipped almost completely while co-occurrence stayed at 100 of 100 traces in both windows, the entry and exit changes appear in directly-follows profiles based on about 200 outgoing transitions per window, and the positional shifts agree; the group's internal, unordered execution is unchanged, so the difference concerns its placement and neighbours rather than its internal structure. Limitations: directly-follows shares are computed over outgoing transitions rather than cases, and the positional evidence uses event positions rather than clock time. [REL015, REL018, REL020, REL024, DIR007, DIR013, POS002, POS004] A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that a rare pre-existing variant with the group before A26 and A27 became dominant rather than the activities being re-placed within one flow; the earlier window shows that order in only about 1–3 of 100 traces. [REL015, REL020]

Other differences: A06 appears to have been added as a step between A46 and A19/A20 — A46 now leads to A06 in about 79 of 100 of its outgoing transitions (before, about 82 of 100 went directly to A19/A20), A06 leads on to A19 or A20 in about 90 of 100 of its transitions, and the A46-to-A19/A20 distance grew by about one step. [OCC002, DIR003, DIR014, REL001, REL002, LAG001, LAG029, LAG030] Separately, A02 and A08 were executed in far fewer traces (about 24 to 12 and about 25 to 7 of 100), with A04 and A10 showing similar drops in observed outgoing transitions, and the tail transitions A15→A16, A49→A50 and A51→A52 became near-exclusive.
