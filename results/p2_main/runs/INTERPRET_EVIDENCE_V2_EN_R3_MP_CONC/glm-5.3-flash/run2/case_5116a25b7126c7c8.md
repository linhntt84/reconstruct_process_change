### Observed differences

- The five activities A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 keep roughly the same participation — each appears in about 15–23 in 100 traces in both windows, and their combined appearances equal the number of cases (200 of 200) in each window, consistent with one of them per case — but their placement changes sharply: their mean event position moves from about 0.30–0.33 of the trace in the earlier window to about 0.58–0.61 in the later window. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- Their routing changes completely: earlier, each of the five led exclusively to A23, while in the later window they lead to A25 in about 37–68 in 100 outgoing transitions (depending on the activity) and to A29 in the remaining about 33–63, and no longer to A23. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- A42 rewires to A23: earlier its outgoing transitions spread across the five activities (about 18–23 in 100 each), whereas in the later window it leads exclusively to A23, and the A42→A23 distance shrinks from two steps to one. [DIR008, LAG004]
- The order between the five and A23/A24 flips: earlier the five always ran before A23 and before A24 (e.g., A14 before A23 in about 22 in 100 traces, never after), while in the later window A23 and A24 always run before them (about 20–23 in 100). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]
- A24 and A25, which earlier led only to A25 and only to A29 respectively, now also reach the five: about 51 in 100 of A24's outgoing transitions go to one of the five (A25 takes the rest), and about 49 in 100 of A25's go to one of the five (A29 takes the rest). [DIR006, DIR007]
- Distances shift accordingly: A24→A25 is now two steps apart in about 51 in 100 cases (earlier always one), A25→A29 two steps in about 49 (earlier always one), and A24→A29 is now always three steps (earlier two). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- A23, A24 and A25 each shift slightly earlier, their mean position dropping by about 0.02–0.04 of the trace length. [POS006, POS007, POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of five alternative activities A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 was relocated from the early segment to the final segment of the process. In the earlier window each case ran A42, then one of the five, then A23, and finally A24, A25 and A29; in the later window A42 leads directly to A23, and the five run after A24/A25 — consistently with about half the cases inserting the group member between A24 and A25 and about half between A25 and A29, after which it leads to A25 or A29. [DIR001, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, REL001, POS001]

The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: successor changes, a complete order flip against A23/A24, a large position shift and the gap changes all describe the same flow change, each difference exceeding about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data, and the 28 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.43 expected without a change, so chance sampling cannot account for the set as a whole. [DIR001, DIR008, REL001, POS001, LAG002]

Limitations: positions and gaps are event-based, so no claim about clock timing is made; the routing splits for the five rest on only about 30–45 outgoing transitions each, so the exact split sizes are uncertain; and the items largely describe one underlying flow change rather than independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the early occurrence of the five was removed and a separate late insertion added, rather than one block being moved — with anonymous activity codes and only these two windows, the two are behaviourally indistinguishable here. [REL001, POS001, DIR008]
