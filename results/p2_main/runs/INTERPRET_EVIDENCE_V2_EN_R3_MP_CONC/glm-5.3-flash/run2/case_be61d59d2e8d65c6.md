### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the optional side-branch formed by A19, A20 and A25 — which before ran in roughly 30–40 in 100 cases, one of the three per case, inserted between A27/A28 and A29 — is in the later window no longer executed, so that A27 now proceeds almost always directly to A28 and A28 directly to A29 in immediate succession. Supporting signs: the near-total participation collapse of the three activities (65, 57 and 81 of 200 traces before versus 1 each after), the concentration of A27's and A28's outgoing transitions on A28 and A29 (from about 52 and 43 in 100 to about 97 in 100), the disappearance of all co-occurrence between the branch activities and A27/A28/A29, and the tightening of the A27→A28→A29 gaps to a single step. That this is not a sampling artefact is supported at the screening level: 30 control-flow items qualified against fewer than 1 expected without change, so the ensemble of differences is far larger than an unchanged process would produce. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, DIR006, REL003, REL010, LAG001, LAG004]

The evidence cannot distinguish whether the branch was removed from the process or is still defined but was skipped in every observed case: zero participation only means the activities were not observed, and each still appeared once in the later window. The single remaining outgoing transitions of A19, A20 and A25 rest on one trace each and do not support any routing conclusion. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

Other differences: A10's outgoing transitions shifted from a profile concentrated on A15, A47 and A16 to one spread over many targets led by A26 and A45; this redistribution is not explained by the branch disappearance. A27's and A29's mean positions also shifted slightly (later and earlier, respectively). [DIR001, POS004, POS005]
