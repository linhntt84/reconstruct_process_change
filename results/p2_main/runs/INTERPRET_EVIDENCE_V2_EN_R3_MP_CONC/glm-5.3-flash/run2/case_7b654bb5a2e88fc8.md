### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-way choice block A14/A15/A20 — exactly one of which executes per case — was relocated from the segment between A40 and A21 to the segment between A22 and A27. In the later window the block runs there in either order with A23 (roughly half of the cases each way), replacing the former fixed chain A22 → A23 → A27, while A40 connects straight to A21. This is shown directly by successor changes (A40 now exiting only to A21; the block now exiting to A23/A27 and being entered from A22/A23), by complete order flips against A21 and A22 plus a new mixed order against A23, by the block's position shift from about one-third to about three-fifths through the case, and by the gap structure (A22–A27 now always three steps, with A23 and the block in either order) [DIR006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, POS001, POS002, POS003].

The volume of qualified evidence far exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces: 24 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.4 expected by chance for the 81 tested units (about 60 times that expectation), and every item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits. Participation is unchanged — A21, A22, A23 and A40 occur in every case in both windows and the block once per case — which fits a relocation rather than activities becoming optional, mandatory or repeated [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004].

Limitations: positions and gaps are measured in event positions, not clock time, so the hypothesis makes no claim about timing; and the two windows cannot distinguish a genuine move of the block from removing it after A40 while inserting an equivalent choice into the A22–A27 segment — that competing reading of two independent rewirings describes the same observed behaviour [LAG004, DIR004, DIR005].

Other differences: A21, A22 and A23 each occur slightly earlier in the trace in the later window (mean relative position lower by about 0.02–0.03), consistent with one event having left the early segment [POS004, POS005, POS006].
