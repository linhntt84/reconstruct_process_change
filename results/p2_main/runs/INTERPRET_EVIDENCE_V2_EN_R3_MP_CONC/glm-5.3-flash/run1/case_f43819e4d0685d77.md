### Observed differences

- Activity A02 was never observed in the earlier window (0 of 200 cases); in the later window it occurs in every one of the 200 cases, exactly once per case, with no repetitions. [OCC001]
- Earlier, A06 always appeared without A02 (100 in 100 cases); in the later window, A06 and A02 always occur together and A06 always comes first. [REL001]
- The successor of A06 changed: in the earlier window A06 was followed by another activity in only about 2 in 100 cases, and in those it was always A04; in the later window, all 200 observed outgoing transitions of A06 lead to A02. [DIR001]
- In the later window, A02 always sits at the same final position (1.0 on the position scale in every case), while A06 moved from a median position of about 1.0 to about 0.8 — consistent with A02 now following A06, though a position shift alone does not establish that an activity was moved. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 was introduced as a mandatory final step executed directly after A06 in every case. In the earlier window, cases effectively ended with A06: A02 never appeared, A06 occupied the last position in nearly all cases, and in the few cases where it had a successor at all, that successor was A04. In the later window, every case ends with the sequence A06 followed by A02: A06 precedes A02 in all 200 cases, A06's outgoing transitions go exclusively to A02, and A02 holds a single fixed final position. [OCC001, REL001, DIR001, POS001, POS002]

The support is direct and consistent: participation and the relation between the two activities swing from 0 to 100 in 100 cases, the new edge from A06 to A02 is observed in all later cases, and all five qualifying items (all control-flow) describe this one structure; five items is about a hundred times the roughly 0.05 items expected by chance in a stable process, and each difference exceeds those seen in about 995 of every 1000 random re-splits. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, POS002, REL001]

Limitations: the evidence shows only that A02 was not observed earlier, so it cannot distinguish a newly added activity from one that existed but was never executed in the earlier sample. The claim that A02 took over a connection from A04 rests on only 4 observed outgoing transitions of A06 in the earlier window, so the earlier routing of A06 — and whether A04 still occurs elsewhere in the process — remains uncertain. [DIR001, OCC001]
