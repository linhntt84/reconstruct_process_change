### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A10, not observed in the earlier window, is executed in about half of the later traces and is inserted into the flow between A05/A11 and A07: A11 now often leads to A10 instead of going directly to A07, and A10 leads on to A07 or to A11, while A05 also reaches A10 directly in about a fifth of its transitions. [OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003]

The change is shown directly and consistently: participation goes from zero to about 47 in 100 traces; A10's own outgoing profile appears; the successor share at A11 shifts from about 96 in 100 to A07 down to about 44, with about 52 to A10; the ordered relation runs A05 before A10 before A07; and the A11→A07 step distance grows from about 1 to about 2, exactly what inserting an activity between them would produce. Fifteen control-flow items qualified against fewer than one (0.12) expected by chance, so the ensemble is not what an unchanged process typically produces — though these items largely describe one underlying difference (A10's appearance and placement) rather than independent confirmations. [OCC003, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG003, POS002]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window only establishes that A10 was not observed there, so a pre-existing but extremely rare path that later became frequent cannot be fully excluded, although with 200 earlier traces and no occurrence this is unlikely to be sampling variation alone. The evidence also cannot show whether A10 is optional in roughly half the traces or tied to specific cases, and the mixed order between A10 and A11 means the insertion point is not strictly fixed. [OCC003, REL003]

Other differences: A06 and A09 appear only in the later window (about 11 and about 6 in 100 traces respectively), but no relational or directional evidence locates them in the flow; A07's position distribution narrowed slightly while its median stayed at about two-thirds of the trace.
