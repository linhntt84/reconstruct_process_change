### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of activities A25, A26, A31, A32 and A33 — previously executed exactly once per trace in a fixed sequence — is repeated within traces in the later window (about three executions per activity overall, about four in the roughly two-thirds of traces that repeat them), and that A33 re-enters this block at A25: about 67 in 100 of A33's outgoing transitions now go to A25, where earlier all went to A20. The repetition is measured directly on the activities themselves (participation unchanged at 100%, mean occurrences 1.0 → 3.06, repeats in 0 → about 66 in 100 traces), and the re-entry edge is observed directly in the successor profile of A33. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001]

The relational and distance evidence agrees with this reading: the fixed pairwise orders among these activities dissolved into interleaved or repeated execution in about 66 in 100 traces, and the gaps between A26, A31 and A32 widened from one–two events to up to five, as expected when other activities run in between repeated executions. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show how many iterations a trace performs by design, whether re-entry always occurs via A25, or whether the behaviour changed abruptly at the cut point rather than gradually beforehand; the many qualifying items also largely describe one underlying behaviour rather than independent changes. [OCC001, DIR001]

Other differences: A17 now appears in about 46 in 100 traces (down from about 56 in 100) and sits later in the trace; its fixed relation to the repeated block weakened — earlier the block preceded A17 whenever they co-occurred (about 56 in 100 traces), while later the pair is interleaved or repeated in about 28 in 100 traces and the fixed order holds in about 19 in 100. A23 remains in every trace but sits later (mean relative position 0.70 → 0.76). These shifts may be side effects of traces becoming longer through the repetition, since positions are relative to trace length, and the evidence cannot rule out that they are independent changes.
