### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative branch formed by A27, A28 and A33 between A21 and A23 is no longer executed, so that A22 has effectively become a mandatory step between A21 and A23. In the earlier window the two branches were taken in roughly equal shares (about 52 versus 48 in 100 cases); in the later window the block branch appears in only 1 of 200 traces, while A21 → A22 → A23 runs in essentially every trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL005, REL012, REL013, REL014, DIR001, LAG001]

Supporting signs: 34 control-flow items qualified against about 0.45 expected without change, far beyond what random re-splits produce; participation, routing, co-occurrence/order and distance indicators all show the same difference directly, and the key items are computed over all 200 traces per window rather than rare variants. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, LAG001]

Limitations: most items describe the same trace-level difference and are not independent confirmations; and because the block still occurs in one later trace, the evidence cannot distinguish a branch that was removed from one that remains executable but is now chosen very rarely — the block's internal later behaviour rests entirely on that single trace. [REL012, REL013, REL014, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

Other differences: none beyond the main hypothesis; the slight forward shift of A23's relative position is a consequence of the block dropping out rather than an independent change. [POS001]
