### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the resource allocation of activity A16 changed: work on A16, previously distributed across many resources (17 codes, top share about 18 in 100), is in the later window handled entirely by the single resource User_8. Both resource indicators show this directly and agree, each with a difference larger than in about 1999 of 2000 random re-splits, and the resource family produced 2 qualifying items where about 0.005 would be expected if nothing had changed — hundreds of times the chance expectation. The unchanged participation share (about 76 vs 75 in 100 traces) fits a reallocation of the same workload rather than a change in how often A16 is executed. Limitations: the two resource items are computed from the same traces and are therefore not independent confirmations; and the evidence cannot rule out that the concentration reflects a changed mix of cases reaching A16 rather than a changed assignment rule, nor does it show whether other activities' allocation changed. [ASSIGN001, CONC001]

Other differences: The elapsed time between consecutive completions of A09 and A23 increased roughly tenfold at the median (about 1.3 to about 16 hours), a timing change that is a hypothesis in its own right; the time family produced 1 item against about 0.015 expected by chance. Because this unit involves different activities and no control-flow or relational evidence qualified (0 items against about 0.19 expected), the evidence cannot show whether this timing change is connected to the A16 reallocation or is an independent change.
