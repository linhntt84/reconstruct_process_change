### Observed differences

- Participation and repetition swapped between A09 and A16. In the earlier window, A09 appeared in every trace, about twice per trace on average, and repeated in about 48 in 100 traces, while A16 appeared in about half the traces, once and never repeated. In the later window, A09 appears in about 49 in 100 traces, once and never repeating, while A16 appears in every trace, about 1.9 times per trace, repeating in about 44 in 100 traces. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A09 and A16 exchanged their places in the flow, each taking over the other's role. Earlier, A09 was the mandatory, often-repeated activity late in the trace (median relative position about 0.86), fed by A04 and A10 and leading to A06, A05 and A14, while A16 was an optional, earlier activity (in about half the traces, median position about 0.40) leading to A13, A10, A04 and A01. In the later window these roles are exchanged: A16 is mandatory, repeats in about 44 in 100 traces and sits late (median about 0.86) with A04 and A10 feeding it and A06, A05 and A14 following it, while A09 is optional (about 49 in 100 traces), sits earlier (median about 0.47), has taken over A16's former successors, and now receives the routing from A01 and A13. The mutual order of the pair flipped from mostly A16-first to mostly A09-first. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, REL012]

The support is direct rather than frequency-based: successor profiles changed at the sources themselves, the pair's order reversed, and step distances exchanged symmetrically (A16→A06 now always one step, where A09→A06 was one step earlier). With 39 qualifying items against fewer than one expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling is implausible as an overall explanation, and the items describe one coherent exchange rather than unrelated differences, though they are not independent confirmations. [DIR002, DIR003, REL012, LAG005, LAG012]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine swap of the two activities' positions from an equivalent relabelling of A09 and A16, which would leave the traces identical; in addition, a few distance items rest on few paired observations (roughly 17–40 occurrences), although every well-supported item points the same way. [LAG003, LAG007, LAG010, LAG013]

Other differences: small share shifts remain in the outgoing transitions of A01 and A13 beyond the A16–A09 switch (for example A01→A13 from about 40 to 43 in 100, and A13→A01 from about 35 to 28 in 100), and A09 now precedes A13, A01 or A14 in roughly 9–13 in 100 traces, which was not observed earlier. [DIR001, DIR005, REL001, REL010, REL011]
