### Observed differences

- A01 and A04 exchanged how often they occur. Earlier, A01 ran in about 52 of 100 traces (roughly 0.96 occurrences per trace) and A04 in about 24 of 100 (about 0.45 per trace); in the later window, A01 runs in about 20 of 100 traces (about 0.39 per trace) while A04 runs in about 52 of 100 (about 0.91 per trace). - Pairwise relations flipped. Earlier, A01 and A06 co-occurred in about 52 of 100 traces, always with A01 first, and A04 appeared with A06 in only about 24 of 100; in the later window, A04 and A06 co-occur in about 52 of 100 traces, always with A04 first, A01 and A06 co-occur in only about 20 of 100, and A06 appears without A01 in about 32 of 100 traces (earlier it appeared without A04 in about 28 of 100). - What did not change: A06's participation (about 52 of 100 traces in both windows), the share of traces containing neither activity of each flagged pair (about 48 of 100 in both windows), and the exit from A06 to A05. [DIR003, REL002, REL003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 and A04 exchanged the positions they held in the repeating fragment around A06: earlier the fragment ran A01 → A06 → A04 and back to A01, with A06 also exiting to A05, while in the later window it runs A01 → A04 → A06 and back to A01, with the exit to A05 unchanged. A04 now behaves as A01 did earlier — present in about half of the traces, near the trace start, always together with A06 and followed by A06 — and A01 behaves as A04 did earlier — rarer (about 20 of 100 traces), positioned later, and closing the loop from A06. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, OCC001, OCC002, REL002, REL003]

The hypothesis is shown directly by relational and positional evidence rather than inferred from frequencies alone: all three successor profiles, both position profiles, all three relation profiles and all six ordered-pair distance profiles flip exactly as this exchange predicts, and the fragment's overall incidence (about half of all traces) is stable, so the change is confined to which activity occupies which slot. Screening qualified 16 control-flow items where about 0.1 would be expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish one coordinated exchange from two independent reroutings of A01 and A04 that produce the same traces, and the position and distance statements rest on event positions within the trace rather than clock time, so no claim about timing is made; A05 itself was not flagged and is characterised only as A06's successor.
