### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the repeated cycling through A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 was largely removed. In the earlier window these activities ran about 3–4 times per case in an interleaved fashion, with A38 typically leading back to A30; in the later window each runs once per case in a partial sequence — A30 first, then A31, A36 and A37 in no fixed order between them but within one to two positions of one another, then A38, whose dominant outgoing transition now points to A25. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: occurrence and recurrence counts, the collapse of interleaving across every pair of the five activities, the halved position spread, and the observed successor change at A38 all describe the same behaviour. Screening qualified 51 items where fewer than 1 (0.81) would be expected without a change, so chance cannot account for the set as a whole, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits — though many items describe the same underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: the indicators are per-window aggregates, so they cannot show how the earlier repetitions were wired or what gated them — one combined cycle versus separate per-activity repetitions cannot be distinguished, and within the block only A38's outgoing transitions are directly observed. About 1–2 in 100 traces still show repeats, and several lag items rest on only 2–3 after-window observations and were not relied upon.
