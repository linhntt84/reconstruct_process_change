### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the work executed after A20 changed from the block A21–A25 to the block A26–A30: earlier, A20 was followed by A21–A25 in about 84 in 100 traces and that block ran in about 85 in 100 traces; later, A20 leads into A29 and the block A26–A30 runs in about 82 in 100 traces, preceding A38/A39 in about 81 in 100, while A21–A25 appear in only about 18 in 100 traces. The change is shown directly by the successor change at A20 (A24 to A29) and by the pair-level relation changes around A20, A21–A25, A26–A30, A34, A38 and A39, and it is consistent across all five activities of each group. Chance is unlikely to explain the evidence as a whole: 138 control-flow items qualified against about 1.3 expected if nothing had changed, although many of these items describe the same underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [OCC006, OCC011, DIR006, REL015, REL020, REL080]

The evidence cannot show whether A21–A25 were removed or only became optional (low participation means only that they were rarely observed), nor can it distinguish a single exchange of one block for the other from two independent changes, one removing A21–A25 and one adding A26–A30; a few later-window relations also rest on modest support, for example the A04-related items on about 24 traces. [OCC002, DIR002]

Other differences: A04 nearly vanished from the end of the trace — earlier A38 was followed by A04 in about 54% of its outgoing transitions and A04 led directly to A39 in about 95% of its outgoing, while later A38 proceeds straight to A39 (about 89% of outgoing, directly preceding it in about 90% of cases) and A04, when present, sits mid-trace; A02, A06, A08 and A10 also occur somewhat more often (about 13–18 in 100 traces versus about 2–4 earlier). These may belong to the same overall change but are not required for the main hypothesis. [OCC002, POS001, DIR002, DIR011, LAG010, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
