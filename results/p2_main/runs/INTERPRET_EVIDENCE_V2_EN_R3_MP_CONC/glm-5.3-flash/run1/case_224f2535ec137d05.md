The evidence supports the possibility that A01 now runs as a new final step, executed once per case immediately after A16, whereas earlier traces ended with A16 and A01 was not observed. In the earlier window A16 was present in every trace, always in the last position, and never followed by another activity; in the later window A01 appears exactly once per trace, always as A16's successor and at the end of the trace, while A16 shifts from last to second-to-last position.
