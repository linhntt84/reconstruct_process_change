### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the region around A10 and the block A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 was restructured: in the earlier window the five block activities were executed about three to four times per trace in an interleaved order that repeatedly returned from A38 to A30, while in the later window each is executed once per trace in a largely fixed sequence (A30 first, A38 last, A31/A36/A37 in variable order between them), and A10 — previously a step in only about 21 in 100 traces — now appears in about 72 in 100 and sits between A41/A14/A15 and the block, which A10 precedes in about 65 in 100 traces. Supporting signs: the repetition drop is measured on all 200 traces in both windows (about 3.6 → 1.0 occurrences; recurrence about 70 → 1 in 100); the ordering change is shown directly by the relation items (interleaved about 70 → 2 in 100) and by A38's redirected exit; A10's new placement is shown directly by its co-occurrence and order relations and by the successor changes at A41 and A10. Screening qualified 116 items where about 1 would be expected by chance in an unchanged process, so the pattern as a whole exceeds what sampling noise produces, although many relation and gap items re-describe the same restructuring and are not independent confirmations. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, REL005, REL013, REL033, REL040, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, LAG003, LAG023, POS001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows draw on different mixtures of process variants rather than a single modified flow; positions rest on event order, not clock time; and the fixed sequence is incomplete, since A15 still interleaves with the block in about 37 in 100 traces and A36/A37 have no fixed mutual order. [REL018, REL040, POS001]

Other differences: A02, A04, A06 and A08 became much rarer (from about 23–31 in 100 traces to about 5–11 in 100) with redistributed successors, but this rests on few later-window observations (9–20 traces) and is not part of the main hypothesis; A19, A20 and A41 now sit slightly later in the trace (mean event position about 0.25–0.30 → 0.32–0.37).
