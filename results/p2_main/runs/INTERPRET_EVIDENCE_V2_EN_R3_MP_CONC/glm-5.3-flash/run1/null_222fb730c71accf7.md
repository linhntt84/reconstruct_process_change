### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the dominant execution order between A36 and A37 reversed, with A33 remaining the activity that follows them: in the earlier window the dominant chain ran A37 → A36 → A33, while in the later window it runs A36 → A37 → A33. The successor profiles of both activities flipped directly and coherently, the consecutive pair A36→A37 roughly doubled in frequency while staying tightly consecutive, all three items show differences larger than about 199 of 200 random re-splits, and 3 qualifying items is about six times the 0.48 expected for an unchanged process. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG001]

Limitations: the three items plausibly describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, and the pair-frequency item rests on moderate counts (32 vs 56 observations). There is no relational evidence on the reverse order, so the data cannot show how often A37 preceded A36 in each window; the change may therefore be a shift in the mix between two coexisting orders rather than a strict reversal of a single fixed sequence.
