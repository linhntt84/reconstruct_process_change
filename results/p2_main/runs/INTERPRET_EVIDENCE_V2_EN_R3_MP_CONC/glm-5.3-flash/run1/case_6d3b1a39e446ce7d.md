- Four activities became effectively mandatory. Before, A27 ran in about 55 in 100 traces and A36, A37 and A42 in about 47 in 100 each; in the later window each of the four runs in essentially every trace, about once per trace (mean occurrences roughly 0.5 → 1.0). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the trio A36, A37 and A42 was made mandatory and repositioned: before, these three activities ran together, in varying order, as an optional branch after A26 instead of A27 in about 46 in 100 cases, with A31 following; in the later window they run in every case, in varying order, between A25 and A28 — that is, before A26 — while A27 also became mandatory in its previous slot between A26 and A31. A28 lost its direct link from A25 and now follows the trio about four steps later, and A31 remains at the end after both the trio and A27. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003, POS006, POS007, POS008]

The support is direct and consistent: the relocation is shown directly by the order reversals between the trio and A26/A28, by A27 and the trio changing from alternatives to always co-occurring, by the rewired successors of A25, A26 and the trio, and by the A25–A28 distance growing from one to about four steps. In total 48 control-flow items qualified where about 0.74 would be expected without a change, far beyond what chance produces; note, however, that many items describe overlapping aspects of one restructuring rather than independent confirmations. [REL006, REL011, REL014, DIR001, LAG001, OCC001]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that making the trio mandatory and rerouting A25 were separate adjustments rather than one coordinated relocation. In addition, the later-window step distances from A26 to the trio rest on only 1–2 observed pairs each, so A26's exact placement rests mainly on the order relations. [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

Other differences: only minor position drifts — A25 slightly earlier (mean position about 0.37 → 0.35) and A31 slightly later (about 0.63 → 0.66) — which fit the same restructuring rather than a separate change. [POS001, POS005]
