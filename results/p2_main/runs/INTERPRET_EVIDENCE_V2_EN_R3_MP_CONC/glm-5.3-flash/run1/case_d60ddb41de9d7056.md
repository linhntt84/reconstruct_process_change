### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that an alternative route between A21 and A23 was introduced alongside the original one. In the later window, in about 43 in 100 cases the flow leaves A21 and passes through the group A27, A28, A33, A34 and A35 — always all five, in varying order — before continuing to A23, bypassing A22; in the remaining about 57 in 100 cases the original A21 → A22 → A23 sequence is retained. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL007, REL008, REL013, REL018]

The supporting signs are that the change is shown directly by directional and relational evidence (A21 now also leads into the group, the group always precedes A23, and A22 and the group never co-occur), the participation figures (about 57 and 43 in 100, with A21 and A23 present throughout) are consistent across the occupancy, direction and relation indicators, and the number of qualifying items (45 against roughly 0.49 expected) far exceeds what random re-splits produce. [DIR001, REL008, REL013, OCC001, screening]

The evidence cannot distinguish a newly inserted alternative within one process from a mixture of two case populations in the later window, and the internal sequencing of the five group activities cannot be pinned down beyond the absence of a fixed pairwise order; zero participation in the earlier window means only that these activities were not observed there. [REL018, OCC002]

Other differences: apart from the slight forward shift in A21's relative position, which is consistent with the new route and not an independent change, the evidence shows no further differences. [POS001]
