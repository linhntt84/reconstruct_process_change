### Observed differences

- The order between the activity group A19/A20/A25 and A26 reversed. In the earlier window the group ran before A26 whenever both occurred (for example A19 before A26 in about 36 of 100 traces, A26 first in essentially none); in the later window A26 ran first (A26 before A19 in about 31 of 100, A19 first in almost none). The same flip holds against A27. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007]
- The group now sits much later in the trace: the mean event position of A19, A20 and A25 moved from roughly one-third to roughly three-fifths of the trace length, while A26 (about 0.35 to 0.33) and A27 (about 0.58 to 0.56) barely moved. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- Outgoing connections changed on both sides. Before, A19, A20 and A25 led almost exclusively to A26 (about 92%, 94% and 99% of their observed outgoing transitions); in the later window they lead mainly to A28 (39–48%) and A32 (45–56%), with A26 nearly gone. Conversely, A27 and A28, which previously led almost only onward (A27→A28 about 98%, A28→A32 about 99%), now send roughly 43% of A27's and 53% of A28's outgoing transitions into A19/A20/A25. A45 flipped from feeding the group (about 94% of outgoing transitions to A19/A25/A20) to feeding A26 directly (about 94%). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- Directly observed successions confirm the rewiring: A45→A26 became immediate (gap of 2 steps in about 94 of 100 cases before, gap of 1 in about 94 of 100 after); A19→A26 and A45→A25 all but disappeared (74 to 2 and 76 to 1 observed pairs); new close successions A27→A19, A27→A20, A28→A19 and A28→A20 appeared (1–2 observations before, 32–75 after, mostly gap 1). Distances through A27/A28 lengthened: A27→A32 shifted from a gap of 2 (97%) to 3 (93%), and A28→A32 from gap 1 (99%) to gaps of 1 or 2 (about 44% / 54%). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011]
- Against A28 the group's order became unfixed: before, A20 and A25 always preceded A28 (in about 24% and 38% of traces, never the reverse); after, both orders appear in similar shares (A20 before A28 about 17%, A28 before A20 about 21%). [REL005, REL008]
- Participation in A24 rose from about 42.5 to about 57.5 in 100 traces (mean occurrences per trace 0.43 to 0.58). [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the activity group A19, A20 and A25 changed its place in the sequence: in the earlier window it ran between A45 and A26 (A45 typically ran into one of the three, and each of the three ran on to A26), whereas in the later window it runs after A26 and A27 — entered from A27 or A28 and leading on to A28 or A32 — while A45 connects straight to A26. The reversal is shown directly by the flipped order against A26 and A27, the position shift of the group with A26 and A27 staying in place, the changed successor profiles, and the gap-level replacement of the old A45 → group → A26 chain by A45→A26 plus new A27/A28 → group successions. With 31 qualifying items against roughly 0.62 expected by chance in the control-flow family, the volume of evidence is far beyond what an unchanged process produces. [REL001, REL003, REL006, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG003, LAG011]

Limitations: the evidence cannot settle whether the group now runs once between A27/A28 and A32 or can repeat within a trace, since A28 leads back into the group in about half of its outgoing transitions while the group also leads to A28; several "before" distributions for the new edges rest on only 1–2 observations, so their earlier absence is weakly documented; and positions and gaps are event-based, so no statement about clock-time timing is possible. A shift in the mix of trace variants (a rare variant becoming common) could produce some of these differences in finite windows; the consistency across occurrence, direction, position, relation and gap indicators makes a pure sampling artefact unlikely but cannot be excluded. [DIR005, LAG002, LAG003, LAG007, LAG008]

Other differences: A24 became more common — observed in about 42.5 of 100 traces before and about 57.5 of 100 after (mean occurrences per trace 0.43 to 0.58); the evidence does not connect this to the reordering of the A19/A20/A25 group. [OCC001]
