### Observed differences

- A01 was observed in about 103 of 200 traces in the earlier window, always as the final event of the trace; in the later window it was not observed at all (0 of 200 traces). [OCC001, POS001]
- A12 kept essentially the same participation (about 103 of 200 traces earlier, 102 of 200 later) but changed role: earlier it sat at roughly 75–90% of the trace length (mean about 82%) and was always followed by A01; later it occupies the final position in every case and no outgoing transition from A12 was observed, consistent with A12 now ending those traces. [POS002, DIR003]
- The relationship between A01 and A12 flipped: earlier about 52 in 100 traces contained both activities, always with A12 before A01; later about 51 in 100 traces contain A12 without A01 and no trace contains both, while about 49 in 100 traces contain neither activity in both windows. [REL001]
- Routing into A10 converged: earlier A06 split its outgoing transitions between A14 (about 54 in 100) and A10 (about 46 in 100), and A14 split between A10 (about 54 in 100) and A15 (about 46 in 100); later both A06 and A14 lead exclusively to A10, and the exits A06→A14 and A14→A15 were not observed. [DIR001, DIR005]
- New successions appeared around A07 and A13: earlier both always led to A05; later A07 splits about evenly between A13 and A05, and A13 splits between A05 (about 49 in 100), A07 (about 30 in 100) and A08 (about 21 in 100), with fewer observed outgoing transitions (71 earlier vs 57 later). [DIR002, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 is no longer executed and that A12, which previously led into A01 near the end of the trace, now itself concludes those traces: the earlier ending "A12 then A01" in about half of the traces became an ending on A12 alone. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, REL001, DIR003]

The change is shown directly by four complementary views: occurrence (about 52 in 100 traces to 0 of 200), position (A01 always last earlier and absent later, while A12 moved from a variable position around 82% of the trace length to the final position in every case), relation (co-occurrence about 52 in 100 down to zero, A12-only up to about 51 in 100) and successors (the A12→A01 succession, previously all 103 observed outgoing transitions of A12, is gone). Nine items qualified in screening against roughly 0.13 expected for an unchanged process, so chance sampling cannot account for the evidence set as a whole, and the A01 difference alone exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, REL001, DIR003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005]

Limitations: the five items on A01 and A12 largely describe one and the same trace behaviour, so they are consistent but not independent confirmations; and because activity codes are anonymous, the evidence cannot distinguish removal of A01 from a relabelling — zero participation only means A01 was not observed in the later sample. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, REL001, DIR003]

Other differences: two routing changes elsewhere in the process are not part of this hypothesis — A06 and A14 now lead exclusively to A10 where about half of each previously exited to A14 and A15 respectively, and A07 and A13 gained mutual successions and an A13→A08 exit where both previously led only to A05; these rest on redirect evidence alone, and their connection to the A01/A12 change is not shown. [DIR001, DIR005, DIR002, DIR004]
