### Observed differences

- **A block of five activities became repeated.** In the earlier window, A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 each occurred exactly once in every trace (mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, no repeats, present in all traces). In the later window, each occurs about 3 times per trace on average (means about 3.06–3.08), and repeats appear in about 66–68 in 100 traces, while every trace still contains each activity at least once. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- **Routing out of A38 shifted.** Earlier, about 99 in 100 of A38's observed outgoing transitions went to A25; in the later window, about 67 in 100 go to A30 and about 33 in 100 to A25, with tiny new shares to A04, A02 and A27 (about 0.5% or less each), based on roughly 613 outgoing transitions in the later window versus 200 earlier. [DIR001]
- **The fixed sequencing inside the block dissolved.** Earlier, A30 preceded A31, A36, A37 and A38 in essentially every trace, and A31, A36 and A37 each preceded A38, while A31/A36/A37 had no fixed order among themselves (about 50/50). In the later window, each pair within the block is interleaved or repeated in about two-thirds of traces (66–68 in 100), with A30 preceding the others in only about 32–34 in 100; the activities still co-occur in all traces in both windows. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]
- **Successions within the block are more spread out.** Earlier, when one block activity followed another (e.g., A36 after A31), the gap was always 1–2 event positions, about two-thirds at 1; in the later window only about 10–13 in 100 successions are 1 position apart and gaps of 3–5 positions appear. A37 self-repetitions within five steps, seen in only 1 of 200 earlier traces, occur in about 109 of 200 later traces. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]
- **Event positions spread out.** The position distributions of the five activities widened (standard deviations from about 0.05–0.06 to about 0.14, higher 75th percentiles), consistent with several occurrences at varied positions; positions are event positions, not clock times. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the block A30 → A31/A36/A37 (in variable order) → A38, previously executed exactly once per trace, is traversed about three times per trace: A38 now leads back to A30 in about two-thirds of its outgoing transitions instead of proceeding to A25, the five activities repeat in about two-thirds of traces, and the earlier fixed sequencing (A30 first, A38 last) gives way to interleaved, wider-spaced occurrences. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, REL001, REL010, LAG001, LAG007]

Supporting signs: the repetition is shown directly by occurrence counts with full support in both windows (200 traces each; recurrence from 0 to about 66–68 in 100), the new dominant A38 → A30 transition is shown directly by the routing profile, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. The relation, lag and position items all match this single underlying behaviour rather than confirming it independently. With 29 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.6 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole, and the repeated behaviour is the majority pattern in the later window, not a rare variant. [OCC002, DIR001, REL005, LAG003, POS001]

Limitations and competing explanation: directional routing is observed only for A38, so how the flow re-enters the block beyond A38 → A30 is inferred from repetition and co-occurrence rather than observed edge by edge; and because the relation measure combines interleaving with repetition, the evidence cannot establish whether each pass keeps a fixed internal order or the order varies between passes — a less structured, uncoordinated repetition of the individual activities is a competing reading the data cannot fully rule out. [REL001, REL005, DIR001]

Other differences: A04, still rare, became more common — it appeared in about 9 of 200 traces in the earlier window and about 29 of 200 in the later window (participation about 4.5% to about 14.5%), and A38 now sends a small share of its transitions (about 0.5%) to A04. [OCC001, DIR001]
