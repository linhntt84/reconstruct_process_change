In the earlier window, A20, A21, A22, A24 and A25 — each present in essentially every trace — sat at about 39–40% of the trace's event positions, while A19, A23, A26, A27 and A28 — each in roughly one in five traces — sat at about 69–72%. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two blocks of activities exchanged their place in the flow. In the earlier window, A44 was followed by the always-present block A20, A21, A22, A24, A25, then A29, then A33, then one of the occasional activities A19, A23, A26, A27, A28, which led to A34; in the later window, A44 is followed by the occasional activity, then A29, then A33, then the A20–A25 block, which leads to A34. A29 and A33 kept their relative order but moved earlier along with the block that now precedes them. Supporting signs: the order reversals are near-deterministic in both directions (about 98–99.5 in 100 co-occurring traces), the position shifts match the reversal in size and direction, the successor changes sit exactly at the block boundaries, and participation shares are unchanged, which rules out path replacement or activities becoming optional, mandatory or repeated differently. The screening context also fits: 66 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected without change, although many items describe the same underlying swap and are not independent confirmations. Limitations: positions and lags are measured in event positions rather than clock time, several lag profiles rest on a single observation in one window (so exact distances, such as A33–A34 within two steps, are indicative only), and the indicators cannot show whether every trace reversed uniformly or the mix of trace variants shifted, nor what happens before A44 or after A34. [REL004, REL014, REL001, REL016, REL027, POS002, POS005, POS011, DIR005, DIR007, DIR011, LAG003, LAG010]

Other differences: a few low-share outgoing transitions appeared in the later window — A19 to A38, A35 and A14, and A23 to A27, each about 3 in 100 — and A19's position spread widened; these are peripheral to the block exchange. [DIR001, DIR005, POS001]
