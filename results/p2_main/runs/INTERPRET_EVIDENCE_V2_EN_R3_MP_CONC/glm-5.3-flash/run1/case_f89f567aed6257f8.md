### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change in which A14 is no longer executed and A01 is now executed, usually repeatedly, in about half of the cases: earlier every trace passed once through A14 near the end as part of an alternating A03–A14 pair, whereas in the later window A14 is unobserved, A03 terminates every trace, and A01 appears mid-trace, receiving transitions from A09 and A13 (now exclusively) and from A08, A10 and A17 (about half of their transitions), and leading on to A11, A09, A05 and A02 in roughly equal shares. The signs are direct and consistent: participation flips that sampling cannot produce (A01 in 0 of 200 vs 112 of 200 traces; A14 in 200 of 200 vs 0 of 200), successor changes in the directional profiles, and co-occurrence flips in the relational items; the screening also flagged 129 control-flow items where roughly 1 would qualify by chance without a change, so the ensemble as a whole is not explainable by sampling. Limitations: the agreeing indicators largely describe the same traces rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot show whether A01 is a genuinely new step or a relabelled one, nor whether A14's disappearance and A01's appearance are a single substitution or two independent changes.
