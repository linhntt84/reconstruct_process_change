### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the four activities formed a fixed chain: after A01, the next activity was A02 in about 87 in 100 of A01's outgoing transitions; A02 led on to A04 in about 94 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, and A04 led to A03 in about 96 in 100. A02 came before A04 in every trace, and the two activities occurred together in every trace. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001]
- In the later window the order between A02 and A04 is no longer fixed: A02 runs first in about 54 in 100 traces and A04 first in about 46 in 100, while the two still occur together in every trace. [REL001]
- The successor profiles shifted accordingly. A01's outgoing transitions now go to A02 in about 50 in 100 and directly to A04 in about 43 in 100 (previously about 87 and 6). A02 now leads directly to A03 in about 44 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, which was not observed before, while A02→A04 fell from about 94 to about 50 in 100. A04 now leads directly to A02 in about 41 in 100, also not observed before, while A04→A03 fell from about 96 to about 49 in 100. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- Step distances changed from one dominant value to a roughly even split: A01→A02 and A04→A03 were almost always 1 step apart (about 96–100 in 100) and now sit near half at 1 step and half at 2; A01→A04 and A02→A03 were almost always 2 steps apart (about 90–94 in 100) and now show a similar even split. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]
- A02 now occurs later within traces (mean relative position about 0.27 versus 0.17 before) and A04 earlier (about 0.28 versus 0.39 before). [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the ordering constraint between A02 and A04 was relaxed: in the earlier window the two were always executed in the sequence A02 then A04, between A01 and A03; in the later window they appear in either order (about 54 in 100 traces A02 first, about 46 in 100 A04 first), both still occur in every trace, and A03 still follows the pair. [REL001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG003, LAG004]

Supporting signs: the relational evidence shows the change directly — co-occurrence remains at every trace while the fixed A02-before-A04 order becomes a mixed order, which is a change in ordering constraints rather than an exchange of positions. The successor, distance and position items all move in the direction this reordering predicts: the previously unobserved direct successions A02→A03 and A04→A02 appear at about 44 and 41 in 100, the direct successions A02→A04 and A04→A03 halve, step distances become mixed, and A02 drifts later while A04 drifts earlier. Ten control-flow items were reported where about 0.1 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the set as a whole is unlikely to arise by chance from the two samples, and every item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. These items largely describe one underlying reordering, so they should not be read as independent confirmations. [REL001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether each case can now take either order or whether the later window mixes two deterministic variants, one keeping A02 before A04 and one reversing it; the exact 54/46 split also rests on 200 traces per window and is subject to sampling variation. [REL001, DIR002, DIR003]

Other differences: minor shifts at the chain's ends — A01's small outputs to A05 and A06 shrink to nearly zero (from about 2 and 1 in 100), while the transitions A04→A01 and A01→A03 rise from about 4 in 100 each to about 11 and 8 in 100. [DIR001, DIR003]
