### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A25, A26 and A31 each appeared in every trace and occurred about 3.4 times per trace on average, repeating within the same trace in about 75 in 100 traces. In the later window, all three still appeared in every trace but occurred about once per trace, with within-trace repetition in essentially no trace (about 1 in 200). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The outgoing flow of A31 shifted: earlier, about 71 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions led to A25 and about 29 in 100 to A20; in the later window, about 96 in 100 led to A20 and only about 4 in 100 to A25 (based on 689 vs 208 outgoing transitions). [DIR001]
- The relation between A25 and A31 changed from mixed to fixed: earlier the two were interleaved or repeated in about 75 in 100 traces (A25 first in about 25 in 100); in the later window A25 came before A31 in about 995 in 1,000 traces, while the two still always occurred in the same traces. [REL001]
- When A25 is followed by A31, the gap tightened: in the later window A31 followed A25 about two event positions later in about 92 in 100 observed pairs, whereas earlier the pair appeared mostly at larger distances or beyond the five-step window. [LAG001]
- The reverse pair, A31 followed by A25 within five steps, was observed 150 times earlier but only once in the later window; this item rests on a single after-observation. [LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the repeated execution of the activities A25, A26 and A31 stopped: earlier these three activities repeated roughly three to four times per trace in most traces and A25 and A31 alternated with each other, while in the later window each is executed once per trace in a fixed order (A25 before A31, about two positions apart), and A31 now continues to A20 instead of leading back to A25. This reading is backed by the screening context — 9 items qualified where about 0.3 would be expected for an unchanged process, a count chance cannot plausibly produce — and by direct, mutually consistent occurrence, successor and ordering evidence, each with full support in both windows (200 of 200 traces) and each larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001, LAG001]

Limitations: A26 has no relational or directional evidence, so its place in the once-per-trace sequence is not established, and its involvement rests only on its occurrence pattern mirroring A25 and A31. The fixed A25-before-A31 order could be a byproduct of the repetition stopping (with one occurrence each, interleaving is impossible) rather than an independent new ordering constraint. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of pre-existing trace variants rather than a structural removal of the repetition, since about 25 in 100 earlier traces already showed no repetition, and about 1 in 200 later traces still do. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, LAG002]

Other differences: the position distributions of A25 and A31 narrowed (spread roughly halved, e.g. A25 from about 0.12 to 0.06) while their mean positions stayed essentially unchanged (about 0.44 vs 0.41 and 0.48 vs 0.48 of the trace), which is consistent with single occurrences at a stable point rather than the activities having moved. [POS001, POS002]
