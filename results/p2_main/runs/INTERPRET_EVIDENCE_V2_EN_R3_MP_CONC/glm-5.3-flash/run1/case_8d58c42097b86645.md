- Activity A01 was not observed at all in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it occurs in about 21 in 100 traces (42 of 200), usually once in the traces where it appears, and around the middle of the trace (median event position about half of the trace length). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new step A01 was inserted between A08 and A10 on one branch of the process. Earlier, A08 passed directly to A10 on about 55 in 100 of its outgoing transitions; in the later window that share passes to A01 instead, A01 in turn always leads to A10, A01 sits around the middle of the trace and precedes A10 in most co-occurring traces, and the A08-to-A10 distance shifted from mostly one step to mostly two steps — the pattern expected when one activity is placed between two others. Nine control-flow items qualified against an expectation of roughly 0.13 without change, so the pattern as a whole is unlikely to arise from sampling variation alone. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR003, REL002, LAG001]

Limitations: the insertion affects only about one branch — A01 appears in about a fifth of traces — and in roughly half of the traces where A01 and A08 co-occur the order is mixed or repeated rather than a strict A08-before-A01, so the insertion is not uniformly clean. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that A01's appearance and the rerouting of A08 are two coincident changes rather than a single insertion, although the shift of the A08-to-A10 distance from odd to even lags favours the insertion reading. [REL001, LAG001]

Other differences: the back-and-forth between A03 and A12 appears to have been removed — earlier A03 could route to A12 (about 34 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) and A12 led back to A03, whereas in the later window A03 always leads to A13 and A12 leads to A13; these routing profiles rest on comparatively few observations (about 20 and 16–17 outgoing transitions). [DIR002, DIR004]
