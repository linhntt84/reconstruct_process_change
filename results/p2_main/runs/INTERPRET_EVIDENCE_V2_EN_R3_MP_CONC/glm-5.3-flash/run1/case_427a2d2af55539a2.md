### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, three activities — A14, A15 and A16 — each ran in a minority of cases (about 70, 59 and 71 of 200 traces, i.e. roughly a third each), never together, and exactly one of the three was present in every single trace. In the later window, none of them appears in any of the 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL008]
- The mirror image holds for A21, A22 and A23: not observed at all earlier, in the later window each occurs exactly once in every one of the 200 traces. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- Earlier, whichever of A14/A15/A16 was present always led directly to A24 (A24 was the only observed successor of each), with A39 always preceding it. In the later window, A39's observed outgoing transitions go instead to A21, A22 and A23 (about 36%, 33% and 32%), each of the three always follows A39 and always precedes A24. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, REL006, REL012, REL017, REL021, REL022, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027]
- The three new activities have no fixed order among themselves: A21 and A22 co-occur in all traces, with A21 first in about 51 in 100 traces and A22 first in about 49 in 100; the splits for A21/A23 (51/49) and A22/A23 (52/48) are similarly even, and each activity's observed successors include the other two as well as A24. [REL019, REL020, REL023, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- The gap from A39 to A24 widened from exactly 2 event positions in every earlier trace to exactly 4 positions in every later trace, and A24's mean position moved later (about 0.39 to about 0.44 of normalised position), consistent with two extra steps inserted between A39 and A24. [LAG001, POS007]
- A39 and A24 themselves occur in all traces in both windows; their participation is unchanged. [DIR007, POS007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the segment between A39 and A24. Earlier, every trace contained exactly one activity chosen exclusively from A14, A15 and A16 (each in roughly 3 in 10 cases, never more than one per trace), placed directly between A39 and A24. In the later window that single step is gone, and three activities — A21, A22 and A23 — each run exactly once in every trace in that segment, with no fixed order among themselves. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, REL019, REL020, REL023, LAG001]

The support is direct and consistent: the participation of both trios flips between 0 and full coverage with substantial backing (59–71 traces for the disappearing activities, all 200 for the new ones), the routing out of A39 switches wholesale from the old trio to the new one, the ordering relations with A39 and A24 are uniform, and the A39→A24 distance grows from 2 to 4 positions, matching the insertion of two additional steps. Screening found 48 qualifying control-flow items where roughly 0.5 would be expected if nothing had changed, so chance sampling in finite windows cannot account for the evidence as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, LAG001]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A14, A15 and A16 were not observed in the later window, so the evidence cannot distinguish a removal-plus-addition from a relabelling of the same steps — an important competing reading. It also cannot distinguish truly parallel execution of A21, A22 and A23 from arbitrary sequential ordering, since both produce the observed mixed orders, and a very rare reappearance of the old activities could be missed in a 200-trace sample. [REL019, REL020, REL023, OCC001]
