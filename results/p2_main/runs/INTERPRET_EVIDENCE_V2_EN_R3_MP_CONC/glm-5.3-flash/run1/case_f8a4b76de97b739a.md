### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural reorganisation in which A02 and A01, not observed in the earlier window, now run in most traces while A09 is no longer observed: A02 executes before A12 in a fixed order, so that A05 and A13 reach A12 through A02 instead of directly, and A16 now routes to A01, whose outgoing flow goes mainly to A03, where earlier it routed to A09, whose outgoing flow also went mainly to A03. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL017, DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, DIR009, DIR001]

The screening produced 67 qualifying items where fewer than 1 (0.76) would be expected without a change, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of a stable process produce. The core claims are shown directly rather than by accumulation: participation jumps from zero to about 87 in 100 traces for A02, the A02-before-A12 order is relational evidence, the rerouting is visible in the successor profiles of A05, A13 and A16, and the disappearance of within-one-step A05→A12 and A13→A12 pairs matches a route that now passes through A02. An important competing explanation cannot be fully excluded: A01/A02 may have been present earlier but too rare to observe (zero participation only means not observed), and A12's own position was not profiled, so a broader shift of the block around A12 cannot be ruled out — although the direct successor evidence favours rerouting through A02. [REL017, DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, DIR009, LAG001, LAG006, OCC001, OCC002]

Limitations: many items describe the same traces (the A02–A12 relation appears in occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators), so they are not independent confirmations, and the earlier outgoing profiles of A14 and A17 rest on only about 26–34 transitions. [OCC001, DIR002, REL017, LAG001, DIR008, DIR010]

Other differences: A14 became nearly always executed and now repeats within traces, forming a close back-and-forth with A17 (A17 leads to A14 in about 89 in 100 of its outgoing transitions; their co-occurrence rose from 0 to about 42 in 100, always interleaved or repeated), the former A07/A08→A17 adjacencies now occur at larger lags, and A08 and A17 sit slightly earlier within traces; the evidence does not show whether these shifts belong to the same restructuring as the A01/A02/A09 changes.
