### Observed differences

- A02 became far more prominent: it appears in about 8 in 100 traces in the earlier window versus about 34 in 100 in the later one (16 vs 68 of 200 traces), its occurrences now sit earlier and much more tightly in the trace (mean relative position about 0.51 vs 0.35), and its outgoing transitions now go mainly to A19, A24 and A21 (about 37, 27 and 24 in 100) instead of being scattered. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001]

- Two groups of activities exchanged their order around A25. Earlier, A20, A22 and A23 ran before A25 in nearly every trace (e.g., A20 before A25 in about 98 in 100), while A25 ran before A19, A21 and A24 (e.g., A25 before A21 in about 37 in 100). In the later window this is reversed: A25 precedes A20, A22 and A23 in essentially all traces, and A19, A21 and A24 precede A25. [REL017, REL024, REL027, REL013, REL021, REL029]

- The two groups also swapped sides relative to each other: earlier, A20/A22/A23 preceded A21 and A24 whenever they co-occurred (about 31–38 in 100 traces); in the later window A21 and A24 precede A20/A22/A23 in about 28–29 in 100 traces. A19 moved the same way: earlier A20/A22/A23 preceded it (about 31 in 100), later it precedes them (about 44 in 100). [REL015, REL019, REL023, REL010, REL011, REL012]

- Successors were rewired accordingly: A19, A21 and A24 earlier led almost exclusively to A30 (about 97–99 in 100 outgoing transitions) and now lead almost exclusively to A25; A20, A22 and A23 lost A25 as a successor and now lead to A30 in about 32–34 in 100 transitions. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR007, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006]

- Positions shifted with the swap: A20, A22 and A23 now occur later (mean relative position about 0.39–0.40 vs about 0.64), A19, A21 and A24 now occur earlier (about 0.67–0.69 vs about 0.35), while A25 stays near the middle (about 0.45 vs 0.39). [POS003, POS005, POS006, POS002, POS004, POS007, POS008]

- A29 and A40 now feed the other group: A29's outgoing transitions moved from A21/A19/A24 to A20/A23/A22, and A29 is followed by A20 within five steps in essentially every later trace (about 1 earlier); A40's successors moved from A22/A23/A20 to A02/A19/A21/A24, with A40 immediately followed by A02 in about 62 later traces (about 2 earlier) and A40 followed by A22 within five steps no longer observed. The gap from A29 to A30 widened from about 2 to about 4 steps. [DIR008, LAG003, DIR009, LAG005, LAG006, LAG004]

- A02 now typically precedes the other activities when it occurs: it co-occurs with A20, A22, A23, A25, A29 and A30 in about 34 in 100 traces (about 8 earlier) and precedes them in about 31–33 of those, whereas earlier co-occurrence (about 8 in 100) showed no consistent order. [REL002, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

- Separately, A41 repeats more often: mean occurrences per trace about 1.5 vs 1.8, with repeats in about 33 vs 40 in 100 traces. [OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, two groups of activities execute in exchanged order around A25: the group A19, A21 and A24 — now commonly entered from the much more frequent A02 and often preceded by A40 — runs before A25, whereas the group A20, A22 and A23 — now typically preceded by A29 — runs after it, with both groups still converging on A30. Earlier, A20/A22/A23 ran before A25 and A25 before A19/A21/A24; in the later window these order relations are reversed on pairs that co-occur in all 200 traces of both windows. [REL017, REL024, REL027, REL013, REL021, REL029, REL015, REL019, REL010]

Supporting signs: the successor changes match the swap (A19/A21/A24 now lead to A25 instead of A30; A20/A22/A23 now lead to A30 instead of A25), positions moved accordingly, and the lag changes match the rewiring (A29→A20 and A40→A02 now immediate). The volume of qualifying evidence (58 items against roughly 0.75 expected without change) is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR007, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, LAG003, LAG005]

Limitations: positions and lags are measured in event positions, not clock time, so the change is established in sequence structure only; the many items largely restate one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations; and the evidence cannot fully rule out that the later window reflects a different mix of case routes (for example A02-initiated traces) rather than a flipped ordering constraint, although the constant full co-occurrence of A20 and A25 with reversed order favours the ordering interpretation. [POS003, LAG004, OCC001, DIR001]

Other differences: A41 now recurs more often within traces (mean occurrences per trace about 1.5 before vs 1.8 after; traces with repeats about 33 vs 40 in 100). [OCC002]
