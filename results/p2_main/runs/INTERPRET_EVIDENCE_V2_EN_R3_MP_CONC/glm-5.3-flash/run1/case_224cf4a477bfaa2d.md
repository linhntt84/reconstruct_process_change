### Observed differences

- **A01 appeared in every case.** In the earlier window, A01 was not observed in any of the 200 traces; in the later window it occurs exactly once per trace (mean 1.0 occurrence, present in 200 of 200 traces, never repeated), and early in the case (median normalized position about 0.2, mean about 0.29). [OCC001, POS001]
- **New routing into and out of A01.** Earlier, A02 led to A04 in about 87 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions; in the later window, about 54 in 100 go to A01 and about 44 in 100 to A04. A04, which earlier never led to A01, now sends about 39 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A01. A01's own outgoing transitions go mainly to A04 (about 46 in 100), with smaller shares to A07 (about 17), A03 (about 16) and A10 (about 10). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- **Co-occurrence and order changed.** Earlier, A02 always ran without A01; in the later window the two always co-occur, with A02 before A01 in all 200 traces. A01 and A04 now always co-occur but in mixed order (A01 first in about 55 in 100 traces, A04 first in about 45 in 100), i.e. no fixed order between them. A01 also co-occurs with A03, A06, A07 and A10 in roughly 45–53 in 100 later traces and precedes them in nearly all of those. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007]
- **Downstream activities sit later.** Mean normalized positions moved later for A03 (0.51 to 0.68), A10 (0.46 to 0.68), A06 (0.80 to 0.99), A07 (0.45 to 0.52) and A04 (0.24 to 0.31), and the step distance from A02/A04 to the downstream activities grew by about one step (e.g. A02→A07 mostly 2 steps earlier vs 3 later; A02→A10 mostly 2–3 vs 3–4; A04→A06 mostly 3 vs 3–4; A02→A04 mostly 1 vs 1–2). This pattern is consistent with one inserted step pushing the rest of the flow later. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]
- **A09 disappeared.** Earlier, A09 occurred in about 45 in 100 traces, always as the final step (mean normalized position 1.0), preceded by A06 (which led to A09 in all of its observed outgoing transitions) and reached after A02/A04; in the later window A09 is not observed in any of the 200 traces. [OCC002, POS006, DIR004, REL006, REL008, REL010, REL013]
- **A06's routing changed, but on very few observations.** Earlier A06 always led to A09; in the later window only 3 outgoing transitions of A06 were observed, leading to A01 (2) and A04 (1). [DIR004, REL011]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change in which **A01 was inserted as a new, mandatory step, executed exactly once per case early in the flow**. A02, which earlier ran almost always straight to A04, now hands over to A01 in about half of its transitions; A04 now leads to A01 in about 39 in 100 of its transitions; and A01 continues mainly to A04, A07, A03 or A10. Because A01 and A04 appear in both orders in similar proportions, the insertion point varies from case to case — sometimes before A04, sometimes after it. The later positions of A03, A06, A07 and A10 and the roughly one-step growth of their distances from A02/A04 are consistent with this single insertion. The screening found 34 qualifying items against roughly 0.28 expected for an unchanged process, so the ensemble cannot be explained as sampling noise, and the occurrence, direction, position, relation and gap indicators all point to the same structure. Limitations: POS and LAG are based on event positions, so the shifts are positional (though here corroborated by the relation and gap items), and the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely new step from a re-labelled existing one, nor does it show why the change occurred. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL003, POS002, POS004, POS005, POS007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

Other differences: A09, previously the closing step in about 45 in 100 cases (always last, following A06), is not observed in any of the 200 later traces — a disappearance the A01 insertion does not explain; strictly this means "not observed", although 0 of 200 after 90 of 200 makes pure sampling an unlikely explanation. A06's new routing (to A01/A04) rests on only 3 later observations and is too weak to characterise. [OCC002, POS006, REL006, REL008, REL010, REL013, DIR004, REL011]
