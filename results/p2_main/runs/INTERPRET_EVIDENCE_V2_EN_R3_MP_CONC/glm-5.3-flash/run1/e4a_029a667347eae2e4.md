### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in timing as a possibility: in the later window, the elapsed time between the completion of A02 and the completion of A08, and between the completion of A06 and the completion of A08, is consistently longer — roughly doubled for A02→A08 (median about 0.9 h to 1.7 h) and about 2.5× for A06→A08 (median about 0.12 h to 0.23 h). Both intervals end at A08, so the behaviour is consistent with longer intervals into A08 from both of its predecessors. Supporting signs: both time items moved upward in every distribution component, each difference exceeds all but about 1 in 2000 random re-splits of the pooled data, and the time family produced 2 qualifying items where roughly 0.012 would be expected if nothing had changed, while the control-flow family produced 0 items against about 0.29 expected, so the structure around these activities shows no qualifying change. Limitations: the two items share A08 and likely reflect one underlying difference rather than two independent confirmations; TWAIT measures elapsed time between consecutive completion events, so the evidence does not show where within the interval the extra time arises.
