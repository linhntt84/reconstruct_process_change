### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added to the process and placed between the upstream activities A02 and A09 (and, on about half of its outgoing transitions, A08) and the downstream activities A03 and A12. In the earlier window A02 and A09 led directly to A03 or A12, each roughly half–half; in the later window both lead exclusively to A01, which hands over to A12 (about 53%) or A03 (about 47%), and every measured distance from A02 or A09 downstream moved about one position later, consistent with A01 sitting in between. The change is shown directly rather than only through frequencies: A01 runs from absent to present in all 200 after-traces with about 2.1 occurrences per trace, the transitions into and out of A01 are observed on the edges themselves, and the A01–A02, A01–A03 and A01–A12 relations flip from never co-occurring to always co-occurring. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL005, LAG001, LAG004, LAG011, LAG014]

The volume of qualifying evidence (39 items against roughly 0.31 expected if nothing had changed) cannot be explained by sampling variation as a whole. What the evidence cannot rule out is that A13's reduced participation is an independent routing change rather than a consequence of A01's addition; also, positions and lags are measured in event positions, so the placement claim holds in ordering terms only, not in clock time. [REL006, DIR005]

Other differences: A13 now always leads to A08 and A08 splits its outgoing transitions between A01 (about 51%) and A13 (about 49%), where before A08 always led to A13; A08 and A13 additionally repeat within traces at short distances in the later window, though the before-side of those repetition profiles rests on only about 5 traces each. [DIR003, DIR005, LAG008, LAG017, LAG019]
