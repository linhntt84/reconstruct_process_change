### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A48 and A33 was restructured: in the earlier window every case executed the five activities A20, A21, A30, A31 and A32 (in varying order) between A48 and A33, whereas in the later window these are no longer executed and a case instead runs exactly one of the alternatives A19, A22, A23, A24 or A25 after A48, leading directly on to A33. Supporting signs: the near-complete participation flip (about 199 of 200 down to 1–3 of 200, and 0–3 up to 32–49 of 200), the rerouting of A48's outgoing transitions toward the new activities with about 200 observations per window, direct edges from each new activity to A33, near-zero co-occurrence among the new activities with shares summing to about one per case, and consistent position shifts. The screening context reinforces this: 158 control-flow items qualified against roughly 1 expected without change, so the ensemble cannot be attributed to random sampling of a stable process. [OCC003, OCC005, DIR012, DIR006, REL059, LAG029, POS014]

The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows draw on different case populations (for example different case types) rather than a modified process, and the old block still appears in a handful of later cases (1–3 of 200); the "exactly one of five" reading is inferred from pairwise co-occurrence and share sums rather than observed for all five activities jointly. [OCC009, OCC010, REL059]

Other differences: A04 became more frequent (about 10 → 34 of 100 cases), occurs earlier in the case, and now leads mostly directly to A16, whereas earlier its outgoing transitions were spread over many activities; A15 and A16 also occur slightly later in the case. These changes may be connected to the same restructuring, but the evidence does not tie them directly to the A48–A33 segment. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, POS001, POS002, POS003]
