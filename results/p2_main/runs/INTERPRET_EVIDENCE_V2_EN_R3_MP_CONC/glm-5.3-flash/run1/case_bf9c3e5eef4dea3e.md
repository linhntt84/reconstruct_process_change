### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the segment around A28: the block A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27, which previously executed shortly before A28 and fed into it, now executes after A28 — A28 precedes the block, A47 (previously a feeder of the block) now leads into A28, and A29/A30 send new transitions to the block before it returns to A30/A34. This rests on direct relational evidence: each block activity still co-occurs with A28 in about 19–24 in 100 traces, but the order between them flipped (block-before-A28 about 17–22 in 100 before, A28-before-block about 20–24 in 100 after); the outgoing transitions of all five block activities moved from A28 to A30/A34; and A47, A29 and A30 rewired as described. The volume of qualifying items (84 control-flow items versus roughly 1 expected without change) and the agreement across occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators make chance an unlikely overall explanation, although these items overlap and are not independent confirmations. Limitations: the exact new path between A28/A29, the block and A30/A34 is only partly resolved (several new adjacencies rest on few observations), and a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants cannot be distinguished from a modified flow.
