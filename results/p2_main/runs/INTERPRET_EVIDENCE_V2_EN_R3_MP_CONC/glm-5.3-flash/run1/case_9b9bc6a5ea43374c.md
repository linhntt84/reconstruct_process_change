### Observed differences

- Activity A03 is not observed at all in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces). In the later window it runs once per trace in about 103 of 200 traces (roughly 52 in 100), never repeats within a trace, and sits at about a third of the trace length (median position ~0.38). [OCC001, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A03 executes as an additional step within the pre-existing optional block formed by A05, A08, A11 and A12: the block still runs in about half the traces, but whenever it runs, A03 runs exactly once with it, placed after A05 and always before A11, interleaving with A08 and A12, with new transitions A05→A03, A08→A03, A12→A03 and outgoing edges from A03 to A08, A11 and A12. This rests on direct participation evidence (0 to ~52 of 100 traces, matching the block's ~51–52 of 100 share), direct relational evidence (co-occurrence with each block member rising from 0 to ~52 of 100 while "member without A03" disappeared), and direct successor evidence (new edges into and out of A03); the outward-shifted step distances between block members are consistent with A03 inserted among them, though many items describe the same underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. The volume of qualifying items also far exceeds what a stable process produces: 17 items against roughly 0.17 expected by chance in the control-flow family, with every item's difference larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A03 already existed earlier but was too rare to observe — zero participation only means it was not observed — and the exact slot of A03 relative to A08 and A12 remains mixed, so its precise placement within the block is only partly pinned down. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, LAG002, LAG005]

Other differences: A05 now typically opens the block (median position 0) and repeats less within its traces (~1.8 vs ~2.4 outgoing transitions per containing trace), and the A05→A11 distance became uniformly 4 steps where it previously took 3 or 5; A11's position barely moved. These may be part of the same restructuring but are not required by the main hypothesis. [POS002, DIR002, LAG001, POS003]
