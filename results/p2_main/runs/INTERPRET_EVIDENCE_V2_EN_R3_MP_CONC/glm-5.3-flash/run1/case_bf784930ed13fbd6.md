### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The 74 qualifying items (all control-flow) are many times the fewer than 1 expected if nothing had changed, so chance alone cannot account for the evidence as a whole. Within that, the evidence supports the possibility that the late-process block was rerouted and reordered: in the earlier window A47 led directly to A28, A28 preceded the group A19, A20, A25, A26, A27, whose exits split between A30 and A34 (with A30 often returning to the group) while A29 fed A30 and the group members; in the later window A47 leads into A19/A20/A25/A26/A27, each member proceeds almost always to A28, and A29, A30 and A34 form a fixed consecutive tail. The routing profiles of A47 and the group members were replaced almost wholesale, the pairwise orders flipped consistently in both relations (relative to A28 and to A29), the group's positions moved from about 60% to about a third of the trace, and step distances changed accordingly (A47 to A28 from one step to two–three; A29 to A30 and A30 to A34 now immediate) [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011, REL015, REL016, REL018, REL020, REL023, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG023, LAG026, LAG027]. Limitations: the routing shares for the group members rest on roughly 30–52 traces per window, the many indicators describe one underlying rerouting rather than independent confirmations, and a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants cannot be distinguished from a rewritten flow on this evidence.
