### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-way choice between A14, A15 and A20, which in the earlier window sat between A36 and A21, is no longer executed in the later window, so that A36 leads directly to A21 and this group of activities is bypassed. This rests on direct relational evidence: A36's successor profile changed from a roughly 35/30/35 split across A20, A15 and A14 to A21 alone, the A36-to-A21 distance shrank from two steps to one in all 200 traces, and participation in A14, A15 and A20 fell from about 30–35 in 100 cases to zero, where previously each of the three had always led to A21. The control-flow family produced 23 qualifying items against roughly 0.37 expected for an unchanged process, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole, and the occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators all describe the same structure consistently. A limitation remains: zero participation strictly means only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed in the later sample, so a very rare continued execution of them cannot be fully excluded, although the all-case lag and successor evidence makes any frequent persistence implausible.
