### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A43 and A28 was restructured from a choice into a full pass: in the earlier window each case executed A43, then exactly one of the five alternatives A14, A17, A18, A19 or A20, then A28, whereas in the later window cases execute A43, then all five of A15, A16, A25, A26 and A27 — each exactly once and in no fixed order between them — and then A28. The occurrence, routing and relation indicators all reflect the same underlying difference: both activity sets switch between 0 and 200 of 200 traces; A43's outgoing transitions move wholly from the old set to the new set; the old set is mutually exclusive while the new set co-occurs universally with roughly even mixed pairwise orders; and A43 shifts earlier while A28 shifts later, as expected when the block between them grows from one activity to five. With 92 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.7 expected if nothing had changed, sampling fluctuation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC002, OCC003, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, DIR011, REL029, REL010, REL018, REL019, POS011, POS012]

Limitations: zero participation only means the old activities were not observed in the later window, so actual removal versus an simply unexercised path cannot be distinguished; the mixed pairwise orders show the absence of a fixed order, not that the five new activities run concurrently; and positions are event positions, not clock times. A complete shift in the mix of two coexisting process variants is a competing explanation that window-level evidence cannot rule out. [OCC001, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL010]

Other differences: The elapsed time between consecutive completions of A44 and A45 roughly halved (mean about 1.0 → 0.5 hours; 157 vs 148 gaps). This is the only qualifying time item (1 against roughly 0.01 expected by chance in its family), so on its own it is the kind of result an unchanged process still produces occasionally, and no other evidence corroborates a timing change.
