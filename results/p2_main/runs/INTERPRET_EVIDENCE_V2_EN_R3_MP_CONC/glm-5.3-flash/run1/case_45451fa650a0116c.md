### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the two alternative paths after A20 exchanged frequencies: the path in which A20 is followed by A21, A22 and A23 — executed together, in either order — became the dominant route (about 4 in 5 traces, up from about 1 in 5), while the path through A24, A25 and A26 became the rare alternative (about 1 in 5, down from about 4 in 5). The shift is visible directly in A20's outgoing shares and consistently reflected in the participation and co-occurrence figures; both branches were observed in a substantial share of traces in both windows, so this reads as a change in how often existing alternatives are taken rather than a new or removed path. With 73 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 1 expected by chance, sampling alone cannot account for the evidence set. [DIR005, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL025]

The many relation items involving A30, A34 and A35 (the new branch group now co-occurs with them in about 79 in 100 traces, the old group in only about 21) mirror the same swap and are consequences of it rather than independent confirmations.
