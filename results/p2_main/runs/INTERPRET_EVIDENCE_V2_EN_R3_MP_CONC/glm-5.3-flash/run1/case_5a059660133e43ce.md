### Observed differences

- A01 was not observed in any of the 200 earlier traces. In the later window it occurs in about 22 in 100 traces, always exactly once and always as the last event of the trace. [OCC001, POS001]
- In the later window, A01 never occurs without A02, and about 21 in 100 of A02's observed outgoing transitions now lead to A01 — an edge not observed earlier; the frequency of this transition matches how often A01 appears. A01 never shares a trace with A06 or with A15. [DIR001, REL001, REL002, REL003]
- A06 changed from an ending role to a mid-process role: earlier it appeared in about 14 in 100 traces, always at the final position, with no outgoing transition ever observed; in the later window it appears in about 16 in 100 traces around the middle (median relative position about 0.5) and all 32 of its observed outgoing transitions lead to A15. [POS002, DIR002]
- A15 now comes later: earlier it typically followed A02 about one step behind (about 52 in 100 observed pairs); in the later window it typically follows about two steps behind (about 67 in 100), which fits A06 now lying between the two. [LAG002]
- A12 receded: participation fell from about 18 in 100 traces to about 10 in 100, its median relative position moved earlier (from about 0.5 to about 0.28), and when it occurs it now follows A02 about one step later rather than two to three steps. [OCC002, POS003, LAG001]
- A08 stopped feeding A12: earlier, about 84 in 100 of A08's observed outgoing transitions went to A12; in the later window none do, and A08 leads mainly to A09 (about 46 in 100), A04 (about 27) and A02 (about 23). The later A08→A12 lag profile rests on only five observations, so that part is weakly supported. [DIR003, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new concluding activity was added to the flow: A01, unobserved in the earlier window, is executed once as the final step in about 22 in 100 later traces, reached directly from A02. The change is shown directly and consistently across indicators that describe the same behaviour — participation (0 of 200 vs 44 of 200 traces), position (always the last event), routing (the new A02→A01 edge at about 21 in 100 of A02's outgoing transitions) and relations (A01 co-occurs only with A02, never with A06 or A15). Fourteen items qualified against roughly 0.23 expected for an unchanged process, far beyond what random re-splits of these windows produce. Limitations: absence in the earlier window strictly means only that A01 was not observed there, and the evidence cannot show whether A01 took over an ending previously formed by another activity. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, REL001, REL002, REL003]

Other differences: A06 ceased to be a terminal activity and now leads to A15, which consequently follows A02 about two steps later instead of directly; separately, A12 became less frequent and earlier, and A08 now routes mainly to A09, A04 and A02 instead of to A12. These reroutings may belong to the same restructuring of the flow around A02, but the evidence does not establish them as a single change. [DIR002, POS002, LAG002, OCC002, DIR003]

A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that A01 is a pre-existing rare variant that happened to appear only in the later sample; 0 versus 44 occurrences in equal-sized windows makes pure sampling fluctuation unlikely, but two finite windows cannot exclude it entirely. [OCC001]
