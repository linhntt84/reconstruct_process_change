### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a timing change on the transition from A10 to A11: in the later window, the elapsed time between consecutive completions of A10 and A11 is about one hour longer than before, roughly doubling from about 1.0 to about 2.0 hours. [TWAIT001]

Supporting signs: in the time family, one item qualified against roughly 0.009 expected without a change — more than a hundred times the chance expectation — while the control-flow family produced none (0 against about 0.26), so the signal is specific to this gap rather than a global slowdown; the change is measured directly by the indicator, is consistent across all distribution components, and is backed by roughly 300 gap observations in each window with a difference larger than in all but about 1 of 2,000 random re-splits. [TWAIT001]

Limitations: TWAIT measures elapsed time between consecutive completion events, so the evidence cannot show whether the extra hour comes from waiting, processing or handover; and a compositional shift — a different mix of cases reaching the A10→A11 pair with longer gaps — that stays below the control-flow thresholds cannot be fully ruled out.
