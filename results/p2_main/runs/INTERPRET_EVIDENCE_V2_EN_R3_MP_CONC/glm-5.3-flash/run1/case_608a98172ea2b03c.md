### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the two-way alternation between A01 and A04 seen in the earlier window was replaced in the later window by a one-way sequence: A06 now always leads to A01, A01 always leads to A04, and A04 exits to A02 or A03, while the direct return A04→A01 and the direct branches A01→A02 and A01→A03 were no longer observed. This is a change in ordering constraints — from a mixed order between A01 and A04 to a fixed A01-before-A04 — together with a rerouting of how A02 and A03 are reached, now only through A04. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001]

The successor profiles rest on about 380 outgoing transitions per window, and the pair relation, positions and distances all shift in the direction this one-way flow predicts; with 20 qualifying items against roughly 0.13 expected by chance for the 27 tested units, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [POS001, POS002, LAG004, LAG008]

Limitations: many items describe the same underlying traces rather than independent confirmations, and an unobserved edge is not proof of removal — the branches absent from the later window (A04→A01, A01→A02, A01→A03) could still occur at low frequency below what 200 traces per window reliably detect. [DIR001, DIR002]

Other differences: repeats of the same activity within five steps — A01→A01 and A04→A04, each in roughly 40–45 of 100 traces in both windows — now occur only at distance 5, where before they spread over distances 4 and 5. [LAG001, LAG011]
