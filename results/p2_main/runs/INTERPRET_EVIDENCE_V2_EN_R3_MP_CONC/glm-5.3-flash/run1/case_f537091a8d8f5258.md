### Observed differences

- The same group of activities (A04, A05, A06, A07, A09) appears in about half of the traces in both windows — about 48 in 100 earlier and about 55 in 100 later — so what changed is their order, not whether they run. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- Before, A05 and A06 ran at the end, after A09: A07 preceded A05 and A09 preceded A05 in every one of roughly 96 co-occurrence observations, and the same held for A06; A05 or A06 coming first was never observed. In the later window this reversed: A05 preceded A07 and A09 in essentially all of roughly 109 co-occurrence observations, and A06 preceded A07 in about 41 in 100 and A09 in about 50 in 100, while the old orders dropped to about 14 and 5 in 100. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]
- The internal order of A05 and A06 also changed: before, both orders appeared about equally often (about 25 vs 23 in 100 traces) and each typically handed on to the other (A05 led to A06 in about 96 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, and A06 to A05 likewise); in the later window A05 always preceded A06 (about 55 in 100 traces) and the A06→A05 transition disappeared. [REL001, DIR003, DIR004]
- Event positions shifted accordingly: A05 moved from a median position of 0.8 to 0.4, A06 from 1.0 to 0.6, A07 from 0.4 to 0.8, and A09 from 0.6 to 1.0, so A09 is now typically the last event — only 12 outgoing transitions from A09 were observed later, against about 96 before — while A04 stayed near the start (about 0.2) in both windows. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, DIR006]
- Successors and spacings match this rewiring: from A01, the share continuing to A04 fell from about 76 in 100 to about 45 while A05 rose from about 1 to about 45; from A04, the share going to A07 fell from about 80 to about 9 while A05 rose to about 51; from A06, the hand-on to A05 (about 96 in 100) was replaced by one to A07 (about 76 in 100). Gaps follow suit: A01→A05 and A04→A05 now sit 1–2 steps apart versus 4–5 and 3–4 before, while A04→A07 stretched from 1 step (89 in 100) to 2–4 steps. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, LAG002, LAG006, LAG008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment A05 → A06 was relocated from the end of the process, where it ran after A09 in no fixed internal order, to a position before A07, where it runs as a fixed sequence with A05 first; as a result A07 and especially A09 now execute later (A09 typically last), while A01 and A04 keep their early positions and now often lead directly into A05. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, LAG002, LAG006]

The order reversals are observed directly rather than inferred, the position shifts of all four affected activities point the same way, and the successor and gap changes match the new adjacencies. With 28 qualifying items against roughly 0.26 expected without change, sampling variation cannot explain the pattern as a whole; and because the A05-first order was entirely absent earlier (0 of about 96 observations), this is a change in ordering constraints, not a shift in how often pre-existing paths are taken. [REL002, REL003]

Limitations: positions are event positions, not clock times, so "relocated" refers to order within traces, not calendar timing; and the evidence cannot fully rule out that a rare pre-existing variant with this order simply went unsampled in the earlier window, though 0 hits in about 96 observations makes that unlikely. [REL002, REL003]

Other differences: A04's position became more variable (its middle 50% of positions spread from a constant 0.2 to spanning 0–0.4) and about 23 in 100 of its outgoing transitions now return to A01, up from about 10 in 100; the slight rise in how often the whole block appears (about 48 vs 55 in 100 traces) may also be sampling noise. [POS001, POS002, DIR002]
