### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which, in the later window, virtually every case executes exactly one of five mutually exclusive alternative activities — A14, A15, A20, A21 or A22 — between A24 and A26, where earlier cases ran directly A24 → A25 → A26; the position of this step varies case by case, directly after A24 in about half the cases and directly after A25 in about half. The pattern is shown directly and consistently: the five activities are absent from all 200 earlier traces yet present in about 17–23% of later traces each, never co-occur and never repeat; A24 and A25 now route about half of their outgoing transitions to them; A24 always precedes and the new activity always precedes A26; and the A24→A26 distance grows from 2 to 3 in 199 of 200 later traces, indicating exactly one such step per case. With 34 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.44 expected for an unchanged process, this pattern as a whole cannot be explained by sampling variation. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR006, DIR007, REL004, REL006, REL009, REL011, REL013, REL015, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: the evidence shows only participation, order and event distances, so it cannot establish whether these five activities are genuinely new work or a finer-grained recording of a step that existed earlier under other codes; zero participation in the earlier window means only that they were not observed there, and the outgoing profiles of the individual new activities rest on only about 33–45 traces each. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

Other differences: the elapsed time between consecutive completions of A10 and A11 halved, from a mean of about 1.0 hour in the earlier window to about 0.5 hour in the later one (median 1.01 to 0.51 hours, with a correspondingly narrower spread), on similar observation counts (319 vs 336); the time family produced 1 qualifying item against about 0.009 expected without a change, so this is a distinct, well-supported timing change in its own right, and the evidence cannot establish whether it is connected to the structural change above. [TWAIT001]
