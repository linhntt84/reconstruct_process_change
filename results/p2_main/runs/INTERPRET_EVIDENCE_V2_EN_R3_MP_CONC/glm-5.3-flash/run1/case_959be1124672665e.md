### Observed differences

- Three activities disappeared in the later window: A02, A03 and A08 ran in about 47, 21 and 47 of 100 traces before (A02 also repeated within about 21 of 100 traces), but none of the three appears in any of the 200 later traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004]
- The flow was rerouted around them: A05 and A18, which previously sent about 50 and 53 of their outgoing transitions to A02, now send about 46 and 59 to A10; A13 and A19, which previously led only to A03, now lead to A05 and A18 (about 59/41 and 50/50); A10, which previously led only to A08, now leads only to A01, which in turn now always leads to A11. [DIR004, DIR013, DIR009, DIR014, DIR008, DIR001]
- Relations confirm the disappearance: A01–A02 and A02–A10 co-occurred in about 47 of 100 traces before and never after, with the surviving partner now appearing alone in about 48 of 100 traces, and A03 and A08 no longer share traces with any former partner. [REL001, REL012, REL023]
- The A01/A16 segment swapped places: before, A16 usually ran before A01 (about 27 of 100 traces) and A01 led to A06, A07 and A14 (about 44/31/25); in the later window A16 leads to A06, A07 and A14 (about 43/37/20), A01 sits earlier (median position about 0.46 vs 0.80) and A16 later (0.80 vs 0.70). [DIR011, REL005, POS001, POS007]
- A07 and A14 became rarer (about 47 down to 17 and 31 of 100 traces) and now occur only at the final position of a trace, where before they sat near the end with A07 leading to A14. [OCC003, OCC005, POS004, POS006, DIR005]
- A09 and A17 now lead to each other in about half of their outgoing transitions (about 51 and 49 in 100), which was not observed before, when both led mainly to A05 and A18. [DIR007, DIR012]
- Many pairwise event gaps shifted by roughly one position (for example A10 to A01 now follows after 1 step in about 57 of 100 observed cases versus 4 steps before), though several of these rest on fewer than 30 observations. [LAG022, LAG003, LAG005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural rewiring in which A02, A03 and A08 are no longer executed and their predecessors connect directly to the activities those three used to reach: A05 and A18 now lead to A10, A13 and A19 now lead to A05 and A18, and the chain A10–A08–A11 now runs as A10–A01–A11. The change is shown directly by three agreeing indicator families — occurrence (participation fell from about 47/21/47 to 0 of 100 traces), outgoing transitions (the predecessors' successor profiles changed exactly at the removed activities), and relations (co-occurrence with A02, A03 and A08 collapsed to zero) — and with 129 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected for an unchanged process, chance is not a plausible explanation for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, DIR008, DIR009, DIR013, DIR014, REL001, REL012, REL023]

Limitations: zero participation shows only that these activities were not observed in the later sample, so the evidence cannot rule out that the later window draws from a different mix of pre-existing variants in which these paths never run; the rerouting through A13 and A19 also rests on fewer observations (about 24–50 outgoing transitions before, 34–38 after) because those activities are rare. [DIR009, DIR014, OCC001]

Other differences: the A01/A16 swap described above, in which A16 took over A01's former successors A06, A07 and A14 and the order between A01 and A16 flipped [DIR011, REL005, POS001, POS007]; the decline and terminal positioning of A07 and A14 [OCC003, OCC005, POS004, POS006]; the new A09–A17 routing [DIR007, DIR012]; and widespread one-step shifts in pairwise gaps, a few of which are based on as few as 2–4 traces. [LAG022, LAG004, LAG006]
