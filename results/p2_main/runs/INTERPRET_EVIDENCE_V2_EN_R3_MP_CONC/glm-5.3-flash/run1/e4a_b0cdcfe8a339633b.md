### Observed differences

- Six activities — A28, A30, A31, A32, A33 and A34 — appeared in roughly half of the traces in the earlier window (49–52 in 100) and in almost every trace in the later window (99–100 in 100), each occurring at most once per trace in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- In the earlier window, the two sets {A28, A30, A31} and {A32, A33, A34} were alternatives: cross-set pairs never occurred in the same trace (0 in 100), and each set appeared alone in about half of the traces. In the later window, cross-set pairs occur together in about 98 in 100 traces, always with a first-set activity before a second-set activity. [REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024]
- A25, present in all traces, preceded whichever set ran in the earlier window (co-occurrence with each of the six at 49–52 in 100, A25 alone otherwise); in the later window it precedes each of the six in about 99 in 100 traces. A26 similarly moved from following only one set per trace to following each of the six in about 99 in 100 traces. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- Successor profiles agree: A25 now leads almost only to A28/A30/A31 (its share of outgoing transitions to A32 fell from about 16 in 100 to under 1 in 100, with A33/A34 no longer observed), and A28/A30/A31 now lead into the second set instead of directly to A26 (A26's share of A28's outgoing transitions fell from about 36 in 100 to under 1 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- Within each set the order remains unfixed (e.g., A28 before A30 in about 49 in 100 co-occurrences in the later window, A30 before A28 in about 50 in 100), so no new fixed order emerged inside the sets. [REL013, REL014, REL018, REL025, REL026, REL027]
- Positions and gaps are consistent with an inserted block: A25's mean relative position moved earlier (0.67 to 0.62) and A26's later (0.80 to 0.83); the step distance from A25 to the second-set activities and from first-set activities to A26 grew from 1–3 steps to 4–5 steps. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]
- On the timing side, the elapsed time between consecutive completions of A20 and A27 roughly doubled (median about 0.93 h to 1.84 h), as did the elapsed time between completions of A29 and A27 (median about 0.14 h to 0.33 h). [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the middle of the process: the two three-activity sets {A28, A30, A31} and {A32, A33, A34}, which in the earlier window were mutually exclusive alternatives (one set per trace, in roughly equal shares, never both), were in the later window executed together in essentially every trace in a fixed sequence A25 → {A28/A30/A31, any internal order} → {A32/A33/A34, any internal order} → A26, turning all six activities from optional (about 50 in 100 traces) into effectively mandatory (99–100 in 100). The relational evidence shows this directly — cross-set co-occurrence rose from 0 to about 98 in 100 with a fixed inter-set order — and participation, successor, position and gap indicators all agree; the volume of qualifying control-flow items (51 against about 0.6 expected without a change) rules out a chance explanation for the family as a whole. Limitations: about 2 in 100 later traces still show only one of the sets, so the old either/or behaviour may persist rarely, and a change in the mix of cases entering the process, rather than in the process itself, cannot be ruled out from these two windows alone. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL015, REL019, REL022, DIR001, DIR002, POS003, POS006, LAG001, LAG004]

Other differences: a timing change stands apart from this hypothesis — the elapsed time between consecutive completion events roughly doubled on two transitions (A20→A27: median about 0.93 h to 1.84 h; A29→A27: about 0.14 h to 0.33 h), a timing hypothesis in its own right whose connection to the structural change the evidence cannot establish. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
