### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change involving A04, A05, A07 and A01: the rework loop in which A05 mediated repeated executions of A04 (A04 → A05 → A04 in roughly 46 in 100 earlier traces) is no longer observed, A04 now repeats directly (a self-loop accounts for about half of its later outgoing transitions), and A01 appears as a new closing step executed once after A07 in every trace, with A07 shifted earlier from the trace end. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS004, REL003, LAG001]

The reading is direct and consistent: the participation changes are large and observed on the activities themselves (A01 from 0 to 200 of 200 traces; A05 from 93 to 0 of 200), the rewiring is seen on the edges rather than inferred (A04→A05 replaced by A04→A04; A07→A01 newly observed), and the relational, positional and lag items agree with the same picture. Screening qualified 16 items where about 0.09 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of stable behaviour produce. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: several items describe the same underlying differences (A05's disappearance and A01's appearance each show in occurrence, positional, relational and routing items), so the items are not independent confirmations. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A05, or A01 in the earlier window, still occurs but was not sampled: zero participation only means the activity was not observed in that window's 200 traces, so outright removal and extreme rarity cannot be distinguished. [OCC001, OCC002]

Other differences: the slight advance of A04's mean relative position (about 0.50 to 0.42) and the wider spread of A04–A07 distances are small shifts consistent with the restructuring but not part of the main hypothesis. [POS002, LAG002]
