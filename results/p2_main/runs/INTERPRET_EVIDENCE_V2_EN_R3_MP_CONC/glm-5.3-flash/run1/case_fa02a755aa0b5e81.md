### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural reconfiguration involving A01, A02, A05, A06, A11 and A12: A02 is no longer executed in the observed traces, and the sequence around A01 was reordered — A01 moved from a late position, preceded by A11 and A12 and followed immediately by A05, to the start of the trace, now followed by A06, while A05 is now reached directly from A11 and A12 rather than from A01. The hypothesis rests on direct relational and positional evidence (a successor change at A01 from 100% A05 to 100% A06, clean order flips with A11 and A12, and A01's shift to the first position), and the indicators agree with one another; the volume of qualifying items (25 control-flow items against roughly 0.22 expected without change) is far beyond what random re-splits of these windows produce, so chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole.
