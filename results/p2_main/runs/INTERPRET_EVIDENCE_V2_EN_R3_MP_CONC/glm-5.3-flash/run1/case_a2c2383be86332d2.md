### Observed differences

- A07 was present in every one of the 200 earlier traces (about 2.1 occurrences per trace, repeated within the same trace in roughly half of the cases), while in the later window it does not appear at all. [OCC003, POS003]
- Routing around A07 changed: in the earlier window every observed outgoing transition of A08 led to A07, and A07's own transitions split about 52/48 between A05 and A06; in the later window A08 leads directly to A05 (about 52 in 100) and A06 (about 48 in 100), essentially taking over A07's earlier split, and A07 shows no outgoing transitions. [DIR002, DIR003]
- A01, not observed at all earlier, appears in about 35 in 100 later traces, always as the first event of the trace, and every observed A01 transition leads to A03. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001]
- Earlier, A03, A06 and A08 always occurred without A01; in the later window A01 co-occurs with each of them in about 35 in 100 traces and precedes them (A01 before A06 in all such co-occurrences). [REL002, REL004, REL006]

- Event-position distances between surviving activities shortened: A08–A06 went from always 2 positions apart to 1 position in about 47 in 100 observations (4 positions in about 18 in 100), A03–A06 from 3 to 2 (or 5), and pairs such as A02–A05, A05–A06 and the A03/A05/A08 self-pairs from 4 to 3. - A02's participation fell from about 48 in 100 earlier traces to about 28 in 100 later traces; earlier it always co-occurred with A07, later it appears only without A07. [OCC002, REL007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural hypothesis: A07 was removed from the flow — or its branch stopped being exercised — so that the earlier chain A08 → A07 → A05/A06 (with A07 recurring about twice per trace) became, in the later window, A08 leading directly to A05 or A06; in addition, about a third of later traces now start with A01, which always leads to A03. The screening context makes chance an implausible explanation for the collection of differences: 35 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.19 would be expected without a change. The hypothesis is shown directly (A07's participation falls from all 200 traces to none, A01's rises from none to 69 of 200; A08's successor profile changes from A07 to A05/A06) and consistently across indicators (A07's co-occurrence with A03, A06 and A08 drops from every trace to none, and event-position distances between downstream activities shorten, e.g. A08–A06 from 2 to 1). Limitations: zero participation only shows A07 was not observed in the later window, so outright removal cannot be distinguished from a path that simply never triggered in this sample, and positions are event positions rather than clock time. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 already existed as a very rare path that was merely unobserved in the earlier 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL008, REL010, REL011, LAG013]

Other differences:
- A02's decline (about 48 to about 28 in 100 traces) may be connected to A07's disappearance, since A02 previously always co-occurred with A07, but the evidence does not establish how. [OCC002, REL007]
- A03 and A08 now sit slightly later within traces (median position from about a quarter to a third of the trace for A03, and from half to two-thirds for A08); these are event-position shifts that may simply reflect the changed trace length. [POS002, POS004]
