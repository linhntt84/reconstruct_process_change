### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that an additional step now runs between A38 and A23: in the later window every case executes exactly one of five alternative activities — A14, A15, A20, A21 or A22 — immediately after A38 and before A23, whereas in the earlier window A38 led straight to A23. The alternative is chosen per case in roughly even shares (about 18–22 in 100 each) and runs at most once per case. [DIR006, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL005, REL006, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016]

The support is direct and consistent: 34 control-flow items qualified where fewer than one would be expected by chance in an unchanged process; A38's successor profile flipped from 100% A23 to a 100% split across the five; the A38→A23 distance moved from one to two events in all 200 later traces; and the five activities went from zero participation to mutually exclusive shares that together cover every later trace, with each item's difference exceeding about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [DIR006, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL001, REL010]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that the five activities were not exercised in those 200 traces, not that they were absent from the process, and the evidence cannot show what determines which of the five alternatives a case takes. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

Other differences: none — all 34 qualifying items concern the A38–A23 segment and the five activities inserted there; A23's slight downstream position shift is part of the same difference. [OCC001–OCC005, DIR001–DIR006, POS001–POS006, REL001–REL016, LAG001]
