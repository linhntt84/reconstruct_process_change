### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, cases split after A26 into two mutually exclusive branches: about 52 in 100 traces ran A26 → A27 → A28 with A27 immediately before A28, while about 48 in 100 ran A26 → a block of A32, A33 and A38 → A28; A27 practically never occurred together with A32, A33 or A38 (at most about 1 in 200 traces). - Within that second branch, A32, A33 and A38 always occurred together (about 48–49 in 100 traces) but in varying order (A32 before A33 in about 24 in 100, A33 before A32 in about 25 in 100), and each of the three preceded A28 in about 48–49 in 100 traces. [REL012, REL013, REL014, REL009, REL010, REL011, DIR003, DIR004]
- In the later window, A27 appears in about 199 of 200 traces (roughly once per trace), whereas A32, A33 and A38 each appear in only about 1 of 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Outgoing transitions from A26 now concentrate on A27 (about 97 in 100, versus about 51 in 100 before, when about 18 went to A38, 16 to A33 and 14 to A32), and A27 leads to A28 in about 97 in 100 transitions before and about 99 in 100 after, always immediately (distance 1). - The A26-to-A28 distance is now 2 steps in about 97 in 100 traces (before: 2 steps in about 50 in 100 and 4 steps in about 47 in 100), and A28's relative position moved slightly earlier (mean 0.55 to 0.53 of the trace). [LAG001, POS001]
- The earlier ties of A26 and A27 with A32, A33 and A38 — for example A32, A33 or A38 preceding A28 in about 48–49 in 100 traces — are gone; in the later window A26 or A27 almost never co-occur with any of the three. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch composed of A32, A33 and A38 — executed together in varying order between A26 and A28 in roughly half of the earlier cases — is no longer executed, so that virtually all cases now follow A26 → A27 → A28, with A27 changing from an option taken in about half of the cases to an essentially mandatory step. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL005, REL006, LAG001] This is a structural difference (a branch disappearing, not a re-weighting of still-observed alternatives), because A32, A33 and A38 are nearly unobserved in the later window while every element of the surviving path (A26, A27, A28 and the A27→A28 edge) is observed throughout in both windows. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR002, POS001] The screening produced 35 qualifying control-flow items where about 0.6 would be expected without a change, so the item set as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, LAG001] The supporting signs are direct and consistent: participation moves by about 48 percentage points in opposite directions, routing from A26 concentrates on A27, and the relational items show the old co-occurrence and ordering patterns vanishing. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, REL009, REL012] A limitation is that nearly all items describe the same underlying trace behaviour and are therefore not independent confirmations, and zero participation in the later window strictly means only that the branch was not observed there. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Competing explanation: the evidence cannot rule out that the A32/A33/A38 branch still exists but was simply not exercised in the later sample; however, a drop from about 97–98 supporting traces to 1 lies well outside the differences that random re-splits of these windows produce. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Other differences: A28's relative position shifted slightly earlier, consistent with the shorter path; A06 is no longer observed as a target of A26 (about 1 in 100 of A26's outgoing transitions before, none after); and a few single-observation outgoing transitions from A26 (to A08, A13, A17, A24, A25, A28) and from A27 (to A09, A14, A15) appear only in the later window. [POS001, DIR001, DIR002]
