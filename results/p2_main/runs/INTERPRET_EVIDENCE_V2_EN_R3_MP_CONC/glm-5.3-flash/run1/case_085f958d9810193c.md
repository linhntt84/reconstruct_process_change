### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment formed by A35, A36, A41, A42 and A43 was repositioned: in the later window these five activities run before A27 and A26, with A25 now flowing into them, whereas in the earlier window they ran after A26 and before A30, and A26 now leads almost directly to A30.
