The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block formed by the repeating A03 and the chain A05 → A02 → A01 is no longer executed and has been replaced by a single execution of A04: A01, A02 and A03, previously present in every trace, are unobserved later; A04, previously unobserved, now runs exactly once per trace; and A05, which previously fed into A02 (onward to A01), now leads directly into A04.
