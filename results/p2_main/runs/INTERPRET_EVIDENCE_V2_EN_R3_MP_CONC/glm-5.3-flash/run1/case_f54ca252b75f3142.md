### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was the universal opening step in the earlier window: it appeared once in every one of the 200 earlier traces, almost always as the first event (median position 0). In the later window it does not appear in any of the 200 traces. [OCC001, POS001]
- Activity A04 appeared in about 51 in 100 earlier traces (102 of 200) and in no later trace. [OCC002, POS004]
- In the earlier window, A01 routed mostly to A03 (about 46% of its outgoing transitions), A04 (about 34%) and A02 (about 16%), with small shares to A05 and A06. In the later window, no outgoing transitions from A01 were observed. [DIR001]
- A04 previously led mostly to A02 (about 70%), besides A06 (about 19%) and A01 (about 11%); in the later window no outgoing transitions from A04 were observed. [DIR003]
- The start of cases shifted: A02 moved from a median position of about 0.67 to position 0 (participation about 51% to about 47%), and A03 moved from a median of 0.5 to position 0 (participation about 49% to about 53%). Their participations sum to the full later window, so every later case begins with exactly one of them and they never occur together. [POS002, POS003]
- A02's routing narrowed: it led to A06 in about 79% of its observed outgoing transitions earlier and in 100% later (about 93 traces); its earlier transitions to A04 (about 14%) and A01 (about 7%) are gone. [DIR002]
- All observed relations involving A01 or A04 collapsed: A01 with A02 co-occurred in about 51 in 100 earlier traces and in none later; A01 with A03 about 49 to 0; A01 with A04 about 51 to 0 (in the later window neither appears in any trace); A02 with A04 about 51 to 0, where earlier A04 usually preceded A02 (about 37% vs about 15%). A03 and A04 were strict alternatives earlier (never together, about 49% vs 51%); later only A03 remains. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]
- A06 had observed outgoing transitions in only about 14 of 200 earlier traces (to A04 about 71%, to A01 about 29%); in the later window none were observed. [DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A01 and A04 are no longer executed and cases now begin directly with A02 or A03. In the earlier window every case opened with A01, which routed to A03 (about 46%), A04 (about 34%) or A02 (about 16%), with A04 typically feeding into A02; in the later window A01 and A04 appear in no trace, A02 (about 47% of cases) and A03 (about 53%) each open the trace at position 0 as mutually exclusive alternatives, and A02 is always followed by A06. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR003, POS002, POS003, DIR002]

The pattern is direct and internally consistent: participation falls from full or half support to zero (200 vs 0 observed traces for A01, 102 vs 0 for A04), all five observed activity pairs involving A01 or A04 collapse from frequent co-occurrence to none, and 15 evidence items qualified against roughly 0.1 expected for an unchanged process, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A01 and A04 were not observed in the later window; the evidence cannot rule out that these activities still run but are no longer recorded under these names (for example renamed or filtered events), and it cannot show whether their former routing was removed or redirected through unobserved steps. [OCC001, OCC002]

Other differences: A06, which earlier led back to A04 or A01 in the few cases where an outgoing transition was observed (about 14 of 200 traces), showed no outgoing transitions in the later window; given the small earlier support this is only weakly grounded and may simply follow from A04 and A01 disappearing. [DIR004]
