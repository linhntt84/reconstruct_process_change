### Observed differences

- A02 became a mandatory opening activity: it appeared in about 49 in 100 traces in the earlier window (mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, median position about halfway through the trace) and, in the later window, in all 200 traces (mean 1.8 occurrences, median position at the very start). [OCC002, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the control flow around A02 and A08 was rewired so that the two activities exchanged roles: A02 took over as the mandatory first activity of every trace, now followed directly by A04 or A03 instead of A01, while A08 — previously an ever-present opener followed by A03 or A04 — became an optional, later activity whose flow returns to A02. As part of the same picture, A01 is now reached from A03 rather than from A02, and A06 now leads to A08 rather than to A02, with A01, A06 and A07 participating in far fewer traces. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS002, POS004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC005]

The signs are direct and consistent: successor profiles changed at A02, A03, A06 and A08; the position shifts match (A02 to the start, A08 later); and the relation changes agree (A02–A04 now always together, A02–A01 and A04–A08 mostly separate). With 64 qualifying items against roughly 0.36 expected by chance in an unchanged process, and each individual difference larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits, sampling variation cannot account for the evidence as a whole. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS002, POS004, REL006, REL011, OCC002, OCC005]

Limitations: most items describe one underlying rewiring rather than independent confirmations; the two 200-trace windows leave the exact shares uncertain; and flows without qualified items (for example A04's outgoing transitions, or what precedes A02) mean the complete new structure cannot be reconstructed. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the reduced participation of A01, A06 and A07 is partly independent of the A02/A08 rewiring rather than a consequence of it. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]

Other differences: gaps between repeated occurrences of the same activity tightened — self-repeats of A01, A02, A03 and A08 moved from about 5 steps apart to about 4 steps apart. [LAG001, LAG009, LAG017, LAG038]
