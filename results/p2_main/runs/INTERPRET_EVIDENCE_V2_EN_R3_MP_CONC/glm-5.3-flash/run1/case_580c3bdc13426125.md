### Observed differences

- Activity A02 disappeared: in the earlier window it ran in every trace (about 2.2 occurrences per trace, repeating in about 59 in 100 traces); in the later window it is not observed at all (0 of 200 traces). [OCC001, POS001]
- Activity A03 appeared: it was not observed earlier, while in the later window it runs in every trace, exactly once, never repeating. [OCC002, REL001]
- Entry routing moved from A02 to A03: earlier, all of A05's observed outgoing transitions went to A02; later, about 97% go to A03, with small shares (~1.5% each) to A06 and A07. [DIR003]
- Exit routing changed accordingly: earlier A02 split to A06 (~55%) and A07 (~45%); later A03 splits to A06 (~50%) and A07 (~50%). A06, which earlier always returned to A02 (100% of its outgoing transitions), now leads to A07 (~51%), to itself (~48%), and rarely to A03 (~2%). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004]
- A02 and A03 behave as strict alternatives: earlier every trace contained A02 without A03, later every trace contains A03 without A02, and the two never co-occur in either window. A03 now always co-occurs with A05 (A05 first) and precedes A07 in about 98.5 in 100 later traces; earlier, A02 always co-occurred with both A05 and A07. [REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL007]

- Placement is preserved at the swap point: A03's mean relative position (~0.64) matches A02's earlier one (~0.64). [POS001, POS002]
- Screening context: 21 of 24 control-flow units qualified, against roughly 0.12 expected if nothing had changed. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR003, REL001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, A03 has taken over the position A02 held earlier — after A05, before the branch into A06/A07 — and the repetition structure around that point changed: the earlier loop in which A02 ran about 2.2 times per trace and A06 always returned to A02 is gone; A03 now runs exactly once per trace, and A06 either repeats itself consecutively or proceeds directly to A07. Supporting signs: the participation swap is observed directly with full support in both windows (200 of 200 traces each way); A05's routing shifted from A02 (100%) to A03 (~97%); A02 and A03 never co-occur, while each pairs with A05 and A07 exactly as the other did; A03 sits at A02's former relative position; and the lag distributions show the loop-through-A02 gaps replaced by direct A06 self-repetition. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL005, POS001, POS002, LAG003]

The volume of qualifying items (21 versus about 0.12 expected without change) rules out chance re-splitting as an explanation for the pattern as a whole, though many items describe the same underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR003, REL001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether A03 is a genuinely new step or the same step recorded under a different code, since a relabelling would look identical; the disappearance of the A02–A06 loop cannot be separated from A02's removal; and positions are event-order based, so "same position" refers to sequence location, not clock time. [OCC001, POS001, LAG003]

Other differences: A05 sits slightly later in the trace (mean relative position about 0.36 earlier versus 0.42 later), and A06 participates in fewer traces (about 59 in 100 earlier versus 49 in 100 later) at a later position (mean about 0.61 versus 0.73); these look like consequences of the same restructuring rather than separate changes. [POS003, POS004, REL003]
