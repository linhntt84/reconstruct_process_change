### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Main hypothesis: the placement of the group A30/A31/A36 relative to A21, A22 and A25 changed. In the earlier window the segment ran A20, then the group A30/A31/A36 (in no fixed internal order), then A22, then A21, then A25 immediately; in the later window it runs A20, A22, A21, then the group A30/A31/A36, then A25. That is, A30/A31/A36 now execute after A21 rather than before it, A22 immediately follows A20, and A25 now follows the group instead of immediately following A21. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Supporting signs: the order reversal is observed directly for all six activity pairs; the successor changes at A20, A21 and the three group members match it; the two distance changes are exactly what this reordering predicts; all five position shifts point the same way; and 22 items qualified against roughly 0.42 expected by chance in a stable process, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of unchanged traces produce. Limitations: the evidence shows the changed relative order but cannot establish which side "moved" — the group moving after A21 and A21/A22 moving before the group are descriptively equivalent, and the evidence cannot distinguish a single restructuring from coincident rerouting at A20 and A21. The A22-before-A21 order in each window is inferred from positions rather than a directly observed pair relation, the internal order of A30/A31/A36 remains unfixed in both windows, and four distance items rest on only 2 later-window traces.
