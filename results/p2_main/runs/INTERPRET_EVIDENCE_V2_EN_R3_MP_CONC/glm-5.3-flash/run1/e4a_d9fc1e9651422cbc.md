- Six activities became optional. Earlier, A22 and each of A31, A32, A37, A38, A39 ran in every one of the 200 traces; in the later window A22 runs in about 45 in 100 traces (91 of 200) and each of the five others in about 55 in 100 (110 of 200). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block A31, A32, A37, A38, A39 was given a new place in the flow and made an alternative to A22. Earlier every case ran A20, then the block (in mixed internal order), then A23 and A21, then A22, then A26. In the later window the frame A20 → A23 → A21 → … → A26 still runs in full, but A20 leads straight to A23, and after A21 the case takes one of two branches: the block, which runs to A26 in about 55 in 100 cases, or A22 alone, which runs to A26 in about 45 in 100; block and A22 essentially never both run, whereas earlier both always did. [DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, OCC002, REL007, REL008, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL019, REL020, REL024, REL025, REL029]

The support is direct and consistent: the control-flow screening produced 72 qualifying items against roughly 0.7 expected for an unchanged process (about 100 times the chance expectation); the relational items show the order reversal (block before A21/A23 in all earlier traces, after them in about 55 in 100 later traces) and the new exclusivity outright; the successor changes at A20 and A21 match them; and the position shifts point the same way. [DIR001, DIR002, REL008, REL012, REL015, REL019, REL020, REL024, POS004, POS006, screening]

Limitations: the ten lag items each rest on a single after-window observation and add little weight; the evidence shows the split between the two branches but cannot show what determines which branch a case takes (any such driver is unobserved); and the block's internal order stays unfixed, so the hypothesis treats the five activities as one unit. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]

Other differences: the elapsed time between consecutive completions of A26 and A19 roughly halved (mean about 0.94 to 0.45 hours, 56 observations in each window); as the only time item (1 against about 0.02 expected by chance in the time family), it is within what an unchanged process occasionally produces, so it is not established as a timing change and is kept out of the structural hypothesis above. [TWAIT001, screening]
