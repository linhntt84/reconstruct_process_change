### Observed differences

- **The order between two groups of activities reversed.** In the earlier window, A26 preceded each of A35, A36 and A41 in about 98–100 in 100 traces, and A27 likewise preceded all three in about 99–100 in 100 traces. In the later window this is inverted: A35, A36 and A41 each precede A26 in about 99 in 100 traces and precede A27 in about 98–99 in 100 traces. All six pairs still occur together in every trace in both windows, so participation did not change — only the order did. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

- **The transitions around these activities were rewired accordingly.** In the earlier window, A25 led to A27 in about 99 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, A26 led to A41 (about 37 in 100), A35 (about 34) or A36 (about 28), and A35, A36 and A41 each exited to A30 in roughly 28–37 in 100 of their outgoing transitions. In the later window, A25 leads roughly equally often to A36, A41 and A35 (about 33 in 100 each), A26 leads to A30 in about 98–99 in 100 cases, and A35, A36 and A41 now exit to A27 in about 31–34 in 100 cases, with A30 almost no longer a target. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

- **Adjacency changed at both ends of the moved segment.** A25 and A27 were immediate neighbours (one step apart) in about 99 in 100 traces in the earlier window and are about four steps apart in about 93 in 100 traces in the later window; A26 and A30 were about four steps apart in about 93 in 100 traces before and are immediate neighbours in about 98–99 in 100 traces in the later window. [LAG001, LAG003]

- **Positions within the trace shifted consistently with this.** A35, A36 and A41 now occur earlier (mean position about 0.41 of the trace length, down from about 0.58–0.59), while A27 (about 0.38 to 0.47) and A26 (about 0.53 to 0.62) occur later. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

- Several further distance items point in the same direction (e.g. A25 now sits one to three steps before each block activity; A35, A36 and A41 now sit one to three steps before A27), but each rests on only 1–2 observations in one window and was not relied upon. [LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two segments of the flow exchanged their order: the block of activities A35, A36 and A41, which in the earlier window executed after A26 in the rough sequence A25 → A27 → A26 → (A35/A36/A41) → A30, in the later window executes between A25 and A27, giving roughly A25 → (A35/A36/A41) → A27 → A26 → A30. The block's entry point moved from A26 to A25, its exit moved from A30 to A27, and A26 now connects directly to A30. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003]

Supporting signs: 26 items qualified where only about 0.63 would be expected for an unchanged process (control-flow family), so the set as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits produce; the reversal is shown directly by six relational items with co-occurrence unchanged at 100 in 100 traces in both windows, which rules out a participation change or the replacement of one path by another and isolates ordering as the difference; the successor changes sit exactly at the three connections the relocation predicts; and the two adjacency items (about 200 observations each) confirm the new neighbours on both sides of the moved block. These items largely describe the same underlying relocation rather than independent confirmations, but they agree in every detail. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Limitations: the link A27 → A26 is not directly observed (there is no outgoing-transition profile for A27) and is inferred from positions and order relations; and because the reversal is near-total in both windows, the evidence cannot distinguish a rewiring of one process from a wholesale switch between two complete variants that differ in this ordering. [POS001, POS002, REL001, REL004, DIR001, DIR002]
