### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a timing change centred on A09 as a possibility: in the later window, the elapsed time between consecutive completion events on the transitions from each of A03, A04, A05, A06, A07 and A08 into A09 roughly doubled, from about 1 hour to about 2 hours. The hypothesis rests on direct and consistent timing evidence: all six pairs into A09 show the same direction and a similar magnitude (about +0.9 to +1.1 hours), each is based on 29–43 observed intervals per window, each difference exceeds essentially all random re-splits, and 6 time items against an expectation of about 0.008 cannot be explained by chance as a whole. The absence of control-flow items (0 against about 0.23 expected) means no structural reorganisation passed the thresholds, which fits a pure timing change.
