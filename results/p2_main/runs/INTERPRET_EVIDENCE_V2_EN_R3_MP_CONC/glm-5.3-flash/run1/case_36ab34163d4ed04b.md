### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the sequence A43 → A19 → A20 → A28 became a mandatory, fixed-order part of every execution: in the earlier window about half of the traces skipped A19 and A20, with A43 passing directly on to A28, while in the later window every trace executes A19 once, then A20, with A43 directly before and A28 after. This rests on direct participation evidence (A19 and A20 rising from about 52 in 100 to 100 in 100 traces), direct relational evidence for the new order (A43 before A19 in about 99 in 100, A19 before A20 in about 99 in 100, A20 before A28 in about 98 in 100), and the disappearance of A43's direct transition to A28. Chance is an unlikely explanation: 73 control-flow items qualified where roughly 1 would be expected without a change, and a shift from 105 to 200 of 200 traces far exceeds sampling variation. Limitations: many items describe the same underlying shift and are not independent confirmations; the evidence cannot rule out that the later window draws on a case mix in which this block is always executed, nor can it distinguish a removed A43 → A28 path from one that is simply no longer taken. [OCC005, OCC006, REL021, REL025, REL026, REL030, REL031, DIR007]
