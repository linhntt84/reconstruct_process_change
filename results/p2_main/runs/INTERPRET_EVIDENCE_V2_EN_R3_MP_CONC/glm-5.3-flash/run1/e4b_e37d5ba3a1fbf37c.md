### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation as a specific possibility: across A01, A03, A13, A14, A17 and A22, assignment work moved from a group of resources active in the earlier window to a largely different group in the later window, and A24 became handled exclusively by User_30, which previously carried only about 19 in 100 of its assignments [ASSIGN001, ASSIGN002, ASSIGN003, ASSIGN004, ASSIGN005, ASSIGN006, ASSIGN007, CONC001]. The signs are direct and consistent: the same gaining and losing groups repeat across all six activities, each difference is larger than about 1999 of 2000 random re-splits, the eight resource items compare with about 0.004 expected without change, and control flow and timing produced no qualifying items, so the difference is confined to allocation rather than to structure or timing [ASSIGN001, ASSIGN002, ASSIGN003, ASSIGN004, ASSIGN005, ASSIGN006, ASSIGN007, CONC001]. Limitations: the resource codes are anonymous, so the evidence shows reallocation between groups of resources but cannot show whether it reflects a changed assignment rule or simply a changed pool of available resources — the evidence cannot separate these two explanations; the six activity profiles may also partly express one shared reallocation rather than independent per-activity changes [ASSIGN003, ASSIGN004].

Other differences: none beyond the allocation items above; no control-flow or timing indicator qualified at the configured thresholds [ASSIGN001, ASSIGN002, ASSIGN003, ASSIGN004, ASSIGN005, ASSIGN006, ASSIGN007, CONC001].
