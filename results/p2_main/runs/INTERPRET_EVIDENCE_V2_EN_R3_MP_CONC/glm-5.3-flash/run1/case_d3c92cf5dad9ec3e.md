### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is that two groups of activities exchanged their place in the sequence. In the earlier window the branch activities A19, A21 and A24 ran early (mean event position about 0.34–0.36) and the trio A20, A22 and A23 ran late (about 0.63–0.64); in the later window A20, A22 and A23 run early (about 0.38) and A19, A21 and A24 run late (about 0.66–0.68). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- All 21 activity pairs spanning the two groups reversed their order, while co-occurrence stayed unchanged. For example, A20 and A25 occur together in every trace in both windows, but A25 came first in about 995 of 1,000 traces before, whereas in the later window A20 comes first in about 995 of 1,000; the same reversal holds for A19, A21, A24, A22 and A23 against A25 and A29. [REL001–REL021]
- Successors were rewired accordingly. Before, A19, A21 and A24 led almost exclusively to A25 (about 97–98 in 100 outgoing transitions); in the later window they lead to A30 (about 95–98 in 100). Before, A20, A22 and A23 each exited to A30 in roughly 29–37 of 100 transitions; in the later window they exit to A25 in roughly 27–37 of 100. A40 now enters the trio A20/A22/A23 (about 93 in 100 combined, versus about 95 in 100 into A19/A21/A24 before), and A29 shows the mirror-image switch. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008]
- Step distances agree with the swap: A40 reached A25 in 2 steps in about 95 of 100 traces before but in 4 steps in about 92 of 100 in the later window; A29 reached A30 in 4 steps before (about 93 in 100) and in 2 steps after (about 99 in 100); A29 followed by A21 within five steps rose from about 1 in 200 traces to 58 of the 59 traces containing A21, now at distance 1. - Adjacencies around A25 flipped as expected: before, A21 and A24 were each followed by A25 within one step in about 69–71 of their traces, and A23 followed by A25 within five steps occurred in only about 1 of 200 traces; in the later window A23 is followed by A25 within one to three steps in essentially all 200 traces, while A21 or A24 directly before A25 is nearly unobserved. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- A25 and A29 kept their places between the two blocks and shifted only slightly later (mean position about 0.38 to 0.45, and 0.57 to 0.64). Participation is stable: A20, A22, A23, A25 and A29 appear in every trace in both windows, and A19, A21 and A24 each appear in about 30–39 of 100 traces in both windows, so this is a reordering of existing behaviour, not activities appearing or disappearing. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports one specific reordering as the reading of these windows: the mutually exclusive branch activities A19, A21 and A24 and the three-activity sequence A20, A22 and A23 swapped places around the A25–A29 segment. In the earlier window the flow ran A40 → one of A19/A21/A24 → A25 → … → A29 → the A20/A22/A23 sequence → A30; in the later window it runs A40 → the A20/A22/A23 sequence → A25 → … → A29 → one of A19/A21/A24 → A30. This rests on direct relational evidence (all 21 cross-pairs reversed with unchanged co-occurrence), on successor changes at A40, A29 and the six block activities, and on consistent position and distance shifts, while participation shares stayed stable — so the change lies in ordering, not in which activities run. With 46 qualified items against fewer than 1 expected if nothing had changed, sampling noise cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [DIR001–DIR008, POS001–POS006, REL001–REL021, LAG005, LAG009]

Limitations: positions and distances are event-based rather than clock-based, so the swap is established for sequence order only. The evidence cannot show whether the internal routing order within the A20/A22/A23 sequence, or behaviour in the segment between A25 and A29 where no items qualified, changed at the same time, and the few-percentage-point shifts in the trio's internal transition shares could still be sampling variation. A few LAG items rest on only 1–2 observations, but the hypothesis does not depend on them. [REL008, REL015, LAG005, LAG009]

Other differences: none — all 46 qualified items are consistent with this single exchange of the two blocks. [DIR001–DIR008, POS001–POS008, REL001–REL021, LAG001–LAG009]
