### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural reorganisation of the segment between A02 and A07: A01 was added as a new mandatory activity, executed once per case between A02 and A03 in varying order among A05 and A06, while A04 was dropped from the flow, so that A03 now leads directly to A07. The participation flips are total (A01: 0 of 200 traces before, 200 of 200 after; A04: 200 of 200 before, 0 after), the successor change at A03 is visible directly in the routing profiles, and the lag shifts (A02→A03 from three to four steps, A03→A07 from two to one) match an insertion before A03 plus a removal after it. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG004, REL002, REL006]

This goes well beyond what an unchanged process typically produces: 34 control-flow items qualified against about 0.17 expected by chance, and every item agrees on the same insertion-and-removal picture, including the rerouting of A02, A05 and A06 towards A01 and the mixed ordering between A01 and A05/A06. [DIR002, DIR005, DIR006, REL004, REL005]

Limitations: zero participation only means "not observed", so the evidence cannot distinguish a complete removal of A04 from it becoming an optional path that was simply never taken in this sample; nor can it exclude that A01 existed at a very low frequency before. Positions and distances are event positions, not clock times. [OCC002, POS003]

Other differences: none beyond the above — the later positions of A03, A05 and A06 and the loosened A05–A06 adjacency follow from the same insertion rather than being independent changes. [POS002, POS004, POS005, LAG006, LAG009]
