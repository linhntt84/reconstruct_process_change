### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The screening comparison rules out chance as an explanation for the evidence as a whole: 62 control-flow items qualified where fewer than one (0.73) would be expected in an unchanged process. The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new stage of five largely alternative activities — A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 — is inserted between A43 and A28 and routed through A04: A43 now typically leads to A04 instead of directly to A28, A04 leads to one of the five, and the five lead on to A28. The successor profiles of A43, A04 and the five show this rerouting directly [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008].

The relational and distance evidence agrees with this placement: A43 before A04, A04 before the five and before A28, and the five after A43 and before A28, A48 and A49 [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017]; A43 to A04 immediately, A04 to A28 at two steps, and A43 to A28 shifted from one to two–three steps [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]. The five's participation rose from 0–1 to about 19–24 in 100 traces [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]. Because many items describe the same traces, the support rests on the coherence of this pattern rather than on independent confirmations. Two limitations remain: the evidence cannot confirm that exactly one of the five is chosen per trace, since pairs of them do co-occur in about 1 in 100 traces [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL027]; and zero participation earlier only means the five were not observed, so it cannot be ruled out that they already ran at very low rates and the change is one of degree rather than an added stage.

Other differences:
- A06 and A08 nearly disappeared, from about 20 in 100 traces each to about 3 and 5; whether they were displaced by the new stage cannot be determined from this evidence, and A06's later routing profile rests on only four observed outgoing transitions, to A32, A04 and A22 [OCC002, OCC003, DIR002].
- Small redistributions at A46 and A48 leave their dominant edges, to A47 and A49 respectively, essentially intact [DIR009, DIR010, LAG005].
