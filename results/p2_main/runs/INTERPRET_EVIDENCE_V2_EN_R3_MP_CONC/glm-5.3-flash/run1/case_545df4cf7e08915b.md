- Activity A01 was not observed in any of the 200 later traces, after running in about 48 in 100 earlier traces (96 of 200, roughly one occurrence per trace on average); conversely, A08 was not observed earlier and runs in about 48 in 100 later traces (96 of 200, about 1.1 occurrences per trace). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A08 executes at the point where A01 previously did: A01 is no longer observed (0 of 200 traces against 96 of 200 earlier) while A08 occurs in about 48 in 100 traces, is reached from A01's former predecessors (A17 exclusively, A03 with about 26% of its outgoing transitions, A02 with a small share), sends its outgoing transitions to A01's former successors A09, A10 and A16 with similar shares, and occupies the same mean relative position (about 0.55 against 0.57). The relational evidence matches one-for-one — A03–A08 co-occur in 48% of traces later, exactly as A01–A03 did earlier, and A01 and A08 never co-occur in either window — and with 53 qualifying items against well under one expected for an unchanged process, the ensemble is not what a stable process produces by chance. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 and A08 are the same underlying activity recorded under different codes, since the two never appear in the same window to be compared directly; the substitution and the A11/A15 exchange below could likewise be two facets of a single re-mapping rather than independent changes.
