### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the either/or routing after A26 was removed and the five-activity group was repositioned. In the earlier window, a trace ran A25 → A28 → A26 and then either A27 → A31 (about 48 in 100 traces) or the group A36/A37/A42/A43/A44 → A31 (about 52 in 100), the two branches being near-perfect alternatives; in the later window, every trace runs A25 → the group, in an unfixed internal order → A28 → A26 → A27 → A31. A27 and the five group activities thereby changed from optional, mutually exclusive alternatives, each present in about half of the traces, into mandatory steps present in every trace, and A28 changed from following A25 directly to following the group. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, REL008, REL015, REL020, POS004]

The change is shown directly by the successor changes at A25, A26 and all five group activities and by the relational order flips (the group now precedes A26, A28 and A27, where it previously followed them or excluded A27), with the participation and position shifts agreeing in direction; 66 items qualified against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process, so chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole. Limitations: the evidence shows event order within traces, not clock time, and cannot distinguish a strictly sequential group from parallel or interleaved execution of its five activities, since both orders occur in similar shares in both windows; the LAG agreement rests on only 1–2 observations in one window, and the two windows remain finite samples. [REL030, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
