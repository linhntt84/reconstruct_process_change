### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A06 became a standard early step executed between A14 and A15: it appears in about 72 in 100 traces (up from about 17), A14 is typically followed immediately by A06, and A06 is typically followed by A15, so the direct A14→A15 succession largely disappeared. This rests on direct relational evidence — the successor profiles of A14 and A06, the co-occurrence and order of A06 with A14 and A15, and the shift of the A14→A15 distance from one step to two — all based on substantial observations in both windows. The 105 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 1 expected by chance, so the pattern as a whole is unlikely to be a sampling artefact; however, the many A06-centred items describe largely the same traces rather than independent confirmations, and several lag items resting on only 1–3 observations were not relied upon. The evidence cannot show whether A06 is a newly added step or an existing rare path that became frequent. [OCC002, DIR002, DIR004, REL001, REL002, LAG003, LAG004, POS001]
