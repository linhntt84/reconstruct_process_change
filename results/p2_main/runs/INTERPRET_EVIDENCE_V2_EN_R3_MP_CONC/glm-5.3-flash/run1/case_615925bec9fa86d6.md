### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured around the group A31, A32, A37, A38, A39: a branch that in the earlier window ran at most once, in about half of the cases, as an alternative to A22 and sat between A21 and A26 (after A23), in the later window runs exactly once in every case, directly after A20 and before A23, while A22 — itself now mandatory — always runs after the group. Accordingly, A20 now leads into the group instead of A23, the group flows on to A23 instead of A26, and A21 always leads to A22 instead of branching into the group. [OCC001, OCC002, REL015, REL020, REL008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS005]

The support is direct and consistent: the order swaps and the alternatives-to-sequential change are shown relationally (co-occurrence of A22 with each group member rises from 0 to 100 in 100; A23–group and A21–group orders flip), the routing change is shown in the successor profiles, and participation, position and relation items all point the same way across all 62 qualifying items — far above the fewer than one item (0.67 of 134 units) expected by chance in a stable process. Limitations: the windows are finite samples, the group's internal sequence remains variable, and the evidence cannot rule out that the later window captures only a subpopulation of cases (for example cases that always execute the group) rather than a modified process. [REL015, REL020, REL008, DIR001, DIR007, POS009]

Other differences: A39 immediately follows A31 in about 52 in 100 later cases versus about 30 in 100 earlier cases (based on 53 and 90 observations), and A20's mean event position moved slightly earlier (about 0.35 to 0.31); both fit the same reordering rather than a separate change.
