### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the process appears to include two previously unobserved activities, A01 and A05, each executed exactly once per trace as a new mandatory segment: A01 typically follows the A03/A04 block and precedes A06, and A05 typically follows A06 as the final activity, yielding the dominant sequence A03/A04 → A01 → A06 → A05; A06 itself became mandatory (about 30 in 100 traces before versus all traces after). The support is direct and consistent — occurrence, direction, pairwise-order, position and gap evidence all show the same pattern — and the volume of qualifying items (21 control-flow items against roughly 0.13 expected without change) rules out chance as an explanation of the pattern as a whole. Limitations: most items describe this single insertion rather than independent confirmations; A01's placement varies in a minority of traces (it precedes A03 in about 14 in 100), so the insertion point is not perfectly uniform; and zero participation in the earlier window strictly establishes only that A01 and A05 were not observed then. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that A01 and A05 belonged to a rare variant that the 200 earlier traces failed to sample, though that would not account for the coordinated rerouting of A03, A04 and A06. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, REL003, REL004, REL007, POS001, POS003, LAG003]

Other differences: In the small number of traces where A05 has a successor (29 observed outgoing transitions), it most often leads back to A04 (about 59 in 100 of those transitions), and A03-to-A04 step distances spread to 4–5 steps in about 28 in 100 later cases; the evidence does not characterise this tail behaviour further.
