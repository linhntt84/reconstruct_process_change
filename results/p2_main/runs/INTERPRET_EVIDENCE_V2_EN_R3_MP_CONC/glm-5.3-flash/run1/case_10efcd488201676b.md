### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening tested 188 control-flow units and flagged 171 items, where an unchanged process would typically produce fewer than one, and each flagged difference is larger than about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the two windows, so the volume of flagged behaviour cannot be explained by random variation [OCC001, OCC004, OCC008, DIR005, REL031].

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in the later window rather than a shift in how often existing paths are taken: A01 and A17 are no longer observed (earlier in about 64 in 100 and in all 200 traces respectively), A07 is newly observed (absent earlier, in about 68 in 100 later traces, connected to A03 and A04), and A06 and A16 — earlier optional and repeating at mid-trace — now run exactly once in every later trace as the closing pair in either order, reached through A04 and A10 in place of the earlier closing pair A10 and A17 [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC007, OCC008, DIR004, DIR005, DIR007, DIR011, POS004, POS011, REL045].

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the participation flips are complete rather than partial (128 to 0, 200 to 0, 0 to 136, and 79 to 200 out of 200 traces), the successor changes are observed at A04, A10, A06 and A16 themselves, and the relational and positional items agree — A04–A06, A04–A16, A06–A10 and A10–A16 co-occur in all later traces in a fixed order, A06–A16 co-occur in both orders, and A06 and A16 sit at the trace end while A10 moved earlier [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC007, OCC008, DIR004, DIR005, DIR007, DIR011, POS004, POS007, POS011, REL031, REL035, REL039, REL045, REL062]. The evidence cannot show whether these differences stem from one coordinated modification or from several independent ones, and zero participation only means an activity was not observed, so very rare occurrences of A01 or A17 in the later window cannot be excluded [OCC001, OCC008].
