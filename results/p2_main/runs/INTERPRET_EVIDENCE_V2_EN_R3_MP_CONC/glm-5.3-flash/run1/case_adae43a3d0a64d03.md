### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 has taken over the role previously played by A05: in the later window A01 occurs once in every trace at the relative event position A05 held, is preceded by the same activities (A02, A06 and A07, with transition shares of about 75, 30 and 66 in 100 versus about 72, 29 and 71 earlier) and is followed by A03, while A05 is no longer observed. The support is direct and consistent rather than scattered: participation flips from 0 to 200 and from 200 to 0 out of 200 traces, the position profiles of A01 later and A05 earlier are identical (mean 0.5, no variation), and every relation A05 had re-appears for A01 while A01 and A05 never co-occur. Eighteen control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.13 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits of a stable process produce. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A05 still occurs but was not sampled in the later window — zero participation strictly means only non-observation — and the logs cannot show whether A01 is a genuinely new activity or A05 recorded under a different code. Other differences: within A06's outgoing transitions, the shares towards A02 and A07 swapped slightly (about 34% and 38% earlier versus 38% and 33% later); the evidence cannot establish whether this small flip is part of the same underlying difference or a separate sampling effect. [DIR004]
