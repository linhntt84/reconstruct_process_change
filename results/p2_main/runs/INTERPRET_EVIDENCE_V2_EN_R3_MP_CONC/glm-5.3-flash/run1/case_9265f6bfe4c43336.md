### Observed differences

- Two groups of five activities swapped how often they run. In the earlier window, A16–A20 each took part in about 85 in 100 traces; in the later window, each takes part in about 18 in 100 (169 vs 36 supporting traces). A21–A25 show the mirror image: about 15 in 100 traces earlier, about 82 in 100 later (31 vs 164 traces). In both windows each of these activities occurs at most once per trace. [OCC001, OCC005, OCC006, OCC010]
- The routing out of A15 flipped accordingly: earlier, about 85 in 100 of A15's outgoing transitions led to A19 and about 15 in 100 to A24; in the later window, about 82 in 100 lead to A24 and about 18 in 100 to A19. A15 itself is present in all traces of both windows. [DIR001]
- The two blocks remained mutually exclusive alternatives: pairs spanning the blocks (one activity from A16–A20, one from A21–A25) never co-occur in either window; earlier the A16–A20 member was the one present, in the later window the A21–A25 member is. [REL015, REL020, REL024, REL039, REL050]
- Relations with A15 flipped the same way: earlier A15 ran before A16–A20 in about 85 in 100 traces; in the later window it usually appears without them (about 82 in 100) and instead runs before A21–A25 in about 82 in 100. [REL001, REL006]
- Within-block co-occurrence flipped as well: pairs inside A16–A20 co-occurred in about 85 in 100 earlier traces, while in the later window usually neither activity of such a pair appears (about 82 in 100); pairs inside A21–A25 moved from about 15 in 100 co-occurrence to about 82 in 100. [REL011, REL013, REL051, REL053, REL060]
- The common block keeps a fixed internal shape in the later window: A24 precedes A21, A22 and A23 (about 82 in 100), each of those precedes A25 (about 82 in 100), and A21, A22 and A23 appear in both orders relative to each other; the earlier common block had the same shape with A19 first and A20 last. [REL013, REL014, REL051, REL053, REL054, REL060, REL063]
- A29 ran in every one of the 200 traces in both windows and switched partners: earlier it co-occurred with A16–A20 (about 85 in 100) and not with A21–A25; in the later window it co-occurs with A21–A25 (about 82 in 100) and no longer with A16–A20. [REL020, REL055]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the frequent continuation after A15 changed: in the earlier window traces typically ran from A15 into the A16–A20 block via A19, while in the later window they typically run into the mutually exclusive A21–A25 block via A24. Both blocks are observed in a substantial share of traces in both windows (about 15–18 in 100 when not taken), no activity is added, dropped or repeated, and the blocks never run together in either window, so this reads as the two existing alternative paths after A15 exchanging their frequencies rather than a path appearing or disappearing. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC005, OCC006, OCC010, REL015, REL020]

The redirection is observed directly in A15's outgoing transition shares; the relation evidence flips consistently across A15, within-block and cross-block pairs; and A29, present throughout, now accompanies the A21–A25 block instead of the A16–A20 block. The volume of qualifying evidence also exceeds what an unchanged process would produce: 78 items passed screening where about 0.84 would be expected by chance, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [DIR001, REL001, REL006, REL011, REL020, REL051, REL055]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of cases reaching A15 — different kinds of cases selecting different branches — rather than a change in the routing itself, since the evidence contains only traces and no case attributes. [DIR001, OCC001]

Other differences:
- From A22, the outgoing transition shares redistributed: A21 fell from about 58% to about 31% of A22's outgoing transitions, while A23 and A25 rose to about 36% and about 33%; the earlier profile rests on only 31 traces, so this may be a sampling effect. [DIR002]
- The positional gap between A18 and A17 (in event steps, not clock time) tightened from roughly half at one step and half at two steps to about 82 in 100 at one step, based on only 17 later-window observations. [LAG001]
