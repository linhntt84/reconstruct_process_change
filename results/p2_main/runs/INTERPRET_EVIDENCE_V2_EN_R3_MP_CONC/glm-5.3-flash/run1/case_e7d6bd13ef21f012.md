### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the linear main flow was restructured in the later window: A04, which previously always ran between A02 and A05, is no longer executed; A03 now runs in that position (A02 → A03 → A05); and A01 was added as a new closing step after A06 — in short, the observed chain changed from A02 → A04 → A05 → A06 to A02 → A03 → A05 → A06 → A01. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation is all-or-nothing (0 of 200 vs 200 of 200) for A04, A03 and A01; the successor changes sit exactly at the splice points; every pairwise relation matches a single deterministic sequence per window; and all 24 qualifying control-flow items compare with about 0.12 expected by chance, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits of an unchanged process produce.
