### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The screening produced 21 qualifying items against about 0.52 expected without change in the control-flow family — roughly forty times the chance expectation — so the set of differences cannot be explained by random sampling alone. The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new intermediate step was inserted between A41 and A26: in the later window, most cases run A41 → one of A19, A20 or A25 → A26, where the three activities act as near-exclusive per-case alternatives, whereas earlier A41 led directly to A26 and the three activities were not observed. The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: the appearance of the three activities, the redirection of A41's outgoing transitions away from A26 towards them, their outgoing transitions leading back to A26, the before/after ordering relations, and the A41→A26 distance shifting from one to two steps. Because the occurrence, direction, relation and lag items largely describe the same underlying trace behaviour, they converge on one change rather than independently confirming it. Limitations: no outgoing profile was measured for A20, so its link to A26 rests on ordering evidence only; positions are event positions rather than clock times; and about 14 in 100 later cases show a three-step gap (with about 18 in 100 earlier cases already at two steps), so additional unobserved intermediates or entry routes into the new step cannot be ruled out.

Other differences: A02's routing to A26 disappeared, though on weak support; A26's position shifted slightly later, consistent with the insertion. [DIR001, POS004]
