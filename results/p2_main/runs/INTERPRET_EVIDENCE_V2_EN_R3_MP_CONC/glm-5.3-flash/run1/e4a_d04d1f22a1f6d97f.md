### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A22 ran exactly once in every trace (participation 100%, mean 1.0 per trace, never repeated); in the later window it appears in only about 51 in 100 traces (mean 0.51). [OCC001]
- A27, A28 and A33 were not observed in any earlier trace; in the later window each appears in about 49 in 100 traces, and each pair of them co-occurs in about 49 in 100 — so the three always run together as a group, positioned around the middle of the trace (mean relative position ≈ 0.49). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL012, REL013, REL014, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- Routing out of A21 changed: earlier, 100% of A21's outgoing transitions went to A22; in the later window about 51% go to A22 and the rest split across A28 (~18%), A33 (~16%) and A27 (~15%). [DIR001]
- The new group is mutually exclusive with A22 (co-occurrence 0 in the later window) and sits between A21 and A23: A21 precedes each of A27/A28/A33 in about 49 in 100 traces, and each of them precedes A23 in about 49 in 100. A23 itself still occurs in every trace in both windows. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011]
- The internal order of the three new activities is not fixed: from each of them, transitions go to the other two and to A23 in comparable shares (e.g., from A27 about 36% to A33, 35% to A28, 30% to A23), and each pair appears in both orders (roughly 22–27 in 100 each way). [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- The distance from A21 to A23 was always 2 steps in the earlier window; in the later window it is 2 steps in about 51 in 100 traces and 4 steps in about 49 in 100 — matching the traces where A22 is skipped and three activities are inserted. [LAG001, REL001, REL005]
- Elapsed time between consecutive completions shrank for both observed pairs: A25→A29 from a mean of about 0.91 h to about 0.48 h (median 0.90 → 0.48 h; 56 vs 57 intervals), and A30→A29 from about 0.18 h to about 0.09 h (41 vs 48 intervals), with narrower spread in the later window. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the segment between A21 and A23. In the earlier window every trace ran A21 → A22 → A23. In the later window, about half the traces keep that path, while in the other half A22 is skipped and a group of three activities — A27, A28 and A33 — runs between A21 and A23 in varying internal order before A23; A22 became optional (skipped in about 49 in 100 later traces) and the group appears only in traces without it. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL009, REL010, REL011, LAG001]

The signs agree across indicator families: A21's outgoing transitions rerouted from entirely to A22 to roughly half A22 and half the new group [DIR001]; the relational items place the new activities after A21 and before A23 and never together with A22 [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011]; the A21→A23 distance doubles from 2 to 4 steps in exactly the later traces without A22 [LAG001]; and the new activities occupy a mid-trace position consistent with A22's former slot [POS001, POS002, POS003]. With 26 control-flow items against roughly 0.4 expected by chance for this family, the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by random re-splits of a stable process.

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows that A27, A28 and A33 were not observed there, not that they were absent, so a rare pre-existing variant that grew in share cannot be fully ruled out. Many items describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations. The internal sequence of the three activities appears in both orders in similar proportions, so no fixed sub-sequence can be inferred. [REL012, REL013, REL014]

Other differences: a timing change stands on its own — the elapsed time between consecutive completions of A25 and A29 fell from a mean of about 0.91 h to about 0.48 h, and between A30 and A29 from about 0.18 h to about 0.09 h, both with narrower spread in the later window; the two time items far exceed the time family's chance expectation (about 0.008), but the evidence does not link this timing change to the structural change around A21–A23. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
