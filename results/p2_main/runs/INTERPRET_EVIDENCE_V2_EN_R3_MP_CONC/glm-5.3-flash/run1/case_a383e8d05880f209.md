### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The number of qualifying items (21, all control-flow) is far above the roughly 0.18 expected for an unchanged process, so sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01's placement changed: it moved from an early position to a late position in the case. Before, A01 ran near the start, typically before A02, A04, A05 and A07, with no fixed order against A02 and A04; in the later window A04 and A07 always precede it (A04 chaining through A07 into A01), A02 usually precedes it, A05 precedes it in about 63 in 100 traces (that relation stays mixed), and A01 now closes the flow, leading directly to A03 in about half the traces. [POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG004, LAG007]

The hypothesis rests on direct positional and relational evidence: A01's position distribution shifted by about 0.39 of the trace with full support in both windows, the A01–A04 and A01–A07 relations changed from mixed to fixed order (a changed ordering constraint, not a path-frequency shift), and the successor and distance changes point the same way. Participation itself did not change — every activity involved appears in all traces in both windows. Limitations: positions are event positions, not clock times, so only order within the case is established; and the evidence cannot distinguish A01 itself moving to the end from the block of A02/A04/A05/A07 being regrouped before it, since only relative order is observed. [POS001, REL002, REL004, DIR004, LAG007]

Other differences: the slight advance of A02, A05 and A07, A02's rerouting from A01 toward A05, the loss of A07's direct transition to A03, and the rarer A01→A05 co-occurrence within five steps are consistent side-effects of the same reordering but are not needed to support it. [DIR002, LAG002, LAG008, POS002, POS003, POS004]
