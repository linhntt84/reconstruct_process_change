### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, about half the cases run A21 → A27/A28/A33 → A23 instead of A21 → A22 → A23: all three new activities execute between A21 and A23 in no fixed order, A22 is bypassed in those cases, and A22 and the new three-activity block act as mutually exclusive alternatives directly after A21. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the participation shifts are large and well supported (A22 from 200 of 200 to about 102 of 200 traces; A27, A28 and A33 from not observed to about 98 of 200 each), the routing split at A21, the strict mutual exclusivity between A22 and the new activities, the A21-before / A23-after ordering on both paths, and the 2-step versus 4-step distance all describe the same restructuring from different angles. The screening count also fits: 26 qualifying items against roughly 0.4 expected by chance in a stable process, far beyond what random re-splits of unchanged behaviour produce. [OCC001, DIR001, REL006, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the later window mixes two case variants rather than reflecting one modified process, and it cannot show whether the new block's internal order is genuinely unfixed or consists of several fixed orders; the new activities' absence earlier only means they were not observed in that window. [REL012, REL013, REL014]

Other differences: none observed — every qualifying item concerns the same restructuring around A21, A22, A23 and the new activities A27, A28 and A33. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, LAG001]
