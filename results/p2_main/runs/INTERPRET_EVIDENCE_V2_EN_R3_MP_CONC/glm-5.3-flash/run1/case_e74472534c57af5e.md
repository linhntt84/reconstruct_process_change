### Observed differences

- The clearest difference is repetition of the A30–A31–A36 chain. In the earlier window A30, A31 and A36 each occurred once per trace (mean about 1.0, with repeats in at most about 1 in 100 traces); in the later window each occurs about 3.5 times per trace, with repeats in about 68 in 100 traces. Participation is unchanged: all three occur in every trace in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A36's outgoing transitions changed accordingly: earlier, about 98 in 100 led to A25 and almost none to A30; later, about 71 in 100 lead to A30 and about 28 in 100 to A25. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the segment A30 → A31 → A36 is executed repeatedly within a trace instead of once: A36, which previously passed control almost exclusively to A25, now returns to A30 in about 71 in 100 of its executions, so the chain cycles roughly three to four times per trace (about 3.5 occurrences of each activity) before exiting to A25 as before, and about 68 in 100 traces show such repeats. This rests on direct signs: the new dominant A36 → A30 transition [DIR005], the A36-then-A30 ordered pair rising from 1 to 136 of 200 traces [LAG002], the roughly threefold increase in occurrences of A30, A31 and A36 at unchanged full participation [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003], the preserved internal order of the chain [DIR003, DIR004], and relation shifts whose size matches the repetition share rather than indicating that A14 or A05 was reordered [REL003, REL004, REL005]. With 19 qualifying items against roughly 0.5 expected if nothing had changed, chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole [OCC001, DIR005, LAG002]. Limitations: the evidence cannot show what determines the number of iterations per trace, and a competing reading in which the block is re-entered from a different upstream point in some traces cannot be fully excluded, although the direct A36 → A30 edge makes the loop-back reading the most direct one; it also cannot establish whether the routing change at A05 belongs to the same modification.

Other differences: A05's outgoing transitions shifted away from A14 (about 35% to 18%) toward A07 (about 19% to 39%), and A05 appears at an earlier relative position; A14's outgoing profile remains dominated by A15 with small new shares to A06 and A02. These are not explained by the repetition hypothesis. [DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS002]
