### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A06 executes as a near-mandatory step between A46 and A19/A20. Earlier, A46 handed over directly to A19/A20 while A06 was rare (about 14 in 100 traces) and without a fixed placement; later, A46 feeds A06 in about 4 of 5 of its outgoing transitions, A06 leads mainly to A19/A20 (typically reaching A20 within one step), and it runs after A46 and before the A25/A26/A27 and A35–A43 activities in about 3 of 4 traces. The successor changes at A46 and A06 show this placement directly, and the occurrence, position, relation and lag items all point the same way. [DIR014, DIR003, LAG001, LAG029, REL012, OCC002, POS001]

With 85 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed, sampling variation cannot account for the evidence as a whole. The competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants — for example a previously rare A06-containing behaviour becoming dominant — rather than a modification of one main path; the newly fixed A46-before-A06 order and the one-step increase in the A46-to-A19/A20 distance argue against a pure mix shift, but two windows of 200 traces cannot definitively separate the two. [REL012, LAG029, LAG030]

Other differences: the middle-block reordering (A25, A26, A27, A30 and the A35/A36/A41/A42/A43 cluster) is a second, directly documented structural difference that the A06 hypothesis does not cover, and A02 and A08 participate in fewer traces in the later window (about 24 to 12 and about 25 to 7 in 100).
