### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the continuation from A29 and A30 into the block of activities A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 is almost never taken: nearly all cases run directly from A29 to A30 and on to A31, and the five block activities are executed in at most about 2 in 100 traces (A26 not at all), whereas each of them ran in about 15–24 in 100 traces in the earlier window. The direct signs are the successor profiles of A29 and A30, each backed by about 200 traces in both windows, showing the share of continuations into the block collapsing from roughly 38% and 60% to about 1–2% and virtually none; the participation, co-occurrence and position items largely restate this same underlying shift rather than confirming it independently, while the lag items show the resulting flow is tighter (A29→A31 typically two steps instead of three, A30→A31 typically immediate). Screening tested 146 control-flow units and 59 items qualified, where fewer than 1 (0.73) would be expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits, so the ensemble is not what an unchanged process typically produces. The main limitation is that the block activities leave only 0–4 occurrences in the later window, so the evidence cannot distinguish a branch that is no longer executed from one still taken in a very small share of cases, nor can it rule out that the later window draws on a different mix of case variants in which this branch simply does not occur. [DIR009, DIR010, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, screening]
