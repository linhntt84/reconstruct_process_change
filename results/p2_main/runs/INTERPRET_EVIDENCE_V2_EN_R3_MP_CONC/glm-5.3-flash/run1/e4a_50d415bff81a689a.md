### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a timing change centred on A28: in the later window, the elapsed time between the completion of each of the preceding activities A33, A34, A35 and A36 and the completion of A28 is about twice as long as before (about 1 hour rising to about 2 hours on average). The supporting signs are that the elapsed-time indicator measures this directly on all four pairs into A28, with consistent direction and magnitude, each difference exceeding about 999 of 1,000 random re-splits, and the time family yielded 4 items against a chance expectation of about 0.02 — far more than an unchanged process typically produces. The four items share A28, so they most plausibly describe one underlying difference rather than four independent ones. Limitations: the indicator measures elapsed time between consecutive completion events, not pure waiting or service time, so the evidence cannot show whether the extra hour is idle time or handling, or where within the interval it arises; and a positional shift of A28's completion relative to its predecessors cannot be fully ruled out, although the absence of any control-flow findings makes a structural reordering less likely.
