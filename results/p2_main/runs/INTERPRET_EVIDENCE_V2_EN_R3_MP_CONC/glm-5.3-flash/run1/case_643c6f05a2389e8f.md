### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the dominant route after A20 changed: in the earlier window a typical trace continued from A20 through A24 into the block A21–A25 (about 85 in 100 traces, with A34 and often A36 or A37 alongside), while in the later window it typically continues from A20 through A29 into the block A26–A30 (about 82 in 100 traces), with A34 now accompanying this block and A36 and A37 co-occurring with it about half the time. The blocks act as alternatives in both windows, and both were still observed in a substantial minority of traces in each window (about 16–19 in 100), so this reads as the two existing alternative paths being taken with different frequencies rather than as activities appearing or disappearing. [DIR003, OCC002, OCC007, REL011, REL016, REL025, REL071, REL030, REL075, REL076]

The support rests on direct routing evidence at A20 rather than participation alone, on participation, co-occurrence and direction items that all point the same way, and on volume: 120 control-flow items qualified against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed, each difference exceeding about 199 of 200 random re-splits, which chance re-splits of a stable process would not produce. [DIR003, OCC002, REL016, REL025]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the flip is a single routing decision at A20 or two independent changes in how often each block runs, and it cannot speak to any cause; the internal ordering of A21–A25 is also largely unobservable in the later window because the block rarely runs. [REL021, REL025]

Other differences: A02 became more frequent (about 5 to about 24 in 100 traces) and now typically leads directly to A37, with a new A36 → A02 transition (about 25 in 100 of A36's outgoing transitions later versus about 1 in 100 earlier); this fragment is reported separately because the evidence does not tie it to the branch switch itself. Outgoing shares at A27 evened out, and the small position shifts of A21 and A24 mirror their lower participation rather than a relocation. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR005, LAG001, DIR004, POS001, POS002]
