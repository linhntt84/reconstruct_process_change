### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural reworking of the segment between A39 and A24 involving A14, A15, A16, A21, A22 and A23: earlier, A39 was followed by exactly one of the mutually exclusive alternatives A14, A15 or A16, each leading straight to A24; in the later window A14, A15 and A16 are no longer observed, and A21, A22 and A23 all execute once per case in varying order before the case reaches A24, lengthening the A39-to-A24 path from two to four steps. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR007, REL019, LAG001]

The support is direct and consistent across the control-flow indicators: participation flipped from about 70/59/71 of 200 cases to 0 for the old trio and from 0 to all 200 cases for the new trio; the relational profiles moved from "exactly one of the three" to "all three co-occurring with roughly even mixed order"; A39's outgoing transitions were rewired from the old trio to the new one; and the new trio sits at about the same relative stage the old one did. With 48 control-flow items qualifying against about 0.5 expected without a change, the volume of flagged behaviour far exceeds what a stable process produces by chance. [OCC001, OCC004, REL019, DIR007, POS004, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that A21, A22 and A23 are re-labelled successors of A14, A15 and A16 rather than newly added steps, since zero participation only means an activity was not observed in that window; and the roughly even mixed order inside the new trio means no fixed sequence among A21, A22 and A23 can be inferred. [OCC004, REL019]

Other differences: the elapsed time between consecutive completions shortened for two pairs outside the reworked segment — A24 to A31 (about 0.9 to about 0.46 hours) and A32 to A31 (about 0.18 to about 0.10 hours); both time items exceed the chance expectation for the time family (2 items against about 0.01), so a timing change is plausible in its own right, but the evidence cannot establish whether it is connected to the structural change. These gaps measure elapsed time between consecutive completion events, not pure waiting or service time. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
