### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change: the segment formed by A20, A21, A30, A31 and A32, which was executed in every earlier case between the A14→A15→A16 run and A33, no longer appears in the later window, and a new optional segment — A19 or one of A22, A23, A24, A25 — now runs in about one in five later cases in the same region, each variant flowing into A33. The rewiring around the removed segment is shown directly: A04, which earlier fed almost exclusively into the removed activities, became rare and late, and A48 now typically reaches A33 within about two steps instead of going to A04. This is far beyond what an unchanged process produces: 165 evidence items qualified against about 1.3 expected by chance, and the key shifts run between 200-of-200 and 0-of-200 participation across 200-trace windows.
