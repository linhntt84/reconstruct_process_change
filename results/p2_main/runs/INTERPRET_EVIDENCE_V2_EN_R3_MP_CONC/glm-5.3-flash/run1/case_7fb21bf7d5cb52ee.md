### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, A10 appears to have taken on a new, frequent early role between A14 and A15: it runs in about 70 in 100 traces (about 19 before) near the start of the case, A14 typically hands over to A10 (about 66 in 100 of its transitions, versus about 86 in 100 straight to A15 before), and A10 in turn leads to A15 in about 85 in 100 of its transitions, usually immediately. Occurrence, position, direction, relation and lag indicators all show this directly and consistently. [OCC003, POS001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, LAG001, LAG002]

Screening produced 77 qualifying control-flow items where about 1 would be expected without a change, so the differences as a whole are unlikely for an unchanged process, although many items describe the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations. The earlier A10 profile rests on about 38 traces, and the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine insertion of A10 into the former A14→A15 path from a newly frequent variant in which A14 is followed by A10 and then A15 while the direct A14→A15 transition survives (about 33 in 100); it also cannot show that A10's rare earlier mid-trace occurrences are the same behaviour as its new early role. [OCC003, DIR003, POS001]

Other differences: the fixed sequence A31→A22→A20→A23→A24→A26→A25→A32 now executes with mixed and partly reversed orders and new branches (A23 often directly to A32, A31 often to A24, and new short transitions back to A22 from A24, A25 and A26) — a relaxation of ordering constraints rather than a swap of positions; A04 and especially A08 participate in fewer traces. [DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, DIR010, REL015, REL020, LAG006, LAG020, LAG022, OCC001, OCC002]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the A10 rise reflects a new frequent path variant rather than a modification of the existing A14→A15 edge, and that its earlier rare mid-trace occurrences were a different behaviour; individual unusual items can also arise by chance in finite windows, though the overall item count (77 versus about 1 expected) cannot be explained by chance as a whole.
