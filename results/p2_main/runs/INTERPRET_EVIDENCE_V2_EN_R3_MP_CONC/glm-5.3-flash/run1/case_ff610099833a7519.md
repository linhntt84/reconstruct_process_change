### Observed differences

- Activity A01, which in the earlier window appeared in about 46 in 100 traces (92 of 200) — about twice per trace where it appeared, around two-thirds of the way through the trace by event position — was not observed in any of the 200 later traces. [OCC001, POS001]
- Activity A13, not observed earlier, appears in the later window in about 53 in 100 traces (106 of 200), also about twice per trace where it appears, at essentially the same relative position A01 used to hold (mean 0.68 vs 0.68, median 0.71 vs 0.71, by event position). [OCC002, POS002]
- The wiring changed accordingly: before, all observed outgoing transitions of A10 went to A01 and all of A01's went to A03; in the later window, all of A10's go to A13 and all of A13's go to A03. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The pair relations flipped in step: before, A01 co-occurred with A03 in 46% of traces (always interleaved or repeated, with no fixed order) and with A10 in 46% (A10 first in 25%, interleaved in 21%), and never co-occurred with A13; after, A13 takes over both relations — with A03 in 53% of traces (interleaved or repeated) and with A10 in 53% (A10 first in about 23%, interleaved in about 31%) — while A01 co-occurs with neither. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]
- A03 itself is unchanged, present in every trace in both windows. [REL001, REL004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was replaced by A13 at the same point in the process: the branch in which A10 leads into an activity that then runs interleaved with, or repeated alongside, A03. Before, about 46 in 100 traces ran A10 → A01 (about twice) together with A03; in the later window, about 53 in 100 traces run A10 → A13 (about twice) together with A03, at the same relative position. The support is direct — the successor change at A10 from A01 to A13 is observed outright, and A13's new edge to A03 mirrors A01's old one — and consistent: all twelve qualifying items fit this single structure, far more than the roughly 0.16 expected by chance among the 32 tested units, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]

The evidence cannot distinguish a genuine substitution by a new activity from a relabelling of the same step, since the activity codes are anonymous; that is the main competing explanation it cannot rule out. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002]

Other differences: the branch ran somewhat more often in the later window (about 53 vs about 46 in 100 traces) and its activity repeated somewhat more often (about 31 vs about 21 in 100 traces showing repeats); both shifts are small relative to the 200-trace samples and may be sampling variation rather than a routing or repetition change. [OCC001, OCC002, REL002, REL005]
