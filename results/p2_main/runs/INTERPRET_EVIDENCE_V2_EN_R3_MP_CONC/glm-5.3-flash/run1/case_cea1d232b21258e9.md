### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a structural change removed A02 from the executed process and rerouted the surrounding flow. In the earlier window A02 ran in about half of all traces (97 of 200); in the later window it does not appear in any of the 200 traces, its outgoing transitions are unobserved, and all nine monitored pairs containing A02 changed from regular co-occurrence to none. Alongside this, A03 and A14 are executed in far fewer traces (about 19 in 100, down from about 49) and later within them, while A10 is executed in more traces (about 46 in 100, up from about 24) and earlier, with successors redirected accordingly: A08 now leads mostly to A03 instead of A10; A09, A15 and A06 now lead mostly to A10 instead of A03; and A14 now leads mostly to A13. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001, REL007, OCC002, OCC003, POS003, POS005, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR010, DIR011]

This reading rests on direct and consistent measurements rather than on the number of items: the participation, direction and position indicators agree, and the key counts are substantial (A02: 97 traces before versus 0 after; its outgoing transitions: 202 observed before versus 0 after). The screening also qualified 60 items against roughly 0.67 expected if nothing had changed, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, so the set of differences as a whole is far beyond what an unchanged process would produce. [OCC001, DIR001]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A02 was not observed; the evidence cannot rule out that the later window covers a different case mix in which A02 is simply never triggered, rather than the activity having been removed. The aggregated profiles also cannot pin down the exact new edge structure, and the many direction, relation and lag items overlap on the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations of one underlying change. [OCC001, DIR002]

Other differences: the distances between consecutive occurrences shifted for several pairs — A03 then A09 now typically sits about 3 steps apart (about 79 in 100) versus 1–2 steps before, A14 then A09 now typically about 2 steps apart (about 83 in 100) versus 1 step before, and A13 to A03 lost its immediate one-step relation — and A11 now occurs somewhat earlier in the trace (mean relative position about 0.49 → 0.40) with little change in how often it appears.
