### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a sequence of activities that was previously skipped in about half of the cases — A41, then A19, then A25, then A20, with A26 after it — is executed in virtually every case in the later window, while its internal order stayed the same. In the earlier window the block ran in about 48 in 100 traces and was absent as a whole in the other half; in the later window A41 precedes A19, A25 and A20 in about 96–97 in 100 traces, the three activities run in a fixed order in almost every trace, and A26, which previously often occurred without the block or directly after A41, now consistently comes after it. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL018, REL019, REL022, REL020, REL021, DIR006, LAG021]

Supporting signs: the participation counts rest on roughly 95–199 traces per window; the pairwise relations move directly from "neither present" to near-universal co-occurrence with an unchanged order; and A41's and A26's routing changes point the same way. In total 62 control-flow items qualified against about 0.84 expected for an unchanged process, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole, and every item's difference exceeds those of about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL018, REL019, REL022, DIR006, LAG021]

Limitations: many relation items describe the same underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations, and the two windows are finite samples. The evidence also cannot rule out that the later window simply contains a different mix of cases — for example only cases of a kind that always executes this block — rather than a change in the process itself. [REL018, REL019, REL022]

Other differences: A06 appears to be inserted into the flow between A15 and A16 — it takes part in about 73 in 100 traces (up from about 24 in 100), sits early in the trace, A15 now leads to it in about 40 in 100 of its outgoing transitions while A15 to A16 dropped to about 58 in 100, and A06 typically immediately precedes A16. A08 nearly disappeared (about 8 in 100 traces, down from about 20 in 100). A few other routing shares shifted only slightly, for example A14 to A15 (about 85 to 97 in 100 of outgoing transitions).
