### Observed differences

- A01 is new to the later window: it is absent from all 200 earlier traces but appears in about 39 in 100 later traces (77 of 200), at most once per trace, around the middle of the trace (median relative position about 0.45). Its observed outgoing transitions go mainly to A09 (about 27%), A07 (about 23%), A12 (about 19%) and A04 (about 10%). [OCC001, POS001, DIR001]

Its earlier outgoing transitions went almost entirely to A15 (about 53%) and A14 (about 46%). [OCC004, POS003, DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural hypothesis: A17 no longer occurs, a new activity A01 appears, and A12 — which previously passed flow on to A17 — now passes it mainly to A15 and A14. The participation changes are direct and large in both directions (A17 from about 53 in 100 traces to none; A01 from none to about 39 in 100), the occurrence, position and outgoing-transition profiles agree, and the relation items show exactly the co-occurrence changes this implies. The shortening of the A12→A14 and A12→A15 distances from about two steps to about one is consistent with A12 now reaching them without A17 in between, though it is not an independent confirmation. With 27 qualifying items against roughly 0.3 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS003, REL005, REL006, LAG003, LAG004]

A limitation the evidence cannot resolve: zero participation only means an activity was not observed, so it cannot distinguish A17 being dropped from the process from A17 simply not being executed in the later window (and likewise for A01 earlier); nor does it show that A01 took over A17's role, since their successors differ (A01 flows mainly to A09 and A07, whereas A17 flowed to A14 and A15). [DIR001, DIR003, OCC001, OCC004]

Other differences: the drops in A06 and A08 participation (roughly 20 in 100 traces each) are not connected to the A17/A01 change by the evidence; and several step-distance shifts (A02→A14, A14→A14, A15→A14, A15→A15) rest on only about 13–37 paired observations and could be sampling variation. [OCC002, OCC003, LAG001, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008]
