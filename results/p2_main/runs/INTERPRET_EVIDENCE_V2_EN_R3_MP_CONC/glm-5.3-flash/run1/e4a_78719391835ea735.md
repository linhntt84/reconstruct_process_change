### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is an exchange of two groups of activities between an early and a late segment. In the earlier window, A14, A16 and A19 each occurred in about 30–36 of 100 traces, at roughly one third of the trace (mean positions 0.34–0.35), were reached from A35 (each taking roughly one third of A35's outgoing transitions, the three shares summing to about one per trace), and all of their observed outgoing transitions went to A20. In the later window, these three activities sit at about two thirds of the trace (mean 0.67–0.68), are reached from A24, and all of their outgoing transitions go to A25. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS003, POS006]

- The mirror image holds for A15, A17 and A18. Earlier, each appeared in every trace at about 0.64 of the trace, was entered from A24 (about 30–37 in 100 of A24's outgoing transitions each) and exited to A25 on about 30–38 in 100 of its outgoing transitions; later, they appear in every trace at about 0.38 of the trace, are entered from A35, and exit to A20 on about 28–37 in 100 of their outgoing transitions. Their routing among themselves is broadly unchanged. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, DIR007, DIR008, POS002, POS004, POS005]

- Pairwise order relations reversed across the two groups: before, A20 preceded A15 and A24 preceded A15 in all traces; in the later window A15 precedes both in all traces. A14, A16 and A19, which previously came before A15, A17, A18, A20 and A24 in the roughly 30 of 100 traces where they co-occur, now come after them; the same reversal holds for the pairs of A16 and A19 with the late-segment activities. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL013, REL020, REL021]

- Step distances changed consistently with the exchange: A35 reached A20 within two steps in essentially all traces before and within four steps after, while A24 reached A25 within four steps before and within two steps after. [LAG001, LAG002]

- Participation and path usage barely moved: A14 in about 30 of 100 traces before and 33 after, A16 about 35 → 29, A19 about 36 → 39, while A15, A17, A18, A20, A24 and A35 appear in every trace in both windows; A20 and A24 shifted slightly later (mean position about 0.38 → 0.44 and 0.57 → 0.64), consistent with the early segment now containing three activities instead of one. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

- The elapsed time between consecutive completion events on the transitions into A08 roughly doubled: after A02 from about 0.9 to about 1.7 hours (26 vs 28 observed transitions), and after A06 from about 0.1 to about 0.3 hours (37 vs 27 observed transitions). [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the two groups of alternative activities exchanged places between the two segments: A14, A16 and A19, which previously formed the three-way choice inside the early segment (entered after A35, exiting to A20), now form the choice inside the late segment (entered after A24, exiting to A25), while A15, A17 and A18, which previously ran together in the late segment, now run together in the early segment and exit to A20. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002]

The signs are direct and consistent: the successor profiles changed exactly at the segment boundaries (A14/A16/A19 from exclusively A20 to exclusively A25; the branch sets of A24 and A35 exchanged), every measured pair across the two groups reversed its order, positions moved by about 0.3 of the trace in opposite directions, and the A35→A20 and A24→A25 step distances swapped (two steps ↔ four steps). Participation shares stayed essentially unchanged, so this is a change in placement and ordering rather than a shift in how often existing paths are taken. With 39 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.5 expected if nothing had changed, sampling noise cannot account for the pattern as a whole, although many items describe the same underlying exchange and are not independent confirmations. [REL008, REL009, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS006, LAG001, LAG002, DIR001, DIR007]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show what precedes A35 or follows A25, whether the choice probabilities within each group changed, or whether a relabelling of activities producing identical traces underlies the exchange; with 200 traces per window, small participation shifts such as A16's 35% → 29% also remain within sampling variation. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, POS003]

Other differences: the elapsed time between consecutive completion events into A08 roughly doubled on both observed incoming transitions (from A02: about 0.9 → 1.7 hours; from A06: about 0.1 → 0.3 hours). With 26–37 observations per window and 2 qualifying time items against roughly 0.01 expected without a change, this looks like a genuine timing change in its own right, but it concerns activities outside the exchanged groups and is not linked to the structural exchange. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
