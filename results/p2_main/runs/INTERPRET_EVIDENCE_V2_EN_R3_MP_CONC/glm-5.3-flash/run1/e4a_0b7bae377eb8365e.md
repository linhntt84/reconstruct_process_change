### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A22, A23 and A24 occurred in every trace and ran in immediate succession: A22 was followed by A23 in all of its outgoing transitions, A23 was followed by A24 in all of its outgoing transitions, A23 sat exactly one step after A22, and A24 exactly two steps after A22. The gap from A22 to A24 grew from two steps in every trace to three steps in about 199 of 200 traces. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a new step is executed in almost every case, taking one of three mutually exclusive forms — A14 (about 29 in 100 traces), A15 (about 41 in 100) or A20 (about 30 in 100) — inserted into the previously rigid A22 → A23 → A24 sequence: always after A22 and before A24, in about 56 in 100 traces between A22 and A23 and in about 43 in 100 between A23 and A24, whereas in the earlier window A22, A23 and A24 followed one another immediately in every trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, DIR004, DIR005]

Supporting signs: the placement is shown directly by relational and lag evidence, not only by frequencies; the shares cross-check almost exactly (the 56 in 100 share of A22→A23 at distance two equals the summed shares of A14, A15 and A20 preceding A23, and the 43 in 100 share of A23→A24 at distance two equals the summed shares of the three following A23); the number of qualified items far exceeds what a stable process produces (28 control-flow items against about 0.4 expected, 2 time items against about 0.01), and every item's difference exceeds virtually all random re-splits. Note that the many agreeing occurrence, direction, relation and lag items largely describe this one insertion rather than independent confirmations. [LAG001, LAG003, REL004, REL008, REL011, DIR004, DIR005]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window means only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed then, so a step that existed but was never executed cannot be distinguished from a newly added one; and the evidence cannot show what governs the choice among the three activities or between the two insertion points, for example whether it follows case characteristics rather than free routing. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: the elapsed time between consecutive completion events into A32 roughly doubled from both observed predecessors (A26: median about 0.9 to about 1.8 hours; A33: about 0.15 to about 0.28 hours, on roughly 45 intervals per window). This is a timing hypothesis in its own right; TWAIT measures elapsed time between consecutive completions, so the increase can include waiting as well as other elapsed time, and the evidence contains no structural items for A26, A32 or A33 that would tie it to the insertion above. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
