### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the control flow around A03 was restructured. In the earlier window, A12 was always followed by A14 and A14 by A03, closing a loop back to A03, and A03 led directly to A13 in about half of its outgoing transitions; in the later window, A12 leads directly back to A03 while A14 is no longer observed, and a new activity A02 sits between A03 and A13 — A03 leads to A02, A02 always leads to A13, and A02 occurs only in traces that also contain A03. A01 also appears as a new activity in about 28 in 100 traces, though the evidence contains no relational items for it, so where it fits remains unknown. The screening context makes sampling noise an unlikely explanation for the pattern as a whole: 40 items qualified where roughly 0.32 would be expected without change, every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, and the structural claim rests on directly observed successor changes (A12: to A14 before, to A03 after; A03: gained A02, lost A13), corroborating lag shifts and the coherent disappearance of every A14 relation.
