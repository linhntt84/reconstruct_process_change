### Observed differences

- **A11 occurs far more often and earlier.** In the earlier window, A11 appeared in about 29 of 100 traces, with a median position at the very end of the trace; in the later window, it appears in about 57 of 100 traces and its median position is at about two-thirds of the trace. It is never repeated within a trace in either window [OCC001, POS003].
- **The A09/A11 alternative became mandatory.** In both windows A09 and A11 never occur in the same trace, so they behave as alternatives; but traces containing neither fell from about 42 of 100 to none — in the later window, every one of the 200 traces contains exactly one of the two. A09 also rose, from about 30 to about 44 of 100 traces, and moved earlier (mean position about 0.97 → 0.75) [REL003, POS002].
- **The chosen alternative now follows A06 directly.** A06→A11 grew from about 4% to about 31% of A06's outgoing transitions, and A06→A09 from about 6% to 23%; the A06–A11 distance fell from mostly 3–4 steps to mostly 1–2 steps (about 85 of 100 cases). Whenever A06 and A11 occur together, A06 comes first — about 57 of 100 traces in the later window, up from 27 [DIR002, LAG003, LAG002, REL001].
- **A08 lost its fixed position before A11.** Earlier, A08 always preceded A11 when both occurred; in the later window, both orders appear about equally often (about 16 vs 15 of 100), and A11 occurs without A08 in about 27 of 100 traces (previously never). A08's outgoing transitions shifted away from A11 and A09 (about 32%/31% → 17%/11%) toward A02 and A06 (about 15%/9% → 33%/23%), and A06–A08 distances grew from mostly 1–2 to mostly 3–4 steps [REL002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG004].
- **Smaller downstream shifts.** A05's successors moved from mostly A01 (about two-thirds) to mostly A09/A11 (about three-quarters); A09's and A11's own successor profiles spread from one dominant successor (A02, A06) across many activities; A08 appears slightly less often (about 59 → 54 of 100 traces) and slightly later. The A05/A09/A11 successor profiles rest on only 7–26 earlier-window observations [DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, POS001].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the mutually exclusive alternative pair A09/A11 became a mandatory step and moved earlier in the trace, now typically executing directly after A06, with A11 the more frequently chosen branch. Before, the pair was skipped in about 4 of 10 traces and, when it ran, it executed late (median at the trace end), usually after A08 and 3–4 steps after A06; in the later window, every trace contains exactly one of A09/A11, A11 is taken in about 57 of 100 traces (up from 29) and A09 in about 44 (up from 30), and the chosen activity follows A06 within 1–2 steps. A08 no longer has a fixed order relative to A11 [REL003, OCC001, POS002, POS003, DIR002, LAG002, LAG003, REL001, REL002].

Supporting signs: the placement change is shown directly by relational and positional evidence (co-occurrence and order, step distances, successor shares), not inferred from frequencies alone; the participation, position, direction and relation items describe one coherent shift; and 16 items qualified against about 0.16 expected by chance for the 32 tested control-flow units, with every difference exceeding about 199 of 200 random re-splits, so sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole [REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS002, POS003, DIR002, DIR003, OCC001].

Limitations: the evidence establishes the behavioural shift but not where the structural edit sits — a rerouting at A06, a change at the A09/A11 choice point itself, or an upstream change that made every trace reach this choice point cannot be separated. Successor details for A05, A09 and A11 rest on only 7–26 earlier-window traces, and positions are event positions rather than clock times [DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, POS002, POS003].

Other differences: A05's successors shifted from mostly A01 to mostly A09/A11 [DIR001]; A09's and A11's own outgoing transitions spread from one dominant successor across several activities [DIR004, DIR005]; A08 occurs slightly less often and slightly later in the trace [POS001].
