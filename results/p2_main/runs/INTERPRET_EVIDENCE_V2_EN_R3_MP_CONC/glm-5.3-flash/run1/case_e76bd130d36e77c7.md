### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the five-activity block A35, A36, A41, A42 and A43 changed its placement in the flow. In the earlier window the fragment ran from A25 into the block and from the block into A27, with A26 leading almost always directly to A30; in the later window, A25 leads directly to A27, A26 leads into the block, and the block leads into A30 — the block now sits after A26 (and, by mean positions, after A27 and A26, which kept their mutual order) instead of between A25 and A27. That this ensemble is not what a stable process produces is supported by the screening result: 43 control-flow items qualified where about 0.8 were expected without change among 160 tested units. The change is shown directly and consistently: the successor profiles of A25 and A26 exchanged shapes, all five block activities replaced A27 with A30 as an exit on about 200 outgoing transitions each, the order flips are near-complete while co-occurrence stays at 100% (so nothing became optional or rarely co-occurring), and the adjacency and position evidence agree with the rewiring. A27 and A30 both exist in both windows; what changed is their upstream connections, and the block's internal transition pattern is similar in both windows.
