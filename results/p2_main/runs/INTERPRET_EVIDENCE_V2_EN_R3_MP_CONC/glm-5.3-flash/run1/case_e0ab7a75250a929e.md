### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a choice between two branches was replaced by both branches running in sequence. In the earlier window, after A26 a case either continued to A27 (about 46 in 100 of A26's observed outgoing transitions) or entered the group A36/A37/A42/A43/A44 (about half of the transitions, in varying order), and A27 never co-occurred with the group; in the later window A26 leads almost exclusively to A27, the group runs in every case between A25 and A28 — A25 now feeds the group instead of A28, the direct A25–A28 adjacency has disappeared, and the group now precedes A28 and A26 whereas both previously preceded it — and A27 follows the group in essentially every case. The signs are consistent across indicator families: participation of all six activities rose from about half of cases to every case; relational items show exclusivity turning into universal co-occurrence with stable relative order (group before A27, A26 before A27, group before A28 and A31); directional and lag items show the same rerouting at A25, A26 and the group members. Screening found 145 qualifying items where about 1.36 would be expected without a change, so sampling chance cannot account for the ensemble. Limitations: the order inside the group shows both directions in similar shares in both windows, so no fixed internal sequence is established, and the evidence cannot show whether this restructuring and the A02 insertion reported below are one coordinated modification or two separate ones.
