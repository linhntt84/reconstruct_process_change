### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a timing change centred on A20: in the later window, the elapsed time between the completion of A14 or A15 and the subsequent completion of A20 is about twice as long as before, with the median gap moving from roughly 1 hour to roughly 2 hours on both incoming pairs. Supporting signs: both pairs shift by the same ~1 hour in the same direction, each backed by about 100 observations per window; both differences are larger than essentially all random-split reference differences; and the time family yielded 2 items against an expectation of about 0.008 without change, a count chance cannot plausibly account for [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]. Limitations: both items share A20, so they may reflect one underlying change rather than two independent ones; TWAIT measures elapsed time between consecutive completion events and cannot separate longer idling from longer processing or handover within that interval; and an indirect structural cause, such as changed routing into A20, cannot be fully excluded, since the evidence contains no relational items about what happens between these completions [TWAIT001, TWAIT002].

Other differences: A32 occurs less often in the later window (participation about 60 → 48 in 100 traces; mean occurrences per trace 1.14 → 0.79); as the single control-flow item against a chance expectation of about 0.25, this difference is within what an unchanged process typically produces and is not part of the main hypothesis [OCC001].
