- The relative order of two activity groups reversed. In the earlier window, A35, A36 and A41 ran before A26 and A27 in about 97–99 in 100 traces (each pair co-occurred in every trace); in the later window, A26 and A27 ran before A35, A36 and A41 in about 97–98 in 100 traces. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment formed by A35, A36 and A41 moved from before A26 and A27 to after them: in the earlier window A25 fed the group and the group flowed on to A27, while in the later window A25 feeds A27 directly, A26 feeds the group, and the group flows on to A30. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG002, LAG005]

The reversal is shown directly by relational evidence on six activity pairs, each with full co-occurrence and 200-trace support in both windows, and the positional, successor and distance items all point the same way; the screening volume (45 qualifying items against roughly 0.83 expected for an unchanged process) also cannot be explained by random re-splitting. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, LAG002, LAG005]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of two pre-existing trace variants that differ in this segment order, rather than a rewiring of a single flow; the aggregate windows also leave the relative order of A26 versus A27 themselves unresolved. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015]
