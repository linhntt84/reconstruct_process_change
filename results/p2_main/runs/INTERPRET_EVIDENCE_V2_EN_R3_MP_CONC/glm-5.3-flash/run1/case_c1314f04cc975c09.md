- **Repetition of A25, A26 and A31.** In the earlier window, each of these three activities occurred exactly once in every trace (mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, no repeats). - **Successors of A31.** Before, all observed outgoing transitions of A31 led to A20; in the later window, about 71 in 100 lead to A25 and about 29 in 100 still lead to A20 (based on 697 observed transitions). [DIR001]

- **Spacing between A25 and A31.** Before, A31 followed A25 exactly two positions later in essentially all cases; in the later window that tight two-step spacing accounts for only a minor share of the pair's lag distribution (about 12 in 100), and a five-step spacing appears (about 5 in 100). - **Positions of A25 and A31.** Their mean positions shifted only slightly (A25 about 0.41 to 0.44; A31 about 0.48 to 0.48), but the spread roughly doubled for both (standard deviation about 0.06 to 0.12), consistent with repeats scattered across the trace instead of one fixed slot. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the process repeats a block of activities within each case. A25, A26 and A31, which previously each ran exactly once per case, are now executed about 3.5 times per case on average and recur in about 68 in 100 cases; A31 now typically flows back to A25 (about 71 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, versus none before), and the previously fixed A25-before-A31 sequence is replaced by interleaving or repetition in about 68 in 100 cases. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001]

The hypothesis is supported directly and consistently: all eight qualifying items describe this one difference, against fewer than one item expected by chance among the 60 tested control-flow units; the repetition is shown directly by the occurrence counts, and the successor, order and spacing items show the cyclic behaviour relationally. The behaviour appears in every later-window trace (participation 1.0 in 200 traces), and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, which makes a rare-variant explanation unlikely. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a strict cycle (A25 … A31 back to A25) from repeated or parallel executions of the same activities, because the relational indicator merges interleaving with repetition; A26's membership in the repeated block rests only on its occurrence profile, as no relational item covers it. The increased positional spread of A25 and A31 is consistent with the same repetition and is not an independent confirmation. [REL001, LAG001, OCC002, POS001, POS002]

Other differences: none beyond the pattern above; the residual A31-to-A20 flow (about 29 in 100) is part of the same routing change. [DIR001]
