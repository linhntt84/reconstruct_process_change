### Observed differences

- **Exits from A02 and A04 changed completely.** In the earlier window, about 91% of A02's observed outgoing transitions went to A03 (about 6% to A12), and about 67% of A04's went to A03 (about 20% to A12). In the later window, both activities lead exclusively to A12 — 100% of observed outgoing transitions in each case. [DIR001, DIR003]

- **Exits from A03 and A16 changed.** In the earlier window, A03's transitions split roughly equally between A11 (~45%) and A12 (~45%), with a small share to A04 (~8%); in the later window they go about 55% to A11 and 45% to A10, with no transitions to A12. A16 earlier split about 52% to A10 and 48% to A09; in the later window it splits about 50% to A09 and 50% to A03, and A10 is no longer a direct successor. [DIR002, DIR004]

- **Co-occurrence around A03 flipped.** A03 and A12 appeared together in about 49 in 100 traces earlier but never in the later window; A02–A03 fell from about 26% to 0% and A03–A04 from about 24% to 0%. Conversely, A03 with A10 and A03 with A16 never co-occurred earlier; in the later window each pair co-occurs in about 30 in 100 traces, and A03, A10 and A16 now always appear in the same traces as one another. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]

- **Immediate succession changed.** In the later window, A12 follows A02 within one step in 100% of observed cases (earlier the gap was mostly two or four steps) and likewise follows A04 within one step (earlier spread over gaps of one to four steps). A10, which immediately followed A16 in about 85 in 100 earlier cases, now follows it only at distances of two to five steps. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

- **Positions shifted consistently with the above (event positions, not clock time).** A12 moved from the end of the trace (mean position about 0.97, at least three quarters of occurrences at the final position) to earlier and more variable positions (mean about 0.71, about a quarter of occurrences at the very start). A03 moved later (mean about 0.44 to about 0.67) and now appears in about 30 in 100 traces versus about 49 in 100 earlier; A16 shifted slightly earlier (about 0.53 to 0.43) and A10 slightly later (about 0.83 to 0.90). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the routing around A03 was restructured. In the earlier window, A02 and A04 typically passed through A03, which then led to A11 or to A12 near the end of the case, while A16 led to A10 or A09 in separate cases. In the later window, A02 and A04 no longer pass through A03: they lead directly to A12, which immediately succeeds them and can appear at the very start of the case, while A03 is now executed in the same cases as A16 and A10 — direct transitions from A16 into A03 and from A03 on to A10 are observed, and A03's remaining exits go to A11. In effect, A12 detached from A03, and A03 now runs in the A16/A10 branch rather than after A02 and A04. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002]

Sixteen control-flow items qualified, where an unchanged process would typically produce about 0.21, so chance alone cannot account for the set. The hypothesis rests on direct relational evidence — successor profiles, pair co-occurrence and step distances — not on position shifts alone, and it is consistent across all four indicator families; every activity involved is observed in a substantial share of traces in both windows (for example A03 in about 49% versus 30% of traces, A12 in about 49% versus 45%). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish whether one block was relocated as a unit or whether A12 and A03 were independently re-attached, and it does not pin down the exact internal order after the change — in the later window a sizable share of the A03–A10 and A03–A16 co-occurrences is interleaved or repeated rather than in one fixed sequence. The new routing also rests on somewhat fewer observations for A02 and A04 (about 28 and 35 observed outgoing transitions, respectively, versus about 47 and 46 earlier). [REL003, REL005, DIR001, DIR003]
