### Observed differences

- Two groups of five activities exchanged their place in the case. In the earlier window, A15, A16, A17, A19 and A20 — each executed in every trace — sat in the first half (mean relative event position about 0.39–0.40), while A14, A18, A21, A22 and A23 — each present in only about 17–23 in 100 traces — sat near the end (mean about 0.69–0.71). In the later window this is reversed: A14/A18/A21/A22/A23 sit early (mean about 0.32–0.35) and A15/A16/A17/A19/A20 sit late (mean about 0.64). Positions are event positions, not clock times. [POS001–POS010]
- The order between these groups and the two connector activities A24 and A28 flipped completely. Earlier, A15/A16/A17/A19/A20 always preceded A24 and A28 (for example A15 before A24 and before A28 in 100 in 100 traces), and A14/A18/A21/A22/A23 followed them (A24 before A18 in the roughly 20 in 100 traces containing both). In the later window, A24 and A28 always precede A15/A16/A17/A19/A20 (A24 before A15 in 100 in 100), and A14/A18/A21/A22/A23 precede them (A18 before A24 in about 19 in 100). Pairs across the two groups reversed the same way (A15 before A18 in about 20 in 100 earlier; A18 before A15 in about 19 in 100 later). [REL004, REL005, REL009, REL018, REL022, REL026, REL028, REL001, REL016]
- Outgoing routing moved with this exchange. Earlier, A14, A18, A21, A22 and A23 each led only to A29; later each leads only to A24 (unanimous in the roughly 34–49 traces per window containing each). Earlier, A15–A20 routed among themselves with near-equal shares (about 0.17–0.24 each) and exited to A24 (about 16–29 in 100 of outgoing transitions); later the same internal pattern exits to A29 (about 18–22 in 100). A28 switched from feeding the rare group (about 17–23 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to each of A14/A18/A21/A22/A23) to feeding A15/A16/A17/A19/A20, and A39 switched the opposite way. [DIR001–DIR012]
- A24 and A28 kept their slots between the two groups but moved earlier: A24 from mean position about 0.49 to 0.37, A28 from about 0.67 to 0.55 — consistent with the first block now containing a single alternative instead of five always-executed activities. [POS011, POS012]
- Participation itself was stable: the five alternatives still occur in about 17–24 in 100 traces each, and A15–A20, A24 and A28 in essentially all traces, in both windows. [POS001–POS012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a specific reordering: the two five-activity blocks exchanged their position in the case while their entry and exit points kept their roles. In the earlier window, the block A15, A16, A17, A19, A20 (all five executed per case in a variable internal order) ran first — entered after A39 and exiting to A24 — and the block A14, A18, A21, A22, A23 (one of the five per case, in roughly one case in five) ran last — entered after A28 and exiting to A29. In the later window the contents are exchanged: A14/A18/A21/A22/A23 run first (entered after A39, exiting to A24) and A15/A16/A17/A19/A20 run last (entered after A28, exiting to A29), with A24 and A28 still between the blocks. [POS001–POS012, DIR001–DIR012, REL001–REL031]

Supporting signs: all 31 pair relations reverse direction, ten of them from a unanimous order in one window to the unanimous opposite order in the other (each of A15–A20 against A24 and A28); all 12 successor profiles and all 12 position distributions shift exactly as the exchange predicts; participation of every involved activity is unchanged, so no activity was added, removed or made optional; and 55 control-flow items qualified where fewer than one (0.58) would be expected by chance if nothing had changed. [REL004, REL005, REL009, REL010, REL014, REL015, REL022, REL023, REL026, REL027, DIR001–DIR012, POS001–POS012]

Limitations: the qualified items overlap heavily — the relation, direction and position indicators largely re-describe the same reordering on the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. The evidence also cannot distinguish whether the two activity groups changed position or whether the connectors A39, A28, A24 and A29 were rewired to the other group; both produce identical traces. The unanimous successor flips for the rare alternatives rest on about 34–49 traces per window. [REL001–REL031, DIR001, DIR005, DIR008–DIR010]

Other differences: none beyond sampling-level fluctuations in how often the rare alternatives occur (for example A18 in 40 vs 38 traces, A22 in 46 vs 49, A23 in 45 vs 43); every qualified item is accounted for by the reordering above. [POS005, POS009, POS010, DIR005, DIR009, DIR010]
