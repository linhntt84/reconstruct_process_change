### Observed differences

- A27, A36, A37 and A42 turned from optional into mandatory, once-per-trace activities: in the earlier window each appeared in roughly 46–54 in 100 traces, while in the later window each appears in about 995 in 1000 traces, still exactly once [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004].

- In the earlier window there was an exclusive choice: A27 never ran together with A36, A37 or A42 (co-occurrence in 0–1 of 200 traces), and A26 routed to A27 in about 54 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and to the trio A36/A37/A42 in the remaining ~45. In the later window this choice is gone: A27 co-occurs with each trio member in about 995 in 1000 traces, with the trio executed before A27 in about 99 in 100 [REL011, REL012, REL013, DIR002].

- The trio moved from after A26 to before it: earlier, A26 preceded A36/A37/A42 whenever they ran (about 46 in 100 traces); later, each trio member precedes A26 in about 99 in 100 traces [REL006, REL007, REL008].

- A25 now feeds the trio instead of A28: earlier, about 98 in 100 of A25's outgoing transitions went to A28; later they split almost evenly across A36 (~35 in 100), A42 (~32) and A37 (~31), with a direct A25→A28 transition nearly unobserved (~0.5 in 100). Each trio member in turn now leads to A28 in roughly 30–36 in 100 of its transitions (earlier they led mainly to A31 and to each other), and the distance from A25 to A28 grew from 1 step in 98 in 100 traces to 4 steps in 97 in 100 [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001].

- Positions shifted accordingly: the trio's mean position moved from about 0.59 to about 0.40 of the trace and A28's from 0.39 to 0.46, while A26, A27 and A31 moved slightly later (0.54→0.60, 0.59→0.63, 0.63→0.66) [POS006, POS007, POS008, POS004, POS002, POS003, POS005].

- Downstream, A31 is now preceded by both the trio and A27 in about 99 in 100 traces, and the A26→A31 distance is consistently 2 steps (about 96 in 100) where it was previously a mix of 2 and 4 steps [REL010, REL017, REL018, REL019, LAG003].

- The trio keeps no fixed internal order in either window: in the later window A36, A37 and A42 co-occur in about 995 in 1000 traces and appear in both orders in near-equal shares (e.g., A36 before A37 in 52 in 100, A37 before A36 in 47) [REL020, REL021, REL022].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the exclusive branch between A27 and the trio A36/A37/A42 was replaced by a fixed sequence: in the later window, A25 is followed by A36, A37 and A42 in varying order, then by A28, while A26 is followed by A27 and then A31. In the earlier window, A25 led directly to A28 and A26 led either to A27 alone (about 54 in 100 traces) or to the trio together (about 46 in 100), so A27 and the trio were mutually exclusive alternatives and all four were optional; in the later window all four run in every trace in fixed positions [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL006, REL007, REL008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001].

The hypothesis rests on direct relational and directional evidence rather than frequencies alone: co-occurrence of A27 with each trio member rose from zero to near-certain with a fixed trio-first order, the order around A26 reversed, A26 now routes almost exclusively to A27, A25's outgoing traffic moved from A28 to the trio, the trio now feeds A28, and the A25→A28 distance stretched from one to four steps. Participation, position and gap indicators all shift consistently with this single resequencing, and the number of qualifying items (45 against roughly 0.7 expected without change) far exceeds what an unchanged process produces; note, however, that the agreeing indicators partly reflect the same trace-level changes, so they corroborate rather than independently confirm [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL006, REL007, REL008, DIR002, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008].

Limitations: the evidence shows no successor profile for A28 or A27 and nothing about what precedes A25 and A26, so the surrounding flow is unobserved. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the later window is dominated by a rare pre-existing variant in which A27 and the trio already ran together (one such trace appears in the earlier window) rather than by a modification of the main flow [REL011].

Other differences: only minor, consistent positional drifts of A25 (slightly earlier) and of A26 and A31 (slightly later), which follow from the trio's relocation; the remaining gap items point in the same direction but rest on about one observation in one window and were not relied upon [POS001, POS002, POS005, LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006].
