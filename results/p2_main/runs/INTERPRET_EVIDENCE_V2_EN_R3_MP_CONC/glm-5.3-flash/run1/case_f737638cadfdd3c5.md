### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 never appear in any trace; in the later window each occurs in a substantial minority of traces — A19 in about 37 in 100, A25 in about 34 in 100 and A20 in about 29 in 100 — always at most once per trace, at roughly one-third of the trace length. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- The successor profile of A41 changed fundamentally: earlier, about 93 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions led to A26; later, about 92 in 100 lead to the three activities above (A19 about 34, A25 about 31, A20 about 27 in 100) and only about 1 in 100 still lead to A26. [DIR004]
- The new activities lead almost exclusively to A26: about 96 in 100 of A19's outgoing transitions, 98 in 100 of A20's and 97 in 100 of A25's go to A26. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The pairwise orders match a chain A41 → one new activity → A26: A41 precedes A19, A20 or A25, and each of them precedes A26 in exactly the traces where it occurs; they also precede A31 and A35 in the traces containing both activities of the pair. [REL003, REL006, REL008, REL010, REL011, REL014, REL004, REL009, REL012, REL005, REL013]
- The three new activities are mutually exclusive: any pair of them occurs together in at most about 1 in 200 traces, whereas in the earlier window neither activity of any of these pairs was ever present. [REL001, REL002, REL007]
- The A41–A26 distance changed accordingly: earlier, A26 followed A41 within one step in about 94 in 100 cases; later, it follows at distance two in about 90 in 100 and distance three in about 9 in 100. [LAG002]
- Smaller shifts: A26 and A31 now appear slightly later within traces (medians about 0.36 to 0.38 and 0.70 to 0.71 of trace length), and the A31–A35 distance moved from mostly one step (about 55 in 100) to mostly two steps (about 62 in 100). [POS004, POS005, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the flow from A41 to A26 no longer runs directly but passes through exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities — A19 (about 37 in 100 traces), A25 (about 34) or A20 (about 29) — which then continues to A26; earlier, A41 was followed directly by A26 in about 93 in 100 of its outgoing transitions. [DIR004, LAG002, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

The hypothesis is supported directly and consistently: the rerouting is observed on A41 with about 200 outgoing transitions behind each window's profile, the A41–A26 distance shifted from one to two steps as expected when a step now lies between them, the successors and pairwise orders of the new activities agree with a single exclusive branch between A41 and A26, and participation of 57–74 of 200 traces is far from a rare one-window variant. The screening count also exceeds chance by a wide margin: 28 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.57 expected without change. [DIR004, LAG002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL007, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A19, A20 and A25 were not observed then, so the evidence cannot rule out that these activities already existed but were exercised too rarely to appear; the many qualifying items also largely describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: A26 and A31 occur slightly later within traces, and the A31–A35 distance shifted from mostly one to mostly two steps; these are consistent knock-on effects of the change above but are not part of the main hypothesis. [POS004, POS005, LAG001]
