### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a group of three previously unobserved activities (A32, A33, A38) runs between A26 and A28 in about half of the traces, while A27 — previously always executed between A26 and A28 — is now skipped in about half of the traces; A27 and the new group act as alternatives, and the new activities run in no fixed order among themselves before A28. Screening flagged 55 control-flow items where fewer than one would be expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. The main limitation is that zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that A32, A33 and A38 were not observed then; with 200 earlier traces, an activity occurring in half of them would be very unlikely to be missed entirely, but the evidence cannot formally exclude that these activities existed without being executed.
