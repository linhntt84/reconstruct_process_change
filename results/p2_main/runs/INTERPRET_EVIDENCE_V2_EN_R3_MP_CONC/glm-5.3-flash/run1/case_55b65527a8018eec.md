### Observed differences

- **Participation in two five-activity groups dropped from every trace to about half.** In the earlier window, A33, A34, A35, A37, A38, A39, A40, A41, A42 and A43 each appeared in all 200 traces (about one occurrence per trace, with essentially no repetition). In the later window, A33, A34, A35, A37 and A38 each appear in about 46–48 in 100 traces, and A39, A40, A41, A42 and A43 in about 55 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010]

- **The two groups became mutually exclusive.** Earlier, a first-group activity such as A33 and a second-group activity such as A39 co-occurred in 100 in 100 traces, with the first group before the second in about 98–99 in 100; in the later window they co-occur in only about 0.5–2 in 100 traces, about 46 in 100 traces contain only the first-group activity and about 53–54 in 100 only the second-group activity — so each later trace contains exactly one of the two groups. Cross-group pairs that lay within five steps in about 120 of 200 earlier traces (e.g., A35–A41, A37–A39) now do so in about 1 trace. [REL023, REL030, REL036, REL041, REL045, LAG005, LAG007, LAG012]

- **Each group still moves as one block.** A33 and A34 co-occurred in 100 in 100 traces earlier and in 46 in 100 later, with neither present in about 53 in 100; A39 and A40 co-occur in 55 in 100 later with neither in about 46 in 100. The order inside each group stays mixed (no fixed order) in both windows. [REL019, REL027, REL040, REL049, REL054]

- **A30 and A31 remain in every trace; A30 now precedes whichever group runs.** Earlier, A30 came before A33 in about 99 in 100 traces; in the later window it comes before A33 in about 45 in 100 and before A39 in about 55 in 100, and its outgoing transitions, earlier aimed only at first-group activities, now split across both groups. [POS001, POS002, REL001, REL006, DIR001]

- **A31's predecessor changed from "always after the second group" to "after whichever group ran".** Earlier, A39 preceded A31 in about 98–99 in 100 traces and A33 preceded A31 in about 99 in 100; in the later window, A33 precedes A31 in about 46 in 100 (matching their co-occurrence) and A39 precedes A31 in about 54 in 100. Transitions from A33, A34, A37 and A38 to A31, not observed earlier, now carry about 14–26 in 100 of those activities' outgoing transitions, and A31 lies within five steps after A35, A37 or A38 in about 90 of 200 later traces versus 1 earlier. [REL010, REL015, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG004, LAG006, LAG011]

- **First-group activities no longer lead into the second group.** Earlier, A34's outgoing transitions included A40, A41, A42 and A43 (about 4 in 100 each); in the later window they go only to first-group activities and A31. Relative positions shifted consistently with shorter traces (A33's mean position from about 0.63 to 0.73 of the trace, A39's from 0.76 to 0.73). [DIR003, DIR004, POS003, POS004, POS008, POS009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the routing around two activity blocks changed structurally: earlier, every trace ran A30, then the block A33/A34/A35/A37/A38, then the block A39/A40/A41/A42/A43, then A31; in the later window, each trace runs A30 and then exactly one of the two blocks — about 46 in 100 the first block, about 55 in 100 the second — followed by A31, whereas earlier the two blocks always ran together in a fixed first-then-second order. The change is shown directly by the relational items (cross-block co-occurrence falling from 100 in 100 traces to about 1 in 100, with the fixed order collapsing), it is consistent across the occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators, and chance is an unlikely explanation for the ensemble: 93 control-flow items qualified where about 1 would be expected without a change. [REL023, REL030, REL010, REL015, DIR002, DIR003, OCC001, OCC006]

Other differences: a few new low-frequency transitions appeared (for example A33 to A47/A28/A10, A37 to A06/A19/A36, A38 to A08, each about 1 in 100 of that activity's outgoing transitions), and the spread of relative positions widened for A35 and A41; both are small and may be side effects of the routing change. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, POS005, POS010]

Limitations: the LAG items rest on very few observations (about 90 later traces versus 1 earlier for A31's new predecessors, and single observations for the cross-block pairs), so they only weakly pin down how close A31 now sits to the first block; several indicators describe the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the choice between the two blocks is driven by case attributes outside the log rather than by a single new routing decision, and the position shifts alone cannot show that any activity was moved. [LAG004, LAG006, LAG011, REL023]
