### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a timing change centred on A29: in the later window, the elapsed time from the completion of any of its predecessors A32, A33, A34 and A35 to the completion of A29 is roughly half of what it was in the earlier window (about 1 hour down to about 0.5 hours), and the gaps also became more uniform. Supporting signs: the four time items agree in direction and magnitude, each rests on 40–62 observed gaps per window, and the time family produced 4 qualifying items where only about 0.01 would be expected if nothing had changed — many times the chance expectation — while the control-flow family produced none (0 against roughly 0.3 expected), consistent with a timing change without structural rearrangement.
