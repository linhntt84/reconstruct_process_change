- In the later window, all four flagged activities occur more often per trace: A03 and A17 rise from about 1.5 to about 2.3 occurrences per trace, A05 from about 0.7 to about 1.2, and A15 from about 0.3 to about 0.6 — roughly half again to nearly twice as often. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the activities A03, A05, A15 and A17 are executed more often and repeated more often: each appears in a larger share of traces, occurs more times per trace, and repeats in more traces than in the earlier window. This reads as part of the process around these activities being performed more frequently, with more repetition per case. The supporting signs are that all items move in the same direction on all three occurrence components, four items qualified where about 0.14 were expected by chance, each difference is larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, and the samples behind them are substantial (43–108 traces per window per activity). The main limitation is that the evidence contains no ordering or relational items, so the mechanism — for example which repeated segment produces the extra occurrences — cannot be located; the identical profiles of A03 and A17 also suggest they may co-occur in the same traces, meaning the change may involve fewer independent behaviours than four. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that these activities belong to one repeated block whose repetition frequency rose, rather than four separate changes; either way, the increase in their occurrence and repetition is directly observed.
