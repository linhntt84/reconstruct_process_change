### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A20 and A26 was restructured. In the earlier window every case ran A20, then the group A31/A32/A37, then A23, A21, A22 and A26, all mandatory. In the later window, A20 leads directly to A23 and A21, after which the case takes one of two mutually exclusive branches — A22 alone in about 54 in 100 cases, or the group A31/A32/A37 in about 47 in 100 — before A26. The group thereby changed from a mandatory stage before A23 into an alternative to A22 placed after A21. The reorder and the exclusivity are shown directly by relational, directional and lag evidence rather than inferred from participation alone; the pattern is consistent across the occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators; and 47 items qualified against roughly 0.5 expected for an unchanged process, so chance sampling cannot account for the ensemble. The evidence cannot show whether the branch choice is a routing decision within each case or a mixture of two case populations that behave differently.
