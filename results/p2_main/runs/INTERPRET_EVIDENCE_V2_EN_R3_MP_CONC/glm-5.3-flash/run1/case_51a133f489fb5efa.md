### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A19, A20 and A25 execute before A26 instead of after it, while A27 runs on to A32 through A28 alone. In the earlier window, A26 almost always preceded A19/A20/A25, which followed A27 and led into A28/A32; in the later window A45 leads into A19/A20/A25, directly or through A08, and they lead straight to A26, while A27→A28→A32 runs as a fixed one-step chain. [REL005, REL007, REL009, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG014] As part of the same pattern, A08 appears in about twice as many traces, sits earlier, and now acts as a step between A45 and A19/A20/A25, preceding A26/A27/A28/A32 in about 27 in 100 traces. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001]

The order flip is shown directly by the relational items and is corroborated by the successor changes, the position shifts of A19/A20/A25 (mean about 0.60 to about 0.32) and the step-distance changes (A19 reaches A26 in one step instead of four; A45 reaches A26 in two to three steps instead of one). Screening found 40 qualifying items where about 0.7 would be expected without a change, so chance cannot account for the set, and the items describe one coherent pattern; note that the relational, positional, lag and routing items partly reflect the same traces, so they are consistent rather than independent confirmations. [REL005, REL009, DIR002, DIR004, POS002, POS004, LAG007, LAG010, LAG013]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the earlier late execution of A19/A20/A25 continues in rare variants rather than having relocated; against this, A25's old links (A25→A32, A27→A25, A28→A25) fell from 41–72 observed pairs to 1–2, and A25's participation dropped from about 36 to 31 in 100 traces. Some earlier-window profiles are thin: A08's previous routing rests on 28 observed transitions, and the A19→A26 and A16→A19 lag comparisons had only one observation before, so the before-window side of those items is imprecise. [LAG008, LAG009, LAG012, POS004, DIR001, LAG007, LAG003]

Other differences: A26 and A27 shifted slightly later in the trace (mean position up about 0.03–0.04), consistent with the extra steps now inserted before A26; A16-related step distances to A19 and A26 lengthened, though the A16→A19 before-profile rests on a single observation; A48 and A50 show routing differences too small to interpret.
