### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A21 were alternatives: each took part in about a third of cases (roughly 35, 30 and 36 in 100), they almost never occurred together, and together they covered essentially every case. In the later window, each of them appears in only about 1 in 200 cases. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL007, REL008, REL016]
- In the later window, A26, A27 and A28 each take part in every case (about once per case), whereas A26 and A27 were not observed at all earlier and A28 was seen in about 1 in 200 cases. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, POS005, POS006, POS007]

- The new trio sits in the same segment the old trio occupied: A44 precedes A26/A27/A28 in about 995 in 1000 later cases (it previously preceded A19/A20/A21 in about 99 in 100 of the cases containing them), and A26/A27/A28 precede A29 in about 99 in 100 later cases, where A19/A20/A21 previously ran immediately before A29. The distance from A44 to A29 grew from about 75 in 100 cases at 2 steps to about 90 in 100 cases at 4 steps, matching three activities now sitting between them instead of one. - A29 still takes part in every case but sits slightly later within the case (mean relative position about 0.40 earlier vs 0.44 later). [POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: the three mutually exclusive activities A19, A20 and A21 — one of which was executed in essentially every case, immediately before A29 — no longer appear, while A26, A27 and A28 are now executed together in every case, in no fixed order, in the same slot after A44 and before A29. Because the old activities are nearly absent in the later window and the new ones were absent in the earlier one, this is a replacement of one activity group by another, not a shift in how often existing paths are taken. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR009, REL031, REL033, REL038, LAG005]

The hypothesis is shown directly by relational evidence — A44's outgoing transitions moved from A19/A20/A21 to A26/A27/A28, and the new activities co-occur with A44 and A29 in the expected order — and it is consistent across the occurrence, position, direction, relation and lag indicators. With 74 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.82 expected without a change, chance cannot account for the evidence as a whole. [DIR009, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, REL031, REL032, REL037, LAG005]

Limitations: each window holds only 200 cases, so a shift in the mix of coexisting process variants (different case populations before and after) would look the same, and the new trio could have existed earlier at a rate too low to observe; the later-window behaviour of A19/A20/A21 rests on a single case each. Several items describe the same underlying traces and are therefore not independent confirmations. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]

Other differences: A10's falling participation and redirected transitions, and A46's tighter coupling to A26, are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001, DIR001, REL036]
