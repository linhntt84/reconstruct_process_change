### Observed differences

- The three activities A19, A20 and A25 each appeared in about 47 in 100 traces in the earlier window and in essentially every trace (about 199 in 200) in the later window; mean occurrences rose from roughly 0.5 to about 1.0 per trace, with no meaningful repetition in either window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The routing out of A41 changed sharply: before, about 52 in 100 of its outgoing transitions went directly to A26 and about 44 in 100 to A19; in the later window, about 92 in 100 go to A19 and only about 1 in 200 to A26. [DIR002]
- Accordingly, the distance from A41 to A26 shifted: before, the two were immediate neighbours in about 52 in 100 cases and four steps apart in about 42; in the later window they are four steps apart in about 89 in 100 cases and almost never immediate. [LAG001]
- The internal order of the three activities is unchanged and now near-universal: A19 before A25 (about 47% → 98% of traces), A25 before A20 (about 46% → 99.5%) and A19 before A20 (about 46% → 98%), with pair co-occurrence rising from about 47% to about 99.5%. [REL001, REL002, REL007]
- The block's placement is also unchanged: A41 precedes each of A19, A25 and A20, and each of them precedes A26, in both windows, with these relations rising from roughly 47% to roughly 97–99% as the block became near-universal; A20 still leads to A26 in about 96% (before) and about 99% (after) of its outgoing transitions. [REL003, REL004, REL008, REL009, REL012, REL013, DIR001]
- A26's mean relative position moved slightly later (about 0.41 → 0.43) and A41's slightly earlier (about 0.33 → 0.31), consistent with the three activities now sitting between them in almost every trace. [POS001, POS002]
- Secondarily, A45, which earlier occurred without A19 in about 33 in 100 traces, is in the later window always observed together with A19/A20/A25 while its overall frequency stayed near 60 in 100 traces; A42 now co-occurs with the block in nearly all traces (about 47% → 99.5%). [REL005, REL006, REL010, REL011, REL014, REL015]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the block A19 → A25 → A20 runs between A41 and A26 in almost every case (about 995 in 1000 traces, once per trace), whereas in the earlier window it ran in about 47 in 100 traces and was bypassed in the rest by a direct A41 → A26 transition; that bypass fell from about half of A41's outgoing transitions to about 1 in 200, and the A41-to-A26 distance moved from typically one step to typically four steps. The block's internal order and its position between A41 and A26 are the same in both windows, so the evidence points to the block no longer being skipped — a participation change — rather than to activities being moved, swapped or replaced. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, LAG001, REL001, REL002, REL007, REL003, REL004, REL008, REL009, REL012, REL013, POS001, POS002]

The volume of flagged items (23, all control-flow) is many times the roughly 0.64 expected without a change, and participation, routing and distance evidence show the same difference directly and consistently. The evidence cannot, however, distinguish a removal of the skip option from a shift in the mix of two pre-existing execution variants (with and without the block), and about 1 in 200 later traces still lack the block, so strictly mandatory execution is not confirmed. [OCC001, DIR002, LAG001]

Other differences: A45 no longer occurs without the block (earlier about 33 in 100 traces showed A45 alone of the pair; now none do) while its overall participation stayed near 60 in 100 traces, and A42's conditional behaviour given the block (A19 before A42 in roughly 60% of shared traces, interleaved in roughly 40%) is similar across windows, so both shifts largely follow from the block's near-universal execution rather than being independent changes. [REL005, REL006, REL010, REL011, REL014, REL015]
