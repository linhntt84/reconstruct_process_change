### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A14, A15 and A20 are never observed (0 of 200 traces each). In the later window, A14 runs in about 29 in 100 traces (58 of 200), A15 in about 41 in 100 (82 of 200) and A20 in about 30 in 100 (59 of 200), in each case exactly once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the later window these three behave as mutually exclusive alternatives: no trace contains two of them together, and their shares sum to about 995 in 1000 traces, so exactly one of them executes in almost every trace. In the earlier window, neither activity of each pair was ever present. [REL001, REL002, REL006]
- Earlier, the fragment A22 → A23 → A24 is tight: A22's only observed successor is A23 and A23's only observed successor is A24, one step apart in every trace. In the later window these direct links loosen: A22 is followed directly by A23 in about 44 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and by one of A14/A15/A20 in about 56 in 100, while A23 is followed directly by A24 in about 57 in 100 and by one of the new activities in about 43 in 100. [DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003]
- The new activities sit inside the former A22–A24 span: A22 always precedes them and they always precede A24, but their order against A23 is mixed — for example, A14 precedes A23 in about 16 in 100 traces and follows it in about 14 in 100, with similar splits for A15 and A20. Each new activity is followed by A23 in roughly 53–58 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and by A24 otherwise. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- Distances and positions move accordingly: the A22→A24 distance grows from always 2 steps to 3 steps in about 995 in 1000 later-window traces, the new activities appear near the middle of the trace (mean position about 0.51), and A22's own mean position shifts slightly earlier, from about 0.48 to about 0.46. [LAG002, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural insertion: in the later window, one of three mutually exclusive activities — A14, A15 or A20 — executes once in almost every trace, placed inside the former A22 → A23 → A24 sequence between A22 and A24, before A23 in roughly 56 in 100 traces and after A23 in roughly 44 in 100. This is not a re-weighting of existing paths, since the three activities were absent from the earlier window; the loosening of previously rigid successions and the shift from a fixed 2-step to a 3-step A22–A24 distance are observed directly. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL005, REL009, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The hypothesis is consistent across all five indicator types — occurrence, succession, relative order, distance and position all describe the same insertion — and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. Chance as a whole is also ruled out by the screening comparison: 27 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.4 would be expected if nothing had changed. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed then, so a competing reading — that these activities existed in the process all along but were simply not exercised before — cannot be ruled out. The evidence also shows no fixed order between the new activities and A23, so it cannot indicate what determines whether the inserted step lands before or after A23. [REL004, REL008, REL011]
