### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the five-activity block A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 now executes after A21 (and after A22) rather than before them: in the earlier window the flow ran from A20 into the block, the block exited to A22, and A21 followed later, always closing into A25; in the later window A20 leads directly to A22, A21 leads into the block, and the block closes into A25. This rests on the complete and uniform order reversal between A21/A22 and each of the five while co-occurrence stays at 100% (which rules out a participation change), on the rewired successors on both sides of the block, and on the consistent position shifts; 34 items qualified against roughly 0.55 expected for an unchanged process, and every item's difference exceeds about 99.5% of random re-splits. Limitations: the items largely describe one underlying rearrangement rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence shows relative order only — it cannot distinguish "the block moved later" from "A21 and A22 moved earlier", nor does it cover the mutual order of A21 and A22 or what happens between them in the later window (A22 and A25 are not analysed as routing sources).
