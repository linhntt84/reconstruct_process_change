### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment formed by A36, A37, A42, A43 and A44 was restructured. In the earlier window it was mandatory in every trace, running between A25 and A28 and followed by A26 → A27 → A31; in the later window A28 directly follows A25, and after A26 the process takes one of two mutually exclusive branches — A27 → A31 in about 45 in 100 traces, or the five-activity segment → A31 in about 55 in 100. The segment thus became optional (skipped in about 45 in 100 traces), moved from before A28/A26 to after A26, and changed its exit from A28 to A31; A27 now runs only when the segment is skipped. Supporting signs: 106 evidence items qualified against about 1.2 expected for an unchanged process (all in the control-flow family), a gap far too large for sampling noise; the successor changes show the rewiring directly, the relation items show the order flip and the mutual exclusivity of A27 and the segment within jointly observed traces, and participation and position shifts agree with the same restructuring rather than with isolated fluctuations.
