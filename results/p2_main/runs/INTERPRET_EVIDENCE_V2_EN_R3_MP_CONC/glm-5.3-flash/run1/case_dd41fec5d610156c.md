### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural reorganisation around the A04–A06 loop: A03 — previously the entry point, loop hub and exit route to A05 — no longer occurs, A02 now starts every trace, and A01 is inserted between the loop and A05. In the later window, traces begin with A02, which either starts the loop at A04 or goes directly to A01; the loop repeats via A06 → A04 and exits via A06 → A01, after which A01 leads to A05. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation in A01 and A02 rose from none to all 200 traces while A03 fell from all 200 to none; the successor profiles of A02, A06 and A01 show exactly this rewiring; the relation indicators show the A02 → A01 → A05 sequence; and the shortening of the A04/A06 repeat distance from about 3 to about 2 steps matches A03 dropping out of the cycle. Screening flagged 29 control-flow items where about 0.17 would be expected without a change, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole.
