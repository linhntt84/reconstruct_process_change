### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two blocks of activities exchanged their places around a preserved middle segment. In the earlier window a case ran from A35 through one of A14, A16 and A19 into A20, continued via A24, and closed with A15, A17 and A18 before A25; in the later window A35 leads through A15, A17 and A18 into A20, the middle segment up to A24 follows, and A24 then leads through one of A14, A16 and A19 to A25. Each block keeps its internal character: the three branch activities remain mutually exclusive alternatives (their trace shares sum to about 100 in 100 in both windows), and A15, A17 and A18 remain a set executed once each in varying order, only now exiting to A20 instead of A25. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG002]

The reading is direct and consistent across all indicator types: every boundary activity shows a complete successor replacement rather than a partial shift; six between-group order relations flip from a perfect order before to the perfect opposite order after; the six block activities shift position by about a quarter to a third of the trace length while A20 and A24 move only about 0.07; and the step-distance swaps match the block sizes (one alternative versus three sequence steps). Participation of every involved activity is essentially unchanged, which fits a relocation of existing behaviour rather than activities becoming optional, repeated or new. With 39 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.5 expected if nothing had changed, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [REL008, REL009, REL015, REL016, REL018, REL019, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG001, LAG002, DIR001, DIR003, DIR006]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is an additional change inside the middle segment between A20 and A24: no evidence item covers that segment, even though A20 and A24 themselves keep their relative order in both windows. [POS007, POS008, DIR007]

Other differences: the share of traces containing A16 fell from about 35 to 29 in 100 while A19 rose from about 36 to 39 (A14 about 30 to 33); these shifts are small, may reflect sampling variation between two 200-trace windows, and do not alter the block-exchange reading. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR006]
