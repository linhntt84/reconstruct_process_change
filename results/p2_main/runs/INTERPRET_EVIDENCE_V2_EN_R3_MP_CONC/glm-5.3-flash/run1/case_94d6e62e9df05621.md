### Observed differences

- Both activities became less frequent. A07 appeared in about 53 in 100 traces in the earlier window and about 23 in 100 in the later window; A08 fell from about 53 to about 29 in 100. Neither activity repeated within a trace in either window. [OCC001, OCC002]
- The relationship between A07 and A08 reversed. Earlier, at least one of the two appeared in about 53 in 100 traces, and whenever one appeared, both did (co-occurrence about 53 in 100, neither activity alone ever); both orders were about equally common (A07 before A08 in about 28 in 100, A08 before A07 in about 25 in 100), so there was no fixed order between them. In the later window, at least one still appeared in about 51 in 100 traces, but the two were never observed in the same trace: A07 alone in about 23 in 100, A08 alone in about 29 in 100, neither in about 49 in 100. [REL001]
- The direct handovers between the two disappeared from the observed transitions. Earlier, about 65 in 100 of A07's observed outgoing transitions went to A08, and about 61 in 100 of A08's went to A07; in the later window no transition between them was observed. Both activities now lead mainly to A01 (about 72 in 100 of A07's observed outgoing transitions, about 71 in 100 of A08's) and otherwise to A05 (about 28 and 29 in 100). These later routing profiles rest on few observations (18 and 17 outgoing transitions). [DIR001, DIR002]
- A08's typical event position moved slightly later in the trace (median from about 0.67 to 1.0); this measures order within the trace, not clock time. [POS001]
- Every item's difference was larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, REL001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A07 and A08 stopped being executed together: in the earlier window they always ran in the same case, in either order and directly handing over to one another, while in the later window they were never observed in the same case and instead occur as mutually exclusive single visits, each proceeding mainly to A01 rather than to the other. At the same time, both activities take part in fewer cases (A07 from about 53 to about 23 in 100 traces; A08 from about 53 to about 29). [REL001, DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, OCC002]

The screening yield (6 qualified items against roughly 0.11 expected without change) is far too large to be explained by chance sampling. The hypothesis is shown directly by the relational evidence (co-occurrence from about 53 in 100 traces to 0, across about 102 later traces containing at least one of the pair) and by the successor changes observed at both activities, with the participation drops agreeing in direction. The six items most likely reflect this one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [REL001, DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, OCC002]

Limitations: the later routing shares rest on only 18 and 17 outgoing transitions, so the new proportions to A01 and A05 are uncertain, and an unobserved edge does not prove the edge is gone. The evidence cannot rule out that joint execution of A07 and A08, or direct successions between them, still occur at a low rate that the later sample did not capture, rather than having stopped entirely. [DIR001, DIR002, REL001]

Other differences: A08's event position shifted slightly later in the trace (median about 0.67 to 1.0), which is consistent with A08 no longer being followed by A07 but, being position-based, does not by itself establish a shift in timing. [POS001]
