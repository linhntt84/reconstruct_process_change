- In the earlier window, each of A31, A32, A37, A38 and A39 appeared in every trace, exactly once per trace; in the later window, each appears in about 55 in 100 traces. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the block A31/A32/A37/A38/A39 became an optional branch after A21 that is skipped in about 45 in 100 traces: A21 now leads either directly to A22 (about 45% of its outgoing transitions, with A22 then running before A26) or into the block (about 55%, with the block then running before A26), and A22 and the block are mutually exclusive, co-occurring in about 1 in 200 traces. The block also changed placement: earlier it always ran between A20 and A23/A21 — A20 entered it and it exited to A23 — whereas in the later window A20 leads directly to A23, A21 feeds the block, and the block exits to A26. [OCC001–OCC006, DIR001–DIR007, REL007–REL029]

The support is direct rather than merely cumulative: the order reversals between the block and A21/A23 and the A22–block exclusivity are observed relationally, the rewiring is observed in the successor profiles of A20, A21 and the block, and the participation and position components shift consistently; 72 items qualified against roughly 0.7 expected for an unchanged process (about 100 times the chance expectation, each item exceeding about 995 of 1000 random re-splits), with 200 before-traces and 91–110 after-traces behind the key profiles. [OCC001–OCC006, DIR001–DIR007, POS002–POS010, REL007–REL029]

The evidence cannot rule out that a comparable skip path already existed as a rare variant (the earlier window, with the block in 200 of 200 traces, would miss anything rarer than about 1 in 200) and only became frequent; nor does the tested relation set cover A23 versus A21 directly, so their order in the new branch is suggested only by mean positions. [OCC002–OCC006, REL001–REL039, POS002, POS004]

Other differences: only minor position drifts for A20 (mean position about 0.32 → 0.35) and A26 (about 0.67 → 0.65), which accompany the restructuring above rather than forming a separate change. [POS001, POS005]
