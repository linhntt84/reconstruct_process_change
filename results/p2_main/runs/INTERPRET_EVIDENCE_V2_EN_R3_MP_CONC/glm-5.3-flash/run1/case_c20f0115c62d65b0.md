### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of three alternative activities A19, A20 and A25 — which in the earlier window was executed exactly once in every trace, inserted between the A27/A28 region and A29 — is no longer executed in the later window, leaving a direct A27 → A28 → A29 course. Participation of all three fell from 28–40% to about 0.5% on solid before-support (56–80 traces each); their co-occurrence with A27, A28 and A29 collapsed in pairs observed in all 200 traces of both windows; and the lag distributions contracted to direct succession exactly where the block used to sit. The screening flagged 27 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected without change, so the ensemble is unlikely to be a sampling artifact. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL005, REL009, REL010, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, DIR004, DIR005]

Limitations: the three activities still appear in one trace each, so complete removal versus extreme rarity cannot be distinguished; the single-trace successor profiles of A19, A20 and A25 cannot establish any replacement activities; and the many agreeing items largely describe one and the same trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the block survives in a now very rare variant rather than having been dropped from the flow entirely. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
