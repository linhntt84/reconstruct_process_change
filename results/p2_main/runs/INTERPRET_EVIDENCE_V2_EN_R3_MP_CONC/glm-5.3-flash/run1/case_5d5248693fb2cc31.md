### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening tested 129 control-flow units and 28 items qualified, where an unchanged process would be expected to produce about 0.64; a count many times larger cannot be explained by chance as a whole. The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the block of alternative activities A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 is executed after A29/A30 rather than before A28: the block sits between A29 and A34 instead of between A47 and A28, and A47 now connects directly to A28.
