### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the block A20, A21, A22, A24, A25 executes before A29 and A33, while the block A19, A23, A26, A27, A28 executes after them — the two blocks swapped their order around A29 and A33, and the handover into A34 now comes from the second block instead of the first. This rests on direct relational evidence (near-total order flips for every cross-block pair and against A29/A33), agreeing positional shifts of about 0.24 earlier and 0.35 later in opposite directions, and consistent successor changes (the A29↔A34 handover swap, A33 and A44 retargeting to the opposite block). Screening produced 93 qualifying items against about 1 expected without change, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a modification of the process from a flipped mix of two pre-existing execution variants; the lag-based items mostly rest on a single observation in one window and add little support; A34's own position was not measured directly.
