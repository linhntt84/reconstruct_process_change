### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a block of five previously unobserved activities — A20, A21, A30, A31 and A32 — now executes in every case, in no fixed order among themselves, between A48/A04 and A33: A04 participates in most cases (about 72 in 100, up from about 10 in 100) and directly follows A48, while the activities that previously ran directly before A33 in a minority of cases (A19, A22, A23, A24, A25) are no longer observed.
