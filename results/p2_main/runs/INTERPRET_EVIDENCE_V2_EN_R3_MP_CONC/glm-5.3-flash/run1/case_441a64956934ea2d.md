### Observed differences

- The two activities A01 and A13 exchanged their overall presence: in the earlier window A01 appeared in about 66 in 100 traces (about 2.2 times per trace) and A13 in about 23 in 100 traces (about 0.4 times per trace); in the later window A01 appears in only about 28 in 100 traces (about 0.6 times per trace) while A13 appears in about 61 in 100 traces (about 2.1 times per trace). [OCC001, OCC002]
- Their typical positions in the trace swapped: A01 moved from early (mean relative position about 0.42) to late (about 0.77), while A13 moved from late (about 0.77) to early (about 0.35). [POS001, POS002]
- Their outgoing transitions swapped almost exactly: earlier, A01's observed transitions went to A08 (about 48%), A15 (about 26%) and A07 (about 26%), while A13 led only to A06; in the later window A13 leads to A08 (about 45%), A15 (about 28%) and A07 (about 28%), while A01 leads only to A06. [DIR001, DIR006]
- Common predecessors redirected accordingly: A02, A04, A07 and A15 now lead to A13 with nearly the shares they previously used for A01 (for example A07: about 51% to A01 earlier, about 50% to A13 later), and A06 now continues to A01 (about 54%) instead of A13 (about 47% earlier). [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR007]
- Co-occurrence followed the same shift: A01–A08 togetherness fell from about 66 to 18 in 100 traces while A13–A08 rose from about 17 to 61 in 100; earlier A01 usually appeared without A13 (about 49 in 100 traces), later A13 usually appears without A01 (about 44 in 100). [REL005, REL006, REL012]
- Short-range sequences transferred as well: A02, A04, A07 and A15 were followed by A01 within 1–2 steps earlier and are now followed by A13 within 1–2 steps, while the residual A01 pairs occur only at distances of 4–5 steps on about 11–15 traces; A08→A01 at distance 3 became A08→A13 at distance 3. [LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG013, LAG014]
- Even self-repetition mirrored the exchange: A01 followed itself mostly at distance 3 earlier and at distance 2 later, whereas A13 followed itself at distance 2 earlier and at distance 3 later. [LAG001, LAG012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 and A13 exchanged their places in the flow: the frequent, early, self-repeating step that fed A08/A15/A07 was executed as A01 in the earlier window and as A13 in the later window, while the rare late step leading to A06 was executed as A13 earlier and as A01 later. This is credible as a whole because 38 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.46 expected for an unchanged process, and the items do not scatter but consistently describe one exchange, with the successor profiles, positions, co-occurrences and lag distributions of the two activities matching each other's earlier values almost exactly [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR006, POS001, POS002, REL005, REL012, LAG004, LAG008]. The main limitation is that traces cannot distinguish a genuine exchange of the two activities' positions from a like-for-like replacement in which each activity simply took over the other's role, and a few later-window lag items rest on only about 11–15 traces [LAG003, LAG005, LAG007, LAG013].

Other differences: none beyond this exchange; A06's second successor A14 kept a roughly stable share of A06's outgoing transitions (about 53% earlier, about 46% later) [DIR004].
