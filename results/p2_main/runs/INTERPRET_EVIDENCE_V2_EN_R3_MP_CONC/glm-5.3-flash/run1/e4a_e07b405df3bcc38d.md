### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, each of A28, A29, A30, A32 and A33 appeared in about 48 in 100 cases, and each of A34–A38 in about 52 in 100; in the later window all ten appear in nearly every case (199 of 200 and 196 of 200 respectively), still at most once per trace in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010]
- The two groups of five were alternatives before: no case contained activities from both groups (cross-group co-occurrence 0 of 200, with about 48 in 100 cases showing only the first group and 52 only the second). In the later window, about 97–98 in 100 cases contain both groups, and whenever they co-occur a first-group activity comes first. [REL025, REL033, REL040, REL046, REL051]
- A25 precedes the groups and A26 follows them in both windows: A25 came before the executed group in about 48–52 in 100 cases earlier and does so in about 98–100 in 100 later; the group preceded A26 in the same shares. [REL001, REL006, REL011, REL016]
- Within each group, pairs appear in both orders in similar shares in both windows (e.g., A28 before A29 in about 20 in 100 earlier vs 50 in 100 later, the reverse about 28 vs 50), so there is no fixed internal order either before or after. [REL021, REL056]
- Direct transitions changed accordingly: earlier, A25's outgoing transitions spread over all ten group activities (about 8–16% each); later they go almost only to the five first-group activities (about 15–24% each, second group about 0.5% each). A28's transition to A26 (about 28% of its outgoing) is no longer observed, while small direct transitions from first-group to second-group activities (about 2–5%) appear. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- Relative event positions shifted consistently with this chaining: A25's mean position fell from about 0.64 to 0.55 of the trace, first-group activities from about 0.73 to 0.63, second-group activities rose from about 0.72–0.73 to 0.76–0.77, and A26 from 0.82 to 0.84 (event positions, not clock times). - Five-step reachability shrank as the groups chained: A25 followed within five steps by a second-group activity fell from 104 traces to 1, and a first-group activity followed by A26 within five steps from 96 to 4; the later profiles rest on only 1–4 observations. [LAG001, LAG006, LAG007]
- The elapsed time between consecutive completion events on the segments A14→A20 and A15→A20 fell from about 1.0 hour to about 0.5 hour, with event counts stable (89→93 and 111→107). [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the exclusive choice between two five-activity blocks between A25 and A26 appears to have been replaced by both blocks executing in every case: first A28, A29, A30, A32 and A33, then A34, A35, A36, A37 and A38, each block without a fixed internal order. This is shown directly by cross-group pairs that never co-occurred earlier (0 of 200) and co-occur in about 97–98 of 100 later cases with the first group always earlier [REL025, REL033, REL040, REL046, REL051]; it is consistent with the near-universal participation of all ten activities [OCC001, OCC006], with A25's outgoing transitions narrowing to the first group while the first group lost its direct transition to A26 and gained direct transitions to the second group [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004], and with the position and reachability shifts [POS003, POS008, LAG001, LAG006]. The screening comparison rules out a sampling artefact: 98 control-flow items qualified where about 1 would be expected without change [OCC001, REL025, DIR001].

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish whether the two blocks now run in parallel or in a varying sequence, since within-group pairs keep mixed orders [REL021, REL056]; it also cannot tell which block was added to the cases that previously ran only the other, as both readings produce the same co-occurrence pattern. A31 does not appear in the evidence and was not tested, and the LAG after-profiles rest on 1–4 observations [LAG001, LAG006, LAG007].

Other differences: the elapsed time between consecutive completion events on the segments ending at A20 (from A14 and from A15) roughly halved, from about 1.0 hour to about 0.48–0.51 hours, with stable event counts; the time family produced 2 items against about 0.03 expected without change, so a consistent timing change on these segments is a hypothesis in its own right, though the evidence offers no link between it and the structural change above. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
