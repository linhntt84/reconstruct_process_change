### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the five-activity block A30, A31, A36, A37, A38 and the A22–A21 segment exchanged their order: in the earlier window a case typically ran A20 → A22 → A21 → the block (in varying internal order) → A25, whereas in the later window it typically runs A20 → the block → A22 → A21 → A25. Screening produced 34 qualifying items from 110 tested units against roughly 0.55 expected for an unchanged process, all in the control-flow family, so the volume of signals is far beyond what random re-splits of a stable process produce. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: all ten relational items flip from a 100% fixed order to the opposite fixed order while co-occurrence stays complete in both windows, which rules out a rare variant appearing in one window or a shift between alternatives and joint execution; the routing changes at the block's entry (A20 into the block) and exits (block to A22 instead of A25; A21 to A25) match the same re-wiring; and all seven position shifts point in the same direction. Every DIR, POS and REL item is backed by all 200 traces in each window.
