### Observed differences

- Earlier, exactly one of the three alternatives A14, A15 or A20 ran in essentially every case (about 38, 31 and 32 in 100 cases respectively), early in the case at mean position about 0.31–0.33: all observed outgoing transitions of A40 went to one of the three, and the chosen alternative was always followed by A21. In the later window the three still appear once per case (about 31, 33 and 37 in 100), but much later at mean positions about 0.59–0.60, and A40 now leads directly to A21 in every case. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG004]
- The order relations reversed: earlier, A14, A15 and A20 always preceded A21 and A22 whenever they co-occurred; in the later window A21 and A22 always precede the chosen alternative (for example, A21 before A14 in about 31 in 100 cases, never the reverse). - The relation to A23 changed from fixed to unfixed: earlier the alternatives always ran before A23; in the later window both orders occur (A23 before A14 in about 16 in 100 cases and A14 before A23 in about 16; A23 before A20 in about 23 and A20 before A23 in about 15). [REL003, REL008]
- Downstream routing changed: earlier, A22 was always followed by A23 and A23 always by A27. In the later window, A22 is followed by A23 in about 52 in 100 cases and otherwise by the chosen alternative; A23 is followed by A27 in about 48 in 100 and otherwise by the chosen alternative; each alternative is followed by A23 or A27 in roughly equal shares (A20 leaning to A27, about 61 vs 39 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- Step gaps match this: A40→A21 is now always 1 step (earlier always 2), A22→A27 is now always 3 steps (earlier always 2), and the A22→A23 and A23→A27 gaps are now 1 or 2 steps (earlier always 1). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]
- The always-present activities shifted slightly earlier: mean positions about 0.35 to 0.32 for A21, 0.58 to 0.55 for A22, and 0.61 to 0.59 for A23. [POS004, POS005, POS006]
- The elapsed time between consecutive completion events from A31 to A22 roughly halved, from a mean of about 1.0 hour to about 0.5 hour (middle half of cases from about 0.9–1.1 hours to about 0.45–0.55 hours), measured in 200 cases in each window. [TWAIT001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-way choice between A14, A15 and A20 — one of which executes in essentially every case — now takes place late in the process, between A22/A23 and A27, whereas it previously took place early, between A40 and A21: earlier, A40 handed over to the chosen alternative, which always ran before A21; in the later window A40 leads directly to A21, and the chosen alternative runs after A21 and A22, is followed by A23 or A27, and has no fixed order relative to A23. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

The hypothesis is shown directly by the successor changes at A40 and at the three alternatives, by the complete reversal of their order relations with A21 and A22, and by gap changes that match the placement change (A40→A21 from two steps to one, A22→A27 from two to three). Participation itself is unchanged — each of the three still occurs in about 30–38 in 100 cases in both windows — so this is a change of placement and ordering, not of how often existing paths are taken. The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process would produce (24 control-flow items against roughly 0.4 expected by chance, with every item's difference exceeding about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits). [DIR006, REL001, REL002, POS001, LAG002, LAG004]

Limitations: positions are event positions rather than clock times, so the evidence shows a change in order and placement, not in timing; and in the later window the data cannot distinguish a genuinely unfixed order between A23 and the chosen alternative from two fixed sub-sequences (A22→A23→alternative→A27 and A22→alternative→A23→A27) each taken in about half the cases. [REL003, REL008, LAG001, LAG003]

Other differences: the elapsed time between consecutive completion events from A31 to A22 halved (mean about 1.0 hour to about 0.5 hour); this timing difference stands on its own — it is the only time item, against far fewer than one expected by chance — and the evidence does not show whether it is connected to the placement change, since A31 appears in no other evidence item. [TWAIT001]
