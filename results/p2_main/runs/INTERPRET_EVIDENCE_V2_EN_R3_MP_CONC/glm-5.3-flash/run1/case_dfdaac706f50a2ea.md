### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural reordering with a new exclusive choice around the A36/A37/A42 group: in the earlier window every trace executed the group between A25 and A28 and then A27 between A26 and A31; in the later window A25 leads directly to A28, and after A26 each trace takes either A27 (about 53 in 100) or the whole A36/A37/A42 group (about 47 in 100) before A31 — the group is skipped in about half the traces and has become mutually exclusive with A27, which previously always ran. This is shown directly by relational evidence (the order between the group and A28, and between the group and A26, is reversed; A27 co-occurs with each group member in about 1 in 100 traces instead of all), by successor changes at A25 and at the group members, and by consistent participation drops from 100% to about 47–54%, each backed by 94–107 traces; 50 items qualified against about 0.72 expected if nothing had changed, so sampling alone cannot account for the pattern. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG005]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the exclusive choice is governed by a single decision point or arises from two independent skip behaviours, nor can it rule out that the later window pools two coexisting trace variants rather than one process with new routing; several distance profiles for pairs involving the group rest on a single observation in one window and are weak. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011]

Other differences: A25's mean position moved slightly later (about 0.34 to 0.36) and A31's slightly earlier (about 0.65 to 0.63), and A27's position became more spread out; these are consistent with the same restructuring rather than a separate change. [POS001, POS005, POS003]
