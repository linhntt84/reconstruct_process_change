### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the five-way alternative step between A38 and A23 is no longer executed in the later window: previously every case ran A38, then exactly one of A14, A15, A20, A21 or A22, then A23, whereas now A38 leads directly to A23 and none of the five appears in 200 later traces. The change is shown directly by the successor change at A38 and by the A38→A23 distance shrinking from two steps to one in every trace, and it is consistent across the participation, position and relation items for the five activities and their downstream partners. [DIR006, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL005, REL006, REL020, REL021]

A chance explanation is implausible: 44 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.54 expected for an unchanged process, and 4 time items against about 0.01, and each of the five activities had 33–49 supporting traces before, so their joint disappearance is not a sampling effect. An important competing explanation remains: the evidence cannot distinguish the block having been removed from it still existing but not being taken in any later trace, since zero participation only shows the activities were not observed. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR006]

Other differences: A41 and A42 now run in about two thirds of traces, up from about half, and no longer appear alongside the five removed activities. [REL008, REL009, REL013, REL014, REL017, REL018, REL023, REL024, REL025, REL026]

The elapsed time between consecutive completion events into A28 (from A16, A17, A18 or A19) roughly doubled, from about 1 hour to about 2.1 hours; this is a timing change in its own right, and the evidence provides no link between it and the structural change above. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004]
