### Observed differences

- The choice between two blocks of five activities flipped between the windows. In the earlier window, A26–A30 each appeared in about 82 in 100 traces while A21–A25 each appeared in only about 19 in 100; in the later window, A21–A25 each appear in about 77 in 100 while A26–A30 each appear in about 24 in 100. All ten activities remain observed in both windows, and each occurs at most once per trace (recurrence support near zero). [OCC001, OCC005, OCC006, OCC010]
- The two blocks are mutually exclusive alternatives in both windows: an activity from A21–A25 and one from A26–A30 hardly ever share a trace (co-occurrence about 0 before and after), so only the choice share between them moved. [REL035, REL039, REL059, REL063]
- Within-block togetherness mirrors the flip: A26–A30 co-occurred in about 82 in 100 traces earlier, whereas in the later window A21–A25 co-occur in about 77 in 100 and A26–A30 in only about 24 in 100. [REL031, REL034, REL071, REL074]
- Routing out of A20 changed accordingly: earlier, about 81% of A20's observed outgoing transitions went to A29 (about 18% to A24); in the later window, about 75% go to A24 and about 23% to A29. [DIR001]
- A14, present in essentially every trace in both windows, now typically precedes the A21–A25 block (co-occurrence about 77 in 100, with A14 first in about 76 in 100), where earlier it co-occurred with A26–A30 in about 82 in 100. [REL011, REL016]
- A05 and A34 now travel with the A21–A25 block instead of the A26–A30 block: A34–A21 co-occurrence rose from about 19 to about 77 in 100 traces while A34–A26 fell from about 82 to about 24 in 100; A05–A21 co-occurrence rose from about 12 to about 35 in 100 while A05–A26 fell from about 38 to about 10 in 100. [REL001, REL006, REL040, REL075]
- Minor, position-based only: gaps between consecutive branch activities redistributed slightly (for example A22→A25 gaps moved from mostly one step to roughly even one-to-three steps), and A29's position spread widened a little on fewer observations; these are event-position measures, not clock times. [LAG002, LAG003, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the choice between two existing alternative paths changed: the path in which A21–A25 execute together — typically preceded by A14 and reached from A20 — is now taken in about three quarters of traces, where before it was taken in about one in five, while the alternative path A26–A30 dropped from about four in five to about one in four. [OCC001, OCC006, DIR001, REL011, REL016]

Supporting signs: all ten activities change participation in the same mirrored pattern; the exclusivity between the blocks and the within-block togetherness are preserved across both windows, and every involved activity is observed in a substantial share of traces in both windows (roughly 19–82 in 100) with at most one occurrence per trace — consistent with existing paths being taken with different frequencies rather than activities being added, removed or repeated. The rerouting is also visible directly at A20, whose dominant successor switches from A29 to A24. [OCC001, OCC010, REL035, REL071, DIR001] The volume of qualifying items also far exceeds what a stable process produces by chance: 103 control-flow items qualified against about 1.15 expected without a change. [screening]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply contain a different mix of cases (for example two case types whose relative frequencies shifted) rather than a modified process, since the same two exclusive paths exist in both windows and only their shares moved. The order within the favoured branch is only partly pinned down; for example A21 and A22 appear in both orders in similar proportions in the later window. [REL031]

Other differences: a negligible redistribution of A25's outgoing transitions (still about 99% to A34), together with the small position-based gap and spread shifts noted above; these appear secondary to the branch-choice change and do not form a separate hypothesis. [DIR002, LAG001, LAG005, POS001]
