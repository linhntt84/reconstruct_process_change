### Observed differences

- **Two alternative activity groups now run together as one block.** In the earlier window, A33, A35 and A36 each ran in about 49 in 100 traces and A37, A38 and A39 each in about 52 in 100; within each group the members appeared together in roughly half of the traces in no fixed order, while no trace contained activities from both groups (cross-group co-occurrence 0 in 100). In the later window, all six activities run in about 98–100 in 100 traces, cross-group pairs co-occur in about 98 in 100 traces, and A33/A35/A36 come before A37/A38/A39 in about 97 in 100 of those traces; the order within each group remains unfixed, with both orders in similar proportions. [OCC004–OCC009, REL037–REL051, POS006–POS011]

- **A31 now follows the enlarged block.** Earlier, A31 directly followed whichever group ran (it received about 30–36 in 100 of the outgoing transitions of A33/A35/A36 and sat 1–3 positions after them); in the later window those direct edges nearly vanish (under 1 in 100) and A31 sits 4–5 positions after A33/A35/A36, still after the whole block in about 98 in 100 traces. [DIR007–DIR009, LAG006–LAG008, REL031–REL036]

- **A30 now enters the block at its start.** Earlier, A30's outgoing transitions spread over both groups (about 15–18 in 100 to each of A33/A35/A36 and to A37–A39); in the later window they lead almost only to A33/A35/A36 (about 30–37 in 100 each), and the distance from A30 to A37/A38/A39 grew from 1–3 to 4–5 positions. A14 and A30 precede the block in about 98 in 100 later-window traces. [DIR006, LAG003–LAG005, REL007–REL012, REL025–REL030]

- **A04 became rarer and less tied to the block.** It ran in about 31 in 100 traces earlier versus about 14 in 100 later; earlier, roughly half of its appearances were in traces without the A33–A39 block, whereas later it appears almost only alongside the block, before it. Its outgoing transitions redistributed (A16 fell from about 81 to 36 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions, with new targets such as A15 and A48), and when present it sits later in the trace (mean relative position about 0.27 versus 0.40). Only 27 later-window traces contain A04, so these estimates rest on few cases. [OCC001, REL001–REL006, DIR002, POS001, LAG001]

- **Smaller shifts.** A06 and A10 occur somewhat more often (about 3 → 11 and 2–3 → 12 in 100 traces) and receive thin new incoming edges from A14, A15 and A47, based on few observations (5–24 traces). A14's position became more regular (spread 0.07 → 0.01) and slightly earlier; A15 now goes to A16 in about 93 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (was 83) while A15→A04 fell from 17 to 3 in 100; the A47→A48 edge eased from about 99.5 to 91 in 100. [OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR010, POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the either/or routing between two activity groups was replaced by both groups running sequentially in the same cases: earlier, a case executed either the group A33/A35/A36 or the group A37/A38/A39 (each in about half of the cases, never both), while in the later window nearly every case executes A33/A35/A36 first and then A37/A38/A39, with A31 following after the combined block instead of directly after either group. [REL039–REL048, OCC004–OCC009, DIR007–DIR009, LAG006–LAG008, REL031–REL036]

The change is shown directly by relational evidence — cross-group co-occurrence rose from 0 to about 98 in 100 traces with a stable A33/A35/A36-before-A37/A38/A39 order — and it is consistent across occurrence (participation of all six activities roughly doubled to near-universal), redirection of A30 into the first group and of A33/A35/A36 towards A37–A39, and the growth of A31's distance from 1–3 to 4–5 positions. A mere shift in how often existing paths are taken is ruled out because the two groups never shared a trace earlier, and the volume of qualifying items (89 versus about 1 expected for an unchanged process) exceeds what chance sampling produces. [REL039, REL046, OCC004, OCC009, DIR006, DIR007, LAG006, LAG008]

Limitations: the evidence shows co-occurrence and ordering in two finite trace samples, not the underlying model; it cannot establish whether the combined routing applies to every case type or whether the earlier either/or routing still exists outside these windows, and the order within each group remains unfixed (both orders in similar proportions), so the internal sequencing of each group is not determined. [REL037, REL042, REL049]

Other differences: A04 became rarer (about 31 → 14 in 100 traces) and now appears almost only in traces that also contain the A33–A39 block, before it, with its outgoing transitions shifted away from A16 (about 81 → 36 in 100) and a later typical position; these estimates rest on only 27 later-window traces. A06 and A10 occur somewhat more often with thin new incoming edges, based on few observations. Minor shifts include a more regular and slightly earlier position for A14, a higher A15→A16 share with a lower A15→A04 share, and a slightly less dominant A47→A48 edge. [OCC001, REL001–REL006, DIR002, POS001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR010, POS002, POS003]
