### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering constraints between the two blocks changed, with A15, A16, A17, A19 and A20 now always executed before A21, A22, A23, A24 and A25, and the routing fixed around them: A20 is always followed by A21 (earlier, A21 followed A20 in about 97 in 100 traces and about half of A20's outgoing transitions bypassed the block straight to A31), A22 always closes into A31, and A30 always precedes A19. In the later window the behaviour is consistent with one fixed sequence where the earlier window showed a variable order with a frequent A20-to-A31 bypass; no activity was added or dropped, as all occur throughout. [REL001–REL022, DIR001–DIR010]

This reading rests on direct rather than cumulative signs: the order change is shown relationally on every tested pair, the routing change appears directly in the successor profiles, and the position and gap shifts agree; moreover, 93 control-flow items qualified against fewer than 1 expected without a change, so sampling noise cannot explain the pattern as a whole. What the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window samples a different mix of pre-existing case variants rather than a modified process, and transitions with a zero share in the later window (such as A20 to A31) are unobserved rather than proven absent. [DIR001–DIR010, POS001–POS010, REL001–REL022, LAG001–LAG051]
