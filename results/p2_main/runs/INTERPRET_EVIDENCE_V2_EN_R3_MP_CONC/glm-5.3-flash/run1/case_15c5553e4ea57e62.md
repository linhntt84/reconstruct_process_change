### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the process ran in two fixed blocks: in all 200 earlier traces, each of A15, A16, A17, A19 and A20 was completed before each of A21, A22, A23, A24 and A25 (before-order share 100% on all 21 cross-group pairs). In the later window the order varies from case to case: A21 precedes A15/A16/A17 in about 73 in 100 traces and A20 in about 93 in 100; A23, A24 and A25 precede A20 in about 76–84 in 100, while against A15/A16/A17 they appear in each order roughly half the time (about 44–49 in 100). A19 still precedes A23/A24/A25 in about 77–79 in 100. Both activities of every pair still occur together in essentially all traces in both windows, so participation did not change — only the order did. [REL001–REL021]
- The two groups moved toward each other in event positions: A21's mean relative position fell from 0.64 to 0.55, A23/A24/A25 from about 0.69 to about 0.63 and A22 from 0.74 to 0.71, while A15/A16/A17 rose from about 0.56 to about 0.62 and A20 from 0.61 to 0.71; positional spread widened for all ten activities (e.g., A15 from 0.047 to 0.072). [POS001–POS010]
- The old bridge between the groups broke up. Before, A20 was always followed by A21; in the later window about 48 in 100 of A20's observed outgoing transitions go to A31 and only about 7 in 100 to A21, with the rest spread over A22–A25. The first-group activities now also transition directly into the second group (about 28–45 in 100 of their outgoing transitions; before: never), and the second-group activities transition back into the first group (about 31–48 in 100; before: never). [DIR001–DIR010]
- Distances match this picture: gaps from the first group to A23/A24/A25 shortened (e.g., A15→A25 lags moved from mostly 4–5 steps to about 42 in 100 at 1–2 steps), while gaps inside each group lengthened (e.g., A15→A16 from lags 1–2 to up to 5; A23→A25 similarly). The ordered pair A15 then A21 within five steps appears in only 54 of 200 later traces versus all 200 earlier ones, and A20→A22 now typically follows within 1–2 steps instead of exactly 5. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG005, LAG006–LAG008, LAG013–LAG015, LAG020–LAG022, LAG028, LAG039, LAG046]
- A30's successor split: before it was always followed by A19; in the later window about 54 in 100 of its transitions go to A19 and about 46 in 100 to A21. [DIR011, LAG051]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the fixed sequencing between the two activity groups was relaxed in the later window: A21, A22, A23, A24 and A25 are no longer always executed after A15, A16, A17, A19 and A20 — A21 now typically runs before A15/A16/A17 and A20, A23/A24/A25 typically run before A20 and in either order relative to A15/A16/A17 — and A20 now typically flows directly to A31 instead of into the second group. [REL001–REL021, DIR005, POS001–POS010]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the order change is observed on all 21 cross-group pairs with co-occurrence unchanged at 100% in both windows, ruling out a participation explanation; the positional shifts and the shortening of cross-group gaps and lengthening of within-group gaps move in the same direction as the new orders; the successor profiles of A20 and of the second-group activities show the same re-wiring; and 93 of 145 tested control-flow units qualified against fewer than 1 expected without change (each with a difference larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits), so the pattern as a whole is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. [REL001–REL021, POS001–POS010, DIR001–DIR011, LAG005, LAG027–LAG031]

An important competing explanation remains: the relation indicators aggregate across cases, so the evidence cannot distinguish genuine per-case order flexibility from a mixture of two trace populations (one keeping the old order, one using a new one). The relaxation is also partial — A19's order relative to A23/A24/A25 is nearly unchanged — and positions and lags use event positions, not clock time, so no timing claim is made. [REL013–REL016, POS001–POS010]

Other differences: A30 now branches to A21 in about 46 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, where before it always went to A19 [DIR011, LAG051]; the final run to A31 lengthened, e.g., A22→A31 within one step in only about 56 in 100 later traces versus all earlier ones [LAG036, LAG040, LAG047]; A03 and A05 are now usually adjacent (lag 1 in about 84 in 100 cases versus about 60 in 100) [LAG001].
