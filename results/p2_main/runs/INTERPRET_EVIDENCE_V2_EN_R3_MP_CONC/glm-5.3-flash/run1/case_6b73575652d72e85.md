### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A01 is executed as a step inserted into existing flows: sequences that previously ran from A03 — and, less uniformly, from A10 and A11 — directly on to A04 now typically pass through A01 first, and A01 in turn leads mainly to A04. Twenty control-flow items qualified against an expectation of about 0.23 without change, so the set of differences is far larger than chance would produce. The hypothesis is shown directly rather than inferred from a single indicator: A01 rises from none of 200 traces to about 94 in 200; A03, A10 and A11 reroute away from A04 towards A01 while about three quarters of A01's outgoing transitions go to A04; the new relations show A03 before A01 and A01 before A04; and existing pair distances lengthen by about one event position, as expected if a step now sits between them. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: A01's placement is not fixed — it also receives small shares from other predecessors, interleaves or is repeated with A04 in about 23 in 100 traces, and several lag items rest on only about 22–26 observations; the agreeing indicators partly describe the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that A01 belonged to a variant already present below detection in the earlier window, although 0 versus about 94 of 200 traces makes sampling noise alone implausible.
