### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the middle segment of the process — in which exactly one of A14, A15 or A20 was executed between A22 and A24, either directly after A22 or directly after A23 — is no longer executed in the later window, so that A22 now passes directly to A23 and A23 directly to A24 in every trace. Supporting signs: the participation of all three activities dropped from 28–40 in 100 traces to 0 of 200; the successor profiles of A22 and A23 each collapsed from four observed outgoing transitions to a single one; and all three lag distributions tightened exactly as expected if the intermediate activity were removed (A22→A24 from always 3 steps apart to always 2). The screening context also matters: 28 items qualified where only about 0.4 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the ensemble is far beyond what random re-splits of a stable process produce. Limitations: zero participation only shows the activities were not observed in the later sample, so the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine removal of A14, A15 and A20 from a branch that remains possible but is simply never taken; and because many items describe the same traces, they are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that these activities still occur but were missed by the later sample — unlikely given their earlier frequency of 28–40 in 100 traces, but formally possible.
