### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, cases branch after A26 between two mutually exclusive options: the previous step A27, taken in about 57 in 100 cases (down from every case), or a group of five activities — A32, A33, A38, A39 and A40, executed in no fixed order among themselves — taken in about 44 in 100 cases and not observed at all in the earlier window. Both options are followed by A28, which still runs in every case, and the new group runs after A15/A16 and A26. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR004, REL025, REL026, REL028, REL029, REL030, REL031, REL036]

Supporting signs: the screening flagged 72 control-flow items where fewer than 1 (0.83) would be expected without change, so random re-splits cannot account for the volume [screening]; the appearance of the five activities and the drop of A27 are observed directly with large supports (87–113 traces per activity), the rerouting at A26 is observed on about 200 outgoing transitions in each window, and the exclusivity and placement around A28 are shown relationally; occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators all describe the same picture. Limitations: positions are event positions, not clock times; the many relation items cover overlapping traces and are not independent confirmations; and the evidence cannot show whether the five activities were newly added or existed earlier without ever being executed, since zero participation only means they were not observed. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the earlier window simply failed to sample the new branch as a rare variant, although a path taken in about 44% of cases would be expected to appear in 200 traces.

Other differences: A06 became less frequent (about 24% to about 10% of traces), later in position, with more spread-out successors and a larger gap to A16 — consistent with A06 belonging to the old A27 branch, but that link is not directly established; A10's rise and the complete change of A04's outgoing transitions rest on only 5–23 observations per window and may be sampling noise; A15, A16 and A26 shifted marginally earlier in position.
