### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the five-way choice block (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22 — exactly one of which executes in each case) was relocated from before A23 to the segment between A24 and A29: before, the flow ran A42 → one of the five → A23 → A24 → A25 → A29; in the later window it runs A42 → A23 → A24 → A25 → A29, with the chosen activity inserted between A24 and A25 in about half the cases and between A25 and A29 in the other half. The change is shown directly by four agreeing indicator families — a complete order reversal with A23 and A24 for every block activity, successor rewiring at A42, the five block activities, A24 and A25, step-distance changes on all four observed pairs that match this placement, and a position shift of about 0.26–0.29 of the case length for all five activities. The figures are internally consistent (the 51 in 100 non-direct A24→A25 transitions match the 51 in 100 two-step A24→A25 distances, and likewise 49 in 100 for A25→A29), screening flagged 28 items where about 0.43 would be expected without a change, so chance sampling cannot account for the collection, and the unchanged participation of every involved activity rules out the block becoming optional or mandatory or being replaced [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008].
