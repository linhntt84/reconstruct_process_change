### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a change in resource allocation: in the later window, the work on activities A01, A03, A13, A14, A17 and A22 was handled by a largely different set of resources than in the earlier window, with a group of about eight holders (largest: User_37) falling from roughly half of each activity's assignments to a few in 100, and a new group led by User_3 and User_42 rising from almost none to roughly 40–50 in 100.
