### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, about 52 in 100 traces (104 of 200) contained a fixed three-step block executed in the order A05, then A06, then A04, at mean positions 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75; in the remaining ~48 in 100 traces none of these activities appeared. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002]
- In the later window, A06 was not observed in any trace (0 of 200), while A05 and A04 still ran together in about 50 in 100 traces (101 of 200). [OCC001, POS003, REL001, REL002]
- A05's observed successor changed completely: before, it was always followed by A06; in the later window, it was always followed by A04. [DIR001]
- The distance from A05 to A04 shrank from two steps to one: before, A04 followed A05 two positions later; in the later window, it immediately follows. [LAG001]
- Before, A06 always co-occurred with A05 and with A04 and preceded A04; in the later window, A05 and A04 occur only without A06 (each alone in about 50 in 100 traces, co-occurrence zero). [REL001, REL002]
- The mean positions shifted slightly: A04 from 0.75 to about 0.67 and A05 from 0.25 to about 0.33, consistent with a middle step disappearing rather than a reordering. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the step A06 is no longer executed between A05 and A04: the earlier sequence A05 → A06 → A04, which ran in about half of the traces, appears replaced by a direct A05 → A04 sequence, with A06 unobserved in the later sample. [OCC001, DIR001, LAG001, REL001, REL002]

The change is shown directly and consistently: A05's outgoing transition flipped from always A06 to always A04, the A05–A04 distance shrank from two steps to one, A06's edge to A04 disappeared along with A06 itself, and A06's co-occurrence with A05 and A04 dropped from about 52 in 100 traces to zero. The overall participation of the block stayed roughly stable (about 52% of traces before versus about 50.5% after), so this is a removal of the middle step rather than a change in how often the block runs. Nine control-flow items qualified against about 0.07 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what random re-splitting produces. [DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, LAG001, REL001, REL002]

Limitations: all items describe one underlying difference and are not independent confirmations, and zero participation only means A06 was not observed in the later window — the evidence cannot rule out that A06 still occurs in variants not captured by this sample. [OCC001]

Other differences: none beyond this change; the small position shifts of A05 and A04 follow from the middle step disappearing. [POS001, POS002]
