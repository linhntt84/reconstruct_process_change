### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A27, A36, A37, A42, A43 and A44 each ran in every one of the 200 cases (about once per case). In the later window they appear in only about 46–56 in 100 cases (A27 about 46, the others about 55–56), and their mean occurrences per case fall in step (about 1.0 → 0.46–0.57), so they are skipped rather than repeated differently. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009]
- In the later window the block also appears later within the case: mean relative event position rose from about 0.41 to about 0.57 for A36–A44, while A26 (about 0.62 → 0.53) and A28 (about 0.49 → 0.39) moved earlier. [POS003, POS005, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010, POS011]
- The order around the block reversed. Earlier, A36–A44 ran before A26 in about 98–99 in 100 joint cases and before A28 in about 97–99 in 100; in the later window A26 precedes the block in about 53 in 100 cases and A28 precedes it in about 53 in 100. [REL042, REL043, REL044, REL045, REL046, REL057, REL058, REL059, REL060, REL061]

A25 earlier spread its outgoing transitions over A36, A37, A42, A43 and A44 (about 17–23% each); in the later window about 98 in 100 of its outgoing transitions go to A28, and the direct adjacencies from A25 into the block fell from about 197–199 traces to 1–2. The block's exit moved from A28 (about 17–26% of outgoing transitions earlier) to A31 (about 11–25% later), with block-to-A28 adjacencies down to about 1 trace and A44-to-A31 up from 1 to about 104 traces. - A26 now routes onward to several activities instead of almost always A27 (about 97% → 43% of its outgoing transitions). The block also lost internal cohesion: pairwise co-occurrence among A36–A44 fell from 1.0 to about 0.55, and A31 — still present in every case — now appears without the block in about 44 in 100 cases, whereas earlier the two always coincided with the block first. A14, A15 and A25, which earlier always coincided with the block members, now appear without them in about 44–54 in 100 cases. - A10 became far less common and later: participation fell from about 77 to about 25 in 100 cases and mean position moved from about 0.16 to 0.42; A14 now routes to A15 in about 78% of its outgoing transitions (earlier A10 took about 74%), and the A14-to-A10 adjacency fell from about 149 to 30 traces. - A02 and A08 appear in roughly three to four times as many cases as before (about 8% → 29% and 6% → 24%). [OCC001, OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the previously mandatory block A36, A37, A42, A43 and A44 (together with A27) can be skipped — it ran in every earlier case but in only about half of later cases — and that the flow around it was rewired: A25 now leads directly to A28 instead of into the block, A26 and A28 now run before the block rather than after it, and the block, when executed, flows into A31 instead of A28. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL042, REL057, DIR005, DIR007, LAG011, LAG016, LAG017, LAG022]

The change is shown directly and consistently: participation rests on all 200 traces per window (per-activity support 200 vs 92–112), the order reversal is visible in the relational profiles, the rewiring in the successor profiles, and the lost and gained adjacencies match them; with 152 qualifying items against about 1.5 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the later window mixes two variants — one with and one without the block — rather than a single modified flow; the reversed order around the block was essentially absent earlier (A26 before the block in 0 of 200 cases), which speaks against a pure re-weighting of previously seen behaviour, but a rare variant missed by 200-case samples cannot be excluded. [REL042, DIR005, LAG011]

Other differences: A10 became much rarer (about 77 → 25 in 100 cases), appears later, and A14 now typically leads to A15 instead of A10; A02 and A08 occur in about three to four times as many cases, though these rest on only about 12–16 earlier traces. The evidence does not connect these shifts to the block hypothesis. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, DIR003, LAG004]
