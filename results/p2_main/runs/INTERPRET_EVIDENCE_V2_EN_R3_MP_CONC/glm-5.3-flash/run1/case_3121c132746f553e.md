### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A38, A14, A15 and A23 each occurred exactly once in all 200 traces, always in the order A38 → A14 → A15 → A23; about 50 in 100 traces also ran A20 followed by A21 between A14 and A15, while the other half ran A22 instead, and A20/A21 never occurred together with A22. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL010, REL012, REL013]
- In the later window, A38 and A23 still occur in every trace, but A14 and A15 appear in only about 53 in 100 traces, A20 and A21 in about 29 in 100, and A22 in about 24 in 100 — each down by roughly 47–48 in 100 traces from the earlier window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL005, REL006]
- The drops are explained by a new direct connection: before, every outgoing transition of A38 led to A14, while in the later window about 48 in 100 of A38's outgoing transitions lead straight to A23; accordingly the A38→A23 distance, previously always 4–5 steps, is now 1 step in about 48 in 100 traces, matching the share of traces in which neither A14 nor A15 (and neither member of the alternative block) appears. [DIR001, LAG001, REL001, REL013]
- When the middle segment does run in the later window, its internal shape is unchanged: A14 still precedes A15, A20 still precedes A21, A22 remains an alternative to the A20/A21 pair, the segment still sits between A38 and A23, and the branch choice stays roughly even (about 55 vs 45 among segment traces, versus about 50 vs 50 before). [REL001, REL002, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL019]
- Two small positional shifts accompany this: A23's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (about 0.44 to 0.41) and A38's slightly later (about 0.30 to 0.32), consistent with traces becoming shorter when the segment is skipped rather than with either activity being relocated. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the segment between A38 and A23 — comprising A14, the alternative block (A20 → A21, or A22 instead) and A15 — is skipped in about 48 in 100 cases, with A38 leading directly to A23, whereas in the earlier window this segment executed in every case. The bypass is shown directly by the new A38→A23 transition and the step-1 A38→A23 distance, and it is quantitatively consistent: the participation of A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 falls by exactly the skipped share while A38 and A23 remain universal, and the internal order of the segment is intact whenever it does run. With 29 qualifying items against roughly 0.45 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the ensemble. [DIR001, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL001, REL012]

Limitations: the many relation items largely restate one underlying behaviour rather than confirming it independently, and the evidence cannot distinguish a newly added skip route within a single process from a shift in the mix of two coexisting variants between the windows. [REL001, REL013, LAG001]

Other differences: the slight rebalancing between the A20/A21 branch and A22 within the segment (about 55 vs 45 after, about 50 vs 50 before) and the small position shifts of A23 and A38 are minor and are plausibly by-products of the skip behaviour rather than separate changes. [REL013, REL019, POS001, POS002]
