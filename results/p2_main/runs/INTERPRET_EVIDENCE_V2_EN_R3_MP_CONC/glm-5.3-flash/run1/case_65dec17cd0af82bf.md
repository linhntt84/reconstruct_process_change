- Two activities that were not observed at all in the earlier window appear in the later one: A02 is present in about 57 in 100 traces (113 of 200) and A01 in about 15 in 100 (30 of 200). [OCC001, OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new concluding stage was added to the process: A02 appears as a new final activity, reached from A16 and A19 — which previously ended traces — and from about half of A09's outgoing transitions, while A01 is a new activity shortly before the end, fed by A10 and A17 and always leading to A16, so that routes such as A10 or A17 → A01 → A16 → A02 now run through the last third of about 15 in 100 traces, and A02 terminates about 57 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, POS002, DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS007, POS008, REL001]

The screening context makes a sampling explanation unlikely: 42 items qualified against roughly 0.44 expected if nothing had changed (all in the control-flow family), far beyond what random re-splits of an unchanged process produce, and the hypothesis rests on direct, mutually consistent occurrence, positional, directional and relational evidence rather than on the number of items; A02's presence in 113 of 200 later traces also rules out a rare variant appearing in one window only. [OCC002, POS002, DIR006, DIR008]

Limitations: the evidence shows only immediate successions and event positions, so it cannot establish which conditions route a trace to the new ending, and it cannot rule out that the later window reflects a different case mix rather than a modified process; the roughly 43 in 100 later traces without A02 are not characterised. [OCC002, REL001]

Other differences: the middle-section restructuring — A09 and A13 occurring in fewer traces, repeating less and sitting at later event positions, A14 sitting earlier and now reached from A07, A03 gaining a direct edge to A09 while its edge to A08 nearly disappeared, and the former co-occurrences of A09 with A14 and A19 and of A13 with A14 largely vanishing — may be part of the same change or an independent difference; the evidence cannot separate the two. [OCC003, OCC004, DIR002, DIR003, POS003, POS005, POS006, REL011, REL012, REL013]
