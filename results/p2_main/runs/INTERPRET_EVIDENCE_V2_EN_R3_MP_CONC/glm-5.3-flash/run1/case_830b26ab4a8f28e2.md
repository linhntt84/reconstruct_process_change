### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was present in every one of the 200 earlier traces — about 3.5 occurrences per trace, recurring within roughly 74 in 100 of those traces — but appears in none of the 200 later traces. [OCC001, POS001]
- A01's outgoing flow disappeared with it: earlier its transitions went to A10 (about 35 in 100), A09 (33), A08 (28) and small shares to A12, A11, A06 and A05; in the later window no outgoing transition from A01 was observed. [DIR001]

- A small further shift: A08–A09 adjacency halved (about 14 in 100 to 7 in 100) with slightly more 4-step gaps. [LAG007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that activity A01 was removed from the process, or is at least no longer executed. Earlier, A01 ran in every case about three to four times and sat at the centre of the flow: A03 and A05 led into it, and A08, A09 and A10 were interleaved with it. In the later window A01 is unobserved, A03 and A05 lead directly to A08, A09 and A10, and the affected activity pairs sit closer together in the trace — a pattern consistent with A01 being cut out of the flow. Since A01 is wholly absent from the later window while A03, A05, A08, A09 and A10 keep their participation, this is a removal with rewired connections, not a shift in how often existing paths are taken. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

Supporting signs: 17 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected by chance among the 52 tested units, so chance alone cannot account for the set; occurrence, direction, relation, position and distance indicators all describe the same underlying difference; and A01's disappearance is measured over all 200 traces in each window, with every item's difference exceeding about 995 in 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, DIR002, REL003, LAG002]

Limitation: zero participation only means A01 was not observed in the later sample, so the evidence cannot rule out that A01 still occurs in a small share of cases; the distance items use event positions, so they show relative ordering rather than clock-time timing. [OCC001, LAG002]

Other differences: the changed spacing between A08 and A09 (adjacency halved, more 4-step gaps), which the evidence does not tie directly to the removal of A01. [LAG007]
