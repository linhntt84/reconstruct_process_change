### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was not observed in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it occurs exactly once in every trace (200 of 200, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, no repetitions). [OCC001]
- In the earlier window, about 78 in 100 of A02's observed outgoing transitions went to A03 and about 22 in 100 to A04; in the later window, about 76 in 100 go to A01 and about 24 in 100 to A04, with no direct A02→A03 transition observed. [DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A01 was inserted as a new, mandatory step between the A02/A04 cycle and A03. In the earlier window, control reached A03 directly from A02 (about 78 in 100 of A02's outgoing transitions) or from A04 (about 22 in 100 of A04's); in the later window, A02 and A04 instead lead to A01 (about 76 and 24 in 100 respectively), and A01 always leads on to A03, appearing exactly once per trace after both A02 and A04. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003]

The redirection is shown directly by the successor profiles of A02 and A04 and by A01's single outgoing edge; the relation items confirm the order A02/A04 before A01 before A03 in every trace; the one-step growth in both measured distances and A02's earlier, more consistent position fit one added step on the exit path, while the A02/A04 cycle itself is essentially unchanged. Eleven items qualified in screening against fewer than 0.1 expected for an unchanged process, so this set of differences is not what random re-splits of a stable process typically produce. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: all items describe one underlying trace-level difference and are not independent confirmations; the evidence cannot show whether A01 is genuinely new or an existing step appearing under a new label, and because positions are event indexes, A01's fixed relative position does not establish clock-time placement. [OCC001, POS001]

Other differences: none beyond the consequences of this addition; the transition shares inside the A02/A04 cycle moved by only about two percentage points. [DIR002, DIR003]
