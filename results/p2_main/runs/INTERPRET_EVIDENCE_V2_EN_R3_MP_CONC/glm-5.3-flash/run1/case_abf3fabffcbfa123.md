### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the branch composed of A32, A33 and A38 — previously executed together in roughly half of the cases and reached from A26 in about 48% of its outgoing transitions — is no longer executed, while A27, previously an alternative taken from A26 in about half the cases, now runs in virtually every case between A26 and A28 (A26 → A27 → A28). This is shown directly by directional and relational items with strong support in both windows (A26's outgoing profile rests on about 200 traces per window; the A26/A27 and A27/A28 relations on all 200 per window) and is consistent across occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators, although several of these items describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations. With 62 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected by chance, the set as a whole cannot be explained by random sampling. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the A32/A33/A38 branch still exists but has become extremely rare: it appears in 1 of 200 later traces, and its post-change routing rests on that single trace.
