### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering hypothesis: in the later window, the segment formed by A35, A36, A41, A42 and A43 is executed after A27 and A26, whereas in the before window it was executed before them, with entry and exit points rewired accordingly — A25 now leads to A27, A26 now leads into the group, and the group flows into A30, which previously followed A26 directly. This is shown directly by the order flip between the two segments at full co-occurrence in both windows, and it is consistently echoed by the successor changes at A25, A26 and the group members and by the opposite position shifts of the two segments; with about 65 qualifying control-flow items against roughly one expected for an unchanged process, the ensemble is not what a stable process typically produces. This remains a possibility supported by the evidence, not a confirmed modification. [REL011, REL016, REL018, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS002, POS003, POS004]

Limitations: each window shows essentially a single ordering, so a changed mix of two pre-existing orderings cannot be ruled out; the item count overstates independence because the relational, directional and positional items describe the same underlying behaviour from different angles; and the lag-based corroboration rests on only 1–3 after-observations per pair.
