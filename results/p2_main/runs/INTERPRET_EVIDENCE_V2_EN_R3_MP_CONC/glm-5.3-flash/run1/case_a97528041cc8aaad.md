### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, two groups of activities exchanged their place in the sequence: A19, A21 and A24, which previously ran after the middle activities A25 and A29 and after A20, A22 and A23, now run before them, while A20, A22 and A23 now run after A25 and A29. The signs are direct and consistent across indicator types: near-deterministic order reversals in pairs that still co-occur in essentially every trace (A20 before A25 in about 98–99 in 100 traces earlier versus A25 before A20 in about 97–98 in 100 later), opposite position shifts of the two groups (about 0.40 to 0.65 for A20/A22/A23 and about 0.68 to 0.37 for A19/A21/A24), and matching successor changes on both sides, including A29 and A40 now leading into the other group. Screening supports the overall picture: 87 control-flow items qualified against about 1 expected for an unchanged process, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. Limitations: positions are event positions rather than clock time, so nothing is claimed about calendar timing; the evidence cannot distinguish a reordering of a single flow from a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants; and the order within each group (among A19/A21/A24 and among A20/A22/A23) is not constrained by the observed pairs.
