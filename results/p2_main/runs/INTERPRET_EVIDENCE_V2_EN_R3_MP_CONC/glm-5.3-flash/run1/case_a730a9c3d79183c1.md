- In the later window, six activities became near-universal: A33, A35 and A36 each ran in about 49 of 100 cases before and about 98–99 of 100 after, while A37, A38 and A39 rose from about 51–52 of 100 to about 99 of 100; each runs about once per case where present. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the exclusive choice between two alternative activity groups was removed: before, each case executed either the group A33/A35/A36 (about 49 in 100 cases) or the group A37/A38/A39 (about 51 in 100), never both, whereas in the later window all six activities execute in about 98–99 of 100 cases, with the first group preceding the second in about 95–97 of 100 joint occurrences and the order within each group remaining unfixed. This alternatives-to-joint-execution change is shown directly for every cross-group pair, is consistent across the participation, relation, direction and distance indicators, and the screening context makes chance an implausible explanation of the pattern as a whole (109 qualifying control-flow items against about 1 expected without change). [REL039, REL041, REL045, REL047, REL050, REL052, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG005, LAG008]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the combined block is now strictly mandatory, since about 1–2 of 100 later cases still miss individual block activities, and it cannot distinguish a routing change from a shift in the mix of case types entering the process; the occurrence, position and relation items for the same activities describe the same traces, so they corroborate rather than independently confirm the change. [OCC005, OCC008, REL039, REL050]

Other differences: A04's decline — participation about 59 → 26 of 100 cases, its dominant link to A15 falling from about 90% to about 40% of its outgoing transitions, and a later position in the case — is a separate participation change not covered by the block hypothesis; the rises of A02, A08 and A10 also stand apart but rest on few observations. [OCC002, DIR002, DIR004, POS001, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, DIR009, DIR010]
