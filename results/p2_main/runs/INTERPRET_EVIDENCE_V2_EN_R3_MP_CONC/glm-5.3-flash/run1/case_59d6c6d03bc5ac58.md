### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the paths leading into A02 were restructured. In the earlier window, traces reached A02 either through A01 (about half of traces, with A01 immediately before A02) or through A04 (the other half, with A04 immediately before A02), the two being mutually exclusive alternatives. In the later window, A01 is no longer observed — in the traces without A04, A02 now appears at the earliest position — and A04 no longer leads directly to A02: the newly observed A05 always sits between them, giving the sequence A04 → A05 → A02 in about 46 in 100 traces. Chance is not a plausible explanation for the set as a whole: 15 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.09 would be expected without a change, and every item's difference is larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits.
