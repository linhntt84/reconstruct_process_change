### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the group of activities A35, A36 and A41, which previously executed after A26 and A27, now executes before them. In the earlier window the flow ran roughly A25 → A27 → {A35, A36, A41} → A30, with A26 also running before the group; in the later window A25 feeds into the group, the group runs ahead of both A26 and A27, the group exits to A27 instead of A30, and A26 proceeds directly to A30. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG001, LAG003]

Supporting signs: the order reversal is near-complete on all six cross-group pairs while co-occurrence stays at 100 in 100, so participation and repetition are unchanged; the position shifts of all five activities point in the matching directions; the successor changes at A25, A26 and the group fit the new order; and 26 control-flow items qualified against about 0.6 expected if nothing had changed, far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces.
