### Observed differences

- Four activities became far more frequent: A31, A32 and A37 each ran in about 46 in 100 cases in the earlier window and in about 199–200 of 200 cases in the later window, and A22 rose from about 54 in 100 cases to all 200. None of the four repeats within a case in either window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A20's routing changed completely: earlier, every observed outgoing transition of A20 went to A23; in the later window its transitions go to A31, A32 or A37 in roughly equal shares (about 32–36 in 100 each), with A23 directly after A20 almost never observed. [DIR001]
- A21's routing narrowed: earlier, about 54 in 100 of A21's observed outgoing transitions went to A22 and the rest went to A31, A32 or A37; in the later window, all of them go to A22. [DIR002]
- The exits of A31, A32 and A37 changed: earlier, each led to A26 in roughly 29–36 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (otherwise to another group member); in the later window, each leads to A23 in about 31–37 in 100 of transitions and to another group member otherwise, with no observed transitions to A26. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- A22 and the group A31/A32/A37 were alternatives earlier — they never occurred in the same case (A22 alone in about 54 in 100 cases, the group alone in about 46) — whereas in the later window they occur together in about 99.5 in 100 cases, with the group before A22. [REL011, REL012, REL013]
- The group's position relative to A23 and A21 flipped: earlier, A23 preceded the group and A21 preceded the group (in the roughly 46 in 100 cases containing both); in the later window, the group precedes A23 and precedes A21 in about 99.5 in 100 cases. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016]
- A22's relationships became universal while keeping their order: earlier, A20, A21 and A23 each co-occurred with A22 in about 54 in 100 cases (A22 absent otherwise); in the later window each co-occurs with A22 in every case, with A23 and A21 before A22 and A22 before A26 as before. [REL001, REL005, REL009, REL010]
- Event distances changed: A23 followed A20 one step later in all earlier cases but about four steps later in about 99.5 in 100 later cases, as if three activities were inserted between them; A26 followed A21 two steps later in about 54 in 100 earlier cases and four steps later in about 46, but uniformly two steps later in the later window. [LAG001, LAG002]
- The group's mean event position moved from about 0.59 to about 0.40 of the trace, while A21, A22, A23 and A26 shifted only slightly later (about 0.02–0.06); A26 remains in every case in both windows. [POS006, POS007, POS008, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- Within the group, the order of A31, A32 and A37 stayed unfixed in both windows, each pair occurring in both orders in similar shares. [REL020, REL021, REL022]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch after A21 was removed and the three-activity group was relocated and made mandatory. In the earlier window, a case ran A20, then A23 immediately, then A21, which led either to A22 and then A26 (about 54 in 100 cases) or to the group A31, A32, A37 in varying order and then A26 (about 46 in 100). In the later window, essentially every case runs A20, then the group A31/A32/A37 (in varying order), then A23, while A21 always leads to A22 and A26 is always reached through A22 — the group became a mandatory block between A20 and A23, and A22 became mandatory. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL011, REL012, REL013, LAG001, LAG002]

The hypothesis rests on direct relational and routing evidence, not on frequencies alone: the group and A22 went from never co-occurring to always co-occurring in a fixed order, the successor profiles of A20, A21 and the group members changed as described, and the A20-to-A23 distance grew from one to four steps. The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process produces (41 control-flow items against about 0.49 expected by chance), though many items describe the same underlying restructuring and are not independent confirmations. [REL011, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, OCC002]

Limitations: direct successor observations exist only for A20, A21, A31, A32 and A37, so links such as A23 before A21 and A22 before A26 are inferred from order and distance rather than observed edges; and the later window cannot show whether the old alternative path still exists but was simply not taken in any of the 200 cases, which would look identical to a removed branch. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL009, REL010, LAG002]

Other differences: A20's own mean event position moved slightly earlier, from about 0.36 to about 0.34 of the trace, consistent with the disappearance of the shorter variant rather than an independent relocation. [POS001]
