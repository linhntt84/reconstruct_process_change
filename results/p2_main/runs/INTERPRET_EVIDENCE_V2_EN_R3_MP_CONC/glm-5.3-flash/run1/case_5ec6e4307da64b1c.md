### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a five-way exclusive choice — A19, A20, A25, A26 or A27, one per trace — is inserted into the segment between A29 and A31: each of the five runs in about 17–23 in 100 later traces after being unobserved in the earlier window, always after A29 and before A31, positioned either directly between A29 and A30 or between A30 and A31 in roughly equal shares, whereas earlier A29, A30 and A31 followed one another directly.
