### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the cyclic segment around A05, A09, A08, A13, A04, A16 and A01 was rerouted and that the previously unobserved A17 was inserted into it. In the earlier window the cycle ran A05 → A01 → A09 → A08 → A13/A04 → back to A05, with A16 looping into A05; in the later window A01 neither receives from A05 nor passes to A09, A09 feeds directly into A05, A05 continues to A04/A13, and the loop closes through A08 → A16/A17 → A09. A01 now sits at the earliest position in its traces, and A05's within-trace repetitions have largely stopped. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS002, OCC003, OCC004]

The reading is direct and consistent: specific successor edges appear and disappear with substantial backing (for example, 104 observed outgoing transitions of A08 later split between A16 and A17, and 51 transitions from A17 to A09), A01's co-occurrence with the loop activities collapsed from every containing trace to none while A17 joined it, and the position and distance indicators agree with the new edge set rather than with a mere shift in how often existing paths are taken. Screening also qualified 42 items against fewer than 0.4 expected if nothing had changed, and each item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits, so the ensemble is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [DIR004, DIR008, REL001, REL008, POS001, LAG008]

Limitations: the later-window profile for A01 shows no outgoing transitions, so the evidence does not show what, if anything, follows A01 now; A05's new outgoing profile rests on only 26 observed transitions (versus 136 earlier); and a shift in the mix of process variants between the windows, rather than a rewiring of one process, is a competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out. Several items also describe the same rewiring, so the agreeing indicators are not independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR003, POS001]

Other differences: A02 and A03 each occur in a smaller share of traces in the later window (about 50→38 in 100 and 29→16 in 100); the evidence does not connect them to the rerouted segment. [OCC001, OCC002]
