### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that resource allocation changed across seven activities. On A01, A03, A13, A14, A17 and A22, assignment shares moved from one group of resources to another: User_104's share roughly halved, User_67's share grew on every one of them, and User_77 took a regular share after being absent from the leading shares; on A07, assignments shifted almost entirely to a different set of resources. Supporting signs: the resource family yielded 7 qualifying items against fewer than 0.01 expected without change, all items agree in direction on every resource they share, the profiles rest on 118–280 observations per window, and no control-flow or timing item qualified, so the difference appears confined to allocation rather than to structure or timing. [ASSIGN001, ASSIGN002, ASSIGN003, ASSIGN004, ASSIGN005, ASSIGN006, ASSIGN007]

Limitations: A13 and A14 show identical profiles and are not independent confirmations; the per-activity shares cannot show whether the rising and falling resources exchanged work on the same cases; and a changed mix of cases reaching these activities, rather than a change in allocation itself, cannot be ruled out, although the absence of qualifying control-flow differences argues against it.
