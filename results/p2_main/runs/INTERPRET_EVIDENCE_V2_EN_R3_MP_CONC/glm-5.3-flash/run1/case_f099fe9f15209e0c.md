- Activity A02 was executed exactly once in every trace in the earlier window (100% participation, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, typically within the first fifth of the trace); in the later window it does not appear at all (0% participation, 0 of 200 traces). [OCC001, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 is no longer executed in the later window: it was mandatory in the earlier window, appearing exactly once and early in all 200 traces, and is absent from all 200 later traces. Since A02 is entirely unobserved afterwards, this is a participation change (an activity dropped or now always skipped in the observed traces), not a redistribution of shares between existing paths. [OCC001, POS001]

Supporting signs: eleven control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.15 expected by chance alone, far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces; the disappearance is directly measured on the occurrence and position indicators and is consistent across every relation — A01, A03, A04 and A05 each flipped from always co-occurring with A02 to never co-occurring with it, and A03's routing to A02 fell from about 38 in 100 outgoing transitions to none. The agreeing relational items describe one underlying behaviour rather than independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]

Limitations: zero participation only means A02 was not observed in the later window; with 200 traces a very rare residual occurrence of A02 cannot be excluded, so complete removal and always-skip in the sampled traces cannot be distinguished. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window sampled only traces of an A02-free variant while A02 persists in variants outside the sample. [OCC001, REL001]

Other differences: the earlier positions of A03 and A04 and the shorter A03→A04 gaps are consistent with knock-on effects of losing an early, mandatory activity, but the evidence cannot confirm they are consequences rather than independent positional shifts. [POS002, POS003, LAG001]
