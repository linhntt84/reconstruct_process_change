### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, each of the activities A25, A26, A31, A32 and A33 occurred exactly once in every trace (mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, participation 100 in 100 traces, never repeated within a trace). In the later window, each occurs about 3 times per trace on average and repeats within a trace in about 67 in 100 traces, while participation remains 100 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- The successor of A33 changed: in the earlier window A33 was always followed by A20; in the later window about 67 in 100 of A33's observed outgoing transitions lead to A25 and only about 33 in 100 to A20 (based on roughly 612 outgoing transitions). [DIR001]
- Pairs among these five activities still co-occur in every trace, but their relationship changed: before, each pair appeared in one fixed order (or, for A26/A31, A26/A32 and A31/A32, in both orders in roughly equal shares); in the later window, about 67 in 100 traces show each pair as interleaved or repeated, with fixed A-before-B shares falling from 100 in 100 to about 34 in 100 (and from about 50 to about 16 in 100 for the previously mixed pairs). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]
- Distances between consecutive occurrences of pairs such as A26→A31 were 1–2 steps apart in essentially all observed cases before; in the later window only about 12 in 100 are 1 step apart, and lags of 3–5 steps, previously absent, now occur. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]
- The event positions of all five activities spread out; for example, A25's median position moves from about 0.36 to about 0.41 of the trace and its spread roughly doubles (standard deviation from about 0.05 to about 0.14). These are event positions, not clock times. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- The elapsed time between consecutive completion events roughly doubles for two activity pairs: A09→A10 goes from a median of about 1.0 hours to about 2.0 hours (quarter quantile from about 0.85 to about 1.77), and A11→A10 from a mean of about 1.0 to about 2.0 hours, with similar observation counts in both windows. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the segment formed by A25, A26, A31, A32 and A33 — previously executed exactly once per trace, with A25 first, A33 last, and A26/A31/A32 in unfixed order between them — is repeated about three times per trace: A33, which previously always led on to A20, now returns to A25 in about two-thirds of its outgoing transitions, and each activity in the segment repeats within about two-thirds of traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001]

The support is direct and consistent: repetition is measured directly on the activities (recurrence support rising from 0 to about 0.67 with full participation in both windows), the loop-back transition A33→A25 is observed directly in the routing profile, and all ten activity pairs shift to interleaved-or-repeated at the same share of about 67 in 100, corroborated by the widening positions and redistributed lags. The volume of control-flow items (27 against roughly 0.44 expected by chance for this family) and the uniform empirical strength of about 0.995 with 200-of-200 trace support make chance sampling or a rare variant implausible as an explanation. [OCC001, OCC005, DIR001, REL001, REL010, POS001, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence shows that the segment repeats but cannot pin down the exact number of repetitions per repeating trace, and it cannot establish whether the five activities always cycle together as one block — the internal order of A26, A31 and A32 was already unfixed before the change, and the observed interleaving is consistent with, but does not prove, a single repeatedly executed block. The evidence also cannot show whether the additional repetitions displaced other behaviour elsewhere in the trace. [REL005, REL006, REL008, OCC001]

Other differences: A timing change stands on its own: the elapsed time between consecutive completion events consistently doubled, from about 1 hour to about 2 hours across the whole distribution, for A09→A10 and A11→A10. This is elapsed time between completions, not pure waiting or service time, and because it concerns activities outside the repeated segment, the evidence provides no way to link it to the repetition change. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
