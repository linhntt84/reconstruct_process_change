### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, exactly one of three activities — A14, A15 or A20 — is executed between A36 and A21: A36 leads to one of them (A14 in about 37 in 100 cases, A20 in about 34, A15 in about 29, with a residual direct A36→A21 in about 1 in 100), and that activity is always followed by A21; before, A36 led directly to A21 in every trace. The three behave as mutually exclusive alternatives, each executed at most once per trace. [DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL005, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

The number of qualifying items (21, against roughly 0.37 expected by chance among the 74 tested units) is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces, and the items do not point in different directions: the routing change at A36, the exclusive co-occurrence pattern, the order relations and the lag change from one to two steps all describe one and the same structure, and the three choice shares sum to about 99 in 100 traces. [DIR004, LAG001, REL003, REL004, REL001]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that A14, A15 and A20 were not executed there, so the evidence cannot rule out that these paths existed before but were never sampled in 200 traces and only later became frequent. The specific choice shares (about 37/34/29) also rest on a single sample of 200 traces, so their stability over time is not established. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: A21's relative position shifted slightly later (mean about 0.37 to about 0.39, spread slightly narrower); this follows directly from one activity now being executed between A36 and A21 and is not an independent change. [POS004]
