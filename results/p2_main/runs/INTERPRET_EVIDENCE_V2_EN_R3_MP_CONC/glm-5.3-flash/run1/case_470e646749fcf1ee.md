### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new segment of activities now runs between A03 and A04 in roughly half of the later executions: A03 frequently passes to A02, which either continues to A13 and then A04, or first loops through A12 back to A02 before reaching A13 and A04. A02, A12 and A13 were not observed in the earlier window, the direct A03 → A04 route that carried about 46 in 100 of A03's outgoing transitions has almost disappeared, and A04 now takes place later in the trace. The control-flow family produced 54 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces, and occurrence, routing, ordering and position indicators all describe the same structure. [OCC002, DIR003, DIR002, DIR007, REL017, POS003, LAG001]

The items are not independent confirmations, since occurrence, direction, position and several relation items summarise the same traces. The evidence also cannot show whether the new segment runs in all cases or only in a subset (for example particular case types), and it cannot rule out that the two windows capture different case mixes rather than a modified single flow. [OCC002, DIR002, REL013]

Other differences: A08's self-loop is replaced by an interleaved A08–A09 exchange in about a quarter of later traces, and A01 is no longer observed while A14 now connects directly to A04 (previously A14 led to A01 and A01 to A04). These fit the same kind of addition and removal of activities but are kept separate from the main hypothesis. [DIR004, DIR005, OCC001, DIR008, LAG004]
