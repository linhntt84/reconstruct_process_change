### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was removed from the process (or is no longer executed in any case), so that the former chain A05 → A01 → A07 is replaced by a direct A05 → A07 step: A01 fell from participation in all 200 earlier traces to none, A05's outgoing flow moved from A01 (about 51 in 100) to A07 (about 49 in 100), the distance from A05 to A07 shrank from typically two steps to one, A01 no longer co-occurs with A05 or A07, and A04 also lost its rare link into A01. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, REL001, REL002] The screening flagged 10 control-flow items where roughly 0.08 would be expected if nothing had changed, and the items point consistently at this one structural difference rather than scattered noise, although they largely reflect a single underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, LAG001] A limitation is that zero participation only shows A01 was not observed in the later window; the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine removal from A01 being renamed or replaced by another activity code.
