### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities ran in every case: A14 in about 35 in 100 traces, A15 in about 30 in 100, and A20 in about 35 in 100, and they never occurred together. In the later window, none of the three appears in any of the 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL003, REL008]
- Before, A36 was always followed by exactly one of these three (about 35% of its outgoing transitions to A20, 30% to A15, 35% to A14), and each of the three in turn led to A21. In the later window, A36 leads directly to A21 in all cases. [DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The distance from A36 to A21 shrank from two steps (one event in between) in essentially all earlier traces to directly adjacent in all later traces. [LAG001]
- The three activities also used to run before A21 (co-occurrence about 30–35 in 100 traces) and before A26; in the later window A21 and A26 still occur in every trace but never together with any of the three. [REL004, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL013, REL014, REL015]
- A21 now sits slightly earlier within traces (mean relative position about 0.39 to 0.37) and A36 slightly later (about 0.32 to 0.33), consistent with traces becoming shorter. [POS004, POS005]
- Before, each of the three activities co-occurred with A19 and preceded it in roughly 15–17 in 100 traces; in the later window A19 occurs without any of them, with its presence rising from about 49 to about 56 in 100 traces. [REL002, REL007, REL012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of three mutually exclusive activities A14, A15 and A20, which sat between A36 and A21 with exactly one of them executed in every earlier case, is no longer executed in the later window, so that A36 now flows directly to A21. This is a removal or bypass of a segment, not a shift of shares between surviving paths, since none of the three is observed afterwards. The change is observed directly (the successor profile of A36 changed from a three-way split to A21 alone, and the A36–A21 lag shortened from two steps to one), it is consistent across all 28 qualifying items, and 28 items against roughly 0.41 expected under no change cannot be explained by sampling; the drop of three activities from 60–70 of 200 traces each to zero also rules out a rare-variant explanation. A limitation is that zero participation only means the activities were not observed: the evidence cannot distinguish a permanent removal from the block still being possible but skipped in every later trace. [DIR004, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL003, REL008]

Other differences: the changed relations of A19 with the three removed activities and the small positional shifts of A21 and A36 are consistent with the same shortening of traces and are best read as consequences of the main hypothesis rather than separate changes.
