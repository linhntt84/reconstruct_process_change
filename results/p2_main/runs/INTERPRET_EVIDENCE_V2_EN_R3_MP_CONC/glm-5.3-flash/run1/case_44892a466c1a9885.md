### Observed differences

- The segment A19 → A20 ran in every trace in the earlier window (200 of 200 traces, with A19 before A20 in about 99 in 100 of them). In the later window it appears in only about 52 in 100 traces and is absent from about 48 in 100; when it does run, A19 still precedes A20. [OCC002, OCC003, REL010]

- The branch that followed shrank in step. Earlier, A25 and A26 ran together (A25 first) in about 50 in 100 traces and A27 alone in the other traces, the two options being mutually exclusive so that exactly one branch ran per trace. In the later window, A25/A26 run together in about 27 in 100 traces and A27 in about 26 in 100, essentially only alongside A19/A20, and in about 48 in 100 traces neither branch runs. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL011, REL013, REL025, REL026, REL031]

- The skip is visible directly at A43: earlier, about 96 in 100 of A43's observed outgoing transitions led to A19; in the later window they split between A19 (about 49 in 100) and A28 (about 46 in 100). The distance from A43 to A28 (in event steps, not clock time) was previously always 4–5 steps (about 46 in 100 at 4, 54 in 100 at 5) and is now a single step in about 48 in 100 cases. A28 itself still occurs in every trace in both windows, and its typical position barely moved (mean about 0.47 vs 0.44 of the trace), nor did A43's (about 0.34 vs 0.35). [DIR005, LAG003, REL014, REL021, POS002, POS003]

- Routing inside the executions that still contain the segment is essentially unchanged: A20 leads to A28 in about 99 in 100 of its outgoing transitions earlier and about 97 in 100 later, and A27 leads to A20 in about 99 vs 96 in 100. [DIR003, DIR004]

- A06 declined and shifted: it appears in about 32 in 100 traces earlier versus about 10 in 100 later; its median event position moved from near the end of the trace (about 0.97) to mid-trace (about 0.41); its outgoing transitions, previously dominated by A49 (about 85 in 100), are now spread, led by A47 (about 24 in 100) and A16 (about 19 in 100) with A49 down to about 5 in 100; and A48 now goes directly to A49 in about 97 in 100 of its transitions (about 71 in 100 earlier), the A48 → A06 edge having nearly disappeared. These figures rest on few later-window observations (about 19–21 traces or transitions; the A06–A49 step-distance comparison on only 6 pairs). [OCC001, POS001, DIR002, DIR007, LAG001, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the segment consisting of A19 → A20 together with the branch that followed it (either A25 → A26 or A27) is skipped in about half of the executions: earlier, every trace contained A19 → A20 followed by exactly one of the two branch options, whereas later about 48 in 100 traces contain none of these activities, and in those cases A43 leads directly to A28. Supporting signs: the participation drops are large (100% to about 52% for A19 and A20; about 50% to about 26% for each branch activity), the bypass is shown directly by A43's successor split and the collapsed A43–A28 distance, the preserved internal order and within-block routing indicate a bypass rather than a reordering or swap of activities, and the pattern is consistent across occurrence, relation, direction and lag indicators. Chance is also an unlikely explanation overall: 59 control-flow items qualified where about 0.85 would be expected for an unchanged process. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window is a mixture containing a case variant that never executed this segment (for example a shorter case type) rather than the same cases having become able to skip it; the finite window sizes also limit precision. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL010, REL014, REL026, DIR005, LAG003]

Other differences: A06 declined (about 32 in 100 traces to about 10 in 100), moved from the end to the middle of traces, and its successors changed from mostly A49 to a spread led by A47 and A16, with A48 → A49 now almost always immediate; this may be a side effect of the same restructuring or a separate difference, and with only about 19–21 later-window observations behind it the evidence cannot distinguish these. A04's outgoing transitions also changed completely, but only 11 were observed per window, so this carries little weight. [OCC001, POS001, DIR002, DIR007, LAG001, LAG004, REL001, REL006, DIR001]
