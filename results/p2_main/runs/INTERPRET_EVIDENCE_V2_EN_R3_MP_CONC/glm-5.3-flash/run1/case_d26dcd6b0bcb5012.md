### Observed differences

- A middle block of activities that was previously optional became near-universal. In the earlier window, A19 and A20 each appeared in about 53 in 100 traces, A25 and A26 in about 24 in 100, and A27 in about 30 in 100; in the later window, A19 and A20 appear in about 99 in 100 traces, A25 and A26 in about 51 in 100, and A27 in about 49 in 100. About 47 in 100 earlier traces contained none of A19/A20, versus about 1 in 100 later traces. All these activities still occur at most once per trace in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL001]

- The shortcut around this block all but disappeared. Earlier, about 45 in 100 of A43's outgoing transitions led directly to A28 and about 49 in 100 led to A19; in the later window, about 90 in 100 lead to A19 and only about 1 in 100 directly to A28. Consistently, A28 immediately followed A43 (distance 1 step) in about 45 in 100 earlier traces but in only about 1 in 100 later traces, with distances of 4–5 steps now covering about 99 in 100. [DIR002, LAG001]

- The ordering inside the segment is the same in both windows: A43 precedes A19, A19 precedes A20, and A20 precedes A28; within the block, A25 precedes A26, and A25/A26 remain mutually exclusive with A27 (co-occurrence at most about 1–2 in 100 in both windows). The difference is how often this path is taken, not a new or reordered structure. [REL001, REL005, REL006, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL016]

- Two small positional shifts accompany the above: A43's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (about 0.36 to 0.33 of the trace) and A28's slightly later (about 0.45 to 0.46). A27's successor profile is essentially unchanged, with A27 → A20 in about 93% versus 99% of its outgoing transitions. [POS001, POS002, DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment A19 → (A25 → A26, or alternatively A27) → A20, located between A43 and A28, changed from being executed in roughly half of the cases to being executed in nearly every case. In the earlier window, about 45–47 in 100 traces skipped the whole segment by going directly from A43 to A28; in the later window only about 1 in 100 do, and A43 now leads into A19 in about 90 in 100 of its outgoing transitions. The either-or choice between A25 → A26 and A27 is preserved but is now made in almost every trace instead of about half of them. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR002, LAG001, REL013]

Supporting signs: the participation shifts are large (about 45 percentage points for A19/A20 and 25–27 points for A25/A26/A27) and each profile rests on roughly 100–200 traces per window; the routing change at A43 and the A43–A28 distance shift show the mechanism directly rather than relying on frequencies alone; 28 items qualified against roughly 0.7 expected by chance in a stable process; and the orderings are stable across windows, so this reads as a shift in how often the existing path is taken rather than a restructuring. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR002, LAG001, REL001, REL012]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply contain different mixes of case types, so that the shift reflects which path the sampled cases follow rather than a modified process; and with the direct A43 → A28 route at about 1 in 100, it cannot be determined whether that route was removed or only rarely taken. [DIR002, LAG001]

Other differences: only the slight relative-position shifts of A43 and A28, which follow from the segment now executing in almost every trace; no further independent differences appear in the evidence. [POS001, POS002]
