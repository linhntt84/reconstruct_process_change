### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was absent from the earlier window — no occurrence in any of the 200 traces — while in the later window it occurs exactly once in every trace (200 of 200, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace). [OCC001]
- A03 appears in all traces in both windows, but its behaviour changed: in the earlier window it was never followed by another activity (no observed outgoing transitions), whereas in the later window every observed outgoing transition of A03 leads to A01. [DIR001]
- The relationship between the two activities flipped from "A03 without A01" (A03 alone in 100% of earlier traces, never co-occurring) to "always together in a fixed order" (both present and A03 before A01 in 100% of later traces). [REL001]
- Positions moved accordingly: A01 sits at one fixed, late position in the later window (mean position 1.0, no spread), and A03 shifted slightly earlier, from a constant position of 1.0 to a mean of about 0.86. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window every case gained a new final step: A01, executed exactly once, immediately after A03. A01 went from unobserved to mandatory (0 of 200 traces to 200 of 200), A03 — previously terminal in the observed traces — now always leads directly to A01, and A03 precedes A01 in every later trace. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001] The positional items fit the same picture: A01 occupies a single fixed position while A03 moved slightly earlier (mean about 0.86 versus 1.0). [POS001, POS002] This is unlikely to be a sampling artefact: 5 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.09 would be expected by chance in an unchanged process, and each difference is larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, POS002, REL001]

Limitations: the five items largely describe one and the same underlying trace change rather than independent confirmations. Two caveats remain: zero participation in the earlier window strictly means only that A01 was not observed there, so the evidence cannot rule out that A01 already existed but was never triggered in the earlier sample; and the empty outgoing profile of A03 earlier shows that no successor was observed, not that A03 had none in the process. [OCC001, DIR001]

Other differences: none — all items reflect the same single behavioural difference involving A01 and A03. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, POS002, REL001]
