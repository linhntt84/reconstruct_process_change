### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative branch formed by A19, A20 and A25 between A41 and A26 is no longer executed in the later window: A41, which previously handed over to exactly one of the three in roughly equal shares, now leads directly to A26, and the three activities themselves are almost entirely unobserved. The change is shown directly and consistently across indicator families — the participation collapse, the redirection of A41's outgoing transitions, the loss of every co-occurrence with partner activities, and the A41–A26 distance shrinking from typically two steps to one, which fits a bypass rather than a mere re-weighting of the old paths. The volume of qualifying control-flow items (36 against fewer than 1 expected for an unchanged process) also makes a chance explanation of the ensemble implausible. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR001, DIR003, LAG002, REL004, REL010, REL019]

Limitations: zero participation strictly means only that the activities were not observed, so the evidence cannot distinguish a removed branch from one that still exists but is chosen in almost no case; and the single later trace in which A20 continues to A30 instead of A26 rests on one observation and could reflect a residual rerouted path. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002]

Other differences: A24–A31 pairs occur one step apart more often than before, A14, A26 and A41 show small positional shifts within traces, and A12 appears in somewhat more traces; these are not part of the main hypothesis. [LAG001, POS001, POS005, POS006, REL001, REL002, REL003]
