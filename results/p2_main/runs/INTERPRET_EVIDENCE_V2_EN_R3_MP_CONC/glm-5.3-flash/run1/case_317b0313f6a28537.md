### Observed differences

- The activities A19, A20 and A25 moved from late to early in the case flow. In the earlier window each of them ran in roughly 3 in 10 traces at a mean relative position of about 0.61 (about 61% through the case's events); in the later window they still run in roughly 3 in 10 traces, but at a mean position of about 0.32–0.34. [POS001, POS002, POS003]

- Their order relative to A26 and A27 reversed completely. Earlier, whenever A19 co-occurred with A26 (about 39 in 100 traces), A26 came first in about 38 of those cases and A19 never came first; in the later window A19 comes first in about 36 in 100 and A26 first in essentially none. The same flip holds for A20 and A25 against A26 and A27 (about 30–35 in 100 traces changing from "A26/A27 first" to "A19/A20/A25 first"). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

- Their exits were rewired. Earlier, A19, A20 and A25 split their outgoing transitions roughly half and half between A28 and A32 (about 43–56% each); in the later window about 88–94% of their observed transitions go to A26. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

- A45 now feeds this block. Earlier, about 85 in 100 of A45's outgoing transitions went directly to A26; in the later window about 27–29% each go to A19, A20 and A25 (about 85% together), and the A45→A26 event gap stretched from one step to two–three steps. [DIR006, LAG007]

- The late segment became a tight chain. A27 now leads to A28 in about 98 in 100 observed transitions (earlier about 47%, with the rest split over A19/A20/A25), and A28 leads to A32 in about 96 in 100 (earlier about 50%). Accordingly, the A27→A28 and A28→A32 gaps collapsed to a single step (from roughly half of cases at one–two steps), and A27→A32 shortened from three to two steps. [DIR004, DIR005, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

- A26, A27 and A28 kept their positions: their mean relative positions shifted only by about 0.02–0.03 (for example A26 from 0.33 to 0.36). [POS004, POS005, POS006]

- The near sequences A19→A32, A25→A28 and A25→A32 within five steps, previously seen in about 33–76 traces, are now almost unobserved (1–3 traces); the later-window lag values for these pairs rest on those very few observations. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

- A15 still occurs in every trace, but repeats less: mean occurrences per trace fell from about 1.9 to about 1.6, and within-trace recurrence from about 47 to 38 in 100 traces. [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of activities A19, A20 and A25 was relocated from the late stage of the case (after A26/A27, leading into A28/A32) to the early stage (after A45, before A26, leading into A26). This is shown directly and consistently across the control-flow families: the pairwise order against A26/A27 flipped from one fixed order to the other [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]; the mean event position of the three activities moved from about 0.61 to about 0.32 while A26, A27 and A28 stayed within about 0.03 of their earlier positions [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]; and the successor profiles changed wholesale — A19/A20/A25 now lead to A26, and A45 leads into A19/A20/A25 instead of A26 [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006]. The collapse of A27 and A28 to near-deterministic single-successor chains and the widened A45→A26 gap match the same rewiring [DIR004, DIR005, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]. The volume of findings also exceeds what chance would produce: 26 control-flow items qualified where about 0.6 would be expected in an unchanged process. Limitations: the later-window lag distributions for A19–A32 and A25–A28/A32 rest on only 1–3 observations, and the block still runs in only about a third of traces in both windows, so the evidence cannot show whether the same kinds of cases now run the block early.

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a change in the case population rather than in the flow — for example, cases that run the block early replacing cases that ran it late — since the two windows are separate samples with similar participation shares (about 3 in 10 traces in both). [POS001, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: A15 now repeats less often (mean about 1.9 down to about 1.6 occurrences per trace, still present in every trace); this repetition change is not part of the relocation hypothesis. [OCC001]
