### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of five alternative activities — A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27, with about one of them executing per case in both windows — is placed earlier in the flow in the later window: it now runs between A47 and A28 near the start of the case, whereas before it ran later, in the segment between A29/A30 and A34, interrupting the A29 → A30 → A34 sequence. This is shown directly by the position shift (mean relative position about 0.62 to about 0.35), the flipped order against A28 and A29, the successor changes at A29, A30, A47 and the five activities, and the new A47 → five → A28 chain alongside the tightened A29 → A30 → A34 chain. All 30 qualified control-flow items point to this one pattern, roughly 50 times the ~0.6 expected by chance in an unchanged process, and every item ranks above about 99.5 in 100 reference comparisons, so sampling variation cannot account for the set as a whole. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG002, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]

Limitation: the evidence shows order and adjacency, not occurrence identity — it cannot fully rule out that the five activities were removed from the late segment and separate occurrences inserted early, rather than one block moving; the complete absence of interleaved or repeated orderings and the stable one-per-case participation make a single relocation the best reading. A few distance details after the change (A19→A34, A20→A34, A29→A19, A29→A20) rest on a single observed case each and are weak on their own; the conclusion rests on the items backed by all 200 cases in both windows.
