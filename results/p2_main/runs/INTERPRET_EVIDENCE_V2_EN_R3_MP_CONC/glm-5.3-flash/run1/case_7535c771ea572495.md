### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural replacement of the segment that follows A02. Before, every case contained the tail A06 → A01 → A03 in that fixed order, with A03 as the final step, and A02 fed into this segment directly (about 53 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) or via A04.
