### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A04 opened about half of all traces (98 of 200) while A01 was not observed at all; in the later window the picture is reversed — A01 opens about half of the traces (99 of 200, about 0.5 occurrences per trace, never more than once per trace) and A04 is no longer observed. [OCC001, OCC002]
- Both activities appear only as the very first event of a trace (mean position 0 with no spread) — A04 in the earlier window, A01 in the later one — and in both windows roughly half of the traces contain such an entry step while half do not. [POS001, POS002]
- The entry step always leads to A03: in the earlier window every observed outgoing transition of A04 went to A03, and in the later window every observed outgoing transition of A01 goes to A03. [DIR001, DIR002]
- A01 and A04 never occur in the same trace in either window. Before, A04 preceded A03 in about 49 of 100 traces; in the later window A01 precedes A03 in about 50 of 100 traces, with A03 otherwise appearing alone. [REL001, REL002, REL003]
- Separately, routing around A07 and A13 shifted: A07's outgoing transitions moved from about 92% to A13 (remainder A11) to about 56% A13, 29% A03 and 10% A05, and A13 now sends about 32% of its outgoing transitions to A07, which was not observed before (A03 still receives the largest share, about 47%). [DIR003, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process entry changed identity: A04, which in the earlier window started about half of the traces and always led directly to A03, is in the later window replaced by A01, which starts about half of the traces and likewise always leads directly to A03. The supporting signs are a near-exact swap in participation (about 49–50 in 100 traces each way), an identical positional profile (first event only), an identical successor (A03 in every observed case), and relational evidence that A01 and A04 are alternatives that never co-occur; eleven control-flow items qualified in screening against roughly 0.14 expected without a change, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, so sampling fluctuation cannot explain the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS002, REL001, REL002, REL003]

The evidence cannot distinguish a genuine replacement of A04 by a new activity A01 from a renaming of the same step, and zero participation formally means only that A04 was not observed in the later sample. [OCC001, OCC002]

Other differences: the redistribution between A07, A13, A03 and A05 — including the previously unobserved transitions A13→A07 and A07→A03 — is not part of the entry-change hypothesis and may reflect a separate routing change; its exact shares rest on only 48–59 outgoing transitions per window, so the proportions are uncertain. [DIR003, DIR004]
