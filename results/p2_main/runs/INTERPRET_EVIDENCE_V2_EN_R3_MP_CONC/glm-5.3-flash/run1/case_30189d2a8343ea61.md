### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the process runs a stage of five mutually exclusive activities — A19, A20, A25, A26, A27 — between A43 and A28, where A43 previously led directly to A28: A43 now routes to one of the five (together about 97 of 100 of its outgoing transitions), that activity then leads to A28, and the A43→A28 distance moves from one event to two, with each alternative taken in roughly 18–23 of 100 traces and exactly one of the five in essentially every later trace.
