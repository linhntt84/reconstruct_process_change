### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural reorganisation around A01, A02, A06, A13 and A14: the segment A02 → A14 → A01, including A01's branches to A07, A08, A10 and A12, was no longer executed in the later window, and A06 and A13, which previously fed into that segment, now hand over directly to A14, which continues with A08 or A07 instead of A01. The successor changes are observed directly in the direction profiles, participation drops from about half of traces to zero, and every relation profile containing A01 or A02 collapses from co-occurrence to "other activity only" — though these many agreeing relation items largely restate one underlying difference rather than confirming it independently. Chance is implausible as an overall explanation, since 107 items qualified where roughly 0.64 would be expected without change. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR004, DIR010, DIR011, REL001, LAG012]

Other differences: the later window also introduces the new activity A09 after A07 (and occasionally after A04) leading to A11 and A12; A08 and A10, previously rare and mutually exclusive, now form a fixed A08 → A10 pair in about half the traces, replacing A08's immediate handover to A05; A11 now also hands over to A13 in about half of its outgoing transitions; A06 and A13 repeat far less often; and A08, A10 and A14 occupy earlier positions within traces. [OCC005, DIR003, DIR007, DIR006, DIR008, OCC003, POS004]

The evidence cannot rule out that A01 and A02 still exist but were simply not triggered, since zero participation means only non-observation, nor can it distinguish a change within one process from a different mix of cases in the later window; the A04 → A09 link in particular rests on very few observations (about 1 in 100 of A04's outgoing transitions). [OCC001, DIR003]
