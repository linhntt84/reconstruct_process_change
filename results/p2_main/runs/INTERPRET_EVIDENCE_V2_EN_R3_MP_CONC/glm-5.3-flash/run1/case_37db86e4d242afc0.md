### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the early stage of the process: A01 — previously executed once in every trace between A02 and A07 — is no longer observed, and A03, previously unobserved, now runs once in every trace in exactly that slot, so the early sequence A02 → A01 → A07 has become A02 → A03 → A07 at the same relative position. The support is direct and consistent: the participation swap is total in both directions (200 of 200 traces each way), A02's outgoing transitions moved completely from A01 to A03, the link into A07 is preserved, A03's position matches A01's (median 0.2), and every relational profile with A02, A05 and A07 flips from A01 to A03 with the same order relations. The screening context reinforces this: 24 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.12 would be expected by chance, so the ensemble cannot be a sampling artefact, though the items describe one coherent difference rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: zero participation only means A01 was not observed in the later window, and the evidence shows that A03 behaves like A01 in this slot, not that it is the same step under a new name. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL005, REL006, REL008, REL010]

Other differences: the end of the process changed as well: A06 was added as a new final activity after A05, appearing in about 46 in 100 after-traces (always last), where earlier about half of the traces ended with A05 itself and the rest continued to A08; A08's overall frequency stayed roughly stable (about 50 in 100 traces before, about 54 in 100 after). Because A06 was absent from the earlier window, this is the addition of a new optional step rather than a re-weighting between existing paths. [OCC003, DIR004, POS003, POS004, REL004, REL007, REL009, REL011, REL012]

An important competing explanation remains: A01 could still occur at a rate too low to surface in 200 after-traces, and A03 could be the same step re-labelled; either would look identical in this evidence. [OCC001, OCC002]
