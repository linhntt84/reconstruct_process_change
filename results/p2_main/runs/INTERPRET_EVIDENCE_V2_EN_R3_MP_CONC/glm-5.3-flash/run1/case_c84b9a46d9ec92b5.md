### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative block of activities A19, A20 and A25, which used to run between A41 and A26, is no longer executed in the later window, so that A41 now flows directly to A26. Earlier, about one third of traces contained exactly one of the three, A41 fed into the block in about 86% of its outgoing transitions, the block led on to A26 in about 95% of cases, and A26 typically followed A41 two positions later; in the later window the three activities appear in 0–1 of 200 traces, A41 points to A26 in about 86% of its outgoing transitions, and the A41–A26 gap is typically one position. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, LAG001, REL004, REL009, REL013]

The screening produced 29 qualifying items against roughly 0.57 expected if nothing had changed, so the ensemble is unlikely for a stable process, and the participating items — the participation collapse, the redirection of A41, the shortened A41–A26 lag and the changed relations — all describe one coordinated structural difference. Because the activities are nearly absent in the later window, this is a removal or bypass of a block, not a shift of shares between surviving paths. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, LAG001]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the block remains in the process but was not triggered in any of the 200 later traces; zero participation only means the activities were not observed, and the later outgoing profile of A19 rests on a single trace. [OCC002, OCC003, DIR001]

Other differences: A21's outgoing transitions redistributed, with A24 rising from about 14% to about 35% and A31 falling from about 54% to about 38% of its observed outgoing transitions; this may be a downstream consequence of the same change, but the evidence does not link it directly to the block's removal. [DIR003]
