### Observed differences

- The direct successors of the cycle activities flipped. Earlier, about 94 in 100 of A01's outgoing transitions went to A07 (the rest mostly to A03 and A04); in the later window none went to A07, about 57 in 100 went to A05, and the remainder split across A06, A04 and A03 [DIR001]. Earlier, about 96 in 100 of A05's outgoing transitions returned to A01; later, about 91 in 100 go to A07 [DIR004]. Earlier, A07's outgoing transitions split between A05 (about 43 in 100) and A06 (about 41 in 100); later, about 94 in 100 go back to A01 [DIR006].
- Immediate succession changed accordingly: the distance-1 pairs A01→A07, A05→A01 and A07→A05 (each roughly a quarter to a third of observed pairs at distance 1 earlier) were not observed at distance 1 in the later window, while A01→A05, A05→A07 and A07→A01 became the dominant distance-1 pairs there (about 20–25 in 100 of observed pairs) [LAG003, LAG009, LAG013, LAG001, LAG011, LAG012].
- A05 and A07 now occur later within traces: A05's mean relative position rose from about 0.47 to 0.57 and A07's from about 0.51 to 0.69 [POS002, POS004]. A06 moved from the very end of traces (mean about 0.96) to the middle (about 0.48) [POS003].
- Participation moved in opposite directions: A01 appears in about 53 in 100 traces in the later window versus about 28 in 100 earlier (mean occurrences per trace about 1.10 versus 0.53), while A07 appears in about 29 in 100 versus about 51 in 100 earlier (mean about 0.57 versus 1.03) [OCC001, OCC002].
- Couplings changed: A03 and A04, which earlier always co-occurred with A07 (together in about 50 in 100 traces, never alone), now appear without A07 in about 24 in 100 traces [REL006, REL007]; A01 and A07, earlier together in about 28 in 100 traces with A07 alone in another 23, are now together in 29 with A01 alone in 24 [REL005]. A03 and A04 also now often lead directly back to A01 (about 34–39 in 100 of their outgoing transitions), which was rare earlier [DIR002, DIR003, LAG004, LAG006].
- A06's outgoing transitions, of which only about 11 were observed earlier (mostly to A03), rose to about 83 in the later window, spread over A01, A03, A05 and A04; the earlier side of this comparison rests on few observations [DIR005].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the execution order of A05 and A07 within the repeating cycle around A01 was reversed: in the earlier window the cycle ran A01 → A07 → A05 → back to A01 (with A07 often passing to A06), while in the later window it runs A01 → A05 → A07 → back to A01 [DIR001, DIR004, DIR006, LAG001, LAG003, LAG009, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013]. The successor profiles of all three activities changed exactly as this re-sequencing predicts, the old distance-1 pairs disappeared while the new ones appeared, and the later positions of A05 and A07 fit; with 35 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.29 expected if nothing had changed, chance cannot account for the ensemble as a whole [DIR001, DIR004, DIR006, POS002, POS004]. Limitations: POS and LAG use event positions, so the hypothesis concerns sequence order rather than clock timing, and the evidence cannot rule out a broader rewiring beyond a two-activity exchange, since A01 simultaneously gained direct transitions to A03, A04 and A06 and the participation of A01 and A07 shifted [DIR001, OCC001, OCC002].

Other differences: A06 moved from the end of traces to the middle and now branches to several successors instead of mainly A03, though the earlier profile rests on about 11 outgoing transitions and some later pair observations are also sparse [POS003, DIR005]. A03 and A04 decoupled from A07, and A01 and A07 now often occur in separate traces [REL005, REL006, REL007].
