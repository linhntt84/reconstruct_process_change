### Observed differences

- Participation in the three activities A19, A20 and A25 collapsed: in the earlier window each ran exactly once per trace (never repeated) in about 33, 29 and 40 in 100 traces respectively (65, 57 and 81 of 200 traces), while in the later window each appears in only 1 of 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the earlier window these three were mutually exclusive alternatives whose shares together cover essentially every trace, so a typical case executed exactly one of them; in the later window about 99 in 100 traces contain none of the three. [REL001, REL002, REL006]
- The block was embedded between A27/A28 and A29: whenever one of A19, A20 or A25 ran, it co-occurred with A27, A28 and A29, always after A27 and before A29, and A28 led into one of the three in about half of its outgoing transitions (roughly 16–19 in 100 to each). In the later window these relations are gone, with about 99 in 100 traces containing only A27, A28 and A29 of this group. [REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, DIR006]
- Routing concentrated accordingly: A28 now leads almost exclusively to A29 (about 97 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, against about 43 in 100 earlier), and A27 now leads almost exclusively to A28 (about 98 in 100, against about 52 in 100 earlier, when it also led directly to A25, A19 and A20, together about 44 in 100). [DIR005, DIR006]
- Distances shrank by about one step: A27 to A29 is now typically 2 positions apart (about 96 in 100) versus typically 3 earlier (about 90 in 100); A28 to A29 is now almost always immediately consecutive (about 96 in 100) versus typically 2 steps apart earlier (about 52 in 100); A27 to A28 is now almost always directly consecutive (about 99 in 100 versus 52 in 100). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG004]
- Separately, the outgoing transitions of A10 were redistributed: earlier they concentrated on A15 (about 20 in 100), A47 (17), A16 (17) and A45 (14); later they spread over roughly twenty successors, the largest being A26 (17), A45 (15), and A16 and A41 (9 each). [DIR001]
- Minor shifts: A27's mean relative position moved slightly later (about 0.46 to 0.48) and A29's slightly earlier (about 0.57 to 0.55); both activities still occur in every trace in both windows. [POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative block formed by A19, A20 and A25 — executed in the earlier window in essentially every case, exactly one of the three per trace, always after A27 and before A29 and closely tied to A28 — is no longer executed in the later window, so that cases now proceed from A28 directly to A29. Screening produced 30 qualifying items (all control-flow) where roughly 0.56 would be expected without a change, so the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by chance sampling. The support is direct and consistent: the participation drop rests on 57–81 traces per activity falling to 1, the relational items show the same three activities moving from exclusive alternatives embedded between A27/A28 and A29 to joint absence, the routing items show A27 and A28 concentrating onto A28 and A29 respectively, and the lag items show the A27→A29 and A28→A29 gaps closing by about one step. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL005, REL012, DIR005, DIR006, LAG002, LAG004]

An important competing explanation remains: the evidence cannot distinguish complete removal of the block from it being skipped in almost all cases, since each of the three activities still appears in one later trace and zero participation only means they were not observed. In addition, the later routing profiles of A19, A20 and A25 rest on a single trace each, so the apparent new successors (A38, A37, A31) are not reliable evidence about where these activities now lead. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: the redistribution of A10's outgoing transitions described above, which the evidence does not connect to the vanished block [DIR001]; and the small opposite position shifts of A27 and A29, which are based on event positions rather than clock time [POS004, POS005].
