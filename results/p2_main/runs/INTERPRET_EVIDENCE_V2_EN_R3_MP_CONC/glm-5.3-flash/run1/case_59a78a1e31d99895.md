### Observed differences

- The activities A04, A06 and A10 became much rarer: each appeared in about 50 in 100 traces in the earlier window (99 and 101 of 200 traces) but only in about 20–22 in 100 in the later window (40–43 of 200), still essentially once per trace when present. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- Earlier, every trace contained exactly one of two alternatives: A04 alone (about 50 in 100 traces) or A06 together with A10 (about 50 in 100; the two always appeared together, in both orders, i.e. without a fixed order between them). In the later window, no A04/A06/A10 activity appears at all in about two-thirds of traces (66–70 in 100), A06 and A10 still only occur together, and A04 now co-occurs with A06/A10 in about 8 in 100 traces, so the earlier strict either-or no longer holds. [REL007, REL010, REL013, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A02 no longer routes into this branch: earlier, about 25 in 100 of A02's outgoing transitions went to A04 and about 13 each to A06 and A10; in the later window no A02→A04/A06/A10 transitions were observed, and A02's transitions go mainly to A05 (about 46 in 100, up from about 23), A03 and A09 (about 21 each) and A01 (about 12, up from about 3). [DIR001]
- The branch is instead entered from A03 and A09 and now flows on into A08: A03, which earlier led only to A07 (about 53) and A08 (about 47) in 100 transitions, now also leads to A04 (about 23), A06 (about 14) and A10 (about 8); A09, earlier mainly to A08 (about 62) and A07 (about 37), now also leads to A04 (about 21) and to A06 and A10 (about 14 each). All 47 observed outgoing transitions of A04 in the later window go to A08 (earlier only one transition was seen, to A01), and A06 and A10 now split their outgoing transitions between A08 (about 44 and 56 in 100) and each other, where earlier each led only to the other. [DIR002, DIR005, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006]
- Accordingly, A04, A06 and A10 now never occur without A08 (earlier each of them typically occurred without it), and the strict A02-before-A04/A06/A10 order disappeared. [REL008, REL011, REL014, REL001, REL002, REL003]
- In event positions (not clock time), A04, A06 and A10 moved from the end of traces towards the middle (medians about 1.0 down to about 0.6–0.67), while A02 and A08 occur later (A02 median 0.50 to 0.73; A08 median 0.63 to 0.83, with A08's participation unchanged at about 33 in 100 traces). Pair distances match: the immediate A02→A04 pair at distance 1 (about 61 in 100 observed pairs) disappeared, while A03→A04/A06/A10 and A09→A04/A06 pairs now sit mostly 1–2 steps apart. [POS002, POS003, POS005, POS001, POS004, LAG001, LAG006, LAG007, LAG009, LAG013, LAG014]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the sub-process around A04, A06 and A10 is rerouted and executed less often. Earlier, A02 selected between two end-of-trace alternatives — A04 alone, or A06 and A10 together — and the chosen branch closed the trace; in the later window A02 no longer reaches A04, A06 or A10 at all, the branch is entered from A03 and A09, and A04, A06 and A10 lead into A08, without which they do not occur; the branch as a whole runs in about 33 in 100 traces, down from every trace. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR005, DIR003, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL014, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

The rerouting is shown directly by the successor profiles, backed by substantial transition counts (A02: 381→219; A03: 87→103; A09: 81→107; A04: 47 transitions, all to A08), and it is consistent across participation, co-occurrence, event positions and pair distances. Chance is also unlikely as an explanation of the pattern as a whole: 45 items qualified from 69 tested units where fewer than one (0.34) would be expected without a change, and every item's difference exceeds about 99 in 100 random re-splits of the same data. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR005, DIR003, POS001, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a modification of the routing itself from a large shift in the mix of pre-existing process variants (for example, a variant containing the A03/A09→A04/A06/A10→A08 path becoming much more common); A04's earlier outgoing profile rests on a single observed transition; and position and distance results use event positions, so they say nothing about clock-time timing. [DIR003, POS002, LAG001]

Other differences: A02 and A08 occur at later event positions in the later window, and A02's outgoing transitions to A05 roughly doubled (about 23 to about 46 in 100), while A08's participation stayed at about 33 in 100 traces. [POS001, POS004, DIR001]
