### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, five activities — A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 — each ran in roughly 16–24 of 100 traces and were mutually exclusive alternatives (pairs of them almost never co-occurred), with A43, which appears in every trace, followed by one of them in about 99 of 100 of its observed outgoing transitions. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the segment of five mutually exclusive activities A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 that previously connected A43 to A28 is bypassed: A43 now leads directly to A28, and the five activities are executed in at most about 1–3 of 200 traces (A26 and A27 not at all), whereas before each of them ran in about 16–24 of 100 traces as the link between A43 and A28. [DIR007, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, LAG011]

Screening expected well under one qualifying item (about 0.72) if nothing had changed, yet 57 items qualified, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. The behavioural signs are also direct and consistent: A43's successor profile flipped from the five activities to A28, the A43→A28 and A16→A28 distances each shortened by one position, and the five activities' participation, their co-occurrence with A43 and A28, and the orderings A43→A20/A25/A27 and A20/A27→A28 all disappeared together. [DIR007, LAG011, LAG008, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL017, REL018, REL021, REL023, REL024]

Limitations: with 0–3 observations of the five activities in the later window, the evidence shows they are no longer executed in practice but cannot distinguish whether they were removed from the process or remain as a path that is simply never chosen — zero participation only means the activity was not observed. The apparent new successors of A20 and A25 rest on 3 and 1 observations and are weak. [DIR003, DIR004, OCC005, OCC006]

Other differences: A02's participation halved (27 to 14 of 100 traces), it now occurs later in the trace, and its successors shifted from mostly A16 to a spread including A49, A28 and A15, while A15's transitions into A02 largely disappeared; this could be an independent change or a side effect of the bypass, which the evidence cannot decide. A15, A16 and A28 show only minor positional shifts. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, POS002, POS003, POS009]
