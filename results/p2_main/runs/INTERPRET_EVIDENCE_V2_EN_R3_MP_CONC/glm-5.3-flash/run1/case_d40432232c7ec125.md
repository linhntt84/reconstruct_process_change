### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening flagged 94 control-flow items where about 1 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the volume of flagged behaviour cannot be explained by random re-splits of a stable process [OCC001, OCC003, DIR002, REL023, LAG023]. The evidence supports the possibility that two segments of the process exchanged their relative order. Before, the segment running from A40 through A19, A21 and A24 into A25 executed before the segment running from A29 through A20, A22 and A23 into A30; in the later window the A29-led segment executes first and the A40-led segment after it. The supporting signs are direct and consistent: every tested cross-pair between the two groups flipped from one near-fixed order to the other, the position distributions shifted in matching directions by about a quarter of the trace, the successor choices at both segment boundaries changed accordingly, and the direct successions that appeared and disappeared match the new sequence. Co-occurrence between the groups stayed at about 29–39 in 100 traces, so the activities still execute together in the same traces; what changed is their order, not their participation in those traces [REL016, REL023, REL025, DIR006, DIR013, POS003, POS005, LAG021, LAG023].

Limitations: positions and lags are measured in event positions, not clock time, so "earlier" means earlier in the trace, not earlier in time. The evidence cannot distinguish a re-ordering applied within unchanged traces from a replacement of one ordered variant by another, and the order of the optional trio A19/A21/A24 relative to the rest is observed only in the roughly three to four in ten traces where they occur together; the evidence also cannot indicate why the order changed [REL016, REL021, REL023].

Other differences: A08 participation fell from about 64 to about 29 in 100 traces and it moved later, losing its dominant direct edge to A16; A04, A06 and A10 participation roughly doubled or tripled with new prominent edges to A46 and A15; and the long-standing successions A14→A15 and A45→A46 persist but weakened (about 98 to 80 in 100 of A14's outgoing transitions, and A45→A46 within one step from about 97 to 84 in 100). These changes may be connected to the reordering, but the evidence does not show how they fit together [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR015, LAG001, LAG030].
