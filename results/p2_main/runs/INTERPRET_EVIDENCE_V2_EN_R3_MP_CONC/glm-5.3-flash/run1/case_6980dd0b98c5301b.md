### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the closing segment of the process was reordered with respect to A01: in the earlier window the segment ran A08 → A06 → A07 → A01, with A01 last, while in the later window it runs A08 → A01 → A06 → A07, with A01 executed immediately after A08 and A07 now last. In behavioural terms, A06 and A07 moved from preceding A01 to following it. [REL001, REL002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the order reversal is shown relationally in 200 of 200 co-occurrence cases in each window; the successor, distance and position items all describe one and the same permutation; every involved activity still occurs in all traces in both windows, so this is a reordering rather than activities being added, removed or skipped; and the qualified item count (11 versus about 0.05 expected without change) far exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces. [REL001, REL002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a reordering within every trace from a drastic shift in the mix of two pre-existing variants (one ending A06→A07→A01, the other A01→A06→A07), because each window shows only a single order; the empty direction profiles only mean no outgoing transition was observed in that window, not that an edge is absent; and the position indicator uses event positions rather than clock time, though the relational and successor evidence shows the order change directly.
