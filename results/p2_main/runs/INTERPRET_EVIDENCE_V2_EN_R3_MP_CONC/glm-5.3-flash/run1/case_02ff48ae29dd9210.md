### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative step formed by A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 is no longer executed: earlier, every trace inserted exactly one of these five mutually exclusive activities either between A24 and A25 (about 39 in 100 traces) or between A25 and A26 (about 61 in 100), whereas in the later window traces proceed directly from A24 through A25 to A26 and each of the five appears in at most 1 of 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR005, DIR006, LAG006, REL004, REL006, REL011, REL015]

The change is shown directly and consistently: participation collapses from 31–46 to 0–1 traces per activity, the successor profiles of A24 and A25 each collapse to a single successor, the A24→A26 distance shortens from 3 to 2 steps, and every pair relation between the five and the anchors flips from always co-occurring to never co-occurring. All 38 qualifying items belong to the control-flow family, against roughly 0.46 expected by chance, so the ensemble is many times what an unchanged process typically produces, and the items describe one coherent structural difference rather than scattered effects. [OCC003, DIR005, DIR006, LAG006, REL011, REL001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish whether the five activities were removed from the process or are now always bypassed, since both produce the same observations, and zero participation only means they were not observed in this sample; the residual after-window routing profiles rest on single traces and may reflect a very small remaining participation rather than a new fixed order.
