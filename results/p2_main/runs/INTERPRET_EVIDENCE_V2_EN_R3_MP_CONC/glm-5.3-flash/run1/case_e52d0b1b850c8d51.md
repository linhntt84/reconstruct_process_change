### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence directly and consistently supports the possibility that the segment A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 became optional: it ran in every earlier trace, while in the later window the three activities run in only about 53–54 in 100 traces, are skipped together in about 46 in 100 (almost never partially), keep their order and their entry from A41 whenever they run, and A41 then leads directly onward to A26. The unchanged exit and downstream behaviour (A20 still leads to A26 in about 97 in 100 of its transitions; A26, A46 and A47 still occur in every trace) supports "skipped" rather than "moved". Screening qualified 59 control-flow items where fewer than 1 would qualify without a change, so the ensemble is not what a stable process produces, and each item's difference also exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits. Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether individual cases skip the segment at random or because of case characteristics, and cannot rule out that the later window simply mixes case types in which the segment does not apply; incoming transitions were not measured, so what precedes A46/A47 when the segment is skipped is unknown.
