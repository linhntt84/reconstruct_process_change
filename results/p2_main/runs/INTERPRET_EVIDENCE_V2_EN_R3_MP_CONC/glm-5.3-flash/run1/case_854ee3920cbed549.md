### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, one additional step is executed within the previously fixed A29 → A30 → A31 sequence: practically every later case inserts exactly one of five alternative activities — A19, A20, A25, A26 or A27 — placed directly before A30 in about half of the cases and directly after A30 in the other half. The relational and positional evidence shows this directly and consistently: the five activities rise from zero participation to about 17–23 in 100 cases with near-zero mutual co-occurrence; A29 and A30 now route about half of their outgoing transitions to them while each of them leads onward to A30 or A31; they run after A29 and before A31 whenever they appear; and all three positional gaps in the segment widened by exactly one step, which distinguishes an insertion from a mere shift in how often existing paths are taken. The volume of qualifying evidence (49 control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected without change) is also far beyond what an unchanged process produces. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, REL010, REL013, REL015, REL018, REL020, REL022, REL024, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The evidence cannot rule out that these five activity codes are relabelled or previously unrecorded occurrences rather than genuinely new steps, and the data do not show why the inserted step falls before A30 in some cases and after it in others. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL014, REL019, REL023]
