### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A01, was added to the flow between the block containing A04 and A09 and A08: in the later window A01 runs exactly once per case at a late position (median 0.8) and always before A08, while A04 and A09 — which previously passed control mainly onward to A08 — now pass it mainly to A01, and the A04→A08 and A09→A08 distances grew by about one step, as expected if A01 sits between them and A08. [OCC001, POS001, REL004, DIR002, DIR003, LAG003, LAG007]

The supporting signs are strong: the participation change is complete (0 of 200 traces before, 200 of 200 after, once per trace), the ordering with A08 is invariant across all later traces, both predecessors redirect consistently, and the lag shifts all point the same way; with 19 items against roughly 0.18 expected without change, chance cannot account for the ensemble. Note that the agreeing items largely describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, REL004, DIR002, DIR003, LAG003, LAG007, screening]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out: A01 may not be genuinely new but simply too rare to appear in 200 earlier traces before becoming frequent; even then, the rerouting of A04 and A09 and the one-step distance increases remain real behavioural differences in the later window. Also, because A06 and A07 now usually precede A01 while their upstream source is not observed, A01's full set of predecessors beyond A04 and A09 is not established. [OCC001, REL002, REL003]

Other differences: A06 now appears somewhat less often (about 55 in 100 traces before vs about 47 in 100 after) and A07 somewhat more often (about 45 vs 54 in 100), which may be side-effects of the rerouting around A01; and the pairs A04→A06, A04→A07 and A09→A06, which were immediate earlier, now mostly sit two steps apart, though these items rest on only about 11–33 observations per window.
