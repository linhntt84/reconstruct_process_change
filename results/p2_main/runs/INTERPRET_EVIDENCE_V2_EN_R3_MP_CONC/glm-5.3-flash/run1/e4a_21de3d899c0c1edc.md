### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the elapsed time between consecutive completion events on the four transitions into A24 (from A27, A28, A29 and A30) sat at about 1 hour (medians roughly 0.95–1.04 hours). In the later window it is about 2 hours (medians roughly 2.0–2.1 hours) — roughly doubled on every one of these transitions [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004].
- The shift is consistent in size, about 1.0–1.1 hours on each transition, and the times also became more variable: the middle 50% of values widened from roughly 0.87–1.14 hours to roughly 1.75–2.31 hours [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004].
- How often these transitions occur barely moved: about 44–60 observed consecutive pairs per window (for example 55 before vs 60 after for A28→A24, and 52 vs 47 for A30→A24), so this is a timing shift, not a change in participation [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004].
- Each of the four differences is larger than in more than 999 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data; the time family produced 4 qualifying items where roughly 0.01 would be expected if nothing had changed, while control flow produced none (0 items vs about 0.3 expected) [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A change in timing: in the later window, the elapsed time between consecutive completion events roughly doubled — from about 1 hour to about 2 hours — on the transitions into A24 from its predecessors A27, A28, A29 and A30. The evidence is direct (the T_WAIT items measure exactly these elapsed times), consistent (all four transitions show the same direction and a similar ~1-hour increase, with stable transition counts), and chance is effectively ruled out: 4 time items against an expectation of about 0.01 without change, and no control-flow items at all. Limitations: T_WAIT is elapsed time between consecutive completions, so the evidence cannot show whether the extra hour accumulates before A24 starts or during its execution; a competing explanation the evidence cannot exclude is that the timing of the preceding activities' completions shifted rather than A24 itself, although A24 is the shared endpoint of all four affected pairs [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004].

Other differences: none — no control-flow or other items qualified at the configured thresholds [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004].
