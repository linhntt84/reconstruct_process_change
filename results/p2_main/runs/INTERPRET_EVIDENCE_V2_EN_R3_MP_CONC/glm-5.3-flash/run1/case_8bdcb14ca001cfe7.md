### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the part of the process around A09 is executed with far more repetitions: A09 runs about four times per trace instead of about two, and its neighbours A13 and A15 — which earlier also exited to A01 and to each other — now lead back into A09 in most of their transitions (about 71 in 100 for A13, about 95 in 100 for A15), so that A09, A13 and A15 behave as a tightly repeating cycle, with A09/A13 co-occurrence mostly interleaved and A09 self-repeats at shorter gaps. This rests on a direct occurrence measurement for A09 plus consistent routing, relation and gap evidence for the same activities; the 29 qualifying items far exceed the fewer than 1 expected by chance among the 86 tested units, and every item's difference exceeds those of about 199 in 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. An important competing explanation remains: the redirection of A13 and A15 toward A09 could follow mechanically from A09 simply occurring much more often (any successor is then likelier to be A09) rather than from a rewiring of those exits, and the evidence cannot separate the two. [OCC002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, REL003, LAG007]

Other differences: The A03/A14/A11 cluster changed separately from this repetition hypothesis: A03 and A14 stopped repeating and now run once, earlier in the trace, always together with A11, which earlier appeared in only about 28 in 100 traces (often without them, and interleaved) and now accompanies them in every such trace in a dominant order with gaps of one to two events; A14's exits also shifted toward A12. In addition, A06 now leads almost exclusively to A01, and an A01→A15 transition was not observed in the later window.
