### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A07 has dropped out of the executed process — it was removed or has become fully skipped. Earlier, A07 ran in about a quarter of cases (46 of 200 traces), late in each trace, passing work on mainly to A03 and A13, and A10 handed over to it in about 13 in 100 of A10's outgoing transitions; in the later window, A07 is not observed in a single trace, has no outgoing transitions, and never co-occurs with A10, which now runs alone in every trace. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, REL001, DIR002]

The support is direct and consistent: participation falls from about 23 in 100 traces to zero, and every relational view of A07 (its successors, its position, its co-occurrence with A10) empties out in the later window. Six control-flow items qualified in screening, against fewer than one expected for an unchanged process, so chance alone cannot account for the set; note, however, that the A07-focused items describe one underlying behaviour and therefore corroborate rather than independently confirm each other. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001, DIR002, LAG001]

Limitations: zero participation strictly means only that A07 was not observed, so a reduction so deep that 200 traces would miss it cannot be distinguished from complete removal, and a relabelling in which another activity code took over A07's role cannot be ruled out. [OCC001]

Other differences: beyond the loss of A07, A10's remaining handovers shifted — A03 and A13 gained share, A04 and A08 lost share, A06 disappeared and A09 appeared — and A10–A03 pairs are now more often immediately adjacent than the earlier 2–3-step clustering. These shifts are consistent with work being rerouted around the missing A07, but the evidence does not establish that rerouting directly. [DIR002, LAG001]
