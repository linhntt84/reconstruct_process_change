### Observed differences

- The clearest difference concerns the three activities A19, A25 and A20. In the earlier window each appeared in every trace (200 of 200), in the fixed order A19 → A25 → A20; in the later window each appears in only about 53 in 100 traces (106–107 of 200), and their order among themselves is unchanged whenever they do run. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL008, REL009, REL014]
- The segment is bypassed rather than replaced: A41 led to A19 in about 96 in 100 of its outgoing transitions before, and now splits roughly 50 in 100 to A19 and 44 in 100 to A26. The distance from A41 to A26 was 4 steps in about 93 in 100 cases before, and is now 1 step in about 44 in 100 cases (segment skipped) versus 4 steps in about 49 in 100 (segment present). [DIR005, LAG002]
- All fifteen activity pairs combining A19, A20 or A25 with each other or with the always-present A26, A41, A46 and A47 drop from co-occurrence in every trace to about 53 in 100; within the segment the remaining ~47 in 100 traces contain neither activity, and for pairs with an always-present activity only the latter is present. - A02 became much rarer and appears earlier: participation fell from 29 in 100 traces (58 of 200) to about 8.5 in 100 (17 of 200); its median position moved from near the end of the trace (about 0.97) to mid-trace (about 0.47); and its outgoing transitions shifted from mostly A47 (85 in 100) to a spread over many targets. - The rare activities A04 and A06 appear somewhat more often, from about 4 in 100 traces (9 and 8 of 200) to about 12 in 100 (24 and 25 of 200). [OCC002, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment A19 → A25 → A20, executed between A41 and A26, became optional: in the earlier window it ran in every trace in that fixed order, while in the later window it runs in about 53 in 100 traces and is skipped in the rest, with A41 then leading directly on to A26. This rests on direct participation evidence (all three activities fall from 100% to about 53% participation), on the matching co-occurrence drop across all fifteen pairs involving the segment members, and on relational evidence of the bypass itself — A41's successor share moving from almost exclusively A19 to roughly half A19 and 44 in 100 A26, and the A41→A26 distance collapsing from typically 4 steps to 1 step in about 44 in 100 cases. The unchanged internal order of the segment when it runs fits a skip rather than a reordering or replacement, and with 41 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window contains a different mix of case types that never run the segment, rather than the segment having become a skippable option within the same cases. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL013, REL014, REL019, REL022, DIR005, LAG002]

Other differences: A02's decline (29 in 100 → about 8.5 in 100 traces, an earlier typical position, and co-occurrence with the segment down from 29 in 100 to about 5 in 100) is steeper than the segment skip alone would produce and may be a related but distinct shift; it rests on only 17 later traces containing A02, and the near-disappearance of A46 → A02 hand-offs follows from it. A04 and A06 also occur somewhat more often than before. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, REL001, REL004, DIR006, LAG003, OCC002, OCC003]
