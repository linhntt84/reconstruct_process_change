### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 never occurred (0 of 200 traces each). In the later window, A19 runs in about 29 in 100 traces, A20 in about 41 in 100 and A25 in about 31 in 100, each occurring once in the traces where it appears. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window a new sequential step was inserted between A27 and A29, executed as exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities: A19, A20 or A25. In the earlier window the flow ran directly from A27 through A28 to A29; in the later window virtually every trace passes through exactly one of the three (A19 in about 29 in 100 traces, A20 in about 41, A25 in about 31), running before A28 in roughly 55 in 100 cases and after A28 in roughly 43 in 100. The support is direct and internally consistent: the three activities appear from zero participation and never co-occur, they always follow A27 and precede A29, they absorb about half of A27's and about four in ten of A28's outgoing transitions, and the widened position gaps between A27, A28 and A29 match an inserted step. The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what a stable process produces: 28 control-flow items qualified where about 0.56 would be expected by chance. Limitations: the indicators locate the step in sequence order (event positions), not clock time, and the evidence cannot show what governs the choice among A19, A20 and A25, nor distinguish a newly added step from activities that already existed but were never executed in the earlier period.
