### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process was restructured around its mid-trace branch block: the alternative activities A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27 (with A02, A06 and A10 much reduced) are no longer executed, the routing that previously dispersed from A29 and A30 into this block now runs directly from A29 to A30 to A31, and A04 — previously one of the mid-trace alternatives, present in about 21 of 100 traces — is now executed as a late step in about 63 of 100 traces, typically between A46 and A47. The participation changes are far too large to be sampling noise in 200-trace windows, the new placement of A04 is shown directly by successor, relation and lag evidence rather than by position alone, occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators all point to the same restructuring, and 87 qualifying evidence items against about 1 expected for an unchanged process rules out chance as an overall explanation. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, DIR001, DIR008, DIR009, DIR011, POS001, REL013, LAG010, LAG021]

The evidence cannot tell whether A04 was relocated from the mid-trace block to the end of the case or whether its mid-trace use was dropped and a separate late A04 step introduced — before the change, A04 already appeared late in a minority of traces — and several lag items rest on only 1–3 later-window observations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window captures a different mix of process variants in which the block activities and the old mid-trace A04 role simply do not occur, rather than a modification of one process. [OCC002, POS001, LAG002, LAG012, LAG018]

Other differences: A31 and A46 occur slightly earlier in the later window (mean relative position about 0.54 vs 0.57, and about 0.82 vs 0.85), and a minority pattern in which A47 is followed by A04 within five steps appears in about 33 of 200 later traces versus 8 of 200 before.
