### Observed differences

- The branching point moved from A20 to A21. In the earlier window, A20's outgoing transitions were spread over A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 (about 19–23% each) while A21 led only to A25 (100%); in the later window, A20 led to A22 in about 99.5% of its outgoing transitions, and A21 branched to those same five activities (about 16–23% each), with A25 almost never taken (about 0.5%). [DIR001, DIR002]
- The five-activity block changed its exit. Earlier, each of A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 led on to A22 in roughly 18–22% of its outgoing transitions and never to A25; later, each led to A25 in roughly 18–23% and practically never to A22 (at most about 0.5%). [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]
- The block changed sides relative to A22 and A21. Earlier, every block activity ran before A21 in 100% of traces and before A22 in 100%; later, A21 ran before each block activity in about 99.5% of traces, and A22 likewise before each block activity. All these activities still occurred together in essentially every trace in both windows. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]
- Positions moved accordingly: the block activities shifted later in the trace (mean position from about 0.44 to about 0.60), while A21 (about 0.65 to 0.52) and A22 (about 0.52 to 0.39) shifted earlier. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007]
- The old adjacencies largely disappeared: the ordered pairs A20 before each block activity, and each block activity before A22, within five steps were seen in about 200 of 200 traces earlier but in only 1 of 200 traces later; each later distance profile rests on a single observation. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]
- Elapsed time between consecutive completions shortened at three pairs: A09 to A10 from about 1.0 to 0.5 hours, A11 to A10 from about 1.0 to 0.5 hours (125 to 110 observed pairs), and A24 to A28 from about 1.0 hour to essentially zero (median 1.0 to 0.0 hours). [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural reordering involving A20, A21, A22, A25 and the block A30, A31, A36, A37, A38: earlier, A20 branched into the block, the block ran before A22 and A21 in every trace and its members led on to A22, while A21 led directly to A25; in the later window, A21 branches into the block, the block runs after A22 and A21 and its members lead on to A25, while A20 leads directly to A22 — in short, A20 and A21 exchanged their outgoing roles and the block's convergence moved from A22 to A25. This rests on direct relational evidence (complete order reversals between A21/A22 and every block activity at unchanged co-occurrence), on the successor rewiring at A20, A21 and all five block activities, and is corroborated by the position shifts and the near-disappearance of the old A20→block and block→A22 adjacencies. Participation itself did not change — every involved activity appeared in essentially all traces in both windows — and the control-flow family produced 34 qualifying items where about 0.55 would be expected without a change, so chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]

Limitations: the later LAG profiles rest on a single observation each, so the new distances are not established; A25's own position is not directly observed and is inferred only from the block's outgoing transitions and A21's abandoned edge; and untested connections (for example between A22 and A21, or other predecessors of A25) could also have changed, so the precise new structure beyond the observed edges is not pinned down. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is a broader re-routing that produces the same order reversals without a clean two-segment exchange. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]

Other differences: an independent timing change — the elapsed time between consecutive completions roughly halved for A09→A10 and A11→A10 (about 1.0 to 0.5 hours) and fell to nearly zero for A24→A28 (about 1.0 hour to essentially zero); all three items decreased consistently, and the time family produced 3 items where about 0.018 were expected by chance. These activities are not part of the structural hypothesis above. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003]
