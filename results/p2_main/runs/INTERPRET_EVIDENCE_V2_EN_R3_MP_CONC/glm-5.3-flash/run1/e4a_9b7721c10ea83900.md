### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a timing change around activity A34: in the later window, the elapsed time from the completion of its predecessors A26, A35 and A37 to the completion of A34 is about one hour longer than before, roughly doubling from about 1 hour to about 2 hours, with the entire distribution shifted upward and variability about doubled. Supporting signs: the shift is measured directly by the timing indicator on three transitions into the same activity, all agreeing in direction and magnitude, which is consistent with a single timing change around A34 rather than unrelated fluctuations; the time family yielded 3 qualifying items against about 0.01 expected without a change, so chance is an unlikely explanation for the set; and the doubling also holds on the A26→A34 pair, observed in every trace in both windows, so it is not confined to a shrinking subset of cases.
