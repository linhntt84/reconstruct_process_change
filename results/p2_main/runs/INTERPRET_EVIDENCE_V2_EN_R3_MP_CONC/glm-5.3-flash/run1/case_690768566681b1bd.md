### Observed differences

- The mid-process activities A19 and A20 appeared in every trace in the earlier window but in only about 53 in 100 traces in the later window; when they do appear, they still occur together and still precede the downstream activities. [OCC006, OCC007, REL022, REL026]
- The downstream activities A15, A16, A28, A43, A48 and A49 still occur in essentially every trace in both windows, but in the later window they run without A19/A20 in about 48 in 100 traces (earlier: essentially never). [REL012, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL033, REL034]
- A10 became much more frequent and earlier: participation rose from about 20 in 100 traces to about 64 in 100, and its mean position moved from about halfway through the trace to about a quarter in (event position, not clock time). [OCC005, POS001]
- A10's routing changed sharply: about 84 in 100 of its outgoing transitions now go to A16 (about 7 in 100 before, when A19 was the most common target at about 20 in 100); A10 is followed by A16 within one step in about 93 in 100 observed pairs (about 27 in 100 before, on few observations); A15 now leads to A10 in about 32 in 100 of its transitions (about 1 in 100 before); and A10 is no longer immediately followed by A19. [DIR004, LAG001, DIR005, LAG002]
- When A10 occurs, it now typically precedes the tail activities: A10 comes first in about 57–59 in 100 traces for A28, A43, A48 and A49, versus about 5–19 in 100 before. [REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011]
- The A25/A26/A27 block, previously present in about half of all traces (A25 with A26 in about 51 in 100, A27 alone in the rest), is now skipped entirely in about 47 in 100 traces; each activity halved to roughly 24–30 in 100, and the block rarely overlaps the tail any more (A28 appears without A25 in about 70 in 100 later-window traces versus about 49 in 100 before). [OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, REL037, REL038, REL043, REL039]
- A43's own routing changed: about 45 in 100 of its outgoing transitions now go to A28 (not observed before), while the share leading to A19 fell from about 80 in 100 to about 51 in 100. [DIR008]
- The rare activities A02, A04, A06 and A08 became rarer still: participation fell from about 17–25 in 100 traces to about 7–8 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, about half of the executions skip the previously mandatory stage formed by A19 and A20 — present in every earlier-window trace but in only about 53 in 100 later-window traces — together with the associated A25/A26/A27 block, while A10 has taken on a much larger, earlier role and typically leads directly into A16; the always-present downstream activities (A15, A16, A28, A43, A48, A49) are thereby reached without A19/A20, which is consistent with a rerouting into A16 that no longer passes through that stage. The signs are direct and mutually consistent across participation, relational and direction profiles, and with 91 qualifying items against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, POS001, DIR004, LAG001, REL022, REL026, REL037]

The evidence cannot distinguish a rerouting within one process from a changed mix of case types between the two windows (for example, two pre-existing variants in different proportions), and it does not show how the skipping traces reach A15/A16 or what precedes A10 in them. [OCC006, OCC007, REL022]

Other differences: the rare activities A02, A04, A06 and A08 declined and show shifted but thinly supported successor profiles; A15, A16 and A43 now occur slightly later within traces (mean position up by roughly 0.02–0.04); and the late transition A48→A49 became near-deterministic (immediately consecutive in about 99 in 100 observed pairs, versus about 79 in 100 before). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG016]
