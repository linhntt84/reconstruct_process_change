### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, two segments that previously executed in most cases are skipped. First, the choice block between A43 and A28: A43 previously passed to one of the five alternatives A19, A20, A25, A26 and A27, which then led to A28, whereas now A43 leads directly to A28 in about 87 in 100 cases and the five are observed in at most about 1 in 100 traces. Second, A10 between A48 and A49 is mostly skipped: A48 now leads directly to A49, and A10 occurs only sporadically at varied positions instead of reliably after A48. The rewiring is shown directly by the successor profiles of A43 and A48, each backed by about 200 outgoing transitions in both windows, and by the one-step shortening of the A43-to-A28 and A48-to-A49 lags; the participation collapse of the five activities and of A10 and the co-occurrence drops agree with it. With 89 qualifying items against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed, the evidence set as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits produce, and every item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits — although many items, notably the co-occurrence series, restate the same two differences rather than confirming them independently.
