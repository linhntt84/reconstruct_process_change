### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activity A01 was not observed at all (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it runs in about 101 of 200 traces (roughly half), about 1.1 times per trace, and it sits early in the case (median relative position about 0.32, middle half roughly 0.20–0.53). [OCC001, POS001]
- In the later window, A01's observed outgoing transitions lead mainly to A05 (about 35%), A03 (about 19%), A06 (about 15%) and A15 (about 14%); in the earlier window A01 had no observed outgoing transitions. [DIR001]
- Several activities gained A01 as a successor: in the later window about 36% of A09's, 28% of A08's, 22% of A06's, 11% of A12's and 7% of A05's observed outgoing transitions go to A01, whereas no transition into A01 was observed before (A01 itself did not occur). [DIR006, DIR005, DIR004, DIR007, DIR003]
- Before, A03, A05, A06, A08, A09, A12 and A15 each occurred only without A01 (alone in about 33–66 of 100 traces, never together with it); in the later window A01 co-occurs with each of them in about 32–50 of 100 traces, usually before its partner (for example before A05 in 20 of 100 and before A12 in 22 of 100 traces), with an interleaved-or-repeated share of about 18–33 in 100. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007]
- The immediate succession A08→A03 and A09→A03 disappeared: before, these pairs were adjacent (distance 1) in about half of their observations; afterwards adjacency never occurs and the pairs appear mostly at the largest measured distance (about 30–35%). [LAG002, LAG005]
- Routing also shifted where A01 is not involved: A16's outgoing transitions moved from A05 (about 34% to 10%) to A02 (about 9% to 43%); A03's outgoing moved away from A05 (about 44% to 27%) toward A02 and A12 (each rising to about 17%); and A03→A08 pairs are now closer together (distance 2 in about 40% of observations, previously distance 5 in about 43%). [DIR008, DIR002, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

As a possibility supported by the evidence, A01 was introduced into the process flow in the later window (or first became executed there): it now runs in about half of the cases at an early position, is entered from A05, A06, A08, A09 and A12, leads mainly on to A05, A03, A06 and A15, and its insertion ended the direct A08→A03 and A09→A03 succession. The change is shown directly and consistently: participation moved from 0 to about 101 of 200 traces, successor and relation indicators independently show A01's new incoming edges and its precedence over seven partners, and the screening produced 25 qualifying items against roughly 0.43 expected for an unchanged process, far beyond what chance would yield. The evidence cannot show whether A01 existed earlier under a different label, and the position figures are event positions within the trace, not clock times. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, LAG002, LAG005]

Other differences: the rerouting around A16 (toward A02) and A03 (toward A02 and A12) and the closer A03→A08 spacing are not connected to any observed A01 edge and may be a separate shift in how often existing paths are taken; the evidence cannot rule out that they are secondary effects of the same restructuring, nor that part of the shift in the rarely observed A16 transitions (about 47–58 supporting observations) is sampling noise. [DIR002, DIR008, LAG001]
