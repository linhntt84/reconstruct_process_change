### Observed differences

- Before, A40 was followed by exactly one of three activities — A14 (about 38 in 100 of its outgoing transitions), A15 (about 31) or A20 (about 31) — and each of these three then led exclusively to A21. In the later window, A40 leads directly to A21 in all observed outgoing transitions, and the distance from A40 to A21 shortened from two steps to one. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, LAG004]

- The trio A14/A15/A20 still runs once per case (their combined trace shares equal 200 of 200 cases in both windows, with A14 in about 38 of 100 cases before and 31 after, A15 about 31 before and 33 after, A20 about 32 before and 37 after), but it moved from early to late: before, it sat at roughly 31–33% of the trace position; in the later window, at roughly 59–60%. [POS001, POS002, POS003]

- All order relations between the trio and A21/A22 flipped: before, A14, A15 and A20 always ran before A21, A22 and A23 (for example, A14 preceded A21 in 38 of 100 cases, its full occurrence share); in the later window, they always run after A21 and after A22 (A21 precedes A14 in about 31 of 100 cases), while their order versus A23 is now mixed (A14 before A23 in about 16 of 100 cases, A23 before A14 in about 15; A23 before A20 in about 23 of 100). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

- The end of the process was rewired: before, A22, A23 and A27 ran strictly consecutively (A22→A23 and A23→A27 each one step apart, A22→A27 two steps). In the later window, A22 leads to A23 in about 52 of 100 transitions and to one of the trio in about 48; A23 leads to A27 in about 48 and to one of the trio in about 52; each trio member then leads to A23 or to A27 (A20 more often to A27, about 61 in 100). The A22→A27 distance is now three steps in every case. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

- A21, A22 and A23 each shifted slightly earlier in the trace (mean position lower by about 0.02–0.03 of the trace length). [POS004, POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative block A14/A15/A20 — exactly one of which executes in each case — was relocated from before A21 (directly after A40) to the end of the process after A22, where the chosen activity now runs either between A22 and A23 or between A23 and A27; as a result, A40 now flows straight into A21. The successor changes at A40, A22, A23 and the trio show this directly [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006], and the complete order reversal against A21 and A22, the mixed order versus A23, the position shift from about a third to about three-fifths of the trace, and the lag changes (A40→A21 from two to one, A22→A27 from two to three, A22→A23 and A23→A27 now split between one and two steps) all describe the same movement [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]. The 24 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.4 expected by chance in the control-flow family, and the trio's unchanged one-of-three participation rules out a mere shift in how often existing paths are taken.

Other differences: none beyond the main hypothesis; the slight backward shift of A21, A22 and A23 is consistent with it. [POS004, POS005, POS006]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that the early block was removed and an equivalent block inserted late rather than moved, since the activities are anonymous codes and the two windows are finite samples; positions are event indices, so no statement about clock-time timing is possible. [POS001, LAG002]
