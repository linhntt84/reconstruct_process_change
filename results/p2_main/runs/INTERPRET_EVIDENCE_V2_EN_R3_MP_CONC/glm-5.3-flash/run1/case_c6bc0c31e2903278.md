### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the control flow rather than a shift in how often existing paths are taken. A specific possibility consistent with the data: in the earlier window every case executed A03 and A10 (in varying order) and about a third of cases closed with A01; in the later window A01 is no longer observed, A03 and A10 have become mutually exclusive alternatives (exactly one per case, about 50/50), the new activity A09 now sits between A06 and A07 in about 18 in 100 cases, and the new activity A08 forms the closing sequence together with A12 in about 48 in 100 cases. The relational and directional evidence shows this directly: the A03–A10 co-occurrence falls from 100% to 0% while one-of-the-two coverage stays at 100%, and the transitions A06→A09, A09→A07, A12→A08 and A08→A12 appear only in the later window. The volume of qualifying items (49 control-flow items against roughly 0.32 expected for an unchanged process, each difference larger than in about 99.5 of 100 random re-splits) cannot be explained by sampling variation as a whole, although the items overlap and are not independent confirmations. Limitations: non-observation of A01 in the later window shows only that it did not occur in these 200 traces, and the 50/50 A03/A10 split demonstrates exclusivity in this sample but cannot establish whether it is a hard routing constraint or two strong preferences; a competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01, or A03–A10 co-occurrence, still occurs but too rarely to be sampled. [REL012, REL009, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, OCC001, OCC004, OCC005, screening]

Other differences: A04's participation rose from about 37 to about 52 in 100 traces [OCC003]; A07 remains in every trace in both windows but its typical position moved slightly later (median position 0.4 to 0.5) [POS002]; pairs among A03, A07 and A10 that were one or two steps apart earlier are now always directly adjacent (distance one in 100% of observed pairs, versus roughly 53–54% before) [LAG001, LAG004, LAG005].
