### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A26, A27 and A28 each ran in every trace (100 in 100 cases, about once per case, with no fixed order among themselves); in the later window each appears in only about 2 in 100 traces, and their mutual co-occurrence fell from 100% to under 1%. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL036, REL037, REL042]

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A21 were rare (about 1–2 in 100 traces each); in the later window A19 runs in about 30 in 100 traces, A20 in about 33 and A21 in about 38, each once per case, and they practically never occur together — their shares add up to essentially all traces, so each later case contains exactly one of the three. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL013, REL014, REL021]

- The exchange concerns the same flow segment. Earlier, A44 preceded A26/A27/A28 in 97–99 in 100 traces and A26/A27/A28 preceded A29 in 97–99 in 100; later, A44 precedes A19/A20/A21 in about 30, 31 and 37 in 100 traces respectively, and A19/A20/A21 precede A29 in about 30, 31 and 37 in 100. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment always executed between A44 and A29 — the block A26, A27, A28, run once in every earlier case — is now occupied instead by exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities, A19, A20 or A21 (about 30, 33 and 38 in 100 later cases), directly after A44 and before A29, at the same relative position the old block occupied. The support is direct and consistent, with the indicators describing one underlying swap rather than independent confirmations: participation moved from 100% to about 1.5% for the old block and from 1–2% to 30–38% for the alternatives, the old block's co-occurrence collapsed from 100% to under 1%, A44's outgoing transitions redirected from the old block to the alternatives, and the alternatives route onward to A29 in about 95% of observed outgoing transitions. With 72 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 1 expected without a change (0.8), chance sampling cannot account for the ensemble. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL013, REL021, REL036, REL038, REL039, REL047, REL048, DIR007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether A19–A21 are newly added activities or previously rare paths that became frequent, since near-zero participation only means they were almost not observed earlier; the earlier routing profiles of A19–A21 rest on only 2–4 traces, and the roughly 2 in 100 later traces still containing A26/A27/A28 are too few to characterise. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows sampled different case mixes (different proportions of existing variants), which would produce the same pattern. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]
