### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the per-case choice in which exactly one of A19, A20 and A21 executed (each about 30–36 in 100 cases, one per trace) between A44 and A29 no longer runs, and a block of three activities — A26, A27 and A28 — now executes together in every trace in that same slot: A44 precedes the block, the block precedes A29, and the block has no fixed internal order. Supporting signs: the appearance and disappearance are observed directly in both 200-trace windows (60–71 traces vs about 1 for the old trio; 0–1 vs 200 for the new block), the relational and direction items show the new block taking over the old alternatives' relations to A44 and A29, and screening produced 69 qualifying items where roughly 0.75 would be expected without a change — far too many for random re-splits of a stable process, with every item's difference exceeding about 995 in 1000 reference comparisons. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL031, REL032, REL037, REL033, REL034, REL038, REL039, REL042, REL043, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009]

Limitations: zero participation in the later window shows only that A19, A20 and A21 were not observed, not that they were removed from the process; the later outgoing profiles of A19, A20 and A21 each rest on a single trace; and the evidence cannot fully distinguish one replacement from two coinciding changes (the old trio stopping and the new block starting independently), although the new block occupying exactly the old slot between A44 and A29 favours the replacement reading. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
