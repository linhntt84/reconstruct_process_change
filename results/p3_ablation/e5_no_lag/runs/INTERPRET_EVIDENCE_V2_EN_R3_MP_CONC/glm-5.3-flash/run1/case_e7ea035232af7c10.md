### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch made up of A36, A37 and A42 — executed together, in mixed order, in about 46 of 100 earlier traces as an alternative to A27, after A26 and before A31 — now runs in every trace, starting after A25 and finishing before A28 and A26, while A27 always runs after A26 and before A31. Earlier, a trace contained A25, then A28, with A26 later in the trace leading either to A27 alone (about 54 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) or into the group, which closed into A31 (about 46 in 100); in the later window every trace contains A25, the group, A28, A26, A27 and A31, with the group now exiting to A28 instead of A31. The support is direct and consistent: strict order flips between the group and A26/A28, new near-universal co-occurrence with A27 in a fixed order, successor changes at A25, A26 and the group members, participation of all four activities rising to essentially every trace, and matching position shifts. Screening context: 39 items qualified where roughly 0.62 would be expected if nothing had changed — about sixty times the chance expectation — and every item exceeds about 99.5 of 100 random re-splits, so the pattern as a whole is not a sampling artefact. The items nevertheless largely describe one underlying restructuring rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR001, REL011]

Limitations: the relative order of A28 and A26 after the group is not directly observed — no relation or successor item covers that pair, so it rests on position data only; the group's internal order is unfixed in both windows and the evidence cannot show whether it is fixed within individual traces; and the evidence cannot distinguish the group having been moved from the old branch being removed and a new execution of the same activities inserted earlier, since each occurs at most once per trace.
