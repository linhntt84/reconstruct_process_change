### Observed differences

- The largest difference concerns a three-activity block, A19, A20 and A25. In the earlier window each appeared in about 47–48 in 100 traces (94–96 of 200) and ran about 0.48 times per trace; in the later window each appears in about 199 of 200 traces and runs about once per trace. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- Routing around this block changed with it. In the earlier window, A41's outgoing transitions split between A26 (about 45 in 100) and A19 (about 37 in 100); in the later window, about 96 in 100 lead to A19. A26 still occurs in every trace in both windows, but in the later window it follows A20 (A20 before A26 in about 97 in 100 traces, versus about 46 in 100 earlier, when it often followed A41 directly). [DIR006, REL023, POS002]
- The block's internal order is fixed in both windows; what changed is how often it runs. In the later window A19 precedes A25 in about 99 in 100 and A25 precedes A20 in about 98 in 100 traces; earlier the same order held whenever the block ran (A19 before A20 in about 46 in 100 traces against co-occurrence of about 47 in 100), but about 52 in 100 traces contained neither A19 nor A20. [REL018, REL019, REL022, DIR004, DIR005]
- A06 also behaves differently: it appears in about 73 in 100 later-window traces versus about 24 in 100 earlier (about 47 vs 146 traces), sits earlier in the trace (median event position about 0.21 versus 0.39, upper quartile about 0.26 versus 0.91), and its outgoing transitions now go to A16 in about 88 in 100 cases, where earlier they were spread over many targets with A15 the largest at about 21 in 100. A15 now routes about 40 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A06 (about 1 in 100 earlier), with A15 to A16 falling from about 94 to about 58 in 100, and A14 precedes A06 in about 69 in 100 later traces (co-occurrence up from about 24 to about 73 in 100). [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR003, REL001]
- A08 became rarer, from about 20 in 100 traces (40 of 200) to about 7.5 in 100 (15 of 200). [OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 → A26, which in the earlier window ran in only about half of the traces, became an effectively mandatory, fixed-order part of nearly every trace in the later window: A19, A20 and A25 rose from about 48 to about 99.5 in 100 traces, A41 now leads into the block (about 96 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) instead of frequently bypassing it straight to A26 (about 45 in 100 earlier), and A26 is now reached after A20 in about 97 in 100 traces. The order within the segment is essentially unchanged; what changed is that traces omitting A19, A20 and A25 have largely disappeared. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR006, REL018, REL019, REL022, REL023]

This is unlikely to be a sampling artefact: 41 evidence items qualified against about 0.6 expected by chance alone for the 127 units tested, and every item's difference exceeds those seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. Occurrence, direction and relation indicators all describe the same participation shift; the many relation items largely restate one underlying trace behaviour, so they corroborate rather than independently confirm it. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR006, REL018]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows capture a different mix of coexisting process variants — for example, traces that skip the block becoming rarer in the later sample — rather than a modification within one process; the aggregate indicators cannot separate these, nor can they show why the bypass disappeared. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

Other differences: A06 is executed in about 73 in 100 later-window traces (about 24 in 100 earlier), is placed earlier and more consistently in the trace, and its outgoing transitions now concentrate on A16, with A15 routing about 40 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A06 (about 1 in 100 earlier); this placement varies, since A06 precedes A16 in only about 36 in 100 traces with about 34 in 100 interleaved or repeated. A08's participation falls from about 20 to about 7.5 in 100 traces, and A26's event position shifts only slightly later (mean about 0.41 to 0.44) while remaining in every trace. These changes are not part of the main hypothesis above. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR003, REL003, OCC002, POS002]
