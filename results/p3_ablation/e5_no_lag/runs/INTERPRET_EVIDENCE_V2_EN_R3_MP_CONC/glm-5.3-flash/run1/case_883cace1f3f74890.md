### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A19, A20 and A25 were not observed in any of the 200 traces. In the later window, A20 appears in about 41 in 100 traces, A19 in about 30 and A25 in about 30, each at most once per trace. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new choice step operates in the middle of the process: exactly one of the three mutually exclusive activities A19, A20 or A25 executes in essentially every trace (about 41, 30 and 30 in 100 traces respectively), whereas none of them was observed in the earlier window. The step runs after A27 and before A29 — it follows A27 in virtually every co-occurrence and precedes A29 in nearly all — with no fixed order relative to A28, and A27 and A28 now route into it in a large share of cases (A27→A28 falling from about 99 to 44 in 100 transitions; A28→A29 from about 99 to 55). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL002, REL006, REL003, REL007, REL010, REL005, REL009, REL012, REL004, REL008, REL011, DIR004, DIR005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

Supporting signs: the participation change is observed directly (0 of 200 traces earlier versus 59, 82 and 60 of 200 later); the relational items show the alternatives pattern and the ordering directly rather than by inference; the routing changes at A27 and A28 match the same picture; and the screening comparison makes chance an unlikely explanation for the set as a whole — 26 control-flow items qualified where about 0.53 would be expected without a change, roughly fifty times that expectation. The slight position shifts of A27 and A29 are consistent with this reading but are not independent confirmation, as they can reflect the same traces. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, DIR004, DIR005, POS004, POS005]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly means the activities were not observed there, so a previously unexercised variant cannot be distinguished from a genuinely added step; and because the order relative to A28 is mixed, the exact insertion point (before versus after A28) cannot be pinned down. Positions are event positions, not clock times, so no timing claim is made. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL004, REL008, REL011, POS001, POS002, POS003]
