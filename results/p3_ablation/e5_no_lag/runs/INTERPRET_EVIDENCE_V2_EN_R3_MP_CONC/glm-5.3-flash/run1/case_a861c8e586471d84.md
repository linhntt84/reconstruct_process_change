### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative block consisting of A19, A20 and A25 between A41 and A26 was bypassed or removed: in the earlier window a trace reaching A41 ran exactly one of the three and then continued to A26, whereas in the later window A41 is followed directly by A26 and the three activities are essentially unobserved. [DIR004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003] The change is shown directly by the successor change at A41 and is consistent across the occurrence, direction and relation indicators; with 34 qualifying items against roughly 0.58 expected for an unchanged process, the pattern as a whole is not what random sampling between two windows produces, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. [REL007, REL009, REL014, REL004, REL010, REL019] A limitation the evidence cannot resolve: zero participation only means the activities were not observed, so complete removal cannot be distinguished from a drastic reduction to near-zero frequency, and the single later A20 occurrence — which on that one trace continued to A30 rather than A26 — is far too weak to establish a residual or re-routed path.
