### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change in which the activity group A36, A37 and A42 became mandatory and was repositioned. In the earlier window this group ran, in an unfixed internal order, as an optional branch chosen after A26 in about 45 in 100 cases (instead of A27) and then exited to A31; in the later window all three activities run once in essentially every trace, directly after A25 and before A28, A26 and A27, while A26 now always leads to A27, A25 almost never leads directly to A28, and A27 has consequently become mandatory as well. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor profiles at A25, A26 and the trio changed wholesale, and the pairwise order between the trio and A28/A26/A27 flipped, which shows the new placement relationally rather than as a mere shift in how often existing paths are taken. The 39 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.64 expected by chance among the 129 tested units, every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, and a participation move from about 47 to about 100 in 100 traces is beyond what sampling variation between 200-trace windows produces. The many pairwise relation items largely reflect this single restructuring rather than independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR002, REL014, OCC002, POS006]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine relocation of the trio from an equivalent re-wiring in which A25 gained the trio as new successors and A26 lost it while the trio stayed in place; nor can it tell whether the trio executes in parallel or in an unfixed sequence, since both produce the observed mixed order. [REL020, REL021, REL022]

Other differences: A31 now sits slightly later in the trace (mean event position about 0.63 to 0.66), and A27 occasionally occurs twice in one trace (about 3 in 100 traces), both minor next to the main change. [POS005, OCC001]
