### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the activities A20, A22, A23, A24, A25 and A26 — reached from A31 — are executed as one fixed sequence, A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25, usually ending with A25 → A32, whereas in the earlier window their order was mixed or partly reversed and each had several alternative successors. This rests on direct relational evidence: all eight monitored pairs moved from mixed or opposite orders to a single order while both activities still occurred in every trace in both windows, and the successor profiles of seven activities collapsed onto one dominant target. With 44 qualifying items against roughly 0.6 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the evidence set as a whole. [REL014, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL021, DIR007, DIR009, DIR011, DIR013]

The evidence cannot distinguish whether the ordering constraints themselves were tightened or whether the later window simply concentrates on one variant that also existed earlier; the evidence is also limited to control-flow indicators. [REL014, DIR007]

Other differences: A08 became far less frequent (about 72 → 29 in 100 traces), now occurs later in the trace when present, and rarely co-occurs with its former partners; A15 now leads to A16 rather than to A08, and A08's dominant exit to A16 weakened. A10's participation rose from about 7.5 to 22 in 100 traces, on few earlier observations. These participation changes are reported separately and are not part of the main hypothesis.
