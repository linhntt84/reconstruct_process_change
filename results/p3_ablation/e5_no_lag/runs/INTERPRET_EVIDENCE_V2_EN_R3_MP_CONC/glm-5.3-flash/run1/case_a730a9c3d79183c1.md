### Observed differences

- A group of six activities became near-universal. In the earlier window, A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 each ran in about 49–52 of 100 traces (mean occurrences per trace ≈ 0.5); in the later window each runs in about 98–100 of 100 traces (mean ≈ 1.0). [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010]
- Earlier, these six formed two mutually exclusive sub-groups, {A33, A35, A36} and {A37, A38, A39}. Within each sub-group the activities always appeared together (A33 and A35 co-occurred in 49 of 100 traces, neither appeared in 50 of 100, one without the other was almost unobserved), while across sub-groups they were strict alternatives: A33 and A37 shared a trace in only about 1 in 100 cases. In the later window, all six co-occur in about 98 of 100 traces. [REL037, REL039, REL044, REL045, REL050, REL055]
- The later window adds a fixed sequence across the former sub-groups: A33/A35/A36 now precede A37/A38/A39 in about 95–98 of 100 joint traces (e.g., A33 before A37 in 97 of 100), whereas earlier such pairs never co-occurred. Order within each sub-group stays mixed in both windows (A33 before A35 in 46 of 100, A35 before A33 in 50 of 100). Successor profiles match: A30's outgoing transitions now go almost only to A35, A33 and A36 (previously spread over all six), and A35/A36 now also lead on to A37, A38 and A39. [REL039, REL045, REL047, REL050, REL052, REL037, REL055, DIR005, DIR007, DIR008]
- The block's surroundings are now fixed as well: A14, A15 and A30 precede it in almost every later trace (co-occurrence 98–100 of 100; A30 before A33 in 97 of 100, versus about 47–49 of 100 earlier, when the block ran in only half the traces), and A31 follows it (A31 after A33 in 97 of 100). - A04 moved the other way: participation fell from 59 to about 26 of 100 traces, its occurrences sit later in the trace (mean relative position 0.20 → 0.38, upper quartile 0.17 → 0.67), and its outgoing transitions shifted from about 90 in 100 leading to A15 to about 40 in 100, spread over many targets; A14's transitions now lead mainly to A15 (about 79 in 100) rather than to A04 (about 12 in 100, down from 57). A14, A15, A30, A31, A47 and A48 now appear without A04 in about 74 of 100 traces, whereas earlier A04 co-occurred with each of them in 59 of 100. - Smaller shifts: A02, A08 and A10 each appear in roughly three to four times as many traces (about 6→18, 8→18 and 6→22 of 100) with redistributed successors; these rest on only 11–15 before-window traces each. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment made up of A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 changed from an optional, two-way alternative — in the earlier window it executed in about half of the cases, either as {A33, A35, A36} or as {A37, A38, A39}, never both — into a near-universal sequential block in the later window: A33/A35/A36 (mutually unordered) followed by A37/A38/A39 (mutually unordered), placed after A14/A15/A30 and before A31 in almost every trace. Supporting signs: participation of all six activities roughly doubled (e.g., A33 in 98 vs 197 of 200 traces), pairs that were strict alternatives now co-occur in about 98 of 100 traces with a consistent order, the surrounding relations to A14/A15/A30 and A31 hold in 97–99 of 100 later traces, and the control-flow screening produced 94 qualifying items where fewer than 1 (0.84) would be expected without change. Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a modification of one process from a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants (for example, a variant containing only one sub-group nearly disappearing from the population), and the placement of A15 relative to the block is only partly fixed (A15 precedes it in about 56–59 of 100 later traces and interleaves in about 41 of 100). Other differences: A04 became rarer (59 → about 26 of 100 traces), occurs later in traces, and its strong A04→A15 edge weakened (about 90 → 40 of its outgoing transitions), with A14 now leading to A15 instead of A04; A02, A08 and A10 appear in more traces than before, though on few before-window observations.
