### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a swap in which of two alternative activity groups runs. Before, A21, A22 and A23 each appeared in about 75 in 100 traces and usually together in the same trace (co-occurrence about 0.75), while A24, A25 and A26 each appeared in only about 25 in 100. In the later window this reversed: A24, A25 and A26 each ran in about 76 in 100 traces, usually together, while A21, A22 and A23 each dropped to about 25 in 100. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, REL013, REL021, REL034, REL039]
- The two groups remained mutually exclusive alternatives in both windows: pairs spanning the groups (e.g., A21 with A24, A23 with A26) almost never co-occurred in either window, but which group executed flipped — traces with A21 but not A24 fell from about 75 in 100 to about 25 in 100, while A24 without A21 rose from about 25 to about 76 in 100. [REL015, REL017, REL022, REL024, REL028, REL030]
- A20 appeared in essentially every trace in both windows and normally preceded the active group: before, about 74 in 100 traces had A20 followed by A21/A22/A23; after, about 74 in 100 had A20 followed by A24/A25/A26. A20's outgoing transitions shifted accordingly, from roughly three quarters leading into A21–A23 to roughly three quarters leading into A24–A26. [REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, DIR003]
- Downstream activities kept their overall presence but changed companions: A30 co-occurred with A21/A22/A23 in about 75 in 100 traces before but with A24/A25/A26 in about 76 in 100 after; A32 and A33 co-occurred with the first group in about 48 in 100 traces before versus about 15 in 100 after, and with the second group in about 17 in 100 before versus about 52 in 100 after. [REL018, REL025, REL031, REL036, REL040, REL043, REL019, REL020, REL037, REL038]
- A10 became more frequent and later: it appeared in about 6 in 100 traces before versus about 21 in 100 after, its median relative position moved from about 0.60 to about 0.86 of the trace, and its outgoing transitions concentrated on A33 (about 90% after versus about 27% before); A32 now leads to A10 in about 20 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions versus about 1 in 100 before. [OCC002, POS001, DIR002, DIR004]
- A02 nearly disappeared, from about 9 in 100 traces (18 traces) to about 1 in 100 (2 traces), with a changed outgoing profile; the after-window picture rests on only two traces. [OCC001, DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the frequencies of two existing alternative branches swapped: before, roughly three in four traces executed the branch in which A21, A22 and A23 run together after A20 (with A30, A32 and A33 following), and roughly one in four executed the branch with A24, A25 and A26; in the later window these shares reversed to about 25% and about 76%. This qualifies as a shift between existing alternatives because all six activities and both branches were observed in substantial shares in both windows (49–152 traces each) and only the shares changed, with the mutual exclusivity between the groups and the A20-before-group order preserved. Supporting signs are the mirrored participation flips, the flipped co-occurrence patterns within and across the groups, A20's redistributed outgoing transitions, and a screening yield (59 qualifying items versus about 0.63 expected without change) far beyond what an unchanged process produces. Limitations: the many relation items describe one underlying behaviour rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply sample two pre-existing case variants in shifted proportions rather than a changed routing rule. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, REL007, REL010, REL013, REL034, DIR003]

Other differences: A10 became about three to four times more frequent, appears later within traces, and now leads almost exclusively to A33, while A32 leads to A10 in about a fifth of its observed outgoing transitions; the evidence does not tie this directly to the branch swap. A02 nearly vanished (18 to 2 traces), which with only two after-window observations may be a rare-variant fluctuation. [OCC002, POS001, DIR002, DIR004, OCC001, DIR001]
