### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the block of activities A19, A20 and A25 is executed before A26 instead of after it: the three activities now run in the first third of the trace, are entered mainly from A08 — whose own participation rose from about 13 to about 30 in 100 traces — and from A45, and lead into A26, whereas before they ran in the last third after A26 and continued into A28 or A32; the remaining route via A27 now runs almost exclusively A27 → A28 → A32. The order reversal with A26, the position shift of all three activities and the successor rewiring show this directly and consistently. [REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, POS002, POS003, POS004, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

The surrounding rewiring matches the same movement: A08's transitions now concentrate on the block, A45 reaches A26 through it, and A27 and A28 lost their branches into the block. The 26 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.6 expected for an unchanged process, so chance sampling cannot account for the ensemble — although the many relation and position items describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]

Limitation: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine relocation of the block from a complete switch between two pre-existing behaviours, because the new order was seen in at most about 1 in 100 traces before and the old order in 0 in 100 after; A08's earlier successor profile also rests on only about 28 observed transitions, so its earlier shares are coarse. [REL005, REL007, REL009, DIR001, DIR002]

Other differences: A26 and A27 now occur slightly later within traces (mean position up about 0.03–0.04), and the successor profiles of A48 and A50 changed only marginally (A48→A49 about 89→99 in 100; A50→A51 about 89→98 in 100), so no further structural difference is indicated at these activities.
