### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A21 each ran in roughly a third of traces (about 70, 60 and 72 of 200 traces) and almost never together (co-occurrence at most about 1 in 200); their shares sum to about all traces, so an earlier trace typically contained exactly one of the three. In the later window each of them appears in only about 1–2 of 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL008]
- A26, A27 and A28 were not observed at all in the earlier window; in the later window each occurs in all 200 traces, about once per trace. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- The new trio is executed together in essentially every later trace but without a fixed order among its members: for each pair, each order occurs in about half of the traces (for example A26 before A27 in about 51 in 100 traces, A27 before A26 in about 49 in 100). [REL019, REL020, REL023]
- A44's successors changed almost completely: earlier, about 85 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions went to A19, A20 or A21 (roughly 30, 30 and 25 in 100); later, about 85 in 100 go to A26, A27 or A28 (roughly 31, 27 and 26 in 100). This rests on about 200 outgoing-transition observations in each window. [DIR007]
- Placement around the block also changed: in the later window A44 precedes A26, A27 and A28 in about 99 in 100 traces, and each of A26, A27, A28 precedes A29 in about 98–99 in 100 traces; A26/A27/A28 also lead onward to A29 in their outgoing profiles. In the earlier window A44 preceded A19/A20/A21 in about 30–36 in 100 traces, and A19/A20/A21 preceded A29 in about 30–35 in 100 traces. [REL022, REL025, REL027, REL021, REL024, REL026, REL007, REL013, REL018, REL006, REL012, REL017, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- A29 itself occurs in every trace in both windows, but its mean event position moved slightly later, from about 0.40 to about 0.44 of the trace length (a positional shift, not evidence of a clock-time move). [POS007]
- The successor profiles observed for A19, A20 and A21 in the later window rest on only 1–2 traces each and are too weak to interpret. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A structural replacement of the segment between A44 and A29 is supported: in the earlier window every trace took exactly one of three alternative activities (A19, A20 or A21), fed by A44 and followed by A29; in the later window that choice is essentially gone (about 1–2 of 200 traces each) and a block of three activities (A26, A27, A28) runs in every trace in no fixed order between them, again with A44 before it and A29 after it. This is supported directly and consistently across indicator families: participation collapsed for A19/A20/A21 (about 70/60/72 of 200 traces down to about 2/1/1) and emerged for A26/A27/A28 (0 up to 200 of 200) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]; A44's redirection is observed with near-full support in both windows [DIR007]; and the relational items show A44 before the new trio and the trio before A29 in about 99 in 100 later traces [REL021, REL022, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027]. The volume of qualifying items (47 against fewer than 1 expected without a change) also cannot be explained as chance sampling. Limitations: with anonymous activity codes the evidence cannot show whether A26/A27/A28 are genuinely new steps or stand-ins replacing the old alternatives, the later routing of A19/A20/A21 rests on 1–2 traces, and the small residual occurrences leave open that the old alternative path still exists but is now rarely taken.
