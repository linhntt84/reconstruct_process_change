### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block A31–A32–A37 became an optional alternative to A22 and now runs after A21 instead of between A20 and A23. In the earlier window, every trace ran A20 into the block, which preceded A23, A21, A22 and A26, with A21 always followed by A22 and A22 by A26; in the later window, A20 leads directly to A23, and A21 has become the branching point — about 54 in 100 traces continue with A22 alone and about 47 in 100 with the block, which then runs in varying internal order before A26 — while A22 and the block almost never both run. [OCC001-OCC004, DIR001-DIR005, REL001-REL022]

The pattern is direct and internally consistent: the collapse of co-occurrence between A22 and each block member to about 1 in 200 traces with one branch present in virtually every trace, the preserved cohesion of the block, the flipped before/after relations with A21 and A23, and the successor changes at A20, A21 and the block all describe one restructuring, and the participation drops and position shifts line up with it. The ensemble also far exceeds what chance produces: 39 of the 88 tested control-flow units qualified against 0.44 expected without change, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001-OCC004, DIR001-DIR005, POS001-POS008, REL001-REL022]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show what determines which branch a later case takes, and the order flips are compatible with two readings — the block moving after A21, or A23 and A21 moving ahead of a stationary block; the relational data show the flipped orders directly but cannot label which activity moved. The branch shares (about 54/47) also carry sampling error in 200-trace windows, and the residual co-occurrence of A22 with a block member in about 1 of 200 later traces could be a rare remaining path or noise. [REL006-REL008, REL011-REL016, DIR001-DIR005]

Other differences: small mean-position shifts for A20 (about 0.34 to 0.36), A22 (about 0.63 to 0.59) and A26 (about 0.66 to 0.63), with unchanged participation for A20 and A26; the evidence does not point to an independent behavioural change behind these shifts beyond the restructuring described above.
