### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the block A36/A37/A42 was repositioned and made conditional. In the earlier window every trace ran A25, then the trio in mixed order, then A28, A26, A27 and A31; in the later window A25 leads directly to A28, and after A26 the trace takes either A27 alone (about 53 in 100 traces) or the trio A36/A37/A42 (about 47 in 100), with both branches leading on to A31. A27 and the trio thus changed from always running in sequence (trio first) to being mutually exclusive alternatives, and the trio moved from before A28/A26 to after A26, being skipped in about half of the traces. [DIR001, DIR002, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL014, REL020, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The support is direct and consistent: the order flips between the trio and A26/A28 are observed relationally rather than inferred from positions alone, the successor changes at A25 and at the trio are visible in the transition profiles, and the participation drops rest on 94–107 traces per activity in the later window. [DIR001, DIR003, REL006, REL014, OCC002, OCC003]

For screening context, 39 control-flow items qualified against about 0.63 expected if nothing had changed, so chance alone cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [screening]

Competing explanations the evidence cannot rule out: what determines which branch a trace takes (for example case characteristics versus random routing) is not visible in these indicators, and it remains possible that in traces without the trio another activity not covered by the evidence takes its place. Many items also describe the same underlying trace behaviour — the participation drops and the co-occurrence drops overlap — so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC002, REL011, REL020]
