### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block A25 – A26 – A31, previously executed exactly once per trace in a fixed order (A25 before A31, with A31 always followed by A20), became repeated in the later window: each activity now occurs about 3.5 times per trace, A31 predominantly flows back to A25 (about 71 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) instead of forward to A20, and the A25/A31 pair is interleaved or repeated in about 68 in 100 traces. The signs are direct and consistent: repetition is shown by the occurrence counts, the new dominant A31-to-A25 transition by the direction profile, and the order change by the relation profile, with the 68% recurrence matching the 68% interleaved-or-repeated share; 7 qualifying items against roughly 0.29 expected for an unchanged process cannot be explained by chance as a whole, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: the relation indicator cannot separate strict repetition from interleaving, A26 has no directional or relational evidence, so its place within the repeated block is inferred only from its identical repetition pattern, and the successor shares are over outgoing transitions rather than cases. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a mixed trace population — about two thirds of later traces following a variant in which the block repeats and the rest keeping the old single-pass path — rather than one uniform repetition; the matching 0.68 figures likely describe the same traces, so the indicators are not independent confirmations.
