### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, two alternative behaviours followed A26: in about 52 in 100 traces A26 was followed by A27 and then A28, while in the remaining ~48 in 100 traces A26 led into a branch containing A32, A33 and A38, which ran together in varying order and each could precede A28. [DIR001, REL001, REL005, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- In the later window, that A32/A33/A38 branch has practically disappeared: each of the three activities appears in only about 1 in 200 traces, down from about 97–98 in 200 (roughly 49 in 100) earlier. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A27 changed from an alternative present in about half of the traces (about 103 in 200, roughly once per trace when present) to near-universal: about 199 in 200 traces, about once per trace. [OCC001]
- Routing out of A26 shifted accordingly: A26 led to A27 in about 51 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions before and about 97 in 100 after, while its transitions into A32, A33 and A38 (together about 48 in 100 before) are no longer observed beyond a single trace. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004]
- Earlier, A27 and each of A32, A33 and A38 were mutually exclusive alternatives (co-occurrence about 0 in 100; each side alone about 50 in 100); in the later window A27 occurs without them in about 99.5 in 100 traces. [REL006, REL007, REL008]
- The A27→A28 link itself is stable: about 97 in 100 of A27's outgoing transitions went to A28 before and about 98–99 in 100 after. [DIR002, REL005]
- A28 now appears slightly earlier within traces (mean relative position about 0.55 before vs about 0.53 after); this is a positional shift consistent with shorter traces and does not by itself show the activity was moved. [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch formed by A32, A33 and A38 — previously executed in about half of the cases between A26 and A28, with the three activities in varying order — is no longer executed in practice, so that virtually every case now runs A26 → A27 → A28 and A27 has become mandatory rather than an alternative to that branch. The signs are direct and consistent: participation of A32, A33 and A38 collapsed from about 49 in 100 traces to about 0.5 in 100 while A27 rose from about 52 to about 99.5 in 100 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]; A26's outgoing transitions moved from roughly half leading to A27 to almost all [DIR001, REL002, REL003, REL004]; and the relational items show the earlier exclusivity between A27 and the branch activities as well as the branch's disappearance from A28's predecessors [REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011]. With 26 qualifying items against roughly 0.54 expected if nothing had changed, chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the branch still exists but runs very rarely: one later-window trace still contains A32, A33 and A38, and every after-window statement about the branch's internal routing rests on that single trace. [DIR003, DIR004, REL012, REL013, REL014]

Other differences: A28's relative position moved slightly earlier within traces, consistent with traces shortening once the branch stopped running, and A26's rare transition to A06 (about 1 in 100 before) is no longer observed. [POS001, DIR001]
