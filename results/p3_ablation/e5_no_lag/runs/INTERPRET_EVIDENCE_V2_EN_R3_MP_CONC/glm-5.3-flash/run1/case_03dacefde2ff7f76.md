### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the group of activities A19, A20 and A25 is executed after A26 and A27 rather than before them: earlier the three activities ran at about one third of the trace, were entered mainly from A45 and flowed into A26, whereas in the later window they run at about three fifths of the trace, are entered from A27 and A28, and flow into A28 and A32, with A45 now flowing into A26 directly. The order change is shown directly by the pairwise relations for all six group-versus-A26/A27 combinations, the positional shift agrees across all three activities, and the successor changes match the same rewiring; every item's difference exceeds all but about 1 in 200 random re-splits, and 19 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.56 expected by chance makes the pattern as a whole unlikely under no change — though the items describe one underlying difference, not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of two existing variants (one with the group before A26, one with it after), which would produce the same indicator pattern; the mixed order against A28 also leaves the group's exact placement relative to A28 open.
