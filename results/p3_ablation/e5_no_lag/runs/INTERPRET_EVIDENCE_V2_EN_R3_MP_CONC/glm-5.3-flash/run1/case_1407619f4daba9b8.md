### Observed differences

- A22 and the group A31, A32, A37 became effectively mandatory. In the earlier window A22 ran in about 54 in 100 traces and each of A31, A32, A37 in about 46 in 100; in the later window A22 ran in all 200 traces and each trio member in about 199 of 200. None of these activities repeats within a trace in either window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

- The trio's internal order remained unfixed in both windows: A31, A32 and A37 occur in both orders in similar shares (for example, A31 before A32 in about half of the traces containing both, in each window). [REL020, REL021, REL022]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural reordering of the segment around A20, A21, A22, A23, A26, A31, A32 and A37: the group A31/A32/A37, previously an optional alternative to A22 that ran after A21 and before A26, now runs as a mandatory block directly after A20 and before A23 and A21, while A22 became mandatory after A21; as a result, A26 is now reached after A22 rather than directly from the trio. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL006, REL011, REL014, REL010]

The reordering is shown directly rather than inferred from frequencies: the direction profiles show the rewired edges (A20→A23 replaced by A20→the trio, the trio's exit to A26 replaced by an exit to A23, A21 now always to A22), the relation items show the change from alternatives to always-executed-together and the order flips around A21 and A23, and the participation and position shifts agree with the same picture. Thirty-nine items qualified against fewer than one expected for an unchanged process, and every item's difference exceeds those of about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits. [DIR001, DIR003, REL011, REL006, OCC001, POS006, screening]

Limitations: the edges out of A23 and the relation between A23 and A21 are not directly observed, so their exact placement after the trio rests on event positions and indirect relations; a competing reading the evidence cannot rule out is that unobserved activities sit between these steps (only eight tested activities appear in the evidence), and no claim about clock-time timing is possible. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS006]

Other differences: A20's mean event position shifted slightly earlier (about 0.36 to 0.34) and the dispersion of positions narrowed for most observed activities, indicating more homogeneous trace structures in the later window; these accompany the reordering above rather than forming a separate change.
