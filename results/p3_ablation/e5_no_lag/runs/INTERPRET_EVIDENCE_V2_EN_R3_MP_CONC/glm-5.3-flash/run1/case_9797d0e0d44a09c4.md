- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 each appeared in about 47 in 100 traces (94–95 of 200) and occurred at most once per trace; in the later window, all three appear in about 995 in 1000 traces (199 of 200), still exactly once per trace, with no repetition in either window. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the sequential block A19 → A25 → A20, executed between A41 and A26, changed from optional to essentially mandatory. In the earlier window the block ran in about 47 in 100 traces and was skipped in the other ~53, with A41 leading directly to A26; in the later window it runs in about 995 in 1000 traces, once per activity, in the same order as before, and the direct A41 → A26 skip survives in only about 1 in 200 traces. The change is shown directly by participation (about 47% to about 99.5% for all three activities, with repetition unchanged) and by A41's routing, and the relation items agree; the 22 qualifying control-flow items far exceed the roughly 0.56 expected by chance, so sampling fluctuation cannot explain the pattern as a whole. Limitations: the many relation items largely re-express one trace-level change rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence shows the skip route became rare, not that it was removed. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, REL003, REL004, REL007, REL008, REL012, REL013]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the direct A41 → A26 route still exists but almost no case now qualifies for it (a shift in routing share or case mix) rather than the skip having been structurally closed; the observed behaviour looks the same under both readings. [DIR002, REL003]

Other differences: A45 became fully nested within A19's occurrence (earlier about 33 in 100 traces had A45 without A19, now almost none), and A42's order relative to the trio became more mixed; both plausibly follow from the block becoming mandatory rather than being separate changes. The small position shifts of A41 and A26 are consistent with the block always executing but, being position-based, do not establish a timing change.
