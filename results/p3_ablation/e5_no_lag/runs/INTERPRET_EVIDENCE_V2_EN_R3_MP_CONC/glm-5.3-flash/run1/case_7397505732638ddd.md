### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The 21 qualifying items are roughly 60 times the about 0.35 expected for an unchanged process (all in the control-flow family), and every item's difference exceeded about 199 of 200 random re-splits, so the ensemble is not what an unchanged process typically produces. The evidence supports the hypothesis that the strict sequencing between the two activity groups was reversed or loosened: the group A19 → A21 → A20, which previously always ran after A17 → A15 → A18, now runs before it in a large share of traces (A19 before A18 in about 85 in 100, before A15 in about 74 in 100; A21 before A18 in about 68 in 100), A26 now starts with A19 in about half of its outgoing transitions, and A18 can be followed directly by A27, which previously closed the trace only after A20 — together consistent with roughly half of the executions running the second group first. This is a reordering, not a participation change: all six activities still occur together in essentially every trace in both windows, and each group's internal order remains the dominant pattern. The relational items show the order change directly, the directional items place the redistribution exactly at the group boundary, and the positional items shift in the matching direction [REL001, REL003, REL006, REL008, DIR003, DIR005, DIR007, POS003, POS004].

The evidence cannot establish a single new sequence: the later-window pair shares (some majority-reversed, others near even) do not fit one clean swapped variant, so the later window likely mixes several orders, and whether these are a few new variants or a generally loosened ordering cannot be decided from these aggregates; directional shares are taken over outgoing transitions rather than cases, so per-case sequence frequencies cannot be derived. Position results alone would not demonstrate movement, but here the relational and directional items corroborate them [REL004, REL005, REL007, DIR002, POS001].

Other differences: none — all 21 items concern the same seven activities and the same group boundary, so they most likely reflect one underlying difference rather than independent changes; the agreeing indicator families should not be read as independent confirmations [DIR001, POS001, REL001].
