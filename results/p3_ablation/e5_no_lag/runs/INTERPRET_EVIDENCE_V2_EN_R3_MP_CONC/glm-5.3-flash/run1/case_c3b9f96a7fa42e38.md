### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment formed by A35, A36 and A41 was repositioned from before A27 and A26 to after them: earlier the flow ran A25 → {A35/A36/A41} → A27 → A26 → A30, while in the later window it runs A25 → A27 → A26 → {A35/A36/A41} → A30. The order reversal is observed directly for all six pairs between the two segments, with co-occurrence unchanged at 100 in 100 traces; the successor changes at A25, A26 and the three group members match the swap at both boundaries; and the position shifts agree in direction for all five activities. Screening produced 21 qualifying items against roughly 0.6 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. Limitations: the link between A27 and A26, and the unordered internal sequence of A35/A36/A41 (which persists in both windows), are inferred from positions and transition shares rather than a directly observed pair relation, and DIR shares are measured over outgoing transitions rather than cases. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is coincidental re-routing in an unchanged process, though a uniform reversal across six pairs with about 200 co-occurring traces each makes this unlikely.
