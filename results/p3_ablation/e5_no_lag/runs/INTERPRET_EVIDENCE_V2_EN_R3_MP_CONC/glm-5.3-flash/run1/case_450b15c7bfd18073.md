The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, an alternative route between A21 and A23 executes the previously unobserved activities A27, A28 and A33 — each once, in varying order — in about half of the cases, in place of A22, which ran in every case before and now runs in the remaining half; A21 and A23 themselves still run in every case.
