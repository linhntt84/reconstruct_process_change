### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the set of alternative activities A19, A20 and A25, which previously ran between A27/A28 and A29 in roughly 30–40% of cases, is effectively no longer executed, so that traces now proceed directly from A27 through A28 to A29. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, DIR006]

The support is direct and consistent: the participation drop is observed for all three activities, the bypass is visible in the successor profiles of A27 and A28, each backed by essentially all traces in both windows, and the relational items confirm the block previously sat after A27 and before A29. With 26 qualifying items against roughly 0.5 expected if nothing had changed, the pattern as a whole is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. The main limitation is that the evidence cannot distinguish a complete removal from execution in a residual share of about 1 in 200 traces, and the behaviour around those rare occurrences cannot be established from single-trace profiles. [REL003, REL005, REL010, REL012, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

Other differences: A10's outgoing routing was redistributed across many more targets, a change the evidence shows but does not tie to the disappearance of the block [DIR001]; A27 and A29 shifted slightly in relative position [POS004, POS005].
