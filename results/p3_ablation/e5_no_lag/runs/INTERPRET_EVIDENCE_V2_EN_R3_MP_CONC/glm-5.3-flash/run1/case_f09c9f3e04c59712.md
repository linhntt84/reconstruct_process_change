### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the two alternative blocks exchanged their places around the unchanged A20 → A24 core: the exclusive choice among A14, A16 and A19 — previously the closing step after A24, each alternative leading to A25 — now runs at the start of the case and feeds into A20, while the always-executed group A15/A17/A18 — previously run before A20 — now runs after A24 and closes into A25. The reordering is shown directly by relational evidence, with order shares flipping between 100% and 0% rather than shifting partially, and it is corroborated by position shifts of roughly a quarter to a third of the case for all six activities and by successor changes at both block boundaries that match this rearrangement exactly; participation is stable where it should be (A15/A17/A18 in 100 in 100 cases in both windows, and exactly one of A14/A16/A19 per case). The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process produces — 38 items against roughly 0.42 expected — though most items express this single rearrangement rather than independent changes. [REL001, REL008, REL009, REL015, REL018, REL010, DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS002, POS005]

The reordering itself is observed directly, so chance is not a plausible account of it; the remaining limitations are that positions are event positions, so the change is established for sequence rather than clock time, and the internal order of the A15/A17/A18 group can only be read from transition shares, which look similar in both windows. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, POS002]

Other differences: the routing frequencies within the alternative block moved toward A14 (about 31 to 44 in 100 cases), with A16 (about 38 to 29 in 100) and A19 (about 32 to 28 in 100) chosen less often; the evidence cannot rule out that part of this share shift is sampling variation between the two 200-case windows.
