### Observed differences

- The three alternative activities A14, A15 and A20 keep their participation: their shares of cases sum to 100 in 100 in both windows (earlier about 38, 31 and 32 in 100 cases; later about 31, 33 and 37 in 100), consistent with exactly one of the three running per case throughout. - In the later window the three alternatives run much later in the case: their mean position moves from about one-third of the trace (0.31–0.33) to about three-fifths (0.59–0.60), a shift of roughly 0.27–0.29. [POS001, POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the block of alternative activities A14, A15 and A20 — exactly one of which runs in every case — is executed at a later position: it previously sat between A40 and A21, preceded A21, A22 and A23 and always continued into A21, whereas in the later window it follows A21 and A22, sits around A23 without a fixed order relative to it, continues into A23 or A27, and A40 feeds directly into A21. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003]

The hypothesis rests on direct and mutually consistent signs: the order reversal is shown directly by the relation profiles, the rewiring by the successor profiles, and both match the position shift; the unchanged participation rules out reading this as the alternatives becoming optional or as existing paths being taken with different frequencies. The 20 qualifying items (all control-flow, from 77 tested units) far exceed the roughly 0.38 expected by chance for an unchanged process, and each item's difference is larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish one relocation of the block from a removal plus re-insertion elsewhere, nor a single rewiring from several separate ones, and it leaves the block's exact placement around A23 open because both orders occur in the later window (A14 before A23 in about 16 in 100 cases, A23 before A14 in about 15 in 100); activities between A21 and A22 are not covered, and the many indicators partly restate one underlying difference rather than confirming it independently. The competing explanation that remains is sampling variation across the two 200-case windows, which the item count makes unlikely for the evidence as a whole but which could still account for small components such as the slight position advances of A21, A22 and A23.
