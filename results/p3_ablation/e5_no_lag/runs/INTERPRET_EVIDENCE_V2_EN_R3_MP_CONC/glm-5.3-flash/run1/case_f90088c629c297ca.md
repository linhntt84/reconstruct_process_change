### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window a block of three mutually exclusive alternative activities — A14, A15 and A20 — is executed between A36 and A21: each trace now picks exactly one of them (A14 in about 37 in 100 traces, A20 in about 34, A15 in about 29), always in the order A36 → chosen activity → A21, whereas in the earlier window A36 was followed directly by A21 in essentially every trace. This reading is direct and consistent across indicator families: occurrence shows the three activities appearing, direction shows A36's outgoing flow shifting from A21 alone to the three activities, and the relation items show both the exclusivity among the three and their fixed placement between A36 and A21. The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process would produce: 20 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.36 expected by chance.

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window only means the activities were not observed there, so the evidence cannot distinguish genuinely added activities from ones that previously occurred too rarely to appear in a 200-trace sample. The small residual direct A36→A21 share (about 1 in 100) and the slight later position of A21 fit the insertion but do not independently confirm it.
