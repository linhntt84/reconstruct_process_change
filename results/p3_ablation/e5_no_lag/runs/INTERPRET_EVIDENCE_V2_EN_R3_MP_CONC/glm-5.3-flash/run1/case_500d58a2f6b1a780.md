### Observed differences

- The three activities A25, A26 and A31 stopped repeating. In the earlier window each appeared in every trace, about 3.4 times per trace on average, and recurred within the same trace in about 75 in 100 traces. In the later window they still appear in every trace, but only about 1 time per trace, with recurrence in essentially none (about 0.5 in 100). Participation did not change — only the number of repetitions per trace did. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A31's outgoing transitions shifted away from A25. Earlier, about 71 in 100 of A31's observed outgoing transitions went to A25 and about 29 in 100 to A20; in the later window, about 96 in 100 go to A20 and only about 4 in 100 to A25 (based on about 690 outgoing transitions earlier and about 210 later). [DIR001]
- The relation between A25 and A31 changed from interleaved/repeated to a fixed order. In both windows the two co-occur in every trace; earlier they were interleaved or repeated in about 75 in 100 traces (A25 first in the remaining 25 in 100), while later A25 comes before A31 in about 995 in 1000 traces. [REL001]
- The relative positions of A25 and A31 are roughly unchanged (mean about 0.44 earlier vs 0.41 later for A25; about 0.48 in both windows for A31), but their spread narrowed, with the standard deviation halving from about 0.12 to about 0.06 for both activities. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Main hypothesis: the repeated execution of the A25–A26–A31 block stopped. In the earlier window the block ran as a repeating cycle: A31 returned to A25 in about 7 in 10 of its outgoing transitions, so A25, A26 and A31 each executed about 3.4 times per case, and A25 and A31 interleaved in about 3 of 4 cases. In the later window A31 proceeds to A20 in about 96 in 100 of its transitions instead of returning to A25, each of the three activities executes once per case, and A25 strictly precedes A31. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001]

The repetition drop, the successor change at A31 and the ordering change show the same behaviour directly and consistently, and the seven qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.29 expected by chance among 59 tested units, so chance cannot account for the set as a whole. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001]

Limitations: the three occurrence items carry identical values and describe one shared behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations, and the positional changes are consistent with the same single-execution behaviour rather than independent confirmation. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is a general shortening of traces (fewer repetitions across the whole process) rather than the specific stopping of this cycle, since occurrence evidence covers only these three activities — although the redirection of A31's transitions to A20 speaks for the cycle-specific reading. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001]
