### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a near-complete reversal between two alternative groups of activities. In the earlier window, A21, A22 and A23 each ran in about 75 in 100 traces, while A24, A25 and A26 each ran in about 25–26 in 100; in the later window the shares are reversed, with A21–A23 at about 25 in 100 and A24–A26 at about 75–77 in 100. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009]

- The two groups behave as alternatives at one shared choice point. A21 and A24 (likewise A22 vs A25 and A23 vs A26) practically never share a trace in either window (co-occurrence at most about 2 in 100): earlier, about 74–75 in 100 traces contained A21 but not A24; later, about 74–75 in 100 contained A24 but not A21. Within each group the three activities occur together or not at all, in no fixed order among themselves (A21/A22 co-occur in 75 in 100 traces earlier and 25 in 100 later, with both orders about equally common; A24/A25 show the mirror image). [REL025, REL027, REL036, REL044, REL049, REL055]

- Both groups keep the same slot in the flow, so the paths themselves did not move. A19 and A20, which occur in essentially every trace, precede whichever group runs (A19 before A21 in about 73 in 100 earlier traces; A19 before A24 in about 75 in 100 later traces), and the running group precedes A34/A35 in about 73–76 in 100 in both windows. A20's observed outgoing transitions flipped from about 75 in 100 leading into A21/A22/A23 to about 75 in 100 leading into A24/A25/A26. [REL013, REL016, REL032, REL053, REL054, DIR002]

- A06 changed both frequency and placement. It ran in about 17 in 100 traces earlier and about 53 in 100 later; its median position moved from about 48% to about 96% of the trace's event positions. Its observed outgoing transitions now lead to A35 in about 90 in 100 cases (earlier about 12 in 100, spread over many successors with none above about 21 in 100), and A34 now leads to A06 in about 50 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (earlier about 2 in 100, with about 89 in 100 going straight to A35). In the later window, roughly half of all traces end A34 → A06 → A35 where they previously ended A34 → A35 directly. [OCC002, POS001, DIR001, DIR005, REL011, REL012]

- Two rare activities lost participation: A02 fell from about 17 to about 7–8 in 100 traces and A08 from about 13 to about 5 in 100; both rest on few observations (about 34→15 and 27→10 traces). [OCC001, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two existing alternative paths swapped their selection frequencies: earlier, about three in four cases executed the block A21–A22–A23 (the three activities run together, in no fixed order) and about one in four executed A24–A25–A26; in the later window the shares are reversed. The support is direct and consistent across occurrence, direction and relation indicators: the six participations swap symmetrically, the blocks remain mutually exclusive alternatives while staying internally unordered, both blocks occupy the same position between the near-universal A19/A20 and the near-universal A29/A34/A35, and A20's outgoing split flipped from about 75% into A21–A23 to about 75% into A24–A26. Because both paths are well observed in both windows and no activity is bypassed, added or removed, this reads as the same two existing paths being taken with different frequencies rather than one path replacing the other. Screening also flagged 79 items where fewer than one (0.72) would be expected by chance in a stable process, so the overall volume of differences is far beyond random splitting. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of cases reaching this choice point, which would flip the observed shares without any change in the routing itself. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL013, REL016, REL025, REL027, REL032, REL049, REL053, REL054, DIR002]

Other differences: A06 became far more frequent (about 17 → 53 in 100 traces) and now executes at the end of the process, typically between A34 and A35, so about half of the later traces end A34 → A06 → A35; the evidence shows this new context directly but cannot distinguish a relocation of A06 from the disappearance of its earlier mid-process role combined with a new late occurrence. Participation in A02 and A08 also decreased, though on few observations. [OCC002, POS001, DIR001, DIR005, REL011, REL012, OCC001, OCC003]
