### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment following A20 was rerouted in the later window: the block A21–A22–A23, executed together in either order, is now taken in about 4 of 5 cases instead of about 1 in 5, the alternative block A24–A25–A26 dropped from about 4 in 5 to about 1 in 5, and A34 now passes directly to A35 in about 9 of 10 of its outgoing transitions instead of often leading to A06, so A06 is skipped in most traces (about 46 → 12 in 100). The new block feeds into the unchanged backbone: A21 precedes A34 in about 78 of 100 later-window traces. The signs are direct (participation counts with 38–163 traces per side, A34's successor profile based on about 200 outgoing transitions in each window, and relational flips between A20 and each block), consistent across many items, and chance is implausible for the ensemble: 67 control-flow items qualified where fewer than 1 was expected without change. [OCC004, OCC007, DIR005, DIR006, OCC003, REL011, REL014, REL023]

Limitation: the evidence cannot show whether the branch swap and the bypass of A06 are one coordinated rerouting or two simultaneous changes, and A06's own outgoing behaviour in the later window rests on only about 26 observed transitions, so its remaining successors are uncertain. [DIR001, DIR006]
