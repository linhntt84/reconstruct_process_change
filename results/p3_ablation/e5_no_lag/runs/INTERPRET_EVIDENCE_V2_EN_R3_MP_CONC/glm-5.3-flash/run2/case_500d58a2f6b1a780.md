### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A25, A26 and A31 each appeared in every trace and were executed about 3.4 times per trace on average, occurring more than once in about 75 in 100 traces. In the later window, all three still appear in every trace but occur about once per trace (mean 1.04), with a repeat observed in only about 1 in 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A31's outgoing transitions shifted: earlier, about 71 in 100 of A31's observed outgoing transitions led to A25 and about 29 in 100 to A20; in the later window, about 96 in 100 lead to A20 and only about 4 in 100 to A25. Per trace, A31→A20 stays at roughly one occurrence in both windows, while A31→A25 falls from roughly two to three per trace to almost none. [DIR001]
- The relation between A25 and A31 changed from repetition/interleaving to a fixed order: earlier the two co-occurred in every trace, interleaved or repeated in about 75 in 100 traces and with A25 before A31 in about 25 in 100; in the later window A25 comes before A31 in about 995 in 1000 traces, with interleaving or repetition nearly gone. [REL001]
- The typical relative position of A25 and A31 within the trace is essentially unchanged (mean positions about 0.43 and 0.48 of the trace), but their positional spread roughly halved (standard deviation from about 0.12 to about 0.06 for both), consistent with a single occurrence at a stable point instead of several occurrences spread through the trace. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the repeated execution of the A25–A26–A31 block stopped. In the earlier window these activities cycled: A31 led back to A25 about two to three extra times per trace before exiting once to A20. In the later window the block runs once per trace, A25 precedes A31 in a fixed order, and A31 proceeds to A20. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001]

The support is direct and consistent: seven control-flow items qualified where fewer than one would be expected without a change (7 vs 0.29), and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. The near-disappearance of repetition for all three activities, the collapse of the A31→A25 transition while A31→A20 remains about once per trace, and the relation change from interleaved/repeated to a fixed A25-before-A31 order all describe one coherent behaviour; the reduced positional spread of A25 and A31 fits the same picture. Note that these items are not independent confirmations — the three occurrence items show an identical pattern, and the direction and relation items reflect the same cycle. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: A26's role is inferred only from its occurrence pattern, as no direction or relation evidence covers it. Residual repetition still appears in about 1 in 200 traces, so the evidence shows repetition nearly stopped rather than made impossible. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is a broader shortening of traces affecting repetition generally; however, the targeted collapse of A31→A25 alongside an unchanged A31→A20 exit, and the fact that no other tested unit qualified, point to the A25–A31 cycle specifically. [OCC002, DIR001, REL001]
