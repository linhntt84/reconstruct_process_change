Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the cycle through A30, A31 and A36, which previously repeated several times per case, now typically runs only once per case. In the earlier window A36 returned to A30 in about 70 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, closing a cycle in which A30, A31 and A36 each occurred about 3.5 times per trace and A30 and A36 interleaved; in the later window A36 exits to A25 in about 92 in 100 transitions, the cycle executes once, each activity occurs once, and A30 reliably precedes A36.
