- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 were not observed in any of the 200 traces; in the later window A19 runs in 59 traces (about 30 in 100), A20 in 82 (about 41 in 100) and A25 in 60 (about 30 in 100) [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening tested 106 control-flow units and qualified 26 items, where about 0.53 would be expected by chance if nothing had changed — roughly 50 times the expectation — so the set of differences cannot be explained as chance sampling of a stable process; every item's difference also exceeds about 995 in 1,000 random re-splits, though that strength alone would not separate a real change from a rare variant [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012].

Main hypothesis: the evidence supports the possibility that a new, mutually exclusive step — one of the previously unobserved activities A19, A20 or A25 — was added between A27 and A29, so that in the later window almost every case runs A27, then the new step (either before or after A28), then A29, instead of the former direct chain A27 → A28 → A29. Supporting signs: the three activities go from zero participation to 30–41 in 100 traces with strict exclusivity; A27's and A28's outgoing transitions are redirected towards them (A27→A28 falling from about 99 to about 44 in 100, A28→A29 from about 99 to about 55 in 100); the relational evidence consistently places the new step after A27 and before A29; and the small opposite position shifts of A27 and A29 fit an additional step between them [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005].

Limitations: the earlier window shows none of the three activities in 200 traces, so a pre-existing path rarer than roughly 1–2 in 100 cases cannot be ruled out as a competing explanation; the evidence also cannot pin down a single insertion point, since both orders relative to A28 occur, and about 1 in 200 later traces contains none of the three, leaving open whether the step is fully mandatory [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL004, REL008, REL011].

Other differences: A10's participation rose from about 5 in 100 traces to about 13–14 in 100; no relational or directional evidence covers A10, so it cannot be connected to the new step [OCC001].
