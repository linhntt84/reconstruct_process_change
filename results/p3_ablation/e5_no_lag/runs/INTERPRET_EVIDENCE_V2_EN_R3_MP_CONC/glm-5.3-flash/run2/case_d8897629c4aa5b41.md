### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a new mutually exclusive choice among three activities — A14, A15 and A20 — was inserted into the previously fixed sequence A22 → A23 → A24: earlier every trace ran A22 → A23 → A24, while in the later window virtually every trace contains exactly one of A14, A15 or A20, placed directly after A22 (before A23) in roughly 56 in 100 traces and directly after A23 (before A24) in roughly 44 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL006, REL003, REL005, REL009, REL012]

Supporting signs: the number of qualifying items (24) is many times the number expected without a change (about 0.39), so chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole; occurrence, direction, relation and position items all describe the same structure — the newer activities are entered only from A22 or A23, always exit to A23 or A24, always precede A24, and never co-occur with one another. [screening, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL004, REL008, REL011, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Limitations and competing explanation: zero participation in the earlier window only means A14, A15 and A20 were not observed there; the evidence cannot distinguish activities newly added to the process from activities that existed but were never executed or recorded in the earlier sample. It also cannot show what determines the choice among the three activities or the placement before versus after A23. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: none beyond this hypothesis; A22's slight forward position shift and the mid-trace placement of the newer activities read as by-products of the same insertions rather than independent changes. [POS004, POS001, POS002, POS003]
