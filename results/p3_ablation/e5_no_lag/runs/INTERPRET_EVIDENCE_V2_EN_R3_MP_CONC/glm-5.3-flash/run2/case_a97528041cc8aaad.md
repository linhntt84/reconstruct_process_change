### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, two blocks of activities exchanged their placement around the shared hub activities A25 and A29: the block A20/A22/A23, which earlier ran before the hub, now runs after it and exits to A30, while the block A19/A21/A24, which earlier ran after the hub and exited to A30, now runs before the hub and exits to A25, with the entries from A40 and A29 swapping targets accordingly. Supporting signs: near-complete pairwise order reversals between the blocks and the hub (for example A20 before A25 in about 99 in 100 cases earlier versus A25 before A20 in about 97 in 100 later), consistent successor swaps at all six activities plus A40 and A29, and matching position shifts; screening qualified 61 items against roughly 0.7 expected if nothing had changed, so the ensemble is not what an unchanged process typically produces, and the many pairwise items describe one underlying reordering rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: the evidence gives pairwise orders and direct successors, not full per-trace sequences, and A20/A22/A23 keep a mixed internal order in both windows, so the hypothesis concerns the blocks' placement relative to each other and the hub, not their internal routing; a competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a changed repetition or looping structure around these activities producing the same pairwise reversals.
