### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural rerouting involving A20, A21, A22, A23, A26 and the block A31/A32/A37. In the earlier window, A31, A32 and A37 formed a mandatory block executed together (in varying order) between A20 and A23, with A21 and A22 running afterwards; in the later window this block runs in only about 47 in 100 cases, in a later position (entered after A21, exiting to A26), as an alternative to A22 — the two are seen together in about 1 in 200 traces — while A20 leads directly to A23 and A21 routes each case either to A22 (about 54 in 100) or into the block (about 46 in 100) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019].
