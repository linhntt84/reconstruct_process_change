### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a choice was introduced between A27 and the group A36/A37/A42, and that the group moved later in the flow: in the earlier window every case executed A25, then A36/A37/A42 in varying order, then A28, A26, A27 and A31; in the later window, cases execute A25, A28 and A26 in every trace and then take exactly one of two mutually exclusive branches — A27 alone, or the group A36/A37/A42 followed by A31 — each in roughly half of the traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL020, DIR001, DIR002, REL006, REL014, REL017]

This is shown directly by relational and routing evidence, not by frequencies alone: co-occurrence of A27 with each group member fell from 100% to about 0–1%, the group's order relative to A26 and A28 flipped, and the successor profiles of A25 and A26 changed; participation, direction and position items all agree, 39 control-flow items qualified where about 0.6 would be expected without change (many times that expectation), and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits. These items describe one coherent underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample two differently composed case populations (a case-mix difference) rather than a single modification, and positions are event-index based, so the relocation concerns order, not clock time.
