### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a flip between two alternative activity blocks. In the earlier window, A21, A22 and A23 each took part in about 75 in 100 traces and ran together (pairwise co-occurrence about 75 in 100); in the later window each takes part in only about 25 in 100 traces and they co-occur in about 24–25 in 100. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL025, REL026, REL034]
- The alternative block moved the other way: A24, A25 and A26 rose from about 25–26 in 100 traces to about 76 in 100, and their pairwise co-occurrence rose from about 25 to about 75–76 in 100. In both windows the two blocks almost never share a trace (together in at most about 2 in 100 traces, with virtually every trace containing one block or the other), so they act as alternatives whose shares exchanged. [OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL049, REL050, REL055, REL027, REL035, REL042]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch choice between two existing alternative blocks changed: the block A21–A22–A23, executed jointly in about three quarters of cases in the earlier window (after A19/A20 and A29, before A34/A35), ran in only about one quarter of later-window cases, while the alternative block A24–A25–A26 rose from about one quarter to about three quarters in the same slot. All six activities and both blocks are observed in substantial shares in both windows and still almost never co-occur, so this reads as existing alternative paths being taken with different frequencies rather than activities being added, removed or bypassed. The change is shown directly and consistently by co-occurrence and ordering items, corroborated by the participation counts and by A20's reversed outgoing shares; with 79 qualifying items against roughly 0.72 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows captured different mixes of pre-existing case types or variants rather than a modified branch choice; the data show only that the proportions flipped.
