### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the routing choice after A15 flipped between two existing alternative branches. Earlier, about 4 in 5 cases ran the branch containing A19, A20 and A21 — three activities executed together in no fixed order, followed by A25 — while about 1 in 5 ran the branch with A16, A17 and A18; in the later window these shares are reversed, with about 4 in 5 cases taking the A16/A17/A18 branch. Supporting signs: all six branch activities shifted participation by the same margin, A15's outgoing transition shares moved in the same direction, and the relational pattern is coherent (the groups never occur together, A15 always precedes the group that runs, A25 stays attached to it). Screening produced 34 qualifying items where fewer than 1 (0.39) would be expected without a change, so chance sampling cannot account for the set as a whole. [OCC001-006, DIR001, REL001-027]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of case types reaching this point (two case variants whose proportions changed would produce the same pattern); the minority branch is also observed in only about 37–42 traces per window, so the exact shares carry some uncertainty. [OCC001, OCC004]

Other differences: none — every qualifying item describes this branch-choice behaviour; within-group ordering is unfixed in both windows and no activity became repeated. [REL007, REL013, OCC001]
