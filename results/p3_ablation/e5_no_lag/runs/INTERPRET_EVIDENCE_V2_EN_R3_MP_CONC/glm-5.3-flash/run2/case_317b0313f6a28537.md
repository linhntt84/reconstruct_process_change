### Observed differences

- In the later window, the three activities A19, A20 and A25 run much earlier in the case: their mean relative position moved from about 0.60–0.61 of the trace in the earlier window to about 0.32–0.34, while their participation stayed similar (A19 in about 39 in 100 cases before and 36 after; A20 about 32→35; A25 about 31→32) [POS001, POS002, POS003].
- In the earlier window, the observed outgoing transitions of A19, A20 and A25 split roughly half to A28 and half to A32 (e.g., A19: about 47 in 100 to A28, 48 to A32); in the later window they go almost exclusively to A26 (about 88–94 in 100) [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003].
- The order between this trio and A26/A27 reversed: before, A26 and A27 came before A19/A20/A25 in about 31–38 in 100 of the cases where they co-occurred and essentially never the other way round; in the later window A19/A20/A25 come first in about 30–36 in 100, with the previous direction nearly gone [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006].
- Feeder routing changed accordingly: A27 previously split its outgoing transitions between A28 (about 47 in 100) and the trio A19/A20/A25 (about 51 in 100 combined) and now leads to A28 in about 98 in 100; A28 previously split between A32 (about 50) and the trio (about 47) and now leads to A32 in about 96 in 100; A45 previously led to A26 in about 85 in 100 transitions and now leads to A19/A20/A25 in about 85 in 100 combined [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006].
- A26, A27 and A28, which appear in every case in both windows, shifted slightly later (mean relative position, e.g., A26 from 0.33 to 0.36) [POS004, POS005, POS006].
- A15 still occurs in every case in both windows but repeats less often: mean occurrences per trace fell from about 1.9 to about 1.6, and the share of cases in which it recurs from about 47 in 100 to 38 in 100 [OCC001].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of three activities A19, A20 and A25 was relocated: in the earlier window it ran late in the case, after A26/A27/A28, with each of the three leading on to A28 or A32; in the later window it runs early, fed by A45 and leading almost exclusively into A26, while A27 and A28 now pass on only to A28 and A32 respectively [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, POS001, POS002, POS003].

The same underlying difference is visible directly in three views: the reversed order relations between the trio and A26/A27 (REL001–REL006), the position shift of about 0.28 earlier for the trio with slight later shifts for A26/A27/A28 (POS001–POS006), and the matching successor changes at A19/A20/A25, A27, A28 and A45 (DIR001–DIR006). The trio's participation is essentially unchanged, so the activities did not become optional or rarer — their placement changed. The volume of qualifying items (19 against roughly 0.54 expected if nothing had changed) is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces [POS001, DIR001, REL001].

Limitations: the routing profiles for the trio rest on about 60–80 observed transitions per window, and with 200 cases per window a shift in the mix of two pre-existing variants (one with the trio late, one with it early) would look the same as a relocation and cannot be ruled out; positions are event positions, not clock times, so no claim about timing is made [DIR002, POS001].

Other differences: A15 occurs in every case in both windows but with fewer repetitions in the later window (mean occurrences per trace about 1.9 → 1.6); this repetition change is not part of the relocation hypothesis above [OCC001].
