### Observed differences

- The order between the activities A19, A20, A25 and the activities A26/A27 flipped. In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 always came first when they co-occurred with A26 or A27: A19 preceded A26 in about 36 of 100 traces (A26 first: about 0.5 in 100), A20 preceded A26 in about 24, A25 in about 38, with near-identical patterns against A27. In the later window this is reversed: A26 precedes A19, A20 and A25 in about 31, 37 and 29 of 100 traces, while the earlier order drops to at most about 0.5 in 100. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007]
- Against A28, a fixed order became a mixed one: earlier, A20 and A25 always preceded A28 when both occurred (about 24 and 38 in 100 traces; A28 first essentially never); later, both orders appear (A20 first about 16 vs A28 first about 21; A25 first about 12 vs A28 first about 17). [REL005, REL008]
- A19, A20 and A25 occur much later in the trace: median event position about 0.30–0.31 earlier vs about 0.60–0.62 later, while A26 (0.34→0.31) and A27 (0.59→0.56) shift slightly earlier. These are event positions, not clock times. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- Successors changed on both sides. Earlier, A19, A20 and A25 sent 92–99% of their observed outgoing transitions to A26; later, about 39–48% goes to A28 and 45–56% to A32, with A26 at only 1–2% (based on 52–77 outgoing transitions per window for these three, about 200 for the activities below). A27 and A28, which earlier sent about 98% and 99% of transitions to A28 and A32, now send about 43% and 53% to A19/A20/A25. A45 earlier routed about 94% of its transitions to A19/A20/A25 (roughly 35/35/24) and now sends about 94% to A26. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- Participation also shifted: A24 appears in about 43 of 100 traces earlier vs about 58 later (mean occurrences per trace 0.43 vs 0.58); within the group, A20 appears more often later (about 25 vs 37.5 in 100) while A19 and A25 appear slightly less often (about 37.5→33 and 38.5→29.5). [OCC001, POS001, POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening flagged 20 of 110 control-flow units, many times the roughly 0.55 items expected by chance in an unchanged process, so the volume of flagged behaviour as a whole is not what random sampling produces. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

The evidence supports the possibility that the group A19, A20, A25 changed its placement relative to the A26–A27–A28 sequence. In the earlier window the group ran before that sequence — entered from A45, always preceding A26, A27 and A28 when they co-occurred, and flowing into A26 — whereas in the later window it runs after A27/A28, is entered from A27 and A28, and flows to A28 and A32 instead of A26, with A45 now leading to A26. The relational items all flip in the same direction, the position shifts agree, and the successor changes on both the entry and exit sides are consistent; every item's difference was larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, POS001, POS002, POS003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

Limitations: with 200 traces per window, the evidence cannot rule out that both orderings already existed and only their frequencies shifted between the windows, rather than a single behaviour having been restructured. Also, against A28 the group's order is now mixed rather than fixed, and the position figures are event ranks, not clock times — the placement reading rests on the relational and successor evidence, not on positions alone. [REL005, REL008, POS001, DIR004, DIR005]

Other differences: A24 occurs more often (about 43 vs 58 of 100 traces; mean occurrences per trace 0.43 vs 0.58), and the group's own participation shifted modestly (A20 up, A19 and A25 slightly down); these are not covered by the placement hypothesis above. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, POS003]
