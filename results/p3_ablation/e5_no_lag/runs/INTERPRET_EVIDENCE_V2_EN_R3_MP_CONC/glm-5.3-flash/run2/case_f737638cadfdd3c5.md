### Observed differences

- In the later window, three activities that never appeared in the earlier window now occur regularly: A19 in about 37 in 100 traces (74 of 200), A25 in about 34 in 100 (68 of 200) and A20 in about 29 in 100 (57 of 200), each at most once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the later window, each of these new activities leads almost always to A26: about 96 in 100 of A19's outgoing transitions go to A26, about 98 in 100 of A20's and about 97 in 100 of A25's; in the earlier window none of these activities was observed, so no outgoing profile existed. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- A41, which is present in essentially every trace in both windows, changed its routing: in the earlier window about 93 in 100 of its outgoing transitions went directly to A26, while in the later window only about 1 in 100 does, and instead about 34 in 100 go to A19, about 31 in 100 to A25 and about 27 in 100 to A20. [DIR004]
- The three new activities behave as mutually exclusive alternatives: A19 and A20 never occur in the same trace, A20 and A25 never, and A19 and A25 co-occur in only about 1 in 200 traces; in the later window at least one of them runs in about 65 in 100 traces, but never two of them together. [REL001, REL002, REL007]
- The ordering evidence places the new activities between A41 and A26: A41 precedes A19 in about 37 in 100 traces, A20 in about 28 and A25 in about 33, and each new activity precedes A26 in exactly the traces where it occurs; the new activities sit at a median relative position of roughly 0.35–0.36, immediately before A26. [REL003, REL006, REL008, REL010, REL011, REL014, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- Downstream activities shifted slightly later: A26's median relative position moved from about 0.36 to about 0.38 and A31's mean from about 0.69 to about 0.71, with both still present in every trace in both windows; the new activities also precede A31 in all their occurrences, and precede A35 in about 20 in 100 (A19) and about 15 in 100 (A25) traces. [POS004, POS005, REL004, REL009, REL012, REL005, REL013]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a block of three mutually exclusive activities — A19, A20 and A25 — was inserted between A41 and A26. In the earlier window, A41 passed work almost directly to A26 (about 93 in 100 of its outgoing transitions); in the later window, A41 routes to exactly one of A19, A20 or A25 (roughly a third each), and that activity in turn leads almost always to A26. Supporting signs: participation rose from zero to about 29–37 in 100 traces for each of the three, with consistent one-per-trace occurrence; direct successor evidence shows the chain A41 → {A19, A20, A25} → A26; relational evidence shows A41 before each new activity and each new activity before A26 in the same traces; the three never run together, which rules out parallel execution; and the screening produced 26 qualifying items against roughly 0.56 expected without change — many times the chance level, so the pattern as a whole is not what a stable process produces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL010, REL011, REL014, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Limitations: the evidence covers only this segment of the process; it cannot show whether the choice among A19, A20 and A25 depends on case characteristics or is a fixed three-way split, nor whether the residual direct A41→A26 transition (about 1 in 100) is a retained rare route or noise. A competing explanation that cannot be fully excluded is that these activities existed before but were not sampled in the earlier window, although their complete absence from 200 earlier traces alongside a ~30% participation rate afterwards makes a stable rare variant implausible. [DIR004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: the slight later positions of A26 and A31, and the new precedence of the inserted activities over A31 and A35, are consistent downstream consequences of the same insertion rather than independent changes; no further behavioural differences appear at the configured thresholds.
