### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

All 39 qualifying items belong to the control-flow family, against fewer than 1 expected by chance among the 126 tested units (about 0.63), so the difference set as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits of unchanged behaviour produce; every item's difference also exceeds about 995 of 1000 reference comparisons.
