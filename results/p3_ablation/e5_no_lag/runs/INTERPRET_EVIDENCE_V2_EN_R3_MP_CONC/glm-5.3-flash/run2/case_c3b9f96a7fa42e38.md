### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment containing A35, A36 and A41 and the segment containing A27 and A26 swapped their order: earlier the flow ran from A25 into the trio, which preceded A27 and A26, with A26 closing into A30; later A25 leads to A27, A26 leads into the trio, and the trio closes into A30. The pairwise order reversal is observed directly for all six cross-pairs while co-occurrence stayed at 100 in 100, the successor changes at A25, A26 and the trio match the swap, and the position shifts agree; with 21 qualifying items against roughly 0.6 expected by chance, the pattern as a whole is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. The evidence shows a reordering of activities that remain mandatory and co-occurring, not their addition or removal; it cannot show why the order changed, and it cannot distinguish a within-trace reorder from a shift in the mix of two full-path variants that contain the same activities in different orders.
