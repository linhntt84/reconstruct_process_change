### Observed differences

- Four activities became mandatory: A22 ran in about 54 in 100 traces before and in every trace afterwards; A31, A32 and A37 each ran in about 46 in 100 traces before and in about 995 in 1000 afterwards. Each appears at most once per trace in both windows (mean occurrences equal participation; no repetitions in either window). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Before, A22 and each of A31, A32, A37 were strict alternatives — they never shared a trace (co-occurrence 0 in 200 traces) — whereas afterwards they occur together in virtually every trace. [REL011, REL012, REL013]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change around the former branch between A22 and the A31/A32/A37 block. Before, a trace ran A20 into A23, then A21, and then took exactly one of two alternatives — A22 alone, or the jointly executed block A31/A32/A37 — with either branch leading on to A26. In the later window, essentially every trace contains all of these activities in one fixed sequence: A20 into the A31/A32/A37 block (internally unordered), then A23, then A21, then A22, with A26 last. A22, A31, A32 and A37 thus changed from optional, mutually exclusive alternatives (each in about half the traces) to mandatory steps executed once per case. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013, DIR001, DIR002, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL010, REL017, REL018, REL019]

Supporting signs: the order reversals and the new co-occurrence are shown directly by the relational indicator rather than inferred from positions; the routing, participation and position indicators all describe the same coherent picture; and the screening produced 39 qualifying items where only about 0.45 would be expected for an unchanged process, so chance cannot account for the evidence as a whole (every item also exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits). [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Limitations and competing explanation: many items describe the same underlying restructuring — the activity pairs overlap heavily — so they are agreeing views of one difference, not independent confirmations; and two window samples cannot rule out that the later window captures a different, now uniformly executed process variant rather than a modification of a single flow, nor do the data indicate what brought the change about.
