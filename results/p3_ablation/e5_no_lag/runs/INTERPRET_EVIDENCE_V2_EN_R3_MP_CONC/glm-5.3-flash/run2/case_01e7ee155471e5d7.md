### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the order of two parts of the flow reversed, together with the matching rewiring of entry and exit points: the block formed by A30, A31 and A36, which before ran after A21 and A22 and exited to A25, now runs before A22 and A21 — entered directly from A20 — and exits to A22, while A21 now leads to A25. Before, A20 fed A22, A21 fed into the trio and the trio exited to A25; in the later window, A20 feeds the trio, the trio feeds A22, and A21 feeds A25. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

Supporting signs: the order reversal is shown directly for all six cross-pairs between {A21, A22} and {A30, A31, A36} and is near-total in both directions, with co-occurrence unchanged at 100 in 100 traces, so no step became optional, was added or was removed; the successor changes at the block's entry and exit match the same rewiring; the position shifts agree in direction and size; and sixteen control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.34 would be expected without a change, so chance sampling cannot account for the set as a whole.
