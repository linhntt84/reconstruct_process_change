### Observed differences

- The relative order of the A26/A27 segment and the A35/A36/A41 block reversed. In the earlier window, A26 came before A35, A36 and A41 in about 99–100 in 100 traces, and A27 before them in about 98–100 in 100; in the later window, the block came before A26 in about 99–99.5 in 100 and before A27 in about 98–98.5 in 100. All six activity pairs still co-occur in every trace in both windows, and interleaving stays rare (at most about 1.5 in 100), so one fixed order flipped to the opposite fixed order rather than becoming mixed. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Relative event positions shifted consistently: A35, A36 and A41 each moved from a median around 0.58–0.59 to around 0.41 of the trace length (about 17–19% earlier), while A26 moved from about 0.53 to about 0.63 and A27 from about 0.37 to about 0.47 (about 9–10% later). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- Successors were rewired consistently. Earlier, almost all outgoing transitions of A25 went to A27 (about 99 in 100), A26 split into the block (A41 about 37 in 100, A35 about 34, A36 about 28), and each block activity exited to A30 (about 28–37 in 100 of its transitions). Later, A25 feeds the block (about one third each to A36, A41 and A35), A26 goes almost always to A30 (about 99 in 100), and the block's edge to A30 is replaced by an edge to A27 (about 31–34 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- Participation and frequency are unchanged: every activity involved occurs in essentially all traces in both windows (about 200 of 200) and roughly once per trace (about 200 outgoing transitions per 200 traces), so this is a difference in order and wiring, not in presence, path frequency or repetition. [REL002, POS003, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the A35/A36/A41 block and the A26/A27 segment exchanged their placement, with matching rewiring: in the earlier window the flow ran A25 directly to A27, then A26 into the A35/A36/A41 block, which exited to A30; in the later window A25 leads into the block, the block exits to A27, and A26 runs directly to A30 — so the block now sits between A25 and A27, with A26 and A27 following it. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

Supporting signs: the order reversal is shown directly by the relational items and is complete rather than partial; the position shifts move the block earlier and A26/A27 later by amounts consistent with that reversal; the successor changes at A25, A26 and the block activities reproduce exactly the rewiring this exchange requires; and in the control-flow family 16 items qualified where about 0.5 would be expected without any change, so sampling alone cannot account for the set. [REL001, REL005, POS003, POS005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR005]

Limitations: all sixteen items describe one underlying reordering, so they are not independent confirmations, and the evidence covers only pairwise order, relative positions and immediate successors; the wider context (what precedes A25 or follows A27 and A30) is not observed, so alternative global arrangements producing the same local order and successors cannot be ruled out. [DIR001, DIR002]

Other differences: scattered successor shares of about 0.5 in 100 (roughly one transition in 200) appear or disappear at several activities (for example A25 to A09/A04, A35 to A28/A11, A36 to A22/A23/A46); at this level they are consistent with single-trace sampling noise and are not part of the hypothesis above. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004]
