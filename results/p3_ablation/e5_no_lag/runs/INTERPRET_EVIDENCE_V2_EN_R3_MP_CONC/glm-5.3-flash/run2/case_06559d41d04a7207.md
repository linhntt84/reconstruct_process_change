### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural restructuring of the segment between A44 and A29: in the earlier window each case executed exactly one of the alternatives A19, A20 or A21 there (roughly 35/30/36 of 100 cases, never more than one), followed by A29; in the later window that per-case choice is gone and the three activities A26, A27 and A28 are each executed once in every case, in varying internal order, between A44 and A29. The support is direct and consistent: participation flips between 0 and 100%, the co-occurrence and precedence changes are observed relationally rather than inferred, and the routing into the segment (from A44) and out of it (to A29) is seen to rewire in both windows. With 69 qualifying control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected by chance, sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. A limitation: the evidence cannot show whether A19–A21 were removed from the process or are merely no longer reached, since a shift in which route cases take would look identical here; and the later-window behaviour of A19–A21 rests on a single trace each, so their new successors are weakly supported [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL007, REL031, REL033, DIR009, POS005, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005].

Other differences: A10 participates less often (about 26% to 11% of traces) and its outgoing transitions redistributed away from the old alternatives toward many targets [OCC001, DIR001]; A46 co-occurs somewhat less with the segment activities than before [REL036]; A14's outgoing transitions remain dominated by A15 (about 99.5% before, 93% after) with only marginal new targets [DIR002].
