### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the six activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 each ran in every trace (100 in 100, once per trace). In the later window each runs in only about 47–57 in 100 traces (A33/A35/A36 about 47; A37/A38/A39 about 56), still once when they run [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006].
- The six still move as two fixed groups: A33, A35 and A36 co-occur whenever any of them runs (later window: all three together in about 47 in 100 traces, all absent in about 53 in 100), and A37, A38, A39 likewise (together about 56 in 100, all absent about 44 in 100) [REL013, REL014, REL018, REL025, REL026, REL027].
- The two groups became mutually exclusive. Earlier, each first-group activity co-occurred with each second-group activity in 100 in 100 traces, always first-group before second-group (e.g., A33 before A37 in 100 in 100). In the later window, cross-group co-occurrence is only about 2–4 in 100 traces: A33 runs without A37 in about 44 in 100, and A37 without A33 in about 53 in 100 [REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024].
- A30 and A31 still run in every trace and keep their roles. Earlier, A30 preceded each of the six in 98–100 in 100 traces and A31 followed them in 98–100 in 100; later these orders still hold whenever the partner runs, and A30-only or A31-only traces (about 44–54 in 100 each) correspond to a whole group being skipped [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012].
- Routing changed accordingly: A30's outgoing transitions, earlier almost entirely to A33, A35 and A36 (about 34, 36 and 29 per 100), now spread over all six group activities (about 13–20 per 100 each) [DIR001]. A33, A35 and A36 now lead directly to A31 in about 24–34 per 100 of their outgoing transitions (unobserved earlier), while their transitions into A37, A38 and A39 fell from about 30–37 per 100 to about 1 per 100 [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004].
- Relative event positions (not clock time) shifted only mildly: A30's mean position moved from about 0.62 to about 0.67 of the trace and A31's from about 0.82 to about 0.80, with group activities moving by about 0.02–0.06 — consistent with traces shortening when a group is skipped, not with any reordering [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A30 and A31, which earlier always executed both three-activity groups in sequence (A30, then A33/A35/A36 in a mixed internal order, then A37/A38/A39, then A31), now skips one of the two groups in almost every trace: about 44 in 100 later traces run only A33/A35/A36, about 53 in 100 run only A37/A38/A39, about 3 in 100 still run both, and essentially none run neither, with the internal order of each group and the positions of A30 and A31 preserved [OCC001, OCC004, REL013, REL015, REL018, REL022, REL025, REL001, REL007].

This rests on direct relational evidence (the collapse of cross-group co-occurrence from 100 in 100 to about 2–4 in 100, while within-group coupling and the A30-before/A31-after relations stay intact), corroborated by participation and routing: A30 now enters whichever group runs, and A33/A35/A36 connect straight to A31 when the second group is skipped. Screening produced 45 qualifying control-flow items against about 0.63 expected if nothing had changed, so the ensemble is far beyond what an unchanged process typically yields; each item's difference is also larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits [REL015, REL019, REL022, REL013, REL025, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]. A limitation: the evidence shows that one group is skipped per trace but not what decides which one; an after-window that mixes two fixed trace populations rather than a per-case choice would look the same [REL015, REL019, REL022].

Other differences: A30 gained three very rare outgoing transitions (to A02, A03 and A06, about 0.5 per 100 of its outgoing transitions each), and the relative positions of A30 (later by about 0.06) and A31 (earlier by about 0.02) shifted as a byproduct of the shorter traces [DIR001, POS001, POS002].
