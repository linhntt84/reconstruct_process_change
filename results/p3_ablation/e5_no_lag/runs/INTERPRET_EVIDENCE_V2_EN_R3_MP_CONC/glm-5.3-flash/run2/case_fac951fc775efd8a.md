### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering hypothesis: the relative order of two activity blocks was reversed. In the earlier window the segment ran A20/A22/A23 → A25/A29 → A19/A21/A24 → A30; in the later window it runs A19/A21/A24 → A25/A29 → A20/A22/A23 → A30, with A02 — previously rare — now occurring in about a third of traces at the head of the segment, before A19, A21 and A24, and fed by A40. Participation of the block activities themselves is similar in both windows (A20, A22, A23, A25 and A29 in all traces in both; A19, A21 and A24 in roughly 29–44 in 100), so the change lies in the ordering, not in how often these activities run. [REL017, REL024, REL025, REL027, REL028, REL010, REL015, REL019, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, OCC001, DIR001, DIR009, REL009]

Supporting signs: the order reversals are near-deterministic within each window (98–100 in 100 co-occurrence traces), the successor changes match them exactly (A19/A21/A24 now lead to A25 instead of A30; A20/A22/A23 now lead to A30 instead of A25; A29's successors flipped from the late block to the early block), and 51 control-flow items qualified against fewer than 1 expected by chance for 129 tested units, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of a stable process produce. [REL017, REL024, DIR002, DIR004, DIR007, DIR008, DIR003]

Limitations: POS is based on event positions rather than clock time, so it corroborates but does not by itself establish movement; the reordering claim rests on the REL and DIR evidence. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that both orders always existed as variants and only their mix shifted between the windows; the near-complete reversal within each window makes a stable mixed-order process unlikely, but variant mixing cannot be excluded with certainty. [REL017, REL024, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: A41 now repeats more often (mean occurrences per trace about 1.5 → 1.8; traces with repeats about 33 → 40 in 100) while remaining present in every trace; this repetition change is not part of the reordering hypothesis. [OCC002]
