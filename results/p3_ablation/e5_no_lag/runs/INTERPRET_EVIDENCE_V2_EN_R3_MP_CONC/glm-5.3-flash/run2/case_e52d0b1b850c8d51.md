### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a middle segment of the process is skipped in roughly half of the cases: the block A19, A20 and A25, which previously always followed A41, is omitted in about 46 in 100 traces (each activity fell from all traces to about 53–54% participation), with A41 now connecting directly to A26 in about four in ten of its outgoing transitions; in addition, A06, previously present in about seven in ten traces between A14 and A15, appears in only about a quarter, with A14 usually proceeding straight to A15. The signs are direct and consistent: large participation drops, joint absence of the block members, directly observed bypass transitions, unchanged relative order among co-occurring activities, and about 50 qualifying control-flow items against fewer than one expected by chance if nothing had changed (the many relation items describe overlapping views of this one difference, not independent confirmations). The evidence cannot rule out that the later window simply mixes in cases of a kind that never executes this segment, rather than the segment having become optional within unchanged cases. These are not part of the main hypothesis, and the participation increases rest on only 29–43 traces. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, DIR002, DIR004, DIR009, DIR010, POS002, POS003]
