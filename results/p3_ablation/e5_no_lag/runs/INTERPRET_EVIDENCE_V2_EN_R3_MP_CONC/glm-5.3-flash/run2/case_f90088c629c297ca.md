### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A14, A15 and A20 were not observed at all (0 of 200 traces). In the later window each occurs in a substantial share of traces — A14 in about 37 in 100, A20 in about 34 in 100, A15 in about 29 in 100 — and always at most once per trace, with no repetitions. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The three activities are mutually exclusive alternatives: in the later window no trace contains more than one of them (co-occurrence is zero for every pair), and about 1 in 100 traces contains none of the three. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- A36's outgoing behaviour changed from a single edge to a choice. Before, all of A36's observed outgoing transitions led to A21; in the later window only about 1 in 100 of those transitions leads directly to A21, while about 37 in 100 lead to A14, about 34 to A20 and about 29 to A15. [DIR004]
- Whenever one of the three new activities occurs, it runs after A36 and before A21: A36 precedes A14, and A14 precedes A21, each in about 37 in 100 traces, with the same pattern for A15 (about 29 in 100) and A20 (about 34 in 100). [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- A21 still occurs in every trace in both windows; its mean relative position moved slightly later, from about 0.37 to about 0.39 of the trace, while the new activities cluster tightly at about 0.35 (roughly a third into the trace). [POS004, POS001, POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a mutually exclusive three-way branch was inserted between A36 and A21: traces that previously moved directly from A36 to A21 now execute exactly one of A14, A15 or A20 — after A36 and before A21 — with the direct A36→A21 transition surviving in only about 1 in 100 cases. This rests on direct relational and directional evidence (A36's edge to A21 falling from all of its outgoing transitions to about 1%, new edges to the three activities, and each new activity co-occurring with A36 and A21 in a fixed order), corroborated by their appearance from zero participation and by A21's slight position shift. The volume of qualifying items (20, against roughly 0.36 expected if nothing had changed) also far exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces. [DIR004, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS004]

Limitations: all items come from a single control-flow family and describe one underlying difference, so they are not independent confirmations; and zero participation in the earlier window strictly means only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed then — the evidence cannot fully exclude that the branch existed earlier but was exercised so rarely it never appeared in 200 traces, although its roughly 30–37% participation afterwards is hard to reconcile with that. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
