### Observed differences

- The clearest difference is a reversal of execution order between two groups of activities that co-occur in every trace in both windows. In the earlier window, A35, A36 and A41 ran before A26 in about 98–100 in 100 traces and before A27 in about 97–100 in 100; in the later window this is reversed, with A26 running before A35/A36/A41 in about 98 in 100 and A27 before them in about 97–98 in 100, while the groups still always occur together. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The screening flagged 36 control-flow items where about 0.6 would be expected by chance in a stable process, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits produce. A specific possibility supported by the evidence is that the execution order of two always co-occurring groups reversed: previously the block A35–A36–A41 ran before A26 and A27, with A25 feeding the block, the block leading on to A27, and A26 leading directly to A30; in the later window A26 and A27 run first, A25 feeds A27, A26 and A27 lead into the block, and the block leads to A30. This is shown directly by six relational comparisons in which co-occurrence stays at 100 in 100 while the order flips from about 97–100 in 100 one way to about 97–98 in 100 the other, corroborated by the successor changes at A25, A26, A35, A36 and A41 and by consistent position shifts (A26/A27 earlier, A35/A36/A41 later). [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

Limitations: the evidence shows the flip but cannot say whether the block or the pair should be seen as the "moved" side, positions are event orders rather than clock times, and the 1–2 in 100 traces that keep the old order in the later window could be a residual variant the samples cannot resolve; a shift in the mixture of coexisting case types between the windows cannot be fully ruled out, though the near-complete flip (from 0 in 100 to about 98 in 100 for the new order) leaves little room for sampling variation alone. [REL010, REL014, POS004]

Other differences:
- A02 was largely dropped from the main flow: A49 now leads directly to A50, and A02 — which earlier always co-occurred with the A25–A41 block and appeared in about 75 in 100 traces — now appears in about 21 in 100, early in the trace and no longer reliably followed by A50. The successor change at A49 supports reading this as A02 becoming skippable, but the evidence cannot fully separate A02 being skipped from the paths containing A02 simply being taken less often. [DIR011, OCC001, DIR001, REL001]
- A06 and A10 participate in more traces than before (about 7 to 19 and 9 to 22 in 100), though on modest observation counts. [OCC002, OCC003]
