### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the three-way alternative block formed by A19, A20 and A25, which in the earlier window executed between A41 and A26, is no longer executed in the later window, with A41 proceeding directly to A26. Before, A41 led to one of the three in about 93 of 100 of its outgoing edges and each of the three led on to A26; the three each ran in roughly 30–36 of 100 traces as mutually exclusive alternatives. In the later window they appear in 0–1 of 200 traces and A41 proceeds directly to A26 in about 92 of 100 cases. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL007, REL009, REL014]

The support is direct and consistent: 34 control-flow items qualified in screening against fewer than one (0.58) expected without change, so chance alone cannot account for the set; the participation drop is backed by about 61–71 traces before versus 0–1 after; A41's successor distribution flips from the three alternatives to A26; and every co-occurrence or ordering relation between the block and A12, A14, A24, A26, A31 or A41 collapses to about zero while those activities keep occurring in essentially all traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL008, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021]

Limitations: zero participation only means the activities were not observed, so a complete removal of the block cannot be distinguished from it having become extremely rare (well under 1 in 100 cases); the later profile of A20 rests on a single trace; and the evidence shows nothing about why the block disappeared. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, POS003]

Other differences: the small positional shifts of A14, A26 and A41 noted above are consistent with a block of events being removed between them, but on their own they do not show that these activities were moved. [POS001, POS005, POS006]
