### Observed differences

- The three activities A19, A20 and A25 each appeared in about 47% of traces in the earlier window (roughly 94–95 of 200 traces, about once when present) and in essentially all traces in the later window (about 199 of 200, about once per trace) — they moved from optional to near-universal participation. - The outgoing transitions of A41 were split in the earlier window between A26 (about 52%) and A19 (about 45%); in the later window about 92% lead to A19 and the transition to A26 has nearly vanished (about 1 in 200). [DIR002]
- A26 still occurs in every trace, but its relation to the block changed: earlier, A26 appeared without A19/A20/A25 in about 53% of traces (co-occurrence about 47%); later, A19, A20 and A25 each precede A26 in about 97–99% of traces and co-occur with it in about 99.5%. [REL003, REL008, REL012]

- The internal order of the block is unchanged — A19 before A25 and before A20, and A25 before A20, in about 98–99% of later traces — and A20 still leads almost exclusively to A26 (about 96% of its outgoing transitions before, about 99% after). [REL001, REL002, REL007, DIR001]
- Minor positional shifts accompany this: A26's median position moved slightly later (about 0.40 to 0.42 of the trace) and A41's slightly earlier (about 0.31 to 0.29). [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the sequence A19 → A25 → A20, executed between A41 and A26, changed from optional to mandatory: in the earlier window roughly 47 in 100 traces executed this sequence while A41 passed directly on to A26 in the remaining traces, whereas in the later window virtually every trace (about 199 in 200) executes the sequence and the direct A41→A26 bypass is no longer taken (about 0.5% of A41's outgoing transitions). This rests on direct and mutually consistent signs: the participation of all three activities rose in lockstep from about 47% to about 99.5%, their co-occurrence with A41 and A26 rose from about 47% to about 99.5% while the "only A41" and "only A26" cases disappeared, and A41's routing shifted from an roughly even split to almost exclusively A19; the internal order A19 → A25 → A20 was preserved. The 22 qualifying control-flow items compare with about 0.56 expected by chance, so the set as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in case mix — for example, only cases of the kind that executes the sequence occurring in the later window — rather than a removal of the bypass branch, since the bypass path is nearly absent in the later sample.

Other differences: A45 now occurs only alongside the block — A45 without A19 fell from about 33% of traces to none, co-occurrence rose from about 27.5% to about 62.5%, while A45's overall participation stayed around 60% [REL006, REL011, REL015]; A42 now always co-occurs with A19/A20/A25 (co-occurrence about 47% to about 99.5%) but with no fixed order (A19 before A42 in about 53% of traces, interleaved or repeated in about 46%) [REL005, REL010, REL014].
