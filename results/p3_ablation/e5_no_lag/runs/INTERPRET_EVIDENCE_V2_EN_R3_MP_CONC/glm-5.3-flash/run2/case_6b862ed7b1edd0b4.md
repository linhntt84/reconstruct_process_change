### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Main hypothesis: in the later window the segment A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 is executed in essentially every case, whereas in the earlier window it ran in about half of the cases — a participation change in which this segment appears to have become mandatory rather than optional. The screening makes the overall pattern unlikely to be sampling noise: 41 items qualified where fewer than 1 was expected for an unchanged process (127 units tested), and every item's difference is larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits. The support is direct and consistent: participation in A19, A20 and A25 rose from about 48 in 100 traces to essentially all, with about one execution per case; the co-occurrence items rose in step (they largely restate this same shift rather than confirming it independently); the internal order A19 → A25 → A20 is the same in both windows, which argues against the segment having been replaced by different behaviour; and A41 now leads to A19 in about 96 in 100 of its outgoing transitions instead of splitting between A26 and A19. The main limitation is that the two windows are finite samples, so a shift in the mix of cases between the windows remains a competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out.
