### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A26, A27 and A28 were mandatory and inseparable: each appeared in every trace, about once per trace, the three always occurred together, and their mutual order varied. In the later window each of them appears in only about 2 in 100 traces, and pairs of them no longer co-occur (in about 97–98 in 100 traces neither member of a pair appears). [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL036, REL037, REL042]

- In the later window, A19, A20 and A21 — nearly absent earlier, at 1–2 in 100 traces — each run in about 30, 33 and 38 in 100 traces respectively; they are mutually exclusive and together cover essentially every trace, so each later trace contains exactly one of the three. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL013, REL014, REL021]

- The exchange happened at the same point in the flow. Earlier, A44 preceded A26/A27/A28 in about 97–99 in 100 traces and the trio preceded A29 in about 97–99 in 100. Later, about 79 in 100 of A44's outgoing transitions lead to A19/A20/A21 (about 82 in 100 previously led to the old trio), each of A19/A20/A21 leads to A29 in about 95 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, and in nearly every trace containing one of them it runs after A44 and before A29. [REL039, REL044, REL048, REL038, REL043, REL047, DIR007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL019, REL026, REL033, REL018, REL025, REL032]

- The old block's other ties dissolved: A11 and A12 preceded the trio in about 46 in 100 traces earlier but co-occur with it in only about 1 in 100 later; the new activities are preceded by A11/A12 in about 16–21 in 100 traces. A46, which followed the old trio in about 58–59 in 100 traces, now appears mostly without any of these activities (about 68 in 100 traces show A46 alone) and co-occurs with A20/A21 in about 22–27 in 100 traces. [REL004, REL005, REL006, REL001, REL003, REL007, REL009, REL041, REL046, REL050, REL028, REL035]

- Smaller shifts: A10's participation rose from about 14 to about 23 in 100 traces, and A29's mean position moved slightly earlier (0.44 to 0.40) while A44's moved slightly later (0.31 to 0.33), both remaining in every trace. [OCC001, POS007, POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the mandatory block of A26, A27 and A28 — three activities that executed together, once each, in every case between A44 and A29 — was replaced at the same point in the flow by three mutually exclusive alternatives, A19, A20 and A21, of which exactly one now executes per case. The change is shown directly and consistently: participation swapped almost completely between the two trios, A44's successors switched from the old block to the new alternatives, the new activities lead on to A29 where the old ones did, their trace positions coincide, and the old block's ties to A11/A12 and A46 dissolved. The volume of qualifying evidence (72 control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected without a change) rules out chance as an explanation of the pattern as a whole. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL013, REL021, REL036, REL041, POS001, POS004]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out concerns the identity and history of the activities: A19–A21 already appeared in 1–2 in 100 earlier traces and A26–A28 still appear in about 2 in 100 later traces, so the windows show an exchange of dominant behaviour rather than a strict appearance and disappearance, and it cannot be determined whether the new alternatives are genuinely different steps or the same steps under different anonymous codes. [OCC002, OCC005, REL013]

Other differences:
- A10 is executed more often, in about 23 in 100 later traces versus about 14 in 100 earlier. [OCC001]
- A29's mean position moved slightly earlier (0.44 to 0.40) and A44's slightly later (0.31 to 0.33), with both activities still present in every trace. [POS007, POS008]
