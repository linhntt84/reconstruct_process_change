### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the branch formed by A32, A33 and A38 — previously executed between A26 and A28 in roughly half of the cases, with the three activities run together in varying order — was dropped or effectively ceased to be executed, so that in the later window virtually every case runs A26 → A27 → A28. A27 changed from an optional step (about 52 in 100 cases) to one executed in nearly every case, while A32, A33 and A38 changed from about 48–49 in 100 cases to not being observed (1 in 200 each). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL012, REL013, REL014]

The screening produced 26 qualifying control-flow items where about 0.54 would be expected by chance alone, and the items are direct and mutually consistent: participation (OCC), routing out of A26 (DIR001) and the relational profiles (REL) all show the same replacement of the A32/A33/A38 route by the A27 route, rather than scattered unrelated differences. [DIR001, REL001, REL005, OCC001, OCC002]

An important competing explanation remains: the evidence cannot distinguish complete removal of the branch from a drastic reduction to a rare path, since zero participation only means the activities were not observed, and the little after-window behaviour recorded for A32 and A38 rests on a single trace each. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR003, DIR004]

Other differences: A27's own outgoing profile is essentially unchanged, still leading to A28 in about 97–99 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions in both windows. [DIR002]
