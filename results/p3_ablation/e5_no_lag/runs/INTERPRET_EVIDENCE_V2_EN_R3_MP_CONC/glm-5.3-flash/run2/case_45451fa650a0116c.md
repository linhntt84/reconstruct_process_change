### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a flip between two blocks of three activities. In the earlier window, A24, A25 and A26 each appeared in about 82 in 100 traces and almost always together, while A21, A22 and A23 each appeared in only about 19–20 in 100 traces. In the later window this reversed: A21, A22 and A23 each appeared in about 79–80 in 100 traces, usually all three together, while A24, A25 and A26 each fell to about 21–22 in 100 traces. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL017, REL025, REL038, REL043]

- A20, present in nearly every trace in both windows, mirrors this flip in its outgoing transitions: before, about 82 in 100 of them led to A24, A25 or A26 (roughly 24–29 in 100 each) and about 19 in 100 to A21, A22 or A23; in the later window, about 78 in 100 lead to A21, A22 or A23 and about 21 in 100 to A24, A25 or A26. The two blocks almost never share a trace in either window (for example, A21 and A24 occur together in about 1 in 100 traces before and 0 after), and within each block both execution orders occur in similar shares in both windows, so the blocks were and remain alternative packages executed together without a fixed internal order. [DIR005, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL025, REL026, REL032]

- A06 fell from about 46 in 100 traces to about 12 in 100, and its event position moved from late in the trace (mean about 0.91 of trace length) to mid-trace (about 0.52). Before, A34 led to A06 in about 45 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, and A06 then led to A35 in about 92 in 100 of its transitions; in the later window, A34 leads directly to A35 in about 90 in 100 of its transitions and to A06 in about 2 in 100. A06's remaining outgoing transitions are spread over many targets (largest share about 23 in 100 to A15, based on only about 26 observed transitions). [OCC003, POS001, DIR006, DIR001, REL009, REL010]

- Participation rose from low levels for A02 (about 8 to 17 in 100 traces) and A04 (about 5 to 19 in 100); both now appear occasionally as successors of A14 and A19, whose outgoing behaviour is otherwise essentially unchanged. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR003, DIR004]

- A08's outgoing mix shifted toward A15, A35 and A33, but this rests on only about 9 versus 23 observed outgoing transitions and is weak. [DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the choice between two existing alternative paths flipped around A20: in the earlier window, traces usually continued from A20 into the block A24–A25–A26 (about 82 in 100 traces, the three activities together in either order), while the block A21–A22–A23 was taken in about 19 in 100; in the later window, traces usually continue into A21–A22–A23 (about 79 in 100) and the A24–A26 block is taken in about 21 in 100. Supporting signs: the mirrored participation swap of all six activities, the matching shift in A20's outgoing transition shares, and co-occurrence patterns showing the two blocks were near-exclusive alternatives in both windows while staying internally together; every involved activity is observed in a substantial share of traces in both windows, so this is a shift in how often existing paths are taken rather than a replacement of one path by another. The screening comparison supports that chance cannot explain the set as a whole: 67 control-flow items qualified where fewer than one (about 0.63) were expected without a change. Limitation: the evidence shows that the shares flipped, not what governs the choice; a change in the mix of cases reaching A20, rather than in the routing itself, cannot be ruled out. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, DIR005, REL011, REL014, REL017, REL019, REL025, REL038]

Other differences: A06 is now usually bypassed — A34 leads directly to A35 instead of via A06, A06's participation fell from about 46 to about 12 in 100 traces and its position within traces moved earlier — a pattern consistent with A06 becoming skippable on that path rather than a mere share shift. Participation in A02 and A04 roughly doubled or tripled from low levels, and A08's outgoing mix changed on very few observations.
