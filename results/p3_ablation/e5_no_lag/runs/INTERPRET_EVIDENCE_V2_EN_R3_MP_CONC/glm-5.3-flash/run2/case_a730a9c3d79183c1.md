### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the either-or choice between two activity blocks appears to have been removed: the trio A33/A35/A36 and the trio A37/A38/A39, which in the earlier window were mutually exclusive (one trio or the other in essentially every case, about 49 versus 51 in 100), are now executed together in about 98 in 100 cases in a fixed sequence — first A33/A35/A36, then A37/A38/A39, each trio still internally unordered — within the existing frame in which A30 (and A14) precede the block and A31 and A47/A48 follow it. This rests on direct relational evidence: all nine cross-trio pairs moved from co-occurrence of about 1 in 100 (with exactly one trio present in about 99 in 100) to co-occurrence of about 98 in 100 with a stable cross-trio order, the participation of all six activities rose to about 98–100 in 100, and the successor profiles of A30, A33, A35 and A36 shift consistently with a single sequential block. The volume of qualifying items (94, against roughly 0.84 expected for an unchanged process, all in the control-flow family, each difference larger than about 995 in 1,000 random re-splits) makes sampling fluctuation implausible as a whole. Limitations: the indicators show co-occurrence, coarse order and routing shares rather than the full routing logic, so the evidence cannot show where the former choice between the trios was made (for example at A30 or further upstream), and it cannot rule out that further routing detail exists inside the combined block.
