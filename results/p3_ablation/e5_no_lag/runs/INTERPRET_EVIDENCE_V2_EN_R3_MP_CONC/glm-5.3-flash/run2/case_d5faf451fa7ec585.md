### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every case contained exactly one of three alternative activities: A14 in about 32 in 100 cases, A15 in about 28 in 100 and A20 in about 40 in 100; the three never occurred together in a case and never repeated. In the later window, none of the three appears in any of the 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006]

- Before, the outgoing transitions of A22 went to A23 in about 55 in 100 observations and to one of A14/A15/A20 in about 45 in 100; the outgoing transitions of A23 went to A24 in about 46 in 100 and to one of the three in about 54 in 100. In the later window, A22 leads only to A23 and A23 leads only to A24. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

- Before, each of A14, A15 and A20 always co-occurred with A22, which always came first, and always came before A24; relative to A23 the order was mixed (for A20: before A23 in about 21 in 100 cases, after it in about 19 in 100), so the single block activity sat either between A22 and A23 or between A23 and A24. In the later window these co-occurrences are gone, while A22, A23 and A24 still appear in every trace. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL010, REL011, REL012]

- The relative positions of the remaining activities moved slightly closer together: A22's mean relative position rose from about 0.46 to 0.47 and A24's fell from about 0.57 to 0.55, consistent with traces shortening once the intermediate block disappeared. [POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the step formed by the three mutually exclusive activities A14, A15 and A20 — exactly one of which was executed per case before, inserted either between A22 and A23 or between A23 and A24 — is no longer executed in the later window, so that A22 now leads directly to A23 and A23 directly to A24, bypassing the whole block. The signs are direct and consistent: participation of the three activities falls from 56–80 of 200 traces to 0 of 200; the routing of A22 and A23 collapses from four-way choices to a single successor each; every relational item flips from "always together with A22/A23/A24" to "never together"; and the small opposite positional shifts of A22 and A24 fit shorter traces. With 25 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling of rare variants cannot explain the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL005, REL010, REL012, POS004, POS005]

The main limitation is that zero participation only shows the activities were not observed in this after sample: the evidence cannot distinguish a formal removal of the block from these 200 cases simply not taking that path, for example because the later window contains a different case mix in which the step is never chosen.
