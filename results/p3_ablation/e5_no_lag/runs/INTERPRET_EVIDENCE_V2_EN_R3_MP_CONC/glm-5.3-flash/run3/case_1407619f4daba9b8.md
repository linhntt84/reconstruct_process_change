### Observed differences

- **Participation became mandatory.** In the earlier window, A22 ran in about 54 in 100 traces and each of A31, A32 and A37 in about 46 in 100; in the later window A22 runs in every trace and A31, A32 and A37 in 199 of 200 traces. - The internal order of A31, A32 and A37 is unfixed in both windows, with each pair appearing in both orders in roughly equal shares [REL020, REL021, REL022].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block A31–A32–A37 was relocated and made mandatory, and that A22 also became mandatory. In the earlier window a typical trace ran A20 → A23 → A21 → then either A22 (about 54 in 100 cases) or the trio A31/A32/A37 in any order (about 46 in 100), followed by A26; in the later window essentially every trace runs A20 → A31/A32/A37 (any order) → A23 → A21 → A22 → A26. The relocation is shown directly by the order reversals around the trio, the successor changes at A20, A21 and the trio, and the trio's position shift from after A21 to before A23 [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS006, POS007, POS008].

The volume of qualifying evidence (39 items, against about 0.45 expected for an unchanged process) rules out sampling fluctuation as a whole, and the items all describe one coherent restructuring rather than unrelated shifts [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL011, REL012, REL013].

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish one coordinated relocation from two independent changes — the trio becoming mandatory and moved, and A22 separately becoming mandatory — and only items that passed the screening threshold are visible, so subtler changes among the 91 tested units cannot be excluded. Positions are event positions, so no statement about clock-time timing is implied [POS006, POS007, POS008].

Other differences: none beyond the above; the small position shifts of A20, A21, A22, A23 and A26 are consistent with the trio being inserted earlier in the sequence rather than being separate changes [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005].
