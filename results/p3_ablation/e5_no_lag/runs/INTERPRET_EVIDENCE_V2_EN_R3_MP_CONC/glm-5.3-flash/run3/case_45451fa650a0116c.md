### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the routing after A20 changed: of two alternative three-activity blocks, the A24/A25/A26 block that followed A20 in about 82 in 100 traces in the earlier window was largely replaced by the A21/A22/A23 block, which now follows A20 in about 79 in 100 traces. This is shown directly and consistently: the six block activities' participation flipped in mirror image, A20's outgoing transitions moved from about 81% to A24/A25/A26 to about 78% to A21/A22/A23, the blocks remain mutually exclusive and tied to A20 in both windows, and the chosen block still precedes A30/A34/A35 — a shift in how often two existing alternative paths are taken, not an addition or removal of activities. Screening corroborates the scale of the signal: 67 items qualified where roughly 0.63 would be expected if nothing had changed. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the flip reflects a different mix of cases entering the later window rather than a changed routing decision itself; the roughly 1-in-5 residual share of each block shows both paths still exist in both windows.
