### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window executes a step that was absent earlier: activities A14, A15 and A20 now run in almost every case — exactly one of the three per case — as strictly mutually exclusive alternatives placed after A22 and before A24, either between A22 and A23 or between A23 and A24, while the previous chain A22 → A23 → A24 continues alongside. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation rose from zero to about 29–41 in 100 cases, twelve relation items show the same exclusivity and placement pattern, and the outgoing transitions of A22 and A23 redistributed exactly as this placement predicts.
