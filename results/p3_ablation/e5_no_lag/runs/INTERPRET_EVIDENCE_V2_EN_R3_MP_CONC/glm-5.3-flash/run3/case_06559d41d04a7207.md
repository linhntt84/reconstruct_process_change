### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the one-of-three choice among A19, A20 and A21, which ran between A44 and A29, is no longer executed, and that the three activities A26, A27 and A28 — now run together in every case and in varying order — have taken over that slot between A44 and A29. The participation flips are near-total in both directions (about 60–71 of 200 traces down to 1; 0–1 of 200 up to 200), the relational evidence places the new block exactly where the old choice sat, and the screening yielded 69 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.75 expected without a change, which sampling alone cannot explain.
