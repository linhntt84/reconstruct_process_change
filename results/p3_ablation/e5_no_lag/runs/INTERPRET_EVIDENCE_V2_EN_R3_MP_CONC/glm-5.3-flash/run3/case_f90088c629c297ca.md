### Observed differences

- Activities A14, A15 and A20 are absent from the earlier window (0 of 200 traces) but appear regularly in the later window: A14 in about 37 in 100 traces, A20 in about 34 in 100, and A15 in about 29 in 100, each occurring at most once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the later window the three never occur together: for each pair, co-occurrence is 0, while roughly 29–37 in 100 traces contain exactly one of the pair and about 30–38 in 100 contain neither. In the earlier window, traces contained neither activity in these pairs. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- Whenever one of the three occurs in the later window, all of its observed outgoing transitions lead to A21; in the earlier window these activities had no observed outgoing transitions because they did not occur. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The successor profile of A36 changed markedly: in the earlier window all of its outgoing transitions led to A21, while in the later window about 37 in 100 lead to A14, about 34 in 100 to A20, about 29 in 100 to A15, and only about 1 in 100 still lead directly to A21. [DIR004]
- Ordering is consistent across pairs: A36 precedes each of the three in every trace where they co-occur (about 37, 29 and 34 in 100 traces for A14, A15 and A20 respectively), and each of the three precedes A21 in the same traces. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- A21 occurs in every trace in both windows; its mean relative position moved slightly later, from about 0.37 to about 0.39 of the trace, while the three new activities sit at about 0.35 of the trace in the later window. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural hypothesis: a mutually exclusive choice among three activities — A14, A15 and A20 — was inserted between A36 and A21. In the earlier window, traces passed from A36 directly to A21; in the later window, about 99 in 100 traces pass from A36 to exactly one of A14, A15 or A20 and then on to A21, while the direct A36→A21 transition survives in only about 1 in 100 traces. [DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: 20 evidence items qualified against roughly 0.36 expected for an unchanged process, far more than chance would produce; the three participation shares (about 37, 29 and 34 in 100) almost exactly match A36's redistributed outgoing transitions; the three are strictly mutually exclusive; each invariably leads to A21; and A36 consistently precedes them while they precede A21. A21's slight shift to a later relative position fits one extra step being executed before it. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL005, POS004]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that A14, A15 and A20 existed earlier but went unobserved, since zero participation only means they were not seen in the earlier sample; however, their appearance in roughly a third of traces, together with the near-disappearance of the direct A36→A21 transition, makes a pure sampling fluctuation unlikely. The positional evidence is based on event positions, so no statement about clock-time timing is possible.

Other differences: none beyond the above; A21's small positional shift (mean relative position about 0.37 to about 0.39) is consistent with the insertion rather than a separate change. [POS004]
