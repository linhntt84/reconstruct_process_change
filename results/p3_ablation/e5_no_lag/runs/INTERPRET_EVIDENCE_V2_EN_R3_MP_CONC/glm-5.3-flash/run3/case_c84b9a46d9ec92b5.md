### Observed differences

- Three activities — A19, A20 and A25 — each ran in roughly a third of traces in the earlier window (about 73, 62 and 70 of 200 traces, i.e. 31–37% participation) and are essentially unobserved in the later window (A19 in 1 of 200 traces, A20 and A25 in none). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- In the earlier window these three were mutually exclusive alternatives: a trace contained at most one of them (co-occurrence about 1 in 100 traces), each appearing alone in roughly 30–36% of traces; in the later window virtually no trace contains any of them. [REL001, REL003, REL008]
- In the earlier window, each of the three led almost exclusively to A26 (about 95–97 in 100 of their outgoing transitions), and A41 in turn led to one of the three in about 87 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (A19 about 29%, A25 about 32%, A20 about 26%) and never directly to A26. - In the later window, A41 leads directly to A26 in about 86 in 100 of its outgoing transitions; A41 and A26 still occur in every trace in both windows. A26's mean event position shifted slightly earlier (about 0.39 to 0.37 of the trace) and A41's slightly later (about 0.32 to 0.33), consistent with the intermediate block disappearing, though position alone does not establish a move. [DIR005, POS004, POS005]
- The relational items agree: A26, A31 and A41, which earlier co-occurred with one of A19/A20/A25 in a fixed order in roughly a third of traces, now occur without any of the three in essentially all traces. [REL004, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL013, REL014, REL015]
- Separately, the outgoing shares of A21 shifted: A21→A24 rose from about 14% to about 35% of A21's outgoing transitions while A21→A31 fell from about 54% to about 38%; A21 itself appears in about 57% of traces before and 50% after. [DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of three mutually exclusive activities A19, A20 and A25, which earlier sat between A41 and A26 (A41 → one of A19/A20/A25 → A26), is no longer executed: in the later window A41 connects directly to A26, and the three activities are essentially unobserved. This is a removal or bypass of a group of activities, not a shift in how often existing paths are taken. Twenty-eight evidence items qualified against roughly 0.55 expected for an unchanged process, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits produce. The key sign is direct: the successor profile of A41 changed from about 87 in 100 transitions leading to A19/A20/A25 to about 86 in 100 leading to A26, with both A41 and A26 observed in all traces in both windows, while participation of A19/A20/A25 collapsed from 62–73 of 200 traces to at most 1. The relational items are consistent across all pairings of the three activities with A26, A31 and A41. Limitations: zero participation only means the activities were not observed, so a residual occurrence at a very low rate cannot be excluded, and the evidence shows the difference across the change point, not its cause; the after-profile of A19 rests on a single trace and was not relied on. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, REL004, REL006, REL013, POS004, POS005]

Other differences: at A21, the branch shares moved — A21→A24 up from about 14% to about 35% of outgoing transitions and A21→A31 down from about 54% to about 38% — a shift between alternatives that both remain well observed in both windows; the evidence does not connect this to the disappearance of A19/A20/A25.
