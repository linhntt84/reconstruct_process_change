### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, none of the 200 cases contained A19, A20 or A25. In the later window these three activities appear in about 29, 41 and 30 in 100 cases respectively (58, 82 and 61 of 200 cases), each at most once per case and around the middle of the case (mean relative position about 0.52) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003].
- The three are effectively mutually exclusive: A19 and A20 never share a case, and A19/A25 and A20/A25 share one in at most about 1 in 200 cases; allowing for these overlaps, virtually every later case (about 199 of 200) contains exactly one of the three, against none earlier [REL001, REL002, REL006].
- Routing into the new step changed at its neighbours. Earlier, about 97 in 100 of A27's observed outgoing transitions led to A28; later, only about 43 in 100 do, while about 52 in 100 lead to A20, A25 or A19 (about 22, 16 and 14 in 100). A28 shifted similarly: about 98 in 100 of its outgoing transitions led to A29 earlier, about 57 in 100 later, with about 41 in 100 now leading to A20, A19 or A25 [DIR004, DIR005].
- The new activities' own outgoing transitions lead mostly to A28 (about 47–56 in 100) or directly to A29 (about 41–47 in 100); in the earlier window they had no observed outgoing transitions because they did not occur [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003].
- Precedence: A27 comes before the new activity in essentially every case where both occur, and the new activity comes before A29 in essentially every such case; the order between the new activity and A28 is mixed, with both orders in similar shares (for A19, before A28 in about 15 in 100 cases and after it in about 14 in 100) [REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, REL004, REL008, REL011].
- A27, A28 and A29 still occur in essentially every case in both windows; their positions moved only slightly (A27 median 0.48 to 0.46, A29 median 0.56 to 0.57), consistent with cases becoming longer around the new step rather than a separate shift [POS004, POS005, DIR004, DIR005].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new step, performed as exactly one of the three mutually exclusive activities A19, A20 or A25, now runs in nearly every case between A27 and A29: it always follows A27 and precedes A29, and it appears either before or after A28, whereas earlier no case contained any of the three and cases ran almost exclusively A27 → A28 → A29. The previous chain still occurs (A27→A28 in about 43 in 100 of A27's outgoing transitions, A28→A29 in about 57 in 100 of A28's) but is no longer the only route [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, REL004, REL008, REL011].

This reading is backed by the screening comparison: 25 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.55 expected if nothing had changed, so the set of differences is far larger than random re-splits of a stable process produce, and the jump from zero to 58–82 of 200 cases per activity is far beyond a rare variant surfacing in one window. Occurrence, successor, relation and position items all describe one coherent structure rather than unrelated shifts [OCC001, DIR004, REL003, POS001].

Limitations: the evidence shows the new step sits between A27 and A29 but cannot fix its relation to A28 beyond "both orders occur", and the near-zero co-occurrences among A19, A20 and A25 cannot prove strict exclusivity — three separate optional activities that rarely coincide is a competing shape the data cannot fully rule out. Zero participation earlier also strictly means only that the activities were not observed in that window [REL004, REL008, REL011, REL001, REL002, REL006].

Other differences: none beyond the change described above.
