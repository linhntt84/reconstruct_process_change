### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace ran the same fixed chain A21 → A22 → A23: A21's only observed successor was A22, A22 and A23 always co-occurred with A22 first, and each of the three ran exactly once per trace. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, REL005]
- In the later window, that chain still runs unchanged in about 51 in 100 traces. [REL001, REL005, DIR001]
- In the other 49 in 100 traces, A22 does not run, and three activities that were absent from the earlier window — A27, A28 and A33 — each run exactly once; all three always appear together (each in 98 of 200 traces, and every pair co-occurs in the same 98). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- A21 now routes to A22 in about half of its outgoing transitions and to A27, A28 or A33 in the other half (roughly 15–18% each). [DIR001]
- Each of the three new activities is followed by A23 and by the other two members of the trio; every pair of the trio appears in both orders in nearly equal shares, so they run between A21 and A23 without a fixed order among themselves. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- The new activities sit around the middle of the trace (median relative position about 0.48–0.50). [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- A22 never occurs together with any of A27, A28 or A33: in the later window the two behaviours split the traces cleanly, about 51% with A22 and 49% with the trio. [REL006, REL007, REL008, OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new alternative branch was inserted between A21 and A23: in the later window, roughly half of the executions keep the original path A21 → A22 → A23, while the other half skip A22 and instead run A27, A28 and A33 as an unordered set of three between A21 and A23, with A23 following whichever of the trio comes last. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL012]

This is unlikely to be a sampling artefact: 25 control-flow items qualified in screening, about 64 times the 0.39 expected if nothing had changed, and the qualifying items agree across participation, routing, relational and positional indicators, each backed by 98–200 traces — though they largely describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, REL012, POS001]

Limitations: zero participation of A27, A28 and A33 in the earlier window strictly means only that they were not observed then; the evidence cannot prove they are newly introduced rather than previously unobserved, although an activity running in about half the traces would essentially never go unseen across 200 traces. The mixed pairwise orders show only that the trio has no fixed sequence; they cannot distinguish true concurrency from variable sequential ordering. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL012, REL013, REL014]

A competing reading — that the trio outright replaced A22 — is not supported, since A22 still runs in about half of the later traces; the evidence instead points to two coexisting alternative paths. [REL001, REL005]
