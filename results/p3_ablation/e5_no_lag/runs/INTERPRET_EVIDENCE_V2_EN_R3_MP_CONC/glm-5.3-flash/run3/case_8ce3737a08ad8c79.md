### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a swap between two blocks of activities. In the earlier window, A24, A25 and A26 each ran in about 82 in 100 traces, while A21, A22 and A23 each ran in only about 19 in 100. In the later window this is reversed: A21, A22 and A23 each run in about 79 in 100 traces, and A24, A25 and A26 in only about 21 in 100. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]
- A20, which appears in every trace in both windows, now leads to the rising block instead of the falling one: before, about 82 of its observed outgoing transitions went to A24/A25/A26 (A24 about 29 in 100, A26 about 29, A25 about 24) and about 18 to A21/A22/A23; after, about 79 go to A21/A22/A23 (A23 about 29, A21 about 29, A22 about 22) and about 21 to A24/A25/A26. [DIR003]
- The rising block's members execute together and without a fixed internal order: after the change, A21, A22 and A23 co-occur in about 79 in 100 traces (before, pairs among them were mostly both absent, about 81 in 100), and each pair appears in both orders in similar proportions (e.g. A21 before A22 in about 40 in 100, A22 before A21 in about 38 in 100). A20 precedes them in about 77–79 in 100 of these traces. [REL009, REL010, REL011, REL015, REL016, REL023]
- The two blocks act as alternatives: cross-group pairs almost never co-occur in either window. Before, about 81 in 100 traces contained only the A24–A26 member of a pair such as A21/A24; after, about 79 in 100 contain only the A21–A23 member, with co-occurrence near zero in both windows. [REL017, REL018, REL019, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL030, REL031, REL032]
- Relations with neighbouring activities flipped consistently: A30 now co-occurs with A21/A22/A23 in about 79 in 100 traces (before it appeared without them in about 81 in 100), while A24/A25/A26 now run without A30 in about 79 in 100 (before they co-occurred in about 82 in 100); co-occurrence of A21–A23 with A32/A33 rose from about 9 in 100 to about 55 in 100, while co-occurrence of A24–A26 with A32/A33 fell from about 52 in 100 to about 15 in 100. [REL020, REL027, REL033, REL038, REL042, REL045, REL021, REL028, REL039, REL043]
- A10 became rarer and less stereotyped: it appears in about 33 of 200 traces before but about 14 of 200 after; its mean relative position in the trace moved from about 0.86 (tightly clustered near the end) to about 0.52 with a much wider spread; its outgoing transitions shifted from about 97 in 100 going to A33 to a spread over many targets (largest about 21 in 100 to A15), and A32's transitions into A10 fell from about 20 in 100 to about 1 in 100. These observations rest on only about 14 after-window traces. [POS001, DIR002, DIR004, REL007, REL008]
- A02 rose from about 2 in 100 to about 9 in 100 traces with a redistributed successor profile, based on only 4 versus 18 traces. [OCC001, DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the choice between two alternative activity blocks after A20 has flipped: in the later window the block A21–A22–A23, whose members run together in no fixed order immediately after A20, is executed in about four of five traces, whereas in the earlier window the block A24–A25–A26 was executed in about four of five, with the other block appearing in the remaining minority in each window. This is shown directly by A20's outgoing redistribution (observed in all 200 traces per window), by the participation flip of all six activities with substantial support on both sides (about 37–38 rising to about 158 traces for the rising block; about 163–164 falling to about 42 for the other), and by relational evidence that A20 precedes the executing block, that block members co-occur, and that the blocks are mutually exclusive. Screening makes chance an unlikely explanation for the ensemble: 59 control-flow items qualified where fewer than 1 (0.63) would be expected if nothing had changed. [DIR003, OCC002, OCC005, REL009, REL012, REL015, REL017]

Limitations: all items describe one underlying control-flow difference, so they are not independent confirmations of each other; the evidence shows the flipped frequencies but cannot show whether they stem from a changed routing rule at A20 or from a changed mix of cases entering the process, and it cannot rule out that the minority branch (about 1 in 5 traces) behaves differently in ways not captured here. [DIR003, OCC002, OCC005, REL017]

Other differences: A10 occurs in fewer traces and its position and successor profile changed (including the near-disappearance of the A32→A10 transition), and A02 occurs somewhat more often with redistributed successors; both rest on few observations (about 14 and 18 after-window traces) and are not part of the main hypothesis. [POS001, DIR002, DIR004, OCC001, DIR001, REL007, REL008]
