### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a mirror-image swap between two groups of activities. A21, A22 and A23 each ran in about 75% of traces in the earlier window (about 150 of 200) but only about 25% in the later window (about 49 of 200), while A24, A25 and A26 each rose from about 25% (about 50 of 200) to about 76% (about 151 of 200) [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008].
- Routing at A20, which appears in virtually every trace in both windows, flipped accordingly: earlier, about 75% of A20's observed outgoing transitions led to A21, A22 or A23 and about 25% to A24, A25 or A26; later, about 75% led to A24, A25 or A26 and only about 24% to A21, A22 or A23. Co-occurrence with A20 shows the same reversal (about 75% versus 25% joint occurrence with the A21-group, and about 25% versus 76% with the A24-group) [DIR003, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012].
- Each group keeps its internal shape: within both groups the activities still occur together and in both orders (no fixed order), but joint occurrence of A21/A22/A23 fell from about 75% of traces to about 25%, while joint occurrence of A24/A25/A26 rose from about 25% to about 76%; pairs spanning the two groups remain almost mutually exclusive, with the majority side swapped [REL013, REL014, REL021, REL034, REL035, REL039, REL015, REL022].
- Downstream activities switched companions: A30, present in essentially every trace in both windows, co-occurred with A21/A22/A23 in about 75% of traces earlier but with A24/A25/A26 in about 75% later; A32 and A33, whose own frequency is roughly stable, shifted from co-occurring with the A21-group in about 48% of traces to about 15%, and with the A24-group from about 17% to about 52% [REL018, REL031, REL036, REL040, REL043, REL019, REL020, REL037, REL044].
- Secondary changes: A10 became more frequent (about 6% of traces, 11 of 200, earlier versus about 21%, 42 of 200, later), its events now fall later within traces (mean event position about 0.57 versus 0.82), and about 90% of its observed outgoing transitions now lead to A33 versus about 18% earlier; A32 now leads to A10 in about 20% of its observed outgoing transitions versus about 1% earlier. A02's participation fell from about 9% to about 1% of traces, but the later profile rests on only 2 traces [OCC002, POS001, DIR002, DIR004, OCC001, DIR001].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the two alternative paths following A20 exchanged frequencies: the variant in which A21, A22 and A23 run together (in no fixed order among themselves) dropped from about three in four traces to about one in four, while the variant in which A24, A25 and A26 run together rose from about one in four to about three in four, with the downstream activities A30, A32 and A33 now accompanying the A24-group path instead of the A21-group path. The participation of all six activities swapped with substantial support in both windows (about 150 versus about 50 traces per activity) [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008], and the change is shown directly and consistently by A20's reversed outgoing transition shares and by the co-occurrence flips around A20, within each group, and with the downstream activities [DIR003, REL007, REL010, REL013, REL018, REL031, REL034, REL036, REL019, REL037]. In total 59 control-flow items qualified, many times the roughly 0.63 expected if nothing had changed, so random re-splits of a stable process cannot explain the pattern as a whole; however, the many agreeing items largely describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply contain different mixes of pre-existing case types rather than a change in routing [screening].

Other differences:
- A10 occurs in more traces (about 11 of 200 earlier versus about 42 of 200 later), sits later within traces by event position, and now sends about 90% of its observed outgoing transitions to A33, while A32 leads to A10 in about 20% of its observed outgoing transitions; these observations rest on only about 11 A10-traces in the earlier window and are not part of the main hypothesis [OCC002, POS001, DIR002, DIR004].
- A02's participation fell from about 9% to about 1% of traces, too few in the later window (2 traces) to characterise its behaviour [OCC001, DIR001].
