### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two groups of activities exchanged their placement around the unchanged A25 → A29 segment: before, A19, A21 and A24 ran before A25 and A29 while A20, A22 and A23 ran after them; in the later window A20, A22 and A23 run before A25 and A29 and A19, A21 and A24 run after them.
