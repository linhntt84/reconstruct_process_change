### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the segment A26 → A27 → A28 ran in virtually every case: A27 occurred in all 200 earlier traces (once per trace), A26 preceded A27 in about 995 in 1000 traces, and A27 preceded A28 in about 995 in 1000. [REL001, REL005, OCC001]
- In the later window, A27 occurs in only about 51 in 100 traces, while three activities that never appeared earlier — A32, A33 and A38 — each occur in about 49 in 100 traces (98–99 of 200 traces each, once per trace when present). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A26's outgoing transitions split: earlier about 99 in 100 led to A27; in the later window about 51 in 100 lead to A27 and about 48 in 100 lead to the new activities (roughly 17 in 100 to A33, 16 to A38, 15 to A32). [DIR001]
- The new activities sit between A26 and A28: A26 precedes each of them in about 49 in 100 traces, each of them precedes A28 whenever both occur, and they appear around the middle of the trace (median event position about 0.5). [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL009, REL010, REL011, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- A27 and the new activities behave as alternatives: A27 co-occurs with A32, A33 or A38 in at most about 1 in 200 traces, while about 49 in 100 traces show a new activity without A27. [REL006, REL007, REL008]
- A28 itself is unchanged: it occurs in every trace in both windows, but in the later window about 49 in 100 traces reach it without A27 having run. [REL005]
- The order inside the new block is not fixed: A32 precedes A33 in about 23 in 100 traces while A33 precedes A32 in about 27 in 100, and the same mixed pattern holds for A32–A38 and A33–A38. [REL012, REL013, REL014]
- A minor difference: A27's outgoing transitions remain dominated by A28 (about 96 in 100, down from about 99.5 in 100), with small new transitions to A09, A34 and A25. [DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change in the segment between A26 and A28: in the later window, roughly half of the cases skip A27 and instead execute the three new activities A32, A33 and A38 — in varying order — between A26 and A28, while the original route A26 → A27 → A28 continues in the other half; A27 changed from mandatory to optional. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL009]

This rests on direct relational and successor evidence rather than on frequency shifts alone: the new activities were absent from all 200 earlier traces and each has about 98–99 traces of support in the later window; the split at A26, the placement of the new block before A28, and the alternative relationship with A27 are each shown directly, and the occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators all describe the same consistent picture. The screening also produced 26 qualifying items where fewer than 1 (0.55) would be expected if nothing had changed, so the set of differences as a whole is far larger than a stable process typically produces. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR003, REL002, REL006, REL012, screening]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window combines two case populations — an alternative route that existed before but was not sampled in the earlier window — rather than a modification of one process; also, the variable order inside the new block is observed, but whether it reflects genuinely parallel execution cannot be decided from these indicators. [REL012, REL013, REL014]

Other differences: the slight redistribution of A27's outgoing transitions noted above, which is small and not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR002]
