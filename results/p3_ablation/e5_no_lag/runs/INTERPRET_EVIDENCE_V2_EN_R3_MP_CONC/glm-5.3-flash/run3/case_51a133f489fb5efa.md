### Observed differences

- The clearest difference is a reversal of execution order between A26 (and A27) and the activities A19, A20 and A25. In the earlier window, whenever one of A19/A20/A25 co-occurred with A26 (about 30–36 in 100 traces), A26 always came first — for example, A26 preceded A19 in about 31 in 100 traces and A19 preceded A26 in about 0–1. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the block A19/A20/A25 is executed before A26 (and before A27) and flows into A26, whereas in the earlier window it executed after A26/A27 and flowed back into A28/A32 — in other words, the block now sits on the upstream side of A26, whose own position stayed roughly unchanged. A08 belongs to the same picture: it now occurs in about 30% of traces (previously 13%), precedes the block, and routes into A19/A20/A25, which in turn lead to A26; A45 also now feeds the block instead of A26. The support is direct and consistent: the relation items show the order reversal on the same co-occurring traces (co-occurrence stays around 30–37 in 100 in both windows, so the same activities are involved and only the order changed), the position and successor evidence point the same way, and 26 flagged items against roughly 0.6 expected by chance makes a chance origin of the pattern as a whole implausible. [REL005, REL007, REL009, POS002, POS005, DIR002, DIR005, OCC001, DIR001]

Limitations: each window is a finite sample of 200 traces, and the evidence shows the reordered behaviour, not the mechanism behind it. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in case mix between the windows (for example, different case types routed through different paths around A26) rather than a modification of one process, since no case attributes are available to check this — although the near-absence of the new order in the earlier window (about 0–1 in 100) makes pure sampling variation unlikely. [REL005, REL007, REL009]

Other differences: A25's trace participation fell slightly, from about 37 to about 31 in 100 traces [POS004]; the outgoing profiles of A48 (still mainly to A49) and A50 (still mainly to A51) were essentially unchanged between the windows [DIR008, DIR009].
