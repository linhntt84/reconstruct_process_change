### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-way choice among A19, A20 and A25 was essentially no longer executed in the later window: earlier, one of the three ran in every trace (about 32/28/40 in 100 traces) between A27/A28 and A29, whereas afterwards each appears in only about 1 in 200 traces, so nearly all traces proceed from A27 via A28 to A29 without this block. Because the activities are nearly absent in the later window, this indicates the block was removed or is now skipped in almost all cases, not a re-weighting of shares between existing paths. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL005, REL012, DIR004, DIR005]

Supporting signs: the participation drop is directly observed on substantial support (64, 56 and 80 traces before versus 1 each afterwards), it is consistent across the occurrence, relation and direction indicators, and the 24 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.52 expected by chance for 104 tested units, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL003, DIR004, DIR005]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish between the block being removed from the process and the block remaining but being skipped in virtually every case — the single residual occurrences show the activities are still possible — and it cannot show whether any other activity took over the block's role. The successors observed for the three residual occurrences rest on one trace each. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, OCC001]

Other differences: A29's mean relative position shifted slightly earlier (about 0.565 to 0.547 of trace length) while its participation stayed at 100 in 100 traces; this small shift is consistent with the block's removal shortening traces but is not independently interpretable here. [POS004]
