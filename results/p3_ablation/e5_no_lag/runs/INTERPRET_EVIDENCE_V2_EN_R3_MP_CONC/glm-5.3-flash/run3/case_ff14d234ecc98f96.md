### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: in the earlier window every trace executed all three of A26, A27 and A28 between A44 and A29, with no fixed order among the three, while in the later window these three activities are essentially no longer executed and exactly one of A19, A20 or A21 runs in that position between A44 and A29, leading on to A29. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL013, REL014, REL020, REL018, REL024, REL029, REL019, REL025, REL030, REL031, REL036, REL038]

The change is shown directly and consistently across indicator families: the participation flips are large and well supported on both sides (200 of 200 traces versus about 1–2 of 200, and 0 versus about 62–74 of 200), A44's successor profile — based on about 200 outgoing transitions in each window — switched almost entirely from the old three activities to the new three, and the relational items show A44 before and A29 after each new activity. With 60 qualifying items against roughly 0.72 expected for an unchanged process, chance alone cannot account for the evidence as a whole. [DIR007, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL018, REL024, REL029, REL019, REL025, REL030]

The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply sample different case mixes (for example a shift in the share of two coexisting variants) rather than a change at this point in time. The later outgoing profiles of A26, A27 and A28 rest on only about 1–2 observations each, and the single earlier A21 observation is too weak to establish what A21 fed into before. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR003]

Other differences: A29's mean event position moved slightly earlier and A44's slightly later, consistent with a shorter segment between them; A11 and A12 now precede the new alternative activities in about 17–20 in 100 traces, where they earlier preceded the old block in about 45 in 100 — a drop explained by the new activities being alternatives rather than a joint block, so it appears to be a consequence of the same restructuring rather than an independent change.
