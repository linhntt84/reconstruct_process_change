### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reversal of the execution order between two segments. In the earlier window, cases typically ran from A40 into the group A20/A22/A23, then through A25 and A29, then through the group A19/A21/A24, which flowed into A30. In the later window this is inverted: A40 — and, in about a third of cases, A02 after it — feeds A19/A21/A24, which now run early and flow almost exclusively into A25, while A29 feeds A20/A22/A23, which now run late and flow into A30; A25 and A29, which previously followed A20/A22/A23, now precede them. A02, previously rare, now participates in about 34 in 100 cases and typically sits between A40 and the A19/A21/A24 group. [REL017, REL025, REL015, REL019, REL010, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR001, OCC001, REL009]

The reordering is observed directly rather than inferred from indirect signs: the pairwise order relations flip from near-deterministic one-way order to near-deterministic the other (for example A20 before A25 in about 98 in 100 cases before versus 0 in 100 after), the successor changes are read off the observed outgoing transitions of the activities involved, and the position shifts of every participating activity point the same way. Screening also makes chance implausible for the pattern as a whole: 51 control-flow items qualified against fewer than 1 expected without a change across 129 tested units. Limitation: the evidence summarises pairwise relations and event positions rather than a reconstructed model, so a changed mix of fixed-order process variants cannot be ruled out as the underlying form of the change; the position statements concern relative event order, not clock time.
