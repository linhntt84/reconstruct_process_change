### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the exclusive choice between the two trios was removed: in the earlier window a case executed either A33/A35/A36 or A37/A38/A39 (roughly 50/50, with cross-trio co-occurrence in only ~1 in 100 cases), whereas in the later window both trios run in sequence in almost every case — A33/A35/A36 in mixed internal order, then A37/A38/A39 — with A30 before and A31 after the combined block. This rests on direct relational evidence (cross-trio co-occurrence rising from ~0–2 to ~98 in 100 with a stable order between the trios), consistently echoed by the participation, routing and position items above; the screening context also fits, with 94 qualifying items against fewer than one (0.84) expected for an unchanged process [REL039, REL041, REL045, REL047, REL050, REL052, REL025, REL031, OCC005, OCC008, DIR005, DIR006].

An important competing explanation remains: the all-six sequence appears in only about 1–3 of 200 earlier traces, so the windows cannot distinguish a removed exclusive constraint from a rare pre-existing path that simply became dominant (a shift in the mix of case variants). The many relational items also describe overlapping behaviour of the same traces and are not independent confirmations [REL039, REL040, REL041, REL045, REL046, REL047, REL050, REL051, REL052].

Other differences: A04 — participation fell from ~59 to ~26 in 100 traces, A14 now typically continues to A15 without passing through A04 (A14→A15 ~43% → ~79% of outgoing transitions; A14→A04 ~57% → ~12%), and A04 sits later in the trace when it occurs; this is consistent with A04 having become skippable or bypassed, but a change in the mix of case types cannot be ruled out. Separately, A02, A08 and A10 occur in more cases than before (~5–8 → ~18–23 in 100) [OCC002, POS001, DIR002, DIR004, REL001, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004].
