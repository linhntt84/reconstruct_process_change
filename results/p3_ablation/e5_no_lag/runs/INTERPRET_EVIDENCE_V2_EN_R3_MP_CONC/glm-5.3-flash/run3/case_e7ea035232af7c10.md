### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The 39 qualifying control-flow items are many times the roughly 0.62 expected for an unchanged process, so chance cannot account for the set as a whole. The evidence supports a structural reordering: the group A36/A37/A42, previously an optional alternative to A27 that ran after A26 in about 46 in 100 cases, became mandatory and now runs directly after A25 and before A28, while A27 became mandatory after A26; A25, A26, A28 and A31 keep their roles as start, mid and closing anchors, with A31 still the common successor. The placement change is shown directly by relational and directional evidence — A25's successor changed from A28 to the group, and the group's order relative to A26 and A28 flipped — not merely by position shifts, and the participation, relation and direction items all agree with one another.
