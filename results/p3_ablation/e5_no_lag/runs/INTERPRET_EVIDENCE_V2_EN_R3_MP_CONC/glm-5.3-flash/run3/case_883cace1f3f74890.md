### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new step was inserted into the segment between A27 and A29: in the later window virtually every trace performs exactly one of the previously unobserved activities A19, A20 or A25 (about 30, 41 and 30 in 100 traces), always after A27 and before A29, placed before A28 in about 55 in 100 traces and after A28 in about 43 in 100, whereas the earlier window showed a direct A27 → A28 → A29 flow with none of the three. The support is direct and consistent: participation rises from zero with substantial counts (59, 82 and 60 of 200 traces), the rerouting at A27 and A28 is measured on about 200 outgoing transitions per window, the relational items show the new co-occurrences and their order, and with 26 qualifying control-flow items against about 0.53 expected without change, the pattern as a whole is roughly 50 times what a stable process would produce. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, POS001, POS002, POS003, screening]

The main limitation is that the many agreeing items largely describe one underlying insertion rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot rule out that A19, A20 and A25 existed earlier but were not recorded (zero participation only means not observed), nor does it show what determines the choice among the three or the placement relative to A28. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL004, REL008, REL011]

Other differences: A10's participation increased from about 5 to about 13 in 100 traces (on thin support of 10 vs 27 traces), and the mean positions of A27 and A29 shifted slightly; these are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001, POS004, POS005]
