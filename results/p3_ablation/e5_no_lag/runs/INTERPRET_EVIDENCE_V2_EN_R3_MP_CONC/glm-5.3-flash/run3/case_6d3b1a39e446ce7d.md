### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the control flow around A25, A26, A27, A28, A31 and the A36/A37/A42 cluster was restructured. In the earlier window, A25 led to A28, after which A26 routed to either A27 (followed by A31) or to the A36/A37/A42 cluster — traversed in both orders — which exited to A31; these were alternatives, each taken in about half of the traces. In the later window, A25 enters the A36/A37/A42 cluster directly, the cluster exits to A28, and the trace continues through A26 and A27 to A31, so that A27, A36, A37 and A42 became mandatory and run in a fixed overall sequence in essentially every trace. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL010, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The screening produced 39 qualifying items against roughly 0.64 expected for an unchanged process, so the ensemble is far beyond what a stable process produces. The change is shown directly: the co-occurrence of A27 with A36/A37/A42 rose from about 1–2 in 100 to about 99 in 100 with a stable order, and the rewiring is observed in the transitions at A25, A26 and the cluster exit, with participation, direction and position indicators agreeing. Note that most items describe one underlying restructuring, so they are not independent confirmations of it. [REL011, REL012, REL013, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Limitations: positions are event ranks, not clock times, so the hypothesis concerns order and participation, not timing. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the later window reflects a pre-existing rare variant (present in about 1–2 in 100 earlier traces) that became dominant, rather than a modification of the main flow, since the windows are finite samples. [REL011, REL001, DIR001]

Other differences: A25's event position moved slightly earlier (mean about 0.37 to 0.35), and in the later window a small share of traces (about 2–3 in 100) shows these activities interleaved or repeated, with recurrence support of up to about 2–3 in 100 for A27. [POS001, REL001, REL005, OCC001]
