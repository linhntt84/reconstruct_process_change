### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of activities A19, A20 and A25 changed its placement: in the earlier window it was selected by A45 and flowed into A26, running at about one-third of the trace, while in the later window A45 leads directly to A26 and the block instead runs after A27 — entered from A27, also revisited from A28, and flowing into A28 or A32 — at about 60% of the trace. The order between the block and A26/A27 flipped completely, the successor profiles of A45, A19, A20, A25, A27 and A28 were rewired consistently with this, and the three activities' participation stayed roughly stable, so this is a placement change rather than activities appearing or disappearing; the 19 qualifying control-flow items are also far above the roughly 0.56 expected for an unchanged process. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the block did not simply move once but became part of a repeating cycle with A27 and A28, since A28 now leads back to the block and the block's order versus A28 is mixed; the relational items also overlap heavily and largely reflect this same single change rather than independent confirmations. [DIR005, REL003, REL008]

Other differences: A26 and A27 each run slightly earlier in the later window (mean position lower by about 0.03 of the trace) while still occurring in every trace in both windows. [POS004, POS005]
