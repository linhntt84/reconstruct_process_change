### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the group A31/A32/A37 was relocated and made optional: it moved from running together between A20 and A23 in every case to running, still together and in unfixed internal order, after A21 and before A26 in about 47 in 100 cases; in the remaining cases A22 follows A21 directly, so A22 and the group now behave as alternatives after A21, and A20 bypasses the group and leads almost always straight to A23. The support is direct and consistent: the order between the group and A21 and between the group and A23 reverses between the windows, the successor profiles of A20, A21, A31, A32 and A37 change accordingly, and the participation and position shifts match the same story; 39 qualifying items against roughly 0.44 expected if nothing had changed makes a chance origin of the evidence as a whole implausible. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the group was not moved as such but was removed from its early position while a new optional occurrence was inserted after A21, since both produce identical traces; the evidence also cannot show what determines the choice between the A22 branch and the group branch.
