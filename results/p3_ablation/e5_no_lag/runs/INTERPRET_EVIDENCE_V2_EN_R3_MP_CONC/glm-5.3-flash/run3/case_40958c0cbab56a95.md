### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of A26, A27 and A28, which every trace executed between A44 and A29 in the earlier window, was replaced in the later window by a mutually exclusive choice among A19, A20 and A21 in the same position: each later trace runs exactly one of the three (about 30, 33 and 38 in 100 traces), after A44 and before A29, while A26, A27 and A28 remain in only about 2 in 100 traces. This is shown directly by relational evidence — the new activities sit between the same predecessor A44 and successor A29 that framed the old block — and it is consistent across occurrence, successor, position and relation indicators. With 72 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected by chance in a stable process, the pattern as a whole cannot be attributed to sampling variation. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, REL013, REL014, REL018, REL019, REL021, REL025, REL026, REL032, REL033, REL036, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Limitations: the earlier-window behaviour of A19, A20 and A21 rests on only 2–4 traces each, so the evidence cannot establish whether they are newly inserted activities or previously rare ones that changed role; the residual traces still containing A26, A27 and A28 leave open whether the old block persists as a rare path. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is a shift in the mix of two pre-existing variants rather than a modified process, although the complete change in the successors of A19–A21 argues against a pure mix shift. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]

Other differences: A10 is executed more often (participation about 14 → 23 in 100 traces); A11 and A12 now precede the chosen alternative in a minority of traces instead of feeding the old block; A45 and A46 relate to the new activities differently than to the old block.
