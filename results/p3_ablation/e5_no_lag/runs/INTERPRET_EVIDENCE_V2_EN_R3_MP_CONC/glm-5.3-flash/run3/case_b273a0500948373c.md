### Observed differences

- Both windows show the same two-branch structure after A15: A15 occurs in every trace, is followed by exactly one of two mutually exclusive activity groups — A16/A17/A18 or A19/A20/A21, which never share a trace (co-occurrence 0 in both windows) — and A25 runs after whichever group is chosen. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, REL009, REL012, REL024]
- In the earlier window the A19–A21 branch dominated: A19, A20 and A21 each appeared in about 82 in 100 traces (163 of 200), A16, A17 and A18 in only about 19 in 100 (37 of 200), and about 82% of A15's observed outgoing transitions led to A19/A20/A21. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, REL004]
- In the later window the shares flipped: A16, A17 and A18 each appeared in about 79 in 100 traces (158 of 200), A19–A21 in about 21 in 100 (42 of 200), and about 79% of A15's outgoing transitions now lead to A16/A17/A18. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, REL001]
- A25 switched with the branch: earlier it co-occurred with A19/A20/A21 in about 82 in 100 traces and with A16–A18 in about 19 in 100; later it co-occurred with A16–A18 in about 79 in 100 and with A19–A21 in about 21 in 100, in both windows following or interleaving with the chosen group's activities. [REL012, REL024, REL027]
- Ordering within the branches did not change in kind: inside each group the activities appear in both orders in similar proportions (in the later window A16 precedes A17 in about 42 in 100 of their joint traces and A17 precedes A16 in about 38 in 100), the same unordered pattern the dominant group showed earlier. [REL007, REL013, REL022]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the routing shares between two existing alternative branches after A15 shifted: the branch made up of A16, A17 and A18, followed by A25, was taken in about 19 in 100 cases in the earlier window and about 79 in 100 cases in the later window, while the alternative branch A19, A20 and A21 fell from about 82 to about 21 in 100 cases. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL004, REL012, REL024]

The support is direct and consistent: occurrence, A15's outgoing-transition shares and the pairwise relations all show the same flip, each with a difference larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits. Both branches and every involved activity are observed with substantial support in both windows (between 37 and 163 of 200 traces), and the exclusive choice after A15, the branch-before-A25 sequence and the unordered within-branch sequencing are unchanged — only the shares between alternatives moved, rather than a path being added, removed, bypassed or reordered. 34 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.39 expected if nothing had changed, so chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole. [DIR001, REL007, REL009, REL022, OCC001]

An important competing explanation remains: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows draw on a shifted mix of cases (different variant populations) rather than a changed routing rule; such a mix shift would look identical to a share change in these indicators. [OCC001, DIR001]
