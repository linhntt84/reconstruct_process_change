### Observed differences

- The relative order of two groups of activities reversed. In the earlier window, A14, A16 and A19 each sat about a third of the way into the trace (mean position about 0.34; occurring in 30%, 35% and 36% of traces respectively), while A15, A17 and A18 appeared in every trace about two thirds in (mean about 0.64). - Participation barely moved: A15, A17, A18, A20 and A24 occur in every trace in both windows, and A14, A16 and A19 each in roughly 29–39% of traces in both windows (A14 30%→33%, A16 35%→29%, A19 36%→39%). - The two always-present anchor activities shifted slightly later: A20's mean position moved from about 0.38 to about 0.44 and A24's from about 0.57 to about 0.64, consistent with the early segment now containing the three-activity cluster A15/A17/A18 instead of a single activity. [POS007, POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence directly supports the possibility that two segments of the process exchanged their places: the segment built around A15, A17 and A18, which earlier ran late in the trace (fed by A24, exiting towards A25), in the later window runs early (fed by A35, exiting to A20), while the step in which one of A14, A16 and A19 executes, which earlier ran early and led into A20, in the later window runs late after A24 and leads into A25. The reordering is shown directly by the complete reversal of all 21 flagged order relations, by the successor changes at A24, A35 and the six segment activities, and by position shifts of about a quarter to a third of the trace length; 37 items qualified against roughly 0.42 expected without a change (about 90 times more), and every item's difference exceeded 199 of 200 random re-splits, so chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole. Stable participation across windows also distinguishes this from a mere shift in how often existing paths are taken. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL008, REL015, REL020]

Limitations: the position indicator uses event positions rather than clock time, so no claim about clock timing is made; and the aggregate evidence cannot distinguish a clean exchange of the two segments from an equivalent re-routing in which the branch points A24 and A35 simply now select from the other pool of activities — both descriptions fit the same observations. [DIR007, DIR008, REL008, REL015]

Other differences: apart from the slight later shift of A20 and A24 (mean position up by about 0.07 each), which is consistent with the exchange described above, the evidence shows no further behavioural differences at the configured thresholds. [POS007, POS008]
