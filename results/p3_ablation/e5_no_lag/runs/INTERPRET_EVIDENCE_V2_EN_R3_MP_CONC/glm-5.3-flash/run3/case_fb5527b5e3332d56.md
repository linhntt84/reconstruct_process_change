### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the segment consisting of A27, A28 and A33 — earlier executed once each, in varying order, between A21 and A23 in about 97 of 200 cases — is almost always skipped, and cases proceed from A21 almost invariably via A22 to A23; A22 in turn became near-mandatory, appearing in about 199 of 200 traces versus about 103 before. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL005]

The routing change at A21 is observed directly, the collapse of A27/A28/A33 participation is consistent across occurrence, direction and relation indicators, and the 26 qualifying control-flow items far exceed the 0.4 expected by chance for 80 tested units, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [DIR001, OCC002, REL006, REL009]

Limitation: the later-window picture of the A27/A28/A33 branch rests on a single trace, so the after-profiles for those activities are weakly supported. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the branch still exists but is now taken extremely rarely rather than having been removed altogether — the one remaining trace shows it was still executed at least once, in the order A28 → A27 → A33 before A23. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL012, REL013, REL014]

Other differences: none beyond the above; the slight forward shift of A23's position is consistent with the block being skipped. [POS001]
