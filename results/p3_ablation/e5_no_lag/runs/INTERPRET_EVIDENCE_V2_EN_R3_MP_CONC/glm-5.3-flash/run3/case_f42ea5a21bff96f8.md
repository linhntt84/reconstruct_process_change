### Observed differences

- Two mutually exclusive activity groups swapped how often they run. In the earlier window, A21, A22 and A23 each took part in about 75 in 100 traces and almost always occurred together (co-occurrence about 0.75); in the later window each took part in only about 25 in 100 traces. The mirror image holds for A24, A25 and A26: together in about 25 in 100 traces before, about 76 in 100 after, again almost always together. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL025, REL026, REL034, REL049, REL050, REL055]
- The swap preserved the groups' surroundings. Before, A19 and A20 led into A21–A23 (A19 before A21 in about 74 in 100 traces) and that group preceded A29/A30/A34/A35 (A21 before A34 in about 73 in 100); in the later window A19/A20 lead into A24–A26 (A19 before A24 in about 75 in 100), which precedes A34/A35 (A24 before A34 in about 74 in 100). A19, A20, A29 and A34 themselves occurred in nearly all traces in both windows. [REL013, REL016, REL030, REL032, REL051, REL053, DIR002, DIR003, POS002]
- The two groups remained alternatives rather than changing order: A21 with A24, A22 with A25 and A23 with A26 virtually never occur in the same trace in either window; only the preference flipped (A21 without A24: about 74 in 100 traces before vs 24 after; A24 without A21: about 25 before vs 74 after). [REL027, REL028, REL029, REL035, REL036, REL042]
- Routing out of A20 followed the same flip: before, its outgoing transitions went mainly to A21 (about 27 in 100), A23 (26) and A22 (23); after, mainly to A26 (28), A24 (25) and A25 (22). [DIR002]
- A06 became both more frequent and differently placed: present in about 17 in 100 traces before vs about 53 after; its typical position moved from mid-trace (median about 0.48) to near the end (median about 0.96); its outgoing transitions, previously spread over many activities (largest share A33, about 21 in 100), now go to A35 in about 90 in 100 cases, with A34 preceding A06 in about 46 in 100 traces (about 2 in 100 before). [OCC002, POS001, DIR001, DIR005, REL011, REL012]
- Smaller shifts: A02 (about 17 → 8 in 100 traces) and A08 (about 14 → 5 in 100) became rarer; both rest on few observations (15 and 10 traces afterwards). [OCC001, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the routing between two existing alternative paths flipped: until the change point roughly three of four cases executed the group A21–A22–A23 (the three activities together, after A19/A20 and before A29/A30/A34/A35) and about one of four executed A24–A25–A26; in the later window the shares are reversed (about 25 vs 76 in 100), with both groups still mutually exclusive, internally intact, and anchored to the same upstream and downstream activities. This rests on mirrored participation shifts of roughly 50 percentage points for all six activities, on many pairwise relations that consistently show the flip while within-group co-occurrence and the surrounding anchors stay in place, and on the redistributed successor profile of A20; the screening produced 79 qualifying items where fewer than one (0.72) would be expected if nothing had changed, so sampling fluctuation cannot explain the pattern as a whole. Limitations: the evidence shows that the branch shares changed, not what drove the change, and the minority branch is observed in only about 50 traces per window, so its internal detail is weakly supported. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the flip reflects a changed mix of cases arriving at the A19/A20 point already routed, rather than a changed choice at that point; both would look identical here. [OCC004, OCC007, REL013, REL016, REL025, REL027, REL049, REL053, DIR002]

Other differences: A06 became about three times more frequent and now occurs late in the trace, typically between A34 and A35 (A34 before A06 in about 46 in 100 traces, A06 before A35 in about 49 in 100, and about 90 in 100 of A06's outgoing transitions go to A35), whereas before it occurred mid-trace with successors spread across many activities; separately, A02 and A08 became rarer, though these decreases rest on few observations.
