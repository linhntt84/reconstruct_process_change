### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that ordering constraints among A20, A22, A23, A24, A25 and A26 tightened: in the earlier window these activities were executed in varying orders with no fixed order between several pairs, while in the later window they follow one fixed sequence, A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25, after which A25 leads to A32. The signs are direct and consistent: all eight pair relations flipped or fixed in the same direction (from mixed or reversed to about 98–99 in 100 in one order), six successor profiles concentrated from 43–66 in 100 shares to about 99 in 100 on single successors, and the position shifts (A20/A22/A23 earlier, A24/A25/A26 later) agree with that chain. Participation of all six is unchanged at 100 in 100 traces in both windows, so none of them was added, removed or made optional. The volume of qualifying items (27 against roughly 0.5 expected without change) makes a chance explanation of the whole pattern implausible. Limitations: DIR shares are over outgoing transitions rather than cases, and the evidence cannot show whether the earlier flexibility was concurrent execution or several alternative sequential routes.
