### Observed differences

- The execution order between two groups of activities flipped. In the earlier window, A27 preceded A35, A36 and A41 in about 99 in 100 traces, and A26 preceded each of the three in about 98–99 in 100 traces. In the later window this is reversed: A35, A36 and A41 each precede A27 in about 97 in 100 traces and precede A26 in about 98 in 100 traces. All six activities still co-occur in 100 of 100 traces in both windows, so none of them became optional or rare. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

- Successor relations moved with the flip. Earlier, about 98 in 100 of A25's observed outgoing transitions went to A27; in the later window they spread almost evenly over A35 (about 32 in 100), A36 (about 31 in 100) and A41 (about 33 in 100). Earlier, about 99 in 100 of A26's outgoing transitions went to one of A35, A36 or A41; later, about 98 in 100 go to A30. Earlier, each of A35, A36 and A41 passed to A30 in roughly 28–34 in 100 of its outgoing transitions; later that handover has nearly disappeared (at most about 0.5 in 100) and each of the three instead passes to A27 in about 31–33 in 100. Within the trio, the mutual order stays mixed in both windows, with each of the three passing to the others in roughly a third of its outgoing transitions. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

- Event positions shifted in the same direction: the mean relative position of A35, A36 and A41 moved from about 0.58 to about 0.42, while A27 moved from about 0.38 to about 0.47 and A26 from about 0.53 to about 0.62, with A27 still tending to run before A26 in both windows. These are positions within the trace, not clock times, and each activity occurs in all 200 traces of each window. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering hypothesis: the block of activities A35, A36 and A41, which in the earlier window ran after A27 and A26 (roughly A25 → A27 → A26 → {A35, A36, A41} → A30), in the later window runs before them (roughly A25 → {A35, A36, A41} → A27 → A26 → A30). A27 and A26 kept their participation and their relative order to each other; what changed is their placement relative to the trio, and the handover to A30 moved from the trio to A26. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Supporting signs: the order reversal is shown directly by six relational items covering every combination of the trio with A27 and A26, each moving from about 98–99 in 100 one way to about 97–98 in 100 the other way; the five successor profiles change exactly as this swap requires; and the position distributions shift consistently. Unchanged participation (co-occurrence in 100 of 100 traces, about 200 traces behind each item in both windows) rules out activities being added, removed or made optional, and the fixed-to-fixed order flip is not a shift in how often existing paths are taken. The 16 qualifying items compare with about 0.5 expected by chance among the 106 tested units, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 in 1,000 random re-splits; the items largely describe one underlying reordering rather than independent changes. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Limitations: the aggregate evidence cannot distinguish whether the trio moved earlier or the pair A27/A26 moved later, since both describe the same observed swap, and it cannot show whether every case now follows the new order or whether two existing execution variants exchanged their shares, as each window is dominated by a single order. Activities outside the tested pairs, such as A30's own successors, are only indirectly evidenced. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

Other differences: a few rare successor edges (about 0.5 in 100 of a source's outgoing transitions) appear in only one window each, for example from A25 to A04, A14, A21, A33, A38 or A45 and from A26 to A02 or A42 in the later window; they are too rare to support any separate conclusion.
