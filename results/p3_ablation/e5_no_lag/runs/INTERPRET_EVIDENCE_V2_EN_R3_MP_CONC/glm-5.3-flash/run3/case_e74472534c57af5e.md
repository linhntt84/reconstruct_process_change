### Observed differences

- The main difference is repetition of A30, A31 and A36. In the earlier window each of the three occurred once in virtually every trace (participation 100 in 100; repeats in at most about 1 in 100 traces). In the later window each occurs about 3.5 times per trace on average, and about 68–69 in 100 traces contain repeats. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The redirection behind this sits at A36: in the earlier window about 98 in 100 of A36's outgoing transitions went to A25; in the later window about 71 in 100 go back to A30 and about 28 in 100 to A25. Meanwhile A30 → A31 and A31 → A36 stay at about 97–99 in 100 in both windows, and the observed outgoing transitions of A30, A31 and A36 roughly tripled (A30: about 202 to 698), matching the ~3.5 occurrences per trace. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- Pairwise order mirrors the same behaviour: A30/A36, A14/A30 and A14/A36 still co-occur in 100 in 100 traces, but the previously fixed order (A30 or A14 first) fell from about 98–100 in 100 traces to about 31–32 in 100, with about 68 in 100 now interleaved or repeated — the same share as the repetition frequency, so these items reflect the repeats rather than an independent reordering. [REL003, REL004, REL005]
- The event positions of A30 and A36 spread later into the trace: A30's spread roughly doubled (standard deviation about 0.06 to 0.12; upper quartile about 0.45 to 0.52 of relative event position), and A36 shows the same pattern. [POS003, POS004]
- At A05, outgoing transitions redistributed: to A14 fell from about 35 to 18 in 100, to A07 rose from about 19 to 39 in 100 (to A09 about 42 to 39 in 100); A05 also appears slightly earlier (mean relative event position about 0.07 to 0.05) and in slightly fewer traces (about 55 vs 52 in 100). [DIR001, POS001]
- A14's position became about three times more variable (standard deviation about 0.02 to 0.05) with a slightly earlier mean, and small new outgoing transitions appear (about 3–4 in 100 to A06), while A14 → A15 stays dominant (about 97 to 91 in 100). [POS002, DIR002]

Every listed difference is larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, DIR005, REL005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the segment A30 → A31 → A36 repeats within traces: A36, which earlier proceeded almost always to A25, now returns to A30 in about 71 in 100 of its transitions, so the chain A30 → A31 → A36 runs about 3.5 times per trace before exiting to A25 (about 28 in 100 of A36's transitions). Supporting signs: all three activities still run in every trace in both windows, so this is a change in repetition rather than in participation; the recurrence share rose from about 0–1 to about 69 in 100 traces; the redirection at A36 is direct and large while the A30 → A31 and A31 → A36 shares are essentially unchanged; the positional spread of A30 and A36 fits repetitions extending later into traces; and 17 qualifying items against roughly 0.5 expected without change means chance alone cannot account for the set. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS003, POS004, REL005]

The evidence cannot distinguish a single behaviour in which every trace loops with roughly a 28 in 100 exit chance per iteration from a mixture of traces with a single pass (about 31 in 100) and traces with several repeats, nor show what determines the exit. [REL003, REL005, DIR005]

Other differences: at A05, routing shifted (A05 → A14 halved to about 18 in 100, A05 → A07 doubled to about 39 in 100) and A05 appears slightly earlier and in slightly fewer traces; the changed A05/A30 and A05/A36 relations (fixed order from about 55 to 17 in 100, interleaved to about 35 in 100) match the loop frequency and likely reflect the A30/A36 repetition rather than an independent A05 change, but the evidence cannot confirm this. A14's position became more variable with small new outgoing transitions. Whether the A05 shift is connected to the loop or independent, and how much of these smaller shifts could be sampling variation, cannot be determined from this evidence.
