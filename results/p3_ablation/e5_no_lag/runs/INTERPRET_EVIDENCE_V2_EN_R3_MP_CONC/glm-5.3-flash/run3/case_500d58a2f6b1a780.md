### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the repeated execution of the activity group A25, A26 and A31 stopped. In the earlier window these activities formed a repeating cycle — each ran about 3.4 times per trace, A31 led back to A25 in about 71% of its outgoing transitions, and A25 and A31 were interleaved or repeated in about 75 in 100 traces. In the later window each activity runs once per trace, A31 proceeds to A20 in about 96% of its outgoing transitions, and A25 precedes A31 in a fixed order. The support is direct (occurrence, successor and relation indicators each show the change on their own scale), consistent (all seven qualifying items describe this one behaviour, against roughly 0.29 items expected by chance, each with empirical strength about 0.995), and the participation of all three activities remains at 100% in both windows, so the change is in repetition, not in presence. The essentially unchanged mean positions of A25 and A31 argue against an alternative reading that the activities were relocated. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: A26 has no directional or relational evidence, so its place within the repeated group is inferred only from its occurrence pattern matching A25 and A31; outgoing transitions of A25 and A26 were not profiled, so the full cycle structure cannot be confirmed; and a small residual repetition (mean 1.04, recurrence in about 1 in 200 traces) cannot be distinguished from complete removal of the loop.
