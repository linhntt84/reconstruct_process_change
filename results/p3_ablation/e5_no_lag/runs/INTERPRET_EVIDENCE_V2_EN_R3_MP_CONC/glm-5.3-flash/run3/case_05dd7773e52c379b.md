### Observed differences

- Six activities became near-universal. In the earlier window, A28, A30 and A31 each appear in about 49 in 100 traces and A32, A33 and A34 in about 52 in 100; in the later window, all six appear in about 99–100 in 100 traces, still at most once per trace. - The two triads were alternatives and now run in sequence. Earlier, no trace contains activities from both triads (for example, A28 and A32 never co-occur; each appears without the other in roughly half the traces). In the later window, every cross-triad pair co-occurs in about 98 in 100 traces and always in one order: A28, A30 or A31 before A32, A33 or A34. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the exclusive choice between the two triads was replaced by sequential execution of both: in the later window, A28, A30 and A31 and A32, A33 and A34 — which earlier never ran in the same case, each case executing exactly one triad in a roughly 50/50 split — now run together in about 98 in 100 cases, always with the first triad before the second, so that the second triad sits between the first triad and A26, whose position accordingly moves later (about 0.80 to 0.83). The support is direct and consistent: six cross-triad pairs flip from zero co-occurrence to near-universal co-occurrence in a single order, participation of all six activities rises to nearly every trace, and the transitions are rewired exactly as this structure implies; 45 qualifying items against roughly 0.5 expected without change also makes chance an implausible explanation of the pattern as a whole. [REL015, REL019, REL022, REL010, OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS002]

The evidence cannot rule out that the old exclusive path still occurs in the roughly 1–2 in 100 later traces that miss a triad, nor distinguish a hard sequencing constraint from an extremely skewed routing preference, and it covers event order and positions rather than clock time. [REL015, REL016, REL019, POS003]

Other differences: A25's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (about 0.67 to 0.62), consistent with more activities now following it. [POS001]
