### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A10 changed from a rare, late, loosely connected activity into a frequent, early step in the main flow: in the later window it appears in about 70 in 100 traces near the start of the trace, is reached mainly from A14 and hands control mainly to A15, and it now typically executes before A20, A22–A26, A31, A32 and A45–A48, with which it previously rarely co-occurred. [OCC003, POS001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]

The change is shown directly by participation, positional, successor and relational indicators that all move consistently, with strong backing (140 of 200 traces contain A10 in the later window), and the number of qualifying items (45 against roughly 0.62 expected if nothing had changed) makes sampling noise unlikely as an explanation of the pattern as a whole. [OCC003, POS001, DIR002, DIR003, REL003] Limitations: the indicators overlap heavily (OCC003, POS001 and the REL items largely describe the same traces), so they are not independent confirmations; and the evidence cannot distinguish whether A10 became a step on a single modified main path or whether the later window blends the earlier behaviour — still visible in the about 30 in 100 of traces where A20–A26 run without A10 — with a new routing through A10. [REL003, REL005, REL008]
