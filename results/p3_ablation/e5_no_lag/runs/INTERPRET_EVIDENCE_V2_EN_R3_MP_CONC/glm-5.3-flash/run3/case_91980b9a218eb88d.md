### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 were early, mutually exclusive alternatives: each ran in about 38, 25 and 39 of 100 traces, roughly a third of the way through the trace (mean relative event position about 0.32–0.33), and 92–99 of 100 of their outgoing transitions went to A26. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the choice block A19/A20/A25 changed its placement and wiring: earlier the flow ran A45 → one of A19/A20/A25 → A26, with the block about a third into the trace; in the later window A45 leads directly to A26, and A19/A20/A25 run after A26 and A27, each leading either to A32 or to A28, which in turn can lead back into A19/A20/A25 — a pattern consistent with the block being repeated after A27/A28. The signs are direct and consistent: the three activities' positions shifted from about 0.32 to about 0.60 of the trace, all six order relations with A26/A27 flipped, their successor profiles changed from A26 to A28/A32, A27 and A28 gained A19/A20/A25 as successors, and A45 now bypasses the block. Twenty of 110 control-flow units qualified, against well under one expected if nothing had changed, so the set as a whole is not a sampling artefact. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine relocation of A19/A20/A25 from the early block being removed and a similar block added after A27/A28, and the repetition reading rests on order data that show mixed sequences without proving the cycle is bounded. The main competing explanation — that only the frequencies of unchanged paths shifted — is largely ruled out because the order between the block and A26/A27 reversed and A45 now bypasses the block, which is more than a share shift. [REL001, REL003, REL005, DIR006]

Other differences: A24 was observed in about 43 of 100 traces in the earlier window and about 58 of 100 in the later one (mean occurrences per trace 0.43 vs 0.58); this increase is not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001]
