### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a previously optional segment became part of the standard flow: the sequence A19 → A25 → A20, which about half of the traces skipped in the earlier window, runs in virtually every trace in the later window with the same internal order, and A06 — previously a rare step with no fixed position — now runs in about 73 in 100 traces as an early step between A14 and this block (A14 before A06, A06 before the block, A06 followed by A16 in about 88 in 100 of its outgoing transitions), with A41 now leading into A19 in about 96 in 100 of its outgoing transitions. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL001, REL004, REL018, REL019, REL022, DIR001, DIR006]

The signs are direct and consistent across occurrence, relation, direction and position indicators: participation of A19/A20/A25 rose from about 48 to about 100 in 100 traces while their mutual order stayed fixed; A06's participation tripled while its position moved earlier and its exits concentrated; and the screening produced 41 qualifying items where only about 0.6 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits of a stable process produce. Limitations: the many occurrence and relation items describe overlapping aspects of the same traces rather than independent confirmations, A06's order relative to A15/A16 remains partly interleaved (about 34 in 100 traces), and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows capture a different mix of case types — ones that always execute this block — rather than a modified process.
