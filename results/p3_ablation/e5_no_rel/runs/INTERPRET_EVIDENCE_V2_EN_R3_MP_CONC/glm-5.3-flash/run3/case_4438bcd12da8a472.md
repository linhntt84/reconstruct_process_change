### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-activity group A14, A15 and A20, which sits between A36 and A21, changed from optional to nearly mandatory. In the earlier window, roughly 47 in 100 cases executed the group (A36 into A14, with A21 following four steps later) while roughly 53 in 100 skipped it and went directly from A36 to A21; in the later window, about 199 in 200 cases execute the group and the direct A36–A21 route is almost never taken. The participation of all three group activities, the routing out of A36, and the A36–A21 distance show this directly and consistently, and A21 and A36 were present in all traces in both windows, so the change concerns the group between them rather than their own execution. The seven qualifying items are roughly twenty times the number expected by chance for the 68 tested control-flow units (about 0.34), so the ensemble is not what an unchanged process typically produces.
