### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A14, A15 and A16 each ran in roughly a third of cases (about 35, 30 and 36 in 100 traces respectively), exactly once when present, and every observed outgoing transition of each of them led to A24. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the segment between A39 and A24: in the earlier window, A39 was followed by exactly one of three alternative activities — A14, A15 or A16, each taken in roughly a third of the cases — with A24 following two steps later; in the later window, A39 is followed by all three of A21, A22 and A23, each executed exactly once in an unfixed order, with A24 following four steps later. Participation of A14/A15/A16 fell from about 30–36 in 100 traces to none observed, while A21/A22/A23 rose from none observed to all 200 traces, and A39's outgoing transitions were fully exchanged from the old trio to the new one. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007]

The relational evidence corroborates this directly: the A39→A24 distance grew from always 2 to always 4 steps, showing that a genuinely larger block now sits between the two activities rather than a mere relabeling of the single intervening one, and the transition mesh among A21, A22 and A23 into A24 is consistent with the three executing in varying order. A24's slightly later relative position is consistent with the larger preceding block. Screening qualified 21 control-flow items where roughly 0.36 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by random re-splits; note, however, that the items are largely facets of this one underlying difference, not independent confirmations. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, POS007]

Limitations: zero participation only means an activity was not observed in that window, so the evidence cannot distinguish a removal of A14, A15 and A16 from these activities still occurring so rarely that 200 traces miss them; and the exact rule behind the varying order of A21–A23 (for example, whether all orderings occur equally often) cannot be confirmed from these indicators. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR004]
