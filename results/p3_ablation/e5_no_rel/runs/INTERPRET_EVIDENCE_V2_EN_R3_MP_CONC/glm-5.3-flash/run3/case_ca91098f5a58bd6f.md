Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the group of activities A14, A15 and A20, which previously sat between A36 and A21, is no longer executed, and that A36 now leads directly to A21: earlier, A36 was followed by exactly one of the three alternatives in roughly equal shares, and that alternative was followed by A21; in the later window A36 is immediately followed by A21 in every trace while the three activities disappear from all 200 traces.
