### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 are not observed at all (0 of 200 traces each); in the later window they occur in about 30, 41 and 30 in 100 traces respectively, each roughly once per trace when present, and sit around the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.51). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- In the earlier window the flow ran as an immediate chain A27 → A28 → A29: about 99 in 100 of A27's outgoing transitions went to A28, about 99 in 100 of A28's went to A29, and A29 followed A27 two steps later in about 99 in 100 cases. [DIR004, DIR005, LAG002, LAG003]
- In the later window this direct chain is interrupted in most traces: A27 now leads to A28 in only about 44 in 100 cases, and to A20 (about 22), A25 (about 17) or A19 (about 15) in the remainder; A28 now leads to A29 in about 55 in 100 cases and to A20, A19 or A25 together in about 43 in 100. [DIR004, DIR005]
- The new activities lead almost exclusively back into the original chain: about 53–56 in 100 of their outgoing transitions go to A28 and about 42–46 in 100 to A29. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- Distances widen accordingly: A28 follows A27 two steps later in about 55 in 100 cases (previously one step in about 99); A29 follows A27 three steps later in about 97 in 100 (previously two steps in about 99); A29 follows A28 two steps later in about 44 in 100 (previously one step in about 99) — consistent with typically one extra activity sitting between them, before A28 in roughly half of the cases and after A28 in roughly the other half. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, DIR004, DIR005]
- Smaller shifts: A10's participation rises from about 5 to about 13–14 in 100 traces (resting on only 10 versus 27 traces), A27's mean position moves slightly earlier (0.48 to 0.46) and A29's slightly later (0.55 to 0.57). [OCC001, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a group of three previously unobserved activities — A19, A20 and A25 — is executed in roughly 30–41 in 100 traces and is woven into the flow between A27/A28 and A29: A27 and A28 each redirect about half of their outgoing transitions to one of these activities, which in turn lead back to A28 or on to A29, so the formerly immediate A27 → A28 → A29 sequence now typically contains one inserted activity (the A27-to-A29 distance grows from two to three steps in about 97 in 100 cases). Seventeen control-flow items qualified where fewer than one would be expected without a change, and every item's difference exceeds those seen in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, so the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by sampling variation; the participation, routing and distance evidence all describe the same arrangement. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot pin down the fine arrangement — whether A19, A20 and A25 are mutually exclusive alternatives or can co-occur in one trace, and whether, in a given trace, the inserted activity sits between A27 and A28 or between A28 and A29 (both successions are observed). "Not observed" in the earlier window means zero participation in that sample rather than proof of impossibility, and the agreeing indicators partly describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG002]

Other differences: A10 is executed more often (about 5 in 100 traces earlier, about 13–14 in 100 later), though this rests on few observations; the small position shifts of A27 (slightly earlier) and A29 (slightly later) are consistent with the inserted activities but too small to carry weight on their own. [OCC001, POS004, POS005]
