### Observed differences

- Six activities — A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 — ran in every one of the 200 earlier-window traces, but in only about 47–57% of the later-window traces (93–113 of 200). None of them repeats within a trace in either window, so this is lower participation, not fewer repetitions. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- A30's hand-offs changed: in the earlier window about 97% of its outgoing transitions went to A33, A35 or A36 (roughly 34%, 34% and 28%); in the later window about half go directly to A37, A38 or A39 (15–19% each), targets that were practically unobserved earlier. [DIR001]
- A33, A35 and A36 now hand over directly to A31 in about 26–34% of their outgoing transitions, which never happened earlier; their combined transitions onward to A37, A38 and A39 fell from about 30–37% to about 1–2%. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- Pairwise distances compressed: A30 to A37/A38/A39, and A33/A35/A36 to A31, lay 4–5 steps apart in essentially every case earlier; in the later window about 94–99% of the observed pairs are only 1–3 steps apart. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG009]
- Event positions moved consistently with a shorter middle section: A30 (mean position 0.62 → 0.67) and A33/A35/A36 (about 0.68 → 0.74) occur later, A39 slightly earlier (0.77 → 0.73), and A31 slightly earlier (0.82 → 0.80) while still appearing in every trace of both windows. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- A few further lag shifts (for pairs such as A33–A38, A35–A37 and A36–A39) also qualify, but they rest on only 4–5 later-window traces and are weak. [LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39, which earlier spanned the roughly 4–5 steps between A30 and A31, became optional: executed in every case in the earlier window, it is skipped in about half of the later-window cases, and when it does run, the route through it is shortened — A30 can enter directly at A37/A38/A39, and A33/A35/A36 can exit straight to A31, cutting the distance from the block to A31 from 4–5 steps to 1–3. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG004, LAG006, LAG009]

This reading is consistent across all 27 qualifying items, all from the control-flow family, against roughly 0.66 expected without a change, so chance cannot account for the set; the participation drops rest on 200 versus 93–113 observations per activity, the relational items on about 90–135 paired observations per item in both windows, and A31 itself keeps full participation, so it is still reached in every case. [OCC001, DIR001, LAG001, POS002]

The evidence cannot distinguish a single modified flow with a new skip route from a later window that mixes two case flows (one with the full block, one without); the five weak lag items, based on 4–5 traces, cannot settle this. [LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011]

Other differences: A31 occurs slightly earlier in the trace (mean position 0.82 → 0.80) with unchanged participation; this is consistent with the same shortening but was not used as support. [POS002]
