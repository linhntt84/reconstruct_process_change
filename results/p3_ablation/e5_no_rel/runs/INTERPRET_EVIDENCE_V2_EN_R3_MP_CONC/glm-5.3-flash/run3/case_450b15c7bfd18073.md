### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A22 ran in every trace (200 of 200, exactly once per trace); in the later window it runs in about 51 in 100 traces (102 of 200), still once per trace when present [OCC001].
- Three activities that never appeared in the earlier window — A27, A28 and A33 — each run in about 49 in 100 later-window traces (98 of 200), once per trace [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004].
- Routing out of A21 changed: earlier, every observed outgoing transition of A21 went to A22; in the later window about 51 in 100 go to A22, with the rest splitting almost evenly to A28 (about 18 in 100), A33 (about 17 in 100) and A27 (about 15 in 100) [DIR001].
- The three new activities form a connected block with no fixed internal order: A27 is followed by A33 (about 36%), A28 (about 35%) or A23 (about 30%); A28 by A27 (about 43%), A33 (about 31%) or A23 (about 27%); A33 by A23 (about 44%), A28 (about 30%) or A27 (about 27%) [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004].
- The distance from A21 to A23 grew accordingly: earlier, A23 followed A21 two positions later in all cases; in the later window this holds in about 51 in 100 cases, while in about 49 in 100 A23 follows four positions later — consistent with three activities now sitting between them [LAG001].
- The new activities sit around the middle of the trace in the later window (median position roughly 0.48–0.50 of trace length) [POS001, POS002, POS003].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, about half of the executions no longer run the segment A21 → A22 → A23; instead, A21 leads directly into a block of three activities — A27, A28 and A33, executed in varying order — which then reaches A23, while A22 is skipped in exactly those cases. The routing change at A21 is observed directly, and the share of A21 transitions leaving the A22 path (about 49 in 100) matches both the drop in A22 participation and the share of traces where the A21-to-A23 distance grows from two to four steps [DIR001, LAG001, OCC001]. The new activities' outgoing edges connect them to each other and to A23, and their positions fall where the gap opened [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003]. Participation of A22 and of the three new activities shifted by the same amount in opposite directions (about 49 in 100 traces) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]. Twelve evidence items qualified in the control-flow family against roughly 0.33 expected if nothing had changed, so the set as a whole cannot be attributed to random sampling [screening].

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly means A27, A28 and A33 were not observed there, not that they were absent from the underlying process, so an earlier but unobserved rare path cannot be fully ruled out. In addition, the order within the new block varies — each of the three can precede the others — so the exact sequencing rule among A27, A28 and A33 cannot be determined from this evidence.
