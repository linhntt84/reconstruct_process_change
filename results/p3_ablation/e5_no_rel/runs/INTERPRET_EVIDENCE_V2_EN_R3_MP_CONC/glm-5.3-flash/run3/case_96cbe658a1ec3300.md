### Observed differences

- **The boundary after A25 and A26 flipped.** In the earlier window, A25's outgoing transitions went almost exclusively to A27 (about 97 in 100), while A26 fed A35, A36 and A41 in roughly equal shares (about 38, 33 and 28 in 100). In the later window, A26 goes almost exclusively to A30 (about 97 in 100) and A25 feeds that same trio in roughly equal shares (about 31–33 in 100 each), with A25→A27 down to about 1 in 200. Each profile rests on about 200 observed transitions per window. [DIR001, DIR002]

- **The group's exit activity changed.** In the earlier window, each of A35, A36 and A41 led to A30 in about 28–34 in 100 of its transitions; in the later window, A30 is down to about 1 in 200 and A27 appears at about 31–33 in 100, while transitions from each of them to the other two group members are roughly unchanged (about two thirds combined) — the group's internal varying order persists in both windows. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

- **Boundary distances swapped.** A27 followed A25 one step later in about 99 in 100 traces in the earlier window, but four to five steps later in the later window (about 90 and 10 in 100); A30 followed A26 four to five steps later earlier (about 93 and 7 in 100) but one step later in about 98 in 100 traces in the later window. [LAG001, LAG003]

- **Event positions moved accordingly.** The mean fractional position of A35, A36 and A41 moved earlier, from about 0.58 to about 0.42 of the trace; A27 moved later (0.38 to 0.47) and A26 later (0.53 to 0.62). The relative order of A25, A27, A26 and A30 among themselves is the same in both windows; only the group's slot between them moved. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG001, LAG003]

- **Pair co-occurrence within five steps mirrors this, but thinly.** A25 with A35, and A35 with A27, occurred in about 1 in 200 traces earlier versus about 199 in 200 later; A26 with A35/A36/A41 and each group member with A30 show the reverse (about 200 in 200 earlier, 1–2 in 200 later). The distance distributions behind these items rest on only 1–2 observations in one window, so they are weak on their own. [LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the group A35/A36/A41 changed its placement: in the earlier window it ran between A26 and A30 (A25 led directly to A27; A26 led through the group to A30), while in the later window it runs between A25 and A27 (A25 leads through the group to A27; A26 leads directly to A30). Put differently, A27 — previously the immediate successor of A25 — now terminates the group's chain, and A30 — previously the group's endpoint — now immediately follows A26, with the order of A25, A27, A26 and A30 themselves unchanged. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003]

Supporting signs: the successor profiles of all five boundary activities changed exactly as this hypothesis requires, each backed by about 200 observations per window [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]; the two boundary distances swapped symmetrically between one step and four to five steps [LAG001, LAG003]; positions shifted in the same direction [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]; and the control-flow screening produced 20 qualifying items where about 0.6 would be expected without a change, many times what a stable process typically yields [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]. Limitations: the indicators overlap — for example DIR001, LAG001 and POS002 all describe A27's changed relation to A25 — so the items are not independent confirmations, and the pair-level items rest on 1–2 observations in one window [LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that both arrangements coexisted in both windows and the mix tipped from one to the other; the near-pure successor shares (about 98 versus 2 in 100) make a substantial coexistence unlikely, but a few per cent cannot be excluded [DIR001, DIR002].

Other differences: only minor signals remain outside this hypothesis — rare outgoing transitions of about 1 in 200 appear or disappear at A25, A35, A36 and A41, and the spread of event positions widened slightly for A26, A27 and A35; both are small and consistent with the same relocation rather than a separate change. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003]
