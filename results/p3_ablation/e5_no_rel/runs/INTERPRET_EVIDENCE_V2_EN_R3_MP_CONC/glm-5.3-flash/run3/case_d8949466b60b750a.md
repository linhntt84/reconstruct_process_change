### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative route through A32, A33 and A38 between A26 and A28 became optional and is now almost always skipped, while A27 became effectively mandatory. Earlier, about half of the traces entered this group after A26 (each activity in about 48–49 of 100 traces) as an alternative to the direct step to A27; in the later window the group is nearly unobserved (about 1 in 200 traces per activity), A26 leads to A27 in about 97 of 100 outgoing transitions, and A27's participation rose from about 52 to about 99.5 in 100 traces, leaving the direct route A26 → A27 → A28 in nearly all cases. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, LAG001]

The pattern is direct and consistent across occurrence, routing and distance indicators, and the screening produced 21 items where fewer than one would be expected without change, so chance sampling of rare variants cannot explain the pattern as a whole. [screening] Limitations: the group still occurs in about 1 of 200 later traces, so the evidence shows near-disuse rather than certain removal; the group's later internal ordering is based on single traces [DIR003, DIR004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]; and a competing explanation — that the choice at A26 merely became near-deterministic in favour of the direct path while the group remains reachable in rare cases — cannot be ruled out.

Other differences: A28's mean position shifted slightly earlier (about 0.55 to 0.53 of the trace), consistent with the intermediate group being bypassed [POS001]; A26 shows a few new successors observed only in single traces (A08, A13, A17, A24, A25, A28) and no longer A06 [DIR001]; A27's own outgoing behaviour is essentially unchanged, still almost always followed immediately by A28 [DIR002, LAG004].
