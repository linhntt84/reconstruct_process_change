### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A26, A27 and A28 each ran exactly once in every one of the 200 traces; in the later window they are nearly gone, appearing in only 1–2 of 200 traces [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- In the earlier window, A19 and A20 were not observed at all and A21 appeared in a single trace; in the later window each runs at most once per case, in about 31, 34 and 37 in 100 traces respectively (62, 67 and 74 of 200) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A44, present in all traces in both windows, previously led almost exclusively to A26, A27 and A28 (about 33%, 28% and 32% of its outgoing transitions); in the later window it leads mostly to A19, A20 and A21 (about 27%, 30% and 36%), with only a small residual share to other activities [DIR007]
- In the later window, A19, A20 and A21 each lead almost always directly to A29 (about 97 in 100 of their observed outgoing transitions); the only earlier occurrence of A21 led to A07 instead, and A19 and A20 had no observed outgoing transitions before [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- In the earlier window, the three old activities followed one another in varying order: each led to either of the other two in roughly two thirds of its outgoing transitions and to A29 in about one third [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- The distance from A44 to A29 shortened: in the earlier window A29 followed A44 typically 4 steps later (93 in 100 cases, the rest at 5); in the later window it follows typically 2 steps later (92 in 100); both activities occur in every trace in both windows [LAG010]
- The new activities occupy about the same relative position the old block held (mean position roughly 0.36–0.37 of the trace in both windows); A29 sits slightly earlier in the later window (mean 0.43 to 0.40) and A44 slightly later (0.31 to 0.33) — position shifts alone do not show that an activity was moved [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the segment between A44 and A29: in the earlier window every case executed the three-activity block A26, A27, A28 consecutively between A44 and A29 (in varying order), whereas in the later window that block is no longer executed and a single activity chosen from A19, A20 and A21 — whose participation shares together add up to about one per case, suggesting they act as alternatives — runs between A44 and A29 and leads almost directly to A29, shortening the A44-to-A29 path from about four steps to about two [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, LAG010]

The support is direct and consistent: the participation changes are far beyond sampling variation (200 of 200 vs 1–2 of 200, and 0 of 200 vs 62–74 of 200), the rerouting is observed at A44 itself, which is present throughout, and the distance change rests on the A44–A29 pair observed in essentially all traces of both windows; the screening produced 31 qualifying items where fewer than 1 would be expected without a change, so the set as a whole is not a chance artefact, although many items describe this same single underlying difference rather than independent confirmations [OCC001, OCC004, DIR007, LAG010]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a restructuring of one path from a shift in the mix between two case variants, because A19 and A20 were entirely absent earlier while A26, A27 and A28 now survive in only 1–2 traces; whether the old block was removed or reduced to a rare residual path cannot be decided from these windows [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC001, OCC002]

Other differences: the later-window routing and lag profiles of the disappearing activities (for example A26 now leading to A27 and A33, A28 to A26 and A07) rest on only one or two traces each and were not used as independent evidence [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
