### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A22 appeared in about 52 of 100 traces; in the later window it appears in nearly all traces (199 of 200), still at most once per trace. [OCC001]
- A27, A28 and A33 each appeared in about 48 of 100 traces in the earlier window, but in only 1 of 200 traces in the later window — they were practically no longer executed. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- The outgoing transitions of A21 were redistributed: earlier they led to A22 in about half of the cases and to A33, A28 or A27 in the rest (roughly 18%, 17% and 14%); in the later window about 995 in 1000 of them lead to A22. [DIR001]
- The distance from A21 to A23 collapsed accordingly: earlier A23 followed A21 within 2 steps in about half of the traces and at 4 steps in the other half; in the later window it follows within 2 steps in about 995 of 1000 traces. [LAG001]
- In the earlier window, A27, A28 and A33 mainly led to one another and then on to A23, forming the alternative route; in the later window this block is observed in a single trace, so its current shape cannot be characterised. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]
- A23 occurs in every trace in both windows; its relative position moved slightly earlier (mean relative position about 0.55 to 0.53), which is a positional, not a clock-time, observation. [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the route through A27, A28 and A33 between A21 and A23 is skipped: in the earlier window traces split roughly in half between A21 → A22 → A23 and a longer route passing through A27/A28/A33 before reaching A23, whereas in the later window nearly all traces run A21 → A22 → A23, with A21 now leading almost directly to A22 and the group A27/A28/A33 bypassed. Supporting signs: the participation of A27, A28 and A33 fell from about 48 in 100 traces to 1 in 200, A22 rose from about 52 in 100 to 199 in 200 traces, A21's outgoing flow concentrated on A22, and the A21→A23 distance shortened from a mix of 2 and 4 steps to almost always 2 steps. The 20 qualifying control-flow items far exceed the roughly 0.38 expected by chance for the 76 tested units, and the slight forward shift of A23's position is consistent with the same behaviour rather than an independent confirmation. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, LAG001, POS001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a complete removal of the A27/A28/A33 route from a drastic reduction to a very rare path, since the block still appears in 1 of 200 later traces; moreover, the later-window structure of that route rests on a single trace per indicator, so how it executes today cannot be described and a competing reading of its current wiring cannot be ruled out. [OCC002, DIR002, LAG002]
