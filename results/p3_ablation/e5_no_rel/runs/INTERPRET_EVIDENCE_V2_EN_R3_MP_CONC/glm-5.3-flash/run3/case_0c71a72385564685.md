### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 were essentially unobserved (A19 and A25 in 0 of 200 traces, A20 in 1 of 200); in the later window they occur in about 38, 30 and 35 in 100 traces respectively (76, 59 and 69 of 200), each roughly 0.3–0.4 times per trace and without repeating within a trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the later window these three activities sit at about 35–37% of the trace length, just before A26, which occurs in every trace in both windows and sits at about 37% of the trace before and about 40% after. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]
- A41's routing changed markedly: in the earlier window about 82 in 100 of its outgoing transitions went directly to A26; in the later window about 87 in 100 go to A19 (about 33), A25 (about 30) or A20 (about 24), while the direct edge to A26 carries only about 1–2 in 100. [DIR004]
- The new activities feed back into A26: about 95 in 100 of A19's outgoing transitions and about 90 in 100 of A25's go to A26. [DIR002, DIR003]
- The distance from A41 to A26 grew: the pair occurred one step apart in about 82 of 100 earlier cases, but in the later window it is two steps apart in about 83 and three steps in about 14 of 100 cases, with a one-step gap in only about 1 in 100; the pair still occurs within five steps in essentially all traces of both windows. [LAG001]
- Other difference: A02's outgoing transitions were redistributed — A26 (about 26 in 100 earlier) is no longer observed as a successor, A16 rose to about 18 in 100, and small new edges (including to A19, A20 and A25) appeared; this rests on only about 35 versus 44 outgoing transitions and is weak. [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window a previously unobserved step is inserted between A41 and A26: where A41 was typically followed directly by A26 (about 82 in 100 of its transitions), it is now typically followed by one — occasionally two — of A19, A20 or A25 (about 87 in 100 combined), which in turn lead predominantly to A26, so the A41-to-A26 gap moves from one step to mostly two or three steps while the pair itself still occurs within five steps in all traces. [DIR004, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001]

Supporting signs: the three inserted activities appear in roughly 30–38 in 100 traces after being unobserved, at positions just before A26; the twelve qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.47 expected by chance among the 95 tested units; and the occurrence, direction, position and lag evidence form one coherent picture, although several items describe the same underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

Limitations: the evidence cannot fully resolve the internal arrangement of the inserted segment — whether A19, A20 and A25 act as alternatives at a single insertion point or sometimes form a short chain (the three-step gap in about 14 of 100 cases suggests the latter occasionally) — and A20's outgoing transitions are not directly evidenced. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that these activities are also reached from predecessors other than A41, since only their outgoing transitions were observed. [LAG001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: A02's successor distribution changed (A26 no longer observed, A16 and small new edges gained), which may reflect the same rerouting but is based on few observations and is not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR001]
