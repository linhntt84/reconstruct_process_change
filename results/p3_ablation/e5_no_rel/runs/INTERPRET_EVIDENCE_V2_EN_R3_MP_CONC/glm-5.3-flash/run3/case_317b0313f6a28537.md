- The activities A19, A20 and A25 each occur in roughly a third of traces in both windows (about 31–39 in 100), but their placement changed: in the earlier window they sat at about 0.60–0.61 of the normalized trace position, in the later window at about 0.32–0.34. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of activities A19, A20 and A25 was relocated. In the earlier window the trio executed in the later-middle of the trace: it was entered from A27 (about half of A27's outgoing transitions, plus about 47 in 100 from A28) and led almost always to A28 or A32. In the later window it executes early in the trace: it is entered from A45 (about 85 in 100 of A45's outgoing transitions) and leads almost always to A26, while A27 now bypasses the trio and runs directly to A28 (about 98 in 100) and A28 directly to A32 (about 96 in 100). The change is shown directly in the successor profiles, the position shift is confined to the trio while A26, A27 and A28 stay in place, and the lag changes (A27→A28 and A28→A32 collapsing to immediate succession, A45→A26 stretching from one to two-three steps) all match the same rewiring. With 20 qualifying items against roughly 0.57 expected by chance across the 115 tested control-flow units, sampling variation cannot explain the pattern as a whole, although most items describe one underlying rewiring rather than independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

Limitations: the near-disappearance of the old pairs A19→A32, A25→A28 and A25→A32 rests on only 1, 1 and 3 after-window observations, so their later lag profiles cannot be described reliably; and small shares of the earlier routing remain (about 1–3 in 100 transitions, for example A25→A32), so the evidence cannot establish whether the old routing survives in a small minority of cases or disappeared entirely. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

Other differences: A15 repeats less often per trace, at about 1.6 occurrences in the later window versus about 1.9 earlier, while still appearing in every trace; this repetition change is separate from the relocation hypothesis. [OCC001]
