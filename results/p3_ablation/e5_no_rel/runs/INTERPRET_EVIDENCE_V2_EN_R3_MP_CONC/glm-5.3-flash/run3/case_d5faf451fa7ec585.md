### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment formed by A14, A15 and A20 — which in the earlier window executed between A22/A23 and A24, one of the three per case (before A23 in about 45 in 100 cases, after it in about 55 in 100) — is no longer executed in the later window, so the flow runs directly from A22 through A23 to A24. Supporting signs: participation of all three activities fell from 28–40 in 100 traces to 0 of 200; A22 and A23 each collapsed to a single observed successor; the A22-to-A24 distance shortened from three to two steps in every trace; and the small opposite position shifts of A22 and A24 fit a shorter middle segment. The screening also flagged 16 control-flow items where roughly 0.33 would be expected by chance in an unchanged process, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of a stable process produce.
