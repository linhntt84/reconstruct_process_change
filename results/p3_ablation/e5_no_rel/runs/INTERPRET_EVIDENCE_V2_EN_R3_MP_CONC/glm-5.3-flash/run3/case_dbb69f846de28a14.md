### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A36, A37 and A42 each ran exactly once in every one of the 200 traces; in the later window each appears in only about 47–48 in 100 traces, still about once when present. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of three activities A36, A37 and A42, which in the earlier window always ran in varying internal order between A25 and A28, became optional and changed place: in the later window A25 leads almost exclusively directly to A28, the block is entered from A26 (about 44 in 100 of A26's outgoing transitions), exits to A31 instead of A28, and alternates with A27 — a later trace typically shows either A26 → A27 → A31 or A26 → A36/A37/A42 → A31, where earlier every trace ran both the block after A25 and A27 after A26. The reroutings are observed directly in the successor profiles of A25, A26, A36, A37 and A42, the A25→A28 distance change rests on about 200 traces in each window, and the participation, direction and position items all point the same way; 26 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.62 expected for an unchanged process places the set as a whole far beyond what chance produces. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS004, POS006]

Limitations: the pair-specific gap items for A25→A36/A37/A42 and block→A28 rest on only 1–2 after-window observations and were not relied upon; the evidence cannot show whether one coordinated move produced the pattern or separate changes to the block's entry, exit and optionality; and strict mutual exclusivity of A27 and the block in every single trace cannot be verified from aggregate counts. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window mixes coexisting old and new routings, since about 1 in 200 of A25's outgoing transitions still point to block activities. [DIR001]

Other differences: minor positional shifts of A25 (mean 0.34 → 0.37) and A31 (0.65 → 0.63), and a few rare new edges at about 1–2 in 100 of a source's outgoing transitions (for example from A25 to A02 or A48, from A36 to A13 or A35, from A37 to A09, A10 or A12); they are consistent with the rerouting above and do not form a separate hypothesis. [POS001, POS005, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004]
