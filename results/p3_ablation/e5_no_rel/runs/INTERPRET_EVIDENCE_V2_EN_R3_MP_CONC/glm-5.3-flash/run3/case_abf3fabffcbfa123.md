### Observed differences

- Three activities — A32, A33 and A38 — each ran in about half of the earlier executions (roughly 97–98 of 200 traces, mean ≈0.49 occurrences per trace); in the later window each appears in only about 1 in 200 traces. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- A27 moved from optional to near-mandatory: earlier it appeared in about 52 in 100 traces (mean ≈0.52 occurrences per trace); in the later window it appears in about 995 in 1000 traces, almost always exactly once (mean ≈1.0). [OCC004]
- Routing out of A26 (present in essentially every trace in both windows) collapsed onto A27: A27 took about 50% of A26's outgoing transitions earlier versus about 98% in the later window, while A32, A33 and A38 together fell from about 48% to about 0.5%. [DIR006]
- The distance from A26 to A28 consolidated: earlier A28 followed A26 two positions later in about 49% of traces and four positions later in about 46%; in the later window about 95% follow at distance two and almost none at distance four. [LAG003]
- A04 became much rarer and, when it occurs, sits later in the trace: participation fell from about 68% to about 22% of traces (mean occurrences 0.71 → 0.24), and its mean relative position rose from about 0.21 to about 0.40, with the upper quartile jumping from about 0.19 to 0.70. [OCC002, POS001]
- A14 (present in every trace in both windows) now leads mostly to A15 instead of A04: A04's share of A14's outgoing transitions fell from about 64% to about 10% while A15's rose from about 36% to about 82%; immediate A14→A04 succession fell from about 99% to about 72% of observed pairs, while immediate A14→A15 rose from about 19% to about 47%. - A02 and A08 occur in more traces in the later window (A02 about 5% → 15%; A08 about 5% → 21%) with substantially reshuffled outgoing transitions; these profiles rest on few observations (about 8–10 outgoing transitions earlier). [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003]
- A15's dominant transition to A16 is essentially unchanged (about 98% → 92% of its outgoing transitions), as are the positions of A14 and A28. [DIR005, POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative branch after A26 — the activities A32, A33 and A38, each of which ran in about half of the earlier executions — was removed or bypassed, with execution consolidating onto A27, which became a mandatory once-per-trace step; as a result, A28 now follows A26 at a fixed short distance (two positions in about 95% of later traces versus a 49%/46% split between distances two and four earlier). Because the three branch activities are nearly absent in the later window, this reads as activities being dropped or a block being bypassed, not as a re-weighting between surviving paths. The change is shown directly and consistently by occurrence, routing, position and gap indicators; the A26 routing profile rests on about 200 traces per window, and the screening produced 32 qualifying items against roughly 0.64 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits produce. Limitations: the later-window profiles of A32, A33 and A38 each rest on a single trace, so their post-change routing is unobservable, and zero participation only means the activities were not observed — an extremely rare continuation cannot be ruled out; several items (the three occurrence, three position and several gap items) describe the same underlying traces and are not independent confirmations. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR006, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG003]

Other differences: A14 now leads directly to A15 in most executions, and A04 participates far less often (about 68% → 22% of traces), sitting later in the trace when it does occur; A04's own outgoing transitions also spread (its A15 share fell from about 90% to about 48%). [OCC002, DIR002, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, POS001] A02 and A08 participate more often with reshuffled successors, though on few observations. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003] A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that A32, A33 and A38 still occur but were simply not sampled in the later window; the data only show they were observed in about 1 in 200 traces rather than not at all.
