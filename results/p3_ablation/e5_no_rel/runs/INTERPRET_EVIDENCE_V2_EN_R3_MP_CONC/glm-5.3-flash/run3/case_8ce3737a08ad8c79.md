- The clearest difference is a swap in how often two groups of activities run. In the earlier window, A21, A22 and A23 each appeared in about 19 in 100 traces; in the later window, each appears in about 79 in 100. Conversely, A24, A25 and A26 each fell from about 82 in 100 traces to about 21 in 100. Each of these activities still occurs at most once per trace in both windows. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the two existing alternative continuations after A20 are taken with very different frequencies: the branch through A21, A22 and A23 rose from roughly 19 in 100 traces to roughly 79 in 100, while the branch through A24, A25 and A26 fell from roughly 82 in 100 to roughly 21 in 100. This is a shift between existing paths rather than a replacement: all six activities remain observed in substantial shares in both windows, A20 still leads to all of them, and repetition stays near zero throughout. The change is shown directly by the successor redistribution at A20 and consistently by the six occurrence indicators moving in the same direction with 37 to 164 supporting traces per window, each difference exceeding about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits — though the occurrence items partly describe the same underlying behaviour rather than independent confirmations. [DIR003, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]

Screening flagged 13 control-flow items where fewer than 1 would be expected without a change across 133 tested units, so the ensemble is far beyond what a stable process typically produces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, LAG001]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that an upstream change altered the mix of cases reaching A20 rather than the branching at A20 itself; the data show the shifted shares but not the decision structure behind them. [DIR003]

Other differences: A10 occurs in fewer traces and earlier within them, its outgoing transitions spread from almost exclusively A33 to seven activities, and the A32→A10 step is nearly gone from A32's outgoing transitions; A02 also appears in somewhat more traces with redistributed successors, though on very few observations. These form a separate cluster not covered by the main hypothesis. [POS001, DIR002, DIR004, LAG001, OCC001, DIR001]
