### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A25, A26 and A31 each occurred exactly once in every trace (participation 100 in 100, no repetitions). In the later window, each of the three occurs about 3.5 times per trace on average and repeats within about 68 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The flow out of A31 changed: earlier, all of A31's observed outgoing transitions (200 in total) led to A20; in the later window, about 71 in 100 of A31's outgoing transitions (of roughly 700) lead to A25, while about 29 in 100 still lead to A20. [DIR001]
- The fixed spacing between A25 and A31 dissolved: earlier, A25 was followed by A31 exactly two positions later in essentially every trace; in the later window this two-step gap accounts for only about 12 in 100 of the measured pair gaps, with about 5 in 100 at five steps. [LAG001]
- A25 and A31 now occur across a much wider band of trace positions (spread roughly doubled, e.g. standard deviation from about 0.06 to 0.12), while their median positions moved only slightly (A25: 0.40 to 0.43; A31: 0.47 to 0.48) — a pattern consistent with the activities recurring at varying spots rather than being relocated. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Main hypothesis: in the later window, the block A25 → A26 → A31 is repeated: A31, which previously always passed control on to A20, now typically routes back to A25, so the three activities run through their cycle roughly two to three extra times per trace before the flow exits to A20 as before. In the earlier window, each of the three activities ran exactly once per trace and A31 always continued to A20; A26 repeats in lockstep with the other two and belongs to the repeated block. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001]

The repetition is shown directly by the occurrence indicators and the rerouting directly by the redirection indicator, while the dissolved two-step A25–A31 gap and the widened position spread fit the same loop behaviour. The rerouting share (about 0.713) almost exactly equals the share of extra occurrences (2.485 of 3.485 per trace), pointing to one mechanism rather than two unrelated changes. Seven items qualified against roughly 0.29 expected for an unchanged process, and because all three activities appear in 100 in 100 traces of both windows (200 traces each), a rare variant or sampling fluctuation cannot explain the differences. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, LAG001, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: the transitions inside the repeated block (A25 to A26, A26 to A31) are not directly observed in the later window, so the internal order of the cycle is inferred from the earlier window's spacing and the matching occurrence statistics; the evidence also cannot distinguish a newly added A31→A25 transition from an existing but previously unobserved one whose share grew. In addition, about 32 in 100 later traces still show each activity only once, so the looping is not universal. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC002]
