### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window one of three activities — A14, A15 or A20 — runs between A36 and A21 in nearly every trace, whereas earlier A36 was immediately followed by A21 in essentially all cases. The three together now account for about 99 in 100 of A36's outgoing transitions (A14 about 37%, A20 about 34%, A15 about 29%), each of them in turn leads only to A21, the A36–A21 distance moved from one to two steps in about 99% of cases, and the new activities sit at relative position about 0.35 just before A21, which itself shifted slightly later — a picture consistent with one new step per trace between the two activities.
