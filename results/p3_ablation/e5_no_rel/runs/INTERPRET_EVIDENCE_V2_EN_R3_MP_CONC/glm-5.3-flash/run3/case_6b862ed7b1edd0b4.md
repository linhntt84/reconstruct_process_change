Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the sequence A19 → A25 → A20, which follows A41, changed from being executed in only about half of the cases in the earlier window to being executed in nearly every case in the later window, with A26 now following this sequence (about four steps after A41) instead of directly following A41.
