### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A02 typically mediates the transition from A14 to A15 on the main flow: A14, which previously passed directly to A15 in about 84 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, now passes to A02 in about 60 in 100, and A02 leads to A15 in about 87 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, while A02's participation rose from about 15 to about 65 in 100 traces and it now occurs near the start of traces. The change is shown directly by the successor shift at A14 with support near 200 traces in both windows, and it is consistent across occurrence, direction, position and gap indicators; with 52 qualifying items against roughly 0.82 expected by chance across 165 tested units, the pattern as a whole is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. Limitations: the rerouting is partial, since about 37 in 100 of A14's outgoing transitions still go directly to A15, and the evidence cannot show whether the later window is a single modified process or a mixture of modified and unchanged variants, because the aggregate shares cannot separate coexisting variants.
