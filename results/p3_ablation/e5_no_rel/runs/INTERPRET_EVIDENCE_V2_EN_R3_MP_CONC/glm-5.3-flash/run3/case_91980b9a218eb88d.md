### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the group of activities A19, A20 and A25, which earlier ran between A45 and A26 (A45 branched to one of them, and each of them led on to A26), in the later window runs after A27 and A28 and feeds into A32: A45 now connects directly to A26, the three activities sit later in the trace (mean position from about 0.33 to about 0.6) while A26 and A27 barely moved, their successors changed from A26 to A28/A32, and they are now reached from A27 and A28 within one to two steps — relations virtually absent before. The stretch of A27→A32 from two to three steps fits something being inserted between them. All involved activities occur substantially in both windows (A26, A27, A28, A45 in nearly every trace; A19/A20/A25 in roughly 25–39% before and 30–38% after), so this is a rewiring of existing behaviour, not activities appearing or disappearing. With 23 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.58 expected without change, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole.
