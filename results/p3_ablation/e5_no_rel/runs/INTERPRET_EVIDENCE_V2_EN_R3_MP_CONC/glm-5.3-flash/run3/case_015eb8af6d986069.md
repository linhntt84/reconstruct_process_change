### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering constraints among A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25 and A32 changed: in the earlier window these activities followed one another in varying orders with several alternative successions and some pairs in both directions, while in the later window they run as one fixed sequence A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32, each step immediately after the previous. The change is shown directly by the successor profile of every activity in the group and consistently by about twenty lag profiles that collapse to the exact step distances of a single chain, including long-range distances such as A31 to A26 at five steps; the alternative and reverse successions that carried most of the traffic before drop to a single supporting trace. Screening flagged 61 qualifying items where fewer than 1 would be expected without change, so the overall pattern is not what an unchanged process typically produces. The evidence shows observed orderings rather than the underlying routing rules, so it cannot rule out that the later window draws almost entirely on one variant that already existed rarely before; however, the earlier mixed order rests on about 200 traces per source activity, making pure sampling variation unlikely.
