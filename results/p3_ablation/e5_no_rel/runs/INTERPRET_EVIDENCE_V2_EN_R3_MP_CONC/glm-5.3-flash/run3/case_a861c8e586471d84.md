### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the intermediate block of activities A19, A20 and A25 between A41 and A26 is bypassed in the later window: A41, which previously led to one of the three activities that in turn almost always led to A26, now leads directly to A26, and the three intermediate activities are no longer executed. The change is shown directly by relational evidence with full support in both windows — A41's successor distribution flipped from about 93 in 100 transitions to the trio to about 92 in 100 to A26, and the A41→A26 distance moved from two steps to one — and consistently by the near-total disappearance of A19, A20 and A25 (from about 31–36 in 100 traces to at most 1 of 200). With 15 qualifying items against roughly 0.5 expected by chance across 102 tested units, sampling variation alone cannot account for the evidence set as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, LAG002]

The evidence cannot distinguish a permanent removal of A19, A20 and A25 from these activities becoming optional and simply not taken in the later sample, since zero participation only means they were not observed there; A20 did appear once. In addition, the occurrence, direction and position items partly describe the same underlying trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations of one another. [OCC002]

Other differences: In the single later trace containing A20, its outgoing transition went to A30 rather than A26, but this rests on one observation and is not relied on here. The A24→A31 distance shift and A14's slight positional shift are additional differences not covered by the main hypothesis. [DIR002, LAG001, POS001]
