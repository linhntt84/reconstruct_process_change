### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: the earlier branch, in which A44 was followed by one of A19, A20 or A21 and that activity then led directly on to A29, is no longer observed, and instead a group of three activities — A26, A27 and A28, executed in varying internal order — runs between A44 and A29. The participation movements are far beyond sampling variation (0 of 200 → 200 of 200 for A26 and A27; about 60–71 of 200 → 1 of 200 for A19, A20 and A21), A44's successor profile moved in step with support from essentially every trace in both windows, and the A44→A29 gap grew by about two steps, matching one intermediate activity giving way to three; overall, 29 evidence items qualified where fewer than 1 would be expected without a change. Limitations: the old branch is near-absent (about 1 in 200) rather than proven removed, and because the activity codes are anonymous, the evidence cannot show whether A26/A27/A28 are genuinely new steps or the former steps under different codes; the many items also overlap, since occurrence, direction, position and gap indicators often describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR009, LAG005, POS008]

Other differences: A10 declined — from about 26 in 100 traces to about 11 in 100 — and its outgoing transitions shifted away from the branch activities that have largely disappeared toward a diffuse set of successors, with its position becoming more variable; this is consistent with the loss of its main successors, but the evidence does not show where A10 now fits in the flow.
