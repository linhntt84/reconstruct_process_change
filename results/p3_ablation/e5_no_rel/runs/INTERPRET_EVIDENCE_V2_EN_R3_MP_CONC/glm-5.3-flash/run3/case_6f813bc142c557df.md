### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering constraints among A20, A22, A23, A24, A25 and A26 changed: in the earlier window these activities followed one another in variable, mixed orders, while in the later window they form a fixed sequence A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32, each activity immediately followed by the next. This is shown directly by the outgoing transitions concentrating on single successors, inter-activity distances that almost always match the chain's step distances, and the near-disappearance of the same pairs in the reverse order; the small position shifts of the individual activities are consistent with this re-sequencing. The screening context fits: 49 control-flow items qualified, many times the fewer than 1 expected by chance in an unchanged process. Limitations: the many lag items describe one underlying re-sequencing rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine tightening of the ordering from a shift in the mix of trace variants in which the fixed chain already existed as one variant; positions are measured in event positions, not clock time.
