- In the earlier window, the activities A15, A17, A18, A19, A20 and A21 followed one another in a flexible order: each of them split its outgoing transitions across three or four different successors — for example, after A15 the next activity was A18 in about 62 in 100 transitions, and A19, A21 or A20 in about 12–14 in 100 each; after A26 the next activity split evenly between A19 and A17. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering constraints between A15, A17, A18, A19, A20 and A21 — with A26 upstream and A27 downstream — changed from a largely unfixed order, in which these activities followed one another in varying orders with both directions common, to a single fixed sequence A26 → A17 → A15 → A18 → A19 → A21 → A20 → A27 executed in essentially every later-window case, while each activity's participation stayed at about 200 of 200 traces in both windows. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG002, LAG014]

The change is shown directly by relational evidence rather than inferred from frequencies alone: successor distributions collapsed from three or four alternatives to one (A17 now leads to A15 in about 995 in 1000 of its outgoing transitions versus about 56 in 100 before), and pairwise distances collapsed to the exact chain distances. [DIR002, LAG002, LAG010] The volume of flagged evidence far exceeds what chance produces: 42 control-flow items qualified where about 0.43 would be expected without a change across 87 tested units, the items describe one consistent chain rather than scattered differences, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [DIR001, DIR003, LAG013, LAG027]

Limitations: the evidence shows aggregate ordering across traces, so it cannot distinguish a genuine tightening of the ordering constraint within individual cases from the earlier window simply pooling several fixed variants of which only one remains in the later window. A few flagged distance items rest on a single later-window observation and carry no weight. [LAG014, LAG015, LAG019, LAG021, LAG028]

Other differences: none beyond the reordering above; the positional shifts in POS001–POS006 reflect the same behaviour and are not treated as independent confirmations. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
