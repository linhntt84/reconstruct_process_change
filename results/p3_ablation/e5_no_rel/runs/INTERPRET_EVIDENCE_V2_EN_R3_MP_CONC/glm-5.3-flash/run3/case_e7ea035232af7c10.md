### Observed differences

- Four activities became near-universal: A27 was present in about 54 in 100 traces earlier and in essentially all traces later; A36, A37 and A42 were each present in about 46 in 100 traces earlier and in about 99–100 in 100 later. Each of the four occurs about once per trace when present, in both windows, with no repetition. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A25's routing changed almost completely: earlier about 98 in 100 of its outgoing transitions led to A28; later they split roughly equally among A36, A37 and A42 (about 32–35 in 100 each), with A28 almost no longer observed as its successor. [DIR001]
- The distance from A25 to A28 changed from one position (about 98 in 100 eligible traces earlier) to four positions (about 97 in 100 later), so three activities now lie between them. [LAG001]
- The exits of A36, A37 and A42 changed: earlier each passed control on to A31 in roughly 29–36 in 100 of its outgoing transitions; later A31 is unobserved there and A28 receives about 30–36 in 100, with the rest going to the other two group members. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- A26's routing consolidated on A27: about 54 in 100 earlier (otherwise A36, A37 or A42) against about 98 in 100 later; and A26→A31, earlier two or four positions apart (about 54 vs 44 in 100), is now almost always two positions apart (about 97 in 100). [DIR002, LAG003]
- Relative positions moved accordingly: A36, A37 and A42 now sit much earlier in the trace (mean position about 0.59 → about 0.40), A28 later (about 0.39 → 0.46), and A26, A27 and A31 slightly later. [POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS002, POS003, POS005]
- Weakly based but consistent pairs: A25→A36 and A36→A28, each seen in only one earlier trace, later occur in about 199 traces with distances spread over one to three positions, indicating no fixed order within the group; A37→A31 and A42→A31, each seen in about 92 earlier traces, are almost unobserved later (one trace each). [LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural hypothesis about placement and participation: the group A36, A37, A42 — executed in varying mutual order — appears to have changed from an optional branch after A26, taken in about 46 in 100 cases as an alternative to A27 and leading on to A31, into a group that runs in essentially every case directly after A25 and leads on to A28; at the same time A27, previously observed in about 54 in 100 traces, now runs after A26 in essentially every case, with A31 following two positions after A26. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003]

This rests on direct relational evidence rather than frequency shifts alone: A25's successor changed from A28 to the group while the A25→A28 distance grew from one to four positions, and the group's own successors changed from A31 to A28; participation, direction and position components move in the same direction for every affected activity. The 23 qualifying control-flow items are many times the roughly 0.59 expected for an unchanged process, so sampling variation cannot account for the evidence as a whole, although each item passed a test that a stable process occasionally passes. [DIR001, LAG001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

Limitations and competing explanation: the evidence cannot distinguish a relocation of the existing group from the old branch after A26 being dropped and an equivalent group inserted after A25, since both produce the observed behaviour; the earlier-window distances for A25→A36 and A36→A28 rest on a single observation each, as does the later near-disappearance of A37→A31 and A42→A31; and only the 118 tested units were screened, so changes outside them would not appear. [LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

Other differences: A25's own relative position shifted slightly earlier (mean about 0.36 → 0.35); this minor shift accompanies, but is not part of, the main hypothesis. [POS001]
