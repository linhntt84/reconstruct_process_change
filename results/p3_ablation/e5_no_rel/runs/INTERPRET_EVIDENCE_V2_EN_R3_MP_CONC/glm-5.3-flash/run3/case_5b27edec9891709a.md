### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural rerouting: in the later window, A20 bypasses the group A31/A32/A37 and passes directly to A23, and the group — still executed in varying order — now runs between A21 and A26 in about 47 in 100 traces as an alternative to A22, which runs in about 54 in 100 traces, the two branches being mutually exclusive in essentially every trace. Since before the change both the group and A22 ran in every trace, the hypothesis is that A22 and the group A31/A32/A37 each changed from mandatory to optional (each skipped in roughly half of the traces), and the group's placement effectively moved from between A20 and A23 to between A21 and A26. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The hypothesis is direct and consistent: the successor profiles of A20 and A21 changed exactly as it predicts, the A20→A23 distance changed from four positions to one, the A21→A26 distance split between two and four positions, and the participation and position changes line up with it. Screening tested 80 control-flow units and about 0.4 qualifying items would be expected without a change, yet 25 items qualified — far more than chance can produce — and every item's difference exceeded those seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Limitations: the flagged items largely re-describe one underlying trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations, and the after-window lag profiles for the relocated pairs rest on a single observation each (1 of 200 traces), so those exact lag values are weakly supported [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]. The evidence also says nothing about the segment between A23 and A21 or about activities outside this set, so additional changes there cannot be excluded; and while the volume of qualifying items makes a mere sampling difference between the windows implausible as an explanation of the whole pattern, it cannot be ruled out for any individual share.
