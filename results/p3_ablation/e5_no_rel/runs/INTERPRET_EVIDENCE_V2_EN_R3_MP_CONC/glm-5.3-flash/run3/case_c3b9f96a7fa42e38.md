### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Twenty-seven control-flow items qualified against well under one (about 0.73) expected by chance, so this ensemble of differences is not what an unchanged process typically produces. The evidence supports the possibility that the unordered block formed by A35, A36 and A41, which in the earlier window ran between A25 and A27, is in the later window executed between A26 and A30: A25 now directly precedes A27, and A26 reaches A30 only through the block. This is shown directly by the successor profiles of A25 and A26 and by the block's exits moving from A27 to A30, by the flipped lags (A25→A27 from about four steps to the next event; A26→A30 from the next event to about four steps), by the consistent position shifts (A27 earlier; A35, A36, A41 later; A26 earlier), and by the old within-five-step pairs disappearing while new ones appear. Every activity involved occurs in essentially all traces in both windows, so this is a change in ordering and placement, not activities becoming optional, and not a mere shift in how often fixed alternative paths are taken. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

Limitations: positions and lags are event ranks, not clock times, so no claim about timing is made; the block's internal order is unordered in both windows; and the evidence cannot distinguish a relocation of the block from an equivalent re-wiring (for example A27 being moved earlier to follow A25 while the block is newly attached after A26) that produces the same predecessor, successor and lag pattern. Several items describe the same underlying difference and are not independent confirmations. [LAG005, LAG009, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

Other differences: A06 participates in fewer traces and later within them, its outgoing transitions are more dispersed (A16's share down from about 86 to 39 in 100, on few observations), A15→A06 became rare, and A02 participation rose on small counts (9 vs 23 traces); these upstream changes are not part of the main hypothesis.
