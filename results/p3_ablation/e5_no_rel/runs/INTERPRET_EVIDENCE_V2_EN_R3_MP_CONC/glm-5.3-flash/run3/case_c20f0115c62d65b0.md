### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the optional block formed by A19, A20 and A25 — executed in roughly three to four of ten traces in the earlier window and inserted between A27 or A28 and A29 — is no longer exercised in the later window, so the flow runs directly from A27 to A28 to A29 in immediate succession, with A29 consequently placed slightly earlier in the trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS004]

The participation drops are large and well backed (56–80 before-window traces against a single after-window trace each); the rerouting of A27 and A28 and the lag tightening are observed in essentially all 200 traces of both windows; and the 15 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.49 expected by chance among 98 tested units, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. The occurrence, direction and lag items largely reflect the same underlying traces, so they corroborate one difference rather than confirming it independently. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The evidence cannot distinguish a removal of A19, A20 and A25 from the block remaining in place but almost never being triggered in the later cases, and it offers no insight into why the block is no longer exercised. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: none beyond the above; the apparent new successors and extreme positions of A19, A20 and A25 in the later window are single-observation artefacts rather than behavioural findings. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003]
