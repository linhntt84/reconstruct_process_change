### Observed differences

- Participation changed from optional to near-universal for four activities: in the earlier window A22 ran in about 54 in 100 traces and A31, A32 and A37 in about 46 in 100 each (their trace groups did not overlap, consistent with two alternative branches); in the later window A22 runs in all 200 traces and A31, A32 and A37 in 199 of 200, each exactly once per trace. - In the later window, A20 no longer leads to A23: before, all observed outgoing transitions of A20 went to A23; after, they go to A31, A32 and A37 in roughly equal thirds, with A23 chosen in only about 1 in 200. [DIR001]
- In the later window, A21 always leads to A22: before, about 54 in 100 of A21's outgoing transitions went to A22 and the remainder to A31, A32 or A37. [DIR002]

- In relative event positions, A31/A32/A37 moved clearly earlier (mean about 0.59 to about 0.40), A23 moved later (about 0.39 to 0.46), and A21, A22 and A26 moved slightly later (about 0.54–0.63 to 0.60–0.66). [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural hypothesis: the two blocks that were previously alternatives are now executed together in a fixed arrangement. In the earlier window, A20 led directly to A23, and after A21 each trace took either A22 → A26 (about 54 in 100 traces) or the A31/A32/A37 block ending in A26 (about 46 in 100). In the later window, A20 routes into the A31/A32/A37 block, that block exits to A23 (so A23 follows A20 about four positions later instead of immediately), and A21 always leads to A22 and then to A26. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002]

The rerouting is shown directly by the successor changes at A20, A21, A31, A32 and A37 [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005] and by the changed distances between A20–A23 and A21–A26 [LAG001, LAG002]; it agrees with the four block activities becoming near-universal [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004] and with their position shifts [POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008]. The 19 qualifying items are about 50 times the 0.38 expected without change in the control-flow family, so chance alone is unlikely to explain the set as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: positions are relative event positions, not clock times, so no clock-time claim is made. The internal order among A31, A32 and A37 stays mixed in both windows (each leads to the others in similar shares), so the hypothesis concerns the block's placement and participation, not a fixed internal sequence. The evidence also cannot rule out that the windows sample two pre-existing process variants in different proportions rather than a single modified process. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008]

Other differences: A20 sits slightly earlier in the trace (mean relative position about 0.36 before, 0.34 after) [POS001], and the dispersion of relative positions narrowed for most observed activities, for example A23's spread shrinking from about 0.060 to 0.047 [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005].
