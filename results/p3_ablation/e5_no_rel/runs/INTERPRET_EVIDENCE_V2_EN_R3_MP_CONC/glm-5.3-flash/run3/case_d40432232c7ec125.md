### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two groups of activities exchanged their placement in the flow: in the later window, A20, A22 and A23 run in the first half of the trace and feed A25, while A19, A21 and A24 run in the second half and feed A30, with A29 and A40 now preceding the opposite group than before. The 58 qualifying items far exceed the fewer than one expected by chance among the 175 tested units, and the support is direct and consistent rather than merely numerous: mirror-image position shifts of about 0.26–0.30 of the trace, successor changes of near-100% magnitude on both sides, and precedence relations that switch between about 1 and about 200 of 200 traces, with the occurrence, direction, position and lag indicators all describing the same reordering. The evidence cannot distinguish a reordering within one flow from a shift in the mix of two pre-existing execution variants, and because A19, A21 and A24 appear in only about a third of traces, the swap concerns where that optional group sits relative to the mandatory one.
