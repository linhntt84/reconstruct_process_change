### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the trio A36, A37, A42 changed its place in the process: earlier it always ran together, in mixed order, between A25 and A28, while A27 always followed A26; in the later window A25 leads directly to A28, and the trio runs in only about half of the traces, after A26 and before A31, as the counterpart to A27 — traces without the trio run A27, and the two shares sum to about 100 in 100. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The change is shown directly at the relational level (successor sets of A25 and A26 changed, and the trio's exit moved from A28 to A31), and participation, lag and position indicators agree; A31 itself continues to run in every trace in both windows. In screening, 28 items qualified against roughly 0.61 expected for an unchanged process, and every item's difference exceeds those of about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits, so sampling variability between the two 200-trace windows cannot explain the pattern as a whole.
