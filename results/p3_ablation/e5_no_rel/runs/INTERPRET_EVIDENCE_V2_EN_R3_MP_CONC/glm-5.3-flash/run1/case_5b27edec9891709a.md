### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment A31–A32–A37 changed from running in every case between A20 and A23 to running in about half of the cases between A21 and A26. Earlier, every trace ran A20, then the three activities in varying order, then A23, then A21, A22 and A26; in the later window A23 immediately follows A20, and after A21 a case either runs A22 and then A26 (about 54 in 100 traces) or runs the three-activity group in varying order and then A26 (about 47 in 100), with A22 skipped in those cases. [DIR001, LAG001, DIR002, LAG005, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, OCC001, OCC002]

This rests on direct relational evidence, not only on frequencies: the successor profile of A20 flipped from the group to A23 while the A20–A23 distance fell from four steps to one, A21 gained the group as new successors while its distance to A26 split between two and four steps, and the group's exits moved from A23 to A26. The participation, position and proximity items all point in the same direction. With 25 qualifying items against 0.4 expected without change, the ensemble is far beyond what random re-splits produce, though the items largely describe one underlying restructuring rather than independent confirmations. [DIR001, LAG001, DIR002, LAG005, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008, screening]

The evidence cannot rule out that both arrangements pre-existed as variants whose shares shifted — although the earlier window shows the group before A23 and the four-step A20–A23 gap in all 200 traces, so the later pattern was absent or rarer than about 1–2 in 100 before, which a stable variant mix would not produce by sampling alone. The after-window lag profiles for pairs such as A20–A31 or A31–A23 rest on a single observation each and carry little weight on their own. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

Other differences: A20's own mean relative position moved slightly later, from about 0.34 to about 0.36; this small shift is not required by the main hypothesis. [POS001]
