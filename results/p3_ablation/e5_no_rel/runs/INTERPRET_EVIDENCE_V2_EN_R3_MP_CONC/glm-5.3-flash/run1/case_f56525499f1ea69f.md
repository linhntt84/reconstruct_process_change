### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a block of six activities — A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 — which in the earlier window was executed in only about half of the cases, is now executed in nearly every case and forms a chain inserted between A30 and A31: A30 leads into A33/A35/A36, these lead on to A37/A38/A39, and A31 follows only after the whole block. The participation change is directly observed for all six activities on roughly 100 versus roughly 200 traces each; the successor changes (the direct transitions A30→A37/A38/A39 and A33/A35/A36→A31 disappearing, edges from A33/A35/A36 to A37/A38/A39 appearing) and the shift of the affected pairs from 1–3 to 4–5 steps apart show the reordering directly; and the screening produced 38 qualifying items where fewer than 1 would be expected without a change, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically yields. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

Limitations: the evidence does not pin down a fixed internal order within the A33/A35/A36 group or within the A37/A38/A39 group, since mutual transitions among A33, A35 and A36 appear in both windows; and it cannot distinguish "the six activities became mandatory" from a shift in the mix of case types, in which traces skipping parts of the block have disappeared — these look the same in this evidence. [DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009]

Other differences: A04 now appears in about 14 in 100 cases instead of about 31 in 100, with its routing moved away from A16 and wider position spread; A06 and A10 became more frequent from a very low base; A14, A16 and A30 occur slightly earlier and A31 slightly later, with A14's position much more uniform; A15 sends relatively more of its traffic to A16 and less to A04, and A47 leads to A48 slightly less exclusively. Whether A04's decline belongs to the same underlying change cannot be determined from this evidence. [OCC001, DIR002, LAG001, POS001, OCC002, OCC003, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, DIR005, DIR010]
