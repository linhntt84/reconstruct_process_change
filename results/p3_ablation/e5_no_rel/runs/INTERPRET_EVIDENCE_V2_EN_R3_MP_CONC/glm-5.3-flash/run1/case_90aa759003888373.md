### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A14, A15 and A20 each ran exactly once in every trace (participation 100%, one occurrence per trace). In the later window, each of them appears in only about 53 of 100 traces (106 of 200), still at most once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The successor behaviour of A36 changed: earlier, every observed outgoing transition of A36 led to A14; in the later window about 53% of its outgoing transitions lead to A14 and about 47% lead to A21. [DIR001]
- The A36–A21 relationship changed accordingly: earlier, A21 followed A36 four steps later in essentially all traces; in the later window about 47% of traces show A21 immediately after A36, while about 53% keep the four-step gap. [LAG001]
- A21 itself is present in every trace in both windows; its relative position moved slightly earlier (mean about 0.43 to about 0.40 of the trace) and became slightly more variable. [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window A36 can be followed directly by A21, and that in those traces A14, A15 and A20 are not executed; this route accounts for about 47 of 100 later traces, while the earlier behaviour — A36 leading to A14, with A15 and A20 following and A21 coming four steps after A36 — still accounts for about 53 of 100 traces. The signs match directly and in size: the new A36→A21 successor share (about 47%), the new immediate A36–A21 adjacency (about 47%), and the participation drop of A14, A15 and A20 (each about 47 percentage points, down to about 53%) line up exactly, A21 remains in every trace in both windows so it was neither added nor removed, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. Six qualifying items against roughly 0.31 expected for an unchanged process also makes chance sampling unlikely as a whole. [DIR001, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001]

Limitations: the internal order among A14, A15 and A20 is not directly observed (no relational items cover them), so the shape of the block that disappears is inferred from their identical profiles; and the evidence cannot fully exclude that the disappearance of A14/A15/A20 and the new A36→A21 adjacency are two separate differences of coincidentally equal size, since it does not show at the level of individual traces that the traces missing A14/A15/A20 are the same ones in which A21 immediately follows A36.
