### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a choice among three activities — A14, A15 and A20 — runs between A36 and A21, where previously every execution went from A36 directly to A21. In about 99 in 100 later traces, exactly one of the three runs in between, and each of the three is always followed by A21. They behave as mutually exclusive alternatives: their participation shares sum to about 99 in 100 traces, matching A36's participation, and the two-step A36→A21 gap leaves room for exactly one of them. Supporting signs: twelve items qualified against roughly 0.32 expected by chance in the control-flow family, so the set as a whole is unlikely for an unchanged process; the change is shown directly (occurrence rising from zero to 29–37 in 100 traces, redirection of A36's outgoing transitions, and the lag growing from one to two steps) and consistently across the occurrence, direction, position and lag indicators, with 57–74 supporting traces per activity — far too many for a rare variant appearing in one window only.
