### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A06 has taken a fixed place on the route from A15 to A16: A15 hands over to A06 in about 40 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (about 1 in 100 before), A06 in turn proceeds almost always directly to A16 (about 88 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, adjacent in about 97 in 100 of observed pairs), and A06 participates in about 73 in 100 traces at an earlier event position, whereas before it was an occasional activity with outgoing flow spread across many targets. The change is shown directly by successor and lag evidence, is consistent across the occurrence, direction, position and lag indicators, and rests on roughly 130–150 later-window traces for the key relations; downstream pairs such as A15→A19 and A15→A41 also now occur typically one event further apart, fitting the same picture. With 36 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.7 expected if nothing had changed, chance is an unlikely explanation for the pattern as a whole, though the agreeing items partly describe one underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that an additional route containing A06 was added alongside the old behaviour rather than A06 being repositioned, since A06's earlier role is documented in only about 47 traces. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR003, POS001, LAG002, LAG006, LAG009, LAG011, LAG013]

Other differences: The previously optional block A19 → A25 → A20 after A41 now runs in essentially every trace (about 48 in 100 before), and A41's direct transition to A26 is no longer observed, with A26 consistently following about four events after A41; because the alternative route vanished rather than merely shrinking, this behaves like the block becoming mandatory rather than a re-weighting between intact alternatives. A08 participation fell from about 20 to about 8 in 100 traces. The evidence cannot tell whether these shifts and the A06 change stem from one restructuring or separate ones.
