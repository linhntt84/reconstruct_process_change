### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening produced 61 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed, so the volume of differences cannot be explained as chance sampling, and the individual patterns below are far too deterministic to be rare-variant noise. [screening]

Main hypothesis: the evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the activities A22, A20, A23, A24, A26 and A25 — entered from A31 and exiting to A32 — are executed in one fixed sequence, A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32, whereas in the earlier window the same activities followed one another in varying orders and at varying distances. This is a change in ordering constraints rather than in participation, since all six activities appear in every trace in both windows. The support is direct and consistent: successor profiles that were split across several targets became single-successor (about 90–99 in 100), lag distributions collapsed from spreads over one to five steps to a single fixed distance in about 99 in 100 later traces, reverse-order pairs fell from dozens or hundreds of traces to about one, and the position shifts (A20/A22/A23 earlier, A24/A26 later) match the new chain.
