### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of three alternative activities A19, A20 and A25 — still executed about once per case, in place of one another — changed its placement in the flow: in the later window A45 connects directly to A26, and the trio runs later in the case, inserted between A27/A28 and A32 (either between A27 and A28 or between A28 and A32), whereas previously it sat between A45 and A26 early in the case. This is shown directly by successor changes at six source activities, by the trio's relative positions moving from about one third to about three fifths of the trace, and by gap changes exactly where the relocation predicts them; with 23 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected if nothing had changed, the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of a stable process produce. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG011]

Limitations: several pair-level gap contrasts rest on very few observations in one window (for example A19→A32, A27→A20, A28→A19 and A28→A20 had a single observation before, and A45→A25 a single observation after), so they are weak on their own and the hypothesis rests on the items backed by roughly 50–200 observations per window. The evidence also cannot distinguish a literal move of the block from removing it at one point and inserting it at another, and it cannot rule out that the two windows sample different pre-existing case variants whose prevalence swapped, since each window is internally near-homogeneous. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010]

Other differences: A24's participation rose from about 43 to about 58 in 100 traces; no relational or positional evidence covers A24, so its role in the change remains unknown.
