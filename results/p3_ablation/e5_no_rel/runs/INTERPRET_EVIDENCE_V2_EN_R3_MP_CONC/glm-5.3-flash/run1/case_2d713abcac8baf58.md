### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: in the earlier window, A44 was typically followed by exactly one of A19, A20 or A21 (each in about a third of cases, leading on to A29), with A29 reached two to three steps after A44; in the later window, A44 instead leads into A26, A27 and A28, which now execute in every case, once each and in varying order, with A29 reached four to five steps after A44. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, LAG001]

Supporting signs: the participation changes are observed directly with roughly 200 traces behind each side of the comparison; A44's successor profile moved from the old trio to the new trio in a comparison covering essentially all traces in both windows; the lengthening of the A44→A29 distance matches a three-activity block taking the place of a single-activity step; and the 21 flagged items are roughly forty times the number expected for an unchanged process (about 0.5), so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [DIR007, LAG001, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a complete removal of A19, A20 and A21 from a drastic drop to rare execution, since they still appear in 1–2 later traces; the routing of those residual cases rests on single observations; and the internal order of A26, A27 and A28 is inferred from aggregate transition shares, so a preferred order cannot be fully excluded. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

Other differences: the rare later executions of A19, A20 and A21 lead to A09/A10, A45 and A34 respectively rather than mainly to A29 as before, and sit at different positions (A19 earlier, A20 and A21 later in those few traces); these rest on 1–2 observations each.
