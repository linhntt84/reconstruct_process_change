### Observed differences

- Two groups of activities exchanged their typical place within the case. In the earlier window, A14, A16 and A19 occurred around two-thirds through the trace (mean relative positions about 0.68–0.69) while A15, A17 and A18 occurred around 39% through; in the later window, A14, A16 and A19 occurred around 34–35% through while A15, A17 and A18 occurred around 64% through — shifts of roughly a quarter to a third of the trace length. [POS001, POS003, POS006, POS002, POS004, POS005]
- Exits swapped accordingly. Earlier, every observed outgoing transition of A14, A16 and A19 went to A25, while A15, A17 and A18 sent roughly 30–39% of their observed outgoing transitions to A20 and none to A25; in the later window, A14, A16 and A19 always led to A20, while A15, A17 and A18 sent roughly 32–35% to A25 and none to A20. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005]
- The feeding activities also swapped targets: A24 earlier led to A16, A14 and A19 (about 31–38% each) and later to A15, A18 and A17 (about 32–35% each), while A35 earlier led to A17, A18 and A15 and later to A19, A16 and A14. [DIR007, DIR008]
- Step distances flipped with the exchange: the distance from A24 to A25 was 2 steps in the earlier window and 4 steps in the later one, and the distance from A35 to A20 was 4 steps earlier and 2 steps later, each value holding in all observed cases of its window. [LAG001, LAG002]
- Within the A15/A17/A18 group the internal order stayed mixed in both windows: each member led to the other two and to the group's exit in roughly equal shares (about 25–39% each) before and after. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR005]
- Participation changed only within the A14/A16/A19 group: A14 ran in about 31 in 100 traces earlier versus about 44 in 100 later, A16 fell from about 38 to 29 in 100 and A19 from about 32 to 28 in 100, with the three shares together still accounting for about one occurrence per case; A15, A17, A18, A20 and A24 appeared in every trace in both windows. [OCC001, POS003, POS006, POS002, POS004, POS005, POS007, POS008]
- A20 and A24 each occur slightly earlier in the later window (mean relative position about 0.46 versus 0.38 for A20, and about 0.65 versus 0.57 for A24). [POS007, POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, two segments of the flow exchanged their places: the segment built around A14, A16 and A19 — earlier sitting late in the case, entered from A24 and always exiting to A25 — now sits early, is entered from A35 and exits to A20, while the segment built around A15, A17 and A18 — earlier sitting early between A35 and A20 — now sits late, is entered from A24 and exits to A25. The change is shown directly by relational and positional evidence, not only by frequencies: complete successor replacements at A14, A16, A19, A24 and A35, the A24→A25 and A35→A20 distances flipping between 2 and 4 steps, and opposing position shifts of roughly a quarter to a third of the trace for all six activities. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

Chance is an implausible explanation for the pattern as a whole: 19 control-flow items qualified where about 0.36 were expected without a change across the 72 tested units, and every qualified item's difference exceeded about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled data. The tight position spreads within each window (standard deviations around 0.05–0.07) also fit a single dominant order per window rather than a blend of orders. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, LAG001, POS002]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample a shifting mix of two case patterns rather than a reordering within one process, and positions and distances are measured in event positions, so the change is established in ordering within the case, not in clock time. The qualified items are also not independent — most describe the same single rearrangement from different angles. [POS002, POS004, POS005]

Other differences: The choice among the three alternatives shifted toward A14, which ran in about 31 in 100 traces earlier and about 44 in 100 later, while A16 (about 38 → 29 in 100) and A19 (about 32 → 28 in 100) became somewhat rarer — a change in how often each alternative is taken that an exchange of places alone would not produce. [OCC001, POS003, POS006]
