### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A40 never led directly to A21: about 38 in 100 of its outgoing transitions went to A14, about 31 to A15 and about 32 to A20, and each of these three always continued straight to A21, so A40 reached A21 two steps later in every trace. In the later window, A40 leads directly to A21 in all cases, and A14, A15 and A20 instead lead to A23 or A27 (A14 about 52 in 100 to A23, A15 about 54 to A23, A20 about 61 to A27). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, LAG004]
- The three activities A14, A15 and A20 sit in a different part of the trace: their mean relative position was about 0.31–0.33 before and is about 0.59–0.60 in the later window. Their participation is essentially unchanged (A14 in about 38% of traces before vs 31% after, A15 31% vs 33%, A20 32% vs 37%), and the three shares sum to about 100% in both windows, so exactly one of them still runs per case. [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- The late segment was restructured. Earlier, A22 was always followed immediately by A23 and A23 immediately by A27, so A22 reached A27 in two steps in every trace. In the later window, A22 leads to one of A14/A15/A20 in about 48 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (otherwise straight to A23), and A23 leads to one of them in about 52 in 100 (otherwise straight to A27); accordingly A22 reaches A23 in two steps in about 48 in 100 traces, A23 reaches A27 in two steps in about 52 in 100, and A22 always reaches A27 in three steps. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, DIR004, DIR005]
- A21, A22 and A23 occur in every trace in both windows and each shifted slightly earlier, by about 0.02–0.03 in mean relative position. [POS004, POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the per-case step — exactly one of A14, A15 or A20 executes in each case — is placed in a different segment in the later window: it used to run between A40 and A21 in every case, whereas now A40 leads directly to A21 and the step is inserted between A22 and A23 (about 48 in 100 cases) or between A23 and A27 (about 52 in 100 cases). [DIR006, LAG004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, DIR004, DIR005]

The change is shown directly and consistently: the successor profiles of A14, A15 and A20 switched from always leading to A21 to leading to A23 or A27, their mean positions moved from about one-third to about three-fifths of the trace while participation stayed stable, and A40's successor profile switched from three branches to A21 alone. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Sixteen control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.36 expected by chance over the 73 tested units, so the set of differences is far larger than an unchanged process typically produces. [screening]

Limitations: positions and step distances are measured in event order, not clock time, so the hypothesis concerns where in the sequence the step runs, not when; and the evidence cannot fully rule out that the early step was removed while a same-labelled step was added in the late segment, although one occurrence per case in both windows and stable participation make a plain relocation the more parsimonious reading. [POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG002]

Other differences: the choice among the three activities shifted modestly (A14 from about 38 in 100 cases to about 31, A20 from about 32 to about 37), and A21, A22 and A23 each drifted slightly earlier (about 0.02–0.03); both are small effects consistent with the relocation rather than separate changes. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
