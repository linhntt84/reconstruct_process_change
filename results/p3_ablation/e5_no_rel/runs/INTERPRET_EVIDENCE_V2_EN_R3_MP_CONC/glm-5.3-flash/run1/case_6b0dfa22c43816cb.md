### Observed differences

- The routing out of A25 and A26 was exchanged. In the earlier window, A25's outgoing transitions split about 38% to A35, 31% to A36 and 27% to A41, while about 99% of A26's transitions went to A30; in the later window, about 96% of A25's transitions go to A27 alone, and A26 splits roughly 35% to A35, 32% to A36 and 31% to A41. The pairwise distances match: A25 was typically followed by A27 four positions later (92 in 100 pair observations) and is now followed immediately (98 in 100), while A26 was followed by A30 immediately (99 in 100) and A30 now follows about four positions later (91 in 100). [DIR005, DIR006, LAG002, LAG005]

- The A35/A36/A41 group changed its convergence target from A27 to A30. Earlier, each group member led to A27 in about 28–36% of its transitions; in the later window A27 accounts for only about 1% of their transitions and A30 for about 30–34%. The ordered pairs A26→A36 and A36→A30, each seen in only 1 trace before, now appear in about 198 of 200 traces; A25→A36 and A25→A41 collapsed from 200 traces to 2 and 3, and A36→A27 from 200 to 4. [DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

- By relative event position, A27 now occurs earlier in the trace (median about 0.46 before, 0.37 after) and A35, A36 and A41 later (about 0.40 before, 0.59 after), with A26 slightly earlier (0.61 to 0.53); all of these activities occur in every trace in both windows, as does A30 within five steps of A26. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG005]

- A02 largely disappeared from the end of the process: it appears in about 21 of 100 traces in the later window, against about 75 of 100 earlier, and its typical position moved from near the end (mean relative position 0.93) to mid-trace (0.55). A49 now reaches A50 directly instead of via A02: A49→A02 fell from about 72% of A49's transitions to about 3%, A49→A50 rose from about 26% to about 83% and became immediate (84 in 100 pair observations at lag 1, versus 27 in 100 before). Where A02 still occurs, its transitions scatter over many targets (largest share about 13%) instead of concentrating on A50 (91% before); the direct A02→A50 pair is now seen in only 14 traces. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR011, POS001, POS007, LAG001, LAG009]

A06's outgoing transitions, previously spread evenly over twelve targets on about 12 observations, now concentrate on A48 (about 17%), A50 (15%), A16 (13%) and A19 (11%); A47's near-exclusive edge to A48 weakened from about 97% to about 81%, with small new shares to A10, A06, A02 and A04. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the control flow around the A35/A36/A41 group was rerouted so that A25 and A26 exchanged their outgoing roles: in the earlier window A25 fed the group, whose members then converged on A27, while A26 led straight to A30; in the later window A25 leads almost always and immediately to A27, A26 feeds the same group, and the group converges on A30, which now follows A26 about four positions later instead of immediately. This is shown directly by the successor profiles of A25, A26, A35, A36 and A41 and by the lag distributions, which flip consistently between the windows, with the position shifts (A27 earlier, the group later) agreeing; every one of these differences is larger than about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, and 30 of 151 tested units qualified against roughly 1 expected without change, far more than an unchanged process produces. [DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

Other differences: A02 lost its place between A49 and A50 — A49 now leads directly to A50 and A02 appears in far fewer traces, around the middle of the trace — and A06 and A10 are executed more often, with A47's edge to A48 weakening; the evidence shows no link between these and the A25/A26 rerouting, though it cannot exclude one. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR011, POS001, POS007, LAG001, LAG009, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, DIR010]

Limitations: the evidence consists of successor shares, positions and pairwise gaps rather than full traces, so it cannot distinguish a genuine exchange of A25's and A26's routing roles from a broader reordering in which the group and the A27/A30 steps were relocated around them — both would produce the same profiles. A few post-change lag profiles also rest on very few observations (A25→A36 in 2 traces, A25→A41 in 3, A36→A27 in 4, A02→A50 in 14), although the corresponding disappearances are corroborated by the successor profiles. [LAG003, LAG004, LAG007, LAG001, DIR005, DIR007, DIR008]
