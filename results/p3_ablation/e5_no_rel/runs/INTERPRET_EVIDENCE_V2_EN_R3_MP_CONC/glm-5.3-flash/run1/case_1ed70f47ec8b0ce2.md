### Observed differences

- Two groups of activities exchanged their places in the case timeline. In the earlier window, A14, A16 and A19 each occurred about a third of the way through the trace (mean relative position about 0.34), while A15, A17 and A18 occurred about two-thirds in (about 0.64). In the later window this is reversed: A15, A17 and A18 sit at about 0.38 and A14, A16 and A19 at about 0.67. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Participation itself did not change: A15, A17 and A18 appear in essentially every trace in both windows, and A14, A16 and A19 each appear in roughly 30–39 in 100 traces in both windows, together accounting for about one occurrence per case — exactly one of the three runs per case, before and after. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- The surrounding activities rewired in mirror image. Before, A24's outgoing transitions led to the trio (about 37/33/30 in 100 to A15/A18/A17) and A35's led to the single activity (about 30/35/36 to A14/A19/A16); in the later window A24 leads to A14/A16/A19 (about 33/29/39) and A35 leads to A15/A17/A18 (about 33/36/31). [DIR007, DIR008]
- The exits of the two groups flipped as well: A14, A16 and A19 were each always followed by A20 before and are each always followed by A25 after; the trio members previously exited to A25 (about 30 in 100 of each member's transitions) and now exit to A20 (about 28–37 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- Distances changed accordingly: A24 and A25 were 4 steps apart in every case before and 2 steps apart after; A35 and A20 were 2 steps apart before and 4 steps apart after. [LAG001, LAG002]
- A20 and A24 each shifted slightly later (mean relative position up about 0.07, roughly two event positions), consistent with a three-activity segment now running before them where a single activity ran before. [POS007, POS008]
- The internal behaviour of both groups is preserved: the trio A15/A17/A18 runs in varying order in both windows (each member follows each other in similar shares), and the choice among A14/A16/A19 stays roughly even (about 30/35/36 before vs 33/29/39 after). [DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, DIR007, DIR008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two process segments exchanged places. In the earlier window, every case ran A35, then exactly one of A14/A16/A19, then A20, and later in the same case A24, then the trio A15/A17/A18 in varying order, then A25. In the later window the contents of these two segments are exchanged: A35 is followed by the trio A15/A17/A18 and then A20, while A24 is followed by exactly one of A14/A16/A19 and then A25. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG001, LAG002]

The change is shown directly and consistently: all six activities reverse their relative positions while their participation stays the same; the successors of both anchors and of all block members flip exactly as this exchange predicts; the two lag distributions move from 4 to 2 and from 2 to 4 steps, matching the exchange of a three-activity segment with a one-activity segment; and A20 and A24 shift about two positions later, as expected when the segment preceding them grows from one activity to three. Eighteen control-flow items qualified against about 0.36 expected if nothing had changed — many times the chance expectation — and every item's difference exceeds what is seen in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: all items describe the same underlying trace behaviour, so they form one consistent picture rather than independent confirmations. The evidence also cannot distinguish one coordinated exchange of the two segments from two independent relocations that happen to produce the same successor, position and lag signatures, and it cannot show what prompted the change. [DIR001, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS002, POS007, POS008, LAG001, LAG002]
