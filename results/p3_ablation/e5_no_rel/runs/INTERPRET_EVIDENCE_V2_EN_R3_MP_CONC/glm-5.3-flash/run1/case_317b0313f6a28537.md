### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening qualified 20 control-flow items where an unchanged process would produce about 0.57, so the set of differences is far larger than random re-splits of these windows would produce, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 reference comparisons. The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of activities A19, A20 and A25 was repositioned from the late stage of the case, where it branched off A27/A28 and led on to A28 or A32, to the early stage between A45 and A26: in the earlier window the three ran at about 0.60 of the trace and led to A28/A32, while in the later window they run at about 0.32–0.34, lead to A26, and A45 reaches A26 through them; as a counterpart, A27→A28→A32 now runs as a tight chain without them [POS001, POS002, POS003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]. The hypothesis rests on direct placement evidence (the roughly 0.28 shift in typical position with stable participation), direct successor changes, and consistent lag changes including the near-disappearance of the old pairs with A28/A32 [POS001, POS002, POS003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]. Limitations: the after-window lag distributions for the vanished pairs rest on 1–3 observations, so only their near-disappearance is interpretable; the direction profiles rest on roughly 60–80 outgoing transitions per window; and a window comparison cannot rule out that the two samples draw on different case or variant mixes rather than a within-case reordering [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003].
