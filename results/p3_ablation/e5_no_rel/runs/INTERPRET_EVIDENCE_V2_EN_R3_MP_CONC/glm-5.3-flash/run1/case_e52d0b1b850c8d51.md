### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a previously mandatory stretch of the process is skipped in many cases. A19, A20 and A25, executed in every earlier trace, run in only about half of the later traces; the frequent A06 step between A14 and A15 is largely bypassed, with A14 leading directly to A15 in about 78 in 100 of its exits (before about 31 in 100) while A06 itself appears in only about a quarter of traces; and A41, which previously handed over almost exclusively to A19, now often connects directly to A26, typically one event after A41 instead of four. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC002, DIR005, DIR003, DIR008, LAG008]

The hypothesis is shown directly, not only through aggregate counts: the rerouting of A14 and A41 rests on roughly 200 outgoing transitions per window, the participation drops on all 200 traces per window, and the occurrence, routing, position and distance indicators agree in direction, while downstream anchors (A15→A16, A20→A26) continue unchanged. The screening context also fits: 29 control-flow items qualified where about 0.73 would be expected by chance, so the pattern as a whole is far larger than random re-splits of an unchanged process produce. [DIR005, DIR008, LAG008, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR006, DIR007, POS001, LAG003, LAG004]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the skipping follows a specific new condition or applies across the board, nor whether the A19/A20/A25 skip and the A06 bypass are one change or two independent ones; an important competing explanation is that the later window mixes a shortened variant with the earlier full variant, which would produce the same aggregate shares. A few supporting items rest on very few observations (for example A06→A19 with 7 pairs and A06→A41 with 13 pairs in the later window, and A15→A26 with a single pair before). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG007]

Other differences: A04, A08 and A10 are executed roughly three to four times as often as before, though their rerouted exits still rest on few observations (A04: 10, A08: 8 outgoing transitions before); A02's outgoing mix also diversified (18 before, 40 after); the A15→A19 and A14→A41 distances each shifted by about one step; A26 occurs slightly earlier in the trace while A14 occurs slightly later and more variably; and A44→A45 and A46→A47 stay dominant but slightly less exclusive (about 96 → 86–88 in 100).
