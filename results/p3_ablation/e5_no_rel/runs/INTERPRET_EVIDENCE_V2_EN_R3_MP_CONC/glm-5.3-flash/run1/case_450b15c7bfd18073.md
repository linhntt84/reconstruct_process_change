### Observed differences

- A22 was executed in every trace in the earlier window (200 of 200, once per trace) but in only about half of the traces in the later window (about 102 of 200; mean occurrences per trace fell from 1.0 to 0.51). [OCC001]
- A27, A28 and A33 were not observed at all in the earlier window; in the later window each occurs exactly once in about 49 in 100 traces (98 of 200 each). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A21's outgoing transitions were redistributed: before, every observed transition from A21 went to A22; in the later window about 51 in 100 still go to A22, while roughly 15–18 in 100 each go to A27, A28 and A33. [DIR001]
- In the later window the three new activities form a block that ends at A23: of A27's outgoing transitions about 36% go to A33, 35% to A28 and 30% to A23; A28 leads most often to A27 (about 43%); A33 leads most often to A23 (about 44%). - The new activities sit around the middle of the trace (median position about 0.48–0.50 of trace length, with a tight spread of roughly 0.05–0.06 around it). [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- The distance from A21 to A23 grew: before, A23 always followed A21 two steps later; in the later window it follows two steps later in about 51 in 100 cases and four steps later in about 49 in 100 cases. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, about half of the executions no longer run the direct segment A21 → A22 → A23; in those cases A21 leads into a block of three activities, A27, A28 and A33, each executed once in varying order, which ends with a transition into A23 — while the remaining executions still run A21 → A22 → A23 as before. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001]

The change is shown directly and consistently by the relational and positional evidence: A21's successors split between A22 and the three new activities, the new activities link to one another and to A23, they appear around the middle of the trace, and the A21→A23 distance grows from two to four steps in about half the cases — exactly what replacing the single activity A22 between A21 and A23 with three activities would produce. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG001]

The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces: twelve control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.33 expected by chance for the 66 units tested, and the underlying differences are large (A22 from 200 of 200 traces to about 102 of 200; the new activities from none observed to 98 of 200). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG001]

Limitations: that A22 and the new block are mutually exclusive is inferred from the shares (about 51% and 49%, with each activity occurring at most once per trace) rather than observed directly, and the evidence cannot show what determines which path a case takes — the near even split could be a fixed routing share or could depend on case attributes not present in the evidence.
