### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a block of six activities — A28, A30, A31, A32, A33 and A34 — changed from optional to mandatory and that the flow through the block was rewired. In the earlier window the block ran in only about half of the cases, A25 could enter it at any of the six activities, and A26 followed A28, A30 or A31 directly; in the later window the block runs in nearly every case, A25 enters it almost solely through A28, A30 and A31, and A26 is reached only several steps later, consistent with A32, A33 and A34 now lying between A28/A30/A31 and A26. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the participation change is measured for each of the six activities on roughly 100 traces before versus roughly 200 after; the rerouting is shown directly by the successor profiles and by the step-distance distributions; the position shifts of all eight activities point the same way; and the screening flagged 24 control-flow items where about 0.45 would be expected if nothing had changed, with every item's difference larger than in about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

Limitations: the evidence contains no outgoing-transition or step-distance items with A32, A33 or A34 as the source, so their exact new sequence and their direct link to A26 are inferred from the shifted positions and distances rather than observed; the participation change and the rerouting could also be two separate modifications rather than one. Positions are event positions, not clock times, and direction shares are over outgoing transitions, not over cases. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
