### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural reordering: in the later window, the block of activities A35, A36 and A41 is executed between A26 and A30, whereas in the earlier window it was executed between A25 and A27; A25 now passes directly to A27, and A26 feeds the block, which then exits to A30. This rests on wholesale successor changes at A25, A26, A35, A36 and A41, each backed by about 200 outgoing transitions in both windows [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]; on the swapped distances A25→A27 (lag 4 to lag 1) and A26→A30 (lag 1 to lag 4), each with 200 traces behind it in both windows [LAG005, LAG009]; and on agreeing position shifts for all five activities [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]. With 27 flagged items against roughly 0.7 expected for an unchanged process, sampling noise cannot account for the ensemble, though several indicators describe the same underlying trace behaviour and so corroborate rather than independently confirm it [DIR003, DIR004, LAG005, LAG009]. Because POS and LAG measure event positions rather than clock time, the claim concerns ordering, not timing; and the evidence cannot rule out that the later window draws on a different mix of case variants rather than a rerouting of the same flow [LAG003, LAG005, LAG009].
