### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of activities A19, A20 and A25 was relocated. In the earlier window they ran in the early part of the case between A45 and A26: A45 led into one of them, and each of them typically led directly to A26. In the later window, A45 flows directly into A26, and A19, A20 and A25 instead run in the later part of the case (around 60% of the trace length), inserted between A27/A28 and A32: A27 and A28 hand over to one of them in about half of their outgoing transitions, and these in turn lead to A28 or A32, so A32 is now typically reached one step later than before. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

The change is shown directly by relational evidence: successor profiles were replaced wholesale (A26 out and A28/A32 in for A19/A20/A25; A19/A20/A25 out and A26 in for A45), pairwise distances changed accordingly, and the immediate A25→A26 succession seen in about 63 traces before vanished. The position shifts of A19/A20/A25 with roughly stable participation (about 32–38 in 100 traces in both windows) fit a move of existing activities rather than activities being added, removed or repeated. The DIR, POS and LAG items cohere, but they largely reflect the same underlying restructuring and are not independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR006, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish one coordinated relocation of the A19/A20/A25 block from two independent rewirings (A45→A26 plus a re-embedding of the three activities after A27/A28), and the before-profiles of the A20-related lag items rest on a single observation each, so the precise earlier arrangement around A20 is weakly supported. POS uses event positions rather than clock time, so no statement about real timing is possible. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG006, POS001, POS002, POS003]
