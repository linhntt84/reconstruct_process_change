### Observed differences

- **Four activities became mandatory.** In the earlier window, A22 appears in about 54 in 100 traces and each of A31, A32 and A37 in about 46 in 100; in the later window, A22 appears in every trace and A31, A32 and A37 in about 199 of 200. When present, each of the four occurs exactly once per trace in both windows (no repetitions). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

- **A20 changed successor.** In the earlier window, all of A20's outgoing transitions led to A23; in the later window, they lead to A31 (about 36 in 100), A32 (about 32) and A37 (about 32), with a direct transition to A23 in only about 1 in 200. [DIR001]

- **The segment changed its exit from A26 to A23.** In the earlier window, each of A31, A32 and A37 sent roughly a third of its outgoing transitions to A26 (about 29–40 in 100); in the later window, each sends roughly a third to A23 (about 31–37 in 100) instead, while transitions among A31, A32 and A37 themselves continue with similar shares. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

- **A21 changed successor.** In the earlier window, A21 led to A22 in about 54 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and to A31, A32 or A37 in the remainder; in the later window, A21 leads to A22 in essentially all cases. [DIR002]

- **Pair distances changed accordingly.** A23 followed A20 after 1 step in all earlier traces but follows it 4 steps later in all but about 1 in 200 later traces; A26 followed A21 after 2 steps in about 54 in 100 earlier traces (after 4 steps, via the three-activity segment, in the rest) and follows it after 2 steps in all later traces. [LAG001, LAG002]

- **Relative positions shifted (event positions, not clock time).** In the later window, A31, A32 and A37 occur much earlier (mean relative position about 0.59 before vs about 0.40 after), while A23 (about 0.39 to 0.46), A21 (0.54 to 0.60) and A22 (0.59 to 0.63) occur later; A20 is slightly earlier (0.36 to 0.34) and A26 slightly later (0.63 to 0.66). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-activity segment A31–A32–A37 changed its place in the process: in the earlier window it ran, in about 46 in 100 cases, as the alternative to A22 between A21 and A26, whereas in the later window it runs in essentially every case between A20 and A23 — A20 now enters the segment (about one third of its outgoing transitions to each of A31, A32 and A37, instead of always to A23), and the segment now exits to A23 (about a third of each segment activity's outgoing transitions) instead of to A26. Together with this, A22 became a mandatory step: A21 now always leads to A22, and A26 always follows A21 two steps later via A22, where before it did so in about 54 in 100 cases and otherwise followed four steps later through the segment. All four affected activities occur exactly once per trace when present. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The hypothesis is supported directly and consistently: the entry and exit changes are read from the successor profiles and pair distances themselves, not inferred from positions alone, and the participation and position indicators point the same way. The 19 qualifying control-flow items are many times the roughly 0.38 expected for an unchanged process, each difference exceeding about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, and the magnitudes (46 in 100 to 199 in 200 traces; an edge share from 100 in 100 to about 1 in 200, on 200 traces per window) make sampling variation implausible as an explanation for the set as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS003, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008, DIR001]

Limitations: the indicators cover only the measured activities, pairs and outgoing transitions, so the hypothesis concerns the observed rewiring; the evidence cannot rule out that further connections of these activities (for example what precedes A20 and A21 or follows A23 and A26) also changed. POS and LAG reflect event positions, not clock time, so no conclusion about timing is implied. [DIR001, DIR003, POS006, LAG001]

Other differences: only small knock-on position shifts remain — A20 slightly earlier (about 0.36 to 0.34) and A21, A22 and A26 slightly later (about 0.54 to 0.60, 0.59 to 0.63 and 0.63 to 0.66) — consistent with the segment now executing before A23; no further independent differences appear at the configured thresholds. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS005]
