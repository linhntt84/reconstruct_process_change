### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The routing at A41 changed: earlier, traces split between a direct continuation to A26 (about half of A41's outgoing transitions) and a branch through A19 that also contains A20 and A25 (about 45 in 100); in the later window, A41 leads to A19 in about 92 in 100 cases, the direct A41→A26 transition is no longer observed in practice, and A19, A20 and A25 have turned from optional steps in about half the traces into near-mandatory steps executed once in virtually every trace. The dominant four-step A41→A26 distance matches this branch now sitting between the two activities. [DIR002, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

This goes beyond chance: eight control-flow items qualified where about 0.57 would be expected without a change, and the participation, routing and distance evidence all point to the same rerouting — though several items describe one underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations. [DIR002, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish whether the direct A41→A26 path was removed or only rarely taken, since it still appears at about 1 in 200 outgoing transitions in the later window; how A19 is entered other than from A41 is not observed; and the internal order among A19, A20 and A25 is only partly constrained (A20 leads to A26 in both windows). [DIR002, LAG001, DIR001]

Other differences: the small positional shifts of A26 and A41 within traces, and the minor reshaping of A20's successor profile, are consistent with the rerouting above rather than separate changes. [POS001, POS002, DIR001]
