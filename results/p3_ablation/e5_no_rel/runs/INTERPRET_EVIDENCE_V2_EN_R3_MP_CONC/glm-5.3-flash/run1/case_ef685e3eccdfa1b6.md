### Observed differences

- Six activities — A28, A30, A31, A32, A33 and A34 — each ran exactly once in every before-trace (100 of 100 cases, never twice). In the later window each appears in only about half of the traces: A28, A30 and A31 in about 47 in 100 cases (93 of 200) and A32, A33 and A34 in about 56 in 100 (111 of 200). A25 and A26 still occur in every trace in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, POS001, POS002]
- Routing out of A25 changed: before, its observed transitions went only to A28 (about 34 in 100), A30 (about 37) and A31 (about 30); in the later window roughly half go directly to A32 (about 16), A33 (about 18) and A34 (about 20), and half still go to A28, A30 and A31 (about 14–18 each). [DIR001]
- A26 became a direct successor of the first trio: before, A26 was not among the observed successors of A28, A30 or A31, which instead led to one another and to A32/A33/A34 (about 8–14 per 100 transitions each); in the later window A28, A30 and A31 each send about 26–36 per 100 of their transitions directly to A26, while their transitions into A32/A33/A34 shrink to about 1 per 100. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the previously mandatory six-activity segment between A25 and A26 became bypassable in the later window: in roughly half of the cases A25 now leads directly to one of A32, A33 or A34, so A28, A30 and A31 are skipped entirely, and in the cases that do run A28, A30 or A31 the flow exits directly to A26, leaving A32, A33 and A34 largely unexecuted. Supporting signs: participation of all six activities fell from 100% to about 47–56 in 100 cases with no change in repetition; direct transitions appeared that were not observed before (A25→A32/A33/A34 and A28/A30/A31→A26) while the trio-to-trio transitions nearly vanished; the paired distances shrank from 4–5 to 1–3 positions exactly for the affected pairs; and the 31 qualifying control-flow items are roughly sixty times the about 0.5 expected without change, with every item's difference exceeding those of about 995 in 1000 random re-splits. Note that these items largely describe one underlying restructuring and are not independent confirmations of it. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG008, LAG011]

Limitations: the later-window routing profiles for A28, A30 and A31 rest on 93 traces each, and no outgoing profiles exist for A32, A33 or A34, so how cases running those activities reach A26 afterwards is not shown. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the original full path through all six activities still occurs in a small share of cases (residual transitions of about 1 per 100 and the slight overlap between the two trios), meaning the segment may be shortened in most but not necessarily all cases.
