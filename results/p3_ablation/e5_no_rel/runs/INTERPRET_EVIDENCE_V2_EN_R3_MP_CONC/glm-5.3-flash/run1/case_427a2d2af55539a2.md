### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured: in the earlier window each case executed exactly one of three alternative activities — A14, A15 or A16 — immediately before A24, while in the later window that alternative block is no longer observed and every case instead executes all three of A21, A22 and A23, once each and in varying order, before reaching A24. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation flips from about a third of traces (or none) to all 200 traces, A39's successor profile switches completely from the old trio to the new one, the A39→A24 distance doubles from 2 to 4 positions, and A24 shifts later while remaining universal. With 21 qualifying items against roughly 0.36 expected if nothing had changed, the volume of evidence is far beyond what a stable process produces by chance. The evidence is also purely control-flow, so the conclusion is limited to structure and ordering. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
