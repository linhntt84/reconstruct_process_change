### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Judged against the screening expectation, 25 control-flow items qualified where fewer than one (0.58) would qualify without a change, so chance alone cannot explain the pattern as a whole. Within it, the evidence most directly supports the possibility that, in the later window, a block of three activities — A32, A33 and A38 — executes between A26 and A28 in about half of the traces, in place of A27: A27, previously executed in every trace, now runs in about half; A26 splits its outgoing transitions roughly evenly between A27 and the trio; the trio members appear in about half of the traces at mid-trace positions, just before A28, which itself sits slightly later; and the A26→A28 distance widened from one intervening activity to either one or three. A presence of about 50 in 100 traces after zero observations in 200 earlier traces is far beyond what one-window sampling of a rare variant would produce. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR006, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG003]

Within the block, no fixed order is observable: A32, A33 and A38 each send transitions to the other two as well as to A28, and each occurs about once per trace containing it, so the block appears to run in varying order before exiting to A28. The items behind this hypothesis draw on largely the same traces, so they should be read as consistent views of one difference rather than independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A27 and the trio are not strictly mutually exclusive in every individual trace; the aggregate transition shares and distance distributions fit exclusive alternatives, but exclusivity per case is inferred, not directly observed. [DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, LAG003]
