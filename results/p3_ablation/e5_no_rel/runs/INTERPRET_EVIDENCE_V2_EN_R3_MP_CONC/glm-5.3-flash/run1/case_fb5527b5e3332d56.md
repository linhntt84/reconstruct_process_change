### Observed differences

- Activity A22 was performed in about 52 in 100 traces in the earlier window and in essentially all traces in the later window (about 199 in 200); it never repeats within a trace in either window. [OCC001]
- Activities A27, A28 and A33 each appeared in about 49 in 100 traces in the earlier window but in only about 1 in 200 traces in the later window — the whole group of three nearly disappeared. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Out of activity A21, the flow was previously split between A22 (about 52% of outgoing transitions), A33 (about 18%), A28 (about 17%) and A27 (about 14%); in the later window it goes to A22 in about 99.5% of cases, with only a residual edge to A28. [DIR001]
- The distance from A21 to A23 was previously 2 steps in about 52% of traces and 4 steps in about 48%; in the later window it is 2 steps in about 99.5% of traces. Together with the routing above, this indicates that before, roughly half the cases took a detour A21 → (A27/A28/A33) → A23, while the other half went directly via A22; afterwards the detour is almost always skipped. [LAG001, DIR001]
- In the earlier window, the three branch activities led mostly to one another and to A23 (for example, from A33 about 42% of outgoing transitions went to A23); in the later window their internal ordering cannot be characterised, because all after-window profiles for them rest on a single trace. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]
- A23 occurs slightly earlier within traces in the later window (mean relative position about 0.55 versus 0.55 before, median about 0.54 versus 0.56), consistent with the shorter path rather than an independent relocation. [POS001]
- For context: 20 evidence items qualified against roughly 0.38 expected by chance at these thresholds, far more than an unchanged process typically produces; however, most items describe the same underlying difference (the branch's disappearance) rather than independent phenomena. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the branch consisting of A27, A28 and A33, which previously ran between A21 and A23 in about half of the cases, is skipped in almost all cases: earlier, cases split between A21 → A22 → A23 (about half) and A21 → A27/A28/A33 → A23 (about half); afterwards nearly all cases run A21 → A22 → A23, and each branch activity appears in only about 1 in 200 traces. The supporting signs are the participation collapse of A27, A28 and A33 (about 97 traces each before, 1 trace each after) against A22 becoming near-universal (about 103 to 199 traces), the consolidation of A21's outgoing flow onto A22, and the A21→A23 distance collapsing from a 2-step/4-step mix to almost always 2 steps — three views that agree on one coherent structural picture. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a complete removal of the branch from it surviving as a rare exception (one observed trace), and the branch's internal after-window behaviour rests on that single trace, so no claims are made about how A27, A28 and A33 order themselves when they do run. The many qualifying items largely restate this one underlying difference and are not independent confirmations. [OCC002, DIR002, LAG005]

Other differences: A23 sits slightly earlier within traces in the later window, which is consistent with the shorter path via A22 rather than a separate move of the activity. [POS001]
