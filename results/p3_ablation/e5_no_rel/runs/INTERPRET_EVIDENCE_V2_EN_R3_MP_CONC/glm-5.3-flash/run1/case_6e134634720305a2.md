### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A30, A31 and A36 each occurred about 3.5 times per trace and appeared in every trace; in the later window each occurs about once per trace, still in every trace. Their recurrence support collapsed from about 75 in 100 traces to about 1–2 in 100, so these activities essentially stopped repeating. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The successor profile of A36 changed fundamentally: before, about 70% of A36's outgoing transitions led to A30 and about 28% to A25; in the later window about 92% lead to A25 and only about 4% to A30, so the return edge from A36 back to A30 has practically disappeared. [DIR002]
- A30's dominant successor is essentially unchanged (A31, about 99% of outgoing transitions before and about 97% after), with only sub-1% shifts among rare edges. [DIR001]
- The ordered pair A30 then A36 within five steps became much tighter: A36 follows A30 at distance 2 in about 89 in 100 traces, versus about 12 in 100 before. [LAG001]
- The reverse pair A36 then A30 within five steps, present in 150 of 200 traces before, appears in only 1 trace in the later window; A36 followed by A36 within five steps falls from 150 traces to 2, though these rest on very few later observations. [LAG002, LAG003]
- The relative event positions of A30 and A36 are stable (means around 0.42–0.44 and 0.49 of trace length in both windows), but their spread narrows (standard deviation from about 0.12 to about 0.07), consistent with a single pass instead of several passes. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the repeated execution of a block containing A30, A31 and A36 stopped: in the earlier window each of the three activities ran about 3.5 times per trace and repeated in about three quarters of traces, whereas in the later window each runs once per trace while still appearing in every trace. The relational evidence agrees: the edge from A36 back to A30, which carried about 70% of A36's outgoing transitions before, now carries about 4%, with A36 continuing to A25 in about 92% of cases, and A36 now follows A30 within two steps in about 89 in 100 traces instead of about 12. The number of qualified items (10 control-flow items from 93 tested units, against roughly 0.46 expected without a change) is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces, and the drop in observed outgoing transitions of A30 and A36 (about 690 to about 210) matches the drop in their occurrences. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001]

Limitations: the disappearance of the pairs A36→A30 and A36→A36 rests on only 1 and 2 later observations, the items describe overlapping aspects of the same traces rather than independent confirmations, and the edges that closed the former repetition cycle apart from A36→A30 (for example around A31) are not directly observed. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is a rerouting in which A36 now always continues to A25; on its own, however, that would not explain the fall from about 3.5 to about 1 occurrences per trace. [LAG002, LAG003, DIR002, OCC001]
