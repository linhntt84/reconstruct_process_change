### Observed differences

- A25, A26 and A31 stopped repeating. In the earlier window each of the three occurred about 3.4 times per trace on average and appeared more than once in about 75 in 100 traces; in the later window each occurs about once per trace (mean about 1.0) and repetition is nearly absent (about 1 in 200 traces). All three activities still occur in every trace in both windows, so they did not disappear — they stopped repeating. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

- A31's outgoing flow flipped from mostly looping back to mostly moving on. Before, about 71 in 100 of A31's outgoing transitions went to A25 and about 29 in 100 to A20 (roughly 690 outgoing transitions across the 200 traces); after, about 96 in 100 go to A20 and only about 4 in 100 to A25 (roughly 210 outgoing transitions). [DIR001]

- The backward relation A31 → A25 within five steps essentially vanished: it was observed in 150 of 200 traces in the earlier window and in only 1 of 200 traces in the later one. [LAG002]

- The forward relation A25 → A31 became fixed at a distance of two event positions: in the later window it sits at distance two in about 92 in 100 of the observed cases, whereas before the distance was spread (about 12 in 100 at two steps, about 5 in 100 at five steps). [LAG001]

- The average event positions of A25 and A31 barely moved (A25 mean relative position about 0.43 before vs 0.41 after; A31 about 0.48 in both), but their spread roughly halved (standard deviation about 0.12 down to about 0.06), consistent with the repeated block collapsing to a single, more regular occurrence. These are event-order positions, not clock times. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the repeating cycle through A25, A26 and A31 was removed. In the earlier window these three activities formed a block that repeated about three to four times per trace, and the cycle was closed by A31 flowing back to A25 in about 71 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, with the A31→A25 pair within five steps present in 150 of 200 traces. In the later window each of the three activities runs once per trace, the back-step to A25 is all but gone (1 of 200 traces), and A31 proceeds almost always to A20 (about 96 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, up from about 29 in 100). The occurrence, direction and lag indicators show this loop removal directly and consistently, and the screening produced 8 qualifying items where about 0.3 would be expected without a change, so the pattern as a whole is unlikely for an unchanged process. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: A26's involvement rests only on its occurrence profile, as no relational evidence shows where it sat in the repeated block; and the evidence cannot show whether the redirect of A31 to A20 and the end of repetition have a common structural origin or are two independent changes, nor whether the work of the former iterations now happens in activities not characterised here. [OCC002, DIR001, LAG002]
