### Observed differences

- The choice made after A15 flipped between two groups of successors. A15's outgoing transitions are observed in all 200 traces in both windows. Before, about 82 in 100 of these transitions went to A19 (29%), A21 (28%) or A20 (24%), and about 18 in 100 to A17 (8%), A16 (7%) or A18 (3%). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the routing immediately after A15 changed: the two alternative continuations out of A15 exchanged their frequencies. Before, roughly 4 in 5 cases continued from A15 into A19, A20 or A21 and roughly 1 in 5 into A16, A17 or A18; in the later window, roughly 4 in 5 continue into A16, A17 or A18 and roughly 1 in 5 into A19, A20 or A21. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

Supporting signs: the successor redistribution at A15 is observed directly, not inferred from occurrence counts alone, and each difference is larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data [DIR001]; the participation shifts of the six activities match the rerouted shares almost exactly (about 19%/82% before, about 79%/21% after) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]; all six activities and both routes remain in use with substantial support in both windows (at least about 37 of 200 traces each) and no repetition change accompanies the shift, which fits a change in how often existing alternative paths are taken rather than paths being added, removed or bypassed [DIR001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]. Seven items qualified where about 0.38 would be expected by chance across the 77 tested control-flow units, so the ensemble is unlikely under sampling noise alone. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

Limitations: the evidence covers only A15's outgoing transitions and the six activities' occurrence counts. It cannot distinguish a change in the selection shares between the two existing routes from a repositioning or exchange of the two activity groups behind those routes, and it says nothing about behaviour elsewhere in the process.
