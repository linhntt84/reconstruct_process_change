### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening produced 55 qualifying control-flow items where roughly 1 would be expected if nothing had changed, so chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole. The main hypothesis the evidence supports is that A10 became a standard early step running between A14 and A15: earlier, A14 typically led directly to A15 and A10 appeared in about 19 in 100 traces around mid-trace; in the later window A14 leads to A10 in about 65 in 100 of its transitions, A10 follows within one step in about 98 in 100 of those pairs, A10 then leads to A15 in about 85 in 100 of its transitions, and A10 appears in 70 in 100 traces at an early position. A15's slightly later position and the A14→A15 gap moving from one to two steps corroborate an insertion in front of A15. [DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, OCC003, POS001, POS002]
