### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that existing alternative continuations after A20 are taken with different frequencies: in the earlier window roughly three of four continuations of A20 — and roughly three of four traces — ran through A21, A22 or A23, whereas in the later window roughly three of four run through A24, A25 or A26. The support is direct and consistent: six participation items move in mirrored directions on 49–152 traces each; the successor distribution at A20 reverses on about 200 outgoing transitions per window while A20's own frequency stays stable; every activity and every A20-outgoing edge involved is observed in a substantial share of both windows, so this is a shift between existing alternatives rather than a path being added, removed or bypassed; A25's event position barely moves while its participation triples, arguing against a mere repositioning; and the control-flow family produced 17 qualifying items against about 0.69 expected if nothing had changed, so sampling noise is unlikely to explain the pattern as a whole. A limitation: A21–A23 appear in more traces than A20's outgoing edges alone can reach, so other predecessors must also have changed, and the evidence does not show those routes; it also cannot verify, absent relational evidence, that the two groups are strict alternatives of a single decision point. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, POS002]
