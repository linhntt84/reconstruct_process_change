### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that control flow around the three-activity block A30/A31/A36 was rerouted: in the earlier window A20 led directly to A22, while A21 fed the block (executed in varying order), which then led to A25; in the later window A20 feeds the block, the block leads to A22, and A21 connects directly to A25 — in effect, the block now runs between A20 and A22, and A22 and A25 have changed predecessors. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002]

The successor profiles shifted almost deterministically with full support (200 of 200 traces per window), the pair-distance flips match the rewiring exactly, and the position shifts agree in direction; with 18 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.38 expected by chance for the 77 tested units, the family-level signal is far larger than random re-splits of unchanged behaviour produce.
