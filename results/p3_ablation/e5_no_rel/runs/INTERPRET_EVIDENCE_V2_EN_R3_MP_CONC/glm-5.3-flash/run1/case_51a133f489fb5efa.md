### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the routing around A19, A20 and A25 changed. In the earlier window these three activities formed a mid-process block, typically entered after A27 and leading on to A28 or A32, while A26 was usually reached without them, with A45 directly before it. In the later window the block sits at the end of the flow: A08 — and one step earlier A45 — precede it, each of A19, A20 and A25 leads almost exclusively and immediately to A26, and A27 proceeds directly via A28 to A32. A08, previously a diffuse connector, now appears in about twice as many traces and acts mainly as the trio's predecessor. The screening produced 30 qualifying items in the control-flow family against roughly 0.67 expected for an unchanged process, so chance cannot account for the set as a whole; every item's difference also exceeds those of about 995 in 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data. The rerouting is shown directly by successor-profile changes with solid support on both sides (60–73 outgoing transitions per window for A19/A20/A25, about 200 for A27/A28/A45) and by adjacency counts (about 200 pairs for the A27/A28/A32 chain in both windows; 69 pairs for the new A19→A26 adjacency), with the position shifts agreeing with — not independently confirming — the same behaviour.
