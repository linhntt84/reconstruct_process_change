### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of three activities A36, A37 and A42, executed once each in varying internal order, changed from an optional late branch into a mandatory early segment: in the earlier window it ran between A26 and A31 in about 46 in 100 cases, as the alternative to A27, whereas in the later window it runs between A25 and A28 in essentially every case, with its exit leading to A28 rather than A31. A27, previously taken after A26 in about 54 in 100 cases, is now observed after A26 in essentially every case and still precedes A31. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The hypothesis is shown directly by relational evidence rather than frequency shifts alone: the successor profiles of A25 and A26 flipped, the A25→A28 distance grew from one to four steps, the pairs linking A26 to the trio disappeared while pairs linking A25 and A28 to the trio appeared in nearly all traces, and the trio's outgoing edges swapped A31 for A28. All 26 qualifying items belong to the control-flow family, against roughly 0.6 expected for an unchanged process, and each item's difference exceeded 199 of 200 random re-splits, so sampling noise cannot account for the set; the items overlap heavily and corroborate one placement change rather than many independent ones. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]

What the evidence cannot rule out: with 200 traces per window, the 1–2 residual observations per affected pair are consistent both with noise and with the old late branch surviving very rarely, and a block that moved cannot be distinguished from an old branch that was removed while an identical one was inserted after A25. Position indicators use event positions rather than clock time, so they corroborate but do not by themselves establish the move. [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, POS006, POS007, POS008]

Other differences: small residuals — in the later window A25 still led to A28 in about 1 in 200 of its outgoing transitions, A27 occasionally recurred within a trace (about 2–3 in 100), and a handful of one-off successors of A25, A36, A37 and A42 appear at about 0.5–1 in 100 on either side; none alters the main picture.
