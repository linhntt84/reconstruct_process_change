- Six activities — A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 — moved from optional to near-universal. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the six activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 — previously executed for only about half of the cases, in varying subsets, between A30 and A31 — were executed in nearly every case, so that the whole group runs as a block between A30 and A31 and A31 is pushed beyond five steps after A30. This rests on direct participation evidence for all six activities (each backed by roughly 100 versus 197–199 traces in the two windows), corroborated by positional evidence showing near-universal participation, and by consistent relational evidence: A30 now leads first to A35/A33/A36, A31 is no longer a direct successor of A33/A35/A36, and the A30→A31 pair within five steps collapses from 199 to 5 traces. Screening produced 46 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of a stable process produce. Limitations: the internal order of A33/A35/A36 remains mixed (both A33→A35 and A35→A33 occur), so the evidence shows the group became mandatory rather than one fixed sequence, and the five-step window limits what can be said about the block's exact full ordering. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG004, LAG008, LAG011, LAG013]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a changed mix of case types between the two windows — for example, the later sample containing mostly cases of a kind that always runs the full group — rather than a modified routing rule; the two windows alone cannot separate these.

Other differences: A04's participation fell sharply (about 59 to 26 in 100 traces) with A14 now leading mostly directly to A15 instead of via A04; A02, A08 and A10 rose to about 18–23 in 100 traces; and immediate A47→A48 succession became slightly less dominant. These are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC002, DIR004, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, LAG015]
