- A27 was executed in about 52 in 100 traces in the earlier window and in essentially all traces in the later window (about 199 in 200), with roughly one occurrence per trace where present [OCC001].
- A32, A33 and A38 each appeared in about 48–49 in 100 traces earlier, but in only about 1 in 200 traces later; their mean occurrences per trace fell from roughly 0.49 to about 0.01 [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the alternative route from A26 through A32, A33 and A38 is effectively no longer executed: these three activities dropped from roughly half of all cases to about 1 in 200 traces, and A26 now leads almost exclusively to A27, which is immediately followed by A28. At the same time A27 became near-mandatory, rising from about half of cases to nearly all. This is a participation and routing change — a branch becoming skipped — supported directly by the participation items for all four activities, the collapse of A26's outgoing distribution onto A27, and the A26→A28 distance concentrating at two steps; the 21 qualified control-flow items far exceed the roughly 0.5 expected without change, and all items point the same way [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, LAG001].

Limitations: A32, A33 and A38 still occur in one later trace, so the evidence shows near-disappearance rather than a confirmed removal of the branch, and the internal routing of that residual case is documented only by single-trace profiles [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR003, DIR004]. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the branch still exists but was chosen in almost no later cases, versus being structurally removed.

Other differences: A28 was present in all traces in both windows and its mean event position shifted only slightly earlier (about 0.55 to 0.53 of normalized position), consistent with the shorter route but on its own not evidence that the activity was moved [POS001].
