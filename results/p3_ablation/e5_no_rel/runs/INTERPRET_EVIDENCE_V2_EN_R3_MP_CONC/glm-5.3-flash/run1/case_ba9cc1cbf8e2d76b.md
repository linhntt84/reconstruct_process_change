- A27, A28 and A29 still run in essentially every case in both windows (about 200 of 200 traces each), so the core sequence remains in place. [POS004, POS005, DIR005]
- In the earlier window, activities A19, A20 and A25 were not observed at all (0 of 200 traces). In the later window, A19 runs in about 29 in 100 cases (58 of 200), A20 in about 41 in 100 (82 of 200) and A25 in about 31 in 100 (61 of 200), each normally once per case; together they account for roughly one occurrence per later-window case. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A change supported by the evidence: in the later window, cases typically execute one additional step — A19, A20 or A25 — inserted into the sequence between A27 and A29. A27 (and, in about four in ten of its outgoing transitions, A28) hands over to the new activity, which then flows on to A28 or A29; consequently the direct successions A27→A28 and A28→A29, previously almost universal, now occur in a minority of transitions, and the step distances between A27, A28 and A29 each grew by about one step. The aggregate frequencies suggest the new step runs between A27 and A28 in roughly half of the later cases and between A28 and A29 in about four in ten. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Supporting signs: 16 qualifying items against about 0.5 expected for an unchanged process, so the differences as a whole are far beyond what random re-splits produce, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits. The appearance of the three activities from zero participation, the new incoming edges from A27/A28, the new outgoing edges to A28/A29, the increased step gaps and the mid-trace positions all describe one coherent insertion; they are mutually consistent but not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A19, A20 and A25 were not exercised there, so the evidence cannot distinguish activities newly added to the process from ones that existed before but were rarely or never run; either way, their execution rose from unobserved to most cases. The within-case arrangement — which of the three runs in a given case, and at which position — is inferred from aggregate transition and gap shares, not observed per case.
