### Observed differences

- The branch choice at A20 reversed between the windows. In the earlier window, A20's outgoing transitions went mainly to A24 (about 29 in 100), A26 (about 29 in 100) and A25 (about 24 in 100), with only small shares to A21 (about 7 in 100), A22 (about 9 in 100) and A23 (about 3 in 100); in the later window, A23 (about 29 in 100), A21 (about 29 in 100) and A22 (about 22 in 100) dominate, while A24, A25 and A26 each fall to about 6–8 in 100. [DIR003]
- Participation moved in step with this reversal. In the earlier window, A21, A22 and A23 each ran in roughly 19 in 100 traces and A24, A25 and A26 in roughly 82 in 100; in the later window, A21–A23 each run in roughly 79 in 100 traces and A24–A26 in roughly 21 in 100. Each activity still occurs about once per trace that contains it in both windows, so repetitions per trace did not change. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]
- Behaviour around A10 changed. A10 appears in about 17 in 100 traces before but only about 7 in 100 in the later window, and its relative event position shifts from tightly clustered near the end of the trace (median about 0.86, spread 0.03) to earlier and far more spread out (median about 0.36, spread 0.28). [POS001]
- A10's relations also shifted: its outgoing transitions went from almost always A33 (about 97 in 100) to a spread over several successors in the later window (A15 about 21 in 100; A16, A27, A34 and A33 about 14 each), A32 now almost never leads to A10 (about 20 in 100 of A32's transitions before, about 1 in 100 after), and the few A32→A10 pairs still observed are spread over gaps of 1–3 steps instead of mostly immediate (about 80 in 100 before). These profiles rest on few observations (33 vs 14 traces for A10; only 4 A32→A10 pairs in the later window). [DIR002, DIR004, LAG001]
- A02 appears slightly more often (about 2 in 100 traces before, about 9 in 100 in the later window) with a completely different outgoing mix; both profiles are based on very few observations (4 vs 18 transitions). [OCC001, DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the existing alternative paths after A20 are taken with different frequencies: flow that previously ran predominantly towards A24, A25 and A26 (about 82 in 100 cases) now runs predominantly towards A21, A22 and A23 (about 79 in 100 cases), the two shares being roughly reversed. The sign is direct — the successor distribution at the branching point A20 itself reverses, and A20 is observed in all 200 traces of both windows — and consistent, since all six activities change participation in matching directions and by a similar size (about 60 in 100 traces) while every one of them remains well observed in both windows (roughly 19–82 in 100 traces) and repetitions per trace stay unchanged, which fits a shift between existing alternatives rather than activities being added, removed or repeated. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of cases reaching A20, which would produce the same reversal without any change in the routing itself; the finite window sizes are a further limit, although 13 qualifying items against roughly 0.66 expected without change makes chance as a whole implausible. [DIR003, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]

Other differences: A10 becomes rarer (about 17 in 100 traces before, about 7 in 100 in the later window), appears earlier and far more variably within traces, rarely leads to A33 anymore (about 97 in 100 before, about 14 in 100 after) and is rarely reached from A32 (about 20 in 100 before, about 1 in 100 after); A02 appears slightly more often with a changed outgoing mix. Both clusters rest on few observations (14–33 traces for A10, 4–18 for A02), so they remain observed differences rather than a supported change, and a position shift alone does not show that A10 was moved.
