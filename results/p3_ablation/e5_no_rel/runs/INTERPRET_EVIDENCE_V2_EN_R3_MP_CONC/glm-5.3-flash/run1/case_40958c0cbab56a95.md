### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the passage between A44 and A29 was restructured: the block A26–A27–A28, previously executed once in every trace between A44 and A29, is no longer observed, and a shorter passage via one of A19, A20 or A21 now sits in that region, with A44 leading to these activities and each of them leading almost invariably to A29. Supporting signs: the near-complete participation reversal in both 200-trace windows (100% to about 1.5% for the old block; 1–2% to 30–38% for the new activities), the direct rewiring of A44's successors observed on roughly 200 outgoing transitions per window, the new activities' stable position where the old block used to sit, and the A44-to-A29 distance shrinking by about two steps. Screening produced 31 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected without a change, so this set of differences is not what an unchanged process typically yields. Limitations: the OCC, DIR, POS and LAG items largely describe one underlying rerouting rather than independent confirmations; no co-occurrence evidence shows whether A19, A20 and A21 are mutually exclusive alternatives (their participations sum to about 100%) or can occur together; and the earlier routing of A19–A21 rests on only 2–4 observations, so their before-window edges cannot be characterised.
