### Observed differences

- Three activities that were not observed in any of the 200 earlier traces appear regularly in the later window: A20 in about 40 in 100 traces (82/200), A19 in about 30 in 100 (59/200) and A25 in about 30 in 100 (60/200). Each occurs at most once per trace (mean occurrences per trace roughly equals the share of traces containing it). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- In the earlier window, the flow around the neighbouring steps was a tight chain: A27 was followed by A28 within one step in about 99 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, A28 was followed by A29 within one step in about 99 in 100, and the distance from A27 to A29 was two steps in about 99 in 100 traces. A27, A28 and A29 themselves occur in essentially every trace in both windows. [DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS004, POS005]
- In the later window this chain is loosened: A27 still leads to A28 in about 44 in 100 of its outgoing transitions but now leads to one of the new activities in about 55 in 100 (A20 about 22, A25 about 17, A19 about 15); A28 still leads to A29 in about 55 in 100 but leads to a new activity in about 43 in 100. [DIR004, DIR005]
- The new activities in turn lead mainly to A28 (about 52–56 in 100 of their outgoing transitions) or to A29 (about 42–46 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The step distances confirm an insertion pattern: A27→A28 within one step fell from about 99 to about 44 in 100 (now often with one event in between, about 55 in 100); A28→A29 within one step fell from about 99 to about 56 in 100; and the A27→A29 distance moved from two steps (about 99 in 100 traces) to three steps (about 97 in 100). The new activities sit roughly halfway through the trace by event order (mean relative position about 0.51). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- Smaller shifts: A10 rose from about 5 to about 14 in 100 traces (10 to 27 traces, no repetition within traces in either window); A27's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (0.48 to 0.46) and A29's slightly later (0.55 to 0.57), consistent with events inserted between them. [OCC001, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new step is inserted into the formerly fixed sequence A27 → A28 → A29 in nearly every trace: the inserted step is one of the previously unobserved activities A19, A20 or A25, placed either directly after A27 (about 55 in 100 of A27's outgoing transitions) or directly after A28 (about 43 in 100 of A28's outgoing transitions), and it then continues to A28 or A29. In the earlier window none of these three activities was observed, and A27, A28 and A29 followed one another directly. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The hypothesis is shown directly, not only through occurrence counts: the successor profiles of A27 and A28 shift from almost single successors to mixed routing, and the lag distributions show the A27→A29 distance growing from two to three steps in about 97 in 100 later-window traces, which matches exactly one inserted activity between them; the small opposite position shifts of A27 and A29 fit the same picture. The 17 qualifying control-flow items are roughly 35 times the 0.48 expected for an unchanged process, and the supports behind the key differences (about 200 outgoing transitions or traces per item, and 0 versus 59–82 traces for the new activities) make rare-variant sampling an unlikely explanation. [DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS004, POS005, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether A19, A20 and A25 are mutually exclusive alternatives within a trace — their participation shares sum to about 100 in 100 traces, but co-occurrence is not reported — nor whether the choice among them and between the two insertion points is random or driven by case attributes not captured here; this is the main competing explanation the control-flow evidence cannot rule out. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

Other differences: A10, observed in about 5 in 100 earlier traces, appears in about 14 in 100 later traces (10 to 27 traces) without repetition in either window; the evidence does not tie this to the insertion pattern. [OCC001]
