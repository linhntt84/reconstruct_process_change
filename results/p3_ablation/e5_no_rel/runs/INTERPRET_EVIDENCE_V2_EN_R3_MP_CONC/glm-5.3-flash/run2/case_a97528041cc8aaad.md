### Observed differences

- **Two mid-process segments exchanged their places.** In the earlier window, A19, A21 and A24 (typically preceded by A29) sent about 95–97 in 100 of their outgoing transitions to A30, while the cluster A20/A22/A23 (typically preceded by A40) sent about 30–38 in 100 to A25. In the later window this is reversed: A19/A21/A24 now continue to A25 in about 97–98 of 100 transitions and are typically preceded by A40, while A20/A22/A23 continue to A30 in about 31–33 of 100 and are typically preceded by A29. A14 now leads to A02 in 60 of 100 of its outgoing transitions (1 before, when A15 took 84; A15 still takes 37), and A02 leads to A15 in 87 of 100 (9 before); A14 is immediately followed by A02 in 98% of later-window cases (121 vs 10 traces) and A02 by A15 in about half (123 vs 4). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two mid-process segments exchanged their places in the case sequence. In the earlier window the flow typically ran from A40 into the A20/A22/A23 cluster, which exited to A25, and from A29 into the A19/A21/A24 group, which exited to A30; in the later window A40 typically leads into the A19/A21/A24 group exiting to A25, and A29 into the A20/A22/A23 cluster exiting to A30 — each feeder now precedes the other group, and each group continues into the target the other used. [DIR005, DIR007, DIR010, DIR011, DIR012]

The signs are direct and consistent: successor profiles flipped almost completely on well-observed activities (A20/A22/A23, A29 and A40 with about 200 outgoing transitions per window; A19/A21/A24 with 58–88), the position distributions shifted in opposite directions exactly as an exchange of places implies, and new immediate successions appeared precisely where the old ones vanished. Because the new continuations were unobserved before and the old ones disappeared, this is not a mere shift in the shares of paths present in both windows; and with 52 qualifying items against roughly 0.8 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole. [DIR005, POS003, POS005, LAG013, LAG018, LAG019]

Limitations: POS and LAG are based on event positions, so "earlier" and "later" mean sequence position, not clock time. The evidence cannot distinguish a wholesale exchange of the two segments from a set of individually re-targeted edges, and it cannot fully rule out that both orders coexisted before with the later one merely rare; items describing the same traces (for example the A19/A21/A24 successor changes together with the A21/A24→A25 distances) are not independent confirmations. [POS003, LAG019, DIR005]

Other differences:
- A02 now sits as a frequent early step between A14 and A15 (participation up from 15 to 65 in 100 traces; the chain A14→A02→A15 is now typical); the evidence cannot tell whether A02 was moved there from its earlier mid-trace position or gained an additional occurrence. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR004, POS001, LAG001, LAG005, LAG006]
- A06 and A10 are now observed in only 5–6 of 100 traces (previously 17–22); the evidence does not show what replaced them. [OCC002, OCC003]
- The existing links A43→A44 and A45→A46 became slightly more dominant (about 84→98 and 86→97 in 100 outgoing transitions). [DIR013, DIR014, LAG026]
