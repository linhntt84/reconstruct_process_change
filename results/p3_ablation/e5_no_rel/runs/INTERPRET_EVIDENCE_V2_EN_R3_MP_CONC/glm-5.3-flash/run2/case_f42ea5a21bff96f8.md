### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Main hypothesis: in the later window the continuation after A20 runs through A24/A25/A26 in about three quarters of traces, where earlier it ran through A21/A22/A23 — the group A21/A22/A23 dropped from about 75 to about 25 in 100 traces while A24/A25/A26 rose from about 25 to about 75, and A20's outgoing transitions flipped accordingly. The signs are direct and consistent: six mirrored participation changes of about 50 in 100 each, a directly observed redirect of A20's outgoing edges, and far more frequent A20→A24/A25 followings; with 29 flagged control-flow items against roughly one expected without a change, the pattern as a whole cannot be a sampling artefact. All six activities remain observed in substantial shares in both windows with unchanged occurrences per trace, so this reads as a shift in how often two existing alternative continuations are taken, not as activities being added or removed [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, DIR002, LAG002, LAG003, LAG009].

What the evidence cannot settle is the exact relation between the two groups: with no direct order observations between A21–A23 and A24–A26, a swap of the two blocks' positions in the sequence cannot be ruled out as an alternative to a change in the choice between alternative continuations after A20 [DIR002, LAG002, LAG003].

Other differences: A06 appears as a new step between A34 and A35 near the end of traces — participation up from about 17 to 52 in 100, position at about the 96th percentile of trace length, A34 leading to it in about half of its outgoing transitions, and A06 immediately followed by A35 in about 97 in 100 cases, which also explains the A34→A35 distance moving from one to two steps in about half the cases; A25 and A26 are also now typically followed by A06 a few steps later, though on few cases [OCC002, POS001, DIR001, DIR005, LAG001, LAG013, LAG004, LAG006]. In addition, A02 and A08 participation decreased [OCC001, OCC003].
