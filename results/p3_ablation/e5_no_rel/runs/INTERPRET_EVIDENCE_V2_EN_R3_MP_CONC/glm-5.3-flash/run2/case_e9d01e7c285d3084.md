### Observed differences

- The activities A30, A31 and A36 were single-pass in the earlier window — each appeared about once per trace and took part in every trace — but in the later window each occurs about 3.5 times per trace, with repeats in about 68 in 100 traces (before: at most about 2 in 100). Participation remains 100% in both windows, so this is added repetition, not new uptake. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The flow out of A36 changed: before, about 97 in 100 of A36's observed outgoing transitions went to A25; in the later window about 70 in 100 go to A30 and about 28 in 100 still go to A25. The number of observed outgoing transitions grew from about 200 to about 700, in line with the repetition of A36. [DIR001]
- The tight single-pass pattern in which A30 was followed by A36 two positions later (about 98 in 100 traces before) largely dissolved in the later window: the two-step gap accounts for only about 12 in 100, with small remnants at three- to five-step gaps. [LAG002]
- The relative positions of A30 and A36 became more spread out (standard deviation about 0.07 → about 0.12) while their medians moved only slightly later, consistent with several executions at varying depths rather than a shift to one fixed later position. [POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window contains a repeated cycle through A30, A31 and A36: three activities that each ran exactly once per trace before are now executed about 3.5 times per trace, and A36's output returns to A30 in about 70 in 100 of its outgoing transitions instead of proceeding to A25 as it did before (about 97 in 100). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: repetition is measured for all three activities with full participation and 200 traces behind each window; the rerouting is observed directly in A36's outgoing transitions; and the dissolution of the once-dominant two-step A30→A36 gap together with the wider positional spread of A30 and A36 fits multiple executions at varying depths. Nine control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.47 expected if nothing had changed — roughly twenty times the chance expectation — and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled windows, so the set as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: the internal wiring of the cycle is not directly observed — there is no relational (REL) evidence and no routing evidence for A30 or A31, so whether A31 sits between A30 and A36 (suggested only by the earlier two-step gap) and whether the three activities repeat as one joint block rather than independently cannot be confirmed; the agreeing items also largely reflect the same traces and are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the repeats arise from a different arrangement, for example with the A36→A25 route persisting in parallel (about 28 in 100 after) rather than as a single exit from a loop. [DIR001, LAG002]

Other differences: A14 now occurs slightly earlier within traces (mean relative position about 0.14 → about 0.12) with more variability, and the gap from A05 to A14 shifted from mostly one step (about 36 in 100) to mostly two to three steps (about 37 and 45 in 100); these concern A05 and A14 and are not part of the main hypothesis. [POS001, LAG001]
