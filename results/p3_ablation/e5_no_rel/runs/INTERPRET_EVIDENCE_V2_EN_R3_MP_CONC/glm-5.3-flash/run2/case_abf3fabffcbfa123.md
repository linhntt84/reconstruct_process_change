### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the alternative route after A26 through A32, A33 and A38 is effectively no longer executed: A26 is followed by A27 in about 98 in 100 cases (about 50 in 100 before), each group activity fell from about 49 in 100 traces to 1 in 200, and A27 is now executed once in almost every trace, while the A26→A28 distance concentrates at two steps, consistent with the shorter route. The routing change is observed directly on A26 with essentially full support in both windows, and the occurrence, position and lag pictures agree; the screening produced 32 qualifying items where about 0.64 would be expected without a change, so the set as a whole is not what a stable process typically yields. Limitations: the group's later-window behaviour rests on a single trace, so the evidence shows the branch became rare rather than strictly removed, and the items describing the group's internal ordering carry only one later-window observation each. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows draw different mixtures of trace variants from a mixed population rather than reflecting a single change point.
