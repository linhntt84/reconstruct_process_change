### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the activities A15, A17, A18, A19, A20 and A21 were executed in a flexible order. Outgoing transitions were spread over several successors: for example, A15 led to A18 in about 62 in 100 observed transitions and to A21, A19 or A20 in about 12–14 in 100 each, and A18 led mainly to A27 (about 53 in 100) rather than to a single step. Several pairs also ran in both orders: A15 preceded A19 within five steps in about 51 of 200 traces while A19 preceded A15 in about 149, and A18 preceded A20 in about 94 traces while A20 preceded A18 in about 106, in each case at mixed gaps of 1–5 positions. [DIR001, DIR003, LAG002, LAG014, LAG011, LAG019]

- In the later window, this flexibility is gone: each of these activities, plus A26, has essentially one successor, forming the fixed chain A26 → A17 → A15 → A18 → A19 → A21 → A20 → A27. A15 leads to A18 in about 100 in 100 transitions (about 62 in 100 before), A17 to A15 (about 56 before), A18 to A19 (about 12 before), A19 to A21 (about 56 before), A21 to A20 (about 66 before), A20 to A27 (about 47 before), and A26 to A17 (50 before, with the other half going to A19). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]

- Pair distances collapsed accordingly: pairs that previously appeared at several gaps and in both orders now occur at exactly one gap in one order, matching the chain. A15 reaches A19 two positions later in about 199 of 200 traces (before: gaps 1–2 in about 51 traces), A17 reaches A20 five positions later in about 200 of 200 (before: spread over gaps 1–5 in about 177 traces), and A26 reaches A19 four positions later in about 200 of 200 (before: directly, one position later, in about half of the traces). The reverse orders are almost unobserved afterwards, at about 1 trace each. [LAG002, LAG009, LAG027, LAG014, LAG019, LAG021]

- Event positions shifted consistently with the new sequence: A17, A15 and A18 now appear earlier in the case (mean relative position about 0.56, 0.59 and 0.62, from about 0.59, 0.64 and 0.68), while A19, A21 and A20 appear later (about 0.65, 0.68 and 0.71, from about 0.59, 0.64 and 0.68). Each of these activities still occurs in essentially every trace in both windows. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering constraints between A15, A17, A18, A19, A20 and A21 changed: in the earlier window they could follow one another in several orders, while in the later window they run as one fixed sequence A17 → A15 → A18 → A19 → A21 → A20, entered from A26 via A17, whereas A26 previously led to A19 or A17 about equally often. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG002, LAG014]

Supporting signs: 42 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.43 expected for an unchanged process across 87 tested units, so the volume of flagged behaviour cannot be explained by random re-splitting of the windows. The relational evidence is direct — successor sets collapsed from three or four alternatives to one, and pairs that ran in both orders at varying gaps now run at a single gap in one order in essentially all traces — and the position shifts agree in direction with the new sequence. Participation did not change (each activity occurs in essentially all traces in both windows), so this is a reordering of the same activities, not activities being added, removed or repeated differently. [LAG002, LAG009, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

Limitations: for a few reverse-order pairs the later window contains only a single observation (A19→A15, A19→A18, A20→A18, A21→A18), so their near-disappearance rests on very little data, and the position indicator reflects event order rather than clock time. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the earlier flexibility was a mixture of several fixed-order variants that later collapsed into one dominant variant, rather than unordered execution within single cases; both would produce the observed before-window profiles. [LAG014, LAG015, LAG019, LAG021]

Other differences: the spread of event positions narrowed slightly for all six activities (standard deviations down by roughly 0.005–0.009), which is consistent with the same tightening of order rather than a separate change. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
