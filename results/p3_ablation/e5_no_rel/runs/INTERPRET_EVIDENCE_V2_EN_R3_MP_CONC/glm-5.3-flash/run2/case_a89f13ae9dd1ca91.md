### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured: a block of three activities (A21, A22, A23) that ran once each in every case, in varying internal order, was replaced by a single activity chosen among three alternatives (A14, A15, A16), each taken in roughly a third of cases. The successor change at A39 is observed directly with full support in both windows [DIR007], the participation of the six activities flipped from 100% to near 0% and from 0% to about 30–37 in 100 traces [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006], and the A39-to-A24 distance halved from 4 to 2 steps with full support in both windows [LAG010]. The screening produced 32 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically yields [screening].

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether A14–A16 are genuinely new steps or activities that existed earlier but were not observed, since zero participation only means not observed; likewise, the single later trace still containing A21–A23 shows the old block can persist rarely, so its current frequency is known only imprecisely [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the old activities still occur at a very low rate below what the sample would detect.

Other differences: A39 sits slightly later within traces (mean relative position about 0.31 before versus 0.33 after) and A40 is essentially unchanged (about 0.85 versus 0.84), both without participation changes; these are minor drifts consistent with the shorter segment rather than separate changes.
