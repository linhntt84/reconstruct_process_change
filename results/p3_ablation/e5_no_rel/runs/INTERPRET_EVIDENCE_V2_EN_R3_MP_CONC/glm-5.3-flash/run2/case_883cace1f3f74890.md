### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, one of the previously unobserved activities A19, A20 or A25 is inserted into the sequence between A27 and A29: the earlier direct hand-off A27 → A28 → A29 now contains exactly one additional step in nearly every trace, placed between A27 and A28 in roughly half the traces and between A28 and A29 in the other half, with A20 the most frequent insert (about 41 in 100 traces) ahead of A19 and A25 (about 30 in 100 each). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The hypothesis rests on direct and mutually consistent evidence rather than on the sheer number of items: participation rises from zero to 30–42 in 100 traces, the new activities' outgoing transitions point at A28 and A29, A27 and A28 reroute roughly half of their transitions to the new activities, and the A27→A29 distance grows by exactly one step in about 97 in 100 traces; the three new activities together occur about once per trace, matching that single extra step. Screening also exceeds what chance produces: 17 control-flow items qualified where about 0.48 were expected without change, and each item's difference exceeded about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Limitations: the evidence contains no direct relations among A19, A20 and A25 themselves, so their mutual order and how the choice between them is made remain open; zero participation in the earlier window strictly means only that these activities were not observed there. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that the new activities run in parallel with the A27–A29 segment rather than strictly between its steps, although the consistent one-step increase in the A27→A29 distance favours insertion. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Other differences: A10 is observed in about 13 in 100 later traces versus about 5 in 100 earlier traces; no relational or positional evidence shows where in the process this increase sits, so it is kept outside the main hypothesis. [OCC001]
