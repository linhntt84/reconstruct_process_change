### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the group A31–A32–A37 was relocated and made mandatory: it previously ran in about 46 in 100 traces as an alternative to A22 between A21 and A26, and in the later window it runs in nearly every trace between A20 and A23. The successor change at A20 and the A20→A23 gap change show the insertion directly [DIR001, LAG001]; the group's participation rose from about 46 in 100 to essentially all traces [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]; A21 now leads only to A22 and the A21→A26 distance is uniformly two positions, so the old branch no longer appears in this sample [DIR002, LAG002]; and the group now exits to A23 instead of A26 [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]. As part of the same change, A22 became mandatory, rising from about 54 in 100 traces to all [OCC001]. The item count (19 versus about 0.38 expected) rules out sampling variation as a whole, and the relational evidence (successor and gap changes) pins down the placement rather than leaving it to position shifts alone.

Limitations: each window holds only 200 traces, so behaviour rarer than about 1 in 200 could be missed — A20 still led directly to A23 in a small number of later traces [DIR001], and the evidence cannot exclude that the old optional branch survives at very low frequency outside the sample. Positions are relative to trace length, so the position shifts are read as consequences of the insertion, not clock-time moves; the internal order of A31, A32 and A37 varies in both windows, so no fixed sequence within the group can be inferred.

Other differences: A26's relative position moved slightly later (mean about 0.63 to 0.66) and A20's slightly earlier (about 0.36 to 0.34), consistent with traces lengthening rather than a separate change. [POS001, POS005]
