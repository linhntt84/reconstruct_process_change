### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-activity group A35, A36 and A41, previously executed between A25 and A27, now runs between A26 and A30: in the earlier window A25 fed the group and the group exited to A27 while A26 led almost always to A30; in the later window A25 leads directly to A27, A26 feeds the group, and the group exits to A30. The group's once-per-trace participation and its mixed internal order are unchanged, so the difference concerns the group's placement between its predecessor and successor, not how often it runs or its internal sequencing. The support is direct and consistent: the successor profiles of A25, A26 and all three group members changed exactly at the group's entry and exit, the A25→A27 and A26→A30 distances flipped from 4 to 1 and from 1 to 4 steps, and the old pairs vanish while the new ones appear, with every involved activity present in essentially all traces in both windows — so a rare-variant reading is ruled out. All 27 qualifying items (against roughly 0.73 expected for an unchanged process, and each difference larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits) far exceed what a stable process typically produces.
