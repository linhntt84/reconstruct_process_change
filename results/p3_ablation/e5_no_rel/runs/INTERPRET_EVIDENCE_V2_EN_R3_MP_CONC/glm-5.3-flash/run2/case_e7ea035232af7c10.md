### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, A36, A37 and A42 run as a mandatory block between A25 and A28, in varying order, and no longer continue into A31; before, A25 led straight to A28, while the same three activities ran in only about 46 of 100 traces, late in the case, each followed by A31. As the counterpart of this move, A26 — which previously split between A27 and the three activities — now routes almost exclusively to A27, which also became mandatory, and A31 is reached two steps after A26. [DIR001, LAG001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG005, LAG006, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS006, POS007, POS008, DIR002, LAG003, OCC001]

The rewiring is shown directly by the successor changes at A25 and at the three activities and by the A25→A28 distance stretching from lag 1 to lag 4 — the gap three inserted activities would leave — and the before-window near-complementarity of A27 (54 of 100 traces) and the three activities (46 of 100) after A26 fits one rearrangement rather than unrelated changes. With 23 qualified control-flow items against roughly 0.59 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole, and every item's difference exceeds those of about 199 of 200 random re-splits. [DIR001, LAG001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR002]

Limitations: the qualified items largely reflect this one underlying rearrangement rather than independent confirmations; whether A31 now directly follows A27 is inferred (from A26→A31 at lag 2 together with A26→A27 in 98.5 of 100 transitions) because no successor profile for A27 or A31 is included, so an alternative route to A31 cannot be excluded; LAG002 and LAG004 each rest on a single before-window observation; and positions and lags are event counts, not clock times. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, DIR002]

Other differences: only small position drifts — A26, A28 and A31 somewhat later, A25 slightly earlier — which are consistent with the repositioning above rather than separate changes. [POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005]
