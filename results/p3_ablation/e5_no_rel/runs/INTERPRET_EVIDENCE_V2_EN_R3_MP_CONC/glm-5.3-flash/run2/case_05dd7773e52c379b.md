### Observed differences

- Six activities roughly doubled their participation. In the earlier window, A28, A30 and A31 each ran in about 97 of 200 cases and A32, A33 and A34 each in about 103 of 200; in the later window all six run in about 197–199 of 200 cases. Each activity still occurs at most once per case (mean occurrences per trace equals the share of cases containing it), so this is not extra repetition. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- In the earlier window the two triads were mutually exclusive alternatives: all ~103 observed A25→A32/A33/A34 pairs sat 1–3 steps after A25 (none at 4–5), all ~97 observed A28/A30/A31→A26 pairs sat 1–3 steps before A26, and A28, A30 and A31 led only to each other or to A26 — no case shows both triads. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- In the later window, A25 leads almost exclusively into the first triad: about 99 in 100 of A25's outgoing transitions go to A28, A30 or A31 (roughly 31, 38 and 30 in 100), whereas earlier they spread evenly over all six activities (about 15–19 in 100 each). [DIR001]
- The first triad no longer exits directly to A26: A28→A26 fell from about 36 in 100 of A28's outgoing transitions to under 1 in 100 (A30 and A31 similar, from about 34 and 30 in 100 to unobserved), while A28, A30 and A31 now also lead into A32, A33 and A34 (about 8–13 in 100 each). [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- Pairwise distances grew by about one triad: the gaps from A25 to A32/A33/A34 and from A28/A30/A31 to A26 moved from 1–3 steps (roughly equal thirds) to 4–5 steps (together about 98 in 100 of observed pairs; 97–103 pair observations before, 123–143 after). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]
- Relative event positions (not clock time) shifted coherently: A28/A30/A31 occur earlier (mean relative position about 0.74 → 0.68), A32/A33/A34 later (about 0.73 → 0.77), A26 later (0.80 → 0.83) and A25 slightly earlier (0.67 → 0.62), consistent with a longer middle section per case; A25 and A26 remain in all cases in both windows. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two three-activity groups, {A28, A30, A31} and {A32, A33, A34}, changed from mutually exclusive alternative branches between A25 and A26 — in the earlier window each case executed exactly one of them (about 97 vs 103 in 200) — into a single sequential passage executed in almost every later case: A25 starts the {A28, A30, A31} group, whose activities then hand over to the {A32, A33, A34} group before A26. Each group was skipped in about half of the earlier cases; in the later window both run in about 99 in 100 cases. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG004]

The change is shown directly and consistently: participation of all six activities rose to near-universal [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]; A25's fan-out collapsed onto the first group [DIR001]; the first group's direct exits to A26 disappeared in favour of exits into the second group [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]; the A25→second-group and first-group→A26 distances grew by about one group length [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]; and the positional shifts agree with a longer middle section [POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]. In total 24 control-flow items qualified, far above the roughly 0.45 expected by chance among the 91 tested units, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

Limitations: about 2–5 of the later cases still lack one of the groups, so the evidence cannot separate "both groups became mandatory" from "both groups are taken in about 99 in 100 cases"; the ordering within each group appears flexible in both windows and any within-group re-ordering is not disentangled here. An important competing reading — that only the shares between existing alternative paths shifted — is contradicted by the near-universal participation and the vanished/new successor relations, but the evidence cannot rule out that the branches remain available in the later process and are merely almost always both taken. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR002, LAG001]
