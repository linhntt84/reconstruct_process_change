### Observed differences

- The three activities A14, A15 and A20 each ran in about 47 in 100 traces in the earlier window (94 of 200) but in essentially all traces in the later window (199 of 200, about 99.5 in 100); neither repeats within a trace in either window. Their figures are identical, consistent with the three always executing together as one block. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- Routing out of A36 changed accordingly: in the earlier window its observed outgoing transitions went to A21 in about 53 in 100 cases and to A14 in about 47 in 100; in the later window they go to A14 in about 995 in 1000 cases and to A21 in only about 5 in 1000. A36 itself occurs throughout both windows. [DIR001]
- The separation between A36 and A21 changed from mixed to fixed: earlier, A21 followed A36 immediately in about 53 in 100 cases and four positions later in about 47 in 100; in the later window it almost always follows four positions later (about 995 in 1000), with immediate succession nearly gone. [LAG001]
- A36 now appears slightly earlier within traces (mean relative position about 0.33 versus 0.30) and A21 slightly later (about 0.41 versus 0.43) — small shifts in line with three activities now sitting between them. [POS002, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the route which skipped A14, A15 and A20 was essentially abandoned: in the earlier window, roughly half of the traces went directly from A36 to A21 without the block, while in the later window A36 leads almost always through A14, A15 and A20 before reaching A21, making the three activities near-mandatory. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation of all three rose from about 47% to about 99.5% of traces [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]; the A36→A21 transition share collapsed from about 53% to about 0.5% while A36→A14 rose to about 99.5% [DIR001]; the A36-to-A21 distance became uniformly four steps [LAG001]; and the small opposite position shifts of A36 and A21 fit three activities being inserted between them [POS001, POS002]. Seven items qualified from 68 tested units against roughly 0.34 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is unlikely for an unchanged process [OCC001, DIR001, LAG001].

Limitations: the three activities show identical statistics in both windows, so the evidence demonstrates a block-level change and cannot establish their internal order or the exact edges among them [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]; the direct A36→A21 transition was still observed about once in 200 outgoing transitions, so the bypass is nearly unused rather than proven removed [DIR001]. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that these are rare-variant sampling differences; the screening comparison and the uniformly high empirical strength (about 0.995 on every item) make this unlikely but do not rule it out [OCC001, DIR001, LAG001].
