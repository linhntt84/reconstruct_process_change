### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A specific possibility supported by the evidence is that the step from **A41** directly to **A26**, which bypassed the group **A19, A20, A25**, was taken in about half of the earlier cases and is essentially no longer taken; as a result, the group A19, A20, A25 — previously executed in about 47 of 100 cases — is now executed in nearly every case between A41 and A26, entered from A41 towards A19 and closed by A20 directly before A26, so that A26 typically sits four steps after A41 instead of immediately after. A41 and A26 themselves occur in all cases in both windows, so this is a change in how often the group is executed, not a replacement or swap of activities. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, LAG001]

The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: the participation of all three group activities rose in lockstep from about 47% to about 99.5% with means near one occurrence per case; the rerouting is visible directly in A41's outgoing shares and in the A41–A26 distance; and A20 → A26 remains dominant throughout, fitting a group that closes into A26. Screening qualified 8 items where roughly 0.57 would be expected if nothing had changed (control-flow family, 114 units tested), far more than an unchanged process typically produces — though the eight items describe facets of one underlying behaviour rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS002, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows reflect a shift in the mix of case types (for example, a case variant that skips the group becoming rare) rather than a modified routing decision. The internal order of A19 and A25 within the group is not established, as no directional evidence between them is present; and since A19 occurs in essentially every later case while A41 → A19 accounts for about 92 of 100 of A41's outgoing steps, a second, less frequent entry into the group may exist. [OCC001, DIR002, LAG001]

Other differences: the routing profiles show scattered tail fluctuations of roughly 0.5–2 in 100 — after A20, A03 and A06 appear in the later window while A02, A28, A30 and A44 (about 1 in 100 earlier) disappear; after A41, small shares such as A06, A10, A27 and A36 appear in the later window.
