### Observed differences

- The six activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 each ran exactly once in every case before (participation 100 in 100); in the later window each appears in only about 47–57 in 100 cases, still at most once per case, so a case now executes only about three of the six on average. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- Routing out of A30 changed: before, its outgoing transitions went almost exclusively to A33 (about 34 in 100), A35 (36) and A36 (28); in the later window they spread over all six block activities (roughly 12–19 in 100 each), so A30 now also leads directly to A37, A38 and A39, which it previously did not. [DIR001]
- A31 became a direct successor of the first-stage activities: before it received virtually no transitions from A33, A35 or A36, while in the later window about 32, 34 and 26 in 100 of their outgoing transitions go to A31; their transitions to A37, A38 and A39 nearly vanished (from about 29–37 in 100 to about 1–2 in 100). [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- Distances collapsed: before, A37, A38 and A39 followed A30 four to five event positions later in essentially all observed pairs, and A31 followed A33, A35 or A36 four to five positions later; in the later window both occur within one to three positions in about 94–97 in 100 of the pairs. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG009]
- The frame of the segment is intact: A30 and A31 still occur in every case in both windows, with A31's mean event position almost unchanged (about 0.82 to 0.80) and A30's slightly later (about 0.62 to 0.67). [POS001, POS002]
- Weak signals: pairs of a first-stage and a second-stage activity (for example A33 with A38, A35 with A37) now co-occur within five steps in only 4–5 traces versus about 200 before, and a few very rare transitions (about 0.5 in 100 of outgoing transitions) from A30, A33, A35 and A36 to other activities appeared; both rest on very few later-window observations. [LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of six activities between A30 and A31 — A33, A35, A36, A37, A38, A39 — changed from a mandatory, fixed two-stage progression into a partially skipped, loosely ordered segment. Before, every case executed all six once, A30 led into one of A33/A35/A36, which led on to one of A37/A38/A39, and A31 followed about four to five events after the first-stage activity; in the later window each of the six runs in only about half the cases, A30 can be followed by any of the six within one to three events, and A31 follows A33/A35/A36 almost immediately. The participation drop, the new and disappeared transitions, and the collapsed distances show this directly and consistently, and with 27 qualifying items against roughly 0.66 expected for an unchanged process, chance cannot account for the ensemble. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG009]

The qualified items do not include the outgoing transitions of A37, A38, A39 or A31, so the complete new routing cannot be reconstructed, and the evidence cannot rule out that the later window mixes two variants — some cases still running the full six-activity block while others run a shortened one — rather than a single new structure; a few relational items also rest on only 4–5 later-window observations. [LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011]

Other differences: A33, A35 and A36 now sit slightly later in the trace (mean event position about 0.68 to 0.74) and A39 slightly earlier (about 0.77 to 0.73); these shifts are small and consistent with the segment being shorter rather than an independent relocation of the activities.
