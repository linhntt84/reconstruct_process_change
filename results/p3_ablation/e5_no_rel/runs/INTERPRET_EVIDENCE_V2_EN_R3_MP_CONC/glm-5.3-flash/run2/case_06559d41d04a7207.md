### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A44 and A29 was rebuilt: in the earlier window essentially exactly one of the three alternatives A19, A20 or A21 executed per case, each leading directly to A29, whereas in the later window these three are no longer observed and A26, A27 and A28 instead each execute once in every case, in varying order, before A29 — A44 now feeds the new trio, the A44→A29 distance is about two steps longer, and A29 itself still runs universally but sits slightly later. The case rests on direct relational evidence (successor profiles of A44 and of the old and new activities, lag distributions, matching placements) rather than on frequency shifts alone, and the screening flagged 29 items where fewer than 1 would be expected without a change, with every item's difference exceeding about 995 in 1000 random re-splits, so chance alone cannot account for the pattern as a whole.
