### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the choice following A21 collapsed onto the A22 path: earlier, a trace continued from A21 to A22 in about half the cases and otherwise to A27, A28 or A33 (roughly 14–18% of A21's observed transitions each), whereas in the later window execution proceeds from A21 to A22 in essentially every trace, A23 consistently follows two steps after A21, and A27, A28 and A33 are nearly no longer executed — consistent with these three branch activities being skipped and A22 having become effectively mandatory. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, LAG001]

Supporting signs: the participation changes are directly observed with substantial support in both windows for A22 (103 vs 199 traces) and a 97-vs-1 contrast out of 200 traces for each disappearing activity; the occurrence, direction and distance indicators all describe the same underlying difference; and screening qualified 20 control-flow items where about 0.38 would be expected without a change, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. Limitations: the later-window profiles of A27, A28 and A33 rest on one trace each, so the evidence shows they are almost unobserved rather than that they were removed from the process; whether the three branches were deleted, are now taken extremely rarely, or were replaced by a direct connection cannot be distinguished, and the agreeing items are not independent confirmations.
