### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two three-activity groups exchanged their place in the sequence and were rewired. In the earlier window, A29 fed A19, A21 and A24 late in the trace and these led almost always to A30, while A40 fed A20, A22 and A23 early in the trace, which led mostly to A25; in the later window, A19, A21 and A24 run early — entered from A02 and A40 and leading almost always to A25 — while A20, A22 and A23 run late, entered from A29 and leading about a third of the time to A30. A02, previously rare (about 8 in 100 traces), now participates in about 34 in 100 and opens the early group, with A40 to A02 immediate and A02 to A25 two steps later.
