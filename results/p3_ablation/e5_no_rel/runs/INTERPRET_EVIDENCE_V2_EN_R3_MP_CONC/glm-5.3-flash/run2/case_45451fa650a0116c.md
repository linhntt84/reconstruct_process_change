### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the existing alternative routes out of A20 are taken with very different frequencies: the route involving A21, A22 and A23, previously taken in roughly 19–20 of 100 traces, is now taken in roughly 78–80 of 100, while the route involving A24, A25 and A26 dropped from roughly 81–82 to roughly 21–22 of 100. This rests on direct and mutually consistent evidence: A20's own outgoing transitions show the flip at its source, all six activities shifted about 60 percentage points in matching directions with large supports (roughly 39 versus 158 traces on one side, 163 versus 42–44 on the other), every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, and the 24 qualified control-flow items far exceed the roughly 0.65 expected if nothing had changed. Because every involved activity remains well observed in both windows, this reads as a re-weighting of existing alternatives rather than activities becoming optional or disappearing. The evidence cannot, however, separate a changed choice at A20 itself from an upstream difference that brings a different mix of cases to this point, and it does not establish the internal order of activities within each route [DIR005, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009].
