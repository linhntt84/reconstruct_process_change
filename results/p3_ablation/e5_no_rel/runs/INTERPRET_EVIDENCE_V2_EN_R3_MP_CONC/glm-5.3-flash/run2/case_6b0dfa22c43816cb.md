### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the flow around A25, A26, A27, A30 and the A35/A36/A41 group runs in a changed order: A25 now leads straight to A27, the A35/A36/A41 group now runs after A26 rather than after A25, and A30 has taken over the place A27 held as the group's co-target, so A27 and A30 have effectively exchanged their positions relative to the group. This is shown directly by successor changes (each profile on about 200 outgoing transitions) and by step-gap changes each backed by about 200 traces in both windows, and it agrees with the position shifts; with 30 control-flow items qualifying against fewer than one expected for an unchanged process, the ensemble of differences is far larger than random re-splits of stable traces produce. An important competing explanation remains: a shift in the mix of coexisting case variants (for example a previously rare routing becoming common) would look identical in aggregate windows, and several items describe the same rerouting rather than independent confirmations.
