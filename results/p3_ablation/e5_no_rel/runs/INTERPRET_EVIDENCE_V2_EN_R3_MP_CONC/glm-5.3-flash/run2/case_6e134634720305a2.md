### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the repeated execution of the cycle through A30, A31 and A36 stopped. In the earlier window a pass typically ran A30→A31 and, via A36, returned to A30, so all three activities ran about 3.5 times per trace; in the later window each runs once, A36 leads to A25 instead of back to A30, and A36 no longer follows itself. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation stays at 100% while mean occurrences and recurrence collapse, the return edge and the A36→A30 / A36→A36 successions disappear in both directional and lag evidence, and the ten qualified items far exceed the roughly 0.5 expected by chance among the 93 tested units. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: a small residual of looping remains in the later window (A36→A30 in about 4 in 100 transitions, within-trace recurrence in about 1–2 in 100 traces), so repetition may be strongly reduced rather than entirely removed; no directional evidence covers the steps between A31 and A36, so the interior of the cycle is unobserved; and direction shares are over outgoing transitions, not cases. [DIR002, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: only second-order effects — the spread of A30's and A36's relative positions narrowed, and A30's outgoing profile shows negligible new slivers of about 0.5% each to activities such as A02, A06 and A42. [POS001, POS002, DIR001]
