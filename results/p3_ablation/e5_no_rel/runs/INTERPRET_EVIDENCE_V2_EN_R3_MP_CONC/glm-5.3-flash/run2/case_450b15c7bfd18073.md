- A22 was executed in every trace of the earlier window (participation 100%, about once per trace); in the later window it appears in only about half of the traces (102 of 200, mean 0.51 occurrences per trace). [OCC001]
- Three activities that were not observed at all in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces) — A27, A28 and A33 — each now appear in about 49 in 100 traces (98 of 200), once per trace where they occur. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The hypothesis best supported by the evidence is that a new alternative branch now runs between A21 and A23, bypassing A22 in about half of the executions. In the earlier window, every observed trace ran A21 → A22 → A23 (A22 in all traces; A23 always two steps after A21). In the later window, roughly half of the traces still take this path, while in the other half A21 routes to the three previously unobserved activities A27, A28 and A33, which each execute once in varying order before the flow reaches A23. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, LAG001]

The change is shown directly and consistently: A21's outgoing transitions split between old and new paths in almost exactly the same shares as A22's participation dropped (about 51% vs 49%), the new activities' outgoing profiles tie them to one another and to A23, and the A21→A23 distance shifts from two to four steps in the same share of traces in which the new activities appear. Each difference is larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data. All twelve qualifying items belong to the control-flow family, against fewer than one (0.33) expected by chance among the 66 tested units, so the set as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces; note, however, that the items overlap heavily and largely describe one underlying difference, so they are not independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that this branch already existed before as a very rare variant that 200 traces failed to sample, or that the later window mixes two coexisting process variants. It also does not show, within individual traces, that A22 and the new block are mutually exclusive; that is inferred from the matching aggregate shares and the lag shift rather than observed directly. [LAG001, OCC001]
