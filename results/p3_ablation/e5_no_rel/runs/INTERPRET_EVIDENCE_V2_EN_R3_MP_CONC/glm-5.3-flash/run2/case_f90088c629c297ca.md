### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A36 was followed immediately by A21 in essentially every case: the pair (A36, A21) appeared with a one-step gap in all 200 earlier traces. In the later window, that direct step covers only about 1 in 100 of A36's outgoing transitions, and in about 99 in 100 cases exactly one activity now sits between A36 and A21 (a two-step gap) [LAG001, DIR004].
- Three activities that were not observed at all in the earlier window — A14, A15 and A20 — now occur at most once per trace, in about 37, 29 and 34 in 100 traces respectively (74, 57 and 67 of 200 traces) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].
- In the later window, all observed outgoing transitions of each of these three activities lead exclusively to A21 [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003].
- A36's outgoing transitions, which previously all went to A21, are now redistributed: about 37 in 100 go to A14, about 34 in 100 to A20, about 29 in 100 to A15, and only about 1 in 100 directly to A21 [DIR004].
- The three new activities sit at roughly one-third of the trace (mean relative position about 0.35), just before A21, whose own mean position moved slightly later (from about 0.37 to 0.39) while still appearing in every trace in both windows [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, one of three activities — A14, A15 or A20 — is executed between A36 and A21 in almost every case, whereas in the earlier window A36 was always immediately followed by A21. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: A36's transitions moved from entirely A21 to mostly the three new activities [DIR004]; each of the three leads only to A21 [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]; the A36–A21 distance grew from one to two steps in about 99 in 100 cases [LAG001]; and the three activities appear only in the later window, at positions immediately preceding A21 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003].

The scale of the qualified evidence also exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces: 12 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.32 expected by chance, and the three participation shares sum to about one with no repeats, suggesting the three act as mutually exclusive alternatives for a given case, though exclusivity is not directly proven [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG001].

Limitations: the evidence cannot show what determines which of the three activities is chosen in a given case, and zero participation in the earlier window means only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed then, not that they were impossible. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the choice among the three depends on case attributes not captured in these windows [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].
