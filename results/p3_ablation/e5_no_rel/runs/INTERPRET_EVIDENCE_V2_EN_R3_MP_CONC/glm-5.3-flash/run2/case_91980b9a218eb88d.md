### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of three alternative activities — A19, A20 and A25 — was relocated. In the earlier window it ran between A45 and A26 (A45 → one of A19/A20/A25 → A26) while A27 → A28 → A32 formed a separate span; in the later window A45 leads directly to A26, and the block runs in the later span, inserted either between A27 and A28 or between A28 and A32, before reaching A32. The successor profiles of all six anchor activities changed accordingly, the block's mean event position moved from about 0.33 to about 0.6, and the pairwise relations match: the old adjacencies A19→A26 and A45→A25 vanished, new adjacencies A27→A19/A20, A28→A19/A20 and A19→A32 appeared, and spans shifted by one step (A45→A26 from two steps to one, A27→A32 from two to three). A shift in how often existing paths are taken cannot explain this, because the successor sets themselves changed rather than only their shares; and with 23 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.58 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole.
