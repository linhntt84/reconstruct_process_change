### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening qualified 30 control-flow items where fewer than 1 (0.67) would be expected if nothing had changed, so chance cannot account for the set as a whole. The evidence supports the possibility of a structural rewiring around the alternative group A19, A20 and A25: in the earlier window this group ran late in the trace (around 60% of the trace), was entered from A27/A28 and led back into A28/A32, with A26 reached directly from A45; in the later window it runs early (around 32% of the trace), is entered mainly from A08 — whose participation doubled from about 13 to 30 in 100 traces — and from A45, and each of A19, A20 and A25 leads directly to A26, while the remaining route A27→A28→A32 became a fixed, immediate chain. The successor changes are observed directly for seven source activities, the position shifts of A08 and the group are large (about 0.15–0.28 of the trace), and the new adjacencies (A08→A19, A19→A26 at distance 1) and vanished adjacencies (A28→A25, A25→A32) corroborate the same rewiring. Limitations: several items describe one underlying rewiring rather than independent confirmations, some successor profiles rest on modest counts (A08: 28 outgoing transitions before versus 58 after), and the evidence cannot distinguish a repositioning of the group from its late occurrences disappearing while new early occurrences appeared.
