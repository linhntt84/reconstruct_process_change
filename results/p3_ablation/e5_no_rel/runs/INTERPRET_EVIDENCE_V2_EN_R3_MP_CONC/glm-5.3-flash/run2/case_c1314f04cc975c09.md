### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A25, A26 and A31 each occurred exactly once in every trace (mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, present in all traces, never repeated). In the later window, each of these three activities occurs about 3.5 times per trace on average and recurs within a trace in about 68 in 100 traces, while still appearing in every trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The flow out of A31 changed: earlier, all of A31's observed outgoing transitions led to A20; later, about 71 in 100 lead to A25 and about 29 in 100 still lead to A20 (based on 200 outgoing transitions before versus about 700 after). [DIR001]
- Occurrences of A25 and A31 spread over a wider range of event positions in the later window (spread roughly doubled, e.g. A25 from about 0.06 to 0.12), while their typical positions barely moved (A25 median 0.40 to 0.43; A31 median 0.47 to 0.48) — a pattern consistent with multiple occurrences per trace rather than a relocation. - The fixed spacing between A25 and A31 largely dissolved: earlier, A31 followed A25 exactly two steps later in essentially all cases; later, a two-step gap accounts for only about 12 in 100 of the observed cases, with about 5 in 100 at a five-step gap. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window a repeating cycle runs through A25, A26 and A31: these three activities, previously executed exactly once per trace, are now executed about 3.5 times each per trace and recur in about 68 in 100 traces, and A31 typically flows back to A25 (about 71 in 100 of its transitions) instead of proceeding on to A20. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001]

Supporting signs: the repetition and the successor change at A31 are each observed directly with full support in both windows; the wider position spread of A25 and A31 and the dissolution of their fixed two-step spacing agree with repeated execution rather than a moved activity; and the seven qualifying items are roughly 24 times the 0.29 items expected by chance for an unchanged process, with every difference exceeding those seen in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, POS001, POS002, LAG001]

Limitations and competing explanation: only the A31→A25 edge is observed relationally, so the evidence cannot show whether A25, A26 and A31 form one cycle or are each repeated within a larger repeated block — A26's role rests only on its identical repetition pattern — and the old A31→A20 path still carries about 29 in 100 of A31's transitions. The items also partly describe the same trace behaviour (the three occurrence items and the position and lag items all reflect the repeated execution), so they are not independent confirmations.
