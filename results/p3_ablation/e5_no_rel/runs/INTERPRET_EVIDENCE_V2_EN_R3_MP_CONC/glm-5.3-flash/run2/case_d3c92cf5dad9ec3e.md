- The successor of the A19/A21/A24 trio flipped: in the earlier window, about 97–98 in 100 of each activity's observed outgoing transitions went to A25; in the later window, about 95–98 in 100 go to A30, and A25 is no longer observed among their successors [DIR001, DIR003, DIR006].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the two activity groups exchanged their places and exit activities in the sequence. In the earlier window, A40 was followed by one of A19, A21, A24 (consistent with exactly one of the three running per case), which led straight on to A25 in the first third of the trace, while A29 later fed the A20/A22/A23 group, which passed among its members and ended in A30; in the later window, A40 feeds the A20/A22/A23 group, which ends in A25, and A29 feeds one of A19, A21, A24, which leads to A30 — each group now occupies the other's former position and flows into the other's former exit activity [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG005, LAG009].

Supporting signs: the successor flips are near-complete rather than gradual share shifts, the position movements are large and run in opposite directions, the gap changes match the new wiring, participation of every involved activity is unchanged, and 25 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.59 expected by chance among 118 tested units — far more than an unchanged process typically produces [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009].

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish one coordinated exchange from two independent relocations of the groups, and the internal order of the A20/A22/A23 group — whose members now reach A25 at varying gaps of 1–3 steps — cannot be pinned down without relation evidence covering both directions; a few gap statements rest on only 1–2 observations in one window [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008].

Other differences: a few percent-level residual transitions changed on both sides (for example, A19's small share moved from A18 to A02, and A24's from A14 to A36, A40, A32 and A04); they are minor and may belong to the same rewiring or to sampling noise [DIR001, DIR006].
