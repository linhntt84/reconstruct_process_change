### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: the three-step chain A28 → A26 → A27, which ran in every earlier trace between A44 and A29, is no longer observed beyond about 1–2 in 100 later traces, while one of A19, A20 or A21 now follows A44 in roughly 30–38 in 100 traces each and leads almost always to A29, shortening the A44-to-A29 span from about 4–5 steps to 2–3 steps. This is a replacement of activities — the old trio effectively disappeared while the new trio appeared — not a re-weighting of paths present in both windows. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, LAG009, POS001, POS002, POS003]

The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: A44's successors switched from the old trio to the new trio, each new activity's dominant successor is A29, the participation profiles of the two groups swapped, and the A44→A29 lag compressed. The screening also qualified 31 control-flow items against roughly 0.64 expected without change, so the set of differences as a whole is far beyond what random re-splitting of a stable process produces. [DIR007, LAG009, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]

Limitations: the earlier-window picture of A19/A20/A21 rests on only 2–4 traces and the later-window picture of A26/A27/A28 on 1–3 traces, so rare residual runs of the old chain cannot be described. With no co-occurrence (REL) items, the suggestion that exactly one of A19/A20/A21 runs per case (their shares sum to about 100 in 100) is not directly confirmed. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows mix two process variants in different shares rather than reflecting a single modified process. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]

Other differences: A10 became more frequent (about 13.5 to about 22.5 in 100 traces; mean occurrences per trace 0.14 to 0.26); the evidence does not connect this to the segment replacement around A44 and A29. [OCC001]
