### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the two three-activity groups A33/A35/A36 and A37/A38/A39, which in the earlier window ran as alternatives (about one group, i.e. about three of the six activities, per trace, with no transitions between the groups), now both execute in nearly every trace in sequence: in the later window A30 leads into A33/A35/A36, these connect onward to A37/A38/A39, and A31 — which previously followed directly after the executing group — now occurs 4–5 event positions later, after the second group. The participation change is observed directly with large support in both windows (97–103 vs 197–199 traces per activity), the new cross-group transitions and the lost direct transitions to A31 agree with it, the lag shifts show the reordering relation directly, and the screening flagged 38 items where about 0.76 would be expected without a change, so the ensemble is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

Limitations: the exact sequence inside each group cannot be fixed (A33/A35/A36 and A37/A38/A39 each show several successors in both windows), and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different case mixes rather than a modified process. [DIR007, DIR008, DIR009]

Other differences: A04's reduced participation and rerouting, and the more frequent occurrence of A06 and A10, are observed but are not part of this hypothesis. [OCC001, DIR002, POS001, LAG001, OCC002, OCC003]
