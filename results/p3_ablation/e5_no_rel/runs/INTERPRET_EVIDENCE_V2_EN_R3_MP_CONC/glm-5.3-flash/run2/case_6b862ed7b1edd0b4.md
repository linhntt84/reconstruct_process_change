### Inference

Screening context: 36 control-flow items qualified, against roughly 0.71 expected if nothing had changed — many times that expectation — so the overall volume of flagged behaviour is not what random re-splits of an unchanged process produce. [screening]

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the sequence A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 is executed in virtually every case, whereas in the earlier window it was executed in about half of the cases and not executed in the rest.
