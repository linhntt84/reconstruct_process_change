### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening flagged 17 control-flow items against about 0.69 expected if nothing had changed, so the volume of flagged behaviour far exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces; every item also shows a difference larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Main hypothesis: in the later window, the choice between two existing alternative paths after A20 flipped — the branch through A24, A25 and A26, previously executed in about a quarter of traces, is now executed in about three quarters, while the branch through A21, A22 and A23 dropped from about three quarters to about a quarter. This is shown directly by the redistribution of A20's outgoing transitions and consistently by the participation of all six activities, each of which remains well observed in both windows (49–152 traces) with negligible repetition, so only the shares between existing alternatives shifted; the A20→A24 lag item agrees. Limitations: the occurrence, direction and lag items describe one underlying difference and are therefore consistent rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply contain different proportions of pre-existing trace variants.
