### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured: in the earlier window each case executed exactly one of the three alternatives A14, A15 or A16 between A39 and A24, while in the later window those three activities are no longer observed and the three activities A21, A22 and A23 instead each execute once in every case, in varying order, so a case now traverses three activities between A39 and A24 rather than one, and A24 follows four steps after A39 instead of two. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, LAG001]

The support is direct and consistent: the participation changes rest on large samples (about 70, 59 and 71 of 200 traces earlier versus 0 later; 0 versus 200 of 200 for the new trio), A39's successor profile switched completely from the old trio to the new one, and the A39–A24 distance changed uniformly in every case. Screening also points to a real difference: 21 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.36 expected if nothing had changed, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data.
