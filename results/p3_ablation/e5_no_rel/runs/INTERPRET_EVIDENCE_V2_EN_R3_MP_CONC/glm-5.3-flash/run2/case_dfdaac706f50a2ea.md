- Before, the three activities A36, A37 and A42 each ran exactly once in every trace (participation 100%, mean about 1.0 occurrence per trace); in the later window each appears in only about 47–48 in 100 traces, still once per trace when present. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the three-activity block A36, A37 and A42 was relocated and made optional, while A28 moved up directly after A25. In the earlier window the observable flow ran A25 → {A36/A37/A42, once each in varying order} → A28, later A26 → A27 with A31 two positions after A26, all in every trace; in the later window A25 leads almost always straight to A28, and after A26 each trace takes one of two nearly exclusive routes — directly via A27 to A31 in about 53 in 100 traces, or through the block A36/A37/A42 to A31 in about 47 in 100 — so the block now behaves as an optional detour rather than a mandatory stage. [DIR001, LAG001, DIR002, LAG005, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The reading is direct and internally consistent: the successor changes and the A25–A28 lag shift show the rewiring around A25 and A28 directly, the rerouting after A26 and the two lag routes to A31 show the block's new position, the participation figures show the block and A27 becoming optional, and the position shifts point the same way. The volume of qualifying items also far exceeds what an unchanged process would produce: 28 control-flow items qualified where well under one (about 0.61) was expected, and every item's difference is larger than about 995 in 1000 random re-splits. [DIR001, LAG001, DIR002, LAG005, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, OCC002, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008]

Limitations: the block's detachment from A25 and from A28 in the later window is corroborated only by lag items with a single observation each, so the relocation rests mainly on the successor and position evidence; and the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine relocation of the block from an equivalent restructuring in which the same three activities were re-inserted as a new optional detour, nor rule out that the roughly 53/47 route split would differ in another sample. Agreeing indicators also partly summarise the same traces and are not independent confirmations. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011]

Other differences: minor shifts outside the main hypothesis — A25 sits slightly later (mean relative position about 0.34 to 0.36) and A31 slightly earlier (about 0.65 to 0.63), and a few successors appear at about 1 in 100 of outgoing transitions (A12 after A36, A22 and A33 after A37, A08 after A42), all plausibly side-effects of the same restructuring. [POS001, POS005, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
