### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained A42, A23, A24 and A25 plus exactly one of five alternative activities (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22 — about 18–22 in 100 traces each). A42's outgoing transitions went entirely to these five, each of the five always led to A23, and A23 always followed A42 by two positions; A24 always led to A25 and A25 always to A29, each adjacent, so A24 and A29 were always two positions apart. [DIR008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]
- In the later window, A42 leads directly to A23 in every trace, one position apart. [DIR008, LAG004]
- In the later window, the five alternatives no longer lead to A23: each leads to A25 in roughly 37–68 of 100 of its outgoing transitions and to A29 otherwise. A24 now passes to one of the five in about 51 of 100 of its transitions (previously always to A25), and A25 passes to one of the five in about 49 of 100 (previously always to A29). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]
- The five alternatives run much later in the trace: their median position moved from about 0.30–0.33 to about 0.59–0.60 of the trace, while A23, A24 and A25 each shifted slightly earlier (mean positions roughly 0.35→0.31, 0.59→0.55, 0.62→0.60). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]
- The order between each alternative and A23/A24 flipped: earlier, an alternative co-occurred with A23 or A24 in about 18–23 in 100 traces and always ran first; in the later window it co-occurs at the same rate but always runs after A23 and after A24. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]
- Distances match one insertion per trace: A24→A25 is now adjacent in 49 of 100 cases and one position apart in 51; A25→A29 is adjacent in 51 and one apart in 49; A24→A29 is always three positions apart (previously always two). This fits the chosen alternative being inserted between A24 and A29 in every trace — directly after A24 in about half the traces and directly after A25 in the other half. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Participation itself did not change: exactly one of the five alternatives runs in every trace in both windows (their per-trace shares sum to 100 in 100 in each window), and A23, A24 and A25 still appear in all traces. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the five-way alternative segment (A14, A15, A20, A21, A22 — exactly one executes per trace) now runs in the late part of the trace instead of the early part: in the earlier window the chosen alternative sat between A42 and A23 while A24, A25 and A29 followed consecutively; in the later window A42 connects straight to A23, and the chosen alternative is inserted between A24 and A29, after A24 in about half of the traces and after A25 in the other half, so A24 and A29 are now always three positions apart. [DIR008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

The change is shown directly on both sides of the segment — A42's successors switched entirely from the five alternatives to A23, the five switched from A23 to A25/A29, and A24/A25 now hand over to the five in about half of their transitions — and it is consistent across successor profiles, order relations, positions and distances, each item differing from about 199 of 200 random re-splits. Screening qualified 28 items where fewer than 1 (0.43) would be expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is not what a stable process produces. [DIR008, DIR006, DIR007, REL001, REL003, POS001, POS003, LAG002, LAG004]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the mix among the five alternatives shifted as well, since per-activity samples are small (30–45 traces each; e.g. A22 led to A25 in about 68 of 100 of its transitions, A15 in about 37). It also cannot show whether the choice of insertion point (before or after A25) is independent of which alternative runs, and it says nothing about the unobserved segment between A23 and A24. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
