### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A22 and the five-activity group A31/A32/A37/A38/A39 became mutually exclusive alternatives and that the group was relocated. Earlier, every trace ran A20, then the five in unfixed order, then A23, A21, A22 and A26. In the later window, A20 leads almost always directly to A23, and the segment between A21 and A26 contains either A22 (about 45 in 100 traces) or the whole five-activity group (about 55 in 100), with both together in only about 1 in 200 traces; A21's outgoing transitions follow the branch (about 45% to A22, about 55% into the group). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL030, REL031, REL032, REL033, REL034, REL035, REL036, REL037, REL038, REL039, DIR001, DIR002, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024]

The reading rests on direct evidence: participation and co-occurrence changes computed on 91–200 traces per item, successor changes at A20, A21 and the group members, and consistent order reversals; every item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits, and 72 items qualified where roughly 0.7 would be expected if nothing had changed, so chance cannot account for the evidence as a whole — though the many items largely describe one restructuring rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, REL015, DIR001, DIR002, REL008, REL020]

Limitations: the exclusivity is not perfectly absolute (one co-occurrence in 200 traces), the exact 45/55 split carries sampling uncertainty, and the LAG profiles rest on single later observations. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the branch is governed by an unobserved routing element: the data show only that A21's outgoing transitions track the choice, not where or how it is made. [REL015, DIR002]

Other differences: small position drifts for A20 (mean about 0.32 to 0.35) and A26 (about 0.67 to 0.65), plausibly consequences of the changed trace composition rather than independent moves. [POS001, POS005]
