- In the earlier window, every trace contained A39 and A24, and exactly one of A14, A15 or A16 sat between them: A39 was followed by A14 in about 35 in 100 traces, by A15 in about 30, and by A16 in about 36; each of the three always led to A24, and the three never occurred in the same trace. - The span between A39 and A24 grew from exactly 2 to exactly 4 event positions, and A24's mean relative position moved later, from about 0.39 to about 0.44 of the trace. [LAG001, POS007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured: in the earlier window it held exactly one of A14, A15 or A16, chosen exclusively in about 35, 30 and 36 in 100 traces respectively, while in the later window A14–A16 disappear from the sample and A21, A22 and A23 instead run in every trace, once each, with no fixed order among the three, after A39 and before A24. Because the old activities are entirely absent from the later window and the new ones entirely absent from the earlier one, this is an exchange of activities rather than a re-weighting of shared paths. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, REL019, REL021, REL022, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027]

The support is direct and consistent across indicators: occurrence counts flip from 0 to full participation (or back) with 59–71 or 200 traces behind each window's profile; the successor profiles of A39 and of A21–A23 change exactly as the hypothesis requires; the relational items show the new activities always co-occurring, ordered after A39 and before A24 with mixed internal order; and the lag and position items corroborate an insertion between A39 and A24 (gap 2 → 4, A24 about 0.04 later in relative position). The screening qualified 48 items where roughly 0.5 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the body of evidence is far larger than random re-splits of a stable process produce. [DIR004, DIR007, LAG001, POS007, screening]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A14–A16 were not observed in the later window, so the evidence cannot show whether they still exist unobserved, and it cannot distinguish a modification of one process from a complete switch in the mix of process variants between the two samples. The "no fixed order" reading of A21–A23 also rests on both orders appearing in similar proportions within these finite windows. [OCC001, OCC004]
