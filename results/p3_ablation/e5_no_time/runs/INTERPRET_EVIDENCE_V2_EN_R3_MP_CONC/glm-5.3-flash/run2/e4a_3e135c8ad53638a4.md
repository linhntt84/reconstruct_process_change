### Observed differences

- Before, every trace contained A31, A32 and A37 exactly once each, as a group sitting between A20 and A23: A20's outgoing transitions split about 35 in 100 to A37, 34 to A31 and 31 to A32, the group members appeared one to three steps after A20, and A23 followed A20 four steps later in every case. In the later window, A20 leads directly to A23 in about 995 in 1000 of its outgoing transitions, and the A20–A23 distance is one step in about 995 in 1000 cases. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS004]

Participation fell from every trace to about 54 in 100 for A22 and to about 47 in 100 for each of A31, A32 and A37, while A23 still occurs in all traces. - After A21 the flow now branches: A21's outgoing transitions go to A22 in about 54 in 100 cases and to A31, A32 or A37 in about 46 in 100, whereas before A21 always led to A22. The A21–A26 distance is two steps in about 54 in 100 later cases (via A22) and four steps in about 47 in 100 (through the group), and A26 now appears without A22 in about 47 in 100 traces. The group still runs as a set of three in mixed order: in the later window A31, A32 and A37 co-occur in the same 47 in 100 traces, and in the other 53 in 100 none of them appears. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the group of three activities A31, A32 and A37, previously always executed together between A20 and A23, was relocated to after A21 and made an exclusive alternative to A22: in the later window A23 immediately follows A20, and a trace takes either A22 (about 54 in 100) or the whole group (about 47 in 100), almost never both. [DIR001, LAG001, REL011, REL012, REL013, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The placement change is shown directly by relational and distance evidence, not by frequencies alone: the group's order relative to A21 and A23 flipped from always-before to (when present) always-after, A21 now branches to A22 or to the group with matching A21–A26 distances of two versus four steps, and the group members lead to A26 instead of A23. Screening also makes chance an unlikely explanation for the pattern as a whole: 47 items qualified where about 0.5 would be expected if nothing had changed. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG005]

Limitations: the many agreeing OCC, POS and REL items describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations; the evidence cannot distinguish relocating the group from removing it at its old position and inserting identically named activities later; and the after-window lag observations pairing A20 or A23 with the group members rest on a single trace each. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

Other differences: A20's mean relative position moved slightly later (about 0.34 to 0.36), while A21, A22 and A26 each shifted slightly earlier; these small shifts are consistent with byproducts of the group's relocation rather than separate changes. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS005]
