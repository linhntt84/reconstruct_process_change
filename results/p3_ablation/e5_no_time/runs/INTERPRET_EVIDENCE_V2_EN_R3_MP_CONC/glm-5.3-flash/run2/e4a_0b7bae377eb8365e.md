### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural insertion in the segment A22 → A23 → A24: in the later window, one of three mutually exclusive activities — A14, A15 or A20 — executes exactly once in almost every case, placed either between A22 and A23 (about 56 in 100 traces) or between A23 and A24 (about 44 in 100), whereas in the earlier window these three activities were not observed at all and the chain ran directly. A22, A23 and A24 themselves remain mandatory in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS004, POS005]

The screening produced 28 qualifying control-flow items where about 0.4 would be expected without a change, far beyond what a stable process yields; the indicators show the same structure directly and agree numerically — A22's transitions to the new activities (about 56%) match the share of traces where the A22–A23 distance becomes 2, A23's transitions to them (about 44%) match the A23–A24 distance change, and the three participation shares sum to the 99.5% of traces at the new A22-to-A24 distance of 3. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

The evidence cannot show what determines the choice among the three activities or between the two positions, since no case attributes are available, and it cannot distinguish genuinely new steps from work that existed before the observed window under other labels; about 1 in 200 later traces still runs the old direct chain. [LAG002, REL001, REL002, REL006]

Other differences: A22 and A24 shift only slightly in mean relative position (A22 about 0.48 to 0.46, A24 about 0.55 to 0.56), which is consistent with an event being inserted between them rather than an independent relocation of either activity. [POS004, POS005]
