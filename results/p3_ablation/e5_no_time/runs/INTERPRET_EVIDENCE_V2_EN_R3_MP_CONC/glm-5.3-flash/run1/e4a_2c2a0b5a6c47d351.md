### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 changed from a single choice among three alternatives to a mandatory block of three activities. In the earlier window, every trace ran A39, then exactly one of A14, A15 or A16 (about 35, 30 and 36 in 100 traces, never together), each leading directly to A24. In the later window, A14, A15 and A16 were no longer observed, while A21, A22 and A23 each ran exactly once per trace in varying mutual order, always after A39 and always before A24; the A39-to-A24 distance grew from 2 to 4 positions and A24 moved later in the trace. The volume of qualifying evidence (48 items against roughly 0.5 expected without change) and its consistency across occurrence, routing, position and relation indicators make chance sampling of the two windows an implausible explanation for the pattern as a whole. [screening]

Limitations: zero participation only means an activity was not observed in that window, so it cannot be ruled out that A14, A15 or A16 still occur in a very small share of later cases; and although both the old alternatives and the new block sit in the same A39–A24 segment, the evidence cannot establish that the new block replaced the old one rather than the two sets changing independently.
