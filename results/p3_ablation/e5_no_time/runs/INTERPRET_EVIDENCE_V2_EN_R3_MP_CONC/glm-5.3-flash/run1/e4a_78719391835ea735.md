### Observed differences

- Two groups of activities exchanged their place in the trace. In the earlier window, A14, A16 and A19 each ran early (average event-order position about 0.34 of the trace length) while A15, A17 and A18 ran late (about 0.64); in the later window the positions are reversed, with A15/A17/A18 at about 0.38 and A14/A16/A19 at about 0.67. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

- Every ordering relation between the two groups flipped. Where an early-group and a late-group activity co-occur (about 30–35 in 100 traces), the group-{A14,A16,A19} member always came first in the earlier window (e.g., A14 before A15 in all 30 co-occurrences), and the group-{A15,A17,A18} member always came first in the later window (A15 before A14 in about 33 in 100). For pairs that co-occur in every trace the flip is total: A20 preceded A15, A17 and A18 in 100 in 100 traces before, and followed each of them in 100 in 100 after; the same reversal holds for A24 versus A15/A17/A18. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009, REL015, REL016, REL018, REL019]

- Successors at the segment boundaries were rewired. Before, A14, A16 and A19 led exclusively to A20 (all observed outgoing transitions); in the later window they lead exclusively to A25. The exits from the A15/A17/A18 group moved the opposite way, from A25 (about 30–38 in 100 outgoing transitions) to A20 (about 28–37 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

- The two routing points changed targets completely. Before, A24 fed into A15 (about 37 in 100 transitions), A18 (33) and A17 (30), and A35 fed into A16 (35), A19 (36) and A14 (30); in the later window A24 feeds into A19 (39), A16 (29) and A14 (33), and A35 into A17 (36), A15 (33) and A18 (31). [DIR007, DIR008]

- Segment lengths swapped along with the contents: the gap from A24 to A25 was always 4 steps before and always 2 steps after, while the gap from A35 to A20 was always 2 steps before and always 4 after; accordingly A20 and A24 each shifted about 0.07 later in relative position. [LAG001, LAG002, POS007, POS008]

- Participation barely moved, so the difference is placement rather than frequency: A15, A17 and A18 appear in all traces in both windows, and each of A14, A16, A19 appears in roughly 3 in 10 traces in both windows (A14 30%→33%, A16 35%→29%, A19 36%→39%), with exactly one of the three per trace (their support counts sum to 200 of 200 in each window). [POS001, POS003, POS006, DIR001, DIR003, DIR006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the two segments between the routing points and their following activities exchanged places. In the earlier window, the trace ran from A35 through exactly one of A14/A16/A19 to A20, and later from A24 through the three-activity sequence over A15/A17/A18 to A25; in the later window, A35 leads through the A15/A17/A18 sequence to A20, and A24 leads through one of A14/A16/A19 to A25. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: all cross-segment ordering relations reversed, including six deterministic 100-in-100 reversals; the successor changes (A14/A16/A19 exiting to A25 instead of A20, the A15/A17/A18 exits moving from A25 to A20) and the complete rerouting of A24 and A35 match the same reordering; and the lag distances swap (4↔2 and 2↔4 steps) exactly as expected when a one-activity and a three-activity segment exchange places, while participation stays unchanged. Chance is also implausible as a whole: 39 items qualified where fewer than one (0.46 for 93 tested units) would be expected without a change, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [REL008, REL009, DIR001, DIR002, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG002, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: almost all items describe one and the same underlying reordering, so they corroborate it jointly rather than as independent confirmations; and because each window shows only one of the two orderings, the evidence cannot distinguish a rerouting of a single process from a switch between two pre-existing variants whose relative share flipped between the windows. [REL001, REL008]
