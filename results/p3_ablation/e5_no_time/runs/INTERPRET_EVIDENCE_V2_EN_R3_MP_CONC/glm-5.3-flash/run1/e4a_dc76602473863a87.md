Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative block formed by A14, A15 and A20 between A36 and A21 is no longer executed: in the earlier window each case ran A36, then exactly one of the three alternatives, then A21, while in the later window A36 is immediately followed by A21 and the three activities are unobserved in 200 traces.
