### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, all of A20, A21, A22, A23, A26, A31, A32 and A37 ran in every one of the 200 traces, in the sequence A20 → A31/A32/A37 (whose internal order varied from case to case) → A23 → A21 → A22 → A26. [REL002, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL014, REL017, REL020, POS002, POS004]
- In the later window, A20, A21, A23 and A26 still run in every trace, but A22 runs in about 54 in 100 traces (107 of 200) and each of A31, A32 and A37 in about 47 in 100 (94 of 200), down from universal participation; none of these activities repeats within a trace in either window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A20 now leads directly to A23: A23 receives about 995 in 1,000 of A20's observed outgoing transitions, whereas earlier A20's outgoing transitions went to A31, A32 and A37 in roughly equal thirds, and the A20→A23 distance shrank from always 4 steps to 1 step in almost all cases. [DIR001, LAG001]
- The group A31/A32/A37 moved after A21: earlier it always preceded A21 and A23; in the later window A21 precedes the group in about 47 in 100 traces while the group precedes A21 in about 1 in 200, the same flip holds against A23, and the group still precedes A26. The group's mean relative position moved from about 0.39 to about 0.58 of the trace, and its outgoing edges now lead to A26 (about 29–36 in 100) instead of to A23 (down from about 33–35 in 100 to about 1 in 100). [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, POS006, POS007, POS008, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- A22 and the group became alternatives: they co-occurred in every earlier trace but in only about 1 in 200 later traces; A21 now routes directly to A22 in about 54 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and to one of A31/A32/A37 in about 46 in 100 (earlier always to A22), so the A21→A26 gap is 2 steps in about 54 in 100 later traces and 4 steps in about 46 in 100 (earlier always 2 steps). [REL011, REL012, REL013, DIR002, LAG005]
- The internal order within A31/A32/A37 remained without a fixed order in both windows. [REL020, REL021, REL022]
- Smaller shifts accompany this: A23's mean relative position moved earlier (about 0.45 → 0.39) and A21's earlier (about 0.60 → 0.54), while A20 moved slightly later (about 0.34 → 0.36) and A26 slightly earlier (about 0.66 → 0.63). [POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change in which the three-activity group A31/A32/A37 was repositioned from between A20 and A23 to between A21 and A26, where it now alternates with A22: in the later window a case typically executes either A22 (about 54 in 100 cases) or the group (about 47 in 100), whereas earlier every case executed both, and the group's exit now leads to A26 rather than to A23. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

The hypothesis rests on direct relational and successor evidence rather than on frequency shifts alone: the group's order relative to A21 and A23 flips between the windows, A20's direct successor becomes A23 with the distance collapsing from 4 steps to 1, and the group's outgoing edges move from A23 to A26; participation, position and distance indicators all agree with this one picture. The control-flow screening flagged 47 qualifying items against roughly 0.5 expected without change, so the volume of findings cannot be explained by random re-sampling of the two windows. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR001, LAG001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG005, screening]

Limitations: the exclusivity between A22 and the group is not absolute (both appear together in about 1 in 200 later cases), the evidence cannot show whether the repositioning and the new optionality stem from one change or two concurrent ones, nor whether the choice between A22 and the group follows case characteristics rather than a plain branch; a few lag items (for example A20 with A31) rest on a single later observation and were not relied on. [REL011, REL012, REL013, LAG002]

Other differences: small shifts in the mean relative position of A20 (slightly later, about 0.34 → 0.36) and A26 (slightly earlier, about 0.66 → 0.63), most plausibly side effects of the restructuring rather than independent changes. [POS001, POS005]
