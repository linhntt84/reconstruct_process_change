### Observed differences

- Ten activities became near-universal. In the earlier window, A28, A29, A30, A32 and A33 each appeared in about 48 in 100 traces, and A34–A38 each in about 52 in 100; in the later window, every one of the ten appears in about 98–100 in 100 traces, exactly once per case (mean occurrences match participation, with no repeats in either window) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010].

- The two groups of five were mutually exclusive before and are jointly executed after. Earlier, no trace contained an activity from {A28, A29, A30, A32, A33} together with one from {A34–A38} (co-occurrence 0; one group alone in about 48 in 100 traces, the other alone in about 52); in the later window both groups run together in about 97–98 in 100 traces, always with the A28-group before the A34-group [REL025, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL033, REL040, REL046, REL051].

- A25 and A26 still bracket the block activities, but now around both blocks: A25 preceded each block activity in about 48–52 in 100 traces earlier and about 98–100 in 100 later, and each block activity preceded A26 with the same shift [REL001, REL006, REL011, REL016].

- Within each group the order remains mixed: for example, A28 precedes A29 in roughly half of the co-occurring cases in both windows, and pairs inside {A34–A38} also split about 50/50 in both windows [REL021, REL030, REL056, REL060].

- Event positions moved consistently with both blocks running in sequence: A25's mean relative position moved earlier (about 0.64 to 0.55) and A26's later (about 0.82 to 0.84); the A28-group moved earlier (about 0.72–0.74 to about 0.63) and the A34-group later (about 0.72 to about 0.76) [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS008, POS011].

- Routing followed the same pattern: A25's outgoing transitions, previously spread over all ten block activities, now go almost only to the A28-group (A34–A38 at about 0.5 in 100 or unobserved); the direct transitions from A28, A30 and A32 to A26, previously 17–28 in 100 of their observed outgoing transitions, have practically disappeared (about 0.5 in 100 or unobserved), replaced by small shares into the A34-group [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004].

- Overall, 98 control-flow items qualified against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process; the LAG items point the same way but rest on only 1–4 later-window observations, so they add little weight [LAG001, LAG002, LAG006, LAG007].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two blocks of five activities that were previously alternatives changed into blocks that are executed together: in the earlier window each case ran either A28, A29, A30, A32 and A33 (about 48 in 100 cases) or A34, A35, A36, A37 and A38 (about 52 in 100), while in the later window essentially every case runs both blocks in a fixed inter-block order — the A28-group first, then the A34-group — with A25 still preceding the blocks and A26 still following them, now later in the case [REL025, REL033, REL040, REL046, REL051, OCC001, OCC006, REL001, REL011, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS011].

Supporting signs: the change from "never in the same case" to "together in about 97–98 in 100 cases" is shown directly for every cross-group pair; participation of all ten activities rose to near-certainty with no change in repetition; the routing and position shifts agree with the same reading; and the volume of qualifying items (98 against about 1 expected without change) cannot be explained by chance as a whole, although most items restate one underlying difference rather than confirming it independently [REL025, REL029, OCC001, OCC010, DIR001, DIR004, POS003, POS012].

Limitations: the removed choice and the block structure are inferred from co-occurrence, routing and positions rather than observed as an explicit decision point; the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply draw different variant mixtures from an unchanged process, and the LAG detail rests on only 1–4 later-window observations [LAG001, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007].
