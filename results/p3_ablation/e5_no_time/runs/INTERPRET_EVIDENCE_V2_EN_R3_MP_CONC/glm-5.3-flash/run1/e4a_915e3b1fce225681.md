### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of five activities A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 now executes after A21 rather than between A20 and A22. In the earlier window the observed flow ran from A20 into the block, whose members could follow one another and exit to A22, after which A21 ran and always led to A25; in the later window A20 leads directly to A22, A21 follows, A21 now feeds into the block, and the block exits to A25. The block's internal successor relations are unchanged; its entry point moved from A20 to A21 and its exit from A22 to A25. This rests on direct and mutually consistent evidence: near-deterministic order reversals in all ten activity pairs with unchanged 100-in-100 co-occurrence, matching redirections at A20, A21 and all five block members, and position shifts that all point in the same direction. Screening tested 110 control-flow units and 34 items qualified, where fewer than one (0.55) would be expected by chance without a change, so the ensemble is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces.
