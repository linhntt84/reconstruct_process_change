### Observed differences

- **A five-activity group now repeats.** In the earlier window, each of A25, A26, A31, A32 and A33 occurred exactly once in every trace (participation 100 in 100, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, never repeated). In the later window, all five still appear in every trace but average about 3 occurrences per trace (3.06), and each repeats within the same trace in about 67 in 100 traces. All five show identical values, suggesting they repeat together as a block. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- **A return transition appears after A33.** Before, every one of the 200 observed outgoing transitions of A33 went to A20. In the later window, about 67 in 100 of A33's 612 observed outgoing transitions go to A25, while about 33 in 100 still go to A20 — roughly one exit per trace, consistent with the flow leaving the group after its final pass. [DIR001]
- **Ordering within the group loosened.** Before, A25 preceded A26, A31, A32 and A33 in every trace, A26, A31 and A32 followed one another in no fixed order, and every pair always co-occurred. In the later window, co-occurrence is still universal, but in about 67 in 100 traces each pair is interleaved or repeated rather than executed once in a fixed order; for example, A25 before A26 falls from all traces to about 34 in 100, and the previously mixed pairs (such as A26/A31, about 53 vs 47) now split roughly evenly around the interleaved majority. - **Gaps between consecutive group members widened.** Before, ordered pairs such as A26→A31 or A31→A32 lay one or two positions apart (about two-thirds at one step, one-third at two). - **Positions spread but did not shift.** The typical position of these activities is similar in both windows (for example, A33's median is about 0.50 in both), but their spread roughly triples (standard deviation about 0.05 to about 0.14) — the pattern expected when an activity occurs several times per trace rather than when it is moved. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the block formed by A25, A26, A31, A32 and A33 — previously executed exactly once per trace, with A25 first, then A26, A31 and A32 in no fixed order among themselves, then A33 — is repeated about three times per trace, with the flow returning from A33 to A25 between passes and about one pass per trace still exiting to A20. The repetition is shown directly by the occurrence counts (mean 1.0 to about 3.1 per trace; within-trace repeats in about two in three traces, from none), the return transition by the routing change at A33 (about two-thirds of its outgoing transitions now go to A25, a transition not observed before, when all 200 went to A20), and the ordering, spacing and position items all match what repeating an otherwise unchanged block produces: unchanged participation and co-occurrence, similar average positions with much larger spread, wider gaps, and interleaved-or-repeated pair orders. All 27 qualifying items belong to the control-flow family, against fewer than 1 expected by chance (0.44), and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, so this collection of differences is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001, REL001, REL005, LAG001, POS001, POS005]

Limitations: the relation evidence records "interleaved or repeated" as a single category, so it cannot show whether every pass runs strictly A33 → A25 or whether the five activities sometimes overlap or alternate; the routing shares are over outgoing transitions rather than cases, and the exit to A20 occurring only after the last pass is an inference from the roughly one-per-trace count, not an observed rule. These items also largely describe one underlying trace behaviour, so they reinforce rather than independently confirm each other. [DIR001, REL001, LAG001]
