### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the five-way alternative block between A38 and A23 was dropped from execution: in the earlier window A38 was always followed by exactly one of A14, A15, A20, A21 or A22 and then by A23, whereas in the later window A38 leads directly to A23 and the five activities are no longer observed. The change is shown directly by A38's successor profile switching entirely from the five activities to A23 and by the A38→A23 distance dropping from two positions to one in all traces, and it is consistent with all five activities disappearing from 200 later traces after appearing in 33–49 of 200 earlier traces; the screening result (44 qualifying items versus about 0.54 expected without change) rules out chance as an overall explanation.
