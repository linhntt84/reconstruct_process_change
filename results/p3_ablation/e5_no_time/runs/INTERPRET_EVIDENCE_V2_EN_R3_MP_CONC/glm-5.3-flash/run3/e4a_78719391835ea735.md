### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the single-choice segment A14/A16/A19 (one of these activities runs in roughly a third of cases) and the always-executed segment A15/A17/A18 have exchanged places around the fixed connectors A20 and A24: A35 now enters the A15/A17/A18 segment, A20 and A24 follow it, and the A14/A16/A19 segment now runs last and exits to A25. [REL008, REL009, REL015, REL016, REL018, REL019, DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG002]

The support is direct and consistent: all 21 cross-segment activity pairs flip a previously fixed order, including pairs that co-occur in every trace; the positional shifts (about 0.26–0.33 of trace length) agree with the successor rewiring at the segment boundaries and with the step-distance changes; and 39 items qualified in screening against fewer than 1 expected without change, so the set of differences is far beyond what a stable process typically produces. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL017, REL020, REL021, POS002, POS004, POS005, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a re-routing within one process from a wholesale switch to a different routing variant between the windows, and it does not show what determines the choice among A14, A16 and A19; no flagged item concerns pairs inside either segment, so their internal ordering is treated as unchanged rather than directly confirmed. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a change in the case population (a different variant becoming the only one observed) rather than a modification of the same process, although the single uniform order within each window makes a within-window mix of variants unlikely. [REL008, REL009, REL015, REL016, REL018, REL019]

Other differences: the occurrence shares of the single-choice activities moved slightly (A16 down from about 35 to 29 in 100 traces, A19 up from about 36 to 39, A14 up from about 30 to 33); these shifts are small, may reflect sampling variation, and are not part of the main hypothesis. [POS001, POS003, POS006]
