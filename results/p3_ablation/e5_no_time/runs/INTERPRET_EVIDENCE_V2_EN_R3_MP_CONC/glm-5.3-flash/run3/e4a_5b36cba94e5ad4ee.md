### Observed differences

- The clearest difference is how two groups of activities are sequenced. In the earlier window, cross-group pairs ran in both directions: A21 preceded A15 in about 77 of 100 traces (A15 first in about 23), A21 preceded A20 in about 98 of 100, and A23 preceded A20 in about 82 of 100, while pairs such as A15–A23 or A16–A25 were close to even. In the later window, all 22 tested cross-group pairs run one way only: A15, A16, A17, A19 and A20 always precede A21, A22, A23, A24 and A25 (100% of 200 traces). [REL001, REL005, REL009, REL018, REL020, REL021]
- Participation itself did not change: every one of these pairs still co-occurs in all 200 traces in both windows, so the difference is a re-sequencing of the same activities, not activities appearing or disappearing. [REL001, REL010, REL018]
- Successor profiles narrowed accordingly. Earlier, A15's outgoing transitions spread over eight different activities (most often A20 about 21, A17 about 16 and A16 about 15 in 100); in the later window they go only to A16 (about 36 in 100), A17 (about 32) and A20 (about 32), and A16 and A17 show the same narrowing. A20, which earlier sent about 51 of 100 outgoing transitions to A31 and only about 3 to A21, now always goes to A21; A22 now always goes to A31 (earlier about 50); A30 now always goes to A19 (earlier about 54 went to A21 and 46 to A19). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, DIR010]
- Relative positions shifted consistently: A20 now occurs at about 0.61 of the trace length on average, down from about 0.71, and A15 at about 0.55, down from about 0.61; A21 moved later, from about 0.53 to 0.63, and A25 from about 0.62 to 0.69. [POS001, POS005, POS006, POS010]
- Distances became short and constant. A15 is followed by A16 within 1–2 steps in every later trace (earlier the gap spread over 1–5 steps); A19 is followed by A21 exactly 5 steps later in all 200 later traces (earlier 1–4 steps, in about 92 traces); A20 is followed by A22 exactly 5 steps later (earlier 1–5 steps); A22→A31 and A30→A19 are now always immediate. Ordered early-then-late pairs within five steps that occurred in only about 28–46 of 200 earlier traces (for example A20 then A24 in 28, A17 then A21 in 45) now occur in all 200. [LAG001, LAG018, LAG026, LAG027, LAG029, LAG035, LAG051]
- Order inside each group remains flexible but tightly packed: A15, A16 and A17 still appear in both orders, now always within 1–2 steps of each other, and the same holds among A21, A23, A24 and A25 (gaps of 1–3 steps). [LAG008, LAG015, LAG032, LAG036, LAG044]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One hypothesis supported by the evidence is that the ordering constraints between two blocks of activities changed: in the earlier window the block A19, A15, A16, A17, A20 and the block A21, A23, A24, A25, A22 were interleaved in variable order, whereas in the later window the first block always completes before the second, consistent with a tight chain A30 → A19 → A15/A16/A17 (variable order) → A20 → A21 → A23/A24/A25/A22 (variable order) → A31, including fixed one-step links such as A30→A19, A20→A21 and A22→A31. [REL001, REL009, REL018, REL021, DIR004, DIR006, DIR010, LAG026, LAG035]

Supporting signs: the screening produced 93 qualifying items against about 0.72 expected if nothing had changed, so the ensemble is far beyond what a stable process typically yields; the order change is shown directly by relational evidence for all 22 tested cross-group pairs, each moving to 100% one-directional across 200 later traces, with every item's difference exceeding 99.5% of random re-splits; and three further indicator families corroborate it (narrowed successor sets, position shifts, collapsed distances). Because all activities still occur together in all traces in both windows, this is a change in ordering constraints rather than a replacement of one path by another or a participation change. [DIR001, POS005, POS006, LAG001, LAG018]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the later window samples a different mix of process variants — for example a previously rare strictly sequential variant — rather than a changed ordering rule applying to all cases; and the position and distance indicators use event positions within the trace, not clock time, so no conclusion about timing is implied. [REL001, REL018, POS001, LAG001]
