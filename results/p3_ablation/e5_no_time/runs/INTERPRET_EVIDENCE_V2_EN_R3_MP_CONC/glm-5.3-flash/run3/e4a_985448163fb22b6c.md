### Observed differences

- Before, each of the activities A25, A26, A31, A32 and A33 occurred exactly once in every trace (participation 100 in 100, mean 1.0 occurrences per trace, no repeats). In the later window each still appears in every trace, but averages about 3 occurrences per trace and recurs within the same trace in about 67 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- Before, the block ran in a largely fixed sequence: A25 always preceded A26, A31, A32 and A33, and A26, A31, A32 each always preceded A33 (only A26/A31, A26/A32 and A31/A32 had no fixed order, each split roughly 50/50). In the later window, in about 67 in 100 traces each of these pairs is interleaved or repeated rather than occurring once in a fixed order. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]
- A33's outgoing transitions changed: before, all of them went to A20; in the later window only about 33 in 100 go to A20, while about 67 in 100 go to A25 — a flow back into A25 that was not observed before (612 outgoing transitions after versus 200 before). [DIR001]
- Gaps between the occurrences of A26, A31 and A32 stretched: before, ordered pairs within five steps were almost always 1–2 positions apart (about two-thirds at 1 step); in the later window 1-step gaps fall to about 10–13 in 100 and gaps of 3–5 steps, previously unobserved, now appear. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]
- The relative positions of the five activities became much more variable (spread roughly 2.5 times larger, e.g. A25's standard deviation from about 0.05 to about 0.14) with slightly later mean positions — a pattern consistent with multiple passes rather than a relocation. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block "A25, then A26/A31/A32 in no fixed order, then A33" — previously executed exactly once per trace and closed by a transition to A20 — is now repeated within traces: about two-thirds of later-window traces contain repeated passes (about 3 occurrences per activity per trace on average), and A33 flows back into A25 in about two-thirds of its outgoing transitions, consistent with cyclic re-entry into the block. The repetition is shown directly by the occurrence counts and recurrence (0 to about 0.665) with participation unchanged at 100 in 100; all ten activity pairs moved from single-pass orderings to interleaved-or-repeated in about 67 in 100 traces; the new A33-to-A25 transition is direct relational evidence of re-entry; and the wider position and lag distributions match multiple passes. The volume of qualifying items (27 control-flow items against roughly 0.44 expected without change) also rules out chance as an explanation of the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

Limitations: the indicators show that the group repeats but cannot fully pin down the exact loop body or confirm that every pass contains all five activities; about one in three later-window traces shows no repetition at all. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window mixes two trace variants — one with the repeated block and one resembling the earlier window — rather than a single uniformly changed flow.
