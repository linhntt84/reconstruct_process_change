### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the alternative step between A36 and A21 — earlier one of A14, A15 or A20, chosen exclusively in each case — is no longer executed, so that A36 leads directly to A21 in every case. The change is shown directly by A36's successor profile switching from the three alternatives (about 35/30/35 in 100 outgoing transitions) to A21 alone, and by the A36-to-A21 distance shortening from two positions to one; it is consistent across occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators, with the three activities falling from about 30–35 in 100 cases to 0 of 200. Screening qualified 23 control-flow items where fewer than one (0.37) would be expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. A limitation: zero participation only means the activities were not observed in the later sample, so the evidence cannot distinguish a removal of A14, A15 and A20 from the step being skipped in all observed later cases [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, POS004, POS005].

Other differences: The shares of A19's outgoing transitions shifted toward A26 (about 41% to about 60%) at the expense of A16 (about 31% to about 16%) and A17 (about 28% to about 23%); all three successors remain observed with substantial support in both windows, so this reads as existing paths being taken with different frequencies rather than paths being replaced, and it is not part of the main hypothesis [DIR003].
