### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new step now runs between A22 and A24 in essentially every trace, performed as exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities — A14 (about 29 in 100 traces), A15 (about 41 in 100) or A20 (about 30 in 100) — placed directly after A22 (before A23) in roughly 56 in 100 traces and directly after A23 (before A24) in roughly 43 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The support is direct and internally consistent: relational and lag evidence shows the placement itself (each new activity is bracketed by A22 before it and A24 after it, and the A22→A24 distance grows by exactly one step), the routing shares of A22 and A23 split exactly along the two insertion points, and the three alternatives' participation shares sum to about 100 in 100 traces, matching one added step per trace. The volume of flagged items also far exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 28 control-flow items qualified where about 0.4 would be expected by chance across the 81 tested units. [REL003, REL005, LAG002, DIR004, DIR005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, screening]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed before, so it cannot distinguish a genuinely added step from activities that already existed but were never triggered in the earlier sample. Positions are event positions rather than clock times, so no claim about timing is made. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: none beyond the above; the small position shifts of A22 and A24 are consistent with the same added step. [POS004, POS005]
