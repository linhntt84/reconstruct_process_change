### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the sampled traces were uniform: A21, A22 and A23 each appeared exactly once in all 200 traces, A21 always came before A22 and A22 always before A23, and no other activity from this group appeared. [OCC001, REL001, REL005]
- In the later window, A22 appears in only about 102 of 200 cases (about 51%, down from 100%). When A22 is absent, A21 and A23 are still both present with A21 first — A22 is skipped rather than relocated. [OCC001, REL001, REL005]
- Three activities not observed at all earlier (A27, A28, A33: 0 of 200 traces each) each occur once in about 98 of 200 later cases (about 49%). They always occur together — every pair of them co-occurs in about 49% of traces — and they never share a trace with A22. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL006, REL007, REL008]
- Successor behaviour at A21 changed accordingly: before, all observed outgoing transitions of A21 went to A22; after, about half still go to A22 and the rest split nearly evenly to A28 (about 18%), A33 (about 17%) and A27 (about 15%). Each of the three new activities in turn leads onward to A23 (roughly 30% of A27's, 27% of A28's and 44% of A33's outgoing transitions). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- The three new activities have no fixed order between them: A27 precedes A28 in about 23 in 100 later cases and A28 precedes A27 in about 27 in 100, with the same both-ways pattern for the other two pairs. [REL012, REL013, REL014]
- The event-position gap between A21 and A23 was 2 positions in all earlier traces; in the later window it is 2 in about 51% of traces and 4 in about 49%, consistent with three activities sitting between them, and the new activities occur near the middle of the trace (median position about 0.48–0.50 of trace length). [LAG001, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- For scale: 26 control-flow items qualified against about 0.4 expected if nothing had changed (80 units tested), and the qualifying items all show differences larger than about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change between A21 and A23: in the later window, about half of the cases skip A22 and instead execute a block of three activities — A27, A28 and A33, each once and in varying order — between A21 and A23, while the other half retain the original A21 → A22 → A23 sequence. A22 fell from appearing in every case to about half of the cases; A27, A28 and A33 rose from unobserved to about half of the cases each. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL005, LAG001]

The hypothesis is direct and consistent across indicators: A21's successors shifted from only A22 to roughly 51% A22 and 49% spread over the trio; the trio leads back into A23; A22 and the trio are strictly mutually exclusive with complementary shares (about 51% vs 49%), which fits one alternative branch rather than two unrelated shifts; and the A21–A23 position gap widens from 2 to 4 exactly in the cases without A22. With 26 qualifying items against about 0.4 expected without change, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL012, LAG001]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window only means A27, A28 and A33 were not observed there, so the evidence cannot rule out that the trio existed at a very low rate before and was missed by the 200-trace sample. It also cannot show whether the trio's internal order is genuinely free or follows a few fixed variant orders, nor whether the skipping of A22 and the insertion of the block are governed by a single routing decision. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL012, REL013, REL014]
