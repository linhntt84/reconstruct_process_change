### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a swap between two alternative blocks of activities after A15. In the earlier window, each of A16–A20 appeared in about 85 in 100 traces, while each of A21–A25 appeared in about 16 in 100; in the later window this reversed, with A16–A20 at about 18 in 100 and A21–A25 at about 82 in 100. None of these activities repeats within a trace in either window. [OCC001, OCC005, OCC006, OCC010]
- The branch choice at A15 flipped directly: A15 occurs in every trace in both windows, and before, about 85 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions led to A19 (about 16 in 100 to A24), while in the later window about 82 in 100 lead to A24 (about 18 in 100 to A19). [DIR001]
- The relational evidence matches the swap: before, A15 was followed by each of A16–A20 in about 85 in 100 traces and by each of A21–A25 in only about 16 in 100; in the later window A15 is followed by each of A21–A25 in about 82 in 100 traces and by A16–A20 in about 18 in 100. [REL001, REL006]
- The two blocks act as alternatives in both windows — a trace with an A16–A20 activity virtually never contains an A21–A25 activity (co-occurrence is zero in both windows) — and within-block togetherness moved with the swap: pairs inside A16–A20 fell from co-occurring in about 85 in 100 traces to about 18 in 100 (now usually neither appears), while pairs inside A21–A25 rose from about 16 in 100 to about 82 in 100. [REL015, REL011, REL051]
- A29 runs in essentially every trace in both windows and changed partner block: before it co-occurred with A16–A20 in about 85 in 100 traces (about half after them, about a third interleaved); in the later window it co-occurs with A21–A25 in about 82 in 100 traces (about 47 in 100 with the block activity first, about 35 in 100 interleaved). [REL020, REL055]
- Smaller secondary differences: among A22's outgoing transitions, A21 fell from about 58 in 100 to about 31 in 100 while A23 and A25 rose to about 36 and 33 in 100; and within the now-rare A16–A20 block, the A18→A16 gap shifted from mostly one event (72 in 100) to mostly two (53 in 100) and A18→A17 from roughly even to mostly one event (82 in 100) — these spacing figures rest on only about 17 observations in the later window and concern event positions, not clock time. [DIR002, LAG001, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the exclusive choice between two existing branches after A15 reversed its frequencies: before, roughly 85 in 100 traces took the branch through A16–A20 (entered via A19) and roughly 16 in 100 took the branch through A21–A25 (entered via A24); in the later window about 82 in 100 take the A21–A25 branch and about 18 in 100 still take A16–A20. Supporting signs: the routing flip at A15 is observed directly; participation of all ten block activities moved in matching directions; the co-occurrence, exclusivity and ordering patterns are consistent throughout, including A29, which continues after either branch and now follows the A21–A25 block. Both branches remain in use in both windows, so this is a change in how often two existing alternative paths are taken, not a path appearing or disappearing. Screening tested 168 control-flow units and flagged 79 items where fewer than one would be expected by chance, and each item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits, so the set as a whole cannot be explained by sampling variation. The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows contain different mixes of case types reaching A15 (a population shift) rather than a changed branch rule, and the internal order within each block is only partly characterized. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC006, REL001, REL006, REL015, REL055]

Other differences: A22's outgoing transitions redistributed (A21 down from about 58 in 100 to about 31 in 100; A23 and A25 up to about 36 and 33 in 100, with only 31 before-window observations behind the earlier shares), and the event spacing between A18 and A16/A17 within the now-minority branch shifted by roughly one position; neither is part of the main hypothesis. [DIR002, LAG001, LAG002]
