### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A specific structural hypothesis is supported: in the earlier window each trace ran A43, then exactly one of five alternative activities (A14, A17, A18, A19, A20), then A28; in the later window each trace runs A43, then all five of A15, A16, A25, A26 and A27 in no fixed order, then A28 — that is, a one-out-of-five optional choice was replaced by a mandatory block of five different activities. [OCC001, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC002, OCC003, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, DIR001, DIR011, REL010, REL018, REL058]

The support is direct and consistent: the control-flow screening flagged 92 items where fewer than one (about 0.72) would qualify by chance in a stable process, so the volume of flagged behaviour cannot be explained by random re-splits of the two windows; the relational evidence shows the change directly (mutual exclusivity of the old five before, full co-occurrence with mixed order of the new five after, fixed placement of the block between A43 and A28), and the occurrence, successor and position indicators all describe this same single difference rather than unrelated changes. [REL003, REL029, REL010, REL018, REL019, DIR011, POS011, POS012]

Limitations: zero participation in a window only means an activity was not observed there, so the evidence cannot rule out that A15, A16, A25, A26 and A27 are relabelled counterparts of A14, A17, A18, A19 and A20 rather than genuinely new work; the position figures are event positions and show relative placement, not clock-time timing. [OCC001, OCC002, POS011, POS012]

Other differences: none beyond this restructuring and its direct correlates (the A43 and A28 position shifts). [POS011, POS012]
