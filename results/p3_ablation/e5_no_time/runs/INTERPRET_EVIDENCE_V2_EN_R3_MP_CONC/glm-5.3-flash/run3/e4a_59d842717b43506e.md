### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, none of the activities A14, A15, A20, A21 and A22 was observed at all (0 of 200 traces). In the later window each occurs in about 17–23 in 100 traces (A14 about 45, A15 about 42, A20 about 44, A21 about 33, A22 about 35 of 200), always at most once per trace and, by event position, around the midpoint of the trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

Their participations sum to about 995 in 1000, matching the share of later traces with an inserted activity between A24 and A26, so essentially every later trace contains exactly one of the five. - In the earlier window the backbone ran A24 → A25 → A26 directly: all of A24's outgoing transitions went to A25 and all of A25's to A26, A24–A25 and A25–A26 were adjacent in every trace, and A24–A26 were two steps apart. [DIR006, DIR007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, a new step is inserted into the previously fixed sequence A24 → A25 → A26 in about 995 in 1000 traces (versus none in the earlier window): it is executed exactly once per trace, as exactly one of the five alternative activities A14, A15, A20, A21 or A22 (about 17–23 in 100 traces each), and it is placed either between A24 and A25 or between A25 and A26 in roughly equal shares. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR006, DIR007, REL004, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

This is a change of structure (an added, essentially mandatory step), not a shift in how often existing paths are taken: the five activities were absent before, while A24, A25 and A26 still occur throughout both windows. The screening context argues against a sampling artefact — 34 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.44 expected without change — and the occurrence, routing, relational and step-distance evidence all describe the same insertion consistently, though they are computed from the same traces and so are not independent confirmations. [DIR006, DIR007, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the inserted step is genuinely new work or a re-labelling of behaviour that previously ran under activity names outside the tested units, nor can it distinguish five true alternatives from five labels for one inserted step, since the five never co-occur and share the same routing and placement. Mutual exclusivity was verified only for pairs among A14, A15, A20 and A21, not for A22, and the least frequent alternatives rest on 33–35 traces each. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL007, REL008, REL012, OCC004, OCC005]

Other differences: none — every qualifying item describes the insertion pattern above. [DIR006, DIR007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
