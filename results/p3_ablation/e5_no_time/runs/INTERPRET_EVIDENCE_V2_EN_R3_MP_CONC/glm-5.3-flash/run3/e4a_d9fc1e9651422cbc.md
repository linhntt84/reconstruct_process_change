### Observed differences

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the five activities A31, A32, A37, A38, A39 became an optional branch instead of a mandatory segment: A20 now leads almost always directly to A23, so the five are no longer executed between A20 and A23; when the five do run (about 55 in 100 traces, previously all), they start after A21 rather than before it and flow to A26 instead of A23. A22, previously executed in every trace after A21, now appears in about 46 in 100 traces and acts as an alternative to the five: a trace contains either A22 or the group, essentially never both. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL008, REL015, REL020, REL025]

The hypothesis is shown directly by the successor changes at A20, A21 and the five activities, and corroborated by the participation drops, the order reversals and the consistent position shifts; with 72 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected if nothing had changed, chance is implausible as an explanation of the overall pattern. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, OCC001, OCC002, REL015, REL020, POS004, POS006]

Limitations: the lag evidence rests on one after-window observation per pair, positions are event positions rather than clock times, and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows draw different mixes of case types rather than a modified process — although the near-disappearance of the old A20-to-group transitions in the later window argues against a simple mixture of old and new cases. [LAG001, POS001, DIR001]

Other differences: A20's mean event position moved slightly later (about 0.32 to 0.35) and A26's slightly earlier (about 0.67 to 0.65), while A23 and A26 remain present in every trace in both windows. [POS001, POS004, POS005]
