### Observed differences

- The same activities occur in every trace in both windows: A20, A21, A22, A25 and the five-activity group A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38 are each present in 200 of 200 traces before and after, and no pair is interleaved or repeated. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the segment consisting of A22 followed by A21 is executed before the five-activity block A30, A31, A36, A37 and A38, whereas in the earlier window it was executed after that block. Earlier, A20 fed directly into the group, the group led on to A22, A21 followed later and always led to A25; in the later window, A20 leads to A22, A21 follows about 0.13 of the trace later and feeds into the group, and the group now exits to A25. Because every activity keeps a 100% trace share and nothing is interleaved or repeated, this is a change in the placement and ordering of these activities, not a change in participation or in how often existing paths are taken. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007]

Supporting signs: the order reversal is shown directly and near-perfectly in all ten activity pairs (from 100% "group first" to about 99.5 in 100 "A21/A22 first"), the successor changes mark the new entry and exit points, and the position shifts and the collapse of five-step proximity point the same way; with 34 items against roughly 0.55 expected without change, chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [REL001, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG001, LAG006, screening]

Limitations: the flagged items largely describe one underlying reorder, so they are not independent confirmations; the relation between A22 and A21 themselves, A22's own successors and A25's new position are not directly observed, so the exact boundaries of the reordering — including what now runs between A22 and A21 — remain unconfirmed. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that both orderings existed as separate variants and only their mix shifted between the windows; the complete uniformity within each window makes sampling noise implausible, but behaviour outside the two windows is not observable.
