### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace ran A39 and then exactly one of three alternatives: A14 in about 35 of 100 traces, A15 in about 30 of 100, A16 in about 36 of 100. The three never occurred in the same trace, and whichever executed was always immediately followed by A24 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, REL001, REL002, REL008].
- In the later window, A14, A15 and A16 are not observed in any of the 200 traces, while A21, A22 and A23 each appear exactly once in every trace (100 in 100) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006].
- A39's outgoing transitions changed accordingly: before, they led only to A14, A15 and A16 (about 35/30/36 out of 100); after, only to A21, A22 and A23 (about 36/33/32 out of 100) [DIR007, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006].
- The three new activities always run together and in no fixed order: each pair co-occurs in all traces, with order splits near even (51/49, 51/49 and 52/48) [REL019, REL020, REL023].
- All three new activities sit between A39 and A24: A39 precedes each of them in 100 of 100 traces, and each precedes A24 in 100 of 100 traces [REL021, REL022, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027].
- The distance from A39 to A24 grew from 2 steps in essentially all earlier traces to 4 steps in all later traces, and A24's mean relative position moved slightly later (about 0.39 to 0.44) while its participation stayed at 100 in 100 [LAG001, POS007].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured: in the earlier window each case took exactly one of A14, A15 or A16 between A39 and A24, whereas in the later window A14, A15 and A16 no longer appear and A21, A22 and A23 each execute once per case, in no fixed order, between A39 and A24, so the segment grew from one activity to three [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, REL019, REL021, LAG001]. The screening produced 48 qualifying control-flow items where about 0.5 would be expected if nothing had changed, so chance alone cannot explain the pattern, and the change is shown directly and consistently by participation (0 in 100 versus 100 in 100), by the successor changes at A39 and the new activities, by the co-occurrence and order relations, and by the A39-to-A24 distance going from 2 to 4 steps [DIR007, DIR004, REL019, REL021, LAG001]. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that both behaviours pre-existed as separate variants and the case mix switched completely, since each window shows only one uniform pattern; also, zero participation strictly means only that A14, A15 and A16 were not observed in the later window [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].

Other differences: none beyond the slightly later position of A24 (mean relative position about 0.39 to 0.44), which follows from the two extra activities inserted before it rather than being an independent change [POS007, LAG001].
