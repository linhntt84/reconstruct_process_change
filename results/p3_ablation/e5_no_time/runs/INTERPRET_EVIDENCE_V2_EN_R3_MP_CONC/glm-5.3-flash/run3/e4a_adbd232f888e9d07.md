Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-way alternative block — exactly one of A14, A15 or A20 per case — was relocated from immediately after A40 to the late stage around A22 and A23, with A21 now executing directly after A40. In the earlier window the flow ran A40 → one of the alternatives → A21 → … → A22 → A23 → A27; in the later window it runs A40 → A21 → … → A22, and then either A23 → one of the alternatives → A27 or one of the alternatives → A23 → A27, each in roughly half the cases.
