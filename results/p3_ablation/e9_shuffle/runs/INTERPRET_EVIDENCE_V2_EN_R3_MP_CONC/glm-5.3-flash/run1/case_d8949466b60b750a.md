### Observed differences

- Before, A26 was followed by one of two routes: in about 52 in 100 traces A27 came directly after A26 and immediately before A28, while in about 48 in 100 traces the three activities A32, A33 and A38 ran between A26 and A28 in varying order; A27 never occurred together with any of the three [REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL012].
- In the later window, A27 participates in about 995 in 1000 traces (up from about 52 in 100) and occurs about once per trace, and A26 now leads to A27 in about 97 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, up from about 51 in 100; the transitions to A38, A33 and A32 (about 18, 16 and 14 in 100 before) are no longer observed [OCC001, DIR001].
- The branch activities nearly disappear: A32 falls from about 49 in 100 traces to 1 in 200, A33 from about 48 in 100 to 1 in 200, and A38 from about 48 in 100 to 1 in 200 [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004].
- Before, each of A32, A33 and A38 preceded A28 in roughly 48–49 in 100 traces and co-occurred with the other two in about half of the traces; in the later window A28 occurs without them in about 995 in 1000 traces, and pairs among the three are no longer observed in the same trace [REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014].
- The gap from A26 to A28 was 2 steps in about half of the earlier traces and 4 steps in about 47 in 100; in the later window it is 2 steps in about 97 in 100 traces, with A27 directly before A28 in essentially all cases [LAG001, LAG004].
- A28 sits slightly earlier within traces in the later window (mean relative position about 0.55 to about 0.53) [POS001].
- A27's rare other outgoing edges changed: A08 (about 2 in 100) and A10 (about 1 in 100) are no longer observed, while A09, A14 and A15 each appear at about 0.5 in 100 [DIR002].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the branch formed by A32, A33 and A38 between A26 and A28 is skipped in almost all cases, so that execution concentrates on the direct route A26 → A27 → A28: A27 rose from roughly half of traces to nearly all, while A32, A33 and A38 fell from roughly half of traces to a single trace each. The signs are direct and consistent across occurrence, relation, direction and lag indicators — 35 items qualified against roughly 0.6 expected without a change — though many of them describe this same underlying shift rather than independent confirmations [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, LAG001, REL001]. An important limitation: A32, A33 and A38 appear in only one later-window trace each, so their current behaviour cannot be characterized, and the evidence cannot rule out that the mix of cases entering the process changed (only one variant being sampled after the point) rather than the routing itself.

Other differences: A28's position within traces moved slightly earlier, consistent with the removed block [POS001]; A27's rare outgoing edges shifted, with A08 and A10 no longer observed and A09, A14 and A15 appearing at about 0.5 in 100 each [DIR002].
