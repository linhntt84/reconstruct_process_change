### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the flow was rerouted around the A36–A37–A42 block. Before, every case ran A25, then the three block activities in varying order exiting to A28, and later A26, A27 and A31. In the later window, A25 leads directly to A28, and the block runs after A26 and exits to A31, so cases take either A26 → A27 → A31 (about 53 in 100) or A26 → A36/A37/A42 → A31 (about 47 in 100); A27 and the block have become mutually exclusive alternatives, while A25, A26, A28 and A31 remain mandatory. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL011, REL012, REL013, LAG001, LAG005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: the successor profiles change exactly at A25, A26 and the block activities; relations move from always-together to almost-never-together for A27 versus each block activity; lags show A25→A28 contracting from about four positions to one and the A26→A31 distance splitting into two routes (2 and 4 positions); participation and position shifts match the relocation. With 50 qualifying items against 0.72 expected without change, chance cannot account for the evidence as a whole. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL011, REL012, REL013, LAG001, LAG005, POS004, POS006]

The evidence cannot rule out that the later window mixes two coexisting case variants rather than reflecting a single routing choice, since only aggregate shares are observed; the segment between A28 and A26 is not covered by any evidence item, so changes there would go unnoticed, and a few sparse lag profiles rest on a single observation on one side. [LAG002, LAG006, LAG007]

Other differences: minor ripples — A25's mean position moved slightly later (about 0.34 to 0.36) and A31's slightly earlier (about 0.65 to 0.63), A27 repeats in about 1 in 100 later traces, and a few rare transitions of about 1 in 100 appeared (for example A36 → A12 and A42 → A08).
