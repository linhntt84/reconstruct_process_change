### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new step, A01, was added to the flow and is executed once per case near the end: A08, which previously proceeded directly to A05, A02 or A06 (and often ended the case), now routes to A01 in about 82 in 100 cases, and A01 is in turn followed by A05 or A06 in most of its observed outgoing transitions. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR001, POS001]

The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation jumps from 0 to 100% of cases, routing changes on both sides of A01 (into it from A08, out of it to A05/A06), the relational profiles flip from "never together" to "always together" with a stable order (A08 before A01 in every case), the gaps from A08 to A05/A06 widen as expected when A01 sits between them, and the existing activities shift slightly earlier in relative position, fitting a longer trace. With 12 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.1 expected if nothing had changed, the ensemble is far beyond what random re-splits of stable traces produce, although the items partly describe the same underlying addition rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

Limitations: A01's outgoing profile rests on about 53 observed transitions, so the split between A05 and A06 as successors is indicative only; the positional findings concern event positions, not clock time. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that A01 existed before as a rare, unobserved path, since zero participation only means the activity was not observed in the earlier window — though 0 occurrences in 200 cases makes a regularly used prior path unlikely. [DIR001, OCC001]
