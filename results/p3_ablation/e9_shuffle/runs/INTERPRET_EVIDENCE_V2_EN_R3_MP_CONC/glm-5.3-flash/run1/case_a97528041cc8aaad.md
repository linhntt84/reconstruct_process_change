### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the ordering constraints in the segment between A40 and A30 changed: the group of A19, A21 and A24, which earlier ran after A25/A29 and immediately before A30, is now executed between A40 and A25, while the group of A20, A22 and A23, which earlier ran between A40 and A25, now runs between A29 and A30. All involved activities occur in substantial shares of traces in both windows (A20/A22/A23/A25/A29 in essentially all; A19/A21/A24 in about 28–44 of 100), so this is a reordering of existing behaviour rather than a replacement of paths. [DIR012, DIR011, POS002, POS004, POS007, LAG019]

The reversal is shown directly and consistently: order shares flip near-completely in pairs observed in all 200 traces of each window (e.g., A22 before A29 in 98 of 100 earlier co-occurrences, after it in 97 of 100 later ones), the successor changes at A40, A29, A19/A21/A24 and A20/A22/A23 point the same way, positions move in opposite directions for the two groups, and adjacencies are lost and gained exactly where the new order predicts. The screening qualified 87 control-flow items where about 1 would be expected without a change, so the evidence as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits of an unchanged process produce. [REL030, DIR012, DIR011, POS003, LAG018, LAG023]

Limitations: positions and lags refer to event indices, not clock time, so no statement about timing is implied; the evidence cannot distinguish one coordinated rearrangement of the two groups from two independent relocations, and it cannot show whether the internal order within each group is fixed, since members of both groups follow one another in varying sequences in both windows. An important competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that this reordering and the changes to A02, A06 and A10 are parts of a single broader restructuring; the evidence shows both but cannot decide their relationship. [DIR008, DIR009, POS008, OCC001]

Other differences: A02 became a frequent, early, fixedly placed step (about 15 to about 65 of 100 traces, typically after A14 and leading to A15) where it was previously rare and variably placed; A06 and A10 are executed in far fewer cases; A43 and A45 now lead almost exclusively to A44 and A46. These are reported separately and are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR004, LAG005, OCC002, OCC003, DIR013, DIR014]
