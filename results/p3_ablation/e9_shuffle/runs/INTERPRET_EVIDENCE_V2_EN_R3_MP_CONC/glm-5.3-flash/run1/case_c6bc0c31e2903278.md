### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural reorganisation around A07. In the earlier window every trace contained A07 with A03 and A10 always together in either order, and A01 followed A07 in about 32% of traces; in the later window A01 is no longer observed, A03 and A10 have become mutually exclusive alternatives that each follow A07 in about half the traces, and a new closing segment appears after A07 in about 48% of traces in which A08 and A12 alternate with each other, optionally reached through A09 inserted between A06 and A07 (A06 → A09 → A07 → A08 ↔ A12) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC006, REL012, REL003, REL018, OCC004, OCC007, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, REL021, REL015].

The hypothesis is shown directly rather than indirectly: participation counts capture the disappearance of A01 and the appearance of A08 and A09, DIR shows the new and removed edges, and REL, POS and LAG agree on the resulting order and placement; with 49 items qualifying against 0.32 expected without change, the volume of differences is far beyond what random re-splits of an unchanged process produce [OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, DIR005, POS003, LAG002].

Limitation: zero participation only means an activity was not observed in that window, so the evidence cannot rule out that a rare pre-existing variant without A01 and with the A08–A12 block was missed by the earlier 200-trace sample and simply became dominant; the qualified items also overlap heavily because many describe the same traces [OCC001, POS001, OCC004, POS003].

Other differences: A04's participation rose from about 37% to about 52% of traces, and A03 and A10 now lead to A04 far more often; A06's participation fell slightly (about 24% to about 18%) and A07's median position moved slightly later (0.4 to 0.5) [OCC003, DIR001, DIR005, REL002, POS002].
