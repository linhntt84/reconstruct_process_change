- Activity A01 disappeared: it appeared in about 48 in 100 traces in the earlier window (about once per trace, around the middle of the trace) and in none of the 200 later traces. [OCC001, POS001]
- Activity A08 appeared: it was not observed earlier, and in the later window it occurs in about 48 in 100 traces (about 1.1 times per trace), likewise around the middle of the trace. [OCC002, POS002]
- A08 took over A01's connections. Before, A01's outgoing transitions went to A09 (about 51 in 100), A10 (about 25) and A16 (about 24); in the later window A08's outgoing transitions go to A09 (about 56 in 100), A10 (about 25) and A16 (about 19). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 no longer occurs and that A08 has taken over its role. Fifty-three items qualified against fewer than one expected if nothing had changed, so chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole. The substitution is shown directly and consistently: A01 fell from about 48 in 100 traces to none while A08 rose from none to about 48 in 100; both occupy the same mid-trace relative position (about 0.55–0.57); A08 reproduces A01's outgoing links to A09, A10 and A16 in nearly the same shares, keeps the same exclusive predecessor A17, and preserves the co-occurrence with A03 at about 48 in 100 traces in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR005, DIR008, DIR003, REL003, REL001, REL011]

The evidence cannot distinguish a one-for-one replacement from A01 and A08 being mutually exclusive alternatives whose selection flipped completely from always-A01 to always-A08, since the two never occur in the same trace in either window; the concurrent repositioning of A11 and A15 also cannot be separated from this substitution as part of one larger restructuring. [REL003]

Other differences: A11 and A15 effectively swapped their late-process placement — A11 moved from a fixed final position (about 52 in 100 traces) to a variable mid-trace position (about 29 in 100) now followed by A03, while A15 moved from a variable mid-trace position (about 32 in 100 traces) to a fixed final position (about 52 in 100) with no observed outgoing transitions; A05 now leads to A15 instead of A11, and the step distances from A18 flipped accordingly. Some of the lag support behind this swap is based on moderate observation counts (roughly 30–80 ordered-pair observations per window), so the exact step distances are less firm than the participation and routing changes.
