### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The 19 qualifying items (all control-flow) far exceed the roughly 0.18 expected for an unchanged process, so the set as a whole cannot be explained by chance sampling. The evidence directly and consistently supports one hypothesis: in the later window a new activity, A01, was threaded into the flow between A04/A09 and A08. A01, unobserved in the earlier window, now runs once per trace at a late position; A04 and A09, which previously led mainly to A08, now lead mainly to A01; A01 always precedes A08 and leads to it in about 72% of its outgoing transitions; and the step distance from A04/A09 to A08 grew by about one step, as expected if one activity now sits between them. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL004, LAG003, LAG007]

Limitations: the placement of A01 relative to A06 and A07 is not fixed — those activities usually precede A01 but occasionally follow it — and the widened gaps among A04/A09, A06/A07 and A08 are consistent with the same insertion but do not pin down a single insertion point. Zero participation in the earlier window strictly means only that A01 was not observed there; an extremely rare pre-existing A01 missed by the 200 earlier traces cannot be fully excluded, though 0 occurrences in 200 traces makes that unlikely.
