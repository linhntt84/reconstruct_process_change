### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, cases split into two mutually exclusive routes after A21: about 52 in 100 traces ran A21 → A22 → A23, while the remaining about 48–49 in 100 inserted a block of three activities, A27, A28 and A33, between A21 and A23; A22 never appeared in the same trace as A27, A28 or A33. [REL001, REL005, REL006, REL012, LAG001]
- In the later window, the block activities have practically disappeared: A27, A28 and A33 each occur in only 1 of 200 traces (about 0.5 in 100), down from 97 of 200 (about 48–49 in 100) each. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A22 became near-mandatory: it appears in 199 of 200 later traces (about 99.5 in 100), up from 103 of 200 (about 52 in 100). [OCC001]
- Routing out of A21 concentrated accordingly: before, about 52% of A21's observed outgoing transitions led to A22, with the rest leading to A28 (about 17%), A33 (about 18%) and A27 (about 14%); in the later window, about 99.5% lead to A22. [DIR001]
- The surviving route is a tight chain: A22 precedes A23 in about 99.5 in 100 later traces (about 52 in 100 before), and the event distance from A21 to A23 is 2 steps in about 99.5 in 100 later traces, whereas before it split between 2 steps (about 52 in 100) and 4 steps (about 48 in 100, the block route). [REL005, LAG001]
- All ties of the block to the rest of the process collapsed: A21 preceding A27, A28 or A33, and each of them preceding A23, fell from about 48–49 in 100 traces to about 0.5 in 100; the block pairs A27–A28, A27–A33 and A28–A33 fell from co-occurring in about 48–49 in 100 traces (in both orders, with no fixed internal sequence) to about 0.5 in 100. The later outgoing profiles of A27, A28 and A33 rest on a single trace each and are not informative. [REL002, REL004, REL009, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- A21 and A23 themselves occur in all traces in both windows; A23's mean relative position moved only slightly earlier, from about 0.55 to about 0.53 of the trace. [POS001, DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the alternative branch formed by A27, A28 and A33 between A21 and A23 is no longer executed: cases that previously ran A21 → (A27/A28/A33 in varying order) → A23 no longer run this block, and A22 changed from an optional choice (about 52 in 100 traces) to near-mandatory (about 99.5 in 100), so that nearly every case now runs A21 → A22 → A23. This is shown directly by participation (each block activity falling from 97 to 1 of 200 traces while A22 rises from 103 to 199), by A21's outgoing share to A22 (about 52% to about 99.5%), and by the A21→A23 distance fixing at 2 steps. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, LAG001]

Chance is an unlikely explanation for the pattern as a whole: screening flagged 34 control-flow items where only about 0.45 would be expected if nothing had changed, every item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits, and the occurrence, relation, direction and lag indicators all describe the same shift. [screening, DIR001, REL005, LAG001, OCC002]

Limitations: the evidence shows that the block is not executed, not whether it was removed from the process or has become a rarely taken option — one later trace still contains A27, A28 and A33 — and the many qualifying items largely restate one underlying shift rather than independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows draw on different populations or variants; pure sampling fluctuation is nonetheless implausible given the drop from about 48–49 in 100 to about 0.5 in 100 traces. [OCC002, REL012, DIR002]

Other differences: none independent of the main change; the only additional signal, a slight advance of A23's relative position (mean about 0.55 to about 0.53), is consistent with the block no longer preceding it. [POS001]
