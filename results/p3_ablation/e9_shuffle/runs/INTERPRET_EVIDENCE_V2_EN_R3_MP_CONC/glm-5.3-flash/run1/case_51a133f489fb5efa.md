- In the later window, A08 appears in about 30 in 100 traces, up from about 13 in 100 earlier (roughly 26 to 59 of 200 traces), and its mean occurrences per trace roughly doubled from 0.14 to 0.30. [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the flow around A08 and the A19/A20/A25 group was rerouted: in the later window, A08 takes part in about twice as many traces and now feeds mainly into A19, A20 and A25, which in turn lead directly on to A26, whereas earlier A08 branched to A16, A26, A15 and A49, and the group ran later in the trace towards A28 and A32 with A26 or A27 typically preceding it. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL005, REL009, POS002, POS004]

This is a rerouting and reordering rather than a shift in how often existing paths are taken: A19, A20 and A25 had no observed transition to A26 earlier and A08's successor set was almost entirely replaced, and the relational evidence (successor changes, order flips, lags) shows the change directly. The 40 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected without change also cannot be explained by chance as a whole. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL005, LAG007]

Limitations: the earlier-window placement of some new steps rests on very few observations (the A08→A19 and A19→A26 lags had one supporting pair before), so the precise previous arrangement is weakly estimated, and the exact split after A08 (about 38/29/21) carries sampling uncertainty in 200-trace windows. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the A08→A19→A26 sequence existed before as a rare path that merely became more frequent rather than being newly created; single before-window observations cannot distinguish these. Several indicators here describe the same underlying rerouting, so their agreement is consistency, not independent confirmation. [LAG001, LAG007, DIR001]

Other differences: A27, A28 and A32 now form a near-fixed chain (A27→A28→A32 at lags 1 and 2) after losing their branches to A19/A20/A25; A45 now feeds A08 and the group instead of leading straight to A26; A26 occurs slightly later after A16, A18 and A45, and A26 and A27 sit slightly later in the trace; A18→A45, A48→A49 and A50→A51 were essentially stable.
