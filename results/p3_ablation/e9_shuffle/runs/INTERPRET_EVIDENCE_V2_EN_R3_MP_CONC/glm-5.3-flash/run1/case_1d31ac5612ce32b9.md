### Observed differences

- A02 ran exactly once in every before-window trace; in the later window it still appears in every trace but averages about 2.1 occurrences per trace and repeats within the same trace in about half of the traces (about 50 in 100, versus none before). [OCC001]
- A04 appeared in every before-window trace; in the later window it appears in only about half of the traces (about 50 in 100). [POS002, REL002, REL003]
- Before, A02's outgoing transitions went exclusively to A04; in the later window they split roughly 53% to A04 and 47% to A03 (over about 422 observed transitions). [DIR001]
- A04's outgoing transitions went exclusively to A03 before; in the later window they go exclusively back to A02 (about 222 observed transitions). [DIR002]
- Before, A02, A04 and A03 always ran together in the fixed order A02 → A04 → A03. In the later window, A02 and A04 co-occur in about half the traces and are interleaved or repeated whenever they co-occur; A04 runs before A03 in about 21% of traces, with about 28% interleaved; A02 and A03 still always co-occur, but about half the traces show them interleaved or repeated instead of a fixed order. [REL001, REL002, REL003]
- Step separations were constant before (A02→A04 and A04→A03 always 1 step, A02→A03 always 2 steps); in the later window they vary (A02→A03: 1 step in about 40% of traces, 3 steps in about 11%, 5 steps in about 5%; A04→A03: 2 steps in about 46%, 4 steps in about 18%; A02→A04: 1 step in only about 18%, with 3- and 5-step separations appearing). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- A02's event position moved from a constant 0.5 to a median of about 0.67 with new spread, while A04's median position moved from 0.75 to about 0.6 alongside its halved trace support. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the fixed block A02 → A04 → A03 no longer runs as a unit: A04 has become optional — skipped in about half the traces — and when it runs it returns to A02 instead of proceeding to A03, so that A02 and A04 alternate repeatedly before A03 closes the trace. A02 remains in every trace but now recurs (about 2.1 occurrences per trace, repeating in about the same share of traces that contain A04, roughly 50 in 100 each), and in the remaining traces A02 proceeds directly to A03, which is still present throughout. [DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, POS002, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The hypothesis is supported directly and consistently: the successor change at A04 (A03 before, A02 after) and the routing split at A02 are observed outright rather than inferred from positions, and the participation, repetition, relational, lag and positional items all describe one coherent picture; 11 qualifying items against about 0.06 expected for an unchanged process is far beyond what chance would produce. [DIR002, DIR001, OCC001, REL002, LAG003]

Several items view the same traces from different angles — the A02–A04 relationship underlies REL002, LAG002 and POS002 — so they are agreeing descriptions of one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, and each window is a finite sample of 200 traces. [REL002, LAG002, POS002]

Competing explanation: the evidence cannot distinguish a single process with a choice between a direct A02→A03 path and an A02–A04 cycle from a later window that mixes two coexisting behaviour patterns; it also shows nothing about activities outside A02, A03 and A04. [REL002, REL003, DIR001]
