### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative block of three activities A19, A20 and A25 was relocated from the segment between A45 and A26 to the segment after A27, around A28 and before A32: before, A45 branched to one of A19/A20/A25, which each led on to A26 (continuing A26→A27→A28→A32); in the later window, A45 connects straight to A26 and the three activities run after A27/A28 and lead to A32. This is shown directly and consistently: the relation indicators record the order reversal, the successor profiles of A45, A19, A20, A25, A27 and A28 changed exactly as the hypothesis predicts, the lag distributions show the old adjacencies vanishing and new ones appearing, and the position shifts corroborate the relocation; with 31 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected without change (0.62), sampling noise cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [DIR006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, LAG001, LAG010, LAG011, LAG002, LAG006, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Limitations: the exact internal routing in the later window is not pinned down — whether the block is mandatory or optional, and where it sits relative to A28 (it appears both before and after A28 in different traces) — and edges outside the tested units, such as A26's successors, are unobserved. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the three activities now run in parallel or interleaved with the A27–A28–A32 segment rather than at one fixed place, which would also produce the observed mixed orders and stretched distances. [REL005, REL008, LAG005, LAG009]

Other differences: A24 occurs more often and in more traces in the later window (participation about 43 in 100 traces before, 58 in 100 after; mean occurrences per trace 0.43→0.58); this increase is not part of the relocation hypothesis above. [OCC001]
