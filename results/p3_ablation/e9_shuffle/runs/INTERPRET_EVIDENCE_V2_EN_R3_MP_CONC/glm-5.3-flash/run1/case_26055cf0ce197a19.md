### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the cyclic sequence among A01, A05 and A07 was reordered: earlier the loop ran A01 → A07 → A05 → back to A01, while in the later window it runs A01 → A05 → A07 → back to A01 — A05 and A07 exchanged places in the cycle. Along with this, A07 is executed in fewer traces (about 50% → 29%) and later in each trace, A01 in more traces (about 28% → 53%) and more often repeated, and A06 moved from the end of the loop (after A07, passing to A03/A04) to the middle, reached from A01 and passing back into A01/A05. The reordering is shown directly by successor and immediate-succession changes that match in both directions (old direct edges vanish, new ones appear), corroborated by the relation profiles (A05 now precedes A07; A06 now precedes A07 where A07 previously preceded A06) and by the position and participation shifts; with 35 qualifying items against about 0.29 expected without change, chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole. [DIR001, DIR004, DIR006, LAG001, LAG009, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013, REL003, REL008, REL009, POS003, POS004, OCC001, OCC002]

Limitations: all items come from a single control-flow family and several describe overlapping aspects of the same rewiring, so they are not independent confirmations; the evidence cannot distinguish whether A07 simply became optional in more traces or whether the loop is traversed differently, since its participation, position and edges moved together; and a few profiles rest on few observations (for example A06's earlier outgoing transitions, about 11). [REL001, REL002, REL004, DIR005, OCC001, OCC002]

Other differences: A03 and A04 now feed into A01 and A06 instead of A07, and their distances changed — A03→A01 and A04→A01 became immediate (lag-1 shares about 25 and 23 in 100, from near zero), while A03→A07 and A04→A07 moved to lags of 3–5 steps. [DIR002, DIR003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG005, LAG008]
