### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a one-of-three choice among A19, A20 and A25 runs between A41 and A26, where earlier A26 followed A41 directly. The successor change is observed directly at the source: A26 falls from about 82% to about 1.5% of A41's observed outgoing transitions while A19, A25 and A20 take about 87% together, and A19 and A25 in turn lead to A26 in about 95% and 90% of their outgoing transitions. The relational and distance evidence agrees — A41 precedes each of the three and each of the three precedes A26 in about 28–37.5% of traces, the A41→A26 pair shifts from one to two steps apart, and participation rises from not observed to about 30–38% per activity with the three co-occurring in only about 0.5–1.5% of traces. Screening qualified 21 control-flow items against about 0.52 expected without change, so the ensemble is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [DIR004, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL001, REL002, REL005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Limitations: all items come from the single control-flow family and several describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations; the 200-trace windows cannot exclude that the three activities occurred at very low rates earlier and only became frequent (A20 shows in one earlier trace), and the small pairwise overlaps leave open whether the choice is strictly one-of-three. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that this block is a previously extremely rare path that became frequent rather than a newly added one. [OCC002]

Other differences: A02's successors moved away from A26 (about 26% of its observed outgoing transitions earlier, none later), with A16 and many small new successors taking that share; this change at A02 is not explained by the main hypothesis. [DIR001]
