### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the process executes two activities that it did not execute before: A01 once per case in the middle — typically after A03 or A04 and before A06 — and A05 once per case at the end, after A06. Earlier, cases ran from A03/A04 onward to A06 (or directly to A02) without these two activities; in the later window the dominant chain is A03/A04 → A01 → A06 → A05. [OCC001, OCC002, REL003, REL004, REL007, DIR001, DIR005]

Supporting signs: participation went from zero to all 200 cases for both activities; each holds fixed-order relations with all major existing activities; A01 and A05 gained outgoing transitions, and A06 — which earlier often ended the case — now leads to A05 in about 84 in 100 of its transitions; and the step distance between A03/A04 and A06 grew by roughly one step, consistent with A01 sitting between them. The screening produced 21 qualifying items where about 0.13 would be expected if nothing had changed, so sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, REL002, REL005, REL006, DIR003, DIR005, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: the items are not independent — most reflect the same two additions rather than separate differences — and the evidence cannot rule out that A01 and A05 were simply not recorded in the earlier window rather than absent from execution. A05's outgoing profile rests on only about 29 cases, so its successors remain uncertain, and A01's placement varies (A04 precedes A01 in only about 69 in 100 cases), so its insertion point is not entirely fixed. [DIR004, REL002]

Other differences: the earlier positions of A04 and A06 and the longer A03–A04 gaps noted above are consistent with consequences of the same additions rather than separate behavioural changes. [POS002, POS004, LAG001]
