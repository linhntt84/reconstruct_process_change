### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A01 was inserted as a new step between A08 and A10. Earlier, the roughly 21 in 100 cases containing A08 and A10 ran directly from A08 to A10; in the later window, A01 appears in exactly that share of traces, is preceded by A08 (about 55 in 100 of A08's outgoing transitions now go to A01, the share that previously went to A10) and is always followed by A10, while the A08–A10 distance grew from mostly one step to mostly two steps. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR003, REL001, REL002, LAG001]

Nine control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.13 would be expected by chance in an unchanged process, and all nine describe this one coherent difference across occurrence, position, direction, relation and distance indicators, so the pattern as a whole is not what random sampling of a rare variant typically produces. [POS001, DIR001, OCC001, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, LAG001, DIR002, REL002]

Limitations: the change touches only about one fifth of the traces, and the indicators use event positions rather than clock time. The evidence cannot fully rule out that A01 is a new parallel branch merging at A10 rather than a strict insertion on the former A08→A10 edge, although the complete disappearance of direct A08→A10 transitions and the one-step increase in their distance favour insertion. [DIR001, DIR003, LAG001]

Other differences: the earlier back-and-forth between A03 and A12 (A03→A12 in about a third of A03's transitions, A12→A03 always) is no longer observed; both A03 and A12 now lead only to A13, and A03 occurs less often. This rests on few observations (only 16–17 transitions behind the A12 change) and is not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR002, DIR004]
