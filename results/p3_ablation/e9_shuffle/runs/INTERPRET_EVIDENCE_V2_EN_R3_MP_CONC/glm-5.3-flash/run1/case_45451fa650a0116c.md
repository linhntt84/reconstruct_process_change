### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a flip between two alternative three-activity blocks. In the earlier window, A24, A25 and A26 each ran in about 80 in 100 traces — typically all three together, in no fixed order — while A21, A22 and A23 each ran in only about 20 in 100 (also together when they ran). In the later window this is reversed: A21, A22 and A23 each run in about 79 in 100 traces as a block, while A24, A25 and A26 each appear in about 21 in 100. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL017, REL018, REL025, REL043]

- The two blocks behave as alternatives occupying the same slot. Cross-block pairs are nearly mutually exclusive in both windows (earlier, about 80 in 100 traces had A24 without A21/A23; later, about 79 in 100 have A21/A23 without A24). A20 preceded the old block in about 81 in 100 traces earlier and now precedes the new block in about 78 in 100, with its outgoing transitions shifting from roughly 82% towards A24–A26 to roughly 78% towards A21–A23. Downstream, A30, A34 and A35 earlier followed A24–A26 (co-occurring in about 81 in 100 traces, with A24–A26 first in about 80 in 100), and now follow A21–A23 (co-occurring in about 79 in 100, with A21–A23 before A34/A35 in about 78 in 100). A26 keeps its relative position (median about 0.65 in both windows) while its participation drops, consistent with an in-place swap rather than a move. [REL032, REL019, REL014, REL011, DIR005, REL023, REL036, REL041, REL048, POS002]

- A06 lost its late-trace slot: it ran in about 46 in 100 traces earlier and in about 12 in 100 later. A34's observed outgoing transitions flipped from roughly 44% to A06 and 56% to A35, to about 90% directly to A35, and A34→A35 became immediately consecutive in about 92 in 100 of those pairs versus about 56 in 100 earlier. The few remaining A06 events sit earlier in the trace (median relative position about 0.45 versus about 0.95) and are no longer preceded by A34, though this rests on only about 24 events. [OCC003, DIR006, LAG006, POS001, REL009]

- Minor shifts: A02 and A04 occur somewhat more often (about 8→17 and 5→19 in 100 traces), with small new outgoing edges from A14 and A19 towards them; A08's outgoing mix also changed, but on very few observations (9 versus 23 transitions). [OCC001, OCC002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the choice between two existing alternative blocks flipped: the segment after A20 and before A30/A34/A35, earlier filled by the A24–A26 block in about 80 in 100 cases, is now filled by the A21–A23 block in about 79 in 100 cases, with both blocks intact, near-mutually exclusive, and every involved activity observed in a substantial share of traces in both windows (about 19–82 in 100). This is shown directly by the participation flips, the mutual-exclusivity and successor changes, and the volume of qualifying evidence — 73 items against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows simply contain different mixtures of the same two coexisting process variants (for example through differing case mix), which would look identical on these indicators. [OCC004, OCC007, REL032, REL011, DIR005, REL023, REL036, REL041]

Other differences: A06, which earlier was often inserted between A34 and A35 (about 44% of A34's outgoing transitions, at a late relative position of about 0.95), is now observed in only about 12 in 100 traces, A34 leads directly and immediately to A35 in about 90 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, and the sparse remaining A06 events sit earlier by event position — consistent with the late A06 step being largely skipped, though its new placement is based on about 24 events. Participation in A02 and A04 also rose modestly (to about 17 and 19 in 100 traces). [OCC003, DIR006, LAG006, POS001, REL009, OCC001, OCC002]
