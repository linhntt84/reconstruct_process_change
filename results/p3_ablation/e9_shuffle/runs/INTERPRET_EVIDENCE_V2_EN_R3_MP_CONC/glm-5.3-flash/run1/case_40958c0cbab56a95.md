### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural replacement of the segment between A44 and A29: in the earlier window every trace ran A44 → A26 → A27 → A28 → A29, while in the later window A26, A27 and A28 are observed in only about 2 in 100 traces and exactly one of A19, A20, A21 — mutually exclusive alternatives split roughly 30/33/38 — runs between A44 and A29, preceded by A44 and followed by A29. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL013, REL014, REL021, DIR007, REL026, REL025, LAG009]

Supporting signs: 81 control-flow items qualified, about 90 times the fewer than one expected by chance, so the ensemble cannot be explained by random sampling; the participation flips are near-total and consistent across occurrence, direction, relation and position indicators; A44's outgoing transitions moved from A26/A27/A28 to A19/A20/A21; the new activities occupy the same trace region the old block did (means about 0.36–0.38 vs 0.37–0.41 of the trace length); and the typical A44-to-A29 distance contracted from 4–5 steps to 2–3 steps, consistent with three consecutive activities being replaced by one. [DIR007, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG009]

Limitations: only 3 later traces contain A26/A27/A28, so whether these activities became rare alternatives or are no longer executed at all cannot be determined — zero participation only means they were not observed; A19/A20/A21 already appeared in 1–2 in 100 earlier traces, so they are not demonstrably new; several qualifying lag and position items rest on only 1–3 later traces and were not relied upon; and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows capture different mixtures of process variants (for example a variant without this segment dominating the later window) rather than a modification of a single process. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
