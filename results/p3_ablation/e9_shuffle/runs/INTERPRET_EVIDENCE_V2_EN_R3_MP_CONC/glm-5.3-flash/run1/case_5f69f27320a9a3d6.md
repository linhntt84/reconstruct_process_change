### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A02 and A07 was restructured: A01, unobserved in the earlier window, is executed in every later trace after A02 and before A03, interleaved with the existing A05/A06 block, while A04, executed in every earlier trace, is no longer observed, and A03 now leads directly to A07 instead of through A04.
