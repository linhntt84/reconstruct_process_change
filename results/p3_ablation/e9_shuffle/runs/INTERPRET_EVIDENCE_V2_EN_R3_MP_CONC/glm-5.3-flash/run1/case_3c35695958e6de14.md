### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was not observed in any of the 200 earlier traces; in the later window it appears in every trace, about 2.1 times per trace on average, repeats within a trace in about 53% of cases, and sits in the earlier half of the case (median relative position ~0.25). [OCC001, POS001]
- In the earlier window A02 led to A03 (~48%) or A12 (~52%) and A09 led to A12 (~55%) or A03 (~45%); in the later window both lead exclusively to A01, and A01 in turn leads to A12 (~52%) or A03 (~48%). [DIR002, DIR004, DIR001]
- The distance from A02/A09 to A03/A12 lengthened by about one step: pairs that mostly sat 1–2 steps apart earlier (e.g., A02→A03 ~62% within 2 steps) now sit entirely at 2–3 steps; pairs among A02, A09 and A08 that were 4 steps apart are now 5 steps apart. [LAG001, LAG004, LAG011, LAG014, LAG003, LAG002, LAG012]
- In the later window A01 co-occurs with A02, A03 and A12 in all traces; A02 precedes A01 in about 47% of traces, and A01 precedes A03 and A12 each in about 47%, with the remainder interleaved or repeated. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- A03 and A12 occur slightly later within the case (A03 mean relative position ~0.50 → ~0.55; A12 ~0.49 → ~0.54). [POS002, POS004]
- Other behaviour involving A13 and A08 also differs: A13 appears in ~38% of traces earlier but only ~17% later; earlier A13 led to A12 (~54%) or A03 (~46%), later only to A08, and A08 now leads to A01 (~51%) alongside A13 (~49%), where earlier it led only to A13. [REL006, DIR005, DIR003]
- The fixed A08-before-A13 order (~29% of traces) disappeared in favour of interleaved/repeated co-occurrence, immediate A08→A13 succession fell from ~75% to ~16%, and A13→A03/A12 distances lengthened from ~1–2 to ~3–4 steps; repeats of A03, A12 and A09 now sit about 5 steps apart rather than 3–4, and A08 repeats within five steps are seen in ~35 later traces versus 5 earlier (small numbers). [REL009, LAG010, LAG016, LAG018, LAG005, LAG015, LAG013, LAG008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added and inserted between A02/A09 and A03/A12: in the earlier window A02 and A09 led directly to A03 or A12 and A01 never occurred, while in the later window A02 and A09 lead exclusively to A01, A01 runs in every case (about twice per trace, repeated in about half) and leads on to A03 or A12, whose distance from A02/A09 grew by about one step and whose positions shifted slightly later. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR001, LAG001, LAG004, POS002]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor sets of A02, A09 and A01 were rewired exactly at the point where A01 sits, the participation change is absolute (0 of 200 versus 200 of 200 traces, far beyond what sampling variation in two 200-trace windows produces), and the order, lag and position indicators agree with one another. Screening also produced 39 qualifying items against roughly 0.31 expected for an unchanged process, so the findings as a whole cannot be explained by chance. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR001, OCC001, LAG001, LAG004]

Limitations: the items share the same traces and are not independent confirmations, and the ~53% interleaved-or-repeated shares leave the exact placement of A01's repeated occurrences unresolved — the evidence cannot show whether A01 acts as one sequential step or occurs at several points around A02, A03 and A12. [REL001, REL002, REL005, OCC001]

Other differences: A13 occurs in far fewer cases (~38% → ~17% of traces) and is rerouted through A08 (A13 now leads only to A08, and A08 leads to A01 about half the time), with the fixed A08-before-A13 order replaced by interleaved/repeated co-occurrence; whether this is part of the same restructuring or an independent change cannot be decided from the evidence. [REL006, DIR005, DIR003, REL009]
