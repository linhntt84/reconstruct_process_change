### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new activity A01 was inserted into the control flow between the A03/A10 block and the downstream activities A04 and A07. A01, unobserved in the earlier window, now runs once in about 60 in 100 traces; it is reached from A10 and A03 through new transitions and hands control back to A03, A10, A04 and A07 in roughly equal shares, always preceding A04 and A07 when they follow while alternating order with A03 and A10. Its insertion explains the wider gaps: A03/A10-to-A04/A07 distances shifted from 1–2 steps to often 3, A03 and A10 are sometimes separated by one intervening step, and A04, A07 and A10 occur at later sequence positions, with no activity's participation dropping. Seventeen control-flow items qualified in screening against about 0.19 expected if nothing had changed, so chance cannot account for the set as a whole. The signs are direct (participation, new transitions, co-occurrence and order) and mutually consistent, but several indicators describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations. The evidence cannot rule out that A01 is a re-labelled existing activity rather than a genuinely new one, and because positions and lags are measured in event positions, "later" means later in the sequence, not later in clock time.
