### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the activities A26, A17, A15, A18, A19, A21, A20 and A27 formed one rigid sequence (A26 → A17 → A15 → A18 → A19 → A21 → A20 → A27): each appeared in essentially all 200 traces, each had exactly one observed successor, every pair occurred in a single fixed order, and the step distance between any pair was constant (for example A15 to A20 always 4 steps, A19 to A21 always adjacent). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG003, LAG015, LAG017]

- In the later window, participation is unchanged — every activity still appears in every trace and each pair still co-occurs in all cases — but the fixed order has loosened: pairs that previously followed one order now occur in both orders. A18 precedes A19 in only about 15 in 100 cases (previously all), A15 precedes A19 in about 26 in 100 (previously all), A15 precedes A21 in about half, while A17 still precedes A21 in about 77 in 100. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, POS001, POS003]

- Successors diversified: A18, previously always followed by A19, now leads to A27 in about 52 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions, with A19, A20 and A21 sharing the rest; A19 still mostly goes to A21 (about 61 in 100) but also to A17, A15 and A18; A20 now goes to A27 in about 49 in 100 with A15, A18 and A17 as alternatives; A26 splits about evenly between A17 and A19. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR006]

- Step distances spread in both directions: the A15–A20 distance, always 4 steps before, is now spread over 1–4 steps; A18 reaches A27 in 1 step in about half the cases (previously always 4); A19–A21, always adjacent before, now shows gaps of 2–4 steps in about 39 in 100 cases; A21–A27 now ranges from 2 to 5 steps instead of always 2. [LAG003, LAG013, LAG015, LAG019]

- Positions within the trace shifted only slightly: A15, A17 and A18 now occur somewhat later (mean relative position up by roughly 0.03–0.06), A19, A20 and A21 somewhat earlier (down by roughly 0.03–0.06), with each activity still present in every trace. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering constraints among this block of activities — A26, A17, A15, A18, A19, A21, A20 and A27, previously one fixed sequence — were relaxed in the later window: all of these activities still run in every case, but the fixed pairwise orders gave way to mixed orders, single successors gave way to several alternatives, and constant step distances became variable. The change is shown directly by relational evidence (all eight observed pairs moved from one fixed order to mixed orders), by successor profiles and by gap distributions; every item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits, and screening flagged 45 items where roughly 0.5 would be expected without a change, so the ensemble is far beyond what a stable process typically produces. [REL001, REL003, REL006, DIR003, LAG003, LAG013, screening]

Limitations: the many items largely reflect one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations; the evidence cannot distinguish whether each individual case now permits flexible ordering or whether the later window mixes several fixed-order variants; and a few gap items rest on fewer observations (for example A15–A19 on about 52 later traces, A18–A21 on about 64), though the main hypothesis does not rest on those alone. Positions and gaps are measured in event positions, not clock time, so no conclusion about timing is possible. [LAG002, LAG012]
