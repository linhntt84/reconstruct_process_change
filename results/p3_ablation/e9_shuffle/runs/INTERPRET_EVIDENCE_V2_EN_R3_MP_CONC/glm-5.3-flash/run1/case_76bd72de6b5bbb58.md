### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was introduced in place of A03 and took over its role in the flow. Earlier, A03 ran in about 45 in 100 traces and A01 was not observed; later, A03 is no longer observed and A01 runs in about 50 in 100 traces at the same relative position. A01 also inherits A03's neighbourhood: A09's handover flipped from A03 to A01, and A01 now feeds A11, A12, A10 and A09 in nearly the same shares A03 did, while the two activities never occur in the same trace. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, REL001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: relational and positional evidence captures the substitution itself, not only its side effects, all nineteen qualifying items describe the same rewiring, and the volume of flagged differences (19 items against roughly 0.33 expected by chance across the 67 tested control-flow units) is far beyond what random re-splits of these windows produce; the non-zero sides of the profiles rest on 56–203 observations each. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that A01 is a re-labelled A03 rather than a distinct new step, since the activity codes are anonymous and the windows are finite samples; and zero participation only means an activity was not observed in that window, so A03 could still occur outside these samples. [OCC002, POS002]
