### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the segment bounded by A40 and A30, the always-executed trio A20, A22, A23 and the per-case choice of one of A19, A21, A24 exchanged places around the stable pair A25–A29: earlier the choice ran directly after A40 and before A25 while the trio ran after A29 and exited to A30; in the later window the trio runs directly after A40 and enters A25, and the choice runs after A29 and exits to A30. The change is shown directly by the successor changes at A40, A29, the trio and the choice, and by all 21 tested activity pairs across the two groups reversing their dominant order with near-total shares; the position and step-distance shifts agree in direction and magnitude; and screening flagged 46 control-flow items against fewer than one expected without a change, so the set of differences as a whole is not what a stable process produces (the items largely describe this one reordering rather than independent changes, and each difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits). The main limitation is that no activity between A25 and A29 was flagged, so that this middle stretch is itself unchanged is inferred from the stable A25–A29 order and gap rather than observed directly; a competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that this unobserved stretch also changed alongside the swap, or that several coincident edge re-wirings rather than one reordering produced the same relations.
