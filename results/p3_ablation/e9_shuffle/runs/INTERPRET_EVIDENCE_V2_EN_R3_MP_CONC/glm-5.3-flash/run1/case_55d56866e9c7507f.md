### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the mutually exclusive alternative step A09-or-A11 now runs directly after A06, earlier in the trace and in every trace, whereas before it ran late in the trace (typically 3–4 steps after A06, behind A08) and in only about 59 in 100 traces; A08 lost its mediating position between A06 and A09/A11, now typically running 3–4 steps after A06 and less often leading to either of them, with its participation roughly unchanged.

Supporting signs: 16 qualifying items against about 0.16 expected without change, so chance cannot explain the set as a whole; the re-placement is shown directly by relational and directional evidence (A11 after A06 in both windows, A06→A11 and A06→A09 succession up, A08's links to A09/A11 down, A06–A08 gap up), with participation, position, relation and lag items all pointing the same way; the A09–A11 exclusivity is preserved while their combined coverage became complete.

Limitations: the items overlap heavily — the participation, position, relation and lag evidence for A11 largely re-describes one underlying shift, so they are not independent confirmations. The evidence cannot determine the exact new structure, and the changed successor profiles of A09, A11 and A05 rest on few earlier-window observations.

Other differences: the outgoing transitions of A09 and A11 now spread across more distinct successors, and A05 more often precedes A09 or A11; these are not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR001]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a re-routing into different path structures — for example A08 becoming optional on a separate branch rather than being displaced — which would look similar in these indicators.
