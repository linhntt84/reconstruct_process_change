### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A25, A26 and A31 each occurred exactly once in every trace (mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, present in all 200 traces, never repeated). In the later window, each of the three occurs about 3.5 times per trace on average and is repeated within a trace in about 68 in 100 traces, while still appearing in every trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A31's successor changed: before, every observed outgoing transition of A31 led to A20; in the later window about 71 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (out of roughly 700) lead to A25 and about 29 in 100 still lead to A20. [DIR001]
- The order between A25 and A31 loosened: before, the two always co-occurred with A25 strictly before A31; in the later window they still always co-occur, but in about 68 in 100 traces they are interleaved or repeated rather than in that fixed order, which remains in about 32 in 100 traces. [REL001]
- The constant spacing between the two disappeared: before, A25 was followed by A31 exactly two positions later in essentially all cases; in the later window only about 12 in 100 of the observed pairs sit at that distance, with pairs at larger gaps (including five positions) now appearing. [LAG001]
- The positional distributions of A25 and A31 became more variable while their average positions barely moved (A25 mean about 0.41 to 0.44, A31 about 0.48 to 0.48; spread in both cases roughly doubling from about 0.06 to 0.12), which fits repetition rather than a relocation. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the sequence A25 → A26 → A31, which previously ran exactly once per trace, is repeated within traces: all three activities occur about 3.5 times per trace and repeat in about 68 in 100 traces, A31 typically leads back to A25 (about 71 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, versus always A20 before), and the previously fixed A25-before-A31 order becomes interleaved or repeated in about 68 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001]

The support is direct and consistent: repetition is measured on the activities themselves, the new A31→A25 transition is observed directly in the routing profile, and the relation and spacing between A25 and A31 change exactly as repetition would produce; the 68-in-100 interleaving share also matches the 68-in-100 recurrence share, and the eight qualifying items are roughly twenty-five times the 0.3 expected for an unchanged process, with every item larger than what random re-splits produce in about 995 of 1000 comparisons. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001, LAG001] The wider but not shifted positional distributions of A25 and A31 fit the same behaviour and are not independent confirmation of it. [POS001, POS002]

Limitations: A26's place in the repeated sequence rests only on its occurrence profile matching that of A25 and A31, since no direct relation between A26 and the other two was measured; and these window-level aggregates cannot distinguish a uniform change from a later window in which a large subset of cases acquired the repetitions while the rest kept the single-pass behaviour. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001]

Other differences: none — all eight qualifying items are accounted for by this single behavioural difference. [OCC001, DIR001, OCC002, LAG001, POS002, OCC003, REL001, POS001]
