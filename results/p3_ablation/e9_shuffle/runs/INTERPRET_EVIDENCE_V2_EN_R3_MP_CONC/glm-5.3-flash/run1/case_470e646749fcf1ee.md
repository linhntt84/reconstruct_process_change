### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural reorganisation of the process middle. In the later window, A01 is no longer observed and the chain it sat in (A14 → A01 → A04) is replaced by a direct A14 → A04 connection; at the same time, a new block (A02, A12, A13) runs between A03 and A04 in roughly half of the traces (A03 → A02, A02 → A12 or A13 with A12 feeding back to A02, A13 → A04), and A09 is inserted into the repetition of A08 (A08 → A09 → A08 instead of a direct A08 repeat). As a result, A04 is executed later in the trace. The change is shown directly rather than inferred: appearance and disappearance are measured on 0 versus about 95 or 51 of 200 traces, successor changes are observed for A03, A08, A14 and the new activities, and the relational, positional and distance items agree with one another; 54 items qualified in screening against roughly 0.36 expected by chance if nothing had changed. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS003, REL012, REL017]

Limitations: zero participation only means "not observed", so it cannot be fully excluded that A01 or the new activities existed before at rates too low to appear in 200 traces; the mixed A02/A12 ordering (both directions observed) leaves open whether the new block is a simple loop or parallel repeats; positions and distances are event positions, so no statement about clock-time timing is possible. [OCC001, REL012]

Other differences: A03's marginal outgoing edges to A05, A06 and A10 (about 0.5–1.5% of its transitions before) are no longer observed; A14 appears slightly later (mean position about 0.58 to 0.70) and in slightly fewer traces (about 40 versus 35 of 200); small distance redistributions appear for A04–A04, and the A14–A14 distance item rests on only 6 and 4 observations. [DIR003, POS007, LAG002, LAG005]
