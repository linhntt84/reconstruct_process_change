### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A03 was inserted into the flow between A07 and the activities A02, A06 and A10. In the earlier window, A03 was not observed and A07 was followed directly by one of A02, A06 or A10 in roughly equal shares; in the later window, every trace runs A07 → A03, and A03 then routes on to A02 (about 39 in 100), A06 (about 33 in 100) or A10 (about 29 in 100). [OCC001, POS002, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the 15 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.12 expected for an unchanged process, and participation, successor changes, relations and step distances all show the same picture — A03 appears in every trace, A07 now leads only to A03, the A07→A02/A06/A10 distances moved one step later, and A02/A06/A10 shifted to later positions, exactly the pattern expected if A03 sits between A07 and its former successors. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A03 was not observed in the earlier window, so a newly added activity cannot be distinguished from one that existed but was never executed or recorded before. Whether the branch-choice probabilities themselves changed (A03's 39/33/29 split versus A07's earlier 33/32/36) cannot be established from these samples, and positions and distances are in event positions rather than clock time, so no timing claim is made.
