### Observed differences

- In the later window, A01 runs much later in every case: its mean relative position moved from about 0.26 (roughly a quarter into the trace) to about 0.65 (about two-thirds in), and its median from about one-sixth to two-thirds; A01 occurs in all 200 traces in both windows [POS001].
- In the earlier window A01 usually came before A05 (about 80 in 100 cases), A07 (about 68), A02 (about 55) and A04 (about 49); in the later window it comes before A05 in only about 37 and before A02 in about 19, and never before A04 or A07 — both of those now always precede A01 [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004].
- A01's outgoing transitions shifted: before, they went mainly to A05 (about 31 in 100), A02 (29) and A04 (18); after, they go mainly to A03 (47) and A05 (27), with A04 and A07 no longer observed as successors [DIR001].
- Feeding activities rerouted: A07 now sends about 68 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A01 (about 18 before) and none to A03 (about 27 before); A04 no longer leads to A01 (about 16 before) and leads to A07 in about 70 in 100 (about 54 before); the mostly immediate A07→A01 pair now occurs in all 200 traces (65 before) [DIR004, DIR003, LAG007].
- Pairwise gaps moved consistently with this: A02→A01 stretched from mostly 1 step (57 in 100) to mostly 3–5 steps; A04→A01 lost its immediate cases (31 in 100 at 1 step before, none after) and now sits 2–4 steps back in about 92 in 100; A05→A01 stretched from mostly 1–2 steps to mostly 3–5 (few before-window observations, 39 of 200 traces); A01→A03 became mostly immediate (47 in 100 at 1 step, versus mostly 4–5 steps before) and appears in all 200 after-traces; A01→A05, where seen, is now almost always immediate (72 in 100 versus 38) but occurs in fewer after-traces (74 of 200 versus 161) [LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG001, LAG002].
- Smaller shifts: A02, A05 and A07 each occur slightly earlier (mean position about 0.08–0.10 of the trace lower); A07→A05 and A05→A03 gaps moved slightly later; A07→A03 is no longer observed at 1 step [POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG009, LAG006, LAG008].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was repositioned to a later phase of the flow. In the earlier window A01 ran early (about a quarter into each trace) and typically preceded A02, A04, A05 and A07; in the later window it runs at about two-thirds of each trace, after all four of these activities, and is followed mainly by A03 (and often A05), while its transitions to A04 and A07 are no longer observed. This rests on direct positional evidence for A01, complete order reversals with all four neighbours, successor redistributions at A01, A04 and A07, and matching lag shifts [POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005]. With 21 qualifying items against roughly 0.18 expected if nothing had changed, chance sampling cannot account for the ensemble, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of unchanged windows.

Limitations: positions are event positions rather than clock time, so the shift is established in sequence order only; several items describe the same underlying traces (for example REL and LAG on the same pairs) and are not independent confirmations; unobserved successors such as A01→A04 or A04→A01 are absences in these samples, not proof that edges were removed [DIR001, DIR003]. A competing reading the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the surrounding flow was reordered around a stationary A01 rather than A01 itself moving; the small shifts of A02, A05 and A07 (about 0.08–0.10 earlier) against A01's 0.39 later shift make the repositioning of A01 the more direct reading [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004].

Other differences: the slightly earlier occurrence of A02, A05 and A07, the slightly later A07→A05 and A05→A03 gaps, and the disappearance of immediate A07→A03 transitions; these fit the same restructuring but are not part of the main hypothesis [POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG006, LAG008, LAG009, DIR004].
