### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A17 is no longer executed while A01 appears as a new step. Earlier, A17 ran in about half of the cases — repeated several times within a case — and A12 handed over to it about half the time; later, A17 is unobserved across 200 cases, A12 passes control to A15 and A14 instead, A01 runs once in about 39 in 100 cases around mid-case (always alongside A02, leading mainly to A09, A07 and A12), and the A14/A15 sequence runs with visibly shorter gaps. The participation evidence is direct and large on both sides (0→77 and 105→0 of 200 cases), the successor and relation items all move in the same direction, and 27 qualifying items against roughly 0.3 expected by chance makes a chance origin of the set implausible; the position and gap shifts agree with the participation change but reflect the same underlying behaviour rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: zero participation only shows A17 was not observed, and because A01 and A17 never co-occur and each appears in only one window, the evidence cannot fully rule out that A01 is a re-labelled counterpart of A17 — its single, non-repeating execution and different successors argue against identity but do not settle it.
