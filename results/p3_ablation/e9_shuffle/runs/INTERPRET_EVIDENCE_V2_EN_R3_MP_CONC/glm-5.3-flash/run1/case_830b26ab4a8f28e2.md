### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was removed from the process, or is no longer executed, and that the flow which previously passed through it now runs directly from A03 and A05 to A10, A09 and A08. The disappearance is observed directly (participation 100% to 0 of 200 traces; outgoing transitions 702 to 0), the rerouting is observed directly on A03's and A05's outgoing transitions, and the relational items show A01's former partners now executing in traces without it while A03 and A05 kept their previous participation levels. This is unlikely to be a sampling artefact: 17 control-flow items qualified where fewer than 1 (0.26) would be expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeds 99.5% of random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A01 was not observed in the later sample, so removal from the process cannot be distinguished from A01 remaining in place but never being executed; the internal order among A10, A09 and A08 after A03/A05 is known only as shares over outgoing transitions and position distances, not as clock-time behaviour; and many items describe the same underlying difference, so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG003]

Other differences: the A08–A09 distance profile shifted mildly in the opposite direction (distance 1 down from about 14 to 7 in 100, distance 4 up from about 4 to 7), a small redistribution whose relation to the rerouting is not resolved by the evidence. [LAG007]
