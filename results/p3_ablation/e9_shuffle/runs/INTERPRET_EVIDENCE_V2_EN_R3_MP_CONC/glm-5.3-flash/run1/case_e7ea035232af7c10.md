### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, every case executes both groups that were previously mutually exclusive alternatives: the block A36–A37–A42 became mandatory (from about 46 in 100 traces) and now runs as a group directly after A25 and before A28, whereas earlier it ran after A26 between A26 and A31; A27 also became mandatory (from about 54 in 100) and keeps its earlier place between A26 and A31. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL006, REL014, REL016, DIR001, LAG001]

The placement change is shown directly, not inferred from participation alone: order flips against A26 and A28, A25's successor changing from A28 to the block, the block's exit changing from A31 to A28, the A25→A28 distance growing from one to four steps, and the block's relative position dropping by about 0.19. The pattern is consistent across all 45 qualifying items, far more than the about 0.7 expected without change, so chance sampling cannot explain the evidence as a whole. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, POS006, POS007, POS008, REL006, REL007, REL014, REL015, REL016]

Limitations: each window holds only 200 traces, so a before-window variant combining A27 with the block at a rate below about 0.5 in 100 (0 of 200 observed) could not have been detected; and the indicators show relative order, so whether the block moved earlier or A26/A28 moved later cannot be distinguished, although A25's new successors point to the block now sitting directly after A25. [REL013, DIR001]

Other differences: A25's own mean relative position decreased slightly (about 0.36 to 0.35) [POS001]; the direction profiles show a handful of rare successors, each at about 0.5–1 in 100 of outgoing transitions, appearing or disappearing between the windows [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005].
