### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window four activities — A07, A15, A16 and A17 — joined the observed process and were inserted into existing paths: A08 now feeds A07, which feeds A18; A18 now feeds A17 (and A14), which feeds A09; and A10 now feeds A15 (which leads back to A10) and A16 (which leads to A03/A02), with the new activities co-occurring with existing activities in roughly a third to seven in ten traces. This rests on direct occurrence evidence (zero participation earlier versus 33–70 in 100 traces later), new successor profiles for the new activities, and new co-occurrence relations; with 92 qualifying items against roughly 0.6 expected if nothing had changed, chance sampling cannot account for the ensemble. Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that these activities were not observed then, so a very rare earlier presence cannot be excluded, and many items describe the same underlying appearance (for example the occurrence, position and direction items for A15), so they are not independent confirmations.
