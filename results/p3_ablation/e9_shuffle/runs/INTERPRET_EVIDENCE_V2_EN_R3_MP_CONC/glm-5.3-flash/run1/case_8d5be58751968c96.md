### Observed differences

- Earlier, A30, A31, A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 each appeared in every trace. In the later window, A30 and A31 still appear in every trace, while A33, A35 and A36 appear in about 47 in 100 traces and A37, A38 and A39 in about 56 in 100; mean occurrences per trace match participation in both windows, so none of the activities started or stopped repeating. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, POS001, POS002]

- Earlier, the two groups always ran together and in sequence: A30 first, then {A33, A35, A36} in mixed internal order (both orders in roughly equal shares), then {A37, A38, A39} in mixed order, then A31; every pair co-occurred in 100 in 100 traces. In the later window, members of the same group still co-occur (about 47 and about 56 in 100 traces, internal order still mixed), but members of different groups are now almost mutually exclusive: cross-group pairs co-occur in only about 2–4 in 100 traces, each appears without the other in about 44 and 53 in 100, and traces containing neither are essentially absent — so almost every trace contains exactly one of the two groups. - Routing reflects this: earlier, A30 led almost only into {A33, A35, A36} (about 99 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) and those activities led on into {A37, A38, A39}; in the later window A30 feeds both groups (about 53 in 100 of its transitions go straight to A37, A38 or A39), and A33, A35 and A36 lead directly to A31 in about 24–34 in 100 of their transitions, bypassing the second group. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

- Step distances shrank where a group is skipped: A30 to A37/A38/A39 moved from 4–5 steps (essentially all observed pairs) to 1–3 steps (about 94–98 in 100), and A33/A35/A36 to A31 likewise from 4–5 to 1–3 steps. Relative positions are consistent with shorter traces: A33/A35/A36 sit later (mean about 0.68 to 0.73 of the trace), A37/A38/A39 slightly earlier (about 0.76 to 0.74), A30 later (0.62 to 0.67) and A31 slightly earlier (0.82 to 0.80). - The cross-group distance items in the later window rest on only 4–5 observations each and are weak on their own. [LAG006, LAG008, LAG009, LAG011, LAG012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the two activity groups {A33, A35, A36} and {A37, A38, A39}, which earlier executed one after the other in every case between A30 and A31, became mutually exclusive alternatives: in the later window each case runs exactly one of the two groups — about 47 in 100 cases the A33/A35/A36 group and about 53–56 in 100 the A37/A38/A39 group — while A30 beforehand and A31 afterwards are unchanged and the mixed internal order within each group is preserved; about 2–4 in 100 cases still run both groups. The signs are direct and consistent: participation halved for exactly the six group members while A30 and A31 stayed at 100 in 100; cross-group co-occurrence collapsed from 100 in 100 to about 2–4 in 100 with "neither" near zero, which also rules out the two groups having become optional independently (that would leave many cases with neither or both groups); A30 now leads straight into the second group and first-group activities lead straight to A31; and step distances shrink exactly where a group is bypassed. Screening makes chance an implausible overall explanation: 57 control-flow items qualified where about 0.79 were expected without a change across the 159 tested units. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, POS002]

What the evidence cannot show is what determines which of the two groups a case takes; the small residual share of cases running both groups could be a persisting old path or sampling noise, and the cross-group distance items after the change rest on only 4–5 observations each. [LAG006, LAG008, LAG009, LAG011, LAG012, REL020, REL023]

Other differences: a handful of rare new transitions appear in the later window (A30 to A02/A03/A06, A33 to A10/A26, A35 to A07/A10/A28, A36 to A11/A21/A27, each at about 1–2 in 100 of the relevant outgoing transitions); they are too rare to characterise further. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
