### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A21 were mutually exclusive alternatives that together covered essentially every trace: A19 ran in about 70 of 200 traces (~35%), A20 in about 60 (~30%) and A21 in about 71 (~35%), and no trace contained more than one of them. In the later window, each of the three appears in only 1 of 200 traces. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL007, REL008, REL016]
- In the later window, A26 and A27, which were not observed at all earlier, and A28, previously seen in 1 of 200 traces, each run in every one of the 200 traces, all at a similar relative position (mean around 0.38 of the trace's event sequence). [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, POS005, POS006, POS007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural reorganisation of the segment between A44 and A29. In the earlier window, each trace passed from A44 through exactly one of three mutually exclusive alternatives — A19, A20 or A21 — to A29. In the later window, A19, A20 and A21 are no longer observed (1 of 200 traces each), and the previously unobserved A26, A27 and A28 all execute in every trace between A44 and A29, in mixed order, each followed by A29. This is a replacement of one path by another — three alternatives no longer taken, three new activities now executed in every case — not a shift in how often existing paths are chosen.

The change is shown directly by relational and directional evidence, not only by frequencies: A44's successors switched from the old trio to the new trio [DIR009]; A44 precedes, and each new activity precedes, A29 in nearly every later trace [REL034, REL039, REL043, REL033, REL038, REL042]; the A44→A29 step distance grew from about 2 to about 4–5 steps [LAG005]; and the old trio's earlier relations (A44 before, A29 after, mutual exclusivity) match the disappeared choice point [REL007, REL008, REL012, REL016, REL020, REL027]. The screening produced 74 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits produce.

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows draw on different process variants or case populations rather than one modified process; also, the near-zero participation of A19, A20 and A21 means only that they were not observed in the later sample (1 of 200 traces each), not that they are impossible.

Other differences: A10's participation fell from ~26% to ~11% of traces, it now co-occurs with the new block when it appears, and its outgoing transitions are diffuse; this rests on only 22 later traces (7 for its lag pairs) and is kept out of the main hypothesis [OCC001, DIR001, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG004]. A29's position shifted slightly later, and A45/A46 now co-occur with the new block as described above [POS008, REL035, REL036].
