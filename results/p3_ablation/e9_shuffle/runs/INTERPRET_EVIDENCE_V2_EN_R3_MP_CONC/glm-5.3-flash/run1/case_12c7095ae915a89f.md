### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A02 and A04 occurred together in every trace and always in the same order: A02 before A04 in 100 in 100 cases. In the later window they still occur together in every trace, but the order is mixed: A02 comes first in about 54 in 100 traces and A04 first in about 46 in 100. [REL001]
- The direct successors of A01, A02 and A04 shifted accordingly. Before, A02 led almost always straight to A04 (about 94 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) and A04 almost always to A03 (about 96 in 100), while A01 led to A02 in about 87 in 100 and directly to A04 in only about 6 in 100. In the later window, A02 leads to A03 in about 44 in 100 and to A04 in about 50 in 100; A04 leads to A02 in about 40 in 100 and to A03 in about 49 in 100; and A01 leads directly to A04 in about 43 in 100. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- Step distances between these activities split accordingly. Before, A01 reached A04 typically two steps later (about 94 in 100) and A02 reached A03 two steps later (about 90 in 100), A04 reached A03 one step later (about 96 in 100), and A01 reached A02 one step later in essentially all cases. In the later window each pair shows both distances: A01–A04 one step in about 49 in 100 versus two steps in about 51 in 100; A02–A03 one step in about 44 in 100; A04–A03 one step in about 49 in 100 versus two steps in about 43 in 100; A01–A02 one step in about 57 in 100 versus two steps in about 43 in 100. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]
- Event positions (positions, not clock time) became more spread: A02's mean position moved from about 0.17 to about 0.27 and A04's from about 0.39 to about 0.28, and the position variability of both roughly doubled (for A04, from about 0.05 to about 0.13 standard deviation). [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the fixed execution order between A02 and A04 was relaxed into an order without a fixed constraint. In the earlier window, every trace containing both executed A02 before A04, embedded in the sequence A01 → A02 → A04 → A03; in the later window the two activities still always occur together but in either order — A02 first in about 54 in 100 traces, A04 first in about 46 in 100 — and the successor shares at A01, A02 and A04, the split step distances, and the wider position distributions all match a mix of these two orders. All involved activities (A01, A02, A03, A04) appear in essentially every trace in both windows, so this is a change in ordering constraints, not an activity appearing or disappearing. The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 10 items against roughly 0.1 expected by chance for the 20 tested control-flow units. [REL001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: the direction, lag and position items largely describe the same traces as the A02–A04 relation, so they corroborate but do not independently confirm the hypothesis. The evidence also cannot rule out that both orders already existed before, with the A04-first order too rare to appear in the 200 earlier traces, since its absence there only means it was not observed. [REL001]

Other differences: A01's rare direct transitions to A05 and A06 (together about 2–3 in 100 of its outgoing transitions in the earlier window) were not observed in the later window, and its direct transitions to A03 rose from about 4 to about 8 in 100; both are small and may be sampling variation. [DIR001]
