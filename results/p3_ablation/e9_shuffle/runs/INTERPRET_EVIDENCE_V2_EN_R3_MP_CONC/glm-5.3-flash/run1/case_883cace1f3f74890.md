### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a specific hypothesis: in the later window, a new mid-process step is inserted into the segment between A27 and A29, executed as one of three nearly mutually exclusive alternatives — A20, A25 or A19 — where previously the flow ran directly A27 → A28 → A29. The new step is reached from A27 in about half of its transitions and leads on to A28 and/or A29, so that essentially every later trace contains exactly one of the three alternatives in place of (or alongside) the former direct hops. This is shown directly and consistently by the rerouted outgoing transitions of A27 and A28, the new co-occurrence and ordering relations (after A27, before A29, mixed around A28), the widened step distances, and the mid-trace positions of the new activities; with 29 qualifying items against roughly 0.54 expected for an unchanged process, the ensemble as a whole is not what random re-splits produce. Limitations: the earlier window only shows that A19, A20 and A25 were not observed, so a genuinely added step cannot be distinguished from previously existing paths that were simply not taken before; and the insertion point is not uniform — the new activity sits between A27 and A28 in about 55 in 100 cases and between A28 and A29 in about 43.
