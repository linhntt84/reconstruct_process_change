### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the process executes A01 — not observed in the earlier window — on the paths leading into A04: A11, A03 and A10, which previously led directly to A04 in the large majority of their outgoing transitions, now frequently lead to A01 first, and A01 in turn leads to A04 in about three quarters of its outgoing transitions. As a result, A04 and the activities around it sit about one step later in the trace [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR005, DIR002, DIR004, DIR003, LAG001, LAG005].

The reading is direct and consistent across participation, position, successor profiles, pairwise relations and step distances, and its overall volume far exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 20 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.23 expected by chance, and every item's difference exceeded that of about 199 in 200 random re-splits of the pooled traces [OCC001, DIR005, REL002, LAG001].

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A01 was not executed in those 200 sampled traces, so a previously vanishingly rare presence cannot be excluded; the placement is predominantly but not exclusively before A04 (A01 follows A04 in about 5 in 100 of their joint traces, and the pair is interleaved or repeated in about 23 in 100); and the many items partly restate one underlying restructuring rather than independent confirmations. The competing explanation — sampling fluctuation in an unchanged process — is made unlikely by the number of qualifying items but cannot be excluded for any single item [REL002, OCC001].

Other differences: beyond the rerouting to A01, A10's outgoing transitions also shifted toward A02 (about 15 to 25 in 100) and A07 (about 20 to 28 in 100), and A04 appears in somewhat fewer traces (about 54 versus 47 in 100) [DIR004, POS002].
