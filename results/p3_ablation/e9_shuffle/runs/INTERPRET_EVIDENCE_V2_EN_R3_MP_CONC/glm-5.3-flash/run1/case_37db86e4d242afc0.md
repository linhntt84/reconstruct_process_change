### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A03 took over the early sequence position of A01 — the fragment A02 → A01 → A07 → A05 became A02 → A03 → A07 → A05 — and that a new optional final activity A06 was inserted after A05, terminating about 47% of traces (93 of 200) that previously ended at A05 itself; A05 is now always followed by A06 or A08. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, DIR002, DIR003, OCC003, POS004, REL011, DIR004, POS003]

The substitution is shown directly by relations and successors, not only by frequencies: A02's only observed successor changed from A01 to A03, A03's only observed successor is A07 (as A01's was), and A03 co-occurs with and precedes A07 and A05 in every later trace exactly as A01 did; A06's role as a new optional ending is shown by its exclusive final position, its precedence after A05 and A07, and the split of A05's outgoing transitions. Screening produced 24 qualifying items against roughly 0.12 expected without a change, so chance as a whole cannot explain the pattern, and all items fit one coherent restructuring with substantial support (200 traces per window; 93 traces behind the A06 profile). [DIR002, DIR001, DIR003, REL006, REL010, REL008, REL011, REL012, POS004, DIR004]

Limitations: zero participation only means an activity was not observed, so a rare presence of A03 or A06 before the change cannot be excluded; A08 is observed only as a successor of A05, without direct occurrence or position measurements; and the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely new activity A03 from the same step recorded under a different code. [OCC002, OCC003, DIR004]

Other differences: A07's position became deterministic and slightly earlier (mean 0.45 → 0.40, upper quartile 0.5 → 0.4), a side effect consistent with the tightened trace tail rather than part of the substitution itself. [POS005]
