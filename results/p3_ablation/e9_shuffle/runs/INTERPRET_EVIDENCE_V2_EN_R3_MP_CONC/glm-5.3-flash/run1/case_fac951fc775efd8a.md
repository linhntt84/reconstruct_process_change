### Observed differences

- The routing into and out of two activity groups was exchanged. In the earlier window, A40's outgoing transitions went to A20, A22 and A23 (about 37, 30 and 27 in 100), each of which led on to A25 in roughly 30–39 of 100 transitions, while A29 led to one of A19, A21, A24 (about 30–38 in 100 each) and each of those led to A30 in about 97–99 of 100 transitions. In the later window, A40 leads to A02 (about 31 in 100) and directly to A19/A21/A24 (about 67 in 100 combined), A19, A21 and A24 lead to A25 (about 95–100 in 100), A29 leads to A20/A22/A23 (about 30–33 in 100 each), and A20, A22 and A23 lead to A30 in about 32–34 of 100 transitions, with the rest going to one another. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR007, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR008, DIR009, DIR001]

- The two groups also swapped their places along the trace. A20, A22 and A23, present in every trace in both windows, moved from a mean position about 39% into the trace to about 64%; A19, A21 and A24 moved from about 67–69% to about 35–37%, with participation changing from about 31, 38 and 32 in 100 traces to about 44, 29 and 29 in 100. A25 shifted slightly earlier (mean position 0.45 to 0.39) and A29 from 0.65 to 0.58. [POS003, POS005, POS006, POS002, POS004, POS007, POS008, POS009]

- Pairwise order flipped consistently between the groups: A20 preceded A25 in 98 of 100 earlier traces but in none later, where A25 precedes A20 throughout; A29 preceded A19 in about 31 of 100 earlier traces, whereas now A19 precedes A29 in about 44 in 100; A20 preceded A21 in 37 of 100 earlier traces, now A21 precedes A20 in about 29 in 100. [REL017, REL014, REL015, REL027, REL028, REL024]

- A02 changed from a rare, loosely connected activity (8 in 100 traces, outgoing spread over many targets) to a frequent one (34 in 100 traces) placed between A40 and the A19/A21/A24 group: A40 is followed by A02 within one step in about 62 later traces (2 earlier), A02 reaches A25 within two steps in about 63 later traces (2 earlier), and its outgoing transitions now go to A19, A24 and A21 (about 37, 27 and 24 in 100). [OCC001, POS001, LAG005, LAG001, DIR001, REL009]

- Step distances moved as this rewiring implies: A29 to A30 stretched from about 2 steps (97 in 100 earlier traces) to about 4 steps (94 in 100); A40 to A25 shortened from 4–5 steps to 2–3; A29 now precedes A20 within 1–3 steps in virtually every trace (seen once earlier); and the within-five-step relations A40 → A22 and A22 → A25, present in almost all earlier traces, are now almost never observed. [LAG004, LAG007, LAG003, LAG006, LAG002]

- Outside this rewiring, A41 recurs more often (about 1.8 occurrences per trace versus about 1.5, with repetition in about 40% of traces versus 32%), while A43's outgoing behaviour is essentially unchanged (A43 → A44 in over 90% of its transitions in both windows). [OCC002, DIR010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural reordering in which the group A19/A21/A24 and the group A20/A22/A23 exchanged places in the flow: in the earlier window the line ran A40 → A20/A22/A23 → A25, with A29 later handing over to one of A19/A21/A24, which closed into A30; in the later window A40 leads to one of A19/A21/A24 — in about a third of traces via the now-frequent A02, which grew from 8 to 34 in 100 traces — and on to A25, while A29 hands over to A20/A22/A23, which now close into A30. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR001, REL017, REL014, POS002, POS003, LAG004, LAG007]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor profiles of A40, A29 and all six group members changed almost completely and in complementary directions, positional shifts of about a quarter to a third of the trace and order flips affecting roughly 28–44 in 100 traces agree across every pair bridging the two groups, and the lag distances change exactly as the exchange predicts. Screening flagged 58 control-flow items where fewer than 1 would qualify by chance in a stable process, so the body of differences cannot be explained by random re-splits, although many of the relation and position items describe this single restructuring rather than independent changes. [DIR008, DIR009, POS006, REL021, REL029, LAG004, LAG007]

Limitations: the evidence shows aggregate behaviour of two 200-trace samples and cannot rule out that the difference reflects a shift in the mix of pre-existing execution variants rather than a modified single flow; position and lag indicators use event order, not clock time, so no timing claim is implied; and a few lag profiles rest on very few observations on one side (1–2 traces), so they support the picture only weakly on their own. [LAG001, LAG003, LAG005]

Other differences: A41 now repeats more often within traces, with about 1.8 occurrences per trace versus about 1.5 earlier and repetition in about 40% versus 32% of traces. [OCC002]
