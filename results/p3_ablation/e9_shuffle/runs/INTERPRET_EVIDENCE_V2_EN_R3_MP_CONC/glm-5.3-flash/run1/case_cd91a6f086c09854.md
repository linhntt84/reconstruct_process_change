### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of activities A30, A31 and A36 changed its placement in the sequence: it ran between A20 and A22 in the earlier window (A20 → A30/A31/A36 → A22 → A21 → A25) and, in the later window, runs after A21 (A20 → A22 → A21 → A30/A31/A36 → A25). A20 now bypasses the block and leads directly to A22, A21 now leads into the block, and the block leads to A25 instead of A22, while its internal, unfixed order is unchanged. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

The change is shown directly and consistently: six relation items record the order reversal itself (100 in 100 one way earlier, about 99 in 100 the other way later, with co-occurrence unchanged), the successor changes at A20, A21 and all three block members match the same relocation, and the position and distance shifts agree in direction and size. In total 22 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.42 expected for an unchanged process, and every item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits, so sampling alone cannot account for them.
