### Observed differences

- **A07 is no longer executed.** In the earlier window it appeared in every trace (200 of 200), about 2.1 times per trace, occurring more than once in roughly half of the traces; in the later window it appears in no trace at all (0 of 200). [OCC003, POS003]
- **A08's outgoing flow changed accordingly.** Earlier, every outgoing transition of A08 led to A07; in the later window about 52 in 100 lead to A05 and about 48 in 100 to A06, with no transition to A07. [DIR003, DIR002]
- **Step distances shortened by about one position** wherever the flow previously passed through A07: A08→A06 went from always 2 steps apart to 1 step in about 47 in 100 cases (and 4 steps in about 18 in 100); A03→A06 went from always 3 steps to 2 steps in about 47 in 100; A02→A06 and A05→A06 went from 4 to 3 steps; the repeat distances A03→A03, A05→A05 and A08→A08 went from 4 to 3 steps. [LAG013, LAG006, LAG002, LAG009, LAG004, LAG008, LAG014]
- **A01 appears only in the later window**, in about 35 in 100 traces, always exactly once, always at the first position, always followed by A03, and preceding A06 in every trace containing both. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, REL004]
- **A07's relations collapsed from universal co-occurrence to absence.** Earlier, A03, A06 and A08 each co-occurred with A07 in 100 in 100 traces (A07 typically after A03/A08 and before A06, otherwise interleaved with repeats); in the later window these activities occur without A07 in 100 in 100 traces. [REL008, REL010, REL011]
- **A02 became less frequent**, from about 48 in 100 traces to about 28 in 100, and never occurs in the same trace as A01 in either window; A03 and A08 also sit slightly later within traces (median normalized position about 0.25→0.33 and 0.50→0.67). [OCC002, REL001, POS002, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening tested 39 control-flow units and 35 items qualified, against fewer than 0.2 expected if nothing had changed; a count this many times above the chance expectation cannot be explained by random sampling, so the two windows differ in behaviour. [OCC003, DIR003, OCC001]

Main hypothesis: the flow was restructured around A07. A07 — previously executed in every trace, about twice per trace, sitting between A08 and the A05/A06 part of the flow — is no longer executed, and A08 now leads directly to A05 and A06; consistent with this, every observed step distance that previously spanned A07 shortened by about one position. As part of the same restructuring, A01 appears as a new opening activity in about 35 in 100 traces, always first and always followed by A03. [OCC003, DIR003, LAG013, OCC001, POS001, DIR001]

The support is direct and consistent: the rerouting is observed at the edge level (all of A08's flow to A07 before; a split to A05/A06 after, with A07's own outgoing edges gone), the participation changes are observed trace counts (A07 200→0, A01 0→69 of 200), and the lag shortenings and relational collapses point the same way — though many of these items describe the same underlying traces rather than independent confirmations. [DIR003, DIR002, OCC003, OCC001, LAG006, REL010]

The evidence cannot distinguish a modification of the process from a complete switch in the variant mix between the windows (every earlier trace contains A07, no later trace does); zero participation only means the activity was not observed. The detailed routing among A05 and A06 afterwards — for example why some activity pairs now fall more than five steps apart — is not fully resolved. [OCC003, LAG013]

Other differences: A02's participation fell from about 48 to about 28 in 100 traces while A01 appeared; the two never co-occur and so behave as alternatives, but the evidence does not show that one replaced the other. The slightly later positions of A03 and A08 are consistent with the shorter traces after A07 disappeared and do not by themselves indicate that these activities were moved. [OCC002, REL001, POS002, POS004]
