### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A41 leads directly to A26, whereas in the earlier window A41 led to one of three alternatives — A19, A25 or A20, each used in roughly a third of traces and essentially never together — which in turn led almost invariably to A26. In the later window this intermediate block is no longer routed through: A19 and A25 are unobserved, A20 appears in a single trace, and A41's transitions go straight to A26. [DIR004, LAG002, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

The hypothesis rests on direct relational evidence, not on participation alone: the successor profile of A41 changed from A19/A25/A20 to A26 with about 200 outgoing-transition observations in each window, and the A41–A26 pair distance collapsed from two event positions (about 93 in 100 traces) to one (about 93 in 100). The shift is consistent across about 36 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.61 expected by chance in that family — far beyond what random re-splits produce — including the relations of the three activities with A14, A31 and A26, and the positional shifts of A41 and A26 point the same way. [DIR004, LAG002, REL012, REL017, REL021, REL010, REL015, REL019, POS005, POS006]

Limitations: zero participation only establishes that A19, A20 and A25 were not observed in the later window, so the evidence cannot distinguish removing these activities from keeping them while always routing around them; and the single later trace containing A20, in which A20 was followed by A30 and sat later in the trace, rests on one observation and cannot be interpreted. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, POS003]

Other differences: the A24–A31 distance tightened from mostly two positions to mostly one, and A14, which all traces contain, shows only a negligible positional shift. [LAG001, POS001]
