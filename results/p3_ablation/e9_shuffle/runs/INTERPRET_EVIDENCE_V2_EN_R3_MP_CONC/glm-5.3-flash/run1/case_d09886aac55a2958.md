### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was introduced into the process flow in the later window and now executes between existing activities: an activity absent from the earlier window runs in about half of the later traces, about a third of the way through each trace, typically after activities such as A08, A09, A12 or A06 and before activities such as A05, A03 or A06. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]

The change is shown directly (participation from zero to about 50 in 100, newly observed edges into and out of A01, co-occurrence rising from never to a third-to-half of traces with A01 usually first), it is consistent across every A01-related item, and the 25 qualifying control-flow items far exceed the fewer than one expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces.
