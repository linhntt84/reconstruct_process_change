### Observed differences

- Participation collapsed for three activities: in the earlier window A19 ran in 32% of traces, A20 in 28% and A25 in 40%; in the later window each appears in only about 1 in 200 traces (0.5%). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A28 changed from a branch point to a near-deterministic step: earlier, its outgoing transitions led to A29 in about 45% of cases and to A25, A19 or A20 in about 55% combined; in the later window about 98 in 100 lead to A29. [DIR005]
- A27 concentrated similarly: earlier it led to A28 in about 53% of its outgoing transitions and directly to A25, A19 or A20 in about 43%; in the later window about 97% lead to A28. [DIR004]
- The retained backbone now appears without the dropped activities: A25 co-occurred with A29 in 40% of traces earlier versus about 0.5% later (A29 alone rises from 60% to about 99.5%); analogous collapses hold for A19 and A20 against A27, A28 and A29. - Earlier, A19, A20 and A25 largely excluded one another (A19 and A20 never co-occurred, with exactly one of the two present in 60% of traces), consistent with an alternative segment from which a case typically contained one activity; in the later window neither is present in 99% of traces. - The surviving sequence became tightly consecutive: A28 is immediately followed by A29 in about 98% of cases later versus 45% earlier; A27 immediately precedes A28 in about 98% versus 54%; and the typical A27-to-A29 distance shortened from three positions to two. [LAG003, LAG001, LAG002]
- A29 itself is stable: present in all traces in both windows with almost the same mean position (about 0.56 vs 0.55). [POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the optional segment formed by A19, A20 and A25 — earlier executed in roughly 30–40% of cases after A27 or A28, often between A28 and A29 — is no longer executed in the later window, while the remaining flow runs directly and near-deterministically from A27 through A28 to A29. Supporting signs: the participation collapse of the three activities, the successor profiles of A27 and A28 (each based on about 200 observed outgoing transitions per window) collapsing onto A28 and A29, the relation items showing the backbone now appearing without the three activities, and the lag items showing immediate adjacency where gaps existed before. Screening produced 27 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected without a change, so the pattern as a whole is not a sampling artefact; the many relation items describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]. The evidence cannot distinguish a genuine removal of A19, A20 and A25 from these activities still existing but simply never being reached in the later period, and their later-window behaviour rests on a single trace each.

Other differences: in the one later trace that still contains each of A19, A20 and A25, each activity shows a successor not observed earlier (A44, A08 and A42 respectively), and A19 and A25 sit later in the trace while A20 sits earlier; each of these observations rests on a single trace.
