### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A01 has taken over the role A04 previously played as the single optional predecessor of A03: earlier, about half of the traces executed A04 followed by A03 while A01 never appeared; later, about half execute A01 followed by A03 while A04 no longer appears, with A03 itself continuing in every trace. The change is shown directly by the relational and directional items — the predecessor of A03 switched from A04 (always A04→A03) to A01 (always A01→A03) — and it is consistent across the occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, POS001, POS002].

The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces: 11 items qualified against roughly 0.14 expected by chance across the 28 screened control-flow units [screening].

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the same underlying step is simply recorded under a new activity code in the later window, since the codes are anonymous; behaviourally, however, the branch preceding A03 runs at the same share of traces in both windows [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, REL003].

Other differences: the redistribution of outgoing transitions at A07 and A13, including transitions A07→A03 and A13→A07 that were not observed earlier; the evidence does not show whether this rerouting belongs to the same change or is independent of it [DIR003, DIR004].
