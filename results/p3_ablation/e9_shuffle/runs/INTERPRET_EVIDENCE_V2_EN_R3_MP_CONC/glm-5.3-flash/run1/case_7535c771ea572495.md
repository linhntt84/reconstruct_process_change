### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural reconfiguration of the control flow. In the later window, the closing segment A06 → A01 → A03 appears to have been replaced by A06 → A07: A01 and A03 are no longer observed in any trace, while A07 always follows A06 and ends the trace. In the same window, A05 appears to have been inserted between A02 and A06: the previously direct A02 → A06 transition (about 53 in 100 of A02's outgoing transitions) is no longer observed, A02 now leads to A05 in about half of its outgoing transitions, A05 always precedes A06, and the A02 → A06 step gap shifted from mostly one to mostly two. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL012, REL014, LAG001]

The screening produced 28 qualifying control-flow items where well under 1 would be expected without a change, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits produce; the key changes are observed directly (participation flips between 0% and 100% on full 200-trace windows, successor changes with full support), and the relational, positional and lag pictures agree. Limitations: zero participation only means an activity was not observed, so removal versus non-triggering of A01 and A03 cannot be distinguished; there is no outgoing profile for A04, so where exactly A05 attaches on the A04 route is not directly shown; and many items describe the same underlying changes, so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, LAG001]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 and A03 still occur in traces outside this sample, since zero participation only means "not observed" in the later window; explaining away A05 and A07 this way, however, would require a variant present in all 200 later traces and none of the 200 earlier ones. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Other differences: none beyond the rewiring above; notably, the share of A02's outgoing transitions going to A04 stayed similar (about 47 in 100 before, about 50 in 100 after) and A02's overall frequency is stable. [DIR002, REL007]
