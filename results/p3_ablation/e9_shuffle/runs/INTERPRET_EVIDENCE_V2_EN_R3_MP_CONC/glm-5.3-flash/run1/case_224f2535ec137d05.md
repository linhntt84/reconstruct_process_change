### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new closing step A01 was introduced after A16: in the earlier window, cases ended with A16 and A01 never appeared, whereas in the later window every case executes A16 and then A01 exactly once. This rests on direct and mutually consistent evidence — A01's participation rose from 0 to 200 of 200 traces with exactly one occurrence per case, the pair relation switched from "A16 only" to "A16 before A01" in all traces, and A16 now leads directly to A01 on every observed occasion, with the positional shifts matching this picture. Five qualifying items against roughly 0.12 expected by chance for 25 tested units is a count that chance cannot plausibly produce, though all five items describe the same underlying trace behaviour and are therefore not independent confirmations.
