### Observed differences

- Participation rose sharply for four activities: A36, A37 and A42 each ran in about 46 in 100 traces in the earlier window and in essentially all traces (about 995 in 1000) in the later window, and A27 rose from about 55 in 100 to all traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the previously optional block of A36, A37 and A42 became mandatory and was repositioned earlier in the flow. In the earlier window, roughly 55 in 100 traces ran A25, A26 and A28 before A27 and A31, while the remaining ~45 in 100 ran A25, A26 and A28 before A36, A37 and A42 (in mixed order) and A31, with the two paths mutually exclusive. In the later window, essentially every trace runs A25, then A36/A37/A42, then A26 and A28, then A27, then A31 — A25 now leads directly into the trio instead of into A28, and A27 always follows it. The signs are direct and consistent: participation of the trio and A27 rose from about 46–55 in 100 to nearly all traces, the pair relations changed from exclusivity to near-universal co-occurrence with a stable new order, the successor changes sit exactly at the block's entry and exit, and positions and step distances move as expected for an inserted block. The evidence volume itself is far beyond what an unchanged process produces (48 qualifying items against roughly 0.74 expected), so chance cannot explain the evidence as a whole. Limitations: the many agreeing items largely describe one underlying restructuring rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot rule out that the later pattern is a pre-existing rare variant that came to dominate the sample rather than a modified flow, since only aggregate windows are observed. Other differences: a handful of rare outgoing transitions (about 1 in 200 cases each) appear at several activities in the later window, and A25 and A31 shifted slightly earlier and later in position; these are minor and consistent with the same restructuring.
