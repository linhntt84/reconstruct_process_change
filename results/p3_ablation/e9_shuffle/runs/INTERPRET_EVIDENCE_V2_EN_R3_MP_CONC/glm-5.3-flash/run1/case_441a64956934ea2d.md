### Observed differences

- The two central activities swapped their occurrence profiles. A01 appeared in about 66 in 100 traces in the earlier window versus about 28 in 100 in the later window, with mean occurrences per trace falling from about 2.2 to about 0.6; A13 rose from about 23 in 100 traces to about 61, and from about 0.4 to about 2.1 occurrences per trace [OCC001, OCC002].
- Their typical positions exchanged: earlier, A01 sat in the first half of the case (median relative position about 0.44) and A13 near the end (about 0.82); in the later window A13 sits early (about 0.32) and A01 late (about 0.82). These are event positions, not clock times [POS001, POS002].
- Their connections swapped in both directions. Earlier, A01's outgoing transitions went mostly to A08 (about 48 in 100), A15 and A07, while A13 led only to A06; in the later window, A13 leads to A08 (about 45 in 100), A15 and A07, while A01 leads only to A06 [DIR001, DIR006]. Upstream, A02 and A04, which earlier sent about a quarter of their outgoing transitions to A01, now send about a fifth to a quarter to A13 instead [DIR002, DIR003]; A06 now leads to A01 (about 54 in 100) where it earlier led to A13 (about 47 in 100), with A14 remaining a successor in both windows [DIR004]; A07 and A15 now lead to A13 about half the time where they earlier led to A01 [DIR005, DIR007].
- Partnerships transferred from A01 to A13. A01 and A13 themselves rarely shared a trace in either window (about 17 in 100 both times) [REL006]; A08 co-occurred with A01 in about 66 in 100 traces earlier but about 18 later, while A08 with A13 rose from about 17 to about 61 [REL005, REL012]; A07, A15, A02 and A04 show the same transfer [REL004, REL007, REL001, REL002, REL011, REL013, REL008, REL009]. A01 is now always accompanied by A06, mirroring A13's earlier exclusive tie to A06 [REL003, REL010].
- Step distances moved the same way: ordered pairs reaching A01 from A02, A04, A07 or A15 became rare (11–15 later-window observations versus 68–86 earlier) and now occur only 4–5 positions apart [LAG003, LAG005, LAG007, LAG013], while the corresponding pairs reaching A13 became common (58–77 observations) and now occur 1–2 positions apart [LAG004, LAG006, LAG008, LAG014].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 and A13 exchanged their places in the flow: in the later window, A13 occupies the position A01 held earlier — early in the case, frequent and repeated, reached from A02 and A04 and followed by A07, A08 and A15 — while A01 occupies the late, rare position A13 held earlier, now tied to A06. The exchange is shown directly and consistently: the outgoing profiles of A01 and A13 are mirror images across the windows, their predecessors and typical positions transferred accordingly, and the co-occurrence and step-distance patterns moved with them [DIR001, DIR006, DIR002, DIR004, POS001, POS002, OCC001, OCC002, REL005, REL012, LAG003, LAG004]. With 38 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.46 expected for an unchanged process, and every item's difference exceeding about 995 in 1,000 random re-splits, sampling variation alone cannot account for the pattern as a whole.

Limitations: many of the 38 items describe the same underlying exchange and are not independent confirmations; the window-level aggregates cannot rule out that the two windows mix two pre-existing case variants in different proportions rather than one flow that was restructured; and the lag items about A01 rest on only 11–15 later-window observations, so the exact distances there are weakly grounded [LAG003, LAG005, LAG007, LAG013].
