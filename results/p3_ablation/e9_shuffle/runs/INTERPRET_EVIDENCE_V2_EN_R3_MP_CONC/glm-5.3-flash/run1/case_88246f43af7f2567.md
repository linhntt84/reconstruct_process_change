- The routing around A03 changed completely. In the earlier window, A02 and A04 led mostly to A03 (about 91% and 67% of their observed outgoing transitions), and A03 led mostly to A12 and A11 (about 45% each). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence directly supports a re-routing of control flow around A03: A03 moved from the branch where A02 and A04 fed into it and it fed into A12, to the branch where A16 feeds into it and it feeds into A10 (A11 remaining a successor in both windows). In the earlier window the chain ran A02/A04 → A03 → A12 with A12 at the end of the trace; in the later window A02 and A04 lead directly and immediately to A12, while A03 sits on the A16 branch between A16 and A10. This is shown by the successor changes of A02, A03, A04 and A16, by A03's co-occurrence partners flipping from A12/A02/A04 to A16/A10, and by the distance changes (A12 now adjacent to A02/A04; A10 now two or more steps after A16). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, POS001, POS003]

The volume of qualified evidence rules out chance as an explanation: 16 control-flow items qualified where about 0.21 would be expected if nothing had changed, and the items describe one coherent restructuring rather than scattered differences. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a single relocation of A03 from two separate rewirings (its removal from the A02/A04 branch plus insertion into the A16 branch), and the "only to A12" pattern for A02 and A04 rests on moderate supports (28 and 35 observed outgoing transitions in the later window), so rare alternative successors could go unobserved. The placement statements concern event order within traces, not clock time. [DIR001, DIR003, POS003]
