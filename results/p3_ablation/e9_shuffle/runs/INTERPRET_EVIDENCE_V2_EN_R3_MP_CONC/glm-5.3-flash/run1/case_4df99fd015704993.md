### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window restructures the cycle-and-exit region around A03, involving A02, A03, A12, A13 and A14: A02 sits between A03 and A13 — before, A03 led directly to A13 in about half of its outgoing transitions, whereas in the later window A03 typically hands to A02, A02 always precedes A13, and the A03-to-A13 gap is typically two positions instead of one; and A14 was dropped from the A03–A12 cycle — before, A12 always led to A14 and A14 back to A03, whereas in the later window A12 leads directly back to A03 and A14 is not observed at all, with the A03 and A12 repeat gaps shortening accordingly. This goes well beyond chance: 40 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.32 would be expected without change, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. The structural reading is shown directly by the successor changes at A03, A12, A02 and A14 and is consistent across the occurrence, position, relation and distance items — though these indicators largely reflect the same traces, so they represent consistency rather than independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, OCC002, OCC007, POS001, POS002, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: zero participation only means A14 was not observed in the later sample, so a persistence below the observed rate cannot be excluded; and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows draw different case mixes rather than a modified process definition, since both old variants shrank at the same time as traces containing none of their activities appeared.
