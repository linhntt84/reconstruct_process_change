### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change in which the repeating A03–A11 block that previously sat between A06 and its outcomes is no longer executed, with A06 now bypassing it and leading directly to A19 and A12, which consequently appear earlier in traces. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: A03 and A11 fell from 57 and 39 of 200 traces to 0 of 200; A06's successor profile flipped from about 86 in 100 transitions to A03 to about 58 to A19 and 37 to A12; all A03 co-occurrences vanished; and the earlier positions and shorter lags of A19 and A12 match the loss of the intervening block.
