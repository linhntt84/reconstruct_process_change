### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The screening produced 71 qualifying items where about 0.64 would be expected for an unchanged process, so this volume of difference cannot be explained by random re-sampling of the two windows [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, LAG010].

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured. Earlier, every case executed the three activities A21, A22 and A23 between A39 and A24 — all three in each case, in no fixed order; in the later window these activities are essentially no longer observed, and in almost every case exactly one of three previously unobserved activities, A14, A15 or A16, runs in that position, so A24 is reached two steps after A39 instead of four. This is a structural change in which activities disappeared and others appeared, not a shift in the shares of pre-existing paths.

The support is direct and consistent across indicators: participation flipped from 1.0 to about 0.005 for A21/A22/A23 and from 0 to about 0.30–0.37 for A14/A15/A16 [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]; A39's successors changed wholesale [DIR007]; the later-window chain A39 before one of A14/A15/A16 before A24 holds wherever these activities occur [REL007, REL015, REL022, REL006, REL014, REL021]; and the A39–A24 distance contracted from four to two positions [LAG010].

An important competing explanation remains: the evidence shows the new activities occupy the same position between A39 and A24 but cannot show that they fill exactly the same role the old block filled. In addition, one later trace still contains A21, A22 and A23, and the later-window profiles behind several direction, lag and position items for these activities rest on that single trace, so a small residual of the earlier behaviour cannot be excluded.

Other differences: A41's participation rose from about 59 to about 69 in 100 traces [REL030, REL035, REL039, REL009, REL017, REL024]; the small position shifts of A24 and A39 are consistent with the restructured segment rather than independent changes [POS007, POS008].
