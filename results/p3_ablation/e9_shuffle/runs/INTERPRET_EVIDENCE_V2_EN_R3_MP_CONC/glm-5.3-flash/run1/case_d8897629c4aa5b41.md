### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the segment ran A22 → A23 → A24 in every trace: A23 was the only observed successor of A22 and A24 the only successor of A23, A23 immediately followed A22 and A24 immediately followed A23, and A24 came two positions after A22. [DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports that in the later window an additional step is executed in nearly every trace inside the former A22 → A23 → A24 segment, taking the form of exactly one of three alternative activities — A15 (about 41 in 100 traces), A20 (about 30 in 100) or A14 (about 29 in 100) — inserted between A22 and A23 in about 56 in 100 traces and between A23 and A24 in about 44 in 100; in the earlier window the segment ran directly from A22 to A23 to A24 and none of the three was observed. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012]

The hypothesis is supported directly and consistently: participation rose from zero to 58–82 traces per activity, new outgoing edges appear from A22, A23 and each new activity, the relational items show a strict A22-before / A24-after envelope around each new activity with co-occurrence equal to its full participation, and the lag distributions shift by exactly one position in almost all traces. The volume of qualified evidence (27 control-flow items, where fewer than 1 would be expected without a change) also exceeds what random re-splits of an unchanged process produce. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, screening]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show what determines which of the three alternatives runs, nor whether the placement before versus after A23 follows a routing rule — the mixed order around A23 is equally consistent with two fixed placements and with an unordered execution of the new activity and A23 — and the exact alternative shares carry sampling uncertainty in 200-trace windows. [REL004, REL008, REL011]

Other differences: A22's mean relative position shifted slightly earlier (about 0.48 to 0.46), which follows from traces containing one more event rather than being an independent relocation. [POS004]
