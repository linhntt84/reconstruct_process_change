### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A26, A27 and A28 each ran in every one of the 200 traces; in the later window each appears in only 1–2 traces (about 1 in 100 cases). [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the always-executed segment made of A26, A27 and A28 — which ran between A44 and A29 in every earlier case, with A44 before it and A29 after it but no fixed order among the three — was replaced in the later window by a single pass through one of three activities, A19, A20 or A21: A44 now leads to one of them and that activity leads to A29, shortening the segment from three activities to one (the A44-to-A29 distance fell from 4 to 2 steps). A11 and A12, which previously fed the old segment, now precede the new activities. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL013, REL018, REL024, REL029, REL030, REL033, REL034, REL036, REL037, REL038, REL039, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, LAG010]

This is direct and consistent across occurrence, relation, direction and lag indicators: 70 items qualified where fewer than 1 would be expected without a change; the participation reversal rests on large supports (about 200 traces before versus 62–76 after for the new activities, and only 1–2 after for the old ones); A44's rerouting is observed with about 200 traces behind it in both windows; and the near-mutual exclusivity of A19, A20 and A21 matches a one-of-three choice. [DIR007, LAG010, REL013, REL014, REL020]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a modification of one process from a shift in the mix of two coexisting variants that the window boundary happens to separate; the 1–2 residual traces with A26, A27 and A28 leave open whether those activities were removed entirely or only rarely triggered; and the lag indicator uses event positions, not clock time. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, LAG010]

Other differences: A29's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (about 0.43 to 0.40) and A44's slightly later (about 0.31 to 0.33), consistent with the shorter segment between them; several direction, lag and position items for A26, A27 and A28 in the later window rest on only 1–2 traces and carry little weight.
