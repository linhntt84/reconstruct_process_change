### Observed differences

- The execution order between two groups of activities reversed. In the earlier window, A26 and A27 each ran before A35, A36 and A41 in about 98–99 in 100 traces; in the later window, A35, A36 and A41 each run before A26 and A27 in about 97–98 in 100 traces. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block formed by A35, A36 and A41 and the segment formed by A27 and A26 reversed their execution order, with A25 and A30 rewired around them. In the earlier window the flow ran A25 → A27, then A26 fed the block A35/A36/A41, which exited to A30; in the later window A25 feeds the block, the block exits to A27, and A26 connects directly to A30, so the block now runs before A27 and A26 while A30 follows A26 instead of the block. The internal transitions among A35, A36 and A41 are unchanged. Supporting signs: the reversal is near-deterministic in both directions with 100% co-occurrence, so participation and repetition are unchanged; the successor changes show the rerouting directly; positions and step distances agree in direction and size; every item's difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits; and 26 qualifying items against roughly 0.63 expected without change makes chance an implausible explanation of the pattern as a whole. Limitations: the six relation items are pairwise views of one reversal and the position, distance and direction items largely re-describe the same swap, so they are not independent confirmations; the after-profiles of the vanished adjacencies rest on only 1–2 traces; and the evidence cannot distinguish a reordering applied to all traces from a shift in the mix of two stable order-variants between the windows.
