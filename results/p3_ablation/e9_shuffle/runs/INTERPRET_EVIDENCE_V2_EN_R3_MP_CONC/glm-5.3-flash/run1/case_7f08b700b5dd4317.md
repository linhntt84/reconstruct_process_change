### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, six activities — A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 — each ran in every trace (100% participation, about one occurrence per trace). In the later window, A33, A35 and A36 each appear in about 47 in 100 traces, and A37, A38 and A39 in about 56 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

- The two groups of three became mutually exclusive. Earlier, every trace contained both groups, with A33/A35/A36 always before A37/A38/A39 (about 99 in 100). In the later window, the groups co-occur in only about 3 in 100 traces: about 44 in 100 traces contain only the first group, about 53 in 100 only the second, and virtually none contain neither group. [REL015, REL017, REL019, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024]

- Each group still runs as a block with an unfixed internal order in both windows: A33/A35/A36 co-occur whenever they appear (cooccurrence about 47 in 100 later, versus always earlier), A37/A38/A39 likewise (about 56 in 100 later), and within each group both orders occur in similar proportions in both windows. [REL013, REL014, REL018, REL025, REL026, REL027]

- The anchors are unchanged: A30 still precedes and A31 still follows whichever of the six activities run, and both keep 100% participation in both windows. [REL001, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL010, REL012, POS001, POS002]

- New direct transitions appear in the later window: A30 leads straight to A37, A38 or A39 in about half of its observed outgoing transitions (about 52 in 100, absent earlier), and A33, A35 and A36 each lead straight to A31 in about a quarter to a third of theirs (also absent earlier). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

- Step gaps shrink accordingly: the distance from A30 to A37/A38/A39 moves from 4–5 steps to 1–3 steps, and from A33/A35/A36 to A31 likewise; the few lag items spanning the two groups rest on only 4–5 later observations. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG009, LAG007, LAG011]

- Relative event positions shift consistently with shorter traces: A30 later (mean 0.62 to 0.67), A33/A35/A36 later (about 0.68 to 0.74), A39 and A31 slightly earlier; these are event positions, not clock times. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window the segment between A30 and A31 is executed as one of two exclusive three-activity alternatives instead of the full six-activity block. Earlier, every trace ran A33/A35/A36 (in either order) followed by A37/A38/A39 (in either order) between the mandatory anchors A30 and A31; in the later window, about 47 in 100 traces run only the first triple, which then exits directly to A31, and about 53 in 100 run only the second triple, entered directly from A30, while about 3 in 100 still run both and virtually none run neither. [REL015, REL017, REL022, REL025, REL026, REL027, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, OCC001, OCC004]

Supporting signs: the participation drops match the exclusivity pattern exactly; the bypass transitions (A30 into the second triple, first triple to A31) are observed directly in the successor profiles; the shortened step gaps and position shifts agree with the same structure; and the screening produced 54 qualifying items against fewer than one expected without change, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits produce. [DIR001, DIR003, LAG001, LAG004, POS001, POS003, OCC001, OCC004, REL015]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a single process with new exclusive routing from a later window that mixes two process variants; the small share of traces running both triples and the exact skip mechanism remain unresolved, and the cross-group lag items rest on only 4–5 observations. [REL015, LAG007, LAG011]

Other differences: a handful of low-frequency outgoing transitions to scattered activities (about 0.5–1 in 100 each) appear or disappear at A30, A33, A35 and A36. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
