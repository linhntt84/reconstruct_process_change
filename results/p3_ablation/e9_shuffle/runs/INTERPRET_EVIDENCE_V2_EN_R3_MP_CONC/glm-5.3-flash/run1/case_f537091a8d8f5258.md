### Observed differences

- The relative order of two groups of activities swapped. In the earlier window, A07 and A09 always ran before A05 and A06: A07 preceded A05 in about 48 in 100 traces and never followed it, and A09 likewise preceded A05 and A06 in about 48 in 100 traces each. In the later window this is reversed: A05 precedes A07 and A09 in about 55 in 100 traces each, A06 precedes A07 in about 41 in 100 (the old order survives only in about 14 in 100), and A06 precedes A09 in about 50 in 100. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]
- A05 and A06, which previously appeared in either order (A05 first in 25 in 100 traces, A06 first in 23 in 100), now run in a fixed order, A05 before A06 in about 55 in 100 traces and never the reverse. [REL001]
- Event positions moved accordingly: A05's median position fell from about 0.8 to 0.4 of the trace and A06's from 1.0 to 0.6, while A07's rose from 0.4 to 0.8 and A09's from 0.6 to 1.0; A04 stayed near the start (about 0.2) in both windows. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS001]
- Step distances agree with a new chain A01–A04–A05–A06–A07–A09: A01 to A05 narrowed from typically 4–5 steps to 1–2, and A04 to A05 from 3–4 steps to 1–2, while A04 to A07 widened from typically 1 step to 2–4 steps and A01 to A09 from about 3 steps to 4–5. [LAG002, LAG006, LAG008, LAG005, LAG007, LAG003]
- Successors changed in the same direction: A04 now leads mainly to A05 (about 51 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions, against about 1 in 100 before, when it led mainly to A07); A06 now leads mainly to A07 (about 76 in 100, against about 96 in 100 to A05 before); A09 now leads to A06 and A04 and no longer to A05. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR006]
- Each of these activities also appears in somewhat more traces in the later window, about 55 in 100 versus about 48 in 100 before. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001]
- A01 now leads directly to A05 in about 45 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions (about 1 in 100 before), and A05 follows A01 within one or two steps in most later traces, so A04 no longer always sits between them. [DIR001, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the execution order of two activity groups was exchanged: A05 and A06, previously executed after A07 and A09 near the end of the case, now run before them, so the typical order changed from A01 → A04 → A07 → A09 → (A05/A06 in either order) to roughly A01 → A04 → A05 → A06 → A07 → A09. The relational evidence shows this directly for all four cross-pairs, with the old order at or near zero in the later window, and the positional, distance and successor shifts all point the same way. The 28 qualifying items (all control-flow) are about a hundred times the number expected without a change (0.26 for 52 units), so sampling variation cannot explain the pattern as a whole, although most items describe one underlying reordering rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: a few items rest on small samples (A09's outgoing profile on 12 later traces, the A09–A06 distance on 10), and the earlier window's 96 traces with these activities cannot exclude that the "new" order existed as a rare variant before, even though it was not observed once. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG002, LAG006, LAG008, DIR002, DIR004, DIR006, screening]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows sample two pre-existing orderings at different frequencies rather than a single reordered behaviour; the complete absence of the new order among the earlier traces makes this unlikely but does not exclude it. [REL002, REL003]

Other differences: the whole group of activities (A04, A05, A06, A07, A09) participates in more cases in the later window (about 55 in 100 traces versus 48 in 100), and A04 is sometimes bypassed, with A01 leading straight to A05 in about 45 in 100 of its outgoing transitions versus about 1 in 100 before; occasional skips also appear inside the new chain (A05 directly to A07 in about 17 in 100 of A05's outgoing transitions, A06 directly to A09 in about 16 in 100). [POS001, POS005, REL001, DIR001, LAG002, DIR003, DIR004]
