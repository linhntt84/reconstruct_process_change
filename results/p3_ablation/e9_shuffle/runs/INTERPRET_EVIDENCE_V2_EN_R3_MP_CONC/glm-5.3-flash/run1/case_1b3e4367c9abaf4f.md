### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was added to the process and inserted into the A15/A16 cycle at the end: A01, not observed earlier, is executed once in every later trace near the end; A15 and A16, which previously led only to each other, now each lead to A01 in about half of their outgoing transitions, and A01 leads back into A15 or A16, with A01 co-occurring with both in every trace in both orders. The change is shown directly by participation (0 of 200 to 200 of 200 traces), position, and successor changes on both sides, and 58 evidence items qualified against roughly 0.53 expected by chance for the 107 tested units, so the overall difference count cannot be explained by random re-splits; the many relation items involving A01 describe the same underlying behaviour rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, REL008, REL009]

Limitations: absence in the earlier window only means A01 was not observed there, so a re-labelling of an existing activity under an anonymous code cannot be ruled out, and the exact number of times the A15/A16/A01 cycle runs per trace cannot be determined from these indicators.
