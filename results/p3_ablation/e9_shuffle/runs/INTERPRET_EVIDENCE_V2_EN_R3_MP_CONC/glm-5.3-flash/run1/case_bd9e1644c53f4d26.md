### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A02 did not occur in any of the 200 traces; in the later window it occurs in all 200, exactly once per case on average (mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, 100% participation). [OCC001]
- When it occurs, A02 sits in the early-to-middle part of the case: median normalized position about one third into the trace, with the middle half of its positions between roughly 0.17 and 0.50. [POS001]
- Earlier, A01, A03, A04 and A06 each appeared without A02 in 100% of traces; in the later window A02 co-occurs with each of them in essentially every trace, and the order between them is mixed rather than fixed: A02 precedes A04 in about 69 of 100 cases, while against A01, A03 and A06 the splits are near even (about 44–56 in 100 either way). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]
- The successor profiles changed accordingly: about 24% of A01's outgoing transitions and of A03's, and about 18% of A06's, now lead to A02 (previously none did), while A01's share leading to A04 fell from about 36% to 19%. A02 itself now leads to A04 (about 26%), A06 (about 25%), A01 (about 19%) and A03 (about 19%). [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR002]
- Existing activity pairs are spread farther apart: four-step gaps between A01 and A04, A03 and A04, and A06 and A04, absent earlier, now account for roughly 23%, 15% and 20% of observations, while one- to two-step gaps shrank (for A01–A04, from about 77% to 51% combined). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- The pre-existing activities themselves remained near-universal: A01, A03 and A06 still appear in about 94–97 of 100 traces in both windows, so no earlier activity was removed. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that activity A02 was added to the process and became mandatory: in the later window it runs exactly once in every case, typically in the first half of the trace, interleaved with the existing activities A01, A03, A04 and A06 without a fixed order, and it is woven into the control flow through new direct successions from A01, A03 and A06 into A02 and from A02 into A04, A06, A01 and A03. The wider event gaps between A01–A04, A03–A04 and A06–A04 are consistent with A02 sitting between existing activities. [OCC001, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The screening comparison supports this reading: 13 items qualified against roughly 0.14 expected without change in the control-flow family (29 units tested), many times the chance expectation, so the set as a whole cannot be explained by random sampling. The change is shown directly by participation, position, co-occurrence and successor evidence; note that these indicators largely re-describe the same trace-level behaviour, so they are consistent rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, POS001, REL001, DIR002]

An important competing explanation remains: zero participation in the earlier window only shows A02 was not observed there, so the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely new activity from one that already occurred at a very low rate (below the reach of the 200-trace sample) and later became universal. The mixed orders also show A02 has no single fixed slot, so the exact points at which it can appear cannot be pinned down. [OCC001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]
