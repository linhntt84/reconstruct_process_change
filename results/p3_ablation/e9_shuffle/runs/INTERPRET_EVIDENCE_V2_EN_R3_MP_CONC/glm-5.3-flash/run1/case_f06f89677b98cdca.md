### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window A01 runs as a step following A18 and taking over most of A18's onward flow: A01, unobserved in the earlier window, now executes once in about 48 in 100 traces, always after A18 and late in the trace, while A18's outgoing transitions concentrate on A01 (about 65 in 100, versus none earlier) and A01 continues into a similar spread of successors that A18 previously fed directly. [OCC001, POS001, REL001, DIR004, DIR001] The placement rests on direct relational and successor evidence rather than on frequencies alone; nine items qualified where roughly 0.2 would be expected by chance in an unchanged process, and each difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [REL001, DIR004, DIR001, OCC001, POS001, DIR002, DIR003, OCC002, OCC003] Limitations: the agreeing items largely describe one underlying behaviour rather than independent confirmations; non-observation in the earlier window shows absence from that sample, not that A01 could not occur; and positions are event positions, so no claim about clock-time placement is possible. [OCC001, POS001, REL001]
