### Observed differences

- Participation flipped for four activities: A01, A02 and A03 occur in every earlier trace (A03 about 2.1 times per trace) but in none of the 200 later traces, while A04 is absent earlier and now occurs once in every later trace, always at the last event position. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS001, POS004]
- The connections around the surviving flow changed: A08 previously always led to A03, and in the later window leads to A06 (about 46 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) or A07 (about 54 in 100); A05 previously always led to A02 and now always leads to A04. The outgoing edges of A02 (to A01) and of A03 (to A06/A07, about 53/47) are no longer observed, since A02 and A03 themselves no longer occur. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- Relations follow the same pattern: pairs such as A01–A02, A02–A03 and A01–A07 went from always co-occurring to never sharing a trace, pairs such as A01–A04 and A02–A04 flipped from "only the first activity" to "only the second", and A04 now co-occurs with A05 and A07 in every trace, with A07 and A05 preceding it. [REL001, REL008, REL006, REL003, REL009, REL019, REL021]
- The surviving activities sit closer together: the A06→A07 gap was always 3 event positions and is now mostly 2, A06→A05 went from 4 to mostly 3, A08→A05 from 3 to mostly 2, and A08→A06/A07 from 2 to mostly 1; A06 and A07 also occur earlier in the trace (A07 mean relative position about 0.48 before, 0.30 after). [LAG001, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, POS005, POS006]
- A06 and A08 appear slightly less often (about 55 in 100 earlier traces vs about 50 in 100 later), and their self-repetition gaps moved from a fixed 3 to a mix of 2 and 4. [REL018, REL020, REL022, LAG002, LAG007]
- For scale: 43 control-flow items qualified, against roughly 0.24 expected by chance in an unchanged process, and every item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled data. [screening]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the closing segment of the process was restructured: A03 (previously repeated, about 2.1 times per trace), A02 and the former final activity A01 are no longer executed, A08 now leads directly into A06 or A07 instead of into A03, A05 flows into A04 instead of A02, and A04 closes every later trace in the last position where A01 used to be. The roughly one-step shortening of all gaps between the surviving activities is consistent with the repeated A03 block being removed from the middle of the flow. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS004, LAG001, LAG003, LAG004]

Supporting signs: the change is shown directly rather than inferred — participation counts (100% to 0% for A01/A02/A03, 0% to 100% for A04), successor profiles at A08 and A05, and positions (A01 always last before, A04 always last after) each show it, with the REL and LAG items consistent across the board; the qualified-item count (43 vs about 0.24 expected) rules out chance as an explanation for the evidence as a whole. Note that the many agreeing items largely restate one underlying difference and are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS004, screening]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A01, A02 and A03 were not observed in the later sample, and the evidence cannot distinguish a restructuring at this point in time from a shift in which process variants the two windows sampled (for example, the later window containing only traces of a variant without the A03–A01 tail). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: the slightly lower participation of A06 and A08 (about 55 to about 50 in 100 traces) and their changed self-repetition gaps; these are small and may reflect sampling variation between the windows. [REL018, REL020, REL022, LAG002, LAG007]
