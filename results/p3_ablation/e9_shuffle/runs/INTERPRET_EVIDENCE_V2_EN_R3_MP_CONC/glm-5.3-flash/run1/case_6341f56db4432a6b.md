### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A01 appeared in about 103 of 200 traces (roughly 52%), always exactly once and always at the final relative position of the trace; in the later window it appears in none of the 200 traces. [OCC001, POS001]
- The ordered pair A12 → A01 disappeared: earlier, A12 and A01 always occurred together (about 52% of traces, always with A12 before A01); in the later window A12 occurs without A01 in about 51% of traces, and the two never co-occur. [REL001]
- A12 became the closing step of those traces: earlier, all 103 of its observed outgoing transitions led to A01, and its median relative position was about 0.86 with visible spread; in the later window A12 still appears in about 102 traces, but no outgoing transition from it is observed and it sits at the final relative position (1.0) in every case where it appears. [DIR003, POS002]
- Hand-overs away from A05 were redistributed: A07 earlier always went to A05, later split its transitions roughly half to A13 and half to A05; A13 earlier always went to A05, later went to A05 in about half of its transitions and newly to A07 (about 30%) and A08 (about 20%). [DIR002, DIR004]
- Routing into A10 became exclusive: A06 earlier split its transitions about 54% to A14 and 46% to A10, later sent all of them to A10; A14 earlier split about 54% to A10 and 46% to A15, later sent all to A10. These routing items rest on only about 24–71 observed transitions per window. [DIR001, DIR005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 is no longer executed and that A12, which previously led into A01 as the final step, now terminates those traces itself. In the earlier window roughly half of the cases (about 103 in 200) ended with A12 followed by A01; in the later window a nearly identical share (about 102 in 200) still contains A12, but as the final event, with no A01 anywhere in the trace. [REL001, OCC001, POS001, DIR003, POS002]

This is shown directly by four complementary views of the same behaviour — occurrence, position, successor and relation — and the screening context backs it: 9 items qualified against roughly 0.13 expected if nothing had changed, a gap chance cannot explain as a whole, and a fall from 103 of 200 traces to 0 of 200 is hard to attribute to sampling of a rare variant. [OCC001, POS001, DIR003, REL001, POS002]

Limitations: zero participation strictly means only that A01 was not observed in the later sample, so the evidence cannot fully exclude that A01 still runs in a very small share of cases; and because the agreeing items describe the same traces, they form one consistent picture rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, REL001]

Other differences: the routing shifts at A07, A13, A06 and A14 — new or exclusive hand-overs away from A05, A14 and A15, described above — are separate observations that are not part of this hypothesis, and their small transition counts leave room for sampling noise. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR001, DIR005]
