### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A03 became an active step in the later window, executed once in about half of the cases and inserted into the existing mid-process block: it follows A05's early segment (mostly after A05, sometimes interleaved), is woven between A08 and A12 in both orders, and always completes before A11. The insertion is shown directly by the new edges A08→A03, A12→A03 and A03→{A08, A11, A12}, by A03's participation rising from 0 to about 52 in 100 traces at a stable mid-early position, and by the one-step widening of previously adjacent pairs. All 17 qualifying items are control-flow items from a screening that expected about 0.17 without change across 35 tested units, and every difference exceeds 99.5 in 100 random re-splits, so the pattern as a whole is unlikely under sampling noise; note that the items describe one underlying difference, so they corroborate rather than independently confirm it. Limitations: zero earlier participation shows only that A03 was not observed before, not that it was newly introduced — a previously very rare variant that grew to half of the cases cannot be ruled out — and the evidence cannot show why only about half of the cases contain A03 (an optional step versus a path taken by half of the cases); the collapse of the A05→A11 distance to exactly 4 steps is also only partly explained by a simple one-step insertion.
