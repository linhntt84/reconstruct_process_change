### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained A36, A21 and A26, plus exactly one of three alternative activities: A14 in about 35 in 100 traces, A15 in about 30, and A20 in about 35; the three never occurred together and their shares sum to all traces [REL001, REL003, REL008, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]. A36 was followed by the chosen alternative (A20 about 35%, A15 about 30%, A14 about 35% of A36's outgoing transitions), that alternative was always followed by A21, and A21 sat two positions after A36 in every trace [DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001]. Where the chosen alternative co-occurred with A26, it preceded A26 [REL005, REL010, REL014].
- In the later window, A14, A15 and A20 are not observed in any of the 200 traces: participation fell from about 35, 30 and 35 in 100 traces to zero [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003]. A36 now leads directly to A21 in every trace, and the A36-to-A21 distance shrank from two positions to one [DIR004, LAG001].
- A21, A26 and A36 still appear in all traces in both windows; A21's mean position moved slightly earlier (about 0.39 to 0.37 of the trace) and A36's slightly later (about 0.32 to 0.33), consistent with one intermediate event being gone [POS004, POS005].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative step between A36 and A21 is no longer executed: in the earlier window every case ran exactly one of A14, A15 or A20 between A36 and A21 (about 35, 30 and 35 in 100 cases respectively), while in the later window none of the three is observed and A36 is followed directly by A21 in all traces. This is a disappearance of the branch rather than a shift of shares between the three alternatives, since the activities themselves dropped out entirely [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, LAG001, REL004, REL009, REL013].

Chance is an unlikely explanation for the evidence as a whole: 28 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.4 expected without a change, and the items agree across occurrence, direction, relation, position and distance indicators — although most of them describe this single underlying difference rather than independent changes [screening].

Limitation: zero participation only means the three activities were not observed; the evidence cannot distinguish removal of the branch from the activities being renamed or recorded outside the log, nor show whether their work was absorbed into A36 or A21 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].

Other differences: A19's trace participation rose modestly, from about 49 in 100 traces in the earlier window to about 56 in 100 in the later one; this is small, could reflect sampling variation, and is not part of the main hypothesis [REL002, REL007, REL012].
