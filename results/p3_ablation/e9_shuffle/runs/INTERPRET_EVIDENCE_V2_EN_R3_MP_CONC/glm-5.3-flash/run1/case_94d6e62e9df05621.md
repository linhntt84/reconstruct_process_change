### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A07 and A08 were inseparable: each appeared in about 53 in 100 traces, always together and never alone, and in both orders in similar shares (A07 before A08 in about 28 in 100 traces, A08 before A07 in about 25 in 100), so there was no fixed order between them. [REL001]

- A08's typical event position shifted slightly later (median from about two-thirds into the trace to the final position), but positions are event indices rather than clock times, and the shift coincides with A08 simply occurring in fewer traces. [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A07 and A08 changed from being executed together to being mutually exclusive alternatives: earlier, the roughly 53 in 100 traces containing either activity always contained both, in either order, each typically followed by the other; in the later window, about 51 in 100 traces still contain this part of the process, but never both activities — A07 alone in about 23 in 100 and A08 alone in about 29 in 100 — and the surviving activity now leads mainly to A01 (and sometimes A05) instead of to its former partner. [REL001, DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, OCC002]

Supporting signs: the relational evidence shows the decoupling directly (co-occurrence falling from 53 in 100 traces to zero across the 102 later traces containing either activity), the directional evidence shows the same successor change from both sides, and the participation drops are the exact flip side of the co-occurrence collapse; all six items show differences larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, and 6 qualifying items is many times the roughly 0.11 expected by chance among the 23 tested units, so chance as a whole cannot explain the pattern. [REL001, DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, OCC002, POS001]

Limitations: the later-window routing shares rest on only about 18 and 17 observed outgoing transitions, so the exact split between A01 and A05 is uncertain, and the evidence cannot show whether the direct A07–A08 link is fully gone or merely unobserved in this sample. The main competing explanation is sampling variation — a rare variant appearing only in the later window — which the item count (6 versus roughly 0.11 expected) makes unlikely in aggregate but cannot exclude for any single share. [DIR001, DIR002]

Other differences: none beyond the above; A08's slight positional shift (median from about two-thirds into the trace to the final position) is best read as a byproduct of its lower participation, since positions are event indices rather than clock times. [POS001]
