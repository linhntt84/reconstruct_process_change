### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening flagged 45 control-flow items where fewer than one would be expected by chance in an unchanged process, so sampling noise cannot account for the evidence as a whole. The main hypothesis is that, in the later window, the block of activities A35, A36 and A41 is executed after A26 and A27, whereas in the earlier window it was executed before them — an exchange of order between the two segments, with A25 now feeding A27 directly and A30 displaced to after the block (about four positions after A26 instead of immediately). The support is direct and consistent: all six order relations between the groups flipped almost completely while co-occurrence stayed at 100 in 100, the relative positions of all five activities moved in the matching directions, and the successor and adjacency changes (A25→A27 now immediate, the block leading to A30 instead of A27, A26→A30 delayed from immediate to about four steps) all agree with the same reordering. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, LAG002, LAG005]

Limitations: positions and lags use event order, not clock time, so no timing claim is made; the evidence establishes the order change itself but cannot distinguish a literal two-segment swap from a different rewiring of predecessors and successors that produces the same near-deterministic order. A few lag items resting on only 2–4 later-window observations were excluded from the support. [LAG003, LAG004, LAG007]
