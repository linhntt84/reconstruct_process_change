### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A21 appeared in every trace and A36 was immediately followed by A21 (a one-step gap in 100% of traces). In the later window this direct step has almost disappeared: A36's observed outgoing transitions lead to A14 in about 37 in 100 cases, to A20 in about 34, to A15 in about 29, and directly to A21 in only about 1 in 100. [DIR004, LAG001]

- A14, A15 and A20 were not observed at all in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces). In the later window each occurs once in roughly three in ten traces — A14 in about 37 in 100, A20 in about 34, A15 in about 29 — always around 35% of the way through the trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

- Each of the three new activities is always followed by A21 (its only observed successor) and is preceded by A36: A36 runs before A14 in about 37 in 100 traces, before A20 in about 34 and before A15 in about 29, and each of these activities in turn runs before A21 in the same shares. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL004, REL009, REL007, REL003, REL008, REL006]

- The three new activities never occur in the same trace: A14 and A15, for example, co-occur in no trace, and in about 35 in 100 traces neither appears — matching the traces in which A20, or the rare direct step, was taken instead. [REL001, REL002, REL005]

- A21 itself remains in every trace in both windows and shifts only slightly later, from about 0.37 to about 0.39 of the trace length on average. [POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, a new step is inserted between A36 and A21 in nearly every trace: exactly one of three alternative activities — A14 (about 37 in 100 traces), A20 (about 34) or A15 (about 29) — runs between A36 and A21, whereas previously A36 was always immediately followed by A21; the direct A36→A21 transition drops from every trace to about 1 in 100. [DIR004, LAG001, REL004, REL009, REL007, REL003, REL008, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

The hypothesis is shown directly by relational, directional and lag evidence rather than inferred from frequencies alone: A36 precedes each new activity, each new activity precedes A21 and has A21 as its only observed successor, and the A36→A21 distance moves from one to two event positions in about 99 in 100 traces. The pattern is internally consistent — the three alternatives are mutually exclusive and their shares plus the residual direct share sum to about one — and 21 qualifying items against well under one expected by chance makes a pure sampling explanation untenable. [REL001, REL002, REL005, DIR004, LAG001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL004, REL009, REL007, REL003, REL008, REL006]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window only means A14, A15 and A20 were not observed there, so the evidence cannot distinguish genuinely new activities from ones that existed but went unobserved; and the position results use event positions rather than clock time, so they corroborate the insertion but do not establish any shift in clock time. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]
