### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained the core activities A22, A23 and A24, plus exactly one of three alternative activities: A14 in about 32 in 100 traces, A15 in about 28, and A20 in about 40. The three alternatives never appeared together, so they formed an exclusive choice present in every single case [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006].
- In the earlier window the chosen alternative always came after A22 and before A24, but had no fixed order relative to A23: it sat directly after A22 in roughly 45 in 100 traces and directly after A23 in the remaining roughly 55. Accordingly, A22 and A23 were adjacent in about 55 in 100 traces, A23 and A24 in about 45 in 100, and A22 to A24 always spanned 3 steps [REL003, REL007, REL010, REL005, REL009, REL012, REL011, DIR004, LAG001, LAG003, LAG002].
- In the later window, A14, A15 and A20 are not observed at all — 0 of 200 traces each, versus 64, 56 and 80 traces before [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].
- Consequently the core now runs back-to-back: all of A22's observed outgoing transitions go to A23 (before, about 55 in 100 went to A23 and the rest to the chosen alternative), all of A23's go to A24 (before, about 45 in 100), A22→A23 and A23→A24 are adjacent in every trace, and A22 to A24 shortened from 3 steps to 2 in every trace [DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003, LAG002].
- All relations between the alternatives and the core collapsed to "only the core activity present": for example, A20 co-occurred with A23 in about 40 in 100 traces before and in 0 afterwards; the same pattern holds for A14 and A15 with A22, A23 and A24 [REL011, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL012].
- Two minor positional shifts accompany this: A24's relative position moved slightly earlier (mean about 0.57 to 0.55) and A22's slightly later (about 0.46 to 0.47), consistent with traces becoming shorter [POS005, POS004].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of three mutually exclusive activities A14, A15 and A20 — of which exactly one was executed in every case before, always between A22 and A24 on either side of A23 — is no longer executed, leaving A22 → A23 → A24 performed back-to-back in every observed trace. The screening produced 28 qualifying control-flow items where fewer than 1 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is not what a stable process typically yields; each difference is also larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data. The signs are direct and mutually consistent (participation of all three activities falling to zero, every core relation collapsing to "core activity only", both core activities narrowing to a single successor, and the step gaps closing exactly where the alternative used to sit), although many of these items describe the same underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL011].

Limitation: zero participation strictly means only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed in this sample of 200 traces, so the evidence cannot distinguish a complete removal of these steps from their being skipped in every case in this window, nor rule out reappearance under conditions absent from the later sample [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].
