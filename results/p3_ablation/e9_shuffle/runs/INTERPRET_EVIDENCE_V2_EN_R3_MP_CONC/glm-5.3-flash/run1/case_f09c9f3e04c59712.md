### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative block containing A14, A16 and A19 — exactly one of which executed in every case in both windows — changed its placement: before, it ran at the end of the case between A24 and A25; in the later window it runs at the start between A35 and A20, so the segment A15/A17/A18 → A20 → A24 now executes after the block rather than before it. This rests on direct relational evidence — complete order reversals in 100% of traces for every tested pair spanning the two segments — corroborated by successor changes (A14/A16/A19 now lead to A20 instead of A25; A35 and A24 now border the opposite segments), by event-distance changes equal to the block sizes, and by position shifts in matching directions. With 40 qualifying items against roughly 0.5 expected in an unchanged process, and every item's difference exceeding 99% of random re-splits, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. Limitations: positions and distances are event ranks, not clock times, and the evidence cannot show whether the mix of execution orders inside the {A15, A17, A18} trio — which had no fixed internal order in either window — changed alongside the relocation.
