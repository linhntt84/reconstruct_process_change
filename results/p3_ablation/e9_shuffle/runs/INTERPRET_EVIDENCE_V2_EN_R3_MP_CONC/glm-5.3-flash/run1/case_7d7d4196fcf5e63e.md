### Observed differences

- **A02 moved from a mid-process step to the opening activity and changed its successors.** Earlier, A02 appeared in about 49 in 100 cases, around the middle of the trace (median relative position about 0.51), roughly once per case, and every observed outgoing transition went to A01. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the routing around A02 was rerouted structurally: earlier, A02 ran in about half of the cases as a step inside a repeating five-step cycle A02 → A01 → A08 → A03 → A06 (with exits from A08 to A04 and from A06 to A07), always handing over to A01; in the later window, A02 opens every case and routes directly to A04 or A03, A03 hands over to A01 or A06, and A08 — now observed in only about 45 in 100 cases, late in the trace — returns to A02. Along with this, the activities around the old cycle (A01, A06, A07, A08) appear in far fewer cases, while A02 and A04 are present throughout. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL011, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC005, POS002, POS004]

The routing change is shown directly by the successor profiles of A02, A03, A06 and A08, the dissolved couplings by the relation profiles, and the occurrence and position shifts agree in direction; screening qualified 64 items where fewer than one (0.36) would be expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled data, so sampling variation alone cannot account for the difference between the windows. [DIR001, DIR004, REL001, REL011, OCC002, OCC005, POS002]

Limitations: the many items describe one underlying restructuring and are not independent confirmations, and POS and LAG are based on event positions, so "start" and the step distances refer to event order rather than clock time; the evidence also cannot rule out that the two windows simply draw different mixes of pre-existing process variants (for example a shifted case population) rather than reflecting a modified process. [POS002, LAG009, OCC002]

Other differences: A07, though about half as frequent, still receives about 47 in 100 of A06's outgoing transitions and now typically continues to A08 within about two steps, whereas earlier it re-entered the cycle at A02 within about two steps; A06 also now recurs two steps later in some cases, consistent with an A06–A07 back-and-forth before A08. [DIR003, LAG031, LAG028, LAG025]
