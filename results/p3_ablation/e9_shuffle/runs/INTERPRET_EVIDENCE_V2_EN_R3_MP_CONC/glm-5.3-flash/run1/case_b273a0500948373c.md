### Observed differences

- The two windows swap how often two groups of activities run. In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A21 each appeared in about 82 in 100 traces (163 of 200), while A16, A17 and A18 each appeared in about 18 in 100 (37 of 200). In the later window, A16, A17 and A18 each appeared in about 79 in 100 traces (158 of 200), and A19, A20 and A21 in about 21 in 100 (42 of 200); every activity still occurs at most once per trace in both windows. - A15 appears in every trace in both windows and always precedes the branch activities; its outgoing transitions flipped with the shares: before, about 82 in 100 led to A19, A20 or A21 (about 29, 24 and 29 in 100) and about 18 in 100 to A16, A17 or A18; in the later window about 79 in 100 led to A16, A17 or A18 (about 29, 22 and 29 in 100) and about 21 in 100 to A19, A20 or A21. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two existing alternative paths between A15 and A25 are taken with reversed frequencies: before, the path containing A19, A20 and A21 (always executed together, in varying order) was chosen in about 82 in 100 cases and the path containing A16, A17 and A18 in about 18 in 100; in the later window the shares reversed to about 79 in 100 for the A16–A18 path and about 21 in 100 for the A19–A21 path. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL009, DIR001]

Supporting signs: 34 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.5 expected if nothing had changed, and all items point the same way with the same size (difference 0.605 on their scale) and empirical strength of about 0.995, i.e. larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits; the participation change is measured directly with solid support in both windows (37 vs 158 traces for the rising branch, 163 vs 42 for the falling branch); both branches are well observed before and after, no activity was added, removed or became repeated, A15 still precedes both branches, and internal orders stay mixed — so bypassing, activities becoming optional or mandatory, and reordering are not what the data show. [OCC001, OCC004, REL001, REL007, DIR001]

Limitations: the evidence locates the difference at the choice between the two branches but cannot show whether the routing itself changed or whether a different mix of cases reached A15 from earlier, unobserved parts of the process; the exact shares also rest on finite samples of 200 traces per window. [OCC001, DIR001]
