### Observed differences

- Activity A02 shows a complete participation change: it appears in 0 of 200 traces in the earlier window, but in all 200 traces in the later window, exactly once per trace and never repeated [OCC001].
- The successor of A06 changed completely: in the earlier window, all observed outgoing transitions of A06 went to A01 (100 in 100); in the later window, they all go to A02 instead, and A02's transitions all go to A01 [DIR002, DIR001].
- The order relations agree: earlier, A01 always appeared without A02, and A06 always without A02; later, A02 co-occurs with both in every trace, always after A06 and before A01 [REL001, REL002].
- The distance from A06 to A01 grew from 1 step in all earlier cases to 2 steps in all later cases, meaning one activity now sits between them [LAG001].
- Event positions are consistent with an insertion: A02 sits at one fixed mid-trace position (about 0.67) in every later trace, while A06's mean position moved slightly earlier (0.60 to 0.50) and A01's slightly later (0.80 to 0.83) [POS002, POS003, POS001].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Nine control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.04 expected if nothing had changed, so chance cannot account for the set as a whole [DIR001, REL002, POS002, DIR002, REL001, POS001, OCC001, POS003, LAG001]. The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 was added as a new, mandatory step inserted between A06 and A01: in the earlier window the observed flow ran from A06 directly to A01, whereas in the later window every trace runs A06 → A02 → A01, with A02 executed exactly once per trace. This is shown directly by the successor change at A06, A02's sole successor being A01, the shift of the A06-to-A01 distance from one step to two, and the order profiles changing from "A01 without A02" and "A06 without A02" to full co-occurrence in a fixed order; the participation change (0 of 200 to 200 of 200 traces) shows A02 went from unobserved to universal [DIR002, DIR001, LAG001, REL001, REL002, OCC001].

Limitations: the items describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations; the evidence covers only the neighbourhood of A01, A02 and A06, so it cannot show whether anything else in the process changed or whether A02 took the place of an activity outside the observed neighbourhood. An important competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A02 existed earlier but was too rare to appear in the 200 earlier traces, since zero participation strictly means only "not observed"; its complete absence there alongside universal presence later makes pure sampling variation unlikely, however.
