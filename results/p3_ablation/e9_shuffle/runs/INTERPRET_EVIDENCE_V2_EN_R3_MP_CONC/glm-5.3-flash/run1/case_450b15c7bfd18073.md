### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the behaviour was uniform: A21, A22 and A23 each appeared in all 200 traces, A21's only observed outgoing transition led to A22, A22 always preceded A23, and the gap from A21 to A23 was always two steps. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, REL005, LAG001]
- In the later window, A22 appears in only about 51 in 100 traces (down from 200 of 200, still once per trace when present), while three activities that were not observed at all before — A27, A28 and A33 — each appear in about 49 in 100 traces, exactly once per trace when present. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A22 and the new activities are mutually exclusive in the later window: no trace contains A22 together with A27, A28 or A33; traces show either A22 (about 51 in 100) or the new activities (about 49 in 100). [REL006, REL007, REL008]
- In the traces without A22, A21 is followed by one of the new activities (A28 in about 18 in 100 of A21's outgoing transitions, A33 about 17, A27 about 15); all three new activities co-occur in the same traces in varying order (both directions are observed for each pair, roughly 22–27 in 100 each), the block always runs after A21 and before A23, and each of the three activities shows new outgoing transitions to the other two and to A23. [DIR001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- The A21-to-A23 distance, always two steps before, is two steps in about 51 in 100 later traces and four steps in about 49 in 100 — consistent with three activities standing between A21 and A23 in the traces without A22. [LAG001]
- The three new activities each appear at nearly the same relative position in the trace (medians about 0.48–0.50, with tight quartiles). [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- When A22 does occur in the later window, the original sequence is preserved: A21 still precedes A22 and A22 still precedes A23, and A21 and A23 themselves still occur in every trace. [REL001, REL005, DIR001, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window about half of the executions bypass A22: after A21, roughly 49 in 100 cases run a block containing all three of A27, A28 and A33 — in variable order rather than one fixed sequence — before reaching A23, while the remaining about 51 in 100 keep the original A21 → A22 → A23 path. The change is shown directly and consistently across occurrence, exclusivity, ordering, routing and distance indicators, and the volume of qualifying items (26 against about 0.4 expected without change, each difference larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits) rules out chance sampling as an explanation for the evidence set as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL002, REL006, REL009, REL012, LAG001]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window establishes only non-observation, so the evidence cannot rule out that this second behaviour already existed at a very low rate before and simply went unsampled in the earlier 200 traces; and because both orders are observed for every pair inside the block, the evidence supports a variable-order block rather than a fixed new sequence. [OCC002, REL012, REL013, REL014]

Other differences: none — every qualifying item describes this single A22-versus-(A27, A28, A33) alternative after A21. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL006, REL012, POS001, LAG001]
