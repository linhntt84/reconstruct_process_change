### Observed differences

- Activity A01 disappeared from execution: in the earlier window it appeared in every trace (participation 100 in 100, about once per trace); in the later window it was not observed in any of the 200 traces. [OCC001, POS001]
- Before, A01's outgoing traffic went almost entirely to A07 (about 99 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions); in the later window A01 has no observed outgoing transitions because it does not occur. [DIR001]
- Before, A01 always co-occurred with A05 (A05 came first in about 55 in 100 traces, interleaved or repeated in the rest) and always co-occurred with A07 with A01 first; in the later window A05 and A07 each occur without A01 in essentially all their traces. [REL001, REL002]
- A05's successors changed: before, about 51 in 100 of its outgoing transitions went to A01 and about 46 to A06; after, about 49 go directly to A07 and about 45 to A06 — the share that previously went to A01 now goes to A07. [DIR003]
- The spacing of the A05→A07 pair collapsed: before it was typically two positions apart (about 50 in 100 cases, consistent with A05→A01→A07); after it was typically immediately consecutive (about 40 in 100). [LAG001]
- Positions moved toward each other: A05's median position shifted slightly later (0.40 to 0.50 of the trace) and A07's slightly earlier (0.80 to 0.75). [POS002, POS003]
- A minor redistribution at A04: its small edge to A01 (about 3.5 in 100) is gone, its edge to A07 rose from about 1.5 to 7 in 100, and its edge to A05 fell from 92 to 87 in 100; the overall shift is small. [DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was no longer executed in the later window, and that the sequence A05 → A01 → A07 was replaced by a direct A05 → A07 connection. Before, A01 ran in every trace, was reached from A05 in about half of A05's outgoing transitions, and led almost always to A07; after, A01 is unobserved, A05 passes directly to A07 in about half of its outgoing transitions, the A05→A07 distance collapsed from typically two positions to immediate succession, and the positions of A05 and A07 moved toward each other, all consistent with the intermediate step being removed. The volume of qualifying evidence (10 items against roughly 0.08 expected without change) and its consistency across occurrence, direction, relation, distance and position indicators make a chance-sampling explanation unlikely as a whole. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, REL002, DIR003, LAG001, POS002, POS003]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A01 was not observed in the later window, so rare continued execution of A01 in a small share of cases cannot be excluded; direction shares are computed over outgoing transitions rather than cases, and the two windows are finite samples of 200 traces each. [OCC001, DIR003]

Other differences: the small redistribution of A04's outgoing transitions described above, which is consistent with the same removal of A01 but too small to characterise further. [DIR002]
