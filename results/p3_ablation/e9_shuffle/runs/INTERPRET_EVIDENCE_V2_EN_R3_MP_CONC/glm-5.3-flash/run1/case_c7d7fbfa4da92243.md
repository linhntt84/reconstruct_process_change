### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A10 was dropped from the flow: sequences that previously ran A04 → A10 → A12 now run A04 → A12 directly, and A10 is no longer observed. A10's participation fell from about 44 in 100 traces to zero, A04's dominant successor switched from A10 to A12, the A04→A12 distance shortened from mostly two steps to mostly one, and the former A04–A10 and A10–A12 co-occurrences were replaced by A04-only and A12-only behaviour. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, REL001, REL002]
