### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a specific possibility: the two activity blocks headed by A05 and A10 exchanged their roles in the process. Before, the A05 block (A05 with A08 and A09) was mandatory, ran late in each trace (relative position about 0.67) with A05 repeated about four times, while the A10 block (A10 with A16 and A17) was optional (about 47 in 100 traces), ran early (about 0.32) with A10 repeated about twice. In the later window these roles are reversed: the A10 block (now with A16 and A09) is mandatory, late and repeated about four times, while the A05 block (now with A17 and A08) is optional (about 50 in 100 traces), early and repeated about twice. The always-present activities A09, A11, A14 and A15 stayed mandatory but re-anchored from A05 to A10, with A06, A11 and A15 now feeding into A10 and A14 feeding into A05. [OCC001, OCC003, POS001, POS003, REL004, REL020, REL022, REL023, REL024, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

Supporting signs: 55 control-flow items qualified against fewer than 1 expected by chance if nothing had changed, so the set of differences is far beyond what an unchanged process produces, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits. Occurrence, position, relation, direction and lag evidence all describe the same reversal, including the exact containment flip between A05 and A10 and the 100% co-occurrence re-anchoring of A09, A11, A14 and A15. [OCC001, OCC003, POS001, POS003, REL004, REL020, DIR002]

Limitations: positions are event positions within traces, not clock times, so "early" and "late" refer to relative placement only; the many relation items reuse the same traces and therefore corroborate rather than independently confirm one another; and the evidence cannot distinguish one coordinated re-arrangement from two mirrored changes (A05 becoming optional and early while A10 becomes mandatory and late) that happen to align. [POS001, POS003, REL003, REL020, OCC001, OCC003]

Other differences: none that fall outside this reading; the remaining qualified items (small self-repetition gap changes for A05, A08, A10 and A16, and co-occurrence shifts among the satellites, e.g. A06 with A16 or A08 with A09) are consistent with the same block exchange. [LAG001, LAG003, LAG004, LAG009, REL012, REL013]
