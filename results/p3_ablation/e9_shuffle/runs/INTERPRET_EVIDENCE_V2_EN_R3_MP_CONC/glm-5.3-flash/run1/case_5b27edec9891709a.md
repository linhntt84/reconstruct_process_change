### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, each trace runs either A22 or the group A31/A32/A37 as alternatives — about 53 of 100 traces run A22 without the group and about 47 run the group without A22, where before every trace ran both — and that the group A31/A32/A37 now runs after A21 and before A26, whereas before it ran directly after A20 and before A23, with A20 now leading directly to A23. The signs are direct and consistent: the order between the group and A21/A23 flips completely between windows, A20's successor changes from the group to A23 while the pair distance collapses from four steps to one, A22 and the group become nearly mutually exclusive, and the group gains A26 as its exit while A26 now often occurs without A22. With 47 qualifying items against about 0.51 expected without change, chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole. The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows capture a different mix of two coexisting case types rather than one modified process, and a few distance items rest on a single later-window trace.
