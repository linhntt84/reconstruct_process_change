### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 took over the role previously held by A02: A02, which appeared in roughly half of the earlier cases always alongside A03, is no longer observed, while A01 now appears in roughly half of the later cases, always together with A03, at a similarly early trace position, with a nearly identical successor set and the same co-occurrence pattern with A06. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, REL002, REL004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL003, REL005] The support is direct and consistent across occurrence, positional, directional and relational indicators, and the 12 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.21 expected by chance for the 43 tested units, so chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A02 was not observed in the later window, and the evidence cannot establish that A01 and A02 are the same underlying step or that A01 occurs in exactly the cases where A02 used to; the replacement reading rests on the strong similarity of their profiles rather than on identity evidence. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is two independent changes — one activity dropped and a different one added — which would produce the same observations.

Other differences: beyond the A02-to-A01 edge swap, A03's remaining outgoing shares shifted slightly, with A10 rising from about 32% to about 37% of A03's outgoing transitions and A11 falling from about 24% to about 19%. [DIR003]
