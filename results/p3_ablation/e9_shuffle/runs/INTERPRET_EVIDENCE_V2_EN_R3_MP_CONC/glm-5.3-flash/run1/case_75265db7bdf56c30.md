The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was added as a new final step executed immediately after A03. In the earlier window, A01 was never observed while A03 sat at a single fixed position with no observed successor; in the later window, A01 runs exactly once per case, always after A03, at a constant position, and A03 now leads to A01 in every case [OCC001, POS001, REL001, DIR001]. The placement change is shown directly by relational and positional evidence rather than inferred from participation alone, and the five qualifying control-flow items far exceed the roughly 0.09 expected by chance alone (19 units tested), so the ensemble is not what an unchanged process typically produces [DIR001, POS001, REL001, POS002, OCC001].
