### Observed differences

- Before, A40 was always followed by one of three alternative activities — A14 (about 38% of its outgoing transitions), A20 (about 32%) or A15 (about 31%) — and each of these was in turn always followed by A21. In the later window, A40 leads directly to A21 in all observed cases, and the two are now adjacent instead of two steps apart. [DIR006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG004]
- The three alternatives moved from early to late in the case: their mean relative position was about 0.31–0.33 before and is about 0.59–0.60 in the later window, while their participation barely moved (A14 in about 38 in 100 traces before vs 31 after; A15 31 vs 33; A20 32 vs 37 — together still about one of the three per case in both windows). [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- Their successors changed: before, A14, A15 and A20 each always led to A21; in the later window they lead to A23 or A27 instead (A14 about 52%/48%, A15 about 54%/46%, A20 about 39% to A23 and 61% to A27). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- Their order relative to A21 and A22 reversed: before, each of A14/A15/A20 came before A21 and before A22 in every case where both occurred (about 31–38 in 100 traces); in the later window A21 comes before them (about 31–37 in 100) and A22 comes before them (about 31–37 in 100). - The alternatives now sit around A23 rather than long before it: before, A22 was always followed directly by A23 and A23 directly by A27; in the later window A22 leads to A23 in only about half of its outgoing transitions (otherwise to A14/A15/A20), A23 leads to A27 in about half (otherwise to A14/A15/A20), and the A22-to-A27 distance is always three steps instead of two. - Relative to A23 the alternatives now appear in both orders (A23 before A14 in about 16 in 100 traces and A14 before A23 in about 15; A23 before A20 in about 23 and A20 before A23 in about 15), whereas before, the alternatives always preceded A23. [REL003, REL008]
- A21, A22 and A23 still occur in every trace in both windows and each shifted slightly earlier (mean position about 0.35 to 0.32, 0.58 to 0.55, and 0.61 to 0.60). [POS004, POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the group of alternative activities A14, A15 and A20 — exactly one of which runs in each case — was relocated: before, it ran between A40 and A21 early in the case, while in the later window A40 leads directly to A21 and the alternatives run after A22, between A22 and A23/A27, in some cases before A23 and in others after it. [DIR006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL006, REL007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

The change is shown directly by all four relational and positional indicator types and is internally consistent: the successor profile of A40 changed completely, the positions of the three alternatives moved by roughly 0.27–0.29 of the trace, every pairwise order with A21 and A22 reversed, and the step-distance changes (A40 to A21 from two steps to one; A22 to A27 from two to three) match the group being removed from its old spot and inserted at the new one. All 24 qualifying items are control-flow items, against roughly 0.4 expected without change, and each difference exceeded 199 of 200 random re-splits; the items largely describe one underlying relocation rather than independent confirmations. [DIR006, LAG004, LAG002, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG001, LAG003, DIR004, DIR005]

Limitations: positions are event positions, not clock times, so this is a change in ordering within the case, not evidence about clock time; the new placement is not a single fixed slot, since the alternatives appear both before and after A23 in the later window. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window contains a mixture of two stable variants (the group before A23 in some cases, after it in others) rather than one new routing rule, and the aggregated windows cannot distinguish these; the directional shares also rest on outgoing-transition counts from about 61–76 traces per activity per window. [REL003, REL008, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003]
