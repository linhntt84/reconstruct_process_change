### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural reorganisation in which A05 is no longer executed and A01 is appended as a new closing step. Earlier, A05 ran in about 46 in 100 traces around the middle of the process, usually returning to A04 (the loop A04→A05→A04); in the later window A05 is not observed at all, and A04 instead repeats directly (A04→A04 in about half of its outgoing transitions, with A06 still following in about half). At the same time, A01 — previously unobserved — now runs exactly once per trace after A07, so traces end with A07→A01 instead of ending at A07. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL003, POS001, POS004]

Sixteen control-flow items qualified, far above the roughly 0.09 expected if nothing had changed, so the set as a whole is not what random re-splits of a stable process produce. The participation changes are observed directly (A01 from 0 to 200 of 200 traces; A05 from 93 to 0 of 200), and the ordering change is shown relationally — A07 before A01 in 100% of later traces, A07's only observed successor being A01 — not merely inferred from positions. The occurrence, relation, direction and position items describe one coherent picture, but they largely reflect the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [POS003, OCC001, REL001, REL005, POS001, POS002, REL003, DIR003, REL004, DIR002, POS004, REL002, OCC002, LAG002, LAG001, DIR001]

The evidence cannot distinguish a genuine removal of A05 (or addition of A01) from these activities simply not being executed in a finite 200-trace sample, since zero participation only means non-observation; it also cannot show whether the disappearance of the A05 loop and the appending of A01 were one modification or two independent ones. [OCC001, OCC002]

Other differences: A04's mean position moved slightly earlier (about 0.50 to 0.42 of the trace) with a narrower spread, and the spacing between A04 and A07 changed from mostly two steps to a broader mix of two and three steps; A02 remains a rare successor of A04 in both windows (about 1 in 100 of A04's outgoing transitions). These fit the structural change above rather than pointing to separate behaviour. [POS002, LAG002, DIR001]
