### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, about 52 in 100 traces (104 of 200) contained the fixed three-step sequence A05 → A06 → A04: every observed outgoing transition of A05 went to A06, every observed outgoing transition of A06 went to A04, and A04 always sat two positions after A05. The remaining ~48 in 100 traces contained none of these three activities. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, REL001, REL002]
- In the later window, A06 was not observed at all (0 of 200 traces, versus 104 of 200 before), while A05 and A04 still appeared in about 50 in 100 traces (101 of 200). [OCC001, POS003, POS002, POS001]
- In the later window, A05's only observed successor is now A04, and the A05-to-A04 distance is always one step instead of two: the two activities are now always adjacent. [DIR001, LAG001]
- A06 no longer co-occurs with A05 or with A04 in any trace; before, each pair always occurred together, in the fixed orders A05 before A06 and A06 before A04. In the later window each of A05 and A04 appears without its former partner in about 50 in 100 traces. [REL002, REL001]
- The positions of the surviving activities shifted consistently with one event disappearing between them: A05's mean position moved from 0.25 to about 0.33, and A04's from 0.75 to about 0.67. [POS002, POS001]
- The share of traces containing none of the three activities stayed essentially the same (about 48 in 100 before, about 50 in 100 after). [REL001, REL002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the step A06 between A05 and A04 was removed or bypassed: the branch sequence A05 → A06 → A04, which ran in about half the traces before, became the direct sequence A05 → A04 in the later window, and A06 is no longer observed. The change is shown directly by A05's successor switching from A06 to A04 and by A06's participation falling from about 52 in 100 traces to zero, and it is consistent across the relation, gap and position indicators; every difference is larger than in about 99 of 100 random re-splits of the pooled data. Nine control-flow items qualified in total, far above the fewer than 0.1 expected for an unchanged process, so the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by chance sampling. [DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, POS003, LAG001, REL001, REL002, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine removal of A06 from its work being merged into or relabelled as part of A05 or A04, since only control-flow information is available; strictly, zero participation means only that A06 was not observed in the later window, although with 200 traces and a prior participation of about 52 in 100, complete absence is hard to attribute to sampling alone. [OCC001, POS003]

Other differences: none — the roughly half of the traces that contain none of A04, A05 and A06 behaved the same in both windows. [REL001, REL002]
