### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural reorganisation around the block A01, A02, A07, A08, A10, A11, A14, A16: in the later window A02, A03 and A08 were no longer observed (previously about 47, 21 and 47 in 100 traces), the former chains A10 → A08 → A11 and A16 → A01 → A06/A07/A14 read A10 → A01 → A11 and A16 → A14/A06/A07, and A07 and A14 now run in far fewer traces (about 17 and 31 in 100 versus 47). The signs are direct and consistent: participation is measured over all 200 traces per window, the successor profiles of A01, A10 and A16 rest on roughly 165–168 outgoing transitions in both windows, the co-occurrence dissolution repeats across many activity pairs, and the position and gap shifts point the same way; the screening flagged 129 items where fewer than 1 would be expected in an unchanged process, so the ensemble is far beyond what chance produces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, DIR001, DIR008, DIR011, REL001, LAG022]

Limitations: zero participation only means the activity was not observed, so removal cannot be distinguished from a drastic drop in frequency; the many relation items share the same underlying traces and are not independent confirmations; and the evidence cannot show whether the rewiring was one coordinated change or several. An important competing explanation is that the later window draws mostly from a variant that never contained A02, A03 and A08 — implausible as pure sampling noise for the ensemble, but not excluded for any single activity. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004]

Other differences: A09 and A17 now largely follow one another (about 51% of A09's observed outgoing transitions go to A17 and about 49% of A17's to A09, whereas before both led mainly to A05 and A18), and A13 and A19, which previously led only to A03, now lead to A05 and A18 — consistent with A03 no longer being observed. [DIR007, DIR012, DIR009, DIR014]
