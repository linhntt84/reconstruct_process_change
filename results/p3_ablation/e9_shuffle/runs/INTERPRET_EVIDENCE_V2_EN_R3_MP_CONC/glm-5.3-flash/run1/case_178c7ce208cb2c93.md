### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added to the flow and is now executed once in every case, between the A02/A04 stage and A03. In the earlier window, cases reached A03 directly from A02 or A04 (which ran in either order, about 78 vs 22 in 100); in the later window, both A02 and A04 lead to A01, which always precedes A03, while A02, A03 and A04 still occur in every trace. The insertion is shown directly: the direct A02→A03 and A04→A03 transitions disappeared, A01's only observed successor is A03, A01 co-occurs with A02, A03 and A04 in every trace in a fixed order, and the A02→A03 and A04→A03 distances widened by exactly one step. The 11 qualifying items (all control-flow, from 12 units tested) far exceed the 0.06 expected without a change, though most items describe this one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, POS001].

An important competing explanation remains: A01 could be an existing activity that was relabelled or relocated from a part of the process not covered by the screening, rather than a genuinely new step; the evidence covers only these four activities and their pairs [OCC001, DIR001].

Other differences: none beyond what follows from the insertion — A02's earlier relative position (median about 0.50 to about 0.33) follows from A01 being added after it, and the mix between the two A02/A04 orders is essentially unchanged (about 78/22 earlier vs 76/24 later) [POS002, DIR002, DIR003].
