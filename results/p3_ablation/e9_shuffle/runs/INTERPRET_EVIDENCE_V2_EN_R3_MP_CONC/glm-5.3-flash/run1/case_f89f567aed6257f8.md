### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A14 was dropped from execution and A01 was introduced, with the surrounding control flow rerouted through A01. Earlier, A14 ran in every trace near the end, between A06 (which led to it in about 48 in 100 of its outgoing steps) and A03, with which it co-occurred in all traces in mixed order; later, A14 is unobserved. A01, unobserved earlier, now runs in about 56 in 100 traces around the middle, often repeated: A09, A13, A08, A10 and A17 now lead into A01 instead of into A11/A02 or among A06/A13/A09, A01 leads out to A11, A09, A05 and A02, A06 now leads only to A03, and A03 ends traces. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR009, DIR011, REL017, POS002]

The signs are direct and consistent across indicators: participation flips completely (A14 in 200 of 200 traces before, 0 after; A01 in 0 before, 112 after), successor profiles and co-occurrence relations change from zero to substantial and vice versa, and the screening produced 129 qualifying items where about 1 would qualify without a change, so the ensemble is not what an unchanged process typically yields — though many items re-express the same rerouting and are not independent confirmations. The evidence cannot show that A01 took over A14's role; it establishes one activity stopping and another starting with rewired edges, and it cannot rule out that the two windows sample different process variants rather than one modified process. Strictly, A01's earlier absence is only non-observation, although over 200 traces a 56%-participation activity going unseen would be a very unusual sample. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR006]

Other differences: A05 participates less and repeats less, and its connections rerouted — A11 now leads to A16 instead of A05, and A05 leads to A13 instead of A16 [OCC002, DIR003, DIR008]. A16 and A11 appear in somewhat more traces (about 62→70 in 100) [POS007, POS005].
