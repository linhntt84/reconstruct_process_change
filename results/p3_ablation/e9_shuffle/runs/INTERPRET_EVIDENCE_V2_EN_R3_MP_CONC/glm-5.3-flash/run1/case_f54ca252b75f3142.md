### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the process no longer executes its opening activity A01 nor the A04 step: A01, which appeared in every earlier trace (200 of 200), and A04, present in about 51 in 100 earlier traces, are unobserved in all 200 later traces. Traces now begin directly with A02 (about 47 in 100) or A03 (about 53 in 100) — the same branch activities that previously followed A01 — and A02 leads only to A06, which now ends the trace instead of occasionally returning control to A04 or A01. Because A01 and A04 are absent from the later window, this is a structural difference (an opening activity and a branch no longer executed), not a re-weighting of paths that all remain observed. [OCC001, OCC002, POS002, POS003, DIR002, DIR004, REL005]

The screening context makes chance an unlikely explanation: 15 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.1 would be expected without a change, and the occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators all describe the same difference (A01 participation 100% to 0%, A04 51% to 0%, A02 and A03 moving to first position, A02→A06 from 79% to 100%, all A01 relations falling from 49–51% to 0%). [OCC001, OCC002, POS002, POS003, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003]

Limitations: zero participation establishes only that A01 and A04 were not observed, not that they were removed from the underlying process — they may still exist but be skipped in every later case. The evidence also does not describe what follows A03 in the later window (the qualified directional items cover only A01, A02, A04 and A06), and the rare edge from A01 to A05 (about 3 in 100 of A01's outgoing transitions earlier) is uncharacterised for the later window.
