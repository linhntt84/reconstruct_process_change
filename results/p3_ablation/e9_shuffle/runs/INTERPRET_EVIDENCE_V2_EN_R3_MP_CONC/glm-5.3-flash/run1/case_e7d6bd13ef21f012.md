### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the routing between A02 and A05 was rerouted: in the earlier window A04 ran once in every case between A02 and A05, while in the later window A04 is not observed at all and A03 runs once in every case in that slot, so the earlier segment A02 → A04 → A05 appears as A02 → A03 → A05. The support is direct and consistent across indicator types: trace participation flipped from 100% to 0% for A04 and from 0% to 100% for A03, A02's single observed outgoing transition switched from A04 to A03, A03 now leads to A05, and every pairwise relation of A04 and A03 with A02, A05 and A06 moved in the same direction, with each item based on all 200 traces per window. The number of qualifying items (24 against roughly 0.12 expected without change) makes chance sampling of rare variants implausible as an explanation of the pattern as a whole. An important competing explanation remains: the evidence cannot rule out that both backbones already existed as variants before the boundary and only their shares shifted completely (from all to none and none to all), and zero participation only means an activity was not observed in that window.
