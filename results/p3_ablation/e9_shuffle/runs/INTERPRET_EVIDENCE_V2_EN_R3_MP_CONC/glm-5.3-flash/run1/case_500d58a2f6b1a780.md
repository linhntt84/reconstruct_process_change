### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the repetition of the block involving A25, A26 and A31 stopped: in the earlier window these activities typically ran several times per trace, with A31 looping back to A25 in most cases, while in the later window each runs once, A31 proceeds to A20 instead of back to A25, and A25 precedes A31 at a fixed short distance. The signs agree across occurrence, routing and relational indicators — the matching occurrence drop for all three activities with participation unchanged, the near-disappearance of the A31→A25 transition, the shift from interleaved/repeated to a fixed A25-before-A31 order, and the A25→A31 pair concentrating at lag two — and the nine qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.3 expected by chance for the 61 tested units. Limitations: these items describe one underlying difference and are not independent confirmations; A26's involvement rests only on its occurrence counts, as no relational or routing item covers it; a residual repeated pattern survives in about 1 in 200 later traces; and the evidence cannot distinguish a removed repetition from a strong shift toward a previously rare single-pass variant. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001, LAG001, LAG002]

Other differences: the position distributions of A25 and A31 kept their location but became markedly tighter (standard deviation roughly halved), which is consistent with fewer repetitions rather than a relocation of these activities. [POS001, POS002]
