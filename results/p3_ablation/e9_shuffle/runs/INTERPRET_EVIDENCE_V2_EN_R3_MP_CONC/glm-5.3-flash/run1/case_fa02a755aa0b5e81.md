### Observed differences

- The fixed order between A01 and A11/A12 reversed: before, A11 and A12 each executed before A01 in about 27% of traces (and never the reverse); in the later window, A01 executes before A11 and before A12 in about 22% of traces each, never the reverse. [REL002, REL003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the execution order of the segment involving A01, A06, A11, A12 and A05 was rearranged: in the earlier window A11 and A12 executed before A01 and A01 immediately preceded A05, while in the later window A01 executes at the start and leads to A06, A11 and A12 execute late and lead to each other and directly to A05, and A05 follows A01 only after about four steps [REL002, REL003, DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG004, LAG005, POS001, POS005, POS006, POS003, LAG002]. The order change is observed directly — co-occurring A01/A11 and A01/A12 traces went from exclusively "A11/A12 first" (about 27% of traces) to exclusively "A01 first" (about 22%) at nearly unchanged co-occurrence levels — and it agrees with the successor changes and position shifts; with 25 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected without change, and every item larger than about 99.5% of random re-splits, sampling differences alone cannot explain the pattern [REL002, REL003, DIR001, POS001]. The evidence shows pairwise order, successors and event positions rather than the full control flow, so it cannot distinguish a reordering within existing variants from a replacement of whole variants, and event positions do not translate into clock-time movement [REL002, POS001].

Other differences: A02, present in about 27% of earlier traces (53 of 200), appears in no later trace and shows no outgoing transitions or co-occurrences; A07 consequently reroutes from A02 to A03 and A13 and occurs earlier; A05 appears without A02 in about 46% of traces, up from about 27% [OCC001, DIR002, DIR003, POS004, REL004]. Zero participation only means A02 was not observed in the later window; the evidence cannot distinguish complete removal from a drop to a level too low to sample.
