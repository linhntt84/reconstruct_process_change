### Observed differences

- The activities A19, A20 and A25 occur in broadly similar shares of traces in both windows (A19 in about 38 in 100 traces earlier vs 31 later; A20 32 vs 34; A25 32 vs 38), but they execute in a different part of the trace: their mean relative position moves from about 0.31–0.34 (first third) to about 0.58–0.59 (last third). [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- Their order relative to A26 and A27 reverses. Earlier, A19, A20 and A25 each preceded A26 in roughly 30–37 in 100 traces and virtually never followed it; in the later window each follows A26 in roughly 30–36 in 100 and virtually never precedes it. The same reversal holds against A27 (earlier about 31–38 in 100 preceding, later about 30–36 following). [REL001, REL004, REL006, REL002, REL005, REL007]
- Against A28, an earlier fixed order (A19 or A25 first, about 31–37 in 100, never the reverse) becomes mixed: later A19 precedes A28 in about 16 and follows it in about 15 in 100, and A25 precedes in about 15 and follows in about 22. [REL003, REL008]
- Successors change on both sides. Earlier, 89–98% of the observed outgoing transitions of A19, A20 and A25 went to A26; later they go mainly to A28 and A32 (about 38–58% each) and no longer to A26. Conversely, A27 earlier led almost only to A28 (98%), later in about half of its transitions to A20, A19 or A25; A28 earlier led almost only to A32 (94%), later in about half to A25, A20 or A19. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- A45 rewires to A26: earlier its transitions spread over A19, A20 and A25 (about 26–31% each) with A26 reached two steps later; later about 89% of its transitions go to A26 at distance one. [DIR006, LAG008]
- Distances in the later segment stretch: A27 reaches A32 three steps later in 97 of 100 observations instead of two steps in 95; A27→A28 and A28→A32, earlier almost always at distance one, later split about evenly between one and two steps. A26 and A27 themselves occur slightly earlier (mean position down about 0.03 each). [LAG005, LAG004, LAG007, POS004, POS005]
- A few new adjacencies (A20→A28, A20→A32, A28→A20 at distance one) appear only in the later window, but each has only one supporting observation in the earlier window; likewise the earlier A25→A26 adjacency at distance one (63 observations) is later represented by a single distance-five observation. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG006, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the group of activities A19, A20 and A25 relocated within the process: earlier it executed in the first third of the trace, between A45 and A26, with each member typically followed by A26; in the later window it executes in the last third, after A26 and A27 and interleaved with A28, with its members typically followed by A28 or A32, while A45 now leads directly into A26. Supporting signs: near-complete order reversals against A26 and A27 with stable participation shares, position shifts of about a quarter of the trace length, coherent successor changes on both sides of the group, and distance changes consistent with the group now sitting between A27/A28 and A32; overall, 27 evidence items qualified against fewer than one expected without any change. [REL001, REL004, REL006, REL002, REL005, REL007, REL003, REL008, POS001, POS002, POS003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG008, LAG005, LAG004, LAG007]

Limitations: many items describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations; the exact arrangement in the later window — the internal order of the group and its placement around A28, where mixed orders occur — is not fully determined, and the new adjacencies involving A20 rest on a single earlier observation. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the group was not displaced as one sequential block but is now interleaved with A27 and A28 in varying orders.
