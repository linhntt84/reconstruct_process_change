### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, execution concentrates on repeated A09: A09 runs about 4.3 times per trace (before about 1.9) with self-repetitions clustered at the shortest distance, and its predecessors A13 and A15 now lead into it in about 71 and 95 in 100 of their outgoing transitions, while A15's transition to A01 is no longer observed. The signs are direct and consistent across occurrence, routing, lag, relation and position indicators; 29 control-flow items qualified against fewer than 1 expected by chance (0.43), so the ensemble is not what an unchanged process typically produces; every involved activity is well observed in both windows (A09 in 62 versus 68 in 100 traces, A13 and A15 with several hundred outgoing transitions in each), which rules out empty-profile artifacts; and every item exceeds roughly 99.5 in 100 random re-splits. Limitations: each window holds only 200 traces, direction shares are computed over outgoing transitions rather than cases, and an edge dropping out of the sample (A15 to A01) is not proof that it was removed [OCC002, LAG007, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, DIR001].

Other differences: A03 and A14 stopped repeating (repetition in about 28 of 100 traces before, none after) and appear earlier; A11 occurs in more traces (28 to 47 in 100), much earlier, and now precedes A03 and A14 in a fixed order where previously the three interleaved without a fixed order; A14 is typically followed by A03 within one step and A03 by A11 within one to two steps; A14 now routes mainly to A12 instead of A11, and A06 leads almost exclusively to A01 [OCC001, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL004, LAG010, LAG005, DIR005, DIR002].

An important competing explanation cannot be ruled out: a shifted mix of pre-existing trace variants — for example more cases of a variant that repeats A09 and does not repeat A03 or A14 — would produce the same occurrence, routing and ordering differences without any modification to the process structure [OCC002, OCC001, OCC003].
