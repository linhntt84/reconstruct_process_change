### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new closing stage was appended to the process in the later window: traces end with the previously unobserved activity A02 (about 57 in 100 traces, always last), into which the formerly terminal activities A16 and A19 now flow, and A09 now flows into A02 instead of A14; in about 15 in 100 traces a new activity A01 is inserted before A16, forming the chain A01 → A16 → A02, with A17 and A10 rerouted from A16 to A01 in roughly half of their outgoing transitions. [OCC002, POS002, DIR006, DIR008, DIR004, OCC001, DIR001, REL001, REL002, DIR007, DIR005]

The placement is shown directly rather than inferred: successor evidence shows A16, A19 and A09 leading into A02 and A01 leading to A16 in essentially all observed transitions; position evidence shows A02 at the trace end and A16/A19 moving from the end to about position 0.85; relation evidence shows A02 co-occurring with seven activities that previously ran without it, always after them. The picture is consistent across occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators, though many items describe the same insertion and are not independent confirmations, and the after-window routing shares rest on moderate counts (about 30–76 outgoing transitions). An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is relabelling: zero participation in the earlier window only shows A02 and A01 were not observed, not that they are genuinely new rather than renamed activities. [POS007, POS008, DIR006, DIR004, REL008, REL003]

Other differences: A14 now runs earlier at similar participation, A13 and A09 occur less often and later, A07 now leads to A14 rather than A09, A03 sometimes leads to A09, and the frequent co-occurrence of A09–A14, A13–A14 and A09–A19 largely disappeared; these are consistent with a reordering around A14 and a reduced A09/A13 segment, but are reported separately from the main hypothesis. [POS006, OCC004, OCC003, DIR003, REL011, REL013, REL012]
