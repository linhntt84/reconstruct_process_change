### Observed differences

- Before, A08 led almost exclusively to A10 (about 50% of its outgoing transitions) and A11 (about 48%); in the later window its outgoing flow is spread over A06 (about 29%), A10 (about 27%), A07 (about 24%) and A11 (about 19%). [DIR001]
- Before, A10 led to A06 and A07 in near-equal shares (about 51%/49%) and A11 led mostly to A06 (about 58%) then A07 (about 42%); in the later window both A10 and A11 lead only back to A08. [DIR002, DIR003]
- Pair distances agree with this rewiring: A08→A06 and A08→A07 moved from a peak at two positions apart (about 0.23 and 0.41) to a peak at one position (about 0.13 and 0.19), while A10→A06, A10→A07 and A11→A06 moved from typically immediate to typically two positions apart, as if A08 now lies between them. [LAG007, LAG008, LAG012, LAG013, LAG015]
- Immediate A10→A08 and A11→A08 pairs, not observed before, appear in the later window (about 0.13 and 0.21 at one position). [LAG014, LAG016]
- A08 was busier in the later window: its observed outgoing transitions rose from about 123 to 203, and the A08→A08 pair within five steps appeared in about 42 traces versus 21 before. [DIR001, LAG009]
- Pairs among A06 and A07 that previously concentrated on one distance now spread over several (e.g., A06→A07 from about 0.74 at three positions to a spread over two to five). [LAG001, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the routing after A08 was rewired. Before, A08 was followed by A10 or A11, which in turn led to A06 or A07; in the later window, A06 and A07 follow A08 directly, while A10 and A11 lead back into A08 rather than into A06/A07, and A08 is revisited more often (A08→A08 within five steps in 42 versus 21 traces). The successor changes are observed directly on substantial samples — A10 with 63 outgoing transitions before, all to A06/A07, versus 55 after, all to A08; A11 with 60 versus 39 showing the same pattern; A08 gaining A06/A07 as direct successors — and the distance distributions shift in exactly the matching direction item by item. With 20 control-flow items against about 0.24 expected without change, chance sampling cannot account for the set as a whole. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG007, LAG008, LAG012, LAG013, LAG015, LAG014, LAG016, LAG009]

Limitations: the LAG items largely re-express the same rewiring shown by the DIR items and are not independent confirmations, and several items rest on few observations (e.g., A07→A06 on 15 versus 7 traces, A07→A07 on 12 versus 6). An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window mixes traces of the old and the new routing rather than replacing one with the other, since only aggregate pair and successor distributions are available. [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

Other differences: the internal distances among A06 and A07 spread from a single dominant value to several (A06→A06 from a peak at three positions to a spread over two to five; A07→A07 and A07→A06 from five positions to four and five), which the rewiring around A08 does not by itself explain. [LAG001, LAG004, LAG005]
