### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the branch containing A02 — which ran together with A03 and A14 in about 49 of 100 earlier traces — was removed from execution, and that its surrounding flows were rerouted: A02 is no longer observed in any of 200 later traces, A03 and A14 now run in only about 19 of 100 traces and later in the case, and transitions that previously fed A02 or A03 now continue to A10, A09, A08 and A13, with A10 executed in about 46 of 100 traces (up from 24) and earlier in the case. Supporting signs: the disappearance is shown directly by participation, position and successor profiles (97 of 200 traces and 202 observed outgoing transitions before, none after); the co-occurrence with A02 drops to zero for all nine partner activities while those partners continue without it; and six source activities consistently redirect away from A02/A03. The screening produced 60 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected without change, so sampling alone cannot explain the pattern as a whole.
