### Observed differences

- A01 was not observed in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it occurs exactly once in every trace (200 of 200, never repeated), at an early position — typically the second event (median ~0.2 of the trace). [OCC001, POS001]
- A01 is wired in directly after A02: A02 precedes A01 in all later traces (the two never co-occurred before), A02's outgoing transitions now go to A01 in about half of the cases (before, ~87% went straight to A04), and A04 now sends ~39% of its outgoing transitions to A01, which never happened before. A01's own outgoing transitions go mainly to A04 (~47%), A07 (~17%), A03 (~16%) and A10 (~10%). [REL001, DIR002, LAG002, DIR003, DIR001]
- A01 precedes the downstream activities: where A03, A06, A07 or A10 occur in the later window, A01 comes first in nearly every such trace (e.g. A01 before A03 in ~46 of 100 traces, versus never in the earlier window). [REL002, REL004, REL005, REL007]
- Everything downstream of the insertion point sits about one step later: the A02→A04 distance split from ~87% immediate to ~44% immediate / ~47% with one activity in between; A02→A03 and A02→A10 moved from typically 2–3 steps to 3–4; A02→A06 from 4 to 5 steps; A02→A07 from 2 to 3. Positions of A03, A10, A07 and A04 shifted later by ~0.1–0.2 of the trace. [LAG002, LAG001, LAG005, LAG003, LAG004, POS002, POS007, POS005, POS003]
- A09 disappeared: it occurred in 45% of earlier traces (always as the last event, median position 1.0) and in none of the later traces. Before, A06's only outgoing transition led to A09, and A02, A03, A04, A06 and A10 each preceded A09 in ~45% of traces; A07 and A09 were strict alternatives that never occurred together. [OCC002, POS006, DIR004, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL013, REL012]
- A06 now ends the affected traces: its median position moved from 0.8 to 1.0, and only 3 outgoing transitions from A06 were observed in the later window (vs ~90 before), so its new routing profile rests on very few observations. [POS004, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which a new activity, A01, was added and executed exactly once per case, inserted early in the process between A02 and the downstream activities (A03, A04, A06, A07, A10): in the earlier window A02 led almost always directly to A04 and A01 was never observed; in the later window A02 precedes A01 in every trace, A01 routes onward mainly to A04, A07, A03 and A10, and all activities downstream of the insertion point occur about one event later, with lags growing by roughly one step and positions by ~0.1–0.2 of the trace. [OCC001, POS001, REL001, DIR002, DIR001, LAG002, LAG001, LAG005, LAG003, POS002, POS007]

The support is direct and consistent: the participation change for A01 is 0 of 200 versus 200 of 200 traces with exactly one occurrence each; new edges into and out of A01 appear directly in the direction profiles; several relational items independently place A01 after A02 and before A03/A06/A07/A10; and the lag and position items all shift in the same direction, as expected when one event is inserted. The number of qualifying items (34, against roughly 0.28 expected for an unchanged process) also rules out sampling variation as an overall explanation. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL007, LAG002]

Limitations: the insertion point relative to A04 varies (A01 precedes A04 in ~55 of 100 later traces, A04 precedes A01 in ~45), so the evidence shows an early insertion with a variable slot rather than one fixed position; and the evidence cannot rule out that the windows straddle a gradual transition, or that A01 is a relabelled existing activity rather than a genuinely new one. [REL003, DIR003]

Other differences: A09, previously the final activity in about 45 of 100 cases (always reached after A06, whose only outgoing transition led to it), is no longer observed at all, and the affected traces now end at A06; A07 and A09, previously strict alternatives, no longer relate because A09 is unobserved. The later-window routing profile for A06 rests on only 3 observed outgoing transitions and should be treated as weak. [OCC002, POS006, DIR004, REL012, POS004]
