### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the step in which A03 followed A02 no longer executes: A03, which previously ran after A02 in every single case, is not observed in the later window, and traces now terminate with A02. The support is direct and consistent across indicators: A03's participation fell from 200 of 200 traces to none [OCC001, POS002]; the A02→A03 transition, previously A02's only outgoing transition, is no longer observed and A02 now has no outgoing transitions, i.e. it terminates traces [DIR001]; the pair changed from fixed co-occurrence to A02 occurring alone [REL001]; and A02 now sits at the end position of traces [POS001]. All five items belong to the control-flow family, where only about 0.04 items would be expected to qualify by chance if nothing had changed, so the ensemble is many times larger than chance would produce, and each difference is larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits. Because A03 is entirely absent from the later window, this is a step that stopped being executed rather than a shift in how often existing paths are taken.

An important competing explanation remains: zero participation only shows that A03 was not observed in the later sample of 200 traces, so the evidence cannot distinguish a complete disappearance of A03 from it still occurring very rarely. The five items also describe one underlying trace change and are therefore not independent confirmations of it. [OCC001, POS002]
