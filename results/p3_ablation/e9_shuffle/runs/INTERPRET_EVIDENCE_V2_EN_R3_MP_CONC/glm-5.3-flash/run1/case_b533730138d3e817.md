### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A possibility directly supported by the evidence is that A01 was introduced as a new concluding activity, executed exactly once after A06: traces that previously ended with A06 now end with A06 followed by A01. The supporting signs are consistent and direct: A01's participation rose from not observed to 100 in 100 traces; the relational evidence shows A01 and A06 co-occurring with A06 first in every later trace; A06's outgoing flow, previously unobserved, now goes entirely to A01; and A06's shift away from the final position matches an insertion after it. The five qualifying control-flow items far exceed the roughly 0.04 items expected by chance across the 8 tested units, so the set of differences is unlikely for an unchanged process. Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window means only that A01 was not observed there, so a previously very rare A01 that became mandatory cannot be distinguished from a newly added activity. The evidence covers only A01 and A06, so any further changes elsewhere in the process would not be visible. [OCC001, REL001]
