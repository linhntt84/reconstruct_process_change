### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every one of the 200 traces ran the same fixed chain A36 → A14 → A20 → A15 → A21: A14, A15 and A20 each appeared exactly once in 100% of traces, A36 and A21 appeared in all traces, and A36's only observed successor was A14. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, LAG001]
- In the later window, A14, A15 and A20 each appear in only about 53 in 100 traces (106 of 200; mean occurrences per trace fell from 1.0 to 0.53), while A36 and A21 still appear in all traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, LAG001]
- In the later window, A36 is followed directly by A21 in about 47 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions (before: always A14), and the A36→A21 step distance changed from always 4 steps to 1 step in roughly 47% of cases versus 4 steps in 53%. This points to the middle segment A14–A20–A15 being skipped in about half of the later executions. [DIR001, LAG001]
- When the three middle activities do run in the later window, their behaviour is unchanged: they still occur together and in the same order (A36 before A14 before A20 before A15 before A21 in ~53% of traces); pairs among them show "neither present" in ~47% of traces, and pairs with A21 or A36 show only one of the two present in ~47%. The segment became optional rather than reordered. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- A21's relative position shifted slightly earlier (mean 0.43 to 0.40 of the trace position), consistent with it sometimes directly following A36; this is a position-based measure, not clock time. [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the segment A14 → A20 → A15 between A36 and A21 is skipped in roughly half of the executions: in about 47 in 100 traces A36 passes directly to A21, while in the remaining ~53% the full chain A36 → A14 → A20 → A15 → A21 runs with its internal order unchanged — in other words, the three middle activities became optional. The participation drop is shown directly for all three activities; the new direct A36→A21 transition and the step-distance change from 4 to 1 show the bypass relationally; all nine activity-pair profiles move consistently with the same 53/47 split; and screening produced 15 qualifying control-flow items where only about 0.35 would be expected without a change, with every item exceeding roughly 995 of 1000 random re-splits. Limitations: the items are strongly correlated — they largely re-describe one behaviour over five activities rather than constituting independent confirmations — and the evidence cannot rule out that the bypass existed before as a variant too rare to appear in the 200 earlier traces. Other differences: none — the small position shift of A21 fits the same skip behaviour, and no other activity or relation qualified. [POS001]
