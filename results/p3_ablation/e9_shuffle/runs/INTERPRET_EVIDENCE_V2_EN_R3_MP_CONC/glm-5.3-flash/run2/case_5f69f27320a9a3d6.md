### Observed differences

- A01 was not observed in any of the 200 earlier traces; in the later window it occurs in all 200 traces, about once per trace, at roughly 0.4 of the trace's event positions. [OCC001, POS001]
- A04 shows the reverse pattern: it was present in all 200 earlier traces, sitting late in the trace (mean position about 0.8) and always followed by A07, while in the later window it appears in none of the 200 traces. [OCC002, POS003, DIR004]
- A03's routing changed completely: earlier, every observed outgoing transition of A03 went to A04; in the later window, every one goes to A07, and the A03→A07 distance shortened from two steps to one. [DIR003, LAG004]
- A01 is wired in through new edges: of A02's observed outgoing transitions, about 38 in 100 now go to A01 (earlier only A05, about 51 in 100, and A06, about 49); A05 and A06 each gained edges to A01 (about 26 and 36 in 100); A01's outgoing transitions go to A05 (about 39 in 100), A03 (about 34) and A06 (about 28). [DIR002, DIR005, DIR006, DIR001]
- The relation profiles agree: in the later window A02 precedes A01 in all traces, and A01 precedes A03 and A07 in all traces, while its order against A05 and A06 is mixed (A01 first in about 55 and 49 of 100 co-occurrences respectively). Earlier, A01 co-occurred with none of these activities. [REL001, REL002, REL006, REL004, REL005]
- Every relation involving A04 collapsed from full co-occurrence to the other activity alone: A03–A04, A04–A07, A05–A04, A06–A04 and A02–A04 each co-occurred in all earlier traces and in none later. [REL008, REL011, REL009, REL010, REL007]
- Later activities sit deeper into the trace (A05 mean about 0.30→0.41, A06 about 0.30→0.40, A03 0.60→0.80), the A02→A03 gap grew from always 3 steps to always 4, and gaps that were always 1 (A05→A06, A06→A05) are now 2 steps in roughly 20–35 of 100 cases. [POS004, POS005, POS002, LAG001, LAG006, LAG009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the control flow was restructured around two activities: A01, previously unobserved, was added to the flow — executed once per case, always after A02 and always before A03 and A07, though its position relative to A05 and A06 varies — while A04, previously executed in every case between A03 and A07, was dropped, so that A03 now leads directly to A07. This rests on direct and mutually consistent signs: the all-or-nothing participation flips (0 of 200 vs 200 of 200 traces in both directions), the complete successor change at A03, the new edges into and out of A01, and the matching gap and position shifts (A02→A03 from 3 to 4 steps, A03→A07 from 2 to 1, later positions for A05, A06 and A03). Screening also qualified 34 items where about 0.17 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits of the two windows produce, even though each item alone would not separate a real change from a rare variant. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG004, REL002, REL006]

Limitations: zero participation only means an activity was not observed, so the evidence cannot show whether A04 disappeared entirely or still occurs in a small share of later cases that the 200-trace sample missed; likewise A01 could have existed before at a very low share. A01's slot is also not a single fixed position, since its order against A05 and A06 is mixed in the later window. The competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is therefore a partial rather than complete removal of A04 (and, symmetrically, a rare pre-existing A01). [OCC002, POS003, REL004, REL005]

Other differences: none that stand apart from this restructuring. In particular, A05 and A06 keep a mixed mutual order in both windows (each order in about half of the traces where the pair is seen), so no change toward a fixed order between them is indicated, and the remaining one-step gap shifts (for example A02→A05, A05→A07) are consistent with the single insertion of A01 rather than separate changes. [LAG006, LAG009, LAG002, LAG003, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010]
