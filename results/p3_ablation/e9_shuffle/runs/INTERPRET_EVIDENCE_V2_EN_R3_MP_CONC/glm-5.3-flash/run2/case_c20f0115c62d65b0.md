### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, exactly one of three alternative activities was executed in essentially every trace: A19 in about 32 in 100 traces, A20 in about 28 in 100 and A25 in about 40 in 100, and the three never occurred together in one trace. In the later window, each of the three appears in only about 1 in 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006]
- In the earlier window these alternatives sat between A27/A28 and A29: A27 preceded the alternative in all shared traces (for example A27 before A25 in 40 of 40), the alternative preceded A29 in all shared traces (A25 before A29 in 40 of 40), and the alternative and A28 appeared in both orders (A25 before A28 in about 21 in 100, A28 before A25 in about 19 in 100). In the later window the alternatives almost never co-occur with A27, A28 or A29 (co-occurrence about 0.5 in 100; A29 runs without them in about 99.5 in 100 of shared traces). [REL010, REL012, REL011, REL003, REL005, REL004, REL008, REL009, REL007]
- Routing into and out of the block changed accordingly. Before, A27 led to A28 in about 53 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and to one of the alternatives in about 44 in 100; in the later window A27 leads to A28 in about 97 in 100. Before, A28 led to A29 in about 45 in 100 and to an alternative in about 54 in 100; in the later window A28 leads to A29 in about 99 in 100. [DIR004, DIR005]
- The span from A27 to A29 contracted: it covered three event positions in about 94 in 100 earlier traces and two positions in about 97 in 100 later traces; the immediate successions A27→A28 and A28→A29 rose from about 53 and 45 in 100 to about 98 in 100 each. [LAG002, LAG001, LAG003]
- A27, A28 and A29 remain present in essentially all traces in both windows, and A29's mean relative position moved only slightly earlier (about 0.56 to 0.55). [DIR004, DIR005, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the alternative block consisting of A19, A20 and A25 — of which exactly one was executed in every earlier trace between A27/A28 and A29 — is bypassed: A27 now passes control almost always directly to A28, A28 directly to A29, and the three alternative activities are no longer executed (about 1 in 200 later traces, down from 28–40 in 100 earlier). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The signs are direct and consistent: the successor profiles of A27 and A28, both observed in essentially every trace in both windows, shift to the direct connections; all twelve activity-pair relations involving the block show the same co-occurrence collapse; and the A27→A29 distance shortens from three positions to two. The volume of qualified evidence (27 items against roughly 0.55 expected without change) and the size of the shifts (for example, participation falling from about 32 in 100 to about 1 in 200 with 200 traces per window) are far beyond what sampling variation produces; each item's difference exceeds those seen in about 995 in 1,000 random re-splits. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, DIR004, DIR005, OCC001]

An important competing explanation remains: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine removal or bypass of the block from a later window that simply contains only cases whose variant never executes it, nor determine whether A19, A20 and A25 were deleted or became optional without any later execution. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: the few remaining later occurrences of A19, A20 and A25 (one trace each) sit at different relative positions than before and have outgoing transitions to activities not previously observed from them (A44, A08, A42), but each of these observations rests on a single trace and cannot be interpreted. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003]
