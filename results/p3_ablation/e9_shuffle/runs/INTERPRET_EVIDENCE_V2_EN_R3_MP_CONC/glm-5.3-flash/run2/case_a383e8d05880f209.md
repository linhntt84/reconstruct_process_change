### Observed differences

- The most conspicuous difference concerns A01. In the earlier window it sat near the start of the case (mean position about 0.26 of the trace, median about 0.17); in the later window it sits about two-thirds in (mean about 0.65, median about 0.67), with a slightly narrower spread. A01 occurs in all 200 traces of both windows. [POS001]
- The order between A01 and the other flagged activities reversed. Earlier, A01 ran before A05 in about 80 in 100 cases, before A07 in about 68, before A02 in about 55, and about as often before as after A04 (49 vs 52 in 100). In the later window, A04 and A07 always run before A01, A02 precedes it in about 81 in 100 and A05 in about 63 in 100. In both windows every pair co-occurs in all traces, without interleaving or repetition. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]
- Successor profiles changed accordingly. Of A01's outgoing transitions, the share to A03 rose from 7% to 47% and to A06 from 9% to 15%, while A04 and A07 were no longer observed as successors of A01 (before 18% and 7%). A07 now leads to A01 in about 68 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (before 18) and no longer to A03 (before 28); A04 no longer leads to A01 (before 16) and leads to A07 in 70 (before 54); A02 leads to A05 in 40 (before 27) and to A01 in only 11 (before 26). [DIR001, DIR004, DIR003, DIR002]
- Step distances match this picture. A01 no longer immediately follows A04 (immediate in 31% of cases before, none after) and A03 no longer immediately follows A07 (28% before, none after), while A03 now immediately follows A01 in about 47 in 100 cases (before 10) and A07 is immediately followed by A01 in about 68 in 100 (before 54; the pair now occurs in all traces versus 65 of 200). A02 and A05 now reach A01 only after several steps: immediate A02→A01 fell from 57% to 13% with the 5-step share rising from 1% to 36%, and A05→A01 moved from 1–2 steps to mostly 4–5 steps. [LAG004, LAG008, LAG001, LAG007, LAG003, LAG005]
- The activities that now precede A01 each sit slightly earlier: mean position about 0.31→0.23 for A02, 0.47→0.38 for A05 and 0.52→0.42 for A07. [POS002, POS003, POS004]
- Smaller shifts: step distances from A05 to A03 lengthened (5-step share up from 12% to 32%) and from A07 to A05 (immediate share down from 48% to 24%); where A05 does follow A01, it now does so immediately in about 72 in 100 such cases (before 38). [LAG006, LAG009, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01's placement changed: in the later window A01 is executed late in the case, after A02, A04, A05 and A07, whereas in the earlier window it ran near the beginning, mostly before A05, A07 and A02 and about evenly with A04. After A01, the flow now continues mainly to A03 (and, in a minority of cases, immediately to A05), while A04 and A07 are no longer observed after it. [POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR001, LAG001, LAG002]

The support is direct and consistent: a positional shift for A01 [POS001], four order relations all flipping in the same direction [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004], successor changes that match those flips [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004], and step-distance changes exactly where the new order predicts (immediate A04→A01 and A07→A03 disappeared; immediate A01→A03 and A07→A01 appeared) [LAG001, LAG004, LAG007, LAG008]. With 21 qualifying items against roughly 0.18 expected without a change in the control-flow family, chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole, and complete co-occurrence without interleaving or repetition in both windows argues against a repetition change. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]

Limitations: positions and step distances are measured in event positions, not clock time, so this concerns placement within the case, not timing; the many items largely describe one underlying reordering rather than independent confirmations; and the evidence cannot establish the exact new sequence among A02, A04, A05 and A07, nor rule out that a broader reordering involving activities not among the qualifying units produced the same pairwise orders. [POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR001]

Other differences: A02, A05 and A07 each shifted slightly earlier in the trace, and the A05→A03 and A07→A05 step distances lengthened slightly; these fit the same reordering but are not independently confirmed parts of the main hypothesis. [POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG006, LAG009]
