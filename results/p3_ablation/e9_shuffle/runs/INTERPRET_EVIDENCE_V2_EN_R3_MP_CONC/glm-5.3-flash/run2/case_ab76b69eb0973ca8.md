### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A08 was a common, very early step: it appeared in about 70 in 100 traces (141 of 200), averaged about 1.1 occurrences per trace, and sat among the first events of a case (median event position 1). In the later window, A08 was not observed in any of the 200 traces. [OCC001, POS001]
- In the later window, A07's routing changed completely: earlier, all 219 observed outgoing transitions of A07 went to A08, whereas its 97 observed transitions now go to A05 (about 39 in 100), A03 (about 27), A04 (about 22) and A02 (about 12), and never to A08. [DIR001, DIR002]
- A08's ties to other activities disappeared: earlier it co-occurred with A07 in about 70 in 100 traces and with A05, A04 and A03 in about 30 in 100 each, appearing without those partners in about 38–42 in 100 traces; in the later window co-occurrence with each partner is zero, A05, A04 and A03 now appear without A08 in about 31–33 in 100 traces, and A07 itself remained about as frequent (about 67 in 100, now without A08). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]
- The activities that used to follow A08 now follow A07 sooner: A07→A05 moved from mostly 2–3 positions after A07 (about 84 in 100) to 1–2 positions (about 54 in 100); A07→A03 from position 2 (about 59 in 100) to position 1 (about 43 in 100); A07→A04 from positions 2–3 (about 84 in 100) to positions 1–2 (about 54 in 100). These are event-position distances, not clock times. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]
- Repeats of A07 and A04 also come sooner: A07→A07 shifted from positions 4–5 to 3–4, and A04→A04 from positions 3–5 to 2–4; both rest on small samples (about 17–39 ordered pairs per window). [LAG001, LAG005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening context: 13 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.21 expected if nothing had changed (42 units tested) — a count many times the chance expectation, so this set of differences is not what an unchanged process typically produces; every item also shows a difference larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005]

Main hypothesis: the evidence supports the possibility that A08 was dropped from the flow between A07 and its successors. Earlier, A08 was an early, recurring step that A07 always fed into and that overlapped with A03, A04 and A05; in the later window A08 is not observed at all, A07 leads directly to A05, A04, A03 and A02, and those activities now follow A07 one to two event positions earlier — consistent with the intermediate step A08 being gone. The change is shown directly by participation, successor and relational evidence and is consistent across all items. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Limitations and competing explanation: zero participation only means A08 was not observed in the later sample, so outright removal cannot be distinguished from A08 becoming optional and skipped in every sampled trace; the four relationship items all reflect the same disappearance of A08 and are not independent confirmations, and the position-based lag shifts corroborate but do not by themselves prove a structural rewiring. [OCC001, REL001, LAG002]

Other differences: repetitions of A07 and A04 now occur at shorter event distances (A07→A07 from positions 4–5 to 3–4; A04→A04 from 3–5 to 2–4), which fits the same tightening of the flow but rests on small supports and is not counted as part of the main hypothesis. [LAG001, LAG005]
