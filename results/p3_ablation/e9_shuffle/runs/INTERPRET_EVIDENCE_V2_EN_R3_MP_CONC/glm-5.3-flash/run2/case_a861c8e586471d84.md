### Observed differences

- The three activities **A19, A20 and A25** were each present in roughly a third of cases before (about 36, 31 and 35 in 100 traces respectively); in the later window A19 and A25 appear in 0 of 200 traces and A20 in only 1 of 200. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS002, POS003, POS004]
- Before, these three were mutually exclusive alternatives: in virtually every trace exactly one of them ran (A19 alone ~35 in 100, A20 alone ~31, A25 alone ~35; they never occurred together or repeated), so they formed a once-per-case three-way choice covering essentially all cases. In the later window, almost no trace contains any of them. [REL007, REL009, REL014, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The wiring around **A41** changed: before, A41's observed outgoing transitions went to A19 (~33 in 100), A25 (~33) and A20 (~27); in the later window A41 leads directly to **A26** in about 92 in 100 cases. [DIR004]
- Before, each of A19, A20 and A25 led almost invariably to A26 (about 97–99 of their observed outgoing transitions); in the later window A19 and A25 have no observed outgoing transitions, and A20's single observed continuation goes to A30. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The positional distance from A41 to A26 shrank: before, A26 typically followed A41 two steps later (about 94 in 100); in the later window it typically follows immediately (about 92 in 100) — the intervening activity is gone. [LAG002]
- Relation items consistently show the same loss: pairs of the three with A14, A26, A31, A41, A12 and A24 previously co-occurred in fixed order (e.g. A19 before A26 in ~35 in 100 traces), and in the later window only the surviving partner remains. [REL001, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021]
- Small positional knock-ons: A14 and A41 sit slightly later in the trace (mean relative position about 0.14→0.15 and 0.32→0.33) and A26 slightly earlier (about 0.39→0.37), consistent with one event having disappeared between A41 and A26. [POS001, POS005, POS006]
- The distance between **A24 and A31** also shrank, from typically two steps (about 59 in 100) to typically one step (about 59 in 100). [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-way alternative block A19/A20/A25, which before was executed exactly once per case between A41 and A26, is no longer executed in the later window, with A41 now flowing directly to A26. This is shown directly by the participation collapse of all three activities (about 31–36 in 100 traces before, 0–1 of 200 after), the redirection of A41's outgoing transitions from the block (about 93 in 100 combined) to A26 (about 92 in 100), and the closure of the A41→A26 gap from two steps to one; the relation items behave consistently across all partner activities. With 36 qualifying items against roughly 0.6 expected for an unchanged process (about 60 times the chance expectation), sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, LAG002, REL007, REL009, REL014, REL004, REL010]

Limitation: zero participation only establishes that these activities were not observed in the 200 later traces; a block that still runs in well under 1 in 100 cases would look identical, and A20's lone later appearance (at a much later trace position, ~0.62 vs ~0.35 before) leaves open that the block survives in a very small share of cases rather than being removed entirely. [OCC002, POS003, DIR002]

Other differences: A24 appears in somewhat more traces (about 49 vs about 56 in 100) and A12 as well (about 42 vs about 51 in 100); together with the A24–A31 gap shrinking from two steps to one, this suggests a further local adjustment, but the evidence does not show what, if anything, disappeared between A24 and A31, so it may also be a knock-on of the block's removal. [LAG001, REL008, REL013, REL018, REL001, REL002, REL003]
