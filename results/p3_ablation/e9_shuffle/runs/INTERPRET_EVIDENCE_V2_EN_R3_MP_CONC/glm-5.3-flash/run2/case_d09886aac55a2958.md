### Observed differences

- Activity A01 did not appear at all in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it runs in about 101 of 200 traces (about 50 in 100), averages roughly 1.1 occurrences per trace (about 2 per trace on the cases that have it) and repeats within a trace in about 25 in 100 traces. [OCC001]
- In the later window A01 sits in the early-to-middle part of traces: its median position is about 32% of the trace length, with the middle half of its positions between roughly 20% and 53%; in the earlier window it had no observed positions. [POS001]
- A01 is wired into the existing flow in the later window: about 35 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions lead to A05, about 19 to A03 and about 15 to A06 (smaller shares to A15, A16, A10, A12); it also appears as a new successor of A09 (about 36 in 100 of A09's outgoing transitions), A08 (about 28), A06 (about 22), A12 (about 11) and A05 (about 7) — none of these links were observed before. [DIR001, DIR006, DIR005, DIR004, DIR007, DIR003]
- The partner activities changed from running without A01 to running with it: before, A05, A12, A08, A09, A03, A06 and A15 each occurred alone in about a third to two thirds of traces while A01 never appeared; after, A01 co-occurs with each of them in roughly a third to a half of traces and usually earlier than they are (for example A01 before A05 in 20 vs 6 in 100 traces, A01 before A12 in 22 vs 4 in 100), with a sizable interleaved-or-repeated share (about 18–33 in 100). [REL002, REL006, REL004, REL005, REL001, REL003, REL007]
- Positional distances between existing pairs widened, as expected if a step were inserted between them: A09 reached A03 within one step in about 50 in 100 cases before and never after, and A08 reached A03 within one step in about 49 in 100 before and never after; distances of 4–5 steps appeared where none were observed (A08→A05 about 15 in 100 at distance 4, A09→A12 about 25 and A08→A12 about 27 at distance 5), while A03→A08 distances shortened (from mostly 5 steps to mostly 2–4). [LAG005, LAG002, LAG003, LAG007, LAG004, LAG006, LAG001]
- Routing between pre-existing activities also shifted: from A16, the share of outgoing transitions to A02 rose from about 9 in 100 to about 43 in 100, while A05 fell from about 34 to about 10 and A15 from about 17 to about 7; from A03, the share to A12 rose from about 1 to about 17 in 100 while A05 fell from about 44 to about 27. [DIR008, DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was introduced into the process (or went from negligible to frequent execution) and was inserted between existing steps: in the later window it takes part in about half of the traces, around the first third of each trace and often more than once, running after A09, A08, A06, A12 or A05 and leading mostly to A05, A03 and A06, while previously adjacent pairs such as A09→A03 and A08→A03 are no longer adjacent. The support is direct and consistent: participation and position move from zero to about 101 of 200 traces, seven relation profiles and five successor profiles show the same new connections, and eight gap distributions shift in the direction an insertion produces; with 25 qualifying items against roughly 0.43 expected for an unchanged process, the ensemble is far beyond what chance produces. The evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely new activity from one previously too rare to appear in 200 traces, and the interleaved-or-repeated shares leave the exact insertion point (single versus multiple positions) open; the rerouting at A16 and A03 could be a knock-on effect of this insertion or an independent change, which the evidence cannot decide. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

Other differences: the rerouting at A16 (more to A02, less to A05 and A15) and at A03 (more to A12, less to A05) involves only activities observed in both windows on both sides, so it is a shift in how often existing paths are taken rather than a replacement of paths; it is not explained by A01's placement and may be a consequence of the same restructuring or a separate change.
