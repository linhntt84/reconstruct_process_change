### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A07 is no longer executed, so that the former path A03 → A07 → A06 is bypassed and A03 now proceeds directly to A06. Earlier, about half of all traces contained A07, always together with A03 and A06, with A03 feeding A07 in about 39 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and A07 leading on to A06 in about 92 in 100 of its own; in the later window A07 is unobserved in all 200 traces, the direct A03 → A06 edge rises from about 5 to about 52 in 100 of A03's outgoing transitions, and the A03–A06 gap drops from typically two or four steps to immediate or near succession.
