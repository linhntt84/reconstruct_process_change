### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the one-per-trace alternative block A14/A15/A20 was relocated from the late segment of the process to the early segment: in the earlier window it always ran after A21 and A22, slotted between A22 and A23 or between A23 and A27; in the later window it always runs between A40 and A21, while A22, A23 and A27 follow one another directly. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG004]

The reading is direct and consistent across indicators: the order between the block and A21/A22 reverses completely while co-occurrence is unchanged, so the block was neither added nor dropped; successor relations change on both sides of the move (A40 now feeds the block, the block now feeds A21, and A22/A23 no longer branch into it); the block's position shifts from about 60% to about 32% of the trace while A21, A22 and A23 move only about 0.02–0.03 later, consistent with a single event inserted ahead of them; and 24 items qualified against roughly 0.39 expected for an unchanged process, far beyond what chance produces, though these items largely describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. Limitations are the finite window samples and that small shifts in how often each block member runs (for example A14 from about 38 to 34 of 100 traces) are within ordinary sampling variation; the relocation is inferred from relational, adjacency and successor evidence rather than observed as a single modification. [REL001, DIR006, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG001]

Other differences: A10 and A11 repeat less often in the later window (about 1.9 versus 1.6 occurrences per trace; repeats in about 47 versus 38 of 100 traces) while still occurring in every trace. [OCC001, OCC002]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the late insertion was removed and the early insertion added as two separate modifications involving the same activities rather than one relocation; the stable per-trace share of the block and the matching rewiring on both sides make a single move the most direct reading.
