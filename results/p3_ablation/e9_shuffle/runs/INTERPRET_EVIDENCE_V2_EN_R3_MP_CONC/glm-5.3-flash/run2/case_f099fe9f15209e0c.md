### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02, previously executed once in every case near the start of the trace, is no longer executed in the later window — either removed from the process or now skipped in all observed cases. The participation drop from 100% to 0% of traces is observed directly, and it is consistent across the relational evidence: A01, A03 and A04, which always co-occurred with A02, now occur without it, and A05, which previously appeared only together with A02, now appears only without it. The empty outgoing profile of A02 and the disappearance of A03's transition into A02 corroborate the same picture. The screening produced 11 qualifying items where about 0.15 would be expected if nothing had changed, so chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole, although the relational items largely reflect one underlying disappearance rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR001, DIR002]

Limitation: zero participation only means A02 was not observed in the 200 later traces; a rare residual execution outside this sample cannot be ruled out, and the evidence cannot distinguish removal from universal skipping. [OCC001]

Other differences: A03 and A04 appear at earlier relative positions and the A03→A04 distance shortened from typically 3–4 to 1–2 steps, and A03's remaining outgoing shares redistributed toward A01, A04 and A05; these are consistent with traces compacting once A02 is gone, but being position-based they do not establish a shift in clock timing. [POS002, POS003, LAG001, DIR002]
