### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the ordering around these activities changed as follows. In the earlier window, A02 or A04 led into A03, A12 followed A03 at the end of the trace, and A16 fed directly into A10 or A09 in traces disjoint from A03. In the later window, A02 and A04 lead immediately and exclusively to A12 near the start, while A03 appears later, never together with A02, A04 or A12, and instead always together with A16 and A10 — typically after A16 and before A10 — so that A03 now leads to A10 (A16's former direct successor) and A16 no longer leads directly to A10. [REL004, REL005, REL003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS001, POS003]

This is not a sampling artefact at the set level: 16 items qualified across 42 tested control-flow units against roughly 0.21 expected without a change, and each item's difference exceeds about 99.5 of 100 random re-splits; successor changes, co-occurrence flips between "never together" and "always together", lost adjacency and position shifts all describe the same reordering. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: the items largely describe one underlying difference, so they are not independent confirmations, and some later-window relations rest on modest support (for example A02→A12 on 28 traces, A04→A12 on 35). The directional evidence covers only A02, A03, A04 and A16 as source activities, so A12's other predecessors are unobserved — A12 occurs in about 45 in 100 traces while A02 and A04 together appear in only about 31 in 100. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the two windows sample two pre-existing variants in different proportions (a previously rare ordering becoming common), although the later pattern — A02→A12 adjacency, A16→A03, A03→A10 — was absent or nearly absent in the earlier window.
