### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A01 no longer runs and the flow that previously passed through it (A14 into A01, alongside A03, into A04) now reaches A04 through a newly inserted segment: A03 flows into A02, which repeats with A12 (A02 → A12 → A02) and closes through A13 into A04, while A14 feeds directly into A04. This is shown directly by the successor changes (A14 switching from A01 to A04, A03 switching from A04 to A02, the new edges A02→A12/A13, A12→A02 and A13→A04, and A01's edge to A04 disappearing), by participation (A01 from about 20 in 100 traces to 0 of 200; A02 and A13 to about 48 in 100; A12 to about 26 in 100), and by consistent positional and distance shifts (A03→A04 gaps stretching to 3–5 steps, A14→A04 tightening to 1 step, A04 moving later in the trace). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS002, POS003, POS006, LAG001, LAG004, REL008, REL017]

The screening qualified 54 control-flow items where about 0.36 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the evidence as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits of stable behaviour produce, and the raw magnitudes (0 of 200 versus 40 of 200 traces for A01; 51–95 of 200 for the new activities) rule out window-to-window sampling fluctuation. The evidence cannot rule out that rare alternative routings of the new segment exist — for example, entry points other than A03 or A14 — because it shows only aggregated shares over 200 traces per window; several relation items also restate the same rewiring and do not confirm it independently.
