Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A06 was removed from (or bypassed within) this fragment: the sequence A05 → A06 → A04, which ran in about half of all cases, became a direct A05 → A04 connection in the later window. The support is direct and consistent: the edge rewiring from A05 to A04 [DIR001], the complete disappearance of A06 and its outgoing edge [OCC001, POS003, DIR002], the collapse of the A05–A04 distance from two steps to one [LAG001], and the loss of all co-occurrence between A06 and its former neighbours [REL001, REL002] all describe the same structural change, while the fragment's overall occurrence rate stayed stable at roughly 50 in 100 cases [LAG001].
