### Observed differences

- Activity A01 disappeared from the later window: in the earlier window it was executed exactly once in every trace (200 of 200 traces, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace) at roughly two-thirds of the trace's length; in the later window it appears in 0 of 200 traces. [OCC001, POS001]
- A01's relations collapsed accordingly: earlier, A01 and A07 always co-occurred with A01 first (100 of 100 traces); later, A07 always occurs without A01. Earlier, A01 and A05 always co-occurred (A05 first in about 55 of 100 traces, interleaved in about 45); later, A05 always occurs without A01. [REL001, REL002]

- A04's outgoing mix shifted slightly: A05 fell from about 92% to 87%, A07 rose from about 1.5% to 7%, and the small A01 share (about 3.5%) is gone. [DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was removed from the executed flow: the earlier behaviour, in which every trace ran A05 → A01 → A07 with A01 mandatory and always preceding A07, became a behaviour in which A05 leads directly to A07 and A01 is not executed at all. [OCC001, REL002, DIR003, LAG001]

The change is shown directly and consistently across indicators: A01's participation fell from 200 of 200 traces to 0 of 200; both of its relations flipped from full co-occurrence to "A01 absent" in every trace; A05's dominant successor switched from A01 (about 51%) to A07 (about 49%); and the A05→A07 distance moved from typically 2 positions (mediated) to typically 1 (direct). Screening found 10 qualifying items from 16 tested units where only about 0.08 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the ensemble is far beyond what random re-splits of a stable process produce (each item also sits at about the 99.5th percentile of reference comparisons). [OCC001, REL001, REL002, DIR001, DIR003, LAG001]

Limitations: zero participation only establishes that A01 was not observed in the later window, so a complete removal cannot be distinguished from A01 becoming very rare (executed in only a small share of traces). The items also describe largely the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations of one another. [OCC001]

Other differences: A04's outgoing transitions shifted slightly (A05 about 92% → 87%, A07 about 1.5% → 7%, A01 about 3.5% → 0), and A07 and A05 moved slightly earlier and later in the trace respectively — all consistent with the rerouting around A01 but not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR002, POS002, POS003]
