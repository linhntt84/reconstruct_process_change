### Observed differences

- Activity A03 disappeared: in the earlier window it ran in every case (200 in 200), almost always as the opening event (median position 0), and about 86 in 100 of its outgoing transitions led to A02; in the later window it does not appear at all (0 in 200). [OCC001, POS002, DIR001]
- Activity A07 appeared: it was absent earlier (0 in 200), while in the later window it runs in every case (200 in 200), always at the same position about three-quarters through the case, and it is always followed by A01. [OCC002, POS004, DIR003]
- A04's successor changed: earlier, about 99 in 100 of A04's outgoing transitions led directly to A01; in the later window all of them lead to A07. A04 also moved one step earlier, from about 0.75 to 0.5 of the case. [DIR002, POS003, REL007]
- A02 moved to the front: from about a quarter into the case (median 0.25) to the first event (position 0), and it now precedes A07 in every case. [POS001, REL004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural hypothesis involving A03, A02, A04, A07 and A01: in the later window the opening activity A03 is no longer executed, a new activity A07 runs in every case between A04 and the final A01, so A04 now leads to A07 instead of directly to A01, and A02 has become the first activity. Nineteen control-flow items qualified where only about 0.13 would be expected by chance in an unchanged process, and each difference exceeds roughly 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation flips are complete on both sides (A03 in 200 of 200 cases before and none after; A07 exactly the reverse), the positional, directional and relational indicators all place A07 directly before A01, and A03 and A07 never co-occur. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS003, POS004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL002, REL006, REL007]

The evidence cannot rule out that A07 is a re-labelled pre-existing activity rather than a genuinely new one, since the codes are anonymous and the two never appear together. It also does not identify the activity occupying the step between A02 and A04 in either window; before the change A03 occasionally led to A05 or A06 (about 5 in 100 outgoing transitions each), and no qualified evidence covers those activities afterwards. The evidence is confined to control flow. [DIR001, REL006]
