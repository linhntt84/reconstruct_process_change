### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the execution order of two groups of activities was reversed around the pair A25–A29. In the earlier window the flow ran from A40 mostly into one of A19/A21/A24, then A25, then A29, then A20/A22/A23, and on to A30; in the later window A40 leads mostly into A20/A22/A23, then A25, then A29, then one of A19/A21/A24, and on to A30. Thus A20, A22 and A23 now execute before A25 and A29 whereas A19, A21 and A24 execute after them, the reverse of the earlier window. [REL023, REL030, REL033, REL016, REL035, DIR006, DIR008, DIR011, DIR012, DIR013, POS003, POS006, POS002, POS007]

Supporting signs: the order reversals are direct relational evidence measured on all 200 traces in both windows, including pairs that always co-occur (A20–A25 flips from about 98 in 100 one way to about 98 in 100 the other), so a change in participation cannot explain them; the positional shifts, the successor changes at exactly the predicted boundaries (A40 and A29 changing the group they lead into, A19/A21/A24 switching from A25 to A30), and the step-distance changes all agree in direction and magnitude. The screening flagged 94 control-flow items where about 1 would be expected by chance in a stable process, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces; the many indicators, however, largely mirror this one underlying reordering rather than independent changes. [REL023, REL016, DIR006, DIR011, DIR012, DIR013, LAG026, LAG029]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish whether the two groups themselves were repositioned or whether the later window draws on different execution variants in which the groups appear in opposite order; A19, A21 and A24 each occur in only about 30–38 in 100 traces, so their ordering is observed only in the subset of traces where they run; and positions are event positions, not clock times. [POS002, POS004, POS007]

Other differences: A08 now takes part in far fewer traces (about 64 to 29 in 100) with its outgoing flow dispersed from mostly A16 to many successors, while A04, A06 and A10 take part in roughly three times as many traces with changed successors for A04 and A10; the evidence does not show whether these participation and routing shifts are connected to the ordering change above.
