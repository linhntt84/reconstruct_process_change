### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added to the process as a step executed after A18: in the later window A01 runs once in roughly half of the traces, always after A18, A18 routes to A01 in about two thirds of its outgoing transitions, and A01 then hands over to a broad set of activities similar to those A18 previously led to directly. [OCC001, REL001, DIR004, DIR001, POS001] The order relation is shown directly (co-occurrence with a fixed A18-before-A01 order, plus the successor change at A18), and the nine qualifying items compare with only about 0.2 expected by chance among the 40 tested units, so the evidence set as a whole is unlikely without a change.
