### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, cases split after A21 into two routes: about 52 in 100 traces ran A21 → A22 → A23, while the remaining ~48 in 100 inserted A27, A28 and A33 between A21 and A23, with all three activities occurring together in no fixed order (both orders appear in similar shares, roughly 24–25 in 100 each). [REL001, REL005, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- In the later window, about 199 in 200 traces run A21 → A22 → A23: A22's participation rose from about 52% to essentially every trace, and A21's outgoing transitions, previously split (A22 about 52%, A33 about 18%, A28 about 17%, A27 about 14%), now lead almost exclusively to A22. [OCC001, REL001, REL005, DIR001]
- A27, A28 and A33 each fell from appearing in about 49 in 100 traces (97 of 200) to about 1 in 200 traces in the later window. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Accordingly, the relations "A21 before A27/A28/A33" and "A27/A28/A33 before A23", each present in about 49 in 100 earlier traces, are now seen in only about 1 in 200; and A22, which previously never co-occurred with A27, A28 or A33 (strict alternatives), now appears without them in virtually every trace. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL006, REL007, REL008]
- The gap between A21 and A23 collapsed from two steps in about 52 in 100 traces and four steps in about 48 in 100, to two steps in about 995 in 1000 traces. [LAG001]
- A23 remains in all traces in both windows, with only a slight shift to an earlier relative position (mean about 0.55 to 0.53 of the trace). [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative branch running A27, A28 and A33 (in varying order) between A21 and A23 is no longer executed in the later window, leaving A21 → A22 → A23 as the only observed route; A22 thereby became effectively mandatory, while A27, A28 and A33 became effectively unobserved. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL012, REL013, REL014, DIR001]

Supporting signs: the participation changes rest on 97–103 traces before versus 1–199 after; the relational items carry full support (200 traces in both windows) and show the routing change directly rather than by inference; and screening produced 34 qualifying items against roughly 0.45 expected for an unchanged process, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits produce. [REL001, REL005, OCC001, OCC002, DIR001]

Limitations: the many items largely describe one underlying trace change rather than independent confirmations; the evidence shows the branch in only about 1 of 200 later traces but cannot distinguish complete removal from continued execution at a very low rate; and several directional and lag items about the branch's internal order rest on a single after-window trace, so they add little on their own. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG002]

Other differences: A23 shows a small shift to an earlier relative position; since A23 occurs in all traces in both windows, the evidence cannot determine whether this is a placement change or a byproduct of the shorter traces. [POS001]

Competing explanation: an extreme sampling fluctuation in the later window — the branch still existing but almost not sampled — cannot be excluded from two finite windows alone, although the screening comparison makes a chance explanation of the overall pattern implausible. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
