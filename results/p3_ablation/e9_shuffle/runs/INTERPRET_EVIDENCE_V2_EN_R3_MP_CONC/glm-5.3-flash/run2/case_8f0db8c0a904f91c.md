### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every one of the 200 traces contained A41, A19, A25, A20, A26, A46 and A47, run in one fixed order: A41 led to A19 in about 96 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions, A25 led to A20 in about 99 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, and A20 led to A26 in about 99 in 100 of its outgoing transitions; the pairs A19–A25, A25–A20 and A20–A26 co-occurred in 100 in 100 traces, always in that order. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL008, REL009, REL014, REL015]
- In the later window, A19, A20 and A25 each appear in only about 53 in 100 traces (about 106 of 200), while A41, A26, A46 and A47 still appear in every trace. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, POS002, POS003, POS004]
- The three activities drop out together: any two of them co-occur in about 53 in 100 later traces and are jointly absent in about 47 in 100; when the block does run, its internal order and its position between A41 and A26 are unchanged. [REL008, REL009, REL011, REL014, REL015, REL019, DIR003, DIR004]
- Every relation between a block activity and an always-present neighbour changed from always co-occurring (100 in 100 traces) to co-occurring in about 53 in 100 with the neighbour now appearing alone in about 47 in 100; before, the neighbour never occurred without the block activity. [REL010, REL012, REL013, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL020, REL021, REL022]
- The bypass is visible directly: A41 now leads straight to A26 in about 44 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (about 50 in 100 still lead to A19), whereas before about 96 in 100 led to A19 and none led directly to A26; the step distance from A41 to A26, previously almost always 4, is now split between 1 step (about 44 in 100) and 4 steps (about 49 in 100). [DIR005, LAG002]
- A02 becomes rare and loses its placement: it appears in about 8–9 in 100 later traces (previously about 29 in 100); before, A02 was inserted between A46 and A47 (A46 led to A02 in about 25 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, A02 led to A47 in about 85 in 100, and the pair was adjacent in about 94 in 100 of observed cases), whereas now the A46 → A02 branch all but disappears (under 1 in 100), A46 and A47 become directly consecutive in about 96 in 100 traces (previously about 75 in 100), A02's typical position moves from near the end of the trace (mean about 0.90) to mid-trace (about 0.51), its successors scatter (A47 drops to about 6 in 100 of A02's outgoing transitions, based on only 17 traces), and its order relative to A19, A20, A25, A26 and A41 is no longer fixed. - Smaller shifts: A04 and A06 now appear in about 12–13 in 100 traces, up from about 4–5 in 100; A08's outgoing profile changed completely, but it rests on very few observations (4 before, 15 after). [OCC002, OCC003, DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the block of activities A19, A25 and A20 — previously executed in every case between A41 and A26 in the order A19 → A25 → A20 — became optional: it runs in about 53 in 100 cases and is skipped entirely in about 47 in 100, with A41 then passing directly to A26; when the block runs, its internal order and placement are unchanged. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL008, REL009, REL011, REL014, REL015, DIR005, LAG002]

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: exactly the three activities whose participation halved are the ones whose co-occurrence with every universal neighbour collapsed from 100 to about 53 in 100 traces; the bypass is visible at the source (A41 → A26 in about 44 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions, previously none) and in the A41–A26 distance becoming bimodal (1 or 4 steps where it was almost always 4); and the volume of qualifying items (41 against roughly 0.7 expected by chance across 145 tested units) rules out sampling variation as an explanation of the pattern as a whole. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL010, REL013, REL017, REL019, DIR005, LAG002]

Limitation: the evidence cannot distinguish a newly optional block within one process from a later window that mixes two case populations (cases with and without the block), and it cannot show what determines whether the block runs in a given case. [REL010, REL019]

Other differences: A02 became rare (about 8–9 in 100 traces, from about 29 in 100) and no longer sits between A46 and A47 — the A46 → A02 branch and the A02 → A47 exit all but disappear, A02 now sits mid-trace with mixed order relative to A19, A20, A25, A26 and A41, and its outgoing transitions scatter across many activities; this is a separate structural difference, not part of the block-skip hypothesis, and its after-side profile rests on only 17 traces containing A02. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR006, LAG001, LAG003, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007] In addition, A04 and A06 are observed more often in the later window (about 12–13 in 100 traces versus about 4–5 in 100). [OCC002, OCC003]
