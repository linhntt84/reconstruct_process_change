### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, each of the six activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 ran in about 49–53 in 100 cases; in the later window each runs in about 98–100 in 100 cases, with mean occurrences per trace rising from about 0.5 to about 1.0. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010]

- Earlier, these six split into two mutually exclusive blocks: pairs inside {A33, A35, A36} and inside {A37, A38, A39} co-occurred in about 48–52 in 100 cases (in mixed order), while any first-block/second-block pair ran together in only about 1–2 in 100 cases. In the later window, every cross-block pair co-occurs in about 98 in 100 cases, each first-block activity precedes each second-block activity in about 95–98 in 100 cases, and the order inside each block stays mixed (both orders in roughly 43–55 in 100). - The frame around the blocks is stable: A14, A15, A30, A31, A47 and A48 appear in essentially every case in both windows; A14 and A30 still precede the six activities in about 95–98 in 100 later cases, A31, A47 and A48 still follow them (about 97–99 in 100), and A15's interleaving or repetition with the block activities rose from about 15–19 to about 41 in 100 cases. - Cross-block pairs that earlier appeared within five steps of each other in only 1 of 200 cases (A33/A37, A36/A37) now do so in about 195 of 200 cases, with varied gaps of 1–5 positions. [LAG009, LAG014]

- Event positions (not clock times) moved accordingly: first-block activities now sit slightly earlier in the case (medians about 0.76 → about 0.68) and second-block activities slightly later (about 0.73–0.75 → about 0.77); A30 moved earlier (median about 0.69 → 0.63) and A31 later (about 0.82 → 0.84). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, cases execute both three-activity blocks where earlier they executed one: the earlier choice between the block {A33, A35, A36} and the block {A37, A38, A39} — each block present in about half of the earlier cases, cross-block pairs together in about 1–2 in 100 — gives way to all six activities running in about 98 in 100 cases, first-block activities in mixed order followed by second-block activities in mixed order, between the unchanged anchors A14/A15/A30 before and A31/A47/A48 after. This is a change from alternatives to joint execution rather than a re-weighting of coexisting paths, since cross-block pairs were almost never seen together earlier. The flip is observed directly on activity pairs with about 200 cases behind each window, is consistent across all nine cross-block and six within-block pairs, and is matched by routing (A30 into the first block, A31 no longer a direct successor of first-block activities) and gap behaviour (A30→A31 within five steps falling from about 199 to 5 of 200, cross-block pairs appearing within five steps in about 195 of 200); the screening flagged 109 items where about 1 would be expected without a change. [REL037, REL039, REL040, REL045, REL050, OCC005, OCC008, DIR005, DIR006, LAG004, LAG009, LAG013]

The later window still contains about 1–2 in 100 cases with only one block's activities, so a mixture of old and new behaviour cannot be ruled out; the many relation items describe overlapping traces and therefore corroborate rather than independently confirm the same difference; and gaps and positions are measured in event positions, so no claim about clock-time duration is implied. [REL039, REL040, POS005]

Other differences: A04, previously a step between A14 and A15 present in about 59 in 100 cases, is now bypassed in the main sequence — present in about 26 in 100 cases, with A14 leading directly to A15 in about 79 in 100 of A14's outgoing transitions and A04's own transitions spread over many successors; A02, A08 and A10 occur in roughly two to four times as many cases as before and receive occasional low-share transitions from A45 and A47, whose main successors A46 and A48 remain. [OCC002, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, REL002, DIR002, DIR004, DIR009, DIR010]
