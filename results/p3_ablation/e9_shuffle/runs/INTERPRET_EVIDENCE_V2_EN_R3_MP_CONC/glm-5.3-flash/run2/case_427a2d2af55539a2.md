### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured: the earlier pattern, in which A39 was followed by exactly one of A14, A15 or A16 (an exclusive choice, each in roughly 30–36 of 100 traces) and then A24, was replaced in the later window by a block in which A21, A22 and A23 all execute between A39 and A24, in no fixed order among themselves. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, LAG001, REL019, REL021, REL022, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027]

The change is shown directly rather than only through frequencies: A39's successor set changed from A14/A15/A16 to A21/A22/A23, and the A39-to-A24 distance grew from 2 to 4 positions, matching the insertion of two additional steps; the participation changes are complementary (the three old activities fell from about 30–36 in 100 traces to 0, the three new ones rose from 0 to all 200); and screening produced 48 qualifying control-flow items where fewer than 1 would be expected if nothing had changed, so chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [DIR007, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

Limitations: zero participation only means A14, A15 and A16 were not observed in the later window, so a residual rare occurrence cannot be completely ruled out; and the equal both-orders shares of the new trio are consistent with an unordered block but cannot distinguish truly concurrent execution from a sequential run whose order varies from case to case. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL019, REL020, REL023]
