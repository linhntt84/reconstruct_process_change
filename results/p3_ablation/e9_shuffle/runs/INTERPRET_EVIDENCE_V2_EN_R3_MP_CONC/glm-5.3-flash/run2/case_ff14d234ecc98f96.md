### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A26, A27 and A28 were mandatory: each appeared in all 200 traces (participation 100 in 100, about one occurrence per trace), with no fixed order among the three. In the later window each appears in only about 1 in 100 traces (1–2 of 200). [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL031, REL032, REL035]
- Three activities that were absent earlier — A19 and A20 (0 of 200 traces) and A21 (1 trace) — now each run in roughly a third of cases (about 62, 67 and 74 of 200 traces). They are almost never combined: any pair co-occurs in at most about 1 in 100 traces, so a later case typically contains exactly one of the three. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL013, REL014, REL020]
- A44's outgoing transitions moved: earlier they went to A26, A27 and A28 (about 33, 28 and 32 in 100 observed transitions); in the later window they go to A21, A20 and A19 (about 36, 30 and 27 in 100). The new activities lead almost exclusively to A29 (about 97 in 100 of their outgoing transitions). [DIR007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The order relations around the block flipped. Earlier, A26, A27 and A28 always co-occurred with A29 and always preceded it, and A44 always preceded them (about 98–100 in 100). Later, A29 still occurs in nearly every trace but without the old block (about 99 in 100), while each new activity co-occurs with A29 exactly within its own traces and precedes it (about 31–37 in 100), with A44 preceding it (about 30–37 in 100). [REL033, REL036, REL038, REL039, REL037, REL034, REL018, REL024, REL029, REL019, REL025, REL030]
- The event distance from A44 to A29 shrank from mostly 4 steps (93 in 100 earlier traces) to mostly 2 steps (92 in 100 later traces), consistent with three intermediate activities being replaced by one. [LAG010]
- Minor shifts: A29's relative position moved slightly earlier (mean 0.43 to 0.40 of trace length) and A44's slightly later (0.31 to 0.33). [POS007, POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the mandatory three-activity block A26, A27, A28 between A44 and A29 was replaced by a single activity chosen per case from three new alternatives, A19, A20 and A21, each executed directly before A29, shortening the A44-to-A29 segment from about four event steps to two. The screening produced 70 qualifying items against roughly 0.8 expected without change, so the differences as a whole cannot be explained by random sampling. The hypothesis rests on direct, mutually consistent signals: the participation flip of both activity groups [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003], the successor changes at A44 and from the new activities to A29 [DIR007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003], the order relations placing each new activity between A44 and A29 [REL018, REL024, REL029, REL019, REL025, REL030, REL033, REL036, REL038], and the drop in A44–A29 distance [LAG010].

Limitations: many items describe the same underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations; the old block still appears in about 1–2 of 200 later traces, so it became rare rather than disappearing entirely; several DIR, LAG and POS items for the old activities rest on only 1–2 later traces; and LAG and POS use event positions, not clock time. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows sample different case mixes or variants rather than a modified single process, although the completeness of the flip (every earlier trace contained the block) makes pure sampling variation implausible. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, LAG001, LAG003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS004, POS005, POS006]

Other differences: A11 and A12, which earlier preceded A27 and A28 in about 46 in 100 of their traces, no longer co-occur with the old block and now precede the new activities in about 16–20 in 100 traces; this fits the replacement above but concerns the upstream context rather than the replaced segment itself.
