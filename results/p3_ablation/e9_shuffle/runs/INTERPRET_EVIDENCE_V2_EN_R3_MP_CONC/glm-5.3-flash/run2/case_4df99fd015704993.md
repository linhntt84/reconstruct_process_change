### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural rewiring of the fragment involving A03, A12, A14, A02 and A13: earlier, traces entering this branch repeated the cycle A03 → A12 → A14 → A03 (about two turns where it ran) before A03 led on to A13, whereas in the later window A14 is no longer observed, A12 returns directly to A03, and the newly observed A02 closes the fragment by always leading to A13. The rewiring is shown directly by the successor changes at A03, A12, A02 and A14, and is consistent across the occurrence and position profiles of A02 and A14 and all five lag shifts, which match a cycle shortened by exactly one step. With 40 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.32 expected by chance among the 65 tested units, and every item's difference exceeding about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole. Limitations: A14's zero participation only shows it was not executed in the 200 later traces, and an important competing explanation remains that the later form existed before as a rare variant containing A02 that the earlier 200-trace sample simply did not capture.
