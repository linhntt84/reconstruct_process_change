### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening tested 151 control-flow units and 58 items qualified, against fewer than one (0.75) expected without change in that family; a volume many times this expectation cannot be explained by random re-splitting, and every item's difference exceeded those of about 995 of 1000 reference comparisons. [DIR002, DIR008, REL017, POS003, LAG004, OCC001]

Main hypothesis: the evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the two groups flanking the A25–A29 segment exchanged their places. Earlier a case ran from A40 through the unordered trio A20/A22/A23, then A25, then A29, then one of A19/A21/A24, reaching A30 from there; in the later window it runs from A40 to one of A19/A21/A24 (about two-thirds of A40's outgoing transitions go directly to one of the three), then to A25, then A29, then the unordered trio, which now leads to A30. Supporting signs: complete order reversals in pairs that co-occur in 100 of 100 traces in both windows, which a participation change cannot produce; matching successor changes at A19, A21, A24, A20, A22, A23, A29 and A40; opposite position shifts of the two groups; and lag changes (A29 to A30 from about 2 to about 4 steps, A40 to A25 from about 4–5 to 2–3 steps) that fit the same picture. [REL017, REL027, REL028, REL014, REL022, DIR002, DIR003, DIR008, DIR009, POS002, POS003, LAG004, LAG007]

Limitations: the relative order of A25 and A29 itself rests on position means rather than a direct relational item, and activities possibly sitting between them are not covered by these items, so the evidence cannot rule out that further unobserved parts of this segment also moved. The shift inside the choice block (A19 up from about 31 to 44 of 100, A21 down from about 38 to 29) could be sampling variation rather than part of the change. The many agreeing items largely mirror this one underlying reordering and are not independent confirmations; all positional and lag evidence uses event positions, not clock time. [POS008, POS009, POS002, POS004]

Other differences: A02 grew from about 8 to 34 of 100 traces and now acts as a step between A40 and the A19/A21/A24 choice, reaching A25 within about two steps — an insertion accompanying the rerouting rather than part of the exchange itself. A41, present in every trace in both windows, is repeated more often in the later window (mean about 1.5 vs 1.8 occurrences per trace). [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, LAG001, LAG005, REL009, OCC002]
