### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new activity, A01, was added as an early routing step between the upstream activities and the A03/A12 stage: A02 and A09, which previously passed directly to A03 or A12 about half each, now run exclusively through A01, which has taken over the A03-versus-A12 hand-over with a similar split, while A13's route now runs through A08 and a short A13–A08 repeat loop before exiting to A01, with A13 consequently appearing in far fewer traces. This rests on direct successor changes at A02, A09, A13 and A08, on A01 appearing from zero to universal participation with outgoing edges only to A03 and A12, and on consistent relational and distance evidence; with 39 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.31 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the ensemble. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR001, OCC001, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL005, LAG001, LAG016, LAG018]

Limitations: only part of the process is observed, so other predecessors of A03/A12 and A13's entry point are unseen; the exact shape of the A13–A08 loop and the reason A01 executes about twice per trace (one repeated step versus two distinct visits) rest on small supports; and a changed case mix in the later window cannot be ruled out for the unobserved parts, although A01's presence in all 200 later traces argues against pure sampling variation for the main difference. [LAG008, LAG019, LAG017]

Other differences: A09 participates somewhat more often (about 33 of 100 traces before versus about 39 in the later window), and A08 sits slightly later in the trace (mean relative position about 0.55 to 0.62) with roughly stable participation; both may be side effects of the rerouting rather than independent changes.
