### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a group of activities that was not executed at all in the earlier window — A07, A15, A16 and A17 — now runs in a substantial share of traces and is spliced into the existing flow: A08 feeds A07, which feeds A18; A10 feeds A15 and A16, with A15 feeding back to A10 and A16 leading to A03 and A02; A18 feeds A17, which feeds A09, and A14. Participation of all four rises from zero to roughly one- to seven-tenths of traces, the successor changes sit exactly where the new activities attach, and the new two-step successions match one inserted intermediary per pair. The screening qualified 92 control-flow items where about 0.6 would be expected without a change, so the differences as a whole are not what an unchanged process produces. [OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, LAG011, LAG027]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window only shows these activities were not executed then, so a path that existed but was never taken cannot be distinguished from genuinely new activities; the high interleaved-or-repeated shares (for example A10 and A16 in about 43 in 100 traces) leave the exact internal order of the new group undetermined; and many items describe the same underlying trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations. [REL029]

Other differences: A05's increased repetition with its earlier position and A12's redirect from A18 to A05, A13's increased repetition, and A18's reduced repetition per trace with its later position are additional differences not covered by the main hypothesis.
