### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A02 was added to the process: in the later window it is executed exactly once in every case, at an early-to-middle position, without a fixed order relative to the existing activities A01, A03 and A06, and tending to precede A04. This rests on direct participation evidence (0 of 200 traces before versus 200 of 200 after, once per trace) [OCC001], relational evidence showing A02 now co-occurring with A01, A03, A04 and A06 in all traces with mixed ordering [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004], successor changes in the routing of A01, A03 and A06 towards A02 [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR002], and growing distances from A01/A03/A06 to A04, consistent with A02 being interleaved between them [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]. The number of qualifying items (13) is far above the roughly 0.14 expected without a change, so chance sampling of the two windows cannot explain the pattern as a whole [screening].

Limitations: because A02 appears in both orders with A01, A03 and A06 in near-equal proportions, the evidence does not identify a single fixed insertion point; A02 may run in parallel with or be flexibly interleaved among these activities rather than occupy one fixed position. Positions and lags are event positions, not clock time, so no statement about timing in time is possible. The evidence also cannot rule out that A02 is a previously existing behaviour relabelled under a new code rather than a genuinely new activity.

Other differences: beyond A02's appearance, the routing profiles show only small redistributions among the original successors (for example A03→A01 down about 9 percentage points, A06→A04 down about 4), and no previously observed activity disappeared from either window. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004]
