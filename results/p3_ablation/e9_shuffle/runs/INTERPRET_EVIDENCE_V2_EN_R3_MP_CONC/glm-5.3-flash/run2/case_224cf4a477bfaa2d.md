### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new activity, A01, was added to every case between A02 and the branching point — A02 now hands over to A01 in about half of its transitions and A04 routes on to A01 in about 39 of 100 — from where work flows into the pre-existing branches (A04, A07, A03, A10, A05), while the closing activity A09, which previously ended about 45 of 100 cases after A06, A03, A04 or A10, no longer runs, leaving A06 as the final step in those cases.
