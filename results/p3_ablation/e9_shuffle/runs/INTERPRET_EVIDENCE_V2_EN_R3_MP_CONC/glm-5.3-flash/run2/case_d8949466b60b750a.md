### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the two-way choice after A26 was eliminated and A27 became a mandatory step executed immediately before A28. In the earlier window, cases ran either A27 alone (about 52 in 100) or the group A32, A33, A38 together (about 48 in 100, in varying internal order), both paths preceding A28; in the later window, A27 runs in essentially every case right before A28, while A32, A33 and A38 each appear in only about 1 in 200 cases. Because the trio is nearly absent in the later window, this is a change in participation (a branch became effectively unused and A27 became mandatory), not a shift of shares between persisting alternatives. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, LAG001, LAG004, DIR001]

The screening found 35 qualifying control-flow items against about 0.6 expected without a change, so the set as a whole is far beyond what an unchanged process produces, and the participation, relational, directional and lag items all describe the same underlying difference rather than isolated quirks. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, LAG001, REL001]

The evidence cannot rule out that the later window reflects a different case mix in which the A32/A33/A38 path is simply rare — it was still observed once — rather than a modification of the process; moreover, any statement about how the trio behaves now rests on a single trace. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR003, DIR004]

Other differences: A28's relative position moved slightly earlier (mean relative position about 0.55 to about 0.53), consistent with fewer activities now running before it; and a few rare outgoing transitions from A26 and A27 to other activities (about 0.5 in 100 each) appear only in the later window.
