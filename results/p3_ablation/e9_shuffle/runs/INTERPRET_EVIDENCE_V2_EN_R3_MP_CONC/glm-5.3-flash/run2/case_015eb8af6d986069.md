### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25, A32 — previously executed in variable and partly reversed orders, with A23 often leading straight to A32 — is executed in the later window as one fixed sequential chain, A31→A22→A20→A23→A24→A26→A25→A32, with A32 now placed after A25 instead of after A23. The change is shown directly and consistently: eight relation profiles flip from mixed or reversed orders to one-sided ones, seven successor distributions collapse to a single dominant successor, the lag distributions settle at one fixed distance each that are mutually consistent with a single chain, and the position shifts point the same way; 82 items qualified against about 1 expected for an unchanged process, so sampling noise cannot explain the pattern as a whole. Limitations: the positional measures use event order rather than clock time, so no timing claim is made, and the evidence cannot fully distinguish a newly fixed ordering constraint from a shift in the mix of pre-existing execution variants toward the sequential one. [REL016, REL019, REL021, DIR013, DIR009, LAG036, LAG012, POS005]

Other differences:
- A08 dropped out of most cases — from about 72 in 100 traces to about 29 — and is now usually bypassed, with A15 leading directly to A16; this looks like A08 becoming skippable in the later window. [OCC001, DIR006, REL005]
- A10 participates more often (about 22 in 100 traces after versus 7–8 before). [OCC002]
- The rare activities A02 and A04 show large shifts in their outgoing transition mixes, but with only about 15–19 supporting traces before, these may reflect sampling of rare variants rather than a change. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004]
