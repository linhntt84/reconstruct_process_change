### Observed differences

- Six activities around A20 swapped their roles. In the later window, A21, A22 and A23 each run in about 79–80 in 100 traces, up from about 19 in 100; A24, A25 and A26 each run in only about 21 in 100, down from about 82 in 100. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]
- A20 (observed in every trace in both windows) now routes differently: before, about 29% of its observed outgoing transitions went to A24, about 29% to A26 and about 24% to A25, with only about 18% combined to A21/A22/A23; in the later window about 29% go to A23, about 29% to A21 and about 21% to A22, with only about 21% combined to the old trio. [DIR003]
- A20 precedes each of A21, A22 and A23 in about 77–79 in 100 traces in the later window, versus about 19 in 100 before. [REL009, REL010, REL011]
- The old trio typically executed together before (co-occurrence about 82 in 100 traces, in varying order) and is now mostly absent together (about 79 in 100 traces contain neither); the new trio shows the mirror image, co-occurring in about 79 in 100 later traces in no fixed order (both orders roughly 37–42 in 100), versus about 19 in 100 before. - Downstream partners switched: A30, present in essentially all traces in both windows, co-occurred with A24/A25/A26 in about 82 in 100 traces before and now co-occurs with A21/A22/A23 in about 79 in 100; A32 and A33 similarly moved from about 52 in 100 with the old trio to about 55–56 in 100 with the new one. [REL038, REL020, REL039, REL021, REL040, REL022]

[POS001, DIR002, REL008]
- A02 rose from about 2 to about 9 in 100 traces with completely changed successors, but only 4 versus 18 traces support this. [OCC001, DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the choice of what runs after A20 flipped between two existing groups of activities. In the earlier window, a case typically executed A24, A25 and A26 together after A20 (about 4 in 5 cases, in no fixed order), while A21, A22 and A23 formed a rare alternative (about 1 in 5); in the later window this reversed — A21, A22 and A23 run together after A20 in about 4 in 5 cases (still in no fixed order) and the old trio survives in about 1 in 5. Consistently, A30, A32 and A33, which previously ran alongside the old trio, now typically run alongside the new one. The participation changes are large and directly measured, A20's rerouting is observed with full support in both windows, and the roughly 60 qualifying items far exceed the fewer than 1 expected by chance among the 180 tested units, so the pattern as a whole is not what a stable process produces. [OCC002, OCC005, DIR003, REL010, REL015, REL023, REL036, REL020, REL038]

The evidence cannot distinguish a rerouting within one process version from a shift in the mix of two coexisting variants, since both trios were already executed in about 1 in 5 cases in the earlier window; it also cannot show whether A24–A26 became optional or were dropped from most cases. [REL036, REL015, DIR003]

Other differences: A10 became rarer (about 17 to about 7 in 100 traces), appears earlier in the trace order and no longer passes almost exclusively to A33; A02 roughly quadrupled in frequency with entirely different successors, though only 4 versus 18 traces back it. [POS001, DIR002, REL008, OCC001, DIR001]
