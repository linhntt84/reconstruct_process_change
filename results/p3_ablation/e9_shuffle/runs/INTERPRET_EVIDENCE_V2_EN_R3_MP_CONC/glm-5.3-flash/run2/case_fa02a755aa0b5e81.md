### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 changed its placement, together with A05, A06, A11 and A12: in the earlier window A01 ran late in the trace, always after A11 or A12 and immediately before A05, whereas in the later window A01 opens the trace and precedes A06, while A11 and A12, which previously reached A05 only through A01, now connect to it directly. [DIR001, POS001, LAG001, REL002, REL003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG004, LAG005]

The placement hypothesis is shown directly by four agreeing views of the same behaviour — A01's successor changed from A05 to A06, its order against A11 and A12 reversed, its distance to A05 grew from one step to about four, and its relative position moved from about 0.83 to 0 — each resting on 43–54 traces per window (about 22–27 in 100). With 25 qualifying items against roughly 0.22 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole, although the items describing the A01/A11/A12 block reflect one underlying restructuring rather than independent confirmations. The evidence cannot fully distinguish A01 having moved within one continuing variant from a variant that starts with A01 replacing the previously observed one, and positions are event orders, so no clock-time shift is implied. [DIR001, POS001, LAG001, REL002]

Other differences: A02, observed in about 27 of 100 traces earlier, was not observed in any of the 200 later traces (non-observation, not proof of removal), and A07, which previously occurred only together with A02 about a quarter into the trace, later occurs only without A02, near the start, leading mainly to A03 (about 48%) and A13 (about 29%) instead of back to A02 (about 34% earlier). [OCC001, POS002, DIR002, REL006, DIR003, POS004]
