### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the trace structure around A02 was reshaped, involving A01, A02, A04 and A05. On the A04 path (about half of the traces in both windows), A05 now runs between A04 and A02: what was A04 followed directly by A02, one step later, now reads A04, then A05, then A02 two steps after A04, with A05 present in about 46 in 100 later traces after being absent from all 200 earlier ones. On the other path, A01 no longer runs ahead of A02: the about 50 in 100 earlier traces that opened with A01 and ran into A02 correspond in share to the about 54 in 100 later traces that begin with A02 and contain none of A01, A04 or A05, so A02 — previously always the final event — is now the opening event in most traces. The support is direct and consistent: the successor profiles of A04 and A05 flipped completely, the measured A04–A02 distance grew from one step to two, and the co-occurrence profiles of A01–A02, A02–A05 and A04–A05 moved as this reshaping predicts; 15 control-flow items qualified where about 0.09 would be expected without change, each item's difference exceeded about 199 of 200 random re-splits, and the magnitudes (about 101 of 200 traces down to none for A01; none up to about 92 of 200 for A05) exceed what rare-variant sampling in 200-trace windows produces.
