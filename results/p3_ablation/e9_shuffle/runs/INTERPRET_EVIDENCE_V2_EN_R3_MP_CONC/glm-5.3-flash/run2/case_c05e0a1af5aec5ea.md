### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the recurring cycle around A09 was restructured: A05 and A08 exchanged their places in the loop (A09 now feeds A05, A04 and A13 feed A08, and the A16 repetition loop is now attached to A08), while A01 left the cycle and now opens traces at the very beginning with no observed tie to the loop, and the previously unobserved A17 closes the cycle from A08 back into A09. Every affected edge shows the successor change directly, and all step-distance shifts move in the direction this rewiring predicts. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR007, DIR008, LAG009, LAG012, LAG014]

Screening qualified 42 control-flow items where fewer than 1 (about 0.4) would be expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole cannot be a sampling artefact; note, however, that the many LAG and REL items largely re-describe the same rewiring from different angles rather than constituting independent confirmations. [DIR005, LAG012, REL004]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows captured a different mix of pre-existing process variants rather than a modified process, although A17's complete absence earlier and A01's empty successor profile later are hard to reconcile with a pure mix shift. It also remains unclear why A01 shows no outgoing transitions in the later window (for example, whether it now connects to activities outside the analysed pairs), and a few distance items rest on only 4–6 observations. [OCC004, DIR001, LAG006]

Other differences: participation fell for A02 (about 50 to 38 in 100 traces) and A03 (about 29 to 16 in 100 traces); no relational evidence links these decreases to the cycle rewiring. [OCC001, OCC002]
