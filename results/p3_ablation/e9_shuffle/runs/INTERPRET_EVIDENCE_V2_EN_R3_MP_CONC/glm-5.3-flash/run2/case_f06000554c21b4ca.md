### Observed differences

- The dominant change is a complete reversal of execution order between two groups of activities. In the earlier window, A26 came before A35, A36 and A41 in about 99–100 in 100 traces, and A27 likewise came before each of them in about 99–100 in 100; in the later window this is inverted, with A35, A36 and A41 each coming before A26 in about 99–100 in 100 and before A27 in about 98–99 in 100. All six activities still occur together in every trace in both windows, so participation did not change — only their relative order. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Event positions shift accordingly: A35, A36 and A41 moved from a mean relative position of about 0.58–0.59 of the trace to about 0.41, while A26 moved from about 0.53 to about 0.62 and A27 from about 0.38 to about 0.47. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- The wiring around this group changed as well. In the earlier window, A25's outgoing transitions went to A27 in about 99 in 100 cases; in the later window they split almost equally among A35, A36 and A41 (about 33 in 100 each), with A27 rarely observed as A25's successor (about 1 in 200). A26 previously led to A41, A35 or A36 (about 37, 34 and 28 in 100 of its outgoing transitions); in the later window it leads to A30 in about 99 in 100. [DIR001, DIR002]
- The group's exit changed too: earlier, each of A35, A36 and A41 led to A30 in roughly 28–37 of 100 outgoing transitions; in the later window A30 is almost never their successor (about 1 in 200) and A27 has become a main successor (about 31–34 in 100) alongside the other group members. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- Pair distances confirm this: A25 reached A27 in one step in about 99 in 100 cases earlier, and in 4–5 steps in the later window; A26 reached A30 in 4–5 steps earlier and in one step (about 99 in 100) in the later window. [LAG001, LAG003]
- A few lag items about pairs that became rare (A26 with A35/A36/A41 and the group members with A30 in the later window; A25 with A35 and A35 with A27 in the earlier one) rest on only 1–2 observations in one window; they point the same way but are individually weak. [LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the three-activity group A35/A36/A41 was relocated along the flow. In the earlier window the sequence ran A25 → A27 immediately, then A26 led into the group, which ended in A30; in the later window A25 leads into the group, the group leads to A27, and A26 leads immediately to A30 — that is, the group now runs before A27 and A26 instead of after A26, and A30 directly follows A26 instead of terminating the group. The variable internal order among A35, A36 and A41 (each a successor of the others in about a third of outgoing transitions) is present in both windows and did not change. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Supporting signs: 26 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.63 expected if nothing had changed, so the ensemble is far beyond what random re-splits produce, and each item's difference exceeds that seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits. The reversal is shown directly by relational evidence with full support (200 traces per window for each pair) and is consistent across the successor, distance and position indicators. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003]

Limitations: the link between A27 and A26 (and whatever leads into A26 in the later window) is not directly observed, so that part of the later sequence is inferred from mean positions only; and the data establish the reversal of relative order but cannot distinguish whether the group moved earlier or A27/A26 moved later — both readings fit the same evidence. [POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR002]

Other differences: the only remaining component changes are single-trace successor edges (about 1 in 200 outgoing transitions) appearing or disappearing at the periphery of A25, A35, A36 and A41; at this rate they are consistent with sampling noise. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
