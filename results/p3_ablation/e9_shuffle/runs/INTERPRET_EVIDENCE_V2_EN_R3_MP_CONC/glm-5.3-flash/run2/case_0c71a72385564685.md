- Three activities that were not observed in the earlier window appear regularly in the later one: A19 rises from 0 of 200 traces to about 38 in 100, A25 from 0 to about 35 in 100, and A20 from about 0.5 in 100 to about 30 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

- A26 itself still occurs in every trace in both windows and moved only slightly later (median position 0.36 to about 0.39). [POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new intermediate step was added between A41 and A26: in the earlier window traces passed from A41 directly to A26, whereas in the later window A41 is followed by exactly one of three alternative activities — A19, A25 or A20, in roughly 38/35/30 of 100 cases — which then leads on to A26. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR002, DIR003, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL001, REL002, REL005, LAG001]

The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: A41's dominant successor switches from A26 to the three newcomers, each newcomer leads almost always to A26, the ordering items show the chain A41 → new activity → A26 with the three mutually exclusive, and the A41–A26 distance shifts from 1 to mostly 2 events. The volume of qualifying evidence (21 control-flow items against roughly 0.5 expected in an unchanged process) is far beyond what chance typically produces. Limitations: the evidence cannot show how the choice among the three alternatives is made, and the new step is not perfectly uniform — about 13 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions still go to other activities, and about 14 in 100 of A41–A26 pairs lie 3 events apart. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a broader re-routing of this fragment rather than a single isolated addition. [DIR004, LAG001]

Other differences: A02's outgoing profile changed — A26, previously about 26 in 100 of A02's outgoing transitions, is no longer observed as its successor, and the remaining transitions spread over more targets — but this rests on only about 35–44 outgoing transitions and is not explained by the main hypothesis.
