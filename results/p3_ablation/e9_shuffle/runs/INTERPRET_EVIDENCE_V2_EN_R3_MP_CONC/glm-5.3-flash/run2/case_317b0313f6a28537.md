### Observed differences

- The execution order between the activities A19, A20 and A25 on one side and A26/A27 on the other reversed. In the earlier window, whenever one of A19, A20 or A25 co-occurred with A26 or A27 (about 31–39 in 100 traces), A26/A27 came first in essentially all of those cases (e.g., A26 before A19 in about 38 of 100 traces, the reverse never); in the later window the trio member comes first at nearly the same frequencies (A19 before A26 in about 36 of 100 traces, A26 first in about 0). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- A19, A20 and A25 now run much earlier in the case: their mean relative position fell from about 0.60–0.61 to about 0.32–0.34, while participation barely moved (A19 in 39% vs 36% of traces, A20 in 32% vs 35%, A25 in 31% vs 32%). A26, A27 and A28 shifted only slightly later (about 0.02–0.04 on the position scale). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- The trio's outgoing flow changed: before, A19, A20 and A25 each split their outgoing transitions roughly half to A28 and half to A32; in the later window each leads to A26 in about 88–94 of 100 observed outgoing transitions. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- A45 changed target: before it led to A26 in about 85 of 100 outgoing transitions; in the later window it leads to A19, A20 or A25 (about 29, 29 and 27 in 100, together about 85) and almost never to A26. [DIR006]
- The A27–A28–A32 chain became near-deterministic: A27 leads to A28 in about 98 of 100 outgoing transitions (before about 47, with about half of the rest going to A19/A20/A25), and A28 leads to A32 in about 96 of 100 (before about 50, again with large branches to the trio). [DIR004, DIR005]
- Step gaps agree with this rerouting: A27→A28 is now adjacent in about 98 of 100 cases (before about 48, half at two steps), A28→A32 adjacent in about 96 (before about 51), A27→A32 mostly two steps (before three steps in about 96 of 100), and A45→A26 now takes two to three steps (before one step in about 86 of 100). [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]
- The old links from the trio into A28/A32 (A19→A32, A25→A28, A25→A32) are nearly absent in the later window, but only 1–3 after-window observations stand behind them, so this is weak corroboration. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- A15 still occurs in every trace but repeats less often: mean occurrences per trace fell from about 1.9 to about 1.6, and the share of traces containing repeats from about 47 in 100 to about 38 in 100. [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A19, A20 and A25 execute before A26 — entered from A45 — whereas in the earlier window they executed after A26 and led into A28 and A32, and that A27→A28→A32 now runs as a near-fixed sequence without branches to the trio. The order reversal is shown directly for all six pairings of the trio with A26/A27 at stable co-occurrence levels; the successor changes (A45 now feeding the trio, the trio now feeding A26, A27 and A28 no longer branching to the trio), the trio's earlier positions with stable participation, and the gap changes (A45→A26 lengthened from one to two–three steps, the A27/A28 chain tightened) all point the same way. Screening qualified 26 control-flow items where about 0.6 would be expected without any change, so the ensemble is unlikely to be a sampling artefact, and each item also exceeds about 99.5% of random re-splits. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

Limitations: A26's outgoing transitions were not profiled, so what follows A26 in the later window is unobserved and the rerouting is established only up to A26; POS and LAG use event positions rather than clock time. An important competing explanation that cannot be fully excluded is a shift in the mix of long-existing variants, although the clean flips — the old order occurs in essentially no co-occurring trace in the later window and the new order in essentially none before — make a pure mix shift hard to maintain with 200 traces per window. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

Other differences: A15 now repeats less often per trace (mean about 1.9 vs 1.6 occurrences; repeats in about 47% vs 38% of traces) while still appearing in every trace; no relational evidence ties this to the reordering above. [OCC001]
