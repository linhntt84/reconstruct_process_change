### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 each appeared in about 47 in 100 traces (once per trace when present); in the later window each of them appears in essentially all traces (about 995 in 1,000), still about once per trace, with A19 occasionally occurring twice. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The outgoing transitions of A41 were redirected: earlier they led to A26 in about 53 in 100 cases and to A19 in about 45 in 100; in the later window about 92 in 100 lead to A19 and only about 1 in 200 still lead to A26. [DIR002]
- Accordingly, the distance from A41 to A26 grew: earlier A26 followed A41 within one step in about 53 in 100 cases (4–5 steps in the remaining ~47 in 100); in the later window the one-step case has practically disappeared (about 1 in 200) and the gap is 4 steps in about 89 in 100 cases and 5 steps in about 11 in 100. [LAG001]
- Pairs among A19, A20 and A25, and their pairs with A41 and A26, co-occurred in about 47 in 100 traces earlier — in the other half one of the two appeared alone — and co-occur in about 995 in 1,000 traces in the later window, with the earlier order preserved: A41 before A19 (about 47 → 97 in 100), A19 before A25 (about 47 → 98 in 100), A25 before A20 (about 46 → 995 in 1,000), A20 before A26 (about 46 → 99 in 100). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL012, REL013]
- A42 occurred in essentially every trace in both windows, but earlier it appeared without A19/A20/A25 in about 53 in 100 traces, whereas in the later window it co-occurs with them in about 995 in 1,000; its order relative to them stays mixed in both windows, with the interleaved/repeated share rising from about 17 to about 46 in 100 traces. - A45 appeared in about 60 in 100 traces earlier and about 62 in 100 later; earlier it often appeared without A19/A20/A25 (about 33 in 100 traces), in the later window essentially always together with them, with A19 preceding it when both occur. [REL006, REL011, REL015]
- Minor shifts: A26's mean event position moved slightly later (about 0.41 → 0.43 of the trace) and A41's slightly earlier (about 0.33 → 0.31); A20's outgoing profile is practically unchanged (A20 → A26 in about 96 in 100 earlier and about 99 in 100 later), its flagged difference being negligible in size. [POS001, POS002, DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A19, A25 and A20, which in the earlier window were executed in only about half of the traces, became near-mandatory in the later window, and that the direct transition A41 → A26, which skipped them in roughly half of the earlier traces, has all but disappeared: A41 now leads to A19 in about 92 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, so traces typically run A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 → A26 in that order, as the earlier minority variant already did. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, LAG001, REL001, REL003, REL007, REL008]

The change is shown directly by the rerouting of A41's successors, the participation rise of A19/A20/A25, and the growing A41–A26 distance, and consistently by some twenty items across the occurrence, relation, direction and lag indicators; the 23 flagged items are roughly 36 times the 0.64 items expected by chance among the 129 tested units, so the set as a whole is not what an unchanged process produces. Limitations: most relation items describe one underlying trace change rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot show whether the old bypass route A41 → A26 was removed entirely or survives as a rare route (about 1 in 200 later traces), which the finite windows cannot distinguish. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, LAG001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015]

Other differences: A42, present throughout, now co-occurs with A19/A20/A25 in nearly every trace and shows a higher interleaved share relative to them; A45 no longer appears without A19/A20/A25; A26 sits slightly later and A41 slightly earlier within the trace. These look like consequences of the main change rather than separate differences, and the evidence cannot rule out that the shortened bypass variant persists at a low level.
