### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the segment around A22 ran as a fixed chain: in all 200 earlier traces A22 was immediately followed by A23, A23 was immediately followed by A24, and A24 came two steps after A22. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, DIR004, DIR005]
- In the later window, three activities that were absent before — A14, A15 and A20 — appear in about 29, 41 and 30 of 100 traces respectively, exactly once per trace, around the middle of the trace (normalised position near 0.51). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- The three new activities are mutually exclusive alternatives: no later trace contains two of them, and together they cover essentially every trace (about 199 of 200). [REL001, REL002, REL006, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- Each new activity sits between A22 and A24: A22 precedes it in every co-occurring trace, and it precedes A24 in every co-occurring trace. Relative to A23 the order is mixed — for example, A15 precedes A23 in about 24 of 100 traces while A23 precedes A15 in about 18 of 100. [REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, REL004, REL008, REL011]
- Successor profiles changed accordingly. Before, every outgoing transition of A22 went to A23 and every outgoing transition of A23 went to A24. In the later window, about 44 of 100 of A22's transitions go to A23 and about 56 of 100 go to A15, A20 or A14; about 57 of 100 of A23's transitions still go to A24 and about 43 of 100 go to the new activities. Each new activity is itself followed by A23 in roughly 53–58 of 100 of its occurrences and by A24 otherwise. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- Step distances widened by one where a new activity was inserted: the A22→A23 distance is now two steps in about 56 of 100 traces (previously always one), A23→A24 is two steps in about 44 of 100 (previously always one), and A22→A24 is three steps in about 995 of 1,000 traces (previously always two). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural addition in the segment between A22 and A24: in the later window, one of three new activities — A14, A15 or A20 — executes in nearly every case as a mutually exclusive alternative, inserted either directly after A22 (before A23, in roughly 56 of 100 cases) or directly after A23 (before A24, in roughly 44 of 100 cases), after which the flow continues to A24. In the earlier window this segment was the fixed chain A22 → A23 → A24 with no activity in between. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The change is shown directly rather than inferred from shifting path shares: participation rises from zero to 29–41 in 100 traces, new outgoing edges from A22 and A23 appear, the order relations place each new activity between A22 and A24, and the step distances widen by exactly one. The numbers cross-check in detail — for example, A14's roughly 58 traces split into about 31 where it immediately follows A22 and about 27 where it immediately follows A23, matching the successor shares of A22 and A23. Screening also found 27 qualifying control-flow items where about 0.4 would be expected if nothing had changed, so chance alone cannot explain the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, REL005, LAG002, POS001]

Limitations: all evidence is control-flow, so the evidence cannot show how the choice among the three activities or between the two insertion points is made, and about 1 in 200 later traces contains none of the three. Several items describe the same insertion behaviour and are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that A14, A15 and A20 already existed earlier at a rate too low to appear in 200 traces; however, any earlier participation near the observed 29–41 in 100 would have been detected, so only a near-zero earlier rate remains compatible. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006]

Other differences: A22's relative position moved slightly earlier (median about 0.48 to about 0.46 of the trace), which is consistent with an activity being added after it rather than an independent change. [POS004]
