### Observed differences

- The activities A04, A06 and A10 occur in far fewer traces: in the earlier window each appeared in about 50 in 100 traces (99–101 of 200), in the later window in only about 20 in 100 (40–43); mean occurrences per trace fell from about 0.5 to about 0.24. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the earlier window these three formed a rigid either/or structure: A06 and A10 always appeared together (in the same ~50 in 100 traces) and never shared a trace with A04, so every trace contained exactly one of A04 or the A06+A10 pair. In the later window about 66 in 100 traces contain none of the three, and A04 and A06 now occasionally appear together (~8 in 100). [REL007, REL010, REL013]
- A02, present in every trace in both windows, no longer feeds this branch: before, about half of A02's outgoing transitions went to A04 (~25 in 100), A06 (~13) or A10 (~13); after, none do, while transitions to A05 rose from about 23 to about 46 in 100 and those to A03 and A09 roughly doubled. [DIR001, REL001, REL002, REL003]
- The branch is now entered from A09 and A03: A09's outgoing transitions, previously almost only to A08 (~62 in 100) and A07 (~37), now include A04 (~21), A06 (~14) and A10 (~14), with A08 no longer observed; A03 likewise gained A04 (~23), A06 (~14) and A10 (~8). A09→A04 now follows within one step in about 38 in 100 cases (before: 3–5 steps), and A03→A06/A10 within one to two steps (before: 3–5 steps). [DIR005, DIR002, LAG013, LAG014, LAG016, LAG006, LAG007, LAG009]
- Distances from A02 and A08 to the branch lengthened: A02→A04 followed within one step in about 61 in 100 cases before but never after (now typically 2–4 steps); A08→A04 moved from two steps (~67 in 100) to three steps (~75 in 100), A08→A10 from two–three to four–five steps, and A02→A06/A10 similarly shifted from one–two to three–five steps. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG010, LAG011]
- The branch's exits changed: before, A04 essentially never had a successor (1 observed outgoing transition despite ~99 occurrences) and about half of A06's and A10's occurrences had none, while the pair otherwise led only to each other; after, every observed occurrence of all three has a successor — A06 and A10 split roughly 56/44 between each other and A08, and A04 leads to A08 in all 47 observed transitions (its earlier successor A01 rests on a single observation). [DIR003, DIR004, DIR006]
- When it occurs, the branch now sits earlier in the trace by event position (mean relative position about 0.9–1.0 before vs about 0.6 after), consistent with it no longer terminating traces. [POS002, POS003, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch formed by A04, A06 and A10 is executed in far fewer cases and is reconnected: in the earlier window about half of all traces ran either A04 or the inseparable pair A06+A10, entered directly from A02 and ending there; in the later window only about one in five traces reaches the branch, entry comes from A09 and A03 at one- to two-step distances, A02 continues mainly to A05 instead, and the branch's activities hand over to A08 rather than ending the trace. This rests on direct participation measurements for all three activities, on successor-profile changes at A02, A03, A09, A04, A06 and A10, and on relational, distance and positional items that all point the same way; 45 items qualified against fewer than one expected by chance among the 69 tested units, and every item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR005, DIR003, DIR004, REL007, REL013, LAG013, LAG001, POS002]

Limitations: the items overlap heavily — the three occurrence items and most relation items describe the same trace-level change, so they are not independent confirmations, and several distance items rest on fewer than about 30 observed pairs. The evidence cannot rule out that the later window contains a different mix of cases that less often reaches this branch, rather than a rewiring; the complete disappearance of A02's edges into the branch despite ~219 observed outgoing transitions argues against a mere frequency shift, and the before-side of the A04 successor change rests on one observation. [DIR001, DIR003, LAG009]

Other differences: A02 occurs later within the trace (mean relative position 0.45 → 0.62) and A08 occurs later (0.57 → 0.72), with A08's distance from A02, A03 and A09 lengthening from about one step to two–three steps; these may be by-products of the same reshaping, and a position shift alone does not show a move in clock time. [POS001, POS004, LAG003, LAG008, LAG015]
