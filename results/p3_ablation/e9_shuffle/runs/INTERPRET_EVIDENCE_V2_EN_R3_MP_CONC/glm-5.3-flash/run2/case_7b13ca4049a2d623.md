### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a step A02 now executes between A06 and A01 in every case: in the earlier window A06 led directly to A01, whereas in the later window A06 leads to A02, A02 leads to A01, and A01 follows A06 two events later instead of immediately, with A02 running exactly once per trace at a fixed position. This is shown directly by the rerouting of A06's outgoing transitions, the order relations between the activity pairs, and the changed A06-to-A01 distance, and it is consistent across all nine control-flow items; 9 qualifying items against roughly 0.04 expected without a change cannot be explained by random re-splitting of the two windows. A limitation is that the evidence covers only A01, A02 and A06 and their immediate succession; in particular, it cannot rule out that A02 already existed as a rare step elsewhere that simply was not observed in the earlier sample (0 of 200 traces) and has now become a fixed step in this position.
