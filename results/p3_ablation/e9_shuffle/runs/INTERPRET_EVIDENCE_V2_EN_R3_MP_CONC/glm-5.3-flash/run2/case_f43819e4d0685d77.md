### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A02 was added as a new final step executed directly after A06: earlier cases typically ended with A06 and never included A02, while in the later window every case runs A06 followed by A02 exactly once. The change is shown directly by the participation jump (0 of 200 vs 200 of 200 cases), by the pair relation flipping from "A06 only" to full co-occurrence in a fixed order, and by A06's outgoing transitions now leading exclusively to A02, with A02 at one constant event position. Five control-flow items qualified where about 0.05 would be expected without a change, and each difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, so sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole.
