Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the control flow was restructured around the disappearance of A02, A03 and A08: these activities, previously in roughly 47, 21 and 47 of 100 traces, were unobserved in the later window; the transitions through them were rerouted (A10 now feeds A01 instead of A08, A05 and A18 lead to A10 instead of A02, A13 and A19 lead to A05/A18 instead of A03, A01 now leads to A11 instead of the A06/A07/A14 tail, and A16 now feeds that tail instead of A01), while A07 and A14 became rare, successor-less end-of-trace activities.
