### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural restructuring of the segment between A20 and A26, involving A20, A23, A21, A22, A26 and the trio A31, A32, A37. Before, every trace executed A20, then the trio in mixed order, then A23, then A21, then A22, then A26; in the later window this segment splits into two mutually exclusive variants — in about 47 in 100 traces the trio executes, now after A21 and before A26, and A22 does not run, while in about 54 in 100 traces A22 runs and the trio does not; in both variants A23 immediately follows A20, where it previously followed the trio. [LAG001, LAG005, REL002, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL017, DIR001, DIR002]

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: relational items capture both the collapse of A22–trio co-occurrence (from 100 in 100 to about 1 in 200) and the order swap between the trio and A23/A21; successor profiles change exactly where the relocation predicts (A20 now to A23, trio members now to A26, A21 now to A22 or the trio); and the participation and relative-position shifts move in the same direction for every affected activity. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008]

The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 47 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.5 expected by chance (about ninety times as many), so the pattern as a whole is not a sampling artefact; most items rest on 94–200 traces per window, though the six lag items documenting the lost close successions rest on a single after-trace each. [OCC001-OCC004, DIR001-DIR005, LAG001-LAG008, POS001-POS008, REL001-REL022]

Limitations: the items are not independent confirmations — the three A22-versus-trio relations and the three occurrence drops describe one and the same split — and, because only executed traces are visible, the evidence cannot rule out that the two after-variants reflect a different mix of incoming cases rather than a change in routing. [REL011, REL012, REL013, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Other differences: A20 sits slightly later in the trace (mean relative position about 0.34 → 0.36) and A26 slightly earlier (about 0.66 → 0.63); both shifts are small and consistent with the restructuring above but are not part of the hypothesis. [POS001, POS005]
