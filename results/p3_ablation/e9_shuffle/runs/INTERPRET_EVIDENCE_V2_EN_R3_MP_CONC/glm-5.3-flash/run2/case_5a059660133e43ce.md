### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening flagged 14 control-flow items where an unchanged process would typically produce about 0.23 (46 units tested), so the volume of flagged differences is far beyond what random re-splits of these windows produce. The evidence supports the possibility that the closing part of the process now runs differently: A01, unobserved in the earlier window, appears in the later window as a final step executed once after A02 in about 22 in 100 traces, while A06 — which earlier closed the traces containing it — now runs around the middle and is always followed by A15, so that A15 is reached via A06 rather than directly from A02. [OCC001, POS001, REL001, DIR001, DIR002, POS002, LAG002]

The support is direct on several complementary angles, though they partly describe the same traces: participation (A01 from 0 to 44 of 200 traces), position (A01 fixed at the final position; A06 moving from a fixed final position to a mid-trace median of about 0.5 at unchanged participation), relation (A01–A02 co-occurrence from never to about 22 in 100; A01 never co-occurring with A06 or A15 in the later window) and direction (A02 gains A01 and loses A15 as a direct successor; A06 gains A15). A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is the surfacing of rare variants in finite samples, but the magnitudes (0 of 200 traces vs 44 of 200 for A01; 27 of 27 A06 events at the final position earlier vs a mid-trace median later) make pure sampling an unlikely account; what remains open is whether A01 replaced a specific earlier behaviour or was appended to traces that previously ended right after A02.
