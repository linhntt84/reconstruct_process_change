### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 took over the structural role previously filled by A05: the mandatory, once-per-trace activity running between the A02/A06/A07 block and A03 changed from A05 to A01. In the earlier window A05 ran in every trace at the middle position and always led to A03, fed by A02, A06 and A07; in the later window A05 is unobserved, A01 runs in every trace at the same position, always leads to A03, and receives the corresponding transitions from A02 (about 75 in 100), A06 (about 30 in 100) and A07 (about 66 in 100). The support is direct — participation, positional and edge-level indicators all show the same substitution — and consistent across all 18 flagged items, a volume far above the roughly 0.13 items expected by chance for an unchanged process, so random re-splits of the two 200-trace windows cannot explain the pattern as a whole. The main limitation is that the activity codes are anonymous: the evidence shows that the occupant of this structural slot changed, but cannot establish whether A01 is a reconfigured or renamed successor of A05 or a genuinely different activity inserted into the same slot.
