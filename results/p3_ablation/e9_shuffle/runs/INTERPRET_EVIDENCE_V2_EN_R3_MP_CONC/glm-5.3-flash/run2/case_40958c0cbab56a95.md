### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A26, A27 and A28 each ran in every one of the 200 traces (about one occurrence per trace); in the later window they appear in only about 3 of 200 traces (~2 in 100) [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, POS004, POS005, POS006].

Consistently, A29's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (0.44 → 0.40) and A44's slightly later (0.31 → 0.33) [LAG009, POS007, POS008].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: the three activities A26, A27 and A28, which earlier executed in every case (in no fixed order among themselves), no longer appear, and a single activity chosen per case from A19, A20 and A21 now runs in that position, so this part of the process executes with one activity instead of three. The signs are direct and consistent across indicators: the participation flip (200/200 → ~3/200 for the old trio; 2–4/200 → 60–75/200 for the new trio with near-zero mutual co-occurrence), the successor change at A44 and the convergence of A19/A20/A21 onto A29, the preserved A44-before-middle-before-A29 ordering, and the A44-to-A29 distance falling from ~4–5 steps to ~2 steps [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL013, REL014, REL021, DIR007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG009]. The screening produced 81 qualifying items where about 0.89 would be expected without any change, so the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by sampling noise [screening].

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A26/A27/A28 were not observed in the later window, so the evidence cannot distinguish their removal from their being skipped in every sampled case; and the "exactly one of three" reading rests on near-zero mutual co-occurrence, so rare traces containing none or two of the trio cannot be excluded. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows capture a shifted mix of pre-existing case types rather than a modification of a single process, although the all-or-nothing participation shift makes pure sampling variation implausible.
