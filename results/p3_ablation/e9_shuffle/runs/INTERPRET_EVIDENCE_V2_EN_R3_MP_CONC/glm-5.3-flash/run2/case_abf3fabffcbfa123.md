### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The screening flagged 62 control-flow items where fewer than 1 (about 0.79) would be expected by chance across the 158 tested units, so the volume of flagged differences cannot be explained by random re-splits of an unchanged process [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL022]. The main hypothesis is that the branch composed of A32, A33 and A38 — executed together in about half of the earlier cases — is no longer executed in the later window, while A27, which previously alternated with that branch, is now executed in virtually every case, typically after A14, A15 and A26 and before A28; in effect a choice between two branches collapsed onto one, with the A32/A33/A38 branch skipped and A27 near-mandatory. This rests on participation changes measured over all 200 traces per window (about 97–99 → 1 traces for the branch activities; 103 → 199 for A27), on the before-window mutual exclusivity (co-occurrence 0) turning into near-universal A27 presence, and on consistent relational and directional items such as A26 now leading almost exclusively to A27 [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC004, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL021, DIR006].

Limitations: the later-window profiles of A32, A33 and A38 rest on a single trace each, so their residual behaviour cannot be characterised; and the evidence cannot distinguish these activities having been removed from the process from the branch simply no longer being taken — near-zero participation looks the same either way [OCC005, POS004, LAG004].

Other differences: A04 is bypassed in most cases — its participation fell from about 68 to 22 in 100 traces, and A14 now leads directly to A15 in about 82 in 100 of its outgoing edges instead of routing through A04, with A04 sitting later in the cases where it does occur [OCC002, DIR004, POS001]. A02 and A08 occur in roughly three to four times as many traces as before (5 → 15 and 5 → 21 in 100) with more varied successors [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003]. The A26→A28 distance consolidated from a mix of 2 and 4 events to consistently 2, consistent with the dropped branch no longer sitting between them [LAG003].
