### Observed differences

- A01 disappeared: about 48 in 100 traces contained it in the earlier window (roughly one occurrence each), while no trace in the later window did. [OCC001, POS001]
- A08 appeared: it was not observed before, but ran in about 48 in 100 later traces (about 1.1 occurrences per trace) at a similar mid-trace position to the one A01 used to occupy. [OCC002, POS002]
- The successor profile was handed over: A01's outgoing transitions (about 51% to A09, 24% to A10, 24% to A16) vanished, and A08's outgoing transitions in the later window are almost the same mix (about 56% to A09, 25% to A10, 19% to A16). [DIR001, DIR005]

- Relationship patterns mirror the swap one-for-one: A01-without-A08 in 48% of traces became A08-without-A01 in 48%; A05's exclusive partner switched from A01 to A08; A03 and A08 went from never co-occurring to co-occurring in about 48 in 100 traces. [REL003, REL002, REL014, REL011]
- The ending role swapped between A11 and A15: before, A11 sat at a fixed late position in about 52% of traces with no observed outgoing transitions, while A15 appeared in about 32% of traces mid-process and always led to A03; after, A15 holds the fixed late position in about 52% of traces with no observed outgoing transitions, and A11 appears in about 29% of traces at variable positions, now leading to A03. - A11 now co-occurs much less with its former partners (with A03 from about 52% to 0, with A05 from 52% to 16%), while A15 co-occurs more with A09, A10, A16 and A17. [REL012, REL015, REL016, REL025, REL027, REL032, REL033]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was dropped from the process and A08 took over the position it previously occupied. Before, A01 ran in about 48% of traces and passed control mainly to A09, A10 and A16; in the later window A01 is not observed, A08 runs at the same participation level with a nearly identical successor mix, and the predecessors that previously continued into A01 (A17 exclusively, A03 for about a fifth of its outgoing transitions) now continue into A08. The co-occurrence structure mirrors this one-for-one exchange, and with 53 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected if nothing had changed, the ensemble of differences is far beyond what random re-splits of stable behaviour produce. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR005, DIR008, DIR003, REL003, REL011, REL014]

Limitations: these indicators are aggregates, so they show that A01 was not executed and that A08 appeared with a matching profile, but not that A08 covers A01's role in every individual trace; zero participation strictly means only that A01 was not observed in the later sample. The evidence also cannot establish whether the A01/A08 handover and the A11/A15 ending-role swap are one connected restructuring or two independent changes. [OCC001, POS003, POS004]

Other differences: the ending-role swap between A11 and A15, with A05 and A18 rerouted and the step distances between A03, A11, A15 and A18 shifted, stands as a further structural change beyond the main hypothesis [POS003, POS004, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, DIR009, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]; A02's outgoing mix changed only marginally [DIR002].
