### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, an optional block of three mutually alternative activities — A19, A20 and A25 — is executed inside the previously rigid A27 → A28 → A29 succession. Before, A27 passed directly to A28 and A28 directly to A29 in about 99 of 100 traces and the three activities never appeared; after, A27 hands over to one of them in about 55 of 100 cases, they run mid-trace and lead on to A28 or A29, stretching the A27–A28 and A28–A29 gaps by one step in roughly half of the traces. Since the block was absent before while A28 and A29 still run in essentially every trace, this is an inserted optional step (the activities became available and are taken in a large minority of cases), not a re-weighting of previously observed paths. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS004, POS005]

The screening produced 29 qualifying items in the control-flow family against roughly 0.54 expected if nothing had changed, so the ensemble cannot be explained by random sampling, and the occurrence, direction, relation, lag and position items all describe one coherent structure rather than independent confirmations. [OCC002, DIR004, REL007, LAG001, POS002]

Limitations: with 200 traces per window the evidence cannot distinguish a newly added block from a variant that previously ran in at most about 1–2 of 100 cases and simply went unsampled; and the exact insertion point is not fully pinned down, since in a minority of traces the new activity follows A28 rather than preceding it (A28 before A20 in about 17 of 100 co-occurrences, before A19 in about 13). [REL008, REL004]

Other differences: A10's participation rose from about 5 to about 13 in 100 traces; it is not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001]
