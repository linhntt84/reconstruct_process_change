### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window the process runs a longer fixed sequence: A04 is followed by A03, then A05, then A06, then A07, and A01 closes the trace, whereas earlier each trace went directly from A04 to A07 and ended there. A01, A03, A05 and A06, unobserved in the earlier sample, now take part in every trace exactly once; A07 has lost its terminal position to A01; and the direct A04→A07 step is replaced by a route through A03, A05 and A06. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS006, LAG001]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: participation for the four added activities moves from 0 to 200 of 200 traces with exactly one occurrence each; every monitored pair co-occurs in all later traces in a single order; the five successor profiles each show one uniform successor; and the position and lag shifts (A07 from position 1.00 to 0.83, A04 from 0.50 to 0.17, A04→A07 distance from 1 to 4 steps) match this sequence exactly. With 30 qualifying items against roughly 0.15 expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeding about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, sampling noise cannot explain the pattern as a whole. Because the four activities were unobserved earlier, this is an addition of steps to the observed behaviour, not a shift in how often existing paths are taken. [screening, OCC001, LAG001, REL001, REL014, DIR001, DIR005]

Limitations: the evidence covers only the tested activities, so changes involving other activities below the thresholds cannot be excluded; zero participation earlier only means these activities were not observed in that sample, so it remains possible they previously occurred in a variant too rare to appear in the earlier 200 traces. The many indicators also describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
