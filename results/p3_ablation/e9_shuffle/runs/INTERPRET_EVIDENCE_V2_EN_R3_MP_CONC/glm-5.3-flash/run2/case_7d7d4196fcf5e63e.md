- Participation shifted markedly: in the earlier window A08 ran in every trace (about 2 occurrences per trace) and A01, A02 and A06 each appeared in about 49 in 100 traces; in the later window A02 appears in all 200 traces (about 1.8 occurrences per trace), while A08 fell to about 45 in 100 traces (about 0.8 occurrences), A01 to about 30, A06 to about 29 (mean about 0.7) and A07 to about 15. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the flow was rewired around A02 and A08: in the later window A02 is a mandatory, first-position activity (present in all traces, median position 0, about 1.8 occurrences per trace) that passes flow to A04 and A03, whereas earlier it was optional (about 49 in 100 traces), sat mid-trace and led only to A01; A08, previously the mandatory early hub running about twice per trace and branching to A03 and A04, is now optional (about 45 in 100 traces), sits later, and leads into A02; A06 now routes through A08 instead of directly to A02, and A03 gained a direct link to A01, which itself became rarer (about 30 in 100 traces, about 0.4 occurrences per trace). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS002, POS004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC005, REL006, REL009, REL011]

The hypothesis rests on direct successor changes in all four direction profiles, and the occurrence, position and relation items point the same way; 64 control-flow items qualified where fewer than one would be expected without change, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces, so sampling variation alone cannot explain the pattern. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, OCC002, OCC005, POS002, POS004, REL006, REL009, REL011]

Limitations: positions and step distances reflect event order within traces, not clock time; several lag profiles rest on few traces (for example A07→A01 with 6 and A06→A01 with 15 traces in the later window); and many items describe overlapping aspects of one rewiring rather than independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that a small residual share of cases still follows the old routing below the detection thresholds. [LAG027, LAG021, LAG035, REL006]

Other differences: A07 became rarer (about 31 to 15 in 100 traces) and sits slightly later, while remaining a successor of A06; repetitions of the same activity are now spaced about 4 steps apart instead of about 5. [OCC004, POS003, DIR003, LAG001, LAG009, LAG017, LAG038]
