### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A01 ran in all 200 traces — about 3.5 times per trace, repeating within a trace in roughly 74 in 100 traces, typically about a third of the way through the case; in the later window, A01 does not appear in any of the 200 traces [OCC001, POS001].
- A01 was previously a hub: of its observed outgoing transitions, about 33 in 100 went to A09, about 35 in 100 to A10 and about 28 in 100 to A08, with small residual shares to A12, A11, A06 and A05; in the later window, A01 shows no observed outgoing transitions at all [DIR001].
- Its predecessors re-routed away from A01. A03 previously sent about 88 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions to A01; in the later window, about 42 in 100 go to A10, about 39 to A09 and about 19 to A08, and none to A01. A05 previously sent about 85 in 100 to A01; in the later window, about 41 in 100 go to A09, about 38 to A10 and about 21 to A08 [DIR002, DIR003].
- Relationships with A01 collapsed from "always together" to "A01 absent": the pairs A01–A09, A01–A08 and A01–A10 co-occurred in every earlier trace (mostly interleaved or repeated), whereas in the later window A09, A08 and A10 each occur without A01 in all 200 traces [REL003, REL004, REL005].
- The partial relations changed the same way: A01–A05 went from co-occurring in about 65 in 100 traces (A01 alone in the rest) to A05 alone in about 67 in 100 and neither in about 33 in 100; A01–A03 went from co-occurring in about 47 in 100 (A01 alone otherwise) to A03 alone in about 46 in 100 and neither in about 54 in 100 [REL001, REL002].
- With A01 gone, the remaining steps sit closer together: the share of A03/A05-to-A09/A10 pairs only one event apart rose several-fold (for example A03–A10 from about 3 in 100 to about 23 in 100), while longer distances lost share; pairs from A03/A05 to A08, previously concentrated four steps apart, now spread across one, three and five steps [LAG002, LAG003, LAG005, LAG006, LAG001, LAG004].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the process runs without A01: an activity that previously executed in every trace (about 3.5 times per trace, repeating in roughly 74 in 100 traces) and forwarded work to A09, A10 and A08 is no longer observed, and its former predecessors A03 and A05 now lead directly to A09, A10 and A08 instead of routing through A01 [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005].

The support is direct and consistent rather than incidental: trace participation falls from 200 of 200 traces to 0 of 200, the successor profiles of A03 and A05 shift almost entirely from A01 to A09, A10 and A08, and every recorded A01 relation flips from co-occurrence to A01-absent; the shorter distances between A03/A05 and A09/A10 fit A01 no longer sitting between them [OCC001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG002, LAG003, LAG005, LAG006]. Seventeen control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.26 expected if nothing had changed, so chance alone cannot account for the pattern as a whole [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007].

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A01 was not observed in the later sample, so a structural removal cannot be distinguished from A01 simply not being executed or recorded in these 200 traces; the lag items largely mirror the same rewiring rather than confirming it independently, and they measure event positions, not clock time [OCC001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG005, LAG006].

Other differences: the A08–A09 distance profile flattened, with one-step pairs falling from about 14 to about 7 in 100 and more mass at four steps, and A05 produced more observed outgoing transitions (about 446 versus about 333) despite similar trace participation, consistent with more repetitions per trace [LAG007, DIR003, REL002].
