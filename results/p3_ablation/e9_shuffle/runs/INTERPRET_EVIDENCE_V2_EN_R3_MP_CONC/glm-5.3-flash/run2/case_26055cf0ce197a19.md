### Observed differences

- The successors of A01, A05 and A07 flipped between the windows. In the earlier window, A01's observed outgoing transitions went almost only to A07 (about 94 in 100), A07's went mainly to A05 and A06 (about 43 and 41 in 100), and A05's went back to A01 (about 96 in 100). In the later window, A01 passes mainly to A05 (about 57 in 100), A05 passes to A07 (about 91 in 100), and A07 passes back to A01 (about 94 in 100). [DIR001, DIR004, DIR006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a rerouting of the control flow in which the sequence through A01, A05 and A07 now runs in the opposite order: in the earlier window A01 passed to A07, A07 passed on to A05 or A06, and A05 returned to A01, whereas in the later window A01 passes to A05, A05 passes to A07, and A07 returns to A01. Together with this, A01 has taken over from A07 as the activity that A03 and A04 feed into, and A01 is now executed in about twice as many traces while A07 appears in about half as many. [DIR001, DIR004, DIR006, DIR002, DIR003, OCC001, OCC002]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor profiles changed in exactly the mirrored directions, one-step successions disappeared and appeared where this rerouting predicts, the co-occurrence relations moved in the same direction (A03, A04 and A06 from often without A01 to almost always alongside it; the A01–A07 relation inverted), and the participation and position shifts point the same way. The 35 qualifying items are far above the roughly 0.29 expected by chance among the 58 tested units, so the ensemble cannot be a sampling artefact. [DIR001, DIR004, LAG003, LAG012, REL001, REL005, OCC001, POS003]

Limitations: many items describe the same underlying traces and are not independent confirmations, DIR shares cover only observed outgoing transitions (an edge absent from the later profile is not proven removed), and the window-level aggregates cannot show whether all cases were rerouted or whether a new behavioural variant was added alongside the old one — such a mixture of two case populations is the main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out. [DIR001, LAG003]

Other differences: A05 and A07, which appeared interleaved or repeated whenever they occurred together before (about 27.5 in 100 traces), now appear in a fixed A05-before-A07 order in about 15 in 100 traces with interleaving in about 14, and A07 without A05 is no longer observed; A01's position within its traces also became more variable (spread of about 0.20 to 0.34). [REL008, POS001]
