- In the earlier window, A26, A27 and A28 each ran in essentially every trace and in a fixed sequence (A26 before A27, and A27 before A28, in all but about 1 of 200 traces), while A32, A33 and A38 were not observed at all. [REL001, REL005, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

- The three new activities run together as a group in about 49 in 100 traces, in both orders in similar shares (for example A32 before A38 in about 27 in 100 and A38 before A32 in about 22 in 100), so the group has no fixed internal order. [REL013, REL012, REL014]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The screening produced 27 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected by chance in a stable process, so this pattern is unlikely to be sampling noise. The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, about half of the cases skip A27 and instead run a block of three previously unobserved activities — A32, A33 and A38, with no fixed order among them — between A26 and A28, while the other half keep the original A26 → A27 → A28 sequence. [REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL013, DIR001, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002]

The supporting signs are consistent in direction and size: all three new activities rise from zero to about 49 in 100 participation; each is exclusive with A27; about half of A26's outgoing transitions are redirected from A27 to the new activities; the new activities lead on to A28; the A26-to-A28 gap doubles in about 48 in 100 traces, as expected if three activities are inserted; and the new activities occupy mid-trace positions. Many of these items describe the same underlying traces rather than independent confirmations, but they agree. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL006, REL007, REL008, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, POS001, POS002, POS003]

An important competing explanation remains: zero participation in the earlier window only means the activities were not observed there, so the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely added block from a rare path that already existed but was not seen in 200 traces and is now taken in about half the cases; it also cannot show why a case takes one branch rather than the other. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Other differences: A27 now also shows small outgoing shares to A09, A25 and A34 (about 1–2 in 100 of its outgoing transitions), A32 to A06 and A25 and A38 to A02 (about 1 in 100 each), and A27 repeats within a trace in about 1 in 200 later traces; all are marginal. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, OCC001]
