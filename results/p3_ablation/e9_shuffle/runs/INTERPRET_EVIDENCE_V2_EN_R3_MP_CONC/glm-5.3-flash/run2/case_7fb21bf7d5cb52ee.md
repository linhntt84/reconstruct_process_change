### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening flagged 77 control-flow items where about 1 would qualify by chance without a change, so the body of evidence cannot be explained as random re-splitting noise. The evidence supports the possibility that A10 came to be executed between A14 and A15: in the later window A14 frequently leads to A10 (about 66 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, where it previously passed directly to A15 in 86 in 100), A10 then leads to A15 (about 85 in 100 of its outgoing transitions), the adjacent one-step lags A14→A10 and A10→A15 dominate, and the direct A14→A15 link weakened while A15 shifted to two steps after A14 — consistent with A10's participation tripling and its position becoming consistently early. Limitations: all items come from a single control-flow screening of the same two windows, and the many A10 relation items largely re-express the same participation rise rather than confirming it independently; the earlier-window samples for the new A14→A10 and A10→A15 lags are small (11 and 3 pairs), so A10's earlier placement is imprecisely known.
