### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, none of the 200 traces contains A14, A15 or A20; in the later window A14 appears in about 37 in 100 traces, A20 in about 34 in 100, and A15 in about 29 in 100 (74, 67 and 57 traces respectively). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The three are mutually exclusive alternatives: in the later window no trace contains two of them together — for example, traces contain A14 only (about 37 in 100), A15 only (about 29 in 100) or neither (about 35 in 100), never both. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- In the earlier window, all of A36's observed outgoing transitions led to A21; in the later window they go to A14 (about 37 in 100), A20 (about 34 in 100) and A15 (about 29 in 100), with a direct transition to A21 remaining in only about 1 in 100. [DIR004]
- In the later window, each of A14, A20 and A15 is always followed directly by A21. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The ordering relations change accordingly: before, A36 co-occurred with A21 in every trace and A14, A15, A20 never appeared; after, A36 precedes A14, A20 or A15 in about 37, 34 and 29 in 100 traces respectively, and each of those in turn precedes A21 in the same shares. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- The distance from A36 to A21 moves from one step in essentially all earlier traces to two steps in about 99 in 100 later traces. [LAG001]
- A21 itself still occurs in every trace in both windows; its mean relative position shifts only slightly later, from about 0.37 to about 0.39 of the trace, while the three new activities sit at about 0.35 — just before A21's typical position. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, one of three mutually exclusive activities — A14, A20 or A15 — now executes between A36 and A21. In the earlier window every trace passed from A36 directly to A21 and the three activities never appeared; in the later window about 99 in 100 traces contain exactly one of them in the fixed order A36 → (A14, A20 or A15) → A21, while the direct A36→A21 transition survives in only about 1 in 100 traces. [DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

This goes beyond what an unchanged process typically produces: 21 control-flow items qualified against 0.37 expected by chance, and the change is shown directly by the successor changes at A36, the order and co-occurrence relations, and the lag between A36 and A21 moving from one step to two; the three new activities each rest on 57–74 traces, and A21's near-unchanged position (it still occurs in every trace) fits a step added before it rather than a displacement of A21. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS004, DIR004, LAG001, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]

Limitations: the many items are not independent confirmations, since most describe the same traces from different angles, and the evidence cannot show where the choice among A14, A20 and A15 is made — a competing account is that this three-way choice is fixed earlier in the case rather than decided at A36; the indicators show the resulting order but not the origin of the choice. The rare direct A36→A21 transition in the later window also shows the earlier behaviour persists in a small share of cases. [DIR004, LAG001]

Other differences: none — the remaining positional results (the new activities at about 0.35 of the trace position) are part of the same behaviour. [POS001, POS002, POS003]
