- A08 becomes about twice as prominent: it appears in roughly 13 in 100 traces in the earlier window and about 30 in 100 in the later window (mean occurrences per trace 0.14 → 0.30), and it now occurs earlier in the case (median position about 0.53 → 0.29 of the trace). [OCC001, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the activities A19, A20 and A25 are executed much earlier in each case and are re-routed: they now follow A08 and lead directly to A26, whereas in the earlier window they followed A27 and led mainly to A28 or A32; A08 itself takes part in about twice as many cases (about 30 in 100 versus about 13 in 100). As part of the same difference, A27 → A28 → A32 runs as an immediate sequence in the later window, and A45 reaches A26 through the A08/A19/A20/A25 group instead of leading straight to A26. [OCC001, POS002, POS003, POS004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, LAG001, LAG002, LAG007, LAG010, LAG013]

The placement change is shown directly, not inferred indirectly: positional indicators put A19, A20 and A25 about 0.28 of the trace earlier while A26 and A27 move slightly later [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]; relational indicators show the order with A26 and A27 flipping from "A26/A27 first" to "A19/A20/A25 first" in essentially all traces containing both [REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]; and the successor profiles change consistently across five source activities [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]. With 40 qualifying items against roughly 0.7 expected without change in the control-flow family, the collection of differences is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]

Limitations: the qualifying items largely describe one coherent trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations, and with 200 traces per window it cannot be ruled out that the later window simply draws a different mix of pre-existing variants — for example a previously rare variant that already contained this ordering becoming the common one — rather than reflecting a modified process. Several lag details also rest on very few observations (A08 → A19 had 1 observed pair before; A27 → A25 only 2 after), so exact step distances are uncertain, and POS/LAG reflect event positions, not clock time. [LAG001, LAG008, LAG009, POS002]

Other differences: the remaining qualified items show no meaningful behavioural change — A48 still leads almost only to A49, A50 to A51, and A18 → A45 stays at lag 1, with differences of about 0.02 or less on their own scales. [DIR008, DIR009, LAG006]
