### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the later window executes a restructured flow in which A09 no longer appears and two previously unobserved activities, A01 and A02, have taken over its part: A01 (about 59 in 100 traces) now receives the handover from A16 and feeds A03, the successor A09 previously fed, while A02 (about 87 in 100 traces) typically runs before A12, with A13 now flowing into A02 rather than directly into A12. The support is direct — occurrence and position data show A09 in about half the earlier traces and none later, and A01/A02 in none earlier and frequent later — and consistent, since every traced partner of A09 flips from co-occurrence to appearing alone while A02's partners flip from appearing alone to a fixed order after A02. The screening comparison also shows the ensemble is far beyond chance: 67 items qualified against fewer than one expected if nothing had changed. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the anonymous codes re-label existing work (for example A09's steps re-mapped under new codes) rather than representing genuinely added steps, and a systematic case-mix difference between the windows cannot be excluded.
