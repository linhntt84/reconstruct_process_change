### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every one of the 200 traces contained A01, A02 and A03: A01 always as the final event, A02 just before it (mean relative position about 0.83, always followed by A01), and A03 about 2.1 times per trace near the start (median relative position about 0.19, repeated in about 56 in 100 traces). In the later window, none of the 200 traces contains any of these three activities [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003, DIR001]

- A04 shows the reverse pattern: it was not observed in the earlier window, while in the later window it occurs exactly once in every trace, always at the final position [OCC004, POS004]

- The direct successors of the surviving activities differ between the windows. A05, previously followed by A02 in all 200 traces, is now followed by A04 in all 200 traces. A08, previously followed by A03 in all of its roughly 223 outgoing transitions, is now followed by A06 in about 46 in 100 and by A07 in about 54 in 100 of its outgoing transitions. The earlier split of A03's outgoing transitions (about 53 in 100 to A06, about 47 in 100 to A07) and the earlier A02 → A01 step are no longer observed [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

- A06 and A07 sit earlier in the trace in the later window: A06's median relative position moved from about 0.16 to 0.0 (present in about 56 in 100 earlier traces versus about 51 in 100 later), and A07's mean position moved from about 0.48 to about 0.30 while remaining in every trace. Distances between activity pairs shrank by roughly one step, for example A08 → A07 from lag 2 to lag 1, A06 → A07 from lag 3 to lags 2 and 4, A06 → A05 from lag 4 to lag 3, and the A06 → A06 and A08 → A08 repetitions from lag 3 to lag 2 [POS005, POS006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

- Co-occurrence relations flipped consistently: pairs among A01, A02 and A03 went from always co-occurring (with A03 first) to never co-occurring; pairs of A01/A02 with A05/A07 went from co-occurring to only the surviving activity being present; and pairs involving A04 went from one-sided (A04 never present) to co-occurring in about 51 in 100 later traces, with the partner executed before A04 [REL001, REL006, REL012, REL014, REL019, REL021]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the process runs without A01, A02 and A03: traces end with A05 → A04 instead of A05 → A02 → A01, and A06 and A07 are reached directly from A08 instead of through A03, with A04 newly occupying the final position in every trace. This is shown directly by participation (A01/A02/A03 falling from all 200 traces to none, A04 rising from none to all 200) and by successor profiles being replaced rather than reweighted, with the relation and distance items all shifting in the same direction. The screening comparison also supports this: 43 items qualified, all in the control-flow family, against roughly 0.24 expected by chance, and every item's difference is larger than what about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled traces produce, so sampling noise cannot account for the pattern as a whole [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, DIR003, DIR004, REL019]

Limitations: zero participation only means an activity was not observed, so a residual occurrence of A01, A02 or A03 in a share of later traces too small to appear in 200 traces cannot be excluded, nor can a relabelling in which A04 simply took over the endpoint role. The earlier positions of A06 and A07 in the later window are also consistent with traces simply becoming shorter once A01–A03 dropped out, rather than with an independent rescheduling of those activities [OCC001, POS004, POS005, POS006]
