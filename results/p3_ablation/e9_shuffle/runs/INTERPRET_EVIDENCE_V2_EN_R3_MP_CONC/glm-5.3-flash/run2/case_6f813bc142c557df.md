### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening qualified 58 control-flow items where about 0.76 would be expected if nothing had changed — roughly 76 times the chance expectation — so the volume of flagged differences cannot be explained by random re-splits of a stable process. The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26 and A25 changed from mixed, partly unordered execution with several alternative routings into a fixed sequence A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 that closes into A32: in the earlier window these activities appeared in both orders in similar shares (for example A24 preceded A23 in 88 in 100 traces) and split their outgoing transitions across several successors, while in the later window each pair follows the chain order in about 98–99 in 100 traces and each activity leads almost exclusively to the next chain step. [REL007, REL002, REL009, REL008, REL004, REL005, REL003, REL006, DIR009, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR004, DIR003, DIR008]

The change is internally consistent across indicator types: every lag distribution now concentrates at the chain's step distance, the former direct shortcut A31→A24 (distance 1 in half of the earlier observations) is replaced by the four-step route through the chain, and all five reverse-order pairs survive in only about 1 trace each. This coherence between directional, relational and distance evidence, together with the item count far above the chance expectation, makes sampling variation an unlikely explanation. [LAG029, LAG031, LAG005, LAG020, LAG028, LAG013, LAG016, LAG017, LAG021, LAG023, LAG030]

Competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out: the later window may simply be dominated by one previously rare execution variant of an unchanged process rather than reflect a modified process, since the windows are finite samples; the evidence also shows ordering and routing behaviour only, not why it changed, and position/lag indicators use event positions, so no clock-time statement is implied. [POS004, LAG029]

Other differences: A08 participates much less often (32 to 13 in 100 traces) at a later average position, with more dispersed successors and less ordered co-occurrence with A32; A10 participates more often (3 to about 12 in 100 traces, on few observations). These sit outside the re-sequencing hypothesis. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, OCC002]
