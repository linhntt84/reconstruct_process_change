### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the control-flow structure was reorganised: A01 and A17 are no longer observed, A07 appears as a new early activity running before A03 and A04, the closing of a case moved from A17 to a mandatory A06–A16 pair that every case now executes exactly once at the end in either order, and A02, A14 and A15 changed from mostly single executions to many repetitions per case, with the outgoing edges of A02, A03, A04, A10, A12 and A13 rerouted accordingly. The appearances, disappearances and once-only end positions are shown directly by the occurrence and position indicators, the rerouting by the direction indicators, and the many relation items agree with this picture; the screening also produced 171 qualifying control-flow items where fewer than one (0.94) would be expected without a change, so chance sampling cannot explain the set as a whole. [OCC001, OCC008, OCC004, OCC003, OCC002, DIR007, DIR002]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that an activity was not observed in a window, and many items describe overlapping behaviour of the same traces (for example, A01's disappearance appears in occurrence, position, direction and numerous relation items), so the items are not independent confirmations. An important competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the later window contains only a new variant while the earlier behaviour still occurs outside the sampled cases, or that the transition was gradual rather than a single change point. [OCC001, POS012, REL014]
