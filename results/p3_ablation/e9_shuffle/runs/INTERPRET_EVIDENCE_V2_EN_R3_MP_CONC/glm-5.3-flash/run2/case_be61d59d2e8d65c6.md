### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative block formed by A19, A20 and A25 — earlier executed once per trace as a choice among the three, inserted between A27/A28 and A29 — is no longer executed in the later window, so traces proceed directly from A27 to A28 to A29: participation of all three collapsed from about 29–41 in 100 traces to 1 in 200, A27 and A28 now lead almost exclusively to each other and to A29, and A29 follows A28 immediately where a block activity previously sat in between. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004, REL003, REL012] The change is shown directly and consistently across occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators, and the 30 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.56 expected by chance across the 113 tested units, so sampling variation cannot account for the set as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG004]

Limitations: zero participation only means the activities were not observed, and each still appears in 1 of 200 later traces, so a rare residual execution cannot be excluded. The evidence also cannot determine whether the block was removed outright or replaced by other activities: in the single later traces, A19, A20 and A25 lead to A38, A37 and A31 respectively, but each of these observations rests on one transition. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

Other differences: A10's outgoing transitions spread from a few main targets (A15, A47, A16, A45, A21) to a diffuse set led by A26 (about 17 in 100); the evidence does not tie this to the block's disappearance. [DIR001]
