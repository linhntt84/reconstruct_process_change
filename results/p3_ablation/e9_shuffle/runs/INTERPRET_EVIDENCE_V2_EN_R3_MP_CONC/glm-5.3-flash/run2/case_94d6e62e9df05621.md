### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A07 and A08 changed from being executed together — co-occurring in about half of the traces, in either order, with mutual handovers — to being mutually exclusive alternatives that never appear in the same trace, each now handing over mainly to A01 instead of to the other, while the share of traces containing either activity stayed at about half. The relational change is shown directly with substantial support in both windows (co-occurrence from about 53 to 0 of 100 traces, profiles backed by 106 and 102 traces), the directional items are consistent with it, and each activity's later participation matches its "alone" share; six control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.11 expected by chance, so this set of differences is unlikely for an unchanged process. [REL001, DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, OCC002]

Competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out: a rare or transient variant confined to the later window. Each item's empirical strength (about 0.995) cannot by itself separate a real change from a rare variant, and the later routing shares between A01 and A05 in particular rest on only about 17–18 outgoing transitions each, so the exact new handover proportions are uncertain. [DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, OCC002, REL001]

Other differences: A08's observed positions shifted slightly later in event order (median from about two-thirds to the final position); this largely reflects its lower participation and does not establish that the activity was moved. [POS001]
