### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the process executes A02 no longer and A03 and A14 only exceptionally: A02 fell from about half of all traces to unobserved, A03 and A14 from about 97 to about 37 of 200 traces, and the previously rigid group (A02, A03, A13, A14 always together) dissolved, with A13 continuing at about its earlier frequency (about 46 in 100 traces) but no longer bound to A03/A14. Flow into this part of the process was redirected towards A10, whose participation roughly doubled, with transitions from A15, A09, A06 and A11 now leading to A10 where they previously led to A03. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL014, REL025, DIR011, DIR006, DIR004, DIR008, POS003]

This rests on direct participation counts for A02, A03 and A14, on the complete and uniform collapse of all nine observed A02 relations while every partner continues, and on successor, co-occurrence and position shifts that all point the same way; 60 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected if nothing had changed rules out chance as an explanation of the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, REL004, REL007, DIR011, POS003]

The evidence cannot distinguish a genuine removal of A02 (and the near-removal of A03/A14) from these activities simply never being triggered in the later sample — for example a different variant mix in which this branch is almost never taken — and many items re-describe one underlying difference (each A02 pair reflects the same disappearance), so they are not independent confirmations; the position and gap shifts are largely downstream consequences of the participation changes rather than separate moves. [OCC001, REL001, POS002]

Other differences: A08 regrouped — before it occurred only together with A10 (co-occurrence about 24 in 100, never apart) and led mostly to A10 (about 81 in 100 of its outgoing transitions), whereas in the later window it always co-occurs with the rare A03/A14 group (about 18–19 in 100 traces) and leads mostly to A03 (about 69 in 100). [REL019, REL011, REL020, DIR005]
