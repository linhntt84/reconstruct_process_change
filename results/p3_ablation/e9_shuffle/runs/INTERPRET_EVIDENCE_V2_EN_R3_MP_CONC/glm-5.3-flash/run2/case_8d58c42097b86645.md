### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A01 is inserted between A08 and A10, so that executions which previously ran from A08 directly on to A10 now run A08, then A01, then A10. The signs line up in one direction: A01 appears only in the later window (about 21 in 100 traces), A08's exits are redirected from A10 to A01 while all of A01's observed exits go to A10, A01 follows A08 and precedes A10 in the relational profiles, and the A08-to-A10 distance shifts from odd to even step counts, which is what inserting a single activity between them produces. [POS001, OCC001, DIR001, DIR003, REL001, REL002, LAG001]

Nine control-flow items qualified, against about 0.13 expected if nothing had changed; a count this many times larger cannot be explained by chance re-splits as a whole, and each item is also larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits. These items describe one underlying difference, so they are consistent with each other rather than independent confirmations. [POS001, DIR001, OCC001, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, LAG001, DIR002, REL002]

Limitations: in about 12 in 100 traces A01 and A08 are interleaved or repeated rather than strictly ordered, so the insertion does not produce one fixed sequence everywhere; the later window shows no A08-to-A10 transitions, which means the direct path was not observed, not that it cannot occur; and the evidence cannot tell whether A01 is a genuinely new step or a previously dormant or renamed one. [REL001, DIR003]

Other differences: A12 now leads directly to A13 where it previously led to A03, and A03 is observed less often and only leads to A13 — consistent with A03 being bypassed on that route, but this rests on only about 16 to 20 observations and is not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR004, DIR002]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that A01 is a rare variant that happened to surface in the later sample; the coordinated routing, relation and distance changes and the qualified-item count make this unlikely, but the two 200-trace windows are finite. [OCC001, LAG001]
