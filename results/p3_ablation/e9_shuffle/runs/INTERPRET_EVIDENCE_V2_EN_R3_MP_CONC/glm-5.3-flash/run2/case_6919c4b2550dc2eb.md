### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two activities, A01 and A05, were added to the process and became mandatory, each executed exactly once per case: A01 runs after the A03/A04 block and before A06, and A05 runs after A06 at the end. Earlier, cases moved from A03/A04 to A06, which usually ended the case; in the later window, cases run A03/A04 → A01 → A06 → A05. [OCC001, OCC002, REL004, REL007, DIR001, DIR005, POS003]

This goes well beyond chance: 21 control-flow items qualified where about 0.13 would be expected without a change, and the placement is shown directly by relational, directional and gap evidence — pairs that never co-occurred now always co-occur in a stable order, new edges lead into A01 and from A01 and A06, and the A03/A04→A06 gaps grew exactly where A01 now appears. Many items describe the same underlying addition, so they corroborate one another rather than confirming independently. [OCC001, OCC002, REL004, DIR001, DIR005, LAG003]

Limitations: the earlier window cannot distinguish between "A01/A05 did not exist" and "occurred extremely rarely" (0 of 200 traces), and the unfixed A01–A04 order leaves open whether A01 is a strictly sequential step or part of a block with variable internal order. [OCC001, REL002]

Other differences: the spacing between A03 and A04 also widened (distances of 4–5 steps, together about 28 in 100 later observations, versus none earlier), which the addition of A01 alone does not explain; and in about 13 in 100 later traces A05 is not the end point — it is followed by A04 (A05 shows an outgoing transition in 29 traces, mostly to A04).
