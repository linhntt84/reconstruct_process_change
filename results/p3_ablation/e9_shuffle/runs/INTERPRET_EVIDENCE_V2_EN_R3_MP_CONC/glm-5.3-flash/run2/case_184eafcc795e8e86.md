### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, two activities became mandatory steps: A01 was placed between the A06/A13 block and A04 — earlier A06 and A13 handed over directly to A04, later they hand to A01, which always passes control to A04 — and A02 was appended after A12, which earlier ended every case and now always hands over to A02 as the final step. Both activities run exactly once per case in the later window. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL003, REL005, REL008, POS002, POS005]

The screening comparison supports treating this as a real difference rather than sampling noise: 25 control-flow items qualified where about 0.19 would be expected without a change, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. The items are direct and mutually consistent — participation rising from 0 to 200 of 200 traces, new successor edges (A01→A04, A12→A02), retargeted exits from A06 and A13, co-occurrence rising from never to always with fixed order, and one-step lag shifts matching the same insertions. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG004, REL001]

Limitations: with 200 traces per window, zero earlier observations cannot exclude that A01 or A02 existed before at a very low rate (roughly up to 1–2 cases in 100), so "became mandatory" cannot be sharply distinguished from "newly added", and the data cannot locate the change within the boundary between the windows. In about half of the later traces the A01–A04 and A02–A04 pairs are classified as interleaved-or-repeated rather than fixed order, hinting that A04 recurs in those cases; no occurrence or successor profile for A04 is included to confirm this.
