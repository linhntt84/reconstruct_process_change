### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the optional, trace-initial step before A03 was carried out by a different activity in the later window: A04, which earlier opened about half of all traces and always led into A03, is no longer observed, while A01 now opens about half of the traces and, like A04 before, always leads into A03. The signs are direct and consistent across four indicator types: both activities occupy the first position whenever observed, both have A03 as their only observed successor, participation swapped at nearly the same share (about 49 in 100 to about 50 in 100), and the relation profiles flip from A04-before-A03 to A01-before-A03 with A01 and A04 never co-occurring. [POS001, POS002, OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003]

The volume of qualified evidence (11 items against about 0.14 expected without change) cannot be explained by chance as a whole, so sampling variation does not account for the pattern. One competing explanation remains: the evidence shows A04 disappearing and A01 appearing in the same optional, trace-initial, A03-preceding role, but it cannot distinguish a substitution within one branch from two independent events (a removal and a separate addition), and the predecessors of A01 and A04 are not observed. [POS001, POS002, OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL003]

Other differences: the outgoing transitions of A07 and A13 were redistributed, with previously unobserved edges A07→A03 and A13→A07 appearing while the direct shares A07→A13 and A13→A03 shrank; with 48–59 traces behind each profile this suggests additional routing or repetition around A07 and A13 before reaching A03, but it is not part of the entry-activity hypothesis and outgoing profiles alone cannot show how these edges arose. [DIR003, DIR004]
