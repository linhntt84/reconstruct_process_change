### Observed differences

- **A02 disappeared.** In the earlier window every one of the 200 traces contained A02 (mean about 2.2 occurrences per trace, repeated in about 59 in 100 traces), typically around two-thirds into the case; in the later window A02 appears in none of the 200 traces. [OCC001, POS001]
- **A03 appeared in the same slot.** A03 was absent earlier; in the later window every trace contains it exactly once (mean 1.0, never repeated), at a mean event position of about 0.64 — virtually the same mean position A02 occupied before (also about 0.64). [OCC002, POS002, POS001]
- **Predecessor and successors retargeted.** Earlier A05 always led to A02, and A02 led to A06 (about 55%) or A07 (about 45%); in the later window A05 leads to A03 in about 97 in 100 cases, and A03 leads to A06 (about 50%) or A07 (about 50%) — almost the same split A02 had. [DIR003, DIR001, DIR002]
- **The loop moved.** Earlier A06 always led back to A02, so A06 recurred at a distance of two steps; in the later window A06 leads to A07 (about 51%) or to itself (about 48%), with self-repeats now one step apart (about 26% of repeated-A06 cases, versus two-step recurrence before), and only about 2% of A06 outgoing edges go to A03. [DIR004, LAG003, LAG004]
- **Co-occurrence and order.** Earlier A05 and A02 always co-occurred (A05 strictly first in 41% of traces, interleaved/repeated in 59%); in the later window A05 always co-occurs with A03 and strictly precedes it, and A03 precedes A07 in about 99 in 100 cases. [REL002, REL005, REL007]
- **Secondary shifts.** A06 now occurs in fewer traces (about 49 vs 59 in 100) and sits later (median position 0.67 → 0.80); A05 also sits slightly later (median 0.33 → 0.50); gaps between activity pairs became shorter and more spread out (for example, A06 and A07 are now directly adjacent in about 35% of cases, versus 0% before). [POS004, POS003, LAG004, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 was removed from the flow and A03 now occupies the same mid-process position, together with a change in repetition: earlier every case ran A05 → A02, with A02 repeating (mean about 2.2 per case) through the loop A02 → A06 → A02 before ending in A07; in the later window every case runs A05 → A03 exactly once, A03 branches to A06 or A07 with nearly the same split A02 had, and A06 either repeats itself or proceeds directly to A07. With 21 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.12 expected if nothing had changed, chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002]

The support is direct and consistent: the participation flip is complete (200/200 → 0/200 for A02 and 0/200 → 200/200 for A03), A03's successor profile mirrors A02's old one, A05's outgoing edge retargeted from A02 to A03, A03 sits at A02's old mean position, and the relational and gap evidence agrees (A05 before A03 in all later traces; A03 before A07 in about 99 in 100). These indicators partly reflect the same trace behaviour, so they corroborate rather than independently confirm. Limitations: positions and gaps are event positions, not clock time, so the later positions of A05 and A06 and the shorter gaps are plausibly consequences of the shorter traces once the A02 loop disappeared, not independent relocations. [REL005, REL007, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG002, LAG004]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A03 is the same activity as A02 under a different anonymous code (a rename) rather than a genuinely new activity; the observable behaviour — same slot, same successors, but no longer repeating — looks identical under both readings. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, POS002]

Other differences: the branch share between A06 and A07 after the mid activity shifted slightly (about 55/45 earlier to about 50/50 later), and A06's participation dropped from about 59 to 49 in 100 traces; both shifts are modest and could partly reflect sampling variation between two 200-trace windows. [DIR001, DIR002, POS004, REL003, REL006]
