### Observed differences

- Participation flipped for four activities: in the earlier window A01 and A03 ran in every case (200 of 200) and A05 and A07 in none; in the later window A01 and A03 appear in no case (0 of 200) while A05 and A07 appear in every case (200 of 200). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- The ending changed: in the earlier window A03 was always the final event of a case; in the later window A07 always occupies that final position. [POS002, POS005]
- A06 remained in every case but moved later in the case, with its median position rising from about one third to about two thirds of the trace and its placement becoming more uniform. [POS004]
- The wiring around A06 changed: earlier, A06 was always followed by A01 and A02 led directly to A06 in about 53 in 100 of A02's outgoing transitions; in the later window A06 is always followed by A07, A02 no longer leads directly to A06, and the new activity A05 always leads to A06. [DIR004, DIR002, DIR003, REL004, REL012, REL014]
- A02's branch choice shifted from A06 versus A04 (about 53/47) to A05 versus A04 (about 50/50), while A02 still occurs roughly twice per case in both windows. [DIR002]
- The gap between A02 and A06 grew: earlier, A02 reached A06 within one step in about 42 in 100 of the observed pairs; in the later window the typical gap is two steps (about 51 in 100), consistent with A05 sitting between them. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the closing segment of the process was restructured. In the earlier window every case ended with the sequence A06 → A01 → A03, with A03 always last; in the later window cases end with A05 → A06 → A07, with A07 always last. A01 and A03 are no longer observed at all, A05 and A07 appear in every case, and A06 now runs later in the case and is reached through A05 instead of directly from A02, whose alternative branch to A04 is retained. This is a replacement of one path segment by another — activities removed and added — not a shift in how often existing paths are taken. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS002, POS004, POS005, LAG001]

The screening comparison supports that this is not sampling noise: 28 of 32 tested control-flow units qualified, against roughly 0.16 expected by chance in an unchanged process, and the occurrence, direction, relation, position and lag indicators all describe one coherent restructuring with complete participation flips on 200-case samples in each window. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR004, POS005, LAG001]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that both behavioural variants existed throughout and the two windows captured a wholesale switch between pre-existing variants rather than a modification at the change point; zero participation in a window only means the activity was not observed there. [OCC001, OCC003]

Other differences: none beyond the above; the remaining items fit the same story, for example A02's mixed ordering relative to its partners persists (before against A01/A03, after against A05/A07 in about 52 in 100 cases with the rest interleaved or repeated), and A04's share of A02's outgoing transitions is essentially stable (about 47 in 100 before, about 50 in 100 after). [REL001, REL006, REL007, REL008, DIR002]
