### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was not observed at all in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces). In the later window it occurs in about 47 in 100 traces (94 of 200), averages just under one occurrence per trace overall — roughly two occurrences in each trace that contains it — and repeats within a trace in about 23 in 100 traces. [OCC001]
- In the later window A01 sits around the middle of the trace: median position about 34% of the trace's events, with the middle half between roughly 20% and 60%. [POS001]
- A01's output is aimed at A04: about 76% of A01's observed outgoing transitions go to A04, with about 10% to A13 and 7% to A11. [DIR001]
- Activities that previously fed A04 directly now often feed A01 instead: A11's outgoing transitions went from about 96% to A04 to about 76% to A01 (A11→A04 down to about 22%); A03's went from about 53% to A04 to about 42% to A01 (A03→A04 down to about 10%); A10's went from about 47% to A04 to about 25% to A01 (A10→A04 down to about 13%). A04 itself, which previously led almost exclusively to A13 (~99%), now also leads to A01 in about 10% of its transitions. [DIR005, DIR002, DIR004, DIR003]
- Spacing shifted by about one step where A01 appears: A03→A04 was adjacent in about 55% of observed pairs before and is now typically two steps apart (~63%); A10→A04 moved from adjacent (~47%) to two steps (~51%); A11→A04 from adjacent (~23%) to two steps (~32%). Repeat distances also moved one step later (A03→A11 and A10→A11 from three steps apart in ~100% and ~81% of pairs to four steps in ~91% and ~82%; A11→A11 from three to four steps; A04→A04 from three to four–five steps). Each of these gap comparisons rests on roughly 20–50 observed pairs. [LAG001, LAG005, LAG007, LAG002, LAG006, LAG008, LAG003]
- The activities moved from "never together" to frequent co-occurrence: before, A04, A03, A10 and A11 always appeared without A01; in the later window A01 co-occurs with A04 in 47 in 100 traces (every A01 trace also contains A04), preceding A04 in about 20 and interleaving or repeating with it in about 23; it co-occurs with A03 in 27 in 100 (usually A03 first, ~15), with A10 in 20 in 100, and with A11 in 23 in 100 with no fixed order (all interleaved or repeated). [REL002, REL001, REL003, REL004]
- A04 now appears in somewhat fewer traces (about 55 in 100 before vs about 47 in 100) and slightly later within traces (mean position about 0.39 → 0.44 of the trace's events). [POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new activity, A01, was added to the process and inserted before A04 on several paths. In the earlier window A11, A03 and A10 led directly to A04 and A01 never occurred; in the later window A01 runs in about 47 in 100 traces around the middle of the trace, is reached mainly from A11 and substantially from A03 and A10, and passes flow on to A04 in about three quarters of its outgoing transitions, while the direct A11/A03/A10 → A04 links shrank to small shares and the distance from these activities to A04 grew by about one step — the pattern expected if A01 now sits between them. A01 always occurs together with A04 and usually before it. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR005, DIR002, DIR004, DIR003, LAG001, LAG005, LAG007, REL002]

This is unlikely to be a sampling artefact: twenty items qualified where fewer than one (about 0.23) would be expected by chance in the control-flow family, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, OCC001, POS001, POS002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]

Limitations: the items describe overlapping traces and are not independent confirmations, and the gap comparisons rest on roughly 20–50 observed pairs each. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 does not occupy a single fixed slot: it also follows A04 in about 10% of A04's transitions and interleaves or repeats with A04 and A11, so one fixed insertion point, several alternative ones, or a repeated step remain indistinguishable. [DIR003, REL002, REL004]

Other differences: A04's participation decreased modestly (about 55 in 100 traces to about 47 in 100) and its position moved slightly later, possibly a side effect of the insertion upstream of it [POS002]; among A10's remaining transitions, share shifted away from A04 toward A02 and A07 [DIR004].
