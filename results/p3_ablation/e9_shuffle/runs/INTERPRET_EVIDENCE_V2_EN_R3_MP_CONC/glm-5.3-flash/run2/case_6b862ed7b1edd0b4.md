### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment A19 → A25 → A20 between A41 and A26, which in the earlier window was executed in about half of the traces — with A41 frequently passing directly to A26 and bypassing the segment — is in the later window executed once, in the fixed order A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 → A26, in virtually every trace. Each link is documented directly: the successor redistribution at A41, the participation rise of A19, A25 and A20, and agreeing order and distance evidence; overall, 62 items qualified against fewer than 1 expected without a change, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, so sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. The evidence cannot show whether the direct A41 → A26 transition was removed or is now merely chosen in about 1 in 200 cases, nor separate a modified routing rule from a shift in the mix of coexisting trace variants.
