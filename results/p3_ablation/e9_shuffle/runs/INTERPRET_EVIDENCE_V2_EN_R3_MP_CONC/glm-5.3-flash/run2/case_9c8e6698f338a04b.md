### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A17 is no longer executed and A01 has been added, with the flow that previously ran through A17 rerouted. In the earlier window A17 ran in about half the cases — usually repeated and followed by A15/A14 — while A01 never appeared; in the later window A17 is entirely absent and A01 runs once in about 39 in 100 cases around mid-trace, leading mainly to A09, A07, A12 and A04. Supporting signs: A12 now routes to A15/A14 (and occasionally A01) instead of A17, all former co-occurrences with A17 dropped to zero while A01 gained co-occurrences with A02, A12, A15 and A14, and A14/A15 now follow their predecessors at shorter gaps. Screening produced 27 qualifying items against roughly 0.3 expected if nothing had changed, far more than chance would produce; note, however, that many items describe this same underlying A17/A01 difference, so they are consistent rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC004, POS001, POS003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL006, REL007, LAG005, LAG006]

An important competing explanation remains: the evidence cannot rule out that this is a shift in the mix of process variants — cases now following a variant that contains A01 instead of a variant containing A17 — rather than a modification of one shared process, and it cannot show why the change happened. "A17 absent" strictly means it was not observed in the later sample of 200 traces, and the gap comparisons use event positions, not clock time. [OCC004, DIR003, LAG006]

Other differences: A06 and A08 each appear in fewer traces in the later window (about 52→31 and 47→30 in 100 traces respectively); the evidence does not relationally tie these participation drops to the A17/A01 change. [OCC002, OCC003]
