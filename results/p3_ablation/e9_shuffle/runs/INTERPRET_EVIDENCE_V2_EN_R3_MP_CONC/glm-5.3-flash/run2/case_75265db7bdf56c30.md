### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new closing activity, A01, was added directly after A03: in the earlier window traces effectively ended with A03 (no observed outgoing transitions, constant final position), whereas in the later window every trace executes A01 exactly once as the step following A03, making A01 a mandatory end-of-process step while A03 continues to run in every trace. [DIR001, REL001, OCC001, POS001, POS002]

The placement rests on direct relational and directional evidence — the A03→A01 succession accounts for all of A03's observed outgoing transitions, and the pair is ordered A03-before-A01 in about 100 in 100 later traces — and the five qualifying control-flow items are many times the roughly 0.09 expected by chance among the 19 tested units, so chance sampling cannot account for the set as a whole. [DIR001, REL001, OCC001, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: all five items describe one underlying trace-level difference, so they are not independent confirmations, and zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that A01 was not observed there; the evidence cannot fully exclude that A01 existed before at a rate so low that a 200-trace sample missed it. [OCC001, POS001]

Other differences: none — the small backward shift of A03's position is a consequence of the same addition rather than a separate change. [POS002]
