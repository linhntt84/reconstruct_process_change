### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the repeated execution of a block involving A25, A26 and A31 stopped: earlier these three activities cycled within the case (about 3.4 occurrences each, with A31 frequently leading back to A25 and the pair interleaved or repeated in about 75 in 100 traces), whereas in the later window each runs once per case in a fixed sequence — A25, then A31 two positions later in about 92 in 100 cases — after which flow continues to A20 (about 96 in 100 of A31's outgoing transitions) instead of returning to A25 (about 4 in 100). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001, LAG001, LAG002]

The screening found 9 qualifying control-flow items where about 0.3 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the set is unlikely under an unchanged process, and the occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators all describe the same behaviour consistently. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: A26's involvement rests only on its occurrence profile matching A25's and A31's exactly, since no relational or directional evidence covers A26; the evidence cannot distinguish a complete removal of the repetition from the same cycle now always executing exactly once, as about 1 in 200 later traces still shows repeats; and the nine items partly reflect one underlying trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the shift of A31's exits towards A20 is partly an independent routing change, although it is consistent with the repetition simply ending.
