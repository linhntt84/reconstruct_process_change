### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the one-of-three choice block formed by A19, A20 and A25 was relocated from early to late in the case sequence: in the earlier window the case ran one of the three between A45 and A26, about a third of the way through, while in the later window the chosen activity runs after A27, inserted either immediately before or immediately after A28 and before A32, about 59 in 100 of the way through. [POS001, POS002, POS003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG004, LAG007]

This hypothesis is supported directly and consistently: the position shift is observed for all three activities and is large (about 0.27–0.29 of the case length); the rewiring shows on both sides of the block (A45 now feeds A26 directly, the block exits to A28/A32 instead of A26, and A27/A28 route into the block in about half of their outgoing transitions); the order relations with A26 and A27 reversed for all three activities; and participation of every involved activity stayed substantial in both windows, ruling out the block having been added or removed rather than relocated. With 27 qualifying items against roughly 1 expected by chance among the 126 tested units, and every item's difference exceeding about 199 of 200 random re-splits, the set of differences as a whole cannot be explained by sampling. [POS001, POS002, POS003, DIR006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out: the items cover only the listed pairs and successors, so it remains unclear whether the activities occupying the stretch between A26 and A27 (about a quarter of the case) also changed placement, or whether the block swapped with part of that segment rather than simply being reinserted around A28; the exact insertion rule (before vs after A28, in roughly equal shares) also cannot be pinned down. [REL003, REL008, LAG004, LAG007]

Other differences: A26 and A27 each shifted slightly earlier in the sequence (mean event position about 0.35 to 0.32 and 0.58 to 0.55), and the choice among the three alternatives shifted modestly (A19 from about 38 to 31 in 100 traces, A25 from about 32 to 38 in 100, A20 roughly unchanged) — a shift small enough that sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows cannot be excluded.
