### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01, unobserved in the earlier window, now runs in about 30 in 100 traces as an intermediate step between A12 and its successors A04 and A13: earlier, execution passed from A12 directly on to A04 or A13 in roughly equal shares, whereas in the later window A12 leads to A01, which then leads on to A04 or A13 in about the same equal shares, around the middle of the trace [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002]. The placement is shown directly by the successor rerouting and corroborated by the one-step growth of the A12→A04 and A12→A13 gaps and the similar one-step lengthening of the repetition gaps of A12, A04 and A13, as expected if a repeating cycle gained one activity [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005]. The relational items agree: A12 now always co-occurs with A01, and A01 co-occurs with A04 and A13 in about 30 in 100 traces where none was seen earlier, while A04's and A13's own participation stayed near 50 in 100 in both windows [REL001, REL002, REL003]. Twelve qualifying items against roughly 0.17 expected without change is far beyond what a stable process typically produces, so the set of differences as a whole is not a plausible sampling artefact [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005].

Limitations: zero participation earlier only shows that A01 was not observed before, not that it was absent from the process, and the successor and gap indicators cover only immediate transitions and distances up to five steps, so A01's exact placement in traces where activities repeat is partly inferred; A01 is also observed only in traces that contain A12, so its behaviour on other paths remains unknown [OCC001, REL002].
