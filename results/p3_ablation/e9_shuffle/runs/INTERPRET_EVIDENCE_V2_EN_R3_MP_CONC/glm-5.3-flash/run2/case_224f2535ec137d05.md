### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a step A01 is executed directly after A16 at the end of every case, whereas earlier cases ended with A16 and A01 was not observed. Earlier, A16 ran in all 200 traces at a constant final position with no observed successors; in the later window, every case runs A16 and then A01 exactly once, A16's observed transitions all lead to A01, and A01 is the final step of the trace [REL001, DIR001, OCC001, POS001, POS002].

The support is direct and consistent: the new succession is shown directly by relational evidence (co-occurrence and A16-before-A01 both moving from 0 to 100 in 100 cases) and by successor evidence (A16 leading to A01 in all observed transitions), with the occurrence and position items agreeing; each difference is larger than in about 99 of 100 random re-splits, and five items qualified against roughly 0.12 expected if nothing had changed, so the set of items is unlikely for an unchanged process [REL001, DIR001, OCC001, POS001, POS002].

Limitations: all five items describe one underlying difference, so they are not independent confirmations of each other [OCC001, POS001, POS002, REL001, DIR001]. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that A01 was a pre-existing but rarely executed step that happened not to appear in the earlier 200 traces — zero participation only means it was not observed there [OCC001]. Likewise, the empty successor profile for A16 earlier does not prove A16 had no outgoing edges, although its constant final position is consistent with it ending the case [DIR001, POS002].
