### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering constraints between A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25 and A32 tightened: in the earlier window all eight activities ran in every trace but in several coexisting orders with successors spread across several activities, while in the later window they execute in the single fixed sequence A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32. [REL016, REL017, REL019, DIR013, LAG032, LAG029]

The support is direct and consistent rather than voluminous: order shares flip from mixed or reversed to one direction in about 96–99 in 100 traces, successor profiles concentrate on single successors, and all pairwise step distances fit one chain; each key item covers about 200 traces per window, and 82 control-flow items qualified against about 1 expected for an unchanged process, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [REL019, DIR013, LAG014, LAG036]

Limitations: the windows show aggregate behaviour, so a shift in the mix of pre-existing execution variants (cases that already followed this sequence becoming dominant) cannot be separated from a genuine tightening of the ordering constraints; step distances are event positions, not clock time. [REL016, LAG010]

Other differences:
- A08 was skipped in most later traces — participation fell from 72 to 29 in 100 traces, it now sits later in the trace, no longer leads almost exclusively to A16, and its former partners usually occur without it; this participation change is not part of the ordering hypothesis. [OCC001, POS001, DIR003, LAG001, REL011]
- A10, A02 and A04 occur more frequently with different successors, but the underlying profiles are small (about 15–49 traces). [OCC002, DIR004]
- A15 → A16 became near-exclusive while A47 → A48 became slightly less so, with small new transitions from A14, A45 and A47 to A02, A04, A06, A08 and A10. [DIR006, DIR015, DIR005, LAG038]
