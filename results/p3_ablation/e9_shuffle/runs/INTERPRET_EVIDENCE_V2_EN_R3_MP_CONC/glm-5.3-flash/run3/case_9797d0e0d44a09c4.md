### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment A19 → A25 → A20, executed between A41 and A26, changed from optional to mandatory: in the earlier window it ran in about 47 in 100 cases and was skipped in the rest, with A41 passing directly to A26, while in the later window it runs in virtually every case. Participation of A19, A20 and A25 rose to near-certainty, the internal and neighbouring order stayed the same, A41's outgoing transitions shifted from about half going directly to A26 to over nine in ten going to A19, and the typical A41–A26 distance moved from one step to four steps — occurrence, relation, direction and lag evidence all describe the same coherent difference. The screening count also exceeds what chance would produce: 23 control-flow items qualified where about 0.64 were expected without change across 129 tested units. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL012, REL013, DIR002, LAG001, screening]

Limitations: the evidence shows behaviour, not its cause — it cannot separate a genuine change in the process from a different mix of cases entering the later window, and it cannot distinguish complete removal of the bypass from the bypass merely becoming very rare (about 1 in 200 later traces still show the one-step A41 → A26 gap). [LAG001, DIR002]

Other differences: A45 now co-occurs with the group whenever it occurs, whereas before it ran without the group in about a third of traces; A42 is interleaved or repeated with the group about three times as often; the small event-position shifts of A26 and A41 and A20's essentially unchanged successor profile are consistent with the main change rather than separate differences. [REL005, REL006, REL010, REL014, REL015, POS001, POS002, DIR001]
