### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment formed by A35, A36 and A41 — always executed together, in varying order — now runs earlier in the case: in the earlier window the flow was A25 → A27 → … → A26 → {A35, A36, A41} → A30, while in the later window it is A25 → {A35, A36, A41} → A27 → … → A26 → A30, with A26 now followed immediately by A30. Equivalently, A27 and A26 now execute after the block rather than before it; every involved activity still occurs in all traces, so this is a reordering of the flow, not activities becoming optional or paths being replaced. The support is direct and consistent: the six order relations flip almost completely between windows (for example, A27 before A36 in about 99 in 100 earlier traces versus about 98 in 100 for the reverse later), each backed by all 200 traces per window; the successor changes match the same rewiring; the LAG distances and position shifts agree in direction and size; and 26 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.63 expected by chance, with every item at the top of its reference comparisons (about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits show smaller differences), so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole.
