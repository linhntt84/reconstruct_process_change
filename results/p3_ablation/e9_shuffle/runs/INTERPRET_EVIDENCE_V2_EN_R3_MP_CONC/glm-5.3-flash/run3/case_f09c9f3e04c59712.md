### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a specific reordering: the segment containing A15, A17 and A18 and the segment containing the single choice from A14, A16 and A19 exchanged places around an unchanged middle. In the earlier window a trace ran A35, then A15, A17 and A18 in varying order, then A20, then A24, then one of A14, A16 and A19, then A25; in the later window it ran A35, then one of A14, A16 and A19, then A20, then A24, then A15, A17 and A18 in varying order, then A25. A20 and A24 kept their middle position throughout. [REL016, REL008, DIR007, DIR008, DIR001, LAG001, LAG002, POS007, POS008]

The support is direct and consistent: all 21 cross-segment relations flipped completely rather than shifting in frequency, the positional shifts match the relation changes, the successor changes occur precisely at the segment boundaries, and the distance changes (A35 to A20 from 4 to 2 steps, A24 to A25 from 2 to 4) are what the swap predicts. The volume of flagged evidence, 40 items against roughly 0.46 expected in an unchanged process, cannot be explained by sampling as a whole. [REL001, REL008, REL016, POS001, POS002, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG002, screening]

Limitations: only the flagged activities are visible, so any additional reordering among unflagged activities between A20 and A24 cannot be checked; the evidence shows the net ordering, not whether the segments moved as units or equivalent re-routing produced the same behaviour. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that the two windows sampled two disjoint mixes of process variants with opposite orderings rather than a modified single process, although the coordinated pattern across relations, positions, successors and distances fits one reordering. [REL016, DIR008, POS001]

Other differences: the choice among the three alternatives shifted toward A14 (about 31 to about 44 in 100 traces, 62 to 87 of 200), with A16 (75 to 57) and A19 (63 to 56) slightly less frequent; all three remain substantial alternatives in both windows, so this is a change in how often existing paths are taken, not a path replacement, and taken alone its size could partly reflect sampling variation. [OCC001, POS001, DIR008, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]
