### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 and A13 exchanged their roles in the process. In the earlier window, A01 was the frequent, early activity fed by A02 and A04 and leading on to A07, A08 and A15, while A13 was a rare, late activity paired with A06; in the later window, A13 occupies that frequent early role with the same predecessors and successors, and A01 occupies the rare late role around A06. The screening produced 38 qualifying items where an unchanged process would be expected to produce about 0.46, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits of unchanged data produce, and successor changes, position shifts, pairwise distances and participation all point to the same exchange.
