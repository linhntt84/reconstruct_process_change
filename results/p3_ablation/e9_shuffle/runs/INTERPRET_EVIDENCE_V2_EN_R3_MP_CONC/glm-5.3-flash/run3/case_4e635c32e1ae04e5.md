### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A02 was not observed at all (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it occurs in every trace, exactly once per case, always at the final position of the trace [OCC001, POS001].
- A05 is present in all traces in both windows, but its position changed: earlier it sat at the very end of every trace (relative position 1.0 in 200 of 200 cases), while in the later window it sits at about 85% of the trace length (median about 0.86) — it is no longer the last step [POS002].
- In the later window, A05 is directly followed by A02 in essentially every case: all observed outgoing transitions of A05 lead to A02. In the earlier window, no outgoing transition from A05 was observed, consistent with A05 ending the trace there [DIR001].
- The relationship between the two activities flipped completely: earlier, A05 always occurred without A02 (100% of traces); in the later window, the two always co-occur, with A05 before A02 in 100% of traces [REL001].
- All five qualifying items are control-flow items with empirical strength of about 0.995, and the screening flagged 5 items where roughly 0.06 would be expected if nothing had changed [OCC001, POS001, POS002, REL001, DIR001].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A02 was added as a new, mandatory final step executed directly after A05: in the earlier window A05 ended every trace and A02 never appeared, while in the later window every trace contains A02 exactly once, immediately after A05, whose position correspondingly moved off the end to about 85% of the trace. This is an addition of an activity to every case, not a redistribution of frequency between existing paths, since A02 was not observed at all in the earlier window [OCC001, POS001, POS002, REL001, DIR001].

The hypothesis rests on direct and mutually consistent signs: participation of A02 jumping from 0% to 100% of traces, an order relation showing A05 before A02 in every later trace with a direct A05→A02 succession, and positional evidence showing A05 vacating the final position exactly as A02 takes it. The number of qualifying items (5) is far above the chance expectation (about 0.06). A limitation is that the five items largely describe one underlying trace-level difference, so they are not independent confirmations of each other [OCC001, POS001, POS002, REL001, DIR001].

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that A02 was a rare variant in the earlier window that happened not to appear in the 200 earlier traces; zero participation strictly means only "not observed". Likewise, the empty outgoing profile of A05 earlier does not prove A05 had no successor before, only that none was observed [OCC001, DIR001].
