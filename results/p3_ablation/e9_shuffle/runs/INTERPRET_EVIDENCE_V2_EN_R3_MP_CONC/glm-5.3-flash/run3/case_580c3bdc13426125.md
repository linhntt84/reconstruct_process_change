### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural replacement in which A03 took over the slot previously held by A02. In the earlier window the flow ran A05 → A02, with A02 repeating about 2.2 times per trace through a loop in which A06 returned to A02 before A02 branched to A06 or A07; in the later window A02 is unobserved, A05 leads to A03, A03 branches about 50/50 to A06 or A07 from the same relative position A02 occupied, and the repetition is now carried by A06 repeating itself rather than by A02 alternating with A06. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, LAG003]

The hypothesis is shown directly by the successor changes at A05, A03 and A06 and by the all-or-nothing participation flip of A02 and A03, and it is consistent across the relation, position and gap indicators; with 21 qualifying items against roughly 0.12 expected if nothing had changed, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole, although many items restate the same underlying difference and are not independent confirmations. [screening, REL001, REL005, REL007, LAG003]

The evidence cannot rule out that A02 still occurs outside this sample, since zero participation only means it was not observed in the later window; nor can it distinguish a genuine removal and insertion from A02 being relabelled as A03, which would look identical at this level. [OCC001, OCC002]

Other differences: in the later window A06 appears in fewer traces (about 49 vs 59 in 100) and later in the trace (mean position about 0.73 vs 0.61), A05 sits slightly later (median about one half vs one third of the trace), and distances between consecutive activities are more varied than before; these fit the rerouting but the evidence does not establish why fewer traces contain A06.
