### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window about half of the cases run, between A26 and A28, a group of three activities — A32, A33 and A38, executed together in varying order — in place of A27: A26 now leads either to A27 (about half of its outgoing transitions, as before) or to one of the three new activities, which then lead via one another to A28, while A27, previously executed in every case, runs in only about 52 in 100 traces and almost never alongside the new group (about 1 in 100). This rests on direct evidence: the three activities appear from zero participation to about 50 in 100 traces with mid-trace positions, relational and directional items place them after A26 and before A28 (including the A26-to-A28 distance growing from two to four steps in about 46 in 100 traces), and A27's drop coincides with A26's routing split. With 55 qualifying items against roughly 0.7 expected if nothing had changed, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. The main limitation is that the evidence cannot show whether this group was newly added to the process or is a previously existing path that was rarely taken — although zero occurrences in 200 earlier traces makes a previously stable half-share path implausible — nor can it rule out that the two windows draw on different case mixes.
