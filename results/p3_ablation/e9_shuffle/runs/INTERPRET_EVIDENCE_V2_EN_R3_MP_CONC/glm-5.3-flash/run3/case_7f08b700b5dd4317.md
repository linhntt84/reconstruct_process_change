### Observed differences

- Six activities — A33, A35 and A36 (group 1) and A37, A38 and A39 (group 2) — appeared in every trace in the earlier window (participation 100 in 100, about one occurrence per trace). In the later window each appears in only about 47 in 100 traces (group 1) and about 56 in 100 traces (group 2), still with about one occurrence when present [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006].
- Earlier, every cross-group pair always occurred in the same trace, with group 1 completed before group 2 (for example, A33 before A37 in 99 in 100 traces). In the later window cross-group pairs co-occur in only about 3 in 100 traces; instead the group-1 activity occurs without the group-2 activity in about 44 in 100 traces, and the group-2 activity without the group-1 activity in about 53 in 100 [REL015, REL017, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024].
- Within each group the activities still go together: A33/A35/A36 co-occur in about 47 in 100 later traces (matching their participation, with essentially no solo occurrences) and A37/A38/A39 in about 55 in 100; in both windows each group shows no fixed internal order, with roughly even shares of both orders [REL013, REL014, REL018, REL025, REL026, REL027].
- A30 and A31 still occur in every trace in both windows [POS001, POS002]. Earlier, about 97 in 100 of A30's outgoing transitions went to group-1 activities; in the later window about 45 in 100 go to group-1 activities and about 52 in 100 lead directly into group-2 activities (A37, A38, A39), which were not observed as direct successors earlier [DIR001]. Group-1 activities now lead directly to A31 in about 26–34 in 100 of their outgoing transitions; no such direct transitions were observed in the earlier window [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004].
- Distances in event positions (not clock time) shrank accordingly: A37, A38 and A39 now follow A30 within about 1–3 positions (earlier 4–5), and A31 now follows A33/A35/A36 within about 1–3 positions (earlier 4–5); each of these rests on roughly 90–135 later-window observations [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG009].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the fixed sequence of two three-activity groups between A30 and A31 appears as a per-case choice: a case runs either group 1 (A33, A35, A36, in no fixed order) or group 2 (A37, A38, A39, in no fixed order) — about 47 in 100 later cases run group 1 only, about 56 in 100 run group 2 only, and about 3 in 100 still run both — whereas earlier every case ran group 1 and then group 2 in full [OCC001, OCC004, REL015, REL025, DIR001, LAG001, LAG004].

The screening found 54 qualifying control-flow items where fewer than 1 would be expected by chance among 160 tested units, so the set of differences is far too large for random window-to-window variation; the pattern is consistent across participation, pairwise relations, direct transitions and positional distances, and every item's difference exceeds those in essentially all random re-splits (empirical strength about 0.995 in 200 reference comparisons). The many agreeing REL items describe one underlying restructuring rather than independent confirmations [OCC001, REL015, DIR001, LAG001].

The evidence cannot show where the choice between the two groups is made, nor distinguish a single either/or branching from the two groups each independently becoming optional; the near-mutual exclusivity (both groups in only about 3 in 100 later cases) makes independent optionalisation unlikely but does not rule it out, and a rare residual combined path remains possible [REL015, REL025].

Other differences: relative event positions shifted slightly — A30 now occurs somewhat later within traces (mean 0.62 to 0.67 of the trace) and A31 somewhat earlier (0.82 to 0.80), and the group activities occur slightly later within the shorter traces in which they appear (for example A33, mean 0.68 to 0.74); these are position-based and consistent with traces shortening when a group is skipped, not clock-time evidence [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]. A few rare outgoing transitions from A30 to activities outside the two groups (A04, A08, A10, A20, A45, A46, about 1 in 200 outgoing transitions each) appear only in the later window [DIR001]. Some cross-group distance items rest on only about 4–5 later-window observations and are too weak to interpret on their own; the conclusion above rests on the high-support items [LAG005, LAG007, LAG010, LAG011].
