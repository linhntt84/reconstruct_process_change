### Observed differences

- **Repetition of A25, A26 and A31.** In the earlier window, each of these three activities occurred exactly once in every trace (mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, no repeats). In the later window, each occurs about 3.5 times per trace, still in every trace, with repeats present in about 68 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- **Successors of A31.** In the earlier window, every observed outgoing transition of A31 led to A20. In the later window, about 71 in 100 of A31's outgoing transitions lead to A25 and about 29 in 100 to A20 (697 transitions observed). [DIR001]
- **Order of A25 and A31.** In the earlier window, the two always co-occurred and A25 came before A31 in every trace. In the later window they still always co-occur, but the fixed A25-before-A31 order holds in only about 32 in 100 traces, while about 68 in 100 show interleaved or repeated execution. [REL001]
- **Spacing of A25 and A31.** In the earlier window the pair was consistently two event positions apart; in the later window that two-step spacing remains for only about 12 in 100 instances (about 5 in 100 sit roughly five positions apart), so the tight spacing largely dissolved. [LAG001]
- **Positional variability.** The average relative positions of A25 (0.41 → 0.44) and A31 (0.48 → 0.48) barely moved, but their spread roughly doubled (standard deviation about 0.06 → 0.12 for both). [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a repetition was introduced around the A25–A31 segment: A31, which previously always proceeded to A20, now typically returns to A25, so that A25, A26 and A31 — previously executed exactly once per trace — run about 3.5 times per trace, and the previously strict A25-before-A31 sequence appears as interleaved or repeated execution in about 68 in 100 traces. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001]

Supporting signs: the new dominant A31→A25 transition is observed directly; the order and spacing items agree with it (fixed order and two-step spacing replaced mostly by interleaving/repeats); participation of all three activities stayed at 100% while per-trace counts rose, fitting "became repeated" rather than "became optional"; the increased positional spread with stable mean positions is consistent with repeats rather than a move; every item's difference exceeds about 99.5% of random re-splits; and eight control-flow items qualified where about 0.3 would be expected by chance, far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [DIR001, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, REL001]

Limitations and competing explanation: A26's place in the repeated segment rests only on its occurrence counts, since no relational item covers it; and the relation evidence cannot separate a strict cyclic loop-back from looser interleaved repetition, so an alternative reading is that the three activities interleave repeatedly without a fixed cycle — though the dominant A31→A25 transition favours a loop-back. [DIR001, OCC002, REL001]
