### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural replacement in the segment between A44 and A29/A45: the earlier per-case choice of exactly one of the mutually exclusive activities A19, A20 or A21, each followed almost directly by A29, is replaced in the later window by the three activities A26, A27 and A28, which now execute together exactly once per case in no fixed order among themselves, in the same position region, with A44 still preceding the block and A29 and A45 still following it. Because A26 and A27 were absent from the earlier sample and A19/A20/A21 are essentially absent from the later one, this is a change in which activities participate (three no longer observed, three newly observed), not a shift of shares between existing paths. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL031, REL034, REL038, REL043, DIR009, LAG005]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: occurrence and position items show the near-disappearance and appearance of the two trios; relational and direction items show the stable anchors A44, A45 and A29 rewired from the old trio to the new one; and the A44→A29 distance grew from about 2 to about 4 steps, matching three activities replacing one. Control-flow screening flagged 74 items where fewer than 1 would be expected without any change, so the volume of flagged behaviour is not what a stable process typically produces. [OCC005, REL034, DIR009, LAG005, REL033]

Limitations: the near-equal both-order shares within the new trio establish the absence of a fixed order but cannot distinguish a concurrent block from a flexibly ordered sequence; zero participation means A19/A20/A21 were not observed in the later sample rather than proving they can no longer occur; and the evidence cannot establish whether the declines of A10 and A46 belong to the same restructuring or are independent changes. [REL031, REL032, REL037, OCC001, REL036]

Other differences: A10's participation halved (about 26 to about 11 of 100 traces) with rewired outgoing transitions, though the later-window lag evidence for A10 rests on only about 7 traces [OCC001, DIR001, LAG001, LAG004]; A46 now occurs in about 57 of 100 traces instead of about 69 and typically follows the new trio [REL036, REL041, REL045]; A29's mean position shifted slightly later [POS008].
