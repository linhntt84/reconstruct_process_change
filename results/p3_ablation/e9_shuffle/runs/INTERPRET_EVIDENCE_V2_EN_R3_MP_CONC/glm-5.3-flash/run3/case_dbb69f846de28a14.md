### Observed differences

- Participation halved for four activities: in the earlier window A27, A36, A37 and A42 each occurred exactly once in every trace, while in the later window A27 occurs in about 54 in 100 traces and A36, A37 and A42 in about 47–48 in 100, still once per trace where they appear. - A27 and the group A36/A37/A42 became mutually exclusive: before, A27 co-occurred with each of them in every trace; in the later window they share a trace in at most about 1 in 100 cases, with A27 alone in about 53 in 100 traces and the group alone in about 46–47 in 100. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a rerouting of the segment between A25 and A31. In the earlier window every trace executed A25, then the group A36/A37/A42 in varying order, then A28, with A26, A27 and A31 following in direct succession. In the later window, A25 feeds A28 directly, after which the process takes one of two mutually exclusive branches: A27 alone in about 53 in 100 traces, or the group A36/A37/A42 executed together in about 47 in 100, both leading on to A31; A27 and the group now hardly ever share a trace. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL020, REL021, REL022, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG005]

This is supported directly and consistently: the exclusivity and the order reversals are shown by relational evidence rather than marginal frequencies alone, the successor changes (A25→A28, group→A31) appear in the direction profiles, and the lag and position shifts match the same rerouting. With 48 qualifying items against roughly 0.73 expected if nothing had changed, chance cannot account for the evidence as a whole, and each item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits; however, most items re-express this single rerouting and are not independent confirmations. [REL011, REL006, REL014, DIR001, DIR003, LAG001, LAG005, POS006]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a single process with a new exclusive choice from a later window that mixes two case types, one per branch; and the relative order of A28 and A26 rests on event positions rather than a directly observed relation between them. [POS002, POS004, REL009]

Other differences: only minor drifts remain — A25's mean position moved slightly later (about 0.34 to 0.37), A31's slightly earlier, and the group's position spread widened; these are small and consistent with side effects of the rerouting above. [POS001, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]
