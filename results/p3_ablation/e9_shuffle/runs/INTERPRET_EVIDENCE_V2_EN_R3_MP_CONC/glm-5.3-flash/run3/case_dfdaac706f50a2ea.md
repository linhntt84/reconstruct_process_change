- In the earlier window, A25, A26, A27, A28, A31, A36, A37 and A42 each appeared in every trace (100 in 100, once per trace). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of activities A36, A37 and A42, executed in mixed order, changed from a mandatory early segment into an optional later alternative: in the earlier window every case ran the trio directly after A25 and before A28, whereas in the later window about 47 in 100 cases run the trio together after A26 and before A31 while the rest skip it, and A27 — previously executed in every case alongside the trio — now appears in about 53 in 100 cases almost never together with the trio, making the trio and A27 mutually exclusive branches. The screening produced 50 qualifying items against roughly 0.72 expected for an unchanged process, and the difference is shown directly and consistently across participation (OCC), co-occurrence and order (REL), successors (DIR), positions (POS) and ordered distances (LAG). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, POS006, LAG001]

Limitations: the many items are not independent confirmations, since the REL order reversals, LAG flips, DIR rewiring and POS shifts largely describe the same trace behaviour from different angles; the evidence also cannot show where the either-or choice between A27 and the trio is decided, nor fully rule out that the later window samples two pre-existing variants that the earlier window did not contain — less plausible given the all-or-nothing co-occurrence pattern before, but not excluded from two windows alone. [REL006, LAG006, DIR002, REL011]

Other differences: small position drifts for A25 (mean 0.34 to 0.36) and A31 (0.65 to 0.63), and a few rare new successors at about 1 in 100 of outgoing transitions (towards A12, A22, A24, A33) that the evidence cannot characterise further. [POS001, POS005, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004]
