### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening produced 129 qualifying control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected without change, so chance sampling cannot account for the ensemble. Within it, the evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change around two activities: A14, previously executed once in every case near the end with its observed transitions leading to A03, is no longer executed, while A01, previously unobserved, is now executed in about 56% of cases about 2.4 times each, interleaved with activities such as A02, A03, A06, A08 and A11 and often directly following A08, A09, A10, A13 or A17. [OCC001, OCC003, POS001, POS006, DIR001, DIR010, DIR009, DIR006]

The support is direct and consistent: the occurrence and position items show both shifts on their own (A14 participation 100% → 0%; A01 0% → 56%), roughly two dozen relational items all move in the same direction (co-occurrence with A14 collapses to partner-only or neither; co-occurrence with A01 appears from zero), and the successor changes line up with the same rewiring — though these items largely reflect the same two underlying shifts rather than confirming them independently. [OCC001, OCC003, REL017, REL026, REL004, DIR009]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that an activity was not observed in a window, and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows draw a different case mix rather than a modified process; A01's new relations are also described only at co-occurrence level, mostly interleaved, not as a fixed placement. [OCC001, OCC003, REL004]

Other differences: A05 is executed in far fewer cases and less repetitively (about 62 → 29 in 100 traces; about 2.0 → 1.2 occurrences per trace); A03 has become the invariable final event where earlier it shared the closing phase with A14; A06 and A11 occur later in the case; A05's and A11's successors changed; and many pairwise distances shifted, with several of those lag profiles based on fewer than 30 later-window traces, so their exact distances are weakly supported.
