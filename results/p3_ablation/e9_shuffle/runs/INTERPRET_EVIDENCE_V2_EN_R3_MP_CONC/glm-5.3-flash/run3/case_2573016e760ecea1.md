### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A06, which previously executed between A05 and A04 in about half of the cases, was no longer executed in the later window, so that A05 now leads directly to A04 — a bypass of A06 rather than a shift in how often existing paths are taken. Supporting signs: the successor change at A05 (from A06 to A04) and the A05–A04 distance dropping from two steps to one show the reconnection directly; A06's participation fell from about 52 in 100 traces to zero while A05 and A04 continued to appear at their previous rates; the relation items show A06 no longer co-occurring with either activity; and the nine flagged items far exceed the roughly 0.07 expected by chance alone. Limitations: the items largely describe the same trace-level behaviour and are not independent confirmations; zero participation only establishes that A06 was not observed in the later sample, so the evidence cannot distinguish A06 being removed from the process definition from it simply not being exercised in this sample. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A06 still occurs in process variants or periods not represented in this sample; the evidence also covers only the roughly half of traces containing A05 and A04, leaving the remainder of the process unobserved.
