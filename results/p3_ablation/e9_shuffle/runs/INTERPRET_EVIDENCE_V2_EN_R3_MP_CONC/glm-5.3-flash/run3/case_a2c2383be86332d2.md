### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the repeating block A03 → A08 → A07 → (A05 or A06) runs in the later window as A03 → A08 → (A05 or A06), with A07 no longer executed, and that A01 was added as a new activity feeding into A03 in about a third of the cases. The change is shown directly: A07 falls from about 2.1 occurrences in every trace to none, A08's exclusive edge to A07 is replaced by direct edges to A05 and A06, A01 appears with A03 as its only observed successor, and every downstream distance — including the gaps between repeated executions — shortens by exactly one position, which is what dropping one activity from the block predicts. [OCC003, POS003, OCC001, DIR001, DIR003, LAG013, LAG006, LAG009, LAG004]

The volume of flagged evidence (35 items against roughly 0.19 expected for an unchanged process, all in the control-flow family) makes a chance explanation of the ensemble implausible, though several items describe the same underlying difference and are not independent confirmations. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A07 has become optional or very rare rather than removed — zero participation only means it was not observed in these 200 later traces — and a link between A01's arrival and A02's decline remains unproven. [OCC003, OCC001, REL001]

Other differences: A02's participation dropped from about 48 to about 28 in 100 traces while A05's rose slightly from about 50 to about 55 in 100, and A03 and A08 now occur slightly later within traces; these shifts are consistent with the reblocking described above, but the evidence does not show how they connect to it.
