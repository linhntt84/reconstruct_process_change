### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A08 took over the role previously played by A01 at the same point in the process. Earlier, A01 ran in about 48 in 100 traces, was reached from A17 (exclusively) and from A03 (about 21 in 100 of A03's outgoing transitions), and led to A09, A10 or A16; in the later window A01 is not observed in any trace, while A08 runs in about 48 in 100 traces at the same position, receives the transitions that previously went to A01, and leads to the same successors. The occurrence and position profiles swap almost exactly, the successor profiles match, and both predecessors reroute directly; the pair (A01, A08) also flips from "A01 without A08" in about 48 in 100 earlier traces to "A08 without A01" in about 48 in 100 later traces. The volume of qualifying evidence (53 items against roughly 0.5 expected without a change, for example [OCC001, DIR001, DIR005, DIR008, REL003]) cannot be explained by sampling fluctuation. Limitations: this shows a behavioural substitution of roles, not that A08 is the same activity renamed, and zero participation means only that A01 was not observed; several items describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that A01 was dropped and A08 inserted as a genuinely new activity at a similar position, which is behaviourally indistinguishable from a rename here.

Other differences: A11 and A15 exchanged their placement in the closing section — earlier A11 closed about 52 in 100 traces (always last, no observed outgoing) while A15 sat mid-trace always followed by A03; later A15 closes about 52 in 100 traces (always last, no observed outgoing) while A11 sits mid-trace and is followed by A03, with A05 now flowing to A15 instead of A11 and A18 to A11 instead of A15. This rerouting is directly observed in successor changes and position distributions, so it is a further structural change rather than a path-frequency shift. Minor redistributions remain in the outgoing shares of A02 and A03. [POS003, POS004, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, DIR009, LAG001, LAG002, DIR002, DIR003]
