### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, two activities were added to the process: A01 was inserted between the A06/A13 stage and A04 — A06 and A13, which previously led directly to A04 in about half of their outgoing transitions, now lead to A01, and A01 always leads to A04 — and A02 was appended at the end, after A12, which was previously the final event. The placement rests on direct relational and positional evidence: the successor profiles of A06, A13 and A12 changed exactly at these points; every observed A01/A02 relation changed from no co-occurrence to full co-occurrence with fixed orders matching one placement; the A06/A13-to-A04/A12 gaps lengthened by roughly one step; and participation in A01 and A02 rose from 0 to 200 of 200 traces with exactly one execution each. The screening expected about 0.19 qualifying items without a change and 25 qualified, so the pattern as a whole is not explainable by chance sampling, although many items describe the same underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL003, REL004, REL008, LAG001, LAG002, POS001, POS002, POS005]

One competing explanation remains: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A01 and A02 were not executed there, not that they were absent from the process, so "added" cannot be distinguished from "previously never triggered"; the two-window sample also cannot show how the transition came about. [OCC001, OCC002]

Other differences: in about half of the later traces the A01–A04 and A02–A04 pairs appear interleaved or repeated rather than in one fixed order; since A01 and A02 each occur only once per trace, this may indicate that A04 occurs more than once in those traces, which the evidence does not further resolve.
