### Observed differences

- **A01 is no longer observed.** In the earlier window it appeared exactly once in every trace, at or near the first position (median position 0); in the later window it appears in 0 of 200 traces. [OCC001, POS001]
- **A04 is no longer observed.** Earlier it ran in about 51 in 100 traces, around the first third of the trace; in the later window in 0 of 200. Earlier it was a strict alternative to A03 (about 51% vs 49% of traces, never both) and co-occurred with A02 in 51% of traces without a fixed order between them. [OCC002, POS004, REL005, REL004]
- **Traces now begin with A02 or A03.** Earlier, A02 (about 51 in 100 traces) and A03 (about 49 in 100) sat around the middle of the trace (mean relative positions about 0.53 and 0.44) and always co-occurred with A01, usually after it; in the later window they occur in about 47 and 54 in 100 traces, always at the very first position, and never together — exactly one of them per trace. [POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002]
- **A02's routing narrowed.** Earlier, about 79 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions went to A06, about 14 to A04 and about 7 back to A01; in the later window all of them go to A06. [DIR002]
- **The outgoing behaviour of A01 and A04 is gone.** Earlier, A01's outgoing transitions were split across A03 (~46%), A04 (~34%), A02 (~16%), A05 (~3%) and A06 (~2%), and A04's across A02 (~70%), A06 (~19%) and A01 (~11%); both profiles are empty in the later window because the source activities are unobserved. [DIR001, DIR003]
- **A06 lost its observed outgoing edges.** Earlier, in the 14 traces where its outgoing transitions were seen, it led to A04 (~71%) or A01 (~29%); in the later window none are observed, although A06 still occurs as the target of A02. [DIR004, DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports, as a possibility, a structural reorganisation at the start of the process: A01 and A04 are no longer observed at all — A01 from every trace to none, A04 from about half of traces to none — while A02 and A03, which previously always ran together with A01, now begin the traces as mutually exclusive alternatives, exactly one of them per case (A02 in about 47 in 100 traces, A03 in about 54 in 100, a similarly even split as before), and A02 is now invariably followed by A06 [OCC001, OCC002, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, DIR002].

The signs are direct and consistent across all four control-flow indicators: occurrence and position items show the two activities falling to zero participation and the two successors moving to the first position, the relation items show every co-occurrence with A01 vanishing (A01 and A04 now share a trace in no case), and the direction items show A02's outgoing traffic concentrating entirely on A06. Because A01 and A04 are fully absent in the later window, this is a change in which activities participate, not a re-weighting of existing paths. With 15 qualifying items against roughly 0.1 expected for an unchanged process, sampling alone cannot explain the overall pattern, and the zero participations rest on large earlier samples (200 and 102 traces); note, however, that the items largely describe this one restructuring rather than independent confirmations of separate changes [OCC001, OCC002, REL003, DIR002].

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a change in recording rather than in execution: zero participation only means A01 and A04 were not observed in the later window, so they could still be running unlogged [OCC001, OCC002]. The later window also leaves the downstream behaviour partly invisible: A06 still occurs but its outgoing transitions are unobserved, which does not prove the edges are gone, and no direction profile for A03 qualified, so A03's successors are unknown [DIR004, DIR002].

Other differences: the rare edge from A01 to A05 (about 3 in 100 of A01's outgoing transitions earlier) disappeared together with A01 and is not part of the hypothesis above [DIR001].
