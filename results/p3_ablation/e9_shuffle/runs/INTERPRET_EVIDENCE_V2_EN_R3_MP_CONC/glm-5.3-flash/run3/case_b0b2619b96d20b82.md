### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the cyclic fragment involving A01, A04 and A06 executes in reversed order: where it previously ran A01 → A06 → A04 → A01 (entered at A01, exited via A06 → A05), it now runs A04 → A06 → A01 → A04 (entered at A04, same exit) — in effect, A01 and A04 exchanged their places around A06. This single reordering directly explains the mirrored participation (A01 down from about half to one fifth of traces, A04 up from about a quarter to half), the flipped before/after relations, the six lag reversals, and the position shifts. The screening context supports this reading: 16 control-flow items qualified where only about 0.1 would be expected by chance, and each difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. The main limitation is that the activity codes are anonymous, so a pure exchange of the labels A01 and A04 between the two windows would look identical to this reordering; the evidence cannot distinguish the two.
