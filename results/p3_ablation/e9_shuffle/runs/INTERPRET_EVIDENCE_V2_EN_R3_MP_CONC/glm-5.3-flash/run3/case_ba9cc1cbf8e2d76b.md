### Observed differences

- **Three activities appear that were absent earlier.** In the earlier window, none of the 200 traces contained A19, A20 or A25. In the later window, A20 runs in about 41 in 100 traces, A25 in about 30 in 100 and A19 in about 29 in 100, each exactly once in the traces where it appears, and around the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.52). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

- **The three are mutually exclusive and together near-universal.** In the later window, each pair among A19, A20 and A25 co-occurs in at most about 1 in 200 traces, and the share of traces containing neither member of a pair (about 30, 41 and 29 in 100) matches the third activity's participation — so essentially every later trace contains exactly one of the three, whereas earlier traces contained none of them. [REL001, REL002, REL006]

- **Routing into and out of the new step.** Earlier, A27's outgoing transitions went almost exclusively to A28 (about 97 in 100) and A28's almost exclusively to A29 (about 98 in 100). In the later window, A27 leads to A20 (about 22 in 100), A25 (about 16) and A19 (about 14), with about 43 in 100 still to A28; A28 leads to A20 (about 16), A19 (about 13) and A25 (about 12), with about 57 in 100 still to A29. The new activities in turn lead mainly to A28 (about 53–56 in 100) or A29 (about 41–47 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

- **Ordering around the new step.** Each new activity runs after A27 and before A29 in essentially every trace containing it (for example, A27 precedes A20 in about 40 of the 41 in 100 traces containing both, and A20 precedes A29 in about 40 of 41). Relative to A28 the order is mixed — A20 precedes A28 in about 23 in 100 traces and follows it in about 18 — so the new step runs between A27 and A28 in roughly half of the traces and between A28 and A29 in about four in ten. [REL003, REL007, REL010, REL005, REL009, REL012, REL004, REL008, REL011]

- **Event distances grew by about one step.** Earlier, A27 and A28 were one event apart in about 98 in 100 cases, as were A28 and A29. In the later window, the A27→A28 distance is two events in about 52 in 100 cases and the A28→A29 distance two events in about 41 in 100, while the A27→A29 distance moved from two events (about 96 in 100) to three events (about 92 in 100). Mean relative positions shifted slightly, A29 later (0.55 to 0.57) and A27 earlier (0.48 to 0.46), consistent with a step now running between them; these distances are in event positions, not clock time. [LAG001, LAG003, LAG002, POS005, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a step that was not observed earlier now runs exactly once in essentially every trace between A27 and A29, realised as one of three mutually exclusive activities — A20 in about 41 in 100 traces, A25 in about 30 in 100 and A19 in about 29 in 100 — where earlier the flow ran directly from A27 to A28 to A29. The placement is shown directly: A27 now routes about 52 in 100 of its outgoing transitions into the new activities (earlier about 97 in 100 went to A28) and A28 about 41 in 100 (earlier about 98 in 100 went to A29); each new activity follows A27 and precedes A29 in essentially all shared traces, with mixed order against A28; and the event distances A27→A28 and A28→A29 each grew by about one step. The 28 qualifying items are roughly fifty times the 0.56 expected by chance among the 113 tested units, so the evidence set as a whole is not what an unchanged process produces, and the occurrence, routing, ordering and distance indicators all describe one coherent behavioural difference. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitation: zero participation in the earlier window only means A19, A20 and A25 were not observed in those 200 traces, so the evidence cannot rule out that the three activities already existed and rose from a frequency too low to appear in the sample; it also cannot establish whether they are three alternative realisations of a single step or three separate steps, although their mutual exclusivity and once-per-trace execution fit one step with three variants. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006]

Other differences: a handful of rare outgoing edges of A27 and A28, each carrying about 1 in 200 transitions, appear or disappear between the windows (for example A27 to A31, A09 or A15 earlier, and to A06, A08, A10, A12, A14, A17, A37 or A42 later), a magnitude within sampling noise. [DIR004, DIR005]
