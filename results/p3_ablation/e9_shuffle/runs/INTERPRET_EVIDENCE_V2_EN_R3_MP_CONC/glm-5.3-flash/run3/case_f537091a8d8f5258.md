### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment A05 then A06, which previously ran at the end of the case after A09 (with A05 and A06 in no fixed order), now runs before A07: A05 directly follows the early activities A01/A04, A06 follows A05 and precedes A07, and A09 — still reached from A07 — now closes the case. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

The hypothesis is shown directly by relational and positional evidence rather than inferred from frequencies alone: every order relation between A05/A06 and A07/A09 flipped, the successor profiles changed exactly where the new order predicts, and all 28 qualifying control-flow items (against roughly 0.26 expected without change) describe this single restructuring, so sampling variation cannot account for the ensemble. [POS001-POS005, LAG001-LAG012, REL001-REL005, DIR001-DIR006]

Limitations: positions are event positions, not clock times, so only the relative order is established, not timing; the later-window successor profile of A09 rests on only 12 observed outgoing transitions; and remnants of the earlier flow persist (A07 still precedes A06 in about 14 of 100 traces, and A06 directly follows A09 in about 10), so the new order is dominant but not universal. [REL004, LAG011, LAG012, DIR006, POS005]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the reordering is coupled with the modest participation increase (the whole chain runs in about 54 of 100 traces versus about 48 before); whether the new order holds in all such cases or only in the additional ones cannot be determined. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Other differences: apart from the reordering, the only further difference is this shared participation increase across A01, A04, A05, A06, A07 and A09; no activity was added to or dropped from the observed behaviour. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001]
