### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A04 was dropped from the end of the process: traces that previously always ended A02 → A04 now end with A02 alone, and A02 has become the final step. The signs are direct and mutually consistent — A04's participation fell from all 200 traces to none, the A02→A04 transition disappeared from A02's outgoing profile, the pair relation flipped from fixed-order co-occurrence to A02-only, and A02's position moved from about 0.8 to the final position. All five qualifying items come from control-flow screening of 5 units where only about 0.02 items would be expected by chance, so chance alone cannot account for the set; note, however, that the items describe one coherent underlying difference, not independent confirmations. Limitations: zero participation only shows that A04 was not observed in the later window, so a permanent removal cannot be distinguished from A04 having become conditional and simply never taken in this sample of 200 traces. Because A04 is absent in one window, this is a case of an activity being skipped or removed, not a re-weighting of alternative paths that all remain in use. The position figures refer to event order within the trace, not clock time; the placement conclusion rests on the combination of participation, transition and relation evidence, not on the position shift alone.
