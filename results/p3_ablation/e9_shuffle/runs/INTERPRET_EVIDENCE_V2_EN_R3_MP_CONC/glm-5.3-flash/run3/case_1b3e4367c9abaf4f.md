### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the middle and end of the flow: A01 appears as a new (or previously unobserved) activity executed once in every case near the end, woven between A15 and A16, which now hand over to it about half the time; A02 and A12 became mandatory; A02 now routes predominantly to A06, which runs in every case and repeats in about half of them; activities that were previously alternatives to A02 (A03, A05, A06, A07, A12, A15, A16) now always execute in the same trace as it, and A11 appears in about half of all cases, never without these core activities. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, POS001, REL012, REL015, REL014, REL025]

Supporting signs: 58 qualifying items against roughly 0.53 expected without any change (over a hundred times the chance expectation), so chance cannot account for the set as a whole; the occurrence, routing and positional items show the change directly (participation moving from about half of traces to all of them, new edges into and out of A01, A02's successor shifting to A06), and the relational items consistently place A01 after A02 and A12 and in mixed order with A15/A16. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, REL001, REL007, REL008]

Limitations: the windows cannot show whether A01 is genuinely new or was only too rare to appear in 200 earlier traces; several items describe the same trace-level behaviour and are not independent confirmations; and a wholesale shift to a different mix of pre-existing variants (one variant containing A01, mandatory A02/A12 and a repeated A06) would look the same in these two samples. [OCC001, POS001]

Other differences: A16 occurs slightly earlier (mean relative position about 0.96 to 0.94), and step distances around A05–A15/A16 and A03–A15 shifted slightly towards larger gaps. [POS005, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]
