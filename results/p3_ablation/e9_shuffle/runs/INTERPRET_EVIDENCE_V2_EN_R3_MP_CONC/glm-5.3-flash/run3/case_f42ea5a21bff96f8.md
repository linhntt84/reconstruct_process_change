### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, two existing alternative activity blocks are taken with different frequencies: the block A21–A23, previously executed together in about 75 of 100 traces between A19/A20 and the stable activities A29/A30/A34/A35, runs in only about 25 of 100 later traces, while the alternative block A24–A26 rises from about 25 to about 76 of 100, keeping the same neighbours and the same mutual exclusivity — a shift in how often existing paths are taken, not the addition or removal of activities. The signs are direct and consistent: reciprocal participation changes each backed by roughly 150 versus 50 traces, co-occurrence flips with every stable partner, and the matching redirection of A20's successors; overall, 92 control-flow items qualified against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process (about 80 times the chance expectation), and every item's difference exceeded about 99.5 of 100 random re-splits. The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply contain different mixes of case types rather than a rerouting of the same cases, and it says nothing about why the shares moved.
