### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added to the process and the flow rerouted through it: before, the late activities A03 and A12 were reached directly from the early activities A02 and A09 (and from A08 via A13), while in the later window A02 and A09 lead exclusively to A01, A08 leads to A01 in about half of its transitions, and A01 in turn leads to A03 or A12, running in every case about twice per trace near the start. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL005]

Supporting signs: A01's participation rose from 0 to 100 of 200 traces with new outgoing edges; the predecessors' outgoing profiles changed exactly at A01; inter-activity distances between early and late activities grew by about one step, as expected when one step is inserted; and the screening produced 39 qualifying control-flow items where about 0.31 would be expected by chance in a stable process, with every item's difference exceeding about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, so the ensemble is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC001, DIR001, LAG001, LAG004, LAG011, LAG014]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A01 was not observed there, so an extremely rare pre-existing occurrence (or a reassignment of activity labels) cannot be ruled out; and many items describe the same rerouting, so they are not independent confirmations of one another. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR004]

Other differences: A08 and A13 now repeat far more often (their self-repeats and the A13→A08 pair rose from about 5 of 200 traces to roughly 9–18 of 100), A08's participation fell slightly (about 38 to 34 of 100 traces) with a later position, and A03 and A12 sit slightly later in the case.
