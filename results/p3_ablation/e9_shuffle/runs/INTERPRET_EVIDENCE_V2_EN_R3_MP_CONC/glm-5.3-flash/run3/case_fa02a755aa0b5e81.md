- A02 appeared in about 27 in 100 traces in the earlier window (53 of 200) but was not observed in any of the 200 later traces; its outgoing transitions to A03, A05, A07, A08 and A13 disappeared with it. [OCC001, POS002, DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the sequence around A01 was reordered: in the earlier window the segment ran A11 or A12 → A01 → A05, with A01 late in the trace (mean position about 0.82) and A05 immediately after it, while in the later window A01 stands at the very start, leads to A06, and A11/A12 now directly precede A05 (gap 1–2 instead of A01 doing so at gap 1). Supporting signs: the order relations between A01 and A11/A12 reversed in every co-occurrence, the successor profiles of A01, A11 and A12 changed consistently, and the gap and position shifts all point in the same direction; with 25 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.22 expected if nothing had changed, chance sampling cannot account for the set as a whole (each item also exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, though that alone cannot rule out a rare variant). Limitations: positions and gaps use event positions, not clock time, so this is a reordering in sequence; several items describe the same underlying reordering rather than independent confirmations; and the evidence cannot show whether A01 exchanged places with A06 specifically or was placed at the front in some other way. An important competing explanation is that this reordering and the disappearance of A02 are two separate changes rather than one restructuring.
