### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of activities A19, A20 and A25, which earlier executed between A45 and A26 (before the A26–A27–A28 part of the process), in the later window executes after A28 and before A32. This is shown directly and consistently: all eight activity-pair relations between the two groups reversed order at roughly unchanged co-occurrence and participation rates; the trio's mean position moved from about a third to about three-fifths of the trace; the successor profiles of A19, A20, A25, A27, A28 and A45 rewired exactly as a relocation would imply; and the lag changes (loss of the A25→A26 adjacency, A27→A32 one step longer, new one-step A28→A20 links) match the same rearrangement. With 27 qualifying items against roughly 0.63 expected without a change, chance cannot account for the set as a whole, and every item's difference exceeded about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [LAG001–LAG008, REL001–REL008, DIR001–DIR006, POS001–POS005]

Limitations: the evidence does not reveal the internal order among A19, A20 and A25, and the later order relative to A28 is partly mixed (A25 before A28 in about 15 of 100 traces, A28 before A25 in about 22), so the trio's new placement may vary from case to case rather than occupying one fixed slot; a few lag items (A20–A28, A28–A20, A20–A32) rest on about 1 trace in the earlier window and are weak on their own, though they agree with the strongly supported relation and direction items. [REL003, REL008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG006]

Other differences: none that stand apart from this rearrangement; the slightly earlier positions of A26 and A27 and the mixed one-to-two-step distances from A27 and A28 onwards follow from the trio no longer sitting between A45 and A26. [POS004, POS005, LAG004, LAG007]
