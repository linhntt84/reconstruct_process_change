### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A02 was added to the process as a new, mandatory once-per-case step: it was not observed in any of the 200 earlier traces but is executed exactly once in every later trace, early to midway through the case, interleaved among the existing activities A01, A03, A04 and A06 without a fixed order (mostly before A04). The signs are direct and consistent across occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators: co-occurrence with each of these activities rises from 0 to 100 in 100 traces, A02 forms new mutual successor relations with A01, A03 and A06, and the step distances from A01, A03 and A06 to A04 stretch in the way expected when an activity is inserted between them. The screening produced 13 qualifying control-flow items where fewer than one (about 0.14) would be expected if nothing had changed, so chance sampling cannot account for the set as a whole, and the underlying supports are large (relations based on all 200 traces per window; directions on roughly 190 in 200). Limitations: the indicators largely describe one and the same trace behaviour — A02's appearance — so they are agreeing rather than independent confirmations; and because zero participation strictly means only that A02 was not observed earlier, the evidence cannot rule out that A02 events went unrecorded in the earlier window rather than the step being genuinely new.
