### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change centred on A31, A32 and A37 together with A22: the three-activity group, which previously executed in every case between A20 and A23, now forms an optional branch placed after A23 and A21, running in about 47 in 100 cases and mutually exclusive with A22. In the later window a case runs either A20 → A23 → A21 → A31/A32/A37 → A26 (about 47 in 100 cases) or A20 → A23 → A21 → A22 → A26 (about 53 in 100 cases), whereas before, every case ran A20 → A31/A32/A37 → A23 → A21 → A22 → A26. The support is direct and consistent: participation fell from 100% to about 47% for the group and 54% for A22, co-occurrence between the group and A22 collapsed from 100% to about 0.5%, the order between the group and A23/A21 reversed, successors changed at A20, A21 and the group members, and the event-distance shifts match; 47 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.5 would be expected by chance, and each item's difference exceeded about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces — though the items largely reflect this one trace-level difference rather than independent phenomena. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL006, REL014, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG005, screening]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show what determines which branch a case takes, positions and distances are measured in event order rather than clock time, and several qualified lag items rest on a single later-window observation and were not used here. A competing explanation that cannot be fully excluded is that the later window mixes two variants of which one was previously rare; the earlier window, however, contained both the group and A22 in 200 of 200 traces, making a large pre-existing alternative share implausible. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Other differences: the internal order among A31, A32 and A37 remained unfixed in both windows (both orders in similar shares), and A20's and A26's typical positions shifted only slightly (about 0.34 to 0.36 and 0.66 to 0.63). [REL020, REL021, REL022, POS001, POS005]
