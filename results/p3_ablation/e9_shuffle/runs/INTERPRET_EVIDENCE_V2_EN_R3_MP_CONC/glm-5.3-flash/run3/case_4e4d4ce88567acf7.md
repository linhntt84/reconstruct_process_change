### Observed differences

- In the later window, activity A01 appears in every trace exactly once (200 of 200 traces, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace), whereas in the earlier window it was not observed at all. It sits late in the case: its median relative position is the final event and its mean is about 0.94 of the trace [OCC001, POS001].
- In the earlier window, A05, A06 and A08 each ran in traces that never contained A01; in the later window all three co-occur with A01 in every trace, and A01 almost always comes after them: A08 precedes A01 in all 200 traces, A05 precedes A01 in about 87 in 100, and A06 precedes A01 in about 75 in 100 [REL001, REL002, REL003].
- Routing changed accordingly: A08's outgoing transitions, which earlier went mainly to A05 (about 51 in 100) and A02 (about 24 in 100), now go to A01 in about 82 in 100 cases, with only small shares left for A05, A06, A02 and A04. A01 in turn leads to A05 (about 45 in 100) and A06 (about 45 in 100), rarely to A02 or A04. A08 had 75 observed outgoing transitions earlier versus 200 later, fitting a step that now always has a successor [DIR002, DIR001].
- The step gap from A08 to A05 grew: earlier it was 1 step in about 66 of 100 observed pairs, later only about 33, with 2-step gaps (about 49) and 3–4 step gaps (about 19) taking over. The A08→A06 gap similarly shifted from mostly 2 steps (about 52) to mostly 3 steps (about 46), with 4–5 step gaps appearing (about 12). These shares rest on 43–73 observed pairs per window [LAG001, LAG002].
- The existing activities sit slightly earlier in relative position in the later window: A05 mean about 0.52 to 0.44, A06 about 0.74 to 0.66, A08 about 0.87 to 0.77 — the pattern expected when every trace gains one event near the end [POS002, POS003, POS004].
- Screening qualified 12 control-flow items where roughly 0.1 would be expected if nothing had changed [OCC001, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window a new activity, A01, runs once in every case near the end of the process, placed between A08 and the activities A08 previously led to: A08, which earlier passed control mainly to A05 and A02, now leads to A01 in about 82 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, A01 follows A08 in every trace and follows A05/A06 in most traces, and A01 takes over the edges to A05, A06, A02 and A04 [OCC001, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, DIR001, DIR002]. The longer step gaps from A08 to A05 and A06 fit A01 sitting between A08 and its former successors [LAG001, LAG002]. The twelve qualifying items, against about 0.1 expected without change, all describe this one picture, though they partly re-express the same trace behaviour rather than constituting independent confirmations. Limitations: the evidence shows A01 was unobserved earlier, not that it was impossible — an extremely rare pre-existing activity cannot be ruled out — and the placement is not fully fixed, since A01 precedes A06 in about 25 in 100 and A05 in about 14 in 100 of the later traces [REL001, REL002].

Other differences: the small backward shifts in the relative positions of A05, A06 and A08 [POS002, POS003, POS004] are consistent with traces becoming one event longer and are not treated as independent moves; positions are event positions, so no claim about clock-time timing is made.
