### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A01 executes between A08 and A10: the segment that previously ran directly from A08 to A10, in about 21 in 100 traces, now runs from A08 through A01 to A10, while the share of traces containing that segment stayed about the same (about 21 in 100 in both windows). The signs are direct and mutually consistent: A01 appears only in the later window at the same participation level as the segment; all of its observed transitions lead to A10; the successor share of A08 that previously went to A10 now goes to A01; and the A08→A10 distance shifted from one step to two, which is what one intervening activity produces. Seven of the nine qualifying items (against roughly 0.13 expected by chance if nothing had changed) describe this one coherent difference. [POS001, OCC001, DIR001, DIR003, LAG001, REL001, REL002]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A01 was not observed then, so "newly added" and "previously too rare to observe" cannot be distinguished; about 12 in 100 later traces show A01 and A08 interleaved or repeated rather than in a fixed A08-then-A01 order, which the evidence does not resolve; and the evidence cannot show what triggers or precedes the segment. The main competing explanation — a rare variant that happened to surface only in the later sample of 200 traces — is made unlikely by the coordinated successor and distance changes but cannot be excluded outright. [POS001, OCC001, LAG001, REL001]

Other differences: the rerouting around A03, A12 and A13 (A12 now proceeds to A13 instead of back to A03, and A03 always proceeds to A13) is not part of the main hypothesis; the A12 change rests on few observations (about 16–17 transitions), so it is only tentatively characterised. [DIR002, DIR004]
