### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Sixteen control-flow items qualified, against fewer than 0.1 expected for an unchanged process, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled traces, so chance alone cannot account for this set of differences. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002]

The evidence supports the possibility that the loop step A05 was removed from the repetition around A04. Earlier, A05 executed in about 46 in 100 traces as a mid-trace step that A04 entered and returned to, so A04's repeats sat two steps apart; in the later window A05 is never observed, and a similar amount of repetition now appears as A04 repeating itself directly (about 49 in 100 of A04's outgoing transitions, mostly immediate one-step repeats), while A04's other exit to A06 continues essentially unchanged (about 52 in 100 before, 50 in 100 after).
