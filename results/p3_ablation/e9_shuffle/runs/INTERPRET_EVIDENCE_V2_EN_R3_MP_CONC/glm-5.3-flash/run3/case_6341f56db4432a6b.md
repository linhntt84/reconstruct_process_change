### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the closing step A01 is no longer executed after A12: traces that previously ended with the sequence A12 → A01 (about half of all executions, 103 of 200) now end at A12. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: A01's participation fell from about 52 in 100 traces to 0 in 100 [OCC001, POS001]; the pair relation changed from always "A12 before A01" when both occurred to "A12 only" [REL001]; A12's only observed successor, A01, is no longer reached [DIR003]; and A12 now sits at the very end of the trace in every occurrence, where A01 used to be [POS002]. The volume of flagged items (9 against roughly 0.13 expected for an unchanged process) is far beyond what random re-splits of a stable process produce [screening]. A limitation: zero participation strictly means only that A01 was not observed in the later 200-trace sample, so a drastic drop to a very low frequency cannot be distinguished from complete removal; the five A01-related items also describe one underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations.

Other differences: Two routing shifts appear separate from the A01 change. Flow after A07 and A13 that previously always went to A05 is redistributed, with new observed transitions A07 → A13, A13 → A07 and A13 → A08 [DIR002, DIR004]; and around A06 and A14 the route via A15 is no longer taken, with both A06 and A14 now always leading to A10 [DIR001, DIR005]. These routing shares rest on modest numbers of outgoing transitions (about 24–71 per window), so sampling variation in the small transition counts cannot be ruled out, even though each difference exceeded about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data.
