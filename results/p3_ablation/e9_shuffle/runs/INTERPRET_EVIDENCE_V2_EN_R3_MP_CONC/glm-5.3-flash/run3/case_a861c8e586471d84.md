### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 each ran in roughly a third of the traces (about 36, 31 and 35 in 100) and almost never together, so nearly every trace contained exactly one of the three; in the later window they have all but disappeared (A19 and A25 in 0 of 200 traces, A20 in 1 of 200). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL007, REL009, REL014]
- In the earlier window, A41 preceded whichever of the three ran (before A19 in about 35 in 100 traces, before A20 in about 31, before A25 in about 34), and each of the three led almost exclusively to A26 (about 97–99 in 100 of their outgoing transitions); in the later window, about 92 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions go straight to A26, which was never an immediate successor before. [REL012, REL017, REL021, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- The A41–A26 distance collapsed: in the earlier window the pair was typically two steps apart (about 94 in 100 observed pairs at lag 2, most of the rest at lag 3), in the later window it is immediate (about 92 in 100 at lag 1). [LAG002]
- Co-occurrence of the three with activities that remain fell to about zero: A19, A20 and A25 each co-occurred with A26 and with A31 in roughly 30–36 in 100 earlier traces (always before them) and now essentially never do, and the same holds towards A14, A12 and A24 (about 15–36 in 100 before, about 0 after). The retained activities themselves still occur in essentially every trace in both windows. [REL010, REL011, REL015, REL016, REL019, REL020, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL008, REL013, REL018]
- Minor shifts: A26 now sits slightly earlier in the trace (mean relative position about 0.39 to 0.37) while A14 and A41 sit slightly later (about 0.14 to 0.145 and 0.32 to 0.33), each observed in every trace of both windows; the gap between A24 and A31 narrowed from mostly two steps (about 59 in 100) to mostly one (about 60 in 100). [POS001, POS005, POS006, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-way choice block between A41 and A26 — A19, A20 or A25, exactly one of which ran in almost every earlier trace — is no longer executed in the later window, so that A41 now leads directly to A26. This rests on direct participation evidence (about 36/31/35 in 100 traces down to 0/0/1 of 200), on A41's successor profile flipping from the three activities (about 93 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) to A26 (about 92 in 100), and on the A41–A26 gap collapsing from two steps to one, with all surrounding co-occurrences disappearing consistently while the rest of the skeleton (A14, A26, A31, A41) continues in every trace; 36 qualifying control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected without a change also rules out random sampling as an explanation of the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG002, REL010, REL019]

Limitations: zero participation only shows the activities were not observed in the later window, so an extremely rare execution or a relocation cannot be fully excluded — notably, the single remaining A20 event sits late in its trace (about 62% of trace length versus about 35% before) and led to A30 rather than A26, though this rests on one trace; several items also describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations. [POS003, DIR002, OCC002]

Other differences: the small relative-position shifts of A14, A26 and A41 are consistent with knock-on effects of the removed block; the narrowed A24–A31 gap could be a consequence of the same removal or an independent small change, which the evidence cannot distinguish. [POS001, POS005, POS006, LAG001]
