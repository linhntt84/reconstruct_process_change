### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was removed from the process (or is no longer executed), with the flow that previously ran through A01 now passing directly from A03 and A05 to A01's former successors A09, A10 and A08. This is shown directly: A01 fell from participation in every trace (about 3.5 occurrences each) to zero of 200 traces, its outgoing edges vanished, and its two predecessors A03 and A05 now lead to A09, A10 and A08 and never to A01 [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]. The relational evidence is consistent: all five partners keep their own participation roughly stable (A09, A10 and A08 still in all traces; A05 about 65→67 of 100; A03 about 47→46) while their co-occurrence with A01 drops to zero, and the step distances between A03/A05 and A09/A10 shorten as expected when a repeated intermediate block disappears [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG002, LAG003, LAG005, LAG006, LAG001, LAG004]. With 17 qualifying items against roughly 0.26 expected if nothing had changed, the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by sampling variation.

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A01 was not observed, so removal cannot be distinguished from A01 being renamed or always skipped; the LAG indicator uses event positions rather than clock time; and many items describe the same underlying behaviour (A01's disappearance), so they are not independent confirmations [OCC001, DIR001, LAG002].

Other differences: the slight shift in distances between A08 and A09 (one-step pairs down from about 14 to about 7 of 100) is not directly tied to the bypass pattern above [LAG007].
