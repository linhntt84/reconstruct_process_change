### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, roughly half of the executions (about 47 in 100) skip A19, A25 and A20: A41 is followed directly by A26 and the trace continues via A46 to A47, while the remaining ~53 in 100 executions still run the full sequence A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 → A26 → A46 → A47 in the original order. This rests on participation drops confined to exactly A19, A20 and A25 (100% to about 53%), co-occurrence losses occurring only on cross-pairs while the trio stays intact, the newly observed direct A41 → A26 transition with its split step distance, and unchanged ordering within the full path; with 41 qualifying items against roughly 0.7 expected by chance, the collection of differences is not what an unchanged process typically produces. The main limitation is that the earlier window never showed the direct A41 → A26 step, so a rare pre-existing shortened variant that merely became frequent cannot be ruled out, although its share must have shifted substantially either way. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL008, REL010, REL016, DIR005, LAG002]

Other differences: the near-disappearance of A02 and the loss of its insert position between A46 and A47 is a distinct change not covered by the bypass hypothesis; its decline is well supported, but A02's new outgoing behaviour rests on only 17 traces. The increases in A04 and A06 and the rerouting of A08 are based on few observations and are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001, DIR006, DIR001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002]
