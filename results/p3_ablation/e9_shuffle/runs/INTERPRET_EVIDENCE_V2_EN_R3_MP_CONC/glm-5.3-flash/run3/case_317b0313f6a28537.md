### Observed differences

- **Order reversal around A19, A20 and A25.** In the earlier window, A26 and A27 always preceded A19, A20 and A25 in the traces containing both: for example, A26 came before A19 in about 38 of 100 such traces and A27 before A25 in about 31 of 100, with the reverse order essentially never observed. In the later window this is reversed — A19, A20 and A25 come first in nearly all joint appearances (about 30–36 of 100 depending on the pair), again with almost no exceptions. All six cross-pairs between the two groups show the same flip. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

- **The three activities now run much earlier in the trace.** Their participation stayed similar (A19 in about 39 of 100 traces before and 36 after; A20 about 32→35; A25 about 31→32), but the mean relative position of A19, A20 and A25 fell from about 0.60–0.61 to about 0.32–0.34. A26, A27 and A28 stayed roughly in place (mean positions about 0.33→0.36, 0.56→0.59 and 0.61→0.62). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

- **Routing changed on both sides of the move.** Earlier, the outgoing transitions of A19, A20 and A25 split roughly half to A28 and half to A32 (from A20: about 56% to A28, 43% to A32); in the later window about 88–94% of them go to A26. A45 earlier sent about 85 in 100 outgoing transitions straight to A26, usually immediately; afterwards it sends roughly 27–29 in 100 each to A19, A20 and A25, with no direct transition to A26 observed. A27 now leads to A28 in about 98 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (earlier about 47, with most of the rest to A19/A20/A25), and A28 now leads to A32 in about 96 in 100 (earlier about 50, with about 47 to A19/A20/A25). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

- **Pairwise gaps match the new arrangement.** A45→A26, previously immediate in about 86 of 100 cases, is now two to three steps apart and never immediate; A27→A28 and A28→A32, previously split about evenly between one and two steps, are now almost always immediate (about 96–98 in 100); A27→A32 shortened from typically three steps to two. The old late-stage pairs A25→A28, A25→A32 and A19→A32 nearly vanished (33→1, 61→3 and 76→1 observed pairs), though the later counts rest on only one to three observations. [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

- **A15 repeats less often.** It still appears in every trace, but mean occurrences per trace fell from about 1.9 to about 1.6, and the share of traces in which it recurs fell from about 47 in 100 to about 38 in 100. [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the group of activities A19, A20 and A25 — each executed in roughly a third of traces — was relocated from the late stage of the process to the early stage. In the earlier window they ran after A26 and A27 (mean relative position about 0.60) and routed onward to A28 or A32, while A45 reached A26 directly; in the later window they run before A26 (position about 0.33), route almost exclusively to A26, and A45 reaches A26 through them. Their participation is unchanged and A26, A27 and A28 keep their positions, so the displacement lies on the side of A19, A20 and A25 rather than of their neighbours. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, POS001, POS002, POS003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, LAG007]

The screening comparison rules out sampling noise as an explanation for the set of differences: 121 control-flow units were tested and 26 items qualified, against roughly 0.6 expected in an unchanged process — a count many times larger than chance. The items are not independent confirmations; the six relation items, three position profiles and the routing and gap items all describe the same relocation. [DIR005, POS002, DIR002, REL005, POS001, DIR003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG002, DIR006, POS003, POS006, REL006, POS004, LAG006, LAG003, DIR001, REL001, POS005, DIR004, OCC001, REL004, LAG007, REL003, REL002, LAG001]

Limitations: the indicators use event positions rather than clock time, so "earlier" means earlier in the execution sequence. An important competing reading the evidence cannot rule out is that the late execution of A19, A20 and A25 was removed and an early execution added for a different set of cases, rather than the same block moving as a unit; the near-empty later counts for the old pairs (one to three observations) cannot settle this. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Other differences: A15 still occurs in every trace but repeats less often — mean occurrences per trace fell from about 1.9 to about 1.6 and recurrence support from about 47 to about 38 in 100 traces; no relational item in the evidence ties A15 to the relocated group. [OCC001] A26, A27 and A28 each drifted slightly later (about 0.02–0.03 on the relative position scale). [POS004, POS005, POS006]
