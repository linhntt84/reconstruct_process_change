### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change in the later window: A01 and A17 are no longer executed, A07 appears as a new early activity whose observed successors are A03 and A04, and A06 and A16 — previously optional, repeated and mid-trace — now occur exactly once in every trace as a closing pair after A04 and A10. [OCC001, OCC008, OCC004, OCC003, OCC007, POS004, POS011, REL045, DIR004, DIR007]

The support is direct and consistent: the participation changes are all-or-nothing with full support behind them (A17 in 200 of 200 earlier traces and none later; A07 in none earlier and about 68 in 100 later), successor profiles are replaced outright rather than reweighted, every relation involving A01 or A17 flips in the same direction, and screening qualified 171 control-flow items where fewer than one would be expected in an unchanged process, so sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC008, OCC004, DIR001, DIR012, REL014, REL063]

Limitations: the qualifying items overlap heavily — the many relation items for pairs involving A01 or A17, and those describing A02's interleaving, reflect the same trace behaviour from different angles and are not independent confirmations — and the evidence cannot show whether the added, removed and newly mandatory activities are functionally connected or independent changes. An important competing explanation is a non-random difference in case mix between the windows, for instance the later window drawing mostly on a variant that was rare before; random re-splits of the pooled data virtually never produce differences of this size, but a systematic sampling difference cannot be excluded. [OCC001, OCC008, REL045, OCC002]

Other differences: A02, A14 and A15 are executed far more often per trace in the later window (about 2 to 9, 1 to 4.5 and 1.1 to 4.6 occurrences respectively) and now co-occur and interleave widely with other activities; A13 now leads to A02 in all observed cases and A12 to A09/A08 instead of A01/A08; and several activities occupy different relative positions (A10 earlier, A09/A11/A12 later, A04 more variable). These fit the same restructuring but are not needed for the main hypothesis. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC006, REL015, DIR009, DIR008, LAG035, POS007]
