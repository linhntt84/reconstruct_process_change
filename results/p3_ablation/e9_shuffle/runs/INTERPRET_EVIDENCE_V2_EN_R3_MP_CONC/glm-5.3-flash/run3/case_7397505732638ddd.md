### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the activities A26, A17, A15, A18, A19, A21 and A20 each had exactly one observed successor, forming one fixed chain A26 → A17 → A15 → A18 → A19 → A21 → A20, with A27 after A20 [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007].
- Every pair among A15, A17, A18, A19, A20 and A21 co-occurred in all 200 earlier traces and always kept the same order — for example, A15 always preceded A19 and A18 always preceded A20 [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008].
- In the later window these activities still occur in essentially every trace, but the fixed order has largely dissolved into mixed orders: A19 now precedes A18 in about 85 in 100 traces (never in the earlier window), A19 precedes A15 in about 74 in 100, A21 precedes A18 in about 68 in 100, A15/A21 and A18/A20 are each close to 50/50, while A17 still precedes A21 in about 77 in 100; correspondingly, A15 before A19 within five steps now appears in only about 52 of 200 later traces, from all 200 [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG002].
- Direct successions changed: in the later window A18 passes directly to A27 in about 52 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions (previously always to A19), A20 reaches A27 in only about 49 in 100 (previously always), and A26 leads to A19 in about 47 in 100 (previously always to A17) [DIR003, DIR005, DIR007].
- Distances that were constant are now spread: A18 and A27 were always 4 steps apart and are now about 1 step apart in about half the cases; A26 and A19 fell from always 4 to about 1 step in about 47 in 100 cases; A17 and A19 fell from always 3 to about 1 step in about half; A19 and A27 moved from always 3 to about 5 steps apart in about half the cases [LAG013, LAG023, LAG008, LAG016].
- Typical relative positions moved in the same direction: A19, A20 and A21 now occur earlier in the trace (mean relative position about 0.59, 0.68 and 0.64, from about 0.65, 0.71 and 0.68), while A15, A17 and A18 occur later (about 0.64, 0.59 and 0.68, from about 0.59, 0.56 and 0.62) [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006].
- Participation itself did not change: every pair still co-occurs in all traces and each activity still appears in essentially all traces in both windows, with no interleaving or repetition in either window [REL001, REL004, POS001, POS004].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the fixed sequential ordering between A15, A17, A18, A19, A20 and A21 — bounded by A26 upstream and A27 downstream — was relaxed into a flexible order: all these activities still run in virtually every case, but their relative order now varies from case to case, A18 typically hands over directly to A27 instead of A19, A26 often reaches A19 without the intermediate activities, and the typical positions of A19, A20 and A21 moved earlier while those of A15, A17 and A18 moved later [REL001, REL006, DIR003, DIR007, POS004, POS001].

The screening produced 45 qualifying control-flow items from 95 tested units, versus fewer than 1 expected by chance when nothing has changed, so the ensemble cannot be explained by random sampling; each item's difference also exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data [REL001, DIR003, LAG013]. The order change is shown directly by relational evidence and successor changes rather than inferred from position shifts alone, and it is consistent across the successor, distance and position indicators [REL006, DIR003, LAG013, POS004]. Because co-occurrence and coverage are unchanged, this is a change in ordering constraints rather than activities becoming optional or being replaced, and the mixed two-way orders (for example A15/A21 near 50/50) rule out a simple exchange of two activities [REL001, REL003, POS001].

Limitations: the evidence cannot show which events now fill the widened gaps between these activities, and window-level aggregates cannot distinguish case-by-case flexibility from a mixture of two or more fixed route variants coexisting in the later window; successor shares are also computed over outgoing transitions rather than over cases [REL003, DIR003].
