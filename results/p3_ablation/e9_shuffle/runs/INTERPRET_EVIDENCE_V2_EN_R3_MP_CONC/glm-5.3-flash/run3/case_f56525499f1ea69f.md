### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the exclusive choice between the two blocks was removed: traces that previously contained either the trio A33/A35/A36 or the trio A37/A38/A39 now contain both trios in sequence — A33/A35/A36 first (in unfixed internal order), then A37/A38/A39 (in unfixed internal order) — making all six activities near-mandatory, with A30 running before the first trio and A31 after the second. This is shown directly by relations that were strictly mutually exclusive before (co-occurrence 0 in 100) and near-universal afterwards (about 98–99 in 100) with a stable between-block precedence, and it is corroborated by participation rising to about 98–100 in 100, by the opposite position shifts of the two trios, by the stretched step distances around the inserted trio, and by the successor changes at A30 and the first trio. The volume of qualifying items (89 against about 1 expected if nothing had changed) cannot be explained by chance as a whole, and every item's difference exceeds those seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits, although the many items largely reflect this one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. The evidence cannot rule out that the later window simply samples a different mix of pre-existing trace types rather than a modified routing, and the position indicators show relative order within the trace, not clock-time movement. [REL045, REL040, OCC004, OCC009, POS006, POS011, LAG003, LAG007, DIR006, DIR008]

Other differences: A04 declined sharply in participation (about 30 to 14 in 100 traces), now occurs later and more variably, and is no longer tightly coupled to A16; A06 and A10 increased slightly on few observations, and the successor profiles of the rare activities A02 and A08 were reshuffled on very few outgoing transitions (13 versus 23, and 9 versus 28), which is consistent with sampling noise rather than a structural change.
