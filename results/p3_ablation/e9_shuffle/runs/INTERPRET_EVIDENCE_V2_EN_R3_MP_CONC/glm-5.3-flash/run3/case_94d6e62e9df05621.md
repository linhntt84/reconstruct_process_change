### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A07 and A08 usually appeared together: about 53 in 100 traces contained both, in either order and with no fixed sequence between them (A07 before A08 in about 28 in 100 traces, A08 before A07 in about 25). In the later window the two never share a trace: A07 appears without A08 in about 23 in 100 traces, and A08 without A07 in about 29 in 100. [REL001]
- Both activities also became less frequent: A07 occurs in about 23 in 100 later traces versus about 53 in 100 earlier (mean occurrences per trace 0.53 to 0.23), and A08 in about 29 versus about 53 (0.53 to 0.29). Neither activity repeats within a trace in either window. [OCC001, OCC002]
- The direct transitions between the two activities are no longer observed. Earlier, about 65 in 100 of A07's observed outgoing transitions led to A08, and about 61 in 100 of A08's led to A07; in the later window both activities lead mainly to A01 (about 72 in 100 of A07's observed outgoing transitions, about 71 of A08's), with A05 taking most of the remainder (about 28–29). These later shares rest on few observations (about 18 and 17 outgoing transitions). [DIR001, DIR002]
- The share of traces containing neither activity barely moved (about 47 in 100 earlier, 49 later), so the change is confined to the traces that involve these activities. [REL001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A possibility supported by the evidence is that A07 and A08 changed from being executed together — in about half of the earlier traces, in either order — to being mutually exclusive alternatives in the later window, where each appears alone in roughly a quarter of traces and the direct hand-offs between them are no longer observed, with both now leading mainly to A01. The relational evidence shows this directly: co-occurrence fell from about 53 in 100 traces to none while each activity appearing alone rose from zero to about 23–29 in 100, and the relation profile rests on about 100 traces per window, so a rare-variant artefact is unlikely. The participation drops point the same way, and the successor changes corroborate the loss of the mutual edges. Notably, at their later frequencies two independent activities would still co-occur in roughly 6 in 100 traces, yet none do, so the drop is not merely a side effect of lower participation. Six items qualified against roughly 0.11 expected for an unchanged process, so chance sampling cannot explain the set as a whole. Limitations: the new successor shares rest on few later-window observations, the six items largely describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot show what mechanism made the activities exclusive.
