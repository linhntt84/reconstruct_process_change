### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a large-scale reordering of the control flow in which the branch block A19/A21/A24 and the sequential block A20/A22/A23 exchanged places around A25 and A29, and A02 took on a new early role. In the earlier window the main sequence ran A40 → A20/A22/A23 (in varying order, the last one leading to A25) → A25 → A29 → one of A19/A21/A24 → A30; in the later window it runs A40 → often via A02 → one of A19/A21/A24 → A25 → A29 → A20/A22/A23 (the last one leading to A30) → A30. The choice among the branch activities also tilted toward A19 (about 31 → 44 in 100 cases). [REL027, REL017, REL014, DIR002, DIR004, DIR007, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR008, DIR009, DIR001, OCC001, LAG004, LAG007, POS002, POS004, POS007]

This goes well beyond what chance would produce: 58 items qualified against fewer than 1 expected without a change (all in the control-flow family), and every item exceeds about 99.5 of 100 random re-splits. The hypothesis is directly and consistently shown: near-deterministic order reversals across all cross-block pairs, successor replacements at A19/A21/A24 (A30 → A25) and at A20/A22/A23 (A25 → A30), and lag-distance changes (A40→A25 from about 4 steps to 2–3; A29→A30 from 2 to 4) that quantitatively match the new layout, including the 3-step share equalling A02's participation. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR007, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, LAG004, LAG007, LAG001, REL027]

Limitations: the exclusivity of the branch (one of A19/A21/A24 per case) is inferred from shares summing to about one per trace, not observed trace by trace; and a mixture of two coexisting variants whose shares flipped between the windows would look identical to a single restructured sequence — the evidence cannot separate these two possibilities.
