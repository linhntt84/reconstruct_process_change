### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a swap between two blocks of activities. In the earlier window, A21, A22 and A23 each ran in about 75 in 100 traces and the three almost always appeared together (co-occurrence about 75 in 100); in the later window each appears in only about 25 in 100 traces and they hardly ever share a trace. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL013, REL014, REL021]
- The mirror image holds for A24, A25 and A26: in the later window each runs in about 75 in 100 traces (up from about 25 in 100), and the three now typically occur together, whereas before they shared only about 25 in 100 traces. [OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, REL034, REL035, REL039]
- The two blocks are mutually exclusive alternatives in both windows: a trace contains one block or the other, essentially never both, in either window. [REL015, REL016, REL022, REL028, REL029, REL030]
- A20 precedes whichever block runs and is present in every trace in both windows; its outgoing transitions moved from mostly A21/A22/A23 (together about 75% of outflows) to mostly A24/A25/A26 (together about 75%). [DIR003, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- Downstream, A30 — present in all traces throughout — shifted from co-occurring with the old block in about 75 in 100 traces to co-occurring with the new block in about 75 in 100; A32 and A33 re-associated the same way (A32 with A21: about 48% → 15% of traces; A32 with A24: about 17% → 52%). [REL018, REL025, REL031, REL036, REL040, REL043, REL019, REL037]
- Ordering inside each block is loose in both windows (e.g., A21 before A22 in about 37% of traces and the reverse in about 37% before; A24 before A25 in about 37% and the reverse in about 38% after), so the within-block ordering behaviour is unchanged. [REL013, REL021, REL034, REL039]
- Where the new block runs, step distances spread: one-step gaps became less dominant (A20→A24 at distance 1: about 54% → 33%; A32→A33: about 56% → 39%); these lag shifts follow from the block swap rather than being separate findings. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- A10 roughly tripled its participation (about 6 → 21 in 100 traces) and now almost always leads to A33 (about 18% → 90% of its outflows), appearing later in the trace among the cases that contain it (mean relative position about 0.57 → 0.82); this rests on 11 vs 42 traces. [OCC002, DIR002, POS001]
- A02 nearly disappeared (18 → 2 traces of 200); its apparent complete change of successors rests on only 2 later-window traces. [OCC001, DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two alternative paths between A20 and the A30/A32/A33 stage swapped frequencies: in the earlier window roughly 75 in 100 traces ran A20 → A21, A22, A23 (in no fixed order) and about 25 in 100 ran A20 → A24, A25, A26; in the later window the shares are reversed, with A30, A32 and A33 now following the A24–A26 block. Every activity involved remains observed in a substantial share of traces in both windows (about 25–75%), nothing is bypassed, and only the shares between the alternatives shift — the signature of changed path frequencies rather than activities being added, removed or bypassed. Supporting signs: participation changes of about 50 percentage points for all six activities, each backed by roughly 150 traces per window; the redirection of A20's outgoing transitions; the preserved mutual exclusivity of the blocks; and the matching re-association of the downstream activities. In aggregate, 62 control-flow items qualified against about 1 expected by chance, so sampling noise cannot explain the pattern as a whole. Limitations: the many relation items describe the same swap from different activity pairs and are not independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot distinguish a changed routing rule from a changed mix of case types (e.g., two variants whose proportions shifted). [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR003, REL015, REL034, REL018, REL036]

Other differences:
- A10's increased participation and its near-exclusive succession to A33, at a later trace position, may belong to the same restructuring or be a separate difference; the evidence does not tie it to the block swap. [OCC002, DIR002, POS001]
- A02's near-disappearance and changed successors are too thinly supported (2 later-window traces) to interpret. [OCC001, DIR001]
