### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window contains a new closing segment at the end of the process: a new activity A02 runs as the final step in about 57 in 100 traces, reached from the previously terminal activities A16 and A19 and from A09, and a second new activity A01 is inserted before A16 in about 15 in 100 traces, so that some runs end A10/A17 → A01 → A16 → A02. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation rises from zero to 113 and 30 of 200 traces; A02 occupies the final position in every occurrence; the successions A16→A02, A19→A02 and A01→A16 are observed without exception in the later window and never before; and A02 begins to co-occur with A03, A07, A09, A13, A14, A16 and A19. The volume of qualifying evidence (42 items against roughly 0.44 expected if nothing had changed) cannot be explained by sampling variation as a whole, although many items describe the same underlying difference rather than independent confirmations.
