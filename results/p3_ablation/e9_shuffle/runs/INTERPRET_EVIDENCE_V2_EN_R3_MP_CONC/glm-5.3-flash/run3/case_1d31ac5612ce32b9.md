### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the fragment A02 → A04 → A03 ran exactly once per trace in a fixed order: A02 occurred once in every trace (mean 1.0, never repeated) at a fixed relative position of 0.5, and A04 occurred once in every trace at a fixed relative position of 0.75. [OCC001, POS001, POS002]
- Routing was rigid: every observed exit of A02 went to A04 and every observed exit of A04 went to A03, with the three activities adjacent (A02→A04 and A04→A03 one step apart, A02→A03 two steps apart). [DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- In the later window, A02 still appears in every trace but now occurs about 2.1 times per trace on average and repeats within a trace in about 50 in 100 traces (recurrence support rose from 0 to about 0.50). [OCC001]
- In the later window, A04 has become conditional: it appears in only about 99 of 200 traces (down from all 200) and, in the traces where it occurs, about 2.2 times on average (222 observed outgoing transitions). [POS002, DIR002]
- In the later window, A02's exits split — about 53 in 100 go to A04 and about 47 in 100 go directly to A03 (roughly one direct handover per trace plus about 220 returns into the cycle) — whereas A04's exits changed completely from all-to-A03 to all-to-A02. [DIR001, DIR002]
- Distances changed accordingly: A02→A03, previously always 2 steps, is now 1 step in about 40 in 100 observed pairs with gaps of 3 and 5 also appearing; A02→A04, previously always adjacent, now also appears at 3 and 5 steps; and A04→A03, previously always adjacent, occurs only at 2 steps (about 46 in 100) or 4 steps (about 18 in 100). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Pairwise relations changed from "always together in a fixed order" to mixed: about half the later traces contain A02 without A04, and co-occurring A02/A04 cases are interleaved or repeated; A02 and A03 still co-occur in every trace but now show A02-before-A03 in about half and interleaved/repeated in about half; about half the traces contain A03 without A04. [REL001, REL002, REL003]
- Relative positions spread out: A02 moved from a fixed 0.5 to a mean of about 0.59 with visible spread, and A04 from a fixed 0.75 to a mean of about 0.56. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which a repeating cycle between A02 and A04 became active: in the earlier window every trace executed A02 → A04 → A03 once, while in the later window about half the traces go from A02 directly to A03 and the other half run one or more A02 → A04 → A02 cycles (about two cycles per such trace on average) before A02 finally proceeds to A03 — so A02 became repeatable, A04 became optional, and A04's former transition to A03 was replaced by a return to A02. [DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, POS002, REL002, LAG003]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor change at A04 is observed directly in the routing profiles, A02's repetition is measured on occurrences, and the relation, distance and position items all match the same loop picture; screening also qualified 11 control-flow items where about 0.06 would be expected by chance in an unchanged process, so the ensemble is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. [DIR002, OCC001, LAG003, DIR001, LAG001, LAG002, REL001, REL002, REL003, POS001, POS002]

The evidence cannot rule out that the later window mixes two coexisting variants (a direct and a looping one) rather than a single modified process, nor can it establish how many cycle iterations are possible per trace; the position shifts of A02 and A04 read as consequences of the added repetitions rather than independent evidence of relocation. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, DIR001]
