### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in which A02, A03 and A08 — previously executed in about 47, 21 and 47 of every 100 traces — are no longer executed at all, and the flow is rewired so that their neighbours connect directly: A18 and A05 now lead to A10 where they led to A02, A10 leads to A01 where it led to A08, A01 leads to A11 where it branched to A06/A07/A14, A13 and A19 lead to A05/A18 where they led to A03, and A16 leads to the end activities A06/A14/A07 where it led to A01, with A07 and A14 becoming rare end-of-trace activities. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation falls from 41–94 of 200 traces to zero, successor profiles change directly, co-occurrences collapse into separate execution, and pairwise distances shift by about one step; the screening flagged 129 items where fewer than 1 would be expected if nothing had changed. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, DIR008, DIR013, DIR001, REL042, LAG023]

Limitations: zero participation shows only that these activities were not executed in the later window, not that they were removed from the process definition, and the pairwise indicators cannot fully reconstruct the new end-to-end paths; many items describe the same underlying rewiring and are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the three activities became optional and were simply never taken in this period rather than being structurally disconnected. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004]

A09 and A17, which previously led mainly to A05 and A18, now often follow one another (A09 to A17 in about half of A09's outgoing transitions, and A17 to A09 in about half of A17's).
