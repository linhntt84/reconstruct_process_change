### Observed differences

- Two activities that never appeared in the earlier window are present in the later one. A08 occurs in about 48 in 100 traces (none before), once per trace and late in the trace; in every such trace A07 comes before it, and A08 is always adjacent to A12 — A12 first in about 30 in 100 traces, A08 first in about 18 in 100. A12 itself rose from about 15 to 48 in 100 traces and now occurs only together with A08. [OCC004, POS003, OCC007, REL018, REL023, DIR003, DIR006]
- A09 occurs in about 18 in 100 traces (none before), early in the trace (mean position about 0.22 of the trace length); it is always preceded by A06, and about 89 in 100 of its outgoing transitions lead to A07. A06 accordingly now leads only to A09, whereas before it led mostly to A07 (about 73 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) and to A10 (about 18 in 100). [OCC005, POS004, REL015, DIR004, DIR002]
- A01 was observed in about 32 in 100 traces before (at the end of the trace) and in none after. [OCC001, POS001, REL003]
- A03 and A10 were present in every trace before (200 of 200) and in only about 50 in 100 after. Before, they always co-occurred, in either order (roughly 50/50); after, they never co-occur — each appears without the other in about 50 in 100 traces, so they changed from being executed together to being alternatives. A10's co-occurrence with A07 likewise dropped from every trace to about 50 in 100. [OCC002, OCC006, REL012, REL009, REL019]
- The successor mix of A03 changed: before, about 49 in 100 of its outgoing transitions went to A10 and about 16 to A01; after, none go to A10 or A01, and the transitions go to A04 (about 38 in 100), A12 (25), A07 (24) and A08 (13). A10's outgoing mix shifted similarly, from mainly A03 to A04 (about 43 in 100), A12 (23) and A07 (20). [DIR001, DIR005]
- Smaller shifts: A04's participation rose from about 37 to 52 in 100 traces; A07's median event position moved from 0.4 to 0.5 and its distance to A03 is now always one step (before one or two); the A06–A10 distance is now mostly three steps (before one to three). [OCC003, POS002, LAG001, LAG004, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in the process around the mandatory activity A07. In the later window, A08 appears as a new optional activity (about 48 in 100 traces), always after A07 and always adjacent to A12; A09 appears as another new optional activity (about 18 in 100 traces), placed early, always after A06 and usually leading to A07. At the same time, A01 is no longer observed (previously about 32 in 100 traces), and A03 and A10 — previously both mandatory and executed together in either order — now appear in only about half the traces and have become mutually exclusive alternatives. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC006, REL018, REL023, REL015, REL012, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

The hypothesis is supported directly and consistently: the participation changes are observed outright (0 to 96 and 0 to 36 traces; 64 to 0; 200 to 100 for both A03 and A10), and the relational and directional items show the new placements themselves (A07 before A08, A06 before A09, A09 to A07, A08 next to A12, A03 and A10 disjoint). With 49 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.32 expected if nothing had changed, chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole; note, however, that several items describe the same trace behaviour (for example, A12's rise is the same event as A08's insertion), so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL012, REL018, REL023, DIR001]

Limitations: zero participation only means an activity was not observed, so it cannot be confirmed that A01 was removed rather than simply not triggered; and the evidence cannot distinguish a modification of a single process from a shift in the mix of pre-existing process variants between the two windows. [OCC001, OCC004]

Other differences: A04's participation increased from about 37 to 52 in 100 traces, A07's median event position rose slightly (0.4 to 0.5) with its distance to A03 now always one step, and A06–A10 distances lengthened to about three steps; these accompany, but are not part of, the main hypothesis. [OCC003, POS002, LAG001, LAG004, LAG003]
