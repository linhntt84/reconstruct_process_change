### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 has taken over the place A03 used to occupy. In the earlier window, A03 ran in about 45 of 100 cases as a mid-trace step with observed outgoing flow mainly to A11, A10, A09 and A12; in the later window, A03 is not observed, while A01 runs in about 50 of 100 cases at the same relative position, with nearly the same successor profile, with the incoming flow from A09 and A11 redirected from A03 to A01, and co-occurring with A03's former partners (A11, A09, A05, A10, A12) at similar rates.
