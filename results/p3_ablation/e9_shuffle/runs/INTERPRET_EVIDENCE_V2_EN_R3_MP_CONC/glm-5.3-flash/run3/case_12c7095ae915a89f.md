### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the core ran as one fixed sequence, A01 → A02 → A04 → A03: about 87 in 100 of A01's outgoing transitions went to A02, about 94 in 100 of A02's went to A04 and about 96 in 100 of A04's went to A03; A02 preceded A04 in all 200 earlier-window traces, A01→A02 was always immediately adjacent, and A04→A03 was adjacent in about 96 of 100 cases. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, LAG001, LAG004]
- In the later window the order between A02 and A04 is no longer fixed: the two activities still occur together in every trace, but A02 now comes first in about 54 in 100 traces and A04 first in about 46. [REL001]
- New direct successions accompany this: A01 now passes straight to A04 in about 43 in 100 of its transitions (about 6 before), A02 passes straight to A03 in about 44 (before essentially never) and A04 passes straight to A02 in about 40 (before never). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- Adjacency shifts match this picture: A01→A04 and A02→A03, previously separated by one activity in about 90–94 of 100 cases, are now immediately adjacent in about half; A01→A02 and A04→A03, previously adjacent, now have one activity between them in roughly 40–45 of 100 cases. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]
- The two activities' typical positions in the trace converge and spread out: A02's mean relative position moves from about 0.17 to 0.27 and A04's from about 0.39 to 0.28, with the positional spread about twice as wide for both (A04's median moves from 0.4 to 0.2). [POS001, POS002]
- The surrounding frame is unchanged: A01 still precedes both activities, A03 still follows them, and A05/A06 remain at most rare successors of A01. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Ten control-flow items qualified where about 0.1 would be expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeds that seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits; a pattern of this size across the family cannot be explained by sampling variation or a single rare variant. [REL001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, POS002]

The evidence supports the possibility that the ordering constraint between A02 and A04 was relaxed. In the earlier window they formed a strict sequence with A02 always before A04, yielding the single route A01 → A02 → A04 → A03; in the later window both orders occur in similar proportions (about 54 vs 46 in 100), so a second route A01 → A04 → A02 → A03 runs alongside the original, while A01 still precedes both activities and A03 still follows both. Because both orders now occur frequently rather than one simply replacing the other, this is a change in ordering constraints, not an exchange of the two activities' positions. [REL001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

The relation itself is measured directly and with full support (both activities occur in all 200 traces of each window), and the successor, adjacency and position shifts all fit the two-route picture; however, those corroborating items largely re-express the same underlying reordering rather than constituting independent confirmations. The evidence also cannot distinguish genuine parallel execution of A02 and A04 from a free choice between two fixed sequences, since only completion orders are observed. [REL001, POS001, POS002]

Other differences: A05 and A06, which together received about 2–3 in 100 of A01's outgoing transitions in the earlier window, appear in only about 0.5 in 100 in the later window; given their rarity, this may be a sampling difference rather than a removed path. [DIR001]
