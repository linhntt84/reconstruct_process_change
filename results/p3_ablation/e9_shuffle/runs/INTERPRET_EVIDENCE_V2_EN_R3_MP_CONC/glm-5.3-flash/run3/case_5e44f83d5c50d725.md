### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening qualified 10 control-flow items where roughly 0.08 would be expected if nothing had changed, so this volume of findings cannot be explained by random re-splitting of stable behaviour. The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was removed from the process (or bypassed): the earlier chain A05 → A01 → A07 was replaced by a direct A05 → A07 hand-off, with A01 no longer executed. The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: A01 fell from full participation (all 200 traces) to none while A05 and A07 still occur in every trace; A05's outgoing share to A01 (about 51 in 100) was taken over by a share to A07 (about 49 in 100); the A05→A07 distance shortened from typically 2 steps to 1 step; and A07's position moved earlier in the trace [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, REL002, DIR003, LAG001, POS003].

Limitations: zero participation only establishes that A01 was not observed in the later window, not why — the evidence cannot distinguish an actual removal from a change in recording that stopped capturing A01, although a drop from 200 of 200 traces to 0 of 200 makes a sampling fluctuation implausible [OCC001]. Direction shares are computed over outgoing transitions rather than cases, so the evidence cannot show whether the new A05→A07 hand-off is one edge or several [DIR003, LAG001].

Other differences: A04's outgoing shares shifted slightly — its transition to A01 (about 4 in 100) is gone and its transition to A07 rose from about 2 to about 7 in 100, while A04→A05 remains dominant (about 92 vs 87 in 100) [DIR002]; the small position shift of A05 is plausibly a side-effect of the shorter traces rather than an independent change [POS002].
