### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the late-middle sequence is reversed: A26 and A27, which previously executed after A35, A36 and A41 in about 99 of 100 co-occurring traces, now execute before them in about 97–98 of 100, with A25 now flowing almost directly into A27 and A30 following A35/A36/A41 a few steps later instead of immediately after A26. The reversal is shown directly by the pair relations, and the position shifts, successor changes and distance changes all agree with it; the 45 qualifying control-flow items are many times the 0.83 expected without change, though they largely describe one underlying reordering rather than independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of case variants with different fixed orders rather than a re-sequencing within one flow, and positions are event positions rather than clock times.
