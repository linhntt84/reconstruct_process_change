### Observed differences

- Activity A02 is absent from all 200 earlier traces, while in the later window it occurs exactly once in every trace: participation and mean occurrences per trace both go from 0 to 1, with no repetition in either window [OCC001].
- In the later window A02 is always the closing event of the trace (relative position 1.0 in 100 in 100 cases) and always follows A06: the two activities co-occur in every later trace with A06 first, whereas earlier A06 appeared in every trace without A02 [REL001, POS001].
- The successor of A06 changes: in the earlier window the few observed outgoing transitions of A06 (only 4 observations) all led to A04, while in the later window all 200 observed transitions lead to A02 [DIR001].
- A06 itself is present in every trace in both windows, but its position shifts: earlier it sat at about 99% of the trace (essentially the final step, median relative position 1.0), in the later window at about 80% (median 0.8) — consistent with one event having been appended after it [POS002].
- For context, 5 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.05 expected by chance alone for the 10 units tested, so the ensemble of items is far larger than an unchanged process would typically produce [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, POS002, REL001].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 was added as a new mandatory final step executed directly after A06. In the earlier window, cases effectively ended with A06 and A02 was never observed; in the later window, every case runs A06 and then A02, with A02 as the last event. The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: A02's participation rises from 0 to 100 in 100 traces with exactly one occurrence each; A06 precedes A02 in every later trace and all of A06's later outgoing transitions lead to A02; and A06's relative position moves from the end of the trace to about 80% through it, matching the insertion of one event after it [OCC001, REL001, DIR001, POS001, POS002].

Limitations: the earlier picture of A06's successor rests on only 4 observations, so the role of A04 before the change is weakly established and the evidence cannot show whether A04 still occurs elsewhere in the process. Strictly, zero participation in the earlier window means only that A02 was not observed there, so a pre-existing but previously unobserved use of A02 cannot be excluded outright. The five items also largely describe the same underlying insertion and are not independent confirmations of it [DIR001, OCC001].
