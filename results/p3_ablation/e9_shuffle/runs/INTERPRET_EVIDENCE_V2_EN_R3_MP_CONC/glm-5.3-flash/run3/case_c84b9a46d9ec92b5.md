### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 were near-exclusive alternatives: each ran in about 31–37 in 100 traces (A19 in 73 of 200, A20 in 62, A25 in 70) and hardly ever together (about 1–2 in 100). In the later window all three are essentially gone — A20 and A25 in 0 of 200 traces, A19 in 1 of 200. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL003, REL008, REL002, REL007, REL012]

- Before, this block sat between A41 and A26: about 87 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions went to one of A25 (~32), A19 (~29) or A20 (~26), and each of the three led on to A26 in about 95 in 100 of its outgoing transitions. In the later window, A41 leads directly to A26 in about 86 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, and no transitions into A19, A20 or A25 are observed. [DIR005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL013, REL014, REL015]

- The gap between A41 and A26 shrank accordingly: before, the pair was 2 positions apart in about 85 in 100 cases (3 apart in about 15); after, 1 position apart in about 86 in 100. [LAG001]

- A26, A31 and A41 still occur in every trace in both windows; A26's mean position moved slightly earlier (about 0.39 to 0.37 of the trace) and A41's slightly later (about 0.32 to 0.33). [POS004, POS005, REL009, REL010, REL015]

- A21's outgoing shares shifted: A31 fell from about 54 to 38 in 100 of A21's outgoing transitions while A24 rose from about 14 to 35 (A21 present in about 57% versus 51% of traces). [DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the alternative block A19/A20/A25 between A41 and A26 is bypassed: earlier, after A41 a case executed exactly one of A19, A20 or A25 (each in roughly a third of traces) and then A26, or skipped the block entirely; in the later window A41 is followed directly by A26 and none of the three alternatives is observed beyond a single trace. The signs are direct and mutually consistent — the redirect of A41's outgoing transitions from the three alternatives (~87 in 100) to A26 (~86 in 100), the collapse of the A41–A26 distance from mostly 2 positions to 1, and the simultaneous disappearance of all three activities together with their fixed precedence to A26 and A31 — although many of these items restate the same underlying trace behaviour rather than confirming it independently. With 29 qualifying items against roughly 0.57 expected without change (each difference larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits), chance alone cannot account for the set. Because the three activities are nearly absent in the later window and a predecessor now leads directly to a successor, this is a removal or bypass of a group of activities, not a re-weighting of surviving paths. [DIR005, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL004, REL005, REL009, REL010, REL013, REL014, REL015]

The evidence cannot distinguish a full removal from the block becoming extremely rare — the one later trace containing A19 shows it followed by A12 rather than A26, but that rests on a single trace — nor can it rule out that the later window samples a subpopulation in which the block was never executed. [DIR001, OCC001]

Other differences: A21's routing shares moved away from A31 (about 54 to 38 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) toward A24 (about 14 to 35); the evidence does not show whether this is connected to the bypass of the A19/A20/A25 block or independent of it. [DIR003]
