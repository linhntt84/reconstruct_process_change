### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, cases split between two alternative middle sections: about 48–49 in 100 cases ran a block of three activities, A32, A33 and A38, together, while about 52 in 100 ran A27 instead; the two sections practically never appeared in the same case (co-occurrence about 0–1 in 200). The three block activities ran in both orders in similar proportions, so they had no fixed order among themselves. In the later window, A32, A33 and A38 each appear in only about 1 in 200 cases. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL028, REL029, REL030]

- A27 became the standard middle step: its participation rose from about 52 in 100 cases to about 99 in 100, with roughly one occurrence per case in the later window (mean 0.52 → 1.0). [OCC004]

- Ordering around A27 changed accordingly: in the later window A27 runs after A14 in about 97 in 100 cases and after A26 in about 99 in 100, and before A28 in about 96 in 100; in the earlier window these predecessors co-occurred with A27 in only about 51 in 100 cases. A28 itself is present in every case in both windows, and the A26→A28 distance collapsed from an even split between 2 and 4 steps to almost always 2 steps — consistent with the three-activity block no longer sitting in between. [REL009, REL017, REL021, POS003, LAG003]

- The front activities are stable in presence: A14, A15 and A26 appear in every case in both windows; before, each preceded the A32/A33/A38 block in roughly 47–49 in 100 cases, and in the later window they precede A27. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL018, REL019, REL013]

- A04 receded sharply: present in about 68 in 100 cases before versus about 22 in 100 after, and when it occurs it sits later and more variably (mean position about 0.21 → 0.40; 75th percentile about 0.19 → 0.70). Of A14's observed outgoing transitions, the share leading to A04 fell from about 63 in 100 to about 10, while the share leading directly to A15 rose to about 82 in 100. [OCC002, POS001, DIR004, LAG001]

- A02 and A08 occur somewhat more often (about 5 → 15 and 5 → 20 in 100 cases) and now spread across many different successors instead of a small fixed set. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening qualified 62 control-flow items where fewer than 1 (0.79) would be expected if nothing had changed, so the set of differences cannot be explained as random sampling variation. [screening]

Main hypothesis: in the later window, the alternative middle section formed by A32, A33 and A38 is no longer executed, leaving A27 as the standard — essentially mandatory — step between the front activities (A14, A15, A26) and A28. Earlier, about half of the cases ran the A32/A33/A38 block before A28 and the other half ran A27, with the two mutually exclusive; later, the block is nearly unobserved while A27 runs once in virtually every case, after A14/A26 and before A28. A28's presence is unchanged. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL021]

The support is direct and consistent: all three block activities show a participation collapse of the same size (about 48–49 in 100 down to about 0 in 100), their mutual exclusivity with A27 in the earlier window is explicit, the surrounding activities (A14, A15, A26, A28) are stable in both windows, and the A26→A28 distance shrank from a 2-or-4-step split to a uniform 2 steps, matching the disappearance of the three-activity block. [REL028, REL029, REL030, LAG003, POS003, REL010, REL018]

Limitations: near-zero participation only means the block was not observed in the later sample — it could persist as a rare path; and the later profiles of A32, A33 and A38 (successors, positions, lags) each rest on a single case, so they say nothing reliable about the block's later shape. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows sample different mixtures of two coexisting case types (the later sample drawing almost only the A27-type cases) rather than the block having been dropped. [DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, POS004, POS005, POS006]

Other differences: A04 lost its early, frequent role — from about 68 to about 22 in 100 cases, at a later and more variable position, with A14 now leading to A15 directly in most cases instead of via A04; A02 and A08 appear somewhat more often with scattered successors. These are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC002, POS001, DIR004, OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003]
