### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A26, A27 and A28 each ran in every trace (participation 100%, about one occurrence per trace, in varying order among themselves). In the later window they were observed in only about 1 in 100 traces (1–2 of 200 cases). [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL031, REL032, REL035]
- Three activities that were absent or nearly absent earlier now run in roughly a third of traces: A19 in about 31 in 100 cases (0 before), A20 in about 34 in 100 (0 before) and A21 in about 37 in 100 (under 1 before). They are almost mutually exclusive: a later trace typically contains exactly one of the three. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL013, REL014, REL020]
- The embedding changed accordingly. Earlier, A44 always preceded the A26/A27/A28 group and A29 always followed it (co-occurrence 100% in a fixed order). In the later window, A44 co-occurs with A19, A20 or A21 in 31–37% of traces (A44 first each time) and each of the three precedes A29 in 30–37% of traces, while A26, A27 and A28 no longer co-occur with A44 or A29. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the fixed three-activity segment A26/A27/A28, which always executed between A44 and A29 in the earlier window, was replaced in the later window by a single alternative activity — A19, A20 or A21 — so that a later trace typically runs A44 → (A19 | A20 | A21) → A29. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation of A26/A27/A28 collapsed from 100% to about 1% while A19/A20/A21 rose from 0% to 31–37% with near-mutual exclusivity [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL013, REL014, REL020]; relational evidence shows A44 now precedes and A29 now follows the alternatives, with the old trio no longer co-occurring with either [REL039, REL037, REL034, REL018, REL024, REL029]; A44's successor profile flipped to the alternatives [DIR007]; and the A44-to-A29 step distance halved from 4 to 2, as expected when a three-activity block becomes one activity [LAG010]. With 70 qualifying items against roughly 0.78 expected without change, the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by random sampling, and because the removed activities are nearly absent later and the new ones were absent earlier, this is a replacement of a segment rather than a shift in how often existing paths are taken.

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different case mixes (for example, a previously rare variant dominating the later sample) rather than a modified process, and it cannot show whether A19/A20/A21 are genuinely new activities or stand-ins for the removed ones. Several LAG, DIR and POS items on A26/A27/A28 rest on only 1–2 later traces, and many REL items restate the same replacement, so they are not independent confirmations. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS004, POS005, POS006]
