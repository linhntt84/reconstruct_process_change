### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the six activities A28, A30, A31, A32, A33 and A34 each occurred exactly once in every trace (200 of 200). In the later window, each occurs in only about half of the traces: A28, A30 and A31 in about 47 in 100 (93 of 200), and A32, A33 and A34 in about 56 in 100 (111 of 200). A25 and A26 still occur in every trace in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, POS001, POS002]
- In the earlier window, the six activities formed two blocks that always ran together in a fixed macro-order: A25 first, then A28/A30/A31 in no fixed order, then A32/A33/A34 in no fixed order, then A26 (for example, A28 preceded A32 and A25 preceded A28 in 100% of traces). In the later window the two blocks became almost mutually exclusive: members of {A28, A30, A31} co-occur in about 47 in 100 traces and are jointly absent otherwise, members of {A32, A33, A34} co-occur in about 56 in 100, and cross-block pairs such as A28–A32 co-occur in only about 2 in 100 traces (previously 100 in 100). Ordering within each block stayed mixed in both windows. [REL007, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL018, REL019, REL022, REL025, REL026, REL027]
- Successors changed accordingly. In the earlier window, A25 led only to block-1 members (roughly a third of its outgoing transitions each to A28, A30 and A31); in the later window its outgoing transitions spread over all six activities (about 14–20% each). A28, A30 and A31, which never led directly to A26 before, now send about 34%, 36% and 26% of their observed outgoing transitions to A26, while their transitions into block-2 activities dropped from about 8–14% to about 1%. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- Step distances compressed: the gap from A25 to A32 was 4–5 positions in essentially all before-window observations and is 1–3 positions in about 97 in 100 of the later-window observations; A31 to A26 shows the same shift (4–5 before, 1–3 after). Relative positions moved only slightly (A25 from about 0.62 to 0.67, A26 from about 0.83 to 0.81, block members converging near 0.74), consistent with fewer activities lying between A25 and A26. [LAG001, LAG011, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]
- A few step-distance items for cross-block pairs (for example A28–A34, A30–A33) also show shifts, but in the later window they rest on only about 4 traces each and are weak on their own. [LAG007, LAG009, LAG010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A25 and A26 was restructured from two mandatory blocks into a choice between them: in the earlier window every trace executed A28, A30 and A31 (in any order) followed by A32, A33 and A34 (in any order); in the later window a trace typically executes exactly one of the two triples — {A28, A30, A31} in about 47 in 100 traces and {A32, A33, A34} in about 56 in 100, with both together in only about 2 in 100 — so each block is skipped in roughly half of the traces, while A25 and A26 remain universal endpoints and the unordered execution within each block is unchanged. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL013, REL015, REL019, REL022, REL025, REL026, REL027, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

The hypothesis is direct and consistent across indicator families: participation shows all six activities dropping from universal to roughly half; co-occurrence shows within-block togetherness persisting while cross-block togetherness collapses from 100% to about 2%; successor profiles show the surviving block connecting straight to A26 and A25 feeding both blocks; and step distances compress accordingly. The screening qualified 58 control-flow items where fewer than one (about 0.63) would be expected by chance in an unchanged process, so the ensemble cannot be explained by random re-splits of the two windows. [LAG001, LAG011, DIR001, DIR004, REL015, REL019]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show what determines which block a trace takes, and it cannot distinguish a single changed routing from a later window that mixes two variants (a case-mix difference rather than one routing rule); the roughly 2 in 100 later traces containing both blocks are not explained by the main hypothesis, and the cross-block step-distance items rest on about 4 traces each. [REL015, LAG007, LAG010]
