### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening qualified 92 control-flow items where fewer than one (about 0.6) would be expected by chance in an unchanged process; a count many times that expectation cannot be produced by random re-splits, so chance cannot account for the flagged differences as a whole. [screening]

Main hypothesis: the evidence supports a structural change in which four previously unobserved activities — A07, A15, A16 and A17 — became part of the executed process in the later window, with the surrounding routing rewired: A08 now leads to A07, A10 leads to A15 and A16, A18 leads to A17 and A14, A12 leads to A05, and A05 leads to A02, while the new activities in turn lead on to A18, A10, A03/A02 and A09; pairs that were alternatives before (only one side ever present in a trace) now typically occur together. This is shown directly: participation rose from zero to 33–70 in 100 traces, the successor profiles of five existing activities changed completely, and co-occurrence between the new and existing activities rose from 0 to as much as about 70 in 100. [OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR009, DIR002, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, REL007, REL029, REL005, REL001, REL014]

Limitations: zero participation only means the activities were not observed earlier, so the evidence cannot distinguish newly added activities from ones that existed but went unexecuted in the earlier sample; and the many agreeing relation and gap items largely re-describe the same rewiring rather than confirming it independently. The competing explanation that the earlier 200-trace window simply sampled a case mix without these paths cannot be fully ruled out. [OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

Other differences: A05 now repeats about twice as often per trace and appears earlier, A18 repeats less often and appears later, A13 repeats about twice as often, and several event gaps became shorter and more concentrated; these are reported separately and not folded into the main hypothesis.
