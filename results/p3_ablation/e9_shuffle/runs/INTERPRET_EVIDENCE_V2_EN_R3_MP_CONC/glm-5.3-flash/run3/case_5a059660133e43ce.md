### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the final stage of the process behaves differently. In the earlier window, A06 terminated traces (about 14 in 100 cases, always the last event, no observed successor), A02 led directly to A15 one step later in about half of the observed pairs, and A01 was not observed. In the later window, A01 appears as a final activity — executed once, at the very end, in about 22 in 100 cases and reached from A02 — while A06 now runs around the middle of the trace and always leads to A15, so A15 is reached from A02 mostly two steps later rather than immediately. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, LAG002]

The placement change is shown directly by positional and relational items rather than frequency shifts alone, and the key profiles rest on moderate support (27–44 traces per window); all 14 qualified control-flow items are roughly sixty times the 0.23 expected if nothing had changed, and each difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits. The items are not independent confirmations, as several describe overlapping aspects of the same traces around A02's ending. [POS001, POS002, DIR002, LAG002]
