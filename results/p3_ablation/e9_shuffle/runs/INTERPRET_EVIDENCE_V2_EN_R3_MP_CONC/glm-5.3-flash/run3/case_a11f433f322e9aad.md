### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, about half of the executions bypass A27: where the earlier window ran A26 → A27 → A28 in every trace, these traces run A26, then the three activities A32, A33 and A38 in no fixed order among themselves, then A28. A27 fell from appearing in every trace to about 51 in 100, while A32, A33 and A38 rose from never appearing to about 49 in 100 traces each, always as a trio and essentially never with A27. The placement is shown directly: A26 precedes each of the three, each precedes A28, the direct flows enter and leave the block, and the A26-to-A28 gap widens from two to four steps in about half the traces. The 27 qualified items are many times the roughly 0.57 expected for an unchanged process, so the differences as a whole are not what a stable process produces; note that the many relational items largely re-describe this one underlying difference rather than confirming it independently. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG001]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A32, A33 and A38 were not executed then, so it is impossible to distinguish activities newly added to the process from an existing path that was simply not taken in the earlier sample; the windows are finite samples, although roughly 98 traces showing the new behaviour make a rare-variant explanation implausible. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Other differences: A27's outgoing transitions now include small flows to A09 (about 2 in 100) and to A34 and A25 (about 1 in 100 each), replacing a rare flow to A04; this minor redistribution, observed on about 103 outgoing transitions, is not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR002]
