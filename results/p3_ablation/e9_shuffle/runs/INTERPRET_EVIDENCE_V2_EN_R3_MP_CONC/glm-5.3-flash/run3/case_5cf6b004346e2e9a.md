### Observed differences

- The activities that follow A10 and A11 changed completely. In the earlier window, A10's observed outgoing transitions went to A07 (about 49 in 100) and A06 (about 51 in 100), and A11's went to A06 (about 58) and A07 (about 42); in the later window, every observed outgoing transition of A10 (55 transitions) and of A11 (39) goes to A08. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the control flow around A08, A10 and A11: in the earlier window the flow typically ran A08 → A10 or A11 → A06 or A07, whereas in the later window A10 and A11 lead to A08 and A08 leads on to A06 or A07 — A08 now typically executes after A10 and A11 and directly before A06 and A07, where previously it executed before A10/A11, which then led to A06/A07. This rests on direct successor changes on substantial supports (A10 and A11 now lead only to A08 after leading only to A06/A07; A08 gained A06 and A07 as successors), on the flipped immediate precedence between A08 and A10/A11, and on lag shifts that match an insertion of A08 between the two groups (A10/A11→A06/A07 moving from lag 1 to lag 2, the adjacent pairs becoming immediate). Screening found 20 qualifying control-flow items where roughly 0.24 would be expected if nothing had changed, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole; each item's difference also exceeds about 995 in 1,000 random re-splits, though the lag items largely mirror the same reordering rather than confirming it independently. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR001, LAG010, LAG011, LAG014, LAG016, LAG012, LAG013, LAG015, LAG007, LAG008]

Limitations: the evidence shows only pairwise order and successors, so it cannot establish the complete sequence nor distinguish "A08 moved later" from "A10/A11 moved earlier" as an absolute placement; A08 still precedes A10/A11 in about 46 in 100 of its later-window outgoing transitions, and its roughly doubled self-repetition could contribute to some lag shifts, so a repositioning cannot be fully separated from a change in how often A08 recurs. [DIR001, LAG009]

Other differences: distances within the downstream pair A06/A07 changed (A06→A06 from a lag-3 peak to a spread including lag 2; A06→A07 from a lag-3 peak to a flatter spread), and several low-support pairs around A07 (A07→A06, A07→A07, A07→A08, A11→A10) also shifted; these may be knock-on effects of the reordering but rest on few observations. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG017]
