### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative branch between A21 and A26 — either A22 alone or the trio A31/A32/A37 — became a single mandatory sequence: in the later window A31, A32 and A37 always execute, directly after A20 and before A23, while A22 always executes after A23 (entered via A21) and before A26; before, each trace executed one branch or the other (about 54 vs 46 in 100) with A23 immediately after A20. This is shown directly by relational evidence (mutual exclusivity rising from 0 to co-occurrence in 199 of 200 traces; the A23–trio and A21–trio orders reversing), by successor changes (A20 now into the trio, the trio now into A23, A21 now only into A22) and by the A20→A23 distance growing from 1 to 4 steps. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL006, REL007, REL008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The volume of qualifying evidence (41 items against about 0.49 expected for an unchanged process, all control-flow) and the size of the shifts (0 to 199 of 200 in co-occurrence; 46–54% to near-100% participation in 200-trace windows) make sampling noise and rare-variant explanations implausible. [REL013, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The evidence compares only aggregate window behaviour, so it cannot rule out that the two windows sample different process versions rather than a single process modified at the boundary; it also covers only qualifying activities, so other activities in the reordered segment are not visible. [REL013, DIR001]

Other differences: small positional shifts for A20 (mean 0.36 → 0.34), A21 (0.54 → 0.60) and A26 (0.63 → 0.66), and a slightly tighter spread of positions for most activities, consistent with three activities now running between A20 and A23; no unrelated behavioural differences appear in the evidence.
