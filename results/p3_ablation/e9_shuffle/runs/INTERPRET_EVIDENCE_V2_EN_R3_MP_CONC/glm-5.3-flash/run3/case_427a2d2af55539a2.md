### Observed differences

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the step between A39 and A24 was restructured: in the earlier window each case executed at most one of three mutually exclusive activities (A14, A15, A16 — exactly one in about 70 in 100 cases, none in about 30 in 100), while in the later window every case executes all three of the new activities A21, A22 and A23, once each, between A39 and A24, in no fixed order among themselves. This is a replacement of activities — the old ones disappeared and new ones appeared — not a shift in how often existing paths are taken. [OCC001, OCC004, REL002, REL019, REL021, REL022, DIR007]

The change is direct and consistent across indicators: participation moved from 0 to 200 of 200 traces (and 200 to 0) for all six activities; every relational item involving the old or new trio agrees on the embedding between A39 and A24; the successor profiles of A39 and of the new activities match that embedding; and the A39-to-A24 distance grew from 2 to 4 steps, as expected when one middle activity becomes three. The screening comparison also rules out chance as an overall explanation: 48 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.5 would be expected for an unchanged process. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, LAG001, DIR007, screening]

The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows capture two coexisting process variants whose mix shifted from one to the other, or a change in how activities are recorded, rather than a modification of a single process; zero participation only means an activity was not observed in that window. [OCC001, OCC004]

Other differences: none beyond the above; the slightly later position of A24 and the longer A39-to-A24 distance are consistent with the same change. [POS007, LAG001]
