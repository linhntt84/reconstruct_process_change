### Observed differences

- A01 changed from an early to a late activity. In the earlier window it sat about a quarter of the way into the trace on average (mean relative position about 0.26, median about 0.17); in the later window it sat about two thirds in (mean about 0.65, median about 0.67). It occurred in all 200 traces of both windows. [POS001]
- The order between A01 and A02, A04, A05 and A07 reversed. Earlier, A01 ran before A07 in about 68 of 100 traces, before A05 in about 81, before A02 in about 55, and before A04 in about 49 (no fixed order); in the later window A01 runs after A07 in all 100 traces, after A04 in all 100, after A02 in about 81, and after A05 in about 63. Each pair still co-occurs in every trace, with no interleaving or repetition in either window. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]
- A01's outgoing flow changed. Earlier its outgoing transitions went to A05 (about 31 in 100), A02 (29), A04 (18), A06 (9), A03 (7) and A07 (7); in the later window they go mainly to A03 (about 47 in 100) and A05 (about 27), with transitions to A04 and A07 no longer observed. [DIR001]
- The flow into A01 changed accordingly. A07's outgoing transitions now lead to A01 in about 68 of 100 cases (about 18 in 100 before) and no longer to A03 (about 28 before); A04's transitions to A01 are no longer observed (about 16 before) while those to A07 rise from about 54 to about 70 in 100; A02's transitions to A01 fall from about 26 to about 11 in 100. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 now executes in a late stage of the process whereas it previously executed early: in the later window A01 consistently follows A07 and A04 and mostly follows A02 and A05, its own flow runs mainly on to A03 and A05, and A07's flow now runs mainly to A01. Before, A01 preceded A02, A04, A05 and A07 in most traces and fed all of them directly. [POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR001, DIR004]

The change is shown directly by positional and relational evidence rather than inferred from frequency shifts alone; all items point the same way (A01 later, predecessor edges into it changed, its fan-out re-centred on A03 and A05); the reversals are large (for example A01 before A07 fell from about 68 to 0 in 100 traces); every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits; and 21 qualifying items against roughly 0.18 expected by chance across 37 tested units is far beyond what an unchanged process produces. [POS001, REL004, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG003]

Limitations: positions and distances are event positions, not clock time, so the hypothesis concerns order within traces; DIR profiles cover observed outgoing transitions, so edges that disappeared (A01→A04, A01→A07, A07→A03, A04→A01) are unobserved rather than proven absent; and many items describe the same underlying relocation, so they are consistent rather than independent confirmations. An important competing reading — that A01 is now executed as an additional late occurrence rather than relocated — is argued against by the tight late position distribution and the absence of interleaving or repetition, but occurrences per trace are not reported, so it cannot be fully excluded. [POS001, REL004, DIR001]

Other differences: the slightly earlier positions of A02, A05 and A07 and the lengthened A07→A05 and A05→A03 distances are consistent with A01's relocation but are not part of the main hypothesis as stated. [POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG006, LAG009]
