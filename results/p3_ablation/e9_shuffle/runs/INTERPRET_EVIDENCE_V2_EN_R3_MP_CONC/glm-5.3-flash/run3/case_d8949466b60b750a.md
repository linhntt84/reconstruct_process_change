### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The 35 qualifying control-flow items far exceed the roughly 0.6 expected if nothing had changed, so the set of differences is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [screening]

Main hypothesis: in the later window, the alternative branch formed by A32, A33 and A38 between A26 and A28 was no longer executed, leaving A26 → A27 → A28 as the uniform path, with A27 changing from an optional step present in about half of the traces to an essentially mandatory one present in nearly all (occasionally repeated). This is shown directly and consistently by participation, co-occurrence, successor and distance evidence, with the main items backed by about 200 traces per window.
