### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the activity group A14 → A20 → A15 became mandatory between A36 and A21: in the earlier window roughly half of the traces executed this group after A36 before reaching A21 (about 47 in 100) while the other half skipped it via a direct A36 → A21 step; in the later window the group runs in virtually every trace (about 199 of 200) and the skip step survives in only about 5 in 1000 of A36's outgoing transitions. The change is shown directly, not only through participation counts: A36's handover redirected from A21 to A14, and the A36-to-A21 distance moved from one to four positions. The picture is consistent across the occurrence, relation, direction and lag indicators, and with 22 qualifying items against roughly 0.41 expected for an unchanged process, chance is implausible for the pattern as a whole. Limitations: most items describe the same underlying traces rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply contain different mixes of case types rather than a removed routing option.
