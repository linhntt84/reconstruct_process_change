### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the step in which A03 followed A02 is no longer executed: A03, which previously ran in every trace as the step after A02, is absent from all 200 later traces, and A02 now occurs alone in every trace with no observed successor. Since A03 is entirely unobserved in the later window, this points to an activity being removed or the A02→A03 step becoming never taken — not to a shift in how often existing paths are chosen. [OCC001, REL001, DIR001]

The five qualifying control-flow items are far above the roughly 0.04 expected by chance for the 9 tested units, so the set of differences is unlikely for an unchanged process. The participation, relational and directional indicators all describe the same underlying change and agree with one another, though they are not independent confirmations; A02's move to the final observed position fits the same picture. [DIR001, OCC001, POS001, POS002, REL001]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A03 was not observed in the later sample, so the evidence cannot exclude that A03 still occurs outside these traces (for example, having become rare). It also cannot show whether A02 now leads to other activities or ends the trace, because the absence of observed outgoing transitions is not proof that the connection was removed. [OCC001, DIR001]
