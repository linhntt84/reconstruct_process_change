### Observed differences

- Activity A03 did not occur at all in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it runs in about 103 of 200 traces (about 52 in 100), essentially once per trace and never repeated. [OCC001]
- In the later window, A03 sits in the early-to-middle part of the trace: median relative position about 0.38, with the middle half of its positions between about 0.25 and 0.42. [POS001]
- In the earlier window, A05, A08, A11 and A12 each appeared in about 51 in 100 traces and never together with A03; in the later window, each of them co-occurs with A03 in about 52 in 100 traces — A03 appears in practically every trace that contains any of them. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A03 became an executed step in the later window, running in about half of the cases (about 52 in 100, versus none before) and being inserted between the early activities A05, A08 and A12 and the later A11: A08 and A12 now frequently flow into A03, A03 flows on to A11, A12 and A08, A03 precedes A11 in every shared trace, and the step gaps from A05/A08/A12 to A11 typically grow by one. [OCC001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR001, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG005]

The support is direct and consistent: participation, relational, directional, positional and lag indicators all show the same picture, and 17 qualifying items against roughly 0.17 expected by chance among the 35 tested control-flow units is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces, so the pattern as a whole is not a sampling artefact. A mere shift in how often existing paths are taken is ruled out, since A03 was entirely absent from the earlier window rather than gaining share over an existing alternative. [OCC001, REL001, DIR001, POS001]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that A03 was not observed then, so a newly added step cannot be distinguished from a previously unexecuted one; the items also largely describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, and why A03 runs in only about half of the cases (for example as a new optional branch) cannot be determined from this evidence. [OCC001]

Other differences: A05 now occurs at the very start of the trace more often (median relative position from about 0.18 to 0), a small shift the insertion hypothesis does not by itself explain, while A11's position is essentially unchanged. [POS002, POS003]
