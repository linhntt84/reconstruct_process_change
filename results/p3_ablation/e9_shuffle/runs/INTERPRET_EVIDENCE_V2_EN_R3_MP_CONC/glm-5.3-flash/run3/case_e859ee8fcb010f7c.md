### Observed differences

- The A01/A02 block disappeared: in the earlier window A01 and A02 each ran in about 50 in 100 traces and always together (A02 before A01 in about 27 in 100 traces, interleaved in about 23 in 100), with A02 leading to A14, A14 to A01, and A01 on to A07, A10, A08 or A12; in the later window neither A01 nor A02 appears in any of the 200 traces [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, REL012, REL023, DIR001, DIR002, DIR011].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block A02 → A14 → A01, which ran in about half of the earlier traces, is no longer part of the observed process and its entry and exit points were rewired: A01 and A02 fell from about 50 to 0 in 100 traces, A06 now leads directly to A14 instead of into A02 or the repeating A06–A13 exchange, A13 now leads to A14 instead of back to A06, and A14 continues into A08 and A07 instead of into A01. This rests on direct observations — participation counts, changed successor profiles, the collapse of co-occurrence with every A01/A02 partner, and shortened step distances along the new route — while A06, A13 and A14 themselves remained well observed in both windows; with 107 qualifying control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected without change, chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, DIR004, DIR010, DIR011, REL001, OCC003, OCC007, POS008, LAG012, LAG032, LAG034, screening].

The evidence cannot rule out that A01 and A02 were merely no longer recorded, or were renamed, rather than removed, since zero participation only means they were not observed; the occurrence, position and relation items for A01/A02 also describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations [OCC001, POS001, REL001].

- A08 and A10 became a frequent, earlier, fixed-order pair (about 50 in 100 traces, always A08 before A10; A08's successor changed from A05 to A10) [OCC004, OCC006, POS004, REL039, DIR006].
