Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the order of the closing segment changed: A01, previously the final activity, now executes directly after A08 and before A06 and A07, while A06 and A07 each moved one event position later, making A07 the final activity. In the earlier window the tail ran A08 → A06 → A07 → A01 in all 200 of 200 traces; in the later window it runs A08 → A01 → A06 → A07 in all 200 of 200 traces. [REL001, REL002, DIR003, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The support is direct and consistent: both order relations reversed entirely rather than partially, the successor of A08 changed from A06 to A01, A01 gained a successor (A06) where none was observed before, A07 lost its successor (A01), and every position and lag shift matches exactly the one-rank displacement this swap implies. Participation is unchanged, so this is a reordering of the same activities, not activities appearing, disappearing or repeating. The volume of evidence also exceeds what chance produces: 11 items qualified where about 0.05 would be expected without a change, and each difference exceeded about 199 of 200 random re-splits; the items are facets of one reordering rather than independent confirmations.
