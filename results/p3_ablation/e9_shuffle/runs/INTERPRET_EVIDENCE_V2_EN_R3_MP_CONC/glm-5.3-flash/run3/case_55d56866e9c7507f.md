### Observed differences

- A11 was executed far more often in the later window: it appears in about 57 in 100 traces, up from about 29 in 100 before (113 vs 58 of 200 traces), and never repeats within a trace in either window [OCC001, POS003].
- A09 also became more frequent, from about 30 in 100 traces before to about 44 in 100 in the later window (87 vs 59) [POS002].
- A09 and A11 never occur in the same trace in either window, so they behave as strict alternatives. Before, both were absent in about 42 in 100 traces; in the later window one of the two appears in every one of the 200 traces [REL003].
- The route into these activities changed. Before, A06's outgoing flow went mainly to A03 (about 28%), A02 (about 18%) and A08 (about 15%), with only about 4% to A11 and 7% to A09; in the later window A11 (about 31%) and A09 (about 23%) dominate while the older targets shrank. A06 itself is observed in nearly every trace in both windows (186 and 200 of 200) [DIR002].
- Succession became immediate: the A06→A11 gap is one to two steps in about 85 in 100 later cases versus about 33 in 100 before (previously three to four steps dominated), and A06→A09 gaps of one to two steps rose from about 40% to about 79% [LAG003, LAG002].
- A11's earlier coupling to A08 dissolved. Before, A11 never occurred without A08 and always after it (A08 before A11 in 29% of traces, the reverse never); in the later window A11 occurs without A08 in about 27 in 100 traces and in both orders (about 16% vs 15%), and the A08→A11 gap lengthened (one-step share 66% down to 39%) [REL002, LAG004].
- A08's outgoing flow turned away from A09 and A11 (about 31% to 11% and 32% to 17%) toward A02 (about 15% to 33%) and A06 (about 9% to 23%) [DIR003].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that flow into the alternative pair A09/A11 was rerouted and intensified. In the later window, A06 leads directly and immediately to A11 and A09 — their shares of A06's outgoing transitions rose from about 4% to about 31% and from about 7% to about 23%, typically within one to two steps instead of three to four — while A11's participation roughly doubled (about 29% to about 57% of traces) and A09's rose to about 44%; the two remain mutually exclusive, but one of them now appears in every traced case where before both were skipped in about 4 in 10. As part of the same shift, A11's earlier strict dependence on A08 disappeared: A11 now occurs without A08 in about a quarter of traces and in either order [OCC001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL003, DIR002, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004].

The hypothesis rests on direct relational and directional evidence, not only on marginal frequencies: A06 precedes A11 in every later co-occurrence (about 57% of traces, up from 29%), A11 appears alone and in mixed order relative to A08, and A06's successor mix shifted while A06 was observed in nearly all traces of both windows. All sixteen qualifying items come from the control-flow family, where only about 0.16 items would be expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeds those seen in about 99.5 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data [REL001, REL002, DIR002, DIR003, LAG002, LAG003].

Limitations remain: the retargeting of A05's, A09's and A11's outgoing transitions rests on only 7–10 before-window transitions each and may partly be sampling variation; whether A08's reduced link to A11 was removed or is now merely used rarely cannot be distinguished; and several items describe the same traces (A11's rise shows in occurrence, position, relation, direction and lag indicators), so they are not independent confirmations [DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, REL002].

Other differences: A08's own position moved slightly later and became more variable (mean position about 0.66 to 0.75; 75th percentile 0.8 to 1.0) while its trace presence stayed roughly stable (about 59% to 54%); the A06→A08 gap lengthened (one to two steps in about 80% of before-cases versus about 38% later); and the outgoing transitions of A05, A09 and A11 spread from one or two dominant targets to a broader set of successors [POS001, LAG001, DIR001, DIR004, DIR005].
