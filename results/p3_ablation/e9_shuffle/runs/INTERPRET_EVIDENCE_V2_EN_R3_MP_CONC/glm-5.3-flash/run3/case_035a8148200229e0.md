### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the branch preceding A03 in roughly half of the cases was replaced: in the earlier window about half of the traces contained A04, always followed by A03, while in the later window A04 no longer occurs and A01 instead appears in about half of the traces, always followed by A03, with A01 and A04 never occurring together. The activities involved are A01, A04 and A03. The hypothesis rests on directly agreeing participation, succession and relation evidence (11 qualifying items against roughly 0.14 expected by chance for the 28 tested units, with about 100 traces behind each side of the key differences). Limitations: with anonymous activity codes the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine substitution of A04 by A01 from a relabelling of the same step, nor can it show whether A01 differs from A04 in any behaviour beyond both always preceding A03; a competing explanation that the two windows sample different process variants rather than a change over time cannot be ruled out from this evidence alone. Other differences: the routing out of A07 and A13 shifted — A07 now reaches A03 directly in about 29 of 100 transitions instead of almost always going to A13, and A13 now often leads to A07 (about 32 of 100) while its share towards A03 fell from about 77 to 47 in 100; both activities also occur in slightly more traces (48 vs 59 of 200). Whether this redistribution is connected to the A04/A01 replacement is not shown by the evidence. [DIR003, DIR004]
