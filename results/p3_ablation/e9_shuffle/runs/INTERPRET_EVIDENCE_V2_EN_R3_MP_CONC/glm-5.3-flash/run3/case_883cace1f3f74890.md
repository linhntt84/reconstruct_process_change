### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the middle of the process was a fixed chain: A27 led to A28 in about 99 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions, A28 led to A29 in about 99 in 100, and A29 followed A27 two positions later in about 99 in 100 traces. [DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003, LAG002]
- In the later window, three activities that appear in none of the 200 earlier traces now occur: A20 in about 41 in 100 traces, A19 and A25 in about 30 in 100 each; they practically never occur together, so almost every later trace contains exactly one of them. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL002, REL006]
- Routing around A27 and A28 changed accordingly: A27 now leads to A28 in only about 44 in 100 cases and otherwise to A20 (about 22 in 100), A25 (about 17) or A19 (about 15); A28 now leads to A29 in about 55 in 100 cases and otherwise to A20, A19 or A25; the new activities in turn lead mostly to A28 (about 53–56 in 100) or to A29 (about 42–46 in 100). [DIR004, DIR005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- Distances lengthened consistently: the A27→A28 gap is now two steps in about 55 in 100 traces (previously one step in about 99), the A28→A29 gap two steps in about 44 in 100 (previously one step in about 99), and the A27→A29 distance grew from two to three steps in about 97 in 100 traces. [LAG001, LAG003, LAG002]
- The new activities run mid-trace (mean relative position about 0.51), after A27 and before A29 in nearly all traces where they co-occur; A27's median position shifted slightly earlier (about 0.48 to 0.46) and A29's slightly later (about 0.56 to 0.57), consistent with something inserted between them. [REL003, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new step was inserted into the previously fixed A27 → A28 → A29 sequence: in the later window almost every trace executes exactly one of the three mutually exclusive activities A19, A20 or A25 between A27 and A29, either directly after A27 (before A28) or directly after A28 (before A29), whereas none of them was observed in the earlier window. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL007, REL009, LAG002]

The screening produced 29 qualifying control-flow items, roughly fifty times the about 0.54 expected if nothing had changed, and the occurrence, direction, relation, gap and position indicators all describe the same insertion, so a sampling artefact is implausible. [DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003, POS002]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A19, A20 and A25 existed earlier as an extremely rare path (fewer than about 1 in 200 traces) that later became frequent, since zero earlier participation strictly means only non-observation; the rule that chooses among the three alternatives is also not identifiable from this evidence. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Other differences: A10's trace participation rose from about 5 in 100 traces earlier to about 13 in 100 later. [OCC001]
