### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of three activities A36, A37 and A42 was relocated and made mandatory: before, this trio ran in about 46 in 100 traces as an alternative to A27 in the later part of the trace (between A26 and A31), while in the later window it runs in essentially every trace, placed between A25 and A28 and therefore before A26 and A27; A27, previously present in about 54 in 100 traces, also became mandatory. The formerly alternative branches — A27 alone versus the trio — now both execute in every trace. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL006, REL014, DIR001, LAG001, OCC001, OCC003]

The placement change is shown directly by relational and successor evidence, not only by position shifts: the trio's order relative to A26 and A28 flipped, A25 now feeds into the trio instead of A28, the trio now feeds into A28 instead of A31, and the A25–A28 distance grew from one to four positions, matching three activities inserted between them. Relocation rather than duplication is supported by the occurrence counts (each of the four activities occurs about once per trace afterwards). With 45 qualifying items against roughly 0.7 expected without a change in the control-flow family, chance re-splits of an unchanged process cannot explain the flagged set as a whole. [DIR001, DIR003, LAG001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Limitations: the trio's internal sequence remains unfixed in both windows, so how the three activities are ordered among themselves cannot be characterised; activities not covered by the evidence (for example between A28 and A26) are unobserved, so the full extent of the restructuring is unknown; and the evidence cannot show whether the earlier two branch shapes reflected a routing choice or two distinct case types. A few lag items rest on single observations and add little weight. [LAG002, LAG005, LAG006, REL020, REL021, REL022]

Other differences: the remaining flagged items echo the same restructuring rather than separate changes — small position shifts of A25, A28, A26, A27 and A31 consistent with three activities being inserted after A25, and the distance from the trio to A31 now usually exceeding five steps (the ordered pair within five steps appears in only 1 of 200 later traces, against 92 before). [POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005, LAG005, LAG006]
