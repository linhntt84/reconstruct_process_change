### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering constraints among A20, A22, A23, A24, A25 and A26 changed: a block previously executed in a variable order (several pairs in both orders in similar shares, others usually reversed) is now executed as one fixed sequence, A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32, with the exit to A32 moved from after A23 to after A25. The change is shown directly by the relation items (order fixed or reversed while co-occurrence stays at 100 in 100, so participation is unchanged), by the successor items (each activity's outgoing transitions collapse onto its chain successor), and by the lag items (distances match one chain exactly, and reverse-order pairs vanish). Chance is also an unlikely explanation for the evidence set as a whole: 58 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.76 would be expected without a change. Limitations: the relation, successor and lag items describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations; a few lag items rest on about one after-window observation, although the forward-direction items each rest on about 200 traces per window; and the evidence cannot distinguish whether the earlier mixed order was one flexible block or a mixture of several fixed variants that was replaced by a single variant.
