### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A09 became a heavily repeated activity within a short repeating cycle that A13 and A15 feed: A09 runs about 4.3 times per trace (about 1.9 before) with repeats in about 60 in 100 traces (about 39 before) while participation rose only from about 62 to 68 in 100, A13 and A15 now lead to A09 in about 71 and 95 in 100 of their outgoing transitions (about 35 and 41 before), A09 and A13 interleave or repeat together in about 60 in 100 traces (about 39 before), and consecutive A09 executions sit mostly two steps apart instead of three to five. [OCC002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, REL003, LAG007]

Supporting signs: the screen qualified 29 control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected without a change, so the set of differences is not what an unchanged process typically produces; the A09 items agree across occurrence, direction, relation and step-distance indicators, and each difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled traces. Limitations: the evidence cannot show which connection closes the cycle or whether the extra A09 executions displaced other work, and direction shares describe outgoing transitions rather than cases; an important competing explanation that remains is a shift in the mix of case variants between the windows (for example more repetition-heavy cases later) rather than a modification of the process itself. [OCC002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, REL003, LAG007]
