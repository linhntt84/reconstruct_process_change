### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A07 was effectively dropped from the process in the later window. Before, A07 ran in about 23 in 100 traces, always together with A10 and usually downstream of it, passing mainly on to A03; in the later window it was entirely absent from the sample, and A10 no longer hands over to it. [OCC001, POS001, REL001, DIR001]

A structural counterpart points the same way: A10's transition to A07 vanished while its direct transitions to A03 roughly tripled in share and the A10→A03 step distance shifted toward 1, consistent with flow that previously ran A10 → A07 → A03 now bypassing A07. Six items qualified in screening where roughly 0.14 would be expected by chance for an unchanged process, and each difference is larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits; note, however, that the participation, positional and relational items describe the same underlying traces and are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, REL001, DIR002, LAG001]

Limitations: zero participation only shows A07 was not observed in the later sample, so an important competing explanation — that A07 still occurs but very rarely (below roughly 1–2 in 100 traces) rather than being fully removed — cannot be ruled out. The rerouting is also only partly visible: direct A10→A03 transitions remain under a tenth of A10's outgoing transitions, so the destination of all former A07 throughput cannot be pinned down. [OCC001, DIR002]

Other differences: A10's remaining outflow also shifted beyond the A07 change — transitions to A04 and A08 lost share while those to A02 and A13 gained, A09 was newly observed and A06 was no longer observed — a redistribution consistent with, but not fully attributable to, the loss of A07. [DIR002]
