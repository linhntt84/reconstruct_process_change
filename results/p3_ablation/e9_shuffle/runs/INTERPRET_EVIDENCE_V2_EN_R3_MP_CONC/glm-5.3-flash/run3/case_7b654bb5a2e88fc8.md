### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every case ran A40 → exactly one of three alternatives → A21 → A22 → A23 → A27: A40 was followed by A14 in about 38 in 100 cases, by A15 in about 31 and by A20 in about 32, each alternative always led on to A21, and A22→A23 and A23→A27 were direct in all cases. [DIR006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003, LAG004]
- In the later window, A40 leads directly to A21 in all cases (one step apart instead of two), and outgoing transitions from A40 to A14, A15 or A20 are no longer observed. [DIR006, LAG004]
- The three alternatives now run much later in the case: their mean event position rose from about 0.31–0.33 to about 0.59–0.60, and their order relative to A21 and A22 reversed — earlier each alternative always preceded A21 and A22, now it always follows them. [POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007]
- The alternatives' successors changed accordingly: A14 now leads to A23 in about 52% of its occurrences and to A27 in about 48% (A15 about 54%/46%, A20 about 39%/61%), whereas before each led only to A21. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- A22 and A23 now sometimes hand over to an alternative: A22 → A23 directly in about 52 in 100 cases and to one of A14/A15/A20 in the remaining 48; A23 → A27 directly in about 48 in 100 cases and to an alternative in the remaining 52. [DIR004, DIR005]
- Consequently the direct successions A22→A23 and A23→A27 each fell from 100% to about half, and the distance from A22 to A27 grew from two steps in all cases to three steps in all cases. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Participation is structurally unchanged — still exactly one alternative per case (76+61+63 = 200 of 200 cases earlier; 61+65+74 = 200 later) — though the choice shifted somewhat (A14 from about 38 to about 31 in 100 cases, A20 from about 32 to about 37), and A21, A22 and A23, still present in every case, sit slightly earlier (mean position down about 0.02–0.03). [POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG002, POS004, POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative step of the process — exactly one of A14, A15 or A20 per case — is now executed later in each case: in the earlier window it sat between A40 and A21 (A40 → alternative → A21 → A22 → A23 → A27), while in the later window A40 connects directly to A21 and the alternative is inserted after A22, either between A22 and A23 (about 48 in 100 cases) or between A23 and A27 (about 52 in 100 cases). [DIR006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL006]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the successor changes appear on both sides of the relocated step (A40 now has only A21 as successor; A14/A15/A20 now lead to A23 or A27 instead of A21; A22 and A23 now sometimes lead to an alternative), the order relations with A21 and A22 reverse completely, the alternatives shift in position by about 0.27 while A21–A23 shift by only 0.02–0.03, and 24 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.4 expected for an unchanged process. [DIR006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007]

Limitations: the evidence covers only these activities and activity pairs, so changes elsewhere in the process would not be visible, and it cannot show whether the roughly even split of the insertion point reflects one flexible rule or two distinct case groups. [DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the observed edges arise from independent rewirings — A40 bypassing the alternatives and the alternatives being re-attached after A22 — rather than from one coordinated change; both produce the same observed behaviour. [DIR006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

Other differences: the choice among the three alternatives shifted somewhat (A14 less often, A20 more often, by about 5–7 in 100 cases), and A21–A23 each sit slightly earlier; these may be knock-on effects of the re-attachment or sampling variation between the two 200-case windows. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
