### Observed differences

- Before, six activities — A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 — each ran in essentially every case (participation 100 in 100 traces, about 1.0 occurrences per trace). In the later window each appears in only about 47–57 in 100 traces, with mean occurrences per trace down to about 0.47–0.56 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006].
- These six form two blocks: an early block (A33, A35, A36) and a late block (A37, A38, A39). Before, every early–late pair co-occurred in 100 in 100 traces, with the early block first in 97–100 in 100 of them. In the later window, cross-block co-occurrence collapsed to about 2–3 in 100 traces: the early block runs alone in about 44 in 100 traces and the late block alone in about 53 in 100 [REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024].
- Within a block, the activities still travel together when the block runs: early-block pairs co-occur in about 47 in 100 later traces (neither present in about 53), late-block pairs in about 56 in 100 (neither in about 43); their mutual order stays mixed, with no fixed sequence, in both windows [REL013, REL014, REL018, REL025, REL026, REL027].
- A30 and A31 still occur in every trace in both windows, and whichever block runs, A30 precedes it and A31 follows it (A30 before the block in all co-occurrences; A31 after it in all co-occurrences) [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, POS001, POS002].
- Routing changed accordingly: in the later window about 24–34 in 100 outgoing transitions of A33, A35 and A36 go directly to A31 (previously never observed), and about 53 in 100 outgoing transitions of A30 go directly to A37, A38 or A39 (before, A30 led only into the early block) [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004].
- Distances shrank accordingly: A30-to-late-block and early-block-to-A31 gaps moved from 4–5 steps in essentially all before-window observations to 1–3 steps in about 95 in 100 of later observations [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG007, LAG010].
- Placements barely moved: mean relative positions shifted by only about 0.02–0.06 (A30 from about 0.62 to 0.67, A31 from 0.82 to 0.80, block members by similar small amounts); the dominant change for these activities is participation, not placement [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the early block (A33, A35, A36) and the late block (A37, A38, A39) became mutually exclusive alternatives: before, every case executed both blocks in sequence (early always before late, framed by A30 before and A31 after); in the later window a case executes one block or the other — about 47 in 100 the early block and about 53 in 100 the late block — with A30 still preceding and A31 still following whichever block runs. In other words, each block became skippable, with A30 routing directly into the late block when the early block is skipped, and the early block routing directly to A31 when the late block is skipped [REL015, REL020, REL024, DIR001, DIR003, OCC001, OCC006].

This is direct, not just circumstantial: the participation drop is measured directly [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006], the relational items show the together-to-alternatives change and the preserved A30-before/A31-after framing [REL001, REL004, REL007, REL010, REL015, REL020], the direction items show the new bypass transitions [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003], and the gap items corroborate the shortened paths [LAG001, LAG004]. With 57 control-flow items flagged against fewer than one expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole, and the 100-in-100 participation of all six activities in the earlier window rules out the explanation that these were pre-existing variants whose shares merely shifted [screening].

Limitations: the evidence cannot show what determines which block a case takes, and the roughly 2–3 in 100 later traces that still contain both blocks — on which the cross-block LAG items rest with only about 4–5 observations each — leave open a small residue of old-style executions [LAG005, LAG006, LAG008, LAG009, LAG011, LAG012]. POS and LAG use event positions, not clock time, so no claims about timing are made.

Other differences: A30's mean relative position moved later (about 0.62 to 0.67) and A31's slightly earlier (about 0.82 to 0.80), consistent with the shorter traces that the main change produces rather than a separate relocation [POS001, POS002].
