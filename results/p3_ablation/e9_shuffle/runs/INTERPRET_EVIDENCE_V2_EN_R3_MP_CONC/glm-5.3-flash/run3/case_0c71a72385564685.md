### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window a step now runs between A41 and A26, executed as one of three alternative activities: A41 is followed by A19 (about 33 in 100 of its outgoing transitions), A25 (about 30) or A20 (about 24) instead of leading directly to A26 (about 1–2 in 100), and the chosen activity in turn precedes A26 — A19 and A25 lead to A26 in about 95 and 90 in 100 of their outgoing transitions, and the orderings A41 before A19/A25/A20 and A19/A25/A20 before A26 each hold in roughly 28–38 in 100 traces. The three activities appear in about 30–38 in 100 traces each, rarely co-occur, and together account for just over one appearance per trace, consistent with one of them being chosen on most traces. [DIR004, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL001, REL002, REL005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
