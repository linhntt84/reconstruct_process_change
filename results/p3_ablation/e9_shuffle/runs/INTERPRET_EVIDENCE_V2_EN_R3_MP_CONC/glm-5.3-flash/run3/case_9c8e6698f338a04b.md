### Observed differences

- **A17 is no longer observed.** In the earlier window A17 ran in about 52 in 100 traces, about twice per trace on average (repeating within the trace in about 37 in 100 of the traces containing it); it was reached mainly from A12 (about 52 in 100 of A12's outgoing transitions) and led mainly to A15 (about 53 in 100) and A14 (about 46 in 100). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in which the repeating branch through A17 was removed and A01 was inserted before A12. In the earlier window, A12 handed work to A17, which repeated within the trace and passed it on to A14 and A15, while A01 never ran; in the later window A17 is unobserved, A12 leads directly to A15 and A14, and A01 runs once in about 38 in 100 traces, after A02 and before A12, leading onward mainly to A09, A07 and A04. [OCC004, DIR003, DIR002, OCC001, DIR001, REL001, REL007]

The screening produced 27 qualifying control-flow items where only about 0.3 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits produce; A17's disappearance and A01's appearance are each shown directly by occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators, and the shortened gaps around A14 and A15 fit the loss of A17's repeats from between them. Many items, however, describe one and the same underlying difference (A17 vanishing) and are not independent confirmations. [OCC004, POS003, DIR003, OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, LAG005, LAG006]

The main limitation: zero participation only means "not observed", so the evidence cannot distinguish a complete removal of A17 (or a brand-new A01) from both activities falling to rates too low to appear in 200-trace samples, and A01's exact placement is bounded only by its observed relations (after A02, often before A12). [OCC004, OCC001, REL001, DIR001]

Other differences: A06 and A08 now run in fewer traces (about 52→30 and 47→30 in 100); whether these drops are an independent change or a by-product of the rerouting cannot be decided from this evidence. [OCC002, OCC003]
