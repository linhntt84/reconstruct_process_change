### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window every case runs through two activities that were absent earlier, A01 and A05, woven between the existing steps: A03 and A04 typically lead into A01, the chain A01 → A06 → A05 is executed in a fixed order in every case, and A05 closes the case, occasionally followed by A04, A02 or A03. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL003, REL004, REL007, POS003]

The support is direct and consistent: participation of A01 and A05 moved from 0 to all 200 traces; the direct-successor profiles of A03, A04 and A06 changed to the new links; the affected pairs moved from never co-occurring to always co-occurring with a stable order; and the earlier positions of A04 and A06, together with the stretched pair distances, match activities being added between existing steps rather than one path replacing another. Screening also qualified 21 control-flow items where roughly 0.13 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the differences as a whole are far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, REL001, REL002, POS002, POS004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A01 and A05 were not observed earlier, so the evidence cannot rule out that these steps existed before without ever being triggered, or that activity codes were relabelled between the windows; A05's successor profile also rests on only about 29 observed transitions.
