### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A02, A09, A12 and A13 were not observed in any of the 200 traces; in the later window A02 appears in about 48 of 100 traces (occurring roughly twice in the traces that contain it), A13 in about 48 of 100, and A12 and A09 in about 26 of 100 each. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC004, OCC003, DIR002]

- The former direct step A03 → A04 was rerouted through the new activities. Earlier, about 46 of 100 of A03's outgoing transitions went to A04 (about 52 to A08); later, about 46 go to A02 and only about 1 to A04. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the flow around A03–A04 was restructured by splicing new activities into existing paths: the previously direct A03→A04 step is now routed A03 → A02, with A02 alternating with A12 (A02→A12→A02) and closing through A13 → A04; in addition, A09 was inserted between consecutive A08 executions, and A01 — which previously sat between A14 and A04 — is no longer observed, with A14 now flowing straight to A04. [DIR003, DIR002, DIR006, DIR007, DIR004, DIR005, DIR001, DIR008]

The hypothesis rests on direct successor evidence (the old A03→A04, A08→A08 and A14→A01 exits disappeared while A03→A02, A02→A12/A13, A12→A02, A13→A04, A08→A09, A09→A08 and A14→A04 appeared), and the occurrence, relation, position and gap indicators all point the same way; with 54 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.36 expected if nothing had changed, chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC005, REL017, LAG001, LAG003, LAG004, POS006]

Limitations: zero participation only means an activity was not observed in a window, so it cannot be excluded that A02, A09, A12 or A13 existed earlier only on rare unsampled paths, or that A01 persists on paths not sampled later — a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants would look similar. Several indicators also describe the same traces, so their agreement is not fully independent confirmation. [OCC001, OCC002]

Other differences: A04 and A14 now occur later within traces (medians up by roughly 0.15–0.17 of the trace), and repetition gaps for A04–A04 and A14–A14 shifted slightly, the latter based on very few observations. [POS003, POS007, LAG002, LAG005]
