### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural reorganisation around the middle of the process: A01 was added and now executes once per case — entered from A02, A05 or A06, placed on average around 40% of the way through the case, and leading to A03, A05 or A06 — while A04, previously executed in every case between A03 and A07, was dropped, so that A03 now leads directly to A07 and runs one step later (trace position 0.6 to 0.8). The participation flips are complete and consistent (0 versus 100 in 100 traces for both activities), the new and lost edges are observed directly, the co-occurrence flips for A01 and A04 with all five other activities match this picture, and the lag redistributions behave as expected if A01 is inserted into the A02/A05/A06 section. With 34 qualifying items against roughly 0.17 expected without change, sampling noise is implausible as an explanation for the pattern as a whole. Limitation: the evidence cannot distinguish whether A01 functionally replaces A04 (a substitution) or whether an addition and a removal occurred independently, since both change at the same window boundary; the exact later routing among A01, A05 and A06 (their mutual orders are near 50/50) also remains underdetermined. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL003, LAG001, LAG004, screening]

Other differences: none beyond this hypothesis — the remaining position and lag shifts (A05 and A06 sitting later, occasional extra step between middle-section pairs) are consequences of the same insertion of A01 and removal of A04. [POS004, POS005, LAG002, LAG006, LAG009]
