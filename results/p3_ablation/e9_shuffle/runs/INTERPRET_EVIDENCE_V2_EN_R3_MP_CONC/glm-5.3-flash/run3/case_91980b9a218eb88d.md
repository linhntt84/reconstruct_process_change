### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative group A19, A20 and A25 — each appearing in roughly a quarter to two-fifths of traces in both windows — was relocated from the segment before A26 to the segment between A27 and A32. In the earlier window the flow ran A45 → one of A19/A20/A25 → A26, followed later by A27 → A28 → A32; in the later window A45 flows directly to A26, and the trio executes after A27, its members leading on to A28 or A32. This is shown directly by the successor changes at A45, A19, A20, A25, A27 and A28, the order flips between A26 and each trio member, the new adjacencies (A27→A19/A20, A28→A19/A20, A19→A32) replacing the vanished ones (A45→A25, A19→A26), and the trio's position shift from about a third to about three-fifths of the trace while A26 moves slightly earlier. [DIR006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG011, LAG010, LAG006, LAG003, LAG004, REL001, REL003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

The screening comparison supports this reading: 31 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.62 expected by chance for 124 tested units, so chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole, and all items describe one coherent relocation rather than unrelated differences. Limitations: positions are event positions rather than clock times, and the internal arrangement of the inserted segment varies in the later window — A28 can fall before or after a trio member, and the A28→A19/A20 adjacencies suggest more than one ordering — so the exact new sequence cannot be fully pinned down. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is two independent reroutings (A45→A26 and the trio reattached after A27) rather than a single move of the segment, although every item is consistent with the single-move reading. [LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, DIR004, DIR005]

Other differences: A24's participation rose from about 43 to about 58 in 100 traces (mean occurrences per trace about 0.43 to 0.58); the evidence does not connect this to the relocation. [OCC001]
