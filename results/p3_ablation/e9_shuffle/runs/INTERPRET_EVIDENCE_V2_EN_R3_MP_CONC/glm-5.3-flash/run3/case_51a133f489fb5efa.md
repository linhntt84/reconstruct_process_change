### Observed differences

- **Order reversal around A27 and A26.** In the earlier window, A27 ran before A19, A20 or A25 in about 30–36 in 100 of the traces where they appeared together (A26 before them in about 31–36 in 100), and the reverse order was essentially unobserved. In the later window this flipped: A19, A20 and A25 each now precede A27 in about 29–35 in 100 cases and precede A26 in about 30–35 in 100, while the old orders drop to about 1 in 100. [REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]
- **The trio moved earlier in the case; A26 and A27 slightly later.** Median event position of A19, A20 and A25 fell from about 0.60 to about 0.31, while their participation stayed roughly stable (about 30–37 in 100 traces in both windows). A26 (present in every trace) shifted from a median position of about 0.30 to 0.34, and A27 from about 0.56 to 0.59. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- **Successors were rewired.** Earlier, A19, A20 and A25 led mainly to A28 and A32 (about 41–56 in 100 of their outgoing transitions each); later they lead almost exclusively to A26 (about 96–100 in 100). A27, which earlier fed A28 in only about half of its outgoing transitions and the trio in about 46 in 100, now leads to A28 in about 98 in 100; A28, which earlier split between A32 (about 48 in 100) and the trio (about 52 in 100), now leads to A32 in about 99 in 100. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- **The late chain became rigid and immediate.** A27→A28 is now at distance 1 in about 99 in 100 cases (earlier roughly half at distance 1, half at distance 2), A28→A32 likewise (earlier about 48/52), and A27→A32 shortened from about 3 steps to 2. [LAG010, LAG011, LAG013]
- **A08 and A45 became new entry points.** A08's trace participation doubled from about 13 to about 30 in 100 (mean occurrences 0.14 → 0.30 per trace), it now occurs earlier and more consistently (median position about 0.53 → 0.29), and its outgoing transitions, previously spread over about 14 activities, now concentrate on A19, A20 and A25 (about 88 in 100 combined, mostly immediately). A45, which earlier led directly to A26 in about 92 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, now leads to A08/A19/A20/A25 (about 98 in 100 combined), and the A45→A26 gap stretched from 1 step to 2–3 steps. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, LAG001, LAG002, DIR007, LAG014]
- **Minor residual differences.** Gaps from A16 and A18 to A26 lengthened (about 3 → 4–5 steps and 2 → 3–4 steps); A50 and A48 show only negligible shifts toward A51 and A49. [LAG004, LAG005, DIR009, DIR008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A specific possibility directly supported by the evidence is that the segment formed by A19, A20 and A25 was repositioned: in the earlier window these three activities ran late in the case, after A27 and after A26, and handed over to A28 and A32; in the later window they run early, before A26 and before A27, are entered from A08 (whose participation doubled) and A45, and hand over to A26, while A27 now passes immediately to A28 and A28 to A32. [REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, POS002, POS003, POS004, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, OCC001, DIR001, DIR007]

The support is direct rather than circumstantial: the order reversals are observed in about 30–35 in 100 co-occurring traces with the old order nearly vanishing, the successor changes are near-total, and the new adjacencies (A08→A19, A19→A26 at distance 1) appear where they were previously absent or seen only once. The screening comparison also rules out sampling noise as an explanation for the pattern as a whole: 40 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.72 expected for an unchanged process, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [screening, LAG001, LAG007]

Limitations: positions and lags use event positions, so "earlier" means earlier in the sequence of events, not earlier in clock time; the three order reversals and three successor changes are three views of the same restructuring, not independent confirmations; and a few lag items rest on very few observations on one side (1–2 observed pairs). The evidence cannot show whether this was one coordinated rewiring or several coinciding local changes, nor whether unobserved activities mediate the new edges, since direction shares cover only observed outgoing transitions. [LAG001, LAG003, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG012]

Other differences: the lengthened A16→A26 and A18→A26 gaps are consistent with the relocation but involve activities outside the main hypothesis; A50 and A48 became marginally more concentrated on A51 and A49, a difference too small to be behaviourally meaningful. [LAG004, LAG005, DIR009, DIR008]
