- The execution profiles of A09 and A16 exchanged. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A09 and A16 exchanged their places in the flow. In the earlier window, A09 was the mandatory, roughly twice-executed late-stage hub — entered from A10 and A04 and leading to A05, A14 and A06 — while A16 was an optional, single, earlier step that occurred only in traces that also ran A09, before it, and led to A01, A04, A10 and A13. In the later window these roles are reversed: A16 is the mandatory, repeated late hub entered from A10 and A04 and leading to A05, A06 and A14, and A09 is the optional single step, skipped in about half of the traces, occurring only inside traces that also run A16 and before it, preceded by A01 and A13 and leading to A01, A04, A10 and A13. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006]

The hypothesis is shown directly by the successor and predecessor rerouting and by the swapped position and frequency profiles, and every partner-level relation mirrors it: each A09 partnership weakened exactly where the corresponding A16 partnership strengthened, and adjacency distances swapped accordingly. All 39 qualifying items involve A09 or A16, their number is about 100 times the roughly 0.39 items expected by chance for the 79 tested units, and each item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data; no behavioural difference outside this exchange appears in the evidence. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013, LAG014, OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

Limitations: all items describe one underlying trace behaviour, so the many agreeing items corroborate rather than independently confirm; positions and distances are event positions, not clock times; some pair-level distances rest on few observations (as few as about 17 pairs). An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the late-stage block was reassigned to A16 and the mid-process block to A09 through two coordinated reroutings — or an equivalent relabelling — rather than a single exchange of the two activities' places; the observable relations would look the same.
