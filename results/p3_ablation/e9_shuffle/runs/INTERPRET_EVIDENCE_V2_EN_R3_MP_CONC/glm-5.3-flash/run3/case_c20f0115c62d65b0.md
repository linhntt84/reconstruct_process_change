### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the per-case step performed by one of **A19, A20 or A25** — previously executed exactly once in virtually every case, between A27/A28 and A29 — is skipped in the later window, so that cases run directly from **A27 through A28 to A29**, with A29 occurring about one event position earlier. The screening found 27 qualifying items where fewer than 1 would be expected without a change, and every item shows the same picture: near-disappearance of the three activities, loss of all co-occurrence with A27/A28/A29, rerouting of A27's and A28's outgoing transitions onto the direct edge, and collapse of the A27→A29 distance from 3 to 2 positions; all differences are larger than in about 99.5 of 200 random re-splits. Note that these items largely express one underlying change in the same traces rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish full removal of the step from it becoming very rare (about 1 in 200 cases), and it cannot rule out that the same work is now performed under other activity labels or absorbed into A28/A29, since the later-window behaviour of A19/A20/A25 is observed in a single trace each. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL012, DIR004, DIR005, LAG002]

Other differences: A27 shows a few new outgoing edges at about 0.5% each in the later window, and the isolated remaining executions of A19/A20/A25 go to successors not seen before (A44, A08, A42), each seen once. [DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
