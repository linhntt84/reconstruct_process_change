### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A01 does not appear at all (0 of 200 cases); in the later window it appears in every case (200 of 200), exactly once per case, always at the same relative position about two-thirds through the case. [OCC001, POS001]
- Before, A02 handed over to A03 in about 78 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions and to A04 in about 22; in the later window A02 hands over to A01 in about 76 in 100 and to A04 in about 24, and a direct A02 → A03 step is no longer observed. [DIR002]
- Before, A04 handed over to A02 in about 78 in 100 and to A03 in about 22; in the later window A04 hands over to A02 in about 76 in 100 and to A01 in about 24, and a direct A04 → A03 step is no longer observed. A01's only observed outgoing transition is to A03 (100 in 100). [DIR003, DIR001]
- Before, A01 never co-occurred with A02, A03 or A04 (in 100 in 100 of the relevant cases only the other activity was present); in the later window A01 co-occurs with each of the three in 100 in 100 cases, always after A02 and A04 and before A03. [REL001, REL002, REL003]
- The event distance from A02 to A03 grew by one step: from one step in about 78 in 100 cases (two steps in the remainder) to two steps in about 76 in 100 (three steps in the remainder); the distance from A04 to A03 likewise grew from one/two steps (about 22/78 in 100) to two/three steps (about 24/76 in 100). [LAG001, LAG002]
- The choice between the two orders of A02 and A04 is essentially stable: before, about 78 in 100 cases ran A04 before A02 and about 22 ran A02 before A04; in the later window the split is about 76 to 24. [LAG001, LAG002, DIR002, DIR003]
- A02's relative event position moved earlier (mean about 0.39 to about 0.25; median from 0.5 to 0.33). [POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added to the flow between the A02/A04 stage and A03: in the earlier window cases ran A04 → A02 → A03 (about 78 in 100) or A02 → A04 → A03 (about 22 in 100), while in the later window the same two orderings persist (about 76 vs 24 in 100) and every case additionally executes A01 once, after A02/A04 and before A03, with A02 and A04 now routing to A01 instead of directly to A03. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002]

The change is shown directly and consistently: participation goes from 0 to 200 of 200 cases, the routing profiles show the successors of A02 and A04 switching from A03 to A01 while A01 leads only to A03, the relation items show A01 moving from never co-occurring to always co-occurring in a fixed order, and both the A02→A03 and A04→A03 distances grew by exactly one step, as expected if a single activity was added. Eleven control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.06 expected for the 12 tested units if nothing had changed, and every difference is larger than in about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled cases. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, POS001]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly means only that A01 was not observed there, so an execution that was vanishingly rare before cannot be excluded with certainty, and the direct A02/A04 → A03 steps could persist at a rate too low to surface in 200 cases. The evidence covers only the neighbourhood of A01–A04, and several items describe the same underlying addition rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR003]

Other differences: A02's relative event position moved earlier (mean about 0.39 to about 0.25); this is a position-based side effect consistent with A01 being added after A02 rather than a separate relocation of A02. [POS002]
