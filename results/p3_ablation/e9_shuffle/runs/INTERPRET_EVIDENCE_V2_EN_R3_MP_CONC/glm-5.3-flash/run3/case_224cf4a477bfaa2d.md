### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new activity A01 was inserted into the flow of every case. In the earlier window A01 was entirely absent; in the later window it runs once per case, always after A02 — directly after A02 in about half of the cases and after A04 in most of the rest — and before A03, A06, A07 and A10 in practically all cases where they run. The signs are direct and consistent across indicator types: the participation change from 0 to 100 in 100 traces, new successor shares for A02 and A04, relation profiles flipping from "never together" to "always together in a fixed order", and a one-step increase in the distances from A02 to all downstream activities. With 57 units tested, 34 items qualified against roughly 0.28 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole cannot be attributed to random sampling, although many items describe the same single insertion and are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005]

Limitation: the evidence cannot rule out that A01 was already executed in the earlier window but not recorded there, which would look identical in this data; the positional shifts alone would not establish the insertion and only corroborate the transition and relation evidence. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS007]

Other differences: A09, which previously closed about 45 in 100 traces as the last step (typically preceded by A06, and also preceded by A03, A04 and A10), is not observed at all in the later window, and A06 now ends those traces; this is consistent with the final step having been removed from the flow, though zero participation only shows A09 was not observed, and A06's later successor profile rests on only 3 outgoing transitions.
