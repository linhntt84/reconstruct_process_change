### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the optional step formed by A14, A15 and A20 between A22 and A24 is no longer executed, so that cases now flow directly A22 → A23 → A24. Earlier, every case inserted exactly one of the three activities between A22 and A24 — before A23 in about 45 in 100 cases and after A23 in about 55 in 100 — whereas later none of the three appears in 200 traces, the successor distributions of A22 and A23 have collapsed to single edges, and the A22-to-A24 distance shortened from always three positions to always two, exactly the loss of one step. The occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators all describe this same flow, every flagged difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled traces, and 28 flagged items against roughly 0.39 expected by chance makes a chance explanation of the overall pattern implausible. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL001, REL002, REL006]

The main limitation is that zero participation only shows the activities were not observed in the later 200 traces: a residual variant still executing A14, A15 or A20 at a very low rate (roughly below 1 in 100 cases) would rarely surface in a sample of this size, so complete removal cannot be distinguished from a drastic reduction. The indicators also largely reflect the same traces rather than independent confirmations, and a pure shift in variant frequencies — a short A22 → A23 → A24 variant previously too rare to appear becoming the observed behaviour — cannot be ruled out, although the earlier window showed the longer form in all 200 traces.
