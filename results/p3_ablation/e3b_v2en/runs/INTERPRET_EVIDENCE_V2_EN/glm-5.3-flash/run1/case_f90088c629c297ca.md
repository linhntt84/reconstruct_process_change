### Observed differences

- In the before window, A36 is followed directly by A21 in all 200 traces: all of A36's observed outgoing transitions go to A21, and the A36→A21 pair sits at a distance of one event throughout. [DIR004, LAG001]
- In the later window, three activities that were not observed before appear: A14 in about 37 in 100 traces (74 of 200), A15 in about 29 in 100 (57 of 200) and A20 in about 34 in 100 (67 of 200), each occurring at most once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The three are mutually exclusive alternatives: no later-window trace contains more than one of them, and together they cover about 99 in 100 traces. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- Each new activity sits between A36 and A21: A36 precedes A14, A15 or A20 in every trace where that activity occurs, and each of the three is then followed by A21, with its outgoing transitions going exclusively to A21. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The direct A36→A21 handover therefore nearly disappears: A36's outgoing transitions now go to A14 (about 37%), A20 (about 34%), A15 (about 29%) and only rarely to A21 (about 1 in 100), and the A36→A21 distance shifts from one event to two events in about 99 in 100 cases. [DIR004, LAG001]
- A21 still occurs in every trace in both windows and shifts only slightly later (mean relative position from about 0.37 to 0.39 of the trace), while the new activities appear at about one third of the trace length. [POS004, POS001, POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a three-way exclusive choice now runs between A36 and A21: in the later window, about 99 in 100 traces execute exactly one of three previously unobserved activities — A14, A15 or A20 — between A36 and A21, whereas previously A36 was followed directly by A21 in every trace, with the direct transition retained in only about 1 in 100 later traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL005, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, DIR004, LAG001]

Supporting signs: 21 evidence items qualified, far more than the roughly 0.37 expected by chance in an unchanged process, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of unchanged data. The hypothesis rests on direct relational and positional evidence, not participation alone: A36 precedes each new activity, each new activity precedes A21, and the A36→A21 distance moves from one event to two. The shares also interlock — the three participation shares (about 37%, 29% and 34%) sum to about 99%, matching the residual direct A36→A21 share of about 1% and the 1% of pairs still at distance one. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001]

Limitations: the 21 items largely re-describe one underlying trace behaviour from different angles, so they are not independent confirmations. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is sampling: the before window shows A14, A15 and A20 in 0 of 200 traces, and an unchanged process can in principle miss a variant in one window, although at the observed later-window frequencies (roughly a third of traces each) this is hard to sustain; the exact split among the three alternatives and the residual direct path may also reflect sampling variation rather than stable long-run shares. Zero participation before means only that these activities were not observed in that window, not that they were absent from the process. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
