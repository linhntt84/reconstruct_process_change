### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, an exclusive three-way choice among A14, A15 and A20 is executed inside the former sequence A22 → A23 → A24: in about 995 of 1000 later traces exactly one of the three runs once, always after A22 and before A24, placed between A22 and A23 in about 56 in 100 of these traces and between A23 and A24 in about 43, while the plain A22 → A23 → A24 path remains for the small remainder. The support is direct and consistent across indicator families: participation rises from zero with substantial backing (58–82 traces each), A22 and A23 lose their exclusive successors and gain transitions to the new activities, the new activities' transitions return to A23 or A24, the relational items bracket every new activity between A22 and A24, and each step distance grows by exactly one in precisely the traces where a new activity sits there. With 27 qualifying items against roughly 0.4 expected if nothing had changed, and every item separating the windows more strongly than about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled traces, chance alone is an implausible explanation for the collection.
