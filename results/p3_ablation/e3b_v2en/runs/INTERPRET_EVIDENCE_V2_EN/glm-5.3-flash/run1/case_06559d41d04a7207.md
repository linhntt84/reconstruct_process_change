### Observed differences

- In the later window, A26, A27 and A28 — not observed before (0 of 200 traces each; A28 in 1) — each occur in essentially every case: 200 of 200, about once per trace. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]
- A19, A20 and A21, previously each present in about 30–36 in 100 cases (60–71 of 200 traces), appear in only 1 trace each in the later window. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Before, exactly one of A19/A20/A21 ran per case: they almost never co-occur (at most about 1 in 200 cases for any pair) and their shares sum to about 100 in 100. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL007, REL008, REL016]
- Each of the three was preceded by A44 and followed by A29: about 97–99 in 100 of their outgoing transitions went to A29 (typically one step away), and A44 came before each of them in essentially every co-occurrence. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG002, REL013, REL021, REL028]
- In the later window, A26, A27 and A28 co-occur in every case in varying order (each pair splits roughly 50/50 between the two orders), and each is followed about a third of the time by each of the other two or by A29. [REL031, REL032, REL037, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008]
- A44's routing moved with this: before, its outgoing transitions went about 23–27 in 100 each to A10, A19, A20 and A21; after, about 30–33 in 100 each go to A26, A27 and A28, with A44 preceding A26 in all but about 1 in 200 later cases and A26 preceding A29 in about 99 in 100. [DIR009, REL034, REL033]
- The distance from A44 to A29 grew accordingly: 2 steps in about 75 in 100 earlier cases (3 steps in about 24) versus 4 steps in about 90 in 100 later cases (5 steps in about 10). [LAG005]
- A29 itself runs in every case in both windows and only sits slightly later (mean relative position about 0.40 to 0.44); A10 became rarer (about 26 in 100 cases before vs about 11 in 100 after) and A44 now rarely leads to it (about 23 in 100 of A44's outgoing before vs about 3 in 100 after). [POS008, OCC001, DIR009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One structural possibility supported by the evidence is that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: the earlier step of running exactly one of A19, A20 or A21 immediately before A29 gave way to running all three of A26, A27 and A28, in varying order, between A44 and A29 in every case. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR009, REL031, REL033, REL034, LAG005]

The support is direct and consistent: relational and lag evidence places the new trio between A44 and A29 (A44 before A26, A26 before A29, and the A44-to-A29 distance growing by about two steps), A44's outgoing transitions moved from the old trio to the new one, and the occurrence, routing, position, relation and lag indicators all describe the same picture. With 74 qualifying items against roughly 0.82 expected without any change, chance is an implausible explanation for the pattern as a whole, and each item's difference is also unusual for random re-splits of the pooled data. [REL033, REL034, LAG005, DIR009, OCC005]

Limitations: with 200 traces per window it cannot be ruled out that the A19/A20/A21 branch still exists but was taken in only 1 of 200 later cases, or that the A26/A27/A28 behaviour existed before as a rare variant the earlier window did not sample (0 of 200); the evidence shows the two behaviours exchanging dominance in the same segment, not how or why. A10's reduced participation (about 26 to 11 in 100 cases) accompanies the change but its role is only loosely pinned down. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC001]
