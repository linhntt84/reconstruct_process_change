- Activities A19, A20 and A25 never occur in the earlier window; in the later window they appear in about 29, 41 and 31 in 100 traces respectively (58, 82 and 61 of 200), each essentially once per case, around the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.52). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new sequential step was inserted into the segment between A27 and A29: in the later window each case executes exactly one of three alternative activities — A19, A20 or A25 — placed either immediately before A28 (about 55 in 100 cases) or immediately after it (about 44 in 100), whereas the earlier window ran directly from A27 to A28 to A29. [REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, REL004, REL008, REL011, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The support is direct and consistent rather than merely voluminous: 28 items qualified, about 50 times the 0.56 expected without change, but they largely describe one underlying difference across occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators. The order claims rest on relational evidence — the new activities follow A27 and precede A29 in essentially all of their occurrences and never co-occur with each other — and each of the two insertion positions is corroborated independently by the routing shares at A27 and A28 and by the one-event stretch in the lag distributions. [REL001, REL002, REL006, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: positions are event positions, not clock time, and the step appears in all 200 later traces, so the evidence cannot distinguish a mandatory step from an optional one that was simply selected in every sampled case. The split between the three variants and between the two insertion positions is estimated from single 200-trace windows and could shift with sampling; a rare variant appearing in one window only cannot explain three activities covering essentially every later case while absent from all 200 earlier ones, but small details — such as the roughly 1–2% of A25's outgoing transitions going to A27, A32 or A44 — are too weakly supported to interpret. [POS001, POS002, POS003, DIR003, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
