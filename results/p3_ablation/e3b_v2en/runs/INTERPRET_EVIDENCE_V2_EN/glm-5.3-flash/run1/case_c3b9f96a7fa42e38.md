### Observed differences

- The clearest difference is a reversal of order around the activities A35, A36 and A41. In the earlier window these three activities came before both A26 and A27 in about 98–100 in 100 traces; in the later window the order is reversed, with A26 and A27 each preceding A35, A36 and A41 in about 97–99 in 100 traces. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Routing changed accordingly. In the earlier window, A25's outgoing transitions went to A35 (about 39 in 100), A36 (32) and A41 (27) and essentially never directly to A27, while A26 passed on to A30 in about 99 in 100 of its outgoing transitions. In the later window, A25 leads almost always directly to A27 (about 98 in 100), A26 branches to A35, A36 and A41 (about 30–34 in 100 each, with the direct step to A30 nearly gone), and A35, A36 and A41 now lead to A30 (about 30–35 in 100 each) instead of to A27 (under 1 in 100). [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]
- Distances moved with the routing: the gap from A25 to A27 shrank from typically 4 steps (about 93 in 100) to 1 step (about 98 in 100), while the gap from A26 to A30 grew from typically 1 step (about 99 in 100) to typically 4–5 steps (about 91 in 100 at 4 steps). [LAG005, LAG009]
- Positions shifted the same way: A27 and A26 now occur earlier in the trace (mean relative position about 0.47 to 0.38 and 0.62 to 0.53), while A35, A36 and A41 occur later (about 0.41–0.42 to 0.58–0.59). [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- A separate cluster concerns A06, A15 and A02. In the later window, A06 appears in fewer traces (about 13 in 100 vs 27 in 100 before) and later within them (mean relative position about 0.25 to 0.44); its outgoing transitions to A16 fell from about 86 in 100 to 39 in 100, spreading over many other targets (based on 57 vs 28 outgoing transitions), and A15's transitions to A06 fell from about 15 in 100 to 3 in 100. A02 appears in more traces (about 12 in 100 vs 5 in 100 before, i.e. 23 vs 9 traces). [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment formed by A35, A36 and A41 changed its placement: previously it ran between A25 and A27 — A25 branched into one of the three, the three led on to A27, and A26 continued straight to A30 — whereas in the later window it runs after A26 and before A30, with A25 connecting directly to A27. This is shown directly by the near-complete order reversal in all six activity pairs, each measured on all 200 traces per window, and it is consistently echoed by the successor changes at A25, A26, A35, A36 and A41, by the gap changes (A25→A27 closing to one step, A26→A30 opening to four–five steps) and by the position shifts. The number of qualifying items (33, against about 0.76 expected without change) is also far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG005, LAG009, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

Limitations: the internal make-up of the segment (which of A35, A36 or A41 runs in a given case, and in what order) rests on shares of roughly 30–35 in 100 over about 200 outgoing transitions each, and several lag details — the old links from A25 to A35/A36/A41 and the pairs A16→A27 and A36→A30 — rest on only 1–4 observations in one window, so those specifics are weak. The changes around A06, A15 and A02 are not explained by this placement difference and may constitute a separate one. [LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, POS001]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows: rare variants can appear in one window only, which could account for the weakly supported lag items. It cannot plausibly explain order relations that hold in about 98–100 in 100 traces in one window and reverse in about 97–99 in 100 in the other, which is what carries the hypothesis. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
