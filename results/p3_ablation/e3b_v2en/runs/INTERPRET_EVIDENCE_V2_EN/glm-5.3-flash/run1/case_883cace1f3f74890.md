### Observed differences

- Three activities that never appear in the earlier window occur throughout the later one: A19 in about 59 of 200 traces (roughly 30 in 100), A20 in about 82 (roughly 40 in 100) and A25 in about 60 (roughly 30 in 100). A10 also rises modestly, from about 5 in 100 traces to about 14 in 100. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC001]
- The three new activities are mutually exclusive: no later-window trace contains both A19 and A20, and the A19/A25 and A20/A25 pairs co-occur in only about 1 in 200 traces each. Their participation shares sum to almost exactly all traces, so a later-window trace typically contains exactly one of the three. [REL001, REL002, REL006]
- Routing changed around the new activities. A27, which led to A28 in about 99 in 100 of its outgoing transitions before, now leads to A28 in about 44 in 100 and to A20, A19 or A25 in about 22, 15 and 17 in 100. A28, which led to A29 in about 99 in 100 before, now leads to A29 in about 55 in 100 and to A20, A19 or A25 in about 17, 13 and 12 in 100. The new activities themselves exit almost entirely into A28 or A29 (for example A20 goes to A28 in about 56 in 100 and to A29 in about 42 in 100 of its outgoing transitions). [DIR004, DIR005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- Step distances grew by one: A27 reached A29 within two steps in about 99 in 100 earlier traces but needs three steps in about 97 in 100 later traces; A27→A28 moved from adjacent (about 99 in 100) to one intervening step in about 55 in 100; A28→A29 moved from adjacent (about 99 in 100) to one intervening step in about 44 in 100. [LAG002, LAG001, LAG003]
- Ordering places the new activities between A27 and A29: A27 precedes A19, A20 or A25 in about 29, 40 and 30 in 100 of the traces containing both, and each of the three precedes A29 in nearly all co-occurrences (about 30, 39 and 30 in 100, with almost no reverse order). Relative to A28 the order is mixed — each new activity precedes A28 in about 16–23 in 100 and follows it in about 13–17 in 100 — matching the two routing placements. [REL003, REL007, REL010, REL005, REL009, REL012, REL004, REL008, REL011]
- Event positions agree: the new activities sit mid-trace (mean relative position about 0.51), while A27's mean position shifts slightly earlier (about 0.48 to 0.46) and A29's slightly later (about 0.55 to 0.57). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, execution includes one additional mid-process step that is one of three alternative activities — A19, A20 or A25 — placed between A27 and A29, either immediately after A27 (before A28) or immediately after A28 (before A29). In the earlier window, traces typically ran from A27 directly to A28 and on to A29 without any of these activities. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL005]

The support is direct and consistent rather than circumstantial: the lag distributions show the extra step between A27 and A29, the relation and direction items show where the new activities sit and where they exit, and the occurrence and position items agree. The number of qualifying items (29, against roughly 0.54 expected if nothing had changed) is far beyond what an unchanged process produces, and each item's difference exceeds those seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [LAG002, LAG001, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, POS002, OCC003]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is sampling: the earlier window of 200 traces may simply not have contained a rare pre-existing variant that already included A19, A20 and A25, so zero participation before means only "not observed", not "absent from the process". The evidence also does not show what determines which of the three activities a trace takes, and the direction shares are measured over outgoing transitions rather than over cases. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR004]
