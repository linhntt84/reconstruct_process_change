### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment A19 → A25 → A20, positioned between A41 and A26, changed from executed in only about 47 in 100 cases — with the flow routed directly from A41 to A26 in the remaining cases — to being executed in virtually every case, giving the through-path A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 → A26. The support is direct and consistent rather than circumstantial: participation of all three activities rose to nearly every trace with exactly one occurrence each, their internal order became fixed, A41's outgoing flow moved almost entirely from A26 to A19, and the A41-to-A26 distance shifted from one position to four, exactly the gap that running the three activities in between produces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL007, DIR002, LAG001]

The many agreeing items largely describe one underlying trace behaviour, so they should not be read as independent confirmations. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is sampling: the direct A41 → A26 behaviour appears in at most about 1 in 200 later traces, and a rare variant can disappear from a finite sample, although a drop from about 53 in 100 to about 0.5 in 100 traces is difficult to attribute to sampling variation alone with 200 traces per window. Whether the direct A41 → A26 route remains possible at a low rate or is simply no longer taken cannot be determined from these windows. [LAG001, DIR002]
