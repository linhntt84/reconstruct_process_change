### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, roughly half of the executions do not execute the segment formed by A19, A20 and A25, with flow continuing from A41 straight to A26, and that A06 is skipped even more often, with flow continuing from A14 straight to A15; the cases that still execute the segment keep the former order. Fifty-nine items qualified against fewer than one expected for an unchanged process, and the participation losses (about 45–48 in 100 traces for each of A06, A19, A20 and A25), the "both absent" relations at about 46 in 100, and the successor shifts (A14 to A15 and A41 to A26 up; A14 to A06 and A41 to A19 down) all describe one coherent rerouting rather than scattered noise [DIR005, DIR008, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL016, REL022].

The main limitation is that the many relation items re-express the same underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations; the evidence also cannot rule out that the later window simply samples a pre-existing shorter variant far more heavily than the earlier window did — a variant absent from about 200 before-traces yet present in roughly 94 of 200 after-traces is hard to attribute to sampling alone, but the finite windows leave this open [REL016, REL018]. The increased presence of A04, A08 and A10 (about 29–40 traces each, up from 8–10) is consistent with additional new routing but is too thinly supported to tie firmly to the same change [DIR002, DIR004, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004].
