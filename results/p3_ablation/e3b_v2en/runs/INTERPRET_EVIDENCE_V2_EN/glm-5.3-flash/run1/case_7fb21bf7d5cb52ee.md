### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A10 runs in most cases as an early step between A14 and A15, and that the previously near-fixed sequence from A31 through A22, A20, A23, A24, A26 and A25 towards A32 is executed in a variable, partially reversed order, while A04 and especially A08 are executed in fewer cases. The placement claim rests on direct relational and positional evidence — A14 immediately followed by A10, A10 followed by A15, A10 preceding the downstream activities, and reversed order within the A20–A26 segment — consistent across occurrence, routing, position, relation and lag indicators, and the 77 qualifying items are many times the roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process. The evidence cannot rule out that the process model is unchanged and only the mix of existing variants shifted between the windows — a variant with early A10 and the reversed segment order could have moved from rare to common, producing the same indicators. Some specifics are fragile: A08's rerouting rests on 8 later transitions, the A25→A22 and A26→A22 lag items each rest on a single earlier observation, and the fourteen A10 relation items describe one underlying behaviour rather than independent confirmations; position and lag comparisons use event order, not clock time.
