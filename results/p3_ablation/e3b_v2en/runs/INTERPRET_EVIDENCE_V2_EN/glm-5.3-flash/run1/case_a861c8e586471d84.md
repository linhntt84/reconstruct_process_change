### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20 and A25 are no longer executed: before, each ran in about 30–36 of 100 traces (71, 61 and 70 of 200 traces) with no repetitions, while afterwards they appear in 0, 1 and 0 of 200 traces; before, the three behaved as mutually exclusive alternatives — they practically never occurred together (co-occurrence at most about 0.5 of 100 traces) and in combination covered essentially every case [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL007, REL009, REL014].
- Routing around these activities changed: before, about 33 of 100 outgoing transitions of A41 went to A19, about 33 to A25 and about 27 to A20 (roughly 93 in 100 combined), whereas in the later window about 92 of 100 outgoing transitions of A41 go directly to A26; the three activities themselves previously led to A26 in 97–99 of 100 observed outgoing transitions and show no observed outgoing transitions in the later window [DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003].
- The distance from A41 to A26 shortened by about one step: before, the pair occurred two positions apart in about 94 of 100 cases (three apart in most of the remainder), while in the later window it occurs one position apart in about 93 of 100 cases; the A24→A31 gap shows a similar shift from typically two positions to typically one [LAG002, LAG001].
- The trio's co-occurrences with their former neighbours vanished: with A26, A31 and A41 these ranged from about 30 to 36 of 100 traces before and are now zero or nearly zero, with the neighbour appearing alone; the same loss shows for A12, A14 and A24 [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL008, REL013, REL018].
- Minor positional shifts accompany the shortening: A26 now appears slightly earlier within traces (mean relative position about 0.39 to 0.37) and A41 and A14 slightly later (about 0.32 to 0.33 and 0.14 to 0.15) [POS005, POS006, POS001].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the set of three mutually exclusive activities A19, A20 and A25, which previously executed between A41 and A26 in essentially every case, is no longer executed in the later window, so that A41 is now followed directly by A26 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, LAG002].

The 36 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.6 expected for an unchanged process, so chance alone cannot account for the evidence as a whole; the decisive relations are observed directly — A41's successor profile, measured on about 201 outgoing transitions per window, shifts from roughly 93 in 100 transitions into the trio to roughly 92 in 100 into A26, and the A41→A26 distance, measured on all 200 traces per window, shortens from typically two positions to one [DIR004, LAG002]. The participation fall from about a third of traces to 0–1 of 200 (each difference larger than about 99.5 of 100 random re-splits) and the agreement across occurrence, routing, relation, lag and position indicators make the hypothesis consistent, though these items largely mirror one underlying difference rather than serving as independent confirmations [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS005].

Limitations: zero participation means only that the activities were not observed, so a rarely executed remnant cannot be excluded — a single A20 occurrence remains, and its after-window profile (a much later relative position of about 0.62 and a transition to A30 instead of A26) rests on one trace; the evidence also cannot distinguish a modification of the process from a shift in the case mix sampled by the two windows, since finite windows can differ in variant frequencies without any structural change, although a drop from about 30–36 of 100 traces to none makes pure sampling noise implausible [OCC002, DIR002, POS003].
