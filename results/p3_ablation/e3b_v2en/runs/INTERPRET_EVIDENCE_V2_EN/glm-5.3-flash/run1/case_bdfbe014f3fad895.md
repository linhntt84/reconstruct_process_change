### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window executes a block of three activities — A32, A33 and A38 together, in varying order, in about half of the cases — positioned between A26 and A28 and running in cases where A27 does not, A27's presence having halved; separately, A04 is now executed in most cases immediately after A14 and before A15. The relational evidence is direct: A26 precedes each block activity in about 48–50 of 100 later traces, each block activity precedes A28 in about 48 of 100, the A26→A28 distance widened accordingly, and A27 co-occurs with the block in about 1 of 100 traces or fewer. Participation, routing, positional and lag indicators all point the same way; every item's difference exceeds all but about 5 in 1,000 random re-splits; the item count (55, against about 0.73 expected without change) is far beyond what an unchanged process produces; and a block absent from all 200 earlier traces but present in about 100 of 200 later ones cannot plausibly be a sampling artefact. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG001, LAG003, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027]

Limitations remain: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that the block was not observed there, so a very rare path that later became frequent cannot be distinguished from a genuinely added one; the near-mutual exclusivity of A27 and the block fits both a new alternative path and a shift in the mix of case variants, which these aggregate indicators cannot separate; the block's internal order varies, so no single fixed sequence is established; and A06's decline rests on only about 8 later observations. Many items describe the same underlying traces and are not independent confirmations. [OCC004, DIR002, REL022, REL028, REL029, REL030]
