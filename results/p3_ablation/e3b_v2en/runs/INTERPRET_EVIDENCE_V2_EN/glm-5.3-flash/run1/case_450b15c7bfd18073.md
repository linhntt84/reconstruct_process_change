### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, roughly half of the cases appear to replace the segment A21 → A22 → A23 with A21 → a run containing A27, A28 and A33 together, in varying order → A23, while the other half keep the original segment. This rests on direct relational and positional evidence: A21 precedes each new activity and each new activity precedes A23 in every co-occurrence, A22 is mutually exclusive with the new activities, the A21–A23 distance grows from 2 to 4 steps in exactly the share of traces where A22 disappears, and A21's routing splits in matching proportions. The 26 evidence items vastly exceed the roughly 0.4 expected by chance for 80 tested units, and although they largely describe one underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations, they are mutually consistent on that single difference. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL009, REL010, REL011, LAG001, DIR001, REL006, REL007, REL008]

Limitations remain: the evidence cannot distinguish a newly introduced branch from a variant that was too rare to appear in the earlier sample and rose to about half of the cases — a variant absent from 200 earlier traces yet present in 98 of 200 later ones makes an unchanged process unlikely, but two finite windows cannot fully rule out a shift in variant frequencies without structural change. The exact internal ordering of the three new activities varies between cases and cannot be pinned down further from these indicators.
