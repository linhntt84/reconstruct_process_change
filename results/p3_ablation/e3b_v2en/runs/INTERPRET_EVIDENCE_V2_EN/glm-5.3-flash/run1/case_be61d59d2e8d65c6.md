### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the optional activities A19, A20 and A25 — which in the earlier window ran in almost every case, one at a time, inserted directly after A27 or between A28 and A29 — are essentially no longer executed in the later window, so that control flow passes straight from A27 to A28 and on to A29. The participation collapse (from 57–81 of 200 traces to 1 per activity) is far beyond what sampling variation in a stable process produces, and the routing, relational and lag items show the resulting direct A27 → A28 → A29 succession directly and coherently. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012] The volume of qualifying items (30, against roughly 0.56 expected if nothing had changed) also cannot be explained by chance as a whole. [screening]

Limitations: the profiles of the few residual occurrences (A19 followed by A38, A20 by A37, A25 by A31, and the single distant A28→A25 pair) rest on one trace each and may be rare variants rather than new routing; A10's successor redistribution is not explained by this pattern and could be a further difference or sampling variation; and finite windows cannot rule out a different mix of trace variants between the windows, although no sampling explanation accounts for activities present in 57–81 of 200 earlier traces falling to 1 of 200.
