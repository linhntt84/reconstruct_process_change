### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process now runs A06 as a late step — typically immediately before A35 and, in about half of the later cases, directly after A34 — and that execution has shifted from the A21–A23 branch to the A24–A26 branch, which now feeds into that late A06→A35 sequence. The number of qualifying items (92, against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed) cannot be explained by chance as a whole, and the placement claim rests on direct relational and positional evidence rather than on frequency shifts alone: A06 precedes A35 in about 49 in 100 later traces, A06's exits concentrate on A35 at lag 1, A34 gains a strong new exit to A06, and A35 follows A34 one position later than before. The participation and routing changes agree across occurrence, direction and relation indicators, each difference being larger than in about 995 of 1,000 comparable random re-splits. [OCC004, OCC007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR005, REL012, LAG001, LAG013]

The evidence cannot rule out that both windows sample the same underlying variant pool with only the variant frequencies shifted: a pre-existing rare variant containing late A06 together with A24–A26 could have become common while an A21–A23 variant became rare, which would reproduce these participation, co-occurrence and routing differences without any structural modification. In addition, many items describe overlapping trace behaviour (for example, the A06 relation items and the A06 lag item draw on the same traces), so they are not independent confirmations, and whether the A06 placement and the branch shift are one modification or two cannot be determined from this evidence. [REL012, REL016, DIR005, LAG001]
