### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window reflects a rerouting of the process in two connected ways. The group formed by A35, A36 and A41, which previously sat between A25 and A27 (A25 branched into it and it flowed on to A27, while A26 ran straight to A30), now sits between A26 and A30: A25 connects directly to A27, A26 feeds the group, and the group flows on to A30. Separately, A02 is skipped in most traces — A49 now leads directly to A50 instead of via A02 — and A06 and A10 run about three times as often, although the evidence does not show where they now fit. [DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR011, REL010, LAG002, LAG005, OCC002, OCC003]

The support is direct and consistent: the order reversal is observed relationally with both sides present in every trace, and the routing, lag and position indicators all point to the same repositioning; the A02 skip is shown jointly by participation, successor and lag changes. With 45 qualifying items against roughly 0.83 expected for an unchanged process, chance alone cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, LAG002, LAG005, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, OCC001, DIR001, DIR011, LAG001, LAG009]

Limitations: many of the relation and direction items describe the same underlying trace changes and are not independent confirmations. The evidence also cannot distinguish a modified process from a shift in the mix of existing variants — for example, a variant that already placed A27 before the A35/A36/A41 group and omitted A02 could have become dominant; sampling differences in variant frequencies can move participation shares such as A02's, although reversing an order seen in about 97–99 of 100 traces is hard to produce by sampling alone. The later routing profile of A02 and the earlier profile of A06 additionally rest on few observations (about 46 and 12 outgoing transitions). [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, REL010]
