### Observed differences

- In the later window, A25, A26 and A31 each occur about once per trace (mean about 1.0) instead of about 3.4 times. All three still appear in every trace in both windows, but repetition nearly disappears: earlier about 75 in 100 traces contained repeats of these activities, afterwards essentially none (about 0.5 in 100). The three activities show identical profiles in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The outgoing flow of A31 is redirected: earlier, about 71 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions (689 in total) went to A25 and 29 to A20; later, about 96 in 100 (208 in total) go to A20 and only 4 to A25. [DIR001]
- The order of A25 and A31 becomes fixed: the two still co-occur in every trace, but interleaved-or-repeated ordering falls from 75 in 100 traces to under 1 in 100, while A25-before-A31 rises from 25 to about 99.5 in 100. [REL001]
- A31 now follows A25 at a distance of two event positions in about 92 in 100 observed instances of the pair, against about 12 in 100 earlier (with another 5 in 100 at distance five); the reverse pair, A31 followed by A25 within five steps, appears in only 1 of 200 later traces versus about 150 of 200 earlier. [LAG001, LAG002]
- The average relative position of A25 (about 0.44 to 0.41) and A31 (about 0.48 in both windows) barely moves, but the positional spread of both roughly halves (standard deviation about 0.12 to 0.06), consistent with one fixed occurrence rather than several scattered ones. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A25, A26 and A31 are executed once per case in a fixed sequence — A25 first, A31 about two positions later, with A31 then continuing to A20 — whereas earlier these activities ran about three to four times per case in interleaved fashion, with A31 frequently flowing back to A25. The relational and routing items show this directly and consistently, and the same change also explains the drop in occurrences and repetition, the near-disappearance of the reverse A31-to-A25 pair, and the halved positional spread of A25 and A31; with 9 qualifying items against roughly 0.3 expected for an unchanged process, and every item unusual for random re-splits of the pooled traces (empirical strength about 99.5 in 100), chance as a whole is not a plausible explanation. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001, LAG001, LAG002, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: A26's involvement rests only on its occurrence profile, as no routing or ordering evidence covers it; and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different mixes of process variants — for example a repeating variant dominating the earlier window and nearly vanishing later — rather than a modification of one underlying structure.
