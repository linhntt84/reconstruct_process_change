### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a cycle from A31 back to A25 now repeats a block containing A25, A26 and A31 in most cases. Before, each of the three activities ran exactly once per trace and A31 always handed over to A20; in the later window A31 routes back to A25 in about 71 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, the three activities recur in about 68 in 100 traces at roughly 3.5 occurrences each, and the fixed two-step A25-to-A31 spacing is gone. The successor change is direct relational evidence, and the occurrence, relation and lag items all point the same way; with eight qualifying items against roughly 0.3 expected by chance in a stable process, the set of findings is not a typical chance outcome. [DIR001, REL001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, LAG001]

Limitations: the eight items largely describe the same underlying repetition, so they are not independent confirmations, and A26 has no direct relational evidence — its place in the repeated block is inferred only from its statistics matching A25 and A31. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a sampling shift in variant frequencies: a rare looping variant could have existed before the change point without appearing in the 200 before-traces, since the earlier window shows no repetition at all. The positional items use event positions rather than clock time, and their small mean shifts do not show that any activity was moved. [OCC002, POS001, POS002, REL001]
