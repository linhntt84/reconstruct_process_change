### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured: a stretch in which A21, A22 and A23 all executed in every case (in varying internal order) now runs as a single pass through one of three alternative activities, A14, A15 or A16, which then always flows into A24. This rests on direct relational and positional evidence, not only on frequency shifts: A39's outgoing transitions were redirected to the new activities, the new activities always lead to A24, and the A39-to-A24 gap contracted from 4 to 2 event positions, exactly the footprint of a three-activity stretch becoming a single activity. Occurrence, routing, positional and relational indicators all tell the same coherent story, and the 71 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.64 expected by chance when nothing has changed — though most of them describe this one underlying shift rather than independent changes. [OCC004, OCC001, DIR007, DIR001, LAG010, REL007, REL006]

Limitations remain. The windows are finite samples, so a shift in the mix of process variants cannot be fully excluded — in principle the later window could over-represent a variant in which the block collapses, although the magnitudes involved (200 of 200 traces versus 1 of 200, and 60–74 traces using each new activity) make pure sampling noise implausible. The evidence also cannot show whether A14, A15 and A16 are genuinely new steps or reworked stand-ins for A21, A22 and A23 reduced from three executions to one, and a single later trace still shows the old three-activity block, so the earlier behaviour was not completely eliminated. [OCC004, OCC001, LAG010, REL025]
