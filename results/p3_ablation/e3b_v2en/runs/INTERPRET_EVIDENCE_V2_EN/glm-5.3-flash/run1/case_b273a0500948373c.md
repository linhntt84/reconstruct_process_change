### Observed differences

- The dominant change is a swap between two activity groups. A16, A17 and A18 each appear in about 37 of 200 traces in the earlier window and about 158 of 200 in the later window (participation up from roughly 19 in 100 to 79 in 100), while A19, A20 and A21 each fall from about 163 of 200 to about 42 of 200 (roughly 82 in 100 down to 21 in 100). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-activity segment following A15 is executed with a different activity group in the later window: where traces previously continued from A15 into A19, A20 and A21 (running together and alongside A25 in about 82 of 100 cases), they now continue from A15 into A16, A17 and A18 in about 79 of 100 cases, with the new group occupying the old group's position — A15 precedes it, its co-occurrence and order patterns with A25 closely match those the old group had, and it never appears together with A19, A20 or A21. The routing change out of A15 mirrors the participation changes activity by activity, the relational evidence shows the substitution directly rather than through frequencies alone, and the item count (34, against roughly 0.5 expected for an unchanged process) together with uniformly high empirical strength (each difference exceeds all but about 1 in 200 random re-splits) makes chance sampling an unlikely explanation for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL004, REL012, REL024, REL009]

The evidence cannot show whether the A19–A21 branch was actually replaced or whether two pre-existing process variants merely changed frequency — the old group still runs in about 21 of 100 later traces, and finite windows can shift the mix of variants without any structural modification. In addition, the internal order of the new group varies (both orders are observed among A16, A17 and A18, and about a third of co-occurrences with A25 are interleaved or repeated), so the exact sequencing inside the segment is not determined. [REL007, REL012, REL013, OCC004]
