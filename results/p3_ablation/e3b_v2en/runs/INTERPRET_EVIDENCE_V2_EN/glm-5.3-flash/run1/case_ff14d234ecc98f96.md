### Observed differences

- In the before window, A26, A27 and A28 each appeared in all 200 traces — essentially once per trace, in varying order — while in the later window they were observed in only about 1–2 of 200 traces each. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL031]
- In the later window, A19, A20 and A21, which were not observed before (A21 appeared in 1 of 200 before-traces), occur in about 31, 34 and 37 of 100 traces respectively (62, 67 and 74 of 200) and almost never together — any pair co-occurs in at most about 1 of 100 later traces — so essentially every later trace contains exactly one of the three. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL013, REL014, REL020]
- Routing changed accordingly: about 94% of A44's observed outgoing transitions previously went to A26, A27 or A28 (roughly one third each), while in the later window they go to A21 (about 36%), A20 (about 30%) and A19 (about 27%); the new activities in turn lead about 97% of the time to A29. [DIR007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The relations flipped in step: before, the three old activities always co-occurred (100 of 100 before-traces) and each preceded A29 in about 98–100 of 100 traces; in the later window the old pairs co-occur in about 1 of 200 traces, while A44 precedes one of A19/A20/A21 (about 30–37 of 100 traces) and that activity precedes A29. [REL031, REL033, REL036, REL038, REL018, REL019, REL024, REL025, REL029, REL030]
- The event distance from A44 to A29 shrank from 4–5 positions (about 93 of 100 before-traces at lag 4) to 2 positions (about 92 of 100 later); A44 and A29 themselves ran in every trace in both windows, with A29's mean relative position moving slightly earlier (about 0.43 to 0.40) and the new activities sitting at about one third of the trace, where the old trio used to sit. [LAG010, POS007, POS008, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- Secondarily, A11 and A12, which in the before window always co-occurred with the A26/A27/A28 block, appear in about 56 of 100 later traces (about 47 before) and precede one of the new activities in about 16–20 of 100 traces. [REL001, REL003, REL004, REL007, REL009, REL010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: in the before window, every trace executed A26, A27 and A28 once each, in varying order, between A44 and A29, whereas in the later window each trace executes exactly one of A19, A20 or A21, which leads directly on to A29. [DIR007, LAG010, REL018, REL019, REL024, REL025, REL029, REL030]

The support is direct and consistent rather than a matter of item count: A44's successor profile switched from the old trio to the new trio, the ordering A44 before chosen activity before A29 occurs only in the later window, and the A44-to-A29 distance contracted from 4–5 event positions to 2, matching the behaviour expected if a block of three always-executed activities gave way to a single chosen activity; the participation flips (200 of 200 down to 1–2 of 200, and 0 up to 62–74 of 200) agree across the occurrence, routing, position, relation and lag families, and the 70 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.78 expected if nothing had changed. [DIR007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG010, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

Competing explanations are only partly excluded. Zero participation shows only that A26, A27 and A28 were not observed in the later window, not that they were removed from the process; the traces cannot distinguish a genuine three-way exclusive choice within one process from three process variants that each use a different one of the three activities, nor determine whether A19, A20 and A21 are new activities or familiar ones under other labels. Sampling differences between the two 200-trace windows can shift variant frequencies without any change, though a fall from 200 of 200 to 1–2 of 200 traces, consistent across all indicator families, is far beyond what random re-splits produce. The few lag measurements for the old trio in the later window rest on only 1–2 observations and were not used. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL013, REL020, LAG002]
