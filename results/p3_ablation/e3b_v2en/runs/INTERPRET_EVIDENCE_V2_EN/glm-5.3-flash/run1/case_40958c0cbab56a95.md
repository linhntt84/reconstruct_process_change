### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block formed by A26, A27 and A28 — previously executed together in essentially every case between A44 and A29 — is no longer executed, and that its place in the flow is now taken by one of A19, A20 or A21, with A44 feeding into that activity and the activity leading on to A29. This rests on direct relational and routing evidence, not frequency shifts alone: A44's outgoing transitions moved from A26/A27/A28 to A19/A20/A21, A19/A20/A21 lead to A29 in about 95 of 100 observed transitions, the order A44 before A19/A20/A21 before A29 holds in about 30–37 of 100 traces for each of the three, and the A44-to-A29 distance shortened from 4–5 to 2–3 events. The new activities occupy roughly the same trace position the old block occupied and were almost never observed together, consistent with at most one of them running per case in that slot. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, REL013, REL014, REL018, REL019, REL021, REL025, REL026, REL032, REL033, REL038, REL039, REL043, REL044, REL047, REL048, LAG009, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

The number of qualifying items (81, against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process) cannot be explained by chance as a whole, and the headline participation shifts (about 200 of 200 traces down to about 3; about 2–4 traces up to 60–75) are far larger than random re-splits of stable behaviour produce. These items are not independent confirmations, however, as many describe the same underlying trace behaviour. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]

Limitations: the earlier routing of A19, A20 and A21 rests on only 2–4 transitions each, so their pre-change behaviour cannot be characterised; the roughly 3 later traces per activity that still contain A26, A27 or A28 are too few to describe, and several direction and lag profiles for these activities rest on 1–3 observations. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is sampling variation in variant frequencies, since a rare variant can appear in one window only — implausible for the main participation changes given their size, but relevant for the small-support profiles. Near-zero participation also means only that A26/A27/A28 were almost never observed in the later window, not that they were provably removed.
