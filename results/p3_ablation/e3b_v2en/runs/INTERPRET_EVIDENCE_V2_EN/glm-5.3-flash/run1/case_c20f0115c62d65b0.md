### Observed differences

- In the later window, A19, A20 and A25 are almost never executed: each appears in about 1 in 200 traces, down from about 32 in 100 for A19, 28 in 100 for A20 and 40 in 100 for A25 in the earlier window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The three activities never occurred together in either window, and in the earlier window each of them co-occurred with A27, A28 or A29 in roughly 28–40 in 100 traces; in the later window those co-occurrences have almost vanished (about 1 in 200 traces) and about 99 in 100 traces contain none of the three. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- Routing consolidated around the trio: A27 now leads to A28 in about 97 in 100 observed outgoing transitions (previously about 53 in 100, with A25, A19 and A20 sharing most of the rest), and A28 now leads to A29 in about 98–99 in 100 transitions (previously about 45 in 100, with A25, A19 and A20 as the other main targets); A27 and A28 themselves still occur in essentially every trace. [DIR004, DIR005]
- Distances shortened accordingly: A29 now follows A27 typically two events later (about 97 in 100 cases) instead of three (about 94 in 100 before), and the pairs A27→A28 and A28→A29 became almost always immediately consecutive (about 98–99 in 100, versus roughly 45–54 in 100 before). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- A29 still occurs in every trace in both windows, with only a slight shift to an earlier mean position (about 0.56 to 0.55 of the trace length). [POS004]
- The three remaining occurrences of A19, A20 and A25 (one trace each) produce single-observation successor and position profiles (for example A19→A44, A20→A08, A25→A42) that are too sparse to interpret. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the set of alternative activities A19, A20 and A25 — mutually exclusive steps that previously executed between A27/A28 and A29 in roughly 28–40 in 100 cases — is effectively skipped, leaving a direct A27 → A28 → A29 flow. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The signs are direct and consistent: the participation drop rests on 56–80 traces before versus 1 after for each activity, the co-occurrence of the trio with A27, A28 and A29 collapses in parallel, and the A27→A29 distance shortens by exactly one event, which is what dropping one intermediate activity would produce. [REL003, REL005, REL009, REL010, REL012, LAG002]

Limitations: the later window contains almost no executions of the trio, so the evidence cannot show whether these activities were removed entirely or still run in well under 1 in 100 cases, and the successor targets observed for the three remaining occurrences rest on single observations. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003] An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is sampling variation in variant frequencies, since a variant frequent in one window can be nearly absent from another by chance; here, however, a fall from 28–40 in 100 traces to 1 in 200 is difficult to explain by sampling alone. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003] Several indicators describe the same underlying trace behaviour, so their agreement is convergent rather than independent confirmation. [OCC001, DIR004, LAG002]
