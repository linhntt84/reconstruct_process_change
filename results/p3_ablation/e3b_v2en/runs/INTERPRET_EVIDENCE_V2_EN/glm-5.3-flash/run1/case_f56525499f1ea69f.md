### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the six-activity set A33, A35, A36, A37, A38, A39 — previously conditional and split into two mutually exclusive trios, each appearing in roughly half of the cases — is now executed in essentially every case, with the A33/A35/A36 trio preceding the A37/A38/A39 trio (about 97 in 100) while order within each trio remains mixed; A30 and A14/A15/A16 feed the first trio, A31 follows the second, and A04 is executed in fewer cases (about 31 versus 14 in 100) with different successors. [OCC004, OCC007, REL039, REL046, REL037, REL049, REL025, REL031, LAG003, LAG006, DIR006, OCC001, DIR002]

The support is direct and consistent: participation shifted by about 50 percentage points (97–103 of 200 traces to 197–199 of 200), relational evidence shows cross-trio co-occurrence rising from 0 to about 98 in 100 with stable ordering, successor and distance profiles move in the same direction, and the number of qualifying items (89) far exceeds the roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed — a swing of about 100 traces out of 200 is not plausibly a finite-sample fluctuation. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL039, REL046, DIR006, DIR007, LAG003, LAG006]

Limitations: the many relation items cover overlapping pairs of the same six activities, so they largely restate one underlying difference rather than confirming it independently, and the evidence cannot rule out that further variant-frequency differences between the two samples contribute to the picture. Items resting on few observations — the increases for A06 and A10, the rerouting of A02 and A08, and the A30–A31 distance profile (about 5 observations) — may reflect sampling differences rather than change. [OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003, LAG002]
