### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the path from A36 to A21 now runs through the sequence A14 → A20 → A15 in nearly every case, whereas before roughly half of the cases went from A36 directly to A21 without these three activities: their participation rose from about 47 of 100 cases to about 199 of 200, and the direct A36-to-A21 step is observed in only about 1 of 200 later cases against about 53 of 100 before. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, LAG001]

The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the participation rise rests on large samples on both sides (94 versus 199 traces), the internal order of the three activities is preserved, A36's routing moved from a near even split to almost exclusively A14, and the A36-to-A21 distance moved from immediate to four positions — matching the three activities now lying between them. The shifts in the relations of A37 and A40 to this block point the same way. All 22 qualifying items show differences larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data, and their number far exceeds the roughly 0.41 expected without a change; however, most items describe this one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR001, LAG001, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL011]

Limitations remain: the two windows are finite samples, so a shift in the mix of case types (variant frequencies) between the windows cannot be ruled out as an alternative to a modified process; the near-absence of the direct A36-to-A21 step means it was essentially not observed in the later window, not that it is impossible; and the exact placement of A37 and A40 relative to the block is only partially characterised (for example, A37 appears on both sides of A14 in about 46 of 100 later cases).
