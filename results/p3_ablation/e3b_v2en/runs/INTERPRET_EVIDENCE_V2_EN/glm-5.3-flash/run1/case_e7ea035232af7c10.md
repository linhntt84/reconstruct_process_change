### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the exclusive branch after A26 was replaced by a fixed sequence in the later window: traces previously ran either A27 or the A36/A37/A42 group (the three always together, in varying order) after A26, whereas they now run the group directly after A25 and before A28, and A27 always after A26, with both blocks still converging on A31. The support is direct and consistent: order relations flip exactly where this requires (A26 and A28 switch from preceding to following the group; A27 and the group switch from mutual exclusion to near-universal, fixed-order co-occurrence), successor profiles change at A25, A26 and all three group members, and the A25–A28 distance moves from one to about four positions, as expected if the three-activity group now sits between them; participation, position and distance indicators all move in the same direction across 200 traces per window, and the 45 evidence items far exceed the roughly 0.7 expected for an unchanged process. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Limitations: the reconstruction rests on pairwise relations and event positions rather than observed end-to-end traces, so behaviour between A28 and A26 — a positional gap in both windows — is not directly covered, and a few distance profiles rest on a single observation (A25–A36 and A36–A28 before; A37–A31 and A42–A31 after, where the group-to-A31 distance now exceeds five steps in almost all traces). [LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, POS002, POS004] Sampling differences in variant frequencies remain a competing explanation for any single item, since a rare variant can appear in one window only, but they are difficult to reconcile with the joint pattern — notably two activities that never co-occurred in 200 before-traces co-occurring in about 199 of 200 after-traces; several items also describe the same underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations.
