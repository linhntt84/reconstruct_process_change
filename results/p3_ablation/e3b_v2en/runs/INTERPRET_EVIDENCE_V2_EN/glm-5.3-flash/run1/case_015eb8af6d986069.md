### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the always co-occurring activities A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26 and A25 execute in a fixed sequence — A31, then A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25, followed by A32 — whereas previously their relative order varied substantially (A24 typically preceded A20 and A23, and A23 often led directly to A32). Separately, A08 is executed in far fewer cases (about 29 in 100 traces versus 72) and, when executed, no longer leads almost exclusively to A16 [REL019, DIR009, DIR003, OCC001].

The order change rests on direct relational, directional and positional evidence that agrees across many activity pairs, each profile backed by roughly 200 traces in both windows, and the shifts are near-total rather than gradual; each listed difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the data, and 82 qualifying items against about 1 expected without a change cannot be explained by chance as a whole [REL014, LAG006, DIR007, POS004].

Limitations: the many relation items involving A08 largely restate one participation drop and are not independent confirmations; the rise of A10 and the routing changes at A02 and A04 rest on roughly 15–50 observations and could reflect sampling differences in variant frequencies; and the evidence cannot establish whether the A08 decline and the resequencing are one change or two, nor why the block's order became fixed — A08's low participation also does not show that it was removed from the process [OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, REL004].
