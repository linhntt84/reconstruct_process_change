### Observed differences

- In the later window, A36, A37 and A42 each appear in only about 47–48 of 100 traces, down from every one of the 200 earlier traces; A27 drops from every trace to about 54 in 100. None of these activities repeats more than before, so this is reduced participation, not extra repetition. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- The order between the A36/A37/A42 block and A26/A28 is reversed. Earlier, A36, A37 and A42 each preceded A26 in about 98–99 of 100 traces and preceded A28 in about 99 of 100; in the later window A26 precedes each of them in about 46–47 of 100 traces and A28 precedes each of them in about 46–47, while the previous orders are almost never seen. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016]
- A27 and the A36/A37/A42 block, which previously always occurred together (block before A27 in about 99–100 of 100 traces), are now almost mutually exclusive: they co-occur in at most about 1 of 100 traces, and each runs without the other in roughly half the traces. [REL011, REL012, REL013]
- Routing changed accordingly. A25's outgoing transitions, previously spread over A42 (about 35%), A36 (about 34%) and A37 (about 31%) with no transition to A28 observed, now go to A28 in about 99 of 100 cases. A26's transitions to A27 fall from about 97% to about 53%, replaced by transitions to A36 (about 14%), A37 (about 16%) and A42 (about 17%). A36, A37 and A42 now lead to A31 in about 28–36% of their outgoing transitions, where they previously led to A28 (about 26–33%, now unobserved). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- Placement shifts match: A28 now appears earlier in the trace (mean relative position about 0.45 → 0.39) and directly after A25 — the A25→A28 gap was 4–5 steps in about 94 of 100 traces before and is 1 step in about 99 of 100 after. A26 moves earlier (about 0.60 → 0.54) while A36/A37/A42 move later (about 0.40 → 0.58); A27 (0.62 → 0.58) and A31 (0.65 → 0.63) shift slightly earlier. [LAG001, POS004, POS002, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS003, POS005]
- Overall shape: before, all eight activities (A25, A26, A27, A28, A31, A36, A37, A42) ran in every trace; in the later window A25, A26, A28 and A31 still do, while in almost every trace exactly one of two alternatives runs — A27 alone or the A36/A37/A42 block. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the A36/A37/A42 block was repositioned and made conditional: it previously always executed between A25 and A28, before A26 and A27, and in the later window it executes after A26 as an alternative branch to A27, running in about 47 of 100 traces, while A28 directly follows A25 and A36, A37 and A42 now lead to A31 instead of to A28. [REL006, REL014, REL011, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, OCC002]

Supporting signs:
- The order changes are shown directly by relational evidence, not inferred from marginal counts: A26 and A28 now precede the block in about 46–47 of 100 co-occurrences where they previously followed it in about 98–99 of 100. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016]
- The branch structure is shown directly: A27 and the block are mutually exclusive, and A26's outgoing transitions split between A27 (about 53%) and the block (about 47% combined). [REL011, REL012, REL013, DIR002]
- Successor and distance changes corroborate the repositioning: A25 routes to A28 in about 99 of 100 transitions at a gap of 1 step (previously 4–5 steps, with no A25→A28 transition observed), and the block's transitions to A28 disappeared in favour of A31. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001]
- Fifty items qualified where the screening configuration expects roughly 0.7 for an unchanged process, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, so chance re-splitting cannot account for the volume of flagged behaviour as a whole. [screening, OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001, LAG001]

Limitations: the 200-trace before window could have missed an existing rare variant — if the later pattern existed before at well under 1% frequency (0 of 200 traces), the change would be a large shift in variant mix rather than a newly introduced path, and the evidence cannot separate these. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004] Many items also describe the same traces — the A36/A37/A42 changes appear across the occurrence, routing, position and relation indicators alike — so their agreement is not independent confirmation. [OCC002, DIR003, POS006, REL006] Lag results resting on a single observation in one window were not used in this judgement. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011]
