### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the single-choice branch after A39 — exactly one of A14, A15 or A16 executing before A24 — no longer runs, and that A21, A22 and A23 now all execute in every case, in varying order between A39 and A24. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, REL019, REL020, REL023, REL021, LAG001]

The support is direct and consistent: 48 evidence items qualified against roughly 0.5 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits produce, and every item's difference exceeds those of essentially all reference comparisons (strength about 0.99). The participation flips rest on large supports (about 70, 59 and 71 of 200 traces before versus 0 of 200 after; 0 of 200 versus 200 of 200), the routing change at A39 is observed with full support in both windows, and the relational and lag evidence shows the new co-occurrence, the roughly 50/50 ordering among A21, A22 and A23, and the A39-to-A24 distance growing from 2 to 4 steps, matching the two additional activities before A24. [DIR007, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL019, REL020, REL023, LAG001, POS007]

Limitations remain: the evidence describes behaviour, not the underlying model, so it cannot distinguish removal of the A14/A15/A16 branch from a rerouting that merely leaves it unobserved — zero participation strictly means only that these activities did not appear in the later sample. Chance differences in variant frequencies between finite windows cannot plausibly explain flips of this size (activities with 30–36 in 100 participation vanishing; previously absent activities appearing in all 200 traces), but finer details, such as whether the order of A21, A22 and A23 is exactly uniformly random, are not established. The many items also largely reflect this one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations.
