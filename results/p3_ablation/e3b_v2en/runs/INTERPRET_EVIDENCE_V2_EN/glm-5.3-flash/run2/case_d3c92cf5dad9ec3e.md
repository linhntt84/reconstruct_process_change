### Observed differences

- The relative order of two groups of activities is reversed between the windows. In the earlier window, A19, A21 and A24 sit around the first third of the trace (mean relative position about 0.34–0.36) while A20, A22 and A23 sit around the last third (about 0.63–0.64); in the later window the groups have exchanged positions, with A19/A21/A24 near 0.66–0.68 and A20/A22/A23 near 0.38. [POS001, POS003, POS006, POS002, POS004, POS005]
- Pairwise order reverses coherently across all 21 measured activity pairs. For example, A20 follows A25 in about 99.5 in 100 traces before and precedes it in about 99.5 in 100 after, with the two co-occurring in every trace in both windows; A19 precedes A20, A22, A23, A25 and A29 in about 30 in 100 traces before and follows them in about 33 in 100 after; A21 and A24 reverse against the other group in the same direction. [REL008, REL015, REL018, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL010, REL014, REL020]
- Outgoing routing moves with the reversal. Before, A19, A21 and A24 lead to A25 in about 95–98 in 100 of their outgoing transitions; in the later window they lead to A30 in about 95–98 in 100. A20, A22 and A23 route to A30 in about 29–37 in 100 before and to A25 in about 27–37 in 100 after. A29's outgoing transitions shift from A20/A23/A22 (about 37/33/30 in 100) to A24/A21/A19 (about 38/29/32 in 100), and A40's shift from A21/A19/A24 to A22/A20/A23. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, DIR007, DIR008]
- Step distances change consistently where both windows have data: A29 reaches A30 mostly 4 steps later before and mostly 2 steps later after, and A40 reaches A25 mostly 2 steps later before and mostly 4 steps after, each based on about 200 co-occurrences. Other lag findings (A21/A23/A24 to A25, A29 to A21, A40 to A20/A22/A23) rest on only 1–2 observations in one window and carry little weight. [LAG005, LAG009, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]
- Participation is essentially unchanged: A20, A22, A23, A25 and A29 appear in all traces in both windows, and A19, A21 and A24 in roughly 30–40 in 100 traces in both windows, so the dominant change is ordering rather than how often activities run. [POS002, POS004, POS005, POS007, POS008, POS001, POS003, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two blocks of activities exchanged their place in the sequence: in the later window, A20–A22–A23 run in the early part of the trace and A19–A21–A24 in the late part, the reverse of the earlier window, while A25 and A29 remain between the blocks (mean position about 0.38 to 0.45 and 0.57 to 0.64). The routing changes follow the same rearrangement: A19/A21/A24 now exit to A30 where they previously exited to A25, A20/A22/A23 take the opposite exit, and A29 and A40 now lead into the block that sits opposite them. [REL008, REL015, REL018, POS007, POS008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR007, DIR008]

The order change is shown directly by relational and positional evidence rather than inferred from frequency shifts, and it is consistent across every measured pair, with near-complete flips on about 200 traces per window (for example, A22 versus A25 goes from about 99 in 100 "A25 first" to about 99.5 in 100 "A22 first" while co-occurring throughout). Screening produced 46 qualifying findings against fewer than 1 expected if nothing had changed, so chance alone cannot account for the set as a whole, and each finding also exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits; the findings nevertheless overlap heavily and are not independent confirmations. [REL015, REL016, POS001, POS002, DIR002, LAG005, LAG009, DIR001-DIR008, POS001-POS008, REL001-REL021, LAG001-LAG009]

The evidence cannot rule out that the flip reflects a complete change in the mix of two pre-existing variants, one running each order, rather than a re-routing within a single behaviour; the windows are finite samples, so variant frequencies can differ between them. The weakly supported lag items (1–2 observations in one window) were not relied upon, unlike the order and routing findings, which each rest on roughly 200 observations.
