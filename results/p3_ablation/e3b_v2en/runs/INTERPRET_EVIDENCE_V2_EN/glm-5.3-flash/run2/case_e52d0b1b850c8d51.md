### Observed differences

- In the later window, three activities that appeared in every one of the 200 earlier cases now appear in only about 53–54 in 100 cases: A19 (200 → 107 cases), A20 (200 → 106) and A25 (200 → 108). [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a large share of executions bypass A06 and run without the A19/A20/A25 block, with A14 handing over mainly directly to A15: about 46–47 in 100 later cases omit A19, A20 and A25 together, about 74 in 100 omit A06, and A14's outgoing transitions to A06 fell from about 69 to about 12 in 100 while those to A15 rose to about 78. The drops are synchronised at roughly 45–47 percentage points, the joint-absence pattern shows the block is skipped as a unit while its internal order is preserved when it does run, and the routing changes at A14 and A41 point the same way. With 200 cases per window and 59 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected for an unchanged process, sampling variation cannot plausibly account for this ensemble. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL010, REL016, REL022, DIR005, DIR008, LAG008]

Limitations: the two windows cannot show whether this bypass is a newly added path or a pre-existing variant that was simply not exercised earlier — A19/A20/A25 appeared in 100% of earlier cases, so any prior skip variant was below roughly 1–2 in 200 cases, and a shift in the mix of executed variants (rather than a structural modification) remains a competing explanation. It is also unclear whether A06 and the block are skipped by one decision or separate ones, the many relational items partly restate the same trace behaviour rather than confirming it independently, and the increased participation of A04, A08 and A10 rests on modest counts (about 8–40 cases).
