### Observed differences

- In the later window, A19, A20 and A25 run much earlier in each case: their mean relative position falls from about 0.60 to about 0.32–0.34, while their participation is essentially unchanged (present in roughly 31–39 of 100 traces in both windows). [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- Their order relative to A26 and A27 reverses. Before, A26 or A27 came first in about 31–38 of 100 co-occurrence cases and the reverse order was essentially never seen; in the later window A19, A20 or A25 come first in about 30–35 of 100 and the old order has all but disappeared. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Routing around these activities is rewired. Before, about 85 of 100 outgoing transitions of A45 went directly to A26, while A19, A20 and A25 led mostly to A28 and A32; in the later window A45 leads to A19, A20 or A25 (about 27–29 of 100 each), and these three activities lead to A26 in about 88–94 of 100 of their outgoing transitions. [DIR006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The late tail tightens into a chain: A27 now leads to A28 in about 98 of 100 transitions (before about 47) and A28 leads to A32 in about 96 of 100 (before about 50); the A27→A32 distance shrinks from mostly three steps to mostly two, and A28→A32 from roughly half one step apart to almost all. [DIR004, DIR005, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]
- A26, A27 and A28 still occur in every trace and only shift slightly later (mean relative position up about 0.02–0.03); the A45→A26 distance grows from mostly one step to mostly two or three. [POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG007]
- Two further differences: A15 still appears in every trace but repeats less (mean occurrences per trace about 1.9 before versus 1.6 after; recurrence in about 47 of 100 traces before versus 38 after), and the ordered pairs A19→A32, A25→A28 and A25→A32, which had 76, 33 and 61 observations before, nearly vanish in the later window (1–3 observations), so the gap lengths seen for them afterwards rest on too few cases to interpret. [OCC001, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment formed by A19, A20 and A25 now executes early in the case, between A45 and A26, whereas it previously executed late, after A26 and A27: in the later window A45 routes into A19, A20 or A25 instead of directly to A26, those three activities then lead on to A26, and their mean position moves from about 0.60 to about 0.32 while A26, A27 and A28 keep their places and the A45→A26 distance lengthens. The order reversal is observed directly in six activity pairs, each backed by roughly 60–78 co-occurrence cases out of 200 traces, and the redirection of outgoing transitions, the position shifts and the longer A45→A26 distance all point the same way; with 26 qualifying items against roughly 0.6 expected for an unchanged process, chance as a whole cannot account for the pattern. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG007]

Limitations: the three activities behave almost identically, so most items describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot show whether they moved as one segment or changed separately; three lag items rest on only 1–3 later-window observations. Sampling differences in variant frequencies cannot be fully excluded, although a complete reversal of order across all six pairs is difficult to produce by sampling alone. The reduced repetition of A15 is a separate behavioural change that this hypothesis does not cover. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, OCC001]
