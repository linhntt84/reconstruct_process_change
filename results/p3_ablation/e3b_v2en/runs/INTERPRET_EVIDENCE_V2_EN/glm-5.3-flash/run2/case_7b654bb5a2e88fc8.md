### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the activities A14, A15 and A20 were relocated between the windows: in the earlier window they ran between A40 and A21 (A40 fed one of the three, and each of the three fed A21), while in the later window A40 leads directly to A21 and the trio appears after A22 and A23, routing into A23 and A27. This is shown directly rather than inferred from frequencies alone: the pairwise order of the trio against A21, A22 and A23 reverses, the trio's mean position moves from about a third to about three-fifths through the trace at near-stable participation, successors change at exactly the boundary activities (A40, A14/A15/A20, A22, A23), and the step distances move consistently with the trio being placed between A22/A23 and A23/A27 (A40→A21 from two steps to one; A22→A27 from two to three; A22→A23 and A23→A27 split between one and two steps). With 24 items against roughly 0.4 expected without a change, and every item's difference exceeding about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, chance alone cannot account for the pattern as a whole. The main competing explanation that remains is a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants rather than a structural modification: both routings could have existed before, with the later window of 200 traces sampling almost exclusively one of them. The completeness of the flips (for example, every observed outgoing transition of A14 went to A21 in the earlier window across 76 traces, and none did in the later window across 61) makes pure sampling noise implausible, but a systematic variant-frequency shift cannot be ruled out.
