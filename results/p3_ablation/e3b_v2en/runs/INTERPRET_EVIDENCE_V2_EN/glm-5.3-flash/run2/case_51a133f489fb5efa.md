### Observed differences

- In the later window, A08 appears in about 30 in 100 traces (59 of 200) versus about 13 in 100 before (26 of 200), and when it occurs it sits earlier in the case (mean relative position about 0.34 versus 0.49). [OCC001, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that flow around the A19–A20–A25 block was rerouted and resequenced: in the later window these activities run early in the case and pass control almost exclusively to A26 instead of A28/A32, A27 leads to A28 and A28 to A32 as a sequential chain, A45 feeds into A19/A20/A25 and A08 instead of going directly to A26, and A08 participates about twice as often, earlier, immediately before A19/A20/A25. This is shown directly by the successor profiles of the source activities and corroborated by reversed order relations, shifted step distances and earlier positions; 40 qualifying items against roughly 0.7 expected if nothing had changed is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces, the core routing items rest on about 60–70 observed transitions each (about 200 for A27, A28 and A45), and each of these differences exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL005, REL007, REL009, LAG007, LAG010, LAG013, LAG014]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a large shift in the mix of pre-existing variants — many more traces following the A19/A20/A25→A26 route — rather than a new structural connection, since only aggregate windows are observed. Many items describe the same underlying rerouting and are not independent confirmations, a few qualified items are behaviourally trivial (A48 and A50 routing is essentially unchanged), and some lag items rest on only 1–2 observations, but the conclusion does not rest on those.
