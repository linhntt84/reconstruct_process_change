### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new step, chosen among A19, A20 and A25, now executes between A27 and A29 in essentially every later case — directly between A27 and A28 in about half of the cases and between A28 and A29 in about 4 in 10. The placement is shown directly rather than inferred from frequencies alone: each new activity always follows A27 and precedes A29, the A27→A29 distance grew from two to three steps, and the successor profiles of A27 and A28 now include the new activities. The 28 qualifying items are about 50 times the roughly 0.56 expected without a change, more than a stable process typically produces, although most items describe this one underlying difference rather than separate changes. Limitations remain: zero participation earlier means only that A19, A20 and A25 were not observed in the first 200 traces, so an earlier participation of up to roughly 1–2 in 100 cannot be excluded, and the exact routing shares (about 29/41/30 across the three activities, and the roughly 52-versus-41 split of the insertion point around A28) carry sampling uncertainty; the near-zero co-occurrence of the three new activities (about 0.5 in 100) is consistent with, but does not prove, a strict three-way exclusive choice.
