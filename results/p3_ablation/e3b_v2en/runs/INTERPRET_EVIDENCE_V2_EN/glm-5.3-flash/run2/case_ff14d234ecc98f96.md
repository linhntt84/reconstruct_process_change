### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the three activities A26, A27 and A28 each ran in every trace (200 of 200); in the later window they appear in only 1–2 of 200 traces each. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- In the later window, three previously near-absent activities appear: A19 in about 31 in 100 traces, A20 in about 34 and A21 in about 37 (earlier: 0–1 of 200). They hardly ever co-occur (at most about 1 in 100 traces contains two of them), and their shares add up to roughly every trace, so a later-window trace typically contains exactly one of the three. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL013, REL014, REL020]
- Routing moved with them: about 94 in 100 of A44's outgoing transitions previously went to A26, A27 or A28 (roughly 33, 28 and 32 in 100), while in the later window about 93 in 100 go to A19, A20 or A21 (roughly 27, 30 and 36 in 100); the new activities' outgoing transitions lead to A29 in about 97 in 100 cases. [DIR007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The order around the new activities is fixed: A44 precedes A19, A20 or A21, and the new activity precedes A29, in essentially every trace where the pairs co-occur; earlier, A44 preceded the trio and the trio preceded A29 in virtually every trace. [REL018, REL019, REL024, REL025, REL029, REL030, REL033, REL034, REL036, REL037, REL038, REL039]
- The event distance from A44 to A29 shrank from about 4 steps (93 in 100 traces) to about 2 steps (92 in 100), and the new activities sit at the same relative position the trio used to occupy (mean about 0.36 vs about 0.37); A29's mean position moved slightly earlier (about 0.43 to 0.40) and A44's slightly later (about 0.31 to 0.33), consistent with a shorter middle segment, while A29 and A44 themselves remain in every trace. [LAG010, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]
- A11 and A12, which earlier appeared only together with A26 and almost always before it (about 47 in 100 traces), now usually appear without the old trio (about 56 in 100 traces contain A11) and precede A19, A20 or A21 whenever they coincide (about 16–20 in 100 traces). [REL004, REL010, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL007, REL008, REL009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A44 and A29, executed in every case, was restructured: the trio A26, A27 and A28, which always ran together there, is in the later window replaced by a single activity chosen per case from A19, A20 and A21 in roughly equal shares, with A44 routing into the chosen activity, the chosen activity routing into A29, and A11/A12 now preceding the new activity instead of A26. This is a possibility supported by the evidence, not a confirmed modification. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, REL018, REL019, REL024, REL025, REL029, REL030, LAG010]

Supporting signs: 70 evidence items were flagged against roughly 0.78 expected for an unchanged process, so the collection as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits produce, even though the items largely describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations; the participation flips rest on all 200 traces per window; the routing shift is measured on about 200 outgoing transitions of A44 in each window; and the placement change is shown directly by relational evidence and by the two-step shortening of the A44-to-A29 distance, not merely by frequency changes. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, REL018, REL019, LAG010]

Competing explanation and limitations: a stable process whose case mix contains both an old-trio and a new-singleton variant could in principle produce windows like these only if each 200-trace window drew almost entirely from a different variant; the reference comparisons make differences this large unusual, but a sampling or mix explanation cannot be fully excluded. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006] In addition, the residual later-window appearances of A26, A27 and A28 (1–2 traces each) and their routing and lag profiles rest on only 1–2 observations, so the behaviour of the remaining old path is essentially uncharacterised. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]
