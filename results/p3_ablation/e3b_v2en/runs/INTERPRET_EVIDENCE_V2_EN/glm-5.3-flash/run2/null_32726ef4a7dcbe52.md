### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A36 and A37 exchanged their typical order: in the later window the segment runs roughly A32 → A36 → A37 and then continues to A33, whereas before it ran roughly A32 → A37 → A36 → A33. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

The pattern is direct and internally consistent: the successor shares of A36 and A37 moved in mirror image, the distances from A32 to the two activities exchanged (A36 from typically two steps to one, A37 from one to two), and the ordered pair A36→A37 became both more frequent and uniformly immediate; each item is unusual for random re-splits of the pooled data (empirical strength about 0.995) and rests on roughly 90 observations each (the A36→A37 pair on 33 and 56 traces), and seven qualifying items far exceed the fewer than one expected by chance in an unchanged process. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, LAG001]

Limitations: the windows are finite samples of 200 traces each, so a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants (both orders already present, drawn in different proportions per window) could produce a similar picture and cannot be ruled out; the rare successor targets appearing or disappearing at about 1 in 100 are consistent with such sampling variation. [DIR002, LAG004] The A07/A14 difference — higher participation and a tighter A07→A14 gap — is a separate behavioural change this hypothesis does not cover, and with only two items it may likewise reflect variant-frequency sampling rather than a distinct structural change. [POS001, LAG001]
