### Observed differences

- In the later window, six activities — A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 — each appear in about 197–199 of 200 traces (roughly 99 in 100), whereas in the earlier window each appeared in only about 97–103 of 200 (about half). [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009]
- In the earlier window these six formed two mutually exclusive triples: cross-triple pairs such as A33–A37 or A36–A39 never co-occurred (0 of 200 traces), with one triple or the other present in roughly a 50/50 split; in the later window they co-occur in about 98 of 100 traces, with A33/A35/A36 preceding A37/A38/A39 in about 97–98 of those, while order within each triple stays roughly 50/50. [REL037, REL039, REL044, REL046, REL048, REL051]
- Routing around the block changed: A30's observed outgoing transitions moved from a fairly even spread over all six activities (about 14–18% each) to a concentration on A35, A33 and A36 (about 37%, 30% and 30%), and A33/A35/A36 gained outgoing transitions to A37/A38/A39 (about 8–12% each) that were not observed before, while their transitions to A31 fell from about 30–36% to under 1%. [DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009]
- Distances grew: A31 followed A33/A35/A36 within 1–3 positions before, but 4–5 positions later in nearly all observed pairs in the after window; A37/A38/A39 similarly now follow A30 at a distance of 4–5 positions rather than 1–3. [LAG003, LAG005, LAG006, LAG008]
- A14, A15, A16 and A30 appear in every trace in both windows; in the later window A14, A15, A16 and A30 each precede A33/A35/A36 in about 97–99 of 100 traces (before about 47–51 in 100, simply because the latter were present only half the time). [REL007, REL013, REL019, REL025]
- A04 became less frequent, appearing in about 14 of 100 traces in the later window versus about 31 of 100 before, and its outgoing transitions spread out (A16 falling from about 81% to about 36% of its observed outgoing transitions, with new shares for A15 at about 18% and A48 at about 14%); A06 and A10 roughly quadrupled, from about 3 and 2–3 of 100 traces to about 11 and 12 of 100. [OCC001, DIR002, OCC002, OCC003]
- Typical event positions shifted consistently with the new block: A30 and A33/A35/A36 occur somewhat earlier (mean relative position about 0.67 to 0.62, and about 0.75 to 0.68), while A31 and A37/A38/A39 occur slightly later (about 0.80 to 0.82, and about 0.73 to 0.77). [POS004, POS005, POS006, POS009, POS011]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the earlier either/or routing between two activity triples was replaced by a single sequential pass through both: before, a case typically ran either the A33/A35/A36 triple (with A31 following within a few steps) or the A37/A38/A39 triple, each in about half of the traces; in the later window nearly every case runs A33/A35/A36 in varying order, then A37/A38/A39, with A31 following the first triple four to five positions later. [OCC004, OCC009, REL039, REL046, LAG006]

Supporting signs: screening flagged 89 items where about 1 would be expected if nothing had changed, so chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole; the sequence change is shown directly by relational evidence (cross-triple co-occurrence rising from 0 to about 98 of 100 traces with a fixed group order, and new observed transitions from A33/A35/A36 to A37/A38/A39 while transitions from these activities to A31 disappeared); the occurrence, routing, lag and position indicators all move in the same direction, and every flagged item's difference exceeds those of 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [REL039, REL048, DIR007, DIR009, OCC004, OCC009]

Limitations: the two windows are finite samples, so a pure shift in the mix of pre-existing variants cannot be ruled out, although the before-window co-occurrence of exactly 0 across six cross-triple pairs in 200 traces makes a hidden sequential variant unlikely; some flagged profiles rest on few observations (for example the A02 and A08 outgoing profiles, and the A30–A31 lag with only 5 after-window observations) and were not relied on here, and many items describe the same underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR003, LAG002]
