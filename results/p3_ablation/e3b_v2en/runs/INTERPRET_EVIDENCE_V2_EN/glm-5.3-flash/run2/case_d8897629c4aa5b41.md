### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained the same consecutive chain A22 → A23 → A24: A22 always led to A23 and A23 always led to A24, both steps sat at distance 1, and A22 reached A24 at distance 2; A14, A15 and A20 were never observed. [DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the later window, A15 runs in about 41 of 100 traces and A14 and A20 in about 29–30 of 100 each, always at most once per trace; the three never occur together, and about 199 of 200 later traces contain exactly one of them (in about 1 of 200, none appears). - The former chain stretched by exactly one event: A22 → A23 is now at distance 2 in about 56 of 100 cases (distance 1 in the rest), A23 → A24 at distance 2 in about 44 of 100, and A22 → A24 at distance 3 in about 995 of 1000 cases (previously distance 2 in all). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Routing around the chain changed accordingly: A22 now leads to A23 in about 44 of 100 of its outgoing transitions (previously all of them) and to A14, A15 or A20 otherwise; A23 now leads to A24 in about 57 of 100 (previously all) and to one of the new activities otherwise; each new activity in turn leads to A23 in roughly 53–58 of 100 of its outgoing transitions and to A24 in the remainder. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the process executes an additional step inside the previously fixed A22 → A23 → A24 sequence: in nearly every trace, exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities is added — A15 in about 41 of 100 traces, A14 in about 29 and A20 in about 30 — placed either directly after A22, before A23 (about 56 of 100 insertions), or between A23 and A24 (about 44 of 100), while the original chain activities continue to run in every trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, DIR004, DIR005, POS004]

The support rests on direct relational and positional evidence rather than on frequencies alone: A22 precedes each new activity in every co-occurrence, each new activity precedes A24 in every co-occurrence, both orders occur against A23, and the A22→A23, A23→A24 and A22→A24 distances each grew by exactly one position, matching a single inserted event. The volume of evidence also exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces: 27 items qualified where about 0.4 would be expected by chance, and every item's difference exceeded about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled windows. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS004]

Limitations: the absence of A14, A15 and A20 from the earlier window means only that they were not observed there, so it cannot be fully excluded that these activities existed before as rare variants missed by the 200-trace sample; their scale in the later window (about 199 of 200 traces containing one of them) and the coherent ordering pattern make a pure sampling explanation difficult to sustain, but the exact shares — which alternative is chosen and where it is inserted — remain sample estimates that variant-frequency differences between samples could shift. The many agreeing items also largely describe one underlying trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
