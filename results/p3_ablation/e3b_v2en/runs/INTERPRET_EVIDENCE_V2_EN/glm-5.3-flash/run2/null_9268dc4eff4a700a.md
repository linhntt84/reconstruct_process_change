### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A30 and A31 exchanged their order in the segment between A26 and A27: in the earlier window the more common sequence ran A26 → A31 → A30 → A27, and in the later window A26 → A30 → A31 → A27. The change is shown directly by the lag distributions of all four activity pairs, which reversed between roughly 60/40 and 40/60, and consistently by the successor shares at A30 and A31, which flipped in mirror image; with seven qualifying items against about 0.33 expected without change, chance is implausible as an explanation of the evidence as a whole, though the items likely describe one underlying rerouting rather than seven independent changes. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, DIR002, DIR003]

The indicators describe aggregate shares, so the evidence cannot distinguish a reordering within individual cases from a shift in the mix of two pre-existing variants, in which both orders already occurred (the minority order accounts for roughly 40 in 100 in each window) and sampling differences in variant frequencies could contribute; in addition, the rise of A32 as a successor of A26 (about 22 to 30 in 100) is a further redistribution that the exchange between A30 and A31 alone does not explain.
