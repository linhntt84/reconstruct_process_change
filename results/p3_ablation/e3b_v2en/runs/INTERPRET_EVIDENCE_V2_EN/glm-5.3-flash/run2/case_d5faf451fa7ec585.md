### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A14, A15 and A20 each appeared exactly once in a subset of traces — A14 in about 32 of 100 traces, A15 in about 28 and A20 in about 40 — and the three never occurred together, so every trace contained exactly one of them (64 + 56 + 80 = 200). In the later window, none of the three appears in any of the 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006]

- Before, A22's outgoing transitions were spread across A23 (about 55 in 100), A20 (about 21), A14 (about 13) and A15 (about 11), and A23's across A24 (about 45) and A14/A15/A20 (about 17–19 each); the three optional activities themselves led only to A23 or A24. In the later window, A22 is always followed by A23, and A23 always by A24. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

- Distances collapsed: A22→A23 and A23→A24 were directly adjacent in roughly half of the earlier traces and two steps apart in the rest, and are now always directly adjacent; A22→A24 was always three steps apart and is now always two. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

- Relations show the same shift: A14, A15 and A20 always co-occurred with A22, A23 and A24 before (in about 28–40 of 100 traces, always placed between A22 and A24 — e.g. A14 preceded A24 in all 64 of its traces), whereas in the later window only the persistent partner of each pair remains. [REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012]

- The remaining activities shifted slightly in relative position — A22's mean position moved from about 0.46 to 0.47 of the trace and A24's from about 0.57 to 0.55 — consistent with traces that previously carried an inserted activity now being shorter. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A hypothesis supported by the evidence is that the mutually exclusive activities A14, A15 and A20 — which in the earlier window were inserted exactly once per trace between A22 and A24, before A23 in about 45 of 100 cases and after it in the rest — are no longer executed in the later window, so that every observed trace now runs directly from A22 to A23 to A24. The support is direct and consistent across indicator families: participation fell from 56–80 of 200 traces to 0 of 200 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]; the successor profiles of A22 and A23 each collapsed to a single transition [DIR004, DIR005]; all measured inter-activity distances shrank to their minimum [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]; and every relation involving the three activities reduced to the persistent partner alone [REL003, REL005, REL009, REL010, REL012]. With 28 flagged items against roughly 0.39 expected under no change, and activities present in 28–40 of 100 earlier traces vanishing from 200 later traces, sampling differences in variant frequencies are not a plausible explanation for the pattern as a whole.

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the branch still exists but is never selected: zero participation only shows that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed in the later window, so a pure routing change and an actual removal of the branch look identical here. The items also overlap heavily — the occurrence, routing, position and relation findings for these activities largely describe the same traces — so they should be read as one coherent shift rather than independent confirmations.
