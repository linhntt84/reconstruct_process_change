### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A14, A15 and A20 never appear. In the later window each occurs in a substantial share of traces and at most once per trace: A14 in about 37 in 100 traces, A20 in about 34, and A15 in about 29. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The three activities never co-occur with one another; together they appear in about 99 in 100 later-window traces, exactly one of the three per trace, so they behave as mutually exclusive alternatives. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- Routing at A36 changed: before, its outgoing flow went exclusively to A21; in the later window it goes to A14 in about 37 in 100 cases, A20 in about 34, A15 in about 29, and to A21 in only about 1 in 100. [DIR004]
- When A14, A15 or A20 occurs in the later window, all of its outgoing flow goes to A21. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- Order relations in the later window: A36 precedes each of the three in every one of their occurrences, and each of the three precedes A21; before, A21 appeared in every trace while none of the three did. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- The step distance from A36 to A21 changed from immediately consecutive (lag 1) in effectively all earlier traces to a distance of 2 in about 99 in 100 later traces. [LAG001]
- The new activities sit at roughly one third of the trace (median relative position about 0.35), and A21, still present in every trace in both windows, shifted slightly later (median about 0.36 to 0.38). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a mutually exclusive choice among A14, A15 and A20 is executed on the path between A36 and A21: one of the three is performed exactly once per trace, after A36 and before A21. This is shown directly by relational evidence (A36 preceding the chosen activity and the chosen activity preceding A21 in all of its occurrences), by the rerouting of A36 away from A21 toward the three activities and of each of the three toward A21, and by the A36-to-A21 distance growing from 1 to 2 steps. Their mutual exclusivity and single occurrence per trace are consistent with one alternative being selected each time. The volume of qualifying evidence (21 items against roughly 0.37 expected if nothing had changed) is far beyond what chance screening would produce. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, REL001, REL002, REL005]

Limitations: zero participation for A14, A15 and A20 in the earlier window means only that they were not observed there, so the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely new block from a pre-existing branch that was rarely or never executed before and became dominant later — a shift in variant frequencies between the two samples. The many items also largely describe one underlying trace pattern and are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
