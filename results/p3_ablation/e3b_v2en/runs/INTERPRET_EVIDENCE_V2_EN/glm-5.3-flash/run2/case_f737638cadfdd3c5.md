### Observed differences

- In the later window, three activities that were absent from the earlier window now take part in a large share of cases: A19 in about 74 of 200 traces (37%), A25 in about 68 of 200 (34%) and A20 in about 57 of 200 (29%). Each occurs at most once per case, at roughly one third of the trace's relative event position. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- These three activities are effectively mutually exclusive: A19 and A20 never occur in the same case, A20 and A25 never do, and A19 and A25 share only about 1 case in 200. Together they cover about 99 in 100 later cases, so almost every case now executes exactly one of them. [REL001, REL002, REL007, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- Routing out of A41 changed markedly. In the earlier window about 93 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions went directly to A26; in the later window only about 1 in 100 does, while about 34 in 100 go to A19, about 31 in 100 to A25 and about 27 in 100 to A20. [DIR004]
- The three activities lead almost exclusively to A26: about 96 in 100 of A19's outgoing transitions, 98 in 100 of A20's and 97 in 100 of A25's. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- Order and distance evidence agrees: when one of the three occurs, it co-occurs with A41 and A26 and always lies after A41 and before A26 (and before A31). The share of cases where A26 immediately follows A41 dropped from about 94 in 100 to about 1 in 100, while a two-step separation now dominates (about 90 in 100). [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL008, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL014, LAG002]
- Downstream shifts are small: A26's mean relative position moved from about 0.37 to 0.39 and A31's from about 0.69 to 0.71, and one-step A31→A35 separations fell from about 55 in 100 to 37 in 100 while two-step separations rose from about 42 to 62 in 100. [POS004, POS005, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One possibility supported by the evidence is that the path from A41 to A26, previously direct in the large majority of cases, now runs through exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities — A19, A20 or A25, chosen in roughly equal shares — with A41 handing over to that activity and the activity in turn leading to A26. The support is direct rather than circumstantial: the rerouting of A41's outgoing transitions is observed outright, the successors of the three activities point almost solely to A26, the relation evidence places each of them after A41 and before A26, and the A41→A26 separation moved from one step to two. All 28 qualifying items show differences larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits, and 28 items against roughly 0.57 expected by chance across 114 tested units cannot be explained by chance as a whole, even though many items describe the same underlying difference. [DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL003, REL006, REL011, REL014, LAG002, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that these three activities already existed in the process but were rarely or never exercised in the earlier sample: zero participation before only means they were not observed in those 200 traces, and variant frequencies can differ between finite windows even without a structural change — although a jump from 0 of 200 to about 198 of 200 cases would itself be an extreme sampling fluctuation. The small downstream shifts in A26 and A31 positions and the A31→A35 lag are consistent with the rerouting but are minor and could partly reflect sampling. Note also that the routing shares are shares of outgoing transitions, not of cases. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS004, POS005, LAG001, DIR004]
