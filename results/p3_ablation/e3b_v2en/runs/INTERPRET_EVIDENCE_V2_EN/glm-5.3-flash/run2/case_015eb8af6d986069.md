### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A08 is skipped in most cases where it previously ran, and the segment A20–A26 entered from A31 now executes in one dominant sequence (A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25, then mostly to A32) instead of in varying orders. Both differences are shown directly: A08's participation fell by about 43 in 100 traces while its partners continue unchanged, and the ordering change rests on relational evidence — order shares flipped while all six activities still co-occur in every trace, lags concentrated at one to two steps, and routing became near-deterministic. Screening also weighs in: 82 items were flagged where about 1 would be expected for an unchanged process, and every item's difference exceeds 199 of 200 random re-splits, so sampling alone cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, REL014, LAG006, DIR009, DIR013, LAG032]

The evidence cannot rule out that the later window simply runs pre-existing variants more often — for example an A08-free variant and a fixed-order variant — rather than that connections themselves were rewired. Some flagged details are weak and were not relied on: A10's increase rests on 15 versus 44 traces, several flagged lags (for example A24 to A20, A25 to A23, A26 to A22) have only one after-window observation, and the outgoing profiles of A02, A04 and A10 rest on only 14–40 transitions. Items describing the same traces, such as the thirteen A08 relations or the many lags within the A20–A26 group, are not independent confirmations.
