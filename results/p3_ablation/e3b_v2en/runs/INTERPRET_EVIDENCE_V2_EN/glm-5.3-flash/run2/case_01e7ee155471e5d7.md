### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the trio A30, A31, A36, which previously ran after A21 and immediately before A25, now runs before A22 and A21: in the later window A20 feeds into the trio, the trio's members lead to A22, and A21 leads directly to A25, whereas earlier A20 led to A22, A21 led into the trio, and the trio led to A25. This is shown directly by the near-complete order reversals with co-occurrence unchanged at 100%, and it is consistent across the successor-share changes at A20, A21 and the trio members, the opposite position shifts, and the step-distance changes, each of the key items being supported by all 200 traces per window; the 24 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.41 expected by chance when nothing has changed, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits. In addition, the six LAG items with one after-window observation cannot independently confirm distances, and the items largely describe one trace-level reordering rather than independent confirmations of it.
