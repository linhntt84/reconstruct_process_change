- In the later window, four activities that appeared in every earlier trace — A27, A36, A37 and A42 — each appear in only about half of the traces (A27 in about 54 of 100; A36, A37 and A42 in about 47–48 of 100), and their mean occurrences per trace fall from about 1.0 to about 0.5. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, every case runs through A25, A28 and A26 and then takes one of two mutually exclusive continuations: A27 followed by A31 (about 54 of 100 traces), or the block A36/A37/A42 — traversed in varying order — followed by A31 (about 47 of 100). A25, A26, A28 and A31 still appear in every trace in both windows, whereas in the earlier window A27 and the block A36/A37/A42 also appeared in every trace in one fixed order, with the block sitting between A25 and A28 rather than after A26. [OCC001, OCC002, REL011, REL020, REL010, REL017, POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005, DIR001, LAG001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL006, REL014, LAG005]

The volume of qualifying items (48, against roughly 0.73 expected if nothing had changed, each larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits) cannot be explained by sampling alone, and the reordering is shown directly by relational and successor evidence — the order reversals with A26 and A28, the new direct A25→A28 transition, and the new transitions from A26 into the block and from the block to A31 — rather than resting only on the drop in participation; the occurrence, direction, relation, position and gap indicators all describe the same restructuring, and the roughly 54/47 split agrees with the participation and co-occurrence figures. The position shifts agree with this reading but are based on event positions and would not establish movement on their own. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, LAG001, LAG005, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the share of a pre-existing variant: participation for A27, A36, A37 and A42 was exactly 1.0 in the earlier window, so an alternative continuation could have existed before at a frequency too low to appear in 200 traces, making the difference a change in how often an existing path is taken rather than a newly added one. The exact shares also carry sampling uncertainty in windows of 200 traces, many relation items restate the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations, and several gap items rest on only 1–2 observations and were not relied upon.
