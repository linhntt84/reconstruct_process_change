### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative branch in which exactly one of A19, A20 or A25 executed between A28 and A29 is no longer taken in the later window, so that traces run directly from A27 through A28 to A29. The signs agree across participation (all three activities near zero), routing (A27's and A28's outgoing transitions concentrating almost entirely on A28 and A29), relations (co-occurrence of the trio with A27, A28 and A29 falling from about 28–40 in 100 traces to about 1 in 200, while A29's participation and position stay stable), and spacing (the A27→A29 and A28→A29 distances each shortening by about one step). The volume of flagged behaviour also far exceeds the fewer-than-one item expected by chance at these thresholds. This remains a possibility supported by the evidence, not a confirmed modification. The rare later appearances of A19, A20 and A25 (one trace each) say nothing reliable about how they behave when they do occur, and zero participation only means they were not observed. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is sampling: with 200 traces per window, variant frequencies can differ between windows without any structural change, although a drop from roughly 56–80 in 200 traces to about 1 in 200, consistent across three activities and several indicators, is difficult to attribute to sampling alone. Many flagged items also describe the same underlying trace behaviour, so their agreement is not independent confirmation. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003]
