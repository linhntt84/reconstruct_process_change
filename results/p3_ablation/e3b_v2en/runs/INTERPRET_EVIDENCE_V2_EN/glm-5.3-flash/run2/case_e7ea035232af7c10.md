### Observed differences

- In the later window, A27, A36, A37 and A42 each appear in essentially every trace (about 995–1000 of 1000), whereas before they each appeared in roughly 46–54 of 100 traces; each now occurs about once per trace (mean occurrences roughly 0.46–0.55 before, about 1.0 after) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004].

- Positions shifted: A36, A37 and A42 moved earlier (mean relative position about 0.59 to about 0.40), while A28 moved later (about 0.39 to 0.46) and A26 later (about 0.54 to 0.60); A25 stayed near 0.35 [POS006, POS007, POS008, POS004, POS002, POS001].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the A36–A37–A42 fragment was repositioned: it previously ran after A26 as an alternative to A27 and before A31, and in the later window it runs directly after A25 and before A28, A26 and A27, with A27 executed in virtually every trace afterwards and A31 still following at the end [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005].

The order and placement reading rests on direct relational and positional signs: the pairwise order of the trio versus A26 and A28 reverses, previously exclusive activities now co-occur, the successor profiles of A25, A26, A36, A37 and A42 change accordingly, and the position and gap shifts (trio earlier, A28 and A26 later, the A25→A28 gap growing from one to four steps) fit the same rearrangement [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL011, REL012, REL013, DIR001, DIR002, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG001]. With 45 qualifying items against roughly 0.7 expected if nothing had changed, and relational supports near 200 traces per window, chance alone is an unlikely explanation for the pattern as a whole [OCC001, DIR001, REL006, LAG001].

Limitations remain: most items describe one underlying rearrangement rather than independent confirmations, and four lag items rest on a single observation on one side (A25→A36, A36→A28, A37→A31, A42→A31), so they were not relied on [LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]. The order among A36, A37 and A42 stays mixed in both windows, so whether they run sequentially or in parallel cannot be determined [REL020, REL021, REL022]. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is sampling variation in variant frequencies — the before window's roughly 54/46 split between the A27-variant and the trio-variant is a finite-sample estimate — although a pure frequency shift cannot explain the within-trace reversal of the trio against A26 and A28 [REL011, REL006, REL014].
