### Observed differences

- In the before window, A19, A20 and A25 each ran in every one of the 200 traces; in the later window each appears in only about 53 in 100 traces (106–107 of 200), with mean occurrences per trace falling from about 1.0 to about 0.53 [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006].
- The three disappear together: their pairwise co-occurrence drops from 100 in 100 traces to about 53 in 100, and in about 47 in 100 later traces none of them appears at all; they almost never occur without each other [REL008, REL009, REL014].
- A26, A41, A46 and A47 still occur in every trace in both windows, but in about 47 in 100 later traces they appear without any of A19/A20/A25, which never happened before [REL010, REL013, REL016, REL021].
- The entry into this segment changed: before, about 96 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions went to A19, while in the later window about 50 in 100 go to A19 and about 44 in 100 go directly to A26. When the segment does run, its internal order is unchanged — A19 before A25, A25 before A20, and A20 still leads to A26 in about 96 in 100 of its transitions [DIR005, REL008, REL009, REL014, DIR003, DIR004].
- A02 became rare and behaves differently: it appears in about 9 in 100 later traces, down from 29 in 100; it no longer follows A46 (that transition fell from about 25 in 100 of A46's outgoing transitions to under 1 in 100), so A47 now immediately follows A46 in about 97 in 100 cases, up from about 75 in 100; and when A02 does occur it sits earlier in the trace (median relative position about 0.97 before vs 0.47 after) with its successors spread over many activities instead of about 85 in 100 going to A47 — though this rests on only 17 later traces [OCC001, DIR006, REL006, LAG003, POS001, DIR001].
- Smaller shifts: A04 and A06 each appear in roughly 12–13 in 100 later traces, up from about 4–5 in 100, and A08's outgoing mix changed completely, but the latter rests on only 4 before vs 15 after transitions [OCC002, OCC003, DIR002].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment A19 → A25 → A20 has become optional and is now bypassed in roughly half of the executions: in the later window A41 routes directly to A26 in about 44 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (previously essentially never), the three segment activities run together — in their original internal order — in only about 53 in 100 traces, and they are jointly absent in the remaining about 47 in 100 while A26, A41, A46 and A47 continue in every trace [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL008, REL009, REL014, DIR005]. The participation, relational and routing signals all show the same skip directly and consistently, and the size of the shift — from 0 of 200 traces without the segment to about 94 of 200, across 41 qualifying items where roughly 0.72 would be expected for an unchanged process — is far beyond what sampling noise in two 200-trace windows produces [OCC004, REL014].

Limitations: the many relational items all describe this one underlying difference and are not independent confirmations of it, and the evidence cannot rule out that the later window simply contains a higher share of a pre-existing trace variant that omits the segment, rather than a modified process — the two look identical in these samples. The secondary observations (A02's decline and earlier, less focused placement, the upticks in A04 and A06, and A08's changed outgoing mix) rest on few observations and should be treated as tentative [OCC001, POS001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002].
