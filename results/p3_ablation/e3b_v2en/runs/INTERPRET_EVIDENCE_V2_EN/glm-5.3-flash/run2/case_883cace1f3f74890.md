### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, an exclusive three-way choice among A19, A20 and A25 executes in essentially every trace and is placed inside the A27–A29 segment — in about 55 in 100 traces between A27 and A28, and in about 43 in 100 between A28 and A29. The support is direct and consistent: relational items show A27 before and A29 after the chosen activity in nearly all co-occurrences; successor profiles show new transitions from A27 and A28 into A19/A20/A25 and from them onward to A28 and A29; and the lag shifts (A27→A29 moving from 2 to 3 steps, with both former immediate successions losing roughly half their share) match the presence of one extra step in this segment. [REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, REL004, REL008, REL011, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The volume of qualifying items (29 against roughly 0.54 expected when nothing changes) far exceeds what chance screening would produce, although many items describe the same underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A19, A20 and A25 were not observed there, and the evidence cannot rule out that the process always allowed this behaviour while a previously rare variant containing it simply became frequent — with 200 traces per window, variant frequencies can shift substantially without any structural change; the data also cannot distinguish a single choice point from three alternative variants, and the near-zero co-occurrence behind the exclusivity claim rests on small counts.
