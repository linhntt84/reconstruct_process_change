### Observed differences

- Six activities — A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 — appeared in every earlier trace (200 of 200) but in only about 47–57 in 100 later traces (93–113 of 200); their mean occurrences per trace fell from about 1.0 to about 0.47–0.57. A30 and A31 still appear in essentially every trace in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, POS001, POS002]

- In the earlier window these six formed one fixed chain: A30 preceded A33/A35/A36 in about 99–100 in 100 traces, that group preceded A37/A38/A39 in about 98–100 in 100, and A37/A38/A39 preceded A31 in about 98–99 in 100, with roughly even internal order inside each group. [REL001, REL002, REL015, REL019, REL022, REL007, REL010, REL013, REL025]

- In the later window the two groups became mutually exclusive: any cross-group pair (for example A33 with A37) occurs together in only about 2–4 in 100 traces, down from 100 in 100; about 44 in 100 later traces contain only the A33/A35/A36 group, about 53 in 100 only the A37/A38/A39 group, and no observed later trace contains neither group. [REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024]

- Each group still moves as a unit and keeps its place between A30 and A31: A33 and A35 (and A37 with A38) still co-occur whenever either occurs, and in nearly all traces where a group co-occurs with A30 or A31, A30 still comes first and A31 last. [REL013, REL018, REL025, REL001, REL004, REL007, REL010]

- Routing changed accordingly: A30's outgoing transitions, previously almost entirely to A33 (about 34%), A35 (about 36%) and A36 (about 29%), now spread roughly evenly over A33–A39 (about 14–20% each); A33, A35 and A36 now send about 33%, 34% and 24% of their outgoing transitions directly to A31, a target not observed for them earlier. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

- Pair distances shrank: A30 to A37/A38/A39, and A33/A35/A36 to A31, sat 4–5 steps apart earlier but 1–3 steps apart in about 94–98 in 100 of later co-occurrences; cross-group pairs such as A33→A37 now have only about 4–5 observable instances within five steps, versus about 196–200 earlier. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG007, LAG010, LAG005]

- Relative event positions shifted modestly (A30's mean position from about 0.62 to 0.67, A33/A35/A36 from about 0.68 to about 0.73, A37–A39 slightly earlier to about 0.74); these are position shifts consistent with shorter traces and do not by themselves show an activity was moved. [POS001, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports, as a possibility rather than a confirmed modification, that the segment between A30 and A31, which earlier always executed both middle groups back-to-back, now routes each case through exactly one of them: in the later window about 44 in 100 traces run only A33/A35/A36, about 53 in 100 run only A37/A38/A39, and about 3–4 in 100 still run both, so each middle group is skipped in roughly half of the cases while A30 and A31 remain in every case. [REL013, REL015, REL019, REL022, REL025, OCC001, OCC004, POS001, POS002]

The support is direct and consistent: the relational items show cross-group co-occurrence collapsing while within-group cohesion and each group's placement between A30 and A31 are preserved; the routing items show A30 now feeding both groups and new direct transitions from A33/A35/A36 to A31; participation halves for exactly the six group activities; and pair distances shrink from 4–5 steps to 1–3 steps. The volume of evidence (57 items versus roughly 0.8 expected for an unchanged process) also exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces, although many items describe the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [REL015, REL013, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, OCC003, LAG001, LAG004]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of pre-existing process variants rather than a newly introduced structure: the earlier window shows none of the single-group behaviour (participation and cross-group co-occurrence are exactly 100 in 100 there), so such variants would have been absent or too rare to surface in 200 earlier traces, and a difference in variant frequencies between the sampled windows alone could in principle produce the later picture. In addition, no outgoing-transition profile for A37/A38/A39 appears in the evidence, so their link to A31 rests on order relations, and the roughly 3–4 in 100 later traces that still execute both groups show the mutual exclusivity is not complete. [REL015, OCC001, REL010, REL011, REL012]
