### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the span between A44 and A29 is executed differently in the later window: traces run all three of A26, A27 and A28, in varying order, between A44 and A29, where they previously ran exactly one of A19, A20 or A21; A19, A20 and A21 are now almost never observed, and A10 participation is reduced. The signs are direct and consistent across indicator families: A44 now precedes the new trio, the trio precedes A29, A44's successors changed accordingly, the trio occupies the old trio's relative position, and the A44–A29 distance grew by about two steps, fitting a longer block of activities between the two. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL013, REL033, REL034, DIR009, POS005, LAG005]

The volume of qualifying items (74, against roughly 0.8 expected for an unchanged process) cannot be explained by chance as a whole, but most items restate one underlying difference: the many activity-pair results largely reflect the same trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations. [OCC002, OCC005, REL013, REL033]

Limitations: the later routing and positions of A19, A20 and A21 rest on a single observed trace each, so their current behaviour cannot be characterised, and A10's reduction is partly the same underlying behaviour, since it often co-occurred with the old trio. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of process variants — a variant containing A26/A27/A28 becoming dominant while a variant carrying A19/A20/A21 fades — which would look identical in these window-level aggregates; with 200 traces per window, sampling differences in variant frequencies remain possible. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG002, REL001, OCC002, OCC005]
