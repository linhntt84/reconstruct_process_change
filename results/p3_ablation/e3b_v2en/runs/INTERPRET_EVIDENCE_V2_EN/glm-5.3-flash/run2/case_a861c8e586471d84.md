### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 each took part in roughly a third of the traces (71, 61 and 70 of 200 traces) and behaved as mutually exclusive alternatives: each pair co-occurred in at most 1 of 200 traces, and their shares sum to about 100 in 100 traces, so virtually every earlier trace contained exactly one of them. In the later window, A19 and A25 appear in no trace at all, and A20 appears in a single trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL007, REL009, REL014]

- This triple sat between A41 and A26. Earlier, about 33, 27 and 33 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions led to A19, A20 and A25 respectively (about 93 in 100 combined), while about 97–99 in 100 of the outgoing transitions of A19, A20 and A25 led to A26; A41 preceded each of the three, and each of the three preceded A26, in roughly 30–35 in 100 traces. In the later window, about 92 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions lead directly to A26. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL010, REL012, REL015, REL017, REL019, REL021]

- The distance from A41 to A26 shortened accordingly: earlier the two were typically two event positions apart (about 93 in 100 cases, i.e. exactly one event in between), whereas in the later window they are typically one position apart (about 92 in 100 cases). [LAG002]

- Co-occurrence of the triple with surrounding activities essentially disappeared: A14 co-occurred with A19, A20 or A25 in about 30–36 in 100 earlier traces but in at most 1 of 200 afterwards, and the same holds for A12 (about 11–16 in 100 before) and A24 (about 16–17 in 100 before). A12 and A24 themselves now appear somewhat more often (from about 42 to 51 and from about 48 to 56 in 100 traces), while A14, A26, A31 and A41 still appear in every trace in both windows. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL008, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL018]

- Smaller shifts are consistent with traces shortening by about one event: A26's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (from about 39% to 37% of the trace length), A14's and A41's moved slightly later (about 14% to 15% and 32% to 33%), and the typical gap between A24 and A31 shrank from mostly two steps to mostly one step. [POS001, POS005, POS006, LAG001]

- The single later occurrence of A20 is atypical: it sits at about 62% of the trace (versus about 36% earlier) and is followed by A30 rather than A26, but this rests on one observation. [OCC002, DIR002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One possibility supported by the evidence is that the alternative segment consisting of one of A19, A20 and A25 between A41 and A26 is no longer executed in the later window: earlier, virtually every trace ran A41, then exactly one of the three, then A26, whereas afterwards A41 is followed directly by A26 in about 92 in 100 traces and none of the three is observed apart from one A20 occurrence. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, LAG002, REL010, REL012, REL019]

The support is direct and consistent across indicator families: participation of the three activities fell to zero (0 of 200 traces for A19 and A25), A41's successor profile flipped from the triple to A26, every co-occurrence and ordering relation of the triple with A14, A12, A24, A26, A31 and A41 vanished, and the A41–A26 distance shortened from two positions to one. The 36 flagged items are many times the fewer than one expected by chance at this screening level, and each item's difference exceeded those seen in more than 99 in 100 random re-splits, although most items describe this one underlying difference rather than independent changes. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR004, LAG002, REL004, REL010, REL021]

The competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is a sampling difference in variant frequencies: the later window could in principle contain almost no traces of the variant that includes the segment even if that variant still occurs. This is unlikely given that the segment appeared in essentially every earlier trace and each window covers 200 traces, but the single later A20 occurrence shows the segment may still be executed in rare cases, and zero participation strictly means only that these activities were not observed in the later window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL007, POS003]
