### Observed differences

- The relative order of two groups of activities flipped. In the earlier window, A24 came before A20 in about 75 of 100 traces and before A23 in about 88 of 100; in the later window, A20 precedes A24 in about 99 of 100 and A23 precedes A24 in about 98 of 100. All eight measured pairs between the groups {A20, A22, A23} and {A24, A25, A26} now follow the A20/A22/A23-first order in 98–99 of 100 traces, where before the order was mixed or reversed. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]

- Routing within this group became concentrated on a single successor. In the later window, A20 leads to A23 in about 99% of its observed outgoing transitions (before about 62%), A22 leads to A20 in about 99% (before about 55%), A23 leads to A24 in about 98% (before about 12%), A24 leads to A26 in about 99% (before about 56%), A26 leads to A25 in about 99% (before about 66%), A25 leads to A32 in about 93% (before about 43%), and A31 leads to A22 in all observed transitions (before about 50%, previously split with A24). [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009]

- Distances between these activities became fixed. Consecutive pairs now sit exactly one event apart in 92–100 of 100 cases (A31→A22, A22→A20, A20→A23, A24→A26, A26→A25, A25→A32), and longer pairs sit at the summed distance — for example A20→A25 four steps apart in about 99.5% of cases (before spread over one to four steps) and A22→A25 five steps apart in about 99.5% (before spread over one to five). The distances are mutually consistent with a single fixed chain A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25, A32. [LAG003, LAG005, LAG008, LAG011, LAG019, LAG022, LAG024, LAG027]

- Typical positions shifted accordingly: A20, A22 and A23 now occur slightly earlier in the trace (mean relative position down by about 0.03–0.06) and A24, A25 and A26 slightly later (up by about 0.03–0.06), while each of these activities still appears in essentially every trace in both windows. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007]

- A second, smaller set of changes concerns A08, A14 and A10. A08 appears in about 13 of 100 later traces, down from about 32 of 100; the share of A08's observed outgoing transitions going to A15 fell from about 91% to about 48%, with other successors appearing (A16 about 15%, A46 about 11%); the share of A14's outgoing transitions going to A08 fell from about 29% to about 7%; and A08–A32 co-occurrence fell from 32 to 13 of 100 traces, with A08 also tending to occur later in the trace. A10's participation rose from about 3 to about 12–13 of 100 traces. These items rest on fewer observations (26–27 after-window traces or transitions for A08; 6 before versus 25 after for A10). [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the activities A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25 and A32 are executed in one fixed order — A31, then A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25, A32 — whereas in the earlier window these activities were routed variably across several successors and their relative order differed from case to case, often placing A24/A25/A26 before A20/A22/A23. The order change is shown directly by the relation and distance indicators (for example, A24 before A20 fell from about 75 to under 1 in 100 traces, and A20→A24 at exactly two steps rose to 100 of 100), the routing change directly by the direction indicators, and both agree with the position shifts; the measured distances are mutually consistent with a single chain, and each flagged difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. The number of flagged items (58, against roughly 0.76 expected for an unchanged process) is far beyond what chance would produce for the ensemble. [REL002, REL004, REL007, DIR003, DIR005, LAG004, LAG009, POS002, POS005]

The evidence describes executed behaviour, not the underlying model, so it cannot rule out that the control flow is unchanged and only the mix of execution variants shifted — for example, an ordering that already occurred in the earlier window becoming dominant. The windows are finite samples of 200 traces each, so variant frequencies can differ without any change and rare variants can appear in one window only; several later-window distance items rest on a single occurrence, and the A08/A10 changes rest on 26–27 after-window observations, so their exact size is uncertain. The many relation and distance items also describe one underlying reordering rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, LAG016, LAG017]
