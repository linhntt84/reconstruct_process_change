### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A14, A15 and A16 each ran in roughly a third of the cases (about 70, 59 and 71 of 200 traces) and never together: every case contained exactly one of the three, always after A39 and always followed by A24. In the later window, none of the three appears in any case. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL008, REL007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- In the later window, A21, A22 and A23 each run exactly once in every case (200 of 200), whereas none of them appeared in the earlier window. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- A39's routing changed accordingly: before, its outgoing transitions led to A14, A15 and A16 (about 35%, 30% and 36%); in the later window they lead to A21, A22 and A23 (about 36%, 33% and 32%). [DIR007]
- In the later window, each of A21, A22 and A23 is followed either by A24 or by one of the other two, each option taking roughly a third of that activity's outgoing transitions. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- The three new activities co-occur in every case but in varying order: for each pair, each ordering occurs in about half the cases (for example, A21 before A22 in about 51 of 100 cases, A22 before A21 in about 49). A39 always precedes them, and they always precede A24. [REL019, REL020, REL023, REL021, REL022]
- The event distance from A39 to A24 changed from always 2 positions to always 4, and A24's mean position moved later, from about 0.39 to about 0.44 of the trace; A24 itself still occurs in every case in both windows. [LAG001, POS007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured: in the earlier window each case executed one alternative chosen from A14, A15 and A16 between A39 and A24, while in the later window all three activities A21, A22 and A23 execute in that segment in every case, in varying order, so the span from A39 to A24 grew from two to four event positions. [OCC001, OCC004, REL019, REL021, LAG001]

The support is direct and consistent across indicator families: 48 items were flagged where only about 0.5 would be expected by chance if nothing had changed; the participation changes are all-or-nothing (about 70, 59 and 71 of 200 cases falling to 0, and 0 rising to 200 of 200), a magnitude that sampling differences in variant frequencies cannot plausibly produce in 200-trace windows; and the relational and successor evidence shows the new placement directly, with every flagged difference larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [DIR007, REL021, REL022, REL019, REL023, LAG001]

Limitations remain: the evidence shows that the order of A21, A22 and A23 varies from case to case but cannot determine what governs that variation, and zero participation only means A14, A15 and A16 were not observed in the later window, so a persistence at a much lower frequency cannot be excluded; the near-50/50 order splits also carry sampling uncertainty. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL019, REL023]
