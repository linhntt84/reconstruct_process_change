### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the flow after A20 now proceeds predominantly to A21, A22 and A23 — which run together in roughly four of five cases, in varying orders — rather than to A24, A25 and A26, which are now not executed in about four of five cases. This rests on direct routing evidence at A20 (outgoing shares moving from about 82 in 100 to A24–A26 to about 79 in 100 to A21–A23), consistent relational evidence (A20 before A21–A23 up from about 18–19 to about 77–79 in 100; A20 before A24–A26 down from about 81 to about 21), and matching participation swaps across all six activities. The number of qualifying items (60) is far above the roughly one expected by chance when nothing has changed, and each difference is larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [DIR003, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]

The evidence describes executed behaviour, not the underlying model, so a competing explanation remains: both branches may have always existed and only their selection frequencies reversed, since a shift in the mix of two pre-existing variants would produce the same participation, routing and relation patterns in finite 200-trace windows. The items around A20 and the A21–A23 block describe one underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations. A10's earlier median position is positional, not clock-time, evidence and does not by itself show it was moved, and the A10 and A02 profiles rest on few later-window observations (14 and 18 traces).
