- Activity A22 ran in every before-window trace (200 of 200, once per trace); in the later window it appears in only about 51 in 100 traces (102 of 200). [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, roughly half of the executions skip A22 between A21 and A23 and instead run a block of three previously unobserved activities — A27, A28 and A33 — between them, in varying internal order, before continuing to A23. The shares line up across indicator families: A22's participation drop (about 51 in 100) mirrors the new activities' participation (about 49 in 100 each) and A21's rerouted transitions; the new activities are strictly mutually exclusive with A22; the relations place them after A21 and before A23; and the A21→A23 step distance stretches from 2 to 4 in exactly the share of traces where the block appears. The 26 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.4 expected for an unchanged process, and every item's difference is larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, LAG001]

Limitations remain: the before window contains no occurrence of A27, A28 or A33, so a pre-existing but very rare variant that 200 before-traces failed to sample cannot be fully excluded, even though a jump from undetectable to about half of executions is hard to explain by sampling alone. The evidence also does not pin down one fixed sequence within the new block (pairwise orders are nearly balanced), and the precise 49/51 split carries the sampling uncertainty of two 200-trace windows.
