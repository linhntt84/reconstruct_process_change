### Observed differences

- Routing collapsed onto a single path. In the earlier window, transitions were spread across several successors: A15 was followed by A18 in about 62 in 100 transitions but also by A21, A19 or A20 (about 12–14 in 100 each); A18 was followed by A27 in about 53 in 100 transitions; and A26 split about evenly between A19 and A17. In the later window, every observed source has essentially one successor — A26→A17, A17→A15, A15→A18, A18→A19, A19→A21, A21→A20 and A20→A27, each at about 995–1000 in 1000. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]

- Pairwise order flipped while participation stayed unchanged. A18 precedes A19 in about 995 in 1000 later traces containing both, versus about 12 in 100 earlier; A15 precedes A19 in about 995 in 1000 versus about 25 in 100; A18 precedes A21 in about 995 in 1000 versus about 30 in 100. In both windows each of these pairs still co-occurs in every trace, so no activity appeared or disappeared. [REL001, REL006, REL008]

- Gaps between activities became fixed and reverse orders vanished. A15 is followed by A18 after one step in about 995 in 1000 later cases (about 62 in 100 before); A15 by A27 after five steps in about 995 in 1000 (previously spread over two to five steps); A17 by A20 after five steps in about 995 in 1000 (previously spread over one to five). Pairs running against this order nearly disappeared: A19→A15, A19→A18, A20→A18, A21→A18 and A26→A20 each occurred in roughly 100–180 earlier traces but in only about one later trace. [LAG001, LAG005, LAG009, LAG014, LAG015, LAG019, LAG021, LAG028]

- Relative positions moved consistently with this re-sequencing: A15, A17 and A18 occur slightly earlier in the trace (mean relative position lower by about 0.02–0.06), while A19, A20 and A21 occur slightly later (higher by about 0.03–0.06). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, these activities execute in one fixed sequence — A26, A17, A15, A18, A19, A21, A20, A27 — whereas the earlier window showed mixed routing in which, for example, A18 typically passed to A27 (about 53 in 100) and A26 chose between A17 and A19. The support is direct and consistent: successor shares collapse to a single target for every observed source, pairwise order flips in the direction the sequence implies, and the observed gaps match the sequence step for step — including A18→A27 moving from one step to four, and A26→A20 dropping out of observation because the two would now sit six steps apart. Participation is unchanged, so the difference lies in order and routing, not in which activities run. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL001, REL004, REL006, REL008, LAG001, LAG005, LAG013, LAG020, LAG027, LAG028]

The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces: 50 items against roughly 0.47 expected from the 95 tested units, and every item's difference is larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. The items overlap heavily (several describe the same ordering, such as A18 before A19), but this count comparison does not depend on merging them. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013, LAG014, LAG015, LAG016, LAG017, LAG018, LAG019, LAG020, LAG021, LAG022, LAG023, LAG024, LAG025, LAG026, LAG027, LAG028, LAG029]

The evidence cannot rule out that the later window is simply dominated by a single variant that was rare before: with 200 traces per window, a shift in variant frequencies alone could produce a near-deterministic order if that variant reached almost all cases, and the finite samples cannot separate this from a modification of the process structure — although the agreement between routing, order, gap and position indicators makes pure sampling noise unlikely. Note also that the direction shares are over outgoing transitions rather than cases, positions and gaps are in event steps rather than clock time, and the vanished reverse pairs rest on about one later observation each, so they are weak individually.
