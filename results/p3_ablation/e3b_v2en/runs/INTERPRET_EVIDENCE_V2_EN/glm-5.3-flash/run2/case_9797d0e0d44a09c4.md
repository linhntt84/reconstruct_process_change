### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the fixed sequence A19 → A25 → A20, which previously ran in roughly 47 in 100 cases, is now executed in essentially every case and lies between A41 and A26. The routing evidence is direct: transitions out of A41 shifted from mostly A26 to mostly A19, and the A41–A26 distance moved from mostly 1 step to mostly 4 steps, consistent with the three activities now sitting between them. The relation items agree throughout — A41 precedes the trio, the trio keeps the order A19, A25, A20 in about 98 in 100 traces, and A26 follows A20 and A25 in about 99 in 100 traces — while participation of all three activities rose in lockstep from about 47 to about 100 in 100 traces. [DIR002, LAG001, REL001, REL002, REL007, REL008, REL012, REL013, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Limitations: the 23 qualifying items (against well under 1 expected if nothing had changed) largely describe this one underlying difference, since several indicators and activity pairs capture the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of complete process variants between the two finite windows of 200 traces each — the earlier window may simply contain more of a variant that does not execute the trio — although a swing of this size, together with the matching routing and distance changes, is far larger than the reference comparisons produce for random re-splits. The evidence also cannot show whether the previously omitted sequence became mandatory or why about half of the earlier traces did not execute it. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, LAG001, REL004]
