### Observed differences

- The activities A16, A17 and A18 were each present in about 19 in 100 traces in the earlier window (37 of 200) and in about 79 in 100 traces in the later window (158 of 200), never repeating within a trace in either window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- Conversely, A19, A20 and A21 each dropped from about 82 in 100 traces (163 of 200) to about 21 in 100 (42 of 200). [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- A15 occurs in every trace in both windows, and its outgoing transitions redistributed: in the earlier window about 82 in 100 of them went to A19, A20 or A21, while in the later window about 79 in 100 go to A16, A17 or A18. [DIR001]
- The two triples act as mutually exclusive, complete blocks: in the earlier window A19, A20 and A21 co-occurred in about 82 in 100 traces (in varying order), and A16, A17 and A18 co-occurred in about 19 in 100; in the later window this roughly inverted (about 21 vs about 79 in 100), and pairs spanning the two blocks never co-occurred — a trace contained one block or the other, not both. [REL022, REL025, REL007, REL013, REL009, REL014, REL018]
- Whichever block runs, A15 comes first: co-occurrence of A15 with A16/A17/A18 rose from about 19 to about 79 in 100 traces (always A15 before the other activity), while co-occurrence with A19/A20/A21 fell from about 82 to about 21 in 100. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- A25 appears in essentially every trace in both windows but changed company: earlier it co-occurred with A19/A20/A21 in about 82 in 100 traces; in the later window it co-occurs with A16/A17/A18 in about 79 in 100 (A16 before A25 in about 45 in 100, interleaved or repeated in about 34 in 100). [REL024, REL026, REL027, REL012, REL017, REL021]
- All 34 qualifying items show shifts of the same size (about 60 percentage points on the participation/co-occurrence scale) with empirical strength near 0.995, meaning each difference exceeds roughly 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch following A15 has roughly inverted: in the later window, cases typically continue from A15 with the activity set A16, A17 and A18, executed together in varying order and accompanied by A25, whereas in the earlier window they typically continued with A19, A20 and A21 together with A25 — the branch choice moved from about 82 in 100 cases on the A19–A21 side to about 21 in 100. [DIR001, REL001, REL004, REL007, REL012, REL024]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor profile of A15, which is observed in all traces in both windows, flipped from the old triple to the new one; the relational items show the two triples are mutually exclusive within a case and each appears as a complete set, so this is a change in which block executes rather than a change in one activity alone; and the screening produced 34 items where roughly 0.5 would be expected without a change, with every item far outside the random re-split range. [DIR001, REL009, REL022, OCC001, OCC004]

Limitations remain. The 34 items are strongly dependent — they largely re-describe the same flip of roughly 120 of the 200 cases per window — so they are not independent confirmations. The evidence also cannot distinguish a rerouting of the process from a large shift in the mix of two pre-existing variants, since the "new" pattern already ran in about 19 in 100 cases before the change point, and a change in case mix would look identical; within-block order varies in both windows, so no fixed sequence can be asserted. [OCC001, REL001, REL007, REL022]
