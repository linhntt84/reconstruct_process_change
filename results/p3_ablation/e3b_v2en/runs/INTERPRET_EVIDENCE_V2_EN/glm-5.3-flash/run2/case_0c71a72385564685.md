### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a step running as exactly one of A19, A20 or A25 was inserted between A41 and A26, executing in roughly 30–38 in 100 traces, after which control typically continues to A26. Supporting signs: the three activities rise from no (or single-trace) participation to about 59–76 of 200 traces each; relationally each follows A41 and precedes A26; A19 and A25 send almost all their observed outgoing flow to A26; A41's outgoing flow redirects from A26 to the three activities; and the A41→A26 step distance widens from 1 to 2–3 steps. The 21 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.5 expected without a change, and the occurrence, routing, position, relation and lag indicators all describe the same placement rather than independent phenomena. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window only shows the activities were not observed, so a rare pre-existing path whose triggering rose sharply cannot be excluded (A20 did appear once before). The "exactly one of three" reading rests on very low co-occurrence (about 1 in 100) and could blur with larger samples, and sampling differences in variant frequencies between two 200-trace windows cannot be fully ruled out — although a behaviour absent from 200 before-traces yet present in about a third of 200 after-traces is difficult to attribute to sampling alone.
