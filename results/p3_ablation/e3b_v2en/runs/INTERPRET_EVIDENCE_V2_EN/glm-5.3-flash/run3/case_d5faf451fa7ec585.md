### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The number of qualifying items (28) is many times the roughly 0.39 expected by chance alone, and every item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled windows; since the items cover the same activities across occurrence, routing, position, relation and distance indicators, they describe one coherent behavioural difference rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The evidence supports the possibility that the optional block of A14, A15 and A20, which in the earlier window executed exactly once per trace between A22 and A24 (in about 45 in 100 traces between A22 and A23, and in about 55 in 100 between A23 and A24), is no longer executed, so that later-window traces run directly from A22 to A23 to A24. The support is direct and consistent: the successor sets of A22 and A23 each collapse from four targets to one, A23 and A24 are now immediate successors in every later trace, A24 moved from three steps to two steps after A22, A14, A15 and A20 no longer co-occur with A22, A23 or A24, and the small position shifts of A22 and A24 point the same way. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL001, REL002, REL006, POS004, POS005]

The main limitation is that zero participation establishes only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed in the later window, so the evidence cannot distinguish removing the block from its branch simply never being taken, nor exclude that the activities persist in variants absent from this sample. Sampling differences in variant frequencies can make a variant vanish from one window, but a behaviour present in 56–80 of 200 earlier traces and absent from all 200 later traces, together with the routing collapse at A22 and A23, is difficult to attribute to sampling alone.
