### Observed differences

- A04 became far more prominent in the later window: it appears in about 60 in 100 traces versus about 19 in 100 earlier, and its typical position moved from mid-trace (median about 0.55 of the trace) to early (median about 0.17). [OCC001, POS001]
- The handover out of A14 changed: earlier, about 87 in 100 of A14's outgoing transitions went to A15; later, about 54 in 100 go to A04 and about 44 in 100 still go to A15. A04 follows A14 within one event in about 96 in 100 of the observed A14–A04 pairs, and about 87 in 100 of A04's outgoing transitions go to A15 — although A04 precedes A15 in about 31 in 100 traces and the two are interleaved or repeated in about 27 in 100, so its placement relative to A15 varies in a sizable minority of cases. [DIR004, LAG001, DIR001, REL002]
- A27 dropped from appearing in every trace (200 of 200) to about 52 in 100, and pairs where it previously always followed A26 or A14 now co-occur in only about half of traces. [OCC003, REL017, REL009]
- Three activities that were absent earlier — A32, A33 and A38 — each now appear in about half of later traces (about 98–100 of 200), typically once, around the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.51). [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- The new trio is integrated into the flow: after A26, outgoing transitions split between A27 (about 51 in 100) and A32/A33/A38 (about 50 in 100 combined); the trio's members co-occur in about 49 in 100 traces in varying order, show transitions among themselves and onward to A28, and precede A28 in nearly all traces where they co-occur. A27 and the trio almost never co-occur (about 1 in 100 or less). [DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, REL028, REL025, REL022]
- The event distance from A26 to A28 grew: a gap of two events in about 91 in 100 earlier traces versus about 50 in 100 later, with a gap of four in about 46 in 100 later, consistent with events now sitting between them. [LAG003, POS002]
- A06 nearly disappeared, from about 20 in 100 traces to about 4 in 100, though only 8 later traces contain it; A10's successor mix also shifted, but on small numbers (28 and 22 outgoing transitions). [OCC002, DIR002, DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of two structural differences in the later window. First, A04 now sits on the main flow between A14 and A15: A14 passes to A04 in about half of its outgoing transitions, A04 almost always immediately follows A14, and A04 leads to A15 in about 87 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, whereas earlier A14 passed directly to A15 and A04 was rare, late and loosely connected. Second, a new alternative block built on A32, A33 and A38 now follows A26 in about half of the traces: the three activities run together in varying order and then hand over to A28, they are mutually exclusive with A27, and their appearance matches A27 falling from universal participation to about half of traces and the longer A26-to-A28 distance. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR004, DIR006, LAG001, LAG003, REL022, REL025, REL028]

The support is direct rather than purely frequency-based: successor changes (A14 and A26 routing), pair-order statistics (A14 before A04, A26 before the trio, the trio before A28, A27 exclusive of the trio) and positional/lag evidence all agree, and the volume of qualifying items (55 against roughly 0.73 expected if nothing had changed) cannot be explained by chance as a whole. Note that the many items largely re-describe these two underlying differences, so they are not independent confirmations. [DIR004, DIR006, REL017, REL022, REL025, LAG003]

Limitations remain. Zero earlier participation for A32, A33 and A38 strictly means only that they were not observed before, and an important competing explanation — that the two windows sample different mixtures of pre-existing variants, for example a variant containing the trio that was rare earlier — cannot be fully ruled out, though its consistency across participation, order and successor evidence in 200-trace windows makes pure sampling variation unlikely for the main differences. The near-disappearance of A06 rests on only 8 later traces, and smaller shifts (around A10, or the slight position drift of A28) are based on few observations and may be sampling noise. [OCC002, DIR002, DIR003, POS002, OCC004]
