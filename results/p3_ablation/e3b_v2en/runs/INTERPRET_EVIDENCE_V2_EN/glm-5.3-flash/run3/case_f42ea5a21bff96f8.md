### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two parts of the process were structurally reorganised. A06 is now executed in about half of the cases and is placed between A34 and A35 near the end of the process: A34 routes roughly half of its flow to A06, A06 in turn leads to A35 in about nine of ten of its outgoing transitions — usually immediately — and the A34-to-A35 distance stretched from typically one step to one or two steps. [OCC002, POS001, DIR001, DIR005, LAG001, LAG013, REL011, REL012]

In addition, the branch following A19 and A20 now targets different activities: A21, A22 and A23, previously the frequent partners in about three quarters of traces, are now rare, while A24, A25 and A26 took their place with about three quarters participation and now run after A19 or A20 in that order. [OCC004, OCC007, DIR002, REL013, REL016, REL022, REL025]

The support rests on convergence across indicator families rather than on item count alone: participation, successor shares, relative position, co-occurrence order and step distances each show the same movements, and 92 qualifying items against roughly one expected for an unchanged process cannot be explained as chance screening noise. [OCC002, DIR001, POS001, REL012, LAG001]

The evidence cannot rule out that both windows sample the same process with a different mix of variants: a rarer variant containing, for example, A06 followed by A35 could have become more frequent without any routing change, and two samples of 200 traces can shift variant frequencies on their own. Several relational items also restate the same underlying behaviour (A06's increased participation), so they are not independent confirmations, and the lag observations for A25 and A26 preceding A06 rest on only about two before-window pairs. [LAG001, LAG004, LAG006]
