### Observed differences

- In the later window, A19, A20 and A25 — previously present in every trace — each appear in only about 53–54 in 100 traces, and A06 falls from about 71 to about 26 in 100 traces. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]
- Three rarely seen activities become more frequent: A08 rises from about 4 to 20 in 100 traces, A10 from about 5 to 16, and A04 from about 5 to 15. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]
- Routing after A14 changes sharply: A06 receives about 12% of A14's observed outgoing transitions in the later window versus about 69% before, while A15 receives about 78% versus about 31%. [DIR005]
- Routing after A41 also changes: A19 receives about 47% of A41's outgoing transitions versus about 96% before, while A26, previously unobserved as a successor, now receives about 39%. [DIR008]
- A06's own outgoing transitions become dispersed: A15 drops from about 92% to about 37% of A06's outgoing transitions, with the remainder spread over many activities. [DIR003]
- Relational measures match this: A06 co-occurs with always-present partners such as A14, A15, A26, A41, A46 and A47 in only about 26 in 100 later traces (about 71 before), appearing without them in about 74 in 100; A19, A20 and A25 co-occur with A14 and A15 in about 53 in 100 traces (100 before) and are jointly absent in about 46 in 100. [REL001, REL002, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL013, REL016, REL022]
- Positions shift accordingly: A06 appears later in traces (mean relative position about 0.18 to 0.43), immediate A14→A06 succession falls from about 98% to 75% of observed pairs, and A14→A15 at distance 1 rises from about 17% to 41%. [POS001, LAG003, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a larger share of traces bypasses the activities A06, A19, A20 and A25, with flow rerouted around them: after A14, control flow passes predominantly to A15 instead of A06, and after A41 it passes to A26 instead of A19. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR005, DIR008]

Supporting signs: the participation drops are large (from 100 to about 53 in 100 traces for three activities, and from 71 to about 26 for A06) and consistent across occurrence, relation, direction and lag indicators; 59 evidence items qualified in total, far more than the roughly one expected for an unchanged process; and the rerouting is directly visible in successor shares with strong support (about 200 observations per window for A14 and A41). [DIR005, DIR008, OCC005, REL016]

Limitations: the apparent increases in A04, A08 and A10 rest on few observations (about 8–43 per window), so the destination of the additional flow is uncertain; many items describe the same underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations; and with 200 traces per window, sampling differences in variant frequencies — for example, a trace variant omitting A19, A20 and A25 being sampled more often in the later window — cannot be fully excluded, although a variant going from effectively 0 to about 47 in 100 traces is difficult to explain by sampling alone. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL016]
