### Observed differences

- Three alternative activities nearly disappeared in the later window. Earlier, A19 ran in about 32 in 100 traces (64 of 200), A20 in about 28 in 100 (56 of 200) and A25 in about 40 in 100 (80 of 200); the three never co-occurred and together appeared in essentially every trace, exactly one of the three per trace. In the later window each appears in only about 1 in 200 traces, and about 99 in 100 traces contain none of them. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006]

- In the earlier window these activities sat inside the stretch between A27, A28 and A29: about 44 in 100 of A27's observed outgoing transitions went to one of A19/A20/A25 (A25 ~20%, A19 ~13%, A20 ~10%) versus about 53 in 100 directly to A28; about 54 in 100 of A28's outgoing transitions went to one of A19/A20/A25 versus about 45 in 100 to A29. When present, A19, A20 and A25 always fell before A29, and A27 preceded them in roughly 28–40 in 100 of the traces where they co-occurred. [DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012]

- In the later window the same stretch runs directly: about 97 in 100 of A27's outgoing transitions go to A28 (previously about 53 in 100), and about 98 in 100 of A28's outgoing transitions go to A29 (previously about 45 in 100). [DIR004, DIR005]

- The gaps between these activities collapsed: A27 is immediately followed by A28 in about 98 in 100 traces (previously about 54, with about 44 having one activity in between); A28 is immediately followed by A29 in about 98 in 100 traces (previously about 45, with about 53 having one activity in between); and the A27-to-A29 distance shortened from 3 steps in about 94 in 100 traces to 2 steps in about 97 in 100 — one intermediate event fewer. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

- The later-window routing profiles suggesting new edges out of A19 (to A44), A20 (to A08) and A25 (to A42), as well as the later position statistics for these three activities, each rest on a single observation and are too weak to interpret. A29's average position shifted slightly earlier (about 0.56 to 0.55 of the trace length). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the mutually exclusive intermediate block A19/A20/A25 — which in the earlier window executed exactly once per trace, inserted either directly after A27 or directly after A28 and always before A29 — is no longer executed in the later window, with traces proceeding A27 → A28 → A29 in immediate succession. This rests on direct relational and positional evidence, not only on frequency counts: the A27-to-A29 distance shortened by exactly one step, the A27→A28 and A28→A29 gaps became immediate successions in about 98 in 100 traces, and routing out of A27 and A28 became almost entirely the direct edges. The participation collapse (64, 56 and 80 of 200 traces down to 1 each) and the disappearance of all co-occurrence and ordering relations between A19/A20/A25 and A27/A28/A29 are consistent with this same single underlying difference, and the 27 evidence items against roughly 0.55 expected by chance, each difference exceeding about 199 of 200 random re-splits, cannot as a whole be explained by sampling noise. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL003, REL005, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, DIR004, DIR005]

The evidence cannot distinguish removal of this block from an extreme shift in the mix of pre-existing process variants: a skip-variant would have had to be very rare earlier (0 of 200 traces skipped the block) and dominate the later window, which would still be a substantial change in execution frequencies but not proof of a structural edit. The apparent new outgoing edges from the single remaining executions of A19, A20 and A25 cannot be interpreted from one trace each. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
