### Observed differences

- In the later window, A22 runs in almost every case — about 199 of 200 traces, up from about 103 of 200 — while A27, A28 and A33 each fall from about 97 of 200 traces to a single trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Routing out of A21 shifts accordingly: transitions from A21 to A22 rise from about 52 in 100 of A21's observed outgoing transitions to essentially all of them, and the combined transitions from A21 into A27, A28 and A33 (about 48 in 100 before) are no longer seen apart from one trace. [DIR001]
- The order A21 before A22 before A23, already the majority route before (about 52 in 100 traces), becomes near-universal (about 199 of 200); A21 previously co-occurred with A27, A28 or A33 in about 97 of 200 traces, in almost none later. - A27, A28 and A33 previously always appeared together (co-occurrence in about 97 of 200 traces, with varying internal order); in the later window about 199 of 200 traces contain none of them. [REL012, REL013, REL014, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- The later routing, position and lag profiles for A27, A28 and A33 each rest on that single trace, so details such as a single dominant successor for these activities are not reliable on their own. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that routing at A21 changed so that, in the later window, nearly all cases take the short path A21 → A22 → A23, while the alternative segment that previously inserted A27, A28 and A33 between A21 and A23 is executed in only about 1 of 200 cases, down from about 97 of 200. The participation drop and the routing shift at A21 are observed directly, the relation and lag evidence matches them (A21 before A22, A22 before A23, A23 at event distance 2 from A21), all 34 qualifying items point the same way — far more than the roughly 0.45 items expected by chance in this screening — and a swing from 97 to 1 cases out of 200 exceeds what sampling variation typically produces for a stable variant split. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL005, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the A27/A28/A33 segment was removed from the process or is still reachable but almost never selected — one later trace still contains all three activities — and the later profiles for those activities rest on that single trace. A shift in the frequencies of pre-existing process variants between the two windows (sampling differences in variant mix rather than a structural rerouting) cannot be ruled out, and the many relation and lag items describe the same underlying rerouting rather than independent confirmations. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL012, REL013, REL014, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
