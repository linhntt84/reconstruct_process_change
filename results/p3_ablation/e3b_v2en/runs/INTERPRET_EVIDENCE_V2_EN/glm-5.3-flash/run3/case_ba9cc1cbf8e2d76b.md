### Observed differences

- Activities A19, A20 and A25 do not appear in any of the 200 earlier traces; in the later window each occurs exactly once in the traces that contain it, covering about 29, 41 and 31 in 100 traces respectively (58, 82 and 61 of 200) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].
- The three activities exclude one another: A19 and A20 never share a trace, and the other two pairs co-occur in only about 1 in 200 traces each, so their combined coverage means almost every later trace contains exactly one of them [REL001, REL002, REL006].
- Each new activity sits near the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.52 in all three cases) [POS001, POS002, POS003].
- Routing into the block changed sharply: A27, which earlier sent about 97 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A28, now sends about 52 in 100 to A19/A20/A25 (roughly 14/22/16) and about 43 in 100 to A28; A28, which earlier sent about 98 in 100 to A29, now sends about 41 in 100 to A19/A20/A25 and about 57 in 100 to A29 [DIR004, DIR005].
- Routing out of the block: A19, A20 and A25, with no observed outgoing transitions earlier, now lead mainly to A28 (about 53–56 in 100) and to A29 (about 41–47 in 100) [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003].
- Order relations place the new activities between A27/A28 and A29: A27 precedes the new activity in the traces where they co-occur (for example A27 before A20 in about 40 in 100 traces), and each new activity precedes A29 in essentially all of its co-occurrences with A29 (A20 before A29 in about 40 in 100 traces); against A28 the order is mixed (A19 before A28 in about 15 in 100, A28 before A19 in about 14 in 100) [REL003, REL007, REL010, REL005, REL009, REL012, REL004, REL008].
- Distances between the surrounding activities shifted by about one step: A27→A28 moved from lag 1 (about 98 in 100) to lag 1 (43 in 100) or lag 2 (52 in 100); A27→A29 from lag 2 (about 96 in 100) to lag 3 (92 in 100); A28→A29 from lag 1 (98 in 100) to lag 1 (56 in 100) or lag 2 (41 in 100). In line with material inserted between them, A27's mean position moved slightly earlier (about 0.48 to 0.46) and A29's slightly later (about 0.55 to 0.57), with both still present in every trace [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS004, POS005].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new step was inserted into the sequence between A27/A28 and A29, executed as exactly one of three exclusive alternatives — A19, A20 or A25 — per case. The support is direct and consistent: participation rises from zero to roughly 30–40 in 100 traces with one occurrence each [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]; A27 and A28 redirect about half and about 40 in 100 of their outgoing transitions to the new activities [DIR004, DIR005]; the new activities run after A27 and before A29 [REL003, REL007, REL010, REL005, REL009, REL012]; and all three surrounding distances shift by about one step, matching the small opposite position shifts of A27 and A29 [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS004, POS005]. The 28 qualifying items are about fifty times the roughly 0.56 expected for an unchanged process, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of a stable process typically produce [OCC001, DIR004, LAG002].

A purely sampling-based explanation — a variant appearing in one window only — cannot be strictly ruled out, but it would require the earlier window to have missed a path that later runs in nearly every trace, which the coherent routing, order and lag shifts make unlikely [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]. The evidence also cannot establish whether the three alternatives differ beyond their near-identical routing, and it shows only that the insertion point varies (directly after A27 in about half of A27's transitions, directly after A28 in about 40 in 100 of A28's), not whether this reflects two distinct placements or one flexible one [DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL006].
