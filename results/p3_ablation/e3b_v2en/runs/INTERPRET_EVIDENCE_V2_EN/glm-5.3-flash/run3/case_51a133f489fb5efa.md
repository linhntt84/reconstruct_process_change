### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the flow around A19, A20, A25 and A26 was rerouted: the A19/A20/A25 group, which previously ran after A26 and was followed by A28 and A32, now runs before A26 and leads directly into it; A27→A28→A32 became a direct chain; and A45, often via the now more frequent A08, enters the A19/A20/A25 group instead of A26. The order reversal is shown directly by the relational items, the successor replacement by the routing items, and the lag shifts match the rerouting quantitatively (A45→A26 and A08→A26 are now typically two steps apart, exactly one more than the new direct links, and A27→A32 shortened by one step); the 40 flagged items also far exceed the roughly 0.7 expected for an unchanged process, although they largely describe this single underlying rerouting rather than independent confirmations. [REL005, REL007, REL009, DIR002, DIR006, DIR007, LAG002, LAG011, LAG014]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of existing variants: the A19-before-A26 order already occurred in about 1 in 200 earlier-window traces, so the later behaviour could reflect a rare path becoming common in a finite 200-trace sample rather than a structural modification. A few lag items rest on only one or two observations (A08→A19, A16→A19 and A19→A26 before; A25→A32, A27→A25 and A28→A25 after) and were not relied on; the conclusion rests on items with roughly 60–200 supporting observations per window. [REL005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG012, DIR002]
