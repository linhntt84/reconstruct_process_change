### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A19, A20 and A25 do not appear in any of the 200 traces; in the later window, A19 occurs in about 30 in 100 traces (59 of 200), A20 in about 41 in 100 (82 of 200) and A25 in about 30 in 100 (60 of 200), so together they appear in essentially every later trace. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- The three activities are almost mutually exclusive: A19 and A20 never occur in the same trace, and A19/A25 and A20/A25 co-occur in only about 1 in 200 traces each. [REL001, REL002, REL006]
- Routing into the new activities changed sharply: A27's outgoing transitions, previously about 99 in 100 towards A28, now go to A28 in about 44 in 100 cases and to A20, A25 or A19 in about 22, 17 and 15 in 100 cases; A28's outgoing transitions, previously about 99 in 100 towards A29, now go to A29 in about 55 in 100 cases and to A20, A19 or A25 in about 17, 13 and 12 in 100 cases. [DIR004, DIR005]
- The new activities themselves lead mainly to A28 (about 52–56 in 100 of their outgoing transitions) and to A29 (about 42–46 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- Order relations place the new activities between A27 and A29: A27 comes before A19/A20/A25 in nearly all co-occurrences (e.g., A27 before A20 in about 40 of the 41 in 100 traces containing both), and the new activities come before A29 in essentially all co-occurrences (e.g., A19 before A29 in all of its 29.5 in 100 co-occurrences), while their order relative to A28 is mixed in both directions. [REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, REL004, REL008, REL011]
- Step distances shifted accordingly: the A27→A29 distance grew from 2 steps in about 99 in 100 traces to 3 steps in about 97 in 100; A27→A28 moved from 1 step (99 in 100) to 2 steps (about 55 in 100); A28→A29 moved from 1 step (about 99 in 100) to 2 steps in about 44 in 100. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- The new activities sit mid-trace, at a mean relative position of about 0.51 in each case; A27's mean position shifted slightly earlier and A29's slightly later, while A27, A28 and A29 continue to appear in essentially every trace in both windows. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG001]
- A secondary difference: A10's participation rose from about 5 in 100 traces to about 13.5 in 100 (10 to 27 traces). [OCC001]
- Every qualifying difference here is larger than those seen in about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled windows, and the 29 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.5 items expected by chance in an unchanged process. [OCC002, DIR004, POS001, REL003, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, an additional step is executed in the segment between A27 and A29: essentially every later trace runs exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities — A19, A20 or A25 — placed either directly after A27 (before A28) or after A28 (before A29), which lengthens the A27→A29 distance from two to three steps. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL005, REL007, REL009, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The support is direct and consistent rather than merely numerical. The placement claim rests on relational and positional evidence, not only on frequency: A27 precedes and A29 follows the new activities in almost every co-occurrence, the A27→A28 and A28→A29 distances each shifted by one step in roughly half of the traces (exactly what one extra step between them produces), and A27 and A28 now route about half of their outgoing transitions to the new activities, whose own successors are A28 and A29. [REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005] The near-perfect mutual exclusivity of A19, A20 and A25, whose participations sum to almost the whole later window, agrees with the lag finding that exactly one position was added in nearly every trace. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL002, REL006, LAG002]

Limitations: the two windows are finite samples of 200 traces each, so the split among A19, A20 and A25 (about 30/41/30) and the split of insertion points carry sampling uncertainty, and a shift in the mix of process variants between the sampled windows is a competing explanation that two windows cannot fully rule out — although going from 0 of 200 to 59–82 of 200 traces is hard to attribute to sampling variation alone. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004] The evidence also cannot determine whether A19, A20 and A25 are three alternative routing branches or three labels for the same additional step, and zero participation earlier only means these activities were not observed in that window. [REL001, REL002, REL006] Finally, the occurrence, routing, position, relation and lag items largely describe one underlying behaviour, so they should be read as one coherent picture rather than independent confirmations. [OCC002, DIR001, POS001, REL005, LAG002]
