### Observed differences

- **The order of A26/A27 relative to A35, A36 and A41 reversed.** Before, A35, A36 and A41 each preceded A26 in essentially all co-occurring traces (about 99–100 in 100) and preceded A27 in about 98–100 in 100; in the later window A26 and A27 each preceded A35, A36 and A41 in about 97–99 in 100 traces. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- **Entry into the A35/A36/A41 group changed.** Before, A25 was followed by A35, A36 or A41 in roughly equal shares (about 39, 32 and 27 in 100 of its outgoing transitions); in the later window A25 leads to A27 in about 98 in 100 transitions. A26 shows the mirror image: before it led almost only to A30 (about 99 in 100), in the later window it leads to A35, A36 or A41 (about 34, 32 and 30 in 100) and rarely to A30 (about 1–2 in 100). [DIR003, DIR004]
- **Exit from the group changed.** Before, A35, A36 and A41 each led to A27 in about 28–37 in 100 of their outgoing transitions; in the later window A27 is almost never their target (about 0.5 in 100) while A30 appears as a target for each (about 30–35 in 100), with routing among the three group members themselves similar across windows. [DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]
- **Distances shifted consistently.** A25 → A27 became immediate (lag 1 in about 99 in 100 cases, versus lag 4 in about 94 in 100 before), and A26 → A30 moved from immediate (lag 1 in 99 in 100) to about four steps later (lag 4 in about 91 in 100). [LAG005, LAG009]
- **Event positions agree.** In the later window A35, A36 and A41 occur later in traces (mean relative position about 0.41–0.42 to about 0.58–0.59), while A27 (about 0.47 to 0.38) and A26 (about 0.62 to 0.53) occur earlier. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- **Peripheral participation changes.** A06 now appears in fewer traces (about 12–13 in 100 versus 27 in 100), matching a much smaller A15 → A06 share (about 3 in 100 of A15's outgoing transitions versus about 15 in 100, with A15 → A16 rising from about 85 to 95 in 100); A02 appears in more traces (about 11–12 in 100 versus 4–5 in 100, i.e. 23 versus 9 traces). A06's own outgoing mix also differs, but rests on only about 28 later-window transitions. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the group of activities A35, A36 and A41 changed its place in the sequence: before, it sat between A25 (which fed into it) and A27 (to which it led), with A27 preceding A26 and A26 leading directly to A30; in the later window it sits between A26 (which feeds into it) and A30 (to which it leads), while A27 now directly follows A25. This is shown directly by the near-complete reversal of the orderings between A26/A27 and each of A35, A36 and A41, each based on essentially all 200 traces in both windows, and it is consistent with the routing changes at A25, A26 and the group members, the shifted distances, and the positional shifts. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG005, LAG009, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

The 33 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.76 expected for an unchanged process, so the evidence set as a whole cannot be explained by chance; however, most items describe this single underlying reordering, so they are not independent confirmations of it. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is sampling difference in variant frequencies for the peripheral details: A06's altered outgoing mix rests on about 28 later-window transitions, A02's rise on 23 versus 9 traces, and several lag profiles on only about 2 later-window observations (LAG003 and LAG011 are based on a single earlier-window observation), so these specifics may reflect rare variants rather than change. The evidence also cannot show whether the group's internal cycling among A35, A36 and A41, or its role, changed beyond its placement in the sequence.
