### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment formed by A19, A20 and A25, which in the earlier window ran between A45 and A26 (A45 branched to one of the three, and each of the three then flowed into A26), in the later window runs after A27/A28 and flows into A28 or A32, while A45 connects directly to A26. This is a placement hypothesis, and it rests on directly observed relational and positional evidence: the order of the trio versus A26/A27 flips in about 30–38 of 100 traces, the trio's mean position moves from about 0.32 to about 0.59, its outgoing transitions shift from A26 to A28/A32, A45's shift to A26, and the distance changes (A25→A26 adjacency lost, A27→A32 lengthened by one step, A45→A26 shortened by one step) all point the same way. The trio's participation is stable at roughly 30–38 of 100 traces, so this is not an activity becoming more or less frequent, and with 27 items against about 0.63 expected under no change, chance alone cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, POS001, POS002, POS003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, LAG003, LAG005, LAG008]

Limitations: the trio's exact later placement is not pinned down, since A27 still sends about 51 in 100 transitions to A28 and A28 about 48 in 100 to A32, so the trio is interleaved with A27/A28 in varying ways rather than sitting at one fixed spot. Some new adjacencies (A20 next to A28, A20→A32) rest on a single before-window observation, so for those details a rare variant appearing in only one window cannot be ruled out, and finite-sample differences in variant frequencies remain a competing explanation for the finer structure. The items also overlap — REL, LAG and DIR partly describe the same traces — so they are not independent confirmations. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG006, DIR004, DIR005, LAG004, LAG007]
