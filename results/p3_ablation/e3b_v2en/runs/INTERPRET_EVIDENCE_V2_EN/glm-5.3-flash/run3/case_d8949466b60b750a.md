### Observed differences

- In the later window, A27 appears in about 199 of 200 traces (roughly 100 in 100, up from about 52 in 100), and its mean occurrences per trace rise from about 0.52 to about 1.0, so it now runs about once per case. [OCC001]
- A32, A33 and A38, each previously present in about 48–49 in 100 traces, are each observed in only 1 of 200 traces in the later window. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Outgoing flow from A26 is redirected: transitions from A26 to A27 make up about 97% of A26's observed outgoing transitions in the later window, up from about 51%; before, the remainder went to A38 (about 18%), A33 (about 16%) and A32 (about 14%). [DIR001]
- The two routes were mutually exclusive before: A27 co-occurred with A32, A33 or A38 in essentially no trace (about 0–0.5%), and each of A32, A33 and A38 preceded A28 in about 48–49 in 100 traces; in the later window A28 occurs without any of them in about 99.5 in 100 traces, and pairs among A32, A33 and A38 no longer co-occur. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- The surviving sequence is A26 → A27 → A28: A26 precedes A27 in about 96.5 in 100 traces (previously about 52), A27 leads directly to A28 in essentially all cases (about 97% of A27's outgoing transitions before, about 98.5% after), and the A26-to-A28 distance is 2 steps in about 97 in 100 traces, where before it split between 2 steps (50 in 100) and 4 steps (about 46 in 100). [REL001, REL005, DIR002, LAG004, LAG001]
- A26 and A28 themselves occur in all traces in both windows; A28's mean relative position in the trace shifts only slightly earlier (about 0.55 to about 0.53). [REL001, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the branch between A26 and A28 running through A32, A33 and A38 is skipped in almost every case in the later window, so that execution runs A26 → A27 → A28 in nearly all traces, whereas before the two routes were roughly even alternatives. The participation and routing changes are direct and large: A27 rose from about 52 to about 100 in 100 traces while A32, A33 and A38 fell from about 48–49 to essentially zero, and the share of A26's outgoing transitions leading to A27 rose from about 51% to about 97%. The 35 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.6 expected if nothing had changed, and although they largely describe one underlying difference, the participation, routing, relation and distance indicators all portray the same rerouting. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, LAG001]

Competing explanations are only partly ruled out. Sampling differences in variant frequencies can shift branch shares between finite windows, and the strength values alone cannot separate a real change from a rare variant, although a coordinated shift from about half to nearly all cases across four indicator families is far larger than the random re-splits of the pooled data produce. In addition, the later-window profiles of A32, A33 and A38 rest on a single trace each, and zero participation shows only that these activities were not observed, not that the branch was removed; it remains possible that the branch is still reachable but almost never selected, or that the mix of cases entering the process shifted rather than the process structure itself. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR003, DIR004]
