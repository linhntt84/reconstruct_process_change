### Observed differences

- In the later window, the activities A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25 and A32 run in one fixed order, whereas earlier they appeared in several alternative orders: A20 precedes A24 in about 99 in 100 later traces versus about 25 in 100 earlier (A24 preceded A20 in about 75 in 100), A23 precedes A24 in about 98 in 100 versus about 12 in 100, and A23 precedes A26 in about 98 in 100 versus about 30 in 100; the other pairs in this group show the same flip to a single order. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment containing A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25 and A32 was restructured from several alternative orderings into one fixed chain, A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32. The support is direct and consistent: relational evidence shows every pair inside the group moving to a single order in about 98–99 in 100 later traces, lag evidence shows each ordered pair sitting at exactly the gap this chain implies, and routing evidence shows one dominant successor per activity; 58 items qualified in total, many times the fewer than 1 expected for an unchanged process, and the same reordering also accounts for the small position shifts of these activities. [REL002, REL007, REL009, LAG003, LAG004, LAG010, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, POS002, POS005]

Two limitations remain. The evidence cannot distinguish a restructured process from a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants — a fixed-order variant that already existed could simply dominate the later sample, and with 200 traces per window such sampling differences cannot be ruled out; the many items also largely describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. In addition, A08's decline (about 32 to about 13 in 100 traces, with its transitions spreading from mostly A15 to many targets, on 26 later traces) and A10's rise (about 3 to about 12 in 100, on only 6 earlier traces) are participation changes on modest counts that the chain hypothesis itself does not explain. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001]
