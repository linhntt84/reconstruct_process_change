### Observed differences

- In the later window, the outgoing transitions of A36 shifted towards A37: A36→A37 carried about 60 in 100 transitions versus about 34 in 100 earlier, while A36→A33 fell from about 64 to about 37 in 100 (based on roughly 90–93 outgoing transitions per window). [DIR001]

- A few new outgoing edges of A36 (to A01, A27, A30) and of A37 (to A10, A22, A32, A43) each appear at roughly 1 in 100 transitions only in the later window; at that size they may be rare variants rather than structural additions. [DIR001, DIR002]
- All three differences were larger than about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data, and three items qualified in total where about 0.48 would be expected if nothing had changed. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the flow among A36, A37 and A33 was rerouted: in the later window A36 typically leads to A37 (about 60 in 100 transitions, up from about 34) and A37 typically proceeds to A33 (about 58 in 100, up from about 35), so the chain A36→A37→A33 appears to have become the dominant route, whereas earlier A36 more often went directly to A33 and A37 more often returned to A36. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG001]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the two directional shifts are measured on the outgoing transitions of exactly the activities concerned, they fit each other, and the A36→A37 pair became both more frequent and strictly adjacent, corroborating the same rerouting; the number of qualifying items is several times the chance expectation, and each difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG001]

Limitations: all three items describe the same small cluster of activities and largely overlapping traces, so they are not independent confirmations; the roughly 1-in-100 new edges may be rare variants; and with 200-trace windows, sampling differences in variant frequencies remain a competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out, even though the aggregate item count is hard to attribute to chance alone. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG001]
