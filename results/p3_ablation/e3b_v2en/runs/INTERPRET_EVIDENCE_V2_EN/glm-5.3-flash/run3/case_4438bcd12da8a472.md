### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the segment A14 → A20 → A15 is executed between A36 and A21 in nearly every case, whereas in the earlier window it was skipped in about half of the cases and A36 passed directly to A21. The routing change out of A36, the shift of the A36-to-A21 distance from a one-or-four-step mix to consistently four steps, and the near-universal participation of A14, A15 and A20 all point to the same structure, and the 22 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.41 expected by chance when nothing has changed. [DIR001, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL003, REL012]

Several items reflect the same trace-level behaviour, so they are mutually consistent rather than independent confirmations. The evidence cannot distinguish a removed skip option from a much more frequent use of an existing path, and about 5 in 1,000 later traces still show A21 without the segment, so a rare skip path may persist; sampling differences in how often the skip variant was drawn in each 200-trace window are a formal competing explanation, though a 94-of-200 versus 199-of-200 split is far larger than random re-splits produce (only about 1 in 200 reference comparisons shows a larger difference). [REL003, OCC001, DIR001]
