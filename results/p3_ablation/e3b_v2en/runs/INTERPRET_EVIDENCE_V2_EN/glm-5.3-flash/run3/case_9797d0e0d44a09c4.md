### Observed differences

- The activities A19, A20 and A25 change from optional to near-universal: each appears in about 47 in 100 traces in the earlier window (94–95 of 200) but in about 199 of 200 traces in the later window, roughly once per trace in both windows, with no meaningful change in repetition. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the later window these three activities almost always occur together and in a fixed order — A19, then A25, then A20 — after A41 and before A26; for example, A19 precedes A20 in about 98 in 100 later traces versus about 46 in 100 earlier traces, and A41 precedes each of them in about 96–98 in 100 later traces versus about 47 in 100 earlier. [REL001, REL002, REL004, REL007, REL009, REL013, REL003, REL008, REL012]
- Routing out of A41 shifted sharply: A41 is followed by A19 in about 92 in 100 of its outgoing transitions in the later window (about 45 in 100 before), while the direct move A41 → A26 drops from about 52 in 100 to under 1 in 100. A20's outgoing behaviour is essentially unchanged, still dominated by A26 (about 96–99 in 100). [DIR002, DIR001]
- The distance from A41 to A26 moved from one step in about 52 in 100 earlier cases (four steps in about 42 in 100) to four steps in about 89 in 100 later cases (one step in under 1 in 100), consistent with three activities now sitting between them. [LAG001]
- Secondary changes: A42 now interleaves with A19/A20/A25 in about 46 in 100 later traces (about 17 in 100 earlier); A45 still runs in about 60 in 100 traces in both windows but no longer appears without A19/A20/A25 (about 33 in 100 earlier traces had A45 alone, essentially none later). A26's typical event position shifts slightly later (mean about 0.41 to 0.43) and A41's slightly earlier (about 0.33 to 0.31). [REL005, REL010, REL014, REL006, REL011, REL015, POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a previously optional block of three activities — A19 followed by A25 followed by A20, executed between A41 and A26 — is now executed in essentially every case, so that the earlier skip path in which A41 passed directly to A26 (about 52 in 100 earlier traces) has nearly disappeared (under 1 in 100 later traces). This rests on direct relational and routing evidence rather than frequency counts alone: the pairwise orderings consistently place A41 before A19 before A25 before A20 before A26 in about 96–99 in 100 later traces, A41's outgoing transitions moved from a near-even split between A19 and A26 to about 92 in 100 towards A19, and the A41-to-A26 distance shifted from typically one step to typically four steps, matching the insertion of exactly three activities. The volume of qualifying items (23 versus about 0.64 expected if nothing had changed) also cannot be explained by chance as a whole, and every item shows a difference larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL004, REL007, REL008, DIR002, LAG001]

This remains a possibility supported by the evidence rather than a confirmed modification. All items describe one underlying shift, so they are not independent confirmations of each other, and the evidence cannot distinguish whether the direct A41 → A26 path was removed or merely became very rare (about 1 in 200 later traces). Sampling differences in variant frequencies are a competing explanation in principle, but a move from 94 to 199 of 200 traces far exceeds ordinary sampling variation at these window sizes. The exact relationship with A42, which interleaves with the block in about 46 in 100 later traces, is not resolved by the pairwise evidence. [OCC001, REL005, LAG001]
