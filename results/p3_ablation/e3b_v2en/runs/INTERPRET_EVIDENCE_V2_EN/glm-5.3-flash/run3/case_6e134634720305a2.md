### Observed differences

- In the later window, A30, A31 and A36 still appear in every trace (participation 100% in both windows), but each occurs about once per trace instead of about 3.5 times, and within-trace repetition has all but disappeared: each of the three recurred in about 75 in 100 traces before, versus about 1–2 in 100 after. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- Routing out of A36 flipped: before, about 70 in 100 of A36's outgoing transitions went back to A30 and about 28 in 100 went to A25; in the later window about 92 in 100 go to A25 and only about 4 in 100 return to A30. [DIR002]
- A30's own succession is nearly unchanged — A31 remains the successor in about 99 in 100 of A30's outgoing transitions before and about 97 in 100 after. [DIR001]
- The relative order of A30 and A36 became fixed: previously the two were interleaved or repeated in about 76 in 100 traces, whereas in the later window A30 precedes A36 in about 98 in 100 traces, typically with A36 two positions later (about 89 in 100 traces). [REL001, LAG001]
- The reverse succession (A36 before A30 within five steps) and A36's self-repetition essentially vanished, from about 150 of 200 traces each to 1 and 2 traces respectively. [LAG002, LAG003]
- A30 and A36 now occupy more consistent relative positions in the trace (spread of about 0.07 of trace length versus about 0.12 before) at roughly the same mean position as before. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the repetitive cycle through A30, A31 and A36 was largely disabled. In the earlier window the block iterated about three to four times per case, with A36 typically looping back to A30; in the later window each activity runs once per case in the fixed order A30 → A31 → A36, after which A36 proceeds to A25. The repetition collapse is observed directly (mean occurrences falling from about 3.5 to about 1 with participation unchanged), the loop-back share of A36's outgoing transitions fell from about 70 in 100 to about 4 in 100 while the exit to A25 rose to about 92 in 100, and the relational evidence agrees: A30 precedes A36 in about 98 in 100 later traces, the reverse succession and A36's self-repetition disappear, while A30 → A31 remains stable, indicating the block's internal order was preserved while its iteration stopped. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: these items describe overlapping views of the same trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations. The evidence cannot distinguish a fully removed loop-back from one still taken in a small minority of A36's outgoing transitions, and it cannot fully rule out sampling differences in variant frequencies — although a flip from about 75 in 100 to about 1 in 100 recurring traces across 200-trace windows is difficult to explain by sampling alone. [OCC001, DIR002, LAG002]
