### Observed differences

- Earlier, the six activities A28, A30, A31, A32, A33 and A34 each ran in every trace (participation 1.0, no repetitions). In the later window, A28, A30 and A31 each appear in about 47 in 100 traces, and A32, A33 and A34 in about 55–56 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- The two groups of three became near-mutually exclusive: within-group pairs still always co-occur when they occur (co-occurrence 0.465 and 0.555, matching participation), while cross-group co-occurrence fell from every trace to about 2 in 100 traces, and virtually no later trace lacks both groups. [REL013, REL018, REL025, REL015, REL019, REL022]
- A25 and A26 still appear in every trace, and their ordering is preserved: A25 precedes whichever group runs, and the group precedes A26. [REL001, REL004, REL007, REL010]
- Routing changed at the edges of this section: A25's outgoing transitions, previously only into the A28/A30/A31 group, now split roughly 47 in 100 to that group and 53 in 100 to A32/A33/A34; the A28/A30/A31 activities also gained direct transitions to A26 (roughly a quarter to a third of their outgoing edges) that were absent earlier. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- Positional gaps collapsed accordingly: A25 to A32/A33/A34 and A28/A30/A31 to A26 were 4–5 event positions apart earlier and are 1–3 apart in about 96–97 in 100 later cases; relative positions shifted consistently (A25 0.62→0.67, A26 0.83→0.81, first group 0.68→0.74, second group 0.77→0.74). [LAG001, LAG003, LAG004, LAG008, LAG011, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS006]
- A handful of lag items about cross-group pairs rest on only about 4 later observations and are too weak to rely on. [LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG009, LAG010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the two middle blocks — A28/A30/A31 and A32/A33/A34 — which earlier always executed in sequence between A25 and A26, now act as alternatives: in the later window a case runs A25, then exactly one of the two blocks (about 47 in 100 the first, about 55 in 100 the second, both in only about 2 in 100), and then A26, with the other block skipped. [OCC001, OCC004, REL015, REL025, DIR001, LAG001, LAG004]

The support is direct and consistent: 58 qualifying items against roughly 0.63 expected by chance alone, every item's difference exceeding about 199 of 200 random re-splits, A25's routing split mirroring the participation split almost exactly, new direct transitions from the first block to A26, and the collapse of positional gaps all showing the skip relation itself rather than only a frequency shift. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG004, LAG008, OCC001]

Limitations remain: the finite windows cannot rule out that this exclusive routing already existed earlier at a frequency too low to surface in 200 traces (participation was 100% before, so any such variant was below detection), the occurrence, relation, direction and lag items partly describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations, and details of the small overlap and some lag distributions rest on about 4 observations. [OCC001, REL015, LAG005]
