### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the three-way alternative segment consisting of A19, A20 and A25 — until now, exactly one of the three executed in nearly every case — is no longer executed in the later window, with A41 now connecting directly to A26. The support is direct and consistent: participation of all three activities collapsed from about 70, 61 and 70 of 200 traces to 0, 1 and 0; A41's outgoing transitions moved from being split across the three (about 93 of 100 combined) to going straight to A26 (about 92 of 100); and the A41–A26 distance fell from two positions to one. With 36 qualifying items against roughly 0.6 expected if nothing had changed, the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by chance. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, LAG002, REL012, REL017, REL021]

The evidence cannot distinguish complete removal of this branch from a drastic drop in how often it is selected, because zero participation only means the activities were not observed in the later window; sampling differences in variant frequencies can also make a variant appear in one window only, although activities present in about 70 of 200 traces are not rare. The single after-window occurrence of A20, which routed to A30 instead of A26, rests on one trace and does not establish a new connection. [OCC002, DIR002, POS003]
