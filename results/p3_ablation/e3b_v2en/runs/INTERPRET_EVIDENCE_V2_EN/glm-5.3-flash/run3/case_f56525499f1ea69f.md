### Observed differences

- In the later window, six activities — A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 — appear in nearly every case (about 98–100 in 100 traces, up from about 49–52 in 100), each occurring about once per trace rather than in only half of the traces. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009]
- Before, these six formed two mutually exclusive trios: {A33, A35, A36} and {A37, A38, A39} always ran together within a trio but never across trios (A33 and A37 co-occurred in 0 of 200 before-traces). In the later window, every cross-trio pair co-occurs in about 98 in 100 traces, with the first trio placed before the second in about 97 in 100 of those, and within-trio pairs rose from about 49 in 100 to about 99 in 100 traces. [REL037, REL039, REL042, REL046, REL049]
- The surrounding anchors moved in step: A30 now precedes all six activities in about 98 in 100 traces (before about 49 in 100) and A31 follows them in about 98 in 100 (before about 49 in 100); A30's outgoing transitions now lead into {A35, A33, A36} in about 97 in 100 cases (previously an even mix of both trios), and A33's direct transition to A31 fell from about 36 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to under 1 in 100, replaced by transitions into A37/A38/A39 in about 35 in 100. [REL025, REL028, REL031, REL034, DIR006, DIR007]
- Step distances grew exactly where a trio would have been inserted: the gap from A30 to A37/A38/A39 moved from mostly 1–3 positions to 4–5, the gap from A33/A35/A36 to A31 likewise moved from 1–3 to 4–5, and the A30→A31 pair within five steps nearly vanished (200 before-traces vs 5 after). Event positions shifted consistently: A33–A36 earlier (mean about 0.75 to 0.68), A37–A39 later (about 0.73 to 0.77), A30 earlier and A31 later. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG005, LAG006, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS009]
- A14, A15 and A16, present in every trace, now co-occur with all six activities (before about half of traces): A14 is almost always before them, while A15 and A16 precede them in about 59 in 100 traces and are interleaved or repeated in about 40 in 100. [REL007, REL013, REL019]
- Separately, A04 became much rarer (about 14 in 100 later traces vs 31 in 100 before), its follow-up spread out (A16 fell from about 81 in 100 of A04's outgoing transitions to about 36 in 100, based on 28 outgoing transitions after vs 64 before) and its position moved later and more variable; A06 and A10 roughly doubled in participation but remain rare (about 11–12 in 100 traces). [OCC001, DIR002, POS001, LAG001, OCC002, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports, as a possibility rather than a confirmed modification, a specific structural hypothesis: the segment between A30 and A31 previously executed an exclusive choice — each trace ran either the trio {A33, A35, A36} or the trio {A37, A38, A39} — and in the later window runs both trios in a fixed order, A30 then {A33, A35, A36} (internal order varying) then {A37, A38, A39} then A31. The support is direct and consistent across indicator families: zero cross-trio co-occurrence became near-universal co-occurrence with stable first-trio-before-second ordering, successor profiles rerouted accordingly (A30 into the first trio; first-trio members into the second trio instead of A31), and the step-distance and position shifts match an insertion of one trio before the other; the volume of qualifying items (89 against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed) also cannot be explained by chance as a whole. [REL039, REL046, DIR006, DIR007, LAG003, LAG006, POS006, POS009]

Limitations: the many items largely restate one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, and the hypothesis covers only the relative order of the two trios, not a fixed order within them. Sampling differences in variant frequencies cannot be fully ruled out — the before window would have to have missed traces containing both trios that later make up about 98 in 100 cases, possible only if such traces were rarer than about 2 in 100 before. A04's reduced participation and rerouting, and the mild rise of A06 and A10, are real behavioural differences the evidence locates but does not connect to the same structural cause. [OCC001, DIR002, OCC002, REL039, REL049]
