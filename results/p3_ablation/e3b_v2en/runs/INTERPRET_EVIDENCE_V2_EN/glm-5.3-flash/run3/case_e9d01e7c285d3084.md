### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window A30, A31 and A36 are executed repeatedly within traces: activities that previously each ran once per trace in the fixed order A14 → A30 → A36, with A36 then flowing to A25, now run about 3.5 times per trace, A36 typically flows back to A30, and the former strict ordering gives way to interleaved or repeated execution in about 68 in 100 traces, while about 32 in 100 traces still show a single strict pass. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL003, REL004, REL005]

The repetition is shown directly by occurrence counts and recurrence shares, the A36 → A30 return edge is observed directly in the routing profile, and the dissolution of the former order is observed relationally and in the position gaps. The 14 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.49 expected by chance for the 99 tested units, every item points in the same direction, supports are large (200 traces per window; about 700 observed outgoing transitions of A36 afterwards), and each difference is larger than all but about 1 in 200 random re-splits. [OCC001, DIR001, REL003, LAG002]

Limitations: most items describe one underlying behaviour rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot rule out that the later window simply contains far more of a previously rare repeating variant — a sampling shift in variant frequencies rather than a modification of the control flow. The interleaved-or-repeated category does not separate interleaving from repetition, so the internal order of the repeated block is not pinned down, and A31 shows the same repetition pattern without any relational or routing evidence covering it.
