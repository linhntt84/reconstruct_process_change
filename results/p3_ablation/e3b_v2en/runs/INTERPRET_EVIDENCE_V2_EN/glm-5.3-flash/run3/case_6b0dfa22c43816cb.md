- Activity A02 becomes much rarer and earlier: it appears in about 75 in 100 traces in the earlier window but only about 21 in 100 in the later window, and its median position moves from near the end of the trace (about 0.97 of the trace) to about the middle (about 0.45). Correspondingly, the other flagged activities now run without A02 in about 79 in 100 traces, whereas before A02 almost always co-occurred with them, after them. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the block A35–A36–A41 executes after A26 and A27 rather than before them, with the connections rewired accordingly: A25 leads directly into A27, A26 opens into the block, and the block closes into A30 (previously A25 opened into the block, the block closed into A27, and A26 passed straight to A30). It also supports a second, separate change in the tail, where A49 usually connects directly to A50 and A02 — previously the intermediate step in about three quarters of traces — mostly drops out and, when present, sits much earlier and routes elsewhere; A06 and A10 are additionally executed about three times as often. These conclusions rest on relational order evidence, transition shares and adjacency gaps that all point the same way, each backed by roughly 200 traces per window. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, LAG002, LAG005, DIR011, LAG009, OCC001, POS001, OCC002, OCC003]

The 45 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.83 expected by chance when 166 units are screened, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. However, many items restate the same underlying traces — the six pair-order items and the five routing items largely reflect one block rearrangement — so they are not independent confirmations. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of process variants between the two 200-trace samples (for example, a variant without A02, or with a different block order, becoming more frequent) rather than a modification of one shared model; the near-deterministic order reversal (from about 99 in 100 to about 1–2 in 100) and the large participation drop make pure sampling noise implausible, but the evidence cannot separate a variant-mix shift from a structural change. A few adjacency items (A25→A36, A25→A41, A36→A27) rest on only 2–4 observations in one window and were not relied upon. [REL010, REL013, DIR005, DIR006, OCC001, LAG003, LAG004, LAG007]
