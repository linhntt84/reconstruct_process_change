### Observed differences

- Activities A10 and A11 still appear in every case, but they repeat less: mean occurrences per trace fell from about 1.9 to about 1.6, and the share of cases in which they recur dropped from about 47 in 100 to about 38 in 100. Both items show identical values, so they likely describe one underlying behaviour. [OCC001, OCC002]
- In the later window, A14, A15 and A20 run much earlier in the case: their mean relative position moved from about 60% to about 32% of the trace length, while their participation stayed roughly stable (about 3 in 10 cases each). [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- Their order relative to A21 and A22 flipped completely. Before, A21 and A22 always came before A14, A15 and A20 when they co-occurred (about 31–38 in 100 cases); in the later window, A14, A15 and A20 always come before A21 and A22 (about 31–35 in 100 cases), with no exceptions in either window. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Routing around these activities changed wholesale: A14, A15 and A20 previously led to A23 or A27 (roughly half each) and now lead only to A21; A40 previously led only to A21 and now leads to A14, A15 or A20 (about a third each); A22 and A23, which earlier also routed into the trio, now lead only to A23 and only to A27 respectively. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- The chain A22 → A23 → A27 became tightly sequential: the A22→A23 and A23→A27 gaps are now always a single step (previously split between one and two steps), the A22→A27 gap shortened from three to two steps, and the A40→A21 gap lengthened from one to two steps, consistent with an activity now sitting between A40 and A21. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the conditional block formed by A14, A15 and A20 — roughly one of the three per case — now executes between A40 and A21, whereas it previously ran inside the late A22 → A23 → A27 segment. The order flip relative to A21 and A22 is complete with no exceptions in either window, the positional shift (about 60% to about 32% of the trace) matches it, and the rerunning of edges fits a relocation exactly: A40 now feeds the trio, the trio leads only to A21, A22 and A23 no longer route into the trio, the A22 → A23 → A27 gaps tightened to one step, and the A40 → A21 gap grew from one to two steps. With 24 qualifying items against roughly 0.39 expected if nothing had changed, chance as a whole cannot account for the pattern. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, POS001, POS002, POS003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Limitations remain: the evidence cannot distinguish a true move of the block from removing it late and inserting an equivalent occurrence early, and it says nothing about whether the choice among A14, A15 and A20 within a case changed. The reduced repetition of A10 and A11 is not linked to any other activity by relational or routing evidence, so it may be an independent difference or a sampling fluctuation; variant frequencies can also differ between finite windows without any change, although the complete rerouting and order reversals observed here are difficult to explain by sampling alone.
