### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in which the segment A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32, previously executed with mixed orders, varying distances and several alternative successors, runs as one essentially fixed sequence in the later window, while A08 is executed in far fewer cases (about 29 vs 72 of 100 traces) with its dominant hand-over to A16 largely replaced by a direct A15 → A16 route, and A10 is executed about three times as often. Supporting signs: 82 items qualified against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process; the order reversals are near-complete on activity pairs observed in all 200 traces of both windows (for example A24 before A20 fell from about 73 to about 0.5 of 100), which random re-splits of stable behaviour do not produce; and the distance, routing and position items all agree with one reorder rather than pointing in different directions. [REL014, REL017, REL019, LAG012, LAG013, LAG021, DIR007, DIR013, POS005]

Limitations: the evidence cannot separate a genuine re-sequencing within each case from a shift in the mix of process variants — cases already following the fixed sequence may have existed before at a low share and simply become dominant, and the A08 decline may reflect a variant containing A08 becoming rarer rather than A08 being dropped from otherwise similar cases. Many items restate one underlying difference (the A08 relation items reflect its participation drop; the lag and routing items reflect one block reorder), so they are not independent confirmations; a few items rest on only 1–3 observations in one window (for example LAG019, LAG020, LAG024, LAG025, LAG027, LAG028) and were not relied upon, and smaller shifts such as A10's increase (15 vs 44 supporting traces) could partly reflect sampling differences in variant frequencies.
