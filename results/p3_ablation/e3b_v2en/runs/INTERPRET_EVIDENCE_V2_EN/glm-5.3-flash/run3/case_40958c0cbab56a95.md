### Observed differences

- In the later window, the three activities A26, A27 and A28 have almost disappeared: each ran in every one of the 200 earlier traces but in only about 2 in 100 later traces (3 of 200), and the three no longer appear together (co-occurrence fell from 100 in 100 traces to fewer than 1 in 100). [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL036, REL037, REL042]
- In the earlier window this trio always sat between A44 and A29: A44 preceded it in about 97–99 in 100 traces and it preceded A29 in about 97–99 in 100; in the later window its co-occurrence with A44 and with A29 is down to about 1–2 in 100. [REL038, REL039, REL047, REL048]
- Three previously rare activities, A19, A20 and A21, now appear in about 30, 33 and 38 in 100 later traces respectively (up from about 1–2 in 100), and they are mutually exclusive: each pair co-occurs in at most about 0.5 in 100 traces, and the share of traces containing neither member of a pair matches the third activity's participation. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL013, REL014, REL021]
- The new activities occupy the old trio's position: A44 precedes A19, A20 or A21 in about 30–37 in 100 later traces, and each of them in turn precedes A29 in about 30–37 in 100; accordingly, A44's outgoing transitions (about 200 observed in each window) shifted from roughly 25–28 in 100 going to each of A26, A27, A28 to roughly 25–27 in 100 going to each of A19, A20, A21. [REL018, REL019, REL025, REL026, REL032, REL033, DIR007]
- The event distance from A44 to A29 shrank: in the earlier window it was typically 4–5 positions (about 78 in 100 at 4), in the later window typically 2–3 positions (about 79 in 100 at 2). [LAG009]
- When they occur, A19, A20 and A21 now sit consistently at about one-third of the trace length (medians near 0.36), whereas their few earlier occurrences were spread widely. [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- Participation in A10 rose moderately, from about 14 to about 23 in 100 traces (mean occurrences per trace 0.14 to 0.26). [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the segment composed of A26, A27 and A28 — previously executed together, between A44 and A29, in essentially every case — is no longer run, and that in roughly 30–38 in 100 later cases a single activity, one of A19, A20 or A21 apparently chosen per case, now runs between A44 and A29, with the remaining cases passing from A44 towards A29 without any of these activities. The volume of qualifying items (81, against roughly 0.89 expected for an unchanged process) rules out chance for the pattern as a whole, and the key signs are direct and consistent across indicator families: the participation swings rest on large support in both windows (200 vs 3 traces for the old trio; 2–4 vs 60–75 for the new activities), A44's successor profile changed with about 200 outgoing transitions observed per window, the ordered relation A44 before the new activity before A29 holds in about 30–37 in 100 later traces for each of the three, and the A44-to-A29 distance shortened from 4–5 to 2–3 positions, matching a three-activity stretch giving way to a single activity. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR007, REL018, REL019, REL025, REL032, LAG009]

Limitations: the old trio still occurs in about 3 of 200 later traces, so it is strongly reduced rather than eliminated, and the later profiles for A26, A27 and A28 rest on only 3 traces each; with two finite windows the evidence cannot distinguish a discrete structural substitution from a shift in the mix of two pre-existing process variants (sampling differences in variant frequencies), and the many relation items describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR004]
