### Observed differences

- The three activities **A19, A20 and A25** were each executed in roughly a third of traces in the earlier window (about 37, 31 and 35 in 100 traces respectively) but are almost completely absent in the later window: A19 appears in only 1 of 200 traces, A20 and A25 in none. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- Routing out of **A41** changed almost completely. Earlier, its outgoing transitions went to A19 (about 29 in 100), A25 (about 32 in 100) and A20 (about 26 in 100), with A26 almost never the direct successor; in the later window about 86 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions go directly to A26. [DIR005]
- The distance from A41 to A26 shrank by one step: earlier, A26 typically followed A41 two positions later (about 85 in 100 cases, three positions in about 15 in 100), while in the later window A26 immediately follows A41 in about 86 in 100 cases. [LAG001]
- The former intermediate activities no longer precede A26 or A31, and A41 no longer precedes them: for example, A19 preceded A26 in about 35 in 100 traces earlier and in essentially none later, with A26 now occurring alone in about 99.5 in 100 traces; the same pattern holds for A20 and A25 with A26 and A31, and for the pairs of A41 with A19, A20 and A25. [REL004, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL013, REL014, REL015]
- A secondary shift at **A21**: the share of its outgoing transitions to A24 rose from about 14 in 100 to about 35 in 100, while the share to A31 fell from about 54 in 100 to about 38 in 100; A24 also increasingly appears without its former partners (A24 alone in about 56 in 100 traces, versus about 32–33 in 100 earlier). [DIR003, REL002, REL007, REL012]
- Small positional shifts accompany this: A26 now appears slightly earlier within traces (mean position about 0.39 to 0.37) and A41 slightly later (about 0.32 to 0.33), with both still present in every trace. [POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the alternative activities A19, A20 and A25 that previously ran between A41 and A26 are no longer executed, so that A41 proceeds directly to A26. The supporting signs are consistent and direct: participation in all three activities fell from 62–73 of 200 traces to at most one; A41's successor profile moved from the three alternatives (about 86 in 100 outgoing transitions combined) to A26 alone; and the A41→A26 distance changed from two positions to one, which is relational evidence of a placement change rather than a mere frequency shift. The 29 evidence items are many times the roughly 0.57 expected by chance when nothing has changed, and every difference is larger than in essentially all random re-splits of the data. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, LAG001]

Limitations remain. The evidence shows execution behaviour, not the underlying model, so it cannot distinguish a branch that was removed from one that still exists but is no longer chosen. The routing shift at A21 towards A24 and A24's higher standalone frequency are consistent secondary changes whose relationship to the main difference the evidence cannot establish, and the after-profile for A19's outgoing transitions rests on a single observation and should not be relied on. Sampling differences in variant frequencies cannot plausibly explain the near-total disappearance of activities that occurred in about a third of earlier traces, but they remain a possible contributor to the smaller position shifts at A26 and A41. [DIR001, DIR003, REL002, POS004, POS005]
