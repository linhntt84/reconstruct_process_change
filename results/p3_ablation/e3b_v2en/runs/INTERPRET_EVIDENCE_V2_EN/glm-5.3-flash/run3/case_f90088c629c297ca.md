### Observed differences

- In the before window, A36 was directly followed by A21 in essentially every trace: the pair occurred at lag 1 in all 200 traces, and A36's only observed outgoing transition led to A21. [LAG001, DIR004]
- In the later window, A36's outgoing transitions are redistributed: it is followed by A14 in about 37 of 100 traces, by A20 in about 34, by A15 in about 28, and by A21 directly in only about 1. [DIR004]
- A14, A15 and A20 were not observed at all in the before window. In the later window each appears at most once per trace (no repetition), in about 74, 67 and 57 of 200 traces respectively, at roughly one third of the trace's position. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- The three activities behave as mutually exclusive alternatives: no later-window trace contains more than one of them, and together they cover about 99 of 100 traces. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- Each new activity sits between A36 and A21: A36 precedes it in every trace containing the pair, and its only observed outgoing transition is to A21. Accordingly, the A36–A21 pair now appears at lag 2 in about 99 of 100 traces instead of lag 1, and A21's mean relative position moved slightly later (about 0.37 to 0.39 of the trace). [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, POS004]
- A21 and A36 themselves still appear in every trace in both windows; only their relationship changed. [POS004, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that an exclusive branch executing exactly one of A14, A15 or A20 was inserted between A36 and A21. This is shown directly, not just inferred from frequencies: A36's outgoing transitions moved from A21 alone to the three new activities (about 99 of 100 later traces), each new activity in turn leads only to A21, the orderings A36-before-branch and branch-before-A21 are observed consistently, and the A36–A21 pair shifted from immediate succession to a lag of exactly 2. The 21 qualifying items far exceed the fewer than one item expected by chance across the 74 tested units, and every item's difference is larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits, so the ensemble is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001, POS004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL005]

Limitations remain. The split between the three branches (roughly 37/34/28 per 100) is a sample estimate and may differ from the underlying routing probabilities. A competing explanation the two finite windows cannot fully rule out is that this branch existed before but was exercised so rarely that 200 traces did not show it; its appearance in about 99 of 100 later traces makes a pure sampling fluctuation implausible but not impossible. The evidence also says nothing about what selects among A14, A15 and A20, and the positional findings rest on event order rather than clock time. [DIR004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, LAG001]
