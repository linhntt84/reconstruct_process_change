### Observed differences

- In the later window, A16, A17 and A18 each appear in about 79 in 100 traces (158 of 200), up from about 18 in 100 (37 of 200) earlier; each occurs at most once per trace in both windows. A19, A20 and A21 show the mirror image, falling from about 82 in 100 traces (163 of 200) to about 21 in 100 (42 of 200). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- The successors of A15 — which appears in every trace in both windows — are redistributed: earlier, about 82% of A15's outgoing transitions led to A19, A20 or A21 (roughly 29%, 24% and 29%) and 18% to A16, A17 or A18 (roughly 7%, 9% and 3%); in the later window, about 79% lead to A16, A17 or A18 (roughly 29%, 22% and 29%) and 21% to A19, A20 or A21 (roughly 7%, 8% and 6%). [DIR001]
- A15 now precedes A16, A17 and A18 in about 79 in 100 traces (about 18 in 100 earlier) and precedes A19, A20 and A21 in about 21 in 100 (about 82 in 100 earlier); the reverse order is never observed for any of these pairs. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- The two activity groups keep the same internal behaviour but swap how often they run: within-group pairs (e.g., A16–A17, A19–A20) co-occur whenever their group appears, with mixed internal order in both windows, while cross-group pairs (e.g., A16–A19) never co-occur — only the share of "one group appears alone" flips from roughly 82/18 to roughly 21/79. [REL007, REL009, REL013, REL018, REL022, REL025]
- A25 is present in every trace in both windows; earlier it co-occurred with A19/A20/A21 (interleaved with A19 in about 36 in 100 traces), in the later window it co-occurs with A16/A17/A18 (A16 before A25 in about 45 in 100, interleaved in about 34 in 100). [REL012, REL017, REL021, REL024, REL026, REL027]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the routing after A15 changed: the path through A16, A17 and A18 is now executed in about 79 in 100 cases instead of about 18 in 100, while the alternative path through A19, A20 and A21 dropped from about 82 in 100 to about 21 in 100, with A15 and A25 present throughout and both paths keeping the same internal composition and order behaviour. The shift is visible directly in the redistribution of A15's outgoing transitions, the participation changes are exactly complementary, and the relational items show the same swap from the pair perspective; with 34 qualifying items against roughly 0.52 expected for an unchanged process, chance alone is an unlikely explanation for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL004, REL009, REL022]

Limitations: the 34 items are strongly correlated — the occurrence, direction and relation indicators largely describe the same traces — so they are not independent confirmations. The evidence shows only that the branch frequencies swapped; it cannot show whether the selection rule itself changed or whether other mechanisms steered more cases into the A16–A18 path. Sampling differences in variant frequencies remain a competing explanation for any frequency shift between two windows, although differences of this size (exceeding about 995 in 1000 random re-splits) make pure sampling noise an unlikely sole cause. [DIR001, OCC001, REL007]
