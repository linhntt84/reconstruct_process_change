### Observed differences

- In the later window, A19, A20 and A25 have all but disappeared: each ran in about 33, 29 and 40 of 100 traces before, but in only about 1 of 200 traces after (65, 57 and 81 traces before versus 1 each after). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- Routing into these activities collapsed at their neighbours: A27 sent about 52 of 100 of its outgoing transitions to A28 before (with roughly 20 to A25, 13 to A19 and 10 to A20), and about 98 of 100 to A28 in the later window; A28 sent about 43 of 100 to A29 before (with roughly 19 to A25, 18 to A19 and 16 to A20), and about 97 of 100 to A29. Both profiles rest on roughly 200 observed transitions per window. [DIR005, DIR006]
- The A27 → A28 → A29 sequence became immediate: A28 followed A27 one step later in about 98 of 100 later traces (about 52 before), A29 followed A28 one step later in about 96 of 100 (about 43 before), and A29 followed A27 two steps later in about 96 of 100, where previously it followed three steps later in about 90 of 100. [LAG001, LAG004, LAG002]
- Co-occurrence of A19, A20 or A25 with A27, A28 or A29 fell to near zero: for example, A25 and A29 appeared together in about 40 of 100 earlier traces (with A25 before A29 in essentially all of those) but in about 1 of 200 later traces, and similarly for A19 and A20; A27, A28 and A29 themselves appear in every trace in both windows. - A10's outgoing transitions were redistributed, from a profile concentrated on A15, A47 and A16 to one spread more widely with A26 the largest at about 17 of 100; this rests on a moderate 35 versus 53 observed transitions. [DIR001]
- Minor positional shifts occurred: A27's mean position moved slightly later (about 0.46 to 0.48 of trace length) and A29's slightly earlier (about 0.57 to 0.55), with both present throughout. [POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in which the alternative routes that passed through A19, A20 and A25 between A27/A28 and A29 are no longer taken: in the later window, work at A27 goes almost always directly to A28, and A28 directly to A29, while A19, A20 and A25 are executed in almost no traces. The thirty qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.56 expected if nothing had changed, and the core findings are direct and mutually consistent across occurrence, routing, relation and lag indicators, each resting on about 200 observations per window [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, DIR006, REL003, REL005, REL009, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004].

Limitations remain: the exact downstream behaviour of A19, A20 and A25 in the later window cannot be established, since the observed successors (A38 after A19, A37 after A20, A31 after A25) and the A28-to-A25 lag item each rest on a single trace [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG003]. The evidence also cannot rule out that A19, A20 and A25 persist on rare paths — each still appears in about 1 of 200 later traces — and a finite-sample under-representation of a rare variant containing them is a competing explanation, although the joint disappearance of three activities together with the routing consolidation at A27 and A28 makes pure sampling variation hard to sustain. Several items describe the same underlying trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations [OCC001, REL005, LAG002].
