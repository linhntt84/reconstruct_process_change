### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new step was added between A41 and A26, executed as one of three alternatives. In the earlier window a case typically went from A41 straight to A26 (about 82 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions, 83 in 100 of the pairs one step apart); in the later window about 87 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions lead instead to A19, A25 or A20 — chosen in roughly equal shares (about 38, 35 and 30 in 100 cases) — and each of these hands over to A26 in about 90–95 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, so nearly every later case passes through exactly one of them where previously it went directly on. [DIR041, LAG002, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, REL001, REL002, REL005]

The placement is shown relationally, not only through frequencies: each new activity follows A41 and precedes A26 wherever both occur, the new activities lead to A26 in about 90–95 in 100 of their outgoing transitions, the A41–A26 gap moved from one to two–three steps, and A26 now sits later in the trace while A41 is unchanged. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, LAG001, POS004, POS005]

Limitations: each window holds 200 traces, so the split among the three alternatives is a sample estimate, and the evidence provides no chance-expectation baseline for the item counts. The rare direct entries into A26 from other activities (A02, A04, A06, A08, A10), each seen only a handful of times earlier and largely absent later, may reflect the same rerouting or only sampling. An important competing reading the evidence cannot rule out is that A19, A20 and A25 are three separate optional activities that happen to exclude each other, rather than alternative variants of one added step. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR006, DIR008, DIR010]
