### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a reordering around A20, A22, A21, A25 and the block A30/A31/A36. In the earlier window, A20's outgoing transitions went to A30, A31 and A36 (about 40, 33 and 28 in 100) and A22 followed A20 about four steps later in every trace, while A21 always led directly to A25 (within one step in 100 in 100 traces). In the later window, A20 leads almost only to A22 (about 99 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, within one step), A21 leads to A30, A31 and A36 (about 36, 33 and 31 in 100) instead of to A25, and the block now leads to A25 (about 30–38 in 100) and practically never to A22. [DIR014, DIR015, DIR018, DIR019, DIR024, LAG001, LAG005]
- Matching this, the ordered pairs A20→A30/A31/A36 and A30/A31/A36→A22, found within five steps in 200 of 200 traces earlier, are found within five steps in only about 2 of 200 traces later (those later profiles rest on very few observations). [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG009, LAG012]
- The relative order of the block and A21/A22 reversed: earlier, A30/A31/A36 came before A21 and before A22 in 100 in 100 traces; later, A21 and A22 come before them in about 99 in 100. All these activities still occur together in every trace in both windows. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Event positions shifted accordingly (positions are event indices, not clock time): A22's mean position moved from about 0.47 to 0.38 of the trace, A21's from about 0.62 to 0.53, and A30/A31/A36's from about 0.41 to about 0.59, while A20 (about 0.35) and A25 (about 0.65) stayed in place. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS005, POS006, POS007]
- Within the block nothing changed: A30, A31 and A36 still co-occur in all traces and appear in both orders in similar proportions (for example A30 before A31 in about 56 in 100 traces earlier and 49 in 100 later), so they have no fixed internal order in either window. [REL007, REL008, REL009]
- Smaller shifts elsewhere: A01's outgoing mix moved (A05 from about 38 to 21 in 100 of A01's outgoing transitions, A03 from about 25 to 38); A34's and A35's outgoing transitions shifted towards A27 (about 42 to 52 and 58 to 48 in 100); A37's trace participation fell from about 21 to 16 in 100 and A38's mean occurrences per trace from about 0.86 to 0.76. [DIR001, DIR022, DIR023, OCC023, OCC024]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two segments exchanged their order: the block A30/A31/A36, which in the earlier window ran directly after A20 and before A22 and A21 (A20 → block → A22, with A21 → A25 at the end), in the later window runs after A22 and A21 (A20 → A22 → A21 → block → A25). The order change is shown directly by the relation items (block before A21/A22 in 100 in 100 traces earlier, after them in about 99 in 100 later, with co-occurrence unchanged at 100 in 100), by the successor changes at A20, A21 and the block, and by the lag changes A20→A22 (four steps to one) and A21→A25 (one step to four); the position shifts point the same way. Because every involved activity occurs in all traces in both windows and co-occurrence is unchanged, this is a reordering of existing behaviour rather than activities becoming optional, being added or removed, or one path replacing another. These items describe one underlying difference, not independent confirmations. [REL001, REL004, DIR014, DIR015, DIR018, LAG001, LAG005, POS002, POS003, POS005]

Limitations: the relation between A22 and A21 themselves is not observed, so whether they moved as one segment is inferred from their positions; the later profiles of the shifted five-step pairs rest on about 2 traces each; and about 1 in 100 later traces still show the earlier order. A competing explanation the evidence cannot exclude is that only the block's exit (now to A25) and A20's entry point (now A22) changed, without the A22–A21 segment moving as a fixed unit. [REL001, REL004, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: A01's routing among its existing successors shifted towards A03 and away from A05, and A34/A35 shifted outgoing share towards A27; A37 became less frequent and A38 slightly less repeated, while A10, A11 and A40 show marginally more occurrences per trace with slightly fewer repeating traces. These shifts are small and mostly within the range random splits produce, so they may be sampling variation; they are not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR001, DIR022, OCC023, OCC024, OCC009, OCC010, OCC025]
