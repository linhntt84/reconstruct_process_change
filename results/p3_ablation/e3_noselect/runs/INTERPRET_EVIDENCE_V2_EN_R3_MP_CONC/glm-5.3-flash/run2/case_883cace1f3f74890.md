### Observed differences

- Three activities that were not executed in the earlier window — A19, A20 and A25 (0 of 200 traces each) — run in the later window in about 30, 41 and 30 in 100 cases respectively (59, 82 and 60 of 200 traces), and they practically never appear together: almost every later case executes exactly one of the three, whereas no earlier case executed any of them. Each of these shifts is larger than in about 199 of 200 reference re-splits of unchanged data. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, REL001, REL002, REL006]

- The new activities are spliced into the segment A27 → A28 → A29. Earlier, A27 was followed directly by A28 in about 99 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, and A28 directly by A29 in about 99 in 100; later, A27 leads to one of the three new activities in about 55 in 100 transitions (A20 about 22, A19 about 15, A25 about 17) and A28 does so in about 43 in 100, while each new activity continues to A28 (about 52–56 in 100) or to A29 (about 42–46 in 100). [DIR027, DIR028, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025]

- Step distances match an insertion: the A27→A28 distance grew from one step in about 99 in 100 cases to two steps in about 55, the A28→A29 distance from one step in about 99 to two steps in about 44, and A27→A29 from two steps in about 99 to three steps in about 97. Each new activity almost always follows A27 and precedes A29, shows mixed order relative to A28, and sits at mid-trace positions (mean about 0.51 of the trace). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, POS001, POS002, POS003]

- Corroborating position shifts: A27's mean position moved slightly earlier (about 0.48 → 0.46 of the trace) and A29's slightly later (about 0.55 → 0.57), both larger than in about 199 of 200 reference re-splits. [POS004, POS006]

- Away from this segment, routing after A42 shifted: the share of A42's outgoing transitions to A43 fell from about 54 to 44 in 100 while the share to A44 rose from about 46 to 55; A43 then ran in about 62 → 56 in 100 cases, while A44 and A45 rose to about 67 in 100 each (from about 57). [DIR041, OCC039, OCC040, OCC041]

- In the A01/A05/A07/A09 cluster, choice points redistributed (A01→A09 about 42 → 21 in 100, A01→A05 about 28 → 40; A05→A09 about 25 → 44, A05→A07 about 38 → 24), with all four activities running slightly more often (about 48 → 54 in 100 cases). [DIR001, DIR005, DIR009, OCC001, OCC005, OCC007, OCC009]

- Smaller shifts, partly on thin support, include A15 and A16 repeating in about 35 → 30 in 100 cases, A10 appearing in about 5 → 14 in 100 cases (only 10 vs 27 traces behind it), and A40 routing to A39 more often (about 24 → 31 in 100). [OCC015, OCC016, OCC010, DIR039]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, cases execute one of three activities — A19, A20 or A25 — that were not executed in the earlier window, inserted between A27 and A29: directly after A27 in about 55 in 100 cases or directly after A28 in about 43 in 100, each continuing to A28 or A29, so that the previously consecutive chain A27 → A28 → A29 now contains one of these three alternatives in nearly every case. The supporting signs are direct and consistent across indicator families: appearance from 0 to 59–82 of 200 traces, new edges from A27/A28 into the three and from the three into A28/A29, strict mutual exclusivity among the three, longer step distances exactly between A27, A28 and A29, and mid-trace positions for the new activities. The main limitation is that the evidence shows the activities were not executed earlier, not why; it cannot distinguish genuinely new steps from re-labelled or previously unrecorded ones, and it cannot rule out that the two windows cover different case mixes in which the path through A19/A20/A25 was simply not exercised before — although the size and consistency of the shifts make a pure sampling accident unlikely. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, DIR027, DIR028, REL001, REL002, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: Routing after A42 moved from A43 towards A44 and A45 (A43 down to about 56 in 100 cases, A44 and A45 up to about 67), and choice points in the A01/A05/A07/A09 cluster redistributed with slightly higher participation for all four; the evidence cannot show whether these are independent changes or knock-on effects of the insertion, and the smaller shifts (A15/A16 repetition, A10, A40→A39) rest on modest support.
