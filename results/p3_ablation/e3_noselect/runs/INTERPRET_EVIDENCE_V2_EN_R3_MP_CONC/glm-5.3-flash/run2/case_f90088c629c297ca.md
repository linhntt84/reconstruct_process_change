### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A36 was always followed immediately by A21: A21 was the only observed successor of A36 (100% of its outgoing transitions), and the A36→A21 pair was adjacent — one event apart — in all 200 cases. A14, A15 and A20 were not observed in any of the 200 earlier cases. [DIR027, LAG001, OCC013, OCC014, OCC019]
- In the later window, A36 and A21 still occur in every case, but A36 now leads to A14 in about 37 in 100 cases (74 of 200), to A20 in about 34 in 100 (67 of 200) and to A15 in about 29 in 100 (57 of 200), while the direct move from A36 to A21 remains in only about 1 in 100 cases. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, exactly one of three activities that were absent from the earlier sample — A14, A15 or A20 — now executes between A36 and A21 in almost every case, in the fixed order A36 → (A14 or A15 or A20) → A21, whereas earlier A36 was always followed directly by A21 and none of the three appeared; the three behave as mutually exclusive alternatives taken in roughly equal shares (about 37, 29 and 34 in 100 cases), with the direct A36→A21 succession surviving in only about 1 in 100 later cases. The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: A36's successor profile changed from only A21 to mostly the three activities, each of their only observed successors is A21, the A36→A21 distance moved from one to two events, ordered co-occurrence places A36 before each new activity before A21, and the three never co-occur; participation rose from 0 of 200 cases to 57–74 of 200 for each. [OCC013, OCC014, OCC019, DIR010, DIR011, DIR016, DIR027, LAG001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

The evidence cannot show whether these are genuinely new steps or the same work recorded under different activity labels, nor what determines which of the three is chosen; the many agreeing items largely describe this single difference and are not independent confirmations. Sampling fluctuation alone is an implausible explanation because the three move from 0 of 200 cases to 57–74 of 200, but a relabelling of existing steps cannot be ruled out. [DIR027, LAG001, OCC013, OCC014, OCC019]

Other differences: the moderate exit-share shifts around A16–A19/A26 and A30–A33 with their exits to A27, the small participation moves of A30–A33, A35 and A13, and the decline of A34 (on few cases) are not part of this hypothesis; they are modest in size, and the evidence cannot show whether they form a second behavioural change or only sampling variability. [DIR019, DIR022, DIR012, OCC020, OCC022, OCC024, OCC025, OCC012]
