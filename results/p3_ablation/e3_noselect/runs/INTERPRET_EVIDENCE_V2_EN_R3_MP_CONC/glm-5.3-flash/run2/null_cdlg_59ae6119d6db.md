### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. Every observed difference falls within the range that random re-splits of the pooled data typically produce (empirical strengths between 0.0 and about 0.48), and the largest routing shifts rest on only about 22 observations per window, so sampling variation alone can explain them.
