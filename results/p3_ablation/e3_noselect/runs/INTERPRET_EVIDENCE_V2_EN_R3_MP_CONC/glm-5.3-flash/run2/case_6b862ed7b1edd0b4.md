### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment A19 → A25 → A20, entered from A41 and exiting to A26, changed from an optional branch — executed in about 47 in 100 cases, with the alternative of going from A41 directly to A26 in about 45 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions — to one executed in virtually every case, while the skip is now rarely observed. The change is shown directly and consistently across several views of the same behaviour: participation of A19, A20 and A25 rose from about 47–48% to about 99.5% with their trace positions unchanged; their co-occurrence rose from about 47 to about 99.5 in 100 cases in a fixed order; A41's successors moved from mostly A26 to almost exclusively A19; and the event distances between all pairs match the single chain A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 → A26. Limitations: the skip still appears in a small number of after-cases, so the alternative looks rarely taken rather than removed, and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply cover a different mix of cases rather than a changed routing rule.
