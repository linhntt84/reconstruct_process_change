### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the segment in which A31, A32 and A37 ran between A20 and A23 is bypassed: A20 flows directly into A23, and the trio — which still runs together in mixed internal order — executes in about 47 in 100 cases, after A21 and before A26, in place of A22, so that A22 and the trio have become near-perfect alternatives; earlier, every trace ran A20 → A31/A32/A37 → A23 → A21 → A22 → A26. [OCC017, OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, DIR014, DIR015, DIR018, DIR019, DIR024, LAG001, LAG005, REL006, REL011, REL014, REL017, REL020]

The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation of A22 and the trio halved (from 200 of 200 to about 94–107 of 200 traces); A20's successors changed from the trio to A23 almost completely and the A20–A23 distance fell from four positions to one; A21's outgoing split between A22 and the trio; the trio's successor A23 disappeared in favour of A26; and the relation items show the co-occurrence collapse between A22 and the trio together with the flipped order around A21 and A23. These items largely describe one coherent restructuring rather than independent confirmations, and the magnitudes (order relations moving from 1.0 to about 0.0, participation from 1.0 to about 0.5, each with empirical strength 0.995) far exceed what randomly re-splitting stable traces produces; the evidence includes no chance-expectation baseline, so the judgement rests on this directness and consistency. [OCC017, OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, DIR014, LAG001, DIR015, DIR018, DIR019, DIR024, REL011, REL006, REL014, REL017]

Limitations: the after-window lag profiles for pairs spanning A20/A21 and the trio rest on very few observations (support 1 of 200), so the trio's exact placement rests on the successor and relation items; the position shifts are event-position effects consistent with the shorter prefix and do not by themselves establish the move. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the exclusivity between A22 and the trio reflects two kinds of cases flowing through the same process rather than one changed routing rule, and that the old full sequence survives as a rare path (at most about 1 in 200 later traces). [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG009, LAG012, DIR014, POS001]
