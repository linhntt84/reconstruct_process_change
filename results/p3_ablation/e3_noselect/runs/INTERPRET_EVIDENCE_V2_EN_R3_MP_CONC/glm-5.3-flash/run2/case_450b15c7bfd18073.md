### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A21, A22 and A23 each ran in all 200 traces in the fixed sequence A21 → A22 → A23: A21's only observed successor was A22, A22 always preceded A23, and the distance from A21 to A23 was always 2 steps. [OCC018, DIR014, REL001, REL005, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change in the segment between A21 and A23: in the earlier window every observed case executed A21 → A22 → A23, whereas in the later window about half of the cases (about 49 in 100) no longer execute A22 and instead execute the three activities unobserved in the earlier sample — A27, A28 and A33 — between A21 and A23, in no fixed order among themselves, with the last of the three leading into A23; the other half still execute the original sequence with A22. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, DIR014, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL012, REL013, REL014, LAG001]

The change is shown directly and consistently by relational, successor, positional and lag evidence: A21 precedes each trio member and each trio member precedes A23, A22 and the trio never co-occur, A21's successor profile split from A22-only to roughly 51/49, the trio's positions fall between A21 and A23, and the A21→A23 distance widened from always 2 steps to 2 or 4 steps. The core items have roughly 100 traces behind each share and empirical strength near 0.995, so sampling variation alone is an unlikely explanation for the main difference. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL009, REL010, REL011, DIR014, DIR017, DIR018, DIR023, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG001, OCC018, OCC019, OCC020, OCC025]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show what determines which of the two paths a case takes, and zero participation in the earlier window means only that A27, A28 and A33 were not observed there (0 of 200 traces) rather than proving absence — although missing a path taken by about half of the later cases in 200 traces is hard to attribute to sampling. The evidence also contains no expected-by-chance item count, so the against-chance judgement rests on the empirical strength values. [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025]

Other differences: the small participation and routing shifts around A16/A17/A18/A24/A25, A29–A32 and A40 (about 2–14 percentage points) are not part of this hypothesis; being far smaller than the 49-point main difference, they could partly reflect sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows.
