### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A30, A31 and A36 each executed exactly once in virtually every case (mean about 1.0 occurrence per case, present in all traced cases, repeats in well under 1 in 100 cases). In the later window, each of the three executes about 3.5 times per case on average and repeats within the same case in about 68 in 100 cases. [OCC027, OCC028, OCC033]
- The exit from this segment changed: earlier, A36 was followed by A25 in about 97 in 100 of A36's outgoing transitions; in the later window, A36 is followed by A30 in about 70 in 100 and by A25 in only about 28 in 100. The internal links A30→A31 (about 98 in 100) and A31→A36 (about 98 in 100) and the entries from A19/A20 into A30 remain dominant in both windows. [DIR036, DIR030, DIR031, DIR019, DIR020]
- The order around the segment changed from fixed to mixed: earlier, A14 preceded A30 in about 99.5 in 100 cases, A14 preceded A36 in about 98.5 in 100 and A30 preceded A36 in about 98 in 100; in the later window the strict order holds in only about 32 in 100 cases, while about 68 in 100 show the activities interleaved or repeated. The activities still appear together in every case. [REL004, REL005, REL006]
- The tight spacing disappeared: A30 and A36 sat exactly two event positions apart in about 98 in 100 eligible cases earlier, but in only about 12 in 100 later, with larger gaps appearing. [LAG002]
- Relative event positions (not clock time) moved consistently with this: the spread of A30's and A36's positions grew from about 0.07 to about 0.12 and their upper quartiles moved later, while A14's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (about 0.14 to about 0.12), as expected when later parts of the case are repeated and cases become longer. [POS003, POS004, POS002]
- Routing inside the earlier A05/A07/A09 loop shifted: A05's transitions to A14 fell from about 35 to about 17 in 100 while A05→A07 rose from about 21 to about 38 in 100, and the gap from A05 to A14 lengthened (one-step gaps about 36 → 17 in 100, three-step gaps about 28 → 45 in 100). - Smaller shifts: A15 and A16 repeat somewhat more often (mean about 1.6 → 1.7 per case; repeats in about 44 vs about 37 in 100 cases); participation rose for A22 (48 → 55 in 100 cases), A08 (17 → 23) and A02 (17 → 23) and fell for A21 (54 → 47). [OCC015, OCC016, OCC022, OCC008, OCC002, OCC021]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment A30 → A31 → A36, which ran once per case in the earlier window between A14/A19/A20 and A25, is executed as a repeated cycle in the later window: A36 now leads back to A30 in about 70 in 100 of its outgoing transitions instead of proceeding to A25, so the cycle runs about 3.5 times per case on average (about 2.5 extra executions) before the case exits to A25, which itself still runs once per case. [DIR036, DIR030, DIR031, OCC027, OCC028, OCC033, OCC025]

Supporting signs: the repetition is measured directly on all 200 cases per window (mean occurrences about 1.0 → about 3.5; repeat-in-case share essentially 0 → about 68 in 100, each difference larger than in about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits); the new back edge A36→A30 is observed directly on about 700 outgoing transitions; and the order, spacing and position items all shift the way a repeated cycle would produce rather than an activity swap or replacement, since all involved activities remain present in every case in both windows. [OCC027, OCC028, OCC033, DIR036, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG002, POS003, POS004]

Limitations: all these items describe one underlying difference and are not independent confirmations; about a third of later cases still show a single pass, so the evidence cannot distinguish a uniform increase in iterations from a mixed population of single-pass and multi-pass cases; and no screening expectation is included, so sampling variability cannot be formally excluded, although a jump from 1.0 to 3.5 occurrences per case is far larger than the differences random re-splits of these windows typically produce. [OCC027, OCC028, OCC033, REL004]

Other differences: the routing shift inside the A05/A07/A09 loop (fewer direct A05→A14 steps, more A05→A07 and A07→A14 steps, longer A05→A14 gaps); the mild increase in repeats of A15/A16; the small participation shifts for A22, A08, A02 (up) and A21 (down); and isolated outgoing-profile redistributions at rarely executed points such as A02 and A45, which rest on few transitions and may be sampling noise. These are not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR005, DIR007, LAG001, OCC015, OCC016, OCC022, OCC008, OCC002, OCC021, DIR002, DIR045]
