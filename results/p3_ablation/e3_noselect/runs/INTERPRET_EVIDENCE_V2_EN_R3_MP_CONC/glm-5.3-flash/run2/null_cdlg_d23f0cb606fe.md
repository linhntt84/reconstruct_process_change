### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests, but does not confirm, that in the later window the paths around A05, A15, A13 and A14 were executed and repeated more often: A05 and A15 each occurred about 0.4 more times per trace and repeated in about 8 more traces per 100, A13 took part in about 9 more traces per 100 (A14 modestly more), while the successor structure around these activities stayed the same — pointing to existing paths being repeated more often rather than any rewiring or reordering. Supporting signs are that all occurrence components rose together for these four connected activities, that the outgoing transition volumes from A05, A15 and A13 grew exactly in proportion to their occurrence counts, and that the successor mixes were stable. What is missing: the key occurrence differences are only moderately unusual (empirical strengths about 0.62–0.66, so roughly a third of random re-splits produce differences at least this large), no screening baseline is provided to judge how many qualifying items chance alone would produce, and the A05 and A15 items are numerically identical, meaning they reflect the same traces rather than two independent confirmations. The competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is sampling variability in an unchanged process.

Other differences: After A01, traffic shifted modestly from A06 toward A05/A15 (about 52 to 46 in 100 for A06), directionally consistent with the main hypothesis but too small to carry it; A06 and A10 declined slightly, the A04/A08/A11/A12 group rose slightly in participation, and all other routing shares were nearly flat.
