### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A02 ran exactly once in every trace (200 of 200 traces, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace) and always near the start (mean position about 0.12 of the trace). In the later window, A02 does not appear at all (0 of 200 traces), and it has no observed outgoing transitions; the difference is larger than in essentially all random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, POS002, DIR002]
- Transitions into A02 disappeared: in the earlier window A01 sent about 12 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A02, in the later window none (its share to A04 rose from about 65% to 73%); A03 sent about 38% to A02 before and none after (shares to A05, A01 and A04 rose to roughly 35%, 30% and 28%); A05 sent about 6% to A02 before and none after (its share to A01 rose from about 67% to 78%). [DIR001, DIR003, DIR005]
- Relations with A02 collapsed from universal to zero: A01–A02 co-occurred in every earlier trace (A02 ran before A01 in about 90 in 100), while afterwards A01 appears in every trace without A02; A02–A03 and A02–A04 co-occurred in every earlier trace, afterwards A03 and A04 always appear without A02; A02–A05 co-occurred in about 47% of earlier traces, and afterwards A05 appears without A02 in about 51% of traces, with neither activity in about 49%. [REL001, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- The remaining activities sit earlier in the trace: A03's mean position fell from about 0.11 to 0.04 (75th percentile from 0.20 to 0), A04's mean from about 0.60 to 0.53, and A05's median from 0.40 to 0.25. Consecutive-pair distances also shortened: A03→A05 within one step rose from about 49% to 69%, A03→A01 from 18% to 31%, A01→A04 from 27% to 35%, and A05→A01 from 67% to 78%. These are event-position shifts, consistent with sequences compressing once A02 no longer sits between the other activities. [POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG004, LAG002, LAG001, LAG009, LAG003]
- Smaller shifts: A04 still occurs in every trace but repeats less (mean about 2.19 vs 1.94 occurrences per trace); A06 occurs in about 53% vs 52% of traces but repeats less (mean 1.19 vs 0.94; traces with repeats about 30% vs 22%); A07's outgoing transitions, observed only about 34–36 times per window, shifted from A01 (about 79% vs 56%) toward A05 (about 12% vs 33%). [OCC002, OCC004, DIR007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 is no longer executed in the later window: an activity that ran exactly once in every earlier trace, near the start and usually before A01, is absent from all 200 later traces, and its former predecessors (A01, A03, A05) now lead directly to their remaining successors (A04, A05, A01) instead of passing through A02. This reads as a structural change in which a mandatory activity was removed or became permanently skipped, with the start of the trace reorganised around A03, which now sits at the very beginning in most traces. The positional and lag shifts are consistent consequences of that removal rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: zero participation only shows A02 was not observed, so the evidence cannot rule out that A02 events stopped being recorded or that the activity was renamed, and no screening baseline was provided, so the judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the items rather than a chance-expectation count. [OCC001, POS002, DIR002, REL001, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR003, DIR005, POS003]

Other differences: A04 and A06 repeat somewhat less often per trace in the later window, and A07's few outgoing transitions shift from A01 toward A05; these modest shifts are not part of the main hypothesis and rest on limited observations for A07. [OCC002, OCC004, DIR007]
