### Observed differences

- In the later window, every observed activity occurs fewer times per trace. The five activities that move in lockstep (A01, A09, A10, A12, A13) fall from about 3.9 to about 3.5 occurrences per trace (roughly 9% fewer), A08 from about 7.8 to about 6.9 (roughly 12% fewer), A11 from about 4.0 to about 3.4, and A05 from about 1.9 to about 1.7. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010]
- This is a repetition effect rather than a presence effect: A01, A06, A08, A09, A10, A12 and A13 still appear in every trace in both windows, and recurrence support barely moves (for A01, about 75 in 100 traces before vs 74 in 100 after; for A08, 87 vs 86). [OCC001, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC009, OCC010]
- Three activities also become somewhat less prevalent: A03 appears in about 52 in 100 traces before vs 47 in 100 after, A05 in about 69 vs 65, and A11 in about 82 vs 78. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC008]
- Successor shares shift after A03: transitions from A03 to A01 rise from about 73 to 88 in 100 of A03's outgoing transitions, while those to A10, A09 and A08 shrink (A10 from about 14 to 6 in 100); this is the most unusual single difference in the evidence, larger than about 99 in 100 reference comparisons. [DIR002]
- Around the recurring group, the choice of successor also moves: after A09, transitions to A08 fall from about 42 to 36 in 100 while A10 and A12 rise; after A10, transitions to A08 rise from about 38 to 42 in 100 while A09 and A01 fall (both differences larger than about 90 in 100 reference comparisons). [DIR008, DIR009]
- Smaller share shifts appear after A04, A06, A07 and A13 (for example, after A07 the exit to A02 rises from about 46 to 53 in 100, and after A06 the share of A04 rises from about 23 to 29 in 100), while the routing profiles after A01, A05, A08, A11 and A12 stay nearly unchanged. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR010, DIR011, DIR012]

### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests that in the later window traces repeat the recurring activities fewer times: A01, A08, A09, A10, A11, A12 and A13 (with smaller absolute decreases for A03, A05 and A06) all show roughly 5–15% fewer occurrences per trace while their presence in traces stays essentially the same, consistent with fewer repetitions per trace rather than with activities being skipped or becoming optional. Supporting signs are the unanimous direction of all ten occurrence indicators with magnitudes roughly proportional to their baselines, unchanged participation for the core activities, and outgoing-transition counts that track the occurrence counts (A01's outgoing transitions fall from 775 to 702). What is missing: the individual differences are only moderately unusual (about 58–84 in 100 reference comparisons), the occurrence items are correlated and can all express one shared trace-length difference rather than ten independent signals, and no screening baseline is provided to compare the number of qualifying items against chance. An important competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the later window simply sampled somewhat shorter traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, DIR001]

Other differences: routing shares between existing successors shifted — most notably after A03, where the return to A01 rose from about 73 to 88 in 100 while the shares of A10, A09 and A08 shrank, and around A09/A10, where the choice among A08, A09 and A10 moved by 3–6 points; smaller shifts occurred after A04, A06, A07 and A13. These are changes in how often existing edges are taken, but the evidence cannot show whether they are independent of the repetition decrease or a byproduct of it. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR008, DIR009, DIR012]
