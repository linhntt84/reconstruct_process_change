### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A06 was removed from the process, or at least is no longer executed, and that the end of the process was restructured accordingly: the earlier alternation of A03 and A06 at the tail (A03 → A06 in about 53 in 100 traces, A06 → A03 in about 47 in 100) is replaced in the later window by A03 alone as the final step of every trace, entered directly from A04 in one step instead of sometimes via A06. [OCC005, POS003, REL002, REL003, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, POS001]

The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: the occurrence of A06 drops from every trace to none (support 200 to 0), its relations to A03 and A04 collapse from universal co-occurrence to none, both of its edges disappear while A04's successor share to A03 doubles, the two-step A04→A03 gap closes to one step, and A03 settles at the last position in all traces. These items come from different indicator families yet describe one coherent structural difference, each with an empirical strength of about 0.995, rather than scattered fluctuations. [OCC005, POS003, REL002, REL003, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, POS001]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A06 was not observed in the later window; the evidence cannot distinguish a formal removal of A06 from the activity remaining in the process but never being reached in this sample, and it offers no explanation why. No screening baseline (items expected without change) is included, so the chance comparison cannot be made formally — although a behaviour present in 200 of 200 traces vanishing from 200 traces is not a plausible sampling fluctuation. [OCC005]

Other differences: the small decreases in participation of A02, A07 and A09 and the slightly lower mean occurrences of A01, A04, A05 and A08 noted above; they are within the range produced by random re-splits and are not part of the main hypothesis.
