### Observed differences

- Activity A02 disappeared. In the earlier window it ran in all 200 traces, about 2.2 times per trace, with repeats in about 59 in 100 traces; in the later window it was not observed in any trace (0 of 200). [OCC001, POS001]
- Activity A03 appeared. It was absent from the earlier window (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it runs in every trace, exactly once per trace (mean 1.0, no repeats). [OCC002]
- A03 took over A02's place in the flow. Earlier, A05 always led to A02, and A02 led to A06 (~55% of its outgoing transitions) or A07 (~45%). In the later window, A05 leads to A03 in about 97 in 100 transitions, and A03 leads to A06 (~50%) or A07 (~50%) — almost the same split A02 had. A06, which previously always returned to A02, now goes to A07 (~51%), back to itself (~48%), or rarely to A03 (~2%). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- A03 occupies the same relative position A02 held: mean event position about 0.64 in both windows. [POS001, POS002]
- Relations flipped accordingly. Earlier, A02 co-occurred with A05 and with A07 in every trace; in the later window A05 and A07 each appear without A02 in all traces, while A03 co-occurs with A05 (always after A05) and with A07 (A03 first in about 98 in 100 traces). [REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL007]
- The repetition loop changed shape. Earlier, repeats of A06 were separated by A02 (gaps of 2 or 4 events); in the later window A06 repeats directly (gap of 1 event in about 26 in 100 cases) and reaches A07 directly (gap of 1 in about 35 in 100, previously never closer than 2). [LAG003, LAG004, LAG002]
- Smaller shifts: A06 participation fell from about 59 in 100 traces to about 49 in 100 (mean occurrences 1.2 → 1.0) and it now occurs later (mean position 0.61 → 0.73); A05 also occurs slightly later (mean position 0.36 → 0.42); A07 remains the final activity in both windows; a new A07 → A03 transition appears but rests on only 3 observed transitions. [OCC003, POS003, POS004, POS005, DIR005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the control flow around A02 and A03 was restructured: A02 no longer occurs, and A03 appears at the same spot in the sequence — A05 now leads into A03, and A03 leads on to A06 or A07 much as A02 did. At the same time the repetition behaviour changed: A03 executes only once per trace, and the loop that previously ran back through A02 (A06 → A02 → A06) is replaced by A06 repeating itself, so the earlier multi-pass behaviour (A02 about 2.2 executions per trace, repeated in roughly 59 in 100 traces) is gone. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR003, DIR004, REL005, REL007, LAG003]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: participation flips completely on both sides (200/200 → 0/200 and 0/200 → 200/200), A02's predecessor and successor profiles transferred to A03, both activities hold the same mean position, and the relational items reverse coherently. Note that the agreeing OCC, DIR, POS, REL and LAG items largely describe this one underlying restructuring rather than independent confirmations of separate changes. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS002, REL002, REL004]

Limitations: the evidence shows removal and insertion at the same position, not that A03 fulfils the same function as A02; a competing explanation — that A02 was removed and A03 added as two independent changes that happen to align — cannot be ruled out. The A07 → A03 edge rests on only 3 observations, and whether A06's slightly lower participation belongs to the same change is not determined. [DIR004, DIR005, OCC003]

Other differences: A06 participates in fewer traces (about 59 in 100 before, about 49 in 100 after) and appears later in the trace; A05's position moved slightly later; A07's position is essentially unchanged (still last). [OCC003, POS003, POS004, POS005]
