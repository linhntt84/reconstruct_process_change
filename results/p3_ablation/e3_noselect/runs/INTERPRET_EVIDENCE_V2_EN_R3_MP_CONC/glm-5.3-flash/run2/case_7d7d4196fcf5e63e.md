### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A02 appeared in about 97 of 200 cases (roughly half), about once per case; in the later window it appears in all 200 cases, about 1.8 times per case, and it now typically occurs as the first event of a case (median relative position moved from around the middle of the case to the very start). [OCC002, POS002]

- A02's successors changed completely: earlier, every observed outgoing transition of A02 led to A01; in the later window, about 57 in 100 lead to A04 and about 43 in 100 to A03, and an immediate A02→A01 step is no longer observed — A01, when present, now follows about two events after an A02 occurrence. [DIR001, LAG008]

- A01 receded and became tied to A02: participation fell from about 49 in 100 cases to about 31 in 100, and mean occurrences per case from 1.0 to about 0.4; earlier, A01 and A02 always ran in the same cases, whereas in the later window A02 runs without A01 in about 70 in 100 cases and A01 never runs without A02. [OCC001, REL001]

- The A08-opened part of the process receded: A08 ran in every case before (about 2.0 occurrences per case) but in only about 45 in 100 cases after (about 0.8 per case), moving from near the start of cases (median relative position about 0.10) to around the middle (median 0.50); its successors changed from A03 and A04 in equal shares to only A02. A04 still runs in every case, but the earlier pattern in which A08 and A04 always co-occurred (A08 immediately before A04) gave way to A04 running without A08 in about 55 in 100 cases. [OCC006, POS007, DIR004, REL018, LAG039]

- A06 and A07 also receded: A06 from about 49 in 100 cases (about 2.0 occurrences per case) to about 29 in 100 (about 0.7), and A07 from about 31 in 100 to about 15 in 100; A06's outgoing traffic shifted from roughly equal shares of A02 and A07 to A08 (about 53 in 100) and A07 (about 47 in 100). [OCC004, OCC005, DIR003]

- A03 kept a similar participation (about 49 in 100 cases before, 45 in 100 after) but changed successors: earlier all of its outgoing transitions went to A06, later about 52 in 100 go to A01 and 48 in 100 to A06. A02 and A04, which co-occurred in only about half of the cases before (A04 usually without A02), now always occur together, with A02 before A04 in about 55 in 100 cases. [OCC003, DIR002, REL008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural re-routing around A02: in the later window A02 is a mandatory, repeated opening activity that leads directly to A03 or A04 (with A03 onward to A01 in about half of its transitions), whereas the earlier A08-opened chain — A08 into A03 and A04, A03 into A06, A06 onward, and A02 into A01 — is executed in a minority of cases; as a result A01 appears only alongside A02, and A04 is now typically reached from A02 rather than from A08, while A08, A06, A07 and A01 participate in a substantially smaller share of cases. [OCC002, OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, REL001, REL008, LAG011]

The rerouting is shown directly in the successor profiles and is consistent across the occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators, with almost all differences exceeding those of about 995 in 1000 random re-splits; participation shifts of this size (A08 from every case to about 45 in 100, A02 from about half of cases to all) are not what a rare one-window variant would produce. [DIR001, DIR004, OCC002, OCC006]

The evidence cannot distinguish a rewiring of a single process from a shift in the mix of process variants between the windows; position and lag statements concern event order rather than clock time; the many pairwise items partly restate the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations; and no screening baseline was provided to compare the number of qualifying items with a chance expectation. [LAG008, POS002]

Other differences: A01's typical position moved earlier within cases, and A01→A04, when both run, now occurs three events apart instead of two; the A03–A04 relation and A04's occurrence and position profiles stayed essentially stable, with differences within the range of random re-splits. [POS001, LAG004, REL012, POS004]
