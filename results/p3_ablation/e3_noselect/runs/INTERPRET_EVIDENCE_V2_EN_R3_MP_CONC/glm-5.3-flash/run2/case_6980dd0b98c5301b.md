### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of an ordering change among A01, A06 and A07: A01, which previously executed last — after A06 and A07, in the sequence A08 → A06 → A07 → A01 — now executes directly after A08 and before A06 and A07, in the sequence A08 → A01 → A06 → A07, leaving A07 as the final step. All four activities occur in every trace in both windows, so this is a change in ordering constraints between existing activities, not a replacement of one path by another or a change in participation. [REL001, REL002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

The hypothesis is shown directly by the relational evidence — the A01–A06 and A01–A07 order reverses in all 200 traces per window — and is consistent across the successor changes at A08, A01 and A07, the changed gaps from A08, and the position shifts; the eleven items largely describe one underlying reordering rather than independent confirmations. The completeness of the reversal in both windows, together with the empirical strength of every item, makes chance sampling of a rare variant an unlikely competing explanation. [REL001, REL002, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Limitations: the evidence covers only these four activities and their relative order, so changes elsewhere in the trace (for example before A08) cannot be excluded; positions and gaps are event positions, not clock times; and the evidence cannot distinguish whether A01 was moved earlier or the A06–A07 block was shifted later, as both describe the same observed reordering.
