### Observed differences

- The directed edges among A01, A04 and A06 were inverted. In the earlier window, every observed outgoing transition of A01 went to A06 (192 transitions), A06 split its outgoing transitions between A05 (about 54 in 100) and A04 (about 46 in 100), and every outgoing transition of A04 went back to A01 (89 transitions). In the later window, every outgoing transition of A01 went to A04 (77 transitions), every outgoing transition of A04 went to A06 (181 transitions), and A06 split between A05 (about 57 in 100) and A01 (about 43 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

- Immediate succession flipped accordingly: in the earlier window the consecutive pairs were A01→A06, A06→A04 and A04→A01 (roughly three tenths of each pair's within-five-step mass at distance one), while A01→A04, A04→A06 and A06→A01 occurred only two steps apart; in the later window this reversed, with the new pairs immediate and the old pairs only two steps apart. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

- Participation moved with the rewiring: A01 appeared in about 52 in 100 traces before but only about 20 in 100 after (mean occurrences per trace 0.96 to 0.39; repetition in about 24 vs about 9 in 100 traces), A04 rose from about 24 to about 52 in 100 traces (mean 0.45 to 0.91), and A06 stayed at about 52 in 100. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC005]

- Which activity accompanies A06 flipped: before, A01 and A06 appeared together in about 52 in 100 traces (A01 first in about 28) while A04 and A06 appeared together in only about 24 in 100 (A06 alone in about 28); after, A04 and A06 appeared together in about 52 in 100 traces (A04 before A06 in about 32) while A01 and A06 appeared together in only about 20 in 100 (A06 alone in about 32). [REL001, REL002, REL003]

- Typical event position was exchanged between A01 and A04: A01's median position moved from the start of the trace to about one third in, A04's moved from about one third in to the start, and A06's stayed at about one third; these are event positions, not clock times. [POS001, POS002, POS003]

- The remaining monitored activities were stable: A03 and A05 each appeared in roughly half the traces in both windows (about 48 and 52 in 100) with no repetition, and A06's frequency and position barely moved. [OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the routing of the repetitive cycle linking A01, A04 and A06 was inverted, with A01 and A04 exchanging their roles around the unchanged middle activity A06: in the earlier window the cycle ran A01 → A06 → A04 and returned to A01, exiting to A05 from A06, while in the later window it runs A01 → A04 → A06 and returns to A01, still exiting to A05 from A06 (about 54 in 100 of A06's outgoing transitions before, about 57 in 100 after). The change is shown directly by the successor profiles of all three activities, and the immediate-succession, co-occurrence and position items flip in exactly the matching way; all sixteen qualifying items show differences larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits and describe one coherent rewiring rather than unrelated shifts. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG002, LAG004, REL002, REL003, POS001, POS002]

A consistent accompanying difference is participation: A01, which previously always co-occurred with A06, now appears in only about 20 in 100 traces, while A04 now appears in about 52 in 100 and always co-occurs with A06. This fits the cycle now being entered at A04 rather than A01, so that A01 executes only when the cycle repeats; however, the evidence covers only the outgoing transitions of A01, A04 and A06, so the edges into the cycle are not observed and the entry-point shift remains an interpretation. [OCC001, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL003]

An important competing explanation cannot be ruled out: A01 may simply have become optional before the cycle (or the entry may have moved for a related reason) rather than the two activities having exchanged roles in a single rewiring. No screening baseline was provided, so the number of qualifying items also cannot be checked against a chance expectation; the judgement rests on the directness and mutual consistency of the relational evidence. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

Other differences: none beyond the participation and position shifts of A01 and A04 described above; A03 and A05 showed no meaningful change (about 48 and 52 in 100 traces in both windows, no repetition). [OCC002, OCC004]
