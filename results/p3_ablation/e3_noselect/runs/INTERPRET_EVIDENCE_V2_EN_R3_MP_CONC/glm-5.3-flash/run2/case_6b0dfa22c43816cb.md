### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the late segment runs in a different order: the trio A35/A36/A41, which previously executed between A25 and A27, now executes between A26 and A30 — A25 connects directly to A27, and A26, which previously led straight to A30, now reaches it through the trio. In the same region, A02 was removed from between A49 and A50, which now connect directly, and A02's participation fell from about 75 to about 21 in 100 traces, its remaining occurrences sitting earlier in the case. This rests on direct relational evidence (order relations reversing from about 99 in 100 one way to about 97–98 in 100 the other, in pairs co-occurring in every trace in both windows), matching successor changes at A25, A26 and the trio, and lag shifts on pairs observed in all 200 traces per window; the many agreeing items describe this one reordering rather than independent confirmations. [DIR025, DIR026, DIR035, DIR036, DIR041, REL016, REL021, LAG013, LAG020, OCC002, DIR049, LAG046]

Other differences: A06 and A10 are executed more often (about 7→19 and 9→22 in 100 traces), A47's outgoing behaviour diversified, and repetitions of A44 and A50 stopped; A50's apparent new branching rests on only 3 observations. [OCC006, OCC009, OCC038, OCC044, DIR047, DIR050]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out concerns A02: it shows that A02 now rarely co-occurs with the late-segment activities, but not whether A02 became skippable in the flow or whether the later window simply contains fewer cases of the kind that includes A02 (a case-mix difference). The near-deterministic order reversals make a pure sampling artefact unlikely, but the reason for A02's decline stays open. [OCC002, REL001]
