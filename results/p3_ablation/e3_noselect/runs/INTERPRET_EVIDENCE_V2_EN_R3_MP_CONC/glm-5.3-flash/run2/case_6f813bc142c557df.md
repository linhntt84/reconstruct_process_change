### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering constraints among always-present activities changed: A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25 and A32 — each executed in every trace in both windows — were executed in varying relative orders in the earlier window and now follow one fixed sequence, A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32, with consecutive activities directly adjacent. The support is direct and consistent: relational items show the order shares moving from mixed or reversed to about 98–99 in 100 in favour of the new sequence; successor profiles collapse to a single dominant target per activity; lag items show the new adjacencies and the near-disappearance of reverse-direction pairs; and the position shifts point the same way. Because every involved activity occurs in all traces of both windows, this is a re-ordering of existing behaviour, not activities appearing or disappearing. The evidence cannot show whether the earlier variability was within-case flexibility or a mixture of several fixed execution variants of which one now remains, nor whether the change was abrupt or gradual. [REL027, REL029, REL032, REL037, DIR020, DIR023, DIR024, DIR026, DIR031, LAG010, LAG016, LAG024, LAG027, POS006, POS007]

Other differences: A08 is executed in far fewer traces (about 32 to 13 in 100) and, when executed, sits later and no longer leads predominantly to A15 (about 91 to 48 in 100 of its outgoing transitions); this participation and routing change for A08 is not tied by the evidence to the re-ordering above and could reflect a shift in which path variant is taken rather than a removal of A08. In addition, A10 (and modestly A02, A04, A06) participate more often, A15/A16 repeat more often within traces, and A40/A41 occur somewhat less while A43/A44 occur somewhat more.
