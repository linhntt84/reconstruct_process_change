### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The only movements are shifts of roughly one to four percentage points between existing successor edges of A01, A02 and A04 that point in different directions and rest on roughly 7–19 observed transitions each, and differences at least this large still arise in roughly 16–28 of 100 random re-splits of unchanged data, so sampling variation in a stable process cannot be ruled out [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004].
