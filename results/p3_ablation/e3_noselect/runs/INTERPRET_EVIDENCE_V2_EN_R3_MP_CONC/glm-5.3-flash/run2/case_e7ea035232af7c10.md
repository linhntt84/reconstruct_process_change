- **A three-activity block became mandatory.** In the earlier window, A36, A37 and A42 each ran in about 46 in 100 traces; in the later window each runs in about 199 of 200 traces. A27 rose the same way, from about 54 in 100 traces to all 200. [OCC031, OCC032, OCC037, OCC025]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window A36, A37 and A42 are executed in every case as a block placed directly after A25 and before A28, while A27 — which previously alternated with this block — is also executed in every case, following A26 and preceding A31. In the earlier window, about 54 in 100 cases ran A27 between A26 and A31 and about 46 in 100 ran the block there instead (in mixed internal order); in both variants A25 led directly to A28 and the segment A28→A29→A30→A34→A35→A26 was unchanged. [OCC025, OCC031, OCC032, OCC037, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR025, DIR026, DIR028, DIR035]

The change is shown directly by relations and successors, not only by frequencies: about twenty pair relations involving A25, A26, A27, A28, A31 and the block flip from "usually apart" to "always together" with a stable order (A25 before the block, the block before A26/A28/A27/A31), each difference exceeding 995 of 1000 random re-splits; successor profiles at A25, A26 and the block members agree with this; and the position and step-gap shifts match the new arrangement quantitatively. Sampling variation in 200-trace windows cannot plausibly produce this pattern of mutually consistent, near-complete flips. [REL005, REL009, REL011, REL017, REL020, REL012, REL013, DIR025, DIR026, DIR036, DIR037, DIR042, POS006, POS003, LAG001, LAG004, LAG010]

Limitations: the block's internal order remains unfixed (each pair runs in both orders at roughly 50/50 in both windows), and the evidence cannot show whether making the block mandatory and relocating it were introduced as one modification or two separate ones. [REL023, REL024, REL025]

Other differences: routing shares in the downstream cluster A21–A24/A32 shifted (A23 now goes to A32 in about 58 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, up from 38; A22 and A21 redirected towards A23), together with participation shifts of 4–8 in 100 for A17, A20, A21, A22, A23 and A24; these may be consequences of the main rearrangement, but the evidence does not tie them to it directly. The remaining changes (A01, A02, A06, A51, A44, A48, A49) are small or rest on fewer than about 25 observations.
