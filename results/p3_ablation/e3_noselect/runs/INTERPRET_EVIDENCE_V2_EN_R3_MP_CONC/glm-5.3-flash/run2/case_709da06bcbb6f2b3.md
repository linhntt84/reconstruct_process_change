### Observed differences

- Activity A01 is completely absent in the earlier window (0 of 200 cases); in the later window it runs exactly once in about 60 in 100 cases (119 of 200), never repeating within a case. [OCC001]
- In the later window, A01 sits early in the case (median relative event position about 0.17, roughly one-sixth into the trace) and its outgoing transitions go to A03 (about 32%), A10 (about 27%), A04 (about 23%) and A07 (about 18%); in the earlier window A01 had no observed occurrences at all. [POS001, DIR001]
- In the later window A03 and A10 hand over to A01 (about 34% of A03's outgoing transitions and about 40% of A10's, previously 0%), while their shares towards A04, A07 and each other shrink (A03→A04 about 26% to 15%, A03→A07 about 29% to 12%, A03→A10 about 45% to 40%; A10→A04 about 25% to 15%, A10→A07 about 20% to 17%, A10→A03 about 55% to 29%). [DIR002, DIR007]
- Before, A01 never co-occurred with A03 or A10 (each appeared in about 56% of cases, always without A01); in the later window A01 co-occurs with A03 and with A10 in every case where it appears, in both orders (A01 first in about 26%, A03/A10 first in about 34%). A01 always precedes A04 (together in about 32% of later cases) and A07 (about 28%), never the reverse, and A04 and A07 still never occur in the same case in either window. [REL001, REL004, REL002, REL003, REL008]
- Downstream activities occur later in the case: A04's median relative position moves from about 0.22 to 0.33, A07's from 0.50 to 0.75, A10's from 0 to about 0.13 and A03's from about 0.09 to 0.11. Step distances grow accordingly: A03→A04 and A10→A04, previously 1–2 steps apart, are now 3 steps apart in about 38 of 100 cases; A03→A07 (about 41%) and A10→A07 (about 29%) show the same drift to lag 3; and A03 and A10, previously always immediately adjacent, now have one event between them in about 29% (A03→A10) and 36% (A10→A03) of cases. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]
- Participation of the surrounding activities is nearly unchanged (A03 and A10 about 56% to 60%, A04 about 28% to 32%, A07 about 28% to 28%), so existing paths did not simply start running more or less often. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC007, OCC010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new step, A01, was introduced into the flow in the later window: it executes once in about 60 in 100 cases, early in the case, positioned between the A03/A10 stage and the A04/A07 stage. A03 and A10 now pass control to A01 in roughly a third of their transitions, and A01 passes control back to A03 or A10 or on to A04 or A07; as a consequence, A04 and A07 occur at later relative positions and the distance from the A03/A10 stage to A04/A07 stretches from typically 1–2 steps to often 3, with A01 occasionally sitting between A03 and A10 themselves. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR007, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, POS003, POS004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG005]

The reading is direct and consistent: appearance from zero participation with a coherent incoming and outgoing edge structure, relation profiles flipping from "never together" to "together in every A01 case", and matching position and lag shifts; a one-window rare variant is not a plausible explanation of 0 versus 119 of 200 cases. The position and lag shifts are consequences of the same appearance rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: the evidence cannot show why cases receive A01 — about a quarter of its occurrences follow activities that were not profiled as sources (or the case start) — and a relabelling of a previously unobserved activity cannot be distinguished from a genuinely new step. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR007]

Other differences: A09's handover shifted from A13 (about 54% to 47% of its outgoing transitions) towards A14 (about 44% to 51%), and A13 occurs somewhat less often (mean 0.34 to 0.28 occurrences per case); A07's follow-up shifted slightly from A06 (about 27% to 20%) towards A05 (about 44% to 50%). These smaller shifts could also be sampling variation. [DIR006, OCC013, DIR004]
