### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in the segment between A39 and A24: in the earlier window this segment consisted of exactly one of A14, A15 or A16 per trace, whereas in the later window it consists of A21, A22 and A23, all three executed once in every trace in no fixed order, with A39 before and A24 after, and A14, A15 and A16 were no longer observed. The change is shown directly by the complete replacement of A39's successor profile, by the all-or-nothing participation of the six activities, and by relational items that flip from "never together" to "always together" with the order at both ends of the block unchanged; the A39-to-A24 distance growing from 2 to 4 steps in every trace corroborates that three activities now run between them. Nearly fifty items across all indicator families describe this one coherent difference with empirical strengths of about 0.995 — differences larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits — although these items largely view the same traces rather than confirming independently. [DIR030, OCC012, OCC013, OCC014, OCC019, OCC020, OCC021, REL001, REL019, REL021, REL022, REL027, LAG001]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A14, A15 and A16 were not observed in the later window, not that they were removed from the process, and with anonymous activity codes a relabelling alongside the restructuring cannot be excluded; the internal sequence of A21/A22/A23 remains unfixed. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window samples a different mix of cases rather than a modified process, although a flip from 0 to 200 and 200 to 0 out of 200 traces makes pure sampling noise implausible. [OCC012, OCC013, OCC014, OCC019, OCC020, OCC021]

Other differences: the shifts after A29 (toward A35/A36) and after A40 (toward A42/A43) are changes in how often existing alternative paths are taken — all involved activities occur in substantial shares in both windows — and smaller redistributions appear around A17–A20, A28, A01 and A38; these are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC022, OCC024, DIR022, DIR031, OCC029, DIR015, DIR029, DIR001, DIR021]
