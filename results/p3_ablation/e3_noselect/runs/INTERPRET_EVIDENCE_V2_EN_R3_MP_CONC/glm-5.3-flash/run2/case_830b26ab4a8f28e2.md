### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 is no longer executed: it ran in every trace of the earlier window (about 3.5 times per trace, followed mainly by A09, A10 and A08) and in no trace of the later window, with the paths that ran through it now leading from A03 and A05 directly to A08, A09 and A10. This rests on direct occurrence evidence over all 200 traces in each window (200 vs 0 supporting traces) and is consistently corroborated by the directional, positional and five relational indicators, all with empirical strength near 0.995 — well beyond what random re-splits of a stable process produce; these items describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, but they agree across indicator types. The main limitation is that zero participation strictly means only "not observed": the evidence cannot distinguish a removed activity from one that is still modelled but never triggered, and the rerouting is documented only for the outgoing transitions of A03 and A05. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, REL003, REL004, REL005]

Other differences: repetition increased for A05, A08, A09, A10, A11, A12 and A13 without their trace participation changing much, and several pair-distance distributions shifted (A03/A05 vs A08/A09/A10 closer together; A08 vs A09/A10 farther apart), some based on modest support of roughly 22–68 traces in a window; these may be downstream effects of the same rerouting, but the evidence does not establish that link. [OCC003, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, LAG001, LAG004, LAG010, LAG012, LAG015, LAG016, LAG021]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 remains part of the process behind a condition that did not fire in the later sample; given its earlier participation of 100 in 100 traces this is unlikely, but occurrence indicators alone cannot exclude it. [OCC001]
