### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the closing segment of the process was restructured: the earlier ending, in which A06 led to A01 and A01 directly to the final A03, is no longer observed, and in the later window every case instead runs A02 → A05 → A06 → A07 with A07 last. A01 and A03 were dropped, A05 and A07 were added, A06 was retained but rewired (predecessor now A05, successor now A07), and A02's branch into A06 was replaced by a branch into A05, while the A02 → A04 branch continued as before. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC005, OCC006, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL013]

The support is direct and consistent: the presence/absence swap is complete (200 of 200 traces in each window), the successor changes are observed on the edges themselves rather than inferred, and the positional and lag shifts match the rewiring; nearly all items show differences larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. Note that the many agreeing items largely describe one underlying change (for example, the occurrence, direction, position and relation items on A01) and are not independent confirmations. [DIR004, POS005, POS006, LAG002, REL015]

The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different case populations or variants rather than a modified single process, and zero participation strictly means only that A01 and A03 were not observed in the later window. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC005, OCC006]

Other differences: A04 repeats somewhat more often than before (about 22 → 29 in 100 of the traces containing it) and A02 averages slightly more occurrences per trace (about 1.9 → 2.0); both are small and could reflect sampling variation (empirical strength about 0.71 for A04).
