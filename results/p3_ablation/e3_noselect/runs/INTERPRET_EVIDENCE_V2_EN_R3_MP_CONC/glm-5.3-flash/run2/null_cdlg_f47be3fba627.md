### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. Participation of every flagged activity is flat to within about one case in 100, and the few routing shifts that stand out (the successor mixes of A05, A10, A06 and A09) rest on roughly 46–53 traces each — with the key edges seen only a handful of times — and point in conflicting directions (A09 gains as a successor of A05 and A10 but loses as a successor of A06, while A04 moves the opposite way), so ordinary sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows cannot be ruled out.
