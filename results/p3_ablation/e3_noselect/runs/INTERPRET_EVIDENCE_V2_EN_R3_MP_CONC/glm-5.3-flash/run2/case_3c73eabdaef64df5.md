### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural extension of the trace sequence: in the later window every case runs A04, then A03, A05 and A06 in sequence, then A07, and finally A01, whereas in the earlier window cases ran A04 directly followed by A07. A03, A05 and A06 are now executed between A04 and A07, and A01 is executed after A07 at the end of the trace; all four went from 0 of 200 traces to exactly one occurrence in every trace. The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: the successor chain, the co-occurrence and fixed ordering of every activity pair, the position shifts and the A04-to-A07 distance change all describe the same underlying difference, and the participation change from 0 to 200 of 200 traces per activity is far outside what random re-splits of these window sizes produce (each item exceeds about 995 of 1,000 reference splits). Because the four activities were absent from the earlier window, this is a lengthening of the sequence with activities now executed in every case, not a shift of shares between pre-existing paths.
