### Observed differences

- The sharpest difference concerns the alternative block A19/A20/A25, of which essentially one activity executes per case in both windows (the three practically never occur together) [REL001, REL002, REL008]. In the earlier window, A45 fed into this block (about 94 in 100 of A45's outgoing transitions went to A25, A19 or A20), and each block activity continued to A26 (92–99 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) [DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, DIR045]. In the later window, A45 leads directly to A26 (94 in 100), and A19, A20 and A25 instead continue to A28 and A32 (A20: about 56 in 100 to A32 and 43 to A28; A19: 45 and 48; A25: 56 and 39) [DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, DIR045].

- In the later window, A27 and A28 route into the block: about 43 in 100 of A27's outgoing transitions and about 53 in 100 of A28's go to A19/A20/A25, where these edges were essentially absent earlier; correspondingly A27→A28 falls from 98 to 54 in 100 and A28→A32 from 99 to 44 in 100 [DIR027, DIR028].

- The block now occurs much later within cases: its mean event position moves from about 0.32–0.33 to about 0.59–0.61, from the region of A26 (mean position about 0.33–0.35) to the region between A27 and A32 (about 0.56–0.65), while A26 and A27 themselves shift slightly earlier (0.35→0.33 and 0.58→0.56) [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]. Order relations flip accordingly: A19 preceded A26 in about 36 in 100 traces earlier and in essentially none later, while A26 preceding A19 rises from near zero to about 31 in 100; A20 and A25 show the same flip against A26 and A27 [REL003, REL004, REL009, REL010, REL014, REL015].

- Step distances change consistently: A45→A26 shortens from mostly 2 steps to mostly 1, and A27→A32 lengthens from 2 to 3 steps in about 92 in 100 cases, indicating the block now sits between A27 and A32; A27→A28 and A28→A32 each split between 1 and 2 steps [LAG014, LAG007, LAG006, LAG010]. Pairs almost unobserved earlier now appear with 32–75 supporting traces (A19→A32, A27→A19, A27→A20, A28→A19, A28→A20), while A19→A26 and A45→A19/A25 shrink to 1–2 traces [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG008, LAG009, LAG012, LAG013].

- Within the block, the choice shifts toward A20: participation rises from 25 to 37.5 in 100 traces, while A19 falls from 37.5 to 33 and A25 from 38.5 to 29.5; together the block still accounts for about one execution per case in both windows [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024].

- Existing branch shares also shift elsewhere: after A32 from A23 to A24 (participation 57.5→43 and 42.5→57.5 in 100 traces; A32's outgoing to A23 29→20 in 100 and to A24 19→26 in 100), after A16 toward A17 (21.5→30.5 in 100) and away from A18 (27.5→23 in 100), and after A33 from A39/A40 toward A41/A42 [OCC022, OCC023, DIR032, OCC016, OCC017, DIR016, OCC031, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, DIR033].

- Repetition decreases around the A43/A44 pair and the A46–A49 loop: mean occurrences per trace fall (A46 1.75→1.53, A44 0.90→0.77, A47 0.89→0.77), with more exits to A45 and A50 (A44→A45 56→63 in 100; A49→A50 53→66 in 100) [OCC035, OCC036, OCC038, OCC039, OCC040, OCC041, DIR044, DIR047, DIR049].

- Participation changes of the rare activities A02 (7.5→11.5 in 100) and A06 (13.5→8 in 100) rest on only 15–27 traces and are weakly supported [OCC002, OCC005].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative block A19/A20/A25 — of which one activity runs per case — now executes later in the process: earlier it sat between A45 and A26, whereas in the later window A45 connects directly to A26 and the block instead runs between A27/A28 and A32, flowing into A28/A32. The change is shown directly and consistently by the near-complete successor replacement at A45 and at A19/A20/A25 (dissimilarity 0.97–1.00, empirical strength 0.995), by the new substantial edges from A27/A28 into the block and from the block into A28/A32, by the position shift from about 0.32 to about 0.59–0.61, by the flipped order relative to A26/A27, and by matching step-distance changes; the block's total participation stays at about one execution per case in both windows, which fits the block having moved rather than a branch being added or removed [DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, DIR045, DIR027, DIR028, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL003, REL009, REL014, LAG007, LAG014, OCC018, OCC019, OCC024].

Limitations: the direction, relation, position and lag items largely re-express one underlying rewiring, so they converge on a single difference rather than independently confirming it, and a few lag items rest on only 1–2 observations [LAG001, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013]. The evidence also cannot distinguish a genuine move of the block from removing the path A45→A19/A20/A25→A26 and inserting a new optional path A27/A28→A19/A20/A25→A28/A32; both would leave the same signature here, and this is the main competing reading the data cannot rule out.

Other differences: the branch-share shifts after A32 (away from A23 toward A24), after A16 (toward A17, away from A18) and after A33 (from A39/A40 toward A41/A42), the reduced repetition around A43/A44 and A46–A49, and the participation changes of the rare activities A02 and A06 are additional differences that the relocation hypothesis does not cover [OCC022, OCC023, DIR032, OCC016, OCC017, DIR016, OCC031, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, DIR033, OCC035, OCC036, OCC038, OCC039, OCC040, OCC041, DIR044, DIR047, DIR049, OCC002, OCC005].
