### Observed differences

- Three activities — A19, A20 and A25 — each ran in about 36, 31 and 35 of 100 earlier traces (71, 61 and 70 of 200) and practically never in the same trace; in the later window they are essentially gone (0, 1 and 0 of 200 traces). [OCC016, OCC017, OCC022, REL015, REL017, REL022]
- Earlier, about 93 of 100 of A41's outgoing transitions led to one of these three (A19 about 33, A25 about 33, A20 about 27), and each of the three led on to A26 in about 97–99 of 100 cases; in the later window, A41 leads directly to A26 in about 92 of 100 of its outgoing transitions. [DIR041, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025]
- Accordingly, the distance from A41 to A26 shrank from typically two steps (about 94 of 100 cases) to one step (about 92 of 100). [LAG006]
- The surroundings of this segment are unchanged: A14, A26, A31 and A34 still occur in every trace, once per trace, in both windows. [OCC012, OCC023, OCC027, OCC028]
- The early routing split shifted: traces containing A01 and its block partners A05/A07/A09 fell from about 59 to 50 in 100, while traces containing A03 and its block partners A11/A12/A13 rose from about 42 to 51 in 100; the order inside the blocks also shifted somewhat (A12 passed straight on to A14 in about 43 of 100 of its outgoing transitions, up from about 26 in 100). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC007, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011, DIR012, LAG001]
- Smaller shifts: A15 and A16 repeated in somewhat more traces (about 27 vs 32, and 27 vs 33 in 100), A17's participation rose from about 21 to 29 in 100, and within the A21–A24 group the mix moved (A23 about 52→45, A24 about 48→56 in 100, with A24 now sitting immediately before A31 more often, about 39→59 in 100). [OCC013, OCC014, OCC015, OCC020, OCC021, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative detour formed by A19, A20 and A25 between A41 and A26 is no longer taken: earlier, essentially every case passed from A41 through exactly one of the three mutually exclusive activities before reaching A26, whereas in the later window A41 leads directly to A26 (about 92 of 100 outgoing transitions) and the three activities appear in at most 1 of 200 traces, against 61–71 of 200 earlier. The signs are direct and consistent across indicator families — participation collapsing to zero, A41's outgoing transitions rewiring from the three alternatives to A26, and the A41→A26 distance shortening from two steps to one — while A14, A26, A31 and A34 still run in every trace, so the surrounding flow is intact and the difference is not an artefact of the neighbouring steps. What the evidence cannot decide is whether the three activities were removed from the flow or remain possible but were not exercised by any case in the later window; that competing explanation cannot be ruled out from these two samples alone. [OCC016, OCC017, OCC022, DIR041, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, LAG006, OCC012, OCC023, OCC027, OCC028]

Other differences: The share of cases taking the early A01 route (with A05/A07/A09) fell from about 59 to 50 in 100 while the A03 route (with A11/A12/A13) rose from about 42 to 51 in 100; all activities involved remain in roughly half of the traces in both windows, so this reads as existing routes being chosen with different frequencies rather than a path replacement. In addition, A15 and A16 repeat slightly more often, A17 participation rose, and the mix within the A21–A24 group shifted; these differences are separate from the main hypothesis.
