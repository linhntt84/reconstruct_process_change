### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A02 executes as a step between A15 and the branch to A07, A08 and A12. Earlier, A15 led directly to A07 (about 52 in 100 of its transitions), A08 (about 25) and A12 (about 23), and A02 never occurred; in the later window A15 hands over exclusively to A02, and A02 passes executions on to A07, A08 and A12 in almost the same shares, leaving the loop one step longer. This is shown directly by the successor profiles of A15 and A02, and corroborated by the one-step growth of the A15→A07/A08/A12 distances and of the loop self-distances, by A02's co-occurrence with all four activities appearing only in the later window, and by A02's position just past the trace midpoint. Sampling explanations are hard to maintain: A02 moves from 0 to about 116 of 200 traces, roughly 470 A15 transitions stand behind each window's profile, and the key items sit at the extreme of their reference comparisons (strength about 0.995).
