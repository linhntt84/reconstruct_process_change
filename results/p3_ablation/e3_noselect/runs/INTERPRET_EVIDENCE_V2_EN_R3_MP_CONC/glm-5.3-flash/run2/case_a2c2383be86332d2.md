### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A07 is no longer executed and that A08 now leads directly to A05 and A06 with nearly the same branch split A07 used to have (about 52/48), while A07 itself fell from every trace (about 2.1 occurrences per trace) to none. This is shown directly by the successor profiles of A07 and A08 and by A07's participation dropping from 200 of 200 traces to 0 of 200; it is corroborated by the collapse of every A07 relation and by the one-step shortening of the distances that previously spanned A07, with A08's own transition volume unchanged, which points to a rewiring at A08 rather than a frequency change at A08. The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different case mixes rather than one modified process, and no screening baseline is included, so the chance comparison rests on the effect sizes themselves — although a disappearance from 200 of 200 traces to 0 of 200, and a complete successor swap at A08, are far outside what random re-splits typically produce.
