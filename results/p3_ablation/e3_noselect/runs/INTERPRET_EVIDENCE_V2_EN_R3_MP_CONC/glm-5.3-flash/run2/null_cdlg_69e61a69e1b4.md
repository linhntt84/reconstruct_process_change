### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence
The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The only items that stand out are the successor shifts at A18 and A15; they are isolated, concern different activities, and in A15's case rest on roughly two dozen observed transitions, so ordinary sampling variation between two windows of 200 traces each cannot be ruled out.
