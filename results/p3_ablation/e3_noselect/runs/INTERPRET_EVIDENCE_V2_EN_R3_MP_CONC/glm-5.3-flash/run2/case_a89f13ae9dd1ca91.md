### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured: in the earlier window, every trace executed the three activities A21, A22 and A23 together, in varying order, between A39 and A24; in the later window, that trio is essentially unobserved, and A39 instead leads to exactly one of three activities not observed in the earlier window — A14, A15 or A16 — each of which then precedes A24 [DIR030, OCC013, OCC014, OCC015, OCC020, OCC021, OCC022, REL025, REL026, REL031]. Supporting signs: the successor flip at A39 was measured on all observed outgoing transitions of A39 in both windows [DIR030]; the appearance and disappearance rest on all 200 traces per window [OCC013, OCC014, OCC015, OCC020, OCC021, OCC022]; the new activities run after A39 and before A24 in exactly their participation shares [REL006, REL007, REL014, REL015, REL021, REL022]; and the A39-to-A24 distance shrank from four to two event positions, exactly what shrinking a three-activity block to a single activity predicts [LAG010, POS007]. These headline differences exceed about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the same data, so sampling variation alone is not a credible explanation [DIR030, OCC020, LAG010]. Limitations: the evidence file contains no screening expectation, so the formal comparison of the item count against the number expected without a change cannot be performed, and one later trace still contains A21–A23, so their removal may not be complete [OCC020, OCC021, OCC022]. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the insertion of A14–A16 and the disappearance of A21–A23 are two independent changes rather than one replacement, although the successor flip at A39 and the exact lag reduction tie both to the same segment [DIR030, LAG010].

Other differences: A40 and A41 occur and repeat more often and are executed together more often, with A41 routing relatively more to A40 and less to A44, and A40 slightly more to A41 [OCC029, OCC030, REL044, DIR031, DIR032, DIR033]. Smaller scattered redistributions at A02, A18, A28 and A33–A36 move individual routing shares by roughly 10–15 percentage points and are not part of the main hypothesis [DIR002, DIR014, DIR021, DIR025, DIR026, DIR027, DIR028].
