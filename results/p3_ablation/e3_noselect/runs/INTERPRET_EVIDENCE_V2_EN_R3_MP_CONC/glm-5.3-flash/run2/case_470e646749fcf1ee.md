A03 remains the first activity in every trace in both windows. [OCC003, POS004, LAG001, POS009, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the flow between A03 and A04 was restructured. In the earlier window, traces left A03 either directly to A04 (about 46 in 100 of A03's outgoing transitions) or to A08 (about 52 in 100, with A08 repeating itself directly), and about 20 in 100 traces later ran A14 → A01 → A04. In the later window, the direct A03 → A04 transition is replaced by a new segment A03 → A02 → A13 → A04 containing a repeating A02 ↔ A12 loop, the A08 self-repetition is replaced by a cycle through the new A09, and A01 no longer occurs, with A14 feeding A04 directly. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC007, OCC010, OCC011, DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011]

The signs are direct and mutually consistent, though several indicator families describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations: participation appears and disappears on a large scale (A02 and A13 from none to about 95 of 200 traces; A01 from 40 to 0), the successor profiles show the rewired handovers, and the relation, position and lag items agree with the new activities sitting between A03 and A04 (A13 occurs only alongside A03 and typically before A04; A02 and A08 became disjoint; A04's position and the A03–A04 distance moved later). Differences of this size cannot plausibly result from sampling a rare variant. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR003, DIR011, REL011, REL020, REL025, POS004, LAG001]

The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different process variants rather than one modified process — the A02 route could in principle have existed before without appearing in the earlier 200 traces, although its complete absence there makes this unlikely. The internal order of the A02 ↔ A12 loop is also only partly resolved (the pair appears as interleaved-or-repeated in about 26 in 100 traces), and A12 occurs in only about half of the A02 traces, so whether that loop is optional cannot be settled. [OCC002, OCC010, REL009, REL013]

Other differences: A04's outgoing shares shifted slightly (A06 up from about 47 to 53 in 100, A05 down from about 26 to 22 in 100), A08 sits slightly later in the trace (median relative position about 0.20 vs 0.25), and A05 and A07 participate somewhat less often (A05 in about 33 vs 30 in 100 traces); these shifts are small and consistent with side effects of the changes above. [DIR004, POS005, OCC004, OCC005]
