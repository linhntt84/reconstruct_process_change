### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the connection between A10 and A03 was rerouted through a different activity: in the earlier window the chain ran A10 → A01 → A03 in about 46 in 100 traces, while in the later window A01 is no longer observed and the chain runs A10 → A13 → A03 in about 53 in 100 traces. The support is direct and consistent: A10's successor profile changes completely from A01 to A13, A13's only observed outgoing edge leads to A03 where A01's did before, A13 appears at the same event position A01 held, and the relations with A03 and A10 flip from "A01 present, A13 absent" to "A13 present, A01 absent". The shares involved (roughly half of the traces in each window) are far too large for a rare variant to explain. The slightly higher loop frequency in the later window (A10 and A13 at about 53 vs 46 in 100 traces, A03 at about 2.1 vs 1.9 occurrences per trace, A03's outgoing split tilting toward A10) is consistent with the same underlying behaviour. Limitations: the many agreeing items largely re-describe one underlying difference and are not independent confirmations; zero participation only establishes that A01 was not observed in the later sample; and the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely new activity A13 from A01 continuing under a different label, since the two behave identically in this segment.
