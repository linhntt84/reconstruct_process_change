### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A22, A23 and A24 formed a fixed chain: A23 immediately followed A22 and A24 immediately followed A23 in all 200 traces, so A24 always fell exactly two positions after A22 [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window an additional, essentially mandatory step is executed inside the formerly fixed A22 → A23 → A24 stretch: exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities — A14, A15 or A20 — is carried out between A22 and A24, placed between A22 and A23 in about 56 in 100 traces and between A23 and A24 in about 44. The placement is shown directly by agreeing relational, lag, positional and successor evidence: the three activities were absent from all 200 earlier traces and now appear in virtually every later trace, never together; A22 precedes and A24 follows the new step in every co-occurrence; the A22–A24 distance grew from exactly two to exactly three positions in virtually all traces, with the two sub-gaps splitting in complementary proportions that match the two insertion slots; and each new activity leads on to A23 or A24. For the key items, about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data produced smaller differences [OCC013, OCC014, OCC019, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL005, REL006, DIR010, DIR018, DIR019, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003].

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly means only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed there, so the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely added step from behaviour that existed earlier but was not sampled, or from a change in how activities were recorded or labelled — although 0 of 200 earlier traces makes pure sampling variation implausible. The evidence also cannot show what determines which of the three alternatives runs in a given trace [OCC013, OCC014, OCC019].

Other differences: modest routing changes not part of this hypothesis — shifted successor shares around A01, A03 and A05 with participation in A01/A03/A04/A05 up about 6 points and A02/A06/A07/A08 down about 6 points, a gain in the A37→A39 branch with higher participation in A39/A40, and small share shifts at A21/A28/A29 and A35/A36 [DIR001, DIR003, DIR005, OCC001, OCC006, DIR029, OCC027, OCC028, DIR017, DIR028].
