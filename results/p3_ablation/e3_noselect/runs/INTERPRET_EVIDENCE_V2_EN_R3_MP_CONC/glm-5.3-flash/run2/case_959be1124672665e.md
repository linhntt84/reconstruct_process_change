### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural rerouting in which A02, A03 and A08 dropped out of the executed behaviour and the paths that ran through them were rewired: the chain A10 → A08 → A11 became A10 → A01 → A11; A16, which previously fed A01's branch to A06/A07/A14, now leads directly to A06, A14 and A07; A05 and A18 now feed A10 instead of A02; A13 and A19 feed A05/A18 instead of A03; A09 and A17, which previously both flowed into A05/A18, now alternate with each other; and A07 and A14 became rare, terminal occurrences. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC008, DIR001, DIR009, DIR013, DIR004, DIR015, DIR011, DIR016, DIR008, DIR014, REL090, OCC007, OCC014]

The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the disappearances rest on large before-samples (94, 94 and 41 of 200 traces) with exactly zero observations afterwards; every successor replacement is observed directly on the source activity's outgoing transitions; and the relation and lag items change precisely on the rewired pairs while unaffected parts stay stable (for example A11's split to A15/A16 and the A10-before-A11 relation are unchanged in both windows), so the differences form one coherent rewiring rather than scattered fluctuations. [DIR010, REL093, REL001, REL006, REL022, LAG029, LAG047, LAG050, LAG052]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A02, A03 and A08 were not observed in the later sample, not that they were removed from the process definition, and both windows are finite samples of 200 traces. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in case mix — the later window could contain only a subpopulation of variants that never executes A02, A03 and A08 — which would produce the same picture without any modification to the process. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC008]

Other differences: small occurrence and recurrence movements for A06, A12, A13 and A19 (changes of roughly 0.02–0.06 on their own scales, empirical strength at or below about 0.64) and minor position drifts for A05 and A06 fall within what random re-splits of a stable process produce and are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC006, OCC012, OCC013, OCC019, POS004, POS005]
