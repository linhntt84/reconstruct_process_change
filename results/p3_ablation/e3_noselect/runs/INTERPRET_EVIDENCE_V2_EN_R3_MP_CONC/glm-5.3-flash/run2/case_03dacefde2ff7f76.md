### Observed differences

- Earlier, A45 was followed by one of three alternative activities — A19 (about 31% of A45's observed outgoing transitions), A20 (about 26%) or A25 (about 28%) — and each of the three led almost always directly to A26 (89–97 in 100 outflows); in the later window, A45 leads directly to A26 in about 89 in 100 outflows, and its edges to A19, A20 and A25 are no longer observed [DIR045, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025]
- A19, A20 and A25 still occur once per case in roughly 30–38 in 100 traces in both windows (A19's share slips from about 38 to 31, A25's rises from about 32 to 38), but in the later window they run much later: their mean event position moves from about one-third to about three-fifths of the case's event sequence, while A26, A27 and A28 shift only slightly earlier [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

- A10's participation falls from about 26 to 20 in 100 traces [OCC010, DIR010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-way alternative block A19/A20/A25 now sits later in the case: earlier it ran between A45 and A26 (A45, then one of the three, then A26), whereas in the later window A45 connects directly to A26 and the three activities run as alternatives inserted between A27/A28 and A32, still executed once per case in roughly a third of traces each. The signs are direct and consistent — near-complete turnover of the outgoing edges of A19, A20, A25 and A45 (dissimilarity about 1.0 with empirical strength about 0.995 each), reversed order relations with A26 and A27, a position shift from about one-third to about three-fifths of the trace, and step-level turnover in the lag data — while the block's participation and once-per-case execution stay stable, which makes a pure shift in the frequencies of unchanged paths an unlikely explanation. Limitations: the many items describe one underlying difference and are not independent confirmations; the exact slot of the block within the A27–A32 stretch varies (sometimes between A27 and A28, sometimes between A28 and A32); and a change in the mix of whole trace variants, rather than a structural rewiring, cannot be fully ruled out at this aggregation level [DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, DIR045, REL003, REL009, REL014, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG004, LAG002]

Other differences: a routing-share shift within the A21–A24 group (the A24 branch taken less often, A23 and A33 more often) and a modest increase in repetitions around A46–A49; neither is part of the main hypothesis [DIR021, DIR022, OCC023, OCC024, OCC041, OCC042]
