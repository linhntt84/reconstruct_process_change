### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is one coherent behavioural change around A01 and A12. In the earlier window, A01 appeared in about 52 in 100 traces (103 of 200), always at the same final event position, and A12 — present at a steady ~52 in 100 traces in both windows — was in every observed case immediately followed by A01. In the later window, A01 was not observed in any of the 200 traces, A12 was never observed leading to another activity (0 outgoing transitions across its 102 occurrences, where previously all 103 went to A01), and A12's event position collapsed from a median of about 0.86 of the trace length (quartiles roughly 0.75–0.89) to always the final position. [OCC001, POS001, REL001, DIR006, POS002]

- A05 was repeated much less: mean occurrences fell from about 0.66 to about 0.29 per trace while participation stayed at about 15 in 100 traces. Its surroundings shifted too: A07's outgoing transitions, previously all to A05, split about evenly between A13 and A05, and A13's, previously all to A05, now go about half to A05, three in ten to A07 and two in ten to A08. [OCC005, DIR004, DIR007]

- Around A06, A10 and A14: in the earlier window A06 led to A14 in about 54 in 100 of its observed transitions (A10 for the rest) and A14 led to A10 or A15 in roughly even shares; in the later window both A06 and A14 led only to A10 in every observed transition, and A10 began to repeat within traces (mean occurrences about 0.13 to 0.26; repeats in about 7 in 100 traces versus none before). These shares rest on only about 24–28 observed transitions per window. [DIR003, DIR008, OCC010]

- Smaller shifts: A11 occurred somewhat more often (mean about 0.49 to 0.61 per trace, participation about 27 to 29 in 100), and A04's outgoing mix tilted from about 52/48 between A12 and A11 to about 46/54, with slightly more A04 occurrences per trace (about 1.00 to 1.12). [OCC011, DIR001, OCC004]

- Most other activities' participation moved by no more than about one or two traces in 100. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC012, OCC016]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 is no longer executed as the final step after A12 — or has become so rare that it no longer appears — leaving A12 as the terminal activity. In the earlier window about half of all traces ended with A12 followed by A01, with A01 always last; in the later window A01 appears in none of 200 traces, the two activities never co-occur, and A12 always occupies the final event position. [OCC001, REL001, DIR006, POS001, POS002] This rests on direct relational, successor and positional evidence rather than frequency alone, the items all describe one underlying behaviour, and each difference is larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits, so sampling variation alone is an unlikely explanation. [OCC001, REL001, DIR006, POS001, POS002] The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 still occurs but at a drastically lower rate (below roughly 1–2 in 100 traces), since zero participation only means it was not observed in the later sample of 200 traces. [OCC001]

Other differences: A05 now repeats about half as often per trace, and A07 and A13, which previously always led to A05, now also lead to A13, A07 or A08; A06 and A14 now lead only to A10 in all observed transitions and A10 repeats within traces, whereas before they also led to A14 and A15; A11 occurs somewhat more often and A04's outgoing mix tilted toward A11. [OCC005, DIR004, DIR007, DIR003, DIR008, OCC010, OCC011, DIR001] These additional differences are based on modest counts (roughly 24–71 transitions or 24–30 participating traces per window), so their exact shares are uncertain and they are not folded into the main hypothesis.
