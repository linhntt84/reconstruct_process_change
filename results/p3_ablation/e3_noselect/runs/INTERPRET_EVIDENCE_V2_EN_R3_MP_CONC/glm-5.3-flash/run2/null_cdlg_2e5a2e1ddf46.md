### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests a repetition change centred on A02, A09 and A06: in the later window these activities are executed more often and repeat more often within a case — A02 and A09 appear in about 27 in 100 traces (up from about 21) with about 2.2 occurrences per containing trace (up from about 1.6), A06 appears in about 23 in 100 traces (up from about 17) with about 2.8 occurrences per containing trace (up from about 2.0) — and the outgoing shares moved toward the edges A02→A09 (about 45% to 57%), A09→A06 (about 19% to 32%) and A06→A02 (about 17% to 27%), while the exit A02→A07 fell from about 36% to 21% of A02's outgoing transitions; the growth in outgoing transitions matches the occurrence growth [OCC002, OCC005, OCC008, DIR002, DIR005, DIR007]. The supporting signs are direct (occurrence and recurrence components) and consistent across the cluster.

What is missing: the evidence carries no screening baseline, so the number of qualifying items cannot be compared with what an unchanged process would produce; the A02 and A09 items have identical profiles and identical support counts, so they likely describe the same traces rather than two independent confirmations; and the absolute differences are modest (about 6 in 100 traces). The competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is ordinary sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows, possibly concentrated in one correlated set of traces [OCC002, OCC008, DIR002, DIR007].

Other differences: A07, A08, A10 and A11 also increased mildly (participation up about 3–6 in 100 traces, empirical strengths about 0.5–0.78), which fits more flow through this part of the process but is too weak to confirm on its own, while A01, A04 and A05 and the routing at A01, A04, A05, A08 and A10 show no meaningful differences [OCC006, OCC007, OCC009, OCC010, DIR009, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, DIR008].
