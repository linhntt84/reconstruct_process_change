### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a step now sits between A41 and A26 where none did before: in the later window, nearly every case passes through exactly one of three activities that were absent from the earlier sample — A19 (about 37 in 100 traces), A20 (about 29 in 100) and A25 (about 34 in 100) — with A41 running before and A26 after this step. The change is shown directly and consistently: A41's direct transition to A26 collapsed from about 93 to about 1 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and was replaced by transitions to the three new activities; each of them leads to A26 in almost all of its transitions; the A41→A26 distance moved from one event to two; and the three are mutually exclusive, indicating a per-case choice among them. The many agreeing items describe one underlying difference and are not independent confirmations. [DIR041, LAG002, OCC017, OCC018, OCC023, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, REL001, REL002, REL007, REL003, REL006, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL015]

The main limitation is that zero participation in the earlier window only means A19, A20 and A25 were not observed then; the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely added step from an existing path that was not taken in the earlier sample and is now taken in nearly every case. [OCC017, OCC018, OCC023]
