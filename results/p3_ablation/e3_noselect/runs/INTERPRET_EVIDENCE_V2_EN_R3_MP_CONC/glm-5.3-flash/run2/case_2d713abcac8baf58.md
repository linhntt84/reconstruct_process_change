### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, almost every case executed exactly one of three alternative activities between A44 and A29: A19 in about 35 in 100 cases, A20 in about 30, and A21 in about 36, while any pair of them co-occurred in only about 0.5 in 100 cases. In the later window these three activities are virtually unobserved (2, 1 and 1 cases out of 200 respectively). [OCC018, OCC019, OCC020, REL001, REL002, REL008]
- Three activities that never appeared in the earlier window (0 of 200 cases) occur in every case in the later window, about once per case each: A26, A27 and A28. [OCC025, OCC026, OCC027]
- In the later window A26, A27 and A28 always occur together and in no fixed order among themselves (A26 before A27 in about 51 in 100 cases and the reverse in about 49; the other pairs are similarly mixed), whereas in the earlier window they never co-occurred. [REL019, REL020, REL023]
- The new trio occupies the segment the old trio occupied: A44 precedes A26/A27/A28 in about 99 in 100 later cases (earlier, A44 preceded A19/A20/A21 in all of their cases), and A26/A27/A28 precede A29 in about 98–99 in 100 later cases (earlier, A19/A20/A21 preceded A29 in all of their cases). The A44–A29 endpoint relation itself is unchanged. [REL007, REL013, REL018, REL022, REL025, REL027, REL006, REL012, REL017, REL021, REL024, REL026, REL028]
- A44's outgoing transitions shifted accordingly: earlier about 30% went to A19, 30% to A21 and 25% to A20; later about 31% go to A26, 27% to A27 and 26% to A28. [DIR044]
- The distance from A44 to A29 grew from typically 2 steps (about 84 in 100 cases) to typically 4 steps (about 82 in 100 cases), matching the replacement of one intermediate activity by three; A29's mean event position moved later (about 0.40 to 0.44 of the trace). [LAG001, POS007]
- Other, smaller differences: participation shifted modestly for several activities (A39 from about 54% to 45% of cases, A40 from 46% to 55%, A41 from 47% to 55%, A46 from 70% to 58%, A47 from 59% to 64%, A48 from 59% to 64%, A18 from 22% to 27%, A42 from 21% to 27%), and A15/A16 repeat slightly more often (mean occurrences per trace up about 0.06–0.08). [OCC035, OCC036, OCC037, OCC042, OCC043, OCC044, OCC017, OCC038, OCC015, OCC016]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: in the earlier window each case chose exactly one of three alternative activities — A19, A20 or A21 — between A44 and A29, while in the later window those three are essentially gone and every case instead executes A26, A27 and A28 once each in that segment, with no fixed order among the three. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, OCC026, OCC027, REL019, REL020, REL023, DIR044]

The change is shown directly rather than inferred: relational evidence shows the co-occurrence and precedence patterns with A44 and A29 flipping between the windows, the successor profile of A44 redistributes from the old trio to the new trio, and the A44→A29 step distance grows from typically 2 to typically 4 steps — exactly what replacing one intermediate activity with three predicts. The key items show differences larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, with roughly 200 cases behind each window's profile; the removed activities' after-side rests on only 1–2 observations, but zero participation only means they were not observed. [REL006, REL007, REL021, REL022, DIR044, LAG001, OCC018, OCC025]

Limitations: the evidence shows where and how behaviour differs but not why, and it cannot establish that the new block functionally replaced the old one rather than the removal and the insertion being independent changes that happen to share the same endpoints. Other differences: the modest participation shifts in A18, A39–A48 and the slightly higher repetition of A15/A16 are not tied to the main hypothesis by this evidence and could be independent differences or knock-on effects of the restructuring. [OCC035, OCC036, OCC037, OCC042, OCC043, OCC044, OCC015, OCC016]
