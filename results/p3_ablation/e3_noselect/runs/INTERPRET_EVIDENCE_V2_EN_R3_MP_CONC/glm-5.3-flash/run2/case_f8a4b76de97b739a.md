### Observed differences

- Two activities that never appeared earlier now run regularly: in the earlier window neither A01 nor A02 occurs in any of the 200 traces, while in the later window A01 runs in about 59 in 100 traces (about 1.5 executions per trace, repeated in roughly 4 in 10 of the traces containing it) and A02 runs in about 87 in 100 traces, exactly once each time. [OCC001, OCC002]

[OCC003, DIR003, POS003, OCC004, OCC010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in which two activities were added and one was removed: A01 and A02, entirely absent earlier, run in most later cases, while A09, previously executed in over half of the cases, is no longer observed. The relational evidence places both additions directly: A02 acts as a new intermediate step between A05/A13 and A12 — A05 and A13 reroute to it, it leads mainly to A12, it always precedes A12, and the former direct A05→A12 and A13→A12 successions are gone — and A01 takes over A09's former position between A16 and A03 with nearly the same routing profile. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR005, DIR009, DIR013, DIR016, REL017, LAG014, LAG041]

The participation differences rest on large supports (0 vs 117 of 200 traces for A01, 0 vs 173 for A02, 108 vs 0 for A09) and are larger than essentially all random re-splits of the pooled traces (empirical strength about 0.995); the outgoing-transition profiles of A01 and A02 appear from empty and A09's disappears whole rather than shifting gradually, and the occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators describe one coherent placement rather than unrelated shifts. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR009, POS001, POS002, POS007]

Limitations: indicators covering the same activity reflect the same traces and are therefore not independent confirmations; direction shares are computed over outgoing transitions rather than cases; zero participation only means an activity was not observed, so the evidence cannot distinguish a true removal of A09 from it simply not being exercised in the later sample; and it cannot tell whether the A01-for-A09 replacement, the A02 insertion and the rewiring reported below are one redesign or separate concurrent changes. [OCC008, DIR009, DIR014, DIR017]

Other differences: A14 became both more frequent and repeating and is now locked in a mutual sequence with A17, with A08 rerouted towards A14 — a repetition and rewiring change in the A07/A08/A14/A17 cluster that the main hypothesis does not cover. [OCC013, OCC016, DIR008, DIR014, DIR017, REL065]
