- **Two alternative branches changed in how often they run.** In the earlier window, A01, A03, A04 and A05 each appeared in about 49 of 100 traces and A02, A06, A07 and A08 each in about 51 of 100; in the later window the first group each appeared in about 58–59 of 100 and the second in about 41–42 of 100. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the routing between A26 and A27 changed: the two-activity segment A30–A31 was executed in fewer traces (about 44 in 100, down from about 52) while the alternative segment A32–A33 was executed in more (about 56 in 100, up from about 48), and within the A30–A31 segment the preferred succession reversed from A26–A31–A30–A27 to A26–A30–A31–A27. The order change is shown directly: the step-distance distributions of all four activity pairs around the segment flipped in a mirrored way (each from roughly 60/40 to 38/62), the successor shares from A30 and A31 swapped almost exactly, and A26's entry shares moved consistently; the frequency change is corroborated by the mirror-image participation of the four activities and by A30 and A31 still appearing only together. [OCC019, OCC020, OCC021, OCC022, REL012, DIR016, DIR019, DIR020, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005]

Two limitations remain. Both internal orders still occur in both windows (the split is about 60/40, not absolute), so the evidence shows a reversed ordering preference between A30 and A31 rather than a wholesale repositioning; and the evidence carries no screening baseline of chance-expected items, so the comparison with what an unchanged process produces rests on the empirical strengths of the individual items and the mirrored consistency of the pattern rather than on an item count. Ordinary sampling variability between two finite windows of 200 traces is the competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out, although a pure-sampling account fits poorly with a coordinated, mirror-image pattern across many indicators. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, REL012]
