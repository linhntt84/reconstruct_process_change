### Observed differences

- The clearest difference concerns activity A02: in the earlier window it appears in none of the 200 traces, while in the later window it appears in all 200 traces, exactly once per case on average (mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, never repeated within a trace). [OCC001, POS002]
- In the later window, A02 has observed outgoing transitions to every other activity — roughly 26 in 100 lead to A04, 25 to A06, 19 to A03, 19 to A01, 7 to A07 and 4 to A05 — whereas in the earlier window A02 had no observed outgoing transitions at all. [DIR002]
- A02 also appears as a new successor of the established activities: about 24 in 100 of A01's outgoing transitions now go to A02 (A01→A04 fell from about 36 to 19 in 100, A01→A06 from about 29 to 22), about 24 of A03's (A03→A01 fell from about 29 to 20), about 18 of A06's, about 9 of A04's and about 28 of A05's; the A05 profile rests on only 28 before / 46 after observations. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, DIR004, DIR005]
- Pairs involving A02 changed from never co-occurring to co-occurring in every trace, with a mixed rather than fixed order: A01 runs before A02 in about 56 in 100 later cases and after it in about 44; A03 before A02 in about 55; A02 before A04 in about 69; A02 and A06 in roughly equal shares. [REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007]
- Consistent with an extra step being inserted, the other activities now occur slightly later in the trace sequence (medians about 0.13 of trace length later for A01, A03 and A06, e.g. A01 from 0.2 to 0.33), and some gaps stretched: A01→A04 within one step fell from about 47 to 26 in 100 while a 4-step gap appeared at about 23 in 100; A06→A04 gained a 4-step gap at about 20 in 100 (previously 0); A03→A04 gained a 4-step gap at about 15 in 100. [POS001, POS003, POS005, POS004, LAG002, LAG012, LAG005]
- Other differences: order shares between pre-existing pairs moved by a few points (A03 before A04 from about 66 to 72 in 100; A04 before A06 from about 67 to 71; A01 before A04 from about 73 to 68), with low-to-moderate empirical strength, so these may be sampling variation. [REL008, REL010, REL003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A02, was added to the process and became a mandatory step executed exactly once per case, inserted at a flexible point among the existing activities: in the later window it follows A01, A03, A04, A05, A06 and A07 in varying shares and has no fixed order relative to A01, A03, A04 and A06 (though it tends to precede A04). Its insertion is accompanied by the downstream shifts — A01, A03, A04 and A06 occurring slightly later in the sequence and several inter-activity gaps lengthening. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, POS002, LAG002, LAG012, LAG005]

Supporting signs: the change is shown directly and consistently by every indicator family — participation moving from 0 in 200 to 200 in 200 traces, a complete new edge profile for A02, co-occurrence jumping from 0 to 100% for four pairs, and matching position and lag shifts. Presence in every one of 200 later traces against absence in all 200 earlier traces makes a rare-variant sampling explanation implausible. [OCC001, DIR002, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, POS002]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely new step from a previously dormant branch that is now always taken, nor pin down a single fixed insertion slot — the mixed orders suggest the insertion point varies from case to case. The A05 connection rests on few observations, and the small order-share shifts between pre-existing pairs are not part of this hypothesis and may be noise. [DIR005, REL008, REL010, REL003]

Other differences: the minor order-share changes among A01, A03, A04 and A06 noted above, plus small lag redistributions of moderate strength, are not explained by the main hypothesis and may reflect sampling variation. [REL003, REL008, REL010, LAG007, LAG009]
