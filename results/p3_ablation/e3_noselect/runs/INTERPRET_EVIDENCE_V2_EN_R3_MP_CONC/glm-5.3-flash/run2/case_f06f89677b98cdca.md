### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was added to the process between A18 and the activities that previously followed it. In the earlier window A01 was not observed in any of 200 traces, and A18 appeared in about 81 of 200 traces always without A01; in the later window every trace containing A18 (about 95 of 200) also contains A01, with A18 always executed first — immediately before A01 in about two-thirds of A18's executions. A18's outgoing flow now runs mainly to A01 (about 65 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions, an edge not seen earlier), and A01 leads on almost exclusively to the targets that previously followed A18 directly, while A18's direct edges to those targets shrank to about a third; A18's relative position also moved slightly earlier, consistent with an activity added after it. [REL001, OCC001, DIR017, DIR001, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: the earlier window cannot distinguish a genuinely new activity from one present at a rate too low to be sampled (below roughly 2 in 100 traces, given 0 occurrences in 200 traces), and A01's exact placement varies, since in about a third of A18's later executions A01 does not immediately follow A18; the occurrence, position and relation items also describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, REL001, DIR017]
