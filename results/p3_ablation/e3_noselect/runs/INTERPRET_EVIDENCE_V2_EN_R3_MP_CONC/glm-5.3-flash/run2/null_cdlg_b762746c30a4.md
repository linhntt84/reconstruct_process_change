### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence
The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. All shifts are small — at most about 7 percentage points in participation and about 10 points on a single transition share — and even the strongest items reach an empirical strength of only about 0.85, a level that random re-splits of unchanged traces still produce in roughly 15 in 100 comparisons, so sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows cannot be ruled out [OCC002, OCC005, DIR002].
