### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, execution frequency shifted between two groups of activities: A05, A09, A10, A11 and A12 repeat more often within a case (for example A09 from about 1.9 to about 2.4 occurrences per trace, A05 from about 1.9 to about 2.3), while A01, A07 and A15 repeat less often (A07 and A15 from about 1.4 to about 1.1, A01 from about 2.5 to about 2.2), with A08 marginally lower. Because the share of traces containing these activities moved by only a few cases in 100, this reads as a change in how often parts of the process repeat, not in whether they run. [OCC001, OCC004, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011, OCC013]

Supporting signs: every named activity is well observed in both windows (A09's counts rest on 375 versus 477 executions, A01's on about 507 versus 431), all occurrence components move in the same direction within each activity, A07 and A15 behave identically down to mirror-image transitions towards A01 and each other, and the traffic leaving A10 grew from about 192 to 218 transitions, consistent with its higher repetition. [DIR006, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, OCC001, OCC004, OCC006, OCC008, OCC013]

Limitations: the two windows are finite samples, each individual occurrence difference is exceeded in roughly 13–34 of 100 random re-splits, and the evidence does not report how many qualifying items an unchanged process would produce, so the case rests on the consistent two-group pattern rather than on any single item; the evidence also cannot show whether the two groups' changes have one shared origin, and it contains no ordering or positional items, so no conclusion about the order or placement of activities is supported. The main competing explanation that remains open is ordinary sampling variation between the two windows. [OCC001, OCC004, OCC008, OCC010, OCC011]

Other differences: at A10 the existing branch to A02 is taken about 1.7 times as often as before (about 19 to about 32 in 100 of A10's outgoing transitions), with the branches to A04, A06, A03 and A16 each a few points lower but still in use, and A02 receives fewer transitions from A08 and A04 than before; A02 and A04 themselves appear in about 6 fewer traces in 100 with flat occurrence counts, a small and ambiguous movement.
