### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added to the process and now sits on existing paths between the other activities: in the later window A01 receives edges from A03, A05, A06 and A11 and leads to A05 and A10, running in about 46 in 100 traces, usually about twice per trace that contains it, around the middle of the trace. This rests on direct relational evidence — the successor profiles of A05 and A06 split from a single successor to roughly half via A01, co-occurrence with A01 rises from zero to 28–46 in 100, and pairwise distances between A03, A05, A06, A10 and A11 stretch by about one position — and every strongly flagged item involves A01, describing one coherent structural addition rather than scattered shifts. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, POS001]

Limitations: no screening baseline (items expected without change) was provided, so the against-chance comparison could not be made numerically; the earlier window shows only that A01 was not observed there, not that it was absent; and the exact placement — which predecessors route through A01 in which cases, and why A01 both follows and precedes A05 — cannot be determined from these profiles. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 existed before as a rare path that happened to be sampled only in the later window, although its presence in 92 of 200 later traces makes pure sampling coincidence unlikely. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR005]

Other differences: participation in A03, A05, A09 and A10 each dropped slightly (about 5–7 in 100 traces, e.g., A05/A09/A10 from about 53 to 46 in 100) and A05 and A10 now occur slightly later within their traces; these small shifts may follow from A01's addition or be sampling noise.
