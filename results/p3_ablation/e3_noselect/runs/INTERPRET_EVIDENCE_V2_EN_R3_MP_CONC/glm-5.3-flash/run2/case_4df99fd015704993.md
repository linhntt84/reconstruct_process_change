### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the cyclic region involving A03 and A12 was restructured in two ways: A12 now leads directly back to A03 where the return previously passed through A14 (an activity no longer observed at all), and the loop's exit now runs A03 → A02 → A13 through the new activity A02, instead of A03 leading directly to A13. The support is direct — the successor profiles of A12 and A03 changed exactly at these points, A02's only observed successor is A13, and A02/A13 always co-occur with A02 first — and consistent across occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators, with the lag shifts (A12→A03 from 2 steps to 1, A03→A13 from 1 to 2, loop gaps from 3 to 2) matching the new wiring. The magnitudes make sampling an unlikely explanation: an activity in ~26% of earlier traces is absent from all 200 later traces, and one absent from all 200 earlier traces appears in ~39% of later traces. Limitations: no screening baseline is provided, so the number of qualifying items cannot be compared with a chance expectation; several items reflect the same underlying traces and are not independent confirmations; and the evidence cannot distinguish an edit of the existing path from the old A03-family variant being replaced wholesale by a differently wired one. [OCC002, OCC014, DIR001, DIR002, DIR010, DIR011, REL006, LAG003, LAG009, LAG001, LAG010]

Other differences: A01 appears in ~28% of later traces with unknown placement, and a group of ~27.5% of later traces contains neither A03 nor A07 (earlier, one of the two was always present), while participation of the A04–A11 family fell from ~50% to ~33.5% of traces — suggesting an additional new or expanded trace family whose contents the evidence does not reveal; A09's routing also shifted slightly toward A06 and A07 away from A10. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that these broader participation changes are an independent change in variant frequencies rather than a consequence of the A02/A14 restructuring.
