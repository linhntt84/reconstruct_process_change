### Observed differences

- **A01 appears in every case in the later window.** In the earlier window A01 was observed in 0 of 200 cases; in the later window it occurs in all 200 cases, exactly once per case. A participation difference of this size arises in fewer than about 1 in 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001]

- **A01 occupies the final position of the case.** In the later window A01 always sits at relative position 1.0 with no spread. In the earlier window that same position was always held by A06, which at that time had no observed outgoing transitions (0 across 200 cases), so this position marks the end of the case. [POS001, DIR002]

- **A06 moved off the final position and now always leads into A01.** Earlier, A06 sat at relative position 1.0 in every case; later it sits at a mean relative position of about 0.87 (median about 0.88, with slight spread), and all 200 of its observed outgoing transitions go to A01. [POS002, DIR002]

- **The A01–A06 relationship flipped from "A06 alone" to "always together, A06 first".** Earlier, in all 200 cases A06 appeared without A01 and the two never co-occurred; later, in all 200 cases they co-occur and A06 comes before A01 every time. [REL001]

- **Other measured activities are essentially stable.** A02 (present in all cases, about 2.0 vs 2.1 occurrences per case, recurring in about 54% vs 52% of cases) and A03 (present in about 54% vs 52% of cases) fluctuate only slightly, and A02's exits split between A04 and A03 shift from about 50/50 to about 48/52; all of these differences fall within the range random re-splits produce (empirical strength about 0.45). [OCC002, OCC003, DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added as a new final step: in the later window every case executes A01 exactly once at the end, directly after A06, whereas in the earlier window A01 was never observed and cases ended with A06. The signs are direct and mutually consistent — participation rises from 0 to 200 of 200 cases with exactly one execution each, A01 always takes the final relative position that A06 alone held before, A06 precedes A01 in every later case, and A06, previously without observed successors, now leads to A01 in all of its outgoing transitions. These items describe one underlying difference from different angles rather than independent confirmations, but each facet (occurrence, placement, relation, succession) is observed directly, and the 0-versus-200 participation contrast makes sampling fluctuation an implausible explanation. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, REL001, DIR002]

One limitation: zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that A01 was not observed then, so the evidence cannot rule out that A01 is a relabelled version of a step that previously ran under a different name; positions are event positions rather than clock times, though the relational and successor evidence pins down the ordering. [OCC001, POS001]

Other differences: the small shifts around A02 and A03 (A02's occurrence and recurrence, A03's participation, and A02's split between successors A04 and A03, from about 50/50 to about 48/52) remain within what random re-splits of unchanged data produce and are not part of the hypothesis. [OCC002, OCC003, DIR001]
