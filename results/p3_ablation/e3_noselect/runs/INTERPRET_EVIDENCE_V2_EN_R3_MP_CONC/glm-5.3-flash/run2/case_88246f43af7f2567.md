### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A structural rerouting around A03 is supported as a possibility: in the later window, A02 and A04 lead directly to A12, so A03 and A11 are skipped where they previously formed the bridge back to A12, while A03 now runs between A16 and A10 (A16 → A03, then A03 → A10 or A11) where A16 previously led straight to A10. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the successor sets of A02, A04, A03 and A16 were replaced rather than merely re-weighted; A03's companionship flipped from A02/A04/A12 to A10/A16; A12 now immediately follows A02 and A04, and A10 follows A16 only after two or more steps; and the position and participation shifts of A03, A11, A12, A10 and A16 match this rerouting. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR010, REL001, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001, LAG003, LAG004, POS002, POS005, POS004, POS006, OCC003, OCC011]

The evidence cannot separate a modification of one process from a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants between the two windows, and no chance-expectation baseline for the number of qualifying items is provided; the apparent change in A12's own outgoing split was not used because its earlier share rests on only about five observed transitions. [DIR007]
