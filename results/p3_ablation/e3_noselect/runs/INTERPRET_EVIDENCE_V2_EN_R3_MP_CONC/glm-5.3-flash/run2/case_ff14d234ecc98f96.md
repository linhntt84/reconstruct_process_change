### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: the three activities A26, A27 and A28, which earlier each executed once in every trace between A44 and A29 in varying order, were replaced by a single pass through one of the three activities A19, A20 and A21, which now act as alternatives — together covering nearly every trace, each appearing in roughly 3 in 10 traces, and each leading directly on to A29 — so that A29 now follows A44 about two event positions later instead of about four. [DIR044, OCC025, OCC026, OCC027, OCC018, OCC019, OCC020, REL018, REL019, REL025, DIR019, DIR020, DIR021, LAG012]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor change is observed at A44 itself, the relation flips are confirmed from both sides (the preceding A11/A12 and the following A29/A44), and the key items rank above essentially all reference comparisons (empirical strength about 0.99), far beyond what random re-splits of 200-trace windows produce. The agreeing items largely reflect this one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [DIR044, LAG012, REL002, REL005, REL023, REL024, OCC025]

Limitations: A26–A28 were still observed in 1–2 of 200 later traces, so a residual execution in a very small share of cases cannot be excluded; the positional evidence uses event positions, so no clock-time claim follows; and the evidence cannot show whether the single new step covers the same work as the three removed activities — a competing reading is that the old steps were succeeded by a different, shorter step rather than a like-for-like consolidation. [OCC025, OCC026, OCC027, POS003]
