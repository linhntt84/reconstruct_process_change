### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the two alternative paths between A20 and A30 swapped how often they are taken: in the earlier window the branch containing A24, A25 and A26 ran in about 82 in 100 traces while the branch with A21, A22 and A23 ran in about 19–20 in 100; in the later window the A21–A23 branch ran in about 79–80 in 100 traces (A20 preceding A21, A22 or A23 in about 77–78 in 100) and the A24–A26 branch in about 21–22 in 100. The change is shown directly in A20's outgoing transitions and in the co-occurrence and ordering of the six activities; both branches are well observed in both windows, the order within each group stays mixed in both windows, all six activities keep similar positions around two-thirds through the trace, and A20 and A30 themselves remain in every trace — this fits a shift in how often existing alternative paths are taken rather than activities being added, removed or reordered. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows sample a changed mix of coexisting process variants rather than a rerouting within one variant.
