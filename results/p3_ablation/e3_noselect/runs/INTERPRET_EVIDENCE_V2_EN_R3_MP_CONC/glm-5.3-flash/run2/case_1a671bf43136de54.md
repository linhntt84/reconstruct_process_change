### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A01 is executed as an additional step in a subset of cases: it was not observed in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces) and appears in about 15 in 100 traces afterwards, placed around two-thirds into the trace, typically directly after A08 and followed by A09 or A10. The occurrence, position, successor and relation items all describe this one underlying difference rather than serving as independent confirmations of it. Other differences: A07, A12, A13 and A14 occur less often in the later window, and A08 repeats fewer times per trace (about 3.4 versus 4.0), while A02, A04 and A06 occur slightly more often; the evidence does not link these shifts to A01's appearance.
