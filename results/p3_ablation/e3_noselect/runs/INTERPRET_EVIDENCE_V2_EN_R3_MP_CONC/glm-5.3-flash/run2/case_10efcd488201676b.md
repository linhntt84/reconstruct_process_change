### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was structurally recomposed in the later window: A01 and A17 are no longer executed, A07 is newly executed near the start of traces (leading on to A03 or A04), A04 always leads directly to A10, and traces close with A06 and A16 executed exactly once each as an adjacent pair in either order — replacing the earlier closing pair A10/A17 after A04 and the earlier optional, repeated mid-trace execution of A06 and A16, with A12 rerouted from A01 directly to A09. Supporting signs: the participation changes are categorical rather than gradual (A01 64 versus 0, A17 100 versus 0, A06/A16 about 40 versus 100 in 100 traces), the rerouting is observed directly in the successor profiles, and the occurrence, position, successor, relation and lag indicators consistently describe the same trace behaviour.
