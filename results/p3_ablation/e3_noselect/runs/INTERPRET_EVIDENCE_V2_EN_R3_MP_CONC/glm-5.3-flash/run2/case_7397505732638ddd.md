### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the ordering constraints and routing among the block A15, A17, A18, A19, A20, A21, together with its links to A26 and A27, were restructured: a fixed sequence (A26→A17→A15→A18→A19→A21→A20→A27) gave way in the later window to a web in which A15/A17/A18 can pass directly to A19/A20/A21, A19/A20/A21 can precede A15/A17/A18, A18 commonly connects straight to A27, and A26 can enter at A19, while every activity keeps appearing in every trace. The change is shown directly and coherently by four independent views — successor profiles changing from one 100% successor to several (DIR), pair orders flipping from 100%/0% to mixed or reversed (REL), positions shifting in matching directions (POS), and fixed step distances dissolving (LAG); about 46 items, each more extreme than in roughly 199 of 200 random re-splits, all describe this one region, far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces, and the perfectly deterministic earlier profiles make the later spread a qualitative change rather than sampling noise. Limitation: these are aggregate shares, so the evidence cannot show whether every case now draws flexibly among the new transitions or whether the later window mixes cases still following the old chain with cases following a rewired one.
