Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new activity, A03, was added in the later window and placed into the existing flow between A05/A08/A12 and their successors A08, A11 and A12: A03 runs once in roughly half of the later traces, early in the trace, with observed edges from A05, A08 and A12 into A03 and from A03 to A08, A11 and A12.
