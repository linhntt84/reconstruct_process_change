### Observed differences

- The three activities A14, A15 and A20 act as exclusive alternatives in both windows: they never occur in the same trace, and exactly one of them runs in every case (their shares sum to 1.0 — about 38, 32 and 31 in 100 before; about 34, 35 and 32 in 100 after). [OCC013, OCC014, OCC019, REL001, REL002, REL008]
- In the earlier window this branch sat late in the case, at a mean position of about 0.60–0.61 of the trace, after A21 and after the A22–A23 block; in the later window it sits right after A40, at a mean position of about 0.32. [POS001, POS002, POS003, REL003, REL004, REL009, REL010, REL014, REL015]
- Successors were replaced, not merely reweighted: before, A40 was always followed directly by A21, and A14/A15/A20 led to A23 (about 50 in 100) or A27 (about 50 in 100); in the later window, A40 leads to one of A14/A15/A20 (roughly 34/35/32 in 100) and each of the three always leads to A21. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a rerouting in which the exclusive three-way branch A14/A15/A20 changed its place in the process: in the earlier window it was embedded in the late A22–A23–A27 segment (executed after A21 and A22, before A27), while in the later window it is executed directly between A40 and A21, so that the former one-step hand-over A40 → A21 now passes through exactly one of A14/A15/A20. [DIR029, DIR010, DIR011, DIR016, DIR018, DIR019, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL003, REL004, REL009, REL010, REL014, REL015, LAG002, LAG004]

This hypothesis is supported directly and consistently by four views of the same behaviour: successor profiles were replaced rather than only reweighted; the branch's position shifted by about 0.29 while A21/A22/A23 moved only marginally; the order against A21 and A22 reversed and against A23 changed from a mixed order to a fixed one; and the step-distance changes match the rerouting exactly. The branch's participation stayed within about ±4 in 100 and its exclusivity remained intact, which argues against the activities having become optional, mandatory, added or removed. [DIR010, DIR011, DIR016, DIR029, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL014, REL015, REL016, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, OCC013, OCC014, OCC019, REL001, REL002, REL008]

Limitations: the evidence shows the resulting behaviour, not the underlying modification — a single relocation of the branch and a combination of separate edge removals and additions would look alike here; positions are event positions rather than clock times, so nothing can be said about timing; and the evidence set carries no screening baseline, so the number of flagged items cannot be checked against a chance expectation. [POS001, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: the decreased repetition of A10/A11 with rerouting toward A39, the shifted choice between A16 and A17 with changed A19/A28 shares, and the mild A41–A45 repetition and share changes are separate differences and are not part of the main hypothesis.
