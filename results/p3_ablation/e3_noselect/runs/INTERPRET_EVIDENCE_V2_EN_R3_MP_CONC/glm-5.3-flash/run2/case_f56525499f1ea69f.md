### Observed differences

- The largest difference concerns two groups of three activities, {A33, A35, A36} and {A37, A38, A39}. In the earlier window every trace executed exactly one of the two groups — the first in about 97 of 200 traces, the second in about 103 — and the two groups never appeared in the same trace. In the later window each of the six activities appears in about 197–199 of 200 traces, both groups run in the same trace in about 98 in 100 traces, and the first group precedes the second in about 97–98 in 100 of those. [OCC030, OCC031, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, OCC035, REL054, REL055, REL056, REL058, REL059, REL060, REL061, REL062, REL063]

- Within each group the activities keep no fixed order in either window: both orders occur in similar proportions (for example, A33 before A35 and A35 before A33 each in roughly half of the traces containing both, before and after). [REL052, REL053, REL057, REL064, REL065, REL066]

- The two groups sit between A30 and A31. In the later window A30 leads almost exclusively into the first group (about 37 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A35 and about 30 each to A33 and A36, versus a mix of both groups before), the first-group activities now lead into the second group instead of directly to A31 (A33 to A31 fell from about 36 in 100 to under 1 in 100), and A31 is reached after the second group. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two alternative three-activity segments — one consisting of A33, A35 and A36, the other of A37, A38 and A39, which in the earlier window were mutually exclusive alternatives run in a roughly 50/50 split — are in the later window both executed in nearly every case, first the A33/A35/A36 group and then the A37/A38/A39 group (each internally unordered), between A30 and A31. The change is shown directly by relational evidence: co-occurrence of the two groups rose from zero to about 98 in 100 traces with the first group first in about 97–98 in 100, and the occurrence, position, step-distance and successor evidence all shift consistently with one extra block of three activities per trace. The many agreeing items describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, and no chance-expectation baseline (screening counts) is provided, so the judgement rests on the directness and coherence of the relational evidence; the evidence also cannot show whether all cases changed or whether a previously rare "both blocks" variant came to dominate the case mix, nor when or why. [OCC030, OCC031, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, OCC035, REL054, REL055, REL056, REL058, REL059, REL060, REL061, REL062, REL063, REL040, REL046, POS005, POS006, LAG023, LAG027, DIR030, DIR033]

Other differences: A04 occurs in far fewer traces and its remaining occurrences sit later in the trace; the rare activities A02, A06, A08 and A10 occur about twice to four times as often on small samples; A15 and A16 repeat slightly more often with a looser A14→A15→A16 order; A43 repeats somewhat less. These are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC004, POS001, OCC002, OCC006, OCC008, OCC010, OCC015, OCC016, REL012, REL013, OCC038]
