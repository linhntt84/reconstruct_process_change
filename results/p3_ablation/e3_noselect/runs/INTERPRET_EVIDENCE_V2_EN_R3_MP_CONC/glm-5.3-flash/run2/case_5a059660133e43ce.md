### Observed differences

- In the earlier window A01 is not observed at all (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it occurs in about 22 in 100 traces (44 of 200), and in every one of those traces it is the final event of the case. [OCC001, POS001]
- A01 always occurs together with A02, with A02 executed before it or interleaved with it (about 11 in 100 traces each), and it never shares a trace with A06 or A15. [REL001, REL002, REL005]

- A02's routing shifts in the same direction: its direct transition to A15 (about 12 in 100 of its outgoing transitions earlier) is no longer observed, transitions to A01 (about 21 in 100) and A12 (about 8 in 100) appear, and the A02–A15 pair, earlier typically consecutive, is now typically two events apart. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the closing of the process was restructured, involving A01, A06 and A15, with A02 as the preceding step. In the earlier window, A06 acted as a terminal step (always at the final position, with no observed outgoing transitions) while A15 ran in separate traces directly after A02, the two never sharing a trace; in the later window, A06 always flows into A15 (32 of 32 observed executions), making "A06 then A15" a fixed closing sequence in about 16 in 100 traces, while A01 — unobserved earlier — closes about 22 in 100 traces as the final event after A02. A01 and the A06–A15 sequence never occur in the same trace, so they behave as alternative endings. [OCC001, POS001, POS003, DIR001, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL005, REL012]

This hypothesis is read directly from relational and positional evidence — a never-co-occurring pair becoming an always-sequential pair, a new activity fixed at the last position, and the disappearance of the direct A02→A15 transition — and it is consistent across the occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators, with the key items showing differences that random re-splits of the same data rarely produce (empirical strengths about 0.99). Limitations: the A01 addition and the A06→A15 coupling could be two independent changes rather than one restructuring, the items describe overlapping traces and therefore do not confirm the hypothesis independently, and with 200 traces per window it cannot be excluded that A01 existed earlier but was simply not observed. [REL012, DIR003, POS001, POS003, DIR001]

Other differences: the A08–A12 subsequence is executed far less often in the later window (each activity in about 10 in 100 traces, down from about 18) and its internal coupling dissolves — A08 no longer leads mainly to A12 but to A09, A04 and A02, A12 leads mainly to A09, and A04 returns to A02 instead of entering A08 — a reduction and rewiring of that part of the process that the evidence shows but that is not part of the closing hypothesis above.
