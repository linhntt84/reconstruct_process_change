### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change in the segment between A44 and A29: in the earlier window, after A44 typically exactly one of the alternatives A19, A20 or A21 executed (each in roughly 30–35 of 100 traces, sometimes preceded by A10) before A29, whereas in the later window A19, A20 and A21 are nearly unobserved and A26, A27 and A28 each execute once in every trace, after A44 and before A29, in no fixed mutual order. [OCC019, OCC020, OCC021, OCC024, OCC025, OCC026, DIR043, REL038, REL043, REL047, REL037, REL042, REL046, REL035, REL036, REL041]

Supporting signs: the participation flips are maximal for 200-trace windows (0 to 200 of 200 traces and back); the rerouting at A44 rests on about 200 outgoing transitions in each window; the relational items show the new trio's placement directly after A44 and before A29; and the A44-to-A29 distance grew from about 2 steps to about 4, consistent with one activity being replaced by three. A10's mediating role in the same segment receded (about 26 to about 11 in 100 traces; A44-to-A10 about 23 to about 2 in 100 of A44's outgoing transitions). [OCC024, DIR043, REL038, LAG009, OCC010]

Limitation and competing explanation: the evidence cannot rule out that the A26/A27/A28 pattern existed earlier only as a rare variant (at most 1 of 200 traces) and that the two windows reflect a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants rather than a modified process; the single later occurrences of A19, A20 and A21 are too few to characterise what, if anything, follows them now. [OCC024, OCC025, OCC026, OCC019, DIR018]
