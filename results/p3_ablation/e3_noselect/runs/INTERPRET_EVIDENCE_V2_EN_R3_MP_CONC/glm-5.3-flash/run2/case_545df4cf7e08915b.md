### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the control flow was restructured in two places. First, A01 was no longer executed and A08 was executed in its place: A01 dropped from about 48 in 100 traces to none, A08 rose from none to about 48 in 100, and A08 inherited A01's predecessor (A17), a near-identical successor mix (A09 about half, A10 and A16 about a quarter of outgoing transitions) and the same mid-trace position. Second, A11 and A15 swapped places: earlier, A11 was the one-shot final activity that always accompanied A05 while A15 sat between A18 and A03; in the later window, A15 is the one-shot final activity after A05 and A11 is the activity that leads back into A03. [OCC001, OCC006, DIR001, DIR006, DIR012, POS001, POS004, OCC009, OCC011, POS007, POS008, DIR005, DIR008, DIR010, REL023, REL024]

The hypothesis rests on relational and positional evidence rather than frequencies alone: the successor profile of A01 earlier matches that of A08 later, and A17's edge switched from A01 to A08; relations containing A01 flip to the corresponding relations containing A08 (A01–A03 co-occurred in 48 in 100 traces earlier while A03 now always appears without A01, mirrored by A03–A08 co-occurrence in 48 in 100 traces later; A01 and A05 were strict alternatives earlier, as A08 and A05, and A08 and A15, are later; the two never co-occur in either window); and for A11 and A15 the position distributions swap exactly (each concentrated at the final position in one window and mid-trace in the other), their order reverses, and the lag partners of A18 exchange between lag 1 and lag 4. [DIR001, DIR006, DIR012, REL001, REL003, REL012, REL002, REL020, REL031, POS007, POS008, REL046, LAG023, LAG024]

Limitations: the activity codes are anonymous, so for the A01/A08 pair the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine substitution of the step from a renaming of the same step. The screening expectation (how many items would qualify by chance without a change) is not part of this evidence, so the formal chance comparison could not be made; however, two activities flipping from complete presence to complete absence across 200-trace windows, with matching edge, position and lag profiles across dozens of consistent items at empirical strengths of about 0.995, is difficult to explain as sampling variation. [OCC001, OCC006, DIR001, DIR006, POS001, POS004, POS007, POS008, LAG023, LAG024]

Other differences: A03 repeats somewhat less in the later window, the A04–A18 branch is executed less often and with fewer repetitions, A07 participation fell from about 38 to 27 in 100 traces, and the A10/A16 routing between A12 and A13 shifted slightly; the evidence does not show whether these frequency shifts follow from the structural reconfiguration or are independent changes.
