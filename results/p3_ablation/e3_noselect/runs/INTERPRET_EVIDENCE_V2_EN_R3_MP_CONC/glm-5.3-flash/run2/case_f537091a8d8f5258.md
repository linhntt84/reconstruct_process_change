### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A01, A04, A05, A06, A07 and A09 each occurred in about 48 in 100 traces and always together (whenever one appeared, all six did); in the later window, each occurred in about 55 in 100 traces (96 vs 109 of 200), again always together. [OCC001, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC009, REL001, REL010]
- The four activities A03, A08, A10 and A11 moved in lockstep in the opposite direction: about 28 in 100 traces earlier, about 21 in 100 later (55 vs 41 of 200). A02 stayed at about 25 in 100 in both windows. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC008, OCC010, OCC011]
- Within the six-activity block, the earlier window ran A01 → A04 → A07 → A09 and finished with A05 and A06 after A09, with no fixed order between them (A05 first in about 25 in 100 traces, A06 first in about 23 in 100). In the later window, A05 and A06 execute directly after A04 and before A07 and A09, always as A05 then A06. [REL010, REL015, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009, DIR003, DIR006]
- Every relation between {A05, A06} and {A07, A09} reversed completely: earlier A07 preceded A05 in about 48 in 100 traces (A05 first: none) and A09 preceded A06 in about 48 in 100 (A06 first: none); later A05 preceded A07 in about 55 in 100 (A07 first: none) and A06 preceded A09 in about 50 in 100 (A09 first: about 5). Relations with A01 and A04 kept their direction in both windows. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL002, REL003, REL007]
- Event positions and step distances match (positions are event positions, not clock time): A05's mean relative position fell from about 0.89 to 0.32 and A06's from 0.89 to 0.65, while A07's rose from 0.36 to 0.74 and A09's from 0.59 to 0.98; A05 now follows A01/A04 within 1–2 steps (earlier 4–5 in about 91 in 100 cases), while A07 and A09 now follow A04 at 3–5 steps (earlier 1–3). [POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG002, LAG003, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG004, LAG005, LAG010]
- Successors moved accordingly: A06 led to A05 in about 96 in 100 of its outgoing transitions earlier but to A07 in about 76 in 100 later (A05 no longer observed as its successor); A04's dominant successor changed from A07 (about 80 in 100) to A05 (about 51 in 100); A01's successor split from mostly A04 (about 76 in 100) into A04 and A05 (about 45 in 100 each). A09, which earlier handed over to A05 or A06 in nearly all of its about 96 traces, was observed with a successor in only about 12 traces later and now sits at the trace end (median position 1.0). [DIR005, DIR003, DIR001, DIR008, POS006]
- The later window also contains a minority ordering practically absent earlier: A05 precedes A04 in about 16 in 100 traces (about 1 earlier) and A04 precedes A01 in about 17 in 100 (about 7 earlier), usually one step apart. [REL006, REL001, LAG012, LAG006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, within the recurring block A01–A04–A05–A06–A07–A09, the placement of A05 and A06 changed: in the earlier window they executed at the end of the block, after A07 and A09, in no fixed mutual order, whereas in the later window they execute between A04 and A07 as a fixed pair (A05 then A06), with A07 shifted later and A09 typically ending the trace. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, DIR005, DIR008]

Supporting signs: the reversal is complete rather than partial (for example A07 before A05 in about 48 in 100 traces earlier versus none later, and A05 before A07 in none versus all later co-occurring traces), positions, step distances and successor shares all shift in the same direction, and the key items exceed differences from about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data, making sampling noise an implausible explanation for reversals of this completeness. [REL011, REL012, POS003, LAG007, LAG009, DIR003, DIR005, DIR001]

Limitations: the OCC, REL, POS, LAG and DIR items largely describe the same underlying trace behaviour, so they converge on one difference rather than independently confirming it; no screening baseline is included, so the expected number of chance findings could not be compared directly; and the evidence cannot distinguish a relocation of A05 and A06 within one path from a replacement of the earlier path variant by a new one that happens to place these activities earlier. The minority later-window pattern (A05 before A04, A04 before A01) is detected but not fully structured by the evidence. [REL006, REL001, LAG012, LAG006]

Other differences: the share of traces containing the block A01/A04/A05/A06/A07/A09 rose from about 48 to about 55 in 100, while the group A03/A08/A10/A11 fell from about 28 to about 21 in 100; the internal routing among A03, A08, A10 and A11 showed no meaningful change. Whether these participation shifts are connected to the reordering cannot be determined from this evidence. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC008, OCC010, OCC011, OCC002, DIR002, DIR007, DIR009]
