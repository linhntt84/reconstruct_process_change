### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A02, a step that was mandatory and executed once at or near the start of every trace — preceding A03 in about 89 in 100 traces and A04 in about 88 in 100 — is no longer executed in the later window, so traces now enter A03, A04 or A05 without passing through A02. The pattern is direct and consistent across four indicator families: participation fell from 200 of 200 traces to 0 of 200, all 199 observed outgoing transitions of A02 disappeared, co-occurrence with A03 and A04 dropped from 100% to 0%, and A02's positional footprint vanished, each on 200 traces per window. Limitations: zero participation only shows that A02 was not observed, so a renaming or merging of A02 into another activity cannot be ruled out; and the small position shifts of A03 and A04 are plausibly mechanical consequences of the removal rather than independent relocations. [OCC001, DIR002, POS001, REL001, REL002]

Other differences: minor redistributions of the surviving routing shares (e.g., A04 → A03 up about 8 points, A03 → A04 down about 6 points) and a slight tilt in the A03/A04 ordering toward A04 first, with A03→A04 gaps concentrating at two steps; these are small and may be side effects of A02's disappearance rather than independent changes. [DIR003, DIR004, REL003, LAG001]
