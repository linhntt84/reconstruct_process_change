### Observed differences

- Activity A06 appeared in about 52 in 100 traces in the earlier window (104 of 200) but in none of the 200 traces in the later window; its participation and mean event position both fell to zero. [OCC006, POS003]
- In the earlier window, all observed outgoing transitions of A05 led to A06, and all observed outgoing transitions of A06 led to A04, forming the sequence A05 → A06 → A04. In the later window, all outgoing transitions of A05 lead directly to A04, and no outgoing transitions of A06 are observed. [DIR001, DIR002, REL002, REL003]
- The ordered pair A05 then A04 still occurs in about half of the traces in both windows, but its spacing changed: A04 followed A05 two steps later in the earlier window and one step later in the later window. [LAG001, REL001]
- A04 and A05 now also appear in traces without A06 (about 50 in 100 traces each), whereas earlier they virtually never appeared without A06 between them. [REL002, REL003]
- Event positions shifted consistently with one event fewer between the two: A04's typical position moved earlier (about 0.75 to about 0.67 of the trace) and A05's moved later (about 0.25 to about 0.33). [POS001, POS002]
- Participation of A01 through A05 changed only marginally (about 1.5 in 100 traces, e.g. A02–A05 from 52% to about 50%); these differences are not unusual for random re-splits of the data (empirical strength about 0.12). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A06 is no longer executed between A05 and A04: the earlier fragment A05 → A06 → A04, present in about 52 in 100 traces, now appears as A05 leading directly to A04, with A06 not observed at all. [OCC006, DIR001, REL002, REL003, LAG001]

The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: the rerouting at A05 is complete, with every observed transition moving from A06 to A04 [DIR001]; A06's disappearance is large and one-sided, from about 52 in 100 traces to zero out of 200 [OCC006]; the A04–A05 relationship itself is stable, with the two still co-occurring in about half the traces and A05 preceding A04 in essentially all of them, while their gap shrank from two steps to one [REL001, LAG001]; and the position shifts of A04 and A05 fit the loss of one intervening event [POS001, POS002].

Limitations: zero participation only means A06 was not observed in the later window. The evidence cannot fully rule out that A06 still occurs but its events are no longer recorded, which would look identical in the log; the complete rerouting of A05 to A04 and the unchanged A04–A05 co-occurrence are, however, consistent with A06 genuinely dropping out of the sequence. No screening baseline for chance expectations was provided, so the comparison against random variation rests on the sample sizes themselves. [OCC006, DIR001]

Other differences: the small participation shifts of A01–A05 (about 1.5 in 100 traces, low empirical strength) are most plausibly sampling variation and are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
