### Observed differences

- In the later window, A43 appears in about 70 in 100 traces, up from about 58 in 100, and occurs about 1.0 times per trace on average, up from 0.78; A43 is still never observed without A42. A42, present in every trace in both windows, now occurs about 1.8 times per trace, up from about 1.6, and recurs within a trace in about 42 in 100 traces, up from about 38 in 100. A43's typical position in the trace is nearly unchanged (mean relative position about 0.90 vs 0.88), so this is a frequency shift, not a relocation. [OCC040, OCC041, POS002]

- The two activities increasingly occur together: traces with A42 but without A43 fell from about 43 in 100 to about 30 in 100, while joint occurrence rose from about 58 to about 70 in 100; among joint traces, A42-before-A43 rose from 27.5% to 32.5% and interleaved-or-repeated orderings from 30% to 37.5%. [REL001]

- Transitions around the pair were re-weighted toward A42–A43: of A42's outgoing transitions, the share to A43 rose from about 48% to about 56% while the share to A44 fell from about 51% to about 43%; of A43's outgoing transitions, the share back to A42 rose from about 38% to about 43% while the share to A46 fell from about 62% to about 53%; similarly, A45 now feeds A42 more often (37% vs 43%) and A46 less often (61% vs 52%). [DIR042, DIR043, DIR045]

- Repeats sit slightly farther apart in the later window: among A43→A42 pairs within five steps, the share at distance 1 fell from about 42% to about 33%, and self-repeats of A42 and A43 likewise shifted from shorter to somewhat longer distances (event positions, not clock time). [LAG001, LAG003, LAG004]

- Participation was also redistributed across many mid-frequency activities: A01, A05, A07, A23, A35, A36 and A39 each lost roughly 6–10 percentage points (e.g., A07 from 58% to 49% of traces), while A03, A11, A12, A13, A24, A37 and A38 each gained roughly 4–9 points (e.g., A38 from 51% to 59%). [OCC001, OCC003, OCC005, OCC007, OCC011, OCC012, OCC013, OCC023, OCC024, OCC033, OCC034, OCC035, OCC036, OCC037]

- Branch shares moved in the same directions: A31 routes to A38 more often (22% vs 32%) and to A36 less (28% vs 17%); A35 routes to A36 more (44% vs 56%) and to A32 less; A36 routes to A32 more (45% vs 55%) and to A35 less; A38 routes to A37 more (46% vs 54%); A37 routes to A32 more (47% vs 55%); A12 routes to A11 less (39% vs 26%) and to A13/A14 more, while A13 routes to A11 more (26% vs 39%); A40 routes to A41 more (61% vs 70%) and to A39 less (37% vs 29%). - A caution: A02, A04, A08, A10 and A47 show almost completely changed successor profiles, but these rest on only about 4–18 outgoing transitions per window and their differences are not unusual for random re-splits (strength 0.0–0.55), so they most likely reflect small-sample instability rather than rewiring. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR008, DIR010, DIR047]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the A42–A43 segment is executed and repeated more often: A43 joins about 12 more traces in 100 (about 58 to about 70 in 100) and occurs about 1.0 times per trace instead of 0.78, A42 occurs about 1.8 times per trace instead of about 1.6, and the A42→A43 and A43→A42 transitions carry larger shares of their sources' outgoing traffic while A42→A44 and A43→A46 carry smaller shares. [OCC040, OCC041, REL001, DIR042, DIR043]

The support is direct and consistent: the co-occurrence shift is shown by a relational item whose difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, the frequency changes occur on activities observed in all or most traces in both windows, and the successor shifts rest on roughly 150–360 outgoing transitions per profile; A43's stable position rules out a relocation. Note that these items overlap in the traces they reflect, so they should be read as one coherent signal rather than independent confirmations. [REL001, OCC040, OCC041, DIR042, DIR043, POS002]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that part of the routing shift (A42→A43 versus A42→A44) is a by-product of A43's higher frequency interacting with sampling variation rather than an independent routing change; A44's own participation barely moved (about 64% to 57% of traces, weak strength), so no alternative path disappeared. [OCC042, DIR042]

Other differences: the broad participation redistribution (A01, A05, A07, A23, A35, A36, A39 down; A03, A11, A12, A13, A24, A37, A38 up) and the branch-share shifts among A31/A35–A38, A11–A13 and A40's successors; these are consistent with flow being re-weighted across existing alternatives, but each rests mainly on single indicators of moderate strength and is not folded into the main hypothesis. [OCC001, OCC005, OCC007, OCC033, OCC034, OCC036, DIR012, DIR013, DIR031, DIR035, DIR036, DIR037, DIR038, DIR040]
