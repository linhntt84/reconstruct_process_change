### Observed differences

- Three activities that did not occur at all in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces each) occur in the later window: A19 in about 29 in 100 traces, A20 in about 41 in 100, and A25 in about 30 in 100, in each case once per trace where it appears. [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025]
- In the later window these three are mutually exclusive alternatives: A19 and A20 never share a trace, and the other pairs occur together in only about 1 in 200 traces; together they cover essentially every trace, whereas earlier no trace contained any of them. [REL001, REL002, REL006]
- The surrounding activities A27, A28 and A29 occur in every trace in both windows, in the fixed order A27 before A28 before A29 (about 98–100 in 100 traces). [POS004, POS005, POS006, REL013, REL015]
- Each new activity always co-occurs with A27, A28 and A29 and always lies between A27 and A29: A27 precedes it and it precedes A29 in virtually every such trace, while its order against A28 is mixed (for A19, before A28 in about 15 in 100 traces and after A28 in about 14 in 100; similar for A20 and A25). [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- Successors changed accordingly: earlier A27 led directly to A28 in about 97 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and A28 led to A29 in about 98 in 100; in the later window A27 leads directly to A19, A20 or A25 in about 52 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (A28 about 43 in 100), and A28 leads directly to one of the three in about 41 in 100 (A29 about 57 in 100). Each new activity is followed by A28 in roughly half of its occurrences and by A29 in most of the rest. [DIR027, DIR028, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025]
- Event distances inside this segment grew: A27 and A28 were 1 step apart in about 98 in 100 traces earlier, and are 2 steps apart in about 52 in 100 later; A28 and A29 were 1 step apart in about 98 in 100 and are 2 steps apart in about 41 in 100; the A27–A29 distance moved from mostly 2 steps (about 96 in 100) to mostly 3 steps (about 92 in 100). [LAG001, LAG003, LAG002]
- When they occur, the new activities sit near the middle of the trace (mean position about 0.52 of the trace length). [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- Beyond this, several long-standing activities show modest participation shifts of roughly 5–11 in 100 (A44 up from about 57 to 68 in 100 traces, A45 up from 58 to 67, A43 down from 63 to 57; A06 and A07 up, A11 and A13 down), and A15 and A16 repeat in about 5 in 100 fewer traces. [OCC042, OCC043, OCC041, OCC006, OCC007, OCC011, OCC013, OCC015, OCC016]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, an exclusive three-way choice between A19, A20 and A25 was inserted into the mandatory A27–A28–A29 segment: earlier none of the three occurred in any of 200 traces, while later exactly one of them executes in nearly every trace (about 29, 41 and 30 in 100 traces respectively), placed either directly after A27 before A28 (about half the traces) or directly after A28 before A29 (about 4 in 10). [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, REL001, REL002, REL006, DIR027, DIR028, LAG001, LAG003]

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: participation rises from zero to 29–41 in 100 traces with essentially maximal empirical strength, which sampling variation in a stable process does not produce; the successor profiles of A27 and A28 now include the new activities; the relational items show both the exclusivity among the three and their unconditional co-occurrence with A27, A28 and A29; and the step distances widen exactly at the two gaps where the new activities sit. [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, DIR027, DIR028, REL003, REL007, REL010, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely new activity from a renamed one, and the evidence file contains no screening expectation, so the number of qualifying items could not be compared with a chance baseline; the judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the items. [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025]

Other differences: several existing activities shifted participation modestly — A44 (about 57 to 68 in 100 traces) and A45 (about 58 to 67) rose while A43 fell (about 63 to 57); A06 (about 15 to 23) and A07 (about 48 to 55) rose while A11 (about 53 to 47) and A13 (about 54 to 47) fell. Routing between existing successors also shifted, e.g. after A42 the A43/A44 split moved from about 53/44 to 43/54, and after A01 the share to A09 fell from about 41 to 22 in 100 while A05 rose from 28 to 38. A15 and A16 now repeat in about 5 in 100 fewer traces. These shifts are small, partly interdependent, and the evidence does not tie them to the inserted choice. [OCC042, OCC043, OCC041, OCC006, OCC007, OCC011, OCC013, DIR042, DIR001, OCC015, OCC016]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a change in case mix: the later window could contain traces of a case type in which this branch always existed, rather than the observed flow having gained a branch. [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, REL001]
