### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. All observed differences are small on their own scales — at most about 7 in 100 traces for participation and about 13 percentage points for routing shares — and of moderate strength, so sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows remains a full explanation for even the most coherent-looking shifts around A03–A05 and A27–A30.
