### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The participation and routing movements are small, largely offset one another across activities, and remain within the range that random re-splits of two 200-trace windows typically produce, while the largest profile turnovers rest on only 1–28 observations per window, so sampling variability cannot be ruled out [DIR002, DIR050, DIR042, OCC018, OCC001].
