### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 took over the role previously played by A03: in the later window A03 is no longer executed, while A01 runs in about half the traces at the position A03 used to occupy, receives flow from the same sources (A09, A11, A02, A05, A07, A10, A14), and passes work on in the same pattern (mainly to A11, then A12, A09 and A10). This is shown directly by the successor changes and the relation profiles, and it is consistent across the occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators; the sizes involved (90 of 200 traces with A03 before versus none after, none with A01 before versus 99 after, with differences larger than essentially all 200 random re-splits) make sampling variation in the finite windows an implausible explanation. Note that these indicators partly describe the same traces, so the many agreeing items are not independent confirmations. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a relabelling of the same activity rather than a genuine substitution of one activity for another. [OCC001, OCC003, POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, DIR008, REL001, REL005]

Other differences: several other activities show modest participation shifts (A05 up from about 64 to 75 in 100 traces, A12 up from 28 to 35, A07 down from 43 to 36, A08 down from 29 to 22), and pairwise event-distance distributions show only small redistributions (for example A05→A12 pairs concentrated at distance four, about 47 to 60 in 100). These are small enough that they could be secondary effects of the substitution or ordinary sampling differences between two 200-trace windows; the evidence cannot distinguish these possibilities.
