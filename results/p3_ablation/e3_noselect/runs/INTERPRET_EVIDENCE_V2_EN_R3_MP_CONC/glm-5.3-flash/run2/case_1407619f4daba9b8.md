### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural re-routing of the middle segment as the main hypothesis, involving A20, A21, A22, A23, A26 and the group A31/A32/A37. In the earlier window, every trace ran A20 → A23 directly, and after A21 the flow split into two alternatives: A21 → A22 → A26 in about 54 in 100 traces, or A21 → A31/A32/A37 (no fixed order) → A26 in the remaining 46. In the later window, these two alternatives appear as two sequential mandatory segments: the group A31/A32/A37 runs in every trace between A20 and A23, and A22 runs in every trace between A21 and A26, i.e. A20 → A31/A32/A37 → A23 → … → A21 → A22 → A26. This is shown directly by the successor profiles of A20 and A21, by relations reversing from mutual exclusivity to universal co-occurrence with reversed order, by the A20→A23 distance moving from one to four steps, and by participation rising from about half of traces to all traces, with the position distributions shifting consistently; the key items rest on 92–200 traces per window and show near-maximal empirical strength. [OCC019, OCC020, OCC021, OCC026, DIR014, DIR015, DIR018, DIR019, DIR024, REL006, REL011, REL014, LAG001, LAG002, POS006]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish one reconfiguration from two coordinated modifications (placing the group after A20 and making A22 mandatory), it shows no fixed order inside the group, and a rare direct A20 → A23 transition persists in the later window (under 1 in 100 of A20's outgoing transitions). [REL020, REL021, REL022, DIR014]
