### Observed differences

- **A05 and A10 moved in opposite directions, mirroring each other.** In the earlier window, A05 ran in every one of the 200 traces (about 4.2 occurrences per trace, repeating in roughly three out of four traces) and sat late in the trace (mean relative position about 0.67), while A10 ran in about 46 in 100 traces (about 1.9 occurrences per trace) and sat early (mean position about 0.32). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A05 and A10 exchanged their places in the flow, each taking over the other's position, predecessors and branch target. In the earlier window A05 was the mandatory, late, repeating hub — present in every trace, reached from A06 always and from about half of the outgoing flow of A11 and A15, and leading on to A08 and A09 — while A10 was an optional, early activity (about 46 in 100 traces) reached from A14 and leading to A16 and A17. In the later window these roles are reversed: A10 is the mandatory, late, repeating hub reached from A06, A11 and A15 and leading to A16 and A09, while A05 is optional and early (about 50 in 100 traces), reached from A14 and leading to A17 and A08. A08 stayed attached to A05 and A16 to A10, so their participation moved with their parents (A08 about 68 → 34 in 100; A16 about 30 → 68 in 100). [OCC004, OCC006, OCC008, OCC012, DIR003, DIR004, DIR007, DIR008, DIR010, DIR011, POS001, POS005, REL002, REL003, REL019, REL025, REL034]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: successor shares were replaced in both directions (A06 switched from A05 to A10 at full share; A14 switched from A10 to A05), the occurrence and position profiles of A05 and A10 swapped almost exactly, and the co-occurrence and step-gap changes line up with the same rewiring; the key items all show differences larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled traces. Limitations: the many relation and lag items largely restate one underlying rewiring rather than confirming it independently, positions and gaps are event positions rather than clock times, and the evidence cannot distinguish a single exchange of the two blocks' places from two independent changes (A05/A08 becoming optional while A10/A16 became mandatory) combined with the same edge rewiring. [DIR004, DIR010, OCC004, OCC008, LAG013, LAG015, LAG024, LAG025]

Other differences: minor shifts were also flagged that are not part of this hypothesis — A14's mean occurrences rose slightly (about 2.05 → 2.15 per trace), A11's mean event position moved slightly later (about 0.48 → 0.49), and several step-gap distributions shifted modestly (for example A15→A06, A11→A09, A06→A06 and A17→A14); these are small and consistent with ripple effects of the rewiring above or with sampling variation. [OCC011, POS006, LAG003, LAG012, LAG017, LAG020, LAG026, LAG027]
