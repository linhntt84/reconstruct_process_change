### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch choice between A27 and the A36-A37-A42 block disappeared and the block was repositioned: in the earlier window a case ran either A27 (about 55 in 100 traces) or the trio A36+A37+A42 together (about 46–47 in 100) after A26, with the trio then exiting towards A31 while A25 ran directly to A28; in the later window essentially every case runs the trio immediately after A25 and before A28, followed by A26, A27 and A31 in sequence. The support is direct and consistent: participation of all four activities rose to essentially every trace; trio–A27 co-occurrence went from about 1–2 in 100 to about 100 in 100 with a stable trio-before-A27 order; the direct-successor profiles changed exactly at the joints of this rewiring (A25, the trio members, A26); and the lag and position distributions shifted in the direction the rewiring predicts (A25-to-A28 from 1 to 4–5 steps, A26-to-A31 fixed at 2 steps). Because the trio's exit target changed from A31 to A28 and its order relative to A25, A26 and A27 flipped, this is relational evidence of a structural and placement change, not merely a shift in how often existing paths are taken. [OCC027, OCC035, OCC036, OCC040, REL002, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL016, REL017, REL018, DIR025, DIR026, DIR036, DIR037, DIR042, LAG001, LAG004, POS006, POS007, POS008]

The evidence cannot show whether the earlier branch reflects one optional choice or a mixture of process variants sampled in different proportions, and POS/LAG are based on event positions, so the repositioning is established in sequence order rather than clock time. [REL016, POS006, LAG001]
