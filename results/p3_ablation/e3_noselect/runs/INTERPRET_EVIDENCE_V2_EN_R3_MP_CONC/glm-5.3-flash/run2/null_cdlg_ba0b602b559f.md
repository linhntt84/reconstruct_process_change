### Observed differences

- The clearest difference is in what follows A06. Earlier, of A06's outgoing transitions, about 53 in 100 went to A07 and about 44 in 100 to A08; in the later window, about 41 in 100 go to A07 and about 53 in 100 to A08, plus a small new transition to A09 (about 4 in 100, roughly 7 transitions). This profile rests on about 190 outgoing transitions in each window, and the difference is larger than in about 97 of 100 random re-splits. [DIR006]
- A07's own outgoing behaviour barely changed: it still led back to A06 in about 99 of 100 transitions earlier and about 96 in 100 later, with only tiny new transitions to A05 and A09 (about 2 and 1 in 100). A07 occurs in about 25 of 100 traces in both windows but repeats less often (mean 0.52 → 0.41 occurrences per trace). [DIR007, OCC007]
- Trace participation shifted by 6–8 percentage points for most activities: A01 and A08 rose from about 45 to about 53 of 100 traces, A05 and A09 from about 46 to about 52; A02 fell from about 55 to about 48, and A03, A12, A04 and A11 from about 55 to about 47. Each of these differences is larger than in roughly 81–89 of 100 random re-splits. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC008, OCC009, OCC011, OCC012]
- A06 itself appears in more traces (about 45 → about 53 of 100) but its mean per trace is flat (0.97 → 0.94), and this difference is not unusual (larger than in only about 20 of 100 re-splits). Repetition also decreased for A03/A12 (mean 1.21 → 0.99) and A10 (0.66 → 0.52). [OCC003, OCC006, OCC007, OCC010, OCC012]
- Smaller edge shifts: A02 leads relatively less often to A03 (about 51 → 42 in 100) and more often to A01/A06, with a new transition to A12; A09 leads less often to A03 (about 48 → 42 in 100) and more often back to A01, A07 and A11; A05 gained a transition to A06 (about 6 in 100). A04's apparent outgoing split between A09 and A05 rests on only 1–2 transitions and is not interpretable. [DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, DIR009]

### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests that in the later window the choice after A06 shifted between its two main successors: the route via A08 is taken more often (about 44 → about 53 of A06's outgoing transitions) and the route via A07 less often (about 53 → about 41), accompanied by a small new A06→A09 transition. The occurrence figures for the same activities point in the matching direction: A08 appears in about 53 of 100 traces versus about 45 earlier, and A07, present in about a quarter of traces in both windows, repeats less often (mean 0.52 → 0.41 per trace). [DIR006, OCC007, OCC008]

Supporting signs: the routing change is measured directly on about 190 outgoing transitions per window and is larger than in about 97 of 100 random re-splits, and the occurrence changes for A07 and A08 are directionally consistent with it. What is missing: the hypothesis rests mainly on this single relational item; the corroborating occurrence shifts are small (6–8 in 100 traces) and partly duplicated (A01/A08, A05/A09, A04/A11 and A03/A12 show identical profiles, so they reflect the same traces rather than independent confirmations), and no screening baseline is provided to compare the number of qualifying items with the number an unchanged process would produce. An important competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is ordinary sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows, especially for the smaller share shifts.
