### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the core sequence is routed differently: the link between the A04/A13 pair and A09 that earlier ran through A05, A16 and A01 (A04 and A13 handed over partly to A05; A05 led to A16, which returned to A05, and to A01, which always led to A09) now runs through A08 and the newly observed A17 (A04 and A13 hand over to A08; A08 leads to A16, which returns to A08, and to A17, which leads to A09), with A09 now handing on to A05, which leads back to A04 and A13. Within the same rerouting, A01 is observed only as an isolated step (once per trace, at a fixed position, with no observed successor, in about 34 in 100 traces), A05 falls from about 21 to about 6 in 100 traces and loses its repeats to A08, whose occurrences per trace roughly tripled. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, DIR007, DIR009, DIR011, DIR012, OCC001, OCC005, OCC008, OCC017, REL007, LAG012, LAG018]

The successor changes are observed directly on sizable samples (A16: 62 vs 53 transitions; A08: 32 vs 104; A09: 74 vs 51; A05: 136 vs 26; A01: 74 vs 0), and the co-occurrence and step-distance items shift in the same direction, although they are computed from the same traces and so corroborate rather than independently confirm one another. The competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that both wirings already existed and only their frequencies shifted between the windows: with 200 traces per window, an absent edge or a zero share only shows a behaviour was not sampled, not that it was impossible earlier.
