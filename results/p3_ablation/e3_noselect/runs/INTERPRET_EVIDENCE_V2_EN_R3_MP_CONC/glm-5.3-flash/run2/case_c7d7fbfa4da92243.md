### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A10 is no longer executed and that the flow previously routed through it now runs directly from A04 to A12. Earlier, the dominant chain was A04 → A10 → A12; in the later window, A10 appears in 0 of 200 traces while A12 immediately follows A04 in 75 in 100 of pairs, A04's successor profile has switched from A10 to A12, and A04 and A12 now occur without A10. Predecessors such as A01, A03, A07 and A11 now lead to A04 or A12 instead of A10 or A02, and with the same rewiring A04 and A12 participate in more traces (about 44 → 58 in 100) while A02 participates in fewer (about 56 → 42 in 100). The key items agree across occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators with empirical strength around 0.99, and the largest of them rest on 50–115 observations per window.
