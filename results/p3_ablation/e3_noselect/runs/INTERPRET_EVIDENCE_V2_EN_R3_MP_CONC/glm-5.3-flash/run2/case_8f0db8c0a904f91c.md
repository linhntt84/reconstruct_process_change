### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the consecutive segment A19 → A25 → A20, executed between A41 and A26, now behaves as optional: in the earlier window it ran once in every trace, while in the later window it is skipped as a whole in about 47 in 100 traces and executed, with its internal order and spacing intact, in about 53 in 100. [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, REL008, REL009, REL014, DIR041, LAG022]

The support is direct rather than only frequency-based: the three activities switch from always co-occurring to co-occurring in about 53 in 100 traces and being jointly absent in about 47 in 100, essentially never appearing singly; the successor profile of A41 changes from almost always A19 to roughly half A19 and half A26; and the A41→A26 distance collapses from 4 steps to 1 step in about 44 in 100 cases. These items show differences larger than in about 99 of 100 random re-splits, and the surrounding activities A41, A26, A46 and A47 keep occurring in every trace with unchanged mutual order, which argues against a sampling artefact. [REL008, REL010, REL012, DIR041, LAG022, OCC037, OCC026, OCC042, OCC043, REL023, REL024, REL025]

Limitations: the evidence shows that the segment is skipped in about half of the later traces but cannot distinguish whether individual cases gained a way to bypass it from whether the case mix shifted toward traces that never contained the segment, and it cannot say why the skipping occurs. No screening baseline is provided, so for the secondary differences below the volume expected by chance cannot be checked; given the two 200-trace windows, sampling variability remains plausible for them. [REL008, DIR041]

Other differences: A02 became much rarer (about 29 to 9 in 100 traces) and, when present, sits earlier in the trace with a changed outgoing profile, though this rests on 17 later traces [OCC002, POS001, DIR002]; routing shares shifted in the A32/A35–A38 region toward A35/A36 over A37/A38 [OCC031, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, DIR035, DIR036] and after A42 toward A44 over A43 [DIR042, DIR043, OCC039]; A23 rose and A24 fell with a matching shift in A22's outgoing transitions [OCC023, OCC024, DIR022]; and the rare activities A04, A06, A08 and A10 appear slightly more often on small counts [OCC004, OCC006, OCC008, OCC010].
