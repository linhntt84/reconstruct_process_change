### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A04 opened about 49 in 100 traces (98 of 200): it was always the first event of the trace and always led directly to A03. In the later window, A04 is not observed at all (0 of 200 traces). [OCC003, DIR003, POS003]
- In the earlier window, A01 was not observed; in the later window, it appears in about 50 in 100 traces (99 of 200), again as the first event of the trace and always leading directly to A03. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001]
- The relation profiles flip consistently with this: earlier, A03 always ran without A01, and A04 ran before A03 in about 49 in 100 traces; later, A01 precedes A03 in about 50 in 100 traces, A04 never occurs, and A01 and A04 never share a trace in either window. [REL001, REL002, REL003]
- A03 itself is stable: it appears in every trace in both windows at essentially the same relative position (median about 0.6 of the trace in both). [POS002]
- Routing after A05 changed: earlier, about 42 in 100 of A05's outgoing transitions went to A07 and about 31 to A12; later, A05→A07 halves to about 21 in 100 and a new succession A05→A13 appears at about 21 in 100. [DIR004]
- Further along, new successions appear: A07 now leads directly to A03 in about 29 in 100 of its transitions (previously unobserved; A07→A13 fell from about 92 to 56 in 100), and A13 now leads to A07 in about 32 in 100 (previously unobserved; A13→A03 fell from about 77 to 47 in 100). A07 and A13 each now appear in about 30 in 100 traces, up from 24. [DIR005, DIR010, OCC004, OCC009]
- Six activities (A02, A09, A10, A12, A14, A15) each appear in about 2–5 in 100 fewer traces in the later window; these are modest differences of a size that random re-splits of a stable process also produce. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC006, OCC008, OCC010, OCC011]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the activity opening one of the process's two main paths was replaced: in the earlier window, about half of all traces began with A04 and continued directly into A03, while in the later window A04 is no longer observed and the same share of traces instead begins with A01, which likewise leads directly into A03; A03's frequency and position are unchanged. The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently by four independent views of the same flip — participation (98 of 200 traces with A04 down to 0, and 0 up to 99 with A01), the single outgoing transition of each activity (A04→A03 earlier, A01→A03 later), the first-position placement of both activities, and three relation profiles that reverse together — with every one of these items ranking at the top of its reference comparison (strength about 0.995). The evidence cannot distinguish a single substitution from two coinciding changes (A04 removed and A01 inserted at the same spot), and with finite windows it cannot fully exclude that A04 still occurs outside the sample, although the complete disappearance of an activity present in 49 in 100 traces makes a sampling explanation implausible. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003, POS001, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL003]

Other differences:
- A routing change among A05, A07 and A13 that is not part of the main hypothesis: the earlier dominant chain A05→A07→A13→A03 lost its exclusivity, with the new successions A05→A13, A13→A07 and A07→A03 appearing alongside it and A07/A13 each appearing in slightly more traces; the evidence provides no link between this and the A01/A04 replacement. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR010, OCC004, OCC009]
- Slight participation decreases for A02, A09, A10, A12, A14 and A15 (about 2–5 in 100 fewer traces each), which a stable process would also produce occasionally; the evidence does not tie them to either hypothesis. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC006, OCC008, OCC010, OCC011]
