### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests that existing alternative paths were taken with different frequencies, centred on the alternative between A04 and A05: in the earlier window about 54 in 100 traces included A04 versus about 47 in 100 for A05, while in the later window this reversed to about 46 in 100 for A04 and about 54 in 100 for A05. Supporting signs are that both activities are well observed in both windows, neither is repeated, their shares exactly complement each other in both windows, and the split at A01 moved in the same direction. [OCC003, OCC004, DIR001]

What is missing: the shift of about 7–8 in 100 traces is also reached or exceeded in roughly 1 in 5 random re-splits of these windows, the corroborating routing evidence at A01 is weak on its own, and no screening baseline is provided to compare the number of qualifying items against the count expected without a change. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is therefore ordinary sampling variability between the two windows; the evidence set also contains no relational items showing how A04 and A05 are reached, so the exact location of the routing change remains uncertain. [OCC003, OCC004, DIR001]
