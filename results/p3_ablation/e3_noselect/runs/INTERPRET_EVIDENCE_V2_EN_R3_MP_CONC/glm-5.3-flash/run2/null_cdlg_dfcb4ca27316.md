### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

Main hypothesis: in the later window, the routing after A06 appears to favour A16 over A15 more than before — the successor split moved from roughly 58/42 to about 50/50 — so A15 is executed less often per trace (about 0.72 → 0.54 occurrences) while A16's volume stayed about constant. The shift is observed directly on successor shares, is the most unusual difference in the evidence (about 92 in 100 reference comparisons smaller, on 250 vs 213 transitions), and A15's occurrence drop matches the A06→A15 drop almost exactly. [DIR005, OCC015]

What is missing: the evidence provides no screening baseline, so the number of qualifying items cannot be checked against what an unchanged process would produce; the two supporting items describe one underlying difference rather than confirming it independently, and with 25 indicators tested a single difference this unusual can still arise without a change. The competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is ordinary sampling variability between the two 200-trace windows. [DIR005]
