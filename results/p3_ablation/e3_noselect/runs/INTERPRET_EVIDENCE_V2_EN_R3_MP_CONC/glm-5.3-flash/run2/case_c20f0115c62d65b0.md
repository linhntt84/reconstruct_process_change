### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every case executed exactly one of three alternative activities: A19 in about 32 of 100 cases, A20 in about 28, and A25 in about 40, and the three never appeared together in one case. In the later window, each of them appears in only about 1 of 200 cases. [OCC017, OCC018, OCC023, REL001, REL002, REL006]
- This block sat between A27 and A29: earlier, A19, A20 and A25 each co-occurred with A27, A28 and A29 in roughly 28–40 of 100 cases (in mixed order around A28); in the later window these co-occurrences drop to about 0.5 in 100, so A27, A28 and A29 occur without any of the three. - Distances shortened by about one position: A27 reached A29 three positions later in about 94 of 100 earlier cases but two positions later in about 97 of 100 later cases; A28 reached A29 immediately in about 45 of 100 earlier cases versus about 98 of 100 later; A27 reached A28 immediately in about 54 versus about 99 of 100. - Separately, A15 and A16 still occur in every case but repeat less: mean occurrences per case fell from about 1.44 to about 1.31 for both, and cases containing a repeat fell from about 32 in 100 to about 24–25 in 100. [OCC013, OCC014]
- Smaller participation shifts: A08 rose from about 6 to about 12 of 100 cases, A02 fell from about 12 to about 7, and A17 fell from about 27 to about 23. [OCC007, OCC002, OCC015]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative block formed by A19, A20 and A25 — exactly one of which was executed in every earlier case between A27 and A29, around A28 — is no longer executed in the later window, so cases proceed A27 → A28 → A29 directly. The participation collapse of all three activities (from 56–80 of 200 cases to 1 each) is direct evidence, and it is consistent across indicator families: their co-occurrence with A27/A28/A29 disappears, A27's and A28's outgoing transitions consolidate onto A28 and A29 respectively, and the A27→A29 and A28→A29 distances shorten by about one position, consistent with the block no longer sitting between them. A limitation is that non-observation means only that: the three activities still appear in one later case each, and their own outgoing profiles in the later window rest on a single observed case, so complete removal cannot be distinguished from execution reduced to a very low rate. [OCC017, OCC018, OCC023, REL003, REL012, DIR027, DIR028, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the branch still exists but is chosen far less often; at this sample size that would look identical to removal. [OCC017, OCC018, OCC023]

Other differences: the reduced repetition of A15 and A16, and the participation shifts of A08, A02 and A17, are not part of this hypothesis and would need separate investigation. [OCC013, OCC014, OCC007, OCC002, OCC015]
