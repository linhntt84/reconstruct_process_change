- A02 was repeated more often per case: it appeared in about 44 in 100 traces in the earlier window and about 46 in 100 in the later window, but its mean occurrences per trace rose from about 0.77 to about 1.0, and the share of traces in which it recurred rose from about 20 in 100 to about 27 in 100. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, more work runs through the A02–A04 portion of the process: A02 recurs in more cases (about 27 in 100 traces versus about 20 before, with mean occurrences up from about 0.77 to 1.0), its continuation shifts toward A04 and away from A05 (about 54 versus 43 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A04, and about 19 versus 28 to A05), and A04 itself is executed and repeated more often (participation about 27 versus 20 in 100 traces). All involved activities and paths are observed in substantial shares in both windows, so this reads as existing paths being taken with different frequencies together with increased repetition of A02 and A04, not as a path being replaced; the three key differences are each larger than about 91–97 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled data and fit one coherent picture. [OCC002, DIR002, OCC004]

Limitations: A04's figures rest on 39 versus 54 traces, the evidence contains no screening baseline so the item-count comparison against chance expectations cannot be made, and A04's outgoing transitions are not observed, so the mechanism (for example, whether a return path to A02 is traversed more often) remains open; sampling variability between the two 200-trace windows cannot be fully excluded, although the reference comparisons make it an unlikely explanation for the A02 and A04 differences. [OCC004]

Other differences: the redistribution of A05's and A09's outgoing transitions (A05→A08 and A09→A05 each gaining about 8 in 100 share at the expense of A05→A09 and A09→A08) is a possible further routing change with only moderate empirical strength (about 70 in 100) and could also reflect sampling variation [DIR003, DIR004]. The small participation movements of the other activities are consistent with an unchanged process at these thresholds. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, DIR001]
