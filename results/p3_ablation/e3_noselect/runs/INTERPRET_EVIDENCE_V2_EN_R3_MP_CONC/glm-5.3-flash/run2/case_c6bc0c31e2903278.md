### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A03 and A10 are executed as mutually exclusive alternatives, whereas in the earlier window they were both mandatory and always executed in the same trace (in either order): co-occurrence fell from all 200 traces to none, each activity alone now covers about 50 in 100 traces, and the direct A03↔A10 transitions disappeared. Occurrence, direction and distance indicators show this consistently, and a swing from "together in every trace" to "together in no trace" across 200-trace windows is far beyond what random re-splits produce. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the windows sample a different mix of case types rather than a modified single process, and the evidence cannot show whether the either-or choice is deterministic or probabilistic. [REL012, OCC003, OCC009, DIR002, DIR008, LAG001, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010]
