### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window executes a more repetitive variant of the process: A03 and A17 are performed and repeated more often per case, their downstream A05 is performed more often, the A07–A15 pair is performed more often and increasingly within the same case (always A07 first or interleaved, never A15 first), A02 itself recurs more within cases, and A04 and A14 are performed less often. The routing shifts at A02 (toward A03, away from A04) and at A07 (toward A15, away from A09) show directly where the additional executions arise, and the unchanged relative positions of A07 and A15 speak against a relocation of activities. [OCC002, OCC016, OCC004, OCC006, OCC014, OCC003, OCC013, OCC001, DIR002, DIR005, REL001, POS001, POS002]

The supporting signs are the agreement of occurrence, routing and relation indicators and the strength of the key items, whose differences exceed roughly 95% to 99.5% of random re-splits of the pooled data. Limitations: no chance-expectation baseline is included, so the number of qualifying items cannot be checked against what an unchanged process would produce; A03 and A17 have identical profiles and A03 leads almost solely to A17, so they describe one behaviour rather than two independent confirmations; and a general lengthening of cases would raise many occurrence means at once, a competing explanation that the specific routing shifts and the A04/A14 decreases only partly rule out.
