### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the flow between the branch point A31 and the convergence point A32 was rerouted: the order of A35 and A36 is reversed — earlier A36 typically led to A35 and A35 on to A32, later A35 typically leads to A36 and A36 on to A32 — and the same reversal appears between A37 and A38 (earlier A38 typically led to A37 and A37 on to A32; later A37 leads to A38 and A38 to A32). Alongside this, the branch through A37/A38 is taken more often (each of A37 and A38 in about 57 of 100 traces, up from about 49) and the branch through A35/A36 less often (about 44 of 100, down from 52). The hypothesis rests on direct successor evidence in both directions of each pair, on the predecessor A31 whose outgoing mix moved in the same direction, and on participation changes that point the same way; the two central successor reversals are larger than in about 99 of 100 random re-splits. The main limitation is that the evidence carries no screening expectation, so it cannot be checked how many such items an unchanged process would produce, and the branch-frequency component (strengths about 78–84 of 100) is moderate enough that sampling variation in the 200-trace windows cannot be excluded. [DIR031, DIR035, DIR036, DIR037, DIR038, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, OCC035]
