### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that existing alternative paths are taken with different frequencies in the later window. The main instance is the choice between the branch A01/A05/A07/A09 and the branch A03/A11/A12/A13, whose shares moved from about 48.5/51.5 to about 55/45.5 per 100 traces, together with shifted successor shares inside both branches (A05→A09 up about 10 points with A05→A07 down about 14; A13→A12 up about 9 with A13→A11 down about 15; A12→A11 up about 11). The same pattern appears after A28, where the sub-path A32/A33 gained about 7 points over A34/A35 with matching edge shifts (A28→A32 up, A28→A35 down, A32→A33 up, A33→A29 up). Every activity involved was observed in roughly 45–55 in 100 traces in both windows and repetition inside the blocks did not change, so this is a redistribution of shares between existing alternatives rather than paths being added, removed or bypassed. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC005, OCC007, OCC009, OCC011, OCC012, OCC013, OCC026, OCC029, OCC030, OCC031, OCC032, DIR005, DIR007, DIR012, DIR013, DIR028, DIR032, DIR033]

The successor-share changes are direct relational evidence with high empirical strength (about 0.81–0.96, i.e., larger differences in only about 4–19 of 100 random re-splits) and stand on roughly 90–200 observed outgoing transitions per profile; the edge-level shifts agree in direction and size with the block-level participation changes. Limitations: the evidence carries no screening baseline against chance, the shifts are moderate (about 6–15 percentage points on 200-trace windows), and items within one branch describe the same underlying behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations. [DIR005, DIR007, DIR012, DIR013, DIR028, DIR032, DIR033, DIR041, OCC001, OCC029]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is sampling variability of the two finite windows: taken one at a time, each shift lies within the range that random re-splits occasionally produce, so without a chance baseline the coordinated pattern cannot be formally separated from an unfavourable pair of samples. [OCC001, DIR005, DIR013]

Other differences: In the cycle around A40–A44, A42 and A43 rose from about 60 to about 65–66 in 100 traces while A41 fell from about 62 to 55, with A41→A40 up about 12 points and A43→A40 down about 6 — a similar share shift, accompanied by slightly more occurrences of A42/A43 per trace (means up about 0.08) on fewer repeating traces. A37 and A38 also repeat slightly more often per trace. The large successor-profile changes for the rare activities A02, A04, A06, A08, A10 and A45 are unreliable.
