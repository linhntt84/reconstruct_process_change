### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-activity group A36/A37/A42 was relocated and made an alternative to A27. In the earlier window every trace contained A25, then the group (in no fixed internal order), then A28, and later A26 and then A27; in the later window the group executes between A26 and A31 in about 47 in 100 traces, A27 executes in that same segment in about 53 in 100, and the two practically never co-occur, so each trace takes one of the two. The signs are direct: the group's order relative to A28 and A26 flips, the A25→A28 gap collapses from mostly four steps to one, the successor profiles of A25, A26 and the group members change accordingly, and A27's co-occurrence with each group member falls from 200 of 200 traces to about 0–2 — a categorical change of the kind random re-splits of an unchanged process do not produce. Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine relocation of the group from a removal at the old position combined with insertion of a new optional branch at the new one (behaviourally equivalent in the log), it does not show whether the roughly 47/53 split between the two options is a fixed routing rule, and the group's internal order remains unfixed in both windows. [OCC026, OCC032, OCC033, OCC037, DIR025, DIR026, LAG002, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021]

Other differences: outside this change, participation shifted moderately (down about 5–9 in 100 traces for A01, A05, A06, A07, A09, A10 and A21; up about 7–9 for A03, A11, A12 and A13) together with successor-share shifts such as A03→A11 rising while A03→A12 falls, A13→A11 falling while A13→A12 and A13→A14 rise, and A07→A09 rising. This could reflect a second, milder change in how often existing paths are taken, but the items behind it are less extreme (empirical strengths roughly 0.7–0.98, no categorical drops), and sampling differences between the two 200-trace windows cannot be ruled out.
