### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A08 was no longer executed in the later window and was dropped from the chain after A07: earlier, A07 led exclusively to A08, and A08 then led on to A05, A03, A04 or A02; in the later window A08 is unobserved and A07 leads directly to those same activities, now at a one-step distance. Occurrence, position, direction, relation and lag indicators all describe this one picture consistently, and the successor mix after A07 closely matches A08's former outgoing mix, which is what a bypass of A08 would produce. [OCC007, POS005, DIR007, DIR008, REL010, LAG013, LAG015]

Limitations: zero participation strictly means A08 was not observed in the later window, not that it cannot occur, and the evidence cannot show why it disappeared. A competing explanation — that A08 became a rarely taken path that the 200 later traces happened to miss — is implausible given it appeared in about 70 in 100 earlier traces, but it cannot be fully excluded. [OCC007]

Other differences: the shortened self-repetition gaps for A03, A04, A05 and A07, and the minor routing-share shifts at A02 and A05, are observed but are not clearly tied to the A08 change by this evidence, and several of them rest on few observed pairs. [LAG001, LAG006, LAG011, LAG016, DIR002, DIR005]
