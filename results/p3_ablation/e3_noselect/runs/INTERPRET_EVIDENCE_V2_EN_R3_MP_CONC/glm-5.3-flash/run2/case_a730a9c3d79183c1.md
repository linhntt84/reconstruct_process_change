### Observed differences

- Before, two three-activity groups were strict alternatives: about 49 in 100 cases ran A33 together with A35 and A36, about 51 in 100 ran A37 together with A38 and A39, and cross-group pairs almost never co-occurred (A33 with A37 in about 2 in 100 cases). In the later window, each of the six activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 runs in about 99 in 100 cases. [OCC029, OCC031, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, OCC035, REL051]

- In the later window the two groups also run in a fixed sequence: A33, A35 or A36 precedes A37, A38 or A39 in about 95–98 in 100 cases (before, these pairs were mutually exclusive), while the order within each group stays mixed, with both directions in similar shares. [REL051, REL057, REL062, REL049, REL067]

- The combined six-activity run now sits between A30 and A31: A30 precedes all six in about 95–98 in 100 cases (before about 47–52, and only the group that ran), all six precede A31 in about 95–98 in 100 (before about 47–52), and the A30→A31 succession within five steps fell from about 199 to 5 of 200 cases. From A30, the outgoing share to A37, A38 and A39 fell from about 15–18% each to about 1% or less, now going almost only to A35, A33 and A36. [REL034, REL037, REL042, REL045, LAG020, DIR030]

- A14 and A15 now accompany the run in nearly every case: A14 precedes each of the six in about 95–98 in 100 cases (before about 48–52, and only when that group ran), and A15 co-occurs with each of the six in about 98–99 in 100 cases (before about 49–52), preceding them in about 56–59 in 100 and interleaving with them in about 41 in 100. [REL015, REL018, REL025, REL028]

- A04 largely dropped out of its old place: it ran in about 59 in 100 cases before versus about 26 in 100 in the later window, sits later in traces (mean position about 0.20 versus 0.38 of the trace), A14→A04 fell from about 57% to 12% of A14's outgoing transitions while A14→A15 rose from about 43% to 79%, and A04→A15 fell from about 90% to 40% of A04's outgoing transitions; the A14→A04 and A04→A15 successions within five steps fell from about 114 and 113 occurrences to about 33 and 27. [OCC004, POS001, DIR014, DIR004, LAG012, LAG003]

- Smaller shifts: the rare activities A02, A06, A08 and A10 each rose from about 6–8 to about 18–23 in 100 cases with reshuffled successors, and A15 and A16 repeat somewhat more often (recurrence support about 0.33–0.34 to about 0.40) while A43–A46 repeat somewhat less. [OCC002, OCC006, OCC008, OCC010, DIR002, DIR010, OCC015, OCC016, OCC039, OCC041]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the two three-activity groups lost their either/or character: A33 with A35 and A36 on the one hand, and A37 with A38 and A39 on the other, which previously alternated as mutually exclusive groups (about half of cases each), are in the later window executed together in almost every case, in the fixed order first group then second group, running between A30, which now always precedes them, and A31, which now always follows them, after the A14/A15 phase. [OCC029, OCC031, OCC033, OCC035, REL051, REL057, REL034, REL042, LAG020]

The change is shown directly by relational evidence rather than inferred from marginals: cross-group pairs moved from near-perfect exclusivity (co-occurrence about 1–2 in 100) to near-universal co-occurrence with a stable order (about 95–98 in 100), the order inside each group stayed mixed, participation of all six activities rose from about 50 to about 99 in 100, and the five-step A30→A31 window closed from about 199 to 5 of 200 cases, consistent with six activities now running between them; successor, position and step-gap indicators agree with the same reading. The evidence cannot separate a routing change within individual cases from a shift in the mix of case types reaching this segment, and it cannot show what the roughly 1–2 in 100 later cases without these activities do instead. [REL051, REL049, OCC029, LAG020, DIR030, POS006, POS009]

Other differences: A04, which previously ran between A14 and A15 (A14→A04 in about 57% of A14's outgoing transitions, A04→A15 in about 90% of A04's), is in the later window executed in only about 26 in 100 cases, appears later in traces and rarely touches either neighbour, so A14 now leads directly to A15 in about 79% of its outgoing transitions; in addition, the rare activities A02, A06, A08 and A10 occur about three times as often as before, and A15/A16 repeat slightly more often while A43–A46 repeat slightly less. [OCC004, DIR014, DIR004, POS001, LAG012, LAG003, OCC002, OCC006, OCC008, OCC010, OCC015, OCC039]
