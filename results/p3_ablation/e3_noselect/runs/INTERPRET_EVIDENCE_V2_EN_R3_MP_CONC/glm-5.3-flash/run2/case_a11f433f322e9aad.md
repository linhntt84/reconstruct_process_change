### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window the process carries an alternative segment between A26 and A28: in roughly half of the executions A27 is skipped and the three activities A32, A33 and A38 run in its place between A26 and A28 in varying order, while the other half still take the original A26 → A27 → A28 sequence; earlier, A27 always ran between A26 and A28 and A32, A33 and A38 were not observed at all. This is a possibility supported by the evidence, not a confirmed modification. The participation changes are large (100% to 51% for A27; 0% to about 49% for each new activity) and each key item is unusual for random re-splits (empirical strength about 0.995, i.e., larger than in about 995 of 1000 reference splits); the relational, directional and lag indicators all show the same structure directly, and the near-perfect mutual exclusivity between A27 and the new activities indicates the two segments are alternatives. Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the choice between the two paths is made per case at random or driven by case characteristics, control-flow evidence alone cannot establish that the trio is newly added rather than simply not exercised earlier, and no screening expectation was provided to check the chance baseline numerically. [OCC027, OCC030, OCC031, OCC036, REL001, REL003, REL006, REL007, REL010, DIR026, DIR032, LAG003]

Other differences: several moderate shifts in routing shares and participation appear elsewhere but rest on single indicators and are within the range sampling can produce: after A23, the share of transitions going to A30 rose from about 43% to about 57%, and after A22 the share going to A23 rose from about 18% to about 31%; participation of A22 rose from about 55% to about 60% while A21 fell from about 45% to about 40%. Rare activities (for example A02, A04, A06 and A08) show reshuffled outgoing transitions, but those profiles rest on only about 2 to 24 observations per window and are weak.
