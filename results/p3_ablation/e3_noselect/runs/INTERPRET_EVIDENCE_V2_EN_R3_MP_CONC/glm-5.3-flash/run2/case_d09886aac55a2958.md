### Observed differences

- Activity A01 is absent from the earlier window — no occurrence in any of the 200 traces — while in the later window it appears in about 101 in 200 traces (roughly half), averages about 1.1 occurrences per trace overall (about two per trace that contains it) and repeats within the same trace in about 25 in 100 traces. [OCC001]
- In the later window A01 fires at varying relative positions, with the middle half of its positions between about 0.20 and 0.53 of the trace (median about 0.32); it had no observed positions in the earlier window. [POS001]
- A01 had no observed outgoing transitions in the earlier window; in the later window it has about 213, going mainly to A05 (about 35 in 100), A03 (about 19), A06 (about 15) and A15 (about 14), with small shares to A16, A10 and A12. At the same time, six activities now send part of their outgoing traffic to A01, which they did not before: A09 about 36 in 100, A08 about 28, A06 about 22, A15 about 16, A12 about 11 and A05 about 7. [DIR001, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR011, DIR013]
- Before, A01 never co-occurred with A03, A05, A06, A08, A09, A12 or A15, which appeared without it in about 33–66 in 100 traces; in the later window each of these pairs co-occurs in about 32–50 in 100 traces, with A01 first in about 4–22 in 100 and interleaved or repeated in about 19–33 in 100. A03, A05, A06, A12 and A15 now never appear in a trace without A01. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007]
- Distances between the remaining activities also shifted: pairs from A08 and A09 into A12 are now most often 5 steps apart (before mostly 3–4), pairs from A08 and A09 into A03 lost their dominant 1-step gap (about half of observed cases before, none now) in favour of 2–5 steps, pairs from A08 and A09 into A05 are now often 4 steps apart (before 1–3), while pairs from A05, A06 and A15 into A03 are now typically 1–2 steps apart (before 2–5). [LAG022, LAG023, LAG025, LAG027, LAG028, LAG030, LAG008, LAG015, LAG039]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A01 has become an executed part of the process sitting between the existing activities: it is reached from A05, A06, A08, A09, A12 and A15 and leads mainly back to A05, A03, A06 and A15, so that A03, A05, A06, A12 and A15 now occur only in traces that also run A01, usually after it, whereas before they ran without any A01. Because A01 both receives from and sends to the same group of activities (A05, A06, A12, A15) and repeats in about half of the traces containing it, it appears to run one or more times within the existing flow rather than at a single fixed point. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007]

Supporting signs: the difference is shown directly by four indicator families — occurrence (0 versus about 101 in 200 traces), A01's new outgoing profile, six predecessors newly routing 7–36 in 100 of their transitions to A01, and the co-occurrence flip in all seven A01 pairs — and the re-spacing of distances between the other activities is consistent with paths now passing through A01. A jump from 0 to about 101 of 200 traces is far outside what sampling variation between two 200-trace windows produces. [DIR001, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR011, DIR013, LAG022, LAG025, LAG027, LAG030]

Limitations and competing explanation: the windows cannot show whether A01 was newly added to the process or had simply not been executed before; and because some pairs moved closer together (from A05, A06 and A15 into A03) while others moved farther apart, an additional reordering of the existing activities beyond A01's appearance cannot be ruled out, nor can a shift in the mix of cases towards traces containing A01. [LAG008, LAG015, LAG039]

Other differences:
- A05, A09 and A12 now co-occur more often than before (together in about 46 in 100 traces versus about 38). [REL016, REL026]
- A03, A05, A06, A09 and A12 sit slightly later within traces (mean relative position up about 0.01–0.03) and A15 slightly earlier. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008]
- A03 now routes more of its outgoing traffic to A02 and A12 and less to A05, A06 and A15; A16's outgoing traffic now goes mostly to A02 (about 43 in 100, before about 34 to A05). [DIR003, DIR014]
- Participation shifted only slightly elsewhere, for example A16 up about 2.5 points and A11 down about 3.5 points. [OCC012, OCC009]
