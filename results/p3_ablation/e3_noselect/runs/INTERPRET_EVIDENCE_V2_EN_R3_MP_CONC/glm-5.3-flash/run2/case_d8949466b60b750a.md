### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A26 and A28 were executed in every trace, A27 in about 52 in 100 traces (103 of 200), and A32, A33 and A38 each in about 48–49 in 100 traces (97–98 of 200). In the later window, A27 is present in about 995 in 1000 traces (199 of 200) with roughly one occurrence per trace, while A32, A33 and A38 each appear in only 1 of 200 traces. - Earlier, A32, A33 and A38 always occurred together as a contiguous block (co-occurrence about 485 in 1000 traces, typically one or two positions apart), reached from A26: about 51 in 100 of A26's outgoing transitions went to A27 and about 48 in 100 went to A32, A33 or A38. In the later window, about 97 in 100 of A26's outgoing transitions go directly to A27. [REL013, REL014, REL015, LAG008, LAG009, LAG012, DIR026]

- The distance from A26 to A28 concentrated: earlier it was 2 event positions in about half of the traces and 4 positions in about 47 in 100 (the route through the three-activity block); in the later window it is 2 positions in about 97 in 100 of traces, with A27 immediately after A26 and A28 immediately after A27. [LAG002, LAG001, LAG006]

- A28's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (about 0.55 to 0.53 of the trace), consistent with the shorter route; A26 and A28 remained mandatory in both windows. [POS003, POS001, OCC025, OCC027]

- Other differences are small: A39's participation fell from about 26 to 20 in 100 traces with fewer repeats, A40 repeats less often at unchanged participation, and A47's outgoing mix changed completely but rests on only 4–5 observed transitions; the remaining activities shift by about 1–4 in 100 traces with low empirical strength. [OCC035, OCC036, DIR047, OCC001, OCC003, OCC021]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the route from A26 to A28 through the block A32–A33–A38 was skipped in almost all later cases, making A27 a near-mandatory step between A26 and A28. Earlier, the two routes were used roughly equally often (about half of traces each); in the later window about 995 in 1000 traces run A26 → A27 → A28, and A32, A33 and A38 are each observed in only about 1 in 200 traces. [OCC026, OCC028, OCC029, OCC034, REL001, REL006, DIR026, LAG002]

The hypothesis is direct and consistent across indicator families: participation (A27 up from 103 to 199 traces; A32/A33/A38 down from 97–98 to 1), relations (A26-before-A27 and A27-before-A28 rising from about 51.5 to 96.5 in 100; the A32/A33/A38 pairs moving from always co-occurring to "neither" in about 995 in 1000 traces), successors (A26's outgoing transitions collapsing onto A27), and lag (the A26–A28 distance concentrating at 2 positions) all show the same change. About 35 items, spread over all five families and mostly at the maximum empirical strength of about 0.995, describe this single underlying difference — far beyond the few items an unchanged process typically produces, even though the evidence states no explicit chance expectation and the items are not independent confirmations. [OCC026, OCC028, REL001, REL006, REL013, DIR026, LAG002, POS003]

Limitations: the evidence shows that the block route was not taken in the later window, not that it was removed — 1 in 200 traces still contains A32/A33/A38, and their after-window successor profiles rest on only 1–2 observations. The evidence also cannot distinguish a routing change at A26 from an upstream change in which cases reach this part of the process. [DIR032, DIR033, DIR038, OCC028, OCC029, OCC034]

Other differences: A39 participates in fewer traces (about 26 to 20 in 100, with fewer repeats), A40 repeats less often at unchanged participation, and A47's outgoing transition mix changed entirely on very few observations; none of these forms part of the main hypothesis. [OCC035, OCC036, DIR047]
