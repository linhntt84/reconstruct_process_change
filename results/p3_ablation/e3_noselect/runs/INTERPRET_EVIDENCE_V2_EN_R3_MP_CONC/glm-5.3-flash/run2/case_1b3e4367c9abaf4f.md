### Observed differences

- A01 was not observed at all in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it occurs exactly once in every trace, near the end of the case (mean relative position about 0.93, i.e., within the final ~7% of events). [OCC001, POS001]
- A01 is spliced into the closing alternation between A15 and A16. In the earlier window A15 always passed to A16 and A16 always back to A15, each one event apart in 100% of observed transitions; in the later window A15 leads to A01 in about half of its transitions and A16 leads to A01 in about half, A01 leads back to A16 (~54%) or A15 (~46%), A05 gained a direct transition to A01 (~17% of its outgoing transitions), and the A15/A16 handover is now one event apart in roughly 63–65% of cases and two events apart otherwise. [DIR001, DIR005, DIR011, DIR012, LAG035, LAG036]
- Two formerly alternative branches became one mandatory sequence. In the earlier window no trace contained both A02 and A06 (they split about 48%/52% as alternatives), and A12 never co-occurred with A02; in the later window A02, A06 and A12 each occur in every trace, A02's outgoing transitions go to A06 in about 90% of cases (earlier they split between A03 ~56% and A07 ~44%), A02 precedes A12 in about 97% of their co-occurrences, and A11's participation doubled from 26% to 53% of traces. [OCC002, OCC006, OCC011, OCC012, DIR002, DIR006, REL012, REL015]
- The merged sequence pushed later work deeper into the case: A03 and A07 now appear later (mean relative position about 0.41–0.42 → about 0.48), and the gap from A02 to A03/A07 grew from 1–2 event positions to mostly 3–5 positions. [POS003, POS006, LAG001, LAG003]
- Repetitions eased slightly: A05's mean occurrences per trace fell from about 2.27 to about 2.02 and A14's from about 1.27 to about 1.02, with participation essentially unchanged. [OCC005, OCC014]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the control flow was restructured in two consistent ways. First, a step A01 — unobserved in the earlier window — now executes exactly once per case near the end, embedded in the closing alternation of A15 and A16, which previously passed only to each other and now hand over to A01 about half the time, with A05 also feeding A01 in a minority of cases. Second, the branches that previously excluded each other — one starting at A02 and one at A06 with A11/A12 — now both run in every case, with A02 routed to A06, so that A02, A06 and A12 became mandatory, A11 runs in about half of the cases, and A03/A07 together with the closing section start later in the case. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC006, OCC011, OCC012, DIR001, DIR002, DIR005, DIR011, DIR012, POS001, POS003, POS006, REL012, REL015, LAG001, LAG035]

Supporting signs: the participation flip for A01 is maximal (0% → 100% of traces, exactly once per trace), and the relational items show the change directly rather than through marginals — A01 goes from never co-occurring to co-occurring in every trace with A02, A03, A05, A06, A07, A12, A15 and A16, and the rerouting at A02 and the alternatives-to-together changes for A02/A06 and A02/A12 are read directly from the transition and relation profiles. Occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators all describe the same underlying difference, with the key items at empirical strength near 0.995 (differences larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits); this agreement reflects one coherent change and is not counted as independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR011, DIR012, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL012, REL015, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely new activity from one that previously occurred too rarely to surface in 200 traces, since zero participation only means A01 was not observed earlier; the exact slot of A01 among the repeated A15/A16 iterations is not pinned down, because A01 and A15/A16 appear in both orders in similar proportions (~48–53% each way); and the relative order of A02 and A06 is not strict (A02 first in about 41% of co-occurrences, interleaved in about 53%), so only joint execution, not a fixed sequence, is supported. [OCC001, REL008, REL009, REL012]

Other differences:
- A05 and A14 repeat slightly less often in the later window (mean occurrences per trace about 2.27 → 2.02 and 1.27 → 1.02, participation roughly unchanged). [OCC005, OCC014]
- A15 and A16 sit marginally earlier within traces (mean relative position about 0.95 → 0.94), consistent with A01 being executed after some of their iterations. [POS009, POS010]
