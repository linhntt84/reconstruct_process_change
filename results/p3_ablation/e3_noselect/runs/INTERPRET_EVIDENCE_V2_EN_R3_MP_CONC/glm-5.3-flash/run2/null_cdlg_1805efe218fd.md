### Observed differences

- The largest difference is in how often a small group of activities repeats within a trace. A09 appeared in about 2 or 3 in 100 traces in the earlier window and about 4 or 5 in 100 in the later window, while its mean occurrences per trace more than tripled (0.07 to 0.23) and its recurrence support doubled (0.02 to 0.04). [OCC009]
- A12 and A13 show the same pattern in the later window: A12's mean occurrences per trace rose fivefold (0.025 to 0.125, participation 2 to 3.5 in 100 traces) and A13's rose from 0.04 to 0.105 (participation 2.5 to 4.5 in 100 traces). [OCC011, OCC012]
- A08 also repeated more within the traces containing it: participation stayed roughly flat (7 vs 6.5 in 100 traces) while mean occurrences rose from 0.12 to 0.185 and its observed outgoing transitions grew from 24 to 37. [OCC008, DIR004]

Among A08's outgoing transitions, the share leading to A09 rose from about 21 to 46 in 100 while the share to A10 fell from 58 to 38; A09's transitions to A12 rose from 38 to 54 in 100; A12 now led only to A09 (previously 80 in 100, with 20 to A11), and A13 now led only to A08 (previously 88 in 100, with 12 to A11). [DIR002, OCC007, OCC010, DIR007]
- A06 was executed somewhat more often (participation 12 vs 17 in 100 traces), and A01's outgoing transitions to A06 rose from 18 to 25 in 100 while those to A03 fell from 7 to 2. [OCC006, DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests that in the later window the repeating cycle through A08, A09, A12 and A13 was executed more often and was more self-contained: A09, A12 and A13 occurred roughly three to five times more often per trace, A08 repeated more within its traces, A08 and A09 routed to A09 and A12 more often, and the exits from A12 and A13 to A11, still observed in about 12 to 20 of 100 of their transitions before, were no longer observed. This is directly measured by both occurrence and routing indicators, points in one consistent direction, and several items are unusual for random re-splits of the data (empirical strengths of roughly 0.83 to 0.96). [OCC008, OCC009, OCC011, OCC012, DIR004, DIR005, DIR008, DIR009]

The hypothesis remains weak because the earlier-window base is very small: A09, A12 and A13 each occurred in only about 4 or 5 of the 200 earlier traces, so the earlier routing shares rest on roughly 5 to 13 observed transitions, and the agreeing items largely describe the same few traces rather than independent confirmations. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is sampling fluctuation on these rare activities (a rare variant surfacing in one window only); no screening baseline is provided to compare the number of qualifying items against chance expectation. [OCC009, OCC011, OCC012, DIR008, DIR009]
