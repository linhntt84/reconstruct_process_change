### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured around the disappearance of A02 and a rerouting of flow through A10. Earlier, about half of the traces passed through A02, which exchanged flow with A13 in both directions and fed A03, whose main successor was A14; A10, then in about 24 in 100 traces, led mainly into A02 and A13. In the later window A02 is not observed at all, A03 and A14 each appear in only about 19 in 100 traces (A14 now leading almost exclusively to A13), and A10 — now in about 46 in 100 traces — leads mainly to A07 and A11, while A09, A15 and A06 lead mainly into A10 and A08 leads into A03. The segment that used to run through A02 and the A03–A14 pair is thus largely replaced by A10-centred execution. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC010, OCC014, DIR002, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR013, DIR014, REL001, REL005, REL007]

The participation changes are direct counts over all 200 traces per window (A02: 97 → 0 traces; A03 and A14: 97 → 37; A10: 48 → 92), the successor redistributions rest on substantial numbers of outgoing transitions in both windows, and the occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag items all describe the same rewiring; the number of strongly qualifying items is far beyond the few that an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC010, OCC014, DIR006, DIR008, DIR009, DIR012, DIR013, DIR014, REL020, REL031, REL036, REL037, REL039]

Limitations: zero participation shows only that A02 was not observed, so the evidence cannot distinguish removal of the step from an optional step that is never taken, nor a single rewiring around A02's loss from several independent routing changes. POS and LAG use event positions, so the apparent position shifts of A03, A14 and A10 accompany the participation and routing changes rather than showing that activities were moved in time; small shifts elsewhere remain within what two 200-trace samples can differ by chance. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC007, OCC009, POS002, POS006, POS009]

Other differences: minor successor redistributions in the stable A01/A04/A12 segment (A01 leads to A12 in about 83 → 91 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) and small position shifts for A07 and A11 are not part of the hypothesis above; the many redistributed lag distributions (for example A10→A09 and A10→A11, whose pairs now occur far more often) read as consequences of the same rerouting rather than separate differences. [DIR001, DIR004, POS003, POS007, LAG028, LAG030]
