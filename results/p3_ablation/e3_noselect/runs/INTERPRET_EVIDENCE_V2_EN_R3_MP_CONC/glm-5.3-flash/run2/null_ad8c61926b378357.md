### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the existing alternative routes after A46 were used with different frequencies: continuations shifted from the route through A48 and A49 (including its loop back over A46) towards the route via A47, which typically proceeds directly to A50 — earlier roughly 54 of 100 continuations from A46 went to A48 and 44 to A47, later roughly 46 and 51. A47 was thereby executed more often (0.77 to 0.91 occurrences per trace; about 61 to 66 of 100 traces) while A48 (0.94 to 0.84) and A49 (0.95 to 0.83) were executed less. The shift is observed directly at the branch point on roughly 340 continuations per window and is larger than about 93 in 100 random re-splits; the occurrence changes match in direction and size (strengths about 0.70–0.94), the outgoing behaviour of A47, A48 and A49 themselves and the endpoint A50 stayed stable, and every involved activity occurs in a substantial share of traces in both windows, which fits a shift between existing paths rather than a structural replacement. Limitations: the shift is moderate (about 7 points), part of the A47 increase could stem from more repeated cycling between A47 and A46 (A47's repeat share rose from about 14–15 to 18–19 of 100 traces) rather than from more traces entering the route, and with many activities and edges tested, a coherent cluster of this size can also arise without a change.
