### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the flow runs through a restructured route built around four previously unobserved activities: A07 typically runs between A08 and A18 (A07 receives about 46% of A08's outgoing transitions, where A05 previously received about 52%, and passes on to A18 in all of its observed outgoing transitions); A10 now continues into A16 and A15 (about 51% and 49%) instead of into A03, A10 itself or A02, with A16 leading on to A03 or A02 and A15 back to A10; and A18 now leads to A17 (about 54%) and A14 instead of only to A02, with A17 leading to A09. A12 now hands to A05 (about 49%) rather than to A18. The former successions A08→A05, A10→A03/A02, A12→A18 and the direct A18→A02 edge are no longer observed among the outgoing transitions of their source activities. [DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011, DIR012]

This hypothesis is supported directly and consistently: the four activities go from zero participation to 33–70 of 100 traces (65–139 supporting traces each), the new edges appear in the direction profiles with 97–206 supporting transitions, pairs that never co-occurred earlier now co-occur in up to about 70 of 100 later traces, and the position and distance shifts match the new wiring (for example, A08→A18 shortening from typically 4 to 2 steps, and A18→A14 becoming adjacent). All indicator families point to the same activities, and the number of strongly qualifying items is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC006, OCC014, OCC015, OCC016, DIR007, DIR010, REL021, REL061, LAG015, LAG038]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that these activities were not observed there, and an unobserved edge does not prove an edge is absent, so small residual rates of the old paths cannot be excluded; several items describe the same rewiring and are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of process variants — a new variant coexisting with residual old-variant traces — rather than a wholesale replacement of the flow. [OCC006, DIR003, DIR012]
