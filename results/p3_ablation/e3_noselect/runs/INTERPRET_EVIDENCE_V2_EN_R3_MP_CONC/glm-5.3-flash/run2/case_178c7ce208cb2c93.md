### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was not observed at all in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it occurs exactly once in every trace (participation 100%, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, no repetitions). [OCC001]
- In the earlier window, about 78 in 100 of A02's outgoing transitions went to A03 and about 22 to A04; in the later window, about 76 in 100 go to A01 and about 24 to A04. A04 mirrors this: about 78 in 100 of its transitions went to A02 and 22 to A03 before, versus about 76 to A02 and 24 to A01 after. [DIR002, DIR003]
- In the later window, A01's only observed outgoing transition is to A03 (100% of cases). [DIR001]
- Before, A01 never shared a trace with A02, A03 or A04; after, it co-occurs with each of them in 100% of traces, always after A02 and A04 and always before A03. [REL001, REL002, REL003]
- The event distance to A03 grew by one step in both entry paths: from A02, gaps of 1–2 events before (about 78% / 22%) became gaps of 2–3 events after (about 76% / 24%); from A04, gaps of 1–2 events (about 22% / 78%) became gaps of 2–3 events (about 24% / 76%). [LAG001, LAG002]
- In the later window, A01 always sits about two-thirds of the way through the trace, with no spread. [POS001]
- The relative order of A02 and A04 is essentially unchanged: A04 came first in about 78 in 100 traces before and about 76 in 100 after. [REL004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added as a new mandatory step inserted directly before A03. In the earlier window, traces ran two variants without A01 — A04, A02, A03 in about 78 in 100 cases and A02, A04, A03 in about 22 — whereas in the later window the same two variants run with A01 placed between the A02/A04 stage and A03: A04, A02, A01, A03 (about 76 in 100) and A02, A04, A01, A03 (about 24). [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, POS001]

The support is direct and consistent across indicators: the direct transitions A02→A03 and A04→A03 disappear exactly as A02→A01, A04→A01 and A01→A03 appear; the order relations place A01 after A02/A04 and before A03 in every trace; and every A02/A04-to-A03 distance increases by exactly one step, which rules out a mere shift in how often existing paths are taken. All key items show differences larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, and the choice between the two variants itself barely moved (about 78/22 before versus 76/24 after). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, REL004]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that A01 was not observed there (0 of 200 traces, which is strong but not proof of absence), and the evidence cannot distinguish the insertion of a genuinely new activity from a relabelling of a step outside the observed fragment. [OCC001]

Other differences: none beyond the above; the slightly earlier positions of A02 (median 0.5 → 0.33) and A04 (mean 0.11 → 0.08) in the later window match traces becoming one event longer and are not treated as separate changes. [POS002, POS003]
