### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a placement change: the trio A35, A36, A41 — three activities that each appear in every trace, in no fixed order among themselves — now executes between A25 and A27, whereas it previously executed immediately after A26. The dominant sequence thus changed from about A25 → A27 → A28 → A29 → A33 → A34 → A26 → (A35/A36/A41) → A30 in the earlier window to about A25 → (A35/A36/A41) → A27 → A28 → A29 → A33 → A34 → A26 → A30 in the later window; A26 now feeds A30 directly, and the trio's exit moved from A30 to A27. [REL008, REL009, REL010, REL012, REL013, REL014, DIR025, DIR026, DIR035, DIR036, DIR041, LAG001, LAG003, LAG010]

The support is direct and consistent: six activity-pair orders flip sides in about 98–100 of 100 traces with empirical strength about 0.995, the successor profiles at the trio's entry and exit exchange targets almost completely, and every activity involved keeps 100 in 100 trace participation in both windows — so activities were neither added nor removed and no path was replaced; only their placement changed. A competing framing the evidence cannot resolve is which block "moved": the trio moving earlier and the chain A27 → A28 → A29 → A33 → A34 → A26 moving later describe the same observations, and POS/LAG use event positions, so no claim about clock-time timing is implied. [REL008, REL012, DIR025, DIR026, POS002, POS005, OCC024, OCC025, OCC031, LAG001]
