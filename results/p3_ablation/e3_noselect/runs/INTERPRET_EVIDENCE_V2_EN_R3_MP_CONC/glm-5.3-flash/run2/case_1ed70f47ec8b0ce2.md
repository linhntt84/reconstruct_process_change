### Observed differences

- The successor structure around two groups of activities was exchanged. Before, exactly one of the alternatives A14, A16 or A19 ran in each trace (participation 30, 35 and 36 in 100 traces; the three never occur together), and each led only to A20; in the later window, each of the three leads only to A25 [DIR010, DIR012, DIR015, REL002, REL005, REL018, OCC013, OCC014, OCC015].
- Conversely, A15, A17 and A18 (all three present in every trace in both windows, in mixed order) previously sent about 30–38 in 100 of their outgoing transitions to A25 and none to A20; in the later window they send about 28–37 in 100 to A20 and none to A25 [DIR011, DIR013, DIR014, REL011, REL012, REL023].
- The two junction activities re-wired accordingly: A24's outgoing moved from A15/A18/A17 (about 37/33/30 in 100) to A19/A16/A14 (about 39/29/33 in 100), and A35's moved from A16/A14/A19 to A17/A15/A18 [DIR017, DIR026].
- Order relations between the two groups flipped completely: A20 preceded A15 in 100 of 100 traces before and follows it in 100 of 100 after; A24 moved from before to after A15/A17/A18; A19 and A16 moved from before to after A20 and A24; A14 moved from before to after A15, A17, A18, A20 and A24 [REL014, REL015, REL025, REL030, REL031, REL019, REL001, REL016].
- Positions moved accordingly: A14, A16 and A19 shifted from about one-third to about two-thirds of the relative trace position, A15, A17 and A18 from about two-thirds to about one-third; A20 (0.38→0.44) and A24 (0.57→0.64) moved slightly later, while A25 and A35 kept their positions [POS001, POS003, POS006, POS002, POS004, POS005, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010].
- Pairwise distances changed in step: A24→A25 shortened from 4 event steps to 2, and A35→A20 lengthened from 2 to 4 [LAG007, LAG008].

Other differences:

- A34 took part in more traces (49 vs 57 in 100) and occurred more often per trace (0.78 vs 0.89, recurrence 17 vs 23 in 100); A11's outgoing shifted toward A34 (30 vs 37 in 100) [OCC021, DIR009].
- After A36, routing shifted toward A37 (48 vs 54 in 100) and away from A38 (52 vs 46 in 100); A37 appeared in more traces (62 vs 67 in 100) while A38 and A39 occurred less often (mean 0.89 vs 0.77; A38 recurrence 19 vs 12 in 100) [DIR027, OCC023, OCC024, OCC025].
- Smaller shifts appeared elsewhere: A07's outgoing moved from A09 (34 vs 21 in 100) toward A06 (31 vs 44 in 100), the A29/A30 pair re-routed about 11 in 100 of their choices, and A12 appeared in fewer traces (25 vs 19 in 100) [DIR007, DIR021, DIR022, OCC011].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two blocks of activities exchanged their places in the flow. Before, exactly one of the alternatives A14, A16 or A19 ran between A35 and A20, while A15, A17 and A18 ran together, in mixed order, between A24 and A25; in the later window A15/A17/A18 sit between A35 and A20 and one of A14/A16/A19 sits between A24 and A25. The blocks kept their internal character (a one-of-three choice versus three always-present activities in mixed order); only their placement changed. This is shown directly by the successor replacements at A35, A24 and the two exit targets, by complete order flips in every cross-block activity pair, by opposite position shifts of about one-third of the trace, and by the A24→A25 and A35→A20 distances changing from 4 to 2 and from 2 to 4 steps. Every involved activity remained frequent in both windows, which fits a relocation of existing blocks rather than activities being added, removed or made optional [DIR010, DIR011, DIR012, DIR013, DIR014, DIR015, DIR017, DIR026, REL014, REL015, REL030, POS001, POS002, LAG007, LAG008].

The evidence cannot distinguish a single coordinated exchange from two separate re-wirings of the edges around A35 and A24, since both would leave identical traces, and it cannot show whether the change fell at one point or gradually across the boundary. The modest shifts in how often each alternative is chosen (for example A16, 35 vs 29 in 100) could also be sampling variation between two finite windows [OCC013, OCC014, OCC015].

Other differences: A34 occurred in more traces and more often per trace, with A11 routing more output to it, and routing after A36 shifted toward A37 and away from A38, with A37 more frequent and A38/A39 less so; smaller shifts at A07, A29/A30 and A12 were also observed. None of these forms part of the block-exchange hypothesis [OCC021, DIR009, DIR027, OCC023, OCC024, DIR007, DIR021, OCC011].
