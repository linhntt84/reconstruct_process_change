### Observed differences

- Activity A02 was not observed in any of the 200 earlier cases; in the later window it appears in all 200 cases, exactly once per case with no repetitions, and always at the final position of the case. [OCC001, POS001]
- A06 previously closed the case: it occurred in all 200 earlier cases at a mean position of about 0.99 of the case length — essentially the last event. In the later window it still occurs in every case, but earlier, at a mean position of about 0.79. [POS002]
- The relationship between the two activities changed accordingly: earlier, A06 appeared without A02 in 100 in 100 cases; in the later window both occur together in every case, with A06 coming before A02 in every case. [REL001]
- A06's outgoing transitions confirm this directly: in the later window all 200 observed outgoing transitions of A06 lead to A02, whereas earlier only 4 outgoing transitions of A06 were observed at all (all leading to A04), consistent with A06 usually being the final step with no successor. [DIR005, POS002]
- Smaller shifts appear elsewhere: from A03, transitions to A06 fell from about 41 in 100 to about 32 in 100, while transitions to A05 rose from about 37 to 41 in 100 and to A04 from about 22 to 27 in 100; A04 also led slightly more often to A06 (about 11 to 16 in 100), and A05 occurred a little more often overall (in about 106 vs 99 of 200 cases). These shifts are modest. [DIR002, DIR003, OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a final step was added after A06: in the later window, A02 runs exactly once at the end of every case, immediately following A06, which in the earlier window was itself the closing activity and now takes place earlier in the case. The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: A02's participation rose from 0 of 200 cases to 200 of 200 with a fixed final position, A06 moved from the last position (mean about 0.99) to about 0.79, A06 precedes A02 in every later case while appearing without A02 in every earlier case, and all of A06's later outgoing transitions lead to A02. Limitations: zero participation earlier only shows A02 was not observed in that window, and A06's earlier successor behaviour rests on only 4 observed transitions; a competing explanation that A02 was merely a rare variant before cannot be fully excluded with finite windows, although 0 versus 200 of 200 cases makes pure sampling variation implausible. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, DIR005, REL001]

Other differences: the minor routing redistributions at A03 and A04 and the slightly higher occurrence of A05 are not part of this hypothesis; they could be sampling variation or knock-on effects of the inserted final step, and the evidence cannot distinguish between these. [DIR002, DIR003, OCC002]
