### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the segment ran as a fixed single pass: A02, A04 and A03 each occurred exactly once in every trace, always in the succession A02 → A04 → A03, with immediate handovers (A02→A04 and A04→A03 always at lag 1, A02→A03 always at lag 2) and fixed event positions (A02 at 0.5 and A04 at 0.75 of the trace, with no variation). [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS001, POS002]
- In the later window, A02 still appears in every trace but occurs about 2.1 times per trace on average instead of once, recurring in roughly half of the traces (about 99 in 200). A04 now appears in only about half of the traces (about 99 in 200, down from all 200) and, when it occurs, it often occurs more than once (about 1.1 occurrences per trace overall, recurrence support about 0.28). [OCC001, OCC002]
- Routing changed directly: A02's outgoing transitions, previously all towards A04, now split between A04 (about 53 in 100) and A03 (about 47 in 100), while A04's outgoing transitions, previously all towards A03, now all lead back to A02. [DIR001, DIR002]
- The pairwise relations follow the same pattern: A02 and A04 co-occur in only about half of the traces (A02 appears alone in the other half) and, when together, are interleaved/repeated rather than in the fixed A02-before-A04 order; A03 and A04 likewise co-occur in only about half of the traces, with A04 before A03 in about 22 in 100 traces (previously all). A02 and A03 still always co-occur, but the strict A02-before-A03 order now holds in only about half of the traces. [REL001, REL002, REL003]
- The tight spacing dissolved accordingly: A02 reaches A03 immediately (lag 1) in about 40 in 100 of the observed pairs within five steps (before, the pair was always at lag 2); the ordered pair A02→A04 within five steps appears in only about 99 of 200 traces (before, all 200) and is mostly no longer immediate; A04→A03 is never immediate any more, now appearing at gaps of two and four events — the signature of routing via A02 (A04 → A02 → A03). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- A02's event position lost its fixed value (spread rising from 0 to about 0.21, median moving from 0.5 to about 0.67), and A04's position shows a similar spread; these are event-index shifts consistent with the added repetitions and longer traces, not evidence of a move in clock time. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change involving A02, A04 and A03: in the later window, A04 appears to have become an optional, repeatable detour — whenever A04 executes it now feeds back into A02, forming a repeatable A02 → A04 → A02 cycle, while A02 can also hand over directly to A03, so that about half of the traces complete the segment without A04 at all. In the earlier window these same activities always ran exactly once in the fixed chain A02 → A04 → A03. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, REL002, REL003, LAG003]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the successor change is observed directly (A04's outgoing profile flipped from entirely A03 to entirely A02; A02's changed from entirely A04 to a roughly even split with A03), participation and repetition move exactly as a re-enterable cycle would predict (A02 mean occurrences 1.0 → about 2.1 with recurrence in about half of the traces; A04 participation 1.0 → about 0.5), and the relation and lag items match the cycle's signature (A02/A04 interleaved-repeated when co-occurring; A04→A03 now at lag 2, i.e. via A02, and never immediate). Every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data, but the eleven items largely describe the same underlying trace behaviour, so they corroborate one another rather than constituting independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR002, OCC001, OCC002, REL002, LAG001, LAG003]

Limitations: no screening baseline (items expected without a change) is included, so the comparison against what an unchanged process produces rests on the empirical-strength values; and with 200-trace windows, a rare pre-existing cycle variant that surfaced only in the later sample cannot be fully excluded, although it would have to be absent from all 200 earlier traces, where A02 occurs exactly once with no variation. The evidence covers only A02, A03 and A04 and their mutual order, so the placement of this cycle within the wider process is not observable. [OCC001, DIR001]
