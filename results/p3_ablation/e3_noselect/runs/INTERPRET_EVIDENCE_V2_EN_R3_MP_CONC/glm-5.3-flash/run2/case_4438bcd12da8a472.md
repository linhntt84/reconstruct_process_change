### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the skip path around the block A14 → A20 → A15 has all but disappeared: in the earlier window about 53 in 100 cases went directly from A36 to A21 while about 47 in 100 executed the block, whereas in the later window about 99.5 in 100 cases execute A36 → A14 → A20 → A15 → A21 and the direct A36 → A21 step remains in only about 0.5 in 100. A14, A20 and A15 thus changed from optional to effectively mandatory, A36's main successor changed from A21 to A14, and A21 is now reached only through the block. The change is shown directly and consistently across participation (about 47 → 99.5 in 100 for each of the three activities, on 94 vs 199 of 200 traces), order relations with A36 and A21 (co-occurrence 0.47 → 0.995 with the order unchanged), A36's successor profile, and the A36 → A21 distance consolidating at 4 steps; the unchanged within-trace positions of the three activities argue against a move or swap. These items describe one underlying behaviour, so they are consistent readings rather than independent confirmations. The evidence cannot distinguish a routing change from a shift in case mix — for example a case variant that always skipped the block leaving the sample — which would look identical, and with 200 traces per window the later participation could be marginally below complete.
