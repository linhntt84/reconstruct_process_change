### Observed differences

- The closing stage of the process became rare. In the earlier window every trace contained a closing activity: A04 in about 50 in 100 traces (essentially always as the final event) or the adjacent pair A06+A10 in about 50 in 100, with A04 and the pair never occurring together and never both absent. In the later window A04 appears in about 20 in 100 traces and A06 and A10 in about 21 in 100 each, and about 67 in 100 traces contain none of them. [OCC003, OCC005, OCC009, POS003, REL012, REL015, REL018]

- A02 no longer routes to the closing activities. Before, A02's outgoing transitions went to A04 (about 25 in 100), A05 (23), A10 (13), A06 (13), A09 (12), A03 (12) and A01 (3); in the later window they go to A05 (46), A09 (21), A03 (21) and A01 (12), with no transitions to A04, A06 or A10. A02 now ends about 45 in 100 of its occurrences (219 outgoing transitions from roughly 400 occurrences, against 381 from 381 before) and sits later in traces (mean relative position about 0.45 to 0.62). [DIR002, OCC001, POS001]

- The mid-chain took over the connection to the closing activities. In the later window A03 leads to A07 (55 in 100 of its transitions), A04 (23), A06 (14) and A10 (8), and A09 leads to A07 (50), A04 (21), A10 (14) and A06 (14); before, A03 led only to A07 and A08 (53/47) and A09 mainly to A08 (62 in 100). The handovers A03→A08 and A09→A08 disappeared. [DIR003, DIR008]

- A08 moved behind the closing activities. Its mean relative position rose from about 0.57 to 0.72; A04, which earlier was the last event in about 99 in 100 of its appearances (only one outgoing transition observed in total), now leads to A08 in all 47 of its observed outgoing transitions, and A06 and A10 lead to A08 in about 44–56 in 100 of theirs; A08 now almost never appears without a closing activity upstream. The former direct successions A02→A08 and A09→A08 now occur only at lags of 3–5 steps. [DIR004, DIR006, DIR009, POS005, REL013, REL016, LAG005, LAG012, LAG027]

- The closing activities themselves occur earlier in the trace than before (A04 mean relative position about 0.99 to 0.60, A06 0.89 to 0.63, A10 0.88 to 0.64): the immediate succession A02→A04 (61 in 100 pairs at lag 1) is gone, and A04, A06 and A10 now follow A03 or A09 within one to two steps. The A06↔A10 adjacency itself persists (lag 1 in 87 in 100 pairs) and the pair still occurs in both orders. [POS003, POS004, POS007, LAG003, LAG010, LAG015, LAG025, LAG029, REL018]

- Looping increased moderately: A05 appears in about 36 in 100 traces (29 before) and A02→A05 doubled as a share of A02's transitions; A07 occurs about 0.56 times per trace (0.38 before). [DIR002, OCC004, OCC006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the tail of the process was rewired and the closing stage made optional. In the earlier window, A02 directly selected a mandatory closing stage — A04 as the final activity, or the adjacent A06+A10 pair — while the chain through A03, A07 and A09 ended in A08. In the later window, A02 no longer connects to A04, A06 or A10; the closing activities are reached from A03 and A09 and hand over to A08, so A08 and the closing block have effectively exchanged places in the sequence; and the closing stage now appears in only about a third of traces, with A02 itself often the final event. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR008, REL012, REL018, POS001, POS005]

Supporting signs: the routing changes are observed directly on the outgoing transitions of A02, A03 and A09 with substantial supports (381/219, 87/103 and 81/107 transitions), and they agree with the co-occurrence and ordering changes (A04 and A06 were strict alternatives covering every trace before and are usually co-absent after), with the position shifts of A02, A04, A06, A08 and A10, and with the lag redistributions; most of these items show differences larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the same data. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR008, REL012, POS001, POS003, POS005, LAG003]

Limitations: A04's outgoing behaviour in the earlier window rests on a single observed transition, so its exact wiring before the change is weakly established; A08's own outgoing transitions are not part of the evidence, so whether A08 now closes the trace or loops back cannot be decided; and several items (notably those about A06 and A10, and the lag items) describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the "new" transitions and the closing-less variant already existed at very low frequency before and simply surfaced in the later sample. [DIR004, LAG018, LAG019, LAG022]

Other differences: the loop via A05 is used more (A02→A05 up from about 23 to 46 in 100 of A02's transitions; A05 in 29 → 36 in 100 traces) and A07 repeats more often (mean 0.38 → 0.56 occurrences per trace), consistent with more looping through the mid-chain; A03 and A09 also occur slightly more often. These are not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR002, OCC002, OCC004, OCC006, OCC008]
