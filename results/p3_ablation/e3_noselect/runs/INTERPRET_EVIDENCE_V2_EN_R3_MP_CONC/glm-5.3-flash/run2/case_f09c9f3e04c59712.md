### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a placement change in which two blocks of activities exchanged their positions in the sequence. In the earlier window each case ran A35 → {A15, A17, A18} → A20 → (middle segment) → A24 → {one of A14, A16, A19} → A25; in the later window the blocks have exchanged places, giving A35 → {one of A14, A16, A19} → A20 → (middle segment) → A24 → {A15, A17, A18} → A25. The change is shown directly by complete successor replacements at A14, A16 and A19 (A25 replaced by A20 in 100% of outgoing transitions) and at A15, A17 and A18 (A20 replaced by A25), by the exchanged target sets of A24 and A35, by complete order reversals against A20 and A24, by opposite position shifts of about a third of the trace, and by gap changes (A35→A20 from 4 to 2 events, A24→A25 from 2 to 4) that match the block sizes; the key items exceed about 199 of 200 random re-split differences. Because every involved activity keeps its participation, this reads as a reordering of existing behaviour rather than activities being added, removed or made optional. [DIR010, DIR011, DIR012, DIR013, DIR014, DIR015, DIR017, DIR026, LAG007, LAG008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL014, REL015, REL025, REL026, REL028, REL029]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish "the blocks moved" from the equivalent reading that the connecting edges at A35, A20, A24 and A25 were rewired around stationary blocks; and the evidence set reports no expected-without-change item count, so the count-based screening comparison cannot be made and the judgement rests on the directness, consistency and reference strengths of the relational items (most at about 0.99). [DIR010, DIR012, DIR015, LAG007, LAG008]

Other differences: the cycle around A36–A39 repeats more often in the later window (a change in repetition frequency, not placement), and within the alternative block the routing shifted toward A14 (about 44 in 100 cases, up from 31) at the expense of A16 and A19; remaining fluctuations elsewhere are small and weak.
