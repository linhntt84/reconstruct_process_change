### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new step, A01, was inserted between A02/A09 and A03/A12. Earlier, A02 and A09 led directly into A03 and A12; in the later window, both lead exclusively to A01, which continues to A03 (about 47 in 100) or A12 (about 53 in 100) and runs about twice per trace in every trace. The rerouting is observed directly in the successor profiles, A01's participation jumps from 0 to 200 in 200 traces, and the one-step growth of the A02/A09→A03/A12 distances, A01's early position, and the unchanged frequencies and mutual order of A03 and A12 all fit A01 sitting between them. Limitations: positions are event ranks rather than clock time, and the placement of the second A01 execution (interleaved with A03/A12 in about 53 in 100 traces) cannot be resolved. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the windows capture different case populations rather than a modified process, although the all-or-nothing appearance of A01 and the complete rerouting of A02 and A09 are unusual for sampling variation alone. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR007, REL001, REL002, REL005, LAG001, LAG004, POS001, OCC002, OCC010, REL012]

Other differences: the A08–A13 segment changed from a linear passage (A08→A13→A03/A12) into a loop in which A13 always leads back to A08 and A08 exits to A01 in about half of its outgoing transitions, with A13 appearing in fewer traces and A08 repeating when present; the small A06/A07 routing shifts noted above also fall outside the main hypothesis. The evidence cannot establish whether these belong to the same modification as the A01 insertion or are independent of it. [DIR006, DIR009, OCC006, OCC011, POS003, LAG013, REL016, DIR004, DIR005]
