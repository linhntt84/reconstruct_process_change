### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that routing through the section following A20 changed. In the later window, more traces passed through the A24–A26 cycle (about 25 in 100 traces versus about 22 in 100) at the expense of the parallel A21–A23 cycle (about 75 in 100 versus about 78–79 in 100); the A24–A26 cycle was entered from A20 mainly via A24 rather than A25, was traversed in the changed order (A26 continuing mainly to A25 instead of back to A24), and exited to A30 mainly from A25 rather than from A24, with A30 and the downstream A32/A33 executed somewhat more often as a consistent consequence. [DIR020, DIR023, DIR024, DIR025, DIR026, OCC019, OCC020, OCC021, OCC022, OCC023, OCC024, OCC028, OCC030, OCC031]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: five direction items on A20, A23, A24, A25 and A26 agree in direction, are individually unusual under random re-splits of the same windows (empirical strengths 0.82–0.98), and stand on adequate observation counts (44–201 transitions per window); the participation and occurrence changes corroborate them in the expected direction. [DIR020, DIR023, DIR024, DIR025, DIR026, OCC019, OCC020, OCC021, OCC022, OCC023, OCC024, OCC028]

Limitations: the participation shifts are small (about 3 in 100 traces) and would be unremarkable on their own; the three cycle activities' profiles may reflect one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, so the evidence cannot show whether the entry shift, the changed traversal order or a different loop length is the primary modification; and no screening baseline was provided to check the flagged items against a chance expectation. [DIR020, DIR024, DIR025, DIR026]

Other differences: a small shift between the two early branches (A01/A05/A07/A09 down from about 56 to 52 in 100 traces; A03/A11/A12/A13 up from about 44–45 to 48–49 in 100) and slightly more repetitions of the A15–A16 pair (about 1.73 versus 1.66 occurrences per trace); both are modest and not part of the main hypothesis. The large-looking rerouting around rarely observed activities (A02, A04, A06, A08, A10, and A35's apparent complete successor change) rests on 1–19 transitions per window and is consistent with sampling noise.
