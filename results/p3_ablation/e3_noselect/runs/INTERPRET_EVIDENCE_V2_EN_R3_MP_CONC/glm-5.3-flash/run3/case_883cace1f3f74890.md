- Three activities that are not observed at all in the earlier window — A19, A20 and A25 — appear in the later window: A19 in about 30 in 100 traces (59 of 200), A20 in about 40 in 100 (82 of 200) and A25 in about 30 in 100 (60 of 200), essentially once per trace where they appear [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new step now runs between A27 and A29: in the later window almost every case executes exactly one of three activities that were absent earlier — A19 (about 30 in 100 cases), A20 (about 40 in 100) or A25 (about 30 in 100) — placed directly after A27 in about 55 in 100 traces and directly after A28 in about 43 in 100, after which the flow rejoins A28 or A29 and continues to A30 exactly as before [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, DIR027, DIR028, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, DIR029].

The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the three activities go from zero to 59, 82 and 60 of 200 traces, differences larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of unchanged data, so rare-variant sampling cannot explain them [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024]; the new activities' successors and the rerouted predecessors are observed directly rather than inferred [DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, DIR027, DIR028]; the A27–A29 spacing grows by exactly one step in about 97 in 100 traces [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]; and the pairwise orderings match the two alternative placements [REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL012, REL004]. Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely added activity from one that existed earlier so rarely that none of the 200 earlier traces contained it, and it cannot show whether the new step occurs in every single case (about 1 in 200 later traces contains none of the three) [REL001, REL002, REL006, OCC018, OCC019, OCC024]. The evidence set also carries no screening baseline, so the number of qualifying items cannot be compared with the number expected without a change, and many items describe the same underlying difference rather than independent confirmations.

Other differences: A43's participation falls while A44 and A45 rise, with A42's outgoing split flipping from majority A43 to majority A44 — a shift in how often existing paths after A42 are taken [OCC039, OCC040, OCC041, DIR041]; around A01, A05, A07 and A09 the shares between existing successors shift, and several small participation changes of about 5–7 in 100 traces remain within the range that sampling variability produces [DIR001, DIR005, DIR009, OCC001, OCC003, OCC011, OCC012, OCC013].
