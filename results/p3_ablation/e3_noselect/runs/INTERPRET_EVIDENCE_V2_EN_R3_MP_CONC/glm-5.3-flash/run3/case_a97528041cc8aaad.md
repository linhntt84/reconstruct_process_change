### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A02 executes as a standard step between A14 and A15, so that A14 → A02 → A15 replaces much of the former direct A14 → A15 transition. The change is shown directly by the edge and order evidence — A14's outgoing share to A15 fell from about 84 to 37 in 100 while its share to A02 rose to about 60, A02's outgoing share to A15 rose to about 87 in 100, and A14 precedes A02 in about 59 in 100 traces, usually at distance one — and is corroborated by A02's participation (about 15 to 65 in 100 traces), its position (about 0.53 to 0.20) and the A14→A15 gap growing from one to about two steps. Limitations: A02 already occurred in about 15 in 100 traces before, so this is an existing activity taking a fixed slot rather than a new activity; the indicators describe one underlying flow difference and are not independent confirmations; and the evidence shows the behavioural difference, not its cause. The main competing explanation — sampling variation between two finite windows of 200 traces each — cannot be ruled out completely, but it fits poorly: A02's participation increased more than fourfold and the key relational shares moved by around 50 points, far beyond what the reference re-splits produce (empirical strength about 0.995 on the key items). No screening summary is provided; judging from the items' own reference comparisons, about 85 of the roughly 260 evidence items exceed the 99th percentile of what random re-splits produce, where only about three or four would be expected if nothing had changed.
