### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added to the process and is now executed exactly once in every case, placed early in the flow: in the earlier window A01 was not observed in any of 200 traces, while in the later window it runs in all 200, typically in the first fifth of the trace. It is wired in directly — A02, A12 and A06 now transition to it, and it leads mainly to A02, A12, A04 and A05 — and it always precedes A04 and A05, while its order relative to A02, A12 and A06 is mixed, so it does not occupy one fixed slot. The widening position gaps (for example, A02→A12 immediate succession down from about 82 to 53 in 100) are consistent with this extra step. These signs come from independent indicator families (occurrence, direction, position, relations) and are each larger than in about 99 of 100 random re-splits. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR006, DIR012, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG004]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly means only that A01 was not observed there, and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows capture a different case mix (for example a case type that always includes A01) rather than a modification of the same flow; the evidence set also reports no chance baseline for the number of qualifying items, so the judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the findings.

Other differences: the activities A03, A07, A08, A09, A10 and A11 — which appear to form a repeating side loop (A08 and A09 feed each other; A03, A07 and A11 lead back to one another) — are executed and repeated less often in the later window (participation down about 4–12 in 100 traces, e.g., A03 from about 52 to 41), while A06 is executed more often (about 48 to 59 in 100) and exits from the loop to A04/A05/A12 increased. This looks like existing alternative paths being taken with different frequencies, but the evidence cannot establish whether this shift is linked to the addition of A01 or is an independent routing change, nor fully rule out a sampling explanation for its size.
