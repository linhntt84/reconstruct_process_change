### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a one-of-three alternative step was inserted between A36 and A21: in the earlier window every observed trace passed directly from A36 to A21, whereas in the later window about 99 of 100 traces run A36 → exactly one of A14, A20 or A15 → A21, the three alternatives are mutually exclusive, and the direct A36→A21 link survives in only about 1 of 100 traces. The hypothesis is shown directly by relational and adjacency evidence — replacement of A36's sole observed successor, the A36-to-A21 event-position gap moving from one to two steps, and each new activity always occurring between A36 and A21 — and it is consistent across the occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators, with A36's and A21's position shifts moving in opposite directions as expected. A limitation is that zero participation in the earlier window only establishes that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed there, so the evidence cannot distinguish genuinely new activities from a previously unobserved, very rare path that became dominant; both windows are 200-trace samples. [OCC013, OCC014, OCC019, DIR010, DIR011, DIR016, DIR027, LAG001, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, POS004, POS005]
