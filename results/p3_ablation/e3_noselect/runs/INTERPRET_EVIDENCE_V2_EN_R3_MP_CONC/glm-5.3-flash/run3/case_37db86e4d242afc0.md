### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A03 has taken over the slot A01 previously held between A02 and A07: in the earlier window every trace executed A02 → A01 → A07 with A01 once per trace at relative position about 0.2, while in the later window A01 is entirely absent and A02 leads directly to A03, which leads directly to A07 at the same relative position. This rests on direct successor evidence (A02's outgoing profile flips from 100% A01 to 100% A03; A01 → A07 disappears while A03 → A07 appears at 100%), complete participation flips (200 of 200 traces to 0, and 0 to 200 of 200), and agreement across the occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators. Uneven sampling of a rare variant cannot explain an activity present in all 200 earlier traces disappearing from all 200 later ones. What the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 and A03 are the same step under different activity codes (a relabelling) rather than a routing change, and it says nothing about activities not covered by the evidence. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, REL001, REL002]

Other differences: A06 appears as a new final step — not observed earlier, now in about 46 in 100 traces, always last and always after A05, so that A05, which previously ended about half of the traces, now always has a successor. A08's participation stayed essentially stable (about 51 vs 54 in 100 traces), so A06 did not displace it. A caveat: A06's zero participation in the earlier window strictly means only that it was not observed in those 200 traces. [OCC003, OCC004, POS004, DIR004, REL011]
