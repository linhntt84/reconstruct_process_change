### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the co-occurring group A35/A36/A41 was relocated: in the earlier window it lay between A25 and A27 (A25 branched into it, and it converged on A27), while A26 fed directly into A30; in the later window A25 connects directly and immediately to A27, and A26 routes through the group, which now converges on A30. All activities involved (A25, A26, A27, A30, A35, A36, A41) occur in every trace in both windows, so this is a rerouting of fixed participants, not an appearance or disappearance. [DIR025, DIR026, DIR035, DIR036, DIR041, OCC022, OCC023, OCC024, OCC027, LAG013, LAG020]

The change is shown directly and consistently: the dominant successors of A25 and A26 swapped, the group's convergence target changed from A27 to A30, the pairwise order between A26/A27 and each group member flipped almost completely, the A25→A27 and A26→A30 distances swapped on about 200 observations each, and previously rare A26→group and group→A30 adjacencies now occur in about 198 of 200 traces; the group's internal mixed ordering persisted, indicating a change of placement rather than of internal ordering. [REL016, REL017, REL018, REL021, REL022, REL023, LAG021, LAG032, POS003, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008, REL031]

Limitations: the evidence file contains no screening baseline, so the number of items expected without a change cannot be compared, and the many agreeing items largely restate one underlying difference (the REL, LAG, POS and DIR items describe the same trace behaviour) rather than independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two rewirings — A25→A27 and A26→group→A30 — are independent changes rather than one relocation of the group; both fit the observed relations. [DIR025, DIR026, LAG013, LAG020]
