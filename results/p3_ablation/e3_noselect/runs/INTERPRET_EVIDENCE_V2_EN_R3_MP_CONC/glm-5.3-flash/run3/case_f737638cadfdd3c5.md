### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, none of the 200 traces contained A19, A20 or A25; in the later window A19 runs in about 37 in 100 traces (74 of 200), A20 in about 29 in 100 (57) and A25 in about 34 in 100 (68). [OCC017, OCC018, OCC023]

- The new activities sit between A41 and A26/A31: A41 precedes the new activity in nearly every trace containing one, the new activity precedes both A26 and A31 in every such trace, and each of the three leads almost exclusively on to A26 (about 96–98 in 100 of its outgoing transitions). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window contains a new step between A41 and A26, performed by exactly one of three alternative activities. Where A41 previously led directly to A26 in about 93 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, it now leads to A19, A25 or A20 (about 34, 31 and 27 in 100 respectively), each of which leads almost exclusively on to A26; the three alternatives never share a trace, keep the same surrounding order (A41 before, A26 and A31 after) in every trace in which they appear, and the A41-to-A26 distance moved from one to two steps. The sign is direct and converging: a successor change at A41, a lag shift, and occurrence counts of 0 in 200 earlier traces against 57–74 in 200 later traces, which sampling of a rare variant cannot plausibly produce. [DIR041, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, LAG002, OCC017, OCC018, OCC023, REL001, REL002, REL007, REL003, REL006, REL012]

The evidence cannot show what decides which of the three alternatives runs in a given trace — an unconditional three-way split and one driven by case characteristics look identical here — and the earlier-window absence strictly means only that A19, A20 and A25 were not observed in that sample; a differing case mix between the two windows also cannot be ruled out. [OCC017, OCC018, OCC023]

Other differences: A35 and A36 became less frequent while A37 and A38 became more frequent, with the preferred order within both pairs reversing and A31's exits shifting accordingly; separately, A21's exits moved from A31 toward A23 and A24, A39 and A40 occurred less often, and A18 more often. These shifts among existing, well-attended paths are not part of the new-step hypothesis above.
