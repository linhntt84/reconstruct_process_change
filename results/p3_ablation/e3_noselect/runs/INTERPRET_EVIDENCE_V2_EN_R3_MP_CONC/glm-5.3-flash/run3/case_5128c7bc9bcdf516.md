### Observed differences

- The clearest difference concerns the group A15, A17, A18, A19, A20 and A21, which sits between A26 and A27. In the earlier window each of these activities had several alternative successors — for example A15 was followed by A18 in about 62 of 100 outgoing transitions but also by A19, A20 and A21 (about 12–14 each), and A18 was followed by A27 in about 53 of 100. In the later window each has essentially one successor: A15→A18, A17→A15, A18→A19, A19→A21, A21→A20 and A20→A27, each taken in about 199–200 of 200 cases, and A26 now always leads to A17 where before A17 and A19 split about 50/50. [DIR012, DIR013, DIR014, DIR015, DIR016, DIR017, DIR018]

- The order between these activities changed from mixed to fixed. Earlier, A15 preceded A19 in only about 26 of 100 traces (A19 came first in about 74), A18 preceded A19 in about 12 of 100, and the pairs A15/A21 and A17/A19 appeared in both orders about equally often; in the later window A15, A17 and A18 each precede A19, A20 and A21 in (almost) every trace, while the activities still occur together in every trace. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]

Earlier, gaps spread over 1–5 positions (A15 to A18 at gap 1 in about 62 of 100 cases, gap 2 in about 25); in the later window adjacent pairs sit at gap 1 in about 199–200 of 200 cases (A26→A17, A17→A15, A15→A18, A19→A21, A21→A20, A20→A27) and non-adjacent pairs at fixed larger gaps (A15→A19 at 2, A15→A21 at 3, A15→A20 at 4, A15→A27 at 5). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the activities A15, A17, A18, A19, A20 and A21 are executed in one fixed sequence, entered from A26 and leading to A27 (A26 → A17 → A15 → A18 → A19 → A21 → A20 → A27), whereas in the earlier window their relative order varied from trace to trace and each had several alternative successors, including transitions back to earlier activities of the group (for example A20 → A18 in about 22 of 100 cases, essentially unobserved later). Because several previously observed transitions disappeared almost entirely while every involved activity still occurs in essentially all traces, this is a change in ordering constraints — from mixed to fixed order — rather than a shift in how often existing paths are taken. [DIR012, DIR014, DIR016, REL003, REL004, REL007, LAG016]

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently by complementary views that agree: successor profiles became near-deterministic, pairwise orders moved from mixed or reversed to one direction in almost all traces, and pairwise distances collapsed to single values that all fit the same sequence (A15 to A19 two steps apart, A15 to A27 five steps apart); every routing, order and position item in this cluster shows a difference larger than in virtually all random re-splits (empirical strength 0.995). The position shifts also match the sequence, and the unchanged positions of A26 and A27 argue against the shifts being a by-product of traces changing length elsewhere. Limitations: a few distance items on transitions that vanished (for example A19 → A15) rest on a single observation in the later window and were not relied on, and the evidence cannot show whether the earlier flexible order reflected a per-case choice among several fixed sequences or unconstrained execution order. [DIR012, DIR013, DIR014, DIR015, DIR016, DIR017, REL001, REL007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG005, POS001, POS003, POS004, POS007, POS008]
