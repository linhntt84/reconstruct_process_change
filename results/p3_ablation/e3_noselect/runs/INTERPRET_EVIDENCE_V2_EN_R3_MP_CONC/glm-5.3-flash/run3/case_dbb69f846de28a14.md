### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the control flow around A25–A31 was restructured: the previously mandatory group A36, A37, A42, which ran between A25 and A28 in every case, now runs together, in varying internal order, in only about 47 of 100 cases and in a new position after A26. Each later case now executes exactly one of two mutually exclusive branches between A26 and A31 — A27 alone (about 53 in 100 cases) or the three activities A36, A37 and A42 together (about 47 in 100) — whereas before, every case executed both (the group early, A27 late). A25 now skips the group in about half of the cases by leading directly to A28, while A27 keeps its former position (after A25, A28 and A26, before A31) and only its frequency dropped. [OCC026, OCC032, OCC033, OCC037, REL011, REL016, DIR025, DIR026, REL014, REL015]

This hypothesis is supported directly and consistently: the four participation drops are large (100% to about 47–53%) and highly unusual for random re-splits of these windows (strength 0.995 on each); the near-zero co-occurrence of A27 with each group member, the order flips against A26 and A28, the rewired successors at A25 and A26, the position shifts and the step-distance changes all describe the same restructuring, and the key lag comparisons rest on about 200 observations per window. The occurrence, relation, direction, position and lag items reflect one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [OCC032, REL016, REL019, DIR025, POS006, LAG002, LAG007]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a single process with a new exclusive decision point from a later window that mixes two coexisting case variants, and it cannot indicate what produced the restructuring; the internal order of the A36/A37/A42 branch remains mixed, so no fixed sequence within it can be inferred. Other differences: the participation shifts of roughly 0.05–0.09 among A01–A13, A20/A21, A48 and A49, and the new recurrence of A26, are not part of this hypothesis and lack relational evidence that would characterise them. [REL016, REL025, OCC001, OCC011, OCC043, OCC044]
