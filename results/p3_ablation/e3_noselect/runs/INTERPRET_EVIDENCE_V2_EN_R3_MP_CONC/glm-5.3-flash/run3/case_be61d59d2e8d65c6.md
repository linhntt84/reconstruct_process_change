### Observed differences

- The largest difference concerns three activities, A19, A20 and A25. In the earlier window they were near-universal, mutually exclusive alternatives: A19 ran in about 33 of 100 traces, A20 in about 29 and A25 in about 40, pairwise co-occurrence was about 0.5 in 100, and virtually every trace contained exactly one of the three. In the later window each appears in only about 1 of 200 traces, and about 99 of 100 traces contain none of them [OCC018, OCC019, OCC023, REL001, REL002, REL006].

- These three activities were inserted inside the A27–A29 segment: each always followed A27 and preceded A29, sitting either before or after A28. In the later window these co-occurrences have essentially vanished (about 0.5 in 100 traces) [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012].

- Routing shows the same shift. Before, about 44 of 100 of A27's observed outgoing transitions went to one of A19/A25/A20 and about 52 went directly to A28; in the later window about 97 of 100 go directly to A28. A28's transitions to the three alternatives (about 52 of 100 before) are gone, and about 97 of 100 now go directly to A29 [DIR027, DIR028].

- Step distances contracted accordingly: A27→A28 is now 1 step apart in about 98 of 100 cases (before about 52), A28→A29 is 1 step apart in about 96 (before about 43), and A27→A29 is typically 2 steps (about 96) instead of 3 (about 90). A29's mean event position also moved slightly earlier, consistent with one fewer activity preceding it [LAG001, LAG002, LAG004, POS006].

- A15 and A16 still occur in every trace but repeat less often: mean occurrences per trace fell from about 1.43 to about 1.31, and the share of traces with a repeat from about 31 to about 24 in 100 [OCC014, OCC015].

- A10 became more frequent and rerouted: present in about 16 of 100 traces before versus about 21 in the later window, with more repeats (mean about 0.18 to 0.27) and a substantially changed outgoing mix [OCC010, DIR010].

- Participation rose modestly for A06, A23, A37, A39 and A40, each up roughly 3–5 in 100 traces [OCC006, OCC021, OCC034, OCC036, OCC037].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-way exclusive choice between A19, A20 and A25 — previously executed exactly once in virtually every trace, inserted between A27 and A29 (before or after A28) — is skipped in the later window: the three activities are no longer observed except in single traces, and the segment now runs as the tight consecutive sequence A27 → A28 → A29 [OCC018, OCC019, OCC023, REL001, REL002, REL006, DIR027, DIR028, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004].

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the participation collapse is large (65, 57 and 81 of 200 traces down to 1 each) and recurs across occurrence, relational, routing and distance indicators — co-occurrence with A27/A28/A29 gone, A27 and A28 now leading almost exclusively to A28 and A29, and step distances collapsed to 1 [OCC018, OCC019, OCC023, REL003, REL005, REL010, REL012, DIR027, DIR028, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004]. A drop of this size (for example 65 to 1 out of 200) is not plausibly a sampling fluctuation between two 200-trace windows.

Limitations: zero participation only means the activities were not observed, so the evidence cannot distinguish the activities genuinely no longer being executed from their events no longer being recorded. It also cannot show whether skipping is now complete: each alternative was still seen once in 200 traces, apparently on a residual path with different successors, but those successor profiles rest on a single trace each and are weak [DIR019, DIR020, DIR025].

Other differences: A15 and A16 repeat less often although they remain in every trace [OCC014, OCC015]; A10 occurs in more traces, more often per trace, and with a changed outgoing mix [OCC010, DIR010]; participation rose modestly for A06, A23, A37, A39 and A40 [OCC006, OCC021, OCC034, OCC036, OCC037]. These are not part of the main hypothesis.
