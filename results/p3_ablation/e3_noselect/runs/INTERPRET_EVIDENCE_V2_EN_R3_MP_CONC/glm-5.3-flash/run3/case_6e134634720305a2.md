### Observed differences

- The starkest difference concerns the three activities A30, A31 and A36. In the earlier window each appeared in every trace, about 3.5 times per trace on average, with repeats in about 75 in 100 traces (roughly 690 executions of each across the 200 traces). In the later window each still appears in every trace but only about once (about 210 executions), with repeats in only about 1 in 100 traces. [OCC028, OCC029, OCC034]

- The routing at A36 shows what changed: earlier, about 70 in 100 of A36's outgoing transitions returned to A30 (about 28 in 100 exited to A25), whereas later only about 4 in 100 return to A30 and about 92 in 100 exit to A25. The forward chain is stable in both windows: A30 led to A31 in about 99 in 100 of its transitions before and 97 in 100 after, and A31 led to A36 in about 98 in 100 in both windows. [DIR030, DIR031, DIR036]

- The relationship between A30 and A36 changed from repeated/interleaved to a fixed sequence. The two co-occur in every trace in both windows, but earlier they were interleaved or repeated in 76 in 100 traces (A30 before A36 in 25 in 100), while in the later window A30 precedes A36 in 98 in 100 traces and interleaving drops to 2 in 100; A36 now follows A30 exactly two positions later in about 89 in 100 traces (about 12 in 100 earlier). The pairs A30→A30, A36→A30 and A36→A36, each previously seen in about 150 of 200 traces, appear in only 1–2 later traces. [REL001, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

- The event positions of A30 and A36 became much less spread out (standard deviation about 0.12 to about 0.07 for both; A30's upper-quartile position fell from 0.52 to 0.45), consistent with a single execution at a stable spot instead of several passes. [POS001, POS002]

- Outside this cycle the differences are small: participation moved by a few percentage points in both directions across many activities, A40 repeats somewhat less (mean about 1.8 to 1.6 occurrences per trace), and A43's exits shifted from A40 towards A44 (44% to 32% versus 54% to 66%). [OCC004, OCC006, OCC008, OCC015, OCC016, OCC017, OCC018, OCC021, OCC022, OCC030, OCC031, OCC035, OCC036, OCC038, DIR043]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the repetition cycle through A30, A31 and A36 stopped running multiple times: in the earlier window the block A30 → A31 → A36 returned to A30 in about 70 in 100 of A36's executions and therefore repeated roughly 3.5 times per trace, while in the later window A36 exits to A25 after a single pass (about 92 in 100 of its transitions) and the return to A30 became rare (about 4 in 100), leaving each of the three activities executed once, in the fixed order A30 then A36 two positions apart. Supporting signs are the collapse in repetitions (about 3.5 to about 1 occurrence per trace, repeats in 75 down to about 1 in 100 traces, with participation unchanged at every trace), the successor shift at A36, the change from mostly interleaved/repeated to a fixed A30-before-A36 order, the concentration of the A30→A36 gap at two positions, and the narrowing position spread of both activities — all of these rank at the top of their reference comparisons (strength about 0.99). Because the forward chain A30 → A31 → A36 is intact in both windows, a swap or relocation of these activities is ruled out; the difference lies in the return transition and the resulting number of repetitions. [OCC028, OCC029, OCC034, DIR030, DIR031, DIR036, REL001, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, POS002]

The evidence cannot show whether the return edge from A36 to A30 was removed or merely rarely taken: it is still observed in about 4 in 100 of A36's later transitions (the A36→A30 pair appears in 1 of 200 later traces), so the residual loop rate in the later window remains uncertain. [DIR036, LAG003]

Other differences: Participation shifted slightly in both directions outside the cycle — down for A04, A06, A08, A20, A23, A37 and A38 (for example A38 from 52 to 44 in 100 traces) and up for A17, A18, A19, A24, A32 and A33 (for example A24 from 52 to 59 in 100) — and A40 became less repeated (mean about 1.8 to 1.6 per trace); A43's exits moved from A40 towards A44 (44% to 32% versus 54% to 66%). These shifts are not explained by the cycle change and may reflect sampling variation or a separate difference. A45's outgoing-transition profile changed sharply in relative terms, but with only 18 versus 6 observed transitions it carries little weight.
