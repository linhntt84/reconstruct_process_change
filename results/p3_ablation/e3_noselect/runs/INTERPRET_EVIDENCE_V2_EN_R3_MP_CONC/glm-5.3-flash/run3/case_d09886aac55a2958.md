### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was absent from the earlier window: none of the 200 earlier traces contain it. In the later window it runs in about 101 of 200 traces (about 50 in 100), roughly once per trace on average, typically in the first third to half of the trace (median relative position about 0.32). [OCC001, POS001]
- In the later window, A01 is connected on both sides. Its observed outgoing transitions go mainly to A05 (about 35%), A03 (about 19%), A06 (about 15%) and A15 (about 14%); in the earlier window it had no observed outgoing transitions. A01 has also become a frequent successor of A09 (about 36% of A09's observed outgoing transitions, previously 0%), A08 (about 28%, previously 0%), A06 (about 22%), A15 (about 16%) and A12 (about 11%). [DIR001, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR011, DIR013]
- Pairs of A01 with A03, A05, A06, A08, A09, A12 and A15 changed from never sharing a trace to co-occurring in roughly 30–50 in 100 traces (with A05 and A12 about 50, with A09 about 46, with A08 about 40). In these co-occurrences A01 usually comes first (A01 before A05 in about 20 in 100 traces versus about 6 the other way; A01 before A12 about 22 versus 4), whereas A08 and A09 more often precede A01 (about 9 versus 4). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007]

Several activities also sit slightly later within traces (A03, A05, A06, A09, A12) and A15 slightly earlier. [DIR003, DIR014, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, activity A01 became an active, frequently used step in the middle of the process, sitting between the A08/A09 stage and the A03/A05/A06/A12/A15 activities. A01 ran in none of the 200 earlier traces and in about half of the later ones; A08 and A09 now send about 28% and 36% of their observed outgoing transitions to A01 and tend to precede it, while A01 in turn usually precedes A05, A12 and A03 when they share a trace; and successions that were immediate before (A08 or A09 directly followed by A03, A05 or A12) are now typically separated by several steps. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR007, DIR008, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG022, LAG027]

The signs are direct and consistent: the participation change is measured on A01 itself (0 to about 50 in 100 traces, a difference larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits), the new edges are observed in both directions, seven activity-pair relations agree on the new co-occurrence and ordering, and the lost adjacencies match the same reading; items from all five indicator families point to it, although they largely describe one underlying difference rather than independent changes. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, REL002, LAG022]

What the evidence cannot rule out: A01 may be an existing activity that was simply never executed in the earlier window (a dormant path becoming used) rather than a newly added one, and no cause can be read from these windows. Its placement is also not fixed — A01 is interleaved or repeated with A08, A09 or A06 in about a quarter to a third of the traces where they co-occur, and it both precedes and follows some of them — so the supported reading is a new step woven into the mid-process, not an insertion at one exact position. [REL003, REL004, REL005]

Other differences: the rerouting at A03 and A16 is not explained by A01's arrival and may be an independent shift in path shares; the small position shifts (A03, A05, A06, A09, A12 slightly later, A15 slightly earlier) are consistent with the extra mid-trace step. [DIR003, DIR014, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008]
