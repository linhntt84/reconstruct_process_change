### Observed differences

- A02 became the mandatory opening activity: before, it appeared in about 49 in 100 traces (about twice each time it appeared, median position roughly halfway through the trace); in the later window it is present in all 200 traces, averages about 1.8 occurrences per trace, and sits at the very start (median position 0). [OCC002, POS002]
- A02's routing changed: before, its outgoing transitions always went to A01; in the later window they go to A04 in about 57 in 100 transitions and to A03 in about 43, typically immediately (one step apart). [DIR001, LAG010, LAG011]
- A08 became optional and moved inward: before, every trace contained it (about twice per trace) near the start (median position about 0.10), leading immediately to A03 or A04 in about equal shares; in the later window it appears in about 45 in 100 traces (about 0.8 occurrences per trace), sits around the middle (median position about 0.50), and all of its transitions now go to A02. [OCC006, POS007, DIR004, LAG037, LAG038, LAG039]
- A03 kept a similar presence (about 49 versus 45 in 100 traces) but was rewired: before it always followed A08 immediately and always led to A06; in the later window it typically follows A02 immediately and leads to A01 (about 52 in 100 transitions) or A06 (about 48). [OCC003, DIR002, LAG010, LAG015, LAG038]
- A01 became rarer and changed role: participation fell from about 49 to 31 in 100 traces and average occurrences from 1.0 to 0.4, with its median position moving earlier (about 0.57 to 0.33); before it followed A02 immediately and reached A04 two steps later, whereas in the later window it follows A03 immediately in about half of its occurrences and then leads back to A02 two steps later (about 77 in 100) or on to A08 immediately. [OCC001, POS001, LAG008, LAG015, LAG002, LAG007]
- A06 and A07 are executed far less often: A06 fell from about 49 to 29 in 100 traces with its repetition largely stopping (about 1.95 to 0.70 occurrences per trace), and its outgoing changed from A02/A07 (about 51/49) to A08/A07 (about 53/47); A07 fell from about 31 to 15 in 100 traces. [OCC004, OCC005, DIR003]
- A04 itself is stable — present in every trace in both windows with an almost unchanged position — but it is now reached immediately from A02 (about 62 in 100 of the A02→A04 pairs) or one step later via A08, whereas before it followed A08 immediately or came three steps after A02 by way of A01. [POS004, LAG011, LAG039, LAG004]
- The relational structure moved accordingly: A02 and A04, which co-occurred in about 49 in 100 traces before (A04 alone in about 52), now always co-occur; A08, which before appeared without A02 in about 52 in 100 traces, now appears only alongside A02; and A06, which previously always came together with A02, now occurs without it in about 71 in 100 traces. [REL008, REL011, REL009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the mandatory entry stage of the process moved from A08 to A02, with the surrounding branch structure rewired. In the earlier window, every trace passed through A08 near the start, which either exited directly to A04 or entered the chain A03 → A06 → (A02 or A07), with A02 leading to A01; in the later window, A02 opens every trace and branches directly to A04 or to A03, A03 leads to A01 or A06, A01 leads back to A02 or on to A08, and A06 leads to A08 — which returns to A02 — or to A07. As part of this, A01, A06 and A07 appear in far fewer traces (about 31, 29 and 15 in 100, versus about 49, 49 and 31 before), A08 became an optional mid-process activity, and A04 — whose frequency and position are essentially unchanged — is now typically reached immediately after A02. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS002, POS004, POS007, LAG011, LAG015, LAG037]

The hypothesis rests on direct rather than aggregated evidence: the successor profiles of A02, A03, A06 and A08 all changed (DIR), the position shift of A02 (median about 0.51 to 0) mirrors that of A08 (about 0.10 to 0.50), and the occurrence, relation and lag indicators agree with one another — for example, repeats of A02, A03, A01 and A08 now recur at four-step gaps, consistent with a cycle that closes at A02 instead of at A08. Agreement across all five control-flow indicator families is not a pattern that sampling noise in two 200-trace windows would typically produce. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS002, POS007, LAG001, LAG009, LAG015, LAG017, LAG037, LAG042]

What the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows sample different mixes of coexisting process variants rather than a single rewired behaviour; the aggregate indicators cannot separate these. The wiring of the rare A07 branch also rests on few observations (about 30 traces in the later window). All position and lag statements concern event positions, not clock time. [OCC005, LAG029]

Other differences: A03's participation dipped slightly beyond its rewiring (about 49 to 45 in 100 traces), and within the traces containing them, A06 and A07 now sit slightly later (median position about 0.40 to 0.44 in both cases). [OCC003, POS005, POS006]
