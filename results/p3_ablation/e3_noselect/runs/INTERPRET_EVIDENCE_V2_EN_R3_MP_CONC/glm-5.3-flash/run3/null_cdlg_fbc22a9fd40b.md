### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests that in the later window the repeating cycle through A01, A08, A09, A10, A11, A12 and A13, which returns to A01 via A05 or A06 and A03, is executed fewer times per case — about 3.9 passes per trace before versus about 3.5 after, with A08 (about 7.8 to 6.9, roughly twice per pass) and A11 (about 4.0 to 3.4) falling in proportion — while every activity keeps essentially the same participation, so cases appear to repeat the existing cycle less often rather than to skip or add activities. The supporting signs are the uniform downward movement of all ten occurrence indicators, flat participation, and transition volumes shrinking in the same proportion. What is missing: the five identical occurrence profiles represent one correlated signal rather than ten independent confirmations, the largest occurrence differences are exceeded by about 16 to 22 in 100 random re-splits, and no screening expectation is provided for how many qualifying items an unchanged process would produce, so a sampling difference between the two windows remains a competing explanation. [OCC001, OCC005, OCC008, DIR001, DIR007]
