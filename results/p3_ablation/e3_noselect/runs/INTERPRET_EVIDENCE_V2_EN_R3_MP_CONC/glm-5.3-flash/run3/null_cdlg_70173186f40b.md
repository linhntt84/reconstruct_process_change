### Observed differences

- Routing after A02 shifted. In the earlier window, about 43 of 100 observed outgoing transitions from A02 went to A03, about 31 to A04 and about 20 to A07; in the later window, about 50 go to A03, about 21 to A04 and about 22 to A07, while the small share returning to A01 (about 6 in 100) is unchanged. A01 itself still leads to A02 in about 85–90 of 100 transitions in both windows. [DIR002, DIR001]

- The route onward from A03 is used more. A03 and A17 rose from about 1.5 to about 2.3 occurrences per trace and from about 47 to 54 participating traces in 100, and A03 still leads to A17 in essentially every observed transition in both windows. A17 in turn leads to A05 in about 50 of 100 transitions versus about 43 before, and A05 rose from about 0.7 to about 1.2 occurrences per trace (about 41 versus 29 participating traces in 100). [OCC002, OCC016, DIR003, DIR009, OCC004]

- The route via A04 is used less. A04 fell from about 1.3 to 1.1 occurrences per trace and from about 61 to 48 participating traces in 100; its successors A13 and A14 also declined (A14 from about 61 to 48 in 100), while A04's own split between A13 and A14 stayed roughly half-and-half in both windows. [OCC003, OCC012, OCC013, DIR004]

- After A07, continuations shift towards A15. About 30 of 100 of A07's outgoing transitions went to A15 in the later window versus about 20 earlier, while the share to A09 fell from about 30 to 20 and the share to A08 stayed near one half. A07 and A15 now occur together in about 38 of 100 traces versus about 22, always with A07 first or interleaved and never A15 first in either window; their typical gap of one step and A15's relative position in the trace (about 0.79) are essentially unchanged, so the same path is simply taken more often. [DIR005, REL001, LAG001, POS002]

- Repetition increased for several activities. A02 appears in every trace in both windows, but its mean occurrences rose from about 1.9 to 2.2 and its recurrence share from about 47% to 54%; A05, A07, A10, A11, A12 and A15 also gained occurrences and participants, while only A04, A13 and A14 lost ground. Positions within traces barely moved for the flagged activities, so these are frequency differences rather than placement differences. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, existing alternative paths are taken with different frequencies and the favoured parts of the process run more often. After A02, continuations shift towards A03 — whose near-fixed successor A17 more often leads to A05 — and away from A04, so A03, A17 and A05 occur in more traces and more often per trace while A04, A13 and A14 decline; after A07, continuations shift towards A15 and away from A09, with A07 and A15 co-occurring in about 38 versus 22 of 100 traces. [DIR002, DIR009, OCC002, OCC004, OCC003, OCC013, DIR005, REL001]

Supporting signs: the share shifts are read directly from the outgoing transitions with several hundred observations per window, and the participation and occurrence movements of the same activities point in the same directions; for A07–A15 the order, the one-step gap and the trace position are unchanged, arguing against an activity having moved or been re-sequenced. Every activity involved remains well observed in both windows (participation roughly 22–61 in 100 traces), so this is a shift between existing alternatives rather than a path appearing or disappearing. [DIR002, DIR005, DIR009, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC016, REL001, LAG001, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: the evidence provides no chance-expectation baseline for the number of flagged items, and a general tendency towards more repetitions per case — A02, A03/A17 and A05 all repeat more — could account for part of the widespread occurrence increases; A03 and A17 also act as one block, so their identical profiles are one piece of evidence rather than two. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, OCC016, DIR003]

Other differences: A15 continues to A11 somewhat more often (about 46 to 53 in 100 transitions, with A12 correspondingly down) at moderate strength; A15 repeats itself at lags 2 and 4 more often, but this rests on only 11 versus 25 observations; the remaining routing differences (after A01, between A13 and A14 after A04, and between A11 and A12 after A09 and after A15) are small and within what random re-splits typically produce. [DIR008, LAG002, DIR001, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007]
