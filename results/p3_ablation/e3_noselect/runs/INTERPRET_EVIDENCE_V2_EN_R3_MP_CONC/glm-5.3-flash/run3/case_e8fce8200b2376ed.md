### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the process executes a restructured flow in which four activities not observed in the earlier window — A07, A15, A16 and A17 — now run and are integrated into existing paths: A08 frequently hands over to A07, which leads on to A18; A10 leads to A15 and A16, which lead back to A10 and onwards to A02/A03; and A18, which previously always led to A02, now leads to A17 (and A17 to A09) or to A14, while the previously direct successions A08→A05, A18→A02 and A10→A02/A03/A10 are no longer observed. [OCC006, OCC014, OCC015, OCC016, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011, DIR012]

The support is direct and consistent: the new activities go from 0 of 200 traces to between a third and seven tenths of traces; the directly-follows profiles show edges appearing and disappearing outright rather than shares between existing alternatives shifting; the relation items show the same activity pairs moving from never co-occurring to co-occurring in a substantial share of traces; and the position and lag shifts point the same way. [OCC006, OCC015, DIR006, DIR007, DIR012, REL021, REL034, LAG005]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly means "not observed", so a rare variant that the 200 before-traces missed cannot be fully excluded — although an activity running in a third to seven tenths of later traces would rarely be absent from 200 before-traces under stable behaviour. Several items describe the same underlying trace changes (for example the occurrence, relation and position items for A07) and therefore corroborate rather than independently confirm, and the evidence cannot show whether the rewired flow replaced the old paths for the same cases or shifted work between variants. [OCC006, OCC014, OCC015, OCC016, REL034]
