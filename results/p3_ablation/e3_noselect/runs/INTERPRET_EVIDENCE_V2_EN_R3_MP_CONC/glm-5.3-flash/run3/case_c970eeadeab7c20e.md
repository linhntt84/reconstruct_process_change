### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A06 is no longer executed, and that A03 has become the fixed final step of the process. In the earlier window A06 was mandatory — present exactly once in every trace — and alternated with A03 as the closing pair (A03 then A06 in about half of traces, A06 then A03 in the rest); A04 could route to A06 (about 23 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) and reached A03 in two steps whenever A06 lay between them. Participation, routing, positional and relational evidence all show this directly and consistently, though they largely describe the same underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations.
