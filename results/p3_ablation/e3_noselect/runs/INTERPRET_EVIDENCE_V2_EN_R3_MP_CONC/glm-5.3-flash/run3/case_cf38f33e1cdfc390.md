### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is between two groups of three activities. In the earlier window, A16, A17 and A18 each ran in about 75 of 100 traces; in the later window, in only about 25 of 100 (support 150 → 49 traces). A19, A20 and A21 each went the opposite way, from about 25 of 100 to about 76 of 100 (support 50 → 151). Each of these six comparisons exceeds the difference seen in 199 of 200 random re-splits of the same data. [OCC013, OCC014, OCC015, OCC016, OCC017, OCC018]

- The two groups were, and remain, mutually exclusive alternatives: an activity from A16/A17/A18 and one from A19/A20/A21 never shared a trace in either window. Earlier, the first group appeared without the second in about 75 of 100 traces and the second without the first in about 25; in the later window these shares reversed. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021]

- In the later window, A15 routes to the second group instead of the first: earlier, about 75 of 100 of A15's outgoing transitions went to A16, A17 or A18 (roughly 27, 23 and 26 per 100) and about 25 to A19, A20 or A21 (roughly 13, 4 and 8); later, about 24 went to the first group and about 76 to the second (A19 about 25, A20 about 22, A21 about 29 per 100). [DIR012]

- Co-occurrence with the anchors moved in step: A15 co-occurred with A16/A17/A18 in 75 of 100 traces before versus about 25 after, and with A19/A20/A21 in 25 before versus about 76 after, always with A15 first. A25, which still runs in every trace, co-occurred with the first group 75 → about 25 and with the second 25 → about 76. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL013, REL018, REL022, REL025, REL027, REL028, OCC021]

- Placement is essentially unchanged: both groups keep a mean position of roughly 0.66–0.70 (about two-thirds into the trace) in both windows, and within each group the three activities still appear in both orders with no fixed internal order. What changed is how often each group runs, not where it sits or how it is ordered internally. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, REL008, REL009, REL014, REL023, REL024, REL026]

- Distances between A15 and the second group shifted somewhat (A19 followed A15 within one step in about 54 of 100 co-occurrences before versus about 33 after), consistent with the changed group composition rather than a separate reordering. [LAG004, LAG005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the shares of two existing alternative paths after A15 reversed: earlier, traces usually continued from A15 into the block of A16, A17 and A18 (about 75 in 100) and rarely into the block of A19, A20 and A21 (about 25 in 100); in the later window the shares are about 25 and about 76 in 100. This looks like a re-weighting of existing paths rather than a structural edit: both blocks are well observed in both windows (49–151 traces each), each block still runs all-or-nothing as a mutually exclusive package, A15 still precedes whichever block runs and the block still precedes A25, positions and internal ordering are unchanged, and no activity became nearly absent. The most direct signs are the reversed successor shares at A15 and the reversed exclusivity pattern between the two groups; the occurrence, co-occurrence and position items agree but largely describe the same traces, so they corroborate rather than independently confirm. Limitations: the evidence set reports no baseline for how many qualifying items an unchanged process would produce, so that check cannot be performed; and the evidence cannot show whether the reversal originates in the branching at A15 itself or in a different mix of cases reaching A15. [OCC013, OCC016, DIR012, REL010, REL019, REL004, POS002, POS005]

Other differences: A10 and A11 repeat slightly more often (mean occurrences per trace 1.72 → 1.83; traces with repeats about 36 → 47 in 100), A12 and A13 run slightly more often (about 47 → 53 in 100 traces), A26 runs slightly less (mean 0.87 → 0.76) while A27 and A28 run slightly more (0.84 → 0.94), A25 routes somewhat more often to A27 than to A26 (about 49/51 → 55/45), and a few other activities show small routing redistributions (e.g., at A08 and A23). These shifts are small and may reflect sampling variation between the windows. [OCC009, OCC010, OCC011, OCC012, OCC022, OCC023, OCC024, DIR020, DIR008, DIR019]
