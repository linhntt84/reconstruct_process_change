### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the relative order of two groups of activities in the middle of the process was reversed, with A02 taking a place in the restructured early segment. In the earlier window a case ran A40 → the always-together trio A20/A22/A23 → A25 → A29 → one of the alternatives A19/A21/A24 → A30, with A02 in only about 8 of 100 cases; in the later window it runs A40 → (in about a third of cases A02) → one of A19/A21/A24 → A25 → A29 → A20/A22/A23 → A30. The order flips are direct and near-deterministic (for example, A20 preceded A25 in about 98 of 100 earlier cases, while A25 precedes A20 in about 100 of 100 later cases), the successor profiles of A19/A21/A24, A20/A22/A23, A29 and A40 change accordingly, and the position and distance shifts agree with the same reordering. These items are not independent — they describe one reordering — but occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators all point the same way. [REL017, REL020, REL024, REL025, REL037, REL042, DIR019, DIR020, DIR021, DIR024, DIR029, DIR040, POS002, POS003, LAG016, LAG017]

A limitation is that the evidence cannot show whether A02's rise (about 8 → 35 of 100 cases, now sitting between A40 and the alternatives and leading mainly into A19/A21/A24), the reversal itself, and the shift toward A19 within the alternative set stem from one coordinated modification or from separate changes; nor can it distinguish a single reordered flow from a changed mix of case variants in which the two groups appear in opposite order. Participation of the trio and the anchors is unchanged, which fits a reordering rather than activities being added or removed. [OCC002, POS001, DIR002, DIR040, REL010, LAG020]

Other differences: the A41–A44 block repeats more often in the later window and A15/A16 slightly less; A10 and A06 participate less; and small routing shifts appear in the early section (for example A01 leads relatively more often to A09, A12 more often to A14 than to A13, and A13 more often to A12). These are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC006, OCC010, OCC015, OCC016, OCC037, OCC039, OCC040, DIR001, DIR012, DIR013, DIR044]
