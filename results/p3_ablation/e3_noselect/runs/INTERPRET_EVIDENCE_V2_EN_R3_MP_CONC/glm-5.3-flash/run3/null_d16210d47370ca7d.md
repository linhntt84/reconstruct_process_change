### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the hand-over shares between A31, A32 and A37 shifted while all three activities kept running in every case: A31 now passes control to A32 more often and directly to A37 less often, A32 passes to A37 more often and to A31 less often, and A37 passes to A31 more often and to A32 less often. The change is direct (the direction profiles measure exactly these shares, each moving by roughly 6–13 in 100 on all 200 traces per window), consistent across indicators (the longer A31→A37 gaps, the tilted order mix and A37's slightly earlier position point the same way), and unusual for random re-splits, with the key differences exceeding about 77–99 in 100 reference comparisons. Since every activity and transition involved was already well observed in both windows and participation did not change, this qualifies as existing paths being taken with different frequencies rather than activities being added, removed or bypassed. [DIR017, DIR018, DIR023, LAG001, REL001, POS002]

A plausible reading is that A31 now reaches A37 via A32 more often, which would explain the shift of A31→A37 pairs from distance 1 to distance 2; the evidence does not directly observe what fills the extra step, so this mechanism remains interpretive. The evidence set also provides no screening baseline, so the expected number of chance-qualified items cannot be compared, and the upstream A20 shift is weak (larger than in only about 42 of 100 reference comparisons). [LAG001, DIR014]

Competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out: the longer A31→A37 gaps could be produced by other activities being inserted between the two instead of A32, or by a subset of cases changing while the rest stayed as before. [LAG001, LAG002]

Other differences: A26 routes more often to A16 and less often to A17, whose participation moved accordingly (about 47→55 and 54→45 in 100 traces); A01's outgoing mix shifted from A05 to A04, with smaller toward-A09 shifts at A03 and A05; A10 and A11 repeat less per trace while still appearing in every trace, and A11 shifts hand-over from A10 toward A12/A13; A39 participates less. These are not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR015, OCC015, OCC016, DIR001, DIR003, DIR005, OCC009, OCC010, DIR009, OCC024]
