### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the process runs without the initial activity A01 and without A04: earlier, every trace passed through A01, which routed work either into the A03–A05 branch or into a fragment in which A02, A06 and A04 followed one another (A04 always accompanying A02, with occasional returns into A01 from A02, A04, A06, A03 and A05); in the later window, traces consist only of A02 followed by A06 (about 47 in 100 traces) or A03 followed by A05 (about 53 in 100), with A01 and A04 entirely absent. The participation drops (200 of 200 traces to 0 of 200 for A01; about 102 of 200 to 0 for A04) are larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits, and the successor changes are observed directly, so this points to a difference in structure and participation rather than a shift of shares between existing paths. Limitations: zero participation only means an activity was not observed, so an important competing explanation cannot be ruled out — a change in recording scope (A01/A04 events no longer logged, or only short completed traces kept) would look identical; the earlier exits from A05 and A06 also rest on only 6 and 14 observed transitions.
