### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the closing sequence A12 → A01 is no longer executed: in the earlier window about half of the cases ended with A01, always immediately after A12, while in the later window A01 is not observed at all and A12 itself terminates the traces in which it appears. This is shown directly by relational evidence (co-occurrence of A01 and A12 drops from about 52 in 100 cases to zero, with A12 now appearing alone), by the disappearance of A12's only observed outgoing transition, and by A12 moving to the final position in every one of its appearances; the participation drop from 103 to 0 of 200 traces, with empirical strengths near 0.995 on all key items, makes ordinary sampling fluctuation an unlikely explanation. Limitations: zero participation only shows A01 was not observed in this sample, so the evidence cannot distinguish removal of A01 from it becoming very rare or being recorded under a different label, and it cannot establish that the A12-only cases are the same cases that previously ended with A01.
