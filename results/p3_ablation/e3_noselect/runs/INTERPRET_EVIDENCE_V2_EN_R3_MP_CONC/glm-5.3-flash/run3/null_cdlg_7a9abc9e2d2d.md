### Observed differences

- The clearest difference concerns what follows A03. In the earlier window, about 28 in 100 of A03's outgoing transitions led to A04 and about 48 in 100 to A05; in the later window, the share to A04 fell to about 22 in 100 while the share to A05 rose to about 53 in 100, with the shares towards A06 (about 10–11 in 100) and A10 (about 14 in 100) essentially stable. In absolute terms, A03→A04 transitions dropped from about 120 of 420 to about 83 of 380, while A03→A05 stayed at about 200 in both windows, so the A04 edge accounts for practically the entire decrease in A03's outgoing transitions. This difference exceeds about 95 in 100 random re-splits. [DIR003]
- A04 took part in fewer traces in the later window: about 30 in 100 traces versus about 38 in 100 before (61 vs 76 traces), with mean occurrences per trace falling from about 0.60 to about 0.42; this difference also ranks in the top few percent of random re-splits. [OCC003]
- A03 still appeared in every trace in both windows but recurred somewhat less: about 2.1 occurrences per trace before versus about 1.9 in the later window, with repetition in about 58% versus about 50% of traces. [OCC002]
- Outside the A03 decision point, A01 and A11 occurred less often in the later window (A01: about 1.04 to 0.88 occurrences per trace, participation about 50 to 46 in 100 traces; A11: about 0.54 to 0.42, participation about 24 to 18 in 100), while their wiring stayed the same — A11 still led almost exclusively to A01, and A01 still split roughly half to A11 and half to A12. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the branch from A03 to A04 is traversed less often while relatively more of A03's output goes to A05, so that A04 participates in fewer traces and A03 recurs somewhat less often. The support is direct — a measured shift in the successor shares of A03 itself — and consistent: A04's participation drop and A03's reduced recurrence point the same way, and the entire decrease in A03's outgoing transitions is concentrated on the A04 edge while A05 stays flat. Both alternatives remain in use (A04 still follows A03 in about 22 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and appears in about 30 in 100 traces), so this reads as a change in how often existing paths are taken rather than a removed or replaced path. Limitations: the shift is modest (about 6 points of A03's outgoing share), the evidence provides no baseline for how many qualifying items an unchanged process would produce, and the items behind this hypothesis partly describe the same trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations. An important competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is ordinary sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows, although the two key differences each rank above about 95 in 100 random re-splits. [DIR003, OCC003, OCC002]

Other differences: A01 and A11 occurred less often in the later window (participation down by about 4 and 6 in 100 traces respectively) with no visible change in how they connect to each other or to A12; the small movements at A07, A09, A12 and in A02's outgoing mix fall within random-split range and are not part of the main hypothesis.
