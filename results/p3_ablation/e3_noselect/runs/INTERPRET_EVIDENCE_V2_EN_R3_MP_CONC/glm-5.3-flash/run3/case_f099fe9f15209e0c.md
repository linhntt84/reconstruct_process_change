### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 was removed from the process, or at least is no longer executed: in the earlier window it ran once in essentially every trace, early in the trace and usually before A01, whereas in the later window it is entirely absent. Consistently with a removal, the flow bypasses it — A03 now leads directly to A01, A04 and A05, and A01 leads directly to A04 — and the downstream activities occur earlier in the trace and at shorter step distances from A03. The disappearance is observed directly (participation from 200 in 200 traces to 0 in 200, with A02's outgoing transitions gone) and the routing, positional and relational indicators all agree, which a sampling fluctuation in an unchanged process would not plausibly produce at this scale. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR001, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, REL001, REL004, REL005, REL006]

The evidence cannot rule out that A02 is still executed but no longer recorded (for example dropped from logging or renamed), nor can it show whether A02 was already conditional before; the modest reduction in A04's and A06's repetitions could be a side effect of A02's removal or an independent change. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004]

Other differences: A07's outgoing transitions shifted from mostly A01 (about 79 in 100) toward A05 (about 33 in 100), but this rests on only about 35 observations per window and may be sampling noise; A04 and A06 repeat slightly less often per trace, as described above. [DIR007, OCC002, OCC004]
