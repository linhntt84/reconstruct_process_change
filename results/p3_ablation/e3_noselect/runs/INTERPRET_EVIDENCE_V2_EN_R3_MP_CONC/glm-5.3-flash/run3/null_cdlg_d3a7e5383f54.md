### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The largest differences — the A04/A05 flip and the rise of A11 — reach empirical strengths of only about 0.82–0.91, meaning roughly 1 to 2 in 10 random re-splits of unchanged traces look at least as different, so a stable process would typically produce a few items of this strength among the 17 evidence items, and no screening baseline is provided to show that the number of qualifying items exceeds chance expectation.
