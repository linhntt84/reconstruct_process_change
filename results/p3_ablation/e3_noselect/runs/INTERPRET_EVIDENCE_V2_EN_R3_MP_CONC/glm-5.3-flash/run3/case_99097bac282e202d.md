### Observed differences

- The largest difference is a reversal between two groups of three activities. In the earlier window, A21, A22 and A23 each ran in about 75 in 100 traces, while A24, A25 and A26 each ran in about 25 in 100; in the later window, A21–A23 each run in about 25 in 100 and A24–A26 in about 75–76 in 100. Within each group the activities still occur together whenever the group runs (co-occurrence tracks the group share: A21–A22 75% → 24.5%, A24–A25 25% → 75.5%), while an activity from one group still almost never co-occurs with one from the other (at most about 1 in 100 traces in either window). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two existing alternative branches swapped their usage shares at the A20 branch point: the branch in which A21, A22 and A23 execute together after A20 (alongside A30) fell from about three of four cases to about one of four, while the branch with A24, A25 and A26 at the same point rose from about one of four to about three of four. This qualifies as a change in how often existing paths are taken rather than a structural replacement: all six activities appear in substantial shares in both windows (about 25 and 75 in 100), the groups remain mutually exclusive, keep their internal togetherness, their order after A20 and their trace positions, and A20's outgoing distribution flipped in step from about 75/25 to about 25/75; the core items agree across occurrence, relation, directly-follows and position indicators with empirical strengths near 0.995. Limitations: the evidence includes no chance-expectation baseline, so the comparison against random variation rests on those empirical strengths, and two 200-trace windows cannot show whether the take-up share changed for all cases or whether the windows contain different proportions of coexisting case types. [OCC021, OCC022, OCC023, OCC024, OCC025, OCC026, DIR020, REL011, REL014, REL020, REL041, POS003, POS006]

Other differences: A10 became about four times as common and is now embedded late in the trace between A32/A20/A30 and A33, a pattern barely present earlier that looks like a second, separate difference rather than part of the branch swap. Smaller participation and recurrence shifts at A02, A06, A08, A15, A16, A17, A18, A27, A31, A32 and A33 are also visible, but several rest on fewer than about 20 traces per window and could be sampling variation.
