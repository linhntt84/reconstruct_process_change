### Observed differences

- After A46 — an activity present in every trace in both windows — the split between its two dominant successors moved: in the earlier window about 53 in 100 of A46's outgoing transitions went to A48 and about 44 in 100 to A47; in the later window about 46 in 100 went to A48 and about 51 in 100 to A47 (roughly 345 outgoing transitions per window; a difference larger than about 93 in 100 random re-splits of the same data). [DIR046, OCC040]
- Downstream of this, A47 took part in about 61 of 100 traces before versus about 67 in the later window (mean occurrences per trace 0.77→0.91; repeats in about 15 vs 19 of 100 traces), while A48's mean occurrences fell from 0.94 to 0.83 and A49's from 0.95 to 0.83, mainly through fewer repetitions (about 22 vs 15 in 100 traces each), with their trace participation nearly unchanged (about 61–63 of 100 traces in both windows). - One group of activities became more frequent: A01, A05, A07 and A09 each rose from about 46 to 53–54 of 100 traces; A19 from about 31 to 38; A24 from about 50 to 57; A43 from about 14 to 21; and A44's mean occurrences rose from 0.71 to 0.81 with repeats up from about 14 to 21 of 100 traces. - A largely disjoint group became less frequent: A03, A11, A12 and A13 each fell from about 55 to 47–49 of 100 traces, A23 from about 52 to 45, and A02, A04, A08 and A25 declined slightly (about 2–4 in 100 traces each). [OCC003, OCC011, OCC012, OCC013, OCC023, OCC002, OCC004, OCC008, OCC025]

- The ever-present activities A15 and A16 repeated slightly more often (repeats in about 35 vs 41 and 42 of 100 traces; means 1.59→1.66 and 1.67), a difference that random re-splits also produce fairly often. [OCC015, OCC016]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that existing alternative routes are now taken with different frequencies around A46: in the later window, of A46's outgoing transitions about 51 in 100 go to A47 (previously 44) and about 46 go to A48 (previously 53); accordingly A47 occurs in more traces (about 61→67 in 100) while A48 and A49 occur less often per trace and repeat less (means 0.94→0.83 and 0.95→0.83). This is shown directly by the successor-share change at A46 on roughly 345 outgoing transitions, and it agrees in direction and size with the occurrence and recurrence shifts of A47, A48 and A49; all four activities occur in a substantial share of traces in both windows, so this is a re-weighting of existing alternatives rather than a path appearing or disappearing. [DIR046, OCC040, OCC041, OCC042, OCC043]

The evidence cannot fully rule out chance as the source of these shifts: no screening baseline is provided to size how many qualifying items an unchanged process would produce, each window holds 200 traces, and predecessors of A47 and A48 other than A46 are not observed, so a minor part of the participation changes could enter from elsewhere in the process. [DIR046, OCC041, OCC042, OCC043]

Other differences: the opposite participation movements of the A01/A05/A07/A09 group (up by about 7–8 in 100 traces) and the A03/A11/A12/A13 group plus A23 (down by about 6–8 in 100 traces) suggest further changes in how often existing paths are taken, but no observed relation ties these groups to a common decision point; successor mixes also shifted after A03 (A12 vs A13), A22 (A24 vs A33) and A41/A42 (A34 vs A42/A41), the A43–A44 back-and-forth intensified (A43→A44 became the sole observed successor; A44→A43 rose from about 33 to 40 in 100), and repetitions of A15, A16 and A44 increased.
