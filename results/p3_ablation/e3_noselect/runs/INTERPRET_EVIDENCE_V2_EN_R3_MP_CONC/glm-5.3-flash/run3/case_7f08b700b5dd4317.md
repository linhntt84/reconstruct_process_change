### Observed differences

- The largest difference concerns six activities — A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39. In the earlier window each of them ran in every trace (200 in 200); in the later window each runs in only about half of the traces: A33, A35 and A36 in about 93–94 in 200, A37, A38 and A39 in about 112–113 in 200. Each of these differences is larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the data. [OCC031, OCC033, OCC034, OCC035, OCC036, OCC037]
- The six activities changed from always running together to being two almost mutually exclusive groups. In the earlier window every cross-group pair (e.g. A33 with A37) co-occurred in all traces; in the later window cross-group pairs co-occur in only about 3 in 100 traces, while within-group pairs (A33 with A35, A37 with A38) still co-occur in practically every trace containing either of them. About 44 in 100 later traces contain only the A33/A35/A36 group, about 53 in 100 only the A37/A38/A39 group, and about 3 in 100 contain both. [REL014, REL016, REL020, REL023, REL026]
- The segment still sits between A30 and A31, which run in every trace in both windows: A30 still precedes the group activities and the group activities still precede A31 whenever they run; A30 now simply co-occurs with each group activity only in the roughly half of traces that contain that group. [POS001, POS002, REL002, REL005, REL008, REL011]
- Routing at the segment boundaries changed. In the earlier window A30's outgoing transitions went almost only to A33, A35 and A36 (about 34%, 36% and 28%); in the later window they spread over all six activities (about 12–19% each). A33, A35 and A36, which previously never exited to A31, now do so in about 32%, 34% and 26% of their outgoing transitions. [DIR030, DIR033, DIR035, DIR036]
- The segment became shorter in event positions: the gap from A30 to A37, A38 or A39, and from A33, A35 or A36 to A31, was 4–5 steps in the earlier window and is 1–3 steps in about 9 in 10 later traces; A30's mean relative position moved later (about 0.62 to 0.67) and A31's earlier (about 0.82 to 0.80). [LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG013, LAG019, POS001, POS002]
- Smaller shifts elsewhere: A44 and A43 occur somewhat more often (A44 in about 68 vs 63 in 100 traces, mean occurrences about 0.94 vs 0.80; A43 mean about 1.72 vs 1.55), and A02 less often (about 13 vs 20 in 100 traces). [OCC042, OCC041, OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the part of the process between A30 and A31 no longer always executes all six activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39; instead, each trace runs one of two alternative groups of three — either A33, A35 and A36, or A37, A38 and A39 — which changed from being executed together in every trace to being mutually exclusive alternatives (joint occurrence in about 3 in 100 later traces, against 100 in 100 earlier). The chosen group is entered directly from A30 and exits to A31, with A33, A35 and A36 gaining direct exits to A31 that were not observed before, and the segment compressing from 4–5 to mostly 1–3 event positions between its boundaries and the group activities. [REL016, REL023, DIR030, DIR033, DIR035, DIR036, LAG004, LAG007]

The main signs are the participation drop of all six activities from 100% to about 47–57%, the co-occurrence restructuring (within-group preserved, cross-group collapsed, with complementary shares), the successor changes at A30, the new exits to A31, and the gap compression; all describe one coherent difference and each is larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits, which random sampling in a stable process does not typically produce. A30, A31 and A34 are unchanged, which localises the difference to this segment. [OCC031, OCC033, OCC034, OCC035, OCC036, OCC037, REL016, DIR030, DIR033, OCC032, POS001, POS002]

The evidence cannot show what decides which group a trace runs (for example a random branch versus routing by case characteristics), and it cannot distinguish a modified routing decision from the later window mixing two variants, although the earlier window shows a single uniform behaviour. A few lag profiles for cross-group pairs rest on only 3–5 observations and were not relied upon. [LAG010, LAG011, LAG012]

Other differences: A43 and A44 occur somewhat more often in the later window, and A02 less often; A48's outgoing mix changed completely but rests on only 8 versus 5 observed transitions, so it is weak evidence; none of these are part of the main hypothesis. [OCC041, OCC042, OCC002, DIR048]
