### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering of A01, A06 and A07 in the closing segment changed: A01, which previously executed last — after A06 and A07 — now executes directly after A08 and before A06 and A07, so A06 and A07 each shift one position later and A07 becomes the final activity. This is shown directly by the successor changes at A08, A01 and A07, by the complete order reversals between A01 and each of A06 and A07, and by position and distance shifts that all match one coherent reordering; the behaviour is uniform in 200 of 200 traces in both windows, which makes a rare-variant or sampling explanation unlikely. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: participation itself did not change — A01, A06 and A07 appear in all traces in both windows — and the evidence covers only these four activities, so any further differences elsewhere in the trace are not evidenced; the strength value expresses unusualness for random re-splits, not a probability of change. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows sample two different fixed order variants (for example a shift in the case mix) rather than a modification within one process, since each window shows a single uniform order.
