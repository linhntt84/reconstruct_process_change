### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A02, A03, A13 and A14 always appeared together — each in about 49 in 100 traces (97 of 200) — and A10 appeared only inside this group, in about 24 in 100 traces (roughly half of the group's traces) [OCC002, OCC003, OCC013, OCC014, REL001, REL007, REL016, REL005].

- A03 and A14 now each appear in about 19 in 100 traces (37 of 200, down from 97), still always as a pair and always inside A13 traces; A13 itself continues at almost the same level (about 46 in 100 traces, down slightly from 49) [OCC003, OCC014, OCC013, REL015, REL016].

- Minor shifts: A01 now sends about 91 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A12 (about 84 earlier), A04 sends about 89 in 100 to A01 (about 81 earlier), and A13's repetition eased slightly (mean about 1.0 to 0.8 occurrences per trace) [DIR001, DIR004, OCC013].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change in the segment built around A02, A03, A10, A13 and A14. In the later window, A02 is no longer executed in any observed case (0 of 200 traces versus 97 of 200), the A03+A14 pair runs in far fewer cases (about 19 in 100 traces, down from about 49 in 100, where it always accompanied A02 and A13), and A10 has become a fixed companion of A13 (about 46 in 100 traces, up from about 24 in 100, now never seen without A13). Routing flipped accordingly: A13 continues mainly to A09, A08 and A10 instead of A02 and A03, A08 continues to A03 instead of A10, A09, A06 and A15 continue mainly to A10 instead of A03 or A14, and A14 leads almost exclusively to A13. In short, a block that used to run through A02/A03/A14 in about half of all cases now runs through A13+A10, with A03+A14 retained in only about 4 in 10 of the A13 traces [OCC002, OCC003, OCC010, OCC014, DIR002, DIR012, DIR008, DIR009, DIR013, DIR014, DIR006, REL016, REL037].

The judgement rests on directness and consistency, and the item count agrees: occurrence, direction, relation, position and lag items all describe the same structure, each window's profile rests on tens to hundreds of observations, and roughly 70 of the 153 evidence items exceed about 99 in 100 random re-splits — far more than the few items an unchanged process typically produces [OCC002, DIR002, DIR012, REL001, POS006].

An important competing explanation remains: zero participation only shows that A02 was not observed in the later window, so an unchanged process in which A02's branch is simply not taken in these 200 cases would look identical; the later A03/A14 shares also rest on only 37 traces, and several items describe the same trace behaviour (the nine A02 relation items mirror one disappearance), so they are not independent confirmations [OCC002, REL001, OCC003].

Other differences: the small redistributions around A01, A04 and A12 (A01→A12 from about 84 to 91 in 100 of A01's outgoing transitions) and the slight easing of A13's repetition (mean about 1.0 to 0.8 occurrences per trace) are reported but not part of the main hypothesis [DIR001, DIR004, OCC013].
