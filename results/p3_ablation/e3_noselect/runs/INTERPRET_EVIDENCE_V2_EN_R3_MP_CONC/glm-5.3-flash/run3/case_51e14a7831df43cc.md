### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A09 and A16 exchanged their places in the process flow. In the earlier window, A09 was a mandatory late step — present in every trace at about two occurrences, always reached from A04 and A10 and leading to A05, A06 and A14 — while A16 was an optional early step in about half of the traces, reached from A01 and A13. In the later window these roles are reversed: A16 is the mandatory late step reached from A04/A10 and preceding A05/A06/A14, and A09 is the optional early step after A01/A13, from which flow continues into A01, A04, A10 and A13. The change is shown directly by the complete successor replacements at A04 and A10, the exchanged outgoing sets of A09 and A16, and the mirrored lag distributions, and it is consistent across occurrence, position and co-occurrence indicators, with the key items near the top of the empirical-strength scale (about 0.995). [OCC007, OCC013, POS004, POS008, DIR001, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, DIR009, DIR011, REL003, REL007, REL021, REL024, LAG004, LAG008, LAG013, LAG028, LAG029]

The evidence cannot distinguish a literal swap of the two activities from an equivalent rewiring in which each activity simply adopted the other's predecessors and successors; with 200 traces per window, a shift in the mix of two coexisting process variants also cannot be fully excluded. [DIR004, DIR006, DIR011]

Other differences: A05, A07, A12 and A14 each occur less often per trace in the later window — A05 from about 1.24 to 0.89 with participation unchanged (about 39 in 100 traces), A07 from about 1.33 to 0.85 with participation falling from about 36 to 28 in 100 traces, A12 from about 2.32 to 1.7, and A14 from about 1.08 to 0.82 — and A12's observed outgoing shares shifted mildly away from A07 (about 57% to 50%) toward A08 and A11; these changes are not part of the exchange hypothesis.
