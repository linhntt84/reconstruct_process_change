### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, existing branches are taken with different frequencies: fewer traces contain A01, A05, A07, A09, A23, A35, A36, A39 and A02 (each down by roughly 6–9 in 100 traces), while more traces contain A03, A11, A12, A13, A24, A37, A38 and A43 (each up by roughly 8–13 in 100), and A42 — present in every trace throughout — is repeated more often (about 1.8 vs 1.6 occurrences per trace). Every named activity is clearly present in both windows and the low-volume activities A04, A06 and A10 are essentially unchanged, so the pattern fits existing branches being run more or less often rather than activities being added or removed. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC005, OCC007, OCC008, OCC010, OCC011, OCC012, OCC022, OCC023, OCC030, OCC031, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, OCC037, OCC038, OCC004, OCC006, OCC009]

Two signs support this beyond any single item: the occurrence and routing evidence agree in the same regions (the rerouting toward A42–A43 and away from A44/A46 matches A43's rise and A42's extra repetitions; the rerouting around A35/A36 versus A37/A38 matches their mirror-image participation change), and the strongest items reach empirical strengths of 0.92–0.98, differences that random re-splits of the pooled traces rarely exceed. [DIR031, DIR035, DIR036, DIR037, DIR038, DIR042, DIR043, DIR045, DIR046, OCC037, OCC038]

The competing explanation that remains is sampling variation between two finite windows of 200 traces: several items individually sit at strength levels (about 0.78–0.88) that an unchanged process still produces occasionally, activities with near-identical profiles (A01/A05/A07/A09, A35/A36, A37/A38) almost certainly fluctuate together, and no expected-count baseline accompanies the evidence, so the judgement rests on the joint, regionally consistent pattern; the evidence also cannot show whether the up- and down-groups are connected as alternatives at a single choice or represent several independent shifts. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC030, OCC031]
