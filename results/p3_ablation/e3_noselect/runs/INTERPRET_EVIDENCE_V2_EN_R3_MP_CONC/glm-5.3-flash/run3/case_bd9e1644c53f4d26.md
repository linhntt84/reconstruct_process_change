### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 was added to the process as a new step executed once per case and inserted into the existing flow at a varying, roughly mid-trace point, with incoming transitions from A01, A03, A04 and A06 and outgoing transitions back to A01, A03, A04, A05, A06 and A07. In the earlier window A02 appears in none of 200 traces; in the later window it appears in all 200, and the redirection of A01's and A03's outgoing transitions towards it is the main routing difference. [OCC001, POS002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006]

The insertion reading is consistent across indicator families: four activity pairs flip from "never together" to "together in every trace" with mixed ordering (so A02 is not fixed in one slot); pairwise distances between pre-existing activities grow and their positions shift slightly later, exactly as expected when a step is placed between them; and the old activities' mutual order stays essentially unchanged, which fits an inserted step rather than a replacement or re-routing of existing paths. These items largely describe one underlying difference and are not independent confirmations, but they agree in direction and none contradicts it. [REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, LAG002, LAG012, LAG005, POS001, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG001]

Limitations: the activity codes are anonymous, so the evidence cannot fully rule out that A02 is a re-labelled or split version of an existing step rather than a genuinely new one; a plain rename is unlikely because every previous activity is still observed in both windows and A02 interleaves with several of them in both orders. The apparent links from A05 and A07 to A02 rest on weak support (A05's profile on few outgoing transitions, A07's difference within chance variation) and are not part of the hypothesis. [POS001, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, DIR005, DIR007]

Other differences: the share of cases in which A03 precedes A04 rose from about 66 to about 73 in 100; this small ordering shift rests on a single indicator with moderate strength and is not explained by the insertion. [REL008]
