- From A01, the share of outgoing transitions to A06 slips from about 72% to about 64%, with small gains to A02 (about 13% to 16%) and A09 (about 15% to 20%); this is well within the random-split range (strength 0.36). [DIR001]

From A04, the share going to A02 falls from about 14% to about 5% while the share going to A06 rises from about 23% to about 31% (strength 0.76); from A08, the share going to A06 falls from about 68% to about 56% while the share going to A02 rises from about 16% to about 27% (strength 0.72, on a smaller base of about 52–56 traces). Even these differences are exceeded by roughly one in four random re-splits of unchanged data. [DIR003, DIR005]

### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The observed differences are of a size that random re-splits of unchanged data regularly produce, and they do not converge on one behaviour — flow into A06 increases from A04 but decreases from A08 and A01 — so sample fluctuation remains a plausible explanation that the evidence cannot rule out.
