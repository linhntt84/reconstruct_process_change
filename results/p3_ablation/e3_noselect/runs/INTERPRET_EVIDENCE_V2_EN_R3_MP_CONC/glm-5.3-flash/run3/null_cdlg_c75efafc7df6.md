### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. All observed differences are small — at most about 4 in 100 traces of participation, routing shifts of about ten percentage points computed from roughly 50–110 transitions, and 1–3 disappearing rare transitions — and are of the size that random re-splits of unchanged data also produce, so sampling variation between the two windows cannot be excluded [OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, DIR006, DIR002].
