### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the segment A19 → A25 → A20 ran in every trace, in a fixed order (A19, then A25 one step later, then A20 one step after that). In the later window it ran in only about 53–54% of traces (about 106–108 of 200), and the three activities appear or are skipped together: in about 46 of 100 later traces none of them occurs, while traces containing only part of the segment are about 1% or fewer. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, REL024, REL025, REL030]
- When the segment does run in the later window, it behaves as before: the internal one-to-two-step lags hold in about 94–98% of cases (previously about 96–99%), the three activities keep their trace positions (medians about 0.34–0.40 in both windows), and their outgoing transitions are nearly identical between windows. [LAG020, LAG021, LAG031, POS004, POS005, POS006, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025]
- A41, which precedes the segment and occurs in every trace in both windows, now often routes around it: A41 → A19 fell from about 96% to about 47% of A41's outgoing transitions, while A41 → A26 rose from essentially unobserved to about 39%, mostly as an immediate step (lag 1 in about 40% of cases versus almost never before). [DIR041, LAG038, LAG041, OCC037]
- A14, also present in every trace throughout, now usually continues straight to A15: A14 → A15 rose from about 31% to about 78% of its outgoing transitions while A14 → A06 fell from about 69% to about 12%, and the A14→A15 step is more often immediate (lag 1 in about 41% of cases versus 17%). [DIR014, LAG010, POS002]
- A06 became rarer and sits later in the trace: it appeared in about 71 of 100 traces before versus about 26 of 100 after, its median position moved from about 0.16 to 0.30 (upper quartile 0.17 to 0.83), and its transitions now spread widely instead of leading to A15 about 92% of the time (now about 37%). [OCC006, POS001, DIR006, REL001, REL002]
- Several rare activities occur roughly twice as often in the later window (A08: about 4% to 20% of traces; A10: 5% to 16%; A04: 5% to 15%; A02: 9% to 18%) with clearly reshaped successor profiles; these comparisons rest on only about 8–43 observations per window. [OCC002, OCC004, OCC008, OCC010, DIR002, DIR004, DIR008, DIR010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the segment A19 → A25 → A20 became optional: it executed in every trace before and in only about half of the traces after (about 106–108 of 200), and when it is skipped its predecessor A41 continues directly to A26 instead of entering the segment. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation of all three activities drops from 100% to about 53–54% with differences larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits; relational evidence shows the three are skipped as a unit (joint absence in about 46 of 100 later traces, partial presence about 1%); their internal order, lags, positions and outgoing transitions are unchanged, pointing to skipping rather than replacement or repositioning; and A41's new direct edge to A26 appears precisely where the segment is missing. A limitation is that the evidence cannot distinguish a newly skippable segment within one variant from a shift in the mix of two pre-existing case variants (one with, one without the segment), and it says nothing about why cases skip it. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, REL024, REL025, REL030, REL011, DIR041, LAG041, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG020, LAG021, LAG031]

Other differences: The A06 step between A14 and A15 is now mostly bypassed — A06 appears in about 26 of 100 later traces (previously 71) and A14 → A15 is the dominant continuation at about 78% — and the rare activities A02, A04, A08 and A10 occur about twice as often with changed successors, though on small numbers (about 8–43 observations per window). [OCC006, DIR014, REL002, OCC002, OCC004, OCC008, OCC010]
