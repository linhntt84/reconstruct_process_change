### Observed differences

- **Two activities appear from nothing.** In the earlier window, A01 and A05 never occur (0 of 200 traces); in the later window, each occurs exactly once in every trace (200 of 200, mean 1.0 per trace, no repetitions). Both differences are larger than those seen in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002]
- **A01 sits between the A03/A04 segment and A06.** In the later window, about 45% of A03's observed outgoing transitions and about 52% of A04's lead to A01, whereas in the earlier window A03 led mainly to A06, A02 and A04, and A04 led mainly to A06 (about 74%). A01's own transitions go about 80% to A06; A01 precedes A06 in all traces where both occur, A03 precedes A01 in about 87% of traces and A04 in about 69%. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR001, REL001, REL002, REL004]
- **A05 sits at the end, after A06.** In the earlier window, A06 typically closed the case: its outgoing transitions were observed in only 59 of 200 traces, and otherwise led back to A02 in about 53% of them. In the later window, about 84% of A06's transitions lead to A05, A06 precedes A05 in all co-occurring traces, and A05 occupies the final position (median relative position 1.0); A03 and A04 precede A05 in about 98% and 87% of traces, and A01 precedes A05 in all. [DIR006, REL010, POS004, REL006, REL008, REL003]
- **Downstream shifts consistent with new steps lying in between.** A04's mean relative position moved earlier (0.68 to 0.47) and A06's as well (0.83 to 0.72); the A04-to-A06 gap lengthened from immediately consecutive in about 74% of cases to about 7%, with gaps of two to three steps in about 93% of cases. Position shifts alone do not prove movement, but they match the new ordering relations. [POS003, POS005, LAG004, LAG001]
- **Minor redistributions.** A02's share of transitions to A03 fell from about 27% to 16%, with small new shares to A01 (7%) and A05 (3%); A03 precedes A04 somewhat less often (about 78% vs 67% of traces); and in about 29 of 200 later traces the flow continues after A05, mostly back to A04 or A02. [DIR002, REL005, DIR005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the later window contains two additional steps: A01, executed between the A03/A04 segment and A06, and A05, executed at the end of the case after A06. In the earlier window, cases ran from A03 and A04 to A06, which usually ended the trace; in the later window, A03 and A04 hand over to A01 in roughly half of their transitions, A01 leads to A06 in about 80% of its transitions, and A06 leads to A05 in about 84% of its transitions. The placement is shown directly by the new successor relations and the relative-order relations (A01 before A06 and before A05, and A06 before A05, each in all co-occurring traces), and it agrees with the participation change (0 of 200 vs 200 of 200 traces) and with A05's end position. Limitations: placement is established in relative-order and event-position terms, not clock time; no chance-expectation baseline is provided in the evidence, although a 0-to-200-of-200 participation change is not a plausible sampling artefact; and the evidence cannot rule out that the new activity codes are re-labelments of previously unrecorded behaviour rather than genuinely new steps, although no previously observed activity disappeared. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL008, REL010, POS004]

Other differences: the minor redistribution around A02, the slightly reduced A03-before-A04 share, and the small subset of later traces continuing after A05 are described above but are not part of this hypothesis. [DIR002, REL005, DIR005]
