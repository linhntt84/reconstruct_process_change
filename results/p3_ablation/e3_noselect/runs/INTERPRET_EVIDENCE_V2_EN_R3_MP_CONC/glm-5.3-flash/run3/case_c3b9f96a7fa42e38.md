### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the group of activities A35, A36 and A41 is executed between A26 and A30 instead of between A25 and A27: earlier the sequence ran A25 → (A35/A36/A41 in a mixed order) → A27 → A28 → A29 → A33 → A34 → A26 → A30, and in the later window it runs A25 → A27 → A28 → A29 → A33 → A34 → A26 → (A35/A36/A41) → A30. The change is shown directly at the group's attachment points — A25's successor changed from the group to A27 and A26's from A30 to the group, each group member's successor changed from A27 to A30, and the A25→A27 and A26→A30 distances moved from four steps to one and from one to four respectively, each with about 200 traces behind it in both windows. [DIR025, DIR026, DIR035, DIR036, DIR041, LAG019, LAG024]

The reading is consistent across indicator families: the pairwise orderings flip only at the group's boundaries while A25-before-A27, A27-before-A26, A26-before-A30 and group-before-A30 stay fixed; the position shifts of A26, A27, A35, A36 and A41 match a group inserted later in the trace; and every involved activity occurs in all 200 traces in both windows, so this is not an activity appearing, disappearing or becoming optional. The group's internal order remains mixed in both windows, so only its attachment points changed. [REL025, REL030, REL031, REL032, REL033, REL034, REL035, REL036, REL037, REL038, REL039, REL040, REL041, POS005, POS006, POS008, POS009, POS010, OCC025, OCC026, OCC027, OCC034, OCC035, OCC040, REL042, REL043, REL044]

Limitations: the two windows are aggregates of 200 traces each, so the evidence cannot show how abruptly the arrangement switched, and it cannot rule out that both arrangements existed as variants in both windows with only their shares exchanged — although the flips concern about 98–100 in 100 traces on each side, which makes a mere share shift between persistent variants unlikely. [REL032, REL033, REL034, REL036, REL037, REL038]

Other differences: The optional detour A15 → A06 → A16, executed in about 50 of 200 traces earlier, is executed in only about 11–15 of 200 later, and A06 overall appears in 25 of 200 traces instead of 54, now later and scattered; since A15 and A16 still occur in every trace and A06 still occurs in about 12 in 100, this fits an existing path simply being taken much less often. Minor rerouting affects A01 (less often via A09, more often via A05) and the A39/A40 order around A32, and the rare activities A02, A04, A08 and A10 appear somewhat more often, though their low counts make those shares fragile.
