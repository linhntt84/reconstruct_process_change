### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new activity, A01, was added at the end of the process and is now executed exactly once per case, immediately after A16 — the activity that previously occupied the fixed final position of every trace. In the earlier window each of the 200 traces contained A16 once and contained no A01; in the later window every trace contains A16 followed by A01, A01 has taken over exactly the position A16 previously held, and every observed outgoing transition of A16 now leads to A01. The pattern is shown directly and consistently by four indicator families — occurrence, succession, position and relation — each with empirical strength about 0.995, and a chance appearance of a rare variant is effectively excluded by participation moving from 0 of 200 to 200 of 200 traces. One competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 events were simply not recorded in the earlier window, which would look identical to a genuine addition. [OCC001, DIR009, POS001, POS002, REL001]
