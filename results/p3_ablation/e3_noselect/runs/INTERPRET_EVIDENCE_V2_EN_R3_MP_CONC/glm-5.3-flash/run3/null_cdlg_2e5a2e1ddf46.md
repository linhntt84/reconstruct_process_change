### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The standout items are few, modest in size, and correlated expressions of one underlying shift in how often the A02–A09–A06 sequence is executed rather than independent confirmations, and with no screening expectation provided it cannot be shown that their number exceeds what an unchanged process would produce, so a chance difference in how often the later window sampled this sequence remains a live explanation [OCC002, OCC005, OCC008, DIR002, DIR007].
