### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, no trace contained A14, A15 or A20 (0 of 200 traces each); in the later window, A14 runs in about 29 in 100 traces (58 of 200), A15 in about 41 in 100 (82 of 200) and A20 in about 30 in 100 (59 of 200), each at most once per trace and around the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.51). [OCC013, OCC014, OCC019, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- The three new activities are mutually exclusive alternatives: they never occur in the same trace, and for each pair the share of traces containing neither (about 30–41 in 100) matches the third activity's participation, so about 995 in 1000 later traces contain exactly one of them. [REL001, REL002, REL006, OCC013, OCC014, OCC019]
- The new activities sit inside the segment A22 → A23 → A24. In the earlier window A22 was always followed by A23 and A23 always by A24; in the later window A22 leads to A23 in about 44 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and to one of the new activities in about 56 in 100, A23 leads to A24 in about 57 in 100 and to a new activity in about 43 in 100, and each new activity is followed by A23 in about 53–58 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and by A24 otherwise. [DIR018, DIR019, DIR010, DIR011, DIR016]
- Event distances changed accordingly: A22 to A24 was always two steps apart and is now three steps apart in about 995 in 1000 traces; A22 to A23 is one step apart in about 44 in 100 cases and two steps apart in about 56 in 100; A23 to A24 is one step apart in about 57 in 100 and two steps apart in about 43 in 100. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- The ordering relations fit a step placed between A22 and A24: A22 always precedes the new activity and the new activity always precedes A24, while relative to A23 both orders occur (A14 precedes A23 in about 16 in 100 traces and follows it in about 14 in 100; similar for A15 and A20). [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- Participation shifted mildly elsewhere: A01, A03, A04 and A05 each rose from about 48 to 54 in 100 traces, while A02, A06, A07 and A08 each fell from about 53 to 47. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]
- Routing shares also moved slightly: from A01, A03 and A05 the outgoing shares redistributed among A03/A04/A05 (for example A01→A05 fell from about 42% to 23% of A01's outgoing transitions while A01→A03 rose from about 28% to 42%); A39 and A40 rose from about 57 to 67 in 100 traces while A38 fell from about 62 to 56, and A37 sent about 56% of its outgoing transitions to A39 instead of about 46%. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR005, OCC027, OCC028, OCC029, DIR029]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new step, executed as one of three alternative activities, was added between A22 and A24: in the later window almost every trace executes exactly one of A14 (about 29 in 100 traces), A15 (about 41 in 100) or A20 (about 30 in 100) between A22 and A24, placed directly after A22 (before A23) in about 56 in 100 traces and directly after A23 (before A24) in about 44 in 100, whereas in the earlier window every trace went directly from A22 to A23 to A24. [DIR018, DIR019, DIR010, DIR011, DIR016, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL001, REL002, REL006, OCC013, OCC014, OCC019]

This hypothesis is shown directly and consistently rather than resting on the number of items: the three activities go from zero participation to 29–41%, the previously unconditional successors of A22 and A23 now include the new activities, the A22–A24 distance lengthens from two to three steps in virtually every trace, and the ordering relations match a step sitting between A22 and A24; the routing split of about 56/44 also reproduces the observed step distances, and three activities appearing in 58–82 of 200 traces after appearing in none of 200 is difficult to attribute to sampling variation between the windows. [OCC013, OCC014, OCC019, DIR018, DIR019, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL003, REL005]

The evidence cannot show whether A14, A15 and A20 are genuinely added steps or existing work appearing under new anonymous codes (possibly three recorded variants of one logical step), because activity identity rests only on the codes; the placement is also established in event positions, not clock time. [OCC013, OCC014, OCC019, DIR010, DIR011, DIR016]

Other differences: the mild participation and routing shifts around A01–A08 and A37–A40 described above are not part of this hypothesis; at sizes of about 6–10 traces in 100 and with single-item strengths reaching about 0.99, sampling variation between the two windows cannot be ruled out for them. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR001, DIR003, DIR005, OCC027, OCC028, OCC029, DIR029]
