### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports an insertion of A01 between A08 and A10 in the later window: where flow previously passed directly from A08 to A10 (about 55 in 100 of A08's outgoing transitions, with A09 as an unchanged alternative at about 45 in 100), it now passes from A08 to A01 and from A01 on to A10. The signs fit a pure insertion: A01 appears from zero to about 21 in 100 traces at mid-trace positions; A08's successor profile swaps A10 for A01 at nearly the same shares while A10's own participation stays at about 21 in 100; A01 precedes A10 in about 18 in 100 traces and follows A08; and the A08→A10 step distance moves from one step to two, exactly the offset an inserted activity produces, with A10 shifted later. Each of these differences is larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 is a re-labelled activity that already existed under another code rather than genuinely new work; positions are also event ranks, not clock times.
