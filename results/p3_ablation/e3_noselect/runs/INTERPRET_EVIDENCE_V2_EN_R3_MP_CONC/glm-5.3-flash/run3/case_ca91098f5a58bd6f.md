### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative three-activity segment between A36 and A21 — A14, A15 and A20, exactly one of which was executed in every case between A36 and A21 — is no longer executed, so that in the later window A36 leads directly to A21. The change is described directly and consistently by all five indicator families: occurrence (three activities at about 60–70 of 200 traces falling to 0 of 200), outgoing edges (A36's flow moves from a roughly 35/30/35 split across the three to A21 in 100 of 100 cases, and the three activities' only outgoing edge, to A21, disappears), relations (co-occurrence of the three with A21, A26 and A36 drops to zero), distance (A36→A21 always two steps before, always one step after) and position (A21 earlier, A36 later, the three in-between positions gone). [OCC013, OCC014, OCC019, DIR027, DIR010, DIR011, DIR016, LAG002, REL004, REL016, POS005, POS007]

Zero participation establishes only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed in the later window; the evidence cannot exclude that they still execute in a very small share of cases, nor distinguish removal from a renaming or replacement — although no new or substitute activity appears anywhere in the evidence. [OCC013, OCC014, OCC019]
