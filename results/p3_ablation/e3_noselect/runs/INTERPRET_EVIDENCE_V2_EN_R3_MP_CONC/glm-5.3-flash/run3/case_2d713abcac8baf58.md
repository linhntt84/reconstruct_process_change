### Observed differences

- Three activities appear only in the later window: A26, A27 and A28 are absent from all 200 earlier traces but run once in virtually every later trace (200 of 200; about 1.0 occurrences per trace), at roughly 37–38% of the trace's relative position. [OCC025, OCC026, OCC027, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Three activities nearly disappear: in the earlier window A19, A20 and A21 each ran once in about 35, 30 and 36 in 100 traces respectively, strictly as mutually exclusive alternatives (each pair co-occurred in at most about 0.5 in 100 traces); in the later window each appears in only 1–2 of 200 traces. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC020, REL001, REL002, REL008]
- A44's routing changed almost completely: earlier, about 85 in 100 of its outgoing transitions went to A19 (about 31), A21 (about 30) or A20 (about 24); in the later window, about 85 in 100 go to A26 (about 31), A27 (about 27) or A28 (about 26). [DIR044]
- Earlier, whichever of A19/A20/A21 ran was followed by A29 in about 95–99 in 100 of that activity's outgoing transitions; in the later window the three new activities lead to one another and to A29, each sending roughly 30–36 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to each of the other two block members and to A29. [DIR019, DIR020, DIR021, DIR026, DIR027, DIR028]
- The new block sits between A44 and A29: A44 precedes each of A26/A27/A28 in about 99 in 100 later traces, and each of them precedes A29 in about 98–99 in 100. [REL021, REL022, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027]
- Inside the block there is no fixed order: each pair co-occurs in every later trace and both orders occur in near-equal shares (for example, A26 precedes A27 in about 51 in 100 traces and follows it in about 49 in 100). [REL019, REL020, REL023]
- The step distance from A44 to A29 grew accordingly: earlier, A29 followed A44 two steps later in about 84 in 100 traces (three steps in about 16); in the later window it follows four steps later in about 82 in 100 (five steps in about 18), and A29's mean relative position moved from 0.40 to 0.44 of the trace. [LAG001, POS007]
- Smaller shifts elsewhere: participation fell for A39 (54 to 45 in 100 traces) and A46 (70 to 58 in 100 traces, mean occurrences 0.92 to 0.77) and rose for A40 (46 to 55), A41 (47 to 55), A47 (59 to 64) and A48 (59 to 64); A15 and A16 repeat slightly more often (mean occurrences 1.63 to 1.69 and 1.61 to 1.68), A45 repeats somewhat less often (45 to 37 in 100 traces), and A44's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (0.33 to 0.31). [OCC035, OCC036, OCC037, OCC042, OCC043, OCC044, OCC015, OCC016, OCC041, POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the segment between A44 and A29 is executed by a different structure: in the earlier window every trace passed from A44 to A29 through exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities — A19, A20 or A21, chosen in roughly 35/30/36 per 100 traces — whereas in the later window that choice stage is gone and every trace instead runs a block of three activities, A26, A27 and A28, between A44 and A29, with no fixed order inside the block. This is a replacement of one path structure by another (activities removed and added, and a one-activity step turned into a mandatory three-activity block), not merely a shift in how often existing paths are taken. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, OCC026, OCC027, DIR044, DIR026, DIR027, DIR028, REL019, REL021, REL022, LAG001]

The change is shown directly by successor profiles, order relations, relative positions and the A44→A29 step distance, which all move consistently; the appearance and disappearance are near-total (0 vs 200 of 200 traces; 60–72 vs 1–2 traces), and the key items exceed about 99 in 100 reference comparisons, i.e., they are far larger than the differences produced by re-splitting a stable process. [DIR044, LAG001, POS007, OCC018, OCC025, REL019]

Other differences: several later-stage activities shifted participation by roughly 5–12 in 100 traces (A39 and A46 down; A40, A41, A47 and A48 up), A15/A16 repeat slightly more often and A45 less often, and some routing shares tilted (for example, from A24 more often to A34, and from A45 more often to A47 than to A46); these may be secondary effects of the restructuring or sampling variation, and the evidence does not separate the two. [OCC035, OCC036, OCC037, OCC042, OCC043, OCC044, OCC015, OCC016, OCC041, DIR024, DIR045]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A26/A27/A28 are the same underlying steps as A19/A20/A21 under new anonymous labels; what the evidence does establish is that the executed structure differs — one exclusive alternative per trace became all three activities in every trace, with the A44-to-A29 distance growing from two to four steps. [OCC018, OCC025, REL019, LAG001]
