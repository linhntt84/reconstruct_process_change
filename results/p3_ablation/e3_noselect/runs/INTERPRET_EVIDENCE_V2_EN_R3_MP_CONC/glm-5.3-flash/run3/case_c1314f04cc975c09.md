### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment containing A25, A26 and A31 came to be executed repeatedly: earlier, each of the three ran exactly once per case in the fixed order A25 then A31, with A31 always followed by A20; in the later window they run about 3.5 times per case, A31 leads back to A25 in about 71 in 100 of its executions and proceeds to A20 in about 29 in 100, and A25/A31 are repeated or interleaved in about 68 in 100 cases. The exit share of about 29 in 100 matches the observed average of about 3.5 executions, and occurrence, direction, relation, lag and position indicators all change coherently, each difference exceeding about 995 in 1000 random re-splits and backed by all 200 cases in both windows. The evidence cannot show whether every case now repeats the segment a varying number of times or only a subset repeats it many times, since about 32 in 100 later cases still show the single A25-before-A31 pattern; A26's place in the repeated segment also rests only on its counts matching A25 and A31, as no directional or relational evidence covers A26.
