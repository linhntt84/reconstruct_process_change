### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural re-routing in which two activity groups exchanged the branches they sit in: the exclusive choice among A14, A16 and A19 moved from the late branch (previously A24 → one of A14/A16/A19 → A25) to the early branch (now A35 → one of A14/A16/A19 → A20), while the trio A15/A17/A18 — which still always occurs together and in no fixed internal order — moved from the early branch (previously A35 → A15/A17/A18 → A20) to the late branch (now A24 → A15/A17/A18 → A25). The change is shown directly by the successor changes at A24, A35, A14/A16/A19 and A15/A17/A18, and corroborated by the complete reversals of the between-group order relations, the opposing position shifts, and the changed A24→A25 and A35→A20 gaps; the relations involved are near-deterministic in both windows (order and lag shares of 1.0 on 200 traces per window), so sampling variation cannot plausibly explain them. The evidence cannot distinguish an exchange of the two groups between the A35- and A24-branches from any other reconfiguration producing the same successor and order changes, nor can it show whether the shifted choice shares (A14 up to about 44 in 100 traces) belong to the same change or are an independent routing shift.
