### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured: in the earlier window each trace executed exactly one of A14, A15 and A16 between A39 and A24, while in the later window every trace executes all three of A21, A22 and A23 in that slot, in varying order, before reaching A24. This is shown directly by the complete successor change at A39, the two activity groups appearing and disappearing in all traces, each new activity preceding A24 in every trace while A24 no longer co-occurs with the old ones, and the A39-to-A24 distance growing from two to four steps; A24's later position within traces is consistent with the longer block. [DIR030, OCC012, OCC013, OCC014, OCC019, OCC020, OCC021, REL019, REL021, REL024, REL026, LAG001, POS007]

Zero participation only means an activity was not observed, so the evidence cannot show whether A14, A15 and A16 became impossible or merely much rarer; and because the activity codes are anonymous, a pure relabelling of the same steps with a changed one-of-three versus all-three rule cannot be ruled out from the behaviour alone.
