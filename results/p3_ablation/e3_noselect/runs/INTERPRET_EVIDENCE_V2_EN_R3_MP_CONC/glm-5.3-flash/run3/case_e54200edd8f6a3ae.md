### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A03 was added to the execution of the process and placed between A07 and the group A02/A06/A10: in the later window every case runs A07 → A03, and A03 then routes to A02 (about 39 in 100), A06 (about 33) or A10 (about 29), closely matching the shares with which A07 previously reached these activities directly (about 33/32/36 in 100). The support is direct and consistent: A03's participation jumps from 0 to 200 of 200 traces; A07's successors collapse to A03 alone; all four A03 relations change from never co-occurring to always co-occurring with a single fixed order (A07 before A03; A03 before A02, A06 and A10); the A07→A02/A06/A10 distances shift exactly one step later; and downstream positions move later while A07 barely moves — each of these items has empirical strength about 0.995, i.e., larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR004, REL001, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that A03 was not executed there, so the evidence cannot distinguish a newly added activity from one that existed but was never triggered before; the evidence set also provides no screening baseline of items expected without change, so the judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the relational and positional items rather than a chance-count comparison. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is a broader re-routing around A07, but the preserved always-together co-occurrence and mixed order among A02, A06 and A10, together with the matching successor shares, make the insertion reading the one best supported by the data. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007]

Other differences: small redistributions of the outgoing shares of A02, A06 and A10 (a few points each), small shifts in the mixed relative order of A02/A06, A02/A10 and A06/A10 (about 4–8 points), and small lag changes among these activities; these are consistent with sampling variation or secondary effects of the insertion and are not part of the main hypothesis.
