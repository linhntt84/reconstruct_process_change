### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a change in the ordering constraints among A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A25, A26 and A32: in the later window these eight activities, each still executed exactly once per trace, follow the single fixed sequence A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32, whereas in the earlier window several of them appeared in both orders about equally (A22/A24, A20/A26, A23/A25) or mostly in the opposite order (A24 before A20 and A23; A26 before A23). The support is direct and consistent: order shares, positional gaps, successor profiles and positions all move together, each pair relation is backed by all 200 traces in both windows, and the core differences exceed those seen in about 99 in 100 random re-splits, so sampling variation is an unlikely explanation for the pattern as a whole. [REL027, REL032, REL037, LAG010, LAG011, LAG026, DIR020, POS006]

A limitation: the chain is reconstructed from pairwise relations, and the evidence cannot distinguish whether the earlier mixed orders came from one flexible rule or from several fixed variant orders that were replaced by a single variant — both look identical in these aggregates. [REL029, REL032, LAG012]
