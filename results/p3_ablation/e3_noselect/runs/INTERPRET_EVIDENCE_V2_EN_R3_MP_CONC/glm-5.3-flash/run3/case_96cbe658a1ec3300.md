### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the flow around the group A35, A36, A41, whose members have no fixed internal order: in the earlier window this group ran between A26 and A30, and in the later window it runs directly after A25 and before A27. A25 therefore feeds into the group instead of into A27, the group exits to A27 instead of A30, and A26 connects directly to A30, so A27 and A26 now sit later in each trace. [DIR025, DIR026, DIR035, DIR041, REL008, REL012, LAG001, LAG004, POS002, POS003]

The support is direct and coherent: successor profiles at A25, A26 and the group members changed as described, order relations flipped from about 98 in 100 to about 97–98 in 100 in the opposite direction, ordered pairs from A25 into the group and from the group into A27 went from about 1–2 of 200 traces to nearly all, while pairs from A26 into the group and from the group into A30 did the reverse, and the internal chain A27–A28–A29–A33–A34–A26 is unchanged. All involved activities occur in essentially every trace in both windows, so this is a reordering rather than activities or paths appearing or disappearing, and the key differences exceed about 199 of 200 random re-split comparisons; the agreeing items describe one underlying difference and are not independent confirmations. [LAG002, LAG005, LAG011, LAG012, DIR027, DIR028, DIR029, DIR033, DIR034, OCC024, OCC025, OCC027, OCC032, OCC033, REL001, POS007]

Competing explanation: the evidence cannot distinguish whether the group moved to before A27 or whether the segment A27 to A34 together with A26 moved to after the group — both descriptions fit the same data, so the direction of the move is not identifiable; no screening baseline is included, so the judgement rests on the size and coherence of the relational evidence rather than an item-count comparison. [REL008, REL012, LAG001, LAG004]

Other differences: A15 and A16 are repeated more often (mean occurrences per trace from about 1.6 to about 1.7, with roughly 40 in 100 traces containing repeats in both windows), A08 participates less often (about 21 to about 14 in 100 traces), A03's outgoing transitions shifted from A11 (about 38 to 28 in 100) towards A12 (about 28 to 40 in 100), and several optional activities show small participation shifts of four to eight percentage points; none of these is explained by the reordering.
