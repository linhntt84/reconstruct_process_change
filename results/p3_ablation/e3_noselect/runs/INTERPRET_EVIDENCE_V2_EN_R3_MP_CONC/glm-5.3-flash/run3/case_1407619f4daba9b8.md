### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment around A20, A21, A22, A23, A26 and the group A31–A32–A37 was restructured: the group, previously an optional alternative to A22 taken in about 46 in 100 cases between A21 and A26, became a mandatory block executed once in virtually every case between A20 and A23, while A22 became mandatory after A21, so that in the later window every case passes A20, the group (in any internal order), A23, A21, A22 and A26, whereas before each case took either A22 or the group after A21. The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation of all four activities rose from roughly half of cases to (nearly) all; the successor profiles of A20 and of the group changed completely; the pair relations flipped from alternatives (A22 and the group never together) to always together with a fixed order; and the A20–A23 distance grew from one to four steps. The evidence cannot rule out that about 1 in 200 later cases still follows a rare path without the full group, and it cannot distinguish a relocation of the group from closing the old branch and re-routing the group into the main path, as both look identical here.
