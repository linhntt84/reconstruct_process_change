### Observed differences

- Activity A01 disappeared from execution: it was present in all 200 earlier-window traces (about 1 occurrence per trace) but in none of the 200 later-window traces. Its positional profile collapsed with it. [OCC001, POS001]
- Consequently A01's outgoing flow vanished: earlier, A01 led to A07 in about 99 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions (A04 for the rest); later, A01 shows no observed outgoing transitions at all. [DIR001]
- A05's routing changed: earlier, about 51 in 100 of A05's outgoing transitions went to A01 and about 46 in 100 to A06; later, about 49 in 100 go to A07 (previously not an observed successor of A05) and about 45 in 100 still go to A06. [DIR004]
- The A05–A07 distance shortened: earlier, A07 typically followed A05 two positions later (about half of observed pairs, ~15 in 100 at four positions); later, A07 follows A05 one position later in about 40 in 100 pairs, while the two-position share dropped to about 4 in 100. [LAG002]
- Relations with A01 changed from universal co-occurrence to separation: earlier, A01 and A05 always occurred together (A05 first in 55 in 100 traces, interleaved/repeated in 45) and A01 always came before A07; later, A05 occurs without A01 in every trace and A07 occurs without A01 in every trace. The A05–A07 relationship itself is essentially unchanged (co-occurrence in all traces, A05 first in ~55–57 in 100). [REL001, REL002, REL003]
- Positions shifted consistently with a shortened path: A05's median position moved later (0.40 → 0.50 of trace length) and A07's moved earlier (0.80 → 0.75). [POS002, POS003]
- A05's own frequency and repetition behaviour, A06's participation, and the small routing shares at A02, A06 and A07 are essentially unchanged between the windows. [OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, DIR005, DIR006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 is no longer executed: earlier it ran in every case, typically after A05 and always before A07, so the flow ran A05 → A01 → A07; in the later window A01 is not observed at all, and cases instead proceed from A05 directly to A07 (A07 is now A05's most common successor at about 49 in 100 outgoing transitions, typically one position away). The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the participation drop from 100 in 100 to 0 in 100 traces across 200-trace windows, the replacement of A01 by A07 in A05's successor profile, the shortening of the A05→A07 gap from two positions to one, the change from co-occurrence to A05-only/A07-only relations, and the opposite position shifts of A05 (later) and A07 (earlier). Each of these differences is larger than roughly 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR004, LAG002, REL001, REL002, POS002, POS003]

Limitations: zero participation strictly means only that A01 was not observed in the later window, so a collapse to a rate too low to appear in 200 traces cannot be distinguished from complete removal; the evidence also cannot show whether this holds beyond the sampled period. Note also that several items describe the same underlying difference, so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, REL001]

Other differences: A04's outgoing shares shifted slightly (to A05 about 92 → 87 in 100, to A07 about 2 → 7 in 100, with the small edge to A01 gone along with A01), and minor redistributions appear at A02 (A04 about 89.5 → 86.5 in 100), A06 (A05 about 97 → 99 in 100) and A07 (A03 about 99 → 97.5 in 100), plus a slightly wider self-repetition gap for A05; these are small and consistent with ordinary sampling variation.
