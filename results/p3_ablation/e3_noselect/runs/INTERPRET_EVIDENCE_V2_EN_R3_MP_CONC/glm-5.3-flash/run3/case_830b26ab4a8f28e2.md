### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process no longer routes through A01: traces that previously ran from A03 or A05 into A01 and then on to A08, A09 or A10 now run from A03 or A05 directly into A08, A09 or A10, and A01 itself is no longer observed in execution. This rests on direct and mutually consistent relational evidence — the successor profiles of A03 and A05 switched from A01 to A01's former targets, co-occurrence of A01 with A03, A05, A08, A09 and A10 dropped to zero, and the positional gaps between predecessors and successors shortened — together with A01's complete disappearance from 200 of 200 earlier traces to 0 of 200 later traces, with the key items showing differences larger than almost all reference comparisons.
