- A01 was not observed before (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it occurs exactly once in every trace and is always the final event of the case. [OCC001, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural reorganisation involving A04, A05, A07 and A01: in the later window the optional detour A04 → A05 → A04 is no longer taken and A04 instead repeats directly (A04→A04 in about half of its outgoing transitions, with repeats one position apart instead of two), while a new closing activity A01 follows A07 at the end of every case. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS003, LAG001]

The support is direct and consistent: participation flips from 0 to 200 of 200 traces for A01 and from 93 to 0 for A05; the successor profiles of A04 and A07 change exactly where this hypothesis predicts; A01 and A04 now co-occur in every trace whereas A04 previously always occurred without A01; and A07 precedes A01 in every later trace. Sixteen of the twenty items show differences larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits, far more than the few items an unchanged process typically produces, and the occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators all describe the same underlying behaviour rather than independent phenomena. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR003, DIR005, REL001, REL003, LAG001]

Limitations: no screening baseline is included, so the expected number of chance qualifications cannot be checked directly; the evidence cannot rule out that A05 was effectively relabelled as repeated A04 rather than removed, since the A04 self-loop appears exactly where the A05 loop used to be, nor that A01 is a relabelled existing closing step. Position and lag results refer to event positions within the case, not clock time. [OCC003, DIR003, POS001, POS004]

Other differences: A04→A07 distances shifted slightly (lag 3 up to about 11 in 100 traces, lags 2 and 4 down), consistent with the shorter path after A05's disappearance but not independent proof of it; the minor routing shifts at A02/A03 and the A04–A07 order remain within chance variation. [LAG002, DIR001, DIR002, REL005]
