### Observed differences

- The order between the group A19/A20/A25 and the activities A26/A27/A28 flipped. In the earlier window, A26 ran before A19, A20 and A25 in about 30–36 in 100 traces while the reverse order occurred in at most about 1 in 200 traces; in the later window A19, A20 and A25 each run before A26 in about 29–35 in 100 traces and almost never after it. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a re-placement of the late block of the process: A19, A20 and A25, which in the earlier window ran after A26 and interleaved with A27/A28 (reached from them and leading on to A28/A32), now run early — entered from A45 directly or via A08, whose presence roughly doubled to about 30 in 100 traces — and lead almost always to A26, while A27 → A28 → A32 became a near-fixed late sequence. The order flip is observed directly in the relation indicators (A26 before A19/A20/A25 fell from about 30–36 in 100 traces to essentially zero, the reverse rising to about 29–35 in 100, with empirical strength about 0.99), the successor changes appear coherently on both ends of the rerouting (A45, A19, A20, A25, A27, A28, A08), and the position and gap indicators agree; participation of A19/A20/A25 itself stayed similar (about 31–37 in 100 traces in both windows), so the difference lies in order and wiring rather than in how often these activities run. Limitations: positions reflect event order, not clock time; several items describe the same underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations; the windows are finite samples and no screening baseline is included, so item counts cannot be compared against a chance expectation. [REL030, REL036, REL041, DIR045, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, DIR027, DIR028, DIR008, OCC008, OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG009]

Other differences: A18 is skipped more often (about 32→20 in 100 traces) while keeping its link to A45; the rare activities A02, A04, A06 and A10 occur at roughly half their earlier rates; A46 repeats more often and A44/A47 occur somewhat more often. These are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC018, DIR018, OCC002, OCC004, OCC006, OCC010, OCC041, OCC040, OCC042]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that a rare pre-existing variant with this early ordering simply became the dominant one rather than the arrangement having been re-wired; the earlier window shows the early order in at most about 1 in 200 traces, so a very rare earlier variant cannot be excluded. [REL030, REL036, REL041]
