### Observed differences

- The three mutually alternative activities A19, A20 and A25 (together running about once per case; pairwise co-occurrence in at most ~2% of traces) changed their neighbours completely. In the earlier window, about 85 in 100 of A45's observed outgoing transitions went directly to A26; in the later window, about 85 in 100 go to A19, A20 or A25 (roughly 27–29% each), and A26 is no longer observed as a direct target of A45. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative step formed by A19, A20 and A25 — one of which runs in essentially every case — is placed between A45 and A26 in the later window, whereas earlier it worked as an optional detour inside the A27–A28–A32 segment: A45 now reaches A26 only through one of A19/A20/A25, these activities lead on to A26 instead of back to A28/A32, and A27 → A28 → A32 run back-to-back without the detour. [DIR045, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, DIR027, DIR028, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011]

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: the successor changes are complementary (A45 lost A26 and gained A19/A20/A25; A19/A20/A25 lost A28/A32 and gained A26; A27 and A28 lost A19/A20/A25), the order flips with A26/A27 and the mixed-to-fixed orders with A28 match the same rerouting, the position shift of A19/A20/A25 from ~0.61 to ~0.32 agrees with it, and each of these items' difference is larger than in about 99 of 100 random re-splits of the pooled data. Every involved activity occurs in a substantial share of traces in both windows, so this is a rerouting of existing activities, not activities being added, removed or skipped. [DIR045, REL003, REL009, REL010, REL014, REL015, REL005, REL011, REL016, POS001, POS002, POS003, OCC019, OCC020, OCC025]

Limitations: the old detour pairs are nearly unobserved in the later window (1–3 traces each), so the evidence cannot distinguish a detour that was removed from one that merely became much rarer; and it cannot be ruled out that the A45 rerouting and the disappearance of the A27/A28 detours are two independent changes rather than one relocation, although the exact complementarity of the edge changes favours a single rerouting. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG005, DIR045]

Other differences: the A15–A16 loop repeated less often (mean occurrences per trace ~1.89 → ~1.58 for both activities, traces with repeats ~47% → ~38%, with A16's exits shifting from A15 towards A44), and A46's repeats fell slightly (~38% → ~31% of traces); small participation shifts in various directions (A21 down to 42%, A22 up to 58%, A06 up, A18/A41/A42 down, A39/A44 up) were also observed. These are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC015, OCC016, OCC040, DIR016, OCC021, OCC022, OCC006, OCC018, OCC036, OCC037, OCC034, OCC039]
