### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the flow between A02 and A07 was restructured: A01 was added as a new step that runs in every case inside the segment between A02 and A03 — always after A02 and before A03 and A07, but in no fixed order relative to A05 and A06 (both orders occur in similar proportions) — while A04 was dropped, so that A03 now leads directly to A07. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL006]

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: participation flips from 0 to 200 of 200 traces for A01 and from 200 to 0 for A04, a swing that sampling variation between 200-trace windows does not produce; A03's entire successor profile switches from A04 to A07; A02, A05 and A06 gain A01 as a successor (26–38 in 100 of their transitions) where none existed before; and the position and lag shifts match inserting one step before A03 and deleting one after it. All qualifying items reach an empirical strength of about 0.995. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR003, DIR002, DIR005, DIR006, POS001, POS002, LAG001, LAG004]

Limitations: the many agreeing items largely describe the same two underlying movements (the insertion of A01 and the disappearance of A04) rather than independent confirmations; the evidence cannot rule out that A01 already existed in the process but was simply not executed in the earlier window, and A04 not in the later one, since zero participation only means an activity was not observed; nor can it establish whether the addition and the removal were one coordinated modification or two separate ones. No screening baseline is included in the evidence, so the judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the items rather than a comparison with a chance expectation for their count. [OCC001, OCC002]

Other differences: none beyond the restructuring above; in particular, the relative order of A05 and A06 is essentially unchanged, with A05 first in about 50 in 100 cases in both windows. [REL012]
