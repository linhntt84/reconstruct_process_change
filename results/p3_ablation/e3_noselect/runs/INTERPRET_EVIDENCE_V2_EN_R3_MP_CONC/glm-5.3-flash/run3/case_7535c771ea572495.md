### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the later segment of the process involving A01, A03, A05, A06 and A07: in the earlier window traces ran A02 (with an optional A04→A02 repeat) → A06 → A01 → A03, ending at A03, whereas in the later window they run A02 → A05 → A06 → A07, ending at A07, with A01 and A03 no longer observed and A05 and A07 now present in every trace.
