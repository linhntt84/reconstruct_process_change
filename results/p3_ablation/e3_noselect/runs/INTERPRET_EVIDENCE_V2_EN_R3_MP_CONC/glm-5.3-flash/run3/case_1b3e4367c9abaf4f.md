### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural reorganisation with two consistent facets. First, A01 now executes once per case near the end, woven between the closing activities A15 and A16 without a fixed order (A01 precedes A15 in about 52 in 100 cases and follows it in about 48), reached from A05 and leading only to A15/A16, always after the start activities A02 and A12. Second, the start was consolidated: A02, previously optional (about 48 in 100 cases) and mutually exclusive with A06 and A12, now always executes first and leads to A06, while A06 and A12 — previously confined to the A02-free cases — now execute in every case. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC006, OCC012, DIR001, DIR002, DIR011, DIR012, POS001, REL001, REL008, REL012, REL015]

The support is direct and consistent: occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators all describe the same rewiring, and the participation jumps (A01 from 0 to 200 of 200 traces, A02 from 96 to 200, A06 and A12 from about 104 to 200) are far larger than differences arising between random re-splits of a stable process. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that A01 existed before but was executed in fewer than roughly 2 in 100 cases and so went unobserved; the earlier window strictly shows only non-observation. The exact slot of A01 relative to A15/A16 also remains undetermined, since both orders occur. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC006, OCC012]

Other differences: A11 now runs in about 53 in 100 cases, up from about 26 in 100, always together with A06; A05 and A14 repeat slightly less often (about 2.27 to 2.02 and 1.27 to 1.02 occurrences per trace). [OCC011, OCC005, OCC014, REL032]
