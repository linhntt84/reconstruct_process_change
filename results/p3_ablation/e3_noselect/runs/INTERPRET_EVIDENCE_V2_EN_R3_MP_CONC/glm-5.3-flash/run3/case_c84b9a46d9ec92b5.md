### Observed differences

- The sharpest difference concerns the three alternative activities A19, A20 and A25. In the earlier window each ran in roughly a third of the cases (A19 in 73 of 200, A20 in 62, A25 in 70) and almost never together (co-occurrence about 1 in 100 cases), so together they appeared in essentially every case. In the later window A19 appears in 1 of 200 cases and A20 and A25 in none. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, REL001, REL003, REL008]
- In the earlier window these three sat between A41 and A26: A41 preceded each of them in every case in which it ran (about 31–37 in 100 cases each), and each of them led to A26 in about 95–97 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions; A26 followed A41 two steps later in about 85 in 100 cases. In the later window A26 immediately follows A41 in about 86 in 100 cases, while A41 and A26 still occur in every case. [REL004, REL006, REL009, REL011, REL016, REL018, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, LAG003, POS005, POS007]
- A41's successor profile flipped accordingly: earlier, about 87 in 100 of its outgoing transitions went to A19 (about 29), A20 (about 26) or A25 (about 32), and none went directly to A26; in the later window about 86 in 100 go directly to A26. The transitions from A19, A20 and A25 to A26 are no longer observed because the activities themselves are (nearly) unobserved. [DIR041, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025]
- Relation items mirror this: pairs such as A20–A26 changed from usually co-occurring in a fixed order (co-occurrence about 31 in 100 cases, A20 first) to A26 occurring alone in 100 in 100 cases; the same flip appears for A19 and A25 against A26, A31 and A41. [REL004, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL016, REL017, REL018]
- Away from this, A15 and A16 (both in every case) repeated slightly more often in the later window: A15 averaged about 1.4 → 1.5 executions per case, with repeats in about 27 → 34 in 100 cases; A16 about 1.4 → 1.5, with repeats in about 27 → 33 in 100; A16's transitions to A40 fell from about 37 to 31 in 100. [OCC015, OCC016, DIR016]
- Many other activities show small participation shifts of 5–10 percentage points: A03, A11, A12 and A13 each rose from about 42 to 51 in 100 cases; A01, A05, A07 and A09 each fell from about 59 to 50; A06 rose (about 12 → 19), A10 fell (about 19 → 12), A17 rose (about 21 → 29); A21–A24 moved by 5–7 points in mixed directions. Successor mixes around A11–A14 and A05/A07/A09 shifted accordingly (for example, A12 now leads more to A13/A14 and less to A11). Individually these are modest and may partly reflect sampling variation. [OCC003, OCC011, OCC012, OCC013, OCC001, OCC005, OCC007, OCC009, OCC006, OCC010, OCC017, OCC020, OCC021, OCC022, OCC023, DIR003, DIR007, DIR009, DIR012, DIR013]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-way alternative step between A41 and A26 — one of A19, A20 or A25, previously executed in essentially every case (each in about a third of cases, nearly mutually exclusive) — is no longer executed in the later window: A41 now leads directly to A26 in about 86 in 100 of its executions, whereas earlier it first passed through one of the three, each of which led on to A26 in about 95+ in 100 of its transitions. A41 and A26 themselves still occur in every case, so the surrounding path remained and the intermediate step disappeared. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, DIR041, LAG003, REL004, REL006, REL009, REL011, REL016, REL018, POS005]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the successor change at A41 is observed on about 200 outgoing transitions in each window, the A41→A26 distance collapsed from two steps (about 85 in 100 cases) to one step (about 86 in 100), A19/A20/A25 fell from 62–73 to 0–1 of 200 cases with differences larger than essentially all random re-splits, and roughly a dozen relation items flip in the same direction. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the work of A19/A20/A25 was absorbed into another activity elsewhere rather than dropped; the evidence only shows that they no longer run between A41 and A26. [DIR041, LAG003, OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL016, REL017, REL018]

Other differences: a slight increase in repetition of A15 and A16, and broad small participation shifts across many activities (A03/A11/A12/A13 up, A01/A05/A07/A09 down, among others) that are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC015, OCC016, DIR016, OCC003, OCC011, OCC012, OCC013, OCC001, OCC005, OCC007, OCC009]
