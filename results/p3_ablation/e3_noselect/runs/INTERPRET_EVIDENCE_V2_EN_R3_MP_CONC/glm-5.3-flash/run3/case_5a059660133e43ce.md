### Observed differences

- **The direct A02 → A15 succession is replaced by a route through A06.** In the earlier window, A15 always came directly after A02 (about 26 transitions, matching all 26 A15 occurrences, roughly 12% of A02's outgoing transitions), and A06 also always followed A02 directly (about 27 of 27 occurrences) but then ended the trace: it sat at relative position 1.0 in every case, no outgoing transition from A06 was observed, and A06 and A15 never shared a trace. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the branch after A02 is rerouted so that A06 is executed between A02 and A15: previously A15 followed A02 directly and A06 was a terminal activity after A02, whereas in the later window the fixed sequence A02 → A06 → A15 occurs in about 16 in 100 traces, A06 has moved from the end of the trace to a mid-trace position, and A15 is observed only together with A06. The rerouting is shown directly by relational and positional evidence — A06 → A15 in 32 of 32 A06 occurrences versus none earlier (when A06 was always the last event), A06/A15 co-occurrence rising from 0 to about 16% with A06 always first, the A02 → A15 distance shifting from about one step to about two, and the direct A02 → A15 edge disappearing — while A06's predecessor stays A02 throughout, and the occurrence, direction, position and lag indicators all describe this same rerouting rather than isolated fluctuations. Limitations: the two windows are finite samples, so these sequences cannot be excluded from having existed at very low frequency earlier, and the indicators say nothing about behaviour outside the observed edges; the competing explanation that a stable process merely sampled rare variants differently is made unlikely by the all-or-nothing edge shares and the agreement across occurrence, direction, position and lag evidence, but it cannot be excluded with certainty. [DIR003, REL012, POS003, LAG005, DIR001]
