### Observed differences

- Two activities that appear nowhere in the earlier window occur in every trace of the later window: A01 and A02 each run exactly once per case (participation 0 of 200 traces before vs 200 of 200 after; mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, no repeats). Both differences exceed those seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002]
- In the later window, every observed outgoing transition of A01 goes to A04; A01 had no observed outgoing transitions before, simply because it did not occur. [DIR001]
- The direct entries into A04 from the preceding stage are gone in the later window: before, about 50 in 100 of A06's outgoing transitions went to A04 (the rest to A13) and about 50 in 100 of A13's went to A04 (the rest to A06); after, A06 sends about 48 in 100 to A01 (52 to A13) and A13 sends about 52 in 100 to A01 (48 to A06), with no observed transitions from either to A04. [DIR004, DIR007]
- A02 behaves as a new final step: before, A12 ended every trace (mean relative position 1.0, no observed outgoing transitions); in the later window A12 sits slightly earlier (mean about 0.94) and all of its outgoing transitions go to A02, which occupies the final position in every trace. [POS005, DIR006, POS002]
- Order relations changed accordingly: A01 and A02, which previously never co-occurred with A04, A06, A12 or A13 (the partner always appeared alone), now co-occur with each of them in every trace, with fixed order — A06 and A13 always before A01, A01 always before A12 and A02, and A06, A13, A12 always before A02; A01 clearly precedes A04 in about 49 in 100 later traces, with the rest classified as interleaved-or-repeated. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- Distances shifted by about one step exactly on the rerouted paths: A06→A04 and A13→A04, previously at lag 1 in about 20–21 of 100 cases, never occur at lag 1 in the later window and now appear mainly at lags 2–3; A06→A12 and A13→A12, previously at lag 2 in about 30–33 of 100 cases, never occur at lag 2 and now appear at lags 3–4. [LAG003, LAG005, LAG004, LAG006]
- Existing activities sit slightly earlier in the later traces (A04 mean 0.84→0.81; A06 0.73→0.65; A13 0.72→0.65), consistent with the two added activities lengthening every trace; these are event positions, not clock times. [POS003, POS004, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, two activities not observed in the earlier window — A01 and A02 — are executed exactly once in every case: A01 is inserted between the A06/A13 stage and A04, so the transitions that previously ran directly from A06 or A13 into A04 now pass through A01 (A06→A01→A04 and A13→A01→A04), and A02 is appended as a new final activity after A12, which was previously the last step of every trace. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR004, DIR007, DIR006, POS002, POS005]

Supporting signs: the participation change is maximal and well backed (0 vs 200 of 200 traces for both activities); the successor profiles change precisely on the affected edges while other routing stays stable (for example, A03's split to A07/A08 remains about 50/50 in both windows); the lag distributions shift by about one step only on paths that now include A01; and the pairwise order relations are mutually consistent. Note that many of these items reflect the same two underlying additions, so they form one coherent pattern rather than independent confirmations. [DIR002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, REL003, REL004, REL008]

Limitations: the evidence shows immediate successors and pairwise order, not a complete process model; the pair-level indicator labels about half of the later A01–A04 traces "interleaved or repeated", which matches A04's recurrence (A04 appears more than once in about 51 in 100 later traces), so the exact position of A01 relative to A04's repeats is not resolved. [REL002, OCC004]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of trace variants between the windows (only traces of a variant containing A01 and A02 appearing in the later sample); however, a change from 0 to 100 in 100 traces would itself be a substantial behavioural difference, so pure sampling noise is implausible at these supports. [OCC001, OCC002]

Other differences: several small shifts are unremarkable for random re-splits (empirical strength roughly 0.1–0.4) and are not part of the hypothesis: A03's mean occurrences per trace rose from about 4.06 to 4.24 with recurrence support from 0.76 to 0.79; A07 and A10 rose slightly (mean about 2.05→2.20, participation 0.69→0.71); minor routing redistributions at A04 (A11 vs A12) and A09 (A05/A06/A13); order shares between A04–A06, A04–A12, A04–A13 and A06–A13 moved by about 0.02; and small lag changes for A04→A04 and A04→A12.
