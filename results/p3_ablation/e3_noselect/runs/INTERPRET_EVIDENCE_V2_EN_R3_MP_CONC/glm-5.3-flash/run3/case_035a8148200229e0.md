### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the opening step of the process passed from A04 to A01: in the earlier window, about half of the cases began with A04, which always fed directly into A03, whereas in the later window A04 is not observed and A01 instead begins about half of the cases, likewise always feeding directly into A03. A01 and A04 never occur in the same case in either window, so they behave as alternatives occupying the same slot before A03, whose occurrence and position are unchanged. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL003]

The change is shown directly by relational, positional and successor evidence rather than by frequencies alone, and every item describing it exceeds all but about 1 in 200 random re-splits, with large shares on both sides (0 of 200 vs 99 of 200 traces) — not the profile of a rare variant appearing in one window. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003, POS001, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish whether A01 took over a role previously filled by A04 or whether an entry choice that previously led to A04 now leads to A01; also, absence of A04 in the later window means only that it was not observed, and the evidence file provides no screening baseline, so the judgement rests on the size, directness and coherence of the differences. [REL002, DIR003]

Other differences: the ordering around A05, A07 and A13 loosened — before, A07 was almost always directly followed by A13 and A13 mainly by A03; after, the reverse transition A13→A07 and a direct A07→A03 occur in a subset of cases, so A07 and A13 no longer follow a single fixed order everywhere, and both occur in slightly more cases. This mid-process reordering is separate from the entry change. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR010, OCC004, OCC009]

The small participation decreases for A02, A09, A10, A12, A14 and A15 and the minor routing redistributions are consistent with sampling variation between two 200-trace windows and are not part of the main hypothesis.
