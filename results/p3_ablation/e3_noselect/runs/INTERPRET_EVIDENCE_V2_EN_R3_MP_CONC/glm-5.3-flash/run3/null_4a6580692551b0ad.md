### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the two existing alternative paths between A31 and A32 are taken with different frequencies than before: in the later window the path through A35 and A36 runs in about 44 in 100 traces (previously about 52) and the path through A37 and A38 in about 56 in 100 (previously about 49). The support is direct and consistent: all six activities involved remain frequently observed in both windows (A31 and A32 in every trace, A35–A38 in about 44 to 57 in 100), the co-occurrence of A35 and of A36 with A31 and with A32 falls in lockstep from 0.52 to 0.44, the branch point A31 reroutes away from A36, no direct A31-to-A32 transition appears that would indicate one group bypassing the other, and the differences exceed almost all random re-splits of the pooled data. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that this shift is driven by the same upstream mix change that shifted the start-of-process paths, since the joint choice of the two branch points is not observed; with 200 traces per window, a coincidental sampling difference also cannot be fully excluded, though the agreement across occurrence, routing, relation and lag indicators makes it unlikely. [OCC031, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, DIR031, POS001, POS002]
