### Observed differences

- **A07 is no longer observed.** In the earlier window it appeared in about 53 in 100 traces (106 of 200), always exactly once per participating trace; in the later window it appears in none of the 200 traces, and its position distribution within traces is gone as well. [OCC005, POS003]
- **Flow that ran through A07 now bypasses it.** Earlier, of A03's outgoing transitions about 56% went to A05, about 39% to A07 and only about 5% directly to A06; in the later window no transitions to A07 are observed and about 52% go straight to A06 (about 49% to A05). A07's own outgoing flow — about 92 in 100 of its transitions led to A06 — is also no longer observed. [DIR002, DIR004]
- **A07's ties to its neighbours ceased.** Earlier it co-occurred with A03 in about 53 in 100 traces (A03 first in about 24, interleaved or repeated in about 29) and with A06 in about 53 in 100 (A07 first in about 20); in the later window it co-occurs with neither, while A03 and A06 each appear without it in about 52 in 100 traces. The small A06→A07 and A09→A07 transitions have likewise disappeared. [REL002, REL003, DIR003, DIR005]
- **The A03–A06 link persists, one step shorter.** The two still co-occur in about 52 in 100 traces in both windows, but their distance within a trace changed from almost exclusively even gaps (about 23 in 100 traces two positions apart, about 20 four apart, odd gaps negligible) to only odd gaps (about 19, 11 and 6 in 100 at one, three and five positions). [LAG002, REL001]
- **Repetitions became rarer while participation held steady.** Mean occurrences per trace fell for A03 (about 1.21 to 1.01), A06 (about 1.17 to 1.01), A05 (about 0.68 to 0.49) and A09 (about 0.64 to 0.49); among traces containing them, each activity ran about 2.2–2.3 times before versus about 1.9 times after, while the share of traces containing each stayed roughly flat (about 52–53 in 100 for A03 and A06, 26–29 for A05 and A09). [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC006]
- **Little else moved.** A02's split between A03 and A04 stayed near 53/47 versus 52/48, A04's and A10's participation was flat, and A06's position within traces was stable. [DIR001, OCC002, OCC007, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the step through A07 between A03 and A06 was dropped from execution: A07, which earlier ran in about half of the cases between A03 and A06, is not observed in the later window, and A03 now proceeds directly to A06 — a transition that earlier carried about 5 in 100 of A03's outgoing transitions and now carries about 52 in 100. The support is direct and mutually consistent: A07 fell from about 53 in 100 traces to 0 of 200 with an empirical strength of about 0.995, a difference sampling variation cannot plausibly produce at that participation level; A07's incoming and outgoing transitions vanished together with it; and the A03–A06 gap flipped from even to odd position distances, the pattern expected when one intermediate step between two activities is removed. [OCC005, DIR002, DIR004, REL002, REL003, LAG002]

Limitations: zero participation only establishes that A07 was not observed in the later window, so complete removal cannot be distinguished from a reduction to a very rarely taken path; the drop in repetitions of A03, A05, A06 and A09 coincides with the rerouting, but the evidence does not show that it is a consequence of it rather than a separate change. [OCC005, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC006]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out: the reduced repetition could reflect an independent change in how often the A06–A09 loop and similar cycles run, since that loop itself is still observed in the later window. [DIR003, DIR005]

Other differences:
- A03 occurs slightly later within traces than before (mean relative position about 0.43 versus about 0.48; a positional, not clock-time, shift). [POS001]
- Among A06's remaining successors, the split moved a few points from A09 (about 54% to 49%) toward A10 (about 43% to 52%). [DIR003]
