### Observed differences

- Activity A03 disappeared from the later window: it occurred exactly once in every earlier trace (participation 200 out of 200 traces, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace) but in none of the 200 later traces. [OCC001, POS002]
- Before, A02 always led to A03 — all of A02's observed outgoing transitions (100%) went to A03, and the two activities co-occurred in every trace with A02 first. In the later window, A02 still occurs in every trace, but never together with A03 (A02 appears alone in 100% of traces), and no outgoing transitions from A02 are observed at all. [DIR002, REL001]
- A02 now sits at the end of the trace: its position moved from a constant 0.8 to a constant 1.0 (the last position) across all traces, whereas A03 had held that final position in the earlier window. [POS001, POS002]
- Routing among the remaining activities is essentially unchanged: A01 still leads mainly to A04 (about 72 in 100 transitions) and A05 (about 24 in 100), A04 to A05 (about 76 in 100) or back to A01 (about 24 in 100), A05 to A06 (about 98 in 100) and A06 to A02 (about 98 in 100); all shifts are roughly 1–1.5 percentage points with low empirical strength (0.18–0.52), i.e., within what random re-splits of unchanged data produce. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the final step A03 is no longer executed: traces that previously ended with the sequence A02 → A03 now end at A02, which has become the last activity. This rests on A03's participation falling from all 200 earlier traces to none, the disappearance of A02's only observed outgoing edge (A02 → A03), the A02–A03 relation changing from always co-occurring in a fixed order to A02 appearing alone, and A02 moving to the final trace position. [OCC001, DIR002, REL001, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: the agreeing items largely describe one underlying difference (A03's disappearance) rather than independent confirmations, and zero participation strictly means only that A03 was not observed. The evidence cannot distinguish a removal of A03 from A03 still existing but never being taken; a chance non-observation after a 200-in-200 participation is rated highly unusual by the reference comparisons (empirical strength about 0.995), but it cannot be fully excluded. [OCC001, POS002, REL001]

Other differences: the small shifts in routing shares out of A01, A04, A05 and A06 (about 1–1.5 percentage points) are within normal sampling variation and are not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
