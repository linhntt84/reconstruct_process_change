- In the earlier window, the segment between A26 and A27 ran as one fixed chain, A26→A17→A15→A18→A19→A21→A20→A27: each of these steps carried all of the source's outgoing transitions, in each case across all 200 traces. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the fixed sequential ordering of the block involving A15, A17, A18, A19, A20 and A21 — entered from A26 and exiting to A27 — was relaxed into variable ordering. Earlier, the block always executed A17→A15→A18→A19→A21→A20; in the later window the two groups {A15, A17, A18} and {A19, A20, A21} interleave in both directions (for example, A19 precedes A18 in about 85 in 100 traces, previously never), the entry point splits between A17 and A19 (about 53/47), and the exit to A27 now also occurs directly after A18 in about half of A18's outgoing transitions. Since every involved activity still appears in every trace in both windows, this points to a change in ordering constraints and exit routing, not to activities being added, removed or made optional. [REL001, REL003, REL007, DIR012, DIR014, DIR016, DIR019, LAG001, LAG013, POS001, POS003, POS004]

The change is shown directly by relational evidence (order shares moving from 100/0 to mixed), by successor profiles turning from one deterministic target into several, and by lag distributions spreading from single fixed distances to ranges, with the positional shifts agreeing in direction; the differences are larger than those in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, and because all involved activities occur in all traces of both windows, rare-variant sampling cannot account for them. [REL001, REL007, DIR014, LAG003, POS004]

Limitations: the many agreeing items largely describe one underlying reordering, so they are not independent confirmations, and no chance-expectation baseline is provided for the screening as a whole. The evidence also cannot determine how the variable order is produced — for example genuine re-sequencing of the steps versus other activities being inserted between them, which would likewise lengthen step distances — nor whether the later window still contains a small set of fixed sequences rather than free ordering. [LAG001, LAG007, REL001]

Other differences: the mirrored participation shift (A01, A03, A04, A05 up from about 40 to 48 in 100 traces; A02, A06, A07, A08 down from about 60 to 52) is a plausible second change in how often existing branches are taken, but no relational or positional evidence covers these activities, so it remains weaker than the main hypothesis.
