### Observed differences

- In the later window, A06 and A08 occur more often per trace while their participation is essentially unchanged: A06 rises from about 0.85 to about 1.18 occurrences per trace (present in about 25 in 100 traces in both windows) and A08 from about 0.38 to about 0.64 (present in about 15 to 19 in 100). This points to more repetitions within the traces that reach them, not to wider use. [OCC006, OCC008]
- The routing around this pair shifted consistently: among A06's observed outgoing transitions, the share continuing to A08 rose from about 44 to 54 in 100 while the share going to A09 fell from about 53 to 45; A08 still returns to A06 in about 97–98 in 100 of its outgoing transitions in both windows. [DIR006, DIR008]
- The increase is specific to this cycle rather than a general lengthening of traces: A01, A03, A10 and A12 stay flat, with participation changes of about 2 in 100 or less. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC010, OCC012]
- Smaller and weaker differences: A01's outgoing transitions moved toward A04 (about 42 to 55 in 100) and away from A11 and A02; A11 and A02 each appear in slightly fewer traces (about 14 to 11 and 12 to 9 in 100); A07's outgoing transitions to A10 rose from about 33 to 63 in 100 and A11's outgoing mix changed markedly — the last two rest on only about 10–20 observations per window. [DIR001, OCC011, OCC002, DIR007, DIR011]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the cycle between A06 and A08 is executed more often per trace: traces that reach A06 pass through A08 more times before leaving the cycle, with A06 up from about 0.85 to about 1.18 and A08 from about 0.38 to about 0.64 occurrences per trace at flat participation, A06's exits shifting from A09 (about 53 to 45 in 100) toward A08 (about 44 to 54 in 100), and A08 returning to A06 almost always in both windows. [OCC006, OCC008, DIR006, DIR008]

Supporting signs: the occurrence items measure the repetition change directly, the direction items agree with it, the differences are larger than in most random re-splits of the same data (strengths of roughly 0.8–0.9), and the flat occurrence of A01, A03, A10 and A12 argues against a generic trace-length effect. [OCC006, OCC008, DIR006, DIR008, OCC001, OCC003, OCC010, OCC012]

Limitations: A08's profile rests on about 30–38 traces per window, the evidence gives no baseline for how many items would qualify by chance among the 24 tested, and the A06→A08 versus A06→A09 share shift may be a by-product of the extra repetitions rather than an independent routing change; an important competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is ordinary window-to-window sampling variation, which occasionally produces differences of this size in a stable process. [OCC008, DIR006]

Other differences: A01 routes more of its outgoing transitions to A04 and fewer to A11 and A02, A11 and A02 appear slightly less often, A04 occurs slightly more often per trace (about 1.00 to 1.08), and A07 and A11 show outgoing-profile shifts based on few observations; none of these is part of the main hypothesis.
