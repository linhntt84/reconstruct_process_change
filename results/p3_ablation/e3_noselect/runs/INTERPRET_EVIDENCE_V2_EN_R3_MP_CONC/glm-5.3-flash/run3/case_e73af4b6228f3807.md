### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new activity, A10, was introduced into the sequence between A05 and A07. In the earlier window this segment ran as A05 → A11 → A07 in about half of the traces and as A05 → A01 → A07 in most of the rest; in the later window A10 runs in about 47 in 100 traces and sits between A05 and A07 — before A11 in about 22 in 100 traces and after A11 in about 26 in 100 — so the earlier chains are extended by one step in roughly half of the cases. [OCC008, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, REL002, REL004, REL006]

The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent, though they largely describe the same traces rather than independent confirmations: A10 moves from absent in all 200 earlier traces to 94 of 200 later traces while four new edges (A05→A10, A11→A10, A10→A11, A10→A07) appear and the direct edges A11→A07 and A05→A11 lose about half of their shares, and each step distance around A11 and between A05 and A07 grows by one in about 44–53 in 100 cases — the pattern expected when a single activity is inserted into a chain. [OCC008, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004]

The evidence cannot rule out that A10 already occurred at a very low rate before and was only captured by the later sample, and because A10 appears in both orders relative to A11 the data do not pin down a single fixed insertion slot. [OCC008, REL006]

Other differences: three further activities not seen in the earlier window (A06, A09, A02) now occur rarely, in each case as new successors of A07 [DIR003, OCC002, OCC005, OCC007]; the small participation shifts for A01, A03, A04, A08 and A11 and the modest tightening of A07's position spread are individually unremarkable and may accompany the same behaviour. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC006, OCC009, POS002]
