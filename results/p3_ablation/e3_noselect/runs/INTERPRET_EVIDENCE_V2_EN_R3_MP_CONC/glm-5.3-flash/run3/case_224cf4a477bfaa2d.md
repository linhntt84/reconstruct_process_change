### Observed differences

- **A01 appears as a new, once-per-case step.** In the earlier window, A01 was not observed in any of the 200 traces; in the later window it occurs exactly once in every trace (200 of 200) and sits early in the case (median normalized position 0.2, mean about 0.29). [OCC001, POS001]
- **A02's handover is rerouted.** Earlier, A02 passed directly to A04 in about 87 in 100 of its outgoing transitions; in the later window it passes to A01 in about 54 in 100 and to A04 in about 44 in 100, and A04 now follows A02 either directly (about 44 in 100) or one step later (about 47 in 100) instead of almost always immediately. [DIR002, LAG002]
- **A01 is wired into the early flow.** Earlier, A01 had no observed transitions; later, A04 sends about 39 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A01 (an edge not seen before), A01 leads on to A04 (about 46 in 100), A07 (about 17), A03 (about 16) and A10 (about 10), and small shares of A03, A05, A07, A08 and A10 transitions (roughly 1–5 in 100) now also go to A01. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR007, DIR008, DIR010]
- **A09 is no longer executed.** Earlier, A09 ran in 90 of 200 traces (about 45 in 100), always as the final event (mean position about 1.0), fed by A06 (all 90 observed outgoing transitions of A06 went to A09); in the later window A09 appears in none of the 200 traces, and pairs such as A03–A09, A04–A09, A06–A09, A07–A09 and A10–A09 changed from co-occurring in about 45 in 100 traces to never co-occurring. [OCC007, POS006, DIR006, REL006, REL011, REL016, REL020, REL023, REL025, REL027]
- **A06 has become the closing step.** Earlier, A06 sat at mean position about 0.8 and always passed flow on; in the later window it sits at the end (mean about 0.99) and outgoing transitions from A06 are almost never observed (3, versus 90 earlier). [POS004, DIR006]
- **Downstream activities shifted about one event position later.** Mean positions moved for A03 (0.51→0.68), A10 (0.46→0.68), A07 (0.45→0.52) and A04 (0.24→0.31), and lags from A02 and from A04 to A03, A06, A07 and A10 each increased by about one step (e.g., A02→A06 from lag 4 in about 99 in 100 cases to lag 5 in about 97 in 100; A04→A06 from lag 3 in about 89 in 100 to lags 3–4). These are event-position, not clock-time, shifts. [POS002, POS003, POS005, POS007, LAG001, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012]
- **New relations around A01.** In the later window A01 co-occurs with A02 in every trace, always after A02; with A04 in every trace but in both orders (A01 first in about 55 in 100, A04 first in about 45 — no fixed order); and with A03, A06, A07 and A10 whenever those run, with A01 first in about 90–98 in 100 of those co-occurrences. Earlier, A01 co-occurred with none of them. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A01 was introduced as a mandatory early step and A09 was dropped as the closing step. Earlier, cases ran A02 → A04 and, in about 45 in 100 cases, ended with A06 → A09; in the later window every case contains exactly one A01 — directly after A02 in about half of the cases (A02→A01 about 54 in 100) and otherwise after A04 — while A09 is no longer observed and A06 has become the final activity. The roughly one-position delay of A03, A04, A06, A07 and A10, together with the one-step increase in lags from A02 and A04 to these activities, is consistent with a step inserted early in the flow, and the new edges A02→A01 and A04→A01, A01's outgoing edges to A04/A07/A03/A10, and the loss of the A06→A09 handover show the rewiring directly. [OCC001, OCC007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR006, POS001, POS004, POS006, LAG002, LAG003, LAG010, REL001, REL003]

Supporting signs: the participation changes are large and direct (A01 from 0 to 200 of 200 traces; A09 from 90 to 0 of 200), all five indicator families — occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag — show the same coherent picture, and the items rest on 90–200 observations with empirical strengths of about 0.995, so sampling fluctuation between two 200-trace windows cannot plausibly explain the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC007, DIR002, POS001, POS006, REL001]

Limitations: zero participation means only that A09 was not observed in the later window, so a routing change that stops cases from reaching A09 cannot be distinguished from its removal; the exact insertion point of A01 is not fully pinned down because A01 and A04 occur in both orders; A06's later outgoing profile rests on only 3 observations; and no screening baseline was provided against which to compare the number of qualifying items. [OCC007, REL003, DIR006]

Other differences: participation of A03, A05, A06, A07, A08, A10 and A11 wobbles by about 2.5 percentage points between the windows (e.g., A05 in 110 versus 105 traces), and the A03–A10 order mix shifts slightly; these differences are small and are also produced by random re-splittings of an unchanged process (empirical strengths about 0.31–0.63), so they are not part of the hypothesis. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC005, OCC006, OCC008, OCC009, REL017]
