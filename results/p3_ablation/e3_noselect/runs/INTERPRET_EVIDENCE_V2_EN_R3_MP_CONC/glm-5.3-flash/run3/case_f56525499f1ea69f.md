### Observed differences

- Six activities — A33, A35 and A36 as one trio, A37, A38 and A39 as the other — each ran in about 49–52 in 100 traces in the earlier window but in about 99 in 100 in the later window (for example A33: 97 of 200 traces before, 197 after). [OCC030, OCC031, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, OCC035]

- In the earlier window the two trios were mutually exclusive: cross pairs such as A33–A37 or A35–A39 never shared a trace (co-occurrence 0), so a case executed one trio or the other. In the later window these pairs occur together in about 98 in 100 traces, with the A33/A35/A36 trio before the A37/A38/A39 trio in about 97–98 of 100; within each trio the activities still appear in both orders, before and after. [REL052, REL054, REL058, REL061, REL064]

- The always-present neighbours moved in step: A14, A15, A16 and A30 (upstream) and A31 (downstream) co-occurred with the six trio activities in only about half of the traces before and in about 99 in 100 after, with the order preserved — A14/A15/A16/A30 before the trio activities, A31 after them. [OCC015, OCC016, OCC038, REL012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the process executes both trios where it previously executed one of them: A33/A35/A36 and A37/A38/A39, mutually exclusive alternatives in the earlier window (each in about half the traces, never together), now run together in nearly every trace — A33/A35/A36 first, then A37/A38/A39, in the segment between A30 and A31, with no fixed internal order within either trio. The relational evidence shows this directly (cross-trio co-occurrence rising from 0 to about 98 in 100 with a stable cross-trio order), corroborated by the participation jump of all six activities, the rerouted direct successions at A30 and at A33/A35/A36, and position and gap shifts in exactly the expected direction. Limitations: the many pairwise items are views of one underlying difference, not independent confirmations; the evidence cannot show whether the earlier either-or routing was removed or still exists but was taken in only about 2–5 of 200 later traces; and a different case mix between the two windows cannot be ruled out, although random re-splits of these windows essentially never produce differences of this size. [REL054, REL061, OCC030, OCC033, DIR030, DIR033, POS007, LAG023, LAG027]

Other differences:
- A04's participation fell from about 30 to about 14 in 100 traces and its successors changed; the evidence does not tie this to the trio change. [OCC004, DIR004, POS001]
- The rare activities A02, A06, A08 and A10 occur about twice to four times as often, on small observation counts. [OCC002, OCC006, OCC008, OCC010]
- A15 and A16 repeat slightly more often and A43 somewhat less. [OCC015, OCC016, OCC038]
