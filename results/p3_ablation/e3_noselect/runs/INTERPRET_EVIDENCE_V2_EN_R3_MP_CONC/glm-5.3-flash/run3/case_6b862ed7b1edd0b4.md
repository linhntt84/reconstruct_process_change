### Observed differences

- The sequence A19 → A25 → A20, which runs after A41 and before A26, was executed in about 48 in 100 cases in the earlier window (each of the three activities in 94–96 of 200 traces) and in essentially every case in the later window (199 of 200 traces each). [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, POS005, POS006, POS007]
- Accordingly, A41's outgoing transitions shifted: earlier, about 45 in 100 went directly to A26 and about 37 in 100 to A19; in the later window about 96 in 100 go to A19, and the direct step to A26 is nearly unobserved (about 1 in 200). [DIR041]
- The order and spacing within the block did not change, only its frequency: A19 precedes A25 in about 98 in 100 observed pairs, A25 precedes A20 in about 99 in 100, and A20 precedes A26 in about 98 in 100, typically one to two steps apart; pairwise co-occurrence rose from about 47 in 100 cases to essentially all cases, and the block now co-occurs with the always-present A14, A15, A16 and A41 in nearly every trace. [REL026, REL027, REL030, REL028, REL011, REL016, REL021, REL029, LAG027, LAG028, LAG033, LAG031, LAG038]
- A06 became much more frequent: about 24 in 100 traces earlier versus about 73 in 100 later; it now sits earlier in the case (typical relative position about 0.50 vs 0.25), A15 leads to it in about 40 in 100 of A15's outgoing transitions (about 1 in 100 earlier), and A06 is followed by A16 within one step in about 97 in 100 of the observed pairs (136 pairs later vs 16 earlier). [OCC006, POS001, DIR015, LAG003, DIR006]
- Several low-frequency activities lost participation: A02 about 12.5 → 6.5 in 100, A04 about 17.5 → 7.5, A08 about 20 → 7.5, A10 about 13.5 → 7, and A18 about 30.5 → 24; the later-window counts behind these are small (13–48 of 200 traces). [OCC002, OCC004, OCC008, OCC010, OCC018]
- A44 and A45 now repeat more often within a case (mean occurrences about 0.76 → 0.92 and 0.76 → 0.89, recurrence support roughly doubling), and A41 also repeats slightly more (about 1.6 → 1.7 per trace). [OCC043, OCC044, OCC041]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch executing A19, A25 and A20 in sequence — entered from A41 and followed by A26 — changed from optional to executed in nearly every case: earlier, roughly half of the cases bypassed it via a direct A41 → A26 transition, while in the later window the block runs in about 199 of 200 traces and the bypass is practically unobserved. This rests on direct participation counts (95–96 vs 199–200 of 200 traces for each activity), the successor profile of A41 flipping from mixed to almost exclusively A19, and an unchanged internal order and spacing (A19 → A25 → A20 → A26 at one-to-two step gaps) with pairwise co-occurrence rising from about 47 in 100 cases to essentially all cases. [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, DIR041, REL026, REL027, REL030, LAG027, LAG028, LAG033, LAG038, POS005, POS006, POS007]

Limitation: with 200 traces per window, a bypass still taken in roughly 1 in 100 cases could escape observation, so the evidence shows the bypass became at least very rare, not that it was removed, and the evidence cannot show why this happened. A competing reading — that the windows simply sampled different mixes of pre-existing variants — is not credible for this block, because participation roughly doubled to a ceiling with large supports on both sides, but it cannot be excluded for the smaller shifts reported below. [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, DIR041]

Other differences: A06 became far more frequent (about 24 → 73 in 100 traces) and is now typically reached from A15 and immediately followed by A16 — a participation and placement change in a different part of the process that the evidence does not link to the mandatory block. Participation of A02, A04, A08 and A10 roughly halved, and A44/A45 together with A41 now repeat somewhat more often; these rest on fewer observations, and chance sampling differences cannot be ruled out for them. [OCC006, DIR015, LAG003, OCC002, OCC004, OCC008, OCC010, OCC043, OCC044, OCC041]
