### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the repeated alternation between A15 and A16 — each is the other's most common successor — is executed fewer times per case in the later window: both activities still run in every case, but each is executed about 0.2 times less per case, the share of cases with a repeat falls from about 44 to 36 in 100, and the edge shares shift consistently, with the A15→A16 continuation and the A16→A15 return losing about 5 and 8 in 100 transitions to the exits towards A08 and towards A39/A18/A17. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the occurrence and recurrence drops match the fall in observed outgoing transitions (about 365 to 320 for each activity), the entry into the pair from A14 is unchanged at about 97 in 100 transitions, so the reduction arises inside the pair rather than from fewer cases reaching it, and every item in this cluster exceeds about 86–94 of 100 random re-splits. The main limitation is that each window holds only 200 cases and the evidence carries no screening baseline, so ordinary window-to-window sampling variation cannot be fully excluded as a contributor to the observed sizes.
