### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the control flow was restructured by adding two activities and dropping one: A02 was placed as an early, once-per-case step between A05/A13 and A12, so the former direct A05 → A12 and A13 → A12 successions now run through A02; A01 took over the flow position A09 had held, fed by A16 and leading mainly to A03; and A09 is no longer observed. The participation differences are direct and large (A02: 0 of 200 traces before versus 173 of 200 after; A01: 0 versus 117; A09: 108 versus 0), the new successions and precedences are observed relationally, and the shift of A12's lag behind A05/A13 from one event to two or more matches a placement between them; each key difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC008, DIR002, DIR005, DIR013, DIR016, REL017, LAG014, LAG041]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 is a relabelled A09 rather than a genuinely new activity, since both occupy the same neighbourhood (A16 upstream, A03 downstream); the evidence also cannot distinguish a modification of one process from a shift in the mix of process variants, and it says nothing about why the change occurred. [DIR009, DIR001, DIR016]

Other differences: A14 became much more frequent and now repeats, A17 now occurs twice per case, and the late block was rewired so that A14 and A17 succeed each other where both previously followed mainly A08; A05 participation dropped slightly. These are not part of the main hypothesis above. The before-shares for A14's and A17's outgoing transitions rest on only about 34 and 26 transitions, so those previous routing shares are uncertain, and because many items describe the same trace-level behaviour from different angles, the agreeing items corroborate but do not independently confirm one another. [OCC013, OCC016, DIR014, DIR017, DIR008, OCC004]
