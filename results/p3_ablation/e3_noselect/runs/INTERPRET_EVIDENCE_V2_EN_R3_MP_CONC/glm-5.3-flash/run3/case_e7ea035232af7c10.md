### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the exclusive either/or relation between A27 and the trio A36, A37, A42 changed so that both are now executed in essentially every case, and that the trio now runs earlier in the case — directly after A25 and before A28 and A26 — while A27 kept its position between A26 and A31. In the earlier window a case ran A25 → A28 → … → A26 and then either A27 (about 54 in 100 cases) or the trio in mixed order (about 46 in 100), followed by A31; in the later window essentially every case runs A25 → A36/A37/A42 (mixed order) → A28 → … → A26 → A27 → A31. [OCC025, OCC031, OCC032, OCC037, REL008, REL009, REL013, REL014, REL017, REL020, DIR025, DIR026, LAG001, LAG004, POS006]

The change is shown directly by relational and positional evidence, not only by frequencies: co-occurrence flips from about 0 to about 99.5 in 100 for A27 with each trio member, the trio's order relative to A26 and A28 reverses (about 46 → about 99 in 100), the successor profiles of A25, A26 and the trio change accordingly, and the lag and position shifts are those expected if the trio now runs between A25 and A28. [REL002, REL008, REL012, REL013, REL017, DIR025, DIR026, LAG001, LAG004, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

Limitations: the evidence provides no screening baseline, so the judgement rests on the size and coherence of the differences rather than on a comparison with a chance expectation; and with two single 200-case windows, the evidence cannot rule out that the later window reflects a different mix of case types rather than a modified process. Items describing the same traces (occurrence, relation, direction, position and lag on the same activities) are not independent confirmations. [OCC025, REL014, LAG001]

Other differences: the routing-share shifts around A21–A24, A31 and A32, the small participation shifts (A17, A19, A22 up; A20, A21, A24 down) and the small repetition changes (A15/A16 up; A44, A48, A49 down) are not part of the main hypothesis and are individually weaker, some resting on fewer than about 25 observations per window.
