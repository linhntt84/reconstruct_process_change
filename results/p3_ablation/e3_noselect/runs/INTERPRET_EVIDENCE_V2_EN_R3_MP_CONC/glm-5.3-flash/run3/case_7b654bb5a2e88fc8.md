### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the group of three alternative activities A14, A15 and A20 — exactly one of which executes per case — was relocated: in the earlier window it lay between A40 and A21 (A40 chose one of the three, which then always led to A21), whereas in the later window A40 leads straight to A21 and the chosen alternative sits in the later segment, entered after A22 or A23 and leading to A23 or A27. [DIR010, DIR011, DIR016, DIR018, DIR019, DIR029, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL014, REL015, REL016]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor changes appear on both sides of the block, the step distances change exactly as this placement implies (A40→A21 from lag 2 to lag 1, A22→A27 from lag 2 to lag 3, each in all 200 traces per window), the order flips with A21/A22, becomes mixed with A23 and stays the same with A40/A27, and all of these items reach an empirical strength of about 0.995 — patterns that hold uniformly in 200 of 200 traces per window are not what random re-splits of an unchanged process produce. [DIR029, LAG004, LAG002, REL003, REL005, REL006, POS001]

Limitations: the indicator families summarise the same underlying trace changes rather than independent confirmations, positions refer to event positions rather than clock time, and the evidence cannot show whether the three activities kept their former role — only that their order and adjacency changed. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the shift in which alternative is chosen (A14 less often, A20 more often) is a separate change accompanying the relocation rather than part of it. [POS001, OCC013, OCC019]
