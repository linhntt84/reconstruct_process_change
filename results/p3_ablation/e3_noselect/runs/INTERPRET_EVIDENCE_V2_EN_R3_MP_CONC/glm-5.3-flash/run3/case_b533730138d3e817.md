### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 now runs as a new final step after A06 in every case: in the earlier window cases ended with A06 (present in all 200 traces at the final position, with no observed outgoing transitions and no A01 anywhere), whereas in the later window every case contains A01 exactly once at the final position, A06 always precedes A01, and A06 → A01 is A06's only observed outgoing transition. [OCC001, DIR002, POS001, POS002, REL001]

The change is shown directly by relational and directional evidence (A06 before A01 in 100 in 100 later traces; the new A06 → A01 transition), and is corroborated by A01's participation jumping from 0 to 200 of 200 traces and by A06's position shifting earlier exactly where the new step follows it; all five items have empirical strength about 0.995, larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits. [OCC001, DIR002, POS001, POS002, REL001]

Limitations: the five items describe one underlying difference, so they corroborate rather than independently confirm it; zero participation in the earlier window strictly means A01 was not observed there, not that it was provably absent; and positions reflect event order, not clock time, so nothing can be said about timing. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 already occurred before the change point but was not recorded in the earlier window, although its appearance in 200 of 200 later traces makes a rare-variant sampling artefact implausible. [OCC001, REL001]

Other differences: the small shifts around A02 and A03 (slightly fewer A02 repetitions and A03 participations, and a marginal rebalancing of A02's exits towards A03) fall within the range produced by random re-splits and are not part of this hypothesis. [OCC002, OCC003, DIR001]
