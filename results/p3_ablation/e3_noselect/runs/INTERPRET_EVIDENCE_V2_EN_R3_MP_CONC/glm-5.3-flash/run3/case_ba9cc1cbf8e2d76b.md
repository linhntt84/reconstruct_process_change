### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a new step chosen among three alternatives — A19, A20 or A25 — is inserted into every case between A27 and A29: earlier the flow ran A27 → A28 → A29 with A28 directly after A27 (about 97 in 100) and A29 directly after A28 (about 98 in 100); in the later window each case additionally executes exactly one of the three (A19 about 29 in 100 cases, A20 about 41, A25 about 30), placed after A27 and before A29, before A28 in roughly 55 in 100 of the cases and after it in about 44, which lengthens the A27→A29 span from two to three steps. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the three activities go from unobserved to 29–41 in 100 traces with the strongest empirical values in the evidence, their order relative to A27 and A29 is uniform, the outgoing profiles of A27 and A28 shift exactly as an insertion predicts, and all three lag distributions move by about one step. Limitations: "absent earlier" strictly means "not observed in that window", the evidence cannot show how the choice among the three alternatives is made, and about 7 in 100 later traces show an even longer A27→A29 span that a single insertion does not fully explain. The competing explanation that these are rare variants surfacing by sampling is implausible for the insertion itself (three activities moving from 0 to 29–41 in 100 traces), but the evidence cannot establish whether the rebalancing after A42 and the shifts in the A01/A05/A07/A09 group belong to the same underlying modification or are independent of it.
