### Observed differences

- Activity A01 is the starkest difference: in the earlier window it never appears (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it appears in about 47 in 100 traces (94 of 200), roughly once per trace on average, occurring more than once in about 23 in 100 traces, and it sits in the early part of the trace (median normalized position about 0.34). [OCC001, POS001]
- Predecessors rerouted to A01. Earlier, A03 sent about 53% of its outgoing transitions to A04 and none to A01; later, about 42% go to A01 and only about 10% to A04. A11 earlier sent about 96% to A04; later about 76% go to A01 and about 22% to A04. A10 earlier sent about 47% to A04; later about 25% go to A01 and about 13% to A04. [DIR003, DIR011, DIR010]
- A01 hands control back mainly to A04 (about 76% of its outgoing transitions), with smaller shares to A13 (about 10%) and A11 (about 7%). Conversely, A04, A13 and A06 now lead to A01 in about 10%, 11% and 6% of their outgoing transitions, respectively. [DIR001, DIR004, DIR013, DIR006]
- The A01–A04 relation changed from never co-occurring (A04 without A01 in about 55 in 100 traces) to co-occurring in about 47 in 100, with A01 before A04 in about 20 in 100 and mixed or repeated order in about 23 in 100. A01 also now co-occurs with A03 (about 27 in 100), A10 (about 20) and A11 (about 23), which never happened earlier. [REL002, REL001, REL003, REL004]
- Gaps between existing activity pairs shifted about one position later: A03→A04 typically 1 step earlier vs 2 steps later; A10→A04 and A11→A04 likewise 1 → 2; A03→A11 and A10→A11 3 → 4; the self-repetition gaps of A11 and A04 moved from about 3 to about 4–5 steps. A04's own position also moved later (mean 0.39 → 0.44). [LAG001, LAG007, LAG009, LAG002, LAG008, LAG011, LAG004, POS003]
- Smaller participation shifts: A02 and A09 each rise from about 46% to about 53% of traces; A05 and A12 each fall from about 55% to about 47%; A04 and A13 occur somewhat less (mean about 1.10 → 0.96 occurrences per trace, participation about 55% → 47%). [OCC002, OCC009, OCC005, OCC012, OCC004, OCC013]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 is a new activity placed between A03/A10/A11 and A04: earlier, A03, A10 and A11 led mostly directly to A04, while later they hand control to A01 in a large share of cases and A01 leads back to A04 in about three quarters of its transitions. This is shown directly by A01's appearance, its outgoing profile, the rerouting of three predecessors, the emergence of co-occurrence with A01, and the consistent one-step stretching of gaps between existing pairs; the old direct edges still occur at reduced shares, which fits a placement between existing steps rather than a replacement of A04. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR003, DIR010, DIR011, REL002, LAG001, LAG007, LAG009]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly establishes only that A01 was not observed there, and the evidence cannot rule out that A01 is a relabelled existing activity rather than a genuinely new one; the placement is also only partially identified, since A01 additionally receives transitions from A04, A13 and A06 in a minority of cases. [OCC001, DIR004, DIR013, DIR006]

Other differences: the participation shifts in A02 and A09 (up) and A05 and A12 (down), the lower occurrence of A04 and A13, and a modest rerouting at A07 (share of A07's outgoing going to A09 up from about 42% to about 49%) are not part of this hypothesis; their sizes leave open whether they are consequences of the A01 change or sampling variation between the windows. [OCC002, OCC009, OCC005, OCC012, OCC004, OCC013, DIR007]
