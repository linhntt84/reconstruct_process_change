### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace ran the fixed sequence A36 → A14 → A20 → A15 → A21: all of A36's outgoing transitions led to A14, the three middle activities each appeared exactly once in all 200 traces in that order, and A21 followed four steps after A36. In the later window, A14, A20 and A15 each appear in only about 53 in 100 traces, and they vanish together: in about 47 in 100 traces none of the three occurs, while A36 and A21 still occur in every trace. [OCC013, OCC014, OCC019, REL001, REL002, REL005, DIR024, LAG001, POS004, POS005]

- The traces without the block take a shortcut: in the earlier window all of A36's outgoing transitions went to A14, whereas in the later window about 53 in 100 still go to A14 and about 47 in 100 go directly to A21; accordingly, the A36→A21 pair, previously always four steps apart, now occurs at distance 1 in about 47 in 100 traces and at distance 4 in the remaining 53. [DIR024, LAG001]

- The block was not moved: when A14, A20 or A15 does occur in the later window, its relative position is essentially unchanged (A14 mean about 0.33 in both windows, A20 about 0.37, A15 about 0.40). A21 remains in every trace but sits slightly earlier (mean relative position about 0.43 before, 0.40 after), and A36 sits slightly later (about 0.30 before, 0.32 after), which fits traces becoming shorter when the block is skipped. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

- Away from this block, routing after A26 shifted toward the A30/A31 branch: A26's outgoing transitions went to A31 in about 18 in 100 cases before and about 27 in 100 after, and to A33 in about 33 before versus 25 after; A30 and A31 now appear in about 52 in 100 traces (up from about 42) and A32 and A33 in about 49 (down from about 59); A30's own split between A27 and A31 moved from roughly 42/58 to 51/49. All six activities remain well observed in both windows, so only the shares between existing alternatives moved. [DIR016, DIR019, DIR020, OCC020, OCC021, OCC022, OCC023]

- Smaller shifts: A38 occurs in about 61 in 100 traces (down from about 70) with fewer repetitions (mean about 1.0 to 0.8 per trace), A37 routes relatively more to A39 (about 44 to 51 in 100) and A38 more to A41 (about 56 to 65 in 100); A18 appears somewhat more often (about 46 to 53 in 100 traces) and A19 less often (about 54 to 47), with A17's outgoing transitions concentrating on A26 (about 51 to 63 in 100) and A19's shifting toward A17 (about 25 to 35 in 100). [OCC027, DIR025, DIR026, OCC017, OCC018, DIR011, DIR013]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the block of activities A14, A20 and A15 — executed between A36 and A21 — became optional: in about 47 in 100 traces the whole block is skipped and A36 is followed directly by A21, while in the remaining traces the original sequence A36 → A14 → A20 → A15 → A21 runs unchanged. The change is shown directly by A36's outgoing transitions (all to A14 before; split about 53/47 between A14 and A21 after) and by the new distance-1 A36→A21 pair beside the unchanged distance-4 connection; occurrence, relation, direction and position indicators all show the same roughly 47/53 split, and the block's internal order and positions are untouched, so this is a skip rather than a rearrangement. The main limitation is that the two windows are finite samples: a drop of this size is far outside what random re-splits of the pooled traces produce (fewer than 1 in 200 reference comparisons reach it), but the evidence cannot distinguish the block having become optional within one process from the later window mixing in a case type that never executed the block. [DIR024, LAG001, OCC013, OCC014, OCC019, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL005, REL008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Other differences: the routing shift after A26 toward A30/A31 and away from A32/A33 is a second difference; since every involved activity stays well observed in both windows and only the shares move, it is consistent with existing alternative paths being taken with different frequencies, though a sampling difference between the two windows cannot be ruled out. The smaller shifts around A37–A41 (A38 less frequent, A37 routing more to A39, A38 more to A41) and A16–A19 (A18 up, A19 down, A17 routing more to A26, A19 more to A17) are of similarly ambiguous size.
