### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that execution of the paths through A07 ceased in the later window. In the earlier window A07 ran in about 23 in 100 cases, typically reached from A01 or A10 and followed by A03 or A13; in the later window A07 is not observed in any of the 200 traces, its co-occurrences with A03 and A10 drop to zero, and the transitions from A01 and A10 that previously led to A07 now flow mainly to A03 and A13 (and, from A10, more often directly to A01). This is shown directly by participation, position and outgoing-transition evidence with empirical strength near 0.99, and consistently across the relational items. The main limitation is that zero participation only establishes that A07 was not observed: a path still executed very rarely cannot be ruled out, although a fall from 46 to 0 of 200 traces is unlikely if behaviour were unchanged, and the evidence cannot distinguish a never-taken path from one removed from the process. [OCC006, POS002, DIR006, REL001, REL003, DIR001, DIR009]

Other differences: A04 and A08 now appear in fewer traces (about 55 to 46 in 100) while still repeating about twice per trace when present; A02 and A09 appear in more traces (about 23 to 29 in 100), with A02 now occasionally followed by A01 or A10 instead of only A09; A13, A11 and A06 participate slightly less often; and A03 tends to follow A10 more immediately than before. These shifts are moderate (empirical strength roughly 0.6 to 0.8), and the identical profiles of the A02/A09 and A04/A08 pairs suggest their items describe the same traces rather than independent changes, so they may be sampling variation or side effects of the A07 change that this evidence cannot separate.
