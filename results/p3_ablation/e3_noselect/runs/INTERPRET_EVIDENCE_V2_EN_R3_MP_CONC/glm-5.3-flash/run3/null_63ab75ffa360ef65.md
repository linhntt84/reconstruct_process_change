### Observed differences

- In the later window, the A15–A16 cycle repeated less often within cases: both activities still ran in every case (participation 100% in both windows), but mean executions per case fell from about 1.7 to about 1.55 for each, and the share of cases with a repeated execution fell from about 40% to about 33% for A15 and from about 40% to about 34% for A16. [OCC015, OCC016]
- The mechanism is visible at A16's exit choice: continuations back to A15 fell from about 40% to about 35% of A16's outgoing transitions, while exits to A41 rose from about 30% to about 34% and to A18 from about 12% to about 16%; the A15→A16 transition itself stayed nearly universal (about 98% before and after), and A18 in turn ran in about 26 of 100 later cases versus about 22 before. [DIR015, DIR016, OCC018]
- Routing in the A43–A46 region moved toward the A43–A44 exchange: after A43, continuations to A44 rose from about 43% to about 49% while those to A45 fell from about 55% to about 49%; after A44, returns to A43 rose from about 35% to about 40% while exits to A47 fell from about 63% to about 56%. [DIR043, DIR044]
- In the same region, A44 ran in about 65 of 100 later cases (about 59 before) and about 0.9 times per case (about 0.8 before), while A45 and A46 each ran in about 62–63 of 100 cases (about 66 before) and about 0.87–0.9 times per case (about 0.95 before). [OCC043, OCC044, OCC045]
- After A14, the branch choice shifted toward A15 (about 35% to about 43%) at the expense of the continuation via A04 (about 64% to about 57%); A04 ran in about 59 of 100 later cases versus about 67 before. [DIR014, OCC004]
- Smaller share shifts of roughly 5–11 percentage points appear at other decision points, for example after A01 toward A05, after A03 toward A11, after A12 toward A13 and after A23 toward A21. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR012, DIR023]
- The successor profiles of rarely executed activities (A02, A06, A08, A10, A48) changed almost completely, but they rest on only about 8–18 observed outgoing transitions per window (A48: 1 before, 7 after) and carry little weight. [DIR002, DIR006, DIR008, DIR010, DIR048]
- Outside these spots, participation stayed essentially stable, with most occurrence differences of only a few percentage points (for example A14, A20, A25 and A47 within about 2 in 100 cases). [OCC014, OCC020, OCC025, OCC046]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the A15–A16 cycle was repeated less often within individual cases: after A16, continuations back to A15 lost about 5 percentage points of share (about 40% to about 35%) in favour of exits to A41 and A18, so A15 and A16 each averaged about 1.55 executions per case instead of about 1.7, and the share of cases repeating the pair fell from about 40% to about 33%, while both activities still ran in every case. [OCC015, OCC016, DIR016]

The support is direct and consistent: the occurrence drop is identical for the two coupled activities, is mirrored in the recurrence measure, matches the observed shift in A16's exit choice, and shows downstream in A18's higher participation; participation of 100% in both windows rules out a rare-variant artefact, and the differences exceed about 80–92% of random re-splits of the same data. [OCC015, OCC016, DIR016, OCC018]

Limitations: each window holds only 200 cases and no screening baseline is provided, so the number of flagged indicators cannot be compared with what an unchanged process would produce; a sampling fluctuation in how often the loop's second iteration falls into the later sample remains a competing explanation, and the evidence cannot show whether the shift originated at A16's exit choice or further upstream. [OCC015, DIR016]

Other differences:
- Routing in the A43–A46 region shifted coherently toward the A43–A44 exchange and away from the A45–A46 branch, with A44 executed more often and A45/A46 slightly less often; all involved paths were observed in substantial shares in both windows, so this is a change in how often existing paths are taken. [DIR043, DIR044, OCC043, OCC044, OCC045]
- After A14, cases moved from the branch via A04 to a direct continuation to A15, with A04 participation falling from about 67 to about 59 of 100 cases. [DIR014, OCC004]
- Smaller routing shifts occurred at other decision points (A01, A03, A12, A23), each of modest size. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR012, DIR023]
- Almost completely changed successor profiles for rare activities (A02, A06, A08, A10, A48) are weak signs based on few observations, not reliable evidence of structural change. [DIR002, DIR006, DIR008, DIR010, DIR048]
