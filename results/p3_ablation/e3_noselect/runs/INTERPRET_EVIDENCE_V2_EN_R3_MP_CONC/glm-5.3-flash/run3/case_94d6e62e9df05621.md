### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A07 and A08 changed from being executed together to being executed as alternatives: in the earlier window they always co-occurred (about 53 in 100 traces, in either order and with no fixed sequence between them), while in the later window each appears only on traces without the other, they are never seen together, and neither hands over to the other — both now proceed mainly to A01. Supporting signs: the co-occurrence drop from about 53 in 100 traces to zero is observed directly by the relation indicator and is larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits; it is consistent with the roughly halved participation of both activities, with the disappearance of the mutual A07→A08 and A08→A07 transitions, and with A01's outgoing share moving from A07 to A08. Limitations: the later direction profiles for A07 and A08 rest on only 18 and 17 outgoing transitions, so a rare residual handover between them cannot be excluded; the participation and position items partly reflect the same traces as the relation item and are not independent confirmations; and no chance-expectation baseline was provided, so the judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the relational evidence. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that a small share of later cases still follows the earlier together-pattern below what this sample would reveal.
