### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: the block A26–A27–A28, which executed together in every earlier trace between A44 and A29, is no longer executed, and in its place exactly one of the three alternatives A19, A20 or A21 runs per later trace, with A44 before it and A29 after it, shortening the A44→A29 distance from 4–5 to 2–3 steps. The signs are direct and mutually consistent across occurrence, relation, direction, lag and position indicators: participation of A26/A27/A28 fell from 200/200 to about 3/200 traces while A19/A20/A21 rose from 2–4 to 60–75 traces; co-occurrence of the old block with A29 and A44 collapsed from about 100% to 0–2%; A44's successors switched from the block to the alternatives; and the lag and position shifts match this picture, with key items showing empirical strengths near 0.99, far beyond what random re-splits of these samples produce. [OCC025, OCC026, OCC027, OCC018, OCC019, OCC020, REL046, REL048, REL049, REL057, REL058, REL022, REL031, DIR044, DIR019, DIR020, DIR021, LAG014, POS009]

The evidence cannot rule out that A26/A27/A28 still execute but have become extremely rare rather than being dropped, since near-zero participation only means they were not observed; nor can it show whether A19/A20/A21 are genuinely distinct steps or the old steps under different anonymous codes, and the earlier profiles of A19/A20/A21 rest on only 2–4 traces. [OCC025, OCC026, OCC027, OCC018, OCC019, OCC020, DIR019, DIR020, DIR021]
