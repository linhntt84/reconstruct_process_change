### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. All shifts are small on their own scales (at most about 9 in 100 traces for participation and about 14 in 100 for successor shares), and even the most coherent pattern — a slight rerouting between the two orders of A37 and A38 after A26 — rests on mutually dependent indicators whose empirical strengths (about 0.84–0.93) stay within the range that random re-splits of unchanged windows produce [DIR025, DIR036, DIR037].
