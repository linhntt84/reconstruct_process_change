### Observed differences

- The starkest difference concerns the segment between A41 and A26. In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 each ran in about a third of the cases (about 36, 31 and 35 in 100), essentially exactly one of the three per case, always after A41 and before A26; in the later window none of the three is observed (0, 1 and 0 of 200 cases), and their co-occurrence with A26 fell from roughly 30–36 in 100 cases to zero. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative steps A19, A20 and A25, which in the earlier window followed A41 as a one-of-three choice before A26, are no longer executed in the later window, so that A41 is now followed directly by A26. This rests on direct relational evidence — the complete change of A41's successor profile from the three alternatives to A26 and the A41-to-A26 distance shrinking from two steps to one — together with the consistent disappearance of all three activities and of their co-occurrence with A26 [DIR041, LAG006, OCC016, OCC017, OCC022, REL018]. The main limitation is that zero participation strictly establishes only that the activities were not exercised in the later sample; the evidence cannot distinguish a permanent removal of this branch from a branch that simply was not taken during this period, and it says nothing about why the flow was rerouted.

Other differences: the split between the A01 route and the A03 route evened out from about 59/41 to about 50/50, A12 connects to A14 directly more often, A24 gained against A23 and connects to A31 directly more often, and A15/A16 repeat slightly more often; these routing and repetition shifts are not part of the main hypothesis, and with two 200-case windows part of them could still be sampling variation [OCC001, OCC002, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011, DIR012, LAG001, DIR024, LAG004, OCC013, OCC014].
