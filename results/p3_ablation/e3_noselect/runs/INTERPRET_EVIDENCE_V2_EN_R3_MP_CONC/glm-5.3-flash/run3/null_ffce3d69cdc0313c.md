### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the execution order of A31 and A32 reversed within the sub-path entered from A27 and closed at A28: in the earlier window the dominant sequence was A27 → A32 → A31 → A28, and in the later window A27 → A31 → A32 → A28. The support is direct and coherent: the pairwise order shares flipped, the one-step adjacency pattern flipped in exactly the matching way, the successor profiles of A27, A31 and A32 redistributed consistently, and both activities' positions tightened — while their participation stayed at about 45 in 100 traces in both windows, speaking against the change being an activity becoming optional, mandatory or skipped. Limitations: both orders still occur in both windows (the dominant order covers roughly two-thirds of co-occurrence cases before and after), so this is a reversal of the dominant order rather than the introduction of a strict sequence; the relational items describe one underlying reordering rather than independent confirmations; and no screening baseline was supplied, so the judgement rests on the size and coherence of the differences. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a changed mix of allowed orders (different proportions of the two orderings) rather than a repositioning of the two activities; on this data the two are behaviourally indistinguishable [REL005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, DIR020, DIR023, DIR024, POS003, POS004, OCC015, OCC016]
