### Observed differences

- The clearest change is a frequency exchange between A01 and A13. In the earlier window A01 appeared in about 66 of 100 traces with about 2.2 occurrences per trace, and repeated within the same trace in 45% of traces; in the later window these fell to about 28 of 100 traces, about 0.6 occurrences per trace and 18% repetition. A13 moved the opposite way: from about 23 to about 61 of 100 traces, from about 0.4 to about 2.1 occurrences per trace, and from 12% to about 44% repetition. [OCC001, OCC013]
- The two activities also exchanged their typical placement: A01's median position moved from about 44% to about 82% of the trace length (later), while A13's moved from about 82% to about 32% (earlier). [POS001, POS007]
- Their observed successors exchanged: earlier, A01 was followed mainly by A08 (about 48% of its outgoing transitions), A15 (about 26%) and A07 (about 26%), whereas in the later window A01 was followed only by A06; A13 shows the mirror image — earlier followed only by A06, later by A08 (about 45%), A15 (about 28%) and A07 (about 28%). [DIR001, DIR010]
- Predecessors switched accordingly: A06, which earlier led to A13 in about 47% of its outgoing transitions, now leads to A01 in about 54% (A14 roughly unchanged at about half); A02, A04, A07 and A15, which earlier sent roughly 20–50% of their outgoing transitions to A01, now send similar shares to A13. [DIR005, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, DIR011]
- Co-occurrence relations flipped in the same direction: A01 with A08 fell from about 66 to about 17 of 100 traces while A13 with A08 rose from about 17 to about 61; the same flip holds with A02 (41%→14% for A01 vs 14%→39% for A13), A07 (43%→11% vs 11%→39%) and A15 (43%→11% vs 11%→39%). A01 and A13 themselves flipped from usually occurring separately with A01 present (A01-only about 49 of 100 traces) to separately with A13 present (A13-only about 44). A01 is now bound to the A06 loop: A01-without-A06 fell from about 34 of 100 traces to 0. [REL005, REL026, REL001, REL012, REL004, REL024, REL007, REL028, REL006, REL003]
- Typical distances exchanged as well: the lag from A02, A04, A07 or A15 to A01 moved from about 1–2 steps to about 4–5 steps, while the lag to A13 moved from about 4–5 steps to about 1–2; the A08-to-activity lag profiles exchanged (A08→A01 was concentrated at 3 steps, now A08→A13 is), the A01→A06 distance shortened (dominant lag 2→1 step) while A13→A06 lengthened (1→2 or more steps), and even the self-repeat gaps exchanged (A01 about 3→2 steps, A13 about 2→3). The later-window samples on the A01 side are small (11–15 pairs). [LAG003, LAG011, LAG020, LAG037, LAG009, LAG017, LAG026, LAG043, LAG028, LAG033, LAG001, LAG036, LAG002, LAG035]
- Smaller shifts outside this exchange: A05's mean occurrences per trace fell from about 2.3 to about 1.9 at unchanged participation (about 67 of 100 traces), and A09 and A12 show similar mean-occurrence drops (about 2.4→1.9 and 1.2→0.9) with slightly higher participation. [OCC005, OCC009, OCC012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 and A13 exchanged the positions they occupy in the process. In the earlier window, A01 was an early, often repeated activity — present in about 66 of 100 traces, about 2.2 occurrences per trace, median position about 44% into the trace — followed mainly by A08, A15 and A07 and reached from A02, A04, A07 and A15, while A13 was a late, rarer activity (about 23 of 100 traces, median position about 82%) whose only observed successor was A06, with A06 leading back to A13 in about 47% of its outgoing transitions. In the later window the roles are exchanged: A13 runs early and often (about 61 of 100 traces, about 2.1 occurrences, median position about 32%) with the same successors and predecessors, while A01 runs late and rarely (about 28 of 100 traces, about 0.6 occurrences, median position about 82%), followed only by A06, which now leads to A01 in about 54% of its outgoing transitions. [OCC001, OCC013, POS001, POS007, DIR001, DIR010, DIR005, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, DIR011]

The exchange is visible directly and consistently across occurrence, position, direction, relation and lag indicators, and the profiles mirror each other closely (for example, A01's earlier successor shares of A08 48%/A15 26%/A07 26% reappear as A13's later 45%/28%/28%); the key differences are larger than those in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled traces. Note that most of these items describe facets of one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: the exchange is not exactly one-for-one — A01's new late role is somewhat more frequent (about 0.6 occurrences, 28 of 100 traces) than A13's old one (about 0.4, 23 of 100) — several later-window lag profiles rest on only 11–15 pairs, the evidence shows immediate successors and positions rather than the full path structure, and no chance-expectation baseline is included, so the judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the mirrored profiles. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two activity labels were exchanged in recording rather than the activities themselves being re-routed; the two are behaviourally indistinguishable here. [DIR001, DIR010, POS001, POS007, REL005, REL026, LAG003, LAG009, LAG001, LAG036, OCC001, OCC013]

Other differences: A05's mean occurrences per trace fell from about 2.3 to about 1.9 at unchanged participation; A09 and A12 show similar drops (about 2.4→1.9 and 1.2→0.9) with slightly higher participation; A02 and A04 occur slightly less often per trace (about 1.1→0.9); A07 and A15 now occur slightly earlier (median position about 44%→33% of the trace). These shifts are small and could reflect sampling variation between the two windows. [OCC005, OCC009, OCC012, OCC002, OCC004, POS005, POS008]
