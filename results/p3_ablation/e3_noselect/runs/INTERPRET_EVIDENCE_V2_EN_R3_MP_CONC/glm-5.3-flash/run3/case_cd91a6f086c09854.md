### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of three activities A30, A31 and A36 — executed in varying order among themselves — and the activity A22 exchanged their places in the sequence, with A21 and A25 marking the block's new surroundings. Before, the flow ran from A20 into the A30/A31/A36 block and then to A22, while A21 was followed immediately by A25; in the later window, A20 is followed directly by A22, and the block sits between A21 and A25, which now follows the block instead of A21 directly. This is shown directly by the successor changes at A20, A21, A30, A31 and A36, by the near-complete order flips between the block and A22/A21 with co-occurrence unchanged at 100 in 100, by the opposite distance changes (A20→A22 from 4 to 1 positions, A21→A25 from 1 to 4), and by the matching position shifts, with differences larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the same data. [DIR014, DIR015, DIR018, DIR019, DIR024, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG005, POS002, POS003, POS005, POS006, POS007]

Limitations: the rerouting is observed only through the edges of A20, A21 and the block members; the outgoing edges of A22 and A25 are not part of the evidence, so their exact continuations are inferred rather than observed. The later-window lag profiles pairing A20 or A21 with block members rest on only 2 of 200 traces each, the several indicator families partly re-describe the same relocation (so their agreement is not independent confirmation), and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample a shifted mix of pre-existing variants rather than a repositioning applied from the boundary onward. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG009, LAG012]
