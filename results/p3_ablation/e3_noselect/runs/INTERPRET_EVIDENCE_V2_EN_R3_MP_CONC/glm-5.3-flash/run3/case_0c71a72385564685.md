### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19 and A25 were not observed in any of the 200 traces and A20 in only 1; in the later window they occur in about 38, 30 and 35 in 100 traces respectively, each roughly a third of the way into the trace (mean relative position about 0.36–0.37) [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, POS001, POS002, POS003].
- In the later window, A41's outgoing transitions go mainly to these three activities — about 33 in 100 to A19, 30 to A25 and 24 to A20 — whereas earlier about 82 in 100 went directly to A26, which now receives only about 1–2 in 100; accordingly, the A41→A26 distance moved from directly adjacent in about 82 in 100 of the earlier pairs to two events apart in about 83 in 100 of the later pairs (about 14 in 100 three apart, about 1 in 100 adjacent) [DIR041, LAG002].
- The new activities sit between A41 and A26: A41 precedes A19, A20 or A25 in nearly every trace containing them (about 37.5, 28 and 33 per 100 traces), each of the three precedes A26 in nearly all of its occurrences (about 37.5, 28.5 and 32.5 per 100 traces), and A19, A25 and A20 lead on directly to A26 in about 95, 90 and 95 in 100 of their observed outgoing transitions; A20 typically follows A41 immediately (one event apart in about 79 in 100 of the observed pairs) [REL004, REL007, REL009, REL003, REL006, REL008, DIR019, DIR025, DIR020, LAG001].
- The three new activities almost never occur together (about 1, 1.5 and 0.5 in 100 traces for A19 with A20, A19 with A25 and A20 with A25), so they behave as alternatives rather than as a fixed sequence [REL001, REL002, REL005].
- A26 still occurred in every trace in both windows; its mean relative position moved slightly later, from about 0.37 to about 0.40 of the trace [OCC025, POS004].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a choice among three mutually exclusive activities — A19, A20 and A25 — takes place between A41 and A26: A41 is now followed by one of the three (about 33, 30 and 24 in 100 of its outgoing transitions), each of the three follows A41 and precedes A26 in nearly all of its occurrences and leads directly to A26 in about 90–95 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, whereas earlier A41 led directly to A26 in about 82 in 100 of the observed pairs; the three almost never co-occur, and A26 remained present in every trace while moving slightly later [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, DIR041, LAG002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL001, REL002, REL005, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, POS004, OCC025].

The main supports are the jump in participation from 0–1 to 59–76 traces out of 200, the relational and adjacency items that place the three activities directly between A41 and A26, and the consistency across occurrence, position, relation, adjacency and direction indicators. Limitations: most items describe one underlying difference and are not independent confirmations; the evidence includes no chance-expectation baseline, so the judgement rests on directness and consistency; and it cannot show what determines which of the three alternatives is taken. A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the three activities existed earlier at a very low frequency (below roughly 1–2 in 100 traces) and rose in frequency rather than being entirely new; the apparent loss of direct A02/A04/A06/A08/A10 → A26 edges, a consistent secondary sign, rests on only about 30–44 observed outgoing transitions each, so the full scope of the rerouting into A26 is uncertain [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, DIR002, DIR004, DIR006, DIR008, DIR010].

Other differences: A39 and A40 participate less often and A40's transitions shifted from A39 toward A41 [OCC034, OCC035, DIR040]; routing shares between existing activities shifted in the A35–A38 cluster (for example after A31, A35 fell from about 29% to 17% in favour of A36 and A37) and A21 shifted from A31 toward A23 and A24 [DIR031, DIR035, DIR036, DIR021]; small participation shifts of a few percentage points appear for several other activities, for example A18 up from 20% to 27.5% [OCC017, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC012, OCC013, OCC021].
