### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window existing alternative paths were taken with different frequencies, while the activities involved and their connections stayed the same. After A43, the branch to A45 lost share to the branch to A44 (about 54:45 in 100 of A43's outgoing transitions earlier, reversed to about 45:54 later), so A44 appeared and repeated more often while A45 and the downstream A46 appeared less often; the same kind of reversal, each of roughly nine percentage points, appears after A32 (toward A36/A37, away from A38/A39), between A22 and A23, and inside the loop over A01, A05, A07, A09 and A14, where A01→A05 and A05→A07 became the dominant follow-ups while A07→A05 halved. [DIR043, DIR032, DIR001, DIR005, DIR007, OCC022, OCC023, OCC035, OCC036, OCC037, OCC038, OCC043, OCC044, OCC045]

Supporting signs: the routing changes are observed directly in the successor shares of A43, A05 and A07, each difference larger than in about 95–98 of 100 random re-splits of unchanged behaviour, and the participation changes mirror them with almost constant combined totals between the alternatives (A44+A45: 256 vs 257; A22+A23: 202 vs 201; A36–A39: 400 vs 401) — the signature of a shift between alternatives rather than of a rare variant. Every involved activity is observed in roughly 20–70 of 100 traces in both windows, and apart from one trace containing A26 no activity appeared or disappeared and no established successor set changed, so the data do not show activities being added, removed, bypassed or swapped. These items describe overlapping trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [DIR043, DIR005, DIR007, OCC022, OCC023, OCC035, OCC038, OCC043, OCC044, OCC045, OCC026]

Limitations: the evidence carries no screening baseline, so the number of qualifying items cannot be compared with the number expected without a change; the shifts are modest (about nine percentage points) and occur at several separate points, so the evidence cannot show whether they form one coordinated change or several independent ones. The main competing explanation — ordinary sampling fluctuation across the many tested activities and edges — is made unlikely by the mirrored, mutually consistent patterns but cannot be fully excluded. [OCC022, OCC023, DIR043]
