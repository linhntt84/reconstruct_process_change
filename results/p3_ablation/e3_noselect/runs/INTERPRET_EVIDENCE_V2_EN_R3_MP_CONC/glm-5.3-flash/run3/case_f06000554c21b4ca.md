- The main difference concerns the group of activities A35, A36 and A41, which run in a mixed order among themselves in both windows. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the A35/A36/A41 group was relocated: in the earlier window it ran between A26 and A30 (entered from A26, exiting to A30), and in the later window it runs directly after A25 and hands over to A27, while A26 proceeds straight to A30 — in effect, the group and the A27…A26 segment swapped their order between A25 and A30. This is shown directly by the near-complete reversal of six order relations, each measured on all 200 traces per window [REL008, REL009, REL010, REL012, REL013, REL014], by the matching successor changes at A25, A26 and the group [DIR025, DIR026, DIR035, DIR036, DIR041], by the step-distance changes [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG010], and by the opposite position shifts of the group versus A27/A26 with A25 and A30 fixed [POS002, POS003, POS005, POS006, POS007]. Because every involved activity keeps full participation, the group's internal mixed order is unchanged and the surrounding chain is intact, this is a change in ordering and placement, not a change in activity presence or in how often existing paths are taken.

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that this arose as two independent reroutings (A25 gaining an edge into the group and A26 gaining a direct edge to A30) rather than one relocation of the group; both produce the same observed order [DIR025, DIR026]. Positions and distances are measured in event order, so no statement about clock timing is possible.

Other differences: A15 and A16 repeat more often per trace while remaining mandatory [OCC015, OCC016]; participation shifted for some rarer activities (A08 up to about 13 in 100, A04 down to about 8, A18 down to about 21) and among A30's alternatives (A23 up, A24 down) [OCC004, OCC008, OCC018, OCC022, OCC023]; A03's exits moved from A11 toward A12 [DIR003]. These lie outside the main hypothesis, and several rest on few traces, so sampling differences remain a plausible explanation for them.
