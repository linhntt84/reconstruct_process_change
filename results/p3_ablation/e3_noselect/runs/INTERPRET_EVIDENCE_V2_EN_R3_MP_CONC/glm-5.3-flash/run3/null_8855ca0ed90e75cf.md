### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The mirrored participation shifts of about 3–7 percentage points (entry branches around A01/A05/A07/A09 versus A03/A11/A12/A13, alternatives A32/A33 versus A34/A35, route A42/A43 versus A41) are of the size that random re-splits of these 200-trace windows regularly produce, and the routing profiles with the largest dissimilarities rest on only 4–25 observed outgoing transitions, so a small real change in path shares or ordering cannot be separated from sampling fluctuation with this evidence [OCC001, OCC008, OCC011, OCC029, DIR002, DIR008, DIR045].
