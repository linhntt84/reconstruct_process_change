### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The three most distinctive signals (A07 additionally observed leading to A05, A09 no longer observed leading to A02, and A03 leading more often to A09) each rest on only 13–40 observed transitions, and three elevated direction profiles among ten is roughly what random re-splits of an unchanged process produce, so sampling variation remains a complete explanation and no screening baseline is provided to compare against.
