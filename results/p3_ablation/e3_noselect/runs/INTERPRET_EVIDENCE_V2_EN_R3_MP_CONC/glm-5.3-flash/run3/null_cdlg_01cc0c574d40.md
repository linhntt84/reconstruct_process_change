### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. All differences are small on their own scales — occurrence means shift by at most about 0.6 occurrences per trace with participation unchanged, and routing shares by at most about 5 in 100 transitions — and even the largest difference falls within the range that random re-splits of unchanged data produce, so ordinary sampling variation between two 200-trace windows can account for the whole pattern.
