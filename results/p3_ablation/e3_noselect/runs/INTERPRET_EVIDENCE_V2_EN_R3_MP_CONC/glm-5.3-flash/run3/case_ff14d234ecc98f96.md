### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every one of the 200 cases executed the three activities A26, A27 and A28 exactly once each, in varying order with no fixed sequence among the three; in the later window they appear in only about 1 in 100 cases (1–2 of 200 traces). [OCC025, OCC026, OCC027, REL036, REL037, REL040]
- In the later window, three previously unobserved activities — A19, A20 and A21 (0–1 of 200 traces earlier) — each run in about a third of cases (62, 67 and 74 of 200) and practically never together (at most about 2 in 200 traces), so a typical later case runs exactly one of the three. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC020, REL018, REL019, REL025]
- The wiring around this segment changed accordingly: earlier, about 33%, 28% and 32% of A44's outgoing transitions led to A26, A27 and A28, which led on to A29 (A26 preceded A29 in about 99 of 100 cases; A44 preceded A26 in about 98 of 100); in the later window, about 27%, 30% and 36% of A44's outgoing transitions go to A19, A20 and A21, about 97% of each new activity's outgoing transitions lead to A29, and A44 precedes the new activity, which in turn precedes A29, in roughly 30–37 of 100 traces for each of the three. [DIR044, DIR019, DIR020, DIR021, REL023, REL024, REL029, REL030, REL034, REL035, REL038, REL039]
- The gap between A44 and A29 shrank from four event positions in about 93 of 100 earlier cases to two positions in about 92 of 100 later cases; A29 now occurs earlier within traces (mean relative position about 0.43 vs 0.40) and A44 slightly later (about 0.31 vs 0.33), while the new activities sit at the same relative position (median about 0.35–0.37) the old group held. A44 and A29 themselves still run in every case in both windows. [LAG012, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010, OCC028, OCC041]
- The two entry routes shifted in how often they are taken: participation in A01, A05, A07 and A09 fell from about 54 to about 44 in 100 cases, while participation in A03, A11, A12 and A13 rose from about 47 to about 57 in 100; A11 and A12 also co-occur more often (47% vs 57% of traces) and A11 precedes A12 more often (19% vs 30%). [OCC001, OCC003, OCC005, OCC007, OCC008, OCC010, OCC011, OCC012, REL001]
- Repetition increased late in the process: A45 averages about 1.8 occurrences per trace (vs about 1.6) and occurs more than once in about 49 of 100 traces (vs 37); A46 appears in about 69 of 100 traces (vs 59) and repeats in about 21 of 100 (vs 12); the share of A46's and A48's outgoing transitions returning to A45 rose to about 45% and 44% (from about 37% and 36%). [OCC042, OCC043, DIR046, DIR048]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: in the earlier window every case ran the three activities A26, A27 and A28 between A44 and A29 in varying order, while in the later window these are essentially unobserved and each case instead runs exactly one of the three alternative activities A19, A20 or A21 in that position, cutting the A44-to-A29 distance from four to two event positions. The change is shown directly by the wholesale successor change at A44, the new activities' outgoing edges to A29, the reversed co-occurrence and ordering relations, and the matching lag and position shifts; its size (from 200 of 200 traces to 1–2, and from 0 to 62–74) is far beyond what different sampling of an unchanged process produces. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A19–A21 are the same work under new activity codes rather than genuinely new activities; the evidence also cannot show what determines which of the three alternatives runs in a given case. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, OCC026, OCC027, DIR019, DIR020, DIR021, DIR044, LAG012, REL023, REL024, REL029, REL034, REL035]

Other differences:
- The two entry routes are taken with different frequencies: the route through A01, A05, A07 and A09 runs in about 44 of 100 later cases (about 54 earlier), the route through A03, A11, A12 and A13 in about 57 (about 47 earlier). All involved activities remain common in both windows, so this looks like existing paths being taken with different shares rather than paths appearing or disappearing. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC005, OCC007, OCC008, OCC010, OCC011, OCC012, REL001]
- The late-stage loop around A45 and A46 repeats more often: A45 repeats in about 49 of 100 later traces (37 earlier) and A46 in about 21 (12 earlier), with more transitions from A46 and A48 returning to A45 (about 37% to 45% and 36% to 44%). [OCC042, OCC043, DIR046, DIR048]
