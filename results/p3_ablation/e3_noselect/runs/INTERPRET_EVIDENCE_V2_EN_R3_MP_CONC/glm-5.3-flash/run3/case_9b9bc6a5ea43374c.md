### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A03, was introduced into roughly half of the executions, placed between the existing activities A05/A08/A12 and A11: A03 appears in about 52 in 100 later traces after being entirely absent, it is reached from A05, A08 and A12, it leads to A11, A12 and A08, and it always executes before A11 when both run. The increased step distances between A08/A12 and A11 in the later window fit A03 sitting on these paths. The change is shown directly by occurrence, direction, relation and position evidence that all agree, and an appearance in about 103 of 200 traces with structured relations to four existing activities is not what a rare variant or sampling noise produces. Limitations: the evidence cannot show under which condition A03 runs (which variants include it), and it cannot separate whether the concurrent decline of A05's repetitions and of A07 is a consequence of this insertion or an independent change; a shift in the mix of existing variants between the windows would look similar.

Other differences: A04's routing between A09 and A13 became more balanced (A09 about 37% vs 53% of A04's outgoing transitions), and A05 now starts earlier in the trace and repeats less often; whether these belong to the same change cannot be determined from the evidence. [DIR002, POS002, OCC005]
