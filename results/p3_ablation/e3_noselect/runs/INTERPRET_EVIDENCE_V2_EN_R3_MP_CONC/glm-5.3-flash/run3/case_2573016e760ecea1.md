### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A06 is no longer executed between A05 and A04: in the earlier window about 52 in 100 traces ran the fixed sequence A05 → A06 → A04, whereas in the later window about 50 in 100 traces run A05 → A04 directly and A06 is not observed at all. This is shown directly by the successor of A05 switching entirely from A06 to A04, by the A05-to-A04 distance falling from two steps to one, and by A06's co-occurrence with A04 and with A05 dropping to zero while both activities keep their roughly 50-in-100 participation [DIR001, LAG001, REL002, REL003, OCC006, DIR002]. The relative order of A05 and A04 is unchanged, so this reads as a removal of the middle activity rather than a reordering [REL001]. Limitations: zero participation only means A06 was not observed in the later 200-trace sample, so a rare residual execution of A06 cannot be ruled out; the relational and lag items also describe one underlying trace change rather than independent confirmations, and no screening baseline was provided to compare the item count against chance expectations [DIR001, LAG001, REL002, REL003, OCC006].

Other differences: Small participation shifts for A01 (about 48 to about 50 in 100 traces) and for A02 and A03 (about 52 to about 50 in 100), each about 1.5 percentage points with low empirical strength, consistent with sampling noise between finite windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
