### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A10 became a regular step between A14 and A15. In the earlier window A10 was rare (about 19 in 100 cases), sat at widely varying event positions with many different successors, and A14 passed almost directly to A15; in the later window A10 runs in about 70 in 100 cases, consistently near the start, A14 leads to it in about two thirds of its outgoing transitions, it leads to A15 in about 85 in 100 of its own, and the A14→A10 pair is almost always adjacent. The same difference shows directly and consistently in occurrence, position, direction, relation and lag indicators, all with reference strengths at or near the maximum, which random re-splits of an unchanged process rarely produce. Limitations: A14 still passes directly to A15 in about a third of its outgoing transitions and A10's order relative to A15 is mixed or interleaved in about 30 in 100 cases, so the step is dominant rather than exclusive; the evidence cannot distinguish a strict insertion of A10 into the existing A14-to-A15 path from a new, usually-taken branch running between them, and no screening baseline was provided to compare the number of qualifying items against a chance expectation.
