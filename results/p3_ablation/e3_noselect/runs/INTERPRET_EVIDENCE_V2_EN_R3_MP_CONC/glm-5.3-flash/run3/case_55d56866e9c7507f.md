### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the A09/A11 branch is executed differently in the later window: A09 and A11, strict alternatives that never occur together, are taken far more often (A09 in about 44 in 100 traces versus about 30; A11 about 57 versus about 29), appear earlier in the trace, and now typically follow A06 directly, whereas in the earlier window they were rarer, sat near the trace end, and typically came immediately after A08, which preceded them in every trace where both occurred. A08's role shifted accordingly: it now occurs later, leads more often to A02 or A06 than to A09 or A11, and its previously fixed order before A09/A11 became unordered. In addition, the later window shows one of A09/A11 in essentially every trace, where before about 42 in 100 traces contained neither. [OCC006, OCC008, POS003, POS004, DIR006, DIR008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG005, LAG006, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

The shift is shown directly by relational, directional and lag evidence rather than by occurrence counts alone, it is consistent across the occurrence, position, relation and lag indicators, and the participation changes exceed those seen in about 99 of 100 random re-splits of unchanged data. Limitations: many items describe the same underlying traces and are not independent confirmations; the earlier-window successor profiles of A05, A09 and A11 rest on only about 7–10 observed outgoing transitions, so the precise earlier wiring around them is uncertain; and positions are event positions, so no movement in clock time can be inferred. An important competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is sampling variation between the two finite windows for the finer redistribution details, such as A08's exact new successor shares.
