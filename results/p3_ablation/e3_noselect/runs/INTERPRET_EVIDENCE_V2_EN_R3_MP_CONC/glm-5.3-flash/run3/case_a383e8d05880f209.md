### Observed differences

- In the later window, A01 runs much later in the case: its median event position moved from about 17% to about 67% of the way through the trace, its lower quartile rose from 0 to 0.5, and its spread narrowed, so early occurrences of A01 largely disappeared. [POS001]

Earlier, A01 ran before A05 in about 80 in 100 cases and before A07 in about 68 in 100, splitting roughly evenly against A02 and A04; in the later window A01 runs after A07 in 200 of 200 cases, after A04 in 200 of 200, after A02 in about 81 in 100, and after A05 in about 63 in 100. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was repositioned from an early to a late stage of the process. In the earlier window A01 executed near the start, typically before A02, A04, A05 and A07, and was followed by A05, A02 or A04; in the later window it executes late, typically directly after A07 and directly before A03, with A04 reaching it via A07 and A02 and A05 mostly preceding it at a distance. [POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR001, DIR006, LAG002, LAG019]

The signs are direct and consistent across indicator families: complete order reversals (A01 after A04 and after A07 in 200 of 200 later cases, versus mixed or opposite orders before), new dominant successor relations in the direction profiles (A07→A01, A01→A03), and lag distributions in which both new adjacencies occur within five steps in every later trace, while A01's position distribution moved from the first third to the second half of the case. [REL002, REL004, DIR006, DIR001, LAG002, LAG019, POS001]

Limitations: the many items largely describe one underlying difference and are not independent confirmations; positions are event positions, so the change is established for ordering within the case, not for clock time; and no screening baseline is present, so the item count cannot be compared against a chance expectation — the judgement rests on the directness and completeness of the relational evidence. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, POS001]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that A01 was not relocated but that a late A07→A01→A03 fragment was added or strengthened in some variants while an early A01 persisted; the tightened position distribution (lower quartile 0.5, no early mass) argues against a remaining early occurrence, but variant mixing cannot be excluded. [POS001, LAG019, LAG002]

Other differences: A02, A05 and A07 each occur slightly earlier in the later window (mean position down by about 0.08–0.10), and A02 now leads to A05 more often (about 40 in 100 of A02's outgoing transitions, up from about 27 in 100); these look like knock-on effects of the same restructuring rather than separate changes. [POS002, POS004, POS005, DIR002, LAG007]
