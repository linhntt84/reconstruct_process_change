### Observed differences

- **A fixed three-activity block became skippable.** In the earlier window, A19, A25 and A20 each occurred once in virtually every trace (200 of 200), always in the fixed order A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 → A26, and A41, A26, A46 and A47 also appeared in every trace. In the later window, A19, A25 and A20 each appear in only about 53 in 100 traces (106, 107 and 106 of 200), while A41, A26, A46 and A47 still appear in all 200. When the three do occur, their order and positions are essentially unchanged (medians at about 0.32, 0.36 and 0.39 of the trace length in both windows). The block is skipped as a unit: in the later window about 44 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions lead directly to A26 (earlier about 96 in 100 led to A19 and virtually none to A26), and the A41→A26 distance is a single step in about 44 in 100 of the observed pairs versus about 7 in 100 earlier, when about 93 in 100 were four steps apart. [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, OCC037, OCC026, OCC042, OCC043, REL011, REL009, REL014, REL015, DIR041, LAG022, POS002, POS003, POS004]

- **A02 became rarer and changed its place.** In the earlier window it appeared in about 29 in 100 traces (58 of 200), near the end of the trace (median about 97% of trace length), typically between A46 and A47: A46 led to A02 in about 25 in 100 of A46's outgoing transitions, and A02 led to A47 in about 85 in 100 of its own. In the later window it appears in about 8.5 in 100 traces (17 of 200), its median position moved to about 47% of trace length, A46 now leads directly to A47 in about 96 in 100 of its transitions, and A02's few remaining outgoing transitions scatter across many different activities (only 17 observations, so these new targets are weakly supported). [OCC002, POS001, DIR002, DIR046, LAG024, REL006]

- **Modest participation shifts elsewhere.** In the later window, A35 (about 42→52 in 100 traces), A36 (about 42→52), A23 (about 46→54) and A44/A45 (about 57→65) take part more often, while A37 and A38 (about 59→49 each), A24 (about 55→48), A18 (about 27→22) and A43 (about 70→61, mean occurrences down from about 1.0 to 0.8) take part less often; A42 recurs slightly less (about 1.8→1.7 occurrences per trace). The rare activities A04, A06 and A08 roughly doubled or tripled (about 4.5→12, 4→12.5 and 2→7.5 in 100), but each rests on fewer than 25 traces per window. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the fixed sequence A19 → A25 → A20, which in the earlier window every case executed once between A41 and A26, became optional: in the later window about 53 in 100 cases still run it in the same order and at the same position, while about 47 in 100 skip the entire block and pass from A41 directly on to A26. The change is shown directly and consistently from several angles of the same trace behaviour — participation falling from 200/200 to about 106/200 for all three activities, co-occurrence with the still-mandatory A41/A26/A46/A47 dropping from 100% to about 53%, a new direct A41→A26 transition covering about 44 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions, and the A41→A26 distance collapsing from four steps to one in about 44 in 100 of pairs — while order and position indicators confirm the block itself is unchanged when present. Because the block appeared in all 200 earlier traces, a skip variant of this prevalence cannot have been hidden there; the competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out concerns what happens in skipped cases — whether anything replaces the block there (the rare new successors of A41, such as A04 and A06, are too infrequent to characterise) and whether skipping depends on case characteristics.
