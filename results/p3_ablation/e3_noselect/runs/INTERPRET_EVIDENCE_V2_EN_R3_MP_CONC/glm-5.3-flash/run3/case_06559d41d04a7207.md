### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured from a choice among alternatives into a jointly executed set: earlier, almost every case ran exactly one of A19, A20 or A21 there, reached directly from A44 or through the optional A10; later, A19, A20 and A21 have essentially vanished while A26, A27 and A28 each run once in every case in no fixed order, in the same slot between A44 and A29, and the A10 detour has largely disappeared (about 51 to 22 of 200 cases). [REL011, REL013, REL035, REL037, REL038, DIR043, DIR025, OCC019, OCC024, OCC010, LAG009]

The support is direct and consistent: the relational items flip from "old activity alone or neither" to "new activity alone in about 199 of 200 cases" and from "never together" to "always together" for the new trio; the occurrence changes (0 of 200 to 200 of 200, and about 70 of 200 to 1 of 200) are far beyond what re-splitting a stable process produces, each key item exceeding about 199 of 200 random re-splits; and the successor, position and lag evidence independently places the new trio between A44 and A29. The items are not independent confirmations, as many re-describe the same traces, and no screening baseline is provided to compare item counts against chance expectations. [REL013, REL021, REL028, REL035, OCC024, OCC019, DIR043, POS007, LAG009]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows draw on different process variants or populations rather than one modified process; it also cannot show whether A26, A27 and A28 are genuinely new activities or the old steps under new labels, although the shift from "exactly one of three" to "all three in every case" is a behavioural difference under either reading. [OCC024, OCC019, REL035]
