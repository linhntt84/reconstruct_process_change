### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the routing around the block A31, A32 and A37 changed: in the earlier window every trace ran A20 into the trio, the trio on to A23, and later A21 into A22 and A26; in the later window A20 connects directly to A23, and after A21 the process either runs A22 and then A26 (about 54 in 100 traces) or inserts the trio, which now continues to A26 instead of A23 (about 47 in 100). A22 and the trio, previously both mandatory, have become mutually exclusive alternatives. [OCC017, OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, DIR014, DIR015, DIR018, DIR024, REL011, LAG001, LAG005]

The hypothesis rests on direct relational, directional and lag evidence rather than marginal frequencies alone: A20-to-A23 went from unobserved to 199 of 200 transitions, trio-to-A23 was replaced by trio-to-A26, A22–trio co-occurrence fell from 200 of 200 traces to about 1 in 200, and the A21-to-A26 distance split into the 2-step and 4-step forms expected from the two branches. The same rearrangement appears across all five indicator families, and the key items have empirical strength 0.995 — differences larger than essentially all 200 random re-splits — so sampling variation alone cannot plausibly account for the pattern. [DIR014, DIR018, DIR024, REL011, REL014, LAG001, LAG005, POS006]

The evidence cannot rule out that the later window reflects a changed mix of two pre-existing execution variants rather than a modified routing; against a pure mix shift, the earlier window shows no trace of the alternative branch, since A22 and the trio appeared in every earlier trace. A few lag items consistent with the same reading (for example A20–A31 and A31–A23) rest on a single after-window observation and were not relied on. [OCC017, OCC018, LAG002, LAG006]

Other differences: the small participation and successor-share shifts around A01–A08 and A16/A17 noted above are separate from this hypothesis and are not explained by it. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC006, OCC007, OCC013, OCC014, DIR002, DIR011]
