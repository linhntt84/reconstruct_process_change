### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the route from A02 to A04 is taken and repeated more often, while the route from A02 to A05 is taken relatively less often. In the earlier window A02 occurred about 0.77 times per trace and repeated in about 20 in 100 traces; in the later window it occurs about 1.0 times per trace and repeats in about 27 in 100 traces, and its outgoing flow moved towards A04 (about 43 to 54 in 100) and away from A05 (about 28 to 19 in 100), with A04 correspondingly present in about 27 versus 20 in 100 traces. This rests on direct routing evidence for A02 backed by 154 versus 201 observed outgoing transitions, and it agrees with the occurrence changes of A02 and A04; these differences exceed roughly 91–97 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled data. Limitations: A04 is the least frequent activity involved (about 39 versus 54 supporting traces), and no screening baseline is included, so the number of flagged items cannot be compared with the number expected for an unchanged process; an unusual chance concentration of repeated A02–A04 behaviour in the later sample therefore cannot be fully excluded. [DIR002, OCC002, OCC004]

Other differences: the redistribution within the A05/A08/A09 triangle described above is only moderately unusual (strength about 0.70) and may reflect the same underlying trace behaviour rather than a separate change. [DIR003, DIR004]

The remaining small participation and routing differences across A01, A03, A05–A10 are consistent with sampling noise between the two finite windows. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, DIR001]
