### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a restructured flow: a new activity A09 now executes between A06 and A07, where previously A06 led directly to A07; the closing segment changed, with A01 no longer observed and a new block of A08 together with A12 (grown from about 15 to 48 in 100 traces) closing about half of the traces after A07, in no fixed order between themselves; and A03 and A10 turned from two activities executed together in every trace, in either order, into mutually exclusive alternatives that each appear in about half of the later traces and never together. This is participation and structural change rather than a re-weighting of unchanged paths: A01, A08 and A09 cross zero participation, and A03/A10 move from always-co-occurring to never-co-occurring. The signs are direct and consistent across indicator families — successor profiles change exactly at the affected points (A06 → A09 → A07; A03 and A10 lose their mutual edges and their edges into A01), the A06→A07 distance shifts from one to two steps exactly as the insertion predicts, and relation profiles flip between "always together" and "never together"; nearly all items behind this picture exceed about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. Limitations: no expected-by-chance figures are provided, so the judgement rests on directness and consistency rather than a count comparison; zero participation only establishes non-observation, so the evidence cannot distinguish whether A01 was removed or now occurs too rarely to appear in 200 traces, nor whether A08 is genuinely new or has taken over the closing role previously filled by A01, since identifiers are anonymous codes; and items describing the same traces (for example the A03/A10 occurrence drop and their relation flip) are not independent confirmations.
