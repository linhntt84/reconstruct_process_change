### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the iterative segment around A03 and A11 was removed or bypassed in the later window. Earlier, A03 and A11 formed a cycle (A03 → A11 in about 44% of A03's outgoing transitions, A11 → A03 in about 97% of its), fed by A06 (about 86% of whose outgoing transitions went to A03) and reachable from A02 (about 9%); in the later window A03 and A11 were not observed at all (0 of 200 traces each, no outgoing transitions), A06 leads directly to A19 and A12, and A02 leads mainly to A04 with small direct edges to A12/A19 — the former entry points now connect straight to the activities that previously followed the loop. [OCC003, OCC009, DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009]

Consistently with this, the remaining group A06/A12/A19 runs in fewer traces (about 22 in 100 vs 29 in 100), repeats less often, is spaced more tightly (A06 → A12/A19 typically 1–2 positions apart; self-repeats 3–4 instead of 5), and A02 occurs slightly less often per trace (about 1.12 → 0.91). Because A03 and A11 are absent from the later window, this reads as a removal or bypass of activities rather than a re-weighting of shares between existing alternatives. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC010, OCC016, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG005, LAG009, POS002, POS004, POS005]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the A03/A11 behaviour continues under other activity codes, since zero participation only means the activities were not observed; no screening baseline is included, so the comparison against chance rests on the size of the shifts (57 and 39 of 200 traces down to 0) rather than a formal expectation; and the reduced frequency of the A06/A12/A19 group could partly be an independent change rather than purely a consequence of the loop's removal. [OCC003, OCC009, OCC005, OCC010, OCC016]

Other differences: small, noise-level shifts elsewhere — A01 occurred slightly less often per trace with its outgoing split moving a few points from A07 to A08, and A09/A18 and A14/A15 show slight occurrence increases; their empirical strengths (about 0.17–0.73) are within the range random re-splits produce. [OCC001, OCC004, OCC007, OCC012, OCC015, DIR001, DIR008, DIR009]
