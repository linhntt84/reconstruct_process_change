### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A02 was added as a new final step executed after A05: in the earlier window, cases ended with A05 — A05 appeared in all 200 traces at a single fixed end position with no observed outgoing transition, and A02 was not observed — whereas in the later window every case continues A05 → A02, with A02 running exactly once per trace and taking over the fixed end position A05 previously held. [OCC001, REL001, DIR002, POS001, POS002]

The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: the relation itself changed from "A05 alone in 100% of traces" to "A05 before A02 in 100% of traces" [REL001]; the new edge A05→A02 accounts for all of A05's observed outgoing transitions [DIR002]; participation moved from 0 of 200 to 200 of 200 traces [OCC001]; and both position items agree with this picture [POS001, POS002]. All five items show empirical strength of about 0.99, and a shift from 0 to 200 of 200 traces is far outside ordinary sampling variation.
