### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. About fifty indicators were screened, and the roughly ten whose differences stand out against random re-splits (empirical strengths above 0.9, at most about 0.98) are scattered over five or six unrelated routing spots and consist solely of share shifts of 5–13 percentage points between alternatives that remain well attended in both windows — a count and a pattern of the same order as an unchanged process produces, with no structural or relational counterpart, so sampling variation in the two 200-trace windows cannot be ruled out.
