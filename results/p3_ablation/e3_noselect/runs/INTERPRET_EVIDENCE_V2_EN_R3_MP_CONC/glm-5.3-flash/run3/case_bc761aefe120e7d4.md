### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A30, A31 and A36 each occurred about 3.4–3.5 times per trace and repeated within a trace in about 75 in 100 cases; in the later window, each occurs about 1.0–1.1 times per trace and repeats in only about 1–2 in 100 traces, while still appearing in every trace of both windows (200 traces each) [OCC027, OCC028, OCC033].
- The order between A30 and A36 changed from mixed to fixed: earlier the two were interleaved or repeated in about 75 in 100 of their shared traces (A30 strictly first in about 25 in 100); in the later window, A30 comes before A36 in about 98 in 100 [REL001].
- The way flow leaves this group changed: earlier, A36 was followed by A30 in about 71 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and by A25 in about 29 in 100; in the later window, A36 leads to A25 in about 93 in 100 and back to A30 in about 4 in 100. A30 → A31 and A31 → A36 remain dominant (about 97–99 in 100) in both windows, and the number of observed outgoing transitions from A30 and A31 fell from about 690 to about 210, consistent with fewer repetitions [DIR030, DIR031, DIR036].
- Distances between the group's events compressed: A30 is followed by A36 within two steps in about 89 in 100 later-window cases versus about 12 in 100 earlier, and the self-repetitions A30→A30 and A36→A36, previously about three steps apart in roughly 30 in 100 of about 150 observed pairs, are almost gone (only 1–2 pairs remain) [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004].
- Both activities' positions became much more regular: the spread of A30's position halves (standard deviation about 0.12 to 0.07, upper quartile from 0.52 to 0.44 of the trace) and A36's spread also halves (about 0.12 to 0.06) [POS001, POS002].
- Smaller participation shifts elsewhere: A38 (mean occurrences about 1.0 to 0.75; traces containing it about 52 in 100 to 44 in 100), A37 (about 0.47 to 0.32) and A23 (about 0.51 to 0.44) decreased, while A24 (about 0.52 to 0.59), A19 (about 0.49 to 0.55) and A18 (about 0.24 to 0.30) increased; A40 repeats somewhat less (mean about 1.76 to 1.56) [OCC035, OCC034, OCC023, OCC024, OCC019, OCC018, OCC036].
- Routing shares moved at a few points: A43 now leads to A44 more often (about 54 to 66 in 100) and to A40 less often (about 43 to 32 in 100) [DIR043]; A27 leads to A24 more often (about 26 to 35 in 100) [DIR027]. Some sharply changed successor profiles (after A02, A06, A10, A45) rest on only about 3–27 outgoing transitions per window and are weak evidence on their own [DIR002, DIR006, DIR010, DIR045].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the cycle among A30, A31 and A36, previously executed about three to four times per trace, is now executed about once per trace: in the earlier window the three activities ran as a repeating cycle (A30 → A31 → A36, returning to A30), whereas in the later window each runs once in the fixed order A30 → A31 → A36, after which the flow leaves the group via A36 to A25. This is shown directly and consistently by the collapse in occurrences (about 3.45 to about 1.05 per trace, repeats falling from 75 to 1–2 in 100 traces, in all 200 traces of both windows) [OCC027, OCC028, OCC033], by the A30/A36 relation changing from mostly interleaved-or-repeated to a fixed A30-before-A36 order in about 98 in 100 traces [REL001], by A36's dominant successor switching from A30 to A25 [DIR036], by the near-disappearance of the A30→A30 and A36→A36 self-lags [LAG001, LAG004], and by the halving of both activities' position spreads [POS001, POS002]. The differences are larger than in essentially all reference comparisons (strength about 0.99), and the drop in outgoing transitions at A30 and A31 (about 690 to 210) matches the same reduction in repetitions [DIR030, DIR031].

Limitation: about 1–2 in 100 later-window traces still show repeats, so the evidence cannot distinguish a completely removed cycle from one now executed at most once per case; the positional indicators measure event positions and steps, not clock time [OCC027, OCC028, OCC033, LAG002].

Other differences: A38 occurs less often and in fewer traces (mean about 1.0 to 0.75; participation about 52 to 44 in 100) [OCC035]; A40 and A37 repeat less [OCC036, OCC034]; A23 decreased while A24, A18 and A19 increased modestly [OCC023, OCC024, OCC018, OCC019]; routing shares shifted at A43 (toward A44) and A27 (toward A24) [DIR043, DIR027]. These secondary differences are not relationally tied to the A30/A31/A36 cycle, so the evidence cannot establish whether they are part of the same restructuring or independent shifts; several large successor-profile changes (after A02, A06, A10, A45) rest on fewer than about 30 outgoing transitions and are weak on their own [DIR002, DIR006, DIR010, DIR045].
