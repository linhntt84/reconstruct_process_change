### Observed differences

- The block of activities A04, A06 and A10 became much rarer: in the earlier window each ran in about 50 in 100 traces (mean about 0.50 occurrences per trace), in the later window each runs in only about 20–22 in 100 traces (mean about 0.24–0.26). [OCC003, OCC005, OCC009]
- Earlier this block was mandatory and exclusive: every trace contained exactly one of A04 or A06 (they never appeared together and were never both absent), and A06 always ran together with A10. In the later window both are absent in about 66–70 in 100 traces, and A04 and A06 sometimes run together (about 8 in 100). [REL012, REL015, REL018]
- Earlier the block closed the trace: A04's median relative position was 1.0 and it showed only one outgoing transition in the whole window, A06/A10 sat around 0.9–1.0, A06→A10 and A10→A06 covered all their outgoing transitions, and A08 typically preceded the block (A08 before A04 in about 9 in 100 traces, A08 before A10 in about 14 in 100). In the later window the block runs before A08: all 47 observed outgoing transitions of A04 go to A08, A06 leads to A08 in about 44 in 100 and A10 in about 56 in 100 of their outgoing transitions, the block's median positions moved to about 0.6–0.67 while A08's median moved later (about 0.63 to 0.83), and the internal A06→A10 chain persists (immediate in about 87 in 100 of later pairs). [DIR004, DIR006, DIR009, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS007, REL013, REL016, REL020, LAG015]
- Entry into the block moved from A02 to A03 and A09: earlier A02 led to A04 in about 25 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and to A06/A10 in about 13 each; later it leads to none of them (0 of 219 observed outgoing transitions), its traffic concentrating on A05 (about 23 to 46 in 100) while its own frequency stayed at about 2 occurrences per trace. A03 now leads to A04/A06/A10 in about 23/14/8 in 100 and A09 in about 21/14/14 in 100, A09's earlier dominant edge to A08 (about 62 in 100) disappeared, and A04 now almost always co-occurs with A03 or A09 (A04 without A09 in about 3 in 100 traces versus about 40 in 100 before). [DIR002, DIR003, DIR008, REL007, REL014, OCC001]
- Distances changed accordingly: A02→A04 was immediate (one step) in about 61 in 100 of the earlier pairs and never in the later window, and A09's links into A04/A06/A10 now run at 1–2 steps where they previously took 3–5. [LAG003, LAG025, LAG026, LAG029]
- Smaller shifts: A02 now occurs later in traces (median relative position about 0.5 to 0.73, and the final event in at least 25 in 100 of its occurrences versus under 25 in 100 before), A07 repeats more often (mean about 0.38 to 0.56 occurrences per trace at nearly unchanged participation), and A05 runs in about 36 in 100 traces, up from about 29 in 100. [POS001, OCC006, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment formed by A04 on one branch and by A06 together with A10 on the other changed from a mandatory end-of-trace block into an optional, earlier-placed block that flows into A08. Earlier, every trace executed either A04 alone as the final activity or the pair A06+A10 chained together near the end, reached from A02 and usually preceded by A08; in the later window the block is skipped in about two-thirds of traces, runs before A08 (A04→A08 in all observed cases, A06/A10→A08 in roughly half of their outgoing transitions), and is entered from A03 or A09 instead of A02, whose edges into the block disappeared. [OCC003, OCC005, OCC009, REL012, REL015, REL018, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, DIR008, DIR009, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL013, REL016, REL020]

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: the participation drop rests on large samples (99→40 and 101→43 traces per window) and exceeds the difference seen in 199 of 200 random re-splits; the strict either-or pattern (both branches absent in 0 in 100 traces) became optional; the edge changes are observed on the successor profiles themselves rather than inferred; the order between the block and A08 reversed; and the block now almost never appears without A03 or A09 (for example A04 without A03 in about 5 in 100 traces versus about 39 in 100 earlier). [OCC003, OCC005, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR008, REL012, REL013, REL014, POS003, POS004, POS007]

Limitations: the before-side of A04's successor profile rests on a single observed transition, although A04's end-of-trace position is independently supported by its position distribution and relation to A08; several items re-describe the same traces (the A06 and A10 figures are identical throughout), so agreeing items are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that later-window traces simply terminate earlier — A02 is now frequently the closing event — which would also cut executions of a late block; the new entries from A03/A09, the exits into A08 and the block's earlier positions argue against this being the whole story, but the two effects cannot be fully separated. [DIR004, POS003, REL013, OCC005, OCC009, REL012, REL015, POS001]

Other differences: A02 and A08 occur later in traces than before (A02 median relative position about 0.5 to 0.73 and frequently the final event; A08 median about 0.63 to 0.83) [POS001, POS005]; A07 repeats more often (mean about 0.38 to 0.56 occurrences per trace) [OCC006]; A05 participation rose from about 29 to 36 in 100 traces [OCC004].
