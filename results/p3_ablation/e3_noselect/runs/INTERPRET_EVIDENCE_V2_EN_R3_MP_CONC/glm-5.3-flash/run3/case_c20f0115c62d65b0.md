### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 formed a mandatory three-way choice: A19 ran in about 32 in 100 cases, A20 in about 28 and A25 in about 40, the three never occurred together, and their shares sum to 100 — exactly one of them executed in every case. In the later window all three have nearly vanished: each appears in only about 1 in 200 cases, and in about 99 in 100 cases none of them appears. [OCC017, OCC018, OCC023, REL001, REL002, REL006]

- Their connections disappeared accordingly. Earlier, about 44 out of 100 outgoing transitions of A27 led to one of A19/A20/A25 (with A28 directly taking roughly 53), and about 54 out of 100 outgoing transitions of A28 led to one of the three (A29 directly roughly 45); the three activities themselves led on to A29 or back to A28. In the later window, A27 leads almost exclusively to A28 (about 97 in 100) and A28 almost exclusively to A29 (about 98 in 100). [DIR027, DIR028, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025]

- The block sat between A27/A28 and A29, and with it gone the step distances shortened: A27 to A29 went from 3 steps apart in about 94 in 100 cases to 2 steps apart in about 97; A28 to A29 went from a mix of 1 and 2 steps (about 45/53) to 1 step in about 98; A27 to A28 went from a mix of 1 and 2 steps (about 54/44) to 1 step in about 98. A27's relative position shifted slightly later and A29's slightly earlier, consistent with one step disappearing between them. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS004, POS006]

- Co-occurrence shows the same picture: earlier, each of A19/A20/A25 co-occurred with A27, A28 and A29 in 28–40% of cases, typically before A29; in the later window these co-occurrences are essentially zero, with A27, A28 and A29 now appearing alone. [REL003, REL005, REL008, REL010, REL012]

- Outside this region, A15 and A16 still occur in every case but repeat less: mean occurrences per case fell from about 1.44 to about 1.31 for both, and traces containing repetitions fell from about 32 in 100 to about 24. [OCC013, OCC014, DIR016]

- Smaller shifts: A02 became rarer (about 12 to about 6.5 in 100 cases) while A08 became more common (about 6 to about 11.5), and A39/A40 occur somewhat more often (A40 mean occurrences about 0.69 to 0.78). [OCC002, OCC007, OCC032, OCC033]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative branch consisting of A19, A20 and A25 — earlier a mandatory three-way choice in which exactly one of the three executed per case, each then leading to A29 or A28 — was removed or is no longer executed, so that in the later window cases run directly from A27 to A28 to A29. The change is shown directly and consistently across indicator families: participation of all three activities collapsed from 28–40% to about 0.5%, the successor profiles of A27 and A28 lost precisely the transitions into these three activities while the direct moves became near-universal, co-occurrence with A27/A28/A29 collapsed to zero, and the A27-to-A29 distance shortened by one step. [OCC017, OCC018, OCC023, DIR027, DIR028, REL003, LAG003]

The evidence shows the branch is no longer taken in the later sample; it cannot distinguish whether the activities were removed from the process altogether or the branch is merely skipped in this period, and it cannot show where, if anywhere, the displaced work went. [OCC017, OCC018, OCC023]

Other differences: A15 and A16 repeat less often than before — a repetition change in a different part of the process, not part of the main hypothesis; A02 decreased while A08 increased, and A39/A40 rose slightly. [OCC013, OCC014, OCC002, OCC007, OCC032, OCC033]
