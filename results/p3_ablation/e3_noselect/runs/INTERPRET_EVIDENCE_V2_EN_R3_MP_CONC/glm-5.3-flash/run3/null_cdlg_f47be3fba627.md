### Observed differences

- Activity participation was practically identical in the two windows: A01 appeared in about 51 of 100 traces before and 50 of 100 in the later window, A03 in about 49 before and 50 after, A09 in about 49 before and 50 after, A11 in about 51 before and 50 after, A05 in about 23 before and 24 after, and A10 in about 26 before and 26 after. No difference reached 2 traces in 100, and each was smaller than the differences seen in at least about 92 of 100 random re-splits. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

- The mix of outgoing transitions also moved only slightly for most source activities: from A04, the share continuing to A02 was about 36% before and 40% in the later window (share to A07 about 18% before, 14% after); from A03, the share to A08 was about 71% before and 74% after; from A11, the share to A08 stayed at about 74% before and 73% after. Differences of this size were matched or exceeded in roughly 40–95 of 100 random re-splits. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR010]

- The largest routing differences cluster around A05, A09 and A10. From A10 (about 52 outgoing transitions before, 53 after), the share leading to A04 fell from about 73% to about 53%, while the share leading to A09 rose from about 12% to about 21%. From A05 (about 46 vs 47 outgoing transitions), the share leading to A09 rose from about 4% (2 transitions) to about 19% (9 transitions). From A09 (about 98 vs 100 outgoing transitions), the share leading to A04 rose from about 11% to 20%, while the share leading back to A10 fell from about 29% to 21%. [DIR004, DIR008, DIR009]

- From A06, whose outgoing transitions were observed in all traces in both windows, the share continuing to A07 rose from 38% to 44%, and the share continuing to A09 fell from about 17% to about 10%. [DIR005]

- These shifts do not point in one direction: A09 drew a higher share of its incoming transitions from A05 and A10 but a lower share from A06, its own participation stayed at about half of the traces in both windows, and the two strongest routing differences were still matched or exceeded in about 7–8 of 100 random re-splits. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR008, DIR009, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The only differences above the ordinary random-split range — the routing shifts around A05, A09 and A10 — rest on roughly 50 outgoing transitions per window, and differences of that size still arise in about 7–8 of 100 random re-splits, so sampling variation in an unchanged process cannot be ruled out.
