### Observed differences

- The clearest difference lies in what follows A06. In the earlier window, about 58 in 100 of A06's outgoing transitions went to A04 and about 42 in 100 to A01; in the later window the split is nearly even, about 50 in 100 each — a shift of roughly 8–9 percentage points toward A01, based on about 182–183 outgoing transitions per window, and larger than in about 88 of 100 random re-splits of unchanged data [DIR003].
- A06's own presence is unchanged: it appears in about 47–48 in 100 traces in both windows, with about 0.9 occurrences per trace [OCC005].
- All other measured behaviour is essentially flat: A01 and A04 occur in every trace in both windows (about 1.9 occurrences per trace), A02 and A05 in about 47 in 100 traces, and the outgoing mixes of A01 and A04 move by at most about 1 percentage point (for example A01→A03 at about 28% before versus 29% after); these differences are smaller than almost all random re-splits (empirical strength 0.00–0.07) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002].

### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests that in the later window the branch after A06 is taken with different frequencies: continuations from A06 to A01 rose from about 42 in 100 to about 50 in 100, while continuations from A06 to A04 fell from about 58 in 100 to about 50 in 100. This is a shift between existing alternatives rather than a change in participation: A06 still occurs in about 47–48 in 100 traces in both windows, and both targets, A01 and A04, occur in every trace in both windows, while A06's occurrences per trace are stable [DIR003, OCC005, OCC001, OCC003].

The supporting sign is the direct directional profile of A06, backed by roughly 182–183 outgoing transitions per window, with A06's occurrence level stable so the shift concerns routing rather than frequency of execution. What is missing: the hypothesis rests on a single indicator, its difference is exceeded by roughly 12 in 100 random re-splits of unchanged data, and no screening expectation is provided to compare the number of qualifying items against chance; no other item corroborates the shift, as the outgoing mixes of A01 and A04 are flat. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is ordinary sampling variation between the two windows [DIR003, DIR001, DIR002].
