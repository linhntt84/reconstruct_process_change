### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A07 was removed from the process and its role absorbed by A08. Earlier, every case executed A08 into A07 (each about twice per case), and A07 then branched to A05 or A06 in a roughly 52/48 split; in the later window A07 is unobserved in all 200 traces and A08 branches directly to A05 (about 52 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) or A06 (about 48 in 100). Supporting signs: the successor change at A08 is observed directly, A07's participation falls from 100 in 100 to 0, all observed A07 relations collapse to the partner running alone, and distances between upstream and downstream activities shorten by about one step, as expected when an intermediate activity no longer executes. [OCC004, DIR002, DIR003, REL014, REL019, REL021, LAG011, LAG015]

This is a removal/bypass pattern rather than a re-weighting of paths present in both windows, because A07 is absent from the later window. Zero participation only shows that A07 was not executed; the evidence cannot distinguish removal of the activity from its branch simply never being taken. The many agreeing occurrence, relation and lag items largely describe the same underlying behaviour and are not independent confirmations, and no chance-expectation baseline is included to quantify how many items an unchanged process would produce. [OCC004, POS004]

Other differences: A01, unobserved earlier, appears once in about 35 in 100 later traces, always followed by A03, and never occurs together with A02, whose participation fell from about 48 to about 28 in 100 (A04 fell from about 53 to about 38 in 100); A03 and A08 occupy slightly later relative positions (A03 median 0.25→0.33, A08 median 0.50→0.67). These changes are not explained by the A07 removal and may be independent differences. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001, OCC002, OCC003, POS002, POS005]
