### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was not observed in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it occurs in every trace, exactly once per trace, always at the final position of the trace (mean position 1.0 with no spread). [OCC001, POS001]
- A03 is present in all traces in both windows, but its role changed. Earlier, A03 had no observed outgoing transitions and sat at the final position (mean 1.0), so it closed each trace. In the later window, all 200 of its observed outgoing transitions lead to A01, and its typical position moved earlier, from 1.0 to a median of about 0.86. [DIR002, POS002]
- The relationship between A01 and A03 flipped: earlier, A03 appeared without A01 in 100% of traces; later, the two occur together in 100% of traces, with A03 before A01 every time. [REL001]
- Two participation shifts are visible but modest: A11 rose from about 24 to 30 in 100 traces, and A06 fell from about 26 to 21 in 100 traces. [OCC006, OCC004]
- Outgoing-transition shares show only small redistributions, e.g. from A06 the share leading to A09 rose from about 4% to 17% while the share leading to A10 fell from about 25% to 14%; from A08 the share leading to A10 rose from about 10% to 16% while the share leading to A07 fell from about 20% to 13%. [DIR005, DIR007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new concluding activity, A01, was appended after A03, which previously ended the process. In the earlier window, A03 appeared in all 200 traces, had no observed successors, occupied the final position, and never co-occurred with A01; in the later window, every trace executes A03 and then exactly one A01, A03 precedes A01 in 100% of traces, and A03's position moved back from the final position to about 0.86 while A01 took over the final position. The five items tell one consistent story, and each difference is larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data, so a chance split is an unlikely explanation. The evidence cannot establish that A01 is genuinely new rather than a re-labelled successor of the previous final step, and control-flow evidence alone cannot show what A01 contributes. [OCC001, DIR002, POS001, POS002, REL001]

Other differences: A11's participation rose (about 24 to 30 in 100 traces) and A06's fell (about 26 to 21 in 100 traces), together with small routing redistributions (A06 now leads more often to A09 and less often to A10; A08 leads more often to A10 and less often to A07). These shifts are small in size, rest partly on only about 40–60 observations per window, and are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC006, OCC004, DIR005, DIR007]
