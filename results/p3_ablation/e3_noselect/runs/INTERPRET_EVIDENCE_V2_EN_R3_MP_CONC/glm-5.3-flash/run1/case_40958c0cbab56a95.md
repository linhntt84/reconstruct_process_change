### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A26, A27 and A28 were mandatory steps: each appeared in all 200 traces (about one occurrence per trace), the three always occurred together (co-occurrence in 100 in 100 traces) and in no fixed order among themselves (each pair about 45–55 in 100 in either direction). They sat between A44 and A29 — A44 preceded them in about 98–100 in 100 traces and they preceded A29 in about 98–100 in 100 — and each could be followed by another block activity or directly by A29. In the later window each of the three appears in only about 3 in 200 traces. [OCC025, OCC026, OCC027, REL046, REL047, REL052, REL048, REL049, REL053, REL057, DIR026, DIR027, DIR028]

- In the later window, A19, A20 and A21 — nearly absent earlier (2–4 in 200 traces) — each occur in about 30–38 in 100 traces (60, 65 and 75 of 200) and never together (co-occurrence at most about 0.5 in 100), so they behave as mutually exclusive alternatives. Each occupies the old block's place: A44 precedes the activity and the activity precedes A29 in about 30–37 in 100 traces, and about 95 in 100 of their observed outgoing transitions lead to A29. A44's outgoing mix moved from A26/A27/A28 (about 25–28 in 100 each) to A19/A20/A21 (about 25–27 in 100 each). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: the always-executed three-activity block A26/A27/A28, executed together in every earlier trace in varying order, is no longer observed (about 3 in 200 traces), and in the later window exactly one of three mutually exclusive alternatives — A19, A20 or A21 — runs between A44 and A29, each followed by A29 in about 95 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions. The support is direct and consistent across indicator families: the participation flips (200/200 to 3/200, and 2–4/200 to 60–75/200) are far beyond what sampling variation produces, relational items show the old block losing its after-A44/before-A29 position while the new activities take exactly that position, the successor profiles at A44 and at the new activities changed accordingly, and the A44→A29 distance shortened by about two positions as a three-for-one replacement predicts. Limitations: the evidence locates the difference but cannot show whether A19/A20/A21 existed earlier as negligible variants, and it cannot distinguish a structural replacement from the same work being re-coded under new activity identifiers; a competing explanation it cannot rule out is that the two windows sample different case mixes, for example a discontinued variant that always contained the old block. [OCC025, OCC027, OCC018, OCC020, REL046, REL048, REL049, REL027, REL042, DIR019, DIR021, DIR044, LAG014, REL061]

Other differences: the take-up of two existing alternative route clusters shifted by about 10 in 100 traces (A01/A05/A07/A09 down, A03/A11/A12/A13 up); both clusters remain well observed in both windows, so this qualifies as a change in how often existing paths are taken, but the evidence cannot tie it to, or separate it from, the block replacement. A10 became more frequent with its successors switching toward the new activities, and A45 and A46 occur and repeat more often in the later window.
