### Observed differences

- Routing out of A05 shifted: in the earlier window, about 39 of every 100 outgoing transitions of A05 went to A01 and about 50 went to A10 or A11 (24.5 and 26 per 100); in the later window, about 49 go to A01 and only about 44 to A10/A11 (21 and 23). The small transitions from A05 to A03, A04 and A06 shrank or were no longer observed. [DIR002]
- Participation moved in the same direction: A01 appeared in 105 of 200 traces in the later window versus 92 before (about 53 vs 46 in 100), while A10 and A11 each appeared in 95 versus 108 traces (about 48 vs 54 in 100), with identical counts for the two activities in both windows. [OCC001, OCC008, OCC009]
- A01's onward behaviour and the A10/A11 pair's internal order were stable: A01 still led to A07 in about 95 of 100 transitions in both windows, and A10/A11 followed each other in both orders, with roughly half of the pair's exits going to A07. [DIR001, DIR004, DIR005]
- A second redistribution is visible at A07: its outgoing transitions shifted toward A02 (about 4.5 to 9 in 100) and A04 (about 18.5 to 23 in 100), and away from A05 (about 7.5 to 3 in 100) and A06 (about 14 to 9.5 in 100). [DIR003]
- Occurrence counts echo that A07 shift: A02 appeared in 18 traces versus 9 before, A06 in 19 versus 29, A04 in 48 versus 43, and A03 in 53 versus 59, while A08 and A09 were essentially flat. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC004, OCC003, OCC006, OCC007]

### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests that, in the later window, execution after A05 more often took the direct route to A01 — which then proceeds almost always to A07 — and less often passed through the A10/A11 pair: the A05→A01 share rose from about 39 to 49 of A05's outgoing transitions while the combined A05→A10/A11 share fell from about 50 to 44 (based on 200 observed outgoing transitions of A05 in each window). This is consistent with the participation changes: about 13 fewer transitions from A05 into the pair, and a 13-trace drop in the pair's participation (108 to 95), alongside A01's rise from 92 to 105 traces. [DIR002, OCC001, OCC008, OCC009]

Supporting signs: the shift is observed directly in A05's outgoing transition shares, and all involved activities were seen in substantial shares in both windows (A05 in essentially all traces, A01 about 46 vs 53 in 100, the pair about 54 vs 48 in 100), so this is a change in how often existing alternatives are taken rather than a path appearing or disappearing. A01's outgoing profile and the pair's mixed internal order did not change, which argues against an activity having been moved. [DIR002, DIR001, DIR004, DIR005]

What is missing: no screening baseline is provided, so the number of qualifying items cannot be compared with what an unchanged process would produce; the corroborating occurrence items reuse the same traces as the routing item and are not independent confirmation; differences of 6–10 percentage points on 200 traces still arise in roughly 1 of 5 random re-splits (strengths near 0.78), so sampling variation cannot be ruled out; and routing also changed at A07, which the evidence cannot link to, or separate from, the A05 shift. [DIR002, DIR003]

Other differences: A07's outgoing transitions were redistributed toward A02 and A04 and away from A05 and A06, with A02 doubling from 9 to 18 of 200 traces (small numbers) and A06 falling from 29 to 19, while A04 (43 to 48) and A03 (59 to 53) moved only slightly and A08/A09 stayed stable.
