### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A07 is no longer executed in the later window, with its former inflows redirected. In the earlier window A07 ran in about 23 in 100 traces, usually reached from A01 or A10 and followed mostly by A03 or A13; in the later window it is absent from all 200 traces, and A01 and A10 — which previously sent about 9 and 13 in 100 of their outgoing transitions to A07 — now send larger shares directly to A03 and A13, with A03 also following A10 at a one-step distance more often. The disappearance is shown directly and consistently by four indicator families (occurrence, directly-follows, position, relation), each with empirical strength about 0.995, and a behaviour present in 46 of 200 earlier traces is very unlikely to be missing from 200 later traces through sampling alone. The evidence cannot distinguish complete removal from a residual rate below roughly 1–2 in 100 traces, and the main competing explanation it cannot rule out is that the A07 path still exists but has become very rare and was simply not sampled in the later window; the evidence contains no time or resource items, so timing and resource allocation cannot be assessed. [OCC006, POS002, DIR006, REL001, REL003, DIR001, DIR009, LAG002]
