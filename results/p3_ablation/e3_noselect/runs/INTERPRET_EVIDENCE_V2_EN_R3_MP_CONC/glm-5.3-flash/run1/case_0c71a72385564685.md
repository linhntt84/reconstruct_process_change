- In the later window, three activities appear that were essentially absent earlier: A19 occurs in about 38 in 100 traces (0 of 200 before, 76 of 200 after), A25 in about 35 in 100 (0 → 69), and A20 in about 30 in 100 (1 → 59). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new step is executed on the path between A41 and A26: A41 is now followed by one of three previously unobserved activities — A19, A20 or A25, chosen per case (roughly 38, 30 and 35 in 100 traces, mutually exclusive) — whose flow then continues into A26, whereas earlier A41 passed directly to A26 in about 82 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, typically one step apart. The hypothesis is supported directly and consistently across indicator families: the three activities rise from zero participation, A41's successor profile flips from A26 to the new activities, the new activities' own transitions run into A26, the order A41 → new activity → A26 holds in nearly every shared trace, the A41-to-A26 step distance moves from one to two-three steps, and A26's participation and its co-occurrence with A41 stay unchanged while its position shifts slightly later — a coherent pattern that rare-variant noise in two 200-trace windows does not plausibly produce. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, DIR041, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001, LAG002, POS004, OCC025, REL010]

Limitations: the new step appears in about 99 in 100 later traces, so a strictly mandatory step cannot be distinguished from one skipped in a small minority of cases, and the evidence contains no screening baseline against which the number of qualifying items could be formally compared. The main competing explanation that remains open concerns scope: several minor activities (A02, A04, A06, A08, A10) also lost their small direct transitions into A26, but each of these is observed only a handful of times per window, so whether they were rewired through the new step or their transitions merely dropped out of the sample cannot be settled. [REL001, REL002, REL005, DIR002, DIR004, DIR006, DIR008, DIR010]

Other differences: Routing around A40 changed — A40 occurs somewhat less often and repeats less (participation about 50 to 45 in 100 traces, mean occurrences about 0.72 to 0.58), its outgoing transitions moved from A39 (about 30 to 22 in 100) towards A41 (about 67 to 78 in 100), and A39's participation fell from about 16 to 10 in 100; because A39 is rare (about 32 versus 20 supporting traces), this rests on modest counts. A02's direct edge into A26 disappeared while A02's participation rose, and the small participation shifts in several other activities are of a size that two 200-trace samples can also show without any change.
