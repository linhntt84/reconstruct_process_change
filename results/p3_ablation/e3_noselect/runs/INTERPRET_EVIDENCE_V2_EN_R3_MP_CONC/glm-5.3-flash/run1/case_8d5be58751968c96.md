### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the six-activity block between A30 and A31 became an exclusive choice between two fixed trios. In the earlier window, every trace executed all six activities once, ordered A30 → {A33, A35, A36} in varying internal order → {A37, A38, A39} in varying internal order → A31; in the later window, about 44 in 100 traces execute only the first trio, about 53 in 100 only the second trio, and about 3 in 100 contain both, so running one trio goes together with not running the other, while A30 and A31 remain mandatory anchors. [OCC029, OCC031, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, OCC035, REL001, REL014, REL016, REL020, REL023, REL026, DIR030]

The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation of all six activities halved with the maximum empirical strength (0.995 in each case); co-occurrence collapsed only across the trios and stayed intact within them; A30's direct successors widened from the first trio to all six; A33, A35 and A36 gained a direct link to A31 that was absent before; and the second trio's event-order distance to A30 and A31 shrank from 4–5 to 1–3 positions, matching a block that shrank from six activities to three. [OCC029, OCC031, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, OCC035, REL002, REL016, REL020, REL026, DIR030, DIR033, DIR035, LAG004, LAG007]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show what determines which trio runs, and it cannot rule out that the two windows simply draw different mixes of cases (for example two case types whose proportions shifted between the windows) rather than a routing change within one process; the roughly 3 in 100 later traces containing both trios also do not fit a strictly exclusive choice. [REL016, REL021, REL025]

Other differences:
- Repetition around A43 and A44 increased (A43 mean occurrences about 1.56 → 1.71 per trace; A44 about 0.79 → 0.94), consistent with a loop that iterates more often, but no relational item connects this to the main change. [OCC039, OCC040, DIR043, DIR044]
- The four alternative branches after A29 shifted shares by about 5–8 in 100 traces (A23 down, A24 up), all four remaining in use. [OCC021, OCC022, DIR029]
- A48's successor profile changed completely, but it rests on only 5 versus 7 outgoing transitions. [DIR048]
