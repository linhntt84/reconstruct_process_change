### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 entered the process as a new intermediate step: unobserved in the earlier window, it now executes in about half of the cases, early in each case, receiving direct transitions from A05, A06, A08, A09, A12 and A15 and passing control on mainly to A05, A03, A06 and A15. Its arrival coincides with the loss of the direct A08→A03 and A09→A03 successions and with wider gaps downstream of A08 and A09 — the pattern expected if A01 was inserted between those steps and their successors. The support is direct rather than circumstantial: participation, successor profiles, pairwise relations and gap distributions each show the change, all centred on A01, with the key differences exceeding those seen in fewer than 1 in 100 comparable random re-splits [OCC001, DIR001, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, REL002, REL006, LAG022, LAG027]. Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely added activity from one that existed but was never executed or recorded in the earlier window; it does not pin down every insertion point, since A01 both precedes and follows several activities; and no screening baseline is included, so the number of qualifying items cannot be checked against a chance expectation.
