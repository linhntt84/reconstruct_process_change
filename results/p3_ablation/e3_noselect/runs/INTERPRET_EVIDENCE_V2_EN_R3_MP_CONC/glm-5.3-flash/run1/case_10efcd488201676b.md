### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the control flow was restructured at the start and the end of the process: the former closing block around A17 — executed in every earlier trace, alternating with A10 — is no longer observed, and in the later window a new closing block appears in which A06 and A16 are each executed exactly once per trace, adjacent in either order after A04 and A10; A01 is no longer observed, and a new activity A07 appears early in about two thirds of traces, leading to A03 or A04. Since activities appear and disappear rather than shares between unchanged paths shifting, this reads as addition and removal of process parts, not a re-weighting of existing alternatives. [OCC001, OCC005, OCC006, OCC014, OCC015, DIR004, DIR006, DIR008, REL040, REL048, REL052, REL058, LAG024, LAG064, POS005, POS014, POS015]

The signs are direct and consistent across occurrence, successor, position, relation and distance indicators: participation moves from 200 of 200 and 128 of 200 traces to 0 of 200 for A17 and A01, and from 0 of 200 to 136 of 200 for A07; successor profiles change outright (A10: only A17 earlier, only A16/A06 later; A04: an A17/A10 split earlier, A10 only later); and the new A06–A16 adjacency is shown relationally (co-occurrence in every later trace, both orders about half) and by distance 1 in both directions. These items describe overlapping aspects of the same trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations, but they all point the same way.
