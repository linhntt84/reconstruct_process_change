### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was executed exactly once in every one of the 200 earlier traces (participation 100%, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace); in the later window it is not observed at all (0 of 200 traces). Its outgoing transitions, which previously went to A07 in about 99 in 100 cases, are likewise gone. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001]
- Before, A05's outgoing transitions led to A01 in about 51 in 100 cases and to A06 in about 46 in 100, with no observed transitions from A05 to A07; in the later window, A05 leads directly to A07 in about 49 in 100 transitions and to A06 in about 45 in 100, and no longer to A01. [DIR004]
- Before, A01 and A05 occurred together in every trace (A05 came before A01 in 55 in 100 traces, interleaved or repeated in the remaining 45), and A01 and A07 occurred together in every trace with A01 always first; in the later window, A05 and A07 each still occur in every trace, but never together with A01. [REL001, REL002]
- The gap between A05 and A07 contracted: before, A07 followed A05 two event positions later in about 50 in 100 cases; in the later window, A07 immediately follows A05 in about 40 in 100 cases. [LAG002]
- A05's mean relative event position moved slightly later (about 0.42 to 0.48) and A07's slightly earlier (about 0.83 to 0.79); these are event positions, not clock times, and are consistent with an intermediate step between the two having disappeared. [POS002, POS003]
- A05's own frequency is essentially unchanged (about 1.9 occurrences per trace, present in every trace in both windows), as are A06's participation (about 45 in 100 traces in both windows) and the small routing shares at A02, A06 and A07. [OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, DIR005, DIR006, REL003]
- A04's outgoing mix shifted mildly beyond the loss of its edge to A01: transitions to A07 rose from about 2 in 100 to about 7 in 100, while transitions to A05 fell from 92 to 87 in 100. [DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A01 was dropped from the flow: in the earlier window every trace ran A05 (which repeated and looped with A06) and then A01, with A01 always preceding A07, whereas in the later window A01 is not observed in any of the 200 traces and A05 leads directly to A07 in about half of its outgoing transitions. [OCC001, DIR004, REL001, REL002]

The support is direct and consistent across indicator families: A01 falls from participation in 100% of traces to 0% (200 of 200 to 0 of 200), which sampling variation between two 200-trace windows does not produce; the successor change at A05 is observed directly (to A01 in about 51 in 100 transitions before, unobserved after; to A07 unobserved before, about 49 in 100 after); both relations involving A01 flip from "always co-occurring" to "never co-occurring"; and the A05→A07 distance contracts from typically two positions to one. The small position shifts of A05 and A07 fit this removal but rest on event positions and are not independent proof. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR004, REL001, REL002, LAG002, POS002, POS003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that A01 was relabelled or merged into another activity rather than truly removed, and it cannot show whether the work A01 performed is now absorbed elsewhere or no longer done; several of the items above describe the same single disappearance rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR004]

Other differences: A04 routes modestly more often to A07 and less often to A05 than before (about 2 to 7 in 100, and 92 to 87 in 100, respectively) [DIR003]; A02 shifts slightly toward A05 (about 11 to 14 in 100) [DIR002]; and the spacing between repeated A05 executions changed only marginally [LAG001]. These shifts are small and may reflect sampling variation between the windows.
