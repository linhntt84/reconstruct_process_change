### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a mid-process segment was reordered: the optional activities A19, A21 and A24, which earlier ran between A29 and A30, now run between A40 and A25 (directly after A40 and leading into A25), while the mandatory A20, A22 and A23, which earlier ran between A40 and A25, now run between A29 and A30 ahead of A30 — the two groups exchanged places around the otherwise stable A25-to-A29 stretch. [DIR019, DIR021, DIR024, DIR029, DIR040, REL053, REL070, LAG046, LAG057, REL085]

The support is direct and consistent: the order relations between the two groups and against the anchors A25/A29 reverse almost completely, the successor profiles of A40 and A29 change accordingly, the new adjacencies appear at a one-step lag, and the trace positions of all six activities move in the direction the exchange predicts. Every activity involved is well observed in both windows (A20, A22 and A23 in all traces; A19, A21 and A24 in roughly 28–44 in 100), so this is a reordering of existing behaviour rather than activities appearing or disappearing; the occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag items all describe this one underlying difference, not independent confirmations. [REL038, REL049, REL058, REL068, LAG029, LAG042, LAG045, LAG053, LAG056, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS009]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows draw on a different case mix in which both orderings already existed; the frequency changes inside the moved group (A19 up, A21 and A24 down) are also entangled with the move, so the evidence shows the new ordering but not whether the routing probabilities around it are otherwise unchanged. [OCC019, OCC021, OCC024]
