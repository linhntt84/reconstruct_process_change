### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A01, was inserted into the process immediately after A08 near the end of each case. In the earlier window, cases ended at A08 or continued from it directly to A05, A02 or A06; in the later window A08 leads to A01 in about 82 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, A01 executes once per case near the end, and from A01 the case either finishes (no observed successor in about three quarters of traces) or returns to A05 or A06 in roughly equal shares. The change is described consistently across occurrence, position, relation, direction and lag indicators: A01 appears in 200 of 200 later traces after 0 of 200, always after A08, while A08's direct exits to A05 and A06 nearly disappear and the distances from A08 to A05 and A06 stretch by roughly one position, as expected when an activity is placed between them [OCC001, POS001, DIR008, DIR001, REL003, LAG004, LAG005]. The earlier positions of A05, A06 and A08 and their new co-occurrence with A01 fit this single insertion rather than separate changes [POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002]. Limitations: A01's exit split rests on 53 observed transitions, and 0 of 200 earlier traces shows only that A01 was not observed before, not that it could not occur; the evidence also cannot distinguish a full insertion on every path from a rerouting in which A08's former exits were moved to A01, since both produce the same observed profiles.

Other differences: minor redistributions in the outgoing shares of A02, A03, A04, A05, A06 and A07, mostly with low empirical strength (for example about 0.37 for A02 and 0.20 for A07), together with A01 appearing as a small new successor of A02, A04, A05 and A06 (roughly 2, 2, 6 and 11 in 100 of their outgoing transitions) [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]; the reduced direct A05→A06 succession and the higher share of A05 before A08 noted above [LAG001, REL004]. The remaining relation and lag items (A06–A08 order, A05–A08 and A06–A08 lags) differ by no more than random re-splits of unchanged behaviour typically produce [REL005, LAG002, LAG003].
