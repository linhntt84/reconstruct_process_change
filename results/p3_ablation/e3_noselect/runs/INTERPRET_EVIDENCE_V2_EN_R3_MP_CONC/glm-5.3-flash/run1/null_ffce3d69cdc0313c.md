- The order of A31 and A32 between A27 and A28 reversed. In the earlier window A32 typically followed A27 directly (one step later in about 64 in 100 of the relevant traces) and A31 followed two steps after A27 (about 64 in 100), with A31 passing directly on to A28 in about 64 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions. In the later window A31 typically followed A27 directly (about 62 in 100) and A32 two steps later (about 62 in 100), with A32 now passing directly on to A28 (about 62 in 100). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A31 and A32 exchanged their typical order within the segment between A27 and A28: in the earlier window the dominant sequence was A27 → A32 → A31 → A28, while in the later window it was A27 → A31 → A32 → A28. The change is shown directly and consistently: all four lag relations between A27/A28 and A31/A32 flipped their dominant step distance, the successor mixes of A31 and A32 swapped almost exactly (A31 to A28 fell from about 64% to 38% of observed outgoing transitions while A31 to A32 rose from about 36% to 62%, mirrored at A32), the directly observed A31-before-A32 versus A32-before-A31 shares reversed, and both activities' position distributions changed markedly at the same time. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, DIR020, DIR023, DIR024, REL005, POS003, POS004]

The difference is specific to ordering: participation of A31 and A32, their co-occurrence with A27 and A28, their repetition, and the positions of the anchor activities A27 and A28 all stayed essentially flat, so a change in how often the activities run cannot explain the pattern. Limitations: both orders occur in both windows, so the change is a reversal of the preferred order (roughly two to one before in favour of A32 first, roughly two to one after in favour of A31 first) rather than a strict re-sequencing; and the evidence cannot rule out that the two orderings belong to two coexisting variants whose mix shifted, for example with case characteristics, rather than to a re-routing within one behaviour. [OCC015, OCC016, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, POS001, POS002]

Other differences: the route through A01/A03/A04/A05 was taken in about 52–53 in 100 traces (from about 42–43) while the route through A02/A06/A07/A08 fell to about 47–48 (from about 57–58) — mirror-image shifts consistent with the first alternative being selected more often, although the sequencing inside both routes also shifted, so the change is not limited to the route choice; the A35/A36 segment ran and repeated more often; and A10/A11 repeated slightly less.
