### Observed differences

- After A13, the choice of follow-up activity shifted toward A05: in the earlier window, roughly 47 in 100 of A13's outgoing transitions led to A05, 28 to A06 and 25 to A10; in the later window, roughly 54 led to A05, 23 to A06 and 23 to A10. This is the largest and least typical transition-profile difference in the sample (empirical strength 0.93, based on about 430 outgoing transitions per window). [DIR008]
- Trace participation moved in the same direction: A05 appeared in about 34 in 100 traces before and 39 in 100 after (mean occurrences per trace rising from about 1.0 to 1.2), A06 in about 37 before vs 33 after, while A10 stayed nearly flat (about 36 vs 35, a weak signal). [OCC004, OCC005, OCC009]
- After A02, transitions to A03 fell from about 50 in 100 of A02's outgoing transitions to about 45, while transitions to A07 rose from about 20 to 24; A07 itself appeared in about 44 in 100 traces before and 51 in 100 after. [DIR002, OCC006]
- A04 appeared in about 57 in 100 traces before and 50 in 100 after; its incoming shares from A01 (about 4% vs 2.5% of A01's outgoing) and from A02 (about 25% vs 24%) both edged down. [OCC003, DIR001, DIR002]
- A02 occurred about 2.1 times per trace before and 2.0 after, remaining in every trace in both windows; A03's participation dipped slightly (about 54 vs 52 in 100 traces) with low empirical strength. [OCC001, OCC002]
- A single A07 → A01 transition was observed, in the later window only; with one observation this cannot be interpreted. [DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

Main hypothesis: in the later window, the existing paths after A13 were taken with different frequencies — cases continued to A05 more often (about 47 → 54 in 100 of A13's outgoing transitions) and to A06 and A10 less often, while all three alternatives remained common in both windows, so this is a shift in branch frequencies rather than a path being added, removed or bypassed. The supporting signs are the direct, atypical shift in A13's transition profile and the matching participation movements of A05 and A06. [DIR008, OCC004, OCC005]

What is missing: the evidence includes no screening baseline, so the number of qualifying items cannot be compared with the number expected if nothing had changed; the corroborating occurrence items carry only moderate empirical strength (about 0.56–0.66) and the shifts amount to a few percentage points, so ordinary sampling variability between the two 200-trace windows remains a competing explanation that the evidence cannot fully rule out. [DIR008, OCC004, OCC005]

Other differences: routing after A02 shifted toward A07 and away from A03 (A07 present in about 44% → 51% of traces), A04 participation fell (about 57% → 50%) together with slightly lower incoming shares from A01 and A02, and A02 repeated slightly less often (about 2.1 → 2.0 per trace, still in all traces); a lone A07 → A01 transition in the later window is too sparse to interpret. These are reported separately and are not folded into the main hypothesis. [DIR002, OCC006, OCC003, DIR001, OCC001, DIR004]
