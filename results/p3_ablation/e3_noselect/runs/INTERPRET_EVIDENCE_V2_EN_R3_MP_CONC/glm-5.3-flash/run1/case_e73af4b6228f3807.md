### Observed differences

- Activity A10 was not observed in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces) but appears in the later window in about 47 in 100 traces (94 of 200), typically around a quarter to a third of the way through the trace. [OCC008, POS003]
- Routing changed around A10: in the later window A11 sends about half of its outgoing transitions to A10 (52%) while its share to A07 fell from about 96% to about 44%; A10's outgoing transitions go mainly to A07 (about 53%) and A11 (about 45%); and A05 now feeds A10 directly (about 20% of its outgoing transitions, previously 0) while its share to A11 halved (about 49% to 23%). - The new coupling also shows in relations: A10 co-occurs with A05, A07 and A11 each in about 47 in 100 later traces (never before); it runs before A07 in every co-occurrence (47% of traces, reverse order 0) and usually after A05 (42.5% vs 4.5% reverse), while its order relative to A11 is mixed (about 21.5% A10-first vs 25.5% A11-first). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A10 was added as a new step between the A05/A11 part of the process and A07: earlier, flow from A11 ran almost exclusively straight to A07 (about 96% of A11's outgoing transitions); in the later window about half of it runs via A10, which appears in about 47 in 100 traces, receives transitions from A11 (about 52%) and A05 (about 20%), and passes flow on mainly to A07 (about 53%) and A11 (about 45%). [OCC008, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005]

This is shown directly and consistently by four indicator families — participation (0 to 94 of 200 traces), new incoming and outgoing edges of A10, order relations (A10 always before A07, usually after A05), and lengthened step gaps between A05/A11 and A07 — and the magnitude (0 of 200 vs 94 of 200) makes a rare-variant or sampling explanation implausible. [OCC008, DIR004, DIR005, REL002, REL004, REL006, LAG001, LAG004]

Limitations: the evidence does not fix a single insertion slot relative to A11, since A10 and A11 occur in both orders (about 21.5% vs 25.5% of traces) and the edges A10 → A11 and A11 → A10 are both substantial; an arrangement where A10 is optional or can sit on either side of A11 cannot be ruled out. "Added" also means only that A10 was not observed in the earlier window, so a previously undetectably rare activity cannot be fully excluded, though it is unlikely at these counts. [REL006, DIR004, DIR005, OCC008]

Other differences: A02, A06 and A09 appear only in the later window in small shares (about 2–11 in 100 traces) as occasional successors of A07; given their rarity, part of this could be sampling noise, although A06 at 22 of 200 traces is harder to dismiss. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC007, DIR003] The small participation shifts for A01, A03, A04, A08 and A11 (about 2–4 percentage points, low empirical strength) are consistent with an unchanged process. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC006, OCC009]
