### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

In the later window, the existing branch from A03 to A04 is taken less often, so A04 is skipped more frequently, while the alternative branch to A05 carries the same absolute volume and therefore a larger share: A04 appears in about 31 in 100 traces versus about 38 in 100 earlier, and its share of A03's outgoing transitions falls from about 28% to about 22% while A05's rises to about 53%. This reads as a change in how often an already-existing path is selected rather than a path replacement: A03 runs in every trace in both windows, A04 is still observed in a substantial share of traces in both, and all four successors of A03 remain in use with only their shares moving. The routing evidence at A03 and the occurrence drop of A04 agree in direction and even in magnitude (about 0.6 A04 occurrences per 2.1 A03 executions, i.e. 28%, before; about 0.4 per 1.9, i.e. 22%, after). [DIR003, OCC003]

What is missing: the evidence carries no screening baseline, so the number of qualifying items cannot be compared with the number expected by chance; across the 19 evidence items covering 13 activities, an unchanged process can still produce one or two items with strength near 0.95, and the two strong items here describe one underlying difference (A04 taken less often) rather than two independent confirmations. An important competing explanation is sampling variability in a naturally optional branch — the A04 drop from 76 to 61 traces is modest — which this evidence cannot rule out. [OCC003]

Other differences: A03 repeats slightly less often (about 2.1 → 1.9 times per trace) [OCC002]; A01 and A11 occur slightly less often, A09 slightly more often and A12 slightly less often, and A02 redirects slightly from A01 toward A07, all shifts of 4 percentage points or less that random re-splits typically produce.
