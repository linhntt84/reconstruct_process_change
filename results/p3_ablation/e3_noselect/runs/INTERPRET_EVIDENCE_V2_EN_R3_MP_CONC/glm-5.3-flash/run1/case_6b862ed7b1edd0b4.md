### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a previously optional part of the flow has become standard: A19, A20 and A25, formerly executed in about 48 in 100 cases and skipped in the rest, now run exactly once in virtually every case in the same fixed order A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 → A26, while the former direct step from A41 to A26 that bypassed them (about 45 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions) is almost no longer taken. [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, REL026, REL028, REL030, REL033, DIR041]

Closely linked to this, A06 — formerly a side activity in about 24 in 100 cases with no fixed order towards the block — now appears in about 73 in 100 cases, sits between A15/A14 and A16 (A15 → A06 in about 40 in 100 of A15's transitions; A06 → A16 in about 88 in 100 of its own, immediately in about 97 in 100 pairs) and precedes the A19/A20/A25 block in about 65 in 100 traces. [OCC006, DIR006, DIR015, LAG003, LAG009, LAG013, REL004]

The hypothesis rests on direct participation, ordering, succession, lag and position evidence that all point the same way; the shifts involve 95–200 traces per window and the related items sit at the top of the reference comparisons (strength about 0.995), so sampling variation in windows of this size cannot explain them. What the evidence cannot rule out is a shifted case mix — for example, the later window containing almost only cases of the full variant rather than a modified process — nor whether the A41 → A26 step was removed or is merely no longer taken; A06 is also still absent in about 27 in 100 later cases, so its integration may be a separate, incomplete change. [OCC006, DIR041, REL004]

Other differences: A02, A04, A08, A10 and A18 were observed in fewer traces in the later window, though each rests on only about 13–15 later traces and is therefore weak; A42, A44 and A45 repeat more often; and routing shares between existing alternatives shifted modestly after A21, A23 and A24 and between A32, A35 and A36.
