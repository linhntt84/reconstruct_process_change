### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the branch around A03 was restructured in the later window, in two related ways. First, A02 now runs between A03 and A13: the earlier direct move A03 → A13 is replaced by A03 → A02 → A13, shown directly by the successor changes at A03 and A02 and by the A03-to-A13 gap moving from one step to two. Second, A14 no longer runs in the repetition loop around A03: before, A12 always led to A14 and A14 back to A03, giving three steps between successive A03 executions; in the later window A12 leads directly back to A03 (two steps) and A14 is not observed at all. [OCC002, OCC014, DIR001, DIR002, DIR010, DIR011, LAG001, LAG003, LAG009, REL001, REL006]

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: successor changes, lag shifts and co-occurrence/order relations each demonstrate the insertion and the removal on their own, and the occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators agree; the sizes involved (A02 from 0 to 78 of 200 traces; A14 from 51 to 0) are far larger than the differences produced by random re-splits of the pooled data (empirical strengths about 0.995 for the key items). The change also looks localised rather than a global redistribution: routing shares elsewhere, such as A04's split between A08 and A09 (about 48/47 in both windows), are essentially stable. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that A02 was a rare, unsampled path in the earlier window and A14 became too rare to sample rather than being added or removed; 0 observations in 200 traces makes a substantial earlier participation for A02 implausible, but the windows are finite. No screening expectation was provided, so the number of qualifying items could not be compared against a chance baseline. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG003, LAG009, DIR003]
