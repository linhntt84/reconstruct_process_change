### Observed differences

- Activity participation was essentially stable: in the earlier window each of the eight tracked activities appeared in roughly 45–55 of 100 traces, and in the later window the same holds, with every shift only about 4 in 100 cases (for example A02 in about 49 of 100 earlier traces versus 45 of 100 later, A04 about 51 versus 55); random re-splits of the pooled data produce differences this large in about 47 of 100 comparisons, so these shifts look like ordinary sampling variation. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]
- The largest routing difference concerns the successors of A02: in the earlier window its outgoing transitions went to A09 in about 50 of 100 cases and to A05 in about 46, while in the later window A05 took about 58 and A09 about 38; this is the most unusual difference in the set, yet random re-splits still exceed it in about 7 of 100 comparisons, and it rests on roughly 98 versus 90 observed outgoing transitions. [DIR002]
- The mirror image appears at A09: in the earlier window about 53 of its outgoing transitions went to A05 and about 44 back to A02; in the later window about 41 went to A05 and about 52 to A02, a difference random re-splits exceed in about 13 of 100 comparisons. Taken together with the A02 shift, this reads as exits from the A02–A09 back-and-forth into A05 happening more often directly after A02 in the later window. [DIR009, DIR002]
- After A07, the rare branch to A06 fell from about 5 to about 2 of 100 outgoing transitions while A10 rose from about 94 to about 97; a small difference that random re-splits exceed in about 25 of 100 comparisons. [DIR007]
- Two items rest on too few observations to describe behaviour: A08 had no observed outgoing transitions in the earlier window and a single one (to A03) in the later window, and A10's split between A06 and A03 is based on only 3 versus 6 transitions. [DIR008, DIR010]
- All other routing distributions (after A01, A03, A04, A05, A06) moved by only a few percentage points and are unremarkable against random re-splits. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence
The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The most suggestive pattern — outgoing transitions of A02 and A09 redistributing between each other and A05 — is of a size that random re-splits of unchanged behaviour also produce (about 7 and 13 in 100 reference comparisons exceed the two differences), so one or two such items are expected among this many tested units even without a change, and the participation differences that would corroborate a routing shift sit at noise level. [DIR002, DIR009, OCC001, OCC003, OCC007]
