### Observed differences

- Three activities dropped out of execution. In the earlier window A19 ran in about 30 in 100 traces, A20 in about 28 in 100 and A25 in about 40 in 100; in the later window each appears in only about 1 in 200 traces. [OCC017, OCC018, OCC023]
- Earlier, A19, A20 and A25 were mutually exclusive alternatives — no trace contained two of them — and each sat inside the A27–A28–A29 stretch, sometimes before and sometimes after A28: A27 preceded one of them in roughly 28–40 in 100 traces, and each of the three was followed by A29 in roughly 27–40 in 100. - The routing into and through these activities disappeared. Before, about 44 in 100 of A27's outgoing transitions went to A19, A20 or A25 (about 53 in 100 to A28), A28 led to one of the three in about 54 in 100 of its transitions, and the three themselves led mainly to A29 or A28. After, about 97 in 100 of A27's transitions go to A28 and about 99 in 100 of A28's go to A29, while each of the three activities is observed only once, so their outgoing behaviour can no longer be characterised. [DIR027, DIR028, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025]
- The remaining chain tightened: A27→A28 went from a mix of 1 and 2 steps (about 54 vs 44 in 100) to about 99 in 100 at 1 step, A28→A29 from a similar mix to about 98 in 100 at 1 step, and A27→A29 from mostly 3 steps to mostly 2. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that an optional segment formed by A19, A20 and A25 — three mutually exclusive alternatives inserted inside the A27–A28–A29 stretch and executed in roughly 30–40 in 100 cases earlier — was removed from the flow or became essentially never executed, so that in the later window A27 → A28 → A29 runs as a direct chain. The near-disappearance of all three activities, the collapse of their co-occurrence with A27/A28/A29, the rerouting of A27's and A28's outgoing transitions, and the tightened step distances all describe this same structural difference and agree in direction, and each key item is larger than in about 995 in 1000 random re-splits. Limitations: each of the three activities still appears once in the later window, so complete removal cannot be distinguished from the branch surviving at a very low rate; the successors of those single occurrences (A19→A44, A20→A08, A25→A42) rest on one trace each and carry no weight; the many relation items are views of one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations; and no screening baseline is included, so the judgement rests on the size and consistency of the differences rather than a chance comparison. Other differences: A15 and A16 remain mandatory but repeat less often per trace, A02 runs in half as many traces while A08 runs in about twice as many, and A39/A40 occur somewhat more often; none of these is part of the main hypothesis. [OCC013, OCC014, OCC002, OCC007, OCC032, OCC033]
