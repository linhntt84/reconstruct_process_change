### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A01 is not observed at all (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it appears in about 95 of 200 traces (roughly 48 in 100), exactly once per trace. [OCC001]
- In the later window, A01 sits late in the trace: its median relative position is about 0.85, and at least a quarter of its occurrences are the final event of a trace. [POS001]
- A01 is placed after A18. Earlier, A18's outgoing transitions were spread over many successors with none above about 19%; in the later window, about 65% of A18's outgoing transitions lead directly to A01. The two activities never co-occurred earlier, whereas later they co-occur in about 48 in 100 traces, always with A18 first. A01 itself is followed by another activity in roughly 6 in 10 of its occurrences (about ten different successors, largest shares A10 about 17%, A07 about 15%, A12 about 14%) and otherwise ends the trace. [DIR017, REL001, DIR001]
- A02 is repeated far more often in the later window: earlier it occurred at most once per trace (mean 0.48, in 48% of traces); later its mean is 1.01 per trace (in 54% of traces) and it recurs in about 25 in 100 traces. More predecessors now route into it: A09 to A02 rises from about 38% to 65% of A09's outgoing transitions, A04 to A02 from 13% to 23%, A05 to A02 from 7% to 15%. [OCC002, DIR008, DIR003, DIR004]
- A08 stops repeating: earlier it recurred in about 20 in 100 traces (mean 0.87 occurrences per trace at 46.5% participation); later it occurs at most once per trace (mean 0.475, participation 47.5%). [OCC008]
- A14 becomes more widespread but stops repeating: participation rises from about 20% to 47.5% while recurrence falls from 12.5% to 0; its outgoing mix shifts away from A08 (63% to 24%) toward A15 (new, about 19%) and A06 (5% to 21%). [OCC014, DIR013, DIR007]
- Smaller shifts: A09 now appears in fewer traces (about 48% to 26%) but recurs in some; the cycle A07 to A12 to A19 back to A07 is traversed more often (A12 to A19 62% to 78%, A19 to A07 56% to 77%, A07 to A12 20% to 30%); A18's mean relative position moves slightly earlier (0.75 to 0.68). [OCC009, DIR011, DIR018, DIR006, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window includes a new activity, A01, executed once near the end of the process and typically directly after A18. In the earlier window A01 is absent from all 200 traces, while in the later window it runs in about 95 of 200 traces, late in the trace (median relative position about 0.85). A18 to A01 becomes A18's dominant outgoing transition (about 65%, versus no successor above about 19% earlier), A01 and A18 co-occur in about 48 in 100 later traces with A18 always first (they never co-occurred earlier), and A01 then branches to about ten different activities or ends the trace. Occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators all show this directly and consistently, and a difference from 0 to roughly 48 in 100 traces is far outside what random re-splits of a stable process produce. A limitation: zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that A01 was not observed then, so a genuinely new activity cannot be distinguished from one previously unsampled, and the placement is directly established only for the A18 to A01 link. [OCC001, POS001, DIR017, REL001, DIR001]

Other differences: A02 recurs in about 25 in 100 later traces (mean 0.48 to 1.01) with increased inflow from A09, A04 and A05; A08 no longer recurs (about 20 in 100 traces earlier, none later); A14 participates in about twice as many traces (20% to 47.5%) but no longer recurs, shifting its successors from A08 toward A15 and A06; the A07–A12–A19 cycle is traversed more often; A09 appears in fewer traces (about 48% to 26%) while recurring in some; A18's position moves slightly earlier. These are reported separately and are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC002, DIR008, DIR003, DIR004, OCC008, OCC014, DIR013, DIR006, DIR011, DIR018, OCC009, POS002]
