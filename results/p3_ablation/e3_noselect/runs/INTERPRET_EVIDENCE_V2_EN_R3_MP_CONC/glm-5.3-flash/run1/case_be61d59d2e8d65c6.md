### Observed differences

- Three activities nearly disappear in the later window: A19 ran in about 33 in 100 traces before but in about 1 in 200 after, A20 in about 29 in 100 before and 1 in 200 after, and A25 in about 40 in 100 before and 1 in 200 after [OCC018, OCC019, OCC023].
- These activities also stopped co-occurring with their surroundings: before, A19, A20 and A25 each co-occurred with A27, A28 or A29 in roughly 28–40 in 100 traces, while in the later window such co-occurrence is essentially absent (about 1 in 200 traces); pairs among A19, A20 and A25 themselves went from mostly separate occurrences to neither appearing [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012].
- Routing into this segment changed accordingly: before, about 52 in 100 outgoing transitions of A27 went to A28 and about 44 in 100 went to A19, A20 or A25, whereas in the later window about 98 in 100 go to A28; A28 likewise led to A29 in about 43 in 100 transitions before (with about 52 in 100 going to A19, A20 or A25) and about 97 in 100 lead to A29 in the later window [DIR027, DIR028].
- The remaining chain became tighter: A27 was followed by A28 within one step in about 52 in 100 cases before and about 98 in 100 after, A28 was followed by A29 within one step in about 43 in 100 before and about 96 in 100 after, and A27 reached A29 typically after three steps before (about 90 in 100) but after two steps in the later window (about 96 in 100); consistently, A27's mean relative position moved slightly later (about 0.46 to 0.48) and A29's slightly earlier (about 0.57 to 0.55) [LAG001, LAG004, LAG002, POS004, POS006].
- A15 and A16 still occur in every trace, but repeat less often: mean occurrences per trace fell from about 1.43 to about 1.31, and traces containing a repeat fell from about 31 in 100 to about 24 in 100 [OCC014, OCC015].
- A10 participates more often (about 16 in 100 traces before, 21 in 100 after; mean occurrences 0.18 to 0.27) and its successor mix shifted, with A26 and A41 gaining share while A15 and A21 lost it [OCC010, DIR010]. Participation also rose modestly for A06 (about 13 to 18 in 100 traces) and A39 (about 16 to 21 in 100) [OCC006, OCC036].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch in which A19, A20 and A25 executed between A27/A28 and A29 is no longer exercised: in the earlier window these activities ran in roughly a third to two fifths of cases, were reached from A27 and A28, and often returned to A28 before the trace reached A29, whereas in the later window each appears in about 1 of 200 traces, A27 and A28 lead almost exclusively to A28 and A29 respectively, and the A27→A28 and A28→A29 steps became immediately adjacent [OCC018, OCC019, OCC023, DIR027, DIR028, LAG001, LAG004, REL003, REL010].

The change is shown directly and consistently across occurrence, relation, direction and lag indicators whose supports cover all 200 traces in both windows, and it does not rest on the after-window outgoing profiles of A19, A20 and A25, which each rest on a single trace and were deliberately not interpreted [DIR019, DIR020, DIR025]. An important competing explanation remains: zero participation only means the activities were not observed, so a removed branch and one that is still possible but now almost never taken look identical here [OCC018, OCC019, OCC023].

Other differences: A15 and A16, while still universal, repeat less often (mean about 1.43 to 1.31 occurrences per trace; repeats in about 31 down to 24 in 100 traces) [OCC014, OCC015]; A10 occurs in more traces and more often per trace and routes differently afterwards [OCC010, DIR010]; and A06 and A39 participate modestly more often [OCC006, OCC036].
