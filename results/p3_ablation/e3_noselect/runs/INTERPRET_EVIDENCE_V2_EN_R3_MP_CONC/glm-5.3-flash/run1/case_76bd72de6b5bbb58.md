### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was substituted for A03 at the same point of the process: A03, which earlier ran in about 45 in 100 traces and was followed mainly by A11 and then A10, A09 and A12, is no longer observed, while A01 now runs in about 50 in 100 traces at the same typical position, with nearly the same successor profile, and with the upstream activities A09, A11, A05, A07, A10, A02, A12 and A14 now leading to A01 where they previously led to A03. The change is shown directly by positional and relational evidence rather than only by occurrence counts, and it is consistent across occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators; A01 and A03 never share a trace, as expected if one took over the other's slot.
