### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The largest shifts — successor shares moving about 10–13 points after A05, A13 and A41, and participation moving 6–7 points in two mirrored activity groups — are of a size that random re-splits of an unchanged process also produce, so sampling variation remains a full explanation.
