### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment formed by A36, A37 and A42 was relocated and made mandatory. In the earlier window the process branched after A26: in about 54 in 100 traces it ran A26 → A27 → A31, and in about 46 in 100 it ran the A36/A37/A42 block (in no fixed internal order) instead of A27, with the block leading into A31, while A25 was followed immediately by A28. In the later window the branch is gone: A26 leads to A27 and on to A31 in essentially every trace, and the block runs in every trace between A25 and A28 (A25 → block of three → A28), before A26, A27 and A31. The supporting signs are direct and consistent across four indicator families — successor changes at A25, A26, A36, A37 and A42 [DIR025, DIR026, DIR036, DIR037, DIR042], co-occurrence and order flips between the block and A25/A26/A27/A28/A31 [REL002, REL005, REL009, REL012, REL014, REL020], the A25→A28 distance moving from one to four steps, matching a three-activity insertion [LAG001], and position shifts in the predicted directions [POS002, POS004, POS006] — though these items largely describe one underlying restructuring rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: the evidence contains no screening baseline, so the number of qualifying items cannot be checked against a chance expectation, and the data cannot distinguish a single move of the block from two separate changes (dropping the old branch after A26 plus inserting a mandatory segment before A28); both windows are finite samples of 200 traces.

Other differences: modest routing shifts between A20–A24 and A32, a rebalancing after A01 toward A05 with higher A17 participation, and slightly fewer occurrences of A44, A48 and A49 in the later window [DIR021, DIR022, DIR023, DIR001, OCC016, OCC019, OCC020, OCC021, OCC022, OCC023, OCC039, OCC042, OCC043].
