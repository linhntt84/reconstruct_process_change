### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A06 was removed from the process (or is no longer executed), eliminating the alternating A03–A06 ending. In the earlier window, traces finished with A03 and A06 adjacent in either order; in the later window, A06 is unobserved in all 200 traces, A03 is the last event of every trace with no observed successor, and A04 now leads straight to A03 (immediately, in all cases) or to A02 in roughly equal shares instead of routing to A06.
