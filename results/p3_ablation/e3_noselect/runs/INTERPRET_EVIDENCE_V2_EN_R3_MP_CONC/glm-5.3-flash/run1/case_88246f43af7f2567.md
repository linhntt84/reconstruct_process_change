### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the routing around A03 was restructured. In the earlier window, A02 and A04 typically led into A03, which led on to A11 or A12, with A12 almost always the final event of the trace; in the later window, A02 and A04 lead straight to A12 without passing through A03, while A03 is reached from A16 and leads on to A10 or A11. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR010]

The change is shown directly and consistently: the successor profiles of A02, A04, A03 and A16 each differ more than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits; co-occurrence of A03 with A02, A04 and A12 fell from 24–49 in 100 traces to zero, while co-occurrence with A10 and A16 rose from zero to about 30 in 100; A02 and A04 are now immediately followed by A12 in every observed pair; and A03's lower participation (49 to 30 in 100 traces) and later position fit the same rerouting. The same small set of activities recurs across the successor, co-occurrence, lag, position and occurrence indicators. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR010, REL001, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001, LAG003, LAG004, OCC003, POS002]

Limitations: the later successor profile of A02 rests on 28 outgoing transitions and A12's earlier profile on only 5, so rarely used edges could be missed by these samples, and the evidence contains no screening baseline against which to compare the number of qualifying items.
