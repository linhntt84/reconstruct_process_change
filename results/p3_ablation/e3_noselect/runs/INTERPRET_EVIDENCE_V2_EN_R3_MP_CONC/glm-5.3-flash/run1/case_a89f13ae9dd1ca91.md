### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured: the block of three activities A21, A22 and A23, which in the earlier window executed in every case between A39 and A24 in varying order, no longer runs, and instead exactly one of three previously unobserved activities, A14, A15 or A16, executes between A39 and A24 in the later window. The change is shown directly and consistently: A39's successors switch from the old trio to the new trio, each new activity is always followed by A24, the A39-to-A24 distance shortens from four to two positions, and the relation pattern flips from "always together" to "essentially never together". With 200 traces per window, participations moving from 200 of 200 to 1 of 200 and from 0 of 200 to 60–74 of 200 are not plausibly explained by rare-variant sampling. [OCC013, OCC014, OCC015, OCC020, OCC021, OCC022, DIR010, DIR011, DIR012, DIR030, LAG010, REL025, REL027]

The evidence cannot show whether the old block was removed entirely or only became optional: one later-window trace still contains A21, A22 and A23, so the old path may persist at a low rate. It also cannot distinguish genuinely new activities from relabelled ones; only the behavioural substitution is established. [OCC020, OCC021, OCC022, REL025]

Other differences: A40 and A41 are executed more often, in more traces, and with more repetition and interleaving with A24, A39 and each other, and A41 takes a larger share of A40's outgoing transitions (about 48 to 53 in 100); several smaller routing-share shifts exist elsewhere, for example after A02, A18, A28 and A33. These differences are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC029, OCC030, REL040, REL041, REL042, REL043, REL044, DIR031, DIR032, DIR002, DIR014, DIR021, DIR025]
