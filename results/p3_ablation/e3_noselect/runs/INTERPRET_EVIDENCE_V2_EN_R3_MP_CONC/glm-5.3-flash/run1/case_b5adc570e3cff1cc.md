### Observed differences

- The three-way choice between A14, A15 and A20 kept its character but changed its place in the flow: in both windows exactly one of the three runs per case and they never occur together — about 38, 31 and 31 in 100 cases in the earlier window, about 34, 35 and 31 in 100 in the later window — while their mean event position moved from about 0.60 to about 0.32 of the trace. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the single-pass choice between A14, A15 and A20 now executes early in the case, between A40 and A21, whereas it previously executed late, alongside A23 between A22 and A27. In the later window, A40 flows into whichever of A14/A15/A20 is chosen, which then flows into A21, and A22 runs directly to A23 and A23 directly to A27; earlier, A40 flowed straight into A21 and the chosen activity ran after A22 (before or after A23) and before A27. Since exactly one of the three still executes in every case, with none added, removed or repeated, this is a change in where an existing block runs, not in how often it runs. [DIR029, DIR010, DIR011, DIR016, DIR018, DIR019, REL001, REL002, REL008, LAG002, LAG004]

The change is shown directly and consistently: complete successor replacement at A40, A14, A15, A20, A22 and A23; a full order flip against A21 and A22 and a change from mixed to fixed order against A23; and step-distance changes matching an insertion before A21 and a removal from the A22–A27 segment. Each of these differences exceeds those seen in essentially all (about 199 of 200) random re-splits of the pooled windows, and the DIR, REL, LAG and POS items describe one underlying difference from different angles rather than independent confirmations. [DIR029, DIR010, DIR011, DIR016, DIR018, DIR019, REL003, REL009, REL014, REL005, REL011, REL016, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Limitations: the trio's profiles rest on about 60–76 traces per window; the small shift in choice probabilities inside the block (A14 about 38 → 34 in 100 cases) is only moderately supported and could be sampling; positions are event positions, not clock times; and no screening expectation was provided to benchmark the item count. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the rewiring at A40 and the simplification of the A22–A23 segment were two coincident independent changes rather than one relocation of the block, although the coherent distance and position changes favour a single relocation. [OCC013, OCC014, OCC019, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: the A10/A11 loop and A41 now repeat less often per case, and after A27 the flow is routed more often via A19 with exits to A28 from A16/A17 instead of A19, with participation shifting from A16 to A17; these are separate from the relocation hypothesis. [OCC009, OCC010, DIR009, OCC026, DIR020, DIR015, DIR012, DIR013, OCC015, OCC016]
