### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the alternative block formed by A19, A20 and A25 between A41 and A26 is no longer executed: earlier, each trace passed from A41 through exactly one of these three alternatives (about 33, 27 and 33 in 100 of A41's continuations) onward to A26, whereas in the later window none of the three is observed and A41 is directly followed by A26 in about 92 in 100 of its executions, with the A41→A26 distance shrinking from two positions to one. The disappearance rests on 61–71 traces per activity in the earlier window, so it is far beyond a rare-variant fluctuation, and the occurrence, successor and lag evidence all show the same structural difference. The evidence cannot distinguish whether the three activities were removed from the process or whether this step is now skipped, nor can it rule out that the two windows sample different process variants rather than a changed one. [OCC016, OCC017, OCC022, DIR041, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, LAG006, REL015, REL018, REL023, REL030]

Other differences: participation of several branch activities moved toward an even split (A01, A05, A07, A09 down about 6–9 points; A03, A11, A12, A13 up about 8–9 points), consistent with the two alternative branches converging on A14 being taken about 59/41 earlier and about 50/50 later, though the branch choice itself is not directly observed; within A21–A24 the route through A24 to A31 became more frequent; A15 and A16 repeat slightly more often. These shifts are not explained by the removal of the A19/A20/A25 block and may constitute additional routing changes.
