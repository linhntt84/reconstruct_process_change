### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the flow gained four activities — A07, A15, A16 and A17 — which in the later window are wired into existing paths while several direct handovers are rerouted: A08 now reaches A18 through A07 instead of handing over to A05 directly; A10 now leads to A16 and A15, which continue to A03/A02 and back to A10, instead of leading to A10, A03 or A02 itself; A17 feeds into A09; A18 hands over to A14 and A17 instead of A02; A12 hands over to A05 instead of A18; and A05 hands over to A02 instead of A09 or A14 [DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011, DIR012].

Supporting signs: the four activities go from no observations to participation in 33–70 in 100 traces, and they co-occur with their entry activities in every trace in which those run (A07 always with A08, A16 always with A10); the new direct handovers appear exactly where the previous ones disappear; the same rerouting is visible consistently across occurrence, direction, relation, position and lag indicators; and the key differences were larger than in 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled windows, so sampling variation alone cannot account for them [OCC006, OCC014, OCC015, OCC016, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL034, REL061].

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A07, A15, A16 and A17 were not observed then, not that they could not occur; the many agreeing items largely re-describe one underlying restructuring rather than providing independent confirmations; and a different mix of case variants between the two windows cannot be ruled out as an alternative to a modified flow [OCC006, OCC014, OCC015, OCC016, DIR006].
