### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the relative use of existing alternative routes after A26 changed: in the later window, routes through A30 and A31 were taken less often (each in about 42 in 100 traces versus about 50 before) and routes through A32 and A33 more often (about 58–59 versus 50), with A26 itself redirecting its outgoing transitions from A31 (about 28% to 18%) toward A33 (about 24% to 32%). Every involved activity remains substantial in both windows and no path appears or disappears, so this reads as existing paths being taken with different frequencies rather than paths being added, removed or bypassed; the pair-internal balance shifts (A30 increasingly continuing to A31, A31 increasingly exiting to A27, A33 increasingly continuing to A32) point the same way. The change is observed directly at the decision point — A26's outgoing distribution differs from more than about 94 in 100 random re-splits — and the four downstream participation shifts are mirror-symmetric (each beyond about 92 in 100). [DIR016, OCC017, OCC018, OCC019, OCC020, DIR019, DIR020, DIR021, DIR022]

An important competing explanation remains finite-sample variation: with 200 traces per window, share differences of 8–10 percentage points can arise without any change, and the evidence file contains no screening baseline against which the number of qualifying items could be compared. The coordinated direction across the decision point, the four downstream activities and the pair-internal transitions makes chance an unlikely but not excluded account, and the evidence cannot distinguish a change in A26's own routing behaviour from a changed mix of cases reaching it. [DIR016, OCC017, OCC018, OCC019, OCC020]

Other differences: (i) the alternative groups A01/A03/A04/A05 versus A02/A06/A07/A08 swapped prevalence (about 57/44 to 48–49/51–52 in 100 traces), with additional internal redirection from A07 and A08; (ii) repetition around A37 and A38 increased, with more A37→A38 traffic, more returns to A37 and fewer exits to A41; (iii) A35's successor mix moved from A34 toward A36, with A34's participation dropping to about 12–13 in 100 traces (only 25 supporting traces, so weak); (iv) within the A16–A19 cluster, A19 and A16 gained while A18 and A17 lost.
