### Observed differences

- The clearest difference concerns repetition of the block A30 → A31 → A36. In the earlier window, each of A30, A31 and A36 occurred about 3.4–3.5 times per trace, was present in every trace, and repeated within traces in about 75 in 100 cases; in the later window each occurs about once per trace (about 1.05) and repeats are almost gone (about 1–2 in 100 cases). [OCC027, OCC028, OCC033]
- The relationship between A30 and A36 changed accordingly: they still co-occur in every trace, but before, the pair was interleaved or repeated in 75 in 100 traces (with a fixed A30-before-A36 order in only 25 in 100), whereas in the later window A30 precedes A36 in 98 in 100 traces. A30 is also followed by A36 within two steps in about 89 in 100 traces, up from about 12 in 100. [REL001, LAG002]
- A36's outgoing transitions shifted from looping back to exiting: before, about 70 in 100 led to A30 and about 29 in 100 to A25; in the later window about 93 in 100 lead to A25 and only about 4 in 100 to A30. The links A30→A31 and A31→A36 remain dominant in both windows (about 97–99 in 100 of outgoing transitions). [DIR036, DIR030, DIR031]
- The event positions of A30 and A36 became much less spread out (standard deviation about 0.12 before versus about 0.07 after for both activities), consistent with a single pass instead of several. [POS001, POS002]
- Repetition also eased elsewhere, but far less markedly: A38 fell from about 1.0 to 0.75 occurrences per trace (participation 52 to 44 in 100 traces) and A37 from 0.47 to 0.32; A40 fell from 1.76 to 1.56 occurrences per trace with recurrence from 42 to 34 in 100, and A41–A43 decreased slightly. [OCC034, OCC035, OCC036, OCC037, OCC038, OCC039]
- A10 was observed in 29 of 200 traces before but only 16 of 200 after (about 15 versus 8 in 100). [OCC010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the repeated execution of the A30–A31–A36 block stopped: in the earlier window these three activities each ran roughly three to four times per trace in interleaved fashion, with A36 returning to A30 in about 70 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, while in the later window each runs once in the fixed order A30 → A31 → A36, after which A36 continues to A25 in about 93 in 100 of its transitions. [OCC027, OCC028, OCC033, REL001, DIR030, DIR031, DIR036, LAG002] The change is direct and consistent across occurrence, relational, lag, positional and successor evidence: the three occurrence items each show a drop of about 2.4 occurrences per trace at empirical strength about 0.995, and the position spread of A30 and A36 narrowed by roughly 40%. [OCC027, OCC028, OCC033, POS001, POS002] Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish whether the repeat cycle was removed entirely or now terminates after far fewer iterations (residual recurrence of about 1–2 in 100 traces), and the lag items on self-repetition after the change rest on only one or two observations; nor can the evidence show whether the change lies in the A36→A30 back-edge itself or in how often the block is iterated. [LAG001, LAG003, LAG004]

Other differences: the milder repetition decreases around A37/A38 and A40–A43, and the lower participation of A10 (about 15 to 8 in 100 traces), are not part of this hypothesis. [OCC034, OCC035, OCC036, OCC037, OCC038, OCC039, OCC010]
