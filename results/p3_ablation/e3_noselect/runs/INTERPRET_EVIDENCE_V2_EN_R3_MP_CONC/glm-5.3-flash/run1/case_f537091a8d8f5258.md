### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the execution order within the jointly occurring block changed: A05 and A06, which previously formed the tail of the block directly after A09 in no fixed mutual order, are now executed in its middle — A05 directly after A01 or A04, then A06, then A07, with A09 as the final activity — and A05 before A06 has become a fixed order. The signs are direct and consistent across four indicator families: complete order reversals between {A05, A06} and {A07, A09} (from about 96 of 200 traces one way and none the other, to about 109 the other way and almost none back), matching position and step-distance shifts, and successor changes (A06→A05 and A09→A05 gone; A06→A07 and A01/A04→A05 new); for the key items, differences this large arose in fewer than about 1 of 200 random re-splits of unchanged data. The evidence cannot show whether a single flow was rewired or the later window contains a different mix of complete case variants, and the surviving minority orderings (A07 before A06, A05 before A04, A04 before A01, each about 14–17 in 100) suggest several variants coexist in the later window; A09's routing after the change rests on only about 12 observed outgoing transitions. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, DIR004, DIR005, DIR008, LAG002, LAG007, LAG009]

Other differences: the A03/A08/A10/A11 block is executed less often (about 28 → 21 in 100 traces) with essentially unchanged internal routing, and the A01/A04/A05/A06/A07/A09 block somewhat more often (about 48 → 54 in 100); these participation shifts are not explained by the ordering hypothesis.
