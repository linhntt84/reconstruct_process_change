### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A01 appears as a step inserted between A12 and its successors: traces that earlier moved from A12 directly to A04 or A13 now move from A12 to A01 first, with A01 then branching to A04 (about 53 in 100) or A13 (about 47 in 100), much as A12 did before. The placement change is observed directly in A12's successor profile, which shifted from a roughly even A04/A13 split to A01 alone while A01's own outgoing transitions reproduce that split, and it is consistent with A01's emergence from unobserved to about 30 in 100 traces, with co-occurrence between A01 and A12, A04 and A13 emerging only in the later window, and with the A12→A04, A12→A13 and A12→A12 step distances each moving about one step later, as an inserted activity would produce. Limitations: each window is a sample of only 200 traces, A01's earlier absence strictly means only that it was not observed, and the evidence cannot distinguish a purely added step from one that partly replaced existing repetitions; a competing explanation it cannot rule out is that the slightly lower participation of A12 and the reduced repetition of A04 and A13 are independent changes rather than consequences of the insertion. [DIR007, DIR001, OCC001, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG005]
