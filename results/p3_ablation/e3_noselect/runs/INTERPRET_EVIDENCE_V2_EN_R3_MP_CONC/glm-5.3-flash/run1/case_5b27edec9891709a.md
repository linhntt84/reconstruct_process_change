### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the three-activity block A31/A32/A37 changed its placement and optionality: it previously ran in every case between A20 and A23, whereas in the later window A20 passes directly to A23, and the block instead runs in about 47 in 100 cases in the slot after A21 and before A26, where it alternates with A22 — the two are now mutually exclusive alternatives. A22, previously executed in every case, is skipped in about 46 in 100 cases, and A23 now runs without A22 in about 47 in 100 cases. The block's internal mixed ordering and its once-per-case execution are unchanged; what changed is where it sits, what it leads to (A26 instead of A23) and whether it runs at all. [DIR014, DIR015, DIR018, DIR019, DIR024, REL006, REL009, REL011, REL014, REL020, LAG001, LAG005]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the successor changes at A20, A21, A31, A32 and A37 are observed in the direction profiles; the order reversals (A23/A31, A21/A31) and the collapse of A22–block co-occurrence are observed in the relation profiles; the lag changes (A20→A23 from four steps to one, A21→A26 from always two to two or four) match the same restructuring; and occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag items all point the same way, with participation drops of 46–53 in 100 traces and empirical strengths of about 0.995. [DIR014, DIR015, DIR018, DIR019, DIR024, REL006, REL009, REL011, REL014, REL017, LAG001, LAG005, OCC017, OCC018, OCC019, OCC024]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a single modification point (for example only A20's outgoing routing changing) from a reading in which A20 skips the block and the block was separately re-inserted as an optional detour after A21; both fit the same observations. The position shifts serve only as corroboration, since event positions alone do not establish that an activity was moved. [DIR014, DIR015, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008]

Other differences: the modest, coordinated participation and routing shifts in the A01–A09 group (about 8 in 100 traces, moderate strengths) are not part of this hypothesis, and the evidence does not establish them beyond what sampling variation between two 200-trace windows could produce. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR002, DIR004, DIR006, DIR008]
