### Observed differences

- The execution order between two groups of activities flipped completely. Before, A30, A31 and A36 always ran before A21 and A22 (100 in 100 traces); in the later window, A21 and A22 ran before A30, A31 and A36 in 199 of 200 traces. All six activities still occurred together in every trace in both windows, and A30, A31 and A36 kept their mixed mutual order, so this is a reordering of blocks, not a participation change. - Successors changed accordingly. Before, A20 led to A30, A31 or A36 (about 40, 33 and 28 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions) and A21 led only to A25; in the later window, A20 led to A22 in about 99 in 100 transitions, and A21 led to A30, A31 and A36 (about 36, 33 and 31 in 100) while its transition to A25 appeared in only about 1 in 100. A30, A31 and A36 each lost their observed transition to A22 (from roughly 28–38 in 100 to under 1 in 100 or unobserved) and gained one to A25 (about 31–36 in 100). [DIR014, DIR015, DIR018, DIR019, DIR024]

- Event positions moved with the same pattern: A22 now sits about 38 in 100 of the way through the trace (before about 47) and A21 about 53 (before about 62), while A30, A31 and A36 moved to about 59 (before about 41); A20 (about 35) and A25 (about 65) stayed in place. - Separations changed to match: A20 was followed by A22 within one step in about 99 in 100 later traces (before: four steps in all traces), and A21 was followed by A25 within four steps (before: one step in all traces). Pairs spanning the old arrangement — A20 with A30, A31 or A36, and A30, A31 or A36 with A22 — occurred within five steps in all 200 earlier traces but in only about 2 of 200 later traces. - Outside this segment the differences are small: A01's outgoing shares shifted away from A05 (about 38 to 21 in 100) towards A03 and A04, A37's participation fell from about 21 to 16 in 100 and A38's mean occurrences from about 0.86 to 0.76; the remaining occurrence and routing differences are of similar or smaller size with low empirical strength. [DIR001, OCC023, OCC024, OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of activities A30, A31 and A36 changed its place in the flow: before, it executed between A20 and A22 (A20 → A30/A31/A36 → A22 → A21 → A25); in the later window it executes after A21 and before A25 (A20 → A22 → A21 → A30/A31/A36 → A25). The change is shown directly by the successor changes at A20, A21, A30, A31 and A36, the complete order flip between {A21, A22} and {A30, A31, A36} across all six activity pairs, and the matching lag changes; the position shifts are confined to the five affected activities while A20 and A25 stay put, which fits a relocation of this middle block rather than a general reshuffle. With the flipped order present in 199–200 of 200 traces and more than twenty consistent items at near-maximal empirical strength, a sampling explanation is implausible. The evidence cannot distinguish whether A30, A31 and A36 moved later or A21 and A22 moved earlier — the two produce identical observations here — and it says nothing about the cause. [DIR014, DIR015, DIR018, DIR019, DIR024, REL001, REL006, POS001, POS005, LAG001, LAG005]

Other differences: A01's routing among A03, A04 and A05 shifted (share to A05 down from about 38 to 21 in 100, to A03 up from about 25 to 38); this rests on one routing item plus weak occurrence changes, so it may be a minor path-share shift or sampling noise, as may the small declines at A37 and A38. [DIR001, OCC023, OCC024]
