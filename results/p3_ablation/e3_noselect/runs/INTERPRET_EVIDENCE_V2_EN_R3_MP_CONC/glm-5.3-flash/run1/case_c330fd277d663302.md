### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A03, previously a mandatory final step executed once per case directly after A02, is no longer executed, so that traces which previously ended with the sequence A02 → A03 now end at A02, which still runs in every case and now occupies the final trace position. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: A03's participation fell from all 200 traces to none (a difference larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits), the A02→A03 transition disappeared, the pair relation flipped from always co-occurring to A02-only, and A02's position moved to the trace end. [OCC001, DIR002, REL001, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A03 was not observed in the later window, so a residual execution rate too low to surface in 200 traces (below roughly 1–2 in 100 cases) cannot be excluded; the five items largely describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, and no screening baseline was provided to compare the item count against chance expectations. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A03 still executes very rarely rather than having been dropped entirely. [OCC001, POS002]

Other differences: the small redistributions in the outgoing transitions of A01, A04, A05 and A06 (each at most about 1.5 percentage points, with low empirical strength) are not part of this hypothesis and show no clear behavioural change. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
