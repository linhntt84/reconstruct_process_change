### Observed differences

- The routes out of A26 changed markedly. In the earlier window, about 51 in 100 of A26's outgoing transitions went to A27, while about 48 in 100 went to one of A32, A33 or A38 (roughly 14–18 in 100 each); in the later window, about 97 in 100 go to A27, with only residual shares to the other three [DIR026].
- A27's participation rose from about 52 in 100 traces to 199 of 200 traces, occurring once per trace where present [OCC026, POS002].
- A32, A33 and A38 each dropped from about 48–49 in 100 traces to a single trace in 200 [OCC028, OCC029, OCC034, POS004, POS005, POS006]. In the earlier window the three ran together in about 48 in 100 traces, in varying order, each following A26 and preceding A28; in the later window they practically never co-occur with each other, with A26 or with A28 [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015].
- The chain A26–A27–A28 accordingly became near-universal: A26–A27 and A27–A28 co-occurrence rose from about 52 in 100 traces to about 995 in 1,000, with A26 before A27 and A27 before A28 in about 96–97 in 100 of those traces [REL001, REL006]. The A26-to-A28 distance changed from a roughly even mix of two and four event positions (50 vs 47 in 100) to two positions in about 97 in 100 cases, and A27 immediately precedes A28 in essentially every later trace [LAG002, LAG006]. A28's mean event position moved slightly earlier, from about 0.55 to 0.53 of the trace [POS003].
- In the A39/A40 area, repetitions decreased: A39 appears in about 26 in 100 traces before versus 20 in 100 after (mean occurrences 0.44 to 0.32), A40 keeps its participation near 49 in 100 traces but falls from about 0.93 to 0.80 occurrences per trace, and A40's outgoing share shifts from A39 (45 to 39 in 100) towards A41 (52 to 59 in 100) [OCC035, OCC036, DIR039, DIR040].
- A few successor-profile changes rest on very few observations and should not be over-read: A47's outgoing profile changed completely on only 4 versus 5 transitions, and A02's and A08's profiles changed markedly on 11–19 transitions [DIR047, DIR002, DIR008]. Mean occurrences of A43, A44 and A45 each fell slightly, by about 0.04–0.06 per trace [OCC038, OCC039, OCC040].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the alternative route after A26 — the block A32, A33 and A38 that previously executed between A26 and A28 in about half of the cases — is effectively no longer run, while A27 has become a mandatory step, so that virtually all cases now execute A26 → A27 → A28. Earlier, A27 appeared in about 52 in 100 traces and A32/A33/A38 in about 48–49 in 100 each, with A26 leading to either route in roughly equal shares; later, A27 is present in 199 of 200 traces, A32/A33/A38 in one trace each, A26 leads to A27 in about 97 in 100 of its transitions, and the A26-to-A28 distance is uniformly two positions [DIR026, OCC026, OCC028, OCC029, OCC034, REL001, REL006, LAG002]. The pattern is direct and consistent across participation, co-occurrence, successor, order and distance indicators, with most differences larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, though many of these items describe the same underlying structural difference rather than independent confirmations [OCC026, DIR026, REL001, LAG002]. Limitations: A32, A33 and A38 still occur in one trace each, so the evidence shows the route became effectively unobserved rather than provably removed, and it cannot show whether the block's work was relocated elsewhere in the process. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is sampling fluctuation between the two 200-trace windows, but three activities falling from about 97–98 to 1–2 traces while A27 rises from 103 to 199 traces goes well beyond what the random re-split references produce [OCC026, OCC028, OCC029, OCC034].

Other differences: the A39/A40 area shows fewer A40 repetitions and a lower A40-to-A39 transition share, a moderate and separate signal whose origin the evidence does not indicate [OCC035, OCC036, DIR039, DIR040]; the successor-profile changes at A47, A02 and A08 rest on too few transitions to interpret [DIR047, DIR002, DIR008].
