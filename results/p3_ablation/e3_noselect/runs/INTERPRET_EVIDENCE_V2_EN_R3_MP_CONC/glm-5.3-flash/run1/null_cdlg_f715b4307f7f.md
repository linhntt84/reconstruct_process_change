### Observed differences

- The clearest difference lies in the branch after A03. In the earlier window, about 57 in 100 of A03's observed outgoing transitions went to A12 and about 43 in 100 to A06; in the later window the preference reversed, with about 44 in 100 going to A12 and 56 in 100 to A06. A03 itself was seen in about 150 of 200 traces before and about 160 of 200 after, so the source activity remained active at a similar level. [DIR003]
- The two branch activities mirror this: A06 appeared in about 45 in 100 traces before and 57 in 100 after (89 vs 114 traces), while A12 appeared in about 56 in 100 traces before and 43 in 100 after (111 vs 86 traces). Both occurred at most once per trace in both windows, and their participation rates sum to 1.0 in both windows, so together they covered every trace throughout and only the split between them moved. [OCC005, OCC011]
- A01 ran somewhat more often in the later window: participation rose from about 62 to 67 in 100 traces and mean occurrences per trace from about 0.9 to 1.0. [OCC001]
- A13 and A10 ran somewhat less often: A13 fell from about 43 to 36 in 100 traces (mean about 0.52 to 0.42) and A10 from about 41 to 37 (mean about 0.50 to 0.42). [OCC012, OCC009]
- The successor mix after A07 shifted toward A03 (about 36 to 46 in 100 observed outgoing transitions) and away from A02 (about 18 to 12) and A12 (about 7 to 3); A07's mean occurrences per trace dipped slightly (about 1.0 to 0.9) while its participation stayed at about 68 in 100 traces. [DIR006, OCC006]
- The remaining differences are small and scattered: slight decreases for A04, A11, A14, A09 and A15 and modest successor-mix shifts after A05, A09 and A15, several of them resting on fewer than about 40 traces per window. [OCC003, OCC010, OCC013, OCC008, OCC014, DIR005, DIR008, DIR013]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the routing choice after A03 moved between two existing alternatives: A03 led to A06 more often (from about 43 to 56 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions) and to A12 less often (from about 57 to 44 in 100). As a result, A06 was executed in more traces (about 45 to 57 in 100) and A12 in fewer (about 56 to 43 in 100). [DIR003, OCC005, OCC011]

This is a shift in how often existing paths are taken, not a replacement of one path by another: A03, A06 and A12 were all observed in substantial shares of traces in both windows (A03 in roughly 150–160 of 200; A06 and A12 each in roughly 86–114), both branches stayed in use, and neither branch activity changed from single to repeated execution (recurrence zero in both windows). [DIR003, OCC005, OCC011]

The supporting signs are the direct branch-split change at A03, which was larger than in about 99 of 100 random re-splits, and the equal-and-opposite participation moves of A06 and A12 (about 25 traces in 200 each) with their combined coverage unchanged at one occurrence per trace — a pattern consistent with a single redistribution between the two alternatives rather than two unrelated changes. Limitations: the three items are complementary views of one shift rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence shows nothing about what A06 and A12 lead to, since neither has an outgoing profile here. The competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is an unusual random split of an unchanged process, although a difference of this size arose in only about 1–2 of 100 random re-splits. [DIR003, OCC005, OCC011]

Other differences:
- A01 was executed somewhat more often in the later window, while A13 and A10 were executed somewhat less often; these moderate changes are not covered by the routing hypothesis. [OCC001, OCC012, OCC009]
- The successor mix after A07 moved toward A03 and away from A02 and A12, and A07 repeated slightly less often per trace; this is compatible with more flow passing through A03 but concerns a different decision point, so it is not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR006, OCC006]
- Small decreases at A04, A11, A14, A09 and A15 and modest successor-mix shifts after A05, A09 and A15 complete the picture; most are weak, and some rest on fewer than about 40 traces per window, so sampling variation cannot be excluded for them. [OCC003, OCC010, OCC013, OCC008, OCC014, DIR005, DIR008, DIR013]
