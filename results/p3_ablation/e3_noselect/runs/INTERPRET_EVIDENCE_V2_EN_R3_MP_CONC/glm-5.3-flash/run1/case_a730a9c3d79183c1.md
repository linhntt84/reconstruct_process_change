### Observed differences

- Six activities changed from an either/or pattern to joint execution. In the earlier window, A33, A35 and A36 ran together in about 97 of 200 cases and A37, A38 and A39 ran together in about 103 of 200, while activities from different sets shared a case in only about 1–3 of 200. In the later window, all six activities occur in about 98–99 of 100 cases, and the A33/A35/A36 set runs before the A37/A38/A39 set in about 95–97 of those 100 cases. [OCC029, OCC031, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, OCC035, REL051, REL057, REL062, POS006, POS009]
- The order inside each set stayed unfixed in both windows (A33 before A35 in about 49 of 100 earlier vs 46 of 100 later cases; A37 before A38 in about 49 vs 54 of 100), so the change is the disappearance of the choice between the sets, not a reordering within them. [REL049, REL056, REL067, REL071]
- The enlarged block sits in the segment between A30 and A31, ahead of the essentially unchanged closing pair A47/A48: A30's outgoing transitions, previously spread almost evenly over A35–A39 (about 14–18 of 100 each), now lead mostly to A35, A33 and A36 (about 36, 31 and 29 of 100) with almost none to A37–A39; A30 precedes A33 in about 96 of 100 later cases (about 47 of 100 before), the second set now follows A30 four to five steps later rather than one to three, and A33 precedes A47 in about 97 of 100 later cases. [DIR030, DIR033, DIR035, DIR036, REL034, REL042, REL054, LAG024, LAG038, POS004, POS005, REL076]
- A04 moved the other way: it appeared in about 59 of 100 cases before and about 26 of 100 after; A14 now passes directly to A15 in about 79 of 100 of its outgoing transitions (about 43 of 100 before) while A14-to-A04 transitions fell from about 57 to 12 of 100; when A04 does occur it sits later in the case (upper quarter of positions beyond about 0.67 of the case vs 0.17 before) and its outgoing transitions spread from roughly 90 of 100 leading to A15 to about 40 of 100. [OCC004, DIR014, DIR004, POS001, REL001, LAG013]
- Smaller shifts: A02, A06, A08 and A10 each rose from roughly 6–8 of 100 cases to 18–23 of 100 (earlier samples of only 11–15 traces make these weaker); A15 and A16 repeat somewhat more often (recurrence about 33 → 40 of 100) and A43 somewhat less (about 45 → 36 of 100). [OCC002, OCC006, OCC008, OCC010, OCC015, OCC016, OCC039]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the either/or choice between two three-activity blocks was removed: A33, A35, A36 and A37, A38, A39, which in the earlier window replaced each other (one block per case, each in about half of the cases), are in the later window executed together in about 98 of 100 cases, with the A33/A35/A36 block consistently before the A37/A38/A39 block in the segment between A30 and A31. Supporting signs: participation of all six activities rose from about 50 to about 99 of 100 cases, each difference larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits [OCC029, OCC031, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, OCC035]; cross-block co-occurrence rose from about 1 of 100 to about 98 of 100 with a stable block-level order [REL051, REL057, REL062, REL053]; the rerouting is visible directly in the successor profiles of A30, A33, A35 and A36 and in the longer distances from A30 to the second block [DIR030, DIR033, DIR035, LAG024, LAG038]. Limitations: the evidence shows participation and ordering outcomes, not the routing mechanism itself; the order within each block remains mixed, so no fixed internal sequence can be claimed; and about 2 of 100 later cases still show only one block, so a residual choice cannot be excluded.

Other differences: A04 is now largely skipped — participation fell from about 59 to 26 of 100 cases, the earlier A14→A04→A15 insertion all but disappeared, and A04 occurs later and with dispersed successors when it occurs [OCC004, DIR014, DIR004, POS001, REL001]; A02, A06, A08 and A10 participate roughly two to four times as often, though on small earlier samples [OCC002, OCC006, OCC008, OCC010]; A15 and A16 repeat slightly more often and A43 slightly less [OCC015, OCC016, OCC039]; A30 sits slightly earlier and A31 slightly later in the case [POS004, POS005].

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the A04 decline and the block enlargement are two aspects of a single restructuring rather than independent changes, and that the second block became unconditionally mandatory versus a selection condition that now almost always triggers both; the data show the outcomes but not which reading applies. [OCC004, DIR014, REL051]
