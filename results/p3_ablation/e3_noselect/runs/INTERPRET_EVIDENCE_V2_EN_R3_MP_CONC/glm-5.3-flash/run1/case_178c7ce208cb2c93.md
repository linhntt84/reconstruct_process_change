### Observed differences

- Activity A01 did not occur at all in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it occurs in every trace, exactly once per trace [OCC001].
- In the later window, A01 is always followed directly by A03 (all of its observed outgoing transitions go to A03); in the earlier window A01 had no observed transitions because it never occurred [DIR001, OCC001].
- The exits into A03 were rerouted through A01. Earlier, about 78 in 100 of A02's observed outgoing transitions went to A03 and about 22 went to A04; later, about 76 go to A01 and about 24 to A04, and a direct A02→A03 transition is no longer observed. A04 shows the same shift: earlier about 78 of its outgoing transitions went to A02 and about 22 to A03; later about 76 go to A02 and about 24 to A01 [DIR002, DIR003].
- A01's relations changed from never co-occurring to always co-occurring: earlier, A02, A03 and A04 each appeared without A01 in all traces; later, A01 co-occurs with each of them in all traces, always after A02 and after A04, and always before A03 [REL001, REL002, REL003].
- The distance from the A02/A04 cycle to A03 grew by one step: A02 reached A03 one step later in about 78 in 100 observed pairs earlier, but two steps later in about 76 in 100 (three steps in the remaining ~24) later; A04→A03 distances moved from 1–2 steps to 2–3 steps [LAG001, LAG002].
- In the later window, A01 always sits at the same relative position, about two-thirds through the trace [POS001].
- The A02/A04 cycle itself is essentially unchanged: A04 precedes A02 in about 78 in 100 traces earlier and about 76 in 100 later, a negligible difference [REL004].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new step, A01, now runs between the repeating A02/A04 phase and A03. In the earlier window the process left the A02/A04 cycle directly into A03 (from A02 in roughly three quarters of exits, from A04 in most of the rest); in the later window both exits pass through A01, which runs exactly once per trace, after A02 and after A04, and immediately before A03. The support is direct and consistent across occurrence, routing, relation and distance indicators, and the surrounding structure — the A02/A04 cycle and its internal order — is unchanged, which fits a new step being added rather than one path replacing another [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, POS001]. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is a rare residual direct exit to A03: an unobserved edge in the outgoing profile does not prove the direct A02→A03 or A04→A03 transitions are gone, and 200 traces per window may not reveal a very infrequent remaining path [DIR002, DIR003].

Other differences: A02 and A04 occupy slightly earlier relative positions in the later window (A02's median position about 0.5 → 0.33; A04's mean about 0.11 → 0.08), which is consistent with traces being one event longer at the exit and does not indicate a separate relocation of these activities [POS002, POS003].
