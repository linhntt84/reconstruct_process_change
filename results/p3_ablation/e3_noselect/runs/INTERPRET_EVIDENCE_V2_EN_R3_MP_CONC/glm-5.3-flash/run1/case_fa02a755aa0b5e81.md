### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural rerouting around A02 and A01: in the earlier window, A02 opened traces and distributed them to A07, A03, A08, A13 and occasionally A05, while A01 closed the A06/A11/A12 branch, running after A11 and A12 and immediately before A05; in the later window, A02 is not executed at all, A01 runs at the start of traces and leads into A06, and A11 and A12 pass directly to A05 instead of through A01. [OCC002, DIR002, POS001, DIR001, REL003, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG007, LAG008]

The support is direct and consistent rather than a matter of item count: A02's participation falls from 53 to 0 of 200 traces together with all five of its outgoing transitions, a difference larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits; A01's successor changes completely, its order relative to A06, A11 and A12 reverses in both directions, and the positional distances to A05 shift exactly as this rerouting implies. Several items describe the same underlying behaviour, so they should be read as one body of evidence, not independent confirmations. [OCC002, DIR002, DIR001, REL003, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG007, LAG008]

An important competing explanation remains: zero participation only means A02 was not observed in the later sample, so A02 and the old A11/A12 → A01 → A05 route could still occur at a rate too low to surface in 200 traces; the evidence shows the old patterns are unobserved, not that they are impossible. [OCC002, REL005, DIR007]

Other differences: the A03/A07/A08/A13 cluster, which A02 used to feed, now enters mainly through an early-positioned A07, with A03, A08 and A13 sending larger shares to A05; A06 occurs less often per trace and later in the trace; and participation shifted slightly for A05, A10, A08, A13, A04 and A09. These are consistent with the same restructuring but rest partly on small samples (about 20–30 traces for A08 and A13).
