- The closing sequence of activities differs between the windows. - All eleven evidence items describe this one difference, and each difference is larger than those seen in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the order of the final three activities changed: A01, which previously ran last in every case after A06 and A07, now runs immediately after A08 and before them, so the closing sequence changed from A08 → A06 → A07 → A01 to A08 → A01 → A06 → A07, with A06 and A07 each shifted one step later while keeping their mutual order. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the pairwise order relations flipped in 100% of traces, the successor of A08 changed from A06 to A01, A01 gained a successor and A07 lost its successor, and the lag distances from A08 shifted by exactly one step; participation of all three activities remained at 100% in both windows, which rules out activities being added, removed or made optional as alternative readings. [REL001, REL002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Limitations: the evidence covers only A01, A06, A07 and A08, so any accompanying changes elsewhere in the trace would not be visible. The competing explanation that a previously rare variant replaced the dominant behaviour cannot be fully excluded, although the perfectly uniform windows (100% shares, zero position spread, 200 traces each) make sampling noise implausible; no screening baseline was provided to compare the item count against a chance expectation.
