### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence directly supports a structural change at the end of the process involving A01 and A12: the fixed closing sequence in which A12 handed over to A01 (about 52 in 100 traces, with A01 always the last event) is gone in the later window — A01 is not observed in any of 200 traces, A12 now ends every trace in which it occurs, and its former transition to A01 (103 observations before) is no longer observed. This is shown directly by relational evidence (the pair changed from always executed together in the order A12→A01 to A12 executing without A01), positional evidence (A12's position moved from about 0.82 to exactly the final position with zero spread), and the vanished successor relation; each of these differences exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, DIR006, REL001]

Limitation: zero participation only establishes that A01 was not observed, so the evidence cannot distinguish a removal of A01 from it having become extremely rare; the five items also partly describe the same underlying traces and are therefore not independent confirmations. [OCC001, REL001]
