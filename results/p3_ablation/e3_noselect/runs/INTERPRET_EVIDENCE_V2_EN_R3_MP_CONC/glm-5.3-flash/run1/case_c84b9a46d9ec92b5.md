### Observed differences

- The starkest difference concerns A19, A20 and A25. In the earlier window each ran in about a third of the cases (A19 in about 37 of 100 traces, A20 about 31, A25 about 35) and almost never together (pairwise co-occurrence about 1 in 100), so nearly every case executed exactly one of the three; in the later window A19 appears in 1 of 200 traces and A20 and A25 in none. Earlier, about 87 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions went to one of the three, each of which led almost exclusively on to A26 (about 95–97 in 100 of their outgoing transitions); in the later window about 86 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions lead directly to A26, and the typical A41-to-A26 distance changes from two steps (about 85 in 100 pairs) to one step (about 86 in 100). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the three alternative activities A19, A20 and A25 no longer execute between A41 and A26: in the earlier window nearly every case ran exactly one of the three after A41 and before A26, whereas in the later window A26 follows A41 directly. The change is shown directly and consistently — the three activities collapse from about 31–37 to at most 0.5 of 100 traces, A41's outgoing transitions redirect from the three (about 87 in 100) to A26 (about 86 in 100), the A41-to-A26 distance shrinks from two steps to one, A26, A31 and A41 now occur without any of the three, and the jump activities that earlier entered the block now lead to A26. Differences of this size arise in almost none of the random reference comparisons, and no activity shows a participation gain of the size that replacing or renaming the block would require. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, DIR041, LAG003, REL004, REL009, REL016, DIR002, DIR004, DIR006, DIR008, DIR010]

The main limitation is that the log cannot distinguish whether the three activities were removed outright or remain possible but were skipped in all 200 later cases, and a subtle replacement by renamed activities cannot be fully excluded even though no candidate is visible; the evidence also cannot say why the block disappeared. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024]

Other differences: The shares of two entry clusters appear to have evened out — A01/A05/A07/A09 together in about 59 of 100 traces earlier versus about 50 later, and A03/A11/A12/A13 about 42 versus about 51 — but the clusters are not directly observed as mutually exclusive alternatives, and differences of this size still arise in roughly one in ten random re-splits, so sampling variation is not ruled out. A15 and A16 repeat more often in the later window, and smaller participation shifts and internal redirections appear around A17, A06, A10, A02, A40 and the A21–A24 group.
