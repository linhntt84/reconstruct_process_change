### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests that in the later window flow is redistributed slightly between existing alternative continuations: after A02, the onward path via A09 is chosen more often than the one via A10 (about 46% of A02's outgoing transitions before, about 51% after), and after A01 the path via A06 is chosen slightly more often than the one via A05 (about 46% before, about 49% after); in step with these shifts, A09 and A02 occur somewhat more often and in somewhat more traces, while A05 and A08 occur somewhat less often and repeat less. Supporting signs: the routing changes are observed directly in the outgoing-transition shares, the occurrence changes move in the same direction, every involved activity is well observed in both windows (A01 and A02 in all traces; A09, A05 and A08 in roughly half to three quarters), and the gains and losses in occurrences per trace are of similar overall size, which fits a change in how often existing paths are taken rather than paths appearing or disappearing. [DIR002, OCC002, OCC008, DIR001, OCC001, OCC004, OCC007]

What is missing: the evidence provides no screening baseline, so the number of qualifying items cannot be compared with what an unchanged process would produce; the shifts are only a few percentage points and the strongest items reach merely about the 86th percentile of random re-splits (about 14 in 100 unchanged re-splits would differ at least as much); near-identical items (A05 with A08, A06 with A07 and A12, and the A02/A09 cluster) likely describe one underlying behaviour each rather than independent confirmations; and A10's own participation is not reported, so its continued use rests on the direction shares alone. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is ordinary sampling fluctuation between two 200-trace windows around near-even branch shares. [DIR002, OCC002, OCC008]

Other differences: A03 appears in more traces (about 56 versus 49 in 100) and A06, A07 and A12 recur somewhat more often within traces; both differences are small and unremarkable against random re-splits (strength about 0.34). The outgoing mixes of A04, A07 and A11 show no meaningful change. [OCC003, OCC005, OCC006, OCC009, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
