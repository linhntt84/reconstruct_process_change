### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window executes a restructured variant of the process. A step A01 that was not observed earlier now runs in about 56 in 100 traces around the middle of the case: it is reached from A08, A09, A10, A13 and A17 (from A09 and A13 exclusively) and leads on to A09, A02, A11 and A05 in roughly equal shares. At the same time the closing sequence changed: A14, previously executed exactly once in every trace alongside A03, is no longer observed, and A03 now always ends the case, immediately after A06. [OCC001, OCC010, POS001, POS003, POS012, DIR001, DIR006, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR012, DIR016, REL002, REL033, LAG024]

The support is direct rather than cumulative: participation moves from zero to about 56 in 100 traces for A01 and from all 200 traces to none for A14; the successor profiles of A08, A09, A10, A13, A17, A06, A05 and A11 change in the directions this requires; A03's relative position is fixed at the last position in every later trace; and the relation items show the same cases switching from "A14 always present alongside A03" to "A03 alone". These differences exceed about 99.5 in 100 of the random re-splits used as reference, so sampling fluctuation alone is an unlikely explanation. Limitations: no screening expectation was supplied, so the number of items expected by chance could not be checked; the many relation and lag items restate the same underlying traces and are not independent confirmations; and "not observed" in one 200-trace window is not proof of absence. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 is a re-labelled existing activity rather than a genuinely new step, since the activity codes are anonymous. [OCC001, OCC010, POS003, DIR003, DIR006, REL033]

Other differences: A05 and A12 run in clearly fewer traces (about 62% to 29% and 37% to 20%), A05 now continues to A13 where it previously continued to A16, A11 and A16 run in about 70% of traces with A11 sitting later and A16 earlier, A11 now continues to A16 instead of A05, A15 leads only to A11, A09 occurs more often per trace (about 0.44 to 0.66 executions), and the A02-to-A03 distance tightened to almost always three steps. Whether these shifts belong to the same reconfiguration or are separate adjustments cannot be determined from this evidence.
