### Observed differences

- **A03 disappeared.** In the earlier window every trace contained A03 exactly once (200 in 200), usually as the first step and usually followed by A02 (about 86 in 100 of A03's outgoing transitions went to A02); in the later window A03 appears in none of the 200 traces. Its near-universal co-occurrence with A01, A02 and A04 (e.g. A03 before A04 in about 99 in 100 before-traces) is replaced by those activities occurring without A03 in all after-traces. [OCC001, DIR003, POS003, REL001, REL003, REL005, REL006]
- **A07 appeared between A04 and A01.** A07 is absent from all before-traces and occurs exactly once in every after-trace (200 in 200); in the later window A04 always hands over to A07 and A07 always to A01, whereas before, A01, A02 and A04 never co-occurred with A07. [OCC004, DIR004, DIR007, POS005, REL002, REL004, REL007]
- **A04's successor changed.** Before, about 99 in 100 of A04's outgoing transitions went directly to A01 (the rest to A03); in the later window all go to A07, so A01 is now reached two steps after A04 instead of immediately. [DIR004, LAG003]
- **A02 now opens the trace.** Its median position moved from the second event to the first; the edge A02 → A03 (about 10 in 100 of A02's outgoing transitions before) is no longer observed, and its remaining outgoing split moved from roughly 42 A05 / 48 A06 to roughly 53 A05 / 47 A06 per 100. [POS002, DIR002, REL003]
- **The middle block kept its shape but shifted.** A02 and A04 stayed two steps apart in both windows, while the A02→A01 distance grew from 3 steps (about 87 in 100 before-traces) to 4 steps (all after-traces) and A04's median position moved from about three-quarters to one-half of the trace. [LAG001, LAG002, POS004]
- **A small shift in the A05/A06 alternative.** A05 participation rose from about 47 to about 53 in 100 traces and A06 fell from about 54 to about 48; exactly one of the two still occurs per trace, and the difference is only moderately unusual for random re-splits. [OCC002, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A03 was removed from the process, A07 was inserted between A04 and A01, and A02 became the first step: earlier traces typically ran A03 → A02 → (A05 or A06) → A04 → A01, with A03 following A02 in a minority of about 14 in 100 traces, while in the later window they run A02 → (A05 or A06) → A04 → A07 → A01. The change is shown directly and consistently across indicator families — all-or-nothing participation flips (200 vs 0 traces in each direction, each difference larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits), a complete successor change at A04, relation profiles flipping between co-occurrence and single-activity occurrence, and position and lag shifts that match this restructuring quantitatively. The agreeing items largely express one underlying trace behaviour and should not be read as independent confirmations. The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample a shifted mix of process variants (an A03-bearing variant giving way to an A07-bearing one) rather than a modification of a single flow, and it says nothing about the cause. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR003, DIR004, DIR007, REL003, REL005, REL007, POS002, POS005, LAG001, LAG003]

Other differences: A05 and A06 now lead only to A04, where before a few in 100 of their outgoing transitions went to A03 — a side effect of A03's disappearance; the roughly 6-in-100-trace shift between A05 and A06 is small and may be sampling variation. [DIR005, DIR006, OCC002, OCC003]
