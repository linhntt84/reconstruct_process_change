### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The largest signals — the roughly four-percentage-point routing shifts at A08, A10 and A06 and the small drop in A02's mean occurrences per trace — are matched or exceeded by a sizable share of random re-splits of the pooled data (strengths of at most 0.86, most items below 0.5), so sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows remains a full explanation, and no screening expectation is provided to size the item count against chance.
