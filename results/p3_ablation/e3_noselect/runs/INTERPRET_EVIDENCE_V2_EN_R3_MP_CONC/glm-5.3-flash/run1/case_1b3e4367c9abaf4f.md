### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new closing step, A01, was added between A15 and A16. In the earlier window A01 never appeared and A15 and A16 always followed one another directly; in the later window A01 runs exactly once per case, is reached from A15 and from A16 (about half of each activity's outgoing transitions) and in a minority of paths from A05, and leads back to A16 or A15, so the former direct A15/A16 succession now alternates roughly equally with a detour through A01. The support is direct and consistent across indicators: participation moved from none of 200 traces to all 200 at a stable late position, the successor profiles of A15 and A16 flipped from wholly each other to roughly half A01, A01 co-occurs with both in every later trace in both orders, and the A15/A16 step distance stretched from one to sometimes two. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR005, DIR011, DIR012, REL008, REL009, LAG035, LAG036]

Limitations: because A01 precedes and follows each of A15 and A16 in similar proportions, the evidence establishes no fixed order between them, so "A01 sits between the two" and "an unordered closing block that always includes A01" fit the data equally well and cannot be distinguished; zero participation in the earlier window also shows only that A01 was not exercised there, not that it was absent from the process. [REL008, REL009, OCC001]

Other differences: the start of the process was restructured. Earlier, A02 (about 48 in 100 traces) led directly to A03 or A07, while A06, A12 (about 52 in 100 each) and A11 (about 26 in 100) formed a partly separate early branch; in the later window A02, A06 and A12 run in every trace, A02 routes to A06 in about 90 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, A06 repeats about twice per trace, and A11's participation doubled, so A03 and A07 now begin three to five steps after A02 rather than one to two, and A02 and A06, previously mutually exclusive, always co-occur.
