### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A30, A31 and A36 each occurred about once per trace (means about 1.0) and essentially never repeated within a trace; in the later window each occurs about 3.5 times per trace and repeats within the same trace in about 68–69 in 100 traces, while still appearing in every trace [OCC028, OCC029, OCC034].
- The order among these activities changed from fixed to mixed: earlier, A14 came before A30 in about 99 in 100 traces and A30 before A36 in about 98–99 in 100; later, these pairs are interleaved or repeated in about 68–69 in 100 traces, and "A05 before A30/A36" fell from about 54–55 in 100 to about 17 in 100 [REL004, REL005, REL006, REL002, REL003].
- Direct succession moved accordingly: of A36's outgoing transitions, the share leading to A25 fell from about 98 in 100 to about 28 in 100, while the share leading back to A30 rose to about 71 in 100; A30→A31 and A31→A36 remain at about 98–99 in 100, and the observed outgoing transitions of A30 and A31 roughly tripled (about 200 to about 700) [DIR036, DIR030, DIR031].
- The lags corroborate this: A30 followed by itself within five steps was observed once earlier versus 136 times later, and the tight two-step gap from A30 to A36 (96.5 in 100 earlier) dropped to about 12 in 100; the position spread of A30 and A36 roughly doubled (standard deviations about 0.06 to 0.12), while earlier activities sit slightly earlier (A05 mean position 0.074 to 0.052), consistent with longer traces [LAG002, LAG003, POS003, POS004, POS001].
- Smaller shifts elsewhere: A15 and A16 repeat somewhat more often (recurrence about 37 to 44 in 100 traces); of A05's outgoing transitions the share to A14 fell from about 35 to 18 in 100 while the share to A07 rose from about 19 to 39 in 100, with similar reshuffles after A01 and A07; A21 lost about 6–7 in 100 traces of participation to A22; A41 was executed in about 65 in 100 traces later versus about 55 earlier, while A42, A43 and A38 declined slightly [OCC015, OCC016, DIR005, DIR001, DIR007, OCC021, OCC022, OCC039, OCC040, OCC041, OCC036].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A30, A31 and A36 are executed repeatedly within traces instead of once each in a fixed sequence: earlier, traces ran A14, then A30, A31 and A36 one time each in order, whereas later these three activities run about 3.5 times per trace each, and A36 typically leads back to A30 (about 71 in 100 of its transitions) rather than forward to A25, so the block A30→A31→A36 can cycle before the flow continues via A25 [OCC028, OCC029, OCC034, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR036]. The occurrence, relation, direction, lag and position indicators all point the same way — tripled transition counts out of A30 and A31, the appearance of A30 self-repetition, and the doubled position spread of A30 and A36 — and sampling variation is an unlikely explanation because all three activities appear in every trace in both windows and the key differences exceed 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data [DIR030, DIR031, LAG002, POS003, POS004, OCC028, OCC029, OCC034]. Limitations: the indicators aggregate over traces, so the evidence cannot show how many repetition rounds a trace contains, whether the three activities always repeat together, or whether only part of the later traces contain the cycle — a mixed later window would look the same; the evidence file also contains no screening expectation, so the judgement rests on the size, directness and consistency of the differences rather than a chance baseline [OCC028, OCC029, OCC034, REL004, REL006].

Other differences: A15 and A16 repeat slightly more often (recurrence about 37 to 44 in 100 traces) [OCC015, OCC016]; routing shares between alternative successors shifted at A01, A05 and A07, for example after A05 the branch to A14 fell from about 35 to 18 in 100 while the branch to A07 rose from about 19 to 39 in 100 [DIR005, DIR001, DIR007]; A21 lost about 6–7 in 100 traces of participation to A22 [OCC021, OCC022]; A41 gained participation (about 55 to 65 in 100 traces) while A42, A43 and A38 declined slightly [OCC039, OCC040, OCC041, OCC036].
