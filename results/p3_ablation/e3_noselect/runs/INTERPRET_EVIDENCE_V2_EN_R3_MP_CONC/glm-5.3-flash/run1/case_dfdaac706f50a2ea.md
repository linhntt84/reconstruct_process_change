### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the always-joint trio A36/A37/A42 no longer runs between A25 and A28. In the earlier window essentially every case went A25 → trio → A28 → … → A26 → A27 → A31; in the later window A25 leads directly to A28 in almost every case, and the trio instead runs after A26, where it alternates with A27 — about 52–53 of A26's outgoing transitions in 100 go to A27 and about 46 go into the trio, with both routes continuing to A31 (A27 directly, the trio after looping). Consequently A27 is skipped in the roughly 46–47 of 100 later cases that run the trio, and the trio is skipped in the roughly 52–53 that run A27. [DIR025, DIR026, DIR036, DIR037, DIR042, LAG001, LAG006, REL011, REL016, REL019, REL022, OCC025, OCC032, OCC033, OCC037]

The hypothesis is supported directly and consistently: the rewiring is visible in the successor profiles of A25, A26 and the trio's members; the trio's order relative to A26 and A28 flipped while it still precedes A31; A27 and the trio moved from always co-occurring to near-perfect mutual exclusivity; and the lag changes (A25→A28 from about 4 steps to 1; A26→A31 split between 2 and 4 steps) match the two routes. Occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators all point to the same structure, with empirical strengths of about 0.995 on the key items, making sampling variation an unlikely explanation. [DIR025, DIR026, DIR036, DIR037, DIR042, LAG001, LAG006, REL011, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL022, POS006, POS007, POS008]

Other differences: the front-end shift described above — A01, A05, A07, A09 and A21 down about 6–9 in 100 cases, A03, A11, A12 and A13 up about 7–9 — looks like a change in how often two existing entry loops are taken, since all involved activities remain common in both windows; with no chance expectation available for comparison, a sampling difference cannot be fully excluded. Smaller shifts of a few cases in 100 concern A48/A49, A10 and A21/A22. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC005, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011, OCC012, OCC020, OCC021, OCC042, OCC043, DIR003, DIR011, DIR013]

Limitations: the samples expose only the dominant transitions, so rare entries into or exits from the trio cannot be excluded, and the evidence cannot distinguish a shift of the trio from dropping it after A25 and inserting a new branch after A26, nor rule out that the two post-A26 routes reflect a stable mix of case types rather than a per-case choice. Items resting on a single co-occurrence in one window (several lag pairs) and A51's completely changed but thinly supported successor profile (12 versus 3 observed transitions) were not used as support. [LAG002, LAG011, DIR051, DIR025, DIR026]
