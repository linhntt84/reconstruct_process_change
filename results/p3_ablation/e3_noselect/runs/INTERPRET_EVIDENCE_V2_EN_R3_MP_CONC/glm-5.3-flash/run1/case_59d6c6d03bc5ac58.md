### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A01 appeared in about 50 of 100 traces (101 of 200) and always led directly into A02; in the later window, A01 is not observed in any of the 200 traces. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001]
- A05 was not observed earlier; in the later window it appears in 92 of 200 traces (about 46 in 100), always preceded by A04 and always followed by A02, and it never occurs without A04. [OCC004, DIR003, REL005, REL006]

- Each of these differences is larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces, and the branches involved contain 92–101 traces per window, so they are not rare-variant noise. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS002, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the run-up to A02 was restructured, involving A01, A04, A05 and A02. Earlier, traces ran either A01 → A02 (about half of cases) or A04 → A02 (the other half), with A02 last in every trace. In the later window, A01 is no longer observed, A05 now executes between A04 and A02 (so the A04 branch reads A04 → A05 → A02), and traces without A04 begin with A02. The change is shown directly: A04's only observed outgoing edge moved from A02 to A05 while A05's only outgoing edge goes to A02; the A04→A02 distance grew from one step to two; A05 co-occurs with A04 in every one of its appearances; and A01, previously a strict alternative to A04, disappeared exactly as A05 appeared. All supporting items rest on 92–101 traces per window. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, REL002, REL005, REL006, POS002]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 was not removed but replaced by an activity outside the tested set: in the roughly 108 of 200 later traces that now start with A02, no predecessor is observed, so what (if anything) now stands where A01 used to cannot be determined. [OCC001, POS002]

Other differences: A03, A04 and A06 each slipped from about 50% to 46% participation (99 to 92 of 200 traces), with the A02–A04 relation shifting by the same small amount; these differences are well within what random re-splits typically produce (about 46 of 100 random splits look this large) and are consistent with sampling variation rather than change. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC005, REL004]
