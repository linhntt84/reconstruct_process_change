### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A01, was inserted into the process: in the later window it runs exactly once in every case, early in the trace, is reached from A02, A12 and A06, and leads mainly back to A02 and A12 and onward to A04 and A05, both of which it always precedes. This is shown directly and consistently across occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators, each backed by all 200 later traces, and the appearance itself (0 of 200 traces before versus 200 of 200 after) cannot be explained as a rare variant surfacing by chance. The evidence cannot pin down a single insertion point — A01 alternates order with A02 and A12, so it may occupy different positions in different cases — nor can it distinguish a genuinely new activity from one that already existed but was never executed in the earlier window. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR006, DIR012, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]

Other differences: the share of traces taking A06 rose (about 48 to 59 in 100) while A03, A08 and A10 — and to a lesser degree A07, A09 and A11 — are executed less often (about 52 to 41 in 100 for the first three), a pattern consistent with the alternative branch being taken less often; the evidence cannot show whether this follows from A01's insertion or is an independent shift in how often existing paths are taken, and shifts of this size can also arise between finite samples. A10's outgoing mix also appears to change completely, but only 6 versus 2 outgoing transitions were observed, too few to interpret. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR010]
