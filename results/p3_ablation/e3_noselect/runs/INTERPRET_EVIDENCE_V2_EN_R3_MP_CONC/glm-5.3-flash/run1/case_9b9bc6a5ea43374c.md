### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A03 was added to the process and now executes inside the block formed by A05, A08, A12 and A11, in the region between the A08/A12 pair and A11. In the earlier window A03 was never observed; in the later window it appears in about half of the cases, exclusively together with that block, always before A11, after or interleaved with A05, and in varying order relative to A08 and A12. The placement is shown directly by the new relations (A03 leading to A11, A12 and A08; A08, A12 and A05 leading to A03) and by the increased step distances A08→A11 and A12→A11; occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators all describe the same picture consistently, and an activity appearing in 0 of 200 versus 103 of 200 traces is not what rare-variant sampling produces. Limitations: the placement is not a single fixed slot — A03 mixes in order with both A08 and A12 — and the evidence cannot establish which activity precedes A03 in every case; the agreeing items reflect one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the A08/A12 pair lost its strict adjacency independently of A03's appearance rather than as a consequence of it. [OCC003, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR001, LAG005, LAG008]
