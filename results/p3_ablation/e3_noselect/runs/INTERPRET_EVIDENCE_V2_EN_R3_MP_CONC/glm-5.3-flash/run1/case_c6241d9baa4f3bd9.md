### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A specific possibility supported by the evidence is that in the later window A07 is no longer executed, and the flow that previously ran A03 → A07 → A06 now goes directly from A03 to A06, bypassing A07. Before, A07 ran in about 53 in 100 traces and almost always handed over to A06; in the later window it appears in 0 of 200 traces, A03's outgoing share to A07 (about 39%) disappeared while its direct share to A06 rose from about 5% to 51%, A03–A07 and A06–A07 co-occurrence dropped to zero while A03 and A06 still co-occur in about half the traces, and the A03→A06 distance shortened by exactly one step. A drop from 106 to 0 participating traces is far outside what sampling variation between two 200-trace windows produces. Limitations: these items all reflect the same underlying difference, so they corroborate rather than independently confirm it; the evidence cannot show whether A07 was removed from the process or still exists but is simply never triggered in the later sample; and the A10→A07 link rests on only two observations. [OCC005, DIR002, DIR004, REL002, REL003, LAG002, DIR006]

A06's outgoing mix also shifted slightly, with A10's share rising from about 43% to 51% after the edge to A07 disappeared. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC006, DIR003]
