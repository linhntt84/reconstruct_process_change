- The three branch activities A32, A33 and A38 all but disappeared: in the earlier window each ran in about 48–49 in 100 traces and the three of them ran together as a group, never alongside A27; in the later window each appears in only about 1 in 200 traces, while A27 runs in about 995 in 1000 traces (up from about 515 in 1000). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the optional three-activity branch through A32, A33 and A38 between A26 and A28 is no longer executed, leaving A27 as the standard continuation. In the earlier window every trace ran either A27 alone (about 52 in 100) or the trio A32, A33 and A38 together (about 48–49 in 100, in varying order); in the later window A26 continues to A27 in about 98 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, A27 participates in about 995 in 1000 traces, and each of A32, A33 and A38 appears in only about 1 in 200 traces. The change is shown directly and consistently by occurrence, direction, relation and distance indicators, and differences of this size (about 49 in 100 vs about 1 in 200 participation) are essentially never produced by random re-splits of two 200-trace windows, with empirical strength about 0.995 on the key items. [OCC025, OCC030, OCC031, OCC036, DIR026, LAG013, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL033, REL034, REL035]

Limitations: the later-window successors of A32, A33 and A38 rest on a single trace each, so the wiring of that branch in the later window cannot be described; and the evidence cannot distinguish a branch that was fully removed from one that has become very rare and was sampled only once in 200 traces. [DIR032, DIR033, DIR038, OCC030, OCC031, OCC036]

Other differences: A04, which previously sat between A14 and A15 in most traces, is now usually not executed (participation about 21–22 vs 68 in 100 traces), with A14 flowing directly to A15; the rare activities A02, A06, A08 and A10 participate roughly two to four times as often and reach different successors; and small shifts affect the event positions of A14 and A28 and the within-trace repetition of A25, A28, A30, A39 and A40.
