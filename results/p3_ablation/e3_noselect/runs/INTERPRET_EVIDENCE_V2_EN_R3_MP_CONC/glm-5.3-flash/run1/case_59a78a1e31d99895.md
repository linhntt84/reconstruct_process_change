### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every one of the 200 traces carried one of two exclusive endings directly after A02: A04 alone (about 50 in 100 traces) or A06 together with A10 (about 50 in 100), with A06 and A10 adjacent in both orders across traces; A04 never co-occurred with A06 or A10. These ending activities sat at the trace end (median relative position about 0.97–1.0), while A02 occurred in every trace around mid-position (median 0.50, about 1.9 occurrences per trace). [OCC001, OCC003, OCC005, OCC009, POS001, POS003, POS004, POS007, REL012, REL015, REL018, DIR002]

- In the later window, A04, A06 and A10 each appear in only about 20–22 in 100 traces (down from about 50); no transitions from A02 to any of them appear among the observed outgoing transitions (previously about 25, 13 and 13 per 100 of A02's outgoing transitions). They now sit mid-trace (median relative position about 0.6–0.67 instead of ≈ 1.0) and are reached mainly from A03 and A09 (A03→A04 about 23, A03→A06 about 14, A09→A04 about 21, A09→A06 and A09→A10 about 14 per 100 of those activities' outgoing transitions). [OCC003, OCC005, OCC009, DIR002, DIR003, DIR008, POS003, POS004, POS007]

- The exclusive ending dissolved: in about 67 in 100 later traces neither A04 nor A06 appears (never observed before), A06 and A10 co-occur in about 21 in 100 traces (down from about 50) and still run back-to-back in most of those cases (about 87 in 100 of 28 observed pairs, after 100 in 100 of 51), and A04 now co-occurs with A06 in about 8 in 100 traces (previously 0). [REL012, REL015, REL018, LAG015]

- A02 itself now occurs later within traces (median relative position 0.50 → 0.73; the upper quartile is 1.0, so a quarter or more of its occurrences are the trace's final event) and, although still present in every trace at similar frequency (about 1.9 → 2.0 occurrences per trace), its observed outgoing transitions fell from about 380 to about 220, consistent with many traces now ending at A02; its handovers concentrate on A05 (about 46 per 100, up from 23) and on A03 and A09 (about 21 each), while A05 still leads back to A02 in nearly all of its outgoing transitions. [POS001, OCC001, DIR002, DIR005]

- Distance evidence agrees: A02 followed by A04 within one step (about 61 in 100 of observed pairs before) is not observed later, and distance-1 A02→A06 and A02→A10 (about 26–28 in 100) also vanish; new distance-1 links appear from A03/A09 into A04, A06 and A10 (about 23–38 in 100 of the smaller later samples), and several pairs that used to sit 1–2 steps from A02 or A08 are now spread over 3–5 steps. [LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG014, LAG023, LAG025, LAG026, LAG027, LAG029]

- Activities around the A02–A05/A03/A09 cycle became somewhat more frequent: A05 participation rose from about 29 to 36 in 100 traces, A07 from about 0.38 to 0.56 mean occurrences per trace, and A09 from about 0.41 to 0.54, while A03 (about 23–25 in 100) and A08 (about 32–34 in 100) stayed at similar participation. [OCC002, OCC004, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the final stage of the process was restructured around A02, A04, A06 and A10. In the earlier window, an exclusive ending — A04 alone, or A06 with A10 adjacent — executed immediately after A02 at the trace end in essentially every trace; in the later window this ending block is largely detached: the three activities run in only about a fifth of traces, sit mid-trace, are entered from A03 or A09 rather than from A02, and often continue to A08, while A02 now occurs late and frequently terminates the trace, handing over mainly to A05 (which feeds back into A02) and to A03/A09. The change is shown directly by relational evidence — A02's observed transitions to A04/A06/A10 disappear, A03 and A09 gain transitions to them, and the A04-versus-{A06, A10} exclusivity dissolves — and it is consistent across occurrence, position, direction, relation and distance indicators, with the key items showing differences larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. [OCC003, OCC005, OCC009, POS001, POS003, POS004, POS007, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, DIR008, DIR009, REL012, REL015, REL018, LAG003, LAG015]

Limitations: positions and distances are event orders, not clock times, so "later" and "mid-trace" describe sequence placement only. The before-profile of A04's outgoing transitions rests on a single observed transition, and several later distance profiles rest on fewer than 10 pairs (for example A08→A04 on 8 and A08→A06 on 6), so the precise new wiring around A04 and A08 is less certain than the overall detachment of the ending. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the two windows sample different mixes of coexisting trace variants rather than one modified behaviour; the disappearance of A02's direct transitions to A04/A06/A10 argues against a pure mix shift, but variant-level data would be needed to exclude it. [DIR004, LAG018, LAG019, LAG022]

Other differences: A07 occurs more often per trace (about 0.38 → 0.56) while its successor split between A03 and A09 stays near 50/50 in both windows; A08 occurs later in traces (median relative position about 0.63 → 0.83) at stable participation; A02's transitions back to A01 rise from about 3 to 12 per 100 of its outgoing transitions; and the typical distance between A02's own repeats shifts towards 2 steps. [OCC006, OCC007, DIR007, POS005, DIR002, LAG001]
