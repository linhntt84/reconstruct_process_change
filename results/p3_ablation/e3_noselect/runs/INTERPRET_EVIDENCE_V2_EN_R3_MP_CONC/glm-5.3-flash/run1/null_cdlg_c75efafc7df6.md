### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The strongest signals — the routing shifts between A02, A10 and A03 — are only moderately unusual for random re-splits (empirical strengths about 0.66 to 0.75), the vanished rare transitions rest on one to three observations each, and the evidence carries no screening expectations, so sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows cannot be ruled out as the source of all observed differences.
