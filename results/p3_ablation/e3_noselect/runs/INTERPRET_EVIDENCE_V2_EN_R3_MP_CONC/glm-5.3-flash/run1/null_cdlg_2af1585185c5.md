### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, each of the activities A05, A07, A09 and A10 occurred about 0.42 times per trace and appeared in about 23 in 100 traces; in the later window each occurred about 0.52 times per trace while participation stayed at about 23 in 100 — among the traces containing them, they ran about 1.8 times per containing trace before versus about 2.2 times after. [OCC005, OCC007, OCC009, OCC010]

- These four activities' observed transitions run mainly among themselves and reach A04 via A09, and the shares between the transitions moved in one consistent direction. After A05, continuations to A10 rose from about 43% to about 54% of outgoing transitions while continuations to A07 fell from about 49% to about 41%; after A07, continuations to A09 rose from about 45% to about 53% while those to A10 fell from about 48% to about 40%; after A10, continuations to A07 rose from about 41% to about 50% while those to A09 fell from about 53% to about 43%; after A09, exits to A04 rose from about 95% to about 99% while returns to A05 fell from about 5% to about 1%. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

- A04 occurred about 1.6 times per trace in the earlier window versus about 2.1 times in the later window, with repeats in about 34 versus about 38 in 100 traces; A11 rose from about 0.8 to about 1.0 occurrences per trace and A12 from about 0.84 to about 1.0. [OCC004, OCC011, OCC012]

- Participation moved in opposite directions at the edges of the process: A01 appeared in about 58 in 100 traces earlier and about 51 in 100 later, while A02, A03, A04 and A12 each rose from about 43 to about 50 in 100 and A06 from about 23 to about 28 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC006, OCC012]

- The remaining successor profiles were essentially stable: A02 still led almost exclusively to A04, A04 still split about half to A11 and half to A12, and A12's successor mix hardly moved; the only qualitative difference is a rare A11 → A02 transition (about 2% of A11's outgoing transitions) observed only in the earlier window. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR007, DIR008]

### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests one main hypothesis: in the later window the cyclic section around A04 is traversed more often and with a different preferred order — A05, A07, A09 and A10 repeat more often per containing trace (about 1.8 versus about 2.2), A04, A11 and A12 also occur more often per trace, and within the A05/A07/A09/A10 group the flow shifted towards the traversal A05 → A10 → A07 → A09, which exits to A04, and away from A05 → A07 → A10 → A09. Supporting signs: all four successor profiles shift in the same direction around the group, each difference being larger than what about 70–90 in 100 random re-splits produce; the occurrence increases for the same activities and for A04, A11 and A12 point the same way; and every transition involved is observed in both windows, so this is a shift between existing alternatives rather than a replacement of paths. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, OCC004, OCC005, OCC007, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011, OCC012]

What is missing: the evidence reports no screening baseline, so the number of qualifying items cannot be compared with the number expected for an unchanged process; the occurrence increases for the loop activities are weak (larger than only about 59 in 100 random re-splits), and the routing shifts rest on about 83–103 outgoing transitions per window, drawn from roughly 45–46 traces per activity. An important competing explanation that remains open is sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows; DIR007 illustrates the risk, since a variant seen in one window only can reach an extreme strength (about 99 in 100) while being negligible in size. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, OCC005, DIR007]

Other differences: the participation shifts around the start of the process — A01 down from about 58 to about 51 in 100 traces while A02 and A03 rise from about 43 to about 50 in 100 and A06 from about 23 to about 28 in 100 — are not part of the loop hypothesis, and without relational evidence the data cannot show whether A01 now connects to these activities more often; A08's participation is essentially unchanged. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC006, OCC008]
