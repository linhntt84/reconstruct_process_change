### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the per-case choice among the three alternative activities A19, A20 and A25 was relocated from the late A27 → A28 → A32 section to immediately before A26. Before, A45 led straight into A26 and the chosen alternative was inserted inside the A27 → A28 → A32 chain; in the later window A45 leads into the chosen alternative — directly, or via A08, which became far more frequent and now typically precedes the alternative — the alternative then leads into A26, and A27 → A28 → A32 runs without the insertion. This is shown directly by successor changes on both sides of the move, by order relations that flipped consistently across all 200 traces in both windows, by position shifts of about a quarter of the trace for A19, A20 and A25, and by lag changes that add one step between A45 and A26 while removing one inside the A27 → A28 → A32 chain. [DIR045, DIR008, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, DIR027, DIR028, REL030, REL036, REL041, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG027, LAG018, LAG019]

The evidence carries no screening baseline, so the overall volume of flagged items cannot be compared with the count expected for an unchanged process, and some corroborating lag items rest on very few observations (the earlier A19→A26 pattern is backed by a single case). A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that A08's higher participation is an independent change that only coincides with the relocation, since its new successors and its doubled frequency are observed only jointly in the later window. [LAG014, OCC008, DIR008]

Other differences: A18 became less frequent (about 32 to 20 in 100 traces), the A44/A46/A47 region shows more repetition and higher participation, and the rare activities A02, A04, A06 and A10 each roughly halved; these are participation changes in their own right and are not part of the relocation hypothesis. [OCC018, OCC041, OCC042, OCC040, OCC002, OCC004, OCC006, OCC010]
