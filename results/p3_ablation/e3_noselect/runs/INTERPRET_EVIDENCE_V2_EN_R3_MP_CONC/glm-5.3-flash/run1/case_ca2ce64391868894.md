### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, cases run a restructured variant of the same flow: the cycle A03 → A06 → A08 → A03 and the closing sequence A05 → A02 → A01 are replaced by a shortened cycle A06 → A08 → (A06 or A07) followed by A07 → A05 → A04, so that A01, A02 and A03 are no longer executed and the new activity A04 always terminates the case. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation flipped from 100% to 0% for A01, A02 and A03 and from 0% to 100% for A04 at exactly A01's former final position; the only two observed successor changes (out of A05 and out of A08) tie A04 into the flow where A02 and A03 used to sit; and every lag inside the cycle shortened by exactly one step, which is what removing A03 from the cycle predicts. Each of these differences is larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled traces. Two limitations remain: zero participation shows only that A01–A03 were not observed in the later sample, not that they were removed from the process definition, and the evidence cannot determine whether the replaced ending and the shortened cycle were one modification or two coordinated ones.
