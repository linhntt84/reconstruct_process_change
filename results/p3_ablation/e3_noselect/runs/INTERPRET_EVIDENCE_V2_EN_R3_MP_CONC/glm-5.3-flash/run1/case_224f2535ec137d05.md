### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window a new closing step was appended to the end of every case: A01, previously unobserved, now runs exactly once as the last event, immediately after A16, which until then had ended every trace. The support is direct and consistent across indicator families: participation (0 of 200 → 200 of 200 traces), position (A01 constant at the final position, A16 moved off the exact end), direction (the new edge A16→A01 in all 200 later traces, where A16 previously had no observed successor) and relation (every trace changed from "A16 only" to "both, A16 before A01"). These items describe one underlying behaviour rather than independent confirmations, and each shows an empirical strength of about 0.995, i.e. a difference larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. Limitations: each window holds only 200 traces, and the evidence cannot show whether A01 is genuinely new or a re-labelled existing step; an important competing explanation it cannot rule out is that the two windows sampled different mixes of process variants (one with, one without this closing step) rather than a single modified variant [OCC001, POS001, DIR009, POS002, REL001].
