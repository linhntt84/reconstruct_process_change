### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the block of activities A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25 and A32 — which co-occur in every trace in both windows — is executed as one fixed sequence, A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32, whereas earlier their order varied from case to case. The change is shown directly and consistently: successor profiles collapsed from three or four near-equal options to one dominant successor per activity, pairwise orders that previously appeared in both directions collapsed to the chain order (for example A23 before A24 in about 12 → 97 in 100 traces), gaps between consecutive chain members became fixed at one step, and the position shifts of A20–A26 match the new chain. These many agreeing items describe one underlying change rather than independent confirmations, and all rest on about 200 traces per window. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of case types between the windows (different proportions of pre-existing orderings) rather than a changed routing rule; the earlier window's several near-equal orderings make a single rare variant unlikely, but aggregate indicators cannot fully exclude mixture effects.
