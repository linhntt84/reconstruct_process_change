### Observed differences

- The clearest difference is a reversal of execution order between the activity group A35/A36/A41 and the activities A26 and A27. In the earlier window, A26 preceded A35, A36 and A41 in about 98–99 in 100 traces and A27 preceded them in about 98–100 in 100; in the later window, A35, A36 and A41 each preceded A26 in about 98 in 100 traces and preceded A27 in about 97–98 in 100. [REL008, REL009, REL010, REL012, REL013, REL014]

- The direct successions around this group were rewired. Before, A25's outgoing transitions led to A27 in about 98 in 100 cases; in the later window they lead to A35/A36/A41 in about 97 in 100 cases (split roughly in thirds), with A27 almost gone. A26's outgoing shifted from the group (about 99 in 100) to A30 alone (about 98 in 100), and each group member's exit shifted from A30 (about 28–34 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) to A27 (about 31–33 in 100). [DIR025, DIR026, DIR035, DIR036, DIR041]

- Event distances changed consistently with this rewiring. A25 to A27 went from immediately consecutive (distance 1 in about 99 in 100 traces) to four to five events apart (about 90 in 100 at distance 4); A26 to A30 went from four events apart (about 93 in 100) to consecutive (about 99 in 100); the near-adjacencies A26→group and group→A30, each seen in about 200 traces before, dropped to 1–2 traces, while group→A27 rose from 1 to about 199 traces. [LAG001, LAG004, LAG002, LAG011, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG012, LAG015, LAG018]

- Relative event positions moved in the same directions: A35, A36 and A41 shifted earlier (mean position about 0.58 to about 0.42), A27 (about 0.38 to 0.47) and A26 (about 0.53 to 0.62) shifted later, while A25 and A30 stayed essentially in place. [POS005, POS006, POS007, POS003, POS002, POS001, POS004]

- Participation was unchanged for every activity involved: A25, A27, A35 and A36 occur in 100 in 100 traces in both windows, A26 and A41 co-occur with A25, A27 and A30 in 100 in 100 traces in both windows, and the chain A27 → A28 → A29 → A33 → A34 → A26 is intact in both windows. [OCC024, OCC025, OCC031, OCC032, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL011, REL014, REL015, DIR027, DIR028, DIR029, DIR033, DIR034]

- Away from this reordering, smaller shifts appear: A08's participation fell from about 21 to about 14 in 100 traces; A15 and A16 occurred about 0.12–0.13 more times per trace; several optional activities moved by roughly 4–8 percentage points; and a few low-volume activities (for example A10 and A50) show redistributed successor shares on only about 4–50 outgoing transitions per window. [OCC008, OCC015, OCC016, OCC001, OCC005, OCC007, OCC009, OCC020, OCC021, OCC022, OCC023, OCC003, OCC011, OCC012, OCC013, OCC018, DIR010, DIR050]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two segments of the process exchanged their relative order. In the earlier window the dominant flow ran A25 → A27 → A28 → A29 → A33 → A34 → A26, then through the group A35, A36, A41 (executed in no fixed order among themselves) and on to A30; in the later window, the group A35/A36/A41 runs directly after A25 and before A27, the segment A27 → A28 → A29 → A33 → A34 → A26 follows it, and A26 leads straight to A30. Every activity involved still appears in all traces in both windows, so this is a change in ordering constraints, not activities being added, removed or executed more or less often. [REL008, REL009, REL010, REL012, REL013, REL014, DIR025, DIR026, DIR035, DIR036, DIR041, LAG001, LAG004, POS002, POS003, POS005, POS006, POS007, OCC024, OCC025, OCC031, OCC032]

The change is shown directly by the order relations — all six cross-pairs between {A26, A27} and {A35, A36, A41} flip from one fixed order to the other, while the group's relations to A25 and A30 and its internal lack of a fixed order stay unchanged — and it is corroborated by the successor, distance and position evidence, giving about 25 agreeing items across four indicator families, most with empirical strength at or near the maximum. What the evidence cannot establish is which segment "moved": only the exchange of relative order is observed, and, because the comparison aggregates 200 traces per window, a shift in the mix of coexisting trace variants rather than one restructured flow remains a competing explanation it cannot rule out. [REL004, REL005, REL007, REL011, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, LAG013, LAG014, LAG016, LAG017, LAG019, LAG020]

Other differences: A08 was executed in about 21 in 100 traces before and about 14 in 100 after; A15 and A16 repeated slightly more often (mean occurrences per trace about 1.6 to about 1.7); and several optional activities shifted participation by roughly 4–8 percentage points in both directions — A01, A05, A07, A09, A21 and A23 increased while A03, A11, A12, A13, A18, A22 and A24 decreased — moderate changes on 44–113 traces per window that sampling variation could partly explain.
