### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A01 is executed as a step between A08 and A10, so that the previously direct A08 → A10 succession now runs A08 → A01 → A10. This rests on direct and mutually consistent signs: A01 appears only in the later window, in about 21 in 100 traces around mid-trace; every observed outgoing transition of A01 goes to A10; the successor share of A08 that earlier went to A10 (about 55 in 100) now goes to A01 while its share to A09 is unchanged; and the A08–A10 distance shifted from mostly one step to mostly two. The new co-occurrences of A01 with A08 and with A10, usually with A01 before A10, agree with this reading, and these differences are larger than those seen in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A01 is a rare variant that happened to surface in the later sample: its absence earlier only shows it was not observed there, not that it could not occur, although the coordinated rerouting of A08's successors and the one-step lag shift make pure sampling noise unlikely [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR004, LAG002, REL001, REL002].

Other differences: A12 changed its relation to A03 — earlier the two formed a loop (A03 led to A12 and A12 always led back to A03), whereas later A03 leads directly to A13, A12 leads to A13, and A12 appears to be entered from A02 instead; A03 also occurs somewhat less often (about 9 in 100 later traces versus about 12.5 in 100 earlier), though these counts are small (18 and 25 traces) and this rerouting is not part of the main hypothesis [DIR003, DIR005, DIR002, OCC003].
