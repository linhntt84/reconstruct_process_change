### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One possibility supported by the evidence is that A01 was removed from the process — or is at least no longer executed — and that the flow which previously passed through it now runs directly from its predecessors A03 and A05 into the block it used to precede, namely A09, A10 and A08. The disappearance is measured directly on every control-flow family (occurrence falling from about 3.5 per trace in all 200 traces to zero, an emptied outgoing profile, position support 1→0, and co-occurrence with A03, A05, A08, A09 and A10 dropping to zero); the rewiring at A03 and A05 is observed directly, with the new targets exactly A01's former successors; and the A03/A05-to-A09/A10 gaps shortened by about one step, as expected when an intermediate activity is gone. The evidence package reports no chance-expectation baseline for the number of flagged items, so this judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the items rather than on an item count; a competing explanation it cannot rule out is that A01 still executes under a label outside the analysed units, which would look identical here.
