- In the earlier window the fixed sequence A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 → A26 ran in every trace. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, two segments that previously ran in (almost) every case became optional. First, the fixed chain A19 → A25 → A20 between A41 and A26 — executed in every earlier trace — is skipped as a whole in about 46 in 100 later traces: the three activities either all occur (about 53 in 100 traces, with unchanged order and spacing) or are all absent, and A41 then connects directly to A26. Second, A06, which previously ran between A14 and A15 in about 69 in 100 traces, is now observed in only about 26 in 100, later in the trace, making A14 → A15 the dominant direct route. The change is shown directly by the successor profiles of A14 and A41, by the co-occurrence collapse with a large "neither" share, and by the newly bimodal A41→A26 distance, and it agrees across the occurrence, direction, relation and position indicators. [OCC006, OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, DIR014, DIR041, REL001, REL024, REL025, REL030, LAG041, POS001]

The evidence cannot distinguish a change in routing (the segments becoming skippable) from a shift in the mix of case types between the windows, such as a new case variant without these segments; and positions are measured in event order, not clock time, so A06's later placement is not evidence about timing. [POS001, REL024]

Other differences: the rare activities A02, A04, A08 and A10 appear in roughly two to four times as many traces (A08 from about 4 to 20 in 100) with new weak outgoing edges from A44, A46, A40 and A41, but this rests on few observations (for example A08 in 8 traces before versus 40 after) and is a tentative reading; branch shares around A35–A38, A42–A44, A24 and A43 shifted by about 10 points; A14 now occurs slightly later and more variably and A26 slightly earlier. These are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC002, OCC004, OCC008, OCC010, DIR044, DIR046, DIR040, OCC031, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, OCC039, OCC023, DIR042, DIR043, POS002, POS007]
