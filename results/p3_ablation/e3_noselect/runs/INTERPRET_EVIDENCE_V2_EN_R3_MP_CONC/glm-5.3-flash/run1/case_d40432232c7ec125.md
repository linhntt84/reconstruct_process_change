### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the relative execution order of two groups of activities was exchanged: A19, A21 and A24, which earlier ran in the first third of the trace and were followed by A25, now run in the last third after A29 and are followed by A30, while A20, A22 and A23, which earlier ran late between A29 and A30, now run early and are followed by A25, with A25 and A29 remaining between the two groups. The change is shown directly and consistently: about twenty pairwise order relations all flipped in the same direction on stable co-occurrence bases, the relative positions of all six activities shifted by roughly 0.26–0.31 of trace length (each larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits), and the successor shares match the new order (A19/A21/A24 now lead to A30 in about 92–97 in 100 instead of to A25; A20/A22/A23 now lead to A25 in about 28–37 in 100 instead of to A30; A29 now leads to A19/A21/A24 instead of A20/A22/A23). Every activity involved is well observed in both windows (A20, A22, A23, A25 and A29 in all traces; A19, A21 and A24 in about 29–39 in 100), so this is a reordering of existing paths rather than paths appearing or disappearing. The evidence cannot show whether the new order holds for all cases or reflects a changed mix of case variants, and it cannot establish whether the A08 decline belongs to the same restructuring.
