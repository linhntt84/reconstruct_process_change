### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 was removed from the process (or is no longer executed), cutting out the step between A03 and its downstream activities: A02 ran in every trace before and in none after, and A03 — which previously handed over to A02 in about 38 in 100 of its outgoing transitions — now leads directly to A01, A05 and A04, taking place earlier in the trace. This is shown directly and consistently by occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators, each based on all 200 traces per window with empirical strength near the maximum (about 0.995), and the relational items confirm that A01, A03, A04 and A05 now occur without A02. [OCC001, DIR002, POS002, REL001, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR003, POS003]

An important competing explanation cannot be ruled out: zero participation only shows that A02 was not observed in the later window, so a relabelling of A02 or a later window drawn from a variant without A02 would look identical. The exact edge-level rewiring is inferred from shifted outgoing shares rather than observed as a single redirection.
