### Observed differences

- Activity A03 is the clearest difference: it does not appear in any of the 200 earlier traces, while in the later window it occurs exactly once in every trace (participation 0 → 200 of 200, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace) [OCC001].
- A07's outgoing behaviour changed completely: in the earlier window it was followed by A02, A06 or A10 in roughly equal shares (about 33, 32 and 36 in 100 observed transitions); in the later window it is always followed by A03 [DIR004].
- A03 in turn is followed by A02 (about 39 in 100), A06 (33) or A10 (29) in the later window — almost exactly the three-way split A07 used to pass on before [DIR002].
- In the earlier window A03 never co-occurred with A02, A06, A07 or A10; in the later window it co-occurs with each of them in every trace, always after A07 and always before A02, A06 and A10 [REL001, REL004, REL005, REL006].
- The distance from A07 to each of A02, A06 and A10 moved one step later, from lags of 1–3 to lags of 2–4, as if one event had been inserted in between [LAG005, LAG006, LAG007].
- Positions agree: A07 remains the first event (relative position 1/8 before, 1/9 after, i.e. traces grew by one event), A03 sits at relative position 2/9, and A02, A06 and A10 each move from a median relative position of 3/8 to 4/9 [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A03 was inserted as a new, always-executed activity between A07 and the A02/A06/A10 group: in the earlier window A07 led directly to one of A02, A06 or A10, chosen in roughly equal thirds, and A03 was never observed, while in the later window A07 always leads to A03, A03 always precedes A02, A06 and A10, and A03 hands over to those three in nearly the same thirds; the one-step shift of all three A07-to-group lags and the position profiles (traces growing from eight to nine events with A03 second) match this insertion directly, and a sampling artefact is implausible because A03 moves from 0 of 200 to 200 of 200 traces [OCC001, DIR002, DIR004, REL001, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]. Limitations: the evidence uses event positions rather than clock time, so it says nothing about timing, and it cannot rule out that A03 was already executed earlier but not recorded, since zero participation strictly means only "not observed" [OCC001].

Other differences: the outgoing shares of A02, A06 and A10, the order split within the A02/A06/A10 trio (for example A02 before A10 rises from about 49 to 55 in 100) and the trio's internal lags shift by a few percentage points with mid-range empirical strength; these are consistent with sampling variation between two 200-trace windows and are not part of the main hypothesis [DIR001, DIR003, DIR005, REL002, REL003, REL007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG008, LAG009].
