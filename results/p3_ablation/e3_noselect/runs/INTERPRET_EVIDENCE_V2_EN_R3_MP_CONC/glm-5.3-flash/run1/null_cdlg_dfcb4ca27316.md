### Observed differences

- Branching after A06 shifted: in the earlier window, about 58 in 100 of A06's outgoing transitions (250 in total) led to A15 and about 42 in 100 to A16; in the later window (213 transitions) the split was about 50/50. In counts, transitions to A15 fell from about 144 to about 107 (roughly a quarter fewer), while transitions to A16 stayed at about 106. This difference is larger than about 92 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled data. [DIR005]
- A06 still appeared in about 22 in 100 traces in both windows, but fired fewer times per trace (about 1.25 before, about 1.07 in the later window), so it repeated less within traces. [OCC006]
- A15 became less frequent overall: it appeared in about 17 in 100 traces before versus about 15.5 in 100 in the later window, with mean occurrences per trace down from 0.72 to 0.535 (about a quarter fewer). Its total executions (about 144 before, about 107 after) match its transitions out of A06 almost exactly, so A15 occurs essentially only after A06. [OCC015, DIR005]

### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests that the choice between the two paths out of A06 changed: in the later window, the branch from A06 to A15 is taken relatively less often and the branch to A16 relatively more often (from about 58/42 to about 50/50 of A06's outgoing transitions), and A15 is executed about a quarter less often overall, while A06 still occurs in about the same share of traces but repeats less within them. Supporting signs: the successor-share shift is observed directly on 250 and 213 transitions and is unusual for random splits; A15's occurrence decline matches the branch shift in both direction and size; and A06's participation is stable, so the change lies in branch choice and repetition rather than in A06 disappearing. What is missing: about 8 in 100 random re-splits still show a difference this large, so sampling variation is not ruled out; the occurrence and direction items largely recount the same events and are therefore consistent rather than independent confirmation; the evidence cannot separate a genuine re-routing between A15 and A16 from A06 simply repeating less often with the A15 branch absorbing the reduction; A16's own share of traces is not measured; and no chance baseline for the number of flagged items is reported. [DIR005, OCC015, OCC006]

Other differences: A11 is reached more often in the later window (about 30 in 100 traces vs 24), partly via a newly observed A02→A11 transition, but the evidence does not show A11's other predecessors, so the mechanism of its increase is unclear; A08 occurs in fewer traces (about 11.5 in 100 vs 16.5). These are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC011, DIR002, OCC008]
