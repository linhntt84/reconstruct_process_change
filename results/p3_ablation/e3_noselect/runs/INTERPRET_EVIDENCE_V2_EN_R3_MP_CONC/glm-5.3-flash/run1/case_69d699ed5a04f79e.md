### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the A05 step was removed from the process (or is no longer executed), with its repetition role taken over by direct repetition of A04. A05 fell from about 46 in 100 cases to 0 of 200; A04's outgoing traffic to A05 (about 46 in 100) re-emerged as self-transitions to A04 (about 49 in 100) while A06 held at about half; and repeated A04s are now adjacent rather than separated by A05. These signs agree across occurrence, direction, position, relation and gap indicators, and each qualifying item shows a difference larger than almost all of 200 random re-splits (strength about 0.995). Limitations: zero participation strictly means only that A05 was not observed in the later window, and no screening baseline was provided to compare the item count against a chance expectation; an all-or-nothing participation flip makes pure sampling noise implausible but not formally excluded.
