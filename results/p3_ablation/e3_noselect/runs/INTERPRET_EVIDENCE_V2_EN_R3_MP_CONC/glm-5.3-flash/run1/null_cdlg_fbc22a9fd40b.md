### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests that in the later window activities are repeated fewer times within each trace — a broad reduction in repetition — while the set of traces containing each activity stays essentially unchanged. It involves all ten monitored activities (A01, A03, A05, A06, A08, A09, A10, A11, A12, A13) and is most pronounced for A08 (about 0.9 fewer occurrences per trace) and A11 (about 0.6 fewer). Supporting signs: all ten occurrence items move in the same direction, participation stays at 100 in 100 traces for seven activities and within about 5 in 100 for the rest, and the largest decreases carry the highest empirical strengths (about 0.82–0.84). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010]

What is missing: no screening expectation is reported, so the number of qualifying items cannot be compared with the count an unchanged process would produce; the empirical strengths are moderate (about 0.58–0.84, none in the extreme tail), and because five activities share identical profiles the occurrence items are correlated rather than independent. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later sample of 200 traces simply contains somewhat fewer repetitions overall by chance, which would produce the same across-the-board decrease without any process change; no global trace-length measure is available to exclude this. [OCC003, OCC005, OCC008, OCC001, OCC006, OCC007, OCC009, OCC010]

Other differences: successor shares shifted between existing alternatives — A03 now continues to A01 in about 88 of 100 of its observed outgoing transitions (previously about 73); within the A08–A09–A10 group, A09 leads more often to A10 and A10 more often to A08; A04 more often continues to A07 and A07 more often to A02 (each from about 46 to 53 in 100); and A06 and A13 send slightly larger shares to A04 and A06 respectively. These are small share shifts between well-observed activities and are kept separate from the main hypothesis; they could be a by-product of the reduced repetition or of sampling.
