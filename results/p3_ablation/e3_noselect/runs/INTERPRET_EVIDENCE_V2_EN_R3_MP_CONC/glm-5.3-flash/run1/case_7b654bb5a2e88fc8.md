### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the one-of-three alternative block {A14, A15, A20} is executed at a different place in the later window: in the earlier window it sat between A40 and A21 (A40 → exactly one of A14/A15/A20 → A21 in every trace), whereas in the later window A40 leads directly to A21 and the block runs later, inserted between A22/A23 and A27, with about half of A22's and A23's outgoing transitions entering it and the block leading on to A23 or A27. The block's own character is unchanged — still exactly one of the three per trace, never co-occurring. The change is shown directly and consistently: complete successor replacement at A40, A14, A15 and A20 (dissimilarity 1.0, larger than in 199 of 200 random re-splits), a position shift of the block from about a third to about three-fifths of the trace, flipped orders against A21 and A22, a mixed order against A23, and matching lag changes; the DIR, POS, REL and LAG items largely describe this one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: the later-window profiles for A14/A15/A20 rest on 61–74 traces each, the evidence cannot fully distinguish a single relocation of the block from a removal after A40 combined with a separate insertion before A27, and the small shift in choice shares within the block may be sampling variation. [DIR010, DIR011, DIR016, DIR029, DIR018, DIR019, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL003, REL009, REL014, REL005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Other differences: after A27, routing shifted toward A18 at the expense of A19 (with A16/A17 also using A28 more), and the A41–A44 loop repeated more often per trace; these are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC017, OCC018, DIR012, DIR013, DIR015, OCC026, OCC027, OCC028, OCC029, DIR031]
