### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the exclusive choice between a branch containing A27 and a branch containing the group A36/A37/A42 was replaced by a single flow in which both always execute: A27, previously run in about 55 in 100 cases as an alternative to the group, now runs in every trace between A26 and A31, and the A36/A37/A42 group, previously an alternative in about 46 in 100 cases positioned after A26, now runs in essentially every case directly after A25 and before A28. This rests on direct and mutually consistent signs: participation rose from substantial shares in both windows to near-universal; the A27–group pairs changed from mutually exclusive (co-occurrence about 1 in 100) to universal (about 99–100 in 100); direct-successor and distance evidence shows the new order directly (A25 now leads into the group, the group precedes A28 and A26, A26→A27→A31 within one step each); and the position distributions shifted accordingly, with the core items all showing reference strength near 0.995. Limitations: the internal order of A36/A37/A42 remains unfixed, so the evidence describes an any-order group rather than a fixed sequence, and only two finite windows of 200 traces are available. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A27 becoming mandatory and the group becoming mandatory and relocated are two independent modifications rather than one; the behavioural description above holds either way.

Other differences: a modest shift in routing among A21, A22, A23 and A24 (participation and successor shares moving towards A23 and A32), and small participation or repetition changes for A02, A08, A17, A20, A44, A48 and A49; these are small (roughly 4–9 in 100) and some may reflect sampling variation between the two windows.
