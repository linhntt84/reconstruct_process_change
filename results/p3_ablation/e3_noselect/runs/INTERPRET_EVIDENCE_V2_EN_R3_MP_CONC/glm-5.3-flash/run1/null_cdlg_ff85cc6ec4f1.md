### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the cycle A06 → A08 → A06 was repeated more often within traces: A06 and A08 occurred more frequently per trace (A06 about 0.85 to 1.18, A08 about 0.38 to 0.64) while the share of traces containing them stayed nearly unchanged, and A06 routed a larger share of its outgoing transitions into the loop (to A08, about 44 to 54 in 100) and a smaller share to the exit towards A09 (about 53 to 45 in 100). [OCC006, OCC008, DIR006, DIR008]

Supporting signs: the increase appears in mean occurrences and recurrence rather than participation, which is the signature of more repetition rather than wider use; the A08→A06 back edge grew from about 75 to about 128 observations while remaining about 97–98 in 100 of A08's outgoing transitions; and the most direct differences are unusual for random re-splits — only about 9 in 100 reference comparisons show a larger difference for A08's occurrence increase and for A06's routing mix, and about 21 in 100 for A06's occurrence increase — resting on 30–38 traces and 170–235 transitions respectively. [OCC006, OCC008, DIR006, DIR008]

Limitations and competing explanation: the evidence file contains no screening baseline, so the number of flagged items cannot be compared with the count expected for an unchanged process; with 24 tested units a stable process also produces a few strong-looking items — for example the sharp redistribution at A11 has very high strength but rests on only 9–10 outgoing transitions — so a chance cluster of loop-iteration fluctuation between the windows cannot be fully excluded. The routing shift at A06 also partly mirrors the same count changes and is not an independent confirmation. [DIR011, DIR006, OCC008]

Other differences: A01's outgoing transitions shifted towards A04 (about 42 to 55 in 100) at the expense of A11, A05 and A02; A04 sent a moderately larger share of its transitions to A06 (about 37 to 43 in 100), which feeds the loop region but is not part of the repetition hypothesis itself; and small-sample redistributions at A07, A02 and A12, plus slight participation dips for A11 and A02, are consistent with sampling noise.
