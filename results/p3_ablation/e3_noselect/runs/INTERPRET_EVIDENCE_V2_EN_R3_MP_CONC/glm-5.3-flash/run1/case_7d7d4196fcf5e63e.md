- A02 moved from optional to mandatory: in the earlier window it appeared in about 49 in 100 traces, once per such trace and around mid-trace (median position about 0.51); in the later window it appears in all 200 traces with about 1.8 occurrences per trace, typically at the very start (median position 0.0). - A08 moved the opposite way: earlier it was in every trace with about 2 occurrences per trace, typically in an early position (median about 0.10); later it appears in about 45 in 100 traces with about 0.8 occurrences, typically around mid-trace (median position about 0.50). [OCC006, POS007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural rewiring of the control flow around A02, A08 and A04. In the earlier window, A08 was a mandatory, early activity that either led straight to A04 or opened a repeating block in which A03 always led to A06, A06 led to A02 or A07, and A02 always led to A01; A01, A02, A03 and A06 always appeared together in the roughly half of traces containing that block. In the later window, A02 is mandatory and sits at the start: A08 (now optional, about 45 in 100 traces) always leads to A02, and A02 routes either directly to A04 (about 57 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) or via A03 (about 43), from which A01 (about 52) or A06 (about 48) is reached; A06 in turn leads to A08 (about 53) or A07 (about 47). A01 thereby lost A02 as its predecessor and is now reached from A03, in fewer traces (about 31 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, OCC002, OCC006, OCC001, REL001, REL008]

The routing change is observed directly as successor redistributions with large support on both sides, and the participation, co-occurrence, position and distance items all move in the same direction — each of these differences is larger than about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data — so the indicator families portray one consistent restructuring rather than unrelated effects; A04's unchanged presence and position localise the change to the paths leading into it. [DIR001, POS004, LAG011, REL008, OCC002]

Limitations: the evidence carries no screening expectation, so the number of qualifying items cannot be checked against a chance baseline, and the windows are finite samples of 200 traces each. A pure shift in the frequencies of pre-existing variants would not explain the complete disappearance of the A02→A01 edge (200 of 200 outgoing transitions earlier) alongside edges never observed earlier (A02→A04, A02→A03, A08→A02); what the aggregate view cannot rule out is that the new routing coexists with old-style cases rather than applying to every case. [DIR001, DIR004]

Other differences: A06 now repeats much less within a trace (mean about 2.0 to 0.7 occurrences per trace; recurrence support 0.35 to 0.17), the A06→A07 branch is taken in fewer traces (A07 about 31 to 15 in 100), and A03 occurs slightly less often (about 49 to 45 in 100 traces); these frequency changes accompany the rewiring above but are not required to state it. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC003, REL021]
