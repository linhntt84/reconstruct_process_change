### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the activities A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25 and A32 — all still executed in every case — follow one fixed sequence, A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32, whereas in the earlier window their relative order was variable (A24 usually preceded A23, and the activity following A23 varied from case to case). The change is shown directly by reversed or tightened pairwise orders (A23 before A24 in 12→98 in 100 traces; A22 before A24 in 50→99 in 100), by each activity narrowing to essentially a single successor, and by pairwise distances collapsing onto the constant values a single chain implies, with matching shifts in event positions. The signs come from four indicator families, agree with one another, and rest on supports of roughly 50–200 traces with empirical strengths of about 0.995. Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely tightened ordering constraint from a shift in the mix of execution variants, and no screening baseline was supplied against which to compare the number of qualifying items. [REL027, REL032, REL037, DIR020, DIR023, DIR026, DIR031, LAG010, LAG015, LAG017, LAG027, POS004, POS007, POS009]

Other differences: A08 was executed in about 32 in 100 traces before and about 13 in 100 after, and the A14→A08→A15 detour was largely replaced by the direct A14→A15 transition (about 29→7 and 70→90 in 100 of A14's outgoing transitions), with A08's own outgoing transitions spreading from almost only A15 to a mix — consistent with A08 having become skippable in most cases, but the evidence does not connect this to the re-sequencing. A15 and A16 repeat in about 7 more traces in 100 than before, and the rare activities A02, A04, A06 and A10 occur somewhat more often, though on few traces. [OCC008, DIR008, DIR014, POS001, OCC015, OCC016, OCC010]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a change in the mixture of process variants between the two windows rather than a modified single process; the diffuse before-window gap distributions (for example, A24 preceded A23 at widely varying distances in about 176 traces) argue against pure sampling noise for the re-sequencing cluster, but a variant-mix shift would produce the same profiles. [LAG027, LAG018, REL037]
