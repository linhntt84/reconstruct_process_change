### Observed differences

- Participation and repetition are essentially stable: A03 occurs in every trace in both windows (about 2.0 occurrences per trace before and after, recurring in about 52% vs 50% of traces), while A04, A05 and A07 each appear in roughly half of the traces in both windows (about 51, 52→50 and 50→49 in 100), with no repeated occurrences of A04 or A07 in either window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Routing is broadly stable as well: A05 is followed by A03 in about 96% of its outgoing transitions before and 94% after, and A03's outgoing transitions go mainly to A05 (about 46% before, 49% after) and A06 (about 40% in both windows). [DIR003, DIR005]
- The largest shifts concern the successors of A04 and A06: after A04, the share of transitions to A01 rises from about 20% to 26% while the share to A06 falls from about 16% to 10%; after A06, the share to A07 rises from about 27% to 43% while the share to A04 falls from 50% to 38%. - Elsewhere the routing shares move by only about 2–5 percentage points (for example after A07, transitions to A03 rise from about 54% to 58% while those to A01 fall from about 23% to 18%), and every item's empirical strength is below 0.5, meaning at least half of random re-splits of the pooled data produce differences at least as large. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR007, OCC001, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. Every observed difference is small on its own scale and none stands out against the reference comparisons — all empirical strengths are below 0.5, so random re-splits of unchanged data routinely produce differences at least this large — and the largest shifts (the successors of A04 and A06) rest on few outgoing transitions.
