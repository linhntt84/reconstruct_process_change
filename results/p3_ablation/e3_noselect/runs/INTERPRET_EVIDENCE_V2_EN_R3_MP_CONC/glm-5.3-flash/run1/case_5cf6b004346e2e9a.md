### Observed differences

- The handover after **A10** and **A11** changed completely. In the earlier window, A10's outgoing transitions split between A06 (about 51 in 100) and A07 (about 49 in 100), and A11's between A06 (about 58 in 100) and A07 (about 42 in 100); in the later window, essentially all of these transitions go to A08 (55 of 55 observed A10 transitions, 39 of 39 A11 transitions). [DIR009, DIR010]
- **A08**'s own routing was redistributed. Before, it led almost exclusively to A10 (about 50 in 100 transitions) and A11 (about 48 in 100); in the later window about 29 in 100 of its outgoing transitions go directly to A06, 24 to A07, 27 to A10 and 19 to A11, and its observed outgoing transitions rose from about 123 to about 203. [DIR007]
- A08 now repeats far more within the same traces: it appears in about 25 in 100 traces in both windows, but mean occurrences per trace rose from 0.62 to 1.02 (roughly 2.4 vs 4.1 occurrences per trace containing it), A08→A08 pairs within five steps doubled (21 to 42), and its median relative position moved later (0.44 to 0.50 of the trace). [OCC007, LAG011, POS003]
- A10 and A11 now appear in fewer traces: A10 in about 13 in 100 (previously 20) and A11 in about 12 in 100 (previously 19). [OCC009, OCC010]
- The fixed spacing inside this loop dissolved. A06→A07 pairs lay exactly three steps apart in about 74 in 100 cases before, but only about 33 in 100 in the later window, spread over lags of two to five steps; immediate A10→A08 and A11→A08 pairs appeared where none were observed before, while immediate A10→A07 pairs (about 58 in 100) disappeared. [LAG002, LAG015, LAG016, LAG021]
- Relations shifted accordingly: A10 and A11 now co-occur in about 6 in 100 traces (previously about 12), the strict A08-before-A10 and A08-before-A11 orders vanished (from about 6 in 100 traces to none), and traces with A08 but without A10 or A11 became more common. [REL008, REL009, REL010]
- Moderately, A05 takes part in more traces (about 40 vs 30 in 100) and now leads to A04 in about 31 in 100 of its transitions (previously 21) instead of returning to A01 (69 vs 79), while A04 returns to A01 more often (about 18 vs 10 in 100). [OCC004, DIR004, DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the cyclic segment around A08 was rerouted. In the earlier window, flow leaving A08 passed through A10 or A11 and then continued to A06 or A07; in the later window, A10 and A11 return to A08 in practically all cases, and A08 itself proceeds directly to A06 or A07 in about half of its outgoing transitions. Together with this rerouting, A08 repeats about four times per trace that contains it instead of about two and a half, appears at a later relative position, and the previously fixed three-step spacing between the loop activities gave way to mixed lags of two to five steps. [DIR007, DIR009, DIR010, OCC007, POS003, LAG002, LAG016]

Supporting signs: the successor changes are observed directly on sizable transition samples (about 55–63 outgoing transitions per window for each of A10 and A11, and 123 vs 203 for A08) and exceed the differences seen in 199 of 200 random re-splits; the occurrence, relation and lag changes for the same activities all point in the same direction. [DIR007, DIR009, DIR010, OCC007, REL008, REL010, LAG002]

Limitations: several indicators describe the same underlying traces, so they corroborate but do not independently confirm one another, and the evidence cannot establish whether the successor rerouting or the increased repetition of A08 is the primary change. Finite-sample variation between the two 200-trace windows remains a competing explanation in principle, although it can hardly account for the complete replacement of A10's and A11's successors. [DIR009, DIR010, OCC007]

Other differences: A05 participates in more traces and hands over to A04 more often, with A04 returning to A01 more often; A02 and A09 appear slightly less often (about 25 vs 27 in 100 traces) and A09 shifts outgoing traffic from A06 (about 32 to 24 in 100) toward A07 (about 25 to 31 in 100). These shifts are moderate and could reflect sampling variation between the windows. [OCC003, OCC004, DIR003, DIR004, OCC002, OCC008, DIR008]
