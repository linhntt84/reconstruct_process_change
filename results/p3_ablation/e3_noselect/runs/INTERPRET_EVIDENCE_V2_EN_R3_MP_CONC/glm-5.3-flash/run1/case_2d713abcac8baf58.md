### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: in the earlier window almost every trace took exactly one of three alternative activities (A19, A20 or A21) between A44 and A29, whereas in the later window that one-of-three choice has disappeared and a block of three activities (A26, A27, A28) runs once in every trace between A44 and A29, in no fixed order among themselves. The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently across the indicator families: participation flips from about a third of traces to none for the old trio and from none to every trace for the new trio; A44's successor mix switches wholesale from the old to the new trio; relational evidence places the new block after A44 and before A29 in nearly every later-window trace; and the A44→A29 distance grows from about two to about four steps, matching the insertion of three activities. Limitations: the 1–2 residual occurrences of A19/A20/A21 are too few to characterise; positions and lags refer to event positions, not clock time; and the evidence cannot rule out that A26/A27/A28 are relabelled versions of A19/A20/A21 rather than genuinely new steps, since the identifiers are anonymous codes — although even under relabelling, the observable structure changed from a one-of-three choice to an always-executed three-activity block. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, OCC026, OCC027, DIR044, REL019, REL021, REL022, LAG001, POS007]

Other differences: participation shifted moderately outside this hypothesis — A39 decreased (about 54 to 45 in 100 traces) while A40 and A41 increased (about 46 to 55 and 47 to 55); A46 decreased (about 70 to 58) while A47 and A48 increased (about 59 to 64 each); A42 and A18 increased modestly (about 21–22 to 27 in 100). Outgoing-transition shares also moved within the A22–A25/A34 cluster (A24's transitions to A34 rose from about 43% to 57%) and in A45's split between A46 and A47 (from about 52/44 to 46/52).
