### Observed differences

- The clearest change concerns the pair A07/A08. In the earlier window they always came as a pair: in about 53 in 100 cases both were present, and no case contained one without the other; they appeared in either order about equally often (A07 first in about 28 in 100 cases, A08 first in about 25 in 100). In the later window they never appear in the same case: A07 alone in about 23 in 100 cases, A08 alone in about 29 in 100, and neither in about 49 in 100 (nearly unchanged from 47 in 100). [REL001]
- Participation dropped accordingly: A07 was present in about 53 in 100 cases before versus about 23 in 100 after; A08 fell from about 53 to about 29 in 100. All other activities moved by only about 2 in 100 (A01 53→51; A02–A06, A09, A10 47→49). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC007, OCC008]
- Routing changed in the same direction. Before, about 65 in 100 of A07's observed outgoing transitions went to A08, and about 61 in 100 of A08's went to A07. In the later window these direct hand-offs are no longer observed; both activities now lead almost exclusively to A01 (about 7 in 10) and A05 (about 3 in 10). [DIR007, DIR008]
- Consistently, A01's outgoing mix shifted from A07 towards A08 (about 45→33 and 35→47 in 100 of A01's outgoing transitions). [DIR001]
- The event positions of A07 and A08 moved slightly later (median position about 0.67→1.0); positions are event ranks rather than clock times, and the dominant positional signal is simply how much less often the two activities occur. [POS001, POS002]
- Other differences: routing out of A02 shifted towards A04 (about 36→49 in 100) at the expense of A06 (about 43→31 in 100); routing out of A09 moved from A04 (about 43→25 in 100) towards A06 (about 19→34 in 100), but this rests on only about 35 outgoing transitions per window. [DIR002, DIR009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A07 and A08 changed from being executed together within the same cases to being executed as alternatives. In the earlier window, every case containing one of them contained both, in either order and with a direct hand-off between them; in the later window, no case contains both, each appears alone in roughly a quarter to a third of cases, and their direct transitions are no longer observed, both now flowing to A01 and A05. The share of cases involving at least one of them stayed roughly stable (about 53 in 100 before, 51 in 100 after), so what changed is how the two activities combine, not how often this part of the process is visited. [REL001, OCC007, OCC008, DIR007, DIR008]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the relation change is observed outright (co-occurrence falling from about 53 in 100 cases to 0 out of 200), the participation drops for both activities and the loss of the mutual transitions point the same way, and each key item shows a difference larger than in about 99 of 100 random re-splits of the pooled data. The observation bases are substantial (106 versus 45 cases for A07, 106 versus 57 for A08). [REL001, OCC007, OCC008, DIR007, DIR008]

Limitations: the screening baseline for how many items an unchanged process typically produces is not part of this evidence, so the usual chance comparison cannot be performed; the disappearance of the two direct transitions rests on only about 18 and 17 observed outgoing transitions in the later window, so a rare A07→A08 or A08→A07 hand-off could still exist; and the evidence cannot show why the two became alternatives. [DIR007, DIR008]

Other differences: a possible shift in routing shares out of A02 (towards A04, away from A06) and out of A09 (towards A06, away from A04, based on few observations); these are not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR002, DIR009]
