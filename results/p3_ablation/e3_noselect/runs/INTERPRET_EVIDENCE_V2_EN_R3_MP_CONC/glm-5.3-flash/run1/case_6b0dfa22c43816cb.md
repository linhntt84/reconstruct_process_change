### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of activities A35, A36 and A41 — whose members mostly follow one another directly — was executed at a different point of the flow: between A25 and A27 in the earlier window (A25 → A35/A36/A41 → A27 → A26 → A30), and between A26 and A30 in the later window (A25 → A27 → A26 → A35/A36/A41 → A30). The change is shown directly and consistently: the successor profiles of A25, A26 and of the block members switched as described, the order relations between the block and A26/A27 flip between the windows, the A25→A27 distance shrinks from about four positions to adjacent while A26→A30 stretches from adjacent to about four, and the position distributions shift in matching directions while every involved activity still occurs in all traces in both windows. A limitation is that each window holds 200 traces, so a single relocation of the block cannot be distinguished from several reroutings with the same net effect. [DIR025, DIR026, DIR035, DIR036, DIR041, REL016, REL017, REL021, REL022, LAG013, LAG020, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

Other differences: A02, previously a near-standard closing step between A49 and A50, now appears in only about 21 in 100 traces (before about 75 in 100), at earlier and varied positions and followed by many different activities, while A49 passes directly to A50 — consistent with A02 having become skippable at the end of the case; in addition, A06 and A10 run in roughly three times as many traces, A42 and A43 somewhat less often, and repeats of A44 and A50 have stopped. [OCC002, POS001, DIR002, DIR049, LAG010, LAG042, LAG046, REL001, OCC006, OCC009, OCC036, OCC037, OCC038, OCC044]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of case variants rather than a modified flow; this concerns mainly the smaller shifts — the rises of A06 and A10 rest on 38 and 43 traces and A02's scattered later successors on 46 observed transitions — whereas the block relocation and the A02 bypass each show up consistently across routing, order, distance, position and occurrence measures.
