### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A27 was executed in every one of the 200 traces; in the later window it appears in only about 52 in 100 traces. When it does occur, it still directly follows A26 and directly precedes A28, as before (each step about 95–98 in 100 of the observed transitions in both windows). [OCC026, DIR026, LAG010, LAG014]
- Three activities that appeared in none of the earlier traces — A32, A33 and A38 — each occur in about 50 in 100 later traces (about 98–100 of 200). They essentially always appear together (co-occurrence about 49 in 100), sit around the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.51), and occur in both orders in similar shares, i.e. without a fixed order among themselves. A27 and each of the three new activities almost never share a trace (co-occurrence about 1–2 in 100), while A26 and A28 themselves still occur in every trace. As a result, the distance from A26 to A28, previously 2 steps in about 90 in 100 of cases, is now 2 steps in about half of the cases and 4 steps in about 46 in 100. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the segment between A26 and A28 is executed in two alternative ways: in roughly half of the traces A26 is still followed by A27 and then A28 as before, while in the other half A27 is skipped and A26 leads through A32, A33 and A38 — three activities not observed in the earlier window — before reaching A28. This rests on converging, directly observed behaviour: A27 falling from all 200 traces to about 103, A32/A33/A38 rising from zero to about 100 of 200 traces, A26's exits splitting about evenly between A27 and the new activities, near-perfect mutual exclusivity between A27 and each new activity, the three new activities always occurring jointly, and the A26-to-A28 distance changing from consistently 2 steps to 2 or 4 steps. [OCC026, OCC031, OCC032, OCC037, DIR026, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030, REL034, REL035, REL036, LAG011]

Limitations: the internal sequence of A32, A33 and A38 appears in both orders in similar proportions, so their internal structure is not pinned down, and the evidence cannot show what determines the choice between the two branches; it also cannot exclude that the new activity codes are relabelled rather than genuinely new behaviour, although their complete absence in 200 earlier traces makes a pure sampling explanation implausible. [REL034, REL035, REL036, OCC031]

Participation in A02, A06 and A08 fell to about 8, 4 and 8 in 100 traces on few observations, and A15, A16 and A42 repeat slightly more often.
