### Observed differences

- Three activities that never occurred in the earlier window appear in the later window: A19 in about 30 of 100 traces (59 of 200), A20 in about 41 (82 of 200) and A25 in about 30 (60 of 200); each difference exceeds roughly 99 of 100 random re-splits. The three almost never run together (pairwise co-occurrence at most about 0.5 in 100), so exactly one of them executes in about 99 of 100 later traces. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, REL001, REL002, REL006]

- The new activities sit inside the A27–A28–A29 segment. Earlier, A27 led almost always straight to A28 (about 99 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) and A28 straight to A29 (about 99 in 100). In the later window, A27 routes about 55 in 100 of its transitions to A19, A20 or A25 (about 44 remain direct to A28), and A28 routes about 43 in 100 to them (about 55 remain direct to A29); the new activities in turn lead almost exclusively onward to A28 or A29 (about 97–98 in 100). [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, one of the previously unobserved activities A19, A20 or A25 is inserted into the previously direct A27 → A28 → A29 sequence, in about 99 of 100 later traces exactly one of them running between A27 and A29 — in about 55 of 100 traces between A27 and A28 and in about 43 of 100 between A28 and A29 — as mutually exclusive alternatives, after which control continues to A28 or A29. The support is direct and consistent across indicator families: the three activities go from 0 of 200 traces to 59, 82 and 60; A27 and A28 reroute about half of their outgoing transitions to them; relational evidence places each new activity after A27 and before A29 in every co-occurrence; and the lag distributions shift by exactly one step, with A27→A29 moving from 2 to 3 steps in about 97 of 100 traces. The evidence cannot show what selects among A19, A20 and A25, and the insertion point varies per trace (before vs after A28); an important competing explanation it cannot rule out is that this step was already executed earlier but only became recorded in the later window, rather than being newly added to the process. [OCC018, OCC019, OCC024, DIR027, DIR028, DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, REL005, REL009, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Other differences: A43 is executed less while A44 and A45 are executed more with additional repetitions (A42's handover shifting from A43 toward A44), and several mid-frequency activities shift by 5–6 points (A01, A05, A07, A09 up; A03, A11, A12, A13 down; A10 up from about 5 to 13 in 100), consistent with moderate shifts in how often existing branches are taken; these are not part of the main hypothesis.
