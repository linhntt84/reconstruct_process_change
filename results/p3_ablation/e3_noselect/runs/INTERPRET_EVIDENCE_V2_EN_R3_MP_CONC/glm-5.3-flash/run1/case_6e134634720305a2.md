### Observed differences

- The dominant difference concerns how often the segment A30 → A31 → A36 runs. In the earlier window each of A30, A31 and A36 occurred about 3.5 times per trace and appeared in every trace, with repeats in roughly 75 in 100 traces; in the later window each occurs about once per trace (about 1.0–1.05) and repeats appear in only about 1–2 in 100 traces. [OCC028, OCC029, OCC034]

- The return edge from A36 to A30 largely disappeared: before, about 70 in 100 of A36's outgoing transitions led back to A30 (about 28 in 100 went to A25); in the later window about 92 in 100 lead to A25 and only about 4 in 100 to A30. The forward edges stayed dominant in both windows (A30 → A31 about 97–99 in 100; A31 → A36 about 98 in 100). [DIR036, DIR030, DIR031]

- The order of A30 and A36 changed from mixed to fixed: before, their executions were interleaved or repeated in about 76 in 100 traces (a fixed A30-before-A36 order in about 24 in 100); in the later window A30 precedes A36 in about 98 in 100 traces, while the two still occur together in every trace in both windows. [REL001]

- Pair distances on the event-position scale moved accordingly: A36 follows A30 within two positions in about 89 in 100 cases in the later window (about 12 in 100 before), and A30-after-A30 and A36-after-A36 repeats within five positions, seen about 150 times before, are nearly gone (1–2 observations after). [LAG002, LAG001, LAG003, LAG004]

- The event positions of A30 and A36 became much more concentrated (standard deviation about 0.12 before vs about 0.07 after for both), consistent with a single execution at a stable position instead of several; POS reflects event positions, not clock time. [POS001, POS002]

- Outside this segment the differences are smaller: occurrences per trace fell for several activities (for example A38 from about 1.0 to 0.75, A40 from about 1.76 to 1.56, A37 from about 0.47 to 0.32) and rose modestly for others (for example A16 from about 1.60 to 1.72, A19 from about 0.49 to 0.55); some routing shares also moved, e.g. A43 now sends about 66 in 100 transitions to A44 (before about 54) and about 32 in 100 to A40 (before about 44). [OCC035, OCC036, OCC038, OCC014, OCC017, DIR043]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the repetition of the segment A30 → A31 → A36 stopped: in the earlier window traces typically cycled through it about three to four times, with A36 returning to A30 in about 70 in 100 of its transitions, whereas in the later window the segment runs about once per trace and A36 exits to A25 in about 92 in 100 transitions instead of returning to A30. The change is shown directly by the relational and successor evidence — a fixed A30-before-A36 order in 98 in 100 traces where the pair was previously interleaved or repeated in 76 in 100, and the return edge falling from about 70 to 4 in 100 — and it is consistent across occurrence counts, pair distances and the halved spread of both activities' event positions. The forward edges A30 → A31 and A31 → A36 remained at about 97–98 in 100 in both windows, so the segment itself persisted; what changed is that its repetition is no longer taken. The magnitudes (about 2.4 fewer occurrences per trace; a 66-point shift in A36's exit shares) and empirical strengths near 0.995 — larger differences in only about 1 in 200 random re-splits — make sampling variation implausible for this cluster. [OCC028, OCC029, OCC034, DIR030, DIR031, DIR036, REL001, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: no screening expectation is reported, so the number of qualifying items cannot be formally compared against a chance baseline; and the evidence cannot distinguish a change in the segment's exit behaviour from a shift in the mix of case variants between the windows (for example fewer cases of a repeating variant), which would look identical here. The after-window profiles for the vanished self-repeats rest on only 1–2 observations, although the near-disappearance of those pairs is itself part of the signal. [OCC028, OCC029, OCC034, DIR036, REL001, LAG001, LAG003, LAG004]

Other differences: several activities away from this segment show smaller shifts in how often they run — decreases for A04, A06, A08, A20, A23, A37, A38 and A40–A43, and increases for A15–A19, A21, A24, A32 and A33 — plus routing shifts such as A43's transitions moving from A40 towards A44. These are an order of magnitude smaller than the repetition change, some have only moderate empirical strength, and the evidence does not establish whether they are secondary effects of the repetition change or independent differences.
