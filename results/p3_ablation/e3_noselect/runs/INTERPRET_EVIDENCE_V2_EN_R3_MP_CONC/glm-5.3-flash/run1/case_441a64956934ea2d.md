- The dominant difference is an exchange between A01 and A13. In the earlier window, A01 appeared in about 66 in 100 traces (about 2.2 occurrences per trace) and, of its observed outgoing transitions, about 48% went to A08, 26% to A15 and 26% to A07; in the later window it appears in only about 28 in 100 traces (about 0.6 occurrences per trace) and all of its observed outgoing transitions go to A06. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A hypothesis supported by the evidence is that A01 and A13 exchanged their positions in the process: in the earlier window A01 was the recurring activity of the block reached from A02 and A04 and followed by A07, A08 and A15, while A13 formed a short loop with A06; in the later window A13 has taken over the block role (A02/A04 lead to it, and it is followed by A07, A08 and A15) and A01 sits in the A06 loop. This is shown directly by the swapped successor profiles (A01 earlier ≈ A13 later: A08/A15/A07; A13 earlier = A01 later: A06 only), the mirrored rerouting of A02, A04, A07, A15 and A06, the mirrored position distributions, the flipped co-occurrence with A07/A08/A15, and the swapped step distances; the two roles' overall participation is roughly preserved (block role about 61–66 in 100 traces, loop role about 23–28 in 100), and the key items sit at empirical strengths of about 0.995, meaning differences this large arose in fewer than about 1 in 200 random re-splits. [DIR001, DIR010, DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR011, POS001, POS007, REL003, REL005, REL026, LAG009, LAG026, OCC001, OCC013]

Limitations: the windows are finite samples of 200 traces each and the exchange is not perfectly one-for-one — the block role's participation slips from about 66 to about 61 in 100 traces and the loop role's rises from about 23 to about 28, with somewhat different repeat rates — and no screening baseline was provided, so the comparison against chance rests on the per-item empirical strengths. The evidence also cannot distinguish a genuine swap of the two activities from two independent replacements (A01 leaving the block as A13 enters it, and the reverse in the A06 loop); behaviourally the two look identical here. [OCC001, OCC013]

Other differences: A02, A04, A05, A09 and A12 repeat less often per trace while their trace participation stays roughly unchanged; A07 and A15 occur slightly earlier in the event order; A06 occurs slightly more often. These small shifts are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC009, OCC012, POS005, POS008, OCC006]
