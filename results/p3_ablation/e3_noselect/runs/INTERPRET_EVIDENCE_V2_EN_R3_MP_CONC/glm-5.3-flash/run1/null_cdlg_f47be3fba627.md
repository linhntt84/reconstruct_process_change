### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The only noticeable signals — A05's and A10's outgoing transitions shifting from A04 toward A09 — are proportions based on roughly 46–53 observed transitions each, are of a size that about 7–8 in 100 random re-splits of unchanged data still match or exceed, point in different directions for A06 (whose transitions to A09 fall rather than rise), and are not accompanied by any participation change or ordering evidence, so a sampling fluctuation in an unchanged process cannot be ruled out.
