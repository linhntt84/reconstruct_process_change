### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was replaced by A03 at the same early point of the case: traces that previously ran A02 → A01 → A07 now run A02 → A03 → A07, with A03 occurring exactly once per trace at the position A01 used to occupy and A01 no longer observed in any trace. The change is shown directly on every available view — participation (all 200 traces to none, and none to all 200), direct succession (A02→A01→A07 becomes A02→A03→A07), event position (median 0.2 in both windows) and pairwise relations (A01-only becomes A03-only with A02, A05 and A07, with the earlier ordering preserved) — and 24 of the 25 items exceed about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, making sampling fluctuation an implausible explanation. The agreeing items largely describe one and the same restructuring, so they form one consistent picture rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: the anonymous activity codes cannot distinguish a genuine replacement of A01 by A03 from a relabelling of the same step, the package provides no chance-expectation baseline beyond the random re-splits, and it cannot be ruled out that the later window samples a different process variant rather than a modified one.
