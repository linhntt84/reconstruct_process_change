### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch around A05 and A07 was re-routed and their mutual order shifted: in the later window A05 precedes A07 in about 34 in 100 traces (about 22 before) while A07 precedes A05 in about 18 (about 28 before); A01 feeds A05 more often (about 28% to 45% of its outgoing transitions), A05 passes on to A07 more often (27% to 44%), and A07 goes to A05 far less often (39% to 18%), turning to A09 and A14 instead. The order change is shown directly by the relational item, whose difference exceeds about 97 of 100 random re-splits, and by the successor profiles of A05 and A07, the sharpest routing shifts in the whole evidence set (each larger than in about 98–100 of 100 random re-splits); lag and position items point the same way. Every activity and path involved was observed in a substantial share of traces in both windows, so this is a reallocation between existing alternatives rather than a replaced path. The main limitation is that the agreeing items describe one underlying re-routing rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence provides no expected-by-chance baseline for the number of qualifying items; a competing explanation it cannot rule out is that some small-share movements reflect sampling noise, for instance the new outgoing shares of A04, which rest on only a few dozen observed transitions. [REL001, DIR001, DIR005, DIR007, DIR009, LAG001, LAG002, POS001, POS002, OCC004, DIR004]
