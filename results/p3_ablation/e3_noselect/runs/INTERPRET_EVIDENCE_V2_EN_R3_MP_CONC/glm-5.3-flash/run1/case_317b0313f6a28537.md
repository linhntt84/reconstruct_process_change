### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative block A19/A20/A25 — exactly one of which executes in almost every case — was relocated: in the earlier window it ran late in the case, entered from A27 or A28 and led back into A28/A32, while in the later window it sits between A45 and A26, so that A45 is typically followed by one of the three alternatives, which then run before A26, and A27, A28 and A32 form a direct chain (A27 → A28 → A32, each immediate). [DIR019, DIR020, DIR025, DIR045, DIR027, DIR028, REL003, REL005, REL009, REL014, LAG007, LAG010, LAG011]

The change is shown directly and consistently by four views of the same behaviour — order statistics, successor profiles, event distances and positions — all moving in the same direction with maximal empirical strength (differences larger than all but about 1 in 200 random re-splits) and based on all 200 cases per window for the key items. Participation of the block and of A26, A27, A28, A32 and A45 is unchanged, so the block was moved rather than added, removed or taken more often, and the mutual order of A26, A27, A28 and A32 stayed fixed. [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS008, REL019, REL022, REL025, DIR019, DIR045, LAG007, LAG010]

Limitations: the evidence is aggregate, so per-case entry and exit points cannot be traced — about 15 in 100 of A45's outgoing transitions still go elsewhere and some early activities now also lead into the block, leaving open whether every case routes the chosen alternative directly from A45 to A26 [DIR045, DIR002, DIR004, DIR006]; and the LAG items documenting the disappeared old adjacencies rest on only 1–3 after-window observations [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG005]. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that the late segment around A27–A32 was restructured and the block's new position is a side effect of that restructuring rather than a direct move; the unchanged position of A45, the fixed internal order of the segment and the specific successor changes at A45 and the block speak against this, but both readings fit the observed order and adjacency changes. [DIR045, POS008, REL019, REL022, REL025]
