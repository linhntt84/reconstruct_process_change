### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A30 and A31 changed from a choice between two alternative three-activity branches — either A33, A35 and A36, or A37, A38 and A39, each taken in about half of the cases and never both — into a single longer sequence in which all six activities execute in nearly every case, ordered A30, then A33/A35/A36, then A37/A38/A39, then A31. This rests on direct relational evidence (cross-group pairs moved from never co-occurring to co-occurring in about 98 of 100 traces, with a consistent across-group order), on participation rising from about 50 to about 99 in 100 cases for all six activities with roughly 100 versus 200 traces behind each measurement, on A30's outgoing transitions concentrating on the first trio, and on agreeing positional shifts (A30 earlier, A31 later, step distances growing from 1–3 to 4–5). Limitations: the order inside each trio remains unfixed, and the indicators cannot separate removal of the routing choice from a case-mix shift in which a new always-both variant replaced the two one-branch variants; no screening baseline is included, so the judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the evidence rather than on a chance-expectation count. [OCC030, OCC033, REL054, REL058, REL061, REL052, REL064, DIR030, REL049, LAG023, LAG027, POS005, POS006]
