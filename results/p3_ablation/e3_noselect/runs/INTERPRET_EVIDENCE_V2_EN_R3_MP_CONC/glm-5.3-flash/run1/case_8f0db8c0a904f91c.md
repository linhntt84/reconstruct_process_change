### Observed differences

- The clearest difference concerns the activity block A19 → A25 → A20. In the earlier window each of the three activities executed exactly once in every trace (200 of 200, mean occurrences per trace ≈ 1.0); in the later window each appears in only about 53% of traces (roughly 106 of 200). [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025]
- The three activities move as an all-or-nothing unit. Before, they co-occurred in 100% of traces, essentially always in the order A19, then A25, then A20. In the later window they co-occur in about 53 in 100 traces, appear without each other in at most about 0.5 in 100 traces, and in about 47 in 100 traces none of the three appears at all. [REL008, REL009, REL014]
- The surrounding always-present activities are unchanged but are now often reached without the block. A26, A41, A46 and A47 still occur in every trace in both windows, and in the later window each of them appears without A19 in about 47 in 100 traces (before: 0). A41's outgoing transitions shifted from about 96% leading to A19 to roughly 50% to A19 and 44% directly to A26 — a direct A41→A26 step not observed earlier; accordingly, A26 now follows A41 within one step in about 44% of traces, whereas before about 93% of A41→A26 pairs were four steps apart, the width of the block sitting between them. [OCC026, OCC037, OCC042, OCC043, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, DIR041, LAG022]
- A02 became much rarer and changed character: it appeared in about 29 in 100 traces before and about 9 in 100 in the later window; its typical position moved from near the end of the trace (median ≈ 0.97 of trace length) to mid-trace (≈ 0.47) with much more spread, and its outgoing transitions shifted from about 85% leading to A47 to a spread over many successors (consistent with A46's transitions to A02 falling from about 25% to under 1%). The after-side of these profiles rests on only 17 traces, so the new routing picture is weakly supported. [OCC002, POS001, DIR002, LAG005, DIR046]
- A middle group shifted in frequency: A35 and A36 became more common (about 42 → 52 in 100 traces each) while A37 and A38 became less common (about 59 → 48–49 in 100); after A35, the split between its successors A32 and A36 evened out from roughly 57%/39% to about 50%/49%. [OCC031, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, DIR035, DIR036]
- Several rare activities rose — A04 (about 5 → 12 in 100 traces), A06 (about 4 → 13), A08 (about 2 → 7) and A10 (about 4 → 8) — and A43 became less frequent (about 70 → 61 in 100 traces, mean occurrences 1.0 → 0.8, with its outgoing share towards A46 rising from about 55% to 64%); these rest on modest counts. [OCC004, OCC006, OCC008, OCC010, OCC039, DIR043]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block A19 → A25 → A20, which in the earlier window executed exactly once in every case between A41 and A26/A46/A47, is skipped in about 47 in 100 cases in the later window, while the block's internal order and the always-present activities A26, A41, A46 and A47 are unchanged. The support is direct and consistent: participation of all three block activities fell from 100% to about 53% on 200 traces per window; their co-occurrence is all-or-nothing in both windows (co-occurrence 100% → about 53%, neither present 0 → about 47%, the block essentially never split); the partners now appear without the block in about 47 in 100 traces; and the bypass is visible both in A41's successor profile (about 96% to A19 before, versus about 50% to A19 and 44% directly to A26 after) and in the A41→A26 distance (about 93% four steps apart before, versus about 44% one step and 49% four steps after). [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, REL008, REL009, REL014, REL010, REL011, DIR041, LAG022, OCC026, OCC037, OCC042, OCC043]

The evidence cannot distinguish between the block having become optional within a single process and a shift in the case mix towards a variant that never executes the block, and it cannot show whether skipping depends on case attributes; either would produce the patterns above. [OCC019, OCC020, OCC025, REL008]

Other differences: A02 became rare (about 29 → 9 in 100 traces) with a shifted typical position and changed successors; A35/A36 rose while A37/A38 fell, with the A35→A32/A36 split rebalanced; the rare activities A04, A06, A08 and A10 rose; and A43 declined with its outgoing share shifting towards A46. These are separate from the block hypothesis and are not part of it. [OCC002, POS001, DIR002, DIR046, OCC031, OCC032, OCC033, OCC034, DIR035, OCC004, OCC006, OCC008, OCC010, OCC039, DIR043]
