### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the activities A20, A22, A23, A24, A25 and A26 occurred together in virtually every trace that contained any of them, but without a fixed order: A24 came before A20 in about 75 in 100 traces (A20 first in about 25), A24 came before A23 in about 88 in 100, and A22/A24 split roughly 50/50. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window the activity group A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25 and A32 is executed as one fixed sequence (A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32), whereas in the earlier window the same activities ran together in every trace but in mixed orders with several possible successors — a change in ordering constraints, not a swap of positions. [REL002, REL005, REL007, DIR003, DIR005, DIR008, LAG003, LAG005]

Supporting signs: 51 evidence items qualified against roughly 0.71 expected by chance for the 142 tested control-flow units, so the volume cannot be explained by sampling an unchanged process; the order change is shown directly by relational evidence while co-occurrence stays at 100 in 100, ruling out a participation change as its source; and the direction, transition and distance indicators all describe the same chain consistently rather than unrelated shifts. [screening, REL002, DIR003, LAG003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different mixes of case variants (for example a fixed-order variant becoming dominant) rather than a changed ordering constraint within unchanged cases, and a few distance items on now-rare reversed pairs rest on a single after-window observation. [LAG016, LAG021]
