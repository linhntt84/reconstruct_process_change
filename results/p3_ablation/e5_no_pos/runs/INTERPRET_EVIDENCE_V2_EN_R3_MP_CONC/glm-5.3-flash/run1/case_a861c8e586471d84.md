### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative block A19/A20/A25 — earlier executed exactly once per trace between A41 and A26, with the choice split roughly 36/31/35 — is no longer executed in the later window, and that A41 now leads directly to A26. This reads as a structural difference in which the middle step is removed or completely bypassed, not as a re-weighting of paths that still exist: the block is absent rather than merely rarer, while A26, A14, A24, A31 and A12 continue at their earlier rates. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, LAG002, REL010]

The support is direct and consistent: the disappearance is far beyond sampling variation (0 of 200 traces against an earlier 71 of 200), the rewiring of A41 is observed in its successor profile, and the A41→A26 distance shrinking from two positions to one confirms the intermediate step is gone rather than reordered. Thirty items qualified against roughly 0.57 expected for an unchanged process, and occurrence, successor, relation and lag indicators all describe this one story. [OCC001, DIR004, LAG002, REL010]

Limitations: the evidence shows the block is no longer executed but cannot distinguish whether the activities were removed from the process or the branch is now simply never entered; nor can it rule out that the two windows draw on a different mix of cases. A20's later outgoing profile rests on a single observation and supports nothing on its own. [OCC002, DIR002]

Other differences: the positional gap between A24 and a following A31 shrank from mostly two positions to mostly one, consistent with one fewer activity lying between them, but the evidence does not show which activity, if any, previously sat there. [LAG001]
