### Observed differences

- The activity block A19 → A20 → A25 ran in essentially every trace in the earlier window (participation 100% each, with A19 before A20 before A25 in about 99 in 100 traces). In the later window each of the three appears in only about 53 in 100 traces, and they appear together or not at all: A19 and A20 co-occur in 53% of traces, and in about 47 in 100 traces neither occurs. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL008, REL009, REL014]
- The block is bypassed: before, A41 led to A19 in about 96 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, and A26 followed A41 four positions later in about 93% of observations. In the later window, A41 leads to A19 in about half of its outgoing transitions and directly to A26 in about 44 in 100, and the A41→A26 distance is one step in ~44% of cases versus essentially never before. [DIR005, LAG002]
- The surrounding activities A26, A41, A46 and A47 still occur in every trace in both windows; only their relation to the block changed, and the earlier relative order (block first, then A26/A46/A47) is preserved whenever they do co-occur. [REL010, REL012, REL013, REL015, REL017, REL019, REL021]
- A02 dropped from about 29 in 100 traces to about 9 in 100; its outgoing transitions, previously about 85% towards A47, are now spread over many targets, though the later profile rests on only 17 outgoing transitions. [OCC001, DIR001]
- The route A46 → A02 → A47 largely disappeared: A46's outgoing transitions to A02 fell from about 25 in 100 to under 1 in 100, while A46 → A47 rose from about 75% to about 96%, and the A46→A47 distance is now one step in ~97% of cases versus ~75% before. [DIR006, LAG003]
- A04 and A06 each appear in roughly 12–13 in 100 traces in the later window versus 4–5 in 100 before, based on small counts (8–9 traces before, 24–25 after). [OCC002, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Main hypothesis: in the later window, the fixed sequence A19 → A20 → A25, previously executed between A41 and A26 in every trace, is skipped in about 47 in 100 traces, with A41 leading directly to A26 — the block appears to have become optional. The screening produced 37 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected for an unchanged process, and the signs are direct and mutually consistent: all three block activities dropped from full participation to about 53% in lockstep, with pairwise co-occurrence falling from 100% to ~53% and "neither" rising to ~47%; A41's outgoing share to A19 halved while a direct A41→A26 transition appeared at ~44%; and the A41→A26 gap moved from almost always four steps to a mix of one step (~44%) and four steps (~49%), while the internal order A19→A20→A25 and A26's position after the block are preserved whenever the block runs. A limitation is that the evidence cannot show whether the direct A41→A26 path is newly added or existed before at a frequency too low to observe (no one-step A41→A26 observations appear in the earlier window). [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR005, LAG002, REL008, REL009, REL014, REL010, REL015, REL019]

Other differences: A02 became much rarer (about 29 in 100 traces before, about 9 in 100 after) and the route A46 → A02 → A47 was largely replaced by A46 going directly to A47; A02's remaining outgoing behaviour is based on few observations. A04 and A06 occur somewhat more often, also on small counts. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window mixes two process variants (one with the block, one without) rather than a single modified process; window-level shares cannot separate these.
