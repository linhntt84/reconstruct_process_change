### Observed differences

- Four activities became mandatory rather than optional: before, A27 ran in about 55 in 100 traces and A36, A37 and A42 in about 46–47 in 100 each; in the later window each of them runs in essentially every trace (199–200 of 200), about once per case. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Routing at the two branch points changed: before, A25 led to A28 in about 97 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, and A26 split between A27 (about 54 in 100) and A36/A37/A42 (about 45 in 100 combined); in the later window A25 leads to A36, A37 or A42 (about 31–34 in 100 each) and A26 leads to A27 in about 98 in 100 transitions. [DIR001, DIR002]
- A36, A37 and A42 turned from an alternative to A27 into a fixed part of every case: before, they co-occurred with A27 in only about 1–2 in 100 traces, while in the later window they co-occur with A27 in about 995 in 1000 traces and precede it in about 96–97 in 100; among themselves they ran in mixed order before (co-occurrence about 46 in 100, order splits near 50/50) and still do in the later window. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL020, REL021, REL022]
- The block of A36/A37/A42 moved from after A26 to before A28: before, A26 preceded the block activities (about 46 in 100 traces each) and A28 preceded them whenever they co-occurred (about 45–46 in 100, essentially never the reverse), with A28 following A25 at a one-step distance in about 96 in 100 cases; in the later window the block precedes A26 in about 98–99 in 100 and A28 in about 98 in 100 traces, and the A25→A28 distance is four–five steps in about 99 in 100 cases. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, LAG001]
- A31 remains the closing activity in both windows: it occurs in essentially all traces throughout, previously reached directly from each block activity (about 29–34 in 100 of their transitions) and after A27; in the later window it consistently follows A27 two positions after A26 (about 97 in 100, previously two steps in 54 and four steps in 42 in 100 depending on the branch), while the block activities now lead to A28 instead. [REL010, REL017, REL018, REL019, LAG003, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the choice between two branches after A26 was replaced by one fixed sequence in which the previously optional block A36/A37/A42 became mandatory and was relocated before A28: in the earlier window each case ran either A26→A27→A31 (about 54 in 100) or A26→A36/A37/A42→A31 (about 46 in 100) with A28 directly after A25, whereas in the later window every case runs A25, then A36/A37/A42 in mixed order, then A28, then A26→A27, then A31. The order reversals between the block and A28 and the rise in block–A27 co-occurrence from about 1 in 100 to about 995 in 1000 show the relocation directly, the successor changes at A25, A26, A36, A37 and A42 agree with it, the A25→A28 distance shift quantifies it, and the 40 qualifying control-flow items far exceed the 0.7 expected without any change. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The evidence shows only relative order, so it cannot distinguish whether the block moved earlier or the A26/A27/A28 part moved later, nor whether the relocation and the disappearance of the branch choice were one coordinated modification or two simultaneous ones; an important competing reading is therefore two simultaneous changes rather than one. Several LAG items rest on only 1–2 observations in one window and were not used to support the hypothesis. [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]

Other differences: small within-trace repetition appeared in the later window — A27 recurs in about 2–3 in 100 cases and A36/A37 rarely, which was not observed before [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]; a few low-frequency outgoing edges (about 0.5–1 in 100 transitions) from A25, A36, A37 and A42 to other activities also appear only in the later window [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005].
