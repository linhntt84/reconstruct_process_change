### Observed differences

- Participation: A27, A36, A37 and A42 each occurred in about 46–54% of cases in the earlier window and in essentially every case in the later window (99.5–100%), exactly once per trace (mean occurrences per trace rose from about 0.5 to about 1.0, recurrence stayed near zero). A25, A26, A28 and A31 were present in all cases in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL009, REL010, REL017]
- In the earlier window, A26 was a choice point: about 54% of its outgoing transitions went to A27 and about 13–17% each to A36, A37 and A42, and A27 never ran together with A36, A37 or A42 (co-occurrence 0–0.5%). In the later window A26 leads almost only to A27 (about 98.5%), and A27 co-occurs with each of A36, A37 and A42 in about 99.5% of cases, with the group member running before A27 (about 99%). [DIR002, REL011, REL012, REL013]
- The group moved earlier in the sequence: before, A25 led almost only to A28 (about 98% of its outgoing transitions) and A28 followed A25 immediately (gap of one position in about 98.5% of cases); in the later window A25 leads to A36, A37 or A42 (about 32–35% each), A28 sits four positions after A25 (about 97%), and A36/A37/A42 now precede A26 and A28 in about 99% of cases, where previously A26 and A28 preceded them in about 46%. A25, which previously often ran without A27/A36/A37/A42 (about 46–54% of cases), now co-occurs with each of them in about 99.5–100% of cases and precedes them. [DIR001, LAG001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016]
- The group's exit changed: before, A36, A37 and A42 each led to A31 in about 29–39% of their outgoing transitions and virtually never to A28; in the later window each leads to A28 about 30–36% of the time and no longer to A31, while A31 keeps its endpoint role after A27 (A27 before A31 in about 99% of cases; the A26→A31 gap is two positions in about 96.5% of cases, against a 54% two / 44% four split before). [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL010, LAG003]
- The internal order of A36, A37 and A42 remains unfixed in both windows (each pair appears in both orders in roughly equal shares); only their co-occurrence changed, from about 46% to about 99.5%. [REL020, REL021, REL022]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the exclusive branch after A26 was replaced by a fixed sequence containing all branch activities: in the earlier window a case ran A25 → A28 → A26 and then either A27 alone (about 54% of cases) or the group A36, A37, A42 once each in varying order (about 46%), both followed by A31; in the later window every case runs A25 → A36/A37/A42 in varying order → A28 → A26 → A27 → A31. A36, A37 and A42 thereby became mandatory and moved before A28 and A26, and A27 became mandatory as well. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL011, REL014, LAG001, LAG003]

Supporting signs: the participation of all four activities rose from about half to all cases while staying once per trace; the order relations flipped consistently (the group now precedes A26, A28 and A27, with which it previously shared no cases or ran in the reverse order); the successor profiles of A25 and of the group members shifted accordingly; the A25→A28 gap grew from one to four positions, matching three activities inserted between them; and A31 kept its endpoint role. The 37 qualifying items far exceed the fewer than one expected by chance among the 132 tested units, and the occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators tell one coherent story — though many relation items describe the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [OCC002, DIR001, REL011, REL014, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence shows the two windows differ but cannot establish that a change occurred at a specific point in time, nor rule out that the windows draw from different case populations; gaps and positions are event positions, not clock times, so no calendar-timing statement is implied. A few lag items rest on a single observation and were not relied upon. [LAG002, LAG005, LAG006]

Other differences: a handful of rare outgoing transitions appear only in the later window (each about 0.5–1% of the source's outgoing transitions, e.g. from A25 to A02/A08 and from A37 and A42 to A06, A10, A21, A39, A46, A02, A14); they are marginal and not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR001, DIR004, DIR005]
