### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a route from A26 to A28 through the three previously unobserved activities A32, A33 and A38 runs in about half of the cases instead of the earlier A26→A27→A28 course: A26 now leads about half of its outgoing transitions to A27 and half to the new activities; the new activities follow A14, A15 and A26, execute in varying mutual order, and lead on to A28, which earlier was reached almost only after A27; A27 itself dropped from executing in every trace to about half. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR006, REL010, REL014, REL018, REL021, REL022, REL025, REL028, LAG003]

This hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: fifty control-flow items qualified where fewer than one would be expected without a change, and the placement of the new activities rests on ordering relations (they follow A14/A15/A26 and precede A28), not on frequencies alone. The main limitation is that zero participation in the earlier window means only "not observed in that sample of 200 traces", so a rare pre-existing variant that the earlier window did not sample cannot be fully ruled out.
