### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the flow around A26 was rewired and its order relative to A19, A20 and A25 reversed: in the earlier window A45 fed A19/A20/A25, which ran before A26 and handed control to it, while A27 led to A28 and on to A32; in the later window A45 feeds A26 directly, A26 and A27 typically run before A19, A20 and A25, and those three now hand over to A28 and A32, with A27 and A28 also branching into them one to two steps earlier. This is shown directly and consistently: screening qualified 26 control-flow items against roughly 0.58 expected for an unchanged process (about forty-five times that expectation), the order reversal is observed by the relation indicator for every pair of A26 and A27 with the group, the successor changes rest on 52–202 observed transitions, and the new adjacencies on 32–75 observations.
