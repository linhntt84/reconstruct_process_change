- In the earlier window, A27, A36, A37 and A42 each ran in every trace (participation 100 in 100, about once per trace). In the later window each runs in only about half of the traces: A27 in about 54 in 100, A36 in about 47, A37 and A42 in about 48 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural reordering of the segment around A25–A31: the trio A36/A37/A42, which previously executed in every trace between A25 and A28 and before A26 and A27, now executes after A26 and directly before A31, as a mutually exclusive alternative to A27. In the later window, about 47 in 100 traces run A25 → A28 → … → A26 → (A36/A37/A42 in mixed order) → A31 and skip A27, while about 53 in 100 run A25 → A28 → … → A26 → A27 → A31 and skip the trio; A25, A26, A28 and A31 still run in (nearly) every trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG005]

The hypothesis is shown directly, not only by frequency shifts: the co-occurrence collapse between A27 and each trio member, the order reversals around A28/A26, and the matching successor and distance changes all point to the same repositioning. The participation drop rests on 94–107 traces per activity, and chance is an unlikely explanation for the ensemble — 40 control-flow items qualified where about 0.69 would be expected without a change, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data (a measure of unusualness, not a probability of change). [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Limitations: the items share traces and are not independent confirmations; the trio's exact placement among the unchanged activities between A28 and A26 is not directly observed (it is only more than five steps after A25 in the later window); and the evidence shows relative order only — it cannot distinguish whether the trio moved after A26 or the A28/A26 part moved ahead of the trio, nor which of the two later variants descends from the earlier single path. Positions are event positions, not clock times. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG009]

Other differences: both windows show a few scattered rare outgoing transitions (about 0.5–2 in 100) from A25, A26 and the trio members to other activities, at similar levels; they are marginal and not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
