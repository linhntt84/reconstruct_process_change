### Observed differences

- The largest difference is a reversal in participation between two groups of three activities. In the earlier window, A24, A25 and A26 each appeared in about 82 in 100 traces while A21, A22 and A23 each appeared in about 19 in 100; in the later window this reversed, with A21–A23 each in about 79 in 100 traces and A24–A26 each in about 21 in 100. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]
- The routing out of A20 flipped accordingly. In the earlier window, about 82 in 100 of A20's outgoing transitions led to A24 (about 29%), A26 (about 28%) or A25 (about 24%), and about 19 in 100 to A21–A23; in the later window, about 79 in 100 led to A23 (about 29%), A21 (about 28%) or A22 (about 22%), and only about 21 in 100 to A24–A26. [DIR003]
- Relations with A20 mirror this: A20 previously shared traces with A24–A26 in about 81 in 100 cases (with A20 first) and rarely with A21–A23 (about 19 in 100); in the later window it shares traces with A21–A23 in about 79 in 100 cases (again A20 first) and rarely with A24–A26 (about 21 in 100). [REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- Group-internal behaviour swapped as well. In the later window, A21, A22 and A23 usually occur together (about 79 in 100) in both orders in similar proportions, i.e. without a fixed order among them; in the earlier window it was A24, A25 and A26 that usually co-occurred (about 81 in 100, also in mixed order), whereas in the later window usually neither activity of such a pair occurs (about 79 in 100). [REL015, REL016, REL023, REL036, REL037, REL041]
- Downstream relations shifted with the swap: A21–A23 now co-occur with A32 and A33 in about 55 in 100 traces versus about 9 in 100 before, A24–A26 correspondingly less (about 52 in 100 down to about 15 in 100), and A30 now usually co-occurs with A21–A23 (about 79 in 100) instead of with A24–A26 (about 82 in 100 before). [REL020, REL021, REL022, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL033, REL034, REL035, REL038, REL039, REL042, REL043, REL045, REL046]
- Smaller differences rest on few observations: A02 rose from about 2 to about 9 in 100 traces with a fully redistributed successor profile (4 versus 18 supporting traces), A10's outgoing transitions spread from about 97% leading to A33 to a spread over several targets (14 after-observations), and the A32→A10 distance redistributed from mostly adjacent to lags of 1–3 steps (4 after-observations). [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the shares of two alternative paths after A20 reversed: in the earlier window A20 was typically followed by A24, A25 and A26, which then usually appeared together in the same trace, whereas in the later window A20 is typically followed by A21, A22 and A23, which usually appear together in no fixed order. Both groups remain observable in a substantial share of traces in both windows (about 19–21 in 100 for the minority side), so the evidence fits a change in how often existing alternative paths are taken rather than activities being added, removed or bypassed. The routing change at A20 is shown directly on all 200 traces of each window, the participation shifts are large (about 60 in 100 traces for each of the six activities), and the relational evidence agrees across many activity pairs; with 59 qualifying items against fewer than one expected by chance, sampling noise cannot account for the pattern as a whole. The evidence cannot rule out that the reversal stems from a changed mix of case types rather than a modified routing decision, and because A21–A23 appear in both orders, no fixed sequence within that group can be asserted. [DIR003, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL023]

Other differences: A02's participation increased (about 2 to 9 in 100 traces) with a redistributed successor profile, and A10's outgoing transitions spread across several targets while the A32→A10 distance shifted; all three rest on very few later-window observations (18, 14 and 4) and may be sampling artefacts. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001]
