### Observed differences

- The dominant change is an exchange between two alternative activity blocks. In the earlier window, A21, A22 and A23 each ran in about 75 of 100 traces and almost always together (joint presence in about 75 of 100 traces), while A24, A25 and A26 each ran in only about 25 of 100 traces, also together, and the two blocks almost never shared a trace (about 1 in 200). In the later window this is reversed: A21–A23 each appear in about 25 of 100 traces (still together whenever they appear), and A24–A26 in about 75–77 of 100, again together. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL025, REL026, REL034, REL049, REL050, REL055, REL027]

- The choice after A19 and A20 flipped accordingly: earlier, A19 and A20 preceded the A21–A23 trio in about 74 of 100 traces and the A24–A26 trio in about 25; later, they precede A24–A26 in about 75 of 100 and A21–A23 in about 24. A20's outgoing transitions show the same flip: about 75% went to A21–A23 earlier versus about 24% later, while A24–A26 rose from about 25% to about 75%. [REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024, DIR002]

- The surrounding orderings are preserved in both windows: whichever trio runs, it co-occurs with A29, A30, A34 and A35 in essentially every such trace, and it precedes A34 and A35 in nearly all joint appearances. Only the frequencies of these relations changed, not their direction. [REL030, REL032, REL033, REL038, REL040, REL045, REL047, REL051, REL053, REL054]

- A06 changed in frequency and placement: it ran in about 17 of 100 traces earlier and about 53 of 100 later. Earlier its outgoing transitions were spread over many successors (largest single share about 21%, to A33); later about 90% go to A35, and in event order A35 follows A06 immediately in about 97 of 100 observed pairs. A34 now leads to A06 in about half of its outgoing transitions (earlier A06 was not among its observed successors), and the direct A34→A35 transition fell from about 89% to about 48% of A34's outgoing, with A34→A35 pairs now split between one and two steps apart (about 49% / 51%) instead of 90% at one step. [OCC002, DIR001, DIR005, LAG001, LAG013, REL011, REL012]

- Smaller changes: A02 and A08 became rarer (participation about 17→8 and about 14→5 in 100 traces), and a few backbone spacings shifted slightly (for example, A29 immediately before A19 in about 84% of pairs earlier versus about 95% later). [OCC001, OCC003, LAG007, LAG012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two existing alternative paths swapped how often they are taken: the path on which A21, A22 and A23 run together after A19 and A20 dropped from roughly three quarters of traces to one quarter, while the alternative path on which A24, A25 and A26 run together rose from one quarter to three quarters. This fits a shift of shares between existing paths rather than activities being added, removed or repeated: every involved activity is observed in a substantial share of both windows (about 25% and 75%), each trio still runs together and still precedes A34 and A35 alongside A29/A30, and repetition stayed negligible in both windows. The volume of qualifying evidence (90 control-flow items against about 1 expected if nothing had changed) rules out chance sampling as an explanation of the differences as a whole. What the evidence cannot establish is the mechanism behind the swap — in particular, it cannot distinguish a change in how cases are routed from a different mix of cases entering the later window. [OCC004, OCC007, REL013, REL016, REL019, REL022, REL025, REL049, REL030, REL051, REL053, DIR002]

Other differences: A06's rise and repositioning stands beside the branch swap: A06 now runs in about half of the traces (earlier about 17 of 100) and typically sits between A34 and A35 — A34 leads to A06 in about half of its outgoing transitions and A06 leads immediately to A35 in about 90% of its outgoing transitions — whereas earlier A06 was rare, usually ran before A34, and led to varied successors; the evidence does not show whether this is independent of the branch swap. A02 and A08 also became rarer (about 17→8 and 14→5 of 100 traces). [OCC002, DIR001, DIR005, LAG001, LAG013, REL011, REL012, OCC001, OCC003]
