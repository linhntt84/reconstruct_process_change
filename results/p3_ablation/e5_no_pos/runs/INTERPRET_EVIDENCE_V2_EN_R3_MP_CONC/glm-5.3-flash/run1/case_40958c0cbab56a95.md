### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A44 and A29 changed from a mandatory block in which A26, A27 and A28 all executed in every case to a choice in which exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities — A19, A20 or A21 — executes (roughly 30, 33 and 38 in 100 cases), still after A44 and before A29. All 73 qualifying items come from the control-flow family, whose chance expectation is about 0.83 items, and every item's difference exceeds those seen in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits; the participation, co-occurrence, successor and positional items all describe this one replacement, and the A44-to-A29 distance shrinking by about two positions matches three activities being replaced by one. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL013, REL018, REL019, REL025, REL026, REL032, REL033, DIR007, LAG009]

Limitations: A26, A27 and A28 still appear in about 3 of 200 later traces, so complete removal versus a rare residual path cannot be distinguished. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that both behaviours pre-existed and the case mix shifted: A19, A20 and A21 did occur in 2–4 earlier traces, and their earlier outgoing profiles rest on only 2–4 observations each, so whether they are new activities or a previously rare variant that became dominant cannot be established. [OCC005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
