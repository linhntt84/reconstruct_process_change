- The block of A35, A36 and A41 reversed its order relative to A26: in the earlier window A26 came before each of the three in about 99–100 in 100 traces, while in the later window each of the three came before A26 in about 99 in 100. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the sequencing around the block A35–A36–A41 was reversed. In the earlier window the segment behaved as A25 → A27, then a stretch of activities the evidence does not identify, then A26 → A35/A36/A41 in varying order → A30; in the later window it behaved as A25 → A35/A36/A41 in varying order → A27, then a further unidentified stretch, then A26 → A30 directly. A25 now enters the trio instead of A27, the trio now hands over to A27, and A30 is reached from A26 instead of from the last trio member. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003]

The reversal is shown directly for all six activity pairs, the successor changes at A25, A26 and the trio agree with it, and the distance changes match; co-occurrence stayed at 100 in 100 with about one occurrence per trace, so participation, optionality and repetition changes are ruled out. The volume of qualifying evidence also exceeds what a stable process produces: 21 control-flow items qualified against about 0.59 expected by chance over 119 tested units (roughly 35 times the expectation), and every item's difference exceeded about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits; the items describe one coherent restructuring rather than unrelated shifts. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]

Limitations: no qualifying item covers the relation between A27 and A26 themselves, so their exact placement relative to each other and what runs between them is inferred rather than observed, and the evidence cannot show whether the trio moved forward or the A27/A26 segment moved backward — only that their order reversed. The change is established in event positions, so it demonstrates a reordering of execution, not a shift in clock timing. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG003]

Other differences: minor routing tails of about 1 in 200 transitions appear or disappear at the same activities — for example A25 later sometimes exits to A09, A35 later to A11, A25 or A28, and A36 and A41 show single-trace exits to A02, A10, A22, A23, A46 and A06, A08, A43, A44; these involve isolated traces and are not part of the main hypothesis.
