### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A10, previously a rare activity, was inserted between A14 and A15. Earlier, A14 led directly to A15 in about 86 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions and A10 appeared in about 19 in 100 traces; later, A14 leads to A10 in about 66 in 100, A10 leads to A15 in about 85 in 100, A10 immediately follows A14 in about 99 in 100 observed pairs, and A10's participation rose to about 70 in 100 traces, typically placed before the A20–A32 activities. The screening produced 69 qualifying control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected without change, so chance sampling cannot account for the set of differences, and successor, adjacency and within-trace ordering evidence all point to the same placement. [DIR003, DIR002, LAG002, LAG001, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL003]

Limitations: the evidence shows the new placement but not its scope beyond these windows, and the many A10 relation items partly describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a reweighting of whole process variants (a variant containing A10 becoming dominant) rather than a uniform insertion; the within-trace ordering data make a pure sampling fluctuation unlikely but cannot exclude variant mixing. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]

Other differences: the strict sequence among A20, A22, A23, A24, A25, A26, A31 and A32 became mixed — A24 now precedes A20 and A23 in most traces, several pairs appear in both orders in similar shares, and inter-event distances that were fixed now range over 1–5; this is a change in ordering constraints shown directly by the order reversals, not a path-frequency shift. [REL015, REL017, REL018, REL020, REL021, REL022, LAG004, LAG010, LAG016] Separately, A04 and A08 participation dropped sharply (A08's routing change rests on very few observations), and A45→A46 and A47→A48 became near-exclusive.
