### Observed differences

- Three activities that appear nowhere in the earlier window occur regularly in the later one: A19 in about 37 in 100 traces (74 of 200), A25 in about 34 in 100 (68 of 200) and A20 in about 29 in 100 (57 of 200). Each occurs at most once per trace, and the three almost never occur together (any pair co-occurs in at most about 1 in 200 traces), so roughly 99 in 100 later traces contain exactly one of them. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL007]
- The new activities sit between A41 and A26: A41 precedes A19, A20 and A25 in essentially every trace in which they appear (about 37, 28 and 33 in 100 traces respectively), each of the three precedes A26 in every trace where both occur, and about 96–98 in 100 of the new activities' outgoing transitions lead to A26. [REL006, REL010, REL014, REL003, REL008, REL011, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- A41's routing changed accordingly: in the earlier window about 93 in 100 of its outgoing transitions went directly to A26; in the later window about 35 in 100 go to A19, about 31 to A25 and about 27 to A20, while only about 1 in 100 still go directly to A26. [DIR004]
- The distance from A41 to A26 moved from immediate to one step removed: about 94 in 100 A41–A26 pairs were one position apart in the earlier window, whereas in the later window about 90 in 100 are two positions apart and about 9 in 100 are three. [LAG002]
- Each of the three new activities also precedes A31 in every trace in which both occur. [REL004, REL009, REL012]
- Smaller shifts: the gap from A31 to A35 moved from mostly one position (about 55 in 100) to mostly two (about 62 in 100); A35 now appears in about 48 in 100 traces, down from about 54, and is preceded by A19 or A25 in about 20 and 15 in 100 traces respectively. [LAG001, REL005, REL013]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new step was inserted between A41 and A26: in the later window almost every trace runs exactly one of three alternative activities — A19 (about 37 in 100 traces), A25 (about 34) or A20 (about 29) — after A41 and before A26 (and before A31), whereas in the earlier window A41 led directly to A26 in about 93 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and none of the three activities was observed. The signs are direct and consistent: participation rose from zero to 57–74 of 200 traces for each activity, A41's outgoing flow moved from A26 to the three new activities, the relational items place A41 before and A26 after each of them, and the A41-to-A26 distance shifted from one position to two. Screening produced 23 qualifying items where fewer than one would be expected without a change, so the item set as a whole is far beyond what a stable process produces; note that the many relational items largely restate this single insertion from different activity pairs rather than adding independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL003, REL006, REL008, REL010, REL011, REL014, LAG002]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that A19, A20 and A25 were not observed there, so the competing reading that the activities existed before but were missed by the 200-trace sample — implausible at these rates — cannot be fully excluded; and the evidence cannot show whether the split among the three alternatives (about 37/34/29) is itself stable, since it is observed in only one window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: the distance between A31 and A35 shifted from mostly one position to mostly two, A35's participation fell slightly (about 54 to 48 in 100 traces), and A35 is now often preceded by A19 or A25 — downstream shifts that this evidence does not further explain. [LAG001, REL005, REL013]
