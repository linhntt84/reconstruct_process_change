### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the mandatory block of A26, A27 and A28 — which executed together in every trace between A44 and A29 — has been replaced by a single execution of one of three mutually exclusive activities, A19, A20 or A21, each selected in roughly a third of traces, so that the path now runs A44, then one of these three, then A29. The change is shown directly by the participation flip (0 to about a third, and 100% to nearly 0), by the successor redistribution at A44 and the near-exclusive edges from A19/A20/A21 to A29, by the order relations (A44 before the chosen activity before A29 in about 30–37 of 100 traces), and by the A44-to-A29 distance shrinking from about four to about two positions. Screening also supports that this is not a sampling artefact: 62 control-flow items qualified where fewer than 1 was expected without a change, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether A26–A28 were removed outright or only became very rare (1–2 of 200 later traces still contain them), nor fully exclude that A19–A21 existed earlier as variants too rare to appear in 200 traces; one competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the old block merely became optional while the new activities form an additional path, though near-total replacement is the more direct reading.
