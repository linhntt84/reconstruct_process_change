### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 each ran in about 47 in 100 traces (94–95 of 200), roughly once when present; in the later window they run in essentially every trace (199 of 200), about once per trace [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].
- The routing out of A41 changed: earlier, about 52 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions went directly to A26 and about 44 in 100 to A19; in the later window, about 92 in 100 go to A19 and under 1 in 100 to A26 [DIR002].
- Pairs among A19, A20, A25 and A26 flipped from partly exclusive to always together: earlier each pair co-occurred in about 47–48 in 100 traces (with A26 appearing alone in about 53 in 100 and "neither" activity in about 52–53 in 100 for the A19/A20/A25 pairs); in the later window each pair co-occurs in about 995 in 1000 traces [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL007, REL008, REL012].
- A fixed order now holds along the chain: A41 before A19 in about 97 in 100 traces, A19 before A25 in 98 in 100, A25 before A20 in essentially all traces, and A20 before A26 in 99 in 100; earlier these orders held only within the roughly 47 in 100 traces that contained the block [REL002, REL004, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL013].
- The distance from A41 to A26 shifted: earlier A26 followed A41 after one step in about 52 in 100 cases and after four steps in about 42 in 100; in the later window one step occurs in under 1 in 100 cases, while four steps account for about 89 in 100 and five steps for about 11 in 100 — consistent with the three activities now sitting between them [LAG001].
- A20's own successors are effectively unchanged — almost always A26 (about 96 in 100 earlier, 99 in 100 later) — so this qualifying item reflects a negligible difference [DIR001].
- A42 ran in essentially every trace in both windows, but its relation to A19 changed: earlier the two co-occurred in about 47 in 100 traces, in the later window in about 995 in 1000, with A19 first in about 53 in 100 and interleaved-or-repeated in about 46 in 100 (earlier about 30 and 17 in 100) [REL005, REL010, REL014].
- A45 kept a similar overall frequency (about 60 in 100 traces earlier, 62 in 100 later), but earlier it appeared without A19 in about 33 in 100 traces, whereas in the later window it never appears without A19 (co-occurrence about 62 in 100; A19 without A45 about 37 in 100) [REL006, REL011, REL015].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the sequence A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 → A26 is executed in essentially every case, whereas earlier the sub-sequence A19 → A25 → A20 was optional: about 47 in 100 cases included it as a block between A41 and A26, and the remaining cases went directly from A41 to A26. In the later window A19, A25 and A20 became mandatory, the direct A41→A26 connection became rare (under 1 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions), and A26 now follows A41 by four to five steps instead of immediately in about half of the cases [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, REL002, REL004, REL007, REL008, LAG001].

The pattern is direct and consistent: participation, pairwise-relation, routing and distance indicators all describe the same picture, the 21 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.6 expected by chance among the 122 tested units, and every item's difference exceeds all 200 random re-splits, so chance sampling or a rare variant does not plausibly explain the pattern as a whole [DIR002, LAG001, OCC001, REL003].

A limitation: the evidence cannot show whether the direct A41→A26 connection was removed altogether or only became very rare, and it cannot distinguish a single structural modification from a shift in the mix of pre-existing process variants between the two windows [DIR002, LAG001].

Other differences: A42 was already near-universal in both windows but now co-occurs with A19 in almost every trace, with somewhat more interleaving than before; A45 kept its overall frequency but is now observed only together with A19, whereas earlier it often appeared without A19 [REL005, REL006, REL010, REL011, REL014, REL015].
