- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 were not observed in any of the 200 traces; in the later window A20 runs in about 41 in 100 traces, A25 in about 31 in 100 and A19 in about 29 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that three activities — A19, A20 and A25 — that were not executed in the earlier window run in the later window as a mutually exclusive three-way alternative (exactly one per trace), inserted into the sequence between A27/A28 and A29: each follows A27, sits before or after A28 depending on the trace, and precedes A29, which lengthens the A27→A28, A27→A29 and A28→A29 step distances by about one step. This is shown directly and consistently: participation rises from zero to about 29–41 in 100 traces, the three almost never co-occur, A27's and A28's outgoing transitions are redirected to them, the order relations place them after A27 and before A29, and the lag distributions shift by roughly one step. With 23 qualifying items against roughly 0.53 expected without change, this pattern is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL006, REL007, REL009, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window only shows that the activities were not executed then, so the evidence cannot distinguish genuinely new activities from existing ones that were never triggered before; the insertion point also varies (between A27 and A28 in about half of the later traces, between A28 and A29 in about 40 in 100), so the evidence locates the new block in this region rather than at one fixed position. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL004, REL008, REL011, LAG001, LAG003]

Other differences: the later window shows scattered rare outgoing edges from A27 and A28 (about 0.5 in 100 each, e.g. from A27 to A06, A08, A10, A12, A14, A17, A37, A42 and from A28 to A10, A22, A38) and rare edges from the new activities (A20→A18; A25→A27, A32, A44), each too small to characterise. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
