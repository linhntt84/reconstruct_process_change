### Observed differences

- The execution order between two groups of activities reversed. In the earlier window, A26 preceded A35, A36 and A41 in about 98–99 in 100 traces, and A27 preceded them in about 99–100 in 100; in the later window, A35, A36 and A41 preceded A26 in about 97–98 in 100 traces and preceded A27 in about 97 in 100. All six activities still occurred together in every trace in both windows, so participation itself did not change. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- A25's successor changed completely: earlier, about 98 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions went to A27; later, they split almost evenly among A36 (about 31 in 100), A41 (about 33) and A35 (about 32), with A27 below 1 in 100. [DIR001]
- A26's successor changed in the opposite direction: earlier its transitions spread across A41 (about 38 in 100), A35 (about 33) and A36 (about 28); later, about 98 in 100 went to A30. [DIR002]
- A30 dropped out of the successors of the A35/A36/A41 group: earlier, each of the three led to A30 in roughly 28–38 in 100 of its transitions; later, A30 is below 1 in 100 for all three, replaced by A27 at about 31–33 in 100. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of an ordering change in which the group A35/A36/A41 changed sides relative to A26/A27 and A30 was reattached. In the earlier window the sequence ran with A25 leading directly into A27, while A26 led into A35/A36/A41 (executed in varying internal order) and the group was followed by A30; in the later window, A25 leads into A35/A36/A41, the group precedes both A27 and A26, A27 now follows the group, and A26 is followed immediately by A30. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003, LAG007]

The reversal is shown directly by relational evidence with near-total shares on opposite sides in the two windows (about 98–99 in 100 versus about 97–98 in 100) while co-occurrence stayed at 100 in 100; the directional and positional items agree with the same rearrangement, and 21 items qualified in screening against roughly 0.6 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is not what a stable process produces. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG003]

Limitations: the evidence establishes that the order between the two groups reversed but cannot tell whether A35/A36/A41 moved before A26/A27 or A26/A27 moved after the group — both readings fit; the relative order of A25, A26 and A27 among themselves is not covered by any item. The lag indicator uses event positions rather than clock time, so no timing claim is implied, and several items describe the same traces, so they corroborate rather than independently confirm the change. [REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG003]

Other differences: a slight rise in interleaved or repeated execution of A27 together with A35, A36 and A41, from roughly 1 in 100 traces or fewer to roughly 2 in 100. [REL004, REL005, REL006]
