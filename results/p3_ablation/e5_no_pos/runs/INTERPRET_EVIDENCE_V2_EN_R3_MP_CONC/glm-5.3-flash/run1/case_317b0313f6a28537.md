### Observed differences

- The successors of A19, A20 and A25 changed almost completely. Earlier, each split its outgoing transitions roughly half to A28 and half to A32 (A19 about 47/48 in 100, A20 about 56/43, A25 about 50/45); in the later window each leads almost exclusively to A26 (about 92, 94 and 88 in 100 transitions), and A28 and A32 no longer appear among their observed successors. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- A27 and A28 became a direct chain on to A32. Earlier, A27 sent about 47 in 100 of its transitions to A28 and about half back to A19/A20/A25, and A28 continued to A32 in only about half of its transitions; in the later window A27 leads to A28 in about 98 in 100 transitions and A28 leads to A32 in about 96 in 100. [DIR004, DIR005]
- A45 changed its entry point: earlier it led to A26 in about 85 in 100 transitions; in the later window it leads to A19, A20 or A25 (about 29 in 100 each) and A26 no longer appears as a successor. [DIR006]
- The order between the two groups reversed. Earlier, A19/A20/A25 co-occurred with A26 or A27 in about 31–39 of 100 traces, and A26/A27 came first in nearly all of those; in the later window co-occurrence stayed at the same level (about 32–36 of 100), but A19/A20/A25 came first in nearly all of those, with the earlier order almost gone. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Positional distances moved accordingly: A27 to A28 became consecutive (gap 1 in about 98 of 100, versus an even split of gaps 1–2 earlier), A27 to A32 shortened from typically 3 positions to 2, A28 to A32 became consecutive (96 of 100 versus about half), and A45 to A26 stopped being consecutive (typically 2 positions apart, versus 86 of 100 adjacent earlier). [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]
- A15 still occurs in every trace but repeats less often: mean occurrences per trace fell from about 1.9 to about 1.6, and recurrence within a trace from about 47 to 38 of 100 traces. [OCC001]
- Three further lag items (A19–A32, A25–A28, A25–A32) show large gap shifts in the same direction, but each rests on only 1–3 after-window observations and is weak on its own. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a rerouting of the flow around A45, A19, A20, A25, A26, A27, A28 and A32 as the main explanation of the differences: earlier, A45 led into A26, and A26 and A27 both ran before A19/A20/A25, which each continued to A28 or A32 (with A28 often returning to A19/A20/A25 before reaching A32); in the later window, A45 feeds into A19/A20/A25, which lead almost exclusively to A26, while A27 leads directly to A28 and A28 directly to A32 — A19/A20/A25 now run before A26/A27 instead of after them. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

Supporting signs: the order reversal is relational evidence observed directly in six activity pairs at unchanged co-occurrence levels, so it reflects an ordering change rather than a participation change; the successor redistributions are near-total at five source activities, each backed by 62–204 transitions; and 20 control-flow items qualified where about 0.56 would be expected if nothing had changed, with every item's difference exceeding about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. These items partly describe the same rearrangement and are not independent confirmations. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

Limitations: no outgoing profile exists for A26 or A32, so what follows A26 in the later window is not directly observed, and three lag items rest on only 1–3 observations. An important competing explanation is that both routing behaviours existed all along and only their frequencies shifted between the windows; the near-absence of the new edges earlier (for example, A19 to A26 was not observed among 79 transitions) makes sampling noise unlikely, but a strong shift in the mix of process variants cannot be distinguished from a structural modification with this evidence. [DIR001, DIR006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Other differences: A15 shows a repetition change — it still occurs in every trace, but its mean occurrences per trace fell from about 1.9 to about 1.6 and its recurrence within traces from about 47 to 38 of 100 — which is not part of the ordering hypothesis above. [OCC001]
