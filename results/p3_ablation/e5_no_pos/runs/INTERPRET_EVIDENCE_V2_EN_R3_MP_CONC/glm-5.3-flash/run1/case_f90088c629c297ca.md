### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a three-way exclusive choice between A14, A15 and A20 was inserted between A36 and A21. In the earlier window, traces passed directly from A36 to A21; in the later window, about 99 in 100 traces pass through exactly one of A14 (about 37 in 100), A20 (about 34 in 100) or A15 (about 29 in 100) between them, with the direct A36→A21 succession surviving in only about 1 in 100 traces. [DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

The screening context supports this reading: 17 items qualified against an expectation of 0.34 items by chance for the 69 tested control-flow units, roughly fifty times the chance expectation, so the set of items is not what an unchanged process typically produces. The hypothesis is also shown directly rather than inferred from marginal frequencies: the redirection of A36's outgoing transitions, the successor relation from each inserted activity to A21, the pairwise ordering A36 before the inserted activity before A21, and the one-to-two position shift of A21 after A36 all point to the same insertion. [DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out: zero participation in the earlier window means only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed then, so a pre-existing but previously unexercised path that later came into use would look identical to a newly added fragment. The evidence also cannot show whether the choice among the three activities is free or driven by case characteristics. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
