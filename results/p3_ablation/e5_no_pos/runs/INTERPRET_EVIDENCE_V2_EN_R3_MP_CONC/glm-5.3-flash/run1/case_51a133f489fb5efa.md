### Observed differences

- Activity A08 became about twice as common: it appeared in about 13 in 100 traces in the earlier window and about 30 in 100 in the later window (26 vs 59 traces), and its outgoing transitions, previously spread thinly over many targets (A16, A26, A15 and A49 at about 14% each), concentrated in the later window on A19 (about 38%), A20 (about 29%) and A25 (about 21%). [OCC001, DIR001]
- A19, A20 and A25 changed successors almost completely: earlier each led mainly to A28 and A32 (about 40–56% each); in the later window each led to A26 in about 96–100 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

- The execution order between the group A19/A20/A25 and A26/A27 reversed: earlier, in traces containing both, A26/A27 came first in about 30–36 in 100 cases and the group first in at most about 0.5 in 100; in the later window the group came first in about 29–34 in 100 and A26/A27 first in about 0–1 in 100. [REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]
- A08's relation to A26, A27, A28 and A32 strengthened and settled into a fixed direction: co-occurrence rose from about 13 to about 30 in 100 traces, A08-first rose from about 6 to about 27 in 100, and the reverse order fell from about 6 to about 2–3 in 100. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening tested 134 control-flow units and 34 items qualified, whereas about 0.67 would qualify by chance in an unchanged process; a volume this much larger than the chance expectation cannot be explained by random re-splits of a stable process, even though the items describe overlapping behaviour [OCC001, DIR002, REL005, LAG010].

The evidence supports the possibility that the sequence around the activities A19, A20 and A25 was reordered. In the earlier window these three activities typically ran after A26/A27 and led on to A28 and A32; in the later window they run before A26, are entered from A08 — whose participation roughly doubled — and from A45, and they no longer hand over to A28/A32, leaving A27, A28 and A32 connected as a direct chain. This rests on direct relational evidence: the within-trace order with A26/A27 reversed, the outgoing transitions of A08, A19, A20, A25, A27, A28 and A45 were almost completely redistributed, and the new adjacencies (A19→A26, A27→A28, A28→A32) replaced the old ones (A25→A32, A28→A25, A45→A26) [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL005, REL007, REL009, LAG007, LAG010, LAG011, LAG013, LAG014].

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the later window samples a different mix of execution variants that already existed earlier rather than a rewiring of a single behaviour; in addition, A08's earlier outgoing profile rests on only 28 observed transitions, and a few adjacency items have only one or two observations on one side, so those exact step distances are indicative only, while the order reversals themselves are based on all 200 traces per window [DIR001, LAG001, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG012].

Other differences: in traces containing A16 or A18, A26 now occurs one to two positions later than before (A16→A26 from lag 3 to lags 4–5; A18→A26 from lag 2 to lags 3–4), and A08→A26 is now typically two positions apart, consistent with the group now sitting between the entry points and A26 [LAG002, LAG004, LAG005].
