### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a reversal of execution order between two groups of activities. In the earlier window, A19, A21 and A24 (each present in roughly a third of traces) ran before A25/A29 and before A20, A22 and A23: A25 preceded A20 in about 98 in 100 traces, A29 preceded A22 in about 97 in 100, and A19 preceded A20 in about 95 in 100 of the traces containing both. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two groups of activities exchanged their place in the sequence: A19, A21 and A24, which earlier ran before A25/A29 and before A20/A22/A23 and led to A25, now run after A25/A29 and lead directly to A30, while A20, A22 and A23 moved into the earlier position and now lead to A25 instead of A30; A25, A29 and A30 kept their positions between and after the groups, and A40 still precedes whichever group runs first. This rests on direct relational evidence — near-complete order reversals across roughly twenty activity pairs with stable co-occurrence, matching successor replacements, and position-gap shifts in the same direction — and the volume of qualifying items (85 control-flow items against roughly 1 expected without a change) exceeds what random re-splits produce. Limitations: the internal arrangement within each group (for example, whether A19, A21 and A24 run one after another or as alternatives) is not directly observed, and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows each captured a different long-lasting variant of the process rather than a single reordering, since each window shows only one near-deterministic order. [REL016, REL023, REL031, DIR006, DIR007, DIR011, DIR012, LAG018, LAG026, LAG029]

Other differences: A08's participation dropped sharply (about 64 to 29 in 100 traces) with weakened links to A14/A15/A16 and rerouting away from A16; A04, A06 and A10 became more frequent with changed successors; a few small new side edges appeared at A14, A43 and A45. These participation and routing changes are not part of the order-reversal hypothesis. [OCC003, OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, DIR002, DIR005, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004]
