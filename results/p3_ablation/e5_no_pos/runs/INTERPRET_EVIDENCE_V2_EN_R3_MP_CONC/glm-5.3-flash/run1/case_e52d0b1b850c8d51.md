- Before the change point, A19, A20 and A25 were each executed exactly once in every trace, A06 appeared in about 71 in 100 traces, and A14, A15, A41, A26, A46 and A47 appeared in essentially all traces; the dominant flow ran A14 → A06 → A15, then A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 → A26, closing with A46 → A47. - In the later window, A19, A20 and A25 each appear in only about 53–54 in 100 traces (about 0.53 occurrences per trace, down from 1.0), and they still appear together or not at all: pairs within the trio co-occur in about 53 in 100 traces, and in about 46 in 100 neither member appears. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL016, REL017, REL022]

The positional distance from A41 to A26 shrank accordingly: four events apart in about 91 of 100 observed pairs before, adjacent in about 40 of 100 pairs after. [DIR008, LAG008]
- The detour through A06 after A14 largely disappeared: A14's outgoing transitions went to A06 in about 69 in 100 cases before versus about 12 in 100 after, and to A15 in about 31 versus about 78; A06 itself fell from about 71 to about 26 in 100 traces, and its co-occurrence with A14 and with A15 dropped from 71 to 26 in 100. Adjacent A14→A06 pairs fell from about 140 observed to about 32. [DIR005, LAG003, OCC002, REL001, REL002]
- Consistent with both bypasses, the A14-to-A41 position gap moved one event closer (five apart in about 71 of 100 pairs before, four apart in about 67 after), A15 reached A26 within five steps in 75 later-window pairs against a single pair before, and A06's outgoing transitions, previously about 92 in 100 to A15, are now spread over many successors with A15 still the most frequent at about 37 in 100. [DIR003, LAG005, LAG007]
- Separately, participation rose for A08 (about 4 to 20 in 100 traces), A10 (about 5 to 16) and A04 (about 5 to 15), and the successor profiles of A02, A04 and A08 were broadly redistributed, though on few observed transitions (roughly 8–43 per window); A46→A47 and A44→A45 remained dominant but slightly less exclusive. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR009, DIR010, LAG009, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, two segments that previously ran in (almost) every trace became skippable. First, the segment A19 → A25 → A20 between A41 and A26 is bypassed in about 46–47 in 100 traces: the trio's participation fell from every trace to about 53 in 100, A41 now leads directly to A26 in about 39 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (essentially unobserved before), and the A41-to-A26 position gap collapsed from four events to adjacent in about 40 of 100 pairs, while A41, A26, A46 and A47 themselves still occur in all traces. Second, the detour via A06 between A14 and A15 is mostly skipped: A14 goes directly to A15 in about 78 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (about 31 before), and A06 appears in only about 26 in 100 traces (about 71 before). [DIR005, DIR008, LAG005, LAG008, OCC002, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL016, REL018, REL019, REL022]

The screening context supports this reading: 56 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.83 expected if nothing had changed, far beyond what random re-splits of stable behaviour produce. The successor change at A41 together with the collapsed position gap shows the bypass directly rather than as a mere shift in path shares, and participation, routing, relation and gap indicators all point the same way — though many of these items view the same two underlying differences from different pairs and are not independent confirmations. [DIR008, LAG008, OCC005, REL016]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that both skipping behaviours already existed as rare variants before the change point and only their frequencies rose; the two windows are finite samples, so the evidence establishes the new shares, not whether the skipped paths are new. [REL010, REL016]

Other differences: participation in A08, A10 and A04 rose (about 4→20, 5→16 and 5→15 in 100 traces) and the successor profiles of A02, A04 and A08 were redistributed on small supports; A46→A47 and A44→A45 became slightly less exclusive. These are not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR009, DIR010, LAG009, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]
