### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the trio A31, A32, A37 was repositioned and made an alternative to A22. Earlier, the three always ran together as a block between A20 and A23, before A21, and A22 always followed A21; in the later window a case runs, after A21, either A22 (about 54 in 100 cases) or the trio A31→A32→A37 in mixed internal order (about 47 in 100), with both branches leading on to A26, and A20 connects directly to A23.
