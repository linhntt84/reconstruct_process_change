### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window the path between A22 and A24 contains one additional step: exactly one of the previously unobserved activities A14, A15 or A20 executes once in nearly every case, placed after A22 and before A24, either directly before A23 (about 56 in 100 later cases) or directly after A23 (about 44 in 100), while the direct A22→A23→A24 sequence continues in the remaining cases; the three activities behave as mutually exclusive alternatives. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL006, LAG002]

This hypothesis is supported directly and consistently: the new activities appear with substantial support (58–82 of 200 cases) after zero participation, a shift too large to be a rare-variant sampling artefact; A22 and A23 gained outgoing transitions to them; relational evidence shows they are always preceded by A22 and followed by A24, with no fixed order against A23; and the A22-to-A24 step distance grows from two to three in virtually every later case, localising the extra step to one of the two gaps. The 23 flagged items far exceed the roughly 0.37 expected for an unchanged process, though many items describe the same underlying behaviour rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the three codes are genuinely distinct alternatives or variants of a single new step, nor whether related behaviour elsewhere in the traces (outside the tested units) also changed; the split between the two positions rests on event-position distances, not clock time, and the exact shares are subject to variation between the two finite windows. [REL001, REL002, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
