### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the execution order of two blocks of activities is reversed: earlier the flow ran from A40 through A20, A22 and A23, then A25 and A29, then A19, A21 and A24 into A30, whereas in the later window A40 leads through A19, A21 and A24, then A25 and A29, then A20, A22 and A23 into A30. The change is shown directly by 21 activity-pair relations that all flip in the same direction with similar magnitudes, by successor profiles of A19/A21/A24 and A20/A22/A23 that move in the mirrored direction, and by consistent position shifts; every activity involved appears in a substantial share of traces in both windows, so this is a reordering of existing behaviour rather than activities appearing or disappearing. Screening produced 78 qualifying items where fewer than 1 would be expected without a change, so sampling noise cannot account for the pattern as a whole, but the many agreeing items describe one underlying reordering rather than independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a changed mix of complete process variants, in which one ordering replaced the other, rather than re-sequencing within the same cases. [REL015, REL020, REL022, REL024, REL029, REL032, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR010, DIR012, LAG017, LAG021]
