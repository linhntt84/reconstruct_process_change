### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the group A27, A28 and A33, previously executed as a block between A21 and A23 in roughly half of all cases, is skipped in almost every later case, so that nearly all cases now run the A22 path A21 → A22 → A23; A22 became near-universal while the three group activities became near-absent, and the mutual exclusivity of the two branches itself is unchanged. The signs are direct and consistent: participation shifted from about 97 to 1 of 200 traces for each group member and from about 103 to 199 for A22; A21's outgoing transitions moved from a roughly even split to essentially only A22; the group no longer co-occurs with A21, with A23 or within itself; and the A21→A23 position gap collapsed from an even split between two and four steps to almost always two.
