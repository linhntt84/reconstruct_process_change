- In the earlier window the sequence A26 → A27 → A28 ran in virtually every case: A27 was present in 100% of traces (once each), A26 led to A27 in about 99% of its outgoing transitions, A27 led to A28 in about 99%, and A28 sat two positions after A26 in about 98% of traces. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in the segment following A26: in the later window, cases divide after A26 into two exclusive branches — about half still run A26 → A27 → A28 as before, while about half run A26 → A32/A33/A38 → A28, where the three new activities each execute once in varying order before converging on A28. Supporting signs: the routing split at A26, the near-perfect mutual exclusivity between A27 and each of A32, A33 and A38, the consistent placement of the new activities between A26 and A28 across the occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators, and a screening yield of 24 qualifying items against roughly 0.54 expected if nothing had changed. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, LAG001]

A limitation: zero participation of A32, A33 and A38 in the earlier window means only that they were not observed there; the evidence cannot rule out that this branch existed before at a very low rate (well under about 2% of cases) and only became frequent in the later window, nor can two windows show whether the roughly even split is stable over time. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Other differences: A27's outgoing behaviour is otherwise nearly unchanged (about 96% still leads to A28), with small new shares to A09 (about 2%) and to A34 and A25 (about 1% each), and A26 gains a small transition to A23 (about 0.5%). [DIR002, DIR001]
