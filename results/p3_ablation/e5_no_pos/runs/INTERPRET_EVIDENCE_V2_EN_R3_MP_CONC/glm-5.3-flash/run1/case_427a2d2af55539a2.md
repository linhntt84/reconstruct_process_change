### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured. In the earlier window, A39 was followed by exactly one of A14, A15 or A16 — mutually exclusive alternatives, each running once and directly before A24. In the later window, A14, A15 and A16 are no longer observed, and instead A21, A22 and A23 each execute exactly once in every case, in no fixed order relative to each other, with A24 following after all three; the A39-to-A24 distance consequently grew from 2 to 4 steps. This is a replacement of one path structure by another (activities removed and others made mandatory), not a shift of shares between paths that persist in both windows.

The support is direct and consistent: occurrence counts go from 70/59/71 of 200 cases to 0, and from 0 to 200 for the new activities; the relational evidence flips from mutual exclusivity to universal co-occurrence with stable surrounding order (A39 first, A24 last); and the lag change matches the size of the inserted block. The number of qualifying items (41 versus about 0.46 expected without change) cannot be explained by random re-splitting.

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A14, A15 and A16 were not exercised in the later sample of 200 cases — the evidence cannot rule out that they still occur in the process but were not triggered, or that the new block existed earlier but was unused. The apparent order-freedom among A21, A22 and A23 rests on the orders observed in this sample; rare orderings could be missed. The evidence covers control flow only, so no statement about timing or resources is possible.
