### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block A14 → A20 → A15, executed between A36 and A21, changed from optional to effectively mandatory: in the earlier window about 47 in 100 traces inserted the three activities in this fixed order between A36 and A21 while the rest went from A36 directly to A21, whereas in the later window essentially every trace runs A36 → A14 → A20 → A15 → A21. Participation of all three activities rose from about 47% to about 99.5% of traces [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003], the routing out of A36 moved from a roughly 53/47 split between A21 and A14 to almost exclusively A14 [DIR001], and the A36-to-A21 distance shifted from a one-step/four-step mix to almost always four steps [LAG001], while the internal order of the block and its position between A36 and A21 stayed the same [REL001, REL002, REL007, REL003, REL008, REL012].

Twenty control-flow items qualified, against fewer than one expected if nothing had changed, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole; the evidence cannot show, however, whether the direct A36 → A21 route still exists in the process but was simply not taken in the later sample, and because all items describe the same underlying trace behaviour they corroborate rather than independently confirm one another. [DIR001, LAG001, OCC001]
