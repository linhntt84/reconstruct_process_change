- In the earlier window, every trace contained A36 and A21, A36's only observed outgoing transition was to A21, and A21 followed A36 one position later in 100 in 100 cases. [DIR004, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a step is now executed between A36 and A21: in nearly every trace exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities — A14 (about 37 in 100 traces), A20 (about 34 in 100) or A15 (about 29 in 100) — runs after A36 and before A21, whereas earlier A36 was directly followed by A21 in all traces. [DIR004, REL003, REL004, LAG001]

This rests on direct and consistent signs: the occurrence items show the three activities only in the later window with substantial participation (57–74 of 200 traces each); the direction items show A36's outgoing flow redistributed from A21 to exactly these three activities and each of them leading only to A21; the relation items place each new activity after A36 and before A21 in every trace where it occurs; and the lag item shows the A36-to-A21 distance moving from one to two positions. The 17 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.34 expected by chance across the 69 tested units, so the set as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC001, DIR004, REL003, LAG001]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window only means A14, A15 and A20 were not observed there, so the evidence cannot show whether they are entirely new steps or were previously executed somewhere outside the sampled behaviour. All items describe one underlying difference, so they are not independent confirmations, and whether the choice among the three (about 37/34/29) follows a decision rule cannot be determined from this evidence. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is a rare variant that happened to be absent from the earlier 200-trace sample, although the consistency of the ordering and lag evidence makes a pure sampling artefact unlikely. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: none — every qualifying item concerns the same behaviour around A36, A21 and the three activities above. [DIR004, LAG001]
