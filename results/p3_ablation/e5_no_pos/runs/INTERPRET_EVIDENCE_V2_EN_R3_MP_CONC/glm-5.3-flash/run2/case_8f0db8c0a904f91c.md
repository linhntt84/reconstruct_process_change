### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the segment A19 → A25 → A20, which previously executed in every case between A41 and A26, became optional and is skipped in about 47 in 100 cases, with A41 leading directly to A26. The screening found 37 qualifying items where fewer than 1 would be expected without change, so the pattern as a whole is not a sampling artefact. The signs are direct and consistent: participation of all three activities dropped in lockstep from 100 to about 53 in 100 cases; routing out of A41 split between the old route into A19 and a new direct route to A26; the A41→A26 distance shortened accordingly; ordering inside the segment and the surrounding activities are unchanged, indicating a skip rather than a reordering or a replacement of paths. Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the direct A41→A26 route existed before but was too rare to observe, and it cannot rule out that the two windows sample different case mixes rather than a behavioural change [DIR005, LAG002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL010, REL014, REL019].

Other differences: A02 occurs far less often and is largely no longer reached from A46, with its own outgoing behaviour redistributed on weak support; A04 and A06 occur somewhat more often from low bases; A46→A47 became almost always adjacent [OCC001, DIR006, DIR001, OCC002, OCC003, LAG003].
