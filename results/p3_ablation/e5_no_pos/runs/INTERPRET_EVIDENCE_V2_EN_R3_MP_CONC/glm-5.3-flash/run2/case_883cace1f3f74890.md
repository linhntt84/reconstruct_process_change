### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the traces ran a fixed short chain: A27 was followed directly by A28 in about 99 in 100 of A27's outgoing transitions, A28 was followed directly by A29 in about 99 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, and the pairs A27–A28 and A28–A29 sat one step apart (A27–A29 two steps) in about 99 in 100 traces. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new sequential stage was added to the former chain A27 → A28 → A29, in which exactly one of three previously unobserved activities — A19, A20 or A25 — runs once per trace: in the later window the three together cover essentially every trace while never running together, whereas before none of them was observed. The stage's placement varies within the later window: in about 55 in 100 traces the new activity runs between A27 and A28 (A27 hands over to it and it leads to A28), and in about 44 in 100 it runs between A28 and A29 (A28 hands over to it and it leads to A29); this reading fits the split of A27's and A28's outgoing traffic, the mixed order against A28, and the uniform shift of A27–A29 from two to three steps apart. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL002, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL004, REL008, REL011, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The 24 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.5 expected by chance among 103 tested units, and the occurrence, direction, relation and lag items all describe the same difference directly, with the relational and lag items resting on all 200 traces in each window; a rare one-window variant therefore cannot explain the pattern. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL007, REL010, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

What the evidence cannot show is what decides which of the three activities runs in a given trace, and whether the varying placement reflects one flexible stage or two distinct insertion points; positions are event positions, so the change is established in sequence order rather than in clock time. [REL004, REL008, REL011, LAG001, LAG003]
