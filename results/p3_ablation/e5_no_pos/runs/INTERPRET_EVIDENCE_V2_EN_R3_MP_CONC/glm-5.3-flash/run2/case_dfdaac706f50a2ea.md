### Observed differences

- Four activities that previously ran in every case now run in only about half of the cases. In the earlier window, A27, A36, A37 and A42 each appeared in all 200 traces, once per trace; in the later window each appears in only about 47–54 in 100 traces (A27 about 54, A36 about 47, A37 and A42 about 48), still at most once per trace, with repetition negligible in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

- A25 no longer feeds the block of three activities. In the earlier window its outgoing transitions split almost evenly among A42 (about 35 in 100), A36 (about 34) and A37 (about 31); in the later window about 99 in 100 lead directly to A28, and the position gap from A25 to A28 shrank from 4 events in about 94 in 100 traces to 1 event in about 99 in 100. [DIR001, LAG001]

- The block now runs after A26 instead of between A25 and A28. Previously A36/A37/A42 ran before A26 (in about 98–99 in 100 traces) and before A28 (in about 99–100 in 100); in the later window A28 precedes the block and A26 precedes the block (each in about 46–47 in 100 traces, essentially every trace in which the block runs). A26's outgoing transitions changed accordingly: from about 98 in 100 leading to A27, to about 52 in 100 leading to A27 plus about 14–17 in 100 leading to each of A36, A37 and A42. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR002]

- The block's exit changed from A28 to A31, while its internal behaviour stayed the same. Before, each of A36, A37 and A42 led to A28 in about 32–33 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (otherwise to another block member); in the later window A28 no longer appears among their outgoing transitions and A31 receives about 28–36 in 100 from each. The three still run as a group: in the later window any two of them co-occur in about 47 in 100 traces and are jointly absent in about 52–53 in 100, versus joint presence in all traces before. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL020, REL021, REL022]

- A27 and the block became mutually exclusive alternatives. Before, A27 co-occurred with each of A36/A37/A42 in all 200 traces, after the block; in the later window co-occurrence is about 0–1 in 100 traces, with A27 alone in about 53 in 100 and the block alone in about 47 in 100. The gap from A26 to A31 changed accordingly: 2 events in about 96 in 100 traces before (A26–A27–A31), against 2 events in about 51 in 100 and 4 events in about 46 in 100 after (the A27 path versus the three-activity path). [REL011, REL012, REL013, LAG005]

- The surrounding skeleton is stable: A25, A26, A28 and A31 still occur in essentially every trace in both windows, and the orderings A28 before A27, A26 directly before A27 and A27 before A31 are preserved whenever both sides run. [REL005, REL009, REL010, DIR001, DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the routing between A25 and A31 was restructured, involving A25, A26, A27, A28, A31 and the block A36/A37/A42. In the earlier window every trace executed the block between A25 and A28 and then A27 between A26 and A31; in the later window A25 leads directly to A28 without the block, and after A26 the trace takes either A27 (about 52 in 100 traces) or the block A36/A37/A42 (about 47 in 100), which now exits to A31 — the block has become an optional alternative to A27 in a different part of the sequence, and A27 is not executed when the block runs. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor changes at A25 and A26, the order reversals between A26/A28 and the block, the retargeted exits to A31, the mutual exclusivity of A27 and the block, and the participation drops all describe one coherent structure. With 42 qualifying items against about 0.68 expected without a change, chance cannot account for the ensemble, and every item's difference exceeds those of about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [DIR001, DIR002, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Limitations: several items re-express the same underlying difference (for example DIR002 and REL006–REL008 both reflect the block's new position), so they are not independent confirmations; the gap evidence is in event positions, not clock time; and the evidence cannot show what determines the roughly even split between the two alternatives. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window mixes two case types, one of which existed before at a rate too low to appear in 200 traces — the block ran in all 200 earlier traces, so such a variant would have had to affect well under about 2 in 100 earlier traces. [DIR002, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Other differences: only marginal rerouting at the level of about 1–2 in 200 outgoing transitions. In the earlier window, rare edges led from A25 to A09/A10, from A26 to A04/A31/A06/A42/A47, from A36 to A20/A08/A29/A06/A26, from A37 to A04/A06 and from A42 to A39/A21/A10/A29/A19; in the later window, rare edges lead from A25 to A36/A12/A24, from A36 to A12, from A37 to A22/A33 and from A42 to A08. These concern single traces, are consistent with sampling variation, and are not interpreted as behavioural change. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
