### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every one of the 200 traces contained exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities: A14 in about 32 of 100 traces, A15 in about 28 of 100 and A20 in about 40 of 100; they never occurred together and never repeated within a trace. In the later window, none of the three appears in any of the 200 traces [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006].
- Earlier, the chosen activity was inserted either directly after A22 (A22's outgoing transitions went straight to A23 in about 55 of 100 cases and to A20, A14 or A15 in about 45 of 100) or directly before A24 (A23's outgoing transitions went straight to A24 in about 46 of 100 cases and to A20, A14 or A15 in about 54 of 100). Each of the three then led on to A23 or straight to A24 in roughly even shares [DIR004, DIR005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003].
- In the later window, A22 leads to A23 in all of its observed outgoing transitions and A23 leads to A24 in all of its observed outgoing transitions; A14, A15 and A20 show no observed outgoing transitions because they no longer occur [DIR004, DIR005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003].
- The gap the three activities used to fill has closed: A22 and A23 were adjacent in about 55 of 100 earlier traces and one position apart otherwise, and are adjacent in all later traces; A23 and A24 were adjacent in about 46 of 100 earlier traces and are now always adjacent; A22 and A24 moved from always three positions apart to always two [LAG001, LAG003, LAG002].
- Relations changed accordingly: earlier, A14, A15 and A20 each co-occurred with A22, A23 and A24 in every trace containing them (A22 always earlier, A24 always later, A23 sometimes before and sometimes after); later, A22, A23 and A24 occur in all traces while the three activities never do [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative step formed by A14, A15 and A20 is no longer executed. In the earlier window exactly one of the three ran in every case, placed either between A22 and A23 (about 45 of 100 traces) or between A23 and A24 (about 55 of 100 traces); in the later window all three have disappeared from the log and A22, A23 and A24 run back-to-back in every trace. Because the affected activities are wholly absent rather than reduced, and A22 now leads directly to A23, this is a case of a group of activities being bypassed or dropped, not a re-weighting between surviving paths [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003, LAG002].

The supporting signs are strong: 23 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.36 expected if nothing had changed, far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces; occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators all describe one coherent picture; the disappearance is unlikely to be a sampling artefact, since the three activities together appeared in about 200 of 200 earlier traces and in 0 of 200 later traces; and a mere relocation within the trace is ruled out because mean occurrences per trace also fell to zero [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR004, REL003, LAG002].

Limitations: the many agreeing items largely restate one underlying trace change rather than constituting independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot distinguish a complete removal of this step from an extreme drop in how often the branch containing it is chosen — a branch still taken very rarely, below what 200 traces can detect, would look identical here [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].
