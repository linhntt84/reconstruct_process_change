### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the six activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 each ran in every single case (participation 100 in 100, about one occurrence per case, never repeated). In the later window they each run in only about half of the cases: A33, A35 and A36 in about 47 in 100, A37, A38 and A39 in about 56–57 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- The six activities split into two trios that became mutually exclusive. Within a trio, the activities still always occur together when they occur at all (A33 with A35 jointly in 46.5 in 100 later cases and in no others; A37 with A38 in 55.5 in 100), whereas pairs across the trios, which always co-occurred earlier (e.g. A33 before A37 in 99 in 100 cases), now co-occur in only about 2–3 in 100 cases. Roughly 99 in 100 later cases contain exactly one of the two trios. [REL013, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL022, REL024, REL025, REL027]
- A30 and A31 still appear in every case, before and after the block respectively. A30's direct successors changed: earlier only A33, A35 and A36 followed it directly (about 28–36 in 100 of its outgoing transitions each), while in the later window A37 (about 15 in 100), A38 (about 17) and A39 (about 19) are also direct successors. [DIR001, REL001, REL004]
- The block now runs directly into A31: about 32 in 100 of A33's outgoing transitions, 34 of A35's and 26 of A36's go to A31, transitions not observed in the earlier window; distances shrink accordingly, with A30-to-A37 and A33-to-A31 moving from 4–5 steps apart to 1–3 steps apart. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the six-activity block between A30 and A31 became a choice between two alternative activity sets: in the later window a case typically executes either the trio A33–A35–A36 (about 47 in 100 cases) or the trio A37–A38–A39 (about 56 in 100), where earlier every case executed all six activities, first trio before second, before reaching A31. This is shown directly by the participation drop of all six activities while A30 and A31 remain universal, by cross-trio co-occurrence collapsing from 100 to about 3 in 100 while within-trio co-occurrence is fully preserved, and by the successor and lag changes consistent with the block shrinking from six activities to three. The volume of qualifying evidence (48 items against roughly 0.76 expected by chance for 152 tested units, all control-flow) cannot be explained by random re-splits of a stable process, and the items, though not independent, are mutually consistent. Limitations: the evidence cannot show what determines which trio a case takes, and the roughly 3 in 100 later cases containing both trios (and about 1 in 100 containing neither) cannot be classified as a residual old path or sampling noise; an important competing explanation that remains is a changed case mix entering this fragment (cases routed differently before A30) rather than a new decision inside it, since no case attributes are available. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL013, REL015, REL025, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG004]

Other differences: a few new low-frequency transitions appear in the later window — from A30 to A04, A08, A10, A20, A45 and A46, and from A33 and A35 to A01, A02, A05, A06 and A08, each about 0.5–1 in 100 of the source's outgoing transitions — and several lag items for cross-trio pairs rest on only 4–5 later observations; these fit the main change but are too weak to support anything on their own.
