### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A21, A22 and A23 each ran in all 200 traces in a fixed chain A21 → A22 → A23, with A23 exactly two event positions after A21; A27, A28 and A33 were not observed in any trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL005, LAG001]
- In the later window, A22 takes part in only about 51 in 100 traces (down from all 200), while A27, A28 and A33 each take part in about 49 in 100 traces (up from none); each of these activities occurs at most once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- In the later window, A21's outgoing flow is redistributed: about 51 in 100 of its outgoing transitions still lead to A22, with the rest leading to A28 (about 18 in 100), A33 (about 17 in 100) and A27 (about 15 in 100); before, all led to A22. [DIR001]
- In the later window, the three new activities run as one block: every pair among them co-occurs in about 49 in 100 traces, each pair appears in both orders in similar proportions (roughly 22–27 in 100 each way, i.e. no fixed order), and each has outgoing transitions to the other two and to A23 (shares roughly 27–44 in 100). [REL012, REL013, REL014, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- In the later window, A22 and the new block are strict alternatives: they never occur in the same trace — about 51 in 100 traces contain A22 only, about 49 in 100 contain the respective new activity only. [REL006, REL007, REL008]
- In the later window, the block sits between A21 and A23: A21 precedes each of A27, A28 and A33, and each of them precedes A23, in about 49 in 100 traces; A23 now runs without A22 in about 49 in 100 traces, whereas before A22 always preceded it. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL005]
- The positional gap from A21 to A23, always 2 before, is now 2 in about 51 in 100 cases and 4 in about 49 in 100, consistent with three activities inserted between them. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the segment between A21 and A23. In the earlier window, A22 was a mandatory step between them in every case. In the later window, about half the cases still run A21 → A22 → A23, while in the other half A22 does not run and a block of three activities — A27, A28 and A33, executed together without a fixed internal order — is inserted between A21 and A23. A21 and A23 themselves still run in every case in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL006, REL012, REL013, REL014, LAG001]

The hypothesis is shown directly by relational and directional evidence, not by frequencies alone: A21's outgoing transitions are observed to redistribute from A22 alone to A22 plus the three new activities, the new activities' outgoing edges and their co-occurrence in both orders place them between A21 and A23, and the gap change from always 2 to 2-or-4 matches three inserted activities. The volume of qualifying items (23 against roughly 0.37 expected without change) cannot be explained by random re-splits of an unchanged process. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL002, REL009, LAG001, screening]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window only means A27, A28 and A33 were not observed there, so an extremely rare pre-existing path that missed all 200 earlier traces cannot be ruled out. Position-based evidence also cannot distinguish a variable sequential order within the block from parallel execution of the three activities. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL012, REL013, REL014]
