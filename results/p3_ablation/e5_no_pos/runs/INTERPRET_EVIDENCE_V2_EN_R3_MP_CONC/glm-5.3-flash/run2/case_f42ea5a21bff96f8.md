### Observed differences

- The selection between two mutually exclusive three-activity blocks reversed. In the earlier window, A21, A22 and A23 each ran in about 75 in 100 traces and the three ran together in about 75 in 100, while A24, A25 and A26 each ran in only about 25–26 in 100 (together in about 25 in 100); the two blocks shared a trace in at most about 1–2 in 100 cases. In the later window the picture is mirrored: A24, A25 and A26 each run in about 75–77 in 100 traces and together in about 75 in 100, while A21, A22 and A23 each dropped to about 25 in 100 (still running together whenever they appear). [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL025, REL027, REL034, REL049, REL055]

In both windows the active block runs after A19/A20 and A29 (A19 and A20 precede it in about 73–75 in 100 co-occurring traces, A29 precedes it in about 73 in 100, with A19 directly after A29 and A20 two positions later) and before A34/A35 (the block precedes them in about 73–76 in 100), with A30 partly interleaved. [DIR002, REL013, REL016, REL019, REL022, REL030, REL031, REL032, REL051, REL052, REL053, LAG007, LAG008]

- A06 became both more frequent and more narrowly placed. It appeared in about 17 in 100 traces before and about 53 in 100 after. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two alternative branches exchanged their selection frequencies: before, roughly 3 of 4 cases ran the block A21–A22–A23 and roughly 1 of 4 ran A24–A26; in the later window, roughly 3 of 4 run A24–A26 and 1 of 4 run A21–A23. Both blocks keep the same members, remain mutually exclusive, and keep their position between A19/A20/A29 and A34/A35, so the evidence fits the same two existing paths being taken with reversed frequencies rather than one path replacing the other or activities being relocated. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, DIR002, REL025, REL027, REL049, REL055, REL013, REL016, REL030, REL032, REL051, REL053]

This goes well beyond chance: 90 of 207 tested units qualified against about 1 expected for an unchanged process, every item's difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits, and participation, routing and relational indicators all show the same reversal with substantial support in both windows (roughly 50–153 traces per activity per window). Limitations: the many relation items restate one underlying shift rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows draw from different case mixes or process variants rather than from one process whose branch selection changed. [OCC004, OCC007, DIR002, REL025, REL049]
