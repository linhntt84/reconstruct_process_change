### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A36 and A21 formed by the three alternative activities A14, A15 and A20 is no longer executed in the later window: A36, which previously passed through exactly one of the three before reaching A21, now leads directly to A21. The signs are direct and mutually consistent — participation of all three dropped from about 30–35 in 100 traces to zero out of 200, A36's outgoing transitions moved from an even split across the three to A21 alone, and the A36→A21 position gap shortened from two steps to one — while A21 and A26 continue to occur in all traces, so the surrounding behaviour is otherwise stable. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, LAG001, REL004, REL013]

With 23 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected by chance among the 76 tested units, sampling noise is unlikely to explain the pattern as a whole. The evidence cannot, however, distinguish whether the three activities were removed from the process altogether or whether their branch still exists but was not taken in any of the 200 later traces; zero participation only means the activities were not observed. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: A19 occurs somewhat less often in the later window (about 56 in 100 traces versus about two in three before); this modest shift could also be sampling variation and is not part of the main hypothesis. [REL002, REL007, REL012]
