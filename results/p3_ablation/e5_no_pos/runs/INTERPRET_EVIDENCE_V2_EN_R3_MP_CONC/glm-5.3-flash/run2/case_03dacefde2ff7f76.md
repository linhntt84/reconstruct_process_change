### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of activities A19, A20 and A25 changed its sequence position relative to the chain through A26, A27 and A28: in the earlier window the flow ran from A45 via one of the block activities into A26, with the block always preceding A26, A27 and A28, and the process continuing A27 → A28 → A32; in the later window A45 leads to A26, A26 and A27 run before the block, and the block is executed around A28 — inserted between A27 and A28 in about half of the traces and between A28 and A32 in the rest — with its activities now leading to A28 or A32 instead of A26. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008]

The hypothesis is supported directly and consistently: 22 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.59 expected by chance (about 37 times the expectation, with every item's difference exceeding about 199 of 200 random re-splits), the order reversal is shown directly by pairwise relations computed over all 200 traces per window, and the successor and gap changes agree with it; the stable co-occurrence shares (about 31–38 in 100 in both windows) rule out the competing reading that the block simply became rarer or optional. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine reversal of the two blocks from the block being removed from its old position and re-inserted after the chain, and the adjacency of A20 to A28 in both directions leaves open whether some traces contain the block on both sides of A28; the exact gap profiles for the A20–A28 and A20–A32 pairs rest on a single earlier-window observation. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007]
