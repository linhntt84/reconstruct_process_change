### Observed differences

- The relative order of two activity groups reversed. In the earlier window, A35, A36 and A41 co-occurred with A26 and A27 in all 200 traces and ran before them in about 97–99 in 100 traces; in the later window the co-occurrence is still total, but A26 and A27 now run first in about 97–98 in 100 traces. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015]
- Successors around this segment changed accordingly. Earlier, A25 led to A35, A36 or A41 (about 38%, 31% and 27% of its outgoing transitions) and A26 led almost exclusively to A30 (about 98%); in the later window A25 leads to A27 in about 96 in 100 transitions, A26 leads to A35, A36 and A41 (about 35%, 32% and 31%), and A35/A36/A41 now each lead to A30 in about 30–34% of their transitions where they earlier led to A27 in about 28–36%. [DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009]
- Adjacency shifted with the reorder: A25 and A27 were usually 4–5 positions apart earlier (about 92 in 100 at distance 4) and are immediate neighbours in the later window (about 98 in 100 at distance 1); A26 and A30 were immediate neighbours earlier (about 99 in 100) and are now usually 4–5 positions apart (about 91 in 100 at distance 4). [LAG002, LAG005]
- A02 largely disappeared from traces containing the other main activities: it appeared in about 75 in 100 traces earlier and about 21 in 100 later, and its co-occurrence with each of A25, A26, A27, A30, A35, A36, A41, A49 and A50 fell from about 75 to about 21 in 100, with about 79 in 100 of those traces now containing the other activity without A02. [OCC001, REL001–REL009]
- The route around A49 changed: earlier, about 72% of A49's outgoing transitions went to A02, whose own outgoing transitions went to A50 about 91% of the time; in the later window A49 goes directly to A50 in about 83% of transitions, usually immediately after (distance 1 in about 84 in 100), while A02's outgoing transitions are spread thinly over many targets, none above about 13%. [DIR001, DIR011, LAG009]
- A06 and A10 occur more often: A06 in about 19 in 100 traces (earlier about 7) and A10 in about 22 in 100 (earlier about 9); A06's outgoing transitions, earlier spread evenly over twelve targets (only 12 such transitions observed), now concentrate on A48 (about 17%), A50 (about 15%) and A16 (about 13%), and A47 and A49 now also lead to A06 or A10 in a small share. [OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, DIR010, DIR011]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening qualified 38 control-flow items where fewer than 1 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the differences as a set are far beyond what random re-splits of these windows produce. [OCC001, DIR005, REL010, LAG002]

The evidence supports the possibility that the order of the mid-process segment was reversed, involving A25, A26, A27, A30, A35, A36 and A41: in the earlier window the block A35/A36/A41 executed between A25 and A26/A27, with A30 immediately after A26, while in the later window A25 flows directly on to A27, A26 initiates the block A35/A36/A41, and A30 follows the block about 4–5 positions after A26. The reversal is shown directly by the order flip between A26/A27 and A35/A36/A41 in pairs that co-occur in every trace of both windows, and it is consistent with the successor changes at A25, A26, A35, A36 and A41 and with the gap shifts, each resting on about 200 traces per window. [REL010–REL015, DIR005–DIR009, LAG002, LAG005]

Limitations: the evidence shows event order and adjacency, not clock time, and the direction shares are computed over outgoing transitions rather than cases; a change in the mix of two pre-existing variants with opposite internal order cannot be fully excluded, although the near-absence of the new edges (A25→A27, A26→A35/A36/A41) earlier and of the old edges later argues against a pure reweighting of unchanged paths. [DIR005, DIR006]

Other differences: A02 was largely bypassed in the later window — A49 proceeds directly to A50 where it previously went via A02, and A02's participation fell from about 75 to about 21 in 100 traces; A06 and A10 appear roughly two to three times as often as before, though their support counts are modest (13–46 traces per window). These differences are well supported but concern activities outside the reordering hypothesis. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR011, LAG009, REL001–REL009]
