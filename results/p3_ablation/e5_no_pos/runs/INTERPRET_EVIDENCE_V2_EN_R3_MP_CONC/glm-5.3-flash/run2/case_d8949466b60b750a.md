Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch in which A32, A33 and A38 were executed between A26 and A28 is skipped in the later window: earlier, roughly half of the cases inserted this three-activity block, in varying order, between A26 and A28 while the other half went directly A26 → A27 → A28; in the later window essentially all cases take the direct route, with A27 in about 199 of 200 cases and A32, A33 and A38 in about 1 of 200 each.
