### Observed differences

- Participation dropped sharply for four activities. A19, A20 and A25 each ran in every trace before but in only about 53–54 in 100 traces in the later window, and A06 fell from about 71 to about 26 in 100 traces. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC002]
- A06 is now usually bypassed between A14 and A15. Before, about 69 in 100 of A14's outgoing transitions went to A06 and about 31 in 100 went straight to A15; in the later window about 78 in 100 go to A15 and only about 12 in 100 to A06, and A06's own transitions on to A15 fell from about 92 to about 37 in 100. A14 and A06 now share a trace in only 26 in 100 cases (A14 runs without A06 in 74 in 100, up from 29), and A14 reaches A15 within one event position in 41 in 100 cases versus 17 before. [DIR005, DIR003, REL001, REL002, LAG004]
- The stretch A19 → A25 → A20 between A41 and A26 became skippable. Before, A41 led to A19 in about 96 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (never directly to A26) and reached A26 four positions later in 91 in 100 cases; in the later window A41 leads to A19 in about 47 in 100 and directly to A26 in about 39 in 100, and A41 reaches A26 one position later in 40 in 100 cases (four positions later still 44 in 100). [DIR008, LAG008]
- The skipped activities disappear rather than move: A19 and A20, together in every trace before, are both absent in about 46 in 100 later traces, and A19 is absent in about 47 in 100 traces that still contain A14. When the group does run, its internal order is unchanged (A19 before A20 in about 52 in 100, A25 before A20 in about 53 in 100). A26, A41, A46 and A47 still occur in every trace, now without A19 in about 46–47 in 100 of them. [REL016, REL010, REL017, REL022, REL018, REL019, REL021]
- The rest of the chain is essentially stable: A15 still leads to A16 in about 93–98 in 100 outgoing transitions in both windows, A46 still leads to A47 within one position in about 87–97 in 100, and A44 still leads to A45 in about 86–96 in 100. [DIR006, LAG009, DIR010, DIR009]
- A few rarely seen activities occur more often in the later window: A08 in about 20 in 100 traces (from 4), A10 in about 16 (from 5) and A04 in about 15 (from 5). [OCC003, OCC004, OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, two segments of a previously near-mandatory chain are skipped in a large share of cases: A06, which sat between A14 and A15, is bypassed in about three quarters of the cases (A14 now leads directly to A15, and A06 appears in only about 26 in 100 traces, down from 71), and the sequence A19 → A25 → A20 between A41 and A26 is omitted in about 46 in 100 traces (A41 leads directly to A26 in about 4 in 10 of its outgoing transitions, and A19, A20 and A25 each appear in only about 53 in 100 traces, down from every trace). [OCC002, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR005, DIR008, LAG008, REL016]

The change is shown directly and consistently across indicator families: participation, successor profiles, co-occurrence/order relations and event-position distances all point to the same two localised changes, while neighbouring edges (A15→A16, A46→A47, A44→A45) stay stable. The screening produced 56 qualifying items where roughly 1 would be expected without a change, so chance cannot account for the ensemble. Two competing readings are ruled out: this is not a re-weighting of existing paths, because A19, A20 and A25 are entirely absent in about 46 in 100 later traces; and it is not a reordering, because the relative order of the activities that still run together is preserved. The main limitations are that many items describe the same traces (for example the numerous A19/A20/A25 pairs) and are therefore not independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot distinguish one process with newly optional segments from a later window that mixes two case variants. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL016, REL022, DIR008, LAG008, DIR006]

Other differences: A04, A08 and A10 occur roughly three to four times as often as before (about 15–20 in 100 traces, up from 4–5), but their outgoing transitions are spread over many successors and rest on only 8–43 observations per window, so their position in the flow remains unclear; the A15→A19 distance is sometimes five positions in the later window (about 30 in 100) versus almost always four before; and A46 reaches A47 within two positions slightly more often (about 12 in 100 versus 2). These differences are not explained by the main hypothesis. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, DIR002, DIR004, LAG006, LAG009]
