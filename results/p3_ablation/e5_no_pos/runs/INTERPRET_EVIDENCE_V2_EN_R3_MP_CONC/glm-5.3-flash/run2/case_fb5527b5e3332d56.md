### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the branch formed by A27, A28 and A33 between A21 and A23 is essentially no longer executed: cases that previously alternated between A21 → A22 → A23 and A21 → (A27/A28/A33 in varying order) → A23 now run A21 → A22 → A23 in nearly every trace, with the trio observed in only 1 of 200 traces. The participation collapse of the trio (about 97 of 200 traces down to 1) together with A22's rise to near-universal participation is direct evidence, and the relational items confirm it: the trio was a strict alternative to A22 before and is now jointly absent, A21's outgoing transitions consolidated on A22, and A23's position consolidated at two steps after A21. Thirty items qualified against roughly 0.42 expected for an unchanged process, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole — though all items describe this one underlying shift, not independent confirmations of separate changes [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001–REL014, DIR001, LAG001].

Limitations: the later profiles of A27, A28 and A33 rest on a single trace each, so the evidence cannot distinguish whether the branch was removed outright or only became very rare; and a later window dominated by one variant (for example a selected or shifted population) cannot be ruled out as an alternative to a behavioural change.

Other differences: none beyond this shift. The only residual detail is the trio's internal ordering, which was mixed in the earlier window and cannot be compared afterwards given the single observation [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008].
