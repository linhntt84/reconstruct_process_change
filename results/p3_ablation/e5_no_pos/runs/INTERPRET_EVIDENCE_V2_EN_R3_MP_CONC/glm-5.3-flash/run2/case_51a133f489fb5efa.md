### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the sequence position of the activity group A19, A20 and A25 changed. In the earlier window these activities ran after A26 (fed by A45) and after A27, and led on to A28 and A32; in the later window they run before A26 and lead directly into it, are fed by A08 and A45, while A27 reaches A32 via A28 without passing through the group. A08's participation roughly doubled (about 13 to about 30 in 100 traces) as part of this new upstream path. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL005, REL007, REL009, LAG007]

The change is shown directly by relational and successor evidence rather than by frequency shifts alone: the order between the group and A26 flipped with large component differences (about 0.30–0.35 on shares of 200 traces each window), successor profiles were almost completely replaced, and the new adjacencies appear in the lag data with solid support (for example A19→A26 in 69 later traces). The 34 qualifying items compare with fewer than 1 expected by chance (0.67 for 134 tested units), each difference was larger than in about 99.5% of reference re-splits, and the items agree on one rewiring rather than pointing in different directions. [REL005, REL007, REL009, DIR002, DIR004, LAG007, LAG008]

Limitations: the evidence is aggregated per window, so it cannot show whether individual traces were rerouted or whether the mix of coexisting process variants shifted toward traces that already ran this sequence; it also cannot establish which side moved, since "the group moved before A26" and "A26 and the A27→A28→A32 chain moved after the group" fit the same observations. Lags and relations use event positions, not clock time, so no timing claim is implied, and a few relational observations rest on very few traces (for example A19→A26 within five steps in only 1 earlier trace). [LAG007, LAG001]

Other differences: A26 also tends to occur later after A16 and A18 than before (A16→A26 shifted from mostly three to four–five steps apart; A18→A26 from two to three–four steps), consistent with the same rearrangement around A26. [LAG004, LAG005] Routing out of A48 and A50 remained dominated by the same successors (A48→A49 about 89%→99%; A50→A51 about 89%→98%). [DIR008, DIR009]
