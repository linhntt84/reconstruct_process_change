- In the earlier window, every one of the 200 traces contained A20, A21, A22, A23, A26 and the three activities A31, A32 and A37 exactly once. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the three-activity block A31/A32/A37, which in the earlier window every case executed between A20 and A23 before continuing via A21 and A22, became an optional branch in the later window: A20 now passes directly on to A23, and after A21 each case takes either A22 (about 54 in 100 cases) or the block A31/A32/A37 (about 47 in 100), which now exits to A26 instead of A23. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation of the block and of A22 fell from all traces to about half; the successor profiles of A20, A21 and the block members changed accordingly; A22 and the block became almost perfectly mutually exclusive; the block moved from before A21/A23 to after A21; and the A20→A23 distance shortened from four positions to one. With 39 qualifying control-flow items against fewer than 0.5 expected by chance among the 94 tested units, sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL006, REL011, LAG001, LAG005]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show what determines the branch choice, as no case attributes are available; the internal order of A31/A32/A37 is not pinned down; and about 1 in 200 later traces still shows the old arrangement, so the exclusivity of the two branches may not be complete. [REL011, REL020, DIR001, LAG001]

Other differences: a handful of lag items rest on a single after-window observation each; together they depict one exceptional trace still running the block directly after A20 and exiting to A23, so they mirror the restructuring above rather than indicating separate changes.
