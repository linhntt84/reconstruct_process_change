### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative block formed by A19, A20 and A25 — of which exactly one was executed per case, between A41 and A26 — is no longer executed: in the earlier window A41 was followed by exactly one of the three (about one third of cases each), which then led to A26, whereas in the later window A41 leads directly to A26 in about 92 in 100 cases and none of the three activities appears. Because the three are (nearly) absent in the later window while A14, A26, A31 and A41 still occur in every trace, this is a bypass or removal of a block of activities, not a shift of shares between existing alternatives. The pattern is direct and consistent — participation fell from 61–72 of 200 traces to 0–1, A41's successor profile flipped on about 201 outgoing transitions in each window, and the A41–A26 distance shortened from two steps to one — and screening produced 30 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.57 expected without a change, far beyond what a stable process yields. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, LAG002, REL007, REL009, REL014]

Limitations: zero participation only means the activities were not observed, so a removed block cannot be distinguished from one that still exists but was skipped in all 200 later cases; the redirected successor of the single remaining A20 occurrence rests on one trace; and the many relation items largely restate the same disappearance rather than adding independent confirmation. [DIR002, REL010]
