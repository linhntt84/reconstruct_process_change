### Observed differences

- Four activities became far more frequent: A22 appears in about 54 in 100 traces in the earlier window and in all 200 traces in the later window; A31, A32 and A37 each rise from about 46 in 100 to 199 of 200 traces (about 99.5 in 100). Each of them now occurs exactly once per trace, and no activity repeats in either window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- In the earlier window, A22 and the group A31/A32/A37 were strict alternatives: they never shared a trace (co-occurrence 0 of 200), with A22 alone in about 54% of traces and the group alone in about 46%. In the later window they co-occur in about 995 of 1,000 traces, always with A31/A32/A37 before A22. [REL011, REL012, REL013]
- The group moved earlier in the trace. Before, A20 was followed directly by A23 in 100% of A20's observed outgoing transitions; in the later window A20 leads to A31, A32 and A37 (about 32–36% each) and almost never to A23 (about 0.5%). The distance from A20 to A23 grows from one step (100% of traces) to four steps (about 99.5%). [DIR001, LAG001]
- A23 stayed in every trace in both windows, but its surroundings changed: before, A23 came before A31/A32/A37 in all of their joint appearances (46% of traces); in the later window A31/A32/A37 come before A23 in about 995 of 1,000 traces, and each of the three now leads to A23 (about 31–37% of their outgoing transitions) where previously each led to A26 (about 29–36%). [REL009, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- A21's routing narrowed: before, A21 led to A22 in about 54% of its outgoing transitions and to A31/A32/A37 in about 46% combined; in the later window A21 always leads to A22, and A26 follows consistently two positions after A21 (before, that distance was two steps in about 54% of traces and four steps in about 46%). A26 itself remained in every trace in both windows. [DIR002, LAG002, REL005, REL010, REL017, REL018, REL019]
- The internal order among A31, A32 and A37 stays mixed in both windows (both orders in similar proportions whenever they co-occur), so no fixed sequence emerged inside the group. [REL020, REL021, REL022]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the earlier choice between A22 and the group A31, A32, A37 after A21 no longer exists: both blocks run in every case, with the group repositioned to directly after A20 and before A23. Previously a trace ran either A22 (about 54 in 100 cases) or the group in mixed order (about 46 in 100), never both, and the block in play ended at A26; now A31/A32/A37 run virtually always between A20 and A23, A23 follows the group (four positions after A20 instead of one), and A22 always runs after A21, followed by A26. A22 and the group thus changed from alternatives into mandatory blocks, and the group was repositioned earlier in the trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013, DIR001, LAG001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: participation of all four activities rises from about half of traces to (nearly) all; strict mutual exclusivity becomes near-universal co-occurrence with stable orders; the successor changes at A20, A21, A31, A32 and A37 and the two distance shifts (A20→A23 from one to four steps, A21→A26 from mixed two/four to two) all match the same repositioning. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, REL011, REL012, REL013]

The volume of qualifying evidence also exceeds what chance produces: 33 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.45 would be expected for an unchanged process (91 units tested). [screening]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows sample different mixes of pre-existing variants (for example, a later window dominated by a variant that was rare before) rather than a modified process; the complete absence of traces combining A22 with A31/A32/A37 among the 200 earlier traces makes this unlikely but cannot exclude it. The evidence covers control flow only, so the conclusion is limited to structure and ordering. [REL011, REL012, REL013]

Other differences: none — the remaining observations (the unchanged mixed internal order of A31/A32/A37 and the absence of repeated activities in both windows) fit the single change described above. [REL020, REL021, REL022, OCC001]
