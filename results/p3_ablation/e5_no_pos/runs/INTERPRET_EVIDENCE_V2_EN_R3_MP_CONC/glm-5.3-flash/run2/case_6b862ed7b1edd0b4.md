### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the route through A19, A25 and A20 between A41 and A26, with A14 before it, is executed in virtually every case, whereas in the earlier window this route was taken in only about half of the cases and A41 passed directly to A26 in about 45 in 100 of its outgoing transitions. The internal order A19, then A25, then A20 already existed earlier but only within the minority of traces containing the trio; in the later window A41 leads almost exclusively to A19, the trio appears in about 995 of 1000 traces, and the direct A41-to-A26 step has nearly disappeared. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR006, REL009, REL018, REL019, REL022, REL025, LAG018, LAG020, LAG019, LAG021]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the participation estimates rest on 95–96 versus 199 traces per activity, the ordering relations hold in about 96–99 in 100 of 200-trace windows, the lag distances are mutually consistent with a single fixed chain, and 60 evidence items qualified against roughly 0.8 expected by chance for the 160 tested control-flow units, so the item count as a whole cannot be explained by random re-splits. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL018, REL025, LAG018, LAG020, LAG019, LAG021]

Limitations: the occurrence and relation items describe the same underlying traces and are therefore not independent confirmations; the evidence cannot rule out that the later window simply contains far fewer cases of a variant that skips the segment — a shift in the case mix — rather than a rerouting within one process definition; and the earlier-window lag profiles for A06 to A19 and A06 to A25 rest on only about 7 observed pairs each. [LAG003, LAG004]

Other differences: A06 now occurs in about 73 in 100 traces (earlier about 24) and typically precedes the segment by four to five positions; its outgoing transitions concentrate on A16, usually immediately; A15 now sends about 40 in 100 of its transitions to A06 (earlier about 1 in 100); A08 became rarer, from about 20 in 100 traces to about 8 in 100.
