### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the earlier either/or routing was replaced by a single mandatory sequence. In the earlier window, A25 was followed immediately by A28, and A26 led into a choice between A27 (then A31), taken in about 54 of 100 cases, and the group A36/A37/A42 in any internal order (then A31), taken in about 46 of 100; A25, A26, A28 and A31 themselves ran in essentially every case throughout. In the later window, essentially every case runs A25, then A36/A37/A42 in no fixed order, then A28, then A26, then A27, then A31: the four formerly alternative activities each execute exactly once, the group moved from after A26 to between A25 and A28, and A28 moved from directly after A25 to four positions later. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL005, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL006, REL014, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003]

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: the structural shift rests on relational and successor evidence (co-occurrence of A27 with the group rising from about 0 to about 995 of 1,000; the A26 and A28 order relations flipping from about 46 to about 99 in 100; the successors of A25, A26 and the group changing; the A25→A28 gap moving from 1 to 4 with about 200 and 198 supporting observations), together with participation of all four activities rising to essentially 100%. The 37 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.66 expected without change, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, so the ensemble is not what an unchanged process produces. Because A27 and the group were mutually exclusive before and co-occur afterwards, this is a change in structure — activities became mandatory and were repositioned — not merely a shift in how often existing paths are taken. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG003]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows draw on different case populations or process variants rather than a modified single process; within each window, however, behaviour is homogeneous (exclusive branches before, one combined sequence after), which sampling noise alone would not typically produce. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL020, REL021, REL022]

Other differences: none beyond the pattern above; the remaining gap items (A25→A36, A36→A28, A37→A31, A42→A31) each rest on about one observation in one window and, while consistent with the sequence described, are too weak to add weight. [LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]
