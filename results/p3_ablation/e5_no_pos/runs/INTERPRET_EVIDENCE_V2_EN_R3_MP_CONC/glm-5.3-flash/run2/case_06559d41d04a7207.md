- A10 receded: it appeared in about 26 in 100 traces before and about 11 in 100 after; its outgoing transitions, previously concentrated on A21, A19 and A20 (about 85 in 100 combined), are now spread thinly over many different activities, based on only 23 later traces. [OCC001, DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured by replacing one branch with another. In the earlier window, A44 was typically followed by exactly one of the alternatives A19, A20 or A21 (each taken in roughly a third of traces), which then led on to A29, with A10 occasionally interposed; in the later window, A19, A20 and A21 are essentially no longer observed, and the three activities A26, A27 and A28 — each executed once in virtually every trace, in varying mutual order — now run between A44 and A29. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR009, REL031, REL033, LAG005]

This is direct and consistent across indicator types: participation counts show the old activities falling from about a third of traces to about 1 in 200 and the new ones rising from zero to every trace; the successor profile of A44 flips from the old alternatives to the new block; relational items show the new block co-occurring and ordered between A44 and A29; and the A44-to-A29 position distance grows from about 2 to about 4 steps, exactly what inserting three activities would produce. The volume of qualifying items (66 control-flow items against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed) also cannot be explained by chance sampling. [DIR009, REL034, REL042, LAG005, OCC005]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows draw on different case mixes rather than a change over time, and because A19, A20 and A21 still appear in 1 of 200 later traces, they may have become very rare rather than removed — their later behaviour rests on a single observation each. [OCC002, DIR003]

Other differences: A10's participation fell from about 26 to about 11 in 100 traces and its outgoing transitions redistributed across many targets; A46 shifted from co-occurring with A19/A20/A21 in about 21–26 in 100 traces to co-occurring with A26/A27/A28 in about 58 in 100, now typically after A26; A14's outgoing flow, previously almost exclusively to A15, remains dominated by A15 with only marginal spread. [OCC001, DIR001, REL036, DIR002]
