### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a swap between two three-activity blocks around A20. In the earlier window, about 82 in 100 traces contained A24, A25 and A26 together, with A20 preceding each of them in about 80 in 100 traces, while A21, A22 and A23 appeared together in only about 19 in 100 traces. - Two activities occur somewhat more often: A02 in about 17 in 100 traces versus about 8 before, and A04 in about 19 in 100 versus about 5 before. [OCC001, OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative path executed after A20 was switched: traces that previously ran, in about 82 in 100 cases, the block A24–A26 together after A20 now run, in about 79 in 100 cases, the block A21–A23 together after A20, with the earlier block retained in only about 21–22 in 100 traces. Both blocks were observed in substantial shares in both windows and remain near-mutually exclusive, so this reads as a swap in how often two existing alternative paths are taken, not as activities appearing or disappearing. The change is shown directly in the successor distribution of A20 itself and is consistent across occurrence, relation and direction indicators; with 70 qualifying control-flow items against fewer than one expected if nothing had changed, sampling variation cannot explain the pattern as a whole. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that both windows contain the same two behavioural variants and only their mix shifted (for example a changed case mix) rather than the routing itself having been modified; the many items also overlap heavily on the same traces and are not independent confirmations. [DIR005, REL011, REL014, REL017, REL019, OCC004, OCC007]

Other differences: A06 was largely bypassed — its participation fell from about 46 to about 12 in 100 traces, and A34 now leads straight to A35 where it previously routed about 45 in 100 of its outgoing transitions through A06; A02 and A04 also became somewhat more frequent (about 8 to 17 and 5 to 19 in 100 traces). The picture for A06 after the change rests on few observations (about 24 traces and 26 outgoing transitions), so its new routing is described only tentatively.
