### Observed differences

- The clearest difference is a reversal between two groups of activities. In the earlier window, each of A21, A22 and A23 appeared in about 75 in 100 traces, while each of A24, A25 and A26 appeared in about 25 in 100; in the later window these shares are reversed, with the first group at about 25 in 100 and the second at about 75 in 100. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]
- A20, which appears in virtually every trace in both windows, now routes differently: earlier its outgoing transitions went mainly to A21 (about 26%), A23 (about 25%) and A22 (about 23%) and rarely to A24/A25/A26 (about 13%/4%/7%); later they go mainly to A26 (about 28%), A24 (about 25%) and A25 (about 22%), with only about 8% each to A21/A22/A23. [DIR003]
- The two groups behave as alternative blocks: A21, A22 and A23 co-occurred in about 75 in 100 traces earlier but only about 25 in 100 later (in about 75 of 100 later traces none of them appears), while A24, A25 and A26 went from co-occurring in about 25 in 100 traces to about 75 in 100; cross-group pairs such as A21 with A24 almost never share a trace in either window. [REL013, REL021, REL034, REL039, REL015, REL024]
- Relations with downstream activities flip in the same direction: A30 co-occurred with A21/A22/A23 in about 75 in 100 traces earlier versus about 25 later, and with A24/A25/A26 in about 25 earlier versus about 75 later; A32 and A33 show the same flip (co-occurrence with the first group about 48% down to 15%, with the second about 17% up to 52%). [REL018, REL031, REL036, REL043, REL019, REL037]
- A10 became more frequent, from about 6 in 100 traces earlier to about 21 later, and its outgoing transitions now concentrate on A33 (about 90% later versus about 18% earlier); A32 also now sends about 20% of its outgoing transitions to A10, versus about 1% earlier. [OCC002, DIR002, DIR004]
- Smaller shifts: A02 participation fell from about 9 to about 1 in 100 traces (backed by only 18 versus 2 traces), and positional gaps spread slightly towards later positions for A20→A24, A25→A30 and A32→A33 (for example, A20→A24 at a gap of one position fell from about 54% to about 33%). [OCC001, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the routing after A20 flipped between two alternative activity blocks: in the earlier window traces typically continued from A20 into the block A21–A22–A23 (about 3 in 4 traces, with the three activities running together), while the block A24–A25–A26 ran in about 1 in 4 traces; in the later window these frequencies are reversed, with A24–A25–A26 in about 3 in 4 traces and A21–A22–A23 in about 1 in 4. The change is shown directly and consistently: participation shares flip for all six activities, A20's successor distribution flips, and the relational items uniformly show the two groups as alternatives whose selection shares swapped, with downstream activities (A30, A32, A33) following the same pattern. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR003, REL007, REL010, REL013, REL015, REL034, REL036, REL019]

Both blocks remain well observed throughout — every involved activity appears in at least about 25 in 100 traces in each window — so this is a change in how often existing alternative paths are taken, not an activity being added, removed or moved. The volume of flagged evidence also exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 60 control-flow items qualified against fewer than one expected by chance across 172 tested units, and the items agree on a single pattern rather than pointing in different directions. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows captured a different mix of pre-existing case types rather than a changed selection rule; the data show that the shares flipped, not what governs the choice between the blocks. [REL015, REL024, REL036, OCC003, DIR003]

Other differences: A10 became more frequent (about 6% to about 21% of traces) and now leads mostly to A33, with A32 sometimes leading to A10; A02 became rare (about 9% to about 1%, based on very few traces); and the positional gaps for A20→A24, A25→A30 and A32→A33 spread slightly towards later positions. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
