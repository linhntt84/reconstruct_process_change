### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every one of the 200 traces contained the same consecutive fragment: A22 was always followed directly by A23, A23 always directly by A24, and A22 and A24 were therefore always exactly two steps apart. [DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural addition: in the later window, almost every case (about 199 in 200) executes exactly one of three activities that were not observed before — A14, A15 or A20, acting as mutually exclusive alternatives — inside the previously fixed consecutive sequence A22 → A23 → A24, in about 56 in 100 of these cases between A22 and A23 and in about 44 in 100 between A23 and A24, with A22 always before and A24 always after the inserted activity. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The support is direct and consistent: 23 control-flow items qualified where only about 0.37 would be expected by chance for the 75 tested units, so the ensemble is far beyond what random re-splits of an unchanged process produce; the placement is established by relational and distance evidence rather than frequencies alone; and the successor redistribution of A22 and A23, the pairwise orders and the lag shifts all agree numerically with one and the same structure. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window means only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed in those 200 traces — they could have existed at a very low frequency that the before-window failed to sample, and the evidence cannot show what determines the choice among the three activities or between the two positions. The many relation items also largely re-describe the same insertion behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
