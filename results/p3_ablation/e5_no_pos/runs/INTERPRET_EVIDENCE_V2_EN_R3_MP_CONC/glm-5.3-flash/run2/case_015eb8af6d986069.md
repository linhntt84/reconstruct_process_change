### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A hypothesis supported by the evidence is that the segment formed by A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A25 and A26 (closing into A32) changed from loosely ordered, variably spaced execution into one fixed sequence with fixed step distances, replacing the earlier direct exit from A23 to A32 with the full chain through A24, A26 and A25. The relational evidence shows the order change directly (mixed or both-orders earlier, one order in about 96–99 in 100 later), and the distance and successor evidence agree with it rather than merely echoing it; with 76 qualifying control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected without change, the ensemble is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. [REL014, REL017, REL019, LAG006, LAG012, LAG021, DIR009, DIR013]

Other differences: A08 is executed in far fewer cases (72→29 in 100 traces) and rarely hands over to A16 any more, A15 now proceeds almost directly to A16, and A10 runs in more cases (about 8→22 in 100 traces); these participation changes are reported separately and are not part of the ordering hypothesis. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR003, DIR006, REL001]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shifted mix of pre-existing execution variants rather than a changed ordering constraint; also, a few distance items rest on only 1–13 later-window observations and were not relied upon. [LAG019, LAG025, LAG027]
