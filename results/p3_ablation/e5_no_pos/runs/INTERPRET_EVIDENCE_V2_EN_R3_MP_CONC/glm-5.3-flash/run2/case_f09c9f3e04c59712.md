### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two segments of the process exchanged their placement around the middle of the trace: the step in which exactly one of A14, A16 or A19 executes moved from after A24, where it closed the case before A25, to directly after A35 and before A20, while the trio A15, A17, A18 — present in every trace in both windows — moved from between A35 and A20 to after A24 and now closes the case before A25; A20 and A24 remained between the two segments in both windows. [DIR001-DIR008, REL008, LAG001, LAG002]

The reordering is shown directly and consistently: every cross-group order relation reverses (21 of 21 pairs), every boundary successor changes in the same direction, and the positional step counts fit one activity versus three activities in each slot. The volume of flagged items also far exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 32 items qualified where about 0.41 would be expected by chance — roughly 80 times that expectation. [REL001-REL021, DIR007, LAG001, LAG002, screening]

The evidence cannot show the relative order of A20 and A24 themselves, since no item covers that pair, so their exact placement between the segments is inferred rather than observed; with 200 traces per window, smaller shifts in the trio's internal routing (each of A15, A17, A18 still leads to the other two or to the group's exit in both windows) also cannot be pinned down.
