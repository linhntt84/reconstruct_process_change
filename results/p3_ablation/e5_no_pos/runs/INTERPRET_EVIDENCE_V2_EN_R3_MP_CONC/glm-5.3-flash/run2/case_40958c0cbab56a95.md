### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One possibility supported by the evidence is a structural replacement around the A44–A29 span: the always-executed segment formed by A26, A27 and A28, which earlier sat between A44 and A29 in no fixed internal order, is no longer observed, and in its place exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities — A19, A20 or A21 — now runs in almost every later trace, each leading almost directly to A29. The change is shown directly rather than inferred: participation flipped from 200 of 200 traces to 3 of 200 for the old trio while the new activities rose from 2–4 to 60–75 traces; A44's outgoing transitions moved from the old trio to the new one; the new activities lead to A29 in about 95 in 100 of their observed outgoing transitions; the relation items show the old trio's co-occurrence with each other, with A11/A12 and with A29/A44 collapsing while the new activities co-occur with A44 before and A29 after; and the A44→A29 position gap shortened from 4–5 to 2–3 steps, as expected when three intervening activities disappear. With 73 qualifying items against roughly 0.83 expected if nothing had changed, sampling variation cannot account for the set as a whole. The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different coexisting process variants or a shifted case mix rather than a single modified process; and since A26/A27/A28 still appear in about 2 in 100 later traces, complete removal versus extreme rareness remains open. The lag items on the old trio's internal distances rest on only 1–3 later-window observations and were not used.
