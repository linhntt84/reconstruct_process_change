### Observed differences

- The fixed order between two groups of activities reversed. Before, when A19 and A26 occurred in the same case, A19 came first in about 36 of 100 cases and the reverse almost never; in the later window, A26 came first in about 31 of 100 cases and the reverse almost never. The same reversal holds for A20 vs A26 (about 24 before, about 37 after, reversed), A25 vs A26 (about 38 vs 29), and for A19, A20 and A25 against A27 (about 24–38 before, about 29–37 after, reversed). Against A28 the earlier one-sided order became mixed (for A20 vs A28: about 17 before A28 vs about 21 after, in the later window). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

Before, A45's outgoing transitions went to A25, A19 and A20 in roughly equal shares (about 35, 35 and 24 in 100), and A19, A20 and A25 led almost exclusively to A26 (about 92–99 in 100 of their outgoing transitions). In the later window, about 94 in 100 of A45's outgoing transitions go to A26, while A19, A20 and A25 lead mainly to A32 (about 45–56 in 100) and A28 (about 39–48 in 100), with A26 almost no longer observed as their successor. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of activities A19, A20 and A25 changed its placement relative to A26, A27 and A28. Before, one of A19/A20/A25 followed A45 and led on to A26, and these three consistently ran before A26 and before A27/A28; in the later window, A45 leads directly to A26, and A19, A20 and A25 run after A26 and after A27/A28, feeding into A28 or A32, so that A27 typically reaches A32 in three steps instead of two. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG005, LAG006, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011]

Supporting signs: the order reversal is shown directly by relational evidence in all eight tested activity pairs, each based on all 200 traces per window; the successor and distance changes agree with the same relocation; and 26 qualifying items against roughly 0.58 expected without change makes a chance explanation of the set implausible, though many items describe the same rewiring rather than independent confirmations. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

Limitations: no relational item covers A26 against A27/A28, so A26's exact position between them in the later window is not established; residual old routing (A25 still leading to A26 in about 2 of 100 of its outgoing transitions, direct A28→A32 in about 45 of 100) means a mixture of old and new variants in the later window cannot be ruled out, nor can increased repetition of A19/A20/A25 within a case, which the links from A28 back to these activities suggest. [DIR003, DIR005, LAG009, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

Other differences: A24's participation rose from about 43 to about 58 in 100 cases (mean occurrences per trace from about 0.43 to 0.58); no relational evidence places A24, so it is not tied to the reordering above. [OCC001]
