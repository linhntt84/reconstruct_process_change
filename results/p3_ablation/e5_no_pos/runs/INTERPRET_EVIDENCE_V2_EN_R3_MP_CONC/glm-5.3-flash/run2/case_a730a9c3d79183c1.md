### Observed differences

- The six activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 each ran in roughly half of the cases before (49–53 in 100 traces); in the later window each runs in nearly every case (98–100 in 100) [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010].
- Before, these six formed two mutually exclusive groups: A33, A35 and A36 always ran together (about 49 in 100 traces) and A37, A38 and A39 always ran together (about 52 in 100), while cross-group pairs such as A33 with A37 co-occurred in only about 1 in 100 traces. In the later window all six run in the same trace in about 98 in 100 cases, with A33/A35/A36 preceding A37/A38/A39 in about 95–98 of 100 co-occurrences; within each group both orders still occur in similar proportions, so there is no fixed internal sequence. Activities that already ran in nearly every case in both windows (A14, A15, A30, A31, A47, A48) now co-occur with the whole block almost always, whereas before they met only one of the two groups [REL039, REL041, REL045, REL047, REL050, REL052, REL037, REL044, REL055, REL059, REL013, REL025, REL042].
- The combined block sits later in the case: A30 previously reached A37, A38 or A39 within 1–3 positions (about 29–35 in 100 observations at each of lags 1–3), whereas in the later window they follow 4–5 positions after A30, and A30's outgoing transitions now go to A35 (about 36), A33 (31) and A36 (29 in 100) instead of spreading evenly over A33–A39. A31 still follows the block but later: A33/A35/A36 reach A31 at 4–5 positions (about 46–53 in 100) versus 1–3 before, and A33 now leads onward to A37–A39 rather than to A31 [LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG011, LAG013, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, REL031, REL032, REL033]. With A15, the block now partly interleaves or repeats (about 41 in 100 traces versus 15–18 before) [REL019, REL020, REL024].
- A04 became much less common, running in about 26 in 100 traces versus 59 before; A14 now continues to A15 in about 79 of 100 outgoing transitions (previously 43) and to A04 in about 12 (previously 57), and A04's own follow-ups spread from about 90 in 100 leading to A15 to about 40, with many other targets appearing [OCC002, DIR004, DIR002].
- A02, A08 and A10 rose from about 5–8 to 18–23 in 100 traces; their successor profiles changed markedly, but these rest on only 11–50 observed transitions [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR003]. Minor: A47 reaches A48 directly in about 84.5 in 100 cases (previously 96.5) [LAG015].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the six activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39, which previously executed as two mutually exclusive blocks — either {A33, A35, A36} or {A37, A38, A39}, each in about half of the cases — now execute together in nearly every case as one combined sequence in which A33/A35/A36 run first (in varying order) and A37/A38/A39 follow, occupying the segment between A30 and A31. The signs are direct and consistent: cross-group co-occurrence rose from about 1 to about 98 in 100 traces with a stable earlier-group-first order across all nine cross-group pairs, participation of all six rose to 98–100 in 100, A30's routing and inter-event distances shifted onto the combined block, and A31 now follows the whole block at a greater distance [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, REL039, REL041, REL045, REL047, REL050, REL052, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, REL031, REL032, REL033]. Chance cannot explain the pattern as a whole: 98 control-flow items qualified where about 1 would be expected without a change, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces [OCC005, REL039, LAG005]. Limitations: a few lag profiles rest on very few observations in one window (for example A33→A37 and A36→A37 had a single supporting occurrence before, and A35→A48 fell to one after), and the aggregate evidence cannot distinguish a modified single flow from a shifted mix of case variants in which a runs-both-blocks variant became dominant [LAG009, LAG012, LAG014].

Other differences: A04's participation fell from about 59 to 26 in 100 traces, and A14 now usually continues to A15 rather than to A04, whose own follow-ups diversified [OCC002, DIR004, DIR002, LAG002]; A02, A08 and A10 occur in roughly three to four times as many traces as before, with rerouted successors based on few observations [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR003]; A47–A48 adjacency loosened slightly [LAG015].
