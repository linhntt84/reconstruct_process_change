### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a block of three mutually exclusive activities — A19, A20 and A25 — was inserted between A41 and A26. In the earlier window the flow ran from A41 directly to A26 and none of the three was observed; in the later window A41 leads into one of the three in about 87 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, the chosen activity leads back into A26, and the A41-to-A26 distance grew from one position to two or three. The placement is shown directly by successor changes (A41, A19, A25), by consistent before/after ordering across all six activity pairs, and by the positional gap shift; the 17 qualifying items against roughly 0.5 expected by chance for the 99 tested units also rules out sampling noise as an overall explanation.
