### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: in the earlier window, A44 was typically followed by exactly one of the alternatives A19, A20 or A21 (each in roughly 30–36% of traces, then leading to A29), while in the later window A44 is followed by A26, A27 and A28, which run together in virtually every trace in no fixed order and then lead to A29, with A19, A20 and A21 all but gone. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, REL019, REL020, REL023, REL021, REL024, REL026, LAG001]

The change is shown directly and consistently by participation, successor, relational and distance indicators: the participation flips are large and well supported on both sides (70/60/72 vs 1/2/1 traces, and 0 vs 200 of 200), A44's successor change rests on about 200 traces in each window, and the distance growth of about two positions fits a three-activity block now sitting between A44 and A29. With 41 qualifying items against roughly 0.6 expected without change, chance sampling cannot explain the evidence as a whole, though many items restate the same underlying trace difference rather than confirming it independently. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later behaviour already existed earlier as a rare variant, invisible at 0 of 200 traces, so a gradual mix shift rather than a boundary-aligned replacement cannot be excluded. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, LAG001]

Other differences: the few residual A19/A20/A21 occurrences in the later window route to other successors (A09/A10 after A19, A45 after A20, A34 after A21), but these profiles rest on only 1–2 traces; A26, A27 and A28 also show rare outgoing edges (about 0.5–1% each) to activities such as A02, A38 and A43.
