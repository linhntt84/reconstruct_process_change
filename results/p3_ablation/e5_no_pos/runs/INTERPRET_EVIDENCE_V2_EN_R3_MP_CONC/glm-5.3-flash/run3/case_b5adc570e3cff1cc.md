### Observed differences

- The order between A21/A22 and the alternative block A14/A15/A20 reversed. In the earlier window, whenever one of A14, A15, A20 ran (about 30–38 in 100 cases each), it always came after A21 and after A22; in the later window it always comes before both — for example, A21 preceded A14 in all 38 of 100 co-occurring cases earlier, while A14 now precedes A21 in all 34 of 100. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

- A40 now feeds the block instead of A21. Earlier, every A40 was followed immediately by A21; in the later window A40 is followed by A15 in about 35 of 100 cases, A14 in 34 and A20 in 32, and A21 now lies two event positions after A40 instead of one. [DIR006, LAG004]

- The block's exits changed. Earlier, A14 led to A23 or A27 in about equal shares (50/50), A15 to A23 in 56 and A27 in 44, A20 to A23 in 52 and A27 in 48; in the later window each of the three leads only to A21. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

- The A22/A23 routing became a fixed chain. Earlier, A22 led to A23 in about 48 of 100 cases and back to the block in 52, and A23 led to A27 in 53 and back to the block in 47, so the block member ran between A22 and A23 in variable order; in the later window A22 leads only to A23 and A23 only to A27, the A22→A23 and A23→A27 distances collapse to exactly one position (earlier often two), and A22 reaches A27 two positions later instead of three. [DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

- How often the block runs barely changed: each member ran in about a third of cases in both windows, and the three together account for 200 of 200 cases in each window (76+63+61 earlier, 68+69+63 later), consistent with one block member per case throughout; its position and connections changed, not its participation. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

- A10 and A11 still occur in every case, but they repeat less often: mean occurrences per case fell from about 1.9 to about 1.6, and recurrence support fell from about 47 to about 38 in 100 cases. [OCC001, OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports one specific structural hypothesis: in the later window, the alternative block A14/A15/A20 (one member per case) is placed directly after A40 and before A21, whereas earlier it ran after A21, interleaved between A22 and A23 in variable order before the A27 exit; with this change, A22 → A23 → A27 became a fixed chain and the block now exits into A21 instead of A23 or A27. This is a possibility supported by the evidence, not a confirmed modification. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: six relational items cover every block member against both A21 and A22, and each flips completely with no mixed orders in either window; the successor changes (A40 to the block, block to A21, A22 and A23 losing their edges back into the block) and the positional shifts (A40→A21 from one to two positions, A22→A23 and A23→A27 collapsing to one, A22→A27 from three to two) all match the same story. Screening flagged 18 control-flow items where about 0.35 would be expected without a change, every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 comparable random splits, and the supports are large (200 cases per window; 61–76 observations behind each block member's outgoing profile). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Limitations: the evidence contains no outgoing profile for A21 and no relational item for the pair A21/A22, so the exact wiring between A21 and the A22–A23–A27 chain in the later window is inferred rather than observed; distances are in event positions, not clock time; and a shift in the mix of two coexisting process variants between the windows would produce the same contrast and cannot be ruled out. [DIR001, DIR006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Other differences: A10 and A11 occur in every case in both windows but repeat less often afterwards (mean occurrences per case about 1.9 → 1.6; recurrence support about 47 → 38 in 100 cases). No relational or directional evidence ties them to the relocated block, so this reduction in repetition stands as a separate difference. [OCC001, OCC002]
