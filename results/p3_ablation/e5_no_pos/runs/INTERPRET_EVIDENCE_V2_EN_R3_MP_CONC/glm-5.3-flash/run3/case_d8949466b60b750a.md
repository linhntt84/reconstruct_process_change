### Observed differences

- A27 changed from an optional step to a near-universal one: in the earlier window it appeared in about 52% of traces (roughly 103 of 200, about once when present); in the later window it appears in about 99.5% (199 of 200), almost exactly once per trace. [OCC001]
- A32, A33 and A38, each present in about 48–49% of traces earlier (97–98 of 200), were observed in only about 1 of 200 traces in the later window. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Routing out of A26 shifted: earlier, about 51% of its outgoing transitions went to A27, with the rest split to A38 (about 18%), A33 (about 16%) and A32 (about 14%); later, about 97% go to A27. [DIR001]
- Earlier, A26 was followed by A27 in about half the traces and by A32, A33 or A38 in the other half, and A27 never co-occurred with any of those three (they were strict alternatives); later, A26 precedes A27 in about 97% of traces, and A32/A33/A38 no longer follow A26. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008]
- Earlier, A32, A33 and A38 typically ran together as a group (co-occurring in about 48–49% of traces, in mixed order) and each preceded A28 in about 48% of traces; later they neither co-occur nor precede A28. [REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- The positional gap from A26 to A28 was earlier split between 2 steps (about 50%) and 4 steps (about 47%); later it is 2 steps in about 97% of traces. A27 is immediately followed by A28 (1 step) in both windows (about 97% earlier, 100% later). [LAG001, LAG004, DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the route from A26 to A28 through A32, A33 and A38 is no longer executed in the later window: earlier, roughly half of the cases ran A26 → A27 → A28 while the other half ran A26 → the A32/A33/A38 group → A28; later, virtually all cases run A26 → A27 → A28, with A27 executed in nearly every case between A26 and A28, and A32, A33 and A38 each observed in only about 1 in 200 cases. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL005, LAG001]

Supporting signs: the screening produced 31 qualifying items where about 0.57 would be expected if nothing had changed — roughly 50 times the chance expectation — so the set of differences is unlikely to arise from random re-splits of stable behaviour; participation, routing, relational and positional indicators all describe the same rerouting consistently, and the retained A27 → A28 succession is essentially unchanged. Note that many items describe the same underlying trace behaviour, so their agreement is not independent confirmation. [screening, DIR002, LAG004]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish whether the A32/A33/A38 branch was removed entirely or still exists but is chosen in only about 0.5% of cases; pure sampling fluctuation is implausible given the size and consistency of the shifts, but this removal-versus-rare-choice distinction remains open. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Other differences: the later window shows sparse new outgoing transitions from A26 (to A28, A24, A13, A17, A08, A25) and from A27 (to A09, A14, A15), each about 0.5% of transitions and backed by roughly one trace, while the earlier A26 → A06 transition (about 1%) is no longer observed; the later-window routing and gap profiles of A32, A33 and A38 likewise rest on a single trace each and are too weak to characterise. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG002, LAG003, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]
