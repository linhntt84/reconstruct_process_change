### Observed differences

- Participation became (near-)universal in the later window: A22 was present in 54% of before-traces and is present in 100% of after-traces; A31, A32 and A37 were each present in 46% of before-traces and are present in about 995 of 1000 after-traces. None of the four activities repeats within a case in either window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- In the earlier window every outgoing transition of A20 led to A23; in the later window A20 leads to A31 (about 36%), A32 (about 32%) and A37 (about 32%), with a direct transition to A23 nearly gone (about 1 in 200). [DIR001]
- In the earlier window A21 led to A22 in 54% of its outgoing transitions and to A31, A32 or A37 in the remaining share; in the later window A21 always leads to A22. [DIR002]
- A22 and each of A31, A32, A37 were mutually exclusive in the earlier window (never in the same case); in the later window they co-occur in essentially every case, with the triple executed before A22. The triple also switched from running after A21 and after A23 to running before them in about 995 of 1000 cases. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016]
- A23 used to follow A20 at a distance of one step in 100% of cases; in the later window the distance is four steps in about 995 of 1000 cases, consistent with the triple now running between them. The A21→A26 distance, previously two steps (54% of cases) or four steps (46%), is now uniformly two steps. [LAG001, LAG002]
- Each of A31, A32, A37 previously led to A26 in roughly a third of its outgoing transitions; in the later window that transition is unobserved, and each instead leads to A23 in about a third of transitions (when it is the last of the triple) and to the other triple members otherwise. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural re-routing: the block A31/A32/A37, previously an alternative to A22 that ran after A21 in about 46 in 100 cases, appears to have become a mandatory block repositioned to run directly after A20 and before A23, while A22 became mandatory after A21 — in the later window essentially every case runs A20 → A31/A32/A37 (in no fixed internal order) → A23 → A21 → A22 → A26. Supporting signs: participation of A22, A31, A32 and A37 rose to (near) 100%; the successor profiles of A20 and A21 changed exactly as this sequence predicts; pairwise relations flipped from mutual exclusivity and "triple after A21/A23" to universal co-occurrence with the triple first, while the A21→A22→A26 tail became universal; and A23's distance from A20 moved from one to four steps. The 33 qualifying control-flow items, against roughly 0.45 expected if nothing had changed, cannot be explained by sampling variation as a whole, and every item's difference exceeds what random re-splits of the pooled data produce. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: the end-to-end sequence is reconstructed from pairwise relations and position gaps rather than from observed complete variants, so details such as whether A23 always immediately precedes A21 cannot be verified, and changes involving activities outside the tested units would not be visible. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows draw from different case mixtures (for example, a pre-existing combined variant that went unobserved in 200 before-traces) rather than from a modified single process; the pairwise indicators cannot distinguish these. [REL011, REL012, REL013, LAG001]

Other differences: none beyond the above. The internal order of A31, A32 and A37 remains unfixed in both windows (each pair occurs in both orders in roughly equal shares), so the change concerns the block's placement and mandatory execution, not its internal sequencing. [REL020, REL021, REL022]
