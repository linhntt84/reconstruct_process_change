### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reversal of the ordering between the segment pair A26/A27 and the three-activity group A35/A36/A41 in the part of the process following A25: earlier, A25 led immediately to A27, A26 directly preceded the group, and the group closed into A30; later, A25 leads directly into the group (about one third each to A35, A36 and A41), the group closes into A27, and A26 directly precedes A30. This is shown directly by the near-complete order reversal in all six relations between {A26, A27} and {A35, A36, A41} (from roughly 99 in 100 one way to roughly 99 in 100 the other) [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006], and it is corroborated by the successor profiles of A25, A26 and the group members [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005] and by the position-gap shifts [LAG001, LAG003]. Because every involved activity co-occurs with the others in essentially all traces in both windows, this is a reversal of ordering constraints, not a change in participation or a replacement of activities. With 21 qualifying items against roughly 0.6 expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeding about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010].

Limitations: the order between A26 and A27 themselves was not directly observed in either window, so their arrangement in the later segment (A27 before A26) is inferred from the gap and successor items rather than a direct relation between the two [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG003]. The evidence also cannot distinguish a flipped ordering constraint applying to all cases from a flip in the dominant mix of two order variants (from about 99:1 to about 1:99), and several of the rare-pair gap items rest on only 1–2 observations, though the hypothesis does not rest on them [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]. Residual transition shares of about 1 in 200 appear as scattered, isolated components and are consistent with sampling noise [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005].
