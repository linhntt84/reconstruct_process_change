### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained the three activities A21, A22 and A23 (participation 100 in 100 traces; they ran adjacently in varying order, all three before A24). In the later window each of them appears in only about 1 in 200 traces, and the single residual trace still contains all three together. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL025, REL027, REL031]
- A14, A15 and A16 were not observed at all in the earlier window; in the later window they run in about 30, 33 and 37 in 100 traces respectively, and never together (pairwise co-occurrence 0), so essentially every later trace contains exactly one of the three as mutually exclusive alternatives. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL010]
- A39's outgoing transitions moved from the old trio to the new one: earlier they went to A21, A22 and A23 in roughly equal shares (about 35, 31 and 35 in 100); later they go to A16, A15 and A14 (about 37, 33 and 30 in 100), with full 200-trace support in both windows. [DIR007]
- Each of A14, A15 and A16 is always followed by A24, and A39 always precedes them; the positional distance from A39 to A24 shrank from 4 event positions in every earlier trace to 2 positions in the later window, consistent with three intermediate activities being replaced by one. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL006, REL007, LAG010]
- Downstream behaviour is largely stable: A24 and A40 still occur in essentially every trace in both windows, while A41 now occurs in about 69 in 100 later traces versus about 59 in 100 earlier. [REL027, REL029, REL030]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured: the block of three always-executed activities A21, A22 and A23 that previously ran between A39 and A24 in varying order is in the later window almost never executed, and exactly one of the mutually exclusive activities A14, A15 or A16 now runs in that position, each followed directly by A24. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR007, DIR001, REL001, LAG010]

The volume of qualifying evidence (62 control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected without change) cannot be explained by random sampling, and the hypothesis is shown directly and consistently by several indicator types: participation flipped from 200 of 200 traces to 1 of 200 for the old trio while the new trio rose from unobserved to 60–74 of 200 traces; A39's successor distribution changed with full support in both windows; and the A39-to-A24 distance halved from 4 to 2 event positions, matching the replacement of three intermediate activities by one. [DIR007, LAG010, OCC001, OCC004]

Limitations: zero earlier participation shows only that A14–A16 were not observed before, not that they are genuinely new; and several direction and lag items about the old trio in the later window rest on the single residual trace, so they carry little weight. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows sample a shifted mix of coexisting process variants rather than a modified single process. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG006, OCC001]

Other differences: A41's participation rose from about 59 to about 69 in 100 traces, and it now co-occurs with the new activities (for example with A14 in about 21 in 100 later traces); this is reported separately and is not part of the main hypothesis. [REL030, REL009]
