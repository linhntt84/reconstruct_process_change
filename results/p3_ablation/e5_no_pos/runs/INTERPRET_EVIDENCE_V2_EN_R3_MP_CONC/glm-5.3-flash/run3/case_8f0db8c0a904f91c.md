### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the chain A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 → A26 ran in essentially every case: A19, A25 and A20 each appeared in 200 of 200 traces, A41 led to A19 in about 95 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, A25 led to A20 in about 99 in 100, and A20 led to A26 in about 99.5 in 100. In the later window, each of A19, A25 and A20 appears in only about 53 in 100 traces (106–107 of 200), while A41, A26, A46 and A47 still appear in all traces. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL010]

- The three activities now appear together or not at all: pairwise co-occurrence is about 0.53 and "neither present" about 0.47 in the later window, against co-occurrence in every trace before. Their internal order is unchanged when they do run: A19 precedes A25 and A25 precedes A20 in essentially all joint appearances in both windows. [REL008, REL009, REL014, REL019]

- The skip happens through a direct A41 → A26 connection: A41's outgoing transitions moved from about 95% leading to A19 to a split of roughly 50% to A19 and 44% directly to A26, and the positional distance from A41 to A26, previously 4 steps in 93% of cases, is now 1 step in about 44 in 100 cases and 4 steps in about 49 in 100 — exactly the pattern expected when the three intermediate activities are skipped. [DIR005, LAG002]

- A02 became far rarer: it appeared in 29% of traces before (58 of 200) and in about 8.5% after (17 of 200). The earlier detour A46 → A02 → A47 largely disappeared: A46 led to A02 in about 25% of its outgoing transitions before and under 1% after, A46 → A47 rose from 74.5% to about 96%, and the one-step A46→A47 gap rose from 74.5% to about 96.5% of traces. A02's own successor profile, previously dominated by A47 (about 85%), is diffuse in the later window, but that profile rests on only 17 observations. [OCC001, DIR006, DIR001, LAG003, REL007]

- Two rare activities increased: A04 from about 4.5% to 12% participation and A06 from 4% to about 12.5%, but on small bases (9→24 and 8→25 traces), so these may be sampling noise. [OCC002, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the activity block A19 → A25 → A20, previously a mandatory chain between A41 and A26, became optional: in the later window the block runs in about 53 in 100 cases and is skipped in about 47 in 100, with A41 then leading directly to A26. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the participation of all three activities fell in lockstep from 100% to about 53% with joint presence or joint absence rather than independent drops, a new direct A41→A26 transition appeared at about 44% of A41's outgoing transitions, and the A41→A26 positional distance shortened from typically four steps to one step in about 44 in 100 cases, matching a three-activity skip. The internal order of the block is preserved when it runs, so this is a change in optionality, not a reordering or a replacement of one path by another. The screening context fits: 37 items qualified where fewer than 1 would be expected without a change. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL008, REL009, REL014, DIR005, LAG002]

The evidence cannot show whether the skip reflects a new routing option applied case by case or a shift in the mix of case types reaching this part of the process, and it provides no information on what determines whether the block runs. [DIR005, LAG002]

Other differences: A02's occasional insertion between A46 and A47 became rare (participation 29% → about 8.5%; A46→A02 from about 25% to under 1% of A46's outgoing transitions), a separate participation change that is not part of the main hypothesis; A04 and A06 show small participation increases on weak support.
