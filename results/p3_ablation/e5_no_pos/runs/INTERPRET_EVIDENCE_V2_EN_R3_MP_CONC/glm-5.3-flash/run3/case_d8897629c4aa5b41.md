### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A14, A15 and A20 were not observed at all (0 of 200 traces each); in the later window each occurs at most once per trace and appears in a substantial share of traces — A14 in about 29 in 100 traces (58 of 200), A15 in about 41 in 100 (82 of 200), A20 in about 30 in 100 (59 of 200) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new sequential step was added to the segment A22 → A23 → A24: in the later window, exactly one of the three alternative activities A14, A15 or A20 executes once in almost every trace, inserted either between A22 and A23 (about 56 of 100 traces) or between A23 and A24 (about 44 of 100), whereas earlier these activities were never observed and A22 was always followed directly by A23, which was always followed directly by A24 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003].

The support is direct and internally consistent: participation rises from zero for all three activities, the incoming and outgoing edges of the inserted step are observed explicitly, the order relations match insertion on either side of A23, and every pair distance shifts by exactly the one step the new activity contributes. The qualified-item count (23 against about 0.37 expected without change) is far beyond what an unchanged process produces, and the quantities interlock — the 56%/44% split in A22's and A23's outgoing transitions matches the lag shifts and the new activities' combined coverage of about 199 of 200 traces [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL001, REL002, REL006].

Limitations: the evidence covers only the neighbourhood of these activities, so it cannot show whether the choice among A14, A15 and A20, or between the two insertion positions, depends on conditions elsewhere in the process. It also cannot rule out that A14, A15 and A20 already existed but were not exercised in the earlier sample, since zero participation strictly means only "not observed" — although with 200 traces and coherent relational evidence a sampling artefact is unlikely [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].
