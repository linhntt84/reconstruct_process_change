### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 each ran in about a third of the cases (roughly 36, 31 and 35 in 100 traces) and never together in the same trace, so essentially every case executed exactly one of the three; in the later window A19 and A25 appear in 0 of 200 traces and A20 in only 1. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL007, REL009, REL014]
- Earlier, each of the three led almost invariably to A26 (about 97–99 in 100 of their outgoing transitions), and A41 led to one of the three in about 93 in 100 of its outgoing transitions; in the later window, A41 leads directly to A26 in about 92 in 100 of its outgoing transitions. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- The A41→A26 distance shortened accordingly: earlier, A26 followed A41 two positions later in about 94 in 100 cases; in the later window it immediately follows A41 in about 92 in 100. Both activities occur in essentially every case in both windows. [LAG002, DIR004, REL010]
- Relation items agree throughout: co-occurrence of A19, A20 or A25 with their earlier neighbours (A14, A12, A24, A26, A31, A41) fell from roughly 15–36 in 100 traces to zero, while A14, A26 and A41 still occur in (nearly) every trace. [REL004, REL008, REL010, REL019, REL021]
- Secondary shifts: the A24→A31 distance moved from mostly two positions (about 59 in 100) to mostly one (about 59 in 100); A24 occurs in about 56 in 100 later traces versus about 48 earlier, and A12 in about 51 versus about 57. [LAG001, REL008, REL013, REL001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative step between A41 and A26 — previously executed as exactly one of A19, A20 or A25 in essentially every case — is no longer executed in the later window, so cases proceed directly from A41 to A26. It rests on direct participation evidence for all three activities (about 30–36 in 100 cases earlier versus 0–1 of 200 later), the flip of A41's successor profile from the three activities to A26, the shortened A41→A26 distance, and about thirty consistent control-flow items against fewer than one expected without change, which sampling variation cannot explain as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, LAG002, REL010]

Limitations: zero participation only means the activities were not observed; one later trace still contains A20 (there followed by A30), so a rare residual occurrence or a replacement activity cannot be excluded, but that after-profile rests on a single trace. The evidence also cannot distinguish whether the step was removed outright or is now skipped on every path. [OCC002, DIR002]

Other differences: the A24→A31 distance shifted one position earlier, and the shares of A24 (about 48→56 in 100) and A12 (about 57→51 in 100) moved modestly; these are plausibly knock-on effects of the removed step, but the evidence does not show how they arise. [LAG001, REL008, REL001]
