### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports, as a possibility, a structural change in the segment following A44: in the earlier window, A44 — present in every case — was followed by exactly one of A19, A20, A21 or A10, with A29 following about two positions later; in the later window, A44 is followed by A26, A27 and A28, which run together in every case in variable order before A29 about four positions later, while A19, A20 and A21 were no longer observed and A10 rarely follows A44. [DIR009, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL031, REL033, REL034, REL042, LAG005]

Supporting signs: the successor change at A44 is shown directly rather than inferred from frequencies alone; the occurrence, direction, relation and distance indicators all point to the same activities; the shift of the A44-to-A29 distance from about two to about four positions matches the three new activities running between them; and 66 qualifying items against roughly 0.76 expected without a change (about 90 times the expectation) means sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [DIR009, LAG005, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]

Limitations and competing explanation: zero participation only shows that an activity was not observed in a window, so it cannot be ruled out that A19, A20 and A21 still run in a share of cases too small to appear in 200 traces rather than having disappeared; the later-window routing profiles of these three activities each rest on a single trace and were not used; and many items describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
