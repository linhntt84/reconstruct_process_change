### Observed differences

- The three activities A19, A20 and A25 were effectively absent from the earlier window (A19 and A25 in 0 of 200 traces, A20 in 1 of 200); in the later window each runs in roughly a third of traces — A19 in about 38 in 100, A25 in about 35 in 100, A20 in about 30 in 100 — and essentially never more than once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A41's hand-over changed: earlier, about 82 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions went straight to A26; later, about 87 in 100 go to A19 (about 33), A25 (about 30) or A20 (about 24), and only about 2 in 100 still go directly to A26. [DIR004]
- The new activities sit between A41 and A26: in the later window, each of them co-occurs with A41 before it and with A26 after it in essentially every trace in which it appears (for example, A19 follows A41 in about 38 in 100 traces and precedes A26 in about 38 in 100), and A19 and A25 in turn lead almost always to A26 (about 95 and 90 in 100 of their outgoing transitions). [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, DIR002, DIR003]
- The three new activities behave mostly as alternatives: each pair shares a trace in about 1 in 100 cases or fewer, so typically exactly one of them runs per trace. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- The event distance from A41 to A26 moved from one step in about 82 in 100 earlier traces (two steps in about 18) to two steps in about 83 in 100 later traces, with three steps in about 14. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a step is inserted between A41 and A26: one of the previously unobserved activities A19, A20 or A25 — largely as mutually exclusive alternatives — now executes after A41 and before A26 in nearly every trace, whereas earlier A41 passed almost directly to A26. [DIR004, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001]

Supporting signs: the appearance of the three activities from zero participation is each backed by 59–76 of 200 traces; A41's redirection is measured on about 200 outgoing transitions in each window; the order relations are observed directly (A41 before each new activity, each new activity before A26, and direct succession from the new activities to A26) rather than inferred from positions alone; and the shift of the A41–A26 distance from one to mostly two steps matches an inserted step. With 17 qualifying items against roughly 0.49 expected for an unchanged process, chance alone cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR002, LAG001, REL003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish whether A19, A20 and A25 are three alternative steps or variants of one step recorded under different labels, and "absent" in the earlier window strictly means only that they were not observed there; in the few traces where two of them co-occur (about 1–2 in 100) their arrangement remains unresolved. [REL001, REL002, REL005, OCC001]

Other differences: the successor mix of A02 changed — A26, earlier about 26 in 100 of A02's outgoing transitions, is no longer observed as its successor, and small shares now go to A19, A20 and A25; this rests on only about 35 and 44 outgoing transitions, so it could also be sampling noise. [DIR001]
