### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained A39 and A24 with exactly one of three alternative activities in between: A14 ran in about 35 in 100 traces, A15 in about 30, and A16 in about 36. These three never occurred in the same trace, and the chosen activity ran after A39 and immediately before A24. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR007, REL001, REL002, REL008, REL006, REL012, REL017]
- In the later window, A14, A15 and A16 are not observed at all (0 of 200 traces, down from 70, 59 and 71 traces), while A21, A22 and A23, previously unobserved, each run exactly once in every trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- A39's outgoing transitions switched entirely: before, they led only to A14, A15 or A16 (roughly 35, 30 and 36 in 100 observed transitions); after, they lead to A21, A22 or A23 (roughly 36, 33 and 32 in 100). [DIR007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- In the later window, A21, A22 and A23 always occur together in the same trace and appear in both orders in near-equal shares (for example, A21 before A22 in about 51 in 100 traces and A22 before A21 in about 49 in 100), so they run in no fixed order among themselves; A39 precedes each of them, and each of them precedes A24 in every trace. [REL019, REL020, REL023, REL022, REL025, REL027, REL021, REL024, REL026]
- As a result, the position gap from A39 to A24 grew from 2 steps in the earlier window to 4 steps in the later one. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural replacement in the segment between A39 and A24: in the earlier window exactly one of the alternatives A14, A15 or A16 executed per trace between A39 and A24, while in the later window that choice was replaced by a block in which A21, A22 and A23 each execute once per trace in no fixed order, still preceded by A39 and followed by A24. This is a replacement of activities rather than a shift in how often existing paths are taken, since the old alternatives are entirely absent in the later window and the new ones entirely absent in the earlier one. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR007, REL019, REL021, REL022, LAG001]

The screening produced 41 qualifying control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected if nothing had changed, so chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole. The occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators all describe one consistent story, and the doubling of the A39-to-A24 distance from 2 to 4 positions matches a single activity being replaced by three. [DIR007, LAG001]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is an extreme shift in the mix of two pre-existing variants rather than a wholesale replacement: a variant present at only a very small share (below roughly 1–2 in 200 traces) would go unobserved in a 200-trace window. The evidence also shows only that A21, A22 and A23 have no fixed mutual order, not the exact routing logic between them. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL019, REL020, REL023]
