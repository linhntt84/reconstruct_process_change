### Observed differences

- The most prominent change involves A19, A20 and A25. In the earlier window each appeared in every trace (participation 100%, about one occurrence per trace); in the later window each appears in only about 53–54 in 100 traces, still about once per trace when present. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment A19 → A20, with A25 executed before A20, is skipped in the later window in about 46 in 100 cases, whereas it was executed in every earlier-window case; when it runs, its internal order and its position between A14/A15/A41 upstream and A26/A46/A47 downstream are unchanged. The signs are direct and consistent: all three activities fall together from universal to about 53–54% participation, the neighbouring activities now regularly appear without them, A41 gains a direct transition to A26, and the A41→A26 distance shortens by three steps, matching removal of exactly that block. Chance is also an unlikely overall explanation: 56 control-flow items qualified against an expectation of 0.83 items without a change. The evidence cannot rule out that the later window simply samples a different mix of pre-existing variants (for instance a variant without this segment) rather than a within-process modification, and many items describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL016, REL022, DIR008, LAG008]

Other differences: A06 changed from a usual step between A14 and A15 (about 71 in 100 traces) to a mostly bypassed one (about 26 in 100); this reduction appears independent of the segment skip, since A06 and A19 co-occur in about 14 in 100 later traces, close to what their separate frequencies imply. A04, A08 and A10 participate more often, though these rest on few observations (about 10–40 traces per window). [OCC002, REL003, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]
