- Six activities — A33, A35, A36, A37, A38, A39 — each ran in about 49–53 in 100 cases in the earlier window but in about 98–100 in 100 cases in the later window, with repetition staying negligible in both. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the two previously alternative branches are executed together as one mandatory sequence: cases that earlier ran either the triple A33/A35/A36 or the triple A37/A38/A39 (roughly 50/50) now run both triples back-to-back — A33/A35/A36 in no fixed internal order, then A37/A38/A39, likewise unordered — in the position between A30 upstream and A31 downstream, with A14 preceding and A15 accompanying the block. This is shown directly by the flip of cross-triple pairs from near-exclusive alternatives (co-occurrence about 1 in 100) to near-universal co-occurrence (about 98 in 100), corroborated by the participation rise of all six activities, the rerouting of successors from A31 into the second triple, and the lengthened position gaps; with 98 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 1 expected without change, chance sampling cannot account for the ensemble. Limitations: a few percent of later traces still miss parts of the block, the mixed internal ordering means the merged block may comprise several orderings rather than one fixed sequence, and a low-rate survival of old single-branch traces cannot be excluded. The main competing reading — that only the take-up frequencies of pre-existing paths shifted — does not fit, because cross-triple pairs were almost never observed together before; what remains unresolved is whether any old-variant traces persist at a small rate. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC008, OCC010, REL039, REL045, REL050, REL025, REL031, DIR007, LAG005, LAG008]

Other differences: A04's decline to a rare, block-accompanied activity with diversified successors [OCC002, REL005, DIR002]; the increased presence and changed successors of A02, A08 and A10 [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR003]; A14's transitions shifting from A04 towards A15 [DIR004]; and the slightly longer A47→A48 spacing [LAG015]. The evidence does not tie these to the branch-merging behaviour, and their independence from it cannot be determined.
