### Observed differences

- A14, A15 and A20 do not appear at all in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces) but appear in the later window: A14 in about 37 in 100 traces, A20 in about 34 in 100, and A15 in about 29 in 100. Taken together, one of the three runs in about 99 in 100 later traces (198 of 200), each at most once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the later window, every observed outgoing transition of each of the three activities leads to A21; in the earlier window these activities had no observed outgoing transitions at all. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- A36's outgoing behaviour changed: in the earlier window all of its observed outgoing transitions went to A21, whereas in the later window about 37 in 100 go to A14, about 34 to A20, about 29 to A15, and only about 1 in 100 still go directly to A21. [DIR004]
- The three activities behave as mutually exclusive alternatives: in the later window no trace contains more than one of them (co-occurrence is zero for every pair), while in the earlier window neither member of any of these pairs was observed. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- Their placement is fixed: A36 precedes each of A14, A15 and A20, and each of them precedes A21 in every trace where they occur together. Accordingly, A21 now follows A36 two positions later in about 99 in 100 traces, whereas it immediately followed A36 in all 200 earlier traces. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window a new intermediate step is executed between A36 and A21: after A36, exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities — A14, A15 or A20 — is carried out (in a roughly 37/34/29 split), and A21 follows it, whereas in the earlier window A36 led directly to A21 in every trace. The placement is shown directly by the relational and directional evidence (A36 before each new activity, each new activity before A21, and A36's transitions shifting away from A21), and it agrees with the occurrence and lag indicators; the three activities never occur together, so they act as alternatives rather than a sequence. The volume of qualifying evidence (17 items against roughly 0.34 expected if nothing had changed) makes a chance origin of the whole pattern implausible, and a step appearing in about 99 of 100 later traces after 0 of 200 earlier traces is far beyond a rare-variant fluctuation. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly establishes only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed there, not that they were absent from the process — this competing explanation is very unlikely given 200 earlier traces, but it cannot be fully excluded. In addition, about 1 in 100 later traces still go directly from A36 to A21, so the new step is optional rather than mandatory, and the evidence cannot indicate what determines the choice among the three alternatives. [OCC001, DIR004, LAG001]
