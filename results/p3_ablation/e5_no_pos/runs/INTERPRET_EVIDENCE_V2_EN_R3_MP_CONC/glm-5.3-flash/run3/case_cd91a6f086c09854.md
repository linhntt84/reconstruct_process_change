### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the group of three activities A30, A31 and A36 was executed at a different place in the sequence: in the earlier window it ran between A20 and A22 (A20, then the three in mixed order, then A22, with A21 later and A25 directly after A21), whereas in the later window A22 directly follows A20, A21 precedes the group, and the group runs between A21 and A25 (A20 → A22 → A21 → the three activities → A25). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG004]

Supporting signs: the change is shown directly by relational evidence (order reversals in about 99–100 in 100 traces for all six pairs), by positional evidence (A20→A22 gap 4→1; A21→A25 gap 1→4) and by successor changes at A20, A21 and the group's exits; the key items each rest on about 200 traces per window, and 17 items qualified against roughly 0.38 expected for an unchanged process, far more than chance would produce. [REL001, REL004, LAG001, LAG004, DIR001, DIR002]

Limitations: the 17 items overlap heavily and largely re-describe one and the same change in ordering, so they are not independent confirmations; the mutual order of A22 and A21 is inferred from the gap constraints rather than observed directly; and the residual of about 2 in 100 later traces with the earlier ordering is documented only by items with 2 supporting traces. A competing explanation that cannot be fully excluded is a rare pre-existing variant with the new order that surfaced only in the later sample, although the earlier window shows the earlier order in 100 in 100 of 200 traces. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG005, LAG006, REL001]
