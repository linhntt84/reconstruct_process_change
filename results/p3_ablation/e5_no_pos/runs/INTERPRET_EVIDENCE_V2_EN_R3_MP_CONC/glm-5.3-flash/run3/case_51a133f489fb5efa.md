### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the ordering and routing around A19, A20 and A25 was restructured. In the earlier window, A26 and A27 typically ran before A19/A20/A25, which then flowed into A28 or A32 (with A28 often leading back into A19/A20/A25); in the later window, A19, A20 and A25 each run almost always in the step immediately before A26, usually after A08, while A27, A28 and A32 form a separate fixed sequence A27→A28→A32. A08 now participates in about 30 in 100 traces (previously about 13) and typically precedes A19/A20/A25, and A45 now leads into A08/A19/A20/A25 instead of A26. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, OCC001, REL005, REL007, REL009, LAG007, LAG010, LAG013]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor profiles of A19, A20, A25, A27, A28 and A45 changed near-completely, six activity-pair order relations flipped in the same direction at roughly stable co-occurrence levels, and the new adjacencies are observed at one step with substantial backing (A19→A26 at one step in 69 later-window observations after appearing only once before; A27→A28 and A28→A32 at one step in about 200 each; A08→A19 and A08→A26 at one and two steps in 22 and 53 observations). In total, 34 control-flow items qualified where about 0.67 would be expected by chance for the 134 tested units, far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, LAG001, LAG002, LAG007, LAG010, LAG013]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows differ in the mix of case variants (one execution pattern becoming dominant) rather than a repositioning within a single process, and DIR shares are computed over observed outgoing transitions rather than cases. A few lag items rest on only one or two observations in one window and were not relied upon. [LAG001, LAG008, LAG012]

Other differences: the distance from A16 and A18 to A26 lengthened — A16→A26 moved from about three steps (about 54 in 100 cases) to four–five steps, and A18→A26 from two steps (about 92 in 100) to three–four steps — consistent with the rerouted block now sitting before A26. The outgoing profiles of A48 and A50 and the A18→A45 gap stayed essentially unchanged (observed changes of about 0.002 or less). [LAG004, LAG005, DIR008, DIR009, LAG006]
