### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the exclusive branch after A20 flipped its preferred path in the later window: the path in which A21, A22 and A23 execute together, preceded by A20 and usually accompanied by A30 (and often A32/A33), fell from about 75 in 100 traces to about 25 in 100, while the alternative path in which A24, A25 and A26 execute together rose from about 25 in 100 to about 75 in 100, with A30 and A32/A33 now accompanying the latter branch instead. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, REL007, REL010, REL013, REL015, REL018, REL036, DIR003]

This reads as a shift in how often two existing paths are taken rather than paths being added, removed or reordered: all six activities and both trios are observed in a substantial share of traces in both windows, the trios remain mutually exclusive with unchanged internal mixed ordering and A20-first precedence, occurrence, relation and direct-successor evidence agree, and the 60 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.9 expected by chance among the 172 tested units, so sampling noise cannot account for the ensemble. [DIR003, REL013, REL015, REL034]

The evidence cannot show where the routing decision itself sits or rule out that an upstream condition whose distribution changed selects the branch, rather than the choice point after A20 itself; the changed company of A30 and A32/A33 rests on co-occurrence, not on direct successor data for those activities. [REL018, REL036, REL019]

Other differences: A10 roughly tripled its participation (about 5 to about 21 in 100 traces) and now sends about 90 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A33, often after the A24–A26 block; A02 was observed in only about 1 in 100 later traces (2 of 200 versus 18 before), too few to interpret; A32's transitions shifted mildly from A33 towards A10; and observed position gaps between activity pairs widened slightly. These are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC002, DIR002, OCC001, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
