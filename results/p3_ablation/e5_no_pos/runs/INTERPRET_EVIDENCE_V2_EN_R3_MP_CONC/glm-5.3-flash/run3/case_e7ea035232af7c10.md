### Observed differences

- Participation: in the earlier window, A27 ran in about 54 in 100 traces and A36, A37 and A42 in about 46 in 100 each; in the later window, each of these four activities runs in essentially every trace (199–200 of 200), exactly once, with no repetition in either window [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004].
- Exclusivity became joint execution: earlier, A27 practically never shared a trace with A36, A37 or A42 (co-occurrence 0–0.5 in 100); in the later window A27 co-occurs with each of them in about 995 in 1000 traces, with the group member preceding A27 in about 99 in 100 of them [REL011, REL012, REL013]. A25 likewise precedes A27 and each group member in essentially every later trace, where earlier it did so in about 46–54 in 100 [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004].
- Order reversals around the branch: earlier, A26 preceded A36, A37 and A42 (about 46 in 100 traces) and A28 preceded them (about 46 in 100); in the later window each group member precedes A26 in about 99 in 100 traces and precedes A28 in about 99 in 100 [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016].
- Relations retained but now unconditional: A28 before A27, A27 before A31, and the group before A31 already held earlier, but only in the roughly half of traces where the activities co-occurred; in the later window they hold in about 99–100 in 100 traces [REL009, REL010, REL017, REL018, REL019].
- Routing: A25 sent about 98 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A28 earlier; in the later window it splits roughly a third each to A36 (35%), A37 (32%) and A42 (32%), with A28 nearly gone [DIR001]. A26 narrowed from four targets (A27 about 54 in 100, the group together about 45 in 100) to A27 alone (about 99 in 100) [DIR002]. A36, A37 and A42 earlier passed to A31 in about 29–39 in 100 of their outgoing transitions; in the later window they pass to A28 in about 30–36 in 100 and no longer to A31 [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005].
- Distances: A25 to A28 moved from adjacent (within 1 step in about 99 in 100 traces) to 4 steps apart (about 97 in 100) [LAG001]. A26 to A31 became uniformly 2 steps (about 97 in 100), where earlier it was a mix of 2 steps (54 in 100) and 4 steps (44 in 100) [LAG003]. A25→A36 and A36→A28, earlier within five steps in only 1 trace each, now sit 1–3 steps apart in roughly a third of cases each [LAG002, LAG004]. A37→A31 and A42→A31 within five steps fell from about 92 traces to 1 [LAG005, LAG006].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural restructuring of the executed order: the two segments that were alternatives in the earlier window — an A27 segment reached from A26 (A26 → A27 → A31) and an A36/A37/A42 segment, also entered from A26, in which the three activities each ran once in varying order before handing over to A31 — are in the later window executed together in every trace, in the arrangement A25 into A36/A37/A42 (in varying order), then A28, with A26 leading almost exclusively to A27 and A27 → A31 closing the trace. Earlier, about 54 in 100 traces took the A27 segment and about 46 in 100 the group segment, never both; later, A27, A36, A37 and A42 each occur once in essentially every trace [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013].

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: the co-occurrence and order flips are observed within traces (A27 with each group member from about 0 to about 99.5 in 100, group before A27) [REL011, REL012, REL013]; the rerouting at A25, A26 and the group members matches the same arrangement [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]; and the position shifts corroborate it — A25 to A28 stretching from 1 to 4 steps shows the group was inserted between them, and A26 to A31 becoming uniformly 2 steps fits an always-executed A26 → A27 → A31 [LAG001, LAG003]. The 37 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.66 expected for an unchanged process, so sampling noise cannot account for the pattern as a whole, although many items describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations [screening].

Limitations: the evidence describes executed traces, not the underlying model, so it cannot distinguish a merged branch from a change in which process variant runs — for example, the earlier window may mix two coexisting variants (about 54/46) while the later window is dominated by a single variant containing all activities. The exact placement of A26 relative to A28 is only inferred indirectly, as no A28–A26 relation was measured [REL005, REL009, DIR002].

Other differences: none beyond components of this restructuring; the order inside the A36/A37/A42 group remains unfixed in both windows (each pair appears in both orders in roughly equal shares, about half each), so no fixed sequence emerged within the group [REL020, REL021, REL022].
