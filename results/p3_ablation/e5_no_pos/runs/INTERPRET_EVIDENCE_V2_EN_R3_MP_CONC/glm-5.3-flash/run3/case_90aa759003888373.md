### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block A14 → A20 → A15 sitting between A36 and A21 became optional: in the later window, roughly 47 in 100 traces run A36 and then A21 directly, bypassing all three activities, while the remaining ~53 in 100 still execute the full sequence A36 → A14 → A20 → A15 → A21 in its original order. This is shown directly by the new A36→A21 transition (about 47% of A36's outgoing flow), by the participation of A14, A15 and A20 dropping from 100% to about 53% while A36 and A21 stay at 100%, and by the A36-to-A21 distance splitting between 1 and 4 positions; the trio's unchanged internal order when it does run fits the same picture. The volume of qualifying evidence (14 items against roughly 0.33 expected by chance among the 66 tested control-flow units) cannot be explained by random sampling as a whole. An important competing explanation the windows cannot rule out is that this direct A36→A21 path already existed before at a rate too low to appear in 200 traces and simply grew; behaviourally the later window looks the same either way.
