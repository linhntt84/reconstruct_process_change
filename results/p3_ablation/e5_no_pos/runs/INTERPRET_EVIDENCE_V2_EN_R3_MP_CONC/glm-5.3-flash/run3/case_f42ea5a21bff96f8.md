### Observed differences

- Two alternative activity blocks swapped how often they run. In the earlier window, A21, A22 and A23 each appeared in about 75 in 100 traces and A24, A25 and A26 in about 25 in 100; in the later window this is reversed, with A21–A23 at about 25 in 100 and A24–A26 at about 76 in 100. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009]
- The switch sits at the same upstream point. A20 occurs in virtually every trace in both windows; before, its outgoing transitions led to A21, A22 or A23 in about 75 in 100 cases and to A24, A25 or A26 in about 25, while in the later window the shares are about 24 and 75. A19 shows the same flip: it preceded A21–A23 in about 73–75 in 100 traces earlier and precedes A24–A26 in about 75 in 100 later. [DIR002, REL013, REL016, REL019, REL022]
- Each block keeps its internal shape. A21–A23 still occur together whenever they occur (co-occurrence about 25 in 100 later, matching their participation, versus 75 in 100 before), A24–A26 now occur together in about 75 in 100 traces (versus 25 in 100 before), and the two blocks hardly ever share a trace in either window. [REL025, REL026, REL027, REL034, REL049, REL055]
- The connections around the block moved with it. Before, A29 preceded A21–A23 (about 73 in 100) and A21–A23 preceded A34 and A35 (about 73 in 100); in the later window A29 precedes A24–A26 (about 73 in 100) and A24–A26 precede A34 and A35 (about 73–76 in 100), while A34 and A35 now usually run without A21–A23 (about 75 in 100). A30 runs partly interleaved with the block in both windows. [REL030, REL031, REL032, REL051, REL052, REL053]
- A06 became a frequent step funnelling into A35. Its participation rose from about 17 in 100 traces to about 53 in 100; its outgoing flow, previously spread over many activities (largest single share about 21 in 100), now goes to A35 in about 90 in 100 transitions, and A35 follows A06 within one step in about 97 in 100 of the later co-occurrences (the earlier gap distribution rests on only 16 observed pairs). [OCC002, DIR001, LAG001]
- A34's routing changed accordingly: before it led to A35 in about 89 in 100 transitions; in the later window it splits about evenly between A35 (48 in 100) and A06 (50 in 100), and the A34-to-A35 distance moved from mostly one step (90 in 100) to mostly two steps (51 in 100), with A34 preceding A06 and A06 preceding A35 in roughly 46–49 in 100 traces each — a pattern consistent with A06 sitting between A34 and A35 in about half of the later cases. [DIR005, LAG013, REL011, REL012]
- Two minor participations dropped: A02 from about 17 to about 7.5 in 100 traces and A08 from about 13.5 to about 5 in 100. [OCC001, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the frequencies of two existing alternative blocks flipped: in the later window, about three in four cases run the block A24–A26 (previously about one in four) where they previously ran A21–A23 (now about one in four), with A19/A20 leading into whichever block is selected, A29 ahead of it and A34/A35 after it. The screening produced about 90 qualifying items where roughly 1 would be expected without a change, so chance alone cannot explain the set; the routing change at A20, the participation flips and the matching co-occurrence and ordering flips on both sides of the blocks show the same picture directly and consistently, although the many relation items largely re-express one underlying behaviour rather than confirming it independently. What the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows capture a shifted mix of coexisting case variants rather than a changed routing rule, and it does not show what determines the choice upstream of A19/A20. [OCC004, OCC007, DIR002, REL013, REL016, REL025, REL051, REL053]

Other differences: A06 rose from about 17 to about 53 in 100 traces and now leads to A35 in about 90 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (within one step in about 97 in 100), while A34 splits its onward flow about evenly between A35 and A06 and the A34-to-A35 distance moved from one step to two — consistent with A06 being placed between A34 and A35 in about half of the later cases, though the evidence shows the relations themselves, not the insertion. A02 and A08 participation roughly halved. [OCC002, DIR001, DIR005, LAG001, LAG013, REL011, REL012, OCC001, OCC003]
