### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Main hypothesis: in the later window, the segment between A21 and A23 has an alternative branch. In about half of the traces, A22 is skipped and a block of the three previously unobserved activities A27, A28 and A33 is executed between A21 and A23, each once and in varying order; in the other half, the original A21 → A22 → A23 sequence is retained unchanged. The two options are strictly mutually exclusive. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL012, REL013, REL014]

Supporting signs: the participation changes are direct and large (A22 from all 200 traces to 102; A27/A28/A33 from 0 to 98 each); the routing evidence shows A21 now leads to A22 in about half of its outgoing transitions and into the new block in the rest, with the block exiting to A23; the relational items place the new activities after A21 and before A23; and the positional gap from A21 to A23 widens from 2 to 4 positions exactly in the traces without A22. In total 23 items qualified against about 0.37 expected by chance for 74 tested units, far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces, and the items describe one coherent structural difference rather than scattered effects. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, REL002, REL009, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that A27, A28 and A33 existed before as a rare path that the earlier 200-trace sample simply did not capture — zero observations make a substantial earlier frequency unlikely but do not prove the activities were newly introduced. The evidence also cannot show what determines which of the two options a case takes, since no case attributes are included. [OCC002, DIR001]
