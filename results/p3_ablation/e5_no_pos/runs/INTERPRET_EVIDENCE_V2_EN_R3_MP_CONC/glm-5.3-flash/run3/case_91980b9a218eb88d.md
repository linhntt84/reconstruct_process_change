### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A45 was followed by one of A25 (about 35% of its outgoing transitions), A19 (about 35%) or A20 (about 24%), and each of the three led almost exclusively to A26 (about 92–99%); A45 reached A26 two positions later in about 93 in 100 traces. In the later window, A45 leads directly to A26 (about 94% of its outgoing transitions, at the very next position in about 94 in 100 traces), and A19, A20 and A25 precede A26 in only about 0–0.5% of traces. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, LAG010, LAG011]
- The order between these three activities and A26 reversed: earlier A19 preceded A26 in about 36% of traces, A20 in about 24% and A25 in about 38%, with the reverse order almost unobserved; in the later window A26 precedes A19 in about 31% of traces, A20 in about 37% and A25 in about 29%, while the old order has nearly disappeared. A26 itself occurs in every trace in both windows. [REL001, REL003, REL006]
- The A27–A28–A32 chain gained steps: earlier A27 led to A28 in about 98% of its outgoing transitions, A28 led to A32 in about 99%, and A27 reached A32 typically two positions later (about 97%). In the later window, A27 also leads to A19 (about 16%), A20 (about 15%) and A25 (about 11%); A28 leads to A32 in only about 45% of cases and to A19, A20 or A25 in about 53%; A27 now reaches A32 typically three positions later (about 93%) and A28 two positions later (about 54%). [DIR004, DIR005, LAG005, LAG006, LAG009]
- Close successions observed in the later window but almost absent earlier include A27→A19, A27→A20, A28→A19 and A28→A20 (1–2 traces before versus 32–75 after, mostly one or two positions apart) and A19→A32 (1 trace before versus about 65 after); the earlier A45→A25 adjacency survives in only 1 later trace versus about 76 before. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010]
- Some pairs lost their fixed order: earlier A20 and A25 essentially always preceded A28 (about 24% and 38% versus 0%); in the later window both orders occur in similar proportions (A28 before A20 about 21% versus 16%; A28 before A25 about 17% versus 12%). [REL005, REL008]
- A24 occurs in about 57 in 100 later traces (115 of 200) versus about 43 in 100 earlier (85 of 200), still at most once per trace. [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment formed by A19, A20 and A25 was re-attached to a different part of the process: in the earlier window it ran between A45 and A26 (A45 → one of the three → A26), while in the later window A45 leads directly to A26 and the three activities instead follow A27 and A28 and precede A32, in varying order. This is stated as a possibility supported by the evidence, not as a confirmed modification. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL001, REL003, REL005, REL006, LAG006, LAG011]

The hypothesis rests on direct relational evidence: the successor profiles of A19, A20, A25, A27, A28 and A45 all changed consistently with the re-attachment (A26 fell from about 92–99% to about 0–2% of the three activities' outgoing transitions), the order relations with A26 flipped, and adjacencies appeared and disappeared exactly where the old and new paths predict. The 26 qualifying items (all control-flow) are roughly forty-five times the number expected by chance in an unchanged process (about 0.58), so the ensemble is unlikely to be a sampling artefact; the items are mutually consistent, though several describe the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a single relocation of the segment from two separate edits (removing it between A45 and A26 while separately inserting it after A28); several lag items rest on only 1–2 observations in one window (for example A19→A26 within five steps in only 2 later traces), although each is corroborated by direction or relation items with full support; and the exact internal sequence of the three activities in the later window is only partly resolved, with mixed orders around A28. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG010, REL005, REL008]

Other differences: A24 participates in a larger share of traces in the later window (about 57 in 100 versus about 43 in 100); the evidence does not connect this to the re-attachment above. [OCC001]
