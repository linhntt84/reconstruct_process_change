### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the flow ran A27 → A28 → A29 in essentially every trace: A27's outgoing transitions went to A28 in about 99 in 100 cases, A28's went to A29 in about 99 in 100, and A29 followed A27 two positions later in about 98–99 in 100 traces. [DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- In the later window, A19, A20 and A25 — none of which was observed before (0 of 200 traces) — appear in about 30, 41 and 30 in 100 traces respectively (59, 82 and 60 of 200), and they practically never occur together, so almost every trace now contains exactly one of the three. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL002, REL006]
- The new activities sit between A27 and A29: A27 precedes them in about 97–100 in 100 of their co-occurrences, and they precede A29 in essentially all of theirs; relative to A28 the order is mixed (new activity before A28 in roughly 16–22 in 100 traces, after it in roughly 13–17 in 100). [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- A27 and A28 now branch: A27 leads to A28 in about 44 in 100 cases and to A20/A25/A19 in about 22/17/15; A28 leads to A29 in about 55 in 100 cases and to A20/A19/A25 in about 17/13/12; the new activities in turn lead to A28 (about 53–56 in 100) or directly to A29 (about 42–46 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- Position gaps shifted accordingly: the A27→A29 distance moved from 2 positions (about 98 in 100) to 3 positions (about 97 in 100), and the A27→A28 and A28→A29 distances split between 1 and 2 positions (about 44/55 and 56/44 in 100). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- A minor difference outside this pattern: A10's trace participation rose from about 5 in 100 to about 13–14 in 100 traces (10 vs 27 traces). [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window a step — executed as exactly one of the three mutually exclusive activities A19, A20 or A25 — is inserted between A27 and A29 in practically every trace, whereas before, all traces went directly A27 → A28 → A29 with none of the three observed. The inserted activity is placed before A28 in about 55 in 100 traces (A27 → new activity → A28 → A29) and after A28 in about 43 in 100 (A27 → A28 → new activity → A29). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Supporting signs: the control-flow family produced 24 qualifying items against roughly 0.5 expected by chance, so the set as a whole is far beyond what an unchanged process typically yields; the relational and directional evidence shows the change directly (the new activities are always preceded by A27 and always precede A29, A27 and A28 gained new outgoing edges, and the A27→A29 gap grew from 2 to 3 positions); the occurrence, direction, relation and gap items tell one coherent story. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG002, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly means only that A19, A20 and A25 were not observed there, so whether they are entirely new activities or previously unsampled rare ones cannot be decided; the many agreeing items largely describe the same underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations; and the gap evidence concerns event positions rather than clock time. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, LAG002, LAG003]

Other differences: A10's participation increased from about 5 to about 13 in 100 traces, but this rests on only 10 versus 27 traces and is not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001]
