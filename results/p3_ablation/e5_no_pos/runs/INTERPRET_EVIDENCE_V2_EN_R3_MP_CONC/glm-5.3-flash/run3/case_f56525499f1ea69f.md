### Observed differences

- The six activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 each appeared in about half of the traces in the earlier window (participation 48–52%, essentially once per trace when present) and in about 99 in 100 traces in the later window, still once per trace. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009]

- In the earlier window these six split into two mutually exclusive triples: every trace contained exactly one of {A33, A35, A36} or {A37, A38, A39} (e.g., A33 without A37 in about 49 in 100 traces, A37 without A33 in about 52 in 100, never both, never neither). In the later window, every cross-pair between the triples occurs together in about 98 in 100 traces, with the A33/A35/A36 side first in about 97 in 100 of those. [REL039, REL040, REL041, REL043, REL044, REL045, REL046, REL047, REL048]

- Pairs inside each triple moved from "together in about half of the traces, neither in about half" to "together in about 99 in 100"; their internal order stayed mixed (both orders in similar shares) in both windows. [REL037, REL038, REL042, REL049, REL050, REL051]

- Follow-on edges were rewired: in the earlier window A33, A35 and A36 each passed about 30–36% of their outgoing transitions to A31; in the later window these edges are near zero and new transitions to A37, A38 and A39 (about 8–12% each) appear. A30's outgoing traffic shifted from being spread over all six activities (about 14–18% each) to A35, A33 and A36 (about 30–37% each), with A37/A38/A39 close to zero. [DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009]

- Event distances moved consistently with this: A31 now follows A33, A35 or A36 at a distance of 4–5 events (earlier mostly 1–3), and A37/A38/A39 now follow A30 at a distance of 4–5 events (earlier 1–3). [LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

- Activities that occur in every trace in both windows (A14, A15, A16, A30, A31) co-occurred with the six-activity block in only about half of the earlier traces and in about 99 in 100 later traces; for A15 and A16, the share of traces where they interleave with the block rose from about 15–18% to about 40%. [REL007, REL013, REL019, REL025, REL031]

- A04 became rarer and tied to the block: its participation fell from about 31% to about 14% of traces; it now occurs almost only alongside the block (A04 without the block fell from about 17% to 0%), and the share of A04's outgoing transitions going to A16 fell from about 81% to about 36%, spreading to A15 (about 18%) and A48 (about 14%). [OCC001, DIR002, REL001, REL004]

- Smaller shifts: A06 rose from about 3% to 11% and A10 from about 2.5% to 12% participation; the outgoing profiles of A02 and A08 changed almost completely, but on few observations (13 → 23 and 9 → 28 transitions). [OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a mandatory either-or choice between the sub-path A33–A35–A36 and the sub-path A37–A38–A39 — each taken in about half of the cases, never both — was replaced in the later window by a sequence that runs both sub-paths in essentially every case, first A33/A35/A36 and then A37/A38/A39, with A30 now leading into the first sub-path and A31 following after the second. This is shown directly by relational evidence (co-occurrence between the triples rose from 0 to about 98 in 100 traces with a fixed order) [REL039, REL046], by the participation of all six activities rising from about half to about 99 in 100 traces [OCC004, OCC007], by the rewiring around A30 and A31 (the direct transitions from A33/A35/A36 to A31 disappeared while new transitions into A37/A38/A39 appeared) [DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009], and by position shifts consistent with the second triple being inserted between the first triple and A31 [LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]. The screening comparison reinforces this: 78 items qualified where about 1 would be expected by chance in a stable process, although many of the pairwise items describe one underlying change rather than independent confirmations [screening].

Other differences: A04 participates in fewer traces (about 31% → 14%) and, when it occurs, now almost only alongside the block, with its follow-on traffic redirected from A16 towards A15, A48 and others [OCC001, DIR002, REL001, REL004]; A06 and A10 participate somewhat more often (about 3% → 11% and 2.5% → 12%) [OCC002, OCC003]; the outgoing profiles of A02 and A08 were redistributed almost completely, but on few transitions (13 → 23 and 9 → 28), so these rest on weak support [DIR001, DIR003].

The evidence cannot rule out that either window mixes process variants — for instance, that the roughly 1–2% of later traces without the block still follow the old either-or behaviour [OCC004] — nor can it establish from event positions alone whether the two triples run strictly back-to-back; the fixed order between them is, however, directly observed [REL039, REL046].
