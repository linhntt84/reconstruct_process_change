### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a restructuring of the segment around A25–A31: the earlier exclusive choice between running A27 or the group A36/A37/A42 appears replaced by an execution of both in every case, with the A36/A37/A42 block (still internally unordered) repositioned from after A26/A28 to directly after A25. Before, A25 flowed into A28 and both A28 and A26 preceded the branch; in the later window, A25 flows into the group, the group exits to A28 instead of A31, and A26 leads almost solely to A27, while the tail A28 before A27 before A31 is preserved and A28 and A31 still run in every trace. [REL011, REL013, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL014, REL010, REL009]

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: participation of all four activities rose from about half to all traces, the alternatives-together relation flipped from about 1 to about 995 in 1000, order relations around A28 and A26 flipped from about 46 to about 98 in 100, and the A25–A28 gap grew from 1 to about 4 event positions; screening produced 40 control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected without change, so the pattern as a whole is not a sampling artefact. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL014, REL006, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence contains no item relating A26 to A28 or A25 to A26, so A26's exact slot in the new order cannot be fixed, and whether the three activities moved as one block or were repositioned individually cannot be distinguished from these indicators. [REL006, LAG004, LAG007]

Other differences: A27 shows slight recurrence in the later window (about 2 in 100 traces contain it more than once), and a few rare outgoing transitions (about 0.5–1 in 100) appear at A25, A26 and the group activities; both are marginal. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002]
