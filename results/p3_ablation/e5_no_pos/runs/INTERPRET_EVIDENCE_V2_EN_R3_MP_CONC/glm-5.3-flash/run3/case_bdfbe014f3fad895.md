### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a block of three activities — A32, A33 and A38 — runs between A26 and A28 as an alternative to A27: in about half of the traces A26 now routes into this block, whose members execute in no fixed order and then lead to A28, bypassing A27; in the other half the earlier A26→A27→A28 course continues, so A27 has become skippable rather than removed, while A14, A15, A26 and A28 still occur throughout. The support is direct and consistent across occurrence, direction and relation indicators: the three activities rise from zero to about half of the traces and appear as a group; A26's outgoing transitions split between A27 and the block; the block's members lead to A28 and to each other; A27 and the block members almost never co-occur; and A28's distance from A26 moved from mostly 2 to mostly 4 positions. With 50 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected without a change, chance cannot explain the pattern as a whole, although many items reflect the same underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations. The evidence cannot rule out that the later window mixes two case types rather than one process with a stable routing split, and it shows nothing about why the split exists.
