### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the activities A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26 and A25, which in the earlier window appeared in varying relative orders (both orders occur in substantial shares for most pairs), are in the later window executed in one fixed sequence, A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25, which then continues to A32. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, DIR007]

The support is direct and consistent: co-occurrence within the group stays at 100 in 100 traces in both windows, so the change concerns ordering rather than participation; the direction items show each group activity now has essentially a single successor; and the distance items collapse to single fixed lags that match this chain at every observed pair. Screening qualified 51 control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected without a change, so the differences as a set are unlikely to be a sampling artefact. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG026, LAG027, LAG028, LAG029, LAG031]

Other differences: A08 participates in fewer traces (about 32 → 13 in 100) and its outgoing transitions redistributed from almost exclusively A15 to a spread including A16 and A46; A14 leads to A08 much less often (about 29 → 7 in 100) and to A15 more often (about 70 → 90 in 100); A10 participates more often (about 3 → 12 in 100). These involve activities outside the main hypothesis and rest on fewer observations (A08: 64 versus 26 traces; A10: 6 versus 25). [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, LAG001]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of case variants — several fixed-order variants earlier versus one dominant variant later — which would produce the same aggregate pattern, because order is measured across traces rather than within single cases.
