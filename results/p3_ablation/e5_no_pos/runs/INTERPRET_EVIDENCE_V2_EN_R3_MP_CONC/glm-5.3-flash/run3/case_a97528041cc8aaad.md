### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the execution order of a segment was inverted. Earlier, the flow passed through A20, A22 and A23 first (in no fixed order among themselves), then A25 and A29, then A19, A21 and A24, before reaching A30; in the later window the same activities run in the opposite order — A19/A21/A24 first, then A25/A29, then A20/A22/A23 — with A30 still following, and A40 now feeding the segment from the opposite side. [REL015, REL020, REL022, REL024, REL029, REL032, REL034, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011, DIR012]

This rests on direct order evidence: every measured order relation between the two groups flipped, each backed by about 200 traces in both windows, and for A20/A22/A23 versus A25/A29 the paired activities co-occur in 100 in 100 traces in both windows, so the flip is in order, not in participation. The successor rewiring at A19, A20, A21, A22, A23, A24, A29 and A40 and the distance shifts point to the same reordering rather than a shift in how often existing paths are taken, and screening produced 78 qualifying items (each difference larger than in about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits) where fewer than 1 would be expected without any change. [REL022, REL029, REL030, REL032, REL033, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011, DIR012, LAG018, LAG019]

Competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out: a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants, each with one fixed order, could mimic the flips; the near-complete reversals (about 98–99 in 100 earlier, 0 later) and the rewired successors argue for a genuine reordering, but a variant-mix shift remains possible. A few distance comparisons rest on 1–3 observations, and the reversal coincides with modest co-occurrence changes (for example A21 with A20/A22/A23/A25/A29 fell from about 38–39 to about 29 in 100 traces) that this hypothesis does not cover. [REL020, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027, LAG013, LAG016, LAG017]

Other differences: A02 became much more frequent (about 15 → 65 in 100 traces) and is now typically executed between A14 and A15, newly preceding A20, A22, A23, A25, A29, A30, A40, A45 and A46; A06 and A10 became rarer (about 22 → 5 and 17 → 6 in 100 traces). These participation changes are reported separately and are not part of the order-reversal hypothesis.
