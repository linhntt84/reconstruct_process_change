### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A25, A26 and A31 each occurred exactly once in every trace (participation 100 in 100, no repetitions). In the later window, each occurs about 3.5 times per trace on average and repeats within the same trace in about 68 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- Before, every execution of A31 was followed by A20. In the later window, about 71 in 100 of A31's outgoing transitions go to A25 instead, while about 29 in 100 still go to A20. [DIR001]
- Before, A25 and A31 always co-occurred in a fixed order, A25 before A31. In the later window they still always co-occur, but in about 68 in 100 traces they are interleaved or repeated rather than strictly ordered; the fixed order remains in about 32 in 100. [REL001]
- Before, A25 and A31 were always exactly two event positions apart. In the later window, that fixed two-position spacing holds in only about 12 in 100 cases, with about 5 in 100 pairs sitting five positions apart. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a block containing A25, A26 and A31 came to be executed repeatedly within traces, whereas before, each of the three ran exactly once per trace in a fixed sequence with A31 always leading to A20. The supporting signs are consistent and direct: the three occurrence profiles changed identically from single to repeated execution; A31 now returns to A25 in about 71 in 100 of its transitions, a successor change that shows the re-entry relationally rather than by position alone; and A25–A31 shifted from a fixed order and fixed two-position spacing to interleaved-or-repeated execution in 68 in 100 traces. All six items belong to the control-flow family, where only about 0.29 items would qualify by chance if nothing had changed — roughly a twentieth of the observed count — and each item exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits, so chance sampling is an unlikely explanation for the set as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, REL001, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot establish the exact boundaries of the repeated block or its entry and exit conditions. A26's involvement rests only on its occurrence profile matching A25 and A31 — no relational item covers A26 — so it could sit inside the cycle or repeat independently; the same holds for whether the three activities repeat as one block or separately, although the A25–A31 relation ties at least those two together. [OCC002, REL001, DIR001]

Other differences: in the later window, A31 still leads to A20 in about 29 in 100 of its transitions; the evidence does not show what role this remaining transition plays alongside the new A31→A25 transition. [DIR001]
