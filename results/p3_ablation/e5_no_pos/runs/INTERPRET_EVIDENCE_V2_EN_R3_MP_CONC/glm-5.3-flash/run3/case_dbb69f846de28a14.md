### Observed differences

- Before, A27, A36, A37 and A42 each ran in every trace (100 in 100, once per trace); in the later window A27 runs in about 54 in 100 traces and A36, A37 and A42 in about 47–48 in 100 each. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A27 and each of A36/A37/A42 always shared traces before; in the later window they co-occur in only about 1 in 100 traces, with A27 appearing without the group in about 53 in 100 traces and the group without A27 in about 47 in 100. [REL011, REL012, REL013]
- The group itself stays intact: A36, A37 and A42 still occur together whenever they occur (jointly present in about 47 in 100 later traces, jointly absent in about 52), with no fixed order among them in either window. [REL020, REL021, REL022]
- A25's outgoing flow changed from leading into the group (about 32–34% to each of A42, A36, A37, with A28 unobserved as a successor) to leading almost exclusively to A28 (about 97 in 100); the A25→A28 distance fell from typically 4–5 event positions to 1. [DIR001, LAG001]
- The group swapped sides relative to A26 and A28: before, A36/A37/A42 each preceded A26 and A28 in essentially all traces; in the later window A26 precedes them in about 45 in 100 traces and A28 precedes them in about 46. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016]
- A26's routing split: before it led to A27 in about 99 in 100 outgoing transitions; in the later window about 52 in 100 go to A27 and about 44 in 100 go to the group members (13–16% each). [DIR002]
- The group now hands over to A31: before, no A36/A37/A42→A31 transition was observed (0 of about 200 outgoing transitions each); in the later window about 26–35% of each member's outgoing transitions go to A31, and A42→A31 within five steps rose from 1 observation to about 93. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG008]
- The A26→A31 distance spread from almost always 2 positions to a mix of 2 (about 53 in 100) and 4 (about 43 in 100), matching the two branch shapes. [LAG005]
- A25, A26, A28 and A31 still occur in every trace in both windows (co-occurrence plus "only" shares sum to 100 in 100). [REL001, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL017]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural routing change around A26, A27 and the group A36/A37/A42. In the earlier window every case ran A25, then the group A36/A37/A42 in varying order, then A28 and A26, then A27, then A31. In the later window, A25 leads directly to A28, and after A26 the flow splits into two mutually exclusive branches: about 53 in 100 cases run A27 and then A31, while about 47 in 100 run the group A36/A37/A42 (in varying order) and then A31 — so the group, previously a mandatory stage preceding A28/A26, now acts as an alternative to A27. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL020, REL021, REL022, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG005]

The support is direct and consistent: successor changes at A25 and A26, order reversals between the group and A26/A28, near-zero A27–group co-occurrence with both sides still frequent, the group's new handover to A31, and the halved participation of all four affected activities all describe the same restructuring, and the screening produced 40 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected by chance for 138 tested units, far beyond what an unchanged process typically yields. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different case mixes (for example two pre-existing variants captured in different proportions) rather than a modified process, since only the two windows are observed. The relative order of A28 and A26, and A31's outgoing behaviour, are not covered by the evidence, so parts of the end-to-end sequence are inferred rather than observed. [REL009, REL010, DIR003]

Other differences: rare outgoing transitions (about 0.5–1.5% each) from A25 and A26 to assorted activities differ slightly between the windows, and several distance items concerning pairs involving the group (for example A25–A36, A37–A28, A42–A28) rest on only 1–2 after-window observations; they are consistent with the group having moved further from A25 but are too thinly supported to add weight beyond the main hypothesis, and they are not independent confirmations of it. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG009]
