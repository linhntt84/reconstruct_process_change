### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the segment formed by A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25 and A32 — activities that already occurred in every trace in both windows — is executed as a fixed, evenly spaced sequence A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32, whereas in the earlier window the same activities ran in mixed orders with branching exits (A31 split about evenly between A24 and A22; A23 and A25 often routed to A32 mid-segment). [REL014, REL017, REL019, DIR009, DIR013]

This rests on direct relational evidence rather than marginal frequencies: all eight measured order pairs among A20–A26 flipped from mixed to near-deterministic with 200-trace support in both windows, successor profiles concentrated onto single successors, the observed step distances reproduce the proposed chain exactly, and reverse-order pairs within five steps fell to about one trace — a change in ordering constraints, not a shift in how often existing paths are taken. [REL014, REL016, REL019, REL021, DIR007, DIR010, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG033, LAG019]

The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 76 control-flow items qualified against fewer than 1 expected (0.93 for 187 units), so random re-splits of the pooled traces cannot account for the ensemble. [screening]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether this re-sequencing and the decline of A08 (below) are two aspects of one restructuring or independent changes, nor what underlies the new fixed order; the old branch exits are now rare or unobserved in the later sample (A23 to A32 about 1 in 200 transitions, A31 to A24 not observed), so the fixed order may still admit rare exceptions. For any single item, sampling differences between the two 200-trace windows remain a competing explanation, but the near-deterministic after-patterns and the item surplus make noise unlikely for the ensemble. [DIR009, DIR013, screening]

Other differences: A08 is executed in far fewer traces (about 72 in 100 before, 29 in 100 after) while usually keeping its place before the block when it runs; A15 now typically continues to A16 instead of returning to A08, and A08's own successors spread across several activities. A10 participates more often with redistributed successors on few observations, and the rare activities A02 and A04 show redistributed successor profiles on very few observations.
