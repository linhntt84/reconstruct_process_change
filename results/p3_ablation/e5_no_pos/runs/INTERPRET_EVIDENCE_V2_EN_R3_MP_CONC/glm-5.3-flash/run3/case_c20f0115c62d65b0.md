### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the one-of-three alternative block formed by A19, A20 and A25 — previously executed exactly once per case between A27/A28 and A29 — is skipped in essentially all cases in the later window, so traces that previously ran A27 → (A19 or A20 or A25) → A28 → A29 now run A27 → A28 → A29 directly. Supporting signs: all three participations collapsed from 56–80 of 200 traces to 1 of 200 each; both predecessors (about 200 observations per window) now route almost exclusively to their direct successors; co-occurrence of the alternatives with A27, A28 and A29 disappeared; and the step distances shrank by exactly the one position the alternatives used to occupy. The 23 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.52 expected by chance for 104 tested units, and they consistently describe one structural difference rather than scattered shifts. Limitations: each of the three activities still appears once in the later window, so complete removal versus reduction to a very rare residual path cannot be distinguished, and the later routing profiles of A19, A20 and A25 rest on a single trace each; the occurrence, relation and lag items also describe overlapping aspects of the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL005, REL010, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
