### Observed differences

- A19, A20 and A25 changed from optional to near-universal: in the earlier window each appeared in about 47 in 100 traces (94–95 of 200), while in the later window each appears in about 995 in 1000 traces (199 of 200). Mean occurrences per trace rose from about 0.5 to about 1, with recurrence near zero in both windows, so each activity runs at most once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The three activities always moved as a block: pairs among them either co-occurred or were jointly absent (co-occurrence about 47 in 100 traces earlier, about 995 in 1000 later), in the same fixed order A19 before A25 before A20 in both windows. [REL001, REL002, REL007]

- Each block activity co-occurred with A26 and with A41 in about 47 in 100 traces earlier, with the partner appearing alone in about 53 in 100; in the later window co-occurrence is about 995 in 1000 and the "alone" case has nearly vanished. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the sequence A19 → A25 → A20 between A41 and A26 changed from optional to effectively mandatory. In the earlier window a case either ran the full chain A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 → A26 (about 47 in 100 cases) or went directly from A41 to A26 (about 53 in 100); in the later window the full chain runs in about 995 in 1000 cases and the direct A41→A26 step survives in only about 1 in 200 traces. The change is one of participation rather than of order or placement: the relative order of all five activities and the routing at A20 are unchanged, and repetition does not increase; because the direct path is nearly absent afterwards, this is a shift in optionality rather than a re-weighting of two alternatives that both remain in use. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, REL003, REL004, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL012, REL013]

The screening produced 21 qualifying control-flow items against about 0.6 expected if nothing had changed, so the ensemble is far beyond what an unchanged process typically yields, and all items describe one coherent difference, each larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the direct A41→A26 path was removed or is still possible but no longer taken, nor why the block became mandatory; a competing explanation it cannot rule out is that the two windows draw different case mixes (the earlier window mixing block and non-block cases, the later one drawing almost exclusively block cases), which these indicators cannot distinguish from a modification of the flow itself. [OCC001, DIR002, LAG001]

Other differences: The relational shifts involving A42 and A45 — A45 no longer observed without A19 (about 33 in 100 traces earlier, none later) at roughly unchanged overall frequency, and A42 now co-occurring with, and increasingly interleaved with, the block members — are consistent with the block becoming universal, but no participation or routing items for A42 or A45 themselves qualify, so their own behaviour is only indirectly observed. [REL005, REL006, REL010, REL011, REL014, REL015]
