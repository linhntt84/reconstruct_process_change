### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the activities between A26 and A27 ran as one fixed sequence, A26 → A17 → A15 → A18 → A19 → A21 → A20 → A27: each activity had exactly one observed successor (A15 always to A18, A18 always to A19, A20 always to A27, A26 always to A17), and each ordered pair always appeared at the same distance, e.g. A15 to A20 always four positions apart. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG003]
- In the later window the same activities still occur in every trace (each tested pair co-occurs in 100% of traces in both windows), but single-successor steps became branches: A15 now leads to A18 in about 68 of 100 transitions and to A19, A20 or A21 in about 11 each; A18 now leads directly to A27 in about 52 of 100 transitions, which was not observed before; and A26 now enters via A17 in about 53% of traces and via A19 in about 47%, where it previously always went to A17. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR005, DIR007, REL001, LAG013]
- Fixed order relations dissolved or reversed: A18 preceded A19 in all traces before, but A19 now precedes A18 in about 85 in 100 traces; A15 before A19 fell from always to about 26 in 100; A15 before A21, A17 before A19 and A18 before A20, previously fixed, now occur in both orders at roughly half each; A18 before A21 fell to about 32 in 100. [REL001, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008]
- Position gaps spread accordingly: A18 to A27 was always four positions apart and is now adjacent in about half the traces; A26 to A19 was always four apart and is now adjacent in about 47 in 100; A15 to A27 was always five apart and is now spread over gaps of two to five. [LAG005, LAG013, LAG023]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering constraints among A15, A17, A18, A19, A20 and A21 changed: in the earlier window they executed as one fixed sequence from A26 to A27, while in the later window that sequence became flexible — pairs that always appeared in one order now appear in both orders or typically in the opposite order (A19 before A18 in about 85% of traces, A19 before A15 in about 74%), and steps that previously had essentially one successor now spread across several (A26 entering via A17 or A19 about 53/47; A27 reached directly from A18 in about half the traces instead of only via A20). Participation itself did not change: every tested pair of these activities still co-occurs in all traces in both windows, so the difference concerns order and branching, not which activities run. [REL001, REL003, REL006, REL008, DIR003, DIR007, LAG013]

The support is direct and consistent: 39 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.4 expected for an unchanged process (about ninety times the chance expectation), every item has empirical strength near 0.995, the relational, directional and gap indicators all describe the same ordering change, and the pair relations rest on 200 of 200 traces in each window, which rules out a rare-variant explanation. [REL001, REL006, DIR001, LAG013]

An important competing explanation remains: the later window's variability could reflect several coexisting execution variants rather than one loosened sequence, and the direction profiles cover only observed transitions, so the exact structure behind the flexibility (and any unobserved edges) cannot be determined from this evidence. [DIR001, DIR003]

Other differences: none — all qualifying items concern the ordering and branching among these activities. [DIR001, DIR007, REL001]
