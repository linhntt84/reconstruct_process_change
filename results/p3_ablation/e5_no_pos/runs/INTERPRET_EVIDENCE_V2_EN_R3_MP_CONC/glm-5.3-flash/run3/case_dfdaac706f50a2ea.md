### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment formed by A36, A37 and A42 — which always execute together, in mixed internal order — changed from a mandatory block between A25 and A28 into an optional alternative to A27 after A26. In the earlier window every trace ran A25 → (A36/A37/A42) → A28 → … → A26 → A27 → A31; in the later window about 53 in 100 traces skip the trio entirely (A25 directly to A28, then A26 → A27 → A31), while about 47 in 100 run A26 → (A36/A37/A42) → A31 with A27 absent, the two alternatives never occurring in the same trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL020, REL021, REL022, LAG001]

The hypothesis is shown directly by relational and routing evidence, not by frequency shifts alone: the routing changes at A25 and A26, the trio's exit shift from A28 to A31, the reversed before/after relations with A28 and A26, and the collapse of the A25→A28 distance from 4 to 1 positions all describe the same relocation, and the participation drops from all traces to about half show the new optionality. With 42 qualifying control-flow items against well under 1 expected without change, chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole, and the 100-in-100 participation of all four activities in the earlier window rules out that the exclusive-alternative pattern existed before at a low rate. The evidence cannot show whether this is one coordinated restructuring or two separate adjustments (removing the trio from between A25 and A28 and adding it after A26), nor what determines which alternative a trace takes.
