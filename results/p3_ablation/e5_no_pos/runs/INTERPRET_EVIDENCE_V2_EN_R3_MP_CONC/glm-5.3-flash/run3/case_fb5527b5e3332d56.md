### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the process had two routes after A21: about 52 in 100 traces ran A21 → A22 → A23, while about 48 in 100 inserted the three activities A27, A28 and A33 — always all three together, in varying internal order — between A21 and A23. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL012, REL013, REL014, DIR001]
- In the later window, A22 appears in about 199 of 200 traces (before about 103 of 200), while A27, A28 and A33 each appear in only a single trace (before about 97 of 200 each). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Accordingly, A21 now leads to A22 in about 99.5% of its observed outgoing transitions, whereas before it led to A22 in about 52% and to A28, A33 or A27 in the remainder. [DIR001]
- The pairs A21–A22 and A22–A23 went from co-occurring in about half the traces (always in the order A21 before A22 before A23) to co-occurring in nearly all traces in the same order; before, each of A27, A28 and A33 was a mutually exclusive alternative to A22 and sat between A21 and A23. [REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL009, REL010, REL011]
- The event distance from A21 to A23 was two steps in about 52% of cases and four steps in about 48% before; in the later window it is two steps in about 99.5% of cases. [LAG001]
- The single later trace that still contains the block executes it as A28 → A27 → A33 between A21 and A23, but this rests on one trace. [REL012, REL013, REL014, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch formed by A27, A28 and A33 between A21 and A23 is now almost always skipped, so that nearly every case runs the short route A21 → A22 → A23. Before, the two routes were roughly equally likely (about 97 vs 103 of 200 traces); in the later window the short route is taken in about 199 of 200 traces, A21 leads almost exclusively to A22, and the branch activities were observed in a single trace. Participation, direct-succession, co-occurrence/ordering and distance indicators all describe this same shift, and the screening produced 30 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.42 expected if nothing had changed. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, LAG001]

Limitations: the items describe overlapping aspects of the same traces and are not independent confirmations; the later-window profile of the branch rests on one trace, so a complete removal of the branch cannot be distinguished from a reduction to a very rare path, and zero participation only means the activities were not observed in that window. [OCC002, DIR002]
