### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that work was reallocated between resources: in the later window, A01, A03, A13 (A14 shows an identical profile, so these two items likely reflect the same events) and A22 were assigned away from a group of resources that included User_78 and User_56 toward a different group that includes User_45, with the largest shift on A22. The resource family produced five qualifying items where about 0.005 would be expected by chance, every item's difference exceeds all but about 1 in 2,000 random re-splits, and the same resources lose and gain share in every profile, so sampling variation is an unlikely explanation. No control-flow item qualified (0 against about 0.16 expected), so the hypothesis concerns allocation rather than structure. [ASSIGN001, ASSIGN002, ASSIGN003, ASSIGN004, ASSIGN005]

Other differences: a timing change in its own right — elapsed time between consecutive completions shortened for A04 to A01 (median about 68 to about 21 hours, 200 intervals per window, with a wider spread afterwards) and for A09 to A23 (median about 13 to about 1.2 hours, 35 versus 25 intervals); the two remaining flagged pairs differ only at the hundredth-of-a-second scale.
