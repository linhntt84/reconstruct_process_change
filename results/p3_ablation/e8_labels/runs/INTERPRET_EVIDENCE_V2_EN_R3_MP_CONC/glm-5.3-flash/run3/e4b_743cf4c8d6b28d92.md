Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that resource allocation changed across the offer-handling activities. Most directly, A13, previously shared across about 45 resources with no single handler above about 8%, is in the later window assigned exclusively to User_3. Alongside this, assignment shares for A01, A03, A17 and A22 shifted: User_91's share roughly doubled or more, a group of previously frequent resources stopped appearing among the top handlers, and new resources took their place. Supporting signs: six resource items qualified where about 0.005 would be expected without a change, far beyond what chance produces; the shift is measured directly by the assignment-distribution and concentration indicators; the direction is consistent across five activities; and the A13 concentration rests on about 269 versus 257 occurrences, too many to be a sampling artefact.
