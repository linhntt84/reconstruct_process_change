### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the allocation of offer work moved from one group of resources to another. A16, previously shared across about 19 resources with the busiest at about 18 in 100 events, was handled exclusively by User_64 (143 events in each window); on A01, A03, A13, A14 and A17, previously frequent resources — including User_3, earlier the largest at about 8-9 in 100 — gave way to a new leading group headed by User_56 at about 10-12 in 100. Supporting signs: the resource family produced seven qualifying items where about 0.005 would be expected if nothing had changed, so chance cannot explain the pattern as a whole; all items point in the same direction, and each difference exceeds about 999.5 of 1,000 random re-splits; participation volumes were essentially flat (for example, 143 A16 assignments in both windows), pointing to a change in who performed the work rather than in how often the activities ran.
