### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The resource family produced 7 qualifying items where fewer than 0.01 were expected without change — a count many times larger than chance can explain — while the control-flow family produced none (0 against about 0.17 expected) and the time family a single item (1 against about 0.015), which is what an unchanged process typically yields. The supported hypothesis is a change in resource allocation: in the earlier window the offer activities (A13 / A14, A17, A01, A03, A22) were performed mainly by one set of resources, and in the later window a largely different set performs them, with User_39's share rising in all seven measured activities; A10 additionally moved from a concentrated small group (top seven about 80 in 100, User_117 about 17 in 100) to a dispersed allocation (pooled remainder about 52 in 100 versus about 14 in 100). The support is direct — the items are the allocation profiles themselves — and consistent, with the same resources losing and gaining shares across seven activities backed by 133–270 observations per window.
