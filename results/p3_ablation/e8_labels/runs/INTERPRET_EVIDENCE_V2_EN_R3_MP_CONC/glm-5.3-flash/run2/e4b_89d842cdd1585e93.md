### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the work on the offer-handling activities — A01, A03, A13, A14, A17 and A21 — was reassigned between resources: the cohort dominating the earlier window (including User_28) largely stopped appearing in these assignments in the later window, a largely different cohort (led by User_78) took over, and User_61 continued at a similar share. This rests on six resource items when about 0.005 would be expected by chance in the resources family — a count far too large for random re-splits to explain — and all six items point in the same direction while activity volumes stayed stable.
