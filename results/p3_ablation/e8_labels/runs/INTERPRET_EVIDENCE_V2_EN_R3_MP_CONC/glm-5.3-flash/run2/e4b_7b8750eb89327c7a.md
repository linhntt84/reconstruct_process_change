- **A08 became concentrated on a single resource.** In the earlier window, A08 assignments were spread across 16 distinct resources, with the largest share (User_115) at about 22 in 100 and several others at roughly 5–13 in 100. In the later window, every observed A08 assignment (106 traces) went to User_115, and the concentration index rose from about 0.11 to 1.0. [ASSIGN003, CONC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that resource allocation changed. In the later window, A08 is carried out entirely by one resource (User_115), where earlier it was shared across about 16 resources with none above about 22 in 100; at the same time, assignment of A01, A03, A13, A14, A17 and A22 shifted away from a group of previously prominent resources (including User_13) toward a largely different set of resources, with User_85 roughly doubling its share of offer-related work. [ASSIGN001, ASSIGN002, ASSIGN003, ASSIGN004, ASSIGN005, ASSIGN006, ASSIGN007, CONC001]

Supporting signs: the resource family produced 8 qualified items against about 0.004 expected if nothing had changed — an excess that chance cannot account for as a whole — and every item's difference was larger than all but about 1 in 2000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. The same direction of redistribution recurs across six activities, the A08 concentration rests on substantial samples (109 versus 106 traces), and the absence of control-flow and timing items indicates the difference concerns which resources carry out the activities, not the structure or timing of the process. [screening, ASSIGN003, CONC001]

Limitations: the A08 concentration is reported by two items that describe the same traces, and A13 and A14 show identical profiles, so these are not independent confirmations. Outside A08, the individual per-resource shifts are small (a few percentage points each) and partly absorbed into a growing unattributed remainder, so the evidence shows that reallocation occurred but not according to which rule. A shift in the mix of cases between the windows, rather than in the allocation itself, cannot be ruled out because the evidence contains no case attributes. [ASSIGN001, ASSIGN002, ASSIGN003, ASSIGN004, ASSIGN005, CONC001]

Other differences: none qualified — no control-flow or timing differences were found at the configured thresholds. [screening]
