### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Resource allocation changed. In the earlier window, one group of resources carried most of the work on A13/A14, A17, A01, A03 and A21; in the later window that group's shares fell to near zero on these activities and a different group took over, and A08 moved from being shared across about 17 resources to being handled exclusively by User_121. The signs supporting this are strong: in the resources family 8 evidence items qualified where about 0.005 would be expected by chance, every item's difference exceeded roughly 999.5 in 1000 random re-splits, the redistribution runs in the same direction across six activities, and the profiles rest on roughly 110–270 observations per activity. [ASSIGN001, ASSIGN002, ASSIGN003, ASSIGN006, ASSIGN007, CONC001]

Limitations: the resource codes are anonymous, and the A13 and A14 items show identical profiles, so they reflect one underlying behaviour rather than two independent confirmations. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the pattern reflects a turnover in which resources were active at all (some codes disappearing, others appearing) rather than a reallocation of the same work between continuously active resources. [ASSIGN004, ASSIGN005]

Other differences: the elapsed time between A04 and A01 completions lengthened in its upper tail (mean about 26.5 to 44 hours, 75th percentile about 31 to 71 hours, median essentially unchanged) — a timing change in its own right, though the evidence cannot show whether it is connected to the resource reallocation; and the sub-second difference in the A20 → A10 gap rests on few observations. Control flow produced no qualifying items (0 against about 0.16 expected by chance), so the hypothesis involves no structural change. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
