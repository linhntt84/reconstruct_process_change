### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: work on offer creation and sending (A13, A14, A17) and on A22 was reassigned from one group of resources to a largely different and slightly more dispersed group, with User_132 remaining the largest single resource at reduced shares. This is shown directly by the assignment profiles, is consistent in direction across all four activities, and the number of qualifying resource items (4 against about 0.004 expected without change) cannot be explained by random sampling of a stable process. [ASSIGN001, ASSIGN002, ASSIGN003, ASSIGN004]

Limitations: the two offer-creation items share one profile, so at most three distinct behaviours stand behind the four items; and the evidence cannot distinguish a deliberate reallocation of work from a change in which resources were active or available, since the resource codes are anonymous and no roles can be inferred from them.
