### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the allocation of offer-creation work to resources changed: in the earlier window, A13 was assigned across about 46 different resources with the busiest handling about 7 in 100 offers, while in the later window a single resource (User_134) carried out all recorded offer-creation assignments (about 280 events). Supporting signs: the resource family produced three qualifying items where about 0.006 would be expected if nothing had changed — a yield chance cannot account for; the assignment-profile and concentration items agree and measure allocation directly; each profile rests on roughly 270 observations per window; and the activity's volume was stable, isolating the difference to allocation rather than participation. Limitations: the two A13 items describe the same underlying difference rather than independent confirmations; the resource codes are anonymous, so nothing about roles, people or teams can be inferred; and the evidence cannot show whether this single-resource allocation is a lasting property of the process or specific to the later period. The main competing explanation — a sampling artefact — is what the screening comparison addresses, and the resource family's yield is many times its chance level. [ASSIGN001, CONC001]

Other differences: A22's assignments moved to a different mix of resources while remaining dispersed (leading share about 10 to about 8.5 in 100; long-tail share about 50 to about 43 in 100); because its work is split into many small shares, a reshuffle of this size can also arise from sampling variation alone, and the evidence cannot rule that out for this activity. The elapsed time between A04 and A01 completions rose (median about 25 to about 42 hours), but the time family yielded a single item where about 0.014 would be expected without change; a lone item is what an unchanged process can produce, so this timing difference remains an observed sample difference rather than an established change. No control-flow items passed screening (0 against about 0.19 expected), so no structural counterpart to these differences was observed. [ASSIGN002, TWAIT001]
