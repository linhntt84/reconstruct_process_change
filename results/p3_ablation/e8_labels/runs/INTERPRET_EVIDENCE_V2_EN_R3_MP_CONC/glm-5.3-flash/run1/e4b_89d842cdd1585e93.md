### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the work on A13 / A14, A17, A01, A03 and A21 was carried out by a substantially different group of resources: a previously central group (including User_28 and User_3, each about 6–8 in 100 assignments earlier) gave way to a new group led by User_78 (about 9–12 in 100 in every activity), with work also spreading somewhat more broadly beyond the top ten.
