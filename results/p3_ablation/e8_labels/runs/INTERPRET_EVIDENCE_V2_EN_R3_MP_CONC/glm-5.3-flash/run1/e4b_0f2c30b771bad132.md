### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that resource allocation changed. Most directly, A11 moved from being distributed across about 21 resources in the earlier window to being assigned to the single resource User_123 in all 113 later-window traces containing it; two indicators — the assignment profile and the concentration index — show this shift directly and agree with each other. [ASSIGN002, CONC001]

In the same direction, assignment of A03 and of A21 concentrated on a smaller set of resources, with User_3's share roughly doubling (A03) and nearly tripling (A21) while many minor resources' shares shrank. [ASSIGN001, ASSIGN003]

Supporting signs: the resources family produced four qualifying items against about 0.006 expected without change and the time family one against about 0.017, while the control-flow family produced none (0 against about 0.17), so the detected differences are confined to allocation and timing; every difference was larger than in all but about 1 of 2,000 random re-splits of the pooled data, and the samples behind each item are substantial (96/113, 200/199, 132/124, and 200/200 traces). The two A11 items describe one underlying behaviour rather than two independent confirmations. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window contains a different mix of cases rather than a changed assignment practice; a shift this complete (113 of 113 occurrences on one resource) makes pure sampling variation implausible, but the evidence shows only who performed the activities, not what drove the shift. [ASSIGN001, ASSIGN002, ASSIGN003, CONC001, TWAIT001]

Other differences: The elapsed time between the completions of A04 and A01 decreased markedly (median about 70 hours to about 12.5 hours), a timing change that stands on its own and needs no structural counterpart; because the indicator measures elapsed time between consecutive completions, the evidence cannot show whether the handling itself sped up or the two events simply came closer together.
