### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a resource-allocation change: the execution of A01, A03, A13/A14, A17 and A22 was handed over from the earlier group of assignees (led by User_37) to a largely different group (led by User_48), and A11, previously distributed across many resources, became executed by User_115 alone. This rests on direct allocation evidence: the resource family produced 8 qualifying items against roughly 0.005 expected without a change — a count chance cannot plausibly explain — all items point the same way (former assignees drop out, new ones take over), the assignment and concentration indicators agree, every difference exceeds all but about 1 in 2,000 random re-splits, and the profiles rest on substantial support (200 of 200 traces per window for A01; 107 vs 101 traces for A11).
