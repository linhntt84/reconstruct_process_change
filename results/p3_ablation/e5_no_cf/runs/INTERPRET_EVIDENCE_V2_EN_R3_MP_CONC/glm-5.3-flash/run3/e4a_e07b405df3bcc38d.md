### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a change in timing: in the later window, the elapsed time between the completion of A14 or A15 and the following completion of A20 is roughly half an hour, versus about one hour in the earlier window. The sign is direct — the indicator measures exactly these intervals — the two intervals move consistently in the same direction and by a similar amount, the whole distribution shifts rather than only a subset of cases, and the links themselves are used at nearly unchanged rates. Two qualifying items against an expectation of about 0.03 makes sampling noise unlikely as an explanation of the pattern as a whole. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]

Limitations: these intervals are elapsed time between consecutive completions, not pure waiting or processing time, so the evidence cannot show which part of the interval shortened. A reordering around A20 — for example, an activity that previously ran between A14/A15 and A20 no longer being executed in between — could produce the same shortening while leaving the pair counts stable, and the evidence contains no relational or positional items to test this. Both items also share the endpoint A20 and may partly reflect the same trace behaviour, so they are not fully independent confirmations. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]
