### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a change in timing: in the later window, the gap between consecutive completions of A01 and A04 is about twice as long as before (roughly 0.85 to 1.75 hours), while A24 and A28, which previously completed about an hour apart, now complete essentially simultaneously. Supporting signs: both items measure this elapsed time directly, the before and after distributions barely overlap, both differences are larger than in all but about 1 in 2000 random re-splits, and two qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.009 expected by chance in the time family. Limitations: this indicator is elapsed time between consecutive completion events, so the evidence cannot show whether waiting, processing or scheduling changed; with no structural or relational items, it cannot rule out that the composition of adjacent event pairs shifted rather than the pace of the same pairs; and it cannot establish whether the two opposite shifts are one redistribution of time or two independent changes.
