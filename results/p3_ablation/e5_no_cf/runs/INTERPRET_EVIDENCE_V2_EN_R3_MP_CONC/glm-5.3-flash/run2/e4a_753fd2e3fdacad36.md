### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a timing change around A09: in the later window, the time from the completion of each of A03–A08 to the next completion of A09 is about half an hour instead of about an hour, and the gaps are noticeably less variable than before. Supporting signs: all six predecessor–A09 pairs move in the same direction with a similar magnitude (about 0.47–0.56 hours), each difference exceeds all but about 1 in 2000 random re-splits of the pooled data, and six time items qualified where about 0.008 were expected without a change — far more than an unchanged process typically produces. The number of measured gaps per pair stayed in a similar range (22–51 before, 29–37 after), so the change lies in duration rather than in how often these sequences occur.
