### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One explanation supported by the evidence is a change in timing between A39 and A41: the elapsed time from the completion of A39 to the completion of A41 increased from about 1 hour per occurrence in the earlier window to about 2 hours in the later window [TWAIT001]. The hypothesis is direct (the indicator measures exactly this interval), consistent (all quantiles moved up together by a similar amount, indicating a level shift of the whole distribution) and well observed (157 versus 173 measurements) [TWAIT001]. Chance is an unlikely explanation: 1 time item qualified where about 0.01 were expected without a change, and the difference exceeds about 9995 of 10,000 random re-splits [TWAIT001].

Limitations: TWAIT is elapsed time between consecutive completion events, so the evidence cannot show whether the extra hour reflects longer waiting, longer processing, or a delay at a handover. An important competing explanation is a change in the mix of cases taking the A39→A41 step (for example a larger share of a slower case type); the evidence set contains only this timing item and cannot rule that out [TWAIT001].

Other differences: the A39→A41 gap was measured slightly more often in the later window (about 173 versus about 157 occurrences), a small difference that sampling variation could explain [TWAIT001].
