### Inference

A timing change around A27 is supported: in the later window, the elapsed time between the completion of the preceding activity and the completion of A27 is about twice what it was before, both when A27 follows A20 (about 0.9 → 1.8 hours) and when it follows A29 (about 0.2 → 0.4 hours). Supporting signs: the number of flagged items (2) is far above the roughly 0.013 expected without a change in the time family; both items move in the same direction with a similar relative magnitude; each rests on roughly 100 observations per window; and both exceed essentially all 2,000 reference re-splits. Limitations: TWAIT measures elapsed time between consecutive completions, so it mixes waiting and processing time, and the evidence cannot localise the added time at A27 itself, at A20/A29, or in between.
