### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a timing change centred on A08: in the later window, the elapsed time between consecutive completion events increased for both observed steps into A08 — from A02 (about 0.88 to about 1.71 hours on average, roughly doubling) and from A06 (about 0.12 to about 0.26 hours, also roughly doubling). The supporting signs are that the indicator measures these gaps directly, both items agree in direction and share A08, both differences exceed more than 999 of 1,000 random re-splits, and two items qualified where fewer than 1 in 100 would be expected without a change. Limitations: both intervals end at A08, so the two items may reflect one underlying difference rather than two independent changes, and this indicator is elapsed time between consecutive completions, so the increase could arise from waiting, processing or scheduling within those gaps. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the longer gaps are a by-product of a structural or participation change around A08 (for example, a different mix of paths leading into it) rather than a pure slowdown, since no structural or ordering evidence is present.
