### Observed differences

- **A01 appears as a new final step.** In the earlier window A01 was not observed at all (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it is executed exactly once in every trace (200 of 200), always at the same normalized position of 1.0, i.e. the last event of the case [OCC001, POS001].
- **A01 sits after A07.** Before, A07 had no observed outgoing transitions — it ended the case; in the later window, all of A07's outgoing transitions lead to A01. The activity pairs (A01,A02), (A01,A03), (A01,A06) and (A01,A07) each changed from "A01 never present" to co-occurrence in 100% of cases, with the other activity executed before A01 [DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL005, REL006].
- **A05 is no longer executed.** It ran in about 46 in 100 cases before (93 of 200 traces, roughly 0.9 occurrences per case) and in none afterwards (0 of 200) [OCC003, POS005].
- **The A04–A05 alternation was replaced by direct A04 repetition.** Before, about 46% of A04's outgoing transitions led to A05 and about 99% of A05's outgoing transitions led back to A04, with the two activities co-occurring and interleaving in about 46% of cases; in the later window A05 has no observed transitions and about 49% of A04's outgoing transitions go to A04 itself, with consecutive A04–A04 pairs shifting from a gap of ~2 steps (separated via A05) to directly adjacent (gap 1). A04's overall load stayed essentially the same (mean ~1.9 vs ~2.0 occurrences per trace; repeated in ~47% vs ~50% of traces) [DIR003, DIR004, REL012, LAG009, OCC002].
- **Downstream positions shifted earlier.** A07 was the last event in every earlier trace (normalized position 1.0) and sits at ~0.83 in the later window because A01 follows it; A06 moved from ~0.80 to ~0.65 and A04 from ~0.50 to ~0.42 in normalized position [POS007, POS006, POS004].
- **Step distances shifted by roughly one step in parts of the flow**, e.g. A02→A07 and A03→A07 gaps moved from mostly 4 (3) steps toward 5 (4) steps in a large share of cases, and A04→A07 gained lag-3 mass — consistent with the loop change and the appended final activity [LAG003, LAG007, LAG011].
- **Routing among the remaining activities is stable:** A02→A03 went from ~75% to ~74% and A03→A04 from ~78% to ~76%; these differences are small and unremarkable against random re-splits of the same data (empirical strength ~0.36–0.48) [DIR001, DIR002, REL007].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change with two facets. First, A05 is no longer executed: it appeared in about 46 in 100 cases before and in none after, and its back-and-forth with A04 (A04→A05→A04 in roughly 46% of cases) was replaced by A04 repeating directly via a self-loop in about 49% of A04's outgoing transitions, leaving A04's overall frequency essentially unchanged. Second, A01 was added as a new closing activity, executed exactly once after A07 in every later case, where A07 previously ended the case [OCC001, OCC003, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL012, LAG009, POS001, POS007]. Supporting signs: the participation swings (0/200→200/200 for A01; 93/200→0/200 for A05) are far beyond what re-splitting 200+200 traces produces; occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag evidence all describe the same structure; and A04's total workload stayed constant, which fits a replacement of the loop rather than added work. Limitations: no screening baseline is included to compare item counts against chance expectations; the windows cannot show whether A05 was removed from the process or merely not triggered, and A05 could still occur at a rate too low to surface in 200 traces; the evidence also cannot establish whether the two facets were one redesign or separate changes.

Other differences: small, statistically unremarkable redistributions in A02/A03 routing and order (A02→A03 ~75%→~74%; A02 before A03 ~75%→~74%) and minor lag-distance shifts across several activity pairs [DIR001, DIR002, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG002].
