### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence
The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The differences are small and point in different directions — participation shifts of at most about 7 in 100 traces, routing and position changes of a few percent, and pair-level movements both up and down — and the direct indicators behind the largest shifts (A09, A12) reach only moderate empirical strength (about 0.47 and 0.72), which is the size of difference random re-splits of two 200-trace windows also produce, especially with roughly 470 indicators tested and no screening baseline provided to say how many should qualify by chance.
