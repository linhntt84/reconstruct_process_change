### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the sequence linking A16, A05, A01, A08 and A09 was rewired and that A17 now sits between A08 and A09: the earlier chain A16 → A05 → A01 → A09 appears in the later window as A16 → A08 → A17 → A09, with A05 reattached after A09 (A09 → A05 → A04/A13) and A01 no longer part of the chain — it now occurs once, at the very start of its traces, without observed successors and without co-occurring with A04–A18. As part of the same reorganisation, A08 took over A16's outgoing flow and became more frequent and more repetitive, while A05 and the A04/A13 block run in fewer traces (about 21 → 6 and 21 → 12–13 in 100). [DIR011, DIR006, DIR012, DIR007, DIR003, DIR001, POS001, REL001, OCC008, OCC005, OCC004]

The change is shown directly by successor profiles that turned over almost completely for A16, A08, A09, A05 and A01, each resting on roughly 25–140 observed transitions per window, with differences larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits; A17 rests on 25 traces rather than being a rare one-window variant; and the lag shifts (A09→A05 now adjacent, A05→A04/A13 now 1–2 steps, A08→A04/A13 now 4–5 steps) and the co-occurrence changes agree with the same rewiring. [DIR011, DIR006, DIR007, DIR003, DIR001, DIR012, OCC017, LAG032, LAG014, LAG017, LAG026, LAG029, REL028]

Limitations: only the tracked activities are covered, so unobserved activities could sit on the new sequences; some lag profiles rest on fewer than about 10 observations; and the evidence cannot establish that A01's detachment and the rerouting are one coordinated modification rather than two simultaneous changes in the same segment. No screening baseline was included, so the comparison against what an unchanged process produces rests on the reference distributions alone. [DIR001, LAG016, LAG030, LAG061]

Other differences: A02 (50% → 38% of traces) and A03 (29% → 16%) became less common, as did A06, A12 and A18 (21% → 12.5%, 10.5% → 6.5% and 10.5% → 6%); these decreases stand on their own and are not part of the rewiring hypothesis above. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC006, OCC012, OCC018]
