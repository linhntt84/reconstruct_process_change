### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 and A04 now form a repeating, optional cycle. In the earlier window every trace executed the fixed sequence A01 → A05 → A02 → A04 → A03 exactly once; in the later window A02 can hand over to A04, A04 returns to A02, and this cycle can run several times before A02 exits directly to A03, while in about half the traces the cycle is not entered at all, so A04 is skipped and A02 occurs once. The rewiring is observed directly in the successors (A04 → A02 in 100% of A04's outgoing transitions, A02 → A03 newly appearing for about 47% of A02's), the occurrence changes are direct (A02 mean 1.0 → about 2.1 with repetitions in half the traces; A04 participation 100% → about 50%), and the relational, positional and gap items all change exactly as this structure predicts, each with empirical strength near the top of the reference scale (about 995 in 1000 reference comparisons showing a smaller difference). [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, REL004, LAG004, LAG006]

Limitations: the indicators cannot show how many loop iterations are possible, and an important competing reading — that the later window mixes two trace populations, one still single-pass and one looping, rather than one process with a skip-or-repeat choice — cannot be ruled out; either way, the two windows behave differently. The evidence file also provides no screening baseline, so the number of qualifying items cannot be checked against a chance expectation; the judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the control-flow items. [OCC002, DIR001, DIR002]

Other differences: A05's relative position shows only a small spread (median 0.25 → 0.33, std about 0.10) with its mean essentially unchanged (0.25 → 0.24); this reads as a side effect of the changed trace lengths rather than an independent shift of A05. [POS003]
