### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, about 57 in 100 traces contained A01 (115 of 200) and about 42 in 100 contained A02, A03, A04 and A12 (85 of 200); in the later window this became about 50 in 100 for A01 (101 of 200) and about 50 in 100 for the A02/A03/A04/A12 group (99 of 200). The two shares sum to all traces in each window, so the difference is a shift of about 7 traces in 100 between two groups of activities. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC012]

- The internal make-up of the A02/A03/A04/A12 group looked the same in both windows: A02 and A03 always occurred together with A02 before A03; A04 appeared in every trace containing A02 and repeated in roughly three quarters of them (about 3.8 executions per participating trace earlier, about 4.2 later); A12 followed A02 in about 20 in 100 traces earlier and 27 in 100 later, interleaved otherwise. Each pair inside the group co-occurred in about 7 more traces per 100, with "neither present" falling by the same amount. [REL001, REL002, REL010, REL011, OCC004]

- Activities inside the group moved in step with it: A06 rose from 45 to 55 traces, A08 from 40 to 44, A11 from 56 to 67, while the A05/A07/A09/A10 group stayed at about 23 in 100 traces in both windows; A06 and A08 never occurred in the same trace in either window. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011, REL036]

- Routing inside the A05/A07/A09/A10 loop shifted a few points (shares of each source activity's outgoing transitions, not of cases): A05's exits to A10 went from about 43% to 54% while exits to A07 fell from about 49% to 41%; A07's exits moved toward A09 (about 45% to 53%); A10's exits moved toward A07 (about 41% to 50%) and away from A09 (about 53% to 43%); A09 led back to A04 in about 95% of traversals earlier and 99% later. - Placement was essentially stable: median event positions barely moved (A02 at the trace start in both windows, A04 around position 0.42, A12 around 0.56), and the gap distributions between activity pairs show only small redistributions — the larger ones rest on roughly 20–30 observations, and several qualifying gap items rest on as few as one to four traces. [POS001, POS002, POS010, LAG004, LAG010, LAG021, LAG024]

### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The one coherent difference — about 7 in 100 traces shifting from containing A01 to containing the A02/A03/A04/A12 group — reaches only moderate empirical strength (about 0.8, meaning roughly one in five random re-splits of unchanged traces produces a difference at least as large), and the many agreeing relation, position and gap items all summarise the same traces, so they cannot be counted as independent confirmations [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, REL010]. The remaining high-strength items are negligible in magnitude or based on a handful of observations, and no relational or positional item involves A01 at all, so an ordinary sampling difference between the two 200-trace windows cannot be ruled out [DIR007, LAG010, LAG021, LAG024].
