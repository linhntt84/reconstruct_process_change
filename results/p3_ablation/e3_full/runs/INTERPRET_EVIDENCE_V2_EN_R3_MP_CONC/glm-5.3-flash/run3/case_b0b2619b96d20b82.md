### Observed differences

- **The successors of A01 and A04 flipped.** In the earlier window, every observed outgoing transition of A01 went to A06, and every outgoing transition of A04 went to A01. In the later window, every outgoing transition of A01 goes to A04, and every outgoing transition of A04 goes to A06. A06's own exits moved from A05 (about 54 in 100) or A04 (about 46) to A05 (about 57) or A01 (about 43). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

- **The repeating block reversed direction.** Earlier, loop traces ran A01 → A06 → A04 → A01 (repeating) and left the loop via A06 → A05 → A02; in the later window they run A01 → A04 → A06 → A01 with the same exit. Every pairwise event gap moved by exactly the one step this predicts: A01→A06 went from a gap of 1 to 2, A01→A04 from 2 to 1, A04→A06 from 2 to 1, A06→A01 from 2 to 1, and the gap from the loop entry to A02 changed from 3 to 4 for the A01 entry and from 4 to 3 for the A04 entry. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the cyclic order of A01, A04 and A06 was reversed: the earlier repeating block A01 → A06 → A04 → A01, entered at A01 and exited via A06 → A05 → A02, became A01 → A04 → A06 → A01 in the later window, with the loop entry moving from A01 to A04. This role swap explains the participation changes directly, because the entry activity runs in every loop pass while the continuation activity appears only from the second pass on — hence A01 fell from about 52 to about 20 of 100 traces while A04 rose from about 24 to about 52, with the loop path's overall share unchanged. The rerouting is shown directly by the successor changes at A01, A04 and A06, and corroborated consistently by one-step shifts in every pairwise event gap, by the swapped position distributions of A01 and A04 with A05 and A06 fixed, and by the inverted co-occurrence pattern (traces with A01 but not A04 became traces with A04 but not A01); roughly twenty items across the occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag families each exceed about 199 of 200 random re-splits in empirical strength, and all describe this single rerouting. The evidence cannot show whether the earlier A01 → A06 → A04 routing disappeared entirely or survives at a rate too low to surface in 200-trace windows, and since no aggregate chance expectation is provided, ruling out sampling noise rests on the per-item empirical strengths alone.
