### Observed differences

- The main difference is a shift between two alternative activity blocks. In the earlier window, about 55 in 100 traces contained the block A03–A12–A11–A04 (these always occur together, with A10 in about half of them), and about 45 in 100 contained A01, A06 and A08 together (plus A07 in roughly half of those); in the later window the shares are reversed, about 47 vs about 53 in 100. The two blocks never share a trace in either window (0% co-occurrence of A01 with A03, A04, A11 or A12, based on all 200 traces per window). [OCC001, OCC003, OCC008, OCC011, REL002, REL003, REL010, REL011, REL022, REL028]
- Per activity: A01, A06 and A08 rise from 90 to 106 of 200 traces (45%→53%), A05 and A09 from about 46% to 52%, while A03, A04, A11 and A12 fall from 110 to 94 traces (55%→47%) and A02 from about 55% to 48%. A05 and A09, which can follow either block, switched from running mostly after the A03-block (52 vs 39 traces) to slightly more often after the A01-block (55 vs 49). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC008, OCC009, OCC011, OCC012, REL004, REL023]
- The entry activity A02 shifted its outgoing split the same way: transitions from A02 into the A03-block (A03, A11, A12) fell from about 53% to about 47% of A02's outgoing transitions, while those into the A01-block (A01, A06, A08) rose from about 37% to about 53%. [DIR002]
- Within the A01-block, A06's successor mix changed: the share of A06's outgoing transitions going to A07 fell from about 53% to about 41%, and those going to A08 rose from about 44% to about 53% — the strongest single signal here (larger than in about 97 of 100 random re-splits). A07 consequently repeated less (0.52 → 0.41 occurrences per trace at unchanged participation of about 25 in 100), and A06's repeats per containing trace also fell (about 2.2 → 1.8). [DIR006, OCC006, OCC007]
- The order inside both blocks was stable (A03 still led to A12 in about 97% of its outgoing transitions, A11 to A04 in about 99–100%, A05 always followed by A09), so the difference lies in how often the routes run, not in their sequence. A09 also sits slightly later in traces in the later window (mean position 0.30 → 0.35), and a few activity-pair gaps shifted slightly (e.g., A05→A03 and A09→A03 gaps lengthened). [DIR003, DIR010, DIR011, REL042, POS009, LAG025, LAG046]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two existing alternative routes are taken with different frequencies: in the later window the route through A01 and A06 (involving A07/A08 and followed by A05–A09) runs in about 53 in 100 cases, up from about 45, while the route through A03, A12, A11 and A04 falls from about 55 to about 47 in 100, with the entry choice at A02 moving accordingly. This qualifies as a change in how often existing paths are taken rather than a restructuring: every involved activity appears in a substantial share of traces in both windows, only the shares between the alternatives moved, and the sequences inside both blocks stayed the same. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC008, OCC011, REL002, REL007, REL022, REL042, DIR002, DIR003, DIR011]

Supporting signs: occurrence, co-occurrence and routing indicators all move in the same direction with empirical strengths around 0.8–0.9, and the mutual exclusivity of the two blocks is observed directly on all 200 traces per window. Limitations: nearly all items reflect this one trace-level shift and so are not independent confirmations, and with 200 traces per window a random re-split still reaches differences of this size in roughly 1 of 10 cases for an individual indicator — a sizable sampling fluctuation of an unchanged process cannot be fully excluded. [REL002, REL003, REL010, REL011, OCC001, OCC003, DIR006]

Other differences: at A06 the continuation to A08 is chosen more often and the loop back through A07 less often than before (about 53% vs 44%, and 41% vs 53%, of A06's outgoing transitions), with fewer A07 and A06 repeats per trace; A09 appears slightly later in traces; several pairwise gaps shifted slightly (A05→A03, A09→A03). These accompany, but are not part of, the main hypothesis. [DIR006, OCC006, OCC007, POS009, LAG025, LAG046]
