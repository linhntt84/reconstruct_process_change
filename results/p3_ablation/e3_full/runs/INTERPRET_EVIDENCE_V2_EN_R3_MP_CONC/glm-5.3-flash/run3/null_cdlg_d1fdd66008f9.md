### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The largest occurrence differences (A03/A12 and A06/A11 repeating somewhat less) are of a size that random re-splits of the same traces produce in about a fifth to a third of cases, the positional items are tiny in absolute terms, and no chance baseline for the number of flagged items is provided to show the pattern exceeds what an unchanged process produces.
