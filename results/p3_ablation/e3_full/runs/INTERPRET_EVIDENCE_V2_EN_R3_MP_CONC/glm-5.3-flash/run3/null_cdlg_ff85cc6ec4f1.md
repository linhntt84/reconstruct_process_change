### Observed differences

- In the later window, A06 and A08 were executed noticeably more often within each case: A06's mean occurrences per trace rose from 0.85 to about 1.18 and A08's from 0.38 to 0.64, while the share of traces containing them stayed nearly flat (A06 about 25 in 100 traces in both windows; A08 15→19 in 100). Traces in which the activity repeated also rose (A06 17.5→19.5 in 100; A08 9.5→13 in 100). These are by far the largest occurrence changes among the twelve activities and exceed about 80 and 90 of 100 random re-splits, respectively. [OCC006, OCC008]
- Execution volume corroborates this: A06 produced about 170 outgoing transitions in the earlier window versus about 235 in the later one, and A08 about 75 versus 128. A08's exits still returned to A06 in about 97–98 of 100 cases in both windows, while A06's exits shifted from A09 (53%→45%) toward A08 (44%→54%). [DIR006, DIR008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the back-and-forth between A06 and A08 was executed more times per case: A06 rose from 0.85 to about 1.18 executions per trace and A08 from 0.38 to 0.64 while their trace participation stayed nearly flat, and A06's exits shifted from A09 toward A08 (44%→54% vs 53%→45%), so the A06–A08 exchange occurred more often within a case. The hypothesis is direct and consistent: it is measured by occurrence counts, corroborated by raw transition volumes (A06 about 170→235; A08 about 75→128), by rising recurrence and co-occurrence with several partners, and by A08 remaining almost exclusively attached to A06. Limitations: both windows are 200-trace samples, A08's profile rests on only about 30–38 traces, no screening baseline is provided to judge how many qualifying items chance alone would produce, and A06's own strength (about 0.79) is moderate. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a chance concentration of extra repetitions of this cycle in the later sample. [OCC006, OCC008, DIR006, DIR008, REL047, REL034, REL041]
