### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the relative execution order of A02 and A09 changed within the branch containing A02, A09, A05 and A08: in the later window A09 runs before A02 in about 59 in 100 of the traces containing both, up from about 47 in 100 before, while A02-before-A09 fell from about 53 to about 41 in 100. Both orders still occur, so this is a shift in the frequency of two existing orders, not the introduction of a fixed sequence. [REL015, LAG013, LAG034]

The change is measured directly and consistently across indicator families: the order proportions themselves flipped, A02's median position moved later while A09's moved earlier, the direct-successor shares of A02 and A09 shifted exactly in the direction the order change implies, and the distances to the unchanged tail (A05, A08) moved in mirror image. Participation of A02 and A09 itself barely moved (about 49 vs 45 in 100 traces, noise-level) and A05/A08 kept their placements, which rules out a participation effect or a broader restructuring as the explanation. [POS001, POS008, DIR002, DIR009, LAG001, LAG008, LAG011, LAG012, LAG014, LAG020, LAG036, LAG037, OCC001, OCC007, LAG004, LAG027]

Competing explanation: the comparison rests on roughly 90–100 traces per window containing both activities, and the most direct single item is exceeded by about 28 in 100 of random re-splits, so a chance imbalance in the order mix cannot be fully excluded — though the coherent direction of all surrounding items (strengths about 0.84–0.93) makes this unlikely. [REL015, DIR002, LAG036]

Other differences: the take-up of the two branch sets shifted by about 4 percentage points (A02/A05/A08/A09 from about 49 to 45 in 100 traces; A04/A06/A07/A10 from about 51 to 55), a difference about half of random re-splits match and therefore consistent with sampling variation rather than a behavioural change; the sparse rare-edge observations noted above are too weak to interpret.
