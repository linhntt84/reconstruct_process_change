### Observed differences

- The choice made after A02 shifted. A02 occurs in every trace in both windows; in the earlier window about 43% of its outgoing transitions went to A03 and about 31% to A04, while in the later window about 50% go to A03 and about 21% to A04. The shares going to A07 (about 20–22%) and back to A01 (about 6%) are roughly unchanged. [OCC001, DIR002]
- The route containing A04 shrank and its alternative grew. A04 and A14, which always occur together, appeared in about 60 in 100 traces before and about 48 in 100 after; A07 and A12, which never occur in a trace with A04, rose from about 40 to about 53 in 100 traces each, moving the A04-versus-A07 split from roughly 60/40 to roughly 48/52. [OCC003, OCC013, OCC006, OCC011, REL048, REL053, REL055]
- The A03–A17 route grew. A03 and A17 always occur together (A03 leads to A17 in essentially every case) and appeared in about 47 in 100 traces before versus about 54 in 100 after. [OCC002, OCC016, DIR003, REL045]
- Downstream routing shares moved the same way. From A17, the share continuing to A05 rose from about 43% to 50% (A05 in about 29 vs 41 in 100 traces); from A07, the share going to A15 rose from about 20% to 30% while the share to A09 fell from about 30% to 20% (A15 in about 21 vs 38 in 100 traces). [DIR009, DIR005, OCC004, OCC014]
- The busier routes are also traversed more often per trace: A02 rose from about 1.9 to about 2.2 occurrences per trace (repeated within the trace in about 47% vs 54% of cases), A03/A17 from about 1.5 to 2.3, and A05 from about 0.7 to 1.2. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004]
- A04 and A13 appear at relatively later positions within traces in the later window (mean relative position about 0.68 vs 0.74 and 0.64 vs 0.72), and A01 is more often interleaved or repeated with A03 and A17 rather than strictly preceding them (about 33% vs 48% of co-occurrences). [POS004, POS012, REL002, REL016]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that parts of the process are executed at different frequencies: the A03–A17 route (with A05 after A17) and the A07/A12 branch are exercised more often — in more traces and, for A02, A03/A17 and A05, also more times per trace — while the A04/A14 route is exercised less often. This rests on routing-share shifts measured directly at A02, A07 and A17, participation changes in the same direction for every activity involved, and relation profiles showing A04/A14 as a block that always co-occurs and never co-occurs with A07 or A12, with only the balance between these alternatives moving. Every involved activity was seen in a substantial share of traces in both windows and no successor relation appeared or disappeared, so this is a re-weighting of existing paths rather than a replacement. Limitations: no screening baseline is reported, so the number of items expected without a change cannot be checked; the OCC, DIR and REL items re-measure the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations; and a different mix of cases between the two windows cannot be ruled out from control-flow data alone. [DIR002, DIR005, DIR009, OCC002, OCC003, OCC006, OCC011, OCC013, OCC016, REL045, REL048, REL053, REL055]

Other differences: A06, A09, A10 and A11 rose modestly in participation (for example A11 from about 18 to 27 in 100 traces) while A13 fell slightly (about 30 to 28 in 100); the positional and ordering shifts of A04, A13 and the A01–A03/A01–A17 pairs noted above accompany these changes but do not by themselves establish that any activity was moved.
