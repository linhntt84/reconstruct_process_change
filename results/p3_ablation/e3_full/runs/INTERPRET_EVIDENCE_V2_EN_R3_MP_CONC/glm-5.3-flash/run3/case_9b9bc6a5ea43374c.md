### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a step A03, not observed in the earlier window, became part of roughly half of the executions — those of the variant that does not contain A04. In the later window A03 runs after A05 (and after A07 when present) and before A01, A02, A06, A10 and A11, sits adjacent to the A08/A11/A12 block with new direct transitions in both directions (A05→A03, A08→A03, A12→A03, A03→A12/A11/A08), and its presence pushed downstream activities about one event position later (e.g., A05→A06 from lag 4 to lag 5). This rests on direct, mutually consistent occurrence, order, direction and distance evidence, and on the fact that no other activity of the variant lost participation, so no existing step was swapped out; A03 also remains exclusive to this variant, which continues in the other ~48 in 100 traces essentially unchanged [OCC003, REL002, REL013, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL028, REL031, DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, LAG002, LAG013, LAG015]. Limitations: the exact slot of A03 next to A08/A11/A12 varies (both orders occur), so the hypothesis fixes A03 between A05/A07 and the A01/A02/A06/A10/A11 tail but not a single position within the middle block; zero earlier participation only shows A03 was not observed before, so a newly added step and a previously dormant one cannot be distinguished; no clock-time evidence is present. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that the two windows sample two coexisting process variants in different proportions rather than a modification — implausible given 0 versus 103 of 200 traces, but not strictly ruled out.

Other differences: A07 occurs less often and repeats less, A05 repeats less at unchanged participation, and the A04-led variant shows a modest internal redistribution (more A04→A09 routing, A09 running later after A04); the evidence does not show whether these are connected to A03's appearance, and the A04-variant shifts rest on only about 50–70 observations. [OCC007, OCC005, DIR002, REL038, POS008]
