### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The few items with high empirical strength (slightly earlier positions for A05 and A06, a slightly later position for A07, and slightly wider gaps around A06) differ by only a few percentage points, rest on 30–71 observations each, and largely re-describe the same pair behaviour rather than independent confirmations, while most other items show differences of 1–6 percentage points with empirical strengths of roughly 0.02–0.55 — a size that random re-splits of a stable process produce regularly [POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG004, LAG018, OCC001, DIR002].
