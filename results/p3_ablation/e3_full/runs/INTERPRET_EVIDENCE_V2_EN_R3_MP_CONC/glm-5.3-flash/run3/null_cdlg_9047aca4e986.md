### Observed differences

- Participation and placement are stable: in both windows every activity A01–A06 occurs in essentially all 200 traces, and event positions barely move — medians and quartiles are identical across the two windows and mean positions shift by at most about 0.02 of a normalised position (A01: 0.24 → 0.25; A04: 0.38 → 0.36). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- Pairwise relations keep the same character: every activity pair co-occurs in 100% of traces in both windows, so no pair moved between being alternatives and being executed together, and order shares shift only slightly — the largest moves are A04 before A06 (51% → 55% of cases) and A03 before A04 (68% → 66%). [REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL007]
- Outgoing routing shares change by only a few points. In the later window, A01 sends to A06 in about 13 of 100 outgoing transitions instead of about 20, offset by more traffic to A05 (9% → 12%) and A04 (38% → 40%); A04's share to A03 rises from about 8% to 14% while its share to A05 falls from 69% to 64%; the remaining sources move by 3 points or less. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- Distances between consecutive activities reshuffle slightly and without a common direction: A04 is followed by A03 within one step more often (27% → 41% of such pairs) and by A05 immediately less often (69% → 64%), while other pairs move by roughly 10 points or less in mixed directions (A03→A01 within one step 40% → 31%; A05→A06 within one step 28% → 39%). [LAG013, LAG014, LAG006, LAG019, LAG005]
- The largest single shifts anywhere in the evidence are the two A04 lag changes above, and only these two items exceed an empirical strength of 0.8 — no more than random re-splits of unchanged data typically produce across 41 tested units, where several items in fact show strengths near 0. [LAG013, LAG014, POS005, REL003]

### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. Participation, co-occurrence, ordering and positions are stable in both windows, and the routing and distance shifts (mainly around A04's transitions to A03 and A05) amount to a few percentage points with only mixed support, so sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows cannot be ruled out [DIR003, LAG013, LAG014, POS001, REL007].
