### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that an activity, A01, was added near the end of the process and now runs exactly once per case after A08, so that flows which previously ran directly from A08 to the closing activities A05 and A06 now pass through A01 first (A08→A01 in about 83 of 100 of A08's outgoing transitions; A01→A05 and A01→A06 in about 45 of 100 each of A01's observed outgoing transitions). The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation went from 0 to 200 of 200 traces, the relation of A01 with all seven pre-existing activities flipped from "never together" to "always together, A01 afterwards", A08's outgoing profile was rerouted onto A01, and lags out of A08 lengthened by about one step; every core item shows an empirical strength of about 0.995, meaning fewer than 1 in 200 random re-splits of the pooled data produce a larger difference. Limitations: A01's outgoing profile rests on only 53 observed transitions, and its placement varies — it precedes A05 in about 14 and A06 in about 26 of 100 traces, with small direct flows from A05 and A06 into A01 — so it is not always the final step; the evidence also cannot rule out that A01 is a re-labelled existing activity or a recording artefact rather than a genuinely new step, and no chance-expectation baseline is provided to compare the item count against.
