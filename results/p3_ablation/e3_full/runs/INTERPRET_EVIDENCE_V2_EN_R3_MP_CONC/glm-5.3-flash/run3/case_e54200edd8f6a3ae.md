### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A03 was added to the process as a new, always-executed activity, inserted directly after A07 and before the A02/A06/A10 group. In the earlier window A03 was never observed and A07 passed on directly to A02, A06 or A10 in equal shares; in the later window A07 always hands over to A03, A03 hands over to that same group in equal shares, and A03 runs after A04 and A07 and before A01, A02, A05, A06, A08, A09 and A10 in every trace [OCC001, DIR002, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011]. The one-step-later positions of all downstream activities, the one-step-longer distances from A04 and A07 with A07 itself unmoved, and A03's fixed third position all match a single insertion and appear consistently across the occurrence, direction, relation, position and lag indicators [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG004, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG016, LAG017, LAG018, LAG019]. Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that A03 was not observed there, so a pre-existing but very rare A03 cannot be ruled out; the many agreeing items largely restate one underlying insertion rather than independent confirmations; and no screening expectation was supplied, so the number of flagged items cannot be compared against a chance baseline [OCC001].

Other differences: the outgoing shares of A02, A06 and A10 and the relative order within the A02/A06/A10 group — which remains mixed, with no fixed order in either window — shifted by a few percentage points, and some lag distributions changed slightly; these are small and consistent with ordinary sampling variation between the windows [DIR001, DIR003, DIR005, REL003, REL004, REL012, LAG002, LAG006, LAG012, LAG022, LAG024, LAG025].
