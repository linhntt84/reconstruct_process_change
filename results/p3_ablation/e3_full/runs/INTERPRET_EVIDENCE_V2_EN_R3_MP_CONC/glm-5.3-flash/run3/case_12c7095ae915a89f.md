### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the fixed sequencing between A02 and A04 was relaxed into a mixed order: the earlier uniform block A01→A02→A04→A03 now alternates with the reverse block A01→A04→A02→A03, which accounts for roughly 45 in 100 later traces, while the original order persists with unchanged adjacency in the rest. The change is shown directly by the order relation between A02 and A04 (from 100% fixed to about 54/46) and by the new direct successions A01→A04, A04→A02 and A02→A03; the position shifts of exactly these two activities and the one-step shifts in their distances to A05/A06 are consistent with the same single underlying difference, and the rest of the process (A03's routing, the A05/A06 pair) shows no comparable movement [REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, LAG001, LAG003, LAG007, LAG008, LAG015, LAG009, LAG010, LAG016, LAG017, POS002, POS004, DIR003]. Fourteen evidence items sit at the very top of their reference distributions (empirical strength about 0.995); they share traces and are not independent confirmations, but they all describe this one coherent difference of a magnitude random re-splits do not typically produce. Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish whether a new alternative route was added or an ordering constraint was removed, and it cannot rule out that the later window simply mixes case groups with different variant shares rather than reflecting a changed routing rule; no screening baseline is included, so the comparison against chance rests on the reference strengths alone [REL006, DIR002, DIR004].
