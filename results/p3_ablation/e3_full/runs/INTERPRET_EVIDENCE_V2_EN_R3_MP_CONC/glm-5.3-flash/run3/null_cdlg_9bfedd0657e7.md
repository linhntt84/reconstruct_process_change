### Observed differences

- Participation is unchanged: A03 and A05 each occur in all traces in both windows and always occur together, in the segment between A02 and A04, running in either order with no interleaving or repetition [REL001, LAG003, LAG005].
- The frequency of the two orders shifted slightly. In the earlier window, A03 before A05 and A05 before A03 were equally common (about 50 in 100 traces each); in the later window, A05 came first in about 58 in 100 traces and A03 first in about 43 in 100 — a shift of roughly 15 cases in 200 [REL001].
- Direct-succession shares moved by the same margin in the same direction. From A02, continuation to A05 rose from 50% to about 58% while continuation to A03 fell to about 43%; from A03, continuation to A04 rose from 50% to about 58% (A03→A05 fell to about 43%); from A05, continuation to A03 rose from 50% to about 58% (A05→A04 fell to about 43%) [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003].

### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The only candidate difference — the A03/A05 ordering moving from an even 50/50 split to about 58 vs 42 in 100 favouring A05 first — amounts to about 15 cases in 200, and all twelve items share the same empirical strength of about 0.81, meaning roughly one in five random re-splits of an unchanged process would look at least this different; the items also all describe this single order relation rather than independent behaviours, so ordinary sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows cannot be ruled out [REL001, DIR001, LAG001].
