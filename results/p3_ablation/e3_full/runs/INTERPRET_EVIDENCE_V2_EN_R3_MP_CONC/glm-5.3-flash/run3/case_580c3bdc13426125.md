### Observed differences

- Activity A02 disappeared: in the earlier window it ran in every one of the 200 traces (about 2.2 times per trace, repeated in about 59 in 100 traces); in the later window it was not observed at all (0 of 200 traces). [OCC001, POS001]
- Activity A03 appeared: absent from all earlier traces, it now runs exactly once in every one of the 200 later traces. [OCC002, POS002]
- A03 took over A02's slot. Earlier, A05 always passed control to A02 (100 in 100 of A05's outgoing transitions), and A02 then went to A06 in about 55 in 100 cases and to A07 in about 45 in 100. In the later window, A05 passes to A03 in about 97 in 100 cases, and A03 splits almost evenly between A06 and A07 (about 50/50). Both activities occupy nearly the same average position in the trace (about 0.64 in both windows). [DIR003, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS002]
- The repetition loop was rewired. Earlier, A06 always led back to A02 (100 in 100 of A06's outgoing transitions), so repeats alternated between A02 and A06, typically two steps apart. In the later window, A06 leads to A07 in about 50 in 100 cases and to itself in about 48 in 100, and the A06-to-A06 and A06-to-A07 distances shortened from typically two steps to one. [DIR004, LAG007, LAG008]
- Relations with the surrounding activities flipped. Earlier, A01, A04 and A05 always co-occurred with A02 (directly preceding it in about 41 in 100 traces, interleaved with its repeats otherwise); in the later window they never co-occur with A02, while A01, A04 and A05 each precede A03 in essentially every trace. A02 and A03 never occur in the same trace in either window. [REL001, REL005, REL006, REL008, REL004, REL002, REL009, REL010]
- Secondary shifts: A06 now appears in fewer traces (about 49 in 100, down from about 59 in 100) and sits later in the trace (median position about 0.67 to 0.8); A04 and A05 also sit slightly later. A07 is essentially unchanged, always the final event. [OCC003, POS005, POS003, POS004, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural reorganisation around the A02/A03 gateway: A02 was removed and A03 now fills the same position between A05 and the A06/A07 branch, and the repetition loop that previously cycled A02–A06 (A06 always returning to A02, with A02 repeated in about 59 in 100 traces) now repeats A06 directly, with the exit to A07 leaving from A06 itself. The signs are direct and consistent across indicator families: both participation flips are complete (200 of 200 traces to 0, and 0 to 200 with exactly one execution each), the predecessor (A05), the successor shares (A06/A07 about 55/45 before vs about 50/50 after) and the average position (about 0.64) match, and the loop distances shorten from two steps to one exactly where the return edge A06→A02 was replaced by A06→A06 and A06→A07; the core differences exceed roughly 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, LAG007, LAG008, REL002, REL009, REL010]

What the evidence cannot rule out is that the gateway activity was merely relabelled from A02 to A03 in the recorded events; however, a pure rename would not explain the loop rewiring (A06 no longer returns to the gateway but repeats itself), so at least that part is a genuine behavioural difference. The branch-share shift (about 55 vs 50 in 100 taking the A06 exit) is small enough to be sampling noise between two 200-trace samples, many items describe the same underlying traces rather than independent confirmations, and no screening baseline was provided to compare the number of flagged items against a chance expectation. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, LAG007, LAG008]

Other differences: A06 participates in fewer traces (about 59 to 49 in 100) and, together with A04 and A05, sits slightly later in the trace; these shifts are plausibly by-products of traces becoming shorter once the two-step A02–A06 cycle is replaced by one-step A06 repeats, and a position shift alone does not prove an activity was moved. An A07→A03 transition appears in only 3 later traces, too few to interpret. [OCC003, POS003, POS004, POS005, DIR005]
