### Observed differences

- Before, A07 and A08 were always executed together: each ran in about 53 in 100 traces, never one without the other, and their mutual order was mixed (A07 before A08 in about 28 in 100 traces, A08 before A07 in about 25 in 100). [OCC007, OCC008, REL040]
- In the later window they became mutually exclusive: A07 appears in about 23 in 100 traces (45 of 200, down from 106) and A08 in about 29 in 100 (57 of 200, down from 106), and co-occurrence of the two fell from about 53 in 100 to 0 in 100. [OCC007, OCC008, REL040]
- The direct hand-over between them disappeared: before, about 65 in 100 of A07's observed outgoing transitions went to A08 and about 61 in 100 of A08's went to A07; in the later window no A07→A08 or A08→A07 transition was observed, and both activities instead lead mainly to A01 (about 72 and 71 in 100 of their observed outgoing transitions). A01's own outgoing transitions shifted accordingly (to A07 from about 45 to 33 in 100, to A08 from about 35 to 47 in 100). [DIR001, DIR007, DIR008]
- Every observed partner pair of A07/A08 shows the same decoupling: A01&A07 co-occurrence fell from about 53 to 23 in 100 while "A01 without A07" appeared in about 29 in 100; A05&A08 fell from about 53 to 29 in 100; pairs with the second path (e.g., A02&A07, A08&A09) changed from "always together" to "usually apart". [REL006, REL007, REL032, REL033, REL014, REL043]
- When A07 or A08 does run, it now sits immediately next to A01 or A05: A01→A07 within one step rose from about 51 to 80 in 100 observed pairs and A01→A08 from about 40 to 81 in 100, while three-step gaps vanished. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG020, LAG021]
- The take-up of the two parallel paths itself stayed stable: A01 and A05 participation moved only from about 53 to 51 in 100 and the second path's activities (A02, A03, A04, A06, A09, A10) from about 47 to 49 in 100, so the change is specific to how A07 and A08 combine, not to how often either path runs. [OCC001, OCC005, OCC002, OCC006]
- A01 now occurs at later positions within traces (mean event position about 0.40 before vs 0.55 after; median 0.33 vs 0.5); positions are event indices, so this indicates a later place in the sequence, not a clock-time delay. [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A07 and A08 changed from being executed jointly to being mutually exclusive alternatives: previously both activities always ran together in the same traces (about 53 in 100, in either order), whereas in the later window a trace runs A07 (about 23 in 100) or A08 (about 29 in 100) but never both, and their former direct successions have been replaced by hand-overs to A01. [REL040, OCC007, OCC008, DIR007, DIR008]

The change is shown directly by relational evidence rather than inferred from marginal frequencies; it appears uniformly across every partner pair of A07 and A08 with empirical strength of about 0.995 in each item; the supports are substantial (45–106 traces per window), which rules out a rare variant or ordinary sampling fluctuation; and the stable participation of A01 and A05 (about 51–53 in 100) shows the path itself still runs as often — only its internal composition changed. [REL040, REL006, REL032, REL041, OCC001, OCC005]

Limitation: the evidence cannot distinguish an explicit either/or routing decision from an arrangement in which each of the two activities independently became skippable and happened not to co-occur in this sample; the complete absence of co-occurrence (0 of roughly 100 path executions, where independent occurrence would be expected to overlap in about 13) argues strongly against the latter but cannot exclude a small residual overlap, and the exact new ordering around A01 is only partly constrained because A01 both precedes and follows A07/A08 in the later window. [REL040, REL006, REL007]

Other differences: In the second path, routing shares shifted mildly — A02's outgoing transitions moved from A06 (about 43 to 31 in 100) toward A04 (about 36 to 49 in 100), and A09's outgoing moved from A04 (about 43 to 25 in 100) toward A06, the latter based on only about 32–37 transitions per window; lag distributions in this cluster also shifted slightly (e.g., A02→A04 within one step from about 40 to 52 in 100, A04→A06 within two steps from about 27 to 46 in 100). The relational indicators in this cluster changed by only about 2–5 in 100 with low empirical strength, so these shifts may be sampling variation rather than a second change. [DIR002, DIR009, LAG005, LAG016, REL018, REL045]
