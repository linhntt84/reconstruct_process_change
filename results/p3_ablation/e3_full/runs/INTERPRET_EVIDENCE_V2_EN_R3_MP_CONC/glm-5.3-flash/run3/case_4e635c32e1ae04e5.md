### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process gained a new final step: in the later window, every case ends with A02 executed exactly once, immediately after A05, which was the terminal activity in the earlier window. Supporting signs: A02 goes from 0 to 200 of 200 cases with a fixed last position; A05→A02 becomes A05's only observed outgoing transition where A05 previously had none; A02 co-occurs with and follows A01, A03, A04, A05 and A09 in every later case while co-occurring with A06, A07 and A08 exactly in the cases where they run; and the uniform earlier shift of all other activities fits an appended event better than a reordering of existing ones. Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that A02 was not observed there, and a two-window comparison cannot rule out that the two windows sample different case mixes or variants rather than a modified process.
