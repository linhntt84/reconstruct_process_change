### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A05 was executed exactly once in every trace (200 of 200 traces, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace) and always sat at the mid-position of the trace (mean event position 0.5, no spread). In the later window, A05 is not observed at all (0 of 200 traces). [OCC003, POS003]
- A01 shows the mirror image: absent from the earlier window, and executed exactly once in every trace of the later window (200 of 200), at the same mid-position (mean 0.5, no spread). [OCC001, POS001]
- The successor stayed the same: earlier, every observed outgoing transition of A05 went to A03; later, every outgoing transition of A01 goes to A03. [DIR001, DIR003]
- The predecessors re-pointed from A05 to A01. Of A02's outgoing transitions, about 72% went to A05 earlier versus about 75% to A01 later (A06 taking most of the rest, roughly 28% vs 25%). Of A07's outgoing transitions, about 71% went to A05 earlier versus about 66% to A01 later. Of A06's outgoing transitions, about 29% went to A05 earlier and about 30% go to A01 later. [DIR002, DIR005, DIR004]
- The relations around this step match one-for-one. Earlier, A05 co-occurred with A03 and with A04 in all traces and always preceded them; later, A01 does. Earlier, A02 preceded A05 in every trace containing A02, A06 preceded A05 in all traces, and A07 preceded A05 in the 53% of traces containing A07; later, A02 and A07 each precede A01 in the 50% of traces where they meet it, and A06 precedes A01 in all traces. A01 and A05 never co-occur: earlier every trace had A05 without A01, later every trace has A01 without A05. [REL012, REL014, REL002, REL003, REL009, REL016, REL017, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL004]
- The rest of the control flow is essentially stable: A02 and A07 remain mutually exclusive alternatives (about 47% vs 53% of traces earlier, 50% vs 50% later), and the event distances from A02/A06/A07 to A03/A04 shift by at most about 5 percentage points, with low empirical strength (0.06–0.50). [REL011, OCC002, OCC004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A05 is no longer executed and that A01 now runs at the point in the flow where A05 used to be: in the later window A01 appears exactly once per trace at the same mid-position, is reached from the same predecessors (A02, A06, A07) with nearly unchanged edge shares (about 72→75%, 71→66%, 29→30% of those predecessors' outgoing transitions), and leads to the same successor (A03, with A04 following), while A05 has disappeared entirely. The main items all exceed about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled data, the occurrence flip is complete across 200-trace windows, and occurrence, position, successor and relation indicators agree — though many of them describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine removal-plus-addition from a relabelling of the same step, since A01 and A05 never co-occur and behave identically; and no screening baseline was included, so the number of qualifying items could not be checked against a chance expectation. [OCC001, OCC003, POS001, POS003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL012, REL014, REL016, REL017]

Other differences: the split between the alternatives A02 and A07 moved from about 47/53 to 50/50 of traces, and small redistributions in event distances appeared (at most about 5 percentage points, empirical strength 0.06–0.50); both are consistent with sampling variation between two 200-trace windows rather than a behavioural change. [OCC002, OCC004, REL011, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]
