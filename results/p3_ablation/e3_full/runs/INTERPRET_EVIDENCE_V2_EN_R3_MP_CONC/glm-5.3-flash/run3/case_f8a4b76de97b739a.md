### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the process runs with two added activities and one dropped activity: A02 was added as a new early step — after the start activity A04, and after A05/A13, which now route to it — leading mainly to A12 (which continues to A20 as before), while A01 was added as a new mid-trace step leading mainly to A03; A09, which previously occupied that mid-trace slot feeding A03 after A16, is no longer observed, so the segment A16→A09→A03 appears to have become A16→A01→A03. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC008, POS001, POS002, POS009, DIR001, DIR002, DIR009, DIR016, DIR005, DIR013, REL021, REL029, REL037]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the presence/absence flips are measured on all 200 traces per window (0 versus 117 and 173 traces for A01/A02; 108 versus 0 for A09) and are larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits; the successor profiles of A01, A02, A09 and A16 change directly (empty versus populated, and A09 replaced by A01 among A16's targets); A01's later occurrence profile (about 1.5 occurrences per trace, repeats in about 39 in 100 of its traces) closely matches A09's earlier profile (about 1.5 occurrences, repeats in about 38 in 100); and the ordering evidence agrees (A04 before A02, A02 before A12/A20). Many relation items describe the same underlying traces, so they corroborate rather than independently confirm the change. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR009, DIR016, REL021, REL029, REL037]

An important competing explanation remains: zero participation only means an activity was not observed in that window, so a rare variant already containing A01/A02 (or still containing A09) in the earlier 200-trace sample cannot be fully excluded, and the placement statements rest on event positions rather than clock time. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002]

Other differences: A14 became far more frequent (about 39 to 89 in 100 traces, with repeats) and now forms a repetition loop with A17 near the end of the trace — A17 leads almost exclusively to A14, A14 leads mainly back to A17, A07/A08 route more to A14, and A14 and A17 moved from never co-occurring to co-occurring in about 42 in 100 traces; this is consistent with the same restructuring but the evidence does not tie it to the A01/A02/A09 difference. [OCC013, OCC016, DIR007, DIR008, DIR014, DIR017, REL172] The slightly lower participation of A05 and A11 falls within the range random re-splits produce. [OCC004, OCC010]
