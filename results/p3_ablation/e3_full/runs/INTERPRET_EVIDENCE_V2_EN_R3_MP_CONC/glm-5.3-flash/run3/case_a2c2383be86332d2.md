### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in the repeating sequence involving A03, A08, A07 and A05. In the earlier window the sequence ran A03 → A08 → A07 → A05 and returned to A03, with the exit to A06 taken from A07; in the later window A07 is no longer executed, the sequence runs A03 → A08 → A05, and the same A05-versus-A06 choice (about 52/48) is made directly at A08. The change is shown directly: A07 falls from participation in all 200 cases to none; A08's successor changes from always A07 to A05/A06 with exactly the shares A07 previously had; all of A07's relations collapse from universal co-occurrence with A03, A05, A06 and A08 to complete absence; and every distance between the surviving activities, including the sequence's repeat distance, shortens by exactly one step (4 to 3). [OCC004, POS004, DIR002, DIR003, REL017, REL021, REL024, REL026, REL028, LAG005, LAG007, LAG014, LAG015, LAG019, LAG020]

Limitations: positions and distances are event ranks, not clock times, so no claim about timing is made. The competing explanation that A07 was merely not triggered in the later sample cannot be strictly excluded, since zero participation only means non-observation; however, with 416 earlier occurrences against none later, and the uniform one-step contraction of all distances, a sampling artefact is highly implausible. No chance-expectation baseline (screening) is included in the evidence, so this judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the items rather than on a comparison with an expected item count. [OCC004, LAG005, LAG020]

Other differences: A01 appears as a new entry path before A03 in about 35% of later cases (always first, always followed directly by A03, never co-occurring with A02 or A04), while the entry shares of A02 (about 48% to 28%) and A04 (about 53% to 38%) fell; because A01 was absent from all 200 earlier cases, a rare pre-existing variant cannot be ruled out, though it is unlikely at the observed later rate. A05's participation rose slightly (about 50% to 55%), a difference consistent with random re-splitting. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001, REL003, OCC002, OCC003, REL009, POS003]
