### Observed differences

- **A01 disappears, A08 appears.** In the earlier window, A01 appeared in about 48 in 100 traces (mean about 1.0 occurrence per trace, roughly twice per trace when present); in the later window, A01 is not observed at all. A08 shows the mirror image: not observed earlier, present in about 48 in 100 traces in the later window (mean about 1.1). [OCC001, OCC006, POS001, POS008]

- **A08 now runs where A01 ran.** Earlier, A01's outgoing transitions went to A09 in about 51 in 100 cases, A10 in 24 and A16 in 24; later, A08's outgoing transitions go to A09 in about 56 in 100, A10 in 25 and A16 in 19. A17 led to A01 in every observed case earlier and leads to A08 in every observed case later; A02 led to A01 in about 4 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and now leads to A08 in about 2 in 100; A03 led to A01 in about 21 in 100 and now leads to A08 in about 26 in 100. A08 also sits at the average position A01 held (about 0.55 versus 0.57). [DIR001, DIR006, DIR012, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS008]

- **Partnerships mirror the exchange.** The (A01, A08) pair itself shows it: earlier, 48 in 100 traces contained A01 only and 52 contained neither; later, 48 in 100 contain A08 only and 52 neither. Pairs that included A01 co-occurred in between about 15 and 48 in 100 traces earlier and never later, while the matching A08 pairs now co-occur at nearly the same shares and order splits — for example, A03 preceded A01 in about 11 in 100 earlier and precedes A08 in about 11 in 100 later, and A01 preceded A13 in about 21 in 100 earlier while A08 precedes A13 in about 19 in 100 later. [REL007, REL001, REL002, REL012, REL023, REL037, REL101]

- **A11 and A15 exchanged places.** Earlier, A11 was the closing step — present in 52 in 100 traces, always at the last position, with no observed outgoing transitions — and A15 was a mid-process step (32 in 100 traces, mean position about 0.49) that always led to A03. Later, A15 is the closing step (52 in 100 traces, always last, no observed outgoing transitions) and A11 is a mid-process step (29 in 100 traces, mean position about 0.54) that always leads to A03. The connections moved with them: A05 now leads to A15 in about 52 in 100 of its outgoing transitions where it led to A11 (its link to A14, about 48 in 100, is unchanged), A18 now leads to A11 in about 26 in 100 where it led to A15 in about 21 in 100, A02 precedes A15 in 52 in 100 traces versus about 22 earlier, and A05–A11 co-occurrence fell from 52 to about 16 in 100. [OCC009, OCC011, POS011, POS014, DIR005, DIR008, DIR010, DIR013, REL026, REL029, REL067, REL070]

- **A strict either/or pairing moved.** Earlier, every trace contained exactly one of A01 or A11 (48% and 52%, never both, never neither); later, every trace contains exactly one of A08 or A15 (48% and 52%, never both). [REL010, REL103]

- **Smaller shifts.** A03 still occurs in every trace but repeats less (mean 2.07 versus 1.82 occurrences per trace); A04 and A18 appear in about 45 in 100 traces versus 50 earlier, with fewer repetitions (mean 2.16 versus 1.65); A07 participation fell from about 38 to 27 in 100 traces. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC014, OCC005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the reading that, in the later window, A08 has taken over the role A01 played: A01 is no longer observed, while A08 appears in about 48 in 100 traces (the share A01 had), occupies the same average position, is followed by the same activities (A09 in about half of its outgoing transitions, then A10 and A16), and is reached from the same predecessors — A17 in every observed case, A02 and A03 in a small share of theirs. The pair (A01, A08) shows the handover directly: the 48 in 100 traces that contained A01 earlier now contain A08 instead, and no trace contains both. [OCC001, OCC006, DIR001, DIR006, DIR012, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS008, REL007]

The support is direct and consistent: successor and predecessor edges flip one-for-one (A17→A01 became A17→A08; A02→A01 became A02→A08; A03→A01 became A03→A08), and each vanished A01 partnership is replaced by the matching A08 partnership at nearly the same share and order split. Each of these differences is larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled windows, and the many matching items describe one underlying exchange rather than independent confirmations. [DIR012, DIR002, DIR003, REL002, REL037, REL012, REL101]

Limitations: the traces cannot distinguish a renaming of A01 from a different activity taking over an identical role — both look the same here — and exact shares (for example 51% versus 56% for the A09 successor) can differ between two 200-trace samples without any change. [DIR001, DIR006]

Other differences: A11 and A15 exchanged places in the later window — A15 became the closing step reached from A02 and A05, roles A11 held earlier, while A11 moved to a mid-process position feeding A03, which A15 did earlier, with A05 and A18 rerouting accordingly and the strict either/or pairing moving from A01/A11 to A08/A15. [OCC009, OCC011, POS011, POS014, DIR005, DIR008, DIR010, DIR013, REL026, REL029, REL067, REL070, REL010, REL103] A few activities also show modest decreases outside both readings: A03 repeats less per trace, A04 and A18 participate less often with fewer repetitions, and A07 participation fell. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC014, OCC005]
