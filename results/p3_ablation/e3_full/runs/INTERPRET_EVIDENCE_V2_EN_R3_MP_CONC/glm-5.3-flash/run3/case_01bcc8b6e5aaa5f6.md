### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A02 ran exactly once in every trace: it is present in 200 of 200 traces, about once per trace, at the very start (mean position ≈ 0.05, typically the first event). In the later window, A02 appears in none of the 200 traces. [OCC001, POS002]

- Before, A02 co-occurred with A01 and with each of A03–A07 in essentially every trace and ran before them (before A01 in about 199 of 200 traces; before A03–A07 in roughly 88–100 of 100 traces). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 is no longer executed in the later window — the step was removed from the process or is now skipped in every case. Earlier, A02 opened each trace and handed over mainly to A03 or A04, running before A01 and before each of A03–A07; in the later window A02 is entirely absent while every other activity keeps its role, with A03–A07 shifted slightly earlier and A03 now often taking the first position. The signs are direct and consistent — participation falling from 200 of 200 traces to 0 of 200, an empty outgoing profile for A02 (199 transitions before, none after), and all five measured relationships flipping from full co-occurrence to partner-only — though these items all describe the same underlying trace change rather than independent confirmations. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A02 still executes but so rarely (well under about 1 in 200 cases) that it missed the later sample; zero participation only means it was not observed. No chance-expectation baseline (screening) was provided, so the item count could not be compared with what an unchanged process would produce; the judgement rests on the size, directness and consistency of the difference. [OCC001, DIR002, POS002, REL001, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, POS003]

Other differences: minor shifts not required by the main hypothesis — A03 precedes A04 in about 53 of 100 traces before but only about 44 of 100 after, and some step gaps changed slightly (A03→A01 gaps of 5 steps fell from about 52 to 46 in 100; A03→A04 gaps now concentrate at 2 steps, about 56→68 in 100). These are small and could reflect sampling variation or side effects of the shortened traces. [REL009, LAG002, LAG003]
