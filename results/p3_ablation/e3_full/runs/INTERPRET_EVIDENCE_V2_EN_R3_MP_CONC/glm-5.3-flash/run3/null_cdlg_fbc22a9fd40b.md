### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, cases pass through the repeating part of the process fewer times: the recurring activities A01, A08, A09, A10, A11, A12 and A13 — together with A03, A05 and A06, which also recur — each execute fewer times per trace (for example A08 about 7.8 to 6.9, the A01/A09/A10/A12/A13 group about 3.9 to 3.5) while the share of traces containing them stays essentially the same, and distances between consecutive executions of the same activity shift to shorter gaps. Supporting signs: all ten occurrence measurements move in the same direction, which would be unusual if nothing had changed given that they cover largely distinct activities; the shortened self-distances and the widespread drop in joint-occurrence shares point the same way. Limitations: the evidence provides no screening baseline, the occurrence items describe largely the same traces and are therefore not independent confirmations, and per-item empirical strengths are moderate (roughly 0.6 to 0.84), so a chance draw of cases with naturally shorter repetition cycles in the later window cannot be ruled out.
