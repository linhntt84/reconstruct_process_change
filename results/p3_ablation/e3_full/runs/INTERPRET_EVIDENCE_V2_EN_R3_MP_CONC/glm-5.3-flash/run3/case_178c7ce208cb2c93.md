### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was absent from the earlier window (0 of 200 traces) and is executed exactly once in every trace of the later window (200 of 200), always at a fixed position about two-thirds into the case. [OCC001, POS001]
- Before, A02 passed control to A03 in about 78 in 100 cases and to A04 in the remainder, while A04 passed to A02 in about 78 in 100 and to A03 in the remainder. In the later window, A02 passes to A01 in about 76 in 100 cases (A04 otherwise) and A04 passes to A02 in about 76 in 100 (A01 otherwise); the direct edges A02→A03 and A04→A03 are no longer observed. [DIR002, DIR003]
- A01 always hands over to A03. [DIR001]
- Before, A01 never co-occurred with A02, A03 or A04; in the later window it co-occurs with each of them in every trace, always after A02 and A04 and always before A03. [REL001, REL002, REL003]
- The distance from A02 to A03 grew from one step (about 78 in 100 cases) to two steps (about 76 in 100) or three steps (about 24 in 100); the distance from A04 to A03 grew by one step in the same way. [LAG001, LAG002]
- The ordering between A02 and A04 is essentially unchanged: A04 runs before A02 in about 78 in 100 earlier cases and about 76 in 100 later cases. [REL004]
- The normalized positions of A02 and A04 shifted slightly earlier (A02 median from 0.5 to 0.33; A04 mean from 0.11 to 0.08), consistent with one event being added after them rather than with any reordering. [POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added as a new, mandatory step inserted directly before A03. In the earlier window, cases ran either A04→A02→A03 (about 78 in 100) or A02→A04→A03 (about 22 in 100) without A01; in the later window the same two routeings persist at nearly the same shares (about 76 and 24 in 100), but each now passes through A01 immediately before A03. This is shown directly by A01 appearing in all later traces at a stable position, the new edges A02→A01, A04→A01 and A01→A03 replacing A02→A03 and A04→A03, the fixed ordering of A01 relative to A02, A03 and A04, and the one-step increase in the A02/A04-to-A03 distances; the unchanged A02–A04 ordering and the nearly identical path shares rule out a mere shift in how often existing paths are taken. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, LAG001, LAG002, REL004]

Limitations: zero participation before only shows that A01 was not executed in that window, so a renamed or previously unused step cannot be excluded from this evidence alone, and the evidence covers only activities A01–A04, leaving the rest of the process unobserved. [OCC001]

Other differences: none beyond effects consistent with the insertion above — the small position shifts of A02 and A04 and the stable A02–A04 ordering follow from the added step. [POS002, POS003, REL004]
