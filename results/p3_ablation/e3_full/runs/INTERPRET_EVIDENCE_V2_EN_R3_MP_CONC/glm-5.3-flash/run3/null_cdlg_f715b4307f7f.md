### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the choice between the two existing alternative paths through A06 and A12 shifted toward A06: A12 ran in about 55 in 100 cases before and about 43 in 100 after, while A06 rose from about 45 to about 57 in 100, with the two activities never sharing a case in either window. The shift is observed directly at the branch point A03, whose outgoing transitions moved from A12 to A06 (about 57/43 to about 44/56 in 100), and the same direction appears in A07's transitions to A12 and in the pairwise relations of A06 and A12 with the rest of the process. Because both activities remain frequent in both windows, keep their positions and do not repeat, this reads as the same two paths being taken with different frequencies rather than activities being added, removed, moved or repeated. [DIR003, DIR006, OCC005, OCC011, POS006, POS012, REL030, REL036, REL066]

Limitations: the occurrence, position and relation items describing this shift reflect the same underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations, and this evidence file provides no screening baseline against which the number of qualifying items could be compared. The main competing explanation — ordinary sampling variation between two 200-case windows — is hard to reconcile with the size of the core differences (about 12–13 points on participation and routing shares, differences exceeded by fewer than about 2–3 in 100 random re-splits), but it cannot be excluded for the smaller secondary shifts. [OCC005, OCC011, DIR003, REL066]

These are not part of the main hypothesis and, being smaller and partly based on few observations, could also reflect sampling variation.
