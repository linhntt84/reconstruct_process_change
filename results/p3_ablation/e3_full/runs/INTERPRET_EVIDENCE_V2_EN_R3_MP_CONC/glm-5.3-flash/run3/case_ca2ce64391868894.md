### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained A03 (about 2.1 occurrences per trace, repeated in about 55 in 100 traces), A05, A07, A02 and A01. A01 was the final event of each trace with A02 immediately before it, while A03 appeared early in the trace (mean relative position about 0.24) and its observed outgoing transitions split between A06 (about 53 in 100) and A07 (about 47 in 100). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- A06 and A08 always occurred together, in about 55 in 100 traces (about 111 of 200), and A08's outgoing transitions all led to A03. [OCC005, OCC006, REL026, DIR004]
- In the later window, A01, A02 and A03 are not observed at all (0 of 200 traces, against 200 of 200 before), and A04, which was unobserved before, occurs exactly once in every trace at the final position. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS004]
- The direct-successor relations rewired accordingly: A05, which previously always led to A02, now always leads to A04; A08, which previously always led to A03, now leads to A06 (about 46 in 100) and A07 (about 54 in 100). [DIR003, DIR004]
- The relations flip in the same direction: A01 and A02, previously always co-occurring (A02 before A01), now never do; A04 now co-occurs with A05 in every trace (A05 before A04) and with A06, A08 and A07 in about 50 in 100 traces, whereas before it co-occurred with none of them. [REL001, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022]
- As a downstream effect, A07 and A06/A08 now appear earlier in traces (A07 mean relative position about 0.30 versus about 0.48; A06 about 0.18 versus about 0.27), and the step distances between A06/A08 and A05/A07 shortened by roughly one step (for example, A06 to A07 at distance 3 before, mostly distance 2 after). [POS006, POS007, LAG001, LAG003, LAG005]
- A small further difference: A06 and A08 now appear in about 50 in 100 traces (about 101 of 200) versus about 55 in 100 before, a modest decrease. [OCC005, OCC006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in which the activities A01, A02 and A03 disappeared from the observed behaviour and A04 was added as the new closing step: before, traces ran through an early, sometimes repeated A03 (re-entered from A08), then A05, A02 and the final A01; in the later window none of A01, A02 or A03 is observed, A05 leads directly to A04, A08 leads directly to A06/A07, and A04 ends every trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR003, DIR004, REL019]

This is not a shift in how often existing paths are taken: A01, A02 and A03 went from universal to unobserved and A04 from unobserved to universal, so activities were removed and one added, with the successor profiles of A05 and A08 switching completely. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR003, DIR004]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the participation changes rest on all 200 traces in each window; the successor, relational, positional and lag differences all match this one rewiring rather than pointing in different directions (they likely reflect the same underlying trace behaviour, not independent confirmations); and every item's difference exceeded all 200 random re-splits of the pooled data (empirical strength about 0.995). [OCC001, OCC004, DIR003, REL019, LAG003]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A01, A02 and A03 were not observed in the later window, not that they were formally removed from the process; the evidence set provides no chance-expectation baseline, so ruling out sampling rests on the size (200 of 200 to 0 of 200) and coherence of the differences; and the evidence cannot show whether A04 takes over the function of the disappeared segment or is unrelated to it. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that the two windows capture different case mixes, although a stable process producing three universal activities in one window and none in the other, plus a new universal activity, is not plausible from sampling alone. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Other differences: the drop in A06/A08 participation (about 55 in 100 traces to about 50 in 100) and their slightly earlier positions are small changes that sampling variation in an unchanged process could also produce; they are consistent with, but not required by, the main hypothesis. [OCC005, OCC006, POS006, POS008]
