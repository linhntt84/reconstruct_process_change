### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that execution frequency was redistributed between two groups of activities: in the later window, A01, A07, A15 and A08 — which occur only in fixed company (A01 always with A08, A07 always with A15, and A07/A15 never without A01/A08) — are executed less often, in fewer traces, and their occurrences sit later within traces, while A05, A10, A09, A12 and A11 are executed more often and sit earlier; A02 and A04 move mildly in the first direction (participation about 56 to 49 of 100 traces, later positions), although their occurrences per participating trace rose slightly. [OCC001, OCC006, OCC013, OCC007, OCC004, OCC008, OCC011, OCC009, OCC010, POS001, POS008, POS005, POS009, OCC002]

The reading rests on occurrence and position measurements whose directions agree across all related activities: the always-co-occurring pairs lose or gain together (A01&A08 co-occurrence falls from 72% to 65.5%, exactly matching both partners' participation drop), the position shifts run in opposite directions for the two groups with empirical strength between 0.90 and 0.99, and the strongest single item in the evidence (the routing change at A10, strength 0.97) shows the redistribution extends to successor choice as well. [REL007, POS001, POS005, DIR009]

Limitations: no single occurrence difference is decisive alone — the main activity-level strengths are about 0.79–0.87, and A08's only 0.47 — so sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows remains a competing explanation for any individual item, and the conclusion rests on the joint coherence of the pattern rather than on any one indicator; positions are event positions, so the later/earlier pattern locates occurrences within traces without showing clock-time movement. [OCC001, OCC004, OCC007, POS001]

Other differences: the routing shifts at A10 (toward A02, away from A04 and A06) and at A08 (away from A02) noted above; the ordered relation A02→A03 — and equivalently A04→A03, A02→A16 and A04→A16 — fell from about 17% to 10% of traces, with A03 and A16 more often appearing without A02/A04; and many pairwise gap distributions changed, such as A02→A15 gaps moving from 2–3 events to 3–4 events apart. [DIR009, DIR007, REL016, REL030, LAG026]
