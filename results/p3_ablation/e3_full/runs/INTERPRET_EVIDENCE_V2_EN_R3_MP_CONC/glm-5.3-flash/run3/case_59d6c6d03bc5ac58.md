### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained A02 exactly once, in one of two shapes: about 50 in 100 traces ran A01 — always the first event — directly into A02, and the remaining traces ran A03 → A06 → A04 → A02, with A02 always ending the trace. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL010, REL012, REL014, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

- In the later window, A01 is not observed at all (0 of 200 traces, against about 101 in 200 before), and the traces that used to begin with A01 now consist of A02 alone; accordingly, A02 — previously always the last event — is now the first or only event in about 54 in 100 traces. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, POS002, REL001]

- A05, not observed before (0 of 200 traces), now occurs once in about 46 in 100 traces, always between A04 and A02: before, every observed outgoing transition of A04 led to A02, while in the later window all lead to A05, and all of A05's observed outgoing transitions lead to A02. [OCC004, DIR002, DIR003, POS004, REL008]

- Consistent with this, the step distance from A04 to A02 grew from one to two, from A06 to A02 from two to three, and from A03 to A02 from three to four, and A05 co-occurs with A03, A04 and A06 in exactly that branch's traces, always after A04 and before A02. The internal order A03 → A06 → A04 is unchanged, and the branch's share barely moved (about 50 in 100 before, about 46 in 100 after — a difference random re-splits produce about as often). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL008, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, OCC002, OCC003, OCC005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window A05 executes between A04 and A02 on the A03–A06–A04 path: traces that previously ran A03 → A06 → A04 → A02 (about 50 in 100) now run A03 → A06 → A04 → A05 → A02 (about 46 in 100). The change is shown directly by the successor profiles (A04's outgoing transitions switched entirely from A02 to A05; A05's only observed successor is A02), by the co-occurrence and order relations (A05 appears in exactly the traces containing A03, A04 and A06, always after A04 and before A02), and by the step distances to A02, which each increased by exactly one. The sizes involved (0 of 200 traces before versus about 92 in 200 after, each larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits) make sampling variation implausible. The main competing explanation the traces cannot rule out is that A05 is a relabelled or reconfigured existing activity rather than a newly added one. [OCC004, DIR002, DIR003, POS004, REL008, REL011, REL013, REL015, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Other differences: A01, which previously opened about half of all traces and always passed control directly to A02, is no longer observed in the later window; those traces now contain A02 alone, which also explains A02's position shift. Whether the A01 path was removed from the process or merely not taken cannot be distinguished from these traces. The slight drop in the A03–A06–A04 branch's share (about 50 in 100 to about 46 in 100) lies within the range random re-splits produce.
