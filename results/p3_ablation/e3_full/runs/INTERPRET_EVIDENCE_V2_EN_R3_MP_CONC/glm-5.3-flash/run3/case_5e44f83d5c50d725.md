### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was executed exactly once in every trace in the earlier window (participation 100 in 100 traces, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, typically at about two-thirds of the trace). In the later window, A01 was not observed in any of the 200 traces. [OCC001, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 is no longer executed in the later window: traces that previously ran A05 → A01 → A07, with A01 occurring once in every trace, now go from A05 directly to A07, and every activity downstream of the old A01 position occurs one step earlier relative to the start of the trace. The support is direct — A01's own participation and position (100 in 100 traces before, 0 in 200 after), the successor change at A05, and the loss of all A01 co-occurrences — and consistent, with all eight spanning lag distributions shortening by exactly one step; each key item shows a difference larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits. Many items describe this single underlying difference, so they should be read as one body of evidence, not independent confirmations. Limitations: zero participation only shows A01 was not observed in the later sample, so survival of A01 in variants not sampled cannot be ruled out, and the position and lag measures reflect event order rather than clock time. [OCC001, POS001, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG005, LAG006, LAG009, LAG010, LAG014, LAG015, LAG019]

Other differences: several small redistributions appear alongside the main difference — A02's split between A04 and A05 moved from about 90/10 to 87/13, A04's outgoing mix shifted slightly from A05 towards A07/A03/A06, A07's outgoing mix shifted slightly from A03 to A04, and the order shares between A02/A03/A04 and A06 moved by a few percentage points. These are small in magnitude and several fall within what random re-splits of a stable process typically produce, so they are not part of the main hypothesis.
