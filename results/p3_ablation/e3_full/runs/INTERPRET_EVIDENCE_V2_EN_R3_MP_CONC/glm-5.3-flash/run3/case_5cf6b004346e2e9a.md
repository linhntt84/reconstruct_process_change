### Observed differences

- The transitions out of A10 and A11 were redirected. In the earlier window, A10's outgoing transitions went to A06 (about 51 in 100) and A07 (about 49 in 100), and A11's went to A06 (about 58 in 100) and A07 (about 42 in 100). In the later window, every observed outgoing transition of A10 (55 transitions) and of A11 (39 transitions) goes to A08 instead. [DIR009, DIR010]
- A08's own role changed in step. Earlier, its roughly 123 outgoing transitions led almost only to A10 (about 50 in 100) and A11 (about 48 in 100); later, on about 203 outgoing transitions, they lead to A06 (about 29 in 100), A10 (about 27 in 100), A07 (about 24 in 100) and A11 (about 19 in 100). A08 also occurs about 65% more often per trace (mean 0.62 → 1.02) while the share of traces containing it stayed flat (about 26 in 100 before, 25 in 100 after) — among cases containing A08, the average count rose from about 2.4 to about 4.1 executions — and A08-to-A08 pairs within five steps doubled (21 → 42). Its mean position in the trace moved slightly later (0.45 → 0.50). [DIR007, OCC007, LAG071, POS007]
- A10 and A11 became rarer and less coupled to their former successors and to each other: each now appears in about 12–13 in 100 traces versus about 19–20 in 100 before; A10 and A11 co-occur in about 6 in 100 traces versus 12–13 before; their co-occurrence with A06 and A07 fell by roughly 4–7 percentage points; and pairs starting at A10/A11 shifted about one event position later (e.g., A11→A04 moved from gap 3 to gap 4). [OCC009, OCC010, REL055, REL044, REL045, REL048, REL049, LAG100, LAG099, LAG088]
- The cluster around A01, A04 and A05 became more active: A05 appears in about 40 in 100 traces versus 30 before, A04 in about 52 versus 45; A05's outgoing shifted from A01 (79% → 69%) toward A04 (21% → 31%), A04's transitions to A01 rose (10% → 18%), and A01–A05, A01–A04 and A04–A05 co-occurrence each rose by about 7–10 percentage points. [OCC004, OCC003, DIR004, DIR003, REL004, REL003, REL028]
- A02 and A09 occur less often per trace (mean 0.59 → 0.45 each; participation 27% → 24.5% of traces) and appear slightly earlier; A06 also occurs less often per trace (mean 0.66 → 0.51). [OCC002, OCC008, POS002, POS008, OCC005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the flow after A10 and A11 was rerouted through A08: in the earlier window A10 and A11 led on to A06 or A07 (with A08 feeding back into A10/A11), while in the later window A10 and A11 lead exclusively to A08, which in turn continues to A06/A07 and recurs more often within cases — that is, A08 appears to have taken an intermediary role on these paths. [DIR009, DIR010, DIR007, OCC007]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor replacement is observed outright on sizeable samples (all 55 of A10's and all 39 of A11's later outgoing transitions go to A08, differences larger than in any of 200 random re-splits of the data), and it is corroborated by A08's redirected successors, its higher repetition with flat participation, the one-step-later positions of downstream pairs, and the weakened coupling of A10/A11 with A06/A07 and with each other. [DIR009, DIR010, DIR007, OCC007, LAG071, LAG100, REL044, REL045, REL048, REL049, REL055]

Limitations: the many LAG and REL items are not independent confirmations, as most plausibly reflect this single rerouting; the evidence set provides no screening summary, so the overall number of qualifying items could not be checked against a chance expectation. A competing reading the evidence cannot fully exclude is that the later A10/A11 successor profile is estimated on a narrower set of cases (their participation dropped to about 12 in 100 traces), so only A08-adjacent occurrences of A10/A11 may remain — old edges could still exist below the observed rate. No cause for the redirection can be inferred from this evidence. [DIR009, DIR010, OCC009, OCC010]

Other differences: the region around A01, A04 and A05 became more active, with more transitions cycling between A05, A04 and A01 and higher co-occurrence of these activities [OCC004, OCC003, DIR004, DIR003, REL003, REL004, REL028]; A02, A09 and A06 occur somewhat less often per trace [OCC002, OCC008, OCC005]. These differences are not explained by the rerouting hypothesis and may be separate changes or sampling variation.
