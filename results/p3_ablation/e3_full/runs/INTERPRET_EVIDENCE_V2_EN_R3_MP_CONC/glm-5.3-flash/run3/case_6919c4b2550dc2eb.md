### Observed differences

- The largest difference is participation: in the earlier window, A01 and A05 appear in none of the 200 traces, while in the later window each occurs exactly once in every trace (200 of 200 traces, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace). The four activities already present (A02, A03, A04, A06) still occur in every trace in the later window, so no activity disappeared. [OCC001, OCC002, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS006]
- In the later window, A01 sits in the middle of the case: its median position is about 60% of the way through the trace, it comes after A02, A03 and A04 in roughly 78, 87 and 69 of 100 traces respectively, it precedes A06 in all 200 traces, and about 80 in 100 of its outgoing transitions lead to A06. The surrounding flow moved with it: A04 now leads to A01 in about 52 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (0 before), while A04 leading to A06 fell from about 74 to about 6 in 100; A03 now leads to A01 in about 45 in 100 (0 before). [POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL005, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004]
- In the later window, A05 closes the case: its median position is the last event, A06 precedes it in all 200 traces, and A06 leading to A05 accounts for about 84 in 100 of A06's outgoing transitions. Before, A06 usually ended the trace (only 59 outgoing transitions across 200 traces) and otherwise led mainly to A02 (about 53 in 100) and A04 (about 29 in 100). A02, A03 and A04 each precede A05 in about 87–98 of 100 traces. [POS005, REL014, DIR006, REL007, REL010, REL012]
- Distances between the early activities and A06 grew by roughly one to two steps: A04 reaches A06 within one step in about 74 of 100 earlier cases but only about 7 in 100 later cases, with gaps of two to three steps now dominant; A02→A06 and A03→A06 show the same lengthening. The relative positions of A02, A03, A04 and A06 all shifted earlier (for example A04's median from about two-thirds to about 40% of the trace, A06's from last to about 80%), consistent with two events being added rather than existing work being re-timed. [LAG008, LAG003, LAG006, POS004, POS006]
- Two smaller shifts fall outside this pattern: A03 precedes A04 in about 67 of 100 later traces versus about 78 before, and in 29 of 200 later traces A05 has a successor — most often A04 — instead of ending the case. [REL009, DIR005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural hypothesis: two activities that were never observed in the earlier window, A01 and A05, became fixed once-per-case steps in the later window, with A01 placed between the early block (A02, A03, A04 — themselves in mixed order) and A06, and A05 appended at the end after A06. A typical case moved from a shape like A02/A03 → A04 → A06 (A06 last) to A02/A03 → A04 → A01 → A06 → A05. The support is direct rather than circumstantial: relations show the new orders (A01 before A06, and A06 before A05, in 200 of 200 traces each), successor profiles show the rewiring (A04→A01 up from 0 to about 52 in 100 while A04→A06 fell from about 74 to 6; A06→A05 about 84 in 100), and the position and lag shifts match the addition of two events. A participation move from 0 to 200 of 200 traces, with the new ordering relations near-perfectly consistent, is not what sampling variability in an unchanged process produces. [OCC001, OCC002, REL004, REL005, REL014, DIR001, DIR004, DIR006, POS001, POS005, LAG008]

Limitations: the evidence provides no screening baseline, so the number of findings expected by chance under an unchanged process could not be compared; the judgement rests on the size and coherence of the differences instead. Zero participation before establishes only that A01 and A05 were not observed earlier — it cannot distinguish activities newly added to the process from ones that existed but were always skipped — and the occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag items describe largely the same traces, so they converge on one structure rather than confirming it independently. A01's exact insertion point also varies (it precedes A04 in about 31 of 100 later traces), so its mid-sequence placement is approximate. [OCC001, OCC002, REL003]

Other differences: the order between A03 and A04 became less one-sided (A03 first in about 67 of 100 later traces vs about 78 before), and in about 29 of 200 later traces A05 is followed by another activity (usually A04) rather than ending the case; neither forms part of the main hypothesis. [REL009, DIR005]
