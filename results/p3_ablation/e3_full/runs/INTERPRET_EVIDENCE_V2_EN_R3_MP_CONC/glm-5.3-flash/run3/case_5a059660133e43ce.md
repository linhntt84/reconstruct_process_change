### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a new early step A01 was added to a subset of cases: A01, unobserved in the earlier window, now runs in about 22 in 100 traces, always as the second event directly after the opening activity A02, and A02 → A01 accounts for about 21 in 100 of A02's observed outgoing transitions. The sign is direct and consistent across occurrence, position, direction and relation items (each at least as extreme as about 995 in 1000 random re-splits), and A01 co-occurs with A02 in every trace in which it appears. Limitations: zero participation before only shows A01 was not observed earlier, so an added step cannot be distinguished from one previously too rare to appear in 200 traces, and this evidence set provides no screening baseline against which the item count could be checked formally. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, DIR001, REL001]

Other differences: the branch A08 → A12 was largely abandoned, with A08 and A12 participation roughly halved, A08 rerouted to A09/A04/A02, A04 returning to A02 instead of continuing to A08, and A12 leading back to A09 rather than to A02; separately, A06 now leads directly to A15, which consequently follows A02 two steps later instead of immediately. These reroutings involve activities whose behaviour changed independently of A01 and are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC008, OCC012, DIR002, DIR004, DIR008, REL067, DIR003, REL055, LAG013]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is sampling variability between the two 200-trace windows, since a rare variant can appear in one window only — this matters most for A01's zero count before; the breadth and mutual consistency of the A01 items across four indicator families makes chance an unlikely explanation for that difference as a whole. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, REL001]
