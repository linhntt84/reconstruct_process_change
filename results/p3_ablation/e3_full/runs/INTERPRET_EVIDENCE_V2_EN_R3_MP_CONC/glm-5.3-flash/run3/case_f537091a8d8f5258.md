### Observed differences

- The traces split into three mutually exclusive groups: one containing A01, A04, A05, A06, A07 and A09 (these six always occur together), one containing A03, A08, A10 and A11 (always together), and a remainder of about 25 in 100 traces containing A02 and neither group; no activity of the first group ever shares a trace with one of the second, in either window. [REL001, REL002, REL010, REL014, REL045]

- The groups' sizes shifted: in the later window, traces of the A01/A04/A05/A06/A07/A09 group rose from about 48 to about 55 in 100 cases, those of the A03/A08/A10/A11 group fell from about 28 to about 21 in 100, and the A02 remainder stayed at about 25 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC008]

- Within the frequent group the sequence was rearranged. Earlier, A05 and A06 executed at the end of the trace (mean normalised positions about 0.9 each) directly after A09, with A07 (about 0.36) and A09 (about 0.59) ahead of them; in the later window A05 sits at about one-third and A06 at about two-thirds of the trace, while A07 (about 0.74) and A09 (about 0.98, the final position) close it. [POS004, POS005, POS006, POS008]

- The mutual order reversed: earlier A07 preceded A05 in 48 of 100 traces and A09 preceded A05 and A06 in 48 of 100 each, essentially never the reverse; later A05 preceded A07 in 55 of 100 and A06 preceded A09 in 50 of 100. The A05–A06 pair, earlier executed in both orders (about 25 vs 23 in 100), later always ran A05 first (55 vs 0 in 100). [REL025, REL026, REL028, REL033]

- Typical step distances changed accordingly: A01 reached A05 within 1–2 steps in all later cases (earlier 4–5 steps in about 92 of 100), and A04 reached A05 within 1–2 steps (earlier 3–4 steps in about 85 of 100), while A04→A07 stretched from 1 step (89 of 100) to 2–4 steps and A04→A09 from 2 steps to 3–5 steps. [LAG002, LAG010, LAG012, LAG013]

- Successors moved the same way (shares of outgoing transitions): after A01, the share to A04 fell from about 76% to 45% while the share to A05 rose from about 1% to 45%; after A04, the share to A07 fell from about 80% to 9% and the share to A05 rose to about 51%; after A06, the share to A05 (about 96%) disappeared in favour of A07 (about 76%). A09, earlier followed by A05 or A06 in about 96 traces, had only 12 outgoing transitions left in the later window, i.e. it became the final step in most later traces. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR005, DIR008]

- The A03/A08/A10/A11 group kept its internal behaviour essentially unchanged: successor shares and step gaps move by only a few points (several items well within the random-split range), A11 still precedes A10 whenever both occur, and the only visible shift is A03 tending to appear slightly later in the trace (mean position about 0.08 → 0.23). [DIR007, LAG006, LAG007, POS002, REL045]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the execution order within the frequent variant changed: A05 and A06, which earlier formed the closing pair immediately after A09 in no fixed mutual order, now execute in the first two-thirds of the trace in the fixed order A05 then A06 — typically A01 → A04 → A05 → A06 → A07 → A09, whereas the earlier variant typically ran A01 → A04 → A07 → A09 → A05/A06 (in either order). This is shown directly by the order reversals, the successor redistribution at A01, A04 and A06, and the step-gap shifts, with the position distributions of all five activities moving in the same direction; the order items show differences larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the same data. [REL025, REL026, REL028, REL033, LAG002, LAG005, DIR001, DIR003, DIR005, POS004, POS006, POS008]

Limitations: a few of the new connections rest on small samples (A09 has only 12 outgoing transitions and the A07–A06 pair only 27 supporting traces in the later window), and all positional and gap evidence is measured in event positions, so nothing follows about clock-time timing. The competing explanation that the two windows simply sample different mixes of pre-existing execution variants cannot be fully ruled out, although the earlier order disappeared completely (for example A07 before A05 in 0 of 100 later traces), which random re-splits hardly ever produce. [DIR008, LAG021, REL026]

Other differences: independently of the reordering, the share of traces between the two exclusive variant groups shifted — the A01/A04/A05/A06/A07/A09 group from about 48 to 55 in 100 and the A03/A08/A10/A11 group from about 28 to 21 in 100, with A02-only traces stable at about 25 in 100 and no activity changing from single to repeated occurrence. All involved activities remain well observed in both windows, so this is a shift between existing alternatives; however, its per-item differences are only larger than in about 72–86 of 100 random re-splits, so sampling alone cannot be excluded as the source of this particular shift. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC008, REL001]
