### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new final step, A01, was appended to the process: in the later window every case executes A01 exactly once at the very end, directly after A06, which had been the last activity in the earlier window. The support is direct and consistent across indicator families — participation rises from 0 to 200 of 200 traces, A01 holds the last relative position in every later trace, A06's only observed successor in the later window is A01, and every relation item places A01 after A02–A08 — while the slight earlier shift of A04–A08 fits traces becoming one event longer and is part of the same change, not separate evidence. [OCC001, POS001, DIR002, POS004, POS005, POS007, POS008, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 existed before but was simply not executed or not recorded in the earlier window, since zero participation only means "not observed"; no screening baseline is present in the evidence, so the judgement rests on the directness of the participation and placement evidence rather than a chance comparison. [OCC001]

Other differences: A03's participation slipped from about 54% to 52% and A02's recurrence from about 54% to 52%, with small shifts in some step-gap distributions; these are minor, consistent with sampling variation between two 200-trace windows, and are not part of the main hypothesis.
