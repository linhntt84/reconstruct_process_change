### Observed differences

- The routing at A02 flipped: in the earlier window A02's outgoing transitions led to A04 in about 56 in 100 cases and to A03 in about 44 in 100; in the later window this is reversed, with about 47 in 100 to A04 and about 53 in 100 to A03. [DIR001]
- Participation moved with this flip: A03, A06, A07 and A10 each appeared in about 44 in 100 traces before and about 53 in 100 after (88 → 106 of 200 traces each), while A04 fell from about 56 to 47 in 100 (112 → 94). A05 rose from about 27 to 29 in 100 and A09 from about 23 to 28 in 100. Repetition per participating trace was roughly unchanged (A03 and A06: about 2.2 occurrences per containing trace in both windows), so the higher mean occurrences per trace track the higher participation, not more repetition. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]
- The two branches are mutually exclusive in both windows: A03 and A04 never occur together and every trace contains exactly one of them (neither in 0 of 200); A04 also never co-occurs with A05, A06, A07, A09 or A10. A03, A06, A07 and A10 always occur together (co-occurrence equals participation, about 44% → 53%), with A05 present in about 60 in 100 of those cases before and about 55 in 100 after, and A09 in about 52–54 in 100 in both windows. A08 occurs in every trace and always precedes A04 when A04 runs. [REL015, REL017, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL033, REL036, REL039, REL025, POS006]
- Co-occurrence with the ever-present A01 and A02 mirrors the shift: pairs with A03/A06/A07/A10 rose from about 44 to 53 in 100 traces, while pairs with A04 fell from about 56 to 47 in 100. [REL001, REL002, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL012, REL014]
- The ever-present activities sit earlier in the later window in relative event positions: A02's median moved from about two-thirds to about one-third of the trace, A08's from about one-third to about one-sixth; since positions are event-based, this fits the changed mix of branch shapes rather than demonstrating a move. [POS001, POS006]
- Step distances inside the A03 branch show only small, direction-inconsistent wobbles (e.g., A08→A07 at lag 3 in about 50 → 60 in 100 of observed pairs; A07→A09 at lag 2 in about 40 → 47 in 100), with low-to-moderate empirical strength. [LAG026, LAG031, LAG035, LAG036]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, a larger share of cases takes the A03 branch at the A02 choice point: the A04 path (A08 followed by A04) dropped from about 56 to about 47 in 100 cases, while the A03 path — A03 together with A06, A07 and A10, with A05 in roughly 55–60 and A09 in roughly 52–54 of those cases — rose from about 44 to about 53 in 100. All involved activities remain well observed in both windows, A03 and A04 remain exactly complementary (one per trace, never both, never neither), and repetition within the branches is essentially unchanged, so this reads as existing paths being taken with different frequencies rather than activities being added, removed or repeated differently. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC007, REL015, REL017]

The routing change is observed directly in A02's outgoing transitions, and the same roughly 9-in-100 shift appears consistently in the participation of every branch activity, in co-occurrence with the ever-present A01 and A02, and in the relative-position medians of A02 and A08; the key items place the observed difference above about 87–94 in 100 random re-splits of these windows. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC002, REL001, REL009, POS001, POS006]

The shift amounts to about 18 cases per 200-trace window, so an ordinary sampling difference between two finite windows remains a competing explanation that cannot be fully excluded; no screening baseline is included to compare the number of qualifying items against chance, and the many agreeing items all reflect this one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [DIR001, OCC002, OCC005]

Other differences: small, mutually inconsistent changes in step distances within the A03 branch (A08→A07, A07→A09, A09→A09, A09→A10) and a slightly later relative position of A05; these are minor and may be sampling variation. One lag item (A09→A07) rests on a single observed pair per window and is not interpretable. [LAG026, LAG031, LAG035, LAG036, LAG034, POS003]
