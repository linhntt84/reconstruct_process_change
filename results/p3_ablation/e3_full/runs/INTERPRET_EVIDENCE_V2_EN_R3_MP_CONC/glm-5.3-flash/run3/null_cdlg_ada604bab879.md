### Observed differences

- In the later window, every measured activity is executed more often per case: mean occurrences per trace rose for A05 from 0.39 to 0.58 (roughly half as many again), A04 from 0.42 to 0.55, A06 from 0.43 to 0.56, the always-together pair A01/A11 from 0.88 to 1.06, A08/A09 from 0.97 to 1.05, and the block A02/A07/A10 from 0.46 to 0.53. - The activities also reach more cases: A05 appears in about 37 of 100 traces versus about 26 before, A04 and A06 in about 34 versus 29, A01/A11 in about 69 versus 65, and A02/A07/A10 in about 33 versus 28; A08/A09 stay flat at about 66 in 100, and A03 is present in every trace in both windows. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the activities of the process are executed more often: most activities occur in a larger share of cases and several repeat more often within a case. A05 shows this most clearly (about 37 of 100 traces versus 26 before; mean occurrences per trace 0.39→0.58), with A04 and A06 following the same pattern more modestly (about 29→34 in 100 traces), the inseparable pair A01+A11 rising from 65 to 69 in 100 traces with repeats up from 19 to 26 in 100, and A08+A09 keeping their participation (about 66 in 100) while repeating more (18→24 in 100). The signs are direct and consistent: all ten measured activities show more occurrences per trace, co-occurrence rises exactly where participation rises (e.g., A03–A05 from 26 to 37 in 100, with A03 present throughout), and self-pairs within five steps become more common. Limitations: each window holds 200 traces, so the smaller gains (A02/A07/A10, about 28→33 in 100) are weak alone; the many agreeing items need not be independent; and the evidence cannot rule out that a single trace-level difference — cases simply containing more executions — underlies all of these shifts rather than separate per-activity changes. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC007, OCC010, REL021, LAG001, LAG105]

Other differences:
- Routing shares shifted without any involved activity disappearing: from A02 the outflow moved from A10 (about 58%→39%) to A07 (35%→58%), and from A10 from A07 (60%→38%) to A02 (35%→50%); from A04 toward A05 (17%→31%) and away from A02 (30%→17%); from A09 away from A03 (55%→43%) toward A04 and A05 (13%→20% and 7%→16%). A by-product of the higher repetition cannot be ruled out as the source of these share shifts. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, DIR008]
- The fixed A08→A09 order loosened (42 versus 49 in 100 traces, more interleaving) and A09→A03 became less immediate (84%→69% within one step). [LAG076, REL050]
- A06's median event position moved later in the trace (about a third to a half of the trace length). [POS005]
