The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added as a new mandatory step inserted directly between A07 and A04: in the earlier window the path ran A07 → A04 in every case and A01 was never observed, while in the later window every trace contains A01 exactly once, A07 leads to A01, A01 leads to A04, and A01 consistently sits after A02, A05 and A07 and before A04, A06 and A03.
