### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 — unobserved in the earlier window — now executes as part of the opening segment in about 60 in 100 cases: once per case, interleaved with A03 and A10 in both orders, and preceding the downstream activities A04, A05, A06, A07, A08, A09, A11, A12, A13 and A14 in essentially all of their occurrences, with new direct successions A03→A01 and A10→A01, A01 leading to A03, A10, A04 and A07, one-step-wider event gaps and later positions for the activities that follow. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR007, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL008, LAG001, LAG007, POS003, POS005]

The support rests on directness and consistency rather than item count: relational, directional, positional and gap evidence all show the same underlying difference in the same direction (the many agreeing items describe one behaviour, not independent confirmations), and the core items exceed the differences seen in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data. Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A01 was not observed then, so a newly added step cannot be distinguished from one that previously never ran; no screening baseline was provided, so the number of qualifying items could not be compared with the number expected without change; and because A01, A03 and A10 appear in both orders, the evidence shows A01 joining an unordered opening group rather than occupying a fixed slot. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in case mix — a population of cases in which A01 always runs alongside the existing flow — rather than a modification of the shared flow. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG007]

Other differences: A02 participation fell slightly (about 44 to 40 in 100 cases) and A13 fell slightly (about 16 to 14 in 100, with a marginally later position); A09's direct successions shifted a few points between A13 and A14. These shifts are small and consistent with sampling variation, and are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC002, OCC013, POS011, DIR006]
