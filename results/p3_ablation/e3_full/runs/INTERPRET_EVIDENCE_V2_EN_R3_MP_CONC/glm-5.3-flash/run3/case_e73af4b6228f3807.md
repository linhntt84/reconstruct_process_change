### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the branch that executes A11 also executes A10: where traces previously ran the fixed sequence A05 → A11 → A07, they now run A05, then A10 and A11 together in either order, then A07, while the alternative branch through A01 is essentially unchanged (A01's transitions to A07 rose only from about 92 to 97 in 100). This is shown directly and consistently: A10 appears in 94 of 200 later traces after appearing in none earlier; A11's routing split from about 96 in 100 to A07 into about 44 to A07 plus about 52 to A10; A10's transitions lead to A11 or on to A07; A10 and A11 always co-occur, never with A01, in both orders; and the distances from A05 and A11 to A07 stretched by exactly one step. A11's own participation barely moved (about 51 vs 47 in 100), so A10 was added alongside A11 rather than replacing it. [OCC008, DIR004, DIR005, DIR002, DIR001, REL055, REL009, LAG010, LAG017, OCC009]

Limitations: the sample cannot show how the order between A10 and A11 is chosen, and the small drift in the branch balance (A01 about 49 to 53 in 100) is within sampling noise. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window draws on a different case mix whose traces simply always include A10 next to A11, rather than the earlier A11 path having gained an activity; the aggregate indicators cannot separate these. [OCC001, OCC009, REL055]

Other differences: three new final steps — A06 (about 11 in 100 traces), A09 (about 6 in 100) and A02 (about 4–5 in 100) — now follow A07 at the end of traces, whereas earlier about 49 of 200 traces ended at A07 and none do later; the new endings never co-occur with the earlier endings A03, A04 or A08, whose shares shrank slightly, consistent with the previously terminating traces now continuing to one of six endings.
