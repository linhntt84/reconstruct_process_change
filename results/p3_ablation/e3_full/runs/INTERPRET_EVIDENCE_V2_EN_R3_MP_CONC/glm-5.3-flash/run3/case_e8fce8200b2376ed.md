### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the control flow was restructured in the later window: the previously unobserved activities A07, A15, A16 and A17 now take part in the flow, and the connections of several existing activities changed — A08 reaches A18 via A07 instead of going to A05, A18 continues via A17 to A09 (and to A14) instead of returning to A02, A12 feeds A05 instead of A18, A05 exits to A02 and A13 instead of A09 and A14, and A10's former exits to A03, to itself and to A02 are replaced by cycles through A15 (back to A10) and A16 (on to A02 and A03). [OCC006, OCC014, OCC015, OCC016, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011, DIR012]

- The core of this hypothesis is observed directly rather than inferred: the four new activities have zero support in the earlier window yet appear in a third to seven tenths of later traces, and every changed successor profile exceeds about 99 in 100 of the reference comparisons, far outside what random re-splits produce. [OCC006, OCC014, OCC015, OCC016, DIR003, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR012]
- The relational, positional and distance items agree with the same routing change, and the new edges form cycles (A10 → A15 → A10 and A05 → A02 → A12 → A05) that fit the repetition changes, so the many flagged items largely reflect one coherent structural difference rather than independent fluctuations. [DIR002, DIR007, DIR009, LAG056, OCC004, REL045, REL087, REL088, REL122]
- Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the restructuring happened at a single point or gradually within the windows, and it cannot rule out that the two windows mix process variants in different proportions (for example a new variant running alongside the old one) rather than one modified process; zero earlier participation also strictly means only that A07, A15, A16 and A17 were not observed before, although with 200 traces per window a later share of 33 to 70 in 100 is hard to attribute to sampling alone. No baseline for the expected number of flagged items is provided, so the judgement rests on the directness and mutual consistency of the items. [OCC006, OCC014, OCC015, OCC016]
