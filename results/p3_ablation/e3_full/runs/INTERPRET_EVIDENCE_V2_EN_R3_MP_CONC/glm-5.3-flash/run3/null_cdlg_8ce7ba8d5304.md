### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The differences that qualified are small and scattered across unrelated activities, and the largest of them (the step-distance shifts around A08 and A03) rest on pairs observed in only about 35–100 traces per window, so sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows can account for the pattern.
