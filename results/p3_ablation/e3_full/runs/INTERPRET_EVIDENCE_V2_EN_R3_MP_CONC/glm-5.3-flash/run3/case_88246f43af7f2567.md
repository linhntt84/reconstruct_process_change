### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A03 and A11 no longer take part in the cycle with A02, A04 and A12: A02 and A04 now lead directly into A12, while A03 — still cycling with A11 — is executed only within the complementary branch together with A05, A06, A09, A10, A15 and A16, typically entered from A16 and leading on to A10 or A11. Before, A03 and A11 were confined to the A02/A04/A12 cases and never appeared alongside A05, A06, A10, A15, A16 or A09; after, the reverse holds. As part of this, A03 and A11 run in fewer cases (about 49 → 30 and 24 → 17 in 100), and A12 is reached earlier in the case instead of always closing it. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR010, REL031, REL032, REL036, REL038, REL042, REL106, LAG007, LAG010, OCC003, OCC011, POS011]

The hypothesis is shown directly by successor changes (A02 and A04 now send all observed outgoing transitions to A12; A03 sends none to A12 or A04 and about 45% to A10; A16 sends about half to A03 where before it sent none), by co-occurrence flips from complete overlap to complete exclusion (A03/A12, A03/A02, A03/A04, A11/A12) and from complete exclusion to complete overlap (A03 with A06/A10/A16), and by adjacency evidence (A02→A12 and A04→A12 at distance 1 in 100% of observed pairs). The participation and position shifts for A03, A11 and A12 point the same way, and most qualifying items exceed 99 of 100 random re-split comparisons, so the pattern as a whole is not what random sampling typically produces. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR010, REL038, REL106, REL032, LAG007, LAG010, OCC003, OCC011, POS003, POS011]

Limitations: the after-profiles for A03 and A11 rest on 60 and 34 cases, and A12's earlier outgoing split on only 5 observed transitions, so routing shares are imprecise; the evidence cannot fully rule out that the two windows sampled different mixes of pre-existing execution variants rather than a modified process, and the exact placement of A03 and A11 inside the other branch (for example relative to A05 or A06) is only partly pinned down, as their order there is largely interleaved or repeated. [OCC003, OCC011, DIR007, REL032, REL042]

Other differences: small, weakly supported lag redistributions around A07, A13 and A14 (for example A07→A14 pairs shifting from distances 2–3 towards 3–5) and a wider spread in A09's self-repetition distances, each based on roughly 10–20 observations per window. [LAG022, LAG021, LAG025]
