### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the control flow was re-routed around A02 and A08. In the later window, A02 is executed in every case and directly precedes A04 or A03, whereas before it ran in only about half the cases and was followed only by A01; A08, which previously ran in every case and fed into A03 or A04, is now executed in about 45 of 100 cases and leads only to A02 — so A02 now occupies the mandatory, early position A08 held before, and A08 has become skippable. Alongside this, A01, A06 and A07 are executed in fewer cases, A03 now sometimes leads directly to A01 where before it led only to A06, and A06 now leads to A08 instead of A02. [DIR001, DIR004, DIR002, DIR003, OCC002, OCC006, OCC001, OCC004, OCC005]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor changes are observed directly in the routing profiles, and the participation, co-occurrence/order and gap items all point the same way, with nearly every item showing an empirical strength of about 0.995 (larger than about 199 of 200 random re-splits). The pattern is structural rather than a re-weighting of existing paths: A02 moved from about half of traces to all of them, A08 from all to about 45 in 100, and four source activities changed their successor sets. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, OCC002, OCC006, REL009, REL021, LAG042, LAG012]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out: an unobserved edge is not a removed edge — A01 and A08 themselves appear in far fewer traces later, so A02→A01 and A08→A03/A04 may still exist at low frequency; likewise, the lower participation of A01, A06 and A07 could be a downstream consequence of the A02/A08 re-routing rather than an independent change. Both windows are also only 200-trace samples. [DIR001, DIR004, OCC001, OCC004, OCC005]

Other differences: A03's and A04's positions within the trace are essentially unchanged (medians about 0.17 and 0.5 in both windows) [POS003, POS004]; A01 now sits earlier in the trace (median about 0.57 → 0.33) and A06/A07 slightly later when they occur [POS001, POS005, POS006]; repeated occurrences of the same activity now follow one another about one step sooner (self-gaps shifting from about 5 to about 4 steps for A01, A02, A03 and A08) [LAG001, LAG010, LAG019, LAG046].
