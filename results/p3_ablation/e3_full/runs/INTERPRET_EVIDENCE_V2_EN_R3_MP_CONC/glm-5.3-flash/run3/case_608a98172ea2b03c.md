### Observed differences

- **Routing out of A01 collapsed to one successor.** In the earlier window, A01's outgoing transitions split three ways — to A04 in about 50 in 100, to A03 in about 29 in 100, to A02 in about 22 in 100; in the later window, all of A01's roughly 380 outgoing transitions go to A04. [DIR001]
- **A04 lost A01 as a direct successor.** Earlier, A04 led back to A01 in about half of its outgoing transitions, to A02 in about 26 in 100 and to A03 in about 23 in 100; in the later window it leads only to A03 (about 53 in 100) and A02 (about 47 in 100), never directly to A01. [DIR002]
- **A06's exit became fixed.** Earlier, A06 was followed by A01 or A04 in roughly equal shares (about 50/50); in the later window it always leads to A01. [DIR003]
- **The order between A01 and A04 tightened from mixed to fixed.** Earlier, A01 preceded A04 only in about 25 in 100 traces, A04 preceded A01 without the reverse in about 28 in 100, and both orders occurred within about 47 in 100 traces; in the later window A01 precedes A04 in about 58 in 100 traces, both orders in about 42 in 100, and traces where only A04 precedes A01 have disappeared. [REL003]
- **Positions shifted accordingly.** A01 now sits earlier within traces (median relative position 0.45 → 0.0, mean 0.40 → 0.29) and A04 later (mean 0.39 → 0.50, 25th percentile 0.0 → 0.27), while A02, A05 and A06 keep their positions. [POS001, POS003, POS002, POS004, POS005]
- **Pairwise distances moved consistently with one re-placement of the optional group A02 → A05 → A06.** A01 reaches A04 within five steps now always at one step's distance (earlier a mix of one, four and five steps); A04 reaches A01 only at about four steps (earlier mostly one); A01→A03 is now always two steps apart (earlier about 55/45 between one and two) and A04→A03 always one (earlier about 45/55); A01→A02 moved from one–two steps to always two, A04→A02 from one–two to always one; A06→A01 is now always direct and A06→A04 always two steps (via A01); self-repeats of A01 and of A04 now sit only five steps apart (earlier four–five). [LAG004, LAG009, LAG003, LAG011, LAG002, LAG010, LAG017, LAG018, LAG001, LAG012]
- **The group's internal behaviour is stable; only a small participation dip appears.** A02, A05 and A06 still occur only together (about 42 in 100 later traces vs about 47 in 100 earlier), still run A02 before A05 before A06, and still finish before A03; their participation fell by about 5 in 100 traces, a difference smaller than typical random re-splits produce. [REL008, REL015, REL006, OCC002, OCC005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A placement and ordering hypothesis is supported: in the later window, every trace appears to run A01 directly into A04, the optional group A02 → A05 → A06 (occurring in about 42 in 100 traces) always executes as one unit immediately after A04 and always returns to A01, and A03 always directly follows the last A04. In the earlier window the same activities were arranged more loosely: A01 could pass to A02, A03 or A04, A04 could pass to A01, A02 or A03, the group could be entered from A01 or from A04 and exit to either, and A03 could directly follow A01 as well as A04. The difference is therefore a tightening of the ordering constraints around the A01–A04 alternation and the group's attachment point, with A01 moving to the start of traces and A04 later — not a replacement of activities, all of which keep substantial participation in both windows. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL003, POS001, POS003, LAG003, LAG011, LAG009]

Supporting signs: the successor redistributions are direct and large, each based on about 380 outgoing transitions per window and exceeding about 995 in 1000 random re-splits; the A01/A04 order change, the position shifts, and roughly fifteen distance distributions all point in the same direction; and the group's internal order and its relation to A03 are unchanged, isolating the difference to where the group attaches rather than to its composition. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL003, LAG001, LAG012, REL006, REL011, REL012]

Limitations: the items largely describe one underlying reordering, so they are not independent confirmations, and pairwise distances cannot fully reconstruct where the group sat in the earlier window; in particular, the evidence cannot rule out that the earlier window mixed two fixed order variants rather than one flexible order. [LAG001, LAG012, LAG007, LAG008]

Other differences: A02, A05 and A06 each appear in about 42 in 100 later traces versus about 47 in 100 earlier, and the repetition support of A01 and A04 slipped from 0.47 to 0.42; these differences are smaller than those typically produced by random re-splits (empirical strength about 0.03) and are best read as sampling variation rather than part of the change. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC005]
