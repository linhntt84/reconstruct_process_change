### Observed differences

- A01 and A04 are no longer observed: in the earlier window A01 appeared exactly once in every trace (200 of 200, always at the first event position) and A04 in about 51 in 100 traces (102 of 200); in the later window neither appears in any of the 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC004, POS001, POS004]

Rare edges from A05 and A06 back towards A01/A04, based on only 6 and 14 observations, also vanished. [DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- The surviving activities now form two immediate two-step sequences: A02 is followed by A06 in 100 of 100 observed transitions (earlier about 79, with about 21 at a distance of 2–3 steps), and A03 is followed by A05 in 100 of 100 (earlier about 95). [DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process no longer executes its opening activity A01 and the middle activity A04, leaving two short, fixed sequences. Earlier, every trace began with A01 and continued either with A03 alone (about 49 in 100 traces) or with the A02+A04 pair (about 51 in 100), closing with A05 or A06 as mutually exclusive alternatives; in the later window only two variants remain — A02 immediately followed by A06 (about 47 in 100 traces) and A03 immediately followed by A05 (about 53 in 100). The support is direct and consistent: participation falls from 100% to 0% for A01 and from 51% to 0% for A04; all five of A01's activity relations dissolve; A02, which never appeared without A04 earlier, now runs without it; the lag-2/3 gaps in A02→A06, where A04 previously sat, close to immediate succession; and the surviving activities shift to the first two event positions while A05/A06 keep their final position. Because A01 and A04 are entirely unobserved later, this is a structural removal rather than a re-weighting of existing paths. The evidence cannot rule out that A01/A04 work still occurs below the resolution of 200 traces or is now recorded under other labels, nor can it distinguish genuine removal from a change in how traces are captured; no chance-expectation baseline is included, so the judgement rests on the directness and internal consistency of the items.
