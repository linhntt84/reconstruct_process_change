### Observed differences

- A01 shifted from an early to a late position in the traces: its mean relative position rose from about 0.26 (median about 0.17) in the earlier window to about 0.65 (median about 0.67) in the later window, with a somewhat tighter spread. [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A specific hypothesis supported by the evidence is that A01 moved from an early stage to a late stage of the process: earlier it executed near the start, typically before A05, A06 and A07 and about evenly split with A02 and A04; in the later window A02, A04, A05 and A07 execute before A01 — A07 typically immediately before it — and A01 now leads mainly into A03. The change is shown directly by the order reversals in all five pairs involving A01, by A01's large position shift while the other activities moved only slightly, by the flipped direction of within-five-steps proximity, and by the successor redistributions at A01, A07 and A04. Participation itself did not change — every activity appeared in every trace and each pair co-occurred in 100 in 100 traces in both windows — and because the order relations themselves flipped rather than only path shares, a pure change in how often existing paths are taken does not fit. Note that these items largely describe one underlying behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, POS001, LAG002, LAG011, LAG016, LAG028, LAG030, DIR001, DIR003, DIR006]

The evidence cannot fully distinguish a relocation of A01 itself from an equivalent reordering that placed the other activities ahead of it, although A01's much larger position shift favours the former; positions are event orders rather than clock times, A01's order relative to A06 remains about balanced in the later window, and DIR shares are computed over outgoing transitions rather than cases. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL004, LAG003]

Other differences: pairs and lags not involving A01 changed only slightly — order shares moved by at most about 7 in 100 traces with low empirical strength (A02/A04, A02/A05, A04/A05, A05/A07, A06/A07) — and A02's outgoing mix shifted toward A05 (about 27 to 40 in 100) and away from A01 (about 26 to 11 in 100), with A02→A05 more often immediate; these are consistent with, but not needed for, the relocation of A01. [REL006, REL007, REL010, REL012, REL013, DIR002, LAG008]
