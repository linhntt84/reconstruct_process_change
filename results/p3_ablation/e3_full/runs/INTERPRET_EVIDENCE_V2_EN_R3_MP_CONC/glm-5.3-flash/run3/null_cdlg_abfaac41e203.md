### Observed differences

- Participation is unchanged: in both windows every trace contains all six activities A01–A06 (each activity appears in 100% of traces), and every tested activity pair occurs together in all traces. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL001]

- Relative order shifted by a few percentage points, most visibly around A02, A04 and A05. In the later window A02 precedes A01 in about 80 in 100 traces (about 86 before), A03 in 45 (51), A05 in 90 (95) and A06 in 86 (90); A04 precedes A05 in about 83 in 100 (90), A06 in 75 (80) and A01 in 65 (68), while A03 precedes A04 more often (about 69 vs 64). [REL001, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL010, REL011, REL003, REL007]

- Mean event positions moved slightly: A02 from about 0.16 to 0.20 of the trace and A04 from about 0.45 to 0.49 (both later), A05 from about 0.96 to 0.94 (earlier), with A01 and A06 each about 0.02 earlier and position spreads slightly wider. [POS002, POS004, POS005, POS001, POS006]

- Some step gaps changed: A01→A04 occurrences at distance 4 rose from about 21% to 40%, A03→A02 at distance 4 from about 6% to 15%, while A02→A01 gaps concentrated at distance 2 (about 37% to 51%). [LAG003, LAG012, LAG006]

- Outgoing routing shares moved by a few percentage points: from A02, transitions to A01 fell from about 16% to 10% while those to A04 rose to about 70%; from A03 and A04, transitions to A01 rose (about 39%→45% and 46%→52%); from A05, transitions to A02 rose from about 39% to 49%, but only about 23 and 39 outgoing transitions were observed (weak). - All of these differences are small (roughly 1–8 percentage points), and the empirical strengths are spread as expected when nothing has changed: only about 4 of the 49 indicators exceed 0.9 and about 12 exceed 0.75 — close to the roughly 5 and 12 expected from random re-splits — while many items sit near 0. [REL010, POS005, LAG014, DIR001, REL002]

### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. A coherent reading of the shifts (A02 and A04 occurring somewhat later, A05 somewhat earlier within traces) is directly visible in the relational and positional items, but its magnitude is only a few percentage points and the spread of empirical strengths across all 49 indicators matches what random re-splits of an unchanged process produce, so sampling variability between the two 200-trace windows cannot be ruled out.
