### Observed differences

- Activity A02 was not observed in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it occurs exactly once in every trace (participation 100%, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, never repeated). [OCC001]
- In the later window A02 always sits at the very end of the trace: its median and both quartiles are at the final position with no spread. In the earlier window it had no observed position at all. [POS001]
- In the earlier window A01 occurred in all 200 traces without A02; in the later window A01 and A02 always occur together, with A01 first in 100% of traces. The same holds for A03, A04 and A06: each precedes A02 in 100% of later traces, whereas earlier they never co-occurred with A02. [REL001, REL003, REL004, REL006]
- A06 was the closing activity earlier: its mean relative position was about 0.99 (last in nearly all traces), and an outgoing transition from A06 was observable in only 4 of 200 traces, all leading to A04. In the later window A06 sits at about 0.79 of the trace and its observed successor is A02 in all 200 traces. [POS005, DIR005]
- A05 remains optional in both windows, present in about 50 in 100 earlier traces and 53 in 100 later traces. Earlier it appeared either without A02 or not at all; later it always co-occurs with A02, coming before it in 29 in 100 traces and interleaved with it (repeats) in 24 in 100. [OCC002, REL005]
- The other activities kept their participation and mutual order (for example A01 still leads to A04 in about 72 in 100 traces versus 75 earlier), but their relative positions shifted slightly earlier (A03 mean 0.48 to 0.36, A04 0.36 to 0.28, A05 0.69 to 0.58), which is consistent with one event being appended at the end rather than with re-ordering among them. [POS002, POS003, POS004, DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a mandatory closing step A02 was appended to the end of every trace. Earlier, traces ended with A06 (last in nearly all 200 traces); in the later window every trace contains A02 exactly once at the final position, preceded by A06 and by A01, A03 and A04, and — when it occurs — by A05, whose participation is unchanged at about half of the traces. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation flipped from 0 of 200 to 200 of 200 traces, A02's position is constant at the trace end, four relational items show every other activity before A02 in 100% of traces, and A06's observed successor changed from A04 (only 4 observations) to A02 (200 observations); each of these differences exceeds what is seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the two windows. [OCC001, POS001, REL001, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR005, POS005]

An important competing explanation remains: since A02 was never observed earlier, the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely new step from one that occurred below the observation rate before and near-universally afterwards, and with anonymous activity codes a re-labelling of an existing activity cannot be excluded. The package also reports no chance-expectation baseline for the number of flagged items, so the judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the relational and positional evidence. [OCC001]

Other differences: small shifts that the evidence does not tie into a separate change — A03's outgoing mix moved a few points (to A06 from about 41 in 100 to 32, to A05 from 37 to 41, to A04 from 22 to 27), A04 preceded A06 in 100% of later traces versus 98% earlier, A03 was followed by A04 within one step in about 95 in 100 later cases versus 86 earlier, the gap between A03 and A06 shifted slightly toward larger distances, and A05's self-repetition gaps changed slightly. These are minor relative to the sample sizes and may reflect sampling variation or knock-on effects of the longer traces. [DIR002, REL010, LAG005, LAG007, LAG011]
