### Observed differences

- **A04 is no longer observed and A01 appears at the same point in the flow.** In the earlier window, A04 ran in about 98 of 200 cases (roughly half), exactly once per case, and every observed A04 was immediately followed by A03. In the later window, A04 is not observed at all (0 of 200), while A01 — not observed earlier — runs in about 99 of 200 cases (roughly half), once per case, and every observed A01 is immediately followed by A03. Both differences are larger than in about 99 of 100 random re-splits of the pooled cases. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003]

- **A01 reproduces the relational profile A04 had.** Both activities co-occur only with A03, A06 and A08 (the three activities present in every case), and in essentially every such case they execute before them — about 49 in 100 cases overall in the later window for A01, about 49 in 100 in the earlier window for A04. A01 and A04 never appear in the same case in either window, and neither co-occurs with any of the other optional activities (A02, A05, A07, A09–A15). [REL002, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL028, REL038, REL040, REL016, REL037]

- **The order between A07 and A13 became flexible.** Earlier, A07 and A13 always ran together (about 48 of 200 cases) in a fixed order A07 then A13, with A13 leading to A03 one step later (lag 1 in about 77% of A13's observed outgoing transitions). In the later window the pair appears in about 59 of 200 cases and both orders occur: A07 first in roughly 6 of 10 joint appearances, A13 first in roughly 4 of 10. [REL069, DIR005, DIR010, LAG059]

- **Distances around the A07/A13 pair shifted by about one step.** A07 now leads directly to A03 in about 29% of its observed outgoing transitions (previously never), and its distances to A03, A06 and A08 each moved about one step closer; A13's distances to the same three activities moved about one step further. A05, which precedes the pair, now sometimes runs directly before A13 (A13 rose from 0% to about 21% of A05's outgoing transitions while A07 fell from about 42% to 21%), and A07's mean event position moved later (about 0.14 to 0.22) while A13's moved earlier (about 0.35 to 0.26). [DIR004, DIR005, DIR010, LAG013, LAG018, LAG021, LAG023, LAG024, LAG059, LAG061, LAG062, POS006, POS012]

- **Small participation shifts.** The share of cases containing the A07/A13 pair rose from about 24% to about 29.5% (48 to 59 of 200 cases), while the remaining optional activities each lost two to six percentage points of participation (for example A10 from about 27% to 21%, A09 from about 15% to 10%); on 200-case samples these are modest differences. [OCC004, OCC009, OCC006, OCC005, OCC002, OCC008, OCC010, OCC011]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 took over the position in the flow that A04 occupied earlier: before, roughly half of the cases executed A04 immediately before A03 (and before A06 and A08); in the later window A04 is not observed, and A01 appears in about the same share of cases (roughly 49–50 in 100) with an identical relational signature — always immediately before A03, always before A06 and A08, and never together with A04 or any other optional activity. This is shown directly by the occurrence counts (98 to 0 and 0 to 99 out of 200 cases), by the successor profiles (A04 was followed by A03 in 100% of its observed transitions earlier; A01 is now followed by A03 in 100% of its transitions), and by the matching co-occurrence and ordering profiles. Limitations: zero participation only shows an activity was not observed in that window, and the evidence cannot distinguish A01 being a re-labelled or re-configured A04 from a different activity taking the same slot, nor fully exclude that the disappearance and the appearance are two independent changes that coincide. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003, REL002, REL003, REL028]

Other differences: The previously fixed order between A07 and A13 became mixed — both orders now occur, roughly 60/40 in favour of A07 first — with A07's distances to A03, A06 and A08 one step shorter, A13's one step longer, and A05 now sometimes directly preceding A13; in addition, the share of cases containing the A07/A13 pair rose from about 24% to about 29.5% while the share containing neither this pair nor the A04/A01 activity fell from about 27% to about 21%, and the small participation drops in A02, A09, A10, A12, A14 and A15 are each within what a 200-case sample can produce without any change. [REL069, DIR004, DIR005, LAG021, LAG059, OCC004, OCC006, OCC002]
