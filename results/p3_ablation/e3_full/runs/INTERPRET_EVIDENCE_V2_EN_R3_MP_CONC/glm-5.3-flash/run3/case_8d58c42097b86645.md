### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was not observed in any of the 200 earlier traces; in the later window it occurs in about 21 in 100 traces (42 of 200), roughly 0.26 times per trace, around the middle of the trace (median relative position about 0.5) [OCC001, POS001].
- The successions out of A08 changed: in the earlier window about 55 in 100 of A08's observed outgoing transitions went to A10 and about 45 to A09; in the later window about 55 go to A01 and about 45 still go to A09, with A10 no longer observed directly after A08 [DIR004].
- In the later window, all 51 observed outgoing transitions of A01 lead to A10, and A01 precedes A10 in about 18 of the 21 co-occurrence traces per 100 (it similarly precedes A04); A01 now co-occurs with A02, A04, A05 and the other established activities, which it never did earlier [DIR001, REL001, REL003, REL009].
- Distances across this point grew by about one step: the A08→A10 pair is now mostly two steps apart (earlier mostly one), A08→A02 and A09→A05 are each typically one step further apart, and A10 sits later in the trace (median relative position about 0.47 → 0.57) [LAG048, LAG040, LAG051, POS009].
- A separate rewiring involves A03, A12 and A13: earlier, A03 led to A13 in about two thirds of its outgoing transitions and to A12 in about one third, and A12 always led back to A03; later, A03 always leads to A13 and A12 always leads to A13. A12 now also follows A02 directly (about 4 in 100 of A02's outgoing transitions, previously unobserved), sits earlier (mean relative position about 0.37 → 0.24, typically one step after A02 in about two thirds of the 16 observed pairs), and A03–A12 co-occurrence fell from about 5 to about 2 in 100 traces; A03 itself occurs somewhat less often (about 12.5 → 9 in 100 traces) [DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, POS011, LAG011, LAG069, REL030, OCC003].
- Smaller shifts: A07, A08 and A09 occur slightly later and A13 earlier (about 0.50 → 0.38); several lag differences rest on only 1–7 observed pairs and were not relied upon [POS006, POS007, POS008, POS012, LAG041, LAG068].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new activity, A01, was added to the flow between A08 and A10. In the earlier window, A08 was followed directly by A10 in about 55 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (A09 taking the rest); in the later window, that link runs via A01 instead — A01 appears in about 21 in 100 traces around mid-trace, all of its observed outgoing transitions lead to A10, and pairs spanning the point (A08→A10, A08→A02, A09→A05) are typically one step further apart, with A10 shifted later. The change is shown directly by the successor profiles of A08 and A01 and is consistent across occurrence, position, relation and lag indicators, with the key differences larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits. Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows A01 was unobserved, not provably absent, and these samples cannot establish whether any direct A08→A10 path remains; a rare variant surfacing only in the later window cannot be fully excluded, although the coordinated successor swap and one-step lag shifts make pure sampling noise unlikely [OCC001, DIR001, DIR004, POS001, POS009, LAG040, LAG048].

Other differences: The A03–A12–A13 rewiring is a distinct change not explained by A01: the earlier A03→A12→A03 succession (A03 to A12 in about one third of its outgoing transitions, A12 always returning to A03) is replaced by A03→A13 and A12→A13, with A12 now typically following A02 directly and sitting about one step earlier, and A03–A12 co-occurrence nearly gone [DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, POS011, LAG011, LAG069, REL030].
