### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A03 was executed exactly once in every case (200 of 200 traces), always as the final event; in the later window A03 is not observed at all (0 of 200 traces). [OCC001, POS003]
- Before, every case ended with the fixed sequence A02 → A03: A02 always sat at relative position 0.8 and A03 at 1.0. In the later window A02 is itself the last event (position 1.0 in every trace), and no outgoing transition from A02 is observed, whereas previously all of A02's outgoing flow went to A03. [POS002, POS003, DIR002, REL005]
- A03 co-occurred with A01, A02, A04, A05 and A06 in 100% of earlier traces but in 0% of later traces, while each of those partner activities still occurs in every later trace. [REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]
- Participation of the remaining activities is unchanged: A01, A02, A04, A05 and A06 each occur in 100% of traces in both windows. [POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- The upward position shifts of the surviving activities (A04 mean about 0.14 → 0.18, A05 about 0.39 → 0.49, A06 about 0.59 → 0.75, A02 from 0.8 to exactly 1.0) match what removing one trailing event would produce, rather than a reordering among these activities. [POS002, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Routing between the surviving activities is essentially stable: A01 still leads to A04 in about 72 in 100 cases and to A05 in about 24 in 100; A04 returns to A01 in about 24 in 100 cases; A05 leads to A06 and A06 to A02 in about 98 in 100 cases. All shifts are at most about 1.5 percentage points with low empirical strength. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL002, REL003, REL004]
- Minor: among the rework returns A04 → A01, the immediate return (lag 1) rose from about 81 in 100 to about 89 in 100, but this rests on only about 55–57 such returns per window. [LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the final step A03 is no longer executed: in the earlier window every case ended with A02 passing to A03, while in the later window cases end at A02 and A03 appears in none of the 200 traces. The signs are direct and mutually consistent — participation falls from 200 of 200 to 0 of 200 traces, all five relational profiles involving A03 flip from always co-occurring (always as the last activity) to never co-occurring while the partners still occur in every trace, and A02 loses its only observed outgoing transition. The stable participation and routing of A01, A02, A04, A05 and A06 localise the difference to A03, and the position shifts of the other activities are consistent with traces simply becoming one event shorter. Note that these items largely describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. Limitation: zero participation only means A03 was not observed in the later sample, so a residual execution rarer than roughly 1 in 100 cases, or a relabelling of A03 under a new activity code, cannot be ruled out. [OCC001, POS003, DIR002, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, POS002, POS004, POS005, POS006, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

Other differences: the small redistributions in A01's outgoing split (A02 and A06 each down about 1.5 points in favour of A04 and A05), the back-edge shares of A04, A05 and A06, and the slightly more immediate A04 → A01 rework return are all within what random re-splits of stable behaviour typically produce (empirical strengths mostly below 0.55, and very few observations for the A05 → A01 return) and are not part of the main hypothesis.
