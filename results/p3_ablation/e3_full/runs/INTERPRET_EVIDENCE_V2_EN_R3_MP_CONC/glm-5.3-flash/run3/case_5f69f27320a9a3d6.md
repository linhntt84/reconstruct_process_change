### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural reorganisation of the segment between A02 and A07: A01, previously unobserved, was inserted into the flow — it now runs in every case, always after A02 and before A03 and A07, at a variable slot in or around the A05/A06 block (directly after A02 in roughly 38 of 100 of A02's outgoing transitions, otherwise after A05 or A06, and between A05 and A06 in roughly a fifth to a third of their ordered pairs) — while A04 was dropped, with A03 now leading directly to A07 instead of via A04. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL006, LAG004, LAG006]

The hypothesis is shown directly by relational and successor evidence rather than by frequencies alone, and the participation extremes (0 to 200 and 200 to 0 out of 200 traces) are far beyond what sampling variation in a stable process produces; every item, including the position and lag shifts, fits this one structure with no contradicting item. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR003, REL003, POS002]

Limitations: the evidence constrains order and co-occurrence, not clock time, and it cannot show whether the A01 insertion and the A04 removal were one coordinated restructuring or two independent changes; A04's absence means only that it was not observed in this sample. [OCC002, POS004]

Other differences: A05 and A06 sit about 0.1 positions later and A03 about 0.2 positions later in the later window, and the mutual order of A05 and A06 remained mixed (roughly 50/50) in both windows; these look like consequences of the insertion and removal rather than independent changes. [POS002, POS004, POS005, REL012]
