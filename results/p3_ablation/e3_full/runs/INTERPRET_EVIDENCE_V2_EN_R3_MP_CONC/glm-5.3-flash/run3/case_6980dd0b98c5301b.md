### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the order of the closing sequence changed: the tail A06 → A07 → A01 in the earlier window became A01 → A06 → A07 in the later window. A01 moved from the end of every trace to directly after A08 and ahead of A06 and A07; A07 became the final activity, and A06 and A07 each shifted one position later. Because all three activities occur in 200 of 200 traces in both windows and always co-occur, this is a reordering of a fixed sequence, not a path being taken more or less often or an activity becoming optional. [REL001, REL002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

The support is direct and consistent: the pairwise order flips appear in 100 in 100 traces, the successor changes at A01, A07 and A08 agree with them, and the lag changes measured from four stable anchors (A03, A04, A05, A08) match the predicted shifts exactly. These items are facets of a single underlying difference, not independent confirmations. [REL001, REL002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG004, LAG005, LAG008]

Limitations: no screening expectation was provided, so the item count cannot be compared against a chance baseline; the judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the relational and positional evidence. The evidence also covers only these activities and distances up to five steps, so it cannot rule out that the reordering is part of a broader change elsewhere in the trace.
