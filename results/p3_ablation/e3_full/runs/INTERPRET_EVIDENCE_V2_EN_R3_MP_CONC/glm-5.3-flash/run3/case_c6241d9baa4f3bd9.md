### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A07 is no longer executed in the later window and that the flow it carried now runs without it: A03, which previously handed over to A07 in about 39 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (with A07 continuing to A06 in about 92 in 100), now leads directly to A06 in about 51 in 100, so the earlier chain A03 → A07 → A06 appears as a direct A03 → A06, and the traces that used to contain A07 are consistent with being one event shorter. The disappearance itself is documented directly — participation fell from 106 to 0 of 200 traces with empty position and outgoing-transition profiles — and the rewiring at A03 is observed directly rather than inferred. [OCC005, POS005, DIR002, DIR004]

The reading is consistent across indicator families: all nine of A07's relations collapse to zero, and the step-distance changes (pairs spanning the missing position moving about one step closer) go in the direction this structural reading predicts.
