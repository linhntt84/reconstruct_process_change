### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural rewiring of the control flow in which A02, A03 and A08 dropped out of the observed behaviour and A01 was repositioned. Before, flow ran A05/A18 → A02 → A10 → A08 → A11 and A13/A19 → A03 → A05/A18, with A16 → A01 → {A06, A07, A14} closing the loop; in the later window A05/A18 feed A10 directly, A13/A19 feed A05/A18 directly, A10 → A01 → A11 replaces the A08 hop, and A16 connects straight to A06, A07 and A14, the targets A01 used to serve. As a consequence, A07 and A14 are reached less often and now end traces, while A09 and A17 repeat more often. [DIR001, DIR009, DIR013, DIR004, DIR015, DIR011, DIR016, DIR007, OCC002, OCC003, OCC008]

The hypothesis is supported directly and consistently: successor profiles change exactly along this rewiring (DIR), the A01–A16 and A01–A11 order relations flip and the step distances shift as expected (REL, LAG), and A02, A03 and A08 fall from 94, 41 and 94 supporting traces to none (OCC, POS). The pattern agrees across all five indicator families, and nearly all qualifying items sit at the maximum empirical strength — differences larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data — which a stable process essentially does not produce in such numbers. [DIR001, DIR009, DIR013, REL009, REL014, LAG032, LAG070, OCC002, OCC003, OCC008, POS002, POS003, POS007]

Limitation: zero participation only establishes that A02, A03 and A08 were not exercised in the later sample of 200 traces; the evidence cannot distinguish a permanent removal from these activities becoming optional and skipped in every later case. Several items also describe the same underlying rerouting (for example DIR001, REL009 and LAG032 all reflect A01's new place), so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC008, DIR001, REL009, LAG032]

Other differences: the new mutual succession between A09 and A17 and their higher repetition rates are consistent with the rerouting but not entailed by it, and the evidence does not establish why they emerged; A12 and A13 show small participation increases (about 12 → 15 and 11 → 12 in 100 traces) with weak empirical strength. [DIR008, DIR014, OCC009, OCC017, OCC012, OCC013]
