### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a new activity A01 was interposed between A12 and its successors A04 and A13: earlier, A12 passed control straight to A04 or A13 in a roughly 50/50 split; later, A12 always leads to A01, which then leads to A04 (about half) or A13 (about half). The hypothesis involves A01 (new, in about 30 in 100 later traces), A12 (rerouted entirely to A01), and A04/A13 (now reached one position later), with A07, A08 and A09 remaining in the same repeating sub-process — A07 and A12 now occur only in traces that also contain A01. Supporting signs are direct and consistent: the complete successor switch of A12, A01's successor profile matching A12's previous one, the one-position shift in A12→A04/A13 distances, and the co-occurrence pattern. Limitations: no screening summary is included, so the volume of qualifying items cannot be checked against a chance expectation; A01's zero earlier participation strictly means only that it was not observed before; and the slight drop in repetitions of A04, A07, A08, A09 and A12 suggests the sub-process may have changed slightly more than by the added step alone, which the evidence cannot separate from the interposition itself.
