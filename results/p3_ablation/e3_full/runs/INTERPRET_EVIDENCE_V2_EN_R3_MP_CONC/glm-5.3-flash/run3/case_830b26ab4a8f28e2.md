### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was removed from the process (or is no longer executed) and that its predecessors now bypass it. In the earlier window every case ran A01 about 3.5 times, and A03 and A05 handed over to A01 in roughly 85–90 in 100 of their outgoing transitions; in the later window A01 is not observed in any of 200 cases, A03 and A05 lead directly into A08, A09 and A10 — the activities A01 used to feed — and the step distances from A03, A05 and A06 into the following block shortened, as expected once A01 no longer sits between them. This is shown directly and consistently across occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators, with empirical strengths of about 0.995 on the core items; a mere shift in how often existing paths are taken does not fit, because A01 is wholly absent from the later window rather than less frequent. The main limitation is that zero participation only means A01 was not observed in the later sample, so the evidence cannot show why it stopped appearing or exclude that it still occurs at a very low rate; also, no screening expectation was provided, so the judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the items rather than a comparison with a chance baseline.
