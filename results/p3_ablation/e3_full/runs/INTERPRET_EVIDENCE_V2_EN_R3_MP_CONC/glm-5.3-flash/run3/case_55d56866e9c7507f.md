### Observed differences

- The clearest difference concerns the alternative step A09-or-A11. In the earlier window A09 ran in about 30 of 100 traces and A11 in about 29, the two never appeared in the same trace, and about 42 of 100 traces contained neither. In the later window A09 ran in about 44 of 100 traces and A11 in about 57, again never together, and no trace contained neither: every later trace executed exactly one of the two. [OCC006, OCC008, REL053]

- The step also sits earlier in the trace. Its mean position moved from about 0.96–0.97 (the final events) to about 0.74–0.75, and even relative to the start activity A01 it now follows after about 2–3 events instead of 4–5. Before, A09 and A11 never co-occurred with A04, A05, A07 or A10; in the later window they co-occur with A05 in about 20 (A09) and 27 (A11) of 100 traces, with A04 in about 13 and 12, and with A07/A10 in about 7–15 — typically before A05 and A07, and in both orders relative to A04. Earlier, every trace with A09 or A11 also contained A08; in the later window about 45 of 100 A09-traces and 47 of 100 A11-traces run without A08. [POS009, POS011, LAG008, LAG010, REL031, REL033, REL037, REL039, REL046, REL048, REL052, REL054, REL049, REL051, OCC006, OCC008]

- The connections into and out of the step were rewired. A06 now leads directly into A11 in about 31 of 100 of its observed outgoing transitions and into A09 in about 23 (about 10 of 100 before), usually immediately (immediate-following share about 53–55 of 100, versus about 13–22 before), while A08's outgoing transitions to A09/A11 fell from about 63 to about 29 of 100; A06's other successors (A02, A03, A08) now tend to follow it one to two positions later than before. Out of the step, A09 and A11 now lead into A04, A05, A07, A08 and A10, where before the few observed outgoing transitions led back to A02, A03 and A06; A05 is now followed by A09 or A11 in about 77 of 100 of its observed outgoing transitions (26 transitions, versus 9 before, which mostly went to A01, A03 and A06). [DIR006, LAG038, LAG040, DIR008, LAG032, LAG033, LAG037, DIR009, DIR011, DIR005]

- Smaller shifts: A08 and A05 remain mutually exclusive alternatives, their shares moving from about 59/41 to 54/46 of 100 traces; A02 and A08 participation each slipped from about 59 to 54 of 100; A04 rose from about 18 to 25 of 100, with its median position moving earlier (0.75 to 0.6). [REL036, OCC001, OCC005, OCC002, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative step formed by A09 and A11 became a mandatory choice and was placed earlier in the flow. In the earlier window the step was optional — about 42 of 100 traces skipped it — and when it ran (A09 in about 30, A11 in about 29, never both) it appeared only alongside A08 at the very end of traces. In the later window exactly one of A09 or A11 runs in every trace, A06 feeds it directly in about half of A06's observed outgoing transitions, and the step now precedes the late activities: it co-occurs with A04, A05, A07 and A10, which never co-occurred with it before, and typically runs before A05, whose observed outgoing transitions now go to A09 or A11 in most cases. [OCC006, OCC008, REL053, REL049, REL051, DIR006, LAG038, LAG040, REL031, REL033, REL037, REL039, REL046, REL048, REL052, REL054, DIR005]

Supporting signs: the pattern is consistent across the occurrence, relation, direction, position and lag indicators; the successor sets of A09 and A11 now point into the late activities (A04, A05, A07, A08, A10) instead of back to A02, A03 and A06; and the disappearance of traces without A09/A11 (83 of 200 to 0) is far outside what two 200-trace samples produce by chance. [DIR009, DIR011, POS009, POS011, REL053]

Limitations: the earlier-window successor profiles of A05, A09 and A11 rest on only about 9, 7 and 10 observed outgoing transitions, so the precise previous wiring around the step is uncertain; and the evidence cannot distinguish a removed skip option from one that has become very rare — a residual skip rate of up to roughly 1–2 of 100 traces would go unobserved in this sample. [DIR005, DIR009, DIR011, REL053]

Other differences: A08 and A05 remain mutually exclusive alternatives with shares shifting from about 59/41 to 54/46 of 100 traces, A02 and A08 participation each slipped from about 59 to 54 of 100, and A04 participation rose from about 18 to 25 of 100 with its median position moving earlier; these participation changes are separate from the hypothesis above. [REL036, OCC001, OCC005, OCC002, POS004]
