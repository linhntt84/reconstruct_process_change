### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new activity A01 was added between A02/A04 and the rest of the process, while the former closing activity A09 was removed. In the later window, A01 runs exactly once per case, reached from A02 in about half of the cases and from A04 in most of the rest, handing over mainly to A04 and to A07, A03 and A10; A04's direct shares to A07, A10, A03 and A05 shrank accordingly (for example A04→A07 from about 37 to 23 in 100). As a result, A03, A05, A06, A07, A08, A10 and A04 each run about one event later, a shift that appears consistently in both the position distributions and the gaps from A02. A09, which previously ran in about 45 in 100 cases as the final step immediately after A06, is no longer observed, and A06 now terminates those cases itself. The hypothesis is shown directly by the directional and relational profiles (new edges A02→A01 and A04→A01, disappearance of A06→A09, A01 co-occurring with every activity while A09 co-occurs with none) and corroborated by positions and gaps; every strongly changed item exceeds the differences seen in 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. One limitation is that A01's exact placement stays mixed between "after A02" and "after A04". [OCC001, OCC007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR006, REL001, REL003, REL042, POS005, POS008, LAG004]

Other differences: Routing shares within the middle of the process moved slightly — A03 now leads to A10 in about 44 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (before about 38) and to A06 in about 49 (before about 58), with a matching rise in A10→A06 (about 41→47); differences of this size still arise in about three tenths to half of random re-splits of an unchanged process, so they may be sampling variation. A few backward-direction gap items (for example A06→A04 and A11→A04) rest on only 1–4 observations and carry little weight. [DIR003, DIR010, LAG023, LAG034]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a wholesale relabelling of the anonymous activity codes between the windows; A01's profile (every case, early position, many successors) is, however, very different from A09's (45 in 100 cases, final position, single predecessor A06), so A01 does not simply take over A09's role. The evidence file also contains no screening expectation, so the judgement rests on the directness and consistency of these profiles rather than on a chance-level item count.
