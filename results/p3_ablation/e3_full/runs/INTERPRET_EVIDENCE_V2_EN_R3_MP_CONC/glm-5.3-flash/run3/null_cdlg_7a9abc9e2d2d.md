### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence
The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. All observed differences are small (roughly 2–8 cases in 100 on windows of 200 cases each) and their empirical strengths are distributed as random re-splits of an unchanged process typically produce — about 9 in 100 of the 187 items exceed 0.9, close to the 10 in 100 expected by chance — so the mutually agreeing items (for example the four branch activities, which move together because they share the same traces) do not constitute independent confirmation of a change.
