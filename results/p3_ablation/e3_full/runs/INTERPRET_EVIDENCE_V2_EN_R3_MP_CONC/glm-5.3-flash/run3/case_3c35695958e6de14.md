### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a step A01 was placed between the entry and rework points of the process and the repeatedly executed A03/A12 block: earlier, A02, A09 and the A08–A13 cycle fed directly into A03 or A12, whereas in the later window all of these pass through A01, which then routes to A03 or A12, with A01 executing about 2.1 times per trace — the same frequency as A03 and A12. The signs are direct and consistent: A01's only observed successors are A03 and A12; A02 and A09 now lead exclusively to A01; the A08/A13 cycle exits through A01; A01 co-occurs with every other activity exactly as often as that activity occurs at all; and every downstream distance from A02, A04 and A09 increased by exactly one step, the pattern expected from a single new placement rather than from sampling variation. A limitation is that zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that A01 was not observed there, so a genuinely new step cannot be distinguished from an existing one that was previously never executed; the finite windows also cannot show whether the drop in A13's frequency is a consequence of the rerouting or an independent change.
