### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was not observed in any of the 200 earlier traces; in the later window it occurs exactly once in every one of the 200 traces, with no repetitions. [OCC001]
- In the later window, A01 sits late in the case (median normalized position about 0.8). It co-occurs with A08 in every trace and always precedes it, and it always follows A02 and A03; with A04, A05 and A09 it co-occurs in roughly half of the traces and then always comes after them. In the earlier window, A01 co-occurred with none of these activities. [POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL007, REL008]
- A01's observed outgoing flow in the later window goes to A08 in about 72 in 100 cases, to A07 in about 17 and to A06 in about 11. [DIR001]
- Routing into A08 changed: before, about 60 in 100 of A04's outgoing transitions and about 69 in 100 of A09's went directly to A08; after, about 88 in 100 of A04's and about 91 in 100 of A09's go to A01, and A08 no longer appears among their observed targets. A06 and A07 gained A01 as a small new target (about 10 and 11 in 100 of their transitions), and A06's share into A08 fell from about 37 to 23 in 100. [DIR004, DIR008, DIR006, DIR007]
- Measured distances into A08 lengthened by about one step: A02→A08 was within five steps in all earlier traces (split between 4 and 5 steps, roughly 52/48) and now sits at 5 steps in the 107 of 200 traces where it is still within five steps; A04→A08 moved from mostly 1 step (about 60 in 100) to 2–3 steps; A09→A08 from mostly 1 step (about 69 in 100) to 2–3 steps; A05→A08, A06→A08, A07→A08 and A03→A08 shifted upward similarly. - A02's own routing stayed essentially unchanged, still sending about 82 in 100 of its transitions to A03. [DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new activity A01 was inserted into the flow between the middle activities and A08: cases that previously reached A08 directly from A04 or A09 now pass through A01, which runs exactly once per case, after A02/A03 and after A04/A05/A09, and leads predominantly to A08 (with A07 or A06 as its immediate successor in roughly 27 in 100 cases). The signs are direct and mutually consistent: A01 went from 0 of 200 traces to all 200, a flip larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits; it precedes A08 in every trace while A04's and A09's direct transitions into A08 disappeared in favour of transitions into A01; and every measured distance into A08 grew by about one step. The many agreeing relation and distance items largely describe this single insertion rather than independent changes. Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that A01 was not observed there, so a prior state in which A01 was a very rare variant cannot be completely excluded, and the routing in the minority of cases where A01 precedes A07 or A06 is only partially resolved. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR004, DIR008, REL007, LAG016, LAG031]

Other differences: A06 and A07, mutually exclusive alternatives that are well observed in both windows, took different shares (about 55/45 before vs about 47/53 after), and A03's outgoing flow shifted moderately from A06 toward A05; the evidence does not establish whether these are side effects of the rerouting or independent changes. [OCC004, OCC005, REL028, DIR003, REL016]
