### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A02 ran exactly once in every one of the 200 traces (100% participation, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, never repeated); in the later window it appears in none of the 200 traces. [OCC001, POS002]
- Earlier, A02 sat at the very start of the trace (median normalized position about 0.06) and passed control to A03 (about 40% of its outgoing transitions), A04 (about 30%), A01 (18%) and A05 (12%); in the later window it has no observed outgoing transitions at all. [POS002, DIR002]
- Predecessors dropped their edges to A02: A01 sent about 12% of its outgoing transitions to A02 earlier and none later (A04 rising from about 65% to 73%); A03 sent about 38% earlier and none later, with the shares moving to A05 (23%→35%), A01 (18%→31%) and A04 (18%→28%); A05 sent about 6% earlier and none later. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR005]
- Every traced relationship involving A02 flipped from joint execution to separation: A02 preceded A01 in 90% of earlier traces, later A01 runs alone in all of them; A02 and A03 always co-occurred (in either order), later A03 always runs alone, and the same holds for A04; A02 always preceded A07, later A07 runs alone; A05 and A06, which each co-occurred with A02 in about half of the earlier traces, never share a trace with it later. [REL001, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]
- The surviving activities sit slightly earlier in the trace and reach each other in fewer steps (A03 mean position 0.11→0.04, A04 0.60→0.53, A06 0.59→0.55; immediate succession A03→A05 up from about 49% to 69% and A05→A01 from 67% to 78%) — the pattern expected once an early activity no longer occupies a position. [POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG006, LAG015]
- A04 and A06 occur somewhat less often: A04 mean 2.19→1.94 occurrences per trace (still in every trace); A06 mean 1.19→0.94, with A06 interleaved with A01 or A03 in about 22% of later traces versus 30% earlier. [OCC002, OCC004, REL004, REL013, REL020]
- A07's outgoing transitions shifted from A01 toward A05 (about 79%→56% and 12%→33%), but this rests on only about 35 outgoing transitions per window. [DIR007, LAG025, LAG026]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One change the evidence supports is that A02 — previously a mandatory, near-start step executed once in every case — was no longer executed in the later window. Earlier it ran once in all 200 traces, preceded A01 in 90% of them and A07 in all, and handed over mainly to A03, A04, A01 and A05; later it appears in 0 of 200 traces, A01, A03 and A05 no longer route to it, and A01, A03, A04 and A07 now run without it. Because A02 is absent from the later window while every other activity remains in half or all of the traces, this is a disappearance of an activity from execution rather than a re-weighting between existing alternatives. The change is shown directly and consistently across occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators, with the key differences exceeding those seen in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data; a sampling explanation for a 200-of-200 to 0-of-200 drop is not credible. Limitations: zero participation establishes only that A02 was not observed, so the evidence cannot distinguish the activity having been removed from it still existing but never triggering, nor why it stopped, nor whether the modest repetition decrease in A04 and A06 is connected to it. [OCC001, POS002, DIR002, DIR001, DIR003, REL001, REL006, REL007, REL010]

Other differences:
- A07's successors moved toward A05 (A01 about 79%→56%, A05 about 12%→33% of its outgoing transitions); with roughly 35 outgoing transitions per window (and only 5 versus 12 for the A07→A05 pair), sampling variation cannot be ruled out. [DIR007, LAG025, LAG026]
- A04 and A06 repeat less often than before (A04 mean 2.19→1.94 per trace; A06 mean 1.19→0.94, with A06 interleaved with A01/A03 down from about 30% to 22% of traces); the evidence does not show whether this is linked to A02's disappearance. [OCC002, OCC004, REL004, REL013, REL020]
