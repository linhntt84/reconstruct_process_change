### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A01 is not observed in any of the 200 cases; in the later window it occurs exactly once in every case (200 of 200), always at the final position of the case (relative position 1.0 in all cases, no spread), and never repeats within a case. [OCC001, POS001]
- In the earlier window, A03 is the last activity of every case: it appears in 200 of 200 cases at relative position 1.0 and shows no outgoing transitions. In the later window, A03 sits directly before A01 — all of its roughly 200 observed outgoing transitions now lead to A01 — and its position moves to about 0.86, i.e., the event immediately before the final one in most cases. [POS003, DIR002, REL002]
- In the later window, every other activity precedes A01 whenever it occurs: A05, A07, A08 and A09 (each present in all cases in both windows) precede A01 in 100% of cases; A02 precedes it in the 49% of cases containing A02, A04 in 52%, A10 in 51%, A06 in 21% and A11 in 30%. In the earlier window none of these pairs co-occurred, because A01 was absent. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]
- Participation of the continuing activities is broadly stable: A02 about 50 in 100 cases before vs 49 in 100 after, A04 about 52 in 100 in both, A05/A07/A08/A09 in all cases in both; A06 falls from about 25 to 21 in 100 cases (51 → 42 cases) and A11 rises from about 24 to 30 in 100 (48 → 60 cases). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- The relative positions of most continuing activities shift slightly earlier (e.g., A09 median 0.80 → 0.67, A05 mean 0.61 → 0.52, A02 mean 0.25 → 0.20), a pattern consistent with one event having been appended at the end of every case; ordering between existing activity pairs stays broadly unchanged, with only small shifts of a few percentage points in some pairs (e.g., A04 before A03 in about 52 in 100 cases in both windows). [POS002, POS005, POS009, REL020, REL028, REL042]
- Smaller redistributions appear in some transition shares and step distances: A04's outgoing share to A09 drops from about 79 to 70 in 100, offset by gains to A08 and A10; A08 shifts from A07 toward A10; several step-distance distributions shift (e.g., A06→A05, A06→A09, A11→A08), mostly on small observation counts. [DIR003, DIR007, LAG013, LAG023, LAG026, LAG065]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new concluding activity, A01, was appended after A03: in the later window every case ends with exactly one execution of A01, which directly follows A03, whereas in the earlier window cases ended with A03 and A01 was not observed at all. [OCC001, POS001, POS003, DIR002, REL002]

The support is direct and consistent across indicator families: A01 goes from 0 to 200 of 200 cases with a final relative position of 1.0 in every case; A03's transition profile changes from empty (terminal) to 100% A01; and all other activities precede A01 in every case where they occur, while their participation levels and mutual ordering stay essentially stable. These differences are larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data, so they are not what sampling between two windows typically produces. The evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely new step from A01 events that were previously unrecorded or relabelled, and it does not explain the modest participation shifts of A06 and A11. [OCC001, DIR002, POS001, REL006, REL020]

Other differences: A06 participation falls from about 25 to 21 in 100 cases and A11 rises from about 24 to 30 in 100 (moderate empirical strength, roughly 0.7–0.8); A04's direct-successor mix shifts away from A09 toward A08 and A10, and several step-distance distributions shift slightly, mostly on modest observation counts — these are not part of the main hypothesis and may reflect sampling differences between the windows. [OCC004, OCC006, DIR003, LAG008, LAG013]
