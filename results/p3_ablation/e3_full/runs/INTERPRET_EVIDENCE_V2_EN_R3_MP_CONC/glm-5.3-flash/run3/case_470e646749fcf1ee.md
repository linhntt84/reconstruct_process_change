### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural reworking of the segment between A03 and A04: in the later window, cases leaving A03 pass through a new fragment — A02, which in about half of its appearances cycles with A12 before A13 — and only then reach A04, whereas earlier A03 led directly to A04. In the same region, A01, which earlier sat on the branch A14→A01→A04, is no longer observed, and A14 now feeds A04 directly [DIR003, OCC002, OCC010, OCC011, DIR002, DIR009, DIR010, LAG001, OCC001, DIR001, DIR011, LAG048].

Supporting signs: the appearance and disappearance are large and one-sided (0 of 200 vs about 95 of 200 traces for A02 and A13; 40 of 200 down to 0 for A01), each at the top of the reference comparisons; the rerouting is shown directly in the successor profiles of A03, A02, A12, A13, A01 and A14 rather than inferred from positions alone; and the lag distances match the inserted and removed steps exactly (3 or 5 steps for A03→A04; 1 step for A14→A04). Occurrence, successor, relation and lag items all describe the same restructuring, and A02 additionally never co-occurs with A08/A10 in the later window, consistent with the two being alternatives after A03 [OCC002, OCC001, DIR003, DIR010, DIR011, LAG001, LAG048, REL019].

Limitations: zero participation only means an activity was not observed in that window, so a very rare residual occurrence of A01 cannot be excluded; and the evidence shows where the structure changed but not whether the new fragment took over A01's role or whether the two are independent modifications.

Other differences: A09 is inserted into A08's repetition cycle (A08→A09→A08 replacing a direct A08→A08 repeat), a separate local change not part of the main hypothesis [DIR006, DIR007, OCC007, LAG032]; the mild frequency and position shifts of A04, A05, A07, A11 and A14 are consistent with the lengthened traces but are not part of the hypothesis [OCC003, OCC004, POS004, POS005].
