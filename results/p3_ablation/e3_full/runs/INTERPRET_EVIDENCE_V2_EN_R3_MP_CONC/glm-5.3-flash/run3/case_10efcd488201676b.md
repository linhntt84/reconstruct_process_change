### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the control flow was structurally reorganised. In the later window A01 and A17 are no longer executed; A07 appears as a new early step handing over to A03 or A04; A06 and A16, previously optional and sometimes repeated, now run exactly once per trace as an adjacent closing pair in either order; A02 becomes a heavily repeated step (about 9 occurrences per trace, up from about 2) whose successors change from A16/A06/A03/A04 to A15/A11/A14/A12; and A14 and A15 become strongly repeated (about 4.5 occurrences per trace). The core signs are direct — participation moves from 128 to 0 of 200 traces for A01, from 200 to 0 for A17 and from 0 to 136 for A07, and whole successor sets are replaced — and they agree across the occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators, with empirical strengths of about 0.995 on these structural items; because activities were added and removed and repetition counts changed, this is not a mere shift of shares between alternatives that are present in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC005, OCC006, OCC012, OCC015, DIR002, DIR004, DIR006, DIR010, POS006, REL047, REL079]

An important competing explanation remains: zero participation only means an activity was not observed, so the evidence cannot distinguish a removal of A01 and A17 from them simply not being exercised in the later window; also, many relation items describe the same underlying disappearances (for example all A01 pairs), so the number of items overstates the independent evidence, and position and lag statements refer to event order rather than clock time. [OCC001, OCC015, REL001, REL016]

Other differences: participation and occurrences rose mildly for A03, A05, A08, A09, A11, A12 and A13 — for example A05 and A13 from about 3.4 to about 4.4 occurrences per trace with participation up from about 64 to 70 in 100 — and the median positions of A09, A11 and A12 moved slightly later; these fit the longer, more repetitive later traces but are not part of the structural hypothesis above.
