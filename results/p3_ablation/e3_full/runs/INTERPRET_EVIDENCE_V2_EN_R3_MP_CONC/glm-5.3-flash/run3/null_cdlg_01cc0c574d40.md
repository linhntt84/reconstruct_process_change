### Observed differences

- Activity participation and frequency are essentially unchanged: every activity appears in a substantial share of traces in both windows (A02 in about 49 in 100 traces before and after; A10 in about 62 before and 64 after), and mean executions per trace move only slightly — the largest shifts are A02 from about 8.5 to 7.9 and A11 from about 4.3 to 3.9 — with none of these differences standing out against random re-splits of the pooled traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC008, OCC009]

- Routing keeps the same shape: every observed source keeps the same successors, and several branch points split almost identically across windows (A01 sends about 55% of its outgoing transitions to A03 in both windows; A02 splits about 50/50 between A11 and A12 in both). The largest share shifts are 4–5 percentage points: in the later window, transitions from A08 go to A05 about 50% of the time versus about 45% before (A04 falls from about 55% to 50%), transitions from A10 go to A16 about 49% versus 45%, and transitions from A06 go to A01 about 55% versus 51%. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

- Typical event positions shift only slightly and in mixed directions: in the later window A05, A06, A09, A14 and A16 tend to appear somewhat earlier (A14's mean relative position falls from about 0.71 to 0.62), while A02 and A11 appear slightly later (A11 from about 0.44 to 0.47). [POS002, POS004, POS005, POS008, POS010, POS013, POS015]

- Typical distances between activity pairs shift in both directions without a common pattern: about 40 of the 98 pair-distance comparisons look individually unusual, some with pairs typically closer (A17–A10 at distance three rises from about 27% to 45% of qualifying traces; A06–A11 and A09–A11 occur within five steps in about 38 traces in the later window versus about 20 before) and some with pairs typically farther apart (A17–A02 at distance five rises from about 5% to 24%; A16–A08 at distance three falls from about 79% to 54%). [LAG016, LAG030, LAG088, LAG091, LAG093, LAG050]

- Relations between activities are stable: co-occurrence and order profiles stay essentially the same (A01 and A03 co-occur in about 72 in 100 traces in both windows, A02 and A04 in about 36 in 100 in both), and no pair switches between being alternatives and being executed together. The one consistent small drift is that A15 co-occurs with most partners about 4–5 times in 100 traces more often than before (with A02 in about 29 versus 25 in 100), while its own repetitions per trace slightly decrease (mean about 1.15 to 1.0) and its positions spread more widely. [OCC013, POS014, REL002, REL019, REL030, REL139]

### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The scattered position and pair-distance shifts run in conflicting directions and come without any counterpart in participation, co-occurrence, ordering or routing, so they cannot be distinguished from sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows. [LAG030, LAG088, POS013, OCC001, REL045, DIR005]
