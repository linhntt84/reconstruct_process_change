### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A01, was added to the process on the paths leading into A04, chiefly between A11 and A04, with additional feeders from A03 and A10. Earlier, A01 was never observed and A11 led directly to A04; later, A01 runs in about 47 in 100 traces, always within the branch containing A04, A05, A12 and A13, is fed mostly by A11 (and A03, A10), and leads mostly back to A04. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR011, REL003]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the activity appears from zero participation, its outgoing profile is dominated by A04, the former direct edges A11→A04, A03→A04 and A10→A04 are replaced by edges through A01, and many otherwise sharp lag distributions across the insertion point move by exactly one step, mostly with empirical strength at the top of the reference range. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 existed as a very rare variant earlier and only surfaced by chance in the later sample, since zero participation only means it was not observed; the breadth of the one-step lag shifts makes this unlikely, but the evidence set contains no screening baseline to check how many items an unchanged process would produce. Lag and position indicators use event positions, so the shifts show an added step in sequence, not a clock-time delay. [DIR011, DIR003, DIR010, LAG057, LAG013, LAG031, LAG050, OCC001]

Other differences: the choice between the two existing branches shifted — the A02/A07/A09 branch rose from about 45 to about 53 in 100 traces while the A04/A05/A12/A13 branch fell from about 55 to about 47; both branches are well observed in both windows, but a shift of this size (roughly 7–8 in 100) can partly reflect sampling variation. The rare-edge changes noted above also fall outside the main hypothesis. [OCC002, OCC004, OCC007, OCC009, OCC013, REL014, DIR005, DIR009]
