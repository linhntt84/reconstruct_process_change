### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The few items with high reference strength (successor shares out of A03, A07 and A09, and several pairwise distances around A09) are scattered across roughly one hundred tested activities, edges and pairs, and several of them rest on fewer than a few dozen observations, while participation, co-occurrence and ordering shares are completely flat — so sampling variation between two 200-trace windows of an unchanged process remains a full explanation.
