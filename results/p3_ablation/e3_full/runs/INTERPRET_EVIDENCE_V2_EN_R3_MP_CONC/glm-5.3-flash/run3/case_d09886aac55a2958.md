### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new activity, A01, was added to the process and placed in its early-to-middle section. A01 is unobserved in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces) and present in about half of the later traces; it is fed by A05, A06, A08, A09, A12 and A15 (about 36 in 100 of A09's outgoing transitions and 28 in 100 of A08's now go to A01) and leads mainly onward to A05, A03, A06 and A15. The placement is shown directly by the relations: pairs that were adjacent before, such as A08→A03, A09→A03 and A09→A10, no longer occur within one step and now span 2–5 steps, consistent with A01 standing between them [OCC001, POS001, DIR008, DIR007, DIR001, LAG055, LAG065, LAG070].

The support is direct and consistent: the occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators all move from "A01 empty" to "A01 integrated", each with empirical strength of about 0.995 (a difference larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits), and fifteen relation items show A01 co-occurring with each other activity after co-occurring with none. The many lag shifts largely re-describe this single insertion rather than adding independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 existed earlier at a rate too low to appear in 200 traces and merely became frequent; the behavioural difference itself — unobserved before, in about half the traces after, with new edges on both sides — holds either way [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001, REL003, REL008].
