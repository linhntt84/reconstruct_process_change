### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A01 appeared exactly once in 103 of 200 traces (about 52 in 100) and was always the final event of those traces; in the later window, A01 does not appear in any of the 200 traces. [OCC001, POS001]
- Before, every observed outgoing transition of A12 led to A01 (103 of 103); in the later window, A12 shows no outgoing transition at all and sits at the final position in every trace containing it (relative position exactly 1.0 in all 102 traces, against a mean of about 0.82 with spread before). [DIR006, POS010]
- A01's co-occurrence with each of the other fifteen activities fell to zero — with A12 and with A04 from about 52 in 100 traces to 0, with A11 from about 27 in 100 to 0; these fifteen pairings all reflect the same single disappearance rather than fifteen separate changes. [REL001, REL011, REL015]
- The surrounding branch continued unchanged in size: A12 still runs in about 51 in 100 traces in both windows, A04 in about 51 in 100, and the alternative ending A16 in about 49 in 100; the share of traces containing neither A01 nor A16 rose from 0 to about 51 in 100, so the cases that previously ended with A01 did not move to the A16 ending. [OCC012, OCC004, OCC016, REL015]
- A14 became rarer and lost two transitions: it appeared in 26 of 200 traces before and 14 of 200 after; A06, which previously led to A14 in about 54 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, now always leads to A10; A14, which previously led to A10 or A15 in roughly equal shares, now always leads to A10 and occurs about twice per participating trace. [OCC014, DIR003, DIR008]
- Within-trace repetition shifted for some infrequent activities: A05 kept its participation (about 15 in 100 traces) but its mean occurrences per trace fell from 0.66 to 0.29 (roughly 4.4 to 2.0 executions per participating trace), while A10 now repeats (mean occurrences per trace 0.13 to 0.26 at stable participation of about 12–13 in 100), and A04 and A11 repeat slightly more. [OCC005, OCC010, OCC004, OCC011]
- Routing into A05 changed: A07, which previously always led to A05, now leads to A13 in about half of its outgoing transitions; A13, which previously always led to A05, now leads to A07 (about 30 in 100) and A08 (about 21 in 100) as well as A05 (about 49 in 100). [DIR004, DIR007]
- Several activities of the branch that used to end with A01 (A04, A09, A10, A11, A12, A15) now sit at later relative positions; this is consistent with those traces ending one event earlier and is not, by itself, evidence that these activities were moved. [POS003, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010, POS013]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the terminal activity A01 was dropped from the process, so that the roughly 52 in 100 cases that previously ended with the sequence A12 → A01 now end at A12.

- The change is shown directly by four indicator families that agree: A01 fell from 103 to 0 of 200 traces and was previously always the final event [OCC001, POS001]; the only observed outgoing edge of A12 led to A01, and in the later window A12 has no outgoing edge and is itself always the final event, at unchanged participation [DIR006, POS010, OCC012]; and A01 no longer co-occurs with any of the fifteen other activities [REL001, REL011, REL015].
- The affected cases did not migrate to the alternative ending A16, whose participation is unchanged while the share of traces with neither A01 nor A16 rose from 0 to about 51 in 100. [OCC016, REL015]
- Limitations: zero participation strictly establishes only that A01 was not executed in the later window traces, and the many agreeing relation items all reflect this one disappearance, so they form one line of evidence rather than independent confirmations; a competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 still belongs to the process but was not triggered in this period, although a drop from 103 to 0 of 200 traces makes pure sampling variation implausible. [OCC001, DIR006]

Other differences:
- A14 appears in about 7 in 100 traces in the later window versus about 13 in 100 before, no longer receives from A06, and now always leads to A10 while occurring about twice per participating trace; these proportions rest on only 14 later traces. [OCC014, DIR003, DIR008]
- A05 repeats far less within its traces (mean occurrences per trace 0.66 to 0.29) at unchanged participation of about 15 in 100. [OCC005]
- A07 and A13, which previously always led to A05, now also route to each other and to A08. [DIR004, DIR007]
- A10, and to a lesser degree A04 and A11, repeat somewhat more often than before. [OCC010, OCC004, OCC011]

These secondary differences are not needed for the main hypothesis, and given the small numbers of traces behind them (for example 14 for A14, 17 for A05's later window), sampling variation cannot be ruled out for their exact sizes.
