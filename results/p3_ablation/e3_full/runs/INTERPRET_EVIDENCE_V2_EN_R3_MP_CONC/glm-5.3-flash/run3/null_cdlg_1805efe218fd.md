### Observed differences

- In the later window, A03 appeared in fewer cases: about 6–7 in 100 traces (13 of 200) versus about 12 in 100 (23 of 200) earlier, and it sat later within the traces that contained it (median position from about 0.43 to 0.60 of the trace). [OCC003, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the chain from A03 through A07 and A11 was traversed less and partly bypassed: A03 ran in fewer cases (about 13 vs 23 of 200 traces) and, when it ran, it returned directly to A01 about twice as often (about 65 vs 35 in 100 of its outgoing steps, against a fall for A07 from about 52 to 18 in 100), with the A03→A07 and A03→A11 successions largely disappearing and the A03→A01 handover moving from typically three steps to one; A07 and A11, which always occurred together, appeared in about 12 vs 17 in 100 traces. The rerouting is observed directly in A03's successor shares and in the lag shift, each larger than in about 98–99 of 100 random re-splits, and the occurrence, position, relation and lag indicators agree in direction for A03, A07 and A11. Limitations: these profiles rest on roughly 13–34 traces per window (some lag items on fewer), the agreeing items partly reflect the same traces, and the evidence cannot rule out that a changed mix of cases reaching A03 — or sampling variation given the modest counts — produces the same signature. [DIR002, LAG014, LAG018, LAG020, OCC003, OCC007, OCC010, REL054]
