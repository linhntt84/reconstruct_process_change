### Observed differences

- The activity group A04, A06, A10 ran far less often in the later window. Before, each of the three appeared in about 50 in 100 traces, and every trace contained exactly one of two options — A04 alone, or A06 together with A10 (the options never mixed and were never both absent). In the later window each appears in only about 20–22 in 100 traces, about 66 in 100 traces contain none of the three, and A04 now sometimes runs together with A06/A10 (about 8 in 100 traces). A06 and A10 still always run together. [OCC003, OCC005, OCC009, REL026, REL030, REL039]

- In the later window the group is reached from different predecessors. Before, about 25% of A02's observed outgoing transitions went to A04 and about 13% each to A06 and A10; after, no A02→A04/A06/A10 succession is observed, and A02's output concentrates on A05 (about 46%, up from 23%). A03 and A09, which previously never led into the group, now do: A03 sends about 23% of its outgoing transitions to A04, 14% to A06 and 8% to A10; A09 sends about 21% to A04 and 14% each to A06 and A10. The immediate A02→A04 succession (61% of pairs at distance 1 before) is no longer seen at distance 1, while A03→A04 and A09→A04 now occur at distance 1 in roughly 38% of their pairs. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR008, LAG013, LAG022, LAG059, LAG024]

- The group sits earlier in the trace in the later window, and A08 moved later with new predecessors. A04, A06 and A10 previously sat at the end of traces (median positions about 0.97–1.0) and now sit around 0.6–0.67. A08 was previously reached from A03 (about 47% of its outgoing transitions) and A09 (about 62%); after, neither succession is observed, and A08 instead follows the group (A04→A08 in all 47 observed A04 transitions; A06→A08 about 44%, A10→A08 about 56%), with its median position moving from about 0.63 to 0.83. - A02 itself occurs later and repeats more often in the later window: its median position rose from 0.5 to about 0.73 (with a quarter of traces at or after the final position, up from none), and the share of traces with repeated A02 rose from about 46 in 100 to about 51 in 100. A01→A02 remains the dominant step (about 94% of A01's outgoing transitions in both windows). [POS002, OCC001, REL001, DIR001]

- Smaller shifts: A05 now appears in about 36 in 100 traces (up from 29), A09 in about 27 (up from 24), and A07's mean occurrences per trace rose from 0.38 to 0.56. [OCC004, OCC008, OCC006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural rerouting around the A04–A06–A10 block. Before, this block was an end-of-process choice made in every trace — A04 alone in about half the traces, A06 with A10 in the other half — fed directly by A02; in the later window it runs in only about one in five traces, is no longer observed after A02 but after A03 and A09, sits earlier in the trace, and is followed by A08, which itself moved later and is no longer observed after A03 or A09. The change is shown directly by successor-profile changes, by the co-occurrence pattern flipping from strict mutual exclusivity to partial co-occurrence, and by immediate successions disappearing (A02→A04) while new immediate successions appear (A03/A09→A04, A03→A06); occurrence and position components move consistently with this. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR008, REL026, REL030, REL039, LAG013, LAG022, LAG024, LAG059, POS004, POS008, OCC003, DIR004]

Limitations: the evidence file provides no screening baseline, so the number of items expected without a change could not be checked formally; A04's before-window outgoing profile rests on a single observed transition, so only the new A04→A08 succession is well supported on the outgoing side [DIR004]; position and lag indicators measure event order, not clock time, so "earlier" means earlier in the sequence of events [POS004, POS008, LAG013]; and many items describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the rerouting affects only a sub-population of cases — it cannot show whether the cases that stopped running the block after A02 are the ones now running it after A03/A09.

Other differences: A02 occurs later in the trace and repeats more often, and A05, A07 and A09 occur somewhat more often; the evidence does not show whether these shifts belong to the same change or are separate from it. [POS002, OCC001, OCC004, OCC006, OCC008]
