### Observed differences

- A01 shows the starkest difference: it is absent from all 200 traces in the earlier window, while in the later window it occurs exactly once in every trace (200 in 200, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, never repeated), typically early in the case (median position about one-fifth of the events) [OCC001, POS001].
- In the later window A01 is wired into the early flow: about 32 in 100 of A02's observed outgoing transitions now lead to A01, as do about 30 in 100 of A12's and about 26 in 100 of A06's — none of these edges appear earlier. A01 in turn leads mainly to A02 (about 30 in 100), A12 (about 27), A04 (about 14) and A05 (about 11), and A03, A07, A09 and A11 show small new links into A01 [DIR001, DIR002, DIR012, DIR006, DIR003, DIR007, DIR009, DIR011].
- The formerly direct successions A02→A12 and A12→A02 became less adjacent: within one step they fall from about 82 to 53 in 100 and from about 77 to 51 in 100 cases, with two-step gaps appearing; lags from A06 to A02 and A12 stretch in the same way [LAG010, LAG073, LAG037, LAG040].
- A02, A04, A05 and A12 run in every trace in both windows. Earlier they never co-occurred with A01; in the later window each co-occurs with A01 in all traces — A04 and A05 always after A01, A02 and A12 in mixed order (roughly half the cases each way) [REL001, REL003, REL004, REL011, REL019].
- Participation of the optional activities shifted: A06 now appears in about 59 in 100 traces (earlier about 48), while A03, A08 and A10 fall from about 53 to 41 in 100, A07 and A11 from about 29 to 24, and A09 from about 28 to 20; A06 never shares a trace with these activities in either window, so the choice between the branches persists but now favours A06 [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, REL022, REL043, REL045].
- Smaller shifts: most recurring activities sit slightly later in the case (A05 mean relative position about 0.63 to 0.73, A04 about 0.63 to 0.70, A02 about 0.21 to 0.26), consistent with one extra early event, and A04 now routes to and precedes A05 more often (about 65 of its outgoing transitions, up from about 50; A04 first in about 58.5 versus 49.5 in 100 co-occurrences) [POS002, POS004, POS005, DIR004, REL029, LAG022].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was introduced into the flow and is now executed exactly once per case at an early position, embedded in the early sequence around A02 and A12: A02, A12 and A06 now route to A01 in roughly a quarter to a third of their outgoing transitions, A01 passes control mainly back to A02 and A12 and onward to A04 and A05 (which now always follow it), and the previously adjacent A02↔A12 successions usually have one event between them; in the earlier window A01 was never observed [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR006, DIR012, REL001, REL003, REL004, REL011, LAG010, LAG073].

The change is shown directly in every indicator family: occurrence jumps from 0 to 200 of 200 traces with exactly one execution [OCC001]; new incoming and outgoing edges appear [DIR001, DIR002, DIR006, DIR012]; co-occurrence with the four ever-present activities flips from never to always [REL001, REL003, REL004, REL011]; and the lag distributions stretch by about one step exactly on the pairs that used to be adjacent [LAG010, LAG073]. A difference of 0 versus 200 in 200 traces is far beyond what sampling variation between two windows of this size produces.

Limitations: the activity codes are anonymous, so the evidence cannot rule out that A01 is a re-labelled or re-coded existing activity rather than a genuinely new one; and the many agreeing items largely describe this single underlying difference, so they are not independent confirmations of it.

Other differences: the existing either/or between the A06 branch and the complementary branch (A03 executed together with A08 and A10, never with A06) shifted toward A06 — about 59 in 100 later traces versus about 48 earlier, with the complementary branch falling from about 52 to 41 in 100 and A07, A09 and A11 (which only run inside that branch) scaling down accordingly [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, REL022, REL024, REL043, REL045]; separately, A04 now precedes A05 more often than before [REL029].
