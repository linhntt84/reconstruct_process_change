### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, cases no longer pass through A02 at all, the A03–A14 sequence is executed in far fewer cases (about 18–19 in 100, down from about 48–49), and A10 — now often repeated — is executed in about twice as many cases (about 46 in 100, up from 24) and earlier in the trace, with the surrounding transitions rewired: A14 is followed mostly by A13, A08 by A03, A06/A09/A11/A15 mostly by A10, and A10 mostly by A07 and A11. Because A02 is absent from the later window and A03/A14 participation fell by roughly 30 percentage points, this is a replacement of paths rather than a reweighting of shares between existing alternatives. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC010, OCC014, DIR006, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011, DIR013, DIR014, POS010, REL038]

Supporting signs: the disappearance of A02 is measured directly (0 of 200 cases after versus 97 of 200 before, with an empty outgoing profile after about 200 observed outgoing transitions before), and the same activities show consistent changes across the occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators — corroborating, though not independent, since they draw on the same traces. The rerouting is read from successor distributions rather than inferred from positions alone. [OCC002, DIR002, DIR013, POS002]

Limitations: zero participation establishes only that A02 was not observed in the later window, so removal and extreme rarity cannot be distinguished; and the items show A10's rise occurring alongside the A02/A03 decline without establishing that one replaced the other. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the case mix between the windows (the later sample may simply contain no cases of the kind that used to run through A02) rather than a modification of the process itself; no screening expectation was supplied, so the judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the items rather than on a chance-level item count. [OCC002, DIR002]

Other differences: A13 occurs about 0.8 times per case in the later window versus about 1.0 before, at unchanged participation of about 46–48 in 100 cases [OCC013]; A06, A07 and A11 tend to occur slightly earlier in the trace [POS006, POS007, POS011]; and a few step-gap distributions shifted mildly, for example A01–A04 gaps moving from mostly one to mostly two steps apart [LAG001].
