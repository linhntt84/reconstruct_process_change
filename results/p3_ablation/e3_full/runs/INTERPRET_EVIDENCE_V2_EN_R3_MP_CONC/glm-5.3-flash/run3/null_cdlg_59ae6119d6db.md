### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence
The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. Every difference is small — mostly at most about five in 100, on units where random re-splits of the same traces regularly produce differences of that size — and the individually most unusual items involve unrelated pairs pointing in different directions, so even the one mildly coherent candidate (A04 tending to appear slightly earlier and closer after A01 in the later window) rests on shifts of three to nine in 100 that sampling variation between two 200-trace windows can readily produce.
