### Observed differences

- The conditional activities changed how often they appear at all: in the later window A03 was present in about 56 in 100 cases versus about 49 in 100 earlier (97 vs 112 of 200 traces), and A09 in about 53 versus 47 (93 vs 106), while A05 and A08, which always occur in the same cases, were present in about 69 versus 74 in 100 (147 vs 138 traces); cases containing neither of them rose from about 27 to 31 in 100. [OCC003, OCC008, OCC004, OCC007, REL040]
- Repetition shifted slightly for the always-present activities: A02 occurred about 2.06 times per case versus 1.85 and recurred in about 53 versus 47 in 100 cases; A06, A07 and A12 recurred in about 56 versus 49 in 100; A01 occurred slightly less often (about 4.06 versus 4.25 times per case). [OCC002, OCC005, OCC006, OCC009, OCC001]
- Routing between existing alternatives shifted: of A01's observed outgoing transitions, the share to A05 fell from about 54% to 51% while the share to A06 rose from about 46% to 49%; of A02's outgoing transitions, the share to A09 rose from about 46% to 51% while the share to A10 fell from about 54% to 49%. [DIR001, DIR002]
- Co-occurrence moved with participation: A03 shared cases with the ever-present activities in about 56 versus 49 in 100 (e.g., with A02 or A06), and A09 in about 53 versus 47, whereas A05 and A08 co-occurred with them in about 69 versus 74. [REL002, REL012, REL024, REL008, REL018, REL047, REL004, REL014, REL038, REL046]
- Within the always-present group, fixed order gave way somewhat to interleaving: for pairs among A02, A04, A06, A07, A10, A11 and A12 the fixed-order share fell by about 7–8 in 100 cases and the interleaved-or-repeated share rose accordingly (e.g., A06 before A07 in about 44 versus 52 in 100 cases; A06 before A02 in about 21 versus 29). - Positions and follow distances moved only slightly: A03, A06, A07 and A12 sat a little earlier in the case (mean relative position about 0.03–0.04 lower, A07 from 0.52 to 0.49) and A05/A08 slightly later, while A04, A10 and A11 were stable, with their routing splits and mutual order staying at about 50/50. ### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests that existing alternative paths were taken with somewhat different frequencies in the later window: the block formed by A05 and A08 was executed in fewer cases (about 69 versus 74 in 100), while the conditional activities A03 and A09 were executed in more cases (about 56 versus 49 and 53 versus 47 in 100). The routing changes agree with this redistribution — after A01, continuations moved from A05 toward A06, and after A02, from A10 toward A09 — and the co-occurrence shifts across many activity pairs move in the same directions. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC007, OCC008, DIR001, DIR002, REL004, REL040, REL002, REL024, REL008, REL047]

What is missing: the differences are small (about 4–8 cases per 100 on 200-trace windows), and the direct occurrence indicators for A03 and for A05/A08 fall within the range that random re-splits of unchanged behaviour produce (empirical strengths of roughly 0.34 and 0.63), while the many agreeing pair-level items largely re-express the same few trace-level shifts rather than independent confirmations. The rise in A03 is also not carried by an observed routing change (the share of A07's outgoing transitions to A03 moved only from about 49% to 50%), and no screening expectation is available to compare the number of flagged items against chance. An important competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is therefore sampling variation between the two windows — for example, a variant present in roughly half of the cases fluctuating by about 7–15 traces. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC007, DIR004]

Other differences: A02, A06, A07 and A12 repeated somewhat more often within cases (recurrence in about 53–56 versus 47–49 in 100 cases; A02 about 2.06 versus 1.85 occurrences per case), and in step with this the interleaved-or-repeated share rose for pairs among A02, A04, A06, A07, A10, A11 and A12; because the ordering shift matches the repetition increase, a genuine change in ordering constraints is not established. A03, A06, A07 and A12 also sat slightly earlier within the case, which a position shift alone cannot tie to a relocation of these activities.
