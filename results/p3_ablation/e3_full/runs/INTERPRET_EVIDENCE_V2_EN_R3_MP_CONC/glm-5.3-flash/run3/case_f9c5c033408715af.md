### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A07 is no longer executed: in the earlier window it ran in about 23 in 100 cases, placed about two-thirds into the trace and followed mainly by A03 and A13, whereas in the later window it is absent from all 200 traces and none of its outgoing transitions are observed. [OCC006, DIR006, POS007]

This rests on direct, mutually consistent evidence across all four indicator families — occurrence (46 of 200 traces to 0), position (median about 0.67 to unobserved), direction (all four previously observed successors disappear) and relations (co-occurrence with each of twelve partner activities drops to zero while the "neither" share rises by the same amount) — and each of these differences is larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits; with unchanged participation, A07 would have been expected in roughly 46 of the 200 later traces rather than none, so sampling variation alone cannot plausibly explain the pattern. [OCC006, POS007, DIR006, REL006, REL017, REL027, REL036, REL044, REL051, REL058, REL059, REL060, REL061, REL062, REL063]

Limitations: zero participation strictly means only that A07 was not observed in the later sample, so a drastic reduction to a very rarely executed step cannot be distinguished from complete removal — the competing explanation that the later window merely missed a still-executed activity is made implausible by the sample size but not fully excluded — and the evidence cannot show whether the shifts below are consequences of A07's disappearance or independent changes. [OCC006]
