### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a step not observed in the earlier window, A01, now executes in the later window immediately before A04 in the branch containing A04, A13, A11, A12 and A05: the hand-offs that previously ran directly from A03, A10 or A11 to A04 pass through A01, A01 leads back to A04 in about three quarters of its transitions, and the rework loop through A04, A13 and A11 back to A04 is one event position longer than before. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR003, DIR010, DIR011, LAG016, POS004]

The support is direct rather than cumulative: the new successor relations are observed in the directly-follows profiles, the one-step gap increases occur precisely on paths crossing A01 while gaps on paths that do not cross it are unchanged, and A01's occurrence profile matches A04's, as expected if A01 runs once before each A04 execution. [DIR003, DIR011, LAG016, LAG021, LAG063, OCC001, OCC004]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly establishes only that A01 was not observed there, this evidence provides no screening baseline against which to set the number of qualifying items, and the minor new edges into A01 (from A04, A06 and A13) rest on small transition shares. The competing explanation that A01 is a previously unsampled variant cannot be excluded by the participation figures alone, although its presence in 94 of 200 later traces together with the coordinated rewiring makes an unchanged process unlikely. [OCC001, DIR004, DIR013, DIR006]

Other differences: the relative frequency of the two mutually exclusive trace families moved by about 7 points (traces with A02, A07 and A09 up from about 46 to about 53 in 100; traces with A04, A05, A12 and A13 down from about 55 to about 47 in 100), a shift small enough that sampling variation between two 200-trace windows could produce it, and A04's and A13's mean occurrences per trace eased from about 1.1 to about 0.96 in step with that shift.
