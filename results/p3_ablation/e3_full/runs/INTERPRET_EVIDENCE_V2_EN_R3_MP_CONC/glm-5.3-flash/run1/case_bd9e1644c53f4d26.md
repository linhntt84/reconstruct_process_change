### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A02 was added to the process and is now executed exactly once per case, placed between the early activities (A01, A03, A06) and the late ones (A04, A05, A07). In the earlier window A02 was never observed; in the later window it runs in all 200 traces, once each, at a mean event position of about 0.37, with direct transitions to and from every other activity, usually before A04 (69 in 100), A05 (89 in 100) and A07 (81 in 100) and in mixed order with A01, A03 and A06 [OCC001, DIR002, POS002, REL008, REL009, REL011, REL001, REL007, REL010].

The change is shown directly and consistently across indicators that all describe the same behaviour: participation jumps from 0 to 100 in 100 traces, A02 gains a full outgoing profile where it had none, co-occurrence with each other activity goes from 0 to 100 in 100, and the modest later drift of the other activities' positions together with the newly appearing large event gaps fit one extra event inserted in the middle of the trace rather than independent relocations; no other activity lost or gained participation, so nothing appears to have been removed or made optional [OCC001, DIR002, REL001, POS004, LAG005, LAG023, POS001, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007].

Limitations: zero participation only means A02 was not observed in the earlier sample, so an extremely rare pre-existing occurrence cannot be ruled out; and because A02's order relative to A01, A03 and A06 is mixed, it does not occupy one single fixed slot [OCC001, REL001, REL007, REL010].

Other differences: minor order-share shifts of a few percentage points among pre-existing pairs (e.g., A01 before A04 from 73 to 68 in 100, A03 before A04 from 66 to 73 in 100) and small position drifts; these are within what finite samples produce and are not part of the main hypothesis [REL003, REL012, POS001, POS003].
