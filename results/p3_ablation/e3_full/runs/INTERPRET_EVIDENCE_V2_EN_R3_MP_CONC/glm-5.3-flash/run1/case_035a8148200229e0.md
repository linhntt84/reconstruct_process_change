### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 took over the role that A04 played in roughly half of the traces. In the earlier window, about 49 in 100 traces contained A04 — always as the first event, always leading directly to A03, and co-occurring only with A03, A06 and A08, all of whom it preceded — while A01 was not observed. In the later window, A04 is no longer observed, and A01 appears in about 50 in 100 traces with the same behaviour in every respect: first position, A03 as the only successor, and co-occurrence limited to A03, A06 and A08, always preceding them. The two activities never share a trace, and the affected group of traces is essentially the same size in both windows (about 98 vs 99 of 200). Occurrence, successor, position and relation indicators all show this substitution directly and consistently, and the key differences are larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of unchanged behaviour, so sampling variation cannot plausibly explain them. What the evidence cannot rule out is that this is a relabelling of A04 as A01 rather than a removal of A04 combined with an insertion of A01 at the same point; the windows show before-and-after behaviour, not the mechanism. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003, POS001, POS004, REL002, REL005, REL007, REL028, REL038, REL040, REL003]

Other differences: A second change affects the ordering around A05, A07 and A13, where a previously fixed A07-before-A13 sequence now appears in both directions, with A13 gaining A07 as a successor, A07 gaining direct transitions to A03 and A05, and their step distances to A03, A06 and A08 shifting about one step in opposite directions [REL069, DIR004, DIR005, DIR010, POS006, POS012, LAG013, LAG018, LAG021, LAG059, LAG061, LAG062]. In addition, A02, A09, A10, A12, A14 and A15 each appear in modestly fewer traces (about 2–6 in 100 fewer); these moderate differences could also arise from ordinary sampling variation between the two windows, which the evidence cannot rule out [OCC002, OCC005, OCC006, OCC008, OCC010, OCC011].
