### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A01 was not observed at all (0 of 200 cases); in the later window it runs exactly once in every case (200 of 200, never repeated), placed early in the case — median about one fifth into the trace [OCC001, POS001].
- The new activity is wired in around the start: A02 now leads directly to A01 in about 54 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, while its share to A04 fell from about 87 in 100 to about 44 in 100; A04 now leads to A01 in about 39 in 100 of its transitions. A01 itself leads mostly to A04 (about 46 in 100), otherwise to A07 (about 17), A03 (about 16) or A10 (about 10); A01 and A04 co-occur in every later case, with A01 first in about 55 in 100 cases and A04 first in about 45 in 100 [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, REL001, REL003].
- A09 disappeared: it ran in about 45 in 100 earlier cases (90 of 200), always as the closing step directly after A06; in the later window it is not observed (0 of 200). A06, which previously always led to A09, is now observed at the very end of the case (mean position about 0.99, up from 0.80) and has almost no observed successors — only 3 outgoing transitions across roughly 95 cases [OCC007, POS005, POS008, DIR006, REL042, REL008].
- The remaining activities sit about one event position later than before: the A02→A06 distance moved from 4 steps in about 99 in 100 cases to 5 steps in about 97 in 100; A02→A08 from 4 (about 93 in 100) to 5 (about 90 in 100); A02→A04 is now 2 steps in about 47 in 100 cases (before essentially always 1); A04→A11 moved from 4 steps (about 83 in 100) to 4–5. Mean positions rose for A03 (0.51→0.68), A10 (0.46→0.68), A05 (0.53→0.60), A07 (0.45→0.52) and A04 (0.24→0.31) [LAG002, LAG004, LAG006, LAG014, LAG018, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS009].
- The branch structure itself is intact: A03/A10/A06 still never co-occur with A05/A07/A08/A11 in either window, and each activity's participation moved by only about 2.5 percentage points (e.g., A06 45%→47.5%), which is within ordinary sampling variation [REL020, REL034, REL040, REL041, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the control flow: A01 was added as a new early step that now runs exactly once per case — directly after the start activity A02 in about half the cases, and after A04 in most of the rest — while A09, previously the closing step that followed A06 in about 45 in 100 cases, is no longer executed, leaving that path to end at A06. Before, cases ran A02 → A04 and, on the A06 branch, ended A06 → A09; in the later window every case contains A01 near the start and no case contains A09. The across-the-board one-step shift of the remaining activities fits A01 having been inserted ahead of them [OCC001, OCC007, DIR002, DIR004, DIR006, REL001, REL003, REL042, LAG002, LAG004].

Supporting signs: the two occurrence changes are direct and extreme — A01 from 0 to 200 of 200 cases and A09 from 90 to 0 — and far exceed what random re-splits of a stable process produce (empirical strength about 0.995). The routing changes at A02 and A04, A01's new relations (it now co-occurs with A02 and A04 in every case, and with A03/A05/A06/A07/A08/A10/A11 whenever they run, almost always before them), A09's vanished co-occurrences, and the consistent position and lag shifts all describe the same insertion-and-removal, while the alternative-path structure is unchanged [OCC001, OCC007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL008, POS001, POS005, POS008, LAG002, LAG004, LAG006].

Limitations: the data do not fix a single insertion slot for A01 — besides following A02 or A04, it occasionally follows A05 or A07 — so "inserted early, at a varying point" is as specific as the evidence gets. Because A06 shows almost no observed successors after the change (3 transitions in ~95 cases), the evidence establishes that A09 is gone but cannot show what, if anything, now follows A06; its new end-of-case role is inferred from its position and near-empty outgoing profile, and zero participation strictly means only that A09 was not observed later. The evidence set also contains no screening baseline, so the usual count-versus-chance comparison could not be performed [DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, POS005, OCC007].

Other differences: participation of the remaining activities shifted by only about 2.5 percentage points (e.g., A05 55%→52.5%, A03 45%→47.5%), consistent with sampling noise; A03 and A10 now occasionally lead to A01 (about 2 in 100 and 1 in 100 of their outgoing transitions), and A06's three observed successors in the later window are A01 and A04 [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC008, OCC009, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR010].
