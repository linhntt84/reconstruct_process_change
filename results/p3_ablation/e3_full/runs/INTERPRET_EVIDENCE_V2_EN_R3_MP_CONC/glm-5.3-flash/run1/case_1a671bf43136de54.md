### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 became an executed, optional step in the later window: it was absent from all 200 earlier traces and appears in about 15 in 100 later traces, placed in the later-middle portion of the trace, reached from A08 (which now transitions directly to A01 in about 8 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) and, in some traces, preceded by A02 or A04, and followed by A09 or A10. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR005, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL006]

The appearance is shown directly by participation (0 vs 31 traces) and corroborated across indicator families — new outgoing edges of A01, a new incoming edge from A08, a new position distribution, and co-occurrence with eleven partners that was previously always "never together" — with empirical strengths of about 0.97 or higher throughout. These relational items all reflect the same underlying appearance of A01 rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR005, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window only establishes that A01 was not observed there. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that A01 existed at a very low rate before and went unsampled in 200 traces; a stable rate near the later level (about 15 in 100) is incompatible with 0 of 200, but the size of any earlier rate below the detection threshold cannot be quantified. The accompanying decreases in A07 and A12–A14 and the reduction in A08 repetitions could be secondary effects of the same restructuring or sampling noise; the evidence cannot separate these.
