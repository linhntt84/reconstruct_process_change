### Observed differences

- Hand-over from A03 changed sharply: in the earlier window about half of A03's outgoing transitions went to A07 (roughly 12 of 23) and about 35 in 100 to A01; in the later window about 65 in 100 go to A01 and only about 18 in 100 to A07, with immediate A03→A07 successions falling from about 92% to about 19% of observed pairs. [DIR002, LAG018]
- The immediate order between A01 and A03 flipped: earlier, A01 was followed immediately by A03 in about 41 of 100 observed A01→A03 pairs; in the later window this is about 3 in 100, while A03 followed immediately by A01 became the usual pattern (about 37 in 100, versus about 25 earlier, where A03 reached A01 mostly at distance 3). [LAG002, LAG014, DIR001]
- The A07–A11 branch is entered less often: A07 and A11 (which essentially only occur together) each appear in about 17 of 100 traces earlier versus about 12 in 100 later (34 vs 24 traces); A07 still leads mostly to A11, but immediate A07→A11 successions fell from about 74% to 48% of pairs. [OCC007, OCC010, REL054, LAG040]
- A03 itself became rarer and later: present in about 11 of 100 traces earlier versus about 6–7 in 100 later, with its median position moving from about 0.43 to 0.60 of the trace. [OCC003, POS002]
- Co-occurrence with the rerouted activities fell: A01 together with A07 or A11 in 17% of traces earlier versus 12% later; A03 with A07 or A11 in about 11.5% versus 6.5%; A01 with A03 in about 11.5% versus 6.5%. [REL005, REL009, REL016, REL020, REL001]
- The late rework loop repeated more in the later window: A09 transitions rose from 13 to 46 (mean occurrences per trace 0.065→0.23), A12 from 5 to 25, A13 from 8 to 21; A08 routed to A09 about twice as often (about 21%→46% of its outgoing transitions) while A08→A10 fell (about 58%→38%), and A12/A13 now lead only to A09/A08. [OCC009, OCC011, OCC012, DIR004, DIR005, DIR008, DIR009]
- A06 participation rose from about 12% to 17% of traces, and A01 routes to A06 more often (about 18%→25% of A01's outgoing transitions). [OCC006, DIR001, REL034]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the traversal through the A03/A07/A11 branch was re-sequenced relative to A01: in the earlier window A03 mainly handed on to A07, which led to A11, whereas in the later window A03 is reached more often from A11 and leads back to A01, the immediate A01→A03 step has nearly disappeared, and the A07–A11 branch is entered in fewer traces. The change is shown directly by A03's successor shares (A03→A07 about 52%→18%, A03→A01 about 35%→65%, a difference larger than all but about 2 in 100 random re-splits) and by the lag profiles in both directions between A01 and A03; it is consistent across direction, lag, position and co-occurrence indicators, and every involved activity and edge is observed in both windows, so this is a re-weighting of existing paths rather than an added or removed path. Limitations: A03 is rare (23 vs 13 traces, only 17 outgoing transitions in the later window), so the successor shares rest on few observations; many corroborating items come from the same traces and are not independent; and no screening expectation was provided, so the item count could not be compared against a chance baseline (numerous low-support lag items with one or two observations were therefore not relied on). An important competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that part of the A07/A11 decline is ordinary sampling variation on a branch that already appeared in only about a sixth of traces. [DIR002, LAG002, LAG014, LAG018, LAG020, REL016, REL020, POS002, OCC003, OCC007, OCC010, DIR007]

Other differences: the rework loop A08→A09→A12/A13 repeated more often in the later window (A09 events 13→46, A12 5→25, A13 8→21; A08→A09 up, A08→A10 down; A12 and A13 now lead only to A09 and A08), and A06 is executed more often (about 12%→17% of traces; A01→A06 up from about 18% to 25% of A01's outgoing transitions) — these rest on few traces (about 5–9 containing A09, A12 or A13) and are not part of the main hypothesis.
