### Observed differences

- A01 and A13 moved in opposite directions: in the earlier window A01 appeared in about 66 in 100 traces (about 2.2 occurrences per trace) and A13 in about 23 in 100 (about 0.4 per trace); in the later window A01 appears in about 28 in 100 traces (about 0.6 per trace) and A13 in about 61 in 100 (about 2.1 per trace). [OCC001, OCC013]
- The two activities exchanged their outgoing routing. Earlier, about 48% of A01's observed outgoing transitions went to A08, about 26% to A15 and about 26% to A07, while all of A13's observed outgoing transitions went to A06; in the later window essentially all of A01's outgoing transitions go to A06, while A13's go to A08 (about 45%), A15 (about 28%) and A07 (about 28%). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 and A13 exchanged their places in the process. In the earlier window, A01 was the frequent, early step — present in about two-thirds of traces, usually more than once, preceded by A02, A04, A07 and A15 and followed by A08, A15 or A07 — while A13 was a rare, late step sitting between A06 occurrences (A06 led to A13 in about 47% of its outgoing transitions, and A13 led back to A06). In the later window these roles are reversed: A13 is the frequent, early step with the same predecessors and successors, and A01 is the rare, late step in the A06 loop. The support is direct and consistent across indicator families: occurrence levels swap almost exactly; A01's earlier outgoing profile matches A13's later one and vice versa; every affected predecessor rewires from one activity to the other at similar shares; median positions and predecessor–successor distances swap; and co-occurrence with A08 mirrors the exchange, with A01 and A13 rarely co-occurring in either window, as expected if they occupy the same slot. [OCC001, OCC013, DIR001, DIR010, DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR011, POS001, POS013, LAG004, LAG016, LAG051, LAG058, REL007, REL089, REL012]

Limitations: each window is a 200-trace sample and the exchange is approximate rather than exact — A01's later participation (about 28 in 100) and mean occurrences (0.62) are somewhat above A13's earlier values (about 23 in 100; 0.44). No screening baseline is included, so the number of qualifying items cannot be benchmarked against a chance expectation. The evidence also cannot rule out that the two windows sample different mixes of process variants, or that two independent adjustments to A01 and A13 coincidentally mirror each other, rather than a single exchange. [OCC001, OCC013]

Other differences: A05, A09 and A12 (mildly also A02 and A04) repeat less often within traces while their trace participation stays similar; A06 occurs slightly more often; and A07, A15 and A08 sit slightly earlier in traces while A09, A11 and A12 sit slightly later. These shifts are small, and the evidence cannot show whether they are knock-on effects of the exchange or independent differences.
