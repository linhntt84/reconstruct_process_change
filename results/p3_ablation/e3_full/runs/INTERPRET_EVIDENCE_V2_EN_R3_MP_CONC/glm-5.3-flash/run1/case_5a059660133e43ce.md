### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, cases can close with a new final step: A02 is followed by A01, an activity not observed in the earlier window, which now runs in about 22 in 100 cases, always as the last event and always directly after A02. The supporting signs are direct measurements of this behaviour in four indicator families — occurrence (0 of 200 vs 44 of 200 traces), position (final position in every occurrence), direction (new A02→A01 edge at about 21% of A02's outgoing transitions) and relation (co-occurrence of the pair rising from 0 to about 22 in 100 traces, with A02 first) — and a jump from 0 to 44 of 200 traces is not what sampling a stable process produces. Limitations: no screening baseline was provided, so the expected number of chance findings could not be checked against the item count; and "not observed before" cannot distinguish a genuinely new activity from one previously unused on this path, although a stable 22% participation would virtually never yield zero occurrences in 200 traces. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that this closing step and the reroutings below stem from the same underlying modification rather than separate ones.
