### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 has taken over the position and role previously held by A02. Earlier, A02 ran in about 46 in 100 cases, early in each case, typically preceded A04, A11, A12, A14 and A15, co-occurred with A03 whenever it ran, and was a strict alternative to A07; in the later window A02 is no longer observed, while A01 runs in about half of the cases at a comparable early position, precedes the same activities (A04, A12, A14, A15 in about 47 in 100; A11 in about 37 in 100), keeps nearly the same successor set, and is now the strict alternative to A07. The change is directly observed in participation (0 of 200 → 101 of 200, and 92 of 200 → 0 of 200) and is consistent across occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators, with the core items exceeding the differences of over 99 in 100 random re-splits [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS002, REL001, REL003, REL006, REL010, REL011, REL013, REL014, REL019].

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether A01 is the same step recorded under a new label or a different activity that assumed the role, and the successor sets are similar but not identical (A02 also led to A08, which is not observed among A01's outgoing transitions) [DIR001, DIR002]. Zero participation only means the activity was not observed in that window, and the many relation items pairing A01 with A04, A11, A12, A14 and A15 largely re-describe the same single placement rather than acting as independent confirmations [REL003, REL010, REL011, REL013, REL014]. An independent removal of A02 plus addition of A01 would look identical on these indicators; the evidence establishes the net behavioural change, not the mechanism behind it [OCC001, OCC002].

Other differences: A03 now repeats more often (mean about 1.9 → 2.2 per case; repeated in about 45 → 53 in 100 cases) and A10 occurs more often and in more cases (about 45 → 53 in 100 participation; mean 0.87 → 1.17), with A10 and A11 more often executed together or interleaved; A04 is more often followed directly by A03 (about 3 → 10 in 100 of its outgoing transitions); A11 sits slightly later in the case (mean relative position 0.48 → 0.53); A05 participation rose (about 21 → 26 in 100) while A09 and A07 fell slightly (about 49 → 43 and 54 → 50 in 100); various step-gap distributions show small shifts (for example A03-to-A11 gaps spreading toward larger distances), though some of these rest on fewer than about 20 cases [OCC003, OCC010, REL091, DIR004, POS011, OCC005, OCC009, OCC007, LAG009, LAG053].
