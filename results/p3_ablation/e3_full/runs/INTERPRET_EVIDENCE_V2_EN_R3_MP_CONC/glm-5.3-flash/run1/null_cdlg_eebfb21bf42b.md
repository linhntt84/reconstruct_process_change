### Observed differences

- The process skeleton is the same in both windows: A01, A02, A03, A04 and A09 run in every trace before and after, while A05, A06, A07, A08 and A10 each appear in about half of the traces; A05 and A06 never share a trace (nor A05 with A10, nor A07 with A08), and A10 always accompanies A06 [REL001, REL006, REL016, REL023, REL026, REL027, REL031, REL032].
- The only consistent movement is in how these optional activities are chosen. In the earlier window the splits were about 52 in 100 traces for A05 vs A06 and about 50/50 for A07 vs A08; in the later window they are about 50/50 and 54/46. Shifts of 2.5–4.5 traces in 100 are matched or exceeded in roughly 40–70 of 100 random re-splits of an unchanged process, so these movements are unremarkable [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL023, REL032].
- The most unusual items concern spacing (events between two activities). In the later window, A05 immediately follows A04 in about 51 of 100 traces containing both (about 39 earlier) and immediately follows A02 in only about 21 of 100 (about 39 earlier), while A08 immediately follows A02 in about 57 of 100 (about 41 earlier); A01 also reaches A08 two events later more often (14→22 in 100) and four events later less often (38→25). For these items, differences this large arise in only about 3–14 of 100 random re-splits [LAG018, LAG010, LAG013, LAG006].
- Other spacing shifts are based on few traces and point in different directions: A07 is immediately followed by A06 in about 82 of 100 such traces (61 earlier; ~31–34 traces), A08 reaches A10 at three events in about 37 of 100 (22 earlier; ~35–41 traces), while A06 precedes A08 immediately in about 14 of 100 (38 earlier; ~14–16 traces) and A10 precedes A08 immediately in about 67 of 100 (90 earlier; ~10–12 traces). Proportions on so few traces carry little weight [LAG036, LAG043, LAG031, LAG046, LAG028].
- Event positions and most direct-succession profiles are essentially flat (mean position shifts of at most ~0.02–0.03; outgoing profiles of A01, A04, A05, A06, A07, A08 and A10 with strengths 0.06–0.67). The one exception is A02's outgoing profile (strength 0.93): transitions from A02 to A05 fall from about 17 to 8 of its outgoing transitions, while those to A07 and A08 rise to about 25–26 in 100 [POS001, POS003, POS004, POS007, POS008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008].

### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The only behaviour that moves consistently — the choice between the optional activities A05 vs A06 (with A10) and A07 vs A08 — shifts by only 2.5–4.5 traces in 100, a size random re-splits of an unchanged process regularly produce, while the few highly unusual spacing shifts around A02, A04, A05 and A08 disagree with neighbouring relations (e.g., A07→A06 becomes more immediate while A06→A08 and A08→A06 become less so) and several rest on fewer than about 45 observations, so no specific structural, ordering or participation hypothesis can be separated from sampling variation [OCC003, OCC004, LAG018, LAG013, LAG010, LAG031, LAG036, LAG046].
