### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence
The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. Participation, co-occurrence and ordering are nearly identical across the two windows, and the few visible movements — the successor split out of A06 (about 58/42 to 50/50) and the slightly longer self-repetition gaps of A01 and A04 — are of a size that random re-splits of unchanged behaviour also produce, so sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows remains a full explanation.
