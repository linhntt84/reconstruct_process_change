### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A01 and A07 exchanged their places in the main sequence. Earlier, control flowed A05 → A01 → A07, with A07 then branching back to A05 or on to A06 and receiving handovers from A03 and A04; in the later window A05 hands to A07, A07 hands to A01, and A01 takes over the branching to A05/A06 and the handovers from A03/A04. The exchange extends to their occurrence profiles: A01 now shows the participation, per-trace frequency and repetition that A07 showed before, while A07 dropped to roughly A01's former levels. [DIR001, DIR004, DIR006, LAG006, LAG017, LAG022, LAG025, OCC001, OCC007]

The reversal is shown directly by successor profiles and by immediate-succession counts moving in opposite directions for the two orders of the pair; positional and relational items agree (A07 moved later in traces, and A01-without-A07 replaced A07-without-A01); nearly all qualifying items reach the maximum empirical strength — differences larger than in all 200 reference re-splits — while describing one coherent rewiring rather than scattered shifts. [DIR001, DIR006, LAG006, LAG025, POS006, REL005, REL009]

Limitations: no screening baseline was supplied, so the expected number of chance findings could not be checked directly; the evidence cannot distinguish a literal exchange of the two activities' positions from an equivalent re-routing that produces the same adjacency changes; and POS/LAG are based on event positions and step distances, so no statement about clock-time timing is possible. [POS006, LAG006]

Other differences: A06 now behaves like an internal activity rather than a terminal one — it moved from the end of traces (median relative position 1.0) to the middle (median 0.44), is reached in fewer steps from A01 and A05, and more often precedes A03/A04; however, its earlier outgoing profile rests on only 11 observed transitions, so the before-picture of A06's routing is uncertain. A05 also occurs slightly later in traces (mean position 0.47 to 0.57). [POS005, DIR005, LAG005, LAG021, REL008, REL011, POS004]
