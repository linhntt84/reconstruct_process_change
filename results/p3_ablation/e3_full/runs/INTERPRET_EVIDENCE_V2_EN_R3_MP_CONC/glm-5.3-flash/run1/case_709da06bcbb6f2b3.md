### Observed differences

- Activity A01 is absent from all 200 traces in the earlier window; in the later window it appears exactly once in about 119 of 200 traces (about 60 in 100), and early in the case (mean relative position about 0.18). [OCC001, POS001]
- In the later window, A01's outgoing transitions go to A03 (about 32%), A10 (about 27%), A04 (about 23%) and A07 (about 18%); in the earlier window A01 did not occur, so it had no observed outgoing transitions. [DIR001]
- A03 and A10 now route a large share of their outgoing transitions through A01: about 34% from A03 and about 40% from A10, while their direct shares shrank (A03→A07 about 29%→12%, A03→A04 about 26%→15%, A10→A03 about 55%→29%, A10→A04 about 25%→15%). [DIR002, DIR007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that activity A01 was added to the process and placed in the early flow between A03/A10 and the downstream activities: in the later window A01 runs once in about 60 in 100 traces where it previously never appeared, A03 and A10 hand over to A01 in about a third and about 40 in 100 of their outgoing transitions respectively, and A01 leads on to A03, A10, A04 and A07; as a result, distances from A03 and A10 to later activities stretch by about one step and A04–A10 occur at later positions. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR007, POS001, LAG001, LAG007, LAG032]

The signs are direct and mutually consistent: a 0-to-119-of-200 participation change, a complete new outgoing profile for A01, new A01 components in the routing of A03 and A10, and coordinated lag and position shifts, each larger than in about 99 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR007, LAG007]

Limitations: the evidence provides no screening baseline, so the number of items expected without a change cannot be checked; the exact placement of A01 relative to the A03–A10 back-and-forth is not pinned down, because both orders between A01 and A03/A10 occur in the later window; and zero participation in the earlier window strictly means only that A01 was not observed there, although 200 traces without a single occurrence make a rare-variant explanation implausible. [REL001, REL008]

Other differences: A13 occurs slightly less often and A09's outgoing share shifts from A13 towards A14; these shifts are small and could reflect sampling variation between the windows. [OCC013, DIR006]
