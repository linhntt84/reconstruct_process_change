### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A07 was removed from the flow between A08 and the A05/A06 branch. Earlier, every trace executed A08 → A07 → A05 or A06, with A07 running about twice per trace; in the later window A07 is never observed and A08 passes directly to A05 (about 52 in 100) or A06 (about 48 in 100). The signature is direct and consistent: the disappearance is seen on participation (200 of 200 traces to 0 of 200), the successor change at A08 is observed directly, A07's relations with the other activities all collapse in the same direction, and every measured distance across the removed step shortens by exactly one position — a coherent structural pattern rather than isolated indicators, and far beyond what a rare variant appearing in one window would produce.
