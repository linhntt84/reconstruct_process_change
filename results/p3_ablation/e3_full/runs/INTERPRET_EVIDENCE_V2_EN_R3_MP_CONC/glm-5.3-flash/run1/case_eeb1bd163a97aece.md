### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new step, A01, now sits between A05 and A06: in the earlier window every A05 was followed directly by A06, while in the later window A05 always leads to A01 and A01 always leads to A06, with A01 executed exactly once per case at a fixed position. The support is direct and consistent — A01 goes from 0 of 200 traces to all 200; A05's outgoing edge is fully redirected from A06 to A01; the relations place A01 after A02, A04 and A05 and before A06 and A03; and the one-step increase in every measured distance spanning the insertion point, together with the small position shifts of A04, A05 and A06, is exactly the pattern an insertion produces, with no contradicting item [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, REL004, REL005, POS001, LAG006].
