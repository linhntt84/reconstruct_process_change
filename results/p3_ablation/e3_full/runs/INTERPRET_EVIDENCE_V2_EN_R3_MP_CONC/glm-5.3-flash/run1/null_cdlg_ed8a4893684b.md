### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The differences amount to only a few cases in 100 traces, they move together because the activities involved share traces (so the agreeing occurrence and co-occurrence items are not independent confirmations), and the direct occurrence differences are all within the range that random re-splits of a stable process produce, so sampling variation alone remains a full explanation.
