### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The participation, repetition and routing gaps all point in the same direction, but they are different views of the same traces, and their empirical strengths reach only about 0.6–0.88 — random re-splits of the pooled traces produce equally large differences in roughly one comparison in eight or more — so sampling fluctuation between the two 200-trace windows cannot be ruled out. [OCC001, OCC004, DIR003]
