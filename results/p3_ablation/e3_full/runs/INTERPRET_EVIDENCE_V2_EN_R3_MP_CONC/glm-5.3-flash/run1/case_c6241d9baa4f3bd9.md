### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A07 was removed from the process (or its execution stopped entirely): in the earlier window A07 ran in about half of the cases, always within the A03 branch, where it followed A03 and typically led to A06 and preceded A10; in the later window A07 is not observed at all, and the flow that previously ran A03 → A07 → A06 now runs directly from A03 to A06, whose share of A03's outgoing transitions rose from about 5 in 100 to about 51 in 100. [OCC005, DIR002, DIR004, REL018, REL033, REL039]

The disappearance is shown directly and consistently across occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators: participation fell from 106 of 200 traces to 0 of 200, all nine activity pairs involving A07 dropped from roughly 53 in 100 co-occurrence to zero, and each of these items shows a difference larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. The rerouting at A03 is observed directly in the direction profiles, and the small occurrence and gap shifts in the surrounding branch are consistent with the same underlying change rather than independent differences. [OCC005, POS005, DIR002, DIR004, REL005, REL012, REL018, REL024, REL029, REL033, REL037, REL038, REL039]

Limitation: the evidence shows only that A07 was not observed in this sample of 200 later traces; it cannot distinguish a permanent removal from a condition that merely stopped triggering A07, and it cannot rule out that A07 still occurs rarely — unlikely given its earlier frequency of about 53 in 100 traces, but not strictly excluded. [OCC005]

Other differences: the A03-branch activities A03, A05, A06 and A09 now repeat less often (mean occurrences per trace down about 0.15–0.2 with essentially unchanged participation), which may be a side effect of taking A07 out of a repeating cycle, but the evidence does not establish that loop structure.
