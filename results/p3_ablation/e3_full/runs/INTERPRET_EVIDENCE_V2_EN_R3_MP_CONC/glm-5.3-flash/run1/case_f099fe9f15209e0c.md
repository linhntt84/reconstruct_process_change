### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A02 has dropped out of the process: cases in the earlier window always executed A02 once, near the start and before A01 (90 in 100) and A07 (always), whereas in the later window A02 is not observed in any of 200 traces, its outgoing edges are unobserved, and its former neighbours are reconnected directly — A03 now routes to A01, A04 and A05 instead of to A02, and A01 leads more often to A04 and A07. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR001, REL001, REL006, REL007, REL010]

The support is direct and consistent: the disappearance is observed on full support in both windows (200 of 200 traces before versus 0 of 200 after), all six relations involving A02 flipped from regular co-occurrence to the other activity appearing alone, and the directional, positional and adjacency shifts (earlier positions for A03, A04, A05, A06; more distance-one pairs) all point the same way, which makes a rare-variant sampling fluke implausible. Limitations: zero participation strictly shows only that A02 was not observed, so the evidence cannot distinguish full removal from A02 being skipped in every case by a routing condition, and no screening baseline was provided to compare item counts against a chance expectation; the position and adjacency shifts are event-rank evidence, not clock-time evidence. [OCC001, REL008, REL009, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

Other differences: A04 and A06 repeat less often in the later window (A04 mean occurrences about 2.2 to 1.9; A06 traces with repeats 30 to 22 in 100) — plausibly a knock-on of A02's disappearance, but the evidence does not link it directly; A07's exits shifted from A01 toward A05, but with only about 35 outgoing transitions per window this could be sampling variation. [OCC002, OCC004, DIR007]
