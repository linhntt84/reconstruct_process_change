### Observed differences

- The relation between A03, A05 and A12 reversed. In the earlier window, every trace contained either A03 together with A12 (about 49 in 100 traces; one never appeared without the other) or A05 alone (about 51 in 100; never together with A03 or A12). In the later window, A03 and A12 never appear in the same trace: about 30 in 100 traces contain A05 together with A03 (A03 never occurs without A05), about 25.5 in 100 contain A05 alone, and about 44.5 in 100 contain A12 without either [REL038, REL031, REL061].
- Predecessor routing changed. Earlier, about 91% of A02's outgoing transitions and about two thirds of A04's led to A03; in the later window, all observed outgoing transitions of A02 (28) and of A04 (35) lead to A12, and immediately so — at distance 1 in every observed pair, where previously the typical distance was two to four steps [DIR002, DIR004, LAG007, LAG010].
- Successor routing changed. A03's outgoing transitions moved from roughly 45% to A11 and 45% to A12 to roughly 55% to A11 and 45% to A10; A16's moved from about 52% to A10 and 48% to A09 to about 50% to A09 and 50% to A03; and A12's outgoing transitions, previously mostly to A02 (about 80% of only five observed transitions), now split about evenly between A02 and A04 [DIR003, DIR010, DIR007].
- Co-occurrences moved with this re-routing: A02–A03, A04–A03 and A11–A12 co-occurrence each fell to zero (from about 26, 24 and 24 in 100 traces), A03 now always co-occurs with A06 and with A10 (about 30 in 100 traces each, previously never), and A11 now co-occurs with A05, A06, A10 and A16 (about 12–17 in 100 traces, previously never), while A11's tie to A03 itself is unchanged — A11 still never appears without A03 and still leads back to A03 in essentially all of its outgoing transitions [REL016, REL030, REL106, REL032, REL036, REL060, REL070, REL100, REL110, REL037, DIR006].
- Participation shifted: A03 fell from about 49 to 30 in 100 traces (mean occurrences per trace 0.96 to 0.67), A12 from about 49 to 44.5, A02 from about 25.5 to 20 and A11 from about 23.5 to 17; A05 rose from about 51 to 55.5, and A06, A16 and A10 from about 26.5 to 30 [OCC003, OCC012, OCC002, OCC011, OCC005, OCC006, OCC016, OCC010].
- Event positions (not clock time) moved consistently: A03 now occurs later (mean normalized position about 0.44 to 0.67), as do A11 (about 0.48 to 0.64) and A10 (about 0.83 to 0.90), while A12 occurs earlier and far more variably (mean about 0.97 to 0.71, with a quarter of its occurrences now at the very start of the trace where it previously always sat near the end) [POS003, POS010, POS009, POS011].
- Secondary distance shifts inside the A05-side branch: A06 to A05 is now always four steps apart (previously typically three), A16 to A05 moved from typically two steps to three-to-five, and A16 to A10 — previously immediate in about 85 in 100 pairs — now lies two or more steps away; A15, A16 and A06 also occur somewhat earlier than before. These items rest on roughly 26 to 53 observed pairs per window [LAG011, LAG039, LAG042, POS014, POS015, POS005].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that routing around A03, A05 and A12 was restructured. In the earlier window, A03 and A12 were always executed together (about 49 in 100 traces, never one without the other) as an alternative to the A05 branch, with A02 and A04 feeding into A03 and A11 looping with A03; in the later window, A03 and A12 never appear together — A03 always co-occurs with A05 (about 30 in 100 traces), is reached from A16 as well as A11, and its observed outgoing transitions go only to A11 or A10 — while A02 and A04 lead directly and immediately to A12, which now forms a separate branch. The support is direct and consistent: the co-occurrence reversal appears on large supports (A03–A12 from about 98 co-occurring traces to none; A03–A05 from none to about 60), the successor changes are complete rather than gradual, the participation and position shifts (A03 rarer and later, A12 earlier and far more variable, A05/A06/A16/A10 slightly more frequent) all point the same way, and each key difference exceeds virtually all random re-splits of the pooled data [REL038, REL031, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR010, OCC003, OCC012, OCC005, POS003, POS011].

Limitations: with 200 traces per window, rare paths may go unobserved, and the many agreeing pair-level items reflect one underlying re-routing rather than independent confirmations; the least certain detail is A12's exact outgoing behaviour in the earlier window, which rests on only five observed transitions [DIR007]. A competing explanation that cannot be fully excluded is a shift in the mix of pre-existing case types between the windows — though the new trace combinations (A03 with A05, A12 without A03) never appear in the earlier window, so this would require them to have been vanishingly rare before.

Other differences: within the A05-side branch, pairwise step distances shifted (A06 to A05 now always at distance four; A16 to A05 from typically two to three-to-five steps; A16 to A10 from typically immediate to two or more steps), A15, A16 and A06 occur somewhat earlier, A02 and A04 sometimes occur in later trace positions, and A06's split between A15 and A16 moved slightly toward A16 (about 59/41 to 53/47 of its outgoing transitions) [LAG011, LAG039, LAG042, POS014, POS015, POS005, POS002, POS004, DIR005].
