### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new final activity, A01, was appended at the end of the process after A06: in the earlier window traces ended with A06, whereas in the later window every trace ends with A06 followed by A01. This is shown directly — A01 goes from no occurrences to exactly one occurrence in all 200 later traces, always at the last position; A06 gains A01 as its sole observed successor; and every other activity (A02–A08) co-occurs with A01 and precedes it, while all of them keep essentially the same participation, so nothing was removed or displaced. The uniform backward shift of A04–A08's positions fits every trace gaining one final event rather than any reordering among the existing activities. These key differences are larger than about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of unchanged behaviour, and an activity absent from 200 earlier traces but present in all 200 later ones is not plausibly a sampling artefact. The evidence cannot show whether A01 is genuinely new or an existing activity under a new anonymous code, and it cannot indicate what prompted the addition.
