### Observed differences

- Activity A10 disappears from the later window: in the earlier window it ran in about 44 in 100 traces (88 of 200) and led to A12 in about 82% of its observed outgoing transitions, while in the later window no trace contains it (0 of 200). [OCC010, DIR009, POS010]
- Before, A10 was tightly bound to A04 and A12: A04 and A10 always appeared together (about 44 in 100 traces, never one without the other), A10 always preceded A12 when both ran, and about 55% of A04's observed outgoing transitions went to A10. In the later window A04 and A12 still always appear together — now in about 58 in 100 traces, up from 44 — but without A10, and A04 leads directly to A12 in about 73% of its outgoing transitions (about 10% before); A01's edge into A10 (about 18% of its outgoing transitions) also vanished. [REL036, REL064, REL038, DIR004, DIR001, OCC004, OCC012]

- Smaller changes: A01 participation fell from about 39 to about 33 in 100 traces, A03 rose from about 9 to about 13 with its observed successors switching from A10/A02 to A04/A12 (only 5 versus 10 observed transitions, so weak), and a few other activity pairs show minor distance redistributions. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR003, LAG013]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A10 is no longer executed in the later window, and that the flow which previously ran A04 → A10 → A12 now passes directly from A04 to A12. In the earlier window A10 ran in 88 of 200 traces, always alongside A04 and almost always followed by A12; in the later window it appears in none of 200 traces, A04's transition into A10 vanished while A04 → A12 rose from about 10% to about 73% of A04's outgoing transitions, and the A04 → A12 distance collapsed from typically two to three steps to a single step. A drop from 88 to 0 traces is not plausibly a sampling fluctuation, and the successor and distance evidence shows the reconnection directly rather than inferring it from frequencies alone. [OCC010, DIR004, DIR009, LAG017, REL036, REL064]

Limitations: the evidence shows that A10 was not observed, not why — removal, a merge into A04 or A12, or a recording difference cannot be distinguished from each other; the many agreeing relationship and distance items largely re-express this one underlying change rather than confirming it independently; and a competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A10 still executes but is no longer recorded. [OCC010, REL019, REL028]

Other differences: the share of traces taking the A02 branch versus the A04/A12 branch moved from about 56/44 to about 43/58, while the two remain strict alternatives in both windows; A01 participation declined slightly and A03 rose slightly, with A03's successors now leading to A04/A12 instead of A10/A02 (thin support). These sit alongside the A10 change and may be related to it, but the evidence does not establish whether they are one change or several. [REL013, REL021, OCC002, OCC004, OCC012, OCC001, OCC003, DIR003]
