### Observed differences

- Every trace contains exactly one of two activity pairs — A01 together with A09, or A02 together with A10 — and the two pairs never occur in the same trace. In the earlier window the A01/A09 pair appeared in about 45 of 100 traces and the A02/A10 pair in about 55; in the later window this is about 48 and 52. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC009, OCC010, REL001, REL008, REL021]
- Within the A02/A10 pair the preferred internal order reversed: in the earlier window A10 preceded A02 in about 31 of 100 traces versus about 25 the other way round; in the later window A02 preceded A10 in about 29 versus about 24. [REL021]
- The direct successors swapped accordingly: earlier, about 26 of 100 outgoing transitions of A02 led to A03 and 45 to A10, while from A10 about 19 led to A03 and 55 to A02; later, about 18 of A02's outgoing transitions led to A03 and 55 to A10, while from A10 about 29 led to A03 and 45 to A02 — each activity's outgoing profile now resembles the other's earlier one. [DIR001, DIR006]
- Event distances to downstream activities swapped in mirror image: A03 followed A02 within one event step in about 58 of 100 cases earlier versus 39 later, while from A10 it moved from about 42 to 61; the same opposite one-step shift appears toward A05, A07, A08, A11, A13 and A14 (for example A02→A13 at four steps fell from about 60 to 35 in 100 while A10→A13 rose from 40 to 65). [LAG001, LAG029, LAG003, LAG031, LAG005, LAG033, LAG006, LAG034, LAG007, LAG035, LAG008, LAG036, LAG009, LAG037]
- A02's typical event position moved slightly earlier (median 0.09 to 0) and A10's slightly later (median 0 to 0.1). [POS002, POS009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A02 and A10 — two activities that always execute together in the same branch — reversed their relative order: in the earlier window the sequence typically ran A10 and then A02, with A03 directly following A02, while in the later window it typically runs A02 and then A10, with A03 directly following A10. This is shown directly by the reversed order proportions, corroborated by the near-exact exchange of the two activities' successor profiles, by mirrored one-step shifts in the distances from A02 and from A10 to the same seven downstream activities, and by their opposite position shifts; a movement of a single downstream activity or the shift in branch shares could not produce this mirrored pattern. [REL021, DIR001, DIR006, LAG001, LAG029, LAG005, LAG033, LAG008, LAG036, POS002, POS009]

Limitations: all cited items express one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, the shifts amount to roughly 5–7 traces in 100, several distance profiles rest on only about 20–35 observations, and the evidence provides no baseline for how many items qualify without a change — so an unlucky split of otherwise stable traces remains a competing explanation, although it fits the mirrored consistency across ordering, successor and position views poorly. [REL021, LAG008, LAG009, LAG036]

Other differences: the share of traces taking the A01/A09 alternative rose from about 45 to 48 in 100 while the A02/A10 alternative fell from about 55 to 52; both branches remain well observed, but this difference is within the range random re-splits typically produce (about half of reference comparisons show a larger one). Participation also edged down for A04 (about 26 to 22 in 100 traces), A06 (31 to 28) and A13 (13 to 10), A08 repeated somewhat less often (recurrence in about 13 to 10 of 100 traces), and a few rare outgoing transitions of A03 and A07 toward A08, A13 and A14 observed in the earlier window were not observed later.
