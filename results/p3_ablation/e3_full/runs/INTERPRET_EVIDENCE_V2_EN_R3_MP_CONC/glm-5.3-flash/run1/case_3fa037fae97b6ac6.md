### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A01, was added to the process and now executes exactly once per case, placed early in the case and integrated between the early activities and the later ones: A02, A12 and A06 now transition into it, it leads to A02, A12, A04 and A05, it always precedes A04 and A05, and its order relative to A02 and A12 varies. The participation change is direct (0 of 200 cases before versus 200 of 200 after, with the difference exceeding about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data), the new edges appear on both the incoming and outgoing side of A01, and the later positions of A04 and A05 together with the larger gaps from A02/A12 to A04/A05 match the direction and rough size expected if one event were inserted early in each case. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR006, DIR012, REL001, REL003, REL004, REL011, LAG010, LAG073, LAG075, LAG076, LAG003, POS004, POS005]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly establishes only that A01 was not observed there, and a relabelling of an existing activity cannot be ruled out from these indicators alone — although no other activity disappeared and every case gained exactly one occurrence, which fits an addition better than a replacement. Several of the A01 relations restate the same underlying presence of A01 and are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]

Other differences: the mutually exclusive choice between A06 and the branch activities A03/A08/A10 (with A07/A11 and A09 attached to that branch) shifted toward A06, from about 48/53 to about 59/41 in 100 cases; all involved activities remain observed in substantial shares in both windows, so this is a change in how often existing alternative paths are taken, not a replacement of one path by another.
