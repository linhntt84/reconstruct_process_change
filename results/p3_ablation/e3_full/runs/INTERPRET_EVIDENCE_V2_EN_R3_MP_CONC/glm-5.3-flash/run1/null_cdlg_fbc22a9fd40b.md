### Observed differences

- The local route through A04 and A07 reversed. In the earlier window, about 55 in 100 outgoing transitions of A07 went to A04 and about 55 in 100 transitions of A04 went to A02, giving a dominant sequence A06 → A07 → A04 → A02; in the later window, A04 sent about 53 in 100 transitions to A07 (about 47 to A02), A07 sent about 53 in 100 to A02 (about 47 to A04), and from A06 the share of transitions to A04 rose from about 23 to about 29 in 100 while those to A07 fell from about 28 to about 26 in 100. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the order of A04 and A07 was reversed on the sequence between A06 and A02: in the earlier window the dominant route ran A06 → A07 → A04 → A02, and in the later window it more often runs A06 → A04 → A07 → A02. The reversal is shown directly by the pairwise order flip (A04 before A07 in about 53 in 100 later traces versus about 46 in 100 earlier, never interleaved in either window), by the mirrored successor shifts at A04 and A07 of about 7–8 in 100 transitions each, and by the consistent lag movements at A06, A04, A07, A02 and A13; every activity involved still appears in essentially all traces in both windows, so this is a reversal of the dominant order, not a path being replaced or an activity appearing or disappearing. The main limitations are the modest size of the shift (about 7–8 percentage points, roughly 15 traces in 200) and that the agreeing indicators partly describe the same traces, so an unlucky random split of an unchanged process cannot be fully excluded; most individual comparisons reach only moderate empirical strength (about 0.8, with one lag comparison near 0.99), and no screening baseline is provided to compare the number of findings against chance. [REL034, DIR003, DIR006, DIR005, LAG020, LAG029, LAG030, LAG036, LAG097, LAG100]

Other differences:

- All measured activities repeat fewer times per trace in the later window with participation essentially unchanged, consistent with the repeated segment being executed fewer times per case rather than any activity becoming optional; this is a separate difference from the ordering reversal. [OCC001, OCC005, OCC008]
- A03 occurs in fewer traces (about 52→47 in 100) and its outgoing transitions concentrate on A01 (about 73→88 in 100), a routing change at A03 that the ordering hypothesis does not cover. [DIR002, OCC002, LAG013]
- Further pairwise step-distance distributions shifted in mixed directions with no single consistent pattern. [LAG061, LAG052]
