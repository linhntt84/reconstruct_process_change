### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A01 has taken over the position in the flow that A03 occupied in the earlier window, with A03 no longer executed. A09 now routes about 55 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A01 where it previously routed about 52 in 100 to A03; A01's successors (mainly A11, then A12, A09 and A10) reproduce A03's earlier successors (mainly A11, then A10, A09 and A12); A01 runs at A03's frequency (about 50 vs 45 in 100 traces, about two executions per containing trace), at A03's position in the trace (mean relative position about 0.41 vs 0.42) and in A03's co-occurrence and ordering pattern with the other activities, while the two never share a trace in either window. This consistent picture across occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators points to A01 taking over A03's role in the flow rather than an added parallel branch or a mere shift in how often existing paths are taken. [DIR006, DIR001, DIR003, OCC001, OCC003, POS001, POS003, REL002, REL008, REL010, REL033, REL035]

The core items are mutually consistent, each of their differences exceeds essentially all 200 random re-splits (empirical strength about 0.995), and they rest on 90–203 observations, so chance sampling or a rare one-window variant is an unlikely account. What remains open: zero participation strictly means only that A03 was not observed in the later window and A01 not in the earlier one, so occurrences below the resolution of a 200-trace sample cannot be excluded, and the evidence cannot establish that the two activities are functionally equivalent. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 is a newly inserted activity and A03 was removed independently, with their similar profiles arising because both sit at the same spot in the flow; the near-identical successor, position and co-occurrence profiles make a simple exchange the more economical reading, but this remains a possibility rather than a confirmed modification. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, POS001, POS003, REL002]

Other differences: A02's occurrences now fall at the very start of traces (median relative position about 0.26 before vs 0 after), while A07's and A13's occur later (medians from 0 to about 0.4); and many activity pairs show small redistributions of their gaps, for example A15 to A04 almost always 2 steps apart in the later window (9 of 10 observations vs about 6 of 10 earlier) and A10 to A02 immediately consecutive in only about 4 of 10 later observations (vs about 6 of 7 earlier) — individually small and mostly based on fewer than 30 observations, so they may be secondary effects of the exchange or sampling variation.
