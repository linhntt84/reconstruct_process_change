- Activity A03 was absent from all 200 earlier traces; in the later window it appears in about 103 of 200 traces (roughly half), typically at an early-to-middle position (median about 38% of the trace's event positions). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A03 was added to the process and is now performed in about half of all cases, placed inside the existing block of activities that runs before A02→A01. Before, cases in this group executed A05, A06, A07, A08, A10–A12 and then A02→A01 without A03; in the later window the same group also executes A03, always together with and before A01/A02, directly followed by A11, A12 or A08 in roughly equal shares, and reached from A05, A08 and A12 through transitions not observed before. [REL003, REL002, REL013, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

Supporting signs: the move from 0 of 200 traces to about 103 of 200 far exceeds what random re-splits of two 200-trace windows produce; the consistent one-step-later positions of many downstream pairs fit an inserted step rather than a re-weighting of existing paths; and the participation of all surrounding activities is unchanged, so this is not one path replacing another. [OCC003, LAG002, LAG010, LAG016, LAG021, OCC001, OCC004]

Limitations: the exact edge into which A03 was placed cannot be fixed from these aggregates — A03 both receives from and leads to A08 and A12, suggesting a position inside a repeated block, but the precise wiring is not recoverable. The evidence also cannot distinguish a genuinely new activity from one that was previously executed but not recorded, since 0 of 200 traces only bounds its earlier frequency below roughly 1–2 in 100 cases. [DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, OCC003]

Other differences: A05 and A07 are now repeated less often (A05 mean occurrences about 1.2 → 0.9 with unchanged participation; A07 about 0.7 → 0.4 with participation down from about 31% to 24%), and within the A04 group the follow-up choice shifted from about 63/37 in favour of A13 to about 53/47 in favour of A09, with A09 occurring later and A04 earlier within traces; these differences are not part of the insertion hypothesis and may be independent changes. [OCC005, OCC007, LAG001, LAG008, DIR002, POS003, POS008]
