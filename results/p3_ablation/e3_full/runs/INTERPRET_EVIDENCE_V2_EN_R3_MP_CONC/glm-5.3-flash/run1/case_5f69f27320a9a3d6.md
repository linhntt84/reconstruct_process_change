### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A04 is no longer executed between A03 and A07 — A03 now leads directly to A07 — and a new activity A01 runs exactly once per case, inserted between A02 and A03 at a varying point within the A05/A06 segment. This is shown directly rather than inferred: participation flips from 0 to 200 of 200 traces for A01 and from 200 of 200 to 0 for A04; A03's outgoing transitions switch entirely from A04 to A07 with the inter-step distance dropping from two to one; and the relational items place A01 consistently after A02 and before A03/A07 while mixed against A05/A06, with the routing and lag changes matching an insertion into that middle segment [OCC001, OCC002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG004, REL001, REL002, REL006, REL004, REL005, DIR001, DIR002].

Limitations: the windows are finite samples, so "absent" strictly means "not observed", although a 0-versus-200-of-200 flip together with the complete rewiring around A03 makes a sampling artefact unlikely. Because the activity codes are anonymous, the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely new A01 plus removal of A04 from A04 being relabelled and relocated — both fit the observed behaviour. No screening expectation was provided, so the item count could not be compared against a chance baseline; the judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the control-flow evidence [OCC001, OCC002, DIR003, LAG004].

Other differences: none beyond the above; the A05/A06 relationship (always co-occurring, no fixed order between them) remained stable across the two windows [REL012].
