### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the process runs without A02 and the flow that previously passed through A02 and its close partners A03, A14 and A08 is rewired around A10: A02 is unobserved, A03, A14 and A08 run in far fewer traces, and A10 runs in about twice as many traces at earlier positions, now followed mainly by A07 and A11 instead of A02 and A13, while A08 leads mainly to A03, A14 mainly to A13, and A13 mainly to A09, A08 and A10. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC008, OCC010, OCC014, DIR002, DIR008, DIR010, DIR012, DIR013, POS010]

This rests on direct, mutually consistent observations rather than on the number of items: A02's disappearance appears in four indicator families alike (about 97 of 200 traces before, zero after), the participation shifts are backed by roughly 37–92 traces per window, and the rewiring is read directly from the outgoing transitions of A08, A10, A13 and A14; the core differences are larger than essentially all random re-splits of the pooled traces (empirical strength about 0.995), so sampling variation alone is an unlikely explanation for the pattern as a whole. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC010, OCC014, DIR002, DIR008, DIR010, DIR012, DIR013, POS002, POS003, POS010, POS014, REL015]

Limitations: zero participation shows only that A02 was not observed in the later 200 traces, and the position statements concern event positions rather than clock time, so no timing change is implied. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows draw on different case mixes — for example, later cases that never enter the A02-containing branch — which would produce the same participation and routing pattern without any modification to the process. [OCC002, POS003]
