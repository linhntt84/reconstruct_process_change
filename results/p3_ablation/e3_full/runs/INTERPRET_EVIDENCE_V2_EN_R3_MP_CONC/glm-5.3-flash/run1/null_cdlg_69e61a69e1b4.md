### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The largest shifts amount to a few traces per 100, the biggest lag changes occur only on activity pairs seen in a handful of traces, and in every indicator family even the most unusual item is no more extreme than random re-splits of these 200-trace windows typically produce when many activities and pairs are tested, so sampling variation alone can explain the whole pattern [OCC008, OCC011, DIR018, POS012, LAG047, LAG172].
