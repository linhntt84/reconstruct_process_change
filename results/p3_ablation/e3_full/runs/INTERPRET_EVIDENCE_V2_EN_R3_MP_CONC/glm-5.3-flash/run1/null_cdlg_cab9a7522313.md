### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. All observed differences are small — participation and routing shifts of at most about five percentage points — and no item's empirical strength exceeds about 0.80, so ordinary sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows remains a plausible explanation for the entire pattern, including the slightly lower participation of A04 [OCC003, DIR001, POS003, LAG002, LAG003].
