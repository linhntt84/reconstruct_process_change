### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new step, A01, was inserted into the flow once per case between the mid-process activities and the closing activity A08: in the later window A01 runs after A02 and A03 and after whichever of A04, A05 or A09 executed, and hands over mainly to A08, with A04 and A09 redirecting their dominant outgoing transitions from A08 to A01 and all five upstream-to-A08 lag distributions shifting about one step later. This is shown directly by occupancy (0 of 200 vs 200 of 200 cases), by A01's new edge profile, and by consistent relational evidence covering every pre-existing activity; a rare variant or sampling artefact cannot plausibly produce a 0%-to-100% participation swing with these relational signatures. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR004, DIR008, REL007, LAG006, LAG016, LAG031]

Limitations: the insertion point is not one fixed edge — A01 sometimes precedes A06 or A07 instead of A08 directly, and A06/A07 still reach A08 without A01 in about a quarter to a third of their transitions; also, event logs cannot distinguish a genuinely new activity from one that existed before but was never exercised in the earlier window. [DIR001, REL005, REL006, DIR006, DIR007]

Other differences: the choice between the alternatives A06 and A07 shifted by about 8–9 in 100 cases toward A07, and A03's routing moved from A06 toward A05; these shifts are moderate (empirical strength about 0.86–0.96) and could partly reflect sampling variation between the two windows. [OCC004, OCC005, REL028, DIR003, REL016]
