### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the terminal activity A03 is no longer executed: earlier, every trace ran the fixed chain A01 → A04 → A05 → A06 → A02 → A03 and ended with A03, whereas in the later window A03 is absent from all 200 traces and A02 has become the last step. The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the disappearance is observed directly (participation 1.0 → 0.0, with 200 versus 0 supporting traces), five relation pairs and the outgoing-transition profile of A02 all independently show A03 absent, and everything else — order shares, step distances and routing among A01, A02, A04, A05 and A06 — is stable, with the position shifts of A02, A04, A05 and A06 exactly matching traces shortened by one final event rather than indicating additional changes. Limitations: the evidence shows only that A03 was not observed in the later window; it cannot distinguish removal of the activity from a condition that now always skips it, and both windows are finite samples. The competing explanation of a sampling artefact is implausible — A03 appeared in 100% of the 200 earlier traces — but cannot be fully excluded.
