### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, about 52 in 100 traces (104 of 200) contained a fixed five-step segment A02 → A05 → A06 → A04 → A03: every observed outgoing transition of A05 went to A06, every observed outgoing transition of A06 went to A04, and each pair involving A06 always co-occurred in this fixed order. [DIR001, DIR002, REL004, REL007, REL009, REL010]
- In the later window, A06 is not observed in any of the 200 traces (participation fell from about 52 in 100 to 0), while A02–A05 still occur together in about 50 in 100 traces; every observed outgoing transition of A05 now goes directly to A04, and the pairs that included A06 now show only the partner activity. [OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, REL004, REL007, REL009, REL010]
- The segment became one event shorter: the gap from A05 to A04 shrank from 2 to 1 steps, from A02 to A04 from 3 to 2, from A05 to A03 from 3 to 2, and from A02 to A03 from 4 to 3; accordingly the mean relative position of A05 moved from 0.25 to about 0.33 and that of A04 from 0.75 to about 0.67, as expected when one event disappears from the middle of the sequence. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, POS002]
- Everything else is essentially stable: A01 and A02–A05 keep their participation (A01 about 48 in 100 traces before and 50 after; A02–A05 about 52 before and 50 after), each occurs at most once per trace, and the order among A02–A05 (A02, then A05, then A04, then A03) is unchanged; the remaining differences are about 1.5 percentage points and individually unremarkable (empirical strength about 0.12). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL005, REL006, REL008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the step A06 is no longer executed on the path that previously ran A02 → A05 → A06 → A04 → A03, so that A05 leads directly to A04 and the segment shortens to A02 → A05 → A04 → A03: A06 fell from about 52 in 100 traces to 0 of 200, the successor of A05 changed completely from A06 to A04, and all four measured position gaps in the segment shrank by exactly one step [OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]. The change is observed directly in the routing out of A05 and is consistent across occurrence, routing, position, relation and gap indicators, with differences larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, while the order and participation of A02, A03, A04 and A05 stayed the same [REL004, REL007, REL009, REL010, POS001, POS002, POS003]. Limitations: zero participation strictly shows only that A06 was not observed in the later sample, and the evidence cannot distinguish a permanent removal of A06 from the segment from a change under which every execution of the segment now skips it; no screening expectation is provided to compare the item count against, and only the roughly half of traces containing these activities are covered [OCC006].

Other differences: participation of A01 rose slightly (about 48 to 50 in 100 traces) and that of A02–A05 fell slightly (about 52 to 50 in 100), shifts of about 1.5 percentage points with low empirical strength that are consistent with sampling variation alone and are not part of the hypothesis above [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL005, REL006, REL008].
