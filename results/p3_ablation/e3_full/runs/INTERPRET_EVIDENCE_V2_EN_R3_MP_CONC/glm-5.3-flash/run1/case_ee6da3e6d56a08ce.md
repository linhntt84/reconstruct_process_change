### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 was added as a new step between A15 and its former successors: in the earlier window A15 led directly to A07, A08 or A12 (about 52, 25 and 23 in 100 of its outgoing transitions), whereas in the later window A15 leads to A02 in all 474 of its observed outgoing transitions, and A02 leads on to A07, A08 or A12 in almost the same proportions (about 52, 27 and 21 in 100). A02 runs about 2.4 times per trace, recurring together with A15, and distances from A15 and from the upstream activities A05, A11 and A14 to downstream activities shifted exactly one step further, as expected when a single step is inserted between them. [DIR011, DIR002, OCC001, OCC013, LAG105, LAG106, LAG110]

The hypothesis is direct and consistent: the successor change is observed on both sides of the insertion (A15's outflow and A02's outflow), A02's appearance rests on 116 traces with differences larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits, and the relational, lag and position items agree because they reflect this single behaviour rather than independent changes. A rare-variant explanation is implausible: A02 is absent from all 200 earlier traces yet present in a majority of later traces, where it takes over A15's successor distribution exactly. [OCC001, DIR002, DIR011, POS002, REL015]

The evidence shows the behavioural difference, not its origin: it cannot distinguish a modification of the process from the later window sampling a different variant or population in which A02 always executes after A15, and an activity unobserved in 200 earlier traces cannot be proven previously absent. [OCC001, DIR011]

Other differences: The participation shifts on the A05–A15 branch (each of A05, A11 and A15 gaining about 17 traces; A10 and A13 about 15; A09 losing about 7) have low empirical strength (at most about 0.44), i.e., within the range random re-splits of an unchanged process produce; A04's slight outgoing shift rests on modest counts; and the mild position shifts (A03 earlier, A12 later) are consistent with traces lengthening where A02 is inserted, not independent evidence that activities moved. [OCC003, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC011, OCC013, DIR004, LAG024, LAG025, POS003, POS012]
