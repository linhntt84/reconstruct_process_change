### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A09 typically appeared around the first third of the trace (median position about 0.29, mean about 0.31); in the later window it appears clearly later (median about 0.43, mean about 0.37). Roughly 100 traces contain A09 in each window, and only about 4 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled data show a larger position shift. [POS008]
- The flow into A09 increased. Of A10's outgoing transitions, the share going directly to A04 fell from about 73% to about 53%, while the share going to A09 rose from about 12% to about 21% (about 52 A10-traces per window). Of A05's outgoing transitions, the share going to A09 rose from about 4% to about 19% (about 46–47 A05-traces per window). [DIR009, DIR004]
- The flow out of A09 shifted in the same direction: transitions from A09 directly to A04 rose from about 11% to about 20%, while transitions from A09 to A10 fell from about 29% to about 21% (about 100 A09-traces per window). [DIR008]
- Event-pair distances around A09 moved coherently: A06→A09 pairs within one step fell from about 38% to about 23% and A08→A09 direct pairs from about 85% to about 52%, while A09→A02 pairs five steps apart fell from about 44% to about 28%, A09→A08 pairs four steps apart fell from about 32% to about 19%, and A09→A04 pairs within one step rose from about 11% to about 20%. [LAG037, LAG056, LAG058, LAG064, LAG060, LAG069]
- Participation was essentially stable: A09 occurs in about 49–50 of 100 traces in both windows, A10 in about 26, and A05 in about 23; no activity appeared or disappeared. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- Smaller shifts elsewhere: A06 is more often directly followed by A07 (about 52% to 59% within one step); A08 precedes A04 more often (about 37% to 42% of traces) and occurs slightly earlier, while A04 occurs slightly later (median about 0.57 to 0.67); A11 occurs earlier (median about 0.50 to 0.43) and A01 precedes A11 in about 21% versus 14% of traces. [LAG035, DIR005, REL027, POS007, POS003, POS010, REL010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A09 runs later within traces and the routing around it shifted: A10 now more often continues into A09 and less often steps directly to A04, A09 itself leads directly to A04 more often and to A10 less often, and A05 more often leads into A09 — a pattern consistent with more cases travelling A10→A09→A04 where they previously went A09→A10→A04 or directly A10→A04. The activities involved are A09, A10, A04 and A05; A09's position moved later (median about 0.29 to about 0.43) and A10's somewhat later as well (median about 0.43 to about 0.57). [POS008, POS009, DIR009, DIR008, DIR004]

Supporting signs: the positional shift of A09 is observed directly on about 100 traces per window; the successor shares at the A10, A05 and A09 sources all point the same way; and the gap distributions change coherently — pairs into A09 (from A06 and A08) spread farther apart while pairs out of A09 (to A02, A08, A04) draw closer, which is what one expects when an activity sits later among a trace's events. Because participation of every involved activity is stable, this reads as existing paths being taken with different frequencies rather than activities becoming added, removed, optional or mandatory. [POS008, DIR009, DIR004, DIR008, LAG037, LAG056, LAG064, LAG058, LAG060, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

Limitations: the A10 and A05 routing shares rest on only about 50 outgoing transitions per window (the A05→A09 change amounts to roughly 2 versus 9 transitions), the A09→A04 increase is only moderately unusual against random re-splits, and the evidence includes no screening baseline against which the number of qualifying items could be compared. An important competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is a changed mix of trace variants (for example different branch frequencies), which could shift many positions and adjacencies at once and produce this pattern without A09 being specifically repositioned. [DIR009, DIR004, DIR008, LAG060]

Other differences: A06→A07 became more often directly adjacent (about 52% to 59% within one step); A08 precedes A04 more often (about 37% to 42%) with A08 slightly earlier and A04 slightly later; A11 occurs earlier (median about 0.50 to 0.43) and A01 precedes A11 less often (about 21% to 14%); A05 also occurs somewhat earlier (median about 0.57 to 0.43); several further activity pairs show small gap redistributions. [LAG035, DIR005, REL027, POS007, POS003, POS010, REL010, POS004]
