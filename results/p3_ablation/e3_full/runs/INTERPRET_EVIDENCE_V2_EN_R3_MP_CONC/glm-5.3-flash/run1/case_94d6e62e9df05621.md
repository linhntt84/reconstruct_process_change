### Observed differences

- Context that is stable across both windows: traces split into two disjoint groups of activities — one around A01, A05, A07, A08 and one around A02, A03, A04, A06, A09, A10 — with zero co-occurrence between the groups in both windows, and each activity occurring at most once per case. [REL001, REL010, REL014]

- A07 and A08 each ran in about 53 in 100 cases before; in the later window A07 runs in about 23 in 100 and A08 in about 29 in 100, while every other activity's participation moved by only about 2 in 100 (e.g., A01 53→51, A02 47→49). [OCC007, OCC008, OCC001, OCC002]

- Before, A07 and A08 were inseparable: they co-occurred in about 53 in 100 cases and never appeared one without the other, usually one directly after the other in either order (A07 before A08 in about 28 in 100 cases, A08 before A07 in about 25 in 100). In the later window they never co-occur: about 23 in 100 cases contain only A07, about 29 in 100 only A08, and about 49 in 100 contain neither (roughly unchanged from 47 in 100 before). [REL040]

- The handovers between them disappeared: before, A07 led to A08 in about 65 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions and A08 led back to A07 in about 61 in 100; in the later window neither edge is observed — A07 now leads to A01 in about 72 in 100 and to A05 in about 28 in 100, and A08 leads to A01 in about 71 in 100 and to A05 in about 29 in 100. A01 in turn feeds A08 more often (about 35→47 in 100) and A07 less often (about 45→33 in 100). [DIR007, DIR008, DIR001]

- What remains of the segment is tighter and later: A01→A07 pairs one step apart rose from about 51 to 80 in 100 observed pairs, A01→A08 from about 40 to 81 in 100, and three-step gaps from A05 to A07/A08 (about 28 and 38 in 100 before) disappeared; A07 and A08 now sit at the end of their cases (median relative position about 0.67→1.0) and A01 sits later too (mean about 0.40→0.55), consistent with the segment after A01 shrinking from two activities to one. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG020, LAG021, POS007, POS008, POS001]

- In the other branch only modest routing shifts appear: A02 led to A04 in about 36 in 100 of its outgoing transitions before versus about 49 in 100 after (A02→A06 about 43→31 in 100), and A09 shifted from A04 (about 43→25 in 100, on roughly 30–35 transitions per window) towards A06 (about 19→34 in 100). [DIR002, DIR009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the consecutive pair A07–A08 became an exclusive choice between the two. Earlier, cases entering this segment executed both A07 and A08, one directly after the other in either order, between A05/A01; in the later window no case executes both — about 23 in 100 cases run only A07 and about 29 in 100 only A08, each immediately after A01 and continuing to A01 or A05, so the segment is entered about as often (about half of all cases) but performs roughly half the work. This is shown directly by the relation between A07 and A08 (co-occurrence from about 53 in 100 cases to zero, with single-only cases appearing), by the disappearance of both directed edges between them, and by their halved participation; the lag and position profiles agree, and the core differences exceed those seen in more than 99 in 100 random re-splits of the same data. [REL040, DIR007, DIR008, OCC007, OCC008, LAG002, LAG003, POS007, POS008]

Limitations: the later direction profiles for A07 and A08 rest on few observations (about 18 and 17 outgoing transitions), so a rare residual A07→A08 or A08→A07 step cannot be excluded from the direction data alone, although zero co-occurrence across roughly 100 cases containing either activity makes that implausible. The aggregates cannot show whether the one-of-two behaviour is governed by a new decision point or by replaced case variants, no screening baseline is provided to compare the number of qualifying items against a chance expectation, and a shift in the mix of case subpopulations between the windows cannot be fully ruled out — though random sampling would rarely produce a co-occurrence drop of this size. [REL040, DIR007, DIR008]

Other differences: the modest routing shifts inside the A02–A10 branch (A02 between A04 and A06; A09 between A04 and A06; A06 feeding A02 and A10 slightly more often) and several small lag redistributions there (A02→A04 and A10→A06 pairs moving closer, A04→A06 pairs farther apart) are not part of the main hypothesis; their empirical strengths are moderate and their supports small to moderate, so sampling variation remains a plausible explanation. [DIR002, DIR009, DIR006, LAG005, LAG037, LAG016]
