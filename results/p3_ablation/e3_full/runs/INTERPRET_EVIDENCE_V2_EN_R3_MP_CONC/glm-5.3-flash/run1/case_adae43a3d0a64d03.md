### Observed differences

- A01 and A05 exchanged presence: in the earlier window A05 occurred exactly once in every trace (200 of 200) at the same mid-trace position (normalized position 0.5, no spread), while A01 occurred in no trace; in the later window A05 occurs in no trace (0 of 200) and A01 occurs exactly once in every trace (200 of 200), again at mid-trace position 0.5 with no spread. [OCC001, OCC003, POS001, POS003]
- Incoming transitions were redirected from A05 to A01 with almost the same shares. Earlier, A02's observed outgoing transitions led to A05 in about 72 in 100 cases (A06 for the rest); later they lead to A01 in 75 in 100 (A06 25). A07: A05 about 71 in 100 before, A01 66 in 100 after. A06: A05 about 29 in 100 before, A01 30 in 100 after. [DIR002, DIR005, DIR004]
- The outgoing side matches: earlier, A05 was followed by A03 in all of its observed outgoing transitions; later, A01 is followed by A03 in all of its outgoing transitions. [DIR003, DIR001]
- A05's relational pattern transferred to A01. Earlier, A05 always co-occurred with A06 and A06 always came first, and A05 always preceded A03 and A04; later, A06 precedes A01 in every trace, and A01 precedes A03 and A04 in every trace. The two never co-occur in either window: earlier every trace had A05 without A01, later every trace has A01 without A05. [REL004, REL012, REL014, REL016, REL002, REL003, REL005]
- Precedence from the branching activities is preserved: earlier A02 preceded A05 in the 47% of traces containing A02, and A07 preceded A05 in the 53% containing A07; later A02 (50% of traces) and A07 (50%) each precede A01 in exactly the traces where they run, and A02 and A07 remain mutually exclusive alternatives in both windows. - Downstream placement is essentially unchanged: the event-position gaps from A02, A07 and A06 to A03 and A04 look the same in both windows (for example A02→A03 two positions apart in about 72% of cases earlier and 75% later). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG005, LAG006]
- Minor wobbles outside the swap: A02 participation moved from 47% to 50% and A07 from 53% to 50%, with a few positional-gap shares shifting two to five percentage points; these items are unremarkable against random re-splits (strength roughly 0.3–0.5). [OCC002, OCC004, POS005, LAG005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A05 was replaced by A01 at the same point of the flow. In the earlier window every trace executed A05 exactly once at the middle position, reached from A06 (always) and from whichever of A02 or A07 ran, and always followed by A03; in the later window A05 no longer occurs, while A01 appears in every trace at the same middle position with the same predecessors — A06 before A01 in all traces, A02 and A07 before A01 whenever they run — and the same successor, A03, in all outgoing transitions. The hand-over is visible on both sides: the incoming transition shares that previously pointed to A05 (about 72, 71 and 29 in 100 from A02, A07 and A06) land on A01 at nearly identical values, the sole outgoing edge to A03 (100%) is reproduced, and the placement of A03 and A04 relative to A02, A07 and A06 is unchanged. Because A05 was present in all earlier traces and absent from all later ones (A01 the reverse), this is a replacement of one activity by another rather than a shift in how often existing paths are taken; the many agreeing items all describe this single underlying swap, not independent changes. Limitation: the evidence covers occurrence, order and relative position only, so it cannot show whether A01 is a genuinely new activity or the same step under a different label, and nothing outside these two 200-trace samples is observed. Other differences: the split between the alternatives A02 and A07 moved slightly (about 47/53 to 50/50) and a few positional-gap shares shifted by two to five percentage points; these are within what random re-splits produce and are not part of the main hypothesis.
