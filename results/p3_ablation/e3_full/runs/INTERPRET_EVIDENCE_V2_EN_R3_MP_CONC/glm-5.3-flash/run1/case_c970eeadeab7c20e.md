### Observed differences

- The sharpest difference concerns A06. In the earlier window it occurred exactly once in every trace (200 of 200 traces, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace) and always sat at the end of the trace (mean relative position about 0.95); in the later window it does not appear in any of the 200 traces. [OCC005, POS006]

- Before, A06 and A03 formed a closing pair: they co-occurred in every trace, with A03 before A06 in about 53 in 100 traces and A06 before A03 in about 47 in 100. In the later window the two never share a trace (co-occurrence drops from 100% to 0%, A03-only 100%); the same disappearance shows against A01, A04, A05 and A08, which previously co-occurred with A06 in 100% of traces. [REL018, REL005, REL023, REL027, REL032]

- The connections around A06 vanished. Before, A03's only observed outgoing transition was to A06 (about 106 transitions) and A06's only outgoing transition was to A03 (about 94); in the later window A03 shows no outgoing transitions at all and sits at relative position 1.0 in every trace, i.e., it is always the final event (previously mean about 0.94, last or second-to-last). [DIR002, DIR004, POS003]

- A04's routing into the tail changed accordingly: before, its outgoing transitions went to A02 in about 52 in 100, to A03 in about 26 and to A06 in about 23; in the later window A04 leads to A03 in about 50 in 100 and to A02 in about 50, with no transition to A06. The gain in A03's share matches the former A04→A06→A03 chain (about 94 transitions) now running directly. [DIR003, LAG014]

- Event distances to A03 collapsed from two values to one: before, A03 followed A04 immediately in about 53 in 100 traces and with one intervening event (A06) in about 47 in 100; in the later window A03 always immediately follows A04, and the distances from A01, A05 and A08 to A03 similarly lost their longer variant. [LAG014, LAG002, LAG019, LAG025]

- Most other activities shifted slightly later in relative position (for example A04 from about 0.59 to 0.65, A05 from 0.52 to 0.56, A08 from 0.44 to 0.47), consistent with every trace becoming one event shorter at the end. Smaller shifts appear for A02, A07 and A09, whose participation fell a few points (about 57% to 53%, 52% to 48%, and 52% to 47% of traces respectively). [POS004, POS005, POS008, POS002, OCC002, OCC006, OCC008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A06 is no longer executed in the later window. In the earlier window every trace ended with the adjacent pair A03–A06 in alternating order (A03→A06 in about 53% of traces, A06→A03 in about 47%), whereas in the later window A06 appears in none of the 200 traces, A03 has become the constant final activity, and A04 — which previously could route through A06 to A03 — now reaches A03 directly and splits about evenly between A02 and A03. [OCC005, REL018, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS003, POS006, LAG014]

The change is shown directly by participation (200 of 200 traces to 0 of 200) and consistently across relational, directional, positional and lag evidence: both edges of the A03–A06 pair disappear, four lag distributions to A03 collapse from two distances to one, and the small position shifts of the remaining activities match traces becoming one event shorter. Each of these differences is larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC005, REL018, DIR002, DIR004, POS003, POS006, LAG002, LAG014, LAG019, LAG025, POS004]

Limitations: zero participation only establishes that A06 was not observed in the later window; the evidence cannot distinguish a complete removal from A06 still existing but not occurring in the sampled cases, and it is limited to control flow. The competing explanation that the later window merely failed to sample a still-existing A06 cannot be fully excluded, although a drop from 100% to 0% over 200-trace windows is far outside what random re-splits typically produce. [OCC005]

Other differences: Participation of A02, A07 and A09 fell by about 3.5–5 in 100 traces, with matching small drops in their co-occurrence with A01 and A03, and repetition of A01, A04, A05 and A08 declined slightly; these differences are unremarkable against random re-splits (empirical strengths roughly 0.02–0.74) and may be sampling noise. Event distances from A02/A04 to A07 shortened while those to A09 lengthened, but each of these items rests on only about 30–52 supporting traces, so the evidence cannot establish a second, independent change there.
