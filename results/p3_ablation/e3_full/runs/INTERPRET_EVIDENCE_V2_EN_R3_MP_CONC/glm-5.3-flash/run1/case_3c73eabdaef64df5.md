### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural addition to the control flow: in the later window, the sequence A03 → A05 → A06 runs between A04 and A07, and A01 runs as a new final step after A07, so a case executes A02 → A04 → A03 → A05 → A06 → A07 → A01 where it previously executed only A02 → A04 → A07. The redirection is observed directly on transitions (A04 now followed by A03 rather than A07; A07 now followed by A01), the four added activities move from 0 of 200 to 200 of 200 traces with exactly one occurrence each, and all pairwise relations and step distances match one fixed chain in 100% of later traces.
