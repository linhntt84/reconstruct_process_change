### Observed differences

- Participation and frequency of the activities are essentially the same in both windows: A01 appears in every trace before and later (about 2.0 occurrences per trace in both), A02, A05 and A07 each appear in about 50 in 100 traces in the earlier window and about 52 in 100 in the later window, and A06 in about 33 in 100 earlier versus about 36 in 100 later (65 vs 71 of 200 traces). Mean occurrences per trace move by at most about 0.15 (A05: 2.03 → 1.87), and these differences are common among random re-splits of the pooled data (empirical strengths roughly 0.3–0.4) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005].
- Branching is effectively unchanged: A01's outgoing transitions split between A02 and A03 about 51/49 in the earlier window and 49/51 in the later one, and A05's split between A06 and A07 moves from about 51/49 to 52/48 — both remain close to even in the two windows [DIR001, DIR002].
- Event positions shift only slightly: in the later window A05 and A06 sit a little earlier in the trace (A06 median position about 0.44 → 0.39 of trace length) and A07 a little later (median about 0.52 → 0.57), while A03 stays at median 0.83 in both. These are the relatively most unusual differences in the sample (strengths about 0.88–0.91) but are small in absolute size [POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006].
- Relations between activities shift by at most about 3 percentage points: for example A01 and A06 occur in the same trace in about 33 in 100 cases earlier and 36 in 100 later; no pair changes between being alternatives and being executed together, and no fixed order gives way to a mixed order or vice versa [REL001, REL005, REL013, REL002].
- Gaps between related activities change only marginally: for pairs around A06 the share of pairs at the largest observed lags rises (A01→A06 tail about 9% → 14% of observed pairs), and the share of A06→A06 repetitions two steps apart roughly doubles (about 14% → 25%), though the latter rests on only 30 versus 41 observations [LAG004, LAG016, LAG018, LAG019].

### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence
The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. Every observed difference is marginal — at most about three percentage points in participation, a few hundredths of normalized position, and small lag-share shifts — and most are within the range that random re-splits of a stable process produce, while the few statistically unusual items (positions of A05–A07, gaps around A06) are minute, describe largely the same traces, and cannot rule out sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows [OCC004, POS005, POS006, LAG004, LAG018].
