### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that an existing exclusive choice between A03 and A15 near the end of the process is now taken with different frequencies: in the later window the A03 branch is executed in about 58 of 100 traces (previously about 48) and the A15 branch in about 42 (previously about 53). The reading is direct and consistent: the pair remains strictly exclusive in both windows, three predecessors (A10, A11 and A13) shifted their outgoing transitions from A15 toward A03, and both activities keep their end-of-trace positions and substantial participation, which is not what a move, a swap, or an activity being added or removed would look like. The shift also appears across upstream branches (A03 now co-occurs more often with A02/A10, A04/A08 and A06/A13, while A15 co-occurs less with A01/A12 and A06/A13). The key differences exceed roughly 95 of 100 random re-splits and the routing differences roughly 80, with occurrence, relation, direction and position indicators all agreeing. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is sampling fluctuation in the 200-trace windows; and because several other option shares moved at the same time, the A03/A15 shift may be one facet of a broader re-routing rather than a single changed branch weight.
