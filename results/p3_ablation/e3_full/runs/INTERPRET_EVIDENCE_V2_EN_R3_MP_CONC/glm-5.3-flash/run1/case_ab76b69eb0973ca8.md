### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A08 was no longer executed and the flow that previously passed through it now runs directly from A07 to A08's former successors (A05, A03, A04, A02). This is shown directly by the routing change at A07 and the disappearance of A08's outgoing edges, corroborated by A08's participation falling from 141 to 0 of 200 traces, by all eight activity pairs involving A08 changing from regular co-occurrence to none, and by the one-step shortening of distances downstream of A07; A07's own frequency barely changed, which fits A08 alone dropping out [DIR007, DIR008, OCC007, POS008, REL034, LAG053, LAG054]. The difference is far beyond what sampling between two 200-trace windows can produce: a behaviour present in about 70 in 100 traces does not vanish by chance, and every A08 item shows near-maximal empirical strength (about 0.995) [OCC007, DIR007, REL034].

Limitations: zero participation only establishes that A08 was not observed in the later window, not that it was formally removed from the process; the evidence cannot distinguish removal from A08 becoming optional and never taken, or from a relabelling or merge, and the bypass reading rests on the similarity between A07's new successor profile and A08's former one [DIR007, DIR008, OCC007].

Other differences: the small position and frequency shifts for A06, A04, A05 and A07, plus a few small redistributions in pair relations and self-repetition distances, are not part of the main hypothesis; many of them are plausibly side effects of one event fewer per trace rather than independent changes [POS006, POS004, POS005, OCC006, REL003, LAG044, LAG064].
