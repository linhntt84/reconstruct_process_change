### Observed differences

- The split at the branch point A02 flipped: in the earlier window A02 was followed by A04 in about 56 in 100 traces and by A03 in about 44 in 100; in the later window the shares are about 47 in 100 for A04 and 53 in 100 for A03. [DIR001]
- Trace participation moved by the same 9-percentage-point margin. A04 appears in 56% of traces before and 47% after, always at most once per trace; A03, A06, A07 and A10 each rise from 44% to 53% of traces (A03 and A06 also recur somewhat more often, mean occurrences per trace 0.96 to 1.21 and 0.95 to 1.17); A05 rises from about 27% to 29% and A09 from about 23% to 29%. A01, A02 and A08 occur in every trace in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, POS001, POS006, REL001]
- The two branches are mutually exclusive alternatives: A04 never shares a trace with A03, A05, A06, A07, A09 or A10 in either window, and A05 and A09 occur only alongside A03. Within the A03 branch, A03, A06, A07 and A10 co-occur in 44% of traces before and 53% after. [REL015, REL016, REL017, REL020, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL027]
- Routing inside the branches is essentially unchanged: the outgoing splits of A03 (mainly to A05 and A07), A06 (mainly to A09 and A10), A07 (mainly to A06) and A09 (almost always to A06) keep their proportions, and the reference comparisons judge these differences unremarkable. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- A02 and A08 sit at earlier relative event positions in the later window (A02's median from about two-thirds to one-third of the trace, A08's from about one-third to one-sixth); positions are event-based, and the shift is in line with more traces taking the longer, activity-richer A03 branch. [POS001, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the two existing alternative paths after A02 are taken with different frequencies: the short path containing only A04 is chosen in about 47 in 100 cases (about 56 in 100 before), while the longer path through A03 — continuing with A06, A07 and A10 and, in roughly half of those cases, also A05 and/or A09 — is chosen in about 53 in 100 cases (about 44 in 100 before). The change is shown directly by the successor shares at A02 itself and is consistent across occurrence, participation and co-occurrence measures, all moving by the same 9-percentage-point margin in the same direction; routing within each path and the participation of A01, A02 and A08 are stable, so the difference is confined to how often the two existing paths are chosen, not to activities being added, removed, repeated differently or moved. The main limitation is that all items derive from the same 200-plus-200 traces, so the agreeing indicators largely mirror one underlying branch assignment rather than independent confirmations, and a random re-split of an unchanged process produces a difference of this size in about 6 in 100 reference comparisons, so a sampling fluctuation cannot be fully excluded. Other differences: a few gap distributions between activity pairs shifted slightly (for example A08→A07 gaps concentrating at a distance of three events, and A09→A09 repetitions spacing slightly differently), consistent with the higher share of longer A03-branch traces; an apparent change in the A09→A07 gap rests on a single trace in each window.
