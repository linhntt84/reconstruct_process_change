### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01, the step that previously closed about half of all traces directly after A12, is no longer executed, and that those traces now end with A12 itself. In the earlier window about 52 in 100 traces contained A01, always as the last event and always directly after A12; in the later window A01 is not observed in any of 200 traces, while A12 sits exactly at the final position and shows no observed outgoing transitions. [OCC001, POS001, DIR006, POS010, REL011]

Supporting signs: the participation drop is direct (103 of 200 traces to 0 of 200), the successor change at A12 is observed in the direction data, and all fifteen relationship items involving A01 collapse uniformly while its former partners continue at stable participation, so the whole branch did not disappear, only its final step. The difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 of the reference re-splits on every A01 item, and a relocation of A01 is ruled out because it is observed at no position in the later window. [OCC001, DIR006, REL001, REL003, REL011, OCC012, POS001]

Limitations: zero participation only means A01 was not observed; the evidence cannot distinguish removal of the activity from it now being consistently skipped or no longer recorded, and no screening baseline is included for a chance-expectation comparison of item counts. [OCC001, POS001]

Other differences: Within the same branch, routing and repetition changed: A06 and A14 now always lead to A10 (previously split roughly 54/46 with A14 and A15 respectively), A07 and A13 now also lead to each other and to A08 instead of always to A05, A05 and A13 repeat less per trace while A10, A11 and A14 repeat more or appear in different shares, and several relative positions shifted. These rerouting changes are directly observed but are reported separately from the main hypothesis; whether they belong to the same underlying modification as the A01 disappearance cannot be determined from this evidence.
