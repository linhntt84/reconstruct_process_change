### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A07 ran in about 23 in 100 traces (46 of 200), typically after A01 and A10 and before A03 or A13; in the later window, A07 does not appear in any of the 200 traces — no occurrences, no observed outgoing transitions, and no trace contains it together with any other activity [OCC006, DIR006, POS007, REL006, REL060].

- A03 and A13 remain alternatives that never share a trace, near the end of the case; A03 rose from about 50 to 56 in 100 traces while A13 fell from about 50 to 45 in 100 [OCC002, OCC011, REL033].

- The early A01/A10 order stayed mixed in both windows (A01 first in about 54 in 100 earlier, about 47 in 100 later), with A01's event positions shifted slightly later [REL009, POS001, POS010].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the executions containing A07 stopped: A07, previously an optional step in about 23 in 100 cases, placed between the early activities A01/A10 and the late alternatives A03/A13, was not observed in any of the 200 later traces, and its former transitions (in from A01 and A10, out to A03, A13, A10 and A01) disappeared with it. The occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators all show the same disappearance, each difference larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits, and the former neighbours adjusted as expected, with A01/A10 now reaching A03/A13 in fewer steps; a chance absence after 46 occurrences in the earlier sample is implausible [OCC006, DIR001, DIR006, DIR009, POS007, REL006, LAG002, LAG048]. What the evidence cannot rule out is that A07 was taken out of the process versus a routing change under which no case reaches it any more; zero participation only shows it was not observed.

Other differences: several optional blocks changed how often they run — A02/A09 up (about 23 to 29 in 100 traces), A04/A08 down (about 55 to 46 in 100), A06/A11 down (about 29 to 24 in 100) — and the split between the end alternatives A03 and A13 moved from roughly 50/50 to about 56/44. Unlike the A07 disappearance, these shifts of 4–9 cases in 100 are small enough that sampling differences between two 200-trace windows remain a plausible competing explanation [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC005, OCC008, OCC009, OCC011, REL033].
