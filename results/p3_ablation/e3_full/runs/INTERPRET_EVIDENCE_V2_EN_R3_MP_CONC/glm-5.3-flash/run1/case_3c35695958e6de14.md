### Observed differences

- **A01 appeared.** In the earlier window, A01 occurs in none of the 200 traces; in the later window it occurs in all 200 (100%), about 2.1 times per trace, repeating in about 53% of traces, at an early-to-middle relative position (median about a quarter into the trace). [OCC001, POS001]

- **The A03/A12 branch is now reached through A01.** Earlier, A02 led directly to A03 (about 49% of its outgoing transitions) or A12 (about 51%), and A09 led to A12 (about 55%) or A03 (about 45%); in the later window, A02 and A09 each lead exclusively to A01, and A01 leads to A12 (about 53%) or A03 (about 47%) — the same branch A02 previously fed directly. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR007]

- **A08 was partially rewired and repeats more.** Earlier, every observed A08 transition went to A13; in the later window, about half go to A01 and half to A13. A08's mean occurrences per trace nearly doubled (about 0.5 to about 1.0) while its participation slightly fell (38% to 34% of traces), i.e., it repeats more within its traces. [DIR006, OCC006]

- **Downstream distances shifted one step later.** The gap from A02 to A03 moved from mostly 1–2 steps to 2–3 steps, from A02 to A05 from 3 to 4 steps, and from A09 to A12 from 1–2 to 2–3 steps; pairs from A04 show the same one-step shift (e.g., A04→A03 from 2–3 to 3–4 steps). [LAG001, LAG003, LAG008, LAG020, LAG025, LAG043, LAG050]

- **A13 became rarer but more repetitive and now leads back to A08.** Its participation fell from about 38 to about 17 in 100 traces (76 vs 34 traces) while occurrences within its traces rose from about 1.3 to about 2.8; earlier it always followed A08 and led to A12 (about 54%) or A03 (about 46%), whereas later it always leads back to A08, forming an A08–A13–A08 cycle. [OCC011, DIR006, DIR009, REL063]

- **A01 now co-occurs with every other activity.** Earlier, no trace contained A01 together with any of A02–A13; later, each activity co-occurs with A01 whenever it occurs at all (A02 and A03 in 100% of traces, A04 in 39%, A13 in 17%). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL012]

- **Elsewhere behaviour is largely stable.** A03's and A12's outgoing behaviour is essentially unchanged (dissimilarity about 0.001, empirical strength about 0.11); the only other shifts are small routing changes at A06 (A07 vs A08 from about 52/48 to 57/43) and A07 (A11 vs A10 from about 57/43 to 51/49). [DIR003, DIR008, DIR004, DIR005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added and wired in between the activities that previously fed the A03/A12 branch and that branch. In the earlier window, A02 and A09 passed directly into A03/A12 (roughly 50/50 each) and A08 reached that branch only via A13; in the later window, A02 and A09 lead exclusively to A01, A08 leads to A01 in about half of its transitions, and A01 routes to A12 (about 53%) or A03 (about 47%). Supporting signs: A01's participation swung from 0 to 200 of 200 traces with about 2.1 occurrences per trace — now matching A03's and A12's frequency, consistent with A01 feeding that pair on each pass; nearly all downstream distances from A02, A04 and A09 increased by exactly one step, fitting a single insertion point; and A03/A12's own outgoing behaviour is unchanged, placing the change upstream of them. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC010, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG008, LAG050]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly establishes only that A01 was not observed there, not why; the exact wiring and frequency of the A08–A13 cycle is only partially resolved; and positions are event positions, so nothing follows about clock-time timing. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that the earlier window sampled a variant of the process without A01 rather than a prior state of the same process — although a 0-to-100% participation swing between 200-trace windows is far outside ordinary sampling fluctuation (empirical strength 0.995 on the occurrence and direction items). [OCC001, DIR002, DIR006, DIR009]

Other differences: the rerouting of A13 into a repeat cycle with A08, with A13's participation halved (76 to 34 of 200 traces) and A08 repeating more often; small routing-share shifts at A06 (toward A07) and A07 (toward A10); and slightly later relative positions for A03, A12 and A08. The evidence does not show whether these accompany the insertion as one modification or are separate changes. [OCC011, OCC006, DIR004, DIR005, DIR009, POS002, POS006, POS010, REL063]
