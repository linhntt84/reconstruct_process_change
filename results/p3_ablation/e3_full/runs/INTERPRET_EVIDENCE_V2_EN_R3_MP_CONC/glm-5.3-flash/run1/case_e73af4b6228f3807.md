### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A10 was added to the branch that previously contained only A11: in the earlier window a case ran A05, then either A01 (about 49 in 100) or A11 alone (about 51 in 100), then A07; in the later window the A11 alternative became A10 and A11 executed together, in either order, before continuing to A07 (about 47 in 100 traces). This is shown directly and consistently: A10's participation rises from 0 to 94 of 200 traces, it gains its own outgoing edges to A07 and A11, A11's dominant successor switches from A07 to A10, A10 co-occurs with A11 in every trace it appears in and never with A01, and one-step lag increases appear exactly on the paths crossing the new pair, while the branch's overall share stays roughly stable (about 51 in 100 before, 47 in 100 after) — fitting an insertion rather than a re-routing of cases. The evidence cannot rule out that A10 existed before at a rate too low to appear in 200 traces, and no screening baseline of chance-expected items accompanies the evidence, so the exclusion of sampling noise rests on the size and coherence of the differences rather than a formal expectation [OCC008, DIR004, DIR005, REL009, REL055, LAG008, LAG017].
