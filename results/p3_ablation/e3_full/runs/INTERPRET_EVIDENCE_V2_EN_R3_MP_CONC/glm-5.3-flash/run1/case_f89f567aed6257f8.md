### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured by adding a new mid-process activity, A01, and dropping the activity A14 that previously closed every trace. In the earlier window A01 never appeared while A14 ran exactly once per trace beside A03 at the end; in the later window A01 runs in about 56 in 100 traces — typically several times per trace, handing control on to A02, A05, A09 and A11 — while A14 is no longer observed and A03 now always ends the trace, reached directly from A06. The transitions of A08, A09, A10, A13 and A17, which previously went straight to A02, A11, A13 or A09, now largely run through A01, and A01 and A09 often alternate or repeat within the same trace. This rests on direct occurrence counts (0 vs 112 of 200 traces for A01; 200 vs 0 for A14), on directional profiles that show the new and lost edges, and on relation items that consistently move from never-together to frequent co-occurrence for A01 and from always-together to absent for A14, with differences larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits; many items describe the same underlying traces (for example, all relation items involving A01 reflect its single appearance), so they corroborate rather than independently confirm one another. Positions are event positions, so "middle" and "end" refer to sequence order, not clock time. [OCC001, OCC010, POS001, POS003, POS014, DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR012, DIR013, DIR016, REL001, REL008, REL013, REL039]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of case variants between the windows — for example, the later sample containing a variant that includes A01 and never reaches A14 — rather than a modification of one and the same process; with 200 traces per window, these data alone cannot separate the two. [OCC001, OCC010]

Other differences: A05 was bypassed on the earlier A11–A05–A16 route — A11 now leads to A15 or A16 in roughly equal shares, and A05, present in only about 29 in 100 later traces, leads to A12 or A13 — and A05 now seldom runs together with A08. A12's participation fell from about 37 to 20 in 100 traces, while A15 (about 33 to 41) and A16 (about 62 to 70) became more frequent and A15's mean position moved later (about 0.38 to 0.50).
