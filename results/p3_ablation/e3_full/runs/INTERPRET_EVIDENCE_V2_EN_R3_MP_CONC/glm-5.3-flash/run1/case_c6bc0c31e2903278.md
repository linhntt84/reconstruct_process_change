### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window runs a structurally different variant of the process: A01 is no longer executed; A03 and A10, previously mandatory and always executed together in either order, now behave as mutually exclusive alternatives, each taken in about half of the cases; the previously unobserved A08 now runs in about half of the cases, always together with A12 and in every case as an alternative to A04 (exactly one of the two per trace); and A09 now runs between A06 and A07 in about one in five cases. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC011, REL028, REL034, REL052, REL060, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, DIR010]

This is shown directly by relational and directional evidence rather than by marginal counts alone: A03/A10 co-occurrence fell from 100% to 0% after both orders had been observed in similar shares [REL028]; A04 and A08 partition all later traces [REL034]; A08's ties to A07 and A12 and the A06→A09→A07 chain are visible in the directly-follows and lag profiles [DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, DIR010, LAG015, REL052, REL060]. Occurrence, position and lag items agree with the same reading, and the qualifying items number in the dozens, most at the top of the reference comparisons (empirical strength about 0.995), well beyond the few isolated findings an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC003, POS005, POS009, LAG002, LAG015]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is sampling variation in finite windows: "not observed" for A01, A08 and A09 means only that they did not appear, so a previously invisible rare path cannot be excluded — although a fall from 64 to 0 of 200 traces is hard to explain by sampling alone. Note also that many items describe the same underlying trace behaviour (A08's appearance, for instance, shows simultaneously in occurrence, position, direction and relation items), so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC007, OCC008, POS005, DIR006, REL052]
