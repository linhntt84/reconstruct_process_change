### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the A11 → A03 segment was removed from the process, so that A06 now continues directly to A19 and A12. In the earlier window, A06 fed into A03 in about 86 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, A11 looped back into A03, and A03 exited to A11, A19 or A12; A03, A06, A12 and A19 always occurred together in about 29 in 100 traces, with A11 in about two thirds of those traces. In the later window, A03 and A11 are not observed in any of the 200 traces (against 57 and 39 before), A06 connects straight to A19 (about 58 in 100) and A12 (about 37 in 100), and the remaining A06/A12/A19 branch runs in about 22 in 100 traces. The signs are direct and consistent: the participations fall from 57 and 39 traces to zero (differences larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits), the successor change at A06 is observed directly rather than inferred from positions, co-occurrence with A03/A11 collapses to zero in every examined pair while A06/A12/A19 stay together, and the shortened event distances (for example A06→A04 from typically 5 to 4 steps) are what removing an intermediate segment predicts. [OCC003, OCC009, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, OCC005, REL030, REL036, REL043, REL070, LAG022, LAG026]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A03 and A11 were not observed, so a renaming into other activity codes cannot be excluded from this evidence, although no activity appears in their place; the evidence set contains no screening baseline, so the judgement rests on directness and consistency rather than a chance-expectation count; and the moderate drop in how often the branch runs (about 29 → 22 in 100 traces, larger than in about 935 of 1000 random re-splits) accompanies the structural change but is the weakest part of the picture. [OCC003, OCC009, OCC005]

Other differences: A02 lost its direct transition into the removed segment and now routes relatively more to A04 (about 46 → 57 in 100 of its outgoing transitions); it also repeats less (mean 1.12 → 0.905 occurrences per trace) and appears earlier in traces, all consistent with the same restructuring but not specific to it. A04, A09, A13 and A18 likewise appear at earlier positions (about 0.06–0.09 earlier on average). The routing shifts at A01, A14 and A17 amount to a few percentage points and may be sampling variation. [OCC002, DIR002, POS002, POS004, POS008, POS012, POS016, DIR001, DIR008, DIR009]
