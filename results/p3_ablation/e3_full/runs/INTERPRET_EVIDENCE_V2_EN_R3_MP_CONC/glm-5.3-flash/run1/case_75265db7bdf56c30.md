### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new closing activity, A01, was appended to the end of every process execution, so that each case now ends with A03 followed by A01. In the earlier window A01 was not observed in any of 200 traces and A03 terminated every trace; in the later window A01 runs exactly once per case at the final position and is the only observed successor of A03 (100 in 100 of A03's outgoing transitions). Occurrence, positional, directional and relational indicators all show this directly and consistently, and the remaining activities keep their participation and mutual order, with position shifts of only about 0.05–0.14 that match the rescaling caused by one extra final event rather than any reordering [OCC001, POS001, DIR002, POS003, REL002, POS005, POS009, REL028]. One competing explanation remains: zero participation before only shows A01 was not recorded, so a logging or recording change that previously suppressed A01 cannot be ruled out.

Other differences: the mutually exclusive alternatives A06 and A11 exchanged a modest share of cases (A06 about 25 to 21 in 100, A11 about 24 to 30 in 100, A02 stable at about 50 in 100); a shift of this size (roughly 9 and 12 cases out of 200) could also arise from sampling between two 200-trace windows, and the related successor and lag wobbles around A06 and A11 rest on small observation counts of roughly 10–60 cases [OCC004, OCC006, REL014, REL019, REL041, DIR005, LAG027].
