### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two activities were added to the process and one was removed: A01 now runs between A16 and A03, in the position A09 previously occupied, and A02 now runs between the A04–A05–A13 region and A12 (continuing to A12 and A20); A09 is no longer executed at all. Earlier the route ran A16→A09→A03 and A05/A13 fed A12 directly; later it runs A16→A01→A03 and A05/A13→A02→A12. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR005, DIR009, DIR013, DIR016, REL021, REL029, REL043]

Supporting signs: the participation changes are large and directly observed (0 of 200 traces rising to 117 and 173 of 200 for A01 and A02; 108 of 200 falling to 0 for A09), the successor profiles show the new and lost edges directly, and the relational items confirm the new orderings (A04 before A02 in about 85 in 100 joint traces, A02 before A12 in about 87 in 100, A03–A09 co-occurrence falling from about 54 in 100 to zero). Occurrence, direction, position and relation indicators agree, each backed by roughly 100–200 traces per window, with differences larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR005, DIR013, DIR016, POS001, POS002, POS009, REL021, REL029, REL043]

Limitations: this evidence file contains no screening counts, so the number of qualifying items cannot be compared with the number expected for an unchanged process; the judgement rests on the size and directness of the participation and routing changes. A competing explanation the data cannot rule out is that A01 and A02 are re-coded or renamed versions of existing activities (and A09 likewise renamed) rather than genuinely new or removed steps, since the evidence shows presence, order and routing but not activity identity or cause. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR009]

Other differences: A14 and A17 changed in ways not covered by the main hypothesis — A14 became near-universal and repeating (participation about 39 to 89 in 100 traces, mean about 0.4 to 1.7, now near the end of the trace), A14 and A17 now co-occur in about 42 in 100 traces (never before) with A14→A17 and A17→A14 as their dominant transitions, and A17 repeats about twice as often per trace. The small position advances and longer step distances elsewhere accompany these changes but are not independent findings. [OCC013, OCC016, DIR008, DIR014, DIR017, POS014, REL172]
