### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window a new step, A01, executes between A07 and A04 in every case: previously A07 was followed directly by A04, whereas now A07 leads to A01 and A01 leads on to A04, while A02–A07 keep their participation and roles. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: A01 goes from 0 of 200 to 200 of 200 traces; the entire observed outgoing flow of A07 switches from A04 to A01 and A01's flow goes entirely to A04; A01's co-occurrence and order with all six other activities match exactly this placement; and all eight measured pair distances spanning that point increase by exactly one step. The small position shifts of A04, A05, A06 and A07 and the longer pair distances are consequences of this same added step rather than separate differences, and each item's difference is larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Limitations: all items derive from the same 200 traces per window, so they are not independent confirmations, and the evidence covers only the measured activities and pairs. The competing explanation that A01 already existed in a rare earlier variant missed by the 200-trace sample cannot be fully excluded, although 0 of 200 before versus 200 of 200 after makes it unlikely.
