### Observed differences

- In the later window, a larger share of traces contains A05, A04, A06 and A01/A11: A05 appears in about 37 in 100 traces versus about 26 before (74 vs 51 traces), A04 and A06 in about 34 versus about 28–29, and A01/A11 in about 69 versus about 64–65; the A02/A07/A10 trio rises only from about 28 to 33 in 100 (a weak signal), while A08/A09 stay at about 66 in 100. Note that A01/A11, this trio and A08/A09 each always occur together and A03 is present in every trace, so each group's figures describe one joint behaviour. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC007, OCC010, REL002, REL010, REL015, REL050]
- Activities also occur more often per trace: mean occurrences rose for A05 (0.39→0.58), A04 (0.42→0.55), A01/A11 (0.88→1.06) and A08/A09 (0.97→1.05). For A08/A09 this came entirely from more repeats within the same share of traces (about 1.5→1.6 occurrences per containing trace), whereas for A05 it came mostly from more traces participating. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC007, OCC010]
- The internal order of the A02/A07/A10 trio reversed: before, about 58% of A02's outgoing transitions went to A10 and 35% to A07, and from A10 about 60% went to A07 and 35% to A02; in the later window from A02 about 58% go to A07 and 39% to A10, and from A10 about 50% go to A02 and 38% to A07 — the typical hand-over changed from A02→A10→A07 to A10→A02→A07, and the direct A02→A04 transition (about 4% before) is no longer observed. Distances match: A02→A07 within one step rose from about 20% to 34% of cases, A10→A07 within one step fell from about 38% to 25%, and A10→A02 at lag 5 fell from 14% to 3.5%. [DIR002, DIR008, LAG018, LAG085, LAG090]
- More flow reaches A04 and A05: from A04, transitions to A05 rose from about 17% to 31% of its outgoing transitions (to A02 fell from 30% to 17%), and the observed outgoing transitions of A04 rose from 84 to 110 and of A05 from 78 to 116; from A09, transitions to A04 rose from about 13% to 20% and to A05 from about 7% to 16%, while transitions to A03 fell from 55% to 43%. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR007]
- Relations and distances shifted broadly: co-occurrence rose with less joint absence around A01, A02, A04, A05 and A06 (e.g., A01/A05 together in 33% vs 23.5% of traces, joint absence 27% vs 33.5%), A08 precedes A09 in 42% of traces versus 48.5% with more interleaving, many pairwise distances changed in both directions (e.g., A09→A11 now usually 3–4 steps apart instead of 5, lag-5 share 45%→12%; A10→A11 rarely within two steps, 29%→5%), and A06 and A01 appear at later event positions (A06 median position 0.33→0.5). [REL004, REL021, REL050, LAG083, LAG094, POS001, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window the part of the process containing A01/A11 together with A04, A05 and A06 is executed in more cases and with more occurrences: A05 participates in about 37 in 100 traces versus about 26 before, A04 and A06 in about 34 versus about 28–29, A01/A11 in about 69 versus about 64–65, and more transitions flow into A04 and A05 (from A04 to A05, and from A09 to A04 and A05). The support is direct and consistent: participation and occurrence counts are measured directly with high reference strengths (0.81–0.97), co-occurrence rises while joint absence falls precisely around these activities, and the routing changes point the same way. Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether this is one underlying modification or several independent ones, and A01 and A11 always occur together, so they contribute one signal rather than two. A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that part of the rise — especially the milder one for the A02/A07/A10 trio (reference strength about 0.47) — reflects the two finite windows sampling different case proportions rather than a durable change. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC010, DIR003, DIR007, REL004, REL021]

Other differences: the A02/A07/A10 trio runs in a changed internal order — the typical hand-over changed from A02→A10→A07 to A10→A02→A07, and the direct A02→A04 transition is no longer observed; this is shown directly by the mirrored successor shares at A02 and A10 and by the shorter A02→A07 and longer A10→A07 distances, though it cannot be ruled out that this is a by-product of more repetitions inside the trio rather than an exchange of the two activities' places. A08/A09 also repeat slightly more often (about 1.5→1.6 occurrences per containing trace) without appearing in more traces, and A06 and A01 appear at later event positions.
