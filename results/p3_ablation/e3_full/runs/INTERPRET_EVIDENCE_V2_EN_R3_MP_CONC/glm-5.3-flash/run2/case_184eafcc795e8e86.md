### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, every case carries two added steps: A01 is executed once, inserted between A06/A13 and A04 so that the previously direct A06→A04 and A13→A04 transitions now pass through A01, and A02 is executed once as a new closing step after A12, which was previously the final event. The signs are direct and consistent: occurrence goes from 0 of 200 traces to 200 of 200 for both activities with maximal empirical strength; the new transitions (A01→A04, A12→A02) and the replaced transitions (A06→A01, A13→A01) are observed directly; A01 co-occurs with each established activity in exactly the share of traces in which that activity occurs, always after A06/A13 and always before A04, A12 and A02; and lag distributions downstream of the insertion point shift by one step, as expected when an event is inserted. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, REL001, REL005, REL012, LAG024, LAG036]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window establishes only that A01 and A02 were not exercised then, not whether they existed unused; positions and lags are event positions, so the change concerns ordering within the case, not clock time. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 and A02 were a pre-existing but previously unobserved rare path rather than newly added steps, although 0 of 200 earlier traces showing them makes this unlikely. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, LAG024]

Other differences: minor shifts outside the main hypothesis — A03's repetitions rose slightly (about 4.1 to 4.2 per trace) and A07/A10 slightly (about 2.05 to 2.20), and a few lag profiles moved modestly (e.g., A10→A07, A11→A11); individually these are small and, given the modest support behind some of them, within the range random re-splits of a stable process can produce. [OCC003, OCC006, OCC009, LAG028, LAG031]
