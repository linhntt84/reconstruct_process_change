### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The largest movements — A03, A09 and A12 appearing in a somewhat larger share of traces, with co-occurrence rises that mirror those gains — are of a size and empirical strength (around 0.45–0.75, i.e., differences of this size arise routinely when the two 200-trace windows are re-split at random) that an unchanged process also produces, so sampling variability cannot be ruled out.
