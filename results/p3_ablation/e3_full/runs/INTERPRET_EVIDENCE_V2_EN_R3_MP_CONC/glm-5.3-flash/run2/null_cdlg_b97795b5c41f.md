### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence
The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The few elevated items (A06's more even A04/A01 split, the slightly wider spacing of A01 and A04 repeats) are small shifts on coupled indicators that likely reflect the same traces rather than independent confirmations, their empirical strengths are exceeded by a sizeable share of random re-splits, and no screening baseline is provided to compare qualified items against chance.
