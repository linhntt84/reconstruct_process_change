### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence directly and consistently supports the hypothesis that A06 was removed from the closing block of the process, or at minimum is no longer executed in any observed case. Before, every trace ran exactly one A06 adjacent to A03 at the end — A03 → A06 in about 53 in 100 traces, A06 → A03 in about 47 in 100 — and A04 reached this ending either directly (about 26% of its outgoing transitions to A03) or through A06 (about 23%). In the later window, A06 appears in none of 200 traces, A04 leads straight to A03 (about 50% of its outgoing transitions), and A03 ends every trace at the final position with no observed successor; the one-step shortening of the A01/A04/A05/A08 → A03 distances in exactly the previously-routed share corroborates this. The change is observed directly by occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators, the core items all reach empirical strength of about 0.995, and a participation drop from 200 of 200 traces to 0 of 200 is not what sampling an unchanged process produces. Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine removal of A06 from it merely never being triggered (for example, if its ending branch became unreachable) or a relabelling outside the observed activity set; also, no screening baseline was provided to compare the item count against a formal chance expectation.
