### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window the control flow was reconfigured around four newly observed activities (A07, A15, A16, A17) together with a rerouting of their neighbours. Before, A08 passed to A05, A12 passed to A18, A18 passed only to A02, and A10 mostly repeated itself or exited to A03 and A02; in the later window A08 passes to A07, which leads to A18; A18 exits via A14 and via A17, which leads to A09; A10 alternates with A15 and exits through A16 to A02 and A03; and A12 passes to A05, which now leads to A02 and A13. Alongside this, A05 and A13 repeat more often per case, and A18 repeats less often while appearing in more traces. [DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011, DIR012, OCC004, OCC012, OCC017]

Supporting signs: the four activities move from 0 of 200 traces to 65–139 of 200, a shift far outside the range of the 200 random re-splits used as reference; the new and the no-longer-observed transitions are seen directly in the direction profiles; the co-occurrence items show the new activities integrated with nearly every established activity only in the later window; and the repetition and position changes fit the same rewiring. [OCC006, OCC014, OCC015, OCC016, DIR007, DIR012, REL045, REL095, POS017]

Limitations: the many relation items largely restate the same appearance of the four activities rather than independent confirmations; the insertion points suggested by the distance items rest partly on small samples (some under 40 traces per window); and the evidence cannot show whether this was one coordinated rewiring or several independent edits. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that the earlier window missed a rare variant containing A07, A15, A16 and A17 — unlikely at zero occurrences in 200 traces against a third to two thirds later, but the windows are finite samples.
