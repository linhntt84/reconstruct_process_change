### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A03 was added as a new, mandatory step executed in every case immediately after A07: in the earlier window A07 passed directly to one of A02, A06 or A10, while in the later window A07 always leads to A03, which then hands over to A02, A06 or A10 in nearly the same shares, and every downstream activity (A02, A05, A06, A08, A09, A10, A01) takes place one event later.
