### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence
The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The only difference between the windows — a shift of about 2 in 100 traces between the two existing alternatives (A01/A02 versus the A03–A06 group) — is well within the range that random re-splits of unchanged data produce, and no screening baseline is provided to weigh the number of qualifying items against chance expectations [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, REL010].
