### Observed differences

- Participation and frequency moved in opposite directions for the two hub activities. In the earlier window, A02 appeared in about 49 of 100 traces (1.0 occurrence per trace) and A08 in every trace (about 2.0 per trace); in the later window, A02 appears in every trace (about 1.8 per trace) while A08 appears in only about 45 of 100 (about 0.8 per trace). A01 fell from about 49 to about 31 of 100 traces (1.0 to about 0.4 occurrences per trace), A06 from about 49 to about 29 (about 2.0 to about 0.7 per trace), and A07 from about 31 to about 15 of 100; A03 stayed at a similar share of traces (about 49% vs 45%) but with fewer occurrences per trace (1.0 to about 0.77). A04 and A05 occur in all traces in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, POS004, REL010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the control flow around A02 was rerouted and A02 became a mandatory, repeated hub. In the earlier window, A02 appeared in about half of the cases and always handed over directly to A01, while A08 occurred in every case, typically near the start, and led to A03 or A04 in equal shares. In the later window, A02 occurs in every case and now leads directly to A04 (with A05 about two positions later) or to A03; the direct A02→A01 hand-over is no longer observed among A02's successors, and A01 — now in about 31 of 100 cases — follows about two positions after A02, typically via A03, which now splits between A01 (about 52%) and A06 (about 48%). A08 became optional (about 45 of 100 cases) and now always returns to A02, so the segment A02→A03→A06→A08→A02 can repeat within a trace, which fits A02's rise to about 1.8 occurrences per trace; A06 now feeds A08 (about 53%) or A07. Together with this rerouting, A01, A06, A07 and A08 are executed in fewer cases and less often per case. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL009, REL011, REL021, LAG012, LAG016, LAG040, LAG009]

This hypothesis is direct and consistent: the successor changes are observed in the routing data itself, the co-occurrence, position and distance items all point to the same connected set of activities rather than scattered differences, nearly every qualifying item shows a difference larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, and A04's own position stayed essentially unchanged, which argues against a wholesale relocation of all activities. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shifted mix of unchanged case variants rather than a modified flow; also, because positions and distances are measured in event steps, part of the one-step shifts could reflect a different number of repetitions of the segment rather than relocated activities, and an unobserved edge such as A02→A01 is not proof that the connection was removed. [DIR001, DIR004, POS004, OCC002, LAG016]

Other differences: A06 and A07 show only small shifts in their self-repetition distances and relative order (e.g., A06→A06 at lag 2 rising from about 10 to about 22 of 100 pair observations), several of which have weak empirical support. [LAG029, LAG030, LAG036, LAG037]
