### Observed differences

- Activity presence is stable: A05, A06, A07, A08 and A10 each occur in roughly half of the traces in both windows (A07 in about 50 of 100 traces in the earlier window and 54 of 100 in the later; A08 in about 50 before and 46 after), and no activity repeats within a trace in either window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

- In the later window, A02 leads directly to A05 in about 8 of 100 of its outgoing transitions, down from about 17 of 100 (roughly 33 vs 16 of 200 traces), while A02→A07 rises from about 20 to 25 of 100 and A02→A08 from about 21 to 26 of 100. [DIR002]

- A05 is reached differently: where A05 follows A04 within five steps, immediate succession rises from about 39 of 100 such pairs in the earlier window to 51 of 100 in the later (distance-2 falls from about 52 to 30 of 100), whereas after A02 the typical gap lengthens (distance-1 share about 39→21 of 100, distance-2 about 25→43 of 100). [LAG018, LAG010]

- A08 moves the opposite way: it now typically follows A02 immediately (distance-1 share about 41→57 of 100) and occurs earlier after the start activity A01 (distance-4 share about 38→25 of 100, distance-2 about 14→22 of 100); its mean event position also shifts slightly earlier. [LAG013, LAG006, POS007]

- The direct A05→A08 succession roughly halves (about 22→12 of 100 of A05's outgoing transitions, i.e. about 23 vs 12 traces; A05-before-A08 order in about 16 of 100 traces before and 11 of 100 after), while A08 hands on more often to A05 (about 13→18 of 100) and A04 (about 14→22 of 100) and less often to the closing A03 (about 23→17 of 100). [DIR004, REL025, DIR007]

- A06 and A10, which always occur together, are now more often reached from A07 (A07→A06 up from about 19 to 26 of 100 of A07's outgoing transitions, typically immediately: distance-1 share about 61→82 of 100) and less often from A08 (A08→A10 down to about 2 of 100 from about 6, with A10 typically two to three steps after A08 instead of one to two); these comparisons rest on only about 30–43 observed pairs per window. [DIR006, LAG036, DIR007, LAG043]

- Other order relations barely move: A04 precedes A02 in about 53 of 100 traces after versus 50 before, and A03 precedes A09 in about 50 of 100 after versus 53 before. [REL006, REL016]

### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests one specific possibility: in the later window, traces more often reach A08 directly from A02 and reach A05 one step later, typically immediately after A04, so that A05 and A08 partially exchange their places in the sequence — the direct successions A02→A05 (about 33 vs 16 of 200 traces) and A05→A08 (about 23 vs 12) carry fewer traces, while A02→A08 and A08→A05 carry more. The change is shown directly in successor shares (DIR002, empirical strength about 0.93 over all 200 traces per window) and in the distance distributions of the pairs involved (LAG018 about 0.97, LAG013 about 0.91, LAG010 about 0.86, each backed by roughly 80–105 traces per window), and it is not an effect of activities appearing, disappearing or repeating, since participation is flat in both windows. [DIR002, LAG018, LAG010, LAG013, LAG006, POS007, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

What is missing: the evidence contains no screening baseline stating how many qualifying items an unchanged process would produce, and across the roughly 100 tested activities and pairs the number of highly unusual items (six with empirical strength above 0.9) is no larger than chance would typically yield; the individual shifts amount to only about 10–20 traces in 200 per window, and several items describe the same rerouting rather than independent confirmations. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is therefore a sampling fluctuation in the frequencies of existing execution variants, without any change to the process. [DIR002, LAG013, LAG018, LAG036, LAG038, LAG043]

Other differences: A06/A10 are now more often preceded by A07 and less often by A08, and A05 leads more often to the closing activities A03/A09 (about 38→46 of 100 of A05's outgoing transitions); both rest partly on pairs with only about 30–43 observations per window. [DIR006, LAG036, LAG043, DIR004]
