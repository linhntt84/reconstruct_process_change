### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added to the process as a new, mandatory step: in the later window every trace executes A01 once, after both A02 and A04 and before A03, so the previously direct transitions A02→A03 and A04→A03 are replaced by routes through A01, while the choice between the two orderings of A02 and A04 (about 78/22 earlier, 76/24 later) is preserved.
