### Observed differences

- The branch A03 → A07 → A11 runs in fewer traces. In the earlier window A07 and A11 each appeared in about 17 in 100 traces — always together, with A07 first in about 15 in 100 — and A03 in about 11 in 100, never without A07; in the later window A07 and A11 each appear in about 12 in 100 (A07 first in about 10 in 100) and A03 in about 7 in 100, again never without A07. [OCC003, OCC007, OCC010, REL016, REL054]
- A03 is rewired at its exit: before, about half of its outgoing transitions led to A07 (52 in 100) and about a third to A01; in the later window about two thirds lead to A01 and under one fifth to A07. The one-step follow A01→A03 fell from about 41% to 3% of observed pairs, A03→A07 within one step fell from about 92% to 19% of pairs, A01 now follows A03 within one step in about 37% of pairs (before mainly at three steps), and A03 occurs at later event positions (median about 0.43 → 0.60). [DIR002, LAG002, LAG014, LAG018, POS002]
- The A08-centred cycle repeats more often: A08's occurrences per trace rose from 0.12 to 0.19 while its trace participation stayed flat at about 7 in 100, and its outgoing transitions shifted from A10 (about 58% → 38%) toward A09 (about 21% → 46%). Occurrences per trace also rose for A09 (about 0.07 → 0.23), A12 (0.03 → 0.13) and A13 (0.04 → 0.11); A12 now leads almost only to A09 and A13 almost only to A08. These occurrence figures rest on only about 4–9 traces per window. [OCC008, OCC009, OCC011, OCC012, DIR004, DIR005, DIR008, DIR009]
- A06 and A05 take part in more traces: A06 in about 12 → 17 in 100 (mean occurrences 0.15 → 0.23) and A05 in about 10 → 13 in 100. Of A01's outgoing transitions, the share to A06 rose from about 18% to 25%, while shares to A03 (7% → 2%) and A04 (31% → 27%) fell. [OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, POS003]
- A01 self-repetitions cluster at shorter distance: among A01–A01 pairs within five steps, the share at lag 2 rose from about 24% to 36%, while lags 4–5 together fell from about 35% to 18%. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

Main hypothesis: in the later window the side branch A03 → A07 → A11 is executed in fewer cases and is rewired at its exit — A03, which in both windows always co-occurs with A07, now typically leads back to A01 (about 65 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, up from about 35) instead of continuing to A07 (under 20 in 100, down from 52), while the A07 → A11 pair runs in about 12 in 100 traces instead of about 17. Supporting signs: the participation drops for A03, A07 and A11 agree with the successor-share shift at A03, with the disappearance of the A03-before-A07 ordering (about 6 in 100 traces to none) and with the collapse of the one-step A01→A03 and A03→A07 follows; the pattern is consistent across occurrence, direction, relation, position and lag indicators, and the A03 successor shift exceeds about 98 of 100 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [OCC003, OCC007, OCC010, DIR002, REL016, REL020, LAG002, LAG014, LAG018, LAG020, POS002]

What is missing: the evidence contains no screening baseline, so the flagged items cannot be checked against the number expected if nothing had changed; about 200 activity, pair and lag comparisons were made, several indicators re-describe the same traces (A07 and A11 have virtually identical profiles, and the A01–A07, A01–A11 and A07–A11 relation items repeat the same 17% → 12% co-occurrence drop), and the later-window shares rest on only 13–34 traces. An important competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is therefore sampling variability of an unchanged process, amplified by many correlated comparisons. [REL005, REL009, REL054, OCC003, DIR002]

Other differences: the A08–A09–A12–A13 cycle repeats more often per trace (A08 mean occurrences up with flat participation; A09, A12 and A13 occurrences up two- to five-fold; A08 now more often followed by A09 than by A10; A12→A09 and A13→A08 nearly exclusive), and A06 and A05 participate in more traces with A01 routing more often to A06; these are kept out of the main hypothesis because their occurrence figures rest on about 4–9 traces per window. A01 self-repetitions also cluster at lag 2 rather than lags 4–5. [OCC008, OCC009, OCC011, OCC012, DIR004, DIR005, DIR008, DIR009, OCC005, OCC006, DIR001, LAG001]
