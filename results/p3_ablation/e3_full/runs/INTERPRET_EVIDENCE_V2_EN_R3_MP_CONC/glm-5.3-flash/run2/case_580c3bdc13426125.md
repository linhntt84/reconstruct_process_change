### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 was removed from the process and A03 took over its position, with the repetition that previously sat on A02 moved onto A06. In the earlier window every trace ran A01, A04 and A05, then A02 about two times (looping back from A06 whenever that activity ran) before A07 closed the trace; in the later window every trace runs A03 exactly once in that same slot, after which A06 may repeat itself before A07 ends the trace. This rests on direct relational and positional evidence — A02 and A03 share the same mean position, every predecessor edge (from A05 and A06) and successor edge (to A06 and A07) moved from A02 to A03, all co-occurrence partners switched with it, and the lag distributions show the loop now closing on A06 itself — and it is consistent across the occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag items, each of the cited differences being larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the data. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A03 is simply A02 under a different label rather than a genuinely different activity, since only control-flow evidence is available; zero participation in a window strictly means only that the activity was not observed there. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL012, LAG007, LAG008]

Other differences: A06 participates in somewhat fewer traces (about 49 versus 59 in 100) and occurs slightly less often per trace, and the small position and lag shifts around A04, A05, A01, A06 and A07 noted above are consistent with knock-on effects of the main change but are not part of the hypothesis itself.
