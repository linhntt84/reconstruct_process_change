### Observed differences

- In the later window, A02 and A11 — which always occur together and never alone — appear in about 40 of 100 cases, up from about 30 of 100 earlier; their mean occurrences per case rose from about 0.43 to 0.59. [OCC002, OCC010, REL019]
- In the later window, A05 appears in about 28 of 100 cases, down from about 39 of 100, and it repeats less often when present (repetition in about 8 of 100 cases versus about 14 earlier); its co-occurrence with A01, with A03 and with the A04/A08/A09/A10 group each fell from about 39 to about 28 of 100 cases, and with A06 from about 24 to about 11. [OCC004, REL004, REL021, REL028, REL035, REL037]
- In the later window, directly after A01 the share of transitions to A05 fell from about 30 to about 20 in 100, while transitions to A02 and to A11 each rose from about 10–11 to about 15 in 100. [DIR001]
- The containing branch is stable: A04, A08, A09 and A10 always occur together in about half of the cases in both windows. Within that branch, cases with A02/A11 but no A05 rose from about 11 to 24 in 100, cases with A05 but no A02/A11 fell from about 20 to 12, cases with both fell from about 19 to 16, and every branch case still contains at least one of the two. [REL013, REL031]
- The choice between the strict alternatives A06 and A07 (never executed together, roughly 50/50 overall in both windows) flipped its association with the A04-group: A06 accompanies the group in about 21 of 100 cases versus about 28–29 earlier, A07 in about 30 versus about 21–22, with the reverse shift outside the group (A06-only cases about 21 to 26, A07-only about 29 to 22). [REL029, REL030, REL041, DIR003]
- Event-position distances shifted consistently with these changes: the gap from A01 to A04, A09 and A10 lengthened by roughly one step (for A01→A09, pairs five positions apart rose from about 19 to 32 in 100), and A10 is immediately followed by A04 more often (about 62 versus 53 in 100). [LAG003, LAG006, LAG007, LAG021, LAG042]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the sub-path through A02 and A11 is taken in a larger share of cases while the sub-path through A05 is taken in a smaller share, both within the branch marked by A04, A08, A09 and A10: A02/A11 rise from about 30 to about 40 of 100 cases, A05 falls from about 39 to about 28, branch cases running A02/A11 without A05 rise from about 11 to 24 in 100 while those running A05 without A02/A11 fall from about 20 to 12, and after A01 the transition to A05 drops from about 30 to about 20 in 100 while A02 and A11 each gain about 4–5 points. All involved activities remain frequent in both windows and no path disappears, so this is a shift in how often existing paths are taken, not a replacement of one path by another. [OCC002, OCC004, OCC010, DIR001, REL013, REL019, REL031]

The occurrence, directly-follows and relational indicators all point the same way, and the core differences (A02/A11 participation, A05 participation, A01's outgoing mix) exceed those of about 96 to 99.5 of 200 random re-splits, making sampling variation an unlikely full explanation for them. Limitations: the A02 and A11 items describe one joint behaviour rather than two independent confirmations, the evidence set contains no screening expectation against which the number of qualifying items can be compared, and sampling differences between two 200-case windows cannot be fully excluded. [OCC002, OCC004, OCC010, DIR001]

Other differences:
- The reversed association between the A06/A07 alternative and the A04/A08/A09/A10 group described above; this pattern is somewhat weaker (strength about 0.89) and could partly reflect sampling. [REL029, REL030, REL041, DIR003]
- After A11, transitions to A10 rose from about 15 to 25 in 100 while transitions to A04 fell from about 32 to 24. [DIR007]
- The event-position distance shifts listed above; being position-based, they are consistent with the changed path shares but do not by themselves show that activities were relocated. [LAG003, LAG006, LAG007, LAG042]
