### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the repetition structure of the process changed. In the later window, A09 repeats far more often (about 4.3 executions per trace versus about 1.9) within a cycle that A13 and A15 now feed back into almost exclusively (A13 → A09 in about 70 in 100 and A15 → A09 in about 95 in 100 of their outgoing transitions, up from about 35 and 40), while A03 and A14 no longer repeat: each occurs at most once per trace (recurrence support about 27 in 100 → 0) at essentially unchanged participation of about 50 in 100 traces, earlier in the trace, with A03 immediately followed by A14 in about 57 in 100 of ordered pairs. [OCC009, OCC003, OCC014, DIR013, DIR015, LAG098, LAG040, POS003, POS014, REL038]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent across the occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators, with the key items exceeding the differences seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled windows. The evidence cannot show that the A09 change and the A03/A14 change stem from a single underlying modification rather than two, and no screening baseline is included, so the number of items an unchanged process would produce cannot be checked; positions and lags are event indices, not clock times. [OCC009, OCC003, DIR015, POS003]

A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the later window simply contains more traces running an existing A09-heavy path several times rather than a modified cycle; participation rose only from about 62 to 68 in 100 traces, so the per-trace repetition increase is not explained by participation alone, but the evidence cannot separate the two. [OCC009]
