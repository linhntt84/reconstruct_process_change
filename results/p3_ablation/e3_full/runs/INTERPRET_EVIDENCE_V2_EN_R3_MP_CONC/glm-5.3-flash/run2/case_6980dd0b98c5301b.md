### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the order of the closing sequence changed between the windows: in the earlier window cases ended A06 → A07 → A01, while in the later window they end A01 → A06 → A07. A01 moved from the final position of the case to directly after A08 and ahead of A06; A06 and A07 each moved one position later, and A07 is now the final activity. [REL001, REL002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

The change is shown directly and consistently: both activity relations flip from 100% one order to 100% the other, the successor changes at A08, A01 and A07 agree, and all ten lag shifts match the same reordering; every one of the seventeen items rests on all 200 traces per window and its difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits. Participation is unchanged — each involved activity appears in all 200 traces of both windows at a fixed position — so this is a reordering of mandatory steps, not activities becoming optional, repeated or replaced.
