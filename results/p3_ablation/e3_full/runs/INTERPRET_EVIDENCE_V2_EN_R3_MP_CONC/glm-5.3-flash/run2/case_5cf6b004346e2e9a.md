### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the ordering around A08 was restructured: in the earlier window the typical sequence was A08 → A10 or A11 → A06 or A07, whereas in the later window A10 and A11, when executed, lead back to A08, and A08 proceeds directly to A06 or A07 in about half of its passes. The former chain thus behaves as a repeatable A08–A10/A11 stretch with a direct exit to A06/A07, which fits A08 occurring about 1.0 times per trace instead of about 0.6 at unchanged participation, and A10/A11 being skipped more often and appearing in fewer traces. [DIR007, DIR009, DIR010, OCC007, OCC009, OCC010]

- The successor changes are direct and extreme: at all three source activities the successor profile reversed, and each difference exceeds those seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the same windows. [DIR007, DIR009, DIR010]
- The transition volumes are mutually consistent: A08's about 203 outgoing transitions split into about 94 to A10/A11 and about 109 to A06/A07, matching A10 and A11's about 94 outgoing transitions, all of which return to A08; earlier, A08's about 123 matched A10+A11's about 123. [DIR007, DIR009, DIR010]
- Occurrence, position and relation indicators line up with the same rerouting rather than pointing elsewhere. [OCC007, POS007, REL051, REL052, LAG071]

Limitations: DIR profiles are shares over observed outgoing transitions, and A10 and A11 rest on moderate counts (about 55 and 39 transitions in the later window), so a rare earlier A10/A11→A08 edge cannot be excluded — the change might be a strengthening of a previously rare path rather than an entirely new one; several relation and gap items echo this same rerouting and are not independent confirmations. [DIR009, DIR010, REL051, LAG064]

Other differences: among A05's outgoing transitions, the share leading to A04 rose from about 21 to 31 in 100 while the share to A01 fell from about 79 to 69, and A04 routes relatively more to A01 (about 10 to 18 in 100); A05's higher co-occurrence with A01, A03 and A04 mirrors its higher participation rather than a new relation. [DIR004, DIR003, REL004, OCC004]
