### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, traces followed one of two shapes: about 50 in 100 ran A01 immediately followed by A02, and the other 50 in 100 ran A03, then A06, then A04, then A02. A02 appeared in every trace and was always the final step. [REL001, REL002, REL010, REL012, REL014, POS002, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- In the later window, the two shapes are: about 54 in 100 traces contain only A02 (among the observed activities), and about 46 in 100 run A03, then A06, then A04, then A05, then A02. A02 is still present in every trace. [OCC004, POS002, REL006, REL010]
- A05 was not observed at all earlier (0 of 200 traces) but appears in the later window in 92 of 200 traces (about 46 in 100), exactly once per trace where it occurs. [OCC004, POS004]
- A05 sits between A04 and A02: earlier, A04's outgoing flow went entirely to A02; in the later window it goes entirely to A05, and A05's outgoing flow goes entirely to A02. A05 co-occurs with A03, A04 and A06 each in about 46 in 100 later traces, always ordered A03/A06/A04 before A05 and A05 before A02; earlier it never shared a trace with them. [DIR002, DIR003, REL008, REL011, REL013, REL015]
- The step distance from the long-branch activities to A02 grew by exactly one step each (A04→A02 from 1 to 2, A06→A02 from 2 to 3, A03→A02 from 3 to 4), while the order among A03, A06 and A04 itself stayed the same. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL010, REL012, REL014]
- A01 ran in about 50 in 100 traces earlier (always immediately before A02, never together with A03) but in no trace later; its outgoing flow to A02 is no longer observed. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, REL002, POS001]
- The long branch's share barely moved (99 of 200 earlier vs 92 of 200 later, about 50 vs 46 in 100); a difference of this size (about 3.5 in 100) is reached by about half of random re-splits, so the choice between the two branches itself shows no reliable shift. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC005, REL010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A05 was inserted as a new step between A04 and A02 in the longer branch, so that in the later window this branch runs A03 → A06 → A04 → A05 → A02 where it previously ran A03 → A06 → A04 → A02. This rests on direct relational evidence: the redirection of A04's outgoing flow from A02 to A05, the new flow from A05 to A02, A05 co-occurring with A03, A04 and A06 in about 46 in 100 later traces always in the order A03/A06/A04 before A05 before A02, and every step distance to A02 increasing by exactly one while the order among A03, A06 and A04 stayed unchanged. All of these items show differences larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data, and participation, edge, order and distance indicators tell the same story. Limitations: only activities A01–A06 are covered, both windows are finite samples of 200 traces, and no screening baseline is included, so the family-level chance expectation could not be checked directly; a different mix of cases between the two windows cannot be ruled out from trace content alone, although the consistency across indicators makes a pure sampling artefact unlikely. [DIR002, DIR003, REL008, REL011, REL013, REL015, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, OCC004]

Other differences: A01, previously executed in about half of all traces as an alternative to the A03 branch (always immediately before A02), was not observed in the later window, and the cases that previously ran A01 → A02 now consist of A02 alone. This is a second structural difference — an activity becoming unobserved — rather than a shift in how often an existing path is taken; strictly, zero participation shows only that A01 did not appear in the later sample. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, REL002, POS001]
