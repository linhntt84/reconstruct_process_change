### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 was added to the process and placed inside the existing flow of the path group containing A03, A04, A05, A06, A10, A11, A12 and A13, most often between A11/A03/A10 on one side and A04 on the other: in the later window A11 leads to A01 in about three-quarters of its transitions (previously almost always to A04), A03 routes to A01 in about 4 of 10 transitions, and A01 in turn leads to A04 in about three-quarters of its transitions, while pairs downstream of the insertion point (A03→A04, A03→A05, A10→A12, A11→A05, A06→A11) now occur one step later than before. A01 runs in about 47 in 100 later-window traces, roughly one-third into the trace, and recurs within some traces. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR003, DIR010, DIR011, LAG007, LAG008, LAG031, LAG051, LAG054]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent across indicator families: rewired successors in the direction data, one-step distance shifts in the lag data, A01's co-occurrence pattern with the A04-side activities and its continued exclusivity with A02/A07/A08/A09, and lengthened repetition gaps consistent with A01 occasionally running between repetitions (A04→A01 occurs in about 10 in 100 of A04's transitions). A01's appearance from 0 to 94 of 200 traces is larger than in essentially all random re-splits of the pooled data (empirical strength about 0.995), and the rewiring of several predecessors points the same way. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR004, DIR011, LAG007, LAG016, REL003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether A01 existed earlier as a never-triggered path — zero participation only means it was not observed in the earlier window — and the placement is not unique, since A01 also receives from A13, A06 and A04 in a minority of transitions and sometimes repeats within a trace, so more than one insertion position may exist. [DIR001, DIR004, DIR013, OCC001]

Other differences: the share of traces taking the A02/A07/A09 path rose from about 45.5 to 53 in 100, while the alternative path containing A04, A05, A12 and A13 fell from about 54.5 to 47 in 100 — a shift in how often two existing, mutually exclusive paths are taken; the evidence cannot establish whether this is connected to the appearance of A01 or is an independent routing shift. [OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC007, OCC009, OCC012, OCC013, REL014, REL036]
