### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch via A07 is no longer executed: in the earlier window A07 ran in about 53 in 100 traces, always after A01 and A02, entered from A03 (about 39 in 100 of A03's outgoing transitions) and leading on to A06 (about 92 in 100 of A07's outgoing transitions); in the later window A07 does not appear in any of 200 traces, and A03 passes directly to A06 in about 51 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, up from about 5. The change is shown directly and consistently across all indicator families — participation (106 of 200 traces down to 0), the predecessor's redirection, co-occurrence with every partner falling to zero, and inter-activity distances shortening by one step — and a difference of this size (106 of 200 against 0 of 200) is far beyond what sampling variation produces; differences this large occurred in fewer than 1 in 100 random re-splits. The evidence shows the branch is no longer executed but cannot distinguish a genuine removal from the branch simply never being taken, and it cannot rule out that A07 still runs in a share of traces too small to surface in 200 later traces. [OCC005, DIR002, DIR004, REL005, REL018, REL033, LAG002, LAG024, LAG025]

Other differences: the reduced repetition of A03, A05, A06 and A09 (mean occurrences down about 0.15–0.20 per trace with participation roughly unchanged) is not established as part of the main hypothesis — it could be a side effect of the vanished branch or an independent change, and the evidence cannot separate the two.
