### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was introduced as a new step that now mediates the entries into the A03/A12 pair: earlier, A02, A09 and A13 (after A08) fed A03/A12 directly, whereas in the later window A02, A09 and A08 lead only to A01, A13 leads only back to A08, and A01 leads to A12/A03 with almost exactly the split A02 used to have. The placement is shown directly by A01's rise from 0 to 200 of 200 traces at an early position, by the collapsed successor profiles of A02, A09 and A08, and by lag distributions that shift by exactly one step from A02 and A09 and by two steps from A13; distances from A04 back to the pair also grew by one step, consistent with its re-entry passing A01 too, though A04's own transitions were not observed. That A03 and A12 keep their occurrence pattern and onward routing fits A01 being placed in front of the pair rather than the pair's paths being redrawn. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR006, DIR007, DIR009, LAG001, LAG043, LAG074, LAG020, OCC002, DIR003]

Two limitations remain: the earlier window contains no A01 events at all, so strictly A01 was unobserved rather than proven absent, and a renaming or recording difference for A01 cannot be excluded from this evidence alone; the evidence file also provides no screening baseline, so the comparison with chance rests on the volume and coherence of the items (dozens at empirical strength about 0.995 describing one structure), and some A13-based lag comparisons rest on only about 10–35 traces. [OCC001, LAG077, LAG078]

Other differences: A13's participation fell from about 38 to about 17 in 100 traces, A08 recurs about twice as often per trace and sits later, and A04 and A09 appear in slightly more traces (about 33% vs 39%). [OCC011, POS011, OCC006, POS006, OCC003, OCC007]
