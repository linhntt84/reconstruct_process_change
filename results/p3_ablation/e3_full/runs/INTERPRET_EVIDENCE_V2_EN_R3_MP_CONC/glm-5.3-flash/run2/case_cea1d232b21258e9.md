### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative branch of the process was restructured around the removal of A02: in the later window A02 is no longer executed, A10 has taken over its role as the core of that branch (it now always runs with A13 and is mutually exclusive with the A01/A04/A12 sequence, exactly the role A02 played before), and A03 and A14, which previously always accompanied A02, have become optional at about 18–19 in 100 traces. The transitions that used to pass through A02 were rewired accordingly: A08 now feeds A03, which leads to A14 and back to A13, while A15, A09 and A06 now lead into A10, and A10 continues to A07 and A11. [OCC002, DIR002, OCC010, REL016, REL092, REL093, OCC003, OCC014, REL038, DIR008, DIR013, DIR014, DIR009, DIR006, DIR010]

Supporting signs: A02's disappearance is observed directly (97 of 200 traces down to 0; 202 observed outgoing transitions down to 0) rather than inferred from indirect shifts; A10's rise reproduces A02's old exclusive relation with A01/A04/A12; and the participation, position and successor pictures agree across the occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators, with the key items at the top of the reference scale (about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data show smaller differences). [OCC002, DIR002, REL016, REL092, OCC010, DIR008]

Limitations: zero participation only means A02 was not observed in the later window, so the evidence cannot fully rule out that the two windows sample a different mix of cases in which the A02 path is simply not used, rather than a modified process; several items describe the same traces (for example the relation and direction items involving A02), so they are not independent confirmations; and a few rerouting details rest on few observations (for example the A06→A10 gap, 3 to 27 observed pairs). [REL016, LAG024]

Other differences: A11 and A06 occur somewhat earlier in traces and A11 slightly more often (mean occurrences 0.39 → 0.46); A04's outgoing transitions shifted further towards A01 (about 81 → 89 in 100); A15 occurs somewhat more often (about 13 → 17 in 100 traces) while A08 became rarer (about 24 → 18 in 100).
