### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that activity A01 was removed from the process — or that the paths containing it are no longer executed — with the control flow that previously ran through it rewired directly between its neighbours. Earlier, A01 was a mandatory, repeatedly executed hub (about 3.5 runs in every trace) that received control from A03 and A05 and passed it mainly to A08, A09 and A10; in the later window A01 is not observed at all, A03 and A05 now lead directly to A08, A09 and A10, and the remaining activities A05, A08, A09, A10, A11, A12 and A13 occur more often per trace at roughly unchanged participation. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, OCC003, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010]

The support is direct and consistent: the disappearance shows in four indicator families at once (occurrence, position, direction, relation), with the key items larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits; the successor redistribution in A03 and A05 matches exactly the targets A01 used to feed; and the roughly one-step-closer lag shifts fit the loss of an intermediate activity on those paths. A fall from 200 of 200 traces to 0 of 200 is far outside what random re-splitting of two 200-trace windows produces, so sampling variation alone does not explain the contrast. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG004, LAG005]

Limitations: zero participation establishes only that A01 was not observed in the later window, so a drastic reduction to a level that 200 traces would not capture cannot be distinguished from complete removal; and the increased repetitions of the remaining activities, while consistent with the same restructuring, are linked to the removal only circumstantially by this evidence. [OCC001, OCC005]

Other differences: the relative positions of A12 and A13 moved slightly earlier, routing at A03, A04, A06 and A07 stayed essentially stable, and a few lag items (for example A06→A13 and A10→A02) rest on fewer than ten paired observations and should be treated as noise. [POS011, POS012, DIR004, LAG018, LAG042]
