### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The indicators with the most unusual differences — the successor shares of A03 and A09, A07's rare transition to A05, and a few event-position gaps — rest on tens of observations each, and overall only about 14 in 100 of the 111 tested items show a difference larger than 70 in 100 of random re-splits, which is no more than an unchanged process typically produces across this many tests; sampling variation between two 200-trace windows therefore remains a full explanation.
