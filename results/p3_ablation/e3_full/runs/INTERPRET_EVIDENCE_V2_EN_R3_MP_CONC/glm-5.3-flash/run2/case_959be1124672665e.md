### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural rewiring of the control flow around the removal of A02, A03 and A08. The directional profiles are consistent with an earlier cycle A16→A01→A06→A13/A19→A03→A05/A18→A02→A10→A08→A11→A16, with A07/A14 as an exit after A01; in the later window the cycle appears to run A10→A01→A11→A16→A06→A13/A19→A05/A18→A10, with A07/A14 as exits after A16, and A09/A17 as a new, more frequent mutual handover inside the A05/A09/A17/A18 cluster. [DIR001, DIR004, DIR008, DIR009, DIR011, DIR013, DIR014, DIR015, DIR016, OCC002, OCC003, OCC008, OCC009, OCC017]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor replacements are observed in the directional profiles themselves; the disappearances are complete (0 of 200 traces, not just rarer); the order flips between A01/A11 and A01/A16 match the rewiring; and the step-distance changes agree with the removed two-activity segment almost exactly, including distances that should stay unchanged. Note that the OCC, POS, REL and LAG items largely describe this same rewiring, so they are consistent rather than independent confirmations. [REL009, REL014, LAG032, LAG073, LAG076, LAG058, LAG012, LAG088, LAG035]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different variants of an unchanged process (for example a shifted case mix) rather than a modified process — the complete disappearance of activities that earlier appeared in about 47 of 100 traces makes pure sampling noise implausible, but the window design alone cannot exclude it. Zero participation also only shows an activity was not observed in that window. In addition, no outgoing profiles for A12 and A15 appear in the directional evidence, so their connections are inferred only indirectly. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC008, DIR005, DIR010]

Other differences: A06's outgoing mix shifted slightly (A19 from about 34 to 25 in 100, A13 from about 16 to 23), A12 occurs somewhat more often (about 15 vs 12 of 100 traces, a weak signal), A19 shows slightly fewer occurrences per trace, and A09, A17, A18 and A19 appear slightly later within traces; A04 and the A11→A15/A16 split stayed essentially unchanged. These shifts are small and may be side effects of the rewiring above. [DIR005, OCC012, OCC019, POS008, POS016, POS017, POS018, OCC004, DIR010]
