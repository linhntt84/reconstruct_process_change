### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A01 was replaced by A08 in the later window: the branch that earlier ran from A17 (and occasionally from A02 and A03) through A01 and on to A09, A10 and A16 now runs through A08, with A17's successor switching completely from A01 to A08, A08's outgoing profile matching A01's earlier one in nearly the same proportions, the same frequency (about 48 in 100 traces in both windows) and a similar position in the flow. The change is shown directly by changed predecessor and successor edges and is consistent across occurrence, position, direction and relation indicators; its size (96 of 200 traces to 0 of 200) cannot be explained by finite-sample noise.
