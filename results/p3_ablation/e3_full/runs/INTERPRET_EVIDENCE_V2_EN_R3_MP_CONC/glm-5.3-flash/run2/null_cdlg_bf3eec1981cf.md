### Observed differences

- A02 occurs in every trace, and its routing changed: earlier, about 56 in 100 of its outgoing transitions led to A04 and about 44 in 100 to A03; in the later window this reversed to about 47 (A04) and 53 (A03). [DIR001]
- Trace participation moved in step: A04 appeared in about 56 in 100 traces before (112 of 200) and about 47 in 100 after (94 of 200), always exactly once when present; A03, A06, A07 and A10 each rose from about 44 in 100 traces (88 of 200) to about 53 in 100 (106 of 200). [OCC002, OCC001, OCC004, OCC005, OCC007]
- The higher occurrence counts come only from participation: A03's mean occurrences per trace rose from about 0.96 to 1.21 and A06's from about 0.95 to 1.17, while occurrences within a containing trace (about 2.2 for both) and the repetition shares stayed roughly stable. [OCC001, OCC004]
- The two activity sets behave as mutually exclusive alternatives: A04 never shares a trace with A03, A05, A06, A07, A09 or A10 in either window, whereas it co-occurs with A01, A02 and A08, which themselves appear in every trace. [REL015, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL026, REL027, REL002, REL025]
- Sub-activities of the A03-side branch rose slightly: A05 from about 27 to 29 in 100 traces and A09 from about 23 to 29 in 100; relative to the branch being taken more often, their conditional frequency within the branch stayed about the same. [OCC003, OCC006, REL016, REL020]
- A02 and A08 sit at earlier relative positions in the later window (A02 median from about two-thirds to one-third of the trace; A08 from one-third to one-sixth), which is consistent with a larger share of longer A03-branch traces rather than with either activity being moved. Beyond this, behaviour is stable: successor shares of A03, A06, A07 and A09, A10's end-of-trace position and the lag profiles show no consistent change; one apparent lag shift between A09 and A07 rests on a single observation per window. [POS001, POS006, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS008, LAG034]

### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests that in the later window, two existing alternative paths after A02 are taken with different frequencies: the single-step path through A04 dropped from about 56 to about 47 in 100 traces, while the alternative branch starting at A03 — continuing through A05 or A07 into the repeating A06/A07/A09 part with A10 — rose from about 44 to about 53 in 100. Supporting signs: the shift is directly visible in A02's outgoing transitions; A04's participation fell while A03, A06, A07 and A10 rose by the same margin; A04 remains mutually exclusive with the A03-branch activities in both windows and no activity appears, disappears or changes its order relative to the always-present A01/A02/A08, so only the shares between existing alternatives moved; and the rises in A05 and A09 plus the earlier relative positions of A02 and A08 all follow from this one difference. [DIR001, OCC002, OCC001, OCC004, OCC005, OCC007, REL015, REL023, REL027]

What keeps this at the weak level: all qualifying items express the same underlying contrast, so the many agreeing indicators are not independent confirmations; the pivotal differences sit at an empirical strength near 0.94, meaning roughly 6 in 100 random re-splits of unchanged data produce a gap this large; and no screening baseline is provided, so the number of qualifying items cannot be compared with a chance expectation. The competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is ordinary sampling variability between two 200-trace windows. [OCC002, DIR001]

Other differences: small relative-position shifts inside the A03-side branch (A03 and A05 marginally later, A06, A07 and A09 marginally earlier, each by roughly 0.01–0.03 of trace position), which may simply follow from the changed branch mix; the lag profiles otherwise show no consistent change. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS007, LAG012, LAG034]
