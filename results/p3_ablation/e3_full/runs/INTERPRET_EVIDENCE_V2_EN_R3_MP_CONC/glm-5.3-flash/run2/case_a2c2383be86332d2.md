### Observed differences

- **A07 disappeared.** In the earlier window it ran in all 200 traces (about 2.1 executions per trace, repeated in about 50 in 100 traces); in the later window it was not observed in a single trace. [OCC004, POS004]
- **The flow around A07 was rewired.** Earlier, A08 was always followed by A07, and A07's outgoing transitions led to A05 in about 52 in 100 and to A06 in about 48 in 100. In the later window, A08 leads directly to A05 (about 52 in 100) and A06 (about 48 in 100) — the same targets in nearly the same split that A07 used to feed — while A08's own frequency is unchanged (about 2.1 executions per trace in both windows). [DIR002, DIR003]
- **Step distances shrank by about one step.** The gaps from A02, A04 and A05 to A05/A06 (e.g., A02→A05, A04→A06, A05→A06) typically spanned 4 event positions earlier and 3 later. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG010, LAG011, LAG015]
- **A01 is newly observed.** Earlier it never appeared; in the later window it occurs exactly once in about 35 in 100 traces, always as the first event and always immediately followed by A03. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001]
- **A02 and A04 became less frequent:** A02 in about 48 in 100 traces earlier versus about 28 in 100 later; A04 about 53 versus about 38 in 100. [OCC002, OCC003]
- A03 and A08 appear slightly later within traces (median relative position about 0.25 → 0.33 for A03, about 0.5 → 0.67 for A08). [POS002, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A07 was dropped from the flow and bypassed: the earlier pattern, in which A08 always led to A07 and A07 then led to A05 or A06 in a roughly 52/48 split, appears in the later window as a direct A08 → A05/A06 transition with almost the same split, while A07 is no longer executed at all. [OCC004, DIR002, DIR003]

This is shown directly by the successor change at A08 and consistently across indicators: A07 fell from 200/200 to 0/200 traces; its relations with every other activity collapsed from always co-occurring to never (e.g., A06–A07 and A07–A08 went from always together to never together); and inter-event distances between upstream activities and A05/A06 shortened by about one step, as expected when one activity is cut out of a sequence. [OCC004, DIR003, REL017, REL026, REL028, LAG015]

An important competing explanation cannot be ruled out: the indicators cannot distinguish removing A07 from the process from a period in which the A07 path was simply not taken, since zero participation only means A07 was not observed in the later sample; the exact arrangement of the roughly two A07 executions per trace in the earlier window also cannot be reconstructed from this evidence. [OCC004, DIR002]

Other differences: A01 was newly observed at the start of about 35 in 100 later traces, always leading into A03, and the share of traces containing neither A02 nor A04 rose from none to about 35 in 100 — the same share as A01's participation; A02 and A04 participation fell; A03 and A08 appear slightly later within traces. [OCC001, DIR001, REL009, OCC002, OCC003, POS002, POS005]
