### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A13 took over the role A01 previously had, and A01 the role A13 previously had. In the earlier window, A01 was a frequent mid-process activity — present in about two thirds of traces, typically around 40–45% of the way through — reached from A02, A04, A07 and A15 and typically followed by A08, A15 or A07, while A13 was a rare late activity (about 23 in 100 traces, around 80% through) joined to A06 in a short loop; in the later window these roles are exchanged. The signs are direct and consistent across occurrence, position, direction, relation and lag indicators, and the rewiring is complete rather than partial: none of A01's 123 outgoing transitions in the later window still go to its former successors, and none of A13's 414 still go to A06. Limitations: each window is a sample of only 200 traces, so a small residual of the old behaviour could remain unseen, and the evidence shows the exchange of control-flow roles without indicating what produced it. A competing explanation — that the later window simply contains a different mix of process variants in which A13 fills the mid-process slot and A01 the late slot — fits less well because the same individual edges are rewired point for point, but it cannot be fully excluded.
