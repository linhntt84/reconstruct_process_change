### Observed differences

- In the later window, two activities appear that were not observed in any of the 200 earlier traces: A02 runs in about 57 in 100 traces (113 of 200) and A01 in about 15 in 100 (30 of 200) [OCC001, OCC002].
- In the later window, A02 always occupies the final position of the traces that contain it (position 1.0 as a fraction of trace length, in every case), while A16 and A19 — which in the earlier window always sat at that final position — now occur around 0.85 of the trace; their participation also slipped (A16 about 20 → 15 in 100 traces, A19 about 29 → 22 in 100) [POS002, POS015, POS018, OCC016, OCC019].
- In the later window, A02 is reached from A16 (30 traces), A19 (44 traces) and A09 (about half of A09's outgoing transitions); in the earlier window, A09 led only to A13 or A14 [DIR011, DIR014, DIR006].
- In the later window, A01's only observed outgoing edge is A01→A16, A01 always precedes A16 and A02, and A01 is fed by A17 (about half of A17's outgoing transitions) and A10 (about 4 in 10, where A16 had been the successor before); A01 occurs around seven-tenths of the way through its traces [DIR001, DIR012, DIR007, REL001, REL015, POS001].
- A02 never occurs in a trace containing A04, A06 or A12; the share of traces carrying those three activities fell from about 51 to about 44 in 100, so the complementary variant — about 49 in 100 traces earlier, ending with A16 or A19 — grew to about 57 in 100, now ending with A02 [REL021, REL023, REL029, OCC002, OCC004, OCC006, OCC012, POS015, POS018].
- A09 and A13 became less frequent (about 37 → 20 in 100 traces and about 23 → 9 in 100; A09's average occurrences per trace fell from about 1.1 to 0.4) and both now occur later in the trace (mean position about 0.26 → 0.59 and 0.28 → 0.54), while A14 occurs earlier (about 0.39 → 0.20) and A07 now leads to A14 rather than to A09 [OCC009, OCC013, POS008, POS012, POS013, DIR004].
- Several mid-to-late activities (A08, A10, A15, A17, A18, A20) now occur earlier in the trace (mean position drops of roughly 0.15–0.27); this broad shift may simply reflect the longer, restructured closing sequence rather than individual relocations [POS007, POS009, POS014, POS016, POS017, POS019].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a new closing step A02 was added to the variant that previously ended with A16 or A19: A02 now ends about 57 in 100 traces (it was unobserved before) and is reached from A16, A19 or A09, and in about 15 in 100 traces a second new activity, A01, is inserted immediately before A16, so that A10 or A17 now lead via A01 and A16 into A02 [OCC001, OCC002, POS002, POS015, POS018, DIR001, DIR011, DIR014, DIR006, DIR007, DIR012].

The signs are direct and consistent across the participation, position, directly-follows and relation indicators: A02 holds a fixed final position while A16 and A19 move off it; the observed predecessor edges of A02 (from A16, A19, A09) and of A01 (from A10, A17) line up with the two activities' trace counts; A01 always precedes A16 and A02, and A16 and A19 always precede A02; and A02 never co-occurs with A04, A06 or A12. The key differences exceed essentially all reference comparisons (empirical strength about 0.995, i.e. larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled traces), so sampling variation in an unchanged process does not plausibly explain them [OCC002, POS002, POS015, POS018, DIR006, DIR011, DIR014, REL001, REL015, REL021, REL033, REL036].

The evidence cannot rule out that A02 or A01 re-label or regroup behaviour that previously ran under other activity codes, or that a different case mix entered the later window, rather than steps being genuinely added; position indicators also measure event order within traces, not clock time [OCC001, POS002, POS015].

Other differences:

- The order of A09 and A14 swapped: before, A07 led to A09, which led to A13 or A14; in the later window, A07 leads directly to A14 (about half of A07's outgoing transitions), A14 leads to A09, and A09 leads to A02 or A13 [DIR004, DIR006, DIR009, LAG027, LAG030].
- A03's outgoing mix changed: A09 appeared as a successor (about 19 in 100 of A03's outgoing transitions) while A08 nearly disappeared from it (about 19 → 3 in 100) [DIR002].
