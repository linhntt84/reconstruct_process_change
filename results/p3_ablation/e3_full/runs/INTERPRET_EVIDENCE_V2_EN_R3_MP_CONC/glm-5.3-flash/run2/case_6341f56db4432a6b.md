### Observed differences

- The most conspicuous difference concerns A01. In the earlier window, A01 ran in about 52 in 100 traces (103 of 200) and was always the final event of those traces; in the later window it does not appear in any of the 200 traces. [OCC001, POS001]

- The end of the trace moved accordingly: earlier, A12 occurred exactly once in the same ~52 in 100 cases and every one of its 103 observed outgoing transitions led to A01, so roughly half of all cases closed with the pair A12 → A01; in the later window A12 still runs in about 51 in 100 traces but is now itself always the last event and shows no observed successor. [DIR006, POS010, REL011]

- Every activity pair involving A01 changed in the same direction: earlier, A01 co-occurred with partners such as A04, A05, A11 and A12 in about half of the traces (with A12 in 52 in 100, always after A12), whereas in the later window A01 never co-occurs with any activity and its former partners appear either alone or not at all. [REL001, REL003, REL004, REL010, REL011, REL015]

- Participation elsewhere was essentially stable: A02 (~49 in 100), A03 (~24 in 100), A12 (~51 in 100) and A16 (~49 in 100) took part at the same shares in both windows, and A02 and A16 still co-occur in about 49 in 100 traces, in both orders, in both windows. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC012, OCC016, REL029]

- Routing around the A05/A07/A13/A08 group became less fixed: earlier, A13 was always followed by A05 and A07 always by A05; in the later window A13's outgoing transitions split into about 49% to A05, 30% to A07 and 21% to A08, and A07 splits roughly 50/50 between A05 and A13. [DIR004, DIR007]

- Around A06/A14/A10 the flow concentrated on A10: earlier, A06 split its outgoing transitions about 54/46 between A14 and A10, and A14 split about 54/46 between A10 and A15; in the later window A06 always led to A10 and A14's outgoing transitions all go to A10. A10, which never repeated earlier, now occurs two or more times in about 7 in 100 traces (mean occurrences per trace 0.13 → 0.26 at unchanged participation of about 12–13 in 100), and the step distance from A06 to A10, previously 1–2 events, now spreads over 1, 3 or 5. [DIR003, DIR008, OCC010, LAG016]

- A05 repeats less: its participation stayed near 15 in 100 traces, but mean occurrences per trace fell from about 0.66 to 0.29, and its self-distance pattern spread from gaps of 2 and 4 events to gaps of 2 to 5. [OCC005, LAG008]

- Several mid-trace activities sit at later relative positions in the later window (A04, A09, A10, A11, A14, A15; e.g., A15 from about 0.36 to 0.44 and A09 from about 0.48 to 0.56 on a 0–1 scale), while A05, A07 and A13 sit somewhat earlier; A14's participation also halved, from about 13 to 7 in 100 traces. [POS003, POS004, POS005, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS011, POS012, POS013, OCC014]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the closing activity A01 is no longer executed in the later window: in the earlier window about half of the cases (103 of 200 traces) ended with A12 followed by A01, with A01 always the final event and always reached from A12, while in the later window A01 is absent from all 200 traces and those cases instead close at A12, which is now the last event whenever it runs. The change is shown directly by the occurrence, position, successor and relation indicators, and all fifteen activity pairs involving A01 move consistently from co-occurrence to absence, so a drop from 103 occurrences to none between two 200-trace windows is not plausibly a sampling fluctuation. What the evidence cannot distinguish is a complete disappearance of A01 from a fall to a rate too low to surface in 200 traces, and it cannot show why the behaviour changed. [OCC001, POS001, DIR006, POS010, REL011, REL015]

Other differences: the later window also shows less fixed routing into and within the A05/A07/A13/A08 group [DIR004, DIR007], a concentration of the A06 and A14 exits on A10 [DIR003, DIR008], new low-frequency repetitions of A10 and fewer repetitions of A05 [OCC010, OCC005, LAG008], and shifted relative positions for several activities together with a halved participation of A14, from about 13 to 7 in 100 traces [POS012, POS013, OCC014]. These secondary differences rest on roughly 10–30 observations per window and several indicators describe the same traces, so ordinary sampling variation in low-frequency paths remains a competing explanation for them that the evidence cannot fully rule out.
