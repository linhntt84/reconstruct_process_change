### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A06 is no longer executed between A05 and A04: the fragment A02 → A05 → A06 → A04 → A03, which ran in about half of the earlier traces, now runs as A02 → A05 → A04 → A03, with A05 leading directly to A04. This is shown directly by the successor change at A05 together with A06's disappearance, and corroborated consistently by the co-occurrence with A06 dropping to zero, all four inter-activity gaps shortening by exactly one step, and the one-step position shifts of A05 and A04 — the pattern expected when a single mid-sequence activity stops executing. The share of traces containing the fragment stayed about the same (about 52 in 100 before, 50–51 in 100 after), so this is not a shift in how often an existing path is taken, nor a re-routing between alternatives. [OCC006, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL004, REL007, REL009, REL010, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

Limitation: zero participation only establishes that A06 was not observed in the 200 later traces; the evidence cannot distinguish a genuine removal of A06 from A06 having become optional and simply never being taken in this sample. The change affects only the roughly half of traces containing this fragment; traces built around A01 show no change. [OCC006, OCC001]

Other differences: none beyond the small participation fluctuations noted above, which fall within the range produced by random re-splits of an unchanged process. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
