### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the routing at the entry, around the repeated centre and at the end of the case was restructured — with steps removed, added and made repetitive — rather than existing paths merely being taken with different shares. Earlier, about 64 in 100 cases passed through A01 between A12 and A09, A02 repeated about twice per case and continued to A03, A04, A06 or A16, and every case ended with A04, A10 and A17. In the later window, A01 and A17 are no longer observed; a new early step A07 feeds A03 or A04; A02 repeats about nine times per case and continues to A11, A12, A14 or A15, with A13 always returning to A02 and A14/A15 now repeating several times per case; and cases end with A04 → A10 followed by A06 and A16, each executed exactly once at the very end in either order. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC005, OCC006, OCC012, OCC013, OCC014, OCC015, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR006, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011, POS006, POS016, REL079]

Supporting signs: the participation shifts are large and well supported — A17 appears in 200 of 200 earlier cases and none later, A01 in 128 of 200 earlier and none later, A07 in 136 of 200 later cases and none earlier — and the successor, position and distance evidence agrees with them (A04 → A10 in all later continuations, A06/A16 at the end in either order, A10 immediately after A04). Differences of this size were larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data (empirical strength about 0.995 on the key items), and because activities appear or disappear and repetition counts change, the pattern points to removed, added and newly repeated steps rather than re-weighted alternatives. [OCC001, OCC006, OCC015, DIR004, POS006, POS016, LAG027]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the later window covers a different mix of cases rather than a modified process, nor can it show whether the entry-side (A01, A07), repetition (A02, A14, A15) and ending (A17, A06, A16) differences stem from one modification or several; zero participation means only that A01 and A17 were not observed in the later sample, and the position statements refer to event order, not clock time. The many agreeing items largely describe the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC006, OCC015]
