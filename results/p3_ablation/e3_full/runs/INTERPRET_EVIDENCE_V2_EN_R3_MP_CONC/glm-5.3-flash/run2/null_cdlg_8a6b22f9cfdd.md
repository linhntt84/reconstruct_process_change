### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the low-volume branch around A01 — executed together with A13 and A14 and always accompanied by A09 — is taken in a larger share of cases and repeats more within traces, and that its routing shifted: it is entered more often from A08 (A08→A01 up from about 49 to about 66 in 100 of A08's outgoing transitions, A08→A11 down) and continues more often to A09 (A14→A09 up from about 43 to about 54 in 100), with A09 and A10 also executed more often, largely in traces that also run the branch. The signs are direct and consistent across occurrence, relation, direction and lag indicators: participation and recurrence gains for A01, A13 and A14 with the group's internal structure unchanged, plus the two rewired edges. [OCC001, OCC010, OCC011, REL008, REL012, REL013, DIR004, DIR008, LAG065, REL074]

Limitations: the branch remains rare (about 1 in 5 traces), several supporting items rest on roughly 20–70 transitions or traces, and no screening baseline is provided, so an upswing of a rare variant through sampling alone cannot be excluded with certainty; for individual items, random re-splits of these windows reach differences this large in roughly 5–40 in 100 cases, and several items describe the same trace behaviour, so the judgement rests on the joint pattern rather than any single indicator. [OCC001, DIR004, LAG065]

Other differences: A03 is executed in fewer traces (about 51 vs 43 in 100) with unchanged order relations; A01 and A03 remain strict alternatives — never in the same trace in either window — so the evidence shows two shares moving without demonstrating that individual cases switched between them; A11's retreat from the branch and slight participation decline; the later median positions of A02 and A07 (event positions, so this does not by itself show the activities were moved); the consolidation of A06→A12; and small increases for A04, A06 and A12 that are weakly supported.
