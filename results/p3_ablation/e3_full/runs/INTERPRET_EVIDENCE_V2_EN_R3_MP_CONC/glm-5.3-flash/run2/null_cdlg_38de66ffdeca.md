### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the stretch containing A05, A09, A10, A11 and A12 is repeated more often per case (each of these activities roughly 13–29 in 100 more occurrences) while the stretch containing A01, A07 and A15 is repeated less often (roughly 15–21 in 100 fewer), with the first group's executions sitting relatively earlier in each case, the second group's relatively later, and cases spanning both groups becoming less common. The support is direct — occurrences per case are the measured quantity for every activity named — and consistent: every activity with a clear occurrence change moves with its group, the position items mirror the occurrence items, the co-occurrence losses sit exactly between the two groups, and the inseparable pairs A07/A15 and A02/A04 behave as units, indicating the change is organised at the level of these stretches rather than of single activities. [OCC001, OCC004, OCC006, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011, OCC013, POS001, POS005, POS008, POS009, POS011, POS012, REL004, REL006, REL014, REL020, REL028]

The evidence cannot rule out that the shift reflects a changed mix of already existing case behaviours — for example a larger share of cases repeating the A05-side stretch more times — rather than a uniform change in every case; the many pair-level relational and gap items overlap heavily and add little independent confirmation, and no screening baseline is included to check the number of qualifying items against what an unchanged process would produce. [OCC001, OCC004, OCC006, OCC013]
