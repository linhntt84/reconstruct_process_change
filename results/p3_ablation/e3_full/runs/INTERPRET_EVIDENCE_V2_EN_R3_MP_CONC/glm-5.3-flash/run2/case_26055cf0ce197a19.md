### Observed differences

- A01 became far more frequent: it appeared in about 28 in 100 traces in the earlier window and about 53 in 100 in the later window, with occurrences per trace rising from about 0.53 to about 1.10. A07 moved the opposite way, from about 51 in 100 traces to about 29 in 100, so the two activities exchanged their participation levels. [OCC001, OCC007]
- The direct flow between them reversed. Earlier, A05's outgoing transitions went almost only to A01 (about 96 in 100) and A01's almost only to A07 (about 94 in 100); in the later window A05 leads to A07 (about 91 in 100) and A07 leads almost only to A01 (about 94 in 100), while A01 now spreads to A05 (57%), A06 (18%), A04 (14%) and A03 (11%) and no longer shows transitions to A07. [DIR001, DIR004, DIR006]
- Inputs moved from A07 to A01: A03 sent about 51 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A07 earlier versus about 2 in 100 later, with about 34 in 100 now going to A01; A04 shifted from about 41 in 100 to A07 to about 39 in 100 to A01. [DIR002, DIR003]
- Immediate (distance-1) successions flipped accordingly: A01→A07 (28% of observed pairs), A05→A01 (29%) and A07→A05 (26%) disappeared, while A05→A07 (about 23%), A07→A01 (about 25%) and A01→A05 (about 20%) appeared; A03→A01 and A04→A01 moved from typical distances of 3–4 steps to 1–2 steps, and A03→A07 / A04→A07 became distant (3–5 steps). [LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG011, LAG012, LAG016, LAG017, LAG022, LAG025, LAG028]
- Pairwise relations moved the same way: A01 co-occurred with A03, A04 or A06 in about 28 in 100 traces earlier versus about 53 in 100 later, with traces containing A03 or A04 without A01 (23 in 100 each) disappearing; A01 now occurs without A07 in about 24 in 100 traces, mirroring the earlier window where A07 occurred without A01 in about 23 in 100. [REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005]
- A06 moved from the end of traces towards the middle: its mean relative position fell from about 0.96 to about 0.48. Earlier it typically ran after A07 (A07 before A06 in 23 in 100 traces) and led only to A03/A04; later it also leads back to A01 (31%) and A05 (18%), and A05→A06 now occurs at 1–2 steps in about 75 in 100 pairs versus 3 steps earlier (84%). [DIR005, LAG021, POS005, REL015]
- Largely stable: participation of A02, A03, A04, A05 and A06 changed by at most about 2–3 in 100 traces, and the A03–A04 relation (co-occurrence about 51 in 100, both orders present) stayed essentially the same. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 and A07 exchanged their places in the flow. In the earlier window the chain ran A05 → A01 → A07, with A07 closing a loop back to A05 or exiting to A06 and receiving the inputs of A03 and A04; in the later window the same chain runs A05 → A07 → A01, with A01 now closing the loop back to A05 and continuing to A03/A04/A06, and A03 and A04 feeding into A01. The exchange also appears in the occurrence profiles, which swapped between the two activities (A01 about 28→53 in 100 traces; A07 about 51→29 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, LAG006, LAG017, LAG022, LAG025, OCC001, OCC007]

The hypothesis rests on direct relational evidence — flipped successor profiles and reversed immediate successions — not on marginal frequencies alone, and the same rerouting shows up consistently across the occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag families; for the key items the differences exceed those seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. Note that several items describe the same underlying edge changes, so they corroborate one picture rather than counting as independent confirmations. [DIR004, DIR006, LAG006, LAG022, LAG025, REL005]

Limitations: the evidence shows reversed successions and rerouted edges but not a full model, so an alternative reading — for example A01 being inserted into a repeated block while A07 was rerouted onto a less frequent path, rather than a strict one-for-one swap — cannot be ruled out; POS and LAG use event positions rather than clock time, and no chance-expectation baseline was included in the evidence, so the role of chance cannot be quantified beyond the per-item comparisons. [POS001, POS006]

Other differences: A06 did not simply keep its old role alongside the exchange — it moved from a late, exit-like position (typically after A07, leading only to A03/A04) to an earlier one that also feeds back into A01 and A05, with A05→A06 shortening from about 3 steps to 1–2 steps; its earlier-window outgoing profile, however, rests on only 11 observed transitions. [DIR005, LAG021, POS005, REL013, REL015]
