### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activity A01 was not observed at all (0 of 200 traces). In the later window, A01 appears in about 119 of 200 traces (roughly 6 in 10), exactly once per trace, and early in the case: its mean position is about 0.18 of the trace, and in at least a quarter of its appearances it is the very first event. [OCC001, POS001]
- In the later window, A01 is wired into the flow around A03 and A10: A03 now leads to A01 in about 34 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions and A10 leads to A01 in about 40 in 100 (neither edge existed before), while A01 leads on to A03 (about 32 in 100), A10 (27), A04 (23) and A07 (18). Before, A03 and A10 mainly fed each other (A03→A10 about 45 in 100, A10→A03 about 55 in 100) and A04/A07. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR007]
- Before, A01 never shared a trace with any of A03–A14; in the later window it co-occurs with each of them in about 14–60 in 100 traces, running before its partner in nearly all shared traces with A04–A14, while with A03 and A10 the order is mixed (A01 before A03 in about 26 in 100 shared traces and after it in about 34 in 100; the same split for A10). [REL001, REL002, REL008, REL011]
- The appearance of A01 pushes later work to later positions: in the later window the median position of A04 moved from about 0.22 to 0.33, A08 from 0.33 to 0.44, A07 from 0.50 to 0.75 and A10 from 0 to 0.13. Step distances from A03 to its successors also grew: A03→A04 is now often three steps apart instead of one to two, and the direct A03→A10 and A10→A03 successions, previously always adjacent, now have a one-step gap in only about 71 and 64 in 100 cases respectively. [POS003, POS005, POS006, POS008, LAG001, LAG007, LAG032, LAG033]
- Smaller shifts: A13 now appears slightly less often (about 27 vs 31 of 200 traces), and A09's handover moved from A13 (about 54% to 47% of its outgoing transitions) toward A14 (about 44% to 51%). [OCC013, DIR006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new activity, A01, was added to the process and inserted into the existing flow around A03 and A10. In the later window A01 executes in about 6 in 10 cases, once per trace and early in the case; A03 and A10 — which previously fed mainly each other and A04/A07 — now hand over to A01 in roughly a third to two-fifths of their outgoing transitions, and A01 continues to A03, A10, A04 and A07. Its presence pushed A04, A07, A08, A09 and A10 to later positions and lengthened the step distance from A03/A10 to their successors, with A01 sometimes sitting between the A03↔A10 handover. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR007, REL001, REL008, POS001, POS003, POS005, POS006, POS008, LAG001, LAG007, LAG032]

The change is shown directly by relational, directional, positional and lag evidence that all describe the same behaviour, and its size (0 of 200 traces before versus about 119 of 200 after, with empirical strength near 0.995) cannot be explained as a rare variant surfacing in one window. [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, POS001, LAG007]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly shows only that A01 was not observed then, so the evidence cannot fully rule out that A01 existed before but was never sampled; and because A01 both precedes and follows A03 and A10, the data do not pin A01 to a single fixed slot in the sequence. [REL001, REL008]

Other differences: A13 occurs slightly less often and A09's handover tilts from A13 toward A14; these shifts are small and could reflect sampling variation between the windows. [OCC013, DIR006]
