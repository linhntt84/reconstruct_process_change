### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A01 and A02 each occurred exactly once in every trace and A03 about 2.1 times per trace (in 100 in 100 traces, recurring in about 56 in 100); in the later window, none of A01, A02 or A03 appears in any of the 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A04 shows the reverse pattern: it was not observed earlier, and in the later window it occurs exactly once in every trace, always at the final position of the trace. [OCC004, POS004]
- The closing sequence changed: earlier, A05 was always followed by A02 (mean position about 0.83 of the trace), which was always followed by A01 (the last event); in the later window, A05 is always followed directly by A04. [DIR001, DIR003, POS001, POS002, REL019]
- The early cycle through A03 disappeared: earlier, every A08 was followed by A03, and A03 led to A06 (about 53 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) or A07 (about 47 in 100); in the later window, A08 leads directly to A06 (about 46 in 100) or A07 (about 54 in 100), and A06, when present, now sits at the very start of the trace (median position about 0.16 down to 0). [DIR002, DIR004, POS006]
- Distances between the surviving activities shortened by one event each: A06→A07 from 3 to 2 steps, A06→A05 from 4 to 3, A08→A05 from 3 to 2, A08→A07 from 2 to 1, and the A06/A08 self-repetition gap from 3 to 2. [LAG001, LAG003, LAG005, LAG007, LAG002, LAG008]
- A07 still occurs in every trace but earlier (mean position about 0.48 → 0.30); A05's position is essentially unchanged. [POS007, POS005]
- The optional A06+A08 block (the two always occur together or not at all) appears slightly less often: about 56 in 100 traces earlier versus about 51 in 100 later. [OCC005, OCC006, REL026]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the process no longer executes A01, A02 and A03 and has a new final activity A04: the closing sequence A05 → A02 → A01 was replaced by A05 → A04, and the leading cycle through A03 (A03 → A06 → A08 → A03, with A03 alternatively leading to A07) was removed, so that A08 now feeds directly into A06 or A07 and every remaining step sits one event earlier. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG003, LAG005, LAG007]

The hypothesis is direct and consistent across indicators: participation of A01/A02/A03 falls from 100 in 100 traces to 0 in 100, and A04 rises from 0 to 100 in 100, each with full support (200 traces) in both windows; the successor changes are observed outright (A05: always A02 before, always A04 after; A08: always A03 before, A06/A07 after, with shares closely matching A03's earlier ~53/47 split); and the uniform one-step shortening of all downstream lags is exactly what removing one activity between the A06/A08 block and A07 would produce. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG003, LAG005, LAG007]

Limitations: the evidence carries no screening baseline, so the number of qualifying items cannot be formally compared against a chance expectation; zero participation strictly means only that A01–A03 were not observed in the later sample, so the evidence cannot rule out that these activities still exist but were not triggered in any of the 200 later cases. Positions and lags are event positions, not clock times, so no timing claim is made. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: the A06+A08 block occurs somewhat less often (about 56 → 51 in 100 traces, with recurrence down from about 29 to 23 in 100); this difference is modest and could reflect sampling variation between the two windows. [OCC005, OCC006, REL026]
