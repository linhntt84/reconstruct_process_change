### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 is no longer executed in the later window, consistent with the activity having been removed from the process or made unreachable. Earlier, A02 was a mandatory early step: it ran exactly once in each of the 200 traces and passed control to A01, A03, A04 or A05; in the later window it is absent from all 200 traces, its four outgoing edges are unobserved, and every monitored A02 relation changed from regular co-occurrence to the partner appearing alone. The rerouting of A01, A03 and A05 away from A02, the earlier positions of A03–A06 and the tighter one-step successions from A03 and A05 all fit the drop of one early, always-present step, and a participation fall from 100% to 0% across 200 traces per window is far larger than random re-splits of the pooled data produce. The evidence cannot rule out that A02 was renamed or that the later window consists only of traces from a variant in which A02 never runs, since zero participation strictly means only non-observation; no screening baseline is included, so the item count could not be compared with a chance expectation. [OCC001]
