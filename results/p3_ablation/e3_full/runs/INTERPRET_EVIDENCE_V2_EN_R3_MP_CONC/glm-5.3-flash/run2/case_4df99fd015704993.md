### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the routing around A03 was restructured. In the earlier window, execution reaching A03 either continued directly to A13 or went A03 → A12 → A14 and back to A03; in the later window, A02 is executed between A03 and A13 (A03 → A02 → A13), and A14 is no longer executed, with A12 leading directly back to A03. The activities involved are A02 (newly executed, between A03 and A13), A03 and A13 (now separated by A02), A14 (no longer observed) and A12 (now followed only by A03). [DIR002, DIR001, REL011, LAG003, OCC002, OCC014, DIR010, DIR011, LAG050]

Supporting signs: the change is shown directly by relational and positional evidence, not only by frequencies — A02 precedes A13 in all of their co-occurrences, the A03→A13 distance moves from lag 1 to lag 2 exactly where A02 sits, A12's outgoing transitions shift entirely from A14 to A03 at lag 1, and the self-repetition distances of A03 and A12 shorten consistently. The shifts are far larger than window-to-window sampling variation (0 vs 78 of 200 traces for A02; 51 vs 0 of 200 for A14), and the occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag items all describe one coherent pattern — though they partly reflect the same traces and are not independent confirmations. [REL011, LAG003, DIR010, LAG050, LAG001, LAG051, OCC002, OCC014]

Limitations: this evidence set provides no screening baseline, so the formal comparison against the number of items expected without a change cannot be made; the evidence also shows the reordering, not its cause. The competing reading — that A02's appearance and A14's disappearance are rare-variant sampling fluctuations — is implausible at these sizes but cannot be formally excluded without that baseline. [OCC002, OCC014]

Other differences: the broadly reduced participation described above (the A04-family activities executed in about 34 in 100 traces instead of about 50, and pairs like A03/A04 now both absent in about 28 in 100 traces) indicates the existing blocks are skipped in a larger share of traces; A01, newly observed in about 28 in 100 later traces, has a share close to that "neither" share, but with no relational or positional evidence for A01 no connection between the two can be established, so it is not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011, OCC012, OCC013, REL013]
