### Observed differences

- Activity A01 was not observed in any of the 200 earlier traces; in the later window it occurs in about 101 of 200 traces (about half), averaging roughly two executions per trace that contains it and repeating in about a quarter of those traces. [OCC001]
- In the later window, A01 sits early-to-middle in the case: its median relative event position is about 0.32 (mean about 0.38); no positions were observable before because the activity never occurred. [POS001]
- In the later window, A01's outgoing transitions go mainly to A05 (about 35 in 100), A03 (about 19), A06 (about 15) and A15 (about 14), with small shares of about 5–6 in 100 each to A16, A10 and A12; in the earlier window A01 had no observed outgoing transitions. [DIR001]
- In the later window, A01 appears as a new successor of several existing activities: about 36 in 100 of A09's outgoing transitions, 28 of A08's, 22 of A06's, 16 of A15's, 11 of A12's and 7 of A05's now lead to A01; none did in the earlier window. [DIR008, DIR007, DIR006, DIR013, DIR011, DIR005]
- Before, A01 never shared a trace with any other activity; in the later window it co-occurs with each of the 15 other activities in roughly 20–50 of 100 traces (with A04, A05 and A12 about 50, with A09 about 46, with A02 about 42), typically with A01 executed first or interleaved with the other activity. Notably, A01 co-occurs with A04 — which runs in every case — in every trace where A01 appears. [REL003, REL004, REL011, REL008, REL001, REL015]
- Distances between activity pairs mostly grew: for A02→A03 the share at gap 2 fell from about 48% to 0 while gaps 3–5 grew to about 85 in 100; for A09→A10 the share at gap 5 rose from 0 to about 43 in 100. A few pairs moved closer, e.g. A03→A02 (gap-4 share down from about 59% to 22% as gaps 1–2 grew) and A16→A02 (gap-1 share up from about 19% to 49%). [LAG001, LAG070, LAG011, LAG139]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One hypothesis supported by the evidence is that A01 became an executed step of the process in the later window, inserted into the existing flow rather than run separately: it was entirely unobserved in the earlier window, then ran in about half of later traces, placed early-to-middle in the case, receiving control from A05, A06, A08, A09, A12 and A15 and passing control mainly to A05, A03, A06 and A15. The change is shown directly and consistently across indicator families — participation from zero to about half, a positional profile appearing from nothing, a full outgoing profile, six new incoming edges, and co-occurrence with every other activity (including A04, which runs in all cases, in every trace where A01 appears) — and the widespread shift of pair distances to larger gaps is consistent with cases containing one extra step, though a position or gap shift alone does not prove an activity was moved. Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether A01 was newly added or was a modelled step that simply was not executed before, and the many relation and lag items largely reflect this same insertion rather than independent confirmations; a competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window mixes in a new kind of case that includes A01 rather than a change to one shared flow. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR008, REL003, LAG001]

Other differences: Routing shares also shifted at A03 (outgoing to A05 down from about 44% to 27%, to A02 and A12 up to about 17% each) and at A16 (outgoing to A02 up from about 9% to 43%, to A05 down from about 34% to 10%, on small support); these may be independent routing changes or side effects of A01's insertion, and the evidence cannot separate the two. [DIR003, DIR014]
