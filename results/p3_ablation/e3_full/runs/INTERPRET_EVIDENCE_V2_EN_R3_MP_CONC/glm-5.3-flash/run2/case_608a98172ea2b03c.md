### Observed differences

- **Hand-over from A01:** in the earlier window, A01's outgoing transitions went to A04 in about 50 in 100 cases, to A03 in about 29 and to A02 in about 22; in the later window, every observed A01 transition goes to A04. [DIR001]
- **Hand-over from A04:** earlier, A04 led back to A01 in about 50 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, to A02 in about 26 and to A03 in about 23; in the later window, A04 no longer leads to A01 in any observed transition and instead goes to A03 in about 53 in 100 cases or A02 in the rest. [DIR002]
- **Hand-over from A06:** earlier, A06 split its outgoing transitions roughly equally between A01 and A04 (about 50 in 100 each); in the later window it always leads to A01. [DIR003]
- **Relative order of A01 and A04:** earlier, both orders appeared — A01 before A04 in about 25 in 100 traces, A04 before A01 in about 28, and the two interleaved or repeated in about 47; in the later window, A01 precedes A04 in about 58 in 100 traces (interleaved or repeated in the remaining 42) and the reverse order is no longer observed. [REL003]
- **Step distances:** earlier, A01 reached A03 within 1 step in about 55 in 100 observed pairs (2 steps in most of the rest) and A04 reached A03 within 1 step in about 45; in the later window, A01→A03 is always 2 steps apart and A04→A03 always 1 step. A01→A04 now appears in every trace and only immediately adjacent (earlier: 144 of 200 traces, adjacent in about 30 in 100 of the observed pairs), while A04→A01 — earlier adjacent in about 31 in 100 of its observed pairs — now appears only 4 steps apart and in 84 of 200 traces. A01→A02 is now always 2 steps apart (earlier 1–2) and A04→A02 always 1 step; repeats of A01 and of A04 now sit consistently 5 steps apart, where earlier gaps of 4 and 5 mixed. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012]
- **Position within the trace:** A01 now occurs earlier (median relative position from about 0.45 to 0.0) and A04 later (mean from about 0.39 to 0.50). [POS001, POS003]
- **Participation:** A02, A05 and A06 each appear in about 42 in 100 traces in the later window, down from about 47 in 100 (84 vs 94 of 200 traces); the shift lies within the range random re-splits of an unchanged process produce (empirical strength 0.03–0.67), and the three activities still only occur together in the same traces. [OCC002, OCC005, REL008, REL015]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the hand-overs around A01 and A04 were redirected into a fixed order: in the later window, A01 always leads directly to A04, A04 no longer hands back to A01 but passes to A03 (about half of its outgoing transitions) or A02, and A06 always feeds into A01 — whereas earlier, A01 and A04 followed one another in both directions and appeared in either order, with A01 also exiting directly to A02 and A03 and A06 splitting between A01 and A04. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL003]

The change is shown directly by the successor and relation data, and each key difference is larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits; the step-distance and position shifts (A01 now at the start of traces, A04 further along, fixed 1–2 step gaps to A03 and A02) all point the same way, and the profiles rest on roughly 380 outgoing transitions per window for activities present in every trace, so the transitions that disappeared (A01→A02, A01→A03, A04→A01, A06→A04) had substantial shares before and are not small-sample artefacts. [LAG003, LAG004, LAG009, LAG011, POS001, POS003]

Limitations: the items reuse the same traces and are therefore not independent confirmations; no screening baseline is included, so the number of qualifying items cannot be compared with a chance expectation; unobserved transitions show absence from the sample, not proven removal; and the evidence cannot distinguish a redirection of the routing itself from a shift in the mix of previously existing trace variants, which would produce the same profiles. [DIR001, REL003]

Other differences: the only remaining difference is the small drop in participation of A02, A05 and A06 (about 47 to 42 in 100 traces), which the strength values place within the range of an unchanged process; it is not part of the hypothesis above. [OCC002, OCC005, POS002, POS005]
