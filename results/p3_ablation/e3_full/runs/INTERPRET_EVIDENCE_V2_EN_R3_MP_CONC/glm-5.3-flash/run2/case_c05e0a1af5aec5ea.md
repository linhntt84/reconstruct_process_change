### Observed differences

- A01 changed from an embedded, late activity to an isolated first event. Before, A01 ran in about 21% of traces (42 of 200), around two-thirds into the trace (median position 0.7), sometimes repeatedly; every trace containing A01 also contained A04, A05, A06, A09 and A13, and all 74 observed outgoing transitions of A01 went to A09. In the later window, A01 runs in about 34% of traces (67 of 200), always as the first event, exactly once per trace, with no observed outgoing transition and no co-occurrence with any of A04–A18. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001, REL006, REL010]

- In the later window, the route through A05 was replaced by a route through A08 and the previously unobserved A17. Before, A16 was always followed by A05, and A05 led to A01 in about 54% of its outgoing transitions and back to A16 in about 46%; in the later window, A16 is always followed by A08, A08 leads back to A16 in about 51% of its outgoing transitions and to A17 in about 49%, and A17 (absent before, now in about 12.5% of traces) always continues to A09. A05 itself fell from 21% to 6% of traces and now leads to A04 or A13, and A08's occurrences per trace roughly tripled (0.16 to 0.52), consistent with the new return edge. [DIR003, DIR006, DIR011, DIR012, OCC005, OCC008, OCC017, POS015]

- Feeder edges were rewired: A04→A05 (about 28% of A04's outgoing transitions) and A13→A05 (about 28%) are no longer observed, while A04→A08 (about 23%) and A13→A08 (about 24%) appeared; A09→A08 (about 43%) disappeared and A09→A05 (about 51%) appeared. [DIR002, DIR007, DIR009]

- Participation dropped broadly beyond the rerouted activities: A02 from 50% to 38% of traces, A03 from 29% to 16%, A06 from 21% to 12.5%, A09 from 21% to 12.5%, A04 and A13 from 21% to 12.5%, A12 from 10.5% to 6.5%, and A18 from 10.5% to 6%. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC006, OCC009, OCC012, OCC018]

- Co-occurrence and lag patterns follow the rewiring: pairs that previously always co-occurred (A04–A05, A05–A09, each in 21% of traces) now co-occur in about 6%, and lag distributions of rewired pairs flipped (A08→A04 from lags 1–2 to 4–5; A09→A05 from lag 4 to lag 1; A05→A04 from lags 4–5 to 1–2). [REL016, REL033, LAG014, LAG026, LAG032]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the paths through A05 and A01 were restructured: the fragment A16 → A05 → (A01 or back to A16), closed by A01 → A09, was replaced by A16 → A08 → (back to A16 or A17), closed by the newly observed A17 → A09; at the same time A01 — previously a late, sometimes repeated activity that always co-occurred with A04, A05, A06, A09 and A13 and always continued into A09 — now appears in about a third of traces as a single first event with no observed successor and no co-occurring activity. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, DIR011, DIR012, OCC001, OCC017, POS001]

The change is shown directly at the edge level on well-observed transitions (A16→A05 with 62 outgoing transitions before versus A16→A08 with 53 after; A01→A09 with 74 before versus none after; A17→A09 with 51 after versus none before), and the occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators all describe the same restructuring; the qualifying items show differences larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data, so sampling variation between two 200-trace windows does not account for them. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR011, DIR012, OCC017, LAG026]

The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows draw different case mixes — for example a new case type producing single-event A01 traces and using the A08/A17 route — which would look the same without any modification to the existing paths. Empty profiles and zero participation mean only "not observed": A01 may still have successors that were not observed, and A17 may simply have gone unobserved before. The many relation items restate the same co-occurrence change and are not independent confirmations. [DIR001, OCC017, REL001]

Other differences: the broad participation declines (A02, A03, A06, A12, A18, and also A04/A13 and A09) are not directly explained by the rerouting and may be a consequence of it or a separate change; several small positional shifts around A07, A10 and A11 showed low empirical strength and are consistent with sampling variation. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC006, OCC012, OCC018, POS005, POS008]
