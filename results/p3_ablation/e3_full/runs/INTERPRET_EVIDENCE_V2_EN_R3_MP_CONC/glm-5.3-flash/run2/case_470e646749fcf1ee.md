### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured around A04: in the later window, roughly half of the cases no longer run directly from the start activity A03 to A04 but pass through a new segment A02 → (optionally A12 and back to A02) → A13 → A04; the A08 branch, which earlier repeated A08 itself, now detours through A09 before returning to A08; and A01, which earlier ran in about 20 in 100 cases between A14 and A04, is no longer executed, with A14 leading straight to A04. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC007, OCC010, OCC011, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, DIR007, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011, REL019, REL024, POS001]

The support is direct and consistent: participation flips from zero to substantial shares and back (A01 from 40 of 200 traces to 0; A02 and A13 from 0 to 95; A09 and A12 from 0 to 51), the successor profiles of A03, A08 and A14 change exactly where the new activities sit, and the co-occurrence, position and gap evidence matches the same rewiring; these differences exceed those seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the two windows. The main competing explanation that cannot be fully excluded is sampling, since each window holds only 200 traces and a rare variant can appear in one window only — though shares of 26 to 48 in 100 (and 20 in 100 for A01) make that unlikely. Several items describe the same underlying trace changes rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence shows nothing about why the flow was rewired. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR003, DIR006, DIR011, REL019, POS002]
