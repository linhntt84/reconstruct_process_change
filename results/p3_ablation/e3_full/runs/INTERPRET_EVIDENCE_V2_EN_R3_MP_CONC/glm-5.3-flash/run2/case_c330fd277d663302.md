### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process no longer executes its closing activity A03: traces that previously ran A01/A04 → A05 → A06 → A02 → A03, with A03 always last, now end with A02, while the order and routing of the remaining activities A01, A04, A05, A06 and A02 are unchanged. [OCC001, DIR002, POS002, POS003, REL005] The support is direct and consistent: A03's participation fell from 200 in 200 traces to 0 in 200, the sole observed transition into A03 disappeared, A02 became the terminal activity, and every relational indicator involving A03 changed from always co-occurring to never co-occurring, while all indicators among the surviving activities — routing shares, co-occurrence and lag distributions — stayed within the range of random variation. [OCC001, DIR002, REL001, REL006, REL007, REL008, DIR001, LAG001] The evidence cannot distinguish a genuine removal of A03 from it being skipped in every single case, since zero participation only means A03 was not observed in the later window, and an extremely rare continued execution cannot be fully excluded. [OCC001, POS003]
