### Observed differences

- Participation and co-occurrence are essentially unchanged: A09 ran in 49% of cases before and 50% after, A05 in about 23% in both, A10 in about 26% in both, and A09 still shared cases with A04 in about half and with A05 or A10 in about a quarter in both windows. No activity was added, dropped, or became noticeably more or less frequent. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL028, REL034, REL049]

- In the later window, A09 appears later within cases: its median relative position moved from about 29% to about 43% of the case length (mean about 0.31 to 0.37), on roughly 100 cases per window — the largest and most unusual positional difference in the evidence. [POS008]

- The order between A09 and the middle activities moved in the same direction in all six pairs: A07 preceded A09 in about 6 of 10 co-occurring cases before versus 7 of 10 after, A08 in 2 of 10 versus 3 of 10, and A11 in about 3.5 of 10 versus 5 of 10; conversely, A09 preceded A03, A05 and A10 in about 7–8 of 10 such cases before versus 6–7 of 10 after. [REL021, REL034, REL043, REL046, REL049, REL050]

- Distances shifted accordingly: A09 reached the closing activity A02 within 4–5 steps in about 55 of 100 such cases versus about 73 before; early A06 reached A09 in one step in about 23 of 100 versus 38; A09 reached the late activities A08, A04 and A05 more often at short distance (e.g., immediately before A04 in about 20 of 100 versus 11). [LAG058, LAG037, LAG060, LAG061, LAG064]

- Direct successions follow the same pattern: A05 was immediately followed by A09 in about 19 of 100 of its outgoing transitions versus about 4 before, and A10 by A09 in about 21 versus 12, while A10→A04 fell from about 73 to 53; A06→A09 fell from about 17 to 10 of 100; and A09's own immediate successor shifted away from A10 (about 29 to 21 of 100). [DIR004, DIR005, DIR008, DIR009]

- A05 also tends to run earlier (median position about 57% to 43% of the case length, with its outgoing distances lengthened) and A10 later (about 43% to 57%). [POS004, POS009, LAG025, LAG027]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A09 is executed later within cases: its typical position moved from about 29% to about 43% of the case length, it preceded the middle activities A03, A05, A07, A08, A10 and A11 less often and followed them more often in every one of the six order relations, and it sits closer to the closing part of the case (A04, A08, A02). In step with this, A05 tends to run earlier and A10 later, so A09 now more often directly succeeds A05 and A10 and more often directly precedes A04. Participation and co-occurrence are unchanged throughout, so this reads as a re-sequencing inside the existing structure — not an activity becoming more or less frequent, and not a replacement of one path by another. [POS008, POS004, POS009, REL021, REL034, REL043, REL046, REL049, REL050, LAG037, LAG058, LAG060, LAG061, LAG064, DIR004, DIR005, DIR008, DIR009, OCC004, REL028]

Limitations: the shift is modest in size, and several corroborating lag profiles rest on few cases (A08→A09 on about 20–29 cases, A09→A06 on 12–16), so the finer details of the new sequencing are uncertain. Positions are event positions, not clock time, so the evidence establishes a change in sequence placement, not in when work happens on a clock. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is a shift in how often already-existing order variants were sampled between the two windows; stable participation and co-occurrence make this unlikely for the core signal, but it cannot be ruled out for the smaller components. [LAG056, LAG062, POS008, OCC004, REL028]

Other differences: A06 is more often immediately followed by A07 (about 52 → 59 of 100 such transitions) and A08 reaches the closing A02 slightly less often in one step (about 62 → 56 of 100); the positions of A07, A08 and A11 also shifted slightly earlier, and A01's median position moved later with weak support. These smaller shifts are consistent with the same re-sequencing but are too weak to fold into the main hypothesis. [LAG035, LAG051, POS001, POS006, POS007, POS010]
