### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the continuation through A04 after A03 is used less often in the later window. A04 is executed in about 30 of 100 traces versus about 38 before, and also repeats less within traces; among A03's outgoing transitions the share routed to A04 falls from about 28% to about 22% while the share routed to A05 rises from about 48% to about 53%. All activities involved (A03, A04, A05) are observed in a substantial share of traces in both windows and no edge disappears, so this reads as existing paths being taken with different frequencies rather than one path replacing another. The change is measured directly by the participation and routing indicators, and every relation item involving A04 moves in the same direction (less co-occurrence, more traces containing neither activity). [OCC003, DIR003, REL014, REL023, REL033, REL039, REL040]

Limitations: the relation and lag items are computed from the same traces as the occurrence and routing measures, so they corroborate but do not independently confirm the change; the evidence carries no screening summary, so the number of qualifying items cannot be compared against a chance expectation; and the repetition component rests on about 61 traces in the later window. Chance sampling between the two 200-trace windows therefore cannot be fully ruled out, although the uniform direction of all A04-related items makes a pure sampling artefact unlikely. [OCC003, REL039]
