### Observed differences

- In the later window, A02, A06 and A09 were executed markedly more often. A02 appeared in about 27 in 100 traces (about 54 of 200) versus about 21 before, A06 in about 23 versus about 17.5, and A09 in about 27 versus about 21. Mean occurrences per trace rose from about 0.34 to 0.59 for A02 and A09 and from about 0.36 to 0.66 for A06, and recurrence within a trace rose too (for example A06: from about 9 to about 15.5 in 100 traces). Per trace containing them, executions rose from about 1.6 to about 2.2 for A02/A09 and from about 2.0 to about 2.9 for A06. These differences exceed roughly 96–97 in 100 random re-splits. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC008]
- A second tier rose modestly: A07 (about 29.5 → 34 in 100 traces), A08 (about 22.5 → 26), A10 (about 14 → 19.5) and A11 (about 16 → 19), with moderate empirical strengths (about 0.5–0.74). A01 (in every trace) and A04 (about 48.5 → 45 in 100) were essentially stable; A05 decreased slightly (about 33.5 → 30 in 100), a weak signal. [OCC006, OCC007, OCC009, OCC010, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]
- Co-occurrence rose for the large majority of the tested activity pairs, concentrated on this group: A02 with A09 from about 21 to 27 in 100 traces, A02 with A06 and A06 with A09 each from about 15 to 22, A04 with A09 from about 21 to 27, with the "neither" share falling accordingly (A02–A09: about 79 → 73 in 100). A04 increasingly appeared together with this group while A04-only traces fell (about 27.5 → 18 in 100 for the A02–A04 pair). [REL017, REL014, REL043, REL032, REL012]
- Routing within the group shifted (shares of outgoing transitions, not of cases): from A02, the share continuing to A09 rose from about 45% to 57% while the share to A07 fell from about 36% to 21%; from A09, the share to A06 rose from about 19% to 32% while the share back to A02 fell from about 55% to 42%; from A06, the share to A02 rose from about 17% to 27%; from A11, the share to A06 rose from about 45% to 58%. Observed outgoing transitions of A02 and A09 grew from 67 to 118 each, consistent with their higher occurrence. [DIR002, DIR007, DIR005, DIR009]
- Step-distance distributions shifted in many pairs but without one consistent direction and often on few observations: A01→A03 became more often immediately consecutive (about 67% → 76% at distance 1), A09→A02 less often immediately consecutive (about 44% → 24%), and A04→A10 gaps lengthened (distance-2 share about 43% → 24%, distance-5 about 17% → 34%). The mean position of A02, A06 and A09 within traces moved slightly later (about +0.04–0.06), consistent with their higher occurrence counts rather than a relocation. [LAG003, LAG064, LAG029, POS002, POS005, POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the activities A02, A06 and A09 — together with A07, A08, A10 and A11 to a lesser degree — are executed more often and more repeatedly: more traces contain them, they recur more within traces, they increasingly appear together in the same traces, and routing between them concentrated on the sequence A02→A09→A06→A02 (A02→A09 about 45% → 57% of A02's outgoing transitions, A09→A06 about 19% → 32%, A06→A02 about 17% → 27%) at the expense of A02→A07 and A09→A02; A04 also appears increasingly embedded in this group rather than on its own. Supporting signs: occurrence, co-occurrence and routing indicators all point the same way, the key items' differences exceed roughly 90–97 in 100 random re-splits, and every activity involved was observed in both windows with dozens of traces (for example A02 in about 42 before and 54 after), so this is not a rare variant appearing in one window only. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC008, DIR002, DIR007, DIR005, REL017, REL014, REL043, REL012]

Limitations: the evidence carries no screening baseline of items expected without change, so the number of qualifying items cannot be benchmarked against chance; many co-occurrence and distance items re-express the same underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations; and the distance shifts are directionally mixed and often rest on few observations. The competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is a sampling fluctuation that happens to enlarge one correlated block of activities, although the breadth and cross-indicator consistency make plain chance unlikely. [OCC002, DIR002, REL017, LAG029]

Other differences: A01→A03 became more often directly consecutive (distance-1 share about 67% → 76%, observed in all 200 traces per window); A05 was slightly less frequent and pairs involving A04 or A05 show slightly lower co-occurrence, both weak signals. [LAG003, OCC004, REL004, OCC001, OCC003]
