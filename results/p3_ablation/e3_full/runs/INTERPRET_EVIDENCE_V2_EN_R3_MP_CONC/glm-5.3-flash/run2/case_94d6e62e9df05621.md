### Observed differences

- The clearest difference concerns A07 and A08. In the earlier window each ran in about 53 in 100 cases, and they always ran together — co-occurring in about 53 in 100 cases, never one without the other, in both orders in similar shares (about 28 vs 25 in 100), i.e. without a fixed order between them. In the later window A07 runs in about 23 in 100 cases and A08 in about 29 in 100, and the two never appear in the same case: about 23 in 100 cases have A07 only, about 29 have A08 only, and about 49 have neither. [OCC007, OCC008, REL040]
- The surrounding branch did not become rarer: A01 and A05 still run in about 51 in 100 cases (about 53 before), and the six activities of the other branch (A02, A03, A04, A06, A09, A10) all stay at about 47–49 in 100; their roughly 2-point movements are within normal sampling variation. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC009, OCC010]
- Because A07 and A08 now run rarely, A01 and A05 frequently appear without them: before, A01 always came with A07 and with A08; in the later window A01 appears without A07 in about 29 in 100 cases and without A08 in about 23 in 100, and the same holds for A05. [REL006, REL007, REL032, REL033]
- The direct hand-offs between the two are no longer observed: before, about 65 in 100 of A07's outgoing transitions led to A08 and about 61 in 100 of A08's to A07; in the later window (18 and 17 observed outgoing transitions, respectively) both lead almost exclusively to A01 (about 72 and 71 in 100). A01's own outgoing shifted from A07 (about 45 in 100) toward A08 (about 47 in 100). [DIR007, DIR008, DIR001]
- When A07 or A08 still run, they sit at the end of the case (median relative position 1.0 versus about 0.67 before), and A01's typical position moved later (median about 0.33 to 0.5), consistent with the branch usually ending after A01. [POS007, POS008, POS001]
- Pairs within the branch are seen within five steps of each other far less often (for example A01–A07: about 81 supporting pairs before versus 30 after; A05–A08: about 92 versus 52), and remaining pairs are almost always adjacent (A01–A07 lag-1 share about 51 in 100 before versus 80 in 100 after). [LAG002, LAG003, LAG020, LAG021]
- In the other branch, A02's outgoing transitions shifted from A06 (about 43 in 100) to A04 (about 49 in 100), and A09's from A04 (about 43 in 100) to A06 (about 34 in 100), the latter based on only about 35 transitions. [DIR002, DIR009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch marked by A01 and A05 — which runs in about half of the cases in both windows — changed from always executing both A07 and A08 to executing essentially exactly one of them. Before, every case in this branch ran both activities in either order with direct hand-offs between them; in the later window A07 runs in about 23 in 100 cases, A08 in about 29 in 100, never together, and the A07↔A08 hand-offs are unobserved, while A01 and A05 themselves run as often as before. [REL040, OCC007, OCC008, OCC001, OCC005, DIR007, DIR008]

Supporting signs: the participation drops of about 30 and 24 in 100 cases are larger than in 99.5 of 200 random re-splits of the same data [OCC007, OCC008]; the A07–A08 relation flips directly from "always together" to "never together" [REL040]; A01 and A05 now often appear without either activity [REL006, REL007, REL032, REL033]; the outgoing-transition profiles change accordingly [DIR007, DIR008]; and the lag and position items point the same way [LAG002, LAG003, LAG020, LAG021, POS007, POS008]. The later window is internally consistent: A07-only plus A08-only (about 23 + 29 in 100) matches the A01/A05 branch share (about 51 in 100), so nearly every branch case still executes one of the two. Note that the many relation and lag items largely re-describe this one underlying difference rather than confirming it independently. [OCC001, OCC007, OCC008, REL040]

The evidence cannot show whether the now-missing activity is skipped case-by-case as a per-case selection or whether the branch was restructured around executing only one of the two, and it cannot exclude that A07/A08 events are under-recorded in the later window; both would produce the same control-flow signature.

Other differences: Within the branch containing A02–A10, A02's outgoing transitions moved from A06 (about 43 in 100 before to about 31 in 100 after) toward A04 (about 36 to 49 in 100), with A02–A04 also more often directly adjacent (lag-1 share about 40 to 52 in 100); A09's outgoing moved from A04 toward A06 on few observations. This shift is either a second, independent routing change or a sampling fluctuation, which the evidence cannot decide. [DIR002, LAG005, LAG006, DIR009]
