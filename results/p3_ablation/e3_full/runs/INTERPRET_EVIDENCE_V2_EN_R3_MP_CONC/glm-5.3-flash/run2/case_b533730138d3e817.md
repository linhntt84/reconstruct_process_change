### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window a new final step, A01, executes exactly once at the end of every case, directly after A06 — the activity that previously closed the traces. The change is shown directly and consistently across indicator families: A01 goes from 0 of 200 traces to all 200, always at the last position; A06, previously terminal (last position, no observed outgoing transitions), now leads to A01 in all 200 traces; and A01 co-occurs with, and follows, A02 and A04–A08 in essentially every trace. The small forward shifts of A04, A05, A07 and A08 fit traces becoming one event longer. Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window only means A01 was not observed there, so an extremely rare earlier occurrence of A01 cannot be excluded; the many agreeing relation and position items largely re-express this single insertion rather than constituting independent confirmations; and no screening baseline is present to compare the number of flagged items against chance expectations.
