### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A03 is a new step in the branch containing A02, A01, A05, A06, A07, A08, A10, A11 and A12 (the alternative to the A04/A09/A13 branch): after being absent from all 200 earlier traces, it executes exactly once in about 103 of 200 later traces — matching that branch's share — and never alongside A04, A09 or A13. It is placed between the repeating A05/A07 part and the A11/A10/A06/A02/A01 tail, around the adjacent A08/A12 step: it always precedes A11, A10, A06, A02 and A01, follows A05 and A07, and sits before A08 in about 23 of 100 occurrences versus after it in about 29 (similarly split for A12). The new transitions into and out of A03 and the roughly one-position-later shift of all downstream activities, including A02 being pushed beyond five steps from A08/A12 in about 35–40 traces, are consistent with such an addition.
