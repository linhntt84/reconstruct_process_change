### Observed differences

- **New closing activity A01.** In the earlier window A01 is not observed in any of the 200 traces; in the later window it occurs exactly once in 44 of 200 traces (about 22 in 100), always at the final position of the trace and always directly after A02 (44 of A02's 208 observed outgoing transitions, about 21%). [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, REL001]

- **A06 now always continues into A15.** Earlier, A06 (27 of 200 traces) always sat at the final position of its trace with no outgoing transition observed, directly after A02, and never shared a trace with A15, which itself always directly followed A02 (26 of A02's 219 outgoing transitions). In the later window, every one of A06's 32 appearances is immediately followed by A15, the pair co-occurs in 16% of traces in the fixed order A06 then A15, A06 has moved to mid-trace (mean relative position about 0.62 instead of 1.0), and A15 typically lies two steps after A02 instead of one. [OCC014, POS004, DIR003, DIR001, REL055, LAG013]

- **The A08 → A12 block became rare.** Earlier, A08 and A12 each appeared in 35 of 200 traces (17.5%), always in the same trace, with A08 → A12 making up 84% of A08's outgoing transitions; later, each appears in only 19 of 200 traces (9.5%), joint occurrence fell to about 3.5% of traces, A08 now leads mostly to A09 (46%), A04 (27%) or A02 (23%), and A12 sits earlier in the trace (mean relative position 0.49 → 0.33). [OCC008, OCC012, REL067, DIR004, DIR008, POS010, LAG042]

- **A04 rerouted.** Earlier, about 69% of A04's outgoing transitions went to A08 (32 transitions observed); later, about 48% go to A02 and 26% to A08 (23 transitions observed — a small base). [DIR002, LAG014]

- **Smaller shifts.** A02 now appears in more traces (46.5% → 54%) with its median relative position at the very start; A07 (23% → 17%) and A03 (53.5% → 46%) participate less; A10's successor mix moved from A13 (59% → 31%) towards A14 (41% → 66%). [OCC002, POS002, OCC007, OCC003, DIR006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A possibility directly supported by the evidence is that the closing segment after A02 was restructured in the later window. A new final activity, A01, was introduced: it runs once, directly after A02, at the very end of the trace in about 22 in 100 cases — a path not observed at all earlier. Alongside it, the earlier pattern in which A02 led directly either to A15 or to a trace-ending A06 now appears as one sequence: A06 always continues into A15 (A02 → A06 → A15, about 16 in 100 cases), where earlier A06 and A15 never appeared in the same trace. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, REL001, DIR003, REL055, POS004]

The support is direct and consistent across indicator families, and the core items all exceed the differences seen in about 99 of 100 random re-splits of the pooled traces: A01's occurrence (0 → 44 traces) at a fixed last position, the newly observed A02 → A01 edge, A06's successor changing from none to A15 in all 32 appearances, A06–A15 co-occurrence rising from 0 to 16% under a fixed order, and A15's distance from A02 shifting from one to two steps, as expected if A06 was inserted before it. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR003, REL055, POS004, LAG013]

Limitations: the two new closing variants never occur in the same traces (A01 never co-occurs with A06 or A15), so the evidence cannot establish whether they were introduced together or by two independent modifications; "absent earlier" for A01 strictly means not observed in that sample, although 0 versus 44 of 200 traces leaves little room for a sampling explanation; and no screening baseline is included, so the judgement rests on the directness and consistency of the items rather than on a chance-expectation comparison. [REL004, REL013, OCC001]

Other differences: the A08 → A12 block became rare (joint occurrence 17.5% → 3.5% of traces, both activities' participation roughly halved, A08 now leading mostly to A09, A04 or A02 instead of A12), A04 rerouted from A08 to A02, and smaller participation shifts (A02 up to 54% of traces; A07 and A03 down) together with a successor shift at A10 from A13 towards A14. [OCC008, OCC012, REL067, DIR004, DIR008, DIR002, OCC002, OCC007, OCC003, DIR006]
