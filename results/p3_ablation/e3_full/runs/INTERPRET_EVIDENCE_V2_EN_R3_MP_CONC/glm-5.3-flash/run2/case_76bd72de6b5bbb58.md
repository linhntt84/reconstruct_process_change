### Observed differences

- Activity A01 is absent from the earlier window (0 of 200 traces) but appears in the later window in about 50 in 100 traces, on average once per trace, repeating within a trace in about 28 in 100 of the traces that contain it. [OCC001]
- Activity A03 shows the mirror image: it ran in about 45 in 100 traces earlier (mean 0.95 occurrences per trace) and is not observed at all in the later window. [OCC003]
- Transitions were rerouted from A03 to A01: earlier, about 52 in 100 of A09's outgoing transitions went to A03, later about 55 in 100 go to A01; A11's transitions to A03 (about 22 in 100) became transitions to A01 (about 18 in 100); smaller predecessor shares also moved from A03 to A01 (A02 about 6 to 17 in 100, A05 about 11 to 9, A07 about 8 to 10, A14 about 10 to 9). [DIR002, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR008, DIR010]

- Trace-level relations flip accordingly: the A01–A03 pair changes from "only A03 present" in about 45 in 100 traces to "only A01 present" in about 50 in 100; A01 now co-occurs with A09, A11, A05, A06, A07, A10, A12 and A15 at shares of roughly 27–50 in 100, close to A03's earlier co-occurrence shares with the same activities, which drop to zero. - Placement is preserved: A01's median relative position in the later window (about 0.38 of the trace) matches A03's earlier median (about 0.39). [POS001, POS003]
- Smaller participation and position shifts exist alongside: A05 rises from about 64 to 75 in 100 traces, A08 falls from about 29 to 22, A07 falls from about 43 to 36 and now occurs at later positions within traces (median 0 to 0.38), A13 also occurs later (0 to 0.42), A02 earlier (0.26 to 0), and A12/A15 rise slightly (28 to 35 in 100). - Some pair-level relations and step distances between other activities shift by about 5–15 percentage points (for example A05–A06 co-occurrence 31 to 41 in 100, A05 without A08 43 to 59 in 100, A10 followed immediately by A02 86 to 40 in 100), but several of these rest on only about 7–30 observations per window. [REL051, REL053, LAG029, LAG009, LAG010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A01 appears where A03 used to appear: A03 is no longer observed, while A01 occurs in about half of the traces at A03's former relative position, receives the transitions that A09, A11 and several other activities previously sent to A03, and leads on to A11, A12, A10 and A09 in almost the same shares as A03 did. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, DIR008, POS001, POS003]

The change is shown directly rather than inferred: participation, transition targets, co-occurrence and position all move together, the key profiles rest on roughly 90–200 observations each, and differences of this size are rare when the two windows are re-split at random (empirical strength about 0.995 on the core items). The evidence cannot distinguish a single substitution from two coinciding changes — A03 being removed and A01 being added at the same spot — since both produce the same traces, nor can it show whether A01 is the same activity under a new code or a different activity. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR006, REL002]

Other differences: A05 runs in more traces (about 64 to 75 in 100), A08 and A07 in fewer (about 29 to 22 and 43 to 36 in 100), A07 and A13 occur at later positions within traces and A02 at earlier ones, A12 and A15 run slightly more often, and several pair-level co-occurrence and step-distance shifts of 5–15 percentage points accompany these, some on small samples (about 7–30 observations). These are not explained by the main hypothesis; they could be side effects of the rerouting around A01 or independent changes, and the evidence cannot separate the two. [OCC005, OCC007, OCC008, OCC012, OCC015, POS002, POS005, POS010, REL051, REL053, LAG029]
