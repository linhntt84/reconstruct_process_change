### Observed differences

- In the later window, several activities run in a larger share of cases: A05 appears in about 37 in 100 traces versus about 26 in 100 earlier (mean occurrences per trace 0.39→0.58); A04 and A06 each rise from about 29 to about 34 in 100; the A01/A11 pair rises from about 65 to 69 in 100 and also repeats more often within a trace (recurrence support 0.19→0.26; mean occurrences 0.88→1.06). The A02/A07/A10 trio rises only modestly (about 28→33 in 100, a difference random re-splits match about half the time), A08/A09 stay at about 66 in 100, and A03 appears in every trace in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC007, OCC010, REL002]
- Co-occurrence shows the same pattern from a second angle: the share of traces containing both activities rises for every pairing with A05 by about 7–12 in 100 cases (e.g., A03&A05 26→37, A01&A05 24→33, A04&A05 19→27, A05&A06 19→26), with the "neither" share falling correspondingly, and the same direction holds for all pairings of A04 and of A06. Note that A01 always occurs together with A11, A02 always with A07 and A10, and A08 always with A09, so items within these groups re-express one behaviour rather than confirming it independently. [REL004, REL010, REL013, REL015, REL021, REL028, REL029, REL035, REL040, REL050]
- Routing between existing successors shifted: from A02, transitions to A07 rose from about 35% to 58% of its outgoing transitions while those to A10 fell from about 58% to 39% (a small A02→A04 share of about 4% is no longer observed); from A10 the mirror image holds (to A02 35%→50%, to A07 60%→38%). From A04, the share going to A05 rose from about 17% to 31% while that to A02 fell from 30% to 17%; from A09, the share to A03 fell from about 55% to 43% while those to A04 (13%→20%) and A05 (7%→16%) rose. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR007, DIR008]
- Pairwise event distances changed: A02→A07 became more often immediately consecutive (adjacent share 0.20→0.34 of observed successions within five steps), while A09→A03 became less often immediate (0.84→0.69, with 5-step gaps rising 0.10→0.22), A09→A11 moved from 5-step to 3–4 step gaps (0.45→0.12 and 0.24→0.42), and A10→A11 moved from 2-step to 3–4 step gaps (0.29→0.05 and 0.32→0.45); these rest on 31–66 observations each and use event positions, not clock time. - A06's median position moved later in the trace (0.33→0.50 of trace length), while A04 and A05 shifted slightly earlier (medians 0.40→0.33 and 0.50→0.47). [POS003, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, an optional part of the process is executed in a larger share of cases: A05 most clearly (about 26→37 in 100 traces; 0.39→0.58 occurrences per trace), together with A04 and A06 (about 29→34 in 100 each), which also increasingly appear together, and the A01/A11 pair (about 65→69 in 100), which additionally repeats more often within traces. All of these activities were observed in substantial shares in both windows and nothing indicates that a predecessor now bypasses them, so this reads as existing optional paths being taken more often rather than activities being added or removed. The signs are direct and consistent: occurrence increases on all components, co-occurrence rises with every partner activity for A04, A05 and A06 while "neither" falls, and the largest differences are rarely matched by random re-splits of the same data (A05 about 0.97, A01/A11 about 0.94, A04 about 0.84, A06 about 0.81). Limitations: each window holds 200 traces, the evidence does not report how many items an unchanged process would produce, and the always-co-occurring groups make many items redundant; an important competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that part of these moderate shifts reflects sampling variation. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC010, REL021, REL028, REL029, REL035]

Other differences: the order within the A02/A07/A10 trio changed — A07 follows A02 directly more often (adjacent share 0.20→0.34), and from A10 the share going to A02 rose (35%→50%) while that to A07 fell (60%→38%); from A07 the share going to A11 fell (29%→19%) with A11 less often immediately after A07 (0.42→0.26); and from A09 fewer transitions go to A03 with more to A04 and A05, consistent with the higher participation of A04/A05. These routing and spacing shifts could also be consequences of the participation changes or sampling noise. [DIR002, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG018, LAG064, LAG090]
