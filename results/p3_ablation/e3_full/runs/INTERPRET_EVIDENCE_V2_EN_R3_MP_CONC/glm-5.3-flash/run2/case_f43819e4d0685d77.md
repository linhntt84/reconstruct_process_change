### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new final step, A02, was appended after A06: cases that previously ended with A06 now end with A06 followed by exactly one execution of A02. A02 goes from absent in all 200 earlier traces to present exactly once in all 200 later traces, always at the last position and never repeated [OCC001, POS001]; it always follows A01, A03, A04 and A06, with none of which it ever co-occurred before [REL001, REL003, REL004, REL006]; and A06's outgoing flow, previously observed only towards A04, now goes exclusively to A02 [DIR005]. Limitations: with anonymous activity codes the evidence cannot show whether A02 is a genuinely new step or a renamed existing one, and since A02 was simply not observed before, a prior presence at a rate below about 1 in 200 cases cannot be ruled out; the earlier A06 routing profile also rests on only 4 observed transitions [DIR005].
