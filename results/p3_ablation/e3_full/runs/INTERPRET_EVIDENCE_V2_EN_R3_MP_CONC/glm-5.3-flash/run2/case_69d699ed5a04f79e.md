### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the rework iteration around A04 was rerouted: in the earlier window, repeated A04 executions passed through A05 (A04 → A05 → A04, with A05 in about 46 in 100 traces), while in the later window A05 is not executed at all and A04 repeats directly (A04 → A04 in about 49 in 100 of its outgoing transitions). This rests on direct edge-level evidence (A04's successor profile swaps A05 for A04 itself; A05's outgoing traffic disappears), on participation (A05 from 93 of 200 traces to none), on relations (A04 and A05 shift from interleaved repetition in about 46 in 100 traces to A04 always occurring without A05), and on the lag profile, where immediate A04 repetition is observed only in the later window; the roughly stable repetition frequency (1.86 → 1.98 occurrences per trace) fits a rerouting rather than a change in how often rework happens. [OCC003, DIR003, DIR004, POS005, REL012, LAG009, OCC002]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A05 was not observed in the later window, so complete removal and "now taken very rarely" cannot be distinguished; and the evidence contains no screening expectation, so the guard against chance findings rests on the size and coherence of the differences (many items at empirical strength ≈ 0.995 across all five indicator families) rather than on a chance-expected comparison. [OCC003, DIR004]

Other differences: A01 behaves as a newly appended final activity — unobserved earlier, executed exactly once at the end of every later trace, always after A07, whose only observed outgoing transition now leads to A01 (A07's mean position moved from 1.0 to about 0.83); an important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 existed before but was never executed in the sampled traces, since zero participation only means "not observed". The small earlier shifts of A02, A03, A04 and A06 are consistent with the shortened traces and with sampling noise. [OCC001, POS001, DIR005, REL006, POS007, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS006]
