### Observed differences

- The clearest difference concerns A09 and A11, which never occur in the same case in either window (they act as alternatives). Earlier, about 30 in 100 cases contained A09 and about 29 in 100 contained A11, leaving about 42 in 100 cases with neither; in the later window A09 appears in about 44 in 100 cases, A11 in about 57 in 100, and every case contains exactly one of the two (neither: 0 in 100). [OCC006, OCC008, REL053]
- Earlier, A09 and A11 appeared only in cases that also ran A02 and A08 — each co-occurred with A02 in essentially all of its cases. In the later window roughly half of their occurrences happen without A02/A08, and they now share cases with the A05-side activities, with which they never co-occurred before: A05 with A09 rose from 0 to about 20 in 100 cases and A05 with A11 from 0 to about 27 in 100; A04 with A09/A11 rose from 0 to about 11-13 in 100, and A07 or A10 with A09/A11 from 0 to about 7-15 in 100. [REL016, REL018, REL031, REL033, REL037, REL039, REL046, REL048, REL052, REL054]
- A09 and A11 now run earlier in the case: mean position falls from about 0.97 to 0.75 for A09 and from about 0.96 to 0.74 for A11. Distances from A01 and from A06 to A09/A11 typically shorten by one to two steps (A06 to A11 from mostly 3-4 steps to mostly 1-2), while distances from A06 to A02, A08 and A05 lengthen (A06 to A08 from mostly 1-2 steps to mostly 3-4). [POS009, POS011, LAG008, LAG010, LAG032, LAG035, LAG037, LAG038, LAG040]
- Routing into and out of these activities changed: A06 now leads to A09 or A11 in about 54 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (about 10 in 100 earlier), A08 leads to them in about 28 in 100 (about 63 in 100 earlier), A03 in about 14 in 100 (about 2 in 100 earlier), and A05 — observed on only 9 outgoing transitions earlier — now leads to A09/A11 in about three-quarters of its outgoing transitions. - Smaller shifts: cases with the A02+A08 pair (the two always occur together) fell from about 59 to 54 in 100, A05-side cases rose from about 42 to 46 in 100, A04 — which occurs only inside A05 cases — rose from about 18 to 25 in 100, and the A07+A10 pair slipped from about 24 to 22 in 100; A02 and A08 now occur at somewhat later positions (mean about 0.53 to 0.57 and 0.66 to 0.75). A01, A03 and A06 still appear in every case in both windows, and A02 and A05 remain mutually exclusive. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the either/or step between A09 and A11 — which still never run in the same case — changed from being skipped in about 42 in 100 cases to being executed in every case, and from being confined to cases that also ran A02 and A08 to running in both case families. In the later window every case executes exactly one of A09/A11 (A09 about 44 in 100, A11 about 57 in 100), the executed activity sits earlier in the case, A05 and A06 frequently lead into it, and it frequently leads on into A04, A05, A07, A08 or A10; earlier, A09/A11 ran only alongside A02+A08, near the end of the case. [OCC006, OCC008, REL053, REL016, REL018, REL037, REL039, DIR005, DIR006, DIR009, DIR011, POS009, POS011]

The signs are direct and consistent: participation and co-occurrence are computed over all 200 cases per window; the share of cases with neither A09 nor A11 falls from about 42 in 100 to 0 while their mutual exclusivity persists; about a dozen relation items show previously unseen combinations appearing at rates of roughly 7-27 in 100 cases; and the successor, position and lag distributions move in the same direction. The numbers are internally consistent: in the later window the A02+A08 cases (about 54 in 100) and the A05 cases (about 46 in 100) each split exactly into one A09 or one A11 execution. [REL012, REL016, REL018, REL031, REL033, REL037, REL039, REL046, REL048, REL052, REL054, DIR006, DIR008, LAG038, LAG040]

Limitations: the earlier-window successor profiles of A05, A09 and A11 rest on only about 7-10 outgoing transitions each, so the precise old routing shares are uncertain; the items overlap heavily (occurrence, relation, position and lag items describe the same cases), so they are not independent confirmations; and the evidence sets out no chance-expectation baseline for the number of qualifying items. An important competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is a shift in the case mix between the windows (a different population entering the later window) rather than a changed routing rule, although the appearance of A09/A11 inside A05 cases — not seen once in about 140 comparable earlier cases — is difficult to explain by sampling alone. [DIR005, DIR009, DIR011, REL037]

Other differences: the balance between the two case families moved slightly (A02+A08 cases about 59 to 54 in 100; A05 cases about 42 to 46 in 100), A04 became more frequent within A05 cases (about 18 to 25 in 100), the A07+A10 pair became slightly rarer (about 24 to 22 in 100), and A02 and A08 now occur at somewhat later positions (mean about 0.53 to 0.57 and 0.66 to 0.75). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC007, POS002, POS008, REL047]
