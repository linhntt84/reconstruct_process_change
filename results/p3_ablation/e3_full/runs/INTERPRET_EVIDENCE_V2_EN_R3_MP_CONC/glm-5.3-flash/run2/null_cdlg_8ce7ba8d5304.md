### Inference

Support level: Insufficient evidence

The evidence is insufficient to infer a change in the process. The differences are uniformly small, point in different directions across unrelated activities and pairs, and the few items with high empirical strength rest on only a few dozen traces, so they cannot be separated from the scatter that random re-splits of a stable process produce across the many units tested here.
