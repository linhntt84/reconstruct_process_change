### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A01 does not appear in any of the 200 traces; in the later window, it is executed exactly once in every trace (participation 100 in 100 cases, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, no repetitions). [OCC001]
- In the later window, A01 sits late in the case, at about 0.76 of the trace length on average (median 0.8). [POS001]
- A01 and A08 now co-occur in every trace, always with A01 first, whereas earlier A08 always appeared without A01. [REL007] A02 and A03 also now always co-occur with A01 and always precede it, and A04, A05 and A09 co-occur with it in about 47–54 in 100 traces, always before it; none of these pairs co-occurred earlier. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL008]
- Routing into the end of the process changed accordingly: A04 led mainly to A08 (about 60 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) and now leads mainly to A01 (about 88 in 100); A09 led to A08 (about 69 in 100) and now leads to A01 (about 91 in 100); A06 and A07, which never led to A01 before, now do so in about 10 in 100 of their transitions. About 73 in 100 of A01's own outgoing transitions lead to A08, the rest to A07 or A06. [DIR004, DIR008, DIR006, DIR007, DIR001]
- Every measured path into A08 became one step longer: A02→A08 from lags 4–5 to consistently 5; A03→A08 from mostly 3–4 to mostly 4–5; A04→A08 and A09→A08 from 1–2 to 2–3; A05→A08 from 2–3 to 3–4. [LAG006, LAG012, LAG016, LAG019, LAG031]
- Consistent with cases growing by one event, the other activities' relative positions moved slightly earlier: A03 from about 0.26 to 0.22, A04 from 0.65 to 0.50, A05 from 0.52 to 0.44, A09 from 0.74 to 0.62 of the trace. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS007]
- Separately, the exclusive choice between A06 and A07 tilted toward A07: A06 ran in about 55 in 100 traces before versus about 47 in 100 in the later window, A07 rose from about 45 to about 54 in 100, and the two still never occur in the same trace; A03 correspondingly routes to A06 less often (about 28% to 20% of its outgoing transitions) and to A05 more often (about 24% to 30%). [OCC004, OCC005, REL028, DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, an additional activity A01 is executed exactly once per case late in the process, between the pre-final activities and A08: cases that previously ran from late activities such as A04 or A09 directly to A08 now pass through A01 first, so A08 occurs one step later in the case. This is shown directly by A01 going from 0 of 200 traces to all 200, by its invariable precedence before A08, by the rerouting of A04 and A09 from A08 to A01, and by the one-step lengthening of all five measured paths into A08; each of these items shows a difference larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, POS001, REL007, DIR001, DIR004, DIR008, LAG006, LAG012, LAG016, LAG019, LAG031]

The surrounding structure fits: A02 and A03 always precede A01, A04, A05 and A09 precede it whenever they co-occur, and the other activities' relative positions shift earlier as cases grow by one event. In about 27 in 100 cases A01 is followed by A07 or A06 rather than immediately by A08, so its exact slot varies slightly. Limitations: the many agreeing items largely describe one underlying change rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely new activity from a re-labelled existing one, since the identifiers are anonymous codes. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL008, REL005, REL006, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS007, DIR001]

Other differences: the share of cases taking A07 rather than the mutually exclusive alternative A06 moved by roughly 8–9 in 100 (A06 about 55→47, A07 about 45→54), with A03 routing to A06 less and A05 more; this shift is modest (larger than in about 86 of 100 random re-splits), and the evidence cannot rule out that it is a sampling fluctuation between the two windows. [OCC004, OCC005, REL028, DIR003, REL016]
