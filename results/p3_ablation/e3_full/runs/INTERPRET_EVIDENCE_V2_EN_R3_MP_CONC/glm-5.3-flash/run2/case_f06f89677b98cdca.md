### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the late stage of the process was restructured around a new activity: A01, not observed in the earlier window, now executes once in about half of the cases, placed directly after A18, which follows A15, with A08 and A14 running before them as a fixed, non-interleaved sequence in which A14 newly takes part (participation about 20 → 48 in 100) and repetitions were removed. The signs are direct and consistent across indicator families: the participation jump rests on 95 of 200 traces, the A18→A01 transition carries about two thirds of A18's outgoing traffic, the relation items show A01 following A08/A14/A15/A18 in every shared case, and the lag and position items match the tightened, non-repeating run. Limitations: the evidence shows where A01 sits but not what determines whether it runs, positions are measured in event order rather than clock time, and the order of A08 versus A14 within the run remains free. [OCC001, DIR017, REL016, LAG084, OCC008, OCC014, POS001]

Other differences: A02 repeats in about a quarter of cases and occurs slightly later; A09 occurs in fewer cases at a later position and sometimes repeats, and A02–A09 and A04–A09 now often appear without each other; A19 routes more of its outgoing traffic to A07. [OCC002, OCC009, POS002, POS008, REL023, REL038, DIR018]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that some of the smaller share and position shifts reflect a changed mix of cases between the windows rather than altered routing; the emergence of A01 itself, however, at 0 versus 95 of 200 traces, is too large to be a sampling artefact. [OCC001]
