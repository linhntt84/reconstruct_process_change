### Observed differences

- **A02 is the clearest difference.** In the earlier window A02 appeared in about 27 of 100 traces (53 of 200), usually as the first activity of its traces; in the later window it appears in none of the 200 traces. None of its five observed outgoing transitions (to A07 about 42 of 100, A03 about 26, A08 about 13, A13 about 11, A05 about 8) is seen afterwards, and it no longer shares a trace with A03, A05, A07, A08 or A13, all of which still occur. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the A02-mediated path was dropped from execution and its flows shortcut. In the earlier window A02 opened about 27 of 100 traces and passed flow to A07, A03, A08, A13 and A05; in the later window A02 is unobserved in all 200 traces, its outgoing transitions are gone, it never co-occurs with its former partners, and those neighbours now connect directly (A07→A03, A07→A13, A08→A05, A13→A08, A03→A05), with A07 typically starting these traces. [OCC002, DIR002, REL011, REL012, REL014, REL015, REL019, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR009, POS005]

Supporting signs: the change is documented directly on the activity itself, on its outgoing edges and on its relations with several partners, and a drop from 53 to 0 of 200 traces is larger than all but about 1 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data, so sampling variation is not credible — A02 was far too frequent (about 27 of 100 earlier traces) to be a rare variant of a stable process. The rerouting shows up as specific new edge shares and shorter step distances while A03 and A07 continue at nearly unchanged rates (about 13 and about 24–27 of 100 traces), fitting a bypass of A02 rather than a decline of the cluster. Limitations: zero participation strictly shows only that A02 was not exercised in the later sample, and the occurrence, relation and distance items partly describe the same traces, so they corroborate rather than independently confirm the rerouting. [OCC002, DIR002, OCC003, OCC007, DIR005, DIR006, LAG009, LAG013]

Other differences: in the traces that never contained A02, the sequence around A01 reversed — before, the A06/A10/A11/A12 block ran first and reached A05 through A01, while in the later window A01 opens the traces and A11/A12 exit directly to A05; A06 and A10 also repeat less often, and small participation shifts (A04 and A09 up about 4 in 100; A05, A01, A11, A12, A07, A08 and A13 down about 2–8 in 100) accompany both restructurings. [REL001, REL004, REL008, REL009, DIR001, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG021, LAG022, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC009, OCC010]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window simply did not exercise the A02 path and the old A01-first sequencing (a different mix of cases) rather than a lasting rerouting; the windows show how execution differed, not what caused it. [OCC002, REL008]
