### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A01 was not observed at all (0 of 200 traces); in the later window it occurs exactly once in every trace (200 of 200), always at the last position of the trace. [OCC001, POS001]
- A03, which in the earlier window was the final event of every trace (position 1.0 in all 200 traces, with no outgoing transitions observed), now leads to A01 in all 200 of its observed outgoing transitions and sits just before the end (median position about 0.86 instead of 1.0). [DIR002, POS003]
- Before, A01 never co-occurred with any other activity; in the later window it co-occurs with each activity at exactly that activity's participation rate and always after it — for example with A03 in 100 in 100 cases, with A04 in about 52 in 100, with A02 in about 49 in 100, and with A06 and A11 in about 21 and 30 in 100. [REL002, REL003, REL001, REL005, REL010]
- The order among the remaining activities stayed the same (e.g., A07 before A09 in about 98–99 in 100 cases in both windows; A04 before A09 in about 52 in 100 in both), while all of them shifted slightly earlier in relative position terms (A09 mean position 0.71 → 0.61, A05 0.61 → 0.52, A04 0.54 → 0.43) — a uniform pattern consistent with one step having been appended at the end, not with the activities having been moved. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, every case ends with a single execution of A01 placed immediately after A03, the activity that previously ended every case. This rests on direct observations: A01 goes from 0 to 200 of 200 traces with exactly one occurrence each, always at the final position; A03, previously terminal with no observed outgoing transitions, now has A01 as its only observed successor (200 of 200 transitions); and A01 co-occurs with, and follows, every other activity at exactly that activity's participation rate. The unchanged pairwise order among the other activities and their uniformly slightly earlier relative positions fit an activity added at the end of the case rather than any reordering of the existing steps. [OCC001, POS001, DIR002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL043, REL028]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A01 already existed but was simply not executed or recorded in the earlier window: zero participation there only means the activity was not observed, and the anonymous codes do not allow distinguishing a genuinely new activity from a newly used one, nor saying what A01 represents. [OCC001]

Other differences: A11 now runs in about 30 in 100 cases versus about 24 in 100 before, and A06 in about 21 versus about 26 in 100 — shifts that are consistent across indicators but of a size that random re-splits of 200-trace windows also produce fairly often, so they are not firmly established; the small redistributions in transition shares and step distances around A04 and A06 likewise show only moderate reference strength and rest on few observations, and are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC006, OCC004, DIR003, DIR005, LAG013, LAG023]
