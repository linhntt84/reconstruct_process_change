### Observed differences

- Activity A01 is no longer executed: in the earlier window it appeared in every trace (200 of 200, exactly once, at the first position in the large majority of traces — median position 0), while in the later window it appears in 0 of 200 traces; the difference is larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits. [OCC001, POS001]
- Activity A04 is also no longer executed: earlier it ran in about 51 in 100 traces (102 of 200), around the middle of the trace (mean position about 0.4); in the later window it appears in 0 of 200 traces. [OCC004, POS004]
- Earlier, A01 handed over to A03 in about 46 of 100 of its observed outgoing transitions, to A04 in about 34, to A02 in about 16, and rarely to A05 or A06; A04 led mostly to A02 (about 70 in 100) and otherwise to A06 or back to A01. In the later window no outgoing transitions from A01 or A04 are observed, because neither activity occurs. [DIR001, DIR004]
- The two surviving branches became short, fixed sequences: A02 is now always followed directly by A06 (earlier about 79 of 100 of A02's outgoing transitions went to A06, with the pair two or three positions apart in about 21 of 100 cases), and A03 is now always followed directly by A05 (earlier about 95 of 100). - A02 and A03 now open their traces (median position 0, against earlier mean positions of about 0.53 and 0.44 behind A01); A05 and A06 still occur (about 54 and 47 in 100 traces) and now always close their traces, with no outgoing transitions observed at all — earlier only 6 and 14 outgoing transitions were seen across 98 and 102 occurrences. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A01 and A04 dropped out of the executed behaviour. Earlier, every trace started with A01 and then ran either the A02/A04/A06 block — typically A01 → A04 → A02 → A06, with A04 preceding A02 in about 72 of 100 joint appearances — or the A03 → A05 block; in the later window, traces consist only of A02 → A06 (about 47 in 100) or A03 → A05 (about 53 in 100), each pair strictly direct. The participation drop is observed directly (200 → 0 and 102 → 0 of 200 traces) and exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits; the occurrence, relation, direction, position and lag indicators all describe the same underlying difference, with A02 and A03 moving to the trace start and the surviving pairs becoming directly consecutive once A01 and A04 were gone. [OCC001, OCC004, POS001, POS002, DIR001, LAG001, LAG002, REL003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a removal of A01 and A04 from a shift in the case mix, in which the later window only contains cases whose paths never pass through those steps; no screening baseline is provided, so the number of qualifying items cannot be compared with a chance expectation; and the many relation items largely restate the two disappearances rather than confirming them independently. [OCC001, OCC004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]

Other differences:
- The split between the two surviving branches moved only slightly (A02/A06 from about 51 to 47 in 100 traces; A03/A05 from about 49 to 54), a difference with empirical strength around 0.53 — within the range random re-splits produce. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC005, OCC006, REL006, REL008, REL012, REL015]
- Earlier, the A02/A04/A06 block showed variable internal ordering (A04 before A02 in about 72 of 100 joint appearances, A02 before A04 in the rest, and A06 before A04 in about 11 of 200 traces); this variability is no longer observed, together with A04 itself. [REL007, REL014]
