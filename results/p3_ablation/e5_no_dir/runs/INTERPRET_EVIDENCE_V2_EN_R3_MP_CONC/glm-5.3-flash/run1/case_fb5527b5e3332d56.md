### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A22 appeared in about 52 of 100 traces (103 of 200); in the later window it appeared in about 995 of 1000 traces (199 of 200), in each case exactly once per trace. [OCC001]
- A27, A28 and A33 each appeared in about 48–49 of 100 traces (97 of 200) in the earlier window, but in only 1 of 200 traces in the later window. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- In the earlier window, A22 and each of A27, A28 and A33 were strict alternatives — they never occurred in the same trace — while A27, A28 and A33 always occurred together and in no fixed order among themselves (each pair appeared in both orders in roughly equal shares). [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- A21 and A23 occurred in every trace in both windows. Before, about half of the traces ran A21→A22→A23 (A23 two steps after A21) and about half ran A21→A27/A28/A33→A23 (A23 four steps after A21); in the later window, A22 sat between A21 and A23 in about 995 of 1000 traces, so the two-step gap now dominates. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL009, REL010, REL011, LAG001]
- A23's relative position in the trace shifted slightly earlier (mean about 0.55 to about 0.53), consistent with traces becoming shorter rather than with a reordering of activities. [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch between A21 and A23 on which A27, A28 and A33 were executed together, in varying order, is no longer taken: in the earlier window, cases were split roughly half between A21→A22→A23 and A21→A27/A28/A33→A23, while in the later window about 995 in 1000 traces run A21→A22→A23 and A27, A28 and A33 appear together in only 1 of 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL005, REL012, LAG001]

In participation terms, A27, A28 and A33 became nearly skipped (each falling from about 48 in 100 traces to 1 in 200) while A22 became executed in virtually every case (rising from about 52 in 100 to 995 in 1000). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The change is shown directly and consistently by the participation, relation and gap indicators, and the screening produced 30 qualifying items where an unchanged process would typically produce about 0.31, so the ensemble is far beyond what random re-splits of these windows produce. [OCC001, REL001, REL006, REL012, LAG001]

An important competing explanation remains: the A27/A28/A33 branch may still exist at a very low rate rather than being fully removed, since one later-window trace still contains all three activities and, with 200 traces, a branch taken in roughly 1 in 100 cases could look like this; most lag items other than LAG001 rest on that single later-window trace and add little weight. [OCC002, REL012, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]
