### Observed differences

- A19, A20 and A25 were not observed at all in the earlier window (0 of 200 traces). In the later window A19 runs in about 37 in 100 traces, A25 in about 34 in 100 and A20 in about 29 in 100, each at most once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The three are mutually exclusive in the later window: A19 and A20 never share a trace, A20 and A25 never, and A19 and A25 co-occur in only about 1 in 200 traces; in all but about two of the 200 later traces exactly one of the three executes. [REL001, REL002, REL007]

In the later window, each new activity runs after A41 and before A26 — A19 precedes A26 in all of its traces, and likewise A20 and A25 in theirs — and the A41-to-A26 distance moved from one step (about 93 in 100) to two steps (about 90 in 100). - The same pattern holds against A31: earlier the three never co-occurred with it, later each precedes A31 in exactly its participation share (about 37, 29 and 34 in 100). A26, A31 and A41 themselves still occur in every trace in both windows. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL009, REL012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a mutually exclusive choice among three previously unobserved activities — A19, A20 and A25 — executes between A41 and A26: a trace runs A41, then exactly one of the three, then A26, whereas earlier A41 led directly to A26 and none of the three ran. The support is direct and consistent: all three went from no occurrences to 29–37 in 100 traces; each follows A41 and precedes A26 and A31 in precisely the traces in which it occurs; the three practically never share a trace; and the A41-to-A26 distance shifted from one step to two. The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces (24 items against roughly 0.33 expected by chance in the control-flow family), and every item's difference exceeds about 99.5% of random re-splits of the pooled data. An important competing explanation remains: A19, A20 and A25 could have existed earlier at rates too low to appear in 200 traces, since zero participation only means "not observed"; the coordinated ordering pattern makes pure sampling noise implausible, but the evidence cannot show whether the choice itself is new or only newly frequent, nor what determines which of the three runs. Other differences: The typical distance between A31 and A35 increased from one step to two, indicating that some activity now usually executes between them; the evidence does not show which. A35 also participates slightly less often (about 54 in 100 traces earlier versus about 48 in 100 later). These differences are not explained by the insertion between A41 and A26.
