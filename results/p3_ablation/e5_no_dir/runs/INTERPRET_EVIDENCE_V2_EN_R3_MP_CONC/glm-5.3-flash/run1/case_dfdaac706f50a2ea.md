### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A27, A36, A37 and A42 each ran in every trace (200 of 200), once per case and without repetition. In the later window each runs in only about half of the traces — A27 in about 54 in 100, A36 in about 47, A37 and A42 in about 48 — still once per case when present. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A27 and each of A36, A37 and A42 switched from always running together to being mutually exclusive: their co-occurrence fell from 100% of traces to about 1% or less (at most about 2 in 200). In the later window almost every trace contains exactly one of the two — A27 without the group in about 53 in 100 traces, the group without A27 in about 47. [REL011, REL012, REL013]
- The three group activities still travel together: A36, A37 and A42 co-occur in about 47% of later traces, are jointly absent in about 52%, and are separated in at most about 1 in 200 traces; among themselves both orders occur in similar shares in both windows, so their internal order is unfixed before and after. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, each case runs either A27 or the group A36/A37/A42 at one point in the sequence, where the earlier window always ran all four in sequence. Before, a case ran A25, then A36/A37/A42 in mixed order, then A28, A26, A27 and A31; after, A25, A28 and A26 still run in every case, and the case then continues with either A27 or with A36, A37 and A42 together before A31 — the group now sits after A26 rather than between A25 and A28, and A27 and the group exclude each other per case (about 53 versus 47 in 100). The signs are direct and consistent: near-perfect anti-co-occurrence between A27 and each group activity, preserved co-occurrence inside the group, order reversals of the group against A26 and A28, participation of all four activities dropping from 100% to about 47–54%, and adjacency changes matching the relocation. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, LAG001, LAG005]

Screening makes chance an unlikely overall account: 45 control-flow items qualified where about 0.47 would be expected without change, far beyond what a stable process produces. Limitations: the many items largely describe one restructuring rather than independent confirmations; positions and lags are event positions, not clock times; the LAG items tying A25 or A28 to the group in the later window rest on a single observation each; and, since only relative order is observed, the evidence cannot distinguish the group moving later from the A28–A26 segment moving earlier past it, nor show what determines the roughly 53/47 split between the two branches. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, REL006, REL014]

Other differences: only minor position shifts accompany the restructuring — A27 slightly earlier (mean relative position about 0.62 to 0.58), A25 slightly later (about 0.34 to 0.36) and A31 slightly earlier (about 0.65 to 0.63) — consistent with traces being shorter when the group is absent, not an independent change. [POS001, POS003, POS005]
