### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities: A19 in about 32% of traces (64 of 200), A20 in about 28% (56 of 200) and A25 in about 40% (80 of 200). Each appeared once per trace when present, and no trace contained more than one of them. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006]
- In the later window, these three activities have nearly disappeared: each occurs in only 1 of 200 traces (about 0.5%), and about 99 in 100 traces contain neither activity of any pair among them. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006]
- A27, A28 and A29 occur in every trace in both windows. Before, each of them always ran alongside exactly one of the three alternatives (about 32% with A19, 28% with A20, 40% with A25), and each alternative ran after A27 and before A29, with no fixed order relative to A28. In the later window, A27, A28 and A29 occur without any of the three in 199 of 200 traces. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- The spacing between A27, A28 and A29 tightened in the later window: A27→A28 became immediately consecutive in about 98–99% of traces (before about 54%, with another 44% at distance 2), A28→A29 likewise became immediate (about 98% versus about 45% before), and A27→A29 shortened from typically three steps (about 94% before) to two steps (about 97% after). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Minor: A29's relative position within the trace shifted slightly earlier (mean about 0.56 to 0.55 of the trace length), with full support in both windows. [POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the mutually exclusive alternative step formed by A19, A20 and A25 — before, exactly one of the three was executed in every trace — is skipped in almost all cases in the later window (each observed in 1 of 200 traces), and that as a downstream effect A27, A28 and A29, between which one of these alternatives used to execute, now run back-to-back at single-step distances. The participation drop is measured directly with substantial support (64, 56 and 80 before-traces versus 1 after-trace each); the relation and lag items all describe this same underlying difference consistently rather than confirming it independently; and screening produced 22 qualifying control-flow items where about 0.31 would be expected without a change, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what random re-splits of a stable process produce. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish complete removal of the three activities from their retention as rarely chosen options, and their later-window position profiles rest on one trace each, so they carry no placement information; the reading that the removed step was interleaved between A27/A28 and A29 is inferred from the gap distributions because no direct successor evidence is available. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that an additional, unobserved change between A27/A28 and A29 contributed to the tightened spacing. [POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Other differences: A29's relative position within the trace shifted slightly earlier (mean about 0.56 to 0.55), a small shift consistent with, but not required by, the main hypothesis. [POS004]
