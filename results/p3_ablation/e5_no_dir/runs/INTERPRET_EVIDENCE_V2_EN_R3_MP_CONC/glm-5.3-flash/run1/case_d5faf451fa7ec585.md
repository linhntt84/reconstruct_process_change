### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every case executed exactly one of three alternative activities: A14 in about 32 in 100 cases (64 of 200 traces), A15 in about 28 in 100 (56 traces) and A20 in about 40 in 100 (80 traces). The three never occurred together in one case, and none of them ever repeated within a case. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006]

- In the earlier window, the alternative activity always ran after A22 and before A24, around the middle of the trace (mean position about 0.51–0.52), and its position relative to A23 varied: for example, A14 came before A23 in about 14 in 100 cases and after it in about 19 in 100. - The remaining segment compacted: before, A22 and A23 were one step apart in about 55 in 100 cases and two steps apart in about 45 in 100, A23 and A24 were one step apart in about 45 in 100 and two steps in about 55 in 100, and A22 to A24 was always three steps; in the later window, A22, A23 and A24 are directly consecutive (a gap of one step in 100 in 100 cases for both adjacent pairs), and A22 to A24 is always two steps. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative step formed by A14, A15 and A20 — previously executed exactly once per case, always between A22 and A24 and on either side of A23 — is no longer executed in the later window, so that every case now runs A22, A23 and A24 as directly consecutive steps. The participation drop is direct and large (from 56–80 of 200 traces to 0 of 200 for each of the three), the relational and distance evidence shows the resulting compaction completely (mixed one-or-two-step gaps becoming fixed one-step gaps, and the A22–A24 distance shrinking from always three to always two steps), and the small opposite position shifts of A22 and A24 fit the same picture. Chance is an unlikely explanation for the evidence as a whole: 23 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.25 would be expected without a change, and each item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: zero participation means only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed in the later window, and the agreeing indicators largely reflect the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the dropped step continues under a different activity label, since no qualifying item shows any activity increasing in participation that would indicate a replacement.
