### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the always-executed three-activity block A26–A27–A28 was replaced by a single per-trace choice among the alternatives A19, A20 and A21, at the same stage between A44 and A29. Before, every trace contained A26, A27 and A28 once each; in the later window every trace contains exactly one of A19/A20/A21 (about 30/33/38 of 100, never together), each bracketed by A44 before and A29 after in nearly all of its occurrences, at the same relative trace position (median about 0.36 versus 0.37), and the A44→A29 distance shortened from 4–5 steps to 2–3 steps, consistent with three consecutive activities giving way to one. The pattern is direct (participation plus relational evidence), consistent across the items, and the screening produced 74 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected without change, far beyond what random window splits produce. The evidence cannot distinguish a modification of the process from a shift in the mix of pre-existing execution variants, and the earlier-window behaviour of A19/A20/A21 is documented by only 2–4 traces each.
