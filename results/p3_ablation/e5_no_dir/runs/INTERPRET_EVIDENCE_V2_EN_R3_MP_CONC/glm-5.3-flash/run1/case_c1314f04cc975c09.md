### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the activities A25, A26 and A31 each occurred exactly once in every trace (participation 100 in 100, no repeats). In the later window, all three still appear in every trace, but each occurs about 3.5 times per trace on average, and in about 68 in 100 traces each of them occurs more than once. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The relationship between A25 and A31 changed: earlier, the two always co-occurred and A25 came strictly before A31 in 100 in 100 traces; in the later window that strict order holds in only about 32 in 100 traces, while in about 68 in 100 traces the two are interleaved or repeated without a fixed order. [REL001]
- The tight spacing between the two dissolved: earlier, A31 followed A25 exactly two event positions later in every trace; in the later window only about 12 in 100 observed pairs still show that two-step gap, with about 5 in 100 at a five-step gap. [LAG001]
- The relative positions of A25 and A31 became more variable rather than shifted: the mean positions stayed nearly the same (A25 about 0.41 to 0.44; A31 about 0.48 to 0.48), while the spread roughly doubled (standard deviation from about 0.06 to about 0.12 for both). [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the activities A25, A26 and A31, previously executed exactly once per trace in a fixed sequence with A25 before A31, became repeatable: in the later window each occurs about 3.5 times per trace, repeats in roughly 68 in 100 traces, and the strict A25-before-A31 ordering gave way to interleaved or repeated execution in the same roughly 68 in 100 traces. The repetition increase and the ordering breakdown are each shown directly by their own indicators, they agree in magnitude (both about 0.68), and the position and lag changes are consistent with repetitions rather than a relocation of the activities. Seven control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.17 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by chance sampling of rare variants, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, LAG001, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: the occurrence, position, relation and lag items here largely describe the same underlying trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations of one another. The evidence also cannot show whether the three activities repeat together as one repeated group or repeat independently at similar rates, because relations between A25 and A26 or between A26 and A31 were not observed, and no transition evidence shows how the repetitions are entered or exited. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001]
