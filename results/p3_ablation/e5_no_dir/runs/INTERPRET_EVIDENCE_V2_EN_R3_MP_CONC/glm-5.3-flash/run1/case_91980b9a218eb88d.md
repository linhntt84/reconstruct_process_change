### Observed differences

- The order between A26 and each of the three alternative activities A19, A20 and A25 reversed. In the earlier window A19 ran before A26 in about 36 of 100 cases, A20 in about 24 and A25 in about 38, with the reverse order almost never seen; in the later window A26 ran first — before A19 in about 31 of 100 cases, before A20 in about 37 and before A25 in about 29 — while the old order fell to about 0–1 in 100. - A24 was observed in about 43 of 100 cases in the earlier window and about 58 of 100 in the later one. [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the placement of the mutually exclusive activities A19, A20 and A25 relative to A26 changed: in the earlier window each case ran exactly one of them shortly before A26, about a third into the case, while in the later window A26 — and A27 and A28 — run first and the single alternative follows around 60% through the case, typically one to two steps after A27 or A28 and with a less fixed position relative to A28. A45 now leads into A26 in one step instead of two, consistent with the alternative activity no longer sitting between A45 and A26. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG003, LAG004, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011]

Supporting signs: the reversal is shown directly by the relation indicators for all three alternatives against both A26 and A27; the positional shift is confined to A19, A20 and A25 while A26 and A27 stay in place; and new close successions from A27/A28 into A19/A20 appear exactly where the old order disappeared. With 25 qualifying items against roughly 0.36 expected in an unchanged process, a chance artefact is implausible for the ensemble, though most items re-describe one underlying move rather than confirming it independently. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG003, LAG004, LAG007, LAG008]

Limitations: the evidence cannot pin down the alternatives' exact new position relative to A28 and A32, and some after-window lag profiles rest on only one or two observations. An important competing explanation it cannot rule out is that the apparent shift in which alternative is chosen (A20 up from about 25 to 38 in 100 cases, A25 down from about 38 to 30) is sampling variation between the two 200-case windows rather than a routing change. [REL005, REL008, LAG001, LAG010, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: A24's participation increased from about 43 to about 58 of 100 cases; no relational or positional item connects this to the placement change above. [OCC001]
