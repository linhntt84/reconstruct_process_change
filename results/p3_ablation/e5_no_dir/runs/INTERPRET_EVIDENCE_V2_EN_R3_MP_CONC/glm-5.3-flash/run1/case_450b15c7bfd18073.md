### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new alternative segment now runs between A21 and A23: in the later window, roughly half of the cases no longer execute A22 and instead perform A27, A28 and A33 — all three together, in no fixed order — between A21 and A23, while the other half still follow the earlier sequence A21 → A22 → A23 [OCC001, OCC002, REL006, REL009, LAG001].

The support is direct and consistent: relational evidence places the trio after A21 and before A23 and shows it never co-occurs with A22; occurrence and position evidence shows A22 falling from every trace to about 51 in 100 while the trio rises from unobserved to about 49 in 100 in the same trace region; and the A21→A23 distance grows from two to four positions in exactly the share of traces that gained the trio. With 22 qualifying items against roughly 0.26 expected by chance for the 52 tested units, and every item's difference exceeding about 995 in 1000 random re-splits, chance sampling cannot plausibly explain the pattern as a whole [OCC001, POS001, REL002, REL005, REL012, LAG001].

Limitations: the absence of A27, A28 and A33 in the earlier window only shows they were not observed then, and the evidence cannot rule out that the later window mixes two case populations — one following the old sequence and one the new — rather than a single process with a new alternative; it also cannot show whether the trio's varying internal order reflects parallel execution or merely an unordered sequence [OCC002, REL012].
