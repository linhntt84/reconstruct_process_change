### Observed differences

- The activities A14, A15 and A20 each ran in about 47 in 100 cases in the earlier window (94 of 200 traces) but in essentially every case in the later window (199 of 200); their mean occurrences per trace rose from about 0.47 to about 1.0, with no repetition in either window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The three activities always appear together and in the fixed order A14 → A20 → A15 in both windows; earlier they co-occurred in about 47 in 100 cases and were jointly absent in the remaining 53, while in the later window they co-occur in about 995 in 1000 cases. [REL001, REL002, REL007]
- The group's surroundings kept the same relative order — A36 before the group and the group before A21 in both windows — but the step distance from A36 to A21 changed: earlier it was one step in about 53 in 100 cases and four steps in about 47 in 100, while in the later window it is four steps in about 995 in 1000 cases, i.e. the direct A36 → A21 transition without the group in between has practically disappeared. [REL004, REL009, REL003, REL008, LAG001]
- A21's average position shifted slightly later (mean normalized position about 0.41 to 0.43) and A36's slightly earlier (about 0.33 to 0.30), the pattern expected when additional activities regularly execute between them. [POS001, POS002]
- A40's overall frequency barely moved (present in about 60 in 100 earlier cases and about 62 in 100 later), but it no longer occurs without the group: earlier about 33 in 100 cases had A40 without A14/A15/A20, in the later window essentially none. [REL006, REL015]
- A37 is present in every case in both windows and now always co-occurs with the group (co-occurrence about 47 in 100 earlier versus about 995 in 1000 later), with mixed ordering in the later window (A14 before A37 in about 54 in 100 cases, interleaved or repeated in about 45 in 100). [REL005, REL014]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the sequential group A14 → A20 → A15, which sits between A36 and A21, changed from optional to executed in essentially every case: in the earlier window about half of the cases skipped the group and ran A36 directly before A21, while in the later window the group runs between them in virtually all cases. This rests on direct and mutually consistent signs — the participation of all three activities rising from about 47 in 100 to about 995 in 1000 cases, the disappearance of the one-step A36 → A21 gap, unchanged relative ordering around the group, and small opposite position shifts of A36 and A21. Twenty-one control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.28 expected if nothing had changed, so the volume of evidence cannot be explained by sampling noise alone [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL007, LAG001]. The main limitation is that the evidence cannot rule out a case-mix explanation: the later window may simply contain a different composition of cases in which the group was always executed, rather than a change in the execution rule itself.

Other differences: A40 now appears only alongside the group although its overall frequency is roughly unchanged, and A37, present throughout, now co-occurs with the group in every case with mixed ordering; both follow from the group's near-universal presence in the later window.
