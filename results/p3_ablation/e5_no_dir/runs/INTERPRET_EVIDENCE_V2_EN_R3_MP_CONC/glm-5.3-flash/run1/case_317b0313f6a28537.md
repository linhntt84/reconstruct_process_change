### Observed differences

- The activities A19, A20 and A25 are executed much earlier in the later window: their mean position moved from about 0.60 of the trace to about 0.32–0.34 (a shift of roughly 0.28), while their participation stayed nearly flat (A19 in 39% vs 36% of traces, A20 32% vs 35%, A25 31% vs 32%). They occur at similar rates — they just occur earlier. [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- Their order relative to A26 and A27 reversed completely. In the earlier window, whenever A19, A20 or A25 co-occurred with A26 or A27 (about 31–39 in 100 traces), they came after them in essentially all such traces (31–38 in 100) and never before. In the later window they come before A26/A27 in about 30–36 in 100 traces, and the old order survives in at most about 1 in 200. Co-occurrence levels are unchanged, so this is a reordering, not a change in how often the activities run together. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- A26, A27 and A28 themselves barely moved (mean positions about 0.33→0.36, 0.56→0.59 and 0.61→0.62), so the displacement is carried by the A19/A20/A25 group, not by the reference activities. [POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Local distances changed consistently with this: A27→A28, previously split between distances 1 and 2 (about 48/50 in 100), is now almost always directly consecutive (98 in 100 at distance 1); A28→A32 likewise became almost always adjacent (96 in 100, previously split about 51/48); A27→A32 moved one step closer (distance 3 in 96 in 100 before, distance 2 in 95 in 100 after); and the gap from A45 to A26 widened from one step in 86 in 100 traces to two–three steps in about 98 in 100, as if activity now lies between them. [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]
- The moved activities also lost their close proximity to A28 and A32: A19 followed by A32 within five steps appears in 76 traces before but only 1 after; A25→A28 falls from 33 to 1 and A25→A32 from 61 to 3. These after-window profiles rest on only 1–3 traces, so the exact new distances are weakly supported. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- A15 repeats less often: about 1.58 vs 1.89 occurrences per trace on average, with the share of traces in which it recurs falling from about 47 in 100 to 38 in 100, while it still appears in every trace in both windows. [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening tested 70 control-flow units and 20 items qualified, against fewer than one (0.35) expected by chance in a stable process, so this set of differences as a whole cannot be explained by random re-sampling of the two windows. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the group of activities A19, A20 and A25 is executed earlier in the case — before A26 and A27 instead of after them — a relocation from roughly 60% to roughly 32% of the trace, with their participation essentially unchanged. The order flip is shown directly and consistently in all six activity pairs with A26 and A27, moving from a fixed "after" order to a fixed "before" order rather than to a mixed order. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006] The positional shift is confined to the three activities while A26, A27 and A28 stay in place, and the local distance changes fit the same picture: the region the group vacated became tighter (A27→A28 and A28→A32 now nearly always consecutive), and the A45→A26 gap widened as if activity had been inserted before A26. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

Limitations: POS and LAG measure event order, not clock time, so the evidence establishes a change in execution order rather than in timing; the new distances to A28 and A32 rest on 1–3 after-window traces; and the evidence cannot distinguish the three activities moving together as one block from three separate shifts that coincide, nor show whether anything took the group's former place. A competing explanation — that the segment was restructured by a routing or participation change rather than a relocation — is made unlikely by the stable participation and co-occurrence shares, but a broader restructuring of the surrounding segment cannot be fully ruled out. [POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Other differences: A15 is repeated less often in the later window (about 1.58 vs 1.89 occurrences per trace; recurrence in 38 vs 47 in 100 traces), an independent repetition change that is not part of the relocation hypothesis. [OCC001]
