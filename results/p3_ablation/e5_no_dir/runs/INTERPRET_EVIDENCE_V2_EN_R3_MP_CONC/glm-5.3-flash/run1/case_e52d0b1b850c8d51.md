### Observed differences

- The largest change concerns the group A19, A20 and A25. In the earlier window all three ran in every case (100% participation); in the later window each runs in only about 53–54 in 100 cases. They move as a block: for A19 and A20, both are present in ~53% of later cases, neither in ~46%, and exactly one in about 1% or fewer (the same pattern for A19–A25 and A20–A25). When the block does run, its internal order is preserved (A19 before A20 in ~98% of joint appearances in both windows). [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL016, REL017, REL022]

- A06 fell from about 71 in 100 cases to about 26 in 100. Earlier it sat early in the case (median relative position ~0.16), immediately after A14 (one-step gap in ~97% of joint appearances), and preceded the A19/A20/A25 block in roughly 65–67 of 100 cases. In the later window, when A06 runs at all, it sits later and far more variably (median ~0.30, upper quartile ~0.83), still usually right after A14 (~75% of the much fewer joint appearances), but it co-occurs with the block in only ~14 of 100 cases, and about 35 in 100 later cases contain neither A06 nor A19 (none earlier). [OCC002, POS001, REL001, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG003]

- The surrounding activities kept their universal participation: A14, A15, A26, A41, A46 and A47 still appear in every later case; in pairs with the block it is always the block side that is missing, never these activities. [REL006, REL010, REL018]

- Three previously rare activities occur more often: A04 in ~15 of 100 later cases (from ~5), A08 in ~20 (from ~4), A10 in ~16 (from ~5). [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]

- Smaller shifts in step distances between always-present activities: A41 to A26 is now often adjacent (one-step gap in ~40% of later cases, from ~0%; the four-step gap fell from ~91% to ~44%); A14 to A41 moved from mostly five to mostly four steps; A14 to A15 from mostly two to mostly one step; A46 to A47 stays at one step in ~88% (from ~97%) with ~12% at two steps. [LAG008, LAG005, LAG004, LAG009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a previously mandatory segment became optional: the block A19, A20 and A25, executed in every earlier case, is skipped as a whole in about 46 of 100 later cases while still running in its original order in the remaining ~53, and A06, which earlier led into this block in about two-thirds of cases, is now executed in only ~26 of 100 cases and later within the case, so the former A06-then-block sequence survives in only ~14 of 100 later cases. Supporting signs: the participation losses are direct and large, the relational items show the three block activities are omitted together while ordering among the remaining activities is intact, and the volume of qualifying evidence (49 control-flow items against roughly 0.64 expected by chance; every item's difference exceeds ~995 of 1000 random re-splits) cannot be explained by sampling noise as a whole. Limitations: without direction/successor evidence the data cannot show whether a predecessor now connects directly past the skipped block, and it cannot distinguish one changed process from a later window that mixes old-style and new-style cases; the lag items involving A06 after the change rest on only about 7–32 joint observations. [OCC002, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, POS001, REL003, REL016, REL017, REL022]

Other differences: the increased participation of A04, A08 and A10 and the small step-distance shifts among always-present activities are not linked to the main hypothesis by any relational evidence in these windows. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, LAG004, LAG005, LAG008, LAG009]
