### Observed differences

- The order between two groups of activities reversed. In the earlier window, A26 and A27 ran before A19, A20 and A25 in roughly 30–36 in 100 traces, while the opposite order occurred essentially never (at most about 0.5 in 100); in the later window, A19, A20 and A25 ran before A26 and A27 in about 29–35 in 100 traces, and the old order survived in at most about 1 in 100. How often each pair shared a trace stayed similar (about 31–37 in 100 before, about 31–36 in 100 after). [REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]

- The relative position of A19, A20 and A25 moved earlier: mean position about 0.6 of the trace before versus about 0.32–0.33 in the later window (medians about 0.60 versus about 0.31). A26, present in every trace in both windows, moved slightly later (mean about 0.32 to about 0.35), as did A27 (about 0.56 to about 0.59). A25's participation slipped from about 37 to about 31 in 100 traces, while A19 and A20 stayed at about 32–36 in 100. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

- Distances from upstream activities to A26 grew in the later window: A16 to A26 from mostly 3 steps apart (about 54 in 100) to 4–5 steps (about 70 and 30 in 100); A18 to A26 from mostly 2 steps (about 92 in 100) to 3–4 steps (about 70 and 30); A45 to A26 from mostly 1 step (about 91 in 100) to 2–3 steps (about 72 and 27). This fits the flipped activities now sitting between these activities and A26. [LAG004, LAG005, LAG014]

- A08 changed in both frequency and order: traces containing it rose from about 13 in 100 (26 of 200) to about 30 in 100 (59 of 200), mean occurrences per trace from 0.14 to about 0.30, and its mean position moved earlier (about 0.49 to about 0.34). Its order relative to A26, A27, A28 and A32, previously mixed (each order about 6 in 100), is now mostly A08 first (about 27 in 100 versus about 2.5 in 100 for A26 before A08), and A08 sits two steps before A26 in about 96 in 100 of the later-window observations (53 pairs) against a spread over 1, 4 and 5 steps before (10 pairs). In the later window A19 also runs immediately before A26 in all 69 observed pairs. [OCC001, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, LAG002, LAG007]

- Around A27, A28 and A32 the sequence tightened: A27-to-A28 became adjacent (1 step in about 51 in 100 before versus about 99 in 100 after), A28-to-A32 became adjacent (an about 48/52 split versus about 99 in 100 at 1 step), and the A27-to-A32 distance shortened from mostly 3 steps to mostly 2. - Several lag comparisons rest on only one or two observations in one of the windows (for example A19-to-A26, A08-to-A19 and A16-to-A19 with one earlier observation; A25-to-A32, A27-to-A25 and A28-to-A25 with one or two later observations), so the specific distance changes behind them are uncertain. [LAG001, LAG003, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window A19, A20 and A25 execute before A26 and A27, reversing their earlier placement when they followed them, and that A08 — previously with no fixed order relative to A26, A27, A28 and A32 — now typically executes before them while also appearing in about twice as many traces (about 13 to about 30 in 100). The order change is observed directly on six activity pairs with stable co-occurrence, the position shifts of all five activities point the same way, the longer upstream distances to A26 fit the flipped activities now sitting before it, and 31 items qualified against roughly 0.47 expected by chance across the 94 tested control-flow units — far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG014, LAG007, OCC001, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]

Limitations: all items come from the single control-flow family and many describe the same underlying trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations; positions are event positions rather than clock times; and the evidence cannot distinguish a re-placement of these activities within one process from a rise in the share of traces that already followed this order in the earlier window. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, OCC001]

- The lag comparisons listed above with only 1–2 observations in one window should not be relied on for the direction of those distance changes. [LAG001, LAG003, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG012]
