### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 changed from a three-way choice — exactly one of A14, A15 or A16 per case — into a block in which A21, A22 and A23 each execute once in every case, in no fixed order among themselves, still between A39 and A24. The screening produced 41 qualifying items where roughly 0.33 would be expected without a change, so this volume cannot be explained by random sampling of a stable process. The change is shown directly rather than inferred: participation moves from about 70, 59 and 71 of 200 cases to 0 of 200 for the old activities, and from 0 to 200 of 200 for the new ones; relational evidence places both the old choice and the new block between A39 and A24; and the A39→A24 distance growing from 2 to 4 steps matches one activity in that segment being replaced by three. The many items largely reflect this single underlying difference, not independent changes. [OCC001, OCC004, REL006, REL019, REL021, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely concurrent block from a sequence whose order simply varies from case to case, and zero participation shows only that A14, A15 and A16 were not observed in the later 200 cases, not that they were removed from the process. Positions and distances are event positions, not clock times, so no claim about timing is made. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL019]
