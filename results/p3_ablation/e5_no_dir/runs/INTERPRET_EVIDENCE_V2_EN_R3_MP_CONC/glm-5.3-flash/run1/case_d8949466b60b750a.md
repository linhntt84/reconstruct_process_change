### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the segment between A26 and A28: the route in which A32, A33 and A38 were executed together, in varying order, between A26 and A28 is no longer taken — each of the three fell from about 48–49 in 100 traces to 1 in 200 — while A27, previously an alternative taken in about half the cases, now executes once in nearly every trace, co-occurring with A26 in about 995 in 1000 later traces and preceding A28 in about 96–97 in 100. This is a change in participation (three activities became essentially unobserved, one became mandatory) rather than a re-weighting of intact alternatives, because the trio is nearly absent in the later window. The signs are direct and consistent: participation and relational items rest on 97–103 traces earlier versus 1–199 later, the ordering and lag distributions match the earlier two-route and later single-route structure, and 31 items qualified against about 0.37 expected if nothing had changed, so sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. The evidence cannot distinguish whether the trio's route was removed from the process or remains reachable but is now almost never selected (one later trace still contains these activities), nor can it rule out that the trio was replaced by activities outside the tested units. Other differences: A28's relative position within the trace shifted slightly earlier in the later window (mean about 0.55 to about 0.53), consistent with traces becoming shorter once the three-activity route disappeared; the position items for A32, A33 and A38 in the later window rest on a single trace each and add no independent information.
