### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment formed by A36, A37 and A42 became an optional, later-running alternative to A27. In the earlier window every case ran A25 → {A36, A37, A42} → A28 → A26 → A27 → A31; in the later window cases run A25 → A28 → A26 and then either A27 (about 53 in 100) or the three activities together (about 47 in 100), followed by A31. A27 itself thus also became conditional, and the block now sits between A26 and A31 instead of between A25 and A28. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL020, REL021, REL022, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The placement change is shown directly by the order reversals relative to A26 and A28, and it is consistent with the co-occurrence collapse between A27 and the group, the participation drops, and the distance changes (the A25–A28 gap closes where the block used to sit; the A26–A31 gap widens exactly in the traces containing the block). Forty-three control-flow items qualified, against fewer than one expected if nothing had changed [screening], with empirical strengths of about 0.995 throughout (e.g. [OCC001, POS006, REL006, LAG001]) and 94–107 supporting traces per changed activity in the later window. Limitations: only the tested activities and pairs are covered, so untested activities between them are not observed, and the indicators measure event order, not clock time. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a changed mix of pre-existing case types rather than a modified process: no earlier case shows the later structure (A26 before the block in 0 of 200 traces), so the history of the second shape cannot be traced further with this evidence.

Other differences: small position shifts of A25 (mean 0.34 → 0.37) and A27 (0.62 → 0.59), which are consistent side effects of the block's removal and the branch structure rather than separate changes [POS001, POS003]; several LAG items show the former short distances from A25/A28 to the block vanishing, but with only 1–2 supporting observations in the later window they add little weight on their own [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG009].
