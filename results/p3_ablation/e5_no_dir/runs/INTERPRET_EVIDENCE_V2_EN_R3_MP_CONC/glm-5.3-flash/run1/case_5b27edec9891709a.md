### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window runs as two mutually exclusive paths: one containing A22 (about 54 in 100 cases, still ordered A20, A23, A21, A22, A26) and one containing the trio A31/A32/A37 (about 47 in 100 cases), which now executes after A21 and A23 and before A26 instead of between A20 and A23; earlier, every case contained both segments. [REL011, REL005, REL009, REL010, REL006, REL014, REL017, LAG001, LAG005, OCC001, OCC002]

The support is direct and consistent: the mutual exclusivity and the order flips around A21 and A23 are observed relationally rather than inferred from position shifts alone, the trio's internal mixed order and its precedence before A26 are preserved, and the occurrence, position, relation and lag indicators all describe the same restructuring; with 42 qualifying items against roughly 0.37 expected without change, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole.
