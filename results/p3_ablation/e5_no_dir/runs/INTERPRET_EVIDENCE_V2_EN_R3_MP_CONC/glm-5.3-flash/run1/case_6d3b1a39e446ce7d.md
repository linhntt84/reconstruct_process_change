### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

- Main hypothesis: in the earlier window a case ran A25 immediately followed by A28, with A26 also before a branch point, and then either A27 (about 55 in 100 cases) or the group A36, A37, A42 in no fixed order (about 46 in 100), followed by A31; in the later window the choice is gone — A36, A37 and A42 run in every case, placed between A25 and A28 (A25 reaches A28 only after about four steps, and A42 one to three steps after A25), while A27 also became mandatory in its earlier place before A31, with A26 running after the group and before A27. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL017, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Supporting signs: screening flagged 43 control-flow items where about 0.5 would be expected for an unchanged process, and every item's difference exceeds those seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits; the participation rise to essentially every case, the switch from mutual exclusivity to near-always-together, the consistent order reversals, and the growing A25-to-A28 distance all point to the same structural difference, which a mere shift in how often existing paths are taken could not produce, since A27 and the group were not observed together before. [OCC002, REL011, REL012, REL013, LAG001, LAG002]
- Limitations: the block's placement rests on step distances within five positions, not on observed direct transitions, so unobserved activities may sit inside or around it; the internal order of A36, A37 and A42 is unconstrained in the data; and a few lag profiles rest on only 1–2 observations. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, REL020]
- A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the later window draws a different mixture of variants rather than the behaviour itself having changed; the near-universal co-occurrence of A27 with the group (about 1–2 in 100 before, about 100 in 100 after) is hard to reconcile with a pure re-weighting of the earlier variants, but the windows are finite samples. [REL011, REL012, REL013]
- Other differences: A27 occurs twice in about 2–3 in 100 later cases, small interleaved-or-repeated shares (up to about 3–4 in 100) appear for pairs such as A25/A27 and A27/A28, and A25's mean position shifted slightly earlier (about 0.37 to 0.35). [OCC001, REL001, REL005, REL009, POS001]
