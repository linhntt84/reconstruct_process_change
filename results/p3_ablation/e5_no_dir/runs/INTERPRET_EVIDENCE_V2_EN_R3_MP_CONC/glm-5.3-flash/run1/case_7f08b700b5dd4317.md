### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, each of the six activities A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 ran in every trace (200 of 200, about one occurrence per trace, no repetition). In the later window, each runs in only about 47–57 in 100 traces (93–115 of 200), still without repetition. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

- In the later window these six split into two mutually exclusive triples. A33, A35 and A36 co-occur in about 46–47 in 100 traces and are jointly absent in about 53; A37, A38 and A39 co-occur in about 56 in 100 and are jointly absent in about 43. Pairs across the two groups (for example A33 with A37, or A35 with A39) co-occur in only about 3 in 100 traces; about 44 in 100 traces show only the first group's activity and about 53 only the second's. In the earlier window, every one of these pairs co-occurred in all traces. [REL013, REL014, REL015, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL021, REL022, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027]

- Participation of the surrounding activities is unchanged: A30 and A31 still run in every trace in both windows. Earlier, A30 preceded each of the six in about 99–100 in 100 traces, and each of the six preceded A31 in about 99–100 in 100. In the later window these orders still hold whenever the block activities run; in the remaining traces only A30 (about 44–54 in 100) or only A31 (about 44–54 in 100) is present. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]

- The span of the block shrank: before, the distance from A30 to A37, A38 or A39, and from A33, A35 or A36 to A31, was almost always 4–5 event positions; in the later window it is mostly 1–3 positions. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG009]

- Minor shifts: A30 sits slightly later within traces (mean relative position about 0.62 to about 0.67) and A31 slightly earlier (about 0.82 to about 0.80), with similarly small moves for the block activities; several cross-group lag items rest on only 4–5 after-window observations and are weak. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening flagged 50 control-flow items where about 0.56 would be expected by chance across the 112 tested units, so the volume of flagged behaviour far exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces. The evidence supports the hypothesis that the six-activity block between A30 and A31 — A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39, which previously executed in full in every case — became in the later window an exclusive choice between two groups of three: roughly 46 in 100 traces run A33, A35 and A36, roughly 56 in 100 run A37, A38 and A39, and almost no trace runs both groups. This is shown directly by the collapse of cross-group co-occurrence from 100% to about 3% while within-group co-occurrence and the ordering relative to A30 and A31 are preserved, and corroborated by the block's positional span shrinking from 4–5 steps to 1–3. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC006, REL001, REL007, REL013, REL015, REL018, REL022, REL025, REL027, LAG001, LAG004]

Limitations: the evidence contains no direct-successor items, so the exact wiring into and out of the block (for example, whether A30 now leads directly to the first activity of each group) cannot be confirmed, and nothing shows what determines which group runs. The internal order within each triple also appears less fixed in the later window, with gaps of 1, 2 and 3 steps in similar shares, so the earlier fixed sequence may have loosened. Chance sampling is unlikely given the screening excess, but both windows are samples of 200 traces. [LAG001, LAG004, LAG006]

Other differences: only the small position shifts of A30 and A31 noted above, which are plausibly consequences of the shorter block rather than independent changes. [POS001, POS002]
