### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the stretch between A26 and A28 has two variants: in roughly half of the cases A27 runs there as before, and in the other half A27 is skipped and the three activities A32, A33 and A38 execute instead, together and in no fixed order among themselves, after A14 and A26 and before A28 (their order relative to A15 is mixed); in the earlier window A27 always ran in that stretch and A32/A33/A38 never appeared. This is shown directly by the mutual exclusivity between A27 and each new activity, their joint appearance in about half of the traces, their consistent order relative to A14, A15, A26 and A28, and the A26→A28 distance stretching from 2 to 4 steps; the 44 qualifying items (all control-flow) far exceed the fewer than 1 expected by chance, though they describe overlapping aspects of the same underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. The evidence cannot rule out that A32/A33/A38 are a re-labelling or relocation of existing rare behaviour rather than genuinely new activities, because there is no direct predecessor–successor (DIR) evidence and the placement rests on co-occurrence and relative order. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL021, REL022, REL025, REL028, LAG003]

Other differences: A04 now runs in about 60 in 100 traces (up from about 19), directly after A14 and before A15/A26/A28 near the start of the trace, and A06 has nearly disappeared (about 20 down to 4 in 100 traces); neither belongs to the branch hypothesis above. [OCC001, POS001, LAG001, OCC002]
