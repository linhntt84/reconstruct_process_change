### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A14, A15 and A20 were not observed at all (0 of 200 traces). In the later window, A14 occurs in about 37 in 100 traces, A20 in about 34 in 100 and A15 in about 29 in 100, always exactly once per trace where it appears (no repetitions). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The three activities are mutually exclusive in the later window: no trace contains two of them, and together they cover about 99 in 100 traces, so a typical later trace contains exactly one of the three. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- A36 and A21 occur in every trace in both windows. Whenever one of the three activities occurs, A36 comes before it and it comes before A21, without exception. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- The distance between A36 and A21 changed accordingly: in the earlier window A36 was immediately followed by A21 in all 200 traces, while in the later window the pair is typically two steps apart (about 99 in 100 traces), with a direct succession remaining in only about 1 in 100. [LAG001]
- The new activities sit at roughly one-third of the trace (mean relative position about 0.35), and A21's mean relative position moved slightly later, from about 0.37 to about 0.39, consistent with something being inserted before it. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a step is inserted between A36 and A21 and is performed as exactly one of three alternative activities — A14 in about 37 in 100 traces, A20 in about 34 in 100 and A15 in about 29 in 100 — whereas in the earlier window A36 was followed directly by A21 and none of the three was observed. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001]

The support is direct and coherent: the relational items place each alternative after A36 and before A21 and show the alternatives never co-occur, the A36–A21 distance shifts from one to two steps in step with the new activities' appearance, and their positions fall between the two neighbours. The screening context reinforces this: 17 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.22 expected by chance, far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [LAG001, REL001, POS001, POS004]

Limitations: all items come from a single evidence family and several indicators describe the same underlying trace behaviour, so they are consistent rather than independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that A14, A15 and A20 existed earlier but were so rare that 200 traces failed to sample them; their complete absence in the earlier window alongside 29–37% participation later makes this unlikely, but it cannot be excluded. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
