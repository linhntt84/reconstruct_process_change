### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a new step was inserted into the sequence between A27 and A29: the earlier segment ran A27 → A28 → A29 with A27, A28 and A29 adjacent, while in the later window every trace carries exactly one extra activity — A19 in about 29 in 100 cases, A20 in about 41 in 100 or A25 in about 31 in 100 — placed after A27 and before A29, sometimes directly after A27 and sometimes directly after A28. The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the three activities go from no participation to 29–41 in 100 traces while A27, A28 and A29 remain in every trace; they act as mutually exclusive alternatives; their order relative to A27 and A29 is consistent across all pairs; and the A27–A29 distance grows from two to three steps with the A27–A28 and A28–A29 spacings shifting exactly as an insertion between them would predict. The 23 flagged items (all control-flow) far exceed the roughly 0.33 expected by chance among the 66 tested units, so the ensemble is not what an unchanged process typically produces.

Limitations: the evidence contains no direct successor (edge) information, so the exact insertion point relative to A28 cannot be fixed beyond the observed mixed order, and nothing shows what determines which of the three alternatives runs in a given case. A competing explanation that cannot be fully excluded is that A19, A20 and A25 also occurred earlier at rates too low for 200 traces to register — zero participation strictly means only "not observed" — although appearance in 29–41 in 100 later traces makes plain sampling fluctuation implausible.
