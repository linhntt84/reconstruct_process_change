### Observed differences

- Three activities stopped appearing. A19 was present in about 36 in 100 cases before (71 of 200 traces) and in none of the 200 later traces; A25 fell from about 35 in 100 (70 traces) to none; A20 fell from about 31 in 100 (61 traces) to a single case. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the earlier window these three were mutually exclusive alternatives: no trace contained two of them (co-occurrence between 0 and about 0.5 in 100), and the share of traces containing none of them matched the third activity's participation, so essentially every case executed exactly one of the three. In the later window, about 99.5–100 in 100 traces contain none of them. [REL007, REL009, REL014]
- The block had fixed neighbours: A12, A14 and A41 came before it (in about 10–16, 31–36 and 30–35 in 100 cases respectively), and A26, A31 and A24 came after it (about 30–36 in 100 cases; A24 about 16 in 100). In the later window these relations are gone and every partner appears without the block — for example A14 occurs in all traces but never together with A19, and A41 is never followed by A19, A20 or A25. [REL001, REL004, REL012, REL021, REL010, REL011, REL008, REL018]
- The distance from A41 to A26 shrank: before, A26 followed A41 two positions later in about 94 in 100 cases; in the later window it follows one position later in about 93 in 100, consistent with the activity that used to sit between them no longer running. [LAG002]
- Smaller shifts: the gap from A24 to A31 moved from mostly two positions (about 59 in 100) to mostly one (about 59 in 100), and the relative positions of A14, A26 and A41 within traces changed by only about 0.5–1.6 points in 100. [LAG001, POS001, POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A possibility supported by the evidence is that the three-way alternative block — A19, A20 or A25, of which every case executed exactly one in the earlier window, positioned after A12/A14/A41 and before A24/A26/A31 — is no longer executed in the later window. The participation drop is direct (71, 61 and 70 supporting traces before versus 0, 1 and 0 after), all ordering relations of the block vanished while each partner continues to occur, and A41 now reaches A26 in one step instead of two. With 32 qualifying items against fewer than one (0.38) expected by chance, the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of an unchanged process produce. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, LAG002, REL004, REL012, REL010]

Limitations: zero participation only shows the activities were not observed; the evidence cannot distinguish a block that no longer runs from one that still exists but was not triggered in the later cases, and the single A20 occurrence shows such traces can still appear rarely. An important competing explanation is a shift in the mix of cases sampled after the change point — only cases that never reach this block — rather than a change in the process itself, which this evidence cannot rule out. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Other differences: small relative-position shifts (A14 about half a point later, A26 about 1.5 points earlier, A41 about 1.6 points later within traces) and the A24→A31 gap shrinking from two positions to one; these are consistent with traces becoming shorter but, being event ranks rather than clock times, do not independently show that an activity was moved. [POS001, POS005, POS006, LAG001]
