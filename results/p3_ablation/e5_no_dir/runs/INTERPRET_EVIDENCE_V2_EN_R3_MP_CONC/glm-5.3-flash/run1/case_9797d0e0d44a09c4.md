### Observed differences

- The three activities A19, A20 and A25 were each present in only about 47 in 100 traces in the earlier window; in the later window they appear in 199 of 200 traces, and their mean occurrences per trace rose from about 0.47–0.48 to about 1.0, i.e. roughly once per case. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 always occurred together as a block (co-occurrence about 47 in 100 traces, absent otherwise) in the fixed order A19 → A25 → A20; in the later window they still occur together and in the same fixed order, but in about 995 in 1000 traces. - The block sits between A41 and A26 in both windows: A41 precedes the block and the block precedes A26 in about 96–99 in 100 co-occurring traces. The difference is that in the earlier window A41 was immediately followed by A26 in about 52 in 100 traces (the block skipped), while in the later window that direct succession nearly vanished (about 0.5 in 100) and the typical gap between A41 and A26 became four steps (about 89 in 100). - Secondary differences: A42 still occurs in every trace, but its order relative to the block became less fixed — the interleaved/repeated share rose from about 17 in 100 traces to about 46 in 100. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block A19 → A25 → A20, executed between A41 and A26, changed from optional to effectively mandatory: in the earlier window it ran in about 47 in 100 cases and was skipped in the rest, with A41 then directly preceding A26, while in the later window it runs in about 995 in 1000 cases with its internal order unchanged. The participation jump is measured directly for all three activities with substantial support in both windows (94–95 vs 199 traces), all fifteen relational items move consistently in the same direction, and the gap and position shifts match the block being inserted in every case. With 21 qualifying items against roughly 0.41 expected without a change, chance alone cannot account for the pattern as a whole. A limitation the evidence cannot resolve: traces alone cannot distinguish a change in the routing rule from a shift in the mix of cases arriving in each window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL007, LAG001, POS001, POS002]

Other differences: A42's ordering relative to the block became less fixed (interleaved/repeated share about 17% → 46%), and A45, while still occurring in only about 60 in 100 traces, no longer appears without the block; these are not part of the main hypothesis.
