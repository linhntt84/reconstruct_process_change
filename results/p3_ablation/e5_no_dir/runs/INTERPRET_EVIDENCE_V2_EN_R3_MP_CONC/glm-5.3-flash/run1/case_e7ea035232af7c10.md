### Observed differences

- Before, A27, A36, A37 and A42 each ran in roughly half of the cases (46–54 in 100 traces, once per case); in the later window each of them runs in essentially every trace (≥ 99.5 in 100), still exactly once. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the exclusive choice between A27 and the activity group A36/A37/A42 was removed in favour of one mandatory combined course: in the later window, A36, A37 and A42 — previously an alternative to A27 in about 46 in 100 cases, placed between A26 and A31 — always execute, relocated to directly after A25 and before A28 and A26, while A27 became mandatory in its previous slot between A26 and A31. Within the group, the three activities still run in no fixed order. [REL006, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, LAG001, LAG003]

The hypothesis rests on direct relational evidence, not on position shifts alone: order relations between the group and A26/A28 flip from about 46 in 100 one way to about 99 in 100 the other way, co-occurrence of A27 with each group member rises from 0 to about 99.5 in 100, and participation, position and spacing indicators all point the same way. Screening produced 40 qualifying items against about 0.44 expected if nothing had changed (all control-flow), so the ensemble is far beyond what an unchanged process typically yields. [REL006, REL014, REL011, POS006, LAG001, LAG003]

Limitations: the combined behaviour was almost unobserved before (co-occurrence of A27 with the group in at most about 1 in 200 traces), so the evidence cannot distinguish whether the choice was removed or a previously rare combined variant took over while the old variants disappeared; the exact distance from the group to A31 rests on lag items backed by a single observation in one window; and activities outside the sampled set were not tested, so accompanying changes elsewhere cannot be ruled out. [REL011, LAG005, LAG006]

Other differences: the always-present activities show small position shifts — A25 slightly earlier (mean position about 0.36 to 0.35) and A26, A28 and A31 slightly later (e.g., A28 about 0.39 to 0.46) — which are consistent with the group being inserted before them rather than being independent changes. [POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005]
