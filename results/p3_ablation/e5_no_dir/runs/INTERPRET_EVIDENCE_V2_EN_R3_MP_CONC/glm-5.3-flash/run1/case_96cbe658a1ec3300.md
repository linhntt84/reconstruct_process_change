### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the relative execution order of two blocks reversed: A35, A36 and A41, which previously ran after A26 and A27, now run before them, with all six activities still executed in every case. The change is shown directly by six activity-pair relations that each flip from about 98–99 in 100 traces favouring one order to about 97–98 in 100 favouring the other, at unchanged 100% co-occurrence, and it agrees with the positional shifts (A26 and A27 later, A35, A36 and A41 earlier) and the step-distance changes. Participation and repetition differences are ruled out as competing explanations because every activity occurs in all traces in both windows and interleaved-or-repeated shares stay at about 2% or less; screening produced 21 qualifying control-flow items against an expectation of about 0.38 without a change, and every item's difference exceeds about 99.5% of random re-splits. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

Limitations: positions and distances are event positions, not clock times, so no statement about timing is possible; and while the pairwise order reversal is directly observed, the evidence cannot distinguish whether the A35/A36/A41 block moved earlier, the A26/A27 pair moved later, or both, nor rule out that additions or removals of other, untested activities between the blocks contributed to the positional shifts. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG001]

Other differences: A30 previously followed A35, A36 and A41 within 1–3 steps in essentially every trace and A26 by 4–5 steps; in the later window it immediately follows A26 and is within five steps after A35, A36 or A41 in only about 1 of 200 traces, and A27's gap after A25 widened from immediate succession to 4–5 steps — both shifts fit the same block reorder. [LAG001, LAG003, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]
