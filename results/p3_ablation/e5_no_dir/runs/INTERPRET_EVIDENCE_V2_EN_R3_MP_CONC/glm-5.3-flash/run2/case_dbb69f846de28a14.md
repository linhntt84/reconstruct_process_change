### Observed differences

- Four activities ran in every trace in the earlier window but in only about half of the traces in the later window: A27 in 100% → about 54 in 100 cases (107 of 200), A36 in 100% → about 47 in 100 (94 of 200), A37 and A42 in 100% → about 48 in 100 each (95 of 200). A25, A26, A28 and A31 still appear in every trace in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005]

- A27 and the group A36/A37/A42 became mutually exclusive. Before, A27 co-occurred with each of A36, A37 and A42 in 100% of traces; in the later window co-occurrence is about 0–1% (roughly 1–2 in 200 traces), and for these pairs no trace contains neither activity — so almost every trace contains exactly one of A27 or the group. The three group members still appear together whenever any of them appears: A36–A37 co-occurrence is 47% with neither present in about 52 in 100 traces and almost no trace containing only one of them. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL020, REL021, REL022]

- The group switched sides relative to A26 and A28. Before, A36, A37 and A42 each ran before A26 in essentially all traces (99.5–100 in 100) and before A28 in about 99 in 100; in the later window, in traces containing both activities, A26 runs before A36/A37/A42 in about 95–96 in 100 and A28 before them in about 95–97 in 100. The group still runs before A31 in both windows. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019]

- A28 now directly follows A25: the A25→A28 gap was 4 steps in about 91% of traces (5 steps in about 9%) before, and 1 step in about 97.5% of traces in the later window. The A26→A31 distance, previously 2 steps in about 97.5% of traces, is now 2 steps in about 53% and 4 steps in about 42.5% — consistent with the three-activity group sitting between A26 and A31 in the traces that contain it. A37 now runs within 1–3 steps before A31 in about 46 in 100 traces (93 of 200), against 1 in 200 before (the before side rests on a single observation). [LAG001, LAG005, LAG008]

- Mean relative positions shifted accordingly: A36, A37 and A42 from about 0.40 to about 0.57 of the trace; A28 from about 0.46 to about 0.39; A26 from about 0.60 to about 0.54; A27 from about 0.62 to about 0.59 in the traces where it occurs. [POS006, POS007, POS008, POS004, POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the later window contains two mutually exclusive behaviours where the earlier window had one fixed sequence: in roughly half the traces the segment between A26/A28 and A31 is filled by A27, and in the other half by the group A36, A37, A42, which now runs after A26 and A28 instead of before them; A28 consequently follows A25 directly. Previously every trace executed all of A25, A26, A27, A28, A31 and the group, with the group between A25 and A26/A28 and A27 between A26 and A31. Supporting signs: the participation of A27, A36, A37 and A42 dropped from 100% to about 47–54 in 100 traces; co-occurrence between A27 and each group member collapsed from 100% to about 0–1% while the group members remain fully co-occurring; the order reversals against A26 and A28 are directly observed relations; and the lag changes (A25→A28 from 4–5 steps to 1; A26→A31 splitting into 2 and 4 steps in proportions matching the two behaviours) corroborate the repositioning. The screening produced 43 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeds about 99.5% of random-split references, so the ensemble is not what the 200-trace windows typically produce without a change. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL020, REL006, REL014, LAG001, LAG005]

Limitations: POS and LAG measure event order, not clock time, so the group's "movement" is a statement about sequence position; the evidence does not show what determines which of the two behaviours a trace follows, since no decision point outside these activities is observed; and a few lag items (e.g., A25→A36, A36→A28) rest on only 1–2 observations in one window and were not used as support. [POS006, LAG002, LAG006]

Other differences: small shifts in mean relative position of A25 (about 0.34 → 0.37, slightly later) and A31 (about 0.65 → 0.63, slightly earlier), consistent with by-products of the segment changes above. [POS001, POS005]
