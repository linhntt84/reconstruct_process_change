### Observed differences

- Two three-activity blocks swapped how often they run. In the earlier window, A21, A22 and A23 each ran in about 75 in 100 traces and essentially only jointly (co-occurring in about 75 in 100), while A24, A25 and A26 each ran in only about 25 in 100 (jointly in about 25 in 100). - A19 and A20, present in essentially every trace in both windows, switched which block they precede: earlier, A19 preceded A21 in about 74 in 100 traces; later, A19 precedes A24 in about 75 in 100 and A21 usually runs without A19. A20 shows the same switch. [REL013, REL016, REL019, REL022]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the closing stage of the process was restructured. In the earlier window the common closing sequence ran A19/A20, then the A21–A23 block (about 75 in 100 traces), then A34 and A35 back-to-back; in the later window the alternative block A24–A26 is the one usually executed (about 75 in 100 traces, up from about 25 in 100) while A21–A23 became the rare alternative (about 25 in 100, down from about 75), and A06 — previously occasional and placed before A34 — now appears in about half of the traces between A34 and A35, directly before A35. This is unlikely to be a sampling artefact: 87 control-flow items qualified against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process. The signs are direct and consistent: mirrored participation changes of similar size on both sides, precedence relations that switch partner block wholesale, and relational plus positional evidence (A34 before A06, A06 immediately before A35, the A34–A35 gap lengthening by one step) that shows A06's new placement directly. Limitations: the many items largely re-describe the same traces rather than constituting independent confirmations; the evidence cannot show whether the block swap and the A06 insertion were one coordinated change or two simultaneous ones, and it cannot rule out that the two windows simply mix two pre-existing variants in different proportions. Other differences: A02 and A08 participate less often in the later window (about 8 and about 5 in 100 traces, down from about 17 and about 14), and minor gap redistributions appear around A29, A30 and A34; these are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001, OCC003, LAG007, LAG012, POS002]
