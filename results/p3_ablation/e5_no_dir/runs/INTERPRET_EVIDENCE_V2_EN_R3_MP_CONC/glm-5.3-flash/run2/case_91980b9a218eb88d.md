### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window A19, A20 and A25 are executed after A26, A27 and A28 rather than before them: the three activities shift from about one-third to about three-fifths of the trace, every tested pair across the two groups reverses or loses its fixed order in the same direction, and the adjacency pattern matches, with A26 now directly following A45 and A27/A28 now followed by A19/A20 and then A32. The reversal is shown directly by relational evidence, consistently across all eight pairs; every involved activity is well represented in both windows, so this is a reordering rather than activities appearing or disappearing or a shift in how often existing alternative paths are taken; and 25 qualifying items against about 0.36 expected in an unchanged process (all control-flow, each difference larger than in 199 of 200 random re-splits) is far beyond what chance would produce. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a reordering within a single behaviour from a shift in the mix of trace variants that happens to place A19/A20/A25 late; several lag profiles rest on only 1–2 observations in one window; and positions are relative event positions, not clock times.
