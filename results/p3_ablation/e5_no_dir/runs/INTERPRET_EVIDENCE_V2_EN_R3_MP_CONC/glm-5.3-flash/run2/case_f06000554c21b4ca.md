### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the activity group A35, A36 and A41 changed its place in the sequence relative to A25, A26, A27 and A30: in the earlier window the group executed between A26 and A30, after A26 which itself followed A27 and A25, whereas in the later window the group executes between A25 and A27, and A26 now runs directly before A30 — the group and the segment containing A26/A27 exchanged their relative order. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG003]

The support is direct and consistent: the order flip is observed for all six cross-pairs at about 98–99.5 in 100 traces with co-occurrence unchanged at 1.0 in both windows, so no activity became rarer or optional; the gap changes (A25→A27 from 1 to about 4 steps, A26→A30 from about 4 to 1) and the disappearance and appearance of exactly the cross-pairs such an exchange predicts fit one coherent movement rather than separate shifts; and 21 control-flow items qualified from 76 tested units against fewer than 1 (0.38) expected by chance, with every item's difference exceeding about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]

The evidence cannot distinguish whether the group A35/A36/A41 moved earlier or the A26/A27 segment moved later, since both describe the same exchange; the internal arrangement of A35, A36 and A41 shows variable spacing (1–3 steps) in both windows, relations among the group members themselves and between A26 and A27 were not tested, and a few "after" pair profiles rest on only 1–2 traces, so the exact boundaries of the relocated segment remain uncertain.
