### Observed differences

- Three activities — A19, A20 and A25 — were a constant feature of the earlier window and are practically gone from the later one. Earlier, each ran in about a third of the cases (A19 in about 36, A20 in about 31 and A25 in about 35 of 100 cases), each at most once per case, and they never ran together (pairwise co-occurrence of at most about 1 in 200 cases), so essentially every earlier case contained exactly one of the three. In the later window, A19 and A25 appear in 0 of 200 cases and A20 in 1 of 200. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL007, REL009, REL014]

- The three occupied a fixed slot between stable neighbours. Earlier, A14 preceded each of them in virtually every case where they co-occurred (about 31–36 in 100 cases), A41 likewise preceded them (about 30–35 in 100), A12 preceded them in roughly 10–16 in 100, and they in turn preceded A26 and A31 (about 30–35 in 100). A14, A26, A31 and A41 themselves occur in essentially every case in both windows. In the later window, none of these neighbours co-occurs with A19, A20 or A25 anymore. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021]

- The slot closed up: before, A26 followed A41 two event positions later in about 94 of 100 cases (three positions later in the remainder); in the later window, A26 follows A41 one position later in about 93 of 100 cases. [LAG002]

- The relative positions of the ever-present activities shifted slightly, in the direction expected when one activity disappears from every trace: A26 now sits slightly earlier within the trace (mean relative position about 0.39 before vs about 0.37 after), while A14 and A41 sit slightly later (about 0.14 vs 0.15, and 0.32 vs 0.33). [POS001, POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of three mutually exclusive activities A19, A20 and A25 — which earlier ran exactly once per case, after A41 (and after A14), and immediately before A26 — is no longer executed in the later window, so that the flow now runs from A41 directly on to A26. Supporting signs: participation in the three fell from about 31–36 in 100 cases each to 0–1 of 200, while their neighbours A14, A26, A31 and A41 remain in essentially all cases; every earlier relation of the block with these neighbours (about 30–36 co-occurrences in 100 cases) dropped to zero; and A41 and A26, previously two positions apart in about 94 of 100 cases, are now directly consecutive in about 93 of 100, which is what closing the slot produces. The screening found 32 qualifying control-flow items against fewer than one (about 0.38) expected for an unchanged process — roughly 80 times the chance expectation — and every item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits, so sampling variation cannot explain the ensemble. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL004, REL010, REL012, LAG002]

Limitations: zero participation only shows the activities were not observed in the later sample, so the evidence cannot distinguish the block having been removed from the branch now always being skipped, and the single later case containing A20 shows occasional execution cannot be excluded; the many agreeing items also largely restate one underlying difference rather than confirming it independently. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the block still executes under conditions that did not arise in the later 200-case window, rather than being gone from the process altogether. [OCC002, POS003]

Other differences: the distance from A24 to A31 moved closer — before, A31 followed A24 two positions later in about 59 of 100 cases and one position later in about 39; in the later window this is roughly reversed (about 59 at one position, 39 at two) — a shift the present evidence does not tie to the disappearing block. In addition, A12 and A24 each appear somewhat more often overall (roughly 42 to 51 and 49 to 56 in 100 cases). [LAG001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL008, REL013, REL018]
