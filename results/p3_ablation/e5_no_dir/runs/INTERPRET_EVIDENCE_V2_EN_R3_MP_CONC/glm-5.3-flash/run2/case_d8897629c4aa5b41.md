### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A14, A15 and A20 were not observed in any of the 200 traces; in the later window each occurs at most once per trace and appears in about 29, 41 and 30 in 100 traces respectively. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The three new activities never occur together: pairwise co-occurrence is zero in the later window, and their participation shares sum to essentially all traces, so exactly one of A14, A15 or A20 ran in nearly every later case. [REL001, REL002, REL006]

- The new activities sit mid-process (mean normalised position about 0.51 in the later window), and A22 remains present in every trace in both windows. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new alternative step was inserted into the sequence between A22 and A24. Before, A22, A23 and A24 executed back-to-back in every case, and A14, A15 and A20 were not observed. After, nearly every case executes exactly one of A14 (about 29 in 100 cases), A15 (about 41) or A20 (about 30) inside that interval — always after A22 and before A24 — placed before A23 in about 56 in 100 cases and after it in about 44. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the relational items show the placement and the mutual exclusivity of the three activities outright; the distance changes match the order splits almost exactly (the 56/44 split against A23 mirrors the 1-step/2-step splits of the two adjacent pairs, and the A22–A24 distance grows by exactly one step); and the screening qualified 22 control-flow items where about 0.25 would be expected if nothing had changed, with every item's difference exceeding about 995 in 1000 random re-splits. Limitations: many items describe the same underlying difference, so they are consistent rather than independent confirmations; zero participation earlier strictly means only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed before, so an extremely rare pre-existing occurrence cannot be excluded; and the varying order against A23 means the evidence cannot pin the new step to one fixed slot between A22 and A24.

Other differences: A22's mean event position shifted slightly earlier (about 0.48 to 0.46) while it stayed in every trace in both windows — a small change compatible with traces becoming one event longer; on its own a position shift does not establish that A22 itself moved. [POS004]
