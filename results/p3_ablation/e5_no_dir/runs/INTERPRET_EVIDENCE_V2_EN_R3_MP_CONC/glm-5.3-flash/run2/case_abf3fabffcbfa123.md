### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the process ran two mutually exclusive variants of roughly equal size: A27 was present in about 52 of 100 traces, while A32, A33 and A38 each appeared in about 49 of 100 traces, always together as a block and never together with A27 (co-occurrence near zero). In the later window, A27 appears in virtually every trace (199 of 200), and A32, A33 and A38 are each observed in only about 1 of 200 traces. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL028, REL029, REL030]
- The block sat between A26 and A28: earlier, the step gap from A26 to A28 was 4 steps in about 46 of 100 traces (block variant) and 2 steps in about 49 of 100 (A27 variant). In the later window the gap is almost always 2 steps (about 95 of 100), A14 and A26 precede A27 in about 98 of 100 traces (A15 usually precedes or interleaves with it), and A27 precedes A28 in about 97 of 100. [LAG003, REL009, REL013, REL017, REL021]
- A04 became much rarer and detached from A14: earlier it occurred in about 68 of 100 traces, almost always immediately after A14 (lag 1 in about 99 of 100 such pairs); in the later window it occurs in about 22 of 100 traces, sits later in the trace (mean relative position about 0.21 to 0.40) and follows A14 within one step in only about 72 of 100 pairs. [OCC002, POS001, LAG001, REL001]
- Participation in A02 and A08 rose modestly, from about 5 to 15 and about 5 to 21 of 100 traces respectively, on small absolute numbers (10 to 30 and 10 to 41 traces). [OCC001, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a mutually exclusive choice between two paths was resolved in favour of one: the path executing A32, A33 and A38 together between A26 and A28 is no longer observed, while A27 — previously executed in about half of the cases as the alternative to that block — now runs in virtually every case, preceded by A14, A15 and A26 and followed by A28. The change is shown directly by participation (A27 from about 52 to nearly 100 of 100 traces; A32, A33 and A38 from about 49 to about 0) and by relations (A27 and each of A32/A33/A38 were strictly alternatives before and are one-sided afterwards), and it is consistent with the A26-to-A28 step gap collapsing from a 2-or-4-step mixture to almost always 2 steps. The volume of qualifying evidence (53 items against roughly one expected if nothing had changed) cannot be explained by sampling variation alone. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the A32/A33/A38 path still exists but was simply not exercised in the later sample, since zero participation only means "not observed" in that window. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL022, REL023, REL024, LAG003]

Other differences: A04 became much rarer (about 68 to 22 of 100 traces) and, when it occurs, no longer immediately follows A14; participation in A02 and A08 rose modestly on small numbers. These are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC002, POS001, LAG001, OCC001, OCC003]
