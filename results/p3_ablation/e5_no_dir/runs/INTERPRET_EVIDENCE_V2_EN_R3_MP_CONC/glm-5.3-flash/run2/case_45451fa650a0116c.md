### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a flip between two three-activity blocks. In the earlier window, A24, A25 and A26 each ran in about 82 in 100 traces and always together, while A21, A22 and A23 each ran in only about 19–20 in 100 traces. In the later window this reversed: A21, A22 and A23 each ran in about 79–80 in 100 traces (together in about 79 in 100), while A24, A25 and A26 each ran in only about 21–22 in 100. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL017, REL038]
- The two blocks behave as alternatives in both windows: an activity from A21–A23 and one from A24–A26 practically never share a trace (co-occurrence about 1 in 100 or less before and after), and within each block the activities still occur together whenever they occur at all. [REL017, REL019, REL026, REL032, REL038]
- A20 occurs in every trace in both windows and precedes whichever block runs: before, A20 came before A24 in about 80 in 100 traces; after, it came before A21, A22 or A23 in about 77–78 in 100. Within each block both orders appear in similar proportions (for example, after the change A21 precedes A22 in about 41 in 100 co-occurrences and A22 precedes A21 in about 38 in 100), so neither block has a fixed internal order. [REL011, REL014, REL017, REL025]
- A06 fell from about 46 in 100 traces to about 12 in 100, and when it occurs it now sits much earlier in the trace (mean relative position about 0.92 before versus about 0.52 after). Before, A06 almost always co-occurred with A20 and usually with the A24–A26 block; after, traces typically contain A20 without A06 (about 88 in 100). [OCC003, POS001, REL001, REL005]
- A02 and A04 became more frequent but remain minority activities: A02 rose from about 8 to 17 in 100 traces, A04 from about 5 to 19 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002]
- The A34–A35 sequence tightened: A34 was immediately followed by A35 in about 56 in 100 pairs before versus about 92 in 100 after; A34 itself runs in every trace in both windows, at a slightly later and more variable position after. [LAG006, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Main hypothesis: the routing between two alternative blocks after A20 flipped. Before, the block A24–A25–A26, executed together in either internal order, followed A20 in about 82 in 100 cases while the block A21–A23 was taken in about 19 in 100; in the later window the shares are about 79 in 100 for A21–A23 and about 21 in 100 for A24–A26. Because both blocks are well observed in both windows (roughly 38–163 supporting traces per activity per window), remain mutually exclusive, and keep their internal co-occurrence, this reads as the same two existing paths being taken with different frequencies rather than activities being added, removed or reordered. The volume of qualifying evidence far exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 67 control-flow items qualified against fewer than 1 expected without change, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows draw different case mixes (for example different variant proportions) rather than a change in routing within the same cases. [OCC004, OCC007, REL011, REL019]

Other differences: A06's participation dropped from about 46 to 12 in 100 traces with an earlier position when present, A02 and A04 became modestly more common, and A34 is now almost always immediately followed by A35; these are not part of the main hypothesis.
