### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A28, A30 and A31 each appeared in about 49 in 100 cases, and A32, A33 and A34 in about 52 in 100 cases; in the later window, each of the six appeared in about 99–100 in 100 cases, exactly once per case in both windows (no repetitions in either window) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006].

In the later window, every cross-trio pair (A28/A30/A31 with A32/A33/A34) co-occurred in about 98 in 100 cases, and in essentially all joint occurrences the A28/A30/A31 activity came first, the reverse order being absent [REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Forty-seven control-flow items qualified, where about 0.44 would be expected by chance for the 89 tested units, and every item's difference exceeded about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data; a volume this far above the chance expectation cannot be explained by sampling variation alone [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006].

Main hypothesis: the evidence supports the possibility that the earlier exclusive choice between two three-activity groups was replaced by both groups executing in sequence in almost every case. In the earlier window, a case contained either A28, A30 and A31 together (about 49 in 100 cases) or A32, A33 and A34 together (about 52 in 100), never both; in the later window, nearly every case runs A25, then A28/A30/A31 in mixed order, then A32/A33/A34 in mixed order, then A26 [OCC001, OCC004, REL013, REL015, REL025, REL001, REL007].

Supporting signs: the change is shown directly and consistently by all nine cross-trio relations, which went from never co-occurring to co-occurring in about 98 in 100 cases with a single fixed order between the groups [REL015, REL019, REL022]; the growth of the A25→A32 and A28→A26 distances by about three positions matches the other trio being placed in between [LAG001, LAG004]; and the participation and position shifts of all six activities, plus the stable anchoring roles of A25 and A26, point the same way [OCC001, OCC006, POS003, POS006, REL001, REL007].

The evidence cannot rule out that a small share of later cases (about 2 in 100) still runs only one of the groups, nor determine whether the order inside each group is genuinely unconstrained rather than fixed in a way this sample cannot resolve; it also covers only the tested units, so changes elsewhere in the process would not be visible here [REL013, REL015].
