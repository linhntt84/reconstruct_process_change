### Observed differences

- Three activities — A19, A20 and A25 — appear in none of the 200 earlier traces, but in the later window A19 occurs in about 58 of 200 traces (29%), A20 in about 82 (41%) and A25 in about 61 (30%), each roughly once per trace in which it appears and essentially never repeated. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The three are mutually exclusive: A19 and A20 never share a trace, and A19/A25 and A20/A25 co-occur in only about 1 in 200 traces each. Together they cover essentially every later-window trace (about 99 in 100), so each later trace contains exactly one of the three. [REL001, REL002, REL006]
- The new activities sit near the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.52). They consistently follow A27 (A27 comes first in all A19 traces, about 99 in 100 A20 traces and about 95 in 100 A25 traces) and almost always precede A29 (in about 96–100 in 100 of the relevant traces). Relative to A28 the order is mixed: the new step comes before A28 in about 55 in 100 later traces and after it in about 44 in 100. A27, A28 and A29 themselves occur in every trace in both windows. [POS001, POS002, POS003, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- The distances between the established activities grew accordingly. Earlier, A27, A28 and A29 ran consecutively: A27–A28 were one step apart in about 98 in 100 traces and A28–A29 one step apart in about 98 in 100. In the later window, A27–A28 are two steps apart in about 52 in 100 traces, A28–A29 two steps apart in about 41 in 100, and A27–A29 three steps apart in about 92 in 100 (four steps in about 7 in 100) — consistent with one extra activity inserted between A27 and A29. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Minor positional shifts accompany this: A29's mean relative position moved slightly later (about 0.55 to 0.57) and A27's slightly earlier (about 0.48 to 0.46). [POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a new step was inserted between A27 and A29 — a stretch where A27, A28 and A29 previously followed one another directly — and that this step is executed as exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities: A19 in about 29 in 100 traces, A20 in about 41 in 100 and A25 in about 30 in 100, placed after A27, almost always before A29, and slightly more often before than after A28. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the three activities go from no occurrences in 200 earlier traces to 58–82 of 200 later traces, a magnitude that sampling differences in a stable process cannot plausibly produce; the relational items show their placement and mutual exclusivity directly; and the step-distance growth between A27, A28 and A29 matches a single insertion. The screening comparison also supports this: 23 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.33 expected if nothing had changed — about 70 times the chance expectation. Limitations: positions and distances are measured in event positions, not clock time, so no timing claim is possible, and the evidence cannot show whether the inserted step replaced earlier behaviour carried out under other activity names, although nothing was observed between A27 and A29 in the earlier window beyond A28 itself. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

An important competing reading the evidence cannot fully rule out is that A19, A20 and A25 are three separate optional activities that happen never to coincide, rather than one choice point with three variants; their near-zero overlap combined with coverage of essentially every later trace makes the one-of-three reading more consistent, but it remains an interpretation. [REL001, REL002, REL006]
