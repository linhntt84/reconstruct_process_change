- In the earlier window, A33, A35 and A36 each ran in about 49 in 100 traces and A37, A38 and A39 each in about 52 in 100; in the later window, all six run in about 99–100 in 100 traces. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the two alternative activity sets became one jointly executed block: in the earlier window {A33, A35, A36} and {A37, A38, A39} ran as alternatives, each in about half of the traces and never together, while in the later window all six activities run in about 98–100 in 100 traces, with A33/A35/A36 before A37/A38/A39 in about 97–98 in 100 of them, no fixed order inside each set, and the block now effectively mandatory and sitting 4–5 event positions after A30 and A31 instead of 1–3. [REL039, REL046, OCC004, OCC007, LAG003, LAG006]

The change is shown directly by relational evidence (cross-set co-occurrence from 0 to about 98 in 100 traces across all six cross-pairs) and is consistent across participation, order and distance indicators; the 79 flagged items are roughly 100 times the 0.78 expected by chance for 156 tested units, so chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole — though many items describe the same underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [REL039, REL041, REL046, OCC004, OCC009, LAG003, LAG008]

Limitations: positions and lags use event order rather than clock time, so the larger distance shows relative placement only; and the evidence cannot distinguish a modified routing rule from a changed mix of trace variants between the windows (for example, an after-window dominated by cases that always ran the full block), since it shows co-occurrence and order but not the underlying mechanism. [LAG003, POS006]

Other differences: A04 participates less (about 30 to 14 in 100 traces) and now occurs only alongside the block, whereas it previously often occurred without it; A06 and A10 occur more often on few observations; A15/A16 interleave with the block in about 40 in 100 traces (previously 15–18); A14's position became more regular. [OCC001, REL001, OCC002, OCC003, REL013, POS002]
