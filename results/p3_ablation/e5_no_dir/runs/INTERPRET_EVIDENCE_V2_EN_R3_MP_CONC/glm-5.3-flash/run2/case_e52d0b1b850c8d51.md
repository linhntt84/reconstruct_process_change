### Observed differences

- Three activities that ran in every case in the earlier window became intermittent in the later window: A19, A20 and A25 each appeared in 100 in 100 earlier traces but in only about 53–54 in 100 later traces (107–108 of 200 traces). [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

A specific hypothesis supported by the evidence: in the later window the segment formed by A19, A25 and A20 — which ran in every earlier trace, between A41 and A26/A46/A47 — is optional, being skipped as a complete group in about 46 in 100 traces and executed in full, in the same internal order, in about 53 in 100. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL016, REL017, REL022]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the participation drop is large and backed by 200 → 107–108 traces per activity; the relation profiles show the three activities are skipped jointly rather than individually; the surrounding activities keep full participation and their order, so this is not a replacement of one path by another; and the A41→A26 distance splitting between 1 and 4 steps matches the skip-versus-execute split almost exactly. In total 49 control-flow items qualified against fewer than one (0.64) expected for an unchanged process, so chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole, and every item's difference exceeds those of about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL016, REL017, REL022, REL018, REL019, LAG008]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the skipping traces all follow one specific shorter route, nor whether the skip option is new or was a variant too rare to appear among the 200 earlier traces — a pre-existing skip rate of more than a couple in 100 would almost certainly have shown up there. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is a shift in the mix of two pre-existing variants (with and without the segment); the screening comparison makes a pure chance artefact unlikely but cannot exclude a variant present at well under about 1 in 100 earlier traces. [REL016, LAG008, OCC005]

Other differences:
- A06 declined from about 71 to about 26 in 100 traces, sits later when it occurs, and its former tight couplings to A14 and to the segment dissolved; the evidence does not establish whether A06 belongs to the same rerouting as the segment or changed separately. [OCC002, POS001, REL001, REL003, LAG003]
- A04, A08 and A10 occur in about 15–20 in 100 later traces versus about 4–5 earlier; these increases rest on modest counts (29, 40 and 31 traces) and their placement in the process is not shown by these items. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]
