### Observed differences

- The block of activities A20, A22, A23, A24, A25 and A26 — each present in every trace in both windows — changed from a mixed or often reversed order to a fixed one. In the earlier window A24 preceded A20 in about 73 in 100 traces and A24 preceded A23 in about 88 in 100, while A20/A26, A22/A24 and A23/A25 appeared in both orders roughly equally often; in the later window A20, A22 and A23 precede A24, A25 and A26 in about 96–99 in 100 traces for every tested pair. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the activities A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25 and A32 (the first seven present in every trace, A32 in nearly all) are executed in one fixed sequence, consistent with A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32, whereas in the earlier window the same activities appeared in varying orders in which A24, A25 and A26 frequently preceded A20, A22 and A23. The change is shown directly by the pair-order shares flipping to about 96–99 in 100 in one direction and by lag distributions collapsing to single fixed distances that all agree with one chain; the position shifts (A20/A22/A23 earlier, A24/A26 later) point the same way. With 67 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.76 expected for an unchanged process, the pattern as a whole is far beyond what random re-sampling produces, and every item's difference also exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits. Limitations: all indicators are position-based, so no claim is made about clock timing, and the evidence cannot distinguish a reordering within a single process variant from a shift in the mix of existing variants towards the new order. [REL014, REL019, LAG006, LAG008, LAG010, LAG031, LAG032, LAG033, LAG034, LAG036, POS002, POS005]

Other differences: A08 participation fell from about 72 to about 29 in 100 traces and, where present, it now occurs later and more variably and no longer reliably one step before A16; A10 participation rose from about 8 to about 22 in 100 traces (15 versus 44 traces, so modestly supported). Whether these shifts belong to the same underlying change as the reordering or are independent cannot be determined from this evidence. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, LAG001]
