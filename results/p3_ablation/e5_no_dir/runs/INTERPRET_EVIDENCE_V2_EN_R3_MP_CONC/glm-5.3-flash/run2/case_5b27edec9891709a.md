### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the segment between A21 and A26. Before, every trace ran a single path: A20, then the block A31/A32/A37 in mixed order, then A23 and A21, then A22, then A26, with all of these mandatory. In the later window, that segment is filled either by A22 alone (about 53 in 100 traces) or by the block A31/A32/A37 (about 47 in 100): the block appears to have moved from between A20 and A23 to after A21, and A22 and the block have become mutually exclusive, each skipped in roughly half the traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL006, REL011, REL014, LAG001, LAG005]

The support is direct and consistent: the order reversals around the block, the collapse of the A20–A23 distance from 4 steps to adjacency, the A21–A26 distance splitting into 2 and 4 steps, and the co-occurrence drop are relational observations with roughly 94–200 traces behind each, and every item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits. The 42 qualifying control-flow items also far exceed the roughly 0.37 expected for an unchanged process, so the pattern as a whole is not what chance re-splits of a stable process produce. [REL006, REL011, LAG001, LAG005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Limitations: the exact spacing of the relocated block in the later window rests on lag items whose after-side support is a single trace each, so the precise placement of A31, A32 and A37 relative to each other is only weakly confirmed. Also, because the block appeared in every before-window trace, the evidence cannot show whether the block-only path is new or existed earlier at a rate below about 1 in 200 traces. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, REL011]

Other differences: the remaining signals are the small shifts in relative event position of the unchanged activities noted above, which are consistent with the changed trace composition rather than independent moves; no further structural, participation or ordering difference appears at these thresholds. [POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005]
