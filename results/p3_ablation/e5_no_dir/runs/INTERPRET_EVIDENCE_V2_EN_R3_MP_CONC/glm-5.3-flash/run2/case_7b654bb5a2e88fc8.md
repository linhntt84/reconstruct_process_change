### Observed differences

- The three branch activities A14, A15 and A20 — exactly one of which appears per case, since their shares sum to about 100 in 100 traces in both windows — kept roughly the same participation but moved much later in the trace: in the earlier window they sat at a mean relative position of about 0.31–0.33, in the later window at about 0.59–0.60, a shift of roughly 0.27–0.29. Their presence changed only modestly (A14 in 76 of 200 traces before vs 61 after; A15 61 vs 65; A20 63 vs 74). [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- In the earlier window, each branch activity always ran before A21 and before A22 whenever they co-occurred (e.g., A14 before A21 in all 38 in 100 co-occurring traces, A20 before A21 in all 32 in 100); in the later window this reversed completely, with A21 and A22 always running first (e.g., A21 before A14 in all 31 in 100, A21 before A20 in all 37 in 100). Co-occurrence itself stayed the same — the branch step still always accompanies the mandatory block. [REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007]
- Against A23 the fixed order became mixed: before, A14 and A20 always preceded A23; in the later window A14 preceded A23 in about 16 in 100 traces and followed it in about 15 in 100, and A20 preceded it in about 15 and followed it in about 22. [REL003, REL008]
- Pair distances show a one-position insertion: A22 to A23 was always 1 step apart before and is now 1 step in about 52 in 100 traces and 2 steps in about 48; A23 to A27 was always 1 step and is now 1 step in about 48 and 2 steps in about 52; A22 to A27 was always 2 steps and is now always 3 — exactly one extra event sits in the A22–A27 span in every later trace. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- A40 to A21 shortened from always 2 steps to always 1 step, consistent with the event that used to sit between them having left that position. [LAG004]
- The mandatory activities A21, A22 and A23 occur in every trace in both windows and shifted only slightly earlier (mean relative position about 0.35 to 0.32 for A21, 0.58 to 0.55 for A22, 0.61 to 0.59 for A23). [POS004, POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the once-per-case branch step — one of A14, A15 or A20 — changed its placement: it previously executed before A21, near the first third of the trace, and in the later window it executes after A21 and A22, landing around A23 inside the A22–A27 span (just before A23 in roughly half of the later traces and just after it in the other half, as directly observed for A14 and A20). This is shown directly and consistently: a large position shift for the branch activities with almost none for A21–A23, a complete order reversal against A21 and A22 at unchanged co-occurrence, a mixed order against A23, and matching distance changes of one added position between A22 and A27 and one removed position between A40 and A21. The 18 qualifying control-flow items are about 75 times the roughly 0.24 expected by chance, and every item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of an unchanged process produce. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Limitations: the items are pairwise views of one underlying relocation rather than independent confirmations; A15's order against A23 is not directly observed (it is inferred from its position and its reversal against A21 and A22); and the evidence cannot rule out that in some traces the extra event between A22 and A27 is something other than the branch step, or that composition changes elsewhere in the trace contribute to the position shifts.

Other differences: the branch mix shifted somewhat — A14 from about 38 to 31 in 100 traces and A20 from about 32 to 37 — a difference of a few cases that two 200-trace samples can also produce without any change. [POS001, POS002, POS003]
