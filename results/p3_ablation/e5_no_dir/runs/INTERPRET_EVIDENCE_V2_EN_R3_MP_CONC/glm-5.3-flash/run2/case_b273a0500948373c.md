### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, about 82 in 100 traces contained A19, A20 and A21, which always ran together as one block, while A16, A17 and A18 appeared together in only about 19 in 100 traces. In the later window these shares reversed: A16–A18 ran together in about 79 in 100 traces and A19–A21 in about 21 in 100. Every trace contained exactly one of the two blocks in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL009, REL014, REL018]
- The two blocks are mutually exclusive in both windows (A16 never co-occurs with A19, A20 or A21, before or after), while membership within each block is unchanged: A16 and A17 co-occur in about 19 in 100 traces before and about 79 in 100 after; A19 and A20 co-occur in about 82 in 100 before and about 21 in 100 after. [REL007, REL009, REL013, REL022]
- The blocks' placement is unchanged. A15 occurs in every trace in both windows and always precedes whichever block runs; A25 also occurs in every trace, never precedes the block, and mostly follows it, with a minority of interleaved/repeated cases in both windows (for A16 and A25: about 68 in 100 joint appearances sequential before, about 57 in 100 after). [REL001, REL004, REL012, REL024]
- Order inside each block shows no fixed sequence in either window: A16 precedes A17 in about 46 in 100 of their joint appearances before and about 53 in 100 after; A19 precedes A20 in about 58 in 100 before and about 52 in 100 after. [REL007, REL013, REL022, REL025]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two alternative paths between A15 and A25 swapped frequencies: before, the path through A19, A20 and A21 (always executed together) was taken in about 82 in 100 traces and the path through A16, A17 and A18 in about 19 in 100; in the later window the path through A16–A18 was taken in about 79 in 100 traces and the path through A19–A21 in about 21 in 100. The composition of each block, its mixed internal order and its position after A15 and before A25 are unchanged, so this is a re-weighting of existing alternatives rather than activities being added, removed, reordered or bypassed. [OCC001, OCC004, REL001, REL004, REL009, REL012, REL022, REL024]

The screening comparison supports this reading: 33 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.41 would be expected if nothing had changed (about 80 times the chance expectation), and all of them describe the same complementary shift of about 0.61 on the participation/co-occurrence scale, with both blocks backed by substantial observations in each window (37–163 traces per profile). [OCC001-OCC006, REL001-REL027]

An important competing explanation remains: the two windows may simply contain different mixes of case types, each routed to a different block, rather than a change in the routing itself; the post-change shares (about 79 and 21 in 100) are also estimates from 200 traces per window. [OCC001, OCC004, REL009]

Other differences: none — every qualifying item reflects the same swap between the two blocks. [OCC001-OCC006, REL001-REL027]
