### Observed differences

- A27 was executed in about 52 in 100 traces in the earlier window (103 of 200) and in nearly all traces in the later window (199 of 200), about once per trace and essentially never repeated [OCC001].
- A32, A33 and A38 each appeared in about 49 in 100 traces in the earlier window (97–98 of 200) but in only about 1 in 200 traces in the later window [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004].
- In the earlier window, A27 and each of A32/A33/A38 were strict alternatives — they never occurred in the same trace — while A32, A33 and A38 ran together as a block in about 48–49 in 100 traces, with no fixed order among the three (each ordering roughly 24–25 in 100) and always before A28 [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL009, REL010, REL011].
- In the later window, A26 precedes A27 in about 96–97 in 100 traces, A27 sits immediately before A28 (a one-step gap in essentially all cases, up from about 97 in 100), and A26, A27 and A28 co-occur in about 199 of 200 traces [REL001, REL005, LAG004].
- The step distance from A26 to A28 collapsed from a mix of two steps (about 50 in 100 traces) and four steps (about 46 in 100) in the earlier window to almost always two steps (about 97 in 100) in the later window, consistent with the three-activity block being replaced by the single A27 [LAG001].
- A28 occurs in every trace in both windows; its mean relative position moved slightly earlier, from about 0.55 to about 0.53 of the trace [POS001].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch formed by A32, A33 and A38 — previously executed as a block between A26 and A28 in about half of the traces — is in the later window almost never executed, while the alternative branch through A27, previously taken in about half of the traces, is now taken in virtually every trace, so that traces run A26 → A27 → A28 with A27 directly before A28 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL006, LAG001, LAG004].

The participation changes are direct and well supported on both sides (about 97–103 supporting traces before versus 1 after for A32/A33/A38, and 103 versus 199 for A27); the relational items show the before-window mutual exclusivity turning into near-universal co-occurrence of A26, A27 and A28, and the A26–A28 gap consolidating at two steps; and with 31 qualifying control-flow items against 0.37 expected without change, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole [OCC002, REL001, LAG001, screening].

The evidence cannot rule out that the A32/A33/A38 branch still exists but is chosen in a small residual share of cases (about 1 in 200 later traces), and since no direct successor/direction evidence is present, the exact wiring around the dropped block is inferred from co-occurrence and step distances rather than observed edges; several items also describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations [OCC002, REL012].

Other differences: none beyond the shift described above; the slight forward shift of A28's mean position is a consequence of the earlier block no longer preceding it in half of the before-window traces [POS001].
