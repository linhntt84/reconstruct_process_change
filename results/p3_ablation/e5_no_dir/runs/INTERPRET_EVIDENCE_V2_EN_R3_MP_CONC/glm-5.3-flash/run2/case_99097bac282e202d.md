### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a swap between two mutually exclusive activity groups. In the earlier window, A21, A22 and A23 each appeared in about 75 in 100 traces, while A24, A25 and A26 each appeared in about 25 in 100; in the later window this reversed, with A21–A23 at about 25 in 100 (49–50 of 200 traces) and A24–A26 at about 75–76 in 100 (151–152 of 200). [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]
- The two groups were alternatives in both windows, not replacements for one another: cross-group pairs such as A21 with A24, or A23 with A26, practically never occur in the same trace in either window (co-occurrence about 1 in 100 or less). Before, about 75 in 100 traces contained an A21–A23 activity without an A24–A26 activity; after, about 75 in 100 contained an A24–A26 activity without an A21–A23 activity. [REL015, REL016, REL017, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL028, REL029, REL030]
- Within-group co-occurrence flipped in step: A21, A22 and A23 ran together in about 75 in 100 traces before versus about 25 in 100 after, while A24, A25 and A26 ran together in about 25 in 100 before versus about 75 in 100 after. [REL013, REL014, REL021, REL034, REL035, REL039]
- A20 and A30 occur in essentially every trace in both windows and anchor the choice. A20 preceded an A21–A23 activity in about 74 in 100 traces before but only about 25 in 100 after, and preceded an A24–A26 activity in about 25 in 100 before versus about 74 in 100 after; A30 likewise shifted from co-occurring mainly with the A21–A23 group to mainly with the A24–A26 group, without a fixed order to the branch activities. [REL007, REL008, REL010, REL011, REL018, REL025, REL031, REL036, REL040, REL043]
- A10 became more frequent and more tightly placed: it appeared in about 6 in 100 traces before (11 of 200) versus about 21 in 100 after (42 of 200), and its mean relative position moved later, from about 0.57 to about 0.82, with the spread narrowing (standard deviation about 0.32 to 0.15). [OCC002, POS001]
- Smaller shifts: A02 fell from 18 to 2 traces (about 9 in 100 to 1 in 100, based on few observations); event-position gaps between A20 and A24 and between A25 and A30 redistributed toward larger lags; and A32 immediately preceded A33 in about 56 in 100 cases before versus about 39 in 100 after, with two-step gaps rising to about 14 in 100 (event positions, not clock time). [OCC001, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two existing alternative paths swapped how often they are taken: the branch in which A21, A22 and A23 run together (after A20, alongside the ever-present A30) fell from roughly three of four traces to roughly one of four, while the alternative branch containing A24, A25 and A26 rose from roughly one of four to roughly three of four. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]

The shift is shown directly and consistently: the six branch activities moved by about 50 percentage points in opposite directions, the groups remained mutually exclusive in both windows (so the branches themselves still exist and only their shares changed), and the relational flips around the always-present A20 and A30 match the same picture. [REL007, REL010, REL015, REL022, REL028, REL034, REL036] Screening also found 58 qualifying items where only about 0.74 would be expected if nothing had changed, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. Limitations: most items describe one underlying flip and are not independent confirmations; routing is inferred from co-occurrence and order relations rather than direct successor evidence; and the evidence shows the reversed shares themselves, not what determines the branch choice.

Other differences:
- A10 occurs in about three times as many traces and sits later and more consistently within the trace (mean relative position about 0.57 to 0.82). [OCC002, POS001]
- A02 was observed in only 2 of 200 traces in the later window versus 18 before — a weak signal given the few observations. [OCC001]
- The A32-to-A33 distance spread out: immediate succession fell from about 56 in 100 to about 39 in 100, with two-step gaps rising to about 14 in 100. [LAG003]
- Position gaps between A20 and A24 and between A25 and A30 shifted toward larger lags, largely accompanying the branch swap. [LAG001, LAG002]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the flip originates in an upstream routing decision or case-mix difference arising before A20, outside the activities covered by these indicators. [REL007, REL010, REL013, REL034]
