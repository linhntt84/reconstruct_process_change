### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A35, A36 and A41 each ran before A26 and A27 in about 98–100 in 100 traces; in the later window this fixed order is reversed, with A26 and A27 running before each of A35, A36 and A41 in about 97–99 in 100 traces. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the relative execution order of two groups of activities was reversed: A35, A36 and A41, which previously ran before A26 and A27 in nearly every trace, now run after them. This is shown directly by all six cross-pairs flipping from one fixed order to the other with full co-occurrence in both windows, and corroborated by the position of each of the five activities shifting in the matching direction and by the distance changes around A25, A27, A26 and A30. With 26 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.5 expected by chance when nothing changed, the ensemble is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG005, LAG009, screening]

The relational evidence establishes the reversal of order between the groups but cannot show whether A35/A36/A41 moved later as one block, A26/A27 moved earlier, or other unobserved changes in the surrounding sequence produced the shift; several lag items (involving A06, A16, A25, A36 and A41, with only 1–11 supporting observations in one window) were therefore not used as primary support. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012]

Other differences: A06 participation fell from about 27 to about 13 in 100 traces with a later position, and A02 participation rose from about 5 to about 12 in 100 traces (small counts); these are separate from the order reversal. [OCC002, POS001, OCC001]
