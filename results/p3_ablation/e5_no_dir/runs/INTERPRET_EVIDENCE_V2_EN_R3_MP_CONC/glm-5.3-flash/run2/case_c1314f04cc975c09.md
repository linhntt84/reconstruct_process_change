### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A25, A26 and A31 each ran exactly once per trace: mean of 1.0 occurrence, present in all 200 traces, and never repeated within a trace. In the later window, each of the three occurs about 3.5 times per trace on average and repeats within a trace in roughly 68 in 100 cases, while still appearing in every trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The fixed order between A25 and A31 dissolved: earlier, the two always co-occurred and A25 preceded A31 in every trace; in the later window that strict order holds in only about 32 in 100 traces, while in about 68 in 100 the two activities are interleaved or repeated. [REL001]
- The tight spacing between them loosened: earlier, A31 followed A25 exactly two event positions later in all observed pairs; in the later window only about 12 in 100 pairs keep that two-step gap and about 5 in 100 sit around five steps apart, so the once-universal fixed spacing has largely disappeared. [LAG001]
- The positions of A25 and A31 within the trace became more spread out: the positional standard deviation roughly doubled for both (for A25 from about 0.06 to 0.12), with slightly later mean positions but medians that moved only marginally. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A25, A26 and A31 became repeated activities: previously executed exactly once per trace in a fixed sequence (A25 always before A31, two positions apart), they are now executed about 3.5 times per trace, repeating within a trace in roughly two thirds of traces, and the executions of A25 and A31 interleave or repeat instead of keeping one fixed order. This is stated as a possibility supported by the evidence, not as a confirmed modification. Chance is an unlikely explanation for the pattern as a whole: seven items qualified where fewer than one would be expected for an unchanged process, and the repetition increase is large in absolute terms (from never repeated to about 68 in 100 traces over 200-trace windows). The signs are direct and mutually consistent — unchanged participation with rising occurrences and recurrence, the order change observed directly on the A25–A31 pair, the dissolution of the constant two-step gap, and the widening position distributions — though they all describe the same underlying behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, LAG001, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether A26 repeats together with A25 and A31 as one jointly repeated block, because no relational item covers A26; its identical occurrence profile is consistent with, but does not prove, joint repetition. The relational category also cannot separate true alternation of A25 and A31 from mere co-occurring repetitions in mixed order. [OCC002, REL001]
