### Observed differences

- Three activities lost almost all participation: A19 ran in about 32 of 100 traces before (64 of 200) but in only 1 of 200 in the later window; A20 fell from about 28 of 100 (56 traces) to 1; A25 fell from about 40 of 100 (80 traces) to 1. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- Before, A19, A20 and A25 were mutually exclusive alternatives: no trace contained two of them, and their shares (32%, 28%, 40%) together cover essentially every trace, so exactly one of the three executed in each case. In the later window, about 99 of 100 traces contain none of them. [REL001, REL002, REL006, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A27, A28 and A29 occur in every trace in both windows. Earlier, each of A19, A20 and A25 always co-occurred with these three whenever it ran, sitting after A27 and before A29 at a varying position around A28; in the later window their co-occurrence with A27, A28 and A29 dropped to about 1 in 200 traces. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012]
- The A27–A28–A29 stretch became tightly consecutive: A27 followed by A28 at distance 1 rose from about 54 of 100 to about 99 of 100; A27 followed by A29 moved from typically three steps apart (about 94 of 100) to two steps (about 97 of 100); A28 followed by A29 at distance 1 rose from about 45 of 100 to about 98 of 100 — the pattern expected if the single optional event that previously sat between them disappeared. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the choice step formed by the mutually exclusive activities A19, A20 and A25 — exactly one of which executed in virtually every earlier trace, between A27 and A29 — is no longer executed, so that A27, A28 and A29 now run directly one after another. The change is shown directly and consistently: participation collapsed from 56–80 traces to a single trace for each of the three activities, their co-occurrence with the ever-present A27, A28 and A29 disappeared, and the distances between A27, A28 and A29 shrank by about one step, matching the loss of one intervening event. The volume of qualifying evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process produces (22 items against roughly 0.3 expected by chance). A limitation: absence of participation only means the activities were not observed, so the evidence cannot distinguish their removal from the process from their being skipped at execution time, nor rule out that an untested replacement activity took their place. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL003, REL005, REL010, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Other differences: A29's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (about 0.56 to 0.55 of the trace), which is consistent with one preceding event having disappeared but is not independent of the main hypothesis; the after-window positions recorded for A19, A20 and A25 rest on one trace each and carry no weight.
