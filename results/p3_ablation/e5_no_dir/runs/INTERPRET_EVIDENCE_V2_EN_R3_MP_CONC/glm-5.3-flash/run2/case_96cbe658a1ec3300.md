### Observed differences

- The relative order of two groups of activities reversed. In the earlier window, A26 came before A35, A36 and A41 in about 98–99 of 100 traces, and A27 likewise came before each of them in about 98–99 of 100 traces. In the later window the opposite order holds: A35, A36 and A41 come before A26 in about 97–98 of 100 traces and before A27 in about 97–98 of 100 traces. Both activities of every pair still occur together in every trace in both windows, and the unordered/interleaved share stays at about 1–2 of 100, so participation and the fixed-order character are unchanged — only the direction of the order flipped. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Relative positions moved accordingly: in the later window A26 sits at about 0.62 of the trace (previously about 0.53) and A27 at about 0.47 (previously about 0.38), while A35, A36 and A41 each sit at about 0.42 (previously about 0.58). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- Distances to neighbouring activities changed as well: A27, which immediately followed A25 before (lag 1 in about 98–99 of 100 traces), now follows it 4–5 positions later (about 90 of 100 at lag 4, about 10 of 100 at lag 5); A30, which followed A26 about 4 positions later before (about 92–93 of 100), now immediately follows A26 (about 98–99 of 100 at lag 1). [LAG001, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Twenty-one control-flow items qualified, many times the fewer-than-one expected by chance in an unchanged process, so sampling variation cannot explain the evidence set as a whole [POS001, REL001, LAG001]. The evidence supports the possibility that the execution order of two groups of activities reversed: A26 and A27, which previously ran before A35, A36 and A41, now run after them, while both groups still occur in every trace, so no activity was added, removed or made optional. The reversal is shown directly by the relation indicators across all six activity pairs (about 97–99 of 100 traces change order with co-occurrence unchanged), and it agrees with the position shifts of all five activities and with the changed distances to A25 and A30 [REL001, REL004, POS001, POS003, LAG001, LAG003]. Limitations: positions and lags are measured in event positions, not clock time, so no statement about timing is possible; and the evidence cannot fully specify the new structure — the changed distances to A25 and A30 are compatible with a broader resequencing of the middle of the process rather than a clean exchange of the two groups, and the evidence cannot distinguish these forms [LAG001, LAG003].

Other differences: several lag profiles changed markedly but rest on only 1–2 observations in one window (for example A26→A35, A26→A36, A26→A41, A35→A30, A36→A30 and A41→A30 with about 200 observations before and 1–2 after, and A25→A35 and A35→A27 the reverse), so they are weakly supported; they are directionally consistent with the reordering above but do not independently confirm it. [LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]
