### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 were essentially unobserved (A19 and A25 in 0 of 200 traces, A20 in 1 of 200); in the later window they run in about 38, 30 and 35 in 100 cases respectively (76, 59 and 69 of 200 traces). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the later window the three behave as alternatives: A19 and A20 occur in the same trace in only about 1 in 100 cases, A19 and A25 in about 1.5 in 100, and A20 and A25 in fewer than 1 in 100, while each appears without the others in roughly 29–37 in 100 cases. Their shares sum to about 100 in 100 cases (slightly more allowing for the rare overlaps), and each occurs at most once per case, so a typical later case runs exactly one of the three. [REL001, REL002, REL005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the later window each of the three sits between A41 and A26: A41 runs before A19, A20 or A25 in about 38, 28 and 33 in 100 cases respectively, and A19, A20 or A25 runs before A26 in about 38, 29 and 33 in 100 cases. In the earlier window, A41 and A26 co-occurred in essentially every trace, and A19, A20 and A25 never appeared alongside either of them. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001]
- The spacing between A41 and A26 grew by about one step: earlier, A26 followed A41 one step later in about 83 in 100 traces (two steps in about 17); in the later window it follows two steps later in about 83 in 100 (three steps in about 14, one step in about 1). [LAG001]
- When they occur, the new activities sit at roughly 0.35–0.37 of the trace's relative position (medians about 0.35 for A19, 0.37 for A20, 0.36 for A25). [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- A26 occurs in every trace in both windows and its relative position moved only slightly later (median about 0.36 to about 0.39), consistent with an activity now running before it. [POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, cases execute one of three mutually exclusive activities — A19, A20 or A25 — between A41 and A26, whereas in the earlier window cases went from A41 directly to A26; a typical later case thus runs A41, then exactly one of A19/A20/A25, then A26. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

Supporting signs: 17 control-flow items qualified in screening against roughly 0.28 expected for an unchanged process (about 60 times the chance expectation), so sampling noise cannot account for the pattern as a whole; the items also agree on one picture — the three activities appearing from near zero, their near-perfect mutual exclusivity, their consistent placement after A41 and before A26, and the one-step growth of the A41–A26 distance. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether A19, A20 and A25 are entirely new or existed earlier at a very low rate (A20 appeared once in 200 earlier traces) and only became frequent, nor what determines which of the three a case runs; several items describe the same underlying behaviour and are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the block replaced behaviour that already occasionally sat between A41 and A26 (about 17 in 100 earlier traces had an extra step there) rather than being added to a uniformly direct A41–A26 step. [OCC002, LAG001]
