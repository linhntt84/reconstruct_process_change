### Observed differences

- In the later window, the three activities A19, A20 and A25 each run much later in the case: their mean relative position moved from about 0.31–0.34 of the trace to about 0.58–0.59, while their participation stayed roughly stable (A19 appears in about 38 of 100 traces before vs about 31 after, A20 about 32 vs 34, A25 about 32 vs 38). [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- Their order relative to A26 and A27 reversed. Earlier, whenever A19, A20 or A25 occurred they ran before A26 and before A27 (in about 30–38 of 100 traces, with the reverse order in at most about 1 of 100); in the later window that earlier order has nearly vanished (about 1 of 100 or less) and A26 and A27 now run before them in about 30–36 of 100 traces. [REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007]
- Relative to A28, a fixed order became mixed: earlier A19 and A25 ran before A28 in about 36 of 100 traces and never after; in the later window both orders occur in similar shares (about 15–16 vs 15–22 of 100). [REL003, REL008]
- Direct successions changed. A25 immediately followed by A26, seen 63 times earlier, is essentially gone later (one observation, at distance 5); A45 now directly precedes A26 (distance 1 in about 89 of 100 traces, previously distance 2 in about 84); the near-constant adjacencies A27→A28 and A28→A32 (distance 1 in about 96–98 of 100) are now split roughly half at distance 1 and half at distance 2; and the A27→A32 distance grew from 2 (about 96 of 100) to 3 (about 97 of 100). [LAG003, LAG008, LAG004, LAG007, LAG005]
- New adjacencies appear in the later window: A20 immediately precedes A28 in 35 of 36 observations and A28 immediately precedes A20 in 30 observations, and A20 reaches A32 within 1–2 steps in about 98 of 100 of 66 observations; earlier, each of these pairs was within five steps in only one observation. [LAG001, LAG006, LAG002]
- The positions of A26 and A27 themselves barely moved (mean relative position about 0.35 to 0.32 and about 0.58 to 0.55; both present in every trace in both windows). [POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the group of alternative activities A19, A20 and A25, each occurring in roughly a third of traces, moved from before A26 to the segment between A27/A28 and A32. Earlier the group ran at about a third of the case, before A26, A27 and A28, with A25 directly preceding A26 and A45 reaching A26 in two steps (fitting A45 → group → A26); in the later window the group runs at about three-fifths of the case, after A26 and A27, with one of its activities inserted between A27 and A32 (distance 3), sometimes directly between A27 and A28 and sometimes directly after A28 — which fits the mixed order versus A28 and the new A20–A28 adjacencies. The screening qualified 21 control-flow items where about 0.37 would be expected without a change, a count many times the chance expectation; the order reversal is observed directly, the position shift is confined to A19/A20/A25 while A26 and A27 stayed in place, and the succession changes match the same move, with all items showing unusually large differences (strength about 0.995). Limitations: three lag items (A20–A28, A20–A32, A28–A20) rest on a single before-window observation each, so the earlier distances to A28 and A32 are weakly grounded, and positions are event positions rather than clock time. Other differences: A26 and A27 show small backward shifts in mean relative position (about 0.03), consistent with the group's move rather than a separate change. [POS004, POS005]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the segment around A28 was restructured (for example A28 itself shifting relative to the group) rather than the three activities moving as one unit, since their order versus A28 became mixed instead of cleanly reversed.
