### Observed differences

- The relative order of two groups of activities reversed. In the earlier window the always-present activities A20, A22 and A23 ran before A25 and A29 (also always present), which ran before the partial-participation activities A19, A21 and A24; in the later window A19, A21 and A24 run first, then A25 and A29, then A20, A22 and A23. Where the pairs co-occur in every trace, the flip is near-deterministic: A20 preceded A25 in about 99 in 100 earlier traces but in 0 of 200 later traces, while A25 precedes A20 in all 200 later traces; A22 preceded A29 in all 200 earlier traces, and A29 now precedes A22 in about 98 in 100. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: the group A19, A21 and A24, which in the earlier window ran after the mandatory core of A20, A22, A23, A25 and A29, now runs before it, and within the core A25 and A29 now precede A20, A22 and A23 instead of following them — the earlier sequence A20/A22/A23 → A25/A29 → A19/A21/A24 became roughly A19/A21/A24 → A25/A29 → A20/A22/A23 in the later window. This rests on direct relational evidence — pairs that co-occur in every trace flip from near-deterministic one order to the opposite (A20–A25 from 98 in 100 A20-first to 100 in 100 A25-first) — together with consistent position shifts and distance changes, and with 48 qualifying evidence items where about 0.5 would be expected if nothing had changed (each item also exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, though such strength alone cannot rule out a rare variant). The many relation items largely describe this single reordering rather than independent confirmations, positions are event positions rather than clock times, and the evidence cannot distinguish a reordering within individual traces from a switch in the mix of two pre-existing order variants; the near-complete disappearance of the earlier order (0 of 200 later traces) makes chance sampling implausible but does not separate these two readings. [REL017, REL024, POS003, POS005, LAG004]

Other differences: A02 became much more frequent and is now placed early, after A40 and about two steps before A25 — a participation and placement change not covered by the reversal hypothesis, and one whose earlier-window picture rests on only 16 traces [OCC001, POS001, REL009, LAG001, LAG005]. A41 now repeats more often within traces [OCC002].
