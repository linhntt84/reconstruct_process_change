### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, activities A19, A20 and A25 were not observed at all (0 of 200 traces). In the later window they occur regularly: A19 in about 30 of 100 traces, A20 in about 41, and A25 in about 30, each executed once per case rather than repeated. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- The three are mutually exclusive alternatives: they practically never occur in the same trace, and in about 99 of 100 later traces exactly one of them runs. [REL001, REL002, REL006]
- When present, the new activities sit around the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.51 in the later window). [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- Their placement is consistent: they follow A27 in about 97–100 of 100 joint occurrences and precede A29 in about 95–100; relative to A28 the order is mixed, with the new activity before A28 in roughly 55 of 100 joint occurrences and after it in roughly 44. [REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, REL004, REL008, REL011]
- Distances between the surrounding activities grew: A27 and A29 were two steps apart in about 99 of 100 earlier traces and are three steps apart in about 97 of 100 later traces; the A27–A28 and A28–A29 distances, previously one step in about 99 of 100 traces, now split between one step (about 44 and 56 of 100) and two steps (about 55 and 44). A27, A28 and A29 themselves still occur in every trace in both windows. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS004, POS005]
- Minor shifts accompany this: A27's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (about 0.48 to 0.46) and A29's slightly later (about 0.55 to 0.57). [POS004, POS005]
- Participation in A10 rose from about 5 of 100 traces (10 of 200) to about 13.5 (27 of 200). [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that an additional step was inserted into the sequence between A27 and A29: in the later window almost every case executes exactly one of the three alternative activities A19, A20 or A25 (about 30, 41 and 30 of 100 cases respectively), placed after A27 and before A29, sometimes before and sometimes after A28, whereas in the earlier window none of the three appeared and A27, A28 and A29 ran back to back with A28 between them. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL003, REL005, LAG002]

The supporting signs agree across indicator families: appearance from zero participation, mid-trace positions, the exclusive one-of-three pattern, stable before/after relations to A27, A28 and A29, and the lengthening of the A27–A29 distance from two to three steps; A28 remains universal, so the new step was added alongside it rather than replacing it. The volume of flagged evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 24 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.31 expected by chance among 63 tested units, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [POS001, POS002, POS003, REL004, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG003]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window only shows that A19, A20 and A25 were not observed then, so a step that already existed but was never triggered in the earlier sample cannot be distinguished from a genuinely new step; positions and gaps are event indices, not clock times, so no timing conclusion is possible. The competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the three activities were pre-existing but dormant and only their execution frequency changed. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS001, LAG002]

Other differences: A10's participation increased from about 5 to about 13.5 of 100 traces; the evidence does not connect this to the inserted step. [OCC001]
