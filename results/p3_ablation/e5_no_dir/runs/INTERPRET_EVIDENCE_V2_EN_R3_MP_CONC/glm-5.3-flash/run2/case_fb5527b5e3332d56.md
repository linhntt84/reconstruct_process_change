### Observed differences

- Participation flipped around a choice between two branches. In the earlier window, A22 appeared in about 52 in 100 traces (103 of 200), while A27, A28 and A33 each appeared in about 49 in 100 (97 of 200). In the later window, A22 appears in essentially every trace (199 of 200), while A27, A28 and A33 each appear in a single trace (1 of 200). A21 and A23 are present in all traces in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS001]
- In the earlier window there were two alternative routes between A21 and A23: about 52 in 100 cases ran A21 → A22 → A23, with A23 two positions after A21; about 49 in 100 cases ran A21 → A27/A28/A33 → A23, with A23 four positions after A21 and the three middle activities in no fixed order (each pair of them co-occurred in about 49 in 100 cases, with both orders roughly 24–25 in 100 each). [REL001, REL005, LAG001, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- In the later window, the A22 route is taken in about 995 in 1000 cases and A23 sits two positions after A21 almost always; the A27/A28/A33 route survives in one trace, and A27, A28 and A33 are no longer seen together (in about 995 in 1000 cases neither pair occurs). [REL001, REL005, LAG001, REL012, REL013, REL014, OCC002]
- Before, A22 and each of A27, A28, A33 were strict alternatives — never in the same trace (co-occurrence 0), A22 alone in about 52 in 100 cases and the other alone in about 49 in 100; in the later window A22 alone accounts for about 995 in 1000 and the others for about 5 in 1000. [REL006, REL007, REL008]
- A minor shift: A23's mean relative position moved slightly earlier, from about 0.55 to about 0.53 of the trace, which matches the disappearance of the longer route rather than an independent relocation. The position and lag profiles for A27, A28 and A33 in the later window rest on one trace each and carry little weight. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the routing between two alternative paths between A21 and A23 changed: in the later window nearly all cases execute A21 → A22 → A23, while the branch in which A27, A28 and A33 run between A21 and A23 is skipped in almost every case — A22 effectively became a near-mandatory step and A27, A28 and A33 became near-skipped. This rests on direct participation changes of about 48 percentage points on both sides, each earlier-window estimate backed by roughly 100 traces; relational items showing the same switch (A21–A22 and A22–A23 co-occurrence rising from about 52 in 100 to essentially all, co-occurrence with A27/A28/A33 falling to near zero); and the A21→A23 distance collapsing from a mix of two and four steps to consistently two. Screening flagged 30 control-flow items where only about 0.31 would qualify by chance, so the pattern as a whole is far beyond what an unchanged process produces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL012, LAG001]

Limitations: the old branch still appeared once in the later window, so the evidence cannot distinguish a removed branch from one that is now only rarely triggered, and it cannot separate a change in routing from a changed mix of cases between the two windows. Items such as the A23 position shift and the A21→A23 lag are consequences of the same branch switch rather than independent confirmations. [OCC002, POS001, LAG001]
