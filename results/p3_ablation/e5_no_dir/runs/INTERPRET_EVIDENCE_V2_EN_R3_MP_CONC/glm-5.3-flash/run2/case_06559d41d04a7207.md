### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: in the earlier window, exactly one of A19, A20 or A21 ran there as alternatives (each in roughly a third of traces, always after A44 and before A29); in the later window that branch is no longer observed, and A26, A27 and A28 instead run together in every trace at that position, without a fixed order among themselves. The screening comparison supports this reading: 65 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected without change for 115 tested units means chance cannot explain the differences as a whole, and the key relations rest on direct relational evidence with strong support in both windows (for example, A44 before A26 and A26 before A29 in about 99–100 of 100 traces each).
