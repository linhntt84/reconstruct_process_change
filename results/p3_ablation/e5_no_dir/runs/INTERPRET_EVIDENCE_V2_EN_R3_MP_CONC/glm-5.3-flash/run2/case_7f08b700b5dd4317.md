### Observed differences

- Six activities — A33, A35, A36, A37, A38 and A39 — ran in every earlier trace (100% participation, about one occurrence per trace, never repeated). In the later window each of A33, A35 and A36 appears in only about 47 in 100 traces, and each of A37, A38 and A39 in about 56 in 100, still at most once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- In the later window these six split into two blocks that are almost never seen together: pairs inside {A33, A35, A36} still co-occur in about 47 in 100 traces (essentially whenever any of them runs, all three run), pairs inside {A37, A38, A39} in about 55–56 in 100, while pairs across the two blocks co-occur in only about 3 in 100 traces. Before, every one of these pairs co-occurred in 100% of traces. [REL013, REL014, REL018, REL025, REL026, REL027, REL015, REL019, REL022, REL024]
- Combining the above, a later trace runs {A33, A35, A36} only in about 44 in 100 cases, {A37, A38, A39} only in about 53 in 100, both blocks in about 3 in 100, and neither in virtually none. [REL015, REL025, OCC001, OCC004]
- The embedding of the block is unchanged: A30 still precedes each of the six, and each still precedes A31 whenever they co-occur, in both windows (about 99–100 in 100 co-occurring traces); the order within each block remains unfixed (both orders in similar shares) in both windows. [REL001, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL010, REL012, REL013, REL025]
- Step distances shrank as expected if one three-activity block is missing: before, A30 to A37/A38/A39 and A33/A35/A36 to A31 were typically 4–5 steps apart; in the later window these pairs are 1–3 steps apart. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG009]
- Relative positions shifted modestly: the median position of A33, A35 and A36 moved later (about 0.69 to about 0.74–0.76) and A39 slightly earlier (about 0.78 to 0.76); A30 moved later (0.63 to 0.69) and A31 earlier (0.85 to 0.83) — consistent with shorter traces rather than a relocation of activities. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the six-activity segment between A30 and A31, which was previously always executed in full (block {A33, A35, A36} first, then {A37, A38, A39}, with free order within each block), now works as an exclusive choice: in the later window a trace typically executes one of the two three-activity blocks between A30 and A31 and skips the other, with each block taken in roughly half the cases (about 44 vs 53 in 100, both in about 3 in 100). [OCC001, OCC004, REL013, REL015, REL025, LAG001, LAG004]

Supporting signs: the participation drop is direct and large (100% to about 47–56 in 100 for all six activities); the co-occurrence structure changed exactly along the block boundary (within-block co-occurrence preserved, cross-block collapsed from 100% to about 3 in 100); the order relative to A30 and A31 is unchanged; and the step-distance shrinkage matches the removal of one block of three. Screening qualified 50 control-flow items where about 0.56 would be expected if nothing had changed, so this volume of consistent behaviour cannot be explained by random re-splits of an unchanged process. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL013, REL015, REL025, LAG001, LAG004]

Limitations: there is no direct successor evidence, so the immediate edges created when a block is skipped (for example A30 leading straight to A37) are inferred from co-occurrence and step distances rather than observed transitions; the cross-group lag items rest on only 4–5 later observations each; and the evidence cannot rule out that the earlier window simply did not sample an already-existing one-block-only variant, nor show whether the choice between blocks depends on case characteristics. [LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011]

Other differences: none independent of the restructuring above; the small position shifts of A30 and A31 (median about 0.63 → 0.69 and 0.85 → 0.83) follow from the shorter later traces rather than forming a separate change. [POS001, POS002]
