### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A10 became a frequent, early-placed step of the main flow: in the earlier window it ran in about 19 in 100 cases around the middle of the trace with no fixed relation to the rest, while in the later window it runs in about 70 in 100 cases, early, directly after A14, and before the A20–A26/A31/A32 group in about 61 in 100 cases. The signs are direct and consistent across occurrence, position and pairwise-order measures, and the 65 qualifying items far exceed the fewer than one expected for an unchanged process, so sampling luck cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [OCC003, POS001, LAG002, REL003]

The evidence cannot distinguish a modified flow from a changed case mix (for example, a larger share of cases whose variant includes A10), the many A10 pair items largely restate the same participation change rather than confirming it independently, and positions and lags refer to event order rather than clock time. [OCC003, REL001, POS001]

Other differences: the fixed ordering inside the always-present block A20, A22, A23, A24, A25, A26 was reversed or relaxed into mixed orders, with fixed gaps spreading into ranges — a change in ordering constraints rather than activities appearing or disappearing; A04 and A08 became rarer (about 21 to 10.5 and 19.5 to 4 in 100 traces); minor position shifts and tighter A45/A47→A48 gaps appeared elsewhere. These are separate from the main hypothesis about A10. [REL015, REL020, LAG006, OCC001, OCC002, LAG031]
