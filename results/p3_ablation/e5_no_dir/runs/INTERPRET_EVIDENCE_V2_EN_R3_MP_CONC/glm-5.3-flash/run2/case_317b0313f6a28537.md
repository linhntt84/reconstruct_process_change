### Observed differences

- The three activities A19, A20 and A25, each present in about a third of traces (roughly 31–39 in 100 before, 32–36 in 100 after), run much earlier in the later window: their average relative position falls from about 0.60 of the trace (60% through) to about 0.32–0.34 (about a third through). [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- Their order relative to A26 and A27, which occur in every trace, flips completely: earlier, A26 or A27 came before A19/A20/A25 in essentially every trace where they occurred together (about 31–38 in 100 traces, reverse order 0); in the later window the A19/A20/A25 activity comes first (about 30–36 in 100, reverse order at or near 0). Co-occurrence shares barely move, so what changed is the ordering, not whether the activities run together. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- The always-present activities stay roughly in place: A26, A27 and A28 occur in every trace in both windows and their average positions shift only by about 0.02–0.03. [POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Spacing among the always-present activities tightens in the later window: A27 is immediately followed by A28 in about 98 in 100 traces (about 48 in 100 before), A28 is immediately followed by A32 in about 96 in 100 (about 51 in 100 before), and A27 typically reaches A32 two steps later instead of three. [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]
- The opposite holds for A45 and A26: adjacent in about 86 in 100 earlier traces, they now typically have one event between them (about 82 in 100 at distance 2, none adjacent). [LAG007]
- A15 still occurs in every trace, but repeats less in the later window: about 1.6 occurrences per trace versus about 1.9 before, recurring in about 38 in 100 traces versus about 47 in 100. [OCC001]
- Apparent distance changes pairing A19/A25 with A28 or A32 (for example A25→A28, adjacent in about 94 in 100 joint occurrences earlier) rest on only 1–3 later-window observations and cannot be reliably read. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the activities A19, A20 and A25 — each executed in about a third of traces — were relocated: in the earlier window they ran late in the trace, after A26 and A27 and around A28, whereas in the later window they run early, before A26. The order flip is complete and consistent across all six pairings of the trio with A26 and A27, co-occurrence is essentially unchanged (so this is a placement change, not a shift between alternatives and joint execution), the position shift is large while A26–A28 stay in place, and the screening produced 20 qualifying items where fewer than 1 would be expected without a change. [POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

An important competing explanation remains: the trio's relocation cannot by itself explain the new one-event gap between A45 and A26, which appears in essentially all traces, so the evidence does not identify which activity now sits there, and the reshuffle around the always-present activities may be partly a separate change. [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

Other differences: A15 repeats less often in the later window (about 1.6 versus 1.9 occurrences per trace, recurrence in about 38 versus 47 in 100 traces) while still appearing in every trace [OCC001]; the distance changes between the optional activities and A28/A32 carry no weight because of their 1–3 later-window observations [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003].
