### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the exclusive choice between A27 and the A36/A37/A42 branch was removed and the trio was relocated to the front of the traced work. In the earlier window, A25 was immediately followed by A28, and the choice between A27 (about 54 in 100 traces) and the trio A36+A37+A42 (about 46 in 100) sat between A26 and A31; in the later window, every trace runs all four activities, with A36, A37 and A42 occupying the three positions between A25 and A28 in mixed order, and A27 keeping its late slot between A26 and A31. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL006, REL014, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004, LAG003, POS006, POS007, POS008, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Supporting signs: the participation of all four activities rose from about half to essentially all traces; A27's co-occurrence with each trio member rose from about zero to about 99 in 100 with a consistent trio-before-A27 order; the order flips relative to A26 and A28, the position shifts and the lag changes all point the same way; and 40 items qualified against roughly 0.44 expected for an unchanged process, so chance cannot account for the set as a whole. Note that these items largely describe one underlying restructuring, so they are not independent confirmations of separate changes. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Limitations: the evidence locates the trio's new slot only relative to A25, A28, A26 and A27, so unobserved activities inside or around the new early block cannot be excluded, and the after-window distances from A37 and A42 to A31 rest on a single observation each. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that this was not one coordinated restructuring but two separate changes — the trio becoming mandatory and moving earlier, and A27 independently becoming mandatory — since the windows show only the combined outcome.
