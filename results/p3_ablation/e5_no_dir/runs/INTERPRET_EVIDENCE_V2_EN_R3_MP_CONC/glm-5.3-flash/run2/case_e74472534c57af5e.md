### Observed differences

- **A30, A31 and A36 now repeat within traces.** In the earlier window each of the three occurred almost exactly once per trace (means about 1.0), and repeats appeared in at most about 1 in 100 traces. In the later window each occurs about 3.5 times per trace on average, and about 69 in 100 traces contain repeats. Every trace still contains each activity in both windows, so participation did not change — only the number of executions per trace did. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

- **The strict order between A14 and these activities dissolved.** A14 occurs in every trace in both windows. Earlier, A14 came before A30 in about 99 in 100 traces and before A36 in essentially all; in the later window these strict orders hold in only about 32 in 100 traces, while about 68 in 100 show A14 interleaved with repeated executions of A30/A36. [REL003, REL004]

- **The order between A30 and A36 changed from one-way to mixed.** Earlier, A30 preceded A36 in about 99 in 100 traces, usually two positions apart (about 97 in 100 traces); in the later window the strict order holds in about 32 in 100 traces with about 68–69 in 100 interleaved or repeated, the tight two-step gap appears in only about 12 in 100 traces (with about 5 in 100 at a five-step gap), and the reverse pair A36 followed by A30 — seen in just 1 of 200 traces earlier — now appears in about 136 of 200 traces. [REL005, LAG001, LAG002]

- **Event positions became more spread out.** The position distributions of A30 and A36 widened sharply (spread roughly doubling, e.g. A30 from about 0.06 to 0.12 in normalised position) with slightly later medians, and A14's position distribution also widened with a slightly earlier median. These are position-distribution changes based on event order, not clock-time shifts. [POS002, POS003, POS004]

- **A05, an optional activity (in about 55 in 100 traces earlier, about 52 in 100 later),** previously preceded A30/A36 in about 54 in 100 traces — essentially every co-occurrence; in the later window it precedes them in only about 17 in 100, while about 35 in 100 traces show interleaving with the repeated executions. [REL001, REL002, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the activities A30, A31 and A36, which in the earlier window were each executed exactly once per trace in a fixed order after A14, are in the later window executed about 3.5 times per trace, with repeats in about 69 in 100 traces, and that the strict ordering between A14, A30 and A36 was replaced by interleaved, repeated execution in roughly two thirds of traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002]

The volume of flagged items (14, all control-flow) is roughly fifty times the number expected for an unchanged process (about 0.27), so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of these windows produce. The repetition change is measured directly for all three activities, each present in every trace in both windows, which rules out the explanation that a rare variant merely surfaced in one window; the ordering change is shown directly and consistently by five relation items and two adjacency items, and every item ranks above about 99.5 in 100 reference comparisons. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: the evidence does not establish the shape of the repeated execution — A31 has no relational or adjacency items, so whether it repeats together with A30 and A36 or independently is not shown. The recurrence share of about 68 in 100 leaves open that only a subset of later-window traces repeats while others keep the old single-pass behaviour, which the evidence cannot resolve. The reversed-adjacency comparison for A36 followed by A30 rests on a single before-window observation, and all position and gap statements concern event positions rather than clock time. [LAG002, OCC002]

Other differences: A05, an optional activity, now interleaves with the repeated A30/A36 instead of strictly preceding them, and its median position moved slightly earlier; this is consistent with the same repetition change but rests on fewer traces (about 110 earlier and 103 later). [REL001, REL002, POS001]
