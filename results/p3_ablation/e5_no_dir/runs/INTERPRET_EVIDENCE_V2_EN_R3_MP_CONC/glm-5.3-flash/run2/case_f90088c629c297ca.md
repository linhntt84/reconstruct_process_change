### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A14, A15 and A20 were not observed in any of the 200 traces. In the later window, A14 runs in about 37 in 100 traces (74 of 200), A20 in about 34 in 100 (67 of 200) and A15 in about 29 in 100 (57 of 200), each at most once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The three activities are mutually exclusive: no later trace contains two of them together, and their shares add up to about 99 in 100 traces, so a later trace typically contains exactly one of the three; in the earlier window, traces contained neither of any pair. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- Whenever one of the three runs, it does so after A36 and before A21 — this order holds in all of its traces (A14: 37 in 100; A15: 29 in 100; A20: 34 in 100). Before, A21 always appeared without any of them. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- Before, A36 was immediately followed by A21, one step apart in all 200 traces. In the later window the distance between them is two steps in about 99 in 100 traces, and one step in only about 1 in 100. [LAG001]
- A21 occurs in every trace in both windows; its relative position moved slightly later (mean from about 0.37 to about 0.39 of the trace), while the three new activities sit at about 0.35 of the trace. [POS004, POS001, POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a step now executes between A36 and A21 where none did before: traces that previously went directly from A36 to A21 now pass through exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities — A14 in about 37 in 100 traces, A20 in about 34 in 100 and A15 in about 29 in 100 — each always ordered after A36 and before A21. This lengthens the A36–A21 distance from one to two steps and shifts A21 slightly later; in about 1 in 100 later traces the direct A36→A21 sequence remains. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001, POS004]

The screening comparison supports this reading: 17 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.22 expected for an unchanged process (44 units tested), far more than chance would produce, and the ordering, gap, occurrence and position items all describe one coherent difference rather than separate findings. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window means only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed there across 200 traces, and the evidence cannot show what determines which of the three alternatives runs in a given case, nor whether these activities existed unobserved elsewhere in the process before. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001]

Other differences: none — the qualifying items overlap heavily and re-describe this single change, so they are not independent confirmations of it. [OCC001, POS004, REL003, LAG001]
