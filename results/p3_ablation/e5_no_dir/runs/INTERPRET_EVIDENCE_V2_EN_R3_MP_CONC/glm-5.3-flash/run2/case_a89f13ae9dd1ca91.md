### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the always-executed block of three activities A21, A22 and A23 — run in every earlier case between A39 and A24, in no fixed order among themselves — was replaced by a choice of exactly one of three alternative activities, A14, A15 or A16, so the middle of the process now runs A39 → (A14 | A15 | A16) → A24 instead of A39 → {A21, A22, A23} → A24. The participation flip is near-total (100% to ~0.5% for the old activities; 0% to 30–37% for the new ones), the relational evidence places the new activities directly in the old slot (always after A39, always before A24, never together), and the A39–A24 distance halved from four to two positions. With 64 qualifying evidence items against roughly 0.5 expected by chance in a stable process, chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole, and every item shows maximal empirical strength (differences larger than any random re-split produced). Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply contain different mixes of case types rather than one modified process, and it cannot show whether A14/A15/A16 are genuinely new activities or stand-ins replacing A21/A22/A23; one later trace still contains the old block, so the old behaviour persists in ~0.5% of cases.
