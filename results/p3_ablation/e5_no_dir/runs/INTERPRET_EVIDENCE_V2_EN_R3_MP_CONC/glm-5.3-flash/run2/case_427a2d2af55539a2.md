### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 changed its content: in the earlier window every case executed exactly one of the alternatives A14, A15 or A16 there, whereas in the later window all three of A21, A22 and A23 are executed in every case, after A39 and before A24, in no fixed order among themselves; with three activities replacing one, A24 moved later in the trace (mean relative position from about 0.39 to about 0.44) and the A39–A24 distance grew from two to four positions. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL001, REL008, REL019, REL021, REL022, REL026, POS007, LAG001]

This is a replacement of one path segment by another rather than a shift in how often existing paths are taken: the earlier activities are entirely absent from the later sample and the later ones absent from the earlier sample. The support is direct and consistent across occurrence, positional and relational indicators, and the number of qualifying items far exceeds the chance expectation; note, however, that most items describe facets of this single underlying difference and are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC004, REL003, REL019, screening]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that A14, A15 and A16 were not observed in the later window (and A21, A22 and A23 not in the earlier one), so actual removal cannot be distinguished from the old alternatives simply never being selected; and the mixed pairwise order of A21, A22 and A23 is compatible both with parallel execution and with arbitrary sequencing, which these indicators cannot separate. [OCC001, OCC004, REL019, REL020, REL023]
