### Observed differences

- A30, A31 and A36 changed from single execution to repetition: in the earlier window each occurred almost exactly once per trace (means about 1.0, present in every trace, repeats within a trace in at most about 2 in 100 traces), while in the later window each occurs about 3.5 times per trace and recurs within a trace in about 68 in 100 traces, with participation still universal. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The fixed sequence among A14, A30 and A36 dissolved. The three activities still co-occur in every trace in both windows, but A14 preceded A30 in about 99 in 100 traces before versus 32 in 100 after; A14 preceded A36 in about 99 versus 32, and A30 preceded A36 in about 98 versus 32, while interleaved-or-repeated orderings rose from about 0–2 in 100 to 68 in 100 for each pair. [REL003, REL004, REL005]
- A05 still co-occurs with A30 and A36 in about half of the traces (55 versus 52 in 100), but its fixed precedence is largely gone: A05 came first in about 54 in 100 traces before versus 16 in 100 after, with interleaved-or-repeated ordering rising to about 36 in 100. [REL001, REL002]
- A30 and A36 now occur at much more variable positions within traces (relative-position spread about 0.07 before versus 0.12 after) at a roughly unchanged average position (about 0.41–0.48 before, 0.44–0.49 after), as expected when an activity runs several times per trace. [POS002, POS003]
- The tight succession in which A36 followed A30 exactly two steps later — about 98 in 100 observed pairs before — largely disappeared: only about 12 in 100 pairs remain two steps apart, with the rest spread over larger gaps. [LAG002]
- Two smaller shifts: A14 now sits slightly earlier relative to trace length (mean relative position about 0.14 versus 0.12, spread up from about 0.02 to 0.03), and the gap from A05 to A14 widened (one-step share about 36 versus 17 in 100, three-step share about 28 versus 45 in 100, based on roughly 110 and 100 traces per window). [POS001, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A30, A31 and A36 became repeating activities and that this dissolved the previously fixed ordering around them. In the earlier window each of the three ran once per trace and A14, A30 and A36 followed one fixed order (A14, then A30, then A36 in about 98–99 in 100 traces); in the later window each of the three runs about 3–4 times per trace, recurring in about 68 in 100 traces, and the order among A14, A30 and A36 is fixed in only about 32 in 100 traces while about 68 in 100 are interleaved or repeated. A05, which still joins A30 and A36 in about half of the traces, now precedes them in only about 16 in 100 instead of nearly always when present. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL001, REL002]

The support is direct and consistent: the repetition is measured on every trace in both windows, the rise in interleaved-or-repeated ordering (to about 68 in 100) matches the rise in recurrence, and the wider position spread of A30/A36 and the collapse of the two-step A30 → A36 succession fit the same picture. With 13 qualifying items against roughly 0.27 expected if nothing had changed (54 units tested), chance alone cannot account for the set, though the items likely reflect one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL003, REL004, REL005, POS002, POS003, LAG002]

Limitations: the evidence does not reveal the structural form of the repetition (whether the three activities repeat together or independently) and contains no relational or positional items for A31, so its placement remains unknown. The main competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that the later window mixes unchanged traces with a new repeating variant — the roughly 32 in 100 traces that keep the fixed order — rather than reflecting one uniform new behaviour. [OCC002, REL003, REL004, REL005]

Other differences: the slightly earlier relative position of A14 and the widened A05 → A14 gap (from about one step to about two–three steps) are consistent with the traces lengthening, but the evidence does not show which activity now runs between A05 and A14, so they are reported separately from the main hypothesis. [POS001, LAG001]
