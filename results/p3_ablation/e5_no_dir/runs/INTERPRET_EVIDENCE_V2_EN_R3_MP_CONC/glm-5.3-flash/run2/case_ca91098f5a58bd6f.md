### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the intermediate step between A36 and A21 — a three-way choice in which every trace executed exactly one of A14, A15 or A20 — is no longer executed, so that A36 now leads directly into A21. The change is shown directly and consistently: participation in the three activities fell from 70, 60 and 70 of 200 traces to 0 of 200; the A36–A21 distance shrank from two positions to one in every trace; and the relation items uniformly show the three activities' co-occurrences with A36, A21 and A26 disappearing while those three activities themselves continue in all traces. The screening produced 24 qualifying items against roughly 0.26 expected without a change, far beyond what random re-splits of an unchanged process produce. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, LAG001, POS004, POS005, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL013, REL014, REL015]

Limitations: zero participation establishes only that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed in the later window, so a reduction to a very low but non-zero rate cannot be strictly excluded — although 0 occurrences in 200 traces after rates of 30–35 in 100 fits removal far better than sampling variation. The evidence also cannot show whether the step was replaced elsewhere in the process; it shows only that nothing observable now sits between A36 and A21. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, LAG001]

Other differences: A19's participation rose from about 49 to about 56 in 100 traces; the evidence does not connect this to the disappearance of the intermediate step. [REL002, REL007, REL012]
