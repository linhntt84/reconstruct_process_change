### Observed differences

- The order of the always-executed closing activities reversed. In the earlier window, A25 and A29 were completed before A20, A22 and A23 in about 97–99 in 100 traces (these five activities occur together in every trace); in the later window, A20, A22 and A23 came before A25 and A29 in about 97–99 in 100 traces. [REL023, REL024, REL030, REL031, REL033, REL034]

- A08 became much rarer and lost its fixed slot: it appeared in about 64 in 100 traces before versus about 29 in 100 after (mean occurrences per trace 0.68→0.32). - Several rare activities became more frequent: A04 rose from about 7 to about 21 in 100 traces, A06 from about 7 to about 24, and A10 from about 8 to about 19. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004]
- The immediate succession of A45 and A46 weakened, from about 97 in 100 observed adjacent pairs to about 84 in 100. [LAG030]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the closing stage was resequenced: the always-executed A20, A22 and A23, which previously ran after A25 and A29 and after the optional A19, A21 and A24, now run before them, with A25/A29 keeping their middle position and A19/A21/A24 moving from the front of this group to its end. The sign is direct and consistent: all 21 affected activity-pair comparisons show the same near-complete order reversal with full support (200 traces in each window), the position shifts of all six activities agree, and the changed A29–A30 and A40–A25 distances point the same way. The volume of qualifying items (79 against fewer than 1 expected without change in the control-flow family) cannot be explained by sampling variation. The main limitation is that the windows are aggregates: the evidence cannot show whether individual cases were re-routed or whether the later window is dominated by a different mix of trace variants, a competing explanation it cannot rule out; LAG items resting on only 1–3 paired observations in one window were not relied upon. [REL016, REL023, POS003, POS004, LAG026, LAG029]

Other differences: A08 was executed in about 29 in 100 traces in the later window, down from about 64 in 100, and no longer occupies a fixed position between A14/A15 and the later activities; A04, A06 and A10 participate more often (about 7→21, 7→24 and 8→19 in 100 traces); the A45→A46 immediate succession weakened (about 97→84 in 100 of observed pairs). These differences are reported separately and are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC003, REL001, REL005, POS001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, LAG030]
