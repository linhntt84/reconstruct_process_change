### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of three mutually exclusive activities — A19, A20 and A25, which before ran in about 99 in 100 cases (exactly one per case), always after A14 and A41 and almost always before A26 or A31 — is no longer executed in the later window, and that cases now proceed from A41 directly to A26: A26 immediately follows A41 in about 92 in 100 observations, where before it followed two steps later in about 94 in 100, with A26's mean relative position moving slightly earlier (about 0.39 to 0.37) and A41's slightly later (about 0.32 to 0.33). This is unlikely to be a sampling artefact: 32 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.38 expected without a change, and the occurrence, relation and lag indicators all describe the same underlying difference consistently. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021, LAG002, POS005, POS006]

Limitations: zero participation only means the activities were not observed, so complete removal cannot be distinguished from a drastic drop to below about 1 in 200 traces — a competing explanation this evidence cannot rule out is that the block still executes at a very low rate. A20's single later occurrence, at relative position about 0.62 against about 0.36 before, rests on one trace and cannot characterise any residual execution. The many relation items re-express the same disappearance on shared traces and are not independent confirmations. [OCC002, POS003, REL007]
