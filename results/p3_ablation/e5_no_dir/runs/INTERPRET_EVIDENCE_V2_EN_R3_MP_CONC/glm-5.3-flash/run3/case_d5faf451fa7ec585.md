### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Main hypothesis: the block of three mutually exclusive alternatives — A14, A15 and A20, of which exactly one executed in each earlier case between A22 and A24 — is no longer executed in the later window, so A22, A23 and A24 now run one after another. The evidence is direct and consistent: each of the three activities fell from 28–40 in 100 traces (56–80 of 200 traces each) to 0 of 200, their co-occurrence with A22/A23/A24 collapsed entirely, and the distances among A22, A23 and A24 contracted to a single step. With 23 qualifying items against roughly 0.25 expected without change, the volume of evidence far exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces. This is a removal of activities rather than a shift in how often existing paths are taken, since the three activities are absent from the later window altogether. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL003, REL005, REL010, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: zero participation only means the activities were not observed in the later window. The evidence cannot rule out that A14, A15 and A20 were replaced by other activities outside the screened set, or that the later window captures only a variant in which this block never ran.
