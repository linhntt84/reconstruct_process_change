### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch containing A32, A33 and A38 — earlier executed together, in varying order, in roughly half of all cases between A26 and A28 — is no longer taken in the later window (about 1 in 200 cases per activity), while the alternative branch through A27, earlier taken in about half of the cases, now runs in nearly every case as A26 → A27 → A28 with A27 immediately before A28. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL012, LAG004, LAG001]

Supporting signs: 31 evidence items qualified against fewer than one (0.37) expected for an unchanged process, so sampling variation cannot account for the ensemble; the participation, co-occurrence and lag indicators all describe the same substitution, and the key orderings hold with substantial support in both windows (103 and about 199 of 200 pair observations for the A27–A28 one-step gap). [REL006, LAG004, LAG001]

Limitations: each of A32, A33 and A38 still appears in about 1 of 200 later traces, so the evidence cannot distinguish a fully removed branch from one that still exists but is chosen only rarely; nor can it confirm that A27 has become strictly mandatory rather than merely taken in almost all cases. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC001]

Other differences: A28's slight positional shift and the consolidation of the A26–A28 distance to two positions are consistent with shorter main-path traces once the A32/A33/A38 branch stopped being taken; a positional shift alone does not establish that A28 itself was moved. [POS001, LAG001]
