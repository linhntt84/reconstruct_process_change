### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 were executed in every trace (participation 100%, about 1.0 occurrences per trace each). In the later window, each appears in only about 53–54% of traces (about 107, 106 and 108 of 200 respectively), with mean occurrences per trace down to about 0.53–0.55. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]
- Activities that previously always co-occurred with this block now often run without it: A14 and A15 still occur in all traces, but their co-occurrence with A19/A20/A25 fell from 100% of traces to about 53–54%, and each now appears without the block in about 46–47% of traces (previously 0%). A26, A41, A46 and A47 show the same pattern, appearing alone in about 46–47% of later traces. [REL010, REL013, REL015, REL018, REL021, REL024]
- The three block members also disappear together: the pairs A19–A20, A19–A25 and A20–A25 fell from co-occurrence in 100% of traces to about 53%, with neither member present in about 46% of later traces, consistent with the same cases skipping all three. Their mutual order (A19 before A25 before A20) is preserved whenever they do run. [REL016, REL017, REL022]
- The surrounding order is unchanged: A14 and A41 still precede the block and A26, A46 and A47 still follow it in nearly every trace where both are present (for example, A14 precedes A19 in 52% of all later traces against 53.5% co-occurrence). [REL010, REL019, REL018, REL027]
- Step distances shifted accordingly: the gap from A41 to A26 is within 1–2 steps in about 47% of later observations (essentially never before, when a 4-step gap dominated at about 91%), while a 4–5 step gap remains in about 53%; the A14–A41 gap tightened from typically 5 steps (about 71% of observations) to typically 4 steps (about 67%). [LAG008, LAG005]
- A06 receded sharply: it appears in about 26% of later traces (52 of 200) versus about 71% (142 of 200) before; where it occurs, its mean relative position in the trace moved from about 0.18 to about 0.43 with much more spread, its usual single-step succession after A14 (about 98% of observed pairs) loosened to about 75%, and it now usually appears without A14 or A15 (co-occurrence down from 71% to 26%). [OCC002, POS001, REL001, REL002, LAG003]
- Three rare activities became more common: A04 from about 5% to about 15% of traces, A08 from about 4% to about 20%, and A10 from about 5% to about 16%. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A19, A20 and A25 — previously executed in every case — are skipped in roughly 46% of cases: each runs in only about 53–54% of traces (about 107, 106 and 108 of 200, versus 200 of 200 before), while the surrounding activities still run in every trace in the same order — A14, A15 and A41 before the block, A26, A46 and A47 after it — and now frequently appear without the block. The three are skipped together: about 46% of later traces contain neither member of an A19–A20, A19–A25 or A20–A25 pair, and the A41-to-A26 step gap collapses from 4–5 steps to 1–2 steps in about 47% of observations, matching the skip share. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL010, REL016, REL019, LAG008]

The reading is direct and consistent: the participation drops are measured on all 200 traces per window; about 49 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.64 expected by chance across 129 tested units (about 75 times the chance count), and each listed item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data; the relational items show the change through co-occurrence and one-sided appearance rather than through position alone. [OCC005, REL016, REL018, LAG008]

Limitations: the many agreeing items largely reflect the same traces and are not independent confirmations; and the evidence cannot distinguish a newly optional block within one process from a later window that mixes two kinds of cases, one of which never executed A19, A20 or A25 — both would produce this pattern. [OCC005, REL016, REL022]

Other differences:

- A06's decline looks independent of the block skip: it now appears in about 26% of traces whether or not the block runs (co-occurrence with A19 in only about 15% of later traces, about 12% with A06 alone, about 35% with neither), sits later in the trace where it occurs, and is decoupled from A14 and A15. [OCC002, POS001, REL003, REL001]
- The rare activities A04, A08 and A10 now occur in about three to five times as many traces as before (about 15–20% versus 4–5%); how they attach to the remaining paths cannot be determined from this evidence. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]
- Minor positional shifts: A14 sits slightly later and more variably in the trace (mean relative position about 0.12 to 0.14, spread roughly tripling), A26 slightly earlier, and the A14–A15 and A46–A47 step gaps changed slightly. [POS002, POS003, LAG004, LAG009]
