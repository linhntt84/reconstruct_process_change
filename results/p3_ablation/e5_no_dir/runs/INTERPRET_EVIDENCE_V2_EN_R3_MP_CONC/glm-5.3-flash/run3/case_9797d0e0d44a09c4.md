### Observed differences

- The activities A19, A20 and A25 went from optional to near-universal: in the earlier window each appeared in about 47 in 100 traces (mean occurrences per trace about 0.47, so at most once when present); in the later window each appears in about 995 in 1000 traces with about one occurrence per trace, and repetition stays negligible in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The three activities form a fixed block: in the later window they co-occur in about 995 in 1000 traces and keep the order A19, then A25, then A20 (each pair in about 98–100 in 100 traces); before, they co-occurred in only about 47 in 100 traces, with the same internal order whenever they did appear. [REL001, REL002, REL007]
- The block sits between A41 and A26: in the later window A41 precedes each of the three in about 96–98 in 100 traces, and each of the three precedes A26 in about 97–99 in 100; before, A26 typically occurred without the block (about 53 in 100 traces had A26 but none of the three). [REL003, REL004, REL008, REL009, REL012, REL013]
- The distance from A41 to A26 widened accordingly: before, A26 followed A41 immediately (one step) in about 53 in 100 traces and four steps later in about 42 in 100; in the later window the one-step case has nearly vanished (about 0.5 in 100) and the four-step gap dominates (about 89 in 100), which is what placing the three activities between them produces. [LAG001]
- Two small positional shifts accompany this: A26's mean relative position moved slightly later (about 0.41 to 0.43) and A41's slightly earlier (about 0.33 to 0.31). [POS001, POS002]
- A45, present in roughly 60 in 100 traces in both windows, now appears only alongside A19: before it occurred without A19 in about 33 in 100 traces, in the later window in none. [REL006, REL011, REL015]
- A42, which occurs in essentially every trace in both windows, keeps no fixed position relative to the block, and its ordering became more mixed (interleaved or repeated with A19 rose from about 17 to about 46 in 100 traces). [REL005, REL010, REL014]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the subsequence A19 → A25 → A20, executed between A41 and A26, changed from optional to mandatory: in the earlier window it was executed once, in this position and order, in about 47 in 100 traces and skipped otherwise, while in the later window it is executed exactly once in essentially every trace (about 995 in 1000), after A41 and before A26. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL007, LAG001]

The hypothesis is direct and consistent: the participation jump rests on 94–95 traces before versus 199 after for each activity, the co-occurrence and ordering relations among the three activities and with A41 and A26 all point the same way, and the A41-to-A26 gap shifting from one step to four steps shows the block's position directly. The screening context reinforces this: 21 control-flow items qualified, roughly 50 times the 0.41 expected if nothing had changed. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, LAG001]

Limitations: the many relation items largely describe one underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations, and because the without-block path is nearly unobserved in the later window (about 1 in 200 traces), the evidence cannot distinguish a removed skip branch from an extreme shift in the mix of two pre-existing process variants. [REL003, REL008, REL012]

Other differences: A45 now co-occurs with A19 in every trace in which it appears, whereas before it appeared without A19 in about a third of traces, and A42's ordering relative to the block became more mixed; both may simply follow from the block now being present in every trace. [REL006, REL011, REL015, REL005, REL010, REL014]
