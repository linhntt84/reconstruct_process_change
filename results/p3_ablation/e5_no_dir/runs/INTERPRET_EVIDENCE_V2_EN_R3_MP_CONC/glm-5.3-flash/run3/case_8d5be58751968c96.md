### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, six activities ran in every single trace: A33, A35 and A36 as one group, and A37, A38 and A39 as a later group (participation 100%, about one occurrence per trace, never repeated). In the later window each of them appears in only about half of the traces — A33/A35/A36 in roughly 47 in 100, A37/A38/A39 in roughly 55–57 in 100 — still at most once per trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- The two groups became mutually exclusive. Before, an activity of the first group co-occurred with an activity of the second in 100% of traces, with the first group always executed first (about 98–100 in 100). In the later window they co-occur in only about 2–3 in 100 traces; the first group runs without the second in about 44 in 100, the second without the first in about 53 in 100, and there is essentially no trace in which both groups are absent. [REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024]
- Within each group the activities still move together: first-group pairs co-occur in about 46–47 in 100 later traces (in about 53 in 100 neither occurs), second-group pairs in about 55–56 in 100 (neither in about 43–44), whereas before, co-occurrence was 100%. [REL013, REL014, REL018, REL025, REL026, REL027]
- The path shortened: the distance from A30 to A37/A38/A39 was 4–5 steps in the earlier window (split roughly 50/50) and is 1–3 steps in the later one; the distance from A33/A35/A36 to A31 shrank the same way. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG007, LAG010]
- Relative positions shifted consistently with shorter traces: A30 moved slightly later (mean 0.62 to 0.67), A31 slightly earlier (0.82 to 0.80), the first-group activities later (about 0.68 to 0.73–0.74) and the second-group activities earlier (about 0.76–0.77 to 0.74). - A30 and A31 still occur in every trace in both windows, and their order relative to the groups is unchanged: A30 still precedes the first group and the group still precedes A31 whenever they co-occur. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural change in the segment between A30 and A31: the two three-activity groups — A33, A35, A36 and, after them, A37, A38, A39 — which were previously both executed in sequence in every case, now behave as mutually exclusive alternatives. In the later window a case typically executes either the first group (about 47 in 100 cases) or the second (about 55 in 100), and only about 2–3 in 100 cases contain both; A30 still opens and A31 still closes the segment, and the internal order within each group is unchanged. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024, REL001, REL007]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: participation of all six activities dropped from 100% to about half; between-group co-occurrence collapsed from 100% to about 2–3% while "neither group" is essentially never observed, so almost every case contains exactly one group; within-group co-occurrence stays tightly coupled; step distances from A30 to the second group and from the first group to A31 collapsed from 4–5 steps to 1–3 steps; and the positional shifts match shorter traces. In total 53 evidence items qualified, against roughly 0.55 expected for an unchanged process, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole, although most items describe the same underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations. [REL013, REL014, REL018, REL025, REL026, REL027, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG007, LAG010, POS001, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

Limitations: the evidence shows the exclusivity but not the mechanism behind it, and it cannot distinguish a routing decision within single cases from a mixture of two case populations that each contain only one group; the small share of cases with both groups is not explained further. Some cross-group distance items rest on only 4–5 later-window observations and are weak on their own. [LAG005, LAG006, LAG008, LAG009, LAG011, LAG012]

Other differences: the spread of A31's relative position widened (standard deviation about 0.07 to 0.09), consistent with the two shorter trace shapes now being mixed. [POS002]
