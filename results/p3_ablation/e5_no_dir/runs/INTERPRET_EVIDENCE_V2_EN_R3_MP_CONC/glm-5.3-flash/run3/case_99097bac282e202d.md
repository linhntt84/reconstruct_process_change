### Observed differences

- The dominant change is a reversal between two alternative activity groups. Earlier, A21, A22 and A23 each took part in about 75 in 100 traces and always executed jointly (co-occurrence about 0.75); in the later window each takes part in only about 25 in 100 traces, still jointly when they appear. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL013, REL014, REL021]
- The mirror image holds for A24, A25 and A26: about 25 in 100 traces earlier, about 76 in 100 in the later window, again always as a joint group. [OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, REL034, REL035, REL039]
- The two groups are alternatives in both windows: pairs such as A21/A24 or A23/A26 essentially never occur in the same trace, before or after (co-occurrence at most about 0.5 in 100). The change is therefore a reversal of which branch is taken, not the appearance or removal of activities. [REL015, REL017, REL022, REL024, REL028, REL030]
- A20, present in essentially every trace in both windows, was followed by the A21–A23 group in about 74 in 100 earlier traces; in the later window it is followed by the A24–A26 group in about 74 in 100 traces, while the old continuation dropped to about 25 in 100. [REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- Downstream activities switched partners accordingly: A30 co-occurred with A21–A23 in about 75 in 100 earlier traces and with A24–A26 in about 76 in 100 later traces; A32 and A33 moved from co-occurring with the old group (about 48 in 100) to the new group (about 52 in 100). [REL018, REL025, REL031, REL036, REL040, REL043, REL019, REL037]
- A10 participates more often (about 6 in 100 traces earlier, 21 in 100 later; 11 vs 42 of 200 traces) and appears later and more tightly clustered within traces (median relative position 0.60 to 0.86, spread 0.32 to 0.15); in the later window A24, A25 or A26 precede A10 in about 15 in 100 traces. [OCC002, POS001, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- A02 was observed in only 2 of 200 later traces, down from 18 of 200 (about 9 in 100 to 1 in 100); this rests on very few cases. [OCC001]
- Step distances (event positions, not clock time) redistributed with the re-pairing: the A20→A24 gap is now mostly 2–3 steps (67 in 100 combined) instead of mostly 1 step (54 in 100); A25→A30 gaps lengthened (1-step share 32% to 18%); A32→A33 spread slightly (1-step 56% to 39%). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the choice between two alternative three-activity branches after A20 reversed: earlier, roughly three in four traces ran the group A21, A22, A23 (executed jointly, accompanied by A30 and A32/A33), while about one in four ran A24, A25, A26; in the later window the shares flipped, with A24, A25, A26 executing jointly in about 76 in 100 traces, A21–A23 persisting as the rarer alternative at about 25 in 100, and A30 and A32/A33 now co-occurring with the new group instead of the old one. The support is direct and consistent: the successor relations of A20 and the partner switches of A30/A32/A33 are observed relationally in each window, the participation flips are about 50 percentage points on 49–152 supporting traces per window, both branches are well observed in both windows (so this is a share reversal between existing paths, not a replacement), and 58 qualifying items against fewer than one (0.74) expected by chance for 148 tested units cannot be explained by sampling noise as a whole. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, REL007, REL010, REL013, REL015, REL018, REL034, REL036, REL039]

Limitations: the evidence shows which relations changed, not why, and it cannot rule out that the two windows draw different case mixes rather than a changed process; the step-distance shifts involve the same traces as the re-pairing and therefore add no independent confirmation. [REL007, REL010, REL015, REL036]

Other differences: A10's higher participation and later, tighter position within traces, now often preceded by the A24–A26 group, is a separate participation/positional shift not required by the main hypothesis. A02's near-disappearance (18 to 2 traces) is too thinly supported to carry weight on its own. [OCC002, POS001, REL004, OCC001]
