### Observed differences

- **Repetition of A25, A26 and A31.** In the earlier window, each of these three activities occurred exactly once in every trace (participation 100 in 100 traces, mean 1.0 occurrence per trace, no repeats). In the later window they still appear in every trace, but average about 3.5 occurrences per trace, with repeats in about 68 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- **Ordering between A25 and A31.** In the earlier window the two always co-occurred and A25 came before A31 in 100 in 100 traces — a fixed order. In the later window that strict order holds in only about 32 in 100 traces, while about 68 in 100 show the pair interleaved or repeated — a change from a fixed order to a mixed one, not a swap of positions. [REL001]
- **Separation between A25 and A31.** In the earlier window the pair was consistently 2 event positions apart; in the later window that fixed two-step gap is largely gone, with only about 12 in 100 pairs at two steps' distance and about 5 in 100 at five steps. [LAG001]
- **Positional variability.** The event positions of A25 and A31 within traces became much more variable (spread roughly doubled, e.g. A25 from about 0.06 to about 0.12) with slightly later median positions; these are position-based, not clock-time, observations. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment formed by A25, A26 and A31 changed from a single, strictly ordered execution per case to a repeated one: previously each activity ran exactly once per case with A25 always preceding A31, whereas in the later window each runs about 3.5 times per case on average, repeats in roughly 68 in 100 cases, and the fixed A25-before-A31 sequencing gives way to interleaving in most cases. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001]

The support is direct and consistent: seven control-flow items qualified where an unchanged process would typically produce fewer than one (about 0.17), far beyond what chance produces; every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits; and participation is 100 in 100 traces in both windows (200 traces each), so the jump from one to about 3.5 occurrences per trace is not a rare-variant sampling artefact. The relational, gap and positional items all point the same way, though they likely describe one underlying behaviour rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, REL001, LAG001]

Limitations: the three activities show identical repetition profiles, but relational and positional evidence covers only A25 and A31, so the evidence cannot show whether all three repeat together as one block or independently; nor can it separate genuinely new interleaving from a byproduct of repetition, as the relation indicator merges the two categories. A competing explanation that remains open is that the loosened ordering is an independent change in ordering constraints rather than a consequence of the repetition. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, LAG001, POS001, POS002]
