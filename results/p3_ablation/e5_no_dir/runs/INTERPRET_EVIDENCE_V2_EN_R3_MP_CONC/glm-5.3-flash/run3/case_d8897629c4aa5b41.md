### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Main hypothesis: in the later window, a new step was inserted into the previously fixed sequence between A22 and A24. The inserted step is realized as exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities — A14, A15 or A20 — present in about 199 of 200 later traces; it always runs after A22 and before A24, placed between A22 and A23 in about 56 in 100 traces and between A23 and A24 in about 44 in 100. This is shown directly by the relational and distance evidence, not merely by frequency shifts: the 56/44 split of the new activities around A23 matches the observed gap changes almost exactly, and the A22→A24 distance growing from 2 to 3 steps in nearly all traces corroborates a single insertion per trace. The three activities appear from zero participation with substantial support (58–82 traces each), are never co-observed, and hold a stable mid-trace position; A22's slight positional shift is consistent with an event added after it. The volume of qualifying evidence (22 control-flow items against roughly 0.25 expected without change) cannot be explained by random re-splitting of an unchanged process.
