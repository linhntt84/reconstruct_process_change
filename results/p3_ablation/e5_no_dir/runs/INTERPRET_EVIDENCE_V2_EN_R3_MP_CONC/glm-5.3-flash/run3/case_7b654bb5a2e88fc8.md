### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the three alternatives A14, A15 and A20 appeared in 76, 61 and 63 of 200 traces respectively, and their shares sum to the whole window; in the later window the pattern is the same (61, 65 and 74 of 200). Their average position within the trace, however, moved from about 0.31–0.33 (early third of the case) to about 0.59–0.60 (late in the case). [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- Their order relative to the mandatory activities A21 and A22 reversed completely. In the earlier window the alternative always ran first (e.g., A14 before A21 in 38 of 100 traces, never the reverse); in the later window A21 and A22 always ran first (A21 before A14 in about 31 of 100, A21 before A20 in about 37 of 100, never the reverse). [REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007]
- Relative to A23 the fixed order became mixed: earlier, the alternative always preceded A23 (31–38 of 100 traces); later, A23 preceded the alternative in about 15–23 of 100 and followed it in about 15–23, i.e., no fixed order. [REL003, REL008]
- The pairwise distances locate the move: the A40→A21 distance fell from always 2 events to always 1 (one activity left that span), while the A22→A27 distance rose from always 2 to always 3 (one activity entered that span). [LAG004, LAG002]
- Within that span, the alternative sits between A22 and A23 in about 48 of 100 later-window traces and between A23 and A27 in about 52 of 100 (both distances grew from always 1 to 2 in roughly half the traces each). [LAG001, LAG003]
- The mandatory activities A21, A22 and A23, present in every trace, shifted only slightly earlier, by about 0.02–0.03 in normalized position. [POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Overall, 18 of 49 tested control-flow units qualified, versus fewer than 1 expected by chance in an unchanged process. [screening]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One possibility supported by the evidence is that the per-case alternative — exactly one of A14, A15 and A20 executes in each case — was relocated: in the earlier window it ran before A21, between A40 and A21 at about a third of the case; in the later window it runs after A22, in the A22–A27 span around A23's position. The order reversal against A21 and A22 is complete and consistent across all six activity pairs; the gap changes show one activity leaving the A40–A21 span and one entering the A22–A27 span; participation is essentially unchanged (each alternative in about 3 of 10 traces in both windows, together covering every case), so this is a change in placement, not in how often existing paths are taken; and 18 qualifying items against about 0.24 expected is roughly 75 times the chance level. [POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, screening]

Limitations: positions and distances are event positions, not clock time, so the evidence shows a change in ordering, not in timing; and the new placement is not a single fixed slot — against A23 the order is mixed (roughly half the cases each way), and the evidence cannot distinguish a flexible slot around A23 from execution alongside the A22–A23 segment. The mutual order among A14, A15 and A20 was not tested, so the "exactly one per case" reading rests on the shares summing to the full window. [REL003, REL008, LAG001, LAG003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: none beyond the slight backward shift of A21, A22 and A23 (about 0.02–0.03), which is consistent with one activity leaving the segment before them rather than an independent change. [POS004, POS005, POS006]
