### Observed differences

- Four activities — A27, A36, A37 and A42 — moved from partial to universal participation: in the earlier window each ran in only about 46–54 in 100 traces (once per trace when present), while in the later window each runs in essentially every trace (about 199–200 of 200), still once per trace. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the exclusive alternative between A27 and the jointly executed trio A36/A37/A42 was replaced by both always running: A27, previously executed in about 54 in 100 cases, and the trio, previously executed in about 46 in 100, now execute in every case. The trio, still without a fixed internal order, now runs early in the trace between A25 and A28, whereas it previously ran between A26/A28 and A31; A27 remains in its earlier slot between A26 and A31. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL006, REL014, POS006, LAG001, LAG003]

The screening found 40 qualifying items against roughly 0.44 expected if nothing had changed, so the differences as a whole are far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces, and every item exceeds about 99.5 in 100 reference splits. The hypothesis rests on direct signs: participation rising to completeness, co-occurrence of A27 with each trio member going from zero to near-certain, order reversals relative to A26 and A28 observed directly rather than inferred from positions alone, and lag changes (A25→A28 from one to four steps; A26→A31 collapsing to a constant two) that match the relocation. [OCC001, REL011, REL006, LAG001, LAG003]

Limitations: positions and lags use event positions, so "earlier" means earlier in the sequence, not earlier in clock time, and the monitored pairs do not pin down the trio's exact neighbours, since unmonitored activities may lie between them. [POS006, LAG002] An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that a rare variant already executing all of A27, A36, A37 and A42 existed before (one earlier-window trace shows A27 together with A36) and simply came to dominate the later window, rather than the routing itself having been rewritten. [REL011]

Other differences: the always-executed activities show only small relative-position shifts (A25 about 0.36→0.35, A26 about 0.54→0.60, A28 about 0.39→0.46, A31 about 0.63→0.66), which are consistent with the trio and A27 being inserted before them rather than independent changes. [POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005]
