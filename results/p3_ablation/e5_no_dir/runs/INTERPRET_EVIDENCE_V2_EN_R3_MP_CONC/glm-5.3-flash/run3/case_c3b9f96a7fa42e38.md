### Observed differences

- The clearest difference is a reversal of execution order between two groups of activities. In the earlier window, A35, A36 and A41 each ran before A26 and A27 in practically every trace (for example, A35 before A26 in 200 of 200 traces, A36 before A27 in about 99 in 100). In the later window, all six cross-pairs between the groups run the other way: A26 before A35 in about 98 in 100, A27 before A41 in about 98 in 100, and the remaining pairs between about 97 and 99 in 100. Both activities of every pair still occur together in all 200 traces in both windows. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Relative positions moved accordingly: A26's mean position within the trace fell from about 0.62 to about 0.53 and A27's from about 0.47 to about 0.38, while A35, A36 and A41 moved from about 0.41–0.42 to about 0.58–0.59; all five activities occur in every trace in both windows, so this is a reordering rather than a change in participation. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Distances to neighbouring activities changed consistently with this swap: A26 and A30 were immediate neighbours before (lag 1 in about 99 in 100) and are now about four to five steps apart; A25 was about four steps before A27 and now immediately precedes it (lag 1 in about 98 in 100); A16 is now typically five steps before A27 in about 149 of 200 traces, where before the ordered pair appeared within five steps in only 1 trace; and A36 now runs one to three steps before A30 in about 198 of 200 traces, against 1 trace before. [LAG003, LAG005, LAG009, LAG011]
- Two activities outside these groups changed how often they occur. A02 now appears in about 23 of 200 traces versus about 9 before, and roughly three times as often per trace (mean 0.14 versus 0.05). A06 now appears in about 25 of 200 traces versus about 54, occurs about half as often per trace (0.15 versus 0.29), and sits later within the traces where it does occur (mean relative position about 0.44 versus 0.25). [OCC001, OCC002, POS001]
- Several lag relations thinned out on very few observations: ordered pairs that occurred within five steps in all 200 traces before (for example A25 before A36, or A41 before A27) now do so in only about 2 traces, and the A06/A16 lag items rest on 11 or fewer later-window observations; individually these are weak, though their collapse fits the two groups having swapped and spread apart. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence directly supports the hypothesis that the relative execution order of two groups of activities reversed: before, A35, A36 and A41 ran before A26 and A27; in the later window, A26 and A27 run before A35, A36 and A41. The support is direct and consistent: all six cross-group order relations flip from 98–100 in 100 to 97–99 in 100 while the activities still co-occur in every trace [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]; the position distributions of all five activities shift in the matching directions with unchanged, full participation [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]; and the screening flagged 26 control-flow items where roughly 0.5 would be expected without a change, so chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole [REL001, POS002, LAG003, LAG009]. Changes in distances to neighbouring activities point the same way, with A26–A30 no longer adjacent and A25–A27 now adjacent [LAG005, LAG009]. Limitations: positions and lags are event positions, not clock time, so the evidence locates the reorder in sequence only; and the tested relations cover only part of the process, so the exact new arrangement around the two groups cannot be pinned down.

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the reorder involved more than these five activities — for example, activities now sitting between the two groups — since several neighbouring pairs are no longer within five steps of each other in the later window and some of those items rest on only a handful of observations. [LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG012]

Other differences: A02 is executed in more cases (about 9 to 23 of 200 traces, about three times as often per trace), and A06 in fewer (about 54 to 25 of 200 traces, about half as often per trace) and later within the traces it occurs in; these participation and placement changes for A02 and A06 lie outside the ordering hypothesis above. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001]
