### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a specific structural hypothesis: in the later window, the segment between A26 and A31 is filled either by A27 alone (about 53 in 100 cases) or by the three activities A36, A37 and A42 in varying order (about 47 in 100), whereas in the earlier window A27 always executed in that segment and the group A36/A37/A42 always executed earlier, directly between A25 and A28. A27 thus became skippable, the group became conditional, and the two became alternatives that virtually never run in the same case; the group's position moved from before A28 to after A26.

The hypothesis rests on direct relational and participation evidence: the order reversals against A26 and A28, and the unchanged relations to A25 and A31, are observed on all 200 traces per window [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019]; the participation drops and the exclusivity are backed by about 94–107 later-window traces per activity, so neither branch is a rare variant [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL011, REL012, REL013]; and the lag distributions match the two-branch structure directly (A25→A28 now adjacent; A26→A31 bimodal at distances 2 and 4) [LAG001, LAG005]. Screening qualified 45 control-flow items where about 0.47 would be expected without change, so chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole.

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the exclusive pattern is an explicit routing decision or correlated skipping of A27 and the group, nor whether the group's relocation and A27's new optionality are one coupled change or two simultaneous ones; the behaviour of activities not covered by the evidence (for example between A28 and A26) is unknown.

Other differences: A25 and A31 show only small position shifts (mean relative position 0.34→0.36 and 0.65→0.63) consistent with knock-on effects of the restructuring, and several lag comparisons between A25/A28 and individual group members now rest on a single paired observation each, adding little beyond confirming that the group no longer runs adjacent to them.
