### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 changed structurally: earlier it held an optional step, filled in about 35 in 100 cases by exactly one of the alternatives A14, A15 or A16, whereas in the later window A21, A22 and A23 form a mandatory block — each executed once in every case, after A39 and before A24, in no fixed order among themselves — while A14, A15 and A16 are no longer observed.
