- In the earlier window, A30, A31 and A36 each occurred once per trace (mean about 1.0 occurrence, present in 100 in 100 traces, repeated in at most about 2 in 100). In the later window, each occurs about 3.5 times per trace on average and repeats in about 68 in 100 traces, while still appearing in every trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A30, A31 and A36 came to be repeated within traces — executed about three to four times each instead of once, with repetitions in about two thirds of traces — and that the previously fixed sequence A14 → A30 → A36 dissolved into interleaved, repeated execution: A14 precedes A30 in only about 32 in 100 traces (about 99 in 100 earlier), and A30 precedes A36 in about 32 in 100 (about 98 in 100 earlier). A05, present in about half of the traces throughout, likewise lost its fixed position before A30 and A36 (about 54 in 100 earlier to about 16 in 100 later, with interleaved order in about 35 in 100). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL001, REL002, LAG002]

The hypothesis is direct and consistent: repetition is measured on every trace in both windows (participation 100 in 100 throughout, so this is not a rare variant surfacing in one window), the order changes are measured on pairs that co-occur in every trace, and the collapse of the regular two-step spacing between A30 and A36 agrees with the relation items. The volume of flagged behaviour also far exceeds chance: 13 control-flow items qualified where about 0.27 would be expected in an unchanged process, and every item's difference was larger than in about 995 in 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: the occurrence and relation items likely describe one underlying behaviour (the new repetition) rather than independent confirmations, and the indicators cannot show the exact structural form — for example, whether A30, A31 and A36 repeat as one block or independently; A31 appears only in the occurrence evidence. Positions and lags are event positions, not clock times. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the mixed orderings are entirely a by-product of the repetitions rather than a separate relaxation of sequencing rules; the data shows both changes occurred together, not that they are independent. [OCC002, REL003, REL005, LAG002]

Other differences: A14's relative position moved slightly earlier and became more variable [POS001]; the A05→A14 gap shifted from one step to two–three steps, based on about 105 traces per window [LAG001]; the within-trace positions of A30 and A36 became more spread out with slightly later medians [POS002, POS003].
