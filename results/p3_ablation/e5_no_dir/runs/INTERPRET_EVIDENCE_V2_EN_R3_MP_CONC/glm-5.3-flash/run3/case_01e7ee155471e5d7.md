### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the execution order between the activity group A30, A31, A36 and the activities A21 and A22 reversed: previously the group ran after A21, between A21 and A25, while in the later window it runs before A22 — consistent with sitting between A20 and A22 — leaving A21 immediately before A25. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Supporting signs: the reversal is shown directly by relational evidence in about 199 of 200 traces across all six activity pairs, with co-occurrence unchanged at 100%, so this is an ordering change rather than activities becoming optional or being replaced; the position and distance shifts agree with one and the same rearrangement; and 19 qualifying items against roughly 0.27 expected without change puts the total far beyond what random re-splits of a stable process produce. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Limitations and competing explanation: the evidence establishes the order reversal but not which side moved in absolute terms, since positions are event positions rather than clock times; the internal sequence of the A30/A31/A36 group in the later window can be read only from the single exceptional trace, so a simultaneous change in the group's internal order cannot be ruled out; and about 1 in 200 later traces still follows the earlier order, so the new ordering is near-complete but not necessarily absolute.
