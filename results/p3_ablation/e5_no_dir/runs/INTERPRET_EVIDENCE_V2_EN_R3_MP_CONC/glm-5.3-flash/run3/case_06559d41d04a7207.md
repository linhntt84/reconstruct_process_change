### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, almost every case executed exactly one of three alternative activities: A19 in about 35 of 100 cases, A20 in about 30, and A21 in about 35, and they practically never appeared together (co-occurrence in at most about 1 of 200 cases). In the later window, each of them appears in only about 1 of 200 cases. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL007, REL008, REL016]
- A26 and A27 were not observed at all earlier, and A28 in only 1 of 200 cases; in the later window each of the three occurs in all 200 cases. Every pair of them co-occurs in every case, with both orders nearly equally frequent (about 50 vs 49 in 100), so the three run together without a fixed internal order. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL031, REL032, REL037]
- The new block occupies the region the alternatives used to fill. Earlier, A44 preceded the alternative and the alternative preceded A29 in essentially all cases containing one (for example, A44 before A19 and A19 before A29 in practically every A19 case). Later, A44 precedes A26 in all but about 1 of 200 cases, and A26 precedes A29 in about 99 of 100 cases. The step distance from A44 to A29 grew from mostly 2–3 steps (about 75 in 100 at two) to 4–5 steps (about 90 in 100 at four). [REL012, REL013, REL020, REL021, REL027, REL028, REL033, REL034, LAG005]
- A10 appeared in about 26 of 100 cases earlier and in about 11 of 100 later, and its relative position became more spread out. [OCC001, POS001]
- A29, A44 and A45 still occur in every case in both windows; A29's average relative position moved slightly later, from about 0.40 to about 0.44 of the case. [POS008, REL034, REL035]
- A46 appeared in about 69 of 100 cases earlier and about 58 of 100 later; in the later window, when it co-occurs with A26, A26 comes first in about 3 of 4 such cases. [REL036]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the earlier pattern, in which each case executed exactly one of the three mutually exclusive alternatives A19, A20 or A21 between A44 and A29, was replaced by a block in which A26, A27 and A28 are all executed in every case, again between A44 and A29 but without a fixed order among the three. The participation evidence is direct and well supported: the alternatives fall from roughly 30–35 in 100 cases each to about 1 in 200, while A26, A27 and A28 rise from not observed (or 1 in 200) to all 200 cases in each window. The relational evidence shows the new behaviour directly — full co-occurrence of each new pair, both orders about equally often, A44 before A26 and A26 before A29 — and the lengthening of the A44-to-A29 distance from 2–3 to 4–5 steps, together with A29's slightly later average position, is consistent with three activities being inserted on that path. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL031, REL032, REL033, REL034, REL037, LAG005, POS008]

The volume of qualifying evidence far exceeds what an unchanged process would produce (65 control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected by chance), and the key participation changes rest on the full 200 cases in each window, so sampling noise cannot account for them. Limitations: many items describe the same underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations; zero earlier participation strictly means only that A26 and A27 were not observed then; and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows capture a different mix of cases (or re-labelled activities) rather than a modification of one and the same process. [OCC005, OCC006, REL031]

Other differences: A10's participation dropped from about 26 to about 11 of 100 cases with a more spread-out position, though its step-distance changes rest on only about 7 later-window observations; A46 became somewhat less frequent (about 69 to 58 of 100 cases) and now typically follows A26; A45 still occurs in every case but now follows A26 in about 64 of 100 and is interleaved with it in about 36. [OCC001, POS001, LAG001, LAG004, REL036, REL035]
