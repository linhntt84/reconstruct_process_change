- Activities that were optional became universal. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the exclusive branch choice between A22 and the three-activity group A31/A32/A37 was replaced by a fixed sequence in which both parts always execute: the group now runs directly after A20 and before A23 and A21, while A22 runs in its previous late position between A21 and A26. Earlier, about 54 in 100 traces executed A22 and the remaining 46 executed the group instead, with the group placed after A21 and A23; in the later window both parts execute in essentially every trace, the group's order relative to A21 and A23 flipped from always "after" to about 995 in 1000 "before", and the A20→A23 distance grew from one to four steps. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL006, REL011, REL014, LAG001, POS006]

The volume of qualifying items far exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 36 control-flow items against roughly 0.35 expected by chance for the 70 tested units (about 100 times the chance expectation), every item's difference exceeding those seen in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. The occurrence, relation, position and lag items all describe one coherent restructuring on large support (200 traces per window) rather than independent coincidences.
