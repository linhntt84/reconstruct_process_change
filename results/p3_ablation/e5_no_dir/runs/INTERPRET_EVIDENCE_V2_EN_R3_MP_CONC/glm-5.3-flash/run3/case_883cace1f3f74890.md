### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new step was inserted between A27 and A29, executed as exactly one of three alternative activities — A20 in about 41 of 100 traces, A25 in about 30 and A19 in about 30 — placed after A27 and before A29, and before rather than after A28 in about 55 of 100 cases. Earlier, A27, A28 and A29 followed one another directly, with nothing between A27 and A29 except A28. The signs fit together across indicator families: appearance from zero participation, mutual exclusivity of the three alternatives, stable ordering towards A27 and A29, widened step distances on all three pairs in that segment, and the small opposite position shifts of A27 and A29 expected when one event is added between them. Chance is implausible as a whole: 24 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.3 would be expected without change, and 0 of 200 earlier traces versus 59–82 of 200 later traces far exceeds sampling variation for windows of this size. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, POS004, POS005]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish activities newly added to the process from existing activities that were simply never triggered in the earlier window — zero earlier participation only means they were not observed there. The insertion point relative to A28 varies, so the new step does not occupy one fixed slot. Many of the relation, lag and position items describe the same underlying insertion and are not independent confirmations. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL004, REL008, REL011]
