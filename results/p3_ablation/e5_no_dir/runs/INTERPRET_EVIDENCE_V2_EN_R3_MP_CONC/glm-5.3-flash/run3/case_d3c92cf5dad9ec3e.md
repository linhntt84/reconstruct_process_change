### Observed differences

- Participation is essentially stable: A20, A22, A23, A25 and A29 occur in every trace in both windows, while A19 appears in about 30 in 100 traces before and 33 in 100 in the later window, A21 in about 35 before and 30 after, and A24 in about 37 before and 40 after [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008].
- The always-present activities changed their placement. Before, A20, A22 and A23 sat in the later part of the trace (mean relative position about 0.63), A19, A21 and A24 in the earlier part (about 0.34–0.36), with A25 at about 0.38 and A29 at about 0.57; in the later window A20, A22 and A23 sit at about 0.38, A19, A21 and A24 at about 0.66–0.68, A25 at about 0.45 and A29 at about 0.64 [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008].
- The order between the two groups reversed almost completely. Before, A25 preceded A20, A22 and A23 in essentially every co-occurrence (about 99 in 100); in the later window A20, A22 and A23 precede A25 in about 99 in 100, with no interleaving in either window [REL008, REL015, REL018]. The same reversal holds between A29 and A20, A22 and A23 [REL009, REL016, REL019].
- Every qualified pair between the sporadic activities (A19, A21, A24) and the ever-present ones also reversed: for example, A19 preceded A20 in all 30 of 100 co-occurring traces before and in none after, where A20 now precedes A19 in 33 of 100 [REL001]; the same flip appears for A19 with A22, A23, A25 and A29 [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005], and for A21 and A24 against A20, A22, A23, A25 and A29 [REL006, REL007, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL017, REL020, REL021]. The sporadic activity also slipped behind A25 and A29: A21 preceded A25 in about 35 of 100 co-occurrences before, while A25 precedes A21 in about 29 of 100 in the later window [REL012, REL004, REL005, REL013, REL020, REL021].
- Adjacencies moved with the reversal. Before, A40 preceded A25 by about two steps in essentially every trace, and the ordered pairs A40→A20, A40→A22 and A40→A23 occurred within five steps in only about 1 in 200 traces; in the later window A40 precedes each of A20, A22 and A23 within one to three steps in essentially every trace, and precedes A25 by about four steps [LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]. Before, A21 and A24 immediately preceded A25 (lag 1 in about 68 of 69 and 71 of 71 such traces); after, A24→A25 within five steps survives in only about 2 of 200 traces, while A23→A25 now occurs within one to three steps in essentially every trace [LAG001, LAG003, LAG002]. A29 is followed by A21 after one step in 58 of the 59 traces containing A21, where before this ordered pair appeared once, and the distance from A29 to A30 shrank from about four steps to about two [LAG004, LAG005].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two segments of the process exchanged their order. In the earlier window a typical trace ran A40, then — in the roughly one trace in three where one of them occurred — A19, A21 or A24, then A25, then A29, and only afterwards the ever-present A20, A22 and A23; in the later window A20, A22 and A23 run directly after A40 (within one to three steps), followed by A25 and then A29, with the A19/A21/A24 activity now after A29 near the end. Participation of every involved activity is stable, so this is a change in ordering, not in which activities run. The support is direct and consistent: all 21 qualified activity pairs between the two groups reverse with near-complete magnitudes, the positional shifts of about a third of the trace match the relational reversals, A40's successors change accordingly, and the 38 qualified control-flow items far exceed the fewer than 1 expected without change [REL001, REL006, REL008, REL015, REL018, REL019, POS002, POS005, POS001, POS006, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009].

Limitations: the many items largely mirror one underlying trace-level difference rather than independent confirmations. The evidence cannot distinguish a single exchange of two segments from two separate relocations (the sporadic activity moving from before A25 to after A29 while A20/A22/A23 move before them), it says nothing about the order within {A20, A22, A23} or within {A19, A21, A24} or about whether A19, A21 and A24 are mutually exclusive alternatives, and POS/LAG reflect event positions, so no clock-time claim is possible [REL004, REL012, LAG006, POS001].

Other differences: only minor participation shifts — A19 in 60 of 200 traces before vs 66 after, A21 in 70 vs 59, A24 in 73 vs 79 — which the evidence cannot separate from ordinary sampling variation between two 200-trace windows [POS001, POS003, POS006].
