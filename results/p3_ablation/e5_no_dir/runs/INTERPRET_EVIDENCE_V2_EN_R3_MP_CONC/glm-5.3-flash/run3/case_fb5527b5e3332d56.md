### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch in which A27, A28 and A33 executed between A21 and A23 became effectively skipped, while A22 — previously an alternative taken in about half of the cases — became executed in nearly every case, so that A21 → A22 → A23 is now the near-universal path. In the earlier window the two branches split cases roughly evenly and were never mixed; in the later window the trio branch appears in only 1 of 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001]

This exceeds what an unchanged process would produce: 30 control-flow items qualified where about 0.31 were expected by chance, a roughly hundredfold excess, and all items describe one coherent shift with empirical strength near 0.995 — participation, co-occurrence/order and distance evidence agree, and the earlier window shows a stable near-even split over 200 traces rather than a rare one-window variant. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the trio branch still exists but is now selected extremely rarely (it was still executed once in the later window), rather than having been removed altogether; with 200 traces per window these look the same, and the internal order of A27, A28 and A33 in the later window cannot be assessed from that single trace.
