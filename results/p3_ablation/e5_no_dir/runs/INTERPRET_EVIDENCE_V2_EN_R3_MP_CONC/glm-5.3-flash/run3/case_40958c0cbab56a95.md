### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of activities between A44 and A29 was restructured: A26, A27 and A28, which every case executed in the earlier window (always all three, in mixed order), are in the later window almost entirely displaced by a single activity chosen per case from the mutually exclusive alternatives A19, A20 and A21, whose shares together cover essentially every case. The new activity keeps the old embedding — A44 before it and A29 after it, with A11 and A12 upstream — and occupies the same relative position (median about 0.36 versus 0.37 of the trace), and the A44-to-A29 distance shortens from about 4–5 to 2–3 event positions, exactly what three consecutive activities giving way to one would produce. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL013, REL014, REL018, REL019, REL021, REL025, REL026, REL032, REL033, REL036, REL038, REL039, REL043, REL047, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG009]

This is unlikely to be a chance artefact: 74 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.64 expected if nothing had changed, and the key counts are substantial on both sides (200 versus 3 traces for the old trio; 2–4 versus 60–75 for the new activities), which rare-variant sampling cannot plausibly produce. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether A19, A20 and A21 are genuinely new activities or renamed counterparts of A26, A27 and A28, and it cannot distinguish a modification of one variant from a shift in the mix of two coexisting variants; about 1.5 in 100 later-window cases still show the old trio, so the earlier behaviour persists rarely. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL036]

Other differences: A10 runs in more cases than before (about 14 to 23 in 100), and the new activities co-occur with A45 and A46, often preceding A46; these shifts are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001, REL020, REL028, REL041]
