### Observed differences

- The earlier window split between two mutually exclusive routes: A27 ran in about 52 in 100 cases, while A32, A33 and A38 ran in about 49 in 100 cases, and the two options essentially never shared a case. In the later window, A27 ran in 199 of 200 cases, while A32, A33 and A38 each appeared in only 1 of 200. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL022, REL023, REL024]
- In the earlier window, A32, A33 and A38 always executed together as a block (each pair co-occurred in about 49 in 100 cases, matching each activity's participation); in the later window, no two of them share more than a single case (neither present in about 995 in 1000). [REL028, REL029, REL030]
- A14, A15, A26 and A28 appear in every case in both windows, with only minor positional shifts (mean relative-position changes of about 0.01–0.02). [POS002, POS003, REL002, REL003]
- A27's relations tightened accordingly: it now co-occurs with A14, A15, A26 and A28 in 199 of 200 cases, versus about 52 in 100 before; A14 and A26 precede A27 in about 97–98 in 100 shared cases, and A28 follows A27 in about 96 in 100, whereas before, A28 ran without A27 in about 49 in 100 cases. [REL009, REL013, REL017, REL021]
- A04 became much rarer and later: about 68 in 100 cases before versus about 22 in 100 in the later window; its mean relative position moved from about 0.21 to 0.40 (upper quartile 0.19 to 0.70). Before, it followed A14 within one step in about 99 in 100 of its co-occurrences and preceded A26 or A28 in about 62–64 in 100 cases; in the later window it precedes them in only about 12–13 in 100 and never co-occurs with A32, A33 or A38 (about 32–33 in 100 before). - Smaller shifts: A15 now follows A14 within one step in about 47 in 100 cases (about 19 before), and A28 follows A26 exactly two steps later in about 95 in 100 (previously split between 2 and 4 steps); A02 and A08 appear in more cases (about 5 to 15, and 5 to 21 in 100). [LAG002, LAG003, OCC001, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative route executing A32, A33 and A38 together was effectively abandoned while A27 became a near-universal step: in the earlier window, cases divided roughly evenly between a route with A27 (about 52 in 100) and a route with the A32/A33/A38 block (about 49 in 100, mutually exclusive with A27), whereas in the later window A27 executes in virtually every case and the block activities are essentially no longer executed. Because the block is nearly absent in the later window, this is a change in participation — A27 became near-mandatory and the alternative branch effectively stopped being taken — not a re-weighting between two still-active paths. Supporting signs: direct participation measurements with substantial support on both sides (A27: 103 vs 199 traces; block activities: 97–98 vs 1 trace each), consistent relational evidence (A27 now co-occurs with A14, A15, A26 and A28 in about 995 in 1000 cases), and a screening yield far above the no-change expectation (53 qualifying items versus about 0.55, all control-flow), which chance cannot explain as a whole. Limitations: the later window contains only one trace with each block activity, so the evidence shows they were essentially not executed, not that they were removed from the process definition; and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows draw on different case mixes rather than a modified process. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL009, REL013, REL017, REL021, REL022, REL024, REL028, REL029, REL030]
