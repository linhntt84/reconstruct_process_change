### Observed differences

- The relative order of two groups of activities reversed. In the earlier window, A35, A36 and A41 ran before A26 and A27 in about 97–99 of 100 traces; in the later window, A26 and A27 ran before A35, A36 and A41 in about 97–98 of 100. All six activities occurred in every trace in both windows, so this is not a path being replaced by another. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015]
- Positions shifted consistently with that reversal: in the later window A35, A36 and A41 sit later in the trace (mean relative position about 0.40 before vs about 0.58 after), while A26 and A27 sit earlier (about 0.60 → 0.53 and 0.46 → 0.39). [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Typical spacings between consecutive activities changed: A25 and A27 were usually four steps apart (92 in 100) and are now adjacent (98 in 100); A26 and A30 were usually adjacent (99 in 100) and are now usually four steps apart (91 in 100); A49 and A50 moved from usually two steps apart (74 in 100) to adjacent (84 in 100). [LAG002, LAG005, LAG009]
- A02 appears far less often: about 75 in 100 traces in the earlier window versus about 21 in 100 in the later window (149 vs 42 of 200 traces), with mean occurrences per trace down from 0.81 to 0.23; when it does appear, it now sits around mid-trace (mean relative position about 0.93 before vs 0.55 after, and much more variable). [OCC001, POS001]
- A02's ties to other activities loosened: before, it co-occurred with each of A25, A26, A27, A30, A35, A36, A41 and A49 in about 75 of 100 traces, usually after them (about 66–68 of 100); in the later window co-occurrence is about 21 of 100, and in about 79 of 100 traces the other activity runs without A02. The same holds for A50, which A02 previously preceded in about 66 of 100 traces. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- Two infrequent activities became somewhat more common: A06 appears in about 7 of 100 traces before vs about 19 after (13 vs 38 traces), and A10 about 9 vs 22 (17 vs 43 traces). [OCC002, OCC003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Thirty-four control-flow items qualified where fewer than one (0.58) would be expected if nothing had changed, so the volume of flagged differences as a whole is not what random re-splits of a stable process produce. [OCC001, REL010]

Main hypothesis: in the later window the execution order between two groups of always-executed activities is reversed relative to the earlier window — A35, A36 and A41 previously ran before A26 and A27, and now A26 and A27 run before A35, A36 and A41. This is shown directly by six relation items in which both activities co-occur in every trace in both windows and the dominant order flips almost completely, corroborated by the opposite position shifts of the two groups and by A25–A27 becoming adjacent. Limitations: the evidence uses event positions and pairwise relations rather than clock time or a full sequence model, so alternative global arrangements that reproduce the same pairwise orders cannot be excluded; a few lag items (for example A25–A36 and A25–A41) rest on only 2–3 after-window observations and were not relied upon. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Other differences: A02 was largely dropped from execution — participation fell from about 75 to 21 of 100 traces, it now appears mid-trace instead of near the end, and A25, A26, A27, A30, A35, A36, A41, A49 and A50 now usually run without it (about 79 of 100 traces); A06 and A10 appear somewhat more often; A49 and A50 are now typically adjacent rather than two steps apart. These participation and placement changes are reported separately from the order-reversal hypothesis, and the A06/A10 increases rest on modest counts (13 → 38 and 17 → 43 traces).
