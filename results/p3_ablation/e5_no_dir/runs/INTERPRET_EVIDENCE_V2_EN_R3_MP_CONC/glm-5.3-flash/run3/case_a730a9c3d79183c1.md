### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two mutually exclusive groups of activities — A33 with A35 and A36 on one side, A37 with A38 and A39 on the other — changed from alternatives to jointly executed stages: in the earlier window a case contained one group or the other (about half of cases each, cross-group co-occurrence about 1 in 100), while in the later window all six activities run in about 99 of 100 cases, the A33/A35/A36 group first and the A37/A38/A39 group after it, with the order inside each group remaining unfixed [REL039, REL040, REL041, REL045, REL046, REL047, REL050, REL051, REL052, REL037, REL044, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010]. The change is shown directly by the relational items and is consistent across participation, co-occurrence, relative order, positions and step distances, including the six activities now sitting between A30 and A31 (their within-five-steps ordered pair fell from about 199 to 5 of 200 cases) and the newly appeared A33→A37 and A36→A37 adjacencies (about 195 of 200 cases) [LAG004, LAG009, LAG014, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010]. Screening produced 99 qualifying control-flow items where fewer than 1 would be expected without change, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, so sampling fluctuation alone cannot explain the pattern [OCC005, REL039, LAG009]. Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a modified routing decision within one process from a shift in the mix of complete case variants between the windows, and positions and lags are event positions, so no claim about clock timing is made.

Other differences: A04 became largely optional, dropping from about 59 to about 26 of 100 cases with a later typical position, while its former partners usually run without it [OCC002, POS001, REL001, REL011]; A02, A08 and A10 occur roughly three to four times as often as before [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]; A47 and A48 are slightly more often separated by one intervening event [LAG015].
