### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A26, A27 and A28 each ran in every one of the 200 traces, essentially exactly once per trace; in the later window they are nearly gone, appearing in only about 1–2 of 200 traces each [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006].
- Earlier, the three always ran together in the same trace (co-occurrence 1.0) without a fixed order among themselves (for example, A26 came before A27 in about 52 of 100 executions and after it in about 47), always after A44 (about 98–99 in 100) and always before A29 (about 99–100 in 100) [REL031, REL032, REL033, REL034, REL035, REL036, REL038, REL039].
- In the later window, A19, A20 and A21 — absent earlier (0–1 of 200 traces) — each take part in roughly a third of traces (about 31, 34 and 37 in 100) and never repeat within a trace [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].
- The three newcomers almost never share a trace (pairwise co-occurrence at most about 1 in 100), so they behave as mutually exclusive alternatives; about two in three later traces contain one of them [REL013, REL014, REL020].
- Each newcomer sits between A44 and A29: it co-occurs with A44 and with A29 in essentially every trace in which it appears, with A44 first (about 30–37 in 100 traces) and itself before A29 (about 30–37 in 100) [REL018, REL019, REL024, REL025, REL029, REL030].
- A11 and A12, when they co-occur with a newcomer, always precede it (about 16–20 in 100 traces); earlier they likewise preceded the old block in nearly every shared trace [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL007, REL010].
- The span from A44 to A29 shrank: earlier, A29 followed A44 four positions later in about 93 of 100 traces; in the later window it follows two positions later in about 92 of 100. Both activities still run in every trace in both windows, with only small shifts in mean relative position (A29 about 0.43 to 0.40, A44 about 0.31 to 0.33) [LAG010, POS007, POS008].
- The newcomers occupy a mean relative position (about 0.36–0.37) close to that of the former block (about 0.37) [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A44 and A29 is executed differently in the later window: the always-executed block A26/A27/A28 no longer runs (from 200 of 200 traces to about 1–2 of 200), and in that segment at most one of the newly appearing activities A19, A20 or A21 executes — mutually exclusive, each in roughly a third of traces, together in about two in three — so the distance from A44 to A29 shortens from about four to about two positions. Participation for all six activities changes directly and with large support in both windows, the relational items place each newcomer after A44 and before A29, and the distance change rests on about 200 traces per window [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL018, REL019, REL024, REL025, REL029, REL030, LAG010]. Screening qualified 63 control-flow items where roughly 0.5 would be expected without a change, so the evidence volume as a whole cannot be a sampling artefact, and the items consistently describe this one difference [OCC004, REL031, LAG010].

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a complete removal of A26, A27 and A28 from a drop to a very small execution share, since only 1–2 later observations exist for each, and the later-window spacing items for the old block rest on single traces [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, LAG001, LAG007]. It also cannot identify which single activity occupies the A44-to-A29 segment in the roughly one third of later traces containing none of A19, A20 and A21 [REL013, LAG010]. A competing reading the evidence cannot exclude is that the old block's disappearance and the newcomers' appearance are two independent changes touching the same segment rather than one exchange [REL015, REL021, REL026].

Other differences: A11 and A12 now appear in about 56 of 100 traces, up from about 47 of 100 earlier, while their ordering role is unchanged — they precede the middle-segment activities (formerly A26/A27/A28, now A19/A20/A21) in nearly every trace in which they co-occur with them [REL001, REL004, REL007, REL010].
