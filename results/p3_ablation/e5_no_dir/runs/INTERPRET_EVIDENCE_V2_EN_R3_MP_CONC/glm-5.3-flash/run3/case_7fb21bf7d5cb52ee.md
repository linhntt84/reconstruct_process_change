### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A10 was a rare, mid-trace step: it appeared in about 19 in 100 traces, at a mean relative position of about 0.55. In the later window it is common and early: about 70 in 100 traces, mean occurrences per trace up from about 0.21 to 0.78, and mean position about 0.19, in the first fifth of the trace (by event order, not clock time). [OCC003, POS001]
- In the later window, A10 typically runs directly after A14: the ordered pair A14→A10 occurs within one step in about 98 in 100 of the after-window observations (about 130 traces), whereas before it was seen in only 11 traces at mixed distances. Direct A14→A15 adjacency fell from about 49 in 100 traces to about 20 in 100 while two-step gaps rose, consistent with A10 now sitting between them; A10→A15 within one step appears in about half of the after-window observations. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence directly and consistently supports the possibility that the ordering structure around A10 and the group A20, A22, A23, A24, A25, A26 changed. In the later window, A10 is a frequent early step — present in about 70 in 100 traces (previously about 19 in 100), typically directly after A14, and preceding each of A20–A26 in roughly 60 in 100 traces — while the group's previously strict internal order (A20, A22 and A23 almost always before A24, A25 and A26) became mixed, with several pairs typically reversed and fixed step distances replaced by varying ones. The relational and positional items show these changes directly and agree with one another; the samples behind them are large (most relations observed in all 200 traces per window, A10's rise resting on 38 versus 140 traces); and the screening flagged about 65 items where fewer than 1 would be expected without a change, with every item's difference exceeding all but about 1 in 200 random re-splits. [OCC003, POS001, LAG002, REL003, REL015, REL020]

What the evidence cannot establish is the case-level form of the new behaviour: pairwise orders, positions and distances cannot distinguish a single new sequence from a mixture of old and new orderings across cases, and the evidence contains no relational items for A04 or A08 that would connect their decline to this reordering. [OCC001, OCC002]

Other differences: A04 and A08 became rarer (about 21 → 11 in 100 traces and about 20 → 4 in 100); A10 now co-occurs with A45 and A46 in about 48 in 100 traces (about 15–16 before) and appears alone with them in about 22 in 100 (about 3–4 before); A15's position moved slightly later; A45→A48 and A47→A48 distances became more concentrated (three steps in about 83 in 100 observations, one step in about 99 in 100).
