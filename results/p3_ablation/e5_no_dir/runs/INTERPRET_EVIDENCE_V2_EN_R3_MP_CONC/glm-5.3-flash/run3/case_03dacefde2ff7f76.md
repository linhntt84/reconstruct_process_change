### Observed differences

- The three optional activities A19, A20 and A25 appeared at similar rates in both windows (roughly 3 in 10 traces earlier, about 3–4 in 10 later), but their placement moved markedly later within the trace: their mean relative position rose from about 0.31–0.34 to about 0.58–0.59, a shift of roughly 0.27–0.29 [POS001, POS002, POS003].
- Their order relative to A26 and A27 reversed. In the earlier window, A19, A20 or A25 preceded A26 and A27 in about 30–38 in 100 traces and virtually never followed them; in the later window they followed A26/A27 in about 30–36 in 100 traces and preceded them in at most about 1 in 100 [REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007].
- Co-occurrence of these pairs barely changed (about 3 in 10 traces in both windows), so this is a reversal of execution order, not a change in how often the activities run together or in how often they occur [REL001, REL004, REL006].
- Relative to A28, the order changed from fixed to mixed: earlier, A19 and A25 always preceded A28 when they co-occurred (about 32–37 in 100 traces); later, both orders appear in similar shares (about 15 vs 15–22 in 100) [REL003, REL008].
- Adjacencies around this block changed accordingly: the immediate succession A25→A26, seen in about 63 of 200 traces earlier, is essentially unobserved later (1 trace, at distance 5); A27→A28 and A28→A32, previously immediate in about 96–98 in 100 traces, later split roughly half at distance 1 and half at distance 2; A27→A32 moved from distance 2 to distance 3 [LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG007].
- New close relations involving A20 appeared: A20→A28 and A28→A20 at distance 1 in about 30–36 traces later (1 trace each earlier), and A20→A32 at distance 1–2 in about 66 traces later (1 trace earlier); the earlier-window counts are too small to characterise the prior behaviour [LAG001, LAG002, LAG006].
- A26 and A27 themselves shifted only slightly earlier (mean position about 0.35→0.32 and 0.58→0.55) [POS004, POS005].
- The gap between A45 and A26 narrowed: A45 preceded A26 at distance 2 in about 84 in 100 traces earlier, and immediately (distance 1) in about 89 in 100 later [LAG008].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of optional activities A19, A20 and A25 — each present in roughly 30–38% of traces in both windows — is executed later in the later window: it previously ran early in the trace, before A26, A27 and A28, and now runs after A26 and A27, around A28 (between A27 and A32, in some traces immediately before A28 and in others immediately after it). The order reversal is shown directly by eight relational items with stable co-occurrence; the position shift of about 0.27 for A19/A20/A25 against only about 0.03 for A26/A27 rules out a uniform shift of all positions; and the lag items corroborate it, with the old A25→A26 adjacency disappearing and one-step insertions appearing between A27–A28 and A28–A32. With 21 qualifying items against roughly 0.37 expected for an unchanged process, sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. Limitations: positions are event-order positions, not clock time; the new placement varies from trace to trace (mixed order around A28), so a single fixed new slot is not established; the new-adjacency items for A20 rest on only one earlier observation each; and the evidence cannot rule out a broader restructuring of the segment around A26–A32 rather than a pure relocation of the three activities [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007].

Other differences: A45 now immediately precedes A26 in about 9 in 10 traces, where a one-step gap was typical earlier; what filled that gap before cannot be determined from this evidence [LAG008].
