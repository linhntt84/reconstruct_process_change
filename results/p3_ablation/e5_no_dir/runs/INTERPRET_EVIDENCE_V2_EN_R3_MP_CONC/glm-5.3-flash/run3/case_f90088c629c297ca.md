### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, exactly one of three activities — A14, A15 or A20 — is executed between A36 and A21, where previously A21 directly followed A36 and none of the three was observed. The choice is exclusive (no trace contains two of the three) and is made in about 99 in 100 later traces; the combined participation of the three (about 37 + 29 + 34) matches the share of traces in which A21 now follows A36 at distance two, with A21 still immediately following A36 in the remaining about 1 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL005, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001]

The screening produced 17 qualifying items in the control-flow family against roughly 0.22 expected without a change, so the volume of evidence cannot be explained by chance re-splits of stable behaviour. The placement claim rests on direct relational and lag evidence rather than on frequency shifts alone, and the occurrence, relation, position and lag figures are internally consistent (they describe one coherent structural difference rather than scattered effects). Limitations: the ordering evidence is based on event positions, not clock time; the evidence cannot show what determines which of the three activities is chosen in a given trace; and finite windows cannot fully exclude a rare pre-existing variant, although three activities each appearing in roughly a third of 200 later traces after appearing in none of 200 earlier traces goes well beyond typical sampling fluctuation.
