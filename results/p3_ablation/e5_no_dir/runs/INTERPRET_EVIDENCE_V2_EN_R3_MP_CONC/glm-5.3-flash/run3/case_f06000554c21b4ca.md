### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of activities A35, A36 and A41 was relocated within the trace: in the earlier window it typically ran after A26 and before A30 (typical order A25 → A27 → A26 → A35/A36/A41 → A30), whereas in the later window it typically runs between A25 and A27 (typical order A25 → A35/A36/A41 → A27 → A26 → A30). As part of this, A30, which previously followed the block, now directly follows A26, and A27 no longer immediately follows A25.
