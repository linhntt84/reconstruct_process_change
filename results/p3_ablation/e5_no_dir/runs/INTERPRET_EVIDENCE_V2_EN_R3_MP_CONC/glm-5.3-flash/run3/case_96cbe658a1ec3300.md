### Observed differences

- The execution order between two groups of activities reversed. In the earlier window, A26 preceded A35, A36 and A41 in about 98–99 in 100 traces, and A27 preceded them in about 99–100 in 100; in the later window, A35, A36 and A41 each preceded A26 in about 97–98 in 100 traces and preceded A27 in about 97 in 100. Both activities of every pair still occurred together in all traces in both windows. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the sequencing between the group A35, A36, A41 and the activities A26 and A27 reversed: in the earlier window A26 and A27 preceded A35, A36 and A41 in nearly every trace, whereas in the later window they follow them, with both sides still executed together in every trace. All six pair-wise relations flip in the same near-complete way, the positional shifts of all five activities point in the matching directions, and each difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Screening makes a sampling artefact unlikely for the ensemble: 21 control-flow items qualified, against roughly 0.38 expected if nothing had changed. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]

Limitations: the evidence shows the order reversal directly but not the exact mechanism — for instance, whether the three activities moved earlier as a block or A26 and A27 moved towards the end — and positions and distances are counted in event positions, not clock time. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a broader re-sequencing of the surrounding steps, suggested by the A25 and A30 changes below, of which the observed reversal is only the visible part.

Other differences: A25 and A27, previously adjacent, were about 4–5 steps apart in the later window; A30 moved from about 4–5 steps after A26 to immediately after it; and A30 no longer follows A35, A36 or A41 within five steps except in about 1–2 of 200 later traces.
