### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 formed a mutually exclusive three-way choice: every case contained exactly one of them — A19 in about 32 in 100 cases (64 of 200), A20 in about 28 in 100 (56), A25 in about 40 in 100 (80) — and the three never occurred together in one case. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006]
- In the later window this branch is essentially gone: each of the three activities appears in only about 1 in 200 cases, and about 99 in 100 cases contain none of them. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006]
- A27, A28 and A29 occur in essentially every case in both windows. Before, the chosen alternative always ran between A27 and A29 (A27 before the alternative, and the alternative before A29, in every co-occurring case), while its position relative to A28 was mixed — roughly half the cases before A28 and half after. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- Step distances tightened accordingly: A27→A28 was two steps apart in about 44 in 100 cases before and is immediately consecutive in about 98 in 100 later; A28→A29 went from about 53 in 100 two steps apart to about 98 in 100 consecutive; A27→A29 went from typically three steps (94 in 100) to typically two (97 in 100). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- Minor: A29's mean relative position shifted slightly earlier (about 0.56 to 0.55 on the position scale), consistent with cases being one activity shorter; the later position profiles of A19, A20 and A25 each rest on a single case and are not reliable. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports that the three-way exclusive choice between A19, A20 and A25 — previously executed exactly once per case, with shares of roughly 32, 28 and 40 in 100 — is no longer taken in the later window, so that cases proceed from A27 through A28 to A29 back-to-back where the chosen alternative previously ran between them. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, REL002, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The participation drop is large (64, 56 and 80 cases down to one each) and consistent across the relational items (co-occurrence of the three activities with A27/A28/A29 falling from about 28–40 in 100 to near zero) and the tightened step distances; 22 evidence items qualified in screening against roughly 0.31 expected for an unchanged process, and each item's difference exceeds all but about 1 in 200 random re-splits. Limitations: with only one observed occurrence of each activity in the later window, the evidence cannot distinguish complete removal of the branch from its having become extremely rare, nor a structural removal from a routing or case-mix change that stops cases from entering it; no replacement activity with increased participation is observed.
