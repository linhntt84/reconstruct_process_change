### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a reversal of execution order between two groups of activities. In the earlier window, A20, A22 and A23 (present in every trace) sat at about 39–40% of the trace length, followed by A25 (~45%) and A29 (~65%), while A19, A21 and A24 (in 31–38% of traces) sat late, at roughly 67–69% of the trace. In the later window this is inverted: A19, A21 and A24 sit early (~35%), A25 (~39%) and A29 (~58%) in the middle, and A20, A22 and A23 late (~64%). [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS009]

- The pairwise relations show the same swap directly. A20 preceded A25 in 98 of 100 traces before, whereas A25 precedes A20 in all traces after; A22 preceded A29 in all traces before, whereas A29 precedes A22 in about 97 of 100 after (A23 similarly); A20 preceded A19 whenever both ran before (about 31 in 100), whereas A19 now precedes A20 in 44 of 100; A20 preceded A21 in 37 of 100 before, whereas A21 now precedes A20 in about 29 of 100; and A25 preceded A24 in about 31 of 100 before, whereas A24 now precedes A25 in 28 of 100. [REL010, REL013, REL015, REL017, REL021, REL025, REL028, REL029]

- A02 turned from a rare into a common early activity: it appears in 16 of 200 traces before (8%) versus 68 of 200 after (34%), its median position moved from about 0.53 to about 0.33 of the trace, it now follows A40 directly (one-step distance in about 62 traces) and reaches A25 within two steps, and it precedes A20, A22, A23, A25 and A29 in roughly 32 of 100 traces each. [OCC001, POS001, LAG001, LAG005, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL006]

- Distances along the sequence changed accordingly: A29 is now followed by A20 within 1–3 steps in essentially all traces (before, this ordered pair was observed only once, at distance 5), the A40-to-A25 distance shortened from 4–5 to 2–3 steps, and the A29-to-A30 distance grew from about 2 to about 4 steps. [LAG003, LAG004, LAG007]

- Participation of the optional activities shifted: A19 now appears in about 44 of 100 traces (31 before), while A21 and A24 appear in fewer (about 28.5 and 29 versus 38 and 31.5 in 100); A19, A21 and A24 still occur only in traces that also contain A20. [POS002, POS004, POS007, REL010, REL015, REL016]

- A41 now repeats more often: about 1.77 occurrences per trace versus 1.51 before, with recurrence support up from 0.325 to 0.40 while it keeps appearing in every trace. [OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process was reordered: the segment containing A20, A22 and A23 and the segment containing A19, A21 and A24 exchanged their positions around A25 and A29, and A02 — previously rare — now runs as a frequent early step directly after A40 and before A25. In the earlier window the order ran A20/A22/A23 → A25 → A29 → A19/A21/A24; in the later window it runs A40 → A02 → A19/A21/A24 → A25 → A29 → A20/A22/A23. [REL017, REL025, REL010, REL029, LAG003, LAG005, POS003, POS002]

This reading is direct and consistent: the order reversals are observed relationally and are near-deterministic (for example 98 of 100 traces one way before and 100 of 100 the other way after, each based on all 200 traces in both windows), the position and distance figures agree with the same sequence, and the 48 qualifying items are roughly ninety times the 0.52 expected for an unchanged process, so the ensemble is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. [REL017, REL025, POS003, LAG003, LAG005]

Limitations: the evidence shows pairwise relations and positions, not complete variants, so it cannot distinguish one coherent block swap from a finer-grained re-sequencing that produces the same pairwise pattern, nor whether untested activities (for example A30) moved with these blocks; POS and LAG use event positions, so no claim about clock-time timing is implied. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows sample different case populations with wholesale-different sequences; the behavioural difference between the windows would still stand, but it could reflect a different variant mix rather than a single re-sequencing. [REL017, LAG004]

Other differences:
- A41 is repeated more often per trace in the later window (about 1.77 versus 1.51 occurrences, recurrence support 0.325 to 0.40, participation unchanged at every trace). [OCC002]
- Participation within the optional group shifted: A19 more common (about 44 of 100 traces versus 31) and A21 and A24 less common (about 28.5 and 29 versus 38 and 31.5 in 100). [POS002, POS004, POS007]
- The distance from A29 to A30 grew from about two to about four steps. [LAG004]
