### Observed differences

- Three activities that were practically absent earlier now occur regularly: in the earlier window A19, A20 and A25 appeared in 0, 1 and 0 of 200 traces, while in the later window they occur in about 38, 30 and 35 in 100 traces respectively, roughly 0.3–0.4 times per trace and essentially never twice within one trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- In the later window these three behave as mutually exclusive alternatives: any two of them occur together in at most about 1–2 in 100 traces, whereas in the earlier window about 99–100 in 100 traces contained neither activity of each pair. Allowing for the small overlaps, about 99 in 100 later traces contain exactly one of the three. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- The three activities sit between A41 and A26. A41 and A26 were each present in all traces in both windows, but in the earlier window never together with any of the three; in the later window, whenever one of the three occurs it almost always follows A41 and precedes A26 — A19 in about 38 in 100 traces on both sides, A20 in about 28–29, A25 in about 32–33. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- Accordingly, the distance from A41 to A26 grew by one step: adjacent in about 82 in 100 earlier traces versus about 1 in 100 later traces, two steps apart in about 83 in 100 later traces (about 18 in 100 earlier), and three steps apart in about 14 in 100 later traces. [LAG001]
- The new activities occupy positions around 0.36–0.37 of the trace, and A26 shifted slightly later, from a mean position of about 0.37 to about 0.40, consistent with a step inserted before it. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]
- All 17 qualifying items show differences larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a step is executed between A41 and A26 in nearly every case, taking the form of exactly one of three alternative activities — A19 (about 38 in 100 cases), A25 (about 35) or A20 (about 30) — whereas in the earlier window none of the three was observed and A41 was typically directly followed by A26. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001]

The placement is shown directly and consistently: the relation items put each of the three after A41 and before A26, the A41→A26 lag distribution shifts from one to two steps, and A26's position moves slightly later. The screening context makes a chance origin of the whole pattern implausible: 17 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.28 would be expected if nothing had changed. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001, POS004]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show how the change came about, and it cannot rule out that the later window simply covers a different case mix in which this three-way step always belonged to a separate variant, rather than a modification of the same process; it also cannot exclude that the step existed earlier at a very low rate (at most about 1 in 100 traces, given 0–1 occurrences in 200). In about 14 in 100 later traces an additional, unidentified step lies between A41 and A26 besides the new activity. [LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
