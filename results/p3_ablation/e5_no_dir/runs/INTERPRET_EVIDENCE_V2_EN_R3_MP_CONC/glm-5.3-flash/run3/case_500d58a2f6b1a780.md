### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, each of the activities A25, A26 and A31 occurred about 3.4 times per case and recurred (more than once per case) in about 75 in 100 cases; in the later window, each occurs about once per case and recurrence is seen in fewer than 1 in 100 cases. Participation is unchanged: every case in both windows contains all three activities. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The relationship between A25 and A31 changed from mixed to fixed: earlier, in 75 in 100 cases the two were interleaved or repeated within the case (and in 25 in 100 A25 strictly preceded A31); in the later window, A25 precedes A31 in about 995 in 1000 cases, with interleaving or repetition almost gone. The two still co-occur in every case in both windows. [REL001]
- The gap structure tightened accordingly: pairs of A25 followed by A31 at a distance of two steps rose from about 12 in 100 to about 92 in 100, while pairs at a distance of five steps nearly disappeared. A31 followed by A25 within five steps, observed 150 times earlier, is observed only once in the later window, though that figure rests on a single observation. [LAG001, LAG002]
- The average relative position of the activities within a case barely moved (A25 about 0.43, A31 about 0.48 in both windows), but the spread around it roughly halved (standard deviation about 0.12 to about 0.06), so the activities sit at the same place in the case but far more predictably. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of activities A25, A26 and A31, which executes together in every case, was repeated roughly three to four times per case in the earlier window (with A31 leading back to A25 between iterations), while in the later window it runs a single time per case in a fixed order, A25 followed by A31 two positions apart. Supporting signs: the drop in occurrences and recurrence is observed directly for all three activities with full support in both windows (200 cases each); the relational and lag evidence shows the same collapse from interleaved/repeated execution to one fixed pass; and the screening produced 8 qualifying items where fewer than 1 (0.18) would be expected by chance, so the set of items as a whole is unlikely for an unchanged process. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001, LAG001, LAG002]

These items largely describe one underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations of each other. No directional evidence qualified, so the exact edges of the former cycle and the position of A26 within the block are inferred from its identical occurrence profile rather than observed directly, and the A31-to-A25 item rests on a single later-window observation. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the earlier pattern was interleaved execution without a strict sequential cycle rather than a loop back to A25; likewise, it cannot distinguish whether the repetition was removed entirely or still exists but is taken in fewer than 1 in 100 later cases.
