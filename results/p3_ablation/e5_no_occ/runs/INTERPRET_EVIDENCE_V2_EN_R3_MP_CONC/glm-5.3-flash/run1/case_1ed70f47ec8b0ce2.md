### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two blocks of the flow exchanged their places around the A20-to-A24 stretch. In the earlier window, each case ran one of A14, A16, A19 between A35 and A20, and the varying-order sequence through A15, A17 and A18 between A24 and A25; in the later window, the sequence through A15, A17 and A18 runs between A35 and A20, and the one-of-three choice among A14, A16, A19 runs between A24 and A25, immediately before A25. [REL008, REL009, DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, DIR007, LAG001, LAG002, POS001, POS002]

The support is direct and consistent: the order reversals are complete rather than partial, the successor profiles of A14, A16, A19, A15, A17, A18, A24 and A35 change exactly as this hypothesis predicts, the two step-gap measures move in opposite directions (A24→A25 from four steps to two; A35→A20 from two to four), and the position shifts of A20 and A24 match the change in block length. Chance is also implausible as an explanation: 39 control-flow items qualified, against roughly 0.34 expected for an unchanged process, and every item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled data. [DIR007, LAG001, LAG002, POS007, POS008, DIR001–DIR008, POS001–POS008, REL001–REL021, LAG001–LAG002]

Limitations: the many pair relations overlap heavily (most describe the same traces), so they are not independent confirmations of one another; and the evidence cannot distinguish a single exchange of the two blocks from two separate relocations, nor does it characterise whatever may run between A20 and A24, so additional changes in that stretch cannot be ruled out.
