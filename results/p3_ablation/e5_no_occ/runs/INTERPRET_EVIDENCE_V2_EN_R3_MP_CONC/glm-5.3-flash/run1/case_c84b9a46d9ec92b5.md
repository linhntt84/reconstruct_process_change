### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative branch formed by A19, A20 and A25 — three mutually exclusive activities that previously lay between A41 and A26 — is no longer executed in the later window, so that A41 now leads directly to A26. Before, roughly 87 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions entered one of the three alternatives and A26 followed two positions later; in the later window the three activities are unobserved, A41 transitions straight to A26 in about 86 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, the A41–A26 distance is one position, and A26 sits slightly earlier. With 72 units tested, 26 items qualified against fewer than 1 expected without a change, and the directional, positional, relational and lag evidence all describe the same bypass. [DIR005, LAG001, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL003, REL004, REL008, REL009, REL013]

Limitations: non-observation does not prove removal — A19, A20 and A25 may still occur very rarely — and the single later occurrence of A19 (routing to A12) rests on one trace and cannot establish whether anything replaced the branch; moreover, many items restate the same underlying trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the three activities were replaced by other activities not visible in this evidence rather than simply skipped. [DIR001, POS001]
