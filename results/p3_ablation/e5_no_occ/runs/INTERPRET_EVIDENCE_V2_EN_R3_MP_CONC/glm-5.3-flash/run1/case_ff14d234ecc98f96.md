### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A44 and A29 was restructured: in the earlier window every case executed the three activities A26, A27 and A28 there (in no fixed order among them), while in the later window exactly one of the three alternative activities A19, A20 or A21 executes in that position in almost every case, with A44 before it and A29 after it. This rests on direct relational evidence: A44's successor profile moved from the trio to the alternatives while A44 itself remained in about all traces in both windows; the trio's co-occurrence fell from 100 in 100 to about 0; the alternatives sit between A44 and A29 in both directions and are mutually exclusive; and the A44-to-A29 distance shortened from 4–5 steps to 2, matching three steps becoming one. The screening context also fits: 64 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.55 expected for an unchanged process, far too many to attribute to chance as a whole. Limitations: the old trio still occurs in about 1 in 100 later traces, so it became rare rather than disappearing; the evidence cannot show whether A19, A20 and A21 are new steps or renamed replacements; and the many agreeing relation items largely restate one underlying difference rather than confirming it independently. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that two different variants of an otherwise unchanged process simply dominate the two windows; the near-absence of each behaviour in the opposite window (0 of 200 traces versus nearly all) makes this unlikely but cannot rule it out completely.
