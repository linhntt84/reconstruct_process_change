### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two blocks of activities exchanged their order around A25 and A29. In the earlier window, a trace ran A40, then typically one of the alternatives A19/A21/A24, into A25, with A29 later and the A20/A22/A23 group last, leading mainly to A30; in the later window, A40 leads mainly into the A20/A22/A23 group, which leads on to A25, followed by A29 and then one of A19/A21/A24, which leads mainly to A30. The change is shown directly and consistently: all 21 observed pairwise relations among these activities flip in near-deterministic proportions (200 traces per window), the position distributions of all eight activities shift accordingly, and both boundary activities rewire — A40 and A29 change their main successors from one block to the other while the exits to A25 and A30 exchange places. [REL016, REL021, REL023, REL024, REL031, POS002, POS003, POS005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR012, DIR013, LAG026, LAG029]

The volume of flagged behaviour is far beyond chance: 90 control-flow items qualified where fewer than 1 (0.84) would be expected without change across 169 tested units. Stable participation for every involved activity rules out the activities being added, removed, made optional or repeated differently, and the near-deterministic order flips at support 200 per window make sampling noise an unlikely explanation for the reorder itself. Two limitations remain: positions and lags are measured in event positions, so the change is established in sequence order rather than clock time; and because each window shows essentially a single order, the evidence cannot distinguish a changed ordering constraint from a changed mix of two pre-existing order-variants. [REL023, REL031, POS003, POS005, DIR013, LAG029]

Other differences: A08 occurs in far fewer traces (about 64 → 29 of 100), sits later and routes much more variably (A08→A16 falling from about 93 to 31 of 100 of its outgoing transitions); A04 and A10 occur in more traces, with new secondary routes out of A14, A43 and A45; and A15→A16 became more dominant. These participation and routing changes are not part of the block-reorder hypothesis. [POS001, DIR002, LAG001, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR014, DIR015]
