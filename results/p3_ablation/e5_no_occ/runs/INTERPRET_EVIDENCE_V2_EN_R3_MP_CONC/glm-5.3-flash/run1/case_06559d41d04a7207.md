- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A21 each ran in roughly a third of the cases (about 35, 30 and 36 in 100) and almost never together (co-occurrence at most about 0.5 in 100), behaving as alternatives to one another; in the later window each appears in only about 1 in 200 cases. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the stage between A44 and A29 was restructured: in the earlier window, one of three alternative activities (A19, A20 or A21) ran per case and was followed directly by A29, while in the later window these alternatives are essentially gone and three activities (A26, A27 and A28) run in every case, in no fixed order among themselves, between A44 and A29. This rests on direct relational evidence (A44 precedes each of A26–A28, and each of A26–A28 precedes A29, in about 99 in 100 cases), on participation evidence (0 of 200 to 200 of 200 cases for A26 and A27; about 35 in 100 down to 1 in 200 for A19–A21), and on the A44-to-A29 step distance growing from about 2 to about 4 steps. With 67 qualifying items against roughly 0.59 expected if nothing had changed, chance cannot explain the evidence as a whole. The main limitation is that this is a possibility supported by two finite samples, not a confirmed modification: zero participation only means an activity was not observed, so the evidence cannot fully exclude that A26–A28 existed earlier but was never exercised, nor can it distinguish a modified process from a switch in which mix of process variants was executed.
