### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the block A35–A36–A41 is executed after A26 and A27 and directly before A30, whereas in the earlier window it ran before them, between A25 and A27 — that is, the block's placement moved from before the A27/A26 segment to after it, with A25 now feeding straight into A27 and A30 following the block instead of immediately following A26. This rests on direct relational evidence: six activity pairs that co-occur in all traces in both windows reverse a near-deterministic order (about 97–99 in 100 one way before, about 97–98 in 100 the other way after), the successor profiles of A25, A26, A35, A36 and A41 change exactly as this hypothesis predicts, and the lag shifts match (A25→A27 from about four steps to one; A26→A30 from one step to about four). The volume of qualifying evidence (42 items against roughly 0.61 expected if nothing had changed) rules out chance as an overall explanation. Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different case mixes or variants of a heterogeneous process rather than a modified single process, and the internal order within the block varies in both windows, so the hypothesis concerns the block's placement relative to A25, A26, A27 and A30, not a fixed internal sequence.
