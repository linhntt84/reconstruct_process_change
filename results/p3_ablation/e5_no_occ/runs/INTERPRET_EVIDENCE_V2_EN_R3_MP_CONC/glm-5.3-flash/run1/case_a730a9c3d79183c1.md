### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the late stage of the process now combines two activity groups that were previously mutually exclusive alternatives: in the earlier window each case executed either A33/A35/A36 (about 49 in 100 traces) or A37/A38/A39 (about 52 in 100), followed by A31, whereas in the later window nearly every case executes all six activities — A33/A35/A36 in varying order first, A37/A38/A39 in varying order after — with A14 and A30 before the block, A15 usually before it, and A31 after it. The change is shown directly by relational evidence computed over all 200 traces in both windows (cross-group co-occurrence rising from about 0–2 to about 98 in 100), it is consistent across the routing, position and distance indicators, and the screening context makes chance implausible for the set as a whole: 99 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 1 expected without a change. [REL039, REL045, REL050, REL013, REL019, REL025, REL031, POS005, POS008, DIR005, DIR006, LAG004]

Limitations: positions and distances are event positions and step counts, not clock time; a few lag items rest on very few observations in one window (for example A33→A47 and A35→A48 with 1–26 observations) and were not relied on; and the finite windows cannot rule out that they sample different case or variant mixes rather than one modified process. [LAG010, LAG012]

Other differences: A04 was largely dropped from this part of the process — participation fell from about 59 to about 26 of 100 traces, A14 now usually continues to A15 rather than to A04, and A04, when present, sits later and more variably; this participation change is distinct from the merge itself. Smaller shifts at the end of the process made the A47→A48 and A45→A46 successions slightly less dominant. [POS001, DIR002, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG015, DIR009, DIR010]
