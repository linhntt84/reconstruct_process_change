### Observed differences

- The largest difference is a reversal in which of two alternative activity blocks follows A20. In the earlier window, about 75 in 100 traces contained A20 followed by the A21–A22–A23 block and about 25 in 100 contained A20 followed by the A24–A25–A26 block; in the later window the shares are reversed (about 25 vs about 75 in 100). [DIR003, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- A20's outgoing transitions show the same redistribution: before, about 26% of its observed outgoing transitions went to A21, 25% to A23 and 23% to A22 (about 13% to A24); after, about 28% go to A26, 25% to A24 and 22% to A25, while A21, A22 and A23 each fall to about 7–8%. A20 itself appears in essentially every trace in both windows. [DIR003, REL007]
- Each block keeps its internal shape in both windows: A21, A22 and A23 always occur together, as do A24, A25 and A26; within each block the activities appear in both orders in similar proportions (no fixed internal order before or after); and the two blocks co-occur in at most about 1 in 100 traces, so they remain mutually exclusive alternatives. [REL013, REL014, REL021, REL034, REL035, REL039, REL015, REL028]
- A30 occurs in essentially all traces in both windows and switches with the executed block: before it co-occurred with A21 in about 75 in 100 traces (interleaved in about 32 in 100) and with A24 in about 26 in 100; after it co-occurs with A24 in about 76 in 100 (interleaved in about 36 in 100) and with A21 in about 25 in 100. [REL018, REL025, REL031, REL036, REL040, REL043]
- Participation moved with the blocks: A25 appeared in about 26 in 100 traces before and about 76 in 100 after, at a similar relative position in the trace (mean about 0.70 vs 0.67). Gaps within the now more frequent path lengthened somewhat: A20→A24 within one step in about 54% of cases before vs about 33% after (three-step gaps up from about 20% to 39%), and A25→A30 within one step down from about 32% to 18%. [POS002, LAG001, LAG002]
- Separately, A10 became about three times more common (about 6 vs 21 in 100 traces) and appears later in the trace (mean relative position about 0.57 vs 0.82). A32 now routes about 20% of its observed outgoing transitions to A10 (about 1% before) while A32→A33 falls from about 95% to 80%; A10 in turn leads to A33 in about 90% of its observed outgoing transitions after (before, these were spread over A35 ~27%, A33 ~18%, A14 ~18% and others, based on only 11 traces), and A32→A33 within one step fell from about 56% to 39%. [POS001, DIR004, DIR002, LAG003]
- A02 was observed in 18 of 200 traces before but only 2 of 200 after, with a completely different successor profile (spread over nine successors before; only A15 and A27 after); with only two supporting traces in the later window this is weakly grounded. [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the choice between two existing alternative paths after A20 reversed: in the later window, about three in four traces execute the A24–A25–A26 block (usually together with A30) where about one in four did before, while the A21–A22–A23 block fell from about three in four to about one in four traces. The reversal is shown directly in A20's outgoing-transition shares (A20 present in essentially all traces in both windows) and consistently in co-occurrence and order relations computed over all 200 traces per window; every activity involved is well observed in both windows, each block keeps its mixed internal order, and the blocks stay mutually exclusive, so only the shares between existing alternatives shift rather than activities being added, removed or reordered. The 54 qualifying items (all control-flow) are about 70 times the roughly 0.74 expected if nothing had changed, and each difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits. Limitations: the many relation items describe overlapping views of the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot rule out that the later window simply contains a different mix of case types (proportionally more cases whose variant uses A24–A26) rather than a changed choice within individual cases — both would look identical here. [DIR003, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL021, REL034, REL039, REL015, POS002]

Other differences: A10 occurs about three times as often (about 6 vs 21 in 100 traces), later in the trace, now typically reached from A32 and followed by A33 — an increased-participation and insertion change around A10, A32 and A33 that is not part of the main hypothesis. A02 was observed in only 2 of 200 later traces (18 earlier) with a changed successor profile, but this rests on too few observations to interpret. [POS001, DIR002, DIR004, LAG003, DIR001]
