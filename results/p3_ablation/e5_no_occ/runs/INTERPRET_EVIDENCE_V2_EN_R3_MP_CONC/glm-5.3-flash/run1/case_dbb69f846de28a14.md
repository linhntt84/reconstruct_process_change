### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the block of A36, A37 and A42 was displaced and made optional: A25 now flows directly to A28, and the triple — still always executed together and in mixed internal order — appears in about 47 in 100 cases as an alternative to A27 on the branch after A26, with A31 closing either branch (A26 → A27 → A31, or A26 → A36/A37/A42 → A31). Earlier, essentially every case ran A25 → A36/A37/A42 → A28 → A26 → A27 → A31 with all activities mandatory. The placement change is shown directly by relational and positional evidence — co-occurrence between A27 and each triple member collapsing from 100 in 100 cases to about 1, the order reversal between A26/A28 and the triple, and the triple's mean position moving from about 0.40 to about 0.57 — and is consistently corroborated by the successor changes at A25 and A26, the participation drops (triple from all cases to about 47 in 100, A27 to about 54 in 100) and the distance changes (A25→A28 from 4–5 steps to 1; A26→A31 splitting into gaps of 2 and 4 steps; A37→A31 within five steps rising from 1 to 93 cases). The 44 qualifying control-flow items compare with roughly 0.5 expected without a change, so chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole, and all items fit this single account.
