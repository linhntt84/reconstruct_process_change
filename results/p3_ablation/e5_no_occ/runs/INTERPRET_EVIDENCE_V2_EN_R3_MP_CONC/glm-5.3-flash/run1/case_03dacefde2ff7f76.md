### Observed differences

- Before, A45's outgoing transitions went mainly to one of A19, A20 or A25 (about 31%, 26% and 28% of them); in the later window, about 89 in 100 go directly to A26, and A45 is immediately followed by A26 (one step apart in ~89 of 100 observed pairs, versus 2–3 steps before). [DIR006, LAG008]
- Before, A19, A20 and A25 led almost exclusively to A26 (about 97%, 89% and 98% of their outgoing transitions); in the later window, they lead mainly to A32 (about 46–58%) and A28 (about 38–51%). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The order between the A19/A20/A25 group and A26/A27 reversed: before, the group activity came first in roughly 30–38 of 100 traces containing both, with the reverse order essentially unobserved; in the later window, A26/A27 come first in roughly 31–36 of 100 traces and the old order has disappeared. - The group activities moved from about 0.31–0.34 to about 0.58–0.60 of the trace position, while their participation stayed similar (each in roughly 30–38 of 100 traces, together about one per case in both windows); A26 and A27 still occur in every trace at nearly unchanged positions (about 0.32–0.35 and 0.55–0.58). - In the later window, A19/A25 and A28 occur in both orders (about 15–16% each way), whereas before the group activity always came first (about 31–37%). [REL003, REL008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the alternative group A19/A20/A25 — of which about one activity runs per case — is executed after A27 and around A28, feeding into A32, whereas before it ran between A45 and A26; A45 now leads straight to A26. This rests on direct signs: the reversed order relations, the rewired successors of A45 and of the group activities, the group's position shift while A26 and A27 stayed in place, and the disappearance of the immediate A25→A26 succession. Participation is essentially unchanged, so this reads as a relocation of an existing block rather than activities becoming optional or removed. The screening context fits: 27 control-flow items qualified where about 0.4 would be expected without a change, and each difference exceeded about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007]

Limitations: the items draw on the same traces, so they corroborate one underlying difference rather than confirming it independently. The precise new arrangement around A28 is not pinned down: the group and A28 appear in both orders in the later window, which fits either two alternative paths or interleaving/looping, and the evidence cannot separate these; a residual mix with the old ordering also cannot be excluded for the A28 relation. Positions are event positions, not clock times. [REL003, REL008, LAG007]

Other differences: the choice among the three alternatives shifted slightly (A19 appears in about 38 versus 31 in 100 traces, A25 in about 32 versus 38), a small difference that two 200-trace windows could produce without any change. [POS001, POS002, POS003]
