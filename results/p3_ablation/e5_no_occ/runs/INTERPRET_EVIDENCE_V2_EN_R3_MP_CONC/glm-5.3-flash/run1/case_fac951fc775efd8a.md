### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Fifty-six control-flow items qualified, against roughly 0.54 expected if nothing had changed, so chance re-sampling of the two windows cannot account for the pattern as a whole [DIR001, DIR008, POS003, REL017, REL024, LAG003, LAG004].

Main hypothesis: the evidence supports that the two middle segments exchanged places and re-targeted their exits. In the earlier window, A29 opened a segment in which one of A19, A21, A24 ran (around two-thirds into the trace) and each of them led almost always to A30, while A40 opened a segment of A20, A22 and A23 (around four-tenths into the trace) that led to A25. In the later window, A29 opens the A20/A22/A23 segment, which now exits to A30, and A40 opens the A19/A21/A24 segment — in about a third of cases via A02 — which now exits to A25; A19 also appears in more traces (44 vs 31 in 100), A21 and A24 in slightly fewer (28 vs 38, and 29 vs 32). [DIR002, DIR004, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, POS002, POS004, POS007, REL017, REL024, LAG004, LAG007]

Supporting signs: every tested pair of activities from the two segments reversed its order almost completely; the successor changes at the six moved activities and at A29/A40 are near-total; the position and distance shifts point the same way; and the picture is consistent across direction, position, relation and lag indicators rather than resting on a single item. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL018, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL025, REL026, REL028, REL030]

Limitations: the indicators describe pairwise orders, marginal positions and local distances, not complete variants, so a different global rearrangement producing the same pairwise picture (for example additional untested entry or exit points around the two segments) cannot be excluded; nor can these windows show whether A02 is a newly inserted step or an existing rare step that was re-used. [POS001, DIR001, REL010, LAG004]

Other differences: A43 occurs in more traces later (about 92 vs 69 in 100) but its outgoing behaviour is essentially unchanged, still leading to A44 in about 93–98 in 100 transitions; the flagged item reflects only small shifts among rare edges. [DIR010]
