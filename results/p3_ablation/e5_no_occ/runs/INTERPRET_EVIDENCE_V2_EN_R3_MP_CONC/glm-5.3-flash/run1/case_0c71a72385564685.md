### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a step was inserted between A41 and A26: in the earlier window A41 was followed directly by A26 (adjacent in about 83 in 100 traces, with A19 and A25 unobserved and A20 in 1 of 200 traces), while in the later window exactly one of A19, A20 or A25 — chosen per case, since the three almost never co-occur — runs between them, with A41 before it and it before A26, and A26 still present in every trace, slightly later in the trace. [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

The screening qualified 18 items where about 0.31 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the set of differences is far beyond what a stable process typically produces, and the insertion is shown directly rather than inferred: the successor change at A41, the new activities' outgoing edges into A26, and the one-step increase in the A41–A26 distance all describe the same behaviour. Limitations: the many relation items reflect one underlying insertion, not independent confirmations; positions are event positions, not clock time, so nothing can be said about elapsed time; and the direct A41→A26 edge is still observed rarely (about 2 in 100 outgoing transitions). An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is what determines the choice among A19, A20 and A25 in a given case, and whether further unflagged activities occasionally sit between A41 and A26 (distance 3 in about 14 in 100 traces).
