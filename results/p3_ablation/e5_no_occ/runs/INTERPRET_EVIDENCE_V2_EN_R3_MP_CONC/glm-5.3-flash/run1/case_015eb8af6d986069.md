### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the activities A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26, A25 and A32 are executed as one fixed sequence (A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32), whereas in the earlier window the same activities — all present in essentially every trace in both windows — were ordered flexibly and partly in the reverse direction (for example A24 before A20 in about 73 in 100 traces, and A20/A26 in both orders about equally often). This is a change in ordering constraints between existing activities, not the addition or removal of a path. [REL014, REL016, REL017, REL019, DIR009, DIR013, LAG034]

The change is shown directly by relational evidence, and three indicator views agree: pairwise orders flipped or became fixed, distances became deterministic and mutually consistent with a single chain (A31 reaches A24 at four steps and A26 at five, exactly as the sequence implies), successor shares concentrated at every link, and the small position shifts point the same way. With 80 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected for an unchanged process, and every involved activity observed in about 100% of traces in both windows, chance sampling or a single rare variant cannot explain the pattern as a whole. [REL016, LAG034, LAG036, DIR011, POS005]

The evidence is aggregate over two windows of 200 traces each; it cannot distinguish a modified process definition from a shift in the mix of pre-existing execution variants between the windows, and it says nothing about what produced the new order. [REL014, LAG006, DIR007]

Other differences: A08's participation fell from about 72 to about 29 in 100 traces, it now occurs later and reaches A16 far less often (about 90→39 out of 100 outgoing transitions; within one step about 99%→43%), and A15 rarely leads into A08 any more (about 42→7 out of 100) — a participation and placement change the reordering hypothesis does not cover; weakly supported redistributions at A02, A04 and A10 and small distance changes around A14/A15/A16 complete the picture. [POS001, DIR003, DIR006, LAG001, DIR001, LAG003]
