### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural repositioning with a participation change: the group A36, A37 and A42, previously an optional branch taken in about 46 in 100 traces after A26 and before A31 (while A26 otherwise went directly to A27), appears in the later window as a mandatory block in nearly every trace, running between A25 and A28; A26 now leads almost exclusively to A27 and then A31, and A28 runs about four positions after A25 instead of immediately after it.
