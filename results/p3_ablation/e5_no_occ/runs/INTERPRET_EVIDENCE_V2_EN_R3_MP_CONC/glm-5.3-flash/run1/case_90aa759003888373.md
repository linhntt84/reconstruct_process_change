### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace ran the same fixed chain: A36 always led to A14, and A14, A15, A20 and A21 always occurred together with A36 in the single order A36 → A14 → A15 → A20 → A21 (co-occurrence 100 in 100 traces for every one of these pairs). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, DIR001]
- In the later window, this full chain still runs in about 53 in 100 traces, but in the remaining about 47 in 100 traces A14, A15 and A20 are all absent together: A14/A15, A14/A20 and A15/A20 now co-occur in only about 53 in 100 traces (down from 100 in 100), with about 47 in 100 containing neither. [REL001, REL002, REL005]
- A36's outgoing transitions changed accordingly: before, A36 always led to A14; in the later window it leads to A14 in about 53 in 100 transitions and directly to A21 in about 47 in 100. [DIR001]
- The A36–A21 distance shrank accordingly: before, A21 followed A36 four steps later in every trace; in the later window the gap is one step in about 47 in 100 cases and four steps in about 53 in 100. [LAG001]
- A21 still occurs in every trace in both windows; its mean relative position moved only slightly earlier (about 0.43 to about 0.40 of the trace) and became more variable, consistent with a mixture of full and shortened traces. [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the block A14 → A15 → A20 between A36 and A21 is skipped in roughly half of the cases: A36 leads directly to A21 (about 47 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, at a one-step gap instead of four), while A14, A15 and A20 drop out together in those traces and continue to run in the original full sequence in the other about 53 in 100. Because a predecessor now bypasses a group of activities rather than merely re-weighting paths that already existed, this reads as the middle activities having become optional rather than a simple shift between alternative routes. [DIR001, LAG001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]

Supporting signs: twelve control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.21 expected if nothing had changed, far beyond what random re-splits of stable behaviour produce; all items describe one coherent difference (the new A36→A21 edge, the shortened gap, and the joint disappearance of A14/A15/A20 in the same ~47% of later traces) rather than independent changes; each item's difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits; and the skip share rests on about 94 of 200 later traces, not on a rare one-off variant. [screening, DIR001, LAG001, REL003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that a direct A36→A21 run existed before at a low rate that the earlier 200 traces did not capture, with only its share rising, nor can it show what determines whether the block is skipped in a given case. The twelve items also largely mirror the same underlying trace behaviour, so they should not be read as twelve independent confirmations. [screening, REL001, DIR001]
