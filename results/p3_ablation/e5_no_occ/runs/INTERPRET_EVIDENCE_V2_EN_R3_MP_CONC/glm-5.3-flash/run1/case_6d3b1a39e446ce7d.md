### Observed differences

- **Activities that were alternatives became near-universal.** In the earlier window, A27 appeared in about 55 of 100 traces and each of A36, A37 and A42 in about 46–47 of 100, and A27 almost never shared a trace with any of the trio (about 1–2 in 100). In the later window, A27 and all three of A36/A37/A42 appear in essentially every trace (199–200 of 200), and A27 co-occurs with each trio member in about 995 of 1,000 traces. [POS003, POS006, POS007, POS008, REL011, REL012, REL013]

- **The A36/A37/A42 trio moved from the late to the early part of the trace.** Earlier, the trio (which always ran together, in no fixed internal order) sat at a mean relative position of about 0.59, after A28 and A26 and before A31; A28 preceded the trio and A26 preceded the trio in every trace where they jointly appeared. In the later window the trio sits at a mean relative position of about 0.41, and the order is reversed: the trio precedes A28 in about 97–99 of 100 traces and precedes A26 in about 98–99 of 100. [POS006, POS007, POS008, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL017, REL018, REL019]

- **Successors changed accordingly.** Earlier, A25 led to A28 in about 96 of 100 of its observed outgoing transitions; in the later window A25 leads instead to one of A36, A37 or A42 (roughly 31–34 in 100 each), and reaches A28 only after the trio — A25 is followed by A28 within one step in about 96 of 100 traces earlier, but within four or five steps in about 99 of 100 traces in the later window, with the trio in between (trio-to-A28 within one to three steps in about 97–99 of 100). The trio's outgoing transitions, which earlier reached A31 (about 29–39 in 100), now reach A28 (about 31–34 in 100). [DIR001, LAG001, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

- **The tail of the process became fixed on A27.** A26 now leads to A27 in about 98 of 100 of its outgoing transitions (about 54 in 100 earlier), A28 precedes A27, and A31 follows A27 two steps later in about 97 of 100 traces; earlier, A31 followed either A27 after two steps (about 54 in 100) or the trio after four steps (about 42 in 100). The mean relative positions of A26, A27, A28 and A31 all shifted later, by roughly 0.03–0.07. [DIR002, REL005, REL009, REL010, LAG003, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

- **What did not change:** the trio keeps no fixed internal order in either window (each pair occurs in both orders at similar shares), and A25's own position is essentially stable (mean relative position about 0.37 vs 0.35). [REL020, REL021, REL022, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports, as a possibility, a restructuring of the control flow between A25 and A31: the trio A36/A37/A42, which in the earlier window ran together (in mixed internal order) as an alternative to A27 between A26/A28 and A31 in about 46 of 100 cases, appears to have become a mandatory stage placed directly after A25 and before A28, while A27 became mandatory, so that in the later window nearly every case runs A25 → A36/A37/A42 → A28 → A26 → A27 → A31. The order and placement change is shown directly by relational evidence (the trio's relation to A28 and A26 reversed; A28 and A26 now both precede A27; A27 precedes A31), corroborated by successor changes at A25, A26 and the trio, by participation rising to nearly 100% for A27 and the trio, and by consistent position shifts; 44 control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.5 expected without change, so chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole [REL011, REL014, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG003, screening].

Limitations: the many agreeing pairwise items largely describe one and the same underlying trace pattern rather than independent confirmations; the exact slot of A26 relative to A28 rests on position statistics rather than a directly observed relation; and a few lag items about the trio's new surroundings rest on only 1–2 observations in one window (for example, the ordered pair A26 then A36 has only 2 supporting observations in the later window). An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows sampled different mixes of pre-existing case types rather than a modified process, although the reversal of the trio's order relative to A28 and A26 and the near-universal co-occurrence of formerly alternative activities are difficult to reconcile with re-sampling alone. [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, REL020]
