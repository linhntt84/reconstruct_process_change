### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every case executed exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities: A19 in about 32 of 100 cases, A20 in about 28, and A25 in about 40; the three never occurred together in the same case. In the later window, each of them appears in only about 1 of 200 cases. [REL001, REL002, REL006, POS001, POS002, POS003]
- The three activities sat mid-trace (mean relative position about 0.51–0.52), always after A27 and before A29; A27, A28 and A29 themselves occurred in all 200 cases in both windows. [REL003, REL005, REL010, REL012, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]
- Routing concentrated around the resulting gap: from A27, the direct transition to A28 grew from about 53% of A27's outgoing transitions to about 97%, while the combined share leading to A19/A20/A25 (about 44 in 100 before) disappeared; A27 reaches A28 within one step in about 98 of 100 later cases, versus about 54 before. [DIR004, LAG001]
- From A28, the direct transition to A29 grew from about 45% to about 98.5% of its outgoing transitions, and A27 reaches A29 typically two steps later instead of three. [DIR005, LAG002, LAG003]
- A29's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (about 0.56 to 0.55), consistent with the shorter remaining sequence. [POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of three mutually exclusive activities A19, A20 and A25 — of which every earlier case executed exactly one, between A27 and A29 — is skipped in almost all later cases, so that control passes from A27 almost always directly to A28 and from A28 directly to A29. [REL001, REL003, REL005, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: 24 control-flow items qualified against about 0.35 expected without change, many times the chance expectation; the participation collapse is observed directly (about 32/28/40 in 100 earlier cases versus about 1 in 200 later cases for each of A19/A20/A25, with A27, A28 and A29 present throughout in both windows), and the successor profiles of A27 and A28 shift coherently with it. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG001]

The evidence cannot distinguish complete removal of the block from near-total skipping, since near-zero participation only means the activities were almost not observed; nor can it characterise what happens in the rare remaining cases. A competing explanation it cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of cases between the windows rather than a modification of the process itself. [POS001, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: in the single later cases that still contain A19, A20 or A25, their outgoing transitions lead to A44, A08 and A42 respectively, but each of these observations rests on one case. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
