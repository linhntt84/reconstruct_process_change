### Observed differences

- Participation shows the largest difference. In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 each appeared in just under half of the traces (about 47 in 100), essentially always together and never without A26, while A26, A41 and A42 appeared in every trace; in the later window, A19, A20 and A25 appear in about 995 of 1000 traces, and the share of traces containing none of them falls from about 53 in 100 to about 1 in 200. [REL001, REL003, REL008, REL012]

- The relative order of these activities is unchanged. In both windows the executed sequence is A41, then A19, then A25, then A20, then A26: for example, A41 precedes A19 in about 47 of 100 traces before and about 97 in 100 after, and A20 precedes A26 in about 46 of 100 before and about 99 in 100 after. [REL002, REL004, REL007, REL008, REL012]

- Routing out of A41 flipped. Before, about 52 of 100 of A41's observed outgoing transitions went to A26 and about 44 of 100 to A19; in the later window, about 92 of 100 go to A19 and fewer than 1 in 100 to A26 (these shares are over A41's outgoing transitions, not over cases). [DIR002]

- The distance from A41 to A26 changed accordingly. Before, A26 followed A41 within one step in about 53 of 100 cases and four or five steps later in about 47 of 100; in the later window, one-step adjacency appears in about 1 of 200 cases, about 89 in 100 show a four-step gap and about 11 in 100 a five-step gap. [LAG001]

- A20's outgoing behaviour is essentially stable — about 96 of 100 of its outgoing transitions go to A26 before and about 99 of 100 after — but the number of traces containing A20 rises from 96 to 199 out of 200. [DIR001]

- A26's average relative position in the trace moves slightly later (about 0.41 to 0.43) and A41's slightly earlier (about 0.33 to 0.31); these are event-position shifts consistent with the block now lying between the two activities in every case, not clock-time evidence. [POS001, POS002]

- A45 keeps its overall frequency (about 60 of 100 traces in both windows) but its relation to A19 changes: before, it appeared without A19 in about 33 of 100 traces, whereas in the later window it always co-occurs with A19 and follows it. A42, present in every trace throughout, shows more interleaving with the block (interleaved-or-repeated in about 17 of 100 traces before, about 46 in 100 after). [REL005, REL006, REL010, REL011, REL014, REL015]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block A19, A25, A20 — executed in that order between A41 and A26 — changed from optional, present in just under half of the cases, to executed in virtually every case, so that the former bypass in which A41 led directly to A26 is now observed only about once in 200 traces. In the earlier window, about 47 of 100 traces contained the block and the remainder went straight from A41 to A26; in the later window, about 995 of 1000 traces contain the block. [REL001, REL003, REL004, REL007, REL008, REL012, DIR002, LAG001]

Three indicator families show this directly and consistently: relational co-occurrence of each block activity with A41 and with A26 rises from about 0.47 to about 0.995 of traces while every ordering relation between these activities is preserved [REL002, REL004, REL007, REL009, REL013]; A41's dominant successor switches from A26 to A19 [DIR002]; and the A41-to-A26 distance shifts from one step to about four steps [LAG001]. Screening qualified 20 control-flow items where about 0.42 would be expected if nothing had changed, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, so the set of differences is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS002, LAG001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show why the block came to be executed everywhere, and it cannot rule out that the later window simply captured a different mix of cases — for example only cases of the variant that includes the block — rather than a modified process; the many relational items also describe overlapping behaviour of the same traces and are not independent confirmations. A rare direct A41-to-A26 step may still exist, as it was observed about once in the later window. [REL001, REL003, DIR002, LAG001]

Other differences: A45 is now always preceded by A19, whereas before it appeared without A19 in about 33 of 100 traces, and A42 interleaves more with the block (about 17 to about 46 of 100 traces); both patterns are most plausibly consequences of A19, A20 and A25 now being present in every trace rather than independent changes. [REL005, REL006, REL010, REL011, REL014, REL015]
