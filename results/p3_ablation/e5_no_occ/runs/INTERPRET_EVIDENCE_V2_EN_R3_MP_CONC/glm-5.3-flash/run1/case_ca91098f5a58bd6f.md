### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained A36 and A21, and A36 was always followed by exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities: A20 in about 35 in 100 traces, A14 in about 35, and A15 in about 30; each of the three was in turn always followed by A21 [DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL003, REL008].

- A26 still appears in every trace in both windows, but whereas it previously always co-occurred with the executed branch activity, it now occurs without any of the three [REL005, REL010, REL014].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the intermediate step between A36 and A21 is no longer executed in the later window: previously one of the three alternatives A14, A15 or A20 always ran between A36 and A21 (taken roughly 35/35/30 in 100 traces), whereas now A36 is immediately followed by A21 in every trace and A14, A15 and A20 are unobserved across all 200 later traces. This rests on direct and mutually consistent relational and positional evidence — A36's successor set collapsed to A21 alone, the A36→A21 distance shortened from two positions to one, and the three activities' participation and co-occurrence fell to zero — while A36, A21 and A26 themselves remain in every trace. The screening context supports this reading: 25 control-flow items qualified where about 0.27 would be expected if nothing had changed, far beyond what random re-splits produce, though these items largely re-describe one underlying difference rather than confirming it independently [DIR004, LAG001, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL003, REL008].

An important limitation: zero participation only shows that A14, A15 and A20 were not observed in the later sample, so the evidence cannot distinguish a permanent removal of these activities from them becoming unobserved for another reason, nor can it show whether the skipped work is performed elsewhere; POS and LAG reflect event positions, not clock time [POS001, POS002, POS003].

Other differences: A19's participation rose modestly, from about 49 in 100 traces to about 56 in 100, and it now always occurs without A14, A15 or A20; given that A19 is present in both windows and the shift is small, this could also be a sampling difference [REL002, REL007, REL012].
