### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the routing choice after A15 between two existing alternative blocks flipped: in the later window, about 79 in 100 cases run A15 followed by the block A16–A18 (whose members still run together in mixed order and relate to A25 as before), whereas in the earlier window about 82 in 100 ran A15 followed by the block A19–A21, which now appears in only about 21 in 100 cases. The swap is shown directly by the successor shares at A15 and by the relation profiles; all 28 items agree on this one underlying difference; and both blocks are well observed in both windows (about 18–82 in 100 cases) with unchanged internal order and an unchanged tie to A25, which fits a shift of shares between existing alternatives rather than activities being added, removed or replaced. [DIR001, REL001, REL004, REL007, REL009, REL012, REL022, REL024]

Limitations: the 27 relation items are pairwise views of the same block-level swap and are not independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot show what determines which block a case takes; in particular, it cannot distinguish a change in the routing itself from a shift in the mix of cases entering the process (for example, more cases of a kind that always took the A16–A18 route). [REL001, REL009, REL012]

Other differences: none — the remaining items (for example REL002, REL017, REL027) repeat the same pairwise pattern of the single swap. [REL002, REL017, REL027]
