### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, nearly every case runs one additional step inside the A27–A28–A29 segment: exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities — A19 in about 29% of cases, A20 in about 41% or A25 in about 30% — executes either between A27 and A28 (about 55% of cases) or between A28 and A29 (about 44%), so the previously near-universal immediate successions A27→A28 and A28→A29 now occur in about 43% and about 56% of the respective transitions. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The placement is shown directly and consistently: successor profiles of A27 and A28 now include the three new activities, which lead back to A28 or A29; the relational items show each new activity always following A27 and preceding A29 while never co-occurring with the other two; the lag items show a one-step shift on all three surrounding pairs; and each new activity is backed by 58–82 traces. In total 25 control-flow items qualified against about 0.34 expected without change, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitation: the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely new step from activities that existed before but were too rare to appear in 200 earlier traces — it only establishes that they were unobserved before and now run in nearly every case; the 29/41/30 split among the three alternatives rests on a single later-window sample. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: A20 occasionally leads to A18 (about 1% of its outgoing transitions) and A25 occasionally to A27, A32 or A44 (about 2% each); these marginal transitions are not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR002, DIR003]
