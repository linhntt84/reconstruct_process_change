### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the branch through A32, A33 and A38 is effectively bypassed and the sequence A26 → A27 → A28 has become the near-universal route, while the earlier detour through A04 between A14 and A15 is largely skipped in favour of a direct A14 → A15 continuation. Earlier, roughly half of the traces routed from A26 through the A32/A33/A38 cluster before reaching A28 and about two-thirds of traces included A04; in the later window A32, A33 and A38 appear in about 1 in 200 traces, A26 leads to A27 in about 98 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, A27 precedes A28 in nearly every trace, and A04 appears in only about 21 in 100 traces. [DIR006, DIR004, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS001, REL017, REL021, REL001]

The change is shown directly by relational and directional evidence rather than resting on a single indicator, the participating activities have substantial bases in the earlier window (roughly 48–201 traces or outgoing transitions each), and the screening produced 55 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected without change, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [DIR006, DIR004, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL028, REL029, REL030]

An important competing explanation remains: the two windows are finite samples, so the evidence cannot distinguish a modification of one process from a shift in the mix of coexisting variants between the windows. Also, A32, A33 and A38 still occur in one later trace, so they may have become optional rather than removed, and the A02/A08 redistributions rest on few observations. [POS004, DIR001, DIR003]

Other differences: A02's and A08's outgoing transitions were redistributed across many successors on small bases; A14's mean position moved slightly later (about 0.135 to 0.148) and A28's slightly earlier (about 0.556 to 0.535), with both present in every trace in both windows; A15→A16 became marginally less exclusive.
