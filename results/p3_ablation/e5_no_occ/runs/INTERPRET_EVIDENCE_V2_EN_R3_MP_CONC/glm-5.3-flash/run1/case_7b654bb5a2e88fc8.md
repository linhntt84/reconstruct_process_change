### Observed differences

- **Routing out of A40.** In the earlier window, every observed outgoing transition of A40 went to one of three activities — A14 (about 38%), A15 (about 31%) or A20 (about 32%) — and A40 never led directly to A21. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One possibility supported by the evidence is that the block of three alternative activities A14, A15 and A20 — exactly one of which executed per case — moved: in the earlier window it ran between A40 and A21, followed by the fixed segment A22→A23→A27; in the later window A40 connects directly to A21, and the block runs after A22 around A23, with A22 handing over to one of the three in about 48 of 100 outgoing transitions, A23 doing so in about 52 of 100, and the block and A23 executing in either order before the segment closes at A27. The change is shown directly and consistently by successor changes at A40, A22, A23 and the three activities, by the order flips with A21 and A22, by the position shift from about 0.31–0.33 to about 0.59–0.60, and by the step-distance changes; 24 items qualified against fewer than 0.3 expected by chance, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of stable behaviour produce. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the block was removed from its old position and separate new occurrences were added after A22 rather than the same steps moved; the data also cannot distinguish a variable-order sequence between the block and A23 from parallel execution, since both orders occur with no recorded interleaving. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

Other differences: the choice among the three alternatives shifted by a few percentage points (A14 down from about 38% to 31% of cases, A20 up from about 32% to 37%), which may be sampling variation or a small separate routing change; A21, A22 and A23 each shifted slightly earlier in the trace, consistent with the block no longer sitting before A21.
