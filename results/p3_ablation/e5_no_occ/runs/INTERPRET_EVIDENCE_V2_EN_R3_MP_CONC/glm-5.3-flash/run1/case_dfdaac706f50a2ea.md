### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural relocation: the group A36/A37/A42, which previously always executed between A25 and A28, now executes after A26 in roughly 47 in 100 traces, where it acts as an alternative to A27 (previously executed after every A26); A25 and A28 have become directly consecutive, A27 and the group have each changed from mandatory to optional, and A31 still executes in every trace, now following either A27 or the group. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL006, REL007, REL008, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG005]

The change is shown directly by relational evidence: the order between A28 and each group member flipped (group member before A28 in about 99 in 100 traces before, A28 before them in about 46–47 in 100 after), the order between A26 and the group flipped the same way, and co-occurrence between A27 and the group collapsed from every trace to about 1 in 100. Successor profiles agree (A25 now exits almost only to A28; A26 splits between A27 and the group; the group exits to A31 instead of A28), participation drops match, and the A25–A28 and A26–A31 spacings shift exactly as this relocation predicts. With 46 qualifying control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected by chance, the pattern as a whole is not explainable by sampling noise, and the old arrangement is essentially absent afterwards (A36 before A28 in about 0.5 in 100 traces; A25 four events before A28 in about 0.5 in 100), so this is a structural change rather than existing paths being taken with different frequencies. [REL014, REL015, REL016, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS003, POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG001, LAG005]

Limitation: the evidence cannot show what determines which of the two later variants (A27 versus the group) a case takes, so a changed mix of case types routed differently cannot be ruled out; several LAG items rest on a single observation in one window and were not relied upon. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011]

Other differences: mean relative positions of A26, A27, A28 and A31 shifted slightly earlier in the later window (for example A28 from about 0.45 to 0.39 of the trace) and A25 slightly later (about 0.34 to 0.36); these are consistent consequences of the relocation rather than separate changes.
