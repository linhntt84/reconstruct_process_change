### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the exclusive choice between two three-activity blocks was removed: in the earlier window each case executed either the block A33/A35/A36 (about 49 in 100 cases) or the block A37/A38/A39 (about 51 in 100), while in the later window every case executes both blocks in sequence — first A33/A35/A36 in no fixed internal order, then A37/A38/A39 — with A30 still preceding the first block and A31 now following only after the second block. [REL039, REL046, DIR006, DIR007, LAG006]

Supporting signs: the relational items show the change directly — cross-block pairs went from 0 co-occurrence to about 98 in 100, always with the first block earlier, and participation of all six activities rose to near-universal (97 → 197–199 of 200 traces, far beyond sampling variation); the successor profiles rewire consistently (A30 now leads into the first block only, and the first block's edges to A31 are replaced by edges into the second block); and the stretched lags to A31 match the second block now sitting between them. Each item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled data, and the screening comparison rules out chance for the ensemble: 80 control-flow items qualified where about 0.81 would be expected without a change. [REL039, POS006, POS011, DIR006, DIR009, LAG006, LAG008]

Limitations: most items describe one underlying restructuring rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot fully distinguish "the exclusive branch was removed and the blocks were chained" from "each block independently became mandatory", although the new first-block-to-second-block edges and the consistent first-before-second order favour the chained reading. [DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, REL039]
