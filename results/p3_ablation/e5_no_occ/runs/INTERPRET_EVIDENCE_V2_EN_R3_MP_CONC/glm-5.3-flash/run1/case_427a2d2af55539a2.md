### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured: in the earlier window exactly one of the alternatives A14, A15 or A16 executed between A39 and A24, whereas in the later window that one-of-three step is no longer observed and instead A21, A22 and A23 all execute in every case between A39 and A24, in no fixed order among themselves, leaving about two extra steps between A39 and A24; A14, A15 and A16 are no longer observed at all. [DIR007, REL019, REL021, REL024, REL026, LAG001, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

This rests on direct relational and directional evidence, not on participation counts alone: A39's successor set changed as a whole, each new activity is bounded by A39 before it and A24 after it in 200 of 200 traces, and the A39–A24 distance shifted consistently from 2 to 4 steps. Screening also qualified 42 items where only about 0.34 would be expected if nothing had changed, far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. [DIR007, REL021, REL022, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027, LAG001, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether A21, A22 and A23 run concurrently or merely in an arbitrary sequence, and it cannot rule out that the two windows sample different case populations or variants rather than a modified single process. Many of the qualified items describe the same underlying difference and are not independent confirmations. [REL019, REL023, POS007]

Other differences: none — all qualified items concern the restructuring around A39 and A24 described above, including A24's later position relative to A39. [DIR007, LAG001, POS007]
