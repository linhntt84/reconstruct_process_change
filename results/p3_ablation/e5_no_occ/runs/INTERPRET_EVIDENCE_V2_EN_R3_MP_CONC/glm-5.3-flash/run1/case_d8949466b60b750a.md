### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch formed by A32, A33 and A38 — earlier executed together in roughly half of the cases between A26 and A28 — is now skipped in almost all cases, so that virtually every case runs A26 → A27 → A28. The participation of all three activities fell from about 49 in 100 traces to about 1 in 200, A26's routing shifted from an even split between A27 and the branch to about 97 in 100 through A27, and the pairwise relations flip consistently: A26–A27 and A27–A28 co-occurrence rose from about 52 in 100 to essentially all traces with the fixed order A26 before A27 before A28, while co-occurrence of A32, A33 or A38 with the rest fell to near zero. With 31 qualifying items against roughly 0.4 expected without change, a chance origin of the overall pattern is implausible, and the change is shown directly by participation, routing and relational evidence rather than by a single indicator. Limitations: the branch still occurs in one later trace, so it became rare rather than strictly removed; the evidence cannot rule out that the later window is a restricted sample of cases (for example only one variant being sampled) rather than a change in the process; and it does not show whether the skipped branch's work was moved elsewhere, since A28's position shifted only slightly earlier. Other differences: A26 and A27 each show a few new, rare successors in the later window (A08, A13, A17, A24, A25, A28 after A26; A09, A14, A15 after A27), each well below 1 in 100 of outgoing transitions; these are minor and not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR001, DIR002]
