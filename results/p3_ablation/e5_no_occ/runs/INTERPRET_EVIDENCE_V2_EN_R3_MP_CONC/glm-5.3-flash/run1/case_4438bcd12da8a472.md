### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the activity block A14 → A20 → A15, sitting between A36 and A21, changed from being executed in about 47 in 100 cases to being executed in nearly every case. In the earlier window the block was skipped in about 53 in 100 traces, with A36 passing directly to A21; in the later window it runs in about 995 in 1000 traces, A36 is followed by A14 in essentially every trace, and A21 follows four positions after A36. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL007, REL012, DIR001, LAG001]

The volume of qualifying evidence far exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 19 items qualified against roughly 0.27 expected by chance among 54 tested units, and the relational, directional and lag evidence all show the same participation change directly, each backed by about 200 traces per window. [DIR001, LAG001, REL001, REL007]

The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different mixes of case variants (for example a shift in which case types enter the process) rather than a change in routing within one process, and it cannot show why the block now runs almost always. About 5 in 1000 later traces still run A21 without the block, so the old direct path is nearly, but not completely, gone. [REL003, LAG001]

Other differences: A37's order relative to A14 became more mixed (interleaved or repeated in about 46 in 100 later traces vs about 17 in 100 earlier), and A40's participation is stable at about 60 in 100 traces but it now always co-occurs with A14, which follows from the block running in every case; the small position shifts of A36 and A21 are positional only.
