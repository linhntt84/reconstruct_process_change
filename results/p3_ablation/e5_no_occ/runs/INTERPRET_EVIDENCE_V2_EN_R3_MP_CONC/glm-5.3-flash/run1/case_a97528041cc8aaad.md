### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two stages exchanged their position between A40 and A30: the stage formed by A19, A21 and A24, which earlier ran after A25/A29 and immediately before A30, now runs directly after A40, while the stage formed by A20, A22 and A23, which earlier ran directly after A40, now runs after A25/A29 and immediately before A30 — the sequence A40 → A20/A22/A23 → A25/A29 → A19/A21/A24 → A30 became A40 → A19/A21/A24 → A25/A29 → A20/A22/A23 → A30. This rests on direct relational evidence (about twenty activity pairs reverse their dominant order), consistent successor changes at the boundary activities, opposite position shifts of the two groups, and matching distance changes; the screening produced 84 qualifying items against roughly 1 expected without change, so chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole, though the items describe one underlying reversal rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: positions and lags are event positions, not clock times, and because each window is dominated by a single ordering, the evidence cannot distinguish a re-sequencing within traces from a complete replacement of one dominant variant by another with the reversed order. [DIR005, DIR011, DIR012, REL015, REL029, REL032, POS003, POS002, LAG019, LAG025]

Other differences: A02 became far more frequent (about 15 to 65 in 100 traces) and much earlier in the case (mean position about 0.53 to 0.20), now routing about 87 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A15, while A14 routes about 60 in 100 of its outgoing transitions through A02 (about 1 in 100 earlier) — consistent with A02 now typically sitting between A14 and A15 early in the case; the earlier-window A02 profile rests on only about 30 traces, and the evidence cannot rule out that this small base distorts the comparison of its routing.
