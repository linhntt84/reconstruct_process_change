- In the earlier window every trace contained all eight activities (A20, A21, A22, A23, A26, A31, A32, A37). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the mandatory block of A31, A32 and A37, which previously always ran between A20 and A23, was bypassed and reopened as an optional branch after A21: in the later window A20 leads almost always directly to A23, and the branch A21 → A31/A32/A37 (the three always executed together) → A26 runs in about 47 in 100 traces, mutually exclusive with A22, which runs in the remaining about 53 in 100 traces; in the earlier window the block ran in every trace before A23 and A22 always followed A21. [DIR001, LAG001, DIR002, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL020, REL021, REL022, POS003, POS006, POS007, POS008]

The hypothesis rests on direct relational and positional evidence, not on frequency shifts alone: the successor profile of A20 collapsed onto A23, the A20→A23 gap fell from four positions to one, the group's order relative to A23, A21 and A26 flipped, its exits moved from A23 to A26, and the two- versus four-position gap from A21 to A26 matches the two alternative branches. Screening qualified 43 control-flow items where fewer than one (about 0.36) would be expected by chance, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces; note, however, that many items describe the same underlying traces and are not independent confirmations. [DIR001, LAG001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022]

An important competing explanation cannot be ruled out: the skipping behaviour could have existed before at a frequency too low to appear in the 200 earlier traces (it was observed in 0 of 200), in which case the change would be a rise of a pre-existing rare path rather than newly added routing. In addition, several gap items about the group rest on a single after-window observation and were not used as support. [POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]
