- In the earlier window, none of the 200 traces contains A19, A20 or A25; in the later window A19 appears in about 30 in 100 traces (59 of 200), A20 in about 41 in 100 (82 of 200) and A25 in about 30 in 100 (60 of 200), each around the middle of the trace (mean relative position ≈ 0.51). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, one of three previously unobserved activities — A19, A20 or A25 — executes in nearly every trace as a mutually exclusive single choice, placed between A27 and A29: it always runs after A27 and before A29, with no fixed order relative to A28 (it precedes A28 in roughly half of its occurrences and follows it in the other half). Earlier, the flow ran from A27 directly to A28 and on to A29; later, A27 and A28 each route to one of the new activities in about 42–56 in 100 of their outgoing transitions instead of only to their former successor, and the new activities in turn lead on to A28 or A29. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

The hypothesis is direct and internally consistent: screening flagged 25 items where about 0.33 would qualify by chance (all in the control-flow family), every item exceeds roughly 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, the relational items show the placement of the new activities directly, and the routing and step-distance items agree with it; the underlying observations are substantial (59–82 traces per new activity, 200 traces behind the A27/A28/A29 relations). [REL001, REL002, REL006, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitation: zero participation in the earlier window means only that A19, A20 and A25 were not observed there, so a variant that existed at very low frequency before and became dominant afterwards cannot be fully excluded — although near-universal presence later, with both A27 and A28 rerouting about half their transitions, makes a pure sampling explanation implausible. Positions are event positions, so the placement claim concerns order, not clock time. [POS001, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: only rare edges (about 1 in 200 transitions) shifted — A27 earlier also led to A06, A24 and A41 and A28 to A11 and A33, shares that vanish later, while the new activities show occasional edges to A07, A14, A26 and A31; these are too infrequent to interpret separately.
