### Observed differences

- A20 runs in essentially every case in both windows, but the group executed after it flipped: in the earlier window A24, A25 and A26 ran together in about 82 in 100 cases, while in the later window they do so in only about 21 in 100; A21, A22 and A23 moved the opposite way, from about 19 to about 79 in 100 cases running together. [REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL025, POS002]
- The transition shares out of A20 mirror this: before, about 82% of A20's outgoing moves went to A24 (29%), A26 (29%) and A25 (24%); in the later window about 78% go to A21 (28%), A23 (29%) and A22 (21%), with the previous targets down to a combined 21%. [DIR005]
- The two groups were and remain near-exclusive alternatives — they share a case in only about 1 in 100 cases in either window — but the preferred one flipped: before, A24 ran without A21 in about 80 in 100 cases; after, A21 runs without A24 in about 79 in 100. [REL019, REL020, REL021, REL026, REL027, REL028]
- The new branch keeps the old branch's place in the flow: A21–A23 now co-occur with A20 in about 79 in 100 cases (about 19 before), usually after A20, and with A34/A35 in about 79 in 100 cases (about 19 before), usually before them; among themselves they appear in both orders in roughly equal shares in the later window. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL017, REL023, REL030, REL036]
- A06 appeared in about 46 in 100 cases before but only about 12 in 100 after, and it now sits earlier in the case (mean event position about 0.91 before vs about 0.52 after); its co-occurrence with A20 or A34 fell from about 46 to about 12 in 100 cases. [POS001, DIR001, REL001, REL009]
- From A34, which still runs in nearly every case in both windows, moves to A06 fell from about 44% to about 2%, while moves to A35 rose from about 56% to about 90%; A35 now follows A34 immediately in about 92 in 100 cases (about 56 before). [DIR006, LAG006, POS003]
- Before, A06 led to A35 in about 92% of its outgoing moves; in the later window such successions are observed in only about 7 of 200 cases, with more varied spacing. [DIR001, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that routing after A20 changed: the branch in which A24, A25 and A26 executed together (about 82 in 100 cases) was largely displaced by the branch in which A21, A22 and A23 execute together (up from about 19 to about 79 in 100 cases), with A20 still preceding whichever branch runs and the branch still running before A34/A35. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL019, REL023, DIR005]

The change is shown directly and consistently by the relational co-occurrence flips and by the outgoing transition shares at A20, which agree; both branches are well observed in both windows (the rarer one at roughly 19–21 in 100 cases), so this is a swap between existing alternatives rather than an activity appearing or vanishing; and 64 items qualified against fewer than one expected by chance (0.74 over 148 units), more than an unchanged process should produce as a whole. [REL014, REL015, REL016, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL019, DIR005]

What the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows simply contain different case mixes — for example, case types that favour different branches — rather than a modified routing rule; it also leaves the internal order of A21–A23 unfixed, since they appear in both orders in similar shares in the later window. [REL017, REL018, REL025]

Other differences: A06 was largely cut out of the later flow — from about 46 to about 12 in 100 cases, now earlier in the case — and A34 now moves directly to A35 (about 90% of its outgoing moves, immediate in about 92 in 100 cases) where it previously reached A06 first (about 44%, with A06 then leading to A35 in about 92% of its moves); this is consistent with the former A34→A06→A35 segment being skipped in most later cases, though A06's later profile rests on only about 26 traces. A08's outgoing mix also differs, but with 9 versus 23 supporting traces it is weakly grounded. [DIR001, DIR006, POS001, REL001, REL005, REL009, LAG001, LAG006, DIR002]
