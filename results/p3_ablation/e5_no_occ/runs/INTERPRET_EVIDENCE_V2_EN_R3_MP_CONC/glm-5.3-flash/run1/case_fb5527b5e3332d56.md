### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, cases split between two routes from A21 to A23: about 52 in 100 traces ran A21 → A22 → A23, while about 48 in 100 (97 of 200) inserted a block of A27, A28 and A33 between A21 and A23, with the three block activities in a mixed order (for example, A27 came before A28 in about 24 in 100 of the traces and after it in about 25 in 100). - In the later window, A21 → A22 → A23 appears in about 99.5 in 100 traces (199 of 200), while A27, A28 and A33 each appear in only 1 of 200 traces and essentially never co-occur with A21, A22 or A23 anymore. [REL001, REL005, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL002, REL006, REL009]
- Routing out of A21 shifted accordingly: before, about half of A21's outgoing transitions led to A22 and about half to A27, A28 or A33; after, essentially all (about 99.5 in 100) led to A22. [DIR001]
- The distance from A21 to A23 narrowed from two event positions in about half of the earlier traces and four positions in the other half to two positions in about 99.5 in 100 of the later traces, and A23's relative position moved slightly earlier (mean about 0.55 to about 0.53). [LAG001, POS001]
- The single later trace that still contains the block executed it as A28 → A27 → A33 before A23, but this rests on one trace only. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG003, LAG008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the alternative branch formed by A27, A28 and A33 between A21 and A23 is skipped in almost all executions: each of the three activities fell from about 97 in 200 traces to 1 in 200, while the route through A22 rose from about 52 in 100 cases to about 99.5 in 100, leaving A21 → A22 → A23 as the observed behaviour in nearly every case. [POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL005, REL002, REL009, DIR001, LAG001]

The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation shares drop from about 48.5 in 100 traces to about 0.5 in 100, co-occurrence of the block activities with A21, A22 and A23 collapses in the same proportion, A21's routing concentrates on A22, and the A21–A23 distance contracts to two positions; every item shows empirical strength of about 0.995, and 30 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.29 would be expected for an unchanged process, so sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [POS002, REL002, DIR001, LAG001]

Limitations: the later-window behaviour of A27, A28 and A33 rests on a single trace, so the evidence shows the branch was skipped in this window but cannot distinguish a branch that is still possible yet almost never taken from one that was removed entirely; and since many items describe the same underlying traces, their agreement is not independent confirmation.
