### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the mandatory block A36/A37/A42 was relocated and made optional: in the earlier window every case ran A25 into the block (unfixed internal order), the block exited to A28, and A26 — which followed the block — led through A27 to A31; in the later window A25 passes directly to A28, and after A26 about half of the cases run A27 while about half run the block, which now exits to A31. A27 and the block, previously both executed in every case, became mutually exclusive alternatives (about 53 versus 47 in 100 cases). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL006, REL011, REL014, LAG001, LAG005, LAG008]

The hypothesis is shown directly and consistently: successor changes at A25, A26 and the block, order reversals between the block and A26/A28, the new close succession A37→A31, and the disappearance of the old adjacencies all describe the same restructuring. Its volume also far exceeds chance: 44 control-flow items qualified where about 0.5 would be expected without change for 100 tested units, and each item's difference exceeds those of about 995 in 1000 random re-splits. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]

Limitations: the flagged direction profiles cover only A25, A26, A36, A37 and A42, so the later hand-over from A25/A28 onward to A26 is inferred from positions and co-occurrence rather than an observed edge, and the evidence cannot show whether the block is simply skipped in A27-cases or replaced by activities outside the tested units.
