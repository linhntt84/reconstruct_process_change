### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, none of the 200 traces contains A19, A20 or A25; in the later window, A19 appears in about 30 in 100 cases, A20 in about 41 in 100 and A25 in about 30 in 100, each around the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.51). - The three are mutually exclusive alternatives: any pair of them co-occurs in at most about 1 in 200 later cases, and for each pair the share of cases containing neither matches the third activity's share, so together they cover essentially every later case. [REL001, REL002, REL006]
- Each of the three almost always follows A27 (A27 comes first in about 97–100 in 100 of their joint appearances) and almost always precedes A29; against A28 the order is mixed (for example, A19 comes before A28 in about 16 in 100 cases and after it in about 13 in 100). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window every case executes one additional step — exactly one of the mutually exclusive activities A19, A20 or A25 — placed within the former A27 → A28 → A29 sequence. In the earlier window the flow ran from A27 almost exclusively through A28 to A29 (about 99 in 100 outgoing transitions at each hop) with none of the three observed; in the later window each case contains one of them after A27 and before A29, sometimes between A27 and A28 and sometimes between A28 and A29, which also accounts for the one-step stretching of the A27→A28, A28→A29 and A27→A29 gaps and the largely unchanged positions of A27 and A29. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

This is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces: 25 qualifying items against roughly 0.33 expected by chance, and appearance rates of 30–41 in 100 cases rule out a rare variant surfacing in one window. Note that the many relation items largely restate this single underlying difference rather than confirming it independently. The evidence cannot show what selects among A19, A20 and A25 in a given case, nor whether they are variants of one new step rather than three distinct behaviours; that competing reading remains open. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: a few outgoing transitions with very small shares (about 1–2 in 100 transitions, e.g. from A27 to A02/A10/A13, from A28 to A32/A42, and from the new activities to A07/A14/A26/A31) appear only in the later window; at this size they are indistinguishable from sampling noise.
