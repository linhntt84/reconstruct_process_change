### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that an exclusive choice step was inserted between A36 and A21. In the earlier window, control flowed from A36 directly to A21; in the later window, in about 99 in 100 traces, exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities — A14 (about 37 in 100 traces), A20 (about 34) or A15 (about 29) — executes between them, and each of the three is always followed by A21. [DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, REL001, REL002, REL005, REL003, REL006, REL008]

The hypothesis rests on direct relational evidence: the successor profile of A36 changed from a single successor to a three-way split, the A36-to-A21 distance grew from one to two events, and the ordering relations place A36 before each new activity and each new activity before A21. The screening comparison also supports this as a whole: 18 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.22 would be expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeded about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled data.
