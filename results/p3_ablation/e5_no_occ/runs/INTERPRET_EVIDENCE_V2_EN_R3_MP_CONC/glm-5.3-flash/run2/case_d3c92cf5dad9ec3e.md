### Observed differences

- The relative order of two groups of activities reversed completely. In the earlier window, whenever one of the alternative activities A19, A21 or A24 (each in about 30–37 of 100 cases, in effect one per case) occurred together with A20, A22, A23, A25 or A29, it came first — e.g., A19 preceded A20 in all of the roughly 30 in 100 cases containing both, and A25 preceded A20 in about 995 of 1000 cases. In the later window every one of these 21 pairings shows the reverse order: A20 preceded A19 in all of the roughly 33 in 100 cases containing both, and A20 preceded A25 in about 995 of 1000 cases. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021]

- Event positions moved accordingly: in the later window A20, A22 and A23 sit at about 0.38 of the case (mean relative position), down from about 0.63–0.64, while A19, A21 and A24 sit at about 0.66–0.68, up from about 0.34–0.36; A25 (about 0.38 to 0.45) and A29 (about 0.57 to 0.64) shifted only slightly later and kept their mutual order. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

- The hand-offs at the segment boundaries changed with them. In the later window A40 leads almost always to one of A20/A22/A23 (about 94 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) where it previously led to one of A19/A21/A24 (about 95 in 100); A29 leads to one of A19/A21/A24 where it previously led into the trio A20/A22/A23; A19, A21 and A24 now lead to A30 (about 95–98 in 100) where they previously led to A25 (about 97–98 in 100); and A20, A22 and A23 now lead to A25 (about 27–37 in 100) or to one another where they previously led to A30 (about 29–37 in 100) or to one another. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008]

- Step distances match a three-activity segment and a single-alternative step exchanging places: A29 reached A30 four steps later in about 93 of 100 cases earlier but two steps later in about 98 of 100 cases in the later window; A40 reached A25 two steps later before (about 95 in 100) and four steps later after (about 92 in 100); A40 to A20/A22/A23, previously almost never within five steps, now sit one to three steps apart in nearly every case; and A21 now immediately follows A29 (all 58 observed pair occurrences) after being practically unobserved in that order before. [LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]

- Participation did not change: A20, A22, A23, A25 and A29 appear in every case in both windows, and A19, A21 and A24 each appear in about 30–40 of 100 cases in both windows, so the difference lies in ordering, not in which activities run. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two segments of the process exchanged their order. In the earlier window the sequence ran from A40 to exactly one of the alternatives A19/A21/A24, then A25, then A29, then the trio A20/A22/A23 in varying order, then A30; in the later window the trio A20/A22/A23 runs in the early slot (after A40, before A25) while the alternative step runs in the late slot (after A29, directly before A30), with A25 and A29 keeping their relative order and shifting only slightly later. The pattern is shown directly and consistently: all 21 activity-pair relations flip in the same direction, the successor changes at A40, A29, the alternatives and the trio describe the same rearrangement, and the distance changes (A29→A30 from four to two steps; A40→A25 from two to four) fit a three-activity segment trading places with a single-alternative step. Unchanged participation shares rule out the competing reading that activities were added, removed or became optional, and the screening yield (46 qualifying items against roughly 0.5 expected for an unchanged process) is far beyond what chance would produce. [REL001, REL008, REL018, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG009, POS001, POS002, POS007, POS008]

Limitations: the qualified items cover pairwise orders and selected adjacencies, so the stretch between A25 and A29 is not observed and accompanying rearrangements among activities there cannot be excluded; a few distance items rest on only one or two observations (e.g., A21–A25 and A24–A25 in the later window), though the conclusion does not rest on them. [LAG001, LAG003]

Other differences: none of substance beyond the reversal; the choice among the three alternatives shifted only modestly (A19 about 30 to 33, A21 about 35 to 30, A24 about 36 to 40 in 100 cases), which the evidence does not show to be a clear change, and a few rare transitions with 1–2 in 100 shares appeared or disappeared, consistent with sampling variation. [POS001, POS003, POS006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
