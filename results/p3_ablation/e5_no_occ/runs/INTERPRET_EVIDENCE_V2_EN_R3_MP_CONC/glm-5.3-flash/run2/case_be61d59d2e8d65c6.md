### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of activities A19, A20 and A25, which in the earlier window executed in roughly 30–40 of 100 cases between A27/A28 and A29, is now skipped or bypassed: in the later window A27 leads almost directly to A28 and A28 to A29, the three activities appear in only about 1 in 200 traces, and the A27→A29 distance shortened by about one step. The participation, routing, co-occurrence and lag items all describe this same difference consistently, and with 27 qualifying items against roughly 0.35 expected by chance for the 70 tested units, the ensemble cannot be explained as sampling variation in a stable process; each item's difference also exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits. Note that the many relation items describe one underlying trace difference rather than independent confirmations. [POS001, POS002, POS003, DIR005, DIR006, REL005, REL009, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

The evidence cannot rule out that A19, A20 and A25 were replaced by other or renamed activities rather than removed, and the single later trace containing each of them supports no conclusion about their residual behaviour — the outgoing edges observed for A19, A20 and A25 in the later window (towards A38, A37 and A31) each rest on one observation. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

Other differences: A10's outgoing flow redistributed and A10 itself appears in more traces (about 35 versus 53 in 200); the evidence does not connect this to the disappearance of A19, A20 and A25, so it stands as a separate control-flow difference in another part of the process. [DIR001]
