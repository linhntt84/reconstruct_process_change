### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the two alternative paths following A20 exchanged how often they are taken: the path executing A21, A22 and A23 together (with A30) rose from about 19 to about 79 in 100 traces, while the path executing A24, A25 and A26 together fell from about 82 to about 21. This is a shift in the frequencies of existing paths rather than a replacement: all six activities were observed in substantial shares in both windows (the rarer trio at about 19–21 in 100), the internal ordering of each trio is unchanged, and A20 still precedes whichever trio runs. The difference is shown directly by relational evidence resting on all 200 traces per window and by the matching successor redistribution at A20, and the screening expected fewer than one qualifying item without a change while 53 control-flow items qualified, so the ensemble is unlikely for an unchanged process. Limitations: the many items describe overlapping trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply draw different case mixes of an unchanged process rather than a change at this point in time. [REL009, REL012, REL015, REL036, DIR003]

Other differences: A10's participation fell from about 17 to about 7 in 100 traces and its embedding changed — its typical event position moved earlier with more spread, its outgoing transitions diversified (previously about 97% to A33, now spread over about eight targets), and A32's transitions into A10 nearly disappeared; these rest on fewer later-window observations (14 traces containing A10) and are not implied by the path-frequency shift alone. A02 shows a successor-profile change with very low support (4 traces before versus 18 after). [POS001, DIR002, DIR004, LAG001, DIR001]
