### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the following possibility: in the later window, the block of activities A19, A20 and A25 executes before A26 rather than after it. It is entered from A45 — with A08 as a second entry point that is now far more frequent (about 30 vs 13 in 100 traces) — and flows directly into A26, whereas earlier these activities followed A26 and alternated with A28 before the flow exited towards A32; at the same time, A27 now passes through A28 straight on to A32. [REL005, REL007, REL009, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR007, LAG007, LAG008, LAG012, POS002, POS003, POS004]

Supporting signs: the order reversal is observed directly on six activity pairs, not inferred from frequencies alone; the new successions (A19/A20/A25 into A26, A45 and A08 into the block) and the vanished ones (towards A32, back into A25) are visible directly in the direction and lag data; the positional shifts match; and 39 qualifying items against roughly 0.49 expected by chance in the control-flow family makes sampling variation implausible as an overall explanation. [REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG007, LAG002, LAG008, LAG009, LAG012, POS002, POS003, POS004]

Limitations: the indicators use event positions, not clock time, and the relational data shows relative order only. The evidence cannot fully rule out that the two windows sample different mixes of pre-existing execution variants, nor determine whether the earlier alternation with A28 was repeated execution or execution without a fixed order. The positional data favours the block itself moving earlier, since A26 and A27 shifted only marginally (about 0.03) while A19, A20 and A25 shifted by about 0.27. [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

Other differences: the greater distances from A16 and A18 to A26 are consistent with the relocated block but were not included in the main hypothesis; A48 and A50 became marginally more concentrated on A49 and A51, differences too small in size to interpret. [LAG004, LAG005, DIR008, DIR009]
