### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, an alternative route is executed between A26 and A28: in about half of the cases A26 is followed not by A27 but by a block of three activities that were not observed before, A32, A33 and A38, which run together in varying order and then lead into A28, while A27 — previously executed in every trace between A26 and A28 — is skipped in about 49 in 100 traces and almost never coincides with the new block. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: A26's outgoing share to A27 falls from about 95 to about 51 in 100; the three new activities appear with substantial support only in the later window (about 100 of 200 traces each, 0 before) and transition into A28 and among themselves; A27's co-occurrence with A14, A15, A26 and A28 drops from 100 to about 52 in 100; and the A26→A28 distance splits between two and four steps. The 49 qualifying control-flow items far exceed the roughly 0.5 expected by chance for the 101 tested units, so this collection of differences is not what random re-splits of an unchanged process typically produce. Limitations: 0 observations in 200 earlier traces cannot prove the block was entirely absent rather than very rare, the evidence cannot distinguish a routing modification from a shift in the mix of cases reaching this point, and the internal order of A32, A33 and A38 shows no fixed sequence [DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL009, REL017, REL021, REL022, REL025, LAG003].
