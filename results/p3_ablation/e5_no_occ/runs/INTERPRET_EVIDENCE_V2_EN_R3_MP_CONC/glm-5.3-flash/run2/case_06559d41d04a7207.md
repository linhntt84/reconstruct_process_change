### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the earlier optional block of alternatives A19, A20 and A21 — each executed in roughly a third of cases, never together, and each leading straight into A29 — was replaced by a new, always-executed cluster A26, A27 and A28 that runs in every case before A29, with A44 now leading into the new cluster instead of the old one. The screening context supports treating this as a real difference rather than sampling noise: 67 items qualified where fewer than one (0.59) would qualify by chance in an unchanged process. The change is structural — formerly optional activities disappeared and new mandatory ones appeared — not a re-weighting of existing paths, since the new activities were entirely unobserved earlier and the old ones are virtually unobserved later. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR009, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, REL007, REL016, REL031, REL033, REL038, REL042, LAG005]

The hypothesis rests on direct relational and directional evidence, not on frequencies alone: co-occurrence and order with A29 flipped from never-together to always-together in a fixed order, A44's successor profile switched wholesale, the A44→A29 distance lengthened consistently, and participation moved from 0 to 200 of 200 and from about 70 to 1 of 200 traces. [REL033, REL042, DIR009, LAG005, POS005, POS002]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that A26, A27 and A28 are re-labelled versions of the earlier steps rather than genuinely new ones; the all-or-nothing participation pattern (0 vs 200 of 200 traces) makes pure sampling variation implausible, but the indicators cannot distinguish addition from renaming. [POS005, POS002]

Other differences: A10's reduced participation and rewired successors, and the changed relations of A45 and A46 with the new cluster, are not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR001, POS001, REL035, REL036]
