### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A45 passed flow on almost directly to A26: about 85 in 100 of A45's observed outgoing transitions went to A26. In the later window, A45 leads instead to A19, A20 and A25, which together receive about 85 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (roughly 27–29 each), and A26 is no longer among its observed successors. [DIR006, LAG007]
- The three activities A19, A20 and A25 changed their successors: in the earlier window each split its outgoing transitions roughly evenly between A28 and A32 (about 43–56% each); in the later window each leads almost exclusively to A26 (about 88–94 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The late part of the flow simplified: earlier, A27's outgoing transitions split between A28 (about 47 in 100) and A19/A20/A25 (about 16–19 each), and A28's split between A32 (about 50 in 100) and the same three activities (about 14–19 each); later, A27 leads to A28 in about 98 in 100 cases and A28 leads to A32 in about 96 in 100. [DIR004, DIR005]
- A19, A20 and A25 occur much earlier within traces in the later window: their mean position fell from about 0.60–0.61 of the trace to about 0.32–0.34, while A26, A27 and A28 stayed at nearly the same positions (about 0.33→0.36, 0.56→0.59, 0.61→0.62). The share of traces containing the trio barely changed (for example A19: 39% before vs 36% after), so they run just as often, only earlier. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- The order between the trio and A26/A27 flipped: earlier, whenever A19, A20 or A25 co-occurred with A26 or A27 (in about 31–39 of 100 traces), A26/A27 always came first and the reverse never occurred; in the later window the trio always comes first (about 30–36 of 100 traces) and the old order is essentially gone. Co-occurrence rates stayed similar, so these activities still run in the same traces. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Step distances changed accordingly: A27→A28 and A28→A32 became immediately consecutive (immediately-following share rose from about half of cases to about 96–98 in 100), A27→A32 shortened from typically 3 steps to 2, and A45→A26, previously immediate in about 86 of 100 cases, is now typically 2–3 steps apart. The lag profiles for A19/A25 with A32 and A25 with A28 point the same way but rest on only 1–3 later-window observations. [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of activities A19, A20 and A25 was moved to an earlier point in the flow. In the earlier window they ran late in the trace (mean position about 0.60), after A26 and A27, and passed flow on to A28 and A32; in the later window they run early (mean position about 0.32–0.34), directly after A45 and before A26, to which each now leads almost always. A side effect is that A27→A28→A32 became a direct consecutive chain. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

This is unlikely to be a sampling artefact: 25 evidence items qualified against roughly 0.38 expected by chance in an unchanged process (all control-flow), and the same rewiring appears consistently across successor, position, order and distance indicators rather than as isolated signals. The relational evidence is direct — the order between the trio and A26/A27 flipped completely while their co-occurrence stayed stable, which is the signature of a relocation rather than one path replacing another; the trio's routing profiles also rest on a substantial base (roughly 60–80 traces per window, about 200 for A27, A28 and A45). [DIR001, DIR004, POS001, REL001, LAG004]

Limitations: positions are event positions, so "earlier" means earlier in the event sequence, not necessarily earlier in clock time. The evidence also cannot fully distinguish a clean relocation of the trio from a rewiring in which A45's branch was rerouted through the trio while its late occurrences were removed; the stable co-occurrence and complete order flip favour the relocation reading, but the exact earlier topology around A27 and A28, where the trio also exchanged flow with A28, is only partially resolved. [POS001, DIR004, DIR005, REL001]
