- In the earlier window, A20's outgoing transitions split across A30 (about 40 in 100), A31 (about 33) and A36 (about 28); in the later window, A20 leads to A22 in about 99 in 100 traces, with the three previous successors almost unobserved (about 1 in 200 each). [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-activity block A30, A31 and A36, which in the earlier window executed between A20 and A22, is executed in the later window between A21 and A25: the sequence reads A20 → {A30/A31/A36} → A22 → A21 → A25 before, and A20 → A22 → A21 → {A30/A31/A36} → A25 after, with A22 continuing to precede A21. [DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL004, LAG001, LAG004, POS001, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Supporting signs: 22 items qualified against roughly 0.28 expected for an unchanged process (about 80 times the chance expectation), and each item exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled traces. The order reversal is shown directly by the relational items, and the distance changes (A20→A22 from 4 steps to 1; A21→A25 from 1 step to 4) are exactly what relocating the three-activity block past the A22/A21 segment predicts; the successor changes at A20, A21 and the group members and the position shifts (group later by about 0.17–0.18, A22/A21 earlier by about 0.09) agree in direction and magnitude. Because co-occurrence remains at 100% and the block's internal routing is similar, this reads as a reordering of existing activities rather than activities being added, removed or repeated. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Limitations: POS and LAG use event positions, so the hypothesis concerns execution order, not clock time; the evidence cannot distinguish whether the group block moved later or the A22/A21 segment was pulled ahead of an unchanged group, since both readings fit the same data; no directional item covers the A22–A21 connection, so that link is inferred from positions only; the items largely describe one underlying reordering rather than independent confirmations; and about 2 in 200 later traces still show the old distances and old order, so a small share of cases may still follow the earlier sequence.
