### Observed differences

- Flow out of A31 was redirected: in the earlier window, about 71 in 100 of A31's observed outgoing transitions went to A25 and about 29 in 100 to A20; in the later window, about 96 in 100 go to A20 and only about 4 in 100 to A25 [DIR001].
- A31 also occurs far less often per trace: about three to four times per trace earlier (689 outgoing transitions over 200 traces) versus about once per trace in the later window (208 over 200) [DIR001].
- The order between A25 and A31 changed from mixed to fixed: the two activities occur together in every trace in both windows, but earlier about 75 in 100 traces showed them interleaved or repeated (A25 strictly first in about 25 in 100, A31 strictly first never), while in the later window A25 precedes A31 in about 995 of 1000 traces [REL001].
- The A25→A31 adjacency tightened: earlier, A31 followed A25 within two steps in about 12 in 100 observed pair occurrences (five-step gap: about 5 in 100); in the later window the two-step gap covers about 92 in 100 [LAG001].
- The reverse adjacency A31→A25 within five steps nearly vanished: observed in 150 of 200 traces earlier, in only 1 of 200 traces in the later window [LAG002].
- Both activities now sit in a much tighter position band: A25's spread halved (about 0.12 to about 0.06) with its median moving slightly earlier (0.43 to 0.40); A31's spread also halved (about 0.12 to about 0.06) with its median unchanged near 0.48 [POS001, POS002].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering constraint between A25 and A31 was tightened and the repetition around them stopped: in the earlier window the two activities were executed in a mixed, mostly interleaved or repeated order, with A31 often followed by A25 again (150 of 200 traces) and A31 firing about three to four times per trace; in the later window A25 consistently precedes A31 (about 995 of 1000 traces, typically within two steps), A31 occurs about once per trace, and its continuation goes to A20 in about 96 in 100 cases instead of back to A25 [REL001, LAG001, LAG002, DIR001]. The halved position spread of both activities fits the disappearance of the interleaving [POS001, POS002]. Six control-flow items qualified against roughly 0.15 expected by chance for the 30 tested units, and each difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled traces, so chance sampling alone cannot account for the pattern [DIR001, POS001, POS002, REL001, LAG001, LAG002].

Limitations: the evidence contains no relational or positional items for A20, so behaviour after the A31→A20 hand-off is unobserved; the after-profile of the A31→A25 gap rests on a single observation [LAG002]. An important competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is an extreme shift in branch frequencies rather than a rewiring, since about 4 in 100 of A31's outgoing transitions still go to A25 in the later window — though the near-disappearance of that adjacency across 200 traces makes a pure sampling artefact unlikely [DIR001, LAG002].
