### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two segments exchanged their places around the unchanged A20 → A24 spine: the group A15/A17/A18, previously executed between A35 and A20, now runs after A24 and exits to A25, while the one-of-three step (A14, A16 or A19), previously running between A24 and A25, now runs between A35 and A20 and exits to A20. The screening produced 39 qualifying control-flow items where fewer than one (0.34) would be expected without a change, so the volume of evidence far exceeds what random re-splits produce. The placement change is shown directly and consistently: all pairwise relations flip entirely rather than partially (co-occurrence stays at 100% while the order inverts), the two segments show large position shifts in opposite directions, all four boundary activities change their successors in DIR, and the lag changes (A24→A25 from 2 to 4, A35→A20 from 4 to 2) fit the exchange exactly. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL008, REL009, REL015, REL016, REL018, REL019, LAG001, LAG002]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that both orders already existed as variants before the cut-off and only their mix shifted (a rare variant in the earlier window could hide in 200 traces); the complete, bidirectional reversals make pure sampling noise implausible, but the later order cannot be proven absent earlier. The internal ordering within the A15/A17/A18 group is also not directly pinned down. [REL008, REL015, REL018]

Other differences: within the single step, the routing shares moved toward A14 (about 31 to 44 of 100 cases, A16 about 38 to 29, A19 about 32 to 28), a shift in choice frequencies alongside the relocation. [POS001, POS003, POS006, DIR007, DIR008]
