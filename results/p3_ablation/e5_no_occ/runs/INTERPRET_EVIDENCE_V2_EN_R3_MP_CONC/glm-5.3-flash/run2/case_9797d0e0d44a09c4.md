### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the block A19 → A25 → A20 between A41 and A26 is executed in essentially every case, whereas in the earlier window it ran in about 47 in 100 cases and the remaining cases went directly from A41 to A26, skipping the block; in other words, A19, A25 and A20 changed from optional to (near-)mandatory and the direct A41 → A26 transition all but disappeared, while the block's internal order and its exit to A26 stayed the same. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL007, REL008, DIR002, LAG001, DIR001]

The change is shown directly by relational evidence (co-occurrence of A19 with A20, A25, A26 and A41 rising from about 47 to about 995 in 1000 traces, with "block absent" shares falling from about 53 in 100 to nearly zero), by the successor profile at A41, and by the A41→A26 distance distribution; every item points the same way, and each difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. At the screening level, 20 control-flow items qualified where only about 0.42 would be expected without a change, far beyond what a stable process produces. [REL001, REL003, DIR002, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish whether the direct A41 → A26 path was removed altogether or remains available but is now chosen in only about 1 in 200 cases; the many relation items describe one underlying block and are not independent confirmations; and positions are event indexes, not clock times, so no timing claim is made.
