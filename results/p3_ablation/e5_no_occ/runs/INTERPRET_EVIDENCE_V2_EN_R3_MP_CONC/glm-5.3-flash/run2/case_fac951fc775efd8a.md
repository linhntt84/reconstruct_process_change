### Observed differences

- In the later window, the activities A20, A22 and A23 — present in essentially every trace in both windows — sit much later in the trace (mean event position about 0.64, against about 0.39 before), while A19, A21 and A24, present in roughly 28–44 in 100 traces, sit much earlier (about 0.35–0.37, against about 0.67–0.69 before) [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007].

- A02 appeared in about 8 in 100 traces before and about 34 in 100 after, and now typically leads to A19, A24 and A21 (about 37, 27 and 24 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, against a spread over many activities before); A40 now leads to A02, A19, A21 and A24 (about 31, 29, 20 and 18 in 100) where before it led to A22, A23 and A20 (about 37, 30 and 27 in 100) [POS001, DIR001, DIR009, REL001, LAG005].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The 56 qualifying items are roughly 100 times the 0.54 items expected by chance for the 109 tested control-flow units, so the set of differences as a whole is not what an unchanged process produces. The evidence supports the hypothesis that the relative order of two groups of activities was reversed: in the earlier window A20, A22 and A23 ran early in each trace and A19, A21 and A24 ran late, with A25 and A29 in between; in the later window A19, A21 and A24 run early and A20, A22 and A23 run late, while A25 and A29 keep their middle position. The order flips are near-complete wherever the activities co-occur (for example, A20 preceded A25 in about 98 in 100 traces before and in none after), the position shifts are large and opposite in direction (about 0.25–0.32 of the trace length), and the successor and distance changes match the same reorder rather than pointing to separate differences [REL017, REL024, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG004].

The evidence cannot distinguish a reordering within one dominant trace variant from a replacement of that variant by one with the opposite order, and A19, A21 and A24 occur in only about 28–44 in 100 traces, so their reversals are observed only on the subset of traces where they appear [POS002, POS004, POS007].

Other differences: A02 became far more frequent (about 8 to about 34 in 100 traces) and now typically precedes A19, A24 and A21, and A40 now leads into A02/A19/A21/A24 where before it led into A20/A22/A23; these participation and routing changes are consistent with the reorder but are not part of the order-reversal hypothesis itself [POS001, DIR001, DIR009, REL001, LAG005].
