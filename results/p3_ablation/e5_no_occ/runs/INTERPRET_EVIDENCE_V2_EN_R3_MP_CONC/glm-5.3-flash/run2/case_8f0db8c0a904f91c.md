### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block A19 → A25 → A20, executed after A41 and before A26, became optional in the later window: it is skipped in about 47 in 100 cases, with A41 then leading directly to A26, whereas in the earlier window it ran in every case; when executed (about 53 in 100 cases), its internal order and its connections to A26 are unchanged. This is shown directly by the co-occurrence profiles (each block activity co-occurs with A26, A41, A46 and A47 in 100 in 100 traces before versus about 53 in 100 after, with order preserved whenever the block runs), by the new direct A41→A26 link (0 to about 44 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions, adjacent in 44 in 100), and by the halved participation of A20 and A25 (about 200 down to about 107 of 200 traces). The screening qualified 35 items against roughly 0.5 expected for an unchanged process, so the ensemble is far beyond what chance produces; the agreeing relation items, however, largely reflect this one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. The evidence cannot distinguish a single optional skip from a mixture of case types in which some are routed around the block, and it cannot show what drives the skip.
