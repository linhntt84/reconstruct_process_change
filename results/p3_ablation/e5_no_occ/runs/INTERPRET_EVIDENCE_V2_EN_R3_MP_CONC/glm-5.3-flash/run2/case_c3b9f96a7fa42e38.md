### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The screening context makes the ensemble meaningful: 31 control-flow items qualified where fewer than 1 (0.53) would be expected without a change, far beyond what random re-splits of these windows produce. The evidence supports the possibility that the sequential block A35, A36, A41 was displaced past A27 and A26: earlier, the flow ran from A25 into the block, the block into A27, with A26 later leading straight into A30; in the later window A25 leads almost directly to A27, A26 leads into the block, and the block leads on to A30. The order reversal is shown directly in all six tested pairs at about 200 of 200 traces each, and the routing, position and gap changes all match the same reordering. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG005, LAG009]

Because co-occurrence stays at 100 in 100 for every pair in both windows, this is a reordering rather than activities becoming optional or being replaced. The main limitation is that the many agreeing items largely mirror one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations, and the relational evidence cannot distinguish whether the group moved later or whether A26 and A27 moved earlier relative to a stationary group; both descriptions fit the observed reversal.
