### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new intermediate step, executed as one of three alternative activities, now runs between A41 and A26. In the later window, A41 is followed by one of three activities that were not observed in the earlier window — A19 (about 38 in 100 traces), A25 (about 35 in 100) or A20 (about 30 in 100) — and that activity is in turn followed by A26, whereas before, A41 led directly to A26 in about 83 in 100 traces. [LAG001, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003]

The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the A41–A26 distance shifted from one event to two or three [LAG001]; A41's transitions moved from A26 to the three new activities [DIR004]; A19 and A25 now lead predominantly to A26 [DIR002, DIR003]; the ordering relations place each new activity after A41 and before A26 in shares matching its occurrence [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]; and A26's participation is unchanged while its position moved only slightly later [POS004]. Screening qualified 18 control-flow items where about 0.3 would be expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, so the ensemble is far beyond what chance produces — though many items describe the same underlying traces rather than independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001]

Limitations: the A20-to-A26 link is inferred from ordering relations rather than a directly observed successor profile, and "not observed" in the earlier window strictly means only that A19, A20 and A25 did not appear there — the evidence cannot distinguish a newly added step from a previously unused one. About 1 in 100 later traces still show A41 directly followed by A26, so the intermediate step need not run in every case. [POS001, POS002, POS003, REL006, LAG001]

Other differences: A02's outgoing transition mix changed as described above; it is weakly supported by the small number of observed transitions and is not covered by the main hypothesis. [DIR001]
