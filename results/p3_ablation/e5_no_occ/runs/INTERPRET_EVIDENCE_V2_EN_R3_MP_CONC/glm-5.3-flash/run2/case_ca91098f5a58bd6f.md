### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of three alternative activities A14, A15 and A20, which previously executed between A36 and A21 — exactly one of them per case, taken in roughly a 35/30/35 split — is no longer executed, and that A36 now leads directly into A21. This is shown directly by the successor change at A36 [DIR004] and by the A36→A21 distance collapsing from two positions to one in all traces [LAG001]; it is consistent with the complete disappearance of A14, A15 and A20 from the later sample [POS001, POS002, POS003] and with the relational items showing that these activities no longer co-occur with, or keep their earlier order to, A21, A36, A19 or each other [REL004, REL006, REL009, REL011, REL013, REL015]. The 25 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.27 expected by chance for the 54 tested units, so sampling fluctuation cannot account for the pattern as a whole, and every item's difference exceeds those seen in about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits. An important limitation: zero participation only shows the activities were not observed in the later window, so the evidence cannot rule out that they were renamed or merged into another activity rather than dropped, nor can it show whether the bypass holds beyond these 200 traces.
