### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative block A19/A20/A25 — of which one activity executed per case — now runs in a different place: in the earlier window it sat between A45 and A26 (A45 → one of the three → A26), whereas in the later window A45 feeds A26 directly and the block executes after A27, in the segment around A28, continuing from there to A28 or A32. This is shown directly by the reversed order relations with A26 and A27 [REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007], the changed successors of A45, A19, A20, A25, A27 and A28 [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006], the changed pairwise distances [LAG003, LAG004, LAG007, LAG008], and the position shift of the three activities [POS001, POS002, POS003]. The pattern is consistent across all 27 qualifying items, far more than the roughly 0.4 expected without a change, and the observations behind it are substantial (relational items cover all 200 traces per window; directional items 61–77 traces).

Limitations: the exact arrangement around A28 cannot be fully resolved — the both-orders pattern for A19–A28 and A25–A28 indicates the block can run either directly after A27 and before A28 or after A28, but the evidence does not establish how often the block executes within one case, and some lag items rest on a single observation in one window [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG006]. Positions are event positions rather than clock times, so clock-time timing is unknown. A competing explanation the evidence cannot exclude is that the block was removed from its old position and separately inserted at the new one rather than relocated; behaviourally the two are indistinguishable here.

Other differences: the choice among the three alternatives shifted slightly — A19 appears in about 31 of 100 later-window traces (about 38 before), A25 in about 38 (about 32 before), with A20 roughly stable at about 33. [POS001, POS002, POS003]
