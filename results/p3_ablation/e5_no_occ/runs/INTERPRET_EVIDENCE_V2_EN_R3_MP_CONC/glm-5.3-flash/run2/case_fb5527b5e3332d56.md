### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window cases no longer pass through the block A27, A28, A33 between A21 and A23: the path A21 → A22 → A23, taken in about half of the cases before, is now taken in virtually all of them, while A27, A28 and A33 appear in only about 1 in 200 traces. The change is shown directly by relational and participation evidence — A21–A22 co-occurrence rose from about 52% to about 99.5% while A21's co-occurrence with each of A27, A28 and A33 fell from about 48.5% to about 0.5% [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]; the block activities' trace participation fell from about 48.5% to about 0.5% [POS002, POS003, POS004]; and the A21→A23 distance collapsed from a mix of two and four steps to almost always two [LAG001]. All 30 qualified items belong to the control-flow family against roughly 0.29 expected by chance, so chance sampling cannot account for the set as a whole, and the items are mutually consistent in pointing to this one structural difference.

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish whether the A27/A28/A33 branch was removed entirely or still exists but is almost never taken, since near-zero participation only means the activities were not observed; and the after-window ordering inside the block rests on a single trace, so the block's internal behaviour in the later window is effectively unknown.

Other differences: A23's slightly earlier position within traces is consistent with the shorter path rather than an independent relocation [POS001].
