### Observed differences

- **A31's successors changed, and A31 now recurs within traces.** In the earlier window, all 200 observed outgoing transitions of A31 (one occurrence per trace) went to A20. In the later window, about 71 in 100 of A31's outgoing transitions go to A25 and about 29 in 100 to A20; the observed outgoing transitions rose to 697 across the same 200 traces, implying A31 runs about 3–4 times per trace in the later window versus once per trace before. [DIR001]
- **The fixed order between A25 and A31 loosened.** Both activities co-occur in every trace in both windows. Earlier, A25 preceded A31 in essentially all traces with no interleaving; in the later window only about 32 in 100 traces keep that one-way order, while about 68 in 100 show the two interleaved or repeated (the reverse order stays at zero). [REL001]
- **The tight spacing dissolved.** Earlier, A25 and A31 sat exactly two event positions apart in virtually every case; in the later window only about 12 in 100 pairs remain two steps apart, about 5 in 100 sit five steps apart, and the rest fall at other spacings. [LAG001]
- **Both activities sit slightly later and far more variably within the trace.** A25's mean relative position moved from about 0.41 to 0.44 and A31's stayed near 0.48, while the spread roughly doubled for both (standard deviation about 0.06 to 0.12); these are event positions, not clock times. [POS001, POS002]
- **Scale of the signal.** Five control-flow items qualified across 29 tested units, against roughly 0.14 expected if nothing had changed, and each item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [DIR001, POS001, POS002, REL001, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the fixed single pass through A25 and A31 was replaced by a repeating, interleaved pattern. In the earlier window, A25 and A31 each ran once per trace in a fixed order (A25 before A31, two positions apart) and A31 always handed control to A20. In the later window, A31 recurs about 3–4 times per trace and returns to A25 in about 71 in 100 of its outgoing transitions instead of proceeding to A20; as a result, the strict A25-before-A31 order survives in only about a third of traces, the pair is interleaved or repeated in about two-thirds, and the previously universal two-step spacing is retained in only about one case in eight. [DIR001, REL001, LAG001, POS001, POS002]

Supporting signs: the relational, directional and lag evidence all show the change directly and consistently on the same activity pair; both activities remain present in all traces in both windows, so this is not a participation or sampling artefact; and the qualified-item count (5 versus about 0.14 expected) cannot be explained by chance as a whole. [DIR001, REL001, LAG001, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: the relation indicator cannot by itself separate interleaving from repetition — the roughly 3.5-fold rise in A31's outgoing transitions points to genuine repetition, but the evidence does not show which activities occupy the space between repeats or whether the cycle involves further activities; and with no time items in the evidence, timing explanations can be neither supported nor ruled out. [DIR001, REL001]

Other differences: none — all five qualifying items describe the same A25–A31 segment, with A20 appearing only as the successor that A31 has largely stopped flowing to. [DIR001, POS001, POS002, REL001, LAG001]
