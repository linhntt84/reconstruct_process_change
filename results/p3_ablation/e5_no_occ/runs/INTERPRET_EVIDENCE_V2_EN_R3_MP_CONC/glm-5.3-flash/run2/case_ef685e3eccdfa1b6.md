### Observed differences

- **Participation of the six middle activities collapsed from mandatory to partial.** In the earlier window, A28, A30, A31, A32, A33 and A34 each appeared in all 200 traces; in the later window A28, A30 and A31 each appear in about 47 in 100 traces (93 of 200) and A32, A33 and A34 each in about 56 in 100 (111 of 200). A25 and A26 still appear in every trace in both windows. [POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

- **The two groups of three went from sequential blocks to near-exclusive alternatives.** Earlier, a first-group activity (A28/A30/A31) and a second-group activity (A32/A33/A34) co-occurred in 100% of traces, always with the first group before the second. In the later window such pairs co-occur in only about 2 in 100 traces; in about 45 in 100 only the first group runs, and in about 54 in 100 only the second. [REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024]

- **A25 became a branch point.** Earlier, its observed outgoing transitions led only to A28 (about 34 in 100), A30 (37) and A31 (30); later, about 46 in 100 lead to A28/A30/A31 and about 54 in 100 to A32/A33/A34. [DIR001]

- **The first group now connects straight to A26.** Earlier, transitions out of A28, A30 and A31 included edges into A32/A33/A34 (about 29–38 in 100) and none to A26; later those edges nearly vanish (about 0–2 in 100) and roughly 26–35 in 100 lead directly to A26. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

- **Distances shrank accordingly.** The gap from A25 to A32/A33/A34 and from A28/A30/A31 to A26 was 4–5 event positions in essentially all earlier traces; in the later window it is 1–3 positions in about 97 in 100 of the relevant traces. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG008, LAG011]

- **Mild position shifts.** A25's mean event position moved from about 0.62 to 0.67 of the trace and A26's from 0.83 to 0.81; the six middle activities converged to a mean position near 0.74 (from about 0.68 for the first group and 0.77 for the second). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

- **Weak items.** Seven lag items on pairs across or within the groups rest on only 4 after-window traces (the roughly 2% of cases containing both groups) and deserve little weight. [LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG009, LAG010, LAG012, LAG013]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the middle of the process changed from two always-executed sequential blocks into mutually exclusive alternatives. Earlier, every trace ran A25, then A28/A30/A31 in mixed order, then A32/A33/A34 in mixed order, then A26. In the later window, A25 is followed by either the group A28/A30/A31 (about 46 in 100 traces) or the group A32/A33/A34 (about 54 in 100), with both groups in only about 2 in 100; the executed group is then followed directly by A26, so one middle group is skipped in roughly 98 in 100 traces and A25 acts as the branch point. [REL015, REL019, REL022, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG004]

The support is direct and consistent: the relational items show the co-occurrence collapse directly (from 1.0 to 0.465/0.555 within a group and 0.02 across groups), the successor items show the new split at A25 and the direct edges to A26, and the position and distance measures move in the same direction. Screening qualified 52 control-flow items where about 0.48 would be expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data; note that the many relation items describe one underlying structural difference, not independent confirmations. [REL015, DIR001, DIR002, POS003, LAG001]

An important competing explanation remains: the two windows could draw different case mixes (for example two case types whose proportions shifted) rather than reflect a modified single process — the about 2 in 100 later traces that still contain both groups show the earlier sequence can still occur. The seven 4-trace lag items cannot sharpen the picture, and positions and lags use event positions, not clock time. [LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG009, LAG010, LAG012, LAG013]

Other differences: none beyond the above; the ordering within each group of three stayed mixed, with no fixed order, in both windows. [REL013, REL014, REL018, REL025, REL026, REL027]
