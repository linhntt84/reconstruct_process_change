### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The 16 qualifying items compare with fewer than 0.3 expected by chance among the 58 tested control-flow units, so the evidence as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002]

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the segment A30 → A31 → A36 — previously executed once per case between A14 and the exit towards A25 — is typically repeated several times per case: A36 now usually leads back to A30 (about 71 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, versus about 1 in 200 before) instead of exiting to A25, and A30, A31 and A36 each occur about three to four times per trace instead of once. The strict orderings tied to the single pass (A14 before A30 and A36; A30 before A36) now hold in only about a third of traces and are replaced by interleaved or repeated patterns in about two thirds — a consequence of the repetition rather than a reordering. Supporting signs: the routing flip at A36 and the appearance of A36 → A30 successions (1 vs about 136 of 200 traces) are observed directly; the occurrence counts of all three activities roughly tripled; the position spread of A30 and A36 about doubled, as expected when an activity recurs at varying depths; and the exit to A25 remains about as frequent as before, fitting an added repetition rather than a replacement. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, REL003, REL004, REL005, POS003, POS004]

Limitations: the indicators aggregate occurrences and pairs, so they cannot show how repetitions are distributed across individual cases — whether most cases now repeat the block a similar number of times or a subset repeats it many times while about a third of traces still resemble the old single pass. The evidence also does not fully explain why the tight two-step A30 → A36 gap largely disappeared even though the immediate chain remains dominant, so the exact shape of the repeated cycle stays inferred. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a changed mix of case-level behaviours between the windows rather than one new typical behaviour. [LAG001, DIR003, DIR004, REL003]
