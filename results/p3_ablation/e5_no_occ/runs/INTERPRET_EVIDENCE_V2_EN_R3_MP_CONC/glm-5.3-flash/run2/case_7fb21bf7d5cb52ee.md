### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A10 executes as a step in the main flow between A14 and A15: A14 now leads to A10 in about two-thirds of its outgoing transitions and A10 leads on to A15 in about 85 in 100, so the previously dominant direct A14→A15 link is largely replaced by A14→A10→A15, and A10 itself changed from a rare (about 19 in 100 traces), late-placed activity to a frequent (about 70 in 100), early-placed one that precedes the mid-process activities. The signs are direct and consistent across successor changes at A14 and A10, immediate successions A14→A10 and A10→A15, co-occurrence and order with A15 (A10 first in about 38 in 100 later traces versus about 1 in 200 before), A15's slightly later position, and screening produced 74 qualifying items where fewer than 1 would be expected without change. Limitations: the A14→A10 succession was observed in only 11 traces before, so this edge may have existed rarely rather than being new, and the evidence cannot separate a repositioning of A10 from a shifted mix of coexisting variants, since about 30 in 100 later traces still show A15 without A10.
