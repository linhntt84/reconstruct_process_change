- The three activities A31, A32 and A37 appeared in about 46 in 100 traces in the earlier window but in essentially all traces (about 199 of 200) in the later window, and their average event position moved from roughly 0.59 to roughly 0.40 of the trace, so they now run much earlier [POS006, POS007, POS008].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block A31–A32–A37, executed together in no fixed order, changed from an optional branch alternative to A22 (running after A21 in about 46 in 100 traces) into a mandatory block executed directly after A20 and before A23, while A22 became mandatory and now always follows A21; the earlier routing "A20 → A23 → A21 → either A22 or the A31/A32/A37 group → A26" appears replaced by a single fixed sequence "A20 → A31/A32/A37 (mixed order) → A23 → A21 → A22 → A26" [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, POS006, POS003].

This is shown directly and consistently: 37 control-flow items qualified where about 0.33 would be expected without a change, so chance cannot explain the pattern as a whole; the placement change is demonstrated by relational evidence (mutual exclusivity becoming co-occurrence with a fixed order, order flips between the group and A23/A21, the A20→A23 lag moving from 1 to 4 steps) and by successor changes at A20, A21 and the group, with participation and position shifts agreeing across the DIR, POS, REL and LAG items [REL011, REL014, LAG001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003].

Limitations: the evidence shows relative orders and event positions, not clock time, and it cannot distinguish whether the A31/A32/A37 block moved earlier or A23/A21/A22 moved later — both fit the same relational changes; activities in the position gap between A23 and A21 are not covered, so the exact local wiring there remains unverified [POS002, POS004, LAG001].

Other differences: A20's average position shifted slightly earlier (about 0.36 to 0.34) and A26's slightly later (about 0.63 to 0.66), consistent with the resequencing but not part of the main hypothesis [POS001, POS005].
