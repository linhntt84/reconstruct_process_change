### Observed differences

- Participation changed sharply: in the earlier window A36, A37 and A42 each ran in about 46 in 100 traces and A27 in about 54 in 100; in the later window A36, A37 and A42 each run in about 995 in 1000 traces and A27 in every trace. A25, A26, A28 and A31 appear in all traces in both windows. [POS003, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005]

[REL009, REL010, LAG003, POS004, POS002, POS003, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural reorganisation involving A25, A26, A27, A28, A31 and the block A36–A37–A42: the block, which earlier ran as an optional branch after A26 in about 46 in 100 cases as an alternative to A27, is in the later window executed in almost every case directly after A25 and before A28, while A27 is now always executed after A26; A28 no longer immediately follows A25 but follows the block, and A31 remains the closing activity, now always preceded by A27. The change is shown directly by reversed order relations (A26 and A28 preceded the block earlier and follow it later), by successor changes at A25, A26, A36, A37 and A42, and by participation moving from partial to near-universal; the screening flagged 41 control-flow items where fewer than one (0.48 over 96 units) would be expected without a change, so sampling variation cannot explain the pattern as a whole. [REL006, REL014, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS006, POS003, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence is control-flow only, positions are event positions rather than clock time, and a few lag observations rest on a single trace and were not relied on; the evidence also cannot show whether unobserved activities sit between the block and A28. A competing explanation is that the two windows sample a shifted mix of two coexisting variants; the near-universal participation and the reversed orders make a pure mix shift unlikely, but changes outside the tested activities cannot be excluded. [LAG002, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

Other differences: A25 sits slightly earlier in the later window (mean relative position about 0.36 to 0.35); A25 reaches A36 within one to three steps in about 199 in 200 later traces (a single earlier observation), and the block rarely appears within five steps before A31 in the later window (1 trace), both based on very few observations in one window.
