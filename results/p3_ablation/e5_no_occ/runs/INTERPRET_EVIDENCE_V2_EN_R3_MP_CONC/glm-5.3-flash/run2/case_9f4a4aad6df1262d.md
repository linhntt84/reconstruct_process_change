### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the six activities formed one fixed chain, A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32: each of these transitions carried about 93–100% of its source activity's outgoing transitions, and every tested pair followed this order in about 98–99 in 100 traces [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL001, REL006].
- In the later window this chain is dissolved: A31 now leads directly to A24 in about 47 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (previously never), A23 goes straight to A32 in about 49 in 100 (previously always to A24), and A24, A25 and A26 each gained edges back to A20, A22 or A23 — for example A25 now returns to A20/A23/A22 in about 51 in 100 of its transitions, against roughly 0 before [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007].
- The order between the two groups of activities flipped or became unconstrained: A20 preceded A24 in 98% of traces before but in 25% after (A24 first in 73%); A23 preceded A24 in about 98.5% before but in 15% after (A24 first in 83.5%); A20/A26, A22/A24 and A23/A25 are now split roughly 50/50 between the two orders [REL001, REL006, REL003, REL004, REL007, REL002, REL005, REL008].
- Reversed sequences that were almost unobserved before are now common: within five steps, A24 followed by A20 occurs in about 148 later-window traces (1 before), A24 → A22 in about 94 (1 before), A26 → A20 in about 101 (1 before) and A25 → A20 in about 64 (1 before) [LAG014, LAG015, LAG021, LAG019].
- Positions shifted accordingly: A20, A22 and A23 now occur later in the trace (mean relative position about 0.59 → 0.63, 0.56 → 0.59 and 0.62 → 0.68), while A24, A25 and A26 occur earlier (about 0.65 → 0.59, 0.70 → 0.68, 0.67 → 0.64) [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006].
- Previously fixed distances are now spread: A20 → A23 was adjacent in 100% of cases and is now adjacent in about 68%; A31 → A24 was always four steps apart and is now one step apart in about 47%; A23 → A32 moved from typically four steps to one step in about 49% [LAG001, LAG027, LAG013]. All six activities still occur in essentially every trace in both windows, so none became optional [REL001, REL006].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the fixed sequential routing through A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26 and A25 (towards A32) was restructured into several routes: the block A24/A25/A26, which previously always ran after A20/A22/A23, now frequently runs before them; A31 often enters the block directly at A24, bypassing A22, A20 and A23; and A23 often exits directly to A32, bypassing A24, A26 and A25. Because every activity remains present in nearly all traces and the reversed orders were almost unobserved earlier (about 1 in 200 observations), this is a change in ordering constraints and routing rather than activities becoming optional or existing paths merely being taken with different frequencies [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR007, REL001, REL006, LAG014, LAG015, LAG019, LAG021].

The control-flow screening produced 49 qualifying items where about 0.47 would be expected without a change, so the collection of differences cannot be explained by chance sampling; the order reversal is shown directly by the relation indicators (pair-order share flips of 0.73–0.84) and by the new reversed adjacencies, and the direction, position and distance indicators all describe the same restructuring [DIR003, DIR007, REL001, REL006, LAG013, LAG027, POS001, POS004]. A limitation is that for several pairs the later window splits roughly 50/50 between the two orders, so the evidence cannot determine whether the later behaviour is a single new route or a mixture of the old chain and new orderings across cases, nor how many distinct routes exist [REL003, REL004, REL007]; the agreeing items also largely describe one underlying restructuring and are not independent confirmations [DIR001, REL001, LAG001].

Other differences: a few new successors appear at about 1 in 100 transitions or less in the later window (for example A20 → A12/A30, A23 → A10/A19, A25 → A27/A48); these are minor and may be rare variants [DIR001, DIR003, DIR005].
