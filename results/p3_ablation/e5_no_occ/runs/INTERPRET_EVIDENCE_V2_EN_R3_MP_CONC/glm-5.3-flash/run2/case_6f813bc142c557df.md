### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the ordering of the block A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A25, A26 (exiting to A32) changed from a flexible, mixed ordering to a single fixed sequence: in the earlier window these activities ran in every trace but in varying orders with several alternative successions, while in the later window they execute as A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25 → A32 in about 99 in 100 traces. This is a change in ordering constraints (a mixed order becoming fixed), not a swap of two activities and not a change in which activities run, since all of them appear in every trace in both windows. The relational evidence shows the change directly — order shares flip or tighten by 0.22–0.87 and reverse-direction pairs drop to about 1 observation — the successor profiles agree, and the position shifts point the same way; with 56 qualifying items against roughly 0.56 expected if nothing had changed, the body of evidence is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. The evidence cannot distinguish a genuine change in ordering constraints from a changed mix of trace variants that concentrates on one dominant sequence, nor determine whether the A08 decline belongs to the same change.
