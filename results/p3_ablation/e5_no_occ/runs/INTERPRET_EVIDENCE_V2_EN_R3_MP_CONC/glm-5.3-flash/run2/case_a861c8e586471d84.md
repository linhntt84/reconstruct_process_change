- In the earlier window, essentially every case executed exactly one of three alternative activities between A41 and A26 — A19 in about 36 in 100 cases, A25 in about 35 and A20 in about 31 — and the three never appeared together in the same case. In the later window, A19 and A25 appear in 0 of 200 traces and A20 in only 1 of 200. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL007, REL009, REL014]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the intermediate step between A41 and A26 was removed or bypassed: in the earlier window each case ran exactly one of A19, A20 or A25 between A41 and A26, whereas in the later window these three activities are no longer observed and A41 leads directly to A26 in about 92 in 100 of its transitions, with the A41-to-A26 distance shrinking from two steps to one. Because the three activities are (nearly) absent in the later window, this is a removal of an activity group rather than a shift of shares between existing alternatives. The change is shown directly by the successor change at A41 and the lag change, and consistently by the collapse of co-occurrence between A19/A20/A25 and A12, A14, A24, A26, A31 and A41; with 33 qualifying items against roughly 0.42 expected if nothing had changed, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. An important competing explanation remains: zero participation only means the activities were not observed in the 200 later traces, so complete removal cannot be distinguished from the step having become extremely rare, and the single later A20 case routing to A30 hints at a rare alternative continuation that one case cannot establish. Other differences: the small positional shifts of A14, A26 and A41, and the A24 changes (higher participation, and A24 now typically one step before A31 instead of two), are consistent with knock-on effects of the removed step — A19, A20 and A25 previously ran before A24 in about 15–17 in 100 cases each — but the evidence does not tie them to it directly.
