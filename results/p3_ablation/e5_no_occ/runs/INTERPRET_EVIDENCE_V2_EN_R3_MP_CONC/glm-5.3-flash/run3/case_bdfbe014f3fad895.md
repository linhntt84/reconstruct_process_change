### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, about half of the cases replace the A27 step between A26 and A28 with a new block of three activities — A32, A33 and A38 — that execute together in varying order: A27 runs in about 52 out of 100 later cases (previously in all), A26 branches to A27 or to the block in roughly equal shares, the block sits mid-trace and precedes A28 in about 48 out of 100 cases, and the four-step A26–A28 distance in about 46 out of 100 later cases matches three inserted activities. The pattern is shown directly by directional, relational, positional and lag evidence that agrees across many items, each resting on roughly 100 later observations, and 49 qualifying items against roughly 0.5 expected without change rules out sampling noise as an explanation of the evidence set as a whole. Limitations: positions and lags reflect event order rather than clock time, and the evidence cannot show whether the two routes serve the same kinds of cases or whether the later window mixes two case populations — a competing explanation it cannot rule out.
