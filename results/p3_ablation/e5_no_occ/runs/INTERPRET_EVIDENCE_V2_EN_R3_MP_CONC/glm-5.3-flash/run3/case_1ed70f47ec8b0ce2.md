### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two segments exchanged their order in the case: the segment containing the mutually exclusive alternatives A14, A16 and A19 — earlier placed between A35 and A20, one of them per case, each followed directly by A20 — now runs after A24, and each of them leads directly to A25; the segment containing the block A15, A17 and A18 — earlier entered from A24 and exiting to A25 — now runs after A35 and exits to A20. A35, A20, A24 and A25 keep their anchor roles, with A20 and A24 each sitting slightly later because the block now preceding them grew from one activity to three. The support is direct and consistent: complete order flips in all 21 observed cross-pairs with unchanged co-occurrence, full successor-set replacements at A24 and A35 and at both blocks' exits, mirrored position shifts of about 0.26–0.35, and matching gap changes (4↔2 event positions); 39 qualifying items against roughly 0.34 expected without change makes chance implausible as an explanation of the pattern as a whole. Limitations: positions are event orders rather than clock times; the evidence covers only these activities, so further changes elsewhere (for example between A20 and A24) cannot be checked; and the evidence cannot distinguish a relocation of the two segments from an equivalent rewiring of edges that produces the same traces.
