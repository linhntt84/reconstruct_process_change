### Observed differences

- Participation rose for all six activities in the two groups: A28, A30 and A31 each appeared in about 49 in 100 traces before and about 99 in 100 after; A32, A33 and A34 each appeared in about 52 in 100 before and in virtually all traces after. A25 and A26 were present in all traces in both windows. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]
- In the earlier window the two groups were alternatives: cross-group pairs such as A28 with A32, A30 with A33 or A31 with A34 never shared a trace (0 in 100). In the later window every such pair co-occurs in about 98 in 100 traces, always with the A28/A30/A31-side activity first. [REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024]
- The anchors are stable: A25 precedes A28/A30/A31 in essentially all co-occurrences in both windows, and A32/A33/A34 precede A26 in about 99–100 in 100 co-occurrences after; A28/A30/A31 preceded A26 in about 49 in 100 before versus about 99 in 100 after. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- Inside each group there is no fixed order in either window: for example A28 before A30 in about 24 in 100 versus the reverse in about 25 in 100 before, and about 49 versus 50 after; the same both-orders pattern holds within A32/A33/A34. [REL013, REL014, REL018, REL025, REL026, REL027]
- Successors changed accordingly: A25's observed outgoing transitions were spread over all six activities (roughly 15–19 in 100 each) before, and afterwards concentrate on A28/A30/A31 (about 30–38 in 100 each), with A32 and A34 at about 1 in 100 and A33 no longer observed; A28, A30 and A31 led to A26 in about 30–36 in 100 of their observed transitions before and essentially never after, now leading instead to A32/A33/A34 (about 10–13 in 100 each). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- Step distances grew: A25 to A32/A33/A34, and A28/A30/A31 to A26, sat 1–3 steps apart before (roughly a third each); afterwards about 90–98 in 100 observed pairs sit 4–5 steps apart. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]
- Relative positions shifted mildly: A25's mean position moved earlier (about 0.67 to 0.62 of trace length) and A26's later (about 0.80 to 0.83), consistent with traces now containing both groups. [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a choice between two alternative three-activity groups was removed: before, a trace ran A25, then exactly one of {A28, A30, A31} or {A32, A33, A34} — each group in about half of the cases, never both, with no fixed order inside the group — followed by A26; in the later window both groups run in almost every case, in the sequence A25 → A28/A30/A31 → A32/A33/A34 → A26, still without a fixed order inside either group. [REL015, REL019, REL022, REL013, REL025, REL001, REL010]

Supporting signs: the relational items show the change directly, from never co-occurring to co-occurring in about 98 in 100 traces in a single fixed cross-group direction; the successor changes at A25 and at A28/A30/A31 match the second group now sitting between the first group and A26; the shift from 1–3 to 4–5 steps matches three activities being inserted in between; and screening qualified 45 control-flow items where about 0.44 would be expected if nothing had changed, far beyond what chance produces. Many items describe the same underlying trace behaviour (the nine cross-group pairs), so they corroborate one change rather than nine independent ones. [REL015, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG004]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample different case mixes — for example a variant that always contained both groups replacing the two single-group variants — rather than a routing change within one process; and LAG and POS use event positions, so the "inserted in between" reading rests on relative order and step gaps, not clock time. [LAG001, POS001]
