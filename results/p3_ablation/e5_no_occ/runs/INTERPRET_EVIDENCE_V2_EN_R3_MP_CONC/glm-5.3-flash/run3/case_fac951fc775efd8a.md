### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the block containing A19, A21 and A24 and the block containing A20, A22 and A23 exchanged their places around the A25 → A29 connector: earlier the sequence ran A40 → {A20, A22, A23} → A25 → A29 → {A19, A21, A24} → A30, while later it runs A40 → {A19, A21, A24} → A25 → A29 → {A20, A22, A23} → A30, with the handovers inside each block preserved. [REL017, REL018, REL010, DIR002, DIR004, DIR007, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR008, DIR009, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007]

The support is direct and consistent: 56 control-flow items qualified where fewer than 1 was expected without a change, so the ensemble is far beyond what an unchanged process produces; the order flip is complete in both directions across about 21 crossing pairs, each backed by roughly 200 traces; positions, pair distances and successor distributions all move as the reordering predicts; and the blocks' participation is essentially unchanged, which fits a reordering of existing activities rather than activities being added, removed or made optional. The evidence cannot rule out that the later window samples a shifted mix of case types with different fixed orders rather than a modified process, and POS and LAG are based on event positions, so the exchange concerns execution order, not clock-time timing. [REL017, POS003]
