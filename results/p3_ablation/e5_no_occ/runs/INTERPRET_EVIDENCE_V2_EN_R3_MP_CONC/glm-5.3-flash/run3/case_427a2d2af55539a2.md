### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured: in the earlier window each case ran exactly one of the mutually exclusive alternatives A14, A15 or A16 between A39 and A24, whereas in the later window every case runs A21, A22 and A23 — all three, in no fixed order — between the same two anchors. The supporting signs are direct and consistent: 42 qualifying items against roughly 0.34 expected by chance in the control-flow family, so chance cannot explain the pattern as a whole; the successor change at A39 is observed directly; the presence flips rest on all 200 traces per window; the relational items show the new block's mixed internal order bounded by A39 before and A24 after; and the position and lag shifts match a block growing from one activity to three.
