### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The 31 qualifying control-flow items are many times the roughly 0.4 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by random window splits, and every item looks larger than in about 99.5 in 100 reference comparisons. The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative segment through A32, A33 and A38, which earlier executed between A26 and A28 in about half of the cases, is skipped in nearly all later executions: earlier, cases split about evenly between A26 → A27 → A28 (about 52 in 100 traces, with A27 directly before A28) and a route that inserted A32, A33 and A38 together, in mixed order, before A28 (about 48–49 in 100 traces); in the later window, about 96–97 in 100 cases take A26 → A27 → A28, and each of A32, A33 and A38 appears in only about 1 in 200 traces, while A26 and A28 themselves remain in all traces. Because the segment's activities are nearly absent in the later window, this indicates a segment being skipped or bypassed by the direct A26 → A27 link, not a mere shift of shares between intact alternatives.
