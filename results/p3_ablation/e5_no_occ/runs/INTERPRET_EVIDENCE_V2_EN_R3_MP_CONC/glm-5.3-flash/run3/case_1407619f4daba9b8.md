### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural repositioning together with a participation change: the three-activity block A31, A32, A37 (executed in no fixed internal order), which before ran in about 46 in 100 cases after A21 and before A26 as an alternative to A22, in the later window runs in essentially every case directly after A20 and before A23; A22, previously the alternative branch covering the other roughly 54 in 100 cases, is now executed after A21 in every case. The typical sequence thus changed from A20, A23, A21, then either A22 or the block, then A26, to A20, the block, A23, A21, A22, A26. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, LAG001, POS003, POS006, POS007, POS008]

The hypothesis rests on direct, mutually consistent relational and positional evidence: the order flip with A21, the switch from mutual exclusion to near-universal co-occurrence with A22, the A20-to-A23 gap growing from 1 to 4 events, and the matching routing changes at A20, A21 and the block members; the 37 qualifying items against about 0.33 expected without change rule out sampling noise as an explanation. Limitations: the indicators use event positions rather than clock time, and the evidence cannot distinguish a true relocation of the block from the old branch being retired and a new earlier placement introduced, since the old pattern is virtually absent afterwards (A21 before the block in none of the later traces). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, REL006, REL011, REL014]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that the two windows draw on different case mixes (for example a new variant dominating the later window) rather than a single modification; the near-total disappearance of the old pattern makes plain variant mixing unlikely, but the underlying reason for the shift is not observable in this evidence. [REL006, REL011, REL012, REL013]

Other differences: the remaining position shifts are small and follow from the same restructuring rather than appearing independent — A20 shifted slightly earlier (mean 0.36 to 0.34), while A21 (0.54 to 0.60), A23 (0.39 to 0.46) and A26 (0.63 to 0.66) shifted slightly later with tighter spread; the A21-to-A26 gap is now always 2 events, where before it was 2 (A22 branch) or 4 (block branch). [POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005, LAG002]
