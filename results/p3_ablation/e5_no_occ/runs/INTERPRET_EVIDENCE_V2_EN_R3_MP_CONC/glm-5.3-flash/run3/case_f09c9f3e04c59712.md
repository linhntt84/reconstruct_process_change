### Observed differences

- The always-executed activities A15, A17 and A18 moved from the early to the late part of the trace: their mean event position went from about 0.39 of the trace in the earlier window to about 0.64 in the later window. In the earlier window each of them ran before A20 and A24 in 100% of the traces where they co-occurred (all 200); in the later window each runs after A20 and A24 in all 200 traces — a complete order reversal. Their internal order stays flexible in both windows (each follows the others with roughly one-third shares). [POS002, POS004, POS005, REL008, REL009, REL015, REL016, REL018, REL019, DIR002, DIR004, DIR005]

- The optional activities A14, A16 and A19 — mutually exclusive alternatives whose shares sum to one occurrence per trace in both windows — moved in the opposite direction: A14 from about 31 in 100 traces at mean position 0.69 to about 44 in 100 at position 0.34; A16 from about 38 in 100 at 0.68 to about 29 in 100 at 0.35; A19 from about 32 in 100 at 0.69 to about 28 in 100 at 0.35. Each was previously always executed after A15/A17/A18/A20/A24 and is now always executed before them. [POS001, POS003, POS006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL017, REL020, REL021]

- Successors were rewired accordingly: A14, A16 and A19 led exclusively to A25 in the earlier window and exclusively to A20 in the later window; A24's outgoing transitions changed from A16 (about 38 in 100), A14 (about 31) and A19 (about 32) to A15 (about 32), A18 (about 34) and A17 (about 35); A35's changed from A17 (about 39), A18 (about 33) and A15 (about 29) to A14 (about 44), A16 (about 29) and A19 (about 28); and A15/A17/A18, which previously could transition to A20, now transition to A25 instead. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008]

- Distances changed consistently: in the later window A20 follows A35 after 2 event positions instead of 4, and A25 follows A24 after 4 positions instead of 2 (in essentially all traces). A20 and A24 themselves sit slightly earlier (mean position about 0.46 to 0.38 and 0.65 to 0.57). [LAG001, LAG002, POS007, POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two segments exchanged their places around the A20–A24 pair. In the earlier window, after A35 every trace executed A15, A17 and A18 in varying order, then A20, then A24, then exactly one of A14, A16 and A19, then A25; in the later window, after A35 every trace executes one of A14/A16/A19, then A20, then A24, then A15/A17/A18 in varying order, then A25. No activity was added or removed: A15, A17, A18, A20 and A24 appear in all traces in both windows, and the three alternatives remain mutually exclusive with shares summing to one per trace. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL008, LAG001, LAG002]

The change is shown directly and consistently: complete order reversals in 200 out of 200 traces for the trio versus A20/A24 (and the reverse for the optional activities), exclusive successor replacements, position shifts, and matching distance changes. Screening qualified 39 control-flow items where roughly 0.34 would be expected by chance among 68 tested units, and every item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits, so the pattern as a whole is not a sampling artefact. Limitations: the many items are overlapping views of the same trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot fully rule out that the two segments were rerouted independently rather than exchanged as one coordinated swap — though the resulting trace behaviour is the same either way. [REL008, REL015, REL001, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG002]

Other differences: the selection among the three alternatives shifted toward A14 — it was the executed alternative in about 31 in 100 traces before and about 44 in 100 after, while A16 fell from about 38 to 29 and A19 from about 32 to 28 in 100; this modest shift in branch frequencies is not explained by the reordering itself, and sampling variation between the two 200-trace windows cannot be excluded as a contributor to its size. [POS001, POS003, POS006, DIR001, DIR003, DIR006]
