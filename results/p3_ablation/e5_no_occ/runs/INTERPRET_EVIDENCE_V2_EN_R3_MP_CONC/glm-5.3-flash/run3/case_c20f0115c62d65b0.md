### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 were strict alternatives: A19 appeared in about 32 in 100 traces, A20 in about 28 and A25 in about 40, never two of them in the same trace, so together they covered essentially every trace. In the later window each was observed in only about 1 in 200 traces. [POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL006]
- In the earlier window, each of the three always ran after A27 and before A29, with A28 sometimes placed before and sometimes after it: A27's outgoing transitions went to A28 in about 53 in 100 cases and to one of the three alternatives in about 43 in 100, and A28's went to A29 in about 45 in 100 and to the alternatives in about 54 in 100. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL003, REL005, REL008, REL012]
- In the later window the routing is concentrated: A27 leads to A28 in about 97 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (previously about 53 in 100), and A28 leads to A29 in about 98 in 100 (previously about 45 in 100). [DIR004, DIR005]
- Succession distances tightened accordingly: A28 follows A27 within one step in about 98 in 100 traces (about 54 in 100 before), A29 follows A28 within one step in about 98 in 100 (about 45 in 100 before), and A29 now follows A27 two steps later in about 97 in 100 traces, where it previously followed three steps later in about 94 in 100. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- The alternatives' ties to the rest of the flow disappeared: earlier, every trace containing one of them also contained A27, A28 and A29; in the later window these co-occurrences drop to about 1 in 200 traces, and A27, A28 and A29 occur without any of the three in about 995 in 1000 traces. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- A29 itself is stable: present in all traces in both windows, with its mean relative position only slightly earlier (about 0.56 versus 0.55). [POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the flow bypasses the block of three alternative activities A19, A20 and A25 that every trace previously executed between A27 and A29: A27 now leads almost directly to A28 and A28 on to A29, while the three alternatives are executed in essentially no trace (about 1 in 200 each, versus one of them in every trace before). The signs are direct and mutually consistent across indicator families: the participation of A19, A20 and A25 collapsed, the outgoing transitions of A27 and A28 concentrated on A28 and A29, the co-occurrence of the alternatives with A27/A28/A29 vanished, and the A27→A28→A29 distances tightened to immediate succession. [DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL003, REL005, REL010, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003] The screening comparison supports this reading: 24 control-flow items qualified where roughly 0.35 would be expected without a change (about 70 times that expectation), and each item's difference exceeded about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003] A limitation is that the later profiles of A19, A20 and A25 rest on a single trace each, so their residual behaviour is weakly supported; the evidence shows the block is no longer executed, not whether it was removed from the process definition.

Other differences: In the single later traces that still contain A19, A20 or A25, these activities lead to successors not observed earlier (A19 to A44, A20 to A08, A25 to A42), each seen in one trace only and possibly rare variants; A29's mean relative position is marginally earlier (about 0.56 to 0.55). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS004]

Competing explanation: the evidence cannot distinguish a lasting removal of the A19/A20/A25 block from a period in which these activities were merely skipped in almost all cases, for example a shift in the mix of trace variants; both look identical in these windows. [REL001, REL002, REL006, POS001, POS002, POS003]
