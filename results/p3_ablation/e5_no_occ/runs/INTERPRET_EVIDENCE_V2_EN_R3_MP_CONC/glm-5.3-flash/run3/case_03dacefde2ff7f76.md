### Observed differences

- The group of three alternative activities A19, A20 and A25 changed its placement in the trace. In the earlier window each of the three appeared in about a third of traces (32–38 in 100) at roughly one third of the trace's length (mean relative position 0.31–0.34); in the later window they appear at about 60% of the trace's length (mean 0.58–0.59), while A26 and A27 sit slightly earlier than before (about 35% → 32% and 58% → 55% along the trace, both present in every trace in both windows). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

- Successors around this group were replaced. Earlier, A19, A20 and A25 led almost exclusively to A26 (about 97, 89 and 98 in 100 of their outgoing transitions); in the later window they lead to A28 (about 38–51 in 100) and A32 (about 43–58 in 100) instead. A45, which earlier sent about 85 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A19/A20/A25 (split roughly 31/26/28), now leads directly to A26 in about 89 in 100. A27 and A28, which earlier led almost only onward to A28 and A32 (about 98 and 94 in 100), now send about 47 and 51 in 100 of their transitions into the A19/A20/A25 group. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

- The order between the group and A26/A27 reversed. Earlier, the group always ran before A26 and A27 when they co-occurred (for example A19 before A26 in 37 of 100 traces, never the reverse); in the later window A26 and A27 always run first (A26 before A19 in about 30 of 100, A19 before A26 in 0). Against A28, the earlier fixed order (group first, in about 32–37 of 100 co-occurring traces) became a mixed order with no fixed direction (A25 before A28 in about 15 of 100, A28 before A25 in about 22 of 100). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

- Direct adjacencies changed accordingly. A25 immediately followed by A26 fell from 63 of 200 traces to 1; A20 immediately followed by A28, A20 by A32, and A28 by A20 rose from 1 trace each to 35, 66 and 30 of 200; A45 followed by A26 moved from distances of 2–3 steps to distance 1 in about 89 of 100 traces. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG006, LAG008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the three-way step formed by A19, A20 and A25 was displaced from the start to the end of the traced flow. Earlier, A45 led into the group (about 85 in 100 of its outgoing transitions), the chosen activity ran immediately before A26, and the trace then continued A27 → A28 → A32. In the later window, A45 leads straight to A26, and the group now runs after A27, where A28 and one of A19/A20/A25 execute in either order before A32 — A27 → A28 → one of the three → A32, or A27 → one of the three → A28 → A32, each in roughly half of the traces. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, POS001, POS002, POS003]

Chance is an implausible explanation at the screening level: 27 control-flow items qualified, about 67 times the roughly 0.4 expected if nothing had changed, and the directional, positional, relational and distance items all describe one coherent rearrangement rather than scattered differences. The two arrangements are nearly perfectly segregated: the later arrangement appears in only about 1 of 200 earlier traces (A20 reaching A28 within five steps), and the earlier arrangement in about 1 of 200 later traces (A25 immediately followed by A26), which a mere shift in how often existing paths are taken would not produce. [LAG001, LAG003, DIR001, REL001]

Limitations: several items describe overlapping aspects of the same trace-level change (the order reversals against A26 and A27 all reflect the group's move past those activities), so they are not independent confirmations, and the new adjacencies A20 → A28, A20 → A32 and A28 → A20 rest on a single earlier-window observation each, though the direction and relation items, backed by roughly 200 traces each, corroborate them. An important competing explanation remains: both arrangements could have existed as two variants before the change point with only their mix shifted; the near-absence of each arrangement in the opposite window makes this unlikely but cannot rule it out completely. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG006, REL001, REL004, REL006]

Other differences: the share of traces containing the group members shifted mildly (A19 from about 38 to 30 in 100 traces, A25 from about 32 to 38 in 100), which reads as an accompaniment of the group's new placement rather than a separate change. [POS001, POS002, POS003]
