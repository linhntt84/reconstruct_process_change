### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of activities A19, A20 and A25 was re-sequenced from the segment after A26 to the segment before it. In the earlier window these activities ran late, after A26, were reached from A27/A28 and continued into A28/A32; in the later window they run early, before A26, are typically entered from A45 — often via A08 — and hand over directly to A26. This is a structural re-sequencing, not a shift in how often existing paths are taken: A28 now bypasses the block straight to A32, transitions into the block from A27/A28 disappeared, and A45 no longer directly precedes A26 but flows through A08 and one of A19/A20/A25 first. [REL005, REL007, REL009, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG014]

The reversal is shown directly by the relational items and is consistent across the direction, position and lag indicators, including the one-step lag increase from A16/A18 to A26 expected from an insertion before A26. The 39 qualifying items are many times the 0.49 expected if nothing had changed, so chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole, and the key activities are well observed in both windows (for example A19 in 63 vs 69 traces, A25 in 73 vs 61, A26 and A45 in essentially all). Limitations: the items overlap heavily because they largely describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations, and the evidence cannot distinguish one coordinated relocation of the block from an excision after A28 plus a separate insertion of the same activities before A26; positions are event positions, not clock times. [LAG004, LAG005, screening]

Other differences: A26 and A27 each now sit slightly later in the trace than before (mean relative position up about 0.03–0.04), consistent with the inserted block; behaviour around A48 and A50 (handovers to A49 and A51) shows no notable change. [POS005, POS006, DIR008, DIR009]
