### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the segment formed by A31, A22, A20, A23, A24, A26 and A25 is executed as one fixed, consecutive sequence (A31 → A22 → A20 → A23 → A24 → A26 → A25, closing into A32), whereas in the earlier window these same activities — each present in essentially every trace in both windows — were ordered flexibly, with pairs running in both directions, variable gaps and several alternative successions per activity. This is a change in ordering constraints together with routing consolidation, not a swap of two activities, not a change in participation, and not a shift in how often existing paths are taken. [REL002, REL005, REL007, LAG003, LAG004, LAG008, LAG016, DIR003, DIR005, DIR009, POS002]

The change is shown directly by relational and positional evidence: all eight monitored activity pairs flip to a single order in about 98–99 in 100 traces, gap distributions collapse to one value, and successor profiles consolidate at every step; roughly 30 items describe one coherent chain, each item's difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 of the random reference re-splits, and the 56 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.56 expected by chance in an unchanged process, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits produce. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants: the later window may simply be dominated by one variant that already occurred earlier, and the earlier window's mixed orders could themselves be a mixture of a few fixed-order variants rather than within-case flexibility. [REL002, REL004, REL009, LAG005, LAG011, DIR004, DIR006, DIR008]

Other differences: A08 participates in fewer traces (about 32 to about 13 in 100), occurs later, and its outgoing transitions redistributed away from A15 (about 91 to about 48 in 100, with A16, A46 and A32 appearing), while A14 now rarely precedes A08; these rest on only about 27 traces containing A08 in the later window, so the new shares are uncertain, and A08 is not part of the main hypothesis. [POS001, DIR001, DIR002, REL001, LAG001]
