### Observed differences

- The clearest change concerns the activity group A19, A20 and A25. In the earlier window they ran in every one of the 200 traces, always together and in the order A19 → A25 → A20 (A19 before A20 in about 99 in 100 cases). In the later window they still run only together and in that same order, but in only about 53 in 100 traces; in about 46 in 100 traces none of the three appears, while their neighbours A14, A15, A26, A41, A46 and A47 still appear in every trace. [REL010, REL016, REL017, REL022, REL023, REL027]

- A06 also became rare: about 71 in 100 traces before, about 26 in 100 after. Before, it always ran alongside A14 and the group (typically immediately after A14 — gap of 1 in about 98 in 100 observed pairs) and about 92 in 100 of its outgoing transitions led to A15. After, it runs without the group in about 12 in 100 traces, the group runs without it in about 39 in 100, and both are absent in about 35 in 100; when A06 does appear it sits later in the trace (median relative position about 0.16 before vs 0.30 after) and its outgoing transitions scatter over many targets, with A15 down to about 37 in 100. [POS001, REL001, REL003, DIR003, LAG003]

- The flow routes around the skipped activities. Of A14's outgoing transitions, the share to A06 fell from about 69 in 100 to about 12, while the share to A15 rose from about 31 to about 78, and A15 immediately follows A14 in about 41 in 100 observed pairs (about 17 before). From A41, transitions to A19 fell from about 96 in 100 to about 47, while A26 — not observed as an A41 target before — now receives about 39 in 100 and immediately follows A41 in about 40 in 100 observed pairs (essentially never before). A20, when present, still leads to A26 in about 97 in 100 of its transitions in both windows. [DIR005, LAG004, DIR008, LAG008, DIR007]

- Distances in the flow shrink accordingly: A14 reaches A41 mostly five steps later before (about 71 in 100) but mostly four steps after (about 67 in 100), and the ordered pair A15 then A26 within five steps, seen in only 1 of 200 earlier traces, appears in 75 of 200 later traces (the earlier profile rests on a single observation). [LAG005, LAG007]

- Smaller shifts: A02, A04 and A08 show more outgoing transitions than before (roughly 18 to 40, 10 to 30 and 8 to 43) with redistributed targets, though the earlier profiles rest on few observations; A14's position is slightly later and more spread out, A26's slightly earlier, and immediate A46 → A47 succession drops from about 97 to about 88 in 100. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, POS002, POS003, LAG009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the segment formed by A19, A20 and A25 — previously executed together in every case, in the order A19 → A25 → A20 — can be skipped (all three absent in about 46 in 100 later traces), and that A06, previously on the main path between A14 and A15 in about 71 in 100 cases, can be skipped as well (absent in about 74 in 100). When they are skipped, control continues directly: A14 hands over to A15 and A41 to A26, shortening the path between the surrounding activities, whose participation (A14, A15, A26, A41, A46, A47) stays at every trace in both windows. Supporting signs: the participation drops are far beyond sampling variation (the group ran in 200 of 200 earlier traces versus about 106 of 200 later ones, while all neighbours remained at 200 of 200); the group's internal order and its link to A26 are preserved whenever it runs; and the new direct successions appear exactly where the skipped activities used to sit. Screening qualified 52 control-flow items where about 0.66 would be expected without a change, so the ensemble cannot be explained by random re-splits of the traces. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window mixes two case populations — one running the full flow and one a shortened flow — rather than a single flow with a newly optional segment; the evidence also cannot indicate why individual cases skip the segment, and all position and gap statements concern event order, not clock time. [REL010, REL016, REL019, DIR005, DIR008, LAG004, LAG008, POS001]

Other differences: the increased transition counts and redistributed successors of A02, A04 and A08, the small positional shifts of A14 and A26, and the slightly longer A46 → A47 gap are not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, POS002, POS003, LAG009]
