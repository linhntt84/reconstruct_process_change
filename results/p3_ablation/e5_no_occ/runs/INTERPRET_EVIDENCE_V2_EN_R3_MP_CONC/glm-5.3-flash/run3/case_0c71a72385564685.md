### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new choice stage now sits between A41 and A26: in the later window A41 is typically followed by exactly one of the previously unobserved activities A19, A20 or A25, which then leads on to A26, whereas in the earlier window A41 passed directly to A26. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: A41's successor profile flipped from mostly A26 to about 87 in 100 transitions going to the three new activities [DIR004]; A19 and A25 in turn lead mostly to A26 [DIR002, DIR003]; the ordering relations hold on both sides, with A41 before each new activity and each new activity before A26 in about 28–38 of 100 traces [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]; the A41–A26 gap widened by about one event [LAG001]; and A26 remains in every trace, only slightly later [POS004]. Screening qualified 18 control-flow items where about 0.31 would be expected for an unchanged process, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, so sampling noise cannot account for the pattern as a whole. What the evidence cannot settle is whether the three activities form a single exclusive decision point or three independently added optional steps: their pairwise co-occurrences rest on only about 2–3 traces, and their absence earlier shows only that A19, A20 and A25 were not observed in that window, not that they could not occur. [REL001, REL002, POS001, POS002, POS003]
