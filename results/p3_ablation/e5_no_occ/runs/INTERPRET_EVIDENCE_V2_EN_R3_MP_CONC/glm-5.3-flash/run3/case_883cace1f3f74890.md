### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A20 and A25 are not observed in any of the 200 traces. In the later window, A19 appears in about 30 in 100 traces (59 of 200), A20 in about 41 in 100 (82 of 200) and A25 in about 30 in 100 (60 of 200); together they occur in essentially every later trace, and no later trace contains more than one of them (pairwise co-occurrence at most about 1 in 200). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003, REL001, REL002, REL006]
- In the earlier window, the segment A27 → A28 → A29 ran as a fixed consecutive chain: about 99 in 100 of A27's outgoing transitions led to A28, about 99 in 100 of A28's led to A29, and the A27→A28 and A28→A29 event gaps were 1 step in about 99 in 100 cases (A27 to A29: 2 steps in about 98 in 100). [DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- In the later window, both routings fan out: about 44 in 100 of A27's outgoing transitions go to A28, with about 22 to A20, 17 to A25 and 15 to A19; about 55 in 100 of A28's outgoing transitions go to A29, with about 17 to A20, 13 to A19 and 12 to A25. [DIR004, DIR005]
- In the later window, the new activities sit between A27 and A29: when one of them co-occurs with A27 it follows A27 in essentially all such traces (A27 before A20 in about 40 of the 41 in 100 traces containing both), and when one co-occurs with A29 it precedes A29 in essentially all such traces (A19 before A29 in all of its roughly 30 in 100 co-occurrence traces). Against A28 the order is mixed — A19 precedes A28 in about 16 in 100 traces and follows it in about 13 in 100. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- Event gaps widen accordingly: A27 to A29 is now 3 steps apart in about 97 in 100 traces (previously 2 steps); the A27→A28 gap is 2 steps in about 55 in 100 later traces (previously 1 step in about 99 in 100) and the A28→A29 gap is 2 steps in about 44 in 100 (previously 1 step in about 99 in 100) — one event is inserted between A27 and A29 in nearly every later trace, before A28 in roughly 55 in 100 cases and after A28 in roughly 44 in 100. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
- A27's mean relative position shifts slightly earlier (about 0.48 to 0.46) and A29's slightly later (about 0.55 to 0.57), consistent with an added event between them; both activities still occur in every trace in both windows. [POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window a step is inserted into the previously rigid consecutive sequence A27 → A28 → A29: essentially every later case executes exactly one of three mutually exclusive activities — A19, A20 or A25 — placed directly after A27 in roughly 55 in 100 traces and directly after A28 in roughly 44 in 100, after which the flow continues to A29. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL010, REL012]

The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the three activities appear only in the later window at 30–41 in 100 traces and never together; their order is fixed against A27 (after it) and A29 (before it) but mixed against A28, matching insertion at either of two points; the A27→A29 step distance grows from 2 to 3 in about 97 in 100 traces; and the rerouted outgoing shares of A27 and A28 match the two insertion points. Screening tested 67 control-flow units and 25 items qualified, far above the fewer than 1 expected for an unchanged process, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of stable behaviour produce. [DIR001–DIR005, POS001–POS005, REL001–REL012, LAG001–LAG003]

Limitations: absence in the earlier window strictly means "not observed" in that 200-trace sample, so a rare pre-existing variant that rose sharply in frequency cannot be completely ruled out; and the DIR, REL and LAG items all describe one underlying insertion, so they are not independent confirmations of it. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Other differences: each new activity shows one or two rare outgoing edges (about 1–2 in 100 of its transitions, e.g. A19 to A07 and A20 to A26) with no earlier counterpart, and the spread of A27's and A29's positions increases slightly. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS004, POS005]
