- The routing out of A36 changed sharply. In the earlier window, A36's observed outgoing transitions split about evenly: to A21 in about 53 in 100 cases and to A14 in about 47 in 100. In the later window, about 99.5 in 100 go to A14 and only about 1 in 200 to A21. [DIR001]

The chain A36 → A14 → A20 → A15 → A21 holds in the same internal order in both windows (each ordered pair at about 47 in 100 before, about 99.5 in 100 after), so only how often this path runs changed, not its sequence. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the continuation after A36 was rerouted: the variant in which A21 immediately follows A36 — running without A14, A20, A15 and A37 — was taken in about 53 in 100 cases before and is now observed in only about 1 in 200 cases, while the full path A36 → A14 → A20 → A15 → A21, with A37 running alongside A14 without a fixed order, is now taken in about 99.5 in 100 cases, up from about 47 in 100. In participation terms, A14, A15, A20 and A37 moved from optional (about 47% of traces) to near-universal execution (about 99.5%), while the internal order of the path stayed the same. [DIR001, LAG001, REL001–REL015]

Supporting signs: the rerouting is visible directly in A36's outgoing transitions and in the A36→A21 step distance, not only in aggregate frequencies [DIR001, LAG001]; all fifteen activity-pair profiles shift consistently in the same direction, each backed by about 94–200 traces [REL001–REL015]; and screening qualified 19 control-flow items where about 0.27 would be expected if nothing had changed, so the set of differences is far beyond what random re-splits of stable behaviour produce [DIR001, POS001, POS002, REL001–REL015, LAG001]. The many pair items describe one trace-level behaviour, so they demonstrate consistency rather than serving as independent confirmations.

Limitations: the direct A36→A21 route still appears about once in the later window, so the evidence cannot distinguish whether it was removed entirely or survives as a rare alternative; the evidence also cannot indicate what produced the rerouting. [DIR001, LAG001]

Other differences: A40's own frequency stayed roughly stable (about 60 in 100 traces in both windows) but it no longer occurs without A14 — before it also ran on the direct variant in about a third of traces, now it appears only alongside the block, usually after A14 [REL006, REL011, REL015]. Small shifts in relative position of A36 (mean about 0.33 → 0.30) and A21 (about 0.41 → 0.43) accompany the rerouting but are minor on their own [POS001, POS002].
