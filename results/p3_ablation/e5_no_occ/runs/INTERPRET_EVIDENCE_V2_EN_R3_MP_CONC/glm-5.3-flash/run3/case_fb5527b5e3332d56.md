### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A21 appeared in every trace and its outgoing flow split about evenly: roughly 52% went to A22, with the rest going to A28, A33 or A27 (about 14–18% each). In the later window, about 99.5% of A21's outgoing flow went to A22, with A28 the only other observed successor (about 0.5%). [DIR001, REL001]
- A27, A28 and A33 formed an all-or-nothing group in the earlier window: each was present in about 97 of 200 traces (about 48.5%), whenever one appeared all three did, and each pair occurred in both orders at similar shares (about 23–26% each way). In the later window, each of the three appeared in only 1 of 200 traces (about 0.5%). [REL012, REL013, REL014, POS002, POS003, POS004]
- Earlier, this group sat between A21 and A23: each of A27, A28 and A33 preceded A23 in about 48.5% of traces and could be followed by either of the other two or by A23 (shares roughly 28–42%), while A22 and any of the three were strict alternatives that never co-occurred. In the later window, A23 occurred without any of the three in about 99.5% of traces, and A22 preceded A23 in about 99.5%. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL011]
- The A21→A23 distance collapsed from an even mix of 2 and 4 event positions (about 52% vs 48%) to 2 positions in about 99.5% of traces, matching the direct two-step path. [LAG001]
- A23 was present in all traces in both windows; its relative position moved slightly earlier (mean about 0.55 to about 0.53), consistent with the missing three-activity block. This is an event-position shift, not a clock-time change. [POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the branch formed by A27, A28 and A33 between A21 and A23 was skipped in nearly all cases. Earlier, the process ran in two forms of similar size: about 52 in 100 cases went directly A21 → A22 → A23, while about 48 in 100 inserted A27, A28 and A33 — always together, in varying order — between A21 and A23. In the later window, about 199 in 200 cases take the direct path, A21 hands over to A22 in about 99.5% of its outgoing flow, and A27, A28 and A33 each appear in only about 1 in 200 traces. The signs are direct and mutually consistent — participation of the three activities fell from about 48.5% to about 0.5% of traces, their co-occurrence with each other and with A23 collapsed, and the A21→A23 distance shrank accordingly — while screening produced 30 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.29 expected for an unchanged process, far beyond what random re-splits explain; the items describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [DIR001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL005, REL009, REL012, LAG001]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out: the branch may still exist and merely be selected very rarely — it fired once in the later window — rather than having been removed or bypassed altogether; moreover, the ordering among the three activities in the later window rests on that single trace. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Other differences: none beyond the above; the slightly earlier relative position of A23 (mean about 0.55 to about 0.53) is consistent with the shorter direct path and is a position shift, not evidence of a clock-time move. [POS001]
