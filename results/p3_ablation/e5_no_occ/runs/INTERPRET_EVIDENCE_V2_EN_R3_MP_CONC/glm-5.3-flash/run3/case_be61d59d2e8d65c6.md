### Observed differences

- Three activities that were frequent before are nearly unobserved in the later window: A19 appeared in about 33 in 100 traces before versus about 1 in 200 after, A20 in about 29 in 100 versus about 1 in 200, and A25 in about 40 in 100 versus about 1 in 200. [POS001, POS002, POS003, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- Before, these three were alternatives to one another (any two of them co-occurred in only about 0.5 in 100 traces) and acted as intermediate steps: A27 sent about 44 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to one of them (A25 about 20, A19 about 13, A20 about 10), and A28 sent about 52 in 100 (A25 about 19, A19 about 18, A20 about 16). [REL001, REL002, REL006, DIR005, DIR006]
- In the later window this intermediate layer is gone: A27 leads to A28 in about 98 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and A28 leads to A29 in about 97 in 100, while transitions from A27 or A28 to A19, A20 or A25 are no longer observed. [DIR005, DIR006, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- The surviving steps became immediate neighbours: A27 followed by A28 within one step rose from about 52 to about 99 in 100, A28 followed by A29 within one step rose from about 43 to about 96 in 100, and the typical distance from A27 to A29 shortened from three steps (about 90 in 100) to two steps (about 96 in 100); A28 followed by A25, previously immediate in all 38 observed cases, appears only once afterwards, five steps later. [LAG001, LAG004, LAG002, LAG003]
- Co-occurrence with the surviving activities collapsed accordingly: before, each of A19, A20 and A25 occurred only alongside A27, A28 and A29 (co-occurrence about 28–40 in 100 traces, essentially always after A27 and before A29); in the later window these co-occurrences fall to about 0.5 in 100. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- Separately, the outgoing transitions of A10 were redistributed: A15 fell from about 20 in 100 to about 6, A47 from about 17 to about 6, A21 and A30 disappeared, and new targets appeared (A26 about 17 in 100, A41 about 9); traces containing A10 rose from about 18 to about 27 in 100. [DIR001]
- Minor: A27's mean event position moved slightly later (about 0.46 to 0.48 of the trace) and A29's slightly earlier (about 0.57 to 0.55), consistent with the shortened traces. [POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the paths through A19, A20 and A25 were dropped from execution: these three activities, previously executed in roughly 30–40 in 100 cases as alternative intermediate steps between the A27/A28 stage and the A28/A29 stage, are nearly unobserved in the later window (about 1 in 200 traces each), and the flow they carried now runs directly from A27 to A28 and from A28 to A29, shortening that segment by about one step. Because the three activities are (nearly) absent afterwards, this indicates removed or bypassed activities rather than a re-weighting among surviving alternatives. [POS001, POS002, POS003, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004, REL003, REL005, REL012]

The screening produced 27 qualifying items against roughly 0.35 expected if nothing had changed, so chance cannot account for the pattern as a whole; the participation collapse is observed directly in both windows, and the rerouting at A27 and A28 plus the tightened adjacency of A27–A28 and A28–A29 consistently match it. Limitations: each of the three activities still occurs in about 1 in 200 later traces, so they became extremely rare rather than strictly absent, and their single later occurrences have successors (A38, A37, A31) not seen before — with one trace each this is too weak to interpret. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the whole A27–A28–A29 segment was rebuilt in a way that coincidentally removed these activities or replaced their role with activities not identifiable here. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG003, DIR001–DIR006, POS001–POS005, REL001–REL012, LAG001–LAG004]

Other differences: the redistribution of A10's outgoing transitions (former targets A15, A47, A21 and A30 lost most or all of their share; new targets A26 and A41 appeared; A10 occurs in more traces, about 18 to 27 in 100) is a distinct structural change not explained by the drop-out above. [DIR001]
