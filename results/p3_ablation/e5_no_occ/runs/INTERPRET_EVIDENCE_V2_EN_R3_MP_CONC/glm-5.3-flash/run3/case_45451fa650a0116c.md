### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the segment following A20 runs along different existing paths: A20 now typically continues into A21, A22 and A23 — executed together in no fixed order and co-occurring with A30, A34 and A35 — where earlier it typically continued into A24, A25 and A26; and on the same segment A34 now typically leads directly to A35 instead of via A06, leaving A06 in only about 12 in 100 traces, down from about 46. [DIR005, DIR006, REL011, REL014, REL019, REL023, POS001]

The shift is visible directly in the routing shares out of A20 and A34, in the co-occurrence and order patterns of both trios, and in the participation of A06 and A26; every involved activity and branch is observed in a substantial share of traces in both windows, and only the shares between existing alternatives moved. The screening found 64 qualifying control-flow items against roughly 0.74 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by chance sampling. [DIR005, DIR006, REL017, REL019, REL041, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: the many relational items give overlapping views of one trace-level switch rather than independent confirmations, and the evidence shows participation and ordering, not the reason for the change. A competing reading the evidence cannot fully exclude is that A06 was repositioned rather than skipped — its median relative position moved from about 0.95 to about 0.45 — although the near-disappearance of the A34→A06 edge and A06's overall decline favour the skip reading, and the position statistics rest on about 24 later traces.
