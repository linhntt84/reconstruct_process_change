### Observed differences

- Before, every trace contained the fixed sequence A41 → A19 → A25 → A20 → A26: the three middle activities each appeared in 200 of 200 traces, always together, in that same order in about 98–100 in 100 traces. In the later window, A19, A25 and A20 each appear in only about 53 in 100 traces (about 106–108 of 200), virtually always together and in the same order, while in the remaining roughly 47 in 100 traces none of the three is executed at all; A41, A26, A46 and A47 still appear in every trace. [REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL014, REL015, REL017, DIR003, DIR004]

- The skip is visible directly in A41's routing: before, about 96 in 100 of A41's outgoing transitions led to A19, and A26 was never observed as a successor; in the later window about 50 in 100 lead to A19 and about 44 in 100 lead straight to A26. The distance from A41 to A26, previously almost always 4 steps (93 in 100 observed pairs, with the group in between), is now 1 step in about 44 in 100 pairs and 4 steps in about 49 in 100. [DIR005, LAG002]

- A02 changed sharply in participation and placement: it appeared in about 29 in 100 traces before (58 of 200), near the end of the trace (median relative position about 0.97, usually after A26 and after A46); in the later window it appears in only about 8–9 in 100 traces (17 of 200), around the middle (median about 0.47), now usually before A46 and with no fixed order versus A26. [POS001, REL004, REL006]

- The end-of-process route through A02 largely disappeared: A46 → A02 fell from about 25 in 100 of A46's outgoing transitions to under 1 in 100, while A46 → A47 rose from about 75 to about 96 in 100 and the two became almost always immediately consecutive (distance 1 in about 97 in 100 pairs, versus 75 in 100 before). A02's own outgoing transitions, previously about 85 in 100 towards A47, are now spread over many activities (largest share about 18 in 100 to A45, A47 only about 6 in 100; based on 17 outgoing transitions). [DIR006, LAG003, DIR001]

- Smaller changes: A08 remains rare (4 traces before, 15 after) with a completely different successor profile, and A20 and A25 gained a few small new outgoing edges (each a few percent of their transitions). [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the activity group A19, A25 and A20 — previously executed in every trace between A41 and A26 — became optional: in the later window the group runs in about 53 in 100 traces and is bypassed in the remaining about 47 in 100, with A41 then leading directly to A26. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the three activities always occur together, so their presence fell jointly from 100% to about 53%; a direct A41→A26 transition appeared that was not observed before; the A41→A26 step distance now splits between 1 and 4 steps in almost exactly the skip/run proportions; and whenever the group runs, its order relative to A41 and A26 is unchanged, which rules out a reordering or swap of activities. The volume of qualifying evidence (35 items against roughly 0.5 expected for an unchanged process) also makes sampling variation implausible as an account of the evidence as a whole. What remains open is whether the bypass is one process with a newly optional group or a mixture of two trace populations, one with and one without the group, and whether skipping depends on case characteristics; the evidence cannot distinguish these. [REL008, REL009, REL011, REL014, REL015, DIR005, LAG002]

Other differences: A02 and the tail of the process. A02 appeared in about 29 in 100 traces before, near the end and usually after A46, with about 85 in 100 of its outgoing transitions going to A47; in the later window it appears in about 8–9 in 100 traces, sits around the middle of the trace, and its outgoing transitions are spread over many successors (A47 only about 6 in 100). A46 now routes almost exclusively to A47 (about 96 in 100, up from 75), and the A46→A47 pair became almost always immediately consecutive. A08's successor profile also changed, but that rests on only 4 before and 15 after occurrences. [POS001, REL004, REL006, DIR001, DIR006, LAG003, DIR002]
