### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a flip in which of two alternative activity groups follows A20. In the earlier window, about 75 in 100 traces went from A20 into the group A21/A22/A23 (about 74% of A20's observed outgoing transitions) and about 25 in 100 into A24/A25/A26; in the later window this reversed, with about 25 in 100 taking the A21-group and about 75 in 100 the A24-group. [DIR003, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]

- The two groups still behave as mutually exclusive alternatives: members of different groups practically never share a trace in either window (A21 and A24 co-occur in about 0–1 in 200 traces before and after), while members of the same group still run together whenever the group runs, in both orders and without a fixed internal order. Participation moved with the swap: A25 appeared in about 25 in 100 traces before and about 76 in 100 after, at a similar relative position (median about 0.71 vs 0.67 of the trace length). [REL015, REL022, REL028, REL013, REL034, REL039, POS002]

- Downstream activities tracked the swap: A30 co-occurred with the A21-group in about 75 in 100 traces before but with the A24-group in about 76 in 100 after, and A32/A33 moved from co-occurring with the A21-group in about 48 in 100 traces to co-occurring with the A24-group in about 52 in 100. Step gaps on the surviving path also lengthened somewhat: A20→A24 at one step fell from about 54% to about 33% of cases (three steps rose from about 20% to about 39%), with similar shifts for A25→A30 and A32→A33. [REL018, REL036, REL019, REL037, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

- Separately, A10 became more frequent and later-placed: about 5 in 100 traces before vs about 21 in 100 after, with median relative position about 0.60 vs 0.86; its observed outgoing transitions concentrated on A33 (about 90% after vs 18% before, on 42 vs 11 observed transitions, when A35 at 27% and A14 at 18% also followed), and A32 now leads to A10 in about 20% of A32's observed outgoing transitions vs about 1% before. [POS001, DIR002, DIR004]

- A02 was observed far less often in the later window (2 observed outgoing transitions vs 18); its apparent concentration on successors A15 and A27 rests on those two observations alone. [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the shares of two existing alternative paths out of A20 exchanged: the path running A21, A22 and A23 together fell from about 75 in 100 traces to about 25 in 100, while the alternative path running A24, A25 and A26 together rose from about 25 in 100 to about 75 in 100, with A20 still preceding whichever group runs. [DIR003, REL007, REL010, POS002]

The change is shown directly and consistently: A20's successor distribution flips, its co-occurrence with each of the six group members moves by about 50 percentage points in the matching direction with support from all 200 traces per window, exclusivity between the groups and A20's precedence are preserved, and the relations of downstream A30, A32 and A33 shift in line with the same swap. Chance is also unlikely as a whole explanation: 54 control-flow items qualified where about 0.74 would be expected if nothing had changed. [REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL018, REL036]

Because both paths are well observed in both windows and only the shares between them move, this qualifies as a shift in how often existing paths are taken rather than a replacement of one path by another. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a changed mix of case types between the windows, which would produce the same aggregate flip; the many relation items also largely mirror one underlying swap rather than serving as independent confirmations. [REL015, REL022, REL028]

Other differences: A10 occurs in roughly four times as many traces, sits later within them, and is now almost always followed by A33 and sometimes preceded by A32; A02 was observed far less often after, though on very few observations. These are not part of the main hypothesis. [POS001, DIR002, DIR004, DIR001]
