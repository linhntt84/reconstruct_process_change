### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, three activities acted as strict alternatives: A19 ran in about 36 of 100 traces (71 of 200), A20 in about 31 (61 of 200) and A25 in about 35 (70–72 of 200), and they practically never occurred together, so almost every trace contained exactly one of the three. In the later window, A19 and A25 were not observed at all and A20 appeared in a single trace (about 0–1 in 200). [POS002, POS003, POS004, REL007, REL009, REL014]
- Before, each of the three alternatives led almost invariably to A26: about 97–99 of 100 outgoing transitions of A19, A20 and A25 went to A26. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- A41's routing changed completely: before, about 93 of 100 of its outgoing transitions went to one of the three alternatives (roughly 33 to A19, 33 to A25, 27 to A20); in the later window, about 92 of 100 go directly to A26. A41 itself still occurs in essentially every trace in both windows. [DIR004]
- The observed distance from A41 to A26 shortened accordingly: before, A26 followed A41 two steps later in about 94 of 100 observations; in the later window it follows one step later in about 93 of 100. [LAG002]
- The surrounding activities remain in place — A14, A26, A31 and A41 still appear in every trace — but every co-occurrence of these partners with A19, A20 or A25 fell from roughly 30–36 of 100 traces to zero. [REL004, REL010, REL011, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021]
- Smaller shifts: A26's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (about 0.39 to 0.37), A12 now appears in about 51 of 100 traces (about 42 before), A24 in about 55 (about 49 before), and the pair A24 then A31 is now observed one step apart in about 59 of 100 cases versus about 39 before (two steps was the earlier mode). [POS005, REL001, REL008, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the three-way alternative block A19/A20/A25, which previously sat between A41 and A26 with exactly one of the three executed in nearly every trace, is no longer executed, and that A41 now leads directly to A26, so that this group of activities is bypassed. Before, A41 passed control to one of A19, A20 or A25 in about 93 of 100 of its outgoing transitions and each of them led on to A26; in the later window, none of the three is observed, A41 connects straight to A26 in about 92 of 100 of its transitions, and the A41–A26 distance dropped from two steps to one. A26's slightly earlier position (about 0.39 to 0.37) is consistent with one step having disappeared ahead of it. [DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG002, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

The screening context argues against a sampling artefact: 33 evidence items qualified where roughly 0.42 would be expected if nothing had changed, and the participation, successor and distance indicators all describe the same structural difference rather than isolated fluctuations. [DIR004, LAG002]

Limitation: zero participation only shows the activities were not observed in the later window, so the evidence cannot distinguish a fully removed block from a branch that still exists but is never taken, nor rule out that comparable choice behaviour now happens in activities outside the tested units. [POS002, POS003, POS004]

Other differences: A12 now appears in about 51 of 100 traces versus about 42 before, A24 in about 55 versus about 49, and the observed A24→A31 distance is now one step in about 59 of 100 observations versus about 39 before; these shifts are not explained by the bypass hypothesis. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL008, REL013, REL018, LAG001]
