### Observed differences

- **Routing out of A19, A20 and A25 flipped.** Before, each of the three led almost exclusively to A26 — about 92 in 100 of A19's observed outgoing transitions, 94 in 100 of A20's and 99 in 100 of A25's. In the later window those edges have nearly vanished (about 0–2 in 100), and the three instead lead mainly to A32 (about 45–56 in 100) and to A28 (about 39–48 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- **Routing into the group flipped as well.** Before, A45 fed the trio (about 35 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A25, 35 to A19, 24 to A20), while A27 led almost only to A28 (about 98 in 100) and A28 almost only to A32 (about 99 in 100). In the later window A45 leads to A26 in about 94 in 100, A27 leads to A19/A20/A25 in about 43 in 100 combined, and A28 leads to them in about 53 in 100 (with A32 dropping to about 44 in 100). [DIR006, DIR004, DIR005]
- **The three activities sit later in the trace.** A19, A20 and A25 occurred at about one third of the trace length before (mean relative position about 0.33) and at about 0.59–0.61 in the later window; A26 (about 0.35 → 0.33) and A27 (about 0.58 → 0.56) barely moved. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- **The order between the trio and A26/A27 reversed.** Before, the trio member came first in about 24–38 in 100 traces (reverse order about 0 in 100); in the later window A26/A27 come first in about 28–37 in 100 (trio first about 0–1 in 100). Relative to A28, the later window shows both orders in similar shares (about 13–21 in 100 each), i.e. no fixed order. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL005, REL008]
- **Adjacency shifted accordingly.** Before, A45 reached A26 two steps later and A19 immediately preceded A26 (about 93–94 in 100 of observed pairs); in the later window A45 reaches A26 one step later (about 94 in 100), and A27 reaches A32 three steps later (about 93 in 100) instead of two (about 97 in 100). [LAG011, LAG010, LAG001, LAG006]
- Participation changed only modestly: A20 appears in 25 in 100 traces before vs 38 in 100 after; A19 in 38 vs 33; A25 in 39 vs 30; A26 and A27 appear in essentially all traces in both windows. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the group of three activities A19, A20 and A25 changed its placement: in the earlier window it ran between A45 and A26 (A45 led to the trio, the trio led almost only to A26, and A26 was reached two steps after A45), while in the later window A45 leads directly to A26 and the trio is executed after A27/A28, typically leading on to A32, at about 60 in 100 of the trace length instead of about a third. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, LAG001, LAG010, LAG011]

The change is shown directly and consistently: the relative order reverses for all six trio–A26/A27 pairs with full-trace support (200 traces in each window); the successor rewiring matches it (A27 and A28 now feed the trio, and the trio leads on to A32 while A28→A32 lost about half of its share); A26 and A27 kept their positions, so the shift is confined to the trio; and the A27→A32 distance grew from two to three steps, as expected if the trio now sits between A28 and A32. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, DIR004, DIR005, LAG002, LAG006, LAG009, POS004, POS005]

Screening makes chance an unlikely explanation for the ensemble: 30 of 81 tested units qualified, against about 0.4 items expected if nothing had changed. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out concerns the trio's exact relation to A28 in the later window: both orders occur in similar shares there, and several adjacency items rest on only 1–2 observations on one side (A28→A19, A28→A20, A27→A20, A19→A26, A45→A25), so an interleaved or partly parallel execution of the trio around A28 cannot be distinguished from a strictly sequential placement. [REL005, REL008, LAG007, LAG008, LAG004, LAG001, LAG010]

Other differences: participation shifted slightly (A20 up from 25 to 38 in 100 traces, A19 down from 38 to 33, A25 down from 39 to 30), which may belong to the same restructuring but is small next to the ordering change. [POS001, POS002, POS003]
