### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block A36–A37–A42, executed in no fixed internal order, previously formed an optional branch — run in about 46 in 100 cases after A26 and before A31, instead of A27 — and that in the later window this block runs in nearly every case directly after A25 and before A28, while A27, previously the alternative to that branch, now follows A26 in essentially every case; both the trio and A27 thus changed from optional, mutually exclusive alternatives into near-universal steps at new positions in the flow. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, POS003, POS006, POS007, POS008]

The ordering change is shown directly by relational and successor evidence: A26 preceding the trio became the trio preceding A26 (about 46 vs about 99 in 100 traces), A28 preceding the trio became the trio preceding A28, and the trio's outgoing transitions moved from A31 to A28. [REL006, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

The participation and adjacency evidence agrees: A27 and each trio member went from practically never sharing a trace to co-occurring in about 995 in 1000 traces, and the A25→A28 distance grew from one to four steps, consistent with the trio now lying between them; screening produced 41 qualifying control-flow items where about 0.5 were expected without a change, so sampling variation cannot account for the ensemble.
