### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A39 and A24 was restructured: the block A21–A22–A23, which every case previously executed between A39 and A24 in varying order, was replaced by a single per-case choice among A14, A15 and A16, so a typical case now runs A39 → one of A14/A15/A16 → A24 instead of A39 → A21, A22 and A23 together → A24. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the successor shift at A39, the participation flip (the new activities from 0 to 30–37 in 100 cases, the old block from 100 to about 0.5 in 100), the exclusive occurrence of the new activities, their stable placement between A39 and A24, and the A39–A24 distance shrinking from four to two steps. With 65 qualifying items against roughly 0.48 expected if nothing had changed, sampling fluctuation cannot explain the pattern as a whole. The evidence cannot show whether A14/A15/A16 are genuinely new activities or relabelled counterparts of A21/A22/A23, nor whether the single later trace still containing A21/A22/A23 is a residual old path or incidental noise.
