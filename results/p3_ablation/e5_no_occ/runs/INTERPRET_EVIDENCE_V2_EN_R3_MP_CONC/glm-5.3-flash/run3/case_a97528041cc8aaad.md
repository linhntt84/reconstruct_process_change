### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reorganisation of control flow with two visible parts. First, A02 now typically runs as an early step between A14 and A15: A14 hands over to A02 in about 60 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (previously about 1 in 100), A02 hands over to A15 in about 87 in 100 (previously about 9 in 100), A02's participation rose from about 15 to about 65 in 100 traces, and its position moved towards the start. Second, the ordering of the block involving A19, A20, A21, A22, A23, A24, A25 and A29 was turned around: the sub-group A19/A21/A24, which earlier ran after A20/A22/A23/A25/A29 and led on to A30, now runs directly after A40, leads to A25, and precedes A20/A22/A23/A29, which in turn now lead to A30. The signs are direct and consistent — relational, positional, lag and successor evidence all point the same way — and the screening produced 84 qualifying items where about 0.77 would be expected without a change, so chance cannot explain the pattern as a whole. Limitations: the many relation items describe the same traces rather than independent confirmations, and pairwise orders plus successor shares cannot fully distinguish a re-sequencing of one path from a changed mix of variants that order these activities differently; lag items resting on only 1–3 observations were not relied upon.

Other differences: The outgoing profiles of A06 and A08 were reshuffled, but the later profiles rest on only 10 and 24 transitions, so these may be sampling noise [DIR002, DIR003]. A43→A44 and A45→A46 were essentially unchanged [DIR013, DIR014, LAG026].
