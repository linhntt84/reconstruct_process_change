### Observed differences

- The relative order of two groups of activities reversed. In the earlier window, A35, A36 and A41 each ran before A26 and before A27 in about 97–99% of traces (each pair co-occurs in every trace in both windows); in the later window, each of them runs after A26 and A27 in about 97–98% of traces. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015]

Earlier, A25 led into the trio (about 38% of its outgoing transitions to A35, 31% to A36, 27% to A41) and A27 followed about 4–5 event positions later, while A26 led straight to A30 (about 99%, next event). In the later window, A25 leads directly to A27 (about 96%, next event in about 97% of cases), A26 leads into the trio (about 35% A35, 32% A36, 31% A41), and A30 now follows about 4 positions after A26 (about 91%). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the mid-to-late sequence as a possibility: the group A35/A36/A41, which in the earlier window was executed between A25 and A27 (A25 → trio → A27, with A26 → A30 immediately after A26), appears in the later window between A26 and A30 (A25 → A27, then A26 → trio → A30). This is shown directly and consistently: the order of the trio versus A26 and A27 reversed in about 97–99% of traces while all activities still co-occur in every trace [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015]; the successors of A25, A26, A35, A36 and A41 changed exactly as such a move implies [DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009]; the A25→A27 and A26→A30 distances swapped from about 4–5 and 1 positions to about 1 and 4 [LAG002, LAG005]; and the position shifts agree [POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]. Overall, 42 items qualified where roughly 0.61 would be expected for an unchanged process, so the ensemble is far beyond what random re-splitting produces. [DIR001–DIR011, POS001–POS007, REL001–REL015, LAG001–LAG009]

Limitations: the positional indicators use event positions, not clock time, and the evidence shows the relative order reversed without distinguishing whether the trio moved later or A26/A27 moved earlier; a few LAG items pairing A25 or A26 with individual trio members rest on only 1–4 observations and were not relied upon. [LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

Other differences: A02, previously a late activity (mean relative position about 0.93) present in about 3 of 4 traces and usually running between A49 and A50, is in the later window present in only about 1 in 5 traces while A49 leads straight to A50 — consistent with A02 becoming skippable, though the evidence cannot show what its remaining, more varied occurrences now follow from, and its outgoing profile rests on 46 transitions. A47's weakened edge to A48 is a further, smaller difference not part of this hypothesis. [POS001, DIR001, DIR011, LAG009, REL001–REL009, DIR010]
