### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window the segment A14 → A20 → A15 became optional and is bypassed in roughly 47 in 100 traces, with A36 then leading directly to A21, while in the remaining ~53 in 100 traces the full sequence A36 → A14 → A20 → A15 → A21 still runs. The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: a new direct transition A36 → A21 accounts for about 47 in 100 of A36's outgoing transitions [DIR001]; A14, A15 and A20 are jointly absent in about 47 in 100 traces while A36 and A21 remain universal [REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]; A21 sits immediately after A36 in about 47 in 100 traces [LAG001]; and the small forward shift in A21's position fits the same behaviour [POS001]. All twelve items describe one underlying 47/53 split backed by 200 traces per window, and a yield of 12 items against about 0.21 expected by chance cannot be explained by sampling. Note this is a bypass in which activities became skippable, not a re-weighting between paths that are always present.

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the later window mixes traces from two coexisting process variants (for example a partial migration) rather than a single process with a new optional skip, and it cannot show whether the skipped work reappears under activity names outside the tested units.
