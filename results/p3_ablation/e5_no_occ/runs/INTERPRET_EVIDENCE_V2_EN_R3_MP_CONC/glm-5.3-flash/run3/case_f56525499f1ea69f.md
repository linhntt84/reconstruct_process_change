### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, six activities formed two mutually exclusive sets: A33, A35 and A36 each appeared in about 49 in 100 cases, and A37, A38 and A39 each in about 52 in 100, with the two sets never sharing a case (cross-set co-occurrence 0 out of 200 cases). In the later window, each of the six appears in about 99 in 100 cases. [POS006, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010, POS011, REL039, REL043, REL046]
- The two sets changed from alternatives to a fixed sequence: in the later window A33/A35/A36 co-occur with A37/A38/A39 in about 98 in 100 cases, with the first set before the second in about 97 in 100; within each set, both orders still appear in roughly equal shares. In the earlier window, such co-occurrence was never observed. [REL039, REL041, REL044, REL046, REL048, REL037, REL049]
- The block gained a stable bracket: A30 now co-occurs with A33–A39 in about 99 in 100 cases and precedes them in about 98 in 100 (co-occurrence was about 49 in 100 before); A31 now co-occurs in about 99 in 100 and follows the block in about 98 in 100 (about 49 in 100 before). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two activity groups that were alternatives in the earlier window — {A33, A35, A36} and {A37, A38, A39}, with one set per case — are now executed together in nearly every case as a fixed sequence, A33/A35/A36 followed by A37/A38/A39, inserted between A30, which now precedes the block, and A31, which now follows it. [REL039, REL046, REL025, REL031, DIR006, DIR007, LAG003, LAG008]

The change is shown directly by relational evidence (cross-set co-occurrence rose from 0 to about 98 in 100 cases with a stable order between the sets), it is consistent across participation, routing and gap indicators, and its scale exceeds what sampling alone produces: 80 control-flow items qualified where about 1 (0.81) would be expected without a change, and every item's difference exceeded those of about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits. [REL039, REL041, POS006, POS009, DIR006, LAG006]

Limitations: the items describe overlapping traces and are not independent confirmations; ordering rests on event positions rather than clock time; and the evidence cannot distinguish a single longer pass through the block from repeated passes, since interleaved/repeated relations between A15/A16 and the block now appear in about 40 in 100 cases. [REL013, REL019]

Other differences: A04 became less involved — present in about 14 in 100 later cases versus about 31 before, with its transitions to A16 falling from about 81 to about 36 in 100 and the block now usually running without it; minor routing shifts at A02 and A08 rest on few observed transitions (13 versus 23, and 9 versus 28) and are not part of the main hypothesis. [POS001, DIR002, REL001, DIR001, DIR003]
