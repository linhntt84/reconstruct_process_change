### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the sequence A19 → A25 → A20, which A41 could previously bypass by transitioning directly to A26, became a mandatory part of every trace: in the earlier window roughly half of the traces skipped A19, A25 and A20 via the direct A41 → A26 route, whereas in the later window virtually every trace executes A19, then A25, then A20 between A41 and A26, and the direct route survives in only about 1 in 200 traces. The support is direct and consistent: the block's co-occurrence rises from about 47 to about 995 in 1000 traces with its internal order preserved, A41's dominant successor switches from A26 to A19, and the A41–A26 distance moves from one step to four; the 20 qualifying items against roughly 0.42 expected without change is far beyond what an unchanged process produces. The items largely re-describe one underlying trace behaviour rather than confirming it independently, and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply draw different case mixes rather than reflecting a modified process.
