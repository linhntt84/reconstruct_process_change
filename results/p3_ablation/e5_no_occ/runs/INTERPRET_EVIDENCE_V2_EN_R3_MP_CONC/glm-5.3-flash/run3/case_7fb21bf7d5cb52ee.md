### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, A10 became a standard early step running between A14 and A15 — typically A14, then A10 immediately, then A15 — whereas earlier A14 usually passed straight to A15 and A10 was an occasional, late activity with unrelated successors. Supporting signs: A10's presence rose from about 19 to about 70 in 100 traces and its position moved from mid-case to early; A14's outgoing traffic shifted from A15 (about 86 in 100) to A10 (about 66 in 100); A10 now leads to A15 in about 85 in 100 of its transitions; the pairs became adjacent in most cases; and A10's co-occurrence with its neighbours rose from about 19 to about 70 in 100. The control-flow screening flagged 74 items where roughly 0.81 would be expected without a change, so this pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact of the two windows. [POS001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, REL001, REL002]

Limitations: A10 already occurred in about 19 in 100 earlier traces with different successors, so the evidence cannot distinguish a newly added step from a redefinition of A10's existing role, and the direct A14→A15 transition persists in about a third of later cases; an important competing explanation is therefore that A10 was relocated from its earlier late position rather than added as a new step. [DIR002, DIR003]

Other differences: The segment A20–A26, entered from A31 and exiting towards A32, changed from a fixed sequence into a partly reversed, loosely ordered one — A24, A25 and A26 now often run before A20, A22 and A23, the gaps between them spread over one to five positions, A31 enters directly at A24 in about 46 in 100 cases, and A23 exits directly to A32 in about 47 in 100; this is a change in ordering constraints and entry/exit routing, not a swap of two activities. The tail A45→A46 and A47→A48 became near-deterministic, and A08 appeared in far fewer later traces, though on few observations. [REL015, REL020, REL022, DIR006, DIR010, LAG016, DIR011, DIR012, DIR001]
