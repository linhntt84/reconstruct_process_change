### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that resource allocation changed for activities A01, A03, A17 and A21: in the later window, assignments moved away from a broad, diffuse set of occasional resources — which previously handled roughly half of each activity's workload — toward a smaller, consistent group including User_48, User_21 and User_3, while a stable set of previously involved resources withdrew from all four activities.
