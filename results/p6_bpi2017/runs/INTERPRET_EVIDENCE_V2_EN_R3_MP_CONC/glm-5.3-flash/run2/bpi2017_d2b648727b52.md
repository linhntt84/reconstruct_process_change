### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that resource allocation changed for activities A01, A03, A13, A14, A17 and A22: work on these activities moved from a group of resources led by User_49 (about 11–16 in 100 assignments per activity beforehand) to a largely different group that includes User_18 and User_39 (about 7–12 in 100 afterwards), with the earlier group's other regulars fading and the pooled minor resources' share shrinking by about 5–8 in 100. Supporting signs: the indicator measures assignment directly, all six activities show the same redistribution, each difference exceeds essentially all random re-splits, and six resource items qualified where about 0.005 were expected without a change — while control flow and timing produced no qualifying items, consistent with an allocation change on top of an otherwise stable process.
