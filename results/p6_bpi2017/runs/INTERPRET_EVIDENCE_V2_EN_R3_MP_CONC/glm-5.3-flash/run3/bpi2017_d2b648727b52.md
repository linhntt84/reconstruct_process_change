Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a change in resource allocation: on six activities (A01, A03, A13, A14, A17, A22), assignment work shifted from a group of resources that previously dominated — led by User_49 with roughly 11–16 in 100 assignments each — to a different, slightly broader group in which User_18 and User_39 now take roughly 7–12 in 100 each. The hypothesis is direct (the assignment indicator measures exactly this allocation), consistent (the same resources gain and lose on every affected activity, and all six differences exceed all but about 1 of 2000 random re-splits), and the resource family's 6 items against about 0.005 expected by chance cannot be explained by sampling alone. No control-flow or timing items qualified, so the reallocation is not accompanied by any detected structural or timing change.
