### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that resource allocation changed: for activities A01, A03, A17 and A21, work was reassigned from one group of resources to another. Before, a set of resources each holding roughly 4–8% of assignments (with about half of all assignments in a long tail) did the work; in the later window a largely disjoint set, including User_21, User_3 and User_51, took the largest shares, the earlier holders dropped towards zero, and the tail shrank to roughly a third to a half of assignments.
