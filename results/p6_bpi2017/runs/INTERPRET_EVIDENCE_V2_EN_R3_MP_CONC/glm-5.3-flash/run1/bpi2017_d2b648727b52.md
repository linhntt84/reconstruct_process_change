### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that resource allocation changed for activities A01, A03, A13, A14, A17 and A22: their execution shifted from one group of resources to another. In the earlier window a group including User_49 held the largest individual shares (about 11–16 in 100 assignments each); in the later window that group fell below roughly 4 in 100, while a new group including User_39 and User_18 rose to about 7–12 in 100 each. This rests on the consistent direction of the shift across all six activities, differences larger than in 1999 of 2000 random re-splits, and a resource-family item count (6) far above the chance expectation (about 0.005); no control-flow or timing items qualified, so the detected change is confined to allocation.
