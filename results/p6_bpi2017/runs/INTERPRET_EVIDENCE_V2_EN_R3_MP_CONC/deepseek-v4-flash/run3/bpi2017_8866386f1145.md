### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a shift in routing: after activity A05, the process increasingly goes to A09 instead of A04. This is directly shown by the change in A05's outgoing transitions [DIR001] and corroborated by the increased participation of A09 [OCC001]. The number of qualifying evidence items (4) is far above the 0.21 expected by chance, making a random fluctuation unlikely.

Other differences: Activity A04 became more concentrated on a single resource, with the top resource share rising from 44% to 64% [CONC001]. The time between A09 and A04 also decreased substantially, with a lower mean and less variability [TWAIT001]. These may be consequences of the routing shift (fewer cases reaching A04, and a different mix of cases between A09 and A04), but they are not directly part of the routing hypothesis. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed shift is a temporary fluctuation, but the consistency across control-flow, time, and resource indicators makes that unlikely.
