### Observed differences

- In the later window, activity A09 occurs in about 74% of traces (up from 60%), and its mean occurrences per trace rose from 0.60 to 0.74. [OCC001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a shift in routing after activity A05: the process now more often proceeds to A09 instead of A04. This is directly shown by the increased share of A05→A09 transitions and the corresponding decrease in A05→A04 transitions, together with the increased occurrence of A09. The screening shows far more evidence items than expected by chance in each family (control flow, time, resources), so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact.

Other differences: The waiting time between A09 and A04 decreased substantially and became more consistent, and activity A04 became more concentrated on a smaller set of resources. These are separate timing and resource-allocation changes that are not part of the routing hypothesis.

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed changes reflect a rare variant that happens to be more frequent in the later window, but the consistency across multiple indicators and the high empirical strengths make this unlikely.
