### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change with two faces. First, A04 is not executed in about 63 in 100 later cases (versus about 24 in 100 before), and A14 typically proceeds directly to A15 instead of through A04, while A04's own follow-up behaviour when it runs is largely retained [OCC001, DIR003, REL001, DIR002]. Second, the sequence A19, A20, A25, A26 is placed later in the case: it is now reached mainly from A28/A29 and leads on to A29/A33, whereas before it was reached after A46 and led to A27, and it now follows A27/A28 in the trace order instead of preceding them; the participation, order, routing, position and distance items show this directly and consistently, and every listed difference is larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled windows [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG015, LAG020].

The evidence cannot rule out that the later window simply contains more of a variant without A04 and with the A19/A20/A25/A26 group in its later position — a shift in the variant mix between two finite 200-trace samples rather than a modification of the process itself — although the size and consistency of the shifts make pure sampling noise an unlikely full explanation. The items are also not independent confirmations: the A04 relations largely re-express the participation drop, and the group's routing, order, position and distance items describe one and the same move; several LAG profiles for the new adjacencies rest on a single before-window observation, and the evidence does not show whether the two shifts are one coordinated restructuring or two separate ones [REL001, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013, LAG016].
