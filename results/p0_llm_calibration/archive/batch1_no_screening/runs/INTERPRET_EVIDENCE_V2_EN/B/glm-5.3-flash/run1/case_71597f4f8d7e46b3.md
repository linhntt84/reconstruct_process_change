### Observed differences

- In the later window, A27 appears in about 61 in 100 cases (122 of 200 traces) instead of in every case; its own routing is essentially unchanged, with about 97 in 100 of its outgoing transitions still going to A28. [OCC002, DIR003]
- Four activities that were virtually absent before — A32 (1 of 200 traces), A33, A38 and A39 (0 of 200) — each occur in about 40 in 100 later cases (79–81 of 200). [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

[DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL023, REL024, REL025, REL028, REL029, REL032]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, about 40 in 100 cases no longer run the segment A26 → A27 → A28 and instead pass from A26 into a block formed by A32, A33, A38 and A39 — executed in varying order, with each of the four followed either by another block activity or by A28 — while A27 is skipped in those cases. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR002, REL007, REL012, REL008, REL019, LAG001]

Supporting signs: the participation shift is large and reciprocal (A27 down about 39 points, each block activity up about 40 points, on 200-trace windows), and A27 and the block are close to mutually exclusive (co-occurring in at most about 2 in 100 cases). Routing, order and distance evidence agree in placing the block after A26 and before A28, and the 5-step A26-to-A28 distance, absent before, appears in about 37 in 100 later cases. Every item cited shows a difference larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR002, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, LAG001]

Limitations: the internal shape of the block is not pinned down — within it both directions occur at similar rates (about 20 in 100 each way per pair), so a fixed sequence, case-dependent ordering or repeated execution cannot be distinguished, and the many relational items largely describe the same about 40 in 100 cases rather than independent confirmations. Sampling remains a competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out: the windows are finite 200-trace samples, and a variant that was previously very rare (the block appeared in at most 1 of 200 earlier traces) could have become frequent without a structural change, although the size of the shift makes pure sampling noise unlikely. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL023, REL028, REL032, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

Separately, A04's halved participation, its rerouting away from A46 and its earlier position, together with A45's near-discontinued transitions to A04, are real behavioural differences that this hypothesis does not cover; the evidence does not show whether they belong to the same structural change or to a separate one. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, DIR008]
