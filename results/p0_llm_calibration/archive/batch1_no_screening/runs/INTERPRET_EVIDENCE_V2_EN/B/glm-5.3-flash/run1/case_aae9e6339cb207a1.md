### Observed differences

- In the later window, the group A15, A16, A18, A19 — present in every trace in both windows — occurs markedly later within the case: its mean relative position rises from about 0.39 to about 0.60 [POS002, POS003, POS005, POS006].
- A14, A17, A20 and A21 shift the opposite way, from a mean relative position of about 0.68 to about 0.38–0.41; each still appears in only about 20–30 in 100 cases, and the four together account for about one occurrence per case in both windows [POS001, POS004, POS007, POS008].
- The order between these groups and the middle activities reverses. Before, A15/A16/A18/A19 preceded A22 and A26 in about 199 of 200 traces; in the later window A22 and A26 come first in about 84 in 100. A14/A17/A20/A21, which previously came after A15/A16/A18/A19 and after A22/A26 in virtually every co-occurrence, now precede them in most co-occurrences (e.g., A17 precedes A15 in about 22 of every 25 co-occurring traces) [REL009, REL010, REL001, REL007, REL008, REL015, REL025].
- Successors change at the group boundaries: A37 leads to A15/A16/A18/A19 in about 995 of 1000 of its outgoing transitions before, but to A14/A17/A20/A21 in about 84 in 100 after; A26 shows the mirror image (about 995 in 1000 to A14/A17/A20/A21 before, about 84 in 100 to A15/A16/A18/A19 after). A14/A17/A20/A21 now lead to A22 in about 78–86 in 100 of their transitions, where before they led almost exclusively to A27; A15/A16/A18/A19 lose A22 as a successor (from about 21–28 in 100 to about 2–6 in 100) and gain A27 (to about 15–25 in 100, from at most about 0.5 in 100) [DIR011, DIR010, DIR002, DIR005, DIR008, DIR009, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007].
- Step distances match this reshuffling: A26→A27 stretches from about 2 to about 5 steps and A37→A22 shrinks from about 5 to about 2, while pairs combining A26 or A27 with A15/A16/A18/A19 — within five steps in only 1 of 200 traces before — become so in about 168 of 200 traces after [LAG009, LAG010, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008].
- A22 and A26 themselves occur slightly earlier (mean relative position about 0.47 to 0.39, and 0.65 to 0.57) [POS009, POS010].
- Separately, A05's outgoing transitions shift towards A04 (about 20 to 44 in 100), away from A03 (about 44 to 31) and A09 (about 36 to 24); nothing in the evidence links A05 to the reordering above [DIR001].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, two groups of activities have exchanged their place in the case: previously a case typically ran A37 → A15/A16/A18/A19 → A22 → A26 → one of A14/A17/A20/A21 → A27, whereas now it typically runs A37 → one of A14/A17/A20/A21 → A22 → A26 → A15/A16/A18/A19 → A27. The order reversal is shown directly by the relation indicators, the two groups move in opposite positional directions, the successor hand-offs change exactly at the group boundaries (A37 and A26 each redirect to the other group; A14/A17/A20/A21 redirect from A27 to A22; A15/A16/A18/A19 gain A27 and lose A22), and the step-distance changes (A37→A22 from about 5 to 2 steps; A26→A27 from about 2 to 5) fit the new arrangement [REL009, REL001, REL025, POS002, POS001, DIR011, DIR010, DIR002, DIR003, LAG009, LAG010].

Limitations: the earlier ordering still occurs in about 16 in 100 later-window traces, and the newer ordering in about 1 in 200 before-window traces, so the evidence cannot distinguish a modified process from a shift in the mix of two pre-existing variants; with 200 traces per window, variant frequencies can differ without any structural change, although differences of this size (each larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits) make pure sampling an unlikely explanation. Many relation items restate the same pair-level behaviour and are not independent confirmations; the before-window lag profiles for the A26/A27-versus-A15/A16/A18/A19 pairs rest on a single observation each, so only their support change is meaningful; and the successor profiles of A14/A17/A20/A21 rest on roughly 40–61 transitions per window. The routing change at A05 is not connected to this hypothesis by the evidence [REL009, LAG001, DIR002, DIR001].
