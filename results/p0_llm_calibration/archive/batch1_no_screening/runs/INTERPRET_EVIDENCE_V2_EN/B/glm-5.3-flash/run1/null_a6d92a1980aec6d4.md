### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A05 followed A09 immediately (one event position later) in about 78 of 100 observed A09→A05 pairs, and two positions later in about 22 of 100; gaps of three to five positions did not occur. [LAG001]
- In the later window, the split is nearly even: about 47 of 100 pairs have A05 immediately after A09 and about 53 of 100 have it two positions later, again with no gaps of three or more positions. [LAG001]
- The comparison rests on a moderate number of observations: the ordered pair within five steps appears in about 55 of 200 traces before and about 47 of 200 after. [LAG001]
- The difference is unusual for random re-splits of the pooled traces: about 199 of 200 reference comparisons showed a smaller difference (empirical strength about 0.995). [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests a specific placement hypothesis: in the later window, A05 tends to occur two event positions after A09 rather than immediately after it, which would be consistent with an additional step now sitting between A09 and A05 in roughly half of the cases where the pair occurs. The shift is direct positional evidence — the lag distribution moved from about 78/22 to about 47/53 between one- and two-position gaps — it is rarely exceeded under random re-splits, and support is comparable in both windows (55 versus 47 pairs). [LAG001]

What is missing: no routing or relational evidence (such as successor profiles or co-occurrence) shows which activity, if any, now lies between A09 and A05, and no second indicator corroborates the shift. The lag is measured in event positions, so the change concerns relative ordering distance between the two activities, not clock time. [LAG001]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is sampling variation in variant frequencies: a variant in which A05 follows A09 at a two-position gap may simply be more represented in the later sample, and with only this single indicator the redistribution cannot be distinguished from such a mix shift. [LAG001]
