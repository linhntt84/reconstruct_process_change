### Observed differences

- In the later window, A27 runs in about 99 in 100 traces (198 of 200) versus about 61 in 100 before, and its mean occurrences per trace rose from about 0.6 to about 1.0. [OCC001]
- Four activities that formed an alternative path — A32, A33, A38 and A39 — have all but disappeared: each appeared in roughly 39–40 in 100 traces before (78–80 of 200) but in only 2–3 of 200 traces (about 1 in 100) after. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- Routing out of A26 shifted accordingly: before, about 59 in 100 of A26's outgoing transitions went to A27 and about 38 in 100 went to A32, A33, A38 or A39; after, about 96 in 100 go to A27 and those four targets together receive about 1 in 100. [DIR001]
- The surviving route is consistently ordered: A26 precedes A27 in about 97 in 100 traces, A27 precedes A28 in about 95 in 100, and A42 precedes A27 in about 97 in 100, with each of these pairs co-occurring in about 99 in 100 traces. [REL006, REL011, REL016]
- Before, the branch activities ran together between A26 and A28 — each co-occurring with A28 in about 39 in 100 traces, mostly before it, and virtually never together with A27 — whereas after, their co-occurrence with A28 or A27 is about 1 in 100 traces. [REL017, REL012, REL021]
- The A26-to-A28 distance shortened: A28 now follows A26 about two positions later in about 94 in 100 traces (about 60 in 100 before), the earlier five-position gap (about 36 in 100) has nearly gone, and A28's mean relative position moved slightly earlier, from about 0.56 to about 0.53. [LAG002, POS001]
- Smaller shifts: A19 now rarely appears without A27 (about 0.5 in 100 traces, down from about 23 in 100) while A27 usually appears without A19; and of A42's outgoing transitions, the share to A19 fell from about 53 in 100 to about 39 while the share to A20 rose from about 36 to about 49. The remaining flagged items about the branch activities (their positions, successors and distances) rest on only about 1–4 after-window observations and repeat the same near-disappearance rather than indicating further independent changes. [REL001, DIR003, DIR002, POS002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative branch in which A32, A33, A38 and A39 were executed between A26 and A28 is now essentially skipped: in the earlier window roughly four in ten cases took that branch instead of A27, while in the later window nearly every case runs A26 → A27 → A28, with A42 preceding A27. [OCC002, DIR001, REL016, REL011, LAG002]

The change is direct and consistent across indicator types: participation of the four branch activities collapsed from 78–80 to 2–3 of 200 traces, A26's outgoing transitions redirected to A27, ordered co-occurrence of A26, A27, A28 and A42 became near-ubiquitous, and the A26-to-A28 distance concentrated at two positions. Counting items that describe the same trace behaviour as one, the evidence holds essentially two distinct differences — the branch's disappearance and the smaller A42 routing shift — against roughly 0.8 items expected by chance if nothing had changed, and each item's difference exceeded about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC002, OCC005, DIR001, REL016, LAG002, DIR003]

Limitations remain: near-zero participation only shows that the branch activities were almost not observed, so the evidence cannot distinguish complete removal from a branch still taken in a small share of cases; the later-window routing details of the branch activities themselves rest on only 1–4 traces and are not reliable; and although sampling differences in variant frequencies are implausible for a drop from about 78 to about 3 of 200 traces, they cannot be excluded for the small residual components. The A42 shift towards A20 is small and could be a knock-on effect of the branch change rather than an independent modification. [OCC002, DIR002, DIR003]
