### Observed differences

- Participation in the intermediate activities rose sharply. In the later window, A19 and A20 each appear in about 995 of 1,000 traces, up from about 48 in 100; A25 and A26 each appear in about 50 in 100 traces, up from about 25 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Routing out of A42 changed markedly. Before, A42's next activity was A27 in about 50 of 100 observed outgoing transitions and A19 in about 45 of 100; in the later window it is A19 in about 92 of 100, while A27 accounts for only about 1 in 200. [DIR004]
- The ordering A42 → A19 → A20 → A27, seen in roughly 48 of 100 traces before, now holds in about 995 of 1,000 traces: A42 precedes A19 and A20, A19 precedes A20, and both precede A27 in nearly every later trace. [REL001, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009]
- A25 and A26 never occur in the same trace in either window. In the later window almost every trace contains exactly one of them (traces containing neither fall from about 52 in 100 to under 1 in 100), with each placed after A42 and A19 and before A20 and A27. [REL010, REL002, REL003, REL006, REL007, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- The positional distance from A42 to A27 shifted: before, A27 followed A42 immediately in about half of the observed pairs and sat four positions later in about 44 of 100; in the later window it sits four positions later in about 90 of 100 and immediately in about 1 in 200. [LAG002]
- Smaller shifts: A27's average relative position moves slightly later (about 0.40 to 0.43 of the trace) and A42's slightly earlier (about 0.33 to 0.31); rare outgoing edges at A20, A25 and A26 (towards A06, A10, A22, A04, each about 1–2 in 100) appear or disappear between the windows, while the A25→A27 distance stays at two positions. [POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the direct route from A42 to A27 has been largely replaced by a route through A19, so the segment A19 → (A25 or A26) → A20 now executes between A42 and A27 in nearly every trace, whereas in the earlier window about half of the traces went from A42 straight to A27 without these activities. This rests on direct evidence: the successor shares at A42 moved from roughly 50% A27 / 45% A19 to roughly 92% A19 / 0.5% A27, and the A42→A27 distance moved from immediate to four event positions, matching the length of that segment. [DIR004, LAG002]

The relational and participation evidence is consistent with this in direction and magnitude: the pairs (A42,A19), (A19,A20), (A42,A20), (A19,A27) and (A20,A27) moved from about 48 to about 99.5 in 100 traces in the same order, and the participation gains of A19, A20, A25 and A26 — together with exactly one of A25/A26 now appearing in almost every trace, a mutually exclusive choice that already existed before — all fit this single picture. These items largely describe one underlying difference across the same six activities rather than independent confirmations, but the number of qualifying items (22 against roughly 0.6 expected for an unchanged process) and effect sizes far beyond what random re-splits of the pooled traces produce (every item exceeds 199 of 200 reference comparisons) make sampling variation implausible for the main pattern. [REL001, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL010]

Limitations remain. The evidence cannot distinguish between removing the direct A42→A27 route and shifting A42's routing probabilities; the direct successorship is still observed in about 1 of 200 cases, so the old route is nearly, but not completely, gone. The rare edge changes at A20, A25 and A26 (A06, A10, A22, A04 at about 1–2 in 100) are the kind of rare-variant wobble that two finite 200-trace windows produce and should not be read as separate structural edits, and the small position shifts of A27 and A42 follow from the segment now always executing rather than showing that either activity was itself relocated. [DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002]
