### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the segment around A15, A16, A18, A19, A20, A21, A22 and A23 ran as a fixed chain: A28 always passed to A18, A18 to A15/A16, A19 always moved on to A20, A20 only to A22/A23, A22/A23 to A21/A22 or A21/A23, and A21 always closed to A29. In the later window these successor shares are redistributed: A19 sends about half of its outgoing transitions directly to A29 (previously none) and only about 8 in 100 to A20 (previously all); A28 passes directly to A20 in about half of its transitions (previously none); A20, A21, A22 and A23 send roughly a third to a half of their transitions back to A15, A16, A18 or A19; and A15, A16 and A18 forward roughly a third to two fifths of theirs to A20–A23. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009]

- The strict order between the two groups broke down. Before, in every one of the 15 monitored pairs the early activity (A15/A16/A18/A19) preceded the late one (A20/A21/A22/A23) in 100 of 100 traces containing both; in the later window the orders are mixed — A20 now precedes A19 in about 93 in 100 such traces and A15 in about 77, A22 and A23 precede A19 in about three quarters, while A21 still follows A15/A16 in about 77–78. Both activities of each pair still occur together in essentially all traces, so this is a change in sequencing, not in participation. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015]

- Distances collapsed where the new transitions appear: A19→A29 and A28→A20 were always five positions apart and are now one position apart in about half of the observed cases; A15→A20 moved from lags 2–3 to lag 1 in about half; A18→A20 from always four to one–two in about seven in ten; A19→A21 from always four to one–two in about seven in ten. The ordered pair A15-then-A20 within five steps now appears in only about 47 of 200 traces, against 200 of 200 before. [LAG003, LAG016, LAG019, LAG022, LAG038]

- Event positions shifted consistently with the reordering: A15, A16 and A19 now occur later in the trace (mean position up by about 0.05–0.08), while A20, A21, A22 and A23 occur earlier (down by about 0.03–0.08). [POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]

- A milder, separate shift concerns A43: it appears in about 61 of 100 traces in the later window versus about 70 of 100 before, and its outgoing transitions now split roughly 51/49 between A40 and A44, against roughly 33/67 before. [DIR010, POS009, LAG039]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the routing and ordering of the segment around A15, A16, A18, A19, A20, A21, A22 and A23 changed in the later window: the previously fixed chain (A28 → A18 → A15/A16 → A19 → A20 → A22/A23 → A21 → A29) is replaced by a pattern in which A28 enters directly at A20 in about half the cases, A19 proceeds directly to A29 in about half, and new transitions connect the early group (A15/A16/A18/A19) and the late block (A20–A23) in both directions, so the late block now precedes the early activities in a large share of traces instead of always following them. This rests on direct relational evidence — the successor profiles show the new transitions, all 15 monitored order relations flipped or loosened exactly where those transitions appear, lag distributions for the newly connected pairs collapsed from four–five positions to one–two, and the position shifts point in the matching directions. Treated as distinct differences (the A15–A23 reordering including the A28 entry and A19 exit changes, plus the milder A43 shift), they clearly exceed the roughly 0.64 items expected by chance among the 129 tested units, and every item exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits; the many items about the same activities are treated as one underlying difference seen from four angles rather than independent confirmations. [DIR004, DIR009, REL001, REL012, LAG022, LAG038, POS001, POS005]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of pre-existing execution variants — for example, traces that already could run these activities in either order or go from A19 directly to A29 being sampled far more often in the later window — since two 200-trace samples can differ in variant frequencies without any structural change; the small A43 shift in particular is consistent with such sampling variation. Note also that the routing shares are taken over outgoing transitions rather than cases, and positions are event positions, not clock time, so no claims about timing are made. [DIR010, POS009]
