### Observed differences

- Five activities that ran in every one of the 200 earlier traces — A27, A36, A37, A42 and A43 — each appear in only about half of the later traces (about 100 of 200). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- A27 and the group A36/A37/A42/A43, which previously always occurred together, now practically never do: they jointly appear in only about 1 in 200 later traces, with A27 alone in about half of the traces and the group alone in the other half. [REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027, REL012, REL013]
- The group also changed place. Before, A25 was directly followed by a group member in about 98% of A25's outgoing transitions, and the group preceded both A28 and A26 in about 97–99% of traces; in the later window A25 is followed directly by A28 in about 96% of transitions, and the group, when it runs, now comes after A26 and A28 (the reversed order holds in about 50% of traces, matching the group's presence in half of them). Mean event positions agree: the group moved from about 0.40 to about 0.58 of the trace, A28 from about 0.47 to 0.39, A26 from about 0.61 to 0.54. [DIR003, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL030, REL031, REL032, REL033, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010, POS005, POS003]
- The distance from A25 to A28 collapsed from about five steps (99% of traces) to immediate succession (about 97%). [LAG002, DIR003]
- The group's exits were rerouted: transitions from its members back to A28 (previously about 20–27% of each member's outgoing transitions) are no longer observed, while exits to A31 now take roughly 18–28% each; accordingly the gap from A26 to A31, previously almost always two steps, is now two steps in about half of the traces (the A27 route) and about five steps in the other half (the group route). A31 itself was present in all traces in both windows. [DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG007, POS006]
- A06 became much rarer and lost its late-process role: participation fell from about 32% to about 10% of traces (63 to 19 of 200), A51 → A06 dropped from 28% to about 2% of A51's outgoing transitions while A51 → A52 rose from 71% to 90%, and A06's position within traces became far more variable. [OCC001, DIR010, REL011, POS001, LAG001, LAG017]
- A02's outgoing transitions spread from five targets to about ten, but this rests on only 5 observed transitions before versus 23 after and is weakly supported. [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the four-activity group A36/A37/A42/A43 was relocated and made optional: it previously always ran directly after A25 and before A28 and A26, whereas in the later window it runs in only about half of the cases and, when it runs, it follows A26 and A28 and typically continues into A31. As a result, A25 now flows straight into A28, and A27 — still positioned after A26 — appears in the other half of the cases as an alternative to the group, with the two practically never co-occurring. [DIR003, LAG002, REL018, REL030, REL024, DIR005, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

A second, distinct difference concerns A06, an optional late activity: it now occurs in far fewer cases and no longer bridges A51 to A52, which now usually follows A51 directly. [OCC001, DIR010, REL011]

The order change is shown directly by relational comparisons with full support in both windows — the share of traces with the group before A26 or A28 fell from about 97–99% to under 1%, while the reverse order rose to about 50%, exactly matching the group's presence in half the traces — and the routing and distance items agree with it. Even after grouping the many items about the same activities into distinct differences, they clearly exceed the roughly 1 item expected by chance when 203 units are screened. [REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL030, REL031, REL032, REL033, DIR003, DIR004, LAG002, LAG007]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply sample different mixtures of pre-existing variants — a flow without the group may have existed before but been too rare to appear among 200 traces. Several lag and routing items rest on very few observations (1–3 later observations for several A25/group pairs, 5 versus 23 transitions for A02) and were not relied upon, and A06's later position profile is based on only 19 traces. [LAG003, LAG005, LAG011, LAG012, LAG014, LAG015, DIR001, POS001]
