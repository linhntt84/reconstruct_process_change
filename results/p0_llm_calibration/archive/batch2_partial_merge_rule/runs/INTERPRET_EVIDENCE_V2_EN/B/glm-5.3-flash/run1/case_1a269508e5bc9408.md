### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of two structural differences. First, in the later window A04 runs on most cases as an early step between A14 and A15 (A14 → A04 → A15), whereas before, A14 led mostly directly to A15 and A04 was rare; A06, A08 and A10 now appear in only about 6–7 in 100 cases, coinciding with A04's rise. Second, the block A19/A20/A25/A26 now executes before A27/A28 — fed by A46 and followed by the tightened A27→A28→A29→A33 sequence — whereas before it ran after A29. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR002, DIR005, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR013, REL001, REL002, REL012, REL016, LAG001, LAG005, LAG019]

The support rests on direct relational and routing evidence rather than position shifts alone, and the distinct differences (at least two or three coherent clusters, clearly more than the roughly 0.86 items expected by chance among 172 tested units) are backed by large supports: about 144–158 traces behind the A04 items, 200 in 200 traces behind the order relations, and 47–63 transitions behind the rerouted activities. [OCC001, DIR002, REL012, LAG019]

Limitations: the many items around A04 describe one underlying difference, not independent confirmations, and the A06/A08/A10 decline may be a by-product of the rerouting through A04 rather than a separate change. Some items rest on very few observations — A02's routing change (20 transitions after) and several lag items with only 1–2 observed pairs after the change point — and could be sampling noise. The evidence cannot rule out that differences in the mix of process variants between the two finite samples, rather than a control-flow modification, account for part of these patterns.
