- Participation dropped for five activities: before, A22 and each of A31, A32, A37 and A38 appeared in every trace; in the later window each appears in only about 50 in 100 traces. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the four-activity block A31/A32/A37/A38, which previously ran between A20 and A23, now runs between A21 and A26, where it appears in about half the cases as an alternative to A22. Before, traces looked like A20 → the four in varying order → A23 … A21 → A22 → A26; in the later window A20 flows straight to A23, and the block sits downstream between A21 and A26, mutually exclusive with A22. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG006, REL013, REL017]

The change is shown directly by relational and distance evidence, not only by frequencies: A20's successor profile switched from the four activities to A23, A21's from always A22 to an even split between A22 and the group, and the group's successors now include A26 instead of A23; the A20→A23 distance collapsed from five positions to one and the A21→A26 distance split into two and five positions, exactly as a relocated four-activity block predicts. About 60 evidence items qualify against roughly 0.62 expected by chance under this screening, and they collapse to this one coherent difference; each item's difference exceeds those of about 995 in 1000 random re-splits. With 200 traces per window, a stable process that ran these activities in every trace would rarely show 50% participation combined with near-zero co-occurrence between A22 and the group. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG006, REL007, REL013, REL017, REL021]

Limitations: this is a possibility supported by the sample, not a confirmed modification. A competing explanation is a shift in the mix of process variants between the two finite windows rather than a structural modification; the before-window's 100% co-occurrence of A22 with the group and the flipped order and distance relations make a pure frequency shift an unlikely account, but it cannot be fully excluded, nor can simultaneous changes in unobserved parts of the process. In addition, several distance items involving the four activities (e.g., A20–A31, A31–A23) rest on a single after-window observation each and were not relied on; the conclusion stands on the fully supported items such as A20–A23 and A21–A26. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]
