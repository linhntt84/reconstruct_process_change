### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the process executes a fixed block A30 → A34/A35 → A36/A37 in essentially every case, with A31 postponed to after A36/A37 (3–5 steps downstream) instead of following A34/A35 directly, and that an A06 step leading to A16 is inserted earlier in about 28 in 100 cases, while A10 is executed far less often. The ordering claims rest on direct relational, routing and distance evidence, not on frequency shifts alone, and the two main differences (the A30–A37/A31 block and the A06 insertion), plus the A10 decline, clearly exceed the roughly 0.76 items expected by chance among the 152 tested units. [REL017, REL026, DIR005, LAG008, OCC001, DIR002, REL003, OCC002]

Limitations: the two windows are finite samples of 200 traces each, so a shift in the mix of executed variants — an existing path now run in all cases rather than a modified process — would produce the same pattern and cannot be ruled out; the before-profile of A06 rests on only 13 traces (and 14 outgoing transitions) and the A10 decline on only 5 later traces. Items sharing the same activities (participation, order and distance for the A30–A37 block) describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002]
