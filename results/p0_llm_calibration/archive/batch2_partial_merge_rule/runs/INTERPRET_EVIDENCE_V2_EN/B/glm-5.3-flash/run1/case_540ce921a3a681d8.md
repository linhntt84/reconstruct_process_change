### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the exclusive choice block of A19, A20, A25 and A26 between A42 and A27 is no longer executed, with cases that previously ran A42 → one of the four → A27 now running A42 → A27 directly. The signs are direct and consistent: the four activities' participation collapsed from about 25 in 100 traces (48–56 supporting traces each) to at most 2 of 200; A42's successor distribution flipped from about 93 in 100 transitions into the four to about 92 in 100 straight to A27, measured on essentially all of its outgoing transitions in both windows; and the A42→A27 distance shortened from typically two positions to one, which is what dropping the intermediate activity would produce. The 22 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.55 expected without change, and they collapse into this one coherent difference rather than scattered, unrelated signals. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR004, LAG002]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that the four activities were not observed in the later window, so the evidence cannot distinguish a removed block from one that still exists but was exercised in almost none of the later-window cases — a sampling difference in variant frequencies; given the earlier frequency of about 25 in 100, seeing at most 2 of 200 traces makes pure sampling unlikely but does not exclude a much rarer remaining variant. The block's own after-window behaviour (its outgoing edges and positions) rests on 0–2 observations, and sporadic new signals such as A20→A15 may be sampling noise. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS004]
