### Observed differences

- The main difference is a reversal in which two-activity route runs after A20. In the before window, A21 and A22 each took part in about 78 in 100 traces, while A23 and A24 appeared in only about 22–23 in 100; in the later window this is reversed, with A21 and A22 down to about 21–22 in 100 traces and A23 and A24 up to about 79–81 in 100. Each shift is roughly 56–58 participation points, backed by 156–161 traces per window, and was larger than in 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- The branch point A20, which appears in essentially every trace in both windows, now routes to the other path: before, about 78 in 100 of A20's outgoing transitions went to A21 and about 22 to A23; in the later window about 78 go to A23, about 21 still go to A21, and two marginal targets (A11, A30) appear at under 1 in 100. [DIR001, REL003, REL004]
- Both routes behave as fixed two-activity blocks. Before, A20 preceded A21 in about 78 in 100 traces and A21 preceded A22 in about 77 in 100, with the pair co-occurring in about 78 in 100; in the later window A20 precedes A23 in about 78 in 100 and A23 precedes A24 in about 78 in 100, with that pair co-occurring in about 79 in 100, while A21 and A22 are now jointly absent from about 78 in 100 traces. [REL001, REL002, REL005, REL012]
- The downstream tie to A28 moved with the block: before, A28 co-occurred with A21 in about 78 in 100 traces and with A22 in about 79 in 100, following or interleaving with them (about 47 in 100 before A28, about 31 in 100 interleaved); in the later window A28 co-occurs with A23 in about 79 in 100 and with A24 in about 81 in 100, showing a very similar mixed pattern (about 45 in 100 before A28, 34–35 in 100 interleaved). [REL008, REL011, REL013, REL014]
- The two routes are alternatives rather than sequential stages: A21 and A23 co-occur in at most about 1 in 200 traces in either window, and the same near-exclusivity holds for A21 with A24 and for A22 with A23 and A24. [REL006, REL007, REL009, REL010]
- Where the old route still runs it keeps its shape — A21 still leads mostly to A22 (about 91 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) and A22 mostly to A28 (about 93 in 100) — and A23 leads to A24 in both windows (about 98–99 in 100). The remaining flagged differences are marginal edge changes of exactly this kind, based on only 45–46 traces in one window, i.e. the size an unchanged process still produces occasionally. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the route out of A20 has switched: traces that previously ran A20 → A21 → A22 (and on towards A28) now mostly run A20 → A23 → A24 instead, with the old A21–A22 route still executed but only in about 21–22 in 100 later-window traces (down from about 78 in 100) and the A23–A24 route now covering about 79–81 in 100 (up from about 22–23 in 100). The support is direct rather than circumstantial: the flip is visible in A20's outgoing shares, in the internal order of both two-activity blocks, and in the transfer of the downstream tie to A28 from A21/A22 to A23/A24, with occurrence, routing and relation indicators all moving consistently together. After grouping the agreeing items into this one underlying difference, it clearly exceeds the roughly 0.5 items screening expects by chance without a change; the remaining flagged items are the marginal, low-support edge changes noted above. [DIR001, REL005, REL012, REL008, REL011, REL013, REL014, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

Two limitations remain. The evidence cannot distinguish a re-routing of the process from a shift in the usage mix of two pre-existing variants, since A23 and A24 already occurred in about 22–23 in 100 before-window traces — the A23–A24 route existed before, and the data cannot show whether the choice is made at A20 itself or upstream of it. Pure sampling differences in variant frequencies are an unlikely explanation, since a participation shift of this size on these support counts arose in fewer than 1 in 200 random re-splits, but chance fluctuation cannot be excluded for the small edge-level changes; A21 and A22 also still occur in the later window, so the old route is reduced in use, not removed. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, DIR003]
