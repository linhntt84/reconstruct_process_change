### Observed differences

- Eight activities previously alternated between two mutually exclusive groups: before, A33, A34, A36 and A37 each ran in about 54-55 in 100 cases and A38, A39, A40 and A41 in about 46-47 in 100, while cross-group pairs shared only about 1-3 in 200 cases. In the later window, each of the eight runs essentially once in every case (199-200 of 200 traces). [OCC002, OCC006, OCC009, REL044]

- The two groups became a sequence rather than alternatives: in the later window, A33/A34/A36/A37 precede A38, A39, A40 and A41 in about 97-98 in 100 cases, whereas before such pairs co-occurred in at most about 2 in 100. [REL044, REL047, REL053, REL058, REL059, REL062]

- The block's embedding changed accordingly. A14 and A30, present in every case in both windows, now precede the block in about 98-99 in 100 cases (about 46-54 in 100 before); A15 precedes it in about 67 in 100 with about 32 in 100 interleaved; and A31 still follows the block but at a larger distance, with gaps from A33/A34/A36/A37 to A31 and from A30 to A38-A41 moving from spread over 1-4 steps to about 96-98 in 100 at the 5-step maximum. A14, A15 and A30 also sit slightly earlier (A30's mean relative position from about 66% to 58% into the case) and A31 slightly later. [REL009, REL017, REL025, REL029, REL033, REL037, LAG004, LAG008, LAG011, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

- Routing matches this: A30's outgoing transitions now concentrate on A33, A34, A36 and A37 (about 19-27% each, versus about 8-14% each spread over all eight before), and A33-A37 lose A31 as a successor (about 21-28% before, near 0 after) while gaining A38-A41. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]

- A06 roughly halved and moved: participation fell from about 28 in 100 to about 14 in 100 cases (57 vs 27 of 200 traces), its mean relative position shifted from about 21% to 36% into the case, and it no longer appears in cases without the block (about 13 in 100 before, none after). A14 also routes to A06 far less often (about 25% to 4% of its outgoing transitions), while A06's own shift away from A15 (about 86% to 29% of its outgoing transitions) rests on only about 28 later cases. [OCC001, POS001, REL001, DIR001, DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window executes a restructured block: the eight activities A33, A34, A36, A37, A38, A39, A40 and A41, which previously ran as two mutually exclusive groups of four chosen per case (about 54 vs 46 in 100), now run in sequence in nearly every case — A30 and A14 leading into A33/A34/A36/A37, then A38/A39/A40/A41, then A31 — while A06 occurs in only about half as many cases and, when it occurs, just before this block. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC009, REL044, REL059, DIR003]

The participation change is near-universal and once per case, the order evidence is direct and consistent across every pair within the block and with A14, A15, A30 and A31, the routing changes line up with the new sequence, and the gap growth to the five-step maximum fits the second group being inserted between the first group and A31. A mere shift in the mix of variants between the windows cannot plausibly explain the core change: with each group present in about half of 200 cases, near-zero cross-group co-occurrence before (about 1-3 in 200) against about 99 in 100 later lies far outside what random re-splits produce. [OCC002, REL033, REL044, REL059, DIR004, LAG008]

Limitations remain: only group-level order is supported, since order within each group stays mixed (A33 precedes A34 in about 49 in 100 later cases and follows it in about 50 in 100); A06's rerouting rests on about 28 later cases and its distance spread on about 13, so sampling could contribute there; and the many qualifying items collapse into a few distinct behaviours — clearly more than the roughly 1 expected by chance among the 236 tested units, but not independent confirmations of one another. [REL041, DIR001, LAG001, LAG002]
