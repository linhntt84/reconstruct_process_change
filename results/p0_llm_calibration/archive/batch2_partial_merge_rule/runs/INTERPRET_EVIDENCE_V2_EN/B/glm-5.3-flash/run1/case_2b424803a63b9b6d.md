### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A02 is executed as a standard late step in the case — after A14/A15 and the A29/A30/A45 stage, and immediately before A46 — in about two in three cases, whereas before it ran in about one in five cases at scattered positions. In the same window, the A21–A24 block, which previously routed mixedly to A30 and among itself, now runs in a fixed order A29 → A21/A22 → A23/A24 → A30, and A06 is executed in far fewer cases. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR011, OCC002, REL001, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, LAG002, LAG013]

The placement hypothesis rests on direct relational and positional evidence, not only on frequency: A02's participation roughly tripled while its position concentrated at the end of the case, the relation items show A02 following A14/A15/A29/A30/A45 and preceding A46, and the direction and lag items show the A45→A02→A46 sequence directly. The chain change is likewise shown by successor shares and lags that agree. Counting the A02 repositioning, the A21–A30 chain and the A06 drop as three distinct differences, this clearly exceeds the roughly 0.7 items expected by chance among the 141 tested units, and each difference is larger than what random re-splits of the pooled traces produced in all but about 1 in 200 reference comparisons. [OCC001, POS001, REL009, REL010, DIR011, LAG012, LAG013, DIR005, LAG003, LAG005, OCC002]

Limitations: the two windows are finite samples, so the evidence cannot rule out that the later window simply contains a larger share of a variant that includes a late A02 and the sequential A21–A30 chain, without a change to the shared structure; it also cannot distinguish A02 having been repositioned from early A02 occurrences disappearing while late ones became standard. Some profiles are thinly supported — A06's later routing rests on 13 traces and one lag item (A24→A21) on a single observation — so beyond its frequency drop, A06's later behaviour remains uncertain. [OCC002, DIR002, LAG006, POS001]
