### Observed differences

- Participation is unchanged: in both windows, every tested pair of activities co-occurs in essentially all traces (100 in 100), so the differences below are about order and routing, not about activities appearing or disappearing. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015]
- The order between two groups of activities has partly reversed. Before, A20, A21, A23 and A24 preceded each of A25, A26, A27 and A28 in at least 97 in 100 traces; in the later window, A25 precedes A24 in about 92 in 100 traces and A20 in about 76 in 100, A27 and A28 precede A24 in about 74 in 100 and A20 or A21 in about half of the traces, while A23 still precedes A27 and A28 in about 73–75 in 100 later traces. [REL001, REL003, REL007, REL008, REL010, REL012, REL014, REL015]
- Relative positions moved accordingly: in the later window A20, A21, A23 and A24 occur later within traces (mean position up by about 0.03–0.07, e.g., A24 from 0.61 to 0.68), while A25–A28 occur earlier (e.g., A25 from 0.64 to 0.56). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]
- Successors changed consistently with this: A24 now passes to A34 in about half of its outgoing transitions (before, A25 took about 99 in 100 and A34 was not observed as a direct successor), and A34 immediately follows A24 in about half of the later observations of this pair, whereas it always sat five positions later before; A33 now leads to A25 in about half of its transitions (before, A23 took about 98 in 100), with A25 directly after A33 in about half of the later cases. [DIR004, DIR009, LAG022, LAG040, LAG042]
- Routing around the block changed in both directions: A20, A21 and A23 now flow into A25–A28 in about 33–43 in 100 of their outgoing transitions (such links did not appear in the earlier profiles), and A25–A28 now continue into A20/A21/A23/A24 in about 34–50 in 100 of theirs. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008]
- Fixed spacings dissolved: pairs that always sat at one fixed distance before (e.g., A20→A26 always five positions apart, A23→A25 always four) are now spread across one to five positions, and the ordered pair A20→A25 within five steps, seen in about 200 earlier traces, now appears in only about 47. [LAG003, LAG004, LAG016, LAG019]
- One minor, separate shift: A48's outgoing transitions moved from A49 towards A45 (about 63 to 48 in 100, and 32 to 50 in 100). [DIR010, LAG043]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment formed by A25, A26, A27 and A28, which previously always ran after A20, A21, A23 and A24, now runs before them in a majority of executions, with A24 continuing directly to A34 and A33 continuing to A25 in about half of the later traces. This rests on direct relational evidence — the order reversal appears across the tested pairs while co-occurrence stays at 100 in 100 traces, so it is order and not participation that changed — together with positional shifts in the implied directions and successor changes (A24→A34 and A33→A25 becoming immediate after having been five positions apart) that are exactly what the block no longer sitting between them would produce. The many qualifying items collapse into one coherent reordering with its direct downstream consequences, plus the small shift at A48; even counted conservatively that is clearly more distinct differences than the roughly 0.9 expected by chance among the 179 tested units, and each item's difference exceeds 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [REL001, REL012, POS004, POS005, DIR004, DIR009, LAG022, LAG042]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a change in the mix of process variants rather than a single uniformly re-routed flow: the reversal is incomplete, with roughly 8 in 100 (A24 versus A25) to about three-quarters (A23 versus A27/A28) of later traces still following the old order, so the later window may blend old and new behaviour, and with two 200-trace windows a rise in the frequency of a previously rare variant cannot be separated from a re-routing with certainty. A few distance profiles also rest on fewer than about 60 later observations, and positions and lags are measured in event positions rather than clock time, so the hypothesis concerns execution order only. [REL001, REL003, LAG003]
