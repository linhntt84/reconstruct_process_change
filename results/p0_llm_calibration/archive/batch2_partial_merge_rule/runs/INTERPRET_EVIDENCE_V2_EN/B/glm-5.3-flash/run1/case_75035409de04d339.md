### Observed differences

- The clearest change concerns A14 and A15. Before, every observed outgoing transition from A14 (about 109 observations) and from A15 (about 91) went to A20. In the later window, about 51 in 100 of these transitions go to A22 and about 47–48 in 100 to A23, with only about 1–2 in 100 still going to A20. [DIR001, DIR002]
- A35 changed successor almost completely: before, its transitions went to A14 (about 55 in 100) or A15 (about 45 in 100); in the later window about 99 in 100 go to A20, and A20 now immediately follows A35 (1 step apart, previously 2). [DIR005, LAG009]
- A21 and A22 gained new successors: before, A21 always led to A22 and A22 always to A23 (100 in 100 each). In the later window, A21 leads to A22 in about 49 in 100 cases and to A14 or A15 in about 51 (about 27 and 24); A22 leads to A23 in about 53 in 100 and to A14 or A15 in about 47 (about 25 and 22). Accordingly, the A21–A22 and A22–A23 step distances now split between 1 and 2 steps (previously always 1), and A21 to A23 is now consistently about 3 steps apart (previously 2). [DIR003, DIR004, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]
- Pairwise order reversed: before, A14 and A15 always occurred before A20 and A21 when both were present (about 55 and 45 in 100 traces, never the reverse); in the later window A20 and A21 come first in about 52 in 100 traces, with the old order nearly gone (about 1 in 200). For A14/A15 versus A22, previously always A14/A15 first, the order is now about even (about 27 vs 25 in 100). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Step distances shift accordingly: A35 to A14/A15 was adjacent (1 step) before and is now 5 steps apart in about 96–98 in 100 of observed pairs; A14/A15 to A22 was 5 steps before and is now adjacent; A35 to A21 moved from 5 to 4 steps; A20 to A22 is now 4 or 5 steps (previously always 4). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG003]
- Positions moved consistently: A14 and A15 now sit around the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.51, previously about 0.36), while A20 and A21 occur slightly earlier (means about 0.36 and 0.46, previously about 0.39 and 0.49). Participation hardly changed: A14 appears in about 52 in 100 later traces (55 before) and A15 in about 48 (46 before). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A14 and A15, which in the earlier window ran between A35 and A20 (A35 → A14/A15 → A20 … A21 → A22 → A23), in the later window run instead between A21/A22 and A23: A35 hands over directly to A20, and A14/A15 follow either A21 (about half of traces, then leading to A22) or A22 (the rest, then leading to A23), always before A23. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG005, LAG009]

The support is direct and consistent: the order relations reversed exactly where a relocation predicts, the step-distance changes mirror each other on both sides (A35–A14/A15 from adjacent to far apart, A14/A15–A22 from far apart to adjacent), the successor changes are near-deterministic (from 100 in 100 to essentially 0), and the position shifts of all four activities agree in direction. Participation of A14/A15 stayed at about half of traces, so this is a change of placement rather than skipping or inclusion. Although 21 items qualify, they largely express one coherent underlying difference with its ripple effects; even counted conservatively, the distinct differences clearly exceed the roughly 0.4 items expected by chance at these thresholds, and every item's difference is larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [REL001, REL004, LAG001, LAG007, DIR001, DIR005, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a rewiring of the control flow from a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants — for example, a rerouted variant too rare to appear among the roughly 109 A14-traces observed before could have become dominant in the later window. Positions and distances use event positions rather than clock time, so no conclusion about timing is implied, and the two activities that separate A20 from A21 in both windows are not covered by this evidence. [DIR001, REL001, POS001, LAG009, LAG010]
