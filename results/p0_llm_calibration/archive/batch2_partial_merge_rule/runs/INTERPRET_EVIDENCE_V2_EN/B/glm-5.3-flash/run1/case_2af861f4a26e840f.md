### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that routing around A06 was restructured: in the later window, most cases run from A38 through A06 into A19 and A20 and on to A30 and A31, which now repeat and interleave with A19, A20, A38, A43 and A44, while the branch containing A04, A08 and A10 is executed far less often. [DIR007, LAG012, DIR002, LAG002, REL003, REL004, OCC005, OCC006, REL014, LAG010, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]

The ordering evidence is direct and relational rather than inferred from frequencies alone, the participation and repetition shifts are large and backed by all 200 traces per window, and the distinct differences — the A06-centred rerouting, the A30/A31 repetition, and the A04/A08/A10 decline — clearly exceed the roughly 0.73 items expected by chance when 147 units are screened. [LAG012, LAG002, OCC005, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004]

An important competing explanation remains: a shift in the mix of process variants between the windows cannot be ruled out, since a path through A06 already existed in the earlier window (A06 in about 18 in 100 traces; A38 leading to A06 in about 1–2 in 100 of A38's transitions), so the later window may simply contain far more of this existing variant rather than a newly placed step. [OCC002, DIR007] In addition, the rerouting details for A04, A08 and A10 rest on few later-window observations, and the position and gap indicators use event positions rather than clock time.
