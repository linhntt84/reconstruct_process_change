### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, roughly half of the executions run an alternative segment between A21 and A23 in which A22 is skipped and a block of four activities — A27, A28, A33 and A34, executed in varying order — appears in their place, while the remaining executions still follow the former A21 → A22 → A23 segment. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, REL011, REL016, REL025, LAG001]

This rests on direct, mutually consistent relational and directional evidence rather than on frequency counts alone: A21's outgoing transitions now include the four activities, the four lead to A23 and to one another, A22 and the block are strictly mutually exclusive, and the A21-to-A23 step distance lengthens from 2 to 5 in about 47 in 100 cases — matching the share of traces containing the block. Occurrence, direction, relation, position and lag indicators all reflect this same underlying difference, and the two distinct differences visible in the evidence (this segment change and the A06 routing shift) clearly exceed the roughly 0.5 items screening expects by chance in an unchanged process. [DIR002, DIR003, REL017, REL021, LAG001, OCC001, DIR001]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window only means A27, A28, A33 and A34 were not observed there, so the evidence cannot rule out that this block existed as a rare variant before and merely rose in frequency, nor that A22 was already skippable at a low level — sampling differences in variant frequencies between two 200-trace windows can produce such shifts. The internal order of the block also varies, so the evidence pins down only that the four activities run between A21 and A23, not a fixed sequence. Finally, the routing shift at A06 toward A07 is a distinct difference that this hypothesis does not explain, and the evidence provides no link between the two. [REL025, OCC002, DIR001]
