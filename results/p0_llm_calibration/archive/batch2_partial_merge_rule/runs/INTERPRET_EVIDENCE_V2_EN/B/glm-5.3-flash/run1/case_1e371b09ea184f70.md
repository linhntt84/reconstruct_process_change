- In the later window, A19 and A20 have all but stopped running: each took part in about half of the earlier traces (roughly 99 and 103 of 200) but in only 1 and 4 of 200 later traces, and before the change they were near-mutually exclusive alternatives, each appearing without the other in about 49–51 in 100 traces. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the alternative pair A19/A20 that each covered about half of the cases is no longer executed, and the new pair A25/A26 now runs in nearly every case on the path between A42 and A27, where A19/A20 previously sat. The participation changes are large and well supported on both sides (about 99–103 of 200 traces before versus 1–4 after for A19/A20, and 0 versus 198 of 200 for A25/A26), which sampling variation in variant frequencies cannot plausibly explain. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The hypothesis is shown directly rather than by accumulation of items: A42's successors flipped from A19/A20 to A25/A26 on about 200 observations per window, and the relational and lag evidence places A25/A26 after A42 and before A27, with the A42-to-A27 distance growing by about one step. Although many items qualify, they express only a few distinct behavioural differences — the disappearance of A19/A20, the insertion of A25/A26, and the secondary routing shifts — clearly more than the 0.65 items expected by chance in this screening, and the many agreeing relation items reflect the same underlying traces rather than independent confirmations. [DIR008, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, LAG004, OCC003]

Limitations remain: zero participation shows only that A19/A20 were not observed in the later sample, not that they are impossible there, and the evidence cannot fully exclude that the disappearance and the insertion are two independent changes that happen to meet at A42. The smaller shifts — the A05/A14 swap at A07 and parts of the A06/A10 redistribution — are of a size that sampling differences between windows can produce and should not be read as separate changes. [DIR002, DIR001, DIR003, OCC001]
