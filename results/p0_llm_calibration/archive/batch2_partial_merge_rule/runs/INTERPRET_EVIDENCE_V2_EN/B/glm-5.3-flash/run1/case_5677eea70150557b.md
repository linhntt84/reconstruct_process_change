### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural reorganisation with three visible facets: in the later window A06 is executed in a much smaller share of cases (about 20 in 100 traces, down from about 63) and A38 rarely routes to it; A30 and A31 are executed once, in a fixed A30-then-A31 sequence, instead of being repeated and interleaved with their surroundings; and A02, A04 and A10 appear in a larger share of cases, including as low-share successors after A14, A15 and A43. About four distinct behavioural differences stand behind this, clearly more than the 0.77 items expected by chance across the 155 tested units, and each facet is visible from several angles — participation, routing shares, relational order and positional lags — with the ordering change shown directly by relational evidence (co-occurrence unchanged at 100 in 100 while strict precedence rises to about 97–99 in 100) rather than by frequency alone. [OCC003, OCC005, OCC006, DIR005, DIR006, REL010, REL016, LAG012, OCC004, DIR003]

Limitations: the rises in A02, A04 and A10 start from only about 10–12 traces in the earlier window, so sampling differences in variant frequencies could account for part of them, and A06's after-window routing and position rest on only about 39–45 traces; the evidence also cannot tell whether the A06 drop and the tightened ordering are one combined rerouting or two separate changes, and an unobserved edge such as A31→A30 is not proof that it was removed from the process.
