### Observed differences

- **A32 and A33 emerge as frequent mid-trace activities.** In the earlier window A33 is never observed and A32 appears in about 1 in 200 traces; in the later window each appears in about 55 in 100 traces, positioned around the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.49). The two usually occur together (about 55 in 100 traces) and in either order, and each precedes A28 in about 55 in 100 traces. [OCC005, OCC006, POS002, POS003, REL014, REL015, REL016]

- **Routing out of A26 is redirected and A27 declines sharply.** Before, about 98 in 100 outgoing transitions from A26 went to A27; in the later window about 44 go to A27, about 33 to A33 and about 20 to A32, with A32 and A33 leading onward mainly to each other and to A28. A27, previously present in every trace, now appears in about 45 in 100, and A28 occurs without A27 in about 55 in 100 traces. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, OCC004, REL006, REL009]

- **The A26→A28 distance lengthens by one step.** It was almost always 2 steps before (about 99 in 100) and is mostly 3 steps in the later window (about 54 in 100), consistent with an activity now sitting between them. [LAG002]

- **Secondary participation shifts.** A04 falls from about 29 to about 11 in 100 traces and, when present, sits earlier in the trace (mean relative position about 0.83 vs 0.61); A23 falls from about 59 to about 41 in 100; A24 rises from about 42 to about 60 in 100. A43's outgoing transitions concentrate on A44 (about 68% to 93%, with A43→A04 nearly disappearing at about 30% to 3%) and the A43→A44 gap shifts toward adjacent positions. [OCC001, POS001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window roughly half of the executions route from A26 through a two-activity fragment, A32 and A33 run together in either order, to A28, instead of through A27; A27, which previously sat on every A26→A28 path, is now executed in about 45 in 100 traces. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL014, REL015, REL016, OCC004, REL009]

The placement evidence is direct and consistent: new successor relations from A26 to A32/A33 and from A32/A33 to A28, co-occurrence of A32 and A33 with both orderings and both preceding A28, a one-step increase in the A26→A28 distance, and a decline in A27 that matches the share of traces taking the new route. The occurrence, routing, position, relation and lag indicators all reflect this same rerouting rather than independent findings, and the number of distinct differences (about five, including the A04, A23, A24 and A43/A44 shifts) clearly exceeds the roughly 0.64 chance-level items the screening anticipates for an unchanged process. [DIR002, LAG002, REL016, OCC004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR005]

Limitations remain: A32 was observed in only 1 earlier trace and A33 in none, so the evidence cannot distinguish a newly introduced fragment from a rare existing path that is now chosen far more often — zero participation only means the activity was not observed. Sampling differences in variant frequencies between the two 200-trace windows are a competing explanation for any single shift, and the participation changes in A04, A23 and A24 are separate behaviours this hypothesis does not explain. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
