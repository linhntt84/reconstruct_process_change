### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, about half of the executions replace the A26→A27→A28 segment with A26 followed by the group A32, A33, A38, A39 (in varying internal order) and then A28, while the remaining executions keep A27. The placement is shown directly by the successor changes at A26 and at the four new activities [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006], by A26 preceding and A28 following the group in about 47 in 100 traces each [REL021, REL024, REL032, REL035], by the near-perfect mutual exclusivity between A27 and the group [REL026, REL029], and by the A26→A28 distance splitting from 2 steps into 2 or 5 steps [LAG002]. Counting items that describe the same traces as one difference, at least two distinct differences remain (this segment change and the A04 shift), clearly more than the roughly one item an unchanged process typically produces in a screening of this size [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003].

Limitations: the internal order of the four activities varies, so the evidence supports a group of activities between A26 and A28 rather than one fixed sequence, and their absence in the earlier window only means they were not observed there [REL036, REL045]. The A04 change is consistently observed, but the evidence does not establish which structural change it corresponds to, and its later profiles rest on only about 22 traces [OCC001, POS001, DIR001]. A competing explanation that two windows cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of variants that already existed earlier at rates too low to appear in 200 traces; the jump from 0 to about 94 in 200 traces for the new activities is far larger than sampling variation alone produces, but a stable new alternative and a changing variant mix look identical here [OCC003, LAG002].
