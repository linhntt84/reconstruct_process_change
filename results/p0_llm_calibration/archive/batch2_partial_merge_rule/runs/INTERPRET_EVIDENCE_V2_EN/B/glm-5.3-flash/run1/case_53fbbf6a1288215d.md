### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19, A21, A22 and A23 each ran in about 20–28 of 100 traces (41–56 of 200) and practically never together; in the later window each appears in only about 1–2 of 100 traces (2–4 of 200) [OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL024, REL040, REL047].
- A20, A28, A29 and A30 go from absent or nearly absent (0–1 of 200 traces) to running in about 985 of 1,000 later traces (197 of 200 each); they co-occur in about 98–99 of 100 later traces, in varying order (each pair splits roughly 45–55 between the two orders) [OCC003, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL035, REL036, REL037, REL056, REL057, REL060].
- A46's outgoing traffic moved from the old group (about 20–28% to each of A19, A21, A22 and A23 before) to the new group (about 19–26% to each of A20, A28, A29 and A30 in the later window); A46 now precedes each new activity in about 97–98 of 100 later traces, where it previously preceded the old activities in about 26–28 of 100 traces [DIR011, REL039, REL059, REL062, REL064, REL031, REL046, REL052].
- Each new activity precedes A31 in about 97–98 of 100 later traces (before, these pairs never co-occurred), A31 sits later in the trace (mean position about 0.40 to 0.46), and the typical gap from A46 to A31 grows from usually about 2 steps to usually 5 steps (the largest lag measured); the old activities had likewise run before A31 in about 25–28 of 100 traces [REL038, REL058, REL061, REL063, POS012, LAG003, REL030, REL045, REL051].
- The new group occupies mid-trace positions (medians about 0.37–0.39) between A46 (mean about 0.30) and A31 (mean about 0.46), much like the mid-trace band (medians about 0.35–0.36) the old group occupied before [POS005, POS009, POS010, POS011, POS013, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008].
- Secondary shifts: A06 participation halves (about 27 to 13–14 of 100 traces), its occurrences move later (median about 0.17 to 0.28) and its outgoing traffic spreads from about 89% to A15 to about 43% to A15 plus many other targets, while A14 routes to A06 about 6% of the time (down from about 25%) and A14/A15 now co-occur with the new group in about 98–99 of 100 traces [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, REL010, REL017].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the segment between A46 and A31 is filled differently: instead of one of the four mutually exclusive alternatives A19, A21, A22 or A23 (about a quarter of cases each), the group A20, A28, A29 and A30 now runs together in nearly every case on the path from A46 to A31 [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, DIR011].

- The routing change is observed directly at A46, with about 200 outgoing transitions behind each profile, and the new group's placement is observed relationally: A46 precedes each of A20, A28, A29 and A30, and each of them precedes A31, in about 97–98 of 100 later traces [DIR011, REL039, REL059, REL062, REL064, REL038, REL058, REL061, REL063].
- The reading is consistent across occurrence, routing, co-occurrence, position and lag indicators and comprises several distinct differences (collapse of the old alternatives, emergence and routing of the new group, A31's later position), not one isolated item [OCC002, OCC007, DIR011, POS012, LAG003].
- An important competing explanation is an extreme shift in variant frequencies between the two finite 200-trace samples: zero participation before means only that A20, A28, A29 and A30 were not observed earlier, and rare variants can appear in one window only; a swing from 0–1 to 197 of 200 traces is unlikely by sampling alone, especially matched by A46's rerouting, but it cannot be fully excluded [OCC003, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, DIR011].
- The detailed redirections measured at the old activities themselves (for example A21 to A27, A22 to A14/A25/A37/A41, A23 to A11/A41) rest on only 2–4 later observations each and should not be read as established new paths [DIR005, DIR006, DIR007].
- The evidence shows where the new group runs, not whether it serves the same purpose as the old alternatives; A06's halved participation and the A14/A15 shifts fit the same restructuring, but their connection is not established by the data [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, REL010, REL017].
