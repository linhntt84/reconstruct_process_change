### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the part of the process surrounding A19 and A20 executes a different activity block: A21–A24, which previously ran together in about 83 in 100 traces, now run in about 28 in 100, A25–A28 have risen from about 18 to about 73 in 100, and A20 now typically continues to A28 rather than to A24. A related shift removes A04 from its earlier role: A04 appears in about 16 in 100 traces (previously about 55), and A31 now passes to A19 directly in about 84 in 100 outgoing transitions instead of splitting between A19 and A04. [OCC004, OCC008, DIR003, DIR004, OCC002, REL013, REL017]

This goes beyond chance: the screening expected about 1.1 qualifying items if nothing had changed, while the evidence contains roughly five distinct differences (the A21–A24 decline, the A25–A28 rise, the A04 decline, the rerouting at A20 and A31, plus the small rises in A02 and A08). The participation and routing changes are direct and rest on large supports — for example 166 vs 56 traces for A21 and 200 vs 202 outgoing transitions for A20 — and the relational items flip in a consistent mirror-image way rather than isolating a single rare variant. [OCC004, OCC008, DIR003, DIR004, OCC001]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of pre-existing process variants — for instance two variants, one containing A21–A24 and one containing A25–A28, whose relative frequencies changed — rather than a modification of the routing itself; the evidence also cannot show whether the change was abrupt or gradual. The small increases in A02 and A08 rest on only 27 and 24 traces and could reflect sampling differences. [OCC001, OCC003, REL013, REL017]
