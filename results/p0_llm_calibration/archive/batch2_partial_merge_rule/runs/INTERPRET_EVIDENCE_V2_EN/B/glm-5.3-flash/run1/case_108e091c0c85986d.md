### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A32 appeared in about 52 of 100 traces and A33 and A34 in about 48 of 100 each, and A32 never occurred together with A33 or A34: each trace contained either A32 or the pair A33+A34. In the later window, all three activities appear in every trace, roughly once per trace each [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL013, REL014, REL015].
- A25's observed outgoing transitions changed from almost always leading to A26 (about 99 in 100) to leading about equally often to A33 and A34 (about 49 in 100 each); A26 now follows A25 about three steps later instead of immediately after it [DIR001, LAG001].
- A33 and A34 moved towards the start of the trace: they now follow A25 within one to two steps (before: four to five steps) and precede A26 in about 97–99 of 100 traces, whereas before A26 preceded them in roughly half of the traces [LAG003, LAG004, REL005, REL006].
- A28's observed outgoing transitions changed from a spread over A32 (about half), A34 (about a quarter) and A33 (about a fifth) to A32 in about 97–98 of 100 cases; A29 now follows A28 two steps later in about 96 of 100 traces, with A32 preceding A29 in about 98–99 of 100 [DIR002, LAG008, REL010].
- A33 and A34 now transition mainly to A26 and to each other (about 48–50 in 100 each), where before they led mostly to A29 (about 55 and 44 in 100); A29 now lies five or more steps after A33 and A34 rather than one to two [DIR003, DIR004, LAG011, LAG012].
- A33 and A34 also now precede A28 (about 99 in 100 traces) where before A28 preceded them in roughly half of the traces; mean relative positions moved earlier for A33 and A34 (about 0.53 to about 0.44) and later for A26, A28 and A29 (about 0.43 to 0.49, 0.50 to 0.55, and 0.58 to 0.62) [REL008, REL009, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS005, POS006].
- A few lag results for the pairs A26–A33, A26–A34, A28–A33 and A28–A34 rest on a single later-window trace each and were not used to draw conclusions [LAG006, LAG007, LAG009, LAG010].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the earlier mutually exclusive choice — each trace running either A32 or the pair A33+A34 — was replaced by a structure in which all three activities run in every trace: A33 and A34 immediately after A25 and before A26 (in either order), and A32 after A28, with A29 following A32. The support is direct and consistent: co-occurrence of A32 with A33 and A34 rose from zero to universal, the observed orders of A33/A34 versus A26 and A28 flipped, and the successors of A25, A28, A33 and A34 changed accordingly; these are about three distinct behavioural differences, clearly more than the 0.65 items expected by chance when nothing has changed, and each item's difference exceeds those seen in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces [OCC001, REL013, DIR001, DIR002, REL005, REL010, LAG003, LAG008].

Limitations: positions and distances are event positions, not clock times, so the description concerns order and spacing rather than timing; transitions that are no longer observed (A25 to A26, A33/A34 to A29) may still occur rarely; and the evidence cannot fully rule out that the two windows sampled different mixes of variants of one unchanged process — although that would require a variant absent from all 200 earlier traces to account for all 200 later traces, which the coherent direction of the order flips makes implausible [OCC001, DIR003, DIR004, REL005].
