### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, cases run exactly one of four alternative activities — A14, A15, A20 or A21 — between A37 and A22, where previously A37 was followed directly by A22; the four behave as exclusive one-per-case alternatives, each taken in roughly a fifth to a third of cases. [DIR005, LAG001, REL004, REL005, REL010]

The placement is shown directly by relational evidence on both sides of the new step and by the A37-to-A22 distance growing from one to two positions in all traces, corroborated by the complete rerouting of A37's outgoing transitions and by the new activities appearing with solid support (42–61 traces each) after complete absence from 200 earlier traces. Screening expected only about 0.4 chance qualifications across the 82 tested units, and the qualifying items here all describe this one coherent difference rather than scattered effects. [REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009, LAG001, DIR005, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Limitations: the windows are finite samples, so the appearance of these activities only in the later window could in principle reflect sampling differences in variant frequencies, although 0 of 200 earlier traces against 42–61 of 200 later traces makes a stable rare variant unlikely. Zero participation earlier also means only that the activities were not observed then, so the evidence cannot distinguish a newly added step from a pre-existing optional path that simply was not taken before; and because the items share the same traces, they corroborate a single underlying difference rather than confirming it independently. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
