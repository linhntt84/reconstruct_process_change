### Observed differences

- In the later window, each of the eight activities A33, A34, A36, A37, A38, A39, A40 and A41 appears in only about 50–52 in 100 traces (mean occurrences per trace about 0.5), down from essentially 100 in 100 traces (mean about 1.0) in the earlier window; A30 still appears in every trace in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, POS001]
- In the earlier window these eight activities always ran together. In the later window they form two mutually exclusive groups: A33, A34, A36 and A37 occur together in about 50 in 100 traces (both absent in about 48), A38, A39, A40 and A41 occur together in about 50 in 100 traces (both absent in about 48–50), and an activity from the first group co-occurs with one from the second group in at most about 3 in 100 traces — so almost every later-window case contains exactly one of the two groups. [REL017, REL024, REL030, REL039, REL042, REL044, REL020, REL026, REL029, REL035, REL038]
- A30 and A31 still frame the executed activities: in the later window each of them co-occurs with each of the eight activities in about 50 in 100 traces, with nearly all remaining traces containing A30 or A31 alone, meaning both remain present in virtually every case. [REL001, REL004, REL008, REL009, REL012, REL016]
- Routing followed the split: in the later window A33, A34, A36 and A37 no longer lead to A38–A41 (earlier roughly 20–30 in 100 of their observed outgoing transitions combined, now essentially none) and instead lead to A31 in about 21–28 in 100 of those transitions; A30's outgoing transitions, about 96 in 100 of which went to A33/A34/A36/A37 earlier, now divide roughly evenly between the two groups. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- Relative event positions shifted consistently with shorter sub-blocks: A38–A41, earlier seen at the maximum measured distance (5 event steps) after A30 and within 5 steps in only about 15–30 in 100 traces, now follow A30 within 1–4 steps in nearly every trace containing them; A31, earlier about 5 steps after A33/A34/A36/A37, now follows within 1–4 steps; mean relative positions of A33/A34/A36/A37 moved later (about 0.65 to 0.73–0.74) and of A39/A40/A41 earlier (about 0.76–0.77 to 0.72–0.73). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG008, LAG013, LAG017, LAG021, POS003, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]
- Several lag comparisons across the two groups rest on only 1–2 later-window observations and carry little weight. [LAG006, LAG007, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG018, LAG020]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the fixed block of eight activities A33, A34, A36, A37, A38, A39, A40 and A41 — previously executed in full in every case between A30 (before) and A31 (after) — now runs as one of two mutually exclusive sub-blocks, A33/A34/A36/A37 in about half of the cases and A38/A39/A40/A41 in the other half, with A30 and A31 still framing whichever sub-block executes. [REL020, REL026, REL029, REL017, REL039, OCC001, OCC005, DIR001, LAG001, LAG005]

The support is direct and consistent: the co-occurrence reversal (from 100 in 100 to at most about 3 in 100 across all sixteen cross-group pairs, while within-group pairs stay near 50 with "neither" near 48, exactly matching the halved participation) is direct relational evidence of exclusivity rather than a marginal shift, and the routing and relative-position changes point the same way; with 207 units screened, only about 1 qualifying item would be expected without any change, far fewer than the coherent pattern observed, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [REL017, REL030, REL044, DIR002, DIR005, POS001, LAG005]

Limitations: the two windows are finite samples and variant frequencies can differ between them without a structural change; a rise in the frequency of a previously unobserved split variant, or a shift in the mix of cases reaching the process, could produce a similar picture and cannot be ruled out from these windows alone — although a pattern absent from all 200 earlier traces and present in about 100 of 200 later traces is not plausibly pure sampling noise. The evidence also cannot show what determines which sub-block a case takes, the exact internal ordering within each sub-block, and the cross-group lag items with 1–2 observations add little. [REL020, LAG006, LAG012, LAG020]
