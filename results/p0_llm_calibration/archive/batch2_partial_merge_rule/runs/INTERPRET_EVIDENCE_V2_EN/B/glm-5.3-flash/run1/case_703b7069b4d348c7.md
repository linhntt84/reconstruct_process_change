### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the segment A20–A28–A29–A30 that previously ran in every case between A46 and A31 is no longer executed, that about a quarter of cases instead run one or more of A19, A21, A22 and A23 into A31, and that A04 has become a frequent step between A15 and A16. The participation changes are large and well supported (A30 in 200 of 200 earlier cases and none later; A19 in none earlier and 53 of 200 later; A04 in 36 versus 136 cases), the routing changes are direct (about 96 in 100 of A46's outgoing transitions now lead to the new activities, and about 90 in 100 of A04's lead to A16), and the relational evidence shows the new order directly (A04 precedes A31 and A46 in about 62% of later cases; A46 reaches A31 in about 2 steps instead of 5). Occurrence, routing, position and relation indicators agree, and even counting items about the same activities as one difference, the distinct differences clearly exceed the roughly 1.14 items expected by chance when screening 230 units. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC011, OCC002, DIR015, DIR002, REL011, LAG009, LAG001]

Competing explanations remain: the evidence cannot distinguish a rewiring of a single flow from a shift in the mix of pre-existing trace variants — the later window may simply contain more cases of a variant without the old block, which would look identical at this level — and zero participation only shows that an activity was not observed in that window. The rerouting details for the low-frequency activities A02, A08 and A10 rest on about 13–19 outgoing transitions in the later window, and the many occurrence, relation and position entries for A20, A28, A29 and A30 describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations.
