### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, one of two near-exclusive alternative steps — A19 in about 46 in 100 cases, A20 in about 54 in 100 — is executed between A40 and A25, where previously A40 was followed directly by A25 in about 90 in 100 traces. The placement is shown directly by relational and positional evidence, not inferred from participation alone: A40's successors shifted from A25 to A19/A20, A19 and A20 now lead almost exclusively to A25, each appears after A40 and before A25 in the traces where it occurs, and the A40-to-A25 distance shifted from one step to two. Screening tested 93 units and would be expected to yield about 0.46 qualifying items if nothing had changed; the 14 items here collapse to this single coherent difference rather than many unrelated ones, and every item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits, making chance a poor explanation. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, POS001, POS002, POS003, OCC001, OCC002]

Limitations remain. The before-side routing profiles of A19 and A20 rest on only 1 and 2 observed transitions (and their earlier positions on 1–2 events), so what these activities fed into previously is weakly established. The evidence also cannot fully rule out that the two windows sample different variant mixes of an unchanged process — finite samples can differ in variant frequency — although a rise from about 0.5–1 in 100 to about 46–56 in 100 traces, with consistent relational shifts, is far outside what random re-splits produce. DIR shares are computed over outgoing transitions rather than cases, and POS/LAG use event positions rather than clock time. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS002, LAG001]
