### Observed differences

- In the later window, A16, A17, A18 and A19 each appear in about 77 in 100 traces (about 154 of 200), up from about 22 in 100 (about 43 of 200) before; when present they occur once per trace, with no repetition in either window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Conversely, A20, A21, A22 and A23 each drop from about 79 in 100 traces (about 157 of 200) before to about 23 in 100 (about 46 of 200) in the later window. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]
- The outgoing transitions of A15 reverse: before, about 79 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions led to A23 and about 21 in 100 to A19; in the later window, about 77 in 100 lead to A19 and about 23 in 100 to A23. [DIR001]
- The two groups of four activities never occur together in either window — pairs such as A16 with A20 show only "one or the other" — while A15 and A27 are present in all traces, with A27 co-occurring with whichever group runs. [REL012, REL001, REL016, REL038]
- Within the A19-led group, A19 precedes A16, A17 and A18 in about 77 in 100 later traces, while A16, A17 and A18 keep a varying mutual order; the corresponding orderings in the A20–A23 group (for example A23 before A20) shrink in step with that group's reduced participation. [REL011, REL018, REL024, REL009, REL037]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the either/or routing after A15 reversed between the windows: in the later window, roughly three in four executions continue from A15 into the branch containing A19 (together with A16, A17 and A18, alongside A27), whereas before, roughly four in five continued into the branch containing A23 (together with A20, A21 and A22). [DIR001, OCC001, OCC005, REL016, REL038]

Supporting signs: the routing change at A15 is observed directly, and its shares (about 79/21 before, about 77/23 after) match the participation shifts of the two activity groups almost exactly; the two groups remain mutually exclusive in both windows, which fits a single branch choice rather than two independent skip decisions; and the pattern is consistent across the occurrence, routing and relational indicators, each with an empirical strength of about 0.995 — a swing of roughly 110 traces per activity, far beyond what random re-splits of 200-trace windows produce, against a screening expectation of only about 0.67 qualifying items without change. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC005, REL012, REL016]

Limitations: the evidence shows that the branch frequencies flipped, not why — the two windows are finite samples, and a shift in the mix of cases or variant frequencies entering the later window, without any change to the process itself, would produce the same pattern and cannot be ruled out. The within-branch orderings (for example the varying order of A16, A17 and A18) look similar in both windows, so what is directly supported is the branch selection, not any rearrangement inside the branches. [REL009, REL017, DIR001]
