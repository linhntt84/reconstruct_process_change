### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured in two coherent ways. First, the segment A14-A04-A15 is now frequently executed without A04: A14 passes directly to A15 in about 80 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, A04's participation fell from about 72 to about 24 in 100 traces, and A15 now typically occurs without A04 while still leading to A16 as before. Second, the block A19, A20, A25, A26, which previously converged on A27 and was fed by A46, now runs after A28/A29 and before A33: its order relative to A27/A28 reversed, A28/A29 fan out to it at lag 1-2, it reaches A33 at lag 1-2, and A46 now routes almost entirely to A27. A02, A06 and A10 also participate more often, though the evidence does not tie them clearly to either change. [OCC002, DIR003, REL004, DIR005, DIR009, DIR011, REL016, LAG015, LAG012, OCC001]

Two features take this beyond chance. The number of distinct differences (at least two, each spanning several activities and indicator types) clearly exceeds the roughly 1 item expected among the 192 tested units if nothing had changed, and the decisive evidence is relational and direct: the order flips between the block and A27/A28 are computed on all 200 traces in both windows, and the successor changes for A19/A20/A25/A26 rest on about 42-58 outgoing transitions per window, too coherent to be a sampling artifact. [REL016, REL022, DIR005, DIR008, OCC002, DIR003]

Limitations remain. The windows are finite samples, so a shift in the mix of process variants — for instance one variant containing A04 and the block-before-A27 becoming rarer while a variant without them becomes commoner — could produce similar aggregate differences, and the evidence cannot distinguish a single relocation of the block from such a variant-mix change. The before-side lag profiles of the new adjacencies rest on very few observations (often one), and A04's low participation in the later window means only that it was rarely observed, not that it was removed from the process. [LAG011, LAG012, LAG015, LAG018, OCC002]
