### Observed differences

- The main difference concerns the segment between A21 and A23. In the earlier window, A21, A22 and A23 each appeared in all 200 traces, A21 was always followed by A22, A22 always preceded A23, and the distance from A21 to A23 was always 2 events. [OCC003, DIR001, REL001, REL004, LAG001]

- In the later window, A22 appears in only about 46 in 100 traces, and A21's outgoing transitions are redistributed: roughly 46 in 100 still go to A22, about 34 in 100 go to A28 and about 21 in 100 to A27. A21 and A23 still appear in every trace, and A21's mean event position barely moves (about 0.46 to 0.44). [OCC003, DIR001, REL001, REL004, POS001]

- Two activities that were absent from all 200 earlier traces, A27 and A28, each occur in about 55 in 100 later traces (109 of 200). They always occur together and never occur alongside A22; A28 precedes A27 in about 34 in 100 traces, A27 precedes A28 in about 21 in 100. [OCC004, OCC005, REL009, REL005, REL006]

- The new pair sits between A21 and A23: A21 precedes A27/A28, and A27/A28 precede A23, each in about 55 in 100 later traces. The A21→A23 distance is now 3 events in about 55 in 100 traces (2 in the rest), and A23 runs without A22 in about 55 in 100 traces. Within the block, A27 is followed by A23 in about 62 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (A28 in the remainder), and A28 is followed by A27 in about 62 in 100 (A23 in the remainder). [REL002, REL003, REL007, REL008, LAG001, REL004, DIR002, DIR003]

- Participation also moved in opposite directions elsewhere: A18 fell from about 59 to about 41 in 100 traces, while A19 rose from about 42 to about 60 in 100; no directional or relational evidence covers these two activities. [OCC001, OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, about half of the executions run A21 → A28 → A27 → A23 (roughly two thirds of those cases, about 34 in 100 traces) or A21 → A27 → A28 → A23 (about 21 in 100 traces) in place of the earlier A21 → A22 → A23 segment: A22 is not executed in those cases, and the new pair A27/A28 runs between A21 and A23. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL002, REL003, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

The support is direct and internally consistent: the rerouting is shown by relational and successor evidence, not only by participation counts; the quantities fit together (A22's 46-in-100 participation plus the pair's 55-in-100 participation account for all traces, the A21→A23 distance grows from 2 to 3 in exactly the share of traces where the pair runs, and A27's and A28's outgoing shares match the two orderings of the block); and the key profiles rest on substantial samples (109 of 200 later traces versus 0 of 200 before). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, REL009, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

For context, 65 units were screened and only about 0.32 qualifying items would be expected by chance without a change; the items here reduce to about three distinct differences (the A21–A23 rerouting, the A18 shift and the A19 shift), and the many items on A21, A22, A23, A27 and A28 reflect that one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, REL001, LAG001]

A separate, weaker observation is the opposite participation movement of A18 (down) and A19 (up), consistent with two alternative activities being executed at shifted frequencies; only the participation change itself is established for them, since no routing or order evidence covers them. [OCC001, OCC002]

Competing explanations remain: the windows are finite samples, so part of the A18/A19 swing could reflect variant-frequency sampling rather than a structural change, and a genuinely rare A27/A28 variant missed by the 200 earlier traces cannot be fully excluded — though a pair absent from 200 traces and then present in 109 makes pure sampling an unlikely complete explanation for the rerouting. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, OCC005]
