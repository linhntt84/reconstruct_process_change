### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a reversal of order between a group of four activities (A30, A31, A36, A37) and A21/A22. In the earlier window, A21 preceded each of the four in 100 in 100 traces, and A22 likewise preceded each of the four in 100 in 100 traces; in the later window, each of the four precedes A21 in about 98 in 100 traces and precedes A22 in about 98 in 100 traces. All six activities still occur together in every trace in both windows. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

- Routing at the edges of this group moved with it. In the earlier window, every outgoing transition of A20 went to A22; in the later window, A22 takes only about 2% of A20's outgoing transitions, while A30, A31, A36 and A37 take about 22–26% each. A21 previously spread its outgoing transitions evenly over the four (about 25% each); in the later window, about 98% go directly to A25. Each of the four used to hand over to A25 in roughly one case in four (22–32%) and never to A22; in the later window, each hands over to A22 in roughly 19–30% of cases and practically never to A25. All of these routing profiles are based on all 200 traces per window. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

- Relative positions shifted in the same direction: in the later window, A21 sits later in the trace (mean relative position about 0.62, up from about 0.51) and A22 later (about 0.48, up from about 0.36), while each of the four sits earlier (about 0.41, down from about 0.58). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

- Step distances match a four-activity group changing place: A20 reached A22 at distance 1 in 100% of traces before and at distance 5 in about 98% after (four events in between), while A21 reached A25 at distance 5 in 100% of traces before and at distance 1 in about 98% after. [LAG001, LAG002]

- The remaining items are minor: small redistributions of within-group step distances (changes of roughly 0.07–0.09 on that scale for the pairs A36–A31 and A37–A31), and a few lag items involving A21, A36, A37 and A25 that rest on only 4 after-window observations and carry little weight. [LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the four activities A30, A31, A36 and A37 run between A20 and A22, whereas in the earlier window they ran between A21 and A25. The rest of the observed sequence keeps its relative order: A20 precedes A22 in both windows, A22 sits before A21 in both windows (mean relative position about 0.37 vs 0.51 before, 0.48 vs 0.62 after), and A25 follows A21 in both windows. Within the group of four, sequencing appears unordered in both windows — each activity hands over to the other three and to the group's exit in roughly equal shares — so the difference concerns the group's placement, not its internal order. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG002, POS001, POS002]

Supporting signs: the order reversal is observed directly on activity pairs rather than inferred from positions alone; the successor changes at both ends of the group, the position shifts and the distance changes all point the same way; the strong items rest on all 200 traces per window; and every item's difference exceeds all but about 1 in 200 random re-splits of the same data (empirical strength about 0.995). The 28 items describe essentially one underlying difference — at most two if the new direct A21→A25 edge is counted separately — which is clearly more than the 0.48 items expected by chance from the 96 tested units when nothing has changed. [REL001, REL005, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish "the group of four moved earlier" from "the A22–A21 segment moved later", since both describe the same observed reversal, and it does not show what (if anything) lies between A22 and A21. It also cannot rule out that both orderings existed as process variants before and after, with the mix shifting from essentially only the late-group ordering to essentially only the early-group ordering; with near-deterministic relations inside each window, such a variant-mix shift and a within-trace reordering look identical here. The lag items based on only 4 after-window observations were not relied on. [REL001, REL005, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG007]
