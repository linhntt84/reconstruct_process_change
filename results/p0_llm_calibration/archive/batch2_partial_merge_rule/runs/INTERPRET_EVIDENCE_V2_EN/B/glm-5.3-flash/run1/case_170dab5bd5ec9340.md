### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that control flow around A27 was re-routed and that A06 took on a far larger, earlier role. In the later window, A19, A20, A25 and A26 — which previously handed over to A33 or A29 about equally often — hand over almost exclusively to A27 and now run before it rather than after it; A46, which previously led directly to A27, now leads into A25/A26/A19/A20, which then reach A27; A28 and A29 now lead almost exclusively onward to A29 and A33; and A06 is executed in about four to five times as many cases, much earlier in the trace, usually followed immediately by A16 and typically preceding A27/A28/A29/A33, with A15 now sometimes leading into A06. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL010, REL014, DIR009, LAG018, DIR007, DIR008, OCC001, POS001, DIR001, LAG001, LAG004]

Supporting signs: the routing changes are read directly from the successor distributions of each source activity (45–63 outgoing transitions behind the A19/A20/A25/A26 profiles, about 200 for A28, A29 and A46), the order flip is observed on full 200-trace windows, and the positional shifts point the same way; the items cluster into only about three distinct behavioural differences, but that still clearly exceeds the roughly 0.8 items expected by chance in an unchanged process, and each item's difference is larger than about 99.5 in 100 of random re-splits. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, REL010, REL014, POS002, POS006, OCC001]

Limitations: the before-picture of A06's outgoing transitions rests on only 13 traces, and several after-profiles of the step-distance distributions rest on 1–2 observations, so those details are fragile; the tightening of the A28→A29→A33 sequence may be a knock-on effect of the re-routing rather than a separate change; and a shift in the mix of already existing variants between the two windows could account for part of these differences, so this remains a possibility supported by the evidence rather than a confirmed modification.
