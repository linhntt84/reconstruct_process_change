### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window runs two routings that were rare or absent before: A14 hands over to A02, which then leads to A15, and A26 is followed by the new activity pair A32/A33, which leads to A28 while bypassing A27, each in roughly half of the traces. [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]

Several signs point at these routings themselves rather than at plain frequency shifts: the successor shares at A14 and A26 changed on the transitions; the order evidence agrees (A14 before A02 in about 54 in 100 traces, up from about 19; A02 before A15 in about 35 in 100, up from about 1; A32 and A33 each before A28 in about 54 in 100); the positional and lag evidence matches an insertion (A02 moving early, the A14-to-A15 and A26-to-A28 distances each growing by about one step); and A27 co-occurs with A32/A33 in almost no traces, so its drop from every trace to about 46 in 100 behaves like a bypassed route. With about four distinct differences against roughly 0.72 items expected by chance in this screening, a pattern of this size is unusual for an unchanged process. [REL001, REL002, REL024, REL025, REL020, REL021, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, OCC006]

Limitations: the remaining shifts rest on participation counts only and are not tied to any specific routing point; A04 and A10 (about 9 after-window traces each) and A08's routing mix (about 13) are thinly supported, and the A23/A24 flip could reflect sampling differences in variant frequencies between two 200-trace windows rather than a structural change. Sampling cannot plausibly explain A32 appearing in 0 of 200 and then 109 of 200 traces, though zero participation before only means A32 was not observed in that window. Directional shares are computed over outgoing transitions rather than cases, and the many relation items about A02 and about A32/A33 each describe one underlying behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR002, OCC007]
