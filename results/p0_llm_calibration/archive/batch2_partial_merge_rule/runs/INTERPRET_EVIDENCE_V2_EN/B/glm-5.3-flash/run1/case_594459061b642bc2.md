### Observed differences

- **Five activities now run in only half the cases.** Before, A27, A36, A37, A42 and A43 each appeared in essentially every case (100 in 100); in the later window each appears in about 50 in 100 cases, with about one occurrence per containing case in both windows. A25, A26, A28 and A31 still appear in every case in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005]

- **A27 and the group A36/A37/A42/A43 have become mutually exclusive.** In the later window, A27 co-occurs with each group member in only about 1–2 in 100 cases (down from 100 in 100), even though each of them is present in about half of all cases. The four group members still appear together (about 51 in 100) and are jointly absent (about 48–49 in 100), so they behave as one block. [REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL025, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030]

- **The group changed place.** Before, it ran between A25 and A28: A25 preceded each member in about 97–98 in 100 cases, each member preceded A28 in about 97–98 in 100, and A25 and A28 were 5 steps apart in essentially every case. In the later window, about 98 in 100 of A25's outgoing transitions go directly to A28, and the A25→A28 distance is 1 step in about 98 in 100 cases. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, DIR001, LAG001]

- **The group now sits between A26 and A31.** Before, A26 came after each group member in about 98–99 in 100 cases; in the later window A26 precedes each member in about 49–50 in 100, while each member still precedes A31 in about 49–51 in 100. A26's outgoing transitions, previously almost all to A27 (99 in 100), now split between A27 (about 48 in 100) and the group members (about 49 in 100 combined), and the group members' successors now include A31 (about 22–26 in 100 each), which was absent before; A27 still leads to A31 in about 97 in 100. - **Positions and gaps shifted consistently.** In relative event position, the group's mean moved from about 0.40–0.41 to about 0.58–0.59, A27's from about 0.64 to about 0.58, A28's from about 0.47 to about 0.39 and A26's from about 0.61 to about 0.53; the A26→A31 gap, previously 2 steps in about 97 in 100 cases, is now 2 steps in about half the cases and 5 steps in about 48 in 100, matching cases with A27 versus the group in between. - Several lag items (for example A25 with A37, or A36 with A28) rest on only 1–2 observations in one window and are not relied on here. [LAG003, LAG007, LAG008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, each case takes one of two mutually exclusive branches between A26 and A31 — one containing A27 alone, the other containing the group A36, A37, A42 and A43 — in roughly equal shares (about 50 in 100 each). Before, every case contained both A27 and the whole group, with the group placed between A25 and A28; in the later window the group runs only after A26 and before A31, A25 passes directly to A28, and A27 keeps its former neighbours (A26 and A28 before it, A31 after it). The group's internal order remains mixed in both windows, and its order before A31 is unchanged. [REL006, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL017, REL021, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

The support is direct and consistent: the exclusivity is observed within the later window itself (A27 and each group member each present in about 50 in 100 cases yet co-occurring in only about 1–2 in 100), a pattern that sampling differences between the two windows cannot produce; the successor changes at A25, A26 and the group members and the position and gap shifts all describe the same structure, and each item's difference exceeds those of about 199 in 200 random re-splits. The distinct differences (halved participation, exclusive routing, relocation of the group) clearly exceed the roughly 1 item expected by chance among the 166 units tested. [REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, LAG001, LAG006]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show what determines which branch a case takes or whether the split is exactly balanced, and it cannot rule out that the later window is a mixture of two case variants (one with A27, one with the group) rather than a single process with a new choice; the small residual co-occurrence of about 1–2 in 100 cases is unexplained, and the exact branch point after A26 is inferred from successor and position data rather than observed directly. [REL013, REL016, DIR002, POS002]
