### Observed differences

- In the later window, four activities that were not observed at all in the earlier window — A19, A20, A25 and A26 — each take part in roughly 21–31 in 100 traces (about 42–62 of 200 traces). They essentially never occur together (co-occurrence at or near zero for every tested pair), so together they appear in essentially every later trace. [OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL024, REL034, REL035, REL043]
- These new activities sit near the middle of the trace (mean position about 0.51) and are spliced into the A28 → A29 → A30 stretch: in the later window about 49 of 100 of A28's outgoing transitions and about 47 of 100 of A29's go to one of them, while the new activities' own outgoing transitions go almost exclusively to A29 or A30. [DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011]
- Event distances point to a single inserted step: A28→A29 and A29→A30 each moved from almost always 1 step apart to roughly 50/50 at 1 and 2 steps, while A28→A30 moved from 2 steps apart in about 96 of 100 cases to 3 steps apart in about 93 of 100. [LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]
- A04 participation fell from about 60 in 100 traces to about 19 in 100 (119 → 37 traces), its mean position moved earlier (about 0.85 → 0.54), and its outgoing transitions shifted from about 92 in 100 going to A46 to only about 17 in 100, now spread over many targets. [OCC002, POS001, DIR002]
- The late fragment A45 → A04 → A46 largely disappeared: A45 now goes directly to A46 in about 81 of 100 of its occurrences (before about 25 in 100), A45→A04 fell from about 75 to about 4 in 100, and the A45→A46, A45→A47 and A45→A48 distances each shortened by about one step. [DIR013, LAG013, LAG014, LAG015]
- A02, A06, A08 and A10 each became more common, from roughly 2–7 in 100 traces to about 17–21 in 100, with broadly spread and changed outgoing profiles. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR012]
- Smaller shifts consistent with the above: A30's mean event position moved slightly later (about 0.54 → 0.56) and A45 occurs slightly more often (about 59 → 65 in 100 traces). [POS006, POS007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the process executes a new mutually exclusive step — exactly one of A19, A20, A25 or A26 per case, inserted into the A28 → A29 → A30 stretch (directly after A28 in about half the cases and directly after A29 in the other half) — and that the late fragment A45 → A04 → A46 is largely replaced by a direct A45 → A46 transition, leaving A04 in about 19 of 100 traces, earlier in the trace and with varied successors. [LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011, DIR013, OCC002, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009]

The placement evidence is relational and directional, not just frequency-based: the new activities' successors are almost only A29 and A30, A28 and A29 now route to them in about half of their outgoing transitions, and the pairwise distances changed exactly as one inserted event would produce; the new activities also follow A14/A15 (in roughly 15–30 of 100 co-occurring traces) and precede A30 and the late activities A45–A48. The A04 change is likewise direct (participation 119 → 37 traces; A45→A04 about 75 → 4 in 100; A04→A46 about 92 → 17 in 100). The distinct differences — the inserted block, the A04 drop, and the increased occurrence of A02/A06/A08/A10 — number about three, clearly more than the roughly 1 item expected by chance at these thresholds, though the many relation items about A04 and about the new activities each describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [DIR010, DIR011, REL016, REL018, REL020, REL022, REL030, REL049, REL050, REL056, REL059, OCC002, DIR002, DIR013]

Limitations: the later-window picture of A04 rests on only about 37 traces (about 42 outgoing transitions), so the wide spread of its new successors may partly reflect sampling; and the role of A02/A06/A08/A10 is not established — their outgoing profiles are diffuse and the earlier-window profiles rest on very few transitions (A10 had only 2), so their increased participation cannot be tied to a specific placement. Sampling differences in variant frequencies remain a competing explanation for small-share successors and for the exact 50/50 split of the insertion position, but they cannot plausibly explain activities going from absent in 200 traces to about 21–31 in 100, or the one-step distance shifts.
