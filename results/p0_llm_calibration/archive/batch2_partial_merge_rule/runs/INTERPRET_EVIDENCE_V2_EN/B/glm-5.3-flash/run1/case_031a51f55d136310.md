### Observed differences

- In the later window, A08 takes part in about 10 in 100 cases, down from about 33 in 100, and its mean occurrences per trace fall from about 0.33 to about 0.11 [OCC001].
- When A08 does occur, it sits later in the case: the median relative position moves from about 0.22 to about 0.43 and the upper quartile from about 0.29 to about 0.87 [POS001]. Its outgoing transitions are also far more dispersed: A16 receives about 20% of them versus about 83% before, with the rest spread over many activities (A44 about 15%; A15, A46 and A33 about 10% each) [DIR001], and A15→A08 transitions drop from about 17% to about 1% of A15's outgoing transitions [DIR002].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, cases are routed through a fixed sequence A29 → A21/A22 → A23/A24 → A30, replacing an earlier pattern in which A21/A22 often proceeded straight to A30 and A29 fed into A23/A24 as often as into A21/A22; in addition, A08 is executed in far fewer cases, appears later in the case, and its former precedence before A23/A24 and its A08→A16 and A15→A08 transitions have largely disappeared. The supporting signs are direct: each transition-share change is backed by roughly 90–115 outgoing transitions, the lag shifts agree with the rerouting mechanism (direct pairs move from distance 1 to distance 2 exactly where an intermediate activity now sits, and A23/A24→A30 become distance-1), and every difference is larger than almost all random re-splits of the pooled traces produce [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG005, LAG006, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013, LAG014, OCC001, POS001, REL003, REL004, DIR002].

The evidence cannot rule out that the two finite windows simply sampled a shifted mix of existing variants, which would produce the same pattern without a structural change; the A08 findings rest on only about 21 traces and about 20 outgoing transitions in the later window (with the A08 lag items based on just 7 and 4 pairs), so its successor dispersion and position shift are weakly supported, and the many A08 items describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations; POS and LAG use event positions, so "later" refers to event order, not clock time [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, LAG001, LAG003].
