### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, every case executes the flow segment containing A27 — entered from A40, A14, A15 or A26 and leading on to A28, A45 and A46 — while the alternative segment containing A32 and A33 is no longer executed, and A02 runs in far fewer cases, only within traces that also contain A27. [REL016, REL019, REL025, REL028, REL031, REL032, REL033, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC001]

The placement of A27 is shown directly by relational evidence: co-occurrence with seven partners rose from about 48 in 100 traces to all 200 with a stable ordering, consistent with A26's rerouting onto A27, and the participation changes are large and directly measured (A27 from 95 to 200 traces, A32 from 107 to 2, A33 from 105 to 1, A02 from 119 to 48). The distinct differences — A27 becoming universal, A32/A33 disappearing, A02 shrinking, and A04/A06/A10 rising — clearly exceed the roughly one item expected by chance (about 0.93 of 186 units tested), and the many A02-pair relations reflect that single participation drop rather than independent confirmations. [REL016, REL019, REL025, REL028, REL031, REL032, REL033, DIR005, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply sample different mixes of pre-existing process variants rather than a changed structure; A32 and A33's absence means only that they were not observed later, their new successor profiles rest on 2 and 1 outgoing transitions and cannot be interpreted, and A04's rerouting rests on 10 versus 41 outgoing transitions.
