### Observed differences

- In the later window, A19 and A20 are almost never executed: A19 appears in about 1 of 200 traces (previously about 91 of 200) and A20 in about 1 of 200 (previously about 109 of 200). Before, every trace contained exactly one of the two and never both; in the later window about 99 in 100 traces contain neither. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001]
- In the later window, A26, A27 and A28 occur almost entirely without A19 or A20: pairs such as A19–A26 or A20–A28 co-occurred in roughly 45–55 of 100 traces before, whereas in the later window about 995 of 1000 traces contain only the partner activity. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007]
- In the later window, routing bypasses A19 and A20: about 97 of 100 observed outgoing transitions of A26 now go to A27 (previously about 45 to A27 and about 50 combined to A19/A20), and about 97 of 100 outgoing transitions of A27 now go to A28 (previously about 51 to A28 and about 48 combined to A19/A20). A26 and A27 themselves still occur at the same rate, with about 200 outgoing transitions observed in each window. [DIR003, DIR004]
- Before, A19 and A20 themselves led almost exclusively to A27 and A28 (about 97 of 100 of their outgoing transitions combined), placing them between A26 and the A27/A28 steps; their after-window successor profiles rest on a single trace each. [DIR001, DIR002]
- In the later window, the remaining path is shorter and more regular: the gap from A26 to A27 is 1 step in about 98 of 100 cases (previously split between 1 and 2 steps), from A26 to A28 it is 2 steps in about 96 of 100 cases (previously 3 steps in about 93 of 100), and from A27 to A28 it is 1 step in about 98 of 100 cases (previously split between 1 and 2). [LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]
- The few items describing A19's or A20's own after-window positions and lags to neighbours each rest on a single trace and carry little weight. [POS001, POS002, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the step executed in every before-window case as either A19 or A20 — a mutually exclusive choice — is no longer executed in the later window, so cases proceed from A26 directly to A27 and then to A28, shortening the A26-to-A28 path by about one step. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: the near-disappearance of both activities, the rerouting of A26 and A27 observed on about 200 outgoing transitions per window, the relational items showing A26/A27/A28 now occurring without A19/A20, and the shortening of the A26-to-A28 distance from about 3 to about 2 steps, which matches the loss of one intermediate step. With only about 0.5 items expected by chance across the 106 tested units, this cluster is well beyond what an unchanged process typically produces, although the items largely reflect one underlying shift rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, DIR003, DIR004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

Two limitations remain. First, zero participation only means A19 and A20 were not observed: one later-window trace still contains each, so the step may be executed rarely rather than having disappeared, and the after-window routing and lag profiles for A19 and A20 themselves rest on a single trace. Second, the evidence cannot rule out a shift in the mix of trace variants between the two finite samples; the magnitude of the drop (from roughly half of traces to 1 in 200, with differences exceeding about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data) makes pure sampling variation unlikely, but a change in the population of cases being executed cannot be excluded. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]
