### Observed differences

- In the before window, eight activities — A33, A34, A36, A37, A38, A39, A40 and A41 — each ran in essentially every trace (about 200 of 200, roughly once per trace). In the later window, each of them runs in only about 50 in 100 traces, still about once per trace when it runs; each estimate rests on about 100 later-window traces [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008].

- The eight activities split into two groups of four: A33/A34/A36/A37 and A38/A39/A40/A41. In the later window, activities of the same group still always appear together (co-occurrence about 50 in 100 traces, neither present in about 49 in 100, one-without-the-other about 1 in 100 or less), while activities from different groups co-occur in only about 1 in 100 traces, down from 100 in 100. Each later-window trace therefore contains exactly one of the two groups [REL017, REL030, REL039, REL044, REL020, REL027, REL035, REL038].

- Before, the order between the groups was fixed: each first-group activity preceded each second-group activity in about 99 in 100 traces; this relation has largely disappeared because the groups no longer co-occur [REL020, REL026, REL031, REL035].

- A30 and A31 still occur in every trace and keep their places around the segment: A30 precedes whichever group runs, and A31 follows it [REL001, REL005, REL009, REL016, POS001, POS002].

- Routing follows the split. In the later window, about half of A30's outgoing transitions go directly to A38, A39, A40 or A41 (about 10–15 in 100 each), which were almost never direct successors before; and A33, A34, A36 and A37 now lead to A31 in about 22–28 in 100 transitions instead of into the second group [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005].

- Event positions shifted consistently with the segment shrinking by about four events: A33/A34/A36/A37 sit later (mean relative position about 0.65 to 0.74), A38/A39/A40/A41 slightly earlier (about 0.77 to 0.73), A30 later (0.58 to 0.66) and A31 slightly earlier (0.84 to 0.82) [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS009].

- Within-segment distances shortened: from A30 to A38/A39/A40/A41 and from A33/A34/A36/A37 to A31, the gap was five or more steps in essentially all before-window cases and is now spread across one to four steps [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG008, LAG014, LAG018]. A few lag items rest on only one or two later-window observations and were not interpreted [LAG006, LAG010, LAG012, LAG015].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the eight-activity segment between A30 and A31, previously executed in full in every case, is in the later window executed as exactly one of two alternative halves — either A33, A34, A36 and A37, or A38, A39, A40 and A41 — with the other half not executed; each half runs in about half of the traces, while A30 still precedes the segment and A31 still follows it [OCC001, OCC006, REL020, REL039, REL001, REL014].

The support is direct and consistent: the participation of all eight activities halved; the co-occurrence pattern shows the two groups are mutually exclusive and internally inseparable; routing shows A30 feeding both groups and the first group now handing over directly to A31; and the order relations around the segment are preserved. Counting related items as one, the distinct differences (halved participation, mutual exclusivity of the two groups, rerouting) exceed the roughly 1 qualifying item expected if nothing had changed, and each qualifying item's difference is larger than in 199 of 200 random re-splits, the ceiling for this reference count [OCC001, REL020, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001].

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is sampling: the exact 50/50 split between the two halves is a sample proportion and could differ in the underlying process, and rare variants can appear in one window only. Sampling alone, however, cannot plausibly account for the pattern, since the before window shows the full eight-activity segment in 200 of 200 traces with co-occurrence 1.0; the main remaining limitation is that the evidence shows co-occurrence, order and routing, not the mechanism that selects which half runs [REL017, REL020, OCC001].
