### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the four activities A30, A31, A36 and A37 each appeared about 3.3 times per case and repeated in about 67 in 100 cases; in the later window each appears almost exactly once per case (mean about 1.0) and repeats have practically vanished (about 0–2 in 100 cases), while participation stays at 100 in 100 cases in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

- In the later window, A37's exit routing is redirected: about 97 in 100 of its roughly 200 observed outgoing transitions lead to A25, whereas before, about 70 in 100 of its roughly 670 observed outgoing transitions led back to A30 and only about 30 in 100 led to A25; none of the later-window transitions from A37 go to A30. [DIR001]

- The mutual order of the four activities changed from interleaved repetition to a fixed sequence: before, about 67 in 100 cases showed each pair as interleaved or repeated; in the later window, A30 precedes A31, A36 and A37 in about 97–99 in 100 cases, and both A31 and A36 precede A37 in about 98–99 in 100 cases. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL005, REL006]

- The middle pair is the exception: in the later window A31 precedes A36 in about 56 in 100 cases and A36 precedes A31 in about 44 in 100, whereas before, the pair was mostly interleaved or repeated (about 68 in 100) with only weak order signals (about 14 vs 19 in 100). [REL004]

- The four activities now follow one another compactly: in the later window, A31 is followed by A36 within one step in about 98 in 100 of the 112 traces where that ordered pair occurs, A36 is followed by A31 within one step in all 88 such traces, and A30 reaches A31 and A36 within one to two steps in almost all cases; before, one-step gaps accounted for only about 10 in 100 of observed pairs, with the remainder dispersed over longer gaps. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006]

- The relative event positions of all four activities became much more concentrated in the later window (positional spread roughly halved, e.g. A30's standard deviation from about 0.14 to about 0.07) while their average positions stayed essentially unchanged, consistent with one fixed slot per case instead of several recurring slots. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block A30 → A31 → A36 → A37, which in the earlier window ran about three times per case with A37 returning to A30 between passes and reaching A25 only at the end, is in the later window executed exactly once per case — A30 first, then A31 and A36 consecutively in either order, then A37 — after which the flow leaves the block to A25. The support is direct rather than circumstantial: the rerouting is visible in A37's transition profile, the order change in the relation profiles (interleaving about 67 in 100 before, essentially 0 after), and the repetition collapse in the occurrence counts (about 3.3 down to about 1.0 per case with participation unchanged). The items reduce to one coherent difference with two directly observed facets, clearly more than the roughly 0.55 items expected by chance among the 110 tested units, and every item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG003, LAG005]

Limitations: items about the same four activities are not independent confirmations, and a few lag items (A37→A31, A37→A36, A37→A37) rest on a single after-window trace and were not relied upon. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is a sampling difference in variant frequencies: with two finite samples of 200 cases each, an unchanged process could in principle present different variant mixes in the two windows; this is unlikely here because behaviour is uniform within each window (100 in 100 participation in both) and the routing and order changes are observed directly, but it cannot be ruled out with certainty. No other activities or pairs appear in the evidence, so nothing can be said about the rest of the process. [LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, OCC001, DIR001, REL001]
