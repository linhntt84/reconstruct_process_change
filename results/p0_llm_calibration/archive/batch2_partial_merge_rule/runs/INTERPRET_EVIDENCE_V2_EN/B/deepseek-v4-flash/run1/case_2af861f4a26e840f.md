### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed such that activity A06 is executed more often and earlier in the trace, activities A30 and A31 are repeated more often, and activity A38's outgoing routing shifted from A20/A19 to A06, while A04, A08 and A10 are used less. The number of distinct differences (A06 participation/position, A30/A31 repetition, A38 routing, A04/A08/A10 decrease) is far larger than the 0.73 items expected by chance under a stable process. [screening, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR002, DIR006, DIR007, REL003, REL007, POS001] The main competing explanation is that these differences arise from sampling variation in variant frequencies, but the consistency across participation, routing, position and lag indicators for the same activities makes this unlikely. Some lag items have low support (e.g., A43–A06), but they are not central to the hypothesis. [LAG017]
