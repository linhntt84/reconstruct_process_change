### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: in the later window, activities A14, A15, A20 and A21 are executed earlier, before A22 and A23, and the flow appears to become A41 → (A14/A15/A20/A21) → A22 → A23 → A24 → A28, whereas before A41 led to A22 and those activities came later. This is directly shown by the reversal of order in the relational evidence, the earlier positions in the positional evidence, and the changed outgoing edges in the routing evidence. The number of distinct differences is far above the 0.46 items expected by chance, and the pattern is consistent across indicators. A competing explanation of sampling variation in variant frequencies cannot be fully ruled out, but the magnitude and coherence of the changes make it unlikely. Some lag items involving A20/A21 with A28 have very low support in the later window (only 1 trace), so those specific lag changes are weak, but the overall reordering is supported by the stronger relational and positional evidence.
