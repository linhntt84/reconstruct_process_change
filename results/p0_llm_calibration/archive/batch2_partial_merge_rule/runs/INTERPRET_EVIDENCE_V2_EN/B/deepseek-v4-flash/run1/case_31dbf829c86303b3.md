### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A25 is now executed immediately after A18 or A19, whereas before it was executed after A16 or A17; and A24 now leads to A16 or A17 instead of A18 or A19. This is directly shown by the successor changes in DIR and the positional shifts in LAG, which are consistent across all involved activities. The number of distinct differences (routing of A16/A17, A18/A19, and A24) is far above the 0.35 items expected by chance, and all items have high empirical strength. A competing explanation—that these differences arise from sampling variation in variant frequencies—is unlikely given the coherence and magnitude of the pattern, but cannot be fully excluded because the windows are finite samples.
