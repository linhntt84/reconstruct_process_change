### Observed differences

- In the later window, A20's outgoing edge changed from A21 (100% of cases) to A27 (about 49%) and A28 (about 49%), with A21 nearly disappearing (about 1.5%). [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: in the later window, the activities A27 and A28 are executed right after A20, before A21 and A23, with A24 still at the end; in the earlier window the order was A20, A21, A23, then A27/A28, then A24. This is shown directly by the successor changes (A20 now leads to A27/A28 instead of A21; A23 now leads to A24), by the relational reversal (A27/A28 before A21/A23 in about 98.5% of later cases), and by the lag and position shifts, all with full support in both windows. [DIR001, DIR002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

A competing explanation is that the two windows simply contain different variant frequencies by chance; however, the changes are large, consistent across four independent indicators, and far exceed the number of items expected without change (0.41). A minor limitation is that a few lag items involving A21/A23 with A27/A28 rest on only 3 supporting traces in the later window, but the main ordering evidence does not depend on them.
