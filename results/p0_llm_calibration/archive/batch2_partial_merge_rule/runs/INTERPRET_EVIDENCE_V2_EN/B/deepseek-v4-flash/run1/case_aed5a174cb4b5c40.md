### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A32, A33, and A34 appear in essentially every trace (about 100 in 100) instead of about half (52, 49, and 48 in 100) before. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A25's successor changed: before it went to A26 in about 97 in 100 cases; after it goes to A33 (about 50) and A34 (about 48), with A26 no longer a direct successor. [DIR001]
- A28's successor changed: before it split among A32 (51), A34 (26), and A33 (22); after it goes to A32 in about 99 in 100. [DIR002]
- A33's successor changed: before it went to A29 (54) and A34 (44); after it goes to A26 (49) and A34 (47). [DIR003]
- A34's successor changed: before it went to A33 (55) and A29 (43); after it goes to A26 (48) and A33 (48). [DIR004]
- Order relations reversed: before A26 preceded A33 and A34 in about half the traces; after A33 and A34 precede A26 in nearly all traces. Similarly, A33 and A34 now precede A28, whereas before A28 preceded them. [REL005, REL006, REL008, REL009]
- A33 and A34 now co-occur with A32 in all traces, whereas before A32 and A33/A34 were mutually exclusive (cooccurrence near 0). [REL013, REL014]
- Lags changed: A25 and A26 are now separated by 3–4 steps instead of adjacent; A25 and A33/A34 are now adjacent (lag 1–2) instead of 4–5 steps apart. [LAG001, LAG003, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the process moved from two alternative branches (one with A32, another with A33/A34) after A25–A26–A28 to a single sequence where A33 and A34 are executed immediately after A25, followed by A26, A28, A32, and A29. This is directly shown by the successor change of A25, the order reversals for A33/A34 relative to A26 and A28, and the increased participation of A33/A34/A32. [DIR001, REL005, REL006, REL008, REL009, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003] A competing explanation is that the observed differences arise from sampling variation in variant frequencies, but the consistency across many indicators and the large effect sizes make that unlikely.
