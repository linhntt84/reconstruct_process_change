### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A06, A19 and A20 appear far less often: A06 participation dropped from about 65% to 16% of traces, A19 from 55% to 0.5%, and A20 from 45% to 1%. [OCC003, OCC006, OCC007]
- Activities A02, A04, A08 and A10 appear more often, with participation rising from about 6% to roughly 18–24% of traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, OCC005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that a group of activities (A06, A19, A20) that were commonly executed and often followed by A46, A40, A45 or A25 are now largely skipped or replaced, while activities A02, A04, A08, A10 and A25 are executed more often, and the routing from A40 changed from A19/A20 to A25. The pattern is consistent across occurrence, routing, co‑occurrence and lag indicators, and the number of distinct differences clearly exceeds what would be expected by chance. A limitation is that the near‑disappearance of A19 and A20 rests on very few traces in the later window (1 and 2 traces), so their exact frequency is uncertain, and the evidence cannot rule out that the increase in A02/A04/A08/A10 is an independent change rather than a direct replacement of the disappearing activities.
