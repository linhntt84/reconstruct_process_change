### Observed differences

- In the later window, A19 and A20 each appear in about 59% of traces, down from 100% before; mean occurrences per trace fell from about 1.0 to 0.59. [OCC001, OCC002]
- A25 and A26 also appear less often: A25 from about 48% to 30% of traces, A26 from about 53% to 30%. [OCC003, OCC004]

- The gap between A42 and A27 changed: before, about 89% of such pairs were 4 steps apart; after, about 40% are 1 step apart and about 53% are 4 steps apart. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a subset of cases (roughly 40%) skip the A19/A20 step, with A42 leading directly to A27 instead of passing through A19. This is shown directly by the drop in A19 and A20 participation from near 100% to about 59%, the appearance of an A42→A27 edge at about 40% while A42→A19 falls from 93% to 55%, and the shift of A42–A27 lag from 4 steps to 1 step in those cases. The many relational items all reflect this same underlying difference, so they are not independent confirmations, but the pattern is consistent across occurrence, routing, and lag indicators. A competing explanation—that the difference is only sampling variation in variant frequencies—is unlikely given the size and coherence of the changes, though the evidence cannot fully rule out that the A25/A26 drop is a separate effect rather than part of the same skipped branch.
