### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which activity A08 is executed much less often and later in the process, and the order of a block of activities (A29, A21, A22, A23, A24, A30) is rearranged: A29 now leads to A21/A22, which lead to A23/A24, which then lead to A30, whereas before A21/A22 directly led to A30 and A23/A24 also led to A30 with more variable lag. This is directly shown by the consistent changes in outgoing transitions (DIR) and lag distributions (LAG) for these activities, together with the drop in A08 participation and its later position (OCC, POS, REL). The number of distinct differences (A08 decrease and the reordering) is far above the 0.65 items expected by chance under no change. A competing explanation—sampling variation in variant frequencies—cannot be fully ruled out, but the consistency across multiple indicators and the high empirical strength of the changes make a structural change the more plausible interpretation. Some lag items involving A08 have low support (e.g., only 7 or 4 traces in the later window), but the main reordering items are based on 88–112 traces per window.
