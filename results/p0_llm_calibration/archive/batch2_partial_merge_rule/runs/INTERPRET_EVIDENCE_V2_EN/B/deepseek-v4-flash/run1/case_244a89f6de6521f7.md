### Observed differences

- In the later window, A22 occurs in about 53% of traces instead of 100%; A27, A28, A33 and A34 each occur in about 47% of traces, having been absent before. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- A21 is always present. Before, it was always followed by A22; in the later window it is still followed by A22 in about 53% of cases, and in about 47% of cases it is followed by A27/A28/A33/A34 instead. [DIR002, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015]
- A23 is always present. Before, A21 and A23 were two steps apart; in the later window they are five steps apart in about 47% of cases, consistent with a block of four activities inserted between them. [LAG001, REL016, REL021]
- A06’s successor distribution shifted: before, A06 led to A07 in about 26% of its occurrences, A08 in 39% and A09 in 35%; in the later window it leads to A07 in about 47%, A08 in 31% and A09 in 22%. [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in about half of cases, a block of activities A27, A28, A33 and A34 is executed between A21 and A23 instead of A22. This is directly shown by the drop of A22 from 100% to 53% participation, the joint appearance of the four new activities in 47% of traces, the change in A21’s successor, and the A21–A23 lag shifting from 2 to 5 steps. The A06 routing shift toward A07 is also visible, but it is not clearly tied to the same replacement and could be an independent change. The main replacement is not plausibly explained by sampling variation because it is large, coherent and supported by occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators.
