### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that activities A14 and A15 were replaced by A20 and A21 in the process: A14/A15 drop from about half of traces to near zero, A20/A21 rise from zero to about 99%, and A37's successors change accordingly, with the A37-to-A22 distance increasing by one step. The change is consistent across occurrence, routing, position, relation and lag indicators, and its magnitude is far beyond what sampling variation would typically produce. A competing explanation—that the later window simply reflects a different mix of rare variants—is implausible given the complete disappearance and appearance of activities that were each present in about half of the earlier traces. The evidence cannot rule out that A20/A21 are renamed versions of A14/A15 rather than genuinely new activities, but either way the observed behaviour indicates a structural replacement in the process.
