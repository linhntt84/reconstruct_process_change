### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A14 and A15 appear in about 44% and 56% of traces respectively; they were absent in all 200 earlier traces. [OCC001, OCC002]
- A14 and A15 never occur together; in almost every trace exactly one of them is present. [REL001]
- In the earlier window, A35 was always followed by A20. In the later window, A35 is followed by A14 in about 44% of traces and by A15 in about 56% of traces, and only rarely by A20. [DIR003]
- Both A14 and A15 are always followed by A20 in the later window. [DIR001, DIR002]
- The gap between A35 and A20 changed from 1 step (immediately consecutive) to 2 steps in about 99.5% of later traces. [LAG001]
- A14 and A15 appear around the middle of the trace (mean position about 0.36 of the trace length). [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a new step was inserted between A35 and A20: the process now chooses either A14 or A15 after A35, and then continues to A20. This is directly shown by the successor change of A35, the successors of A14 and A15, and the lag shift from 1 to 2 between A35 and A20. The mutual exclusivity of A14 and A15 indicates a choice between two alternatives. A competing explanation that the two activities existed but were never observed before cannot be fully ruled out, but the consistent routing and lag changes make the insertion hypothesis the most direct interpretation.
