### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which a block of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26) moved from before A27/A28 to after them, and their successors changed from A27 to A29/A33. A46 now goes directly to A27 instead of to that block. The change is directly shown by relational order reversals [REL001–REL008], successor changes [DIR004–DIR007, DIR010], and lag shifts [LAG007–LAG011]. The decrease in A04 and increase in A08 are additional changes. A competing explanation of random sampling differences is unlikely given the number of distinct differences and their consistency, though some individual lag items have low support (e.g., LAG002, LAG007) and the exact new order is not fully specified.
