### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed so that A25 now leads directly to A26, and A33/A34 now lead to A29, with A32, A33, and A34 occurring less often. The successor relations (DIR) and the relative order (LAG, REL) directly show this re-routing and reordering. The main competing explanation is that the differences arise from sampling variation in the two windows, but the consistency across many indicators and the high empirical strength make that unlikely. A limitation is that the evidence cannot confirm a permanent structural change; it could reflect a temporary variant or a different execution mode.
