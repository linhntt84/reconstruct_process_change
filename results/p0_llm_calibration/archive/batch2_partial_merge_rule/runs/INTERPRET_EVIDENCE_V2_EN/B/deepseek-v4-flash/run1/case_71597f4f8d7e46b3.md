### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that, in the later window, a new set of activities (A32, A33, A38, A39) is inserted into the process, often between A26 and A28, while A27 and A04 are executed less frequently, and A45 now more often leads to A46 instead of A04. The pattern is consistent across occurrence, transition, relation and lag indicators: A26 now often leads to the new activities instead of A27, A28 often follows the new activities instead of A27, and A04’s role as a successor of A45 and predecessor of A46 is largely replaced. A competing explanation is that the observed differences reflect sampling variation in variant frequencies, but the number of distinct, interlocking changes is far larger than expected by chance, and they point to the same structural shift. A limitation is that A04’s support is small (61 before, 22 after), so some details of its change are less certain.
