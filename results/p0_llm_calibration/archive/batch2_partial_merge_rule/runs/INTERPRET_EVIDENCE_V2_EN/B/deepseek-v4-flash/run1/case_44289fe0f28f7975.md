### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which activities A30, A31, A36 and A37 are now executed repeatedly — about three times per trace — and interleaved, rather than once in a fixed order. This is shown directly by the large increase in occurrence counts and recurrence, the shift from ordered pairs to interleaved/repeated relations, the widening of lags between occurrences, and the change in A37's successor from A25 to A30. The main indicators have full support in both windows, and the differences are far larger than expected by chance. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that a loop was added around these activities rather than a simple repetition block, but both possibilities represent a structural change in the process.
