### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed such that a set of activities (A20, A21, A22, A23) now tend to occur earlier relative to another set (A15, A16, A18, A19), and that new direct transitions were introduced (e.g., A19 to A29, A28 to A20). The supporting signs are the consistent reversal in pairwise order across many pairs, the corresponding position shifts, and the redistributed outgoing transitions. A competing explanation is that the differences reflect sampling variation in variant frequencies, but the magnitude and consistency across many pairs make that unlikely. A limitation is that the exact nature of the change (e.g., whether it is a swap of two blocks or a more complex reordering) is not fully specified.
