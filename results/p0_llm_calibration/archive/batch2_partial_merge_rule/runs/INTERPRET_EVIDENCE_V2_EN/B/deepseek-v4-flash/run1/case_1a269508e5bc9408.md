Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured so that A04 is now an early step, and the order of several activities changed: A14 now leads to A04, A04 leads to A15, and A19/A20/A25/A26 now lead to A27 instead of A29/A33, while A28 leads to A29 and A29 leads to A33. Limitations: the evidence comes from two finite samples, and some LAG items have low support, but the main DIR and REL items have strong support. A competing explanation is that the observed differences are due to sampling variation, but the number and consistency of changes across many indicators make that unlikely.
