- In the later window, A21 and A22 occur later in the trace: the mean relative position of A21 rises from about 0.51 to 0.62, and A22 from about 0.36 to 0.48. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process in which A21 and A22 now occur after A30, A31, A36 and A37, whereas before they occurred before that block. A25, which previously appeared after those activities, now appears immediately after A21, and A22 is no longer the immediate successor of A20. The order reversal is shown directly by the relational evidence for all eight pairs, and the position, successor and lag changes are consistent with the same reordering. The support is strong, with 200 traces in each window for the main indicators. A competing explanation based on sampling differences in variant frequencies is unlikely given the size and consistency of the changes, although the evidence cannot rule out that some rare variant patterns contributed to the observed differences.
