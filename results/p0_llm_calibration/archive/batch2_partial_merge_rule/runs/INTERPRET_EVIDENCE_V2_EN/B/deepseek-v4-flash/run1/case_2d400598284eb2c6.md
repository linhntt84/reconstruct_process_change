### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a single path that always included A27 to a process with an alternative branch: in about half the traces, A27 is skipped and instead A32 and A33 are executed, and A02 is often inserted between A14 and A15. The occurrence, successor, relation, position, and lag indicators all point to the same restructuring. The main limitation is that the exact control flow is not fully specified; for example, A02 is not always with A32/A33 (co-occurrence about 27%), and some traces have A02 without A32. The smaller changes in A04, A10, A23, and A24 may be part of the same change or separate, but the core branch change is consistent across many indicators. The evidence cannot rule out that some of the smaller changes are sampling variation, but the main branch change is too large and consistent to be explained by chance alone.
