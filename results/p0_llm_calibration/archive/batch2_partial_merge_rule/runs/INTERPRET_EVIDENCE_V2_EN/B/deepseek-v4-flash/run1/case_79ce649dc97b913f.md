### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which activity A06 is inserted early in the process, followed by a block of activities A34–A37 that are now executed in nearly every case, while A31 is moved later and A10 becomes less frequent. The occurrence, routing, relational and lag indicators all point consistently to this same pattern: A06 appears before the block, A30/A34/A35 now route into A36/A37 instead of A31, and A31 is pushed to a later position. The main limitation is that the two windows could represent different case types rather than a temporal change, but the coherence across multiple independent indicators makes a sampling artefact unlikely.
