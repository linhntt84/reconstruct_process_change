### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process shifted from a path dominated by A04, usually followed by A46, to a set of new or previously rare activities (A19, A20, A25, A26, and increased A02, A06, A08, A10), with A04 no longer feeding A46 as before.
