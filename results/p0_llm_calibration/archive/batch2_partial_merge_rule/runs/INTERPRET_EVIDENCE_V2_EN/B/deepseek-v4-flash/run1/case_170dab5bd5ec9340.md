### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: activities A19, A20, A25 and A26 now occur before A27/A28 instead of after, and A06 appears more frequently early in the process, often leading to A16 and then to the later activities. The successor changes (DIR), order reversals (REL), position shifts (POS) and lag changes (LAG) are consistent across many activities and are large relative to the expected chance variation. A competing explanation is that the differences reflect sampling variation in variant frequencies, but the magnitude and consistency of the changes make that unlikely. Some lag items have very low support in the later window (e.g., A19–A29, A19–A33), but the main order relations are based on nearly all traces.
