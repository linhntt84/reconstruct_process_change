### Observed differences

- A02 participation dropped from about 60% of traces to 24%, and its mean occurrences per trace fell from 0.62 to 0.28. When A02 occurs, it now appears later (mean position from 0.20 to 0.36) and its successors are more varied: before, about 94% of A02 transitions went to A15; after, only about 45% go to A15, with the rest spread over many other activities. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001]

- A27 became universal: participation rose from about 48% to 100% of traces, and mean occurrences per trace from 0.48 to 1.02. It now co-occurs with A14, A15, A26, A28, A40, A45, and A46 in essentially all traces, and typically precedes them. [OCC005, REL016, REL019, REL025, REL028, REL031, REL032, REL033]

- A32 and A33 almost disappeared: A32 participation fell from about 54% to 1% (2 traces), A33 from about 53% to 0.5% (1 trace). Their relations with many activities changed accordingly, e.g., A02-A32 cooccurrence fell from 32% to 0%, and the share of traces containing neither rose from 19% to 75%. [OCC006, OCC007, REL008, REL009, REL036]

- A04, A06, and A10 increased: participation rose from about 5%, 4%, and 6% to about 20%, 20%, and 21% respectively. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

- A26's successor changed: before it went to A27 (47%), A33 (26%), and A32 (26%); after it goes to A27 in about 99% of its transitions. [DIR005]

- A14's successor changed: before it went to A02 in about 58% of transitions and A15 in 42%; after it goes to A15 in about 77% and A02 in only 12%. [DIR003]

- A15's successor became more diverse: before about 98% went to A16; after about 90% go to A16, with small shares to A04, A10, A06, A08, A02, etc. [DIR004]

- Lag distributions shifted for several pairs, e.g., A02-A20 moved from always lag 5 to a mix of lag 1 (39%) and lag 5 (39%); A26-A28 moved from lag 2/3 to mostly lag 2 (95%). [LAG001, LAG011]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A02 is largely removed from the main flow, A27 becomes a mandatory step, and A32/A33 are no longer used. The supporting signs are large, consistent changes across participation, successor distributions, and relational patterns: A02's drop is mirrored in its relations with many activities, A27's rise to universal presence is confirmed by co-occurrence with nearly all other main activities, and A32/A33 disappear from both occurrence and relational data. A04, A06, and A10 also increase, suggesting they are added or become more common. A limitation is that the later window contains only 2 traces with A32 and 1 with A33, so their complete absence cannot be confirmed, but the drop from over 50% participation is substantial. Sampling variation in variant frequencies is unlikely to explain the magnitude and consistency of these changes, though the low support for A32/A33 after means their disappearance could be partly due to sampling.
