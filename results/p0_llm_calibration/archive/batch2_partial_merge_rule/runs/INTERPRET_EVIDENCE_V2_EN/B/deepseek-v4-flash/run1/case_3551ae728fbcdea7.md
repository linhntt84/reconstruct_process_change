### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A33, A34, A36, A37, A38, A39, A40 and A41 each appear in about half of the traces (participation dropped from about 100% to about 50%), while A30 and A31 remain present in all traces. [OCC001–OCC008, POS001–POS002]
- The later window splits these eight activities into two mutually exclusive groups: {A33, A34, A36, A37} and {A38, A39, A40, A41}. For example, A33 and A38 co-occur in only about 1% of traces, whereas before they co-occurred in all; each appears alone in about half of the traces. [REL020, REL026, REL031, REL035]
- A30's outgoing transitions now go to activities from both groups, and A31 appears as a new successor from many of these activities. [DIR001–DIR006]
- Positions shifted: A30 moved later (mean position from about 0.58 to 0.66), A33–A37 moved later, A38–A41 moved earlier, and A31 moved slightly earlier. [POS001–POS009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process now has two alternative branches: one branch includes activities A33, A34, A36 and A37, and the other includes A38, A39, A40 and A41, with A30 and A31 always present. This is directly shown by the halved participation of the eight activities, the mutual exclusivity between the two groups, and the changes in routing and ordering. A competing explanation of sampling variation is unlikely given the large and consistent differences across many indicators, though the exact placement of A31 and the order within branches are less certain due to weaker support in some lag items.
