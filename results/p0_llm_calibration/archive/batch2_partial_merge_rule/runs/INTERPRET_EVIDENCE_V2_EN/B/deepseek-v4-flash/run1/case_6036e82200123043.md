### Observed differences

- In the later window, the relative order of two groups of activities reversed: A26 and A27 now occur after A35, A36, A41 and A42, whereas before they occurred before them. For example, A26 preceded A35 in about 98% of cases before, but in the later window A35 preceded A26 in about 97% of cases; the same reversal holds for A26 with A36, A41, A42, and for A27 with all four. - Position shifts match this reversal: A26 and A27 moved later (mean relative position increased by about 0.12 and 0.11), while A35, A36, A41 and A42 moved earlier (mean decreased by about 0.16, 0.15, 0.16 and 0.16). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Successor distributions changed substantially. A25's main successor changed from A27 (about 98%) to a spread among A42, A36, A35 and A41 (each about 25%). A26's main successor changed from a spread among A41, A35, A42 and A36 (each about 25%) to A30 (about 94%). A35, A36, A41 and A42 now often lead to A27 (about 26%, 29%, 21% and 17% respectively) and almost never to A30 (down from about 22–31% to near 0–1%). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- Lag distributions confirm the reordering: A25–A27 lag changed from 1 step (97%) to 5 steps (97%); A26–A30 lag changed from 5 steps (100%) to 1 step (94%). [LAG001, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the order of two activity groups was swapped: A35, A36, A41 and A42 now occur before A26 and A27, and A30 now follows A26 instead of following the former group. This is directly shown by the consistent reversal in relational order (REL001–REL008), the corresponding position shifts (POS001–POS006), and the successor changes (DIR001–DIR006). The effect sizes are large (e.g., about 97% of cases reversed) and the pattern is coherent across multiple indicators. A competing explanation—that these differences arise from sampling variation in variant frequencies—is unlikely given the magnitude and consistency, although some lag items have low support in one window (e.g., LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG009, LAG010 have only 4–5 supporting traces in the later window). The core reversal, however, rests on REL and POS items with full support.
