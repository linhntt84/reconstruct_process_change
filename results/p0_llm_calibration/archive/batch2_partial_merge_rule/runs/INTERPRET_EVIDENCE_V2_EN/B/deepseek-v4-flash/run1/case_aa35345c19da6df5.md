### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in which the relative order of A19/A20 and A21/A22 was reversed, and the routing around A23 and A28 was swapped: A19/A20 now lead to A28 instead of A23, while A21/A22 now lead to A23 instead of A28. The main limitation is that the windows are finite samples, but the size and consistency of the differences make sampling variation an unlikely explanation. The evidence does not identify why the change occurred.
