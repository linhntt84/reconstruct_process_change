- In the later window, activities A14 and A15 appear in far fewer traces: A14 in about 26% of traces (down from 49%), A15 in about 28% (down from 51%). [OCC001, OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A35 now often routes directly to A20, bypassing A14 and A15, which are executed in about half as many traces; and the order of A27 and A28 is reversed relative to A20 and A21. The routing change is directly shown by the shift in A35's outgoing transitions and the corresponding drop in A14/A15 participation and co-occurrence. The order swap is directly shown by the successor distributions and lag distances among A20, A27, A28, and A21. The number of distinct differences (two) clearly exceeds the 0.42 expected by chance, and the changes are consistent across multiple indicators. A competing explanation of mere sampling variation in variant frequencies is unlikely given the size and consistency of the shifts, though the two changes could be part of a single larger modification rather than independent ones.
