### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the process moved from two alternative branches — one containing A32 and one containing A33/A34 — to a single sequence where A33 and A34 occur early after A25, followed by A26, A28, A32 and A29. Specifically, A25 now routes to A33/A34 instead of A26, A28 now routes almost exclusively to A32, and A33/A34 now route to A26 instead of A29. The order changes are directly shown by relational and lag evidence, and the participation changes are consistent with the disappearance of the two-branch split. A competing explanation of sampling differences in variant frequencies cannot be fully excluded, but the large, consistent differences across occurrence, routing, position, relation and lag indicators make that unlikely. Some lag items in the later window rest on very few traces (e.g., A26–A33, A26–A34, A28–A33, A28–A34 each have support of 1 trace), so those specific lags are weak, but the main order relations are supported by hundreds of traces.
