### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured so that A19 and A20, which previously occurred early and were followed by A25, A26, A27, and A28, now occur later, after A25/A26/A27, with A40 routing to A25 instead of to A19/A20. In addition, A02 participation decreased and A06 increased, and A45 now routes to A46 instead of to A02. The supporting signs are consistent across routing, order, position, and lag indicators, and the effect sizes are large. A competing explanation is that the differences arise from sampling variation in variant frequencies, but the consistency and magnitude across multiple indicators make this unlikely. Some individual changes rest on small support (e.g., A06, A02 after), but the main reordering is well supported.
