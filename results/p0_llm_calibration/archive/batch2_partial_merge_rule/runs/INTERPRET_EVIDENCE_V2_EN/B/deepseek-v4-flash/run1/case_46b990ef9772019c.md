### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A33, A34, A36, A37, A38, A39, A40, and A41 each appear in about 99–100% of traces, up from about 47–55% before. Their mean occurrences per trace roughly doubled from about 0.5 to about 1.0. [OCC001–OCC008]
- Before, A33–A37 and A38–A41 were mutually exclusive: for example, A33 and A38 co-occurred in only about 1% of traces, while A38 and A39 co-occurred in about 46%. After, A33 and A38 co-occur in about 99% of traces, and all these activities co-occur. [REL020, REL039, REL001–REL008, REL009–REL016, REL017–REL044]
- The outgoing transitions of A30 changed: before it led to A33–A41 with small shares; after it leads mainly to A33, A34, A36, and A37, and no longer to A38–A41. Instead, A33, A34, A36, and A37 now have outgoing transitions to A38–A41, which they did not before. [DIR001–DIR005]
- A30 moved earlier in the trace (mean relative position from about 0.65 to 0.59), A33–A37 moved earlier (from about 0.73–0.74 to about 0.65), and A38–A41 moved later (from about 0.73–0.74 to about 0.76–0.77). [POS001–POS009]
- The order between A30 and A31 changed: before they were 5 steps apart in all traces; after they are more than 5 steps apart in most traces (only 3 of 200 traces have them within 5 steps). [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed from a choice between two alternative sets of activities (A33–A37 and A38–A41) to executing all of them in sequence, with A30 preceding them and A31 following. The participation of A33–A41 increased from about half to nearly all traces, their co-occurrence changed from mutually exclusive to almost always together, and the routing changed so that A33–A37 now lead to A38–A41. The position shifts are consistent with this: A30 and A33–A37 moved earlier, A38–A41 moved later. A competing explanation of sampling differences in variant frequencies is unlikely given the magnitude and consistency of the changes across multiple indicators. A limitation is that some LAG items have very low support in the later window (e.g., A30–A31), but the main evidence from OCC, REL, DIR, and POS is strong.
