### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a block of activities was reordered: before, the typical order ran A25 → A32/A33 → A26 → A28 → A29; after, it runs A25 → A26 → A28 → A32/A33 → A29. This is supported directly by successor changes at A25, A28, A32 and A33, by the reversal of order relations between A26/A28 and A32/A33, and by the corresponding lag and position shifts. The number of distinct differences is far above the roughly 0.6 items expected by chance, and the indicators agree with each other rather than pointing in different directions. The main competing explanation is sampling variation in variant frequencies between the two finite windows, but the coherence and size of the pattern make that unlikely. The peripheral A02/A14 changes are based on fewer observations and are not needed for the main conclusion.
