### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed so that A37 now almost always leads to A14 instead of A22, and A14/A15 (followed by A20 or A21) are executed much more often, with A22 occurring later in the trace. This is directly shown by the routing change from A37 to A14 [DIR001], the large participation increases for A14, A15, A20, and A21 [OCC001–OCC004], and the positional and lag shifts for A22 and A37 [POS001, POS002, LAG001]. The relational evidence consistently shows the new order A14→A15→(A20 or A21)→A22/A37 [REL001–REL014]. A competing explanation is that these differences arise from sampling variation in variant frequencies, but the magnitude and consistency across multiple indicators make that unlikely. The main limitation is that all items concern the same set of activities, so they are not independent confirmations, but the direct routing and order evidence is strong.
