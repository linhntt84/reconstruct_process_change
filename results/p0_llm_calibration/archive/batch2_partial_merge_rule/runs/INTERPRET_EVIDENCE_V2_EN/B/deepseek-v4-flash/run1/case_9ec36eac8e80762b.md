### Observed differences

- In the later window, A30 and A31 each occur about once per trace instead of about 1.6 times; they still appear in every trace, but the share of traces with repeated occurrences dropped from about 37% to about 1%. [OCC003, OCC004]
- The order between A30, A31, A43 and A44 became much more fixed: in the earlier window, about 63% of traces had A30 before A31 (and similarly for the other pairs), with about 37% interleaved or repeated; in the later window, about 99% have A30 before A31, and interleaving or repetition nearly disappeared. - A31's immediate successor changed: before, about 37% of A31 transitions went to A30 and 62% to A25; after, about 99% go to A25 and none to A30. [DIR003]
- A02 became more frequent (participation from about 12% to 25%) and occurs later in the trace (median relative position from 0.67 to 0.97); its outgoing transitions now go mostly to A44 (about 84% vs 28% before). [OCC001, POS001, DIR001]

- A04 became less frequent (participation from about 10% to 3%). [OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which a previously repeated and interleaved segment involving A30 and A31 became a single, strictly ordered pass: A30 then A31, followed by A43 and A44. This is directly shown by the drop in repetition of A30 and A31, the near-disappearance of interleaving between these activities and A43/A44, and the change in A31's successor from A30 to A25, which removes the loop back to A30. The same change also appears in the lag distribution, where A30 now immediately precedes A31 in almost all cases. A02's increased frequency, later position, and shift toward A44 as its main successor suggest that A02 was added or moved into a role just before A44, but this could be a separate modification rather than part of the same change. A competing explanation of sampling variation in variant frequencies is unlikely given the large, consistent differences across occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators, all based on substantial support (200 traces for the A30/A31 segment). The main limitation is that the evidence does not show whether the A02 and A04 changes are causally linked to the A30/A31 change or are independent adjustments.
