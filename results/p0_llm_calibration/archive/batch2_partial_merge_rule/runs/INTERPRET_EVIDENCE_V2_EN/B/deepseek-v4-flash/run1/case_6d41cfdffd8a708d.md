### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A14, A15, A20 and A21 each appear in about 1% or fewer of traces, down from about 25% of traces each in the earlier window (A14 from 26% to 1%, A15 from 26% to 0%, A20 from 23% to 0.5%, A21 from 25% to 0%). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Routing from A23 and A24 changed: A23 now goes to A24 in about 99.5% of its outgoing transitions (up from about 50%), and no longer to A14, A15, A20 or A21; A24 now goes to A25 in about 99% of its outgoing transitions (up from about 50%). [DIR005, DIR006]
- The four activities no longer co-occur with A23, A24 or A25: before, each co-occurred in about a quarter of traces, while in the later window they almost never appear together. [REL004, REL005, REL006, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018]
- Position and lag indicators reflect the same near-disappearance: the positions of A14, A15, A20 and A21 essentially vanish, and the few remaining occurrences shift in lag relative to A25. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a set of activities (A14, A15, A20, A21), which were executed in about a quarter of cases in the earlier window, are essentially no longer executed in the later window. Routing from A23 and A24 now goes to A25 instead of to these activities, consistent with a branch or segment of the process being dropped. The change is consistent across occurrence, routing, relation, position and lag indicators. A competing explanation of sampling differences in variant frequencies is unlikely given the magnitude of the drop (from about 25% participation to 0–1%) and its consistency across four activities and multiple indicators. The main limitation is that the later window contains very few traces with these activities (at most 2), so the exact routing after them is uncertain, but their near-absence is clear.
