### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed from a choice between two alternative branches – one containing A33, A34, A36, A37 and the other containing A38, A39, A40, A41 – to a version in which both branches are executed in sequence, while A06 is largely bypassed. This is directly supported by the near‑universal participation of all these activities in the later window, the shift from mutual exclusivity to co‑occurrence between the two sets, and the ordering changes (e.g., A38 and A31 now appear much later relative to A30 and A33, while A40 appears shortly after A37). The evidence cannot rule out that the change arose from a different mechanism, such as a new variant that happens to include both branches, but the consistency across occurrence, routing, co‑occurrence and lag indicators makes a structural change the most plausible interpretation.
