### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed its routing after activity A20: instead of proceeding to A23 and then A24, it now proceeds to A21 and then A22. This is directly shown by the shift in A20's successor distribution [DIR001], and consistently reflected in the increased participation of A21/A22 and decreased participation of A23/A24 [OCC001–OCC004], the corresponding changes in co-occurrence and order with A20 [REL001–REL004], and the new A21-before-A22 pattern [REL005]. The number of distinct differences (one underlying routing change) clearly exceeds the 0.47 items expected by chance under screening [screening]. A competing explanation that the differences arise from sampling variation in variant frequencies is unlikely given the magnitude and consistency of the evidence, though the two windows are finite samples and the change could reflect a shift in case mix rather than a deliberate process modification.
