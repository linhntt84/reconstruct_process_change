### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed such that a set of activities (A21–A24, and A04) was largely replaced by another set (A25–A28, and A02/A08) in the later window. This is directly shown by the drop in participation of the former and rise of the latter, and by the routing changes: A20 now leads to A28 instead of A24, and A31 now leads to A19 instead of A04. The co-occurrence and lag patterns are consistent with this swap. A competing explanation that the later window simply contains more cases of an alternative branch cannot be ruled out, but that would still be a structural change in routing shares. The evidence does not indicate why the change occurred.
