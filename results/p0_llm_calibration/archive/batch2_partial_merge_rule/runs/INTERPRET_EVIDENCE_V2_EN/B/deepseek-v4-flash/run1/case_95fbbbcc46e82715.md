### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19 and A20, which were almost absent before (about 0.5% and 1% of traces), appear in about 45% and 55% of traces respectively. [OCC001, OCC002]
- The successor of A40 changed: before, about 88% of its outgoing transitions went to A25; after, about 44% go to A20 and 37% to A19, while A25 drops to about 0.5%. [DIR004]
- A19 and A20 now lead to A25: A19 goes to A25 in about 97% of its occurrences, and A20 goes to A25 in about 95% of its occurrences. [DIR002, DIR003]
- Ordering relations confirm that A40 now precedes A19 and A20, and A19/A20 precede A25. In the later window, A40 appears before A19 in about 44% of traces and before A20 in about 55%; A19 appears before A25 in about 43% and A20 before A25 in about 54%. [REL003, REL005, REL002, REL004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window a new branch was inserted between A40 and A25, where the process goes from A40 to either A19 or A20 and then to A25, instead of directly from A40 to A25. This is directly shown by the shift in A40's successor distribution, the new successor relations from A19/A20 to A25, and the ordering and lag evidence. The change is large and consistent across multiple indicators. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A19 and A20 existed but were extremely rare before; however, the magnitude and coherence of the change make a structural modification the most plausible interpretation.
