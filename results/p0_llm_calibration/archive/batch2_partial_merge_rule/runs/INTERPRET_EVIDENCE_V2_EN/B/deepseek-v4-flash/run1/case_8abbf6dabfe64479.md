### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20, A25 and A26 appear in about 31%, 26%, 22% and 23% of traces respectively, whereas before they were each present in at most 0.5% of traces (A25 not at all). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- A06 participation rises from about 12% to 28% of traces, and its mean position moves from about 0.46 to 0.89 of the trace length. [OCC001, POS001]
- A06's outgoing transitions become dominated by A48 (about 86% of its occurrences) instead of being spread over many targets; A47's outgoing to A48 drops from 96% to 75%, with A06 rising from 1% to 24%. [DIR001, DIR007]
- A42's outgoing transitions shift from A27 (about 92% before) to A19, A20, A25 and A26 (about 29%, 26%, 21% and 23% after). [DIR006]
- The new activities A19, A20, A25 and A26 now lead to A27 in about 95–98% of their occurrences, and they precede A27 in about 30%, 26%, 21% and 23% of traces respectively. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL010, REL016, REL021, REL025]
- A42 and A27 are now typically two steps apart instead of adjacent (lag 2 in about 97% of cases after vs. lag 1 in 92% before). [LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in which a block of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26) is introduced and executed before A27, while A06's routing is redirected to A48 and A42's routing is redirected to these new activities instead of A27. The appearance of these activities from near zero, their consistent routing to A27, the order relations showing they precede A27, and the shift in A42–A27 lag all point to the same change. A competing explanation that these are just sampling differences in variant frequencies is unlikely given the large and consistent differences across multiple indicators, but the evidence cannot rule out that the change is a combination of several modifications rather than a single one.
