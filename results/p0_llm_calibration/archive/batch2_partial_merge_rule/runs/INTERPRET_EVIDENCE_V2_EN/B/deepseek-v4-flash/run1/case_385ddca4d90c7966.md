### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a segment of the process previously executed as A26 → A27 → A28 is now, in about half of the cases, executed as A26 → A33/A32 → A28. This is directly shown by the drop in A27 participation, the appearance of A32 and A33, the change in A26's outgoing transitions, and the shift in the A26–A28 lag from 2 to 3 steps. The consistency across occurrence, direction, relation, and lag indicators makes a sampling artefact unlikely. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window simply contains a different mix of variants, but the magnitude and coherence of the changes argue against that. The changes involving A04, A23, and A24 appear separate and are not part of this hypothesis.
