### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural reordering in which A10 is largely absent from most later cases, A25 now routes to A27 instead of to A35/A36/A41/A42, A26 and A27 occur before A35/A36/A41/A42 rather than after them, and A30 occurs later, after those activities, instead of immediately after A26. The direct routing, order and lag evidence is consistent across multiple activities and is based on large support in both windows. A competing explanation that the differences are only sampling variation in variant frequencies is unlikely given the size and consistency of the relational and positional changes, although the small number of later traces containing A10 means its near-disappearance could partly reflect a rare-variant shift.
