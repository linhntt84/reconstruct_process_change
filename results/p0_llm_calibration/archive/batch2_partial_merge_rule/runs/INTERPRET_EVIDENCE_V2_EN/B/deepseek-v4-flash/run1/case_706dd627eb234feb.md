### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process: before, the flow ran A25 → {A35, A36, A41, A42} → A27, with A26 → A30; in the later window it runs A25 → A27 and A26 → {A35, A36, A41, A42} → A30. The successor changes, the earlier positions of A26/A27 and later positions of the group, the reversed order relations, and the adjacency changes all point to the same swap. The number of qualifying items far exceeds the 0.7 expected by chance, and the signs are consistent across indicators. A competing explanation—that the difference reflects sampling variation in variant frequencies—is not fully excluded, but the magnitude and consistency of the reordering make it unlikely. Some lag items rest on very few observations in one window, but the core reordering is supported by the stronger relational and positional evidence.
