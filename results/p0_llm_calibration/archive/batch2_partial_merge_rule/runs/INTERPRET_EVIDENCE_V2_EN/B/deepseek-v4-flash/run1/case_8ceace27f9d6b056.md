### Observed differences

- In the later window, A06 appears in about 57% of traces instead of about 22%, and its mean occurrences per trace rise from about 0.24 to 0.60; it also occurs earlier in the trace (mean relative position from about 0.43 to 0.18). [OCC001, POS001]
- A08 appears less often: participation drops from about 22% to 8% of traces, and mean occurrences per trace from 0.23 to 0.08. [OCC002]
- Activities A19, A20, A25 and A26 are essentially new in the later window, appearing in about 30%, 27%, 22% and 24% of traces respectively, from near zero before. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- A14's outgoing behaviour changes: before, about 83% of its transitions went to A15; later, about 53% go to A06 and 45% to A15. [DIR003]
- A06's successor becomes almost always A15: about 90% of its outgoing transitions later, versus about 21% before. [DIR001]
- A42's successor changes strongly: before, about 82% of its transitions went to A27; later, it goes to A19 (about 30%), A20 (24%), A26 (22%) and A25 (19%), and almost never to A27. [DIR007]
- The new activities A20, A25 and A26 lead to A27 in about 94%, 88% and 96% of their outgoing transitions respectively. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- Ordering evidence places A14 immediately before A06 in about 96% of co-occurring traces later, A06 before A15 in about 30% of traces later, A42 before A19/A20/A25/A26, and those new activities before A27. [LAG002, REL002, REL021, REL025, REL028, REL030, REL024, REL027, REL029]
- The lag between A42 and A27 shifts from mostly 1 step before to mostly 2 steps later, and the lag between A15 and A27 shifts from 4–5 steps to 5 steps. [LAG007, LAG005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a segment of the form A14 → A06 → A15 → … → A42 → (one of A19/A20/A25/A26) → A27 appears, replacing an earlier route in which A42 went directly to A27 and A06 was less central. The supporting signs are consistent across occurrence, successor, relational and lag indicators: A14 now often leads to A06, A06 almost always leads to A15, A42 no longer leads to A27 but to the newly appearing activities, and those activities lead to A27. [DIR003, DIR001, DIR007, REL021, REL025, REL028, REL030, REL024, REL027, REL029]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two windows are finite samples and some of the differences, especially the appearance of A19/A20/A25/A26, reflect a shift in variant frequencies rather than a deliberate process modification. However, the number of distinct, mutually consistent differences is far above what would be expected by chance, and the ordering evidence directly shows the new sequence. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, LAG002, LAG007]
