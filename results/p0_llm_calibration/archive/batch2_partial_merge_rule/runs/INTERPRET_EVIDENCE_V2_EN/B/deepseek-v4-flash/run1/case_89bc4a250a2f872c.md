### Observed differences

- In the later window, A32, A33 and A34 each drop from appearing in essentially every trace to about 46%, 55% and 55% of traces; their mean occurrences per trace fall by roughly 0.45–0.55. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A25's successor changes: before, about 45% of its outgoing transitions went to A33 and 52% to A34; after, about 96% go to A26. [DIR001]
- A28's successor also shifts: before about 95% went to A32; after about 43% go to A32, 24% to A34 and 30% to A33. [DIR002]
- A33 and A34 now lead to A29: A33's outgoing to A29 is about 44% (before 0%), and A34's to A29 about 54% (before 0%); their previous transitions to A26 largely disappear. [DIR003, DIR004]
- Relational evidence shows A25 no longer co-occurs with A32/A33/A34 in about half the traces, and A32 almost never co-occurs with A33 or A34 after (co-occurrence near 0% vs 100% before). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL013, REL014]
- Ordering changes: A25 and A26 become adjacent (lag 1 in about 96% of cases, vs lag 3 in 94% before); A25 to A33/A34 moves from lag 1–2 to lag 4–5; A33/A34 to A29 moves from lag 5 to lag 1–2. [LAG001, LAG003, LAG004, LAG007, LAG008]
- Positionally, A33 and A34 occur later in the trace (mean relative position rises from about 0.43 to 0.54–0.55), while A25, A26 and A28 shift only slightly. [POS005, POS006, POS001, POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window reflects a different main flow: A25 is now followed almost immediately by A26, whereas before it was followed by A33 or A34; A33 and A34, when they occur, are now followed by A29 rather than feeding into A26; and A32, A33 and A34 are no longer mandatory steps. The supporting signs are direct: successor distributions, co-occurrence and order, and step distances all point to the same rearrangement, and the changes are large relative to the 200-trace samples. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window is a mixture of two stable variants, one with A25→A26 and one with A25→A33/A34, rather than a single changed process; the evidence does not separate a mixture from a structural shift.
