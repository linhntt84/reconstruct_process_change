### Observed differences

- In the later window, all four activities run in far more cases: A29 and A30 appear in about 90 of 100 traces (previously about 58 in 100), and A31 and A32 in about 88 of 100 (previously about 43 in 100). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- The clearest relational change: before, no trace contained one of A29/A30 together with one of A31/A32 (0 of 200 traces); in the later window they appear together in about 79 of 100 traces, always with A29/A30 before A31/A32. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013]
- Within-pair behaviour is stable in both windows: A29 and A30 still co-occur whenever either appears, in either order, and the same holds for A31 and A32. [REL009, REL014]
- Routing shifted accordingly: from A25, transitions previously spread evenly over A29, A30, A31 and A32 (roughly 19–34% each) and now go almost only to A29 (about 44%) and A30 (about 47%); A29 and A30 now hand over to A31/A32 in about 20–27% of their outgoing transitions and reach A26 directly in only about 6–8% of cases (previously about 42–58%). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The distance from A25 to A26 grew from always 3 event positions to 5 positions in about 79 of 100 cases (3 positions in the remaining ~21); distances from A25 to A31/A32 and from A29/A30 to A26 similarly grew from 1–2 to 3–4 steps. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005]
- Relative positions moved only slightly: A25, A29 and A30 sit a little earlier and A26, A31, A32 a little later, with mean shifts of roughly 0.02–0.05 on the relative-position scale. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block between A25 and A26 was restructured: previously each case executed exactly one of two exclusive pairs — A29 with A30 (about 58 in 100 cases) or A31 with A32 (about 42 in 100) — whereas in the later window about 79 of 100 cases run both pairs in sequence, A29/A30 first and A31/A32 second, while the old single-pair behaviour persists in about 21 of 100 cases (about 12 with only A29/A30 and about 9 with only A31/A32). [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, LAG001, DIR001]

The support is direct and internally consistent rather than a pile-up of independent confirmations: the new co-occurrences with a stable order, the widened A25–A26 distance, the rerouting from A25 and from A29/A30 towards A31/A32, and the participation increases all describe the same block, and the shares interlock (about 79 + 12 + 9 of 100 traces, with the ~21 of 100 cases still at distance 3 matching the single-pair residues). The number of qualifying items far exceeds the roughly 0.41 expected by chance in this screening, and grouping items by shared activities still leaves one large, coherent difference. [REL010, LAG001, DIR002, OCC003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot fully rule out that the two windows sample different variant mixes of an otherwise stable process, although a jump from 0 of 200 to about 157 of 200 traces for the combined behaviour is not a plausible sampling fluctuation. POS and LAG are based on event positions, so the conclusion concerns order and distance within traces rather than clock time, and because the internal order within each pair varies across cases, the hypothesis is about which activities run between A25 and A26, not about one fixed total sequence. [REL010, LAG001, POS005, POS006]
