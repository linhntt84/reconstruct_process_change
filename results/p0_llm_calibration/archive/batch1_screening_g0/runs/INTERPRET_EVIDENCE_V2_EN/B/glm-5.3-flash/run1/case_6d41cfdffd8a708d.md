### Observed differences

- The four alternative activities A14, A15, A20 and A21 were frequent before: each ran in about 24–28 in 100 cases, they never co-occurred, and their shares sum to roughly every case, so before, virtually every case contained exactly one of them. In the later window, each appears in only about 4–8 in 100 cases, so about 78 in 100 cases contain none of them [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL002, REL003].
- Their presence alongside the main-chain activities collapsed in the same way: A15 or A21 co-occurred with A23, A24 or A25 in about 25–28 in 100 cases before, but in only about 4–5 in 100 cases in the later window, with the profiles collapsing to "only the A23/A24/A25 side present" [REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009].
- Routing concentrated on the direct chain: before, about 48 in 100 of A23's outgoing transitions went to A24 and about 52 in 100 to one of the four alternatives; in the later window, about 91 in 100 go to A24. Likewise A24 → A25 rose from about 53 to about 87 in 100 of A24's outgoing transitions [DIR001, DIR002].
- Spacings tightened accordingly: the A23 → A25 distance was always 3 event positions before, and is 2 positions in about 78 in 100 later cases; A23 → A24 and A24 → A25 now sit at 1 position in about 9 in 10 cases, versus roughly half before [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003].
- Among the few remaining occurrences of A15, the mean event position moved slightly earlier (about 0.52 to 0.48 of the trace), but this rests on only about 9 later cases and is weak [POS001].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative branch built around A14, A15, A20 and A21 is now usually skipped. Before, exactly one of these four mutually exclusive activities was inserted into essentially every case — between A23 and A24 in about half the cases, and between A24 and A25 in the other half — whereas in the later window the plain sequence A23 → A24 → A25 dominates, running in about three quarters of cases with no alternative activity at all. The signs are direct and consistent: participation of all four activities collapsed (47–55 supporting traces before versus 9–16 after) [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004], successor shares of A23 and A24 concentrated almost entirely on A24 and A25 [DIR001, DIR002], the A23 → A25 spacing shortened from three positions to two [LAG002], and pairwise relations collapsed to "neither present" in about 90 in 100 cases [REL001, REL002, REL003]. With 86 units tested, only about 0.43 items would qualify by chance without a change, far fewer than the distinct differences observed here.

The evidence shows a strong reduction in how often this alternative branch is executed, not its removal, since the four activities still occur in about 4–8 in 100 later cases. The main competing explanation — the two windows sampling different mixtures of case variants, so that variant frequencies differ without a structural change — cannot be fully ruled out, although a drop from near-universal coverage to about one in five cases, consistent across occurrence, routing, relational and spacing indicators, is difficult to attribute to sampling alone. The many evidence items largely mirror one underlying shift rather than independent confirmations, and the small positional shift of A15 rests on too few cases to carry weight [POS001].
