### Observed differences

- In the later window, A16 and A17 each appear in about 33 in 100 traces, down from about 78 in 100 earlier; neither activity repeats within a trace in either window [OCC001, OCC002].
- In the later window, A18 and A19 each appear in about 68 in 100 traces, up from about 23 in 100 [OCC003, OCC004].
- The observed continuations from A15 shifted: the share of A15's outgoing transitions leading to A16 fell from about 78% to about 33%, while the share leading to A18 rose from about 23% to about 68% [DIR001].
- The four activities behave as two blocks. A16 and A17 occurred together in about 78 in 100 earlier traces (always A16 before A17), while in the later window both are absent together in about 68 in 100 [REL005]; A18 and A19 show the mirror image, both absent in about 78 in 100 earlier and together in about 68 in 100 later, always A18 before A19 [REL012].
- A16 and A18 were never observed in the same trace in either window; the balance flipped from A16-without-A18 (about 78 in 100) to A18-without-A16 (about 68 in 100), and the same flip appears for A17 against A18 and A19 [REL006, REL007, REL009, REL010].
- A15 and A23 appear in every trace in both windows. A23 ran mostly alongside A16/A17 earlier (co-occurrence about 78 in 100, A23 alone about 22) and mostly without them later (A23 alone about 68 in 100) [REL008, REL011]; conversely, A18/A19 now co-occur with A23 in about 68 in 100 traces, up from about 23 [REL013, REL014].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the path out of A15 was rerouted between the windows: earlier, traces typically ran A15 → A16 → A17 (about 78 in 100), while in the later window they typically run A15 → A18 → A19 (about 68 in 100); the two paths are mutually exclusive, and the earlier path is still taken in about 33 in 100 of later traces [DIR001, REL001, REL005, REL003, REL012].

The rerouting is observed directly in A15's successor profile rather than only in marginal frequencies, and it is consistent across all three indicator types: the two pairs' participation moved by the same 0.45 in opposite directions, every pairwise relation flips in lockstep, and A23's company changed exactly as this substitution implies while A23 itself stayed in all traces [DIR001, OCC001, OCC003, REL008, REL013]. The signal also far exceeds what the screening expects without a change: 19 items qualified where about 0.32 would qualify by chance across the 64 tested units, and they collapse into this one coherent difference, each larger than about 199 of 200 random re-splits [OCC002, DIR001, REL006].

The evidence cannot rule out a shift in the mix of two pre-existing process variants: if both paths already existed, two 200-trace samples could in principle show different variant shares without any structural modification, although a 45-point swing is far beyond typical sampling variation at this sample size. Note also that the routing shares are computed over A15's observed outgoing transitions rather than over cases, and a low participation figure means only that an activity was rarely observed in that window, not that it is absent from the process [DIR001, OCC001].
