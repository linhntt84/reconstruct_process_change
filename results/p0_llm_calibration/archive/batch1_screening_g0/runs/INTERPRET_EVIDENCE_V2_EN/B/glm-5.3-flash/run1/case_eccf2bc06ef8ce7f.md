### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of two structural differences. First, the A04 step is now skipped in many traces: A14 proceeds directly to A15 where it previously passed through A04, which accounts for A04's participation halving and for its former partners now usually appearing without it. Second, the late-process segment formed by A19, A20, A25 and A26 now runs after A28/A29 and before A33, whereas it previously ran between A46 and A27; the routing is consistent with a shift from "A46 → one of A19/A20/A25/A26 → A27 → A28 → A29 → A33" towards "A46 → A27 → A28 → A29 → one of A19/A20/A25/A26 → A33". [OCC001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, REL002, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016]

The placement reading rests on direct relational and positional evidence, not on frequency alone: five activity pairs reverse their typical order, the block's mean position moves later while A27/A28 move slightly earlier with the block's participation roughly stable, and new lag-1 adjacencies (A28→A19, A29→A19, A46→A27) appear exactly where the new arrangement predicts. The old routing largely collapsed rather than coexisting (A19 to A27 fell from about 95% to 17%), and the roughly three distinct differences (A04, the block reordering, the smaller rise in A10) exceed the about one that this screening configuration would produce by chance, making sampling noise alone an unlikely explanation. [REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG012, LAG016, LAG020, DIR004, OCC002]

Limitations remain: the later window could reflect a different mix of process variants — for example a new variant added while the old routing persists at low share — rather than a single clean relocation, and the evidence cannot fully separate these. Some lag details rest on very few before-window observations (A19→A29, A26→A29 and A29→A19 had only 1 supporting case before), so the fine sequencing of the new arrangement is less certain than its direction, and the rise in A10 is based on small counts. [LAG007, LAG010, LAG016, OCC002]
