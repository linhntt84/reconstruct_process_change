### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is a reversal of execution order between two groups of activities. Before, A15, A16, A18 and A19 preceded A22 and A26 in about 995 of 1,000 traces; in the later window, A22 and A26 precede these four activities in about 84 of 100 traces, with roughly 16 in 100 keeping the old order. [REL009, REL013, REL020, REL023, LAG009, LAG010]
- The optional activities flipped the same way: A14, which previously always came after A15/A16/A18/A19/A22/A26 (in all of the roughly 31 in 100 traces containing it), now precedes them in about 18 of 100 traces; A17 and A20 likewise moved from after to before A15/A16/A18/A19 and A22/A26. [REL001, REL007, REL008, REL015, REL025]
- Successors changed accordingly: A14, A17, A20 and A21, which previously led almost exclusively to A27 (98–100 in 100 of their observed outgoing transitions), now lead mostly to A22 (78–86 in 100); A15, A16, A18 and A19 now lead to A27 (16–25 in 100) where they previously led to A22 (21–28 in 100, now 2–6). A26 now leads to A15/A16/A18/A19 (about 20–22 in 100 each) instead of A14/A17/A20/A21, and A37 shows the mirror image, now leading to A20/A17/A21/A14 instead of A15/A16/A18/A19. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, DIR008, DIR010, DIR011]
- Event-position distances swapped: A27 now follows A26 about 5 steps later (84 of 100 traces) versus 2 steps before (995 of 1,000), and A22 now follows A37 about 2 steps later (84 of 100) versus 5 steps before (995 of 1,000). [LAG009, LAG010]
- All ten activities shifted position consistently: A15, A16, A18 and A19 occur later in the trace (mean relative position about 0.39 → 0.60), A14, A17, A20 and A21 earlier (about 0.68 → 0.39), and A22 and A26 slightly earlier (about 0.47 → 0.39 and 0.65 → 0.57). Participation is nearly stable except A14 (about 31 → 22 in 100 traces) and A20 (about 20 → 29 in 100). [POS001, POS002, POS007, POS009, POS010]
- Pairs such as A15→A27 and A26→A15, observed in only 1 trace before, occur in about 168 of 200 traces in the later window; the before-side of these items rests on a single observation. [LAG001, LAG005]
- Separately, the observed outgoing transitions of A05 redistributed towards A04 (about 20 → 44 in 100), away from A03 (44 → 31) and A09 (36 → 24). [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two segments of the process exchanged their relative order: before, the four activities A15, A16, A18 and A19 (in varying order) sat between A37 and A22, which followed about 5 steps after A37, while one of A14, A17, A20 or A21 sat between A26 and A27, which followed 2 steps after A26; in the later window this is reversed, with one of A14/A17/A20/A21 between A37 and A22 (now 2 steps) and the four activities between A26 and A27 (now 5 steps). [DIR010, DIR011, LAG009, LAG010, REL009]

The support is direct and consistent: the order relations flip with full co-occurrence and 200-trace support in both windows; the successor changes at A26, A37 and the eight segment activities all point the same way; the two lag distances swap symmetrically; and the position shifts of all ten activities match in direction and size. The distinct differences (this order reversal, plus the separate rerouting at A05) clearly exceed the roughly 0.6 items expected by chance across the 129 tested units. [REL009, DIR010, DIR011, LAG009, LAG010, POS002, DIR001]

Limitations: the evidence shows that order and routing changed, not how; a variant-mix explanation, in which an already existing rare order became dominant, would look identical here, although a near-complete reversal (about 995 of 1,000 traces to 84 of 100 in the opposite order) is unlikely to arise from sampling variation alone with 200 traces per window. The before-side lag values for pairs like A15→A27 rest on a single observation each and are weak, so the conclusion rests on the fully supported relational, directional and lag items; the rerouting at A05 is a separate difference this hypothesis does not cover. [LAG001, LAG009, LAG010, DIR001]
