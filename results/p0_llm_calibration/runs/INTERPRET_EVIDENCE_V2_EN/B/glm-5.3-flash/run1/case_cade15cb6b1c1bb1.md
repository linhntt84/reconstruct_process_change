### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A20, A28, A29 and A30 each ran in all 200 traces (participation 100%, about one occurrence per trace). In the later window they have practically disappeared: A28 appears in 1 of 200 traces, A20 and A29 in 2 each, and A30 in 3 — under 2 in 100. [OCC002, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]
- The reverse holds for A19, A21, A22 and A23: absent or nearly absent earlier (0–2 of 200 traces), in the later window each participates in about 21–29 in 100 traces (43–57 of 200). They practically never co-occur (pairwise co-occurrence at most about 0.5 in 100) and their shares sum to roughly all traces, so nearly every later trace contains exactly one of them. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL018, REL019, REL025]
- The relational behaviour flipped accordingly. Earlier, A46 preceded the four activities (A46 first in about 99–100 of 100 co-occurrences) and each of the four preceded A31 (about 99–100 of 100), with no fixed order among the four themselves. In the later window, A46 precedes A19, A21, A22 or A23, and each of these in turn precedes A31, in essentially every trace in which it appears. [REL013, REL016, REL017, REL038, REL039, REL041, REL042, REL043, REL044, REL008, REL009, REL023, REL024, REL029, REL030, REL034, REL035]
- A46's outgoing transitions moved from roughly even splits to A20, A28, A29 and A30 (about 21–25% each) to splits towards A19, A21, A22 and A23 (about 20–28% each); the new activities themselves lead almost exclusively to A31 (about 98% of their observed outgoing transitions). [DIR009, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- The span from A46 to A31 contracted: earlier the pair occurred at a distance of five event positions in essentially all traces where it was seen within five steps; in the later window the distance is two positions in about 93 of 100 traces. [LAG016]
- A31 and A46 still run in every trace in both windows. A31's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (about 0.45 to 0.40) and A46's slightly later (about 0.30 to 0.33), with the new activities sitting between them at mean positions around 0.35–0.37. [POS009, POS010, POS001, POS003, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A46 and A31 is executed differently in the later window: the four activities A20, A28, A29 and A30, which previously always ran between A46 and A31, are no longer executed (1–3 of 200 traces), and each case instead runs exactly one of A19, A21, A22 or A23 in that same position. This is a hypothesis supported by the evidence, not a confirmed modification. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, REL008, REL009, REL023, REL024, REL029, REL030, REL034, REL035]

The support is direct and consistent: relational evidence places each new activity between A46 and A31 in nearly all of its appearances, A46's successor distribution shifted from the old group to the new set, and the A46→A31 distance shrank from about five positions to about two — matching four always-present activities giving way to a single one. The participation changes are large and internally consistent (a mutually exclusive new set covering essentially all traces; the old set collapsing from full coverage), and the 87 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.82 expected by chance in an unchanged process, with every item's difference larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits. [DIR009, LAG016, REL008, REL023, REL029, REL034, OCC001, OCC003, OCC006]

Limitations remain: the two windows are finite samples, and the evidence cannot fully rule out that both behaviours pre-existed as variants of one process whose mixture proportions differed sharply between the sampled windows — although swings from 200/200 to 1–3/200 and from 0–2/200 to 43–57/200 go well beyond typical sampling variation. The residual later appearances of A20, A28, A29 and A30 (1–3 traces each) are too few to characterise, zero participation in a window only means the activity was not observed there, and most other lag differences rest on only 1–3 later traces and were not relied upon; several items also describe the same underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, LAG001, LAG003, LAG006]
