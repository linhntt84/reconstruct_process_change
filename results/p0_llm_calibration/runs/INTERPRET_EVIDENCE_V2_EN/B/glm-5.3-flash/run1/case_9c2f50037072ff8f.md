### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the flow after A40 was rerouted and A02's late branch receded: A40 passes directly to A25 instead of through A19/A20; A19 and A20 occur just as often but about five steps after A40, are now reached from A26/A27, and lead to A27/A28 rather than to A25; and A02 occurs in far fewer traces, earlier in the trace, with its link to A46 rarely observed while A45 leads to A46 directly. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR006, DIR007, OCC001, POS002, POS003, LAG010, LAG013]

The hypothesis rests on direct relational evidence rather than frequency shifts alone: successor redistributions with substantial support in both windows (A40 with about 200 outgoing transitions per window; A19 with 109 vs 106; A26/A27 with about 200 each), observed order flips between A19/A20 and A25/A26, and gap changes that match the new routes; the roughly 40 qualifying items also far exceed the 0.68 items expected by chance in an unchanged process. [DIR006, DIR002, DIR004, REL007, REL010, LAG002, LAG010]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of existing variants (or of cases) rather than a modified structure: each after-window route is backed by roughly 45–55 traces, far too many to be a rare-variant fluke, but the sample cannot show whether this routing existed before as a rare variant that became dominant. Details resting on few observations — A02's new successor spread (about 23 outgoing transitions), the A06 increase (4 vs 19 traces) and the A02 gap items (4 pairs after) — remain weak, and items describing the same traces, such as the six A02 relation items, overlap rather than confirm independently. [DIR001, OCC002, LAG001, LAG012, REL001]
