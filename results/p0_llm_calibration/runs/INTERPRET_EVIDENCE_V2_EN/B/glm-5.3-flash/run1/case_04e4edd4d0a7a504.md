### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the case flow was restructured so that the four activities A36, A37, A42 and A43 — previously an optional group appearing together in about half of the cases, after A26 and before A31 — now run in every case as an early stage entered from A25 and followed by A28, while A27 became an every-case step after A26 with A31 following it. The one-to-five-step stretch of the A25-to-A28 distance matches the four activities now sitting between A25 and A28. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC005, OCC006, REL002, REL013, REL017, DIR001, LAG001]

The hypothesis rests on direct relational and positional evidence rather than frequency shifts alone: co-occurrence of the four activities with A25, A26, A27, A28 and A31 went from partial (about 54 in 100) to complete with stable orderings on each side, the successor profiles of A25, A26 and the four activities were rerouted accordingly, and positions moved in the same direction. Occurrence, position, relation, routing and lag indicators describe one coherent picture, every flagged difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data, and 60 flagged items against roughly one expected by chance for this screening cannot be explained by noise as a whole. [REL002, REL017, REL021, DIR002, DIR003, POS006, LAG001]

Limitations: many items are facets of the same trace behaviour — for example A36's participation, position, relations and routing — so they are not independent confirmations; the internal order of the four activities varies from case to case, and the evidence shows event order and positions rather than clock time, so whether they now run one after another or overlap cannot be decided. Several lag comparisons rest on only one to three observations and were not relied on here. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is a sampling difference in variant frequencies: the earlier window may under-represent a variant in which the four activities and A27 always run. That would require five activities to move from roughly half to full participation across two 200-trace samples by chance alone, which the consistent order, routing and lag changes make unlikely, but finite samples cannot rule it out. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC005, OCC006]
