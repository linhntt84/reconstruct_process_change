### Observed differences

- The clearest difference is an order reversal between two groups of activities. In the earlier window, A15, A16, A18 and A19 each came before A20, A21, A22 and A23 in 100% of the traces containing each of the 15 pairs. In the later window these orders are reversed or balanced: A20 precedes A15 in about 77 of 100 such traces (before: 0), precedes A16 in about 75 of 100 and A19 in about 92 of 100; A22 and A23 precede A19 in about 75 of 100; A21 precedes A15 or A16 in about 22–23 of 100. The paired activities still co-occur in essentially all traces, with no repetition or interleaving in either window. [REL001-REL015]
- Routing was redistributed accordingly. In the earlier window the group formed a fixed chain: A19 always passed to A20, A21 always passed to A29, and A28 always fed A18. In the later window, A19 sends about half of its outgoing transitions to A29 and most of the rest to A21, A22 or A23 (A20 only about 8 in 100); A21 goes to A29 only about half the time and otherwise returns to A15, A16, A18 or A19; A20, A22 and A23, which previously only flowed onward within their own group, now send roughly 30–45 in 100 transitions back to A15, A16, A18 or A19; A15, A16 and A18 in turn transition directly to A20–A23 in about 30–40 of 100 of their transitions; and A28 feeds A20 in about half of its transitions. [DIR001-DIR009]
- Event positions moved consistently with the reversal: A20's mean position moved earlier in the trace (about 0.64 to 0.56), A22 and A23 slightly earlier (about 0.68 to 0.62), while A15 and A16 moved later (about 0.57 to 0.62–0.63) and A19 later (about 0.61 to 0.69). [POS001-POS008]
- Distances changed in the same direction: A15 and A16, always adjacent before, are adjacent in only about 54 of 100 cases afterwards; the ordered pair A15 then A20 within five steps occurs in about 47 of 200 later-window traces (before: 200 of 200) and is then typically one step apart; A19 reaches A29 one step later in about half of the traces (before: always five steps); A28 reaches A20 one step later in about half (before: always five steps). [LAG001, LAG003, LAG022, LAG036, LAG038]
- A separate, smaller shift concerns A43: the share of its outgoing transitions to A40 rose from about 34 to about 51 in 100 (A44 fell from about 66 to 49 in 100), A43 appears in about 61 of 100 traces versus about 70 before, and immediate A43→A44 successions fell from about 70 to 54 in 100. [DIR010, POS009, LAG039]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the relative execution order and wiring between the group A15/A16/A18/A19 and the group A20/A21/A22/A23 changed: in the later window, A20, A21, A22 and A23 frequently run before A15, A16, A18 and A19, and transitions from the first group back to the second appear that were absent before; A19 and A21 also reach A29 much sooner (A19 passes to A29 directly in about half of its transitions), and A28 feeds A20 rather than A18 in about half of its transitions. [REL001-REL015, DIR001-DIR009, LAG022, LAG038]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: 73 items qualified where about 0.64 would be expected for an unchanged process, so the set as a whole is not explainable by sampling noise, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits; the order change is shown by relational evidence rather than inferred from position shifts alone; and the indicators agree quantitatively — for example, A15 precedes A20 in about 24 of 100 later-window traces, matching the 47-of-200 support for that ordered pair within five steps. Co-occurrence stays at 100% with no repetition or interleaving, which rules out activity removal or new repetition as competing explanations. [REL001-REL015, LAG003, LAG019, LAG020]

Limitations remain: each window holds only 200 traces, so a shift in the mix of process variants — for instance an alternative path that was absent or rare before and frequent afterwards — could produce the same reversal, and the evidence cannot separate a rewiring of one process from a changed variant frequency. Many items describe the same underlying reordering and are not independent confirmations. The routing shift around A43, A40 and A44 is a separate change whose connection to the reordering, if any, the evidence does not show. [DIR010, POS009, LAG039]
