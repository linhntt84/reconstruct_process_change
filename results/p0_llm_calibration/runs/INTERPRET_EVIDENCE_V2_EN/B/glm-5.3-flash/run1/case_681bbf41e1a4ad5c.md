### Observed differences

- In the later window, routing out of A26 is almost entirely redirected: about 98 in 100 of A26's outgoing transitions go to A27 (about 47 in 100 before), while its transitions to A33 (about 26 in 100 before) and to A32 (about 25 in 100 before) are no longer observed [DIR005].
- A32 and A33 have nearly disappeared: each ran in about 53 in 100 earlier traces but in only 1–2 of the 200 later traces, and their co-occurrence with activities that run in every trace (A14, A15, A26, A28) falls from about 52–54 in 100 traces to at most 1 in 100; A32 and A33 also no longer co-occur with each other [OCC006, OCC007, REL017, REL020, REL026, REL034, REL036].
- A27 became a universal step: it appears in all 200 later traces (about 95 in 200 before) and co-occurs with A14, A15, A26, A28, A40, A45 and A46 in 100 in 100 traces; A26 and A40 precede it in about 98 in 100 cases, and A28, A45 and A46 follow it in about 97–99 in 100 [OCC005, REL016, REL019, REL025, REL028, REL031, REL032, REL033].
- The side-step through A02 between A14 and A15 is much rarer: A02 runs in about 24 in 100 later traces (about 60 before) and, when it runs, sits at a later mean relative position (about 0.20 to 0.36); A14 now continues directly to A15 in about 77 of 100 of its outgoing transitions (about 42 before) and to A02 in about 12 (about 58 before), while A02's own output, formerly about 94 in 100 to A15, is dispersed with only about 45 in 100 still going to A15 [OCC001, DIR001, DIR003, REL002, REL003, POS001].
- A04, A06 and A10 each appear in roughly 20 in 100 later traces, up from about 4–6 in 100 [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004].
- Consistent with a shorter route, A26 and A28 are now two steps apart in about 95 in 100 later traces (about 47 before, when about 51 in 100 were three steps apart) [LAG011].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the branch running from A26 through A32 and A33 (which previously passed on to A28) is no longer executed, with A26 now leading almost exclusively to A27, which has become a step in every case, placed after the A14/A15/A26/A40 block and before A28/A45/A46. The rerouting at A26 is measured on about 200 outgoing transitions in each window, and the collapse of A32/A33 co-occurrence is measured over full 200-trace windows against activities that themselves run in every trace, so the pattern is direct and internally consistent; the tightened two-step spacing from A26 to A28 fits the same shortening. With 77 qualifying items against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process, the volume of evidence cannot be explained by random re-splitting as a whole [DIR005, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, REL017, REL025, REL028, REL031, LAG011].

A second directly supported change is the reduced use of the A02 side-step: A14 usually continues straight to A15, A02's participation fell from about 60 to 24 in 100 traces with its successors dispersed, and A04, A06 and A10 now run in about 20 in 100 traces [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR003, REL002, REL003].

The evidence cannot show outright removal: A32 and A33 were still observed in 1–2 later traces, and an unobserved transition does not prove an edge is absent from the process. The main competing explanation is a shift in the mix of variants between two finite samples — the earlier window may over-represent a variant containing A02, A32 and A33 — although participation swings of about half the traces, replicated across many activity pairs, are hard to produce by sampling alone. The later-window routing and position profiles for A32 and A33 rest on only 1–2 observations and were not relied on, and many of the relational items restate one underlying shift rather than confirming it independently [OCC006, OCC007, DIR006, DIR007, POS002, POS003].
