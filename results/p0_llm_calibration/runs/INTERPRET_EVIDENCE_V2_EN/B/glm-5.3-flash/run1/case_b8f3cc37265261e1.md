### Observed differences

- The relative order of two groups of activities reversed. Before, A19, A22, A25 and A26 ran before A20, A21, A23, A24, A27 and A31; in the later window they run after them. For example, A20 preceded A27 in essentially no cases before and in about 99 in 100 cases in the later window, while A27 before A20 dropped from about 99–100 in 100 to about 0.5 in 100 [REL010, REL015, REL023, REL027]. The same flip appears in the cross-group pairs, e.g. A22 before A20 fell from about 26 in 100 co-occurring cases to about 0.5 in 100, with A20 before A22 rising to about 27 in 100 [REL001, REL006, REL007, REL009, REL012, REL014, REL017, REL020, REL021, REL026, REL029, REL032].

- In the later window, A20, A21, A23 and A24 occur earlier in the case (mean event position about 0.63 of the case before, about 0.40 after), while A19, A22, A25 and A26 occur later (about 0.34 before, about 0.68 after); A27 (about 0.37 to 0.47) and A31 (about 0.56 to 0.66) shift slightly later [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010].

- Routing at the segment boundaries changed accordingly. In the later window, A19, A22, A25 and A26 send about 95–98 in 100 of their observed outgoing transitions to A32, where before about 91–100 in 100 went to A27 [DIR001, DIR004, DIR007, DIR008]. A20, A21, A23 and A24 now send about 21–28 in 100 to A27, where before similar shares went to A32 [DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006]. A31 now continues to A19/A22/A25/A26 instead of A20/A21/A23/A24, and A42 now leads into A20/A21/A23/A24 instead of A19/A22/A25/A26 [DIR009, DIR010].

- Event distances match this: the distance from A31 to A32 shortened from 5 events (about 100 in 100 observed pairs) to 2 events (about 98 in 100) [LAG011], the distance from A42 to A27 lengthened from 2 events (about 92 in 100) to 5 events (about 99 in 100) [LAG014], and A26 to A32 became immediate (1 event apart in about 96 in 100 of 55 pairs) [LAG006]. Several other lag items rest on only 1–3 observations and are individually weak [LAG001, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG012, LAG013].

- Participation barely moved: A20, A21, A23, A24, A27 and A31 appear in all traces in both windows, and each of A19, A22, A25, A26 appears in about 20–28 in 100 traces in both windows, with the four shares together summing to roughly one activity per case [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports, as a possibility rather than a confirmed modification, that two segments of the process exchanged their placement. In the later window, A42 is followed by the block A20, A21, A23, A24 (internal order varying), then A27, then A31, then one of the branch activities A19, A22, A25 or A26, and then A32; before, the branch activity ran between A42 and A27, and the four-activity block ran between A31 and A32. This is shown directly by relational evidence: the pairwise order between each branch activity and each block activity, A27 and A31 reverses almost completely (changes of 0.97–1.0 on a 0–1 scale, each larger than in about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits) [REL005, REL006, REL010, REL011, REL015, REL016, REL019, REL020, REL023, REL024, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030, REL031, REL032].

The placement reading is consistent across the other indicator types: the successor sets at both segment boundaries flip exactly as the exchange predicts [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010], the position shifts of the two groups go in opposite directions [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008], and the A31–A32 and A42–A27 distances move as expected, with 176–200 supporting observations on both sides [LAG006, LAG011, LAG014]. With 66 evidence items against roughly 0.81 expected by chance under this screening, the volume is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces.

Limitations: the windows are finite samples of 200 traces each, so variant frequencies can differ without any change and a rare variant can appear in one window only; that competing explanation cannot be fully excluded, although near-complete order flips in about 99–100 in 100 traces make pure sampling noise implausible. Many items describe the same underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations, and the lag items based on 1–3 observations cannot support the hypothesis on their own [LAG001, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG012, LAG013]. The evidence also does not pin down the exact internal sequence of the block A20/A21/A23/A24, only its placement relative to A42, A27, A31 and A32.
