- The order between the A32/A33 pair and the A26/A28 pair is reversed. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment formed by A32 and A33 (which typically follow one another) is placed differently: in the later window the flow reads A25 → A26 → A28 → A32/A33 → A29, whereas in the earlier window it read A25 → A32/A33 → A26 → A28 → A29. This rests on direct relational evidence — the pairwise order of A32/A33 versus A26/A28 is reversed in about 97–99 in 100 traces — and is consistent across the successor changes, event distances and relative positions, observed on essentially all 200 traces in each window, with unchanged participation for all activities involved. The number of flagged items (22, against roughly 0.62 expected if nothing had changed) also cannot be explained by chance screening as a whole. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG009, LAG010, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

Limitations: with only two aggregate windows, the evidence cannot distinguish a reordering applied within traces from a shift in the mix of two process variants in which both orders already existed at different frequencies. The A02/A14 differences rest on few observations (roughly 14–46 traces) and may reflect rare-variant sampling rather than change; they are not needed for the reordering hypothesis. Positions are event positions, so "earlier/later" refers to sequence placement, not clock time. [POS001, DIR001, LAG001, LAG002]
