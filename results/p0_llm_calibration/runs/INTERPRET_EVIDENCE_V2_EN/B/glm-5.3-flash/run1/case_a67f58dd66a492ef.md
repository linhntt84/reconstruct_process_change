### Observed differences

- In the before window, A15, A23, A24 and A25 each appeared exactly once in every case (200 of 200 traces); in the later window none of the four was observed (0 of 200). [OCC002, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]
- Four activities absent before — A14, A16, A17 and A18 — appear in the later window in about 27, 20, 29 and 26 out of 100 cases respectively (53, 39, 57 and 51 of 200 traces), never more than once per case. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- The four new activities are mutually exclusive: no later case contains two of them, and their shares together cover every case. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL018, REL019, REL023]
- Before, A15, A23, A24 and A25 always occurred together in varying order (each pair split roughly 50/50 on which came first), and each routed onward in roughly equal quarters to the other three and to A26. [REL013, REL014, REL015, REL034, DIR002, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008]
- In the later window, A14, A16, A17 and A18 each route only to A26, and A41's outgoing transitions moved from the old four (about 25% each) to the new four (about 20–29% each). [DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR009]
- The gap from A41 to A26 shortened from 5 steps in every before-case to 2 steps in every later case; A26's mean position moved earlier (about 0.45 to 0.40 of the trace) while A41's moved later (about 0.29 to 0.34). [LAG001, POS009, POS010]
- Where a new activity occurs, it sits between A41 and A26 — A41 precedes it and it precedes A26 in all such cases (observed directly for A14, A17 and A18). [REL008, REL009, REL027, REL028, REL032, REL033]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A41 and A26 was restructured: the fixed block of four activities A15, A23, A24 and A25, which every case previously executed between A41 and A26, appears to have been replaced by exactly one activity chosen from A14, A16, A17 and A18 per case. [OCC002, DIR009, REL013, REL018, LAG001]

The support is direct and consistent: the participation swap (200 of 200 to 0 of 200, and 0 to 39–57 of 200) is far beyond what sampling variation between two 200-trace windows produces; routing and relational evidence agree that A41 now leads to the new activities, which lead only to A26 and never co-occur; and the A41–A26 distance shrinking from 5 to 2 steps matches four activities being replaced by one. The volume of qualifying items (70, against roughly 0.6 expected if nothing had changed) and their strength (differences larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits) cannot be explained by random re-splitting as a whole. [OCC002, OCC006, DIR009, REL018, LAG001]

Limitations: zero participation only means A15, A23, A24 and A25 were not observed in the later sample, so rare occurrences outside it cannot be excluded; the exact split among the new activities (about 20–29 in 100) may reflect sampling variation around a common probability rather than genuinely different frequencies; and the mixed ordering of the old block means the evidence cannot establish what rule, if any, governed its internal sequence. The main competing explanation — that the two windows simply sampled different variant frequencies from an unchanged process — is implausible for the all-or-nothing participation changes but cannot be ruled out for these finer frequency differences. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL013]
