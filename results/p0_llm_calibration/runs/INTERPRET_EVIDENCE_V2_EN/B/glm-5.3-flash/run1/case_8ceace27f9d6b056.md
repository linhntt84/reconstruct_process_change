### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A06 takes place in the main sequence between A14 and A15, and one of four mutually exclusive activities, A19, A20, A25 or A26, takes place between A42 and A27, which previously followed A42 directly; A08 is executed far less often. The support is direct and consistent rather than merely numerous: relational, directional and lag evidence independently shows the same orderings, participation, direction and position components move together for each activity, and 57 items qualified in total against fewer than 1 expected for an unchanged process, so chance cannot account for the set as a whole.
