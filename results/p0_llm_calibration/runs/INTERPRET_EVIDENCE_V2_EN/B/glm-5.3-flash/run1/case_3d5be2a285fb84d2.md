### Observed differences

- In the later window, the eight activities A33, A34, A36, A37, A38, A39, A40 and A41 each appear in about 199 of 200 traces; in the earlier window each appeared in only about 93–110 of 200 (roughly 46–55 in 100). [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009]
- Earlier, these eight split into two four-activity groups that were almost mutually exclusive: cross-group pairs such as A33 with A38 co-occurred in only about 1 in 200 traces, with exactly one of the pair present in about 99 in 100; within a group (e.g., A33 with A34) both were present in about 54 in 100 traces and neither in about 46 in 100. In the later window, every pair among the eight co-occurs in about 99 in 100 traces. [REL041, REL044, REL059, REL068]
- The eight now run as one sequence between A30 and A31: A30 precedes each of them in about 98–99 in 100 later traces and A31 follows each of them in about 99 in 100, whereas earlier A31 followed A33 in about 53 in 100 traces and appeared without it in about 46 in 100. A14, which occurs in every trace, precedes the eight in about 99 in 100 later traces. [REL025, REL033, REL037, REL009, REL013]
- Spacing changed accordingly: pairs that were previously 1–4 steps apart — A30 to A38/A39/A40/A41, and A33/A34/A36/A37 to A31 — are now almost always at the 5-step maximum (about 96–98 in 100 of observed pairs); A37 to A40 within five steps, seen in only 1 earlier trace, now occurs in about 166 of 200 traces. [LAG004, LAG007, LAG008, LAG011, LAG012]
- Routing matches this picture: A30's outgoing transitions, previously spread over about eight targets (roughly 9–15% each), now go about 97% to A33/A34/A36/A37; A31 has all but disappeared as a direct successor of A33/A34/A36/A37 (from about 25 in 100 outgoing transitions to under 1 in 100) while A38–A41 appear as new successors (about 6–8 in 100 each). [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]
- Mean event positions moved consistently: A30 earlier (about 0.66 to 0.58 of the trace), A33/A34/A36/A37 earlier (about 0.74 to 0.65), A40/A41 slightly later (about 0.75 to 0.77) and A31 slightly later (0.82 to 0.84). [POS004, POS005, POS006, POS010, POS011]
- A06 participation halved (about 57 to 27 of 200 traces) and it now occurs later in the traces where it appears (mean 0.21 to 0.36), appearing only alongside the eight activities and mostly before them; its outgoing transitions became much more varied (A15 falls from about 86% to 29% of observed outgoing transitions), though this rests on only about 28 observed transitions. [OCC001, POS001, REL001, DIR001, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window the eight activities A33, A34, A36, A37, A38, A39, A40 and A41 are executed together in essentially every case — the first four followed by the last four, with variable order inside each group of four — positioned after A30 and before A31; earlier, only one of the two four-activity groups ran per case (each in roughly half the cases), directly between A30 and A31. [REL025, REL033, REL044, REL055, REL041, LAG004, LAG012, DIR003]

Supporting signs: participation of all eight rises from roughly half of traces to about 199 in 200; cross-group co-occurrence goes from about 1 in 200 to about 99 in 100; order relations with A30 and A31 hold in about 98–99 in 100 later traces; the successor profiles at A30 and at A33/A34/A36/A37 change exactly as this arrangement implies; and the positional shifts all point the same way. About 108 evidence items qualify against roughly 1.2 expected if nothing had changed, and the occurrence, routing, relational, lag and positional indicators all describe one coherent underlying difference rather than isolated anomalies (each item's difference also exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits). [OCC002, OCC008, REL044, REL033, DIR004, LAG008, POS006]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a newly introduced sequence from an existing rare variant becoming dominant — cross-group co-occurrence was already about 1–3 in 200 traces earlier — so a drastic shift in variant frequencies between the samples would look identical; the order inside each group of four is close to 50/50 for many pairs, so any single fixed path would overstate the regularity; and A06's halved participation and broadened successor profile rest on about 27 later traces and 28 outgoing transitions, so no specific change in A06's routing is established. Many qualifying items restate the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [REL044, REL041, REL068, OCC001, DIR001]
