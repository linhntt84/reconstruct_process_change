### Observed differences

- In the later window, A19 and A20 were almost no longer observed: A19 ran in about 55 in 100 traces before but in only 1 of 200 after, and A20 in about 45 in 100 before versus 2 of 200 after; before, the two never occurred in the same trace, consistent with a choice between them [OCC006, OCC007, REL007].
- A06 became much rarer (about 65 in 100 traces before, about 16 in 100 after) and, when present, sits earlier in the trace (median position about 0.97 versus 0.50) and reaches A46 far less often (about 90% of its outgoing transitions before, about 15% after) [OCC003, POS001, DIR003].
- A40's outgoing transitions flipped from mostly A19 (about 54%) and A20 (about 43%) to mostly A25 (about 80%), and the A40→A25 distance shortened from mostly two steps (about 96 in 100 observed pairs) to one step (about 81 in 100) [DIR006, LAG002].
- Order relations that ran through A19 or A20 vanished: A19 preceded A25 in about 54 in 100 traces before and essentially never after, with A25 now appearing without A19 in about 99.5 in 100 of the observed pairs; the same holds for A20 with A25, A40, A45 and A46, and A06 no longer co-occurs with A19 or A20 (both absent in about 83 in 100 traces after, versus about 17–19 before) [REL001, REL002, REL008, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015].
- The closing sequence was shortened: A45 now goes to A46 in about 89% of its outgoing transitions (about 35% before) instead of to A06 (about 63% before, about 2% after), and the A45→A46 gap is mostly one step (about 88 in 100) where it was mostly two (about 65 in 100) [DIR008, LAG003].
- A02, A04, A08 and A10 each roughly tripled their participation (for example A02 from about 6.5 to about 22 in 100 traces) and now often lead to A25, A44 or A15 rather than their earlier targets [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the flow runs differently: the choice after A40 no longer continues through A19 or A20 but passes directly to A25, and the closing segment A45 → A06 → A46 lost its A06 step, with A45 flowing straight to A46; A19 and A20 were almost not observed afterwards, A06 became rare and earlier in the trace, and A02, A04, A08 and A10 participate about three times as often, frequently followed by A25, A44 or A15 [DIR006, DIR008, LAG002, LAG003, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007].

The support is direct and consistent: the successor mixes of A40 and A45 themselves changed (A40 from about 54% to A19 and 43% to A20, to about 80% to A25; A45 from about 63% to A06 to about 89% to A46), the A40→A25 and A45→A46 step distances both shortened as the intermediate activities disappeared, and the order relations that required A19, A20 or A06 in between collapsed [DIR006, DIR008, LAG002, LAG003, REL005, REL008, REL012].

Limitations: many items restate the same underlying trace behaviour — the relation items about A19 and A20 largely repeat their disappearance, and the A06 items share the same traces — so they are not independent confirmations. A shift in the mix of process variants between the two sampled windows (for instance, the later window drawing mostly cases of a variant without the A19/A20 branch and the A06 step) could produce similar participation and routing differences, and near-zero participation of A19 and A20 strictly means only that they were almost not observed in the later sample [OCC006, OCC007, REL007].
