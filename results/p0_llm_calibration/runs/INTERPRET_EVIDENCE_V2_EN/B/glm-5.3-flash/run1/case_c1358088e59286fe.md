### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A46 and A31 was restructured: in the later window, every observed case executes A20, A28, A29 and A30 — in varying order — between A46 and A31 and after A14/A15/A16, whereas before, A46 was typically followed by one of A19, A21, A22 or A23 and then A31 directly. A related change is that A04, previously executed before A46 in about 62 of 100 cases, runs in only about 26 of 100 later cases. [OCC005, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011, OCC004, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC002, DIR013, LAG013, REL055, REL056, REL079, REL084, REL088]

Supporting signs: about 140 evidence items qualified, versus roughly 1 expected without change, so screening noise cannot account for the set as a whole; the participation flips (0 of 200 versus 200 of 200 traces, and 44 of 200 versus 0) are far beyond what sampling variation produces for activities at these frequencies; and the relational and lag evidence shows the block's position directly — A46 before it, A31 after it, A14/A15/A16 before it — rather than inferring placement from frequencies alone. The occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag items tell one consistent story, although many of the relation items restate the same trace-level changes rather than adding independent confirmation. [OCC005, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011, OCC004, DIR013, LAG013, REL017, REL055, REL079]

Limitations: the windows are finite samples, so the exact shares — the near-even ordering mix within the new block, the modest rises of A02 and A06, and the residual after-profiles of A21, A22 and A23, which rest on only 1–4 traces each — may not reflect the underlying process, and a shift in the mix of existing variants between the samples remains a competing explanation for those finer details. The evidence also cannot show whether A04's decline belongs to the same restructuring or is an independent shift, nor whether the new block contains structure beyond the observed five-step lag window. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, REL052, REL083, LAG013]
