### Observed differences

- The activities A02, A19 and A20 all appear in roughly half as many traces in the later window: A02 in about 9 in 100 traces (from about 30; 59 vs 18 traces), A19 in about 26 (from about 50; 99 vs 52), and A20 in about 28 (from about 51; 102 vs 55). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- Before, essentially every trace contained at least one of A19 or A20, almost always exactly one (about 49% vs 51%, rarely both); in the later window about 47 in 100 traces contain neither of them. [REL005]
- A40 and A25 still occur in virtually every trace in both windows, but A40's outgoing routing shifted: transitions to A02 fell from about 25% to about 1%, transitions to A25 rose from none observed to about 44%, and shares to A19 and A20 fell (about 37% to 23%, and 37% to 26%). [DIR003]
- Ordering and co-occurrence between the A02/A19/A20 activities and A40/A25 roughly halved: A02 before A25 about 26% to 6% of traces, A40 before A02 about 28% to 4%, A19 before A25 about 49% to 25%, A40 before A19 about 49% to 25%, A20 before A25 about 51% to 27%, A40 before A20 about 51% to 28%; co-occurrence of A02 with A19 or A20 nearly disappeared (about 15% to 2% and 16% to 4%). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- Event distances shortened: A40 is followed by A25 mostly 2–3 positions before but mostly 1–2 positions in the later window, and A40 is followed by A20 at a one-position gap in about 95 in 100 cases, up from about 73. [LAG003, LAG004]
- When A02 does occur in the later window, its successors differ (before mainly A19 about 38% and A20 about 45%; after mainly A15 about 39% and A44 about 17%) and its position within the trace spreads out (median about 0.33 to 0.19 of the trace, upper quartile about 0.39 to 0.74); both observations rest on only 18 A02 occurrences. [DIR001, POS001]
- Routing elsewhere is essentially stable: A14 still leads almost always to A15 (about 98% to 95%) and A43 to A44 (about 99% to 92%). [DIR002, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window the segment built around A02 and the A19/A20 alternative is executed in far fewer cases: about 47 in 100 later-window traces contain none of A02, A19 or A20 (essentially none before), and A40 — still present in virtually every trace — now leads most often directly to A25 (about 44 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, against none observed before). A25 itself still occurs in every trace in both windows, so the difference lies in what precedes it, not in A25's presence. [REL005, DIR003, REL003, REL008]

The pattern is direct and consistent: participation of A02, A19 and A20 each roughly halved on substantial trace counts, every ordering share between these activities and A40/A25 roughly halved, and the event distance from A40 to A25 shortened, all pointing to the same trace-level behaviour. Nineteen items qualified in total against well under one expected by chance across the 109 tested units, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL003, REL004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, LAG003, LAG004]

Limitations: the items largely describe one underlying trace-level difference and are not independent confirmations, and sampling differences in variant frequencies between the two 200-trace windows remain a competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude, even though shifts of this size (for example A40-to-A02 routing falling from about 25% to about 1%) are unusual for random re-splits. Details of A02's behaviour when it does occur — new main successors A15 and A44 and a wider position spread — rest on only 18 observations, and two lag comparisons on 2 and 5 observations, so they are weakly supported. [DIR001, POS001, LAG001, LAG002]
