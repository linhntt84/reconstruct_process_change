### Observed differences

- In the later window, A34, A35, A36 and A37 each run in about 99–100 in 100 traces (before: about 40–60 in 100), with about one occurrence per trace (before: about 0.4–0.6); A10's participation rises from about 22 to 61 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

One structural possibility supported by the evidence is that, in the later window, the segment between A30 and A31 runs all four activities A34, A35, A36 and A37 in essentially every case in a fixed order (A30, then A34/A35 in either order, then A36/A37 in either order, then A31), whereas before it ran only one of them per case (each in roughly 40–60 in 100 cases, almost never together); additionally, A10 — previously present in about 22 in 100 cases — now runs in about 61 in 100 cases, typically directly after A43 and before A44/A45/A46. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL011, REL015, REL020, REL026, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR002, REL009, REL010]

The support is direct and consistent: precedence and co-occurrence move from partial and mixed to near-deterministic, the A30–A31 distance grows by about two steps as expected if two further activities are inserted between them, and the successor profiles of A30, A34, A35, A43 and A10 all shift in the same direction; the number of qualifying items (82, against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process) is also far beyond what chance would produce. [REL011, REL013, REL020, LAG003, LAG010, DIR004, DIR005, DIR007]

Limitations: the order within the pairs {A34, A35} and {A36, A37} remains mixed even in the later window (roughly 45–53 in 100 each way), so the block is not fully sequenced; many items describe the same trace-level behaviour, so the item count overstates the amount of independent confirmation; and a competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is that the two windows sample different variant mixtures — the earlier window under-representing a variant that already contained the full four-activity sequence — although near-100% participation across 200 traces per window makes a pure sampling fluctuation unlikely. [REL019, REL032, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
