### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The screening produced 74 evidence items against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed (0.95 over 190 units), a count chance cannot explain as a whole. Within that, the evidence directly and consistently supports the possibility that, in the later window, many traces run an additional mid-process step chosen from A19, A20, A25 and A26 — one alternative per trace — placed after A28 (sometimes after A29) and before A30, with onward flow to A29 or A30. The placement rests on order and adjacency evidence, not participation alone: A28 and A29 gained outgoing edges to the four while their direct edges towards A29/A30 shrank, A28 precedes and A30 follows the four in about 20–30 in 100 traces, and the A28→A29, A28→A30 and A29→A30 gaps stretched by about one event position. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL016, REL025, DIR007, DIR008, REL019, REL034, REL021, REL036, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

A second, behaviourally distinct shift is the heavier and earlier use of A04: from a rare, later-placed activity (10 in 100 traces) to one in about 28 in 100 traces that typically sits directly between A14 and A15, and which precedes the new mid-process step in a minority of traces. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]

Limitations: the windows are finite samples, so the competing explanation that these four activities belong to a pre-existing rare variant that happened not to appear among the 200 earlier traces cannot be fully excluded, although 0 of 200 earlier versus 41–61 of 200 later makes a pure sampling difference unlikely; the smaller shifts around A30 and A43–A47 may be sampling noise; and many items rest on the same traces (for example the relation items pairing each new activity with A28, A29 and A30), so they describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [OCC002, OCC005, POS006, LAG005, REL019, REL020, REL021]
