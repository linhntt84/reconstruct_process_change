### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the earlier exclusive choice between "A27 only" and "A28 with A29" was replaced by a flow in which A28 and A29 (in either order) always execute directly after A20, ahead of A21 and A23, with A27 then running before A24 — so all three activities now occur in every case instead of in roughly half. The relational evidence is direct: the order between A28/A29 and A21/A23 flipped from "A21 and A23 first" in every earlier trace to "A28 and A29 first" in every later trace, the previously unobserved co-occurrences of A27 with A28 and with A29 rose from 0 to 200 of 200 traces, and the successor profiles of A20 and A23 changed completely, which shows a placement and participation change directly rather than a mere frequency shift [REL005, REL006, REL008, REL009, REL013, REL014, DIR001, DIR002].

The 36 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.44 items expected by chance when screening 89 units, so chance alone cannot account for the evidence as a whole; each item's difference is also larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits, and the occurrence, routing, relation, position and lag items all fit one coherent reconstruction, with proportions resting on 96–200 observations per window [OCC001, REL015, LAG001, LAG002, POS005, POS006].

Limitations: this describes behaviour across two finite windows, not a verified model modification. A shift in the mix of pre-existing variants between the windows is the main competing explanation; the completeness of the changes (0 to 200 of 200 traces, order flips in every trace) makes pure sampling fluctuation implausible, but the evidence cannot show whether sequence parts not covered by the tested pairs (for example between A21 and A23) also changed, and items sharing the same traces are not independent confirmations [REL015, LAG007, LAG008].
