### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the dominant route through the process was rerouted: after A20, the main path now runs via A21 and A22 (about 8 in 10 later traces, previously about 2 in 10) instead of via A23 and A24, with both branches continuing through A28; and A30, which previously led almost directly to A31, now often leads to A06, which in turn leads directly to A31 late in the trace, while A02 is now rarely observed. [DIR003, DIR005, DIR007, DIR008, REL009, REL013, REL016, REL021, LAG002, LAG014, OCC001]

The support is direct and consistent: successor profiles changed at A20, A06 and A30, the relational evidence shows the new order explicitly rather than only frequency shifts, and lag and position data confirm A06's new adjacency and late placement; the 65 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.74 expected by chance if nothing had changed. The main competing explanation is a difference in the mix of process variants between the two finite samples of 200 traces rather than a structural change; swings of about 57 percentage points reproduced across occurrence, routing, relational and lag indicators make that hard to maintain, but it cannot be excluded, and many items describe the same underlying trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations.
