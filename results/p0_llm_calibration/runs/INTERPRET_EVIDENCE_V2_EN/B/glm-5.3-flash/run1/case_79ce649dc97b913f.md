### Observed differences

- The four activities A34, A35, A36 and A37 became near-universal in the later window: A34 and A35 now appear in essentially every trace (up from about 61 in 100), and A36 and A37 in about 99 in 100 (up from about 40 in 100). [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]
- Before, these two pairs acted as exclusive alternatives: a trace contained A34/A35 or A36/A37, and both pairs together in only about 1 in 200 traces. In the later window, all four co-occur in about 99 in 100 traces, with A34 and A35 preceding A36 and A37 in about 98 in 100 of them; the order within each pair remains mixed. [REL025, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030]
- The block sits between A30 and A31 in both windows: in the later window A30 precedes each of the four activities, and each of them precedes A31, in about 97–100 in 100 traces. The event distance from A30 to A31 grows from typically 3 steps to 5, and the distances from A30 to A36/A37 and from A34/A35 to A31 grow from 1–2 steps to 3–4 steps, as expected if the other pair's two activities now run in between. Successor profiles agree: A30's transitions now go almost entirely to A34/A35, and A34/A35's transitions include A36/A37 (about 20–28% each) while A31 all but disappears from them. [REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]
- A06 now appears in about 28 in 100 traces (up from about 7 in 100) and sits earlier within traces; about 90% of its outgoing transitions go to A16, usually immediately (a one-step gap in about 98 in 100 cases), and A15 routes to A06 in about 15% of its outgoing transitions, up from under 1%. [OCC001, POS001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, REL001, REL002]
- A10 participation falls from about 10 in 100 to about 2.5 in 100 traces. [OCC002]
- A02's outgoing transition mix changes almost completely, but only about 17 versus 10 observed transitions back the two profiles, so this is weak evidence. [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, nearly every case runs both middle pairs in sequence between A30 and A31 — A30, then A34 and A35 in either order, then A36 and A37 in either order, then A31 — whereas before, each case ran only one of the two pairs (A34/A35 in about 60 in 100 cases, A36/A37 in about 40 in 100, both together in about 1 in 200). This rests on direct relational evidence of co-occurrence and precedence, corroborated by the successor profiles of A30, A34 and A35 and by the widened event distances; with 60 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected for an unchanged process, the set as a whole cannot be explained by chance. A second directly supported shift is the path A15 → A06 → A16, now exercised in about 28 in 100 traces with A06 immediately preceding A16 in most cases. [OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG005, LAG006, LAG008, REL017, REL019, REL021, REL023, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030]

Limitations: the many relation items largely restate one underlying difference and are not independent confirmations. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a sampling shift in variant frequencies: traces containing all four activities already existed at about 0.5 in 100 before, and the later window could in principle over-represent such a pre-existing variant, although a swing from roughly 1 to about 198 of 200 traces is difficult to attribute to sampling alone. The decline of A10 and the changed routing out of A02 are observed but too small or thinly supported to tie to the main hypothesis. [OCC002, DIR001, REL026]
