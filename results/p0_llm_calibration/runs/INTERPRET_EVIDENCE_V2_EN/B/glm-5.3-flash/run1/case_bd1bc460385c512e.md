### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of activities A14, A15, A20 and A21 was relocated: in the earlier window exactly one of them ran between A41 and A22 in each case (their trace-shares sum to about 100 in 100), while in the later window A41 connects directly to A22 and the four activities run after A23, inserted one at a time either between A23 and A24 or between A24 and A28. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

The placement change is shown directly and consistently: relational items show the order flip against A22 and A23 for all four activities; successor items show A41 now leading only to A22, the four leading to A24/A28, and new edges from A23 and A24 to the four; and the gap evidence agrees quantitatively with the routing shares — A23 reaches A24 in two steps in about 48 in 100 cases, matching its roughly 48 in 100 transitions to the four activities, and A24 reaches A28 in two steps in about 52 in 100, matching its edges to the four, while A23 reaches A28 in exactly three steps in every later-window case, consistent with exactly one of the four sitting between them. [REL001, REL003, REL005, REL007, DIR002, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

In total 26 evidence items qualified, against about 0.37 expected if nothing had changed, and all but one describe this same coherent relocation, so chance as a whole cannot account for the pattern. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

Limitations: all items come from the same two 200-trace samples, and items of the same indicator type for the four activities describe overlapping trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations; the four activities appear in only about 40–56 traces per window, so the exact split between insertion before and after A24 is uncertain. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is a shift in variant frequencies between the samples — for example a late-placement variant that existed before but only became frequent in the later window — though the agreement across four indicator types and the matching shares make pure sampling variation unlikely. The routing shift at A03 is not explained by this hypothesis and would be a separate change. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]
