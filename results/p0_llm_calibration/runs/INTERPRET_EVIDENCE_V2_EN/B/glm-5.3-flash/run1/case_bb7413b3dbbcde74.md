### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural rerouting: the A08 step that previously sat between A45 and A46 is now usually skipped, with A45 passing to A46 directly, and the mid-process branch is rewired so that A40 passes to A25 while A19 and A20, now occurring later and after A25/A26/A27, pass mainly to A27 and A28, which in turn sometimes pass back to A19/A20; A04, A06 and A10 are also executed more often. [OCC003, DIR003, DIR013, DIR007, DIR008, DIR011, REL010, REL013, LAG003, LAG017, LAG020]

The support is direct and consistent: successor profiles, order relations and step distances all point the same way (A45 → A46 instead of A45 → A08; A19/A20 → A27/A28 instead of → A25; A40 → A25; reversed order between the A25/A26/A27 group and A19/A20), the 57 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.77 expected if nothing had changed, and the best-supported pieces rest on large samples — the order reversals on all 200 traces per window, the A08 decline on 138 vs 31 traces, and the A19/A20 rerouting on about 100 outgoing transitions per window. [OCC003, DIR007, DIR008, REL010, REL012, REL015, LAG004, LAG012]

The evidence cannot rule out that the underlying model is unchanged and only the mix of pre-existing variants shifted — for example, traces that always skipped A08 or always routed A19/A20 to A27/A28 becoming more common — which would look identical in two 200-trace samples. Some finer details also rest on few observations (A04/A06/A10 routing with about 5–7 observations before; several lag items with 1–3 observations in one window), and items sharing the same activities describe overlapping behaviour rather than independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, LAG002, LAG005, LAG006, LAG019]
