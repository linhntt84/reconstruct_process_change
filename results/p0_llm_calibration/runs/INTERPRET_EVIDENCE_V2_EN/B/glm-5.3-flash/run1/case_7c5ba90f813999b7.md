### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A37 and A22 was restructured: a single alternative step — A14 in about half of the earlier cases, A15 in the other half — was replaced by two steps, A20 and A21, which now both execute in nearly every case, in either order, between A37 and A22. [REL010, REL011, REL013, LAG001, DIR004]

The support is direct and consistent: 25 evidence items qualify against about 0.4 expected for an unchanged process, so chance alone cannot account for the set; the change is shown by relational, directional and distance evidence rather than by frequency shifts alone; and the magnitudes (A14 from 98 of 200 traces to 0, A20 and A21 from 0 to 198 of 200) far exceed what sampling variation of variant frequencies typically produces, with every item's difference larger than in about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits. Note that the many relation items largely describe this one underlying change rather than independent confirmations. [REL002, REL006, REL010, LAG001, OCC001, OCC003]

Limitations: the evidence describes executed behaviour, not the underlying model, and the either-order pattern of A20 and A21 (about half the traces each way) cannot distinguish a parallel block from an unordered sequence or two alternating routes. [REL010, DIR002, DIR003] The main competing explanation — the two windows sampling different variant mixes without any structural change — is implausible at these magnitudes but cannot be fully excluded, and the residual A15 occurrences in about 1 in 100 later traces could be either a lingering variant or sampling noise.
