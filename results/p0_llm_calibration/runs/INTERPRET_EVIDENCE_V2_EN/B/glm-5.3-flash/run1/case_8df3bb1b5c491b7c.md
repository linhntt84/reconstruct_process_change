### Observed differences

- The clearest difference is an order reversal around A27 and A28. In the earlier window, each of the four activities A19, A20, A25 and A26 (each present in roughly 20–28 of 100 traces) ran before A27 and before A28 in about 20–28 of every 100 traces and virtually never after them; in the later window the order is flipped, with A27/A28 preceding them in about 21–27 of every 100 traces, while the original order survives in at most about 0.5 in 100. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]
- The four activities also occur much later in the trace in the later window: their mean event position moved from about 0.32–0.34 to about 0.59–0.61 of the trace, while A27 and A28 shifted only slightly earlier (mean 0.35 to 0.32, and 0.59 to 0.56). Their participation stayed roughly stable. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Routing changed accordingly. Earlier, about 95–96 of every 100 outgoing transitions of A19, A20, A25 and A26 went to A27; in the later window they go mainly to A29 and A33 (about 41–55 of 100 each). A46, which previously spread its transitions across those four (about 19–27 of 100 each), now goes to A27 in about 93 of 100 transitions. A28 and A29, previously almost exclusively followed by A29 and A33 respectively, now also lead to the four activities (about 9–14 of 100 transitions each). [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010]
- Step distances moved in the same direction: A15 now reaches A27 in 4 steps instead of 5 and A16 in 3 instead of 4; A46 reaches A27 in 1 step instead of 2; A28 reaches A33 in 3 steps instead of 2; and A28→A29 and A29→A33 now take 2 steps in roughly half of the cases where they previously took 1. [LAG005, LAG006, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011]
- Two participation changes stand apart: A04 appears in about 9 of 100 later traces, down from about 27 in 100 (mean occurrences per trace 0.30 to 0.10), and its outgoing transitions spread from mostly A16 (about 80 of 100) to a broad mix still led by A16 (about 42 of 100), though the later profile rests on only 19 transitions; A08 appears in about 15 of 100 later traces, up from about 4 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the four activities A19, A20, A25 and A26 — which previously ran between A46 and A27, before A27 and A28 — now run after A27 and A28, in the region around A28/A29 and before A33. [REL001, POS001, DIR004, LAG009]

Supporting signs:
- All eight tested pairs between the four activities and A27/A28 show the same reversal, each measured over all 200 traces in both windows, with the original order almost disappearing rather than a rare variant appearing. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]
- The four activities moved later by about a quarter of the trace while A27 and A28 moved only slightly earlier, pointing to the four as the activities that changed place rather than A27/A28. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]
- Successor changes line up with the new placement: the four now lead to A29/A33 instead of A27, A28 and A29 gained transitions into the four, and A46 now leads almost solely to A27. [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010]
- Distance changes fit the same picture: A15/A16 and A46 reach A27 in fewer steps once the four have left that region, while A28→A33 lengthens by one step and A28→A29 / A29→A33 often take an extra step, as expected if one of the four now sits there. [LAG005, LAG006, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011]
- 37 evidence items qualified against roughly 0.66 expected if nothing had changed, far beyond what chance would produce. [REL001, DIR010, POS005]

Limitations and competing explanations:
- The agreeing items are not independent: the order, position, routing and distance changes largely read the same underlying difference through different indicators, so the breadth of agreement reflects one behavioural shift rather than many confirmations. [REL001, POS001, DIR004, LAG005]
- The exact placement is not pinned down: A28 and A29 both now lead into the four, and the four lead to both A29 and A33, so a branching arrangement rather than a single linear insertion cannot be ruled out. [DIR004, DIR008, DIR009]
- The A04 decline and A08 increase may belong to the same restructuring or be separate; their routing details rest on few observations (19 and 29 later transitions, only 8 earlier for A08), and some related lag items rest on 1–2 observations, so those specifics are fragile. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, LAG002, LAG004, LAG007]
- Sampling differences in variant frequencies between finite windows remain a competing explanation for the weaker items, though they can hardly account for a near-total order reversal measured over all 200 traces per window. [OCC002, DIR002, REL001]
