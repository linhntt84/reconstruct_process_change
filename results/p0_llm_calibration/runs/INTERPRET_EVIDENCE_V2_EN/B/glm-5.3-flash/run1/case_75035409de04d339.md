### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the either-or step A14/A15 now executes later in the case: previously A35 fed into A14 or A15 and then into A20, whereas in the later window A35 flows almost always straight into A20, and A14/A15 runs after A21, inserted in roughly equal shares immediately before A22 or between A22 and A23, so that exactly one activity separates A21 from A23 in about 99 in 100 cases; the rest of the chain, including two unflagged steps between A20 and A21, keeps its internal order. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG005, LAG009, LAG010, POS001, POS002]

This rests on direct relational and routing evidence rather than volume alone: successor profiles at A35, A14, A15, A21 and A22 changed coherently, pairwise order against A20 and A21 flipped almost completely, and each step-distance item shifted by exactly the amount this repositioning implies, while 21 flagged items against roughly 0.4 expected without change is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. Duplication of the step is unlikely because interleaved-or-repeated co-occurrence stays at zero and the old order has all but vanished; skipping is unlikely because participation is essentially the same in both windows. [REL001, REL002, REL004, REL005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

Limitations: with 200 cases per window, the evidence cannot exclude that both routings already existed and only their frequencies shifted strongly between the samples — unlikely given that the behaviour spans nearly all cases in both windows and every item is individually unusual for random re-splits (strength about 0.995), but not impossible. It also cannot tell whether the varying position of A14/A15 around A22 (about 27 in 100 cases before it, about 25 after) reflects one behaviour with a variable insertion point or two coexisting variants, and the middle segment between A20 and A21 is inferred only from step distances because those activities were not themselves flagged; several items describe the same underlying difference and are not independent confirmations. [REL003, REL006, LAG003, LAG010]
