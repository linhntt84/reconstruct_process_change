### Observed differences

- In the early window, A27, A28 and A29 each appeared exactly once in every one of the 200 traces. In the later window, A27 appears in about 45 in 100 traces (91 of 200) and A28 and A29 in about 55 in 100 (109 of 200); none of the three repeats within a trace in either window, so mean occurrences per trace fell in step with participation (A27 from 1.0 to about 0.45; A28 and A29 from 1.0 to about 0.55). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The later window splits into two clean trace groups: A27 never co-occurs with A28 or A29, and A28/A29 always appear together. About 45 in 100 later traces contain A27 and neither A28 nor A29; about 55 in 100 contain A28 and A29 and not A27. In the early window all three co-occurred in every trace, with A28/A29 in either order (about 47% vs 53%). [REL013, REL014, REL015]
- A28 and A29 changed place. Early, A20 was always followed by A28 or A29 (about 47%/53% of A20's outgoing transitions) and the pair ran before A21 and A23; later, A20 is always followed by A21, and A21 and A23 now run before A28/A29 in every trace where they co-occur. The pair now sits only 1–2 events before A24, where previously it was 5 events away. [DIR001, REL005, REL006, REL008, REL009, LAG003, LAG004, LAG007, LAG008]
- Positions and gaps moved consistently with that relocation: the mean relative position of A28/A29 shifted from about 0.43 to about 0.55 of the trace, A21 from about 0.48 to about 0.43 and A23 from about 0.54 to about 0.50; the A20→A21 gap shrank from 3 events to 1 and A20→A23 from 5 to 3. [POS002, POS003, POS006, POS007, LAG001, LAG002]
- A23's outgoing transitions changed: early always to A27; later about 46% to A27, 30% to A28 and 25% to A29 (shares of A23's observed outgoing transitions, not of cases), matching the two trace groups. A27 keeps its place between A23 and A24 wherever it occurs, and A24 still follows A27, A28 and A29 in the same order as before. [DIR002, REL007, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- The two later trace groups also differ internally: where A27 runs, A23 and A24 are 2 events apart; where A28/A29 run, they are 3 events apart (about 45% vs 55% of traces). [LAG005, LAG006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the process runs two variants around the A23–A24 segment: in about 45 in 100 cases that segment contains A27 while A28 and A29 are skipped entirely, and in about 55 in 100 cases it contains A28 and A29 (in either order) while A27 is skipped. In addition, A28 and A29 no longer execute directly after A20 — A21 now immediately follows A20 — and the pair instead appears immediately before A24. In the earlier window, every trace contained A27 between A23 and A24 as well as the A28/A29 pair right after A20. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL013, REL014, REL015, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG007]

This goes beyond a participation drop because the placement change is shown directly: order relations between A28/A29 and A21/A23 flip, the successor distributions of A20 and A23 change, and event distances shift accordingly, with the position statistics agreeing. The volume of qualifying items (37, against roughly 0.4 expected for an unchanged process) and the exactly complementary group sizes (91 vs 109 of 200) make sampling noise alone implausible; every item's difference also exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [DIR001, DIR002, REL005, REL006, REL013, LAG001]

Limitations: the windows are finite samples, so the evidence cannot distinguish a newly introduced exclusive choice between A27 and the A28/A29 pair from two pre-existing variants that were very rare early on and rose in frequency — although A27, A28 and A29 appearing in all 200 early traces means such variants would have to have been absent from every early case, making a pure variant-frequency sampling difference unlikely even though it cannot be strictly excluded. The many items also largely describe one underlying restructuring rather than independent confirmations, and only screened activities and pairs are visible. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL013]
