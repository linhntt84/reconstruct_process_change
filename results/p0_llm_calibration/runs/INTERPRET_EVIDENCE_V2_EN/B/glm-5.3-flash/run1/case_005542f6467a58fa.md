### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was rerouted around A10 while the mid-process group A19, A20, A25 and A26 is now rarely executed: in the later window A10 runs in about three in five cases and much earlier, leads almost exclusively to A16, and precedes the later activities A27, A28, A29, A30, A42, A47 and A48 in about 55–58 in 100 cases, while A19, A20, A25 and A26 — each previously present in about a quarter of cases between A28/A29 and A30 — now appear in only about 1–3 in 100, with flow typically passing from A28 via A29 to A30 without them. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, POS001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR008, DIR009, REL007, LAG001, LAG025, LAG026, LAG030]

This judgement rests on directness and consistency rather than item count alone: the placement change is shown relationally (A10 before the late activities, A15 feeding into A10), not only through frequency shifts; occurrence, routing, position, co-occurrence and gap indicators all point the same way, with large supports (for example 123 cases behind A10's occurrence profile and about 200 outgoing transitions behind the A28 and A29 routing profiles); and 120 qualifying items against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process is far beyond what chance screening produces. [OCC003, DIR002, POS001, REL007, LAG001, DIR008, DIR009]

An important competing explanation cannot be ruled out: a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants between the two 200-case windows — a later window that simply contains more A10-centred cases and fewer cases running the A19/A20/A25/A26 group — would produce the same participation, routing and order differences without any structural modification. In addition, several gap items about the vanished activities rest on only 1–3 later-window observations and were not relied on. [LAG012, LAG014, LAG022, LAG029, OCC004, DIR005]
