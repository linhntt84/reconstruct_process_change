### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was re-routed between the windows in two ways. A27, previously executed in every trace between the A14/A15/A26 part and the A28/A45/A46 part, is skipped in more than half of the later traces, and an alternative segment in which A32 and A33 — usually both, in either order — run between those same parts is executed in about half of the traces, with the two routes almost never used together. In addition, A02 is now executed early, directly after A14 and before A15, in about half of the traces, whereas earlier it appeared late and rarely alongside these activities. [OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL024, REL025, REL026, POS001, DIR003, LAG002, DIR001, LAG001]

This exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces: 56 qualifying items against roughly 0.72 expected by chance, and the routing and order changes are shown directly by relational and lag evidence rather than inferred from frequencies alone, consistently across the occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators. [OCC001-OCC008, DIR001-DIR009, POS001-POS004, REL001-REL030, LAG001-LAG005]

Limitations remain: the evidence cannot show whether the A32/A33 route is genuinely new or a pre-existing rare variant that became frequent, since A32 went unobserved in 200 earlier traces and A33 was seen once, and it cannot exclude a mere shift in the mix of pre-existing variants rather than a model change, as rare variants can appear in one 200-trace window only; several items also describe the same underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations, and the routing shift seen at A08 rests on only about 13 later observations. [OCC007, OCC008, DIR002]
