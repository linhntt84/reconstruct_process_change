### Observed differences

- The two alternative activities A14 and A15 appear in far fewer traces in the later window: A14 in about 26 of 100 traces (previously about 49) and A15 in about 28 of 100 (previously about 51); neither activity ever repeats within a trace. [OCC001, OCC002]
- Before, every trace contained exactly one of A14 or A15 — they never co-occur, and no trace contained neither. In the later window, about 47 in 100 traces contain neither activity, while the rest still contain exactly one; their co-occurrence with each downstream activity (A20, A21, A27, A28, A35) falls by the same 23–24 points, mirroring the participation drop rather than indicating separate effects. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011]
- Routing out of A35 changed: previously its outgoing transitions led only to A15 (about 51%) or A14 (about 49%); in the later window, about 47 in 100 lead directly to A20, with the remainder keeping the earlier A14/A15 split. [DIR004]
- Positions shift accordingly: A35 is followed by A20 one event later in about 47 of 100 later traces, whereas before the pair always sat two positions apart (consistent with A14/A15 in between); A35 reaches A21 four positions later in about 47 of 100 cases (before always five); A35 reaches A27 and A28 about one position earlier for roughly a fifth to a quarter of cases. [LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]
- In the A20 → A27/A28 → A21 stage, where the A27/A28 pair can repeat, transition shares moved by about 11–12 points: out of A20, transitions to A28 rose from about 46% to about 58%; out of A27, exits to A21 rose from about 46% to about 58%; out of A28, exits to A21 fell from about 54% to about 43% — the more common traversal order of the pair flipped from A27-first to A28-first, and the position distances shift the same way. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the alternative A14-or-A15 step that previously always followed A35 is skipped in about 47 of 100 executions, with control passing directly from A35 to A20; A14 and A15 are not removed from the process, since about 53 in 100 later traces still contain exactly one of them. [DIR004, LAG005, LAG006, REL001, OCC001, OCC002]

The change is shown directly, not only through frequencies: a direct A35→A20 transition appears where none was observed before, the A35→A20 pair moves from two positions apart to immediate adjacency in the same share of traces, and the share of traces containing neither A14 nor A15 rises from zero to about 47 in 100. Participation, relation, direction and lag indicators all align on the same ~47% share, with the residual A14/A15 shares keeping their earlier ~49/51 split. The volume of evidence also exceeds what this screening expects by chance if nothing had changed: 25 qualifying items against roughly 0.42 expected, each with a difference larger than in about 99.5 of 100 random re-splits. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR004, REL001, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011]

Limitations remain: with 200 traces per window, the evidence cannot rule out that the later window simply samples a rare "no-block" variant more often, or that the mix of process variants shifted, rather than the control flow having changed. The A27/A28 routing reversal is observed only as changed shares among outgoing transitions, and it cannot be determined whether it is an independent adjustment or a property of the executions that skip A14/A15.
