### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that routing and ordering around the A27/A28/A29/A33 block were restructured, and that A06 took on a new, much more frequent early role. In the later window, A19, A20, A25 and A26 lead almost exclusively to A27 instead of branching between A29 and A33; A46 feeds into A19/A20/A25/A26 instead of running straight to A27; A28 and A29 behave as near-fixed consecutive steps into A33; and A06 — previously rare (about 7 in 100 traces) and late — now occurs in about 3 in 10 traces, early, after A15 and before A16, and ahead of A27/A28/A29/A33/A46. The flipped ordering between the A19/A20/A25/A26 group and A27/A28 is shown directly by the relation and event-distance evidence, not by position shifts alone. [DIR003, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, REL010, REL017, LAG001, LAG004, LAG015]

The support is direct and consistent: successor profiles, pairwise relations, event distances and positions all point to the same rerouting, and 53 qualifying items is many times the 0.78 expected by chance when screening 156 units, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of unchanged behaviour typically produce. [OCC001, DIR001, REL005, LAG001]

Limitations remain: several items describe one underlying trace behaviour — the A06 items (occurrence, direction, position, relations, distances) overlap heavily, as do the four rerouted activities — so they are not independent confirmations, and a few later-window profiles rest on only 1–2 observations. An important competing explanation cannot be ruled out: a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants, for example a variant that already contained A06 and the A46→A19/A20/A25/A26→A27 routing simply being sampled more often in the later window, would produce the same differences without any structural change. [LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, OCC001, DIR009]
