### Observed differences

- In the later window, four activities are almost never executed: A19, A20, A25 and A26. Before, each ran in about 23–27% of cases (roughly 47–53 of 200 traces each); after, participation is 0–1.5% (0–3 traces), and mean occurrences per trace fell from about 0.24–0.27 to about 0–0.03. Each difference is larger than about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Before, these four behaved as mutually exclusive alternatives: no trace contained two of them together (co-occurrence about 0–1%), while each appeared without the others in roughly 23–27% of traces. In the later window, about 98–100 in 100 traces contain neither activity of each pair. [REL013, REL014, REL015, REL019, REL020, REL024]
- The four lost their upstream links: A14 and A15 each preceded one of them in about 12–26% of traces before, versus about 0–1.5% in the later window. [REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- They also lost their downstream links. Before, A28 preceded one of the four in about 22–26% of traces, one of the four preceded A30 in about 23–26%, and A29 appeared on either side in about 11–15%; in the later window these orderings are essentially gone (about 0–1%). Co-occurrence of the four with A04 fell from about 5–7% to about 0–0.5%. [REL016, REL017, REL018, REL021, REL023, REL025, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030, REL001, REL004]
- The surviving route tightened: in the later window about 99% of A28's observed outgoing transitions go to A29 (before about 49%, with roughly 10–14% going to each of the four), and about 96% of A29's outgoing transitions go to A30 (before about 50%). A28→A29 and A29→A30 became almost always immediately consecutive (about 99% and 97% at one step apart, before about 50% each), and A28→A30 now typically sits two event positions apart instead of three. [DIR006, DIR007, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]
- Routing out of A04 dispersed: A15 took about 86% of A04's observed outgoing transitions before versus about 33% in the later window, with targets appearing that were not observed before (A09, A16, A27 at about 11% each; A46, A48 at about 7%), and the A04→A15 distance spread from mostly one step to one to five steps. A14 now sends about 91% of its outgoing transitions to A15 (before about 78%) and only about 4.5% to A04 (before about 21%). [DIR001, LAG001, DIR002]
- Minor positional drifts: A14 now occurs slightly later within traces (mean relative position about 0.133 before versus 0.138 after) and A30 slightly earlier (about 0.564 versus 0.547). [POS001, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative branch formed by A19, A20, A25 and A26 — four mutually exclusive activities that each ran in roughly a quarter of cases, after A14/A15 and around the A28/A29 steps before A30 — is no longer executed in the later window, so that the route A28 → A29 → A30 runs directly. About 75 times as many items qualified as the roughly 0.7 expected by chance if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by an unchanged process; the collapse is consistent across occurrence, position, direction, relation and lag indicators, and across six partner activities (A04, A14, A15, A28, A29, A30) as well as among the four themselves; and the relational and lag evidence shows the change directly, with the precedence links into and out of the four disappearing while A28→A29 and A29→A30 consolidate and become immediately consecutive. The A04 and A14 routing changes may belong to the same restructuring or be independent of it. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR006, DIR007, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, REL016, REL018, REL023, REL030]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of process variants: if the later window simply contains mostly traces of variants that never included this branch, the observed behaviour would be identical to a structural removal, and zero participation only shows the activities were not observed. The after-picture of the four activities rests on 0–3 traces each (their outgoing profiles on 0–5 transitions), and A04's new target shares rest on 27 outgoing transitions, so the detail of the post-change routing is uncertain.
