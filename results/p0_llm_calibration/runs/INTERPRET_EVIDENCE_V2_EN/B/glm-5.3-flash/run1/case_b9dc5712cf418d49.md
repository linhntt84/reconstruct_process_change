### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the part of the process executing A32 and A33 — previously run in about half the cases, always jointly, between A40/A26 and A28 — is no longer executed in the later window, with A27 now running in every case in that role (A40 before A27, A27 before A28, and A26 routing almost exclusively to A27). A related possibility is that A06 now regularly takes a place in this flow, appearing after A14/A40 and before A26/A27/A28 and leading to A19. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR002, REL017, REL020, REL023, REL026, REL002, REL003, LAG001]

Supporting signs: participation, routing and relational evidence all point the same way — A32/A33 fell from about 52 in 100 traces to none while A27 rose to all traces, A26's outgoing share moved from A32/A33 to A27, A40's precedence flipped from A32/A33 to A27, and the A26→A28 distance concentrating at two positions matches A27 now always lying between them. The 53 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.75 expected without a change, and the key routing items rest on about 200 observations per window. [DIR002, REL021, REL022, LAG006]

Limitations: the later-window behaviour of A32 and A33 themselves rests on a single observed trace each, so any statement about what exactly replaced their outgoing edges or positions is weakly supported. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is sampling: with 200-trace windows, variant frequencies can shift substantially without any change, and many of these items describe the same underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations — although a fall from 105 in 200 to 1 in 200, together with the matching rerouting of A26 and A40, is difficult to attribute to sampling alone. [DIR004, DIR005, POS002, POS003, LAG007]
