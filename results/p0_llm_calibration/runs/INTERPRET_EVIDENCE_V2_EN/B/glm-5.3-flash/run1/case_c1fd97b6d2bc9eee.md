### Observed differences

- In the later window, A14 is almost never executed: it appears in about 1 in 200 traces (0.5%), down from about 110 in 200 (55%); A15 falls from about 90 in 200 (45%) to about 2 in 200 (1%). Neither activity repeats within a trace in either window. [OCC001, OCC002]
- The flow out of A35 is redirected: earlier, about 55% of its observed outgoing transitions went to A14 and about 45% to A15, with no direct transition to A20; in the later window, about 99 in 100 go directly to A20 and only about 1.5 in 100 combined go to A14 or A15. [DIR001]
- The A35→A20 distance shrinks from exactly 2 event positions in all earlier traces to 1 position in about 99 in 100 later traces. [LAG001]
- The earlier orderings disappear: A14 preceded A20 in about 55 in 100 traces and A15 preceded A20 in about 45 in 100, always after A35; in the later window, A20 appears without A14 in about 99.5 in 100 traces and without A15 in about 99 in 100, A35 appears without A14/A15 at similar rates, and A14 and A15 are jointly absent from about 99 in 100 traces (they never co-occur in either window). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]
- A20 itself is unchanged in coverage — present in every trace in both windows — and only shifts slightly earlier within the trace (mean relative position about 0.39 to 0.37). [POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the intermediate step between A35 and A20 — previously executed as either A14 (about 55 in 100 traces) or A15 (about 45 in 100) — is skipped in the later window, so that A35 is directly followed by A20. Eleven qualifying items were found against roughly 0.31 expected by chance across the 63 tested units, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. The hypothesis rests on direct routing and relational evidence rather than frequency drops alone: A35's outgoing transitions moved almost entirely to A20, the A35→A20 distance shortened from 2 to 1 event positions in about 99 in 100 traces, and the earlier A35→A14→A20 and A35→A15→A20 orderings gave way to A20 and A35 appearing without A14/A15 in about 99 in 100 traces, consistent with the collapse of A14 and A15 participation and with A20 remaining in every trace, only slightly earlier in position. These items largely describe one underlying shift, so their agreement shows consistency rather than independent confirmation. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, POS002, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a change in case mix or routing under which the A14/A15 branch is almost never chosen, rather than a structural change that skips it: near-zero participation only shows the activities were almost not observed in the later window, the residual occurrences (one trace with A14, two with A15) could be rare variants, and the after-window positional profile for A14 rests on a single trace. Even so, a branch present in over half of 200 traces falling to about 1 in 200 is far larger than the differences produced when the pooled traces are re-split at random, where fewer than 1 in 200 re-splits reach this size.
