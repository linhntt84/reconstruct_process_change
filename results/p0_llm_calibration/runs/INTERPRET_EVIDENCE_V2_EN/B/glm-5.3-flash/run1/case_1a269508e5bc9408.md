### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural rerouting with two visible parts. Early in the flow, A04 now sits between A14 and A15: A14 passes to A04 about 70 in 100 times and A04 to A15 about 89 in 100 times, A14-to-A15 pairs moved from one to typically two steps apart, A04's participation rose from about 15 to about 72 in 100 traces, and A04 now precedes the late-block activities A27, A28, A29, A33 and A46 in about 64–65 in 100 of their co-occurrences. In the later section, flow that previously scattered from A19, A20, A25 and A26 directly to A33 or A29, and reached A27 from A46, now runs as one near-deterministic sequence A46 → A19/A20/A25/A26 → A27 → A28 → A29 → A33 with typically immediate handovers. These are direct relational and routing changes — observed order reversals and successor redistributions, not just position shifts — consistent across participation, routing, order, distance and position indicators; every item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits, and with about 68 qualifying items against fewer than 1 expected for an unchanged process, chance cannot explain the pattern as a whole, while the many agreeing items largely describe these two underlying shifts rather than independent phenomena. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that both windows sample the same underlying model with a shifted mix of allowed variants — for instance, an A04-between-A14-and-A15 variant and a sequential-chain variant that already existed but were rare before — so a structural modification remains a possibility supported by the evidence rather than a confirmed fact.
