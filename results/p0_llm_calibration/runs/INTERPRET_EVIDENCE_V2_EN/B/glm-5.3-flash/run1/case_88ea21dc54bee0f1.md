### Observed differences

- In the later window, A19 and A20 appear much earlier in the case: their mean relative position falls from about 0.67 to about 0.37, while their coverage stays near half of the cases (A19 in about 103 of 200 cases before and 105 after; A20 in about 97 and 95). [POS001, POS002]
- A21 and A22, present in every trace in both windows, move much later: their mean relative position rises from about 0.38 to about 0.65. A23 and A27 shift only slightly earlier (about 0.03–0.04 on the position scale). [POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- The order between the two groups flips completely. Before, A21, A22, A23 and A27 each preceded A19 in essentially every case containing both (about 52 in 100 cases, no exceptions observed); in the later window, A19 precedes each of them in about 52 in 100 cases, again with no exceptions. The same reversal holds for A20 (from about 47–49 in 100 before to about 46–47 in 100 after). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]
- The order between A21/A22 and A23/A27 also reverses: before, A21 and A22 preceded A23 in about 98 in 100 cases and A27 in about 99 in 100; after, A23 and A27 precede A21 and A22 in about 98–99 in 100 cases. [REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]
- Outgoing transitions are rewired consistently. A19 and A20 now lead to A23 (about 94–99 in 100 of their observed outgoing transitions) where they previously led to A28 (about 97–99 in 100). A21 and A22 now lead to A28 (about 46–50 in 100) instead of A23 (previously about 48–49 in 100). A27 now leads to A21 and A22 (about 47–51 in 100) instead of A19 and A20, and A38 now leads to A19 and A20 (about 42–44 in 100) instead of A21 and A22 (about 40–43 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- Immediate successions change accordingly: A27 followed by A21 or A22 within one or two steps, seen only once before, now occurs in about 200 of 200 cases; the near-immediate A20 → A28 succession (about 99 in 100 before) is now seen only once; the A27 → A28 gap lengthens from two to about three steps; and the A38 → A23 gap shortens from about three to about two steps. The A27 → A21/A22 and A20 → A28 lag details rest on a single observation on one side, so they are weakly supported. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two segments of the process exchanged their placement: the segment containing A19 and A20, which previously ran late — after A21, A22, A23 and A27 — and flowed into A28, now runs early, before A23; the segment containing A21 and A22, previously early and flowing into A23, now runs after A27 and flows into A28. The surrounding spine keeps its order: A38 still precedes the early segment, A23 and A27 keep their relative order with only small position shifts, and A28 remains the downstream target of the late segment. [REL001, REL005, REL009, REL011, POS001, POS003, POS005, DIR001, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, LAG004]

Supporting signs: the order reversals are relational evidence observed directly and are complete on both sides (about 0 versus about 100 in 100 co-occurrence cases, over roughly 100–200 cases per pair), the position shifts match them in direction and magnitude, and the successor rewiring plus the new immediate successions agree with the same rearrangement; every item's difference also exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, and 29 qualifying items against roughly 0.58 expected under no change cannot be explained by chance as a whole — although many items describe the same underlying behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [REL001, REL009, POS001, POS003, DIR001, DIR003, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: the lag details for A27 → A21/A22 and A20 → A28 rest on a single observation on one side, and the finite windows cannot fully rule out that the two windows sample different mixes of process variants — although complete, mutually consistent order reversals across six activity pairs are not what sampling differences in variant frequencies typically produce. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, REL001, REL009]
