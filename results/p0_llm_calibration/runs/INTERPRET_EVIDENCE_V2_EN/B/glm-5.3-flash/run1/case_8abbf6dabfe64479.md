- In the later window, four activities that were almost unobserved before — A19, A20, A25 and A26, each present in at most 1 of 200 earlier traces — appear in about 43–62 of 200 traces (roughly 21–31% participation). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the passage between A42 and A27 runs through one of four mutually exclusive activities — A19, A20, A25 or A26 — replacing the previously direct A42-to-A27 transition in nearly every trace, and that A06 has additionally taken on a late-trace role immediately before A48, sitting directly between A47 and A48 in about a quarter of traces. Behaviourally, A42 now feeds the four alternatives in about 98 of 100 of its outgoing transitions, each alternative in turn precedes A27, and A06 appears in about 28% of traces (up from about 12%) almost always followed by A48. [DIR006, REL010, REL016, REL021, REL025, LAG002, DIR001, LAG001, DIR007, LAG003]

The order and placement claims rest on direct relational and adjacency evidence rather than participation alone, and the occurrence, routing, position, relation and lag indicators all describe one coherent picture; the scale of the shifts (the four activities from at most 1 in 200 traces to 43–62 in 200, the direct A42-to-A27 share from about 92% to about 0) and the number of qualifying items (50 versus roughly 0.69 expected if nothing had changed) are far beyond what random re-splits of stable behaviour produce. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR006, REL010, LAG002]

Limitations remain: the earlier routing of A19, A20 and A26 rests on a single trace each and A25 was not observed at all before, so their prior behaviour is not reliably known; the exact split of traffic among the four alternatives and the small new edges of about 2 in 100 could partly reflect sampling differences in variant frequencies between the finite windows; and the evidence cannot show whether the rerouting around A42 and the changed role of A06 are one coordinated modification or two independent ones, since several items describe the same underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
