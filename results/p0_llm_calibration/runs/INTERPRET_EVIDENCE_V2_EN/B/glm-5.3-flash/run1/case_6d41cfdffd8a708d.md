### Observed differences

- In the before window, each of the four activities A14, A15, A20 and A21 ran in roughly 23–26 of 100 cases (46–52 of 200 traces), never more than once per case. In the later window they are nearly gone: A15 and A21 in 0 of 200 cases, A14 in 2 and A20 in 1. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Before, the four were strict alternatives: no case contained two of them and their shares sum to about 100 in 100 cases, so essentially every before-case executed exactly one of the four. In the later window, about 99 of 100 cases contain none of them. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL007, REL008, REL012]
- The routing around them changed accordingly. Before, about half of A23's observed outgoing transitions went to A24 and the rest to one of the four (A24 about 505 in 1000; A14 about 130; A15 about 145; A21 about 120; A20 about 100); in the later window, about 995 in 1000 go to A24. A24's outgoing transitions similarly went from about half to A25 and half to the four, to about 99 in 100 to A25. [DIR005, DIR006]
- Order and spacing moved in line with this. Before, A23 always preceded the executed alternative, the alternative always preceded A25, and A23 and A25 sat three events apart in essentially all cases; in the later window A23 and A25 are two events apart in about 985 of 1000 cases, A24 is immediately followed by A25 in about 99 of 100 (before an even split between one and two events), and A25 still occurs in every case at a slightly earlier mean relative position (about 0.56 vs 0.55). [REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, POS005]
- The after-window profiles of the four activities themselves (their routing, positions and distances) rest on 0–2 observations each and say little about how they would behave if they still occurred. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative step in which every before-window case executed exactly one of A14, A15, A20 or A21 between A23 and A25 (either directly after A23 or after A24) is no longer executed, or is executed only in a small residual of cases, in the later window, so cases run directly from A23 through A24 to A25. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007]

Supporting signs: about 40 evidence items qualify against roughly 0.43 expected if nothing had changed, so chance cannot explain the pattern as a whole; the routing and spacing changes rest on all 200 traces of each window rather than on a handful of cases; participation, routing, co-occurrence and spacing items all describe one coherent shift, and A25's slightly earlier position fits one mid-trace activity being dropped. Every qualifying difference is also larger than about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [REL001, REL004, REL006, DIR005, DIR006, LAG006, POS005]

Limitations: zero participation only shows that the four activities were not observed in the later window; with 200 traces, complete removal cannot be distinguished from a drastic drop to a very low rate (at most about 1 in 100 after-cases). Sampling differences in variant frequencies are in principle a competing explanation for a variant missing from one window, but variants that each ran in about a quarter of before-cases falling to 0–2 of 200 cases, alongside a routing shift visible in all 200 traces, make an unchanged process implausible; what remains unresolved is removal versus drastic rate reduction. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR005, DIR006]
