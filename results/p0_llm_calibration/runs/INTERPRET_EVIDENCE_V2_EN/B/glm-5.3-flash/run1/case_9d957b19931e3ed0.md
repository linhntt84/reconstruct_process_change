### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the segment containing A40, A19, A20, A25, A26, A27 and A28 runs in a different order: A19 and A20, which previously ran after A25 and then continued to A27 or A28, now run directly after A40 and before A25, and A26 → A27 → A28 run back-to-back where they previously competed with exits to A19/A20. In addition, A04 runs in about twice as many cases and is now placed late in the case, typically between A43 and A44. [REL008, REL011, DIR002, DIR003, DIR008, LAG011, LAG013, DIR004, DIR005, OCC001, POS001, DIR009]

- The order change is observed directly rather than inferred from marginals: A25-before-A19/A20 fell from about 42–56 in 100 to about 0–1 in 100 while A19/A20-before-A25 rose from 0 to about 48–52 in 100, each based on roughly 90–115 co-occurring traces per window, and the successor and lag changes match the same reordering. The A04 change is likewise direct, with participation, position and successor shifts moving together. [REL008, REL011, DIR002, DIR008, LAG011, LAG013, LAG008, OCC001, POS001, DIR009]
- In total 44 items qualified, roughly 60 times the 0.72 expected by chance across the 144 tested units, so chance screening cannot account for the pattern as a whole; each item's difference also exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [OCC001, DIR002, REL008, LAG011]
- Limitations: the two windows are finite samples of 200 traces each, so variant frequencies can differ without any change and a rare variant can appear in one window only; the main reversals are far larger than sampling alone plausibly produces, but smaller shifts such as the A36/A37 swap may not be. Several items describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations (the REL, DIR and LAG items on A19/A20, A25 and A26/A27/A28 all reflect the same reordering). The indicators also aggregate each window, so a reordering within individual cases cannot be distinguished from a shift in the mix of variants with different fixed orders, and the A04 change cannot be tied to the same modification; a few lag items rest on a single after-window observation and were not relied upon. [DIR006, DIR007, REL008, LAG003, LAG005, LAG007]
