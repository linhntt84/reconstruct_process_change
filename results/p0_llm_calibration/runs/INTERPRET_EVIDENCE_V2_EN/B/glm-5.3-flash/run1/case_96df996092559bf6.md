### Observed differences

- A19 and A20 go from being present in about 48 in 100 traces (about 95 of 200, roughly once per trace when present) to being present in about 199 of 200 traces in the later window, still about once per trace with negligible repetition. [OCC001, OCC002]
- A25 and A26 each rise from about 24 in 100 traces to about 50 in 100; in both windows they act as mutually exclusive alternatives, co-occurring in almost no traces (about 0 before, 1–2 in 100 after). [OCC003, OCC004, REL014]
- The outgoing transitions of A42 are redistributed: the share leading to A19 rises from about 39% to about 86%, while the share leading directly to A27 falls from about 41% to under 1% (shares over roughly 200 outgoing transitions per window). [DIR001]
- The gap between A42 and A27 lengthens: one or two events apart in about 53 of 100 traces before, the pair is four or five events apart in about 99 of 100 later traces, with the one-event gap almost gone. [LAG001]
- A19 and A20 now co-occur with the always-present A42, A27 and A47 in about 995 of 1000 traces (about 475 in 1000 before), with the same ordering in both windows: A42 first, then A19 before A20, then A20 before A27/A47. [REL001, REL004, REL005, REL007, REL010, REL011, REL013]
- The A25/A26 choice sits inside that segment in both windows: A19 before A25 or A26, and A25 or A26 before A20, each rise from about 24 in 100 to about 49–50 in 100, matching the doubled participation of A25/A26. [REL002, REL003, REL008, REL009, REL015, REL018, REL019, REL021]
- A27 itself is present in every trace in both windows and occurs only slightly later (mean position about 0.41 vs 0.43 of the trace); A46 is now nearly always present and typically preceded by A19/A20 (about 49 in 100 vs 19 in 100 before). [POS001, REL006, REL012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change under which the segment A19 → (A25 or A26) → A20 is executed in nearly every case in the later window, whereas in the earlier window about half of the cases (about 95 of 200) did not execute it; as a consequence, the direct A42 → A27 route is almost no longer taken and A27 is reached only indirectly, four to five events after A42. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, LAG001]

The signs are direct and mutually consistent: participation of A19/A20 rises from about 48 in 100 to about 199 in 200 traces (support of 95 vs 199 traces); the routing shift at A42 is measured on about 200 outgoing transitions per window; the internal order of the segment is unchanged, with A25 and A26 remaining exclusive alternatives each running in about half the cases, so what changed is how often the segment runs rather than how; and 28 qualifying items against roughly 0.69 expected by chance in a stable process is far beyond what random re-splits of unchanged traces typically produce. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL014, DIR001]

Limitations remain: the evidence describes executed traces, not the underlying model, so it cannot distinguish a removed alternative path from a shift in case mix or in the frequencies of existing variants (for example, cases of a type that omits the segment no longer appearing in the later sample), even though a jump from about 48% to about 99.5% participation is far outside typical sampling variation; moreover, the many relational items largely restate the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations, and about 1 in 200 later traces still lacks A19/A20, so the segment is near-universal rather than strictly mandatory. [REL001, REL004]
