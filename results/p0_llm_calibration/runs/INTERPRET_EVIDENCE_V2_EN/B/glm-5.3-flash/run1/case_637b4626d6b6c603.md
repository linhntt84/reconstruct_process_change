### Observed differences

- In the later window, A04 appears in about 19 in 100 traces, down from about 60 in 100, and when it appears it sits earlier in the trace (mean relative position about 0.54 versus 0.85, with roughly twice the spread). [OCC002, POS001]
- A04's routing collapsed from near-exclusive: about 92 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions went to A46 in the earlier window, about 17 in 100 in the later one, with the rest spread over many targets (A48 and A27 about 14 in 100 each, A15 about 12, A16 about 10); the A04→A46 pair, previously about 99 in 100 observations at 1 step apart, now rests on only about 10 traces. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured in two consistent ways. First, the late A04 step is now largely bypassed: it runs in about 19 in 100 traces instead of about 60, its near-exclusive successor link to A46 dissolved, and A45 now flows mostly directly to A46 (about 81 in 100 of observed outgoing transitions) rather than to A04 (about 4 in 100), with late-process distances shortening by about one step. Second, a new mid-process stage appears in which exactly one of four mutually exclusive activities — A19, A20, A25 or A26 — executes in roughly 21–31 in 100 traces, entered from the A14/A15 stage (and partly from A28/A29) and exiting almost exclusively to A29/A30; the lengthening of A28→A30 from 2 to 3 steps and of A28→A29 and A29→A30 from 1 step to a mix of 1 and 2 fits one additional step on these paths. [OCC002, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, DIR002, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011, DIR013, REL016, REL025, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG013, LAG015]

This goes well beyond chance-level signalling: 106 evidence items qualified, about 100 times the 1.06 expected in an unchanged process, and each item's difference exceeds those seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. The participation, routing, co-occurrence and distance evidence all describe the same two shifts rather than scattered noise, and activities running in 21–31 in 100 later traces after 0 occurrences in 200 earlier traces are very hard to attribute to sampling variation alone. [OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, DIR002, DIR013, REL012, LAG008]

Limitations remain. The two windows are finite samples, so variant frequencies can differ without any change and a rare variant can appear in one window only; that competing explanation is strained for the four new activities but cannot be excluded for the smaller increases in A02, A06, A08 and A10. The evidence also cannot establish whether the A04 reduction and the new mid-process stage are one coordinated modification or two independent ones, and the exact wiring around A04 is uncertain because the after-profiles for A04→A46 and A45→A04 rest on only about 10 and 12 traces; routing shares are computed over observed outgoing transitions, so an unobserved edge does not prove absence. [LAG001, LAG012, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR002]
