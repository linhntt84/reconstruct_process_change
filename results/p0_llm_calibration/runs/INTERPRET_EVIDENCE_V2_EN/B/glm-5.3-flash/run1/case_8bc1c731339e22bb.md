### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a mid-process section was re-sequenced in the later window: A26 and A28, which previously executed after A32 and A33 (A25 → A32/A33 → A26/A28 → A29), now execute before them (A25 → A26/A28 → A32/A33 → A29). The reversal is shown directly by the order relations (A32 before A26 in about 100 in 100 earlier traces containing both, but about 2 in 100 later), and it matches the successor changes at A25, A28, A32 and A33, the lag shifts, and the position shifts of all four activities; the key items rest on roughly 200 traces per window with differences larger than virtually all random re-splits of the pooled data. With 51 qualifying items against roughly 0.8 expected by chance across the 162 tested units, chance alone cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, DIR011, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, LAG004, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013, LAG014]

A second directly supported change concerns A10: in the later window it participates in about 26 in 100 traces (previously about 58 in 100), appears later and far more variably when it does occur, is largely dropped as A14's continuation, and its own successors spread from almost exclusively A15 to many targets. This is consistent with A10's early role being largely removed rather than merely reduced, although a shift in the relative frequency of trace variants containing an early versus a late A10 would look similar. [OCC004, POS001, DIR005, DIR006, LAG002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a change in the mix of pre-existing process variants rather than a rewiring within individual traces: if both orderings of the A26/A28 and A32/A33 blocks already existed as separate variants, a shift in their relative frequencies would produce the same aggregate reversal. The increases in the rare activities A02, A04 and A08 rest on 34–49 later-window traces versus 8–16 earlier, so sampling differences in rare-variant frequencies remain plausible there, though their simultaneous rise and redirected outgoing transitions give the pattern some coherence.
