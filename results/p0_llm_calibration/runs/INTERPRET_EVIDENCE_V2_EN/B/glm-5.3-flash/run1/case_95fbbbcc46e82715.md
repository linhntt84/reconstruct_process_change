### Observed differences

- A19 and A20 were almost never observed in the earlier window (A19 in 1 of 200 traces, A20 in 2 of 200). In the later window they appear once in the traces that contain them, and those traces make up about 45 in 100 (A19) and about 55 in 100 (A20) of the sample. [OCC001, OCC002]
- In the later window the two activities act as alternatives: about 99 in 100 traces contain exactly one of them (A19 only about 44, A20 only about 55, both about 1), whereas before, about 98 in 100 traces contained neither. [REL001]
- A40's outgoing transitions shifted from mostly A25 (about 88 in 100 before) to mostly A20 (about 44 in 100) and A19 (about 37 in 100), with a direct A40→A25 transition now seen in under 1 in 100. [DIR004]
- The new steps feed back into the flow: about 97 in 100 of A19's observed outgoing transitions go to A25, and about 95 in 100 of A20's do. Their before-window successor profiles rest on only 1 and 2 observations. [DIR002, DIR003]
- Order and distance evidence agrees: A40 precedes A19 in about 44 in 100 traces and A19 precedes A25 in about 43 in 100; A40 precedes A20 in about 55 in 100 and A20 precedes A25 in about 55 in 100. A19 follows A40 within one step in about 84 in 100 of observed pairs, and the A40→A25 distance moved from one step (about 88 in 100 of pairs) to two steps (about 80 in 100). [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002]
- Positions are consistent with this: A19 and A20 occur around 36–40% of the way through the trace, and A25's median position moved slightly later, from about 0.37 to about 0.39 of trace length. [POS001, POS002, POS003]
- A04's outgoing mix also redistributed (A46 from about 27 to about 5 in 100, A25 from about 12 to none observed, rises for A16, A44 and A19/A20), a further routing shift whose link to the A40–A25 pattern is not established by the evidence. [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a step performed as either A19 or A20 — the two rarely occurring together — is executed in nearly every trace between A40 and A25: A40 now runs on to A19 or A20, each of which is in turn followed by A25, whereas before, A40 led directly to A25 in about 88 in 100 of its outgoing transitions. This placement is shown directly by the successor, order and distance evidence, and A25's slight positional delay fits the same shift. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002, POS003]

Limitations remain: the evidence cannot distinguish a newly introduced activity from a pre-existing branch that was previously taken in only about 1 in 100 traces and is now taken in about half of them, since A19/A20 behaviour in the earlier window rests on 1 and 2 traces. The rerouting observed at A04 is not tied to this pattern. And although a jump from about 1–2 to about 90–111 traces in 200, with a coherent relational structure, is hard to attribute to sampling alone, finite windows can shift variant frequencies without any change; most qualifying items describe this one underlying shift rather than independent changes. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001]
