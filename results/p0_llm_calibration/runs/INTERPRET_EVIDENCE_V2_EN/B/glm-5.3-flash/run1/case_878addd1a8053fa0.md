- The routing out of A41 changed completely. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the optional step of the process — exactly one of A14, A15, A20, A21 per case — was moved from the late segment (between A23/A24 and A28) to the early segment (between A41 and A22). The 30 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.46 expected from screening if nothing had changed, so the evidence as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

The placement reading is direct and consistent rather than a restatement of a frequency change: successor changes show A41 now followed by the optional step, which now leads to A22, while A23 and A24 lost their detour branches; the pairwise order of the optional step against A22 and A23 reversed completely; the distances A23→A24, A24→A28, A41→A22 and A23→A28 all changed exactly as a move implies (1, 1, 2 and 2 steps in the later window); and the mean position of the optional step shifted from about 0.6 to about 0.33 while A22, A23 and A24 moved only slightly. Participation of the optional step is essentially unchanged (its four trace counts sum to 200 in each window), which fits a move rather than a step being added or dropped. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants: none of the roughly 200 observed occurrences of the optional step was early before and none was late after, but with 200 traces per window a variant present in only a few cases could be missed, so a pure frequency swap between two long-standing placements cannot be excluded by sampling alone — the fact that every item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits does not by itself settle this. The four lag items about the old late routing rest on one after-window observation each, and since the items largely describe the same trace behaviour, they should be read as one consistent picture rather than independent confirmations.
