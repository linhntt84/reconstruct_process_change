### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the stage containing A26 and A27 runs before the group A35, A36, A41 and A42, whereas it previously ran after it: A25 now leads directly into A27, A26 feeds into that group, and the group hands over to A30 instead of to A27. In the same windows A10 is skipped in most cases where it previously appeared (about 8 versus 35 in 100 traces). These signs are direct and mutually consistent: the order flip is near-deterministic on both sides with full support (200 traces in each window), the edge changes match it, position and adjacency measures corroborate it, and each difference exceeds roughly 99.5% of random re-splits; the number of qualifying items (46 against roughly 0.83 expected without change) is far beyond what an unchanged process produces. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, OCC001]

Limitations remain. The details concerning A10 — its own outgoing profile, its position and its gaps to other activities — rest on only 1–15 after-window observations and are weak. The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample process variants unevenly (for example, a variant that is rare overall dominating one window), although order flips of this size at full support are hard to produce by sampling alone; nor can it establish that the A10 decline and the reordering are a single structural modification rather than two concurrent ones.
