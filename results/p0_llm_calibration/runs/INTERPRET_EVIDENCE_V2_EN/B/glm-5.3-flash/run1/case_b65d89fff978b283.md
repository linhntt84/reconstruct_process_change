### Observed differences

- The four activities A34, A35, A36 and A37 ran in every trace before; in the later window they each run far less often — A34 and A35 in about 56 in 100 traces, A36 and A37 in about 46–47 in 100. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]
- Before, the four always appeared together, with A34 and A35 in either order followed by A36 and A37 in either order. In the later window the pairs stay internally coupled (A34 with A35 in about 55 in 100 traces, A36 with A37 in about 46 in 100) but almost never combine: A34 and A36 share a trace in only about 2 in 100 cases (100 in 100 before), and A35 with A36 or A37 in about 3 in 100. Taken together, nearly every later trace runs exactly one of the two pairs. [REL025, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030, OCC005, OCC007]
- A30 and A31 still run in essentially every trace and keep their roles: A30 still precedes the four when they run, and A31 still follows them. But A30 now co-occurs with A34 in about 56 in 100 traces (before 100 in 100) and with A36 in about 47 in 100 (before 100 in 100); A31 shows the same halving. [REL017, REL019, REL021, REL023]
- The distance between A30 and A31 shortened from always 5 event steps to 3 steps in about 93 in 100 observed cases; A34 and A35 now reach A31 in 1–2 steps instead of 3–4, and A30 reaches A36/A37 in 1–2 steps instead of 3–4. [LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG008]
- Successor profiles moved accordingly: of A30's observed outgoing transitions, only A34 (about 56%) and A35 (about 44%) were targeted before, while in the later window they spread over all four (roughly 19–27% each). A34 now leads to A31 (about 50%) or A35 (about 48%) instead of to A35/A36/A37, and A35 now leads to A31 (about 46%) or A34 (about 48%). [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- A15 and A16 still run in every trace, and A15 leads to A16 in about 93% of its observed outgoing transitions (up from about 82%), but they now co-occur with A34–A37 in only about 47–56 in 100 traces (before 100 in 100); their average event positions shifted slightly later (about 0.18 to 0.20 and 0.22 to 0.24 of the trace). [REL009, REL011, REL013, REL015, DIR002, POS001, POS002]
- A06 now appears in about 10 in 100 traces, down from about 30 in 100, and its observed outgoing transitions concentrate less on A16 (about 45%, down from about 90%). [OCC003, DIR001]
- The rare activities A02, A04 and A08 became more frequent (A02 in about 13 in 100 traces, up from about 2–3; A04 and A08 in about 7 in 100, up from about 1), but these figures rest on only 2–5 traces before and 14–26 after. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the closing stage between A30 and A31, which previously ran as a fixed sequence of all four activities A34, A35, A36 and A37, now runs as one shorter alternative per case: in the later window a trace contains either the pair A34/A35 (about 55 in 100 traces) or the pair A36/A37 (about 46 in 100), almost never both, with A30 still in front and A31 still behind — making the stage two activities long instead of four. [OCC005, OCC007, REL025, REL026, REL030, LAG003]

The support is direct and consistent: participation of all four activities halved in step [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]; within-pair co-occurrence persists while cross-pair co-occurrence collapsed from 100 to about 2–3 in 100 traces, well below the roughly 26 in 100 expected if the activities occurred independently of one another [REL026, REL027, REL028, REL029]; the order relations around the stage are preserved [REL017, REL021]; and the rerouted successors of A30, A34 and A35 together with the shortened A30-to-A31 and A34/A35-to-A31 distances match the same picture [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG003, LAG006, LAG008]. The reduced overlap of A15/A16 with the stage and the greater rarity of A06 are consistent secondary reflections of the same behaviour. [REL009, REL013, OCC003, DIR001]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of pre-existing process variants: the one-pair behaviour appears in none of the 200 before-window traces, so it could have existed before only below roughly 2 in 100 traces, and finite-sample frequency differences could then mimic part of the change. In addition, the apparent rise of A02, A04 and A08 rests on very few before-window observations and may be sampling noise, and several evidence items describe the same underlying traces rather than independent confirmations. [OCC005, REL026, OCC001, OCC002, OCC004]
