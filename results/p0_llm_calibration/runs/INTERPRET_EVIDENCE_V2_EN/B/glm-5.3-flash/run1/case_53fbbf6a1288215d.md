### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment running after A14/A15 and A46, and before A31, is executed differently in the later window: earlier traces contained a single activity chosen from A19, A21, A22 and A23 in that segment, while later traces instead run A20, A28, A29 and A30 there — each once, in varying order. About 100 evidence items qualify, against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC003, DIR011, REL035, REL038, LAG003]

The support is direct rather than merely cumulative: the participation flip shows the old activities are no longer executed and the new ones now are; A46's rerouting and the A14-precedes relations locate the new activities between A46/A14 and A31; and the A46-to-A31 distance growing from about 2 to 5 positions matches four activities running where one did before. The old four behaved as mutually exclusive single picks, while the new four co-occur in the same traces. [OCC002, OCC003, DIR011, REL010, REL024, REL035, LAG003]

Limitations: the windows are finite samples, so an extreme shift in the mix of pre-existing variants cannot be strictly excluded — the new run simply not drawn in the 200 earlier traces and the old pick nearly not drawn later — although a behaviour at about 99 of 100 frequency appearing 0 times in 200 earlier traces makes that hard to sustain. Zero participation only means an activity was not observed, not that it was removed, and many items restate the same trace behaviour, so their agreement is not independent confirmation. [OCC003, OCC002, REL035]
