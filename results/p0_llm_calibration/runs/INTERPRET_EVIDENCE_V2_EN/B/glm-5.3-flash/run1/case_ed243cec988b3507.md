### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A25 and A26 now executes two activities instead of four: in the later window each case runs either the pair A29+A30 (about 56 in 100 cases) or the pair A31+A32 (about 46 in 100), rarely both (about 3 of 200), so A26 is reached three positions after A25 rather than five. The pair structure is observed directly in the co-occurrence and ordering data [REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014], the shortening of the A25-to-A26 distance from 5 to 3 positions is observed directly [LAG001], new successors A29→A26 and A30→A26 appear [DIR002, DIR003], and the participation drops match the pair structure exactly [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]. With 31 qualifying items against roughly 0.44 expected by chance across the 88 tested units, the pattern as a whole is unlikely for an unchanged process.

This remains a possibility supported by the evidence, not a confirmed modification. Sampling differences in variant frequencies cannot be fully ruled out: all 200 before-cases show the four-activity block, so a pre-existing rare two-activity variant would have had to be very rare to be missed, and the about 3 in 200 later cases containing all four activities could be residual old behaviour or noise. Two lag items (A29→A31 and A30→A32) rest on only 3 later observations and were not relied upon. [LAG005, LAG007]
