### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the placement of two activity groups was exchanged. In the earlier window A19 and A20 ran in the first part of the case and were followed by A23, while A21 and A22 ran late, after A27, and exited to A28; in the later window A21 and A22 run early and are followed by A23, while A19 and A20 run late, after A27, and exit to A28, with A38 now feeding A21/A22 instead of A19/A20. In addition, A36 is executed in fewer cases and receives less routing from A37. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR007, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG004, OCC001, DIR006]

This is a hypothesis about order and placement, and the relational evidence shows the change directly: the order between A21/A22 and A23/A27 flips in about 97–99 in 100 traces while these activities co-occur in all traces, so the flip is near-universal rather than a rare variant surfacing in one window; successor shares move by roughly 45–95 percentage points on about 100–200 outgoing transitions per window; and the position and adjacency changes point in the same direction. With 43 qualifying items against roughly 0.67 expected for an unchanged process, the set of results is far beyond what random re-splits of the pooled data produce. [REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG004, LAG007, LAG013, LAG017, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

Limitations: each window is a finite sample of 200 traces, so secondary share changes — notably A36's fall from about 25 to 13 in 100 traces and the small shifts for A23 and A27 — could partly reflect sampling differences in how often existing variants appear; in principle, a shift in the prevalence of two pre-existing variants (one per order) could mimic the flip if the minority order stayed below about 2 in 100 traces in each window. Several lag comparisons rest on 1–3 observations, and many items describe the same movement of the two groups, so they are not independent confirmations; positions are event positions, not clock times. [OCC001, POS005, POS006, LAG002, LAG005, LAG008, LAG010, LAG012, LAG015, LAG016]
