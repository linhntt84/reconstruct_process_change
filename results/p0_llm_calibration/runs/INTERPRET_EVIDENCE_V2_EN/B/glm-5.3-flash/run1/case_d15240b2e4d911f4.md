### Observed differences

- Successors were rerouted at several points. In the later window, A14 and A15 (each present in roughly half of the traces, about 52 and 48 in 100) are followed by A18 in all of their observed outgoing transitions, whereas previously they were always followed by A23. A16 and A17, which appear in every trace, now lead to A23 in about half of their outgoing transitions each (about 48–53 in 100), whereas previously they led to A18. - Pairwise distances changed consistently with these reversals: A23 now occurs 3 event positions after A22 in every trace (previously 2), and A18 now occurs 2 positions after A33 (previously 3). [LAG001, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the placement of the A14/A15 step and the A16/A17 step was exchanged in the later window: the optional A14/A15 step (exactly one of the two per trace, about 52/48) now sits directly after A33 and before A18, while the mandatory A16/A17 step (both activities in every trace, still in either order, about 52/48) now sits after A22, so that A18 follows A14/A15 and A23 follows A16/A17. This is shown directly by relational evidence — complete order reversals in all 200 traces of each window for A16/A17 versus A18/A22 — together with matching successor changes at A14, A15, A16, A17, A22 and A33, and lag changes (A22→A23 from 2 to 3 positions, A33→A18 from 3 to 2) that fit the new arrangement. The volume of evidence (22 items against roughly 0.36 expected by chance when screening 72 units) cannot be explained by random re-splitting alone. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations remain: positions and lags are event positions rather than clock time, so nothing is implied about timing; the evidence covers only the tested activities, so behaviour between A18 and A22 is not described; and sampling differences in variant frequencies can produce order reversals in finite windows, although complete reversals across every trace in both windows make a pure sampling explanation unlikely. [REL009, LAG001, POS003]
