### Observed differences

- The order of two activity groups reversed. Before, A19 and A20 sat in the first third of the case (mean relative event position about 0.36 for both) while A21 and A22 sat about two-thirds in (about 0.65); in the later window A21 and A22 sit at about 0.38 and A19 and A20 at about 0.67. A23 and A27 stayed near their old positions, shifting only slightly later (A23 about 0.40 to 0.43; A27 about 0.60 to 0.64). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

- Pairwise order relations flipped almost completely. Before, A19 preceded A21, A22, A23 and A27 in about 53 of 100 cases, and A20 preceded them in about 46 of 100; in the later window those activities precede A19 in about 47 of 100 cases and A20 in about 52 of 100. A23 preceded A21 in about 99 of 100 cases before and follows it in about 99 of 100 after; the same near-total reversal holds for A23 versus A22 and for A27 versus A21 and A22 (about 98–99 in 100 each way). A21 and A22 still co-occur in every case in both windows. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]

- Routing moved with the reversal. A19's observed outgoing transitions went from almost all to A23 (about 100 in 100) to almost all to A28 (about 98 in 100); A20 moved likewise (about 99 to A23 before, about 97 to A28 after). A21 and A22 now lead mainly to A23 (about 46–50 in 100) instead of A28 (about 47–51), while still feeding one another. A38 now enters A21 or A22 (about 45–48 in 100) where it previously entered A19 or A20 (about 41–51), and A27 now enters A19 or A20 (about 47–51) where it previously entered A21 or A22 (about 45–50). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR008]

- Typical distances along the flow changed consistently: A38 to A23 stretched from about 2 steps (in 93 of 100 cases) to 3 steps (in 90 of 100), and A37 to A23 from about 3 to 4 steps, while A27 to A28 shortened from about 3 steps (94 of 100) to 2 steps (97 of 100). [LAG019, LAG014, LAG008]

- A36 became less common: it appeared in about 24 of 100 cases before and about 13 of 100 in the later window (mean occurrences per trace 0.39 to 0.18), and A37 routed onward to A38 more often (about 56 to 72 in 100 transitions, with the immediate A37-to-A38 step rising from about 45 to 66 of 100) and back to A36 less (about 44 to 25). [OCC001, DIR007, LAG015]

- Several other flagged lag differences rest on only one or two observations in one window and were not relied on here. [LAG001, LAG016]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment containing the A19/A20 pair (each appearing in roughly half the cases) and the segment containing the ever-present A21/A22 pair appear in opposite order relative to A23, A27 and the rest of the flow, with their hand-over targets exchanged: before, the flow ran from A38 into A19-or-A20 and on to A23, with A27 later leading into A21-and-A22 and out to A28; in the later window A38 leads into A21-and-A22 and on to A23, while A27 leads into A19-or-A20 and out to A28. This is shown directly by the successor changes at A38, A27, A19, A20, A21 and A22, by the near-total reversal of the pairwise order relations, and by the matching position and distance shifts; the 46 flagged items also far exceed the roughly 0.67 expected from screening alone, and each difference exceeded those in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG008, LAG014, LAG019]

Limitations: many flagged items describe the same underlying trace behaviour — the twelve relation items and several lag items all reflect the one reversal — so they are not independent confirmations, and some lag evidence rests on very few observations. A competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is a sampling difference in variant frequencies, for instance two variant families with opposite segment order where the later window draws almost entirely from the other one; the near-complete reversal within cases that still contain all these activities makes pure sampling noise an unlikely sole cause, but it cannot be excluded. The reduced participation of A36 rests on a single occurrence indicator (48 versus 26 of 200 traces) and could reflect sampling differences; it is consistent with the A37 routing shift but not directly tied to the segment reversal. [OCC001, DIR007, REL009, REL011, LAG001, LAG016]
