### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A41 and A26 was restructured: in the earlier window each trace executed exactly one of four alternative activities (A14, A16, A17 or A18) between A41 and A26, whereas in the later window that single step appears replaced by a block in which A15, A23, A24 and A25 each execute once in an order that varies from trace to trace, placing A26 five positions after A41 instead of two. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC006, DIR009, LAG001, REL013, REL016, REL017]

Seventy-two evidence items qualified, many times the roughly 0.62 expected by chance for this screening, so the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by random re-splits; each item's difference is also larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits. The change is shown directly by relational evidence (co-occurrence and precedence), by the A41-to-A26 lag, and by successor and position shifts, and the all-or-nothing participation pattern (0 of 200 versus 200 of 200 traces for eight activities) is far beyond what sampling differences in variant frequencies typically produce. [OCC002, OCC006, DIR002, LAG001, REL016]

Limitations remain: zero participation only means A14, A16, A17 and A18 were not observed in the later window, not that they were removed from the process, although their earlier rates of about 21–29 in 100 traces make their absence in 200 traces unlikely if behaviour were unchanged. The many relation items largely restate one underlying restructuring rather than independent confirmations, and the indicators cannot establish the exact rule governing the order of the new block — the near-even directional shares are consistent with, but do not prove, a random ordering.
