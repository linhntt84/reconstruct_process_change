### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that an additional step, executed as either A19 or A20 (each in about half of the traces, never both), was introduced between A26 and A28: in the later window A19/A20 always follow A25/A26/A40 and precede A28/A45/A46, A26 and A27 now route about half of their outgoing flow through them, exactly one extra event separates A26 from A28 (lag 2 to lag 3), and A27 falls either before or after the new step. In the same window A10, previously present in about 60% of traces around that region, is skipped in about three quarters of traces. These signs are direct — order, successors and event distances, not only frequencies — mutually consistent, and the screening flagged 78 items where fewer than one would be expected by chance under an unchanged process. [OCC006, OCC007, POS002, POS003, REL018, REL022, REL028, DIR009, DIR010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013]

Limitations: this is a possibility supported by the window comparison, not a confirmed modification. The evidence does not show that the A10 decline belongs to the same change — in the later window A10 co-occurs with A19/A20 at about the level expected if they were independent (about 12 in 100 traces) — so the A10 drop may be a separate change. Sampling differences between the finite windows also cannot be ruled out for the smaller participation increases (A02, A04, A06, A08) and for the routing redistributions at A02/A04/A06, whose before-samples are very small. [REL003, REL004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
