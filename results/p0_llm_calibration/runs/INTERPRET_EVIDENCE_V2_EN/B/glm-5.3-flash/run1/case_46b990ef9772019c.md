### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, each of A33, A34, A36 and A37 appeared in about 54 of 100 cases, and each of A38, A39, A40 and A41 in about 46–47 of 100; in the later window all eight appear in 199 of 200 cases, roughly once per case, with no repetition in either window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]
- The two groups of four were alternatives before: a case ran one group or the other, and a case containing A33/A34/A36/A37 almost never contained A38/A39/A40/A41 (co-occurrence about 1–2 in 100 cases). In the later window the two groups co-occur in about 99 of 100 cases. [REL020, REL023, REL027, REL031, REL035, REL038]
- A30 and A31 occur in essentially every case in both windows. Before, A30 came before the block activities and the block activities before A31 whenever a block ran (about 45–55 of 100 cases overall); in the later window A30 precedes all eight activities in about 97–99 of 100 cases, and all eight precede A31 in about 95–97 of 100. [REL001, REL005, REL008, REL009, REL013, REL016, POS001]
- Routing moved the same way: A30's outgoing transitions earlier spread over all eight activities (about 9–15% each) and now go about 95% to A33, A34, A36 and A37; A31 fell from about 20–27% to nearly 0% of the outgoing transitions of A33/A34/A36/A37, replaced by A38–A41 (together roughly a quarter). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- Distances changed accordingly: before, A38–A41 followed A30 within 1–4 steps and A31 followed A33/A34/A36/A37 within 1–4 steps; in the later window these pairs sit in the largest measured distance bucket (5 steps) in about 92–98 of 100 observed cases, and cross-block pairs such as A33–A41 or A34–A40, seen within five steps in only 1–2 earlier cases, now appear in about 150–160 cases with distances spread over 1–5 steps. [LAG002, LAG004, LAG006, LAG008, LAG012, LAG016, LAG007, LAG010]
- Relative event positions shifted consistently: A30 moved earlier (about 65% to 59% of the case), A33/A34/A36/A37 moved earlier (about 73–74% to 65%), and A38–A41 moved slightly later (about 73–74% to 76–77%). [POS001, POS002, POS004, POS006, POS008]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the exclusive choice between the two four-activity blocks was removed: earlier, a case ran A30, then either the block A33/A34/A36/A37 (about 54 of 100 cases) or the block A38/A39/A40/A41 (about 46 of 100), then A31; in the later window, cases run A30, then the first block, then the second block, then A31, with the order inside each block still varying from case to case (each within-block ordering split roughly half and half in both windows). [REL020, REL027, REL035, REL001, REL009, REL017, OCC001, OCC005]

The support is direct and consistent: the relation items show the exclusivity-to-co-occurrence reversal and the between-block ordering outright; the routing items show A30 now leading only into the first block and A31 no longer directly following first-block activities; the distance and position items match the other block being inserted between A30 and the second block and before A31. With 83 qualifying items against about 1 expected by chance if nothing had changed, the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [REL001, DIR001, LAG002, POS001, OCC001]

Limitations: the second block's link to A31 in the later window is established through order and distance items rather than direct successor shares, since no routing profile for A38–A41 as sources is present; several before-window distance profiles rest on only 1–2 observations; and several indicators re-express the same trace behaviour, so their agreement is not independent confirmation. The main competing explanation is a sampling difference in variant frequencies between the windows — the "both blocks" behaviour does appear in about 1–2 of 100 earlier cases — but a stable variant mixture would not plausibly produce 199 of 200 later cases running both blocks, so while this cannot be fully excluded, the size and consistency of the shift make it hard to sustain. [LAG007, LAG013, REL020, OCC001]
