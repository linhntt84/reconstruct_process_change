### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the branch in which A27 and A28 executed between A21 and A23 is no longer taken, so that in the later window every observed case runs A09 → A21 → A22 → A23, with A22 — previously an alternative to the A27/A28 pair in roughly half of the cases — now executing exactly once in every trace. This is shown directly at three levels: participation (A22 up from about 48 in 100 traces to all 200; A27 and A28 down from about 53 in 100 to none), routing (A21's only observed successor is now A22), and relations (A22 sits between A21 and A23 in every later trace, while the earlier A27/A28 sequences are entirely absent). The 29 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.44 expected for an unchanged process under this screening, so the pattern as a whole is unlikely to arise by chance, even though many items restate one underlying trace difference rather than confirming it independently.
