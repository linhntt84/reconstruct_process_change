### Observed differences

- In the later window, the execution order between two sets of activities is reversed or mixed. In the earlier window, A20, A21, A23 and A24 came before A25, A26, A27 and A28 in about 97–100 in 100 traces containing both; in the later window, A25 precedes A24 in about 92 in 100 such traces (previously about 1 in 100), A27 precedes A20 in about 48 in 100 (previously essentially never), and A25 precedes A23 in about 51 in 100 (previously essentially never). The same reversal, in varying degrees, appears in all fifteen measured pairs. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015]
- The two sets also shifted position in opposite directions: A20, A21, A23 and A24 now occur later in the trace (mean relative position up by about 0.03–0.07, e.g. A24 from about 0.61 to 0.68), while A25, A26, A27 and A28 occur earlier (e.g. A25 from about 0.64 to 0.56). Participation itself did not change — each of these activities still occurs in essentially every trace in both windows. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]
- Routing around the same activities was redistributed. Earlier, virtually all observed outgoing transitions of A24 went to A25 and virtually all of A26 went to A34; in the later window, A24 sends about half to A34 and roughly four in ten to A26, A27 or A28, while A26 splits about half to A34 and half back to A20, A21, A23 or A24. New transitions also connect A20, A21 and A23 forward to A25–A28 and A25, A27 and A28 back to A20, A21, A23 and A24, and A33 now divides roughly evenly between A25 and A23 (previously about 98 in 100 to A23). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009]
- Measured distances shrank accordingly: pairs that were always at the maximum measured distance of 5 steps in the earlier window — such as A24–A34, A20–A26 and A33–A25 — now mostly lie closer, with about half at a single step for A24–A34 and A33–A25, and A24–A26 moving from always 4 steps to 1–2 steps in about two-thirds of cases. [LAG004, LAG019, LAG022, LAG042]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the activities A25, A26, A27 and A28 are executed before A20, A21, A23 and A24 — reversing the order that held in almost every earlier trace — and that A34, previously always at the maximum measured distance after A24, now directly follows it in about half of the cases. The direct support is relational: all fifteen measured pairs show the order flipped or mixed while the activities still co-occur in every trace, with about 200 observations behind each pair in each window. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, DIR004, LAG022]

The routing, positional and distance items corroborate the same underlying difference rather than adding independent confirmations: the two sets moved in opposite positional directions, A24 and A26 lost their near-exclusive successors while new transitions appeared in both directions between the sets, and former maximum-distance pairs became close. The volume of evidence also far exceeds what an unchanged process produces by chance: 76 qualifying items against roughly 0.89 expected without change, and because co-occurrence and participation stayed constant, explanations in which these activities simply occur more or less often are ruled out. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013, LAG014, LAG015, LAG016, LAG017, LAG018, LAG019, LAG020, LAG021, LAG022, LAG023, LAG024, LAG025, LAG026, LAG027, LAG028, LAG029, LAG030, LAG031, LAG032, LAG033, LAG034, LAG035, LAG036, LAG037, LAG038, LAG039, LAG040, LAG041, LAG042, LAG043]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is a shift in the mix of pre-existing trace variants rather than a reordering of a single flow: in the later window several pairs are near even splits (A20–A27, A21–A28, A23–A25 and A24–A26 at roughly 50 in 100 each way), which a changed frequency of two coexisting orderings would also produce, and sampling differences between the two 200-trace windows could contribute to parts of the pattern. A few distance items rest on only 1–3 observations in one window and were not relied upon, and transition shares are computed over outgoing transitions rather than cases.
