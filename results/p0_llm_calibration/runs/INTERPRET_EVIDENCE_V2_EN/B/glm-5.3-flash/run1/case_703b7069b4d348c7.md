### Observed differences

- In the earlier window the activities A20, A28, A29 and A30 each ran in all 200 traces; in the later window they are nearly gone — A20 in about 3 of 200 traces, A28 in 2, A29 in 1, and A30 is not observed at all. [OCC005, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011]
- The fixed block around them dissolves: earlier, A15 and A16 each co-occurred with A20/A28/A29/A30 in 100 of 100 traces (A15 typically before them, and the block before A31/A46/A51/A52 in about 98–99 of 100), while in the later window this co-occurrence is at most about 2 of 100 and A31, A46, A51 and A52 now run without the old block in about 99–100 of 100 traces. - In the later window, four previously near-absent activities — A19, A21, A22 and A23 (0–1 of 200 earlier) — each run in roughly 20–29 of 100 traces, at a similar trace position (around 38 of the trace length) as the old block, and they never co-occur with one another (co-occurrence about 0 for each pair), so typically exactly one of them runs per trace. - Participation in A02 and A10 falls (each from about 21 to 6–8 of 100 traces), though their later routing profiles rest on only about 13–18 outgoing transitions and are weak. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the always-executed block A20, A28, A29 and A30 between A15/A16 and A31 is no longer run, that about a quarter of traces instead execute exactly one of the previously near-absent activities A19, A21, A22 or A23 — after A15/A16 and A46, and leading almost exclusively into A31 — and that A04 now runs in about two thirds of traces, usually leads directly to A16, often follows A15, and precedes A31/A46/A51/A52. [OCC005, OCC011, OCC004, OCC006, DIR015, DIR006, DIR002, LAG001, LAG009, REL011]

Supporting signs: the participation flips are far outside what sampling can produce (200 of 200 falling to 0–3 of 200, and 0–1 of 200 rising to 39–58 of 200), the rewiring is shown directly by successor, co-occurrence and distance evidence rather than by frequencies alone, the picture is consistent across all five indicator families, and about 135 items qualified against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process, so chance cannot explain the pattern as a whole. [OCC005, OCC011, OCC007, DIR006, DIR015, LAG001, LAG009, REL046]

Limitations: the many relation items restate the same trace-level changes and are not independent confirmations; the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows draw different mixtures of process variants (a shift in how often each variant occurs) rather than a modification of one process, although the all-or-nothing participation pattern makes pure sampling noise implausible; A30's zero participation only means it was not observed in the later window; the routing details for A02 and A10 rest on few observations; and whether the block change and the A04 change are one modification or two independent ones cannot be determined. [REL016, REL019, OCC011, OCC001, DIR001, DIR004]
