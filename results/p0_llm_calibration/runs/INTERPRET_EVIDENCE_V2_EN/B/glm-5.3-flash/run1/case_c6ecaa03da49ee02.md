### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, executions route from A40 to A25 via A19 or A20 — split roughly evenly between the two, which almost never both occur — in place of the direct A40 → A25 transition seen before, with A23 also appearing in fewer traces. This rests on direct relational and positional evidence: the successor profile of A40 shifted from mostly A25 to mostly A19 and A20, A40 consistently precedes and A19/A20 consistently precede A25 in about half of the later traces, and the A40 → A25 step distance moved from 1–2 to 2–3, consistent with one intermediate activity. [DIR001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001]

The nine items far exceed the roughly 0.5 expected by chance at these thresholds, and the participation, routing and ordering items all describe one coherent rerouting rather than independent phenomena. Limitations: the direct A40 → A25 transition being unobserved in the later window shows only that it did not occur among the roughly 203 sampled outgoing transitions, not that the edge is gone; a sampling-driven shift in the mix of pre-existing variants could contribute to the participation changes, although a fall from about 46 in 100 transitions to none is hard to explain by sampling alone; and no relational item connects A23 to the rerouting, so the reason for its lower participation remains open.
