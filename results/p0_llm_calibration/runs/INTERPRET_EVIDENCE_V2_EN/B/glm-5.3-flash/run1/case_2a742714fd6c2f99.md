### Observed differences

- Participation moved in opposite directions for two groups of four activities. In the later window, A16, A17, A18 and A19 each appear in about 27 in 100 traces (54 of 200), down from about 83 in 100 (165 of 200), while A20, A21, A22 and A23 each appear in about 73 in 100 traces (146 of 200), up from about 18 in 100 (35 of 200). None of the eight activities repeats within a trace in either window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]
- The two groups behave as sets and are mutually exclusive. Pairs spanning the groups never co-occur in either window: earlier, about 83 in 100 traces contained A16–A19 without A20–A23, while in the later window about 73 in 100 contain A20–A23 without A16–A19. Within each group, members run together when the group runs: A16–A19 jointly in about 83 in 100 traces earlier (jointly absent in about 73 in 100 later), and A20–A23 jointly in about 73 in 100 later (jointly absent in about 83 in 100 earlier). [REL009, REL012, REL017, REL030, REL035, REL039]
- A15, which appears in all traced cases in both windows, now routes differently: among its observed outgoing transitions, the share towards A19 fell from about 83% to about 27%, while the share towards A23 rose from about 18% to about 73%. [DIR001]
- A15's company changed accordingly: earlier it co-occurred with A16–A19 and preceded them in about 83 in 100 traces; in the later window it co-occurs with A20–A23 and precedes them in about 73 in 100 traces, while A16–A19 typically no longer run alongside it (about 73 in 100 traces show A15 without them). [REL001, REL005]
- A27, also present throughout, changed company: earlier it co-occurred with A16–A19 in about 83 in 100 traces (A16 before A27 in about 47 in 100, interleaved or repeated in about 36 in 100) and ran without A20–A23 in about 83 in 100; in the later window it co-occurs with A20–A23 in about 73 in 100 traces (A20 before A27 in about 46 in 100, interleaved in about 27 in 100) and runs without A16–A19 in about 73 in 100. [REL016, REL038]
- Internal order is only partly fixed: earlier, A19 preceded A16, A17 and A18 in about 83 in 100 traces containing those pairs, with A16–A18 in mixed order; in the later window A23 precedes A20, A21 and A22 in about 73 in 100, with A20–A22 mixed. Where the old group still runs, spacing tightened slightly: A17 now sits 2 event positions after A15, and 1 event position after A19, in about half of the remaining co-occurrences, against an even spread over 2–4 and 1–3 positions before; these spacing items rest on only 54 after-window traces. [REL009, REL011, REL018, REL024, REL035, REL037, REL040, REL042, LAG001, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A15 and A27 is executed with a different group of activities in the later window: cases tend to run A20–A23 there — A23 first, then A20–A22 in mixed order — after A15 and before A27, where earlier cases tended to run A16–A19 with A19 first. The signs are direct and consistent: participation of both groups shifted by about 55 in 100 traces, with 165-versus-54 and 35-versus-146 traces behind the two sides; A15's outgoing mix flipped from mostly A19 to mostly A23; and the relational items show directly that A15 precedes and A27 follows whichever group runs, while the two groups never appear in the same trace. The volume of qualifying items (56) is also far above what the screening calibration expects if nothing had changed (about 0.66). [OCC001, OCC004, OCC005, OCC008, DIR001, REL001, REL005, REL011, REL016, REL037, REL038]

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is sampling: the two windows are finite samples, so the difference could reflect a shift in the relative frequency of two pre-existing variants rather than a modification of the process. In addition, the old group is still observed in about 27 in 100 later traces, so nothing shows it was removed entirely, and the order within A16–A18 and A20–A22 remains mixed, so no fixed inner sequence can be claimed. [OCC001, REL009, REL035]
