### Observed differences

- The prevalence of two activity pairs swapped almost exactly. In the later window, A21 appears in about 22 in 100 traces (previously about 78) and A22 in about 22 in 100 (previously about 79), while A23 rises to about 79 in 100 (previously about 22) and A24 to about 80 in 100 (previously about 23). None of the four repeats noticeably within a trace in either window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the flow out of A20 switched from the A21 → A22 route to the A23 → A24 route, with A28 now following A23 and A24 instead of A21 and A22. This is shown directly by the successor change at A20 (about 78% of outgoing transitions to A21 before, about 78% to A23 after), by the stable A23 → A24 step, and by the order relations: A20 precedes A23 and A24 in about 78 in 100 traces, A23 precedes A24 in about 78, and A28 — present throughout — now co-occurs with A23/A24 (about 79–80 in 100) where it previously co-occurred with A21/A22 (about 78–79). Participation moved in mirror image: A21 and A22 fell from about 78 to about 22 in 100 traces, A23 and A24 rose from about 22 to about 80. [DIR001, DIR004, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL003, REL004, REL008, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]

The volume of evidence makes chance as a whole unlikely: 22 items qualified, about 50 times the 0.47 expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]

The evidence cannot distinguish a structural replacement of the A21 → A22 segment from a shift in the mix of two pre-existing variants: the old edges A21 → A22 and A22 → A28 are still observed in the later window, though on only about 45–46 outgoing transitions, and low participation does not show that A21 or A22 left the process. The minor new successors around A21 and A22 (about 2% each, roughly one transition) are too weak to read as new edges, and sampling variation in variant frequencies remains a competing explanation for part of the shift, though not for differences of this size and consistency.
