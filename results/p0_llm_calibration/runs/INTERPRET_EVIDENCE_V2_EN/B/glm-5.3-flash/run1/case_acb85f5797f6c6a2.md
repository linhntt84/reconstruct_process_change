### Observed differences

- In the later window, A21 and A22 each occur before all four of A30, A31, A36 and A37 in essentially every trace (100 in 100), whereas in the earlier window the order was exactly reversed: each of those four came before A21 and A22 in essentially every trace. - The four activities A30, A31, A36 and A37 now sit later in the trace: their mean relative position rose from about 0.41 to about 0.58, while A22 moved earlier (about 0.48 to 0.37) and A21 moved earlier (about 0.62 to 0.51). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Successors changed at the edges of this group: A20, which earlier split its outgoing flow roughly evenly over A30, A31, A36 and A37 (about 25 in 100 each), now always leads to A22, with the transitions to the four no longer observed; A21, which earlier always led to A25, now leads to one of the four (roughly 19–30 in 100 each); and each of the four now leads to A25 in roughly 21–27 in 100 cases, where earlier about 24–26 in 100 led to A22 and A25 was not observed as a successor. - Distances changed in a mirrored way: A20 and A22 went from five event positions apart to directly consecutive, while A21 and A25 went from directly consecutive to five positions apart. [LAG001, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of four activities A30, A31, A36 and A37 was placed later in the flow: in the later window it runs after A21 (and after A22) and before A25, whereas in the earlier window it ran between A20 and A22, with A25 directly after A21. This is shown directly by the complete order flip between A21/A22 and each block member, and consistently by the position shifts, the successor changes at A20, A21 and the block members, and the mirrored distances (A20–A22 closing from five positions to one exactly as A21–A25 opens from one to five); the twenty qualifying items also far exceed the roughly 0.44 expected by chance if nothing had changed, so chance alone cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: the items overlap heavily — the eight relation items and the direction, position and lag items largely describe the same single relocation rather than independent confirmations; positions are event positions, so the change concerns relative order rather than clock time; and finite samples cannot fully rule out a shift in variant frequencies (for example a differently ordered variant appearing in only one window), although complete order flips backed by all 200 traces per window make a pure sampling explanation unlikely.
