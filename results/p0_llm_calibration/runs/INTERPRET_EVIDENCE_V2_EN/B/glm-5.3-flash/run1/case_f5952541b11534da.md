### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, roughly 4 in 10 cases take an alternative route on which A27 directly follows A42, bypassing the segment containing A26, A19, A25 and A20, while the remaining cases run the original sequence unchanged. The relational evidence is direct: A42→A27 appears as a successor taking about 34 in 100 of A42's outgoing transitions, the A42→A27 distance contracts from 4–5 positions to 1–2 in about 42 in 100 pairs, and the same roughly 41 in 100 traces that show the short A42→A27 link are those in which A19, A20, A25 and A26 drop out together. The volume of qualifying evidence (19 items against roughly 0.6 expected if nothing had changed) also exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces, and every item's difference is unusual for random re-splits of the pooled traces. Limitations: these items largely describe one shared trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations; the absence of A42→A27 from the earlier routing profile does not prove the connection was previously absent, and with 200-trace windows a shift in the mix of existing variants — for instance a rare bypass variant that the earlier window mostly missed — remains a competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out.
