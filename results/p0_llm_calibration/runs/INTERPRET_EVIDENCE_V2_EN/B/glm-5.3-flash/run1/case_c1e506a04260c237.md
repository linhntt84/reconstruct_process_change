### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace contained the chain A37 → A14 → A15 → A22, with A22 four event positions after A37; A14 and A15 each appeared in 100% of traces with no repetitions, and the branch between A15 and A22 used A20 in about 48 in 100 traces (96 of 200) and A21 in about 52 in 100 (104 of 200), never both. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL010, LAG001]
- In the later window, A14 and A15 each appear in only about 59 in 100 traces (117 of 200), A20 in about 29 in 100 (58) and A21 in about 30 in 100 (59), while about 42 in 100 traces (83) contain none of these four activities. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL010]
- A37 and A22 still occur in every trace, but A37's outgoing routing changed: before, all of its observed outgoing transitions led to A14, whereas in the later window about 58.5% lead to A14 and about 41.5% lead directly to A22, a transition not observed earlier. [DIR002]
- Matching this, the distance from A37 to A22, always 4 positions in the earlier window, is now 1 position in about 41.5% of traces and 4 positions in the rest. [LAG001]
- The order of the activities that still run is unchanged (A37 before A14 before A15, with A20 or A21 between A15 and A22), and among traces that still execute the branch the A20/A21 split stays roughly 50/50, so the branch itself looks intact and is simply executed less often. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009, REL011, REL012, OCC003, OCC004]
- A22 remains in every trace; its relative position shifted slightly earlier (mean about 0.44 to about 0.41 of the trace, with somewhat more spread), which fits the shortened traces rather than A22 being moved. [POS001]
- Separately, routing out of A18 redistributed: the share of its observed outgoing transitions to A16 fell from about 36% to about 18%, while the share to A27 rose from about 40% to about 58% (A17 steady at about 24%), based on roughly 100 outgoing transitions per window. [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that in the later window about 4 in 10 cases go directly from A37 to A22, so that A14, A15 and the A20/A21 branch between them are not executed, whereas previously every trace ran A37 → A14 → A15 → (A20 or A21) → A22. This rests on directly observed relational evidence — the new A37→A22 transition and the shortened A37→A22 distance — together with participation drops of the same size (about 41.5% of traces) for A14, A15, A20 and A21 while A37 and A22 remain universal, and with the surviving cases keeping the original order and branch split. Eighteen indicators qualified in total, far more than the roughly 0.4 expected for an unchanged process, and each difference exceeds those seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data, so the ensemble is unlikely to be a screening artefact. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR002, LAG001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that this direct A37→A22 behaviour existed earlier as a rare variant that the 200 before-traces did not sample — A14 appeared in all 200 earlier traces, so any earlier rate must have been very small, but a low pre-existing rate cannot be excluded, and finite-sample differences in variant frequencies remain a possible partial explanation. The routing shift out of A18 involves activities not covered by any other item and rests on a single indicator, so it could reflect sampling variation in transition shares rather than a durable change; moreover, most items describe one underlying trace behaviour, so their agreement is consistency rather than independent confirmation. [OCC001, REL001, DIR001]
