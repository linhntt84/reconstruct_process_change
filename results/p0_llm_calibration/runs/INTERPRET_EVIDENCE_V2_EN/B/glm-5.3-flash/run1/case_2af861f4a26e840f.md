### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a substantially larger share of cases runs through A06 in a specific placement: A38 typically leads into A06 one step earlier, A06 leads to A19 and A20 within one to two steps and to A30 and A31 three to four steps later, and this configuration appears in about 68 in 100 traces versus about 17–18 in 100 before. This placement reading rests on directly observed relation, lag and successor evidence rather than on the frequency rise alone, and it is consistent across the occurrence, position, direction, relation and lag indicators; the number of flagged items (54, against roughly 0.73 expected for an unchanged process) also exceeds what chance would produce as a whole. A consistent companion change is that A30 and A31 now repeat in about 37 in 100 traces, loosening the previously strict ordering inside the A19–A44 block, while A04, A08 and A10 are executed in fewer traces. [OCC002, OCC005, DIR002, DIR007, REL003, REL007, LAG004, LAG012]

The limitations are that many flagged items describe the same underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations, and that the apparent rerouting of A04, A08 and A10 rests on only about 12–17 after-window observations each; a shift in the mix of existing variants between the two 200-trace windows — for example a previously rare A06-containing variant becoming dominant, or the drops in A04, A08 and A10 being sampling fluctuations — cannot be ruled out with this evidence.
