### Observed differences

- In the later window, A14 runs in about 47 in 100 traces (about 94 of 200) versus about 23 in 100 before, and A15 in about 53 in 100 (about 106 of 200) versus about 22 in 100 before; neither activity repeats within a trace in either window. [OCC001, OCC002]
- The direct transition A35 → A20 is no longer observed. Before, A35's observed outgoing transitions led to A20 in about 56 in 100 cases, to A15 in about 22 and to A14 in about 23; in the later window they split between A15 (about 53 in 100) and A14 (about 47 in 100). [DIR001]
- A14 and A15 are mutually exclusive alternatives in both windows, never occurring together. In the later window exactly one of them runs in every trace (A14 alone in about 47 in 100, A15 alone in about 53 in 100), whereas before neither appeared in about 55 in 100 traces. [REL001]
- A20 itself appears in every trace in both windows; what differs is how it is reached. Whenever A14 or A15 occurs, A35 precedes it and A20 follows it, and the shares of such traces rose in step with participation (for example A15 before A20 from about 22 in 100 to about 53 in 100). [REL002, REL003, REL005, REL006]
- The positional distance from A35 to A20 was 1 step in about 56 in 100 cases and 2 steps in about 45 in 100 before; in the later window it is 2 steps in all observed cases. [LAG001]
- A secondary reflection of A15's higher participation: A15 precedes A18 in about 21 in 100 traces in the later window versus about 8.5 in 100 before. [REL004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the route from A35 to A20 now always includes an intermediate step: in the later window every observed trace executes A35, then exactly one of A14 or A15, then A20, whereas before about 55 in 100 traces went directly from A35 to A20 and only about 45 in 100 had A14 or A15 between them. This placement difference is shown directly by the successor profile of A35 (no transition to A20 observed in the later window, transitions redistributed to A14 and A15), by the A35–A20 distance shifting from mostly 1 step to always 2 steps, and by the relation profiles showing A35 before A14/A15 before A20 in every trace containing them. [DIR001, LAG001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL005, REL006]

The screening baseline is clearly exceeded: nine items qualify where only about 0.34 would be expected by chance at these thresholds, and every item shows an empirical strength near 0.995, meaning differences this large rarely arise from random re-splits of unchanged behaviour. The items are mutually consistent, but the occurrence, direction and relation indicators largely describe the same underlying trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, REL001, LAG001]

Limitations remain: the absence of A35 → A20 in the later window shows only that the edge was not observed, not that it is absent from the process, and finite 200-trace windows cannot fully rule out a shift in the mix of trace variants — for example the direct variant becoming very rare rather than disappearing — as the source of the pattern, although a transition seen in about 56 in 100 cases before is very unlikely to go unobserved in a sample of this size by chance alone. The evidence also cannot show how the choice between A14 and A15 is made. [DIR001, LAG001, REL001]
