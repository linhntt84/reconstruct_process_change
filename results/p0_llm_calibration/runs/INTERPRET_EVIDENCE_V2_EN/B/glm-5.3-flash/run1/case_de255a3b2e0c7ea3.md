### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that routing around several activities was restructured in the later window: A19 and A20, which previously ran after A25/A26 and led mainly into A27/A28, now run before A25 — typically entered from A40, which previously went to A25 directly — with A26 → A27 → A28 following in tight succession; A10, previously a mid-process activity in about 20 in 100 cases, now appears in about 60 in 100 cases as the late segment A45 → A10 → A46; and A04 and A06 are executed in fewer cases (roughly half and a third of their earlier participation). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR006, DIR007, DIR011, REL010, REL013, LAG011, POS001, DIR004, DIR013, LAG001]

The placement claims rest on relational and positional evidence rather than frequency alone: the order reversals are direct (A19/A20 before A25/A26/A27 rose from zero to about half of cases while the reverse order collapsed to near zero), the new adjacencies are direct (A40→A19/A20, A45→A10 and A10→A46 one step apart in about 94–99 in 100 of instances), and the routing, participation and position indicators agree on the same activities. The 53 qualifying items far exceed the fewer than 1 expected by chance among the 144 tested units, and each difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits, so the pattern as a whole is unlikely if nothing had changed. [REL010, REL013, LAG011, LAG001, DIR008, OCC003, POS001]

Limitations: the windows are 200-trace samples, so part of the frequency shifts — particularly the declines of A04 and A06 and their reshuffled outgoing profiles, which rest on 22 and 12 later-window transitions — could reflect sampling differences in variant frequencies rather than a structural change; a few lag items rest on a single observation on one side (LAG003, LAG006, LAG007); items describing the same behaviour (for instance REL003–REL008, which all capture A10's new late position) are not independent confirmations; and routing shares cover only observed outgoing transitions, so an unobserved transition such as A02→A44 is not proven absent.
