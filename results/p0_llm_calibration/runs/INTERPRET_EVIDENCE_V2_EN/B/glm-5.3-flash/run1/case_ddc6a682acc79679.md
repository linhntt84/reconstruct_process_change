### Observed differences

- A20 and A21 were present in every earlier trace (200 of 200); in the later window they appear in only about 1 in 100 traces (2 of 200), with a single occurrence when present. [OCC003, OCC004]
- A14 and A15 were not observed in the earlier window; in the later window A14 appears in about 48 in 100 traces (96 of 200) and A15 in about 51 in 100 (102 of 200), each occurring exactly once when present. [OCC001, OCC002]
- In the later window A14 and A15 are mutually exclusive: about 48 in 100 traces contain only A14, about 51 only A15, and the two never occur in the same trace. [REL001]
- The outgoing transitions of A37 changed: earlier, A37 was followed by A21 in about 54 in 100 cases and A20 in about 46; in the later window it is followed by A15 in about 51 and A14 in about 48, with A20 and A21 almost never observed (about 1 in 200 each). [DIR003]
- Whenever A14 or A15 occurs, it follows A37 and precedes A22, and A22 is their only observed successor. [DIR001, DIR002, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009]
- The earlier coupling disappeared: A20 and A21 previously always co-occurred with each other and with A22, always between A37 and A22; in the later window A22 occurs without either of them in about 99 in 100 traces. [REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- The distance from A37 to A22 shortened from three event positions in all earlier traces to two positions in about 99 in 100 later traces. [LAG001]
- A22 itself is stable: present in all traces in both windows, only slightly earlier in relative position (mean about 0.41 to 0.39), while A14 and A15 now occupy a relative position (about 0.36) similar to where A20 and A21 used to sit. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the exclusive branch after A37 was redirected from A20 and A21 to the newly appearing activities A14 and A15, which run between A37 and A22 and shorten that segment from three to two event positions. The routing change at A37 is observed directly, the participation shifts are near-complete on both sides, every observed co-occurrence places A14 or A15 after A37 and before A22, and the A37-to-A22 lag moved accordingly; with 25 qualifying items against roughly 0.42 expected if nothing had changed, chance alone cannot account for the pattern as a whole, though many items describe the same underlying shift rather than independent confirmations. [DIR003, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009, LAG001]

Two limitations remain: the earlier path is not fully eliminated, since A20 and A21 still occur in about 1 in 100 later traces, and the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows sample coexisting process variants in shifted proportions — for example a new variant running alongside a retained old one — rather than a modification of a single process; the activity that sat between A20/A21 and A22 in the earlier window is also not identified, so that part of the earlier structure remains unobserved. [OCC003, OCC004, REL010]
