### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the exclusive branch — in which A32 was executed in about half the cases instead of A33 and A34, after A28 — was replaced by a single path in which A33 and A34 (in either order) run immediately after A25 and before A26, and A32 runs between A28 and A29, with all three activities now executed in every case. The order flips are shown directly by the before/after relation profiles, the successor changes by the routing profiles, the distance changes by the gap profiles, and the shift from about half to full participation by the occurrence profiles; the number of qualifying items (37) is far above the roughly 0.64 expected by chance alone, and the completeness of the flips (0 of 200 co-occurrences before versus all 200 after) makes sampling noise an unlikely explanation. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL005, REL006, REL008, REL009, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG003, LAG004, LAG008, LAG009]

Limitations: the exact placement of A32 between A28 and A29 rests on routing and relation evidence rather than a direct distance measure for that pair; one distance item (A26–A34) rests on a single observation and was not relied on; and the items describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is a shift in the mix of process variants — the later window reflecting one variant that happened to be absent from the 200 earlier traces — although a variant absent from 200 earlier cases yet covering all 200 later cases makes this unlikely. [LAG006, REL013, OCC001]
