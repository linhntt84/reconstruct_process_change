### Observed differences

- The four activities A29–A32 all moved toward near-universal execution: A29 and A30 appear in about 60 in 100 traces in the earlier window and in all 200 traces in the later window; A31 and A32 rise from about 40 in 100 to about 99 in 100. None of the four shows repetition in either window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Before, the pairs A29–A30 and A31–A32 were mutually exclusive: A29 never co-occurred with A31 or A32, and A30 never with A31 or A32 (0 of 200 before-traces), while A29–A30 co-occurred in about 60 in 100 traces and A31–A32 in about 40 in 100. In the later window all four co-occur in about 99 in 100 traces, with A29 before A31/A32 and A30 before A31/A32 in essentially all of them; the internal order of each pair remains mixed in both windows. [REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- Successor shares changed accordingly: of the outgoing transitions observed from A29, the share leading to A26 fell from about 57% to under 1%, with new transitions to A31 (about 28%) and A32 (about 26%); from A30, the share to A26 fell from about 43% to under 1%, replaced by transitions to A31 (about 26%) and A32 (about 21%). [DIR002, DIR003]
- From A25, about 40% of observed outgoing transitions previously went directly to A31 or A32; in the later window only transitions to A29 (about 47%) and A30 (about 53%) are observed, yet A25 still precedes A31/A32 in about 99 in 100 later traces — consistent with A29/A30 now sitting between them rather than the edges being removed. [DIR001, REL003, REL004]
- Event distances grew by roughly two positions: A25 to A26 went from a 3-step gap in all before-traces to a 5-step gap in about 99 in 100 after-traces; A25 to A31/A32 from gaps of 1–2 steps to 3–4; A29/A30 to A26 from 1–2 steps to 3–4. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005]
- Relative event positions shifted only slightly: A26 and A31/A32 sit a little later (mean relative position about 0.79 to 0.82 and 0.74 to 0.77), A25 and A29/A30 a little earlier; these are position-scale shifts, not clock-time moves. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the exclusive branch choice after A25 gave way to a fixed sequence: in the earlier window a trace contained either the pair A29–A30 or the pair A31–A32 (each in either internal order), with A26 following three positions after A25, whereas in the later window essentially every trace runs A25 → A29/A30 (either order) → A31/A32 (either order) → A26, so A31 and A32 now execute after the A29–A30 pair rather than instead of it. [REL010, DIR002, LAG001]

The item count (32) is many times the roughly 0.41 expected by chance if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeds those seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. The hypothesis is shown directly rather than through frequencies alone: pairs that never co-occurred (0 of 200 before-traces) now co-occur in a fixed order in about 99 in 100 traces, the A26-leading transitions from A29/A30 were replaced by transitions into A31/A32, and the distance increases match the two extra positions the additional pair implies. [REL010, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG004]

The main competing explanation — a shift in variant frequencies between finite samples — cannot be fully excluded, but going from zero A29–A31 co-occurrence in 200 before-traces to about 198 of 200 after is difficult to produce by sampling alone, since a stable process with the later behaviour should show some co-occurrence earlier. The positional shifts are small and on the event-position scale, so they corroborate rather than carry the conclusion. [REL010, OCC003, POS005]
