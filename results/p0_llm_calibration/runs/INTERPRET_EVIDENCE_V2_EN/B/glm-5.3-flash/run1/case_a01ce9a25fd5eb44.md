### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, one of four mutually exclusive activities — A14, A15, A20 or A21 — occurs in nearly every case between A23 and A25, placed before A24 in about half of the cases and after A24 in the other half, whereas earlier A23, A24 and A25 always ran directly in sequence. This rests on direct relational and positional evidence, not just frequency counts: each of the four always lies after A23 and before A25; the outgoing transitions of A23 and A24 shifted exactly at their former edges; and all three measured successions (A23→A24, A24→A25, A23→A25) moved by exactly one step. The amount of evidence also exceeds what an unchanged process typically yields: 43 items qualified from 103 tested units against roughly 0.5 expected by chance, and every item's difference is larger than in 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL004, REL006, REL011, REL013, REL017, REL019, REL022, REL024, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL009, REL010, REL016, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

Limitations: the many relation items largely restate the same underlying behaviour for the four activities and are not independent confirmations; with two finite 200-case samples, sampling differences in variant frequencies cannot be strictly excluded, although 0 of 200 earlier versus about 199 of 200 later makes a pure sampling explanation implausible. The evidence also does not show what determines the choice among the four activities or between the two positions, and the accompanying drop in A39 (about 72 to about 56 of 100 cases) is not accounted for by this reading and may be a separate difference.
