Several other lag items that fit the new routing (for example A19→A29, A26→A33, A28→A19) rest on a single before-window observation each and are weak on their own. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the flow through this region was re-ordered and re-routed: the block A19, A20, A25 and A26, which previously ran before A27/A28 and was followed by A27 in about 93–97 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions, now runs after A27/A28 and is followed by A29 and A33 instead, with A46 now feeding A27 rather than the block; in the same windows, A04, which previously preceded A15 and the A27–A33 activities in about 43–65 in 100 traces, is executed in only about 24 in 100 traces, down from about 72 in 100 [REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR011, OCC002, REL004, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013].

The order reversal is measured directly on the activity pairs rather than inferred from marginal frequencies, and it occurs while co-occurrence stays roughly stable (about 20–29 in 100 traces per pair in both windows, i.e. about 40–58 co-occurring traces per window), making a flip from about 95 in 100 to about 0 in 100 hard to attribute to sampling noise; the positional and routing changes point the same way, all qualifying items exceeded about 199 of 200 random re-splits, and 70 items qualified in total against roughly 1 expected for the 192 tested units. The relational, positional, routing and lag items describing the block, however, largely reflect one and the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations [REL016, REL018, REL020, REL022, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008].

The evidence cannot rule out that the two 200-trace windows simply contain different mixtures of process variants (for example, case types arriving in different proportions), which would produce differences of this size without any single trace's routing having changed; it also cannot establish whether A04's reduced participation belongs to the same structural change or is a separate one. Positions and gaps are measured in event positions rather than clock time, and several lag items fitting the new routing rest on a single before-window observation [LAG011, LAG012, LAG013, LAG014, LAG015, LAG018, POS003].
