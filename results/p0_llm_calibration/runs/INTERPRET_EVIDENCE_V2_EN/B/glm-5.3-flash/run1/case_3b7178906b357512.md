### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A25 and A26 each appeared in every trace, about 1.6 times per trace on average, and each recurred (appeared more than once in a trace) in roughly 37 in 100 traces; in the later window, each occurs exactly once per trace and recurrence is no longer observed. [OCC001, OCC002]
- In the earlier window, about 37 of A26's observed outgoing transitions in 100 led to A25 and about 63 in 100 led to A20; in the later window, all observed outgoing transitions of A26 lead to A20, and A25 is no longer seen as a successor of A26. [DIR001]
- A25 and A26 still occur together in every trace in both windows, but in the earlier window about 37 in 100 traces showed the pair interleaved or repeated, while in the later window A25 precedes A26 in a single pass in every trace. [REL001]
- The gap from A25 to A26 tightened: in the earlier window A26 immediately followed A25 in about 36 in 100 cases, with wider gaps of three or five event positions in the remainder; in the later window the gap is one position in every trace. [LAG001]
- Each of these differences is larger than those arising in about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, REL001, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Five evidence items qualified where fewer than one (about 0.29) would be expected if nothing had changed, so this set of findings is not what an unchanged process typically produces. The evidence supports the hypothesis that the repetition between A25 and A26 is no longer executed: in the earlier window A26 could return to A25 and both activities could appear two or more times (in roughly 37 of 100 traces), whereas in the later window every trace runs A25 once, A26 immediately after it, and A26 then proceeds to A20. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, REL001, LAG001]

The change is visible directly in the routing out of A26, in the recurrence of both activities dropping to zero, and in the relational and positional structure (strict A25-before-A26 with a one-position gap in all traces), and all items agree on one coherent picture. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC002, REL001, LAG001]

Limitations: the five items describe one underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations, and each window is a finite sample of 200 traces. A competing explanation is that the repeating variant still exists but did not appear in the later sample; this is unlikely given the variant was present in about 73 of 200 earlier traces yet in none of 200 later traces, but sampling differences in variant frequencies cannot be fully excluded. The absence of A26→A25 transitions in the later window also shows only that this edge was not exercised in the sample, not that it is absent from the process. [DIR001, OCC001]
