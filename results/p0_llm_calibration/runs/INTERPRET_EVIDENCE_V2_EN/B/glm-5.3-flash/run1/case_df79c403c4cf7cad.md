### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of two routing changes in the later window. First, an A02 path now runs in about three in ten cases, placed after A14 and before A15: A14 hands over to A02 in about a quarter of its outgoing transitions, A02 leads mainly to A15, A02 precedes A15/A25/A40 in 13–26 in 100 traces, and its median position moved from about 0.69 to 0.17 of the trace. Second, the mutually exclusive A19-or-A20 step, previously executed in about 45 in 100 cases, now executes in every case and the route to A25 runs through it: A40 now leads to A19 or A20 instead of directly to A25, and the A40 → A25 distance grew from mostly one step to two. These are direct relational, directional and positional changes, consistent across the occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators, and the 23 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.64 expected by chance if nothing had changed. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, LAG003]

The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply sampled different mixes of existing process variants: the early-A02 path may be a rare variant whose frequency rose, and "no longer observed" for A40 → A25 means only that it did not appear in about 202 outgoing transitions, not that the edge is absent from the process. Several items also describe the same trace behaviour (for example, the A02 occurrence, position and relation items all reflect one path), so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, POS001, DIR003, REL005, REL006]
