### Observed differences

- In the later window, about 48% of A25's outgoing transitions go to A33 and about 47% to A32, whereas earlier about 97% went to A26; the A25→A26 transition has nearly disappeared (about 1%). [DIR001]
- In the later window, A28 leads to A29 in about 94% of its transitions (earlier about 53% went to A32 and 45% to A33), and A32 and A33 now lead to A26 in about 47–49% of their transitions, while their transitions to A29 fell from roughly half to about 1%. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- All four monitored order relations reversed: A26 ran before A32 in 99% of traces earlier but after it in about 97.5% in the later window; the same flip holds for A26/A33 (99.5% to about 97.5%), A28/A32 (99% to about 96.5%) and A28/A33 (99.5% to about 96.5%), while both activities of each pair still occur together in every trace. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A32 and A33, which earlier ran after A28, now run directly after A25 and before A26 and A28, while A29 — which earlier followed the A32/A33 block — now follows A28 directly: earlier a typical trace ran A25, A26 immediately after, A28 about three steps after A25, then A32/A33 in either order, then A29; in the later window it runs A25, A32/A33 within one to two steps, A26, A28, and A29 immediately after A28. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, LAG001, LAG003, LAG007]

The order reversals are relational evidence observed directly in all four activity pairs, the successor changes at A25, A28, A32 and A33 match this reordering, and the lag and position shifts move exactly as it predicts; activity frequencies and co-occurrence are unchanged, so the difference lies in routing and order rather than participation. Several indicators describe the same traces, so the items corroborate one reordering rather than independent changes. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG001, LAG003, LAG010]

The windows hold only 200 traces each, so a shift in the mix of two pre-existing ordering variants — a variant that was rare earlier becoming dominant later — would produce a similar picture, although a near-complete reversal in about 97–99% of traces goes well beyond the differences random re-splits of a stable process produce. The evidence also cannot identify the activity that appears between A26 and A28 in both windows, and positions and lags are measured in event positions, not clock time. [REL001, LAG001, LAG002]
