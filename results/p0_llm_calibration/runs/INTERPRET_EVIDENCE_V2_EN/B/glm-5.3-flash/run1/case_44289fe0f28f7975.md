- In the later window, A30, A31, A36 and A37 each occur about 3 times per trace (mean about 3.1, up from about 1.0) and are repeated within a trace in about 69 in 100 cases, up from about 1–2 in 100; participation is unchanged, with every trace containing each of the four in both windows. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the fragment containing A30, A31, A36 and A37 runs repeatedly within a case: after A37 the flow typically returns to A30 (about two in three transitions, versus about 1 in 100 before) instead of proceeding to A25, so the block completes roughly two extra cycles per case while the exit to A25 continues about once per case. The routing change at A37 is observed directly; the roughly threefold increase in occurrences and the new within-trace repetition of all four activities agree with it; the pairwise orderings switch from largely fixed to interleaved or repeated; and the wider position and gap distributions are consistent with the block being traversed several times. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG005, LAG004, LAG009, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

With 22 qualifying items against roughly 0.57 expected if nothing had changed, chance alone cannot account for the evidence as a whole; the items largely describe one underlying behaviour rather than independent confirmations, but they converge on the same routing-and-repetition picture, and differences of this size (repetition rising from about 1 in 100 to about 69 in 100 traces) are unlikely to be sampling artefacts between the two 200-trace windows. [DIR001, OCC001, REL001]

Limitations: the evidence shows that the block repeats and that A37 now usually feeds back to A30, but it cannot establish the exact construct — a direct return transition A37→A30 is the simplest reading, yet the same behaviour could arise if the fragment sits inside a larger repeated region or if concurrent execution produces the interleaving; the re-entry into the block is observed only at A37→A30, and several ordered-pair gap profiles rest on only 1–3 before-window observations, so their before-window shape is weak. [DIR001, LAG003, LAG004, LAG007, LAG009, LAG011, LAG012]
