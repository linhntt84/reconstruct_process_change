### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a middle segment of the process executes in a different order: the activities A20, A21, A23 and A24, which previously ran before A27 and A31, now run after A31 and lead on to A32, while A19, A22, A25 and A26 moved to before A27 and now lead to A27 rather than A32. In addition, A08 is executed in far fewer traces (about 19 in 100 versus about 62 in 100) and no longer sits near the end leading almost exclusively to A48, while A02, A06 and A10 take part in roughly three times as many traces. [REL029, DIR016, DIR008, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS001]

The order flips are near-deterministic with full support in both windows (200 of 200 traces per pair), a reversal that random re-splits of a stable process do not produce; the successor changes rest on 40–205 outgoing transitions per window, and the position and lag movements point the same way. The roughly 120 flagged items far exceed the about 1.3 expected without a change, though most of them reflect these two underlying behavioural differences rather than independent confirmations. [REL029, REL047, DIR016, POS003, LAG025]

Limitations: the indicators show that relative order and direct succession changed, but not the exact resulting structure, and they cannot rule out that a shift in the mix of process variants (for example a previously rare end-to-end variant becoming dominant) produces the same pairwise flips; whether the A08/A02/A06/A10 participation change belongs to the same restructuring or is a separate change in how often a path is taken is not decidable here. A few flagged items rest on only one or two observations in a window (for example DIR002, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008) and add little weight individually. [DIR002, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]
