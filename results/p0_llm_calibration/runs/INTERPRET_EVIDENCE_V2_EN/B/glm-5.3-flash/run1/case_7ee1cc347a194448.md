### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process now runs a single dominant path in which A26 is followed by A27 and then A28, with A28 two event positions after A26, while the block of activities A32, A33, A38 and A39 that previously executed between A26 and A28 in roughly four in ten cases is now largely skipped; in addition, A10 appears in about a quarter of later-window cases, late in the flow, typically chained between A45 and A46. Sixty-four qualifying items stand against roughly 0.82 expected if nothing had changed, and the ordering evidence is direct rather than inferential: A26 precedes A27 in about 97 in 100 later-window traces, A27 precedes A28 in about 98 in 100, A28 sits at lag 2 after A26 in about 98 in 100, and the four branch activities fall from 78–79 supporting traces to 2–3, with occurrence, routing, position, relation and lag indicators all describing the same restructuring.
