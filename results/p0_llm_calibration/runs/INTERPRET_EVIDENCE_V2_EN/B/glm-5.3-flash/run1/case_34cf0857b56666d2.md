### Observed differences

- In the later window, A27 appears in about 99 in 100 traces, up from about 61 in 100, and its frequency rises from roughly 0.6 to 1.0 occurrences per trace. [OCC001]
- Four activities — A32, A33, A38 and A39 — each fall from roughly 39–40 in 100 traces to about 1–3 in 100; pairs among them that previously co-occurred in about 39 in 100 traces now almost never do. Each of these differences is larger than in all but about 1 in 200 random re-splits of the data. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL021, REL028]
- Before, A27 and these four were mutually exclusive alternatives: about 61 in 100 traces contained A27 without them and about 39 in 100 contained one of them without A27, with essentially no co-occurrence; in the later window about 98–99 in 100 traces contain A27 and none of the four. [REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015]
- Outgoing transitions of A26 are redirected: before, about 59% led to A27 and about 38% combined led to A32/A33/A38/A39; after, about 96% lead to A27. [DIR001]
- The main sequence tightens: A26 followed by A27 rises from about 60 to 97 in 100 traces and A27 followed by A28 from about 60 to 95; A42, which previously appeared alongside the branch activities in about 39 in 100 traces, now precedes A27 in about 97 in 100. [REL006, REL011, REL016, REL024]
- A28 occurs slightly earlier within traces (mean relative position about 0.56 vs 0.53), and the gap from A26 to A28 is now almost always 2 event steps (about 94 in 100, versus about 60 before, with the former 5-step gap of about 36 in 100 gone). [POS001, LAG002]
- The few remaining occurrences of the four activities (2–3 traces each) are too few to characterise their residual routing or timing. [OCC002, DIR002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative branch formed by A32, A33, A38 and A39 — previously executed in about 39 in 100 cases as a mutually exclusive alternative to the A27 segment between A26 and A28 — is now skipped or no longer reached, so that nearly all cases run through A26 → A27 → A28, typically with A42 (and often A19) preceding A27. The number of qualifying items (53, against roughly 0.8 expected if nothing had changed) cannot be explained by chance as a whole, and the participation collapse of the four activities, the redirection of A26's outgoing flow towards A27, and the co-occurrence reversals consistently describe the same routing change across indicators. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001, REL006, REL011, REL012, REL016]

The evidence shows how often activities execute and how flow is shared, not whether the branch was removed from the process or merely not triggered: a case-mix or sampling shift, in which the later window happens to contain almost no cases of the branch variant even though it still exists, cannot be ruled out — although a simultaneous fall from about 78 to 2–3 traces out of 200 for four activities is difficult to attribute to sampling alone. Items describing the residual behaviour of the branch activities (such as DIR002 and most lag items) rest on only 1–3 later-window traces, and many items restate the same underlying trace behaviour rather than confirming it independently. [DIR002, LAG003, POS002, OCC002]
