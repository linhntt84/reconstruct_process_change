### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative step between A21 and A23 — a choice between A14 and A15 — is no longer executed, so that in the later window traces run directly from A21 via A22 to A23. Previously every trace contained exactly one of A14 or A15 (about 46 versus 55 in 100) between A21 and A23; in the later window A14 is absent, A15 appears once, and A21, A22 and A23 still occur in all traces. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, POS003]

The support is direct as well as consistent: the successor profiles of A21 and A22, measured on all 200 traces in both windows, lost their A14 and A15 components, and the A21–A23 distance shortened from three positions to two; because A14 and A15 each ran in roughly half of the earlier traces, their disappearance is unlikely to be a rare-variant sampling effect. [DIR003, DIR004, LAG004, OCC001, OCC002]
