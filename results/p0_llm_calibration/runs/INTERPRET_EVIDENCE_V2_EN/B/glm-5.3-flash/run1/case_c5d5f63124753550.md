### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A25 and A26 each ran exactly once in every one of the 200 traces, at about 36–37% of the way through the trace; in the later window, each appears in only about 3 of 200 traces (roughly 1.5 in 100). [OCC003, OCC004, POS003, POS004]
- In the later window, A19 and A20 — not observed at all earlier — each run about once per containing trace, A19 in about 96 of 200 traces (48 in 100) and A20 in about 102 of 200 (51 in 100), at essentially the same relative position (mean about 0.37) that A25 and A26 used to hold. [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002]
- A19 and A20 are mutually exclusive: they never occur in the same trace, and about 99 in 100 later traces contain exactly one of them (about 1 in 100 contains neither). [REL001]
- Routing into and out of this segment moved with them: A42's observed outgoing transitions, previously mostly to A26 (about 47 in 100) and A25 (about 39 in 100), now go mostly to A20 (about 41 in 100) and A19 (about 39 in 100), while A19 and A20 each lead directly to A27 in about 95 in 100 of their observed outgoing transitions. [DIR005, DIR001, DIR002]
- The order around the segment is preserved: earlier, A42 always preceded A25 and A26, the two ran in either order (A25 first in about 46 in 100 traces), and both preceded A27 in nearly every trace; in the later window, A42 precedes A19 in about 46 in 100 traces and A20 in about 50 in 100, and each of A19/A20 precedes A27 whenever it occurs. - The gap from A42 to A27 shortened from typically 3 event positions (about 82 in 100 traces) to typically 2 (about 80 in 100), consistent with one activity now sitting between them instead of two. [LAG001]
- A42 and A27 themselves continue to appear in every trace in both windows. [LAG001, DIR005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The volume of evidence alone clears the chance bar: 28 items qualified against roughly 0.57 expected if nothing had changed, and rather than scattered anomalies they converge on a single, directly observed difference. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, LAG001]

One possibility supported by the evidence is that, in the later window, the segment between A42 and A27 is executed with a single activity instead of the two that previously always ran there: earlier, every trace performed both A25 and A26 once, in either order, between A42 and A27; in the later window, about half of the traces perform A19 in that slot and about half perform A20, never both, with A42 still before and A27 still directly after. The support is direct and relational, not just a participation count: A19 and A20 occupy the same relative position A25 and A26 held, A42 reroutes from A25/A26 to A19/A20, A19/A20 lead to A27 in about 95 in 100 of their outgoing transitions, and the A42→A27 distance shortens from typically 3 event positions to typically 2 — exactly what one activity between them instead of two would produce. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR005, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, LAG001]

Limitations: the earlier behaviour has not vanished entirely — about 3 in 200 later traces still contain A25 or A26 — so the evidence cannot distinguish a wholesale modification from a shift in how often two coexisting execution modes occur. Sampling differences in variant frequencies are a competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude: A19 and A20 were simply not observed earlier (0 of 200 traces), so a previously very rare variant surfacing later is conceivable, although a behaviour present in roughly half of the later traces would hardly go unseen in a 200-trace sample. Details of the residual A25/A26 behaviour in the later window rest on only 3 traces each and are therefore weak. [OCC003, OCC004, DIR003, DIR004, POS003, POS004, REL010]
