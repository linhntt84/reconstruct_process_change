### Observed differences

- Repetition of A30, A31, A36 and A37 largely stopped. Before, each of the four activities appeared about 3.3 times per trace on average and occurred more than once in about 67 in 100 traces, while being present in every trace; in the later window each appears about once per trace, repeats are seen in roughly 0–2 in 100 traces, and participation is still 100 in 100 traces. [OCC001-004]
- Routing out of A37 changed: before, about 68 in 100 of A37's outgoing transitions led to A30 and about 29 in 100 to A25; in the later window about 97 in 100 lead to A25, and a transition from A37 to A30 is no longer observed. The observed outgoing transitions of A37 fell from about 670 to 200, matching the drop from about 3.4 to 1 occurrence per trace. [DIR001, OCC004]
- The order among the four activities became largely fixed. Before, each pair of them (for example A30 and A31, or A36 and A37) was interleaved or repeated in about 67 in 100 traces; in the later window interleaving is nearly gone (0–2 in 100), A30 precedes A31, A36 and A37 in about 98–99 in 100 traces, A36 precedes A37 in all traces, and A31 precedes A37 in about 995 in 1000. The remaining open relation is A31 versus A36: each trace now has one in a definite order, but across traces A31 comes first in about 56 in 100 and A36 first in about 44 in 100. [REL001-006]
- Distances between these activities collapsed: pairs such as A30→A31, A30→A36, A31→A36, A36→A31, A31→A37 and A36→A37, previously spread over one to five events apart, now occur one or two events apart in the large majority of cases (A31→A36 within one event in about 98 in 100 of the 112 traces showing that pair). Backward pairs (A31→A30, A36→A30, A37→A30, A37→A31) and self-repeats of A30 or A31, previously seen in about 134–161 traces, each remain in only one later-window trace. [LAG001-012]
- The four activities also occur at more consistent positions within the trace: the spread of their relative positions fell from about 0.13 to about 0.05–0.06, with mean positions broadly unchanged (about 0.40–0.50 of the trace). [POS001-004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the group of activities A30, A31, A36 and A37, previously executed about three times per trace in a varying, interleaved order with A37 typically leading back to A30, is now executed once per trace in a largely fixed sequence: A30 first, then A31 and A36 adjacently in either order, then A37, which continues to A25 instead of returning to A30. [OCC001-004, DIR001, REL001-006]

The change is measured directly rather than inferred indirectly: occurrence profiles show the repetition drop for each activity, relation profiles show the order change for each pair, and the direction profile shows the routing change on A37's outgoing transitions. The items are also internally consistent — A37's transition counts (about 670 before, 200 after) match its occurrence means, and the split order of A31 and A36 matches the one-step lag counts of about 112 and 89 traces. [OCC001-004, DIR001, REL001-006, LAG006, LAG009]

The volume of evidence (27 qualifying items against roughly 0.56 expected for an unchanged process) cannot be explained by chance as a whole, and every item's difference is larger than those seen in 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001-004, DIR001, POS001-004, REL001-006, LAG001-012]

Limitations remain: the four activities move together, so the items largely describe one underlying trace behaviour rather than independent confirmations; the later-window profiles for self-repeats and backward pairs rest on a single trace each, so a small residual share of traces that still repeat these activities cannot be excluded; and although sampling differences in variant frequencies between two 200-trace windows are an unlikely explanation for a pattern with 100% participation in both windows and effects this large, they leave exact later-window shares uncertain — for example the 56-vs-44 order split between A31 and A36, and whether the A37-to-A30 transition is truly gone or merely unobserved in this sample. [LAG001, LAG004, LAG005, LAG008, LAG011, LAG012, REL004, DIR001]
