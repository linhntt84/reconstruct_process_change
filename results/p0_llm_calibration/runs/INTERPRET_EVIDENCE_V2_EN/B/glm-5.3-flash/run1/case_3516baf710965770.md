### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, roughly 40 in 100 executions omit A19 and A20, with A42 then followed directly by A27 instead of by A19. The signs are direct and mutually consistent: a new A42→A27 transition carries about 40% of A42's outgoing transitions; the A42→A27 gap is 1 step in about 40 in 100 later traces against 4–5 steps almost always earlier; A19 and A20 fall from universal presence to about 59 in 100 traces and are always omitted together; A27 appears without A19 in about 42 in 100 later traces, while the A42-before-A19 and A19-before-A27 orders are preserved whenever A19 still runs. The 20 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.61 expected by chance when nothing has changed, and each difference exceeds about 995 in 1000 random re-splits; the many relation items restate one underlying pattern rather than adding independent confirmations. [DIR002, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, REL001, REL004, REL005]

Limitations: each window is a finite 200-trace sample; A19 appears in all 200 earlier traces, so the omitting behaviour was rare or absent there, but this evidence cannot separate a routing modification from a shift in the mix of case variants, some of which may legitimately not require A19 and A20. Minor new targets of A25's outgoing transitions rest on about one transition each and carry little weight. [OCC001, DIR001]
