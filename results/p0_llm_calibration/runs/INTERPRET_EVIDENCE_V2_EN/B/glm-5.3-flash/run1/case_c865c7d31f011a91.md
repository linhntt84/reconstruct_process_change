### Observed differences

- Participation became universal. In the earlier window, A22 ran in about 46 of 100 traces (93 of 200) and each of A31, A32, A37 and A38 in about 54 of 100 (107 of 200); in the later window all of these, like A20, A21, A23 and A26, run in every trace (200 of 200). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

- An either-or routing disappeared. Earlier, A22 and the four-activity block A31/A32/A37/A38 never occurred in the same trace (co-occurrence 0; A22-only about 46 in 100, block-only about 54 in 100), and A21 led to A22 in about 47 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and to one of the four block activities otherwise; the A21-to-A26 distance was 2 steps in about 46 of 100 traces (via A22) and 5 steps in about 54 of 100 (via the block). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the exclusive branch between an A22 path and an A31/A32/A37/A38 path was replaced by a single flow in which the four block activities run, in varying order, directly after A20 and before A23, while A22 always runs after A21 with A26 after it. About 52 evidence items qualify against roughly 0.6 expected for an unchanged process, and the change is shown directly rather than through frequencies alone: co-occurrence of A22 with each block activity went from 0 of 200 traces to 200 of 200, the order relations flipped completely (A21 and A23 preceded the block in about 54 of 100 traces earlier; the block precedes both in all traces later), A20's successor changed from A23 alone to the four block activities, and the A20-to-A23 distance moved from 1 to 5 steps; each item's difference also exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data. [REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001]

Limitations: each window is a 200-trace sample, and many items describe the same underlying trace behaviour (for example, the four A22–block pair items and the A22 occurrence item all reflect A22 becoming universal), so they are not independent confirmations, and a high empirical strength alone cannot separate a real change from a shift in variant frequencies. The competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is therefore a change in the mix of process variants rather than one routing change, since with samples of this size a rare variant can appear in only one window; here, however, the earlier window shows two mutually exclusive variant families at about 46 and 54 in 100 traces with zero combination, while the later window shows only the combined flow in all 200 traces, which re-drawing 200 traces from a stable mix cannot plausibly produce. On this evidence, a restructuring of the routing among A20, A21, A22, A23, A26, A31, A32, A37 and A38 is the possibility best supported.
