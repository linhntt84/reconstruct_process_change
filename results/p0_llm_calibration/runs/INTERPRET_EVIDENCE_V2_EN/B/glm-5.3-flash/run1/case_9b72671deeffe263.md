### Observed differences

- Before, the stretch from A24 to A25 ran in one fixed order: A24 handed on only to A16 (about 50 in 100 of its observed outgoing transitions) or A17 (about 50 in 100); A16 led only to A18 or A19 and A17 mainly to A19 (about 61 in 100) or A18 (about 39 in 100); A18 and A19 led exclusively to A25, always one event later. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG003, LAG004]
- In the later window, A24's observed outgoing transitions spread over four targets: A18 (about 28 in 100), A19 (about 26), A16 (about 26) and A17 (about 21); A18 and A19 were not observed as successors of A24 before. [DIR005]
- In the later window, A18 and A19 no longer lead only to A25: about half of their observed outgoing transitions still go to A25, while the rest go to A16 (about 22–36 in 100) or A17 (about 22–28 in 100). [DIR003, DIR004]
- In the later window, A16 and A17 lead directly to A25 in about half of their observed outgoing transitions (about 52 and 55 in 100), a successor pair not observed before, while their links onward to A18/A19 shrank. [DIR001, DIR002]
- Event distances moved in the matching direction: A24 to A18/A19 is now one event apart in about half the cases (always two before), A24 to A16/A17 two events apart in about half (always one before), A16/A17 to A25 one event apart in about half (always two before), and A18/A19 to A25 two events apart in about half (always one before). [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]
- The relative order of A17 and A19 changed: A17 before A19 fell from about 30 to about 9 in 100 traces containing both, while A19 before A17, not seen before, now occurs in about 10 in 100; the share of traces containing both fell from about 30% to about 19%. [REL001]
- A17's typical event position shifted slightly later (mean from about 0.63 to 0.66 of the trace) and it appears in slightly fewer traces (about 50% versus 47%). [POS001]
- No activity appeared or disappeared; the routing changes occur at similar participation levels (for example A24 is present in all 200 traces in both windows, the other sources in roughly 90–110 traces per window). Overall, the later window looks like roughly half of the executions running the stretch between A24 and A25 in the opposite order — A24, then A18 or A19, then A16 or A17, then A25 — while the original order continues in the other half. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, REL001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the fixed sequence between A24 and A25 gave way to mixed orders: about half of the executions run the two activity pairs in reverse — A24 → A18/A19 → A16/A17 → A25 instead of A24 → A16/A17 → A18/A19 → A25 — so that A25 now often directly follows A16 or A17, and A18 or A19 now often precede them. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, REL001]

The support is direct and internally consistent rather than a matter of item count: the successor changes at all five sources are exactly those the reversal predicts, all eight distance changes shift by exactly the one event the reversal implies and each affects roughly half the cases, and the A17/A19 pair order flips in the same direction; fifteen qualifying items against about 0.36 expected by chance among 72 tested units also makes the pattern as a whole unlikely for an unchanged process, and every item's difference exceeds those seen in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, REL001, POS001]

Limitations: the windows are finite samples, so a rare reversed variant that already existed before could in principle have surfaced only now — the before window shows essentially none of this behaviour across roughly 90–150 observations per indicator, which makes pure sampling fluctuation implausible but does not exclude a low-frequency variant rising. The aggregate transition and distance shares also cannot show whether the later behaviour is one coherent new variant or a mixture (for example, executions where A16/A17 skip A18/A19 entirely would look similar), and pair distances are only observed within five steps. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG002, LAG005, LAG006, REL001]
