### Observed differences

- In the later window, A31 and A32 each appear in about 53 in 100 traces, up from 40 in 100 before (105 vs 80 of 200 traces); neither activity repeats within a trace in either window. [OCC001, OCC002]
- In the later window, A33 and A34 each appear in about 48 in 100 traces, down from 60 in 100 before (95 vs 120 of 200 traces), again with no within-trace repetition. [OCC003, OCC004]
- All four participation changes have the same size, 12.5 percentage points, in opposite directions, and each difference is larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- For the activity pair A16 followed by A27 within five steps, immediately consecutive occurrences (distance 1) rose from about 41 in 100 observations to about 59 in 100, while distance-2 occurrences fell from about 59 to about 41; the number of pair observations also grew from 88 to 109. [LAG001]
- The identical values for the two increased and the two decreased activities suggest each pair reflects the same underlying traces rather than two independent changes. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests that in the later window roughly one case in eight shifted from paths containing A33 and A34 to paths containing A31 and A32: participation in A31/A32 rose by 12.5 points while participation in A33/A34 fell by the same amount, and five qualifying items against fewer than one expected by chance (0.27 from 55 units tested) means the pattern as a whole is unlikely if nothing had changed. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, LAG001]

What is missing is any relational or routing evidence linking A31/A32 with A33/A34 within the same traces, so a genuine substitution — the same cases switching between the two groups of activities — is not established; the increases and decreases could be independent participation changes, and the paired items are not independent confirmations of one another. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

The A16→A27 lag redistribution is a separate behaviour involving different activities and cannot be tied to the participation shift; a redistribution across distances in event positions does not show that an activity was repositioned, and the growth in pair observations (88 to 109) could itself contribute to the shifted distance mix. [LAG001]

Sampling differences in variant frequencies between two 200-trace windows remain a competing explanation for the size and pairing of the participation changes, even though the number of qualifying items exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, LAG001]
