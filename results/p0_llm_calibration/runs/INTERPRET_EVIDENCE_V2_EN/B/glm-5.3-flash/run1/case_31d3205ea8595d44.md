### Observed differences

- Participation split into two mutually exclusive sets: before, A22 and each of A31, A32, A37 and A38 ran in essentially every trace; in the later window each runs in only about half the traces (A22 in 100 of 200, the others in about 101 of 200), and A22 co-occurs with any of the four in only about 1 in 200 traces, down from 200 in 200. The four still run together as a group: pairs among them co-occur in about 50 of 100 traces and are jointly absent in the rest, with one-without-the-other essentially unobserved. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL025, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030]

- Routing into A23 changed: A20's outgoing transitions previously went to the four block activities (roughly a quarter each) and A23 followed five events later; in the later window A20 is followed almost always by A23 (about 99.5% of its outgoing transitions) and that five-step gap became an immediate succession in about 995 of 1,000 cases. [DIR001, LAG001]

- A21's follow-up split: A21 previously always led to A22; in the later window about half of its outgoing transitions still go to A22 and the other half go to one of A31, A32, A37, A38. [DIR002]

- The block's placement changed: before, A31/A32/A37/A38 ran before A23 and before A21 in essentially every trace; in the later window A23 and A21 precede them in all of their roughly 50-in-100 co-occurrences. Edges from the block into A23 are no longer observed (down to about 1 in 100 of A38's outgoing transitions), while new edges from each block activity into A26 appear at roughly 22–28%. The distance from A21 to A26 is two events in about half the traces and five events in the other half (previously always two), consistent with A21→A22→A26 in one group of traces and A21→(four block activities)→A26 in the other. - Event positions moved accordingly: A23 now occurs earlier in the case (mean relative position about 0.47 before vs 0.38 after), A21 earlier (0.61 vs 0.54), and the four block activities later (about 0.40 vs 0.58); A22, when present, is slightly earlier (0.64 vs 0.59). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in which the segment A31–A32–A37–A38 now runs after A21 as an alternative to A22: before, a trace ran A20 → (the four block activities in a variable order) → A23 → A21 → A22 → A26, while in the later window about half the traces run A20 → A23 → A21 → A22 → A26 with the block skipped, and the other half run A20 → A23 → A21 → (block) → A26 with A22 skipped. [REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG006]

This judgement rests on direct relational and routing evidence rather than frequency shifts alone: A22 and each block activity went from always co-occurring to almost never (about 0.5%) while each still occurs in about half the traces; the block's order relative to A23 and A21 flipped from block-first in essentially every trace to block-last in every co-occurrence; the A20→A23 relation changed from five steps to one; and block→A26 edges appeared while block→A23 edges disappeared. About 60 qualifying items against roughly 0.62 expected without change is far beyond what a stable process produces, and the participation, routing, order and position indicators all describe the same two-variant split rather than unrelated fluctuations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL007, REL017, LAG001, DIR003, DIR006]

Limitations: the exact 50/50 split and the residual co-occurrence of about 1 in 200 traces are sample-level figures that could differ in another sample, and a few lag items on the block's internal distances (e.g., A20→A32, A31→A23) rest on a single after-window observation and were not relied on. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot fully exclude is a shift in the frequencies of pre-existing variants — the later window could be mixing variants that already existed — but the before window shows one homogeneous pattern (100% participation and a fixed order in every trace), so pure re-mixing of variants would not explain the order flip of the block relative to A23 and A21. [REL013, REL017, LAG002, LAG007, OCC001]
