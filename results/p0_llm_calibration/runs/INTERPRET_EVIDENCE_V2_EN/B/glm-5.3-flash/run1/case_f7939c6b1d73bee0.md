### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two parts of the process were rewired. First, the earlier chain in which A45 handed to A02 and A02 almost always passed on to A46 — with A02 present in about 65 in 100 traces near the trace end — is largely replaced in the later window by a direct A45→A46 handover, while A02 becomes much rarer (about 20 in 100 traces), sits mid-trace, and exits to a wide set of activities instead of almost only A46. Second, in the A21–A24/A29/A30 segment, A30 is now reached directly from A21 or A22 in about half of their outgoing transitions, whereas before it was reached almost only through A23 or A24, which in turn now often pass back to A21/A22; A06 and A10 also participate in about three times as many traces and feed toward A15 and A46. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR011, LAG003, LAG005, LAG006, LAG013, POS001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, DIR003]

The support is direct and consistent: the rerouting appears in successor shares computed on well-observed transitions (A45 with about 200 outgoing transitions per window; A21, A22, A23, A24 and A29 with about 90–110 each), in adjacent-pair lags that match it (A45→A46 adjacent in about 85% of later cases; A21/A22→A30 adjacent in about half), and in the A02 participation drop backed by 130 versus 39 traces. With 45 qualifying items against roughly 0.73 expected by chance in this screening, the set of differences is far larger than an unchanged process typically produces, and each item's difference exceeds what random re-splits of the pooled traces produce (strength about 99.5 in 100). [DIR011, LAG013, DIR005, DIR006, LAG005, LAG006, OCC001, DIR001]

Limitations: the two windows are finite samples of 200 traces each, so variant frequencies can differ between them without any change, and many items restate one underlying behaviour — the ten A02 relation items largely re-express A02's participation drop, so the breadth of the evidence overstates its independence. A few items rest on few observations (the A02→A15, A02→A45 and A14→A02 lags with about 1–11 observations per window, the after-window side of the A02→A46 lag with 16, and the A04/A10 routing with about 12–20 before-window transitions) and would not stand alone; an unobserved transition, such as A04→A06 in the later window, does not show that the edge is absent from the process. [REL001, REL002, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, DIR002, DIR003]
