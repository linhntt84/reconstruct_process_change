### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that control flow changed in two places. Around the A21/A22/A23/A24 fragment, A29 now enters almost only via A21 or A22; A21 and A22 no longer hand over directly to A30 but pass on to A23 or A24; and A23 and A24 lead almost exclusively and immediately to A30 — so A30, previously reached directly from each of the four activities about half the time, is now reached almost only through A23/A24. In addition, A08 is executed in far fewer cases (about 10 in 100 traces versus about 33 in 100) and, when executed, tends to sit later in the trace and no longer leads predominantly to A16. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG005, LAG006, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013, LAG014, OCC001, POS001, DIR001]

The support is direct and consistent: the routing reading comes from the successor shares of the activities themselves, each backed by roughly 90–115 outgoing transitions per window (A29: 200 in each), and the step-distance items reproduce the new wiring in detail, with new direct handovers at one step and detoured paths at two. The A08 decline is corroborated by occurrence, relation and direction items alike, and the 30 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.65 expected for an unchanged process, so chance as a whole cannot explain the pattern. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG005, LAG006, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013, LAG014, OCC001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR002]

The evidence cannot rule out that the two windows simply sampled different mixes of process variants, which would shift aggregate routing and participation frequencies without any modification to the process. Details of A08's new surroundings are also uncertain: its after-side transition and position profiles rest on about 20 traces, the A08→A16 and A16→A08 step distances on 7 and 4 observations, and the A23→A21 and A24→A21 distance changes on only 2–3; several items further describe the same underlying behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [DIR001, POS001, LAG001, LAG003, LAG007, LAG009]
