### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative route which replaces A22 with a block of A27, A28, A33 and A34 between A21 and A23 is now rarely executed: in the earlier window roughly 39 in 100 traces left A21 into this four-activity block before reaching A23, whereas in the later window about 99 in 100 traces run A21 → A22 → A23 and the block appears in only about 1 in 100 traces. [OCC002, DIR002, REL001, REL006, LAG001, REL015]

Supporting signs: 43 evidence items qualified, many times the roughly 0.52 expected by chance across the 105 tested units, and every item's difference exceeds about 99.5% of random re-splits of the pooled traces. Participation, routing, co-occurrence and order indicators all move consistently and complementarily — A22's rise from about 61 to about 99 in 100 traces mirrors the block's fall from about 39 to about 1 — a combination that sampling variation alone is unlikely to produce.
