### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the path chosen after A20 flipped between two existing branches: in the later window, most cases leave A20 towards A24 and then run A21–A24 (A24 first, with A21–A23 in variable order), whereas before, most cases left A20 towards A28 and ran A25–A28. Both branches already executed in both windows; what changed is which one carries the majority, shifting from roughly 79/21 to roughly 23/77 between the two groups. [OCC001, OCC005, DIR001, REL001, REL005, REL011, REL035]

The support is direct rather than purely frequency-based: the successor shares of A20 reversed, with A28 falling from about 78% to about 23% of A20's outgoing transitions while A24 rose from about 21% to about 77%. [DIR001]

A20's precedence ties switched from A25–A28 to A21–A24, and the participation of all eight activities moved consistently with this rerouting. [REL001, REL005, OCC001, OCC005]

Fifty-three items qualified in total, many times the roughly one (0.8) expected by chance in an unchanged process, and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data. Limitations: the many relational items largely restate one underlying swap and are not independent confirmations; the windows are finite samples, so differing variant frequencies alone could produce some of this, although a coordinated reversal across eight activities plus a reversed successor profile is hard to attribute to sampling.
