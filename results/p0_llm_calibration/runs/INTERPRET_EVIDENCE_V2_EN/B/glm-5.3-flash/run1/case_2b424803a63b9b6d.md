### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports, as a possibility, that the late part of the flow changed so that A45 now reaches A46 via A02: A02 is executed in about 65 in 100 cases, sits near the end of the trace, is typically preceded one step earlier by A45 and followed one step later by A46, and the formerly mostly immediate A45-to-A46 handover now spans two steps — a relational and positional pattern consistent with A02 now sitting between the two [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR011, REL009, REL010, LAG002, LAG012, LAG013]. The evidence also supports a second, separate shift in which the A21/A22/A23/A24/A29/A30 block changed from a branch point (A29 feeding four activities, each reaching A30 directly) into the fixed sequence A29 → A21/A22 → A23/A24 → A30 [DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, LAG003, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011, REL011]. A06, by contrast, is now executed in only about 6 in 100 cases, down from about 20 in 100 [OCC002].

The support rests on 39 qualifying evidence items against roughly 0.7 expected for an unchanged process at these thresholds, and on participation, routing, positional and ordering indicators converging on the same traces [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR011, REL009, REL010, LAG002, LAG012, LAG013]. Note that the many A02 relation items largely restate one underlying shift in A02's behaviour rather than independent confirmations [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010].

The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is sampling: with 200 traces per window, a shift in the frequency of whole variants — for example a variant containing the late A45–A02–A46 segment simply becoming more common without any change to the process structure — would produce the same participation and ordering differences. Some details also rest on very few observations, such as A06's rerouting (13 later transitions), the A02–A15 gap (5 later observations) and the A24-to-A21 gap (1 later observation) [DIR002, LAG001, LAG006].
