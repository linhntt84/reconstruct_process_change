### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was rerouted: the alternative block A21–A24, which in the earlier window typically ran after A19/A20 behind the segment through A04, was largely set aside in favour of the block A25–A28, and the A04 segment was largely bypassed, with A31 now typically continuing directly to A19 and A20 and onward to A25–A28. In the later window A21–A24 appear in about 28 in 100 traces (down from 83), A25–A28 in about 73 (up from 18), and A04 in about 16 (down from 55). [DIR003, DIR004, REL017, OCC004, OCC008, OCC002]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor shares at A20 and A31 shift exactly where the participation swings predict, and the relational evidence places A19/A20 and A31/A32 before A25–A28 in the large majority of later traces. [DIR003, DIR004, REL017, REL062] In total 99 evidence items qualified, many times the roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process at these thresholds, and each item's difference exceeded about 199 of 200 random re-splits of the pooled data; note that the many relation items describe overlapping pairs of the same underlying behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR001, POS001, REL001, LAG001]

Limitations: with 200 traces per window, a difference in the mix of case variants between the windows could produce the same pattern without any change to the process, and the evidence cannot rule this out. The old path is used less often rather than shown removed — A21–A24 still appear in about 28 in 100 later traces and A04 in about 16 — and the small rises for A02 and A08 and the rerouting at A06 rest on few observations. [OCC004, OCC002, OCC001, DIR002]
