### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the block around A21, A22, A23, A24 and A29 is rerouted — A21 and A22 frequently proceed directly to A30, A23 and A24 frequently hand over to A21/A22 before A30, and A29 frequently enters via A23/A24 — and that A06 takes a much larger, later role, typically reached from A43 (and after A29/A30) and followed immediately by A44, while A04 appears in fewer traces. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, OCC001, OCC002, LAG001, LAG012, REL004]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor-share shifts are measured on roughly 90–110 outgoing transitions per window (about 200 for A29), the lag distributions move in the direction the rerouting predicts, and the relation items agree (A22-before-A24 down from about 30 to 8 in 100; A06-before-A44 up from about 5 to 24 in 100); the item count (29 versus about 0.64 expected without change) cannot be explained by chance as a whole. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG002, LAG004, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, REL004, REL005]

Limitations remain: with 200 traces per window, variant frequencies can differ between samples, and several component profiles rest on few observations (A04 on 8 later traces; the before-profiles of the A06–A44, A43–A06 and A23–A22 lags on 6, 9 and 1 observations), so those details in particular could be sample-specific rare variants; DIR shares describe outgoing transitions rather than cases; and many items re-express the same trace behaviour (for example DIR004/DIR005, LAG006/LAG007 and REL005 all reflect the A23/A24 rerouting), so they are not independent confirmations.
