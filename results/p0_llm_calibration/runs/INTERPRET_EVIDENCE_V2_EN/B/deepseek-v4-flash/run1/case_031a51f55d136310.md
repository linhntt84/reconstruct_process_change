### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A08 is largely skipped or moved later, and the routing through A21/A22/A23/A24/A29/A30 becomes more sequential: A29 → A21/A22 → A24/A23 → A30, rather than the earlier pattern where A21/A22/A23/A24 could go directly to A30 and A08 often preceded A16, A29, or A30. This is supported by the consistent successor changes (DIR003–DIR007), the lag shifts (LAG005–LAG014), and the drop in A08 co-occurrence and position shift (OCC001, POS001, REL001–REL006). A competing explanation—that the differences arise from sampling variation in variant frequencies—is unlikely given the large number of consistent items (30 items vs 0.65 expected by chance), though some individual lag items have small support (e.g., LAG003, LAG007, LAG009) and the exact trace order cannot be fully reconstructed from these indicators.
