### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured: a segment that used to run through A20, A28, A29 and A30 was replaced or bypassed by a segment in which A04 is executed earlier and more often, is followed by A16, and new activities A19, A21, A22 and A23 lead into A31. This is supported by consistent participation, routing, relational and lag changes across many indicators. The main competing explanation is that the two windows sample different variants of a stable multi-variant process; the evidence cannot fully rule that out, but the scale and consistency of the changes make a structural change the more direct interpretation.
