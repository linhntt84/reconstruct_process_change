### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19 and A20 appear in about 58–59% of traces instead of 100%, with mean occurrences per trace dropping from about 1.0 to about 0.59. [OCC001, OCC002]
- A25 and A26 also appear less often: A25 from about 48% to 29.5% of traces, A26 from about 52.5% to 30%. [OCC003, OCC004]

For example, A19–A27 co-occurrence fell from 100% to about 58.5%, and A27 alone rose from 0% to about 41.5%. [REL001, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a subset of cases (about 40%) follow a path where A42 is followed by A27 early, while A19 and A20 are skipped; the remaining cases retain the previous pattern with A42 leading to A19 and A27 appearing later. This is directly shown by the change in A42's successor distribution [DIR002], the shift in the A42–A27 lag [LAG001], the drop in A19/A20 occurrence [OCC001, OCC002], and the co-occurrence patterns where A27 appears without A19 or A20 [REL004, REL005, REL008]. The number of consistent indicators (20 items versus 0.61 expected by chance) makes random sampling variation an unlikely explanation. A limitation is that the exact proportion of cases taking the new path is approximate, and the evidence does not establish whether this is a permanent structural change or a temporary variant.
