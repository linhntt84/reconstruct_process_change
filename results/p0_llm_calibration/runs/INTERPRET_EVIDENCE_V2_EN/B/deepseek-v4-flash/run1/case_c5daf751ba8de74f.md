### Observed differences

- In the later window, activity A04 appears in about 30% of traces, up from about 12%, and its mean position shifts later in the trace (from about 0.64 to 0.88). [OCC001, POS001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the later window reflects a reordering of a central part of the process: A35, A36, A41 and A42 now occur before A26 and A27 instead of after them, A25 now routes into that block rather than to A27, A26 now routes to A30, and A04 becomes more frequent and appears later, often after that block. The supporting signs are direct and consistent: relational order reversals for multiple activity pairs, successor changes for A25, A26, A35, A36, A41 and A42, and matching positional shifts for A26, A27, A35, A36, A41 and A42.
