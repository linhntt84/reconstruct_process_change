### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new branch or alternative path involving activities A32, A33, A38 and A39 was introduced in the later window, and that the path through A27 (and to a lesser extent A04) is used less often. A26 now routes to the new activities instead of almost always to A27, and A04's dominant successor A48 is much less frequent. The appearance of these activities from zero participation, the drop in A27 and A04 participation, and the routing changes are consistent and direct. A competing explanation is that the new activities existed but were simply not sampled in the earlier window; however, zero occurrences in 200 traces makes that unlikely. Sampling differences in variant frequencies could also produce some of the observed shifts, but the number of qualifying evidence items (86 vs 0.86 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation implausible.
