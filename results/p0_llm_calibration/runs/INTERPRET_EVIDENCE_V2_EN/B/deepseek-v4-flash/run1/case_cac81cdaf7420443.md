### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the routing after A15: in the later window, A15 is followed by A16/A17 instead of A18/A19. This is directly shown by the successor distribution of A15 (DIR001) and by the relational order of A15 with A16/A17 versus A18/A19 (REL001-004). The participation changes (OCC001-004) are consistent with this shift. The number of evidence items (19) far exceeds the 0.32 expected by chance under a stable process, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artifact. A competing explanation that the two windows simply differ in variant frequencies is possible, but the consistency and magnitude of the change make it less plausible. The evidence does not indicate why the routing changed, only that it did.
