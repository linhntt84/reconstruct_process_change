### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, activities A19 and A20 became a nearly universal early step in the process, executed before A25, A26, A27, and A47, while A42's routing shifted from A27 to A19, pushing A27 later in the trace. This is directly shown by relational and positional evidence: A19 and A20 precede the other activities in almost all cases, and the A42→A27 lag increases. The number of qualifying evidence items (28) far exceeds the 0.69 expected by chance, and the items are consistent across occurrence, routing, relation, and position indicators. A competing explanation—that these differences arise from sampling variation in variant frequencies—is unlikely given the volume and consistency of the evidence, though it cannot be entirely ruled out.
