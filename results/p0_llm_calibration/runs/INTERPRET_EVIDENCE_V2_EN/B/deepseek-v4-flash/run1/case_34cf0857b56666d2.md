### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the process no longer executes activities A32, A33, A38 and A39, and instead executes A27 in nearly every case, with A26 now almost always leading to A27. This is directly shown by the large participation drops for those four activities, the near-universal presence of A27, and the routing change at A26. The relational evidence is consistent: A27 co-occurs with A26 and A28 almost always, while the disappeared activities no longer co-occur with A19 or A26. A secondary change is a modest shift in A42's successors from A19 toward A20. The main limitation is that the later window contains only a handful of traces with A32, A33, A38 or A39, so their residual presence is uncertain, but the magnitude and consistency across many indicators make a sampling artefact unlikely.
