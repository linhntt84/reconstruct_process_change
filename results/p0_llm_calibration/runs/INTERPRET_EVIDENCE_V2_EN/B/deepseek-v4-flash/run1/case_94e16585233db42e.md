### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed structurally: a branch or segment involving A06, A19 and A20 was largely removed or replaced, while A02, A04, A08 and A10 were added or became much more frequent, and the routing around A40, A45 and A06 shifted accordingly. The changes are consistent across occurrence, routing, relation and lag indicators, and the magnitude (e.g., A19 from 55% to 0.5% participation) is far beyond what random sampling of a stable process would typically produce. A competing explanation that the before window simply contained a distinct variant that disappeared cannot be fully excluded, but the consistency and scale make that unlikely. The A06–A46 lag change rests on only 12 after-window traces, so the exact ordering details for that pair are weaker than the overall pattern.
