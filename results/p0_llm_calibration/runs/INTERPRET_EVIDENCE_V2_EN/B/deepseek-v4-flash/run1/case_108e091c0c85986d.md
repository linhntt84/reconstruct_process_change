### Observed differences

- In the later window, A32, A33 and A34 are present in all 200 traces (100%) and occur on average about once per trace, whereas before they appeared in about half the traces (48–52%) with mean occurrences around 0.5. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- A25's successor changes: before, about 99% of its outgoing transitions went to A26; after, about 49% go to A34 and 49% to A33, with A26 no longer a successor. [DIR001]
- A28's successor concentrates on A32: before about 52% to A32, 21% to A33, 26% to A34; after about 97.5% to A32. [DIR002]
- A33 and A34 now lead to A26 (about 48–50%) and to each other (about 48–50%), whereas before they led mainly to A29 and to each other. [DIR003, DIR004]
- Positions shift: A33 and A34 occur earlier (mean position down by about 0.09–0.10 of trace length), while A26, A28 and A29 occur later (mean up by about 0.04–0.05); A32 also occurs slightly later. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Relational order changes: A25 precedes A33/A34 in about 98–99% of traces after vs about 48% before; A33/A34 precede A26/A28 in about 97–99% after vs essentially never before (before A26/A28 preceded them). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]
- Lags confirm: A25 to A33/A34 drops from 4–5 steps to 1–2; A25 to A26 increases from 1 to 3; A25 to A28 increases from 3 to 5. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural reordering and participation change: in the later window, A33 and A34 (and A32) become mandatory, and A33/A34 are placed immediately after A25, before A26 and A28, while A25 no longer routes to A26. This is directly shown by the successor changes (A25→A33/A34), the relational reversals (A33/A34 before A26/A28), and the lag shifts. The large number of qualifying items (40 vs 0.65 expected) makes chance an unlikely explanation. A competing explanation of mere sampling variation in variant frequencies is not ruled out with certainty, but the consistency across OCC, DIR, REL, LAG and POS makes it weak. Some LAG items for A26/A28 with A33/A34 rest on only one after-trace, but they are corroborated by REL and DIR.
