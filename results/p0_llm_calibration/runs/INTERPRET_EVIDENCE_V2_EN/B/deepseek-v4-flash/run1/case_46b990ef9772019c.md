### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed from a variant where a block of activities (A33–A41) was executed in about half the cases, split into two alternative sub-blocks (A33–A37 vs A38–A41), to a variant where all these activities are executed in every case, sequentially with A33–A37 before A38–A41. This is directly supported by the large increases in participation and co-occurrence, the consistent ordering relations (A30 and A31 before the block, A33–A37 before A38–A41), the position shifts, and the lag changes. The number of qualifying evidence items (83) far exceeds the expected count without change (1.0), so the overall pattern is not attributable to chance. A competing explanation—that the before window simply sampled two distinct variants of a stable process—cannot be fully ruled out, but the scale and consistency of the relational and positional evidence make that unlikely.
