### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed so that A37 now routes to A14 instead of A22, and A14/A15 are executed in nearly all cases, with A20/A21 as a mutually exclusive branch and A22 occurring later. The DIR shift, the large increases in A14/A15 participation, the consistent ordering relations, and the LAG change all point to the same underlying difference. The number of qualifying evidence items (22) far exceeds the 0.39 expected by chance under no change. A competing explanation—that the windows simply contain different mixes of case types—cannot be fully ruled out, but the consistency across indicators makes that less likely.
