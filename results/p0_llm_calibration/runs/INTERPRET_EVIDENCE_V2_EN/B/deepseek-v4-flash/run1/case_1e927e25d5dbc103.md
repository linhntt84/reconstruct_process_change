### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which activity A10 is executed much less often, and the relative order of two groups of activities is reversed: a group containing A26 and A27 now occurs before a group containing A35, A36, A41 and A42, whereas before it occurred after. This is accompanied by A25 now routing to A27 instead of to the latter group, and A26 now routing to that group instead of to A30. The number of evidence items (46) far exceeds the 0.83 expected by chance, and the pattern is consistent across occurrence, routing, position, relation and lag indicators. Limitations: some lag items have very low support in one window (e.g., LAG003-005, LAG009), and the A10-related changes rest on only 15 after traces; sampling differences in variant frequencies cannot be fully excluded, but the overall consistency makes a real change the more plausible explanation.
