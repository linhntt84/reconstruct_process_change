### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the previously universal A26→A27→A28 sequence is partly replaced by a variant that goes through A32/A33 before A28, and the A43→A04→A44 branch is largely replaced by direct A43→A44. The signs are consistent: A27 participation halves while A32/A33 appear in the same ~55% of traces; A26's successor distribution shifts from A27 to A33/A32; A28 remains universal; the A26-to-A28 lag moves from 2 to 3 steps; and A43's successor share to A04 drops sharply while A44 increases. The main limitation is that the order between A32 and A33 is not fixed (both A32-before-A33 and A33-before-A32 occur), and the A04-related lag evidence after the change rests on few traces, so the exact internal order of the new branch is less certain. The evidence cannot rule out that the new branch has two order variants rather than one fixed sequence.
