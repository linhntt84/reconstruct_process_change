### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a single path in which A25 was followed by all four activities A29, A30, A31, and A32, and then by A26, to a process with a choice between two alternative branches: one containing A29 and A30, the other containing A31 and A32, both followed by A26. This is directly supported by the near-total mutual exclusivity between the two pairs, the new outgoing edges from A25 to A31/A32 and from A29/A30 to A26, and the corresponding shortening of lags between these activities. The evidence does not reveal the condition or rule that selects the branch, and the order within each pair remains variable, but sampling differences in variant frequencies are unlikely to explain the consistent disappearance of co-occurrence between the two pairs.
