### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a path through A19 or A20 between A40 and A25 is executed in about half the cases, replacing the direct A40→A25 edge. The order evidence directly shows A40 before A19/A20 and A19/A20 before A25, and the routing evidence shows A40's outgoing transitions moving to A19/A20, which then lead to A25. The number of evidence items (16) is far above the 0.48 expected by chance, and the indicators are consistent across occurrence, routing, order, and lag. A competing explanation that the increase of A19/A20 is merely a sampling artifact is unlikely given the coherent relational and positional changes, but the evidence cannot determine whether this is a newly introduced branch or a rerouting of an existing rare path, nor can it identify the cause of the change.
