### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process has been reordered: a group of activities (A20, A21, A23, A24) now occurs earlier, while another group (A08, A19, A22, A25, A26, A27, A31) occurs later, and the common successor of many activities changed from A27 to A32. Additionally, A08’s role expanded and its successor became predominantly A48. The supporting signs are numerous (88 evidence items vs. 0.94 expected by chance) and consistent across occurrence, successor, position, and order indicators. A competing explanation—that the differences reflect sampling variation in variant frequencies—is unlikely given the scale and coherence of the changes, though it cannot be fully excluded. Some individual lag items rest on very few observations (e.g., only one trace in one window), but the overall pattern is robust.
