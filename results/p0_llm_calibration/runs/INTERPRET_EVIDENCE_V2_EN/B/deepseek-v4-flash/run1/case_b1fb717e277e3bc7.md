### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, cases predominantly follow the path A21 → A22 → A23, whereas before they often branched through one of A27, A28, A33 or A34 before reaching A23. This is supported by the near‑doubling of A22 participation, the near‑disappearance of the four alternative activities, the concentration of A21's outgoing transitions on A22, and the A21–A23 lag being almost always 2 steps. The pattern is consistent across occurrence, transition, relation and lag indicators. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the after‑window sample happened to contain very few cases of the alternative branches; however, the large number of consistent indicators makes this unlikely. The rare appearances of A27, A28, A33 and A34 in the later window (about 1% of traces) are too few to characterise their positions or lags reliably.
