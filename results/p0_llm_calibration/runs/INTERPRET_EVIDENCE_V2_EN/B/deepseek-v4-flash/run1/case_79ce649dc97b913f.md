Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that, in the later window, the process executes a sequence A30 → A34/A35 → A36/A37 → A31 in nearly all cases, with A06 appearing earlier in about 29% of traces and being followed by A16. This is directly shown by the routing changes (A30 no longer goes to A36/A37, A34/A35 no longer go to A31), the co-occurrence and order relations (A30 before A34/A35/A36/A37, A31 after them, A34/A35 before A36/A37), and the lag distributions (A30–A31 now far apart, A30–A36/A37 later, A06–A16 immediate). The large number of consistent items across multiple indicators makes a chance explanation unlikely. A competing explanation based on sampling differences in variant frequencies cannot be fully excluded, but the coherence of the routing, order and lag evidence strongly favours a structural reordering. Some lag items rest on few observations (e.g., A06–A16 before window had only 5 supporting traces), so the exact placement of A06 relative to the main sequence is less certain.
