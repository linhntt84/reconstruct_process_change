### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the relative order of two groups of activities reversed: activities A14, A17, A20 and A21 moved from early to late, while A15, A16, A18 and A19 moved from late to early, and the successor activities A22 and A27 swapped accordingly. This is directly shown by positional shifts, successor changes, and pairwise order reversals across many indicators. The number of qualifying evidence items (54) far exceeds the 0.57 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that the change is only a shift in variant frequencies is largely ruled out by the complete reversal of pairwise order in the co-occurring cases. The evidence cannot, however, determine whether this reflects a deliberate redesign or a change in the population of cases, and it does not identify any cause.
