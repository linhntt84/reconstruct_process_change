### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A20, A21, A22 and A23 appear earlier in traces, while A15, A16, A18 and A19 appear later. For example, the mean relative position of A15 rose from about 0.57 to 0.63, while A20 fell from about 0.64 to 0.56. [POS001, POS005]
- For many pairs, the order reversed: before, A15, A16, A18 and A19 always preceded A20–A23; after, the opposite order occurs in a large share of cases. For instance, A15 preceded A20 in 100% of cases before but in only about 24% after; A19 preceded A20 in 100% before but in about 8% after. [REL001, REL012]
- The distance between these activities shrank: e.g., the lag between A15 and A20 was 2–3 steps before and is now mostly 1–2 steps; the lag between A19 and A20 was 2–3 before and is now mostly 1. [LAG003, LAG020]
- Outgoing transitions from A15, A16, A18 and A19 now include A20–A23, which were absent before. For example, A15 now goes to A20, A21, A22 and A23 (about 40% combined), whereas before it went only to A19 and A16. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- A19's successor changed dramatically: before it always went to A20; after, it goes to A29 (about 50%), A21, A22 and A23, with only about 8% to A20. [DIR004]
- A28's successor changed: before it always went to A18; after, about half of its transitions go to A20. [DIR009]
- A43's successor shifted from A44 to A40: the share to A40 rose from about 34% to 51%. [DIR010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the order of a group of activities changed: activities A20, A21, A22 and A23 now tend to occur before A15, A16, A18 and A19, whereas before they occurred after. This is directly shown by the reversal in relative order for many pairs, the shift in positions, and the new outgoing edges. The change is consistent across many indicators and is far more than would be expected by chance. A competing explanation that the differences are due to sampling variation is unlikely given the large number of items and the consistency, but the evidence cannot rule out that the process changed in a more complex way, such as a new variant with a different order becoming dominant, or that some activities (like A29) were introduced or moved. The A43/A40/A44 change appears separate and smaller.
