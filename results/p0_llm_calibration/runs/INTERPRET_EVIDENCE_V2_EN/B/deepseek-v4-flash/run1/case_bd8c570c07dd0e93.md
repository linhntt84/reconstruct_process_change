Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the order of activities changed: A27 and A28, which previously occurred early (right after A20) and before A21/A23, now occur later (after A21/A23) and before A24. The number of qualifying items (20) is far above the 0.39 expected by chance. A competing explanation of sampling variation in variant frequencies is unlikely given the magnitude and consistency of the changes across all indicators. A limitation is that the evidence does not show whether this reflects a deliberate process redesign or a different execution pattern, but the structural change in ordering is well supported.
