### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A14 and A15 appear in about 48% and 52% of traces respectively, while they were completely absent in the earlier window. [OCC001, OCC002]
- A14 and A15 occur near the middle of the trace (mean position around 0.51 and 0.52). [POS001, POS002]
- A21 previously always led to A22; in the later window it leads to A14 (about 25%), A15 (about 28%), and A22 (about 48%). [DIR003]
- A22 previously always led to A23; in the later window it leads to A23 (about 53%), A15 (about 24%), and A14 (about 24%). [DIR004]
- A14 and A15 each lead to A22 and A23 in roughly equal shares. [DIR001, DIR002]
- A14 and A15 never co-occur; each appears alone in about half the traces. [REL001]
- A14 and A15 occur after A21 (A21 before A14 in 48%, A21 before A15 in 52%) and before A23 (A14 before A23 in 48%, A15 before A23 in 52%). [REL002, REL004, REL007, REL009]
- The lag between A21 and A22 increased: before always 1 step, after 1 step in 48% and 2 steps in 52%. Similarly, A21–A23 lag increased from 2 to 3 steps in almost all cases, and A22–A23 lag increased from 1 to 2 steps in about half. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, two alternative activities A14 and A15 were inserted into the middle of the process, between A21 and A22/A23, in about half of the traces. The appearance of A14 and A15, their position around the middle, their outgoing edges to A22 and A23, their placement after A21 and before A23, and the increased lags between A21–A22, A21–A23, and A22–A23 all point consistently to this insertion. The number of evidence items (24) far exceeds the 0.39 expected by chance under a stable process, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that the activities existed before but were simply not observed is implausible given they appear in roughly half of the later traces; however, the exact share of traces using the new path could vary in other samples.
