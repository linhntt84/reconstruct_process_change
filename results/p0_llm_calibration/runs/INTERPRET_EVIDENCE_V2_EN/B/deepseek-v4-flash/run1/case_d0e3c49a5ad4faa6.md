### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the process after A20 more often takes a path through A24 and the A21–A24 group, and less often through A28 and the A25–A28 group. This is directly shown by the shift in A20's successor distribution and by the co-occurrence and order relations between A20 and these activities, and it is consistent with the large participation changes of A21–A24 and A25–A28.
