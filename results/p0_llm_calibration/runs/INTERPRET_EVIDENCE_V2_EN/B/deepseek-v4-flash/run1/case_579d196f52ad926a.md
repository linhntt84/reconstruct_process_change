### Observed differences

- In the later window, A14 disappears entirely (0 of 200 traces vs 91 of 200 before, about 45.5%) and A15 nearly disappears (1 of 200 vs 109 of 200 before, about 54.5%). [OCC001, OCC002, POS001, POS002, REL001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the later process no longer executes A14 and A15 as intermediate steps, so cases now go from A21 to A22 to A23 instead of passing through A14 or A15. This is supported by the near-total disappearance of A14 and A15, the routing of A21 and A22 almost exclusively toward A22 and A23, and the shortening of the A21–A23 lag from 3 to 2 steps.
