### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a set of activities — A14, A15, A20 and A21 — which were executed in about a quarter of cases before, are no longer executed in the later window, and that the process now routes directly from A23 to A24 to A25. The number of qualifying evidence items (40) is far above the 0.43 expected by chance, and the signs are consistent across participation, routing, position, co-occurrence and lag indicators. A competing explanation — that these activities were moved elsewhere in the process rather than removed — is not supported because they are absent from the traces and their outgoing transitions are gone; however, the evidence cannot fully rule out that they occur in a rare variant not captured in the 200-trace window.
