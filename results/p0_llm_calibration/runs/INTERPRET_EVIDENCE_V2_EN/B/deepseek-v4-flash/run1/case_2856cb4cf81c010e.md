### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the later process largely skips A04 and replaces the A21–A24 block with A25–A28, with A31 and A20 routing directly to the new block. The participation swap, the successor changes for A31 and A20, and the relational and lag evidence all point in the same direction: A31 and A19/A20 now lead to A25–A28 instead of to A04 and A21–A24. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011, DIR003, DIR004, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL025, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL062, REL066, REL069, REL071, LAG007, LAG008, LAG010]

A competing explanation is that the windows simply contain different case mixes, and the variant-frequency differences alone could produce some of the participation changes. However, the direct successor and order changes are not explained by sampling alone. A limitation is that A04-related evidence in the later window rests on only about 31 traces, and some lag details on even fewer, so the exact new position of A04 is less certain.
