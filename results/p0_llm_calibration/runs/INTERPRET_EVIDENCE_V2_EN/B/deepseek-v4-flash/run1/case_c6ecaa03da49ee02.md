### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A19 and A20 are executed in most cases (previously in about a fifth of cases), A40 now routes to A19 and A20 instead of to A25, and A23 is executed less often. The direct successor change of A40, the increased participation of A19 and A20, and the consistent relational orderings (A40 before A19/A20, and A19/A20 before A25) all point in the same direction. The number of qualifying items (10, against about 0.5 expected by chance) makes a chance explanation unlikely. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the observed windows sample different variant mixes of an unchanged process, but the size and consistency of the differences make that less plausible.
