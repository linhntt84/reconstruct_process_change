### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was structurally changed by reordering A19/A20 and A21/A22: A19 and A20 moved from an early position to a later position, while A21 and A22 moved from a late position to an earlier position, with A23 and A27 remaining in the middle. The evidence cannot fully rule out that A28 is a newly introduced or renamed activity, but the core reordering of A19/A20 relative to A21/A22 is directly and consistently supported.
