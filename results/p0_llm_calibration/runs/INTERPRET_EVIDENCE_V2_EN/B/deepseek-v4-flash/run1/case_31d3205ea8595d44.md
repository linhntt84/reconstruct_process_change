### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in which the process splits into two alternative branches: one branch contains A22 but not A31/A32/A37/A38, and the other contains A31/A32/A37/A38 but not A22. In addition, A23 moves to immediately after A20, and A21 and A23 now occur before the A31/A32/A37/A38 group, whereas before that group preceded them. The supporting signs are direct and consistent: participation drops, mutual exclusivity between the two activity groups, successor changes at A20 and at A31/A32/A37/A38, and the lag change between A20 and A23.
