### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a block of activities A32, A33, A38 and A39 was introduced or became common in the later window, inserted between A26 and A28, and that the previously dominant A26→A27→A28 path was partially replaced. In the later window, A26 still occurs in every trace, A27 drops to about half of traces, A28 remains in every trace, and the new activities appear in about half of traces with connections among themselves and to A28. The A26–A28 lag shifting from mostly 2 steps to often 5 steps is directly consistent with extra steps between them. The number of qualifying evidence items, 72, far exceeds the 0.83 expected by chance, and the occurrence, routing, relational and positional indicators agree. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the new activities existed before but were too rare to be observed in the 200-trace before window; the evidence also does not show whether the old A26→A27 path was removed or merely became less frequent. The A04-related changes are based on few traces and are not needed for the main conclusion.
