### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed so that activities A32 and A33 now occur before A26 and A28, with the routing from A25 and A28 adjusted accordingly. The reversal in relative order is shown directly by the relational indicators, and the positional and lag indicators confirm the same shift. The routing changes are consistent with this new order: A25 now leads to A32/A33 instead of A26, and A28 now leads to A29 instead of A32/A33. The number of qualifying evidence items (23) is far above the expected count without change (0.57), and the pattern is consistent across multiple indicators. A competing explanation—that the differences arise from sampling variation in variant frequencies—is unlikely given the magnitude and consistency, though a few lag items in the later window rest on very few observations (e.g., A26–A33, A28–A32, A28–A33), so those specific lags are weakly supported.
