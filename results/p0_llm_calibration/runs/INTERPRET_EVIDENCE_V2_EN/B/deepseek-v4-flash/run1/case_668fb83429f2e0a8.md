### Observed differences

- In the later window, the order between two groups of activities reversed. For example, A25 appeared before A20 in about 76% of cases (was 0%), while A20 before A25 dropped from 99% to about 24%. [REL001, REL005, REL012]
- Similar reversals hold for A21, A23, A24 versus A25, A26, A27, A28: A24 before A25 dropped from 100% to about 8%, and A25 before A24 rose to 91%; A21 before A25 dropped from 99.5% to 25%, and A25 before A21 rose to 75%. [REL012, REL005, REL007, REL008, REL014, REL015]
- Positions shifted accordingly: A20, A21, A23, A24 moved later (mean relative position increased by about 0.05–0.08), while A25, A26, A27, A28 moved earlier (mean decreased by about 0.03–0.08). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, POS007, POS008]
- Successor distributions changed: A24 previously went to A25 in 100% of observed transitions; in the later window it went to A34 in about 49%, A26 17%, A27 13%, A28 11%, and A25 only 7%. A20's successors shifted from A24/A21 (about 99% combined) to a mix including A25–A28 (about 40% combined) while still keeping A24/A21 (about 60% combined). [DIR004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- The number of evidence items (72) is far above the ~0.84 expected by chance under a stable process. [screening]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the relative order of two groups of activities changed: activities A25, A26, A27, A28 (and possibly A34) now tend to occur before A20, A21, A23, A24, whereas before they occurred after. This is directly shown by the reversal in pairwise order (REL), the shift in relative positions (POS), and the change in successor distributions (DIR). The consistency across many items and the large effect sizes make a sampling artefact unlikely. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed windows come from different variant mixes of a stable process, but the number and coherence of the differences make that implausible. The evidence does not indicate why the order changed.
