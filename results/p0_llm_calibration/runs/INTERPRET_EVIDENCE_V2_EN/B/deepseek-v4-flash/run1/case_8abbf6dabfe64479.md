### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20, A25 and A26 appear in about 31%, 26%, 22% and 23% of traces respectively, whereas before each appeared in at most 1 in 200 traces and A25 not at all. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- A06 participation rises from about 12% to 28% of traces. [OCC001]
- A42's outgoing behaviour changes sharply: before, about 92% of its transitions went to A27; after, none go to A27, and instead about 29% go to A19, 26% to A20, 21% to A25 and 23% to A26. [DIR006]
- Each of A19, A20, A25 and A26 now leads almost exclusively to A27 (about 95–98% of their outgoing transitions), while before they were essentially absent or led elsewhere. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- A06's successor changes from a spread over many activities to about 86% going to A48. [DIR001]
- A47 still mostly goes to A48 (about 75%), but also to A06 in about 24% of its transitions. [DIR007]
- Positionally, A06 moves later in the trace (mean position from about 0.46 to 0.89), while A19, A20, A25 and A26 appear around position 0.35–0.37, just before A27 (mean about 0.40). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- Relational evidence shows A42 preceding A19/A20/A25/A26 in about 30%, 26%, 22% and 23% of traces, and each of those activities preceding A27 in similar shares. A27 also precedes A06 in about 26% of traces, and A47 precedes A06 in about 24%. [REL010, REL011, REL016, REL017, REL021, REL022, REL025, REL026, REL005, REL006]
- Lag distances change consistently: A42 to A27 shifts from mostly 1 step to mostly 2 steps; A06 to A48 becomes almost always 1 step; A47 to A48 sometimes becomes 2 steps. [LAG002, LAG001, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a new step is inserted between A42 and A27: A42 now leads to one of A19, A20, A25 or A26, and that activity then leads to A27, instead of A42 leading directly to A27. This is directly shown by the routing changes of A42 and of the four new activities, by the relational order A42 → new activity → A27, and by the lag shift from one to two steps between A42 and A27. The same evidence also indicates a related re-routing around A06 and A48: A47 increasingly leads to A06, and A06 then leads to A48, with A06 moving later in the trace. The number of qualifying evidence items is far above what an unchanged process would typically produce, and the occurrence, routing, position, relation and lag indicators all point in the same direction. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the before window simply did not sample these rare activities, but their combined appearance with consistent ordering makes that unlikely to account for the whole pattern.
