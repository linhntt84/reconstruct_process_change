### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was modified to insert a set of new activities (A19, A20, A25, A26) into the middle of the process, between the earlier activities (A14, A15, A27, A28, A42) and the later activities (A29, A30, A46, A47, A48), while the previously common activity A04 is used much less.
