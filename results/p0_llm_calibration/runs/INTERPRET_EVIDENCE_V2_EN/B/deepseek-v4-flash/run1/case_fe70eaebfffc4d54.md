### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a segment involving A02, A19, A20, A25 and A26 was removed or bypassed in the later window. A42, which previously routed to these activities, now routes to A27, and A45, which previously went through A02 on the way to A46, now goes directly to A46. The drop in participation is large and consistent across occurrence, routing, relation and position indicators. A limitation is that A19, A20, A25 and A26 are each supported by only a handful of traces in the later window, so their exact successor distributions are uncertain; however, the overall disappearance of this group from the main flow is well supported.
