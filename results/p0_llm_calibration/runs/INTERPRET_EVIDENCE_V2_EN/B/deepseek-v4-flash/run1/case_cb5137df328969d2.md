### Observed differences

In the later window, activities A19, A20, A25 and A26 almost disappear: each was present in about 26% of traces before, and in the later window A20 and A26 are absent, A19 appears in about 1.5% of traces and A25 in about 0.5% [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]. Co-occurrence of these four activities with A04, A14, A15, A28, A29 and A30 drops from about 24–27% of traces to near zero, and those other activities now appear alone in the same traces [REL001–REL012, REL016–REL030]. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that a block of activities A19, A20, A25 and A26, which was executed in about a quarter of cases before, is no longer executed (or executed extremely rarely) in the later window. This is directly shown by the drop in occurrence and participation, the disappearance of their outgoing transitions, and the loss of co-occurrence with the surrounding activities A04, A14, A15, A28, A29 and A30. The changes in lag between A28–A30 and A29–A30 are consistent with the removal of these intermediate steps. A competing explanation that the later sample simply missed a rare variant is unlikely given the size of the drop and the number of indicators, but the evidence cannot rule out that these activities still exist in the process but were not observed in this sample.
