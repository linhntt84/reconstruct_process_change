### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed so that the A25–A26 pair is no longer repeated or interleaved; instead, each case executes A25 then A26 exactly once, with A26 always followed by A20. The supporting signs are the drop in mean occurrences and recurrence for both activities, the disappearance of the A26→A25 edge, the loss of interleaving, and the constant lag of 1. The number of evidence items (5) far exceeds the 0.29 expected by chance, and the indicators are mutually consistent. A competing explanation of sampling variation is unlikely given the uniformity across 200 traces in the later window, though the evidence cannot rule out that the after window represents a rare variant of the process.
