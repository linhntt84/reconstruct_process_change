### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process changed around A08 and the A30/A31 block: A08 is executed much less often, and A30/A31 are now repeated and interleaved with A19/A20 rather than appearing once in a fixed order after them. The supporting signs are consistent: A08 participation drops from about 28.5% to 9%, A30/A31 mean occurrences and repetition increase, and the relational order among A19/A20/A30/A31 shifts from a strict sequence to interleaving in about 37% of traces. A limitation is that the A08-specific successor and lag profiles rest on few later-window traces, so the exact new routing after A08 is less certain. Sampling differences in variant frequencies cannot be fully excluded, but the volume and consistency of the evidence argue against that as the main explanation. [DIR002, LAG001-004, LAG010]
