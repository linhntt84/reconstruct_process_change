### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in the process: activity A04 is now executed much more often and earlier, often immediately after A14, and before a set of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26) that previously occurred later. In addition, many activities that previously routed to A29 or A33 now route to A27. These changes are consistent across occurrence, routing, position, and ordering indicators. The number of evidence items (68) far exceeds the 0.86 expected by chance, and the patterns are mutually consistent. A competing explanation—that the differences arise from sampling variation in variant frequencies—is unlikely given the magnitude and consistency of the changes, though it cannot be entirely ruled out. The evidence does not identify the cause or business meaning of the change.
