### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A25 and A26 almost disappear: A25 participation drops from 100% to about 2.5% of traces (mean per trace from 1.0 to 0.025), and A26 from 100% to about 1.5% (mean from 1.01 to 0.015). [OCC003, OCC004]
- Instead, A19 and A20 appear: A19 is present in about 48.5% of traces (mean 0.495) and A20 in about 51% (mean 0.52), while before they were essentially absent (0% and 0.5%). [OCC001, OCC002]
- A19 and A20 rarely co-occur: in the later window, about 48% of traces have A19 only, 50.5% have A20 only, and only 0.5% have both. [REL001]
- The ordering relative to A27 changes: before, A25 and A26 preceded A27 (A25 before A27 in 99.5% of traces, A26 before A27 in 98.5%); after, A19 precedes A27 in about 46.5% of traces and A20 precedes A27 in about 50% of traces, with A27 still present in all traces. [REL004, REL008, REL011, REL013, POS005]
- A42 now precedes A19/A20 instead of A25/A26: before, A42 preceded A25 in 99% and A26 in 98% of traces; after, A42 precedes A19 in 47% and A20 in 50% of traces. [REL005, REL009, REL012, REL014]
- The lag between A20 and A27 changes from 5 steps to 1 step (in the later window, 98% of A20–A27 pairs are adjacent), and between A42 and A27 from 3 steps to 2 steps. [LAG001, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a segment where A42 was followed by both A25 and A26 (in either order) and then A27, to a segment where A42 is followed by either A19 or A20 (not both) and then A27. This is directly shown by the disappearance of A25/A26, the appearance of A19/A20, and the consistent relational and directional evidence that A42 precedes the new activities, which precede A27. [OCC001–OCC004, REL001, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009, REL011–REL014, DIR002, DIR003] The main limitation is that the evidence cannot rule out that A19 and A20 are simply renamed versions of A25 and A26, or that the change reflects a sampling difference in variant frequencies, though the large number of consistent indicators makes that unlikely.
