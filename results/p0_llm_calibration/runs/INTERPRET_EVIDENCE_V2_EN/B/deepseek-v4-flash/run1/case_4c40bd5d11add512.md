The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a segment consisting of A19, one of A25 or A26, and A20 is executed in nearly all cases between A42 and A27, whereas before it was executed in only about half of cases, with the other half going directly from A42 to A27. The number of qualifying evidence items is far above what would be expected by chance under a stable process, and the indicators point in the same direction.
