### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a structural change in which activity A06 is executed much less frequently, activities A30 and A31 are no longer repeated (they occur once per trace), and the ordering among several activities becomes more sequential (less interleaving/repetition). Activities A02, A04, and A10 also appear more often. The number of evidence items (58) far exceeds the 0.77 expected by chance, and the changes are consistent across occurrence, transition, relation, and lag indicators. However, some lag items have very low support in the later window (e.g., 1–16 traces), so the specific lag shifts are less reliable, but the overall pattern is consistent. A competing explanation of sampling differences in variant frequencies is unlikely given the volume of evidence, but cannot be entirely ruled out for the low-support items.
