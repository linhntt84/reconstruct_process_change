### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A35 now often leads to A20 instead of A14/A15, and A14/A15 are used less frequently; the routing among A20, A27, A28, and A21 also shifted. The direct successor change at A35 (DIR004), the drop in A14/A15 occurrence (OCC001, OCC002), the rise in traces with neither A14 nor A15 (REL001), and the positional shifts of A35 relative to A20/A21/A27/A28 (LAG005–LAG008) are consistent. The routing changes among A20/A27/A28/A21 are directly shown by successor and lag shifts (DIR001–DIR003, LAG001–LAG004). A competing explanation is that the two windows are finite samples with different variant frequencies; however, the number of qualifying items (25 vs 0.42 expected) and their mutual consistency make a structural change the more plausible interpretation. The change is not a complete removal of A14/A15, as they still appear in about a quarter of later traces.
