### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a single sequential block of activities (A33–A41) was replaced by a choice between two alternative sub‑blocks: one containing A33, A34, A36, A37 and the other containing A38, A39, A40, A41, both still preceded by A30 and followed by A31. The supporting signs are consistent: each activity’s participation halved, the two groups became mutually exclusive, A31 still appears in all traces, and the lag distances and position shifts match the shorter branch structure. A competing explanation—that activities are simply skipped independently—is unlikely because the mutual exclusivity between the two groups is clean and systematic, and A31’s presence in all traces argues against a general shortening of cases. Some lag items rest on very few observations in the later window, but the core pattern is corroborated by many independent indicators.
