### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which a new block of activities, A20, A28, A29 and A30, is inserted after the early activities and before A31, while the previous middle activities A19, A21, A22 and A23 are largely skipped and A04 is used less often. The number of qualifying evidence items is far above the roughly 1.2 expected by chance, and occurrence, routing, relational and positional indicators all point to the same shift. The relational evidence directly shows the new ordering, not merely a change in frequency. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two windows sample different coexisting case types or variants rather than a change over time; however, the near-universal appearance of the new activities and the near-disappearance of the old ones make a simple sampling difference unlikely.
