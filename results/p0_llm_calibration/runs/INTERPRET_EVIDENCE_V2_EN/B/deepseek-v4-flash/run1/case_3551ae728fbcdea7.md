### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process now has two alternative branches after A30: one branch includes A33, A34, A36, and A37, and the other includes A38, A39, A40, and A41. Before, all these activities were executed in sequence; now they are mutually exclusive, with each branch appearing in about half the traces. This is directly shown by the drop in participation, the near-zero co-occurrence across the two groups, and the new successor relations (A31 appearing after the first group, and A30 now leading to the second group). The position and lag changes are consistent with this reordering. A competing explanation of sampling variation is ruled out by the large number of consistent indicators and the strong mutual exclusivity. The evidence cannot determine the exact internal order within each branch or the reason for the split, but the structural change is well supported.
