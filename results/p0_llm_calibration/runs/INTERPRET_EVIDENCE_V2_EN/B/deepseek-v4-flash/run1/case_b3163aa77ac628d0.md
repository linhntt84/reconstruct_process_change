### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A28, A29, A31, and A32 appear in about half of traces (50%) instead of all traces; A33–A36 also appear in about half (50–51%). [OCC001–OCC008]
- A25's outgoing transitions now include A33–A36, which were not successors before; A28, A29, A31, and A32 now have A26 as a successor, which was not present before, and they no longer lead to A33–A36. [DIR001–DIR006]
- A25 and A26 still appear in all traces, but each of A28–A36 co-occurs with them in only about half of traces. A28–A32 and A33–A36 are almost mutually exclusive: pairs such as A28–A33 co-occur in about 0.5% of traces after, with each occurring alone in about half. [REL001–REL044]
- Positions shift: A25, A28, A29, A31, and A32 move later (e.g., A25 mean position from 0.58 to 0.66; A28 from 0.66 to 0.74), while A33–A36 move slightly earlier (e.g., A33 from 0.77 to 0.75). [POS001–POS010]
- Lags shorten: A25 to A33–A36 changes from always 5 steps to 1–4 steps; A28/A29/A31/A32 to A26 changes from always 5 steps to 1–4 steps. [LAG001–LAG005, LAG008, LAG013, LAG017]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural split in the process: before, all activities A25, A26, A28–A36 occurred in every trace in a single sequence; in the later window, the process appears to have two alternative variants — one containing A25, A28, A29, A31, A32, and A26, and the other containing A25, A33, A34, A35, A36, and A26. Each variant occurs in about half of traces, with the other block absent. This is supported by the halved participation of A28–A36, the new successor A26 from A28–A32 and new successors A33–A36 from A25, the near mutual exclusivity between the two blocks, and the shortened lags within each block. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the later window contains two distinct case types rather than a temporal change, but the consistency across occurrence, routing, relation, position, and lag indicators makes a structural change the most direct interpretation.
