### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, activities A30, A31, A36 and A37 are executed repeatedly and interleaved within cases, rather than each occurring once in a fixed order. The number of qualifying evidence items is far above the 0.57 expected by chance under no change, and the occurrence, relation, lag and successor indicators all point in the same direction: these activities are repeated, their relative order is no longer fixed, and A37 now often leads to A30 instead of A25.
