### Observed differences

- In the later window, each of activities A25, A26, A31 and A32 occurs exactly once per trace on average (mean 1.0), whereas before each occurred on average about 3.3 times per trace; the share of traces with repeated occurrences of these activities fell from about 67% to 0% [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004].
- The relative order of these activities became fixed: for most pairs (A25–A26, A25–A31, A25–A32, A26–A32, A31–A32), the first activity always precedes the second in the later window (100% of traces), while before only about 33% of traces had that order and 67% had interleaved or repeated occurrences [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL005, REL006].
- For the pair A26–A31, both orders occur in the later window (about 56% A26 before A31, 44% A31 before A26), but interleaving/repetition disappeared (0% vs 67% before) [REL004].
- The distance between these activities shrank: in the later window, the lag between consecutive occurrences is always 1 or 2 steps (e.g., A25–A26: 56% lag 1, 44% lag 2; A26–A31: 100% lag 1), whereas before lags of 3–5 steps also occurred [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006].
- The positions of these activities became much more concentrated: the standard deviation of their relative positions dropped from about 0.13–0.14 to about 0.05, and the upper quartile moved closer to the middle (e.g., A25 q75 from 0.52 to 0.43; A32 q75 from 0.60 to 0.52) [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004].
- The successor of A32 changed: before, about 70% of its outgoing transitions went to A25 and 30% to A20; in the later window, 100% go to A20 [DIR001].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process changed from a repetitive, interleaved execution of activities A25, A26, A31 and A32 to a single sequential pass over these activities, each occurring exactly once, with a largely fixed order (A25 before A26, A25 before A31, A25 before A32, A26 before A32, A31 before A32; A26 and A31 may appear in either order but always adjacent). This is directly shown by the drop in mean occurrences and recurrence to zero, the disappearance of interleaving/repetition in all pairwise relations, the concentration of lags to 1–2 steps, the reduced spread of positions, and the change in A32's successor from A25 to A20. The number of qualifying evidence items (20) far exceeds the 0.38 expected by chance, and the indicators are mutually consistent. A competing explanation—that the differences arise from sampling variation in variant frequencies—is implausible given the magnitude and coherence of the changes, though the exact order between A26 and A31 remains variable and the process may still contain other unobserved variation.
