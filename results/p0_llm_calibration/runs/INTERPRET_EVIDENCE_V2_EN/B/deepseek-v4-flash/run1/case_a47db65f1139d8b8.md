### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a segment involving activities A02, A19 and A20 is executed much less often in the later window, and that the flow from A40 now more often leads to A25 instead of to A20, A02 or A19. The participation drops, the widespread co-occurrence decreases, and the successor changes for A40 and A02 all point in the same direction. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL001–REL009, DIR001, DIR003, LAG004] The number of qualifying evidence items (21) is far above the 0.54 expected by chance, and the changes are consistent across occurrence, relation and direction indicators. A competing explanation that the differences are only sampling variation in variant frequencies is unlikely given the volume and consistency of the evidence, though it cannot be fully excluded. The evidence does not show why this segment became less frequent, only that its execution and its connections to A25 and A40 changed.
