### Observed differences

- In the later window, A25 is almost always followed by A27 (about 98 in 100 outgoing transitions), whereas before its successors were split among A42, A36, A35 and A41. [DIR001]
- A26's outgoing transitions changed from mostly A30 (about 96 in 100) to a split among A36, A41, A35 and A42. [DIR002]
- A27 disappears as a successor of A35, A36, A41 and A42; A30 appears as a successor of these activities. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- A26 and A27 occur earlier in the trace: mean relative position dropped from about 0.62 to 0.51 for A26 and from 0.48 to 0.37 for A27. [POS001, POS002]
- A35, A36, A41 and A42 occur later: mean relative position rose from about 0.41 to about 0.58. [POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- For each pair of A26 or A27 with A35, A36, A41 or A42, the order reversed: before, the latter preceded the former in about 99 in 100 cases; after, the former precedes the latter in about 97–99 in 100 cases. [REL001–REL008]
- A25 and A27 are now adjacent (lag 1 in about 98 in 100 cases) instead of five steps apart. [LAG001]
- A26 and A30 are now five steps apart instead of adjacent (lag 1 in 96 in 100 cases before). [LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a reordering of the process in which A25 is now followed immediately by A27, and A26/A27 are moved earlier relative to the block of activities A35, A36, A41 and A42, while A30 is moved later (or appears after that block). The supporting signs are consistent across multiple indicators: successor changes (DIR), position shifts (POS), order reversals (REL) and lag changes (LAG) all point to the same rearrangement. The main limitation is that the two windows are finite samples, so variant‑frequency differences could in principle produce some of the observed shifts; however, the large effect sizes and the agreement across many independent indicators make a structural reordering the most plausible explanation. Some lag items (e.g., A25–A35, A25–A42) have very small support in the later window, but they are not needed for the main conclusion.
