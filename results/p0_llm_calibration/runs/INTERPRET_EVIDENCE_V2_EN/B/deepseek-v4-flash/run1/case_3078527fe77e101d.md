### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the path from A25 to A28 no longer passes through A36, A37, A42 and A43. In the later window, A25 goes directly to A28, while those intermediate activities now lead to A31 and occur in only about half of cases. This is supported directly by the successor changes (A25→A28, and A36/A37/A42/A43→A31), the lag change between A25 and A28, and the reversals of order between A26/A28 and the A36/A37/A42/A43 group. The number of consistent indicators is far above what an unchanged process would produce by chance. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that some of the participation drop reflects sampling differences in variant frequencies, but the direct successor and order changes are not explained by that alone.
