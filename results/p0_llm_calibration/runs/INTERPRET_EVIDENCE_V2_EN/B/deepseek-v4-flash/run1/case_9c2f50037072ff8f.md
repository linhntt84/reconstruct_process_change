### Observed differences

- In the later window, A02 appears in far fewer traces: participation fell from about 30% to 12%, and mean occurrences per trace from 0.32 to 0.12. A06 rose from about 2% to 10% of traces. [OCC001, OCC002]
- A19 and A20 almost completely stopped routing to A25. Before, about 99% of their outgoing transitions went to A25; after, roughly half go to A27 and half to A28, with A25 nearly absent. [DIR002, DIR003]
- A40's outgoing behaviour reversed: before, about 54% went to A19 and 44% to A20; after, about 90% go to A25. [DIR006]
- A26 and A27 also changed their successors: A26 went to A27 in about 97% of cases before but only about 49% after, with A19 and A20 appearing; A27 went to A28 in about 98% before but only about 52% after, with A19 and A20 appearing. [DIR004, DIR005]
- The order of A19/A20 relative to A25/A26 flipped. Before, A19/A20 preceded A25/A26 in about 45–55% of traces; after, A25/A26 precede A19/A20 in about 46–51% of traces. [REL007, REL008, REL010, REL011]
- Lags confirm the reordering: A19–A27 and A20–A27 moved from a lag of 5 steps to a lag of 1 step in about 96% of cases; A40–A19 and A40–A20 moved from lag 1 to lag 5; A40–A25 moved from lag 2 to lag 1. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]
- Positional shifts match: A02 moved earlier (mean relative position from about 0.92 to 0.52), while A19 and A20 moved later (from about 0.35 to about 0.50–0.52). [POS001, POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a segment of the process was reordered and rerouted: previously A40 led to A19/A20, which led to A25, and A25 later led toward A27/A28; in the later window A40 leads to A25, while A19/A20 occur after A25 and now lead to A27/A28. This is supported directly by successor changes, relative-order flips, and lag shifts involving A19, A20, A25, A27, A28 and A40. The evidence also shows a separate reduction in A02 participation and an increase in A06, which may be an independent change. A competing explanation based only on variant-frequency sampling is unlikely to account for the consistent relational and positional evidence, though the exact cause of the reordering cannot be inferred.
