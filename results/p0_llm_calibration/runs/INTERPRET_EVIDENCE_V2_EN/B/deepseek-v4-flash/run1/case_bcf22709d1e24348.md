### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the roles of two hubs, A27 and A32, are swapped in the process, and A08’s participation is greatly reduced and its position moves earlier. Activities that previously routed through A32 (A19, A22, A25, A26) now route through A27, while activities that previously routed through A27 (A20, A21, A23, A24) now route through A32. This is directly shown by the successor changes in DIR and by the order reversals in REL, and it is consistent with the position shifts in POS. A08, which used to be a common late activity leading to A48, is now rare and occurs earlier. The number of evidence items (120) far exceeds the 1.26 expected by chance under no change, and the pattern is consistent across indicators. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a change in the mix of case types (variant frequencies) rather than a change in the process structure itself; however, the breadth and consistency of the evidence make this less likely.
