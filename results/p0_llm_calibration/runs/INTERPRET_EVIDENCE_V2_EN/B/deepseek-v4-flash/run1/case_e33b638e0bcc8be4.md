### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change: the process moved from a repeated, interleaved execution of A30, A31, A36 and A37 to a single sequential pass through them, and A37's outgoing routing changed from A30 to A25. The drop in mean occurrences and recurrence, the near-elimination of interleaving in favour of strict ordering, the narrowed position distribution, and the shortened lags all point to the same change.
