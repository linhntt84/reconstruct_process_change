The evidence supports a reordering of the main process flow: A20 now leads to A27/A28 instead of A21, A27/A28 now lead to A21 instead of A24, and A23 now leads directly to A24. This shifts A27/A28 earlier in the trace and A21/A23 later, with A24 later still relative to A27/A28. Sampling differences in variant frequencies are unlikely to explain the consistent reversal of order across multiple indicators.
