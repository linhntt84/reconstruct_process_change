### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the process moved from two alternative branches — one containing A28–A32 followed by A26, and another containing A33–A36 — to a single flow where A28–A32 are followed by A33–A36, and A26 appears much later, about five steps after the A28–A32 group. This is supported by the shift from zero co-occurrence to near-universal co-occurrence of the two activity groups, the change in A25's routing away from A33–A36, and the lag shift for A26. The number of qualifying indicators is far above what an unchanged process would typically produce, so sampling variation in variant frequencies is an unlikely explanation. The main limitation is that the evidence does not specify the exact control-flow position of A26 in the later window, only that it is no longer a direct successor of A28–A32 and is separated from them by about five steps.
