### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a branch of the process containing A14, A15, A20 and A21 is executed less often in the later window, being skipped in about 42 in 100 cases, and that A37 now sometimes routes to A22 instead of A14. The supporting signs are direct and consistent: participation of A14 and A15 dropped from universal to about 59 in 100, A20 and A21 dropped by roughly 20 points, the "neither" component for pairs among these activities rose to about 42 in 100, and A37's successor distribution plus the A37–A22 lag both show a new A22-only path. The number of qualifying items (20) is far above the 0.38 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a random artefact. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the later window is a different sample of variant frequencies rather than a structural change, but the breadth and consistency of the items make that less plausible.
