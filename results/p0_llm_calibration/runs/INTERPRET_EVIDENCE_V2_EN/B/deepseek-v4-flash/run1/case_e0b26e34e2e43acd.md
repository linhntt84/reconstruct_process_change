### Observed differences

- In the later window, A19 and A20 almost disappear: participation drops from about 49% and 52% of traces to about 1% and 2%, while A25 and A26 appear in about 99% of traces where they were absent before. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- A19 and A20 previously led almost always to A27 (about 97% and 96% of their outgoing transitions); in the later window A25 and A26 lead to A27 (about 49% and 50%) and to each other (about 50% and 49%). [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]
- A14 and A42, which before were followed by A19/A20, are now followed by A25/A26: in the later window A14 precedes A25/A26 in about 98% of traces, and A42 precedes A25/A26 in about 98%, whereas before A14/A42 preceded A19/A20 in about 49–52% of traces. - The position of the new activities matches the old ones: A25/A26 occur at a mean relative position of about 0.38, similar to A19/A20 before (about 0.37), while A19/A20 positions essentially disappear. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]
- The gap between A42 and A27 shifts from mostly 2 steps to mostly 3 steps, consistent with an extra activity inserted between them. [LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A19 and A20 were replaced by A25 and A26 as the intermediate activities between A14/A42 and A27. Occurrence counts, outgoing routing, pairwise order, position and lag all point in the same direction: A19/A20 fall from about half of traces to near zero, A25/A26 rise from zero to nearly all traces, and the relational evidence shows A14/A42 now precede A25/A26, which in turn precede A27, whereas before A14/A42 preceded A19/A20, which preceded A27. The lag shift from 2 to 3 steps between A42 and A27 is consistent with an additional activity in that segment. A competing explanation—that the two windows simply sample different case variants—cannot be fully excluded, but the consistency across occurrence, routing, relation, position and lag indicators makes it unlikely.
