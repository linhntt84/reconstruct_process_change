### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured to include A10 as an early step, followed by A16 and then later activities such as A27, A28, A29, A30, A42, A47 and A48, while activities A19, A20, A25 and A26 are no longer executed. This is supported consistently by occurrence counts, routing shares, positional shifts, co-occurrence relations and lag distributions. The main limitation is that the later-window samples for A19, A20, A25 and A26 are very small (2–5 traces), so their exact routing after the change is uncertain, but their near-disappearance is clear. Sampling differences in variant frequencies are an unlikely explanation given the magnitude and consistency of the changes, though they cannot be fully excluded for the rare later-window activities.
