### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the routing from A40 changed so that A25 is now a frequent successor, largely replacing A19 and A20, which become less frequent overall. The supporting signs are direct: A25 appears as a new major successor of A40 (about 40% of outgoing transitions), while A19 and A20 each drop from about 40% to about 22–24% [DIR001]; co-occurrence of A19 and A20 with A25 and with A40 falls sharply, with A25 alone increasing [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]; and A25 now appears closer to A40 (lag 1 in 40.5% of cases vs. 0% before) [LAG001]. The drop in A19 and A20 occurrence is consistent with this replacement [OCC001, OCC002]. The number of evidence items (11) far exceeds the 0.52 expected by chance, so the pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that A19 and A20 still occur in other contexts not captured by these pairings, but the relational and positional evidence directly shows their reduced association with A40 and A25. The separate increase in A44 after A43 is an additional change but does not affect this conclusion.
