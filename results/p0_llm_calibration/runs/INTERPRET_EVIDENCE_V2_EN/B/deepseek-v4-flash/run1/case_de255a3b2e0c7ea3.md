### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process was restructured so that A10 is executed later and more often, with A46 immediately after it, while A19 and A20 are moved earlier, before A25/A26/A27, and A40 now leads to A19/A20 instead of A25. This is supported directly by positional and relational indicators (POS, REL, LAG) and by the successor changes in DIR. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the differences reflect a change in the mix of trace variants rather than a single structural modification; some individual items rest on few observations (e.g., A04/A06 support of 41/21 and 38/12, and a few LAG items with only one after‑trace), but the consistency across many indicators makes a structural change the more plausible interpretation.
