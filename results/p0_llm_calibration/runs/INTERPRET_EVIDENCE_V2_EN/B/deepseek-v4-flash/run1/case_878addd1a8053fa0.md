### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which a group of activities (A14, A15, A20, A21) moved from after A22 to before A22, and the routing from A23 and A24 became more deterministic, with A23 leading almost always to A24 and A24 leading almost always to A28. This is directly shown by relational and positional indicators: the order of A22 relative to A14/A15/A20/A21 reversed, and the positions of those activities shifted accordingly. The change is consistent across many indicators, far more than expected by chance (30 items vs 0.46 expected). A competing explanation of sampling variation in variant frequencies is unlikely given the number and consistency of the items, though the evidence does not identify the cause or whether the change is permanent. Some lag items have very low support in the later window (only one trace), but the main pattern is supported by high-support items such as the routing changes for A23 and A24 and the relational flips.
