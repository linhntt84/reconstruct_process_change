- In the later window, activities A27, A28, and A29 appear in every trace (100%) instead of about half of traces before (52%, 48%, 48%). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- The successor of A20 changed from always A21 to about half A28 and half A29. [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured so that A28 and A29 now occur in every case and early, immediately after A20, and then lead to A21/A23, then A27, then A24. Before, A20 led directly to A21, and A28/A29 occurred later and only in about half of cases. The supporting signs are consistent across occurrence, successor, ordering, and position indicators. The evidence cannot rule out that the observed differences are due to a change in the mix of trace variants rather than a single structural change, but the consistency across many indicators makes that unlikely.
