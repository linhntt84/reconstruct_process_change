### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A19 and A20 are no longer executed in the later window, while A08 is executed more often, later in the trace, and after A26/A27/A28, with A44 as its main successor. The disappearance of A19 and A20 is directly shown by their participation and outgoing transitions, and the new A26→A27→A28 chain with A08 placed after it is shown by successor, relation and lag evidence. A competing explanation that the later window is simply a different variant mix cannot be fully excluded, but the size and consistency of the changes — A19/A20 falling from roughly 45–55% of traces to about 0–1%, A08 rising from 7% to 28%, and the systematic shift of A26/A27 successors — make sampling variation an unlikely sole explanation.
