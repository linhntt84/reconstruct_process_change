### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A06 is executed more often and later in the process, typically after A29, A30 or A43, and that the routing among A21–A24 and A30 changed so that A30 is no longer the dominant immediate successor of A23 and A24, while it became a successor of A21 and A22. The number of qualifying items (29) is far above the 0.64 expected by chance under a stable process, and the positional, relational and successor indicators point in the same direction. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the two windows are simply different samples of variant frequencies, but the volume and consistency of the differences make that unlikely to account for the whole pattern.
