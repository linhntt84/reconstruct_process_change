### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process now has two main branches: in about half of cases the earlier pattern continues, with A25 preceding A36/A37/A42/A43 and A26 preceding A27 before A31; in the other half, A27, A36, A37, A42 and A43 are skipped, A25 is followed almost immediately by A28, and A26 routes to A36/A37/A42/A43 or A27 depending on the branch. The number of qualifying evidence items (63) is far above the 0.83 expected by chance, and occurrence, routing, position, relation and lag indicators agree on the same underlying shift. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the two windows reflect different case types or process variants rather than a modification of one process; also, some lag estimates for pairs involving A36/A37/A42/A43 in the later window rest on only one or two traces, so the exact lag structure of the new branch is uncertain.
