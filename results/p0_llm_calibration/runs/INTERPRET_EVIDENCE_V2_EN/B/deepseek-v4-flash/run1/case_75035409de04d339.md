### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A14 and A15 moved from an early position immediately after A35 to a later position after A20/A21, while A35 now goes directly to A20. This is shown directly by successor changes, order reversals, position shifts, and lag changes that all point in the same direction: A35–A20 become close, A35–A14/A15 become distant, and A14/A15–A22 become close. The exact new control flow is not fully resolved — for example, A21 and A22 also feed back into A14/A15, so the later window may contain a loop or alternative branch rather than a simple linear reordering. Sampling differences in variant frequencies cannot be completely excluded, but the number and consistency of the items make that an unlikely explanation for the whole pattern.
