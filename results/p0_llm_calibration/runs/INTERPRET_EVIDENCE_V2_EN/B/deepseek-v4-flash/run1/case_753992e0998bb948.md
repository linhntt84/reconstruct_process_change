### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which a group of activities (A27, A36, A37, A42, A43) become mandatory and occur earlier, with a new successor A28, while A31 moves later and A02 becomes more frequent with a new successor A15. The change is directly shown by participation, routing, position and order indicators that agree with each other. The number of qualifying items far exceeds the chance expectation (about 1 item expected, many observed), so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a random split artefact. A competing explanation is that the later window simply contains a different mix of trace variants, but the consistency across indicators and the large number of items make a structural modification the more plausible interpretation. Some individual items rest on few observations (e.g., LAG001 before support 1, LAG006 after support 1), but the main pattern is supported by many well-supported items.
