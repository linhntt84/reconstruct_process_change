### Observed differences

- In the later window, activity A10 is much less frequent: it appears in about 60% of traces before and about 24% after, and its mean occurrences per trace drop from 0.66 to 0.245. [OCC005]
- Two activities, A19 and A20, appear in the later window: A19 in about 48% of traces and A20 in about 52%, while they were essentially absent before (0% and 0.5%). [OCC006, OCC007]
- A19 and A20 never occur together: in the later window, about 48% of traces have A19 only and about 51.5% have A20 only, with no co-occurrence. [REL018]
- A10's outgoing behaviour changes: before, about 82% of A10 transitions went to A16; after, only about 33% go to A16, with increased shares to A15 (12%), A25 (16%), A46 (14%), and A44 (8%). [DIR004]

- A19 and A20 appear in the middle of traces: mean relative position around 0.51–0.52. [POS002, POS003]
- A10's co-occurrence with many activities (A15, A16, A25, A26, A27, A28, A40, A41, A45, A46) drops from about 60% to about 24%, and those activities increasingly appear without A10. [REL001, REL002, REL005-010, REL012-013]

- Lags between several activity pairs change: e.g., A10 to A16 was almost always lag 1 (95%) before, now only 56% at lag 1; A26 to A27 was 97.5% at lag 1, now 46% at lag 1 and 49.5% at lag 2; A27 to A28 was 98.8% at lag 1, now 51.5% at lag 1 and 47.5% at lag 2. [LAG001, LAG011, LAG013]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process now includes an exclusive choice between two new activities, A19 and A20, which appear in almost every case, while activity A10 is executed much less often and its position and successor distribution change. The direct signs are the appearance of A19 and A20 from zero participation, their mutual exclusivity, their consistent placement relative to other activities, and the large reduction in A10's frequency and co-occurrence. A competing explanation that sampling differences in variant frequencies could produce these patterns is unlikely given the large number of consistent items and the zero-before appearance of A19/A20. However, the evidence does not show a direct substitution between A10 and A19/A20 (e.g., no transitions from A10 to A19/A20 are observed), so it cannot rule out that A10's decrease and the introduction of A19/A20 are separate changes rather than a single replacement.
