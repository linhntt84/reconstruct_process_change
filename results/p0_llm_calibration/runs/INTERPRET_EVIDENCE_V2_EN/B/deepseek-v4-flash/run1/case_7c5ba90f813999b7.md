### Observed differences

- In the later window, A14 and A15 essentially disappear: A14 falls from about 49% of traces to 0%, and A15 from 51% to 1%. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001]
- A20 and A21 appear in about 99% of traces in the later window, up from 0% before. [OCC003, OCC004, REL010]
- A37 and A22 remain present in all traces in both windows. [DIR004, POS005, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- The routing from A37 changes: before, A37 went to A15 in about 51% and A14 in 49%; after, it goes to A20 in about 50% and A21 in 49%, with A15 nearly gone. [DIR004]
- The order changes: before, A37 was followed by either A14 or A15, then A22; after, A37 is followed by A20 and A21 (in either order, about half each), then A22. [REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL013, REL014]
- The gap between A37 and A22 widens from 2 steps to 3 steps in almost all cases. [LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the segment between A37 and A22 changed from a choice between two alternative activities (A14 or A15) to a segment containing both A20 and A21 in variable order. In the later window, A14 and A15 are almost absent, while A20 and A21 appear together in about 99% of traces, with either order, and A37 now routes to A20/A21 instead of A14/A15. The lag between A37 and A22 increases by one step, consistent with an additional activity in between. The evidence is direct and consistent across occurrence, routing, ordering, and lag indicators. A competing explanation that the activities were merely renamed is unlikely because A14 and A15 were mutually exclusive, whereas A20 and A21 co-occur; however, the evidence cannot rule out that A20 and A21 are new activities that replaced the old alternative branch, or that the order between A20 and A21 is genuinely variable rather than a fixed sequence. Sampling differences in variant frequencies are an unlikely explanation given the magnitude and consistency of the changes.
