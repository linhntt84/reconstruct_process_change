### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that a set of activities A33–A41 became part of the standard flow, appearing in nearly all traces, while A06 became rare and only appears together with that set. This is supported by the large increase in participation of A33–A41, the near-universal co-occurrence of A14, A15, A30, A31 with them, and the consistent changes in successor distributions and positions.
