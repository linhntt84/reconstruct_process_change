### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed so that after activity A20, the process now executes activities A25–A28 instead of A21–A24. This is directly shown by the routing change from A20 to A28 (DIR002) and by the consistent drop in participation of A21–A24 and rise of A25–A28 (OCC002–OCC009), as well as the relational co-occurrence changes (REL009, REL013). The large number of evidence items (88 vs 1.05 expected by chance) makes it unlikely that this is a random fluctuation. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed windows are just different samples of a process with two alternative branches, but the magnitude and consistency of the change across many indicators support a structural shift. The change in A08 is based on few observations and is not central to the hypothesis.
