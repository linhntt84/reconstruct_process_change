### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the branch through A28/A29 was moved later in the process, while A21 and A23 became an earlier common path. Specifically, A20 now always leads to A21 instead of branching to A28/A29; A23 now branches to A27 or to A28/A29; A28/A29 now lead to A24 instead of back to A21; and A27 no longer appears together with A28/A29. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL013, REL014] The consistency of the successor, ordering, co-occurrence and position indicators makes it unlikely that this is only a sampling artefact, although finite variant frequencies cannot be completely ruled out as a competing explanation.
