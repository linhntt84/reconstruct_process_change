### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured so that A14 and A15 are executed earlier, between A35 and A20, while A21–A22–A23 form a more direct chain. This is supported directly by the reversed order between A14/A15 and A20/A21/A22, the earlier positions of A14/A15, the new routing of A14/A15 to A20, and the new routing of A35 to A14/A15. The number of qualifying evidence items (31) is far above the 0.42 expected by chance, and the pattern is consistent across directional, positional, relational and lag indicators. A competing explanation that the windows are simply different samples of a stable process with rare variants cannot be fully excluded, but the consistency and volume of the evidence make that unlikely. Some individual lag items rest on very few traces, but the main pattern is supported by high-support items.
