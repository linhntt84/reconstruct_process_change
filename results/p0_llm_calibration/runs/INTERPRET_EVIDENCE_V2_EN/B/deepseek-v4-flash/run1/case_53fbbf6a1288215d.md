### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a branch of activities (A19, A21, A22, A23) that occurred in about a quarter of cases was replaced by a new branch (A20, A28, A29, A30) that now occurs in nearly all cases, inserted between A46 and A31. The consistency across occurrence, routing, position, and lag indicators points to a structural change rather than isolated fluctuations.
