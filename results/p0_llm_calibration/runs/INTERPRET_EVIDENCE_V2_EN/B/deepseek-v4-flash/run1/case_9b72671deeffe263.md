- In the later window, outgoing transitions from A16 and A17 are redistributed: about half now go to A25, whereas before they went almost entirely to A18 and A19. [DIR001, DIR002]
- Outgoing transitions from A18 and A19 are also redistributed: before they went almost entirely to A25, now about half still go to A25 and the rest go to A16 and A17. [DIR003, DIR004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the relative order of activities A16/A17 and A18/A19 changed in the later window: A16 and A17 now often directly precede A25, while A18 and A19 now sometimes precede A16/A17, and A24 now tends to precede A18/A19 rather than A16/A17. This is directly shown by the successor changes (DIR), the lag shifts (LAG), and the reversal of the A17–A19 order (REL). The number of qualifying items (15) far exceeds the 0.36 expected by chance, and the items are mutually consistent. A competing explanation—that the differences arise from sampling variation in variant frequencies—cannot be fully ruled out, but the consistency and volume of evidence make a structural change the more plausible interpretation.
