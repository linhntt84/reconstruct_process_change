### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured so that a block of activities A36, A37, A42 and A43 is now executed early in every case, before A28, A26, A27 and A31, and that A28 now follows this block rather than directly following A25. This is supported by the near-universal participation and occurrence of these activities, their earlier positions, the reversal of their order relative to A26, A28 and A31, the change in A25's successor from A28 to these activities, and the increased lag between A25 and A28. [OCC001-006, POS006-009, REL001-030, DIR001-006, LAG001] A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a change in the mix of trace variants rather than a change in the underlying process structure; however, the consistency across occurrence, position, order and successor indicators makes a structural change more plausible. Some lag estimates in the later window are based on very few traces (e.g., 1-3), so those specific distances are weak. [LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]
