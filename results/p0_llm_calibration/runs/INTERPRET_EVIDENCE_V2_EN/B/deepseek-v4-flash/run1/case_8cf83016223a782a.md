- In the later window, two activities that were completely absent before now appear: A14 occurs in about 44 in 100 traces and A15 in about 55 in 100 traces; neither ever repeats within a trace. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new step was inserted between A35 and A20 in the later window: A35's successor changed from A20 to either A14 or A15, and those new activities then lead to A20, increasing the A35-to-A20 gap from one to two steps. The signs are direct and consistent across successor shares, pairwise order, and lag. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the later window simply samples a different variant mix, but the coherence of the successor, ordering, and lag changes makes that unlikely to account for the whole pattern.
