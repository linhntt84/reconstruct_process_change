### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process was reordered: a set of activities (A19, A22, A25, A26) moved earlier in the trace, and A27 and A31 also moved earlier relative to A20, A21, A23 and A24, with a corresponding swap in successor relations between A27 and A32. The order reversals are shown directly by relational and positional indicators with full support in both windows, and the successor changes are consistent with the same reordering. The number of qualifying items (65) is far above the 0.8 expected by chance, so sampling variation in variant frequencies is an unlikely explanation. A limitation is that some lag-based items have very low support in one window, but the core order reversal is well established by the REL and POS items.
