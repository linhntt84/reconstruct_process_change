- In the later window, A14 and A15 occur much later in traces: their mean position rises from about 0.36 to about 0.66–0.67, while A16 and A17 occur much earlier, falling from about 0.66 to about 0.37. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the order of two activity groups swapped: A14 and A15 moved from an early position to a late position, while A16 and A17 moved from a late position to an early position, with A18, A22 and A23 remaining in the middle and at the end. This is shown directly by the reversed successor relations (A14/A15 now feed A23 instead of A18; A16/A17 now feed A18 instead of A23), by the large positional shifts, and by the relational reversals between the two groups. The number of qualifying evidence items is far above what an unchanged process would typically produce, and the different indicators agree on the same reordering. A competing explanation based only on sampling variation in variant frequencies is unlikely because the relational order changes are direct and consistent. A limitation is that some lag estimates involving A23 in the later window rest on very few traces, but the main conclusion does not depend on those weak items.
