### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed by replacing the middle block of activities A15, A23, A24, A25 with a new block A14, A16, A17, A18, while keeping A41 early and A26 late. The disappearance and appearance are complete and large, the successor and order relations are consistent with this replacement, and the lag between A41 and A26 shortened from 5 to 2 steps. [OCC001-OCC008, DIR001-DIR009, REL001-REL042, LAG001] A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the new activities are simply different labels for the same underlying steps, or that the process was redesigned with different activities; the evidence shows a change in observed activities and their relations, not the reason or semantic equivalence. Sampling differences in variant frequencies are unlikely to explain the magnitude, but the two windows are finite samples.
