### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural reordering rather than chance variation: the number of flagged items is many times larger than the 0.67 expected if nothing changed. The positional, relational, and successor indicators agree that A19 and A20 moved from an early position before A21/A22/A23/A27 to a later position after them, while A21 and A22 moved earlier; A28 became the successor of A19/A20 instead of A23, and A36 became less frequent. A competing explanation based solely on sampling differences in variant frequencies is unlikely given the consistency of the order reversals, though the underlying business reason is not identified.
