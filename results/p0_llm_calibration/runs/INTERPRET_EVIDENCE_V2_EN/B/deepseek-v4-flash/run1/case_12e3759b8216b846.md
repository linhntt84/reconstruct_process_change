### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the process has shifted its main branch after activity A20: instead of predominantly routing through A21→A22 (and then to A28), it now predominantly routes through A23→A24 (and then to A28). The shift is large (about 56–58 percentage points) and consistent across occurrence, routing, and relational indicators. The number of qualifying evidence items (22) far exceeds the 0.47 expected by chance, so the overall pattern is unlikely to be a sampling artifact. A competing explanation of random variation in variant frequencies is largely ruled out by the consistency and magnitude, though the two windows are finite samples and the later-window support for the A21/A22 routing details is based on only about 45 traces.
