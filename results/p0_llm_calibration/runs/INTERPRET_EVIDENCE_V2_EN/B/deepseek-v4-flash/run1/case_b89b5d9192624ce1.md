### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, activities A19 and A20 are no longer executed as intermediate steps between A40 and A25, and A40 now leads directly to A25. This is directly shown by the successor distribution of A40 (A25 replacing A19/A20), the lag shift from two steps to one step between A40 and A25, and the disappearance of the A40-before-A19/A20 and A19/A20-before-A25 relations.
