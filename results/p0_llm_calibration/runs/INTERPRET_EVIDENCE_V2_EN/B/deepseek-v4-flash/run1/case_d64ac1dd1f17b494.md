### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A19, A20, A25 and A26 appear in about 24%, 21%, 31% and 26% of traces respectively, while they were absent from the earlier window. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a set of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26) was introduced into the process, inserted between the A14/A15 segment and the A28/A29/A30 segment, and that A04 became a more common early step leading to A15. The new activities appear only in the later window with substantial participation, sit around the middle of traces, route to A29/A30, and are reached from A28 and A29; lag distances between A28, A29 and A30 increase by one step, and A14 now sometimes routes to A04, which then leads to A15. The volume and consistency of the indicators make a structural difference the most plausible interpretation, although the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows come from different process variants or from a heterogeneous population rather than a change over time; the exact ordering among the new activities is also not fully fixed, with some interleaving observed.
