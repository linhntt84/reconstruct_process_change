### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process changed from having a single middle activity chosen from A14, A16, A17 or A18 between A41 and A26, to having all four activities A15, A23, A24 and A25 between A41 and A26. In the later window, every trace contains A15, A23, A24 and A25, all after A41 and before A26, while the earlier middle activities are absent; the lag between A41 and A26 increases from 2 to 5 positions, consistent with a longer middle segment. The change is consistent across occurrence, direction, position and lag indicators. A competing explanation that the two windows simply sample different process variants cannot be fully excluded, but the complete disappearance of one set of activities and the universal appearance of the other in 200-trace windows makes sampling variation an unlikely sole explanation.
