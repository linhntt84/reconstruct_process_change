### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A34, A35, A36 and A37 each appear in about half of traces instead of every trace: A34 from 100% to about 56%, A35 to 56%, A36 to 47%, A37 to 47%. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]

- A02, A04 and A08 appear slightly more often: A02 from about 3% to 13%, A04 from 1% to 8.5%, A08 from 1% to 7.5%. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004]
- Outgoing routing from A30 changed: before it went almost only to A34 (55%) and A35 (45%); after it goes to A36 (25%), A37 (19%), A35 (27%), A34 (27%) and several small targets. [DIR003]
- Outgoing routing from A34 and A35 changed: before A34 went mainly to A35, A36 and A37; after A34 goes to A31 (50%) and A35 (48%), with A36/A37 almost absent. A35 similarly goes to A31 (46%) and A34 (48%) instead of A36/A37. [DIR004, DIR005]

- Relations confirm that A34–A37 are often absent together with A06: for example, A06 and A34 co-occur in 30% of traces before vs 6.5% after, and both are absent in 41% after (0 before). [REL005, REL007]
- A15, A16, A30 and A31 remain present in all traces, but their co-occurrence with A34–A37 drops correspondingly. [REL009, REL017, REL021]
- Positional lags changed: A30 before A31 changed from typically 5 steps to 3 steps; A30 before A36/A37 changed from 3–4 steps to 1–2 steps; A34/A35 before A31 changed from 3–4 steps to 1–2 steps. [LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG008]
- A15 and A16 occur slightly later in the trace (mean position up by about 0.02), and A30/A34/A35 also later by about 0.05–0.06. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that, in the later window, a block of activities (A34, A35, A36, A37, and to a lesser extent A06) is executed less often, and the routing around A30, A34 and A35 is rearranged. These activities were present in every trace before and in only about half of traces after; A06 dropped from 30% to 10% participation. The routing changes are consistent with this: A30 now often leads to A36/A37 instead of A34/A35, and A34/A35 now often lead to A31 instead of A36/A37. The positional lags also show A31 is now closer to A30/A34/A35. The changes are large and appear across many indicators, so they are unlikely to be a single rare variant. [OCC003, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, REL005, REL007, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG008]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that the two windows sample different case variants rather than a change over time; the later window could contain a mix of cases that skip this block. The exact cause or business meaning of the change is not identifiable from this evidence. Some order-related details, especially lags involving A36/A37, rest on few traces after the change and are therefore less certain. [LAG007, LAG009]
