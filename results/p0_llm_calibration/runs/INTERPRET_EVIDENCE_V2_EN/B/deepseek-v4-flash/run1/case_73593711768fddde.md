### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a new alternative path was introduced after A21, bypassing A22 and going through A27/A28 to A23. In the later window, about 55% of cases take this new path, while the remaining 45% still follow the original A21→A22→A23 sequence. This is directly shown by the drop in A22 participation, the appearance of A27 and A28, the redistribution of A21's outgoing edges, and the relational and lag changes. The large number of consistent indicators (21 items vs. 0.32 expected by chance) rules out chance as a whole. A competing explanation of random sampling differences in variant frequencies is unlikely given the magnitude and coherence of the changes, but the evidence cannot confirm the cause or exact structure of the new path (e.g., whether A27 and A28 always appear in a fixed order).
