### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window reflects a more standardized sequence: A30 → A34/A35/A36/A37 → A31/A43 → A10 → A44/A45/A46, with A10 becoming more frequent and placed later, and A43 now feeding A10 instead of A44. This is supported directly by the large increases in participation of A34–A37 and A10, the consistent shifts in outgoing transitions (A10 to A44, A43 to A10, A30 to A34/A35, A34/A35 to A36/A37), and the relational order changes showing A30 before A34–A37, A34–A37 before A10, and A10 before A44–A46. The number of qualifying evidence items (82) far exceeds the expected count under no change (about 1), so the overall pattern cannot be attributed to chance. A competing explanation—that variant frequency differences alone could produce these patterns—is largely ruled out by the direct relational and positional evidence, though sampling variation in rare variants cannot be fully excluded.
