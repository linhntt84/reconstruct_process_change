### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the process moved from two alternative branches — one containing A22 and one containing A31/A32/A37/A38 — to a single sequence that includes all of these activities. In the later window, A20 no longer leads to A23; instead it leads to the four activities A31/A32/A37/A38, which are then followed by A23, A21, A22 and A26. This is directly shown by the successor changes, the reversal of order between A21 and the four activities, the universal participation of previously optional activities, and the lag changes. The large number of qualifying evidence items (52 vs. 0.58 expected by chance) makes a sampling artefact unlikely. A limitation is that the exact order among the four activities A31/A32/A37/A38 is not fully determined, and the change could be described as a reordering of existing activities rather than the addition or removal of activities, but the relational and positional evidence directly demonstrates the reordering.
