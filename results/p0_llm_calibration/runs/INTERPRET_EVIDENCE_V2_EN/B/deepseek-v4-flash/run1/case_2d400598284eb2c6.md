### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which a new path is introduced: A14 → A02 → A15, and new activities A32 and A33 appear, often leading to A28, while A27 is skipped in about half the cases. The change is directly shown by occurrence increases/decreases, routing shifts, positional moves, and relational/lag patterns that all point to the same reorganisation. The main competing explanation—that the later window simply contains a different mix of pre-existing variants—is unlikely given the large number of indicators (56 items vs 0.72 expected by chance) and their consistency. The evidence cannot, however, reveal the cause or business meaning of the change.
