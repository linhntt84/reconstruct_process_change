### Observed differences

- In the later window, A02 appears in about 20% of traces instead of 65%, and its mean occurrences per trace drop from about 0.70 to 0.22. Its position shifts from near the end of the trace (mean 0.92) to the middle (mean 0.55). [OCC001, POS001]
- A06 and A10 become more frequent: A06 rises from about 7% to 18% of traces, and A10 from about 6% to 17%. [OCC002, OCC003]
- A02's outgoing behaviour changes sharply: before, about 93% of A02 transitions went to A46; after, only about 14% go to A46, with A44, A15 and A20 becoming more common successors. [DIR001]
- A02 co-occurs with A14, A15, A29, A30, A45 and A46 in 65% of traces before, but only about 20% after. In those pairs, the other activity occurs without A02 in about 80% of traces after, versus 35% before. For A02–A46, A02 before A46 drops from about 60% to 18%; for A02–A45, A45 before A02 drops from about 60% to 3%. [REL001, REL002, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]
- The lag between A02 and A46 lengthens: before, 99% of A02–A46 pairs were one step apart; after, only 32% are one step apart, with lags of 3–5 steps appearing. [LAG003]
- In the A21/A22/A23/A24/A29/A30 cluster, successor choices are redistributed. A21 and A22, which mostly led to A23/A24 before, now often lead to A30. A23 and A24, which almost always led to A30 before, now also lead to A21 and A22. [DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008]
- Lag distances in this cluster shift by one step: A29–A21 changes from mostly lag 1 to a mix of lag 1 and lag 2; A29–A23 and A29–A24 change from mostly lag 2 to a mix of lag 1 and lag 2. [LAG009, LAG011, LAG012]
- A45–A46 lag changes from mostly lag 2 (64%) to mostly lag 1 (85%). [LAG013]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which A02 is executed much less often and, when it is executed, occurs earlier and no longer directly precedes A46 or A45; instead, A46 and A45 are reached without A02 in most cases. This is supported by the large drop in A02 participation, the change in A02's position, the collapse of A02–A46 and A02–A45 co-occurrence, and the lengthened lag between A02 and A46. [OCC001, POS001, REL010, LAG003, DIR001]

The evidence also supports a re-routing in the A21/A22/A23/A24/A29/A30 segment: A30 becomes a successor of A21 and A22, while A21 and A22 become successors of A23 and A24, with lag distances shifting by one step. This is shown consistently by successor distributions and lag changes for these activities. [DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012]

A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the later window contains a different mix of case variants in which A02 is simply absent more often, rather than a deliberate reordering. However, the positional and lag changes among traces that still contain A02, together with the consistent successor changes, make a simple sampling difference unlikely. [POS001, LAG003, DIR001]
