### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured so that a branch involving A32 and A33 was removed or replaced, A27 became a near-universal step, A02 became less central, and A04, A06 and A10 became more frequent. The signs are consistent across occurrence, routing, relational, positional and lag indicators: A32 and A33 disappear from almost all traces, A27 becomes universal and precedes many other activities, and A02's role shrinks and shifts later. A competing explanation that the differences are only sampling variation in variant frequencies is unlikely given the large number of qualifying items and their consistency. A limitation is that A32 and A33 have very few observations in the later window, so their exact remaining behaviour cannot be characterised, but their near-absence is corroborated by many relational pairs.
