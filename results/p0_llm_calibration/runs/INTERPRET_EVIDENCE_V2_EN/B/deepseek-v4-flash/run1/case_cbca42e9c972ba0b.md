### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which a block of activities (A30, A31, A36, A37) is executed repeatedly—about three times per case instead of once—and is interleaved with A15 and A16 rather than strictly following them. The number of qualifying evidence items (39) far exceeds the 0.68 expected by chance, and the pattern is consistent across occurrence, position, relation, lag, and successor indicators. A competing explanation—that the differences arise from sampling variation in variant frequencies—is unlikely given the magnitude and consistency of the changes. The evidence cannot, however, rule out that the underlying modification is a different structural change that produces the same trace behaviour, such as a new repetition that also includes A15/A16.
