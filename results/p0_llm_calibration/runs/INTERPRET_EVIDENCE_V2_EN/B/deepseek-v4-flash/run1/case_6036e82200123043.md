### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A26 and A27 occur later in traces: the mean position of A26 rose from about 0.51 to 0.62 of the trace, and A27 from about 0.37 to 0.48. [POS001, POS002]
- Activities A35, A36, A41 and A42 occur earlier: their mean positions dropped from about 0.58 to about 0.42. [POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006]
- The order between these two groups reversed. Before, A26 and A27 preceded A35/A36/A41/A42 in about 97–98% of cases; after, they follow them in about 96–97% of cases. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]
- Successor shares changed sharply. A25's main successor went from A27 (about 97.5%) to a mix of A42, A35, A41 and A36 (each about 25%). A26's main successor went from a mix of A41/A35/A42/A36 (each about 25%) to A30 (about 93.5%). [DIR001, DIR002]
- A30 is no longer a common successor of A35, A36, A41 or A42; instead A27 appears as a new successor for these activities. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- Lag distances changed accordingly: A25–A27 went from mostly 1 step apart to mostly 5 steps apart, while A26–A30 went from 5 steps apart to 1 step apart. [LAG001, LAG002]
- Several lag items for pairs involving A26, A35, A41 or A42 with A30 have very few supporting traces in the later window (4–5 traces), so those specific lag estimates are weak. [LAG003, LAG004, LAG006, LAG009, LAG010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which activities A26 and A27 moved later in the process, after the group A35/A36/A41/A42, and A30 now directly follows A26. This is supported by consistent positional shifts, a reversal of the relational order between the two groups, and matching successor changes. The main limitation is that some lag items have very low support in the later window, so the exact new ordering among the later group is not fully pinned down. Sampling variation in variant frequencies is an unlikely explanation given the number and consistency of the items, but it cannot be entirely excluded for the low-support lag measurements.
