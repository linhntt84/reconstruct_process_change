### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which a new activity, A06, is inserted early in the process, and a re‑routing occurs: many activities (A19, A20, A25, A26) that previously led to A33 or A29 now lead to A27, while A46 no longer leads to A27 but instead to A25, A26, A19 and A20. The order of A19/A20/A25/A26 relative to A27/A28 also flips, consistent with these activities moving earlier. The number of qualifying evidence items (53) is far above the 0.78 expected by chance, and the pattern is consistent across occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators. A competing explanation is that the two windows simply contain different mixes of process variants; however, the breadth and consistency of the differences make a genuine structural change the more plausible interpretation. Some lag items involving A06 rest on very few before‑window observations (1–2 traces), so those specific lag estimates are weak, but they are not needed for the main conclusion.
