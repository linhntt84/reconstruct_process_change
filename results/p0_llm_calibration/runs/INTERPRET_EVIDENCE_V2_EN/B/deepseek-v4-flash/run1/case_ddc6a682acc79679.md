### Observed differences

- In the later window, activities A14 and A15 appear in about half of the traces (48% and 51%), whereas they were completely absent before. [OCC001, OCC002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the pair of activities A20 and A21 was replaced by a single activity, either A14 or A15, placed between A37 and A22. The signs are consistent across participation (A14/A15 appear, A20/A21 nearly vanish), routing (A37 now leads to A14/A15 instead of A20/A21, and A14/A15 lead to A22), relations (A37 before A14/A15 before A22), and lag (the A37-to-A22 distance drops from 3 to 2 steps). A competing explanation—that A14/A15 are merely rare variants—is unlikely given they appear in about half of the later traces, while A20/A21 drop from 100% to about 1%.
