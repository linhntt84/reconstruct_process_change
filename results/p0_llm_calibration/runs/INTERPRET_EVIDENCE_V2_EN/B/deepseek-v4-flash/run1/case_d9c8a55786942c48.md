### Observed differences

- In the later window, the outgoing transitions of several activities became much more concentrated. For example, A24 went from having five successors (A34 45%, A26 20%, A27 15%, A28 12%, A25 9%) to almost only A25 (99%); A26 went from A34 55%, A24 20%, A20 12%, etc. to A34 100%; A33 went from A23 54%, A25 46% to A23 99.5%. [DIR003, DIR005, DIR008]
- The relative order of activities changed: for pairs like (A20,A25), (A21,A25), (A24,A25), (A24,A27), the share where the first activity precedes the second rose from about 10-50% to 97-99%, and the share where the second precedes the first fell to 0%. For example, A24 before A25 went from 9% to 97.5%; A20 before A25 from 28.5% to 98.5%. [REL001, REL010, REL004]
- Positions shifted: A20, A21, A23, A24 moved earlier (mean position decreased by about 0.02-0.08 of trace length), while A25, A26, A27, A28 moved later (mean increased by about 0.03-0.08). [POS001-POS008]
- Distances between activities changed: for some pairs (e.g., A20-A21, A23-A20, A25-A27, A27-A26) the lag became mostly 1 or 2, while for others (e.g., A20-A26, A24-A26, A24-A34, A33-A25) the lag increased to 3-5. [LAG001, LAG013, LAG024, LAG028, LAG004, LAG019, LAG022, LAG042]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that the process was restructured so that a set of activities (A20, A21, A23, A24) now consistently occur before another set (A25, A26, A27, A28), with more deterministic successor relations, whereas before the order was mixed and successors were more varied. This is supported by the consistent changes in successor distributions (DIR), relative order (REL), position shifts (POS), and lag distances (LAG). The large number of evidence items (71) far exceeds the expected number under no change (0.86), making chance an unlikely explanation. A competing explanation that cannot be fully ruled out is that the observed differences reflect a change in the mix of case variants rather than a structural change, but the consistency across multiple independent indicators makes this less plausible.
