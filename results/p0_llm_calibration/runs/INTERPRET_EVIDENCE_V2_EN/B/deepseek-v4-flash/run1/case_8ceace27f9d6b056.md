### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process was restructured to include a new set of activities (A19, A20, A25, A26) that are executed after A42 and before A27, while A06 becomes more frequently executed and appears earlier in the trace, and A08 becomes less frequent. This is supported by the appearance of these activities in the later window, their routing to A27, the routing of A42 to them, and the relational/lag data showing the order A42 → new activities → A27, as well as A14 → A06 → A15. The main competing explanation, that these differences arise from sampling variation in variant frequencies, is unlikely given the large number of consistent indicators, but cannot be entirely ruled out.
