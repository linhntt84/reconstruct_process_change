### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a structural change in which the later window largely removes A32 and A33 from the main flow, makes A27 followed by A28 a near-universal step, and adds or expands A06 as an additional step that occurs before A27/A28 and after A14/A40. The change is directly visible in occurrence, successor, relative-order and lag indicators, and the number of qualifying items (53) far exceeds the 0.75 expected by chance. A competing explanation that the differences are only sampling variation in variant frequencies is unlikely given the consistency across many indicators, although the exact replacement edges for A32 and A33 rest on only one trace each.
