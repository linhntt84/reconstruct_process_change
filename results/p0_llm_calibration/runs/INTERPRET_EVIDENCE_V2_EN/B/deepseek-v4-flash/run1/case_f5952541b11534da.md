### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a hypothesis that, in the later window, A27 is executed earlier in the trace and often without the preceding A19/A20 activities, while A19, A20, A25 and A26 are executed in fewer traces. The supporting signs are direct and consistent: A27 appears alone in a large share of traces where it previously co-occurred with A19 or A20, the lag from A42 to A27 shifts to much shorter values, A27's position moves earlier, and A42's successor distribution changes from predominantly A19 to a mix of A19 and A27. A competing explanation that the differences merely reflect sampling variation in variant frequencies is unlikely given the large number of consistent indicators, but the evidence cannot rule out that some of the decrease in A19/A20 participation is due to a change in case mix rather than a modification of the process itself.
