### Observed differences

- The starkest difference is a reversal of execution order between the activities A32/A33 on one side and A26/A28 on the other. In the earlier window, A32 ran before A26 in about 99 in 100 traces and before A28 in essentially all of them; A33 ran before A26 in about 98 in 100 and before A28 in about 99 in 100. In the later window this flipped: A26 ran before A32 in about 98 in 100 traces and before A33 in about 97 in 100, and A28 ran before A32 in about 98 in 100 and before A33 in about 98 in 100. All four activities still occurred together in every trace in both windows, so their participation did not change. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]
- Direct successors changed accordingly. A25's outgoing transitions went from roughly 55 in 100 to A32 and 44 in 100 to A33 in the earlier window to about 96 in 100 to A26 in the later window. A28's outgoing transitions went from about 98 in 100 to A29 to roughly 49 in 100 to A32 and 49 in 100 to A33. A32's transitions to A26 fell from about 44 in 100 to about 1 in 100 while transitions to A29 rose to about 47 in 100; A33's transitions to A26 fell from about 54 in 100 to about 1 in 100 while transitions to A29 reached about 49 in 100. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]

From A25 to A26 the typical gap shrank from 3 steps (about 96 in 100) to 1 step (about 96 in 100); from A25 to A28 it shrank from 5 steps (all observed pairs) to 3 steps (about 95 in 100); from A25 to A32 and A25 to A33 it grew from 1–2 steps to 4–5 steps; from A26 to A29 it grew from 3 to 5 steps; from A28 to A29 it grew from 1 step (about 98 in 100) to 3 steps (about 95 in 100); and from A32/A33 to A29 it shrank from 4–5 steps to 1–2 steps. - The internal order of A32 and A33 shows no fixed sequence in either window: before, about 55 in 100 of A32's outgoing transitions went to A33 while about 43 in 100 of A33's went to A32; after, the split is about 50 versus 49 in 100, with A33 immediately preceding A32 when that order occurs. Its routing loosened: about 89 in 100 of its outgoing transitions went to A15 before versus about 38 in 100 after, spread over many activities (A19 about 12 in 100, about 13 others roughly 4 in 100 each); A14 routed to A02 in about 20 in 100 of its transitions before versus about 5 in 100 after, while A14 to A15 rose from about 79 to 90 in 100. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block of activities A32 and A33 changed its place in the sequence: in the earlier window the two ran between A25 and A26 (A25 → A32/A33 → A26 … A28 → A29), whereas in the later window they run between A28 and A29 (A25 → A26 … A28 → A32/A33 → A29). Participation is unchanged — A25, A26, A28, A29, A32 and A33 still occur in essentially every trace, and A32/A33 still co-occur with A26 and A28 in all traces. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]

The supporting signs are direct and mutually consistent: the order between the moving pair and A26/A28 reverses almost completely across all four activity pairs; the successor changes at A25, A28, A32 and A33 match the relocation; every measured distance shifts in the direction the relocation predicts; and the position shifts of all four activities agree. The volume of flagged evidence also exceeds what an unchanged process produces: 26 items qualified against roughly 0.62 expected by chance, so the set as a whole is not a chance artefact. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, LAG001, LAG002, LAG011]

Limitations: the evidence uses event positions and order relations, not clock time, and it cannot rule out that both arrangements already existed as variants, with the late-block variant rare (at most about 2 in 100 traces) before and dominant after — an extreme shift in how often existing paths are taken rather than a rewiring. The near-total flips (the new order in 0 of 200 traces before; the old order in about 2–4 of 200 after) make a plain share shift between two common paths unlikely. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]

Other differences: A02 participates less often (about 23 in 100 traces before, 14 in 100 after), appears at later positions, and its close succession with A15 and A14 loosened, as described above. These figures rest on few observations (A02 in about 26–27 traces and about 14 pair observations in the later window), so they could equally reflect sampling variation around a rare behaviour rather than a second structural change. [POS001, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG002]
