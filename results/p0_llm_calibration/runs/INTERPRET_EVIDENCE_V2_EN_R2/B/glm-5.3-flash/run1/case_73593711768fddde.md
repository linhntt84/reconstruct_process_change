### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, roughly 55 in 100 traces replace the previously mandatory A22 step between A21 and A23 with the pair A27 and A28, while the remaining roughly 45 in 100 traces keep the original A21 → A22 → A23 sequence. A22 and the pair A27/A28 behave as mutually exclusive alternatives, and A27 and A28 always run together with a mixed internal order. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001, REL001, REL005, REL006, REL009, LAG001]

The support is direct and consistent: 19 evidence items were flagged where fewer than 1 would be expected by chance in an unchanged process; routing out of A21, the relations of A22 and of A27/A28 with A21 and A23, and the A21–A23 distance all show the same structure, and each difference is larger than in virtually all random re-splits of the pooled data. Note that many of these items describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [DIR001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL007, REL008, LAG001, POS001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show what determines the choice between the two routes — in particular, it cannot distinguish a per-case branch from a shift in the mix of case types between the windows — and it cannot establish whether the A22 route persists beyond this sample. [DIR001, REL005, REL009]

Other differences: A18 participation decreased (about 59 in 100 traces to about 41 in 100) and A19 increased (about 42 in 100 to about 60 in 100); the evidence contains no relational or routing data on these two activities, so their connection to the main change remains unknown. [OCC001, OCC002]
