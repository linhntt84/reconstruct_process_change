### Observed differences

- The sharpest difference is the branch choice after A26. In the earlier window, about 47 in 100 of A26's outgoing transitions went to A27 and about 52 in 100 to A32 or A33; in the later window, about 98 in 100 go to A27, and transitions to A32 or A33 are no longer observed (200 vs 201 outgoing transitions behind these shares) [DIR005].
- A27 is now executed in every one of the 200 later traces, against about 95 of 200 earlier; A32 appears in about 2 of 200 later traces (about 107 of 200 earlier) and A33 in about 1 of 200 (about 105 of 200 earlier) [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007].
- In the earlier window, A27 and A32/A33 were mutually exclusive alternatives: A27 co-occurred with A32 in only about 1 in 100 traces and with A33 in none, while A32 and A33 appeared together in about 53 in 100 traces. In the later window, A32 and A33 essentially never occur, alone or together [REL029, REL030, REL036].
- The backbone A14, A15, A26, A28, A40, A45 and A46 runs in all traces in both windows, but in the later window it is always accompanied by A27 (co-occurrence in 100 of 100 traces, with A14 before A27 in about 98 of 100 and A40 before A27 in about 98 of 100), whereas earlier A27 accompanied it in only about 48 of 100 traces; the event distance from A26 to A28 is now almost always two positions (about 95 in 100) instead of a mix of two and three [REL016, REL019, REL025, REL028, REL031, REL032, REL033, LAG011].
- A02 has become largely detached. It appears in about 24 of 100 later traces, down from about 60 of 100; earlier, whenever A02 appeared, A14, A15, A26, A28, A40, A45 and A46 all appeared with it, while in the later window they usually run without it (about 76 of 100 traces). When A02 does occur, it sits later in the trace (mean relative position about 0.20 vs 0.36), A14 now passes to A15 in about 77 of 100 of its outgoing transitions (about 42 of 100 earlier) rather than to A02 (about 58 of 100 earlier, now about 12 of 100), and A02's own successors are spread over many activities instead of about 94 of 100 going to A15 (now about 45 of 100) [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR003, REL002, REL003, REL005, REL007, REL010, REL011, REL012].
- A04, A06 and A10 now each appear in about 20 of 100 traces, up from about 4–6 of 100 [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004].
- A13 and A20, which earlier often occurred separately from A27, now always occur together with it; A13's participation rose from about 47 to about 55 of 100 traces, while A20's stayed at about half [REL001, REL004, REL013, REL022].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the branch through A32 and A33, which in the earlier window was taken in about half of the cases as an alternative to the branch through A27, is in the later window essentially no longer taken, while the A27 branch is taken in every case; at the split point A26, the share continuing to A27 rose from about 47 in 100 to about 98 in 100. The signs are direct and consistent: participation and routing shares backed by roughly 200 observations per window, the earlier mutual exclusivity of A27 with A32/A33, and a stable backbone that now always includes A27. With 77 qualifying items against roughly one expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, DIR005, REL016, REL025, REL028, REL029, REL030, REL036].

Limitations: A32 and A33 were still observed in 2 and 1 later traces, so the branch is rare rather than demonstrably removed, and the successors observed after those rare executions rest on only 2 and 1 transitions. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of cases reaching the process — different kinds of cases taking different existing paths — rather than a change in routing; both would look identical in these windows [DIR006, DIR007].

Other differences: A02 now participates in about 24 of 100 traces instead of about 60 of 100, occurs later within traces, and is no longer a regular companion of the A14/A15/A26/A28/A40/A45/A46 backbone (which still occurs in every trace in both windows); A04, A06 and A10 now appear in about 20 of 100 traces each instead of about 4–6 of 100; A13 and A20 have become fixed companions of A27 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS001, DIR001, DIR003, REL001, REL004, REL013, REL022]. These differences are consistent with the same re-weighting of existing paths, but the evidence does not show directly how they connect to the branch shift.
