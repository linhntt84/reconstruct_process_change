### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the two previously alternative branches — one containing A27, the other containing the jointly executed block A36, A37, A42 and A43 — are executed together in the later window as one sequence in every trace: the block runs earlier in the case (between A25 and A28), A26 runs after the block and before A27, A27 runs in every trace after A26, and A25 reaches A28 only indirectly through the block. The supporting signs are direct and consistent: participation of all five activities rose from 93–108 to 200 of 200 traces; A27–block co-occurrence rose from 0–1 in 200 to 200 in 200 with a fixed order; the order of A26 and A28 relative to the block flipped in the same direction in every measured pair; the routing of A25, A26 and the block members and the A25→A28 distance changed accordingly; and 82 qualifying evidence items against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process makes the overall pattern incompatible with random re-splits of a stable process. [REL032, REL033, REL034, REL035, REL026, REL029, REL036, REL039, REL025, DIR003, DIR004, LAG004, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007]

Limitations: the items overlap heavily because many describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations; the internal order of A36/A37/A42/A43 remains mixed in both windows, and the exact adjacency of A28 and A26 is inferred from event positions rather than a directly measured pair. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that a rare combined variant already existed earlier (at most about 2 in 100 traces, since 0 of 200 showed it), although such a variant could not produce the later 100% co-occurrence by re-weighting the previously observed paths alone. [REL033, REL034, REL035, REL044, REL047, POS004, POS006, LAG004]
