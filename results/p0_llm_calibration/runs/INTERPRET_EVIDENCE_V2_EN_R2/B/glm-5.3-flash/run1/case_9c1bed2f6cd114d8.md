### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block containing A19 and A20 and the block containing A21 and A22 exchanged their order around the unchanged A23 → A27 segment: earlier the flow ran A38 → A19 or A20 → A23 → A27 → A21 and A22 (in either order) → A28, and in the later window it runs A38 → A21 and A22 (in either order) → A23 → A27 → A19 or A20 → A28. All four moved activities shifted by roughly 0.28–0.31 of trace length, and the boundary activities rerouted their outgoing transitions exactly as this exchange implies. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR008]

The reading rests on direct relational evidence: the order flips are near-deterministic (about 98–99 in 100, with 200 traces behind each window), and participation is unchanged (A21, A22, A23 and A27 ran in every trace in both windows; A19 and A20 kept complementary shares of about half the traces each), so the same activities and paths occur and only their order and routing shares changed. The 46 flagged items also far exceed the roughly 0.67 expected by chance across the 135 tested units, so sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [REL009, REL011, POS003, POS004, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: the outgoing transitions of A23 and A28 were not profiled, so the exact edges around the middle segment are inferred from order, position and distance evidence rather than observed directly; positions use event order, not clock time; and many items restate the same underlying exchange, so they are not independent confirmations. The evidence also cannot rule out additional changes in unprofiled parts of the process. [DIR001, DIR008, POS005, LAG001]

Other differences: the A36–A37 loop is traversed less often in the later window (A36 in about 13 in 100 traces vs about 24 earlier; A37 continuing to A38 in about 72 vs 56 in 100 of its outgoing transitions), a change in how often an existing repetition is taken that is separate from the block exchange. [OCC001, DIR007, LAG015]
