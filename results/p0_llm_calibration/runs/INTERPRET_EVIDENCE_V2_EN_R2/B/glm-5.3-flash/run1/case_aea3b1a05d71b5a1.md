### Observed differences

- The relative order of the A27/A28 pair versus A21 and A23 reversed. Before, A21 ran before A27 and before A28 in all 200 traces, and A23 also ran before both in all traces; in the later window, A27 precedes A21 in about 99 in 100 traces, A28 precedes A21 in about 99 in 100, and the same reversal holds for A23 against A27 and A28. All six activities still occur together in every trace in both windows. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]
- Successors changed at the boundaries of this segment. Before, every observed outgoing transition of A20 went to A21; in the later window about 50 in 100 go to A27 and about 49 in 100 to A28, with only about 2 in 100 still going to A21. Before, A23 led to A27 (about 54 in 100) or A28 (about 46 in 100); in the later window it leads almost always directly to A24 (about 99 in 100). A27 and A28, which previously led only to each other and to A24, now lead about half the time to A21. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- A27 and A28 now appear earlier in the trace (mean relative position about 0.55 before versus about 0.45 in the later window), while A21 (about 0.43 to 0.50) and A23 (about 0.50 to 0.57) appear later. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]
- Step distances changed exactly as an exchange of the two segments implies: A20 reaches A21 in 3 steps instead of 1 and A23 in 5 instead of 3; A23 reaches A24 in 1 step instead of 3, and A21 reaches A24 in 3 instead of 5; A20 reaches A27 and A28 within 1–2 steps, while A27 and A28 reach A24 only after 4–5 steps instead of 1–2. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG008, LAG011, LAG012]
- The internal structure of both segments is preserved: A27 and A28 still run in either order in roughly equal shares before and after, and the spacing between A21 and A23 stays two positions. [LAG003, LAG004, LAG009, LAG010, LAG001, LAG002]
- A small residue of the old order remains: in about 3 of 200 later-window traces A20 is still directly followed by A21 and A21 still precedes A27/A28; the distance items behind this residue rest on only 3 observations each. [DIR001, REL001, LAG006, LAG007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block formed by A27 and A28, which execute one after the other in either order, was repositioned from between A23 and A24 to between A20 and A21: before, the observed flow ran A20 → A21 → A23 → {A27/A28} → A24, while in the later window it runs A20 → {A27/A28} → A21 → A23 → A24. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]

The support is direct and consistent: the order reversal is observed directly on all four activity pairs spanning the two segments while co-occurrence stays complete; the successor changes sit exactly at the four boundary activities (A20, A23, A27, A28); and the position and distance shifts match the relocation quantitatively, with A23→A24 shortening from 3 steps to 1 while A20→A21 lengthens from 1 to 3. The unchanged either-order character of the A27/A28 block and the unchanged A21–A23 spacing indicate that an existing segment was moved rather than paths being replaced, and with 20 qualifying items against roughly 0.41 expected if nothing had changed, sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG008, LAG011, LAG012]

Limitations: the evidence shows only relative order among the six observed activities, so it cannot distinguish the block moving earlier from the A21–A23 segment moving later, nor what happens between A21 and A23; and about 3 in 200 later-window traces still follow the old order, so a small old-order variant may persist rather than being pure sampling residue. Positions are event positions, not clock time, so no conclusion about timing is possible. [DIR001, REL001, LAG006, LAG007]
