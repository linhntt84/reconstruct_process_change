- A02, A19 and A20 all take part in far fewer traces in the later window: A02 appears in about 30 in 100 traces earlier versus about 9 in 100 later; A19 falls from about 50 to about 26 in 100; A20 falls from about 51 to about 28 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]

- The gap between A40 and A25 narrowed: A25 followed A40 mostly 2-3 events later in the earlier window (about 73 in 100 at lag 2, 26 at lag 3), and mostly 1-2 events later in the later window (about 44 at lag 1, 52 at lag 2). [LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility of a shift in how often existing paths are taken: the branch running from A40, optionally through A02, to the A19/A20 alternative and on to A25 is executed far less often in the later window, while A40 is now frequently followed directly by A25. Earlier, essentially every trace contained one of A19/A20 and A40 typically preceded them; in the later window about 47 in 100 traces contain neither, A02 takes part in only about 9 in 100 traces, and A25 is A40's most common observed successor at 1-2 events distance instead of 2-3. Twenty-one items qualified against roughly 0.54 expected if nothing had changed, and although several items describe the same underlying trace behaviour, the participation, routing, relational and lag evidence all point the same way, and the unchanged orderings where activities still co-occur fit a frequency shift rather than a reordering. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, REL005, DIR003, LAG004, REL006, REL007, REL008, REL009]

A limitation: the direct A40-to-A25 transition was not among A40's observed outgoing transitions in the earlier window, so the evidence cannot show whether it is a new connection or a previously rare one now taken more often; an unobserved edge does not mean the edge was absent. [DIR003]

Other differences: A02's successor mix changed (mainly A15 and A44 in the later window) and its relative position in traces became more dispersed, but both rest on about 18 later-window occurrences and event positions rather than clock time; small new shares appeared for A14-to-A02 (about 4 in 100 of A14's transitions) and A43-to-A02 (about 2 in 100), and the A40-to-A20 gap tightened to one event in most later cases.
