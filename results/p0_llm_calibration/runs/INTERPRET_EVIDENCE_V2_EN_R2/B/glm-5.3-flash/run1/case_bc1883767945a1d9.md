### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, four activities — A14, A15, A20 and A21 — each took part in roughly a quarter of traces (23–27 in 100 cases, never repeating within a trace). In the later window they are almost absent: A20 and A21 were not observed at all, A14 appeared in about 1 in 100 traces (2 of 200) and A15 in about 0.5 in 100 (1 of 200). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- These four activities were mutually exclusive alternatives: in the earlier window no trace contained more than one of them (co-occurrence was 0 for every pair), and about half of all traces contained none of them; in the later window virtually no trace contains any of them (99–100 in 100 contain neither). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL006, REL007, REL010]
- The routing out of A37 changed completely. Earlier, A37's observed outgoing transitions went to A14 in about 27% of cases, A15 in 25%, A20 in 23% and A21 in 25%; in the later window A37 is followed directly by A22 in about 98.5 in 100 cases, with only residual shares to A14 and A15. [DIR003]
- Earlier, A20 and A21 always led to A22 when they occurred; in the later window neither activity was observed, so no outgoing transitions from them were seen. [DIR001, DIR002]
- The distance between A37 and A22 changed from exactly 2 positions in all earlier traces (always one activity in between) to 1 position in about 98.5 in 100 later traces (directly adjacent), with about 1.5 in 100 still at distance 2. [LAG001]
- When one of the four activities occurred earlier, it always sat between A37 and A22: each co-occurred with A22 exactly as often as it occurred at all (23–27 in 100 traces), always after A37 and before A22. In the later window this co-occurrence dropped to about 0–1 in 100. [REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- A22 itself still occurs in every trace in both windows, with its mean position shifting slightly earlier (from about 0.39 to 0.38 of the trace length), consistent with one step before it having disappeared. [POS005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the intermediate step between A37 and A22 is no longer executed in the later window: earlier, every trace passed from A37 through exactly one of four alternative activities (A14, A15, A20 or A21) and then on to A22, whereas in the later window A37 flows directly to A22 in nearly all traces and the four alternative activities are almost never observed. The support is direct and consistent: the A37–A22 distance dropped from 2 to 1 positions [LAG001], A37's outgoing flow moved from the four activities to A22 [DIR003], participation in all four activities collapsed from about 25% to 0–1% [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004], and their co-occurrence with A22 disappeared [REL004, REL008, REL011, REL013]. The 25 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.38 expected without a change, and the participation, routing and relational indicators all describe the same underlying difference rather than unrelated effects.

Limitations: zero participation only means the four activities were not observed in the 200 later traces, so they could still occur at a very low rate below detection. Also, a direct A37→A22 transition was not observed earlier (0 of 200 traces at distance 1), so the evidence cannot fully exclude that a very rare direct path already existed and merely became dominant — an extreme shift in path frequencies rather than a removal of the intermediate step — although the simultaneous collapse of all four alternatives makes pure sampling variation unlikely.

Other differences: the few residual occurrences of A14 and A15 in the later window sit at roughly the same relative position as before (about one-third through the trace), but this rests on only 1–2 observations and carries little weight. [POS001, POS002]
