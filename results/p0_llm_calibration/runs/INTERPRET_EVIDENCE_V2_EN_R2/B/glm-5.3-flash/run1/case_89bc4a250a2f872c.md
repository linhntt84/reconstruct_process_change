### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block formed by A33 and A34 changed its place in the flow, with A32, A25, A26, A28 and A29 as the counterpart activities. Before, the flow ran A25 → A33/A34 (in no fixed order between themselves) → A26 → A28 → A32 → A29. In the later window, A25 leads directly to A26, A28 follows about three positions later, and the A33/A34 pair — still without a fixed internal order — runs after A28 and about one to two positions before A29. As part of the same reorganisation, the segment between A28 and A29, previously always occupied by A32, now contains either A32 (about 45 in 100 traces) or the pair A33+A34 (about 55 in 100): A32 is skipped in roughly half of the executions and never runs together with A33 or A34. [DIR001, DIR002, REL009, REL013, REL014, LAG007]

The change is shown directly by relational and positional evidence: the A33/A34-versus-A26/A28 order flips from about 97–99 in 100 one way to about 54 in 100 the other, the successor profiles of A25, A28, A33 and A34 change almost completely, and orders that never occurred in the earlier window (A26 or A28 before A33/A34, a direct A25-to-A26 step) appear in the later one — so this is not merely existing paths being taken with different frequencies. In total 36 items qualified, far more than the roughly 0.6 expected by chance across the 120 tested units, each difference exceeding about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, and the pattern is visible across occurrence, direction, position, relation and gap indicators alike. [REL005, REL006, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, OCC001, POS005]

Limitations: all placement evidence rests on event order and relative positions, not clock time, so the evidence cannot distinguish "A33/A34 moved later" from "A26/A28 moved earlier" as an absolute statement — both descriptions fit the same relative-order change, and this is the main competing reading the data cannot settle. Many items also describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations, and behaviour outside the observed activities and edges is unobserved. [POS002, LAG003, REL005, REL006]

Other differences: A32's mean event position moved slightly earlier (about 0.58 to 0.54), and a handful of outgoing edges of A25, A28, A33 and A34 carrying about 1% or less of observations appeared or disappeared between the windows; both are minor next to the reordering above.
