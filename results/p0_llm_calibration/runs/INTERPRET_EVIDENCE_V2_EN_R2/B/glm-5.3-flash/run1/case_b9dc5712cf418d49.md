### Observed differences

- The path choice after A26 collapsed onto a single route. In the earlier window, A26's outgoing transitions led to A27 in about 47 in 100 cases and to A32 or A33 in about 26 in 100 each; in the later window, about 98 in 100 lead to A27, and transitions from A26 to A32 or A33 are no longer observed (each profile rests on about 200 outgoing transitions). [DIR002]
- A32 and A33 all but vanished: in the earlier window each appeared in about 52–53 in 100 traces, always together in either order and followed by A28; in the later window each appears in only 1 of 200 traces, and their few remaining outgoing transitions (to A44 and A24) come from those single occurrences. [OCC004, OCC005, REL026, DIR004, DIR005]
- A27 became universal and firmly ordered: it appeared in about 48 in 100 traces before and in all 200 after. A26 before A27, and A27 before A28, each previously seen in roughly half of the traces (only when the A27 route was taken), now hold in about 99–100 in 100 traces; before, A27 and A32/A33 were strict alternatives that practically never co-occurred. A14, present in every trace in both windows, now precedes A27 in about 99 in 100 traces versus about 48 before. [OCC003, REL017, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL011, DIR003]
- A06 is used differently in the later window: it appears in about 29 in 100 traces versus about 7 before; its outgoing transitions, previously spread over many activities (A46, A15, A16 and others, on only about 14 observations), now go mainly to A19 (about 55 in 100) and A20 (about 27 in 100); A40 now leads to A06 in about 25 in 100 of its transitions, where this was practically absent before; and A06 precedes A26, A27 and A28 in about 25 in 100 traces versus about 1–3 before, typically one step before A19 and at a more consistent position within the trace. [OCC002, DIR001, DIR006, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL007, LAG001, POS001]
- Smaller shifts: A02 participation fell from about 11 to 2 in 100 traces; the gap from A26 to A28 tightened to 2 steps in about 98 in 100 cases (previously split between 2 and 3 steps); and A13 and A19, present in roughly half of the traces in both windows, now always occur before A27 (about 54 in 100 traces) instead of only partially overlapping it. [OCC001, LAG006, REL008, REL014]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the branch after A26 was rerouted: the alternative sub-path in which A32 and A33 ran together (in either order) before reaching A28 is now taken rarely or is no longer observed, while A27 — followed by A28 — is executed in every trace. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR002, REL021, REL022, REL026, REL017, REL020]

Supporting signs: the routing shift at A26 is measured on about 200 outgoing transitions in each window and moves in exactly the complementary direction to the participation counts (A27 up from 96 to 200 of 200 traces; A32 and A33 down from 105 to 1 each), and the relational items show the earlier state was a genuine two-way branch rather than two unrelated activities. With 53 qualifying items against roughly 0.75 expected if nothing had changed, chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole. Limitations: near-zero participation only means A32 and A33 were almost not observed in the later sample, so a surviving rare path cannot be excluded; the occurrence, routing and relation items overlap heavily and are consistent views of one difference rather than independent confirmations; and the evidence cannot show whether this rerouting and the changed use of A06 are one restructuring or two separate ones. [DIR002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL021, REL026]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of pre-existing variants: the A32/A33 path may still exist but be chosen in only about 1 in 200 cases; the windows demonstrate the change in path frequencies, not whether the branch was structurally removed. [OCC004, OCC005]

Other differences: the more frequent and re-routed use of A06 (new A40 → A06 transition, A06 now leading mainly to A19/A20), the decline of A02, and the tightening of the A26 → A28 distance to two steps; these are not part of the main hypothesis above. [OCC002, DIR001, DIR006, OCC001, LAG006]
