### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19 and A20 were essentially not executed (observed in about 1 and 2 of 200 traces respectively); in the later window, A19 runs in about 90 of 200 traces (45%) and A20 in about 111 of 200 (56%). The two behave as near-alternatives: in the later window exactly one of them appears in about 98 in 100 traces, both together in only about 1 in 100. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001]
- A40's routing changed: earlier, about 88 in 100 of its outgoing transitions went directly to A25; in the later window, about 44 in 100 go to A20 and about 37 in 100 to A19, while a direct transition to A25 is almost unobserved (about 0.5 in 100). [DIR004]
- The new activities lead into A25: in the later window, about 97 in 100 of A19's outgoing transitions go to A25 (earlier, the single observed transition went to A26), and about 95 in 100 of A20's outgoing transitions go to A25 (earlier, two observed transitions went to A37 and A14). [DIR002, DIR003]
- Order and distance agree: in the later window A40 precedes A19 in about 44 in 100 traces and A20 in about 55 in 100, A19 precedes A25 in about 43 in 100 and A20 precedes A25 in about 55 in 100; A19 follows A40 after one step in about 84 in 100 later cases (the single earlier observation lay four steps away), and the A40-to-A25 distance moved from one step (about 88 in 100 of earlier cases) to two steps (about 80 in 100 of later cases). - A25 still occurs in every trace in both windows, with its relative position slightly later (mean about 0.38 to about 0.40 of the trace); in the later window A19 and A20 sit at about 0.36–0.37 of the trace, just before A25's typical position. [POS003, POS001, POS002]
- A secondary redistribution at A04: its outgoing transitions shifted, with A46 falling from about 27 in 100 to about 5 in 100, A25 no longer observed as a successor, and A19/A20 now appearing (about 9–10 in 100 each). [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, a choice between A19 and A20 is executed between A40 and A25: A40, which earlier led directly to A25 in about 88 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, now leads first to A20 (about 44 in 100) or A19 (about 37 in 100) — almost always exactly one of the two — and that activity then leads to A25 in about 95–97 in 100 of its transitions. The near-disappearance of the direct A40-to-A25 transition, together with the A40-to-A25 distance moving from one to two steps, fits a step inserted between the two activities rather than only a change in how often existing paths are taken, and A25's unchanged participation with a slightly later position is consistent with something now being executed before it. The signs agree across the participation, successor, ordering and distance views (though they partly describe the same trace behaviour, not independent confirmations), and fourteen qualifying items against well under one expected by chance makes an unchanged process unlikely as an overall explanation. [DIR004, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, LAG001, LAG002, POS003]

Limitations: the earlier behaviour of A19 and A20 rests on only one and two observations, so the evidence shows they were essentially not executed before but cannot describe how they behaved; the residual direct A40-to-A25 transition (about 0.5 in 100 of A40's outgoing transitions) may be a rare retained path or sampling noise; and whether A19 and A20 are strictly mutually exclusive cannot be settled, since both occur together in about 1 in 100 later traces. [DIR004, REL001, OCC001, OCC002]

Other differences: A04's outgoing transitions redistributed as described above; the evidence does not tie this directly to the rerouting between A40 and A25, and A04 occurs in only about 42 of 200 traces, so this rests on moderate support. [DIR001]
