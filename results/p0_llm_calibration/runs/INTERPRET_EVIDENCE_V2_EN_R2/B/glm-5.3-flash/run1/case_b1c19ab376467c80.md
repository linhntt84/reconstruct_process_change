### Observed differences

- The order between the A14/A15 step and A20/A21/A22 reversed. In the earlier window, A20 and A21 each ran before A14 in every case where they co-occurred (about 43 in 100 traces for A14, 57 in 100 for A15), and A22 stood in both orders relative to A14/A15 (roughly 22 vs 22 and 25 vs 33 in 100). In the later window, A14 runs before A20, A21 and A22 in about 52 in 100 traces (over 99% of co-occurrences) and A15 before them in about 48 in 100 (essentially all co-occurrences). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- A35's successor changed: earlier, all of its observed outgoing transitions went to A20; later, about 52 in 100 go to A14 and 48 to A15, with A20 almost unobserved. The distance from A35 to A14/A15 shrank from 5 steps to 1, while A35 to A20 grew from 1 to 2 steps and A35 to A21 from 4 to 5. [DIR007, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013]
- A14 and A15 now feed into A20: earlier, A14 was followed by A23 or A22 (about 50/50 of outgoing transitions) and A15 by A23 or A22 (57/43); later, A14 is followed by A20 in about 99 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and A15 always by A20. [DIR001, DIR002]
- The tail of the case became a fixed chain: earlier, A21 split into A22 (54%), A15 (25%) and A14 (22%), and A22 split into A23 (46%), A15 (33%) and A14 (22%); later, A21 is always followed directly by A22 and A22 almost always directly by A23, with A21 reaching A23 in 2 steps instead of 3. [DIR003, DIR004, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]
- The old late placement of A14/A15 nearly disappeared: A14 followed by A23, A20 followed by A14, and A21 followed by A14 each appear in only about 1 later trace (86 earlier), and A14/A15 now reach A22 only at 5 steps' distance (103 and 96 traces) instead of directly (43 and 49). [LAG002, LAG004, LAG006, LAG001, LAG003]
- Positions and participation shifted accordingly: A14's mean relative position moved from about 0.52 to 0.37 of the trace and A15's from about 0.51 to 0.37, while A20, A21 and A22 moved slightly later (0.36→0.40, 0.46→0.50, 0.51→0.53); A14 appears in 52% of later traces (43% before) and A15 in 48% (57% before). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005]
- Separately, the routing at A31 and A32 flipped: earlier, A31 was followed by A26 in about 58% of its outgoing transitions and by A32 in 42%; later, about 37% and 63%, with the mirror-image flip at A32. [DIR005, DIR006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the A14/A15 step was moved from the late stage of the case to immediately after A35 and before A20. In the earlier window, A35 led to A20, and A14 or A15 ran after A20 and A21, in both orders relative to A22, and generally before A23; in the later window, A35 leads directly to A14 (about 52 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) or A15 (about 48), which then lead to A20, so the sequence reads A35 → (A14 or A15) → A20 → … → A21 → A22 → A23, and the previously flexible ordering around A22 has become a fixed A21 → A22 → A23 chain. [REL001, REL004, DIR007, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG010, LAG001, POS001, POS002]

The support is direct and consistent: the pairwise orders between A14/A15 and A20/A21/A22 flipped from one fixed direction to the other, the successor, lag and position items show the same shift from both endpoints, and the old late placement survives in only about 1 of 200 later traces. The 31 qualifying items are also many times the roughly 0.42 expected by chance in an unchanged process, so sampling variation cannot explain the pattern as a whole — though the relation, lag, direction and position items largely describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations. [REL001, DIR007, LAG010, POS001, LAG002]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that both placements already existed as variants in both windows and only their frequencies swapped, with an early-placement variant rising from near zero to nearly all cases; with 200 traces per window, such an extreme frequency shift is indistinguishable from a repositioning of the step. [REL001, LAG002]

Other differences: the choice after A31 between A32 and A26, and after A32 between A31 and A26, flipped (about 42/58 to 63/37 at A31 and the reverse at A32) — a change in how often existing paths are taken, not a replacement of paths, and separate from the repositioning above. [DIR005, DIR006]
