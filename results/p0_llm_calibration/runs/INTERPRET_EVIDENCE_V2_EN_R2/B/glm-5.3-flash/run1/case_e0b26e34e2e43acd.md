### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19 ran in about 49 in 100 cases (98 of 200) and A20 in about 52 in 100 (103 of 200), with exactly one of the two present in virtually every case and never both; in the later window A19 appears in about 1 in 200 cases and A20 in about 3 in 200. [OCC001, OCC002, REL009]

- A06's outgoing transitions were redistributed (earlier mainly to A46, A47 and A48; later including A25 and A16), but this rests on only 11 and 15 observed outgoing transitions. [DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the earlier alternative in which each case executed either A19 or A20 — exactly one of the two, never both — between A14/A42 and A27 is no longer taken in the later window, and that both A25 and A26 now run in that same slot, in either order, with the old path still taken in roughly 1–3 in 100 cases. The participation flip (about half of cases to almost none for A19/A20; zero to about 99 in 100 for A25/A26) is directly anchored by relational and directional evidence: A25 and A26 inherited the predecessors (A14, A42) and the successor (A27) of A19/A20, A42's outgoing transitions shifted from A19/A20 to A25/A26, and the A42-to-A27 distance grew by one event, consistent with one extra step inside the block. The 39 flagged items far exceed the roughly 0.64 expected by chance in an unchanged process, and the occurrence, direction, relation, position and lag indicators all converge on the same behavioural slot change. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL005, REL007, REL009, REL013, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, DIR006, LAG002]

Limitations: the evidence shows the behavioural substitution but cannot establish whether A25/A26 are genuinely new activities or the same tasks under new labels, nor whether the old A19/A20 branch was removed from the process or is only almost never selected, since A19 and A20 still occur in a few later cases; the later outgoing behaviour of A19 and A20 rests on only 1 and 3 observations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the exclusive A19-or-A20 branch still exists and its routing share collapsed to near zero while A25/A26 were added — behaviourally indistinguishable from the hypothesis above. [OCC001, OCC002, REL009, DIR002, DIR003]

Other differences: A06's outgoing transition profile changed (earlier mainly A46/A47/A48, later including A25 and A16), but with only 11 versus 15 observed outgoing transitions this is weakly supported and is not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR001]
