- A25 and A26 all but disappear: earlier, each appeared exactly once in every trace (100 in 100), around 36–37% of the way through the trace; in the later window each appears in only about 1.5 in 100 traces (about 3 of 200). [OCC003, OCC004, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A42 and A27 is executed differently in the later window: earlier, every observed trace ran from A42 through both A25 and A26 (in either order) before reaching A27, whereas in the later window traces run from A42 through exactly one of A19 or A20 — two mutually exclusive alternatives splitting cases roughly evenly (about 48 vs 51 in 100) — and then to A27, while A25 and A26 are executed in only about 3 of 200 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, DIR005]

This is shown directly by relational and successor evidence rather than participation counts alone: A42's successors moved from A25/A26 to A19/A20, A19/A20 lead almost solely to A27, the A25–A26 and A25/A26–A27 relations collapse, the A42→A27 distance shortens from three to two positions, and the new activities occupy the same relative position (about 37% of the trace) that A25/A26 previously held. With 24 qualifying items against roughly 0.57 expected for an unchanged process, the pattern as a whole is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR005, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014, LAG001, POS001, POS002]

Limitations: zero participation earlier establishes only that A19/A20 were not observed in the earlier sample, so the evidence cannot rule out that both routings already existed as variants whose mix shifted strongly, rather than one routing being newly introduced; several items also describe the same underlying trace behaviour (participation, relation and direction items overlap), so they are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

Other differences: in the roughly 3-in-200 later traces that still contain A25 or A26, these activities sit somewhat earlier in the trace and show outgoing transitions to A15 or A14 alongside A27; these profiles rest on only 3 traces and are too weak to interpret. [DIR003, DIR004, POS003, POS004]
