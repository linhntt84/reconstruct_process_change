### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, four activities — A19, A20, A25 and A26 — each took part in about a quarter of traces (roughly 47–53 of 200 traces each), always around the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.51–0.53). No two of them occurred together in more than about 1 of 100 traces, so together they covered essentially every trace. In the later window they have practically disappeared: A20 and A26 appear in no trace, A19 in 3 and A25 in 1 of 200. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, POS002, POS003, POS004, POS005, REL013, REL019, REL024]
- The block's ties to the rest of the process collapsed. Earlier, A14 and A15 each co-occurred with each of the four in about 24–27 of 100 traces (always before them), and A28, A29 and A30 each co-occurred with the four in about 24–27 of 100 traces (A28 before them, A30 after them, A29 in both orders); in the later window these co-occurrences are at most about 1–2 in 100. [REL005, REL009, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL021, REL023, REL028, REL030]
- Routing consolidated around the vanished block. Earlier, about half of A28's observed outgoing transitions went to one of the four (A20 about 14%, A19 about 13%, A26 about 12%, A25 about 10%) and about half to A29; in the later window about 99 in 100 go to A29. Earlier, A29 sent about half of its outgoing transitions to the four and about 49% to A30; in the later window about 96 in 100 go to A30. [DIR006, DIR007]
- The A28 → A30 distance shortened by one step: earlier it was almost always 3 event positions (about 98 in 100), in the later window almost always 2 (about 96 in 100); A28 → A29 and A29 → A30, previously split roughly 50/50 between 1 and 2 steps, are now almost always 1 step. A30 also sits slightly earlier in the trace (mean relative position about 0.56 versus 0.55). [LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, POS006]
- Separately, A04 became less frequent (present in about 24 of 100 earlier traces versus about 13–14 later) and its outgoing transitions spread out: earlier about 86% went to A15, later only about 33%, with several new successors appearing (A09, A16, A27 at about 11% each); the A14 → A04 → A15 chain loosened (A14 → A04 fell from about 21% to about 4.5% of A14's outgoing transitions, and the share of A04 → A15 at distance 1 fell from about 68% to about 37%, based on 11–13 observed pairs in the later window). [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative block of A19, A20, A25 and A26 is no longer executed. In the earlier window essentially every trace ran exactly one of these four activities, placed after A14/A15 and A28, in varying order around A29, and before A30; in the later window none of them is observed in more than 3 of 200 traces. Since the surrounding activities and their order are present in both windows and only the use of this branch disappeared, this reads as an existing path — inserting one of the four between A28/A29 and A30 — that is no longer taken, not as a replacement of one path by another. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL005, REL016, REL017, REL018]

The routing and distance evidence is consistent with this and hard to explain otherwise: the roughly 50% of A28's and A29's outgoing transitions that previously led to the four collapsed to almost zero while A28 → A29 and A29 → A30 became near-deterministic, and the A28 → A30 distance shortened from 3 to 2 event positions, the shift expected when one intermediate activity drops out of a chain. The volume of qualifying evidence (55 items against roughly 0.73 expected for an unchanged process under this screening) also exceeds what sampling alone would produce. [DIR006, DIR007, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

Limitations: zero participation only means the activities were not observed; the evidence cannot distinguish a removed branch from one whose selection probability has become very small but non-zero, and 200 traces per window cannot rule out such a residual path. The few remaining occurrences of A19 and A25 (3 and 1 traces) are too few to characterise their context. [OCC001, OCC003]

Other differences: A04 participates in fewer traces (about 24 versus about 13–14 in 100) and its outgoing transitions are more dispersed (A15 share down from about 86% to about 33%), with the A14 → A04 → A15 chain loosening; A14's mean position shifted only marginally (about 0.133 to 0.138 of the trace). These changes are not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG002, REL001, POS001]
