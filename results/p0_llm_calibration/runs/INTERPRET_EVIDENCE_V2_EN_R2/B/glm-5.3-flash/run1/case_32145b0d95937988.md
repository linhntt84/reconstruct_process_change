### Observed differences

- Repetition of four activities stopped: in the earlier window, A30, A31, A36 and A37 each occurred about 3.3 times per trace and recurred within a trace in about 67 in 100 traces; in the later window, each occurred about once per trace and repeated in at most about 2 in 100 traces. Every trace still contained each of the four activities in both windows. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- The successors of A37 changed: earlier, about 70 in 100 of A37's outgoing transitions went to A30 and about 30 in 100 to A25; later, about 97 in 100 went to A25, and A30 was not observed as a successor in any of the 203 outgoing transitions (against 668 earlier). [DIR001]
- Ordering within the block became fixed: earlier, A30 and each of A31, A36, A37 were interleaved or repeated in about 67 in 100 traces, with A30 first in only about 33 in 100; later, A30 came before each of them in about 97–99 in 100 traces. A31 and A36, earlier mostly interleaved (about 68 in 100), later appeared in both orders in similar shares (about 56 vs 44 in 100) with no interleaving. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Gaps between block activities tightened: earlier, event-position gaps within five steps were spread across 1–5 steps; later, consecutive occurrences were 1–2 steps apart almost always (A31 before A36 at gap 1 in about 98 in 100; A36 before A31 at gap 1 in all observed cases). The later gap profiles for pairs starting with A37 rest on a single observation each and are weak. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]
- The four activities now sit in a narrower band of trace positions: the position spread (standard deviation) fell from about 0.135 to about 0.055–0.073, with medians roughly unchanged (A30 median about 0.43 vs 0.39). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the repetitive pass over the block A30, A31, A36, A37 is no longer taken: in the earlier window traces cycled through these four activities about three times each, in interleaved order, with about 70 in 100 of A37's outgoing transitions returning to A30, whereas in the later window each activity runs exactly once per trace, A30 precedes the other three in about 97–99 in 100 traces, and the flow leaves A37 towards A25 in about 97 in 100 transitions. Read as path usage, the repeating path was taken in essentially all earlier traces and in none of the later ones, while the single-pass exit to A25 rose from about 30 to about 97 in 100 of A37's outgoing transitions. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

Supporting signs: 19 evidence items qualify, far above the roughly 0.55 expected for an unchanged process at these thresholds, so chance cannot explain the set as a whole; every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data; repetition, successor choice and ordering each show the change directly rather than by implication; and the samples are large (200 traces per window, 668 vs 203 outgoing transitions of A37). [OCC001, DIR001, REL001, LAG003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish whether the cycle was removed from the process definition or whether the looping path simply was not chosen in the later window; an unobserved A37→A30 transition shows only that it was not taken, not that the edge is absent; and the later gap profiles for pairs with A37 as the earlier activity rest on one observation each, so they were not relied on. [DIR001, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]

Other differences: none beyond the above; the narrower position spread of the four activities is consistent with the same single-pass behaviour rather than a separate change. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]
