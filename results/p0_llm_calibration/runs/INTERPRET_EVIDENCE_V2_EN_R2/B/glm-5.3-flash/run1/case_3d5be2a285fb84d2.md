### Observed differences

- The eight activities A33, A34, A36, A37, A38, A39, A40 and A41 went from appearing in roughly half of the cases to appearing in nearly all of them: earlier each of A33/A34/A36/A37 was present in about 54 in 100 traces and each of A38–A41 in about 46–47 in 100; in the later window all eight are present in 199 of 200 traces, about once per trace. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009]

- Earlier, the two groups of four were near-exclusive alternatives: for any pair spanning the groups, the two activities occurred in the same trace in only about 1 in 100 cases, while one group appeared without the other in about 99 in 100. In the later window they occur together in about 99 in 100 traces, with the A33/A34/A36/A37 activity before the A38/A39/A40/A41 activity in about 97–98 of those. - Within each group, co-occurrence rose from about 46–55 in 100 traces to about 995 in 1000, and the order between members stayed mixed in both windows (both orders taken in roughly equal shares), so there is no fixed order inside either group before or after. [REL041, REL042, REL048, REL049, REL054, REL063, REL064, REL066, REL068]

- The routing around the block changed: A30's outgoing transitions, earlier spread over both groups (roughly 8–15% per target), now go about 97 in 100 to A33/A34/A36/A37; transitions from the first group to A31 (about 21–28% of each source's outgoing) nearly vanish (about 0.5% or less) and are replaced by transitions into A38–A41 (about 3–8% each); A31 now comes after each first-group activity in about 99 in 100 traces. - Spacing became regular: the gap from A30 to each of A38–A41, and from each of A33/A34/A36/A37 to A31, is now almost always exactly five events (about 96–98 in 100), whereas earlier these gaps spread over one to four events. [LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011]

- A14 and A15, present in every trace in both windows, now co-occur with each of the eight in about 995 in 1000 traces (earlier about 47–55 in 100); A14 precedes them in about 98–99 in 100, A15 in about two-thirds with about one-third interleaved. [REL009, REL013, REL017, REL021]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two four-activity blocks that were previously alternative path segments — A33/A34/A36/A37 on one side and A38/A39/A40/A41 on the other — are in the later window executed together in sequence within the same case: earlier a case contained one block or the other (about 54% versus 46% of traces, both together in about 1 in 100), while in the later window about 99 in 100 cases contain both blocks, the first preceding the second in about 97–98 in 100, with A30 leading into the combined block and A31 following it at a consistent five-event spacing. [REL044, REL045, REL055, REL059, OCC002, OCC006, DIR003, DIR004, LAG004, LAG008]

The support is direct and consistent: the relation items show the change from alternatives to joint execution for all sixteen cross-group pairs, the participation items show all eight activities becoming near-universal, the successor profiles show A30 now feeding the first block and the first block feeding the second block instead of A31, and the lag items show regular block boundaries; with 108 qualifying evidence items against roughly 1.2 expected if nothing had changed, sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [REL044, REL059, OCC002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG004, LAG008]

Limitations: the combined path already existed earlier at low frequency, so the evidence shows an existing path becoming dominant rather than a new one appearing, and it cannot show whether the cross-block connections themselves are new or were present but rarely exercised; the order within each block remains unfixed in both windows, and a small residual of later cases still shows only one block. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a pure shift in how often pre-existing paths are taken, without any new ordering constraint. [REL044, REL047, REL059]

Other differences: A06 declined — participation fell from about 29 to about 13 in 100 traces, its outgoing traffic concentrated on A15 earlier (about 86%) is now spread over many targets (about 29% to A15), A14→A06 became rare, and A06 now appears only alongside the block activities; A14 and A15 now co-occur with the block activities in nearly every trace, and A06's event position became later and more variable. These changes are consistent consequences of the main difference but are not part of the hypothesis itself. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, REL001, REL009, REL017]
