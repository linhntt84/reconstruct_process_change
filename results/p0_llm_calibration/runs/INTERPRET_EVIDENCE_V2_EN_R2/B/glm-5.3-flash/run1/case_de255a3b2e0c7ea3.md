### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the ordering between A19/A20 and the A25–A26–A27–A28 sequence changed: in the earlier window A19 and A20 typically ran after A25/A26 and immediately before A27/A28, while in the later window they typically run between A40 and A25, and A27/A28 now follow A26 directly. This rests on direct relational evidence — the order shares between A19/A20 and A25/A26 flipped completely — corroborated by the successor changes at A40, A19, A20, A26 and A27, by step-distance shifts consistent with A19/A20 sitting between A40 and A25 (A40→A25 from one to two steps; A19→A27 from one to five steps, matching A19→A25 at one plus A25→A27 at about four), and by consistent position movements (A19/A20 earlier, A25/A26 slightly later). [REL010, REL011, REL013, REL014, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR011, LAG002, LAG013, POS002, POS003]

With 53 flagged items against roughly 0.7 expected by chance for the 144 tested units, the volume of evidence is far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces, though many items describe the same trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations. The competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in how often existing paths are taken rather than a new ordering constraint: the A19/A20-before-A25 order was never observed in the earlier window (in about 86 traces where both occurred, A25 always ran first), so a very rare pre-existing order could have gone undetected and simply become dominant. [REL010, LAG002]

Other differences: A10 became a frequent late activity that typically runs between A45 and A46 (participation about 21 to 60 in 100 traces; A45's outgoing split moved from about 88 in 100 to A46 towards about 57 A10 and 42 A46), A04 and A06 are executed in fewer traces (about 21 to 11 and 19 to 6 in 100), with A02/A04/A06 successors now spread thinly over many targets, and A36's A31/A37 shares reversed. These are not part of the main hypothesis above. [OCC003, OCC001, OCC002, DIR004, DIR013, LAG015, DIR010]
