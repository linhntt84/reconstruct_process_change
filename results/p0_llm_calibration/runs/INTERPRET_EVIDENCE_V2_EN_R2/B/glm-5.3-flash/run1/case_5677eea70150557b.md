### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A30 and A31 each ran in every trace but occurred about 1.6 times per trace, repeating in roughly 37 in 100 traces; in the later window they still ran in every trace but essentially once each (mean about 1.0, repeats in at most about 2 in 100 traces). [OCC005, OCC006, LAG014]
- The two became a fixed, immediate sequence: A30 was followed by A31 within one step in about 35 in 100 observations before and about 97 in 100 in the later window, while the reverse pair (A31 then A30 within five steps) fell from 73 observed pairs to 1; A31's outgoing transitions moved from A25 (about 63 in 100) and A30 (about 37 in 100) to almost only A25 (about 97 in 100). [LAG012, LAG013, DIR005]
- Ordering across the wider block tightened: for pairs among A14, A19, A20, A30, A31, A38, A43 and A44, which co-occur in every trace in both windows, interleaved or repeated orderings fell from about 34–38 in 100 traces to about 1–4 in 100, while a single fixed order rose from about 62–63 in 100 to about 96–99 in 100. [REL010, REL012, REL016, REL017, REL020]
- Consecutive activities also moved closer together: A19 and A20 followed A30 within one or two steps in about 54–57 in 100 observations before versus about 97–98 in 100 after, and A38 reached A30 mostly at three steps (about 82 in 100) in the later window, against a spread over three to four steps before. [LAG008, LAG010, LAG018]
- A06 receded sharply: it appeared in about 63 in 100 traces before and about 20 in 100 after; its successors changed from mostly A19 (about 50 in 100) and A20 (about 44 in 100) to a spread with no dominant target (largest shares about 13–16 in 100), and its position became far more variable (middle half of positions from roughly 0.30–0.38 to roughly 0.18–0.86 of the trace). [OCC003, DIR001, POS001]
- A06's coupling to the block broke: before, it co-occurred with each of A14, A15, A19, A20, A30, A31, A38, A43 and A44 in about 63 in 100 traces in a stable order (A06 before A19 in about 60 in 100; A38 before A06 in about 60 in 100); after, co-occurrence fell to about 20 in 100 and in about 80 in 100 traces the partner ran without A06. A38's main successor switched from A06 (about 60 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) to A19 and A20 (about 44 and 42 in 100). [REL003, REL007, REL009, DIR006, LAG015]
- Participation rose for three previously rare activities: A02 from about 6 to 15 in 100 traces, A04 from about 5 to 16 in 100, and A10 from about 6 to 26 in 100, with A10's outgoing transitions spreading from nine observed targets to about twenty. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, DIR002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window is dominated by a different mix of the same execution paths: traces in which A30 and A31 each run exactly once in immediate succession (A30 before A31), with A14, A19, A20, A38, A43 and A44 in one fixed order around them and without A06, became the prevailing behaviour, whereas the earlier window had a substantial share of traces in which A30 and A31 repeated, the block's activities interleaved, and A06 ran alongside (fixed-order share up from about 63 to about 97–99 in 100 traces; A06 co-occurrence down from about 63 to about 20 in 100). Supporting signs: the participation, repetition, successor, co-occurrence and distance indicators all point the same way; 58 evidence items qualified against roughly 1 (0.77) expected if nothing had changed, each with a difference larger than in about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits; and the affected behaviours are high-frequency, so a rare variant appearing in one window cannot explain them. [OCC005, OCC006, DIR005, REL003, REL010, LAG012, LAG013]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the A06 decline and the tightening around A30 and A31 stem from one structural change or two independent ones, and several indicators describe overlapping trace behaviour, so agreeing items are not independent confirmations; a few distance results rest on very few later-window observations (1 to 6 pairs), making those post-change distances uncertain; all shares come from finite samples of 200 traces per window. [LAG003, LAG013, LAG014]

Other differences: A02, A04 and A10 took part in more traces in the later window (about 6 to 15, 5 to 16, and 6 to 26 in 100 respectively), with A10's successors spreading widely; small new successor edges appeared after A14, A15 and A43 alongside their dominant successors (A15 about 81 in 100, A16 about 91 in 100, A44 about 85 in 100). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR007]
