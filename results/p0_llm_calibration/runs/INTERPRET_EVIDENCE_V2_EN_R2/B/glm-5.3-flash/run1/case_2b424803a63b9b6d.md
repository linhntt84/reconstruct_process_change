### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that A02 now takes a fixed late position in the process, executed in about 65 in 100 cases immediately between A45 and A46 (A45 → A02 → A46), whereas before it ran in about 20 in 100 cases, usually before A45, at varying positions and with varied successors. The signs are direct and consistent: the order between A02 and A45 reverses, A02 sits at a fixed position near the trace end while A45 stays where it was, succession on both sides becomes immediate (A45 → A02 and A02 → A46 at a one-event gap in nearly every observed pair), A45's successor share shifts from A46 to A02 while A02's shifts onto A46, and A45 → A46 moves from immediate to two events apart. Occurrence, position, direction, relation and lag indicators all show the same picture, and the 39 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.7 expected if nothing had changed. [OCC001, POS001, POS002, DIR001, DIR011, LAG002, LAG012, LAG013, REL009, REL010]

An important competing explanation remains: a rare A45 → A02 route already existed before (about 6 in 100 of A45's transitions), so the evidence cannot distinguish a relocation of A02 from a shift in how often existing paths are taken — the previously rare end path becoming dominant while A02's earlier mid-trace usage disappeared. The rising co-occurrence of A02 with A14, A15, A29 and A30 follows mechanically from its higher participation and adds no independent confirmation. [DIR011, REL001, REL002, REL007, REL008]
