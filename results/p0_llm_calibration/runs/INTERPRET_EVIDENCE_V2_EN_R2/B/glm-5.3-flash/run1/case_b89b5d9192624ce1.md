### Observed differences

- Activities A19 and A20 all but disappeared. A19 ran in about 55 in 100 traces in the earlier window (110 of 200) but in only about 1 in 200 traces in the later window; A20 fell from about 45 in 100 traces (91 of 200) to about 4 in 200. [OCC001, OCC002]
- In the earlier window A19 and A20 were strict alternatives: in roughly 55 in 100 traces only A19 ran, in roughly 45 in 100 only A20, and both ran together in only about 1 in 200 traces. In the later window neither activity ran in about 97–98 in 100 traces. [REL001]
- A40 still ran in essentially every trace in both windows, but its successor changed: earlier, about 50 in 100 of A40's outgoing transitions went to A19 and about 43 in 100 to A20; in the later window, about 93 in 100 go directly to A25. [DIR002]
- The distance from A40 to A25 shortened: earlier A25 followed A40 about 2 steps later in about 92 in 100 cases (the rest 3 steps); in the later window it follows immediately, 1 step later, in about 93 in 100 cases. [LAG002]
- The orderings A19 before A25 (about 55 in 100 traces earlier) and A20 before A25 (about 45 in 100 earlier) vanished: in the later window A25 runs without either of them in about 98–99.5 in 100 traces. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]
- The relative positions of A19 and A20 within their traces barely moved (mean about 0.35 of the trace earlier vs about 0.33 later); what changed is that they stop occurring, not that they occur somewhere else. [POS001, POS002]
- Two further items are too thinly supported to interpret on their own: A20's outgoing profile in the later window rests on 4 observations, and the A40→A20 distances on 3. [DIR001, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative intermediate steps A19 and A20 between A40 and A25 are essentially no longer executed: earlier, traces ran either A40 → A19 → A25 (about 55 in 100) or A40 → A20 → A25 (about 45 in 100), whereas in the later window A40 passes directly to A25 in about 93 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, one step apart instead of two, while A25 and A40 themselves still run in nearly every trace. This is shown directly by the successor profile of A40, the collapse of A19/A20 co-occurrence with A25, and the shortening of the A40→A25 distance, each based on about 200 traces per window, and it is consistent with the near-disappearance of A19 and A20; the unchanged relative positions of the two activities argue against their having been moved rather than skipped. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, LAG002, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, POS001, POS002]

Twelve items qualified in total, against roughly 0.5 expected by chance if nothing had changed, so the set as a whole is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. The residual occurrences (A19 in 1 trace, A20 in 4 of 200) mean the evidence shows these paths are now rarely taken rather than strictly removed, and it cannot distinguish an outright removal of the intermediate step from an extreme shift in how often existing paths are taken; a competing explanation it cannot rule out is that the later window samples a different mix of cases in which the A19/A20 branch simply never runs. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, POS001, POS002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001, LAG002]

Other differences: in the later window, A20's rare outgoing transitions spread across A25, A09 and A27, and the few A40→A20 distances scatter over lags 1, 2 and 5, but both rest on only 3–4 observations and are not interpretable on their own. [DIR001, LAG001]
