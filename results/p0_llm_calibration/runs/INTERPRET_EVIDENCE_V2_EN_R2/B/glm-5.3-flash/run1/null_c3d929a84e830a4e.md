### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that existing paths are now taken with different frequencies: in the later window, execution shifted toward paths containing A31 and A32, which appear in roughly 13 more traces per 100, and away from paths containing A33 and A34, which appear in roughly 13 fewer traces per 100. Supporting signs are the four consistent, equal-sized participation shifts in opposite directions with complementary shares, no change in within-trace repetition, each difference being unusual for random re-splits, and five qualifying items against roughly 0.27 expected if nothing had changed. The main limitation is that no relational evidence (co-occurrence or ordering) among A31–A34 was collected, so the reading of two alternative branches rests on the matching participation values rather than direct observation; an important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the four participation shifts are independent changes in each activity's execution rather than one coordinated switch between paths.
