### Observed differences

- The routing out of A37 was redistributed: in the earlier window its outgoing transitions went to A22 in about 52 in 100 cases and to A14 in about 48 in 100; in the later window about 995 in 1000 transitions go to A14 and only about 5 in 1000 still go directly to A22. [DIR001]
- A14 and A15 each appeared in about 48 in 100 traces in the earlier window but in essentially every trace (about 199 of 200) in the later window, with no repetition in either window. [OCC001, OCC002]
- Correspondingly, A14 and A15 ran together in about 48 in 100 traces before versus about 995 in 1000 after, always in the order A14 then A15; in the earlier window about 52 in 100 traces contained neither of them. [REL001]
- A20 and A21 each roughly doubled their presence (A20 from about 24 in 100 to about 51 in 100 traces; A21 from about 24 in 100 to about 49 in 100). They never occur in the same trace in either window, and in both windows the order is A14 before A20 (or A21) before A15, with exactly one of the two accompanying practically every A14 execution; so they remain a two-way choice inside the A14 branch. [OCC003, OCC004, REL002, REL003, REL006, REL007, REL010]
- The spacing from A37 to A22 changed from a mix of 1 step (about 52 in 100) and 4 steps (about 48 in 100) to almost always 4 steps (about 995 in 1000), matching the route through the A14 block being taken nearly always. [LAG001]
- A22 and A37 still occur in every trace in both windows, and the orderings A37 before A14 and A14 before A22 are unchanged; before, in about 52 in 100 traces A22 appeared without A14, which is now about 5 in 1000. [REL004, REL005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that existing paths are taken with different frequencies: the branch A37 → A14 → (A20 or A21) → A15 → A22, taken in about 48 in 100 traces in the earlier window, is taken in essentially every trace in the later window, while the direct transition A37 → A22 is nearly unused (about 5 in 1000, down from about 52 in 100). Supporting signs: the redistribution of A37's outgoing flow is observed directly; 21 evidence items qualify, many times the roughly 0.39 expected by chance, and each difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits; participation, co-occurrence, ordering and spacing items all fit one consistent structure in which both routes exist in both windows and only their shares changed; and the unchanged order relations (A37 before A14, A14 before A15, A14 before A20/A21, A20/A21 before A15, A14 before A22) rule out a swap or repositioning of activities. Limitations: the direct A37 → A22 transition is still observed once in the later window, so the evidence shows a strong shift in branch frequency rather than removal of the branch; and with 200 traces per window it cannot be ruled out that the two windows sampled different case mixes rather than that the routing itself changed. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL010, LAG001]

Other differences: A22's mean relative position moved slightly later (about 0.40 to 0.43) and A37's slightly earlier (about 0.33 to 0.31); these small shifts are consistent with the A14/A15/A20/A21 block executing in nearly every trace and do not by themselves indicate that either activity was moved. [POS001, POS002]
