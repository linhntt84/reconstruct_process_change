### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A14 and A15 were not observed at all (0 of 200 traces); in the later window, A14 appears in about 44 in 100 traces (88 of 200) and A15 in about 56 in 100 traces (111 of 200), in each case exactly once when present. [OCC001, OCC002]
- In the later window, A14 and A15 behave as mutually exclusive alternatives: about 44 in 100 traces contain only A14, about 56 in 100 contain only A15, about 1 in 200 contain neither, and the two never occur in the same trace. In the earlier window, neither activity appeared. [REL001]
- In the earlier window, the outgoing transitions of A35 went to A20 in essentially every case; in the later window they go to A15 in about 56 in 100 cases and to A14 in about 44 in 100, with a direct A35→A20 transition remaining in only about 1 in 200 cases. [DIR003]
- In the later window, all observed outgoing transitions of A14 and of A15 lead to A20. [DIR001, DIR002]
- In the later window, A35 precedes A14 and A15 in every trace where they co-occur, and A14/A15 precede A20 in every such trace; earlier, A20 always appeared without A14 or A15. [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005]
- The step distance from A35 to A20 moved from 1 in essentially all earlier traces to 2 in about 99.5 in 100 later traces, consistent with exactly one activity now sitting between them. [LAG001]
- Both new activities occur at a similar mean relative position of about 0.36 of the trace (medians about 0.35). [POS001, POS002]
- All thirteen evidence items show differences larger than roughly 99.5 in 100 random re-splits of the pooled data. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, POS001, POS002, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, an exclusive choice between A14 and A15 was inserted between A35 and A20: A35 now leads to A15 in about 56 in 100 traces and to A14 in about 44 in 100, and the chosen activity then leads on to A20, whereas earlier A35 led directly to A20 and neither A14 nor A15 was observed. A short direct A35→A20 path remains in about 1 in 200 later traces. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001]

The hypothesis is supported directly and consistently: participation rises from zero to 44% and 56%, both new activities point exclusively to A20, A35's outgoing flow redistributes to exactly these two activities, the order relations place A35 before and A20 after them, A14 and A15 never co-occur, and the A35→A20 distance grows by exactly one step. The number of qualifying items (13) is many times the roughly 0.33 expected if nothing had changed, so chance alone cannot explain the evidence as a whole. [DIR003, REL001, REL002, REL005, LAG001]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A14 and A15 were not observed then, so the evidence cannot distinguish a newly added step from a branch that existed before but went unobserved in the earlier sample; the driver of the roughly 44/56 split between A14 and A15 is also not observable. In addition, many items describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations of one another. [OCC001, OCC002]
