### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the execution order of two groups of activities was exchanged: A20, A21, A23 and A24, which previously ran between A42 and the A27/A31 pair and before A19, A22, A25 and A26, in the later window run after A27/A31, while A19, A22, A25 and A26 now run before A27; A27 and A31 keep their place between the groups, and the surrounding A14→A15→A16 chain and the A31→A32 link persist. The hypothesis rests on direct relational evidence — near-deterministic order reversals in one consistent direction for every pair across the two groups — corroborated by opposite position shifts, by the successor changes at A19/A22/A25/A26, A20/A21/A23/A24, A31 and A42, and by the changed step distances; about 120 items qualified in total, roughly 95 times the number expected for an unchanged process, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. Limitations: the items describe one underlying trace-level difference rather than independent confirmations, positions are event ranks rather than clock times, and the two-window design cannot rule out that the samples come from different fixed-order variants whose mix shifted rather than from a single modified process.
