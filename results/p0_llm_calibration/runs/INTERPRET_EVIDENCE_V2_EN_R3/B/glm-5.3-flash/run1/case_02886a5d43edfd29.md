### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19 and A20 were almost never executed (each in only about 1–2 in 200 traces); in the later window, A19 appears in about 46 in 100 traces and A20 in about 56 in 100 [OCC001, OCC002].
- In the earlier window, A40's outgoing transitions led almost exclusively to A25 (about 90 in 100); in the later window they lead mainly to A20 (about 53 in 100) and A19 (about 42 in 100), while the direct transition to A25 has nearly disappeared (about 0.5 in 100) [DIR003].
- In the later window, A19 and A20 each lead almost always to A25 (about 97 in 100 of their observed outgoing transitions); their earlier outgoing profiles rest on only 1–2 observations and are weak [DIR001, DIR002].
- The distance from A40 to A25 shifted from mostly one step (about 90 in 100 cases) to mostly two steps (about 93 in 100) [LAG001].
- In the later window, A40 runs before A19 in about 45 in 100 traces and before A20 in about 56 in 100, and A19 runs before A25 in about 46 in 100 and A20 before A25 in about 55 in 100; in the earlier window, A25 and A40 essentially always appeared without A19 or A20 [REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005].
- A19 and A20 behave as alternatives in the later window: at least one of them is present in about 99 in 100 traces, but they co-occur in only about 2 in 100 [REL001].
- A25 still occurs in every trace in both windows, and its position moved only slightly later (median from 0.36 to about 0.39 of the trace length) [POS003].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, an intermediate step — A19 or A20 — now runs between A40 and A25: A40, which previously led directly to A25 in about 90 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, now leads to A20 or A19 in about 95 in 100, and each of those in turn leads to A25 in about 97 in 100 of its transitions [DIR003, DIR001, DIR002, LAG001].

The relational evidence shows this directly and consistently: the A40-to-A25 distance moved from one step to two, the orderings A40 before A19/A20 and A19/A20 before A25 appear only in the later window, A19 and A20 are nearly mutually exclusive (consistent with one intermediate step chosen per case), and A25 itself remains universal and only slightly later, fitting a single added step rather than a broader reorganisation [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, POS003]. The volume of qualifying items (13, against roughly 0.46 expected if nothing had changed) also exceeds what an unchanged process typically produces [OCC001, OCC002, DIR003, LAG001].

Limitations: the earlier profiles of A19 and A20 rest on only 1–2 traces, so the evidence cannot distinguish a newly added activity from one that previously occurred extremely rarely, and it cannot show what determines whether A19 or A20 runs. An important competing explanation that cannot be ruled out is that A19 and A20 were pre-existing but very rare paths whose usage rose sharply; the two-step route via them is nonetheless directly observed in the later window [OCC001, OCC002].

Other differences: small residual routing remains in the later window — A19 and A20 each send about 1 in 100 outgoing transitions to activities other than A25, and A40 keeps small shares to A02, A06 and A23 [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003].
