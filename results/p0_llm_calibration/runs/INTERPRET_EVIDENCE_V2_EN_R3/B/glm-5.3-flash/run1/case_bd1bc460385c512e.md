### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A41 (present in every trace) was followed by one of four alternative activities — about 28 in 100 of its outgoing transitions went to A14, 26 to A15, 26 to A20 and 20 to A21 — and each of the four always led directly into A22, which ran two steps after A41. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the group of alternative activities A14, A15, A20 and A21 — of which each case executed exactly one — is now executed after A23 instead of before A22: earlier, A41 was followed by one of the four, which always led into A22, after which A23, A24 and A28 followed in fixed adjacency; in the later window, A41 leads straight to A22, and the single alternative activity is inserted in the A23–A28 segment, directly after A23 in about 48 in 100 traces or directly after A24 in about 52 in 100. The order reversal is shown directly by the relation items, the rerouting by the direction items, and the insertion points by the lag items, while the participation rates of the four stayed roughly stable, so this is not a mere shift in how often existing paths are taken; with 26 qualifying items against fewer than one expected if nothing had changed, and every difference larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [DIR002, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, POS004, REL001, REL008]

The evidence covers outgoing transitions and pairwise relations rather than complete models, so it cannot distinguish a relocation of the same step from the early occurrence being removed and a same-labelled step inserted later, and A22's own outgoing transitions are unobserved, leaving the A22-to-A23 connection inferred; whether the choice of insertion point (after A23 vs after A24) follows a rule or chance cannot be determined from these windows. [DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG002]

Other differences: at A03, outgoing transitions shifted away from A04 (about 40% to about 22%) towards A09 (about 21% to about 35%); this routing change is independent of the hypothesis above. [DIR001]
