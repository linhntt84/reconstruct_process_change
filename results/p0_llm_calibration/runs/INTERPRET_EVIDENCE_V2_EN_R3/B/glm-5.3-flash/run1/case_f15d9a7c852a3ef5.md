### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A19 and A20 were essentially not executed: A19 in 0 of 200 traces, A20 in 1 of 200. In the later window, A19 runs in 96 of 200 traces (about 48 in 100) and A20 in 103 of 200 (about 52 in 100), each at most once per trace, and never together — about 199 of 200 later traces contain exactly one of the two. These participation differences exceed those seen in about 99.5% of random re-splits of the data. [OCC006, OCC007, REL018]

- The two newcomers sit inside the existing mid-process flow. Before, they never co-occurred with A15, A16, A25, A26, A27, A28, A40, A45 or A46; in the later window they co-occur with each of these in roughly 48–52% of traces, always after A25, A26 and A40, always before A28, A45 and A46, and after or interleaved with (never before) A15 and A16, which still run in every trace. [REL014, REL015, REL019, REL020, REL022, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL031, REL035, REL036]

- Successor mixes moved accordingly: of A26's outgoing transitions, about 97% went to A27 before, while afterwards about 45% go to A27, 23% to A19 and 26% to A20; of A27's, about 98% went to A28 before, afterwards about 52% to A28 and about 24% each to A19 and A20; A19 and A20 themselves lead mainly to A27 (about 50%) and A28 (about 47%). Pairwise distances grew by one step: A26→A27 within one step fell from about 97 to 46 in 100 cases (about 50 in 100 now at two steps), A27→A28 within one step fell from about 99 to 52 in 100, and A26→A28 moved from two steps (about 97 in 100) to three steps (about 92 in 100). [DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013]

- A10 is executed far less often and in a different context: participation fell from 121 of 200 traces (about 60 in 100) to 47 of 200 (about 24 in 100); before it usually ran within the main flow (together with A15/A16 in about 60 in 100 traces), afterwards usually instead of it (together in about 24, with those activities running alone in about 77 in 100). Its outgoing transitions shifted from about 82% to A16 to about 33% to A16, with new targets A25 (about 16%), A46 (about 14%) and A15 (about 12%), and its position became later and more variable (median 0.23 to 0.29, upper quartile 0.29 to 0.82). A10 and A19/A20 are now largely alternatives (co-occurrence about 11–12 in 100, versus A10 alone in about 60 in 100 before). [OCC005, DIR004, POS001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]

- Smaller shifts: A02, A04, A06 and A08 rose from about 3–7 to about 17–19 in 100 traces; the successor mixes of A02, A04 and A06 changed markedly but rest on only 4–15 outgoing transitions before, so they are weakly grounded; and immediate A45→A46 succession fell from about 99.5 to 82 in 100, with about 17 in 100 now at distance two. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG016, DIR013]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, one of the two previously unobserved activities — A19 or A20 — executes in almost every case as a single mid-process step between A26 and A28, roughly half the time between A26 and A27 and half between A27 and A28, with A19 and A20 acting as mutually exclusive alternatives. This is a possibility supported by the evidence, not a confirmed modification. The signs agree across indicators: participation rose from 0/200 and 1/200 to 96/200 and 103/200 with zero co-occurrence between the two; co-occurrence with the surrounding activities rose from zero to about 48–52 in 100 with a consistent order (after A25/A26/A40, before A28/A45/A46); A26 and A27 now send about half of their outgoing transitions to A19/A20, which lead back to A27/A28; and the A26→A27, A27→A28 and A26→A28 distances each stretched by exactly the one step this placement predicts. [OCC006, OCC007, REL018, REL022, REL031, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window strictly means only that A19 and A20 were not observed then, so the evidence cannot distinguish activities added to the process from existing ones that were simply never triggered before; event positions show placement within the trace but not the exact control-flow construct behind it. The main competing explanation — a stable process in which a rare variant surfaced only in the later sample — cannot be ruled out for any single item, but with 78 qualifying evidence items against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process, chance as a whole is not a plausible account of the pattern. [OCC006, OCC007, REL018]

Other differences: A10 is executed far less often (about 60 to about 24 in 100 traces) and now typically runs instead of, rather than within, the flow around A15/A16 and A25–A28/A40–A46, at a later and more variable position; whether this is connected to the A19/A20 change or is an independent shift cannot be decided from this evidence. Participation also rose modestly for A02, A04, A06 and A08 (about 3–7 to about 17–19 in 100), and a few local successions shifted slightly (for example, direct A45→A46 succession). [OCC005, REL001, REL003, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, LAG016]
