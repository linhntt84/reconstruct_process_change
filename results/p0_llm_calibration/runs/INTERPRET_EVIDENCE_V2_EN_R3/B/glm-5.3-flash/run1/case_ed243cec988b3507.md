### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A29, A30, A31 and A32 each occurred exactly once in every trace (participation 100 in 100). In the later window they still occur at most once per trace, but A29 and A30 appear in only about 56 in 100 traces (111 of 200) and A31 and A32 in about 46 in 100 (92 of 200). A25 and A26 still occur in every trace. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL005]

- The two activity pairs became mutually exclusive. Earlier, A29/A30 co-occurred with A31/A32 in every trace, always with A29 or A30 before A31 or A32. In the later window, A29 co-occurs with A31 (likewise A29–A32, A30–A31, A30–A32) in only about 2 in 100 traces: in about 54 in 100 traces only the A29/A30 pair is present, and in about 44 in 100 only the A31/A32 pair. Within each pair the activities still always appear together and in either order (A29 before A30 in about 28 in 100 traces and A30 before A29 in about 28; A31 before A32 about 26, A32 before A31 about 20). [REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]

- Routing into and out of the block changed. Earlier, A25 was followed only by A29 (about 55 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) or A30 (about 45 in 100); in the later window A25 is followed by A29, A30, A31 and A32 in roughly equal shares (about 28, 28, 26 and 19 in 100). A29 and A30, which earlier led mostly to A31 or A32 (about 45 in 100 of A29's transitions and 55 in 100 of A30's), now lead to A26 in about half of their observed transitions, while their transitions to A31/A32 have nearly disappeared (about 1–2 in 100). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]

- Distances contracted accordingly: the gap from A25 to A26 was 5 steps in all earlier traces and is 3 steps in about 99 in 100 later traces; the gaps from A25 to A31/A32 and from A29/A30 to A26 shrank from 3–4 steps to 1–2 steps. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006]

- A25, A29 and A30 sit slightly later within traces (mean relative position about 0.64 to 0.70 for A25, about 0.69 to 0.75 for A29 and A30); these are event positions, not clock times. [POS001, POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A25 and A26 changed from a fixed sequence containing both activity pairs into a choice between two alternative paths: earlier, every trace ran A25, then A29 and A30 in either order, then A31 and A32 in either order, then A26; in the later window, each trace runs A25 and A26 with exactly one of the two pairs in between — about 56 in 100 traces take A29 with A30, about 46 in 100 take A31 with A32, and both pairs together occur in only about 2 in 100 traces. The change is shown directly and consistently: participation falls from 100 in 100 to about 56/46 in 100 exactly at pair level; cross-pair co-occurrence collapses from 100 in 100 to about 2 in 100 while within-pair co-occurrence stays complete; A25's outgoing transitions spread from two targets to all four activities; A29 and A30 now lead to A26 instead of to A31/A32; and the A25–A26 distance contracts from 5 to 3 steps, exactly what removing one pair from the middle predicts. With 31 evidence items against roughly 0.44 expected for an unchanged process, sampling fluctuation cannot explain the pattern as a whole. The evidence cannot rule out that the later window reflects a different case mix (for example traces of a second variant that previously did not reach this log) rather than a routing change, and it does not show what determines which branch a trace takes; the roughly 2 in 100 later traces containing both pairs also show the exclusivity is not absolute. Other differences: A25, A29 and A30 occupy slightly later relative positions in the later window, consistent with the middle block containing one pair instead of two; two further lag changes involving A29–A31 and A30–A32 rest on only about 3 later traces and are too weak to interpret.
