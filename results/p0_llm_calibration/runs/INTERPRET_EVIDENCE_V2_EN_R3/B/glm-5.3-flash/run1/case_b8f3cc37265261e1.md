### Observed differences

- The clearest difference is a reversal of execution order between two groups of activities. In the earlier window, A19, A22, A25 and A26 ran before A27, A31 and before A20, A21, A23 and A24 — for example, A27 preceded A20 in about 99 in 100 traces containing both, and A22 preceded A23 in about 26 in 100. In the later window, all these relations are reversed: A20, A21, A23 and A24 now run first (A20 before A27 in about 99 in 100; A23 before A22 in about 27 in 100). [REL010, REL011, REL017, REL023]

- Event positions mirror this reversal. In the earlier window A20, A21, A23 and A24 sat at mean relative position about 0.63 of the trace, and A19, A22, A25, A26 at about 0.34; in the later window the first group sits at about 0.40 and the second at about 0.68. A27 and A31 shift only moderately later (about 0.37 to 0.47, and 0.56 to 0.66). [POS002, POS003, POS005, POS006, POS001, POS004, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010]

- Successors changed hands. Earlier, A19, A22, A25 and A26 led almost exclusively to A27 (about 91–100% of their outgoing transitions), while A20, A21, A23 and A24 led to A32 in about 21–26% of theirs; in the later window the first group leads to A32 (about 95–98%) and the second to A27 (about 21–28%). [DIR001, DIR004, DIR007, DIR008, DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006]

- The activities framing the two segments swapped partners: A42's outgoing transitions moved from A19/A22/A25/A26 (about 22–25% each) to A20/A21/A23/A24, and A31's moved from A20/A21/A23/A24 to A19/A22/A25/A26. [DIR009, DIR010]

- Typical distances changed accordingly: A42 reached A27 about 2 events later before versus about 5 events later after, and A31 reached A32 about 5 events later before versus about 2 events later after. The former follow-ups A31→A20/A21/A23/A24 and A20/A21/A23/A24→A32 (each in about 200 of 200 traces before) drop to about 1–3 traces, while A42→A20 rises from 1 to about 199 of 200 traces and A26→A32 from 1 to 55. [LAG014, LAG011, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG001, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG012, LAG006]

- Participation is essentially unchanged: A20, A21, A23 and A24 occur in every trace in both windows, and A19, A22, A25, A26 each occur in about 50–55 of 200 traces before and after (A19 about 45 and 40), so the difference lies in ordering, not in how often activities run. Within the four-activity group the internal order varies in both windows (A20 leads to A21, A23 and A24 in comparable shares). ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two blocks of activities exchanged their places in the process flow. In the earlier window, a choice among A19, A22, A25 and A26 — essentially one of the four per trace — ran between A42 and A27, while the four-activity sequence A20, A21, A23, A24 (all four in every trace, in varying order) ran between A31 and A32. In the later window, the four-activity sequence runs between A42 and A27, and the choice among A19/A22/A25/A26 runs between A31 and A32; A42, A27, A31 and A32 keep their places and hand their predecessors and successors to the other block. [DIR009, DIR010, DIR001, DIR002, LAG011, LAG014, REL010, REL023, POS002, POS008]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: all 32 relation items show the same near-complete order reversal between the two groups and towards A27/A31; the successor and adjacency changes in the direction and lag items agree in detail; positions move in opposite directions; and participation shares are stable, which argues against the activities having been added, removed or made optional. The 66 qualifying items also far exceed the roughly 0.81 items expected by chance when nothing changed, so the pattern as a whole is not a plausible sampling artefact. [REL007, REL017, REL023, REL031, DIR002, DIR009, LAG011, POS002, POS008]

Limitations: the evidence shows order, short-range adjacency and relative positions, not the complete model. The segment between A27 and A31 is not covered by the evidence, so the exact insertion points of the two blocks cannot be verified directly, and a rewiring of the surrounding routing that produces the same observable order would look identical here. The proportions for A19, A22, A25 and A26 also rest on only about 50 traces per window. [POS001, POS004, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010]

Other differences: a handful of weak outgoing edges appear only in the later window — each about 1 in 200 transitions, for example from A24 to A09/A26/A37, from A23 to A46/A08, from A20 to A05/A07/A10/A14 and from A21 to A40/A10; at this size they are consistent with sampling noise on rare paths and are not part of the main hypothesis. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006]
