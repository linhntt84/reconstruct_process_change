### Observed differences

- Two activities that each ran in about half of the earlier traces all but disappeared: A19 fell from about 99 to about 1 of 200 traces, and A20 from about 103 to about 4 of 200 traces. Earlier they acted as strict alternatives — they co-occurred in only about 1 in 100 traces and almost every trace contained exactly one of them; in the later window they almost never appear at all [OCC001, OCC002, REL009].
- In the later window, two previously unobserved activities now run in almost every trace: A25 and A26 each went from 0 of 200 traces to about 198 of 200 (about 99 in 100), occurring once per trace [OCC003, OCC004].
- The new pair runs together without a fixed order: A25 and A26 co-occur in about 99 of 100 later traces, with A25 before A26 in about 50 in 100 and A26 before A25 in about 49 in 100; earlier they were never seen in the same trace [REL018].
- The handover from A42 changed directly: of A42's observed outgoing transitions, about 42 in 100 went to A20 and about 40 in 100 to A19 earlier, while in the later window about 43 in 100 go to A25 and about 41 in 100 to A26 (based on roughly 200 traces in each window) [DIR008].
- The new pair occupies the old position: earlier, A42 preceded A19 in about 49 of 100 traces and A20 in about 52 of 100, and A19/A20 in turn preceded A27 in about 48–51 of 100; in the later window, A42 precedes A25/A26 in about 98 of 100 traces and A25/A26 precede A27 in about 98–99 of 100 [REL012, REL013, REL016, REL017, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022].
- A14 shows the same flip: earlier it preceded A19/A20 in the roughly half of traces containing them and never co-occurred with A25/A26; in the later window it precedes A25/A26 in about 98–99 of 100 traces [REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008].
- The step distance from A42 to A27 grew: A27 followed about 2 steps after A42 in about 82 of 100 earlier cases, but about 3 steps in about 78 of 100 later cases (and 4 steps in about 21 of 100), consistent with one more activity now sitting between them [LAG004].
- Smaller redistributions appear elsewhere: A27's relative position moved slightly later (mean about 0.41 to 0.43 of the trace), and the outgoing shares of A06, A10 and A07 shifted (A06 toward A46/A25/A26, A10 away from A46/A48 toward A15/A16, A07 from A05 toward A14) [POS005, DIR001, DIR003, DIR002].
- Some after-window profiles rest on very few observations — A19's and A20's own outgoing transitions on 1 and 4 transitions, and two lag profiles on 1–2 pairs — and are weak on their own [DIR004, DIR005, LAG002, LAG003].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the either/or step between A42/A14 and A27 was replaced by a two-activity step: earlier, each trace executed exactly one of A19 or A20 (each in about half of the traces, never both), whereas in the later window A19 and A20 are essentially not executed and both A25 and A26 run in nearly every trace, one after the other in either order, before A27 [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL009, REL018, REL019, REL021].

Supporting signs: the participation swaps are near-complete and well supported (about 99 and 103 of 200 traces before versus about 1 and 4 after for A19/A20; 0 versus about 198 of 200 for A25/A26); A42's outgoing transitions moved directly from A19/A20 to A25/A26 on about 200 traces in each window; the new pair sits where the old one did — after A42 and A14, before A27, in about 98–99 of 100 traces; and the A42→A27 distance grew from about 2 to about 3 steps, consistent with one extra activity in the segment [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR008, REL007, REL008, REL020, REL022, REL019, REL021, LAG004].

Limitations: zero participation only means A25/A26 were not observed in the earlier window, so a rare pre-existing occurrence cannot be excluded; the evidence also cannot show whether the disappearance of A19/A20 and the appearance of A25/A26 were one coordinated replacement or two independent changes at the same location, and the residual outgoing details for A19/A20 rest on only 1–4 observations [OCC003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG002, LAG003].

Other differences: the outgoing shares of A06, A10 and A07 were redistributed (A06 toward A46/A25/A26, A10 away from A46/A48 toward A15/A16, A07 from A05 toward A14), and A27 sits slightly later within traces; these are not part of the main hypothesis [DIR001, DIR003, DIR002, POS005].
