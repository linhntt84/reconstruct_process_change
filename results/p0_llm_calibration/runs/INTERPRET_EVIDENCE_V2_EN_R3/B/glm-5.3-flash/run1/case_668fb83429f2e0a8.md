### Observed differences

- **A fixed order between two groups of activities dissolved.** In the earlier window, every trace ran A20, A21, A23 and A24 strictly before A25, A26, A27 and A28: for all fifteen cross-pairs this order held in 96–100 in 100 traces. In the later window it is no longer fixed: A25 now runs before A20 and before A21 in about 75 in 100 traces (before: about 99 in 100 the opposite way) and before A24 in about 91 in 100 (before: always after); A27 and A28 now run before A24 in about 74 in 100 traces; and for pairs such as A20–A27, A20–A28, A21–A28, A23–A25 and A24–A26 both orders occur in roughly equal shares (about 46–52 vs 48–52). All fifteen pairs still co-occur in 100 in 100 traces in both windows, so all eight activities remain mandatory — only their ordering changed. - **Successors at the boundaries of the two groups changed.** A24, previously followed by A25 in essentially all cases, is now followed by A34 in about 49 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and by A25 in only about 7 in 100. A33, previously followed by A23 almost always, now leads to A25 in about 50 in 100 and to A23 in about 49 in 100. A26, previously followed by A34 almost always, now goes to A34 in about 50 in 100 and back into the first group in about 48 in 100. New transitions connect the groups in both directions: A20, A21 and A23 now lead to A25/A26/A27/A28 in about 30–45 in 100 of their outgoing transitions, and A25–A28 lead back into A20/A21/A23/A24 in about 30–50 in 100. - **Relative positions shifted consistently with the reordering.** A20, A21, A23 and A24 occur later in the trace than before (mean relative position, e.g., A24 from about 0.61 to about 0.69), while A25, A26, A27 and A28 occur earlier (A25 from about 0.64 to about 0.56; A27/A28 from about 0.68 to about 0.62). - **Fixed step distances collapsed where new direct transitions appear.** A33→A25 was always 5 steps apart and is now 1 step apart in about half of the traces; A24→A34 was always 5 steps apart and is now 1 step apart in about half; A20→A25 was 2–3 steps apart and is now 1 step apart in about half of the far fewer traces where the ordered pair still occurs within 5 steps (about 47 in 200, down from 200 in 200). - **One unrelated routing shift.** After A48, the split between A49 and A45 moved from about 65/32 to about 48/51, with both transitions well observed in both windows (about 180 traces each). [DIR010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the strict sequencing between the segment A20–A21–A23–A24 and the segment A25–A26–A27–A28 was relaxed: where the first segment previously always preceded the second, the later window shows both orders — A25 typically before A20/A21/A24, A27/A28 typically before A24, and no fixed order for several other cross-pairs — together with a new entry into the second segment (A33 now leads to A25 in about half of its occurrences) and a new exit from the first (A24 now leads directly to A34 in about half). [REL001, REL005, REL012, REL014, REL015, DIR004, DIR009]

This goes far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces: 72 evidence items qualified against roughly 0.84 expected by chance, every item's difference exceeds about 99.5 in 100 random re-splits, and the fifteen pair relations — each measured on all 200 traces in both windows — all point the same way, with the directly-follows, position and distance indicators corroborating one and the same reordering rather than independent phenomena. Participation is unchanged (all eight activities occur in every trace in both windows), so the difference lies in the ordering constraints, not in activities becoming optional or paths being replaced. [REL001, DIR004, POS004, LAG022]

Limitations: the pairwise order shares cannot fully distinguish a freely relaxed order from a small set of fixed route variants chosen per case, and they leave the detailed new structure open (for example, A23 still precedes A27 in about 72 in 100 traces while A20–A27 is about 50/50); the hypothesis is therefore a possibility supported by the evidence, not a confirmed model of the new flow. [REL003, REL010]

Other differences: the shift in A48's outgoing split between A49 and A45 (about 65/32 to about 48/51) is a modest change in how often existing paths are taken and is not part of the hypothesis above; a few new transitions occur at roughly 1 in 200 traces (e.g., A24→A05, A27→A09), and one pair relation (A26 before A23) rests on only 1 before / 16 after observations, too weak to interpret. [DIR010, DIR004, DIR007, LAG027]
