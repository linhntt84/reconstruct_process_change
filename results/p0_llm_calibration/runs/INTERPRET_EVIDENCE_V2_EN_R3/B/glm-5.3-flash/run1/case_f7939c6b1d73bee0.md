### Observed differences

- A02 nearly dropped out of the flow and moved earlier: in the earlier window it ran in about 65 of 100 traces, almost always once and near the end (median relative position about 0.97); in the later window it runs in only about 20 of 100 traces, at earlier and much more variable positions (median about 0.55). [OCC001, POS001]
- A02's handover changed completely: in the earlier window about 93 of 100 of its observed outgoing transitions went to A46, typically immediately (gap 1 in about 99 of 100 observed A02→A46 pairs); in the later window only about 14 go to A46, while A44 (about 27), A15 (about 14) and A20 (about 11) take over, and observed A02→A46 pairs within five steps fell from 127 to 16. [DIR001, LAG003]
- A45 now bypasses A02: in the earlier window about 63 of 100 of A45's outgoing transitions went to A02 and about 36 to A46, usually two steps later (consistent with A02 sitting in between); in the later window about 84 go straight to A46, about 85 of 100 of them immediately, and the share of traces in which A45 precedes A02 fell from about 60 to about 3 in 100. [DIR011, LAG013, REL009]
- The middle block A21–A24 lost its fixed chain: in the earlier window A29 led to A21 or A22 (about 49 each), these led on to A23 or A24, and A23/A24 led to A30 in one step (about 93 and 98 of 100); in the later window A29 feeds all four activities (about 20–26 each), A21/A22 go to A30 about half the time and A23/A24 only about 40–50, and the previously one-directional A22-before-A24 order (30 of 100 traces, never reversed) became mixed (8 vs 10 in 100). [DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, REL011, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012]
- A06 and A10, previously seen in about 6 of 100 traces each, now appear in about 18 and 17 of 100, with A10's outgoing traffic now aimed mainly at A15 and A46; both rest on modest counts (13→36 and 11→34 traces). [OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, DIR003]
- The rest of the backbone barely moved: A15, A29, A30 and A45 kept full participation and their relative positions shifted only slightly later (about 0.01–0.03 on the position scale). [POS002, POS005, POS006, POS007]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the late A02 step between A45 and A46 was largely skipped in the later window. In the earlier window roughly 65 of 100 traces ran A45 into A02 and A02 on to A46 at the very end; in the later window A02 appears in only about 20 of 100 traces, A45 leads directly and immediately to A46 in about 84 of 100 of its outgoing transitions (previously about 36), and A02's own handover to A46 nearly vanished (about 93 down to about 14 of 100). Participation, positional, directional and relational indicators all describe this same shift, and with 45 flagged items against roughly 0.7 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. An important competing reading remains open: A02 still occurs in about 20 of 100 later traces, now earlier and with dispersed successors, so the evidence shows the A45→A02→A46 relay was abandoned in most traces but cannot rule out that A02 keeps a residual, optional role elsewhere rather than having been removed from the process. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR011, REL009, LAG003, LAG013]

Other differences:
- The reordering of the A21–A24 block — a fixed two-stage chain in the earlier window becoming a flexible block whose members can be bypassed or taken in varying order, with the A22/A24 order now mixed — is a distinct change in ordering constraints and is not folded into the A02 hypothesis. [DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, REL011, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012]
- The increased appearance of A06 and A10, with new outgoing edges towards A15, A46 and A44, is a separate participation change observed on low volumes (about 11–36 traces per window), so those shares are tentative. [OCC002, OCC003, DIR002, DIR003]
