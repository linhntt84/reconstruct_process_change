### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, the segment between A46 and A31 appears to be carried by a new set of activities: A20, A28, A29 and A30 — not observed in the earlier window — now each execute once in nearly every trace, after A46 and before A31, in no fixed order among themselves, while the activities that previously followed A46 on this branch — A19, A21, A22 and A23, each present in roughly 20–28 in 100 traces and leading mainly to A31 — are now executed in only about 1–2 in 100 traces. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, REL038, REL039, DIR011]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the ordering after A46 and before A31 is shown relationally in about 97–98 in 100 traces for each new activity; A46's successor set changed from the old four to the new four; the A46-to-A31 distance grew from about two steps to beyond five; and A31's position moved later, all pointing the same way. About 100 evidence items qualified in total against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed, so the pattern as a whole is not what random re-splits of a stable process produce. [REL035, DIR011, LAG003, POS012]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that the new activities were not observed then, not that they were impossible; the old activities still occur in about 1–2 in 100 later traces, so the evidence shows they became rare rather than that they were removed, and their few later occurrences (2–4 traces) are too few to characterise their current routing. A competing explanation the evidence cannot fully rule out is that both activity sets existed all along as rare and dominant variants and the windows caught a shift between them, although a near-complete flip across 200-trace windows makes pure sampling fluctuation unlikely. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]

Other differences: A06's participation halved (about 27 to 13–14 in 100 traces) with a later, more spread position and a much smaller share of its outgoing transitions going to A15 (about 89% to 43%, with new successors such as A52 appearing), and A14's outgoing transitions shifted towards A15 (about 75% to 93%); these are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002]
