### Observed differences

- Repetition of four activities stopped: in the earlier window, A30, A31, A36 and A37 each occurred about 3.3 times per trace, and each was repeated within a trace in about 67–68 in 100 traces. In the later window, all four still appear in every trace, but each occurs about once per trace, with repetition in at most about 2 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

- The return path of the cycle disappeared: earlier, about 68 in 100 of A37's outgoing transitions led back to A30 (about 29 in 100 led to A25); in the later window, about 97 in 100 lead to A25, and transitions from A37 to A30 are no longer observed. [DIR001]

- Ordering became fixed: the four activities still co-occur in 100 in 100 traces in both windows, but earlier each pair was interleaved or repeated in about 67–68 in 100 traces, whereas in the later window A30 precedes A31, A36 and A37 in about 98–99 in 100 traces, A36 precedes A37 in all traces, and A31 and A36 appear in both orders in similar shares (about 56 vs 44 in 100), i.e. without a fixed order between them. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

- The block became compact: in the later window, A31→A36 and A36→A31 fall one step apart in about 98–99 in 100 observed cases, and A30→A31, A30→A36, A31→A37 and A36→A37 fall one to two steps apart in about 97–99 in 100 cases; earlier these gaps were spread out across one to five steps, with a visible share at four to five steps. Several further gap items for the later window rest on a single observation each and carry little weight. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG006, LAG007, LAG009, LAG010, LAG001, LAG004, LAG005, LAG008, LAG011, LAG012]

- Positions within the trace became more regular: mean relative positions barely moved (for example, A30's median about 0.43 to 0.40), but the spread narrowed sharply, with standard deviations falling from about 0.13 to about 0.05–0.06 and A37's interquartile range tightening from about 0.41–0.59 to about 0.48–0.53. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the repeated, interleaved execution of the block involving A30, A31, A36 and A37 stopped: in the earlier window these four activities each ran about 3.3 times per trace in an interleaved cycle in which A37 typically returned to A30, while in the later window each runs about once per trace in a compact fixed sequence — A30 first, then A31 and A36 adjacent in either order, then A37, which now continues to A25 instead of returning to A30. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

The signs are direct and consistent: participation stayed at 100 in 100 traces while recurrence collapsed, interleaving gave way to fixed order in all six activity pairs, close one-to-two-step succession and tighter position spread corroborate a single compact pass, and the volume of qualifying items (27, against roughly 0.56 expected if nothing had changed) cannot be explained by chance as a whole — though the items partly describe the same underlying trace behaviour and are not independent confirmations. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG006, LAG007, LAG009, LAG010, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, OCC001, REL001]

Limitations: the evidence cannot rule out that the two windows draw different mixes of process variants (for example a repeating and a single-pass variant) of an otherwise unchanged process; unobserved edges such as A37→A30 show absence from the sample, not removal from the process; and the exact within-block gap measurements in the later window rest on very few observations, in some cases a single one. [DIR001, LAG001, LAG004, LAG005, LAG008, LAG011, LAG012]
