### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the exclusive branch between A22 and the four-activity block A31, A32, A37, A38 was replaced by a fixed sequence in which both are always executed: in the earlier window, each trace ran either A22 alone (about 47 of 100 traces) or the whole block (about 53 of 100) between A21 and A26, whereas in the later window A20 leads into the block, the block leads into A23, and A21 leads through A22 onward to A26, so every trace now contains both the block and A22. The support is direct and consistent: 52 evidence items qualified against fewer than 1 expected by chance across 116 screened units, so sampling noise cannot explain the pattern as a whole; the alternatives-together change is shown directly by co-occurrence rising from 0 to 100 of 100 traces, the relocation by order flips against A21 and A23 together with matching successor and distance changes, and every affected activity is well observed in both windows (about half of traces before, all traces after), which rules out an explanation resting on unobserved or rare behaviour. One competing reading the evidence cannot fully exclude is that this arose as two separate changes — the block becoming mandatory after A20 and A22 becoming mandatory after A21 — rather than a single restructuring of the branch point; the observed behaviour is the same under either reading, and differences below the reporting thresholds elsewhere in the process would not be visible.
