### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, an alternative route through A27, A28, A33 and A34 was inserted between A21 and A23: in about 47 in 100 executions A22 is skipped and all four new activities run once each, in no fixed order, before continuing to A23, while the original A21 → A22 → A23 route is still taken in about 53 in 100. The support is direct — A21's outgoing transitions split exactly along this choice, A22 and the new block are mutually exclusive, and the A21-to-A23 distance changed from always 2 to 2 or 5 — and consistent across occurrence, routing, relation and lag indicators; the number of qualifying items (47) is also far above the roughly 0.5 expected by chance under this screening, so sampling noise alone cannot explain the ensemble [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, REL011, REL017, REL021, LAG001]. Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window only shows the four activities were not observed then, so a pre-existing very rare route cannot be ruled out, and the evidence cannot show what determines which route is chosen.

Other differences: A06's choice of successor shifted toward A07 (from about 26% to about 47% of A06's outgoing transitions, with A08 and A09 declining); this upstream redistribution is not part of the main hypothesis and rests on about 90–95 traces per window [DIR001].
