### Observed differences

- **A04 became far more frequent and appears earlier.** In the earlier window A04 ran in about 15 in 100 traces (about 30 of 200); in the later window it runs in about 72 in 100 (about 144 of 200), with mean occurrences per trace up from about 0.16 to 0.79, and it now appears early in traces (median relative position about 0.15 versus about 0.37 before). [OCC001, POS001]

- **A14 now hands over to A04, and A04 to A15.** Previously about 83 in 100 of A14's outgoing transitions led to A15 and only about 2 in 100 to A04; in the later window about 70 in 100 lead to A04 and about 29 to A15. A04's own outgoing transitions, previously spread thinly over many activities (at most about 17 in 100 to one successor), now lead to A15 in about 89 in 100 cases. [DIR005, DIR002]

- **Adjacency and order match this rewiring.** A04 immediately follows A14 in about 98 of 100 observed pairs (about 142 pairs versus 5 before), and A15 follows A04 within one step in about half of about 143 pairs (versus 7 before); across all traces, A14 precedes A04 in about 65 in 100 traces (before, the two appeared together in only 15 in 100 traces, almost always A14 without A04), and A04 precedes A15 in about 40 in 100 traces, interleaved in about 31. [LAG005, LAG001, REL001, REL002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, A04 takes a place in the flow between A14 and A15: A14, which previously passed flow on directly to A15 in about 83 of 100 of its outgoing transitions, now leads to A04 in about 70 of 100; A04 leads on to A15 in about 89 of 100; the A14-to-A04 handover is immediate in nearly all observed pairs; and A04's participation rose from about 15 to 72 in 100 traces at an early position, while A15 moved slightly later. [DIR005, DIR002, LAG005, LAG001, REL001, REL002, OCC001, POS001, POS002]

The support is direct and consistent: the successor change is observed on both edges (A14's and A04's outgoing shares), the order relation changed from "A14 without A04" (about 85 in 100 traces) to "A14 before A04" (about 65 in 100), and one-step lags confirm adjacency; the 68 qualifying items are many times the roughly 0.86 expected without a change, and the pattern cannot be reduced to A04 merely occurring more often, because the destination shares of A14's and A04's outgoing transitions themselves changed. [DIR005, DIR002, REL001, LAG005]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show that A04 sits on every passage from A14 to A15 — about 29 in 100 of A14's outgoing transitions still lead straight to A15, and A04 and A15 interleave in about 31 of 100 traces — so the new placement is dominant rather than exclusive, and the exact shares remain subject to sampling variation in the two 200-trace windows; several items also describe the same traces and are not independent confirmations. [DIR005, REL002]

Other differences: a second, directly observed difference is that A19, A20, A25 and A26 now execute before A27 and A28 (previously after them), lead predominantly to A27, and are fed by A46, which previously led mainly to A27, while A28, A29 and A33 now form an immediate chain; separately, A06, A08 and A10 became rare (about 6–7 in 100 traces, from about 18–22), though the later window rests on few traces for them.
