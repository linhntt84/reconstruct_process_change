### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the block A36, A37, A42, A43 executes after A26 as an alternative to A27 — in about half of the traces the block runs and A27 is skipped, in the other half A27 runs and the block is skipped — while A25 passes directly to A28 and A31 closes either branch; earlier, every trace executed A25, then the block, then A28, A26, A27 and A31. [DIR003, LAG002, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027, DIR004, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

The hypothesis is shown directly by relational and lag evidence rather than position shifts alone: A25 reaches A28 within one step, A28 and A26 now precede the block where they previously followed it, A27 and the block are mutually exclusive, and the successor changes at A25, A26 and the block members match this picture. The volume of qualifying items (96 against about 1 expected without a change) rules out chance re-splits as an explanation of the pattern as a whole, and the block's internal behaviour is unchanged — its four activities still run together in mixed order whenever they run. [LAG002, REL024, REL025, REL026, REL027, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, REL038, REL047]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the block-skipping path already existed as a rare variant before (it appears in 0 of the 200 earlier traces, which is compatible with a prevalence below about 1–2 in 200) and only became frequent, or that the windows sample a shifted mix of two coexisting variants; the samples also cannot establish whether other, unobserved paths changed. [REL024, OCC002]

Other differences: A06 participation fell from about 32 in 100 to about 9.5 in 100 traces and its position became more variable, but its later routing profile rests on only about 19 traces and 17 outgoing transitions, so its new targets are weakly supported; A02's outgoing mix also changed, but with only 5 outgoing transitions observed earlier this is consistent with a rare variant.
