### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Screening flagged 38 items where roughly 0.6 would be expected if nothing had changed, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, so chance cannot explain the volume as a whole. Within it, the evidence supports the possibility that the mutually exclusive alternative pair A19 and A20 — which before ran in every trace between A26 and the A27→A28 ending, in either order relative to A27 and virtually always before A28 — is no longer executed in the later window, so cases proceed directly A26 → A27 → A28. Supporting signs: A19 fell from 91 to 0 of 200 traces and A20 from 109 to 2; all co-occurrence of A19/A20 with A26, A27 and A28 disappeared; A26 and A27 now route almost exclusively to A27 and A28; and the A26→A28 distance shortened from three steps to two, exactly what dropping one intermediate step predicts.
