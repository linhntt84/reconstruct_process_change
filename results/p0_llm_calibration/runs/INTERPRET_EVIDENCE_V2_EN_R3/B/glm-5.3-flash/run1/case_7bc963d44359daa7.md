### Observed differences

- The step performed by A14 or A15 collapsed from mandatory to often absent. In the earlier window every trace contained exactly one of the two — A14 in about 49 in 100 traces, A15 in about 51 — and they never appeared together. In the later window A14 appears in about 26 in 100 and A15 in about 28, and about 46 in 100 traces contain neither. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001]

- In the later window A35 passes directly to A20 in the traces without A14/A15. Earlier, A35 was always followed by A14 or A15 (about 49 vs 51 in 100 of its outgoing transitions), and A20 always came two event positions after A35, A21 five positions after A35, in all 200 traces. In the later window, in about 46 in 100 traces A20 immediately follows A35 and A21 comes four positions after A35 — a direct A35→A20 transition that was not observed earlier (0 of about 200 outgoing transitions). [DIR004, LAG005, LAG006]

- When A14 or A15 still runs in the later window, its placement is unchanged: A35 still precedes it, and it still precedes A20, A21, A27 and A28 in every such trace; its co-occurrence with the downstream activities fell in proportion (for example A14 with A20 from about 49 to 26 in 100). The downstream activities A20, A27, A28 and A21 still occur in every trace in both windows. [REL002, REL003, REL006, REL007]

- The two alternative orders of A27 and A28 between A20 and A21 shifted in frequency: the order A20→A27→A28→A21 was taken in about 54 in 100 traces earlier and about 43 in 100 in the later window, while A20→A28→A27→A21 rose from about 46 to about 58 in 100. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the alternative step between A35 and A20 — executed as exactly one of A14 or A15 in every earlier trace — became skippable: in the later window about 46 in 100 traces omit it entirely and run directly from A35 to A20, while traces that still include A14 or A15 keep the earlier placement, so the step was skipped rather than relocated. The skip interpretation is shown directly: the "neither" relation rose from never occurring to about 46 in 100 traces, A35 gained a direct transition to A20, and the lag shifts (A20 at one position, A21 at four positions after A35 in those traces) show that nothing was inserted between A35 and A20 and that the downstream segment simply moved one position closer. The drop rests on substantial counts in both windows (about 98–102 traces earlier vs 52–55 later per activity), the pattern is consistent across all 25 qualifying items — far more than the roughly 0.42 expected if nothing had changed — and each item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits of the pooled data. The evidence cannot show whether the roughly 46-in-100 skip share is a stable new behaviour or part of a gradual shift, nor can it rule out that the two windows differ in case mix rather than in the process itself. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, DIR004, LAG005, LAG006]

Other differences: the relative frequency of the two orderings of A27 and A28 between A20 and A21 changed (A27 before A28 from about 54 to 43 in 100; A28 before A27 from about 46 to 58). Both orders are taken in substantial shares in both windows and all involved activities occur throughout, so this is a change in how often existing alternative orders are chosen, not a repositioning; the evidence does not show whether it belongs to the same underlying change as the skippable A14/A15 step. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004]
