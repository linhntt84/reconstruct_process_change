### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window the stretch between A26 and A28 runs over a single route: the block A32, A33, A38 and A39, which in the earlier window executed together between A26 and A28 in about 39 in 100 traces, is skipped in almost every later trace (each present in only about 1 in 100 traces), while the path through A27 became the only observed route — A26 leads to A27 in about 97 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (about 60 in 100 before), A27 precedes A28 in 98 in 100 of their shared traces, and A28 follows A26 within two steps in about 97 in 100 traces (61 in 100 before, with about 37 in 100 five steps apart). The redirection is shown directly and consistently by participation, routing, co-occurrence and distance indicators, and the number of qualifying items (64, against fewer than one expected for an unchanged process) rules out chance as an overall explanation; the earlier block is documented by about 78 traces per activity, so its near-disappearance in an equally sized sample is not a rare-variant artefact. The evidence cannot distinguish removal of the four activities from their replacement by behaviour these indicators do not capture (zero participation only means they were not observed), the later-window profiles of the dropped activities rest on only 2–3 traces, and a coinciding shift in the mix of cases entering the window cannot be ruled out. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR003, REL006, REL011, LAG001, REL007, REL018]

Other differences: A10 became more frequent and repositioned (about 8 to 25 in 100 traces, mean relative position about 0.62 to 0.81), is now a frequent target of A45 — A10 follows A45 within one step in about 70 in 100 of the observed pairs versus about 44 in 100 before, on few observations — and A27 now precedes A10 in about 22 in 100 traces (about 3 in 100 before); independently, A11's outgoing transitions shifted from A12 towards A13. These changes are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR006, LAG009, REL001, DIR002]
