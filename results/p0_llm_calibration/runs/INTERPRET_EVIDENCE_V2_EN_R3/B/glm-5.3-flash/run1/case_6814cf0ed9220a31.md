### Observed differences

- Four activities that were universal became optional: A34, A35, A36 and A37 each appeared in all 200 earlier-window traces, but in the later window they appear in only about 46–56 in 100 traces (A34 and A35: 112 of 200; A36: 92; A37: 94). [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]

- The two pairs became mutually exclusive. Before, A34 co-occurred with A36 in 100 in 100 traces (likewise A34–A37, A35–A36, A35–A37); after, these cross-pair co-occurrences survive in only about 2–3 in 100 traces, while about 54 in 100 later traces contain the A34/A35 pair only and about 44 contain the A36/A37 pair only — virtually every later trace now contains exactly one of the two pairs. Within the pairs, A34 still appears together with A35 (together in 55 in 100, both absent in 44) and A36 together with A37 (together in 46, both absent in 53). [REL029, REL030, REL031, REL036, REL037, REL042]

- Routing follows this split. A30's outgoing transitions, previously almost only to A34 (54%) and A35 (44%), now divide between A36 (24%), A35 (28%), A34 (26%) and A37 (18%); A34 and A35, which previously led mostly to each other and on to A36/A37, now lead directly to A31 in about 46–47% of their outgoing transitions. [DIR006, DIR007, DIR008]

- Step distances shrank as if one pair were skipped: A30 to A31 went from a 5-step gap in all observed cases to a 3-step gap in 87 in 100, and A34 to A31, A35 to A31 and A30 to A36/A37 moved from typical gaps of 3–5 steps to 1–2 steps. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG009, LAG015]

- The surrounding frame is stable: A14, A15, A30 and A31 still occur in every trace, A30 still precedes A34/A35 and A34/A35 still precede A31 where they co-occur, and the positions of A14 and A15 shifted only slightly. [REL021, REL022, REL025, POS002, POS003]

- A10 nearly disappeared: present in 62.5 in 100 traces before (125 of 200) versus 15 in 100 after (30 of 200). Before, A43 led to A10 in 71% of its outgoing transitions and A10 led to A44 in 93%; after, A43 leads directly to A44 in 84% and to A10 in about 3%, and former partners such as A14 now appear without A10 in 85 in 100 traces (37.5 before). [OCC004, DIR003, DIR009, REL001]

- A04, A06 and A08 became more frequent, from about 5–7 in 100 traces to about 15–20 in 100 (A08: 14 to 41 of 200 traces), appearing as minor successors of several activities. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

About 100 evidence items qualified, many times the roughly 1 expected by chance in this screening, so the pattern as a whole cannot be explained by random re-sampling of a stable process [OCC005, REL030, DIR006, LAG002]. The evidence supports the hypothesis that the fixed block between A30 and A31 — the pair A34/A35 followed by the pair A36/A37, executed in every earlier trace — became optional in the later window: each trace now typically executes exactly one of the two pairs, about 55 in 100 taking A34+A35 and about 45 taking A36+A37, with the A34/A35 branch then continuing directly to A31. This is shown directly by the collapse of cross-pair co-occurrence from 100 to 2–3 in 100 while within-pair co-occurrence persisted, and it is consistently echoed by A30's outgoing share splitting between the pairs, by A34/A35 now leading to A31, and by the corresponding shrinkage of the A30→A31 and A34→A31 step distances [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, REL029, REL030, REL031, REL036, REL037, REL042, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG002, LAG009]. The evidence describes behaviour in two finite samples and cannot show whether the later window reflects one modified process or a changed mixture of pre-existing variants, although the complete absence of any skipped pair in 200 before-traces makes a previously common skipping variant unlikely; a few lag items rest on 1–4 observations in one window and were not relied on [REL030, LAG012, LAG013, LAG014].

Other differences:
- A10 became skippable: before, most traces ran A43 → A10 → A44, whereas in the later window A43 leads directly to A44 in 84% of its outgoing transitions and A10 appears in only 15 in 100 traces, with its former partners usually executing without it. [OCC004, DIR003, DIR009, REL001, REL011]
- A04, A06 and A08 occur in roughly three times as many traces as before (about 15–20 in 100 versus 5–7); the routing detail behind this rests on limited observations, so it is a weaker observation. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002]
