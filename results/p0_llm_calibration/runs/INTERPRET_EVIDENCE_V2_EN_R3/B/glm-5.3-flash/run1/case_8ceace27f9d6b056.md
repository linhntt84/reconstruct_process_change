### Observed differences

- In the later window, four activities that were almost or completely absent earlier now run regularly: A19 in about 30 in 100 traces (about 1 in 200 before), A20 in about 27 in 100 (0 in 200 before), A25 in about 21 in 100 (0 before) and A26 in about 24 in 100 (about 1 in 200 before) [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006].
- These four act as mutually exclusive alternatives: any two of them co-occur in at most about 1 in 200 traces, so traces contain one of them rather than several [REL017, REL018, REL019, REL022, REL023, REL026].
- Routing around this group changed: earlier, A42 was followed by A27 in about 82 in 100 of its outgoing transitions; in the later window about 95 in 100 of A42's outgoing transitions lead to one of the four (A19 about 30, A20 about 24, A26 about 22, A25 about 19), and A20, A25 and A26 each lead on to A27 in about 88–96 in 100 of their outgoing transitions [DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007].
- Relational and distance evidence agrees: A42 precedes each of the four in about 21–31 in 100 traces and each of the four precedes A27 in about 21–30 in 100 traces, relations that were essentially absent earlier; the A42-to-A27 distance moved from one step (about 82 in 100 of pairs) to two steps (about 92 in 100), and the A15-to-A27 distance moved from mostly four steps to almost always five [REL020, REL021, REL024, REL025, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030, LAG005, LAG007].
- A06 became far more prominent and earlier-placed: participation rose from about 22 to about 57 in 100 traces and its mean relative position moved from about 0.43 to about 0.18; A14 now leads to A06 in about 53 in 100 of its outgoing transitions (about 5 in 100 before), A06 immediately follows A14 in about 96 in 100 of observed pairs, and A06 is followed by A15 in about 90 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, where earlier its successors were spread over many activities with A15 at about 21 in 100 [OCC001, DIR001, DIR003, POS001, REL001, REL002, LAG002].
- A08 receded, appearing in about 8 in 100 traces versus about 22 in 100 earlier, with a differently spread successor profile; this rests on only 16 traces in the later window [OCC002, DIR002].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the process now contains a new alternative step between A42 and A27: in the later window A42 is followed by exactly one of four mutually exclusive activities (A19, A20, A25, A26) in about 95 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, and these in turn lead to A27, whereas earlier A42 led directly to A27 in about 82 in 100 of its transitions and the four activities were seen in at most about 1 in 200 traces. The change is shown directly by routing, co-occurrence and distance evidence that all point the same way, and with 57 qualifying items against roughly 0.75 expected without change, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole; however, the items share the same traces, so they are facets of one difference rather than independent confirmations [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL020, REL021, REL024, REL025, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030, LAG005, LAG007]. The evidence cannot rule out that the four activities already existed but were executed so rarely earlier that they did not appear in the 200 earlier traces, since zero participation only means they were not observed [OCC004, OCC005].

Other differences: A06 now typically executes immediately after A14 and before A15 in about half of the traces, whereas earlier it was a rarer activity with scattered successors; the evidence does not show whether this is a repositioning of A06 or a newly frequent path, and it is not clearly part of the A42–A27 change [OCC001, DIR001, DIR003, POS001, REL001, REL002, LAG002]. A08 occurs in fewer traces and its outgoing transitions are redistributed, but the later profile is based on few observations [OCC002, DIR002]. A27 sits slightly later within traces than before (mean relative position about 0.39 to about 0.41), consistent with the added step [POS005].
