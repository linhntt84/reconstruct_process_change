### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, every trace executed exactly one of A14 or A15: A14 in about 46 in 100 traces (91 of 200) and A15 in about 55 in 100 (109 of 200), never both and never neither. In the later window, A14 was not observed in any of the 200 traces and A15 in only 1 of 200; in about 995 in 1000 traces neither activity appeared. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001]

- The surrounding activities A21, A22 and A23 still ran in every trace in both windows. Before, A21 was followed next by A22 in about 48 in 100 traces and by A14 or A15 otherwise, and A22 was followed next by A23 in about 52 in 100 traces and by A14 or A15 otherwise, so A22 and the A14/A15 step appeared in either order between A21 and A23. In the later window, A21 is followed directly by A22 in essentially all traces, and A22 by A23 in about 995 in 1000. [DIR003, DIR004, REL003, REL006, LAG003, LAG005]

- The distance from A21 to A23 was always 3 event positions in the earlier window; in the later window it is 2 positions in about 995 in 1000 traces, and A23's mean relative position moved only slightly earlier (about 0.57 to 0.55), consistent with an intermediate step disappearing rather than A23 being relocated. [LAG004, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative step between A21 and A23 — the execution of either A14 or A15, of which every earlier trace contained exactly one — is no longer executed in the later window, so that A21 now flows directly to A22 and on to A23. Nineteen evidence items qualified, far more than the roughly 0.37 expected for an unchanged process, and the change is shown directly: participation in A14 and A15 fell from about 46 and 55 in 100 traces to 0 and about 1 in 200, A21's outgoing transitions collapsed from a split between A22 and A14/A15 to A22 only, and the A21→A23 distance shortened from 3 to 2 positions. Because A14 is absent and A15 nearly absent in the later window, this is a removal or bypassing of a block rather than a shift of shares between existing paths. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, DIR003, DIR004, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005]

Zero participation only means A14 and A15 were not observed: the evidence cannot distinguish a complete removal of this block from it having become extremely rare, nor rule out that an activity outside the tested set took over this step. The many REL, LAG and DIR items describe the same underlying difference from different angles, so they are consistent views of one change rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002]

Other differences: none; the small shift in A23's average position is a by-product of the shorter traces rather than a separate relocation. [POS003]
