### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a new alternative route through A32 and A33 is executed between A26 and A28, bypassing A27: earlier, traces ran A26 → A27 → A28 in essentially every case, whereas in the later window about half take A26 → A32/A33 → A28 while A27 is skipped, its participation falling from all traces to about 46 in 100. The support is direct and consistent: A26 now leads to A32 or A33 in about half of its outgoing transitions, A32 and A33 lead on to A28 and to each other, A27 and A32/A33 almost never occur in the same trace, A32/A33 precede A28, A28 occurs without A27 in about half of the later traces, and the A26→A28 distance grew by about one step. A32's rise from 0 of 200 to 109 of 200 traces, and the 56 qualifying items against roughly 0.72 expected without change, cannot be explained by sampling variation as a whole.
