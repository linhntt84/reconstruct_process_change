### Observed differences

- The four activities A19, A20, A25 and A26 still occur at similar rates in both windows (each in roughly 20–28 of 100 traces), but in the later window they execute much later in the case: their mean relative position moved from about 0.32–0.34 to about 0.59–0.61, and the flow that previously ran from each of them into A27 (about 95–96% of their outgoing transitions) now runs into A29 and A33 instead (roughly 40–55% each), with no flow into A27. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]

- Their order relative to A27 and A28 reversed. Before, each of the four came before A27/A28 in about 20–28 of 100 traces and after in at most about 0.5 of 100; in the later window each comes after A27/A28 in about 20–27 of 100 traces and before in at most about 0.5 of 100. Co-occurrence with A27 stayed nearly unchanged (about 20–28 of 100 traces in both windows), so the order changed, not whether the activities appear together. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

- The flow around A28 and A29 absorbed the group: before, A28 led almost only to A29 (about 98%) and A29 almost only to A33 (about 98%); in the later window A28 and A29 each lead about half the time to one of A19/A20/A25/A26 (about 46–50% combined), the A28→A29 and A29→A33 distances are one step longer in about half of the traces, and A28 typically reaches A33 in three steps instead of two. [DIR008, DIR009, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]

- Upstream, A46 previously led to one of the four activities (about 97% of its outgoing transitions combined) and reached A27 two steps later; in the later window it leads directly to A27 (about 93%, one step). The distance from A15 and A16 to A27 shrank by about one step (typically 5 then 4 steps from A15, 4 then 3 from A16), while A27 and A28, which occur in every trace in both windows, shifted only slightly earlier (mean position about 0.35 to 0.32 and 0.59 to 0.56). [DIR010, LAG005, LAG006, LAG011, POS005, POS006]

- Two activities changed participation independently of this pattern: A04 now appears in far fewer traces (about 27.5 of 100 before vs 8.5 of 100 in the later window; 55 vs 17 traces), its outgoing flow into A16 dropped from about 80% to about 42% (later profile based on only 19 observations), A15's flow into A04 fell from about 14% to 2%, and the immediate A04→A16 succession became less dominant (about 95% of pairs at distance 1 before, about 49% in the later window, based on few observations). A08 appears in more traces (about 4 of 100 vs 14.5 of 100; 8 vs 29 traces) with a substantially different successor mix, though its earlier profile rests on only 8 observations. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the group of activities A19, A20, A25 and A26 was relocated: in the earlier window these four activities executed shortly before A27, around one third into the case, each followed almost always by A27; in the later window they execute after A28 and A29, around three fifths into the case, and are followed by A29 or A33 instead of A27. [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the order reversal is observed relationally for all four activities against both A27 and A28 while co-occurrence stays stable, so the activities did not become optional or rarer; the positional shift and the successor change agree with it; and the surrounding distances change exactly as a relocation would imply — A46 and A15/A16 now reach A27 about one step sooner, while A28→A29, A29→A33 and A28→A33 each became about one step longer. These items describe one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, LAG005, LAG006, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011]

An important competing explanation remains: without trace-level linkage, a move of the block cannot be fully separated from the early occurrence being dropped while a similar late occurrence is added; the stable participation of all four activities (about 20–28 of 100 traces in both windows) argues against a plain removal, and the insertion point varies (between A28 and A29 in about half of the later traces, between A29 and A33 in about half). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, DIR008, DIR009, LAG008, LAG010]

Other differences: A04 is executed in far fewer cases (about 55 of 200 traces earlier vs 17 of 200 in the later window) with redistributed successors, and A08 is executed in more cases (8 vs 29 traces) with a different successor mix; both routing profiles rest on few observations. These participation and routing changes are not part of the relocation hypothesis above. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, LAG001]
