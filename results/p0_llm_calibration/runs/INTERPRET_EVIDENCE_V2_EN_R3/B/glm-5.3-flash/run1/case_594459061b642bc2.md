### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, A27, A36, A37, A42 and A43 each appeared in every trace, about once per trace; in the later window each appears in only about 50–52 in 100 traces, still about once when present. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- A25's routing changed almost completely: earlier about 96 in 100 of its outgoing transitions went to A36, A37, A42 or A43 (roughly a quarter each), while in the later window about 98 in 100 go directly to A28; the A25–A28 gap accordingly shrank from five steps in nearly all earlier observations to one step in about 98 in 100 later traces. [DIR001, LAG001]
- A26's routing split: earlier it led to A27 in about 99 in 100 transitions; in the later window it leads to A27 in about 48 in 100 and directly into A36, A37, A42 or A43 in about 49 in 100 combined. [DIR002]
- The order between A26 and those four activities reversed: earlier they ran before A26 in about 98–99 in 100 traces; in the later window A26 runs before them in about 49 in 100 traces, with the two co-occurring in about 51 in 100. [REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010]
- The four activities still run as a set when they run at all: earlier every pair of them co-occurred in all traces in mixed internal order; in the later window each pair co-occurs in about 50–51 in 100 traces and neither occurs in about 48–50 in 100, with both internal orders still present. [REL025, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030]
- The group now hands over to A31: earlier A36, A37, A42 and A43 led almost only to each other or to A28, while in the later window each sends roughly 22–26 in 100 of its outgoing transitions to A31; A27's own edge to A31 is essentially unchanged (about 97 in 100 in both windows). [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]
- A27 and each of the four became mutually exclusive: earlier each pair co-occurred in every trace (group before A27); in the later window co-occurrence is about 1–2 in 100 traces, each side occurring alone in about 49–50 in 100, and A31 occurs without A27 in about half the traces (earlier A27 preceded A31 in about 995 in 1000). [REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016]
- The A26→A31 span changed shape: earlier it covered two steps in about 97 in 100 traces (A26→A27→A31); in the later window it is two steps in about half the traces and five steps — through the four activities — in about 48 in 100. [LAG006]
- Event positions moved consistently with this: the four activities shifted from about 0.40 to about 0.58 of the trace length, while A28 moved earlier (mean 0.47 → 0.39) and A26 earlier (0.61 → 0.53). [POS002, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the four activities A36, A37, A42 and A43, which previously always executed together between A25 and A28, no longer run there in the later window — A25 proceeds directly to A28 — and instead execute in about half of the cases between A26 and A31, where they alternate with A27: a trace takes either the A27 path (A26→A27→A31) or the group path (A26→ the four activities →A31), roughly 50/50, and A31 is reached either way. [DIR001, DIR002, LAG001, LAG006, REL007, REL013, REL012, OCC001]

The hypothesis rests on direct routing and relational evidence rather than position shifts alone: A25's successor changed from the group to A28, the A25–A28 distance collapsed from five to one, the order between A26 and the group reversed, the group gained edges to A31, and A27 and the group became mutually exclusive while all five activities settled at about half participation. With 63 qualifying items against roughly 0.83 expected for an unchanged process, and every item pointing to the same restructuring, sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. [DIR001, LAG001, REL007, DIR004, REL013, OCC001, DIR002, LAG006]

Limitations: the evidence shows nothing about what connects A28 to A26 in the later window (no routing profile for A28), it cannot tell whether the removal from the A25–A28 segment and the insertion after A26 are one coordinated change or two separate ones, and it offers no explanation for the roughly even split between the two paths. An important competing reading that cannot be ruled out is that the early group was dropped and a newly composed optional segment of the same four activities was added after A26, rather than the same group being moved; the observed behaviour is the same under both readings. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR004]

Other differences: A25's own position shifted slightly later (mean 0.34 → 0.36) and A31's slightly earlier (0.67 → 0.64), both consistent with the main change; several distance items (A25 paired with a group activity, or a group activity paired with A28) rest on only 1–2 after-window observations and carry little weight individually, although they point the same way. [POS001, POS005, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG008, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012]
