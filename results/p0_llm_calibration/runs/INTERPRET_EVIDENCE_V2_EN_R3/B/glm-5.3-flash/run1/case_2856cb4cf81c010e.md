### Observed differences

- The largest shift is between two alternative groups of activities. In the earlier window, A21, A22, A23 and A24 each ran in about 83 of 100 traces, while A25, A26, A27 and A28 each ran in only about 18 of 100. In the later window, A21–A24 each run in about 28 of 100 traces, while A25–A28 each run in about 73 of 100. [OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, OCC010, OCC011]
- The two groups act as alternatives in both windows: cross-group pairs such as A21 with A25 share a trace in only about 1–2 of 100 cases in either window, and one group or the other is present in essentially every trace. [REL032-REL035, REL040, REL047, REL053]
- Each group also runs as a set. Earlier, A21–A24 co-occurred in about 83 of 100 traces; in the later window they co-occur in about 27 of 100, with none of them present in about 71 of 100. A25–A28 moved from rarely sharing a trace (about 18 of 100) to co-occurring in about 73 of 100. [REL029-REL031, REL038, REL039, REL046, REL059-REL061, REL064, REL065, REL068]
- Routing at A20 flipped directly: earlier, about 83 of 100 of A20's observed outgoing transitions went to A24 and about 17 to A28; in the later window, about 27 go to A24 and about 72 to A28, on roughly 200 outgoing transitions in each window. [DIR003]
- A19 and A20 appear in essentially every trace in both windows and precede whichever group runs: earlier they came before A21–A24 in about 81 of 100 traces; in the later window they come before A25–A28 in about 71–73 of 100. [REL013-REL016, REL021-REL024, REL017-REL020, REL025-REL028, DIR003]
- A separate change concerns A04. Earlier, A04 ran in about 55 of 100 traces, A31 led to A04 in about 52 of 100 of its outgoing transitions (and to A19 in about 45), and A04's own transitions went to A19 in about 87 of 100. In the later window, A04 runs in about 16 of 100 traces, A31 leads almost always directly to A19 (about 84 of 100 outgoing transitions; the distance-1 share rose from about 45 to about 84), and A04's remaining occurrences spread across many successors. [OCC002, DIR004, DIR001, LAG007, LAG008, REL001, REL011]
- Smaller shifts: participation in A02 rose from about 5 to about 14 of 100 traces and in A08 from about 3.5 to about 12, both on few observations (10→27 and 7→24 traces); A04's remaining occurrences also sit at more varied positions, based on 31 traces. [OCC001, OCC003, POS001, LAG001-LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the choice between two existing alternative paths flipped in how often each is taken: the branch running A21–A24 (always together, after the near-universal A19 and A20) and the branch running A25–A28. Earlier, roughly 83 of 100 traces took the A21–A24 branch and about 18 the A25–A28 branch; in the later window, about 28 take A21–A24 and about 73 take A25–A28. Supporting signs: both paths are observed substantially in both windows and their internal structure is unchanged (each group still runs as a set, and the groups remain mutually exclusive), so this is a shift in how often existing paths are taken rather than activities appearing or disappearing; the flip is directly visible in A20's outgoing transitions; and 99 qualifying items against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process, each with a difference larger than in about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, cannot be explained by chance sampling as a whole. Limitations: direct routing evidence covers A20 only — for A19 the flip is inferred from co-occurrence and ordering — and the evidence cannot distinguish a changed routing rule from a changed mix of cases entering the process, which would look identical here. [OCC004-OCC011, DIR003, REL029-REL031, REL059-REL061, REL032, REL017]

Other differences: A04 is now usually bypassed on the path into A19 — A31, which earlier led to A04 in about half of its outgoing transitions, now leads almost always directly to A19, and A04's participation fell from about 55 to about 16 of 100 traces; this is a predecessor reaching its successor directly, not a share shift within the branch choice. In addition, participation in A02 and A08 rose slightly, and A04's remaining occurrences show more varied successors and positions; these rest on small supports and are not part of the main hypothesis.
