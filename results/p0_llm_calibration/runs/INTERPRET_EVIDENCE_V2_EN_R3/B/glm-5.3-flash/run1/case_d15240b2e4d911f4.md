### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that two segments of the flow exchanged their order: the mandatory pair A16+A17, executed together in either order, ran before directly after A33 and before A18/A22, while the exclusive choice between A14 and A15 ran between A22 and A23; in the later window, the A14-or-A15 choice runs directly after A33 and before A18, and the A16/A17 pair runs after A22 and before A23, with A18 and A22 keeping their approximate positions and A33 and A23 remaining the surrounding anchors. [REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL001, REL005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG001, LAG002, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

The support is direct and consistent: the order reversal between {A16, A17} and {A18, A22} is observed relationally and completely (co-occurrence stays at 100% of traces while the order flips from 100%/0% to 0%/100%); the successor changes at A14, A15, A22, A33, A16 and A17 all match one rewiring; only the relocated activities show large position shifts; and the gap changes (A22→A23 from 2 to 3 events, A33→A18 from 3 to 2) are exactly what placing a two-activity block versus a single-choice activity between those anchors predicts. The 26 qualifying items are many times the fewer than one expected for an unchanged process, so the pattern as a whole is not a sampling artefact. Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish a single exchange of the two segments from two independent relocations that produce the same net order.
