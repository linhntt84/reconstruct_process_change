### Observed differences

- Two groups of four activities exchanged how often they run. In the earlier window, each of A16, A17, A18 and A19 appeared in about 83 in 100 traces; in the later window, each appears in only about 27 in 100. The mirror image holds for A20, A21, A22 and A23: about 18 in 100 traces earlier, about 73 in 100 in the later window. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]
- The routing out of A15 flipped. Earlier, about 83 in 100 of A15's observed outgoing transitions led to A19 and about 17 in 100 to A23; in the later window, about 27 in 100 lead to A19 and about 73 in 100 to A23. [DIR001]
- The two groups act as mutually exclusive alternatives in both windows: for example, A16 and A20 never occur in the same trace; earlier a trace typically contained A16 but not A20 (about 83 in 100), in the later window it typically contains A20 but not A16 (about 73 in 100), and the same flip holds for every cross-pair of the two groups. [REL012, REL019, REL025, REL030]
- Togetherness within the groups flipped accordingly: pairs among A16–A19 co-occurred in about 83 in 100 traces earlier versus about 27 in 100 in the later window (with both absent in about 73 in 100), while pairs among A20–A23 co-occurred in about 18 in 100 earlier versus about 73 in 100 in the later window. [REL009, REL017, REL024, REL035, REL039, REL042]
- A15 itself ran in essentially all traces in both windows, but its company changed: earlier it usually ran before the A16–A19 group (about 83 in 100 traces) and without the A20–A23 group (about 83 in 100); in the later window it usually runs without A16–A19 (about 73 in 100) and before A20–A23 (about 73 in 100). [REL001, REL004, REL005, REL008]
- A27 also ran throughout and stayed with whichever group was active: earlier it co-occurred with A16–A19 in about 83 in 100 traces and appeared without A20–A23 in about 83 in 100; in the later window it co-occurs with A20–A23 in about 73 in 100 and appears without A16–A19 in about 73 in 100. [REL016, REL034, REL038, REL044]
- Minor positional shifts appear inside the declining group: the A15-to-A17 gap is now most often 2 event positions (about 52 in 100, versus about 33 earlier) and the A19-to-A17 gap most often 1 position (about 52 in 100, versus about 33 earlier), with a small shift toward longer gaps for A17-to-A27. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the choice between two alternative paths after A15 shifted its frequencies: in the earlier window, traces usually continued from A15 into the path containing A16, A17, A18 and A19 (entered via A19), whereas in the later window they usually continue into the path containing A20, A21, A22 and A23 (entered via A23). A27 runs in essentially every trace in both windows and remains attached to whichever path is active. [DIR001, OCC001, OCC005, REL016, REL038]

The routing change is observed directly at the branching point, with A15's outgoing shares to A19 and A23 moving from roughly 83/17 to roughly 27/73 [DIR001]. Both paths were exercised in substantial shares in both windows (the rarer one in about 18 and about 27 in 100 traces respectively), the two groups never co-occur, and the order relations inside each group are preserved, so only the shares between existing alternatives changed [REL012, REL035, REL037, REL011]. In total 56 items qualify — where an unchanged process would typically produce fewer than one — and all of them describe this one coherent shift, so sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole [OCC001, OCC005, DIR001, REL001, REL012].

Limitations: the evidence shows that the path frequencies changed, not what governs the choice at A15, and it cannot rule out that the two windows sampled case mixes with different path preferences rather than a modified routing rule. [DIR001, REL012]

Other differences: the small positional shifts within the declining path (A15-to-A17, A19-to-A17 and A17-to-A27 gaps) are minor and consistent with the changed path mix; on their own they are not evidence that any activity was moved. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003]
