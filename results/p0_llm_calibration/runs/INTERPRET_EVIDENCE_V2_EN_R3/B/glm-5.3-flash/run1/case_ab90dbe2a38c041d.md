### Observed differences

- The execution order between two groups of activities reversed. Earlier, A26 and A28 ran before A32 and A33 in nearly every trace (A26 before A32 in about 99 in 100 traces; A28 before A33 in about 99–100 in 100). In the later window this flipped: A32 preceded A26 in about 97 in 100 traces and A33 preceded A28 in about 96–97 in 100. All four activities still occurred together in every trace in both windows, so their presence did not change — only their sequence. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]
- Direct successors changed accordingly. Earlier, A25 led almost always to A26 (about 97 in 100 of its outgoing transitions) and A28 led mainly to A32 and A33 (about 53 and 45 in 100); in the later window, A25 leads to A33 and A32 in nearly equal shares (about 48 and 47 in 100, with A26 down to about 1 in 100) and A28 leads almost always to A29 (about 94 in 100). A32 and A33, which earlier led mainly to each other and to A29, now lead mainly to A26 and to each other, with transitions to A29 nearly gone. Each profile rests on about 200 traces per window. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004]
- Relative positions moved in opposite directions: in the later window A26 and A28 sit later in the trace (mean relative position from about 0.43 to 0.51 and from about 0.49 to 0.57), while A32 and A33 sit earlier (from about 0.54 to 0.46 and from about 0.55 to 0.46). [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]
- Step distances moved consistently with a reordering: A25 to A26 widened from typically 1 step apart (97 in 100) to 3 steps apart (93 in 100); A25 to A28 from 3 to 5 steps (93 to 98 in 100); A28 to A29 narrowed from 3 steps to 1 (96 to 95 in 100); A26 to A29 from 5 steps to 3 (all traces to 90 in 100); A25 to A32 and A25 to A33 narrowed from 4–5 steps to 1–2; and A32/A33 to A29 widened from 1–2 steps to 4–5. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG007, LAG010, LAG011]
- The once-close forward pairs A26→A33, A28→A32 and A28→A33 (previously about 1–2 steps apart in essentially all 200 traces) were observed within five steps in only 3–4 of the later traces. [LAG006, LAG008, LAG009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the pair A32/A33 and the segment containing A26 and A28 exchanged their order along the flow, with A25 at the start and A29 as the downstream anchor. Earlier the dominant flow ran A25 → A26 → (about one step) → A28 → A32/A33 → A29; in the later window it runs A25 → A32/A33 → A26 → (about one step) → A28 → A29, so A29 now follows A28 directly instead of following A32 or A33. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG005, LAG007, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]

The order change is shown directly: for all four cross-pairs the dominant direction flipped from about 99 in 100 one way to about 96–98 in 100 the other way, while the activities still co-occurred in 100 in 100 of traces in both windows — a reordering of mandatory activities, not a change in participation. Successor, position and distance evidence all shift in the directions this exchange predicts, the internal spacing of each block was preserved, and the 21 qualifying items (against roughly 0.57 expected for an unchanged process, each difference larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits) cannot be explained by chance as a whole; no qualifying item describes behaviour outside this reordering. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011]

Limitations: the items describe one underlying difference from several angles and are not independent confirmations; the exact boundaries of the exchanged segments are not observed (for example whatever runs between A26 and A28, or behaviour beyond the tested pairs and the five-step horizon); and three lag items rest on only 3–4 later-window observations. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG006, LAG008, LAG009]

Competing explanation: a pre-existing rare ordering that simply became dominant cannot be fully excluded, although the later-dominant order (A25 directly followed by A32 or A33) was at most marginally present earlier, which argues against a mere shift in the mix of two established paths. [DIR001, LAG003, LAG004]
