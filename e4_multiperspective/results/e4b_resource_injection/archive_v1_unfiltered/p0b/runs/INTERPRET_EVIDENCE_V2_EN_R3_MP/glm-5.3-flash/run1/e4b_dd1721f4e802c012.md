### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis of a change in resource allocation: in the earlier window, A01, A03, A13, A14, A17 and A22 were each executed by a broad mix of resources dominated by one group of codes (including User_16), while in the later window a largely different group (including User_13) took over these activities, and A01 came to be executed exclusively by User_86.
