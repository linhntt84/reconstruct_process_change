### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

In the later window, A22 is executed in a larger share of cases and is more often placed between A17 and A03: A17 now continues to A22 in about half of its outgoing transitions (up from about a third), the direct step to A03 — previously A17's most frequent continuation at about 45 in 100 — is taken in only about 31 in 100, and A03 consequently follows A17 one position later in most cases rather than immediately.
