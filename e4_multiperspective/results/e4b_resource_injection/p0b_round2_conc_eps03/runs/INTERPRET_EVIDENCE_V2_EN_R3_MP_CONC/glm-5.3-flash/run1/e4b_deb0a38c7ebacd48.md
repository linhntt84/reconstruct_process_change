### Observed differences

- All nine flagged items come from the resource family; the control-flow and time families produced none (0 items against about 0.2 and 0.02 expected by chance in a stable process). [screening]
- For A01, A03 and A22, the resources carrying the activity changed markedly: in the earlier window User_18 held the largest share (about 10 in 100 assignments) alongside several other resources at about 5–9 in 100; in the later window User_18 and most of that group fell to zero or near zero, while a largely different group led by User_41 (about 9–11 in 100) took over the work. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports, as a possibility rather than a confirmed modification, a change in resource allocation in the later window affecting A01, A03, A13, A14, A17 and A22. For A01, A03 and A22, a group of resources that previously held the largest shares (including User_18) was replaced by a largely different group (including User_41); for A13, A14 and A17, allocation additionally concentrated on User_27, whose share rose from about 5 in 100 to about 18–19 in 100 assignments while the number of distinct resources involved shrank. [ASSIGN001, ASSIGN002, ASSIGN003, ASSIGN004, ASSIGN005, ASSIGN006, CONC001, CONC002, CONC003]

Supporting signs: nine resource items were flagged where about 0.01 would be expected without any change — a count chance cannot explain; all items describe redistribution in the same direction with overlapping gaining and losing resources across activities; the assignment-share and concentration indicators agree for A13/A14/A17; and no control-flow or time items appeared, so the difference is confined to how work was distributed over resources. [screening, ASSIGN001, CONC001]

Limitations: the resource codes are anonymous, so the evidence cannot distinguish a change in assignment behaviour from a change in which resources were available at all; and the identical A13/A14 profiles mean those items are not independent confirmations of the concentration shift. [ASSIGN003, ASSIGN004, CONC001, CONC002]
