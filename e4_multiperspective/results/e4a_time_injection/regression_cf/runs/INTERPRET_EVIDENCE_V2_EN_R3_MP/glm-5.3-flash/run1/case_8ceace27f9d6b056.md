### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the later window runs a structurally extended variant: an optional block in which one of the alternatives A19, A20, A25 or A26 executes between A42 and A27 — pairwise co-occurrence puts at least one of them in roughly 45–55 of 100 later traces — where earlier A42 led almost directly to A27; and A06 became a frequent, early step that A14 feeds and that continues mainly into A15, where earlier A14 led mostly directly to A15 and A06 appeared in only about 22 of 100 traces. The reading rests on direct relational evidence: the successor profiles of A14 and A42 each have about 200 outgoing transitions behind them in both windows, the orderings A42 before the new activities and the new activities before A27 are observed directly, A14→A06 occurs within one step in about 96 of 100 near-pairs, and the A42→A27 and A14→A15/A42 distances shifted by about one step. Overall, 33 evidence items qualified against roughly 0.75 expected by chance across 151 tested units, and every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits, so the pattern as a whole is not what an unchanged process typically produces. [DIR003, DIR007, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, LAG002, LAG007, LAG003, LAG004, REL020, REL021, REL024, REL025, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030, REL017, REL022, REL026, OCC001, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the A06 restructuring and the new block are one coordinated change or two independent ones, because A06 and each of the four new activities share a trace in only about 10–20 of 100 later cases; nor can it pin down A06's placement exactly, since about 26 of 100 traces show A06 and A15 interleaved rather than strictly ordered. Positions are relative event positions, so "earlier" refers to sequence order, not clock time. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL002, POS001]

Other differences: A08's participation fell from about 21 to 8 of 100 traces, marginalising an activity that previously ran in about 43 of 200 traces, though its later routing profile rests on too few traces (16) to describe its new behaviour; the routing at A45 and A47 was flagged but changed only negligibly (differences of about 0.003 and 0.002, still dominated by A46 and A48 respectively).
