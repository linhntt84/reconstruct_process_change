### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the sequence around A27 was reordered: the four activities A19, A20, A25 and A26, which previously ran together before A27 and each led into it, now run after A27 and A28 and lead into A29 and A33 instead; A27 and A28 sit slightly earlier in the trace, and A46, which previously fed into that group, now leads directly into A27. [REL001, REL003, REL005, REL007, POS001, POS003, DIR004, DIR006, DIR008, DIR010, LAG011]

Screening tested 133 units and would flag about 0.66 items by chance if nothing had changed, yet 31 items qualified — far beyond what an unchanged process typically produces. The order reversal is shown directly for all eight activity pairs with every trace classified in both windows, and the routing changes on both sides (away from A27, and into the group from A28/A29) plus the position shifts match it; the step-gap changes for A15, A16 and the A28→A29→A33 chain are consistent with the same reordering. These items describe overlapping aspects of the same traces, so they corroborate one underlying difference rather than independently confirming it. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010]

An important competing explanation remains: the evidence shows the relative order flipped but cannot establish which side moved — a later execution of the A19/A20/A25/A26 group and an earlier execution of A27/A28 fit the same flips, and positions are event positions rather than clock time. The group's exact new placement also varies, since it now interleaves with the loosened A28→A29→A33 chain and splits its exits about evenly between A29 and A33; the group members also co-occur with A27/A28 in only about a fifth to a quarter of traces.
