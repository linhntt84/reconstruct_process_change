### Observed differences

- The four-activity block A34, A35, A36, A37 ran in every trace in the earlier window (each in 200 of 200 traces, ordered as A34/A35 in either order followed by A36/A37 in either order, sitting between A30 and A31). In the later window each of the four runs in only about half of the traces (A34 and A35 in about 56 in 100, A36 and A37 in about 46–47 in 100), and the block now appears as exactly one of the two pairs: A34 with A35 in about 56 in 100 traces, A36 with A37 in about 46 in 100, both pairs in the same trace in only about 2 in 100, and at least one pair in essentially every trace. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, REL021, REL025, REL029, REL030, REL036, REL042]
- Routing around this segment changed accordingly: earlier, A30 led almost exclusively to A34 (about 54% of its outgoing transitions) and A35 (about 44%), and A34/A35 led on to A36/A37 (about 44% of A34's outgoing); later, A30 feeds both pairs roughly equally (A36 about 24%, A35 about 28%, A34 about 26%, A37 about 18%) and A34/A35 now lead mainly to A31 (about 45–47%). [DIR006, DIR007, DIR008]
- Distances inside the segment shrank: A30 to A31 went from 5 steps apart in virtually all observations to 3 steps apart in about 87 in 100; A30 to A36/A37 and A34/A35 to A31 went from typically 3–4 steps to 1–2 steps. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG009, LAG015]
- A10 lost its fixed slot between A43 and A44: earlier it ran in about 62 in 100 traces at a late, very consistent position (mean relative position about 0.87), A43 led to A10 in about 71% of A43's outgoing transitions, and A10 led to A44 in about 93% of A10's; later it runs in only about 15 in 100 traces at earlier, far more variable positions (mean about 0.57), A43 leads directly to A44 in about 84% of its outgoing transitions (up from about 29%), the direct one-step A43→A44 succession rises from about 6% to about 50% of observations, and A10's outgoing transitions are spread over many successors (none above about 19%). [OCC004, POS001, DIR003, DIR009, LAG022, REL010]
- Three rare activities became more common: A04 from about 5 to about 19 in 100 traces (9 to 37 traces), A06 from about 6 to about 15 (11 to 29), A08 from about 7 to about 21 (14 to 41). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003]
- Minor shifts: A30, A34 and A35 now sit slightly later within traces (mean relative position about 0.63 to 0.70, 0.68 to 0.74 and 0.67 to 0.75 respectively), while the dominant successions A14→A15 and A45→A46 persist in both windows (about 98% of outgoing transitions before, 88% and 87% after). [POS004, POS005, POS006, DIR004, DIR010]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A30 and A31 was restructured from a mandatory four-activity sequence into a choice between two two-activity pairs: earlier, every trace executed A34, A35, A36 and A37 (A34/A35 in either order, then A36/A37 in either order); in the later window, each trace executes exactly one pair — A34 with A35 in about 55 in 100 traces or A36 with A37 in about 45 in 100 — with the pairs mutually exclusive (both in about 2 in 100, neither in essentially none) and the surrounding order preserved (A30 before the pair; A31, A45 and A46 after it). Supporting signs: participation of all four activities fell from 100% to 46–56% while co-occurrence within each pair stayed near-complete; cross-pair co-occurrence collapsed from 100% to about 2%; A30 and the pair activities re-routed as described; and every gap inside the segment shrank consistently. With 101 qualifying items against roughly 1 expected in an unchanged process, the evidence as a whole cannot be a sampling artefact. [OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, REL029, REL030, REL036, REL042, REL021, REL025, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG002, LAG003, LAG009, LAG015]

Limitations: this is a structural replacement — part of the block became skippable and the two pairs became alternatives — not a re-weighting of paths that already existed in both windows; and the window-level aggregates cannot rule out that the two windows sample different mixtures of process variants (for example, a large subpopulation following a different variant in the later window) rather than one modified process. A few gap items rest on very few observations (for example A34→A45 within five steps: 1 observation before, 45 after) and were not relied on. [LAG012, LAG013, LAG014]

Other differences:

- A10 is typically skipped on the A43→A44 path in the later window: A43 now leads directly to A44 (about 84 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, up from about 29), the A43→A44 gap shrank from mostly 2 steps to 1 step, and A10's participation collapsed from about 62 to about 15 in 100 traces with earlier, much more variable positions; its roughly 30 remaining occurrences are too few to characterise what role it now plays. [OCC004, POS001, DIR003, DIR009, LAG022]
- A04, A06 and A08 now appear in roughly three to four times as many traces as before (about 5 to 19, 6 to 15 and 7 to 21 in 100); how they connect into the flow remains unclear because their routing profiles rest on few observations (A04: 7 outgoing-transition observations before versus 41 after). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, DIR002]
