### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A42/A14 and A27 was restructured by exchanging its activities: earlier, each case executed either A19 or A20 (about half of the cases each, essentially never both) in that position, while in the later window A19 and A20 are nearly never executed and almost every case instead executes both A25 and A26 there, in either order. The support is direct and consistent across indicator families — participation counts, A42's successor profile (about 200 outgoing transitions in each window, moving from A19/A20 to A25/A26), co-occurrence and order relations with A42, A14 and A27 (each backed by about 200 traces in both windows), and the A42-to-A27 distance stretching from about 2 to about 3 steps — and the volume of qualified evidence (43 items against roughly 0.65 expected without a change) far exceeds what random window splits produce. The evidence cannot rule out that A25/A26 are renamed versions of the same steps rather than genuinely new activities, nor determine whether the residual A19/A20 executions (1 and 4 traces) still form an intended rare path.
