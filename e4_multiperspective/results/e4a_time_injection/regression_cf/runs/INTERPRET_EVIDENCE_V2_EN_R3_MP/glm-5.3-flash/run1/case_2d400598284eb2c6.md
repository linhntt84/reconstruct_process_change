### Observed differences

- **The middle block no longer always runs through A27.** In the earlier window, A27 executed in every trace (200 of 200), always before A28; A26 led to A27 in about 97 in 100 of A26's outgoing transitions, and A27 co-occurred with A14, A15 and A26 in every trace. In the later window, A27 appears in only about 46 in 100 traces (92 of 200) — still preceding A28 when it occurs — while A28 now runs without any preceding A27 in about 54 in 100 traces. [OCC006, DIR004, REL010, REL013, REL016, REL019]

- **Two activities newly appear in the middle of the trace.** A32 was not observed at all before (0 of 200 traces) and A33 in about 1 in 200; in the later window each appears in about 55 in 100 traces (109 and 111 of 200), around the middle of the trace (mean relative position about 0.5). [OCC007, OCC008, POS002, POS003]

- **A32 and A33 sit between A26 and A28.** In the later window, A26 leads to A33 in about 33 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and to A32 in about 21 in 100, beside A27 (about 44 in 100); A32 leads mainly to A28 (about 60 in 100) or A33 (about 38 in 100), and A33 mainly to A32 (about 58 in 100) or A28 (about 36 in 100). A32 and A33 now co-occur in about 55 in 100 traces, in both orders (A32 first in about 21 in 100, A33 first in about 34 in 100), so they run together without a fixed order. The distance from A26 to A28 grew from usually 2 steps (about 97 in 100) to usually 3 steps (about 51 in 100), consistent with two activities inserted between them. [DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, REL026, LAG004]

- **A32/A33 act as an alternative to A27.** In the later window, A32 or A33 occur without A27 in about 54 in 100 traces, while they co-occur with A27 in at most about 1–2 in 100; A28 follows A32 in about 54 in 100 of their shared traces and follows A33 similarly (about 55 in 100). Before, A32 and A33 were essentially never seen. [REL020, REL021, REL024, REL025]

- **A02 became frequent and moved to the start of the flow.** Its participation rose from about 20 to about 56 in 100 traces, and its mean position moved from about 0.68 to about 0.19 of the trace. Its outgoing transitions changed from spread over many targets (A46 about 27 in 100, A44 about 17, A16 about 15) to A15 in about 95 in 100; A14 now leads to A02 in about 55 in 100 traces (about 1 in 100 before), typically immediately (lag 1 in about 99 in 100 of the observed pairs). [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, REL001, REL002]

- **Smaller participation shifts.** A04 fell from about 21 to 5 in 100 traces and A10 from about 17 to 5; A23 declined from about 59 to 41 while A24 rose from about 42 to 60. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

- **The late tail is stable.** A45 still occurs in every trace at about the same late position (mean about 0.95–0.96 in both windows) and still leads to A46 directly (lag 1 in about 98 in 100 after vs about 86 in 100 before). [POS004, LAG005, DIR009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that a two-activity block, A32 and A33, was inserted into the middle of the process between A26 and A28, and that A27 — previously executed in every trace and always followed by A28 — became optional. In the earlier window traces ran A26 → A27 → A28; in the later window about 46 in 100 traces still take that route, while in the rest A32 and A33 both execute between A26 and A28 in either order, with A28 following them, and A32/A33 almost never co-occur with A27. The change is shown directly: A32 and A33 appear from 0 and about 1 in 200 traces to about 55 in 100, new outgoing transitions link A26 to A32/A33 and A32/A33 to A28, and the A26→A28 step gap moves from 2 to 3. In total 33 items qualified against fewer than 1 expected by chance across 144 tested units, so chance sampling cannot explain the set as a whole; note, however, that many items describe the same traces, so they are one consistent picture rather than independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that this is not one inserted block but two separate adjustments — A27 made optional and A32/A33 added independently — since the data show both changes but not the mechanism linking them. [OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR004, DIR006, DIR007, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL024, REL025, REL026, LAG004, POS002, POS003]

Other differences: A02 became about three times more common and moved from a late, varied position to an early one, now sitting between A14 and A15 (A14 leads to A02 in about 55 in 100 traces; A02 leads to A15 in about 95 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, where it previously spread over many targets). A04 and A10 nearly disappeared, A23 declined and A24 grew. A08's outgoing mix also changed, but the later profile rests on only 13 traces, so this is weak evidence. The late A45 → A46 tail is essentially unchanged. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR003, LAG001, LAG002, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR002, POS004, LAG005]
