### Observed differences

- The dominant difference is the near-disappearance of two activities. A14 ran in about 91 of 200 traces before (roughly 46 in 100) and in none of the 200 later traces; A15 ran in about 109 of 200 traces before (roughly 55 in 100) and in only 1 later trace. Neither activity repeated within a trace in either window. [OCC001, OCC002]
- Before, A14 and A15 behaved as strict alternatives: they never occurred together, and every trace contained exactly one of them (A14 alone in about 46 in 100 traces, A15 alone in about 54 in 100). In the later window, both are absent in about 995 of 1000 traces. [REL001, OCC001, OCC002]
- This alternative pair sat between A21 and A22. Before, A21's observed outgoing transitions went to A22 in about 48 in 100 cases, to A14 in about 25 and to A15 in about 28; in the later window, A21 leads to A22 in every observed transition. A22's outgoing transitions previously went to A23 in about 52 in 100 cases, to A15 in 27 and to A14 in 21; in the later window, A22 leads to A23 in about 995 of 1000 transitions. [DIR003, DIR004]
- The remaining chain tightened accordingly: the A21→A22 distance changed from a mix of one and two steps (about 48/52) to always directly adjacent; A22→A23 changed from about 52/48 to almost always adjacent; and A21→A23 shortened from always three steps to always two — one fewer intervening activity. [LAG003, LAG005, LAG004]
- A23's relative position moved slightly earlier (mean position fraction about 0.57 to 0.55), consistent with one step having been removed upstream of it. [POS003]
- A few items describe A15's behaviour in the single later trace that still contains it (A15 leading only to A23, at lag 1); these rest on one observation and carry little weight. [DIR002, LAG001, LAG002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative block formed by A14 and A15, which sat between A21 and A22, is no longer executed in the later window: traces that previously ran A21 → A14 or A15 → A22 → A23 now run A21 → A22 → A23 directly. Before, roughly 45 in 100 traces took the A14 branch and 55 in 100 the A15 branch; in the later window A14 is unobserved across 200 traces, A15 appears once, A21 always flows to A22, A22 almost always to A23, and the A21→A23 distance shrinks from three to two steps. Because A14 and A15 are (nearly) absent in the later window, this reads as a block that was removed or is no longer taken, not as a re-weighting of still-existing alternatives. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, DIR003, DIR004, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005]

The pattern is direct and consistent: the disappearance is measured on 91 and 109 before-traces against 0 and 1 after-traces, the routing and lag changes have full support in both windows and all point the same way, and 19 qualifying items against roughly 0.37 expected by chance among 75 tested units makes a chance origin of the set as a whole implausible. The evidence cannot distinguish whether A14 and A15 were removed from the process altogether or remain defined but are now never selected — the single residual A15 trace shows the block can still occur — and the items describing that trace's behaviour rest on one observation.
