### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, two groups of activities exchanged their places in the sequence: the block A20–A21–A23–A24, which previously ran between A42 and A27, now runs between A31 and A32, while the single choice among A19, A22, A25 and A26, which previously ran between A31 and A32, now runs between A42 and A27, with A27 and A31 keeping their relative order. This rests on direct relational evidence — every cross-group pair reverses its within-trace order while co-occurrence for the A27/A31 pairs stays at 100 in 100 — and is corroborated by the opposite position shifts, the successor changes at A42, A31 and in both groups, and the step-distance changes. With about 120 qualifying items against roughly 1.3 expected for an unchanged process, chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole. What the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows captured a switch between two pre-existing ordering variants (a case-mix change) rather than a modified sequencing rule, and it offers no view on why the behaviour differs. [REL029, REL030, REL034, POS003, POS002, DIR017, DIR016, LAG033, LAG027]

Other differences: A08 became rare and is now bypassed, with A47 reaching A48 directly instead of via A08; A02, A06 and A10 participate in about three times as many traces; and routing after A01 shifted from A07 towards A09 and A05. These are separate from the group exchange above and are not part of the main hypothesis. [OCC003, DIR019, LAG034, OCC001, OCC002, OCC004, DIR001, DIR004]
