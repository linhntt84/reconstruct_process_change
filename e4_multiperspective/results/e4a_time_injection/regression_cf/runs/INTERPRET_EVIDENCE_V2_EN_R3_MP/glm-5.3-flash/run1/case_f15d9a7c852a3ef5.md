### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, a step that runs as either A19 or A20 — never both — is executed in almost every case and is placed inside the former A26→A27→A28 stretch: in roughly half of the traces directly after A26 (before A27) and in roughly the other half directly after A27 (before A28), around the middle of the trace. The appearance rates (0 and 1 of 200 traces before versus 96 and 103 of 200 later, with strict mutual exclusivity), the successor changes at A26 and A27, the successor profiles of A19 and A20, the one-step distance shifts on A26→A27, A27→A28 and A26→A28, and the co-occurrence and ordering relations all point to the same placement, and the number of qualifying items (78 against roughly 1 expected for an unchanged process) far exceeds what a stable process produces. Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A19 and A20 were not observed then, so the evidence cannot distinguish a genuinely added step from one that existed but was almost never executed; and nothing in the data shows what selects between A19 and A20, or between the two placements. [OCC006, OCC007, POS002, REL018, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, LAG011, LAG012, LAG013, REL022, REL031]

Other differences: A10 is executed far less often and the A15→A10 branch has largely given way to a direct A15→A16 transition; this looks like a separate change, since A10 still runs in about 24 in 100 traces and co-occurs with A19/A20 in only about 12 in 100 — about the rate expected if the two were independent. The participation increases of A02, A04, A06 and A08 are also separate; for these rare activities, sampling variation in a finite window cannot be ruled out as part of the explanation.
