### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the one-per-case alternative block A14/A15/A20/A21 was relocated from the early stage between A41 and A22 to the late stage between A23/A24 and A28: earlier, each case ran A41 → one alternative → A22; later, A41 runs straight to A22 and the chosen alternative is inserted into the A23–A24–A28 sequence, before A24 or between A24 and A28. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, REL007, REL008]

The change is shown directly, not inferred from frequency shifts alone: the order between each alternative and A22/A23 flips completely while co-occurrence is unchanged, the successor profiles of A41, A23, A24 and of the four alternatives change accordingly, the alternatives' positions move from about a third to about three fifths of the trace, and the step distances match removal and re-insertion (A41→A22 from two steps to one; A23→A28 from two to three in every case). The 24 items far exceed the roughly 0.4 items expected by chance across the 74 tested units, every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits, and the block still runs once per case (its trace shares sum to about 100 of 100 in both windows), which fits a moved block rather than an added, removed or duplicated one.
