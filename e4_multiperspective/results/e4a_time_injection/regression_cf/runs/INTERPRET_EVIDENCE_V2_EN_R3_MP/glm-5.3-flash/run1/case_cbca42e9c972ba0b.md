### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the segment A30, then A31 and A36 in either order, then A37 — previously executed exactly once per case — runs about three times per case in roughly 69 in 100 cases, with A37 leading back to A30 in about two thirds of its executions instead of proceeding to A25; the repeated passes interleave with one another, and the earlier-running A15 and A16 now interleave with them rather than running before them as a single fixed pass. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR004, REL009, REL014, REL001, REL008]

The support is direct and consistent: the repetition itself is measured (occurrences up from about 1.0 to about 3.1 per trace, recurrence from near zero to about 69 in 100), the return edge is observed outright (A37→A30 rising from 0 of 200 outgoing transitions to about 67 in 100), and the ordering, position-spread and succession items all shift the way this repetition predicts. In total 39 items qualified against fewer than 1 expected by chance in an unchanged process, so chance sampling cannot account for the evidence as a whole; however, many items describe the same underlying repetition and are not independent confirmations. [OCC001, DIR004, POS003, LAG006]

Limitations: the evidence shows the choice at A37 changed but not what governs leaving versus repeating the segment, and it cannot rule out that the A37→A30 return existed as a very rare path before (none of 200 pre-change outgoing transitions went to A30, so only a return rarer than about 1 in 200 could have been missed); what is established is that this behaviour became dominant, not that it is newly possible. The routing change at A10 is not explained by this hypothesis. [DIR004, DIR002]

Other differences: A10 now spreads its outgoing transitions over many activities (to A16 down from about 84 to about 32 in 100, with the immediate succession down to about 40 in 100) [DIR002, LAG001]; minor successor-share shifts at A15 (to A16 up from about 85 to 92 in 100), A43 (to A44 down from about 98 to 91) and A45 (to A46 down from about 99.5 to 93) [DIR003, DIR005, DIR006]; and A08's successor profile changed completely, but with only 7 outgoing transitions before and 21 after, this is weakly grounded [DIR001].
