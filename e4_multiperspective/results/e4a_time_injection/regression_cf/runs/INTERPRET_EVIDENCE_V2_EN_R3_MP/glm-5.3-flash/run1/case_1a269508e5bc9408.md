### Observed differences

- Activity A04 moved from the margins to the mainstream: in the earlier window it ran in about 15 in 100 traces (about 0.16 occurrences per trace) and sat in the later half of traces (median event position about 0.37, upper quartile about 0.91); in the later window it runs in about 72 in 100 traces (about 0.79 occurrences per trace) and clusters near the front (median about 0.15, upper quartile about 0.16). [OCC001, POS001]

- The routing around A14 and A15 changed: in the earlier window about 83 in 100 of A14's observed outgoing transitions went to A15 and fewer than 2 in 100 to A04; in the later window about 70 in 100 go to A04 (A15 down to about 29 in 100), and about 89 in 100 of A04's outgoing transitions go to A15, where A04's transitions were previously scattered (largest single share about 17 in 100). [DIR005, DIR002]

- The new successions are tight and ordered: in the later window A14 is immediately followed by A04 in about 98 in 100 of the roughly 142 traces containing the pair (only about 5 such traces existed before), A04 is followed by A15 within one step in about half of the roughly 143 relevant traces (about 7 before), and the A14→A15 gap shifted from mostly one step (about 45 in 100) to mostly two steps (about 40 in 100); overall, A14 precedes A04 in about 65 in 100 traces (12 in 100 before) and A04 precedes A15 in about 40 in 100 traces, with about 31 in 100 interleaved (both near zero before). [LAG005, LAG001, LAG006, REL001, REL002]

- Three activities receded: A06, A08 and A10 each ran in about 18–22 in 100 traces in the earlier window but in only about 6–7 in 100 in the later window. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]

- The mid-process block A19, A20, A25 and A26 was rerouted and shifted earlier: in the earlier window each of them led mainly to A29 or A33 (about 39–55 in 100 of their outgoing transitions each); in the later window each leads to A27 in about 92–97 in 100, and their mean event positions moved from about 0.59–0.61 to about 0.34–0.36. Their order versus A27 and A28 flipped: A27/A28 used to come first in nearly every trace containing both (about 22–28 in 100 traces overall), now A19/A20/A25/A26 come first (about 22–30 in 100 traces overall). [DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, DIR010, POS003, POS004, POS005, POS006, REL012, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL018, REL019]

- Further downstream, A28 and A29 became near-deterministic: A28's transitions go to A29 in about 97–98 in 100 (about 49 in 100 before, otherwise spread over A26, A25, A19 and A20) and A29's go to A33 in about 99 in 100 (about 47 in 100 before); both links are now immediate in about 98–99 in 100 of observed cases, where gaps of one and two steps were previously mixed, and the A28→A33 gap shortened from about three to two steps. A46, which sent about 83 in 100 of its transitions to A27, now spreads them over A25 (about 29 in 100) and A26, A19, A20 (about 21 in 100 each), typically immediately before A26. [DIR011, DIR012, LAG014, LAG018, LAG015, DIR013, LAG019]

- The new A04 route often bypasses the mid-block: in about 49–56 in 100 traces A04 is present while A19/A20/A25/A26 is absent, versus about 10–13 in 100 before, when the two typically did not both occur. [REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]

- Smaller changes: A15, A27, A28, A29 and A46 each occur slightly later (mean event position up about 0.02–0.05), while some routing stayed essentially stable — A15 still leads to A16 in about 91 in 100 before and 97 in 100 after. [POS002, POS007, POS008, POS009, POS010, DIR006]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, the main flow from A14 passes through A04 before reaching A15 (A14 → A04 → A15) instead of going directly from A14 to A15; A04 itself changed from a rare, late activity (about 15 in 100 traces, second half of the trace) to a frequent, early one (about 72 in 100 traces, near the front). This is shown directly at the routing level — A14's dominant successor switched from A15 (about 83 in 100 of roughly 200 observed transitions) to A04 (about 70 in 100), and A04's transitions now concentrate on A15 (about 89 in 100) — and consistently across relation and gap evidence: A14 immediately precedes A04 in about 98 in 100 of observed pairs, A04 precedes A15 within one step in about half of them, the A14→A15 gap moved from mostly one to mostly two steps, and A04 co-occurs with A14 and with A15 in about 72 in 100 traces each (15 in 100 before). [DIR005, DIR002, OCC001, POS001, LAG005, LAG001, LAG006, REL001, REL002]

The evidence cannot distinguish a re-routing of a single main path from a shift in the variant mix — a new variant containing A14 → A04 → A15 replacing older variants would look the same at this level of aggregation — and the before-side of the gap comparisons rests on very few observations (about 5–7 traces); the evidence also says nothing about what produced the change. [LAG001, LAG005, DIR005]
