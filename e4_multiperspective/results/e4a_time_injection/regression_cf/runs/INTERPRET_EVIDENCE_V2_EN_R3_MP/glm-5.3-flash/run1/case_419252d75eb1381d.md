### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment between A41 and A26 was restructured from a one-of-four choice into a mandatory four-activity block: earlier, each case ran exactly one of A14, A16, A17 or A18 directly before A26; in the later window those four no longer appear, and A15, A23, A24 and A25 each run exactly once per case, in an unfixed order, between A41 and A26. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR009, LAG001]

The signs are direct and consistent: participation moved from 41–58 of 200 cases to 0 of 200 for the old four and from 0 to 200 of 200 for the new four; A41's successors switched from the old four to the new four, the old four's only outgoing edge (to A26) disappeared, and the new four link to one another and to A26; the new four went from never co-occurring to co-occurring in every case with mixed order, always after A41 and always before A26; and the A41-to-A26 distance grew from 2 to 5 steps in every case. In total 72 items qualified against roughly 0.62 expected if nothing had changed, and each item's difference exceeds that seen in about 199 of 200 random re-splits, so chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole; note, however, that the many items largely describe one underlying block change rather than independent confirmations. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008, DIR009, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL036, REL037, REL038, REL039, REL040, REL041, REL042, REL043, REL044, LAG001]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out: zero participation only means an activity was not observed in that window, so "activities added and removed" cannot be distinguished from existing activities whose execution probability moved between partial and zero or one. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008]

Other differences: none — the shifts in A26's and A41's positions and the longer A41-to-A26 distance follow from the same block change. [POS009, POS010, LAG001]
