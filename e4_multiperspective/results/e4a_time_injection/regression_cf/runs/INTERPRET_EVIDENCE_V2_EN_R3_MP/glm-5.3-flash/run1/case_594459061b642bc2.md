### Observed differences

- Participation dropped for five activities: before, A27 and each of A36, A37, A42 and A43 ran in every case (100 in 100); in the later window each appears in only about 50 in 100 cases, while A25, A26, A28 and A31 still appear in every case. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, POS001, POS002, POS004, POS005]
- In the later window, A27 and the group A36/A37/A42/A43 have become alternatives: before, A27 co-occurred with each group member in every case; after, they co-occur in only about 1–2 in 100 cases, with roughly half of the cases containing A27 but no group member and the other half the group but no A27. The four group members still run together (co-occurrence about 51 in 100; neither present in about 49 in 100). [REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL025, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030]
- The group also changed place. Before, it ran between A25 and A28: A25 led to a group member in about 96 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, each member preceded A28 in about 97–99 in 100 cases, and A28 followed A25 five steps later in essentially all cases. In the later window, A25 leads directly to A28 (about 98 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, one-step gap in about 98 in 100), the group runs after A26 (A26 precedes each member in about 49 in 100 cases, whereas before the member preceded A26 in about 98 in 100) and before A31 (each member precedes A31 in about 49–51 in 100 cases; the members' outgoing transitions now go to A31 in about 18–26 in 100, where they previously went to A28). [DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG001, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024]
- A26's routing split accordingly: before it led to A27 in 99 in 100 of its outgoing transitions; in the later window it leads to A27 in about 48 in 100 and to one of A36/A37/A42/A43 in about 49 in 100. [DIR002]
- Positions and distances moved consistently: A28 now sits earlier (median position 0.47 → 0.38) and A26 earlier (0.62 → 0.54), while the group members, when present, sit later (median about 0.40 → 0.58–0.59). The distance from A26 to A31, previously two steps in about 97 in 100 cases, is now two steps in about 50 in 100 and five steps in about 48 in 100 — matching A26→A27→A31 versus A26→group of four→A31. [POS002, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS009, LAG006]
- Some lag items rest on only 1–2 observed pairs in one window and are weak on their own; the picture above does not depend on them. [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that in the later window each case executes either A27 or the four-activity group A36/A37/A42/A43 — roughly a 50/50 split — whereas previously every case executed both. The group now executes in the segment between A26 and A31, where it alternates with A27, instead of between A25 and A28, and A28 now directly follows A25; put differently, about half of the later cases skip the group A36/A37/A42/A43, the other half skip A27. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL017, REL021, DIR001, DIR002, DIR004, LAG001, LAG006]

The supporting signs are direct and consistent: the participation drop from 100 to about 50 in 100 is matched by relational evidence of mutual exclusivity (co-occurrence falling to about 1–2 in 100), by successor changes (A25→A28 at about 98 in 100; group members now leading to A31 rather than A28) and by the A26→A31 distance splitting into two and five steps. Occurrence, direction, position, relation and lag indicators all show the same picture, each resting on roughly 100–200 observations per window (about 100 traces per variant after the change), and the 63 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 1 expected by chance (0.83). [REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, DIR001, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007, LAG001, LAG006, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

Limitations: the evidence cannot show whether the 50/50 split is one new routing rule or a mixture of two case populations within the later window; the segment between A28 and A26 is not covered by any item, so the path there is unobserved; and many items describe the same underlying trace behaviour, so they are not independent confirmations. [REL013, REL025, LAG006]

Other differences: A25 sits slightly later and A31 slightly earlier in the later window (median 0.33 → 0.35 and 0.67 → 0.65), a few rare transitions (about 1 in 100 of a source's outgoing transitions) appeared or disappeared at A25, A26 and A27, and A27's main outgoing behaviour is essentially unchanged, still leading to A31 in about 97–98 in 100 of its transitions in both windows. [POS001, POS005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
