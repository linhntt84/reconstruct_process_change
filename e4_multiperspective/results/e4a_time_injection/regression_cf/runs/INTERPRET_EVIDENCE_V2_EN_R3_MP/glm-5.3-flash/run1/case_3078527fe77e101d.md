### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

With 96 qualifying items against roughly one expected for an unchanged process, chance cannot explain the evidence as a whole, and the rerouting is shown directly by successor, order and gap evidence rather than by participation shares alone. The evidence supports the hypothesis of a structural rerouting around the group A36, A37, A42, A43: in the earlier window this group ran as a mandatory block between A25 and A28, together with A27, in every trace; in the later window A25 leads directly to A28, and the group executes in only about half of the traces, after A26, as an alternative to A27, with the group and A27 almost never appearing in the same trace. [DIR003, REL024, LAG002]

The change is visible exactly at the junctions: A25's successors switch from the group (about 98%) to A28 (about 97%), and A26's successors switch from A27 (about 98%) to an even split between A27 and the group; order relations reverse only for pairs spanning the group's old and new neighbourhood, while pairs such as A25→A36, A27→A31 and A36→A31 keep their direction. [DIR003, DIR004, REL030, REL018, REL012, REL023, REL034]

The gap profiles mirror the two branches — A25→A28 tightening from five steps to one, and A26→A31 becoming two steps (A27 branch) or five steps (group branch) in roughly equal halves — and the group keeps its all-or-nothing co-occurrence and mixed internal order, so the evidence reads as one block becoming optional and relocated rather than four independent participation drops. [LAG002, LAG007, REL038, REL047]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the later window mixes case groups whose choice between A27 and the group follows some unobserved case attribute; the data show only that the two branches are mutually exclusive and balanced about 50/50. The decline of A06 is not covered by this hypothesis, and positions are event-order based, so no clock-time claim is made. [REL024, OCC001]

Other differences: A06's participation fell from about 32 in 100 earlier traces to about 10 in 100 later traces, its mean relative position moved earlier with much more spread (0.91 to 0.59), and its dominant edge to A52 weakened from about 88% to about 29% of its outgoing transitions, with the near-adjacent A06→A52 pair observed in only 7 later traces versus 57 before. [OCC001, POS001, DIR002, LAG001] A02 shows a different outgoing-transition profile in the later window, but with only 5 versus 23 observed transitions this is weak evidence. [DIR001] The A51→A52 gap is one step in about 90% of later traces versus about 72% earlier. [LAG018]
