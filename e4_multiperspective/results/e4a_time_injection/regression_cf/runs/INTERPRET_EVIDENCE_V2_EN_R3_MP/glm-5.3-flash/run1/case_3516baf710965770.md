### Observed differences

- Before, A19 and A20 each ran in all 200 traces, always together and in the order A19 then A20 (about 99 in 100 traces). In the later window they run in about 59 in 100 traces (117 of 200), still only together, and in about 42 in 100 traces neither activity occurs at all. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001]
- A42 and A27 still run in every trace in both windows. Before they always co-occurred with A19 and A20; in the later window they appear without them in about 42 in 100 traces. [REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009]
- A42's routing changed: before, about 93% of its observed outgoing transitions led to A19; in the later window only about 55% do, while about 40% lead directly to A27, a successor not observed for A42 in the earlier window. [DIR002]
- The gap between A42 and A27 shrank accordingly: before, A27 followed A42 about 4 steps later in about 89% of traces and never immediately; in the later window A27 immediately follows A42 in about 40% of traces, while the 4-step gap covers about 53%. [LAG001]
- A25 and A26, previously alternatives that each appeared in roughly half the traces (about 48 and 53 in 100) and almost never together, now each appear in about 30 in 100 traces, with neither present in about 41 in 100; they still occur only alongside A19 and A20. [OCC003, OCC004, REL010, REL002, REL003, REL006, REL007, REL011, REL012]
- Smaller shifts: A27's mean relative position moved slightly earlier (about 0.44 to 0.41 of the trace, in event positions, not clock time), and A25's outgoing transitions remain almost exclusively towards A20 (about 99% before, 95% after) with small new shares of about 2% each to A30, A13 and A48. [POS001, DIR001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

Main hypothesis: in the later window, the segment formed by A19 and A20 — which also contains the A25/A26 alternative pair, since these occur only when A19/A20 do — is skipped in about 42 in 100 traces, and in those traces A42 leads directly to A27 instead of the flow passing through A19 and A20. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, DIR002, LAG001]

Supporting signs: A19 and A20 fell from full participation to about 59 in 100 traces while A42 and A27 stayed universal, so the missing occurrences are genuine omissions rather than relocations; the new direct A42→A27 succession (about 40% of A42's outgoing transitions) and the immediate A42→A27 distance (about 40% of traces) both match the share of traces without A19/A20 and were not observed before; the internal order (A42 before A19, A19 before A20, both before A27) is unchanged whenever the activities co-occur. The 18 evidence items far exceed the roughly 0.61 expected by chance for 122 tested units, and although many relation items mirror the same skip, the pattern is consistent in size (about 41–42 in 100 traces) across occurrence, routing, distance and relation indicators. [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, REL004, REL005, REL008, REL009, DIR002, LAG001]

Limitations: the evidence shows which activities are skipped and what replaces their position, but not the internal structure of the skipped region — in particular whether A25 and A26 are skipped by the same choice as A19/A20 or by a separate one; it only shows they never appear without A19/A20. [REL002, REL003, REL006, REL007, REL010]

Other differences: the slight forward shift of A27's position and the minor redistribution of A25's outgoing transitions are small and may be side effects of the skip rather than separate changes. [POS001, DIR001]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out: the direct A42→A27 path was not observed in the earlier window, so a newly introduced skip cannot be distinguished from a rare pre-existing variant that 200 earlier traces did not sample — though either way its later share of about 40 in 100 traces is far above what the earlier window shows. [DIR002, LAG001]
