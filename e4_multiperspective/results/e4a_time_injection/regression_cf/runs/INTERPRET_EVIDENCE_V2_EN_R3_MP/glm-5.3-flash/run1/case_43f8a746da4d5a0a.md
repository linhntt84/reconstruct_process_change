### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block formed by A32 and A33 was relocated within the sequence: before, it ran after A28 and before A29 (roughly A25 → A26 → … → A28 → A32/A33 → A29); in the later window it runs directly after A25 and before A26 (roughly A25 → A32/A33 → A26 → … → A28 → A29). As part of the same reordering, A26, previously the direct successor of A25, now follows A32/A33, and A29, previously following A32/A33, now directly follows A28; A32 and A33 remain an unordered pair in both windows. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009]

The reordering is shown directly by the near-complete order reversals and the successor changes at A25, A28, A32 and A33, with the distance and position shifts consistent with one relocation rather than unrelated moves. The 19 qualifying items are roughly 30 times the about 0.58 expected by chance in an unchanged process, every item's difference exceeds about 199 of 200 random re-splits, and nearly all profiles rest on about 200 traces per window, so sampling variation or a one-window rare variant cannot plausibly explain flips of this size. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, LAG001, LAG003, POS001, POS003]

A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a shift in the mix of two pre-existing variants, one with each ordering, rather than a modification of a single dominant variant, since only the two aggregate windows are available. [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004]

Other differences: a few outgoing transitions from A25 (to A08, A13, A22, A31) and from A28 (to A05, A30, A37) appear only in the later window, each at about 1 trace in 200; at this size they are consistent with sampling noise. [DIR001, DIR002]
