### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A30 and A31 — the A34/A35 pair followed by the A36/A37 pair — changed from a mandatory four-activity block into an optional either/or choice: before, all four activities ran in every case as one block (each pair in either internal order); in the later window each case runs only one of the two pairs (A34+A35 in about 55 in 100 cases, A36+A37 in about 47 in 100), A30 still precedes and A31 still follows the chosen pair, and the A30→A31 distance shrinks from 5 to 3 steps. This rests on direct participation, co-occurrence, successor and gap evidence that all show the same restructure, with 33 qualifying items against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed and every item exceeding about 995 of 1,000 random re-splits; the main limitations are that the evidence cannot show what determines which pair is chosen, and it cannot rule out that the later window simply mixes two case populations (a full and a shortened variant) rather than a within-case routing change.
