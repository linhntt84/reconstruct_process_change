### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the mutually exclusive step "A19 or A20", placed between A40 and A25, became mandatory in the later window: earlier it ran in about 45 in 100 traces and was skipped in about 55 in 100, while later exactly one of A19 or A20 ran in about 99 in 100 traces, essentially never both, and the direct A40→A25 route was no longer observed — A40's transitions now go to A20 (about 47 in 100) or A19 (about 41 in 100), and the A40-to-A25 distance moved from mostly 1–2 steps to 2–3 steps [OCC001, OCC002, REL001, DIR001, LAG001]. A40 and A25 themselves occurred in every trace in both windows, so the change is confined to this block [REL002, REL003].

Roughly 0.5 qualifying items would be expected by chance at these thresholds, against the nine observed, so chance alone cannot explain the set; the eight items describing this block agree in direction, and because they partly describe the same traces they corroborate one another rather than independently confirming the change [OCC001, OCC002, DIR001, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, LAG001]. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a changed mix of case types in the later window — for example a variant that always includes the A19/A20 step — rather than a modified routing rule; the A40→A25 edge is also unobserved in the later window, not proven removed [DIR001, LAG001].

Other differences: A23 was executed in fewer traces in the later window (about 42 in 100 versus about 56 in 100); no relational evidence ties it to the A19/A20 change [OCC003].
