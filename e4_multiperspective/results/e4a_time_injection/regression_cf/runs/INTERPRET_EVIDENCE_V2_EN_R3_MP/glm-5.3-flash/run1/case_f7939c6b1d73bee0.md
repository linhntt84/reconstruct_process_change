### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that A02 became a largely optional activity and was disconnected from the process ending. In the earlier window the standard ending ran A45 → A02 → A46: A45 handed over to A02 in about 63 in 100 of its outgoing transitions and A02 went straight on to A46 in about 93 in 100. In the later window A02 runs in only about 20 in 100 traces, A45 proceeds directly to A46 in about 84 in 100 (usually immediately, where the pair was previously two steps apart), and the immediate A02→A46 step survives in only 16 of 200 traces; the residual A02 executions sit earlier in the trace (median position about 0.55 versus 0.97) and branch to varied successors such as A44, A15 and A46, with thin new incoming edges from A43 and A14 suggesting where they are now triggered. This is shown directly and consistently across occurrence, routing, distance and relation indicators with substantial backing (130 versus 39 traces for A02's participation; 201/204 and 200/200 for A45's rerouting), and the 45 qualifying items far exceed the roughly 0.73 expected by chance in an unchanged process, so sampling noise cannot account for the ensemble. [OCC001, DIR001, DIR011, LAG003, LAG013, POS001, DIR010, DIR004]

Limitations: the many relation items about A02 largely restate the same participation drop and are not independent confirmations; the new incoming edges to A02 rest on small shares, so how its residual executions are triggered remains uncertain; and the evidence cannot rule out that the rerouting in the A21–A24/A29/A30 block is a separate change rather than part of one restructuring, nor fully exclude sampling differences between the two finite windows of 200 traces.
