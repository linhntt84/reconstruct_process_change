### Observed differences

- Earlier, A29, A30, A31 and A32 each ran exactly once in every case (participation 100% of 200 traces); in the later window each runs at most once and only in about half of the cases — A29 and A30 in about 56 in 100 cases (111 of 200), A31 and A32 in about 46 in 100 (92 of 200). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- The two pairs became mutually exclusive: A29 and A30 still always occur together when they occur at all (co-occurrence 100% earlier, 55.5% later, neither present in 44.5% of later cases), and likewise A31 with A32 (co-occurrence 46%, neither 54%). Earlier the two pairs always shared a case (A29/A30 first, then A31/A32); later they share one in only about 2 in 100 cases (3 of 200) — A29 without A31 in 54% of cases, A31 without A29 in 44.5%. [REL009, REL010, REL011, REL012, REL013, REL014]
- A25 and A26 still occur in every case in both windows, and their relative order is unchanged: where they co-occur with the pairs, A25 still comes first and A29/A30 still precede A26. [POS001, REL001, REL005]
- Routing changed at the edges of the block: earlier A25 led only to A29 (about 56% of its outgoing transitions) or A30 (44%); later, about 45 in 100 of A25's outgoing transitions go directly to A31 (25.5%) or A32 (19%). A29 and A30, which earlier never led to A26, now lead directly to A26 in about half of their outgoing transitions (A29 about 50%, A30 about 48%), while their transitions to A31/A32 nearly disappear. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003]
- Step distances shrank accordingly: A25 reached A26 five steps later in every earlier case but three steps later in about 99 in 100 later cases; A25→A31/A32 and A29/A30→A26 shortened from 3–4 steps to 1–2 steps. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG006]
- Relative positions moved consistently with shorter cases: A25's mean position rose from about 0.64 to 0.70 of the case length, and A29/A30 (within the cases where they run) from about 0.69 to 0.75. [POS001, POS002, POS003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the block between A25 and A26 — earlier always executed in full as A29 and A30 followed by A31 and A32 — now typically runs as mutually exclusive alternatives: in the later window a case executes either the pair A29–A30 (about 55 in 100 cases) or the pair A31–A32 (about 46 in 100), with A25 entering the chosen pair directly and A26 directly following it, so a case runs two of the four activities instead of all four. This is shown directly by the collapse of co-occurrence between the pairs, the new direct transitions A25→A31/A32 and A29/A30→A26, and the shortened step distances, with the participation drops and position shifts in agreement; 31 qualifying items against roughly 0.44 expected by chance, each difference larger than in about 199 of 200 random re-splits, rules out sampling variation as an explanation of the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC003, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, REL009, REL010, REL014, LAG001, LAG002, LAG004]

Limitations: the many items largely mirror one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations; the evidence cannot show what determines which pair runs, nor distinguish a newly introduced exclusive choice from a rare pre-existing variant that became the majority behaviour. About 2 in 100 later cases still execute both pairs, and two lag items (A29→A31, A30→A32) rest on only 3 later observations and were not relied on. [REL010, LAG005, LAG007]

Other differences: none beyond the above; the later relative positions of A25, A29 and A30 are consistent with the same cases becoming about two activities shorter, not with an independent relocation. [POS001, POS002, POS003]
