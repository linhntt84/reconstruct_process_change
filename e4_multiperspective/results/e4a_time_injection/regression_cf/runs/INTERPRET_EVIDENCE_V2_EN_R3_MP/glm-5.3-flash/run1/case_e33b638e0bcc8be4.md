### Observed differences

- Repetition stopped: in the earlier window, A30, A31, A36 and A37 each occurred about 3.3 times per trace and repeated within the same trace in about 67 in 100 traces; in the later window, each occurs about once per trace — still in every trace — and repeats in at most about 2 in 100 traces [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
- Ordering became sequential: before, about 67–68 in 100 traces showed these four activities interleaved or repeated (for example, A30 came before A31 in only about 33 in 100 traces); in the later window, A30 precedes A31, A36 and A37 in about 98–99 in 100 traces, A31 precedes A37 in about 995 in 1,000 and A36 precedes A37 in all traces, while A31 and A36 appear in both orders in similar proportions (about 56 vs 44 in 100) with virtually no interleaving [REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006]
- Routing out of A37 changed: before, about 68 in 100 of A37's observed outgoing transitions went to A30 and about 29 in 100 to A25 (670 outgoing transitions observed); in the later window, about 97 in 100 go to A25 (200 outgoing transitions) and transitions to A30 are no longer observed [DIR001]
- Gaps compressed: pairs that now follow one another (A30→A31, A31→A36, A36→A31, A31→A37, A36→A37) lie one to two steps apart in about 97–100 in 100 observed cases, where before they were spread over gaps of one to five steps; conversely, ordered pairs reflecting the old back-and-forth (for example A31 followed by A30, or A30 followed by itself) were observable in about 134 of 200 traces before but in only 1 trace after, so each of those after profiles rests on a single observation [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012]
- Positions became more regular: the spread of each of the four activities' relative positions shrank from about 0.13–0.14 to about 0.05–0.06 (standard deviation), with mean positions nearly unchanged [POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the repetitive, interleaved execution of A30, A31, A36 and A37 gave way to a single sequential pass through these activities: in the earlier window each of the four ran about 3.3 times per trace, the pairs among them were interleaved or repeated in about two thirds of traces, and control returned from A37 to A30 in about 68 in 100 of A37's outgoing transitions, whereas in the later window each activity runs once per trace, A30 comes first and A37 last with A31 and A36 adjacent in either order between them, and A37 then continues to A25 in about 97 in 100 transitions [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001]

The change is shown directly rather than inferred: repetition and participation are measured per activity with 200 of 200 traces in both windows, the ordering change is relational, and the successor change at A37 matches the disappearance of the return transitions to A30; the compressed gaps and the much tighter position spread are consistent with single, fixed-order execution [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, DIR001, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, LAG002, LAG003, LAG006, LAG007, LAG009, LAG010]

With 27 qualifying items against roughly 0.56 expected if nothing had changed, the set as a whole is unlikely to be a chance artefact, but the items converge on this one underlying shift and are not independent confirmations of it [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, DIR001, POS001, POS002, POS003, POS004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL004, REL005, REL006, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010, LAG011, LAG012]

Limitations: the six gap items on residual self-repeats and backward pairs rest on one after-window observation each and carry little weight, and the evidence cannot rule out that the later window reflects a different mix of pre-existing variants (a sequential variant displacing a heavily repeating one) rather than a modification of a single process; it also cannot say why the behaviour changed or how activities outside this block behaved [LAG001, LAG004, LAG005, LAG008, LAG011, LAG012, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004]
