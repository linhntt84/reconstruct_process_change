### Observed differences

- The relative order of two groups of activities reversed. In the earlier window, A19, A22, A25 and A26 — each present in about 20–28% of cases, together roughly one occurrence per case — always came before A20, A21, A23, A24, A27 and A31 when they co-occurred: A27 preceded A20 in about 995 of 1,000 cases, and A19 preceded A20/A21/A23/A24/A27/A31 in all of the roughly 23 in 100 cases where A19 appeared. In the later window all 32 tested pairs between these groups run in the opposite direction: A20 precedes A27 in about 985 of 1,000 cases, and A19 comes after A20, A21, A23, A24, A27 and A31 in about 19 of 100 cases. [REL001, REL007, REL010, REL015, REL023]
- Relative positions (by event order) moved coherently: A19/A22/A25/A26 shifted from mean positions of about 0.32–0.35 to about 0.68, A20/A21/A23/A24 from about 0.63 to about 0.40, A27 from 0.37 to 0.47, and A31 from 0.56 to 0.66. [POS001, POS002, POS004, POS008, POS009, POS010]
- The handovers swapped accordingly. Before, about 91–100 in 100 of the outgoing transitions of A19/A22/A25/A26 went to A27, while A20/A21/A23/A24 each sent about 21–26 in 100 of their outgoing transitions to A32; in the later window the four occasional activities send about 95–98 in 100 to A32, the always-executed four send about 21–28 in 100 to A27 and virtually none to A32, A31's outgoing transitions moved from A20/A21/A23/A24 to A19/A22/A25/A26, and A42's moved from A19/A22/A25/A26 to A20/A21/A23/A24. [DIR001, DIR004, DIR007, DIR008, DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR009, DIR010]
- Distances changed consistently: A31 reached A32 five steps later in about 96 of 100 cases before but two steps later in about 98 of 100 after; A42 reached A27 two steps later in about 92 of 100 before and typically five steps later after; pairs spanning the reordered region (A31 with A20/A21/A23/A24, and A20–A24 with A32) fell within five steps in essentially all 200 earlier cases but only about 1–3 of 200 later cases, while A26 now reaches A32 in one step in nearly all of its later occurrences. [LAG011, LAG014, LAG007, LAG001, LAG005, LAG006]
- Participation barely moved: each of A19/A22/A25/A26 still appears in about 20–28% of cases (A19 about 23 in 100 before, 20 in 100 after) and A20/A21/A23/A24/A27/A31 in all cases in both windows, so the difference lies in ordering, not in how often activities run. [POS001, POS004, POS007, POS008, POS002, POS009]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that two blocks of activities exchanged their place in the case flow around an unchanged middle segment. In the earlier window a case ran A42, then one of the alternatives A19/A22/A25/A26, then A27, then A31, then the block A20/A21/A23/A24 (in varying internal order), with A32 following about five steps after A31; in the later window A42 is followed by A20/A21/A23/A24, then A27, then A31, then one of A19/A22/A25/A26, with A32 about two steps after A31. [REL010, REL023, DIR001, DIR002, DIR009, LAG011, LAG014]

The reversal is shown directly by the relational items (all 32 cross-pairs flip, many from near-certainty in one direction to near-certainty in the other), the positional and distance shifts match it in direction and size, and the successor changes at the block boundaries (A27 ↔ A32, plus the entry points at A42 and A31) are exactly what this reorder predicts. Unchanged participation shares rule out the competing reading that activities were added, removed or executed more or less often, and the volume of qualifying evidence (66 items against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed, each with empirical strength about 0.995) rules out chance — though the items largely mirror one underlying change rather than independent confirmations. [REL001, POS009, LAG011, DIR010, POS001]

Limitations: the evidence shows the net reorder but cannot distinguish a single exchange of the two blocks from two independent moves with the same net effect, and it does not characterise the varying internal order of A20/A21/A23/A24; a few distance profiles (for example A42–A26) rest on only 1–3 later cases, so their exact distances are weakly supported even though the within-five-steps presence or absence rests on about 200 cases. [LAG013, LAG002]

Other differences: a handful of rare outgoing transitions (each about 0.5–2.5 in 100 of a source's outgoing transitions) appeared or disappeared, for example from A20/A21/A23/A24 to A05, A07, A10, A14 and A40, and from A19 and A22 to A05, A29, A30 and A40; they do not change the main picture. [DIR002, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR001, DIR004]
