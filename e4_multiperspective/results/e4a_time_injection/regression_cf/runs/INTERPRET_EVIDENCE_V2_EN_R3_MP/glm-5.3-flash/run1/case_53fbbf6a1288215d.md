### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A46 and A31 was restructured: in the earlier window each case executed exactly one of A19, A21, A22 and A23 there, as mutually exclusive alternatives each leading to A31 within about two steps, whereas in the later window cases execute A20, A28, A29 and A30 together, in mixed order, in that same segment — A46 precedes the four in about 97–98 of 100 cases, the four precede A31 in about 97–98 of 100 cases, and the A46-to-A31 distance stretched from about 2 steps to 5 or more. About 100 evidence items qualified against roughly 1 expected if nothing had changed, and the occurrence, routing, ordering and distance indicators all point to the same placement, which the relational items show directly. [OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, DIR011, REL035, REL038, REL039, REL058, REL061, REL063, LAG003]

Limitations: zero participation in the earlier window shows only that A20, A28, A29 and A30 were not observed there — if they existed at all before, they ran in well under about 2 of 100 cases — and the later outgoing profiles of A19, A21, A22 and A23 rest on only 2–4 observations each. Many relation items re-describe the same swap from different activity pairs, so they are not independent confirmations. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the two windows draw on different case populations, so the swap could reflect which cases were sampled rather than a modified single process; positions are event positions, so no clock-time timing claim is implied. [OCC003, OCC007, OCC008, OCC009, DIR003, DIR005, DIR006, DIR007]

Other differences: A06 participation halved (about 27 to about 14 of 100 cases), it occurs later and more variably when present, its outgoing traffic spread from mostly A15 to many successors, and A14 → A06 fell to about 6% of A14's outgoing; A14, A15 and A46 each occur slightly earlier (mean-position drops of about 0.01–0.03). The evidence does not show whether A06's change belongs to the same restructuring. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, POS002, POS003, POS013]
