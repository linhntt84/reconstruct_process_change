### Observed differences

- Five activities became universal. In the earlier window A22 ran in about 46 in 100 traces and A31, A32, A37 and A38 each ran in about 54 in 100; in the later window all five run in every trace, exactly once per trace (mean occurrences equal participation, no repetition in either window), so previously optional activities became mandatory rather than repeated. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]
- Entry routing changed. In the earlier window A20 always passed directly to A23, and A21 split its outgoing transitions between A22 (about 47%) and the four-activity block A31/A32/A37/A38 (about 53%). In the later window A20 always passes into the block (A38 about 29%, A32 about 27%, A37 about 26%, A31 about 19% of its outgoing transitions) and A21 always passes to A22. [DIR001, DIR002]
- Exit routing of the block changed. In the earlier window each block member passed on to A26 (about 22–30% of each one's outgoing transitions, besides transitions to other block members); in the later window the block members pass to A23 (about 21–29%) and to each other, and no longer to A26. [DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006]
- Order relations around the block flipped. Before, A21 preceded the block whenever both ran (about 54 in 100 traces) and A23 likewise preceded it; in the later window the block precedes A21 in every trace and precedes A23 in every trace. A22 and the block, previously never in the same trace (about 46 in 100 traces A22-only, 54 block-only), now co-occur in every trace with the block first. [REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016]
- A22's context stabilised. A20 before A22, A21 before A22, A23 before A22 and A22 before A26 each held in about 46 in 100 traces before (exactly the traces containing A22) and in every trace in the later window. [REL001, REL006, REL011, REL012]
- Positions and distances moved accordingly. The block's mean position fell from about 0.58–0.59 to about 0.40 of the trace, A23's rose from about 0.39 to 0.47, A21's from about 0.54 to 0.61 and A22's from about 0.59 to 0.64; the A20→A23 distance went from always 1 step to always 5 steps, and the A21→A26 distance from 2 steps (about 47% of traces) or 5 steps (about 53%) to always 2 steps. [POS003, POS004, POS006, POS007, POS008, POS009, LAG001, LAG002]
- The block's internal order stayed unfixed in both windows: for example A31 preceded A32 in about half of the traces containing both before and about 44 in 100 after, with both orders common throughout. [REL025, REL026, REL027, REL028, REL029, REL030]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the four-activity block A31, A32, A37, A38 became mandatory and was relocated: in the earlier window it formed an optional branch after A21, run in about 54 in 100 cases instead of A22 and leading to A26, whereas in the later window it runs in every case between A20 and A23, while A22 — previously the alternative in about 46 in 100 cases — now runs in every case after A21 and before A26. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL007, REL013, REL017, LAG001, LAG002]

The placement change is shown directly by successor changes (A20 now enters the block, the block now exits to A23, A21 now always goes to A22) and by the order flips from "A21/A23 first" to "block first", with the lag data matching exactly (A20→A23 distance 1 → 5 steps, i.e. the four block activities now sit between them; A21→A26 distance always 2 steps). Fifty-two evidence items qualified against roughly 0.58 expected for an unchanged process, so chance re-splits cannot account for the pattern as a whole; note that the items describe one coherent restructuring rather than independent confirmations. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR006, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, LAG001, LAG002]

Limitations: outgoing transitions of A23 and A26 were not profiled, so the connection between A23 and A21 in the later window is inferred from co-occurrence, order and positions rather than observed edges — the evidence cannot rule out unobserved activities or wiring between them. Positions are event positions, so they show the block now sits earlier relative to the other activities, not a change in clock-time timing. [REL011, REL006, POS002, POS004, LAG001]

Other differences: only minor knock-on position shifts — A20 slightly earlier (mean about 0.36 to 0.33) and A26 slightly later (about 0.64 to 0.66) — consistent with the block's new placement and not part of the main hypothesis. [POS001, POS005]
