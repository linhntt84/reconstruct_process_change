### Observed differences

- Before, A32, A33, A38 and A39 each ran in about 39 in 100 traces, and they ran together as one block (pairwise co-occurrence about 39 in 100, in varying order); in the later window each appears in only about 1–2 in 100 traces, and for pairs inside the block "neither present" rose to about 99 in 100. [OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, REL022, REL031]
- Before, 60 in 100 of A26's observed outgoing transitions went to A27 and roughly 38 in 100 went to A32, A33, A38 or A39; in the later window 97 in 100 go to A27 and the branch transitions have practically disappeared. A26 precedes A27 in about 96–97 in 100 traces after, versus 61 in 100 before. [DIR003, REL006, REL007]
- A27 became near-mandatory: it appeared in 61 in 100 traces before and 99 in 100 after (mean occurrences per trace 0.6 to 1.0). Before, A27 and the four branch activities were strict alternatives, never occurring together; after, A27 runs in almost every trace while they do not. [OCC002, REL012, REL015]
- The branch sat between A26 and A28: before, A28 followed A26 two positions later in 61 in 100 of cases and five positions later in 37 in 100; after, the two-position gap holds in about 98 in 100 cases, and A28 follows A27 in 98 in 100 traces (60 in 100 before). [LAG001, REL011, REL018]

[OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR006]
- Smaller shifts: A11's outgoing mix moved from A12 (40 to 21 in 100) toward A13 (22 to 40 in 100), and A27 now usually co-occurs with A45 and A46 (35 to 63 in 100 traces). [DIR002, REL016]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the alternative branch formed by A32, A33, A38 and A39 between A26 and A28 is now almost always bypassed. Before, about 39 in 100 traces left A26 into this four-activity block and reached A28 five positions later, while the other 61 in 100 went via A27; in the later window A26 leads to A27 in about 97 in 100 of its transitions, A27 appears in 99 in 100 traces, and A28 follows A27 in 98 in 100 traces, two positions after A26 — the block is skipped, and A27 has effectively become mandatory after A26. [DIR003, OCC002, OCC003, REL006, REL011, LAG001]

Supporting signs: 64 qualifying items against well under one expected by chance if nothing had changed; the core items rest on all or nearly all traces in both windows (DIR003 with 200 and 203 transitions, REL006–REL011 with 200 traces per window, LAG001 with 195 and 200), and the occurrence, direction, relation and lag indicators all describe the same collapse rather than one unusual indicator; every item's difference exceeds about 995 of 1000 random re-splits. [DIR003, REL006, LAG001, OCC003]

Limitations: the evidence cannot distinguish whether the four activities were removed outright or remain optional but chosen in only 1–2 in 100 traces; directional detail inside the dropped branch rests on only 2–3 after-observations; and the two-window design cannot show whether the switch was abrupt or gradual. Several indicators describe the same traces, so they are not independent confirmations. [DIR004, DIR005, OCC003]

Other differences: A10 became more frequent (8 to about 25 in 100 traces), occurs later in traces, now sends most of its flow to A46 and receives more flow from A45, often directly after it; A11's mix shifted from A12 toward A13; A27 now usually co-occurs with A45 and A46. These may be part of the same restructuring or independent of it — the evidence cannot tell. [OCC001, POS001, DIR001, DIR002, DIR006, LAG009, REL016]
