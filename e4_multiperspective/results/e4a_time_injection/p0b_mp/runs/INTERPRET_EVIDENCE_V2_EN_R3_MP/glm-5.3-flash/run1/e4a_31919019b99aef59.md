### Observed differences

- In the earlier window, the elapsed time from the completion of each of A16, A17, A18 and A19 to the completion of A27 averaged about 1.0–1.1 hours (medians about 1.02–1.08 hours). In the later window, the same elapsed times averaged about 2.1–2.2 hours (medians about 2.07–2.23 hours) — an increase of roughly one hour on every one of the four steps into A27 [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004].
- These times also became more spread out: standard deviations rose from about 0.13–0.16 hours to about 0.27–0.31 hours [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004].
- How often each step occurs barely moved: each pair was observed 45–60 times per window (for example A19→A27 55 times before vs 60 after, A18→A27 53 vs 48), and the four pairs together account for about 200 observations in each window, roughly one per trace [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004].
- Each of the four timing differences is larger than in about 1999 of 2000 random re-splits of the pooled data [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004].
- No control-flow indicator qualified: 0 items against about 0.28 expected by chance among 56 tested units [screening].

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a timing change centred on A27: in the earlier window, cases reached A27 about one hour after completion of the preceding activity (A16, A17, A18 or A19), while in the later window this elapsed time roughly doubled to about two hours, consistently across all four steps into A27, while the frequency of each step stayed stable [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004]. Supporting signs: four of the 29 tested time units qualified where only about 0.014 (roughly 1 in 70) would be expected if nothing had changed — a count many times the chance expectation — each difference exceeds about 1999 of 2000 random re-splits, and no control-flow item qualified, so the difference appears confined to timing rather than structure [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004, screening]. Limitations: T_WAIT measures elapsed time between consecutive completion events, so it cannot show where the extra hour accrues, and the four items all share A27, so they may reflect one underlying difference rather than four independent confirmations [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004]. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the additional time arises somewhere in the interval between the predecessor's completion and A27's completion rather than at A27 specifically [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004].

Other differences: none observed; the control-flow family produced no qualifying items (0 items vs about 0.28 expected by chance) [screening].
