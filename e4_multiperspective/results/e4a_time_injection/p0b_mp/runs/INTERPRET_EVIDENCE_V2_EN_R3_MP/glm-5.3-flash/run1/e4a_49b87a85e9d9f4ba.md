### Observed differences

- Two groups of activities swapped participation. In the earlier window, A14, A16, A17 and A18 each ran in roughly 20–29 of 100 traces (41, 51, 50 and 58 of 200) and never together — their shares sum to one per trace, so exactly one of them ran in each case. In the later window, none of the four was observed (0 of 200). Conversely, A15, A23, A24 and A25 were absent earlier and ran in every later trace (200 of 200). [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, REL002, REL018, REL019, REL025]

- A41's routing changed completely: earlier, its observed outgoing transitions went only to A14/A16/A17/A18 (about 21/26/25/29 in 100 transitions); later, only to A15/A23/A24/A25 (about 28/27/21/24 in 100). [DIR009, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, DIR002, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008]

- In the later window, every pair among A15/A23/A24/A25 co-occurs in all traces and appears in both orders in near-equal shares (e.g. A15 before A23 in about 53 of 100 traces, A23 before A15 in about 48), i.e. no fixed order among them; each of the four always runs after A41 and before A26. Their outgoing transitions also spread almost evenly over the other three and A26, consistent with this unfixed order. [REL013, REL014, REL015, REL036, REL037, REL040, REL016, REL038, REL041, REL043, REL017, REL039, REL042, REL044, DIR002, DIR006, DIR007, DIR008]

- Earlier, the single alternative activity sat on the A41–A26 path: whenever A14 (or A16/A17/A18) occurred, it was always after A41 and before A26. [REL008, REL009, REL023, REL024, REL029, REL030, DIR001, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005]

- The distance from A41 to A26 grew from 2 steps in essentially all earlier traces to 5 steps in all later traces; A26's mean position moved later (about 0.39 to 0.45 of the trace) and A41's slightly earlier (about 0.33 to 0.29). [LAG001, POS009, POS010]

- The elapsed time between consecutive completions of A28 and A29 fell from a median of about 1.0 hour to about 0.5 hour (mean about 1.01 to 0.50 hours), based on 200 observations in each window. [TWAIT001]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports a restructuring of the segment between A41 and A26. In the earlier window, each trace ran A41, then exactly one of the mutually exclusive alternatives A14, A16, A17 or A18, then A26 two steps later; in the later window, A14/A16/A17/A18 were no longer observed, and A15, A23, A24 and A25 all ran in every trace between A41 and A26, in no fixed order relative to each other, so A26 now lies five steps after A41. This is shown directly and consistently: the participation swap is categorical (0 vs 200 of 200 traces), A41's successor set changed as a whole, the relational items place all four new activities after A41 and before A26, and the A41→A26 lag moved from 2 to 5 steps; with 72 control-flow items against roughly 0.6 expected by chance, sampling variation cannot explain the pattern as a whole. Limitations: zero participation shows only that A14/A16/A17/A18 were not observed later, not that they became impossible, and the mixed orders cannot distinguish a truly parallel block from a sequence whose order varies case by case. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, OCC006, OCC007, OCC008, DIR009, REL013, REL016, REL017, LAG001]

Other differences: the elapsed time between the consecutive completions of A28 and A29 halved (median about 1.0 to about 0.5 hour) — a consistent timing change that stands as a hypothesis in its own right but that the evidence does not connect to the structural change above. [TWAIT001]
