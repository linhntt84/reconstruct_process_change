Main hypothesis: a change in timing — in the later window, the elapsed time from the completion of A02 to the consecutive completion of A07 is roughly twice as long as before (mean about 0.86 h → about 1.79 h, with the middle half of intervals shifting from about 0.75–0.98 h to about 1.64–1.97 h). Chance is also an unlikely explanation at the screening level: the time family produced one item against about 0.008 expected without change across 17 time units — an excess an unchanged process would show in only about 1 of 125 analyses — while the control-flow family produced no items (0 against about 0.28 expected across 57 units), so no structural counterpart is indicated and none is required for a timing hypothesis.
