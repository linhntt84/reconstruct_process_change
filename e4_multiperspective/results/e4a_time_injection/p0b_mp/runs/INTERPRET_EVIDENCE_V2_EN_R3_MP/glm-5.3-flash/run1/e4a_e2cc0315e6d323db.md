### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that, in the later window, about 42 in 100 traces skip A14, A15 and the A20/A21 step, with A37 now leading directly to A22 instead of through A14: in these traces A14, A15, A20 and A21 are all absent, while A37 and A22 still occur in every trace, and the remaining traces keep the earlier structure, including the A14-before-A15 order and the roughly even choice between A20 and A21. The sign is direct — the A37→A22 transition and the one-step A37→A22 succession appear only in the later window — and consistent, since the same share of about 42 in 100 recurs across the participation, co-occurrence and lag indicators; with 20 control-flow items against fewer than 1 expected without change, chance sampling cannot account for the pattern as a whole. The main competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that this is a re-routing of a subset of cases within an otherwise unchanged process rather than a newly introduced optional path, and the two aggregate windows cannot show when the behaviour began. [DIR002, LAG001, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, REL001, REL002, REL003, REL010, REL011, POS001]

Other differences: Timing changed in ways the structural hypothesis does not explain: elapsed time between consecutive completions into A10 halved for both A09→A10 and A11→A10, and the A37→A14 elapsed time doubled in the traces that still run A14 — a timing change in its own right. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003] Routing from A18 also shifted, with more outgoing transitions to A27 and fewer to A16. [DIR001]
