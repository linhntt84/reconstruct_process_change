### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the segment formed by A14 and A15, containing the either/or choice between A20 and A21, became skippable between A37 and A22: in the later window, in about 42 in 100 cases A37 leads directly to A22 and none of A14, A15, A20 or A21 is executed, while in about 58 in 100 cases the original sequence A37 → A14 → (A20 or A21) → A15 → A22 is retained. This rests on direct relational evidence — the new direct A37→A22 successor and the A37–A22 distance dropping from always four steps to one step in about 42 in 100 cases — together with the joint disappearance of A14/A15 and the proportionate drop of A20/A21 in exactly that share of traces, while A37 and A22 remain in all traces and the internal A20/A21 split is unchanged. The 22 evidence items far exceed the roughly 0.47 expected by chance for 95 tested units, though the many relation items largely mirror one underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. Limitations: two finite windows cannot show when within the later window the behaviour changed, and an important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the window boundary cuts through a gradual transition, so the roughly 58-in-100 retention could be a mixture of old and new behaviour rather than a stable skip rate.

Other differences: the routing shares out of A18 changed (A27 up from about 40% to 58%, A16 down from about 36% to 18%) [DIR001]; the elapsed time between A09/A11 and A10 completions halved [TWAIT001, TWAIT002]; and in traces that still contain A37 followed by A14, the elapsed time between them roughly doubled, a timing change the structural hypothesis does not explain [TWAIT003]. A22's slight positional shift is consistent with the skip [POS001].
