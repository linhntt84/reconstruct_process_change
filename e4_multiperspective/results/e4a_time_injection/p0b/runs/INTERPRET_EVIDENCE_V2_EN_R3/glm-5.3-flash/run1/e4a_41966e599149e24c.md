### Observed differences

- Five activities — A22, A31, A32, A37 and A38 — ran in every trace in the earlier window (participation 100 in 100, at most once per trace), but in the later window each runs in only about 50 in 100 traces. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the flow was rerouted around the four-activity block A31/A32/A37/A38 and that this block became an alternative to A22. In the earlier window every trace ran A20, then the four activities in a mixed order, then A23, A21, A22 and A26; in the later window A20 leads directly to A23, and after A21 the trace runs either A22 or the four activities — essentially never both, roughly 50 in 100 traces each — with the block, when it runs, flowing into A26.

The successor changes at A20 and A21 are observed directly, as is the mutual exclusivity between A22 and each block activity; the order relations place the block after A21/A23 and before A26 in the later window; the participation drops to about half for all five activities match a 50/50 branch; and the A21-to-A26 distance split of two versus five steps matches the two branches [DIR001, DIR002, REL013, REL014, REL015, REL016, REL007, REL008, REL009, REL010, REL017, REL018, REL019, REL020, REL021, REL022, REL023, REL024, OCC001, OCC002, OCC003, OCC004, OCC005, LAG001, LAG006]. With 61 evidence items against roughly 0.74 expected for an unchanged process, sampling variation cannot account for the pattern as a whole. A competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the aggregate 50/50 split reflects two systematically different case populations in the later window rather than a per-case routing choice; the indicators are aggregate and cannot distinguish these. A few lag items rest on a single later-window observation and were not relied upon [LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG007, LAG008, LAG009, LAG010].

Other differences: the elapsed time between consecutive completions of A42 and A43 roughly doubled (median about 1.0 hour to about 2.0 hours), a timing change not covered by the routing hypothesis above. [TWAIT001]
