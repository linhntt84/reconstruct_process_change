In the later window it was typically about 2 hours (medians about 2.0–2.2 hours, 38–64 pairs) — an increase of roughly 1 hour, i.e. the gap roughly doubled for all four activity pairs. ### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests one specific behavioural change: in the later window, the elapsed time between the completion of each of A16, A17, A18 or A19 and the subsequent completion of A27 is about one hour longer than before, with typical gaps of about 2 hours instead of about 1 hour — a consistent timing shift centred on A27 that affects all four predecessor activities alike. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004]

Supporting signs: four qualifying items were flagged where only about 0.37 would be expected by chance across the 74 tested units, all with the same direction and near-identical magnitude, and each with an empirical strength of 0.995. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004]

What is missing: the evidence contains only timing items, with no participation, ordering, routing or positional indicators, so it cannot show whether any control-flow structure changed or which structural change, if any, produced the longer gaps; the four items also all involve A27, so they may reflect a single underlying difference rather than independent confirmations. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004]

An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out: the process structure may be unchanged and only the timing between these consecutive completions may have shifted; since the indicator measures elapsed time between completion events, the increase could arise anywhere along the span between the two activities and need not point to a specific activity or path. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004]

Other differences: the frequency of observed consecutive pairs moved slightly and inconsistently (A16→A27 up, the other three pairs slightly down); at 200 traces per window these shifts are small and the evidence cannot separate them from sampling variation. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004]
