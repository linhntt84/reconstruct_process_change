### Observed differences

- **Routing out of A24 narrowed from four options to two.** In the earlier window, A24's observed outgoing transitions were spread almost evenly over A16, A17, A18 and A19 (roughly 23–28 in 100 each); in the later window, about 55 in 100 go to A17 and 44 in 100 to A16, while A18 and A19 together receive only about 1–2 in 100. [DIR005]

- **The direct transitions from A16 and A17 to A25 have practically disappeared.** Earlier, about 52 in 100 of A16's and 48 in 100 of A17's outgoing transitions went straight to A25; in the later window this holds for only about 2 and 1 in 100, and A16/A17 instead send about 57/40 and 54/45 in 100 of their transitions to A19/A18. [DIR001, DIR002]

- **A18 and A19 now lead almost exclusively to A25.** Earlier, about 48 in 100 of A18's and 51 in 100 of A19's outgoing transitions went to A25, with most of the rest returning to A16 or A17; in the later window about 98–99 in 100 go to A25 and the returns to A16/A17 are no longer observed. [DIR003, DIR004]

- **Positional gaps became fixed.** Earlier, the distance from A24 to each of the four activities, and from A16/A17 to A25, was mixed between one and two steps (roughly half each); in the later window, A24 is followed by A16 or A17 after one step and by A18 or A19 after two steps (at least 98 in 100 each), A18/A19 are followed by A25 after one step (at least 98 in 100), and A16/A17 reach A25 within five steps almost only at distance two, i.e. with one activity in between. [LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008]

- **Elapsed time between consecutive completions grew on exactly these transitions.** For the pairs A16/A17 followed by A18/A19, the mean rose from about 0.1–0.2 hours to about 1.0 hour, and the number of such consecutive pairs roughly doubled to tripled (for example A17 then A19: 20 to 60); for A18/A19 followed by A25, the mean rose from about 1.05 to about 2.0 hours, with counts rising as well (A19 then A25: 48 to 111). [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004, TWAIT005, TWAIT006]

- **Participation shifted only mildly.** A18 appears in 53 in 100 traces earlier but 44 in 100 later, A19 in 47 versus 56 in 100; both occur slightly later within the trace (mean relative position about 0.65 to 0.68). [POS001, POS002]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the possibility that the block of activities A16, A17, A18 and A19 between A24 and A25, which earlier could be traversed flexibly — A24 leading to any of the four, A25 directly following A16/A17 in about half of their outgoing transitions, and A18/A19 often leading back to A16/A17 — now runs as a fixed two-stage sequence A24, then A16 or A17, then A18 or A19, then A25. In the later window the direct transitions A16 to A25 and A17 to A25 and the direct entries from A24 to A18/A19 are no longer observed, A18 and A19 lead almost solely to A25, and the elapsed time between the two stages grew roughly seven-fold (about 0.1–0.2 to about 1 hour) and between the second stage and A25 nearly doubled (about 1 to 2 hours). [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG001, LAG002, LAG003, LAG004, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004, TWAIT005, TWAIT006]

The hypothesis rests on direct, mutually consistent relational evidence: eighteen items qualified where about 0.44 would be expected for an unchanged process, all five routing and all eight gap items describe the same rewiring, each item is backed by roughly 88–200 observations per window, and every difference exceeds 99.5 in 100 random re-splits, making rare-variant and sampling explanations unlikely. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, DIR005, LAG005, LAG006, LAG007, LAG008, TWAIT004, TWAIT006]

Limitations: the near-zero shares show that the old direct edges were not observed in the later window, not that they are impossible — a residual direct path from A16/A17 to A25 taken at about 1–2 in 100 of transitions cannot be excluded; and the timing increases, while occurring exactly on the transitions the new sequence uses, could be a separate timing change on the same transitions rather than a consequence of the reordering. [DIR001, DIR002, DIR003, DIR004, TWAIT005, TWAIT006]

Other differences: the choice between the two second-stage activities shifted somewhat toward A19 (A18 down from 53 to 44 in 100 traces, A19 up from 47 to 56 in 100), and both now sit slightly later within the trace; these small shifts are consistent with the fixed sequence rather than independent changes. [POS001, POS002]
