- The set of activities running between A41 and A26 changed completely. ### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that the segment between A41 and A26 was restructured: in the earlier window each case executed exactly one of A14, A16, A17 or A18 between A41 and A26, whereas in the later window every case executes A15, A23, A24 and A25 once each, in no fixed order among themselves, still between A41 and A26. The change is shown directly and consistently: participation flipped with full 200-trace support in both windows, A41's successor profile changed completely, the relational items show the old four behaving as mutually exclusive alternatives while the new four always execute together in mixed order, and the A41→A26 distance grew from 2 to 5 event positions. Screening expected only about 0.71 qualifying items without a change, against 73 observed, so chance sampling cannot explain the pattern as a whole. [OCC001, OCC002, OCC006, DIR009, REL013, REL016, LAG001]

Limitations: zero participation strictly means only "not observed in that window", and the evidence cannot rule out that A15, A23, A24 and A25 are relabelled replacements of A14, A16, A17 and A18 rather than genuinely added work; either way the executed structure differs, but the added-versus-renamed question remains open. The mixed order among the new four is a change in ordering constraints, not an exchange of positions. [OCC001, OCC002]

Other differences: the elapsed time between consecutive completions of A28 and A29 roughly halved (median about 1.0 to about 0.5 hour); the evidence provides no link between this and the reordering above. [TWAIT001]
