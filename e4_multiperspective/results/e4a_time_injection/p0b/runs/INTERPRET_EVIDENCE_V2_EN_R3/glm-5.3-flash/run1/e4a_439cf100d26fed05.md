### Observed differences

- **A25 and A26 now repeat within traces.** In the earlier window, every trace contained A25 exactly once and A26 exactly once (participation 100 in 100 traces, mean 1.0 occurrence each, no repeats). In the later window, both still appear in every trace but average about 1.6 occurrences each, with more than one occurrence in about 36 in 100 traces (previously none). [OCC001, OCC002]
- **A26 gained an additional successor.** Earlier, A26 was followed by A20 after essentially all of its executions (100 in 100 of its outgoing transitions). In the later window, about 64 in 100 executions still continue to A20, while about 36 in 100 go to A25 — a successor not observed for A26 in the earlier window (based on 200 vs 312 outgoing transitions). [DIR002]
- **The fixed A25→A26 order loosened.** Earlier, A25 preceded A26 in every trace, always immediately (one position apart). In the later window, that single fixed sequence holds in about 64 in 100 traces, about 36 in 100 traces show the two activities interleaved or repeated, and immediate succession accounts for only about 37 in 100 of the observed A25→A26 pairs, with small shares at position gaps of three and five. [REL001, LAG001]
- **Branching at A01 shifted between existing alternatives.** Earlier, A01's outgoing transitions led to A04 in about 42 in 100 cases, to A05 in about 24 and to A03 in about 34; in the later window the split is about 20 to A04, 45 to A05 and 34 to A03 — flow moved from A04 to A05 while A03 stayed stable (97 vs 88 outgoing transitions). [DIR001]
- **Elapsed completion-to-completion times roughly halved.** For A12–A33 the median fell from about 2.0 to about 1.0 hours (59 observations in each window), for A13–A33 from about 2.0 to about 1.0 hours (42 vs 50), and for A32–A33 from about 1.1 to about 0.5 hours (99 vs 91). [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003]

### Inference

Support level: Supported hypothesis

The evidence supports the hypothesis that, in the later window, the A25–A26 sequence became repeatable within a single trace: A25 and A26, previously executed exactly once per trace in a fixed immediate order with A26 always continuing to A20, are now executed about 1.6 times per trace on average, appear interleaved or repeated in about 36 in 100 traces, and A26 is followed again by A25 in about 36 in 100 of its executions. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, REL001, LAG001]

The signs are direct and mutually consistent: a newly observed successor for A26, the pair's relation changing from a fixed order to interleaving/repetition in about a third of traces, matching rises in occurrence counts, and the loss of immediate succession — all of similar size (roughly 36 in 100) on 200-trace windows, each difference larger than in all but about 1 in 200 random re-splits; the nine qualifying items also far exceed the roughly 0.36 expected for an unchanged process, so the ensemble is unlikely to be a sampling artefact. [OCC001, OCC002, DIR002, REL001, LAG001]

Limitations: the five items all describe one underlying trace behaviour, so they form one line of evidence rather than independent confirmations, and the outgoing transitions of A25 were not profiled, so the exact course of the repetition — what follows the repeated A25, and whether other activities sit between repeats, as the wider position gaps suggest — cannot be determined from this evidence. [LAG001, DIR002] An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is that the extra executions of A25 and A26 stem from a second, structurally separate occurrence of the pair rather than from a direct return from A26 to A25. [DIR002]

Other differences: routing at A01 redistributed between existing alternatives (A04 from about 42 to about 20 in 100 of its outgoing transitions, A05 from about 24 to about 45, A03 stable at about 34; both target paths are well observed in both windows, so this is a shift of shares, not a replaced path). [DIR001] Separately, the elapsed time between consecutive completions shortened to roughly half for A12–A33, A13–A33 and A32–A33; this measures elapsed time between completion events, not pure waiting or processing time, and the evidence does not link it to the A25/A26 change. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003]
