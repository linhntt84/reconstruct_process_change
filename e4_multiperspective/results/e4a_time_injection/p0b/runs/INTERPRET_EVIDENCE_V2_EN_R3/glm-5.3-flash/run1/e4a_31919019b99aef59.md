### Inference

Support level: Weak hypothesis

The evidence suggests that A27 now takes place about one hour later after its predecessors than before: in the later window, the elapsed time between the completion of A16, A17, A18 or A19 and the completion of A27 is consistently about 2.1 hours instead of about 1 hour, while these successions still occur at similar rates (roughly 23–30 in 100 traces in both windows). Supporting signs: four predecessor pairs show the same shift, each with an empirical strength of about 0.995, and four qualifying items are roughly ten times the number expected by chance without a change (0.42), so the pattern as a whole is unlikely under random sampling — although the four items may partly reflect the same traces and are not fully independent confirmations. [TWAIT001, TWAIT002, TWAIT003, TWAIT004]

What is missing: the evidence contains no relational or positional items, so it cannot show whether an activity was inserted between the predecessors and A27, whether A27 was moved, or whether only the timing changed with the control flow untouched; T_WAIT measures elapsed time between consecutive completion events, not pure waiting or processing time, so the extra hour cannot be attributed to a specific cause. An important competing explanation the evidence cannot rule out is a scheduling or calendar change around A27 that produces the same timing shift without any structural difference.
